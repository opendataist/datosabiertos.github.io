Exigen informar sobre emisiones contaminantes

The INAI mandated Pemex to provide detailed greenhouse gas emission reduction data, ruling that merely publishing online links is insufficient for true transparency. This decision emphasizes that public access requires delivering specific, usable information rather than just directing citizens to websites where the actual details are missing. Transparency demands that entities proactively supply accessible data, ensuring citizens can effectively consult and utilize the information they request. The ruling clarifies that digital publication alone does not fulfill legal obligations if the specific, granular data remains difficult to obtain or is not properly organized for the user. This case is crucial for open data as it establishes a precedent for data usability and granularity. It reinforces the principle that open data must be machine-readable and easily accessible, pushing organizations to move beyond basic disclosure toward meaningful, user-centric transparency standards in environmental reporting.
