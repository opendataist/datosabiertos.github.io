The 2024 El Buen Fin event significantly exceeded expectations, driving substantial economic reactivation in Mexico through enthusiastic consumer participation. Electronic and technological products emerged as the dominant sector, reflecting a clear shift in purchasing power toward digital goods. This success highlights the effectiveness of strategic promotional tactics, particularly direct discounts and financing options, which aligned perfectly with current market demands. Social media played a pivotal role in disseminating offers, underscoring the critical importance of digital channels in modern retail strategies. Furthermore, the inclusion of diverse sectors like tourism and education broadened the campaign’s appeal, demonstrating the commercial sector’s adaptability to evolving consumer trends. This widespread engagement not only stimulated internal consumption but also generated employment, reinforcing the event’s status as a major economic driver. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates how aggregated public commerce metrics can reveal macroeconomic health and consumer behavior patterns. Transparent access to such sales data allows researchers and policymakers to analyze market dynamics accurately. By making these preliminary results publicly available, stakeholders can better understand the impact of digital transformation on traditional retail, fostering data-driven decision-making for future economic initiatives and ensuring greater accountability in commercial reporting.
