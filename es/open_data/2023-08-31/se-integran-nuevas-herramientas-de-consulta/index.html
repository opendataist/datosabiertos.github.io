Se integran nuevas herramientas de consulta

The Ministry of Finance introduced interactive dashboards on its transparency portal to enhance social audit and accountability. These tools simplify access to complex budget execution data, allowing citizens to easily retrieve specific or detailed information through intuitive graphs and tables. By providing user-friendly interfaces and educational resources, the initiative empowers citizens to monitor government spending effectively. This design focuses on meeting diverse informational needs, ensuring that fiscal data is accessible and understandable for non-technical users seeking transparency. This development is crucial for open data as it transforms raw financial statistics into actionable, reusable insights. By lowering barriers to data consumption, it fosters civic engagement and strengthens democratic oversight, demonstrating how well-designed open data platforms can drive societal accountability and informed public discourse.
