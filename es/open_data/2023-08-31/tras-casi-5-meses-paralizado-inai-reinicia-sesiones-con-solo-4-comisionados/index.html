Tras casi 5 meses paralizado, INAI reinicia sesiones con sólo 4 comisionados

The Mexican Transparency Institute regained legal quorum to resume operations after a Supreme Court ruling validated meetings with only four commissioners. This decision breaks months of political paralysis caused by Senate failures to appoint full leadership. The institute can finally process thousands of pending transparency appeals, including high-profile cases involving former officials. This restores its capacity to enforce public access to information and protect personal data rights. This development is vital for open data as it reactivates the primary mechanism for citizen oversight. It ensures the government remains accountable, allowing the public to demand and receive essential information about state actions and expenditures.
