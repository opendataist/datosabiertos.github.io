Galicia, calle a calle ante las elecciones municipales: el mapa de la renta en cada barrio y parroquia

The INE atlas reveals stark economic and educational inequalities in Galicia at a hyper-local level, showing that significant disparities exist even within individual neighborhoods. By linking household data with tax records, this open resource allows citizens to visualize income and education levels in their specific census sections, rather than relying on broad municipal averages. This granular transparency highlights that few areas have high concentrations of university-educated residents, while rural zones with older populations lag behind in educational attainment. Such detailed geospatial data empowers voters to understand the socio-economic context of their communities before casting ballots in the municipal elections. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publishing high-resolution, linked datasets fosters civic engagement and social awareness. It illustrates the value of accessible, precise information in revealing hidden structural inequalities, enabling informed public discourse and policy-making based on real, localized evidence rather than generalized statistics.
