Bloqueos evistas generaron el 3,77% de inflación en 2024

Blockades driven by political conflict significantly exacerbated Bolivia’s inflation, causing severe financial strain on families. This instability highlights how social unrest distorts market mechanisms, directly impacting economic stability and household welfare. The substantial cost of these disruptions underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible data to monitor such socio-economic impacts effectively. Reliable open data enables policymakers and citizens to understand and mitigate the real-world consequences of political volatility on inflation and general well-being.
