Al menos 8,565 personas murieron o desaparecieron en las rutas migratorias en el mundo

This article highlights a critical open data initiative by the IOM, which maintains the only public database tracking migrant deaths. This transparency is vital for accurately documenting the humanitarian crisis, revealing that 2023 was the deadliest year on record with over 8,500 fatalities. The data exposes a 20% increase from the previous year, underscoring the urgent need for safe migration pathways. The database’s accessibility allows researchers and advocates to identify high-risk zones like the Mediterranean and Sahara. However, the IOM notes that actual figures likely exceed recorded numbers due to difficulties in collecting data from remote areas. This gap illustrates the limitations of current open data efforts in fully capturing global mobility challenges, particularly in inaccessible regions. Ultimately, the report emphasizes that while open data improves visibility, systemic issues persist. Governments are urged to ensure secure migration routes, as available legal options remain insufficient. The publication reinforces the importance of maintaining robust, open datasets to drive policy changes and protect vulnerable populations moving across borders.
