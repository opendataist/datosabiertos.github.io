INE prepara la Encovi 2023

The INE is conducting the Encovi 2023 to update national cartography and assess living conditions through home visits. Citizens are urged to participate, as their confidential data is vital for understanding socioeconomic realities. This initiative determines the living standards of the population. This effort highlights the importance of reliable national statistics for open data ecosystems. Transparent, accessible, and accurate data enables better policy analysis and public accountability. It ensures that societal conditions are measured rigorously. Ultimately, this survey provides the foundational dataset required for tracking development goals. By prioritizing privacy and verification, it builds trust in official statistics. This fosters an environment where open data can effectively inform decision-making and social progress.
