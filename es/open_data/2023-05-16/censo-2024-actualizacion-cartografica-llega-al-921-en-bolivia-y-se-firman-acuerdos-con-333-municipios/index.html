Censo 2024: Actualización Cartográfica llega al 92,1% en Bolivia y se firman acuerdos con 333 municipios

The Bolivian National Institute of Statistics has secured cooperation agreements with nearly all municipalities, demonstrating high-level state prioritization for the upcoming census. This widespread collaboration ensures that demographic data collection will be supported by comprehensive local engagement and administrative coordination across the country. Parallel efforts in cartographic updates are progressing significantly, though remote areas face challenges from weather conditions. These geographical updates are critical for defining precise work zones, allowing officials to accurately estimate necessary ballots, personnel, and logistical resources for the national count. This progress is vital for open data as it establishes a robust, geographically accurate foundation for future statistical transparency. By finalizing these maps before the census, the INE enables more precise, accessible, and reliable public data, fostering better informed decision-making and accountability in governance.
