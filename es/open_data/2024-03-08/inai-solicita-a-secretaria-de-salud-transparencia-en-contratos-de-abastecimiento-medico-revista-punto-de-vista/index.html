INAI solicita a Secretaría de Salud transparencia en contratos de abastecimiento médico | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI urges the Health Secretariat to disclose detailed contract information regarding medicine and vaccine acquisitions, addressing critical supply shortages. This mandate stems from public demand for transparency in how resources are managed to ensure adequate healthcare coverage, highlighting the government's obligation to provide clear data on procurement progress. Transparency is essential to monitor large-scale initiatives like the Megafarmacia del Bienestar. By revealing contract specifics, authorities can verify whether public funds effectively reach clinics and pharmacies. This oversight helps citizens understand the reality of drug availability and holds institutions accountable for unfulfilled promises regarding medication distribution. This case underscores open data’s role in strengthening democratic accountability. When essential public services face crises, accessible and detailed information allows society to track governmental efficiency and resource allocation. Ensuring such data is publicly available empowers citizens to exercise their right to know, fostering trust in health policies and improving service delivery through informed public scrutiny.
