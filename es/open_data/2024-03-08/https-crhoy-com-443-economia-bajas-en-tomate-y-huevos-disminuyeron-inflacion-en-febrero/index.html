Datos Abiertos (Open data)

The Consumer Price Index showed a slight annual decrease, driven by mixed fluctuations across goods and services. This highlights how diverse market forces interact to shape broader economic indicators. Understanding these variations is crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent price tracking enables public accountability. It allows citizens and researchers to monitor inflation trends and assess the real impact of economic policies on daily life. Ultimately, such data supports informed decision-making by revealing which sectors are becoming more affordable or expensive. This transparency fosters trust in statistical institutions and empowers society to engage with economic realities through accessible, reliable information.
