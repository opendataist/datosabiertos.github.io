El precio de la vivienda se acerca a los niveles de la burbuja tras encarecerse un 4% en 2023

The article concludes that Spanish housing prices have shown remarkable resilience, continuing to rise significantly despite sharp increases in mortgage rates and the broader Eurozone trend of cooling markets. This sustained growth highlights a distinct local dynamic where demand continues to outpace supply, creating upward pressure on prices that monetary tightening has failed to curb effectively. This divergence from regional trends is driven by a structural imbalance between housing supply and household formation, compounded by an industry that remains constrained following the previous real estate bubble. Consequently, new construction has stalled, forcing reliance on the secondary market while intensifying price tensions in demographically active regions, even as slight quarterly moderation occurred at the end of the period. This analysis is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical indicators, such as the INE’s Housing Price Index, to track long-term market behavior and validate economic hypotheses. By comparing these public datasets against regional and international benchmarks, stakeholders can accurately diagnose supply-demand disequilibria and assess the real impact of policy measures, ensuring transparency in one of the most critical sectors of the national economy.
