"Ataques día a día": Julieta del Río, comisionada del INAI (EN VIVO)

The INAI, a crucial guardian of transparency, faces persistent political pressure aimed at its elimination, yet it continues to uncover corruption through investigative collaborations. Its current operational status relies on judicial intervention after legislative failures to appoint commissioners, highlighting the fragility of its institutional independence. This situation underscores the vital role of open data in maintaining democratic accountability and exposing government misconduct. When transparency institutions are weakened, access to public information diminishes, directly impairing citizens' ability to monitor power and ensuring the integrity of public administration systems.
