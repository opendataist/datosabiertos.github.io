El Ministerio de Defensa aprueba más de 4.800 millones para su plan de compras de 2026 - Crónica de Cantabria

The Ministry of Defense approved its 2026 procurement plan, aiming to enhance transparency and efficiency through open data. This initiative provides detailed contractual information to economic operators, ensuring clear visibility of future acquisitions. Consequently, it supports open data principles by promoting accessible, structured public procurement information for broader scrutiny and market participation.
