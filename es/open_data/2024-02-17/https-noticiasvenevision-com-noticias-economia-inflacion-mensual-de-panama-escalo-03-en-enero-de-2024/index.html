Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Panama’s statistical institute reports a slight rise in January consumer prices, driven primarily by housing energy costs and air travel. This data highlights how specific sectors influence national economic indicators, offering transparency into price fluctuations for residents and investors. The release of this official inflation metric exemplifies open data principles by making economic health accessible to the public. Such transparency allows citizens to track living costs, fostering accountability in how government statistics reflect real-world purchasing power changes. Open data empowers society to verify government narratives through independent analysis. By providing granular details on price drivers, institutions enable deeper civic engagement and more informed public discourse regarding monetary policy and economic stability.
