El precio de la vivienda seguirá por encima del IPC hasta 2029

The recent surge in mortgage signings and rising housing prices indicates the emergence of a new real estate cycle, driven by increased financial appetite for credit despite higher interest rates. This trend suggests a robust market recovery where lenders are actively granting loans, signaling a shift from previous crisis periods toward a phase of renewed investment and transaction activity. Predictions highlight that residential prices will accelerate their growth throughout the year, varying significantly by location. This divergence underscores the heterogeneous nature of the market, where regional dynamics and property types dictate specific trajectories, yet the overall national trend points toward sustained appreciation supported by economic stabilization and employment consistency. Relevance to open data lies in the critical role of transparent, official statistics from institutions like the INE in shaping these market analyses. Accurate public data on mortgages and valuations enables experts to identify regional disparities, forecast trends, and understand the complex interplay between rental growth, supply shortages, and credit access. Such transparency is essential for stakeholders to navigate an increasingly complex housing landscape and make informed decisions based on empirical evidence rather than speculation.
