INEGI reporta crecimiento económico en Tamaulipas superior al 3% - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

The 2024 Economic Census reveals that Tamaulipas’ economy grew beyond initial forecasts, offering a comprehensive snapshot of the state's commercial landscape. This exhaustive data collection covers all municipalities, ensuring no sector is overlooked in assessing current economic structures. Unlike previous cycles, this survey provides crucial insights into post-pandemic recovery and structural shifts. It enables policymakers and businesses to identify leading sectors and understand variations in establishment counts, facilitating more informed strategic decisions. This update is vital for open data initiatives, as it fills the gap since 2019 by making detailed, localized economic metrics accessible. Transparently sharing these findings supports accountability and allows the public to engage with verified, high-resolution data regarding regional development and recovery trends.
