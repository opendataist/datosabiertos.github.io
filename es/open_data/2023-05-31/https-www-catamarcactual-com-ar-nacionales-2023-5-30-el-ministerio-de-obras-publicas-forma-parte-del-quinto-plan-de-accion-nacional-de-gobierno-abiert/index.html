Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Fifth National Action Plan for Open Government 2022-2024 emphasizes transparency, accountability, and citizen participation as essential tools for innovating public management. By involving various government ministries and civil society organizations, the plan aims to build an open, federal state that delivers effective policies and promotes social inclusion. This collaborative approach seeks to restore public trust by ensuring that government actions are responsive to societal needs and demands. A central focus is the Ministry of Public Works’ commitment to institutionalizing citizen oversight in public infrastructure projects. Through the creation of an Observatory and a manual of standards, the ministry fosters a model where accountability is a driving force for improvement. This strategy integrates local and provincial actors, reorienting priorities based on civil society feedback and establishing a cooperative framework for managing public resources and infrastructure. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it operationalizes transparency through digital tools like MapaInversiones. By providing real-time visibility into public investments and enabling citizens to report irregularities, the platform transforms raw data into actionable public oversight. Furthermore, the creation of a bank of experiences and shared information platforms demonstrates how open data facilitates collective policy improvement, ensuring that civic engagement is grounded in accessible, reliable, and timely information.
