Baja tasa de desempleo en México 2.7% en primer trimestre del 2023: Inegi

Mexico’s labor market demonstrated significant recovery in early 2023, with unemployment dropping to historic lows and active workforce participation rising substantially. This improvement reflects a robust reintegration of workers into the formal economy, particularly in commerce and manufacturing sectors. However, a substantial portion of the labor force remains underemployed, highlighting challenges in job quality and hours availability despite the overall decline in joblessness. Crucially, this positive employment trend coexists with a rise in labor informality and persistent gender disparities. Over half of the workforce is engaged in informal activities, lacking social protections, while female labor participation remains significantly lower than that of men. These structural issues undermine the sustainability of job growth and indicate that economic recovery has not been evenly distributed across demographic groups or sectors. This data is vital for the open_data community as it underscores the necessity of accessible, granular statistical information to monitor socio-economic conditions. Transparent labor metrics enable researchers, policymakers, and civil society to identify inefficiencies, advocate for equitable reforms, and hold institutions accountable. Open access to such detailed indicators fosters evidence-based decision-making and promotes transparency in public administration.
