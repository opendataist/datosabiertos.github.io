Senado tiene 3 días para nombrar a comisionado

A federal judge ordered the Senate to convene extraordinary sessions to appoint a new INAI commissioner. This ruling ensures the institution maintains its independent oversight role, which is vital for protecting citizens' right to information. Strengthening this body reinforces transparency and accountability in public administration.
