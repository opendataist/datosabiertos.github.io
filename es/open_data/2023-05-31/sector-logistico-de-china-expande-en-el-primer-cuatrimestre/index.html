Sector logístico de China expande en el primer cuatrimestre

China’s logistics sector demonstrates stable growth in the early months of the year. Industrial logistics and total revenues both show positive trends, reflecting a resilient economic activity. This data highlights the dynamic performance of the Chinese supply chain. The consistent rise in key metrics indicates sustained demand and operational efficiency within the industry. Such detailed statistical reporting is vital for open data initiatives.
