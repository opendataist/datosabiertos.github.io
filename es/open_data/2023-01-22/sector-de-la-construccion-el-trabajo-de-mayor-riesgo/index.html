Sector de la construcción, el trabajo de mayor riesgo

Open data reveals critical workplace safety gaps in Coahuila, highlighting construction and food sectors as high-risk environments. This transparency allows stakeholders to identify vulnerable demographics and common injury patterns without relying on opaque reporting. The data exposes severe human costs, including permanent disabilities and fatalities, primarily affecting middle-aged workers. These insights are vital for open_data initiatives, as they democratize access to public health information. Such transparency empowers communities and policymakers to demand stricter safety regulations. By making accident statistics accessible, open data fosters accountability and drives evidence-based interventions to protect workers’ rights and well-being.
