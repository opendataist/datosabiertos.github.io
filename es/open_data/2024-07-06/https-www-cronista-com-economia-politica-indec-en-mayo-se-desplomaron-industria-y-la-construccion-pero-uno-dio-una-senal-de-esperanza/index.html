The article reveals a significant contraction in Argentina’s industrial and construction sectors during early 2024. Manufacturing output declined sharply across almost all categories, indicating a stalled recovery and deepening economic challenges. Only the petroleum refining sector showed marginal improvement, highlighting the fragility of the broader industrial landscape. Despite the overall downturn in construction activity, the desseasonalized data offered a glimmer of hope by registering a positive monthly variation. This suggests a potential stabilization trend, even as the consumption of key construction inputs and building permits continued to fall significantly. Employment in the sector also decreased, underscoring the persistent difficulty in reviting economic activity. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the critical need for accessible, granular, and timely statistical information. Reliable public datasets allow researchers, businesses, and citizens to analyze complex economic trends independently. By providing transparent official figures, institutions like INDEC enable better decision-making and foster accountability, ensuring that the public can accurately assess the health of essential industries.
