Solicita INAI dialogar su futuro con nuevas titulares de Segob y SFP | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INAI urges the incoming government to engage in constructive dialogue regarding its constitutional mandate, signaling potential tension over proposed reforms that could dismantle the agency. This interaction highlights the vulnerability of autonomous bodies during administrative transitions, emphasizing the need for continuous institutional stability. The situation is relevant to open data as it underscores how political shifts directly impact transparency mechanisms and citizen rights. Preserving independent oversight is crucial for maintaining public trust and ensuring the uninterrupted flow of accessible government information to society.
