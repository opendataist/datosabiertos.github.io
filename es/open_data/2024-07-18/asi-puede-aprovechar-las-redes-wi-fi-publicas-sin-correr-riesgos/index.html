Así puede aprovechar las redes Wi-Fi públicas sin correr riesgos

The article warns that while public Wi-Fi offers essential connectivity in various venues, it exposes users to significant cybersecurity risks. Criminals frequently create fake networks or intercept data from legitimate ones, endangering personal information, banking details, and login credentials through eavesdropping and phishing attacks. To mitigate these threats, users are advised to connect only when necessary, verify network authenticity with staff, and avoid sensitive transactions. Furthermore, relying on reputable Virtual Private Networks provides crucial encryption, ensuring that online activities remain private and protected from unauthorized access or surveillance even in open digital environments. This content is highly relevant to the open_data movement because it highlights the inherent security vulnerabilities in open, unsecured digital infrastructures. It underscores the critical need for robust privacy-preserving technologies, such as encryption, to protect individual autonomy and data integrity when interacting with public networks, reinforcing the ethical imperative to safeguard personal information in shared digital spaces.
