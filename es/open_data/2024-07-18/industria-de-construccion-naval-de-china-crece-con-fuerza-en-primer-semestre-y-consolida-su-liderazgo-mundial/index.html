Industria de construcción naval de China crece con fuerza en primer semestre y consolida su liderazgo mundial

China’s naval construction sector demonstrated robust growth in the first half of the year, significantly strengthening its global leadership position. This expansion is evident across key metrics, including completed tonnage, new orders, and existing order backlogs, all of which showed substantial year-on-year increases. These figures highlight not only the industry's internal vitality but also its dominant share of the worldwide market, surpassing previous records and underscoring the sector's resilience amid global economic uncertainties. Despite challenges such as trade protectionism and geopolitical shifts, the industry remains a critical barometer of China’s macroeconomic health and a pillar of the global economy. The surge in shipbuilding activity correlates with rising port traffic in major hubs like Ningbo-Zhoushan and Guangzhou, reflecting a continuous recovery in maritime trade. This growth indicates that China continues to process a vast portion of its foreign trade via sea transport, maintaining its status as a central support for international shipping despite the broader economic headwinds. This article is highly relevant to open data because it relies on transparent, official statistics from government ministries and industry associations. By making granular data on tonnage, orders, and port volumes publicly available, it enables researchers and policymakers to monitor industrial trends, assess economic health, and verify market claims. Such openness fosters trust in economic indicators and allows for better analysis of how open data can serve as a tool for understanding complex global supply chains and industrial resilience.
