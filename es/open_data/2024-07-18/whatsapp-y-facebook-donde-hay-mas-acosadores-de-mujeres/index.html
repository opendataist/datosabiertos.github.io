WhatsApp y Facebook, donde hay más acosadores de mujeres

Veracruz ranks among Mexican states with high cyberbullying incidence, particularly affecting women. This data highlights the urgent need for reliable, disaggregated open data to understand digital safety disparities and design targeted interventions. The prevalence across genders and specific platforms like WhatsApp reveals systemic vulnerabilities in communication networks. Recognizing these patterns through transparent data sharing allows researchers and policymakers to identify where protections are most needed. Ultimately, this dataset underscores the value of accessible public statistics in addressing digital violence. By making such information open, society can better monitor trends, hold platforms accountable, and foster a safer online environment for all users.
