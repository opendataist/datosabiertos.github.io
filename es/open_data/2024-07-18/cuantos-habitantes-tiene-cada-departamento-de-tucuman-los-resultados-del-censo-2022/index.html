¿Cuántos habitantes tiene cada departamento de Tucumán?: los resultados del Censo 2022

The release of disaggregated census data by INDEC allows for precise local demographic analysis, highlighting significant population shifts in Tucumán. This granular visibility is crucial for open data initiatives aiming to support evidence-based local governance and resource allocation. Urban centers like San Miguel de Tucumán show steady growth, while rural departments experience rapid expansion. These trends reveal evolving settlement patterns that impact infrastructure planning. Understanding these dynamics helps communities anticipate future needs and adjust development strategies accordingly. Open access to such detailed statistics empowers citizens and researchers to scrutinize territorial changes. It fosters transparency and encourages civil society engagement. Ultimately, this data democratizes information, enabling more informed public discourse regarding regional development challenges and opportunities.
