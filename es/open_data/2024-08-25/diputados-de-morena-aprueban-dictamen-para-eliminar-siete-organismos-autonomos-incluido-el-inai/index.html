Diputados de Morena aprueban dictamen para eliminar siete organismos autónomos, incluido el INAI

The Mexican Congress approved dissolving key autonomous bodies, including the INAI, sparking intense controversy. Critics argue this move undermines democratic accountability and institutional checks rather than achieving fiscal austerity, representing a severe regression for public oversight. This proposal is critically relevant to open data because eliminating the INAI directly compromises the right to access public information. Without these independent regulatory bodies, transparency mechanisms are weakened, potentially hiding government actions from public scrutiny. Ultimately, the decision threatens the infrastructure of accountability necessary for an open society. By removing oversight agencies, the government risks reducing citizen ability to monitor state conduct, thereby impacting data openness and institutional integrity significantly.
