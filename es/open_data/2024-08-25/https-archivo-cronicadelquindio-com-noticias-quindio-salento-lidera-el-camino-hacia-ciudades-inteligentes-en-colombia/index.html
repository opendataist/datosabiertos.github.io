Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Salento has become the first pilot site in Colombia for "Intelligent City" initiatives, demonstrating how public utility infrastructure can be leveraged to generate open, real-time data for citizens and tourists. This project, led by EDEQ in collaboration with local governments, transforms physical networks into digital assets that provide immediate insights into environmental conditions, traffic flow, and security. By making this information publicly accessible through a dedicated platform, the initiative establishes a precedent for transparent governance and data-driven tourism management. The core implication of this deployment is the enhancement of user experience through transparency. Tourists can now plan their visits based on accurate, live data regarding parking availability, weather patterns, and vehicular congestion, which significantly reduces uncertainty and improves safety. The integration of artificial intelligence for facial recognition and intelligent lighting not only optimizes municipal resources but also creates a safer, more sustainable environment. This approach highlights how smart city technologies can directly support economic competitiveness by making destinations more attractive and easier to navigate. This article is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the practical application of making municipal data freely accessible to the public. By releasing live feeds on traffic, climate, and parking, the project moves beyond closed administrative systems toward open ecosystems that empower individuals. It illustrates how utilities and government entities can collaborate to produce public goods that foster trust, encourage innovation, and improve quality of life, serving as a replicable model for other regions aiming to adopt smart city frameworks.
