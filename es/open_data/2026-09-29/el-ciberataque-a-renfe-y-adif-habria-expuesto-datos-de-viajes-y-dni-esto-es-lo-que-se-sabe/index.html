El ciberataque a Renfe y Adif habría expuesto datos de viajes y DNI: esto es lo que se sabe

The Adif-Renfe cyberattack may involve significantly larger data exposure than initially reported, with hundreds of gigabytes and millions of personal records potentially compromised. This expansion implies severe privacy risks, as the stolen information includes names, ID numbers, contact details, and specific travel histories. This incident highlights critical vulnerabilities in managing sensitive citizen data within essential public transport infrastructure. The scale of the breach demonstrates how interconnected systems can amplify the impact of security failures, affecting millions indirectly through detailed behavioral profiling via ticket records. This case is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of strict data minimization and robust anonymization techniques. It serves as a cautionary example for how poorly secured datasets can become targets, reinforcing the argument that transparency must be balanced with rigorous privacy protections to maintain public trust and security.
