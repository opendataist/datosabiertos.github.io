De 2020 a 2022 bajaron 30% enfrentamientos entre policías y delincuentes, en Veracruz

Veracruz significantly reduced police confrontations and seized fewer weapons between 2020 and 2022, mirroring a national downward trend. This shift suggests improved public safety dynamics, though the correlation with weapon seizures remains ambiguous. Open data initiatives highlight this transparency, allowing citizens to track security metrics and hold authorities accountable. By exposing these statistical changes, CNSPE fosters informed public discourse on governance and the effectiveness of current law enforcement strategies.
