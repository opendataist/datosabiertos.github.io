Las ventas de las empresas caen un 6,7% en agosto, el mayor descenso en más de dos años

Spanish businesses face significant revenue drops, marking the sharpest decline since early 2021. Despite seasonal adjustments stabilizing monthly figures, five consecutive years of interannual growth losses highlight persistent economic headwinds. Only the service sector managed to expand, while industry and energy sectors struggled. This widespread contraction underscores the fragility of the commercial landscape outside of services. For open data enthusiasts, this INE report exemplifies vital public statistics. Tracking such business cycles enables better analysis of economic trends and informs policy decisions regarding market transparency and recovery strategies.
