México terminó el 2022 con más de 23 mil nuevos casos de cáncer de mama

This article highlights the significant burden of breast cancer in Mexico, identifying it as the leading cause of cancer-related mortality among women. The data reveals substantial regional variations in incidence and mortality rates, with certain states experiencing disproportionately high levels compared to the national average. These findings underscore the critical need for targeted public health strategies and resource allocation based on precise epidemiological data. By understanding geographic disparities, health authorities can better address specific local challenges and improve prevention and treatment outcomes for affected populations. This information is vital for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistical data empowers policymakers and researchers. Open access to such health metrics fosters accountability, enables evidence-based decision-making, and facilitates the development of effective interventions to combat this major public health issue.
