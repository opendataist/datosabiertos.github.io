Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The publication of detailed financial data by the RFEF’s transparency portal highlights a significant shift toward institutional openness regarding executive compensation. By disclosing specific salary figures for top officials, including the substantial remuneration of the federation’s president, the organization demonstrates a commitment to making internal financial structures accessible to the public, thereby enhancing accountability in sports governance. This revelation underscores the necessity of standardized, publicly available datasets to verify claims about financial practices within powerful sporting entities. The clear breakdown of gross and net earnings, alongside the clarification that lower-ranking board members are unpaid, provides a concrete example of how open data can dispel ambiguity and establish factual baselines for public scrutiny. The article’s relevance to open data lies in its validation of transparency portals as essential tools for democratic oversight. When organizations proactively release verifiable information, it empowers citizens and journalists to engage in informed discourse, reinforcing the principle that transparency is not just a legal requirement but a fundamental mechanism for trust in public institutions.
