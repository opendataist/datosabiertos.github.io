La Diputación de Valladolid impulsa el proyecto Patrimonio Inteligente en el Museo del Vino

The Diputación de Valladolid successfully modernized the Wine Museum in Peñafiel by implementing a smart heritage solution. This initiative utilizes a network of sensors to monitor environmental conditions and visitor flow, enabling proactive conservation efforts and optimizing energy efficiency. By automating these processes, the project preserves cultural resources more effectively while reducing maintenance costs and enhancing the overall tourist experience through better data-driven management. This effort is part of the larger POCTEP Smart Rural Digital project, a cross-border European initiative aiming to enhance public service management in rural areas. The broader strategy includes developing digital services and territorial sensorization across multiple municipalities in Spain and Portugal. The project demonstrates how coordinated regional efforts can deploy intelligent technologies to support local administrations, fostering sustainable development and improving the quality of life in rural communities through interconnected digital infrastructure. This case is highly relevant to open data as it highlights the critical role of data platforms in managing smart city resources. The collected data is accessible via specific platforms for decision-makers, illustrating the importance of data transparency and interoperability in public administration. It underscores how open access to operational and environmental data empowers stakeholders to make informed choices, ensuring that technological investments yield tangible benefits for cultural preservation and efficient public service delivery in a connected digital ecosystem.
