Nuevo récord de presión humana en Baleares: 1.858.077 personas en un día

Tourist numbers in the Balearic Islands remained remarkably high throughout September, indicating sustained demand for island destinations without significant seasonal decline. This stability highlights the region's continued economic reliance on mass tourism despite broader European travel trends. The data is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how public sector statistics can be used to monitor socio-economic impacts and resource allocation in popular tourism hubs. Such granular, time-series data allows researchers and planners to analyze pressure on infrastructure and environment, supporting evidence-based policy decisions for sustainable tourism management.
