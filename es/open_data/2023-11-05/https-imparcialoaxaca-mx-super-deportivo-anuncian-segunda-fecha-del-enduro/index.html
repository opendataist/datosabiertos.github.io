The article outlines the logistics for an upcoming enduro cycling event in Oaxaca, emphasizing open participation across multiple age and skill categories. Relevance to open data lies in the potential for collecting standardized, open-access information on sports participation. By tracking registration data, participant demographics, and safety compliance metrics, local authorities and organizers can generate valuable datasets. These insights can improve future event planning, resource allocation, and community engagement strategies.
