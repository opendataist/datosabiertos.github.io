The INAI warns that WhatsApp’s widespread use makes it a prime target for digital criminals seeking to steal personal information. To mitigate this risk, the authority recommends enabling two-step verification, which adds a PIN and email recovery option to secure accounts against unauthorized access. This guidance highlights a critical intersection between private platform security protocols and public data protection advocacy. By urging users to implement these safeguards, the INAI emphasizes that individual responsibility is essential for preserving personal privacy in digital environments. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the urgent need for robust privacy frameworks when handling personal information online. It underscores that while data transparency is vital, protecting user privacy from exploitation remains a fundamental challenge in the broader open data ecosystem.
