Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Mexico’s tourism sector demonstrates robust recovery and growth, with international arrivals significantly outperforming pre-pandemic levels and showing a steady upward trend despite fluctuations in average spending. This data highlights the sector's critical role in the national economy, driven largely by increased visitor volume rather than higher individual expenditure. The relevance to open data lies in how these comprehensive statistics, published by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography, enable transparent monitoring of economic impacts. By making granular travel and spending metrics publicly available, stakeholders can better analyze sector health and inform policy decisions. Such accessible, high-quality datasets foster accountability and support evidence-based strategies for sustaining tourism growth. They illustrate the importance of open government data in tracking national economic indicators, allowing for precise assessment of how global trends impact local development and fiscal stability.
