Pierde fuerza economía mexicana: cae 0.5% en noviembre

Mexico’s economy experienced a contraction in November, marking a second consecutive month without growth. This unexpected decline highlights significant weakness in the service sector, overshadowing slight gains in primary activities. The data indicates that economic momentum is fading, with consumer and commercial services facing notable setbacks. The severity of the downturn exceeds initial forecasts, suggesting a deeper structural challenge than anticipated. Analysts warn that this trend signals a loss of force in the overall economy. If the negative trajectory persists into the following month, it could impact quarterly performance expectations, reflecting broader uncertainties in domestic demand and industrial activity. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical indicators to track real-time economic health. Access to granular, timely datasets allows experts to detect early signs of volatility across specific sectors. Understanding these patterns requires transparent data availability, enabling precise modeling of economic resilience and informing policy decisions aimed at stabilizing growth trends.
