Inegi: Covid "baja escalones"; es sexta causa de muerte en Puebla

The article reveals that chronic conditions like heart disease and diabetes have surpassed the coronavirus as the leading causes of death in Puebla and across Mexico during the first half of 2022. This shift signifies that the pandemic, while still a top-five killer, is no longer the primary driver of mortality compared to long-standing non-communicable diseases. The data highlights a persistent excess in mortality rates nationally, indicating ongoing public health challenges beyond the acute crisis. Relevant to open data, this report underscores the critical role of transparent, accessible statistical information from institutions like INEGI. By publishing granular details on death certificates and causes, such datasets enable researchers and policymakers to track epidemiological trends accurately. Open access to these statistics facilitates rigorous analysis of mortality patterns, allowing for evidence-based interventions and a deeper understanding of the health landscape. Ultimately, the findings emphasize the importance of continuous monitoring through reliable data infrastructure. As chronic diseases remain the dominant threat, open data serves as a vital tool for identifying vulnerable demographics and evaluating the effectiveness of healthcare strategies. This transparency supports informed decision-making, ensuring that resource allocation addresses the most significant burdens on the population effectively.
