Las pernoctaciones en alojamientos rurales subieron un 3,7 % en 2023 - EFEAgro

The data reveals a significant shift in rural tourism dynamics, where international visitors are increasingly driving growth while domestic participation stagnates. This trend highlights a changing market composition, suggesting that rural accommodations are becoming more attractive to foreign travelers despite overall slower growth rates compared to previous years. This article is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the value of standardized statistical releases for tracking nuanced consumer behavior shifts. By making such granular data accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand the evolving nature of regional economies and the specific drivers behind sectoral changes in tourism. Understanding these patterns allows for more targeted development strategies and resource allocation. The availability of detailed occupancy and guest origin data empowers stakeholders to adapt to shifting demand, ensuring that rural tourism infrastructure meets the needs of an increasingly globalized clientele.
