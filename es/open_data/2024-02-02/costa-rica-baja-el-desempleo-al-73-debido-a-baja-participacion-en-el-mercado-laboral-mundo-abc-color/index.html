Costa Rica baja el desempleo al 7,3 % debido a baja participación en el mercado laboral - Mundo - ABC Color

The data reveals that declining unemployment rates are driven by reduced labor market participation, particularly within the informal sector. This structural shift suggests that fewer people are seeking work rather than finding jobs, creating a misleadingly positive economic indicator that masks underlying instability in the labor force. Sectoral analysis highlights significant job losses in construction and support services, contrasting with modest gains in health and education. These divergent trends indicate a reallocation of economic activity rather than overall growth, emphasizing the need to look beyond aggregate numbers to understand the true state of employment. This insight is vital for open data initiatives, as it underscores the importance of publishing granular, multidimensional statistics. Transparency requires more than just headline figures; it demands accessible, detailed datasets that allow researchers and citizens to interpret complex socio-economic realities, ensuring that policy decisions are based on comprehensive rather than superficial understanding.
