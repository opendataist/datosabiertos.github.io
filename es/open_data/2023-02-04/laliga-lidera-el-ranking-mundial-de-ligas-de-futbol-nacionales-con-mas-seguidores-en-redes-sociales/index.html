LaLiga lidera el ranking mundial de ligas de fútbol nacionales con más seguidores en redes sociales

LaLiga demonstrates the power of social media as a primary metric for institutional relevance, leading global football leagues with 167 million followers across all platforms. This dominance extends to TikTok, highlighting the competition's strong appeal to younger demographics and confirming its status as a digital leader alongside the Premier League. These figures validate historical trends showing LaLiga’s rapid growth in digital engagement, positioning it as a major player compared to other international sports competitions. The data underscores how strategic communication initiatives, supported by financial investments, allow both the league and its clubs to significantly increase their online presence and global reach. This article is crucial for open data as it illustrates how publicly available social media metrics serve as transparent indicators of organizational impact and public interest. Analyzing such openly accessible data allows stakeholders to understand digital influence trends without relying on proprietary information. It emphasizes that open datasets regarding social engagement are vital for benchmarking performance and understanding modern media consumption patterns in the sports industry.
