Alertan senadores por ‘focos rojos’ ante inflación

Mexican lawmakers and political parties criticize the government’s delayed and inadequate response to high inflation, arguing that relying solely on monetary policy harms low-income families and stifles economic growth. They emphasize the urgent need to boost the productive sector to create jobs and control rising costs for basic goods. This situation highlights the importance of open data, as transparent economic statistics are essential for scrutinizing government performance and informing public debate. Without accessible, reliable data, citizens cannot accurately assess the true impact of inflation or hold institutions accountable for their decisions. The lack of comprehensive, open economic information exacerbates the disconnect between policy makers and the affected population. Ensuring data availability allows for better analysis of regional disparities and poverty levels, facilitating more effective and equitable solutions to inflationary pressures.
