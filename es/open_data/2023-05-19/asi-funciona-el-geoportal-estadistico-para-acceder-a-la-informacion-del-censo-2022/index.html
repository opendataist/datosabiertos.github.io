Así funciona el geoportal estadístico para acceder a la información del Censo 2022

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses has launched a comprehensive geoportal providing provisional data from the 2022 Census. This georeferenced application allows users to map population and housing statistics across Argentina’s administrative divisions, offering a robust tool for visualizing demographic trends at jurisdictional and local levels. By integrating statistical data with various geographic layers, such as infrastructure, climate, and environmental factors, the platform enables deep comparative analysis. Users can overlay indicators, customize map visualizations, and download datasets in multiple formats, facilitating interoperability and detailed research into how social demographics intersect with physical and regional characteristics. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it democratizes access to official census information through a transparent, interactive interface. It transforms raw statistical outputs into actionable, spatially aware knowledge, encouraging public scrutiny, academic research, and informed decision-making by making complex demographic data freely accessible and easily understandable for diverse audiences.
