Unidades de Transparencia se resisten a informar

The article emphasizes that the right to information access remains constitutionally guaranteed, despite some transparency units falsely claiming the process has ended due to institutional issues. This highlights the critical importance of maintaining operational transparency mechanisms to protect citizen rights against bureaucratic resistance. Furthermore, the commission highlights ongoing promotion efforts within the National Transparency System to ensure compliance among obligated subjects across the federation. This underscores the necessity of robust enforcement and coordination to prevent gaps in information accessibility. Finally, the introduction of an AI-powered gender search tool on the National Transparency Platform demonstrates technological innovation in open data. This advancement improves information retrieval, showcasing how modern tools can enhance transparency and make public data more accessible and usable for citizens.
