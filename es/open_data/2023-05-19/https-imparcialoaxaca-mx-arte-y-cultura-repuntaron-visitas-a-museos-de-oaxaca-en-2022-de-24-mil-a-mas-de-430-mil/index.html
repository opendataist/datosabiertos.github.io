Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Oaxaca’s museums have experienced a significant resurgence in visitors during 2022, marking a dramatic recovery from the pandemic’s halt in 2020 and 2021. Although attendance remains below pre-pandemic levels, the surge indicates a strong return of public interest in cultural heritage. This rebound demonstrates the resilience of the cultural sector and highlights the critical role museums play in community revitalization and social cohesion after periods of global crisis. The statistical analysis of museum attendance is particularly relevant to open data initiatives, as it relies on transparent, standardized metrics from institutions like INEGI. By making this visitation data accessible, policymakers and researchers can accurately assess the health of the cultural economy. The availability of such granular geographic and temporal data allows for evidence-based decisions regarding funding, accessibility, and strategic planning for regional development, ensuring that resources are allocated where they are most needed to support cultural institutions. Furthermore, the recovery underscores the evolving mission of museums toward sustainability and well-being, as emphasized by international councils. Institutions are increasingly viewed as vital spaces for education and creative discussion, rather than just repositories of artifacts. The open nature of these datasets enables the public to track these shifts in cultural engagement, fostering greater transparency and encouraging civic participation in preserving and promoting local heritage through informed advocacy and community involvement.
