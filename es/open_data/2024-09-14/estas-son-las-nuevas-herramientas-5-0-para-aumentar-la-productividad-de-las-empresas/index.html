Estas son las nuevas herramientas 5.0 para aumentar la productividad de las empresas

The article outlines the emergence of "Society 5.0," a technological frontier driven by cyber-physical systems and synthetic data, which merge the digital and physical worlds to enhance productivity and efficiency. While these systems offer significant value through automation and real-time integration, they introduce complex cyber-physical security risks that require robust corporate governance to manage potential threats to both infrastructure and personnel. Synthetic data, generated artificially via AI to mimic real-world patterns, presents a crucial opportunity to bypass privacy regulations and data scarcity issues. By providing a cost-effective and rapid source of information, it enables the training of machine learning models and accelerates business transformation. However, its effectiveness depends heavily on the quality of the source algorithms, as underlying anomalies or privacy leaks can compromise the integrity and representativeness of the generated data. This report is highly relevant to open data because it highlights a transformative shift in how organizations approach information access and utility. It underscores the necessity of rigorous auditing and human oversight to ensure synthetic data does not create feedback loops of inaccuracy. For the open data community, it emphasizes that while synthetic generation can democratize access to insights and overcome confidentiality barriers, maintaining trust and data quality remains paramount to sustaining reliable, ethical, and effective data-driven ecosystems.
