Censo 2024: INE presenta resultados del conteo poblacional del departamento del Beni

The National Statistics Institute has published the latest population census results for the Beni department, highlighting the vital role of citizen participation in ensuring data accuracy. This collaborative effort underscores how open data initiatives depend on public trust and active engagement to capture reliable demographic information. These statistics provide essential insights into social, economic, and housing conditions, enabling governments to understand population distribution and living standards. Such comprehensive data is crucial for evidence-based policy making, allowing for more effective resource allocation and targeted public services that reflect actual community needs. This release is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates the importance of accessible, transparent government statistics. By making these findings widely available, the INE fosters accountability and empowers researchers and citizens to analyze regional trends, ultimately driving informed decisions that benefit society as a whole.
