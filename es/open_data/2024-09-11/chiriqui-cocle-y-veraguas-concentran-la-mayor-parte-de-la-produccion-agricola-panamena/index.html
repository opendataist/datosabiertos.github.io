Chiriquí, Coclé y Veraguas concentran la mayor parte de la producción agrícola panameña

The national agricultural census reveals significant growth in producers and farms, reflecting sector evolution. This data supports open data initiatives by providing transparent, updated statistics for public analysis. Increased product coverage and regional inclusion enhance data granularity. Such comprehensive datasets allow researchers to better understand shifting agricultural landscapes and inform policy decisions. Ultimately, releasing these figures promotes accountability and facilitates evidence-based planning. Open access to census results empowers citizens and institutions to drive sustainable development in the agricultural sector.
