El INE abre a la revisión internacional el Censo 2024; la mesa técnica será el lunes

Public distrust regarding Bolivia’s 2024 census results has prompted the National Institute of Statistics to initiate a technical review process. By inviting a broad coalition of regional authorities and institutions to a review board, the Institute aims to address widespread skepticism. This move highlights the critical importance of transparency in government statistics, as open data initiatives rely heavily on public confidence to remain viable and useful for societal planning. Regional governments in key areas have challenged the preliminary figures, citing discrepancies with local projections and demanding independent verification. Consequently, technical commissions have begun working to contrast national data with regional demographic indicators. This friction underscores a major challenge in open data: the necessity for rigorous validation mechanisms. Without such mechanisms, local discrepancies can erode trust, making it difficult for accurate, open datasets to be integrated effectively into regional decision-making processes. However, the review process faces logistical hurdles, with some local leaders imposing conditions on participation to ensure accountability from the Institute’s director. This standoff illustrates the complex negotiation required between central statistical bodies and local entities. For open data communities, this case serves as a vital lesson on the need for inclusive, verifiable data governance. Establishing clear, accessible channels for data verification is essential to resolving disputes and ensuring that statistical information serves all stakeholders equitably.
