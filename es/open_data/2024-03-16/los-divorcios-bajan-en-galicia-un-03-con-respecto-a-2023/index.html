Los divorcios bajan en Galicia un 0,3% con respecto a 2023

Galicia’s family court statistics reveal a stable total of marital breakups in 2023, despite internal shifts between consensual and contested categories. The data highlights significant growth in non-consensual modifications regarding child custody and support, indicating complex post-separation dynamics. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it provides granular, official insights into social trends. Access to such detailed judicial records allows researchers to analyze the evolving nature of family law and societal needs. Ultimately, this dataset underscores the importance of transparent public statistics. It enables policymakers and citizens to understand the practical implications of legal frameworks, fostering informed discussions on social welfare and judicial efficiency.
