Zapping sobre presunto hackeo: “Descartamos la filtración de datos sensibles"

A security incident involving Zapping sparked controversy when researchers reported a data leak affecting over 100,000 users. The exposed information allegedly included personal details and partial payment data, raising concerns about the platform's cybersecurity practices and potential regulatory repercussions from consumer protection agencies. The company’s CEO clarified that only registration attempt data was exposed during a brief period in Peru, explicitly denying the compromise of sensitive credit card information. He emphasized that such financial data is never stored in their databases, aiming to mitigate user anxiety and distinguish between minor technical logs and critical security breaches. This situation highlights the critical importance of transparency and accurate data handling in the open data ecosystem. It demonstrates how public disclosures about data vulnerabilities, regardless of their severity, significantly impact user trust and regulatory scrutiny, underscoring the need for robust privacy standards in digital services.
