Economía peruana se recupera con un crecimiento del 1.37% en enero: ¿Qué sectores destacaron?

The Peruvian economy showed a significant recovery in early 2024, with production expanding after a prolonged period of stagnation and contraction. This positive growth is primarily attributed to a favorable base effect from the previous year, which was heavily impacted by social unrest and protests. While the annual trend remains negative, this monthly rebound signals a potential turning point in economic activity, driven by strong performance in key sectors such as mining, construction, and services. Despite the overall growth, the economic landscape remains uneven, with several critical sectors still struggling. Industries like manufacturing, fishing, and agriculture faced notable declines, reflecting ongoing challenges in production and external demand. This divergence highlights the fragility of the recovery, as the expansion relied on specific areas like hydrocarbons and public works, while other vital parts of the industrial and agricultural base continued to contract, indicating that the economic revival has not yet been broadly inclusive. This report is highly relevant to the open data community because it exemplifies the importance of accessible, high-quality statistical indicators for analyzing economic health. By publishing detailed technical reports from the INEI, authorities enable researchers and the public to verify official narratives against underlying sectoral data. Transparent access to these metrics allows for a more nuanced understanding of economic trends, fostering accountability and supporting data-driven decision-making in both policy and private sectors.
