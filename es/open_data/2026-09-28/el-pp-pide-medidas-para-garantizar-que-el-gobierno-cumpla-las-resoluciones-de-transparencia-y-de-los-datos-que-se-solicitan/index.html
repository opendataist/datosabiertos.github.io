El PP pide medidas para garantizar que el Gobierno cumpla las resoluciones de Transparencia y dé los datos que se solicitan

The Popular Party proposes significant legislative changes to strengthen the Council for Transparency, Access to Information and Good Governance, aiming to eliminate the gap between legal rulings and their practical execution. Central to this initiative is the mandatory execution of favorable resolutions within strict deadlines, supported by public quarterly tracking reports and regular congressional updates on compliance. This approach ensures that citizens do not face repeated bureaucratic hurdles after winning a transparency request, thereby reinforcing the effectiveness and authority of the independent oversight body. To address the growing caseload and resource shortages, the proposals call for substantial increases in the Council’s human, material, and technological funding. Furthermore, the party seeks to improve the quality of proactive disclosure by establishing a standardized, interoperable model for all public entity websites. This standardization aims to enhance data clarity and reusability, while strict oversight mechanisms are introduced for underperforming organizations, linking compliance rates to the performance evaluations of senior public administrators. These measures are directly relevant to open data because they shift the focus from mere publication to meaningful accessibility and reuse. By enforcing standardized formats and interoperability, the legislation seeks to transform raw public information into usable assets for society. Additionally, linking compliance to administrative accountability ensures that open government commitments are not just nominal but are actively monitored and enforced, fostering a culture of genuine transparency and data-driven governance.
