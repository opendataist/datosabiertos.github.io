Inflación en México sigue al alza y se ubica en 4.69% en mayo: Inegi

Mexico’s annual inflation reached 4.69 percent in May, remaining above the central bank’s target. Although the general index showed a slight monthly decline, the underlying price trajectory indicates persistent upward pressure. This data highlights the complexity of price stabilization efforts and the gap between official targets and economic reality. The divergence between underlying and non-underlying inflation reveals distinct trends. Food prices rose significantly for certain agricultural products, while energy tariffs dropped due to seasonal adjustments. These contrasting movements illustrate how volatile components can mask broader inflationary pressures within the national price index. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the necessity of granular, transparent statistical access. Understanding these specific price dynamics allows researchers to analyze inflation drivers more accurately. Open access to such detailed indices empowers better policy evaluation and deeper economic research, fostering informed public discourse.
