Inflación en Ecuador se reduce por primera vez en cinco meses y cae al 2,53% | La República EC

Ecuador’s inflation rate declined to 2.53 percent, marking the first drop in five months and confirming a stabilization trend despite being higher than the previous year. Regional disparities persist, with coastal areas experiencing rates above the national average compared to the Sierra region. This data is relevant to open_data because it highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible, and granular statistical releases. When national entities like INEC publish detailed, timely datasets, they enable accurate monitoring of economic health and facilitate comparative analysis across different geographic regions. Furthermore, the ability to benchmark Ecuador’s performance against neighboring countries demonstrates the value of standardized, interoperable data. Open statistics empower researchers and policymakers to identify structural trends, assess regional inequalities, and make informed decisions based on reliable, publicly available evidence rather than opaque summaries.
