Encuesta de cobertura del Censo llega al 49% y el INE afirma que tasa de omisión será la más baja de la región

The National Institute of Statistics confirms that the post-census survey is nearly halfway complete, aiming to validate the census quality by measuring coverage rates. This operational phase is crucial for identifying any omissions or errors in the initial count, ensuring the dataset’s reliability before final publication. Concurrently, data processing has advanced significantly, with six departments fully cleaned and validated. This systematic verification of location and population counts is essential for transforming raw survey material into accurate, usable statistics. The rigorous steps taken here guarantee that the final results will accurately reflect the demographic reality of each region. This process is vital for open data initiatives as it establishes a trustworthy foundation for public information. By prioritizing transparency and quality control, the INE ensures that citizens and policymakers can rely on these statistics for decision-making, fostering greater accountability and informed engagement with national development goals.
