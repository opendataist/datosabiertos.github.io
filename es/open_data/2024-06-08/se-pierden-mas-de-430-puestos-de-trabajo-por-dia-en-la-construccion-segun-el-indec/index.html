Se pierden más de 430 puestos de trabajo por día en la construcción, según el Indec

The Argentine construction sector has suffered a severe collapse in formal employment due to the nationwide halt in public works projects. This paralysis caused a dramatic reduction in jobs, with the majority of losses attributed directly to the suspension of state-funded initiatives, while large private construction remained relatively more stable compared to the devastated public sector. Beyond immediate job losses, the industry faces deep uncertainty regarding future activity. Business surveys indicate unfavorable expectations, with most firms predicting stagnant or declining operations in the coming months. This pessimistic outlook suggests that the downturn is not merely a temporary pause but reflects broader structural challenges and a lack of confidence in the sector’s near-term recovery. This data is crucial for open data advocates because it highlights how transparent statistical reporting from national institutes can effectively trace the real-world economic impact of policy decisions. By making employment and activity metrics publicly accessible, governments enable civil society and researchers to hold institutions accountable and analyze the tangible consequences of administrative standstills on labor markets.
