La inflación industrial sube en diciembre un 2,3%, la tasa más alta desde febrero de 2023

The recent rise in industrial prices marks a significant reversal, ending a prolonged deflationary trend and signaling renewed inflationary pressure. This shift is primarily driven by sharp increases in energy costs, particularly refining and electricity, which now dictate the overall sector's trajectory. Conversely, consumer goods prices continue to decline, highlighting a complex divergence within the industrial landscape. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, granular economic indicators for effective monitoring. Accessible, detailed price indices enable researchers and policymakers to accurately assess regional disparities and sector-specific impacts. By making such statistical information open, society can better understand economic volatility and demand evidence-based interventions. Ultimately, the availability of this data fosters trust and facilitates informed decision-making across various stakeholders. It allows for the creation of analytical tools that predict trends and evaluate policy effectiveness in real-time. Ensuring these datasets are open promotes accountability and empowers communities to engage with economic realities, reinforcing the value of transparency in public administration and market stability.
