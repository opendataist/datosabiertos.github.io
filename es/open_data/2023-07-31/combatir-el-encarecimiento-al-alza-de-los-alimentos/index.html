Combatir el encarecimiento al alza de los alimentos

The Spanish government’s recent policies to curb inflation have proven ineffective, as food prices continue to rise significantly despite tax reductions and subsidies. With core inflation rising, the cost of living remains unmanageable for many households lacking corresponding wage increases, indicating that current measures are insufficient to stabilize consumer prices. This economic strain is exacerbated by central banks raising interest rates to maximum levels, further burdening citizens and limiting economic flexibility. The persistent increase in essential goods prices highlights a disconnect between policy interventions and actual household purchasing power, leaving families vulnerable to ongoing financial pressure without adequate compensation. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistics to track inflation accurately. Reliable data allows for the evaluation of policy effectiveness and reveals the true impact on vulnerable populations, enabling better-informed decisions and ensuring that open data initiatives directly address societal needs and economic realities.
