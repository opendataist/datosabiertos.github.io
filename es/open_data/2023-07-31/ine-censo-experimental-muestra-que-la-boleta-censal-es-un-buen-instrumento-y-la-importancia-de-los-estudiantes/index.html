INE: Censo experimental muestra que la boleta censal “es un buen instrumento” y la importancia de los estudiantes

The experimental census in Capinota proceeded smoothly without complications, demonstrating the effectiveness of the census tools and the strong collaboration of the local population. This success highlights the importance of community engagement and institutional support in ensuring accurate data collection, which is fundamental for producing reliable national statistics. Local student participation proved crucial, as their knowledge of the region and native languages facilitated faster mobility and better communication with residents. Their involvement underscores a key strategy for improving data quality in diverse geographic areas, emphasizing the need for inclusive approaches that leverage local assets. For open data, this case illustrates how successful field operations rely on transparent processes and community trust. The seamless execution validates the data’s reliability, encouraging broader public access to census results and fostering a culture of accountability in statistical governance.
