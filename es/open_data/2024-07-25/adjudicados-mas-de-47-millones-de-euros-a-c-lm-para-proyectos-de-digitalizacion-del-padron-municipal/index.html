Adjudicados más de 4,7 millones de euros a C-LM para proyectos de digitalización del padrón municipal

This article highlights a significant public investment in modernizing municipal registry systems to enable real-time data exchange between local governments and the national statistics institute. By upgrading these infrastructures, authorities aim to ensure more accurate, synchronized, and efficient demographic management across Spain. The initiative is part of a broader national strategy to digitally transform local entities, leveraging European recovery funds to adapt to updated legal frameworks. It prioritizes connectivity and interoperability, empowering provinces and municipalities to streamline administrative processes through technological innovation rather than relying on fragmented, outdated methods. This development is crucial for open data because it establishes a foundational layer of high-quality, standardized demographic information. When local registries communicate seamlessly with national databases, it reduces data silos and enhances the reliability of statistical outputs, fostering greater transparency and enabling more effective policy-making based on precise, accessible public information.
