Castilla-La Mancha registró 14.075 nacimientos y 19.447 defunciones durante el año 2023

The article highlights a significant demographic shift in Spain, characterized by a historic low in births and a growing elderly population. Despite a slight decline in mortality rates, the gap between deaths and births has widened, resulting in a substantial negative natural growth. This trend marks a continued decline in fertility rates over the last decade, accompanied by a delay in motherhood and an increase in the proportion of births to foreign mothers. These statistics reveal deep structural changes in Spanish society, including a rise in life expectancy and an aging workforce. The data shows that people over 65 now constitute more than 20% of the population, while marriage rates continue to fall, with civil unions becoming increasingly dominant over religious ones. The delay in marriage and childbirth reflects evolving social norms and economic realities, fundamentally altering the country’s demographic profile. This report is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides high-quality, granular demographic indicators essential for policy-making, urban planning, and social service allocation. By making such detailed statistical series publicly available, governments enable researchers and developers to analyze long-term trends, predict future needs, and create data-driven solutions for aging societies. Transparent access to these metrics fosters accountability and innovation, allowing stakeholders to better understand and respond to the complex challenges of modern demographic evolution.
