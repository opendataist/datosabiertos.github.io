Mueren 2 mil 247 migrantes en México en 10 años

The article highlights a tragic decade-long trend of migrant deaths in Mexico, revealing that thousands perish annually due to violence, drowning, accidents, and lack of medical care. This data underscores the extreme vulnerability of displaced populations, emphasizing that migration remains a perilous journey with fatal consequences for many, particularly along border regions and dangerous crossing points. The specific causes of death, including homicides and drownings, reflect systemic failures in protection and humanitarian aid. The high number of violent deaths and disappearances suggests organized criminal involvement, while accidental deaths point to unsafe transit conditions and inadequate infrastructure for travelers. This information is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how publicly accessible, detailed databases can expose human rights violations and inform policy changes. By making such granular mortality data available, civil society and international organizations can better monitor trends, hold authorities accountable, and advocate for safer migration policies based on empirical evidence.
