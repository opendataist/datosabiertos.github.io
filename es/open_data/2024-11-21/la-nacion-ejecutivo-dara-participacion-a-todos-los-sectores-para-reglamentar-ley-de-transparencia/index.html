La Nación / Ejecutivo dará participación a todos los sectores para reglamentar ley de transparencia

The executive branch is implementing a participatory process to regulate legislation governing non-profit organizations. This approach aims to ensure transparency and accountability without imposing additional bureaucratic burdens. By engaging directly with these sectors, the government seeks to clarify regulatory details while avoiding administrative overload. The initiative addresses significant transparency gaps regarding non-profit activities and beneficiary identification. Establishing a clear map of where these organizations operate allows for better coordination with state efforts. This strategic alignment is intended to maximize social impact by leveraging the existing experience and reach of non-profits in various territories. This development is relevant to open data as it mandates greater disclosure of non-profit operations and funding sources. Enhanced transparency provides more accessible information for public scrutiny and analysis. Such data improves civic oversight and enables informed decision-making regarding social programs and resource allocation in the public sector.
