The data reveals significant gender disparities in labor participation, with men predominantly engaged in commerce and agriculture, while women cluster in education, health, and domestic work. These structural differences highlight deep-seated inequalities in employment types and sectors, suggesting that gender-responsive policies are necessary to address varied professional experiences. Unemployment remains stagnant, disproportionately affecting women compared to men. This persistent gap indicates systemic barriers that prevent equitable labor market access. Understanding these trends is crucial for identifying the specific challenges different demographics face in securing stable employment and economic independence. This article is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how transparent, granular statistical information can expose social inequities. By making detailed demographic breakdowns publicly available, stakeholders can analyze labor market dynamics, hold institutions accountable, and design evidence-based interventions to promote fairer economic opportunities for all genders.
