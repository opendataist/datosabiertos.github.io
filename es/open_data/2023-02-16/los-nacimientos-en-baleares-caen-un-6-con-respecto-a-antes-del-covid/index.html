Los nacimientos en Baleares caen un 6 % con respecto a antes del COVID

The article highlights a significant demographic imbalance in the Balearic Islands and Spain, where mortality rates consistently surpass birth rates, resulting in a negative natural population growth. This trend is evident across different age groups and islands, indicating a structural challenge for regional sustainability and future planning. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of accessible, granular vital statistics for effective societal analysis. Transparent access to such information allows researchers and policymakers to monitor demographic shifts, assess public health impacts, and develop evidence-based strategies to address population decline and aging societies.
