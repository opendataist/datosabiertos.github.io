Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Municipality of Chihuahua hosts a week of open government lectures at local universities, inviting students and the public to understand the authority’s data sharing policies. Officials and transparency commissioners will explain these protocols to foster a culture of openness and collaborative governance among the youth. This event highlights Chihuahua’s integration into the global Open Government Alliance, aligning with UN initiatives that promote horizontal governance. By emphasizing accountability and citizen participation, the city demonstrates a commitment to modernizing public administration through inclusive practices rather than restrictive secrecy. Relevance to open data lies in the institutionalization of transparency principles. By training future leaders and engaging society, these efforts ensure data is not just released but actively used for democratic collaboration. This approach transforms raw information into a tool for civic empowerment and trust-building within the open data ecosystem.
