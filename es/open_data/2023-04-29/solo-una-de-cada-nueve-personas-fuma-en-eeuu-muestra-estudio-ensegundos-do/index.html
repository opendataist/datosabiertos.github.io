Solo una de cada nueve personas fuma en EEUU, muestra estudio - Ensegundos.do

The article highlights a historic decline in cigarette smoking in the US, alongside a rise in vaping. This data is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how public health agencies leverage transparent, large-scale surveys to monitor trends and inform policy. Ultimately, despite the lowest recorded smoking rates, tobacco remains the leading cause of preventable deaths. The decreasing prevalence underscores the long-term effectiveness of government interventions against smoking. However, the concurrent growth in electronic cigarette usage introduces new complexities. Tracking these shifting consumption patterns is crucial for understanding evolving public health challenges and evaluating the impact of regulatory measures.
