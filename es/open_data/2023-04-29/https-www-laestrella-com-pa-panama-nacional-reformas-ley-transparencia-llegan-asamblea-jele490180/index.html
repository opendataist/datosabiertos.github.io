Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Panamanian government has introduced reforms to the Transparency Law, aiming to strengthen accountability by defining strict procedures and sanctions for officials who fail to comply with information access obligations. This legislative update seeks to ensure that public information is provided in a timely, complete, and reliable manner, thereby reinforcing citizens' rights to access government data. Relevant to open data, these measures directly address the operational gaps in current transparency frameworks, emphasizing the need for robust enforcement mechanisms. By clarifying the handling of information requests, the reform enhances the predictability and quality of public data release, which is essential for maintaining trust and facilitating civic oversight in an open government context. The initiative underscores the critical link between data transparency and anti-corruption efforts. By mandating greater diligence in information management, the proposal aims to combat corruption, a major threat to state integrity globally. This shift highlights the evolving nature of open data, moving beyond mere availability toward enforceable standards of quality and responsibility in public administration.
