Avanza economía 1.1% y habría dejado atrás, por fin, a la pandemia

Mexico’s economy exceeded expectations in early 2023, demonstrating resilient growth driven primarily by robust consumer spending in the service sector. This positive momentum suggests that internal consumption is the key engine behind the national recovery, positioning the country to potentially surpass historical annual growth averages despite broader global economic decelerations. The data highlights a significant shift in growth patterns, as Mexico continues to expand while major partners like the United States face slowing economies. Analysts project that this strong performance provides a generous foundation for the remainder of the year, indicating that the nation is well-positioned to maintain steady progress through the interplay of domestic demand and international trade dynamics like nearshoring. This report is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of timely, high-quality public statistics from institutions like INEGI for economic analysis. Reliable, accessible data enables experts to accurately monitor real-time trends, validate economic forecasts, and understand structural shifts, thereby facilitating informed decision-making for policymakers, investors, and the general public who depend on transparent information to navigate the evolving economic landscape.
