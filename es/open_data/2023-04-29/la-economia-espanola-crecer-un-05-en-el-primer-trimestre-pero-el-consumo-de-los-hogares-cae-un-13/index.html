La economía española crecer un 0,5% en el primer trimestre, pero el consumo de los hogares cae un 1,3%

The Spanish economy demonstrated notable resilience by growing despite global uncertainty and internal contractions in household spending. This progress was primarily driven by robust external demand through exports and strong domestic investment, which offset the negative impact of reduced public expenditure and consumer spending. Sectoral performance varied significantly, with agriculture, construction, and industry leading growth while the services sector experienced a slight decline. Although employment hours increased, full-time equivalent jobs grew minimally. Nevertheless, the economic recovery is viewed as solid, with national output nearly returning to pre-pandemic levels. This data is relevant to open_data as it relies on the National Statistics Institute’s transparent, accessible statistical frameworks. Open government data enables independent verification of economic trends, fostering public trust and allowing researchers to analyze the drivers of growth without relying solely on official narratives.
