Inegi recibe 17 mil 947 denuncias en contra de sus colaboradores durante 2022 - Noticaribe

The Inegi’s internal control reports a massive scale of administrative irregularities among its staff, highlighting a systemic failure in accountability and ethical governance within Mexico’s primary statistical institution. The overwhelming majority of complaints relate to the non-compliance of finality personnel with asset and interest declarations. This suggests that the core issue is not merely individual misconduct, but a widespread lack of transparency and integrity in how temporary staff manage their public duties, potentially undermining the credibility of the data they produce. The disparity between the volume of investigations and the severity of resulting sanctions raises serious concerns about the effectiveness of internal disciplinary mechanisms. With thousands of cases deemed to have administrative responsibility, the imposition of relatively minor penalties indicates a potential culture of impunity. This weak enforcement risks normalizing unethical behavior and eroding public trust, as the consequences for misconduct appear insufficient to deter future violations or ensure strict adherence to legal and ethical standards. This situation is directly relevant to open data because Inegi is a fundamental source of statistical information for the public and private sectors. If the integrity of the personnel generating this data is compromised by irregularities, bias, or misuse of information, the reliability of open datasets is jeopardized. Ensuring strict accountability among data custodians is essential to maintain the quality, neutrality, and trustworthiness of open government information, which stakeholders rely on for critical decision-making and analysis.
