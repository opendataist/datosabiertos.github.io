Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article claims the Mexican government is deliberately undermining the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information, and Personal Data Protection. This obstruction allegedly serves to hide corruption and wasteful spending by current leaders, preventing public accountability regarding resource allocation. Such actions threaten to erase decades of institutional progress achieved through previous transparency bodies. By silencing oversight mechanisms, authorities risk returning to a regime of opacity that prioritizes political campaigns over public interest, thereby eroding democratic safeguards and citizen rights. This issue is central to open data because institutional independence ensures mandatory disclosure of government spending. Without a functioning regulatory body, citizens lose the ability to access critical information, making it impossible to monitor public finances or demand transparency in an open data framework.
