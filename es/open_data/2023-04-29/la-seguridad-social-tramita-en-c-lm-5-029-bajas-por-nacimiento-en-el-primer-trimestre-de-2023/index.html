La Seguridad Social tramita en C-LM 5.029 bajas por nacimiento en el primer trimestre de 2023

This article highlights the shift towards gender equality in Spanish parental leave, where the unified benefit replaced separate maternity and paternity rights. The core conclusion is that men are increasingly utilizing their non-transferable leave, indicating a cultural and administrative normalization of shared caregiving responsibilities across the country. The data reveals a rising trend in both the volume of claims and financial expenditure, suggesting greater accessibility and recognition of this individual right. Although women still take slightly longer leaves, the significant participation of fathers demonstrates that the policy is effectively breaking traditional norms, allowing for more balanced family dynamics without financial penalty. This is relevant to open data because it showcases how public sector statistics can measure social progress and policy effectiveness. By making detailed demographic and financial data transparent, the INSS enables researchers and citizens to track behavioral changes, ensuring that legal frameworks for equality are genuinely implemented and understood in real-world contexts.
