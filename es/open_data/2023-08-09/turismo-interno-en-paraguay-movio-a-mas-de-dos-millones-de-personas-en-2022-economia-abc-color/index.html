Turismo interno en Paraguay movió a más de dos millones de personas en 2022 - Economía - ABC Color

The recent joint survey by Paraguay’s tourism and statistics agencies reveals a substantial rebound in the nation's travel sector, highlighting a significant economic impact driven by domestic tourism. This resurgence, measured against 2019 benchmarks, underscores the critical role internal travel plays in the country’s economic recovery. The data indicates that local journeys, primarily motivated by leisure and family visits, generated considerable revenue through spending on accommodation, food, and transport. This trend demonstrates that domestic mobility is not merely a social activity but a vital engine for local commerce and regional development. Beyond domestic trends, the inclusion of new metrics for incoming visitors provides a more holistic view of the tourism landscape. For the first time, statistical bodies analyzed inbound travel patterns, distinguishing between tourists and day-trippers at major border points. The findings show that while many visitors come for family reunions or business, a large segment is driven by shopping, particularly at borders. This insight allows policymakers to tailor strategies that maximize the economic benefits of international arrivals, ensuring that infrastructure and services meet the specific needs of these distinct traveler groups. This report is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the power of cross-institutional collaboration to produce comprehensive, high-quality statistical datasets. By making detailed information about travel volumes, spending habits, and destination preferences publicly available, the government fosters transparency and enables researchers, businesses, and developers to create data-driven solutions. Access to such granular data encourages innovation in the tourism sector, allowing stakeholders to identify emerging trends, optimize resource allocation, and enhance the overall visitor experience through evidence-based decision-making.
