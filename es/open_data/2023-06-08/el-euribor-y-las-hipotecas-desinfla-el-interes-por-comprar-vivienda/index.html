El Euribor y las hipotecas desinfla el interés por comprar vivienda

Spanish real estate portals report that housing prices are rising at a moderated pace, driven by owners of existing homes who face lower financing needs. Despite higher mortgage costs, urgency to buy before conditions worsen sustains demand, preventing the expected price corrections. This matters for open data because portals like Idealista and Fotocasa generate granular, real-time market insights that complement official statistics. Their data reveals behavioral nuances, such as how financial pressures accelerate transactions or segment demand by home ownership, providing essential context for understanding true market dynamics beyond aggregate indices.
