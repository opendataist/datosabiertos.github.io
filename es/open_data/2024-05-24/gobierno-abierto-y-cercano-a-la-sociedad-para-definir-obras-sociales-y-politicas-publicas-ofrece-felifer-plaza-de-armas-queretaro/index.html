Gobierno abierto y cercano a la sociedad, para definir obras sociales y políticas públicas, ofrece Felifer - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

The article highlights a commitment to open governance by subjecting a significant portion of the social works budget to citizen consultation. This approach empowers residents to define urgent priorities, ensuring public investment aligns with community needs rather than being dictated solely by officials. It further proposes institutionalizing transparency through independent evaluations by civil society and establishing mechanisms like a "Citizen Council" for direct democratic participation. These initiatives ensure that design approvals and administrative plans remain accessible and accountable to the public from the outset of any administration. This case is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how making fiscal and planning data accessible enables active civic engagement. By revealing how resources are allocated and allowing public scrutiny, it illustrates the practical application of transparency principles, fostering trust and ensuring that public services truly reflect the priorities of the governed.
