INEGI revela cambios demográficos en México | Revista Punto de Vista

The INEGI reveals that Mexico is undergoing a significant demographic shift, characterized by fewer children and an aging population. This transformation highlights changing family structures, with more single-person households and unions rather than traditional marriages, signaling a new social reality. These trends are vital for open data as they demonstrate how accessible statistical insights empower citizens to understand national evolution. Transparently sharing such detailed demographic shifts fosters accountability and enables better societal planning, proving that public data is essential for comprehending the country's future trajectory. Consequently, relying on these open sources allows researchers and policymakers to track societal changes accurately. By analyzing these patterns, stakeholders can address emerging challenges, ensuring that public services and policies adapt to the evolving needs of a younger shrinking core and a growing elderly sector.
