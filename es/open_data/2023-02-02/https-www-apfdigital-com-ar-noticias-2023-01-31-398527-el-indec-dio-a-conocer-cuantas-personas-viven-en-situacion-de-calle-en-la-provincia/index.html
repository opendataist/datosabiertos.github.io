The census reveals significant homelessness in Entre Ríos, with nearly 140 individuals living on the streets and nearly 10,000 in collective shelters. This data highlights the scale of vulnerable housing situations across various departments, emphasizing the urgent need for social support systems. These figures are crucial for open data initiatives as they demonstrate the value of transparent government statistics in identifying social inequalities. By making such granular demographic information publicly accessible, communities and policymakers can better analyze resource allocation and improve living standards for marginalized populations.
