Cumple Tecnológico con Ley de Transparencia | La Opción de Chihuahua

The ITSNCG achieved full compliance with transparency laws in 2022, receiving a perfect score for disclosing public information obligations. This achievement highlights the institution’s commitment to accountability and effective data governance within its operations. Such adherence is vital for open data ecosystems, ensuring public trust and enabling researchers to rely on accurate, accessible institutional records for further analysis and civic engagement.
