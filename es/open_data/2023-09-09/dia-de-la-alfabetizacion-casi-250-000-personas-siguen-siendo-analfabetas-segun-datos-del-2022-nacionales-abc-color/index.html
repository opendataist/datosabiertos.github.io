Día de la Alfabetización: casi 250.000 personas siguen siendo analfabetas, según datos del 2022 - Nacionales - ABC Color

The article highlights a significant decline in illiteracy rates in Paraguay over the past two decades, emphasizing progress toward a more just society. By tracking demographic shifts, it reveals that rural populations remain disproportionately affected compared to urban areas. This disparity underscores the ongoing challenge of achieving equitable access to education resources across different regions. Open data initiatives are crucial for monitoring such socioeconomic trends accurately. Transparent statistical reporting allows policymakers to identify marginalized groups and allocate resources effectively. Without reliable, publicly available data, addressing structural inequalities in literacy becomes difficult, hindering the development of targeted educational strategies. Ultimately, the availability of clear statistical information enables evidence-based decision-making. It supports the implementation of policies that uphold human rights and dignity through education. Ensuring data accessibility promotes accountability and helps sustain long-term improvements in social equity, making open data a foundational tool for measuring and driving societal progress.
