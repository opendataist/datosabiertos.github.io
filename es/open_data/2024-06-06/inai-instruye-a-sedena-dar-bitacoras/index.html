Inai instruye a Sedena dar bitácoras

The INAI ruled that the National Defense Secretariat must release monthly work logs for the Nogales railway relocation project. Despite the institution’s claim that documents should remain at the construction site, the authority determined this data is public information due to significant opacity regarding the new track alignment. This decision reinforces the principle that government-contracted infrastructure reports are accessible to citizens, challenging institutional resistance to transparency. It ensures accountability for public funds spent on critical transportation developments and sets a precedent for enforcing open data standards in defense-related civilian projects.
