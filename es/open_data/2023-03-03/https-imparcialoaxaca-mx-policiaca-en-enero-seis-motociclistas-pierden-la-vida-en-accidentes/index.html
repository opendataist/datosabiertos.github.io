Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The rising lethality of traffic accidents highlights a critical public safety crisis, particularly affecting vulnerable road users like pedestrians and cyclists. This trend underscores systemic failures in infrastructure design and enforcement, demanding immediate attention to prevent further loss of life in urban centers. Data reveals a disturbing increase in fatalities among motorcyclists and pedestrians, indicating that current safety measures are insufficient to protect non-motorized transport users. The disparity in risk exposure points to a need for prioritizing safety for those most at risk, rather than solely focusing on vehicular flow. This issue is relevant to open data because transparent, accessible statistics are essential for holding authorities accountable and driving evidence-based policy changes. Without reliable, publicly available data on accident causes and locations, it is impossible to effectively monitor trends or implement targeted interventions to reduce these preventable deaths.
