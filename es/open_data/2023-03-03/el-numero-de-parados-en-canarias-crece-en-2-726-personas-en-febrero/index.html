El número de parados en Canarias crece en 2.726 personas en febrero

Canary Islands registered unemployment rose slightly month-over-month, marking the second highest increase nationally. However, the year-on-year trend shows a significant decline, improving labor market conditions over time. This data highlights the importance of open data in tracking regional economic disparities and employment dynamics. Transparent access to such statistics enables better policy analysis and helps stakeholders understand the impact of local versus national labor trends effectively.
