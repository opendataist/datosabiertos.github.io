Aviso de Privacidad

This privacy notice outlines how 13 MEDIA, S.C. manages personal data in strict compliance with federal regulations. The company emphasizes that the collection and handling of information are governed by core principles such as licitness, consent, proportionality, and responsibility. Data is gathered directly from users through digital and physical channels to verify identity, understand client needs, and fulfill legal obligations, ensuring that all processing remains transparent and respectful of the individual’s rights. The organization shares personal information only with specific third parties, such as legal professionals or public notaries, when necessary to formalize documents or meet statutory requirements. Users are granted the ability to restrict the use or disclosure of their data by contacting designated privacy officers via email or phone, unless legal mandates prevent such limitations. This framework allows clients to maintain control over their information while acknowledging that certain data sharing is unavoidable for regulatory compliance. This document is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical boundary between transparent organizational practices and the protection of sensitive personal information. While open data advocates for the free flow of non-personal public information, this notice demonstrates the mandatory mechanisms required to safeguard individual privacy rights. It illustrates that effective data governance involves not just openness, but also rigorous adherence to legal standards that protect citizens, ensuring that data initiatives do not compromise personal security or consent.
