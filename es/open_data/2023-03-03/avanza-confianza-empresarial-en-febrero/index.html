Avanza confianza empresarial en febrero

Business confidence in Mexico’s key sectors rebounded in February, signaling a recovery in economic sentiment. Manufacturing and service industries showed significant gains, though construction and commerce remain below optimistic thresholds, indicating persistent caution among entrepreneurs. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides public, timely insights into the national economic climate. Such accessible statistics enable researchers and analysts to monitor market trends, fostering transparency and supporting evidence-based decision-making for policymakers and businesses alike.
