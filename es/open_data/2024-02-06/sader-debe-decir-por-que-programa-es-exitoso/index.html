Sader debe decir por qué programa es exitoso

The INAI mandates the SADER ministry to disclose the underlying data supporting claims made by its head regarding the success of the "Fertilizers for Wellbeing" program. This decision emphasizes that public officials must provide verifiable sources for information shared via social media to ensure transparency and public trust in government communications. This ruling is crucial for open data because it establishes a legal precedent requiring accountability for digital communications. It reinforces the principle that government data cannot be presented as facts without accessible, verifiable documentation, thereby empowering citizens to validate official statements. Furthermore, rejecting excuses about lacking authority over social media platforms underscores that public servants remain bound by transparency laws regardless of the medium. This ensures that open data principles extend to all official channels, preventing the concealment of information behind technicalities and promoting genuine access to governmental records for societal oversight.
