Abierto registro para Beca Contigo Soy Capaz

The article details eligibility criteria and deadlines for a specific disability scholarship, emphasizing strict residency and dependency requirements. This highlights the gap between localized administrative processes and the principles of open data, which demand transparent, standardized, and accessible public information. True open data initiatives should ensure such social benefits are discoverable and understandable for all citizens without navigating complex, opaque bureaucratic hurdles. This case illustrates the need for governments to publish assistance programs in machine-readable formats, ensuring equitable access to public resources for vulnerable populations.
