Una familia tipo necesitó en noviembre $390.456 para no ser pobre

The article highlights that rising prices for basic food and total essential goods have significantly increased the income required for families to avoid poverty and indigence. This underscores how inflation directly impacts social welfare thresholds, making economic survival increasingly difficult for average households. These data illustrate the severity of the current economic crisis by quantifying the gap between necessary earnings and actual family budgets. The divergence between the cost of basic nutrition and total living expenses reveals deepening social inequalities and financial stress across different family sizes. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, standardized metrics on national well-being. Open access to such statistical indicators allows researchers and citizens to monitor inequality, advocate for policy changes, and ensure accountability in government economic management.
