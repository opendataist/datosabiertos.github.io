Castilla-La Mancha sigue siendo la segunda Comunidad Autónoma que más ha reducido el peso de la deuda publica en los últimos cuatro años

Castilla-La Mancha has significantly reduced its public debt-to-GDP ratio, outperforming the national average over the last four years. This substantial fiscal consolidation demonstrates that regional authorities can achieve economic stability without compromising essential social support or economic stimulation, proving that responsible management and social commitment are compatible goals. The continuous decline in debt weight highlights effective long-term financial governance within the autonomous community. By consistently reducing this burden relative to economic output, the region sets a positive example for other territories seeking to balance their books while maintaining vital public services. This case is relevant to open data because it showcases how transparent, publicly available economic statistics enable accountability and informed public debate. Accessible data allows citizens and analysts to verify government claims regarding fiscal health, fostering trust in open governance models where evidence-based policy decisions are clearly documented and monitored.
