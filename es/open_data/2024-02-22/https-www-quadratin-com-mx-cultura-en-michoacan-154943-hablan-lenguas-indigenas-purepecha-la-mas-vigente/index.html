Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Michoacán highlights the urgent need to preserve its diverse indigenous languages, revealing that thousands of residents speak only native tongues without Spanish. This linguistic reality underscores the critical importance of accessible, multilingual open data to ensure equitable information access for marginalized communities. The state recognizes that despite technological advancements, discrimination and historical displacement threaten these cultural assets, necessitating proactive revitalization policies. Protecting these languages is not merely about heritage but about ensuring inclusive digital environments where all citizens can participate fully in societal information systems. This initiative aligns with global efforts to promote linguistic diversity, emphasizing that open data must be inclusive and culturally sensitive. By supporting these preservation strategies, we advance the principle that information sovereignty belongs to all, regardless of language, making this article vital for understanding the intersection of digital inclusion and cultural rights.
