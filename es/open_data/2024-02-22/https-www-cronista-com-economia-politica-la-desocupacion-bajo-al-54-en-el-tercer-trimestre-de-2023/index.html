Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

INDEC reveals that Argentina’s labor market shows improved employment rates and reduced unemployment compared to the previous year. This data highlights the scale of underemployment and hidden job seekers within the active population, indicating structural vulnerabilities despite headline gains. The findings emphasize regional disparities, with varying employment and unemployment rates across provinces. These geographic differences suggest that economic recovery is uneven, requiring targeted policies to address local labor market specifics rather than relying on national averages. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it enables transparency in socioeconomic tracking. By analyzing such granular, freely available statistics, researchers and developers can build tools that expose inequality and drive evidence-based decision-making for better labor inclusion policies.
