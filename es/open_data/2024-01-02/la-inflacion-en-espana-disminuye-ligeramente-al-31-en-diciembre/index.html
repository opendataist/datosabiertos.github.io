La inflación en España disminuye ligeramente al 3,1% en diciembre

Spain’s inflation has shown a slight deceleration, driven largely by stabilized electricity and food prices. This trend reflects a broader cooling in consumer costs, with underlying inflation also declining significantly. The government’s temporary subsidies have helped mitigate extreme price spikes, although energy support is gradually being withdrawn as the economy stabilizes. This data is relevant to open_data initiatives because accurate, timely, and accessible statistical information from the INE allows researchers and citizens to verify economic trends independently. Transparent data empowers communities to understand the real impact of policy measures and market fluctuations. Open access to these indicators fosters accountability and enables deeper analysis of how fiscal interventions influence daily life. By making such data freely available, society can better assess the effectiveness of governmental actions and advocate for informed economic decisions.
