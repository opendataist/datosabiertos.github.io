La actividad económica descendió un 0,1 % mensual en abril

Argentina’s economic activity declined in April, marking the second year of contraction driven by high inflation and severe fiscal adjustments under the current administration. Key sectors like construction and manufacturing saw significant drops, contrasting with agricultural growth, while overall demand suffered from collapsing consumption. This trend confirms a deteriorating economic scenario that has persisted for months, with the first quarter showing a cumulative decline. Private economists predict a full-year contraction, reflecting deep structural challenges as the government implements deregulation and austerity measures. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, official statistical releases allow citizens and analysts to independently verify government narratives and assess the real impact of economic policies. Reliable access to such indicators is crucial for accountability and informed public debate regarding macroeconomic health.
