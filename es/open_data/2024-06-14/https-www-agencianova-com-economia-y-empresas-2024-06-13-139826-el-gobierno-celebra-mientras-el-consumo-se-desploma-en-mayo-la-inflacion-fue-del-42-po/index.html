Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The INDEC’s data highlights how state intervention can temporarily moderate inflation figures, creating a facade of economic stability despite underlying high cumulative price increases. This manipulation underscores the critical need for transparent, unaltered public statistics to accurately reflect real economic conditions. Relevance to open data lies in the potential manipulation of official metrics, which compromises data integrity. Open data frameworks must ensure algorithmic transparency and raw data accessibility to detect such distortions, preserving trust in statistical institutions and enabling informed public debate.
