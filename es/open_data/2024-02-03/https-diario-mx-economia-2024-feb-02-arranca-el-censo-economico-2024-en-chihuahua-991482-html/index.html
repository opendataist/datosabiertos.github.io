Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The INEGI is conducting the 2024 Economic Census in Chihuahua, emphasizing family cooperation to gather vital socioeconomic data. This collaborative effort enables universities to perform deeper research, identifying specific areas of national opportunity and growth based on accurate household information. Understanding local conditions allows authorities to pinpoint populations requiring targeted attention. This process highlights how comprehensive data collection directly supports evidence-based policy and academic research, revealing critical insights into regional development needs. This initiative is crucial for open data because it generates high-quality, granular datasets. Access to such detailed socioeconomic statistics empowers researchers and the public to analyze trends transparently, fostering informed decision-making and promoting accountability in public resource allocation and social strategies.
