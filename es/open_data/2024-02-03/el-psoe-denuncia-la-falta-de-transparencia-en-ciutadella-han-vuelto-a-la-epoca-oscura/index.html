El PSOE denuncia la falta de transparencia en Ciutadella: «Han vuelto a la época oscura»

The opposition demands full economic transparency, criticizing the current government for reverting to opaque practices that violate established codes of good governance. This shift contrasts sharply with the accessible information era under previous administrations, highlighting a significant regression in public accountability. Ensuring open access to financial data is crucial for maintaining trust and adhering to open data principles in municipal management.
