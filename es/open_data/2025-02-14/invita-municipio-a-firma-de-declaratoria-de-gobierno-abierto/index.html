Invita Municipio a firma de declaratoria de Gobierno Abierto

Chihuahua initiates its second Open Government Action Plan through a formal declaration, inviting citizens to join the driving group. This collaborative effort aims to enhance transparency, honesty, and public access to information. The initiative strengthens the local management model by actively involving society in decision-making. It fosters a robust culture of accountability, ensuring governance remains close to the people. This matters to open data as it commits to improved information accessibility and transparency. By engaging citizens in policy design, it promotes the open exchange of government data, essential for democratic participation and trust in public institutions.
