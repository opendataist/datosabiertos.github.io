Reconocerá el IZAI las mejores prácticas de transparencia proactiva

The Zacatecas Institute for Transparency announces a call to recognize public institutions that exceed mandatory transparency obligations through innovative, proactive disclosure practices. This initiative aims to foster a culture where government entities proactively share useful information, going beyond basic legal requirements to better serve society and empower citizens with efficient, timely public data. Such recognition highlights the value of voluntary transparency efforts that enhance public trust and access. By evaluating and rewarding these extra-mile initiatives, the institute encourages other agencies to adopt similar strategies, thereby raising the overall standard of openness and information quality across the state’s public administration. This matters for open data because it incentivizes the creation of high-quality, accessible public information beyond minimum compliance. It promotes a shift from passive compliance to active dissemination, ensuring that open data initiatives yield tangible societal benefits. Encouraging proactive disclosure strengthens the ecosystem of open government by valuing usability and innovation, ultimately fostering a more informed and engaged citizenry through improved public data availability.
