Guatemala has launched "Guatemala Does Not Stop" to enhance digital rights and government transparency. A key initiative, "Info Segura," aims to secure public information and strengthen cybersecurity. This effort involves the Presidential Commission for Open Government, UNDP, and civic groups, focusing on protecting citizen data while promoting safe digital access. The initiative highlights the urgent need to safeguard personal information against growing cyber threats. By establishing technical working groups, the government seeks to balance data protection with public accessibility. This reflects a broader shift toward responsible digital governance in the region. This is relevant to open data as it underscores the critical intersection of transparency and security. True openness requires robust frameworks to protect user privacy while sharing public information. Without such safeguards, public trust in digital government services may erode, hindering effective civic engagement.
