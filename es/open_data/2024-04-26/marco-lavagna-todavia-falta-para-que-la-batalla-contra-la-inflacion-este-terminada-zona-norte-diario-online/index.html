Marco Lavagna: "Todavía falta para que la batalla contra la inflación esté terminada" - Zona Norte Diario Online

The head of INDEC emphasizes that the battle against inflation is far from over, urging caution despite recent decreases in official rates. This stance underscores the critical role of independent national statistical bodies in providing accurate, unpressure data essential for reliable economic assessment. By rejecting external political narratives, INDEC maintains its integrity as the sole source of truth for public inflation metrics, a cornerstone of transparent open data ecosystems. To enhance accuracy, INDEC is updating its Consumer Price Index basket to reflect current consumption habits, a technical adjustment validated by the IMF. This modernization highlights the importance of keeping open data methodologies relevant and reflective of real-world changes. Such updates ensure that statistical outputs remain useful and credible, fostering trust in public institutions and enabling citizens and analysts to make informed decisions based on current realities rather than outdated models. Furthermore, the distinction between national and local indices, such as those in Buenos Aires, illustrates the complexities of standardizing data across different regions. By clarifying these methodological differences and detailing the extensive data collection process, INDEC promotes transparency regarding how metrics are generated. This openness allows for better scrutiny and understanding of statistical discrepancies, reinforcing the value of accessible, well-documented public data for democratic accountability and effective policy-making.
