Apuesta por un centro de proceso de datos en el macropozo de La Sagrera

The proposal to repurpose the La Sagrera macropozo into a data center highlights a strategic move toward localized digital infrastructure. By situating servers directly within the city, authorities aim to significantly reduce latency, thereby enhancing the speed and efficiency of public and private digital services. This proximity fosters a more responsive technological environment, crucial for modern governance and business operations in the region. Furthermore, the project emphasizes energy efficiency and sustainability through innovative design. Leveraging existing substation power and industrial cooling systems from Districlima, the facility seeks to create an eco-friendly ecosystem. This approach addresses the high energy demands of data centers by promoting the circular economy, such as reusing heat, which aligns with broader environmental goals while maintaining operational viability. This initiative is highly relevant to open_data as it prioritizes data sovereignty and accessibility. By reserving significant capacity for public use, the city ensures that critical civic information remains under local control. This infrastructure supports transparency and facilitates better data-driven decision-making, empowering citizens with immediate access to government resources while positioning Catalonia as a competitive digital hub in Southern Europe.
