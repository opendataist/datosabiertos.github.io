Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The INEGI report reveals that Mexico’s stable unemployment rate masks underlying labor market fragility. Despite a positive rise in economic participation, the persistence of subemployment and high informality indicates that many workers lack security and operate below their potential capacity. This data highlights a disconnect between headline stability and the actual quality of employment experiences across the population. Service sector dominance and moderate economic growth further illustrate the structural limitations of job creation. While agriculture remains resilient, the overall economy struggles to generate sufficient high-quality positions, emphasizing the urgent need for policies that foster formalization and improve social protections. Without addressing these disparities, the market fails to provide equitable opportunities for all contributors to the economy. This analysis is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public statistics must go beyond simple aggregates to expose nuanced social realities. By making detailed labor metrics accessible, policymakers and citizens can identify critical gaps in informality and subemployment. Open data thus serves as a vital tool for holding institutions accountable and driving informed debates on how to build a more inclusive and dynamic labor market through transparent evidence.
