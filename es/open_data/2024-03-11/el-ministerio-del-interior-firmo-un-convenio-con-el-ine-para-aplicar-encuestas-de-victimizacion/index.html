El Ministerio del Interior firmó un convenio con el INE para aplicar encuestas de victimización

The Ministry of the Interior and the National Institute of Statistics have established a strategic partnership to standardize security data through periodic victimization surveys. This agreement mandates the application of rigorous statistical quality norms to the Ministry’s records, ensuring that public safety metrics adhere to internationally recognized standards. By integrating official complaints data with survey results, the state aims to create a comprehensive and reliable picture of crime, moving beyond simple reportage to a deeper understanding of social dynamics. This collaboration prioritizes transparency and evidence-based policymaking. Access to high-quality, certified data allows authorities to design targeted security initiatives that address the root causes of crime rather than merely reacting to symptoms. The initiative reflects a political consensus on building a more transparent state, responding to public demands for accurate information. Consequently, decision-making processes are strengthened, enabling the government to implement policies that are closely aligned with the actual experiences and needs of citizens. This development is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how institutional cooperation can enhance the reliability and utility of public statistics. By committing to quality certification and periodic data collection, the partnership ensures that security information is not only accessible but also trustworthy and comparable. This sets a precedent for using robust data ecosystems to drive sustainable development goals and foster civic trust through the transparent sharing of critical societal metrics.
