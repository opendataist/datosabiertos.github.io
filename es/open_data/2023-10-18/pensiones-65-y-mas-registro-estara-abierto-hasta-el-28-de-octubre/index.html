Pensiones "65 y más": registro estará abierto hasta el 28 de octubre

The article announces a specific registration window for adults who have not previously enrolled in the welfare pension program. This extension allows late applicants to access social protection, ensuring that eligible citizens are not excluded due to initial administrative barriers or scheduling conflicts. Registration requires visiting designated local modules with specific identification and residency documents. The process is structured alphabetically to manage volume efficiently. This standardized approach aims to streamline service delivery while ensuring that all necessary legal criteria for vulnerability and age are properly verified before benefits are granted. This initiative is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for accessible, transparent public information infrastructure. The reliance on a unique identifier (CURP) and the requirement to check official government portals underscore how open digital platforms must be user-centric and reliable. It demonstrates the intersection of public policy and data accessibility, where clear, open communication channels are essential for equitable social program implementation and citizen trust.
