Chihuahua, segundo estado con más maquileros: Inegi

Chihuahua’s manufacturing sector shows a divergence between high employment and lower sales, revealing a gap between production capacity and market demand. This highlights data limitations, as current metrics exclude subcontracted workers, potentially skewing the true economic impact. Understanding these structural gaps is vital for open data initiatives, which must strive for comprehensive coverage. Transparent, complete datasets enable policymakers and citizens to accurately assess industrial health beyond superficial employment figures. Ultimately, improving data granularity supports better economic decisions. By integrating subcontracted labor into official records, open data frameworks can provide a more realistic view of regional industrial dynamics and labor markets.
