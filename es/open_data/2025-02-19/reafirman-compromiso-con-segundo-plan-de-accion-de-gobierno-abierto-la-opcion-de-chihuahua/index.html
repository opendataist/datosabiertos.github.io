Reafirman compromiso con Segundo Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua launches its second Open Government Action Plan, reinforcing its commitment to transparency and citizen participation through collaborative public policies. By engaging citizens in a steering group, the administration aims to enhance governance accountability and ensure public information remains accessible and responsive to community needs. This initiative is relevant to open data as it structurally integrates civil society into decision-making, promoting a model where government transparency and participatory governance are sustained through ongoing, inclusive dialogue rather than isolated transactions.
