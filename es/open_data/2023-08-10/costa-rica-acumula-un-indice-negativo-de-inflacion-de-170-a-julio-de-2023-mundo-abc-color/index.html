Costa Rica acumula un índice negativo de inflación de -1,70 % a julio de 2023 - Mundo - ABC Color

Costa Rica experienced its lowest inflation rate in a decade, driven by significant price drops in essential goods and services like fuel, food, and tourism. This negative trend marks a substantial economic shift, highlighting how specific market dynamics can drive broader deflationary pressures across the consumer basket. The Central Bank revised its economic growth forecast upward, anticipating that annual inflation will finish below its official target range. This projection underscores a successful stabilization of prices, reversing the sharp increases seen in previous years and validating the institution’s cautious approach to monetary policy adjustments. This case is vital for open_data because it demonstrates how transparently reported macroeconomic indicators enable real-time monitoring of policy effectiveness. By making detailed indices publicly accessible, researchers and citizens can verify official claims, fostering accountability and deeper analysis of how monetary decisions impact everyday economic stability.
