Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Major media groups and tech firms emphasize that AI developers must respect journalistic copyright, acknowledging AI's potential benefits while warning that unchecked data scraping harms intellectual property rights. This open letter demands transparent practices, including the mandatory disclosure of AI-generated content, to prevent the proliferation of misinformation derived from authentic news sources. The article highlights the urgent need for global regulatory standards and collective negotiations to ensure ethical AI development. It stresses that transparency and clear attribution are essential to maintain trust and protect original creators in an era of synthetic content generation at unprecedented scales. This is relevant to open data because it challenges the assumption that web-scraped content is freely available for AI training. It underscores the tension between data accessibility and intellectual property, urging the open data community to support frameworks that balance innovation with the legal and ethical protection of original information sources.
