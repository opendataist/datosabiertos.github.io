Mexican business confidence is declining due to economic uncertainty, reduced investment, and political instability. This pessimism across key sectors like manufacturing and construction threatens economic growth, employment, and production stability. The shift reflects a cautious environment where inflation and global slowdowns discourage enterprise expansion. This trend is relevant to open data because transparent, accessible government statistics are essential for diagnosing these economic shifts. When reliable indicators like the Business Confidence Index are publicly available, stakeholders can better understand market conditions and policy impacts. Ultimately, accurate data empowers citizens and experts to demand effective public policies. By monitoring these metrics, society can advocate for measures that restore trust and stimulate investment, ensuring that data serves as a tool for economic accountability and recovery.
