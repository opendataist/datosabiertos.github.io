Economía de Brasil crece 1,4 por ciento en segundo trimestre - Noticias Prensa Latina

Brazil’s industrial sector drove economic growth, expanding significantly in utilities, construction, and manufacturing, while services also rose. This performance contributes to a robust annual GDP increase, positioning the nation favorably in global rankings and signaling improved employment and living standards. This report is relevant to open data because national economic indicators like GDP require transparent, accessible datasets for public scrutiny. Reliable, open statistical data allows citizens and researchers to verify claims about national progress, fostering accountability and informed civic engagement regarding the country's development trajectory.
