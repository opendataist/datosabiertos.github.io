Aún no se recibe orden para nombramientos

The Senate’s First Commission has not yet received the judicial order forcing them to call an extraordinary session to appoint missing commissioners for the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection. This delay highlights a critical governance gap, as legislators face significant financial penalties for non-compliance with the court’s mandate. The situation underscores the tension between legislative procedure and judicial oversight. Without urgent action, individual lawmakers risk severe fines for contempt. This standoff illustrates how internal political decisions can directly obstruct the implementation of court rulings, creating uncertainty in the executive branch's regulatory appointments. This case is vital to open data because it demonstrates the fragility of institutional independence. An incomplete INAI leadership compromises the enforcement of transparency laws, potentially weakening citizens' rights to access public information. Ensuring timely appointments is essential for maintaining the integrity of the data ecosystem and public trust in governmental accountability mechanisms.
