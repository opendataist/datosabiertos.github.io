LORCA / El Ayuntamiento constituye la Unidad de Control del Empadronamiento (UCE) que combatirá los fraudes en el padrón municipal - murcia.com

Lorca has established a specialized unit to detect and combat fraud in its municipal registry, aiming to dismantle the "bedroom community" model. This initiative prioritizes transparency and accurate data management by coordinating police, urban planning, and statistical services for targeted home visits. The relevance to open data lies in improving the integrity of foundational civic datasets. Reliable population registers are essential for effective urban planning and resource allocation. By actively verifying residence status, the municipality ensures its public statistics reflect reality, enhancing the trustworthiness of locally generated information. Ultimately, this move demonstrates how proactive data validation supports better governance. It highlights the importance of maintaining clean, accurate registers not just for legal compliance, but for creating a trustworthy information ecosystem that benefits both administrators and residents.
