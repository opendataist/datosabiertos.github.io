La firma de hipotecas se desploma: bajaron un 18,3% en abril, quedándose en 27.053 préstamos

The Spanish housing market experienced a significant contraction in April, with mortgage signings dropping to their lowest levels since early 2021. This sharp decline reflects the impact of rising interest rates, which have reached a six-year high, thereby suppressing demand and reducing the total capital lent for property purchases. A notable structural shift occurred as borrowers increasingly favored fixed-rate mortgages over variable ones to mitigate financial uncertainty. While new lending contracts decreased, the number of mortgage modifications also fell significantly, indicating reduced flexibility for existing homeowners adapting to current economic conditions. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical releases enable real-time analysis of macroeconomic trends. By making detailed metrics on loan types and regional variations publicly available, such datasets support academic research, policy formulation, and informed decision-making within the financial sector.
