SEP debe informar sobre objetos peligrosos en revisiones

The INAI ruled that the SEP failed to guarantee transparency by providing generic documents instead of specific operational results regarding school supply inspections. This decision highlights the critical importance of releasing precise, actionable data rather than merely pointing to existing guidelines. True open data requires fulfilling specific public inquiries to ensure meaningful access to government information.
