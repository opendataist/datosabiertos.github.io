China tiene más de 4,5 millones de empresas en industrias clave de economía digital - Confirmado

China’s digital economy enterprises surpassed 4.57 million, marking significant growth. This expansion highlights the increasing reliance on digital technologies and data-driven models across key industries, reflecting a robust shift toward digitalization. The surge underscores the vital role of digital infrastructure and services in modern economic development. As enterprises proliferate, the need for accessible, standardized data becomes critical for innovation and competitive analysis within this rapidly evolving sector. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the scale of digital economic activity. Understanding these trends requires transparent data access, enabling better policy-making and fostering an ecosystem where information flows freely to support sustainable digital growth.
