INE recluta a 234.065 censistas voluntarios, el 34% del total requerido para el censo de 2024

The Bolivian National Statistics Institute has secured a significant portion of volunteers for the 2024 census, highlighting strong civic engagement. This recruitment effort, crucial for generating accurate population data, demonstrates effective collaboration between public institutions and educational sectors. Young people and teachers dominate the participant pool, indicating a widespread commitment to supporting national data collection initiatives. This demographic involvement ensures broader community representation in the upcoming census operations scheduled for March. This article is relevant to open_data as it illustrates the foundational human infrastructure required for high-quality statistical outputs. Transparent recruitment and broad participation are essential for producing reliable, publicly accessible datasets that inform policy and societal development.
