El IPC de Guatemala registró una variación de -0,66% en noviembre de 2023

Guatemala’s November inflation data reveals a notable deceleration in price increases, reflecting a broader economic stabilization trend. This slowdown suggests that previous monetary pressures are easing, offering a glimpse into the country’s evolving cost-of-living dynamics for residents and policymakers alike. The report highlights specific sectoral shifts, with food and fuel costs driving negative variations while leisure and travel expenses saw modest gains. Understanding these granular changes is crucial for identifying which goods and services contribute most to the national price index over time. This dataset is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the need for accessible, high-quality statistical releases. Transparent publication of such indices allows researchers and citizens to independently analyze economic health, fostering greater accountability and informed public discourse regarding inflation trends.
