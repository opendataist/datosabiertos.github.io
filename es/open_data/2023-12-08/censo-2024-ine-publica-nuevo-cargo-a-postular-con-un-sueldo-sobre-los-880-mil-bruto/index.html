Censo 2024: INE publica nuevo cargo a postular con un sueldo sobre los $880 mil bruto

The National Statistics Institute has expanded its recruitment for the 2024 Population and Housing Census by introducing a new role for group coordinators. This expansion highlights the institutional effort to mobilize human resources necessary for comprehensive data collection, ensuring that every household and individual is accurately counted. By integrating leadership positions alongside administrative and field roles, the INE aims to strengthen the operational framework required for such a massive national exercise. This recruitment drive underscores the logistical complexity inherent in large-scale statistical surveys. The specific requirements for experience in field supervision and technical proficiency emphasize the need for qualified personnel to maintain data quality and operational standards. The structured application process, involving psychometric and curriculum evaluations, reflects a rigorous approach to selecting staff capable of handling the responsibilities of census coordination effectively. This news is relevant to open data because the census constitutes a foundational source of official demographic statistics. Accurate census data is essential for creating transparent, reliable, and accessible public datasets that inform policy and research. Ensuring robust data collection mechanisms directly impacts the integrity of open data ecosystems, making effective staffing and operational planning critical for maintaining high-quality, publicly available statistical information.
