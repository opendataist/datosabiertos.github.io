Senado mantiene rechazo a ternas del INAI pese a riesgo de destitución por parte de la Corte - Noticaribe

The Mexican Senate failed to appoint new commissioners for the INAI, highlighting significant legislative gridlock. This deadlock undermines the institute’s ability to function with a full panel, despite a Supreme Court order to resolve the vacancies by December 15. The inability to reach the required supermajority demonstrates deep political divisions. The Supreme Court has mandated the appointment of these missing members, emphasizing the constitutional necessity of a complete commission. Failure to comply could lead to severe consequences, including the potential dismissal of senators. This judicial pressure underscores the critical importance of timely institutional appointments to maintain governance standards. This situation is vital for open data because the INAI safeguards citizens' rights to access information and protect personal data. Without a fully operational body, transparency mechanisms are weakened, and accountability suffers. Ensuring the INAI’s full capacity is essential for maintaining public trust in government transparency and the integrity of open data frameworks in Mexico.
