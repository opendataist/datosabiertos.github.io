La creación de empresas sube un 11,8% y registra su mejor agosto desde 2007

The article highlights a significant surge in Spanish business activity, with new company formations reaching their highest August levels in over a decade. This growth is driven not just by volume but by a substantial increase in the capital invested per entity, particularly in the services sector. Simultaneously, company dissolutions have decreased, indicating a healthier economic environment where businesses are surviving and expanding rather than closing down. This positive trend underscores robust entrepreneurial confidence and investment capacity across key autonomous communities like Madrid and Catalonia. The data reveals that while retail and real estate drive formation numbers, the service sector attracts the largest capital shares. Regional disparities exist, yet most areas show increased creation rates, suggesting broad-based economic resilience rather than isolated booms in specific zones. For open data enthusiasts, this report exemplifies the value of transparent, standardized statistical publications by national institutes. It demonstrates how accessible, granular datasets enable analysts to track macroeconomic health, identify sectoral trends, and assess regional disparities in real-time. Such data is crucial for fostering accountability, guiding policy decisions, and supporting research that relies on precise, up-to-date economic indicators to understand societal dynamics.
