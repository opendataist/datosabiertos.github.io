España recibió 11,9 millones de viajes de turistas en 2022 por motivos culturales (una cifra cercana a la de 2019)

The report highlights a robust recovery in Spain’s cultural tourism sector, marked by increased trip durations and spending for both international visitors and domestic residents. This resurgence indicates a strong consumer willingness to prioritize cultural experiences, suggesting that cultural motivations are becoming a significant driver of travel behavior post-pandemic, with domestic travelers returning to near-2019 participation levels while international stays lengthened. Geographic analysis reveals distinct regional strengths, where certain autonomous communities lead in both traveler volume and expenditure. Madrid, Catalonia, Andalusia, and Valencia dominate in absolute numbers, yet smaller regions like the Basque Country show higher per capita engagement. This disparity implies that tourism strategies must be tailored to local contexts, recognizing that high participation rates do not always correlate with the highest economic outputs per resident. This data is relevant to open_data initiatives as it provides granular, geospatially tagged insights into tourism dynamics. Open datasets enable policymakers and researchers to visualize these regional trends, fostering evidence-based decision-making for infrastructure investment and marketing. By making such detailed metrics accessible, open data supports transparency and allows for a deeper understanding of how cultural tourism contributes to regional economic resilience and development.
