Censo: solucionan conflictos en Abapó y Santa Martha; Colomi espera hoy al INE

The article highlights how census data irregularities, particularly regarding municipal codes and territorial boundaries, require urgent resolution by the National Institute of Statistics. Conflicts in various regions led to community blockades, underscoring the critical need for accurate geographic classification to ensure legal recognition and resource allocation. A significant aspect of this process is the upcoming redistributive action by the Supreme Electoral Tribunal. Once official population figures are finalized, political representation will be reassigned across departments without increasing the total number of parliamentary seats. This adjustment demonstrates how demographic data directly influences political power distribution and administrative boundaries. This case is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates the tangible societal impact of data accuracy and accessibility. Transparent and correct public statistics are essential for resolving civic disputes and ensuring equitable political representation. It emphasizes that open data is not merely technical information but a foundational tool for democratic governance and social justice, requiring robust validation mechanisms to prevent institutional friction.
