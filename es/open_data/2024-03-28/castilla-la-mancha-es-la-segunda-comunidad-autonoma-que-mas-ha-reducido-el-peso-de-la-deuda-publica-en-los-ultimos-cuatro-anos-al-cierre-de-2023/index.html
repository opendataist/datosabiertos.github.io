Castilla-La Mancha es la segunda Comunidad Autónoma que más ha reducido el peso de la deuda pública en los últimos cuatro años al cierre de 2023

Castilla-La Mancha significantly reduced its public debt-to-GDP ratio in 2023, outperforming the national average. This fiscal consolidation demonstrates that rigorous financial management can coexist with robust social spending and economic stimulation. The region proves that debt reduction does not necessitate austerity measures affecting vulnerable populations. This balance highlights a sustainable governance model where economic health supports social welfare. By maintaining sane public finances, the administration secures long-term stability while continuing to invest in quality job creation and support for those in need. This case is relevant to open_data as it illustrates how transparent, standardized economic indicators allow for comparative regional analysis. It underscores the importance of accessible, accurate datasets in evaluating public policy efficacy and holding governments accountable for balancing fiscal responsibility with social commitments.
