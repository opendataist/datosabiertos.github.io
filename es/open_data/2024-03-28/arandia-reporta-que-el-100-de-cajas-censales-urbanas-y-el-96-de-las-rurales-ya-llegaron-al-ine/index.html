Arandia reporta que el 100% de cajas censales urbanas y el 96% de las rurales ya llegaron al INE

The article highlights the INE’s confirmation that the post-census phase has officially begun, marking the successful return of nearly all census data boxes to central processing centers. While most logistical and boundary disputes were technically resolved, a minor conflict in Colomi remains pending. The director emphasized that the census was the most transparent and participatory to date, relying on high-tech data processing and continuous international oversight to ensure accuracy. This robust framework underscores the credibility of the collected information, which is now moving toward scanning and result preparation. This process is vital for open data because the resulting high-quality, verified datasets will drive future policy decisions, such as economic redistribution and political seat allocation. Ensuring this data is accurate and transparent establishes a reliable foundation for public accountability and informed decision-making.
