Empleo ‘fortachón’: tasa de desempleo en México toca nivel más bajo desde 2005

Mexico’s labor market demonstrated robust performance in early 2023, achieving the lowest unemployment rate since records began. This significant decline in joblessness highlights a strong economic recovery, suggesting that increased activity is effectively absorbing available labor resources across various sectors. However, this positive trend reveals a persistent structural challenge: the rise in informal employment. While formal jobs were created, informal positions grew alongside them, indicating that a substantial portion of the workforce remains outside protective labor frameworks. This dynamic underscores the complex nature of current job creation. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it illustrates how public statistics can reveal nuanced socioeconomic realities beyond headline figures. Analyzing such granular datasets allows for better policy formulation and academic research, emphasizing the need for transparent, accessible government information to address informal labor issues effectively.
