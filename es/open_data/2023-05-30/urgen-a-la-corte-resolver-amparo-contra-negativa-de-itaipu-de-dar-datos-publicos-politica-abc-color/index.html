Urgen a la Corte resolver amparo contra negativa de Itaipú de dar datos públicos - Política - ABC Color

Itaipú Paraguay attempts to withhold payment details for external lawyers by challenging public information laws, despite having a well-funded internal legal department. The Vice Prosecutor General recommends rejecting this lawsuit, reinforcing that administrative transparency is mandatory. This legal battle highlights critical tensions between state-owned entities and citizen rights to access fiscal data. Open data advocates view this case as a vital precedent for enforcing transparency in binational entities. It underscores the risk of arbitrary decision-making when public funds and contracts are concealed behind institutional secrecy. The push for court resolution ensures that legislative inquiries and citizen requests are respected rather than ignored. Ultimately, the case emphasizes the necessity of robust legal frameworks to prevent opacity in public administration. It validates the role of oversight bodies in maintaining accountability, proving that public interest must prevail over institutional convenience. This outcome strengthens the broader open data movement by affirming that access to information is a fundamental right, not a negotiable privilege.
