Madrid celebrará DataMad 2026, competición universitaria de datos abiertos

DataMad 2026 promotes the public use of open government data by challenging students and professionals to solve real societal problems. By requiring participants to utilize the Regional Administration’s open data catalog, the initiative highlights the practical value of transparent information access for driving social, economic, and environmental improvements. The event emphasizes the importance of code-open systems in creating solutions accessible to all citizens. This focus on transparency ensures that developed applications and services can be widely utilized, reinforcing the principle that open data should empower the community rather than restrict access to public resources. This contest is relevant to open data as it actively demonstrates how public information can be leveraged for innovation. It fosters a culture where developers and analysts engage directly with government datasets, proving that open data is a vital tool for creating impactful, sustainable, and inclusive technological solutions.
