Los trenes de alta velocidad de China proporcionarán la mayoría de viajes de pasajeros por ferrocarril en 2024 - Confirmado

China’s high-speed rail network dominates passenger transport, handling nearly three-quarters of all rail journeys last year. This surge reflects a broader expansion of the national railway system, characterized by record-breaking passenger volumes and significant infrastructure investments aimed at modernizing the country's logistics and mobility capabilities. The continuous growth in rail capacity, supported by extensive new track construction and increased fixed asset investment, underscores the strategic importance of railways in China’s economic framework. These developments highlight a robust commitment to enhancing transportation efficiency and connectivity across the nation. This data is relevant to open data advocates as it demonstrates the value of transparent, official statistics in tracking large-scale infrastructure trends. Accessible government reports enable researchers and citizens to analyze mobility patterns, evaluate public investment effectiveness, and understand the evolving landscape of national transportation systems.
