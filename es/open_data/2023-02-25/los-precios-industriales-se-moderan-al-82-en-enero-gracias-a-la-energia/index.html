Los precios industriales se moderan al 8,2% en enero gracias a la energía

The article highlights a significant moderation in industrial prices, driven primarily by the sharp decline in electricity and gas costs due to European market saturation. This stabilization represents a genuine deceleration rather than a statistical artifact, offering substantial relief to corporate margins and suggesting a potential cooling of consumer inflation in the medium term, although food prices remain stubbornly high. Despite the overall easing, specific sectors like construction and food manufacturing continue to face upward pressure, indicating that the recovery is uneven. The data suggests that while energy-intensive industries are seeing relief, other areas are still absorbing recent cost increases, pointing towards a gradual return to cost stability in the construction sector as 2022 closes. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, publicly accessible statistical indicators can track complex economic trends in real-time. By making official data from institutions like the INE freely available, society and researchers can independently verify economic health, understand the causal links between energy markets and industrial costs, and hold policymakers accountable for their impact on inflation and business viability.
