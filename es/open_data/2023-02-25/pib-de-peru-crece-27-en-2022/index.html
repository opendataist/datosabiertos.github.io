PIB de Perú crece 2,7% en 2022

Peru’s economy demonstrated resilience in 2022 with moderate growth, primarily driven by robust internal demand and strong family consumption. This positive trajectory persisted into the final quarter, despite a challenging global economic environment and sluggish performance from the mining sector. The recovery was further supported by increased public investment and rising employment levels, highlighting the crucial role of domestic stability in sustaining economic momentum. This narrative holds significant relevance for open data advocates, as it underscores the necessity of accessible, timely, and granular economic statistics. The INEI’s detailed breakdown of sectors and demographic indicators illustrates how transparent data empowers stakeholders to understand complex economic dynamics. Reliable open data infrastructure is essential for validating these insights, enabling researchers and policymakers to move beyond aggregate figures and examine specific drivers like private versus public investment or sector-specific performance. Ultimately, the report highlights that data transparency fosters better decision-making and accountability. By making such comprehensive reports widely available, governments can enhance public trust and facilitate informed debates on policy effectiveness. This case serves as a compelling argument for maintaining high standards in public data publication, ensuring that economic performance is not just measured, but clearly communicated to benefit society at large.
