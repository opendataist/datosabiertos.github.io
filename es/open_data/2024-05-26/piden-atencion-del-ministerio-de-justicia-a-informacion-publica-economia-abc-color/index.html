Piden atención del Ministerio de Justicia a información pública - Economía - ABC Color

Recent administrative reforms in Paraguay’s Public Information Access system have inadvertently restricted transparency by limiting requests to those with electronic identity, thereby excluding foreigners. While organizations are collaborating to optimize the portal, a significant drop in requests highlights how digital barriers undermine equitable access to information. Furthermore, allowing requesters to claim confidentiality risks obscuring the identities of those seeking data, potentially hindering accountability. The Ministry of Justice must actively enforce legal mandates to guarantee this right, ensuring that procedural changes do not erode the fundamental ability of citizens and entities to obtain public records. This issue is crucial for open data because transparency combats corruption and builds the trust necessary for economic investment. Without accessible, unrestricted information flows, the potential for open government initiatives diminishes, directly impacting institutional credibility and the integrity of public data ecosystems.
