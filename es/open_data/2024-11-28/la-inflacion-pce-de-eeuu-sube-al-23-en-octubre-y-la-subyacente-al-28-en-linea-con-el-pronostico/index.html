La inflación PCE de EEUU sube al 2,3% en octubre y la subyacente, al 2,8%, en línea con el pronóstico

The US personal consumption expenditure index rose slightly in October, with core inflation hitting 2.8%. This indicates that service prices are driving the increase, while goods and energy costs declined. The Federal Reserve’s preferred inflation gauge suggests that the central bank should maintain a cautious approach to interest rate adjustments. Market analysts view the data as moderately warm but within expected ranges. Consequently, traders anticipate stability leading up to the Thanksgiving holiday. The underlying trend supports a gradual, rather than aggressive, easing of monetary policy to manage inflation effectively. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how real-time, publicly available economic indicators directly influence global financial markets and policy decisions. Transparency in government-published metrics allows analysts and citizens to track inflation trends accurately. Understanding these open datasets is crucial for interpreting the broader economic narrative and predicting future monetary actions.
