Los creadores del exitoso Dave the Diver cambian de registro con Nakwon, su nuevo juego de zombis con prueba abierta el 29 de noviembre

MINTROCKET, backed by Nexon, is shifting from its hit Dave the Diver to Nakwon Last Paradise, a Korean-style zombie survival game. This new title emphasizes extraction mechanics, PvPvE dynamics, and strategic combat within a lawless Seúl setting, exploring themes of human greed in a post-apocalyptic regime. The developers view community collaboration as central to the design process. By inviting players to shape the project during early development stages, they prioritize feedback to refine the multiplayer experience and ensure the final product resonates with its target audience. This approach is highly relevant to open data principles, as it mirrors the iterative transparency and user-driven development often seen in open-source projects. Engaging users early fosters accountability and allows for dynamic improvements based on real-world usage data, demonstrating how public involvement enhances software quality and relevance.
