Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Argentina releases the definitive results of its 2022 National Census, incorporating all validated variables for population, households, and housing. This publication marks the culmination of a historic hybrid survey model that successfully combined traditional face-to-face methods with innovative digital responses. The widespread adoption of online participation significantly engaged citizens, establishing a new precedent for future national data collection efforts. The final data reveals a growing population and detailed housing conditions, such as high access to water and internet connectivity. By providing comprehensive and verified statistics, the government ensures that policymakers and researchers have accurate, granular information to understand demographic shifts and infrastructure needs across the country. This completeness eliminates previous uncertainties associated with provisional figures, offering a reliable baseline for national planning. This release is vital for open data as it democratizes access to critical social statistics. Making this extensive dataset freely available allows for independent analysis, fosters transparency, and supports evidence-based decision-making in public policy. It exemplifies how modernizing statistical methods and embracing digital participation can enhance both the quality of data and its utility for society, reinforcing the importance of accessible, validated public information.
