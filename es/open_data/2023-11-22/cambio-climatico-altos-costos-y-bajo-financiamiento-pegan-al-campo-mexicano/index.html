Cambio climático, altos costos y bajo financiamiento pegan al campo mexicano

Mexico’s agricultural sector achieved significant grain production in 2022, yet faces severe challenges including high input costs, climate-related losses, and soil degradation. Climate change poses a growing economic threat, potentially reducing regional GDP significantly by 2050 due to intensified weather shocks and risks. Access to essential financial instruments like credit and insurance remains critically low, limiting producers' resilience against these escalating operational and environmental pressures. This data is relevant to open data because transparent, accessible statistics are crucial for informing policy and driving solutions for food security and climate resilience.
