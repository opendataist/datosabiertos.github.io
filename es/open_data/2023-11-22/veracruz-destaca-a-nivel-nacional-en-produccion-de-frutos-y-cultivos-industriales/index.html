Veracruz destaca a nivel nacional en producción de frutos y cultivos industriales

The definitive results of the 2022 Agricultural Census reveal that Mexico’s agricultural sector is robust, with significant production of fruits, vegetables, grains, and industrial crops. The data highlights distinct regional specializations, where states in the Pacific, north, and southeast dominate specific sectors. This geographic concentration underscores the diverse agro-climatic capabilities across the country and identifies key regions driving national food security and export capacity. Veracruz emerges as a critical leader, particularly in industrial crops and fruits, accounting for a substantial share of national sugar cane and fruit output. The census data clarifies the magnitude of land use, labor demographics, and production volumes, emphasizing the sector's reliance on male-dominated labor while showing regional variations in workforce participation. These figures provide a granular view of how natural resources and human capital are distributed across the territory. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of transparent, standardized statistical reporting in shaping policy and economic understanding. By making detailed census results publicly accessible, institutions like INEGI enable researchers, businesses, and policymakers to analyze trends, verify supply chains, and allocate resources effectively. Open access to this rigorous demographic and production data fosters accountability and drives informed decision-making in the agribusiness sector.
