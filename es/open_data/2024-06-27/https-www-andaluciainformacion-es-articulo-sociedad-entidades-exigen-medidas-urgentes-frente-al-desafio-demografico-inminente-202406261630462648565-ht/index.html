The National Statistics Institute projects a significant aging demographic by 2055, prompting urgent calls for strategic solutions. Experts emphasize redefining pensions as essential investments and establishing state care pacts to manage this imminent challenge effectively. Crucially, combating unwanted loneliness has emerged as a primary societal priority for public administrations. This shift highlights the need for cooperative frameworks that address the holistic well-being of older adults beyond mere medical care. This is highly relevant to open data because tracking demographic shifts, care efficiency, and social isolation requires robust, transparent datasets. Accessible statistical information enables policymakers to design evidence-based strategies that respond accurately to the complex needs of an aging population.
