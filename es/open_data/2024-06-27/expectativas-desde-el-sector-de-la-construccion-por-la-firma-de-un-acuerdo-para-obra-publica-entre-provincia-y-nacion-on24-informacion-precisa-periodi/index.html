Expectativas desde el sector de la construcción por la firma de un acuerdo para obra pública entre Provincia y Nación | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

The construction sector is facing a severe crisis driven by soaring costs that significantly outpace inflation, particularly regarding labor and materials. Industry leaders warn that without immediate government intervention and the reactivation of public works, the industry risks further decline, as current activity levels remain insufficient to sustain businesses amid escalating expenses. The core challenge lies in the disparity between rising operational costs and stagnant project volume. While material prices have seen some stabilization following previous volatility, labor costs have surged sharply, creating a "explosive" economic environment. This pressure necessitates a strategic focus on stabilizing costs and securing funding to prevent the collapse of construction enterprises that are barely holding their structures together. This situation is crucial for open data because transparent, timely statistics on construction costs and public investment flows are essential for informed decision-making. Reliable data allows policymakers and stakeholders to accurately assess economic health, identify bottlenecks, and design effective interventions. Without accessible and precise information, the gap between government announcements and on-the-ground reality remains difficult to bridge, hindering the sector's recovery.
