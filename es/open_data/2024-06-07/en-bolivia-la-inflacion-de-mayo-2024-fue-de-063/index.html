En Bolivia la inflación de mayo 2024 fue de 0,63%

Bolivia’s consumer prices rose modestly in May 2024, driven by increases in food, housing, and transport costs across major cities. This data highlights regional inflation disparities, with Tarija and Trinidad experiencing the most significant hikes, while certain agricultural products saw price reductions. The findings underscore the volatility of essential goods, illustrating how localized economic pressures impact national indicators. Such granular data is crucial for understanding the nuanced dynamics of cost-of-living changes, revealing which demographic segments face the greatest financial strain due to specific commodity fluctuations. This report exemplifies the value of open data in public administration. By making detailed statistical releases accessible, the National Institute of Statistics enables researchers and policymakers to analyze economic trends transparently. Open access to such indices fosters accountability and supports evidence-based decision-making regarding social welfare and economic stability.
