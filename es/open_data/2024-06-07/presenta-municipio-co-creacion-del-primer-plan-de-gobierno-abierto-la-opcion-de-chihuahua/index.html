Presenta municipio co-creación del primer plan de gobierno abierto | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua has successfully co-created its first Open Government Plan following its 2022 declaration, prioritizing citizen-driven themes identified through extensive public consultation. This collaborative approach ensures that government initiatives directly address community needs, fostering transparency and active civic participation in municipal governance. The resulting plan focuses on three critical areas: violence prevention, economic ease for new businesses, and support for rural development. By involving specialized departments and multi-disciplinary citizen groups, the municipality translates public interest into actionable commitments, emphasizing inclusivity and targeted policy improvements across these diverse sectors. This initiative is highly relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how structured citizen engagement can shape data-centric public policies. It highlights the importance of accessible information and participatory mechanisms in creating responsive governance, serving as a model for how open data principles can empower communities and enhance municipal accountability through co-creation.
