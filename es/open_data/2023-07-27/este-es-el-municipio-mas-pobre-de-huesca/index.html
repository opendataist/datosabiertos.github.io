Este es el municipio más pobre de Huesca

The article highlights significant economic disparities within Aragón, using the INE’s 2020 income atlas to reveal stark contrasts between wealthy Pyrenean zones and impoverished rural areas. It identifies specific municipalities like Chalamera as extreme examples of low income, driven primarily by agricultural reliance and geographic isolation from major tourist centers. This data visualization exposes how wealth distribution is unevenly concentrated, leaving many small towns with minimal financial resources compared to urban or high-tourism counterparts. The narrative underscores the methodological differences between governmental reports, noting that official regional studies often exclude smaller settlements, thereby obscuring the plight of smaller communities. By contrast, the INE’s comprehensive map includes nearly all populated areas, offering a granular view of socioeconomic conditions that broader statistical summaries might miss. This distinction is crucial for understanding the true depth of regional inequality, as it ensures that very small populations are not statistically invisible. This resource is vital for open data advocates because it demonstrates how granular, accessible datasets can reveal hidden social realities that aggregate statistics often mask. By making detailed income information available to the public, the INE enables greater transparency and facilitates informed discussions about economic justice. Open data initiatives like this empower citizens and researchers to identify neglected areas, holding policymakers accountable and ensuring that development strategies address the needs of the most vulnerable populations rather than just the majority.
