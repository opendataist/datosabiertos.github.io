Francia registra inflación interanual del 2,3 por ciento en mayo - Noticias Prensa Latina

French inflation continues to erode purchasing power, driven by rising energy and food costs. This economic strain has fueled political instability, leading to significant electoral setbacks for the ruling party and subsequent legislative elections. The data highlights the critical link between macroeconomic indicators and public sentiment, demonstrating how price fluctuations directly impact social cohesion and governance stability.
