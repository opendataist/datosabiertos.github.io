Viajes de pasajeros por ferrocarril en China alcanzan nuevo récord en período enero-septiembre

China’s railway system achieved record passenger volumes in 2024, driven by robust tourism demand and stable, safe operations. This surge highlights the critical role of accurate, timely transportation data in monitoring economic activity and infrastructure efficiency. The significant growth in both domestic and cross-border services underscores the importance of transparent data sharing for regional connectivity. Such information facilitates better planning and integration across international networks, fostering stronger economic ties. Understanding these mobility trends through open data is essential for policymakers and researchers. It enables evidence-based decisions that improve service reliability and support sustainable growth in the transport sector.
