INE distribuirá más de 100.000 cajas censales para el empadronamiento

The National Statistics Institute distributes secure, waterproof census boxes nationwide to ensure accurate data collection during the upcoming population and housing census. This logistical preparation highlights the critical infrastructure required for robust national data gathering. Significant volunteer recruitment efforts aim to reach the massive workforce needed for field operations. Engaging diverse sectors, from students to teachers, demonstrates the broad social participation essential for comprehensive demographic coverage. This initiative is relevant to open data as it establishes the foundational public dataset on population and housing. Transparent, high-quality census results enable evidence-based policy-making and provide essential inputs for civic oversight and future statistical transparency.
