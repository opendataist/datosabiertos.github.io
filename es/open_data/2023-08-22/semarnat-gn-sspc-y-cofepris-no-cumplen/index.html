Semarnat, GN, SSPC y Cofepris no cumplen

Mexico’s INAI reveals that only a tiny fraction of federal institutions fully comply with personal data protection laws. This stark statistic highlights a systemic failure across the vast majority of public bodies, leaving citizens’ information vulnerable. The report identifies specific agencies with perfect scores and others with zero compliance, demonstrating a severe inconsistency in how data is managed. This disparity underscores the urgent need for stronger oversight mechanisms to ensure responsible data handling across all government levels. This case is vital for open data advocates, as it exposes the gap between legal mandates and actual practice. Without strict enforcement and transparency, open government initiatives remain compromised by inadequate privacy safeguards.
