Rescatan a hombre que cayó en un registro abierto en Guaymas

A resident of Guaymas fell into an uncovered utility hole, highlighting severe public safety issues caused by poor infrastructure management. Residents are forced to improvise dangerous signage for open trenches, underscoring a lack of official responsibility. This incident demonstrates how unreported or ignored civic issues create hazardous conditions. The reliance on citizens to manage these dangers reveals critical gaps in municipal oversight and emergency response protocols. This case is vital for open data as it illustrates the need for transparent, real-time datasets on infrastructure maintenance. Accessible information would empower citizens to monitor government accountability and ensure public safety improvements through verified, actionable records.
