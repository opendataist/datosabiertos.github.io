Empleo informal en Chiapas es de 75.4 %

Chiapas exhibits exceptionally high labor informalization, ranking among the states with the worst employment conditions. A significant majority of the workforce operates without formal protections, reflecting deep structural vulnerabilities. This lack of formalization severely limits social security and economic stability for residents, highlighting a critical governance challenge. The data reveals that informal economic activities are expanding, indicating a systemic shift away from regulated labor markets. This trend suggests that traditional employment metrics may underestimate the true scale of economic activity, necessitating more nuanced analytical frameworks to capture the reality of the workforce. This report is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the need for transparent, granular labor statistics. Accurate, accessible data empowers policymakers and citizens to advocate for better labor rights and economic policies, ensuring that informal workers are not excluded from social development discussions.
