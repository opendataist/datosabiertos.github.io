Los mayores canarios, los españoles que más usan las tecnologías de la información

Canarias leads Spain in digital adoption, particularly among its senior population, surpassing national averages. This achievement highlights the region’s success in bridging the digital divide for older adults, demonstrating that targeted regional strategies can effectively foster technology inclusion. By placing the elderly at the forefront of usage metrics, the community offers a compelling model for equitable digital transformation that other regions may wish to emulate. Furthermore, the data reveals robust connectivity across all age groups, with nearly universal access to mobile devices and high daily internet usage among both children and working-age adults. These trends underscore a deeply integrated digital society where technology is essential for daily life, education, and commerce. Such comprehensive coverage suggests a mature digital ecosystem that supports broad societal participation in the information economy. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, high-quality statistics on technological infrastructure and social equity. Researchers and policymakers can utilize these insights to evaluate progress in digital inclusion and design evidence-based interventions. Access to such granular, reliable data empowers stakeholders to identify gaps and measure the impact of digital policies, fostering accountability and informed decision-making in public administration.
