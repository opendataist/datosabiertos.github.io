Elecciones municipales 2024: ¿Cómo me registro y aplico para el voto accesible?

The Dominican Republic’s electronic ballot templates enable voters with visual impairments to exercise their right to vote independently. This initiative ensures full electoral participation by removing physical barriers through assistive technology. Such inclusive digital tools promote transparency and accessibility in public administration. They align with open data principles by making voting processes more equitable and accessible for all citizens.
