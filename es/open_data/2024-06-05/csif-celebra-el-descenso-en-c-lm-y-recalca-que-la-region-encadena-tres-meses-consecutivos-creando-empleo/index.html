CSIF celebra el descenso en C-LM y recalca que la región encadena tres meses consecutivos creando empleo

Castilla-La Mancha experiences a third consecutive month of job growth, primarily driven by the services sector. This creates a positive economic narrative, highlighting a general reduction in unemployment across the region’s provinces. However, gender disparity persists as women remain the majority of the unemployed. The share of female unemployment continues to rise, indicating that labor market improvements are not yet equitable, necessitating targeted interventions to address this imbalance. This article is relevant to open_data as it underscores the importance of accessible, granular statistics. To effectively monitor regional development and identify systemic inequalities, detailed and transparent data on employment distribution is essential for public accountability and policy design.
