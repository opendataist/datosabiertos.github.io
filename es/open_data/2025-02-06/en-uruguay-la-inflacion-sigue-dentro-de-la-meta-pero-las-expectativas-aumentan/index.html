En Uruguay la Inflación sigue dentro de la meta, pero las expectativas aumentan

Uruguay’s January inflation slowed to 5.05%, remaining within the central bank’s target range for twenty consecutive months. This stabilization suggests effective monetary policy control and predictable price dynamics. The release highlights the critical role of official statistics in monitoring economic stability and guiding public policy decisions.
