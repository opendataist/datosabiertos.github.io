Censo: El INE realiza un trabajo táctico y técnico en 6.612 centros de operaciones

The 2024 Census marks a significant technological leap for national statistics, primarily through the completion of a comprehensive digital cartographic update. This unprecedented digitization ensures every household is georeferenced nationwide, correcting historical gaps in coverage. This digital foundation establishes a high-quality sampling framework that will enhance the precision and scope of all future statistical operations, rather than just capturing a single moment in time. Furthermore, the initiative demonstrates a massive expansion in civic engagement and logistical readiness. The recruitment of hundreds of thousands of volunteer census takers represents a substantial increase in community participation compared to previous cycles. This surge in human resources, supported by extensive material distribution, underscores a renewed social commitment to accurate data collection and validates the operational scale required for such a complex national endeavor. This process is vital to open data as it transforms raw demographic counts into a publicly accessible, spatially accurate digital infrastructure. By releasing standardized, geo-referenced data, the census empowers researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze societal trends with greater transparency. It moves beyond static reports, providing reusable datasets that foster accountability, improve public service delivery, and encourage innovative applications based on trustworthy, open information.
