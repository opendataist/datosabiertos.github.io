Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The INE dismisses allegations of census data tampering, attributing visible corrections to handwriting issues and involuntary errors. Officials urge volunteers to rectify information directly during interviews to ensure accurate digital processing and maintain public trust. This transparency is vital for open data, as it safeguards the integrity and reliability of the official population statistics released for public use.
