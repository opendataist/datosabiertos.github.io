This initiative encourages young people to use creative media to promote transparency and accountability, fostering a culture of openness. By inviting youth to produce short videos, the government highlights the power of public engagement in driving ethical governance and social responsibility. The competition emphasizes accessibility and broad participation, allowing various forms of digital expression. This approach underscores the importance of integrating new technologies in civic education, making transparency concepts more relatable and actionable for younger generations across the state. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent governance can be communicated effectively to the public. It illustrates that open government principles thrive when citizens actively participate in creating and sharing accessible, understandable information through modern digital platforms.
