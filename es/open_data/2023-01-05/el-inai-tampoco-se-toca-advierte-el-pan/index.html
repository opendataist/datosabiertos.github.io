“El INAI tampoco se toca”, advierte el PAN

The National Action Party defends the INAI’s autonomy against presidential attacks, arguing that independent oversight bodies are essential for controlling government excesses and protecting citizens' rights. This defense highlights the critical role of transparency institutions in ensuring democratic accountability and preventing authoritarianism by maintaining strict limits on executive power. Such institutional independence is directly relevant to open data because it guarantees the structural integrity required for transparent public information systems. Without protected autonomy, access to data could be restricted or manipulated, undermining the fundamental principles of openness and civic trust that enable society to monitor public spending and hold officials accountable.
