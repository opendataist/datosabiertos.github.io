Editorial – MORENA en Soledad y el uso indebido de datos personales | Revista Punto de Vista

This article exposes the illicit use of protected personal data in Mexican electoral campaigns, highlighting a specific case where citizens' addresses appeared on energy bills alongside political propaganda. The incident, involving prominent political figures, demonstrates a severe breach of privacy laws and ethical standards, raising urgent concerns about data security in public administration. The misuse of sensitive information undermines democratic integrity and erodes public trust in electoral processes. It suggests that political actors are prioritizing campaign gains over citizens' fundamental rights, creating a dangerous precedent where confidential state records are weaponized for partisan ends. This erosion of trust threatens the legitimacy of future elections and the broader social contract. This case is critical to open data discussions because it illustrates the risks of poor data governance and the lack of transparency in how government-held information is accessed and utilized. It underscores the necessity for robust legal frameworks and strict accountability mechanisms to prevent the exploitation of public data. Ensuring that open data initiatives do not facilitate privacy violations is essential for maintaining democratic values and citizen confidence in digital governance.
