Gobierno abierto para la democracia en Guatemala (II)

Guatemala’s recently relaunched Open and Electronic Government Commission aims to strengthen democracy by fostering transparency, accountability, and active citizen participation in decision-making processes. This initiative, titled "Open Government from the People for the People," emphasizes inclusivity across the country’s four recognized ethnic groups, acknowledging their collective role in defending constitutional order and democratic values. The relaunch signifies a commitment to listening to diverse societal needs and integrating marginalized communities into governance. By prioritizing collaboration and innovation in public management, the government seeks to bridge the gap between state institutions and citizens, ensuring that policy-making reflects the realities and demands of a multicultural society. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the institutional framework necessary for meaningful civic engagement. Effective open data initiatives rely on this foundational trust and inclusive infrastructure, ensuring that information is not only accessible but also utilized by all population segments to influence public policy and foster a truly participatory democracy.
