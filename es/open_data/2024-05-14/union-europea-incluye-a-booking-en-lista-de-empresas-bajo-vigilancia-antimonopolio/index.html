Unión Europea incluye a Booking en lista de empresas bajo vigilancia antimonopolio

The European Union has classified Booking as a "gatekeeper" under the Digital Markets Act, subjecting it to strict oversight alongside other tech giants like Google and Amazon. This designation aims to ensure fair competition by requiring these firms to prove user data protection, assess risks, and increase advertising transparency. Booking now has six months to align its operations with these rigorous new standards. This move highlights the EU’s commitment to curbing the market power of dominant digital platforms. By imposing heavy surveillance, regulators seek to prevent anti-competitive behaviors and protect consumers from potential price hikes. The recent blockage of Booking’s acquisition of eTraveli underscores the bloc's determination to maintain a balanced digital marketplace where smaller entities can compete fairly. This development is crucial for open data, as it forces major platforms to expose their data handling practices and algorithmic transparency. Enhanced accountability measures may lead to greater availability of information regarding how user data is used for advertising and platform governance. Consequently, this regulatory pressure promotes a more transparent ecosystem, potentially opening pathways for better data interoperability and consumer trust in digital services.
