El PSOE lleva al juzgado la petición a David Moreno (Vox) para que rectifique

Socialist politicians in Spain have initiated legal proceedings against Vox leadership to address defamatory accusations regarding fraudulent voter registration. They assert that municipal staff do not influence these processes and that their administration has consistently cooperated with police investigations, contradicting claims of complicity or prior knowledge. This case highlights open_data relevance by demonstrating how public datasets, like registry records, are central to political accountability and transparency disputes. Ensuring accurate, accessible data helps verify official statements, protect reputations, and maintain trust in civic institutions during electoral or administrative controversies.
