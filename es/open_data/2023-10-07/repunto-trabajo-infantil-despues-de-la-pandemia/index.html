Repuntó trabajo infantil después de la pandemia

Child labor remains a persistent crisis in Mexico, highlighting a urgent need for better policy enforcement and social protection systems. This issue directly impacts open data initiatives, as transparent, accessible statistics are essential for accountability and tracking progress. Without robust data infrastructure, societies cannot effectively address the root causes of exploitation. Reliable datasets empower advocates and governments to identify high-risk regions and allocate resources strategically to eradicate harmful labor practices among minors. Ultimately, the availability of clear, standardized information drives meaningful social change. Open data serves as the backbone for monitoring compliance with international labor standards, ensuring that vulnerable populations receive the necessary support to leave the workforce and access education.
