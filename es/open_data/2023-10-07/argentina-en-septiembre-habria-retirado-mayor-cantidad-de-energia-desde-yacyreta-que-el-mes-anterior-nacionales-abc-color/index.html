Argentina en septiembre habría retirado mayor cantidad de energía desde Yacyretá que el mes anterior - Nacionales - ABC Color

Argentina increased its energy withdrawal from the Yacyretá dam despite official requests to reduce it, shifting the operational burden and costs onto Paraguay. This disproportionate extraction allows Argentina to maintain its supply while avoiding immediate consequences for unpaid debts. The financial implications are significant, with Paraguay absorbing substantial additional operational expenses for the month alone. This situation exacerbates an already massive accumulated debt, highlighting a severe imbalance in the bilateral agreement where Paraguay bears the economic weight. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent access to energy statistics can expose discrepancies between political rhetoric and actual resource flows. By making generation and withdrawal figures public, citizens and analysts can hold governments accountable for energy sovereignty and financial transparency.
