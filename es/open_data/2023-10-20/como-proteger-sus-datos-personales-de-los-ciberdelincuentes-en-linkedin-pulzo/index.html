Cómo proteger sus datos personales de los ciberdelincuentes en LinkedIn - Pulzo

The article highlights a growing cybersecurity threat where cybercriminals systematically harvest personal and professional data from LinkedIn. By using automated tools like web crawlers, attackers easily access valuable information without purchasing leaked lists. This poses significant risks as criminals build targeted employee lists and map corporate supply chains by analyzing professional interactions, turning a networking platform into a strategic resource for malicious activities. To mitigate these dangers, experts emphasize the critical need for users to actively manage their digital footprint. Recommendations include strictly adjusting privacy settings to limit visibility, exercising caution when accepting contact requests, and minimizing the personal details shared on public profiles. Regularly reviewing connections and being selective about real-time professional updates are also essential practices to prevent criminals from exploiting new status changes for phishing or malware distribution. This content is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the dual nature of publicly accessible information. While open data aims to increase transparency and connectivity, the same visibility can be weaponized by bad actors to compromise individual privacy and organizational security. The article serves as a crucial reminder that data openness must be balanced with robust privacy controls, demonstrating how unregulated public data aggregation can facilitate cybercrime rather than just foster innovation.
