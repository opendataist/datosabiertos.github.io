La inflación en Turquía se dispara al 59 %

Turkey faces accelerating inflation, driven by the lira’s depreciation, which severely impacts household living costs. Official figures may understate the crisis, as independent estimates suggest much higher price increases than reported by state authorities. The Central Bank has significantly raised interest rates to combat this trend, projecting a peak before stability returns in 2025. This ongoing economic volatility highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible economic data to foster public trust and informed decision-making. This situation is relevant to open_data because robust, open economic statistics are essential for verifying official claims, enabling independent analysis, and holding institutions accountable during financial instability.
