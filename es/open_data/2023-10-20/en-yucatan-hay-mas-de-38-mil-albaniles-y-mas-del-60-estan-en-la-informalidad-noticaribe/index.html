En Yucatán hay más de 38 mil albañiles y más del 60% están en la informalidad - Noticaribe

The article highlights the precarious conditions of informal construction workers in Yucatán, emphasizing the severe health risks and frequent fatalities associated with their unregulated labor. Legislator Vida Gómez Herrera demands accountability regarding the ‘Dignificar’ program, questioning whether state investments genuinely improve workers’ quality of life despite the booming real estate sector. Relevance to open data lies in the public’s right to transparency and measurable outcomes from government programs. The call for specific reports on training investments and support distribution underscores the necessity of accessible, detailed data to verify if public funds effectively target marginalized populations. Ultimately, the piece argues that economic growth must be accompanied by verifiable social benefits. It illustrates how open data serves as a critical tool for civic oversight, ensuring that administrative claims align with reality and that vulnerable workers receive the intended protection and support from local authorities.
