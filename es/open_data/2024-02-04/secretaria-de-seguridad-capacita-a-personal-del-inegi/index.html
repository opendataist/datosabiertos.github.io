Secretaría de Seguridad capacita a personal del INEGI

The Security Secretariat trained INEGI staff to ensure the safe execution of the 2024 Economic Census, establishing operational plans that prioritize officer support for surveyors in the field. This collaboration guarantees that data collection occurs in a secure and orderly environment, facilitating the gathering of accurate economic information from thousands of local enterprises. This partnership is vital for open data as it ensures the reliability and accessibility of high-quality statistical inputs, empowering citizens and researchers with trustworthy datasets for informed decision-making.
