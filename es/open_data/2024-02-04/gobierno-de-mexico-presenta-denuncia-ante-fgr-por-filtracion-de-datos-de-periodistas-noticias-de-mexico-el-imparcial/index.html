Gobierno de México presenta denuncia ante FGR por filtración de datos de periodistas | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

The Mexican government filed a formal complaint against those responsible for the illegal theft of journalists' personal data from the presidential daily briefing accreditation system. This cyberattack exposed sensitive information, including names, addresses, and official identifiers, compromising the safety and privacy of over two hundred media professionals attending the events. In response, authorities implemented immediate security measures and provided protective accompaniment to affected journalists through specific human rights mechanisms. These actions highlight the critical intersection between digital security and the physical safety of press members, emphasizing the state's commitment to defending freedom of expression against digital threats. This incident is highly relevant to open_data because it illustrates the severe risks associated with the mishandling of personal identifiable information. It underscores the necessity for robust data governance and ethical standards in public databases, demonstrating how failures in data protection can directly endanger individuals and undermine democratic freedoms.
