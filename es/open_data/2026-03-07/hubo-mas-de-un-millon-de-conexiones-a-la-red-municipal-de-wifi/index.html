Hubo más de un millón de conexiones a la red municipal de WIFI

General Pueyrredon consolidates a robust technological inclusion policy, evidenced by sustained user growth and stable network performance. This initiative demonstrates significant public trust and usage intensity, confirming the Wi-Fi infrastructure as a vital tool for fostering digital participation and social innovation across various city locations. The project utilizes data generated from connectivity usage to optimize infrastructure planning and maintenance through evidence-based management. By analyzing these metrics, the municipal government ensures the system adapts to citizen needs, thereby enhancing the quality of public services and supporting a more responsive and transparent local administration. This strategy is deeply relevant to open data as it exemplifies how transparent governance and citizen participation intersect. By operating under the Open Government Partnership framework, the municipality leverages open data practices to drive innovation, ensuring that technological access serves as a foundation for a more engaged and informed society.
