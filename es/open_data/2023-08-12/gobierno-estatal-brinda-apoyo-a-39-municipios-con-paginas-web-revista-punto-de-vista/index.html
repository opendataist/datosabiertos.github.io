Gobierno Estatal brinda apoyo a 39 municipios con páginas web | Revista Punto de Vista

The state government supports San Luis Potosí municipalities by providing free, secure hosting for websites and email storage. This initiative enables local administrations to fulfill transparency laws efficiently, particularly assisting smaller towns with geographical or logistical challenges. By centralizing this infrastructure, the state reduces municipal budget burdens while ensuring reliable digital platforms for public information. This collaborative effort significantly advances open data principles by guaranteeing consistent and accessible government information. The program’s success is evident, with the majority of municipalities now utilizing the state server. This standardization ensures that citizens can reliably access official data and communications, strengthening trust in local governance. This case highlights the critical role of state-level support in achieving open government goals. It demonstrates how shared infrastructure can overcome resource limitations, allowing all municipalities, regardless of size or location, to comply with transparency mandates. Such centralization promotes equity in digital access to public records, making government operations more inclusive and accountable for every citizen.
