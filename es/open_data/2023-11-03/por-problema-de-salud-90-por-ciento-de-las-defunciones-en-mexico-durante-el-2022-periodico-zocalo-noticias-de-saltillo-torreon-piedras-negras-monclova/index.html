Por problema de salud, 90 por ciento de las defunciones en México durante el 2022 | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INEGI’s definitive 2022 mortality data highlights a significant public health burden, with the vast majority of deaths stemming from chronic diseases rather than external causes. This statistical reality underscores the critical need for transparent, high-quality public data to monitor health trends and allocate resources effectively in Mexico. The data reveals stark disparities in medical investigations, noting that most disease-related deaths lack necropsies while external cause deaths are thoroughly documented. This inconsistency exposes gaps in health information systems, illustrating why open data initiatives must prioritize completeness and standardization to ensure accurate societal analysis. Ultimately, this dataset is vital for open data ecosystems as it provides the foundational evidence needed for policy making and academic research. By making such granular mortality information accessible, stakeholders can better address systemic health inequalities and improve institutional accountability through data-driven governance.
