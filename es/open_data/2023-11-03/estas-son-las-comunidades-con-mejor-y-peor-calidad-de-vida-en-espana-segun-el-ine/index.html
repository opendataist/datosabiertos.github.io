Estas son las comunidades con mejor y peor calidad de vida en España según el INE

The INE’s 2022 Multidimensional Quality of Life Report reveals a slight national decline in living standards, highlighting deteriorations in governance, social relations, and environmental factors. While regions like Navarra and La Rioja maintain high rankings, significant disparities persist across Spain, with southern regions reporting the lowest scores. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how multidimensional indicators transform complex societal metrics into accessible, standardized statistics. Such transparent, granular datasets enable researchers, policymakers, and citizens to monitor well-being trends objectively, fostering evidence-based decision-making and promoting greater accountability in public health and social welfare policies.
