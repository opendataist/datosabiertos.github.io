Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The United States has recorded its highest-ever national debt, surpassing thirty-four trillion dollars. This historic milestone underscores the accelerating accumulation of public liabilities, driven by expansive government spending, tax reductions, and diminished fiscal revenues. The rapid growth indicates a structural shift in the nation’s financial obligations, highlighting the unsustainable trajectory of current economic policies and their long-term impact on fiscal stability. This transparency in fiscal data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it ensures that citizens and analysts have access to authoritative, real-time government financial records. Accessible, high-quality datasets allow for independent verification and deeper understanding of macroeconomic trends, fostering accountability and informed public discourse regarding national economic health. The article’s relevance lies in demonstrating how open data platforms can democratize complex financial information. By making such significant economic metrics publicly available and easily interpretable, stakeholders can critically assess government policy effectiveness. This promotes a culture of transparency where data serves as a vital tool for civic engagement and policy evaluation, rather than remaining an opaque bureaucratic secret.
