Castila-La Mancha se sitúa como la región que más crece en viajeros en turismo rural y apunta a marcar un año de récord en el conjunto del alojamiento reglado

Castilla-La Mancha has established itself as the leading region in growth for travelers and the second-best performer in rural overnight stays for the first eleven months of 2023. The data confirms an exceptional year for non-hotel accommodation, with rural demand hitting historical highs. This surge significantly outpaces the general sector’s growth, highlighting a strong regional shift toward rural tourism experiences that are gaining substantial traction compared to previous years. The momentum suggests that 2023 will definitively set new annual records for both travelers and overnight stays in rural establishments even before December data is included. This trend extends beyond rural segments, as the broader non-hotel accommodation sector also achieved historic volumes. The consistency of this performance indicates a robust and sustained increase in regional tourism appeal, moving beyond temporary spikes to represent a structural improvement in visitor attraction and retention capabilities. This article is highly relevant to open data because it relies on official statistics from the National Institute of Statistics, demonstrating how transparent, public datasets enable precise monitoring of sectoral trends. Such accessible data allows researchers and policymakers to verify regional performance, compare autonomous communities objectively, and understand the evolving dynamics of Spain’s tourism economy. By making these metrics publicly available, open data fosters evidence-based decision-making and enhances accountability in tourism development and economic planning across different regions.
