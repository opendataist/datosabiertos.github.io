Confianza del consumidor en EE. UU. cae al nivel más bajo en 9 meses

U.S. consumer confidence plummeted to its lowest level since mid-2022, driven by worsening economic outlooks and depressed future expectations. Despite this pessimism, current household conditions surprisingly improved, highlighting a stark divergence between present reality and future anxiety.
