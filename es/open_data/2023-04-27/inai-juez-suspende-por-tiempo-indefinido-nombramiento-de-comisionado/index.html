INAI: Juez suspende por tiempo indefinido nombramiento de comisionado

The suspension of Ana Yadira Alarcón’s appointment to the INAI highlights the fragility of institutional independence in Mexico. By blocking the presidential objection without a Senate replacement, the ruling temporarily leaves the institute understaffed, demonstrating how executive overreach can paralyze bodies designed to safeguard democratic rights. This legal standoff underscores the tension between constitutional mandates and political discretion. The court clarified that the president’s power is not arbitrary but strictly regulated, emphasizing that legal processes must protect elected officials from unfounded political interference. This ensures the integrity of appointments remains grounded in law rather than personal preference. This case is vital to open data because a weakened INAI directly threatens transparency and access to information. Protecting the autonomy of such institutions is essential for maintaining public trust in government data. Without fully operational commissions, the enforcement of data protection laws and citizen rights to access information becomes significantly compromised.
