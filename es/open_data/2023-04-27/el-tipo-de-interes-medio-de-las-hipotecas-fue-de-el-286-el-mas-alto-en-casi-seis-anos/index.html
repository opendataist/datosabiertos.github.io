El tipo de interés medio de las hipotecas fue de el 2,86%, el más alto en casi seis años

Spanish mortgage rates hit a six-year high in early 2023, reflecting broader monetary tightening. Consequently, the volume of home loans declined slightly, signaling a cooling housing market amid rising borrowing costs. This shift is vital for open data initiatives because it highlights how macroeconomic indicators directly impact housing accessibility. Transparently tracking these interest rate fluctuations allows for better analysis of financial stress on households and market volatility. Understanding these trends helps policymakers and researchers detect early signs of economic contraction. Openly accessible datasets enable the verification of these patterns, ensuring that responses to inflation and interest rate hikes are grounded in accurate, real-time evidence.
