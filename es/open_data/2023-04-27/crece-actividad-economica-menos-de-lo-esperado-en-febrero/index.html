Crece actividad económica menos de lo esperado en febrero

Mexico’s economic activity slowed in February, driven primarily by a stagnation in the services sector despite strong industrial performance. This deceleration highlights the fragility in domestic demand and service-oriented consumption, contrasting with resilience in manufacturing and mining. The overall growth remained positive but below initial expectations, signaling a moderation in momentum after a strong start to the year. The decline in services, including hospitality and government activities, underscores the uneven recovery across different economic pillars. While external demand and nearshoring interests continue to support fundamental strength, price pressures and global slowdowns create headwinds. Consequently, the economy maintains a modest but positive trajectory, consistent with forecasts for slight annual expansion, reflecting a complex balance between internal weaknesses and external opportunities. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it relies on official statistical indicators, like the INEGI IGAE, to monitor economic health. Transparent access to such granular sectoral data allows analysts and policymakers to validate official narratives, identify emerging trends early, and foster accountability in economic reporting. Open data facilitates deeper understanding of how specific sectors, such as the lagging services industry, impact national stability and guides evidence-based decision-making for future development strategies.
