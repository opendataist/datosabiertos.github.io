La OMS exige a China transparencia sobre el origen del COVID-19 a cinco años de su aparición

The World Health Organization emphasizes the urgent need for Chinese transparency regarding coronavirus data, arguing that shared information is a scientific and moral imperative. This lack of openness hinders global preparation for future health crises, highlighting how opaque data sharing delays effective pandemic response and prevention strategies worldwide. Recent international studies point toward wildlife transmission at Wuhan markets, casting doubt on laboratory origin theories. Although initial joint investigations deemed animal spillover probable, the absence of definitive transmission routes remains a critical gap. Clarifying these origins through open scientific cooperation is essential to understanding viral spread mechanisms and preventing similar outbreaks. This situation is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates the tangible consequences of restricted information flow during global emergencies. Without accessible, high-quality datasets on pathogen origins and transmission, the international community cannot build resilient health systems, underscoring that open data is fundamental for effective public health governance and crisis management.
