Nuevo récord de temperatura media diaria en el Planeta - Noticias Prensa Latina

The article highlights unprecedented global temperature records, emphasizing a shift beyond typical El Niño influences to sustained extreme heat. This trend, marked by consecutive monthly record-breaking temperatures, indicates that we are entering uncharted climatic territory where new highs are likely. These findings underscore the urgent acceleration of global warming, with the most recent decade exhibiting the highest average daily temperatures. The analysis points to specific regional factors, such as reduced sea ice in Antarctica, as contributing drivers to this extraordinary warmth, signaling a significant departure from historical climate patterns. This data is crucial for open_data because it demonstrates the value of transparent, high-quality climate datasets like ERA5 in monitoring planetary health. By making such rigorous, verifiable climate records accessible, we enable scientists and policymakers to understand, validate, and respond to the escalating climate crisis with greater precision and urgency.
