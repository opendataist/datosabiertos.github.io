El déficit comercial de México se reduce un 15,5 % en la primera mitad de 2024 - Mundo - ABC Color

Mexico’s trade balance improved significantly compared to 2023, driven by robust non-petroleum export and import growth. Despite a recent deficit in June caused by declining oil sales, the broader trend shows resilience in non-energy sectors. This economic performance highlights Mexico’s strategic integration within the USMCA framework, which supports sustained growth. The country has successfully reduced its commercial deficit, demonstrating enhanced competitiveness beyond traditional hydrocarbon reliance. This case is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical information. Reliable economic metrics enable stakeholders to analyze trade dynamics, assess policy impacts, and make informed decisions regarding market stability and international agreements.
