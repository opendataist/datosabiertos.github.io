La inflación PCE de EEUU cae al 2,5% y la subyacente se mantiene en el 2,6%

The US PCE inflation rate remained sticky in June, exceeding market expectations for moderation. This persistence suggests that price pressures are proving more resilient than anticipated, complicating the narrative of a smooth return to central bank targets. Despite the higher-than-expected figures, analysts believe this moderate increase still supports the view that inflation is gradually trending toward the 2% goal. Consequently, the Federal Reserve is likely to maintain its confidence in eventually cutting interest rates, provided no unexpected inflationary shocks occur in the coming months.
