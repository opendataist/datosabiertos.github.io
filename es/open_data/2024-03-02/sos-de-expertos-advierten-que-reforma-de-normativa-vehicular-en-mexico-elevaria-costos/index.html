SOS de expertos, advierten que reforma de normativa vehicular en México elevaría costos

Experts warn that Mexico’s vehicle norm reform introducing RFID chips will drastically increase costs for users while compromising data privacy. This shift violates data protection principles by enabling potential unauthorized surveillance and data extraction from sensitive personal information. The relevance to open data lies in the critical balance between technological innovation and fundamental rights. This case illustrates how mandatory hardware integration can undermine transparency and privacy, serving as a cautionary example for open government initiatives that must prioritize citizen data security over administrative convenience.
