Crece valor de economía informal un 4.6 % en México

The article highlights that Mexico’s informal economy has expanded significantly, now accounting for more than half of all jobs. This substantial growth indicates widespread economic precarity, where a majority of workers lack social security and formal benefits despite rising remuneration in the sector. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for open data initiatives because precise metrics on informality expose gaps in social protection and labor rights. Open data allows researchers and policymakers to analyze regional disparities and track the true scale of unregistered economic activity, which traditional official statistics might underrepresent or obscure. Consequently, this data drives transparency efforts aimed at formalizing labor markets. By making such information accessible, open data supports evidence-based policies designed to integrate informal workers into the formal economy, ensuring better protections and fostering equitable economic development across different Mexican states.
