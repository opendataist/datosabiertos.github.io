España superará los 40 millones turistas en verano - Noticias Prensa Latina

Spain demonstrates strong tourism leadership, driven by rising visitor numbers and significantly higher spending. This economic growth supports job quality and sustainability, reinforcing the country's position as a premier global destination despite inflationary pressures. The government emphasizes a shift toward inclusive, sustainable tourism that benefits both workers and residents. This strategic focus aims to preserve destinations while improving livelihoods, ensuring that the industry's expansion translates into tangible social and environmental improvements for local communities. This case is vital for open data, as it highlights the necessity of transparent, standardized statistical tracking to guide policy. Reliable data enables the monitoring of digital transformation projects, ensuring that public investments in tourism innovation are measurable, accountable, and effectively aligned with broader economic and social goals.
