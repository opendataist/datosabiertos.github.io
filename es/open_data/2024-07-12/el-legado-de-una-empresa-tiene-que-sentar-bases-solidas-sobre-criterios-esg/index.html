El legado de una empresa tiene que sentar bases sólidas sobre criterios ESG

The article highlights that corporate governance leadership is increasingly vital for ESG execution, though family businesses lag in comprehensive implementation. This disparity underscores the urgent need for robust open data frameworks to transparently track and validate sustainability efforts across different corporate structures. Integrating diverse perspectives, particularly women’s leadership, is presented as a key strategy to harmonize business goals and expand strategic vision. This shift requires accessible, standardized data to measure the tangible impact of gender diversity on governance and social outcomes. Consequently, open data becomes essential for linking corporate social responsibility with actual business performance. By making ESG metrics transparent and comparable, organizations can better align their strategic plans with societal expectations, driving meaningful progress in both governance and social equity.
