El Rey recibió una asignación de 270.000 euros y la Reina de 168.000 en 2023

The report highlights that Spain’s Royal Household saw a slight budget increase in 2023 despite official freezes, driven by broader public sector wage adjustments and expanded international diplomatic activities. This financial shift underscores the tension between state-imposed austerity measures and the actual operational costs of maintaining a global royal presence. Significant allocations were directed toward personnel expenses, revealing higher-than-expected compensation for senior officials. The inclusion of family members without dedicated budget lines further complicates the transparency of public spending, suggesting a need for clearer fiscal categorization within the monarchy’s financial structure. Relevance to open data lies in the necessity for granular, accessible financial transparency. By auditing these figures, citizens can demand more detailed breakdowns of royal expenditures, ensuring that public funds are accounted for with precision. This promotes accountability and supports informed public discourse regarding state spending priorities and fiscal responsibility.
