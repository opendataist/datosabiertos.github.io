Realizarán conferencia sobre datos abiertos y transparencia

The Municipality of Chihuahua is reinforcing its commitment to open data through a collaborative portal built with civil society and academia. This initiative highlights how co-creation and active listening are essential for building trustworthy, high-quality public information systems. A new citizen-led advisory council has been established to strengthen governance and ensure the strategic impact of data. By fostering multiactor participation, the municipality aims to enhance the reliability and utility of information for researchers and decision-makers. This event aligns with Open Data Day to promote a culture of transparency and social use of information. It demonstrates how open data empowers citizens, drives innovation, and supports informed decision-making, ultimately contributing to a more open and participatory society.
