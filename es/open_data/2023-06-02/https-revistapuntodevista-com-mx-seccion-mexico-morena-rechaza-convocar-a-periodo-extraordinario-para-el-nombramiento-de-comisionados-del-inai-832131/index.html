Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Senate majority blocked an extraordinary session to appoint pending INAI commissioners, prioritizing political control over institutional continuity. This decision highlights a systemic resistance to transparency mechanisms that safeguard against corruption, directly undermining the principles of open data that ensure accountability and public access to information. Legislators accused the judiciary of overreach while opposition figures argued that the refusal to appoint independent commissioners aims to obscure government actions. By denying these appointments, the majority effectively weakens the regulatory framework necessary for enforcing data protection laws and maintaining an open, transparent administrative environment. This conflict is critical for open data advocacy, as an under-staffed or non-functional INAI compromises the integrity of public data ecosystems. The struggle reflects a broader tension between executive power and institutional independence, illustrating how political maneuvering can erode the foundational structures required for true information transparency and civic oversight in Mexico.
