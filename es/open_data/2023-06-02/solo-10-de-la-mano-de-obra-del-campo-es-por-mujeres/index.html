Solo 10 % de la mano de obra del campo es por mujeres

The preliminary results of the 2022 Agricultural Census reveal that Chiapas holds a significant share of Mexico’s agropecuary land, highlighting regional disparities in rural resource distribution. This comprehensive data source provides essential statistics on economic, technological, and environmental characteristics, serving as a critical foundation for understanding the sector's current state. Notably, female participation in agricultural labor has declined sharply compared to previous years, dropping from over sixteen percent to barely five percent. This trend suggests shifting social dynamics and potential challenges in sustaining labor forces within the rural economy, requiring further attention regarding gender roles and workforce engagement. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it promotes transparency in public statistics. By making such detailed census results accessible, policymakers and researchers can better analyze land use, labor trends, and agricultural viability, fostering informed decision-making and encouraging accountability in national resource management.
