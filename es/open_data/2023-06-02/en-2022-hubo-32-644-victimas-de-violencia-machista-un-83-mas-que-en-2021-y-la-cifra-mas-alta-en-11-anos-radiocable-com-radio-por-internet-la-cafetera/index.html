En 2022 hubo 32.644 víctimas de violencia machista, un 8,3% más que en 2021 y la cifra más alta en 11 años | Radiocable.com - Radio por Internet - La Cafetera

The article highlights a significant rise in gender-based violence victims in Spain during 2022, marking the highest annual figure since 2011. This alarming trend underscores the persistent and worsening nature of structural inequality and systemic violence against women, contrary to hopes of sustained decline following previous years. Conversely, general domestic violence cases saw a slight decrease, suggesting that gender-based aggression remains a distinct and escalating crisis requiring targeted interventions. The data reveals that legal responses and condemnations are increasing, yet the core issue of victimization among women continues to grow, indicating gaps in preventive measures and social support systems. This report is highly relevant to open_data because it exemplifies how transparent, publicly available statistical information is crucial for holding institutions accountable. By making such detailed metrics accessible, open data enables researchers, journalists, and policymakers to track trends, evaluate the effectiveness of public policies, and advocate for necessary legislative changes to protect vulnerable populations.
