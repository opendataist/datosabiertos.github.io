El 10.6% de la fuerza laboral del campo duranguense son mujeres

The INEGI census highlights significant gender disparities in Mexican agricultural labor, revealing that women comprise a larger share in Durango but a smaller portion nationally compared to previous decades. This data underscores the complex, evolving role of women in rural economies, suggesting local trends may diverge from national patterns. Open data initiatives are crucial for uncovering these nuanced social dynamics by providing disaggregated statistics that might otherwise remain obscured. Detailed, accessible records allow researchers to analyze structural inequalities and track subtle shifts in workforce composition over time with precision. Ultimately, this census exemplifies how high-quality, open government data empowers evidence-based policy making. By making such comprehensive information public, stakeholders can better understand economic realities and design targeted interventions to support equitable development in the agricultural sector.
