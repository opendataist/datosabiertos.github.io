Los pedidos de las fábricas alemanas aumentan más de lo esperado en diciembre y alejan el temor a la recesión

German factory orders declined sharply, revealing that late-year growth relied heavily on major contracts rather than broad demand. This disparity highlights how aggregated statistics can mask underlying economic weakness, particularly when excluding large-scale deals. National and Eurozone orders showed modest gains, contrasting with global declines, while intermediate goods surged and consumer goods fell. These divergent trends illustrate the complexity of industrial health beyond headline figures. This data matters for open data because it underscores the need for granular, disaggregated metrics. Analysts must examine specific sub-sectors to avoid misinterpreting economic health, ensuring transparency in how industrial performance is reported and understood.
