INAI sesionará con 4 integrantes

The Supreme Court’s decision to allow the INAI to operate with four commissioners is vital for maintaining institutional balance and the rule of law. This ruling ensures that the institute can continue its critical function of resolving pending cases, directly addressing the rights of individuals whose access to information or data protection has been violated. By restoring full operational capacity, the institution can finally deliver resolutions to citizens awaiting justice, reinforcing the practical efficacy of transparency mechanisms. The ability to deliberate and decide prevents a legal vacuum where rights remain unaddressed due to incomplete quorums. This development is highly relevant to open data because it safeguards the independent body responsible for enforcing transparency laws. A functional INAI is essential for upholding public trust in data governance, ensuring that government information remains accessible and personal data rights are protected without political or procedural obstruction.
