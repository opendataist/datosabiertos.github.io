Aerolíneas y hoteles se recuperan mejor de la pandemia que OTAS y el alquiler de coches | Noticias de turismo REPORTUR

The recovery of the travel sector post-pandemonium shows airlines and hotels surpassing Online Travel Agencies and car rentals in growth rates. This trend highlights a structural shift in market power, favoring direct suppliers over intermediaries. Such data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it reveals emerging economic patterns within the tourism industry. By analyzing these distinct recovery trajectories, researchers and policymakers can better understand consumer behavior and market dynamics, fostering more accurate and insightful data-driven decisions.
