Más del 65% de censistas voluntarios inscritos se capacitaron en el país

The National Statistics Institute reports that over 65% of registered volunteer census takers have completed their training across all departments. This significant progress reflects a robust mobilization effort where recruitment targets have not only been met but exceeded by a substantial margin. A notable demographic trend shows that the majority of these volunteers are young women, indicating strong community engagement and diversity in the workforce preparing for the census. Chuquisaca leads in training completion, while efforts continue to increase participation in areas with lower enrollment rates. This article highlights open data relevance by demonstrating the critical infrastructure required for accurate public statistics. The systematic preparation of volunteers and logistics ensures that the resulting census data will be reliable, transparent, and freely accessible for societal use, underpinning effective governance and informed decision-making.
