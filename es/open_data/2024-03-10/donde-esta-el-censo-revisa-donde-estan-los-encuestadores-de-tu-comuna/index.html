¿Dónde está el Censo? Revisa dónde están los encuestadores de tu comuna

The 2024 census employs a non-intrusive methodology, allowing data collection from outside homes or via virtual codes if residents are absent. This approach prioritizes privacy while ensuring comprehensive coverage through a structured follow-up system that guarantees no household is overlooked during the three-month fieldwork period. The resulting demographic data serves as a critical foundation for designing effective public policies and allocating resources. It enables governments to address housing deficits, health needs, and migration trends accurately, ensuring that societal planning is grounded in verified, up-to-date population realities rather than estimates. For the open data community, this census highlights the vital intersection between government statistical operations and societal transparency. The release of detailed geographic and demographic information empowers both the private sector for market analysis and citizens for informed civic engagement. Such large-scale datasets exemplify how standardized, accessible data drives evidence-based decision-making across public and private domains.
