Comienza el Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024 con múltiples medidas de seguridad | Puranoticia.cl

The 2024 Population and Housing Census represents Chile’s largest statistical operation, employing tens of thousands of interviewers to collect essential data over several months. To ensure public trust, the National Statistics Institute has implemented rigorous identification protocols, including advanced, counterfeit-resistant credentials and strict procedures preventing personnel from entering homes during interviews. These safety measures are supported by coordinated efforts with national security forces, emphasizing the importance of protecting both staff and citizens while gathering nationwide demographic information. A significant aspect of this operation involves safeguarding the massive amount of personal data collected through mobile capture devices. The institute has established robust cybersecurity frameworks to control access, trace data usage, and secure communications. These devices are exclusively configured for census tasks and feature encryption and remote locking capabilities to prevent data loss or misuse. This emphasis on digital security ensures that the integrity of the information remains intact throughout the collection and processing phases, addressing modern concerns regarding data privacy in large-scale surveys. This census is crucial for informing public policy and infrastructure planning for the next decade, guiding decisions on housing, healthcare, and education. Its relevance to open data lies in the balance between generating high-quality, trustworthy statistical datasets and implementing transparent security standards. By ensuring data accuracy and protecting individual privacy, the operation lays a foundation for reliable open government data initiatives, allowing researchers and policymakers to utilize censal information for evidence-based decision-making while maintaining public confidence in statistical authorities.
