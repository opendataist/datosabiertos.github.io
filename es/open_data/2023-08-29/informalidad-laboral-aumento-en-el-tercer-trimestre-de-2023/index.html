Informalidad laboral aumentó en el tercer trimestre de 2023

The article highlights a paradoxical labor market trend in Mexico where a record increase in informal employment coincides with a reduction in unemployment rates. This duality suggests that while job availability has grown, the quality of these positions remains precarious, forcing more workers into the informal sector without social security or stability. Regional disparities are stark, with southern states exhibiting significantly higher rates of informality compared to northern, industrialized states. This geographic imbalance indicates that economic development and labor formalization are not evenly distributed, potentially affecting investment decisions and regional policy effectiveness. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the necessity of granular, high-frequency statistical transparency to understand complex socioeconomic realities. Accessible, detailed datasets allow researchers and policymakers to identify structural inequalities and evaluate the true impact of employment policies beyond aggregate unemployment figures, fostering informed public debate and accountability.
