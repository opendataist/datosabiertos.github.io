Compendio del INE identifica participación femenina

The INE released a gender-focused statistical compendium to aid public policy implementation. This open data resource promotes gender equity by consolidating national information. It is vital for open data initiatives, enabling organizations and individuals to access structured statistics on women’s participation across various social and economic sectors for informed decision-making.
