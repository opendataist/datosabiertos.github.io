Desempleo en México se sitúa en 2,8% en segundo trimestre de 2023

Mexico’s unemployment rate dropped in the second quarter of 2023, signaling a tightening labor market with millions more people employed compared to the previous year. This decline highlights a significant recovery from pandemic-era lows, driven largely by growth in commerce, finance, and manufacturing sectors. Despite these gains, the data reveals persistent challenges regarding job quality. A substantial portion of the workforce remains informally employed, and millions are underemployed, indicating that many workers still seek additional hours. This structural weakness underscores the gap between headline employment figures and the actual economic security experienced by laborers. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how publicly accessible statistical indicators can drive informed policy decisions. By making granular details about sector distribution and informal employment available, transparent data allows stakeholders to identify vulnerabilities beyond simple unemployment rates, fostering accountability and targeted economic interventions.
