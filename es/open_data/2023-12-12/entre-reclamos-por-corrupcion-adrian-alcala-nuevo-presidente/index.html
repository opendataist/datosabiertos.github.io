Entre reclamos por corrupción, Adrián Alcalá nuevo presidente

Adrián Alcalá Méndez assumes leadership of the INAI amidst allegations of internal opacity and ethical concerns. His election highlights the fragility of institutions guarding transparency when internal governance fails, demonstrating how leadership disputes can undermine public trust. Alcalá Méndez prioritizes institutional unity and zero tolerance for harassment to counter political pressures. This focus on internal integrity is crucial, as effective oversight requires staff to feel safe and respected within their professional environment. The controversy underscores the vital link between organizational ethics and open data governance. Ensuring transparency in public institutions is not just about accessing information, but also about maintaining the moral authority and operational independence necessary to protect citizen rights against corruption.
