AMLO no desistirá de intentar la desaparición de organismos autónomos - Noticaribe

The president proposes eliminating autonomous bodies like the INAI, arguing they serve minorities rather than the public good. This move threatens the institutional independence essential for safeguarding citizens' rights to information. Such a drastic reduction in transparency mechanisms undermines the accountability structures that open data ecosystems rely on. Weakening these entities risks restricting public access to government data and obscuring administrative decision-making. This development is critical for open data advocates, as it signals a potential rollback of digital transparency. It highlights the fragility of data governance frameworks when political priorities shift away from institutional autonomy and public oversight.
