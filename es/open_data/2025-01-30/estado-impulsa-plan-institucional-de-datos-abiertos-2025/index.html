Estado impulsa Plan Institucional de Datos Abiertos 2025

San Luis Potosí launches its 2025 Open Data Plan to enhance transparency, efficiency, and citizen participation across state institutions. This initiative strengthens accountability by ensuring public organizations publish relevant information, thereby empowering civil society and improving governance through technological tools and open access policies. The state platform now hosts hundreds of datasets from numerous public entities, significantly expanding available information. By making these resources accessible, the government supports a culture of oversight and trust, allowing citizens and organizations to engage more deeply with public administration and monitor institutional performance effectively. Furthermore, the agency provides free online courses on open government to educate officials and citizens alike. This educational component is crucial for open_data ecosystems as it builds technical capacity and fosters active civic engagement, ensuring that transparency efforts translate into meaningful societal impact and informed decision-making.
