La economía española se acelera en 2024 y cierra el año con un crecimiento del 3,2%

The Spanish economy demonstrated robust vitality in 2024, achieving its highest recorded gross domestic product through strong domestic consumption and investment. This internal momentum significantly outpaced weaker external demand, allowing Spain to lead growth within the Eurozone. The expansion reflects a balanced recovery where household spending and business investment drove quarterly gains, signaling confidence and economic resilience despite global headwinds. Sectoral performance highlighted a broad-based recovery, with agriculture, services, industry, and construction all contributing positively to the national output. The labor market also showed strength, evidenced by increased hours worked and full-time equivalent positions, although productivity faced minor declines. This structural progression underscores the economy's ability to generate employment and value across diverse industries, reinforcing the foundation for sustainable long-term development and stability. This data is highly relevant to open_data as it illustrates the critical importance of transparent, high-quality statistical outputs for understanding macroeconomic trends. Reliable, publicly accessible datasets enable researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze complex economic dynamics, such as the interplay between domestic demand and external factors. By providing clear metrics on growth, employment, and sectoral performance, official statistics empower evidence-based decision-making and foster accountability in how national resources are utilized and expanded.
