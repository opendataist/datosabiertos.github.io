Europa: estadísticas Fruit Logistica 2025

The European Statistics Handbook serves as a critical resource for understanding the complex interplay of economic, environmental, and political forces shaping the European fruit and vegetable sector. By aggregating comprehensive data on production, trade, and market trends, it highlights how external shocks, from extreme weather to geopolitical conflicts, directly influence supply chains and consumer accessibility, offering essential insights for industry stakeholders navigating these challenges. Relevant to open data, this publication demonstrates the value of accessible, standardized statistical information in fostering transparency and informed decision-making within agricultural markets. It illustrates how detailed metrics on pricing, production volumes, and operational costs empower businesses to adapt to fluctuating conditions, such as rising living expenses or labor shortages. The availability of such structured data supports analytical rigor, enabling stakeholders to identify patterns and vulnerabilities that might otherwise remain obscured. Ultimately, the handbook underscores the importance of reliable data ecosystems in addressing sector-wide sustainability and resilience goals. By providing a clear view of how climate events and policy debates affect trade flows, it facilitates better strategic planning. This resource exemplifies how open, high-quality statistical collections can bridge the gap between raw data and actionable intelligence, helping industries respond effectively to both immediate economic pressures and long-term structural shifts in global food systems.
