Los Ayuntamientos deben de publicar en su portal web el padrón de prestadores de servicios | Revista Punto de Vista

The legislative reform in San Luis Potosí mandates that municipal governments publicly maintain and regularly update an online registry of service providers assisting migrants. This initiative transforms transparency into a strict obligation, requiring mayors to publish this data on their official portals to ensure maximum accessibility for the community. By centralizing this information, the state aims to prevent fraudulent practices and protect vulnerable populations from misleading services. This update ensures that the registry reflects real-time changes, including new registrations or cancellations, thereby guaranteeing the accuracy of available information. The move supports the principle of maximum publicity, allowing citizens to easily verify the legitimacy of businesses offering migration-related support. Such continuous monitoring fosters accountability and trust between migrants and the institutions serving them. This case is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates the practical application of proactive transparency in social governance. It highlights how structured, accessible digital registries can enhance public oversight and protect citizen rights. By treating essential service information as open data, governments can empower communities with reliable resources, illustrating the critical role of data availability in promoting social justice and administrative integrity.
