La inflación acumulada el 2024 en Bolivia llegó al 9,97%, la más alta desde 2018

Bolivia’s 2024 inflation reached nearly 10%, the highest level since 2018, significantly surpassing the government’s initial 3.6% projection. This sharp rise, driven by broad price increases across essential goods like food, transport, and housing, signals a substantial erosion of purchasing power and economic stability for citizens. The discrepancy between official estimates and actual data highlights a critical gap in predictive economic modeling and public communication. Such misalignments undermine trust in institutional forecasts and complicate policy responses needed to address cost-of-living pressures affecting vulnerable populations. This case is vital for open data advocates as it underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and timely statistical reporting. Ensuring the public can easily verify official figures fosters accountability, enabling civil society to monitor economic health and demand evidence-based governance when government projections diverge significantly from ground reality.
