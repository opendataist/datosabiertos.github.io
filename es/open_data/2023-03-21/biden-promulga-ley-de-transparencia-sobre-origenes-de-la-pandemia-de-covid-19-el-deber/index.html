Biden promulga ley de transparencia sobre orígenes de la pandemia de covid-19 | El Deber

The US administration’s decision to declassify pandemic origin documents prioritizes transparency and historical accountability. This move aims to resolve longstanding uncertainties by sharing intelligence, regardless of the conflicting expert theories regarding a natural outbreak versus a laboratory leak. Relevant to open data, this initiative highlights the critical need for accessible, verified information in public health crises. Establishing clear origins allows for better global preparedness, demonstrating how open access to government records can directly inform scientific strategies for preventing future outbreaks. However, this transparency carries significant political and diplomatic risks, particularly with China. The tension between demanding full disclosure and maintaining national security illustrates the complex balance open data policies must navigate. Ultimately, prioritizing truth over political convenience serves the broader goal of global safety and scientific integrity.
