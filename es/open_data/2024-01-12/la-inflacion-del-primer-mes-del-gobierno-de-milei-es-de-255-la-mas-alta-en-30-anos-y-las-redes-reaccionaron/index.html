La inflación del primer mes del gobierno de Milei es de 25,5%, la más alta en 30 años y las redes reaccionaron

The article reports a sharp acceleration in Argentina’s inflation, driven by economic deregulation and devaluation under the new administration. This surge highlights the immediate volatility resulting from rapid policy shifts, leaving citizens vulnerable to sudden cost-of-living increases. This context is crucial for open data, as it underscores the necessity for transparent, real-time statistical monitoring to verify official figures and understand policy impacts. Without accessible, granular data, the public cannot accurately assess the validity of government claims or the true extent of economic distress. Ultimately, robust open data initiatives provide essential accountability in times of crisis. By enabling independent analysis and public scrutiny, open data empowers citizens to navigate and challenge the narrative during periods of significant economic instability and structural change.
