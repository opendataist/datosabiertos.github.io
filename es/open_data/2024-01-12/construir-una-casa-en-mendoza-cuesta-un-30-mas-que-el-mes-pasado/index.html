Construir una casa en Mendoza cuesta un 30% más que el mes pasado

The report highlights that construction costs in Mendoza surged significantly in January, driven primarily by a 49 percent monthly increase in material prices. While labor costs remained static, specific building materials experienced extreme price jumps due to currency devaluation, drastically inflating the total expense for erecting both basic and medium-quality homes. This data serves as a crucial case study for open data, demonstrating how transparent, monthly tracking of economic indicators reveals real-time inflation impacts on essential sectors. By making detailed price evolution publicly available, organizations empower stakeholders to understand hidden economic pressures beyond headline inflation figures. Ultimately, this transparency underscores the value of open data in fostering accountability and informed decision-making. It allows citizens and experts to analyze granular trends, verifying official statistics and recognizing how macroeconomic shifts directly affect housing affordability and construction feasibility in real-world scenarios.
