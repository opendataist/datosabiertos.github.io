Palma ha construido 6.500 viviendas en diez años con 32.000 residentes más

Palma has experienced significant urban growth over the last decade, driven by a surge in both housing construction and population. This expansion highlights the intense pressure on municipal resources and infrastructure, raising concerns about sustainable city planning and equitable living conditions. The data reveals a societal shift towards smaller households and an aging demographic, alongside a growing foreign-born community. These changes necessitate adaptive public policies that address diverse housing needs and social integration, reflecting broader European urban transformation trends. This article is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how transparent statistical indicators allow citizens and researchers to track urban dynamics. Access to such granular, comparable data empowers communities to hold local governments accountable for development policies and ensures informed decision-making regarding future urban challenges.
