El mercado laboral en México retrocede al reportar la pérdida de más de 275.000 empleos

Mexico's labor market contracted in September, shedding significant employment. This decline highlights a shift toward informality, where the majority of lost jobs occurred in the informal sector. The trend raises concerns about the quality of work and social protection for a large portion of the workforce. This data underscores the importance of open data in tracking economic health accurately. Public access to detailed employment statistics allows researchers and policymakers to identify structural weaknesses, such as high informal employment rates. Transparent datasets enable better analysis of labor market trends and inform evidence-based policy decisions. Relevance to open data lies in the ability to monitor societal indicators like the ENOE. By making these figures accessible, citizens and institutions can verify official reports and advocate for labor rights. Open data fosters accountability and supports a deeper understanding of the economic challenges facing the region.
