SpaceX apuesta por inteligencia artificial y datos abiertos para proteger los satélites

SpaceX introduces Stargaze, a revolutionary system utilizing vast networks of stellar trackers to detect objects in low Earth orbit with unprecedented frequency and precision. By significantly enhancing situational awareness, this technology drastically reduces the uncertainty inherent in traditional daily observation methods, thereby lowering the probability of catastrophic collisions between satellites and space debris. Crucially, the company pledges to share these conjunction data and orbital predictions freely with all space operators. This commitment to open access aims to foster global coordination, encouraging industry-wide adoption of similar transparency practices to mitigate the escalating risks posed by rocket bodies and anti-satellite tests. This initiative is vital for open data as it demonstrates how proprietary technological advancements can drive public safety when information is liberated from corporate silos. By providing free access to critical orbital intelligence, SpaceX sets a precedent for collaborative space traffic management, proving that open data exchange is essential for maintaining the long-term sustainability and safety of the orbital environment.
