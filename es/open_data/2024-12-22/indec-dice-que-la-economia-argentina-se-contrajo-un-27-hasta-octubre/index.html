Indec dice que la economía argentina se contrajo un 2,7% hasta octubre

Argentina’s economy contracted significantly in October, driven by strict austerity measures that halted public works. Despite this decline, President Milei declares the recession over, arguing that recent quarterly data shows a recovery trend. This assertion contrasts with the broad sectoral drops reported by official statistics. The government’s claim relies on a stabilization of monthly economic indicators and growth in specific areas like mining and agriculture. By highlighting these positive signals, officials aim to validate their economic strategy. However, the overall annual performance remains deeply negative, reflecting the harsh reality of the implemented policies. This scenario is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how statistical metrics can be selectively interpreted to support political narratives. Transparent access to raw economic data allows citizens to verify government claims independently. It highlights the essential role of unfiltered information in holding leaders accountable during periods of significant economic transformation.
