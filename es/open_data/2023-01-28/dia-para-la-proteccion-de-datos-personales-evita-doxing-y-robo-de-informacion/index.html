Día para la Protección de Datos Personales: evita doxing y robo de información

This article highlights the urgent need for users to actively manage their digital privacy, emphasizing that the consequences of data theft extend beyond the virtual world into real-life financial and legal repercussions. By understanding legal frameworks and recognizing the risks of sharing sensitive information, individuals can better safeguard themselves against fraud and identity theft facilitated by dark web markets. Key recommendations include utilizing password managers to generate unique credentials and employing two-factor authentication, which adds a critical security layer even if passwords are compromised. Furthermore, users are advised to carefully review app permissions and read terms and conditions, ensuring they maintain control over who accesses their data and how it is treated. This content is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of individual agency in data protection. It illustrates the tension between data openness and personal security, reminding users that while data systems may be transparent, individuals must remain vigilant to prevent their personal information from being exploited or misused without consent.
