El Gobierno espera un «buen año» tras ver superadas sus previsiones de 2022

Spain’s economy demonstrated remarkable resilience in 2022, surpassing growth expectations despite global crises. This performance highlights the effectiveness of targeted government interventions, particularly those aimed at shielding households from inflation. The administration emphasizes that structural reforms and social protections successfully mitigated economic adversity. By maintaining lower inflation relative to neighbors, the state proved its capacity to stabilize living standards during turbulent times. This narrative is relevant to open data as it illustrates how public statistical transparency underpins political accountability. Reliable, accessible metrics enable citizens to verify government claims and assess policy efficacy, fostering trust in democratic institutions.
