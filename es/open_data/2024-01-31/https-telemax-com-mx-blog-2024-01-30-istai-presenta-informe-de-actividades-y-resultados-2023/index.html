Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Sonora Institute for Transparency significantly improved its technological and regulatory framework in 2023, successfully handling thousands of information requests. This modernization aimed to enhance citizen trust and ensure certainty for those exercising their right to access public data. The institution resolved the majority of review resources in favor of applicants, demonstrating a strong commitment to transparency. These results indicate that streamlined processes and specialized administrative units effectively strengthen democratic institutions by making transparency a practical tool for governance. This article is relevant to open data as it highlights how institutional reforms and technological upgrades are essential for maximizing the utility and accessibility of public information. It underscores the importance of robust legal frameworks and efficient administrative mechanisms in fostering a transparent society where open data serves as a cornerstone for accountability and civic engagement.
