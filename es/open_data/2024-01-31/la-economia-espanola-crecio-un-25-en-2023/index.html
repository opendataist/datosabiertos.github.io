La economía española creció un 2,5% en 2023

Spain’s robust 2023 economic growth, surpassing official forecasts, highlights the resilience of domestic consumption and tourism, which drove significant increases in both internal demand and exports despite a gloomy international context. This sustained performance underscores the effectiveness of government measures to support purchasing power and capitalizes on record visitor numbers in the services sector. The data reveals a positive divergence from pessimistic expectations set by major organizations, suggesting Spain’s economy is recovering more dynamically than anticipated. This strong showing provides a solid foundation for future projections, although subsequent forecasts for 2024 remain more conservative than the government’s optimistic targets. This article is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how publicly available official statistics, such as those from the INE, enable real-time verification of economic health. Reliable open datasets allow researchers and citizens to independently analyze trends, cross-reference institutional forecasts, and hold policymakers accountable regarding fiscal planning and recovery strategies.
