Se garantizará seguridad de encuestadores del INEGI

The State Security Secretariat and INEGI have established a collaborative operational plan to ensure the successful execution of upcoming economic and security censuses across San Luis Potosí. This partnership guarantees surveyors receive necessary support and protection while gathering vital data on local businesses and public safety institutions. These censuses are fundamental for generating accurate statistics that inform new public policies and support economic growth. By capturing detailed information from enterprises and security systems, the government can better evaluate its performance and implement measures that genuinely benefit the population. This initiative is crucial for open_data as it highlights the interoperability between state security agencies and national statistical bodies. Transparent data collection enhances public accountability and provides open access to reliable metrics, enabling researchers and citizens to understand governmental efficacy and drive evidence-based societal improvements.
