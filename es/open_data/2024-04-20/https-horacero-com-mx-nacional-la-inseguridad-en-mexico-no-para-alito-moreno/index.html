Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This article highlights the growing disconnect between official government narratives and citizen reality regarding public safety in Mexico. It underscores how statistical data, specifically from INEGI, reveals a rising perception of insecurity despite contrary political claims. This divergence is critical for understanding the challenges of transparent governance and public trust. The text illustrates the power of open data in holding authorities accountable. By publishing objective metrics on fear and safety perceptions, institutions provide the evidence needed for political opposition and civil society to challenge misleading statements. It demonstrates how data serves as a counter-narrative to political spin. For open data initiatives, this case emphasizes the necessity of accessible, reliable statistics to facilitate informed public debate. Without such transparency, governments can manipulate perceptions of security, eroding democratic accountability. Thus, robust data infrastructure is essential for verifying claims and empowering citizens with the truth they need to evaluate their leaders effectively.
