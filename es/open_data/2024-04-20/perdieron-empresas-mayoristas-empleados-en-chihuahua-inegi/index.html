Perdieron empresas mayoristas empleados en Chihuahua: Inegi

INEGI’s latest commercial enterprise survey reveals mixed economic signals, highlighting divergent trends between wholesale and retail sectors. Chihuahua notably experienced a decline in total employment within wholesale firms, contrasting with slight national growth in retail employment. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how standardized statistical information enables granular regional economic analysis. Such transparency allows researchers to identify localized labor market shifts and assess the real impact of remuneration changes across different commercial activities.
