Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article underscores the critical necessity of digital hygiene, warning that indiscriminate sharing on social networks exposes individuals to significant security risks. Experts emphasize that even minor personal details can be weaponized by criminals for identity theft and fraud, challenging the modern habit of broadcasting every life event online. The core message is that users must exercise caution, recognizing that digital footprints directly impact physical safety and financial stability. Privacy settings are insufficient on their own; proactive management is required to mitigate these dangers. This involves regularly auditing friend lists to exclude unrecognized contacts and restricting visibility of personal data. By treating social media interactions with the same privacy standards as face-to-face conversations, individuals can significantly reduce the probability of malicious actors exploiting shared information for illicit purposes. This guidance is highly relevant to open data principles as it illustrates the real-world consequences of unchecked information disclosure. While open data promotes transparency and accessibility, this article highlights the vital balance between sharing and privacy. It serves as a cautionary example for open data practitioners, reinforcing that while data openness drives innovation, the protection of personal identifiable information remains a fundamental ethical and security imperative in the digital age.
