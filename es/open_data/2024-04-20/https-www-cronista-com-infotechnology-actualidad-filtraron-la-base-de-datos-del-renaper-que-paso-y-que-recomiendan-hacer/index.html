The massive leak of Argentine citizen data from RENAPER highlights critical vulnerabilities in public database security, emphasizing the urgent need for robust data protection standards. This incident underscores the risks associated with exposed APIs and source code, revealing how interconnected digital systems can amplify breaches when access credentials remain unprotected across various institutions. The event is highly relevant to open data discussions, as it demonstrates that transparency must be balanced with strict security protocols to prevent sensitive personal information from being exploited or sold on illegal markets.
