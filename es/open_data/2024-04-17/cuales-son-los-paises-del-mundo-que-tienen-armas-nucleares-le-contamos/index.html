¿Cuáles son los países del mundo que tienen armas nucleares? Le contamos

The article details the devastating impact of nuclear weapons, explaining how their explosions release immense energy, creating fireballs, intense radiation, and long-term radioactive fallout. This physical destruction drives global efforts for abolition, as nine nations currently possess thousands of warheads, with the United States and Russia holding the vast majority of the world's arsenal. Regarding open data relevance, this summary highlights the critical importance of transparent, accessible datasets in monitoring nuclear proliferation and stockpile reductions. Reliable data allows international organizations and civil society to track military capabilities, verify compliance with treaties, and assess risks to public health and the environment. Without accurate information, it is impossible to effectively advocate for disarmament or manage the associated humanitarian crises. Consequently, maintaining open access to nuclear stockpile statistics fosters accountability and informs policy decisions aimed at reducing global threats. As these arsenals evolve in potency, continuous data sharing becomes essential for understanding trends in military escalation. Ultimately, democratizing this information empowers communities and researchers to analyze potential dangers, ensuring that the implications of nuclear weapons remain a central focus in discussions about global security and transparency.
