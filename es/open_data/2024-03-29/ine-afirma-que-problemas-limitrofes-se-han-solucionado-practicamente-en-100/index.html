INE afirma que problemas limítrofes se han solucionado prácticamente en 100%

The INE resolved nearly all municipal border disputes hindering the census, eliminating conflicts that risked inaccurate population counts due to conflicting jurisdictional claims. This administrative closure ensures data integrity and prevents double-counting or omissions across regions like La Paz and Santa Cruz. Access issues persist in remote areas, though nearly all urban zones are now under official possession. The remaining gaps reflect logistical challenges in isolated terrains rather than political obstruction, allowing the institution to finalize the physical collection phase effectively. This resolution is vital for open data initiatives as it establishes a reliable, unified national dataset. Clear jurisdictional boundaries enable accurate geospatial analysis and public transparency, ensuring that census information serves as a trustworthy foundation for policy-making and resource distribution.
