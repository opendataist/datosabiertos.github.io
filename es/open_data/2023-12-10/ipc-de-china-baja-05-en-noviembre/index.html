IPC de China baja 0,5% en noviembre

China’s consumer prices fell slightly in November, marking a monthly decline despite stable year-to-year growth. Food costs dropped significantly, offsetting minor increases in non-food items. This data highlights how open data enables real-time tracking of economic trends, empowering analysts to assess inflationary pressures and market stability accurately.
