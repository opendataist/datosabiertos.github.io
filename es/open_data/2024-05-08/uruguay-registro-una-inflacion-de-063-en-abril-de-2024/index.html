Uruguay registró una inflación de 0,63% en abril de 2024

The Uruguayan INE’s recent inflation report highlights significant price volatility across essential sectors, driven primarily by sharp increases in food and transportation costs. This data illustrates the dynamic nature of economic indicators, emphasizing the critical need for precise, granular statistical releases to monitor market trends accurately. By detailing specific price changes in categories like fruits, vegetables, and apparel, the publication underscores the value of open data in enabling transparent public scrutiny. Access to such disaggregated metrics allows researchers and citizens to understand inflationary pressures beyond aggregate figures, fostering greater accountability in national statistics. Ultimately, this release exemplifies how open government data empowers society with the tools to analyze economic health effectively. It reinforces the importance of accessible, detailed official datasets in supporting informed decision-making and ensuring transparency in public administration, which are foundational principles of the open data movement.
