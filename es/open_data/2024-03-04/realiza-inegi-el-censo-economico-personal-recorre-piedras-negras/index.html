Realiza INEGI el censo económico, personal recorre Piedras Negras

The INEGI is conducting a comprehensive economic census to map the structure and dynamics of Mexico’s commercial landscape. By collecting detailed data on business operations, production values, and employment across millions of establishments, the institute aims to create a precise snapshot of the national economy. This large-scale data collection effort relies on cooperation from business owners and the general public to ensure accuracy. The gathered information, which covers sectors like manufacturing and services, will be used exclusively for statistical purposes to analyze economic trends and inform policy decisions. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it generates massive, high-quality public datasets. These resources enable researchers, developers, and policymakers to access transparent economic insights, fostering better analysis of market behaviors and supporting evidence-based decision-making in both public and private sectors.
