Intensifican socialización del proceso censal en Oruro y en Pando encuestadores reciben material informativo

The National Statistics Institute is intensifying its efforts to socialize the upcoming Population and Housing Census across Bolivia, particularly in Oruro and Pando. By training educators and distributing detailed logistical materials, the government aims to ensure comprehensive public awareness and accurate data collection at the community level. This initiative emphasizes transparency and engagement with civil society, including universities and social organizations. The process involves extensive field preparation, such as mapping sectors and training thousands of volunteers, to guarantee that demographic, economic, and social data reflect the true characteristics of the nation’s inhabitants and their housing. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical phase of data generation and public dissemination. Understanding how statistical bodies mobilize resources and engage citizens provides insight into the reliability and accessibility of the resulting datasets, which form the foundation for future open government and analytical initiatives.
