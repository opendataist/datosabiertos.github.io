Ética realiza panel sobre Gobierno abierto y transparencia

The article highlights the Dominican government's commitment to transparency as a fundamental tool for empowering citizens and building a just society. Officials emphasized that the "right to know" is not merely a principle but a practical mechanism that enables the exercise of other human rights, such as health and education. By ensuring open access to public information, the state fosters an environment where every voice is heard, transforming governance into a more participatory and accountable process. Budget transparency and direct dialogue with communities were identified as key strategies for implementing effective public policy. The government has achieved significant regional standing in budgetary openness, which serves as a catalyst for institutional trust. This approach relies on local development councils to channel citizen demands, ensuring that decisions are grounded in real societal needs rather than bureaucratic isolation, thereby reinforcing the link between government action and public welfare. Recognizing institutions with high transparency scores promotes a culture of openness essential for democracy. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible public information directly strengthens civic oversight and improves service quality. By celebrating entities that exceed transparency benchmarks, the government encourages a systemic shift where data availability becomes a cornerstone for democratic participation and continuous improvement in public management.
