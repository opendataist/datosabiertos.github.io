Reduce 0,8% valor de la industria del chocolate y confitería en México en 2022

The Mexican chocolate and confectionery industry demonstrated resilience by maintaining a positive international trade balance despite a slight annual decline in production value. This stability persists, with economic output remaining significantly higher than pre-pandemic levels, indicating strong structural health within the sector. Employment figures reveal a robust labor market supporting both chocolate and confectionery sub-sectors, reflecting steady growth in the number of economic units over recent years. The data highlights the sector's importance as a substantial employer, with clear gender distribution across manufacturing roles, underscoring its social and economic footprint. This report is vital for open data initiatives as it exemplifies how government statistical agencies can make granular industrial metrics publicly accessible. By transparently sharing detailed production, employment, and trade statistics, institutions empower researchers and policymakers to analyze trends rigorously, fostering informed decision-making based on reliable, publicly available evidence.
