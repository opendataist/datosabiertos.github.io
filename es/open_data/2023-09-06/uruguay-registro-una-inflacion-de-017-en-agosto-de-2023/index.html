Uruguay registró una inflación de 0,17% en agosto de 2023

Uruguay’s August 2023 inflation data reveals modest monthly growth but highlights significant divergences across key sectors. Food prices, particularly fresh produce, declined sharply, offering temporary relief to household budgets, while housing and education costs continued to rise steadily. Transportation expenses also increased, driven by higher fuel and travel costs. This mixed inflationary pressure illustrates the complex interplay between global energy trends and local service demands, affecting overall consumer purchasing power differently depending on expenditure categories. This report is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the necessity of transparent, granular statistical dissemination. By providing detailed breakdowns from the National Institute of Statistics, the data empowers researchers and citizens to analyze economic trends, ensuring accountability and enabling informed public discourse on cost-of-living impacts.
