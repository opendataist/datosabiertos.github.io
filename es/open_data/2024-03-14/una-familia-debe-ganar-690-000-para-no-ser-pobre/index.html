Una familia debe ganar $690.000 para no ser pobre

The Basic Total Basket rose significantly faster than general inflation, redefining poverty thresholds as prices surged for essential non-food goods. This divergence highlights a widening gap between food affordability and overall living costs, impacting how socioeconomic vulnerability is measured in Argentina. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it provides the official baseline metrics used to calculate poverty and indigence. Transparent access to these evolving baskets allows researchers and citizens to independently verify living standards and assess the real impact of economic policies on household welfare.
