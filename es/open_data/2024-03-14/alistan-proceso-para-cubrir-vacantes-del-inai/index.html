Alistan proceso para cubrir vacantes del INAI

Mexican lawmakers reached a consensus to restart selecting two commissioners for the INAI, the body enforcing the right to information. Although launching the selection process does not guarantee a qualified majority for appointment, legislators expressed a firm willingness to attempt filling these critical vacancies. This development is relevant to open_data because the INAI safeguards transparency and data protection rights. Filling these leadership roles is essential for ensuring the institution’s independence and its ability to effectively enforce access to public information for citizens.
