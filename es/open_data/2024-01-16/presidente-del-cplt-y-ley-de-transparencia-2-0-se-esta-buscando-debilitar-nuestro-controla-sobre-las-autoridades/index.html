Presidente del CPLT y Ley de Transparencia 2.0: "Se está buscando debilitar nuestro controla sobre las autoridades"

The transparency board warns that proposed legislative reforms threaten its autonomy and reverse progress by weakening oversight mechanisms. These changes are seen as detrimental to the principle of open government, which relies on robust, independent institutions to ensure public accountability. This issue is vital for open data, as institutional independence is essential for enforcing transparency standards.
