Google usa inteligencia artificial para preservar el ADN de especies en peligro de extinción

The accelerating loss of biodiversity threatens to erase species before their genetic information is documented, making urgent genomic sequencing vital for preservation. By integrating artificial intelligence with global scientific initiatives, researchers can now rapidly decode genomes to identify risks and design precise conservation strategies. This approach transforms biological data into actionable tools for saving endangered species and understanding evolutionary history, offering broader applications in agriculture and health. Google’s contribution highlights how advanced algorithms dramatically reduce the time and cost of sequencing, making comprehensive biological catalogs feasible for the first time. This technological leap enables scientists to analyze vast amounts of genetic data efficiently, supporting projects that aim to sequence thousands of species. The collaboration demonstrates a powerful synergy between tech innovation and ecological science, pushing the boundaries of what is possible in genomic research. Crucially for open data, all generated genomes are made freely accessible to researchers and conservationists worldwide. This commitment to open access ensures that valuable biological data is not siloed but shared to accelerate global efforts in species protection and environmental study. It underscores the importance of transparent, open scientific resources in addressing the biodiversity crisis.
