The article provides step-by-step instructions for temporarily disabling popular browser ad blockers to access specific websites. This process highlights the tension between user privacy tools and content access, a core issue in open data distribution. Many open datasets and portals rely on advertising for sustainability, inadvertently restricting access for users prioritizing ad-free experiences. Understanding these technical friction points is relevant to open data advocates.
