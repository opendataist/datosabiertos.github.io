C-LM alcanzó más de 3,9 millones de pernoctaciones hoteleras en 2023 y llegó a los 2,2 millones de viajeros

The article presents comprehensive tourism statistics for Castilla-La Mancha and Spain in 2023, highlighting a robust recovery in hotel occupancy and revenue. While national figures show significant growth, particularly among international tourists, regional data reveals specific trends in stay duration, accommodation capacity, and seasonal fluctuations. These metrics illustrate the varying degrees of tourism maturity and economic impact across different autonomous communities, with some regions outperforming others in attracting both domestic and foreign travelers. This data is highly relevant to open_data because it exemplifies the value of standardized, public statistical reporting by national institutes like the INE. By making detailed metrics on occupancy, overnight stays, and hotel pricing freely available, governments enable researchers, analysts, and citizens to conduct transparent, data-driven evaluations of the tourism sector. This accessibility fosters accountability and allows for precise benchmarking between regions. Furthermore, such open datasets empower stakeholders to identify economic disparities and optimize resource allocation. When granular information about traveler demographics and revenue trends is publicly accessible, it supports evidence-based policy-making and strategic business decisions. Ultimately, the availability of this information underscores how open data serves as a critical infrastructure for understanding complex socio-economic activities and driving informed development in the hospitality industry.
