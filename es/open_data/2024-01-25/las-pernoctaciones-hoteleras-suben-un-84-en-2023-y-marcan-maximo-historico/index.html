Las pernoctaciones hoteleras suben un 8,4% en 2023 y marcan máximo histórico

The 2023 tourism sector achieved record overnight stays, signaling a robust recovery and sustained demand for hospitality services. This surge reflects a strong rebound in visitor confidence, with significant growth in both domestic and international travel, highlighting the industry's resilience and capacity to handle increased volumes. Revenue metrics also showed notable improvement, with hotel prices rising significantly compared to the previous year. This increase in average daily rates and revenue per available room indicates that hotels were able to capitalize on higher demand, improving financial performance despite economic pressures. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, high-quality indicators for economic analysis. It enables researchers, policymakers, and businesses to monitor tourism trends accurately, supporting evidence-based decision-making and fostering greater accountability in the reporting of public economic statistics.
