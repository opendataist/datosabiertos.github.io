Promueven transparencia e innovación en el Open Data Day | La Opción de Chihuahua

The University of Chihuahua hosts an Open Data Day to promote transparency, innovation, and intelligent decision-making through public data. This initiative invites citizens, students, and public servants to engage with open datasets, emphasizing their role in civic empowerment. The event highlights the transformative power of data accessibility, featuring key speakers who discuss how open information drives better governance. By lowering barriers to data use, the conference aims to foster a culture of accountability and collaborative problem-solving within the community. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates grassroots efforts to democratize information. It illustrates how local institutions can leverage open data principles to enhance public trust and encourage active citizen participation in governmental processes through accessible educational events.
