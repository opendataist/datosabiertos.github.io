La Suprema Corte ordena al Senado completar nombramientos en el Inai | Revista Punto de Vista

The Supreme Court ordered the Senate to finalize pending appointments for the transparency institute, acknowledging that excessive delays undermined citizens' rights to access information and data protection. The ruling allows the institute to function with fewer commissioners if all designated members are present, mitigating operational paralysis caused by political inaction and preserving essential democratic safeguards. This decision is vital for open data, as a fully staffed institute ensures robust oversight of public transparency and data privacy. Without it, citizen guarantees weaken, hindering the accountability and information flow fundamental to an open society.
