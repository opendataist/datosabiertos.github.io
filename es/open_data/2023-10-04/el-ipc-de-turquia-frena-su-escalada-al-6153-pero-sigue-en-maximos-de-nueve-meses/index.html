El IPC de Turquía frena su escalada al 61,53%, pero sigue en máximos de nueve meses

Turkey’s inflation moderated in September, marking a slight retreat from recent peaks, though it remains at multi-month highs. This tentative stabilization reflects the impact of the central bank’s aggressive monetary tightening, which has raised interest rates to historic levels to combat price surges. The data highlights significant disparities across sectors, with hospitality and education experiencing extreme price increases while housing saw relatively modest growth. Such granular insights are essential for understanding the uneven economic burden on households and businesses during periods of hyperinflation. This article is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the critical need for accessible, high-quality statistical infrastructure. Reliable public datasets empower analysts to monitor policy effectiveness and hold institutions accountable, ensuring that transparency drives informed economic decision-making rather than speculation.
