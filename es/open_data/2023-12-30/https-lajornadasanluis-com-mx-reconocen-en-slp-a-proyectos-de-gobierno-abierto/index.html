Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The government of San Luis Potosí is strengthening administrative efficiency through open data principles. By rewarding citizen and institutional initiatives, the state promotes transparency, accountability, and innovation. This approach encourages the use of digital tools and information systems to improve public services. Civil society projects highlighting accessibility and AI-driven information access demonstrate how open practices empower marginalized groups. Simultaneously, government efforts in digitizing procedures show a commitment to modernizing infrastructure. These winning ideas illustrate practical applications of open government values in real-world scenarios. This recognition mechanism fosters a culture of continuous improvement in public management. It highlights the critical role of open data in bridging the gap between citizens and the state. Ultimately, these efforts ensure that governance becomes more inclusive, responsive, and aligned with the needs of the community.
