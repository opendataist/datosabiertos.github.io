Las recomendaciones contra ciberdelitos

The INAI warns that engaging with unverified online promotions and public networks exposes users to data theft and fraud, urging caution with digital footprints and banking details. Securing personal information against cyber threats requires strict hygiene, such as disabling unused connections and protecting physical assets like mailboxes. This guidance is vital for open data advocates, as it highlights how easily accessible personal information in digital spaces can be exploited, reinforcing the need for robust privacy safeguards.
