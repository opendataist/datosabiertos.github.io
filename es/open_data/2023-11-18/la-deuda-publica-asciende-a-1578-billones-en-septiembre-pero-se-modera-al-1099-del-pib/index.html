La deuda pública asciende a 1,578 billones en septiembre, pero se modera al 109,9% del PIB

Spanish public administration debt increased slightly in late 2023, though the debt-to-GDP ratio moderated. This data highlights the critical need for transparent, granular financial reporting to monitor fiscal health effectively. Open data initiatives ensure these statistics are accessible for detailed analysis across different administrative levels. By standardizing and publishing such figures, stakeholders can track intra-sector obligations and territorial disparities. Ultimately, this transparency fosters accountability and informed policy-making. It allows researchers and citizens to understand how debt is distributed among central, regional, and local entities, strengthening democratic oversight of public finances.
