La Euipo protegerá productos de artesanía e industria

The EUIPO now protects geographic indications for industrial and artisanal products, extending European safeguards beyond agriculture to items like crystal and porcelain. This shift centralizes national registrations into a unified framework, ensuring consumers can identify high-quality goods tied to local heritage while giving producers stronger market positioning. Crucially, this evolution supports regional development by encouraging investment from small enterprises and micro-businesses. By creating distinct market niches, the regulation incentivizes quality preservation and innovation, turning cultural heritage into an economic driver that benefits local communities and enhances brand value across the single market. This is relevant to open data as it establishes a centralized, transparent registry of protected names. Such standardized, accessible datasets enable better market analysis, consumer trust, and digital integration. Open access to these indicators facilitates research, compliance, and innovation, demonstrating how structured public data empowers economic growth and cultural preservation.
