Contraloría avanza en Gobierno Abierto con capacitación | Revista Punto de Vista

San Luis Potosí initiated a governance boost by training 70 public officials across 13 educational institutions in transparency and open government principles. This strategic capacity building aims to enhance administrative efficiency and strengthen institutional accountability mechanisms for citizens. The initiative promotes innovative projects grounded in transparency, participatory work, and collaboration. By empowering officials, the government seeks to improve decision-making quality and actively encourage citizen involvement in public administration processes. This effort is highly relevant to open data as it establishes a foundational culture of transparency within the public sector. Strengthening these institutional committees ensures that data and services are not only accessible but also managed with a focus on civic engagement and digital innovation.
