C-LM pide a Ministerio Sanidad que subsane un "error" en datos de listas de espera y reclama su "correcta publicación"

Castilla-La Mancha detected a significant data error in the Ministry of Health’s waiting list report, prompting a request to correct and re-cite the figures. The regional government insists its transparent portal already holds the accurate metrics, highlighting a commitment to truth over the erroneous national publication. This situation underscores the critical importance of open data practices. It demonstrates that while transparency tools exist, they must be consistently utilized and trusted by central authorities to ensure public access to reliable, verifiable health information.
