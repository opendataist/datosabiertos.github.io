Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Chihuahua is launching its Open Government Action Plan to bridge the gap between citizens and authorities by enhancing transparency and accessibility. This initiative responds to public consultations, aiming to ensure that critical information regarding security, rural development, and business support is readily available to the community. The plan focuses on strengthening communication channels and creating networks for citizen surveillance and entrepreneurial support. By addressing issues like water scarcity in rural areas and the perceived lack of visibility in government programs, the municipality seeks to foster greater trust and active participation from the society in monitoring public resources. This effort is relevant to open data because it prioritizes the release of actionable information to facilitate civic oversight and economic participation. It demonstrates how structured transparency empowers citizens to engage with governance, ensuring that data drives accountability and accessible services rather than remaining restricted within administrative silos.
