The Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía reports a significant surge in light vehicle sales during March 2023, marking a substantial recovery for the domestic automotive industry. This growth not only exceeded official estimates but also surpassed sales figures from the pre-pandemic era of 2019 for that specific month, indicating strong consumer demand and improved inventory levels across various brands. Despite this monthly success, the first quarter of 2023 remains slightly below 2019 levels, reflecting the lingering effects of inflation and economic uncertainty. While general price indices and specific vehicle price increases persist, broader economic expectations for 2023 show modest GDP growth and a projected decline in inflation, suggesting a gradual stabilization of the macroeconomic environment. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical role of transparent, publicly accessible government statistics in monitoring economic health and guiding business strategies. By leveraging official datasets from the INEGI, analysts and stakeholders can accurately assess market trends, validate industry associations' claims, and make informed decisions regarding production and investment, thereby ensuring accountability and efficiency in the public data ecosystem.
