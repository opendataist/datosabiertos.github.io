La pérdida de empleos en junio borra avance acumulado del mercado laboral, según Inegi - Noticaribe

The article highlights a significant reversal in Mexico’s labor market dynamics, where substantial job losses in June erased the employment gains achieved during the first half of the year. This negative shift primarily impacted women and key sectors like industry and construction, revealing a fragility in the economic recovery. The data suggests that recent electoral transitions may have contributed to a reduction in formal positions, indicating instability within the official labor statistics. A major implication is the rise in precarious work conditions, characterized by lower wages and increased working hours. The growth of the informal sector and jobs earning minimal wages signals a deterioration in labor quality, even as the informal rate technically decreased due to the loss of formal roles. This trend underscores a concerning move toward economic insecurity for a large portion of the workforce, challenging narratives of steady progress. This analysis is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical importance of transparent, high-frequency statistical sources like the ENOE. Accurate, publicly accessible data allows researchers and citizens to identify subtle but damaging shifts in labor quality that aggregate economic growth might obscure. Without such detailed, open datasets, the nuance between job quantity and job quality remains hidden, hindering effective policy responses to labor market vulnerabilities.
