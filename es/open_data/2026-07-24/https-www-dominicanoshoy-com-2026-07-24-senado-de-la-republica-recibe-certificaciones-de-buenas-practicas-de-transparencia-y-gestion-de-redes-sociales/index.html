Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Senate of the Republic has obtained national certifications validating its commitment to transparency and digital transformation. These accreditations recognize the institution’s adoption of standardized practices for managing public information and institutional social media, establishing a modern governance model that prioritizes efficiency and citizen proximity. A key implication of this achievement is the formal guarantee that citizens have agile, timely access to reusable public data. By meeting rigorous technological standards, the Senate ensures that open data is not only available but also accessible, reinforcing the legal right to information and fostering a stronger, more trustworthy bond between the legislature and the public. This development is highly relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how institutional accountability mechanisms can drive the practical implementation of open government principles. It serves as a replicable reference for other entities, proving that standardized digital transformation and audited transparency protocols are essential for creating usable, reliable data ecosystems that serve the common good rather than existing as mere technical exercises.
