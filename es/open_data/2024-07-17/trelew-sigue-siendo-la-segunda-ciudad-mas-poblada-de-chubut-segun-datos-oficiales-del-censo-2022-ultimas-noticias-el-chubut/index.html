Trelew sigue siendo la segunda ciudad más poblada de Chubut según datos oficiales del Censo 2022 - Últimas Noticias: El Chubut

The article highlights significant demographic shifts in Chubut, revealing that while some areas experienced substantial population growth, others faced notable declines. This dynamic redistribution underscores the evolving urban landscape and rural depopulation trends within the region. A key finding is the robust expansion in the Cordillera departments, particularly Cushamen, which saw the highest growth rate. Conversely, areas like Gastre suffered significant population loss, primarily driven by youth migration in search of better economic opportunities. These contrasting trajectories illustrate the unequal distribution of development and resources across different localities. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible government statistics enable detailed sociological analysis. By making census information freely available, researchers and citizens can critically examine regional disparities, support evidence-based policy-making, and understand the socio-economic forces shaping community resilience and decline.
