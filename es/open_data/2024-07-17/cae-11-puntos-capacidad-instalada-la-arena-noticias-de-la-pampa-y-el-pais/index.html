Cae 11 puntos capacidad instalada | La Arena - Noticias de La Pampa y el país

Argentina’s manufacturing sector experienced a significant contraction in May, with installed capacity utilization dropping sharply compared to the previous year. This decline highlights a broader economic slowdown, particularly in construction and heavy industry, driven by reduced public works and lower demand for agricultural machinery and household appliances. While sectors like oil refining and chemicals showed resilience, the overall industrial activity reflects weakening domestic conditions and reduced investment. The data reveals stark disparities across industries, with non-metallic minerals and metal-mechanical sectors suffering severe drops in production. These trends are directly linked to a slump in construction activity and a decrease in the output of essential goods like food and beverages. The narrative suggests that without policy interventions to stimulate demand and investment, these negative trends could persist, impacting job creation and economic stability. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of transparent, publicly accessible statistical indicators like those from INDEC. Open data allows researchers, journalists, and citizens to track economic health in real-time, fostering accountability and enabling evidence-based public debate. Accessible datasets empower society to understand complex economic shifts, holding institutions responsible for communicating the state of the nation’s industries accurately and promptly.
