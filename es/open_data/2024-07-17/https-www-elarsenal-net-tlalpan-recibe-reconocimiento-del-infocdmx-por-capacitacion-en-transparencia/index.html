The Tlalpan municipality received significant recognition from INFOCDMX for achieving full staff capacity in transparency, accountability, and personal data protection. This achievement underscores the local government’s commitment to fostering a culture of legality, demonstrating that continuous education is vital for preventing corruption and safeguarding citizens' rights. By fully training its administrative structure, the administration proves that weakening transparency institutions erodes public trust and democracy. The initiative highlights how cooperation between various bodies and secretariats is essential for promoting legal culture and ensuring the effective exercise of human rights, moving beyond mere compliance to genuine civic engagement. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical role of institutional capacity building in maintaining transparent governance. It shows that robust open data practices depend on well-trained personnel who can manage information requests and protect privacy, thereby strengthening the infrastructure necessary for public accountability and democratic integrity.
