El Indec dio nuevos datos provisorios del Censo 2022: Argentina tiene 46.082.160 habitantes - Diario Panorama

Argentina releases provisional 2022 Census results, revealing a population slightly lower than initial estimates. This data update corrects previous figures, offering a more accurate demographic baseline for public policy. The findings detail household structures and gender distribution, including a small non-binary segment. These adjustments impact how the state understands social needs and resource allocation across provinces and departments. This release is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the cycle of data validation and correction in public statistics. Transparency in updating provisional figures ensures citizens and researchers rely on accurate, evolving information for analysis and decision-making.
