Censo 2022: hay 46.044.703 habitantes ¿y solo 2.962 en situación de calle? – 4SEMANAS.COM

Argentina has published provisional census results, revealing a growing population with a notable gender distribution favoring women and thousands of people in homeless situations. These early figures highlight shifts in demographic structure across provinces, offering immediate insights into housing needs and urban challenges, though officials caution they represent field trends rather than final counts. The survey marks a significant technological leap, with the majority of respondents participating digitally, surpassing expectations and demonstrating successful modernization of statistical operations. This shift toward electronic data collection improved territorial coverage and operational efficiency, setting a new standard for future censuses by leveraging technology to reach broader segments of society more effectively. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides granular, location-based statistics essential for evidence-based public policy and academic research. By making detailed demographic and housing information accessible, authorities empower citizens and organizations to monitor social indicators, analyze inequality, and hold institutions accountable, thereby strengthening transparency and community-driven governance through the widespread availability of verified statistical resources.
