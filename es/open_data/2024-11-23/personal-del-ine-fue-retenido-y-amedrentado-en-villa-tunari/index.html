Personal del INE fue retenido y amedrentado en Villa Tunari

National statistical authorities faced obstruction and intimidation while attempting to conduct household surveys in a politically sensitive Bolivian region. Authorities condemned this breach of institutional respect, emphasizing that collecting data is essential for evidence-based national planning and public policy development. This incident highlights the critical vulnerability of statistical integrity when local power structures challenge legal mandates for information gathering. Ensuring the freedom of movement and work for statisticians is paramount to maintaining reliable national datasets. The situation underscores the tension between political opposition and technical governance. Open data initiatives depend on the ability to collect unbiased information regardless of local political climate. Protecting these processes ensures transparency and supports democratic accountability through accurate, accessible statistics.
