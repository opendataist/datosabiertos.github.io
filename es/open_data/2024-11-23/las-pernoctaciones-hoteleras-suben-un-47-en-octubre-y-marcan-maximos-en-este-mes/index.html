Las pernoctaciones hoteleras suben un 4,7% en octubre y marcan máximos en este mes

October hotel stays reached a historic high, driven primarily by a significant surge in international tourism. While domestic travel remained relatively stable, the strong growth from foreign visitors highlights the country’s increasing appeal as a global destination. This trend suggests a robust recovery and expanding competitiveness in the international market. Revenue metrics also improved considerably, with hotel prices and average daily rates rising. This price increase, coupled with high occupancy levels, indicates strong demand and effective pricing strategies across the sector. The financial health of hotels appears strengthened, particularly in key tourist regions like the Balearic and Canary Islands, which saw the highest occupancy rates. This data is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent, official statistical series enable stakeholders to monitor economic trends accurately. By providing accessible metrics on occupancy and pricing, open government data supports evidence-based decision-making for businesses and policymakers aiming to optimize the tourism industry’s sustainability and growth.
