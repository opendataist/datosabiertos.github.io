Destacó IMSS en Primer Informe de Seguimiento de la Política de Transparencia, Gobierno Abierto y Datos Abiertos

The Mexican Social Security Institute (IMSS) achieved perfect compliance in Open Government standards, distinguishing itself among federal entities. This success reflects significant advancements in transparency protocols and web integration, demonstrating a strong institutional commitment to administrative efficiency and the strategic alignment of data practices with national development goals. Furthermore, the IMSS markedly exceeded the average compliance rate for open data usage across the public administration. By prioritizing these open data initiatives, the institution not only meets regulatory requirements but also actively promotes accountability and fights corruption, ensuring that public information remains accessible and useful for citizens and workers alike. This achievement is highly relevant to open_data as it exemplifies how robust data governance frameworks can be successfully implemented within large bureaucratic organizations. It serves as a model for other public institutions, illustrating that consistent adherence to transparency policies and the proactive sharing of data are essential tools for enhancing democratic oversight and institutional trust.
