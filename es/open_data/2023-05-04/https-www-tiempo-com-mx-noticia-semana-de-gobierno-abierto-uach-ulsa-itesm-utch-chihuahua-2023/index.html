Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Municipality of Chihuahua recognizes open government as the core of transparency, launching an international week to foster innovation and civic engagement. By integrating artificial intelligence and encouraging youth participation, the administration aims to prevent institutional opacity. This approach emphasizes flexibility and active listening, inviting university students to propose solutions that directly benefit the citizenry rather than imposing rigid policies. The events focus on critical themes such as digital contracting, anti-corruption measures, and inclusive participation mechanisms. Speakers from international organizations and local institutions will address how open data and accessible information systems can modernize governance. These discussions highlight the necessity of adapting to technological changes to maintain public trust and ensure that government processes remain accountable and responsive to societal needs. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates a practical commitment to transparency through actionable digital transformation. It illustrates how open government frameworks rely on the continuous flow of accessible information and citizen feedback. By prioritizing digitalization and ethical accountability, the project serves as a model for leveraging data to enhance democratic participation and reduce corruption, showing that open data is not just a technical requirement but a fundamental tool for building trust.
