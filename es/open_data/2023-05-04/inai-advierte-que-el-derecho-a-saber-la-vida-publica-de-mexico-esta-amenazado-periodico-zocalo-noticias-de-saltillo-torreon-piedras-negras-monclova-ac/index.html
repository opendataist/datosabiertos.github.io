INAI advierte que el derecho a saber la vida pública de México está amenazado | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INAI warns that Mexico’s right to access public information is under severe threat, potentially blocking society’s right to truth if the transparency body is eliminated. This role is crucial for defending citizens against authorities and exposing corruption, ensuring governance remains accountable rather than subservient to political power. The absence of the INAI would represent a significant democratic regression, pushing the country back toward authoritarianism. Without this independent oversight, transparency standards erode, leaving citizens without the legal mechanisms necessary to scrutinize public administration and demand truth from their leaders. Currently, unresolved complaints highlight the urgency of maintaining the agency’s functionality. This situation is vital for open data as it underscores the necessity of independent institutions to enforce transparency laws, ensuring public information remains accessible and verifiable for all citizens.
