PortCastelló estrena portal de transparencia

The Port Authority of Castellón has launched a transparency portal that achieves nearly full compliance with official standards, making it the most transparent entity in Spain’s port system. This initiative fulfills legal requirements while providing reusable open data, thereby empowering citizens and enhancing the organization’s reputation. By prioritizing accessible information formats, the port not only meets European and national directives but also fosters public trust through open governance. This demonstrates how rigorous adherence to transparency laws can significantly improve institutional credibility. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates the practical implementation of legal mandates into user-friendly digital infrastructure. It highlights the value of structured, reusable public information in promoting accountability and civic engagement.
