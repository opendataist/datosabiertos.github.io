Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Monterrey government is actively engaging private innovators to modernize public administration through its SIGA Award. This initiative seeks to transform municipal services by identifying technological solutions that address specific bureaucratic challenges, thereby fostering a more efficient and digitally advanced urban environment. Two primary objectives drive this competition: eliminating paper-based workflows and enhancing the city’s transparency portal. The focus on digital signatures aims to reduce administrative delays and resource consumption, while the push for proactive transparency demands that public data be accessible, reusable, and machine-readable. This shift prioritizes citizen-centric access to information over mere regulatory compliance. This effort is significant for the open data community as it mandates the use of open formats and machine-readable metadata. By incentivizing the release of granular, reusable data, the city promotes the principles of openness and interoperability. Ultimately, the award serves as a practical application of open government values, encouraging startups to develop tools that make public information truly accessible and usable for society.
