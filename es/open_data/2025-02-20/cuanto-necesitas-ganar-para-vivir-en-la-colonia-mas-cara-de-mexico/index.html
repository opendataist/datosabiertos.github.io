¿Cuánto necesitas ganar para vivir en la colonia más cara de México?

The article highlights extreme economic disparity in Mexico, where a tiny fraction of the population enjoys high income levels. It details Polanco as an exclusive enclave in Mexico City, illustrating the immense financial barriers to entry through high property and rental costs. This data underscores the significant gap between average citizens and the elite, revealing the real cost of living in privileged areas. This information is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible statistics from institutions like INEGI. By making socioeconomic classifications and housing market data publicly available, governments empower citizens to understand structural inequalities. Open data allows researchers and the public to analyze these disparities critically. Ultimately, the narrative emphasizes that without public access to precise economic indicators, understanding the true extent of wealth concentration remains difficult. Providing clear, verifiable data helps bridge the information gap, fostering greater accountability and enabling informed public discourse on social justice and resource distribution within the country.
