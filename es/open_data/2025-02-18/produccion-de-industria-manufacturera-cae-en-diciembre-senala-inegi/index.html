Producción de industria manufacturera cae en diciembre, señala Inegi

The latest manufacturing survey reveals a concerning contraction in physical production volume, accompanied by a sharp decline in non-dependent workforce hours. While overall employment levels remained stable, the reduction in flexible labor indicates a sector under pressure, reflecting broader economic challenges that could influence labor policies in the coming year. The textile and leather industries have suffered the most significant setbacks, driven largely by unfair competition from subsidized foreign imports. In response, the government has implemented increased tariffs to protect domestic production, aiming to stabilize these vulnerable sectors against external market distortions that have severely impacted local manufacturing capacity and employment. This data is crucial for open data advocates as it highlights the tangible economic impacts of trade imbalances and the necessity for transparent, accessible statistics. By making such detailed employment and production metrics publicly available, researchers and policymakers can better analyze the efficacy of protectionist measures and advocate for data-driven decisions that ensure fair labor practices and economic resilience in the global trade landscape.
