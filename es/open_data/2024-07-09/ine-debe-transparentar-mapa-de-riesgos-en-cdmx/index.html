INE debe transparentar mapa de riesgos en CDMX

The INAI ordered the INE to publish its electoral risk maps for Mexico City, rejecting claims that the information did not exist. This decision mandates transparency in identifying unsafe zones for polling stations and security operations during voting. This ruling highlights the critical importance of proactive open data in democratic processes. By forcing the disclosure of operational security details, it ensures public trust and allows citizens to monitor electoral safety measures effectively. Ultimately, this case reinforces the principle that all public entities must make their administrative data accessible. It sets a precedent for treating internal risk assessments as public information, thereby strengthening accountability and citizen oversight in future elections.
