La Nación / Red Ciudadana apoya y respalda la sanción de la Ley de Transparencia de las ONG

A network of organizations supporting childhood and family interests has endorsed a bill mandating transparency for non-governmental organizations. They argue that many groups claiming to represent civil society actually serve foreign political and economic agendas rather than national well-being. The coalition contends that these NGOs promote specific ideological agendas, including changes to family structures and gender policies, often without accountability. They believe these groups operate with impunity while hypocritically demanding transparency from others, thereby undermining national sovereignty and social cohesion. This is relevant to open data because it highlights the ongoing debate surrounding the necessity of public transparency for civil society organizations. The article emphasizes the demand for data visibility to ensure these entities are accountable to the public and to verify that their operations align with national interests rather than hidden external agendas.
