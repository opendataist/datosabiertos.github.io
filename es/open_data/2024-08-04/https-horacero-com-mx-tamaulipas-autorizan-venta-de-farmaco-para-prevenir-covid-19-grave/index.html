Mexico has authorized Paxlovid as the first drug to prevent hospitalization and death from Covid-19. This approval ensures rigorous quality and safety standards are met, though strict medical prescription remains mandatory to prevent misuse and ensure patient safety. Health authorities emphasize that this treatment does not replace vaccines and warns against indiscriminate use. Proper medical supervision is crucial to avoid health risks associated with incorrect administration or irregular commercialization, which could endanger public health. This update is vital for open data initiatives as it highlights the critical role of transparent regulatory decisions in public health infrastructure. Accessible information about authorized treatments ensures equitable healthcare delivery and helps combat misinformation, fostering trust in official medical guidance and supporting evidence-based policy implementation.
