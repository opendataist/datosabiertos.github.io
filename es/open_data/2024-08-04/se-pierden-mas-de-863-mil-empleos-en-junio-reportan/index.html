Se pierden más de 863 mil empleos en junio, reportan

Mexico experienced a net loss of formal and informal jobs in June, raising the unemployment rate. This decline reflects broader economic shifts impacting sectors beyond standard industrial statistics, highlighting data complexities in tracking official versus informal labor markets. The rise in unemployment underscores the fragility of the job market, suggesting that losses extend to public sector roles. Consequently, understanding these dynamics requires distinguishing between reported figures and underlying structural changes in employment. This context is vital for open_data initiatives, as it demonstrates the need for transparent, granular data to accurately interpret labor trends. Without clear access to disaggregated statistics, assessing true economic health remains challenging for researchers and policymakers alike.
