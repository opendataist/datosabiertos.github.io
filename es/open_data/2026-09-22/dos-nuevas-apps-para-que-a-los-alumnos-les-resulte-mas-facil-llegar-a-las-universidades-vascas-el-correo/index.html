Dos nuevas apps para que a los alumnos les resulte más fácil llegar a las universidades vascas | El Correo

Student developers transformed public mobility challenges into functional applications by prioritizing user-centric design over technical complexity. One team integrated comprehensive local data, including translations and points of interest, to assist international students navigating unfamiliar cities. Another focused on streamlining the user journey by eliminating intermediate steps, leveraging efficient routing engines to deliver immediate solutions without requiring manual map checks. These initiatives highlight the critical role of open data in solving real-world urban problems. Although the data is publicly available, its true value is realized only when citizens actively utilize it to create practical tools. As emphasized by city officials, unused public data remains ineffective; it is the innovation driven by open access that converts raw information into tangible benefits for society. This story is vital to open data because it demonstrates that data alone is insufficient without human creativity and application. By turning abstract datasets into user-friendly mobility services, these projects validate the open data principle: accessibility must lead to utility. It proves that when individuals can freely access and manipulate public information, they can drive significant social improvement and bridge the gap between academic learning and community needs.
