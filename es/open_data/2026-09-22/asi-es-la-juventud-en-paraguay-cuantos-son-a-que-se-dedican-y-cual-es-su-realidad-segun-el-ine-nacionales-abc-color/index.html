Así es la juventud en Paraguay: cuántos son, a qué se dedican y cuál es su realidad, según el INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

This dataset provides a comprehensive demographic profile of Paraguay’s youth, highlighting key disparities in education, employment, and poverty. Such granular information is vital for open data initiatives, enabling researchers and policymakers to analyze social trends and identify vulnerable groups needing targeted intervention. By making these statistics accessible, the INE supports evidence-based decision-making. The data reveals that while most young people work, only slightly over half attend school, and rural areas face higher poverty rates, emphasizing the need for equitable development strategies.
