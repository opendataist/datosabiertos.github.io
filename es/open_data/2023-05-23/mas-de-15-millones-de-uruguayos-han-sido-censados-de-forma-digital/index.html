Más de 1,5 millones de uruguayos han sido censados de forma digital

The INE highlights the successful digitalization of the national census, noting high citizen participation via online platforms. This achievement allows households that completed the process to receive a completion code, streamlining the subsequent phase. The system’s stability under heavy user load demonstrates the viability of digital infrastructure for large-scale statistical operations. This efficiency reduces the burden on the next stage, where enumerators will visit addresses in areas with lower initial participation rates. This case is highly relevant to open_data as it validates the potential for modern, user-centric data collection methods. It illustrates how leveraging digital tools can improve data quality and accessibility, setting a precedent for future transparent and efficient government data initiatives.
