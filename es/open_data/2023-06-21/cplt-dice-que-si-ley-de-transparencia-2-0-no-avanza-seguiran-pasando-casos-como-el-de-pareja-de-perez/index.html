CPLT dice que si Ley de Transparencia 2.0 no avanza seguirán pasando casos como el de pareja de Pérez

The case reveals that state-funded foundations operate outside transparency and audit regulations, allowing conflicts of interest to persist unchecked. This highlights a critical gap in open data governance where public money lacks visible oversight mechanisms. The article underscores the urgent need for legislative updates, specifically the stalled Transparency Law 2.0, to enforce accountability. Without these changes, reliance on journalistic exposure remains insufficient for ensuring public sector integrity. This situation is vital to open data discussions as it illustrates how legal exemptions hinder the flow of accountability information. It demonstrates that technical openness is ineffective without corresponding legal mandates to regulate private entities handling public funds.
