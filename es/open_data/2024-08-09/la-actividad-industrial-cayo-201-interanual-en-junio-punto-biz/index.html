La actividad industrial cayó 20,1% interanual en junio | Punto Biz

The article highlights a significant contraction in Argentina’s manufacturing sector during the first half of 2024, with no signs of recovery. All sixteen manufacturing divisions experienced year-over-year declines, reflecting a broad-based economic downturn that impacts productivity and employment metrics. The construction industry faced an even sharper decline, recording a drop twice as severe as that of manufacturing over the same period. Although a slight monthly improvement occurred in June, the overall trend remains negative, signaling deeper structural challenges in these core economic pillars. Transparent reporting allows researchers and citizens to analyze economic health, demand accountability from institutions, and understand the real-world impact of policy decisions on industrial output.
