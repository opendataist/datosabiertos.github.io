Inflación sigue bajo control

The Consumer Price Index reflects inflation within the central bank’s target range, indicating that monetary expectations align with current economic reality despite rising food costs. This data highlights significant price disparities between urban and rural populations, revealing how essential goods impact households differently across geographical sectors. Such transparency is vital for open data initiatives, as it empowers citizens to monitor inflation trends and understand the real economic pressure on their daily lives.
