Abordarán protección de datos personales en foro de transparencia

The upcoming transparency forum prioritizes personal data protection, aiming to position Juárez as an icon of open government. By connecting institutional actors with civil society, the event seeks to foster a robust dialogue on how transparency intersects with individual privacy rights in the digital age. Experts will discuss the value of personal data versus social media engagement, examining the delicate balance between public accountability and private information. These conversations highlight the critical need for clear frameworks that protect citizens while maintaining governmental openness. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it addresses the ethical management of personal information. It underscores that true transparency requires not just releasing data, but doing so responsibly. This ensures that open government practices respect privacy, building public trust in digital governance and data handling.
