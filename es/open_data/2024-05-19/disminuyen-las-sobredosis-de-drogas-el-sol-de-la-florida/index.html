Disminuyen las sobredosis de drogas - El Sol de la Florida

US drug overdose deaths showed a slight decline, marking a potential shift in public health trends. While opioid-related fatalities decreased, deaths involving stimulants rose, and states decriminalizing possession experienced significant increases, highlighting complex local dynamics. This data underscores the importance of transparent, granular open data for evaluating policy impacts and guiding equitable health interventions.
