El presidente del Senado, Alejandro Armenta, propone desaparecer al Inai | HidrocalidoDigital.com

The Senate president proposes extinguishing the INAI and absorbing its functions into the Public Function Secretariat. This move aims to consolidate oversight under a single entity, arguing that shared objectives justify merging transparency and data protection duties with anti-corruption efforts. Such centralization fundamentally alters the independent oversight model, risking the loss of autonomy in handling public information requests. By transferring these powers to an executive body, the structural balance required for effective government accountability is potentially compromised. This development is highly relevant to open data because it challenges the institutional independence crucial for transparent governance. Consolidating authority within the executive branch may undermine public trust and hinder the free flow of information, directly impacting the principles of open data and civic access.
