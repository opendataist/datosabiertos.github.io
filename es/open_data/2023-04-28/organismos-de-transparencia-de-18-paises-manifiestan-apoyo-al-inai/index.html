Organismos de Transparencia de 18 países manifiestan apoyo al Inai

The Ibero-American Transparency Network strongly supports Mexico’s National Institute for Transparency, emphasizing that undermining its independence threatens democratic consolidation and human rights protections across the region. This alliance highlights how safeguarding these institutions is crucial for maintaining established democratic standards and trust in public governance. The institute is recognized as a regional benchmark for transparency and data protection, directly contributing to the realization of sustainable development goals. Its role extends beyond national borders, illustrating how robust access to information frameworks support broader international agendas and reinforce the rule of law through accountable institutions. These developments are vital for open data because they demonstrate that transparent governance relies on autonomous institutions. Protecting the integrity of such bodies ensures the reliable flow of public information, which is foundational for civic engagement, accountability, and the effective implementation of open data initiatives globally.
