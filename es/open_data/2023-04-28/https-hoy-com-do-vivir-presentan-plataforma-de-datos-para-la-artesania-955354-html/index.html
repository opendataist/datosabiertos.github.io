Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

FODEARTE launched a national open data platform to map Dominican artisans, significantly improving public access to local craftsmanship. By centralizing censuses and contact details, the initiative bridges the knowledge gap between creators and consumers, fostering greater visibility and market integration for traditional crafts. The database currently features detailed profiles of over a thousand artisans across diverse sectors, with plans to expand continuously until all national practitioners are recorded. This living resource ensures comprehensive geographic location and demographic information, empowering stakeholders to support the artisanal community more effectively. This project is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible information can drive cultural preservation and economic development. It serves as a model for using public datasets to empower marginalized sectors, proving that open access to civic information directly enhances community engagement and sector growth.
