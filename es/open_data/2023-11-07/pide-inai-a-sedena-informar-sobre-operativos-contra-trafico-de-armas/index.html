Pide INAI a Sedena informar sobre operativos contra tráfico de armas

The INAI ordered the Sedena to disclose details on its operations to curb illegal arms trafficking after initially withholding this data. This mandates transparency regarding coordinated security efforts and the identification of smuggling routes, emphasizing that openness is essential for verifying institutional effectiveness and public accountability. The article highlights that illicit weapon flows directly fuel Mexico’s violence crisis, linking organized crime and high homicide rates to these imports. By exposing these patterns, open data enables civil society to analyze the root causes of insecurity and hold authorities responsible for managing security infrastructure effectively. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency laws compel the release of sensitive security statistics. It underscores the necessity of accessible government information to combat impunity, allowing researchers and citizens to track trends in illegal trade and demand evidence-based policy changes to improve national safety.
