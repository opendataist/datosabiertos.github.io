Invitan a participar en el 'Premio SLP de Gobierno Abierto 2024'

The article highlights the closing of registrations for an open government award in San Luis Potosí, aiming to incentivize projects that enhance transparency through innovative information technology. It specifically showcases a successful initiative for adult literacy where QR codes bridged language gaps, demonstrating how digital tools can effectively serve marginalized communities. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the practical application of accessible, machine-readable formats to improve public services and civic inclusion. By encouraging participants to submit initiatives in government and civil society categories, the contest promotes accountability and citizen engagement. Ultimately, it underscores the importance of leveraging technology to make governmental processes more transparent and responsive to diverse societal needs.
