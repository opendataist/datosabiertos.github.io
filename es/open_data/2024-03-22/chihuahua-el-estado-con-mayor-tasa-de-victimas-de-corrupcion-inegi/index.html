Chihuahua, el estado con mayor tasa de víctimas de corrupción: Inegi

Chihuahua leads Mexico in corruption victimization rates, yet records the lowest average cost per victim. This contrast highlights significant regional disparities in how corruption manifests and impacts citizens differently across states. Such data is vital for open governance, revealing hidden costs and enabling targeted anti-corruption strategies based on transparent, granular statistical evidence.
