Producción láctea: estancada hace más de 20 años, pero con potencial intacto | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

The article concludes that Argentina’s dairy sector possesses significant untapped potential, capable of dramatically increasing both domestic consumption and export revenues by 2030. Despite a history of stagnation compared to global growth rates, the industry can achieve substantial expansion if supported by a stable macroeconomic framework that prioritizes long-term confidence and investment. Relevance to open_data lies in the critical need for transparent, accessible, and consistent statistical information to inform these policy decisions. The current low productivity and reliance on disparate data sources, such as those from OCLA, highlight how fragmented or insufficient data hinders effective sector planning. Open, high-quality datasets are essential for accurately tracking production metrics, validating growth models, and ensuring that regulatory frameworks are evidence-based rather than speculative. To realize this potential, the country must shift focus from short-term political disputes to establishing clear, enduring rules that encourage investment in value-added activities. By leveraging robust data to guide macroeconomic equilibrium and fiscal incentives, stakeholders can create an environment where dairy and other agricultural sectors thrive. This approach ensures that resources are allocated efficiently, fostering sustainable economic growth and reducing informal practices through transparency and data-driven accountability.
