Esta es la previsión de la Aemet para el otoño tras el sexto verano más caluroso desde que hay registros

The article concludes that the 2024 summer was the sixth hottest on record, reinforcing the undeniable trajectory of global warming. By highlighting that nine of the ten warmest summers have occurred in the 21st century, the report underscores a clear climatic shift where extreme heat has become the new normal, a trend further evidenced by the concentration of recent high temperatures within the last decade. Looking ahead, autumn is projected to be warmer than average across most of the country, with significantly reduced rainfall expected in western and southern regions. This forecast aligns with the historical context that cold autumns are increasingly rare, having not occurred since 2010. The combination of persistent heat and potential aridity in specific areas suggests a continuation of the anomalous weather patterns observed in recent years. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the critical role of transparent, accessible, and long-term meteorological records. Publicly available datasets from the Aemet allow researchers and citizens to verify trends, understand climate risks, and make informed decisions. The clear visualization of warming patterns through open historical records facilitates better public awareness and supports evidence-based policy-making regarding climate adaptation.
