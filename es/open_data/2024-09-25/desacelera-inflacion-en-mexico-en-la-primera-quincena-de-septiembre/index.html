Desacelera inflación en México en la primera quincena de septiembre

Mexico’s inflation decelerated significantly in early September, reaching a historically low annual rate that fell below analyst consensus. This notable slowdown is primarily driven by substantial price decreases in fresh produce and fuel, signaling a favorable trend for consumer purchasing power and economic stability. The underlying inflation index also recorded a decline, reflecting reduced volatility in core goods and services prices. This broader cooling suggests that monetary pressures are easing across various sectors of the economy, moving beyond just volatile food and energy items. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how publicly accessible statistical indicators from institutions like INEGI enable transparency in economic monitoring. Such open datasets allow analysts and citizens to independently verify trends, fostering trust in official statistics and supporting evidence-based decision-making regarding market expectations and policy impacts.
