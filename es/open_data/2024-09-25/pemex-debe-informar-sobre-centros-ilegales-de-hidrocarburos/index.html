Pemex debe informar sobre centros ilegales de hidrocarburos

The National Institute for Transparency ordered Pemex to disclose detailed data on illegal hydrocarbon storage sites, including capacities, detainee counts, and casualties. This decision mandates transparency regarding state efforts against fuel theft, correcting prior opacity in reporting these security operations. This ruling is crucial for open_data as it establishes that public oil companies must release granular operational statistics. It reinforces the principle that citizens have the right to access specific metrics on law enforcement outcomes, ensuring accountability through standardized, machine-readable formats like Excel rather than opaque summaries.
