The article highlights a significant deterioration in household purchasing power, revealing that rising food prices have substantially increased the income threshold required to avoid poverty and indigence. This trend underscores the severe impact of inflation on basic living standards, particularly for families, demonstrating how economic pressures directly restrict access to essential goods and services. The disparity in price increases between food and other goods further complicates household budgeting, with food items driving the majority of the inflationary pressure. By detailing the specific components of these baskets and the factors influencing their costs, the report illustrates the complex interplay between agricultural conditions, market dynamics, and consumer prices, offering a granular view of economic hardship. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it exemplifies how official statistics provide transparent, quantifiable insights into social welfare. Open access to such detailed inflation and basket metrics enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to verify government reports, analyze inequality trends, and hold institutions accountable, thereby fostering a more informed and engaged society capable of scrutinizing economic policies.
