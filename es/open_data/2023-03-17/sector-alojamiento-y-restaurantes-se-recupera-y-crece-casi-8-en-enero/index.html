Sector alojamiento y restaurantes se recupera y crece casi 8% en enero

The Peruvian hospitality sector demonstrates resilience amid broader economic contraction, highlighting the uneven impact of sociopolitical instability on specific industries. While overall national production declined, the lodging and restaurant sector expanded, driven largely by increased domestic demand for dining rather than international tourism. Restaurant revenues surged due to seasonal promotions, extended hours, and delivery services that attracted local families during holidays. In contrast, the accommodation subsector contracted as social conflicts and roadblocks deterred tourists, causing significant financial losses in the broader tourism industry. This dynamic illustrates the critical role of granular, sector-specific open data in policy-making. By isolating performance metrics, analysts can distinguish between localized demand shifts and macroeconomic shocks. Such transparency allows stakeholders to understand how political unrest affects diverse economic activities differently, fostering more targeted and effective recovery strategies.
