IVAI apoya a ayuntamientos rurales para que cumplan con Ley de Transparencia

The IVAI assists small municipalities by providing a model portal, ensuring they meet transparency obligations despite infrastructure limitations. This support helps local authorities overcome technical barriers to publish public information effectively. This initiative is vital for open data, as it democratizes access to public records and promotes accountability across underserved regions.
