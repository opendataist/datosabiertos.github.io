Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article outlines the procedural steps for citizens to request public information through Mexico’s National Transparency Platform. It details the necessary registration requirements and the systematic process for filling out request forms, including selecting institutions and specifying information needs. This guide empowers users to effectively engage with the transparency system by providing clear instructions on how to navigate the interface and submit valid requests. Accessing this data is crucial for fostering accountability and enabling informed public discourse. By democratizing access to government records, the platform supports open data principles that promote transparency and reduce information asymmetry between state authorities and civil society. The ability to track request status ensures that institutions remain answerable for their responses within legal timeframes. This resource is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it bridges the gap between data availability and user accessibility. It demonstrates how technical platforms can facilitate citizen participation in governance. By simplifying the mechanism for obtaining public records, such tools encourage the reuse and analysis of government data, thereby strengthening democratic oversight and enhancing the practical utility of open information ecosystems.
