AMLO celebra datos del Inegi sobre inflación y crecimiento económico | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The article highlights Mexico’s recent economic improvements, noting that inflation has declined below market expectations while the economy continues to grow. This trend supports the government’s stance that wage increases can occur without triggering further price hikes, reinforcing their policy approach amidst broader global comparisons. These figures are crucial for open data because they demonstrate the vital role of transparent, timely statistical releases by institutions like INEGI. Accessible data allows citizens, researchers, and analysts to verify government claims and assess economic health independently. Ultimately, this case underscores how open statistical data empowers public accountability. By making growth and inflation metrics readily available, the government enables a more informed civil society capable of engaging in meaningful economic discourse, which is foundational to democratic transparency and trust in public institutions.
