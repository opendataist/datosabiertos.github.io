Bolivia nears full volunteer readiness for its upcoming census, highlighting the critical role of civic participation in generating accurate demographic data. The statistical institute emphasizes that widespread involvement ensures comprehensive coverage, which is essential for reliable open data. Public engagement directly impacts data quality, as missing volunteers create gaps in the census. Achieving the goal of 100% participation allows for a complete dataset, reducing bias and enhancing the transparency and usability of the resulting information. This process underscores the importance of public sector transparency. Successful censuses provide foundational open data that supports evidence-based policy-making, allowing citizens and researchers to access and utilize detailed national statistics for societal improvement.
