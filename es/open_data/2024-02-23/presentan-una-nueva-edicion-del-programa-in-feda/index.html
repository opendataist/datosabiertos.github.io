Presentan una nueva edición del programa IN-FEDA

The IN-FEDA 2024 program represents a strategic initiative to empower regional businesses through advanced training in innovation, digitalization, and internationalization. By expanding its reach beyond the capital to various provincial delegations, the program ensures broader accessibility to critical knowledge. This decentralization strategy reinforces the commitment to supporting local enterprises with practical tools for navigating complex global markets, ensuring that growth opportunities are not limited by geographic location. Key themes include leveraging artificial intelligence for operational cost reduction, utilizing blockchain for transparent transaction management, and protecting intellectual property rights abroad. These topics address immediate business challenges, offering actionable insights for enhancing competitiveness. The focus on tangible benefits, such as improved traceability and asset protection, highlights the program's emphasis on applying modern technologies to real-world commercial scenarios, thereby fostering a more resilient and adaptable business ecosystem. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it underscores the growing necessity for transparency, interoperability, and secure information exchange in international trade. Blockchain’s decentralized nature promotes trust through open verification mechanisms, while the protection of intellectual property relies on robust, accessible data registries. Furthermore, the program’s success in facilitating international cooperation aligns with open data principles by encouraging the sharing of market insights and best practices, ultimately contributing to a more open and collaborative economic environment.
