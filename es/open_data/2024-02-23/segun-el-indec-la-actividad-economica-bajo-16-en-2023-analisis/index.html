Según el INDEC, la actividad económica bajó 1,6% en 2023 | Análisis

Argentina’s economic activity contracted in December and throughout the year, driven significantly by declines in manufacturing and commerce. Conversely, agricultural and fishing sectors showed resilience, highlighting a divergent recovery where primary industries outperformed industrial and retail sectors. This report is relevant to open_data because it exemplifies the critical need for transparent, accessible, and frequent official statistical releases. Accurate macroeconomic indicators like the EMAE are essential for researchers and citizens to monitor economic health, fostering accountability and informed public debate on data governance and policy effectiveness.
