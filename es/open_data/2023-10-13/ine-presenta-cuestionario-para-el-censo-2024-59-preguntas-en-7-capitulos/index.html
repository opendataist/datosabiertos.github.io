INE presenta cuestionario para el Censo 2024: 59 Preguntas en 7 Capítulos

Bolivia’s latest census questionnaire introduces targeted questions on health insurance and employment, aiming to enhance the precision of individual data. This granular information is essential for crafting effective public policies across central, departmental, and municipal levels, ensuring government decisions are grounded in accurate, up-to-date demographic realities rather than estimates. By integrating new metrics on indigenous identity and specific health indicators, the census shifts from mere population counting to a tool for social protection. This evolution allows authorities to design preventive health policies and cultural recognition strategies that truly reflect the country’s diversity, moving beyond traditional demographics to capture the nuanced lived experiences of citizens. For open data advocates, this initiative highlights the critical role of standardized, accessible demographic datasets in driving transparency and accountability. When governments release detailed, high-quality census data, it empowers researchers, civil society, and developers to analyze social trends, evaluate policy impacts, and foster informed public debate, thereby strengthening the democratic use of statistical evidence in governance and community development.
