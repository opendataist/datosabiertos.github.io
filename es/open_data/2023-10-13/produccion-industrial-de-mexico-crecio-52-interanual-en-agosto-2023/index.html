Producción industrial de México creció 5,2% interanual en agosto 2023

Mexico’s industrial output expanded in August, driven primarily by a strong surge in construction and energy sectors, despite a slight contraction in manufacturing. This mixed performance highlights the uneven recovery across different economic activities, with infrastructure and utility industries outperforming traditional manufacturing lines. Over the first eight months of the year, industrial production maintained a positive annual growth trend, supported by broad-based increases in construction, energy, and mining. These figures align with broader national economic indicators, suggesting a steady, albeit modest, recovery trajectory following the significant post-pandemic contraction experienced in previous years. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of accessible, timely, and granular statistical information for analyzing regional economic resilience. Transparent access to such detailed sectoral performance metrics enables researchers and policymakers to better understand recovery dynamics and make informed decisions about future industrial strategies.
