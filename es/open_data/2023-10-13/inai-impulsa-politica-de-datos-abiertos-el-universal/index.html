Inai impulsa política de datos abiertos | El Universal

The launch of Datacon 2023 marks a pivotal step toward implementing Mexico’s national open data policy across all government levels. Organized by the INAI and the National Transparency System, this itinerant conference aims to transform public information into accessible open data, fostering collaboration and shared knowledge among obligated entities nationwide. This initiative represents a significant shift in transparency efforts, emphasizing collective problem-solving rather than isolated actions. By uniting forces, the government seeks to address societal challenges more effectively, ensuring that open data becomes a robust tool for improving governance and public service delivery through coordinated inter-institutional efforts. This development is crucial for open data because it establishes a standardized, national framework for information accessibility. It moves beyond isolated projects to create a cohesive ecosystem where citizens can freely access comprehensive country data. This systematic approach enhances accountability and empowers communities with the information needed to engage meaningfully in democratic processes and civic oversight.
