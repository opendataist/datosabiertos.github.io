Massa sigue batiendo récords: 12,7% de inflación mensual y 138% anual

September’s inflation reached 12.7%, significantly exceeding private sector forecasts and highlighting severe economic instability. This surge, driven primarily by essential costs like food and clothing, underscores the persistent erosion of purchasing power across all regions. The data, released just before crucial presidential elections, reveals that high annual and cumulative inflation rates will likely persist regardless of the political outcome. Credit agencies predict even sharper increases in the coming year, indicating that the current trajectory is deeply structural rather than temporary. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible, and timely statistical releases. Reliable public data allows citizens and analysts to verify official figures against independent estimates, fostering accountability and enabling informed civic engagement during critical democratic processes.
