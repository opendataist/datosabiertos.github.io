La inflación en Estados Unidos subió por segundo mes consecutivo

The article highlights persistent inflationary pressures in the United States, driven by a robust labor market that sustains consumer demand. This economic resilience complicates the Federal Reserve's efforts to stabilize prices, potentially necessitating further interest rate hikes to combat rising costs. Consequently, market participants face increased uncertainty regarding monetary policy direction and its broader impact on economic stability.
