Inai urge a Monreal la designación de comisionados faltantes

The Mexican Transparency Institute warns that without appointing missing commissioners by late March, it will lose its legal quorum to function. This legislative gap threatens the agency's ability to conduct sessions and fulfill its mandates, prompting imminent legal action against the Senate to force the necessary appointments. This situation is critical for open data governance because the institute’s paralysis directly impedes its oversight of transparency laws and personal data protection. Without a complete plenum, the body cannot effectively enforce access to information or hold public entities accountable, creating a dangerous vacuum in regulatory oversight. Consequently, the potential legal confrontation highlights the fragility of institutional checks when political appointments stall. It underscores how administrative delays can jeopardize citizen rights, emphasizing the urgent need for political consensus to maintain the functional independence required for robust open data and transparency frameworks.
