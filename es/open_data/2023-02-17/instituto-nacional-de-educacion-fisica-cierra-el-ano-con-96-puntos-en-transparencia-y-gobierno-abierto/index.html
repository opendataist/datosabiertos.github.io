Instituto Nacional de Educación Física cierra el año con 96 puntos en Transparencia y Gobierno Abierto

The INEFI achieved high marks for transparency, demonstrating how public institutions can effectively manage open data to build trust. This success highlights the critical role of ethical governance in improving administrative quality and citizen services. Such compliance ensures that citizens have reliable access to public information through standardized portals. It reinforces the legal framework that mandates openness, making government operations more accountable and efficient for everyone. This case is relevant to open data because it proves that robust data practices directly enhance the integrity of public administration. By prioritizing transparency, organizations not only meet legal requirements but also actively serve the community’s right to know.
