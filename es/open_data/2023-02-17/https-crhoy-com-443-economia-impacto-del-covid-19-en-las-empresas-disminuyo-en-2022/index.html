Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The pandemic severely impacted manufacturing and commerce, primarily through reduced revenues and increased operational costs, while minimal business benefits were realized. This highlights the critical need for open data to transparently track economic shocks and identify sectors most vulnerable to external crises, enabling better policy design. Although the vast majority of firms received no aid, those that did benefited mainly from financial debt adjustments and tax postponements. Transparent reporting of these interventions is essential to evaluate the effectiveness of support mechanisms, ensuring that public resources are allocated efficiently to sustain business continuity during emergencies. Future economic outlooks remain cautiously stable, with most companies expecting to maintain their workforce and activity levels. Monitoring these perceptions through accessible datasets allows researchers and policymakers to understand business resilience over time, fostering informed decisions that strengthen the economic ecosystem against future health or market disruptions.
