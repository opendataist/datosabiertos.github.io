El INDEC trabaja en una actualización del índice para medir la inflación

The INDEC is updating its Consumer Price Index by replacing an outdated two-decade-old basket with recent household expenditure data. This shift ensures inflation measurements accurately reflect current consumption patterns, addressing significant changes in how populations spend on goods and services rather than relying on historical anomalies. Crucially, the new methodology has been technically reviewed and approved by the International Monetary Fund and provincial authorities. This international validation aims to eliminate doubt regarding statistical neutrality, ensuring the index’s credibility for both domestic stakeholders and global financial institutions that rely on precise economic indicators. This update is vital for open data because it enhances the transparency and reliability of public statistics used for poverty lines and national accounts. Accurate, openly accessible data is essential for designing effective public policies and allowing citizens to trust the economic reality portrayed by official government figures.
