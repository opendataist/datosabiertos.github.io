La población gallega residente en extranjero crece pero la Xunta ve una “nueva realidad migratoria”

The demographic landscape of Galicians abroad is shifting, marked by fewer direct emigrants but a growing total population due to local-born descendants. This structural change transforms the diaspora into a crucial ally for combating Galicia’s internal demographic decline, offering a strategic resource beyond immediate borders. This evolution highlights the value of accurate, open population data in revealing nuanced migration trends. By distinguishing between emigrants and their descendants, authorities can better understand long-term community resilience and the success of repatriation initiatives like Galicia Retorna, which has facilitated significant returns. Such detailed statistical transparency is essential for open data ecosystems, enabling policymakers to identify hidden strengths in dispersed populations. Recognizing these demographic shifts allows for more effective strategies that leverage global connections to address local challenges, demonstrating how accessible data drives informed social and economic planning.
