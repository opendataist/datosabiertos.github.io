Se inician capacitaciones para probarla ENEIC

The National Statistics Institute is training surveyors for the ENEIC pilot, which will generate quarterly data on labor market evolution. This initiative aims to improve the frequency and accuracy of employment statistics, filling critical gaps in current official records. By refining field methods and updating geographic data, the survey ensures more precise mapping of households and businesses. This methodological rigor is essential for producing reliable, timely economic indicators that reflect real-time labor dynamics. This effort is vital for open data initiatives as it commits the state to releasing high-frequency, granular labor metrics. Transparent access to such detailed, regular datasets empowers researchers and citizens to analyze economic trends with greater precision and accountability.
