INE aclara que no habrá multa para quienes no sean censados y reitera que el día del Censo no estarán abiertas ni tiendas de barrio

The INE director clarified that individuals who do not participate in the census will not face fines, directly refuting earlier rumors. However, strict public health restrictions remain in place across Bolivia to ensure the census proceeds smoothly and safely. Authorities emphasize that staying home is mandatory, with local businesses and recreational activities suspended for the event. These measures are enforced under specific government orders valid for several days, aiming to prevent unnecessary movement and protect public health during this critical national exercise. The police are tasked with maintaining order and ensuring compliance with these temporary regulations. This information is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accurate, universally accessible communication regarding data collection efforts. Ensuring the public understands that participation is essential yet not penalized through fines supports ethical data practices and builds trust in official statistics, which are foundational for transparent and effective governance.
