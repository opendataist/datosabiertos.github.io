Mucho ojo con los datos del DNI que te piden: ¡Hasta 100.000 euros de multa!

The Spanish Data Protection Agency warns that entities collecting unnecessary ID copies face significant fines, emphasizing strict compliance with privacy laws. This measure highlights the critical need for organizations to minimize data collection, ensuring only essential information is processed. Such over-collection poses severe risks, including identity theft and financial fraud, undermining individual security. By protecting sensitive details, companies can prevent malicious exploitation while respecting citizens' fundamental rights to digital privacy. This case is relevant to open data as it underscores the balance between transparency and protection. It reminds developers and policymakers that responsible data practices require rigor in handling personal information, even when promoting openness.
