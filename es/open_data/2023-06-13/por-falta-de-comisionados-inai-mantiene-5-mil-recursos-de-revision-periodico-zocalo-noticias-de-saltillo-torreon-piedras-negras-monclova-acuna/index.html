Por falta de comisionados, INAI mantiene 5 mil recursos de revisión | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INAI’s prolonged inability to hold regular sessions due to unfilled Senate appointments has left thousands of transparency review cases unresolved. This legislative delay prevents the full commission from addressing substantive issues, severely limiting institutional effectiveness and access to information. By functioning without a quorum, the agency is forced to handle only urgent legal mandates rather than systemic problems. This paralysis highlights how political appointments directly impact the operational capacity of oversight bodies, creating a backlog that undermines public accountability. This situation is critical for open data because it demonstrates that transparent governance requires not just laws, but fully functional institutions. Without a complete commission, citizens cannot rely on timely resolutions, weakening the entire ecosystem of data protection and governmental transparency.
