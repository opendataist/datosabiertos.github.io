A 73 días sin quórum, Inai sesiona otra vez de manera extraordinaria por orden de jueces

The INAI has been unable to hold regular sessions for 73 days due to a lack of quorum, relying on court orders for isolated decisions. This paralysis leaves thousands of review resources unresolved, severely hindering the institute's ability to ensure transparency and data protection. This situation is critical for open data, as it demonstrates how institutional instability directly obstructs citizens' access to public information and the effective enforcement of privacy rights.
