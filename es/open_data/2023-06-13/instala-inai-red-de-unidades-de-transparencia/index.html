Instala INAI red de unidades de transparencia

The Mexican Federal Transparency Institute established a network connecting transparency units across the country to optimize public information practices. This initiative aims to standardize processes by facilitating the exchange of experiences and solutions among hundreds of obligated subjects. By consolidating as a forum for analysis, the network strengthens the democratic link between institutions and citizens, ensuring more effective protection of personal data and information access rights through collaborative alliances. The agenda focuses on three critical areas: implementing proactive transparency policies and generating open data formats, managing the lifecycle of citizen information requests, and adhering to data protection laws. This structured approach ensures that units are equipped to handle modern transparency demands efficiently. It emphasizes moving beyond reactive measures toward creating accessible, reusable public data that genuinely empowers societal engagement. This development is highly relevant to open_data because it explicitly links institutional transparency with the production of information in open formats. By integrating open data generation into the core responsibilities of federal transparency units, the initiative promotes a culture where data is not just released, but structured for public use. This systemic alignment supports greater transparency, accountability, and the potential for civic innovation through accessible government information.
