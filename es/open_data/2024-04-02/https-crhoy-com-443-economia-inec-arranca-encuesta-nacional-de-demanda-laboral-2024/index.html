Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The National Statistics Institute is conducting a survey to analyze the national labor market’s demand, gathering detailed data on job roles, requirements, and vacancies. This initiative aims to produce accurate official statistics that reflect the structure and dynamics of employment opportunities across various sectors. Statistical confidentiality is strictly guaranteed by law, ensuring that no individual business information is disclosed or shared with authorities for non-statistical purposes. This legal framework protects respondents, allowing companies to share sensitive operational details without fear of exposure or financial repercussions. This article highlights open data principles by emphasizing the transparent, anonymized collection of economic indicators. It demonstrates how robust data privacy frameworks enable the generation of public, high-quality datasets that inform policy and economic understanding while maintaining public trust in official statistics.
