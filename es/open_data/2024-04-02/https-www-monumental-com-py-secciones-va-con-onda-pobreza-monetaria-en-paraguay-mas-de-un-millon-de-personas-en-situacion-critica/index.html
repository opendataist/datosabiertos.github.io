Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This article highlights how official poverty metrics in Paraguay rely on the cost of basic food and consumption baskets. By defining poverty through the inability to afford these essentials, the data provides a clear, standardized framework for analyzing economic vulnerability. This methodology is crucial for identifying those hovering near the poverty line who remain precarious despite small income fluctuations. The findings reveal significant income disparities, with many workers earning below the minimum wage. Such granular data exposes structural inequalities, particularly the gender gap in earnings. Understanding these distribution patterns helps stakeholders pinpoint where economic support is most needed to stabilize households and reduce extreme deprivation. This information is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistics can drive informed policy decisions. By making detailed income and poverty datasets public, governments and researchers can foster accountability and design targeted interventions. Open data thus transforms raw numbers into actionable insights for social equity and economic planning.
