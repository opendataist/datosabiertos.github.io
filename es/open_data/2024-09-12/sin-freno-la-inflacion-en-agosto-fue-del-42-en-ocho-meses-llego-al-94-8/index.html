Sin freno… La inflación en agosto fue del 4,2% en ocho meses llegó al 94.8%

The reported retail inflation exceeded all private sector forecasts, signaling a complex economic reality where overall prices continue to rise despite some signs of deceleration in the annual trend. This data highlights a significant gap between official statistics and market expectations, underscoring the critical importance of transparent and accessible statistical releases for maintaining public trust and accurate economic analysis. A deeper look reveals that core inflation, which excludes regulated and seasonal items, increased for the second consecutive month. This persistent rise in underlying prices suggests that structural pressures within the economy remain strong, rather than being driven solely by temporary fluctuations or government-controlled tariffs. Understanding these dynamics is essential for policymakers and analysts aiming to address the root causes of price instability. This case is highly relevant to the open_data movement because it illustrates the necessity of independent verification of government statistics. When official data diverges sharply from private estimates, open access to methodologies and raw data becomes vital for cross-checking, fostering accountability, and enabling a more robust public debate on economic health.
