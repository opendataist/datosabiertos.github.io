Veracruz, con la mayor tasa de muertes por males cardiacos y diabetes, en 2023

Veracruz topped national mortality rates for heart disease and diabetes in 2023, according to INEGI’s official statistics. These findings highlight critical regional health disparities driven by non-communicable diseases, with cardiac conditions and diabetes claiming the most lives among the state’s population. The data further reveals that while malignancies and suicide rates varied, cardiovascular issues remain the primary cause of death, significantly impacting both male and female demographics. This concentrated mortality burden underscores the urgent need for targeted public health interventions tailored to the specific epidemiological profile of the region. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent, official statistical releases allow for granular geographic analysis. By making granular mortality metrics publicly available, stakeholders can identify health crises, allocate resources effectively, and monitor the efficacy of medical policies across different Mexican states.
