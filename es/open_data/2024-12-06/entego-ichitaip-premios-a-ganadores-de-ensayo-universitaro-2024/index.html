Entegó Ichitaip premios a ganadores de Ensayo Universitaro 2024

This event underscores the critical role of youth engagement in public transparency. By recognizing student essays, the institution highlights that social change requires active citizen participation rather than relying solely on government initiatives. It reinforces the necessity for individuals to exercise their right to access information as a tool for societal progress. The winning contributions emphasize the urgent need for public institutions to listen to citizens and integrate public information into decision-making processes. This approach ensures that people are not merely passive recipients of authority but active participants in solving social problems, thereby fostering a more responsive and accountable governance model. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the practical application of transparency principles through education. It demonstrates how disseminating public information empowers citizens, encouraging them to challenge administrative opacity. Ultimately, it validates open data not just as a technical asset, but as a foundational element for democratic participation and civic empowerment.
