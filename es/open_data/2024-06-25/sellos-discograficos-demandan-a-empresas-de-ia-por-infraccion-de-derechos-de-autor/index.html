Sellos discográficos demandan a empresas de IA por infracción de derechos de autor

Major record labels are suing Suno and Udio, alleging their AI tools replicate copyrighted songs and impersonate famous artists. This marks the first music-specific litigation, shifting from previous text-based AI cases. The companies argue their operations fall under fair use, despite raising millions in funding. The dispute centers on whether training generative models on protected content without permission constitutes infringement or permissible innovation. This case is vital for open data as it tests the boundaries of using creative works for AI training. It will influence how data ownership and fair use are defined in the evolving landscape of generative technologies.
