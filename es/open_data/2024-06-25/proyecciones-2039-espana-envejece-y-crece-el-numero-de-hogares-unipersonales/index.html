Proyecciones 2039: España envejece y crece el número de hogares unipersonales

Spain is projected to undergo significant demographic transformation, marked by a substantial aging of its population and a shift toward smaller, single-person households. This structural change indicates deep social and economic shifts, requiring adaptive policies to manage future challenges effectively. The nation will rely heavily on immigration to sustain population growth, as native birth rates decline and life expectancy increases. Regional disparities will emerge, with coastal areas attracting more residents while inland regions face depopulation, altering the geographic distribution of the country’s inhabitants. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical role of high-quality statistical indicators in forecasting societal trends. Transparent access to such demographic projections enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to understand long-term impacts on healthcare, housing, and social services, fostering evidence-based decision-making for a changing society.
