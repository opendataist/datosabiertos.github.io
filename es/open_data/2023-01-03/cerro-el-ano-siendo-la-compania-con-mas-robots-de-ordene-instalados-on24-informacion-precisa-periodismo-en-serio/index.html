Cerró el año siendo la compañía con más robots de ordeñe instalados | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

Lely Argentina has achieved significant market dominance in automated milking technology, holding nearly half the national market share. This rapid adoption reflects a growing industry shift toward automation to enhance efficiency and animal welfare. The technology’s versatility allows it to integrate seamlessly into various farming systems, from pasture-based to indoor setups, demonstrating its broad applicability and adaptability across different agricultural contexts. The company actively promotes transparency and education through field events and visits to sustainable facilities. By allowing producers to witness the technology in action and engage in technical discussions, they facilitate knowledge transfer regarding robotic milking benefits. These initiatives serve as crucial platforms for demonstrating best practices and providing post-sales support, thereby encouraging wider industry acceptance and understanding of automated solutions. This article is highly relevant to open_data because it highlights the importance of accessible, real-world data in validating technological trends. The detailed distribution of installations and operational metrics provides a transparent view of market penetration, offering valuable insights for researchers and policymakers. Such open information fosters trust in automation’s environmental and economic sustainability, encouraging further innovation and informed decision-making within the agricultural sector.
