Repuntan exportaciones mexicanas en julio, reporta Inegi

July’s trade data reveals a significant rebound in merchandise exports, driven largely by non-petroleum sales to the United States and the rest of the world. This surge was partially offset by a decline in petroleum-related transactions, highlighting the diversification of Mexico’s export base beyond traditional energy sectors. Imports also experienced substantial annual growth, particularly in consumption and intermediate goods, reflecting strong domestic demand and industrial activity. The balanced increase in both import and export categories contributed to a notably reduced trade deficit, signaling an improvement in the external sector’s performance compared to the previous year. This information is relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, timely government statistics enable real-time economic analysis. By making such granular trade figures publicly accessible, the data empowers researchers and businesses to monitor economic trends, assess policy impacts, and make informed decisions based on reliable, up-to-date national metrics.
