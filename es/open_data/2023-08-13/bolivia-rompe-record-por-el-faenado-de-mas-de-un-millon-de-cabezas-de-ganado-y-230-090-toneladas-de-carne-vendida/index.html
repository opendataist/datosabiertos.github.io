Bolivia rompe récord por el faenado de más de un millón de cabezas de ganado y 230.090 toneladas de carne vendida

Bolivia’s bovine slaughter and meat production reached an eight-year high in 2022, surpassing previous annual peaks. This resurgence indicates a robust recovery in the agricultural sector, reversing declines seen in prior years and marking the strongest performance since 2018. These official statistics highlight regional disparities, with Santa Cruz dominating production volume. The significant increase in both domestic availability and export volumes underscores the sector's vital role in the national economy and international trade relations. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of transparent, high-quality official statistics. Accessible records like this enable researchers and policymakers to accurately track economic trends, ensuring that development strategies are based on reliable and verifiable information.
