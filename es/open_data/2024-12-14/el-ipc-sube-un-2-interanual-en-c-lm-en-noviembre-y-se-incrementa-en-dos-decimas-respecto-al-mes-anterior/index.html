El IPC sube un 2% interanual en C-LM en noviembre y se incrementa en dos décimas respecto al mes anterior

The article reports a rise in the Consumer Price Index in Castilla-La Mancha, highlighting specific sectors driving inflation while noting decreases in transport and household goods. This data illustrates how regional economic indicators reflect broader cost-of-living trends, showing variations between autonomous communities and national averages. Understanding these inflationary pressures is crucial for the open data community, as it underscores the importance of accessible, granular statistics for analyzing economic impact. By making such detailed indices available, society can better assess how price fluctuations affect different demographics and regions, fostering transparency in public administration. Ultimately, this relevance lies in the ability of open datasets to empower researchers and citizens to track economic health accurately. It encourages the use of official statistics to identify disparities, ensuring that policy decisions are informed by reliable, publicly available evidence rather than opaque or aggregated figures that might obscure local realities.
