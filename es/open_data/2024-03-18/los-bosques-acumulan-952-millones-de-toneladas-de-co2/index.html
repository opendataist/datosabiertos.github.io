Los bosques acumulan 95,2 millones de toneladas de CO2

The article highlights the launch of Castilla y León’s new Forest Data Portal, a crucial instrument for responsibly managing the region's vast forest resources. By aggregating essential information on biomass, carbon accumulation, and timber volume, this platform empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions regarding ecological preservation and sustainable economic development. Central to the portal’s value is its comprehensive mapping of forest distribution and economic impact across provinces. It reveals significant disparities in carbon storage and forest cover, particularly identifying León as the leader in carbon accumulation. Simultaneously, it quantifies the sector’s contribution to the local economy, detailing the number of enterprises and jobs tied to forestry and wood production, thereby illustrating the tangible link between natural assets and regional prosperity. This development is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible public data can drive evidence-based policy and industry planning. By making critical environmental and economic statistics available to citizens and businesses, the portal fosters accountability and encourages sustainable practices. It serves as a model for how open data can bridge the gap between scientific understanding of ecological limits and practical economic management, ensuring that resource utilization remains balanced and responsible.
