No hay que perder la Plataforma Nacional de Transparencia, afirma presidenta del Inai

The INAI president urges citizens to defend the National Transparency Platform against institutional threats, emphasizing its vital role in protecting against government opacity. This tool democratizes access to previously hidden fiscal data, transforming past secrecy into public accountability. The platform represents the culmination of decades of civic struggle for information rights, serving as a concrete achievement of democratic values and human rights. It stands as a testament to persistent civil society demands for open governance. This news is relevant to open_data as it highlights the critical importance of robust digital infrastructure for public transparency. Maintaining accessible, standardized data portals is essential for sustaining civic trust and ensuring that government information remains freely available to the population.
