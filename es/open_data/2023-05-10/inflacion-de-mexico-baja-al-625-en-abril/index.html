Inflación de México baja al 6,25 % en abril

Mexico’s general inflation rate has declined to its lowest level since late 2021, marking three consecutive months of decrease. Although the annual figure remains above the central bank’s target, the downward trend signals a potential stabilization in the cost of living after reaching multi-year highs. The data distinguishes between volatile core prices and stable underlying trends, revealing that while some essential goods and services continue to rise, energy and government-regulated tariffs have helped moderate overall consumer pressures. This divergence highlights the complex dynamics affecting household budgets and economic stability. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on transparent, regularly updated statistics from official institutes like INEGI. Such accessible datasets enable researchers and the public to monitor economic health, verify institutional claims, and analyze policy impacts, fostering accountability and informed public discourse.
