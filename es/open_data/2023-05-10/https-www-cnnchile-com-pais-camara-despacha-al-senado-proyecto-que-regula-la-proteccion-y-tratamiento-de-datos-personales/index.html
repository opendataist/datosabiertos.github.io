Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Chile advances a new law to strictly regulate personal data processing, emphasizing consent, transparency, and security standards. The proposal also establishes an independent agency dedicated to safeguarding individuals' privacy rights against unauthorized data usage. This legislation explicitly guarantees inalienable rights such as access, correction, deletion, and portability of personal information. These measures aim to prevent any contractual limitations, ensuring robust individual control over digital identities. This development is crucial for open data, as it clarifies the boundary between public transparency and private privacy. It sets a precedent for handling sensitive information in government datasets, ensuring that while data is open, individual rights remain strictly protected under rigorous legal oversight.
