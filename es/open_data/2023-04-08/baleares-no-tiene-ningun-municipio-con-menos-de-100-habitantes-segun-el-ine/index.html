Baleares no tiene ningún municipio con menos de 100 habitantes, según el INE

Aragón exhibits a high concentration of municipalities under one hundred residents, reflecting Spain’s severe rural depopulation crisis. Provinces like Soria and Guadalajara lead this demographic shrinkage, while coastal and urban regions remain unaffected. This disparity highlights critical gaps in open data regarding local administrative boundaries and population density. Accurate spatial datasets are essential to monitor these trends and support sustainable regional planning policies.
