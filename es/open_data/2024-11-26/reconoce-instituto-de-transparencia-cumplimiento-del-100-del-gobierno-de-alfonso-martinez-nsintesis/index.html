Reconoce Instituto de Transparencia cumplimiento del 100 % del gobierno de Alfonso Martínez • Nsíntesis

The government of Morelia achieved a perfect transparency score for the fourth consecutive year, affirming its unwavering commitment to accountability and open governance. This consistent recognition highlights how total disclosure of public resource usage fosters trust and enables meaningful civic participation. As the state’s largest municipality, Morelia proves that comprehensive transparency is achievable even with substantial budgets and extensive documentation. This success demonstrates that openness scales effectively, setting a vital benchmark for other large administrative bodies aiming to improve their data accessibility standards. This achievement is highly relevant to open data because it validates the real-world impact of releasing granular government information. It encourages broader adoption of open practices, showing citizens that transparent data directly supports efficient program evaluation and strengthens democratic engagement through informed public oversight.
