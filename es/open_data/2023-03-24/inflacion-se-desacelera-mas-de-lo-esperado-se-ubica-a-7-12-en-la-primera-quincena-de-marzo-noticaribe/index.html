Inflación se desacelera más de lo esperado; se ubica a 7.12% en la primera quincena de marzo - Noticaribe

The report highlights a notable deceleration in Mexico's general inflation, marking its lowest level in over a year. This trend suggests a stabilization in consumer prices, potentially easing economic pressure on households. The underlying inflation metric also indicates broader price stability when excluding volatile items. Specific sectors show diverging trends, with significant price hikes in transportation and certain food items contrasting with drops in energy and basic agricultural goods. This disparity reflects complex supply chain dynamics and shifting demand patterns within the Mexican economy during early 2023.
