Inflación se frena durante la primera mitad de marzo y alcanza 7.12%; baja el precio del huevo, pollo y gas LP

The article highlights Mexico’s inflation dynamics, noting that underlying rates aligned with expectations while non-underlying inflation declined. This shift, driven by lower agricultural and energy prices, suggests a stabilization in core price trends, offering a clearer view of economic stability by filtering out volatile sectors. This information is relevant to open_data because transparent, granular price datasets enable independent verification of official statistics. By analyzing detailed components like food and energy costs, researchers can assess policy impacts and improve models for forecasting economic indicators, fostering greater accountability and deeper public understanding of market forces.
