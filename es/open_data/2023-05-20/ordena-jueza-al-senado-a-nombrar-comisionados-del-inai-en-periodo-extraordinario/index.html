Ordena jueza al Senado a nombrar comisionados del INAI en periodo extraordinario

A federal judge ordered the Senate’s Political Coordination Board to convene an extraordinary session to appoint the missing INAI commissioner. This ruling forces the legislative body to propose candidates, ensuring the oversight institute can resume its essential functions for data protection and transparency. The immediate implication is the restoration of institutional capacity. By mandating this appointment, the judicial intervention safeguards the public’s right to access information, which is currently hindered by leadership vacancies. This highlights the critical link between judicial enforcement and the effective operation of transparency bodies. This case is vital to open data because it underscores how legal mechanisms protect data governance institutions. It demonstrates that without functional leadership, public accountability erodes. The decision reinforces the necessity of judicial support to maintain robust frameworks for information access and privacy in Mexico.
