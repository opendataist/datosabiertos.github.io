Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The coordinated effort between state finance authorities and the national statistical institute establishes a robust framework for generating reliable, actionable data. This collaboration aims to enhance public decision-making by providing a comprehensive understanding of the population's demographics, activities, and educational levels to better serve citizens. This initiative is highly relevant to open_data as it underscores the critical importance of inter-agency cooperation in producing high-quality, accessible public information. By standardizing statistical and geographic data collection, the state creates a foundation for transparency, allowing stakeholders to utilize accurate metrics for policy development and civic engagement. The approval of annual work programs and the identification of committee needs demonstrate a commitment to continuous improvement in data governance. This structured approach ensures that the resulting information is not only collected but also effectively utilized to address the specific needs of the community, reinforcing the value of open and reliable data in modern public administration.
