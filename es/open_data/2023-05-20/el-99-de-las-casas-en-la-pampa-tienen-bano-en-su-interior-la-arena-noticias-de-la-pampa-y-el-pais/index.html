El 99% de las casas en La Pampa tienen baño en su interior - La Arena | Noticias de La Pampa y el país

The recent report by INDEC, derived from the 2022 Census, highlights a significant shift in housing conditions across Argentina, particularly regarding tenure and connectivity. While essential infrastructure such as plumbing and sanitation has reached near-universal saturation in provinces like La Pampa, the most critical finding is the steady decline in home ownership. This trend indicates a growing reliance on rental arrangements and informal housing tenure, signaling increased housing insecurity and shifting economic pressures on households nationwide. Simultaneously, the data reveals that digital access has become a standard utility rather than a luxury. High rates of internet and mobile connectivity suggest that technological inclusion is now intrinsic to basic living standards. However, disparities remain, as certain regions and demographics lag in accessing advanced devices like computers, reflecting a digital divide that complements the broader socio-economic inequalities observed in housing tenure. This article is highly relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how aggregated census metrics can uncover nuanced societal trends beyond simple infrastructure counts. By releasing granular data on tenure and technology, INDEC enables researchers and policymakers to analyze the intersection of digital rights and housing stability. Such transparency is essential for fostering data-driven decision-making that addresses both digital exclusion and the housing crisis through evidence-based public policy.
