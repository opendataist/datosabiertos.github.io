Castilla-La Mancha registró el año pasado 3.294 abortos voluntarios de los 98.316 que hubo en España

The publication of 2022 data on voluntary pregnancy terminations in Spain highlights a significant trend toward medicalization and patient autonomy. The majority of procedures occurred within the first trimester and, crucially, without mandatory medical referral, relying solely on the woman’s decision. This shift underscores the effectiveness of policies prioritizing informed consent and accessible healthcare, ensuring that reproductive choices are managed efficiently and safely within the public health framework. Socio-demographic analysis reveals distinct patterns in who accesses these services, with varying rates across different regions and nationalities. The data also sheds light on the importance of sexual education, as a notable portion of women did not use contraceptive methods, suggesting ongoing challenges in prevention. By breaking down these demographics, the information provides essential insights into the specific needs of different populations, allowing for more targeted public health strategies that address disparities in access and outcomes. This dataset is vital for the open data community as it transforms raw statistical records into actionable knowledge for civil society and policymakers. Transparent access to such detailed health information enables independent analysis, fostering accountability and driving evidence-based reforms. It empowers researchers and advocacy groups to monitor progress toward gender equality and sexual health rights, ensuring that public administration remains responsive to the real-world experiences of citizens.
