La inflación de la eurozona se relaja al 5,2% y la subyacente baja al 5,3% en agosto

The article reports a slight deceleration in eurozone inflation, highlighting a shift toward economic stability after recent highs. This moderation suggests that the European Central Bank has likely peaked its interest rate hikes, signaling an end to the tightening cycle. Analysts interpret this trend as confirmation that further aggressive monetary adjustments are unnecessary, as underlying inflation continues to ease despite persistent concerns in food and energy sectors. Looking ahead, forecasts predict a steady decline in inflation rates through the coming months and into next year. Experts expect consumer prices to drop significantly, potentially returning near target levels by late 2024. This trajectory supports the view that the central bank will soon pivot toward easing policies, balancing the need to control prices with the broader economic slowdown.
