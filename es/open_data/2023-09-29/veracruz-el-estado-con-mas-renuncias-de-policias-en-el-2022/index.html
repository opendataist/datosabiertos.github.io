Veracruz: el Estado con más renuncias de policías en el 2022

Veracruz experienced the highest attrition in Mexico’s public security personnel last year, reflecting a critical stability crisis in the state’s law enforcement agencies. This surge in resignations, coupled with a significant reduction in total staff, indicates deep institutional weaknesses that likely undermine public safety effectiveness and operational continuity across the region. The data reveals that many officers remain underpaid compared to other states, with the majority earning modest salaries while top earners are concentrated elsewhere. This wage disparity suggests financial dissatisfaction as a primary driver for the high turnover rate. Consequently, the state struggles to retain experienced personnel, leading to a cycle of recruitment losses and a depleted workforce ready to serve the community. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights how transparent, publicly available census statistics can expose systemic governance issues. By making these employment and salary metrics accessible, citizens and researchers can hold local governments accountable, pushing for necessary policy reforms to improve working conditions and restore trust in public security institutions through evidence-based advocacy.
