La actividad económica de México crece 0.1% a tasa mensual y 3.9% a tasa anual, durante febrero de 2023

The economic activity indicator shows modest growth, driven primarily by the agricultural sector, while tertiary activities stagnated. This performance fell short of initial projections, revealing a slower-than-expected recovery for services like construction and professional services compared to pre-pandemic levels. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how transparent, timely statistical releases from the INEGI allow stakeholders to track economic nuances. Access to such granular, disaggregated data is essential for analyzing sectoral disparities and validating official forecasts against real-world outcomes.
