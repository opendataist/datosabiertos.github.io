Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This report highlights a notable deceleration in Mexico’s annual inflation, dropping to 6.24% as essential goods like poultry and fuel experience price hikes while others fall. It reveals regional disparities, particularly in Oaxaca where inflation remains higher than the national average, indicating uneven economic impacts across different states. The findings are crucial for open data initiatives as they demonstrate the value of transparent, granular statistical tracking. By making detailed price variations publicly accessible, this data empowers citizens and researchers to monitor cost-of-living changes accurately. Such accessibility fosters greater accountability and allows for deeper analysis of how specific commodities affect local economies. Ultimately, the availability of this detailed inflation index supports informed decision-making for policymakers and the public. It underscores the necessity of robust open data ecosystems that provide clear, accessible metrics on economic indicators. This transparency enables better understanding of purchasing power trends and helps identify specific sectors driving or mitigating inflationary pressures nationwide.
