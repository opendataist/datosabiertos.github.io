La compraventa de viviendas se hunde un 13,1% en febrero pero los precios vuelven a subir ligeramente

The Spanish housing market is experiencing a sustained downturn, marked by five consecutive months of declining transactions. This consolidation of the sector’s stagnation indicates a significant reduction in sales volume across most autonomous communities, with only minor exceptions. The sharp drop in activity highlights structural challenges facing real estate operations rather than temporary fluctuations. Despite the fall in volume, price trends reveal a mixed landscape where averages have slightly recovered compared to the previous year. However, this growth is uneven, with some regions seeing notable increases while others continue to face price reductions. The divergence between sales volume and unit prices suggests complex market dynamics that vary significantly by geographic location and property type. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of transparent, granular real estate information. Accessible datasets allow researchers and policymakers to analyze regional disparities and market health accurately. Such transparency fosters informed decision-making and helps stakeholders understand the evolving relationship between property availability and pricing in a changing economic environment.
