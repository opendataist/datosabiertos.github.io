Cae demanda de petróleo en Estados Unidos: las reservas crecieron en 800 mil barriles

The US commercial oil reserves increased, reflecting a significant decline in demand for refined products like gasoline and jet fuel. This drop in consumption, coupled with higher imports and lower refinery activity, signals weakening market conditions despite stable production levels. This data is relevant to open_data as it highlights the critical role of transparent, standardized energy statistics from agencies like the EIA. Open access to such raw data enables independent analysis, allowing stakeholders to verify supply-demand dynamics and assess economic implications without relying solely on mediated financial forecasts.
