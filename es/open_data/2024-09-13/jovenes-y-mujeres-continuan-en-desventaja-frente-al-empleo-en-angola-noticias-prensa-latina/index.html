Jóvenes y mujeres continúan en desventaja frente al empleo en Angola - Noticias Prensa Latina

Angola’s recent labor data reveals a persistent gender disparity, where women face higher unemployment rates despite representing the majority of the active population. This highlights a critical gap in disaggregated labor statistics, essential for identifying systemic inequalities and designing effective, evidence-based social policies under open data principles. While youth employment shows improvement, middle-aged groups remain the most employed, indicating specific economic vulnerabilities. These findings underscore the need for transparent, accessible labor metrics that allow for precise analysis of demographic trends and socioeconomic dynamics. Upcoming national censuses promise more accurate data, reinforcing the value of open, high-quality government statistics. Such transparency enables better public scrutiny and policy formulation, ensuring that development efforts address the actual needs of diverse population segments rather than relying on incomplete or aggregated indicators.
