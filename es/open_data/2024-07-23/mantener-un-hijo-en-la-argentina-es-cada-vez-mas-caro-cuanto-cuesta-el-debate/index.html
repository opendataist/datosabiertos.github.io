Mantener un hijo en la Argentina es cada vez más caro: cuánto cuesta - EL DEBATE

The article reveals that the cost of raising children in Argentina has become prohibitively expensive, often exceeding half of the basic family basket and surpassing the minimum vital salary. This financial burden renders child-rearing an activity accessible only to a privileged minority, forcing many families to rely on external support or forgo having children altogether due to the stark contrast between actual costs and average incomes. These findings are critically relevant to open data initiatives because they highlight the necessity of transparent, accessible, and frequent statistical tracking of household expenditures. When official indicators like the Child Raising Index are publicly available and clearly disaggregated by age and component, they empower citizens, researchers, and policymakers to understand the true economic impact of social policies. The study underscores that without such granular data visibility, the gap between policy intentions and lived reality remains obscured. By exposing how inflation and wage stagnation directly threaten family stability, this data serves as a vital tool for advocating for social protection measures. It demonstrates that open, detailed economic statistics are not just academic exercises but essential instruments for identifying urgency and driving meaningful socioeconomic interventions.
