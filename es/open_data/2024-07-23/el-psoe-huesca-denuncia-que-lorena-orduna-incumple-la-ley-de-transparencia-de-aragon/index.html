El PSOE Huesca denuncia que Lorena Orduna incumple la Ley de Transparencia de Aragón

The Socialist group in Huesca criticizes Mayor Lorena Orduna for repeatedly violating transparency laws by failing to publish her institutional agenda. This omission is highlighted as a fundamental breach of statutory duties, ensuring that political decisions and official engagements remain opaque rather than publicly accessible. The criticism underscores that transparency is not merely rhetorical but a legal obligation essential for democratic accountability. Despite prior complaints to the Transparency Council, the administration continues to withhold information from both political opposition and citizens. Decisions are often made without prior consultation through informative commissions, with opposition members only learning about matters through media leaks. This systemic lack of disclosure impedes the media’s ability to perform their oversight role effectively and deprives the public of timely access to relevant governmental activities. This situation is critical for open data because it demonstrates how legal frameworks can be ignored in practice, preventing the creation of machine-readable, accessible datasets regarding public officials' actions. True open data requires proactive disclosure of agendas and decision-making processes, not just passive availability. When governments hide agendas, they obstruct the flow of information necessary for independent analysis, civil society engagement, and the verification of public service integrity, ultimately undermining the core principles of open governance.
