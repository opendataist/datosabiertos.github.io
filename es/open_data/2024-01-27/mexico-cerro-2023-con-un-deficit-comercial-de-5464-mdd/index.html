México cerró 2023 con un déficit comercial de 5,464 mdd

Mexico’s trade deficit significantly narrowed as export growth, driven by manufacturing, outpaced imports. This shift indicates improved economic resilience and reduced reliance on external borrowing. The data highlights structural shifts in trade dynamics, with automotive sectors leading gains while other areas contracted. These trends suggest a maturing industrial base capable of sustaining higher value-added exports. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent metrics for analyzing economic health and trade policy effectiveness. Researchers can use these verified figures to model future trends and assess the impact of international agreements on domestic industries.
