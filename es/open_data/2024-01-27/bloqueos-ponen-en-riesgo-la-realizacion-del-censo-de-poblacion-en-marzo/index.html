Bloqueos ponen en riesgo la realización del Censo de Población en marzo

The upcoming national census faces significant jeopardy due to extensive road blockades and a stagnant political climate. The analysis suggests that underlying political disputes, rather than logistical needs, are driving the instability. This environment threatens the integrity of essential demographic data collection, as mobility restrictions directly hinder necessary pre-census technical preparations and operations. Economic impacts are already severe, with substantial daily losses reported in the agricultural sector. These disruptions highlight how civil unrest rapidly translates into tangible resource destruction and price increases, affecting society at large. The inability to transport goods underscores the fragility of supply chains during periods of intense social confrontation, creating broader systemic risks beyond just political grievances. This situation is critically relevant to open data initiatives because accurate, timely censuses are foundational for evidence-based policy and resource allocation. When political maneuvering disrupts statistical processes, the resulting data gaps or delays compromise transparency and accountability. Reliable population data is essential for understanding societal needs, and its corruption or absence weakens the infrastructure required for effective, data-driven governance and public trust.
