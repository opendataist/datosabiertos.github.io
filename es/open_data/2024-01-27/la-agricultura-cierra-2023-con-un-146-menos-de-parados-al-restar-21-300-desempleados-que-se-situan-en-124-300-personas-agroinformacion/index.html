La agricultura cierra 2023 con un 14,6% menos de parados al restar 21.300 desempleados, que se sitúan en 124.300 personas - Agroinformacion

The Spanish agriculture sector showed significant resilience in 2023, marked by a notable decline in unemployment and a substantial increase in employment. This positive trend reversed previous losses, highlighting a robust recovery within the industry despite broader economic challenges. These improvements reflect a stronger labor market overall, contributing to the national reduction in joblessness. The sector’s ability to generate jobs during this period underscores its stability and growing contribution to the country’s economic recovery efforts. This data is relevant to open_data because it illustrates how transparent, high-quality statistical datasets empower stakeholders to track sector-specific trends. Reliable public records enable accurate analysis of economic shifts, facilitating better decision-making and policy development based on real-time labor market conditions.
