CEOE CEPYME Cuenca señala que los datos de la EPA son resultado de la complicada situación empresarial

Business leaders in Cuenca emphasize that enterprises face a severe operational climate, characterized by persistently high costs and increasingly difficult access to credit. These economic pressures significantly hinder investment and job creation, creating a challenging environment for local employers to sustain growth or expand their workforce effectively. The labor market reflects this instability, with unemployment rising sharply while employment levels stagnate. The surge in joblessness is attributed not only to business struggles but also to a substantial influx of individuals joining the active workforce that the current job market cannot absorb, resulting in a net increase in those seeking work despite some marginal employment gains. This analysis is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how raw statistical indicators must be contextualized to reveal underlying socio-economic realities. By combining datasets on costs, credit availability, and labor force participation, researchers can better understand the complex dynamics driving unemployment, offering policymakers a more nuanced view than isolated employment figures alone can provide.
