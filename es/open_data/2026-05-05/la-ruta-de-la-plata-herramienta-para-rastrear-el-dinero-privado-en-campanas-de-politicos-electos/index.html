‘La Ruta de la Plata’: herramienta para rastrear el dinero privado en campañas de políticos electos

The article introduces "La Ruta de la Plata," a new data visualization tool launched by Panama’s chapter of Transparency International. This initiative transforms private campaign finance reports from elected officials into an accessible, open-data format. By converting previously closed PDF documents into structured, machine-readable information, the project enables citizens and journalists to easily track the flow of private money within electoral processes, significantly enhancing public oversight. The core value of this tool lies in its ability to facilitate deep analysis and accountability. Rather than presenting aggregated totals, it disaggregates data to reveal specific details about donors, spending patterns, and discrepancies between reported income and actual expenditures. This transparency highlights potential irregularities, such as undeclared donations or discrepancies in gender-based funding, empowering users to draw independent conclusions and challenge official narratives regarding financial transparency in politics. This development is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how transforming static, inaccessible government records into dynamic, open formats can unlock civic engagement. By making complex financial data understandable and analyzable, the project sets a precedent for how open data can bridge the gap between administrative bureaucracy and democratic participation. It proves that when financial information is freely available and interoperable, it becomes a powerful instrument for ensuring integrity and fostering trust in electoral systems.
