La producción de autos en México sube un 35,77 % en octubre y la exportación un 18,11 % - Mundo - ABC Color

The Mexican automotive sector demonstrates robust recovery, significantly surpassing pre-pandemic performance through substantial growth in light vehicle manufacturing, internal sales, and exports. This surge highlights the industry's critical economic role, contributing heavily to national GDP and dominating the manufacturing sector. This expansion marks a decisive turnaround from recent years hindered by global supply chain disruptions, including the semiconductor shortage and pandemic-related shutdowns. The data reflects a resilient rebound, validating the sector's ability to adapt and scale production capabilities effectively despite previous external shocks. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it provides essential, high-quality metrics on industrial recovery and economic resilience. Transparent access to such granular manufacturing statistics enables better policy analysis, supports data-driven economic research, and fosters accountability regarding trade flows and sectoral health.
