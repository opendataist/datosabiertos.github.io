INE habilita plataforma para solicitar permiso de circulación de vehículos en el día del Censo

The National Institute of Statistics has established a digital platform to facilitate the request for special vehicle circulation permits during the upcoming National Census. This administrative measure aims to minimize traffic disruption while ensuring that essential services and emergency response vehicles can operate without hindrance. By prioritizing media outlets, diplomatic missions, continuous production companies, and critical infrastructure operators, the state seeks to balance civic rights with the logistical necessities of a nationwide data collection effort. The process relies on a streamlined digital procedure where entities submit sworn declarations and supporting documents, such as driver licenses and vehicle registration, through a dedicated online module. This shift towards electronic management highlights the government's effort to modernize bureaucratic processes, reducing physical paperwork and accelerating approval times. However, the final distribution of permits remains in-person, requiring designated personnel to collect physical documents after verification, creating a hybrid model that combines digital efficiency with strict physical oversight. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the tension between public accessibility and administrative secrecy in state operations. While the application process is transparent and digital, the actual issuance and physical distribution of permits remain opaque and restricted to authorized individuals. For open data advocates, this serves as a case study on how governments manage data flows during critical events, raising questions about whether permit issuance records could be made publicly available to enhance accountability and transparency in resource allocation during the census.
