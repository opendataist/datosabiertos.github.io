Bolivia has completed its statistical cartography update, transitioning to the census phase ahead of the March 2024 national survey. This effort involved extensive fieldwork across diverse terrains to digitize geographic and housing data, ensuring accurate enumeration frameworks. The finalized digital cartography enables precise planning for survey logistics, determining the necessary number of ballots and enumerators nationwide. This structural clarity supports efficient data collection and improves the overall reliability of the upcoming census operations. This update is crucial for open data as it creates a transparent, georeferenced foundation for future statistical releases. By providing accurate spatial coordinates and housing counts, it facilitates better data integration and accessibility for researchers and citizens.
