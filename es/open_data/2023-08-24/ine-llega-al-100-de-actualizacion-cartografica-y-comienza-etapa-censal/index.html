INE llega al 100% de actualización cartográfica y comienza etapa censal

Bolivia has achieved full coverage of its Statistical Cartographic Update, marking a historic transition to fully digitalized, georeferenced mapping. This foundational step ensures the upcoming census accurately identifies over five million households, significantly improving the precision and inclusivity of national data collection efforts. The completion of this digital cartography allows for superior planning of the census logistics, enabling enumerators to precisely locate infrastructure and geographic features. This technical advancement is crucial for designing effective public policies based on accurate, high-quality demographic information rather than estimates. This development is highly relevant to open data as it establishes a standardized, digital geographic framework. By making spatial data rigorous and accessible, the government facilitates better transparency and supports the creation of open, interoperable datasets that can be utilized for research, civic engagement, and evidence-based decision-making.
