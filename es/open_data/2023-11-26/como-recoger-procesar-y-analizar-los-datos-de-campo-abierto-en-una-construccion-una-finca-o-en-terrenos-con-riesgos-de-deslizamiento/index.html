¿Cómo recoger, procesar y analizar los datos de campo abierto en una construcción, una finca o en terrenos con riesgos de deslizamiento?

The article highlights how the integration of drones and artificial intelligence revolutionizes data collection across sectors like agriculture, construction, and public safety. By replacing labor-intensive traditional surveys with aerial captures, organizations achieve significant reductions in time and costs, moving from weeks to mere days for large-scale assessments. This synergy allows for the rapid processing of complex spatial data, enabling real-time visualization and automated analysis that significantly outperforms manual methods in both efficiency and accuracy. The relevance to open data lies in the transformation of how geospatial and environmental information is generated and accessed. Drones equipped with various sensors capture precise, high-resolution data that can be processed into open, accessible formats for public and private use. This democratizes access to critical information, such as landslide risks or resource management metrics, allowing municipalities and companies to make informed, data-driven decisions. The underlying datasets, once made available, foster transparency and enable broader community or scientific analysis of territorial changes. Ultimately, this technological shift supports the open data ecosystem by lowering barriers to entry for high-quality spatial analysis. As adoption grows, the standardization and sharing of these AI-processed datasets can enhance disaster response, environmental monitoring, and urban planning. The move towards automated, accurate, and cheaper data acquisition encourages the creation of richer, more dynamic open data repositories, empowering stakeholders to address complex challenges with greater speed and precision.
