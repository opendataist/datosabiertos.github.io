México Necesita una Ley de Ciberseguridad: INAI - Periódico El Orbe.

The article emphasizes the urgent need for Mexico to modernize its legal framework by enacting a Cybersecurity Law and updating data protection regulations. The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) argues that current norms are outdated and no longer sufficient to guarantee citizens' right to privacy in the face of rapid technological advancement. This legislative update is crucial to ensure robust digital rights and secure institutional operations against emerging cyber threats. Transparency and accountability are presented as foundational pillars of constitutional democracy, essential for building public trust. Regional transparency initiatives focus on diagnosing the specific challenges faced by smaller municipalities, such as infrastructure deficits and educational gaps, which hinder their ability to handle information requests and protect personal data. By addressing these structural barriers, government bodies can ensure that public information is accessible and that the exercise of public power is subject to clear, verifiable scrutiny by society. This topic is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical balance between data availability and security. Open data initiatives cannot thrive without a solid legal foundation that protects individual privacy while ensuring institutional autonomy. The article underscores that strengthening these protective mechanisms and supporting local transparency organs are vital for maintaining a democratic system where citizens can effectively access information without compromising their personal data rights.
