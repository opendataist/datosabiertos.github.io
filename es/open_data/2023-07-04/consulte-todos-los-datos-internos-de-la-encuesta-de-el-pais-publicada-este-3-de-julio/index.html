Consulte todos los datos internos de la encuesta de EL PAÍS publicada este 3 de julio

EL PAÍS and SER provide free access to all raw polling data and methodologies from their July survey, empowering the public and experts to conduct independent analyses. This transparency allows for the creation of personal voting projections using the original datasets rather than relying solely on published results. This initiative exemplifies open data principles by removing barriers to scientific information, fostering reproducibility and deeper understanding of electoral trends. It highlights the value of making sociological foundations publicly available for scrutiny and secondary research. Relevant to open data, this case demonstrates how media organizations can enhance trust and utility by releasing complete, structured datasets alongside their reports, enabling broader societal engagement with statistical findings.
