Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The Coalición Cívica proposes establishing an Open Data Portal for Importations in Argentina to radically increase transparency and combat corruption within the current import licensing system. By mandating that all application details, such as product types, amounts, and statuses, are published digitally in open formats, the initiative aims to eliminate the arbitrary discretion that currently characterizes administrative procedures. This shift is crucial because opaque decision-making processes foster an environment where officials can abuse their power, leading to unreasonable restrictions on trade and significant risks of corrupt practices. The proposal challenges the existing framework, which has undergone frequent modifications resulting in unclear parameters and a lack of reasoned rejections for import authorizations. Under the suggested legislation, administrative procedures would be simplified, with strict timelines enforced: if authorities do not resolve a request within a set period, the license is automatically approved. Additionally, standardized criteria for financial capacity would be published to ensure predictability. This structure seeks to replace a system focused on limiting imports to protect foreign currency reserves with one based on regulatory simplicity and public accountability. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how structured, accessible public information serves as a critical mechanism for governance and anti-corruption efforts. By advocating for open formats and real-time updates, the project illustrates the practical application of data transparency principles to reduce information asymmetry between the state and citizens. It highlights that open data is not merely about publishing records, but about designing systems that enforce accountability and prevent the abuse of administrative discretion, thereby fostering trust in public institutions and ensuring fair market conditions.
