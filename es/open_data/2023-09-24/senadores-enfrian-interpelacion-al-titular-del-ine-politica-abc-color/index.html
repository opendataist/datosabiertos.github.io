Senadores enfrían interpelación al titular del INE - Política - ABC Color

The Interpellation of the National Statistics Institute director regarding census validity faces indefinite postponement in the Senate, highlighting deep political divisions over data credibility. This delay shields officials from immediate accountability, suggesting that institutional pressure, rather than scientific resolution, dictates the legislative agenda. Relevance to open data lies in the erosion of public trust when statistical integrity becomes subject to political maneuvering. When governments obstruct transparency mechanisms like interpellations, the reliability of official statistics diminishes, undermining the foundational principles of accessible and verified data for democratic oversight and policy-making.
