Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Spanish internal R&D spending reached a significant milestone in 2022, driven primarily by corporate investment which now constitutes the main engine of national innovation growth. Despite this progress, the sector remains underperforming compared to global peers, highlighting a persistent gap in achieving strategic European targets. The reliance on large enterprises underscores a structural trend where major corporations, rather than public administrations or universities, are spearheading the country's technological advancement and economic competitiveness. This corporate dominance has fostered an emerging ecosystem of innovation hubs, particularly within the telecommunications and security sectors. Major firms are establishing physical centers dedicated to open innovation and co-creation, focusing on advanced technologies like AI, IoT, and cybersecurity. These initiatives represent a strategic shift towards integrating research with business development, aiming to create sustainable solutions and enhance customer satisfaction through cutting-edge experimentation and specialized talent attraction. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for transparency and interoperability within these new innovation frameworks. As companies centralize their R&D and adopt open innovation models, the generation, sharing, and analysis of data become critical assets. This trend demands robust open data practices to facilitate collaboration between the private sector, public institutions, and academia, ensuring that the valuable data produced in these hubs contributes to broader societal benefits and accelerates technological progress across the economy.
