Crecen al doble sanciones en contra de servidores públicos de Coahuila, revela el Inegi

Coahuila doubled administrative sanctions against public servants in 2022, rising from 21 to 44 individuals. This significant increase highlights improved internal oversight mechanisms within state secretariats to address irregularities and denunciations. However, the penalties applied were exclusively for non-serious infractions. This distinction suggests that while procedural compliance is being enforced, the state has not encountered or prosecuted major misconduct during this period. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the value of national statistical censuses like INEGI’s. Such transparent, accessible datasets enable public accountability by revealing trends in government administration and disciplinary actions across different states.
