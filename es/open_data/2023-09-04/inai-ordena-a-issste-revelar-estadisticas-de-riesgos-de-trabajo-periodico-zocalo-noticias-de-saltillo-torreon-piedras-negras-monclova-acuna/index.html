Inai ordena a ISSSTE revelar estadísticas de riesgos de trabajo | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INAI mandated ISSSTE to publish detailed work risk statistics, rejecting the institution's refusal to create specific documents. This ruling emphasizes that public authorities must provide exhaustive data on labor conditions to foster a culture of prevention. Such transparency enables evidence-based public policies that protect workers’ health and safety. By ensuring access to comprehensive labor statistics, society can make informed decisions that improve the lives of Mexican employees, particularly given their long working hours. This case highlights the critical role of open data in holding state institutions accountable. It demonstrates how enforcing transparency laws transforms raw information into actionable tools for social welfare, reinforcing the necessity of accessible public data for democratic oversight and policy improvement.
