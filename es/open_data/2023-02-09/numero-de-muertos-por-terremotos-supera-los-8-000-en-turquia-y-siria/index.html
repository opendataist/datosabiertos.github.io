Número de muertos por terremotos supera los 8.000 en Turquía y Siria

The devastating earthquakes in Turkey and Syria have caused thousands of deaths and injuries, highlighting the critical need for accurate, timely, and accessible disaster data. Reliable information enables coordinated international rescue efforts and efficient resource allocation in crisis zones. This situation underscores the importance of open data in humanitarian responses, where real-time updates on casualties and infrastructure damage guide decision-making. Transparent data sharing reduces confusion among diverse aid teams operating across borders. Open data practices ensure that all stakeholders, from governments to NGOs, access consistent information. This transparency fosters trust, improves operational efficiency, and ultimately saves lives during large-scale emergencies.
