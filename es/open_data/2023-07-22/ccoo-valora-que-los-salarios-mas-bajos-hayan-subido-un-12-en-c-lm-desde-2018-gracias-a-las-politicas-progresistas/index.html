CCOO valora que los salarios más bajos hayan subido un 12% en C-LM desde 2018 gracias a las políticas progresistas

The article highlights that rising wages, particularly in smaller enterprises, are largely driven by increased minimum wage policies, demonstrating that left-wing governance effectively protects vulnerable workers during economic crises. This suggests that political action is crucial for achieving equitable labor improvements, as smaller businesses saw significant relative gains compared to larger corporations. Analysis of labor cost data reveals persistent disparities based on company size and sector, with large firms offering substantially higher salaries than smaller ones. Although overall regional wages increased, the structure of pay remains unequal, indicating that while minimum wage hikes helped the lowest earners, structural issues regarding salary distribution across different business scales and industries persist. This data is relevant to open_data because it underscores the importance of accessible, detailed labor statistics in informing public policy and social advocacy. By making granular information about salaries and employment conditions available, researchers and civil society can better analyze economic trends, advocate for fairer labor practices, and hold governments accountable for their social and economic outcomes.
