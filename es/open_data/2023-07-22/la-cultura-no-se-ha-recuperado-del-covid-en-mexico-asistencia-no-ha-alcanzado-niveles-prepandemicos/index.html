La cultura no se ha recuperado del COVID en México: Asistencia no ha alcanzado niveles prepandémicos

The data reveals a significant decline in cultural participation, particularly among older demographics, highlighting a widening generational and gender gap in attendance. This trend is exacerbated by rising disinterest in the general population, which poses a challenge for inclusive cultural policies and public engagement strategies. Understanding these participation patterns is crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent statistics allow researchers and policymakers to identify accessibility barriers. By analyzing this information, stakeholders can design targeted interventions to bridge gaps and promote equitable access to cultural resources for all age groups and genders.
