Ocho diarios estadounidenses demandan a OpenAI y Microsoft por derechos de autor

This case highlights the intense legal conflict between tech giants and media publishers over copyright infringement in AI training. The central tension revolves around the use of publicly accessible internet data, with companies claiming fair use while publishers demand compensation or licensing. The relevance to open data is critical, as it questions the boundaries of accessing public information for commercial model development. It underscores the urgent need to balance data openness with intellectual property rights, influencing future access frameworks for training datasets.
