Participación en el mercado laboral de Costa Rica sigue estancado

The recent labor data from Costa Rica reveals a positive shift in employment dynamics, marked by a significant decline in unemployment and informal work. These trends suggest a strengthening job market where formal opportunities are expanding, thereby improving economic stability for workers and reducing the prevalence of precarious labor conditions. This transition is particularly notable for the female population, which saw substantial gains in both occupation and reduced unemployment rates. The data indicates that structural changes in the labor sector are benefiting women disproportionately compared to previous periods, highlighting progress toward greater gender equity in professional access and security. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of transparent, publicly available statistics from national institutes like INEC. Reliable, granular datasets enable policymakers and researchers to accurately track socio-economic indicators, fostering evidence-based decisions that address labor market disparities and promote inclusive growth.
