Censo: INE programa encuesta de cobertura con cuestionario electrónico del 23 de mayo al 22 de junio

The National Statistics Institute is launching a post-census survey to rigorously evaluate the coverage of the 2024 Population and Housing Census. This critical assessment aims to identify undercounting, verify residential locations, and characterize phenomena such as population displacement during the census period. By using electronic questionnaires across all departments, the INE ensures a comprehensive national and departmental analysis of occupancy and demographic shifts. This methodology allows for the quantification of census performance and the detection of potential errors or gaps in data collection. It serves as a vital quality control mechanism to validate the reliability of the initial census figures, ensuring that statistical outputs accurately reflect the reality of households and living conditions throughout the country. This process is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it establishes the credibility and transparency of official statistics. High-quality, verified public datasets are essential for fostering trust in government information, enabling researchers and citizens to access reliable data for analysis, policy-making, and accountability without the noise of unverified or incomplete records.
