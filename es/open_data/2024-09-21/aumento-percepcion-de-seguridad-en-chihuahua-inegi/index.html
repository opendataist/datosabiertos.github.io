Aumentó percepción de seguridad en Chihuahua: INEGI

The article highlights the critical distinction between subjective public perception and actual crime reality, a core tenet of open data analysis. By making victimization surveys publicly accessible, INEGI enables citizens to evaluate safety trends independently. This transparency allows society to observe that negative perceptions are declining significantly despite ongoing concerns about specific illegal behaviors. Such data empowers communities to form evidence-based opinions rather than relying solely on fear. Ultimately, this initiative demonstrates how government transparency fosters trust. Open data serves as a vital tool for democratic engagement, allowing the public to critically assess institutional performance and societal well-being.
