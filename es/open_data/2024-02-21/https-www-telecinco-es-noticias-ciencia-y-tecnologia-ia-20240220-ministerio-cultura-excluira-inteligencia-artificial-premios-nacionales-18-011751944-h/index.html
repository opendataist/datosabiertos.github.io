The Spanish Ministry of Culture proposes excluding works created exclusively by artificial intelligence from national awards to protect human creators' rights. This measure ensures that intellectual property laws are respected within the cultural sector, reinforcing the principle that artistic value stems from human creativity rather than automated generation. By integrating clauses into public grants and contracts, the administration mandates transparency regarding AI usage. These regulations require that artificial intelligence serves only as a supportive tool, never replacing human supervision or creative effort, thereby preserving the legal and ethical standards of property rights while allowing technological assistance in professional workflows. This initiative is significant for open data as it establishes clear precedents for transparency in AI applications within public funding. It highlights the tension between open technological access and the protection of human-generated content, urging stakeholders to define precise boundaries for algorithmic intervention in creative processes to ensure accountability and respect for intellectual property in the digital age.
