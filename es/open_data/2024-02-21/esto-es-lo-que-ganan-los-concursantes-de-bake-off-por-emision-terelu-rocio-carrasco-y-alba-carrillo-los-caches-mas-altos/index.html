Esto es lo que ganan los concursantes de 'Bake Off' por emisión: Terelu, Rocío Carrasco y Alba Carrillo, los cachés más altos

The success of "Bake Off: Famosos al horno" highlights the tangible value of open data initiatives, particularly regarding public broadcasting. By publishing detailed budget information through its Transparency Portal, RTVE demonstrates a commitment to fiscal accountability, allowing citizens and journalists to scrutinize how public funds are allocated for popular programming. Although individual contract specifics remain confidential, the published aggregate figures reveal the estimated costs associated with contestants and jurors. This approach ensures that while private negotiations between the broadcaster and celebrities are not disclosed, the overall financial structure of the production remains visible and auditable to the public eye. This transparency is crucial for understanding the economic realities behind public entertainment. It empowers society to evaluate the cost-effectiveness of cultural projects and reinforces trust in public institutions by proving that taxpayer money is managed with clarity and oversight, rather than hidden behind proprietary business details.
