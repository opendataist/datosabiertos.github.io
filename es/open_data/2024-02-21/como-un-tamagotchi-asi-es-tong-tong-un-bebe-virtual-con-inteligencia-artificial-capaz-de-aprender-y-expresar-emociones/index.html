Como un Tamagotchi: así es Tong Tong, un bebé virtual con inteligencia artificial capaz de aprender y expresar emociones

BIGAI presents Tong Tong, a virtual child AI capable of autonomous learning and emotional expression. This system marks a shift toward AI that evolves beyond static training data, behaving like a human child who adapts to circumstances and develops common sense over time. Its ability to refine linguistic skills and respond with humor makes it ideal for customer service and therapeutic support. By bridging the gap between bot and avatar, Tong Tong aims to forge emotional connections, demonstrating the potential for AI to understand social nuances and complex human interactions. This development is crucial for open data, as it highlights the necessity for diverse, dynamic datasets to train emotionally intelligent systems. It underscores how open access to varied human interaction data enables AI to learn social cues and empathy, essential for creating more natural and effective human-machine interfaces in real-world applications.
