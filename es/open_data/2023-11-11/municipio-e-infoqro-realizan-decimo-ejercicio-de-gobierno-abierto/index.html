Municipio e Infoqro realizan décimo ejercicio de Gobierno Abierto

The municipality implemented participatory governance, allowing residents to co-design public infrastructure. This initiative fosters community identity and ensures urban projects align with citizen needs and available budgets. This process highlights the relevance of open data by demonstrating how transparent, inclusive decision-making integrates local knowledge. It proves that sharing design and budget information empowers communities to validate and select public works, enhancing civic trust. Furthermore, collaboration with university engineers underscores the value of open technical data in public planning. By making design constraints and costs visible, the government enables informed citizen participation, reinforcing the link between transparency and effective democratic engagement.
