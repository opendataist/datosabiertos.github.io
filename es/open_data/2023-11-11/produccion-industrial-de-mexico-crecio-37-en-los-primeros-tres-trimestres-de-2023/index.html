Producción industrial de México creció 3,7% en los primeros tres trimestres de 2023

Mexican industrial production demonstrates robust growth in 2023, driven significantly by construction and energy sectors. This expansion aligns with broader economic recovery and record foreign direct investment, largely attributed to nearshoring trends that are reshaping regional supply chains. The data reveals a complex monthly landscape where manufacturing momentum contrasts with declines in construction and mining. These fluctuations highlight the nuanced dynamics of economic recovery following the pandemic-induced contraction, showcasing resilience across diverse industrial pillars. This report is vital for open data enthusiasts as it exemplifies how public statistical agencies like INEGI release granular, time-series data. Such transparency enables researchers to model economic trends, validate investment forecasts, and analyze the real-world impact of global trade shifts on national productivity metrics.
