Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Governor participated in the opening of the XII Ordinary General Assembly of the Federal Council for Transparency, underscoring the critical role of intergovernmental cooperation in establishing a unified national framework for transparency. This gathering highlights the shift from isolated provincial efforts to a coordinated federal dialogue, essential for strengthening institutional capacities and promoting citizen participation in governance across different jurisdictions. A key focus of the assembly is the advocacy for a Personal Data Protection bill, signaling a proactive stance on digital rights and privacy. By aligning provincial perspectives, the Council aims to influence national legislation, ensuring that data protection standards are robust and consistent. This collective approach demonstrates how regional entities can amplify their voice to shape federal policies that safeguard individual rights while enhancing public trust. This event is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessity of standardized, interoperable frameworks for information access and data privacy. Transparent, coordinated policies ensure that data remains a public asset rather than a fragmented resource. By fostering collaboration among provinces, the Council promotes an ecosystem where data availability and protection coexist, enabling citizens to hold authorities accountable while securing their personal information against misuse.
