YouTube y Netflix podrían lanzar futuros contenidos exclusivos para el códec AV1

The article highlights a critical shift in the streaming industry, where major platforms like YouTube and Netflix are effectively mandating AV1 codec support for future high-quality content. This strategic move leverages AV1’s superior efficiency and open-source nature to reduce bandwidth costs while maintaining high video quality. Consequently, device manufacturers are pressured to integrate hardware compatible with this new standard into their latest smart TV and Android TV releases to avoid obsolescence. This development is highly relevant to open data and open standards because it validates the practical power of open-source technologies in displacing proprietary, royalty-heavy alternatives. The widespread adoption of an open codec demonstrates how community-driven, transparent solutions can set industry-wide norms, forcing big tech companies to align their ecosystems with open specifications. This trend encourages innovation based on shared knowledge rather than restricted access, fostering a more competitive and accessible digital environment for developers and consumers alike. Ultimately, the implication is that technical compatibility will soon become a strict barrier to entry for media consumption. As manufacturers rush to update their product lines, the market will likely see AV1 becoming a standard feature rather than a premium option. This shift ensures that open standards drive hardware evolution, promoting interoperability and reducing fragmentation. Users will benefit from better efficiency and broader content availability, reinforcing the importance of open protocols in shaping the future of digital media infrastructure.
