Inauguran el Primer Encuentro Nacional de Ciencia de Datos 2023 - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The article highlights the First National Data Science Encounter 2023, convened to address critical challenges like cybersecurity and generative AI. This gathering underscores Puebla’s strategic ambition to become a regional innovation hub through coordinated efforts among major universities and the national supercomputing laboratory. A key focus is the synergy between higher education institutions and the private sector. Leaders emphasize that collaboration is essential to raise awareness of technology’s importance and to develop cutting-edge computational solutions, positioning the state as a unique center for data science advancement. This event is relevant to open_data as it illustrates the infrastructure and multi-stakeholder partnerships necessary to support open innovation. By discussing ethical implications and enterprise applications of data science, the encounter promotes transparency and the responsible sharing of technological resources, which are foundational principles for an open data ecosystem.
