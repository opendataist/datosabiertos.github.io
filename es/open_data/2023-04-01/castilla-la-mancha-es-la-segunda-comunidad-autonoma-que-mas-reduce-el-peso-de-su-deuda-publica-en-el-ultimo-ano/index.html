Castilla-La Mancha es la segunda Comunidad Autónoma que más reduce el peso de su deuda pública en el último año

Castilla-La Mancha significantly reduced its public debt burden relative to GDP, outperforming the national average for autonomous communities. This trend of fiscal consolidation aligns with maintaining sound public finances while prioritizing resources for vulnerable groups and economic activity. This achievement is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible economic statistics allow for clear monitoring of regional financial health. Such data empowers citizens and analysts to evaluate government performance, ensuring accountability in balancing fiscal responsibility with social welfare commitments.
