Anuncia Museos Puebla nuevo titular: Luis Ricardo Morán queda al frente

The leadership change at Museos Puebla reflects a strategic move toward open government principles. By appointing a director with specific training in open administration, the institution emphasizes transparency and citizen engagement. This shift is relevant to open data as it prioritizes officials who understand digital governance. Such leadership fosters better information accessibility, ensuring that cultural institutions operate with greater accountability and modern administrative standards for public benefit.
