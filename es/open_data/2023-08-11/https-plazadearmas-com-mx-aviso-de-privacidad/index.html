Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article outlines Plaza de Armas' data collection practices, demonstrating how media entities operationalize legal compliance under privacy laws. It details the systematic gathering of user information through cookies, log files, and third-party integrations like Google services and Facebook. This transparent disclosure aims to inform users about the extent of digital tracking and the mechanisms used to analyze site performance, prevent errors, and personalize advertising, thereby fulfilling regulatory obligations regarding personal data treatment. The implications for data governance are significant, as the text reveals a reliance on external vendors for monetization and analytics. By integrating tools such as Google Analytics and Ad Manager, the site aggregates behavioral data to optimize user experience and serve targeted ads. Furthermore, the use of embedded social media comments introduces additional layers of data sharing with external platforms. This structure highlights the complex ecosystem of data flow where a primary website acts as a conduit for extensive tracking by third-party services, raising questions about user consent and the transparency of these indirect data exchanges. This content is relevant to open data because it represents the counterpart to open government information: private sector transparency. While open data advocates for the public release of non-personal datasets, this privacy notice illustrates the necessary legal frameworks that protect individual privacy while allowing for the analysis of aggregate behavior. Understanding such notices helps clarify the boundaries between open, public information and protected personal data, emphasizing that even in open ecosystems, responsible data stewardship requires clear communication about how user information is collected, used, and shared.
