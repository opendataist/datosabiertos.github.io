Jueza ordena al Senado nombrar consejero del INAI; de negarse violará mandato judicial

The article highlights a critical conflict in Mexico where the Senate blocked the appointment of an INAI commissioner, rendering the autonomous institution inoperative by lacking the legal minimum number of members. This political obstruction, driven by strong criticism from the executive branch, directly undermines the agency's ability to function and protect citizens' rights to information. The relevance to open data is profound, as an ineffective INAI jeopardizes transparency and accountability mechanisms essential for public scrutiny. When oversight bodies are paralyzed, access to public information becomes fragile, weakening democratic structures and eroding trust in government institutions that are meant to guarantee data openness and legal compliance for all citizens.
