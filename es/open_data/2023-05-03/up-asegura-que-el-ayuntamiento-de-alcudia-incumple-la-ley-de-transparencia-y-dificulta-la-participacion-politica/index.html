UP asegura que el Ayuntamiento de Alcúdia "incumple" la Ley de Transparencia y "dificulta" la participación política

The local government faces criticism for failing to comply with Balearic Transparency laws, hindering political participation. The opposition demands telematic access to administrative records to ensure proper oversight. This highlights open data’s role in enabling effective civic control. When governments withhold digital information, it prevents the public and representatives from holding power accountable. True transparency requires proactive data sharing. This case illustrates how legal mandates must translate into accessible digital infrastructure to protect democratic rights and ensure governmental accountability.
