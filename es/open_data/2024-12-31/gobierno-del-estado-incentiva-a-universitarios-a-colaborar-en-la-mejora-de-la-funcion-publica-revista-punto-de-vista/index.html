Gobierno del Estado incentiva a universitarios a colaborar en la mejora de la función pública | Revista Punto de Vista

The San Luis Potosí government actively engages university students in the Open Government model, inviting them to submit technological prototypes that enhance public services. This initiative bridges the gap between academia and the state, fostering innovation through AI and sustainable technologies to improve administrative efficiency and citizen interaction. Such collaboration highlights the transformative power of youth-led technological solutions in modernizing governance structures. By integrating student ingenuity into public policy, authorities aim to create more responsive and transparent systems that directly address societal needs while strengthening the trust relationship between the state and its citizens. This effort is crucial for open data as it demonstrates a practical pathway for integrating citizen-generated insights into governmental decision-making. It encourages the development of innovative, tech-driven public services, proving that active participation and accessible information are key to achieving effective, inclusive, and modern open government practices.
