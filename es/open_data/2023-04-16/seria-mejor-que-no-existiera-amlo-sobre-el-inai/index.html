Sería mejor que no existiera: AMLO sobre el Inai

The President argues that existing oversight bodies already handle transparency duties, questioning the necessity and cost of the National Institute for Transparency. He criticizes the Institute as an ineffective facade that previously concealed corruption rather than ensuring accountability. This highlights a critical tension in open data governance, where political pressure threatens the independence of institutions designed to safeguard public information access.
