Las ventas en supermercados cayeron en la medición interanual, pero registran una leve suba este año, según el Indec

Retail sales reveal divergent trends amid high inflation and income inequality, with supermarkets contracting while major shopping centers surged. This polarization highlights a deepening social divide, as wealthier consumers maintain spending power while others reduce consumption. The data underscores how price surges distort nominal growth, masking real stagnation in essential goods. Consumers are shifting toward larger formats, straining smaller retailers. This shift reflects a fragile economic landscape where purchasing power is increasingly unequal. These insights are crucial for open data, as they demonstrate how transparent statistical analysis exposes underlying economic pressures. Public access to such granular details allows for better understanding of consumer behavior and policy impacts during inflationary periods.
