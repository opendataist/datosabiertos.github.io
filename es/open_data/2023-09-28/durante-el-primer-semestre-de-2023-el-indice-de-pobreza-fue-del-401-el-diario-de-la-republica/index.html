Durante el primer semestre de 2023, el índice de pobreza fue del 40,1% | El Diario de la República

Recent data reveals a concerning rise in poverty and indigence rates, driven by significant inflation outpacing salary growth. This economic contraction directly impacts household stability, highlighting how rapidly changing macroeconomic variables can erode social welfare indicators. For open data practitioners, this case underscores the critical need for transparent, timely, and accessible public statistics. Accurate tracking of such metrics is essential for holding institutions accountable and enabling civil society to analyze the real-world impact of fiscal policies on vulnerable populations.
