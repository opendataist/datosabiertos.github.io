En 8 años crecieron solicitudes de información al Gobierno de Veracruz

The surge in transparency requests highlights a critical gap in public administration capacity. With fewer than half of state agencies possessing dedicated transparency units, the system struggles to meet the growing demand for information. This institutional deficiency threatens the effective realization of citizens' right to access public data. The high volume of inquiries across Mexico indicates increasing public engagement with transparency mechanisms. However, the response rates reveal significant limitations, as a substantial portion of requests are denied or declared incompetent. This pattern suggests that many institutions remain unprepared to handle open data obligations efficiently. This scenario is vital for open data because it underscores the urgent need for structural modernization. Strengthening transparency units is essential to ensure that public information is not only available but also accessible and usable. Enhancing institutional capacity will foster greater accountability and trust in democratic governance.
