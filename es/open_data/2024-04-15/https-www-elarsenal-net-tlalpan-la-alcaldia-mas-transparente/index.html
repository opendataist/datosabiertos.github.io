Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Tlalpan achieved top national rankings in the 2023 Open Government Metric, demonstrating exceptional leadership in transparency and citizen participation. This success highlights how rigorous measurement frameworks can effectively drive administrative improvements and establish clear standards for accountability. The assessment underscores the critical value of standardized tools in evaluating government openness. By measuring how accessible information is and the extent of public influence on decisions, such metrics provide essential benchmarks for local authorities seeking to enhance democratic engagement and operational integrity. This case is highly relevant to open data as it proves that robust data transparency directly correlates with better governance outcomes. It illustrates that when municipalities prioritize accessible information, they not only comply with oversight requirements but also foster a more inclusive and responsive public sector, serving as a replicable model for other jurisdictions.
