IA debe garantizar la protección de datos personales y la no discriminación: comisionada del INAI

Josefina Román Vergara emphasized that artificial intelligence must ensure the proper handling of personal data and avoid discrimination. She highlighted the risks of biased algorithms, urging society to reflect on how AI treats different demographics unequally. This perspective places the ethical management of data at the core of technological deployment, stressing the need for transparency and fairness in automated systems. Norma Julieta del Río Venegas and Adrián Alcalá Méndez stressed the indispensable role of the INAI in protecting access to information and personal data. They warned that without autonomous guaranteeing bodies, individuals would remain vulnerable to indiscriminate data use. The implementation of AI for search improvement demonstrates technology’s potential, yet it must never compromise fundamental privacy rights, requiring robust institutional oversight to maintain balance. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical intersection between transparency initiatives and privacy protection in the digital age. It illustrates that while open data platforms leverage AI for efficiency, they must also integrate rigorous safeguards against bias and misuse. The discourse reinforces that sustainable open government requires not just data availability, but also ethical AI governance and strong institutional frameworks to protect citizen rights in an increasingly automated society.
