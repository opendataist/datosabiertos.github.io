IMSS debe entregar información sobre atención a víctimas

The INAI ordered the IMSS to release detailed data on sexual assault victim care, emphasizing that transparency empowers citizens to influence public policy. This ensures medical attention meets victims' needs, reinforcing the social utility of information access as a tool for protecting human rights. This ruling highlights the critical need for gender-perspective data to hold the state accountable. Reliable, updated information is essential for designing effective public policies against violence toward women, moving beyond initial claims of zero cases to actual statistical reality. This case is relevant to open data because it establishes that government agencies must proactively share granular, disaggregated information. Without such transparency, societal participation in designing effective interventions remains impossible, undermining the core principles of open government and data-driven accountability.
