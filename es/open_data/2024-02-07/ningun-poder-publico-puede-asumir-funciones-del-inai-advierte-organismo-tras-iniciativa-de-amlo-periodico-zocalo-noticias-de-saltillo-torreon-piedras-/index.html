‘Ningún poder público’ puede asumir funciones del INAI, advierte organismo tras iniciativa de AMLO | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INAI demonstrates willingness to discuss presidential reforms aimed at eliminating autonomous bodies, emphasizing open dialogue and public deliberation as democratic essentials. This stance reflects an intent to engage constructively while ensuring the institution’s core mission remains protected during any constitutional transition or debate. However, the authority firmly rejects the notion that any public power can assume its functions, asserting that essential roles like transparency and data protection require an independent, specialized, and impartial body. Merging these duties into existing entities would compromise the necessary guarantees for human rights regarding information access and privacy. This development is crucial for open data because it highlights the vital link between institutional autonomy and data integrity. Without a protected, independent watchdog, the mechanisms ensuring transparent governance and public access to information are at risk of being compromised by political control or lack of impartiality.
