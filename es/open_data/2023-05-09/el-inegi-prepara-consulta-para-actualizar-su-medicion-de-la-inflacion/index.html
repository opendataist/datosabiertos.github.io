El Inegi prepara consulta para actualizar su medición de la inflación

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (Inegi) is initiating a public consultation to modernize the Consumer Price Index (INPC), ensuring it accurately reflects post-pandemic consumption habits. This update is crucial because the previous adjustments date back to 2018 and no longer capture significant shifts in household spending patterns. By engaging citizens in this process, the institute aims to refine the basket of goods and services, incorporating emerging trends such as increased spending on pet care and digital streaming platforms while reducing the weight of physical media consumption. A central objective of this revision is the implementation of chained annual indices, which align with international recommendations and facilitate more frequent updates. This methodological shift allows for the seamless integration of new items and the removal of obsolete ones, addressing the limitations of the static weighting system used previously. The process will also leverage data from the National Income and Expenditure Survey to adjust the structure of weights, ensuring that the index remains a precise instrument for measuring inflation and economic well-being in a rapidly changing market. This initiative is highly relevant to the open data community as it demonstrates the importance of periodic data governance and public participation in maintaining statistical integrity. Openly consulting citizens not only improves the accuracy of essential economic indicators but also promotes transparency in how public data is constructed. Furthermore, the reliance on updated household surveys underscores the need for accessible, high-quality underlying datasets. For developers and researchers, these changes signal evolving variables that must be accounted for in future economic analyses and applications relying on Mexican inflation metrics.
