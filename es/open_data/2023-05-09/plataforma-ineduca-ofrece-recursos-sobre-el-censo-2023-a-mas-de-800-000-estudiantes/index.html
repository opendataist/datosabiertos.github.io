Plataforma Ineduca ofrece recursos sobre el Censo 2023 a más de 800.000 estudiantes

The joint initiative between the National Statistics Institute and the Education Administration introduces Ineduca, a dedicated digital platform offering diverse educational resources regarding the upcoming Census. By providing videos, didactic suggestions, and interactive games, the project aims to effectively engage a vast network of schools, ensuring that students and teachers are well-informed about the census's purpose and methodology. This collaborative effort highlights how institutional partnerships can democratize access to statistical information, making complex data topics accessible and engaging for younger audiences. A key focus is empowering students to act as multipliers who disseminate the importance of participation within their families. By encouraging households to engage with the census, the initiative leverages the social influence of youth to improve response rates and public understanding. This strategy transforms the census from a mere administrative task into a community-wide educational experience, fostering a culture of civic participation and awareness about national development needs. This effort is crucial for open data because it builds long-term public trust and literacy in statistical processes. When citizens understand the value and mechanics of data collection, they are more likely to support transparency and accurate reporting. Furthermore, by integrating census education into the curriculum, the state ensures that future generations view data as a vital tool for societal planning, thereby strengthening the social contract necessary for sustainable open data ecosystems and informed public policy.
