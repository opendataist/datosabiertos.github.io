Bienestar debe transparentar censo a damnificados: INAI

The INAI mandated the Welfare Secretariat to locate and disclose data on hurricane victims. This ruling counters the agency's claim of lacking records, ensuring critical impact statistics are accessible for public transparency. Such accountability is vital for open data frameworks, particularly during crises. It guarantees that citizen requests for detailed disaster impact metrics are not dismissed without thorough administrative searches, maintaining trust in government information systems. Ultimately, this case reinforces the principle that governments must actively document and share data about public welfare events. It highlights the necessity of robust, searchable databases to support accurate, open information regarding natural disasters and their societal effects.
