Las residencias gallegas tendrán un registro electrónico de usuarios único y accesible por la Xunta en marzo

Galicia implements a unified electronic registry for all residents in elderly and disability care centers, consolidating previously fragmented physical or digital records. This centralized system ensures that updated user information is instantly accessible to the regional administration, fundamentally transforming how facility data is managed and monitored across the region. The initiative, driven by lessons learned during the pandemic, highlights the critical inefficiency of isolated record-keeping during emergencies. By establishing a continuous, up-to-date census, authorities can now perform permanent occupancy controls and respond immediately to exceptional circumstances, addressing the previous delays inherent in requiring formal requests or physical visits for inspection purposes. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the power of centralized, standardized digital infrastructure in public administration. While currently limited to basic identification details rather than full health records, the system creates a foundational model for interoperable public services. It illustrates how moving from siloed records to a shared digital ecosystem enhances transparency, improves coordination with the healthcare system, and provides a replicable framework for future expansions of accessible, real-time public data.
