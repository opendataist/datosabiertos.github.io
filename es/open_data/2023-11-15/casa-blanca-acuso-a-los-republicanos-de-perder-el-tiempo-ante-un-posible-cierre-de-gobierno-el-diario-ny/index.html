Casa Blanca acusó a los republicanos de "perder el tiempo" ante un posible cierre de gobierno - El Diario NY

The White House accuses Republican leadership of wasting critical time in budget negotiations, warning that their internal divisions and extreme proposals jeopardize the continuation of government funding. This political dysfunction threatens a temporary government shutdown, which would disrupt essential services and compromise national security. The administration urges Republicans to abandon partisan infighting and engage in bipartisan cooperation to maintain open government operations. Negotiations are under a tight deadline, with a recent extension providing a short window to agree on long-term spending before funds expire again. The political turmoil intensified after the previous House Speaker was ousted due to disagreements over budget deals, leaving the current leadership struggling to unite their party. Consequently, the situation remains unstable, with the administration expressing deep concern over the lack of serious legislative progress and the risk of a shutdown caused by Republican intransigence. This article is relevant to open data because government shutdowns directly impact the availability and accessibility of public records and statistical resources. When agencies close or operate under continuing resolutions, the flow of data to the public and researchers is often halted or delayed. Ensuring open data ecosystems function reliably requires stable government funding, highlighting the importance of political stability for maintaining transparent, continuous access to societal information.
