La inflación se mantiene en el 3,5% en octubre por la bajada de los carburantes y la moderación de los alimentos

The article confirms that Spanish inflation stabilized at 3.5% in October, driven primarily by a significant moderation in food and fuel prices. This slowdown represents a positive economic signal, indicating that the cost-of-living pressures are easing as core inflation reaches its lowest level since the previous June, reflecting the effectiveness of recent government economic policies. This development is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of publicly accessible, high-quality statistical datasets in monitoring macroeconomic trends. Transparent reporting from national institutes allows researchers, policymakers, and citizens to verify economic health, assess policy impact, and make informed decisions based on reliable metrics rather than speculation. Ultimately, the stabilization of consumer prices demonstrates how open data infrastructure supports democratic accountability and economic analysis. By providing clear, verifiable evidence of price evolution across various sectors, these datasets enable a deeper understanding of how specific goods and services influence overall inflation, fostering trust in public institutions and facilitating evidence-based dialogue on fiscal measures.
