En primer semestre de 2023, asesinados 153 menores

This article highlights the severe vulnerability of children to violence, revealing that gender and weapon type significantly impact homicide rates among minors. Such data exposes systemic failures in child protection, demanding urgent policy reforms rather than mere statistical reporting. The prevalence of gun-related deaths and specific regional hotspots indicates a need for targeted public safety interventions. Understanding these patterns is crucial for identifying where protective measures are most critically lacking in Mexican society. For open data, these statistics exemplify the necessity of transparent, accessible datasets to hold governments accountable. Without open access to such granular evidence, civil society cannot effectively advocate for the fundamental rights and safety of the youngest citizens.
