Georgia anima a los diplomáticos occidentales a sumarse a las discusiones de polémica ley - Mundo - ABC Color

The article highlights the tension in Georgia regarding new legislation mandating financial disclosures for NGOs and media. The government frames this as a European transparency value, dismissing opposition as attempts to overthrow democracy, while critics argue it mirrors Russian tactics to suppress dissent. International condemnation, particularly from the EU, criticizes the state’s violent response to peaceful protests. This clash underscores the fragility of civil society rights in candidate nations, raising concerns about democratic backsliding despite the path toward European integration. This case is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how transparency laws can be manipulated to restrict rather than enhance civic freedom. It warns that mandatory data publication without safeguards can become a tool for political repression, challenging the open data community to distinguish between genuine accountability and authoritarian control mechanisms.
