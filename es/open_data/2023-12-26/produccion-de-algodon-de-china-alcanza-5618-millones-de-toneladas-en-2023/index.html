Producción de algodón de China alcanza 5,618 millones de toneladas en 2023

China’s 2023 cotton production declined due to adverse weather and reduced planting areas, particularly in Xinjiang. However, yield per hectare improved slightly nationwide, reflecting better management in other regions. This data highlights the critical role of accurate agricultural statistics in monitoring food security and supply chain resilience, essential components of transparent open data practices for global markets.
