Exhorta INAI a proteger datos personales para evitar ser víctima de ciberdelitos

The INAI emphasizes the urgent need for citizens to protect personal data during the vacation season, a period marked by a significant surge in online transactions. This guidance highlights the critical relationship between individual digital hygiene and broader information security standards, serving as a practical case study for open data principles. Key recommendations include restricting public network usage, securing mobile devices with encryption, and avoiding the real-time sharing of location data on social media. These actions mitigate the risk of identity theft and financial fraud, illustrating how individual behaviors impact the integrity and privacy of digital ecosystems. This article is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of robust privacy frameworks when personal information is processed or shared. It reinforces that transparency and access must be balanced with security measures, ensuring that open initiatives do not compromise citizen safety or enable misuse of personal details.
