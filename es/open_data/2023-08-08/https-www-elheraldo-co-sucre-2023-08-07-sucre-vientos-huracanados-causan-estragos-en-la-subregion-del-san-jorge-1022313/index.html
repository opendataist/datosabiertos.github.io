Hurricane-force winds devastated parts of Sucre, Colombia, causing widespread damage to homes, trees, and power lines. Community reports highlight significant impacts in Caimito and San Marcos, though official casualty or damage statistics remain unverified by authorities. This incident underscores the critical need for open_data in emergency management. Reliable, transparent data on disasters facilitates faster resource allocation and accountability, ensuring that affected communities receive timely support based on accurate information rather than rumors. Open_data standards are essential for bridging the gap between local reports and official records. By making disaster data accessible and verifiable, stakeholders can improve response efficiency, foster trust, and implement better predictive models for future extreme weather events.
