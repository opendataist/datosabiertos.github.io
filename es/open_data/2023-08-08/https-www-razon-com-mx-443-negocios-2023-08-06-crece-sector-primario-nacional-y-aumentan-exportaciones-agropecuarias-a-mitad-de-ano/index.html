The national primary sector demonstrates robust economic stability and growth in the first half of the year. This resilience is characterized by a steady increase in gross domestic product for primary activities, outperforming secondary sectors. The government highlights this trend as evidence of effective management, ensuring consistent food supply across the country without shortages, which supports social stability and economic continuity. Simultaneously, agricultural and fisheries exports reach historic levels, generating significant foreign exchange. The surge in international sales for key products like strawberries and beef not only boosts the trade surplus but also surpasses revenue from traditional pillars such as tourism and petroleum. This shift underscores the increasing strategic importance of the agri-food sector in the national economy, providing substantial financial inflows and job creation opportunities. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it illustrates the tangible socioeconomic impact of transparent government statistics. By making granular export and production metrics publicly available, institutions like INEGI enable researchers and citizens to analyze trade balances and sectoral performance. Such accessibility fosters accountability, allowing for better policy evaluation and demonstrating how raw data translates into national wealth and food security.
