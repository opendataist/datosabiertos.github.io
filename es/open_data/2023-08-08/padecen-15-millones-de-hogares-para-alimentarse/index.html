Padecen 15 millones de hogares para alimentarse

The survey reveals that rising food prices significantly impacted Mexican households' ability to meet dietary needs in 2022. Although the absolute number of affected families decreased compared to previous years, inflation masked what would have been an even worse situation. This highlights the critical role of open data in tracking multidimensional poverty indicators. Accurate, accessible statistics allow researchers to contextualize economic pressures and identify underlying trends. Without transparent datasets, it is impossible to distinguish between genuine improvements and temporary fluctuations caused by external shocks. Open data thus serves as a vital tool for evaluating social welfare policies accurately. Relevance to open data lies in its capacity to foster accountability and informed decision-making. By making such granular household expenditure information publicly available, society can better understand structural challenges. This transparency ensures that poverty measurements reflect reality, guiding effective interventions and resource allocation.
