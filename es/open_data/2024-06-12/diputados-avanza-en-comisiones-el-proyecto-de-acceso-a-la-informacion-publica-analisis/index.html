Diputados: avanza en comisiones el proyecto de acceso a la información pública | Análisis

Provincial legislators are debating a new legal framework to guarantee free, timely, and comprehensive access to public information. This initiative aims to establish a dedicated office within the Ministry of Government to actively promote transparency and ensure citizens can effectively exercise their right to know, addressing a long-standing constitutional debt. The proposal elevates administrative decrees to a higher legislative rank, aligning provincial rules with national standards. By creating a specialized body to manage these requests, the law seeks to modernize state institutions, ensuring that all government branches and public entities provide accurate data without unnecessary bureaucratic hurdles. This development is highly relevant to open data because it legally mandates the availability of public information, laying the groundwork for data transparency. Such legislative frameworks are essential for transforming raw government data into accessible, usable resources, thereby fostering accountability and enabling civil society to leverage public information for informed decision-making and oversight.
