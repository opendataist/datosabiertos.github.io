Solicita PRI a la SHCP informe de recorte presupuestario al INAI

The PRI parliamentary group has demanded a detailed report from the Ministry of Finance regarding the significant and continuous budget cuts imposed on the INAI since 2018. This legislative action highlights the severe financial deterioration of the institution, which has suffered substantial nominal and real decreases in both its operational budget and commissioners' salaries. The inquiry seeks to understand the reasons behind these reductions and the impact of unspent funds on the agency’s ability to function effectively despite government austerity measures. Furthermore, the article underscores a critical governance crisis: the INAI lacks a quorum, preventing it from holding plenary sessions and performing essential duties. Without the full complement of commissioners, the institute cannot issue resolutions on data protection requests, propose sanctions for non-compliance, or fully oversee transparency laws. This operational paralysis threatens the core mechanisms of accountability and protection that the institution was designed to provide to citizens. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how political and financial pressures directly undermine institutional integrity. When oversight bodies are starved of resources and leadership, the transparency infrastructure collapses, leaving citizens with reduced access to information and weaker protections for their personal data. It serves as a cautionary example of how defunding transparency agencies compromises the very foundations of an open and accountable society.
