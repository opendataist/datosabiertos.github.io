OMS: El mundo perdió 337 MM de años de vida en 2020-21 por COVID-19

The WHO report reveals a devastating loss of 337 million years of life during the 2020-2021 pandemic period, highlighting the severe global health impact. This staggering figure underscores how premature mortality has drastically reduced collective lifespan, serving as a critical metric to quantify human suffering beyond mere death counts. Furthermore, the data indicates that progress toward universal health coverage and Sustainable Development Goals has stalled. Many health indicators remain behind, suggesting that the pandemic reversed gains made previously. Without urgent acceleration of efforts, societies risk failing to improve quality of life and preventable deaths will continue to rise. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical reporting drives global policy awareness. By making complex health metrics publicly accessible, such data empowers researchers and citizens to hold institutions accountable, fostering informed dialogue on equity and health system resilience in an open information ecosystem.
