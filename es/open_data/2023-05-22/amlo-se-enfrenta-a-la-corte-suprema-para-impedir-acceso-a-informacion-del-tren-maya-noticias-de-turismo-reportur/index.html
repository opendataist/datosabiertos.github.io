AMLO se enfrenta a la Corte Suprema para impedir acceso a información del Tren Maya | Noticias de turismo REPORTUR

The INAI challenged a presidential decree that classifies major infrastructure projects, such as the Tren Maya, as national security issues. This move effectively restricts public access to relevant information, directly opposing judicial suspensions intended to protect transparency rights. By reclassifying these public works under security exemptions, the government limits citizens' right to know about the planning and execution of projects funded by public resources. The INAI argues this violates constitutional guarantees and undermines democratic accountability, seeking to extend previous court rulings to invalidate this specific decree. This conflict is crucial for open data advocates, as it highlights the tension between state security claims and the fundamental principle of transparency. It underscores the ongoing struggle to ensure that critical public information remains accessible, preventing the misuse of security classifications to hide operational details or prevent public scrutiny of government spending.
