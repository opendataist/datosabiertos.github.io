Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article highlights that during the Hot Sale, the intensive use of personal data by companies for marketing and transactions significantly increases privacy risks. Consequently, the national transparency institute issued recommendations to protect consumers, emphasizing that individuals must carefully identify sellers and understand the specific purposes for which their information will be used before sharing it. Shoppers are advised to adopt strict digital hygiene practices, such as avoiding public Wi-Fi networks, ensuring device security, and verifying the authenticity of websites to prevent fraud. It is crucial to provide only the necessary data, read privacy notices, and actively manage consent settings to stop unauthorized marketing use. Furthermore, monitoring bank alerts and utilizing secure digital payment tools helps detect and mitigate potential financial threats early. This content is vital for open data because it illustrates the delicate balance between commercial data exploitation and individual privacy rights. It underscores the necessity for transparent data handling practices and empowers citizens to exercise their control over personal information, reinforcing the importance of accountability and security in the digital economy.
