CEFIM ofrece páginas web para municipios

The state government in San Luis Potosí collaborates with numerous municipalities to provide free, secure web hosting and email accounts. This initiative ensures compliance with transparency laws, allowing local governments to reliably publish required information and maintain an open government presence. This centralized service significantly reduces budget expenditures for individual towns while guaranteeing data security. It is particularly vital for remote areas with connectivity issues, offering technical support and capacity building. Consequently, it serves as a successful national model for subsidizing digital infrastructure to support administrative obligations. This program is highly relevant to open data as it removes financial and technical barriers that often prevent municipalities from publishing data. By standardizing and securing the platforms where information is released, the state ensures that transparency mandates are met effectively. It demonstrates how shared digital infrastructure can facilitate broader access to public information across all local levels of government.
