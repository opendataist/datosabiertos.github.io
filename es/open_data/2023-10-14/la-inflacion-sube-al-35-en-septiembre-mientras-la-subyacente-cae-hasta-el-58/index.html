La inflación sube al 3,5% en septiembre mientras la subyacente cae hasta el 5,8%

The article highlights how rising energy and fuel costs have driven September’s inflation to its highest level since April, underscoring the persistent pressure on household budgets. Although underlying inflation shows slight improvement, the overall index remains elevated, reflecting the complex interplay between volatile energy prices and broader economic trends in the Eurozone. Key drivers include significant increases in housing and transport costs, alongside sustained high prices for essential food items. This data reveals a stark contrast between stable sectors and those heavily impacted by global supply chain fluctuations and geopolitical factors, illustrating the nuanced reality of consumer price dynamics.
