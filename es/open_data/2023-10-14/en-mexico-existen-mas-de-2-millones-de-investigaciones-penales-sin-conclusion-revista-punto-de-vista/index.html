En México existen más de 2 Millones de investigaciones penales sin conclusión | Revista Punto de Vista

Mexico’s judicial system faces mounting pressure, evidenced by rising criminal investigations and a significant backlog of unresolved cases. This data highlights systemic inefficiencies, particularly as state attorneys handle the vast majority of pending matters. The struggle for transparency is critical, as individuals often encounter obstacles accessing their investigation files. Legal precedents emphasize that due process and information access are fundamental rights, not merely procedural formalities. This article underscores the vital role of open data in identifying judicial bottlenecks. Transparent statistics enable public scrutiny and drive necessary reforms to ensure justice is accessible and effective for all citizens.
