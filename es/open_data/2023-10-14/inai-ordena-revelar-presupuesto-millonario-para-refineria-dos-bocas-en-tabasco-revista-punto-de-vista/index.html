Inai ordena revelar presupuesto millonario para refinería Dos Bocas en Tabasco | Revista Punto de Vista

The National Institute for Transparency (Inai) has ordered the Ministry of Energy to disclose detailed budget information regarding the Dos Bocas refinery. This decision addresses initial claims by the ministry that no resources had been allocated, which contradicted public data and raised serious doubts about administrative honesty. The ruling emphasizes that public accountability is essential for large-scale projects, especially when initial cost estimates have doubled significantly. Transparency is the only way to evaluate whether the investment yields a favorable cost-benefit ratio and to justify the substantial use of public funds as construction continues. This case is vital for open data because it demonstrates how independent bodies can compel the release of critical government spending information. By forcing the disclosure of budget execution data, it reinforces the principle that citizens have the right to access accurate, granular financial records to monitor public resource management effectively.
