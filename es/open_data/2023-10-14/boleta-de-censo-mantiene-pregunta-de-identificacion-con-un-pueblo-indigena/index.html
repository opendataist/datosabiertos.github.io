Boleta de censo mantiene pregunta de identificación con un pueblo indígena

The INE introduces a streamlined census questionnaire designed to efficiently gather precise demographic data. By refining the structure based on experimental feedback, the updated form facilitates easier completion while ensuring comprehensive coverage of household and individual characteristics. This approach aims to enhance data accuracy for better public policy formulation. Crucially, the survey captures detailed information on migration patterns and mortality causes, including recent health crises. It also highlights social structures by including questions on indigenous identity and educational attainment. These metrics are vital for understanding population distribution and the specific needs of marginalized communities across Bolivia. This article is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how government statistical agencies can improve data collection methodologies. By making censuses more accessible and precise, authorities can generate higher-quality open datasets. Such transparent, well-structured data empowers researchers and citizens to analyze social trends and hold governments accountable for evidence-based policy decisions.
