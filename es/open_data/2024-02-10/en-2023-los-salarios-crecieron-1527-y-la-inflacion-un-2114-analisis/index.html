En 2023 los salarios crecieron 152,7 % y la inflación un 211,4% | Análisis

The report highlights a significant erosion in purchasing power, as salary growth trailed substantially behind retail inflation. This disparity implies that workers across all sectors experienced a real decline in income, worsening economic conditions for both formal and informal employees despite nominal wage increases. The government’s convening of the Council for a new minimum wage threshold underscores the urgency of addressing this gap. Labor unions are actively negotiating to recover lost ground, emphasizing that current policy dialogues are critical for restoring the financial stability of the most vulnerable populations who have been increasingly impoverished. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it reveals the complex dynamics between official wage indices, inflation metrics, and diverse employment categories. By providing transparent, granular statistics on salary evolution, such datasets empower analysts and citizens to accurately assess economic inequality and hold institutions accountable for fair labor practices and effective social protection policies.
