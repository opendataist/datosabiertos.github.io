La producción minera y de hidrocarburos crece un 9,7 % en 2023

Argentina’s industrial output in hydrocarbons and mining grew significantly in 2023, driven by robust increases in oil services and non-metallic minerals like lithium. However, this positive trend contrasts sharply with substantial declines in precious metal extraction, highlighting a divergent performance across resource sectors. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of granular, sector-specific statistics in understanding national economic shifts. Detailed, publicly accessible indicators allow researchers to analyze not just aggregate growth, but the structural changes within specific industries, revealing where investment and production are concentrating or receding. Ultimately, providing clear metrics on these contrasting trends enables better policy analysis and market transparency. By making such detailed production indices available, governments and analysts can more accurately assess the sustainability of resource-dependent growth and the shifting dynamics of the extractive industry.
