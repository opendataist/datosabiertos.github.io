“Claridad” ofrece Pepe Chedraui en su administración, ante cualquier solicitud de información

The Mayor of Puebla launched a virtual training course to enhance government transparency, emphasizing that officials must effectively handle information requests. This initiative aims to ensure that public resources are managed honestly and that the administration remains accountable to citizens who actively participate in public policy creation. By providing staff with the necessary tools to clarify resource usage, the government seeks to build trust and foster open dialogue. The training reinforces the commitment to clear communication, allowing residents to verify the proper use of funds and engage meaningfully with municipal decisions. This effort is crucial for open data as it promotes the proactive disclosure of public information. Improved transparency mechanisms facilitate better access to government data, enabling civil society to monitor performance and hold leaders accountable, thereby strengthening democratic engagement and institutional integrity.
