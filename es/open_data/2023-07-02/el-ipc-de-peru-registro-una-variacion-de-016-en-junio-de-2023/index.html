El IPC de Perú registró una variación de -0,16% en junio de 2023

The latest economic data from Peru reveals a nuanced inflation landscape, characterized by a decline in consumer prices for basic necessities offset by increases in services and specific food items. This divergence highlights the volatility within the market, where reductions in food and transport costs provided relief to households, while rising expenses in hospitality, health, and personal services maintained upward pressure on overall living costs. Understanding these opposing trends is crucial for interpreting the true economic pulse of the nation. From an open data perspective, this report exemplifies the value of granular, disaggregated statistics. By breaking down inflation into specific categories and geographic regions, the data transforms abstract macroeconomic indicators into actionable insights. The detailed breakdown of price changes for individual products allows researchers and policymakers to identify precise drivers of economic behavior, moving beyond aggregate numbers to understand the structural shifts affecting different sectors of the economy. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for transparency and accessibility in such detailed reporting. When statistical agencies publish comprehensive indices, they empower citizens, businesses, and analysts to make informed decisions based on verifiable evidence. This level of detail fosters accountability and enables the public to scrutinize economic policies, ensuring that data serves not just as a historical record, but as a tool for democratic engagement and effective resource allocation.
