Queda sin energía eléctrica el INEGI y dos edificios por una sobrecarga que incendió un transformador

A power surge caused an INEGI facility transformer fire, disrupting electricity for the institute and surrounding local businesses. Emergency crews extinguished the blaze, while utility technicians isolated the damaged unit to prevent further incidents. This event highlights the critical physical infrastructure supporting statistical data collection. Operational continuity relies on stable power, making infrastructure resilience a key concern for maintaining accurate and timely public information. For open data, this underscores that data accessibility is vulnerable to localized physical failures. Ensuring robust power backups and contingency plans is essential to protect the integrity and availability of publicly shared statistical records against unexpected equipment damage.
