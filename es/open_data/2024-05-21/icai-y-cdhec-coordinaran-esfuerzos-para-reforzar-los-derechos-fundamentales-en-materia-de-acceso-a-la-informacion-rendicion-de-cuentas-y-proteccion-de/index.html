ICAI y CDHEC coordinarán esfuerzos para reforzar los derechos fundamentales en materia de acceso a la información, rendición de cuentas y protección de datos personales

The ICAI and CDHEC are aligning to strengthen access to information, transparency, and data protection in Coahuila. This collaboration aims to establish joint agendas that promote these fundamental rights, ensuring they benefit the public through coordinated institutional efforts. By prioritizing inclusivity, the partnership specifically seeks to ensure vulnerable groups can easily access information. This focus guarantees that essential data protection measures reach those who need them most, fostering a more equitable society. This initiative is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of integrating transparency with robust privacy safeguards. It highlights how institutional cooperation can effectively balance the public’s right to know with the critical protection of personal information.
