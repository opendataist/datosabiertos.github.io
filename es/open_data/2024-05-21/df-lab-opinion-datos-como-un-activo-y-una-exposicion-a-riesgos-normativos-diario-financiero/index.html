DF Lab Opinión /Datos como un activo y una exposición a riesgos normativos | Diario Financiero

The article highlights a critical disconnect between the widespread adoption of advanced data-driven strategies and the low awareness of upcoming personal data protection laws. Despite most leaders recognizing data as critical, a significant majority remain unaware of the new regulatory framework, creating a dangerous blind spot in corporate governance. This oversight poses substantial legal and financial risks, as efficient data management requires not only technical quality but also legal licitness. The proposed law mandates risk management and due diligence, necessitating comprehensive staff training to identify and prevent violations. Without this legal perspective, organizations face potential fines and costly compliance adjustments. This issue is vital for open data because it underscores that data utility is inextricably linked to ethical and legal compliance. Open data initiatives must prioritize governance frameworks that ensure lawful processing. Ignoring regulatory contexts undermines trust and sustainability, making legal literacy as essential as technical expertise for responsible data ecosystems.
