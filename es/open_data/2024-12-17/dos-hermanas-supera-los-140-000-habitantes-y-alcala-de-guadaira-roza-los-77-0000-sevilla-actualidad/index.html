Dos Hermanas supera los 140.000 habitantes y Alcalá de Guadaíra roza los 77.0000 | Sevilla Actualidad

The latest municipal census data from the Instituto Nacional de Estadística reveals a clear demographic shift within the province of Seville. While major municipalities like Dos Hermanas and Seville capital have experienced consistent population growth, reflecting an overall provincial increase, Seville has lost its ranking as Spain’s fourth most populous province to Alicante. This transition highlights evolving regional dynamics where peripheral areas are gaining ground even as the central city attempts to reverse years of decline and reclaim its historical population peak. These findings are highly relevant to open data initiatives, as they demonstrate the critical value of standardized, publicly accessible demographic records for real-time societal analysis. By making this granular data available, researchers and policymakers can move beyond static statistics to understand the fluid nature of urbanization. The transparency of such datasets allows for the identification of precise growth trends across different administrative levels, fostering evidence-based decision-making that responds to current rather than historical realities. Ultimately, the article underscores how open statistical infrastructure supports democratic accountability and urban planning. The ability to track these subtle shifts empowers communities to anticipate service demands and address the challenges of municipal expansion. When citizens and institutions can freely access and interpret this information, it strengthens the link between government data and public interest, ensuring that development strategies are grounded in accurate, timely, and comprehensive demographic evidence.
