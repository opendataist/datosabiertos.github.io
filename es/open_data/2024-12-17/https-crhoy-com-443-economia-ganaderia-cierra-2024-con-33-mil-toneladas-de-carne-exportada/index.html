The national bovine market experienced significant stability in 2024, driven by robust international demand and a diversified export strategy that moved away from heavy reliance on single markets. This diversification strengthened the local economy, allowing for higher export prices and maintaining a positive trade balance despite slight downward trends in producer prices. Consequently, increased supply and reduced consumer prices boosted domestic consumption significantly, favoring nationally sourced meat over imports. This article is relevant to open_data as it highlights the importance of transparent, accessible agricultural statistics from entities like Corfoga for monitoring market health. Reliable data enables stakeholders to understand complex dynamics, such as the shift from import dependency to export-led growth. Open access to such detailed metrics fosters better decision-making, supports economic forecasting, and ensures that the benefits of a stable, transparent market are widely understood and utilized by the public and industry professionals alike.
