Es necesaria la transparencia para la justicia electoral: Ibarra Cadena

Blanca Lilia Ibarra asserts that transparency is the fundamental condition for electoral justice and democratic trust. She argues that public access to information ensures informed participation and robust judicial reasoning. This underscores the relevance of open data in strengthening institutional legitimacy and citizen engagement in democratic processes.
