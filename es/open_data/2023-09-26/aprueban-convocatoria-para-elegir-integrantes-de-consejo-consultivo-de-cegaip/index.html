Aprueban convocatoria para elegir integrantes de consejo consultivo de Cegaip

The legislative commission in San Luis Potosí has approved a call to elect members for the advisory council of the state’s access to information guarantee body. This step is crucial for establishing a diverse, gender-balanced oversight committee drawn from civil society and academia. The initiative underscores the vital role of public participation in strengthening institutional accountability and ensuring the proper use of resources. By inviting societal engagement, the government aims to enhance transparency and build trust in how public information is managed and protected. This process is highly relevant to open data as it formalizes mechanisms for citizen oversight of information rights. It demonstrates how legal frameworks and inclusive governance structures directly support the principles of transparency, ensuring that access to information remains robust, inclusive, and subject to public scrutiny for greater democratic accountability.
