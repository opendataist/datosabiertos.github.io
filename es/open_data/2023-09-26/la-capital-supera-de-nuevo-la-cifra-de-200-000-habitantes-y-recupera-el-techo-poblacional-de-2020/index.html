La capital supera de nuevo la cifra de 200.000 habitantes y recupera el techo poblacional de 2020

The municipal government of Almería has officially validated its updated population registry, revealing a total of over 200,000 inhabitants. This data highlights a significant demographic shift, with a net increase in residents driven largely by growth in the foreign population, particularly non-EU nationals. The approval of these figures serves as a crucial baseline for administrative planning, resource allocation, and understanding the city’s evolving social fabric. Beyond demographic statistics, the council addressed urgent urban and legal matters, including the approval of measures to clear specific agricultural facilities to allow for necessary urbanization projects. This decision underscores the interplay between regulatory enforcement and urban development, demonstrating how local governance balances legal procedures with the need to resolve property disputes and unlock stalled infrastructure improvements. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the practical impact of official statistical records on civic life and policy-making. Transparent, accurate population data allows citizens and analysts to track migration trends, urbanization rates, and public service demands. Furthermore, the publication of such administrative outcomes, including voting records and legal decisions, supports institutional transparency, enabling the public to hold local authorities accountable for their regulatory and developmental choices.
