Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Severe rains caused homes to collapse in Petare, highlighting the urgent need for transparent disaster data to protect vulnerable communities. Open data is crucial for mapping risks and tracking affected families, ensuring rapid response. Authorities deployed teams for inspections and evacuations, emphasizing community vigilance. The incident underscores how accessible environmental and infrastructure data can prevent casualties by enabling early warnings and resource allocation. Victims are being sheltered, yet systemic gaps remain. Enhancing open data initiatives allows for better analysis of weather impacts on housing, fostering resilience and informed policy-making for future storm preparedness.
