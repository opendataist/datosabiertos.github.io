El turismo en Latinoamérica se recupera un 85 % y prevé llegar a nivel prepandemia en 2024

The global tourism sector is experiencing a robust recovery, with Latin America reaching eighty-five percent of pre-pandemic levels. This resurgence is primarily driven by a qualitative shift in traveler behavior, where younger generations prioritize experiences over material goods and demonstrate a natural inclination toward sustainability. Rather than being forced into eco-friendly practices, these demographics inherently seek integration with local communities and nature, viewing travel as a form of self-realization and a "revenge on life" after years of restrictions. Consequently, the market is adapting to accommodate more responsible tourists who value authentic cultural connections and environmental preservation. The implications for the region are significant, as Latin America is poised to become a central hub for the future of sustainable tourism. By leveraging its status as a global reserve for sustainability, the area can transform tourism into a primary engine for economic development, social integration, and peace. This evolution suggests that successful destinations must prepare to meet the needs of a new type of traveler—one who seeks identity and environmental harmony. The region’s ability to align its offerings with these emerging values positions it favorably for long-term growth and resilience in the post-pandemic global economy. This article is relevant to open data because the rapid evolution of tourist preferences and the precise measurement of recovery rates rely heavily on transparent, accessible statistics. Understanding demographic shifts and sustainability trends requires analyzing diverse datasets from international organizations. Open data facilitates this scrutiny, allowing researchers and policymakers to validate claims about youth behavior and regional performance. Furthermore, transparency in tourism metrics encourages accountability and supports the development of data-driven strategies that align economic growth with the ethical demands of modern travelers.
