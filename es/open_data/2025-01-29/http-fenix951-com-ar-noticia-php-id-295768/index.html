La Rioja experienced a significant decline in formal private sector employment, driven by broader economic recession, reduced sales, and the halt of public works. This downturn reflects national trends impacting provincial labor markets, highlighting the vulnerability of regional economies to federal policy shifts and general economic contractions. The article underscores the critical importance of high-quality, standardized official datasets for analyzing these labor market shifts. By utilizing the Integrated Social Security System, the report demonstrates how centralized, transparent data enables precise tracking of employment dynamics and wage trends, facilitating accurate economic assessments. This resource is relevant to open data because it illustrates the value of accessible, granular statistics in revealing structural economic issues, such as the erosion of purchasing power despite nominal wage increases. It emphasizes that reliable open data is essential for understanding complex socioeconomic realities and informing effective policy decisions in times of crisis.
