Lanza IEEPCO convocatoria para elegir titular de la Unidad de Género

Oaxaca’s electoral institute is launching an affirmative action initiative to appoint a woman from indigenous or Afromexican communities to lead its Gender Equality Unit. This move addresses historical debts by ensuring these marginalized groups participate in public decision-making roles, fostering greater institutional diversity and inclusion. The selection process emphasizes transparency and legal compliance, involving document validation and profile evaluation before a final proposal is made. By prioritizing qualified candidates through a rigorous, open procedure, the institution aims to secure the most competent leader for advancing gender equity and non-discrimination policies within its framework. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how public institutions can leverage transparent, accessible procedures to promote social equity. It highlights the importance of open government practices in ensuring diverse representation, proving that accessible data on recruitment processes can empower underrepresented communities and strengthen democratic engagement.
