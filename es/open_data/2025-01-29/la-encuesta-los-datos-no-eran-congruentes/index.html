La encuesta: los datos no eran congruentes

The previous administration's national drug survey was declared a failure due to methodological errors, prompting the current government to discard the incongruent data. Health authorities have launched a collaborative revision with public health institutes to ensure results accurately reflect reality. This highlights the critical open data necessity for transparent methodologies and validating datasets. Reliable information is essential for effective public policy, demonstrating that poor data integrity undermines trust and decision-making capabilities.
