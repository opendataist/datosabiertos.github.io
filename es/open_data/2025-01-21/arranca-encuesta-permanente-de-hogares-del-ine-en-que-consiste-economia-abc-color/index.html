Arranca encuesta permanente de hogares del INE: ¿En qué consiste? - Economía - ABC Color

The National Statistics Institute has launched the first quarter data collection for the Permanent Household Survey, aiming to gather essential socioeconomic indicators. This ongoing operation ensures continuous tracking of employment, income, and living conditions across Paraguay, forming the backbone for future public policy decisions. By capturing detailed household characteristics and social metrics, the survey enables authorities to monitor population welfare trends accurately. The comprehensive coverage in key regions ensures representative data, allowing for robust analysis of economic shifts and societal well-being over time. This initiative is crucial for open data as it provides high-quality, structured raw data accessible for public scrutiny and analysis. Such transparency fosters accountability and empowers researchers, journalists, and citizens to validate official statistics, driving informed debate and enhancing trust in government metrics.
