DF Lab Opinión/ Datos abiertos, fuente de inspiración y transparencia (parte 1) | Diario Financiero

Chile faces significant limitations in accessing public data, hindering the development of an AI-driven innovation ecosystem. While open data is essential for fostering entrepreneurship and transparency, the country must balance accessibility with strict privacy protections. The recent implementation of Law 21.719 mandates the anonymization of personal information before publication. This legislative step is crucial for enabling safe data usage, thereby establishing a secure foundation for researchers and businesses to innovate without compromising individual rights. The Estonian model demonstrates the transformative potential of robust open data policies. By investing in advanced digital infrastructure and interoperable government platforms, Estonia successfully stimulated economic growth and citizen engagement. This approach has empowered local startups to create globally competitive services, proving that accessible public data serves as a catalyst for technological advancement and broad societal participation in decision-making processes. This article highlights the critical link between legal frameworks for data privacy and the vitality of the open data movement. It underscores that successful data ecosystems require not just technological readiness, but also regulatory clarity that protects citizens while maximizing information utility. For Chile, adopting similar principles is vital to unlocking the economic and social benefits of AI. The narrative emphasizes that responsible data governance is the cornerstone of modern democratic innovation.
