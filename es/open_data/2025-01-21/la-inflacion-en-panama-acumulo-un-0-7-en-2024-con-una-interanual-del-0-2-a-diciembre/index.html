La inflación en Panamá acumuló un 0.7% en 2024, con una interanual del -0.2% a diciembre

Panamá experienced a significant shift in its economic landscape, concluding 2024 with virtually flat inflation and slight deflation. This contrasts sharply with the moderate inflation rates seen in recent years, highlighting a unique stabilization of consumer prices driven by decreases in communications, health, and clothing sectors. The relevance to open data lies in the transparency of this statistical tracking. By making these detailed Index of Consumer Prices metrics publicly accessible, institutions enable rigorous analysis of economic trends. This data empowers researchers and policymakers to identify sector-specific deflationary pressures and understand the underlying drivers of consumer behavior across different economic indicators. Ultimately, this transparency fosters trust and supports evidence-based decision-making. Accessible, granular data allows for a deeper comprehension of market dynamics, ensuring that stakeholders can accurately assess economic health and formulate informed strategies based on reliable, publicly available statistical evidence.
