Ordenan a Pemex informar sobre envíos de combustible a Cuba | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI mandates Pemex disclose detailed records of fuel shipments to Cuba, rejecting claims of missing documentation. This ruling emphasizes that international transfers must be transparently justified, whether as commercial transactions or humanitarian aid, ensuring public accountability for resource management. Transparency enables citizens to scrutinize Mexico’s international relations and public fund usage effectively. The decision underscores that administrative entities must actively seek relevant information rather than asserting its absence, reinforcing the duty to provide comprehensive data on state actions. This case highlights the critical role of open data in enforcing transparency rights. By compelling the release of specific operational details, it demonstrates how legal frameworks protect access to information, ensuring that government dealings remain visible and subject to public oversight.
