"Una falta de respeto": Ola de críticas por cómic del CPLT que costó $1 millón

The Transparency Council commissioned a comic to promote transparency laws, spending one million pesos on illustrations widely criticized for poor quality. Social media users condemned the project as a disrespectful waste of public funds, arguing the illegible graphics fail to represent the institution’s serious mandate. This backlash highlights the tension between innovative communication strategies and public expectations of fiscal responsibility in government entities. The controversy underscores the importance of accountability in how public institutions utilize taxpayer money for educational initiatives. By highlighting the discrepancy between the stated goal of fostering transparency and the perceived opaqueness of the procurement process, the incident reveals how operational decisions can undermine institutional credibility. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates that transparency extends beyond data availability to encompass the transparency of administrative processes themselves. It serves as a cautionary tale for organizations managing public information, emphasizing that clear, accountable, and high-quality communication is essential for maintaining public trust and effectively disseminating critical civic knowledge.
