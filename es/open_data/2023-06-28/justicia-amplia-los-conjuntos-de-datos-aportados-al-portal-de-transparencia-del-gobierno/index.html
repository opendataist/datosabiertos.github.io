Justicia amplía los conjuntos de datos aportados al portal de transparencia del Gobierno

The Ministry of Justice advances open data compliance by publishing diverse public information, including gender-based violence and toxicology statistics. This initiative integrates justice sector data into Spain’s open data ecosystem, fostering inter-administrative coordination and encouraging the creation of societal benefits through shared information. By enabling non-experts to analyze complex datasets via interactive visualizations, the portal democratizes access to judicial metrics. A practical example demonstrates how cross-referencing toxicological results with vehicle registration data reveals critical safety ratios, highlighting the power of linked open data for public insight. This effort is relevant to open data as it exemplifies statutory compliance and the practical utility of transparent government information. It showcases how combining disparate datasets can generate meaningful analytics, promoting innovation and accountability within the broader open government framework.
