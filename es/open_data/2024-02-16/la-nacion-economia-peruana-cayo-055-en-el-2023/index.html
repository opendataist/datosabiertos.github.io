La Nación / Economía peruana cayó 0,55 % en el 2023

Peru’s economy contracted in 2023, driven by political instability and extreme weather, significantly impacting manufacturing and agriculture. This downturn highlights how governance crises and climate shocks disrupt national production, affecting vulnerable populations most severely. For open data, this scenario underscores the critical need for transparent, real-time monitoring systems. Reliable statistics are essential to identify sectoral vulnerabilities quickly and enable timely policy interventions. Without accessible, high-quality data, stakeholders cannot accurately assess the magnitude of economic disruptions or coordinate effective responses. Ultimately, this case illustrates the vital role of open data in fostering resilience. By making economic indicators publicly available, governments and researchers can better understand the intersection of climate, politics, and finance, leading to more informed decision-making and stronger safeguards against future crises.
