El Ayuntamiento reforzará la unidad del padrón municipal

Local authorities are reinforcing municipal registry checks to correct fraudulent registrations uncovered by police arrests. This initiative aims to ensure population data accurately reflects reality, moving away from uncertain estimates toward verified facts. The effort underscores the critical role of open data in public administration by prioritizing transparency and reliability. Accurate datasets are essential for effective governance, as they provide the trustworthy foundation needed for decision-making and maintain public trust in institutional processes.
