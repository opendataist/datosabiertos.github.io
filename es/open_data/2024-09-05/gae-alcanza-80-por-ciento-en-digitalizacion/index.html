GAE alcanza 80 por ciento en digitalización

The government has significantly advanced the simplification of administrative requirements, eliminating unnecessary processes and transitioning remaining procedures to digital platforms. This shift, driven by legislative mandates for open government, modernizes state operations and enhances accessibility for citizens through electronic services. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it establishes the digital infrastructure necessary for transparent, efficient, and publicly accessible information systems.
