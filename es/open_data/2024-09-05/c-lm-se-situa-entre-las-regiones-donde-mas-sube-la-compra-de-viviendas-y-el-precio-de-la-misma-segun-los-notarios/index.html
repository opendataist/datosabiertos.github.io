C-LM se sitúa entre las regiones donde más sube la compra de viviendas y el precio de la misma, según los notarios

The article highlights a robust growth in the Spanish housing market, characterized by a significant rise in the volume of transactions across all autonomous communities. This expansion coincides with an overall increase in property prices, although regional disparities are evident, with some areas experiencing sharp price hikes while others see declines. Notably, Castilla-La Mancha reflects the national trend with substantial growth in both sales and prices, yet it also exhibits a contraction in average property size, suggesting a shift toward smaller living spaces despite higher demand. Simultaneously, there is a marked increase in mortgage lending, indicating strong consumer confidence and financing availability. Although the average loan amount has slightly decreased, the volume of new mortgages has surged nationwide, with every region reporting growth. This financial activity underscores the sector's vitality, as nearly half of all home purchases are now funded through credit, reinforcing the link between market liquidity and property acquisition trends. This data is highly relevant to open_data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of transparent, granular, and geographically disaggregated statistics. By making such detailed regional metrics available, policymakers, researchers, and the public can better understand local market dynamics, identify emerging trends, and hold institutions accountable. Open access to this specific data fosters greater transparency in the real estate sector, enabling more informed decision-making and equitable resource allocation.
