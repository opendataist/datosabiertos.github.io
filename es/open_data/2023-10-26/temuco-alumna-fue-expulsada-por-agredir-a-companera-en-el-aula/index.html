Temuco: Alumna fue expulsada por agredir a compañera en el aula

A violent incident where a student was assaulted and had her hair cut at ISETT prompted the immediate expulsion of the aggressor. Authorities emphasize a zero-tolerance policy, initiating legal proceedings and providing comprehensive psychological support to the victim while addressing accomplices. The case highlights systemic failures, exacerbated by similar ongoing violence in nearby Freire that triggered strikes. These events underscore the urgent need for robust safety protocols and community oversight in educational institutions. This situation is vital for open data because transparent recording of such incidents enables public scrutiny. Aggregated data on school violence allows for better policy analysis, accountability tracking, and the development of evidence-based strategies to foster safer, more respectful learning environments.
