Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Tamaulipas Transparency Institute emphasizes that maintaining rigorous standards in information access and data protection is non-negotiable, regardless of political cycles. This commitment ensures that citizens can effectively hold public institutions accountable through their right to know, reinforcing democratic integrity. High volumes of citizen requests and review resources indicate an active society demanding transparency. By actively enforcing compliance through administrative measures, the institution addresses failures in information publication, thereby validating the legal framework that empowers individuals to seek public records and protect their privacy. This narrative is crucial for open data as it demonstrates the practical necessity of robust institutional enforcement mechanisms. It highlights that technical openness must be backed by legal accountability to ensure data accessibility and reliability, showing how transparency laws directly enable the open data ecosystem to function meaningfully for the public.
