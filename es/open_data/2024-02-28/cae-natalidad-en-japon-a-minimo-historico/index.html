Cae natalidad en Japón a mínimo histórico

Japan’s population is shrinking rapidly due to record-low births and rising deaths, signaling a deepening demographic crisis. This decline, paired with fewer marriages and more divorces, highlights significant societal shifts toward delayed family formation and unstable unions. These trends critically impact open data, as accurate population metrics are essential for planning sustainable social services and understanding changing community structures.
