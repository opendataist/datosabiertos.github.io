Inai, Cofece y otros, proponen eliminarlos: Luisa Alcalde

This initiative argues that past autonomous bodies increased costs and duplicated functions without improving governance. Consequently, it proposes eliminating several key regulatory agencies and decentralizing their roles into standard ministries. This restructuring aims to restore state authority over policy design while centralizing administrative efficiencies. By consolidating these entities, the government seeks to eliminate bureaucratic redundancies and streamline public administration under direct ministerial control. The relevance to open data lies in the potential impact on INAI, a crucial institution for transparency. Its removal raises concerns about reduced access to public information, challenging the core principles of open data and democratic accountability in governance.
