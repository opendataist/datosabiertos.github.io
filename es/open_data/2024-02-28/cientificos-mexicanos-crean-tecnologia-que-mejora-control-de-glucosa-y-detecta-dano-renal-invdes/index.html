Científicos mexicanos crean tecnología que mejora control de glucosa y detecta daño renal - INVDES

Researchers from prestigious Mexican institutions have developed a novel diagnostic technology that significantly improves the management of diabetes and enables the early detection of kidney damage. Utilizing photoacoustic spectroscopy, this innovation allows for the precise measurement of glycated hemoglobin and other key biomarkers in blood and urine. Unlike conventional methods, it requires only minute sample volumes and eliminates the use of toxic substances, offering a safer, more efficient, and highly sensitive approach to monitoring metabolic health in patients suffering from this epidemic. This advancement is particularly critical given the sheer scale of the diabetes crisis in Mexico, where it ranks as the second leading cause of death. By providing timely insights into organ damage, the technology aims to prevent the progression to renal failure, thereby reducing long-term healthcare burdens and mortality rates. The method’s ability to analyze a wide absorption spectrum ensures comprehensive data collection, facilitating better clinical decision-making and proactive treatment strategies for millions of affected individuals across the country and globally. The relevance of this development to the open_data movement lies in its potential to democratize high-quality health information. As the software undergoes copyright registration and seeks international patent approval, ensuring transparency in data analysis and algorithmic logic becomes paramount. Open scientific collaboration and accessible data standards will be essential for the technology’s validation by health authorities and its eventual international acceptance, fostering trust and enabling broader adoption to combat public health challenges effectively.
