El IPC se sitúa en el 2,9% en lo que va de año en la región

The article details the evolution of the Consumer Price Index in Castilla-La Mancha and Spain, noting a monthly increase driven by fuel costs and tourism-related services, while underlying inflation remains elevated compared to the general rate. Although food price growth has moderated due to stable dairy and egg prices alongside cheaper fruits, overall inflation persists above target levels. The Spanish government interprets these figures as evidence of successful economic policies that have reduced inflation significantly compared to the previous year, thereby protecting purchasing power and enhancing corporate competitiveness. This data is crucial for the open data community because it highlights the importance of transparent, timely, and granular statistical publication by national institutes. Open data initiatives rely on the accessibility of such official metrics to enable independent verification, foster public trust, and facilitate rigorous analysis by researchers and journalists. Without direct access to these detailed breakdowns, it would be difficult for civil society to hold policymakers accountable or to understand the nuanced impacts of economic measures on different sectors. Furthermore, the release of this index demonstrates how open data serves as a foundational resource for economic literacy and policy evaluation. By making raw data public, institutions allow for the creation of diverse analytical tools and visualizations that can reveal trends beyond official summaries. This transparency empowers citizens to make informed decisions and encourages a culture of data-driven discourse, ensuring that economic narratives are grounded in verified facts rather than mere political rhetoric.
