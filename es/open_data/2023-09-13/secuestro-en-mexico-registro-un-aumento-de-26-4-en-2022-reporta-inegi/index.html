Secuestro en México registró un aumento de 26.4% en 2022, reporta Inegi

Mexico’s security landscape took a sharp turn in 2022, marking a significant reversal of the downward trend in kidnappings observed since 2019. This resurgence indicates that previous safety improvements were fragile, highlighting a critical shift in crime dynamics that demands immediate policy attention and resource reallocation. To address these challenges, transparent and accessible crime data is essential. Surveys like the National Victimization Survey help approximate reality by capturing unreported incidents, providing a more accurate picture than official police records alone. This data transparency is crucial for identifying hotspots and understanding the true scale of victimization across different demographics and regions. Access to such comprehensive datasets allows researchers and civil society to analyze safety perceptions and crime distribution more effectively. By making this information open, stakeholders can better evaluate the impact of security measures, foster public trust through accountability, and drive evidence-based solutions to combat rising insecurity and protect vulnerable communities.
