The ANSES confirms an 11% increase in Argentine pensions, ensuring minimum benefits reach a protected floor through direct adjustments and supplemental bonuses. This update reflects a shift toward monthly inflation-based corrections, prioritizing consistent purchasing power maintenance over rigid biannual reviews. By aligning payments with recent consumer price indices, the government aims to prevent benefit erosion between major updates. This approach guarantees that retirees receive the higher amount calculated under either the standard mobility law or the new monthly formula, effectively shielding vulnerable populations from sudden economic volatility. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible price indices like INDEC’s IPC. Reliable public statistical data is fundamental for verifying governmental claims, auditing social security formulas, and allowing citizens to independently calculate their updated benefits, thereby ensuring accountability in public spending and policy implementation.
