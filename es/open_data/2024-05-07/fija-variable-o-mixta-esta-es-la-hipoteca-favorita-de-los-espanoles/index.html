¿Fija, variable o mixta? Esta es la hipoteca favorita de los españoles

Spanish borrowers are strongly avoiding variable interest rates, shifting heavily toward fixed or hybrid mortgages. This trend reflects a desire for stability after previous volatility, even as markets anticipate imminent rate cuts by the European Central Bank. The surge in new construction sales is largely driven by high-income buyers who secured financing in recent years. These transactions, originally reserved during 2022 and 2023, are now being formalized, highlighting how early market positioning impacts current closing volumes. This article matters for open data because it illustrates the real-world impact of interest rate fluctuations on consumer behavior. Analyzing such granular transaction data reveals clear patterns of risk aversion and demand shifts, which are essential for building accurate economic models and transparent market analyses.
