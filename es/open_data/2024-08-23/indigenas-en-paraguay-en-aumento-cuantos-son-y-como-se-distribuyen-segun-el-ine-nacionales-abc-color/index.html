Indígenas en Paraguay en aumento: cuántos son y cómo se distribuyen, según el INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

The 2022 Indigenous Census in Paraguay reveals a significant demographic shift, marking a substantial growth in the indigenous population over the last decade. This surge highlights the urgent need for updated statistical frameworks that accurately reflect cultural diversity and ensure that minority groups are not invisible in national planning. Recognizing this growth is essential for validating the existence and rights of these communities within the broader societal context. Geographically and culturally, the data shows distinct concentrations and linguistic predominant groups, yet the indigenous community remains a small fraction of the total national population. This disparity underscores critical gaps in resource allocation and policy implementation. Understanding these spatial and cultural distributions allows for more targeted interventions that respect territorial autonomy and linguistic heritage, moving beyond generic national averages. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how disaggregated, accessible statistics empower advocacy and accountability. By making specific demographic details publicly available, it enables researchers, activists, and policymakers to challenge official projections and demand inclusive development strategies. Transparent data serves as a foundational tool for social justice, ensuring that marginalized populations can effectively claim their rights and participate in decision-making processes.
