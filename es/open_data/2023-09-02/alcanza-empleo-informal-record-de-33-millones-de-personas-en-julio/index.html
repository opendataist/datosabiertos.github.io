Alcanza empleo informal récord de 33 millones de personas en julio

The article highlights that over half of the workforce operates informally, revealing widespread economic vulnerability characterized by lower wages and a lack of social protection. This structural imbalance indicates that formal job creation fails to offset informal sector growth, leaving the majority of workers without essential benefits despite record overall employment figures. Unemployment rates show a slight increase, with men experiencing a more significant rise than women, who maintain historically low levels. However, the data suggests that labor market stability is fragile, as the persistence of informality undermines the quality of work and economic security for a substantial portion of the population. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible labor statistics. By making such detailed metrics available, policymakers and researchers can better analyze employment quality, advocate for social reforms, and track progress toward reducing inequality and strengthening labor rights.
