Ejecutivo refuerza la transparencia en la gestión pública

Guatemala launched its 2023-2025 Open Government Action Plan, prioritizing transparency and institutional coordination to strengthen open data initiatives. This multi-sectoral effort builds on previous successes, demonstrating sustained state commitment to public accountability and collaborative governance frameworks. The plan addresses critical societal challenges through specific institutional commitments, ensuring that open government principles directly contribute to improving health, education, and security outcomes. By integrating diverse agencies, the initiative aims to enhance the practical application of transparent data in addressing public needs. This development is highly relevant to open data as it formalizes the state’s obligation to release accessible, usable information. It reinforces the ecosystem where citizens and organizations can leverage government data to demand accountability and drive policy improvements, validating open data as a core tool for democratic engagement.
