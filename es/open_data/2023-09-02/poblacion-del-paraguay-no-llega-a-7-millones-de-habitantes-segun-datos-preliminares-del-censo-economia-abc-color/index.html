Población del Paraguay no llega a 7 millones de habitantes, según datos preliminares del Censo - Economía - ABC Color

The preliminary results of Paraguay’s 2022 census reveal a significant discrepancy between projected figures and the actual count, showing a population substantially lower than official estimates. This shift is primarily attributed to extensive international migration, particularly to Argentina and Spain, alongside a notable decline in fertility rates and a reduction in life expectancy driven by the pandemic. These demographic changes challenge the statistical baselines established in previous years, highlighting the limitations of models that rely on outdated data or incomplete prior censuses. For open data initiatives, this case underscores the critical necessity of updating foundational demographic datasets to ensure the accuracy of derived economic and social indicators. The divergence forces government institutions to revise key metrics such as GDP per capita, debt-to-GDP ratios, and labor market variables like employment and poverty rates. Consequently, maintaining transparent, precise, and regularly updated population data is essential for reliable economic forecasting and policy formulation, as outdated assumptions can lead to significant distortions in national statistics. Ultimately, this event demonstrates how demographic data directly influences fiscal health and international financial relationships. A lower population count alters per capita calculations, potentially affecting a country’s eligibility for concessional credit lines and broader economic projections. Therefore, the integration of real-time, verified census data into open data ecosystems is vital for policymakers to adjust strategies effectively and for the public to understand the true socioeconomic landscape of the nation.
