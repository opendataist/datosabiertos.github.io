Los precios del aceite de oliva ya superan los 10 euros

Surging olive oil prices, driven by drought-induced harvest collapses, disproportionately burden consumers rather than producers, revealing significant inefficiencies in the distribution chain. This economic distortion highlights the urgent need for transparent data to track value distribution and identify where margins are unjustly captured. Open data initiatives are crucial here to ensure accountability and protect stakeholders by making supply chain economics visible and auditable for everyone.
