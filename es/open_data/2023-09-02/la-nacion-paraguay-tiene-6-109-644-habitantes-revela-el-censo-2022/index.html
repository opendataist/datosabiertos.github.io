La Nación / Paraguay tiene 6.109.644 habitantes, revela el Censo 2022

The recent national census reveals that Paraguay’s population is significantly lower than previously estimated, challenging long-standing demographic projections based on outdated data from 2000. This correction highlights a sustained decline in fertility rates, reflecting shifting social dynamics where families are choosing to have fewer children. Consequently, national planning models must be recalibrated to reflect these realistic figures rather than relying on historical overestimations. Beyond population counts, the census provides crucial georeferenced data on public infrastructure, enabling precise alignment of services with actual demand. By mapping educational, health, and security facilities against residential clusters, authorities can now identify specific gaps in coverage. For instance, officials can determine exactly how many families are within range of schools or clinics, allowing for optimized resource allocation and targeted service delivery across different regions. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the transformative power of transparent, granular datasets. The release of detailed census results and spatial infrastructure maps fosters accountability and empowers policymakers to make evidence-based decisions. It underscores how open, accessible geospatial information can improve public service efficiency, ensure equitable resource distribution, and support broader societal development through data-driven governance.
