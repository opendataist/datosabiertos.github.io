Derechos para pasajeros con discapacidad o movilidad reducida en vuelos: Lo que las aerolíneas deben garantizar

The article highlights a significant gap in Spain where hundreds of thousands of individuals with disabilities face travel limitations despite having equal rights. This discrepancy underscores the critical need for transparent data to monitor the effectiveness of current accessibility laws and service provision, ensuring that legislative promises translate into tangible mobility for all citizens. It details the specific assistance mechanisms mandated by European regulations, including priority handling and support at every stage of airport transit. These provisions ensure comparable access to air travel, reinforcing the principle of non-discrimination and guaranteeing that vulnerable groups, including the elderly and families, receive the necessary support to exercise their right to free movement effectively. This topic is relevant to open data because it reveals systemic disparities in service delivery that require rigorous, accessible tracking. By making performance metrics regarding assistance requests and complaint resolutions publicly available, stakeholders can hold airlines and airports accountable, driving informed policy improvements and ensuring equitable treatment for people with reduced mobility across the European transport network.
