Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The national statistics institute is refining an experimental census questionnaire through a pilot phase, incorporating specific inquiries into indigenous identity and pandemic-related mortality. This methodological adjustment aims to enhance data collection accuracy by directly addressing demographic and health dimensions previously underrepresented in historical records. This update is relevant to open data as it expands the granularity of available public statistics. By capturing nuanced details on cultural identity and recent mortality trends, the initiative generates richer datasets for researchers and policymakers, fostering more inclusive and precise societal analysis.
