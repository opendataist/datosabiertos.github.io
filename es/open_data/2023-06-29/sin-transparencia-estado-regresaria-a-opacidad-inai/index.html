Sin transparencia, Estado regresaría a opacidad: INAI

The Senate’s continued refusal to appoint an INAI commissioner threatens state transparency, risking a return to secrecy. Experts emphasize that access to information is a fundamental human right essential for democracy. The National Transparency Platform democratizes data, empowering citizens to monitor public officials and investigate government actions. This tool ensures that official documents remain accessible for public scrutiny and dissemination. This situation highlights the critical link between institutional integrity and open data. Without functioning oversight bodies, the mechanisms that guarantee transparency and hold power accountable are significantly weakened, undermining public trust.
