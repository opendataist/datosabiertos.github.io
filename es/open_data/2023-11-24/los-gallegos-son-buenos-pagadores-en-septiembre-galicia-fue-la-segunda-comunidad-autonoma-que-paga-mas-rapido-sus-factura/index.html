Los gallegos son buenos pagadores: en septiembre Galicia fue la segunda comunidad autónoma que paga más rápido sus factura

Galicia pays suppliers faster than the national average, significantly reducing commercial debt relative to GDP. This performance ensures better liquidity for businesses and strengthens the local economy. These results are relevant to open data by demonstrating how transparent financial metrics can highlight government efficiency and accountability in public procurement.
