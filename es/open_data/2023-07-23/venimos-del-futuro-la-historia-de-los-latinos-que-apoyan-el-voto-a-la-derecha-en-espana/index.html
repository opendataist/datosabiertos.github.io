“Venimos del futuro”, la historia de los latinos que apoyan el voto a la derecha en España

The article highlights a significant political trend among Latin American immigrants in Spain, who predominantly support right-wing or far-right parties like the People’s Party and Vox. Driven by traumatic political memories from their countries of origin, these voters actively oppose leftist governments, fearing a replication of socialist policies they associate with instability and dictatorship. This alignment is less about specific Spanish domestic policies and more about a shared ideological defense against perceived authoritarian threats, leading many to view current Spanish leadership unfavorably. The relevance of this phenomenon to open data lies in the need for granular, transparent demographic and electoral statistics to understand this shifting political landscape. While national statistics confirm the large population of Latin American residents, open data initiatives are crucial for tracking voting patterns, civic engagement, and the specific political preferences of subgroups like naturalized versus non-voting immigrants. Accessible, detailed datasets allow researchers and policymakers to move beyond anecdotal evidence, enabling a comprehensive analysis of how migration history influences democratic participation and political affiliation in host countries. Ultimately, the narrative underscores how personal histories and trauma shape civic behavior in new democracies. The strong emotional connection these communities feel toward specific parties, often framed as protective "families," suggests that political integration is deeply intertwined with identity and past experiences. Recognizing these complex motivations is essential for fostering inclusive civic dialogue. Accurate, accessible data helps bridge the gap between statistical presence and actual political voice, ensuring that the distinct perspectives of Latin American communities are accurately represented in broader sociopolitical analyses and public discourse.
