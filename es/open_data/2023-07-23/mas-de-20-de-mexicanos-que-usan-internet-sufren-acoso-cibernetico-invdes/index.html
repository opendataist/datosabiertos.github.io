Más de 20% de mexicanos que usan internet sufren acoso cibernético - INVDES

Recent statistics reveal that a significant portion of Mexican internet users experienced cyber harassment in 2022, with women disproportionately affected compared to men. Geographic disparities are evident, as certain states report much higher prevalence rates than others, highlighting the need for localized awareness and intervention strategies. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of accessible, detailed statistics to drive policy. By analyzing gender and regional variations, researchers can advocate for better digital safety measures and support victim protection laws. Ultimately, providing transparent information on digital violence enables citizens to understand their risks and adopt protective behaviors. Open access to such data empowers communities to demand accountability and fosters a safer online environment through informed public discourse.

Source: invdes.com.mx
