PSOE recuerda que los datos de población de Talavera son del INE y son corregidos antes de su publicación

The article highlights a conflict between local government officials regarding the interpretation of official population data from 2022. The socialists emphasize that these figures, corrected by the National Statistics Institute (INE), reflect a rigorous administrative process rather than inaccuracies. This correction mechanism removes residents who fail to provide required documentation, illustrating how statistical bodies ensure data integrity through systematic verification. The narrative criticizes a councilwoman for attempting to claim credit for a police-led investigation, arguing that such actions undermine municipal staff and create unnecessary political friction. The socialists assert that the current administration should focus on improving economic indicators, such as employment and business installation, rather than disparaging previous management achievements or engaging in institutional disloyalty. This situation is crucial for open data because it demonstrates the tension between raw administrative records and scientifically corrected statistical outputs. It underscores the importance of understanding that official datasets often undergo significant cleaning processes to maintain accuracy. Furthermore, it reveals how political actors may misinterpret or politicize standardized data releases, affecting public trust in transparent governance and the reliable use of open statistical resources.
