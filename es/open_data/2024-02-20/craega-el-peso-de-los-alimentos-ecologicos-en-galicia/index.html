CRAEGA: el peso de los alimentos ecológicos en Galicia

The article highlights the exponential growth of the organic food sector in Galicia, driven by a significant increase in certified land and operators. This expansion demonstrates how small and medium-sized farms utilize organic certification to add value and ensure economic viability, while increased production has stimulated local processing industries. The shift from niche specialty stores to mainstream supermarkets reflects improved accessibility and consumer adoption. Consumer awareness has reached near-universal levels, with the vast majority of the population familiar with organic concepts and understanding their core attributes, such as being free from synthetic chemicals. This high level of information has fostered a loyal customer base with deep interest in product composition and production processes. Consequently, organic items have become a staple in a substantial portion of household shopping baskets, indicating a permanent change in dietary habits rather than a transient trend. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it illustrates the importance of transparent, accessible public statistics in driving market trends and consumer trust. The CRAEGA’s public barometers and certification records provide a clear example of how open, reliable data can inform policy, support industry growth, and educate the public. By making these metrics available, stakeholders can better understand sector dynamics, validate the efficacy of regulatory frameworks, and encourage further adoption of sustainable practices through informed decision-making.
