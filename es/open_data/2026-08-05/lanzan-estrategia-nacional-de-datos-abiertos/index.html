Lanzan Estrategia Nacional de Datos Abiertos

The launch of the National Strategy for Open Data represents a critical modernization of the digital state, aiming to strengthen transparency and accountability within public administration. By establishing clear, precise, and accessible information about government management, this initiative empowers citizens with the tools necessary for effective supervision and active participation in public affairs. It marks a shift toward a government model where institutions produce and share information differently, fostering a closer relationship between the state and its citizens through digital transformation. The strategy emphasizes that accessible, reusable data promotes accountability while generating opportunities for innovation, research, and economic development. Guided by international standards to ensure quality and security, the approach relies on multisectoral collaboration involving civil society, academia, and media. This inclusive process ensures the strategy is built on technical criteria and broad societal input, reflecting a commitment to creating transparent, efficient, and citizen-centric governance structures. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how structured, high-quality public datasets serve as the foundation for democratic engagement and efficient state management. It highlights the transition from passive data storage to active data usage, demonstrating how open standards enable civic oversight and drive policy improvements. Ultimately, it illustrates the potential of open data ecosystems to enhance institutional credibility and drive sustainable development through informed public participation.
