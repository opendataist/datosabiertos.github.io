Desacelera inflación en primera quincena

September saw historic low inflation, significantly easing economic pressure on consumers. This unexpected deceleration suggests improved market stability and reduced cost-of-living burdens. The drop in food and fuel prices directly benefits household budgets, offering tangible relief to families. Such positive trends signal a potential turning point in monetary policy effectiveness and economic health. For open data, these statistics highlight the critical need for transparent, accessible pricing datasets. Reliable public information empowers researchers and citizens to monitor economic shifts accurately.
