Venezolanos por el Mundo: Latinoamericanas en España reclaman derechos de trabajadoras del hogar

More than thirty percent of Spanish domestic workers lack formal contracts, denying them healthcare, social security contributions, and future pensions. Consequently, many migrants earning minimum wage struggle to survive amid high rental costs and remittance obligations. This highlights urgent open data needs regarding informal labor markets. Improving data transparency is essential to address systemic vulnerabilities and protect these workers' fundamental rights.
