Inflación en México continúa cayendo... se ubica en 6.85 por ciento en marzo; hila dos meses a las baja

Mexico’s inflation rate has decelerated to its lowest level since late 2022, falling below analyst expectations for the first time in two consecutive months. This downward trend marks a significant shift in the economic landscape, offering a glimmer of relief to consumers and policymakers who have been grappling with persistently high price increases. While the decline is encouraging, the current rate remains substantially higher than the central bank’s long-term target, indicating that the fight against inflation is not yet won. The Banco de México recently raised its benchmark interest rate to a new high to curb these pressures, adopting a cautious approach by slowing the pace of monetary tightening. The central bank has signaled that future decisions will depend heavily on the evolving inflationary panorama. This strategy highlights the delicate balance required to stabilize prices without stifling economic growth, as authorities navigate the aftermath of aggressive monetary hikes implemented since 2021.
