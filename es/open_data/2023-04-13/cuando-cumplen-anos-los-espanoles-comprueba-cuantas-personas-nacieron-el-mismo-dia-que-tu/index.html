¿Cuándo cumplen años los españoles? Comprueba cuántas personas nacieron el mismo día que tú

The analysis reveals that September is the month with the highest number of birthdays in Spain, while December sees the lowest figures. This distribution challenges intuitive assumptions about seasonal conception, highlighting the need to examine statistical realities over folklore when interpreting demographic trends. Furthermore, the data shows a significant decline in births occurring on weekends and public holidays. This shift reflects the increasing prevalence of medically scheduled induced labor, demonstrating how healthcare practices directly influence birth statistics. This article is relevant to open data because it showcases how accessible public datasets from the National Institute of Statistics can reveal societal shifts. By visualizing birth patterns, citizens can better understand the impact of modern medical interventions on national demographics, emphasizing the value of transparent data in uncovering hidden social realities.
