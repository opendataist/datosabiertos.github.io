Llamativa coreografía: Bad Bunny tiene a los tejanos como zombies

Viral videos depict Texas clubgoers performing synchronized zombie-like dances to Bad Bunny’s hit, sparking confusion online. However, these movements are simply participants practicing line dance, a common regional social activity. The phenomenon highlights how easily shared cultural practices can be misinterpreted when viewed outside their original context, leading to humorous viral speculation about apocalyptic scenarios. This incident is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the critical importance of contextual metadata. Without understanding the cultural origin of raw observational data, algorithms and users may draw inaccurate conclusions, emphasizing the need for rich, explanatory documentation in open datasets.
