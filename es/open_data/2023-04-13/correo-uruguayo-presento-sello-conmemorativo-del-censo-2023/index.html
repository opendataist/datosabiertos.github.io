Correo Uruguayo presentó sello conmemorativo del Censo 2023

The launch of a philatelic stamp commemorating Uruguay’s 2023 National Census highlights a vital synergy between government statistical agencies and postal services. This collaboration underscores the professional capacity of the postal institution to support national data collection initiatives, thereby enhancing the operational efficiency of critical public surveys. By integrating logistical support from the post office, the National Institute of Statistics improves its ability to gather accurate demographic data. This cooperative model facilitates the transition from self-reporting to field verification, ensuring that the census captures a comprehensive and reliable picture of the population for future policy-making. This partnership is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how diverse public entities can align to generate high-quality, authoritative datasets. When institutions collaborate to produce robust statistical foundations, they enable more transparent and evidence-based decision-making, which is essential for the development and implementation of effective public policies in a modern administrative framework.
