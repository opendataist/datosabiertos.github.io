Inflación de Guatemala fue de 0,63% en agosto de 2023

Guatemala’s Consumer Price Index shows inflation slowing significantly, marking a crucial shift in economic stability. This deceleration highlights improved macroeconomic management compared to previous years, offering a more predictable environment for local businesses and consumers alike. The data reveals specific sectors driving these trends, with food and transport costs seeing modest increases while leisure expenses dropped. Understanding these granular shifts is essential for analyzing household purchasing power and identifying emerging market pressures in the region. This report is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how public statistical institutions make critical economic metrics transparent. By providing accessible, detailed inflation data, the INE enables researchers and developers to build tools that monitor economic health and support informed policy decisions.
