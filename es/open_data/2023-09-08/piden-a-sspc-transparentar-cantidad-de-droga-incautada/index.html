Piden a SSPC transparentar cantidad de droga incautada

The INAI ordered the Security Secretariat to disclose details on seized fentanyl and arrests, rejecting the agency’s claim of incompetence. This ruling underscores that proper institutional documentation and clear role definition are essential for delivering accurate information to the public. Such transparency ensures accountability in combating drug trafficking, allowing citizens to access crucial data on enforcement actions and results. It highlights the necessity of robust internal processes to fulfill transparency obligations effectively. This case is relevant to open data because it establishes a precedent for mandatory disclosure of public security information. It reinforces that government bodies must proactively manage and release specific operational data rather than withholding it, promoting greater openness and trust in public institutions.
