Firman convenio de colaboración para fortalecer la transparencia en Loreto

Three Baja California Sur institutions have signed a collaborative agreement to enhance transparency and public access to information. This partnership establishes a coordinated framework for managing public data, specifically focusing on the protection of personal information while ensuring citizens can actively access government documents. The initiative introduces a dedicated microsite for delivery-receipt documents, making critical administrative records more accessible and clear. By simplifying access to this specific data, the agreement aims to demystify government operations and provide citizens with straightforward tools to monitor public management. This development is relevant to open data as it exemplifies how institutional cooperation can directly improve the availability and usability of public information. By proactively releasing specific datasets through digital platforms, authorities are fostering a more open administration, which strengthens citizen trust and supports accountability through accessible data.
