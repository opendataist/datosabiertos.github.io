Baja el paro en Guadalajara en 134 personas, acumulando 13.187 parados a final de mes

Castilla-La Mancha experienced a slight increase in registered unemployment, marking a fifth consecutive monthly rise. Despite this local uptick, the total number of unemployed individuals remains the lowest for November since 2007. While the services sector saw job losses, other areas like agriculture and industry showed modest improvements, and the annual figure still reflects a notable decrease in overall unemployment compared to the previous year. Contraction in indefinite contracts and a rise in temporary hires suggest lingering instability in the labor market, even as national trends show a broader decline in unemployment. The regional data highlights a contrast between local fluctuations and the national recovery, with services driving much of the country’s improvement. However, the persistence of temporary contracts indicates that job quality and stability remain significant concerns for the workforce. This article is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how granular, sector-specific datasets can reveal nuanced economic realities that aggregate statistics might obscure. By providing detailed breakdowns by age, gender, and province, the data allows for targeted policy interventions and a deeper understanding of labor market dynamics. Such transparency is essential for analyzing the true health of the economy beyond simple headline numbers.
