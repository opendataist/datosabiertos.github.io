Una familia necesitó más de $828.000 para no ser pobre en abril - EL DEBATE

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses revealed that April’s food and basic basket prices rose but remained below the overall inflation rate. This indicates that while the cost of living continues to escalate, the immediate erosion of purchasing power slowed slightly compared to previous months. The data highlights the severe economic strain on households, with specific income thresholds defined to measure poverty and indigency. These metrics serve as crucial social indicators, showing the minimum resources required for families to avoid destitution or extreme poverty, thereby quantifying the depth of the current crisis. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, quantitative evidence of economic hardship. By making these indices accessible, stakeholders can analyze trends in social welfare, advocate for policy changes, and better understand the real-world impact of inflation on vulnerable populations.
