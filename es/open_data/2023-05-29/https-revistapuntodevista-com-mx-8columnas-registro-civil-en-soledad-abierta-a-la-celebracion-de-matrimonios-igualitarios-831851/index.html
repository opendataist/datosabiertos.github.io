The municipality of Soledad de Graciano Sánchez actively maintains the reception of applications for equal marriages across eight Civil Registry offices. This institutional commitment ensures that all individuals, regardless of sexual orientation, can legally formalize their unions and build a shared life without discrimination. Administrative procedures are accessible to both local residents and those from neighboring areas, demonstrating that legal equality is effectively implemented at the municipal level. The office facilitates these ceremonies with standardized requirements and flexible scheduling, highlighting that bureaucratic frameworks can successfully support inclusive social practices. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how local governments can transparently manage civil status records. By openly defining requirements and tracking marriage statistics, authorities promote accountability and accessibility. Such practices encourage the publication of demographic data, fostering trust and allowing citizens to verify the availability of equal rights through clear, publicly accessible administrative information.
