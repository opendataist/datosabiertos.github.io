Reporta Inegi 349.3 mdd en exportaciones

Chiapas’ export performance reflects its limited national impact despite its agricultural strengths. The state contributes minimally to the country’s total trade volume, highlighting a significant disparity compared to northern industrial hubs. This data underscores the geographic concentration of Mexico’s export economy and the challenges faced by southern regions in competing on the global stage. The region’s international sales rely heavily on specific agricultural commodities such as bananas, coffee, and diverse fruits. These primary products represent the core of Chiapas’ commercial presence abroad. Consequently, the local economy remains vulnerable to commodity price fluctuations rather than benefiting from diversified industrial manufacturing, which limits sustainable growth potential in the open market. This information is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how granular public statistics reveal structural economic inequalities. By accessing and analyzing such disaggregated datasets, researchers and policymakers can identify disparities between regions. This transparency fosters better-targeted development strategies and ensures that resource allocation addresses the specific needs of underrepresented economic areas.
