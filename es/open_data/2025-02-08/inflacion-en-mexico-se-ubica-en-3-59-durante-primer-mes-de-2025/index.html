Inflación en México se ubica en 3.59 durante primer mes de 2025

Inegi reports that Mexico’s annual inflation has declined to 3.59%, signaling a positive trend compared to the previous year. The core price index rose modestly, while volatile sectors like agriculture saw price drops, indicating stabilizing consumer costs. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it highlights the importance of accessible, high-quality statistical indicators. Transparency in such economic metrics allows citizens and developers to build applications that better monitor financial health and public policy impacts. Understanding these underlying trends helps society evaluate the real cost of living without noise from volatile items. Reliable public data fosters informed decision-making, enabling more accurate economic analyses and promoting accountability in national statistical reporting systems.
