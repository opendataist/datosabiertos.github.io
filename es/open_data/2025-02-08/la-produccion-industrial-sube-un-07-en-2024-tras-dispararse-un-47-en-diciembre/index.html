La producción industrial sube un 0,7% en 2024 tras dispararse un 4,7% en diciembre

The article highlights significant growth in Spain’s industrial output during 2024, driven primarily by the electronic, pharmaceutical, and paper sectors, while contrasting declines occurred in textiles and leather industries. Regional variations were marked, with autonomous communities like Castile and León and Catalonia leading the expansion, whereas others experienced contraction. This data reveals a complex economic landscape where certain high-value manufacturing segments are thriving while traditional industries struggle. Relevance to open data lies in the critical need for transparent, accessible, and granular statistics to monitor such economic shifts. The publication of detailed industrial production indices by official institutes like the INE exemplifies the importance of standardized, publicly available datasets. Without open access to this structured information, stakeholders cannot effectively analyze trends, compare regional performances, or identify sector-specific challenges. Ultimately, this report underscores how open data facilitates informed decision-making for policymakers and businesses. By providing clear metrics on production trends and sectoral performance, it enables a deeper understanding of industrial dynamics. Ensuring such data is freely accessible allows for better economic forecasting and resource allocation, reinforcing the role of open data as a foundational tool for transparency and evidence-based governance in public administration.
