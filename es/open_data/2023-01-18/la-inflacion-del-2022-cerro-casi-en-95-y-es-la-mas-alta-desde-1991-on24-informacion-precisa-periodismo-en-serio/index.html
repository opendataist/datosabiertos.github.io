La inflación del 2022 cerró casi en 95% y es la más alta desde 1991 | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

The article reports record-high inflation in Argentina, highlighting a significant annual increase that surpasses historical peaks. This surge reflects severe economic instability and a drastic rise in the cost of living, with essential categories like food, housing, and transportation seeing substantial price hikes. The data underscores the erosion of purchasing power and the widening gap between current prices and historical benchmarks, marking a critical shift in the country’s financial landscape. This report is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical outputs from public institutions to track macroeconomic indicators. It demonstrates how transparent, accessible government data serves as a fundamental resource for analyzing societal well-being. By making these indices publicly available, citizens and researchers can verify official narratives and understand the real-time impact of economic policies on everyday life. Ultimately, the availability of such granular data allows for a deeper understanding of inflationary pressures across different regions and sectors. It empowers stakeholders to assess the effectiveness of economic measures and identify vulnerable populations. This transparency is crucial for fostering accountability and enabling evidence-based discussions regarding future policy decisions and their consequences on the population.
