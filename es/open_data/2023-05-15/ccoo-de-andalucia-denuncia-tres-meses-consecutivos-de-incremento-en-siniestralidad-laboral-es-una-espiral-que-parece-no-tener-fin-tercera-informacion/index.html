CCOO de Andalucía denuncia tres meses consecutivos de incremento en siniestralidad laboral: “Es una espiral que parece no tener fin” - Tercera Información

Andalusia is experiencing a persistent rise in workplace accidents, particularly fatal ones and incidents occurring during working hours, contrary to the decrease in commutes. This trend highlights a critical disconnect where increasing corporate profits coincide with deteriorating safety standards, suggesting that companies are deprioritizing risk prevention to cut costs. The union demands the immediate implementation of a shock plan against workplace casualties and stricter government enforcement of safety legislation. There is an urgent need to hold employers accountable and ensure that legal compliance and preventive measures are rigorously monitored to reverse this dangerous upward spiral of injuries and deaths. This situation is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistics to reveal systemic failures in labor protection. Transparent access to disaggregated accident data allows civil society to identify dangerous trends, such as the spike in fatalities among the self-employed, and effectively advocate for policy changes and regulatory accountability.
