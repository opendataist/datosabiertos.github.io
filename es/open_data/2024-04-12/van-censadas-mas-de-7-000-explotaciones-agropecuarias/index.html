Van censadas más de 7.000 explotaciones agropecuarias

The agricultural census demonstrates the viability of hybrid data collection, combining digital, telephonic, and field methods to achieve broad coverage. This approach highlights the importance of accessible, multi-channel strategies for gathering reliable public statistics while respecting data privacy laws. The transition to predominantly remote collection mechanisms underscores the efficiency and scalability of digital tools in modern census operations. This case is relevant to open data as it validates robust, compliant methods for acquiring comprehensive sectoral information, enabling better transparency and future data utilization.
