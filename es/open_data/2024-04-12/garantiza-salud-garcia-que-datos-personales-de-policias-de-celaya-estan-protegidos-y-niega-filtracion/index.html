Garantiza Salud García que datos personales de policías de Celaya están protegidos y niega filtración

The article highlights the vulnerability of municipal police officers, who face significant risks during their rest days, as evidenced by recent murders in Celaya. Despite these tragic incidents, local authorities firmly deny any data breaches or leaks of personal information from the Security Secretariat. This stance is crucial for open data contexts, as it underscores the tension between transparency and citizen privacy. The narrative emphasizes that personal data is strictly protected under confidentiality clauses, aiming to maintain public trust in institutional security protocols while acknowledging the severe safety threats faced by law enforcement personnel. The relevance lies in demonstrating how governments handle sensitive data amid crises. It illustrates the importance of maintaining robust data protection measures to prevent misuse of personal information, even when high-profile security failures occur. This balance is essential for preserving ethical standards in data management and ensuring that operational security does not compromise individual privacy rights unnecessarily.
