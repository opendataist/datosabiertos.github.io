Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This article is a standard privacy policy for the website Hipertextual, detailing how personal data such as cookies and usage metrics are collected and processed. It establishes the legal framework for data handling, citing compliance with Spanish electronic commerce laws and broader European protection standards. The text outlines the specific purposes of data collection, which include service provision, advertising, and web analytics, while identifying the entities involved in this processing, both internally and through third-party contractors. The policy highlights the user's rights to access, rectify, and delete their data, as well as the ability to object to processing or withdraw consent. It emphasizes that while some data collection is mandatory for service functionality, other aspects rely on legitimate interests or contractual necessities. Crucially, it notes that users under higher protection standards, typically those in the EU, have enhanced rights regarding data transfer and monitoring, ensuring transparency about where data is stored and how it might be shared across borders. This document is relevant to open data discussions because it illustrates the baseline legal and ethical constraints surrounding data privacy in the digital ecosystem. Even for organizations aiming to publish open data, understanding the distinction between anonymous aggregated statistics and personal identifiable information is vital. This policy serves as a practical example of how entities must balance operational data needs with individual privacy rights, reinforcing the principle that open data initiatives must not compromise personal security or violate established user consent mechanisms.
