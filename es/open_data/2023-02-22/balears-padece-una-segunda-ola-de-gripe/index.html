Balears padece una segunda ola de gripe

The pandemic fundamentally disrupted the traditional seasonality and behavior of respiratory viruses, making rare phenomena like dual influenza waves and prolonged symptoms prevalent. This shift indicates a significant departure from historical patterns, as SARS-CoV-2 has dominated the epidemiological landscape, altering how different viruses interact and circulate simultaneously. This unpredictability creates substantial uncertainty for public health planning, as experts can no longer rely on established annual models for vaccine development or disease management. The co-circulation of multiple viruses challenges previous assumptions about viral dominance and seasonal expectations. The article is relevant to open data because understanding these complex, non-linear epidemiological shifts requires transparent, accessible health statistics. Open data facilitates the analysis of these unprecedented trends, enabling researchers and policymakers to monitor evolving viral behaviors and adapt public health strategies to new, unpredictable realities.
