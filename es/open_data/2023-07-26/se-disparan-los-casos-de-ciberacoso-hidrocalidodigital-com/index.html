Se disparan los casos de ciberacoso | HidrocalidoDigital.com

Aguascalientes exhibits significantly higher rates of cyberbullying compared to the national average, particularly affecting women through the receipt of sexual content. This stark disparity highlights a critical regional vulnerability where digital safety mechanisms are disproportionately failing female users. The data reveals that a substantial portion of the internet-using population has experienced harassment, with sexual content delivery being a primary form of abuse. This trend underscores the urgent need for targeted interventions to protect vulnerable demographics from online exploitation and gender-based violence in the digital sphere. This report is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how granular statistical evidence can expose localized inequalities in digital rights. By making such specific metrics accessible, it empowers civil society to demand better data protection policies and hold institutions accountable for ensuring equitable online safety standards.
