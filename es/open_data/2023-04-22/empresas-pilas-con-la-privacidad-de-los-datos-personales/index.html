Empresas: pilas con la privacidad de los datos personales

The article highlights the growing tension between rapid digital transformation and the necessity for robust data privacy protections in Colombia. As internet usage and digital banking operations surge, the exposure of personal information increases, leading to a significant rise in consumer complaints and regulatory investigations. This trend underscores the urgent need for organizations to prioritize data security amidst an expanding digital economy. A central implication is the critical importance of legal compliance and transparency under Colombia’s data protection framework. Entities must secure informed consent, clearly communicate data usage purposes, and ensure individuals can access or rectify their information. The concept of demonstrated accountability requires companies to not only adhere to regulations but also actively prove they have implemented appropriate measures to safeguard privacy rights and manage risks effectively. This content is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the foundational legal and ethical constraints that govern data handling. Understanding these privacy boundaries is essential for anyone designing open data initiatives, as it ensures that publicly shared datasets respect individual rights and regulatory standards, thereby fostering trust and ensuring sustainable, compliant data ecosystems.
