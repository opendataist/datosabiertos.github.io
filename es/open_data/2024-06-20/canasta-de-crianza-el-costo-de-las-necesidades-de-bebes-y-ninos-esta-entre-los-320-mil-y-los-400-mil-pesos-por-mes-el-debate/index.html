Canasta de Crianza: el costo de las necesidades de bebés y niños está entre los 320 mil y los 400 mil pesos por mes - EL DEBATE

The publication of the "Canasta Crianza" reveals that economic conditions force most children to suffer from deprivations of basic rights. By quantifying the essential costs for goods, services, and care time across different age groups, the data highlights a significant gap in social protection, underscoring the urgent need to address how financial instability directly impacts childhood well-being and development. This official index serves as a critical tool for the judiciary, specifically to support litigation regarding unpaid child support. By providing standardized, monthly valuations of a child’s needs, it establishes a concrete basis for legal enforcement. This shifts the conversation from abstract estimates to measurable economic realities, ensuring that family law decisions are grounded in accurate, up-to-date cost assessments rather than arbitrary figures. For open data communities, this initiative demonstrates how transparent statistical methods can bridge the gap between policy and justice. The availability of granular data on consumption and care costs allows for better monitoring of social rights and facilitates evidence-based advocacy. It showcases the power of standardized, publicly accessible datasets to empower marginalized groups and hold institutions accountable for meeting human rights obligations.
