El INDEC publicó por primera vez la Canasta Crianza, el índice que servirá como referente para establecer la cuota alimentaria en la Justicia

The INDEC launched the new Child Raising Basket index to objectively quantify essential costs for children, serving as a crucial reference for determining alimony. This data-driven approach aims to standardize legal rulings and reduce disputes between parents regarding necessary expenses. Crucially, this transparency supports the millions of households, predominantly female-led, that struggle to receive timely child support payments. By providing an authoritative metric, it empowers custodial caregivers to validate their claims and secure adequate financial resources for their children’s well-being. This initiative is vital for open data because it demonstrates how publicly accessible, standardized statistics can directly improve social justice. It transforms raw economic figures into actionable tools for policy enforcement, highlighting the real-world impact of transparent government data on vulnerable populations and family rights.
