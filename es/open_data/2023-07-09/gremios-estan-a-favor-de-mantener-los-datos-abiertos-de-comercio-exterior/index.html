Gremios están a favor de mantener los datos abiertos de comercio exterior

The disappearance of extensive foreign trade data from Paraguay’s customs website, driven by judicial orders protecting individual importers' privacy, has sparked significant concern. Industry associations argue that prioritizing personal interests over the general public good undermines transparency and deprives citizens of essential tools for oversight. This regression highlights the critical tension between data privacy rights and the public’s right to access information. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the fragility of public information ecosystems when faced with legal challenges. The conflict demonstrates how privacy claims can lead to the suppression of large-scale datasets that are vital for societal accountability and economic analysis. To resolve this, authorities and business leaders are negotiating a compromise. The proposed solution involves anonymizing specific importer details while preserving the aggregate dataset. This approach aims to balance privacy concerns with the necessity of maintaining open, accessible data for the public and international partners.
