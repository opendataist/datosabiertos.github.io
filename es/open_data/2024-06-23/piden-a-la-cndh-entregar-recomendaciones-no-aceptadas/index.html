Piden a la CNDH entregar recomendaciones no aceptadas

The INAI ordered the CNDH to fully disclose unaccepted recommendations, rejecting incomplete data. This ruling emphasizes that transparency enables public scrutiny to ensure human rights effectiveness. It reinforces open data principles by mandating complete, accessible information for accountability and verifying the actual enforcement of administrative or legal actions.
