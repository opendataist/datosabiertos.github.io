La inflación interanual de Argentina alcanza los tres dígitos

Argentina’s severe inflation threatens to miss official annual targets, highlighting a critical gap between government projections and economic reality. This discrepancy underscores the urgent need for reliable, accessible statistical data to inform public policy and restore economic trust. Regional and sectoral variations reveal how specific shocks, such as drought, drive localized price spikes. Understanding these granular patterns requires transparent datasets that allow analysts to identify root causes and measure policy effectiveness accurately. This situation demonstrates why open data is essential for independent verification of economic indicators. Without transparent, timely information, stakeholders cannot effectively assess the success of stabilization programs or the true impact on citizens' purchasing power.
