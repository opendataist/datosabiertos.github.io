Datos Abiertos (Open data)

Chihuahua invites citizens to co-create solutions for violence and economic challenges through open government working groups. This participatory approach aims to shape the city’s first Open Government Action Plan, emphasizing collaborative policy design. It highlights open data’s role in fostering transparency and civic engagement in municipal governance.
