The INAI advises launching a constitutional challenge against the electoral "Plan B" to protect citizens' personal data. This plan would relocate voter service centers to municipal buildings, removing them from the direct control of the electoral institute. Moving these facilities creates significant security risks, as third-party control complicates the safeguarding of sensitive voter information. The institute faces difficulties ensuring the physical safety of equipment and electronic records containing biometric and demographic details outside its own premises. This issue is vital for open data because it highlights the tension between institutional flexibility and privacy obligations. It underscores the necessity of robust legal frameworks and strict security protocols when handling public information. Ensuring that government operations do not compromise individual privacy rights remains a fundamental principle for maintaining trust in open and transparent governance systems.
