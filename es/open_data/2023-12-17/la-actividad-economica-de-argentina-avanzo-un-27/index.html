La actividad económica de Argentina avanzó un 2,7 %

Argentina’s economy showed mixed signals in late 2023, posting a quarterly growth of 2.7% year-over-year despite an overall annual contraction. While sectors like hospitality and mining expanded, agriculture and manufacturing suffered significant declines, highlighting the fragility of the economic recovery amidst a political transition. The government immediately implemented strict austerity measures, including a major currency devaluation, to stabilize the market following the new administration's inauguration. These adjustments aim to address deep-seated structural issues, reflecting a shift toward stringent fiscal policies intended to curb inflation and restore confidence in the financial system. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible, and timely economic statistics. Reliable official indicators from institutions like INDEC allow researchers and citizens to verify claims, analyze sectoral performance, and hold leaders accountable, ensuring that policy decisions are grounded in accurate, publicly available information rather than opaque narratives.
