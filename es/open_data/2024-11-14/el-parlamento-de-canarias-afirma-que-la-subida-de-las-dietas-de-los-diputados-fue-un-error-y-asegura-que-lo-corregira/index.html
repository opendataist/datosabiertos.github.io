El Parlamento de Canarias afirma que la subida de las dietas de los diputados fue un "error" y asegura que lo corregirá

The Canary Islands Parliament is correcting its transparency portal after public scrutiny revealed significant errors in published data regarding deputies' salaries and allowances. This update is crucial because the previous information contained obsolete concepts and miscalculated amounts, misleading the public about actual expenditures. Correcting these discrepancies highlights the importance of data accuracy in government transparency initiatives. When official datasets contain inconsistencies, they undermine public trust and obscure the true cost of political operations, necessitating rigorous verification processes. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates that publishing data alone is insufficient if the underlying information is flawed. It underscores the need for continuous maintenance and validation of open datasets to ensure they remain reliable tools for accountability and civic engagement.
