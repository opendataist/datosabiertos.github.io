Inegi medirá pobreza multidimensional en los 2 mil 478 municipios del país - La Jornada San Luis

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography has released key data from the Intercensal Survey to the General Directorate for Poverty Evaluation, establishing the foundation for next year’s multidimensional poverty estimates at the municipal level. This release marks a significant operational shift, as the institute now directly provides the essential inputs previously managed by other bodies. This transfer of responsibility enables a comprehensive analysis of poverty across all Mexican municipalities, moving beyond previous regional generalizations. By utilizing this extensive survey data, authorities can now track poverty dynamics with greater precision and granularity at the local level, identifying trends that were previously obscured by broader administrative boundaries. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how interconnected statistical surveys create robust, actionable public knowledge. By making survey results available to specialized measurement agencies, the government enhances transparency and improves the quality of publicly accessible socioeconomic indicators, allowing for more informed policy decisions and civic oversight.
