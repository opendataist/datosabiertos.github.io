Increíble: este país ya tiene más de 100,000 personas que superan los 100 años

Japan has witnessed a historic milestone with its centenarian population exceeding 100,000, a figure that underscores the nation’s rapid demographic aging and the critical challenges associated with a shrinking workforce. This surge in extreme longevity, driven by advancements in healthcare, diet, and lifestyle, highlights a societal shift where maintaining a sustainable social security system is increasingly urgent as the proportion of elderly citizens continues to rise significantly. Concurrently, the country faces a profound demographic decline, with fertility rates falling well below the replacement level and forecasts predicting a substantial reduction in the total population over the coming decades. Young adults express growing skepticism toward traditional family structures due to the high costs of raising children and the burden of caregiving, reflecting a broader societal tension between individual well-being and the need to reverse population contraction. This report is relevant to open data as it exemplifies how public statistics drive evidence-based policy discussions on sustainability and social welfare. By making such demographic indicators transparent, governments and researchers can analyze trends in aging and birth rates, fostering informed debates on resource allocation and the long-term viability of social systems in an increasingly elderly society.
