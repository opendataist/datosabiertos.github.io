Según INE hay 96.500 nuevos empleos y Peña se entusiasma con cumplir promesa de 500 mil trabajos

Peña highlights substantial job creation and falling unemployment rates in Paraguay, emphasizing the government's ambitious five-year goal of half a million new positions. This progress reflects strong growth in commerce and social services, signaling improved economic stability and reduced informal labor. The article demonstrates the power of open data by using official INE statistics to verify government claims and track societal impact. Transparent, publicly accessible metrics allow citizens to understand policy outcomes, ensuring accountability and enabling informed public discourse on employment trends. Ultimately, this transparency fosters trust in public institutions. When data is open and rigorously reported, it empowers the community to assess real progress, driving better governance decisions and holding leaders accountable to their stated social and economic objectives through verifiable evidence.
