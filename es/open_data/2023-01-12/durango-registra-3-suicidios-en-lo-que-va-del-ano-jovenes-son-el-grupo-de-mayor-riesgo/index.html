Durango registra 3 suicidios en lo que va del año; jóvenes son el grupo de mayor riesgo

The article highlights a troubling rise in suicide rates among young people in Durango and Mexico, emphasizing that depression is a critical driver of this trend. With young individuals now identified as the highest-risk group, local experts warn of a significant disparity between completed suicides and attempted cases. This situation underscores the urgent need for enhanced prevention strategies and stronger public health interventions to curb the increasing numbers. Depression is presented as a widespread and severe health issue that disrupts daily life, work, and social connections, often leading to tragic outcomes if untreated. The text stresses that depression affects millions globally and requires specialized medical attention. Despite its prevalence, the condition often goes unrecognized, particularly among adolescents and the elderly, making early diagnosis and public awareness essential for effective management and support. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how accessible statistical information can inform public policy and social advocacy. By making mortality and mental health statistics transparent, governments and organizations can better allocate resources and design targeted interventions. Open data enables researchers and citizens to track trends, hold institutions accountable, and promote evidence-based solutions to combat the growing mental health crisis among vulnerable populations.
