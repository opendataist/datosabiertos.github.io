The report reveals a strong recovery in Mexico’s tourism sector, driven primarily by international visitors who spend significantly more than border tourists. This surge in foreign exchange earnings indicates a robust rebound of the national economy from pandemic lows, signaling renewed global confidence in Mexican destinations. The data highlights that overnight international tourists are the main engine of growth, while border tourism has slightly declined. This shift underscores the importance of attracting travelers who stay longer and spend more deeply in local economies, rather than short-term cross-border visits. This information is relevant to open data because it provides essential, transparent economic indicators for analyzing tourism trends. Public access to such detailed statistics enables researchers, policymakers, and businesses to make evidence-based decisions and monitor the effectiveness of tourism strategies over time.
