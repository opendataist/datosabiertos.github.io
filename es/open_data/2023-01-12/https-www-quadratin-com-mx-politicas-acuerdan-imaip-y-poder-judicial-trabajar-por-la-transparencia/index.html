Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Judicial Power of Michoacán and the state’s data protection agency have strengthened their collaboration to promote an open justice system. This ongoing partnership focuses on transparency and safeguarding citizens' personal information, aiming to shift institutional culture toward greater civic responsiveness. Recognized for its proactive transparency efforts, the judiciary is working with the oversight body to deepen public trust. Their joint strategy includes implementing new educational initiatives designed to train personnel on legal obligations regarding accountability and data protection standards. This initiative is vital for open data because it bridges the gap between legal procedures and public access. By fostering a culture of transparency within the judiciary, the agreement ensures that judicial information is more accessible, demonstrating how government entities can practically apply open governance principles to serve society better.
