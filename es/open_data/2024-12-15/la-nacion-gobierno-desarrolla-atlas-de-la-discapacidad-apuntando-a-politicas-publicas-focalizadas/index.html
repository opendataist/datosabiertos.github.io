La Nación / Gobierno desarrolla Atlas de la discapacidad apuntando a políticas públicas focalizadas

The National Institute of Statistics has launched Paraguay’s first Disability Atlas, a unified digital platform integrating standardized data from government bodies and civil society organizations. By consolidating previously fragmented information, this initiative aims to provide a comprehensive and accurate overview of the disability population, marking a significant shift toward inclusive and coordinated public administration. This centralized resource serves as a vital tool for designing evidence-based public policies and optimizing resource allocation. It enables authorities to precisely locate service needs and tailor interventions, thereby improving the efficiency of both state agencies and non-governmental organizations working toward social inclusion and equity. The project is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized, and accessible information can drive societal change. By making aggregated statistics publicly available, the atlas promotes accountability in public spending and encourages informed decision-making, setting a precedent for using open datasets to address complex social issues effectively.
