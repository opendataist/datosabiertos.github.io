Gobierno someterá actualización de leyes de Protección de Datos Personales y Ciberdelito

The Dominican government is updating its data protection, high-tech crime, and cybersecurity laws to safeguard citizens’ rights amid rapid digital transformation. This legislative shift highlights the critical need for robust open data frameworks that balance innovation with fundamental privacy protections, ensuring transparency and accountability in an increasingly connected society. Regulating artificial intelligence requires addressing algorithmic transparency to prevent bias and inequality, relying on clear governance norms. Furthermore, the creation of ICT satellite accounts aims to accurately measure the economic impact of digital technologies. These initiatives underscore the importance of high-quality, accessible open data for tracking technological progress and informing evidence-based policy decisions. These efforts are vital for open data advocates as they demonstrate how transparent, reliable data systems support both economic measurement and ethical AI implementation. By prioritizing detailed digital investment tracking and strong privacy laws, the country sets a precedent for using open data to foster trust, ensure equitable technology use, and drive sustainable digital development in the region.
