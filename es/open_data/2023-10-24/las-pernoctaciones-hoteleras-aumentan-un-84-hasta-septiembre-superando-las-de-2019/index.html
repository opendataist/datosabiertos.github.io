Las pernoctaciones hoteleras aumentan un 8,4% hasta septiembre, superando las de 2019

Spain’s tourism sector has demonstrated a robust recovery, with hotel overnight stays surpassing pre-pandemic levels from 2019. This growth is driven by both domestic and international travelers, indicating a strong demand for Spanish destinations across key regions like Andalusia, Catalonia, and the Balearic Islands. The resilience of the industry suggests that the market has not only regained its footing but is currently outperforming previous benchmarks, reflecting a healthy rebound in consumer confidence and travel activity. Revenue metrics further highlight this positive trajectory, as daily average rates and total billing increased significantly compared to the previous year. While inflation has led to higher hotel prices, the rate of increase has moderated relative to the previous year’s peak. This balance between higher occupancy and controlled price growth implies improved profitability for hotels without completely alienating price-sensitive consumers, sustaining a viable economic model for the sector. Transparent, standardized metrics allow researchers, policymakers, and businesses to monitor economic trends accurately.
