Abinader defiende transparencia con estadísticas sobre casos dengue

The Dominican government emphasizes absolute transparency regarding dengue statistics, rejecting allegations of data concealment as dishonest and comparing them to misinformation spread during the pandemic. Officials assert that while methodological errors may occur, there is no intentional manipulation of figures. This stance aims to maintain public trust by ensuring that health metrics accurately reflect the reality of the disease's incidence and mortality rates. Ministerial reports indicate a declining trend in dengue cases, with positive confirmation rates among suspicious cases dropping significantly. The authorities attribute this improvement to recent fumigation efforts and clarify that other circulating viruses often mimic dengue symptoms, complicating diagnosis. Consequently, the epidemiological alert remains active, urging vigilance as the government seeks to minimize deaths and further reduce transmission through targeted health interventions. Beyond immediate outbreak control, the administration highlights substantial investments in healthcare infrastructure, including hospital renovations and expanded insurance coverage. These initiatives aim to improve overall health outcomes, evidenced by reduced maternal mortality and increased access to specialized oncology and trauma care. This context reinforces the importance of open data in validating public health successes and demonstrating governmental commitment to systemic improvements in national health services.
