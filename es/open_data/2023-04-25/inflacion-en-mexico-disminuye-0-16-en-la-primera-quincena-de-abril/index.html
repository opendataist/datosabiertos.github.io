Inflación en México disminuye 0.16% en la primera quincena de abril

The article highlights that Mexico’s annual inflation fell to 6.24%, marking its lowest level since late 2021. This decline exceeded analyst expectations, driven by a significant drop in core inflation as well. However, prices remain substantially above the central bank’s target range, indicating that economic stabilization is still ongoing after prolonged periods of elevated costs. This data is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates the critical role of transparent, timely statistical publications from institutions like INEGI. Such accessible data allows for accurate public monitoring of economic health, enabling citizens and experts to verify official figures against market forecasts. Ultimately, the release of this information fosters accountability and trust in government statistics. By making detailed inflation metrics publicly available, the state supports evidence-based decision-making and facilitates a deeper understanding of how national economic indicators impact everyday life and policy effectiveness.
