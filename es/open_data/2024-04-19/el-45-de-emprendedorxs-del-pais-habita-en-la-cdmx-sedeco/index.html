El 45% de emprendedorxs del país habita en la CDMX: Sedeco

The article highlights that Mexican micro, small, and medium enterprises drive a significant portion of the national economy and employment, with a substantial concentration of entrepreneurs in Mexico City. This underscores the critical role these businesses play in sustainable economic development and stability across the country. To support this vital sector, local government initiatives provide accessible training in financial health and business planning. By lowering barriers to entry through free courses, authorities aim to empower new business owners with the necessary tools for long-term success and responsible management.
