"EN LA TARDANZA ESTÁ EL RIESGO": Sin fecha para iniciar censo de casas a beneficiar con el programa 'Barrio Mágico' en Chetumal, a seis meses que fue anunciado - Noticaribe

The article highlights the critical delay in launching a census required to identify eligible homes for the "Barrio Mágico" revitalization program in Chetumal. This hesitation poses a severe risk to the preservation of the city’s remaining historic wooden houses, which have drastically decreased in number over decades due to neglect and lack of maintenance resources. The urgency stems from the fact that these architectural heritage sites are rapidly deteriorating, with many already lost to decay or demolition. Without immediate official action to inventory the remaining structures, the cultural identity of the declared magic town is in danger of irreversible disappearance, undermining the very purpose of the government’s tourism initiative. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of timely, transparent, and accessible public datasets for effective cultural heritage management. Reliable data on the number and condition of historic homes is essential for accountability, resource allocation, and ensuring that public programs accurately target the beneficiaries they claim to protect.
