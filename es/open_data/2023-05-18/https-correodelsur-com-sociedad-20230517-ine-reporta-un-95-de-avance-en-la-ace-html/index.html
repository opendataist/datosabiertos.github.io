Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The statistical institute is nearing full cartographic coverage in Chuquisaca, aiming for completion by May. This progress supports open data initiatives by ensuring comprehensive geographic accuracy for future census experiments and recruitment. A pilot census will soon test methodologies in mid-sized municipalities. These trials are crucial for refining data collection protocols, enhancing transparency, and improving the reliability of open statistical resources for public use. Large-scale volunteer recruitment for March 2024 highlights the operational scale required for national censuses. Such extensive engagement underscores the need for robust open data frameworks to manage, share, and analyze the vast amount of demographic information generated.
