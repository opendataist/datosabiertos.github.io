Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Delfina Gómez pledges to prioritize anti-corruption efforts in the State of Mexico through a government based on transparency and accountability. She aims to empower citizens to verify how public resources are spent, ensuring every peso is openly accounted for. This initiative seeks to address the state’s poor rankings in transparency and high perception of corruption. By strengthening oversight mechanisms and encouraging civic participation, the administration plans to eliminate illicit practices and restore public trust. This aligns with open data principles by advocating for the public availability of government spending information. It highlights how accessible financial data can empower citizens to monitor state actions, serving as a crucial tool for preventing corruption and promoting institutional integrity.
