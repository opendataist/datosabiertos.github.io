Recomiendan desactivar la Inteligencia Artificial de WhatsApp

The integration of Meta AI into WhatsApp has sparked significant controversy regarding user privacy and data sovereignty. Critics argue that the tool exacerbates the platform’s history of surveillance capitalism by granting Meta deeper access to personal conversations and behavioral patterns. This raises serious concerns about how sensitive information is managed, potentially leading to intrusive personalized advertising and algorithmic profiling that invades user autonomy. Users are increasingly seeking ways to mitigate these risks, as the AI cannot be permanently removed but its presence can be significantly reduced. By employing methods such as deleting or archiving the specific chat thread, individuals can reclaim a greater sense of control over their digital environment. These technical steps serve as a practical response to the fear of unintended data sharing and the potential for AI errors that might misinterpret personal interactions. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical tension between proprietary AI integration and individual rights to transparency and consent. It underscores the need for robust data protection frameworks in open source communities and public discourse, emphasizing that technological innovation must not come at the cost of fundamental privacy rights. The ongoing debate reflects a broader call for ethical standards in AI deployment within widely used communication platforms.
