Arrancan negociaciones Segob-INAI para evitar la extinción

The INAI successfully secured the protection of its workforce from elimination, despite political pressures to dismantle the agency. While the Ministry of Interior remained non-committal on structural reforms, this dialogue highlights the ongoing struggle to preserve institutional independence. This interaction is crucial for open data, as it safeguards the entity responsible for enforcing transparency and ensuring public access to government information.
