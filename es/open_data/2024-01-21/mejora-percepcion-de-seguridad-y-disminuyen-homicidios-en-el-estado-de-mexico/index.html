Mejora percepción de seguridad y disminuyen homicidios en el Estado de México

The article highlights a positive shift in public safety metrics within the State of Mexico, attributing improved security perceptions and reduced homicide rates to coordinated efforts between federal and state authorities. This collaborative approach has fostered a more tranquil environment for citizens, demonstrating that unified governance strategies can effectively enhance community safety and judicial performance. These developments are particularly relevant to open data initiatives, as they underscore the necessity of transparent, accessible statistics for evaluating policy success. Publicly available survey results and crime reports allow citizens and analysts to verify official claims, fostering trust in government institutions and enabling evidence-based decision-making for future security measures. Ultimately, the improvement serves as a case study for how data-driven transparency supports democratic accountability. By making these indicators openly available, the administration not only validates its actions but also encourages continuous public engagement. This openness is essential for maintaining societal trust and ensuring that security policies remain responsive to the actual needs and perceptions of the population.
