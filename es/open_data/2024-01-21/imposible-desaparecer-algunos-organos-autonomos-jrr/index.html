Imposible desaparecer algunos órganos autónomos: JRR

The article emphasizes the critical importance of maintaining autonomous bodies like the central bank and electoral institute, despite calls for reduced bureaucratic spending. This highlights the ongoing tension between implementing austerity measures and preserving institutional independence within Mexico’s governance framework. Such debates are highly relevant to open data, as these autonomous organizations are primary custodians of public statistical and electoral information. Ensuring their operational stability is vital for the continued production and transparency of high-quality public datasets that drive accountability and informed civic participation. Ultimately, the political focus remains on reviewing constitutional reforms through legislative consensus rather than immediate rejection. This collaborative approach aims to balance fiscal efficiency with the necessary structural integrity of state agencies responsible for managing and disclosing essential national data.
