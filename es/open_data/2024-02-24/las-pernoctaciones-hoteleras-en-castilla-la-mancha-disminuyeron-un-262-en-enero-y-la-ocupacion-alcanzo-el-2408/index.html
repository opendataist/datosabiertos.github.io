Las pernoctaciones hoteleras en Castilla-La Mancha disminuyeron un 2,62% en enero y la ocupación alcanzó el 24,08%

Castilla-La Mancha experienced a decline in total tourists in January, despite an increase in foreign visitors and higher room rates. While domestic travel dropped, the hotel sector still managed to expand its workforce significantly during this period. This data highlights the importance of granular tourism statistics for regional economic analysis. Understanding these shifts helps policymakers and investors assess market resilience and adjust strategies based on changing traveler demographics and pricing trends. For open data initiatives, such detailed, disaggregated information ensures transparency and supports evidence-based decision-making. Publicly accessible datasets on tourism metrics empower stakeholders to identify patterns, fostering informed discussions about sustainable development and resource allocation in the hospitality industry.
