Castelló y la Vall d’Uixó, los grandes municipios de la provincia que pierden población

The National Statistics Institute’s release of municipal demographic data reveals that most municipalities in Castellón are growing, with only the capital and Vall d’Uixó experiencing population declines. This shift highlights a broader trend of demographic stability or expansion in the region, contrasting with recent historical losses in specific areas. These findings are relevant to open data as they demonstrate the transparency of government statistics, allowing citizens and researchers to monitor local development trends. Access to such granular data supports evidence-based decision-making for urban planning and resource allocation. By providing detailed insights into population movements, this dataset empowers communities to understand regional dynamics. It underscores the importance of accessible public information for analyzing social changes and planning future infrastructure needs effectively across different municipal zones.
