Inflación anual en diciembre de 2022 se ubicó en 7.82%: Inegi

Mexico’s inflation eased in December as core price pressures finally reversed their two-year upward trend. This deceleration suggests the central bank’s aggressive interest rate hikes may be beginning to stabilize the economy, offering hope for a return to long-term monetary targets. This data is relevant to open_data because it highlights the critical importance of publicly accessible, high-frequency economic indicators for market transparency. Accurate, timely government statistics allow researchers and citizens to monitor policy effectiveness and assess financial health without relying on filtered narratives. Ultimately, the report demonstrates how open statistical data empowers informed public discourse. By making these metrics available, institutions foster trust and enable a deeper understanding of inflation dynamics, ensuring that societal responses to economic shifts are grounded in verified facts rather than speculation.
