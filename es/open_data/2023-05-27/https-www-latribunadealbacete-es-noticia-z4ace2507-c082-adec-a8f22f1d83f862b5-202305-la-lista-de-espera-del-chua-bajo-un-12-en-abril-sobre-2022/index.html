The article highlights a significant decrease in the patient waiting list at the Albacete University Hospital Complex, signaling a positive trend for regional health managers. This reduction, particularly evident in surgical specialties, suggests that despite persistent infrastructure challenges like operating room shortages, current strategies are effectively moving patients toward care. However, the data reveals that wait times remain critically high in key areas such as trauma and ophthalmology. Although the absolute number of patients has dropped compared to the previous year, the average duration patients must wait for surgery has actually increased in some specialties, indicating that while volume is down, systemic delays persist. This report is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the practical value of transparent, publicly accessible healthcare metrics. By making waiting list statistics available, authorities enable citizens to monitor service performance and hold institutions accountable, fostering trust through data-driven governance and ensuring that health system efficiency is visible and verifiable by the public.
