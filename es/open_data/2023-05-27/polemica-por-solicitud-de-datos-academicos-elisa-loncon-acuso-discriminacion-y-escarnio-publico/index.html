Polémica por solicitud de datos académicos: Elisa Loncon acusó "discriminación" y "escarnio público"

Elisa Loncon faces legal pressure to release her academic records, arguing these disclosures constitute public humiliation and discrimination against her. The Transparency Council insists that such data are inherently public, rejecting her claim of persecution while demanding full transparency. This conflict highlights the tension between individual privacy rights and the open data principle that government-related information must be freely accessible.
