Ciberataque: filtran una base de datos con 11 millones de registros de ciudadanos argentinos - La Voz de Cataratas

Cybersecurity experts have revealed the illegal publication of a database containing millions of Argentine citizens' records, likely originating from an electoral register. This breach exposes sensitive personal information, including full names, identification numbers, and home addresses, raising significant concerns about the privacy and security of personal data held by public institutions. Additionally, the attackers compromised access to over a hundred security cameras by exploiting an unpatched, years-old vulnerability in specific camera models. This incident highlights a critical systemic issue: manufacturers often fail to provide timely patches for discontinued products, leaving users vulnerable to unauthorized access even when the severity of the flaw is widely recognized. This event is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of strict data governance and security standards. It serves as a stark warning that open access mechanisms must be balanced with robust protection protocols to prevent mass privacy violations. Without stringent safeguards, the pursuit of transparency can inadvertently facilitate large-scale data exploitation and erode public trust in digital systems.
