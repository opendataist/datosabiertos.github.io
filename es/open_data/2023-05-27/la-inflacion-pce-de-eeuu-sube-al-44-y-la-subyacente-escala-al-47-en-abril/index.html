La inflación PCE de EEUU sube al 4,4% y la subyacente escala al 4,7% en abril

The recent uptick in the U.S. Personal Consumption Expenditures price index signals persistent inflationary pressures, catching markets off guard. This resurgence challenges expectations of easing and intensifies scrutiny on monetary policy, suggesting that central bankers may face difficulties in achieving their inflation targets without further tightening measures. The divergence between rising service costs and stable goods prices highlights structural economic shifts. Analysts interpret this data as a catalyst for renewed debate regarding interest rate trajectories, with some predicting potential hikes while others maintain forecasts of stability. The ambiguity in these projections underscores the delicate balance policymakers must strike to curb inflation without stifling growth.
