Según el INDEC, los salarios privados y públicos crecieron más que la inflación | Análisis

Argentina’s salary index rose sharply in July, outpacing inflation and allowing the president to celebrate a recovery in real wages. This growth reflects increases across both public and private sectors, with the unregistered private segment showing the most significant gains. Despite the positive headline, this represents the second-lowest monthly increase this year. The data highlights that while nominal wages climbed, the rate of growth has generally slowed compared to previous months, suggesting a moderation in the pace of wage recovery. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the necessity of official statistical transparency. By isolating variables like hours worked and performance bonuses, INDEC provides a clearer metric of economic reality, enabling policymakers and citizens to distinguish between genuine wage improvement and other structural factors.
