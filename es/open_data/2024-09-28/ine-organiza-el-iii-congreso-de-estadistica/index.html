INE organiza el III Congreso de Estadística

The National Institute of Statistics hosted a congress to promote statistical best practices and data culture for better public policy. This highlights how reliable numbers are essential for informed decision-making and transparency. The institution continues updating vital surveys and economic indicators, demonstrating the ongoing effort to maintain high-quality, accessible datasets. These updates ensure statistical tools remain relevant for measuring living conditions and service quality. This initiative is relevant to open data as it emphasizes the public sector’s role in generating trustworthy, usable information that empowers citizens and supports evidence-based governance through shared knowledge.
