Consejo de Autonomías aprueba siete puntos rumbo al Censo 2024 y respalda trabajo del INE

Bolivia’s National Council of Autonomies has reached a consensus on key operational points for the upcoming 2024 Census of Population and Housing, following a technical assessment by the National Institute of Statistics. The agreement highlights the collaborative efforts across all government levels, emphasizing that this joint work ensures the census serves the general well-being of the Bolivian people. This unified approach provides essential certainty for future economic and social planning at both national and local scales, reinforcing the institutional integrity of the data collection process. Significant attention is given to logistical resilience and cultural inclusivity. Authorities are coordinating contingency plans with autonomous territorial entities to mitigate risks from adverse weather conditions and implementing communication campaigns to address internal migration impacts. Furthermore, the process explicitly strengthens coordination with indigenous and campesino nations, ensuring their specific territorial, cultural, and identity characteristics are respected according to their own norms and procedures. Reaffirming the National Institute of Statistics as the sole competent entity for producing official statistics, the council committed to preventing the politicization of the census. Regular progress reports will continue to be issued, and all participants have pledged full compliance with the resolution. This event is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a transparent, unified framework for generating high-quality, standardized official statistics. By prioritizing technical consensus and legal clarity over political interference, the initiative safeguards the reliability and accessibility of future demographic data, which is critical for evidence-based governance and public transparency.
