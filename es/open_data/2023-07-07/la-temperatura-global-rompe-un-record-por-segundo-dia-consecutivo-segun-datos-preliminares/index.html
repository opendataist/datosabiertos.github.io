La temperatura global rompe un récord por segundo día consecutivo, según datos preliminares

Global temperatures have shattered historical records, with recent days marking the warmest on record since monitoring began decades ago. This unprecedented heat underscores the accelerating impact of fossil fuel combustion on the climate system, signaling a critical departure from normal seasonal fluctuations. The convergence of confirmed data from major meteorological agencies highlights the severity of current warming trends. Furthermore, the onset of El Niño suggests that temperatures may continue to rise, compounding existing climate stresses and pushing planetary boundaries further. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible, and real-time climate monitoring. By making such critical environmental datasets widely available, society can better understand, track, and respond to the urgent challenges posed by global warming.
