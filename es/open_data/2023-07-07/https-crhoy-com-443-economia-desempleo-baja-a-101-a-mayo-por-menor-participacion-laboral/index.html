Unemployment rates dropped significantly for both genders, yet overall employment fell sharply. This reveals that reduced unemployment stemmed from decreased labor market participation rather than improved job availability, as many withdrew from the workforce entirely. Subemployment remains a critical issue, affecting a notable portion of the employed population. These dynamics highlight the importance of open data in exposing the nuanced reality behind headline employment statistics. Understanding these hidden labor market shifts is essential for policymakers and researchers. Accessible, granular employment data allows for better analysis of economic health, ensuring that improvements in unemployment rates are not mistaken for genuine progress in job quality or availability.
