La DIGEIG reconoce a la CAASD en transparencia y gobierno abierto

The article highlights a stark contrast in the CAASD’s governance record, presenting both significant achievements and serious regulatory violations. Initially, the agency received high recognition for transparency and open data standards, scoring exceptionally well in government integrity assessments. This acknowledgment underscores the importance of accessible public information, as the evaluations specifically measure how well institutions facilitate the use, reuse, and redistribution of data through their open data portals, directly aligning with core open data principles. However, this positive narrative is severely undermined by a subsequent audit from the Comptroller General, which revealed widespread irregularities involving billions of pesos. The findings indicated a lack of transparency in financial management, with issues ranging from improper purchases to suspiciously timed contracts during emergency periods. These anomalies suggest that despite public-facing commitments to integrity, actual operational practices often violated public spending laws, raising critical questions about the reliability of self-reported metrics. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessity of verifying institutional claims through independent audit trails. While the agency claims to have corrected most issues and maintains a facade of functional transparency, the audit exposes a gap between reported performance and actual accountability. True open data requires not just the availability of information, but also verifiable integrity in how public resources are managed, making independent oversight essential for meaningful civic engagement.
