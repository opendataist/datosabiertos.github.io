El Indec actualizó multas a quienes no den información o la falseen

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses has significantly increased financial penalties for entities that fail to provide, falsify, or withhold essential data for statistical reports. This update ensures that legal obligations to supply information are strictly enforced, protecting the integrity of national statistics by penalizing non-compliance with timely and accurate reporting requirements. These measures are grounded in laws that mandate data provision while guaranteeing strict confidentiality, ensuring individual privacy and commercial secrets remain protected within aggregated compilations. By adjusting fines semi-annually based on inflation, the system maintains the deterrent effect of penalties, reinforcing the importance of reliable data submission for public interest purposes. This update is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the legal framework necessary for high-quality, accessible statistical information. Robust enforcement mechanisms encourage transparency and accuracy, which are foundational for trustworthy open datasets. Ultimately, these regulations support the ecosystem of reliable public data that drives informed decision-making and accountability in government and society.
