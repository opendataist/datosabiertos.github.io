Está en juego la garantía de derechos con parálisis del INAI: Blanca Ibarra

The INAI president highlights that the paralysis of the transparency commission threatens not just an institution, but two fundamental rights: public access to information and data protection. This dysfunction undermines democratic consolidation by removing a critical mechanism for citizen oversight, which is essential for exposing corruption and holding public officials accountable. The article underscores that transparency is a multifaceted tool that empowers citizens, strengthens freedom of expression, and supports the system of checks and balances. Transparency is relevant to open data because it establishes the legal and ethical foundation for making government information accessible. Open data initiatives rely on this principle of civic control to function effectively. By ensuring that public information belongs to society, open data becomes a practical instrument for scrutiny. This approach prevents abuse of power and fosters a culture of accountability, demonstrating how structured data release directly supports democratic integrity and anti-corruption efforts.
