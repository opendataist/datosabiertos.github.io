Registró México 15 mil asesinatos en el primer semestre de 2023: Inegi

The article highlights that Mexico’s homicide rate in early 2023 was the lowest since 2018, marking a significant improvement in public safety metrics. This data relies on official civil and forensic records, demonstrating the critical importance of standardized death certificates. These records serve as fundamental open data sources, capturing precise causes of death and demographic details. By digitizing and sharing this information, governments can enhance transparency and allow for better analysis of violence trends. Understanding these underlying data structures is vital for researchers and citizens seeking accountability. Reliable, accessible open data empowers communities to monitor security outcomes and demand evidence-based policy changes, turning raw statistics into actionable insights for societal well-being.
