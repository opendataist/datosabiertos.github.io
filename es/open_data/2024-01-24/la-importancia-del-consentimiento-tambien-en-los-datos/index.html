La importancia del consentimiento, también en los datos

The article emphasizes that protecting personal data is a shared responsibility, extending far beyond individual user precautions to encompass the ethical obligations of professionals handling consumer information. This perspective highlights the critical role of traceability and responsible usage in maintaining public trust during an era of heightened digital privacy concerns. It argues that reliable audience measurement now depends on panels built from individuals who have explicitly consented to share their data. In the post-cookie landscape, these transparent, auditable, and voluntary data sources are becoming indispensable for media and advertising sectors to understand cross-platform consumer behavior accurately and effectively. This topic is vital for open data because it underscores that true data value and sustainability rely on consent, transparency, and privacy respect rather than extraction. The focus on voluntary participation and ethical standards promotes a data ecosystem where openness is balanced with security, encouraging responsible innovation and ensuring that data utility does not compromise individual rights or democratic integrity.
