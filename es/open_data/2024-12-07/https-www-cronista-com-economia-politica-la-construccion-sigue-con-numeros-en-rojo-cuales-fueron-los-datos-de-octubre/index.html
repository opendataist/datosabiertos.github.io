ES EN

The National Statistics Institute (INDEC) revealed that Argentina’s manufacturing and construction sectors experienced significant contractions in October 2024. Both industries showed negative year-over-year comparisons, with construction suffering steep declines in activity and employment. This downturn is further evidenced by substantial drops in the consumption of essential building inputs, indicating a broad-based lack of demand and a severe slowdown in economic activity within these critical infrastructure sectors. Manufacturing production also retreated, with the majority of industrial divisions recording interannual decreases. While specific sub-sectors like food, beverages, and automotive production managed modest growth, the overarching trend remains negative. The persistence of declines across most manufacturing branches highlights a widespread stagnation, reflecting reduced industrial output and the challenging macroeconomic conditions facing the private sector. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the critical need for transparent, granular statistical dissemination to monitor economic health. By providing accessible metrics on production, employment, and input usage, such data enables researchers and policymakers to analyze trends accurately. Open access to these official indicators facilitates evidence-based decision-making, allowing stakeholders to assess the impact of economic policies and identify areas requiring urgent intervention.
