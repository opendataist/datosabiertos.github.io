El déficit del Estado baja al 0,04% del PIB y el público sube al 0,78%

The state deficit has significantly decreased, continuing a downward trend driven by improved economic activity and employment. This positive evolution highlights the effectiveness of recent fiscal management strategies in stabilizing public finances. While the general government deficit rose slightly, the state’s performance underscores stronger economic fundamentals. These figures demonstrate how economic growth directly impacts fiscal health, a key metric for assessing government transparency and accountability. This data is relevant to open data as it provides essential, publicly available fiscal indicators. Access to such detailed, timely information enables citizens and analysts to monitor government performance, fostering greater trust and informed public debate on economic policies.
