Invitan a artesanos de Celaya a inscribirse en padrón municipal

The municipality of Celaya has implemented a regional registry for artisans to better identify and support practitioners. This initiative aims to accurately catalog the number of artisans and their specific craft specializations, ensuring that limited public funding programs can be allocated more effectively to eligible individuals. By centralizing this data, the local cultural institute can streamline connections with national entities like Fonart. The registry acts as a dynamic tool to quickly detect eligible candidates during short-term funding calls, thereby improving advisory services and ensuring that support reaches those who meet professional artisan criteria rather than casual hobbyists. This measure is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of structured, accessible registries in bridging the gap between government support and local communities. When such information is organized and made available, it enhances transparency, reduces administrative bottlenecks, and ensures that public resources are distributed based on verified needs and professional standards.
