Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

“Miradas Comunitarias” showcases how participatory photography empowers marginalized communities to narrate their own identities and histories. By providing accessible analog tools, the initiative shifts the gaze from external observers to local residents, validating their perspectives on territory, tradition, and daily life. This approach highlights the value of inclusive visual storytelling, demonstrating how grassroots creative projects can foster social cohesion and cultural preservation. The exhibition serves as a testament to the enduring impact of community-led documentation, proving that personal narratives are vital for understanding complex social landscapes. For open data, this case underscores the importance of diverse, citizen-generated content. It challenges traditional data sourcing by emphasizing qualitative, human-centric information. Integrating such participatory visual datasets enriches open repositories with authentic, localized insights that official statistics often miss, promoting a more equitable and comprehensive data ecosystem.
