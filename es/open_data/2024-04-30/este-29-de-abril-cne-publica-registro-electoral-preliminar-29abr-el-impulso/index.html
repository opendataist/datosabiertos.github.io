▷ Este 29 de abril CNE publica registro electoral preliminar #29Abr - El Impulso

The Venezuelan National Electoral Council is releasing the preliminary electoral register, initiating a critical phase for citizens to verify their data and submit challenges. This transparent step ensures the accuracy of the voter database before the final roster is published, a process vital for maintaining the integrity of the upcoming presidential election. Concurrently, audits of electoral software and systems are conducted to guarantee technical security and fairness. These measures aim to establish public trust in the digital infrastructure, ensuring that the mechanisms used to cast and count votes are reliable and free from manipulation. Finally, the selection and notification of polling station members mark the logistical groundwork for a functioning democracy. This openness regarding personnel assignment supports accountability, demonstrating how accessible electoral data and clear procedures are essential for validating democratic processes and fostering civic engagement.
