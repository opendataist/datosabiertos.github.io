Voluntarios para el Censo alcanzan el 74,1%

The INE reports reaching 74% of its volunteer target for the upcoming census, heavily supported by youth participation. With most logistics materials prepared and distributed, the institution is ensuring operational readiness across departments. This progress highlights the critical role of community engagement and logistical coordination in generating reliable demographic data. High volunteer turnout is essential for accurate population counts, which form the basis for equitable resource allocation and policy planning. The article matters to open data because it underscores the infrastructure required to produce transparent, public statistics. Efficient census execution ensures that citizens and developers can access high-quality, authoritative data to drive evidence-based decisions and digital innovation in the public sector.
