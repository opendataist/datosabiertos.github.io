Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This article serves as a formal privacy policy for the website Hipertextual, detailing how personal data is collected, processed, and protected under Spanish law. It establishes the legal framework governing user information, including the specific data categories such as cookies and usage statistics, while clarifying that providing certain data is mandatory for service provision. The document outlines the company’s responsibilities regarding data security and the lawful bases for processing, ranging from user consent to contractual obligations and legitimate interests. The policy emphasizes the technical aspects of data handling, disclosing the use of third-party analytics tools like Google Analytics and advertising services that involve international data transfers, particularly to the United States. It informs users about the retention periods for their data, explaining that information is kept only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or legal requirements. Additionally, it highlights the roles of various authorized personnel and external contractors who may access the data to ensure the website functions correctly. This document is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the transparency requirements central to modern data ecosystems. By explicitly listing data processing activities, legal bases, and user rights, it demonstrates how organizations manage the intersection of proprietary digital services and individual privacy. Understanding such policies is crucial for developers and analysts who rely on public data, as it defines the boundaries of acceptable data usage and the extent to which digital interactions are tracked and shared with third parties.
