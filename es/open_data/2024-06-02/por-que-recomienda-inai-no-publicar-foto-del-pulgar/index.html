Por qué recomienda Inai no publicar foto del pulgar

The INAI advises citizens to refrain from posting photos of their inked finger or voter ID on social media to prevent the misuse of personal data during elections. This guidance highlights the critical need for individuals to actively protect their privacy and control how their biometric and identification information is shared publicly. These recommendations underscore the importance of safeguarding sensitive personal data against unauthorized use, particularly in high-stakes environments like voting processes. By avoiding the public exposure of such identifiers, citizens can mitigate risks associated with identity theft and unauthorized tracking. This is relevant to open data as it illustrates the tension between transparency and privacy. It emphasizes that while data openness is valuable, protecting individual privacy rights through informed consent and restricted sharing is essential to maintain trust in both digital platforms and democratic institutions.
