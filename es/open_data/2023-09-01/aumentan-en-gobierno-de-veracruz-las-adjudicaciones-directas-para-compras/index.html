Aumentan en Gobierno de Veracruz las adjudicaciones directas para compras

Veracruz significantly increased its reliance on direct awards for public contracts between 2021 and 2022, marking it as the second state in Mexico with the highest percentage of such allocations. This shift toward non-competitive procurement undermines transparency and reduces public accountability in fiscal management. Concurrently, the state diminished its use of public bidding processes, prioritizing restricted invitations instead. This trend highlights a broader structural move away from open competition in government spending, which often leads to inefficiencies and potential corruption risks within administrative frameworks. This article is relevant to open_data because it illustrates how centralized statistical data can expose gaps in procurement transparency. By analyzing government census records, stakeholders can identify systemic weaknesses in fiscal oversight, demonstrating the critical role of accessible, granular data in advocating for fairer and more accountable public contracting systems.
