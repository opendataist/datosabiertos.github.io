Hacienda entrega estimaciones para formular el Presupuesto 2024: PIB de 2,3% y cobre a US$3,86

The article highlights the crucial role of expert committees in determining Chile’s fiscal policy by independently estimating key economic indicators like non-mining GDP growth and copper prices. This process ensures that the government’s budget projections are grounded in objective, technically sound data rather than political bias. The independence of these calculations is vital for maintaining credible macroeconomic frameworks. By relying on established institutional mechanisms that have operated for two decades, the state guarantees transparency and technical rigor in forecasting structural revenues and expenditure limits. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, independent technical assessments underpin public governance. Openly accessible, reliable data generated by external experts allows citizens and institutions to verify fiscal health, fostering trust in government accountability and the integrity of public financial management.
