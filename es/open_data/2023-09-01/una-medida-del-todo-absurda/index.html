Una medida del todo absurda

The ongoing flexibility in the Mexican Liga MX transfer window creates significant instability, undermining the integrity of team rosters and disrespecting players, coaches, and fans. This lack of clear regulation complicates the understanding of each team's final composition, leading to constant uncertainty regarding who is actually available to play during the tournament. The situation reveals a profound opacity in player registration and roster management, a core open data concern. The absence of official, timely updates on the league’s website prevents stakeholders from accessing accurate, real-time information about team compositions. This informational vacuum hinders transparency and trust in the organization's administrative processes. This case highlights the critical need for standardized, accessible public data in sports governance. Without reliable, open datasets regarding player movements and roster limits, accountability suffers. Improving open data practices would ensure fair competition, enhance fan engagement, and restore seriousness to the league by providing clear, verifiable information to all involved parties.
