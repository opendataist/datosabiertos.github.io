INEGI alista resultados del Censo Agropecuario 2022

The INEGI’s upcoming agricultural census represents a pivotal moment for transparency, aiming to deliver the most comprehensive historical data on Mexican farming practices. By achieving near-total coverage and making this information publicly available, the initiative establishes a robust baseline for analyzing sector evolution over the last sixteen years. Open data becomes crucial here as it empowers society and decision-makers to verify production realities, from who produces what to how resources are utilized. The availability of this verified information allows for objective comparisons of agricultural conditions, moving beyond speculation to evidence-based understanding of whether the sector is improving or deteriorating. Ultimately, this dataset serves as the foundation for designing effective public policies. By ensuring accurate, accessible statistics, stakeholders can identify real problems and track trends over time. This commitment to truth and accessibility highlights how open data directly supports better governance and informed resource allocation in the agricultural field.
