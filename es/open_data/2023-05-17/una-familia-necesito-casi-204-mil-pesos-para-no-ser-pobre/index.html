The article highlights a significant increase in the cost of basic food and total baskets, indicating a sharp rise in poverty lines across Argentina. For open data, this report exemplifies the critical role of official statistical agencies in providing transparent, quantifiable indicators of social well-being. Understanding these metrics is vital for analyzing trends in inequality and living standards.
