Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Alicia García proposes transforming Ávila’s municipal government into a transparent and innovative entity that prioritizes citizen needs over political interests. By implementing external financial audits and establishing an open government area, the strategy aims to foster social dialogue and public-private collaboration. This structural shift seeks to rebuild trust by ensuring the administration is accessible, organized, and actively responsive to the community’s demands through continuous accountability mechanisms. Central to this vision are two major initiatives: the creation of 'Ávila abierta' as an innovation lab and 'Ávila Data' to position the city as a benchmark in open data. These projects facilitate knowledge generation by involving employees, the private sector, and residents in improving public services. The focus is on leveraging technology and cooperative frameworks to enhance service delivery, ensuring that relevant municipal projects, including budgets, are developed through participatory processes that incorporate citizen feedback and agreement. This proposal is highly relevant to open data as it explicitly commits to upgrading the municipality’s transparency portal into an accessible, permeable open data hub. By establishing a bidirectional citizen line for reporting and resolving issues, the plan demonstrates a practical application of open government principles. It moves beyond mere information disclosure to active civic engagement, showing how open data infrastructure can drive democratic participation, improve administrative efficiency, and strengthen the relationship between local institutions and their constituents through shared responsibility and transparency.
