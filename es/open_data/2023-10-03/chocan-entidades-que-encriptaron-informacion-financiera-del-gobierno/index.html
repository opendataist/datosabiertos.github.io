Chocan entidades que encriptaron información financiera del Gobierno

The Colombian government faces a conflict between transparency mandates and data protection orders. While law requires high officials' tax declarations to be public, regulators recently anonymized them, creating legal and ethical tensions. This dispute highlights the critical balance between open data principles and privacy regulations. It raises questions about how public information should be handled to ensure both accountability and individual rights are respected. The situation is relevant to open data because it illustrates the challenges in implementing transparent governance. It emphasizes the need for clear guidelines on when and how to release sensitive government information without compromising security or privacy.
