Censo de Población, Hogares y Viviendas culminó el relevamiento a nivel nacional

Uruguay has successfully completed the 2023 census, marking a significant milestone in national data collection. By prioritizing digital participation, the country achieved the highest web incidence rate in Latin America, demonstrating a strong commitment to modernizing statistical methods and enhancing data accessibility for the public. This achievement underscores the critical importance of open data in fostering transparent governance and efficient resource allocation. By making comprehensive demographic and housing information available, authorities can better plan public services and policies, ensuring that decisions are grounded in accurate, up-to-date evidence rather than assumptions. The subsequent analysis phase will transform raw field data into actionable insights, further promoting an open data ecosystem. This process highlights how robust statistical infrastructure supports societal understanding and drives informed decision-making, serving as a model for how governments can leverage census data to improve public welfare and administrative efficiency across the region.
