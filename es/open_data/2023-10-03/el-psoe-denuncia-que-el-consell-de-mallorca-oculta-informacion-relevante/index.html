El PSOE denuncia que el Consell de Mallorca «oculta información relevante»

The inoperative Transparency Portal on Mallorca infringes citizens' right to information, demanding immediate restoration. This outage represents a critical failure in administrative accountability, as it obscures essential government data from public scrutiny. This situation highlights the urgent need for functional digital access to state information, a core principle of open data. Ensuring online portals remain active is vital for maintaining transparency and allowing civic oversight of public administration. Furthermore, the incident underscores the necessity of independent oversight mechanisms to enforce ethical standards. Restoring access and reviewing potential violations ensures that institutions remain answerable to the public, reinforcing the integrity of open governance frameworks.
