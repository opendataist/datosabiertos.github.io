Gobierno municipal de Acatlán de Pérez Figueroa viola ley de transparencia - Plumas Libres

Acatlán’s local government ignored a legal transparency request regarding municipal staff numbers. This failure to respond within the mandated timeframe violates national transparency laws and disregards citizens’ right to equal access to public information. The incident highlights a critical gap between legislative guarantees and actual administrative compliance. It raises serious concerns about the municipality's commitment to accountability and suggests systemic issues in enforcing transparency standards at the local level. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how legal frameworks alone are insufficient without active adherence. It underscores the need for robust mechanisms to ensure that promised transparency translates into accessible, usable public data rather than remaining theoretical.
