Censo: el INE alcanza el 70% de actualización cartográfica nacional

The INE reports reaching 70% national cartographic update, signaling steady progress toward the census completion. This milestone highlights the effectiveness of their near real-time monitoring system, ensuring data accuracy and operational transparency. The achievement underscores the reliability of open government initiatives, as precise geographic data is essential for public accountability. By making these updates visible, the institution fosters trust and enables citizens to verify administrative progress. Consequently, this data serves as a critical foundation for open data ecosystems, allowing researchers and developers to build applications that enhance civic engagement and improve service delivery based on verified, up-to-date spatial information.
