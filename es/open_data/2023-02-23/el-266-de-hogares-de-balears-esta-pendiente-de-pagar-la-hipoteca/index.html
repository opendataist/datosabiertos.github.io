El 26,6 % de hogares de Balears está pendiente de pagar la hipoteca

The article highlights significant regional disparities in Spain’s housing market, revealing how historical and social factors shape homeownership. Northern regions like the Basque Country and Galicia show high rates of fully paid homes, often through inheritance, while southern and island territories rely more on mortgages or rentals. This geographic divide underscores structural inequalities in access to secure housing across the country. These patterns are crucial for open data initiatives because they demonstrate how public statistics can expose socio-economic gaps. By making such data freely available, governments and researchers can foster transparency, support evidence-based policymaking, and promote equitable urban planning. Understanding where people own, rent, or inherit their homes helps identify areas in need of intervention. Open access to detailed housing statistics empowers communities to advocate for better services and enables scholars to study long-term trends in housing affordability and regional development.
