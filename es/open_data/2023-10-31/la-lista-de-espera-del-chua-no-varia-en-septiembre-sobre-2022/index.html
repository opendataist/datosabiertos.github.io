La lista de espera del CHUA no varía en septiembre sobre 2022

The data reveals that waitlist dynamics at Albacete’s university hospital remain largely stable, highlighting a structural shift in patient demand rather than systemic improvement. While surgical and external consultation queues show minimal change, the notable increase in diagnostic test waitlists signals a growing bottleneck in technical services that offsets progress in clinical access. This imbalance suggests that reducing patient volume in certain areas has inadvertently increased pressure on diagnostic infrastructure. The stability in surgical lists indicates persistent challenges in managing complex interventions, particularly in neurosurgery and ophthalmology, where long-term delays continue to impact care quality and patient outcomes significantly. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency portals enable the tracking of nuanced healthcare trends over time. By making granular, localized health statistics publicly available, policymakers and researchers can identify specific inefficiencies and advocate for targeted resource allocation, ensuring that digital transparency directly supports public health decision-making and accountability.
