Junto a expertos de Cuba buscan erradicar el analfabetismo en la capital de Honduras

The article highlights Honduras’ collaboration with Cuba to launch the “Yo sí puedo” literacy program in Tegucigalpa, aiming to eradicate illiteracy among adults and vulnerable groups. This tripartite initiative leverages local community networks for outreach while relying on Cuban specialists for instruction. Relevant to open data, such large-scale social interventions depend on precise demographic mapping and transparent reporting to identify needs and measure success. Accessible data on participation rates and outcomes ensures accountability and allows other regions to replicate the model effectively. Ultimately, this effort underscores how open data practices are essential for optimizing public policy implementation and achieving measurable social impact.
