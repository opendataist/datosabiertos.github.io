Ejecutivo Estatal fortalece acciones de Gobierno Abierto | Revista Punto de Vista

The government of San Luis Potosí has launched the second session of its Open Government Committee, highlighting significant progress in the State Information System. This initiative aims to strengthen transparency and citizen access to information by integrating diverse data sources, including census results, poverty metrics, and public investment records. A central focus is the enhancement of the citizen budget platform, which now features dynamic interaction modules and detailed financial data across state and municipal levels. By sectorizing information on education, health, and the economy, the administration seeks to provide clearer insights into public spending and improve accountability. This development is relevant to open data as it demonstrates a concrete commitment to digital governance and proactive transparency. By making complex sociodemographic and fiscal data accessible, it empowers citizens to engage with public policy. Ultimately, it fosters trust through improved visibility of government actions and resource allocation.
