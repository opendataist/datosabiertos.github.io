Instaló Ichitaip red local de derecho a acceso a la información

A university in Chihuahua joined a local network dedicated to implementing the PlanDAI, aiming to coordinate actions for the right of access to information. This collaborative effort highlights the vital role of autonomous bodies in strengthening democracy and fostering transparency. By uniting academic, media, and civil society stakeholders, the initiative seeks to create effective communication channels for deliberating on crucial public issues. The partnership specifically focuses on water quality and availability, leveraging institutional support to investigate environmental challenges. Stakeholders emphasized that transparency is essential for understanding and addressing these local problems. The engagement demonstrates how multi-sector alliances can drive accountability, ensuring that public administration decisions are open to scrutiny and public oversight. This event is highly relevant to open data because it reinforces the principle that citizen trust depends on accessible, verifiable information. By prioritizing transparency in critical areas like water resources, the project illustrates how open data principles empower civil society. Such initiatives prove that sharing data is not just a legal requirement but a fundamental tool for democratic participation and informed decision-making.
