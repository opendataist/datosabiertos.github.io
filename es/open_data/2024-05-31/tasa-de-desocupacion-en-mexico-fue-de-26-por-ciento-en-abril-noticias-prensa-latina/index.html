Tasa de desocupación en México fue de 2,6 por ciento en abril - Noticias Prensa Latina

Unemployment fell, reflecting improved labor market conditions. This positive trend underscores the necessity of robust open data for accurate economic monitoring and informed policy decisions. The rise in the economically active population and overall employment indicates growth. Transparent access to these labor statistics allows for better analysis of workforce dynamics and structural shifts. Although underemployment increased slightly, the overall trajectory remains favorable. Reliable open datasets are crucial for tracking these nuances, ensuring stakeholders understand the real impact of employment policies.
