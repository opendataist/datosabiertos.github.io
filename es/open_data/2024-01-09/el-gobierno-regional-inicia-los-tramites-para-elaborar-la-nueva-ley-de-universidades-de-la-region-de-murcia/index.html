El Gobierno regional inicia los trámites para elaborar la nueva Ley de Universidades de la Región de Murcia

The Regional Government of Murcia is initiating the process to draft a new University Law, aiming to adapt to the recent national LOSU legislation while addressing local needs. Unlike the central government, which developed the national law without university consultation, the regional executive emphasizes a participatory approach. This strategy seeks to ensure the new legal framework genuinely reflects the demands of the academic community, correcting the perceived exclusionary process of the state-level decree. A key element of this legislative update is the commitment to extensive public consultation before finalizing the draft. By inviting feedback from representative organizations, student associations, and university bodies, the administration intends to create a law that fosters stability and dialogue. This inclusive methodology contrasts sharply with the improvisation observed in the implementation of certain LOSU measures, such as social security contributions for internships, which have caused significant confusion and financial uncertainty for institutions previously ignored by the central government. This development is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical importance of transparency and stakeholder engagement in shaping public policy. The region’s pledge to publish the draft on its transparency portal and actively seek input from affected groups exemplifies how open governance mechanisms can improve legislative quality. Furthermore, the emphasis on providing clear, communicated guidelines addresses the information gaps and administrative opacity that often hinder effective data usage and accountability in educational sectors.
