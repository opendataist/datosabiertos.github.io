“La propaganda del INE está fallando”: exdirectora observa tres deficiencias en el proceso del Censo

The article highlights critical delays in Peru’s census preparation, noting insufficient volunteer recruitment and a lack of mobilization committees. It emphasizes the urgency of intensive training due to complex forms and calls for intensified work hours near the March 2023 deadline. This is relevant to open data as census reliability depends on precise execution; poor preparation risks compromising data integrity, transparency, and the utility of subsequent statistical releases for public accountability.
