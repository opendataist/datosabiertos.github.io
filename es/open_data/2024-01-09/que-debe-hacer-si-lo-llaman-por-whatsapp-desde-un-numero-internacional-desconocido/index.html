¿Qué debe hacer si lo llaman por WhatsApp desde un número internacional desconocido?

WhatsApp has become a prime target for cybercriminals exploiting its popularity to conduct fraud via calls and messages from unknown international numbers. These bad actors use spoofed or acquired international lines to mask their true locations, making them harder to trace and identify. This tactic allows scammers to operate with relative impunity, turning a widely trusted communication tool into a vector for malicious activity and data theft. The primary threat involves extracting personal data through deceptive offers, such as promises of easy money, loans, or investment returns. Victims are lured by clickable links that may compromise their security or lead to financial loss. Users are advised to ignore these interactions, as engaging confirms their contact information is active and invites further harassment. The recommended response is to neither answer nor reply, but rather to report and block the suspicious sender immediately. This content is crucial for open data initiatives as it highlights the real-world risks associated with platform data integrity and user privacy. Understanding these manipulation tactics informs better data governance, helping researchers and developers create more robust security standards. By analyzing the patterns of these fraud attempts, the open data community can contribute to global efforts in detecting and preventing digital exploitation, ensuring safer digital ecosystems for all users.
