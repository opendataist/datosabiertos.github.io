ES EN

Galicia faces a shifting demographic reality where direct emigration declines, yet the total population abroad grows due to local-born descendants. This trend reveals a complex migration pattern rather than simple depopulation. The government views this diaspora as a vital ally against Spain’s demographic crisis. By leveraging these international ties, Galicia aims to mitigate internal population challenges through sustained engagement. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives, as it highlights the need to track generational migration flows. Accurate statistics enable better policy design, showing how diaspora networks can effectively support regional development and return strategies.
