Cochabamba: INE comienza el censo con el registro de efectivos policiales

The National Institute of Statistics is conducting the national census, starting with police and military personnel to ensure they can later secure the general census day. This approach highlights the critical role of state coordination in gathering reliable demographic data. Such institutional collaboration is vital for open data ecosystems, as it ensures the completeness and accessibility of foundational public information. By securing the environment for accurate data collection, authorities support transparent governance. Ultimately, high-quality census data enables better policy-making and public accountability. This example demonstrates how integrating security forces into data processes enhances the integrity of national statistics, reinforcing trust in open government initiatives and their underlying data structures.
