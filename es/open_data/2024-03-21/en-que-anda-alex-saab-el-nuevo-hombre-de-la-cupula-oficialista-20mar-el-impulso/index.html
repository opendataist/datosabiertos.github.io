▷ ¿En qué anda Alex Saab? el nuevo hombre de la cúpula oficialista #20Mar - El Impulso

The article highlights the political and economic controversy surrounding Alex Saab’s return to Venezuela, emphasizing how the government transformed an illicit operator into an official state actor. This shift underscores a broader trend where opaque dealings are legitimized through public institutions, eroding the separation between private illicit networks and state administration. The core issue lies in the complete lack of transparency regarding international investments. By assigning Saab to lead a new investment center, the government bypasses standard regulatory oversight and public scrutiny. The absence of verifiable data on projects and funding illustrates a systemic rejection of open data principles, making it impossible for citizens or independent analysts to verify economic claims or hold officials accountable. This situation is critical for open data advocates because it exemplifies how authoritarian regimes weaponize secrecy to maintain power. Without accessible, verifiable information on state contracts and foreign relations, civil society cannot monitor corruption or economic policy effectiveness. The article demonstrates that restricting data access is not merely an administrative choice but a deliberate strategy to conceal governance failures and illicit financial flows.
