Los 10 errores que están poniendo en riesgo sus datos personales en internet

This article highlights that negligence in digital habits poses severe risks to personal data security. It identifies common errors such as using weak passwords, ignoring two-factor authentication, and connecting to unsecured public Wi-Fi as primary vulnerabilities. These oversights expose individuals to immediate threats like identity theft and financial fraud, demonstrating how easily unprotected information can be compromised by malicious actors. The narrative emphasizes that privacy breaches extend beyond direct hacking, often involving data collection by companies and social media oversharing. By neglecting to review app permissions or privacy policies, users inadvertently allow third parties to exploit their behavioral and personal data. This lack of awareness facilitates manipulation, targeted advertising, and even discrimination, revealing that passive acceptance of digital terms significantly erodes individual autonomy and control over one’s digital footprint. This content is highly relevant to open data discussions because it underscores the critical importance of data protection principles when information is accessible online. Open data initiatives rely on transparency, but this article illustrates the dangerous intersection where public accessibility meets personal vulnerability. It advocates for robust security hygiene and privacy literacy, reminding users that without strict safeguards, the openness of digital ecosystems can lead to severe personal and societal harms, including identity theft and surveillance.
