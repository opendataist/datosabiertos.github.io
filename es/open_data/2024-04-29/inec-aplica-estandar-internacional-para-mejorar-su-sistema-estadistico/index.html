INEC aplica estándar internacional para mejorar su sistema estadístico

The National Institute of Statistics and Census has adopted the international DDI standard to significantly enhance the documentation, dissemination, and usability of statistical data. By implementing this framework, the institution aims to create comprehensive catalogs of microdata that allow diverse users, including researchers and businesses, to easily understand and interpret information. This transition represents a major step toward modernizing statistical production, ensuring that data is not only accessible but also clearly contextualized and credible for public use. A primary implication of this adoption is the alignment of national data practices with global interoperability standards, which facilitates international cooperation and enhances the credibility of the country’s statistics. The initiative supports the management of emerging technologies like artificial intelligence and big data, ensuring that the statistical system remains robust and relevant in a rapidly changing digital landscape. Furthermore, utilizing free, internationally recognized software reduces financial barriers, allowing for sustained investment in data quality and user engagement without imposing new costs on the state. This effort is particularly relevant to the open data community because it prioritizes the structural integrity and transparency of public information. By focusing on the standardization of metadata and the creation of interactive tools, the institute ensures that data is machine-readable and reusable, which are core principles of open data ecosystems. This move not only improves internal processes but also empowers external actors to leverage high-quality data for innovation and evidence-based decision-making, setting a benchmark for transparency in the region.
