ATA destaca el crecimiento de C-LM en 126 autónomos según los datos de empleo del mes de febrero

Spain experienced a slight annual decline in self-employed registrations in February, contrasting with growth in most autonomous communities. Castilla-La Mancha aims to reverse this trend through effective government measures, while women significantly drove new registrations nationwide, highlighting gender disparities in entrepreneurial growth across regions. Sectoral analysis reveals notable losses in agriculture, hospitality, commerce, and finance, suggesting structural challenges in traditional industries. These specific declines indicate shifting labor market dynamics where certain sectors struggle to retain new self-employed workers despite overall policy efforts to stimulate entrepreneurship and formalization. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it enables granular analysis of labor market trends, policy effectiveness, and sectoral health. Accessible statistics support evidence-based decision-making for policymakers, researchers, and citizens monitoring economic resilience and the evolving landscape of self-employment in Spain.
