El espectacular aumento del número de desahucios en el primer semestre del año en Asturias

Asturias experienced a dramatic surge in evictions during the first half of the year, marking a stark reversal from the previous year’s decline. This sharp increase highlights emerging vulnerabilities in regional housing markets, signaling a return to higher instability for tenants following a period of relative improvement. The data reveals that unpaid rent is the primary driver behind this trend, accounting for the vast majority of cases. This indicates that financial pressures on households are intensifying, creating a urgent need for effective social housing policies and rental protection measures to prevent widespread displacement. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical value of transparent, granular judicial statistics. By making eviction metrics publicly accessible, policymakers and researchers can identify regional disparities and trends, enabling data-driven interventions to address housing insecurity and improve societal outcomes.
