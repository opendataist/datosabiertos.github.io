Seis de cada diez españoles están reduciendo su gasto en productos no esenciales

The report highlights how high inflation forces Spanish consumers to substitute brands and cut spending, causing widespread anxiety over rising energy and food costs. Essential items remain stable, but financial strain is evident as many struggle to maintain their previous consumption levels. This underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible open data on price dynamics. Such data empowers citizens to navigate economic uncertainty and allows policymakers to monitor real-time impacts on household welfare, fostering informed public discourse.
