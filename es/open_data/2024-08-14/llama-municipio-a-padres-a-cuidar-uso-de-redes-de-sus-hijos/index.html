Llama Municipio a padres a cuidar uso de redes de sus hijos

The article highlights a municipal initiative prioritizing personal data protection and citizen safety, particularly for minors facing online fraud. It urges parents to configure privacy settings, teach secure practices, and maintain open dialogue to foster a safe digital environment for adolescents. This aligns with open data principles by emphasizing transparency in safeguarding information rights. Providing direct contact channels for data protection queries further demonstrates the municipality’s commitment to accessible and accountable governance regarding privacy.
