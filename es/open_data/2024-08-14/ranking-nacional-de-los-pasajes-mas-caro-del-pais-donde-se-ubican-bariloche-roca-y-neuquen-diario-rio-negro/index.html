Ranking nacional de los pasajes más caro del país: dónde se ubican Bariloche, Roca y Neuquén - Diario Río Negro

The article highlights discrepancies in a recent public ranking of bus fares compiled by a transportation association, specifically noting inaccuracies in the reported costs for cities like Roca. This reveals that data released by industry groups may not always reflect current, real-world pricing, as seen in the divergence between claimed and actual ticket rates. These inconsistencies matter significantly for open data advocacy because they demonstrate the necessity of verifying published datasets against ground truth. When public or industry-led data lacks accuracy, it undermines trust in information transparency and hinders reliable analysis for policymakers and citizens seeking to understand economic impacts, such as those following subsidy cuts. Ultimately, the case underscores that simply making data available is insufficient if the underlying information is flawed. Ensuring the integrity of open datasets requires rigorous validation processes, especially in sectors like public transport where pricing directly affects social equity and accessibility, reminding stakeholders that data quality is just as critical as data availability.
