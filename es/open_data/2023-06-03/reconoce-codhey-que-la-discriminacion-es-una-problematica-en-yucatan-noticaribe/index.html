Reconoce Codhey que la discriminación es una problemática en Yucatán - Noticaribe

Yucatán leads national statistics in discrimination, with nearly a third of adults reporting victimization. This persistent social problem has remained largely unchanged over nine years, highlighting a critical need for immediate intervention by local authorities to promote a culture of respect and address systemic inequality. The data reveals that indigenous individuals and members of the sexual diversity community are the most vulnerable groups. Discrimination extends beyond ethnicity to include prejudice based on appearance, social class, religion, and origin, indicating deep-rooted societal biases that require comprehensive educational and institutional reforms to mitigate. This report is vital for open data as it demonstrates how public statistical transparency can expose hidden social injustices. By making such findings accessible, citizens and policymakers can utilize empirical evidence to drive accountability and design targeted strategies for human rights protection, ensuring that marginalized voices are recognized and supported.
