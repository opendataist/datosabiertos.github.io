La economía de la región Puno se recupera y destaca por su ejecución presupuestal

Regional governments in Peru are increasingly publishing budget execution data online, highlighting how open data exposes performance disparities. This transparency allows citizens to track fiscal efficiency and hold authorities accountable for spending delays. Such accessible information empowers public oversight, ensuring that resources are managed effectively despite political instability.
