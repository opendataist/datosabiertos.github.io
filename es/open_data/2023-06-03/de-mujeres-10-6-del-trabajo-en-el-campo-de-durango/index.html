De mujeres, 10.6% del trabajo en el campo de Durango

The 2022 agricultural census reveals distinct regional disparities in female workforce participation, with Durango showing a slight increase compared to a national decrease from previous decades. This highlights the critical importance of granular, disaggregated data in understanding local economic dynamics rather than relying solely on aggregated national trends. These findings underscore the necessity of open data initiatives that make census information accessible and detailed by municipality and production type. Such transparency allows for more accurate social analysis and policy-making, ensuring that specific demographic contributions are recognized and addressed effectively. Ultimately, the census serves as a comprehensive source for economic and social characteristics of agricultural units. Its availability promotes accountability and deeper insights into gender roles within rural sectors, reinforcing the value of open datasets for fostering informed public discourse and targeted development strategies.
