Los trabajadores ocupados en el sector turístico suben un 2,3% respecto a hace un año y superan los 2,6 millones

The Spanish tourism sector demonstrated significant growth and structural improvement during the final quarter of 2023, marking a successful period for the industry. Employment figures rose substantially, with total workers exceeding three million, indicating a robust recovery and expansion of the labor market. This increase reflects not just higher volume but a qualitative shift towards more stable and secure working conditions within the sector. A crucial development is the steady rise in permanent contracts and the concurrent decline in temporary employment, highlighting a maturation of the industry. The reduction in the temporary labor rate and the increase in full-time salaried positions suggest a move away from precarious work arrangements. This transition signifies a healthier labor model that offers better job security, which is essential for the long-term sustainability and social responsibility of the tourism economy. This report is highly relevant to open data because it showcases how transparent, granular labor statistics can track complex socioeconomic trends. By analyzing disaggregated data across regions and contract types, stakeholders can identify patterns of inequality or growth, such as the gender gap in unemployment or regional disparities in job creation. This accessibility empowers policymakers and researchers to evaluate the effectiveness of labor policies and understand the true impact of tourism on social equity and regional development.
