INE y Gobernación de Oruro unen fuerzas para el Censo 2024

The Government of Oruro formally commits to supporting the National Institute of Statistics (INE) for the 2024 Census. This agreement ensures the provision of physical spaces, vehicles, and personnel, establishing a legal and technical framework for cooperation. Such logistical backing is essential for efficient data collection across the region. A key outcome involves incorporating specific questions regarding rural-to-urban migration and inter-departmental movements into the census questionnaire. These additions allow for targeted policies benefiting rural areas and specific departments. The inclusion of these topics reflects a strategic effort to address demographic shifts through empirical evidence rather than assumptions. This article highlights the critical role of open data in governance. By generating comprehensive demographic insights, the census enables evidence-based public policy. It demonstrates how accessible, high-quality statistical data empowers local authorities to design interventions that genuinely improve citizens' quality of life and service delivery.
