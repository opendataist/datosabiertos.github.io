La nueva plataforma de streaming que busca desplazar a Netflix

Kodi offers a free, open-source alternative to expensive streaming services, allowing users to centralize and view local or streamed content across multiple devices. Its open-code nature fosters community-driven extensions and ensures legal flexibility, challenging proprietary subscription models. This matters to open_data as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible software empowers users to control their media consumption without vendor lock-in, promoting digital autonomy and data portability.
