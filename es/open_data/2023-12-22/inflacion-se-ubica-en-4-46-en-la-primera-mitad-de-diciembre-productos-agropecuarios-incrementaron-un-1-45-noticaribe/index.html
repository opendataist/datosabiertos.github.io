Inflación se ubica en 4.46% en la primera mitad de diciembre; productos agropecuarios incrementaron un 1.45% - Noticaribe

The article highlights Mexico’s continued inflationary trend, with annual rates rising in December. This shift underscores the importance of accurate, timely public data in tracking economic volatility and guiding policy responses to persistent price increases. It also demonstrates how official statistical agencies manage data integrity during crises. By adjusting for supply disruptions caused by natural disasters, the INEGI ensures transparency, a core principle of open data that maintains trust in public statistics even under emergency conditions. This case is relevant to open data because it shows how accessible, real-time inflation metrics empower citizens and researchers to understand economic impacts. Transparent reporting of both standard trends and exceptional adjustments fosters accountability and enables better-informed societal decisions regarding cost of living and monetary policy.
