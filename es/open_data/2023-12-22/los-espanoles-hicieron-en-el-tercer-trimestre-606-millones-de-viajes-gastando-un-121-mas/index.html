Los españoles hicieron en el tercer trimestre 60,6 millones de viajes gastando un 12,1% más

The latest tourism data reveals a significant recovery in travel activity, with both the volume of trips and total expenditure rising substantially compared to the previous year. This surge indicates a robust restoration of consumer confidence and mobility, highlighting the dynamic nature of current travel behaviors across domestic and international markets. Domestic travel remains the predominant trend, with the vast majority of trips staying within national borders and often utilizing accommodation with family or friends. While international trips also grew, they constitute a smaller fraction of the total. The primary driver for this movement is leisure, particularly summer vacations, although business travel showed notable growth, suggesting a broadening of travel purposes beyond simple tourism. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the value of publicly accessible, high-frequency statistical indicators for analyzing economic trends. By providing granular details on spending patterns, accommodation types, and transport modes, such datasets enable researchers and policymakers to model sector resilience, understand shifting consumer preferences, and make informed decisions based on transparent, standardized metrics.
