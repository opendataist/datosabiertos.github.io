El oro llega su tercer mayor pico de 2024, la onza troy cotiza en $us 2.398

Gold prices reached unprecedented levels in July 2024, reflecting a significant global trend of increased metal valuation compared to the previous year. This surge marks a continuation of a long-term upward trajectory in international commodity markets, establishing new financial benchmarks for precious metals. Conversely, Bolivian gold exports experienced a substantial decline in monetary value despite these higher unit prices. The nation saw a dramatic drop in total export revenues during the first half of the year, indicating a divergence between global price trends and local production or sales volumes. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it highlights the importance of accessible, transparent government statistics for analyzing economic trends. By making such detailed commodity records publicly available, institutions like the INE enable researchers and citizens to understand the complex relationship between global market forces and domestic economic performance.
