Las tendencias emergentes en el sector de la hospitalidad

The study reveals that travelers increasingly prioritize deep integration with their destination, valuing authentic local culture over isolated hotel amenities. This shift indicates that the community and surrounding context are decisive factors in booking decisions, driving hotels to collaborate with locals for genuine immersive experiences rather than offering generic in-house events. A key implication is the rise of hyper-localized and regenerative practices. Hotels are adopting designs and services that support environmental sustainability and community well-being, while leveraging technology and artificial intelligence to personalize interactions. This combination allows guests to feel like residents of the place, engaging in activities that strengthen local tourism networks and foster a genuine connection with the destination’s unique identity. This article is relevant to open data because understanding these nuanced consumer preferences requires analyzing diverse, often unstructured datasets regarding travel behavior, local partnerships, and environmental impact. Open data initiatives can aggregate insights from community interactions and sustainability metrics, helping the industry make evidence-based decisions that align with the growing demand for transparency, authenticity, and responsible tourism.
