Jubilaciones y pensiones aportaron el 31% de la reducción total del gasto público

The article analyzes the distribution of reduced public spending, highlighting that pensions absorbed the largest share of the cuts, followed by investment and provincial transfers. This data is relevant to open data as it underscores the critical need for transparent, granular fiscal datasets to verify government claims and ensure accountability in public expenditure. Such information allows citizens and analysts to monitor the impact of austerity measures on essential services and social welfare, fostering greater oversight.
