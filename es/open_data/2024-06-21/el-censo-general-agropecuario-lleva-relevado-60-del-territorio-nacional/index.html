El Censo General Agropecuario lleva relevado 60% del territorio nacional

The Uruguayan government emphasizes that completing the agricultural census with broad territorial coverage is essential for strengthening institutional capacity and designing effective public policies. Authorities warn that insufficient participation increases statistical margins of error, thereby compromising the reliability of data used for strategic planning by both the public sector and private industry. The census serves as a critical tool to characterize agricultural exploitation, identifying owners, workers, and production types without collecting sensitive financial data. By offering accessible methods like online self-reporting and phone lines, the initiative aims to gather comprehensive information that supports decision-making and facilitates services tailored to the agricultural community. This initiative is highly relevant to open_data as it generates foundational, standardized statistics necessary for transparency and informed governance. Accurate, publicly available agricultural metrics empower stakeholders to analyze trends, evaluate policy impacts, and foster accountability, demonstrating how robust data collection directly enhances societal understanding and economic development.
