The data reveals a significant improvement in Mexico’s labor market, with employment reaching high levels and unemployment dropping notably. This trend highlights substantial gains in job availability and stability for the workforce. Simultaneously, underemployment decreased, indicating that more workers have access to sufficient hours. Concurrently, real salaries showed annual growth, particularly in specific regions, reflecting improved economic conditions for employees. This information is vital for open data because it provides transparent, high-quality statistics on labor metrics. Such accessible data allows researchers and citizens to analyze economic health, fostering informed public debate and accountability regarding social policies and labor rights.
