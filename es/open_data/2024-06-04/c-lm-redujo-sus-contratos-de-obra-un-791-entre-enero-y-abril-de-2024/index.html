C-LM redujo sus contratos de obra un 79,1% entre enero y abril de 2024

The significant rise in public works contracting highlights how major infrastructure managers and local governments are driving market activity, while several autonomous regions are sharply reducing their procurement. This divergence indicates a centralization of investment in specific federal departments and municipalities, contrasting with declines in regional spending and specific sectors like airports and ports. Open data is crucial here because transparent access to these contracting figures allows analysts to verify the distribution of public funds and identify regional inequalities. By monitoring these trends, stakeholders can assess government efficiency and ensure that infrastructure investments align with national priorities rather than being unevenly distributed across different territories.
