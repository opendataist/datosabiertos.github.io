Una fuente de datos abierta revela el estado de financiación de proyectos climáticos - Mundo - ABC Color

The article reveals that global financing for indigenous land rights remains insufficiently directed toward local communities, who mostly receive indirect support through NGOs rather than direct aid. This disparity highlights a critical governance gap in forest management funding, where major donors like the World Bank operate through multilateral channels. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives because it creates transparent, accessible financial records from the Rights and Resources Initiative and the Rainforest Foundation Norway. By making these figures publicly available, stakeholders can easily identify funding gaps. Consequently, this transparency empowers communities to advocate for equitable resource distribution. It demonstrates how open data drives accountability and supports the conservation of vital landscapes by ensuring that financial flows align with the actual needs of those protecting them.
