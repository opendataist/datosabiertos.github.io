La Nación / Cantidad de denuncias de violencia familiar aumenta año tras año

The Ministry Public’s open data platform reveals a startling surge in family violence complaints, with daily victim counts rising significantly in the current year compared to recent historical averages. This transparent reporting highlights that reported cases have more than tripled since 2015, indicating a critical escalation in societal harm that demands immediate institutional and public attention. Geographic analysis within the dataset shows a heavy concentration of incidents in central departments, particularly Central and Asunción, while rural areas report fewer cases. This spatial distribution suggests that urban centers face disproportionate pressures, likely due to higher population density and reporting accessibility, necessitating targeted regional interventions rather than uniform national strategies. This article underscores the vital role of open data in exposing hidden social crises. By making granular, real-time statistics publicly accessible, citizens and policymakers can track trends, allocate resources effectively, and hold authorities accountable. Ultimately, transparency transforms raw numbers into actionable intelligence, empowering society to confront domestic violence with evidence-based solutions rather than anecdotal assumptions.
