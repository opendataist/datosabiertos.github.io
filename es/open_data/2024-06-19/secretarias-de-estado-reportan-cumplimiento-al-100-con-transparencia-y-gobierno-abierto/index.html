Secretarías de Estado reportan cumplimiento al 100% con transparencia y gobierno abierto

Mexican state secretaries have achieved full compliance with open government and transparency mandates, as confirmed by the Secretariat of Public Administration. This achievement underscores a systemic commitment to institutional accountability, reinforcing the rule of law and establishing a robust framework for public oversight across federal entities. The coordination mechanism highlights critical areas for improvement while celebrating the dedication of public servants in safeguarding fundamental human rights, such as information access and data protection. By emphasizing key international dates, the government aims to deepen the cultural appreciation of transparency and anti-corruption efforts among civil servants. This development is vital for open data, as it validates the reliability of the underlying information ecosystem. Ensuring rigorous adherence to transparency obligations guarantees that the data published by these agencies is accurate and accessible, thereby empowering citizens and fostering trust in democratic institutions through verified, high-quality public information.

Source: eluniversal.com.mx
