Israel arrestó a 640 menores palestinos en Cisjordania desde octubre - Noticias Prensa Latina

Reports highlight severe systematic abuse, including torture and medical neglect, inflicted upon Palestinian detainees within Israeli prisons, with conditions notably worsening since the conflict in Gaza escalated. These accounts underscore a critical disregard for human rights and international legal standards regarding the treatment of prisoners, affecting thousands including minors and women. The relevance to open data lies in the urgent need for transparent, verifiable, and accessible information regarding human rights violations. Reliable data collection and publication are essential for holding authorities accountable, enabling civil society to monitor abuses, advocate for justice, and ensure that empirical evidence of these allegations remains preserved and auditable for the global community.
