La Asamblea Regional debatirá y votará, la próxima semana, la modificación de la Ley de Transparencia - murcia.com

The Regional Assembly of Murcia is set to debate and vote on a legislative proposal to modify the existing transparency and citizen participation law. This procedural step highlights the region’s ongoing effort to adapt its legal framework governing public information access. By reviewing amendments and finalizing the draft within the Commission of General Affairs, lawmakers aim to update mechanisms that facilitate how citizens interact with government bodies. The urgency of this debate underscores the growing demand for greater institutional accountability. Updating transparency regulations is critical for maintaining public trust, as it determines the ease with which information is disclosed and how citizen requests are processed. The legislative change seeks to modernize these processes, ensuring they remain effective tools for democratic engagement rather than outdated bureaucratic hurdles. This development is highly relevant to open data because transparency laws form the legal foundation for data publication policies. Without robust legal mandates for openness, the initiative to publish raw, machine-readable government data lacks binding enforcement. Therefore, modifying the transparency law directly impacts the viability and scope of open data ecosystems, ensuring that digital accessibility to public administration remains legally supported and practically enforced.
