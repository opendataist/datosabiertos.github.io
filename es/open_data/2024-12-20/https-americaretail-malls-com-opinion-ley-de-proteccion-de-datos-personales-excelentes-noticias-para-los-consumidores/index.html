Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The new Personal Data Protection Law empowers Chilean consumers by granting them greater control over their privacy. Companies must now be transparent about data collection and protection, making compliance a mandatory standard rather than an optional choice for engaging with the market. This legislation transforms data management into a strategic advantage. Organizations are compelled to adopt advanced analytics and automated safeguards to ensure accuracy and trust. The shift requires robust infrastructure to handle sensitive information securely while maintaining operational transparency. This is highly relevant to open data as it highlights the critical balance between data utility and individual rights. It underscores that ethical data practices and strong governance are foundational for public trust, demonstrating how regulatory frameworks shape responsible data ecosystems and influence how information is shared and utilized in society.
