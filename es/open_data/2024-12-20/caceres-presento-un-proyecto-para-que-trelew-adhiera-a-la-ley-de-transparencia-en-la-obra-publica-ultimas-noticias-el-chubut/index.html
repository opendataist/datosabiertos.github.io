Cáceres presentó un proyecto para que Trelew adhiera a la Ley de Transparencia en la Obra Pública - Últimas Noticias: El Chubut

The regulation aims to enhance transparency and accountability in public works by mandating recent, professionally certified budget declarations. This strict timeline ensures data accuracy, treating budgets as sworn statements with serious legal consequences for non-compliance. Such measures foster direct citizen participation in public bidding processes, reinforcing institutional trust. By clarifying professional responsibilities and temporal constraints, the update addresses historical gaps in budget verification. This initiative is relevant to open_data as it establishes standardized, verifiable datasets for public spending. It promotes the release of timely, reliable information, enabling citizens to monitor and evaluate government efficiency effectively.
