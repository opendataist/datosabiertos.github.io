Actividad económica de México avanza un 2,5% interanual en abril 2023

Mexico’s economy demonstrated resilience in April, expanding by 2.5% annually driven primarily by robust performance across all sectors, particularly services. This growth follows a strong first quarter and suggests a positive short-term economic trajectory, reinforcing expectations for sustained annual expansion near three percent. This data highlights the importance of high-frequency economic indicators like the IGAE for monitoring real-time market conditions. By capturing sectoral shifts immediately, such metrics allow analysts to track recovery patterns more effectively than slower annual reports, providing essential granularity for economic assessment. The relevance to open data lies in the value of accessible, timely statistical releases from institutions like INEGI. Transparent dissemination of these indicators empowers researchers and citizens to validate economic narratives, fostering informed public discourse and enabling better analysis of post-pandemic recovery trends through reliable, publicly available datasets.
