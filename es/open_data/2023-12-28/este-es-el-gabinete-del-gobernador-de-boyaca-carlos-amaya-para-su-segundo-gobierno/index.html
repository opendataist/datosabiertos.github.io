Este es el gabinete del gobernador de Boyacá, Carlos Amaya para su segundo gobierno

The inauguration of Governor Carlos Amaya in Guacamayas marks a significant shift in Boyacá’s administrative approach, emphasizing accessibility and the inclusion of remote regions in central governance decisions. By holding the ceremony in an area with poor infrastructure yet overwhelming electoral support, the administration signals a commitment to addressing regional disparities and improving connectivity, ensuring that development policies are not concentrated solely in urban centers. A cornerstone of this new government’s strategy is the creation of a Secretariat of TIC and Open Government, led by former Minister Sandra Milena Urrutía. This appointment underscores a strategic priority to leverage technology for transparency and citizen engagement. By integrating digital tools into public administration, the department aims to streamline services, reduce bureaucratic barriers, and foster a culture of accountability, positioning Boyacá as a model for modern, responsive governance in Colombia. This focus on digital transformation and institutional transparency is highly relevant to open_data initiatives. Establishing a dedicated secretariat for open government and telecommunications provides the structural foundation necessary for systematic data publication, interoperability, and citizen access to public information. It ensures that open data is not merely a technical exercise but a core component of public policy, enabling informed decision-making, enhancing civic oversight, and driving sustainable development through the effective use of public resources and information.
