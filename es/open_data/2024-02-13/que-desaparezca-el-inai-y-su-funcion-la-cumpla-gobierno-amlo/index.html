Que desaparezca el INAI y su función la cumpla gobierno: AMLO

The article highlights President López Obrador’s criticism of INAI as ineffective and corrupt, labeling it a facade that fails to address past administrative misconduct. This perspective drives a proposal to dismantle independent transparency and regulatory bodies, arguing they are useless in combating corruption. The plan involves centralizing these functions directly within government ministries and state-owned enterprises. By transferring oversight responsibilities to executive branches and legislative bodies, the administration aims to streamline operations while eliminating what it perceives as redundant or obstructive institutions. This shift is highly relevant to open_data as it threatens the independence crucial for genuine transparency. Centralizing control over information access risks undermining public trust and the autonomy required to ensure unbiased, accessible data for citizens and civil society.
