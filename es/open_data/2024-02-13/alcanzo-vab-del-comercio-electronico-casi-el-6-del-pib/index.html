Alcanzó VAB del comercio electrónico casi el 6% del PIB

Mexico’s e-commerce contributed nearly 6% to GDP in 2022, marking a significant real growth that signals economic recovery post-pandemic. While retail sales declined as people returned to physical stores, service sectors like transport and hospitality saw increased participation. This data highlights the evolving digital landscape and consumer behavior shifts. It is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how statistical institutions track emerging economic trends, providing transparent insights into digital transformation impacts.
