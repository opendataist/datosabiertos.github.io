Castilla-La Mancha recuerda que hasta el 31 de mayo se pueden recoger aportaciones para la elaboración del Plan de Atención Sociosanitaria

Castilla-La Mancha has launched a participatory consultation to gather insights from health and social professionals on coordinating sociosanitary care. This initiative aims to improve the joint management of chronic patients by aligning health and social services, forming the basis for a new regional action plan. The process seeks diverse perspectives to identify current gaps and synergies in service delivery. By involving third-sector entities and frontline staff, the government intends to design a more effective, integrated framework that enhances care quality and resource efficiency across both sectors. This effort is relevant to open_data as it relies on transparent public consultation channels to collect structured feedback. It demonstrates how governmental transparency portals can facilitate meaningful civic engagement, ensuring that data-driven policy decisions are grounded in the practical experiences of those directly involved in the healthcare and social welfare systems.
