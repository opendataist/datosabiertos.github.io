EEUU: las cámaras del Congreso aprobaron una medida para mantener el Gobierno abierto por 45 días - Monumental

The US Congress successfully averted a government shutdown by passing a temporary funding extension. This legislative action ensures the continuity of federal operations and prevents severe economic disruptions, demonstrating that bipartisan cooperation remains the prevailing force in American politics despite intense partisan friction. Critically, the approved measure excludes contentious provisions regarding aid to Ukraine and border security. By stripping these polarizing issues from the bill, leadership secured sufficient cross-party support to pass the legislation. This strategic compromise highlights the difficulty of reaching consensus on foreign policy and immigration, pushing these debates into future negotiations rather than resolving them immediately. This event is relevant to open data because government shutdowns threaten the stability and accessibility of public information systems. When agencies halt operations, the collection, processing, and dissemination of critical data often suffer, affecting transparency and public trust. Ensuring continuous funding is essential for maintaining the infrastructure that supports open data initiatives, guaranteeing that citizens have reliable access to governmental records and statistics without interruption.
