Tasa de utilización de capacidad industrial de China alcanza 73,6% en primer trimestre

China’s industrial capacity utilization slightly declined in early 2024, revealing sectoral disparities within its economy. The manufacturing and mining segments showed mixed performance, indicating structural shifts in production efficiency. This data highlights the gap between installed capacity and actual output across key industries. Such trends reflect broader economic adjustments and demand fluctuations impacting industrial productivity. For open data enthusiasts, this release exemplifies how large-scale government statistical surveys enable transparent monitoring of national economic health. Accessible datasets allow for global analysis of industrial trends, fostering accountability and informed policy discussion.
