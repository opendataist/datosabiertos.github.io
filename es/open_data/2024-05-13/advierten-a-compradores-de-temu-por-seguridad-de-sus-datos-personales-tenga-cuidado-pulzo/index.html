Advierten a compradores de Temu por seguridad de sus datos personales; tenga cuidado - Pulzo

The article warns that Temu’s business model may prioritize data extraction over retail, suggesting that the platform installs malware to harvest personal information from users. This stolen data is allegedly sold to third parties for purposes ranging from marketing to fraudulent activities like "Wangiri" scams. Consequently, the company’s profitability relies heavily on monetizing user privacy rather than merely offering low-cost goods, raising serious ethical and security concerns. This situation is crucial for open_data discussions as it highlights the critical importance of transparent data practices and user consent. It underscores how opaque privacy policies can enable malicious data collection, contradicting the principles of data openness that prioritize user rights and security. Furthermore, the incident illustrates the need for rigorous scrutiny of digital platforms, especially those with hidden or complex terms. Users must remain vigilant about security updates and privacy settings, as unchecked data harvesting threatens individual privacy and undermines trust in digital ecosystems.
