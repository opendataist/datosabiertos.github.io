Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The National Institute of Statistics releases granular census data, enabling precise demographic mapping across municipalities. This transparency allows researchers to identify population disparities and urban trends with high specificity. Such accessible, detailed public data is fundamental for open data initiatives, fostering evidence-based policy and academic analysis through granular community insights.
