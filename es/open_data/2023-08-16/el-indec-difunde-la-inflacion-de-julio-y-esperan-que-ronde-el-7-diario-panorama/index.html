El INDEC difunde la inflación de julio y esperan que ronde el 7% - Diario Panorama

The upcoming INDEC inflation data is critical for assessing Argentina’s economic trajectory following the PASO elections, where Javier Milei’s victory significantly influenced market expectations. This political shift has already triggered a sharp rise in the official exchange rate, which acts as a primary driver for domestic price increases and signals a potential return to double-digit inflation soon. Government measures, such as new taxes on imports and the "agro dollar," have struggled to contain rising costs, particularly for basic food items like meat. These policies, combined with currency devaluation, are causing prices to jump despite previous efforts to stabilize the economy, indicating that the anticipated slowdown in inflation has been effectively overturned by recent fiscal and monetary adjustments. This information is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the tension between official statistics and independent economic indicators, such as those from the IET. For researchers and citizens, accessing and comparing these diverse datasets is essential to understand the true impact of government policies and political outcomes on inflation and consumer welfare in real time.
