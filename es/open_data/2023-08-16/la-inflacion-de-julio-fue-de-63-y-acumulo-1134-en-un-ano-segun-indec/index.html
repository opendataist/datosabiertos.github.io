The recent consumer price index surge indicates severe inflationary pressure, driven notably by communications and recreation costs. This economic volatility directly impacts the purchasing power of citizens and highlights the critical need for timely, transparent public statistics. This article underscores the importance of open_data in fostering economic transparency.
