La inflación de eurozona se frena en julio al 5,3%, pero la subyacente se queda en el 5,5%

The article highlights that Eurozone inflation has decreased in July, marking the lowest rise since early 2022, while underlying inflation remains steady. Experts predict a continued moderation throughout the summer, followed by a significant drop in the fourth quarter as temporary service price pressures dissipate. This trend suggests that the European Central Bank may implement one final rate hike before data forces a policy pivot. This development is relevant to open_data because it underscores the critical role of transparent, timely statistical releases from institutions like Eurostat. Reliable open data enables independent economists and analysts to validate economic forecasts, assess regional disparities across member states, and scrutinize monetary policy decisions. Without accessible, standardized datasets, the public cannot fully evaluate the effectiveness of central banking strategies or understand the nuanced shifts in consumer price dynamics that drive financial markets. Ultimately, the convergence of official statistics and private sector analysis demonstrates how open data fosters accountability and informed debate. By making granular economic indicators publicly available, society gains the tools to challenge narratives, track long-term inflation trends, and ensure that policymakers remain answerable to evidence. This transparency is essential for maintaining trust in economic governance and allowing diverse stakeholders to contribute to the understanding of complex macroeconomic realities.
