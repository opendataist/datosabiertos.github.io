Este enero fue el más caluroso registrado hasta ahora en el mundo

Copernicus confirms that January 2025 was the hottest January ever recorded, defying the cooling influence of La Niña. This milestone highlights the relentless momentum of global warming, with temperatures significantly exceeding pre-industrial levels and marking a continuation of unprecedented heat trends observed over recent months. The data reveals distinct regional disparities, with Southern and Eastern Europe experiencing exceptional warmth while other areas faced colder conditions or severe drought. These inconsistencies underscore the complex and localized impacts of climate change, illustrating how global averages mask severe regional extremes that affect weather patterns and environmental stability worldwide. This report is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the critical role of transparent, accessible climate monitoring in verifying scientific consensus. By publicly sharing such comprehensive meteorological datasets, organizations like Copernicus enable researchers and citizens to track environmental shifts, fostering accountability and informed decision-making regarding the urgent climate crisis.
