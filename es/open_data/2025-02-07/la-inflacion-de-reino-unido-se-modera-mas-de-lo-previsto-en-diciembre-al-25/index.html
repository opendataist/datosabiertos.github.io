La inflación de Reino Unido se modera más de lo previsto en diciembre, al 2,5%

The United Kingdom’s recent inflation data reveals a significant cooling trend, with annual rates dropping to 2.5% and underlying inflation slowing to 3.2%. These figures fall below market expectations, signaling that price pressures are easing more rapidly than anticipated. This moderation is crucial for policymakers, as it validates the trajectory toward the central bank’s 2% target, although persistent service sector prices indicate some remaining stickiness in the economy. Consequently, these results are expected to facilitate a near-certain interest rate cut by the Bank of England in February. Markets anticipate this move will stabilize government bond yields, although underlying concerns about the UK’s fiscal fragility suggest that market confidence may remain tentative despite the immediate positive sentiment.
