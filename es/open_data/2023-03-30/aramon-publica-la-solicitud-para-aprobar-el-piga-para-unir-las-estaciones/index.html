Aramón publica la solicitud para aprobar el Piga para unir las estaciones

Aramón has published project documentation for linking Formigal-Panticosa and Astún ski resorts via a cable car on its transparency portal. This move demonstrates corporate commitment to open data by making critical environmental and territorial planning files publicly accessible for scrutiny. The proposal aims to boost the regional economy and generate jobs, highlighting how private infrastructure decisions impact public resources. Publishing these documents allows stakeholders to assess the project's validity and aligns with transparency standards in public-interest developments. This relevance to open data lies in the proactive disclosure of administrative and environmental records. By ensuring citizens can access detailed project information, the company supports informed public debate and accountability in regional territorial planning and environmental impact assessments.
