Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Argentine economy is experiencing a severe contraction driven by aggressive fiscal adjustments and hyperinflation, which have drastically suppressed domestic demand. Industrial and construction sectors face steep declines as rising costs and reduced consumer purchasing power force businesses to scale back production. This downturn mirrors pandemic-era disruptions, signaling a deep structural crisis rather than a temporary fluctuation. The relevance to open data lies in the critical need for transparent, high-frequency statistical monitoring during such volatile periods. Reliable public data is essential to verify official figures, analyze the real-time impact of policy decisions, and hold institutions accountable for economic indicators that directly affect livelihoods. Without accessible data, understanding the true extent of the recession and its sectoral disparities remains difficult for researchers and the public. Ultimately, this situation underscores how data governance intersects with economic stability. Open access to rigorous, timely statistics empowers citizens to comprehend the consequences of austerity measures. It fosters informed debate and ensures that the narrative surrounding economic recovery is grounded in verifiable evidence rather than speculation, protecting the integrity of public discourse during national crises.
