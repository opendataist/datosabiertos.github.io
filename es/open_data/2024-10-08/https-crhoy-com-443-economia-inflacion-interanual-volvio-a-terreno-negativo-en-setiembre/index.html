Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The recent decline in the Consumer Price Index, driven by significant price drops in essential food and transport sectors, offers temporary relief to consumers. However, this fluctuation highlights the critical need for transparent, timely economic data to accurately assess inflation trends and household purchasing power. Open data initiatives are vital here, allowing researchers and citizens to verify official statistics and analyze the distribution of price changes across various goods. This transparency ensures that monetary policy decisions are based on accessible, understandable evidence rather than opaque figures, fostering greater public trust in economic institutions. Ultimately, the divergence between current inflation rates and central bank targets underscores the complexity of the economy. Reliable open datasets empower stakeholders to understand these dynamics, ensuring that policy interventions are responsive to real-world conditions and effectively address the needs of the population.
