El Govern, admite su preocupación, y trabaja en un plan para reducir el absentismo laboral

The Balearic Islands government is launching a comprehensive strategy to investigate the underlying causes of labor absenteeism, with a specific focus on addressing mental health challenges. Although current absence rates are lower than the national average, officials prioritize understanding both work-related and personal factors contributing to long-term absences to implement effective preventive measures. This initiative involves direct dialogue with medical institutions to analyze trends and develop targeted solutions for worker well-being. By tackling absenteeism holistically rather than isolating specific categories, the administration aims to foster a healthier work environment and reduce the socioeconomic impact of frequent or prolonged work stoppages. This development is relevant to open data because transparent, accessible datasets on labor health and absence reasons are essential for validating these policy decisions. Open data allows researchers and the public to scrutinize the causes of absenteeism, ensuring accountability and enabling evidence-based improvements in occupational safety and mental health support systems across the region.
