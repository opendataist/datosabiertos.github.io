The Teletón Institute integrates virtual reality and artificial intelligence into pediatric rehabilitation, using interactive games to motivate physical therapy. This inclusive technology accommodates various physical abilities, allowing children to engage with digital environments regardless of their motor limitations. Consequently, the initiative transforms traditional treatment by making recovery more accessible and effective through immersive experiences. Simultaneously, the program serves as a critical educational tool for digital literacy and online safety. Children learn to protect their personal data and navigate the internet responsibly, preventing cyberbullying. This dual approach ensures that as patients heal physically, they also develop the competencies necessary to participate safely in the modern digital world. This initiative highlights the broader relevance of open data by demonstrating how accessible technology and educational content can drive social inclusion. It illustrates the importance of making digital resources available to vulnerable populations, fostering empowerment. By combining health interventions with digital safety education, it shows how transparent, user-centered technologies can improve quality of life and prepare children for responsible citizenship in an increasingly connected society.
