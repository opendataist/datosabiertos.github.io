Bienestar Chiapas se Compromete con la Transparencia: Moguel Palacios - Periódico El Orbe.

Chiapas promotes open data and government transparency to build public trust and ensure honest institutional performance. This approach empowers citizens by making government metrics and actions accessible, reinforcing accountability. Such initiatives are vital for open data as they demonstrate how transparency strengthens the relationship between the state and society.
