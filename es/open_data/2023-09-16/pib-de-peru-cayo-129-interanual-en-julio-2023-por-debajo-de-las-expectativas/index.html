PIB de Perú cayó 1,29% interanual en julio 2023, por debajo de las expectativas

Peru’s GDP contraction in July highlights economic fragility, driven primarily by severe declines in primary sectors like fishing due to El Niño, while mining growth provided only limited offset. This performance reinforces the ongoing technical recession, signaling that climate-related disruptions significantly hinder production stability despite some industrial resilience. The data underscores the critical importance of open data in monitoring real-time economic health and identifying sector-specific vulnerabilities. By making such statistical indicators publicly accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand the impact of environmental shocks on national economies. This transparency allows for more informed decision-making regarding resource allocation and recovery strategies. Ultimately, accessible data empowers stakeholders to anticipate risks and develop robust responses to climate-induced economic downturns, ensuring greater accountability and efficiency in public policy.
