Alcalá de Henares, capital de la Transparencia y el Gobierno abierto · Noticias Jurídicas

The upcoming congress serves as a vital convergence point for international experts, activists, and officials dedicated to advancing transparency and open government. By facilitating the exchange of experiences and research across borders, the event underscores the global necessity for robust mechanisms that ensure public access to information. This collaborative environment highlights how shared strategies and cross-institutional dialogue are essential for strengthening democratic accountability and fostering a culture of openness worldwide. Key discussions will critically examine the judicial role in defending transparency and the ongoing reform of transparency laws, emphasizing the intersection of legal frameworks and civic engagement. Participants will analyze how civil society leverages transparency to monitor public powers, while also addressing the ethical implications of new technologies, such as artificial intelligence and algorithmic transparency. These debates illustrate the evolving challenges of maintaining integrity in an increasingly digital public sphere, requiring adaptive legal and technical solutions. This gathering is highly relevant to the open data movement because it bridges the gap between legal regulation and practical technological implementation. The focus on digital tools for compliance and secure reporting demonstrates how open data infrastructure supports whistleblower protections and organizational integrity. By integrating software solutions for ethical conduct and normative updates, the congress highlights the practical application of data transparency to combat corruption and enhance public trust in institutional governance.
