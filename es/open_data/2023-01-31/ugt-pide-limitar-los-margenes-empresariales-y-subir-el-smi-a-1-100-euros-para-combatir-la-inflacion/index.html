UGT pide limitar los márgenes empresariales y subir el SMI a 1.100 euros para combatir la inflación

The article highlights a conflict between official inflation data and union perceptions of corporate price gouging. UGT argues that despite moderate inflation figures, companies are abusing their market power to inflate prices, particularly in essential sectors. This disconnect reveals critical gaps in how economic indicators capture the real impact on consumers, emphasizing the need for better data transparency regarding corporate margins and pricing behaviors. To protect purchasing power, the union demands strict limits on business profits and a significant increase in the minimum wage. These measures aim to counteract the erosion of household buying power caused by rising costs. The emphasis on policy intervention underscores the importance of accessible economic data in shaping effective social protection strategies for vulnerable populations. Relevance to open data lies in the demand for granular, transparent information on corporate pricing and wage levels. Current aggregated indices may obscure the specific mechanisms driving inflation, such as excessive margins. Openly sharing detailed financial and pricing data would allow for more accurate monitoring of economic fairness and empower policymakers to implement targeted solutions that genuinely reflect market realities and protect workers.
