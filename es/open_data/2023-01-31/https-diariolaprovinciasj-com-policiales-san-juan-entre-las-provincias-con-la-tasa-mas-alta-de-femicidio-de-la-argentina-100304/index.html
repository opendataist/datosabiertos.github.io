San Juan ranks among Argentina’s provinces with the highest femicide rates, highlighting a severe nationwide crisis that demands urgent attention. The data reveals significant regional disparities, particularly in the North-West, where several provinces show alarmingly high trends compared to historical averages. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent statistics expose systemic gender violence patterns, enabling policymakers and civil society to monitor progress and advocate for effective, evidence-based interventions.
