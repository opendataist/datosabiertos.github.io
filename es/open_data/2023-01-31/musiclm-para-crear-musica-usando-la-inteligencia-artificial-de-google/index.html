MusicLM, para crear música usando la Inteligencia Artificial de Google

MusicLM demonstrates that AI can generate diverse, complex musical compositions from text or audio descriptions, marking a significant leap in creative technology. This capability highlights the potential for generative models to understand abstract concepts and blend genres, pushing the boundaries of what is possible in digital art and composition. However, current limitations like voice incoherence and copyright risks prevent public release. The system’s tendency to reproduce training data raises serious licensing questions, suggesting that future AI music tools will need robust legal frameworks to operate ethically and safely within existing intellectual property structures. This development is crucial for open data as it forces a reevaluation of how training datasets are curated, licensed, and shared. It underscores the urgent need for transparent, legally compliant data sourcing in AI research to ensure that technological progress does not come at the cost of creators’ rights or data sovereignty.
