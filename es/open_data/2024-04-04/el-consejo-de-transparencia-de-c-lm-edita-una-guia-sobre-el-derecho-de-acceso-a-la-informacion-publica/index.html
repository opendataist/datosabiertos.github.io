El Consejo de Transparencia de C-LM edita una guía sobre el derecho de acceso a la información pública

The regional transparency council launched an accessible guide designed to simplify citizens' requests for public information, fostering a robust culture of transparency. This initiative aims to bridge the gap between administrators and the public by demystifying legal deadlines and limits through straightforward language and digital tools like QR codes. The guide empowers individuals to effectively exercise their right to access information while outlining clear procedures for filing appeals against unsatisfactory administrative responses. It emphasizes reducing bureaucratic barriers, ensuring that residents can interact with governance bodies efficiently without unnecessary physical displacement or complex paperwork. This development is vital for open data as it actively promotes accountability by making government information more reachable and understandable. By institutionalizing easy access and independent oversight, it strengthens democratic engagement and ensures that transparency mechanisms are not just theoretical rights but practical, user-friendly tools for society.
