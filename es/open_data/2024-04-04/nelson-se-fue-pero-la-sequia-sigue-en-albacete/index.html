'Nelson' se fue, pero la sequía sigue en Albacete

The recent storm Nelson provided only a temporary respite from the prolonged drought in Albacete, revealing that the region remains far from recovery despite localized heavy rainfall. While certain areas experienced significant precipitation that temporarily revived river flows, the overall improvement was marginal and insufficient to resolve the underlying water crisis. National meteorological data confirms that the province continues to face substantial deficits in rainfall and ground moisture. Reservoir levels, although slightly replenished, remain critically low, with most storage facilities operating well below their maximum capacity. This indicates that the recent wet spells were inadequate to offset the accumulated shortfall, leaving the water security situation precarious for the near future. This report is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical importance of accessible, granular meteorological records for environmental monitoring. By making specific measurements from various pluviometers and reservoirs publicly available, stakeholders can better understand localized impacts versus provincial averages. Transparent data facilitates more accurate assessments of drought severity, enabling informed decision-making for resource management and public awareness regarding the true state of water reserves.
