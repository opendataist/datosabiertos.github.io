Las pernoctaciones hoteleras caen un 1,2% en septiembre en C-LM, mientras los precios se disparan un 4,45%

The hospitality sector shows resilience through rising prices and employment, despite a slight decline in tourist arrivals. This dynamic highlights the shift toward higher revenue per guest rather than volume expansion, challenging models reliant solely on footfall growth. Occupancy rates remain significantly lower than leading coastal regions, suggesting untapped potential for market expansion. The data reveals a disparity in regional competitiveness, urging local authorities to investigate barriers to attracting international visitors and improve strategic positioning. These insights are vital for open data initiatives, as transparent statistics enable analysts to benchmark performance and identify gaps in tourism distribution. Such accessible metrics foster informed policy-making and encourage competitive innovation across the hospitality industry.
