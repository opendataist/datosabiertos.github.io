Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The INAI initiated an official investigation after the Presidential Office published documents containing personal data related to the Márquez Padilla case. This action violates the General Law of Protection of Personal Data, as the administration failed to protect individuals' privacy despite its legal obligations to ensure data confidentiality and secure processing. The Commission President strongly condemned this breach as an attack on privacy and freedom of expression in a democracy. Such unauthorized disclosures undermine public trust and highlight the critical need for government entities to respect individual rights. Ignoring the severity of these leaks poses significant risks to citizens' fundamental liberties. This case is vital for open data because it illustrates the tension between transparency and privacy. It emphasizes that while public access to information is essential, it must be balanced with rigorous protection of personal data. Understanding these boundaries helps organizations implement compliant practices that foster trust and uphold democratic values.
