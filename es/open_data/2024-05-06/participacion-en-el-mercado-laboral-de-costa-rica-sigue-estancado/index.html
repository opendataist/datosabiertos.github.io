Participación en el mercado laboral de Costa Rica sigue estancado

Costa Rica shows a stable labor market with a significant shift toward formal employment. The reduction in informal work highlights a successful transition, suggesting that economic policies are effectively integrating workers into the regulated sector rather than leaving them in precarious conditions. This structural change improves job security and social protection for the majority of the workforce. Simultaneously, the labor market became more inclusive for women, evidenced by a notable rise in their participation and a sharper decline in female unemployment. The data indicates that gender gaps are narrowing as more women enter the workforce and find formal positions. This trend signals progress toward equity, reflecting a more resilient and balanced labor ecosystem compared to previous years. This dataset is crucial for open_data advocates as it exemplifies how standardized, publicly accessible statistics empower researchers and policymakers. By making granular employment metrics available, the INEC enables transparent analysis of social progress. Such data facilitates evidence-based decision-making, allowing citizens to monitor government performance and advocate for inclusive economic strategies without relying on opaque or restricted sources.
