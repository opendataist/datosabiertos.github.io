Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This initiative demonstrates how public art serves as a powerful medium for disseminating critical concepts regarding transparency and data protection. By transforming abstract legal rights into visible community murals, authorities effectively engage citizens in conversations about personal information privacy and the right to access public records. This approach lowers the barrier to understanding complex governance issues, making them accessible and relatable to the general population. The project highlights the essential role of civil society and artists in advocating for open data principles. It underscores that protecting personal data is not merely a technical obligation but a fundamental human right that requires societal awareness. The involvement of local government leaders further validates the importance of these norms, showing institutional commitment to fostering a culture where transparency is actively promoted and respected within communities. Relevance to open data lies in this innovative strategy to democratize knowledge and enhance public oversight. It proves that visual communication can effectively bridge the gap between technical regulations and civic engagement. This method encourages citizens to question authority, demand accountability, and understand the value of their digital footprint, thereby strengthening the social contract necessary for a truly open and transparent government system.
