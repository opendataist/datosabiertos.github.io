3,760 fallecidos por covid en España en el primer semestre, un 82 % menos que en 2022

The INE report reveals a dramatic decline in pandemic-related mortality, as COVID-19 ceased to be among the leading causes of death in early 2023. This shift highlights how public health data must adapt to evolving health landscapes, moving from crisis monitoring to routine vital statistics analysis. Cardiovascular diseases and cancer remain the primary drivers of mortality, underscoring the persistent burden of chronic conditions. This transition in data focus illustrates the dynamic nature of epidemiological tracking, where open datasets help identify long-term health trends rather than just acute emergencies. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of accessible, timely mortality records for understanding societal health shifts. By making these statistics transparent, researchers and policymakers can accurately assess the post-pandemic era, ensuring resources target the most significant current health risks effectively.
