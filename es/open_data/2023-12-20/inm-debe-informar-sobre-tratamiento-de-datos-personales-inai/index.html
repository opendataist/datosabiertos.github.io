INM debe informar sobre tratamiento de datos personales: INAI

The INAI ordered the INM to disclose how it uses biometric data at airports, emphasizing legal bases and privacy impact assessments. This transparency is crucial for open data, as it allows citizens to scrutinize how personal information is processed and stored. Such oversight prevents generalized surveillance and protects fundamental rights like privacy and freedom. Ultimately, ensuring these systems respect proportionality and consent is vital for democratic accountability and the ethical handling of public data.
