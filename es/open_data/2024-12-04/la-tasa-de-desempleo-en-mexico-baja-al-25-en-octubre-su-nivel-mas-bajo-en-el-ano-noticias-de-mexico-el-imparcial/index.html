La tasa de desempleo en México baja al 2,5% en octubre, su nivel más bajo en el año | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

Mexico’s October labor report highlights a significant drop in unemployment, reaching a historic low of 2.5%. This decline indicates a robust recovery in job availability, with the employed population growing substantially compared to the previous year. Such trends signal improving economic stability and increased workforce participation, reflecting positive momentum in the national economy despite global challenges. However, the data reveals underlying vulnerabilities through high subemployment and informal sector engagement. A considerable portion of workers require more hours, while over half of the employed remain in informal roles. These figures suggest that while quantity of jobs has improved, quality and stability remain critical issues for many, pointing to structural labor market weaknesses that persist even during periods of growth. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the vital role of transparent, official statistics from institutions like INEGI in shaping economic analysis. Reliable, accessible public data enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to accurately assess labor trends, identify disparities between formal and informal employment, and drive informed decisions regarding social programs and economic policy implementation.
