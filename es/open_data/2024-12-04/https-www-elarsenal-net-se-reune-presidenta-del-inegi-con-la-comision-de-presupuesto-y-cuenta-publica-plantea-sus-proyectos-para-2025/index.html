Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The head of Mexico’s statistics institute proposed integrating data for broader legislative design, ensuring its utility extends beyond mere budget analysis. This strategic shift aims to embed statistical evidence directly into the creation of laws, promoting more informed policy-making across government branches. By absorbing Coneval’s functions, the institute plans to streamline operations rather than double efforts, pending final legislative reforms. This consolidation represents a significant structural change, potentially centralizing social evaluation and statistical generation under one unified administrative framework for greater efficiency. This development is crucial for open data advocates, as it signals a move toward institutionalizing data transparency. Ensuring that statistical outputs actively inform legislative processes reinforces the principle that public data should be accessible and instrumental in shaping transparent, evidence-based governance.
