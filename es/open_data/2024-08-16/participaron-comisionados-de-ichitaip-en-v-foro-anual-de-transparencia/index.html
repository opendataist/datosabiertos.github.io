Participaron comisionados de Ichitaip en V Foro Anual de Transparencia

The article emphasizes that access to public information strengthens citizen participation and enhances government transparency. By enabling people to understand their administration better, it serves as a crucial tool for accountability and combating corruption. This foundational openness is essential for building trust between citizens and state institutions. However, the event highlighted significant risks associated with digital technology and social media platforms. Although these services appear free, they often collect personal data that remains vulnerable to misuse. Experts warned of dangers such as cyberbullying, fraud, and data trafficking, which can lead to severe crimes. This topic is relevant to open data because it underscores the need to balance transparency with privacy protection. While open data promotes accountability, it must coexist with robust safeguards for personal information. Strengthening digital education and updating legislation are vital to ensuring that the benefits of accessible information do not compromise individual security in an interconnected world.
