Datos Abiertos IMSS

This dataset reveals discrepancies between registered jobs and insured individuals, highlighting significant underreporting in Mexican employment statistics. The granular breakdown by sector and demographics offers critical transparency into labor market structures, essential for accurate economic analysis and policy formulation. Such open data is vital for identifying informal work trends, fostering accountability, and improving the reliability of official socioeconomic indicators.

Source: datos.imss.gob.mx
