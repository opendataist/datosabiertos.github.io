El padrón municipal de Vigo vuelve a superar los 300.000 habitantes

Vigo’s municipal council reports a population exceeding 300,000, highlighting a significant discrepancy with the national statistics institute’s lower count. This gap stems from individuals registering in the city for administrative access despite residing elsewhere, illustrating common census duplication issues. This data underscores the importance of transparent and accurate open data regarding residency definitions. Understanding these methodological differences is crucial for policymakers relying on reliable demographic information to ensure effective urban planning and resource allocation.
