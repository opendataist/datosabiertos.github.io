Castilla-La Mancha es la tercera Comunidad Autónoma que más reduce el peso de su deuda pública en el último año

Castilla-La Mancha significantly reduced its public debt relative to GDP, outperforming the autonomous community average. This achievement demonstrates that fiscal consolidation can coexist with sustained social support for vulnerable groups and economic stimulation, validating prudent financial management. This transparency in regional public finance metrics aligns with open data principles by providing clear, accessible insights into government fiscal health. Such data allows citizens and analysts to verify policy effectiveness, fostering trust in how resources balance debt reduction with social welfare investments.
