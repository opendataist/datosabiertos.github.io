Actúa Vietnam contra la infracción de derechos de autor en plataformas digitales | Cie-Tec | Vietnam+ (VietnamPlus)

Vietnam faces a severe digital copyright infringement crisis as it aggressively pursues digital transformation in its media sector. The widespread adoption of online platforms by news agencies has coincided with a surge in content piracy, particularly among young users who favor free, illegal sources. This "epidemic" not only erodes creativity and mental well-being but also inflicts substantial economic losses, threatening state revenue and the country’s reputation among foreign investors. Recognizing the cross-border nature of this challenge, Vietnam is spearheading regional cooperation within ASEAN to establish unified regulations for media platforms. The core consensus emphasizes that new technologies bring both opportunities and risks, necessitating joint efforts to protect journalistic works. By aligning with neighboring countries, Vietnam aims to create a resilient framework where transnational platforms adhere to common legal standards and codes of conduct, mitigating the negative societal and political impacts of unchecked digital dissemination. To combat these violations, the article advocates for shared data mechanisms and stricter liability for intermediary service providers. By holding internet operators and hosting services accountable for implementing technical safeguards, authorities can effectively target pirate sites regardless of their location or anonymity. This approach is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the need for transparent, interoperable systems that enable real-time coordination between agencies. Such frameworks ensure that data flows securely while protecting intellectual property, demonstrating how open data practices can support legal enforcement without compromising user privacy or innovation.
