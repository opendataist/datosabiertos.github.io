PIME pide que las licitaciones valoren el arraigo en la zona

Business leaders are urging the government to establish a public transparency portal for contracting. This initiative ensures real-time visibility into public bids, which is a fundamental open data principle. Such openness promotes accountability and allows for better monitoring of public spending. The request specifically demands criteria adapted to small businesses and local roots. By making tender details accessible, the portal would help level the playing field. This transparency supports fair competition and reduces barriers for autonomous workers and smaller enterprises seeking government contracts. Overall, this proposal highlights the critical role of open data in fostering trust. It demonstrates how accessible information can empower local entities and ensure equitable opportunities. The emphasis on clarity and availability aligns directly with open governance goals.
