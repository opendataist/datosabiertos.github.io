Milton Morrison resalta el aporte de datos abiertos para la transparencia administrativa

Edesur Dominicana transformed its institutional culture by replacing opacity with radical transparency through an agreement with the OEA. This partnership enabled comprehensive staff training, ensuring that sensitive operational areas like finance and logistics are managed with openness. The initiative successfully shifted the organization’s internal mindset toward accountability. This case demonstrates how structured data governance frameworks can drive cultural change within public utilities. By prioritizing accessible information, Edesur empowers citizens and clients to hold the institution accountable. The move from secrecy to openness serves as a practical model for other state entities seeking to improve their transparency records. The article is relevant to open data as it highlights the tangible impact of institutional agreements on public access. It illustrates that successful open data implementation requires not just technology, but also employee education and a committed leadership strategy to foster lasting transparency and trust.
