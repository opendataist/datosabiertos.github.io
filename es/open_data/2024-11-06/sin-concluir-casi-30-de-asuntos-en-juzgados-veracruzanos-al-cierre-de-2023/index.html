Sin concluir, casi 30% de asuntos en juzgados veracruzanos al cierre de 2023

The article highlights a significant backlog in Veracruz’s judicial system, where nearly a third of cases remained unresolved at the end of 2023. This delay undermines timely justice delivery, emphasizing the urgent need for administrative efficiency within the legal framework. Nationally, penal causes have steadily increased in local courts over recent years, reflecting growing systemic pressure. The data reveals a persistent trend of rising caseloads, which strains judicial resources and requires strategic management to prevent further bottlenecks. This report is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how transparent government statistics can expose operational inefficiencies. By making these metrics publicly available, stakeholders can analyze trends, advocate for reforms, and ensure accountability in public service delivery.
