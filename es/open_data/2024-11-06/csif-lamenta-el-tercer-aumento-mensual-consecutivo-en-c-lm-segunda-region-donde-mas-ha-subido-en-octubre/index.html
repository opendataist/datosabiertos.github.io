CSIF lamenta el tercer aumento mensual consecutivo en C-LM, segunda región donde más ha subido en octubre

Unions highlight that despite annual improvements, consecutive monthly job losses reveal a fragile labor market, particularly in healthcare. This decline underscores the urgent need for transparent public employment data to assess sectoral health accurately. The article argues for policy reforms protecting workers affected by extreme weather, emphasizing that statistical reporting must distinguish between temporary and permanent employment impacts. This is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how granular, accessible statistics enable civil society to verify government claims and advocate for evidence-based labor protections during crises.
