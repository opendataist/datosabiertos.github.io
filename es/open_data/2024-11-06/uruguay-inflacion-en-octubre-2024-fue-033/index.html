Uruguay: inflación en octubre 2024 fue 0,33%

The article details Uruguay's latest inflation trends, highlighting how specific sectors like food, clothing, and transport drive price fluctuations. This data exemplifies open data in action, as the National Statistics Institute (INE) publishes granular, accessible information that allows society to monitor economic health transparently. By breaking down indices into specific categories, the report reveals nuanced market dynamics, such as seasonal price changes in apparel and energy cost decreases. This level of detail is crucial for understanding the real-world impact of inflation on households, moving beyond aggregate numbers to show exactly where costs are rising or falling. Ultimately, this open dataset empowers citizens, researchers, and policymakers to make informed decisions based on accurate, timely evidence. It demonstrates the value of public statistics in fostering accountability and enabling a deeper, more democratic engagement with national economic indicators, ensuring that such vital information remains available for public scrutiny and analysis.
