Revela Inegi rezago en impartición de Justicia en Quintana Roo - Noticaribe

The 2024 census by Mexico’s statistical institute highlights a severe backlog in Quintana Roo’s state justice system, where only about a third of entered cases were resolved. This significant lag suggests systemic inefficiencies in handling judicial workloads, indicating that the current infrastructure struggles to process legal demands effectively. Demographic data reveals that women hold nearly half of the judging positions, signaling progress in gender diversity within the judiciary. However, the disparity between cases entered and concluded points to structural bottlenecks that may require broader administrative reforms rather than simple personnel adjustments to ensure equitable access to justice for all citizens. This information is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistics empower civil society to evaluate government performance. By making these judicial metrics publicly available, stakeholders can advocate for policy changes, while open datasets enable researchers to analyze trends in legal efficiency and resource allocation across different regions and time periods.
