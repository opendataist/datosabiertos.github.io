Registro Poblacional Municipal de la Alcaldía se realizó en el Distrito 15

A local government verifies population data against national institute reports, claiming discrepancies due to recent territorial expansions. The municipal registry aims to correct statistical errors, specifically regarding areas previously omitted or misassigned. This initiative highlights the critical need for accurate, granular census data in open government contexts. Discrepancies between national statistics and local records can lead to misallocated resources and poor planning. Such grassroots validation efforts underscore the value of accessible, detailed municipal data. Ensuring data integrity empowers communities and improves transparency, which are fundamental goals for open data initiatives in public administration.
