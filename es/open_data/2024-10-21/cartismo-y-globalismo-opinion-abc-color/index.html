Cartismo y globalismo - Opinión - ABC Color

The article concludes that the Cartismo political movement acts as a deceptive fifth column, prioritizing globalist interests over national sovereignty. Despite projecting an antiglobalist image to its supporters, the group is accused of actively implementing corporate-led agendas that undermine democratic processes. This strategy serves to anesthetize the populace, preventing effective resistance against the erosion of local governance in favor of a worldwide state controlled by international entities. Key actions cited include dismantling transparency laws for non-governmental organizations and enforcing policies contrary to local science, all under the guise of international agreements. These measures allegedly allow external entities to dictate public policy without popular consent, effectively bypassing constitutional safeguards. The author argues this behavior legitimizes leadership through submission to global powers rather than through genuine national representation, revealing a complicity that contradicts proclaimed patriotic values. This narrative is critical for open_data advocates as it highlights how the suppression of transparency laws creates opaque environments where corporate influence over public policy remains unchecked. Without robust access to information, citizens cannot verify claims about data governance or hold leaders accountable for aligning with extranational agendas. The text underscores the urgent need for transparency mechanisms to expose hidden power structures and protect democratic integrity against hidden globalist interventions.
