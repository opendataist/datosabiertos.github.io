Autoridades del Beni, INE y TIPNIS llegan a acuerdos que garantizan el operativo del Censo

The National Institute of Statistics, the Beni Department, and Indigenous leaders have reached a consensus to ensure the 2024 Census proceeds smoothly. This agreement resolves territorial disputes and guarantees that Indigenous communities are counted appropriately, fostering stability and trust among regional authorities. The census serves as the essential mechanism for generating reliable data to shape effective public policies. By addressing these political tensions through dialogue, the government ensures that decisions are grounded in the actual reality of the population, particularly in complex regions like the Beni. This development is highly relevant to open data because a successful census provides the foundational dataset for future transparency. Access to accurate, comprehensive demographic information allows citizens and organizations to analyze governance outcomes, advocate for rights, and hold institutions accountable based on verifiable statistics rather than speculation.
