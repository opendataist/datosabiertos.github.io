Menorca ha ganado 1.861 habitantes durante el último año

The island recently surpassed 100,000 residents, marking a modest growth despite not reaching the peak recorded later that year. This demographic shift highlights a significant aging population, where adults aged 45 to 54 form the largest cohort while minors represent a minimal percentage of the total. This evolution underscores persistent structural challenges, specifically the decline in youth presence and the proportional increase of elderly citizens. These trends suggest long-term pressures on social services and economic vitality, indicating a society increasingly defined by its mature demographics rather than its younger segments. Such granular data is vital for open_data initiatives, enabling transparent public scrutiny of municipal planning and resource allocation. By exposing these inequalities, open datasets empower communities to advocate for sustainable policies that address the specific needs of aging populations and support balanced demographic development.
