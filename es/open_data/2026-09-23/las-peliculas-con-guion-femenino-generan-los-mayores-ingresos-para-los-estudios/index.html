Las películas con guion femenino generan los mayores ingresos para los estudios

The study reveals a significant discrepancy between financial performance and gender representation in the film industry, demonstrating that productions with female screenwriters consistently outperform those with exclusively male crews. This finding challenges the traditional assumption that male-dominated teams ensure profitability, suggesting instead that the current allocation of capital is inefficient and ignores high-performing alternatives. Despite this clear economic advantage, the industry exhibits a structural bias that restricts women from accessing the largest budgets and key creative roles. The slow growth in female participation indicates that decision-makers prioritize risk avoidance and familiarity over data-driven efficiency. Consequently, resources remain concentrated in male-dominated circles, perpetuating a system where proven financial success is not being fully leveraged to promote diversity. This research is highly relevant to open data because it utilizes large-scale analytical methods to expose systemic inequalities through empirical evidence rather than anecdotal arguments. It highlights the power of data transparency in identifying misalignments between organizational practices and actual outcomes, encouraging stakeholders to adopt more inclusive strategies based on verifiable financial insights rather than entrenched biases.
