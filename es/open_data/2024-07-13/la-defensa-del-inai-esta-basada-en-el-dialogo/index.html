La defensa del INAI está basada en el diálogo

The INAI commissioner urges dialogue before deciding the institute's future, emphasizing the societal cost-benefit of maintaining transparency mechanisms. She advocates for an open parliament to debate reforms, highlighting that democratic values and citizen rights must not be sidelined. This article is relevant to open_data as it underscores the critical role of independent institutions in guaranteeing access to information. Protecting such bodies ensures the infrastructure and legal frameworks necessary for transparent data governance remain intact, preventing arbitrary decisions that could undermine public trust and digital rights.
