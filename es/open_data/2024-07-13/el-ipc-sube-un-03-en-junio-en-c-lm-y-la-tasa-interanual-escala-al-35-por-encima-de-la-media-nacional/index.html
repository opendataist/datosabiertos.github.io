El IPC sube un 0,3% en junio en C-LM y la tasa interanual escala al 3,5%, por encima de la media nacional

The article reveals that inflation in Castilla-La Mancha has risen, driven significantly by housing and food costs, reflecting broader economic pressures on local households. Nationally, the Consumer Price Index shows a slight increase in June with a moderate annual rate, influenced by decreases in fuel and oil prices despite expectations of higher growth.
