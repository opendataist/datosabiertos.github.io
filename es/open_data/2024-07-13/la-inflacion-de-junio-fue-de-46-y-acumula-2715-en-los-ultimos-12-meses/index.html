La inflación de junio fue de 4,6% y acumula 271,5% en los últimos 12 meses

Argentina’s June inflation rose to 4.6%, ending a five-month downward trend due to regulated tariff hikes. This reversal highlights the persistent pressure of price controls and government spending on the cost of living. Market expectations and private surveys confirm this uptick, projecting an annual inflation rate exceeding 138%. The divergence between official figures and private estimates underscores the complexity of measuring economic stability amid volatile pricing structures. This data is crucial for open data because it demonstrates the necessity of cross-referencing multiple sources. Transparent access to diverse inflation metrics allows researchers to validate claims and understand the real economic impact beyond official narratives.
