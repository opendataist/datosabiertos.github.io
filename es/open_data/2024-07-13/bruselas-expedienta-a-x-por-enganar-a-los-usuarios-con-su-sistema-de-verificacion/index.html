Bruselas expedienta a X por "engañar a los usuarios" con su sistema de verificación

The European Commission has determined that X violates the Digital Services Act regarding dark patterns, advertising transparency, and researcher data access. This ruling highlights a significant shift in holding major platforms accountable for user manipulation and opaque systems. The Commission argues that X’s verification system misleads users, undermining informed decision-making and enabling malicious actors. Furthermore, the platform’s advertising repository lacks necessary transparency features, preventing adequate oversight of online risks. These structural flaws suggest that current design choices prioritize business models over user safety and regulatory compliance. This case is crucial for open data because it enforces the legal requirement for researchers to access public platform data without prohibitive barriers. By mandating fair API access and banning restrictive practices like anti-scraping clauses, the DSA aims to foster independent scrutiny of algorithmic impacts. This ensures that scientific research can effectively monitor digital ecosystems, promoting accountability and integrity in the digital public sphere.
