Bolivia registra un superávit de $us 68 millones a mayo de 2024

Bolivia achieved a trade surplus in May 2024, driven by significant growth in both traditional and non-traditional exports. Key sectors like soy derivatives, meat, and zinc minerals demonstrated robust performance, strengthening the nation’s economic position. This data highlights the diversification of Bolivia’s export portfolio beyond traditional resources, indicating resilience and competitiveness in global markets. The stability in import costs further contributes to maintaining this favorable balance of trade. Understanding such macroeconomic indicators is crucial for open data initiatives. Transparent, accessible trade statistics empower researchers and policymakers to analyze economic trends, fostering informed decision-making and promoting accountability in national economic management.
