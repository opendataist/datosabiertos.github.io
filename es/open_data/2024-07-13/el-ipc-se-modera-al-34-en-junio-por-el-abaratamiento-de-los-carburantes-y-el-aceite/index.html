El IPC se modera al 3,4% en junio por el abaratamiento de los carburantes y el aceite

The final data from Spain’s National Statistics Institute confirms a significant moderation in inflation, marking a turning point after three consecutive months of increases. Food and beverage prices saw their lowest annual growth in two and a half years, driven by drops in specific categories like oils and fats, while energy prices also contributed to the overall decline. This decrease signals a potential stabilization in consumer price pressures, offering relief to households and businesses facing high costs. On a monthly basis, however, prices continued to rise due to increases in tourism packages, electricity, and accommodation services. This divergence between annual and monthly trends highlights the complex dynamics of the economy, where temporary shocks in specific sectors can mask broader underlying trends. The underlying inflation rate remained stable, suggesting that core price pressures are not accelerating, which is crucial for monetary policy considerations. This information is vital for open data because it demonstrates how transparent, official statistical releases allow for independent verification and deeper economic analysis. By providing accessible, granular data on regional variations and sectoral trends, the INE enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to move beyond headline figures. This transparency fosters accountability and helps the public understand the nuanced reality of inflation, rather than relying on simplified narratives.
