Estas son las 184 reducciones de condenas y 19 excarcelaciones en Madrid por la Ley 'solo sí es sí

The article reveals updated statistics on penalty reductions and releases under the "Only Yes is Yes" law, highlighting significant judicial outcomes across Spain. These figures demonstrate the law’s tangible impact on sentencing justice, marking a shift in how gender-based violence cases are adjudicated nationwide. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives as it illustrates the practical application of legal reforms. Transparent access to such statistics allows researchers and citizens to monitor judicial efficiency and evaluate the effectiveness of anti-violence policies. Open data ensures accountability and facilitates informed public debate on human rights advancements within the justice system.
