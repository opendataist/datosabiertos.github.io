La obesidad como desencadenante de muerte cardíaca se triplicó en 20 años en EEUU

The article highlights a stark divergence in US cardiovascular mortality trends, where deaths attributed to obesity-linked heart disease surged significantly between 1999 and 2020, contrasting sharply with an overall decline in general heart-related fatalities. This rise underscores obesity as an escalating public health crisis, disproportionately affecting specific demographics and reflecting broader epidemiological shifts driven by increased prevalence of the condition. The relevance to open data lies in the study’s reliance on publicly accessible, granular records from the CDC. By analyzing open death certificate databases, researchers can uncover critical, time-sensitive health patterns that aggregate reports might miss. This case demonstrates how open data facilitates transparency in public health, enabling the identification of emerging crises and supporting evidence-based policy interventions to address systemic health disparities effectively.
