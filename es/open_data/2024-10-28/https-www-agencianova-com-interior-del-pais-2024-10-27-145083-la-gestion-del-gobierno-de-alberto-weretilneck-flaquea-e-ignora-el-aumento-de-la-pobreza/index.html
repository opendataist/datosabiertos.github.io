Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Economic instability and fiscal shortfalls severely hinder government capacity to fund essential public services, directly impacting social well-being and local business growth. This crisis highlights the critical need for transparent fiscal monitoring and effective policy reforms to restore trust. The situation underscores the importance of open_data in economic governance. Accessible, accurate fiscal data enables better analysis of tax evasion and spending cuts, facilitating evidence-based decisions. Ultimately, transparency empowers citizens and policymakers to address revenue drops and implement impactful strategies. Open data is vital for accountability, ensuring that public resources are managed efficiently to mitigate the adverse effects of economic uncertainty on vulnerable communities.
