Un primer vistazo a la próxima evolución de Galaxy AI - La Capital

Samsung’s introduction of One UI 7 and the Personal Data Core marks a pivotal shift in mobile technology by seamlessly integrating artificial intelligence with uncompromising user privacy. This new platform represents more than an incremental update; it is a fundamental redefinition of the smartphone experience, moving beyond reactive tools to proactive, personalized assistance that anticipates user needs. By embedding AI directly into the operating system, Samsung aims to eliminate daily friction, allowing users to focus on what truly matters while the device handles complex contextual adjustments autonomously. The core innovation lies in the architectural protection of personal information, ensuring that this heightened personalization does not come at the cost of security. Through Knox Vault and post-quantum cryptography, sensitive data is stored in a secure, isolated environment, safeguarding users against emerging threats. This robust privacy framework is essential for building trust in AI-driven services, as it guarantees that individual lifestyle data remains private even as it powers highly tailored interactions. This balance allows for deep system integration without exposing users to unauthorized data exploitation. This development is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it highlights the critical tension between data utilization for personalization and the imperative of data sovereignty. It underscores that for AI to be widely accepted, it must operate on architectures that prioritize user control and security over unrestricted data harvesting. By demonstrating that advanced personalization can coexist with strict privacy controls, Samsung provides a case study for how future digital ecosystems might responsibly manage the vast amounts of personal data required to power intelligent systems.
