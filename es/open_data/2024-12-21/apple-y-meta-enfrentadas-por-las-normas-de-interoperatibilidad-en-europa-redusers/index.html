Apple y Meta enfrentadas por las normas de interoperatibilidad en Europa - RedUSERS

The European Commission’s push for service interoperability aims to dismantle closed ecosystems and foster fair competition, directly challenging tech giants like Apple. This initiative seeks to prevent companies from creating barriers that stifle market entry and innovation through proprietary control over digital services. Apple opposes this mandate, arguing it compromises user data security, particularly regarding Meta’s access requests. Conversely, Meta contends that Apple uses privacy as a pretext to maintain monopolistic power, highlighting a fundamental tension between corporate security claims and the open standards necessary for a dynamic digital economy. This dispute is crucial for open data because it determines whether digital infrastructure remains restrictive or becomes accessible. Ensuring interoperability guarantees that data flows freely across platforms, empowering users and developers while preventing any single entity from controlling the digital landscape, which is essential for true transparency and competition.
