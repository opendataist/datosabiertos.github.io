El dato más crudo del Censo 2022: en Mar del Plata hay 238 personas en situación de calle

The 2022 Census reveals a significant discrepancy in housing security and homelessness data in Mar del Plata, highlighting the severity of vulnerability. Official figures indicate more than double the homeless population previously estimated by municipal authorities, aligning closer to independent social organization reports. This divergence underscores critical gaps in local data collection and administrative awareness regarding the true scale of the housing crisis. These findings are vital for open data initiatives as they demonstrate the necessity of transparent, verifiable public statistics to expose discrepancies between government estimates and ground-level realities. Reliable open data enables civil society and policymakers to accurately assess resource needs, ensuring that interventions for housing and social support are based on comprehensive evidence rather than underreported figures, thereby fostering accountability and effective public policy.
