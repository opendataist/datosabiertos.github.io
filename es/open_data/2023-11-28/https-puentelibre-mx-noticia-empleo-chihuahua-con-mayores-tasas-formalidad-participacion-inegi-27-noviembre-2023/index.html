Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

INEGI’s latest employment data highlights stark regional disparities in labor informality and participation across Mexico, revealing that while some states struggle with widespread informal work, others show higher economic engagement. Chihuahua emerges as a notable exception, demonstrating strong formal employment rates and lower pressure compared to the national average, despite significant population figures. This contrast underscores the uneven impact of economic structures on workers' security and stability. These findings are vital for open data initiatives, as they provide transparent, granular insights into labor market dynamics. By making such detailed statistics accessible, stakeholders can better analyze regional inequalities and advocate for targeted policies to improve working conditions.
