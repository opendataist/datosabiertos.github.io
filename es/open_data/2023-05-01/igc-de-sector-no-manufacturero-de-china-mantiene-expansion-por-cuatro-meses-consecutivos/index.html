IGC de sector no manufacturero de China mantiene expansión por cuatro meses consecutivos

China’s non-manufacturing sector sustained its expansion trend in April, driven by a robust recovery in services and construction. This consistent growth highlights increasing business confidence and rising demand, signaling that the economy is moving beyond initial pandemic-related disruptions toward stable momentum. These positive trends indicate strong underlying resilience in the service and construction industries. As new orders and business expectations rise, the data reflects a solidifying recovery, suggesting that economic activities are becoming more stable and sustainable over time. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, freely accessible government statistics enable real-time monitoring of economic health. Such data allows researchers and the public to verify national trends, fostering accountability and informed decision-making based on verifiable, public records.
