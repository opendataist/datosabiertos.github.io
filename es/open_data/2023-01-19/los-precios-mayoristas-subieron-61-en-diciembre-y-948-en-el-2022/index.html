Los precios mayoristas subieron 6,1% en diciembre y 94,8% en el 2022

This report highlights the persistent inflationary pressures in the wholesale and construction sectors, marking a critical juncture for economic transparency. The data reveals that wholesale prices and construction costs surged significantly throughout the year, driven by steep increases in raw materials, energy, and labor. These figures underscore the severe erosion of purchasing power and the high operational costs facing industries, illustrating the urgent need for reliable, timely statistical data to understand the true state of the economy. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity of accessible, granular metrics for public accountability and informed policy-making. By making detailed price indices publicly available, institutions like INDEC empower citizens, researchers, and businesses to track economic trends independently. This transparency is vital for validating government claims, fostering trust in public statistics, and enabling civil society to analyze the distribution of economic burdens across different sectors. Ultimately, this information is crucial for assessing the effectiveness of government stabilization policies. With officials aiming to curb inflation through price agreements and wage controls, open data provides the empirical foundation to evaluate whether these measures are working or if structural issues persist. Therefore, maintaining robust, free access to such economic indicators is essential for democratic oversight and the development of evidence-based solutions to Argentina’s ongoing economic challenges.
