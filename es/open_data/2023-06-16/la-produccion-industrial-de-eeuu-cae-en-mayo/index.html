La producción industrial de EEUU cae en mayo

Industrial activity shows mixed trends, with manufacturing slightly improving while mining and public services decline. Capacity utilization remains below its long-term average, reflecting broader economic uncertainties. This data coincides with the Federal Reserve’s pause in interest rate hikes, aimed at stabilizing growth and employment. The pause follows ten consecutive increases, leaving rates at their highest since 2007, while unemployment rises and GDP growth slows. Relevance to open_data lies in the need for transparent, real-time access to these economic indicators. Open data facilitates public scrutiny of monetary policy impacts, enabling researchers and citizens to verify official statistics and understand how macroeconomic shifts affect local industries and employment rates.
