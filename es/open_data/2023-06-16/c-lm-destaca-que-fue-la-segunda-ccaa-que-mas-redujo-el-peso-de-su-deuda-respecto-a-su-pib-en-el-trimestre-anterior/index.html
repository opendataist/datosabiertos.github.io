C-LM destaca que fue la segunda CCAA que más redujo el peso de su deuda respecto a su PIB en el trimestre anterior

Castilla-La Mancha significantly reduced its debt-to-GDP ratio, surpassing the national average, while demonstrating superior financial discipline by paying suppliers faster than the national mean. This efficiency highlights the critical importance of transparent, timely public spending data. Open data enables rigorous tracking of fiscal responsibility, fostering trust in government operations and allowing citizens to verify economic improvements.
