La UNMDP lanzó su Portal de Transparencia

La Universidad Nacional de Mar del Plata launched its Transparency Portal, a public platform that enhances institutional accountability and the right to information. This tool allows citizens to access data on personnel, academic offerings, and financial budgets, promoting open government practices. The initiative supports social control over public funds and fosters a culture of transparency. By centralizing accessible, timely information, the university enables stakeholders to monitor resource usage effectively. This aligns with national and international open government standards. This development is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how higher education institutions can operationalize data openness. It shows the practical application of publishing structured public sector data, facilitating civic engagement and reinforcing democratic oversight through accessible digital platforms.
