El IPC subió oficialmente un 7,8% durante mayo en Argentina

Argentina reported a significant consumer price index surge, highlighting severe inflationary pressures driven by essential services and food costs. However, widespread public skepticism emerged, with citizens rejecting the official figures as unrealistic compared to their daily shopping experiences. This disconnect underscores a critical open data challenge: the necessity for statistical transparency and methodological clarity to build public trust and ensure data credibility when official metrics conflict with lived realities.
