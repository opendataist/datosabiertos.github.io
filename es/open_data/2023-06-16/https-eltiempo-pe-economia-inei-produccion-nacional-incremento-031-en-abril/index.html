The article reveals a complex economic reality where aggregate national production saw a slight monthly increase, driven by sectors like mining and commerce, yet remains under pressure with cumulative declines early in the year. Despite this marginal growth, significant structural challenges persist, as evidenced by substantial business closures primarily affecting retail and services. A notable trend is the sharp rise in business deregistrations, indicating fragility in the entrepreneurial ecosystem. The data highlights that small retail enterprises and service providers are disproportionately affected, suggesting that recovery is uneven and that operational sustainability is becoming increasingly difficult for smaller entities facing economic headwinds. This report is vital for open data advocates because it demonstrates how granular, publicly accessible administrative data from institutions like INEI can uncover hidden economic vulnerabilities. By analyzing such datasets, researchers and policymakers can identify specific at-risk sectors and geographical areas, fostering transparent governance and more targeted interventions to support economic resilience and small business survival.
