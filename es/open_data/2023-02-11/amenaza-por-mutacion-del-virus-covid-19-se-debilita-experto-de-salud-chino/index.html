Amenaza por mutación del virus COVID-19 se debilita: experto de salud chino

China’s health expert highlights that while the virus mutates, its lethality has significantly declined, posing a reduced threat to life. This shift suggests future variants are unlikely to become more infectious or revert to highly pathogenic strains. This data is vital for open data initiatives, enabling transparent tracking of epidemiological trends and supporting evidence-based public health policies globally.
