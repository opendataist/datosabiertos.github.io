‘No es para alarmarse’: AMLO minimiza incremento de inflación

Government leaders argue that inflation control requires more than just raising interest rates, emphasizing active support for production and price stability in fuel. This approach highlights the tension between monetary policy autonomy and broader economic intervention. The discourse challenges standard central bank recommendations, suggesting that fostering growth is as critical as managing inflation. This perspective impacts how economic data is interpreted and utilized by policymakers, reflecting a preference for administrative tools over pure monetary adjustments in the Mexican context. Relevant to open data, this article illustrates the political use of economic indicators. It underscores the need for transparent, accessible data to scrutinize official claims and evaluate whether non-monetary interventions are effectively contributing to stability, ensuring accountability in governance.
