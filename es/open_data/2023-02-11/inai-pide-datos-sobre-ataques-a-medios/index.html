INAI pide datos sobre ataques a medios

The INAI ruled that the National Human Rights Commission must disclose data on attacks against media outlets, rejecting its claim of incompetence. This decision ensures transparency regarding violence against journalists, a critical aspect of open data in human rights monitoring. By mandating a comprehensive search across administrative units, the ruling establishes that such statistics belong to public record. It prevents the deflection of requests to other agencies, reinforcing the duty to provide accessible information on threats to free press and civil defenders. This is relevant to open data because it sets a precedent for holding state bodies accountable for publishing granular security statistics. It emphasizes that raw data on abuses must be freely available to society, facilitating research and accountability rather than being withheld under procedural arguments.
