Bolivia realizará simulacro de Censo de Población y Vivienda - Noticias Prensa Latina

Bolivia’s national statistics institute confirms that preparations for the upcoming population and housing census are in their final stages, with volunteer recruitment exceeding targets and extensive training underway. This rigorous logistical setup ensures that millions of homes nationwide will be accurately surveyed, marking a critical step in generating reliable demographic data. The exercise serves as a vital rehearsal for citizens to understand the process before the official count begins. The government emphasizes that the census protocol was developed in coordination with all regional governments, explicitly denying claims that it alters territorial boundaries. Officials view political opposition as misinformation tactics aimed at disrupting this essential national challenge. By clarifying the technical nature of the exercise, authorities aim to maintain social cohesion and ensure public cooperation during the data collection period. This event is relevant to open data as it represents the foundation for a comprehensive, publicly accessible national database. Accurate census results enable transparent governance, informed policy-making, and resource allocation based on verified realities rather than speculation. Ultimately, this massive data collection effort underscores the importance of standardized, high-quality official statistics for fostering an informed society and supporting evidence-based decision-making across all sectors.
