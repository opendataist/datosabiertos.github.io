Lana supera a la carne ovina en exportaciones

Uruguay’s wool sector dominates early 2024 exports, surpassing meat sales in both value and volume. This shift highlights the enduring market preference for fine fibers, particularly ultra-fine wools, which maintain higher profitability despite broader industry fluctuations. The surge in raw wool volumes signals robust international demand, reshaping the national agricultural export balance toward fiber-based products. China emerges as the primary driver of this growth, absorbing nearly half of all wool exports with exponential volume increases. This concentration underscores the critical importance of Asian markets in sustaining Uruguay’s wool industry. Meanwhile, diverse product categories show mixed results, indicating that while raw fiber demand is strong, processed goods face varying levels of international competitiveness. This dynamic is highly relevant to open data, as transparent, accessible trade statistics are essential for stakeholders to monitor market trends and policy impacts. Reliable public data enables producers to anticipate demand shifts, optimize supply chains, and make informed decisions regarding quality investment. Furthermore, open datasets facilitate academic and commercial analysis, fostering innovation and strategic development within the global textile and agricultural sectors.
