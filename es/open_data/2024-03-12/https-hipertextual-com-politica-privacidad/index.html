Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This document outlines the data processing activities and privacy standards of Hipertextual, a Spanish digital media entity. It establishes the legal framework under which the company collects and manages user information, emphasizing that data collection is often mandatory for service provision. The text details the specific types of data gathered, primarily cookies and usage statistics, and clarifies that these are essential for delivering the website’s functionality and personalized content. The core mechanism involves the use of third-party analytical and advertising services, notably from Google, which process data in the United States. This raises significant implications for data sovereignty and privacy, as users are subject to varying levels of protection depending on their geographical location. The policy explains that while some users benefit from higher European standards, others may face weaker protections during international data transfers, highlighting the complex jurisdictional challenges inherent in modern digital services. This text is highly relevant to open data discussions as it exemplifies the opacity surrounding commercial data ecosystems. It demonstrates how private entities utilize aggregated behavioral data for profiling and commercial gain, often obscuring the specific data flows through complex legal disclaimers. Understanding such privacy policies is crucial for analyzing the transparency of data practices, the extent of corporate data harvesting, and the regulatory gaps that persist despite legal frameworks like GDPR.
