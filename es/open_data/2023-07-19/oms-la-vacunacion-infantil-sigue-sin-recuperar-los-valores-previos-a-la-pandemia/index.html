OMS: La vacunación infantil sigue sin recuperar los valores previos a la pandemia

Global child vaccination rates have not recovered to pre-pandemic levels, with stagnation or decline persisting in numerous countries. Despite incremental progress in some large nations, significant inequalities remain hidden by average figures, revealing a stark disparity between high-resource and low-income regions. The report highlights that millions of children still miss essential routine immunizations, particularly in areas prone to ongoing setbacks. This lack of progress undermines collective health security and demonstrates that the pandemic's disruption had lasting, uneven effects on public health infrastructure worldwide. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of transparent, granular datasets to track health equity accurately. Without accessible, high-quality open data, policymakers cannot effectively identify marginalized populations or allocate resources to address the persistent gaps in global immunization coverage.
