This article highlights a permanent recruitment drive by Mexico’s Secretariat of National Defense for youth aged 18 to 29. It outlines eligibility criteria, required documentation, and the benefits of joining the Army or National Guard, such as healthcare and career advancement opportunities. Regarding open_data, this text is relevant because it serves as a clear example of public sector transparency and citizen accessibility.
