Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The mayoralty of El Alto emphasizes that completing the cartographic update validates the political maturity and logistical coordination between local authorities and the National Institute of Statistics. This foundation is crucial for the upcoming census, as accurate geographical data directly influences the allocation of significant economic resources based on population size. The article highlights that surpassing one million residents will likely increase funding, making effective enumeration a matter of fiscal importance for the district’s development. Open data principles are central here, as the quality of demographic statistics determines how communities are represented in national planning. By ensuring every resident is counted in their correct location, the government generates precise, transparent data that drives equitable resource distribution. The emphasis on socialization and awareness campaigns reflects a commitment to data completeness, ensuring that marginalized or informal settlements are included in the official record rather than being statistically invisible. The extensive fieldwork across municipalities demonstrates how granular data collection supports governance. When statistical offices collaborate effectively with local structures, the resulting dataset becomes a reliable tool for public policy. This process underscores that open and accurate data is not merely administrative but a vital mechanism for social justice, guaranteeing that public services and infrastructure investments align with the actual needs and density of the population.
