La actividad económica cayó 5,5% interanual en mayo

Argentina’s economy contracted in May, marking a second consecutive month of decline according to INDEC’s latest economic activity estimates. This downturn reflects a broader negative trend in the first five months, driven primarily by significant drops in agriculture and fishing sectors, despite modest growth in industrial and service industries. The data reveals a stark divergence across economic activities, with mining and hospitality showing resilience while utilities and transportation weakened. This mixed performance highlights the uneven recovery and structural challenges within different segments of the national economy, impacting overall production and consumption patterns. This report is crucial for open data communities as it underscores the importance of accessing raw, disaggregated economic indicators. Transparent, timely statistics enable independent analysis of sectoral trends, fostering better accountability and allowing researchers to verify official narratives against granular dataset entries for deeper insights.
