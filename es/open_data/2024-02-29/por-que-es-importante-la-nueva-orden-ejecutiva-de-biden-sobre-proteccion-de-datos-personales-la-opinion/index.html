¿Por qué es importante la nueva orden ejecutiva de Biden sobre protección de datos personales? - La Opinión

President Joe Biden has issued a significant executive order to strengthen the privacy protections of sensitive American personal data. This initiative specifically targets high-risk categories such as genomic, biometric, health, and geolocation information, aiming to prevent unauthorized access by foreign entities. The administration views this as a critical step in safeguarding individual rights against intrusions that could lead to surveillance, fraud, or blackmail. The order primarily addresses national security risks posed by countries like China and Russia, which are scrutinized for potential data exploitation. By restricting how these foreign groups can acquire sensitive information, the policy seeks to prevent the use of personal data for coercion, intimidation of dissidents, and compromising national security figures. This measure highlights the global threat of data trafficking and its impact on civil liberties and democratic freedoms. This development is highly relevant to open data because it establishes strict boundaries on data flows, emphasizing that not all data should be freely accessible or transferable. It underscores the necessity of robust privacy frameworks that balance transparency with security. Understanding these protective mechanisms is essential for developers and policymakers who design data ecosystems, ensuring that openness does not compromise individual safety or national integrity.
