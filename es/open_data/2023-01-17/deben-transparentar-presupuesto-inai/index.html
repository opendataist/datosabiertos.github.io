Deben transparentar presupuesto: INAI

The INAI ordered Semarnat to disclose budget details and results for the "Limpiemos Nuestro México" campaign, criticizing the agency's failure to provide relevant data despite holding underlying contracts. This ruling highlights a critical transparency gap in government environmental programs, emphasizing that administrative oversight is essential for validating public initiatives. Mexico’s massive waste production underscores the urgent need for accountable environmental management. By mandating the release of this information, the decision reinforces that citizens have the right to verify how public funds support private-led initiatives involving state participation. This case is vital for open data because it establishes that governments cannot claim information is nonexistent when underlying records exist. It strengthens the principle that accessible, comprehensive data is required to assess program effectiveness, ensuring that environmental efforts are subject to rigorous public scrutiny and factual verification.
