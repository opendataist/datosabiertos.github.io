El número de matrimonios en China cayó en 2024 a su mínimo desde 1980 - Mundo - ABC Color

China faces a declining marriage rate, reversing a post-pandemic recovery. This trend stems from demographic shifts, gender imbalances, and rising living costs, alongside evolving social attitudes toward union. The government recognizes this crisis as critical to national stability, emphasizing the urgent need to lower family expenses and boost birth rates. These measures aim to counteract sustained population contractions, marking the first sustained decline since the early 1960s. This data highlights the importance of open data in monitoring societal health. Transparent statistics allow policymakers to identify structural challenges like shrinking workforces. Accurate, accessible information is vital for crafting effective social policies that address demographic transitions and ensure long-term economic resilience.
