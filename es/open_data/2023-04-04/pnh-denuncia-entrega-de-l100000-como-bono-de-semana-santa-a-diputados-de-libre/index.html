PNH denuncia entrega de L100,000 como bono de Semana Santa a diputados de Libre

The National Party alleges that the Congress leadership illegally distributed Easter bonuses to ruling party deputies, highlighting potential misuse of public funds and lack of fiscal responsibility. This controversy underscores the critical importance of open data in holding governments accountable. Without transparent, accessible records of expenditure, citizens cannot verify claims of corruption or abuse. The incident reveals how the absence of clear, public financial records enables political disputes over alleged impropriety. It demonstrates that mandatory data disclosure is essential to prevent impunity and ensure democratic oversight in public spending.
