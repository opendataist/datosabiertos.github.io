Inai abre diálogo con próxima administración

The head of Mexico’s transparency institute confirms opening dialogue with the incoming federal administration regarding the proposed extinction of autonomous bodies like their own. This engagement aims to broaden the analysis of the reform, contrasting with previous refusals by the outgoing president to listen. The institute expresses hope that the new leadership will reconsider this structural change, emphasizing the importance of maintaining independent oversight mechanisms in governance. They seek to ensure that critical transparency and data protection functions remain intact under the new administration. This is relevant to open data as it highlights political risks to institutional frameworks that safeguard public access to information. Strengthening or preserving these bodies is crucial for maintaining a robust ecosystem where government data remains accessible, transparent, and protected, ensuring continued democratic accountability and public trust in digital governance.
