Consejo de Autonomías señala que el INE socializará resultado del censo por departamento

The National Council of Autonomies has officially endorsed the preliminary census results, validating the technical quality and accuracy of the work conducted by the National Institute of Statistics. This endorsement signifies a critical milestone in Bolivia’s data governance, as it confirms that the population figures meet rigorous international standards and technical parameters. By agreeing that the census was free from political interference, state authorities have established a trusted, unified dataset, which is essential for restoring public confidence in official statistics and ensuring that demographic information reflects reality rather than partisan interests. These validated figures will directly drive the implementation of a new tax revenue distribution system starting in September. This shift highlights the practical importance of accurate open data in fiscal federalism, as precise population counts determine how resources are allocated among departments and indigenous autonomies. The immediate application of these results underscores that statistical data is not merely informational but a functional tool for equitable economic planning, ensuring that funding matches the actual needs and size of each territorial entity. Stakeholders are now demanding granular access to this data at municipal and regional levels to facilitate detailed analysis and further dialogue. This demand for transparency and localized data availability emphasizes the broader relevance of open data principles. By sharing specific departmental and municipal breakdowns, the government enables local entities to plan effectively and engage in productive negotiations, demonstrating how accessible, high-quality statistical data fosters democratic accountability and informed decision-making across all levels of government.
