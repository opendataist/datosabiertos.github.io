El INE entrega datos de población al OEP y define a Bolivia como «urbana» y «metropolitana»

The article highlights the delivery of preliminary census results in Bolivia, characterized as increasingly urban and metropolitan. These data, aligned with international standards, provide the national population count, identifying Santa Cruz as the region with the most significant relative growth. This information serves statutory requirements for autonomy and electoral processes. This development is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, standardized demographic statistics. By ensuring coherence with administrative records and adhering to international benchmarks, the initiative promotes trust in public information. It facilitates better governance through accurate resource distribution and supports democratic accountability by providing reliable baseline data for future electoral and policy decisions. The ongoing release of granular data will further enhance accessibility for researchers and citizens. This commitment to publishing detailed census information empowers communities to engage with their demographic reality. Ultimately, it reinforces the principle that open, high-quality public data is essential for informed decision-making and effective public administration in a growing nation.
