El INE señala que Bolivia es "urbana" y "metropolitana" y envía los resultados del censo al Ejecutivo y TSE

The National Institute of Statistics confirms Bolivia is becoming increasingly urban and metropolitan, with significant population growth concentrated in central departments like Santa Cruz, La Paz, and Cochabamba. This shift requires the government to adjust the distribution of legislative seats and fiscal resources, fundamentally altering how public services and political representation are allocated across the country. Crucially, the Institute emphasizes the transparency and reliability of these findings, validating them through international endorsements and contrasting them with less accurate local estimates. By defending the methodological rigor of the census, the data serves as an authoritative benchmark that minimizes political disputes and establishes a clear, factual basis for national development planning and resource management. This article highlights the critical role of open data in democratic governance. Publishing verified demographic statistics allows citizens and institutions to access objective information, fostering accountability and ensuring that political decisions regarding territorial distribution and taxation are grounded in reality rather than speculation.
