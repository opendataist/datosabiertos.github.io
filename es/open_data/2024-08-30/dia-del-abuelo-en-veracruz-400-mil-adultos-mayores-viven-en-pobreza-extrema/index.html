Día del Abuelo: En Veracruz, 400 mil adultos mayores viven en pobreza extrema

Veracruz experiences a significant demographic shift, with its adult population aged 60 and older growing steadily. This data highlights a clear gender disparity in longevity, where women consistently outnumber men in senior age brackets, indicating distinct social dynamics that require tailored policy responses. However, this aging demographic faces severe economic challenges. A substantial portion of these individuals lives in extreme poverty, with Veracruz ranking second nationally in this regard. These statistics reveal urgent social vulnerabilities that current welfare measures must address to ensure adequate support for the elderly population. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly available statistics from INEGI and CONEVAL drive evidence-based decision-making. By making such census and poverty evaluation data transparent, governments and researchers can analyze trends, identify vulnerable groups, and design effective social programs grounded in factual, accessible information.
