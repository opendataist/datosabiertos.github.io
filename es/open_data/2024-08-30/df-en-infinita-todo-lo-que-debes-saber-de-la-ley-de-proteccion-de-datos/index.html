DF en Infinita: Todo lo que debes saber de la Ley de Protección de Datos

Chile has enacted a data protection law mandating an independent agency to oversee compliance. This regulation restricts personal data usage to specific, consensual purposes, ensuring transparency and confidentiality for citizens. Businesses in sectors like telecommunications and finance face strict compliance requirements and significant penalties for violations. While industry groups criticize the severity of fines, others view the measure as a necessary modernization step for the digital economy. This development is crucial for open data as it establishes the legal framework governing information flow. By clarifying how personal data can be shared and protected, it balances public privacy rights with the ethical requirements for accessing and utilizing data in a democratic society.
