INEI realizará Encuesta Económica Anual 2023: ¿Qué empresas están obligadas a participar?

The INEI has authorized the Annual Economic Survey 2023, targeting businesses across key sectors like commerce, manufacturing, and services. The primary goal is to gather reliable financial data for constructing robust macroeconomic aggregates and sectoral indicators, ensuring accurate statistical availability for national planning. Participating companies are obligated to register their 2022 economic and financial information via a secure web application. Access requires specific credentials provided by the institute, and deadlines are staggered based on tax registration numbers to manage submission volumes efficiently throughout the year. This process is vital for open data because it generates the foundational dataset required for transparent, evidence-based public policy and private sector analysis. By mandating standardized reporting from diverse industries, the survey enhances the quality and accessibility of official statistics. However, non-compliance risks penalties, emphasizing the legal framework’s role in maintaining data integrity and ensuring citizens and institutions can trust the aggregated economic indicators derived from these submissions.
