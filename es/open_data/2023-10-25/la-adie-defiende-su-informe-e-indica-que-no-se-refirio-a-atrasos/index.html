La ADIE defiende su informe e indica que no se refirió a atrasos

The Dominican Association of the Electrical Industry asserts that its first-half 2023 sector performance report relies on official state data, refuting claims of mixed indicators. The association highlights positive generation results and timely debt payments, emphasizing that honoring these financial commitments is crucial for the electrical system’s sustainability. This dispute underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible official data to validate private sector analyses. Reliable public information enables stakeholders to accurately assess industry health and resolve conflicting narratives. Consequently, the article highlights how open data serves as the essential foundation for accountability and trust between government bodies and private entities, ensuring informed decision-making in critical infrastructure sectors.
