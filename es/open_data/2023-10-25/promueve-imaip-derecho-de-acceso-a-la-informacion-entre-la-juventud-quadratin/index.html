Promueve Imaip derecho de acceso a la información entre la juventud - Quadratín

The University Weeks for Transparency initiative successfully bridges the gap between institutional mandates and student empowerment. By engaging the academic community in debates and training, these programs foster a robust culture of transparency and data protection. This approach ensures that young citizens understand and can actively exercise their rights to access public information and safeguard their personal data in both academic and daily contexts. This effort is particularly relevant to open data because it highlights the foundational importance of civic literacy. Open data initiatives rely not just on government disclosure, but on an informed public capable of utilizing these resources. When students are educated on transparency laws and data rights, they become effective agents for accountability, driving better governance and encouraging the practical application of open datasets to solve community problems. Ultimately, the event underscores that transparency is a shared responsibility requiring continuous dialogue. Strengthening the legal and cultural frameworks surrounding information access ensures that open data becomes a tool for meaningful participation rather than mere administrative compliance. Empowering the next generation with these skills creates a more resilient society capable of demanding openness and effectively leveraging public information for the common good.
