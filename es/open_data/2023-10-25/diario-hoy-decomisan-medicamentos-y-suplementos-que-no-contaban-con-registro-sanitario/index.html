The National Youth Secretariat partners with educational institutions to expand academic opportunities, addressing current youth interests in technology and commerce. This initiative prioritizes accessible virtual programs, ensuring equitable access for students across the country regardless of their geographic location. Selection criteria rely on transparent academic performance metrics, requiring specific grade averages for partial and full scholarships. By streamlining the application process through simple documentation, the program aims to reduce bureaucratic barriers and promote merit-based inclusion for young people seeking higher education. This approach is vital for open data as it demonstrates how public entities can leverage transparent, accessible information systems to democratize educational resources. It highlights the importance of clear, publicly available criteria and digital accessibility in fostering trust and ensuring that marginalized populations can effectively participate in national development programs.
