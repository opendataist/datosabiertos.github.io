Mexico’s inflation has significantly decreased since late 2022, driven primarily by declines in food and energy prices. However, core underlying inflation remains sticky, moving at a slower pace and suggesting that interest rate cuts by the central bank will be delayed. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how publicly accessible economic metrics enable independent expert analysis. By visualizing trends in consumer price indices, stakeholders can better assess monetary policy implications and economic health without relying solely on official narratives.
