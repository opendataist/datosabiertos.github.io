UGT C-LM valora el dato del IPC pero se muestra preocupado especialmente por el precio de la vivienda

The article highlights a disconnect between modest wage growth and soaring housing costs in Castilla-La Mancha, rendering homeownership unattainable for many workers despite improved collective bargaining outcomes. This economic imbalance underscores the critical need for open data to transparently track price evolution and wage negotiations, empowering citizens and policymakers to identify systemic inequalities. Transparent, accessible data is essential for designing effective housing policies that align costs with household incomes, ensuring that economic recovery genuinely protects purchasing power rather than leaving vulnerable populations behind.
