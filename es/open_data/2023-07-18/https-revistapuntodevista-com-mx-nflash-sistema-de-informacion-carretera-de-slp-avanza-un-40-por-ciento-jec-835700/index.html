Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The San Luis Potosí government is developing an open road information system to enhance transparency and citizen engagement. This platform aims to simplify infrastructure repair requests and provide public access to geospatial data. By visualizing project details and investment impact, the initiative fosters accountability and informed decision-making. It strengthens technological innovation while ensuring that residents can effectively monitor state road works. This effort is highly relevant to open data as it transforms government records into accessible, actionable resources. It exemplifies how proactive data sharing empowers communities and improves administrative efficiency through digital inclusion.
