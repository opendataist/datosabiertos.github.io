The lack of commissioners at INAI has paralyzed its sessions, creating a massive backlog of unresolved cases. This institutional paralysis prevents the body from addressing failures at local levels, directly undermining the legal certainty and investment confidence crucial for economic stability. Transparency is vital for fostering trust in public institutions. When key bodies like INAI falter, it creates significant uncertainty that negatively impacts investor relations and erodes public faith in the government's ability to guarantee rights and enforce legal standards effectively. This situation highlights the critical role of open data in maintaining democratic accountability. Robust, functioning transparency agencies are essential for ensuring citizens can access information and hold power accountable, forming the backbone of a transparent and trustworthy society.
