Las ventas minoristas en EEUU superan previsiones en marzo y alejan los recortes de tipos de la Fed

US retail sales exceeded expectations in March, signaling robust consumer resilience. This strong growth suggests the economy is maintaining momentum, which complicates the Federal Reserve's monetary policy decisions. The data implies that inflationary pressures may persist longer than anticipated. Consequently, interest rate cuts are likely to be delayed, affecting global financial markets and investment strategies that rely on stable monetary conditions. Reliable open datasets enable independent verification and accurate forecasting, fostering trust in market mechanisms and aiding policymakers in making informed decisions based on real-time evidence.
