Recuperación febrero uso del Bowí; 747 mil pasajeros: Inegi

The INEGI reports on Chihuahua's urban transport, highlighting data inconsistencies such as naming the Bowí system as "Vivebús." This oversight reveals critical gaps in official open data regarding public transit identification and accuracy. Such discrepancies undermine the reliability and usability of statistical data for researchers and citizens, emphasizing the need for rigorous data validation in public sector releases.
