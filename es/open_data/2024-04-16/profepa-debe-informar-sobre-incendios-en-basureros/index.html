Profepa debe informar sobre incendios en basureros

The INAI mandated Profepa to assume jurisdiction and disclose detailed data on landfill fires from 2018 to 2024. This directive emphasizes that responsible waste management requires coordinated efforts among all government levels and the public, ensuring authorities actively monitor and report environmental violations. Transparency in surveillance actions is crucial for raising public awareness about proper waste disposal. The commission highlighted that educating citizens is fundamental to preventing planetary contamination, while states must take necessary steps to mitigate the damage caused by improper waste handling. This case is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of accessible environmental statistics. By requiring specific incident details, the ruling reinforces the principle that citizens must have transparent access to information regarding ecological risks to foster accountability and informed public participation in sustainability efforts.
