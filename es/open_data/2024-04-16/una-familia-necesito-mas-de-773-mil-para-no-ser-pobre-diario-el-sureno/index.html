Una familia necesitó más de $773 mil para no ser pobre - Diario El Sureño

The significant rise in the cost of basic goods highlights the deepening economic crisis, making it increasingly difficult for families to afford essential food and general necessities. This trend directly correlates with high poverty and indigence rates, illustrating how inflation erodes purchasing power and pushes more households into destitution without adequate income support. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent, official statistics empower researchers and civil society to analyze structural inequalities effectively. By making such detailed economic indicators accessible, stakeholders can advocate for informed policy decisions aimed at mitigating poverty and addressing the root causes of social exclusion in Argentina.
