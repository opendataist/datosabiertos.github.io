The article details daily fluctuations in electricity prices, highlighting periods of high costs during evening and morning peaks, while identifying afternoon hours with negative rates as optimal for consumption. This volatility underscores the importance of strategic energy management based on real-time market data. Transparency in these pricing mechanisms is essential for consumers to make informed decisions about when to use high-consumption appliances. Access to clear, timely information empowers users to reduce expenses and supports broader energy efficiency goals. This is relevant to open data because public access to hourly electricity price data enables individuals and businesses to optimize usage. Open datasets foster market transparency, encouraging sustainable practices and allowing for the development of tools that help society adapt to dynamic energy markets.
