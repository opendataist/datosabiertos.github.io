El libro del Padrón Municipal de Habitantes de 1905 vuelve al Ayuntamiento tras su restauración - Águilas - murcia.com

The municipality of Águilas successfully restored and digitized its 1905 municipal census, securing this vital historical asset for future generations. This initiative addresses the severe physical degradation caused by time and improper handling, ensuring the document’s long-term survival and integrity. The digitization, achieved through regional heritage projects, allows unrestricted public access to the data without risking the fragile original. This balance between preservation and accessibility highlights the critical role of digital technologies in safeguarding cultural memory while democratizing information. This case is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how structured archival processes enable the transformation of static historical records into accessible, machine-readable digital assets. It exemplifies the transition from physical limitation to open availability, fostering transparency and research opportunities.
