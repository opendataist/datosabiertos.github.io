El I Foro Urbano de la Región conoce los 100 proyectos que conforman la Estrategia Murcia 2030 del Ayuntamiento

Murcia presents its 2030 Urban Strategy, centering on an open government model that actively incorporates citizen contributions into strategic planning. This approach ensures that local development is transparent, collaborative, and aligned with diverse societal interests, thereby strengthening civic engagement in municipal governance. The implementation relies on securing diverse funding sources while rigorously assessing technical and budgetary viability. This process highlights the necessity of financial transparency and strategic resource management in modern urban planning, ensuring that projects are sustainable and responsibly executed over time. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it underscores the critical link between administrative agility, public participation, and accessible information. By fostering an open administration, the city promotes data-driven decision-making and accountability, demonstrating how transparency empowers communities and enhances trust in public institutions.
