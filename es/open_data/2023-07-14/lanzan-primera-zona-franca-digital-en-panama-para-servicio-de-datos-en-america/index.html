Lanzan primera zona franca digital en Panamá para servicio de datos en América

Panama has officially launched the Panama Digital Gateway, establishing the nation’s first digital free zone and a strategic hub for data services across Central America, the Andean region, and the Caribbean. By leveraging existing free zone benefits for digital infrastructure, this initiative marks a significant shift from traditional logistical and manufacturing focuses toward a diversified, digital-driven economy. This move underscores Panama’s ambition to solidify its status as a premier global communications nexus, capitalizing on its unique geographical position at the bridge between the Atlantic and Pacific oceans. The center operates as a key landing point for submarine cables, significantly enhancing data concurrence efficiency and reducing costs for regional operators. Designed with a strong emphasis on sustainability, the facility features low energy consumption and serves a broad spectrum of clients, including corporations, scientific entities, and NGOs. This architectural and operational approach not only supports high-performance data outsourcing and storage but also aligns with modern environmental standards, offering a robust, green infrastructure that attracts international investment and technological partnerships. This development is highly relevant to open data as it creates a transparent, accessible, and efficient physical foundation for data exchange in Latin America. By providing open infrastructure for diverse sectors, it facilitates greater collaboration and innovation in information management. The gateway’s role in connecting major global internet flows and its inclusive access model promote the free flow of information, enabling researchers, businesses, and civil society to leverage high-quality data resources more effectively, thereby fostering a more open and interconnected digital ecosystem.
