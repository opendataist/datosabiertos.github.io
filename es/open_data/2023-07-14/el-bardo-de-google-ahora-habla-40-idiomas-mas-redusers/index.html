El bardo de Google ahora habla 40 idiomas más - RedUSERS

Google’s Bard now supports dozens of languages, reaching Europe and Brazil, with text-to-speech and image interpretation features enhancing user interaction. These expansions demonstrate the rapid evolution of multilingual AI capabilities. Crucially, Google leverages public access data to train models like Bard and Translate. This practice highlights how major tech firms rely on accessible information to refine their systems. This is relevant to open data as it underscores the critical role of publicly available information in advancing artificial intelligence. The case illustrates the ongoing dependence of corporate AI development on open data sources for continuous model improvement.
