Capinota serves as the test site for Bolivia’s experimental census, allowing authorities to refine data collection procedures before the national count. This trial ensures that instruments and timelines are optimized for accuracy and efficiency. The selection highlights the strategic value of pilot studies in validating statistical methodologies. By testing processes in a representative municipality, the National Institute of Statistics can identify flaws and improve operational workflows prior to full implementation. This initiative is crucial for open data because transparent, reliable census foundations enable high-quality public datasets. Accurate demographic information supports evidence-based policy and fosters trust in official statistics, which is essential for data-driven governance and civic engagement.
