EEUU abrió investigación sobre OpenAI y ChatGPT para determinar si pone en riesgo los usuarios

The Federal Trade Commission has launched a comprehensive investigation into OpenAI to determine if ChatGPT has violated consumer protection laws. By scrutinizing the company’s data handling practices and training methodologies, regulators aim to assess whether the widespread popularity of the tool has come at the cost of user privacy and data security. This inquiry highlights the critical importance of transparency in how artificial intelligence systems process and protect personal information. A central concern involves the phenomenon of "hallucinations," where the AI generates factually incorrect but plausible-sounding content. High-profile incidents, such as false claims about real individuals, demonstrate significant risks to consumer reputation and trust. These errors underscore the urgent need for accountability in AI development, as unchecked inaccuracies can cause tangible harm to individuals and erode public confidence in emerging technologies. This case is highly relevant to the open data movement because it questions the balance between innovation and ethical responsibility. It emphasizes that open access to data must be accompanied by rigorous safeguards against misuse and misinformation. Ultimately, the investigation serves as a precedent for how AI companies must align their operations with strict consumer protection standards, ensuring that technological advancement does not compromise individual rights or data integrity.
