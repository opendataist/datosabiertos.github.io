El tiempo es el principal escollo de Milei

The article highlights that Javier Milei’s initial popularity is declining as his austerity measures, particularly public service tariff hikes, begin to impact the purchasing power of his own voter base. This shift is particularly concerning because these economic burdens are falling on the middle class rather than the political elite as originally promised, creating a risk of social and political backlash. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible, and timely official statistics to monitor public sentiment and policy impacts. By analyzing real-time opinion and economic indicators, researchers and citizens can better understand the true social cost of governance decisions.
