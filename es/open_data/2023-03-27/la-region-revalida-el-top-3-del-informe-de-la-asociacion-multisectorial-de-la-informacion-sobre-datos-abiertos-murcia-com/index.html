La Región revalida el 'Top 3' del Informe de la Asociación Multisectorial de la Información sobre 'Datos Abiertos' - murcia.com

The Region of Murcia has once again joined the prestigious 'Top 3 ASEDIE' ranking, demonstrating consistent compliance with agreements to publish open data. By successfully releasing key datasets on agricultural societies, energy certifications, and industrial zones, the regional government reaffirms its commitment to transparency and good governance practices within the public administration framework. This achievement highlights Murcia’s pivotal role in the expanding infomediary sector, which drives economic growth and job creation through the value-added reuse of public information. The region stands out not only for its immediate adherence to data accessibility standards but also for its significant proportional growth in this data-driven industry over the last decade. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how structured transparency initiatives directly stimulate the data economy. By harmonizing public data sources and making them available for reuse, regional authorities prove that open data is a powerful engine for generating wealth, fostering innovation, and empowering society through shared knowledge and services.
