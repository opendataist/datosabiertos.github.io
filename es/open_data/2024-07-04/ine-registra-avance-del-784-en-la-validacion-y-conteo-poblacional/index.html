INE registra avance del 78,4% en la validación y conteo poblacional

The INE is advancing its post-census data processing, achieving significant progress in validating population counts and scanning questionnaires. This phase ensures the accuracy and international standards of Bolivia’s demographic "snapshot," moving from field collection to rigorous technical verification before final publication. Standardized procedures, including geographic validation and optical character recognition, are crucial for maintaining data integrity. By cross-referencing information for consistency and quality, the institute can reliably analyze completeness. This technical rigor transforms raw census materials into reliable statistical evidence for decision-making. This update is vital for open_data as it demonstrates how large-scale government data undergoes systematic cleaning and digitization. Transparent reporting on processing milestones builds public trust in official statistics. It highlights the complexity of transforming primary census inputs into accessible, high-quality public datasets that inform policy and research.
