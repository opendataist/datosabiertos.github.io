Crisis y reconfiguración laboral: el empleo autónomo crece ante la caída del trabajo formal

The Argentine labor market is undergoing a profound structural shift driven by economic crisis and state reduction policies. Formal salaried jobs are declining sharply, while self-employment and the informal sector are expanding rapidly as essential survival strategies for workers facing precarious conditions and reduced public sector opportunities. This trend highlights a significant implication for open data initiatives, as traditional official statistics often fail to capture the true scale of the informal economy. Accurate, accessible data on non-registered employment is crucial to understanding labor realities, yet such information remains fragmented or difficult to obtain, hindering effective social policy design and resource allocation for vulnerable populations. Consequently, empowering civil society and researchers with better open data tools is vital to monitor these informal labor dynamics. Improving data transparency allows for a clearer picture of income distribution and demographic participation, ensuring that marginalized groups, including women and youth, are not invisible in national economic planning and advocacy efforts.
