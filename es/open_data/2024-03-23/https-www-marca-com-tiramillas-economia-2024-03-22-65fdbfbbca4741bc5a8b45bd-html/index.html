Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Electricity prices exhibit extreme volatility, dropping to zero during midday hours while spiking significantly in the evening. This stark contrast highlights the necessity of shifting energy consumption away from peak demand periods to minimize costs. The data reveals specific temporal windows where energy is either abundant or scarce. Users can optimize their schedules by utilizing appliances during the zero-cost afternoon slot, avoiding high-expense evening hours when supply dynamics drive prices upward. This information is crucial for open_data initiatives focused on energy transparency. By making granular pricing data publicly accessible, stakeholders can promote informed decision-making, encourage renewable integration, and support policies that balance supply and demand through shared, raw market insights.
