Junta de Aviación Civil recibe reconocimiento de la Digeig por altos estándares en transparencia y datos abiertos

The Civil Aviation Junta received official recognition for its adherence to transparency standards and guaranteeing public access to information. This acknowledgment highlights the successful implementation of open data principles within government institutions, demonstrating a firm commitment to ethical governance and managerial clarity under current leadership policies. This achievement underscores the legal obligation to publish standardized data through official portals, directly supporting the national framework for free access to public information. By consistently meeting high-quality metrics, the agency proves that structured data release is essential for institutional accountability and trust. The event also celebrated journalistic investigations leveraging these released government datasets, emphasizing the practical value of open data in society. This relevance illustrates how accessible public information empowers independent research and fosters integrity in data usage, particularly as artificial intelligence reshapes information analysis landscapes.
