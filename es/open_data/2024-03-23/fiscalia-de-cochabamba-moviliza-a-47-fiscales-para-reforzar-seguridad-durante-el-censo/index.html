Fiscalía de Cochabamba moviliza a 47 fiscales para reforzar seguridad durante el Censo

Cochabamba deploys 47 prosecutors and forensic experts on 24/7 shifts to ensure security during the national census. This proactive measure aims to guarantee immediate legal response to any potential criminal incidents, maintaining public order throughout the data collection period. Such institutional coordination ensures the integrity and safety of the census process, preventing disruptions that could compromise data quality. It highlights the critical role of judicial oversight in supporting national statistical operations. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how legal frameworks protect the reliability of public datasets. By preventing crime, authorities safeguard the authenticity of the census, which serves as a foundational source for transparent, evidence-based public policy and accountable governance.
