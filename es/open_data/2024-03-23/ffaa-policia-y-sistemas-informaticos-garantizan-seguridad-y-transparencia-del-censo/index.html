FFAA, Policía y sistemas informáticos garantizan seguridad y transparencia del censo

The 2024 Bolivian Census represents a significant evolution in public data management by integrating advanced artificial intelligence for data processing. This technological leap ensures higher accuracy and efficiency in digitizing responses, marking a modernization of the country’s statistical infrastructure. By reducing human error and streamlining data structuring, the state enhances the reliability of the demographic information generated. Security and transparency are paramount, with the military and police ensuring the physical safety of personnel and material throughout the process. The use of QR codes enables remote tracking of census boxes, guaranteeing the integrity of the physical documents from collection to storage. This multi-layered security approach, supported by international observers, builds public trust in the neutrality and rigor of the census operation. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the foundational role of secure, accurate, and modernized data collection in establishing trustworthy public datasets. The implementation of automated, high-precision systems and transparent oversight mechanisms demonstrates how rigorous technical and logistical standards are prerequisites for producing open data that is both usable and credible for societal development and policy-making.
