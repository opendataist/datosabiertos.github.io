Argentina’s economy reflects a fragmented recovery, with primary and service sectors driving growth while manufacturing and utilities contract. This divergence highlights structural imbalances, showing that recovery is uneven across different economic pillars. The decline in investment and public consumption suggests weakening domestic demand, posing challenges for sustainable expansion despite modest progress in private spending. These trends indicate that short-term gains may not be sufficient to stabilize the broader economic foundation. This analysis is relevant to open_data as it illustrates the importance of transparent, granular data in diagnosing complex economic shifts. Accessible sector-level metrics enable policymakers and researchers to accurately track these disparities and formulate evidence-based strategies for inclusive growth.
