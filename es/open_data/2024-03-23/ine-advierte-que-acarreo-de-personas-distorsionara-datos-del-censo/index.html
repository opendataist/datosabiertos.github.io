INE advierte que acarreo de personas distorsionará datos del Censo

The National Statistics Institute warns that the mass movement of people before the census will distort data collection. This displacement occurs because no regulations currently prohibit such travel, making it difficult to control. Consequently, statistical accuracy suffers as individuals move away from their usual residences. The lack of legal frameworks to restrict this mobility undermines the reliability of population and housing records. This highlights open data relevance, as mobile populations challenge traditional static survey methods. Accurate open datasets require robust strategies to account for transient groups, ensuring public information remains trustworthy despite logistical challenges.
