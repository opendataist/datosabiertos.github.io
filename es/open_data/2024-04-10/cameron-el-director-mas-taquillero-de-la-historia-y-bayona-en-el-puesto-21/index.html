Cameron, el director más taquillero de la historia, y Bayona en el puesto 21

James Cameron dominates global box office history with multiple top-tier films, while Spanish director Juan Antonio Bayona achieves notable international recognition. This data highlights the current landscape of cinematic success, where modern blockbusters accumulate record-breaking revenues due to factors like higher ticket prices, contrasting with older classics when inflation is not considered. This information is relevant to open_data because public box office metrics serve as a primary example of accessible, real-world datasets. These figures allow researchers to analyze trends, economic impacts, and cultural preferences across different eras, demonstrating how open data facilitates comparative studies in media economics and audience behavior without proprietary barriers. Understanding these revenue patterns provides insight into the evolving nature of global entertainment consumption. The ability to freely access and compare such historical data empowers analysts to identify lasting influences and shifts in the industry, supporting a deeper, data-driven comprehension of how films achieve and sustain worldwide popularity over time.
