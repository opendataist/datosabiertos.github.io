Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article explains how voters in Galicia can permanently remove their addresses from electoral databases shared by political parties, allowing them to opt out of receiving campaign propaganda. This mechanism highlights the practical application of open data rights, demonstrating how individuals can control the use of their personal information held by public institutions like the INE. By leveraging digital identification methods, citizens can exercise agency over their data privacy. This process underscores the importance of transparency in public data management, as it empowers users to actively manage their participation in political communication channels without relying on passive acceptance of mass mailing practices. This topic is relevant to open data because it illustrates the balance between data utility for democratic processes and individual privacy rights. It showcases how open government data structures can include mechanisms for consent and withdrawal, fostering trust in digital governance while ensuring that public information remains accessible yet respectful of personal boundaries.
