Acuerdos sanitarios para exportar agroalimentos a 181 países - El litoral

Senasa highlights the critical role of sanitary authority in expanding Argentina’s global agro-industrial market access. By coordinating with national agencies, it secures agreements that certify product safety, enabling exports to over two hundred countries. This institutional presence is vital for navigating complex international trade requirements and maintaining the country’s favorable phytosanitary status, directly supporting economic growth through sustained market openings. The relevance to open data lies in the transparency and standardization required for these international negotiations. Successful trade expansion depends on sharing precise, verifiable sanitary and quality data with foreign regulators. Open access to such standardized information facilitates trust, reduces trade barriers, and allows for better monitoring of compliance across diverse supply chains. Ultimately, this framework demonstrates how regulatory data transparency underpins global commerce. By harmonizing norms and sharing technical experiences, Senasa ensures that Argentine products meet international standards. This case illustrates that accessible, reliable data is not just a technical necessity but a strategic asset for maintaining competitive advantage and ensuring the long-term sustainability of agricultural exports in the global market.
