Sepa cuántos ganan en Paraguay por debajo del salario mínimo legal - Economía - ABC Color

The 2022 Population Census exposes severe economic inequalities, revealing that the minimum wage fails to support workers and that high informality disproportionately affects rural sectors. This data highlights the critical need for accurate, granular statistical indicators to understand labor market dynamics and inform policy. Significant gender income gaps and unmet basic consumption costs underscore structural vulnerabilities within households. These findings demonstrate how open census data can drive transparency in social welfare, allowing researchers and advocates to pinpoint disparities and demand equitable economic adjustments. Relevance to open data lies in using such transparent statistics to hold institutions accountable and guide precise interventions. By making this information accessible, stakeholders can better analyze poverty trends and advocate for policies that address the specific needs of marginalized populations.
