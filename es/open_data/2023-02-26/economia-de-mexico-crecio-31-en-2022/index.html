Economía de México creció 3,1% en 2022

Mexico’s economy grew by 3.1% in 2022, driven by industrial activity linked to the United States. While this performance exceeded initial expectations, analysts warn of an imminent slowdown due to global deceleration, inflation, and tightening monetary policies. The erosion of purchasing power is expected to dampen private consumption and investment. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the critical importance of accessing transparent, official economic statistics for accurate forecasting. Publicly available, high-quality datasets allow researchers and institutions to track these complex macroeconomic trends effectively. Understanding these shifts requires reliable information infrastructure. Open data facilitates better analysis of how external factors influence local economies, ensuring that stakeholders can make informed decisions based on verified, accessible metrics rather than relying solely on preliminary or private estimates.
