Reservas de petróleo bajan más de lo previsto en EEUU - Ensegundos.do

US commercial oil reserves fell more than expected, while gasoline stocks and strategic reserves remained relatively stable, resulting in neutral market signals. This data highlights the critical role of transparent energy statistics in maintaining market equilibrium and influencing price stability. Such official reporting underscores the importance of open data in global commodity markets. By providing timely and accurate information, authorities enable stakeholders to make informed decisions, ensuring that economic expectations align with reality and reducing uncertainty in international trade and investment flows.
