Senadores escucharán al titular del INE - Política - ABC Color

Senators recently shielded Iván Ojeda from questioning regarding census irregularities, prioritizing political alignment over transparency. This maneuver highlights how legislative bodies can obstruct accountability mechanisms, directly undermining the principles of open data governance. Opposition voices criticized the dismissal of scientific doubts, arguing that withholding information erodes public trust. The incident illustrates the critical need for independent verification of state data, as political protection often supersedes the duty to provide accurate, accessible information to citizens. This case is relevant to open data because it exposes systemic risks when officials control information flow for political gain. It emphasizes that robust open data frameworks require legal safeguards against partisan interference, ensuring that public statistics remain reliable, transparent, and subject to rigorous scrutiny rather than political discretion.
