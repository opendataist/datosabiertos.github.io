Exige INAI a Pemex revelar avance en construcción de refinería

The National Institute for Transparency has ordered Pemex to disclose detailed project data regarding the Olmeca Refinery, including construction plans, schedules, and progress reports. This ruling emphasizes that public access to information on major state-led infrastructure projects is a matter of public interest, particularly when such initiatives are highlighted by the executive branch as key to national energy self-sufficiency. By mandating the release of real-time progress updates, the decision facilitates social control and accountability. It empowers citizens to audit government actions and verify claims made by authorities regarding project timelines and outcomes, ensuring that transparency mechanisms function effectively even when administrative boundaries between parent companies and subsidiaries are involved. This case is relevant to open data because it reinforces the principle that government entities must proactively share granular, timely information about public expenditures and infrastructure developments. It demonstrates the critical role of open data in enabling civic oversight, allowing society to transform opaque bureaucratic processes into transparent, verifiable, and accountable operations.
