Este es el municipio más rico de Euskadi, que supera a las tres capitales vascas

The article highlights significant regional economic disparities within the Basque Country, contrasting high-income industrial hubs like Getxo with lower-earning areas such as Sestao. This variation underscores how local economic structures, particularly reliance on specific sectors, directly influence household wealth and living standards across different municipalities. Data reveals that while the Basque Country generally outperforms national averages, internal inequalities remain stark. Families in affluent zones report substantially higher incomes than those in less prosperous regions, demonstrating that aggregate regional success does not guarantee equitable distribution of wealth among all residents. This analysis is relevant to open data as it illustrates the importance of granular, publicly accessible statistics in understanding socio-economic realities. By making detailed income distributions available, institutions enable researchers and policymakers to identify precise areas of need, fostering transparency and supporting evidence-based strategies to address regional inequalities and promote social cohesion.
