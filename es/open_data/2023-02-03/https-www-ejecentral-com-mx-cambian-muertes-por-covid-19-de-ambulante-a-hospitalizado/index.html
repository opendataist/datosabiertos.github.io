Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article reveals that open health data in Mexico was manipulated to reclassify nearly all pandemic deaths occurring outside hospitals as hospitalizations. This retroactive alteration aimed to politically obscure the reality of the crisis, directly contradicting official claims that no patient died without access to hospital beds. The relevance to open data lies in the demonstration of how easily public datasets can be altered to serve specific narratives. It highlights the critical need for data integrity, transparency, and independent verification mechanisms to ensure that open information remains a reliable tool for accountability rather than a subject of political manipulation.
