Inflación en Perú aumenta 0,46% en enero

Peru’s consumer prices rose in early 2023, driven largely by food, beverages, and hospitality sectors. Annual inflation remained high, reflecting broader economic pressures and supply chain disruptions caused by regional blockades and ongoing social protests. This data highlights how political instability and physical infrastructure blockades directly impact market availability and price volatility. Such real-world dynamics illustrate the critical need for open data initiatives to monitor economic resilience and transparency during crises. Open data is essential for understanding these complex correlations between social unrest and inflation trends. By making official statistics accessible, researchers and citizens can better analyze how structural factors influence everyday costs, fostering informed public discourse.
