Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The signing of Convention 108 marks a pivotal global step for personal data protection, highlighting the urgent need for organizations to verify their compliance with current regulations. This underscores the necessity of robust data governance and clear policies to safeguard individual rights and manage security risks effectively. This context is crucial for open data because ensuring high standards of privacy and legal adherence builds the public trust required for responsible data sharing. Without such rigorous frameworks, the potential benefits of open data cannot be realized safely or ethically.
