Jueza concede suspensión que ordena nombrar a comisionado del INAI | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

A judicial ruling mandates the Senate to appoint the remaining INAI commissioners, reinforcing the constitutional independence of this vital oversight body. The Senate’s legal challenge to halt this process was dismissed, confirming that no political entity can disable essential democratic institutions. This case is crucial for open data as it ensures the agency retains the capacity to enforce transparency and protect personal information. By securing the institute’s full operational status, citizens are better protected against arbitrary government actions and data misuse.
