Arranca a finales de este mes FGR Banco de Datos Forenses

The Forensic Data Bank is finally launching, addressing years of delay and legal mandates. This consolidation integrates multiple fragmented systems, ensuring a more comprehensive and efficient approach to identifying individuals and solving missing person cases. By centralizing genetic, biometric, and criminal data, the initiative significantly improves interoperability among government agencies. This structural integration allows for faster cross-referencing of information, reducing bureaucratic silos that previously hindered progress in forensic investigations. This development is crucial for open data advocates as it establishes a unified national standard for forensic information. It demonstrates the value of transparent, interconnected data systems in promoting accountability and justice, setting a precedent for how public databases can be leveraged to protect human rights effectively.
