La pobreza en Perú aumentó a 27,5% en 2022 | El Deber

Peru’s poverty rates have risen, exceeding pre-pandemic levels and highlighting persistent socioeconomic challenges. This trend underscores the urgent need for comprehensive data collection to monitor welfare gaps effectively. Reliable statistics are essential for policymakers to design targeted interventions that address the root causes of inequality and prevent further regression in living standards. Disparities are evident across gender and education, with women and those with less schooling facing higher risks. Furthermore, while digital connectivity has improved, access to basic services like sanitation remains limited. These insights reveal that poverty is multidimensional, requiring open data initiatives that integrate social, educational, and infrastructure metrics to provide a holistic view of vulnerability and guide equitable development strategies. The article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistical information is crucial for accountability and effective governance. By making detailed poverty indicators public, citizens and organizations can analyze trends, demand better public services, and hold institutions responsible. Open data transforms raw statistics into actionable knowledge, fostering informed debate and ensuring that resources are allocated where they are needed most to reduce inequality.
