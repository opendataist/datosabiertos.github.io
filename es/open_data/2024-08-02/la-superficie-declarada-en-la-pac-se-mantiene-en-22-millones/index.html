La superficie declarada en la PAC se mantiene en 22 millones

The consolidation of the new Common Agricultural Policy strategy reveals a significant structural shift toward larger, more sustainable farming operations. While the number of individual agricultural holdings has decreased, their average size has increased, indicating a sectoral trend toward concentration. This evolution suggests that remaining farms are adopting more efficient scales to meet the updated regulatory and environmental standards established for the 2023-2027 period, thereby influencing how resources are allocated across the rural landscape. A pivotal outcome of this transition is the widespread adoption of eceregimes, which link financial support to voluntary environmental practices. The overwhelming majority of applicants have embraced these measures, demonstrating strong acceptance of policies designed to enhance soil health, biodiversity, and carbon capture. This high engagement rate validates the policy’s approach of incentivizing ecological stewardship, proving that economic incentives can effectively drive the agricultural sector toward more sustainable and climate-resilient practices without compromising production stability. This data is highly relevant to open_data initiatives as it underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and standardized datasets to monitor the implementation of public policies. By analyzing trends in land use, beneficiary demographics, and environmental compliance, open data frameworks enable rigorous scrutiny of how public funds are utilized. Such transparency is crucial for holding institutions accountable, optimizing future resource distribution, and ensuring that agricultural subsidies genuinely contribute to broader societal and environmental goals rather than merely sustaining existing structures.
