Centro Carter duda que TSJ haga peritaje independiente a resultados de comicios – Correo del Caroní

The Carter Center questions the independence of the judicial audit ordered by Maduro regarding disputed election results. By demanding transparency directly from the electoral council rather than relying on the Supreme Court, experts emphasize that authorities can and should publish full voting data independently to meet international standards. This situation highlights the critical importance of verifiable, granular data in assessing electoral integrity. When vote counts remain opaque, it becomes impossible to validate results or ensure fairness, undermining public trust and international legitimacy. For open data advocates, this case illustrates how accessible, detailed datasets are essential for accountability. Without public, machine-readable election records at the precinct level, verifying outcomes is impossible, making open data a prerequisite for legitimate democratic processes.
