INAI ordena a Propefa revelar dictámenes de explotación ilegal de totoaba

The INAI ordered Profepa to publish public versions of damage repair rulings for illegal totoaba exploitation. This transparency allows society to understand the specific harms caused to this endangered species and the ecosystems it inhabits. By revealing these remediation measures, the decision highlights how legal accountability contributes to restoring biodiversity balance. It serves as a concrete example of using administrative sanctions to protect marine life from illegal activities like overfishing. This case is crucial for open data as it demonstrates how making environmental enforcement information accessible empowers public oversight. It shows that transparency in administrative records is essential for effective conservation, allowing citizens to track and understand the protection of national biological heritage.
