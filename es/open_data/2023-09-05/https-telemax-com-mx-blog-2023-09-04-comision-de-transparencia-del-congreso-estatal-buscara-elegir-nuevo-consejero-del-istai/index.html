Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Sonora Legislature is prioritizing the selection of a new transparency commissioner to maintain institutional integrity after the current president’s appointment. This rigorous process aims to ensure the highest standards of public accountability and data protection within the state’s oversight body. Twelve validated candidates are ready for evaluation, with interviews planned for a single session to streamline proceedings. The Congress will independently assess each profile’s expertise to identify the most suitable leader, emphasizing that the institute itself does not interfere in this legislative decision. This selection is vital for open data because the commissioner oversees public information access and personal data protection. A competent leader strengthens transparency mechanisms, ensuring citizens can effectively exercise their right to know and trust the reliability of government data.
