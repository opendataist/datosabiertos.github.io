Se generaron más de 29 mil empleos: Inegi

Mexico’s second quarter data reveals that job creation was dominated by the informal economy, highlighting the limitations of official statistics which often exclude these vital livelihoods. While formal employment grew marginally, the informal sector absorbed the majority of new workers, suggesting current open data frameworks must better integrate non-traditional labor metrics to reflect economic reality accurately. This disparity underscores the urgent need for more transparent, granular data sources that capture the full scope of employment beyond formal contracts. Open data initiatives should prioritize bridging this gap, ensuring policy decisions are based on comprehensive insights that acknowledge the informal sector’s significant role in the national economy.
