Economía dominicana registra expansión de 2.9 % en el mes de julio - Barrigaverde.net

The Dominican Republic’s central bank reports a positive economic trajectory for July 2023, driven by effective monetary stimuli that have accelerated recovery across key sectors. This improvement highlights how transparent, timely data dissemination allows economic agents and the public to understand the immediate impact of policy decisions, such as liquidity provision and interest rate adjustments, on productive activities like manufacturing and construction. Tourism emerged as the primary growth engine, with record-breaking visitor numbers significantly boosting the hospitality sector. This surge demonstrates the strong link between external demand and local economic performance, while also showing how agricultural and construction sectors are recovering through targeted financial support. The data illustrates the multiplier effect of credit expansion on consumption and investment, validating the transmission mechanisms of monetary policy in real-time. This article is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the value of official statistical transparency in fostering trust and informed decision-making. By making preliminary economic indicators and policy outcomes publicly available, institutions enable stakeholders to analyze trends, assess the efficacy of government measures, and anticipate future economic conditions, thereby promoting a more resilient and informed economic ecosystem.
