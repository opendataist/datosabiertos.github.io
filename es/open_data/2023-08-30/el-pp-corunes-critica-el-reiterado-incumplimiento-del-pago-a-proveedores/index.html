El PP coruñés critica el "reiterado incumplimiento" del pago a proveedores

The PP condemns the A Coruña city council for repeatedly exceeding legal payment deadlines to suppliers in 2023, citing months with averages over thirty days. This delay stems from the lack of an approved budget, which creates liquidity issues for businesses and potentially jeopardizes local employment stability. This situation highlights critical open data relevance, as public payment timelines are essential transparency metrics. Citizens and watchdogs must monitor these figures to ensure government accountability and protect the economic health of the private sector relying on public contracts. Ultimately, the article underscores that timely data publication is vital for enforcing fiscal responsibility. Without clear, accessible records of payment performance, it remains difficult for stakeholders to demand corrections, making open data a fundamental tool for preventing administrative abuse and ensuring fair commercial practices.
