El gasto en pensiones sube en agosto hasta los 12.039 millones

Spain’s pension expenditure rose slightly in August, reaching 11.8% of GDP, with the majority of funds allocated to retirement benefits. The average monthly pension amount increased, highlighting significant disparities between general wage earners and self-employed individuals. A critical finding involves the gender gap compensation, where over half a million pensions received supplements to address inequalities. Notably, the vast majority of these beneficiaries are women, indicating a systemic financial disparity that requires targeted intervention to ensure equity in social security outcomes. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the necessity of transparent, granular datasets to monitor social equity. By making detailed breakdowns of spending and demographics publicly accessible, governments can better analyze and rectify systemic inequalities like the gender pay gap within pension systems.
