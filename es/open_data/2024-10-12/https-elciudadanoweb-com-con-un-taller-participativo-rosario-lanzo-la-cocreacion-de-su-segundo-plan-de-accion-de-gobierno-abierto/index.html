Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The municipality of Rosario has launched the co-creation process for its second Open Government Action Plan, reinforcing its commitment to the Local OGP program since 2020. This initiative prioritizes the active involvement of civil society organizations, acknowledging that public institutions must collaborate with citizens to design effective policies. By integrating diverse stakeholders, including universities, associations, and community groups, the process aims to ensure that governance strategies are inclusive, transparent, and deeply rooted in local expertise and experience. Key thematic areas for this new cycle include strengthening civil society institutions, enhancing digital participation tools, and improving collaboration in public health. A significant focus is placed on using open data to transform sectors like tourism through the "Inspira Rosario" initiative. These themes reflect a strategic shift towards leveraging technology and clear communication methodologies to make government services more accessible. The goal is to foster a closer relationship between the state and the citizenry, recognizing that open data and digital tools are essential for modernizing public engagement. This article is highly relevant to the open data community as it highlights the practical application of data principles in municipal governance. It demonstrates how open data serves not just as an administrative tool, but as a catalyst for social transformation, particularly in areas such as tourism and gender-focused policies. The plan’s emphasis on digital participation and data-driven decision-making underscores the importance of maintaining data openness to empower communities and improve public services, setting a precedent for how local governments can utilize data transparency to build trust and accountability.
