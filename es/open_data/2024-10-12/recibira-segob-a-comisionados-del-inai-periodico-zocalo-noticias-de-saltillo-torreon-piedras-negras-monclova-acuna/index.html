Recibirá Segob a comisionados del INAI | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The National Institute for Transparency (INAI) is set to hold crucial meetings with the Secretary of Governance, marking a significant shift toward direct dialogue with the federal executive. This engagement represents a potential breakthrough after years of exclusion and hostility under the previous administration, signaling that the current government remains open to discussions regarding the institution’s future role and legitimacy. Central to this rapprochement is the urgent debate over legislative proposals to eliminate the INAI and redistribute its functions. By highlighting the institute’s valuable work in archival management, commissioners aim to demonstrate their continued relevance and align their efforts with the new president’s priorities. This strategic focus on shared objectives seeks to secure the institution’s survival against attempts to dismantle it. This development is vital for open data advocates because it directly impacts the structural independence of Mexico’s primary transparency body. A resolution that preserves the INAI ensures robust mechanisms for citizen access to public information. Conversely, its elimination could weaken accountability frameworks, making this diplomatic turning point essential for maintaining transparent governance and protecting data rights in the country.
