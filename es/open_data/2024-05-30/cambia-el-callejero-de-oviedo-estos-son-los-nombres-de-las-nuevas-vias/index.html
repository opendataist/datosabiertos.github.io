Cambia el callejero de Oviedo: estos son los nombres de las nuevas vías

Oviedo has updated its official urban map and population registry to reflect recently approved street name changes, distinct from previous efforts to remove Francoist nomenclature. This administrative move ensures that civic services and official documents accurately represent the city’s current geographical reality. The inclusion of seven new denominations and one reclassified plaza highlights the continuous refinement of municipal spatial data. By synchronizing these updates with the resident registry, the local government maintains precision in its demographic and geographic records. This process demonstrates the critical role of open data in urban governance. Accurate, publicly accessible geospatial information is essential for transparency and efficient service delivery. Such updates ensure that digital maps and civic resources remain reliable tools for residents and administrators alike, reinforcing the importance of maintaining living, accurate datasets in public administration.
