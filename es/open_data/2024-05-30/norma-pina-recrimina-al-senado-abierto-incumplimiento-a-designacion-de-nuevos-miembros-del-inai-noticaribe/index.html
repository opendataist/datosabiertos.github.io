Norma Piña recrimina al Senado 'abierto incumplimiento' a designación de nuevos miembros del INAI - Noticaribe

The Mexican Supreme Court has formally declared the Senate in open disobedience for failing to appoint new commissioners to the national transparency institute within the legally mandated deadline. This ruling highlights a critical breakdown in the constitutional mechanism for selecting officials who oversee information access and data protection, signaling serious institutional friction between the legislative and judicial branches regarding accountability and the rule of law. The Court’s decision carries severe implications, as it initiates a process that could lead to political and legal sanctions against responsible senators, including potential removal from office and criminal charges for contempt. This escalation underscores the binding nature of judicial orders and the strict consequences for legislative bodies that ignore mandatory appointments, setting a precedent for enforcing compliance in matters of public oversight and democratic integrity. This situation is particularly relevant to the open_data community because the transparency institute is the guardian of citizens' rights to access government information. The prolonged vacancy of these crucial posts weakens the enforcement of transparency laws and hinders the protection of personal data, potentially leaving the public with fewer mechanisms to hold state authorities accountable and access critical open information.
