Aumenta el desempleo en 9,5 % y la informalidad en 0,8 %, según el INEC

The 2024 labor market survey reveals a worsening employment crisis, marked by rising unemployment and persistent informality. This indicates that the labor sector is failing to provide stable, formal opportunities, leaving a significant portion of the workforce in precarious conditions without social protections. Data shows a clear trend of formal job losses being offset by increases in informal employment. Consequently, nearly half of all occupied individuals now hold informal positions, highlighting a structural shift away from regulated labor markets toward precarious work arrangements that lack long-term security. This article is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical records. Reliable data exposes these hidden vulnerabilities, enabling policymakers and civil society to demand evidence-based interventions. Without such openness, the true scale of informal labor and economic exclusion remains obscured, hindering effective social and economic reform.
