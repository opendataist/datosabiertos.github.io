Órganos autónomos enfrentan asedio

Mexican autonomy organs face coordinated efforts to weaken them, threatening the constitutional balance of power and violating fundamental rights. The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection suffers from operational paralysis due to Senate inaction, leaving thousands of citizen requests unresolved. This crisis endangers the core guarantees of accessing information and protecting personal data, highlighting the fragility of institutions essential for open governance. Open data initiatives rely heavily on the robustness of institutions like INAI to ensure transparency and citizen trust. When these bodies are paralyzed or deliberately undermined, the flow of public information stops, rendering open data principles ineffective. Consequently, the lack of functioning oversight mechanisms directly impedes the ability of society to monitor government actions, making the strengthening of autonomous bodies critical for maintaining a functional open data ecosystem.
