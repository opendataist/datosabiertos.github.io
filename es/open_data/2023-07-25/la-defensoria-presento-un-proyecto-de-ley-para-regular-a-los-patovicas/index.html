La Defensoría presentó un proyecto de ley para regular a los patovicas

The People’s Ombudsman of Santa Fe has proposed legislation to strictly regulate the private security sector, establishing it as a subordinate and complementary service to public policy. The core objective is to ensure that companies possess adequate training, equipment, and legal authorization, thereby preventing unregulated operations and ensuring strict adherence to constitutional and international human rights standards. A central mechanism of this proposal is the creation of a transparent public registry managed by the provincial Ministry of Security. This registry will serve as an essential tool for accountability by documenting every authorized entity, its personnel, equipment, and inspection results. By mandating clear eligibility criteria and excluding individuals with relevant penal records or specific public security backgrounds, the law aims to professionalize the sector and reinforce state oversight through effective inspection and sanctioning powers. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it fundamentally relies on the publication of administrative and operational information to guarantee transparency. By requiring a public registry that details corporate structure, staff qualifications, and enforcement actions, the proposal promotes civic oversight and data accessibility. This approach transforms regulatory compliance into a public resource, allowing society to verify the legitimacy of security providers and fostering trust in the governance of a sensitive industry.
