CCOO C-LM pide dotar a los pueblos más pequeños de recursos necesarios para facilitar la vida

This article highlights how open data from the National Institute of Statistics reveals critical social challenges in Castilla-La Mancha. By analyzing demographic and housing metrics, the union argues that location and living arrangements are fundamental for effective social policy planning. This demonstrates the power of accessible public data in identifying regional inequalities and shaping targeted interventions. The data shows a high prevalence of single-person households, particularly in smaller towns, where over half of homes are occupied by individuals living alone. A significant portion of housing stock remains vacant, indicating a mismatch between available resources and actual community needs. These insights emphasize the importance of using granular statistical information to address the specific realities of rural populations and aging demographics. Ultimately, the analysis underscores the necessity of adapting housing and social services to prevent rural depopulation and support independent living. This case illustrates why open_data is vital for evidence-based governance, allowing policymakers to allocate resources efficiently. It proves that transparent, detailed statistics are essential for creating inclusive policies that maintain the vitality of smaller municipalities and enhance quality of life for vulnerable groups.
