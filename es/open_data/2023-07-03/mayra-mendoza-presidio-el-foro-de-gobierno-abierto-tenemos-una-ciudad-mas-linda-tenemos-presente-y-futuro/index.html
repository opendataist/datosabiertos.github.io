Mayra Mendoza presidió el Foro de Gobierno Abierto: "Tenemos una ciudad más linda, tenemos presente y futuro

The article highlights the success of the "Open Government Forum" in Quilmes, where the mayor presented a comprehensive review of the current administration's achievements and outlined ambitious plans for the next term. This event served as a platform for thousands of residents to evaluate municipal progress and collaborate on future public policies, demonstrating a sustained commitment to participatory democracy and local governance. Key initiatives include significant infrastructure improvements such as paving thousands of streets, constructing a new hospital, and enhancing educational facilities. The administration also prioritized public safety by increasing police presence and installed modern surveillance systems. These actions reflect a strategy focused on tangible urban development and security, aiming to maintain the trust and support of the local community through visible, result-oriented work. This case is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the operationalization of government transparency. By publicly reporting on completed works and linking them to specific management commitments, the municipality creates an accountable framework. This approach allows citizens to access and verify information regarding public spending and policy outcomes, fostering a culture where open data serves as a tool for civic engagement and continuous evaluation of government performance.
