La creación de trabajo en EE. UU. sigue su ralentización mientras que el desempleo baja

US job growth slowed significantly in July, falling well below the recent annual average, while the unemployment rate remained exceptionally low at 3.5%. This divergence highlights a cooling labor market despite persistent tightness. These official Bureau of Labor Statistics figures demonstrate the critical importance of open data.
