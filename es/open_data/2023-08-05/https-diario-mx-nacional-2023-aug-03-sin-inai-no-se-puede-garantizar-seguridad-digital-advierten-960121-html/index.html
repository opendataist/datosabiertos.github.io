Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Mexico’s data protection agency, the INAI, cannot function without a legal quorum in its governing body, jeopardizing digital security and democratic integrity. The commission emphasized that autonomous institutions are vital for safeguarding privacy and ensuring algorithmic transparency in the private sector. The absence of oversight leaves personal data vulnerable to arbitrariness and injustice, undermining citizens' rights. This legislative paralysis prevents the agency from addressing social risks associated with emerging technologies like artificial intelligence. This situation is critical for open data because transparent governance and protected personal information are foundational to public trust. Restoring the quorum is essential to ensure that data practices remain accountable, ethical, and aligned with democratic principles rather than remaining subject to unchecked corporate or state power.
