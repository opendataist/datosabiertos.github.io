La producción industrial se deja un 3,4% en junio en C-LM

Castilla-La Mancha’s industrial output lags behind the national average in June, though year-to-date growth remains positive. Nationally, the sector experienced a decline in production, marking a return to negative annual rates after a slight spring uptick. Energy and durable goods drove these downward trends, contrasting with gains in equipment and non-durable consumer items. This seasonal adjustment highlights a broader cooling in industrial activity across the country. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible statistical indicators. Reliable public datasets allow stakeholders to accurately monitor economic health, regional disparities, and industrial trends for informed decision-making.
