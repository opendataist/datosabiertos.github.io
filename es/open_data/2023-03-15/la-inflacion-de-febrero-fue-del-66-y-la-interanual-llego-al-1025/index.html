La inflación de febrero fue del 6,6% y la interanual llegó al 102,5%

The Consumer Price Index reflects significant economic volatility, driven primarily by sharp increases in food and beverages, communication, and hospitality sectors. This trend highlights how essential goods and services are facing substantial cost pressures, creating a challenging environment for household budgets and indicating a broader inflationary momentum that extends beyond isolated price spikes. The data distinguishes between regulated, seasonal, and core price categories, revealing that while regulated services and seasonal items show distinct patterns, the core index leads the acceleration. Regional variations further emphasize the uneven impact of these economic forces, with some areas experiencing significantly higher inflation rates than others, thereby complicating regional economic planning and resource allocation. This information is relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical data for public scrutiny. By making detailed inflation metrics available, institutions like INDEC enable researchers, journalists, and citizens to analyze economic trends critically, fostering accountability and informed decision-making in a society affected by rapid price changes.
