'Escándalo en la votación de La Cubana, un fraude con más de 800 errores en el Padrón municipal', denuncian desde el PSOE - Alhama de Murcia - murcia.com

The article exposes severe irregularities in a local government’s consultation regarding the La Cubana park project, highlighting a corrupted electoral register with over 800 errors that allowed ineligible votes. This compromise of data integrity renders the results unrepresentative and legally invalid, as the underlying population records were inaccurate and included deceased or non-resident individuals. The authors argue that these flaws demonstrate a systemic failure in administrative transparency and data management. By submitting complaints to the data protection agency, they emphasize that mishandling voter information is not merely an operational mistake but a deliberate attempt to obscure managerial incompetence and justify costly contractual disputes. The lack of access to raw voting data further deepens suspicions of concealment regarding these procedural failures. This case is highly relevant to open data advocacy because it illustrates the critical necessity of accurate, transparent, and accessible public datasets for democratic legitimacy. It serves as a cautionary tale on how poor data governance and restricted information flow can undermine trust in public institutions, validating the argument that open, verified data is essential for ensuring accountability and preventing manipulation in civic processes.
