INE hará encuesta de cobertura para verificar la calidad del censo

The National Statistics Institute is conducting a post-census survey to rigorously evaluate the census's overall quality and coverage. This initiative aims to detect undercounting among vulnerable populations, such as undocumented immigrants, and to correct potential overcounting driven by local political interests. These findings are crucial for ensuring the data reflects the true demographic reality. Furthermore, the process seeks to identify content errors in key variables, thereby enhancing the accuracy of the final population figures. This comprehensive audit covers both individual records and household structures, ensuring that the statistical framework is robust and reliable for future planning and resource allocation. This work is vital to open data because it demonstrates a commitment to transparency and data integrity. By actively correcting biases and verifying completeness, the state provides more trustworthy datasets for researchers and citizens. Accurate public statistics enable better civic oversight and informed decision-making, which are foundational principles of the open government movement.
