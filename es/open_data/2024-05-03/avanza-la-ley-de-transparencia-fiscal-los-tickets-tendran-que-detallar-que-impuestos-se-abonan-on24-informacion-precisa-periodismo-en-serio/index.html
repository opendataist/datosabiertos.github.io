Avanza la ley de transparencia fiscal: los tickets tendrán que detallar qué impuestos se abonan | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

The article discusses a fiscal transparency initiative aiming to mandate the detailed breakdown of national taxes on consumer receipts. By clearly separating the net price from tax amounts, the measure intends to reveal the actual fiscal burden on everyday goods and services. This approach seeks to strengthen civic fiscal awareness, allowing individuals to understand how their spending contributes to public expenditure and fostering a deeper connection between consumption and government revenue. Furthermore, the legislation proposes a cultural shift by prohibiting terms like “free” in public service advertisements. Instead, it requires clarifying that such services are funded by taxpayer contributions. This directive extends to national, provincial, and municipal levels, encouraging local governments to adopt similar transparency measures. The goal is to expose the sources of jurisdictional financing and emphasize that all public provisions ultimately rely on collective financial support. This topic is relevant to open data because it promotes the systematic publication and structured disclosure of fiscal information. By standardizing how tax data is presented to citizens, it facilitates greater accountability and public access to economic data. Such transparency initiatives can inspire open data ecosystems where financial flows and public spending are easily accessible, enabling better civic engagement and data-driven analysis of fiscal policies.
