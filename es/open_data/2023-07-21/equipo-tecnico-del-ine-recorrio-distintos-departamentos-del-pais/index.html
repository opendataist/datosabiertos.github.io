Equipo técnico del INE recorrió distintos departamentos del país

The Institute of Statistics (INE) actively promoted the completion of the 2023 Census through regional visits, emphasizing the critical role of accurate demographic data in shaping public policy. The initiative aimed to encourage residents who had not yet participated to finalize their responses, highlighting that updated statistics are essential for effectively directing human, economic, and social resources at both municipal and departmental levels. A significant finding of this campaign is the widespread adoption of digital census methods across various regions. This shift indicates a growing public preference for online participation, which facilitates data collection and modernizes administrative processes. The high rates of digital response in diverse areas demonstrate a successful transition toward technology-driven governance, allowing for more efficient and scalable data gathering compared to traditional face-to-face methods. This report is highly relevant to the open data community as it underscores the importance of robust, accessible, and modernized statistical infrastructure. Accurate census data forms the foundational layer for open government initiatives, ensuring that public datasets are reliable and representative. Furthermore, the push for digital participation aligns with open data principles by promoting transparency and ease of access, ultimately enabling better analysis and decision-making for civic engagement and policy development.
