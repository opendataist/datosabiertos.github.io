Con Mar del Plata a la cabeza, la Provincia tuvo el mejor diciembre de los últimos 5 años

The province of Buenos Aires recorded a significant surge in tourism for December, marking its best performance in five years with an 18% growth. Mar del Plata emerged as the primary destination, followed by La Costa, both experiencing high hotel occupancy rates. This success underscores the region's capacity to attract visitors and highlights the economic importance of tourism to the local production and employment sectors. This positive trend is attributed to strategic public policies, specifically the ReCreo program, which offers free cultural, recreational, and sports activities through five dedicated pavilions. By providing accessible entertainment and promoting engagement across 135 municipalities, the initiative aims to strengthen family tourism while boosting economic activity. The government emphasizes that these offerings create an "imposing" summer season, reinforcing the province's status as a top tourist choice due to its diverse attractions. The relevance of this article to open data lies in the digital infrastructure supporting these efforts: the ReCreo app. By aggregating and publishing agenda and attraction information from numerous municipalities, the government leverages open data principles to enhance transparency and user accessibility. This platform demonstrates how centralized, publicly available digital information can effectively connect citizens with services, illustrating the practical utility of open data in improving public policy delivery and citizen experience.
