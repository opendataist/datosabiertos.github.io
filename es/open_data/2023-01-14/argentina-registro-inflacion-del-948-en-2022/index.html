Argentina registró inflación del 94,8% en 2022

Argentina faced a record-breaking 94.8% inflation rate in 2022, driven by persistent macroeconomic instability and aggressive central bank interventions in financial markets. Analysts warn this surge reflects deep structural issues affecting essential sectors like food and health, with potential for continued price escalation into 2023 if corrective measures are not accelerated. This data highlights the critical importance of high-quality, timely statistical information in monitoring economic health. Transparent and accessible official statistics empower researchers and citizens to understand systemic risks and hold institutions accountable. The relevance to open data lies in the need for robust, machine-readable datasets that facilitate real-time analysis of economic indicators. Accessible information enables better decision-making, fosters public trust, and supports the development of solutions for complex socio-economic challenges like hyperinflation.
