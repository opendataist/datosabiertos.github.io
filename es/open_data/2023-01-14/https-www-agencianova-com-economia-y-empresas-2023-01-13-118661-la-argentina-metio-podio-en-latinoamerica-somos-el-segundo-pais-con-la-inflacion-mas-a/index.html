Global economic growth is slowing sharply due to high inflation and geopolitical tensions, risking a worldwide recession. While countries in conflict often maintain lower inflation rates than Argentina, the latter’s fragile economy is heavily constrained by its soaring price levels. This highlights the critical need for open data in tracking such disparities, ensuring transparent monitoring of global economic health and informing better policy responses to prevent further instability.
