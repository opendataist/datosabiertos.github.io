Multan a la Federación Mexicana de Futbol por el registro de asistentes a juegos de la Selección

The Mexican Football Federation faces penalties from the National Institute for Transparency for mishandling stadium attendance data. This incident highlights critical governance failures in protecting personal information during public events. Such breaches underscore the urgent need for robust data stewardship in sports management. Organizations must ensure transparent and secure handling of fan information to maintain public trust and legal compliance. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the consequences of opaque data practices. It serves as a cautionary tale for the importance of accountability and ethical data governance in all sectors.
