Enfrentamiento por mes, vivió la Policía Estatal en 2023

The 2024 INEGI census reveals a sharp rise in security incidents and civilian casualties across Mexico, highlighting significant challenges in public safety management. Chihuahua ranks tenth nationally, illustrating the geographic disparity in violence and the varying effectiveness of state-level security strategies. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it enables researchers and policymakers to analyze trends, evaluate intervention impacts, and demand greater transparency in institutional performance.
