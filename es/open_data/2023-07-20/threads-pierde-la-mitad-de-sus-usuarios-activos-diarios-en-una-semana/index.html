Threads pierde la mitad de sus usuarios activos diarios en una semana

Threads suffered a dramatic decline in active daily users and engagement time shortly after its rapid initial rise to prominence. This sharp drop-off highlights the challenge of sustaining user interest in new social platforms, even those backed by established tech giants with existing ecosystems. The disparity between initial registration spikes and actual daily active usage suggests that hype alone does not guarantee long-term platform viability. Understanding these behavioral shifts is crucial for evaluating the true adoption rates and retention strategies within the competitive digital landscape. This data is relevant to open data because it relies on third-party analytics providers to estimate platform health, demonstrating how public metrics often depend on inferred data rather than direct transparency from the platforms themselves. Analyzing such trends helps stakeholders assess the reliability of publicly available usage statistics.
