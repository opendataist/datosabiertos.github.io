La economía argentina se contrajo 5,5% durante mayo y se acerca a su sexta recesión

Argentina’s economy contracted significantly in May, driven primarily by severe drought that devastated agricultural output. While services and mining showed slight growth, the overwhelming decline in primary sectors dragged overall activity into stagnation for the year’s first five months. This report is crucial for open_data enthusiasts as it highlights the importance of accessible, granular economic indicators for analyzing climate impacts. By tracking such detailed sectoral data, researchers can better understand how environmental shocks propagate through national economies. Ultimately, it underscores the need for transparent statistics to inform policy and resilience strategies.
