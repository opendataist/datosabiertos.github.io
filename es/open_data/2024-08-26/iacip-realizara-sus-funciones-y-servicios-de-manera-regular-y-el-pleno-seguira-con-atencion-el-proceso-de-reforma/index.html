Iacip realizará sus funciones y servicios de manera regular y el pleno seguirá con atención el proceso de reforma

The Institute of Access to Public Information of Guanajuato confirms it will continue operating normally, despite the Chamber of Deputies approving a constitutional reform to extinguish the INAI. This approval is merely an initial legislative step, not an immediate dissolution, meaning the state institute maintains its full capacity to serve citizens and protect transparency rights while monitoring the broader national changes. This situation underscores the critical role of autonomous bodies in fostering government openness and protecting data rights. By sustaining regular operations and engaging in dialogue with local powers, the institute aims to harmonize state laws with any future federal adjustments. This continuity ensures that citizens can still access public information and exercise their rights without interruption during the uncertain transition period. The article is relevant to open_data because it highlights the fragility of institutional frameworks that guarantee data transparency. It demonstrates how local entities remain vital for maintaining open government standards and public trust, even when national structures are under threat. The institute’s continued leadership in openness rankings shows that local commitment to transparency persists independently of federal political shifts, preserving essential mechanisms for public accountability.
