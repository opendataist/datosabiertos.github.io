Aumenta tasa de divorcios en México: se duplica en 12 años, según Inegi | Revista Punto de Vista

The INEGI report highlights a dramatic surge in Mexico’s divorce rate, doubling over twelve years to nearly one-third of all marriages. This shift reflects changing social dynamics, indicating that long-term unions are increasingly unstable and that family structures are evolving beyond traditional models. Concurrently, the data reveals a complex landscape of relationship statuses, with significant portions of the population remaining single or cohabiting without marriage. The correlation between age and marital status further illustrates generational differences in how Mexicans approach commitment, suggesting a broader cultural transition in interpersonal relationships. This article is vital for open_data as it demonstrates how high-quality, freely accessible government statistics can drive social analysis. By making such detailed demographic and subjective well-being data public, institutions enable researchers to track societal trends transparently, fostering informed policy discussions and deeper understanding of evolving community needs.
