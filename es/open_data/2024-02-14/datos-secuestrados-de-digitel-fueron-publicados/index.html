Datos secuestrados de Digitel fueron publicados

A ransomware attack compromised Digitel’s security, exposing sensitive personal and corporate data despite the company’s claims of successful defense. This incident highlights the fragility of centralized data repositories and the critical need for robust cybersecurity measures to protect citizen privacy against digital extortion. The breach underscores the importance of transparency and accountability in the telecommunications sector, revealing how corporate security failures directly impact individual rights. It serves as a stark reminder that without strong safeguards, sensitive information remains vulnerable to malicious actors demanding exorbitant ransoms. For the open data community, this event is crucial as it demonstrates the real-world consequences of data mismanagement. It reinforces the argument for strict data minimization and secure handling practices, emphasizing that protecting personal information is a fundamental aspect of responsible data governance and public trust.
