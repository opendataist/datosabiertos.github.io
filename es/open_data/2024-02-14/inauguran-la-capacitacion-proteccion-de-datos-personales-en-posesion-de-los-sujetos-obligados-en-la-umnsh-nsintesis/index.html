Inauguran la capacitación “Protección de datos personales en posesión de los sujetos obligados” en la UMNSH • Nsíntesis

The UMSNH and the IMAIP have strengthened their collaboration through specialized training on personal data protection for transparency officers. This initiative aims to enhance the institution's compliance with legal obligations regarding information access requests and data privacy. Such continuous education is vital for improving operational efficiency and safeguarding citizens' rights in the public sector. This partnership highlights the critical role of open data frameworks in ensuring transparency and accountability. By equipping staff with the necessary tools, the university fosters a culture of responsibility and trust. These efforts demonstrate how public institutions can proactively address data management challenges while maintaining high standards of service. Relevant to open data, this case illustrates the importance of capacity building for effective information governance. It shows that sustainable transparency requires ongoing investment in human resources and technical knowledge. Ultimately, these measures support the broader goal of accessible, secure, and reliable public information systems.
