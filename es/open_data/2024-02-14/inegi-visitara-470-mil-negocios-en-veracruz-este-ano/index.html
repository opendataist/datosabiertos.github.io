INEGI visitará 470 mil negocios en Veracruz este año

The economic census serves as Mexico’s most comprehensive statistical source, capturing detailed indicators across diverse sectors like manufacturing, services, and agriculture. By gathering data on thousands of businesses regarding employment, income, and technology use, it enables precise geographic and sectorial analysis essential for understanding the national economic landscape. This data allows for historical comparisons dating back to 1930, tracking long-term trends through periodic collections. The ongoing operation covers millions of establishments, ensuring that micro, small, and medium enterprises are represented alongside large corporations, providing a holistic view of business activity and economic health across the country. This initiative is vital for open data initiatives as it generates high-quality, granular datasets that promote transparency and informed decision-making. Accessible economic statistics empower researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze market dynamics, fostering accountability and supporting evidence-based strategies for regional development and policy formulation in an increasingly data-driven society.
