WhatsApp: cuatro ajustes que debes hacer en la aplicación para mejorar tu privacidad

The article highlights that while messaging platforms like WhatsApp offer real-time connectivity, they simultaneously expose users to significant privacy risks through data collection and activity tracking. A primary conclusion is that many users remain unaware of how easily their personal information can be accessed by strangers due to default or misconfigured privacy settings. This lack of control over visible data points creates a vulnerability where personal details, which users intend to keep secret, can inadvertently be shared with unintended audiences. To mitigate these risks, the text emphasizes the importance of actively managing visibility settings for profile elements and communication behaviors. Restricting access to profile photos, online status, and last seen times to contacts only, or even no one, is crucial for safeguarding identity. Furthermore, users are advised to disable read receipts and silence calls from unknown numbers to prevent unwanted surveillance and intrusive interruptions. These adjustments allow individuals to curate their digital footprint, ensuring that their interactions remain private and that they are not inadvertently signaling their availability or location to others. This content is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical intersection between public accessibility and personal data protection in everyday digital tools. It underscores that "openness" in communication apps often defaults to exposing user metadata, which can be harvested and analyzed by third parties. By demonstrating how simple configuration changes can restrict this data flow, the article advocates for user agency in controlling what information is shared, reinforcing the broader open data principle that transparency should not come at the cost of individual privacy and security.
