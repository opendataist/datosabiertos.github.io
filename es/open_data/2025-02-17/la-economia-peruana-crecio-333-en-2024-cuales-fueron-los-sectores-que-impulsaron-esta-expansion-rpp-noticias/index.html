La economía peruana creció 3,33 % en 2024: ¿cuáles fueron los sectores que impulsaron esta expansión? | RPP Noticias

The national productive activity reached its highest post-pandemic growth rate, driven by positive performance across nearly all sectors except construction. This expansion is largely explained by significant increases in manufacturing, agriculture, and fishing, which together account for the majority of the country’s total production. The data highlights a robust recovery and growth trajectory, particularly in export-oriented industries like agribusiness and maritime fishing, signaling strong resilience in the real economy despite broader challenges. Specific sub-sectors demonstrated exceptional dynamism, with agriculture expanding significantly due to record harvests of key crops like mango and paprika, while fishing saw a dramatic surge in industrial catch volumes. Simultaneously, manufacturing and commerce showed sustained momentum, supported by increased energy generation and retail sales. Although mining and hydrocarbons experienced mixed results with some metal production down, the overall trend reflects a broad-based economic improvement where primary and secondary sectors are effectively driving national output. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how standardized, publicly available statistical information is crucial for monitoring economic health and informing public policy. By making detailed sectoral data accessible through institutions like the INEI, citizens, researchers, and policymakers can analyze trends, verify government claims, and hold institutions accountable. Transparent access to such high-frequency economic indicators ensures that decisions regarding resource allocation, industrial strategy, and regional development are grounded in empirical evidence rather than speculation.
