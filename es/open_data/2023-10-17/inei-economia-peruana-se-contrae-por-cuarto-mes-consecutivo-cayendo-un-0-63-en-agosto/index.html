INEI: Economía peruana se contrae por cuarto mes consecutivo, cayendo un 0.63% en agosto

The Peruvian economy experienced a consecutive monthly contraction in August, driven by significant declines in key sectors such as agriculture, manufacturing, and construction. This downward trend is largely attributed to adverse climatic conditions and the onset of the El Niño phenomenon, which disrupted agricultural cycles and reduced overall industrial output. The negative performance across these major components highlights a fragile economic environment facing external environmental shocks. Simultaneously, while some sectors like fishing, mining, and commerce showed growth, their positive impact was insufficient to offset the losses elsewhere. Experts anticipate that the upcoming severe contraction in the fishing sector will further exacerbate the national economic decline. This pattern suggests that short-term gains in specific industries are not enough to stabilize the broader macroeconomic indicators, signaling potential risks for the country's Gross Domestic Product in the coming months.
