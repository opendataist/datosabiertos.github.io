INAI ordena a Bienestar revelar millonario traspaso para programas de niños | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI has mandated the Ministry of Welfare to disclose details regarding the transfer of nearly forty-eight million pesos to its youth welfare division, challenging the agency's previous refusal to provide this information. This ruling addresses significant concerns about accountability and the lack of transparency in how indirect expenses for subsidy programs are managed, emphasizing that major budget allocations must be open to public scrutiny. The decision underscores the necessity of strict adherence to access-to-information laws, particularly given the substantial increase in the Ministry’s budget. By insisting that all financial transactions, from purchases to authorized expenses, remain transparent, the resolution reinforces the government’s commitment to delivering benefits without intermediaries while ensuring that public funds are used responsibly. This case is crucial for open data as it establishes a precedent for forcing the publication of detailed fiscal information in complex government programs. It highlights how regulatory bodies can leverage legal frameworks to compel institutions to release specific spending data, thereby promoting greater visibility and trust in the management of public resources aimed at vulnerable populations.
