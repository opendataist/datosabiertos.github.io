La economía de España creció un 5,5% en 2022, según cifras definitivas

Spain’s economy demonstrated remarkable resilience in 2022, growing by 5.5% despite high inflation and geopolitical instability. This performance significantly exceeded expectations from both the national government and international bodies, driven largely by a robust recovery in the tourism sector post-pandemic. Such strong results provide political momentum for the current administration ahead of upcoming legislative elections. However, this rapid expansion occurred amidst an economic environment where inflation severely impacted household purchasing power. While the government projects continued growth for the coming year, albeit at a reduced rate, this outlook remains more optimistic than conservative estimates from major financial institutions. The data highlights the volatility of recent recovery patterns following a historic contraction in 2020. This article is relevant to open data because it showcases how independent statistical institutes, like the INE, release authoritative datasets that challenge or confirm official narratives. Transparency in these economic metrics allows citizens, researchers, and opposition parties to verify claims, fostering accountability. Access to such granular, timely data is essential for democratic oversight and informed public debate regarding fiscal policy and economic health.
