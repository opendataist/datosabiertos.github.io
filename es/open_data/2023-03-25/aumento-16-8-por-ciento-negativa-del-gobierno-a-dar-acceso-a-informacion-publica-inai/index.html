Aumentó 16.8 por ciento negativa del Gobierno a dar acceso a información pública: Inai

Federal authorities increasingly deny public information access, revealing systemic opacity. This trend undermines citizen trust and highlights the urgent need for greater governmental accountability and transparency. Such restrictions impede civic engagement and oversight, making the protection of open data principles critical for a functioning democracy. Strengthening legal safeguards and promoting open data initiatives are essential to reverse this exclusionary pattern and ensure equitable information access for all citizens.
