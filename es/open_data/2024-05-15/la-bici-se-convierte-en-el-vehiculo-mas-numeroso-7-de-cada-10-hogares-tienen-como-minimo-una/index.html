La bici se convierte en el vehículo más numeroso: 7 de cada 10 hogares tienen, como mínimo, una

The article highlights the bicycle’s emergence as a dominant vehicle in urban mobility, driven by widespread household ownership and significant daily travel distances. However, transforming this potential into normalized usage demands sustained, aligned public policies rather than mere statistical growth. Success is evidenced by the increased inclusion of women and diverse age groups in regions with robust cycling infrastructure, proving that rigorous planning normalizes usage. This inclusivity serves as a key indicator of a healthy, sustainable shift in transport habits. This matters for open data because measuring such social inclusion and policy effectiveness requires transparent, accessible datasets. Tracking gender ratios and usage patterns allows communities to evaluate interventions, ensuring that sustainability goals are met through accountable and data-driven governance.
