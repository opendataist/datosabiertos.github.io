Más vivienda residencial en venta y en alquiler

The Balearic Government launches a program to rent empty homes and sublease them affordably to residents, aiming to reduce the severe housing shortage. This initiative addresses owner hesitancy and illegal tourism rentals, seeking to stabilize the market where residents spend most of their income on rent. The urgency stems from alarming scarcity and rising costs, highlighting the need for immediate action to ensure dignified housing for locals. This policy is relevant to open data as it relies on accurate statistics to identify empty properties and monitor market trends. Transparent data on housing occupancy and pricing is crucial for evaluating the program's success. By making this information accessible, stakeholders can better understand supply gaps and assess whether public interventions effectively increase affordable rental availability for the community.
