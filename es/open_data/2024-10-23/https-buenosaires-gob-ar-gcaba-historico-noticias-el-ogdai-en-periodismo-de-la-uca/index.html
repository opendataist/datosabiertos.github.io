Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Access to public information is vital for democracy, strengthening state transparency and accountability. The press plays a crucial role by acting as a watchdog and agenda-setter, facilitating public deliberation and ensuring institutional oversight through various strategic and functional relationships with data rights. This article is highly relevant to open data because it positions transparent information as the foundational prerequisite for exercising other rights. Effective implementation of access rights enables the data ecosystem necessary for democratic governance, linking legal frameworks directly to societal trust and informed civic participation. Ultimately, the discussion underscores that advancing in the practical application of these rights is not merely legal compliance but a condition for consolidating democratic governments. This highlights the necessity for open data initiatives to prioritize accessibility and usability to truly empower citizens and uphold democratic values.
