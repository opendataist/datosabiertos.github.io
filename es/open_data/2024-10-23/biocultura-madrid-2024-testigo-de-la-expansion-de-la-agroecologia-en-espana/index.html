BioCultura Madrid 2024, testigo de la expansión de la agroecología en España

Spain has emerged as a European leader in organic production, surpassing France in utilized agricultural area dedicated to ecological farming. This significant expansion demonstrates the sector's robust growth capacity and establishes Spain as a key supplier within the European supply chain, highlighting its potential to drive continental sustainability standards and secure a strategic role in the regional agro-food market. However, a critical disparity remains between production volume and domestic consumption. Despite the market reaching billions in value, per capita spending in Spain lags significantly behind other European nations. This gap reveals a structural weakness where the country excels in supply but struggles with demand, indicating that the industry must shift focus from mere export volume to cultivating a stronger local consumer base to achieve true sustainability. This dynamic is crucial for open data because it underscores the need for transparent, accessible statistics to guide policy and business strategies. Reliable data on both production and consumption patterns is essential for identifying these imbalances and advocating for fiscal incentives that boost internal demand. By leveraging open datasets, stakeholders can better understand market trends, support evidence-based decision-making, and promote equitable access to organic food, ensuring that ecological benefits are distributed widely rather than remaining concentrated in production metrics.
