Crecimiento de EE.UU. repuntó en tercer trimestre de 2023 y fue del 1,2%

The US economy demonstrated robust growth in the third quarter, surpassing the previous slowdown with strong performance driven by household consumption, rising exports, and increased private and public investment. This resilience highlights a dynamic market that continues to expand despite ongoing macroeconomic challenges, signaling that the largest global economy remains on an upward trajectory. The data suggests the economy is not cooling down, which influences monetary policy decisions as the Federal Reserve considers further interest rate hikes. With inflation remaining above target levels and unemployment low, policymakers must maintain tight financial conditions to effectively combat persistent price pressures and achieve their economic objectives. This information is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent, high-frequency economic statistics enable real-time analysis of national performance. By making such detailed metrics publicly available, researchers and the public can track trends, assess policy impacts, and understand the interconnectedness of consumer behavior, trade, and government spending in a democratic society.
