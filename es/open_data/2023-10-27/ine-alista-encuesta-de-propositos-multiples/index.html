INE alista encuesta de propósitos múltiples

The National Statistics Institute is updating maps to prepare for the Multiple Purposes National Survey. This foundational work ensures accurate data collection on critical social indicators like employment and housing, which are essential for informed public policy. Accurate geographic frameworks are vital for high-quality statistical output. By securing precise data, governments can better address national challenges, demonstrating why reliable infrastructure supports transparent and effective open data ecosystems for public benefit. This process highlights the importance of robust data preparation. It underscores how systematic information gathering enables evidence-based decision-making. Such transparency fosters trust and allows society to utilize government data for better planning and resource allocation.
