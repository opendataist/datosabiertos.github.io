Desempleo en México descendió al 2,9% en septiembre 2023

Mexico’s unemployment rate dropped to a historic low, signaling robust labor market health and strong economic growth. This decline reflects increased job creation and a rising economically active population, suggesting that the broader economy is expanding effectively and generating opportunities for workers across various sectors. Despite this positive trend, significant challenges persist regarding job quality and informality. A substantial portion of the workforce remains in informal employment, highlighting structural weaknesses in labor stability. Furthermore, the rise in underemployment indicates that many workers are not fully utilizing their capacity, pointing to ongoing issues in matching labor supply with adequate working hours. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible statistical reports. Reliable public datasets enable researchers and policymakers to monitor economic shifts, assess social policies, and hold institutions accountable for labor conditions, fostering informed decision-making for societal well-being.
