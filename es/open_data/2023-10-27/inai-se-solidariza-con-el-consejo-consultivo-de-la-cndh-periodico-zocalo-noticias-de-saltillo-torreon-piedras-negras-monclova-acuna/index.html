INAI se solidariza con el Consejo Consultivo de la CNDH | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INAI supports CNDH advisors who resigned, affirming that institutional autonomy and respect for constitutional mandates are essential for democracy. This solidarity highlights how threats to independent bodies undermine the rule of law and citizen trust in transparency. This situation is relevant to open data because transparency and data protection rely on autonomous institutions free from political interference. When leadership suppresses recommendations or ignores accountability, it erodes the systemic integrity required for public trust in information access and data rights. The conflict illustrates that technical open data frameworks fail if governance structures lack genuine independence. Protecting the operational autonomy of oversight agencies ensures that transparency principles remain robust, safeguarding citizens' rights to hold power accountable through accessible and reliable information systems.
