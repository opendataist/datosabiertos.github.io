La Federación Europea de Periodistas (FEP) pide el alto el fuego y la protección del derecho a informar en Israel y Palestina - Tercera Información

Journalist federations urgently call for a ceasefire to protect press freedom and ensure safe access to information in conflict zones. They demand that combatants respect journalists as non-combatant witnesses and conduct transparent investigations into attacks against media professionals, emphasizing their essential role in documenting potential war crimes. These organizations condemn deliberate violence against reporters and highlight the severe humanitarian crisis, urging immediate cessation of hostilities to safeguard civilians and facilitate the release of hostages. The stance reflects a broader international solidarity with victims while demanding strict adherence to international humanitarian law. This article is crucial for open data as it underscores the necessity of preserving independent information sources during crises. Reliable, unrestricted access to truthful reporting prevents information vacuums that hinder accountability and evidence-based analysis, reinforcing the link between press security and societal transparency.
