El índice preferido por la Fed para medir la inflación se mantiene en julio en el 2,5%

US inflation remains stable at 2.5% annually, closely aligning with the Federal Reserve’s 2% target, while core prices hold steady at 2.6%. This equilibrium signals progress in curbing price hikes without triggering immediate rate cuts, as the Fed prioritizes sustained evidence of cooling inflation before adjusting monetary policy. The stability supports consumer purchasing power and business competitiveness, though vigilance remains essential to prevent economic stagnation or labor market disruptions. This data is highly relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how transparent, timely government statistics empower public scrutiny of macroeconomic health. By releasing accessible metrics like the personal consumption expenditure price index, the US government enables researchers and citizens to validate official narratives against real-world costs. Open access to such granular inflation data fosters accountability, allowing independent analysts to assess policy efficacy and predict economic trends, thereby democratizing financial literacy and strengthening democratic oversight of institutional decision-making.
