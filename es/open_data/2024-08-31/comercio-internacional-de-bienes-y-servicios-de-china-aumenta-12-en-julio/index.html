Comercio internacional de bienes y servicios de China aumenta 12% en julio

China's international trade surged in July, driven by robust goods exports that created a substantial surplus. This growth highlights the nation's strong manufacturing competitiveness, contrasting with a services sector deficit that reveals areas for potential economic diversification. These official statistics are vital for open data as they provide transparent, verifiable metrics of global economic activity. Such high-quality, publicly available datasets enable researchers and developers to analyze trade patterns, build economic models, and foster innovation through unrestricted access to real-world financial flows.
