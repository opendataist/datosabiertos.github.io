Obispo afirma que datos incompletos del Censo 2024 no le hacen bien a nadie

The Auxiliary Bishop criticizes the incomplete census data, arguing that its inaccuracy harms vulnerable populations like the poor and immigrants. This lack of reliability undermines trust in institutions and reflects a culture of partial implementation that fails to serve those who need it most. The article highlights the social implications of flawed statistical records, showing how bureaucratic failures prevent citizens from exercising their rights, such as voting. It connects these administrative shortcomings to a broader moral crisis, warning that superficial data collection creates a disconnect between official narratives and the lived reality of the community. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical importance of accurate, comprehensive public statistics for ensuring equity and civic participation. Reliable data is essential not only for transparency but also for upholding social justice and empowering marginalized groups within a democratic society.
