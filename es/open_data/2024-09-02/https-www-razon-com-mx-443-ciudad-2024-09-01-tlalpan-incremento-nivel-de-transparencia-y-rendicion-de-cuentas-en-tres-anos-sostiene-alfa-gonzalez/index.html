Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Tlalpan borough has significantly advanced its transparency and accountability metrics, rising from seventy percent to nearly ninety-six percent compliance with public information obligations. This substantial improvement demonstrates a robust administrative shift toward openness and effective anti-corruption strategies, establishing the local government as a national leader in open governance standards among Mexican municipalities. By securing top rankings in government openness and achieving full certification for transparency committees and archival systems, Tlalpan highlights the critical importance of institutional capacity building. The initiative not only recognized structural excellence but also empowered civil servants to become trainers, fostering a sustainable culture of transparency that ensures long-term compliance and improved service delivery for citizens exercising their right to information. This achievement is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates how rigorous adherence to transparency frameworks directly enhances data accessibility and public trust. When local governments prioritize these standards, they create the necessary infrastructure for citizens to effectively access, analyze, and utilize public data. Consequently, such municipal leadership sets a replicable benchmark for other jurisdictions aiming to strengthen democratic engagement through the free flow of information.
