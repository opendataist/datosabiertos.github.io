Constructoras registran crecimiento en febrero

Mexico’s construction sector shows marginal recovery in February, remaining significantly lagged compared to pre-pandemic levels and historical peaks. Despite being one of the most delayed economic areas, specific sub-segments like electricity and telecommunications recorded notable growth, indicating resilience in certain infrastructure categories. This data is relevant to open_data because it highlights how official statistics from institutions like Inegi provide essential, granular insights into economic trends. Access to such detailed breakdowns allows analysts to identify which industries are recovering, fostering transparency and supporting evidence-based decision-making in policy and business strategies.
