Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Spanish workplace safety data reveals a significant decline in fatal accidents, highlighting improved conditions for both employees and self-employed workers. This trend suggests that current labor regulations and safety protocols are effectively reducing mortality rates, a crucial indicator for monitoring social welfare and occupational health standards. Conversely, accidents occurring during commutes have risen notably, indicating a persistent risk factor that remains unaddressed by traditional workplace safety measures. This shift underscores the complexity of defining and managing occupational hazards in modern labor markets, where mobility plays an increasingly critical role in worker safety outcomes. These statistics are vital for open_data initiatives, as they provide transparent, evidence-based metrics for evaluating government policy efficacy. By making such granular data accessible, researchers and citizens can better analyze labor trends, advocate for targeted legislative changes, and hold institutions accountable for protecting worker rights and public health.
