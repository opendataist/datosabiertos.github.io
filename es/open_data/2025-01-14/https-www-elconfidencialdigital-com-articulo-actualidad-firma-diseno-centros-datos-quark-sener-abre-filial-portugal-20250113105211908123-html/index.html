The company’s significant growth in contracts and workforce reflects a broader industry shift towards more advanced, high-capacity facilities. This news is relevant to open data because the rapid scaling of data center infrastructure directly impacts the physical layer of data availability and accessibility. As digital services expand, the underlying technological backbone must support increased data storage and processing capabilities. Understanding these infrastructure trends helps stakeholders anticipate changes in data accessibility, ensuring that open data initiatives can effectively leverage expanding digital resources without being bottlenecked by physical or logistical constraints.
