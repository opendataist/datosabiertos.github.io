La inflación de diciembre se ubicaría por debajo del 3 %

The article highlights a significant deceleration in Argentina’s inflation rate, which is projected to close 2024 well below previous highs. This substantial reduction in price increases represents a critical shift in the country's economic trajectory, signaling that aggressive stabilization policies are beginning to yield tangible results in moderating the cost of living for consumers. Private consultancies confirm this downward trend, noting that monthly price rises remain low while the annual cumulative rate drops drastically compared to the previous year. Although some sectors like housing and food experienced specific increases, the overall narrative is one of controlled inflation, breaking the high-growth cycle that characterized 2023 and offering a reprieve to economic stability. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible, and timely statistical releases from institutions like INDEC. Reliable public data allows analysts and citizens to verify official figures against private estimates, fostering trust in economic indicators. Furthermore, the publication of such granular inflation metrics enables better monitoring of social welfare and supports evidence-based policy making in a volatile economic environment.
