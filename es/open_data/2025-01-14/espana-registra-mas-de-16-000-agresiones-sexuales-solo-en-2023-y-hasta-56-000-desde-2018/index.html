España registra más de 16.000 agresiones sexuales solo en 2023 y hasta 56.000 desde 2018

The report reveals a severe public safety crisis in Spain, highlighting that group sexual aggressions increasingly involve minors as both perpetrators and victims. This demographic shift signals a disturbing societal trend, particularly as most offenders are Spanish nationals targeting strangers with extreme violence. The data exposes a systemic failure in youth protection and justice, where group dynamics exacerbate the trauma against victims who are often unknown to the attackers. The prevalence of these crimes in domestic and public spaces underscores the urgent need for broader societal intervention beyond mere statistical recording. This analysis is crucial for open data initiatives, demonstrating how transparent access to granular crime statistics empowers civil society to demand policy reforms. By making such sensitive information accessible, we can foster evidence-based debates on prevention strategies and resource allocation for vulnerable populations.
