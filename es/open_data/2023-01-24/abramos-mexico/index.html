Abramos México

The initiative "Abramos México" marks a pivotal step toward establishing a National Open Data Policy in Mexico, driven by the INAI to foster public, collaborative, and transparent governance. This strategic framework aims to define clear guidelines for both the publication of data by public institutions and its effective utilization by citizens, thereby enhancing societal engagement and accountability. The implementation follows a structured three-phase roadmap designed to institutionalize open data practices across the country’s regions. By organizing regional forums and engaging local stakeholders, including academic and civil society representatives, the strategy ensures broad participation and territorial coverage, transforming abstract policy concepts into actionable local agendas that align with national transparency goals. This article is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates the critical transition from mere information disclosure to strategic policy-making. It highlights how institutional leadership and multi-sector collaboration are essential for creating an ecosystem where open data serves as a tool for empowered citizenship, demonstrating that sustainable open government requires deliberate, structured, and inclusive governance frameworks rather than isolated technical releases.
