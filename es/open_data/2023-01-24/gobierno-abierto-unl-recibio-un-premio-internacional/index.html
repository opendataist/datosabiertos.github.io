Gobierno Abierto: UNL recibió un premio internacional

The National University of Litoral’s Open Government Portal has been recognized as an outstanding academic practice by UN bodies and the Ibero-American Academic Network for Open Government. This distinction highlights the institution's success in integrating transparency, participation, and collaboration, demonstrating how higher education can actively foster open state policies. The initiative is praised for its innovative use of visual data and its capacity to serve as a replicable model for other universities in the region, emphasizing the importance of coherent and demanding open data actions in contexts where such practices are still developing. This recognition underscores the critical role universities play in improving public policies and strengthening democracies through the strategic use of technology and the publication of information. The award reflects a strong institutional commitment and interdisciplinary collaboration, proving that open data initiatives require dedicated cross-sectoral efforts involving statistics, IT, and communication. By prioritizing transparency and participatory budgeting, the portal illustrates how academic institutions can generate collaborative, multidisciplinary actions that extend beyond mere information disclosure to meaningful civic engagement. This case is highly relevant to the open_data community as it validates the impact of well-structured, accessible data presentation on democratic processes. It serves as a benchmark for how educational organizations can leverage open data not just for internal governance, but as a tool for public accountability and innovation. The recognition confirms that when universities actively manage and visualize their data effectively, they become key actors in driving open government agendas, offering a proven framework for others to adopt in their own transparency efforts.
