La industria y la construcción crecieron en abril

The INDEC data reveals a dynamic recovery in Argentina’s industrial and construction sectors during early 2023. While manufacturing showed sustained growth across key divisions like metals and automotive, the construction industry faced annual declines but demonstrated strong monthly momentum and rising input costs. These indicators highlight specific areas of economic activity that are expanding, offering insights into sectoral health and supply chain pressures. The positive trend in industrial output and construction inputs suggests a stabilization in economic activity despite broader inflationary challenges affecting raw material prices. This report is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of publicly accessible, high-frequency statistical indicators. By tracking these granular metrics, researchers and analysts can better understand economic trends, fostering transparency and enabling more accurate data-driven policy analysis and business decisions based on verified national statistics.
