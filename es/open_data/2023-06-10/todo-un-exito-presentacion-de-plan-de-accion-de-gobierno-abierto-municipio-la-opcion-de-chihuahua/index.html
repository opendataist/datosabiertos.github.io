Todo un éxito Presentación de Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto: Municipio | La Opción de Chihuahua

The Chihuahua government unveiled an open government action plan developed through a year-long collaboration with citizens and civil society. This initiative prioritizes transparency, accountability, and social innovation to address community problems and define joint solutions. Key commitments include campaigns against violence and programs to boost rural development through citizen cooperation. Additionally, the strategy aims to strengthen digital tools that foster dialogue and support local entrepreneurship, ensuring active public engagement in municipal progress. This effort is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how releasing government plans and fostering public participation can drive tangible social improvements. It highlights the power of transparent collaboration between state and society in creating responsive, accountable governance structures.
