25N: A nivel país, 1 de cada 3 mujeres ha sufrido algún tipo de violencia, según el INE

The Ministry of Women in Paraguay has highlighted the urgent need for a societal pact to eradicate violence against women, underscored by alarming statistics revealing that a significant majority of women have experienced some form of violence throughout their lives. This data serves as a critical reminder of the scale of the challenge and the necessity for comprehensive, coordinated action rather than isolated interventions. To address this crisis, the institution has expanded its support network, providing thousands of services and launching prevention campaigns that engage both public and private sectors. Emphasis is placed on technological innovation and capacity building for police and civil servants to improve response mechanisms, while collaborative efforts with international bodies ensure that communication strategies remain sensitive and effective in raising awareness. This article is relevant to open_data because it relies on national statistical surveys to contextualize the scope of gender-based violence, demonstrating how official data informs policy and public health strategies. By making these findings public, the government facilitates transparency and accountability, allowing civil society and researchers to analyze trends, evaluate the impact of interventions, and advocate for evidence-based solutions that prioritize prevention and victim protection.
