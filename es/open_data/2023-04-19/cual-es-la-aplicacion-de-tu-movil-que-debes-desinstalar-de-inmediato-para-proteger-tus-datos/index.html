¿Cuál es la aplicación de tu móvil que debes desinstalar de inmediato para proteger tus datos?

The article warns that Spain faces significant cybersecurity threats, ranking third globally, largely due to invasive mobile applications that compromise user privacy. It specifically identifies "XYZ," a messaging app removed from Google Play, as a critical danger. The app demands excessive permissions, collects data without consent, and shares it with third parties for commercial purposes, while also containing security vulnerabilities that expose users to malware and data theft. Consequently, the main conclusion is that users must immediately uninstall XYZ and switch to reputable alternatives such as Signal, Telegram, or Threema. These recommended apps prioritize privacy through end-to-end encryption, avoid storing unnecessary metadata, and do not monetize user data, offering a secure environment for communication. The article urges users to verify application permissions and download software only from official stores to mitigate risks. This content is highly relevant to open_data discussions as it highlights the critical importance of transparency in data handling practices. It underscores the ethical obligation of developers to respect user autonomy and privacy, contrasting opaque data exploitation with open-source, privacy-centric solutions. By advocating for tools that do not commodify personal information, the article reinforces the open data principle that data sovereignty belongs to the individual, not to corporations or malicious actors, promoting a healthier digital ecosystem based on trust and security.
