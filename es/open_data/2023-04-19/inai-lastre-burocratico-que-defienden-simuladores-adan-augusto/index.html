Inai, lastre burocrático que defienden simuladores: Adán Augusto

The Mexican government labels the national transparency institute as bureaucratic waste and opaque, explicitly preferring its operational paralysis caused by unfilled commissioner positions. This stance rejects the institution’s role in ensuring accountability, viewing its inactivity as the ideal state for current administrative goals. This attack undermines the foundational mechanisms of open data, which rely on independent oversight to enforce access to public information. By dismissing transparency bodies as unnecessary, the administration jeopardizes the legal frameworks that enable citizen scrutiny and data accessibility. The narrative relevance lies in highlighting how political power can actively degrade open data ecosystems. It demonstrates that transparency is not merely technical but political, showing how institutional weakening directly threatens the availability and integrity of public data for society.
