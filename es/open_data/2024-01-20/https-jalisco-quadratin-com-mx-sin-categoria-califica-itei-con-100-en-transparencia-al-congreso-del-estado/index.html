Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Jalisco Congress received maximum transparency ratings after the ITEI verified its online publication of fundamental, financial, and administrative data. This achievement demonstrates total compliance with state laws, ensuring that previously unclear information on contracts and official expenses is now accessible and clear for citizens. This outcome highlights the critical role of regulatory oversight in improving public sector accountability. By enforcing strict standards for data accessibility, institutions are compelled to rectify opacity, thereby fostering greater trust in governmental operations and ensuring that public resources are managed openly. The article is relevant to open data as it illustrates the tangible impact of transparency frameworks on institutional behavior. It shows how mandatory disclosure requirements drive the publication of high-quality, usable datasets, serving as a model for other bodies aiming to enhance democratic participation through accessible government information.
