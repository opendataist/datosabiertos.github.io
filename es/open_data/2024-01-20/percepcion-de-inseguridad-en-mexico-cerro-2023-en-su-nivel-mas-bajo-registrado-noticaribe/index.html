Percepción de inseguridad en México cerró 2023 en su nivel más bajo registrado - Noticaribe

Mexico’s perception of insecurity reached its lowest level in a decade during late 2023, marking a statistically significant decline despite high-profile organized crime attacks. This shift indicates a potential improvement in public sentiment, contrasting with previous years’ heightened anxiety. A persistent gender gap remains evident, with women reporting significantly higher levels of fear than men. This disparity highlights the nuanced nature of safety experiences, emphasizing that aggregate national figures may mask distinct vulnerabilities faced by different demographic groups. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public institutions like INEGI release granular, time-series statistics that allow for rigorous analysis of social trends. Transparent access to such data enables researchers to verify official claims, uncover underlying social divides, and inform evidence-based policy decisions regarding public safety and equity.
