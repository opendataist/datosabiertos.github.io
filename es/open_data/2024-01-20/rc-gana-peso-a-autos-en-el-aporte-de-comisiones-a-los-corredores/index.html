RC gana peso a Autos en el aporte de comisiones a los corredores

The insurance brokerage sector experienced a structural shift in 2022, with auto insurance commissions declining despite higher transaction volumes. Concurrently, professional liability and household multi-risk policies gained significant market share, reflecting changing revenue drivers for brokers. This reallocation highlights how intermediary earnings are increasingly diversified away from traditional auto lines toward general liability and home insurance sectors. These trends indicate a strategic pivot in the types of risks brokers prioritize to maintain profitability amid evolving client demands and market dynamics. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it reveals the underlying economic health of the insurance distribution network. Tracking such shifts helps researchers and policymakers understand how digital mediation influences revenue stability and market competition within the broader insurance ecosystem.
