Según el Indec, la industria creció 6,5% en 2022 y completó dos años seguidos de mejora | Análisis

The article reveals a robust annual economic expansion of 5.2%, contrasting sharply with recent monthly declines. This surge was primarily driven by significant growth in mining, hospitality, and fishing sectors, highlighting specific areas of resilience despite broader economic volatility. The data demonstrates a strong post-pandemic recovery trajectory, although industrial and construction outputs showed mixed signals with recent setbacks. Meanwhile, the agricultural sector continued to boost the economy through substantial export growth, underscoring its critical role in the national financial health. This report is relevant to open data because it showcases how official statistical institutes aggregate diverse sectoral metrics into comprehensive economic indicators. Understanding such data structures helps analysts trace how localized sectoral performance contributes to macro-level trends, enabling better transparency and informed decision-making in economic monitoring.
