Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Bolivia advances its Statistical Cartographic Update, a critical foundational step for the upcoming Census. By establishing precise geographic boundaries and work zones, the National Institute of Statistics ensures efficient resource allocation and accurate population counting. This spatial modernization directly impacts open data by creating a standardized, high-quality geographic infrastructure. It enables researchers and developers to reliably intersect demographic data with location-based analytics, fostering transparency and precision in future statistical releases. Ultimately, this process transforms raw enumeration into structured, georeferenced information. It supports the open data ecosystem by providing verified, up-to-date spatial frameworks that facilitate reproducible research and informed public policy decisions across the nation.
