Jujuy: en 5 años hubo más de 100 llamadas a la linea 145 por supuestos casos de trata de personas

Jujuy reports 109 assistance cases via Line 145 between 2018 and 2022, with sexual exploitation being the primary concern. This data highlights systemic challenges in trafficking, where high call volumes contrast sharply with low judicial resolutions, exposing gaps in victim protection and legal enforcement. The relevance to open_data lies in the necessity for transparent, accessible statistics to monitor human rights violations effectively. By analyzing these distributed records, researchers and policymakers can identify vulnerable demographics, such as young adults, and assess the efficacy of state interventions across different jurisdictions. Ultimately, publishing granular data allows for critical evaluation of public safety mechanisms. It empowers civil society to demand accountability and better resource allocation, ensuring that statistical visibility translates into tangible justice for victims and more robust prevention strategies against exploitation.
