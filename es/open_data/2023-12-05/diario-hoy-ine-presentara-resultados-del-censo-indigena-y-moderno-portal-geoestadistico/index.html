Diario HOY | INE presentará resultados del Censo Indígena y moderno portal geoestadístico

Paraguay launches its Geostatistical Portal, a historic tool providing open data from the 2022 Census for public and private decision-makers. This platform enables territory-specific policy monitoring through accessible maps and datasets. Simultaneously, final results of the Fourth Indigenous Census are released, offering updated demographic, social, and economic insights across departments. These initiatives strengthen the National Statistical System, ensuring diverse community needs are accurately represented in national planning. This development is crucial for open data because it democratizes access to critical statistics. By making data downloadable in open formats, it fosters transparency, supports academic research, and empowers evidence-based governance, ensuring broader societal benefits from official statistical production.
