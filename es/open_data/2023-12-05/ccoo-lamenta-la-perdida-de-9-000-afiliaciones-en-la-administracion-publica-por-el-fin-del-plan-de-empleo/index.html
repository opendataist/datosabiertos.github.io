CCOO lamenta la pérdida de 9.000 afiliaciones en la Administración pública por el fin del Plan de Empleo

The article highlights that while sectors like education and logistics are driving job creation in Castilla-La Mancha, public administration faces significant losses due to ending employment plans. This duality reveals a labor market where stability is emerging, yet structural challenges persist, particularly regarding the quality and security of employment contracts rather than mere quantity. A critical focus is the widening gender gap, where women are disproportionately pushed into precarious roles due to discriminatory corporate practices and insufficient public support for caregiving. The lack of corresponsibility and flexible work arrangements forces women to absorb unpaid care duties, exacerbating their vulnerability and limiting career progression, which underscores the urgent need for systemic policy reforms to ensure equitable labor participation. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, disaggregated statistics are essential for identifying hidden social inequalities and guiding effective public policy. By making employment metrics publicly accessible and detailed, stakeholders can hold institutions accountable, advocate for gender equality, and design targeted interventions to support vulnerable populations who lack social protection benefits.
