The surge in Spanish job resignations highlights a shift toward demanding better work-life balance, despite low productivity. This trend challenges traditional employment models where higher well-being often correlates with reduced income. The data suggests that workers are prioritizing personal time over financial stability, driven by growing insatisfaction. Experts warn this could lead to systemic adjustments in compensation structures as organizations struggle to adapt to new expectations without harming output. This article is relevant to open data because transparent labor metrics reveal these critical socioeconomic shifts. Accessible datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze the true cost of changing workplace values, fostering informed debates on future labor rights and economic sustainability.
