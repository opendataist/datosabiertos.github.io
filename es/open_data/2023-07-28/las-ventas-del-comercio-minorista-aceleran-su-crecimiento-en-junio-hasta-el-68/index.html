Las ventas del comercio minorista aceleran su crecimiento en junio hasta el 6,8%

The retail sector demonstrated robust momentum in June, characterized by sustained year-over-year sales growth driven primarily by non-food items and large chain operators. This continuous expansion highlights a resilient consumer demand pattern, suggesting that the economy is benefiting from increased purchasing power in discretionary categories. The data reveals that while food sales remain modestly positive, the significant surge in other goods indicates a shift in consumer spending habits toward durables and personal equipment, signaling confidence in the market despite broader economic uncertainties. Employment in the sector also accelerated, reflecting the operational demands of this growth. The rise in jobs, particularly within small chains and large supermarkets, underscores the industry’s role as a key employer. This correlation between sales volume and workforce expansion suggests that businesses are actively scaling operations to meet rising customer needs. The consistent hiring trend across most autonomous communities, with only a few experiencing slight reductions, points to a generally healthy labor market within retail, reinforcing the sector's stability as an economic pillar.
