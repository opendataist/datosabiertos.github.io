Reporta INEGI baja de la desocupación en junio, se crearon 419 mil empleos

Mexico experienced a significant surge in employment during June, driven primarily by the informal sector and industrial activities. This growth contributed to a historic low in unemployment rates, reflecting strong labor market resilience despite minimal formal job creation. The stark contrast between informal gains and formal losses suggests structural shifts in how employment is recorded and valued. While industry and services expanded, sectors like commerce and professional services contracted, highlighting uneven economic recovery across different fields. This data is crucial for open_data as it demonstrates the importance of integrating diverse statistical sources. Understanding these discrepancies ensures accurate policy-making and fosters transparency in how economic health is measured and reported to the public.
