Obligatorios ejercicios de participación ciudadana y gobierno abierto en capital

The Municipality of Querétaro has legally mandated open government practices and citizen participation mechanisms for future administrations through its newly approved Regulation. This establishes a binding framework ensuring continuity of democratic engagement, shifting these initiatives from voluntary political gestures to obligatory public policy tools. Key mechanisms such as referendums and public initiatives now carry specific response deadlines, enhancing accountability and responsiveness. By defining clear procedures, the regulation empowers residents to actively influence decisions, ensuring that their contributions receive timely administrative treatment rather than languishing in bureaucratic delays. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it institutionalizes the cocreation of policies with citizens. It fosters a culture of transparency and collaboration, creating a sustainable ecosystem where public information and participatory governance are permanent features, thereby strengthening trust and enabling more effective, inclusive democratic processes in the region.
