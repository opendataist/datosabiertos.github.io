Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article highlights INEGI’s dual role in monitoring inflation and conducting the Economic Censuses. It emphasizes that while inflation has decreased, the most significant drop occurred in seasonal components, revealing underlying price stability trends beyond volatile goods. This nuanced understanding aids policymakers in distinguishing between temporary and structural economic shifts. Crucially, the text underscores INEGI’s commitment to transparency by making comprehensive business demographic data publicly available. This open approach ensures that citizens, private enterprises, and government agencies alike can access vital economic indicators. By democratizing access to data on business lifecycles and activities, the institute fosters informed decision-making across all sectors of society. This relevance to open data lies in INEGI’s proactive strategy to disseminate detailed economic and household income information, including remittances. By providing free, high-quality datasets to the public and private sectors, the institution supports evidence-based analysis and empowers stakeholders. Ultimately, this transparency enhances accountability and allows for more robust economic planning and academic research grounded in accessible, real-world statistics.
