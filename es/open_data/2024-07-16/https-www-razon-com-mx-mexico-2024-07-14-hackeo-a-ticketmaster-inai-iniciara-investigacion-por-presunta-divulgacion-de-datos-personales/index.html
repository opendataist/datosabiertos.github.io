Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The INAI has launched an investigation into Ticketmaster for the alleged disclosure of personal data, highlighting the strict legal obligations companies must meet to protect user information. This case underscores the critical need for robust security measures to prevent unauthorized access and misuse of private data, ensuring accountability when breaches occur. This incident is highly relevant to open data because it emphasizes the balance between transparency and privacy. While open data initiatives promote accessibility, they must coexist with strong personal data protection frameworks. Understanding these boundaries helps stakeholders design systems that respect individual rights while maintaining public trust. The INAI invites affected individuals to file complaints, reinforcing the importance of vigilance and active participation in data governance. This process serves as a reminder that protecting personal information is a shared responsibility. It illustrates how regulatory bodies enforce compliance, ensuring that data handling practices align with legal standards and ethical expectations.
