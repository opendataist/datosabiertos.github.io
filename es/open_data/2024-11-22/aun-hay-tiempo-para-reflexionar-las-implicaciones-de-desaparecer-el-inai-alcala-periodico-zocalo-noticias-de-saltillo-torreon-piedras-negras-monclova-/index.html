“Aún hay tiempo para reflexionar” las implicaciones de desaparecer el Inai: Alcalá | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The head of the INAI emphasizes that even though legislative efforts to dissolve the institute are progressing, there remains time to reflect on the implications for citizens' rights to information and data protection. This debate highlights a critical juncture for Mexico’s transparency framework, urging lawmakers to prioritize a robust state guarantee model rather than simply eliminating an existing institution. The article is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the fragility of institutional safeguards that ensure public access to government information. Without clear secondary regulations, the future of the National Transparency Platform and data protection mechanisms remains uncertain, potentially hindering the reliable availability of public datasets essential for transparency and accountability. Ultimately, the core message advocates for collective responsibility in shaping a new normative structure. It calls for adapting to change while preserving the essential functions that have historically protected access rights, ensuring that any structural transformation strengthens rather than diminishes the infrastructure supporting open and accessible public information.
