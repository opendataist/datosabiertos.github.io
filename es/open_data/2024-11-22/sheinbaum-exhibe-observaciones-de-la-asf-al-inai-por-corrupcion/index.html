Sheinbaum exhibe observaciones de la ASF al INAI por corrupción

Presidenta Sheinbaum highlights severe corruption and nepotism within the former Federal Auditor, arguing these systemic irregularities were previously ignored. This exposes a culture of financial misconduct, including withheld salaries and improper use of public funds by officials. The narrative shifts to reforming the National Institute for Transparency, asserting that ending its autonomy will eliminate these internal abuses. Rather than reducing oversight, the administration claims this move enhances accountability by integrating supervision into the Public Function Secretariat. This development is relevant to open data as it restructures transparency mechanisms. By centralizing data oversight, the government aims to improve citizens' access to expenditure information, potentially streamlining how public data is managed and utilized for greater governmental accountability.
