La inflación cae en marzo al 3,3% mientras la tasa subyacente frena ligeramente al 7,5%

The article highlights a significant moderation in inflation, reaching its lowest level in over two years. This trend reflects a broader economic shift where key cost drivers, such as energy and transportation, have seen substantial price reductions compared to the previous year. Consequently, consumer price pressure has eased considerably, marking a notable improvement in macroeconomic conditions. Several major expenditure categories contributed to this deceleration. Housing costs dropped sharply, primarily due to falling electricity and heating gas prices. Similarly, declines in fuel costs drove down transportation expenses. Although food prices remain high, their rate of increase has slowed, helping to stabilize the overall index, while leisure costs saw a slight uptick. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible, and timely statistical information for analyzing economic health. By making these metrics publicly available, institutions like the INE enable researchers and the public to track inflation trends, assess regional disparities, and make informed decisions based on reliable, real-world data.
