García-Page se reunirá en Bruselas con la comisaria de Cohesión y Reformas de la UE para avanzar en el acceso universal a la fibra óptica

Emiliano García-Page announces plans to visit Brussels to secure European funding for universal fiber optic access across Castilla-La Mancha. By treating high-speed internet as a fundamental right essential for healthcare, education, and business development, the region aims to become the first autonomous community to achieve total coverage. This strategic move highlights the critical role of open data infrastructure in ensuring equitable digital inclusion and supports the broader goal of leveraging European resources to bridge the digital divide. The President also addressed the pressing economic challenges of inflation and energy costs, noting that regional data places Castilla-La Mancha within the national average. He contrasted this with other regions that, despite being frequently cited as examples, exhibit significantly higher inflation rates. This analysis underscores the importance of transparent, accessible economic data in formulating effective regional policies and providing accurate contexts for public debate on fiscal health. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how statistical transparency empowers political accountability and justifies major infrastructural investments. By publicly comparing regional inflation metrics, the government demonstrates how open data facilitates evidence-based decision-making. Furthermore, the push for universal connectivity emphasizes that open digital infrastructure is a prerequisite for a society that can effectively access and utilize open data for social and economic advancement.
