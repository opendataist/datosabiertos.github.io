El INE de Uruguay inicia la fase virtual del censo 2023

Uruguay has launched a hybrid census combining virtual and in-person data collection to capture a precise snapshot of its population. This comprehensive data collection is crucial for shaping public policy over the next decade, guiding decisions on essential infrastructure like schools and healthcare facilities to meet evolving societal needs. The initiative ensures accurate demographic and socio-economic insights, moving beyond simple headcounts to understand living conditions. By establishing a reliable baseline for future governance, the census supports long-term strategic planning across multiple administrative terms. This process is vital for open data as it generates authoritative, high-quality statistical foundations. Transparent access to such rigorous demographic datasets empowers researchers, developers, and citizens to build data-driven solutions that address real-world challenges and improve public service delivery.
