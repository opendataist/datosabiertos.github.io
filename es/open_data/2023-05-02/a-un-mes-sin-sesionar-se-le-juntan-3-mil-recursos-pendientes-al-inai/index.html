A un mes sin sesionar, se le juntan 3 mil recursos pendientes al INAI

The INAI lacks a legal quorum due to missing commissioners, paralyzing its ability to vote on pending transparency cases. This structural void prevents the resolution of thousands of resources, effectively suspending the enforcement of citizens' access to information rights. This situation highlights the critical importance of open data governance, as institutional paralysis directly obstructs accountability mechanisms. Without functional oversight bodies, the transparency infrastructure essential for democratic integrity and public trust collapses. The crisis underscores that open data initiatives depend heavily on robust, operational regulatory frameworks. It serves as a reminder that technical access to information is insufficient without the institutional capacity to process and enforce related rights, making political stability a prerequisite for effective data openness.
