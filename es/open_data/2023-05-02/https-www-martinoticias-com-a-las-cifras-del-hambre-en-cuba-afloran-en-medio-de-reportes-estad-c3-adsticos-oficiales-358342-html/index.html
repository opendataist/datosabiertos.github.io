Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Recent official statistics reveal a severe crisis in Cuba’s food industry, exposing the state’s inability to mitigate widespread malnutrition. The collapse extends beyond production to critical agricultural inputs, indicating a systemic failure rather than temporary shortages. This highlights the urgent need for transparent, accessible open data to accurately assess and address such complex socio-economic challenges. The dramatic deflation-adjusted decline in food sales demonstrates a brutal contraction in consumer power, disproportionately affecting workers and retirees. Real wages have failed to keep pace with skyrocketing prices, forcing households into severe dietary restrictions. This narrative underscores how open data, when freely available and analytically accessible, empowers citizens to verify government claims and understand the true cost of living adjustments. Ultimately, the disconnect between official narratives and statistical reality emphasizes the necessity of robust data transparency. Without reliable, open information, citizens cannot hold authorities accountable for food security failures. This case illustrates that open data is not merely technical but essential for democratic oversight, enabling society to comprehend the depth of economic distress and demand effective policy interventions.
