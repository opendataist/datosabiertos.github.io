El 63,7% de los incendios forestales registrados en la Región en 2022 fueron intencionados o por negligencias

The data highlights that human negligence and intentional acts drive most forest fires, leaving only lightning as a significant natural cause. This trend underscores the critical need for open data to inform prevention strategies and public accountability. By making incident reports transparent, authorities can identify high-risk zones and allocate resources more effectively to mitigate avoidable damage. Open datasets enable researchers and citizens to analyze patterns, fostering community engagement and supporting policy changes aimed at reducing human-caused environmental destruction.
