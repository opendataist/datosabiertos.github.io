La Agrupación de Hostelería señala el notable aumento del turismo rural en la provincia a mitad de año

Rural tourism in Cuenca has demonstrated significant growth, with overnight stays increasing substantially in the first half of the year. This surge reflects the province’s considerable potential to attract visitors through its unique combination of landscape and cultural heritage, validating the sector’s expanding infrastructure. The industry plays a crucial role in revitalizing depopulated areas such as Alcarria and Serranía by stimulating local activity and creating jobs. Both domestic and international visitor numbers have risen, with foreign travelers showing particularly strong momentum, indicating an broadening appeal beyond local borders. This article is relevant to open_data as it highlights how official statistics from the National Statistics Institute drive economic analysis and strategic planning. Transparent access to such data allows industry groups to validate trends, demonstrate regional value, and advocate for sustainable development in underserved territories, ensuring that public information directly supports local economic resilience.
