Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The government of Nuevo León launched its Open Data portal, marking a strategic shift toward radical transparency and legitimacy. By making public data accessible across five key categories, the administration aims to demonstrate that openness prevents corruption and builds public trust. This initiative transforms the state into a model of integrity, proving that there is nothing to hide when government operations are fully visible to society. Central to this effort is the adoption of CKAN, an open-source platform recognized globally for its efficiency and international compatibility. This technological choice empowers citizens, researchers, and developers to analyze, visualize, and reuse public information. It facilitates the creation of new applications and informed decision-making, ensuring that data is not just stored but actively utilized to drive social and administrative innovation. The relevance to open_data lies in the structural commitment to standards and community engagement. The government is establishing a specialized technical committee to define norms and train officials, ensuring sustainable governance. Furthermore, the DataLABNL program encourages the creative application of these datasets to solve public problems, fostering a culture where open data drives tangible solutions and civic empowerment across all sectors.
