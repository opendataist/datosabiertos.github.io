La Seguridad Social gana casi 9.500 afiliados extranjeros en Baleares durante julio, un 6,5 % más que en 2023

The article highlights the significant and growing contribution of foreign workers to Spain’s Social Security system, which has nearly doubled since 2019. Despite a slight seasonal dip in July, the underlying trend shows record-high employment levels for non-resident employees, reflecting a robust recovery in the labor market. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, granular statistics enable the monitoring of economic integration and demographic shifts. By making such detailed breakdowns by nationality and regime publicly available, policymakers and researchers can better assess labor market dynamics. Understanding these trends supports evidence-based decision-making regarding migration and employment policies. The consistent release of this information fosters accountability and allows for deeper analysis of how foreign labor impacts national economic stability and social welfare systems over time.
