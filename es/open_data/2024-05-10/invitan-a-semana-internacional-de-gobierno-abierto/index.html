Invitan a Semana Internacional de Gobierno Abierto

Chihuahua hosts the Open Government Week, aligning with global standards to boost transparency, accountability, and civic collaboration. This event highlights open data alongside inclusive transparency and innovation, demonstrating municipal commitment to accessible governance. It serves as a vital case study for open data implementation, showing how local authorities actively engage citizens through structured thematic activities and public resources to foster trust and participation in democratic processes.
