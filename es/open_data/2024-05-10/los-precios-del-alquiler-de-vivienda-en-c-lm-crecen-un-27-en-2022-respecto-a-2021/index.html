Los precios del alquiler de vivienda en C-LM crecen un 2,7% en 2022 respecto a 2021

The Spanish rental market experienced its second-highest annual price increase since 2012 in 2022, driven largely by a sharp rise in new contracts while existing agreements remained relatively stable. This disparity highlights how market dynamics affect newcomers versus incumbents, revealing regional disparities where coastal areas faced steeper hikes than inland provinces. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular insights into housing affordability trends across Spain. By making such detailed indices publicly available, policymakers and researchers can better understand spatial inequalities and the impact of regulatory measures on different segments of the rental market, fostering informed public discourse.
