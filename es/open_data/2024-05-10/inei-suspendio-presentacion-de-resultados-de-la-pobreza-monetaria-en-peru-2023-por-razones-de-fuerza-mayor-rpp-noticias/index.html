INEI suspendió presentación de resultados de la Pobreza Monetaria en Perú 2023 por "razones de fuerza mayor" | RPP Noticias

The INEI’s last-minute cancellation of its poverty data presentation raises transparency concerns, delaying public access to critical metrics on national deprivation. This suspension highlights the tension between government opacity and the public’s right to timely, accurate information, which is essential for holding officials accountable in the open data era. Previous figures reveal a significant regression in poverty levels, reversing years of progress due to economic stagnation and inflation. The delayed report underscores how hidden or inaccessible statistical data prevents citizens from understanding the true scale of social hardship and policy failures. Open data is vital for fostering informed public debate and effective governance. When institutions withhold such key indicators, they undermine trust and hinder the ability of researchers and civil society to analyze trends, propose solutions, and ensure that poverty reduction efforts remain evidence-based and transparent.
