Basque Country and Navarra record lower gender-based violence rates than the national average, highlighting regional successes in prevention. This trend underscores the importance of localized data in identifying effective strategies to reduce domestic abuse. However, overall national figures show rising victimization and accused men, emphasizing the persistent nature of the issue. These trends suggest that while some regions excel, broader systemic challenges remain unresolved across the state. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistics enable comparative analysis. By making such detailed demographic and regional information public, policymakers can evaluate interventions and allocate resources more effectively to protect vulnerable populations.
