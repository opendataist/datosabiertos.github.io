Costa Rica. Diputados presentan proyecto de ley elaborado por ChatGPT

Costa Rica has introduced a groundbreaking legislative proposal to regulate artificial intelligence, uniquely drafted entirely by ChatGPT-4. This initiative prioritizes human rights, aiming to ensure transparency, privacy, and data security while fostering the development of explainable AI systems. By mandating a registry for algorithms and security measures, the law seeks to protect citizens in an increasingly automated landscape. This project is highly relevant to the open data community because it establishes a framework for mandatory disclosure of AI functionalities and underlying algorithms. Such transparency requirements directly encourage the publication of structured data regarding system operations, which is essential for auditing, research, and public accountability. It demonstrates how open information policies can coexist with robust ethical regulations. Ultimately, this move highlights the potential of using generative AI as a tool for legislative drafting itself. It signals a proactive national stance on emerging technologies, encouraging other nations to consider similar regulatory models. The proposal underscores the intersection of open governance and technological innovation, setting a precedent for how societies might govern digital futures through inclusive, data-informed policy making.
