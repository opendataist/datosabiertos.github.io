Aumenta la violencia de género en Balears: 2 de cada 1.000 mujeres fueron víctimas en 2022

The data reveals stark regional disparities in gender-based violence, highlighting how open data exposes geographic hotspots and allows for targeted policy interventions. By quantifying victimization rates, authorities can identify areas requiring urgent support and allocate resources more effectively to protect vulnerable populations. Beyond specific incidents, the statistics underscore the interconnected nature of domestic abuse, showing how violence within cohabitation significantly impacts children and family members. This broader view emphasizes the need for comprehensive societal responses that address the root causes of aggression rather than treating cases in isolation. Ultimately, this transparency fosters public accountability and drives social awareness regarding the prevalence of abuse. Open data transforms raw statistics into actionable insights, enabling researchers and policymakers to monitor trends over time and measure the effectiveness of existing legal frameworks in reducing violence against women and families.
