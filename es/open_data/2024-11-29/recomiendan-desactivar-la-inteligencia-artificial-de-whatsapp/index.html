Recomiendan desactivar la Inteligencia Artificial de WhatsApp

The introduction of Meta IA in WhatsApp highlights significant concerns regarding data privacy and algorithmic surveillance. Users fear that activating the tool allows Meta to collect sensitive information for personalized advertising, potentially compromising personal security given the company's historical handling of user data. These issues are particularly relevant to open data, as they underscore the critical need for transparency in how artificial intelligence processes and leverages personal information. The controversy illustrates the tension between technological innovation and individual rights, emphasizing that without clear data governance, AI integration can lead to invasive profiling and loss of user autonomy. Consequently, many users are choosing to disable the feature to maintain control over their digital footprint. This trend demonstrates that public resistance to opaque data practices can drive demand for stricter privacy controls, reinforcing the importance of open standards and user agency in the development and deployment of emerging technologies.
