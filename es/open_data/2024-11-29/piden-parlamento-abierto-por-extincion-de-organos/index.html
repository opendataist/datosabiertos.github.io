Piden parlamento abierto por extinción de órganos

Adrián Alcalá urged the Senate to hold an open parliament session to decide the future of the INAI and other autonomous bodies. He argued that public institutions must embrace open discussion to solve common problems and improve public service quality effectively. Commissioner Ibarra Cadena emphasized that transparency underpins open governance. She highlighted the necessity of public scrutiny and understanding judicial criteria to ensure laws are applied correctly, fostering trust in democratic processes. This issue is vital for open data because the INAI protects citizens' right to access government information. Its potential extinction threatens the legal framework that ensures public transparency, directly impacting data availability and accountability in Mexico.
