El IPC acelera seis décimas en noviembre, hasta el 2,4%, por la luz y los carburantes

Spain’s inflation rose in November, primarily driven by higher electricity and fuel prices. This rebound highlights the volatility of energy costs and their significant impact on consumer price indices. The increase interrupts a period of decline, pushing the rate to its highest level since July, despite underlying inflation showing a slight decrease. The economic ministry attributes this rise mainly to base effects from previous energy price drops. However, they emphasize that the annual average inflation remains significantly lower than the 2022 peak. This suggests that current economic policies are effectively balancing growth with gradual price stabilization, even as short-term fluctuations occur due to seasonal or supply-side factors. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of accessible, timely statistical indicators for public transparency. Open datasets allow researchers and citizens to verify official claims, analyze energy market trends, and understand economic health independently. Reliable access to such raw data fosters trust in institutions and enables deeper scrutiny of how policy measures influence everyday costs.
