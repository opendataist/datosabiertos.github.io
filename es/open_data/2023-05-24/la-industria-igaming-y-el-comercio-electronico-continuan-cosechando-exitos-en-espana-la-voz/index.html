La industria iGaming y el comercio electrónico continúan cosechando éxitos en España - La Voz

The article highlights the rapid expansion of the online gaming sector in Spain, identifying it as a significant economic driver. Data indicates that a majority of the adult population frequently engages in online gaming, transforming it from a niche activity into a mainstream component of digital leisure. This surge is attributed to the widespread adoption of digital devices and the acceleration caused by the pandemic, which forced physical establishments to shift operations online. Beyond consumer behavior, the industry’s growth has prompted regulatory changes in various regions, including Venezuela, as governments seek to capitalize on the substantial revenue generated by iGaming. The sector has established itself as a major player in e-commerce, ranking highly in both transaction volume and the sheer number of transactions, demonstrating its deep integration into the broader digital economy. This content is relevant to open data because it relies on large-scale public surveys, such as EDADES, to track societal trends and economic shifts. Analyzing such extensive datasets allows policymakers and researchers to understand how digital behaviors evolve and to evaluate the effectiveness of public health and economic policies. The transparency of these findings underscores the value of accessible statistical information in guiding legislative decisions and assessing the societal impact of emerging digital industries.
