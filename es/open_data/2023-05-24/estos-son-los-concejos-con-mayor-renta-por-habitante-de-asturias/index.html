Estos son los concejos con mayor renta por habitante de Asturias

The INE’s 2023 Urban Indicators report reveals significant disparities in living standards across Spain, highlighting that net income per capita correlates directly with better employment and health outcomes. While Asturian municipalities like Oviedo lead their region and exceed the national average, the data shows a stark concentration of wealth in specific neighborhoods of Madrid and Barcelona. These areas possess net incomes nearly seven times higher than those in the poorest districts of Sevilla, illustrating deep intra-urban inequality that often overshadows differences between entire municipalities. This document is critically relevant to open data initiatives because it utilizes the European Urban Audit framework, which provides standardized, granular statistics on socio-economic conditions in major European cities. By publishing detailed metrics on unemployment and life expectancy alongside income, the INE enables researchers and policymakers to conduct precise comparative analyses of urban quality of life. This transparency allows for a deeper understanding of how localized economic factors impact broader social indicators, fostering evidence-based decision-making. Ultimately, the study underscores the importance of accessing disaggregated data to address regional inequalities effectively. It demonstrates that aggregated municipal averages can mask extreme variations between neighborhoods, necessitating high-resolution datasets for accurate policy formulation. The availability of such structured information supports open government principles by empowering citizens and organizations to visualize trends, hold institutions accountable, and develop targeted interventions for the most vulnerable communities.
