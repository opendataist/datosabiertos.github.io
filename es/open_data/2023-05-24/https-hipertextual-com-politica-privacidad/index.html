Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article establishes that Hipertextual S.L. operates as the data controller, collecting personal information such as cookies and usage data to provide its services, manage advertising, and perform statistical analysis. It emphasizes that while many users are subject to basic protection standards, those in specific jurisdictions benefit from higher safeguards, particularly under European regulations. The site asserts its commitment to security and outlines the legal bases for processing, ranging from contractual necessity to legitimate interest, ensuring transparency about how data is handled internally and by third-party contractors. A critical aspect highlighted is the extensive reliance on external analytical and advertising services, specifically from US-based entities like Google Analytics and ComScore. This introduces the issue of international data transfers, which may involve moving personal data outside of the European Union. The policy details that while some providers adhere to specific privacy frameworks, users must be aware that their behavioral data is monitored and shared across borders for commercial and statistical purposes, creating a complex landscape of data governance that transcends local boundaries. This document is highly relevant to open_data as it exemplifies the stark contrast between transparent, accessible data practices and the opaque mechanisms of commercial data harvesting. While it lists the entities and purposes involved, it does not offer actual data sets or open APIs, instead focusing on privacy rights and legal compliance. It serves as a case study for understanding the "data economy" where information flows from users to corporations through tracking technologies, highlighting the importance of data portability and the right to oppose commercial profiling in the broader conversation about digital transparency and user sovereignty.
