Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Chuquisaca completes its cartographic update for the upcoming census, joining other departments in finalizing this critical data foundation. This achievement ensures accurate geographic and housing statistics are ready for reliable national demographic analysis. The completion of this phase highlights the substantial logistical effort required to map millions of dwellings across diverse regions. With most departments nearing or reaching full coverage, the foundation for precise census enumeration is now firmly established across Bolivia. This process is vital for open data initiatives, as high-quality, freely accessible geographical and demographic datasets enable researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze regional development, plan public services, and hold institutions accountable through transparent, evidence-based governance.
