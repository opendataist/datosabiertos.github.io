INAI invita al 4to Concurso Nacional de Periodismo de Investigación

This competition promotes investigative journalism and social science research, rewarding outstanding work with cash prizes. It encourages professionals and students to submit reports or essays, emphasizing strict adherence to registration deadlines to ensure participation validity. The initiative is relevant to open data because it supports the creation and dissemination of high-quality, evidence-based reporting. By incentivizing rigorous investigation, it fosters a culture of transparency and critical analysis, which are essential for utilizing open data to hold power accountable and inform public discourse. Ultimately, this contest strengthens the ecosystem where data meets storytelling. It validates the intersection of academic inquiry and journalistic practice, demonstrating how structured data and open information can drive meaningful societal impact when combined with deep investigative methods and ethical reporting standards.
