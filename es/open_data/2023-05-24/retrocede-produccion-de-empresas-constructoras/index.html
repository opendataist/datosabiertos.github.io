Retrocede producción de empresas constructoras

The Mexican construction sector faces a persistent downturn, with February production dropping monthly and remaining significantly below pre-pandemic peaks. This sustained contraction highlights deep structural weaknesses in key sub-segments, particularly edification, which carries the highest economic weight. Revealing such granular, timely metrics is vital for open data advocates. It demonstrates how accessible government statistics empower analysts to track economic health beyond headline figures. Transparent data allows for a nuanced understanding of sectoral vulnerabilities and informs better policy and investment decisions.
