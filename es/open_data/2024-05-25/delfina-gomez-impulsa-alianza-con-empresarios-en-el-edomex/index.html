Delfina Gómez impulsa alianza con empresarios en el Edomex

The governor of Mexico State reaffirms her commitment to fostering collaborative, transparent governance through dedicated working groups with the business sector. This initiative aims to streamline proposals and combat corruption, which currently hinders the creation of new enterprises. Such openness is crucial for open data, as it promotes the accountability and accessibility required for effective economic monitoring. Simultaneously, the administration is implementing security and innovation strategies to attract diverse investments. By unifying efforts across logistics, industry, and agriculture, the state seeks to build a robust economic policy that leverages its strategic location and skilled workforce. This holistic approach ensures that data-driven decisions can accurately reflect and support multifaceted regional growth. These efforts highlight the importance of open data in facilitating public-private partnerships. Transparent information enables businesses to make informed decisions and allows citizens to verify government efficiency. Ultimately, this model demonstrates how accessible data can drive investment, reduce barriers to entry, and ensure that progress benefits all sectors of society in the state.
