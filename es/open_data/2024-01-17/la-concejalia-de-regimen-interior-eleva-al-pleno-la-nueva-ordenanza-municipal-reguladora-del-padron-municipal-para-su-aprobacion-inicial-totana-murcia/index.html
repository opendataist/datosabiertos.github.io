La Concejalía de Régimen Interior eleva al pleno la nueva Ordenanza Municipal Reguladora del Padrón Municipal para su aprobación inicial - Totana - murcia.com

The article highlights Totana’s initiative to enforce compliance with municipal registry obligations through a new sanctioning ordinance. By establishing clear penalties for failures to register or report changes in residency status, the local government aims to eliminate discrepancies between the official population register and actual demographic reality. This regulatory framework empowers municipal authorities to address omissions and falsifications, ensuring that the census accurately reflects the community's true composition. This measure is directly relevant to open data initiatives because the municipal registry serves as a foundational dataset for public administration. Accurate population data is essential for resource allocation, urban planning, and policy-making. When official records do not mirror reality, the integrity of the entire open data ecosystem is compromised, leading to inefficiencies and unfair burdens on compliant citizens. Ultimately, the ordinance seeks to compel legal adherence to registration duties, thereby safeguarding data quality. By reducing the gap between recorded statistics and lived experience, the municipality ensures that its digital public records remain reliable. This reliability is crucial for maintaining trust in open data systems and ensuring that public services are distributed based on verified, transparent, and current information.
