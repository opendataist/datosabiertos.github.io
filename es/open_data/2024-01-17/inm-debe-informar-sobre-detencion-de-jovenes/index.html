INM debe informar sobre detención de jóvenes

The INAI ordered the National Migration Institute to release detailed records regarding the detention of migrants on a highway, emphasizing that migration policies must be transparent to prevent racist or discriminatory practices. This mandate ensures that inspection sites remain safe for all individuals, challenging potential biases in enforcement criteria. The resolution highlighted procedural failures by the migration institute, specifically its failure to consult relevant protection departments and its incomplete adherence to transparency laws when classifying information. By correcting these administrative oversights, the case underscores the critical link between legal compliance and the protection of fundamental human rights in data disclosure. This situation is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency mechanisms actively safeguard against discrimination. It reinforces the necessity of rigorous data accessibility to hold government agencies accountable, ensuring that public information supports equity rather than masking biased operational conduct.
