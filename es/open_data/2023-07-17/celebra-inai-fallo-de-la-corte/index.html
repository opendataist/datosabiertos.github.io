Celebra INAI fallo de la Corte

The Supreme Court has determined that the Senate’s failure to appoint three commissioners invalidates the INAI, as its reduced quorum prevents it from functioning or exercising its constitutional powers. This ruling confirms that the absence of full membership violates the institutional design necessary for transparency and data protection guarantees. This decision is crucial for open data because it underscores the link between institutional integrity and effective rights enforcement. When the oversight body is paralyzed, citizens lose reliable mechanisms to access public information and protect their privacy. Restoring the quorum is essential for maintaining democratic accountability and ensuring that transparency laws are actively applied. Ultimately, the case highlights that structural gaps in governance directly impact the realization of fundamental rights. It serves as a reminder that legal frameworks must be supported by complete institutional representation. Without a fully staffed body, the promise of open data remains theoretical, affecting public trust and the rule of law in Mexico.
