INAI, organismo autónomo resulta incómodo ante el poder: Ibarra Cadena | HidrocalidoDigital.com

The INAI is increasingly positioned as a crucial citizen defense mechanism against state arbitrariness, prioritizing technical expertise over political pressures. By acting with autonomy and impartiality, the institution resolves the vast majority of citizen grievances in their favor, thereby reinforcing the system of checks and balances and ensuring that government power is effectively monitored and restrained. Transparency and data protection are identified as fundamental "key rights" that enable access to other essential services such as health, education, and justice. Furthermore, these rights drive economic development by generating investor certainty, reducing information asymmetries, and preventing capital flight. For businesses, a robust regulatory framework also fosters consumer trust and aids in cybersecurity prevention, demonstrating the dual social and economic value of open data principles. However, the absence of three commissioners has paralyzed the institute’s plenary, leaving thousands of appeals unresolved and hindering its international obligations. This institutional paralysis undermines legal certainty and investor confidence, highlighting the critical relevance of open data governance: functional transparency institutions are indispensable for maintaining democratic legitimacy, combating corruption, and sustaining a stable economic environment.
