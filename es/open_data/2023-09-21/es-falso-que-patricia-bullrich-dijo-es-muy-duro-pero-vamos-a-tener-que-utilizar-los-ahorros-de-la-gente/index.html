Es falso que Patricia Bullrich dijo: “Es muy duro pero vamos a tener que utilizar los ahorros de la gente”

The article debunks a viral disinformation campaign claiming that Argentine presidential candidate Patricia Bullrich intends to confiscate citizens' bank savings. Analysis confirms that the accompanying image attributed to the media outlet Infobae is a fabrication, as is the alleged quote. There is no public record of Bullrich ever stating she would "use people's savings," a claim often shared out of context to mislead the public during the electoral period. The confusion stems from distorted interpretations of Bullrich’s 2022 statements regarding bimonetarism, an economic model where the peso and dollar coexist. Her actual proposal focuses on encouraging the legalization of foreign currency held informally ("under the mattress") to integrate it into the productive, savings, and investment circuits. This aims to restore trust in the banking system and stimulate credit, rather than forcibly appropriating existing deposits, which contradicts the malicious narrative spread online. This verification effort is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how digital evidence can be manipulated to create false narratives quickly. It highlights the critical need for cross-referencing primary sources, such as original media archives and official platforms, to detect tampered visuals and verify claims. By exposing the lack of authentic records, the story underscores the importance of transparent, verifiable data in combating information fraud and maintaining integrity in democratic processes.
