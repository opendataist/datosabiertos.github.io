Muertos, nacimientos y matrimonios, según las estadísticas del Registro Civil

The Provincial Registry has launched an open data portal covering vital statistics from 2020 to 2024, offering detailed, disaggregated information on births, deaths, marriages, and adoptions by municipality. This platform enables deep demographic analysis, revealing significant societal shifts such as the pandemic’s demographic impact and evolving family structures, including the rise in same-sex unions. Crucially, the data highlights complex local dynamics, such as how hospital locations in neighboring districts can skew municipal birth records. It also tracks cultural trends through naming patterns and parental nationality, providing a nuanced view of community composition and migration that transcends simple administrative counts. This initiative is vital for open data advocates because it democratizes access to critical civic information, fostering transparency and accountability. By making granular demographic data publicly available, it empowers researchers, journalists, and citizens to conduct independent analysis, thereby enhancing evidence-based public policy and social understanding through free, accessible knowledge.
