La creación de empresas se dispara un 9,1% en 2024 y alcanza su mayor cifra desde 2007

Spanish business registrations reached their highest level since 2007, marking a significant resurgence in entrepreneurial activity after pandemic-related declines. This surge indicates a robust recovery and renewed confidence in the economy, with the service and real estate sectors leading the formation of new entities across key regions. However, this growth contrasts with a simultaneous increase in company dissolutions, suggesting a dynamic market characterized by high churn. While more businesses are entering the market, a notable proportion is also closing, reflecting underlying economic pressures or strategic adjustments within the commercial landscape. This data is vital for open_data initiatives as it provides transparent, high-quality statistical evidence for economic analysis. By making such detailed, standardized information publicly accessible, researchers and policymakers can better monitor market health, support evidence-based decision-making, and enhance transparency in understanding the structural changes of the national economy.
