Puno: Más del 25% de productos que componen la canasta básica han subido de precio

The report highlights a moderate monthly inflation increase in Puno’s Consumer Price Index, driven largely by rising costs in beverages, communications, and essential household items. This reflects a broader trend where specific sectors experience price volatility while others, such as clothing and accommodation, see slight decreases. At the national level, the Consumer Price Index also registered a moderate rise in July, indicating a consistent pattern of gradual inflation across the country. These figures suggest that while overall price stability is maintained, households continue to face pressure from essential goods, impacting daily economic planning for families in regions like Puno. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the critical role of transparent, publicly available statistical indicators in monitoring economic health. By providing detailed breakdowns of price changes across various consumer baskets, such open datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze inflationary pressures, identify vulnerable sectors, and formulate evidence-based strategies to support economic resilience and consumer protection.
