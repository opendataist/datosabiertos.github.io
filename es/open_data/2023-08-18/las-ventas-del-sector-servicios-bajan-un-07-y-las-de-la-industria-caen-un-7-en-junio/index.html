Las ventas del sector servicios bajan un 0,7% y las de la industria caen un 7% en junio

The service sector experiences a significant downturn, marking its first sales drop in over two years despite mixed results across specific sub-sectors. This contraction is particularly notable as manufacturing revenues decline sharply, with industrial output facing negative growth across both economic destinations and geographic markets. The divergence between service stability and industrial weakness highlights a fragile economic landscape where regional disparities further complicate recovery efforts. Employment trends offer a contrasting perspective, with service sector jobs increasing annually while industrial sectors struggle with reduced internal and external market demand. The data reveals that while some regions benefit from strong service growth, others suffer severe industrial declines. This bifurcation underscores the uneven impact of economic shifts, creating a complex environment for labor and business performance across different autonomous communities. By analyzing detailed breakdowns by sector and geography, we can identify hidden vulnerabilities and regional imbalances that aggregate national data might obscure.
