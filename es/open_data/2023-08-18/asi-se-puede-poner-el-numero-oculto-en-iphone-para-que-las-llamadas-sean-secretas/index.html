Así se puede poner el número oculto en iPhone para que las llamadas sean “secretas”

The article details the implementation of privacy features in digital communications, specifically focusing on how users on Apple devices can conceal their phone numbers during voice calls. It outlines technical methods to hide caller ID permanently or per call, ensuring that the recipient sees an anonymous label instead of the user's personal information. This capability highlights the growing demand for granular control over personal data exposure in an era where digital privacy is increasingly contested. Furthermore, the text extends this concept to the messaging platform WhatsApp, describing new beta features that allow users to hide their phone numbers within community groups. This development signifies a shift towards more robust privacy settings in social applications, enabling users to interact without revealing their primary contact details. These updates represent a proactive response by tech companies to user concerns regarding safety and anonymity in online interactions. This content is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the tension between data transparency and user privacy in digital ecosystems. While open data initiatives often promote accessibility and sharing of information, these privacy tools demonstrate the counter-movement where individuals actively seek to restrict data visibility. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for policymakers and developers to balance the openness of digital services with the fundamental right to privacy and the security of personal identifiers.
