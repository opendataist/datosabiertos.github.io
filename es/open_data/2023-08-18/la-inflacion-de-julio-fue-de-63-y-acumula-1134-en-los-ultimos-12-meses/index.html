La inflación de julio fue de 6,3% y acumula 113,4% en los últimos 12 meses

The article highlights Argentina’s persistent high inflation, with consumer prices rising significantly in July. This surge underscores the urgent need for transparent, accessible economic data to understand cost-of-living pressures. Open data initiatives are crucial for democratizing such statistical information, allowing citizens and researchers to analyze trends beyond official headlines. By making indices like the IPC freely available, society can better grasp the impact of price fluctuations on daily life and essential goods. Ultimately, this transparency fosters accountability and informed public discourse regarding monetary policy. Reliable open datasets empower stakeholders to scrutinize government reports, ensuring that economic indicators are not just numbers, but tools for social and political awareness.
