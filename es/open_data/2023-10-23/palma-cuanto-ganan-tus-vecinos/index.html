Palma: ¿Cuánto ganan tus vecinos?

This article highlights significant income disparities across Spanish municipalities, using Palma’s postal codes to illustrate stark contrasts between affluent areas like Monti-Sion and less prosperous zones like s'Arenal. By analyzing median disposable income, it reveals how local wealth distribution varies drastically even within the same city, with some neighborhoods earning more than double others. Nationally, the data identifies La Moraleja as the wealthiest area and Torreblanca as the poorest, emphasizing the extreme geographic inequalities in Spain. These figures, derived from tax agency records, underscore the concentration of wealth in specific regions while excluding smaller towns and certain autonomous communities with independent fiscal systems. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how publicly accessible government statistics can expose socioeconomic divides. By making such granular income data available, researchers and citizens can better understand inequality patterns, fostering transparency and enabling evidence-based policy discussions regarding regional development and social equity.
