Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article critiques the dehumanized approach of certain leaders in rural census operations, highlighting how a lack of empathy and poor leadership negatively impact field teams and community relations. It argues that statistical work requires more than technical skills, demanding integrity, respect, and humanistic values to ensure social welfare is genuinely served. This narrative is relevant to open data because the quality of public statistics depends heavily on the ethical conduct and human interaction of those collecting data. When data collection processes ignore the dignity of participants and field workers, it undermines trust in the resulting datasets. Ultimately, the piece emphasizes that accurate and useful open data relies on humane methodologies. By prioritizing respect and empathy alongside technical rigor, institutions like the INE can produce more reliable information that truly reflects societal needs, fostering greater transparency and public confidence in official statistics.
