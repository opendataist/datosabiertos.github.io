Los brasileños que se declaran mulatos superan a los blancos por primera vez desde 1991 - Mundo - ABC Color

Brazil’s latest census reveals a significant shift in racial demographics, with the mulato population rising while the white majority declines. This change reflects evolving social contexts and self-identification rather than just demographic shifts, highlighting how identity is fluid and context-dependent. The data underscores the importance of accurate census categories for tracking social equity. Understanding these trends helps policymakers address regional disparities and the aging population, ensuring that public policies effectively serve diverse communities across different geographic areas. This article is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how granular, transparent demographic datasets reveal hidden social dynamics. By making this information accessible, stakeholders can better analyze inequality and drive evidence-based decisions that promote inclusion and reflect the true complexity of societal changes.
