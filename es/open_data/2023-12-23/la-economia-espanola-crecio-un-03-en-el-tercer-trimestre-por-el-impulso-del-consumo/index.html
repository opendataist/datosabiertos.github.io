La economía española creció un 0,3% en el tercer trimestre por el impulso del consumo

The Spanish economy experienced a slowdown in the third quarter, with growth decelerating compared to previous periods despite a positive interannual performance. Domestic demand remained the primary driver of this expansion, sustained by resilient household consumption and public administration spending, while external demand contributed minimally. This indicates an economy increasingly reliant on internal consumption rather than international trade to fuel its momentum. Labor market dynamics reveal a divergence between wage growth and employment stability. Although salaries and the number of employees saw significant increases, the actual hours worked grew at a much slower pace. This trend suggests structural shifts in working conditions, such as reduced average hours per job, which complicates the interpretation of job quality and productivity alongside headline employment figures. This report is highly relevant to open data because it showcases the INE’s release of granular, multi-dimensional national accounts. By providing detailed breakdowns of demand components, sectoral value-added, and specific labor metrics like hours worked versus full-time equivalents, the dataset enables researchers to uncover deeper economic narratives. It demonstrates the importance of accessing comprehensive statistical infrastructure to move beyond surface-level indicators and understand the underlying drivers of economic change.
