Lanzamiento del nuevo Anuario Estadístico y el Informe de Comercio Exterior 2023

The presentation of Guinea Ecuatorial’s 2023 Statistical Yearbook highlights a significant transformation in the nation’s data ecosystem, driven by the government’s strategic implementation of Ministerial Statistical Services. This initiative has substantially improved both the volume and quality of data derived from administrative records. The main conclusion is that these structural reforms have successfully strengthened the National Statistical System, ensuring that official statistics are more robust, reliable, and reflective of the country’s current socio-economic reality. A key implication of this progress is the demonstrated efficacy of inter-institutional collaboration, as the statistical production process relied on extensive cooperation among all government ministries and official entities. While this partnership facilitated the creation of a comprehensive document covering social, demographic, economic, and financial indicators, it also exposed persistent challenges in data harmonization and availability. The narrative underscores that sustainable statistical capacity building requires continuous technical and financial support from partners, as well as broader public engagement to fill remaining data gaps. This development is highly relevant to open data advocacy because it illustrates a critical step toward transparent, evidence-based governance. By making detailed sectoral data—from public health metrics to foreign trade statistics—available through an official electronic portal, the government enhances accountability and facilitates the reuse of information by researchers, journalists, and civil society. The emphasis on improving data quality and accessibility provides a foundational framework for future open data initiatives, encouraging stakeholders to leverage official statistics for informed decision-making and policy evaluation.
