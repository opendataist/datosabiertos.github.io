OIT sobre las políticas de Perú para el empleo juvenil: no han apuntado a resultados

Perú's youth employment policies failed to achieve intended results, highlighting a critical gap between official strategies and tangible outcomes. This failure suggests that current frameworks lack the necessary efficacy to address youth unemployment effectively. The relevance to open data lies in the urgent need for transparent, accessible policy evaluations. Without detailed, public information on policy performance, stakeholders cannot identify specific shortcomings or drive meaningful improvements. Consequently, leveraging open data to track and analyze these policies is essential. It enables accountability and provides the empirical basis required for designing more effective, evidence-based interventions for young workers.
