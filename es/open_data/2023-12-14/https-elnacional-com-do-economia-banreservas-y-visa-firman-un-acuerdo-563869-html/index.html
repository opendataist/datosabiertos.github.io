Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Banreservas joined Visa B2B Connect to streamline cross-border business payments, prioritizing speed, security, and data uniformity. This integration merges traditional banking with Visa’s global multilateral network, offering enterprises a predictable and efficient transaction experience enhanced by advanced security technologies. This development is crucial for open data because it establishes standardized, transparent data flows in international commerce. By adopting a unified global network, financial institutions promote interoperability and consistency in payment records. Such transparency supports open data principles by reducing information silos and ensuring reliable, structured data exchange. This alignment fosters trust in digital financial ecosystems and encourages broader adoption of open standards for corporate transactions.
