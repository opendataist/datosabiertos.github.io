Eliminación de autónomos, grave retroceso: Medina

The article warns that dismantling autonomous bodies undermines democratic safeguards like transparency, data protection, and fair markets. Weakening these institutions concentrates power dangerously, threatening the separation of powers achieved through decades of civic struggle. This stance is vital for open_data advocates, as it highlights how independent regulatory bodies are essential for enforcing accountability and ensuring citizens' rights to access public information are legally protected against political interference.
