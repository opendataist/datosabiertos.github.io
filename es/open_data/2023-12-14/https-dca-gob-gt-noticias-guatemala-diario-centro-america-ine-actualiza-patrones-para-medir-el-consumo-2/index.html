Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The National Institute of Statistics updates its consumption basket to better reflect household spending patterns, incorporating new items like mobile phone services. This methodological revision aims to improve the accuracy of macroeconomic indicators by aligning them with contemporary family needs. Such rigorous data collection and transparency are vital for the open_data community, enabling independent verification of inflation metrics and fostering trust in public statistics. Accessible methodologies support civic engagement and informed decision-making regarding economic realities. Ultimately, this update enhances the quality of public data, ensuring that official indicators remain relevant and representative. It demonstrates how government agencies can leverage open data principles to serve the public interest through accurate, modernized statistical frameworks.
