Los inmigrantes están mejor integrados en el mercado laboral que hace diez años según datos de la OCDE

The report highlights that immigrant employment rates are gradually converging with native populations, driven by a significant influx of highly qualified workers. This demographic shift suggests a more skilled workforce integration across OECD nations, marking substantial progress over the last decade despite existing disparities. However, significant challenges remain, particularly for women who face higher unemployment rates than men despite superior educational qualifications. Structural barriers often prevent immigrants from securing appropriate jobs, forcing many into positions below their skill level while exposing them to greater risks of poverty and inadequate housing conditions. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible, standardized datasets are crucial for monitoring integration trends. Reliable open data enables policymakers to identify specific gaps, such as gender disparities or credential recognition issues, facilitating evidence-based interventions to improve social cohesion and labor market equity.
