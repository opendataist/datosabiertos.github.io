Harán parque público en Jardines de Jurica

The Municipality of Querétaro is implementing an open government initiative in the Jardines de Jurica delegation, inviting residents to co-design a new urban park through participatory workshops. This approach empowers the community to select specific infrastructure elements and layout configurations, ensuring the public space directly reflects local needs and preferences rather than being imposed from above. Transparency and accountability are central to this process, with oversight provided by electoral and transparency commissions to validate the voting mechanisms. By allowing citizens to decide how municipal funds are allocated for physical improvements, the administration fosters trust and demonstrates a commitment to inclusive governance that values collective decision-making over top-down planning. This case is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how participatory governance can be documented and verified. The structured input from residents creates a clear trail of democratic engagement, highlighting the importance of making civic processes accessible and the need for data transparency regarding how public resources are managed and improved based on community feedback.
