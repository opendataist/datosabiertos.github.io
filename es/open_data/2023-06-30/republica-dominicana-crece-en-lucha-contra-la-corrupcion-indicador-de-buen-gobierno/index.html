República Dominicana crece en lucha contra la corrupción, indicador de buen gobierno

The 2023 Corruption Capacity Index highlights a significant regional effort to strengthen legal and institutional frameworks against corruption. The report emphasizes that robust capabilities in detecting and sanctioning corrupt actors are essential for restoring public trust and ensuring effective governance across the Americas. Dominican Republic’s notable improvement reflects a strategic commitment to transparency and judicial independence. Key legislative advancements, such as the law on asset forfeiture, demonstrate a political will to dismantle criminal networks. However, the analysis suggests that sustained progress requires increased resources for anti-corruption bodies to enforce laws impartially and without exception. This narrative is vital to open data because it underscores the necessity of accessible public information and media freedom as core indicators of governance quality. The index explicitly values transparency, civil society oversight, and the quality of public data flows. By linking institutional performance to the availability of information, the report illustrates how open data serves as a critical mechanism for accountability, enabling citizens and journalists to monitor government actions effectively and reduce impunity.
