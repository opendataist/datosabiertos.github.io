El INE prevé sólo cambios de forma en la boleta tras el censo experimental

The National Institute of Statistics confirms that the 2024 Census will primarily undergo minor formatting adjustments to its survey form following an experimental test in Cochabamba. This final phase aims to refine logistical and procedural details, ensuring that the substantial content of the questionnaire is fully defined and optimized. The experiment serves as a critical checkpoint to validate the clarity and quality of the data collection tools before the official rollout. The census holds profound relevance for open data initiatives, as it provides the essential statistical baseline required to understand the current demographic and socioeconomic reality of the nation. Accurate, transparent data enables informed decision-making regarding resource distribution, political representation, and fiscal agreements. Without a reliable "radiography" of the population, efforts to achieve social equity and effective governance lack the empirical foundation necessary for meaningful progress. Stakeholders emphasize that transparency and social control are vital to maintaining public trust in the process. The completion of the statistical cartographic update across most departments signals high operational readiness, ensuring precise geographic coverage. Ultimately, the success of this open data exercise depends on delivering credible, accessible information that reflects the true diversity and needs of the population, fostering accountability and democratic participation.
