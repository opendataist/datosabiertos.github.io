UE.- La UE acuerda reforzar la transparencia de los datos de mercado para empoderar a inversores

The European Union has reached a provisional agreement to revise MiFIR and MiFID II regulations. This update aims to empower investors by enhancing market transparency and boosting the global competitiveness of EU capital markets. The core change involves centralizing fragmented trading data into consolidated sources across the Union. By making key information like prices and volumes easily accessible, it removes barriers for both professional and retail investors who currently struggle with dispersed platforms. Additionally, the agreement bans "payment for order flow," eliminating incentives that could distort trading practices. This is crucial for open data as it promotes a fairer, more transparent ecosystem where data availability drives informed decision-making rather than opaque financial arrangements.
