G. 5 millones más para legisladores: ¿cuánto subió el salario de los trabajadores en 10 años? - Economía - ABC Color

Paraguay’s recent labor data highlights a persistent gender wage gap, with men earning significantly more than women despite overall minimum wage increases over the last decade. The National Statistics Institute confirms that while salaries have risen steadily, this progress has not been equitable, underscoring ongoing structural inequalities in the labor market that demand policy attention. Furthermore, the high rate of informal employment remains a critical issue, affecting the majority of the workforce. This prevalence of informality complicates efforts to ensure fair compensation and social protection for workers. The data reveals that statutory minimum wage adjustments do not necessarily reflect the reality faced by those outside the formal economy, creating a disconnect between policy and practice. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it exposes the necessity for transparent, accessible, and disaggregated statistics. Reliable public data allows researchers and citizens to monitor labor rights, advocate for equity, and hold institutions accountable. By making such detailed economic indicators openly available, society can better understand and address systemic disparities that remain hidden in aggregate figures.
