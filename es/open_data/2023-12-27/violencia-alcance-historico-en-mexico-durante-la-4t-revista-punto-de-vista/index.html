Violencia alcance histórico en México durante la 4T | Revista Punto de Vista

Mexico has witnessed unprecedented violence levels, with official statistics revealing a staggering number of murders during the current administration. While some reports suggest a slight decrease in homicide incidence, international bodies caution that organized crime groups maintain influence over territories and markets. This persistent threat highlights the complex nature of security challenges, where criminal dynamics continue to shape public safety despite official narratives of improvement. The heavy reliance on firearms drives much of this lethal violence, with gun-related crimes affecting countless lives beyond just homicides. The data underscores a critical distinction between general homicides and gender-based killings, emphasizing the severity of violence against women. Certain states bear a disproportionate burden, concentrating the majority of victims and illustrating the geographic unevenness of insecurity across the nation. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency in statistical reporting exposes underlying social crises. Accessible government records allow for independent verification of security trends and hold institutions accountable. By making detailed information on violence and weapon usage publicly available, open data facilitates a deeper understanding of systemic issues, enabling civil society and researchers to analyze the root causes of insecurity rather than relying solely on political interpretations.
