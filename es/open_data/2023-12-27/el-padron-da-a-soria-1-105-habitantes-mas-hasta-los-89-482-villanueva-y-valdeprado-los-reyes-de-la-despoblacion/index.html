El Padrón da a Soria 1.105 habitantes más hasta los 89.482. Villanueva y Valdeprado, los 'reyes de la despoblación'

Soria defieses rural depopulation trends by gaining residents, driven primarily by migration rather than natural growth. This demographic shift indicates that targeted migration policies can effectively reverse population decline in struggling regions, challenging the narrative of inevitable rural emptiness. The data highlights a stark internal disparity, with the capital absorbing much of the growth while numerous small municipalities face extreme depopulation. This uneven distribution underscores the urgent need for differentiated local strategies, as uniform national policies often fail to address the unique challenges of highly isolated areas versus urban centers. For open data communities, these statistics illustrate the value of high-resolution, localized demographic datasets. Access to granular municipal records allows for precise analysis of migration drivers and resource allocation, enabling researchers and policymakers to monitor territorial balance and design evidence-based interventions to sustain vulnerable communities.
