Recomendaciones del INAI para proteger tu seguridad en compras en línea y viajes | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI provides essential guidelines for safeguarding personal data during online transactions and travel, emphasizing proactive digital hygiene. Key measures include avoiding suspicious websites, securing wireless connections, and restricting sensitive information sharing on social media to prevent identity theft and cybercrime. Crucially, the advice highlights protecting mobile devices through encryption and backups, securing physical mail, and carrying minimal documentation while traveling. These steps collectively reduce vulnerabilities to both digital fraud and physical security breaches, ensuring a safer user experience. This content is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical link between individual data privacy and public trust in digital systems. By promoting responsible data handling practices, it supports the broader open data ecosystem, which relies on citizens feeling secure enough to engage with and benefit from transparent, accessible information resources without fear of exploitation.
