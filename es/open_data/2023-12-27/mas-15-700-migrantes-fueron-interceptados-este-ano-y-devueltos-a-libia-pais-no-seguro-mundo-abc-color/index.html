Más 15.700 migrantes fueron interceptados este año y devueltos a Libia, "país no seguro" - Mundo - ABC Color

The Central Mediterranean route remains deadly, with rising migrant deaths prompting MSF to blame European border externalization policies for preventing rescue efforts. Reports of severe abuses in Libyan detention centers further highlight the human rights crisis facing displaced individuals. Human rights organizations urge the EU to suspend cooperation with Libyan authorities, labeling the country unsafe for deportations. This call for policy revision addresses the systemic mistreatment and arbitrary detention faced by thousands of migrants currently held in Libya. This issue is crucial for open data because transparent, accessible datasets on migration flows and detention conditions are essential for verifying reports. Reliable open information enables civil society to monitor policy impacts, advocate for accountability, and expose the real-time humanitarian consequences of current border management strategies.
