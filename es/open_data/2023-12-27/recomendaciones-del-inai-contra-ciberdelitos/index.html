Recomendaciones del INAI contra ciberdelitos

The INAI urges citizens to protect their personal data from cybercrime during online transactions and travel by avoiding suspicious websites and unsecured digital environments. This guidance highlights the critical importance of verifying the integrity of information sources, a core tenet of open data ecosystems where trust and accuracy are paramount. By refusing dubious links and public connectivity, individuals safeguard the authenticity of their digital identity against manipulation and theft. Furthermore, the agency emphasizes minimizing the digital footprint by withholding real-time location data and limiting the sharing of personal documents. This advice underscores the risks associated with excessive data exposure, illustrating why controlling data access is essential for preserving individual rights and security in an interconnected world. These measures are relevant to open data because they define the necessary boundaries for responsible data handling. They reinforce the principle that while information should be accessible, it must also be protected from malicious use, ensuring that the availability of data does not compromise the safety and privacy of the citizens whose information it contains.
