Ministerio de Economía premia al INE por el censo 2022, pese a innumerables críticas - Policiales - ABC Color

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) was awarded a Transparency Innovation Prize for its Transparent Census initiative, which publicly details the financial resources used for the 2022 census. This recognition highlights the potential of open data platforms to enhance governmental accountability and resource visibility among state entities. However, this achievement contrasts sharply with significant criticisms regarding the census execution. The Comptroller General identified numerous administrative failures and financial irregularities, while widespread public distrust emerged over demographic discrepancies and perceived undercounting. These controversies underscore the challenges of maintaining data integrity amidst political scrutiny. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the complex relationship between transparency and trust. While publishing financial data is a step toward openness, the lack of confidence in the final results demonstrates that technical transparency alone cannot resolve deeper issues of statistical credibility and effective governance, which remain critical for open data ecosystems.
