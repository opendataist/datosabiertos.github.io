Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article underscores the critical role of transparent data sharing in restoring public trust during disaster recovery. By openly communicating scientific findings, authorities aim to justify the gradual return of residents to affected areas, ensuring that decisions are rooted in empirical evidence rather than emotional pressure. This approach highlights how open access to reliable information is essential for navigating complex post-crisis situations effectively. Scientific advisory committees provide the necessary validation for policy changes, defending their methodology against unfair criticism. Their work demonstrates that objective analysis is superior to political expediency, establishing a precedent where community safety is prioritized through rigorous, data-driven protocols. This reinforces the importance of independent expertise in guiding public administration during emergencies. This narrative is relevant to open data because it illustrates the practical necessity of data transparency in governance. It shows that when citizens can access and understand the underlying data behind administrative decisions, compliance with safety protocols improves. Ultimately, the text argues that open data is not merely a technical requirement but a foundational element for democratic accountability and successful societal recovery after natural disasters.
