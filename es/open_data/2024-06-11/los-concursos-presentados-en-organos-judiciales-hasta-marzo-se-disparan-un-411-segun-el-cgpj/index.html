Los concursos presentados en órganos judiciales hasta marzo se disparan un 41,1%, según el CGPJ

The first quarter of 2024 saw a significant surge in legal proceedings, marked by a sharp rise in insolvency cases for individuals and companies, alongside substantial increases in dismissal and wage claim disputes. This data reveals a growing strain on the judicial system, driven largely by personal financial distress and shifting labor market dynamics rather than just corporate failures. The uneven geographic distribution highlights that regions like Catalonia, Madrid, and Andalusia are bearing the brunt of these legal challenges. While corporate insolvencies rose steadily, individual non-business bankrupts skyrocketed, indicating widespread personal economic vulnerability. Conversely, business owner insolvencies fell, suggesting a divergence in how different economic agents are experiencing current pressures. This dataset is crucial for open_data initiatives because it provides transparent, granular insights into the health of the judicial and economic systems. By making such detailed statistics accessible, researchers and policymakers can better analyze the root causes of legal disputes, track regional disparities in real-time, and develop evidence-based strategies to support vulnerable populations and optimize public resource allocation.
