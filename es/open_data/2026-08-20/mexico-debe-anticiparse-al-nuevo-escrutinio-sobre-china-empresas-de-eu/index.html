México debe anticiparse al nuevo escrutinio sobre China: empresas de EU

The American Society in Mexico warns that US scrutiny of Chinese-origin goods under the USMCA is intensifying, urging Mexico to address this with technical precision. The core issue lies in distinguishing legitimate international supply chain integration from illegal circumvention. Mexico must recognize that while its manufacturing sector legitimately adds value, the reliance on Asian inputs creates vulnerabilities. Failing to proactively manage this perception risks damaging the reputation of compliant Mexican exporters, as vague narratives can lead to broad punitive measures rather than targeted corrections. Open data emerges as the critical solution to this diplomatic and economic challenge. By implementing robust traceability mechanisms and sharing transparent data, Mexico can prove the genuine origin and transformation processes of its exports. This transparency is essential for maintaining trust and ensuring that trade benefits are accurately attributed to domestic production. Without open, verifiable data, the entire manufacturing sector faces the risk of being tainted by the actions of a few, making data integrity a fundamental commercial asset rather than just a regulatory requirement. The article highlights that proactive transparency allows Mexico to shift from a defensive stance to a strategic advantage. Establishing clear, data-driven protocols for origin verification not only protects legitimate businesses but also strengthens North American industrial resilience. Ultimately, the ability to demonstrate value creation through open information is vital for future trade security, ensuring that Mexico’s role in global supply chains remains trusted and sustainable amidst geopolitical tensions.
