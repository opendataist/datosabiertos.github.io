Lanzaron el Censo 2023: el 29 de abril se iniciará la etapa digital y se podrá ingresar la información a través de la web

Uruguay has launched its 2023 Population and Housing Census, initiating a digital self-counting phase before proceeding to field visits. This massive statistical effort aims to characterize living conditions and societal needs, providing the essential data required to shape effective public policies. The government emphasizes that comprehensive information is vital for making informed decisions that improve the quality of life for all citizens. This initiative underscores the critical role of accurate demographic data in transparent governance. By combining digital access with traditional census methods, the state seeks to maximize participation and ensure no community is overlooked. The resulting dataset will serve as a foundational resource for analyzing social dynamics and addressing structural inequalities across the nation. The relevance to open data lies in the potential to transform these collected statistics into accessible public resources. When properly published, such census data enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to monitor government performance and advocate for evidence-based reforms. Ultimately, releasing this granular information fosters accountability and empowers society to engage with the realities behind the numbers, driving continuous improvement in public services.
