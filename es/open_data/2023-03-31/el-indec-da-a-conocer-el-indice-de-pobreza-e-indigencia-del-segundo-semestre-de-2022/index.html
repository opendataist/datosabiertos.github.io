El INDEC da a conocer el índice de Pobreza e Indigencia del segundo semestre de 2022

Argentina’s poverty rates are projected to rise in late 2022 due to food price inflation, potentially nearing forty percent. This shift highlights the growing struggle between household incomes and the rising costs of basic food baskets, indicating worsening economic conditions for millions of citizens. Transparent statistical data is crucial for understanding this decline in purchasing power. High-quality, accessible public records allow researchers and policymakers to track these trends accurately, ensuring that the impact of inflation on vulnerable populations is clearly visible and documented. Open data initiatives are essential here, as they enable independent verification of official statistics. By making this information freely available, society can better analyze the implications of these economic indicators and demand effective policies to address the rising levels of destitution and social inequality.
