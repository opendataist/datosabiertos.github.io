Frenazo de la inflación: Se sitúa al 3,3 % al compararse con el dato del inicio de la guerra en Ucrania

Spain’s core inflation has declined for the first time since September, reaching 7.5% due to a significant drop in electricity prices. This sustained decrease, driven by the Iberian solution, positions Spain among European countries with the lowest inflation rates, contrasting sharply with the high levels seen a year prior. This trend highlights the effectiveness of targeted energy policies in stabilizing consumer prices. The widening gap between general and core inflation underscores how energy costs have disproportionately impacted overall figures compared to underlying economic pressures. The article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, timely statistical indicators like advance IPC data empower policymakers and the public to monitor economic health. It emphasizes the importance of accessible data in evaluating the real-world impact of governmental measures on inflation and cost of living.
