Pobreza: más de 18 millones de argentinos son pobres

Argentina’s official statistical agency revealed a significant rise in poverty during the second half of 2022, driven by the rapid growth of the basic food basket outpacing household income. This disparity highlights how economic pressures directly impact vulnerable populations, particularly children, across all regions. The data underscores the urgent need for transparent, accessible official statistics to understand social vulnerability. By making these figures public, the state enables civil society and researchers to analyze the real-time effects of inflation on living standards and resource accessibility. This transparency is crucial for open data initiatives, as it allows for independent verification and deeper analysis of socioeconomic trends. Such accessible information empowers citizens and organizations to advocate for effective policies and hold institutions accountable regarding the welfare of the most affected demographics.
