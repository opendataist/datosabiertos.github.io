Con una apuesta por el "autocenso digital", Uruguay lanza su censo 2023 - Mundo - ABC Color

Uruguay introduces digital self-census as a primary strategy, joining Latin American leaders in leveraging online participation for efficient data collection. This shift emphasizes the strategic value of modernizing census methods to engage citizens directly through accessible electronic devices, moving away from traditional reliance solely on in-person visits. The initiative aims to generate high-quality, up-to-date information crucial for designing effective public policies and supporting academic research. By establishing a robust new sampling frame, the census ensures that subsequent surveys benefit from accurate demographic baselines, enhancing the overall reliability of statistical data available to the public and researchers. This approach is highly relevant to open data as it promotes the creation of comprehensive, modernized datasets. The resulting information supports transparency and evidence-based decision-making, illustrating how national statistics agencies can utilize innovative technologies to improve data accessibility and utility for society.
