The Bolivian Institute of National Statistics has finalized cartographic updates in major urban centers, covering 80% of the population. This progress ensures precise operational zones for census takers ahead of the upcoming national count. By establishing accurate geographic boundaries, the initiative significantly improves data quality for the upcoming census. This technical foundation is vital for generating reliable statistical information that reflects the true distribution of the populace. This work is essential for open data as it provides the structured, granular geographic framework necessary for accurate spatial analysis. High-quality base maps enable transparent, reproducible insights into demographic patterns, fostering better public policy and civic engagement through accessible, precise data.
