La inflación de la OCDE se moderó en mayo al 6,5%, su menor nivel desde diciembre de 2021

Global inflation moderated in May, driven by a sharp decline in energy prices, though significant disparities persist across member nations. Food costs also eased, indicating a broader trend of price stabilization in many advanced economies, despite extreme volatility in specific countries like Turkey and Hungary. This data is crucial for open_data as it highlights the necessity of accessible, granular economic indicators. Transparent metrics allow researchers to analyze inflation trends and regional inequalities effectively. High-quality public data enables better scrutiny of monetary policies and cost-of-living impacts. By making such statistics readily available, society can foster informed debate and hold institutions accountable for managing economic volatility and ensuring equitable outcomes.
