The article concludes that the national economy is poised for continued growth, as key cyclical indicators in Mexico have consistently remained above their long-term trends. This positive trajectory, highlighted by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography, suggests that short-term economic momentum is strong and stable across various sectors. Three primary components drove the current economic activity, including industrial output and permanent employment, which outweighed declines in retail revenues and imports. Similarly, forward-looking indicators reflect optimism through improved business confidence, stock market valuations, and international market correlations, despite mixed signals in manufacturing employment and interest rates. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how diverse, high-frequency statistical datasets can be integrated to create reliable composite indices. By making these underlying economic signals transparent and accessible, such data enables policymakers and analysts to monitor real-time economic health, fostering greater accountability and informed decision-making based on empirical evidence rather than speculation.
