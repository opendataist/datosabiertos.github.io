Municipios de Beni y Santa Cruz sellan convenio con el INE para coordinar labor pre censal

The National Institute of Statistics has successfully secured partnerships with the vast majority of local governments in Santa Cruz and Beni to coordinate pre-census activities, specifically the Statistical Cartographic Update. This strategic coordination has enabled the statistical agency to overcome logistical challenges and significant delays, such as those caused by recent strikes, ensuring that preparatory work remains on track for the upcoming national census. A critical implication of this effort is the successful inclusion of remote indigenous territories, notably the Isiboro-Sécure National Park and Indigenous Territory in Beni. By collaborating with local authorities and indigenous leaders, the INE was able to guide teams into hard-to-reach communities. This demonstrates how inter-institutional agreements are essential for achieving comprehensive geographic coverage, ensuring that marginalized populations are not excluded from national demographic data. This progress is vital to open data because it establishes the foundational geospatial infrastructure necessary for accurate, inclusive, and transparent public statistics. Reliable cartographic updates ensure that subsequent census data is correctly mapped, enhancing the quality and accessibility of government information. Furthermore, the emphasis on coordinating with diverse local entities highlights the importance of collaborative governance in producing trustworthy public datasets that truly reflect the entire population’s reality.
