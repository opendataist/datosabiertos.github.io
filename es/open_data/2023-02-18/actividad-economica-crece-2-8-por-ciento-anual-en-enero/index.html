Actividad económica crece 2.8 por ciento anual en enero

The Mexican economy demonstrated resilience in late 2022 and early 2023, driven primarily by the recovery of the service sector. Despite facing inflationary pressures and a slowdown in US industrial activity, the economic indicators suggest stability and continued growth. This data highlights the sector's ability to withstand complex external challenges during a volatile period. This information is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of accessing official statistical sources, such as INEGI’s IOAE and IGAE. These public datasets are crucial for analysts to track real-time economic performance, enabling accurate forecasts and informed decision-making based on transparent, government-generated metrics. Understanding these public indicators allows stakeholders to interpret broader economic trends beyond raw numbers. By utilizing open datasets, experts can assess the impact of monetary policy and international relations on local markets, ensuring that public policy and business strategies are grounded in verified, accessible factual evidence.
