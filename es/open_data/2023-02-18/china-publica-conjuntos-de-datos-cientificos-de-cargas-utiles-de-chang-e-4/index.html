China publica conjuntos de datos científicos de cargas útiles de Chang'e-4

China has released new scientific data from the Chang'e-4 mission on the Moon's far side, continuing the legacy of Yutu-2. This initiative expands access to deep-space exploration results, enhancing global understanding of lunar geology and environment. This release is relevant to open data as it democratizes high-value scientific resources. By making such datasets freely available, it fosters international collaboration and accelerates research through transparent, unrestricted access to space mission findings.
