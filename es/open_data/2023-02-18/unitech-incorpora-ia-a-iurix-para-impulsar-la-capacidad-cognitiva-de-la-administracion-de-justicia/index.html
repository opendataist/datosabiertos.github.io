Unitech incorpora IA a IURIX para impulsar la capacidad cognitiva de la administración de justicia

Unitech has integrated advanced Transformer models into its judicial management platform, IURIX, to significantly enhance the cognitive capabilities of the legal system. This implementation aims to streamline judicial administration by automating complex language tasks, thereby increasing operational efficiency and effectiveness across various courts. The technology enables critical functions such as automatic document analysis, summary generation, entity extraction, and sentence anonymization. By leveraging natural language processing and generation, the system reduces judicial delays and improves the overall handling of legal cases. This update is relevant to open_data as it highlights the growing intersection of artificial intelligence and legal transparency. The ability to anonymize judgments and automatically extract entities facilitates the cleaning and structuring of sensitive legal data, making it more accessible and secure for public scrutiny and further data-driven research in the justice sector.
