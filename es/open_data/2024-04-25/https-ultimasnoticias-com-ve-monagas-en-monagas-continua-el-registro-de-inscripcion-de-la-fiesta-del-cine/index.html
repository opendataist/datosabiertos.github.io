The Festival Internacional de Cine Secuencias continues to promote audiovisual talent by accepting short film registrations until May 30. As a unique national event, it highlights the global reach of Venezuelan cinema through strict artistic constraints. This initiative demonstrates how cultural platforms foster community engagement and social transformation. By expanding its influence beyond national borders, the festival proves that localized artistic movements can achieve significant international relevance and recognition. This case is relevant to open_data as it illustrates the importance of documenting and sharing cultural participation metrics. Transparent records of submissions and selections enable better analysis of artistic trends, supporting data-driven decisions that amplify the visibility and impact of local creative industries on a global scale.
