The article reports a devastating explosion in San Cristóbal that resulted in numerous deaths, widespread material destruction, and profound psychological trauma for survivors. As authorities investigate the causes, the nation grieves collectively, with the tragedy transcending local boundaries to evoke sorrow across the entire country and beyond. Poet José Reyes contributes to this mourning by sharing evocative verses that capture the horror and human cost of the event. His work reflects on the ambition and negligence behind the disaster, contrasting the suffering of vulnerable workers with the ego that may have facilitated such catastrophe, thereby giving voice to the collective anguish through artistic expression. This content highlights the emotional and societal dimensions of open data, reminding us that behind every dataset of casualties and damages are human lives and stories. Understanding the context of such tragedies is crucial for transparent governance and effective policy. Open data should not only quantify loss but also foster empathy and accountability, ensuring that statistical information serves to support communities and prevent future preventable disasters.
