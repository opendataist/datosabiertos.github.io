Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Argentina's National Statistics and Census Institute recently released detailed reports highlighting positive trends in the industrial and construction sectors. The manufacturing industry experienced significant growth, driven by robust performance in key areas such as vehicles, metal products, and machinery. This expansion contrasts with declines in chemical and textile sectors, revealing a diverse economic landscape where specific industrial branches are thriving while others face contraction. Simultaneously, the construction sector showed modest annual growth in March, although early-year figures indicated a slight decline. Material consumption data reveals increased demand for foundational inputs like concrete and granite, signaling ongoing project activity. However, reduced usage of certain building materials suggests shifting dynamics within the sector. These divergent trends in material consumption provide a nuanced view of current construction efforts and resource allocation across the country. This information is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of freely accessible, granular statistical records for public scrutiny and analysis. By making such detailed sectoral breakdowns available, institutions empower researchers and citizens to track economic health independently. This transparency fosters informed decision-making, allowing stakeholders to validate official narratives against raw data, thereby strengthening accountability and promoting evidence-based policy discussions in governance.
