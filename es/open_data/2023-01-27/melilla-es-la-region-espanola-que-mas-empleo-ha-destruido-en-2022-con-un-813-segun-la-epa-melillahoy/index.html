Melilla es la región española que más empleo ha destruido en 2022 con un 8,13%, según la EPA - MelillaHoy

The article highlights stark regional disparities in Spain’s labor market, with Melilla suffering significant job losses and high unemployment, while territories like Ceuta and the Balearic Islands saw substantial employment growth. Nationally, the trend is positive, as overall unemployment dropped and job creation increased in 2022, signaling a general recovery despite local extremes. This data is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates the importance of high-quality, accessible statistical sources like the EPA. Transparent employment metrics allow for precise geographic analysis, enabling researchers and policymakers to identify structural inequalities and allocate resources effectively to regions in crisis. Ultimately, the narrative underscores that aggregate national improvements can mask severe local challenges. For open data advocates, this reinforces the need for granular, spatially disaggregated datasets to ensure that policy interventions address the specific needs of struggling areas rather than relying solely on broad national averages.
