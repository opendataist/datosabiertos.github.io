El PIB de EEUU crece más de lo esperado en el cuarto trimestre y sube un 2,9%

US GDP growth in late 2022 exceeded analyst expectations, ending the year with a solid 2.1% annual expansion that alleviated recession fears. Despite this positive performance, future outlooks remain cautious due to aggressive interest rate hikes, creating uncertainty about whether the Federal Reserve will maintain its current stance or adjust its policy based on these resilient data points. This economic update is highly relevant to open_data as it relies on timely, transparent reporting from the Bureau of Economic Analysis. The availability of such high-quality, public macroeconomic statistics allows researchers and developers to build accurate predictive models, analyze market trends, and ensure accountability in financial forecasting without relying on proprietary or obscured datasets.
