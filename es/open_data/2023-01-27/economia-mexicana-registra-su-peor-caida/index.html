Economía mexicana registra su peor caída

Mexico’s economy contracted more than anticipated in late 2022, signaling a significant loss of resilience and raising concerns about a potential regional recession. Experts warn that the recovery remains fragile, particularly as external risks from the United States could exacerbate local economic stagnation. This downturn highlights vulnerabilities across key sectors, with services and industry experiencing notable declines while only agriculture showed modest growth. The broader implications suggest that previous assumptions about economic stability are no longer valid, demanding urgent policy attention to prevent further deterioration. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, high-frequency economic indicators like the IGAE enable real-time monitoring of national health. Access to such granular, timely data allows analysts and the public to challenge official estimates and hold institutions accountable for accurate, timely reporting on national performance.
