La pobreza subió al 32,3% en el primer semestre de 2026: la indigencia alcanzó el 7,5%, según el Indec

The official data reveals a significant deterioration in economic conditions, with poverty rates surpassing thirty percent and indigence increasing notably between the analyzed periods. This rise is fundamentally driven by a widening gap where the cost of basic living baskets outpaced the growth of household incomes, effectively eroding the purchasing power of families and pushing more citizens below the poverty line. The impact is not uniform, demonstrating clear vulnerabilities across different demographics and regions. Children and adolescents are disproportionately affected, with nearly half of those under eighteen living in poverty, highlighting a severe intergenerational risk. Regionally, areas such as the Northeast and greater metropolitan zones show higher concentrations of economic hardship, indicating that specific localities face structural challenges that exacerbate national trends. This information is critical for the open data community as it exemplifies the necessity of transparent, accessible, and granular statistical releases. By making detailed demographic and regional breakdowns available, institutions enable researchers, journalists, and civil society to move beyond aggregate figures, fostering a deeper understanding of social inequalities and holding policymakers accountable for the real-world impact of economic decisions.
