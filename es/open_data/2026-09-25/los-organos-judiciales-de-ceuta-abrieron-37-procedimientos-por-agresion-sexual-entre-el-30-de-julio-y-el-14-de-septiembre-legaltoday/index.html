Los órganos judiciales de Ceuta abrieron 37 procedimientos por agresión sexual entre el 30 de julio y el 14 de septiembre - LegalToday

The report reveals a disturbing pattern of sexual aggression in Ceuta, highlighting the judicial system's struggle to address a high volume of cases involving mostly female victims, many of whom are minors. This disparity underscores systemic vulnerabilities where a significant portion of alleged aggressors remain unidentified, complicating accountability and justice. Understanding this data is crucial for open data initiatives because it exposes transparency gaps in judicial statistics. Publishing detailed, anonymized datasets on case progression and victim demographics allows for independent analysis, fostering public trust and enabling policymakers to design targeted interventions against gender-based violence.
