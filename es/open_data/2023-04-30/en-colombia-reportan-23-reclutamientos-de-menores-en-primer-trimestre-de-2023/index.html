En Colombia reportan 23 reclutamientos de menores en primer trimestre de 2023

The Defensoría del Pueblo reports a disturbing increase in the recruitment of children and adolescents by illegal armed groups across Colombia. This trend highlights a critical deterioration in security, particularly in rural and ethnic communities where state presence is minimal. The surge indicates that armed factions are consolidating territorial control while exploiting vulnerable populations for their conflicts. This issue is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible information to monitor human rights violations effectively. Detailed, disaggregated data on these incidents allows for better analysis of geographic hotspots and demographic vulnerabilities. Such transparency empowers civil society and international bodies to track trends, demand accountability, and design targeted interventions that protect children’s rights in conflict zones.
