Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Citizen Front challenges the municipal administration's claim of open governance, highlighting a significant breakdown in coordination regarding the solid waste management council. Critics emphasize that the lack of formal agendas and meeting minutes undermines transparency and effective dialogue between civil society and the government. Furthermore, severe concerns are raised about the unsafe working conditions for waste pickers, noting that improper waste handling exposes them to continuous health risks. This neglect demonstrates a failure to prioritize worker safety and public welfare, contradicting the administration’s promises of closeness and care for citizens. This situation is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical gap between proclaimed transparency and actual data accessibility. Without shared meeting records, schedules, or health reports, civil society cannot verify progress or hold officials accountable, proving that true open data requires proactive information disclosure rather than just rhetorical commitments.
