The Mexican automotive industry experienced robust growth in early 2023, with both production and exports rising significantly compared to the previous year. This positive trajectory indicates a recovering sector where light trucks dominated manufacturing, driving overall volume increases across the first quarter. Internal market sales also surged, reflecting strong domestic demand alongside export achievements led by major manufacturers like General Motors and Stellantis. The data highlights a comprehensive expansion in the industry, benefiting from high production rates and successful international trade dynamics that boosted the national economic contribution of the sector. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on public statistics from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography. It demonstrates how government-generated datasets on industrial activity provide transparent, verifiable evidence of economic trends. Such accessible official records are essential for researchers, analysts, and policymakers to accurately assess industrial performance and make informed decisions based on reliable, publicly available information.
