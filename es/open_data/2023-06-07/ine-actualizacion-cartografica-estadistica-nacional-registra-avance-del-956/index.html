INE: Actualización Cartográfica Estadística nacional registra avance del 95,6%

Bolivia has nearly completed its statistical cartographic update, securing precise georeferencing of households across all departments. This foundational work transforms raw field data into structured spatial information, essential for accurate census planning. The update enables the National Statistics Institute to determine optimal resource allocation and census logistics by defining questionnaire counts and enumerator zones based on actual dwelling distributions. This achievement is vital for open data because it establishes a transparent, reliable, and geospatially aware baseline. Such granular, publicly verifiable data supports accountability and enhances the quality of future demographic analysis and civic engagement.
