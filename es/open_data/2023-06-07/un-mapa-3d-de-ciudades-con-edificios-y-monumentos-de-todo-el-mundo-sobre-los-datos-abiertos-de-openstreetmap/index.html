Un Mapa 3D de ciudades con edificios y monumentos de todo el mundo sobre los datos abiertos de OpenStreetMap

F4Map demonstrates the potential of OpenStreetMap by transforming collaborative geographic data into interactive, fluid 2D-to-3D experiences. This technology highlights how public contributions enable detailed urban modeling, showcasing buildings, monuments, and natural elements like water and trees across major cities with varying levels of precision. The project illustrates the commercial and creative utility of open data, serving sectors from civil engineering to video game development. By leveraging freely available public information, it proves that community-driven datasets can support sophisticated, custom mapping solutions for virtual worlds and specialized projects. This is relevant to open data as it validates OpenStreetMap’s infrastructure as a robust, versatile foundation. It shows that non-proprietary, crowd-sourced geospatial information is not merely for navigation but can be repurposed for high-fidelity applications, encouraging broader reuse and innovation within the open data ecosystem.
