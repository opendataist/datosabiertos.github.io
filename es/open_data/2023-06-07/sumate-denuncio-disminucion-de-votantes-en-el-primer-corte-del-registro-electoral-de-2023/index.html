Súmate denunció disminución de votantes en el primer corte del Registro Electoral de 2023

The Venezuelan electoral registry shows a significant decline in registered voters, contradicting efforts to ensure inclusive democratic participation. This reduction highlights systemic failures in maintaining an accurate and up-to-date electoral roll, leaving millions of citizens effectively disenfranchised. The trend of decreasing registration raises serious concerns about the legitimacy and representativeness of future elections. When the state fails to update records promptly, it undermines the fundamental right to vote and creates barriers for eligible populations to exercise their civic duties. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible, and timely public datasets are crucial for holding institutions accountable. Without reliable open electoral data, civil society cannot verify claims or advocate for the necessary updates to guarantee fair and inclusive democratic processes.
