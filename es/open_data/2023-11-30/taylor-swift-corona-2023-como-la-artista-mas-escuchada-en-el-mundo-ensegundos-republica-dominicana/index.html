Taylor Swift corona 2023 como la artista más escuchada en el mundo Ensegundos República Dominicana

Taylor Swift has claimed the title of the world’s most-streamed artist, surpassing previous leaders, while Miley Cyrus’s song topped the annual charts. This shift in streaming dominance highlights the dynamic nature of global music consumption, driven by platform algorithms and viral trends. Regional Mexican music also achieved significant visibility, with Peso Pluma rising to prominence alongside established stars. The surge of this genre indicates a broadening of listener preferences beyond traditional pop, suggesting that diverse musical styles are gaining substantial traction in the digital marketplace. These findings are relevant to open data as they demonstrate how large-scale public datasets reveal shifting cultural trends and market dynamics.
