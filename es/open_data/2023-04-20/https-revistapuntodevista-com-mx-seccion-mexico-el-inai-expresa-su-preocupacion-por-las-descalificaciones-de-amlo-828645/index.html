Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) expresses deep concern over the continuous denigration by the Executive Branch and the Secretary of the Interior towards its autonomous functions. These attacks threaten not only the existence of this vital democratic institution but also undermine fundamental constitutional rights, including the right to access public information and the protection of personal data. The INAI serves as a critical mechanism for citizens to verify social programs, request medical records, and demand accountability for data misuse. Through its resolutions, it has successfully exposed significant corruption cases, such as Segalmex and the "Estafa Maestra," demonstrating its essential role in transparency. As the sole authority protecting personal data across both public and private sectors, its autonomy is indispensable for maintaining democratic integrity and ensuring human rights are respected. This situation is highly relevant to open data because the INAI acts as the primary guardian ensuring that information is not only available but also secure and accessible for civic oversight. By defending its independence against political pressure, the INAI safeguards the infrastructure that allows civil society to exercise their right to know, which is foundational for a transparent and accountable open government ecosystem.
