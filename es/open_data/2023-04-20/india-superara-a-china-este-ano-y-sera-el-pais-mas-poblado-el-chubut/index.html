India superará a China este año y será el país más poblado - El Chubut

The UNFPA report highlights a historic demographic shift as India surpasses China to become the world’s most populous nation. This milestone underscores the end of China’s long-standing dominance, driven by its declining birth rates and aging workforce following the relaxation of strict family planning policies. Conversely, India’s rapid growth presents significant challenges, including the urgent need to provide employment, housing, and resources for a young, expanding population. The relevance to open data lies in the critical lack of current demographic transparency in India. The government has repeatedly postponed the national census, creating a substantial gap in reliable, up-to-date statistical information. This delay raises concerns about data integrity and accessibility, as critics suspect political motivations behind the withholding of figures that could impact electoral outcomes and policy formulation. Globally, the narrative signals a transition toward a population peak followed by a gradual decline, with Africa poised for significant growth while Europe and Asia face shrinking demographics. This article is vital for open data advocates because it emphasizes how incomplete or delayed official statistics hinder effective global understanding and resource allocation. Reliable, timely, and transparent population data is essential for governments and organizations to accurately address these divergent demographic trends and plan for future socio-economic stability.
