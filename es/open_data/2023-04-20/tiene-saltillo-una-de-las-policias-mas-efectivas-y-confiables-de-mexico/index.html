Tiene Saltillo una de las policías más efectivas y confiables de México

Saltillo stands out as Mexico’s most secure capital, with a majority of residents feeling safe and viewing their municipal police as highly effective. The city ranks among the top nationally for both police efficacy and public trust, surpassing most other urban centers in these perceptions. This highlights the critical role of open data in transparency and governance. By publicly sharing detailed survey results, institutions like INEGI allow citizens to verify safety metrics objectively. Such accessible information fosters accountability and helps identify successful public security models. Ultimately, this case demonstrates how open data empowers communities. When governments release granular safety statistics, it encourages comparative analysis and drives policy improvements based on empirical evidence rather than anecdote.
