La facturación de la industria sube un 8,8% en febrero y la de los servicios, un 7,7%

The Spanish industrial and service sectors have achieved twenty-four consecutive months of growth following the pandemic shock, indicating a robust and sustained economic recovery. This steady expansion suggests that the national economy has successfully stabilized and is generating consistent momentum across key production areas, moving beyond initial post-crisis volatility toward a more resilient operational baseline. Geographically and sectorally, the benefits of this recovery are widespread, though specific regions like the Basque Country and tourism-dependent islands show the most significant gains. The broad increase in business turnover across nearly all autonomous communities highlights a unified national trend of revitalization, demonstrating that the rebound is not limited to isolated hubs but reflects a systemic improvement in commercial activity throughout the country. Furthermore, this economic upturn is supported by a parallel rise in employment, with job numbers increasing across most regions and sectors. This correlation between revenue growth and hiring underscores the positive multiplier effect of the current economic phase, reinforcing the stability of the labor market as a direct consequence of the improved industrial and service performance.
