Puede llevar más de 6 meses que logres formar el hábito de ir al gimnasio: estudio

Research from Caltech reveals that forming a gym habit takes an average of six months, debunking the idea of a universal "magic number." The study, the first to apply machine learning to habit formation, analyzed vast datasets to identify complex behavioral patterns in real-world settings. Key factors influencing consistency include the time since the last visit and specific days of the week, while other variables like time of day proved irrelevant. This approach moves beyond traditional surveys, leveraging automated tools to discover significant predictors of behavior without pre-existing hypotheses. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the power of large-scale, naturalistic datasets combined with advanced analytics. It highlights how open, diverse data sources can unlock deep insights into human behavior, offering a scalable model for social science research that relies on comprehensive, real-world evidence rather than controlled, artificial environments.
