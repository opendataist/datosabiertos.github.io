Informalidad crece 115% más que toda la economía del país: Inegi

The article highlights the alarming expansion of Mexico’s informal economy, which has grown significantly faster than the national GDP. This disparity reveals a deepening structural issue where informal activities dominate a substantial portion of national resources, undermining formal economic stability and growth metrics. Regional variations show distinct leadership in this trend, with certain states driving the increase while others experience declines. These shifts indicate that informal work is not uniformly distributed but concentrated in specific areas, reflecting diverse local economic pressures and labor market dynamics across different federative entities. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives as it exposes the limitations of official statistics in capturing the true economic landscape. Transparently mapping informal sectors helps policymakers identify gaps in social protection and tax bases, fostering better data-driven strategies to integrate these activities into the formal economy and improve overall national development.
