Voto libre y razonado: ¡Conóceles! - La Capital

Electoral cycles demand informed decision-making, where the right to access information empowers citizens to evaluate candidates objectively. This process transforms voting from a mere ritual into a reasoned act of reflection, requiring transparency to ensure that public trust and participation are upheld. Technological platforms serve as critical instruments for democratizing access to reliable candidate profiles and campaign proposals. By centralizing this data, these systems reduce information asymmetry, allowing voters to align their choices with community interests and ensuring that electoral outcomes genuinely reflect the will of the populace. This initiative is vital for open data because it demonstrates how structured, accessible digital resources enhance civic engagement. Making candidate information publicly available fosters accountability and supports a more educated electorate, proving that open data is not just a technical standard but a fundamental pillar of a healthy, participatory democracy.
