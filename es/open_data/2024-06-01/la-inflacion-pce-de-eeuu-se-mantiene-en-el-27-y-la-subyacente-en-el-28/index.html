La inflación PCE de EEUU se mantiene en el 2,7% y la subyacente, en el 2,8%

The US personal consumption expenditure deflator remained stable in April, with core inflation holding at 2.8 percent, aligning with analyst expectations. This stability suggests that the US economy is continuing its gradual disinflationary path, offering welcome relief to policymakers. The slower monthly increase in core prices indicates that underlying price pressures are cooling, reinforcing the trend toward lower inflation without causing significant volatility in consumer spending or personal income. These data points are crucial for the Federal Reserve’s monetary policy decisions, as they provide the confidence needed to consider interest rate cuts. Experts believe this softer inflation trend increases the likelihood of the Fed reducing rates by autumn, contrasting with market expectations for a later December move. Continued evidence of cooling price pressures over the next few quarters will likely solidify the case for easing monetary restrictions, impacting broader economic stability and borrowing costs.
