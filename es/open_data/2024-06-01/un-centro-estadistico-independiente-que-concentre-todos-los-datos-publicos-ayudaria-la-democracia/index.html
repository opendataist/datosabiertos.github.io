Un centro estadístico independiente que concentre todos los datos públicos ayudaría la democracia

The article emphasizes that reliable, independent statistical data is crucial for guiding democratic governments and shaping effective public policies. It argues that centralizing statistical functions within a constitutional, autonomous national institute prevents political manipulation and ensures the credibility necessary for managing critical sectors like health and economics. Furthermore, the piece highlights that trust in official data strengthens democratic institutions by providing an objective foundation for decision-making, contrasting this with authoritarian regimes where statistics are often controlled or distorted. This independence from government management is presented as essential for accurately measuring economic growth, inflation, and social indicators. This topic is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the foundational need for institutional integrity and transparency in data production. Open data initiatives rely on trustworthy sources; if the underlying statistics are perceived as biased or politically influenced, the broader ecosystem of open information loses its value and utility for citizens and policymakers alike.
