The article highlights a critical failure in verifying public officials' credentials, exemplified by a former senator imprisoned for holding a falsified law degree. This incident underscores the urgent need for transparency and accountability in government hiring processes to prevent unqualified individuals from occupying high-ranking positions. José Duarte advocates for a comprehensive audit of university titles for all public servants, starting with those in leadership roles and graduates from questionable institutions. This verification process aims to purge the public function of individuals who lack the necessary academic preparation, ensuring that only qualified professionals wield public power. This issue is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessity of accessible, verifiable digital records for educational credentials. Implementing open data standards for degree validation would allow citizens and institutions to independently verify qualifications, thereby preventing fraud and restoring trust in the integrity of public service.
