El personal calificado representa la principal dificultad de empresas para reclutar empleados - Negocios - ABC Color

The study reveals a structural disconnect in Paraguay’s labor market, where a significant portion of companies struggle to fill vacant positions due to a lack of qualified personnel. This issue is particularly acute in industrial and service sectors, which require specialized technical skills that the current workforce often lacks. Consequently, businesses face substantial hurdles in meeting their human resource needs, highlighting a critical misalignment between available talent and industry demands. A primary barrier identified is the insufficient technical training among potential employees, exacerbated by a shortage of relevant work experience and socioemotional competencies. The education system appears misaligned with market realities, failing to equip individuals with the specific abilities required by employers. This gap not only hinders recruitment efforts but also impacts overall productivity, as companies cannot find workers who possess the necessary blend of hard and soft skills to operate effectively in competitive environments. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, government-collected statistics can diagnose systemic economic challenges. By making such labor market insights publicly available, policymakers, educators, and businesses can collaborate on targeted solutions. Open data facilitates evidence-based decision-making, enabling the development of educational reforms and public-private partnerships that directly address these identified skill gaps, ultimately fostering sustainable economic growth and improved employability.
