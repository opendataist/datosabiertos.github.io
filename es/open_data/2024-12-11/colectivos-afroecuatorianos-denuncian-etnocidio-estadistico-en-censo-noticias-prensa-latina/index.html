Colectivos afroecuatorianos denuncian etnocidio estadístico en censo - Noticias Prensa Latina

Black organizations in Ecuador have filed a legal action to invalidate the latest census results, arguing that the methodology failed to properly identify Afro-descendant populations. This omission significantly underrepresents their numbers, violating fundamental rights to identity and non-discrimination. Consequently, these groups are demanding that previous data be considered to ensure accurate recognition and protection of their community. The drastic statistical decline in the Afro-Ecuadorian population has raised serious doubts about the accuracy of the current official counts. Activists assert that hundreds of thousands were not adequately censated, undermining the validity of the demographic study. This legal challenge highlights the critical necessity for inclusive data collection methods. This case is highly relevant to open data as it underscores how flawed data collection processes can lead to the invisibility of marginalized groups. Accurate, inclusive datasets are essential for policy-making and human rights advocacy. Without reliable metrics, the rights of specific communities remain unprotected, demonstrating the vital role of transparent and representative public data.
