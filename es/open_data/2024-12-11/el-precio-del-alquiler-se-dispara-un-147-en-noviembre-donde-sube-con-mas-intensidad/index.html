El precio del alquiler se dispara un 14,7% en noviembre: ¿dónde sube con más intensidad?

The rental market in Spain has reached unprecedented historical highs, driven by a critical imbalance between scarce housing supply and intense demand. This structural deficit has pushed prices to their highest levels since 2018, signaling a trend that is expected to continue upward in the near future. The situation highlights a deeply rigid market that is becoming increasingly difficult for citizens to navigate, particularly those with lower incomes who are being effectively excluded from housing opportunities due to unaffordable rates. This affordability crisis exposes significant social vulnerabilities and inequalities, as low-income households are forced out of the market. The widespread nature of these price increases, affecting nearly all regions and provinces, demonstrates that the housing shortage is not isolated to specific urban centers but is a nationwide issue. Consequently, the inability to secure stable housing at reasonable costs is exacerbating social stratification and limiting access to basic living conditions for a substantial portion of the population. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the urgent need for transparent, accessible, and granular housing information. Accurate, real-time metrics on rental prices and availability are essential for policymakers to design effective interventions and for the public to understand market dynamics. Furthermore, such open data facilitates academic research and advocacy, enabling stakeholders to identify disparities and propose solutions that promote housing equity and stability across diverse socioeconomic groups.
