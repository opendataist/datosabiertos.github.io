La inflación interanual en Argentina supera el 270% en febrero, la cifra más alta desde hace tres décadas

Argentina reports historic inflation, with consumer prices rising 276.2% annually in February, the highest in thirty years. Despite this severe surge, the monthly rate has begun to decelerate, suggesting a potential stabilization trend after months of rapid acceleration across all sectors. The data highlights significant increases in essential services and communications, reflecting widespread economic pressure on households. Although monthly growth slows, the annual cumulative impact remains drastic, indicating that underlying price instability persists across the broader economy. This report is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of transparent, high-frequency government statistics. Accurate, publicly accessible datasets allow citizens, researchers, and developers to monitor economic health in real-time, fostering accountability and enabling better analysis of complex financial trends in volatile environments.
