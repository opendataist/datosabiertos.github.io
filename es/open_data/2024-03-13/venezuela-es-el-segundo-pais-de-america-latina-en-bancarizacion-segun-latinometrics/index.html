Venezuela es el segundo país de América Latina en bancarización, según Latinometrics

Venezuela ranks second in Latin America for bank account penetration, a legacy of dollarization and cash scarcity. However, experts caution that widespread inactivity and informal economic practices diminish the practical benefits of this financial inclusion. This case highlights critical open data challenges regarding data quality and context. It demonstrates the necessity of verifying metrics against underlying structural realities to ensure accurate, meaningful insights.
