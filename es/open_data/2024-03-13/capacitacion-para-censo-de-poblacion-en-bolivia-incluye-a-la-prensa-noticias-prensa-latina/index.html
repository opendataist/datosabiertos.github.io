Capacitación para Censo de Población en Bolivia incluye a la prensa - Noticias Prensa Latina

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) has finalized its preparation for Bolivia’s upcoming national census through an extensive communication strategy, training programs, and simulation exercises. With volunteer recruitment exceeding targets and significant training completion rates across departments, the agency ensures readiness to survey over five million households. This comprehensive approach aims to guarantee accurate data collection, highlighting the operational scale and logistical precision required for such a massive demographic undertaking. The census will occur over three days, with urban areas surveyed in one day and rural areas in three. Officials have coordinated with all nine departments to establish administrative measures encouraging citizens to remain home during the count. This cooperation underscores the collaborative framework necessary for successful execution, emphasizing that the process does not alter territorial boundaries, despite some political opposition attempting to create confusion or seek political prominence through misinformation. This update is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical importance of transparent, well-communicated data collection processes. By addressing political interference and ensuring broad public cooperation, the state aims to produce reliable, high-quality census data. Accurate foundational data is essential for generating meaningful open datasets that inform policy, resource allocation, and democratic accountability, proving that robust institutional coordination directly impacts the integrity and utility of public data.
