Inflación en Argentina aumentó 13,2 por ciento en febrero - Noticias Prensa Latina

Argentina’s inflation surge, driven by food, transport, and utilities, severely impacts household affordability. The basic food basket’s steep rise highlights the vulnerability of essential goods, while high thresholds for poverty avoidance underscore the economic strain on families. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent metrics expose inflation’s social impact. By making such economic indicators accessible, citizens and researchers can analyze cost-of-living trends and advocate for policies addressing food security and financial stability.
