Arranca el censo experimental en el municipio de Capinota

The National Institute of Statistics initiated an experimental census in Capinota to validate census methodologies, logistics, and instruments. This trial aims to refine procedures and address potential issues before the main national census scheduled for March 2024. This initiative is relevant to open data as it ensures the future collection of accurate, reliable demographic information. Validating these processes is crucial for generating trustworthy public statistics that support evidence-based policy-making and transparent governance.
