La actividad económica acumuló un retroceso del 3,4% en el primer semestre del año

The national economy has contracted significantly, marking consecutive quarterly declines driven by sharp drops in private consumption and investment. This downturn suggests that economic stability has not yet been achieved, challenging optimistic government forecasts of a quick recovery. The imbalance between falling private demand and public spending highlights structural weaknesses in the economic model. Expert analysis indicates that the current slump is deeper than initially projected, raising concerns about the sustainability of public policies and the severity of the recession. This data is crucial for open data because it illustrates the importance of transparent, accessible economic indicators for public scrutiny. When statistical offices provide granular, timely information, it enables independent verification of government claims, fostering accountability and allowing society to critically assess policy impacts.
