Castilla-La Mancha registró 8.204 nacimientos hasta julio, un 6% menos que el año pasado

The article highlights a persistent decline in Spanish births, contrasting with a slight recent uptick. This trend underscores the urgency for transparent, real-time demographic data to monitor societal aging. Open data enables researchers and policymakers to analyze these shifts accurately. By making vital statistics accessible, stakeholders can identify patterns in fertility rates and mortality without relying on opaque or delayed reports. Ultimately, this transparency fosters better-informed public health strategies. Understanding these demographic changes through open data is crucial for addressing the long-term impacts on Spain’s social security and family support systems.
