España revisa al alza su crecimiento de 2021-2023 pese a subida de tipos y la inflación

Spain has upwardly revised its annual economic growth for 2021-2023, demonstrating resilience against inflation and rising interest rates while outperforming regional peers. This stronger, more balanced expansion allowed the government to achieve debt reduction targets ahead of schedule, reflecting a rapid post-pandemic recovery. The article highlights Spain's superior performance compared to the broader eurozone, driven by robust consumer spending and a tourism boom. Consequently, national institutions raised growth forecasts, contrasting with the European Central Bank's downgraded regional predictions. This data is relevant to open_data as it exemplifies how transparent, official statistical updates from national institutes like the INE enable real-time economic analysis. Reliable, publicly accessible datasets allow researchers and institutions to validate trends, adjust forecasts, and compare economic health across borders effectively.
