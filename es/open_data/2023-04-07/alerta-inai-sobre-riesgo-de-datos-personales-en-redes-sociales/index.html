Alerta INAI sobre riesgo de datos personales en redes sociales

The INAI warns that sharing personal data on social media poses serious risks, enabling criminals to locate individuals and plan crimes like home invasions. This vulnerability is particularly acute among young users, who dominate social media usage in Mexico. Consequently, the institute urges cautious behavior, such as securing accounts, avoiding strangers, and carefully managing the public visibility of personal information. This warning is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical intersection between public information access and personal privacy rights. It emphasizes that data shared openly can be exploited, demonstrating the urgent need for robust data protection frameworks. Understanding these risks is essential for promoting ethical data practices and ensuring that the benefits of open information do not come at the cost of individual security. Ultimately, the guide serves as a vital resource for users to configure privacy settings effectively. By encouraging responsible data sharing and digital literacy, the INAI aims to empower citizens to navigate the digital landscape safely. This approach supports a balanced ecosystem where transparency and privacy coexist, fostering trust in both public institutions and digital platforms.
