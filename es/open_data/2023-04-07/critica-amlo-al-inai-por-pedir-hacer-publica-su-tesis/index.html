Critica AMLO al Inai por pedir hacer pública su tesis

The Mexican Transparency Institute ordered the release of the president’s university degree and thesis before his term ended. This decision highlights the ongoing tension between political leaders and institutions mandated to ensure government transparency. The incident underscores the critical role of open data initiatives in holding public officials accountable. It demonstrates how mandatory disclosure policies can bypass political resistance to enforce information access. Ultimately, it reinforces the principle that public service requires rigorous adherence to openness standards.
