Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The RansomHouse attack on Hospital Clínic demonstrates the severe risks of data exfiltration in critical infrastructure. By threatening to leak sensitive patient and staff information, the perpetrators highlighted the vulnerability of health data, even when partially secured. This incident underscores the challenge of mitigating deep web leaks, which are difficult to erase once published. The demand for a substantial ransom illustrates the financial pressure placed on organizations to prioritize data protection over operational continuity during such crises. Relevance to open data lies in the delicate balance between transparency and security. While open data initiatives aim for accessibility, this attack reminds us that unauthorized exposure of private health information constitutes a breach of trust, reinforcing the need for robust ethical frameworks and security measures in all data handling practices.
