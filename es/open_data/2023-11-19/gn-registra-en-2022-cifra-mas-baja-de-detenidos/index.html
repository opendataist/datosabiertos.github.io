GN registra en 2022 cifra más baja de detenidos

The National Guard recorded its lowest detention figures since its inception, marking a significant operational shift compared to previous years. This decline suggests a strategic pivot away from high-volume arrests toward different security metrics, reflecting broader changes in federal policing tactics under the current administration. Simultaneously, there was a substantial increase in the seizure of objects, indicating a continued focus on property-related interventions despite fewer personnel detentions. This divergence highlights how law enforcement priorities may have adapted, emphasizing asset recovery over traditional incarceration rates in the evolving security landscape. These trends are crucial for open data enthusiasts, as they demonstrate how statistical transparency reveals nuanced policy impacts beyond headline numbers. Accessible census data allows analysts to decode these shifts, fostering informed public debate on the effectiveness and direction of national security strategies and accountability.
