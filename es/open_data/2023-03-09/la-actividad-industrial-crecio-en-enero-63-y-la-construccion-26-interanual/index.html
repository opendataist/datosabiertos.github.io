La actividad industrial creció en enero 6,3% y la construcción 2,6% interanual

The industrial and construction sectors demonstrated significant recovery in early 2023, reversing recent downward trends with notable monthly growth. This resurgence highlights broad-based expansion across most manufacturing divisions, signaling improved economic vitality and resilience in key productive areas after a period of contraction. Simultaneously, the construction industry showed strong momentum in material consumption, particularly for cement and structural inputs, despite declines in ceramic finishes. While employment in the private construction sector increased substantially during the previous year, authorized building surfaces have decreased, creating a complex picture of sectoral dynamics where labor demand does not fully align with new construction volume approvals. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the critical role of official statistical agencies in making high-frequency economic indicators accessible. By providing detailed breakdowns of activity indices, material consumption, and employment metrics, governments enable transparent monitoring of economic health, allowing researchers and the public to verify trends and make informed decisions based on standardized, verifiable datasets.
