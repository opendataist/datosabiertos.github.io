Un respiro en la inflación: en cuánto cerraron los alimentos en febrero

The article reveals a notable deceleration in food inflation, marking a significant drop compared to official January data. This downward trend serves as a positive signal for the government’s economic team, supporting projections that broader inflation will decline substantially toward double digits in February. Specific categories like fruits and vegetables showed price decreases, while meats drove most of the weekly increase. The methodology behind these figures relies on continuous web scraping of supermarket prices across Buenos Aires, offering a high-frequency, granular view of the market that contrasts with traditional monthly surveys. This report highlights the value of open and alternative data sources in monitoring economic indicators. By providing transparent, real-time price tracking, such consultancies demonstrate how accessible data can empower users to verify official statistics and understand immediate economic impacts on consumer baskets.
