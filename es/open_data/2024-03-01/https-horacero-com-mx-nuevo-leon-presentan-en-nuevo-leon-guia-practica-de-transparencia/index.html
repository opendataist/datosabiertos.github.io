Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Nuevo León ha presented a practical guide for proactive transparency as part of its Local Action Plan for Open Government 2022-2024. This collaborative effort involves state and municipal governments alongside civil society organizations. The primary conclusion is that transparency must evolve beyond merely responding to formal requests. Instead, public entities are committed to actively generating, publishing, and disseminating information to foster a governance model that reconnects society with its government. The guide serves as a didactic tool for government dependences, offering clear classifications and practical exercises to improve their performance. Stakeholders emphasize that this initiative stems from a commitment to transcend traditional bureaucratic methods. By adopting an open design approach, the resource aims to be accessible and useful for all obligated subjects, encouraging them to go beyond legal minimums and integrate proactive practices into their daily operations. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the shift from passive compliance to active data release. It underscores the importance of making government information readily available without requiring specific citizen requests. By providing structured tools for proactive transparency, the initiative supports the broader open data goal of enhancing public accountability and citizen participation through accessible, high-quality public information.
