Consejo Económico y Social: Queremos romper con el secretismo de otros gobiernos - Primera Fuente

The Economic and Social Council serves as a vital platform for generating public policy ideas, integrating social participation into governance. This collaborative approach ensures that future decisions reflect diverse societal needs rather than top-down mandates. By addressing key areas like urban mobility and international cooperation, the council fosters cross-sectoral alliances. These discussions aim to create more inclusive transportation solutions and innovative technological frameworks that benefit the entire community. This initiative is crucial for open_data because it demonstrates how transparent, participatory mechanisms can drive effective governance. By formalizing recommendations through structured commissions, the city creates a model where citizen input directly shapes policy, highlighting the intersection of civic engagement and data-driven decision-making.
