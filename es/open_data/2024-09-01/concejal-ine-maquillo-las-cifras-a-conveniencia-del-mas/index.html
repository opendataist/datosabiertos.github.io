Concejal: INE “maquilló las cifras” a conveniencia del MAS

The article highlights allegations that national statistical authorities manipulated census figures to align with political interests, resulting in a significant undercount of the population in specific regions. This discrepancy raises serious concerns about the transparency and methodological integrity of official data collection processes, as the reported population growth contradicts historical projections and logical demographic trends. Such inaccuracies have direct socioeconomic implications, as municipal funding and resource allocation for essential services like health and education depend heavily on accurate population metrics. When official statistics fail to reflect reality, communities risk receiving insufficient support, thereby undermining equitable development and public trust in state institutions responsible for data management. This situation is crucial for the open data community because it illustrates how political interference can compromise data quality and public accountability. It underscores the vital need for verifiable, transparent, and independently auditable statistical frameworks to ensure that public information serves the citizenry rather than partisan agendas.
