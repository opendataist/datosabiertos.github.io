La actividad industrial creció un 1,1% interanual en mayo

Mayo revealed divergent economic trends, with industrial activity rising while construction contracted. This contrast highlights sector-specific resilience versus vulnerability within the broader economy, suggesting uneven recovery patterns. Ten manufacturing sub-sectors achieved interannual growth, driven by metals, chemicals, and vehicles. Conversely, sectors like paper, minerals, and machinery experienced declines. This mixed performance indicates structural shifts rather than uniform stagnation or boom. This data is vital for open data initiatives, which rely on accurate, granular statistical records. High-quality public indices enable transparent analysis of economic health, allowing researchers and policymakers to identify emerging trends and make evidence-based decisions regarding industrial policy and investment.
