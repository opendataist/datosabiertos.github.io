En 2022, sequía causó pérdidas a más de 425 mil productores en Veracruz

The INEGI report highlights how severe droughts and flooding significantly disrupted Mexico’s agricultural sector in 2022, causing widespread losses among producers. This data underscores the vulnerability of rural economies to climate variability, emphasizing that water scarcity, rather than just abundance, poses a critical threat to food production stability. Understanding these impacts is vital for open data initiatives, as transparent statistics enable researchers and policymakers to identify at-risk regions and allocate resources more effectively. Public access to such granular information fosters accountability and supports evidence-based strategies for disaster mitigation and sustainable land management practices. Ultimately, this study illustrates the necessity of integrating environmental and economic datasets to address climate challenges. By making these insights widely available, society can better comprehend the socio-economic consequences of extreme weather, driving informed decisions that protect livelihoods and enhance national food security through resilient data-driven solutions.
