Ecuador con la cifra más alta de desempleo en casi dos años - Noticias Prensa Latina

Ecuador faces significant labor market challenges, with high unemployment and a workforce predominantly engaged in informal employment. Official data contradicts government claims of substantial job creation, revealing recent net job losses instead. This discrepancy is highly relevant to open data, highlighting the critical need for transparent, independent statistics to hold leaders accountable and ensure public trust in national metrics.
