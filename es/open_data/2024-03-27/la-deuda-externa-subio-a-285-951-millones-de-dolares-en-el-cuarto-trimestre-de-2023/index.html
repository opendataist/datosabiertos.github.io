La deuda externa subió a 285.951 millones de dólares en el cuarto trimestre de 2023

Argentina’s external debt increased significantly in late 2023, driven by rising obligations among non-financial corporations and households, despite reductions in government and central bank liabilities. This divergence highlights shifting financial pressures across different economic sectors. The disparity between nominal and market values underscores the low valuation of Argentine sovereign bonds. Furthermore, while debt with the IMF decreased slightly, the overall trajectory indicates growing international indebtedness that impacts the country’s fiscal stability and credit risk profile. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the importance of transparent, accessible statistical records. Open government data allows citizens and analysts to independently verify national accounts, fostering accountability regarding public finances and enabling informed decisions about economic health and policy effectiveness.
