Chihuahua experienced modest growth in secondary and tertiary economic activities during the first quarter, positioning the state as a contributor to the northern region’s dominant role in Mexico’s overall economy. This performance highlights the resilience of the state’s industrial and service sectors, suggesting steady, albeit small, progress in key areas like manufacturing and knowledge-based services. The data underscores the critical importance of high-quality, standardized economic indicators in monitoring regional development. By utilizing rigorous methodologies similar to those for GDP calculations, the INEGI ensures that regional statistics are comparable, reliable, and timely, forming a robust foundation for evidence-based policy and economic analysis. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how public statistical agencies make granular, localized data accessible to citizens, researchers, and policymakers. Transparent access to these economic indicators enables informed public debate, fosters accountability in government management, and allows for the creation of innovative data-driven applications that can further illuminate regional economic trends.
