A 5 días de abrir inscripciones, UASLP ha recibido más de 3 mil solicitudes

The UASLP has launched its 2025 admission process with significant early interest, introducing a unified academic exam weighted heavily in final scores. This shift aims to ensure equitable evaluation of core competencies across all disciplines, including humanities and sciences, thereby standardizing merit-based selection. The update is designed to maintain educational quality while simplifying the administrative journey for applicants through a streamlined, personal registration procedure accessible via official digital channels. Simultaneously, the psychological assessment has evolved to prioritize critical thinking and socio-emotional skills alongside traditional reasoning metrics. By integrating these new areas, the university seeks a more holistic view of applicant potential. The strict requirement to complete both exams underscores a commitment to comprehensive student profiling, ensuring that admitted individuals are not only academically prepared but also possess the necessary cognitive and emotional frameworks for success. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it leverages publicly accessible digital platforms for registration and study guide distribution. By providing free, standardized resources and transparent admission criteria, the university enhances institutional transparency and equal opportunity. The digital nature of these processes creates potential for analyzing application trends and improving future educational accessibility through data-driven insights.
