La Cámara de Senadores tratará el miércoles 20 el pedido de interpelación para el titular del Censo - Política - ABC Color

The Senate’s impending interrogation of the INE director highlights critical governance challenges regarding the transparency and reliability of national census data. This political friction underscores how statistical integrity directly impacts public trust and the legitimacy of government institutions, a core concern for open data advocates. Interrogators are demanding detailed explanations for significant discrepancies between census results and prior projections, as well as the technical reasons behind the sudden shift in poverty indicators. These questions emphasize the necessity for robust, auditable methodologies in public data production to ensure accuracy and prevent misleading socioeconomic assessments. Ultimately, this case illustrates the vital link between accurate data and effective policy implementation. When statistical results drastically alter indicators like per capita income or debt, it forces a reevaluation of social programs, demonstrating that open, verifiable data is essential for accountable and equitable public resource management.
