Analiza Senado reforma para blindar Inai con suplencia | HidrocalidoDigital.com

Senator Rojas proposes a legal reform to ensure the INAI always functions, even during commissioner vacancies. By allowing the Secretary of Agreements to temporarily fill these roles, the institution maintains its critical operations without interruption. This measure safeguards the body's autonomy and impartiality, ensuring it remains operational at all times to hold public officials accountable. The initiative highlights the vital link between institutional continuity and effective oversight. Keeping the INAI active is essential for protecting citizens' right to access information and demanding transparency from government entities. Without this mechanism, gaps in leadership could undermine the agency's ability to enforce accountability standards across public administration. This development is highly relevant to open data because functional transparency institutions are the primary guarantors of citizen rights to access government information. Strengthening the INAI ensures that data remains accessible and that the systems supporting open information remain robust. Ultimately, this protects the infrastructure necessary for a transparent society, where public funds and actions are consistently subject to scrutiny and public review.
