La pobreza afecta al 29% de la población de Perú | HISPANTV

A significant portion of the population lives in poverty, unable to afford basic necessities like food and health. This widespread deprivation highlights severe socioeconomic challenges that disproportionately impact children and rural communities, reflecting a crisis comparable to pandemic-era conditions. The government’s censorship of the report revealing these statistics demonstrates a lack of political will to address the root causes of this inequality. By withholding transparent data and limiting their response to vague commitments, authorities are failing to implement effective solutions for the vulnerable sectors most affected by extreme poverty. This case is critical for open data advocates because it illustrates how suppressing statistical transparency obstructs accountability and public debate. Without access to unfiltered, accurate information, citizens cannot effectively demand policy changes or monitor progress. Ensuring data freedom is essential for empowering societies to confront and resolve deep-seated structural issues like economic inequality.
