La compraventa de viviendas crecen en noviembre un 14,2%: ¿dónde sube con más fuerza?

The Spanish real estate market shows sustained recovery in late 2024, driven by lower interest rates. While growth moderated in November, transaction volumes and mortgage signings remain significantly higher than the previous year, indicating stabilized financing conditions. Regional performance varies, with inland areas experiencing strong growth and coastal regions showing mixed results. This geographical divergence highlights differing local demand dynamics and economic resilience across Spain’s autonomous communities. This data is relevant to open data as it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible statistics for tracking economic trends. Reliable public records enable better policy decisions and help stakeholders assess market health beyond isolated metrics.
