La carga a nivel mundial y local

The recent surge in global air cargo capacity and demand signals a strong post-pandemic recovery, driven by lower fuel costs and optimized operational efficiency. This growth indicates that the sector is poised for high profitability in the coming year, although vigilance is required regarding potential economic and geopolitical risks that could hinder this positive trajectory. Regional performance varies significantly, with Asia-Pacific leading in growth while other areas show modest increases or declines. In Mexico, the air cargo sector is experiencing robust expansion, bolstered by major international carriers and low-cost airlines leveraging belly-hold capacity. This national strength positions the country favorably for 2025, reinforcing its role as a strategic logistics hub amid increasing indirect consumer reliance on air transport for essential goods. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how public statistics from entities like IATA and national aviation agencies enable transparency in global supply chains. By making performance metrics accessible, stakeholders can analyze market trends, assess economic resilience, and understand the critical infrastructure behind everyday products, fostering informed decision-making and accountability in the transportation sector.
