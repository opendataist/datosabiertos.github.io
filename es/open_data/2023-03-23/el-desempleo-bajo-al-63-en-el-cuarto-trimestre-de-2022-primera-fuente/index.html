El desempleo bajó al 6,3% en el cuarto trimestre de 2022 - Primera Fuente

The article reveals that despite significant inflation and economic growth, unemployment and underemployment rates decreased in the final quarter of 2022. Educational disparities persist, with the majority of both the unemployed and employed having only secondary education, while occupational structures remain heavily skewed toward operational roles rather than professional ones. This data is crucial for open_data because it highlights the importance of accessing granular, official statistical indicators. By analyzing such detailed labor metrics, researchers and policymakers can identify structural vulnerabilities and trends. Transparent data access allows for a deeper understanding of how macroeconomic factors like inflation intersect with micro-level employment realities. Ultimately, the report underscores the need for high-quality, publicly available datasets to accurately monitor social welfare. Without open access to this specific information, it would be difficult to assess the true impact of economic policies on different demographic groups, thereby hindering effective decision-making and advocacy for labor rights.
