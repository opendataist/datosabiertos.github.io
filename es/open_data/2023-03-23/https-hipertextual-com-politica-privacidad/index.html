Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This privacy policy outlines the data practices of Hipertextual, detailing how personal information, including cookies and usage data, is collected and processed. The site distinguishes between mandatory and optional data, ensuring that non-compliance with optional fields does not hinder service availability. By clarifying these obligations, the text establishes transparency regarding user responsibilities and the extent of data contribution required for full service participation. The operator emphasizes strict security measures and compliance with legal bases for data treatment, such as contract fulfillment or legitimate interest. It lists specific third-party analytics and advertising services, noting their role in monitoring user behavior and displaying personalized content. The policy also explains data retention periods and cross-border transfer mechanisms, ensuring that users under stricter protection standards are informed about the safeguards in place for their information. This document is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the baseline transparency required for ethical data ecosystems. By explicitly defining data subjects' rights—such as access, rectification, and portability—it reinforces the principle that user control is fundamental to trust. Understanding such policies helps citizens navigate the boundary between open service provision and personal privacy, highlighting the need for clear, accessible information in digital interactions.
