Aumentó la actividad manufacturera en agosto, calcula Inegi

The IMoam data signals a recovery in the Mexican manufacturing sector, marking a positive shift after earlier declines. This timely indicator provides early evidence of industrial growth trends for policymakers. Such preliminary metrics are crucial for open data ecosystems, as they offer immediate, high-frequency insights into economic health. Transparent, accessible data allows researchers to track real-time production dynamics rather than relying solely on lagging official statistics. Ultimately, this highlights the value of timely, publicly available economic indicators. It supports better decision-making and fosters trust in official statistics through open, verifiable, and early-access data sharing.
