Inflación en Uruguay fue 0,37% en septiembre 2024

Uruguay’s September inflation shows modest monthly growth with significant food price spikes, particularly in fruits and vegetables. This highlights how consumer basket fluctuations impact household budgets and purchasing power, creating a need for transparent, timely data to understand these economic pressures. Financial and insurance services also contributed to rising costs, reflecting broader inflationary trends across essential sectors. These shifts demonstrate the interconnectedness of price movements, requiring comprehensive monitoring to assess economic stability and inform policy decisions effectively. This report is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of accessible, granular statistical information. By making such detailed indices available, authorities enable better public scrutiny and research into cost-of-living changes, fostering informed civic engagement and economic transparency.
