Exdirectores afirman que el INE tiene oportunidad de recuperar credibilidad

Former directors of the National Institute of Statistics emphasize that the institution must swiftly and transparently release census results to restore public trust. They highlight that this data is crucial for redistributing political seats and fiscal resources, noting that timely access is essential for equitable regional planning and policy implementation. The experts stress the need to leverage modern technology and improved methodologies for future censuses. By prioritizing efficient data processing, the statistical office can avoid delays that might undermine the utility of the collected information. This approach ensures that the census serves as a reliable technical tool rather than a political controversy. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of timely, accessible, and transparent information dissemination. When statistical agencies operate openly, they enable accurate resource allocation and foster democratic accountability. Ensuring that citizens can access raw data and final results builds credibility and supports evidence-based decision-making in governance.
