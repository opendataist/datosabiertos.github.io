Registro de Deudores Alimentarios bajará violencia económica que sufren mujeres en Coahuila

The establishment of the National Registry of Alimony Obligations marks a significant step toward ending impunity and reducing economic violence against Mexican women. By mandating that courts share debtor information with family development systems, this reform aims to address the widespread failure of parents to meet financial support obligations. Restrictions will bar non-compliant individuals from obtaining essential documents, conducting major transactions, or holding public office and judicial positions. These measures are designed to deter intentional defaulting, which currently affects a substantial portion of divorced families, by linking alimony compliance to critical civil and political rights. This development is highly relevant to open data as it requires institutional transparency and standardized data sharing across government entities. Creating a unified registry facilitates accountability, allowing stakeholders to monitor compliance and enforce legal judgments effectively through accessible, interoperable information systems.
