Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

National poverty rates reveal significant socioeconomic challenges, highlighting the critical need for open data to track inequality and guide public policy. Regional disparities, such as those in Gran Santa Fe, demonstrate how localized datasets can pinpoint specific areas requiring targeted social intervention and resource allocation. Transparent, accessible statistical information empowers citizens and researchers to analyze these trends, fostering accountability and more effective strategies to combat poverty.
