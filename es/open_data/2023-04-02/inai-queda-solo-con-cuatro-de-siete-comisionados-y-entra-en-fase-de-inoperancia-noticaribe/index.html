INAI queda solo con cuatro de siete comisionados y entra en fase de inoperancia - Noticaribe

Mexico’s transparency institute has become inoperative due to a lack of legal quorum, severely compromising the protection of citizens' personal data and the right to information. This institutional crisis highlights the fragility of oversight mechanisms, demonstrating that without active and complete regulatory bodies, fundamental democratic safeguards are vulnerable to political interference and neglect. The situation underscores the critical importance of maintaining fully functional open data frameworks to ensure public accountability. When key monitoring entities lose their capacity to operate, the flow and verification of public information stagnate, weakening the societal trust necessary for effective data governance and institutional balance. This event is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates how political struggles can disrupt the infrastructure required to manage and protect citizen information. It serves as a warning that open data initiatives rely not just on technology, but on robust, independent institutions capable of enforcing transparency standards and preventing the misuse of public records.
