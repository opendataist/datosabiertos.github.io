Terminan mesas de trabajo del Plan de Apertura de Datos Abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua concludes the development of its Municipal Government Open Data Policy through collaborative workshops with civil society and experts. This initiative aims to make public information accessible, fostering civic collaboration and diverse perspectives on data utility. The resulting framework ensures that open data serves as a public resource for civil organizations and researchers. By leveraging this accessible information, stakeholders can drive research and propose policies that actively improve urban life and governance. This progress is vital for open data because it institutionalizes transparency and public participation. It transforms raw government information into actionable knowledge, demonstrating how open data empowers citizens and enhances democratic accountability through direct community engagement.
