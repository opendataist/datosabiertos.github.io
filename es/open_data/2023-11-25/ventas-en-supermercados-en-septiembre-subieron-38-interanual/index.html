Ventas en supermercados: en septiembre subieron 3,8% interanual

September’s economic data reveals sharp nominal growth in retail sales and significant retail price inflation, highlighting the intense volatility of the market. This reflects broader inflationary pressures impacting consumer spending patterns across supermarkets, wholesale clubs, and shopping centers, with essential goods seeing substantial price hikes. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of accessible, standardized statistical sources like INDEC. Transparent public data allows analysts to track economic trends, verify official figures, and understand real-world implications of inflation and sales shifts. Such openness empowers citizens and researchers to create independent visualizations and analyses. By making these datasets available, governments support accountability and enable a deeper public understanding of complex economic indicators, fostering informed discussion about financial health and policy effectiveness.
