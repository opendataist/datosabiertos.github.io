The report highlights a statistically significant reduction in national unemployment, driven largely by a sharp decline in female joblessness. This improvement coincides with a notable increase in the overall labor force participation rate, indicating a strengthening engagement of women in the formal economy. Despite these gains, substantial gender disparities persist in occupational rates and informal employment levels. These structural imbalances suggest that while overall employment metrics are improving, the quality and equality of labor market integration remain critical areas requiring attention. This data is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the necessity of disaggregated, high-quality statistical access. Transparent, detailed public indicators enable stakeholders to identify inequalities, monitor policy effectiveness, and advocate for equitable economic reforms based on evidence rather than aggregated averages.
