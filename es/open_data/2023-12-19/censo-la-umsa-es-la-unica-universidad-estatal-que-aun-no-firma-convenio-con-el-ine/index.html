Censo: la UMSA es la única universidad estatal que aún no firma convenio con el INE

The UMSA’s refusal to collaborate with the INE for the 2024 census highlights significant institutional barriers to data collection. This non-participation undermines census completeness, directly impacting the quality and representativeness of national statistics. Consequently, it restricts open data accessibility, demonstrating how administrative gaps hinder transparent, citizen-driven information systems essential for informed public policy and accountability.
