Que AICM explique qué hace con biométricos de pasajero: INAI

The INAI demands transparency regarding biometric data processing at Mexico City Airport, ensuring citizens understand how their personal information is handled during identity verification. This ruling highlights the critical importance of public access to information regarding automated surveillance systems, reinforcing accountability for government entities deploying biometric technologies. Open data advocates must ensure that such sensitive personal information remains transparent to protect individual privacy rights against opaque state surveillance practices.
