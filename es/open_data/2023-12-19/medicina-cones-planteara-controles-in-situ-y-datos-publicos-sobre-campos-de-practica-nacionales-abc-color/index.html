Medicina: Cones planteará controles in situ y datos públicos sobre campos de práctica - Nacionales - ABC Color

The Ministry of Education proposes stricter oversight for medical residency fields to address current systemic weaknesses. This plan involves random inspections and stricter convention supervision, ensuring that health institutions directly manage these educational partnerships rather than leaving them unregulated. Central to this reform is the derogation of outdated resolutions and the establishment of clear accountability for both public and private facilities. By requiring prior communication regarding student numbers and mandating additional agreements when capacity limits are exceeded, the framework aims to prevent the exploitation of residents and ensure adequate training conditions. This development is highly relevant to open_data because it highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible data on educational-contractual agreements. Public access to convention details, inspection results, and resident placement statistics would allow society to verify compliance, fostering accountability and improving the quality of higher education and healthcare training through evidence-based governance.
