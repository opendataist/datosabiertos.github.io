INEGI revela cifras de homicidios en México en 2022 | Revista Punto de Vista

The INEGI report on Mexico’s 2022 homicide statistics reveals a critical trend in public health and safety data. The most significant finding is that violence has become a leading cause of death in numerous states, highlighting systemic security challenges that transcend traditional medical causes. This shift underscores the necessity for granular, accessible data to understand local versus national violence patterns effectively. Furthermore, the data exposes a stark gender disparity and age-specific vulnerability, with young men facing disproportionately high risks. These insights demonstrate how open government data can illuminate demographic disparities that might otherwise remain obscured in aggregate reports. By breaking down statistics by gender, age, and geography, researchers can better analyze the structural roots of violence and its social impact. This information is highly relevant to open data advocates because it illustrates how transparent, standardized datasets empower civil society to hold institutions accountable. Access to such detailed statistics allows for evidence-based policy debates and fosters public awareness. Ultimately, it reinforces the argument that releasing raw, unprocessed government data is essential for democratic oversight and effective social intervention.
