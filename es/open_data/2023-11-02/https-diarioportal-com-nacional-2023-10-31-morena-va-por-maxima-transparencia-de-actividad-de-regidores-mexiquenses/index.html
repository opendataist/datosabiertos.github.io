Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

This legislative proposal aims to significantly enhance transparency and accountability in Mexican municipalities by mandating the public disclosure of council members' activity reports and meeting agendas. By requiring these documents on official digital platforms, it ensures citizens can easily access information about local governance without incurring additional costs for municipal budgets. The initiative also establishes the right for individual votes to be formally recorded in session minutes. This measure promotes pluralism and clarity, allowing the public to understand the specific reasoning behind voting decisions rather than facing opaque outcomes, thereby strengthening democratic debate at the local level. This article is highly relevant to open data as it advocates for standardized, accessible digital publication of government operations. It underscores the necessity of proactive transparency, aligning with global open data principles that prioritize user-friendly access to public information for fostering trust and civic engagement in local government activities.
