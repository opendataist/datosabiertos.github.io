La inflación nacional de noviembre fue de 2,4%: los precios que más subieron

Argentina’s November inflation slowed significantly, revealing critical price dynamics across essential sectors. Education and housing costs surged, while food prices remained relatively stable, highlighting uneven economic pressures on households. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of transparent, timely, and accessible government statistics. Reliable public metrics enable citizens and analysts to track economic trends, fostering accountability and informed decision-making within the digital society.
