Solo 68.5% de los mexicanos alfabetas de 18 años y más leyeron libros o revistas en 2022 - Noticaribe

Recent data reveals a significant decline in Mexico’s literacy engagement, with fewer adults consuming print and digital media compared to previous years. This trend highlights a growing disconnect between the population and cultural consumption, suggesting that reading is no longer a universal habit despite the availability of information. The shift toward digital formats indicates evolving consumption patterns, yet underlying causes reveal systemic gaps. Many non-readers lack early exposure to books and libraries, pointing to a need for stronger foundational support in educational and domestic environments to foster lifelong reading habits. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible statistics in monitoring social development. Reliable data enables policymakers and civil society to identify disparities and design targeted interventions, ensuring that open government efforts directly address the root causes of declining cultural engagement.
