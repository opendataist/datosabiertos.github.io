La confianza empresarial sube un 2,7% en el segundo trimestre por mejores expectativas

The Harmonized Business Confidence Index (ICEA) has increased by 2.7 percent in the second quarter of 2023, reflecting a notable improvement in optimism among Spanish company managers. With 20.6 percent of executives anticipating favorable business evolution, the positive sentiment balance has reached 3.4 points, marking a significant turnaround from the previous quarter. This shift highlights a growing belief that businesses are moving past recent uncertainties, with sectors like Transport, Hospitality, and Commerce showing the strongest upward trends in confidence. Small enterprises with fewer than ten employees exhibit the highest level of optimism, whereas larger corporations remain relatively cautious. This divergence suggests that smaller businesses may be more resilient or agile in adapting to current market conditions. Concurrently, the Industrial sector drives much of this momentum, with many firms planning to increase capacity utilization significantly. This increased productivity capacity serves as a key indicator of renewed vigor within the industrial landscape, contributing substantially to the overall positive trajectory of the economy. Prices are expected to remain largely stable for most businesses, although a substantial portion anticipates potential increases. This stability contrasts with the previous period, indicating a moderation in pricing pressures. These findings are highly relevant to open data initiatives, as they demonstrate how public statistical indicators can effectively monitor economic health. Such transparent, aggregated datasets allow researchers and policymakers to track real-time confidence shifts, enabling data-driven decisions that support sustainable business growth and informed economic planning across different regions and sectors.
