Presentan plataforma de datos abiertos sobre la industria de la artesanía dominicana

This article highlights a significant initiative in open data for cultural heritage. Fodearte has launched a public platform cataloging Dominican artisans, their workshops, and socio-economic conditions. By making this detailed information freely accessible, the government aims to bridge the gap between creators and consumers, fostering direct engagement and reducing reliance on intermediaries. The relevance to open data lies in its potential for civic innovation and policy-making. The dataset includes demographic and logistical details that enable the public and developers to create value-added services. This transparency not only empowers artisans economically but also provides the empirical basis necessary for crafting effective, evidence-based public policies that support the sector’s sustainable development. Ultimately, this project demonstrates how open government data can drive social and economic inclusion. It transforms static census records into a dynamic tool for community development. By democratizing access to critical industry information, the initiative promotes transparency and accountability, serving as a model for how open data can directly benefit specific cultural communities and inform broader governmental strategies.
