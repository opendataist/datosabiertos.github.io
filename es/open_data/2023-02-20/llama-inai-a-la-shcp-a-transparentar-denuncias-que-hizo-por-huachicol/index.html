Llama INAI a la SHCP a transparentar denuncias que hizo por huachicol

The INAI requires the SHCP to disclose detailed records of penal complaints regarding fuel smuggling across recent presidential terms. This move highlights the critical role of transparency in combating organized crime and public funds misappropriation, ensuring citizens can verify official efforts against illicit activities. Such data is vital for assessing the effectiveness of anti-theft strategies implemented by multiple government agencies. Open data principles demand that these results be accessible to society, allowing for independent evaluation of how authorities manage complex issues involving collusion between officials and criminal groups. Ultimately, this request underscores the importance of open government in maintaining accountability. By making these specific records public, the state enables civil society to measure real impacts on economic losses and institutional integrity. This transparency fosters trust and ensures that anti-corruption measures are not only declared but demonstrably effective in protecting national resources.
