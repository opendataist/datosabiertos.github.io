Cerca de 12.000 personas han engrosado las listas del censo habitacional en las zonas de riesgo grave de despoblación en Castilla-La Mancha

Castilla-La Mancha has implemented a pioneering legal framework to combat rural depopulation, a strategy the regional president credits with attracting nearly twelve thousand residents to vulnerable areas. This approach highlights the region's success in leveraging political autonomy to reverse demographic decline, contrasting with neighboring territories. The narrative emphasizes that this growth stems largely from migration driven by new opportunities rather than natural birth rates. By actively fostering an environment for newcomers and returnees, the autonomous community demonstrates how targeted policy can effectively draw people seeking economic prospects, thereby stabilizing and revitalizing struggling municipalities over time. This case is relevant to open_data as it showcases a successful model of governance data utilization. The initiative underscores how transparent tracking of census registers and specific demographic indicators can validate policy effectiveness. Such data transparency is crucial for assessing the impact of legislative measures against depopulation, offering a replicable blueprint for other regions facing similar rural challenges.
