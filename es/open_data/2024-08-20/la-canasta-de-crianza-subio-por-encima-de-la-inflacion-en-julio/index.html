La canasta de crianza subió por encima de la inflación en julio

The INDEC data reveals that raising a child in Argentina has become significantly more expensive, with costs surging well above inflation across all age groups. This tripled annual expenditure highlights a severe economic burden on families, affecting everything from basic needs to childcare. These figures are crucial for calculating alimony in legal separation cases, directly impacting judicial decisions on child support. The indicator reflects the total value of goods, services, and time required for proper child development, providing a standardized metric for legal obligations. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives as it offers transparent, granular insights into household economics. By making such detailed cost structures public, citizens can better understand the financial reality of parenting and hold institutions accountable for supporting family welfare policies.
