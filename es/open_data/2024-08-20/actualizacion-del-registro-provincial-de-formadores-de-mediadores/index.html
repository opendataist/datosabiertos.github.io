Actualización del registro provincial de formadores de mediadores

The Justice Secretariat is modernizing the mediator trainer registry through digital transformation to ensure service quality and validate academic content. This centralization allows authorities to supervise projects and homologate training programs, guaranteeing that mediators receive adequate professional education. This update is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the shift toward transparent, accessible public service registries. By maintaining a valid, digitally managed list of authorized entities, the government enhances accountability and facilitates public verification of provider credentials, fostering trust in legal mediation services through improved data governance.
