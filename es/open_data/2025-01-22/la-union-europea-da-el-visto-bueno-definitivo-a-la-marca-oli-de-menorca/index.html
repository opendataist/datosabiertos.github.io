La Unión Europea da el visto bueno definitivo a la marca 'Oli de Menorca'

The certification highlights a commitment to ecological production and strict mechanical extraction within a specific geographic area. This protects traditional methods and ensures quality, reinforcing the link between product characteristics and their territorial origin through European safeguards. Such protection adds value to unique regional goods, distinguishing them in the market. It serves as a vital tool for preserving agricultural heritage, demonstrating how localized standards can maintain distinct sensory profiles like balanced bitterness and pungency. This is relevant to open data as traceability systems require transparent, standardized datasets.
