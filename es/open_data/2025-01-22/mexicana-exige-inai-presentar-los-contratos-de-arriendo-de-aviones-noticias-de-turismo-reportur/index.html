Mexicana: exige INAI presentar los contratos de arriendo de aviones | Noticias de turismo REPORTUR

The INAI ruled that Mexican Airlines must disclose aircraft leasing contracts, rejecting the claim that they are protected due to ongoing litigation. This decision affirms that such financial agreements constitute public information, regardless of legal disputes arising later. Consequently, transparency authorities upheld the citizen’s right to access these details, preventing corporations from hiding commercial arrangements behind judicial processes. This ruling is crucial for open data because it establishes a clear precedent for treating corporate financial contracts as public goods rather than confidential legal assets. By prioritizing accountability over secrecy, it ensures citizens can monitor how public funds and state-owned enterprises operate. It reinforces the principle that information created for public purposes remains accessible, supporting democratic oversight. Ultimately, the decision highlights the necessity of transparent data regarding state assets. When public investment is involved, the public has a legitimate interest in understanding associated contractual obligations. This case strengthens the framework for open data by ensuring that economic transparency prevails, allowing society to verify the efficient and lawful use of national resources and maintaining trust in public institutions.
