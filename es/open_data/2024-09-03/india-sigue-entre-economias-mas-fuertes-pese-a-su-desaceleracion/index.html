India sigue entre economías más fuertes, pese a su desaceleración

India remains the world’s fastest-growing economy, surpassing China despite a recent quarterly slowdown attributed to reduced government spending. Experts view this deceleration as temporary, driven by falling inflation and anticipating a rebound in public expenditure, with medium-term projections exceeding 7% if structural reforms continue. This context is vital for open data as it underscores the importance of transparent, timely economic indicators in fostering investor confidence and monitoring policy impacts. Accessible data allows stakeholders to verify claims about growth trends and evaluate how political shifts, such as coalition governance, influence economic stability and structural reforms in emerging markets.
