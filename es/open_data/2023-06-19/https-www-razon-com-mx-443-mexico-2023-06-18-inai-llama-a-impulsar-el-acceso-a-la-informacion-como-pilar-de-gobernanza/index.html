Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection emphasizes that global access to information is a fundamental pillar for democratic governance, economic stability, and social inclusion. This perspective highlights how open data practices empower societies by fostering transparency and accountability across borders. International collaboration remains crucial for addressing modern challenges, including the use of artificial intelligence and open contracting for public sector efficiency. By strengthening networks among information commissioners, institutions can better navigate the complexities of the digital age while ensuring equitable rights for all citizens. This article is relevant to open data because it advocates for the socialization of information access as a tool for development. It underscores the need for robust institutional frameworks that guarantee public rights, ensuring that data transparency contributes directly to preserving democracy and promoting inclusive growth in a globalized world.
