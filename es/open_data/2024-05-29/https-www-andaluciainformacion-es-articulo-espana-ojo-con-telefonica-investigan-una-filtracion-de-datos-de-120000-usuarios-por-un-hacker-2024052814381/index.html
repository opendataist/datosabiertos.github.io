Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Telefónica is currently verifying the legitimacy of a claimed data breach involving 120,000 users and employees, investigating whether the vulnerability originated from an external provider. Crucially, the reported data does not contain highly sensitive information like bank accounts or passwords, limiting the immediate personal risk to those affected. This incident highlights the critical importance of supply chain security in telecommunications. As major providers increasingly rely on third-party vendors, the transparency of security audits and the verification of external data handling practices become paramount for maintaining user trust and regulatory compliance. The situation is particularly relevant to open data advocacy because it underscores the tension between proprietary security risks and public accountability. It demonstrates how unverified claims in hacker forums can impact corporate reputation, emphasizing the need for standardized, transparent incident reporting mechanisms that allow the public and independent auditors to assess the true scope of data exposures without relying solely on corporate statements.
