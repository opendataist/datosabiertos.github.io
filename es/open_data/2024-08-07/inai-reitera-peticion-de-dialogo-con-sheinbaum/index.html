INAI reitera petición de diálogo con Sheinbaum

The INAI warns that abolishing autonomous bodies threatens international trade agreements and data protection standards. This structural weakening undermines Mexico’s diplomatic and commercial credibility, as secure data management remains essential for global relations. Consequently, the proposed reform poses significant risks to the nation’s standing in international markets. Furthermore, the claim that dissolution yields substantial fiscal savings is misleading. Actual budget reductions would be minimal compared to the projected figures, disproving the economic justification for dismantling these institutions. The financial impact does not support the narrative of significant public resource optimization through this constitutional change. This matters to open data because autonomous organizations guarantee transparency and protect citizens' rights. Their removal risks eroding the institutional framework that supports information access. Maintaining independent oversight is crucial for preserving trust in government data practices and ensuring robust protection for personal information in a connected world.
