El precio de la luz marcará el Día de Reyes el nivel más bajo en casi un mes

The article highlights a recent drop in wholesale electricity prices, noting that while current lows are attractive, experts foresee an upward trend in 2025 due to natural gas volatility. This creates significant uncertainty for consumers, as short-term market fluctuations are becoming harder to predict despite recent annual decreases. Relevance to open_data lies in the evolving regulatory framework for the regulated electricity price. The system is gradually shifting its calculation method to incorporate medium-to-long-term futures markets, reducing reliance on immediate wholesale data. This transition aims to stabilize costs while maintaining incentives for efficient consumption. This structural change implies that open data initiatives must track not just spot prices but also futures market indicators. Stakeholders need access to comprehensive, transparent datasets to understand how the blend of short and long-term pricing affects final consumer costs over time.
