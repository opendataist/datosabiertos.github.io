▷ #PulsoEmpresarial Un primer vistazo a la próxima evolución de Galaxy AI - El Impulso

Samsung announces One UI 7, a mobile operating system deeply integrated with artificial intelligence that fundamentally changes how users interact with their devices. This new platform prioritizes proactive assistance and unprecedented personalization, allowing the smartphone to anticipate needs and streamline daily tasks without requiring manual input. The goal is to reduce stress and enhance productivity by making the device an intuitive extension of the user’s lifestyle. A critical component of this evolution is the introduction of Personal Data Core, which addresses user concerns regarding privacy and security. By storing personalized data in a secure, encrypted environment protected by Knox Vault and post-quantum cryptography, Samsung ensures that sensitive information remains on the device. This architectural shift allows for advanced, tailored experiences while maintaining rigorous protection against data misuse and emerging technological threats, thereby building trust in AI-driven mobile functions. This article is relevant to the open data community because it highlights the ongoing tension between data utilization for personalization and data sovereignty. While the technology demonstrates sophisticated on-device processing, it underscores the necessity for transparent data governance and robust security standards. As AI becomes more pervasive in consumer devices, understanding how proprietary systems handle, protect, and potentially restrict access to personal data is essential for advocating for open standards, user rights, and ethical data practices in the mobile ecosystem.
