España: récord de 88,5 millones de turistas hasta noviembre, que gastaron 118.693 millones

Spain’s tourism sector achieved record-breaking figures in late 2024, driven by a significant surge in international visitors and substantial spending. The data highlights a robust recovery and expansion, with visitor numbers and total expenditure both growing at double-digit rates compared to the previous year. This trend indicates strong market confidence and sustained demand for Spanish destinations. The composition of tourism consumption reveals shifting preferences, particularly in accommodation usage and trip duration. Non-market lodging and longer stays saw notable growth, while independent travel without package deals increased significantly. These patterns suggest travelers are seeking more personalized and flexible experiences, impacting how hospitality providers adapt their offerings to meet evolving consumer demands. This article is vital for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, standardized statistical series like Frontur. Reliable public datasets enable accurate analysis of economic impacts and sector trends. Open access to such granular information supports informed decision-making for policymakers, researchers, and businesses, fostering greater accountability and facilitating evidence-based strategies in the travel and tourism industry.
