DIF municipal invita a las parejas a participar en el programa ‘Da el Sí con la Familia’

The San Luis Potosí DIF is offering free simultaneous weddings to remove financial barriers and strengthen families. This initiative highlights how public institutions can use transparent, accessible processes to promote social inclusion and equal rights for all citizens. The program requires specific documentation to ensure legal validity and support for children. By streamlining these administrative steps, the government demonstrates a commitment to reducing bureaucratic friction. This approach ensures that civil union becomes a reachable reality rather than an exclusive privilege. This is relevant to open data as it showcases the need for clear, publicly available guidelines on civic services. Transparent information about eligibility and requirements empowers communities. When governments publish accessible criteria, they foster trust and ensure equitable access to fundamental civil rights.
