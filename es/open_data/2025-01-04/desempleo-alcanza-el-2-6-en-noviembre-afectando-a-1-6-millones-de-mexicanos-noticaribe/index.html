Desempleo alcanza el 2.6% en noviembre, afectando a 1.6 millones de mexicanos - Noticaribe

Mexico’s unemployment rate experienced a slight increase in November, reversing a historic low recorded in October. Despite this marginal rise, the labor market remains largely stable, with the vast majority of the economically active population employed. This stability is significant for understanding the resilience of the national workforce amidst broader economic fluctuations. However, the data highlights a critical structural issue: over half of the employed workforce operates in the informal sector. This high level of informality suggests that while job availability exists, the quality and security of employment remain concerns. The distribution of labor across sectors further illustrates an economy heavily reliant on services and commerce, rather than industrial or agricultural production. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the importance of transparent, accessible government statistics for analyzing labor markets. Open data allows researchers and policymakers to scrutinize indicators like informal employment rates and sectoral distribution. Such transparency fosters better-informed decisions regarding economic policy and social welfare, ensuring that official metrics accurately reflect the realities of the workforce.
