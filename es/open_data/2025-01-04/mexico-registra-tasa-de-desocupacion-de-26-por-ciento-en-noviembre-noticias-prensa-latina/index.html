México registra tasa de desocupación de 2,6 por ciento en noviembre - Noticias Prensa Latina

The data reveals a labor market characterized by high informality and significant underemployment, indicating that many workers lack stable, full-time contracts despite relatively low headline unemployment rates. This structural weakness suggests that economic growth is not yet translating into secure employment for a large portion of the workforce, raising concerns about sustainable development and worker protection. For the open data community, these findings highlight the critical need for transparent, granular labor statistics. Accessible, high-quality datasets enable researchers and policymakers to identify vulnerabilities within specific sectors and demographic groups, fostering evidence-based reforms. By democratizing access to such nuanced economic indicators, society can better monitor progress toward formalization and equitable labor conditions.
