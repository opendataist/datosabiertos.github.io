Inegi afirma que la inflación en México se desaceleró en febrero; se ubica en 7.62%

Mexico’s annual inflation rate has decelerated to its lowest level since early 2022, breaking a recent trend of increases and falling below market expectations. This slowdown indicates that price pressures are beginning to ease, offering a sign of stabilization after reaching historic highs in the previous year. The underlying inflation measure, which strips out volatile items, shows persistent price growth, while specific categories like eggs and gas continue to rise. Conversely, certain agricultural products experienced significant price drops. This divergence highlights that while some costs remain sticky, others are becoming more manageable for consumers amidst the broader economic context. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of accessible, high-quality statistical information for public accountability. Transparent reporting from institutions like INEGI enables analysts to track economic trends accurately, helping citizens and policymakers understand inflation dynamics beyond headline numbers to make informed decisions.

Source: vanguardia.com.mx
