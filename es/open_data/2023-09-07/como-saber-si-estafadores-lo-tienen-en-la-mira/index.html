¿Cómo saber si estafadores lo tienen en la mira?

The article underscores the growing vulnerability of individuals in the digital age, where extensive daily screen time facilitates cybercriminal activity. It argues that acquiring robust digital literacy is the primary defense against online fraud, emphasizing that prevention and prudence are essential for protecting personal data in an increasingly connected society. Key warning signs include unsolicited requests for confidential information, offers that seem too good to be true, and tactics that create a sense of urgency or pressure. The text advises verifying the identity of online contacts and ensuring website authenticity before sharing any details, as legitimate organizations always provide verifiable contact information and use secure communication channels. This content is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for public awareness regarding data security and privacy. As open data initiatives promote transparency and accessibility, they also expose users to risks if ethical standards and security protocols are not prioritized. Educating citizens on how to identify and avoid data exploitation strengthens the integrity of digital ecosystems, ensuring that the benefits of open information do not come at the cost of individual security.
