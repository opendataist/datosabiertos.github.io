Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The article highlights the inclusion of a new module on pet ownership and vaccination in the ENAHO survey. This initiative aims to generate official statistics that allow the government and private sector to evaluate public policies and direct investments to improve living conditions. By capturing detailed data on household pets, institutions can formulate more effective actions and monitor health outcomes annually, ensuring that resource allocation is based on current, accurate realities. Data confidentiality is strictly protected under national law, guaranteeing that individual information remains anonymous and is used solely for official statistical purposes. This legal framework ensures that personal details are never shared with judicial or financial authorities, fostering public trust. The emphasis on security encourages participation, as respondents are assured their privacy is safeguarded against any misuse beyond statistical analysis, which is crucial for maintaining high-quality data collection efforts. This content is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how rigorous confidentiality frameworks enable the creation of trustworthy, high-quality public datasets. By balancing transparency in statistical objectives with strict privacy protections, governments can produce reliable indicators that inform policy and encourage civic engagement. Understanding these mechanisms is essential for promoting open data ecosystems where public trust and data utility coexist, allowing society to benefit from evidence-based decision-making without compromising individual rights.
