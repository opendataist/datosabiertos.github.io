Goodyear continúa su caída con pérdidas netas de 282 millones de euros hasta junio

Goodyear reports significant financial losses and reduced sales for the first half of the year, citing weaker demand in replacement and commercial vehicle sectors. These underwhelming results have negatively impacted stock performance and led to lowered expectations for the remainder of 2023. The company aims to improve operational margins and achieve profitability in key regions by year-end. This turnaround strategy focuses on addressing declining volumes and rising costs to stabilize the business amidst challenging market conditions.
