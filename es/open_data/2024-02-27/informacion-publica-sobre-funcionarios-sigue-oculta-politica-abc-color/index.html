Información pública sobre funcionarios sigue oculta - Política - ABC Color

The unavailability of official government payroll data for recent months severely hinders transparency regarding public sector hiring and termination trends. This gap contradicts previous standards where such information was promptly updated, leaving citizens unable to verify the actual number of state employees or detect irregularities in real-time. Recent revelations of nepotism among high-ranking officials, particularly within the legislative branch, were uncovered precisely because of access to this public financial data. These cases highlight how open information serves as a critical tool for the public to hold leaders accountable and expose conflicts of interest that might otherwise remain hidden from scrutiny. Furthermore, the government has introduced stricter requirements for requesting information, mandating digital identity registration. This policy shift creates significant barriers for individuals and organizations fearing retribution, effectively restricting the fundamental right to access public records. Such limitations undermine the core principles of open data, which aim to promote accountability and civic engagement by ensuring information remains accessible to everyone, regardless of their technical identity.
