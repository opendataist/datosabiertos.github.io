El partido de Hun Sen gana elecciones al Senado camboyano y afianza el regreso del exlíder - Mundo - ABC Color

Cambodian elections demonstrate how state-controlled mechanisms can entrap political power. The ruling party’s overwhelming victory, achieved through restricted participation and opposition disqualification, highlights a system where democratic processes serve to legitimize dominance rather than reflect public will. The transition from prime minister to Senate president confirms the persistence of entrenched leadership structures. Despite formal handovers, key figures maintain influence, ensuring continuity of control. This arrangement underscores how institutional roles are repurposed to sustain authority beyond official term limits, limiting genuine political alternation. This case is vital for open data because it illustrates how electoral statistics can obscure systemic disenfranchisement. Transparent data must capture not just outcomes, but the procedural exclusions and structural barriers that determine those results. Without such context, quantitative records risk validating authoritarian consolidation rather than revealing democratic deficits.
