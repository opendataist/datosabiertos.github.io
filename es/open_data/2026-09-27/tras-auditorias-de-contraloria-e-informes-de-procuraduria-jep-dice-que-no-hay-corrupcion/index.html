Tras auditorías de Contraloría e informes de Procuraduría, JEP dice que no hay corrupción

The JEP clarifies that international trips are essential mission activities, allowing magistrates to share Colombia’s pioneering restorative justice model with the world and demonstrate compliance with peace agreement obligations. This counters narratives that portray these movements as mere personal travel, emphasizing their role in transparency and global diplomatic engagement. Regarding infrastructure, the entity refutes misconceptions about building costs by distinguishing between obsolete cadastral values and current commercial estimates. The acquisition process was halted after a negative feasibility assessment, highlighting the importance of rigorous administrative evaluation and fiscal responsibility in public institutions. This explanation is vital for open data as it underscores the necessity of accessible, accurate, and verifiable public information. By directing users to a transparency portal for budgets and contracts, the JEP reinforces the principle that accountability in transitional justice relies on the public’s ability to access and understand institutional actions.
