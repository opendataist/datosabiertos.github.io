La nueva edición del Anuario Estadístico sistematiza la información de los principales sectores del país

The article emphasizes that statistical data functions as a public good, requiring coordinated efforts across government and private sectors to ensure quality and reliability. This shared responsibility is crucial for effective public policy planning, decision-making, and research, highlighting the necessity of integrating various institutional inputs to maintain robust national data systems. To meet future challenges, such as upcoming national censuses, institutions must strengthen administrative records and accelerate digitalization. Modernizing these processes and enhancing technical capacities are essential steps to guarantee data coherence and validity, ensuring that statistics remain a trusted resource for evaluating policies and supporting socioeconomic development. This narrative is relevant to open data because it underscores the imperative for transparent, accessible, and high-quality information to empower citizens and organizations. By advocating for better data sharing and modernization, the article promotes the open data principles of accessibility and utility, demonstrating how reliable statistics can drive accountability and informed societal progress.
