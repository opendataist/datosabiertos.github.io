IVAI ya podrá verificar que sujetos obligados cumplan Ley de Transparencia

The article highlights the strengthening of the Veracrz transparent institution’s legal authority to conduct inspections and enforce compliance with transparency laws. By empowering its legal department to verify that obligated entities fulfill their duties, the reform aims to guarantee citizens effective access to public information. This shift ensures that oversight is not merely reactive but actively integrated into the regulatory framework, enhancing accountability for public bodies. The ability to issue binding recommendations and monitor their resolution marks a significant step toward stricter adherence to transparency norms. This mechanism allows the institution to directly address non-compliance and ensure that corrective actions are taken promptly. It reflects a move toward a more robust system where legal consequences for failures in transparency are clearly defined and enforceable. This development is crucial for open data advocacy, as it establishes a clearer path to challenge opaque institutions. When access to information is legally backed by strong supervisory powers, data availability improves for researchers and citizens. Ultimately, it transforms transparency from a passive obligation into an actively monitored right, supporting a more informed and accountable society.
