El Indec actualizó multas a quienes no den información o la falseen

Argentina has updated financial penalties for entities failing to provide accurate statistical data to the National Institute of Statistics and Censuses. This adjustment reinforces the legal obligation of public and private organizations to cooperate with national censuses and surveys, ensuring the integrity of official statistics through stricter enforcement mechanisms. The regulations guarantee that while data submission is mandatory, all information remains strictly confidential. Data is published only in aggregate compilations, protecting commercial and individual privacy. This balance between mandatory disclosure and data anonymity is crucial for maintaining public trust and encouraging full participation in statistical collection processes. This development is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical link between regulatory compliance and data availability. By penalizing non-compliance and ensuring confidentiality, the framework safeguards the quality and accessibility of essential public statistics, which form the foundation for transparent and informed decision-making in the open data ecosystem.
