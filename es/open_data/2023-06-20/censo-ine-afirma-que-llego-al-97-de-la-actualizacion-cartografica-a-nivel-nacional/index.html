Censo: INE afirma que llegó al 97% de la actualización cartográfica a nivel nacional

The Statistical Cartographic Update is nearing completion, serving as a vital pre-census phase for Bolivia. This operation allows the national statistics institute to precisely determine questionnaire volumes and deploy census takers efficiently. By updating housing data and georeferencing locations, the initiative ensures accurate demographic planning for the upcoming census, bridging the gap between raw geographic data and actionable administrative resources. Furthermore, the field teams are actively revising settlement names and mapping dispersed areas. This granular data collection is essential for modernizing national cartography. It transforms traditional maps into dynamic, digital assets that reflect current population realities. Such precision supports equitable resource distribution and improves the overall reliability of public policy decisions based on geographic and demographic indicators. This update is relevant to open data as it lays the groundwork for transparent, accessible census information. High-quality, georeferenced public data empowers researchers and citizens to analyze social trends. By releasing accurate spatial and demographic datasets, the government fosters accountability and enables third-party innovation. Ultimately, this structured data collection promotes a more informed society capable of utilizing open government information for sustainable development.
