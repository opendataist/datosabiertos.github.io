Actividad de restaurantes retrocede 1,35%: ¿Qué negocios fueron los más golpeados?

The restaurant sector experienced a contraction, marking a second consecutive month of decline. This downturn was primarily driven by reduced customer traffic and the temporary closure of branches within traditional dining categories, highlighting vulnerabilities in the face of shifting consumer habits and operational challenges. Conversely, specialized segments such as catering and beverage services demonstrated robust growth. Catering benefited from increased demand for corporate and social events, while beverage establishments leveraged strategic marketing and extended hours to attract patrons, indicating a polarization where adaptive businesses thrive despite broader sectoral headwinds. This data is relevant to open_data as it illustrates the value of disaggregated statistical indicators. By revealing distinct trends across sub-sectors, open data enables stakeholders to move beyond aggregate averages, facilitating precise policy making and strategic investments that target specific growth areas rather than treating the industry as a monolith.
