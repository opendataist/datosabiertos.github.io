Según INEGI, en Coatzacoalcos la gente se siente un poco más segura

Coatzacoalcos achieved a significant reduction in perceived insecurity, distinguishing itself among Mexican municipalities. This decline in fear levels correlates with a sharp decrease in high-impact crimes like kidnapping and homicide, validating local safety efforts. The case is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how INEGI’s ENSU provides critical, granular evidence for evaluating public policy efficacy. Such transparent datasets empower citizens to understand safety trends and hold officials accountable. Ultimately, this highlights the power of accessible statistical data to reveal positive societal shifts. By making survey results public, institutions enable a clearer narrative of progress, fostering trust and informed civic engagement regarding urban security improvements.
