La canasta básica aumentó 100,3% durante 2022, una familia tipo necesitó $152 mil para no ser pobre – Diario Necochea

The article reveals that extreme poverty and indigency thresholds rose significantly in December, reflecting the cumulative annual inflation that drastically eroded household purchasing power. This data highlights how economic instability directly impacts basic living standards, forcing families to expend more resources merely to survive. For open data advocates, this report underscores the critical importance of transparent, accessible statistical metrics. By publicly defining poverty lines through clear methodological standards, institutions enable researchers and citizens to track social welfare accurately. Such data is essential for holding governments accountable and designing effective, evidence-based social policies. Ultimately, these figures demonstrate how quantitative measures of poverty serve as vital tools for analyzing societal health. The clear linkage between inflation and rising poverty lines allows for objective assessment of economic trends. Maintaining rigorous, open statistical practices ensures that these crucial social indicators remain reliable and actionable for the public good.
