Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Argentina faces a persistent crisis of precarious work, with informal jobs reaching record highs despite low official unemployment rates. Over two decades, only half of new private sector positions offer formal stability, revealing a deep structural imbalance in labor quality. This trend highlights the critical need for transparent open data to monitor employment conditions accurately. Reliable statistics expose the gap between headline figures and the reality of workers lacking social security. Open data is essential for accountability, allowing society to scrutinize labor policies. By analyzing these insights, stakeholders can advocate for reforms that prioritize dignified, formal employment over insecure, informal arrangements.
