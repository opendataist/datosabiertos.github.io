Espacio de información en construcción en Aranda de Duero

The local government of Aranda champions transparency as a core political value, yet its municipal rating dropped significantly recently. This decline is not due to reduced information disclosure, but rather to stricter evaluation criteria from the Dyntra platform, which added new indicators primarily affecting public contracting compliance. Despite the lower score, the municipality remains a regional leader in transparency. Officials argue that much of the required documentation already exists on other official state platforms but lacks direct integration on the local site. Consequently, administrative efforts are now focused on linking these existing resources to meet the updated standards and improve the ranking. This situation highlights the critical role of data integration and standardization in open government initiatives. The administration plans to leverage future Smart City projects to significantly boost open data efforts, demonstrating how technological infrastructure is essential for making public information truly accessible, interoperable, and useful for citizens.
