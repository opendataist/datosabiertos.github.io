PRI va contra reforma del Poder Judicial

The PRI bloc opposes electoral judicial reforms, arguing that popular voting threatens judicial independence. They warn against weakening autonomous oversight bodies, insisting justice must remain free from political influence to ensure equity. This stance is vital for open data as it protects the transparency institutions that guarantee public access to information and safeguard data rights against government overreach.
