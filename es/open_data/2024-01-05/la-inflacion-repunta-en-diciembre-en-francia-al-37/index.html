La inflación repunta en diciembre en Francia al 3,7%

French inflation rose in December, driven by higher energy and service costs rather than food prices. This shift indicates that while immediate crisis impacts are fading, the energy transition maintains pressure on price levels. Although short-term spikes have subsided, inflation is projected to remain above pre-crisis norms. The data suggests a structural economic change, with energy costs playing a decisive role in future price trajectories for consumers. This case highlights the importance of granular, timely data in tracking economic transitions. Open data initiatives provide essential visibility into sector-specific trends, allowing policymakers and citizens to understand the underlying drivers behind macroeconomic indicators.
