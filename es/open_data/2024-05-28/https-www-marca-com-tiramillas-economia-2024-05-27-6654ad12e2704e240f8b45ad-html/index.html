The article highlights significant daily fluctuations in electricity prices, advising consumers to shift heavy usage away from expensive evening hours and towards midday when costs are negligible. This dynamic pricing structure forces users to actively manage their consumption patterns based on real-time market data to reduce expenses. This phenomenon is highly relevant to open data because it relies on transparent, publicly accessible hourly pricing metrics published by market operators. Understanding and utilizing this open data enables better energy efficiency, promotes consumer empowerment, and supports the development of smart grid technologies that optimize demand through data-driven decision-making.
