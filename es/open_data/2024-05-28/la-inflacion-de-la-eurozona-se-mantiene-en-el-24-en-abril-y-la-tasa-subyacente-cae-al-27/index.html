La inflación de la eurozona se mantiene en el 2,4% en abril y la tasa subyacente cae al 2,7%

Eurostat reports stable inflation in the eurozone and the EU for April. The core rate decreased, while energy prices fell, with services remaining the primary inflation driver. Divergent national rates highlight economic variations across member states. This data is crucial for open_data as it demonstrates the value of standardized, accessible statistical indicators. Public access to such transparent metrics allows citizens to verify economic health and hold institutions accountable. Open data initiatives rely on this reliable information flow. By disseminating official statistics, governments foster trust and enable informed public debate. This transparency is essential for a democratic society to understand complex economic trends.
