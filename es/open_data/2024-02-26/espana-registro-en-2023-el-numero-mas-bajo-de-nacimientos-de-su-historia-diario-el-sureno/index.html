España registró en 2023 el número más bajo de nacimientos de su historia - Diario El Sureño

Spain’s 2023 births hit historic lows, causing natural population decline due to deaths exceeding births. This demographic shift, marked by later parenthood and low fertility, highlights significant societal changes in family planning and aging. Despite this contraction, the total population grew, driven substantially by immigration from countries like Colombia, Ukraine, and Venezuela. This surge in foreign residents offset the negative natural growth, maintaining the country’s overall population size above 48 million. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how diverse datasets—birth rates, mortality, and migration—must be integrated to reveal the true picture of demographic dynamics. It underscores the importance of accessible, comprehensive data for understanding complex societal trends beyond simple birth statistics.
