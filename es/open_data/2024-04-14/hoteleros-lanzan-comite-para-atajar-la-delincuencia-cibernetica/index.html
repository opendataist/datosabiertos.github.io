Hoteleros lanzan comité para atajar la delincuencia cibernética

Acotur has established a dedicated committee focused on data protection and cybersecurity, prioritizing the safeguarding of personal information across its tourism sector members. This initiative aims to proactively prevent cyber threats and raise awareness regarding digital safety, ensuring that associated hotels and resorts operate with enhanced security standards in an increasingly vulnerable digital landscape. The association also emphasizes professionalizing industry staff through educational programs designed to foster loyalty and attract new talent. By promoting best practices and strengthening institutional relationships, Acotur seeks to elevate the quality of service in the Mexican Caribbean, positioning the sector as a viable and modern career path for graduates and professionals seeking stability and growth. This development is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical intersection between tourism services and personal information management. It demonstrates a growing industry recognition that robust data governance and security are fundamental to consumer trust. As tourism entities increasingly handle vast amounts of user data, understanding these internal protective measures offers valuable insights into how private sector organizations manage privacy, a key component of responsible open data ecosystems.
