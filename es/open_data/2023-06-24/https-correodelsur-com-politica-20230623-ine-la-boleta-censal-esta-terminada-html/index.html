Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The final design of the 2024 census questionnaire is ready, featuring 58 questions aimed at gathering critical sociodemographic data on location and mortality. This streamlined structure reflects a collaborative consensus among universities and territorial entities, ensuring broad stakeholder alignment before the final September presentation. Pilots indicate a forty-minute completion time for average families, though optimizations aim to reduce this further. These efficiency measures are crucial for ensuring the operational success of the upcoming national census, balancing comprehensive data collection with respondent convenience. This development is vital for open data because the resulting dataset will form the foundational infrastructure for public transparency. Accessible, high-quality census data empowers researchers, civil society, and government bodies to make evidence-based decisions, fostering accountability and informed policy-making regarding population dynamics and resource distribution.
