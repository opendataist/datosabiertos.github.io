La actividad económica creció 2,6% interanual en noviembre | Análisis

The article reports a year-end economic expansion driven by broad sectoral gains, despite drought impacts on agriculture. The government projects sustained growth, aiming for consecutive annual increases to restore purchasing power and credit stability. This data is relevant to open data initiatives by demonstrating the utility of high-frequency economic indicators like the EMAE for real-time monitoring. Transparent access to such granular, timely statistics enables independent verification of national performance and fosters trust in public policy decisions. Ultimately, the release of detailed activity estimates supports accountability and informed analysis. Open access to these metrics allows researchers and citizens to scrutinize the divergence between official growth claims and the lived economic realities of the population, essential for democratic oversight.
