Baleares lideró la ocupación hotelera en 2022

Baleares demonstrates robust tourism recovery in 2022, significantly exceeding 2021 figures and slightly surpassing pre-pandemic visitor counts, although overnight stays remain below 2019 levels. The region led Spain in hotel occupancy rates and established itself as a premier international destination, highlighting strong demand for high-value tourism. Despite rising prices, profitability indicators like revenue per available room showed substantial growth compared to the previous year, reflecting a resilient and competitive market position. The tourism data reveals distinct patterns in traveler origins and distribution. While the islands rank high for international guests, they place lower among domestic tourists, who predominantly favor mainland regions. This contrast underscores Baleares’ specific appeal to foreign markets and its reliance on global tourism flows. Furthermore, major tourist zones like Mallorca and Palma drove the majority of activity, indicating a concentration of benefits in key areas rather than a widespread regional distribution. This article is highly relevant to open data advocates because it illustrates the necessity of transparent, standardized statistical reporting in the public sector. Accurate, publicly accessible metrics on occupancy, pricing, and traveler demographics are essential for policymakers, researchers, and businesses to analyze trends and make informed decisions. Without such open datasets, understanding the true economic impact and sector health remains difficult, hindering effective governance and sustainable tourism planning.
