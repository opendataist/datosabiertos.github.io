Insabi deberá informar consultas a pacientes con secuelas de COVID-19

The INAI ruled that the Institute for Welfare Health must disclose data on consultations for post-COVID patients. This decision resolves jurisdictional disputes, ensuring accountability for health services addressing long-term sequelae. The ruling highlights the severe physical and mental impacts of the virus, including pulmonary fibrosis and psychological disorders. Public authorities are compelled to monitor these effects, recognizing the potential strain on millions of individuals across the region. This case is relevant to open data as it establishes a legal precedent for mandatory transparency in public health records. It reinforces the right of citizens to access specific medical statistics, promoting accountability and informed public policy regarding pandemic aftermaths.
