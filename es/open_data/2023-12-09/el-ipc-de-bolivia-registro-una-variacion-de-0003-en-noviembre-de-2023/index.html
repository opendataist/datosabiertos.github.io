El IPC de Bolivia registró una variación de -0,003% en noviembre de 2023

Bolivia’s November 2023 data reveals near-zero monthly inflation and low annual rates, driven by price drops in specific goods like poultry and transport. This stability highlights distinct regional trends, particularly in major urban centers where deflationary pressures were most pronounced. The distribution of price changes across diverse product categories underscores the complexity of consumer basket dynamics. Such granular tracking of commodity shifts is essential for understanding broader economic health beyond aggregate figures. This report is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent, localized statistical releases empower independent analysis. Accessible government data enables researchers to verify official narratives and identify emerging economic patterns for public accountability.
