Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Prices for essential food and telephony dropped significantly, driving a notable decline in the consumer price index. This trend resulted in negative annual and cumulative inflation rates, indicating a broad deflationary pressure on everyday goods and services. The data reveals that nearly half of the tracked items saw price reductions, outweighing the increases in sectors like air travel. This widespread decrease highlights shifting market dynamics and cost reductions across major economic categories during the observed period. This information is vital for open data initiatives focused on economic transparency, allowing citizens and researchers to monitor inflation trends and assess the purchasing power of households based on accessible statistical metrics.
