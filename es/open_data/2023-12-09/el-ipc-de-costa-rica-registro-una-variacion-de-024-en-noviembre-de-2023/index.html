El IPC de Costa Rica registró una variación de -0,24% en noviembre de 2023

Costa Rica experiences negative inflation, indicating a general decline in consumer prices. This trend, driven by falling costs in food and transport, reflects shifting economic dynamics for households and markets. The data highlights significant price reductions in essential items like vegetables and mobile services. Conversely, travel and housing costs rose. This divergence underscores the complex interplay between supply chains and consumer behavior during periods of deflationary pressure. This article is relevant to open_data because the Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos (INEC) publishes detailed indices. Access to such transparent, granular datasets allows analysts to track economic health, validate models, and foster informed public discourse on national financial trends.
