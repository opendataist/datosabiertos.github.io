Ingresó al Concejo de Trelew un proyecto para adherir a la Ley de Transparencia en la Obra Pública - Últimas Noticias: El Chubut

Trelew has introduced a legislative initiative to adhere to provincial transparency laws for public works and to amend its municipal ordinances. The primary goal is to enhance accountability and citizen participation in contracting processes. This move seeks to ensure that public resources are managed with greater integrity, directly addressing past administrative flaws by implementing stricter controls over public spending and procurement. The proposal mandates that public works budgets must be prepared by qualified professionals and dated within thirty days of the bid opening. By treating these budgets as sworn declarations of accuracy, the measure imposes serious legal consequences for discrepancies. This strict requirement aims to prevent cost inflation and ensure that financial estimates reflect current market realities, thereby safeguarding the municipality against potential fraud and mismanagement in project valuation. This article is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a foundational framework for transparency. By requiring rigorous documentation and public access to bidding stages, it creates the necessary conditions for data availability. Open data initiatives rely on such regulatory mandates to ensure that public contract information is not only published but also accurate and verifiable, empowering citizens to monitor government actions effectively through accessible, reliable datasets.
