Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Severe flooding in Santa Marta has forced authorities to initiate comprehensive impact assessments and emergency relief operations. This situation highlights the critical importance of open data in disaster management, as transparent, real-time information enables effective resource allocation and rapid community assistance. Publicly accessible datasets regarding affected households and infrastructure damage allow for better coordination of humanitarian aid and preventive measures. By making this information available, governments can ensure accountability and improve the speed of response to climate-related emergencies, protecting vulnerable populations from further harm. Ultimately, this event underscores how open data serves as a vital tool for resilience. It transforms raw emergency statistics into actionable insights, fostering trust between citizens and institutions while guiding long-term strategies to mitigate future climate risks in urban and rural areas alike.
