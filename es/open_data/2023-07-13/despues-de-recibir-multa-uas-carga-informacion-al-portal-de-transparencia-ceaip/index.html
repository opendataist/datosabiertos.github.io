Después de recibir multa, UAS carga información al portal de transparencia: Ceaip

The Autonomous University of Sinaloa has begun uploading compliance data to its transparency portal following a significant fine for failing to meet its disclosure obligations. This corrective action addresses previously hidden information regarding public servant salaries and social communication agreements, marking a crucial step toward institutional accountability. However, the process remains incomplete because paying the penalty does not exempt the university from its duty to maintain accurate records. Persistent gaps exist in the publication of acquisitions, which must align with federal systems like Compranet. The authority emphasizes that true transparency requires ongoing, parallel updates rather than one-time fixes, ensuring that all procurement data is consistently visible to the public. This case highlights the critical role of open data in maintaining institutional integrity. It demonstrates that mere compliance with deadlines is insufficient; data must be accurate, accessible, and regularly updated. For the open data community, this serves as a vital reminder that effective governance relies on the continuous quality and transparency of public information, not just its initial publication.
