Firma Agepro convenio con Inegi para instalar Oficina Virtual de Información Económica - Noticaribe

The strategic collaboration between Agepro and INEGI establishes the OVIE platform to enhance economic decision-making through precise, updated data. This initiative directly supports transparency and sustainable regional development by ensuring stakeholders have access to reliable statistical resources. By providing advanced analytical tools and official statistics, the system fosters better governance and promotes rigorous economic research. The agreement ensures operational sustainability through dedicated funding and strict adherence to regulatory standards, prioritizing measurable progress and institutional accountability. This partnership is vital for open data because it bridges official statistical authority with local strategic planning. It demonstrates how integrating authoritative datasets into specialized platforms enables informed policy-making and strengthens democratic oversight through accessible, high-quality information.
