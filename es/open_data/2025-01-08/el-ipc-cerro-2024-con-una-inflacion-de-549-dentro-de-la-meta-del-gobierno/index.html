El IPC cerró 2024 con una inflación de 5,49%, dentro de la meta del Gobierno

The Consumer Price Index confirms that annual inflation remained within the government’s target range, validating current stabilization policies. This achievement is crucial for open data as it demonstrates the value of transparent, timely statistical tracking in verifying macroeconomic goals and ensuring public accountability regarding price stability. However, significant divergences across sectors highlight underlying economic pressures. Rising costs in food and recreation contrast with drops in housing due to specific subsidies, illustrating the need for granular data analysis to understand how targeted interventions affect different consumer groups. This case underscores the importance of accessible, detailed inflation metrics for policymakers and researchers. By examining these variations, stakeholders can better assess the real impact of economic measures, reinforcing the role of open data in diagnosing sectoral challenges and guiding future strategic decisions.
