Venezolanos por el Mundo: En el 2023 más de 60 mil venezolanos solicitaron asilo en España

Spain’s soaring asylum applications highlight its role as a key EU destination, driven largely by Venezuelan, Colombian, and Peruvian nationals. This surge necessitates efficient data management to track demographic trends and regional distribution across Madrid and Andalusia. The government’s decision to grant Catalonia separate migratory competencies introduces significant complexity to national data integration. This administrative divergence threatens data harmonization, requiring robust interoperable systems to ensure consistent monitoring across different regional frameworks. This development is crucial for open data advocates, illustrating the challenges of decentralization. It underscores the need for standardized, transparent data infrastructures that can reconcile divergent regional requirements while maintaining comprehensive, accessible insights into migration flows for public accountability.
