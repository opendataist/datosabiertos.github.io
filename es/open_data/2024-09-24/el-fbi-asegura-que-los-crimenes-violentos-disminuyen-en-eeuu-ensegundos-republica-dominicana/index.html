El FBI asegura que los crímenes violentos disminuyen en EEUU Ensegundos República Dominicana

FBI data reveals a decline in US violent crimes in 2023, directly contradicting political claims of uncontrollable violence. This underscores the critical role of open data in verifying factual realities and debunking misinformation based on anecdotal or unverified assertions. Transparent, consistent statistics empower citizens to hold leaders accountable, ensuring public discourse remains grounded in empirical evidence rather than unfounded political narratives. Such openness fosters trust in institutions and highlights how accessible data serves as a vital tool for democracy, enabling informed debate and preventing the manipulation of public perception through selective or false reporting.
