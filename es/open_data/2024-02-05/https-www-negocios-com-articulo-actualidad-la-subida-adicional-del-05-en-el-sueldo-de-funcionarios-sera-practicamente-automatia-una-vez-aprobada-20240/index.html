Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Spanish government is preparing to approve an additional salary increase for public employees, contingent upon receiving official certification from the National Statistics Institute regarding 2023 GDP growth. This procedural step highlights the critical role of authoritative, verified statistical data in validating policy decisions. The government cannot proceed with the ministerial council approval without this formal confirmation, demonstrating how public administration relies on precise institutional metrics to execute fiscal agreements. The impending wage adjustment stems from a prior agreement linking salary increases to economic performance, specifically when GDP growth exceeds initial projections. By conditioning the raise on confirmed macroeconomic indicators, the process ensures that public spending adjustments are grounded in transparent, objective evidence rather than estimates. This dependency underscores the necessity of high-quality, official data as the foundation for equitable labor negotiations and budgetary planning. This situation is highly relevant to open data advocates because it illustrates the practical necessity of accessible, trustworthy, and timely statistical information. When government actions are directly tied to published official figures, the integrity and availability of that data become paramount for democratic accountability. It reinforces the argument that open data must be reliable and standardized, as these metrics directly influence citizens' rights and economic stability.
