Secretaría General de Gobierno cumplió al 100% con Ley de Transparencia

The government achieved full legal compliance in transparency and access to public information during 2023. This sustained commitment demonstrates a robust framework for accountability, effectively combating corruption and fostering civic engagement through open governance practices. Monthly processing of numerous information requests highlights the institutional dedication to transparency. By systematically addressing citizen inquiries across various administrative areas, the administration ensures that the right to information is actively respected and accessible to the public. This success is particularly relevant to open data, as it establishes a culture where data availability supports democratic oversight. Such adherence encourages the release of actionable public datasets, empowering citizens to participate more effectively in governance and reducing opportunities for illicit activities.
