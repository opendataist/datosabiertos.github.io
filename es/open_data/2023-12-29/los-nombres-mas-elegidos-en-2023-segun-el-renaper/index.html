Los nombres más elegidos en 2023, según el Renaper

This article highlights the utility of open data by demonstrating how public repositories, such as Argentina’s Portal de Datos, transform raw government statistics into accessible, meaningful societal insights. By leveraging data from the National Registry of Persons, citizens can explore long-term cultural trends, revealing that naming conventions evolve rapidly in response to social phenomena, historical events, and international influences rather than remaining static traditions. The analysis underscores the dynamic nature of cultural identity, showing a clear shift from traditional compound names to shorter, unique, and often foreign-inspired choices. This trend illustrates how open datasets can reflect real-time societal changes, allowing users to track the rise and fall of specific names linked to global sports successes or religious events, thereby providing a quantitative measure of cultural shifts over the past century. Relevant to open data, this case proves that transparent access to public records fosters civic engagement and cultural awareness. It validates the importance of making administrative data user-friendly and comprehensive, as it empowers individuals to understand their place within broader historical and demographic contexts. Ultimately, it showcases how open data serves not just as a statistical tool, but as a bridge connecting personal identity with collective history.
