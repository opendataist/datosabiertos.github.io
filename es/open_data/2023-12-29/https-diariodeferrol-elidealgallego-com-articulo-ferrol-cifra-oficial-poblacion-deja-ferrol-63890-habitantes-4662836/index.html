The official municipal census confirms a continued, albeit slowing, demographic decline in Ferrol, which has dropped below 64,000 residents. While this decrease is less severe than historical averages, indicating a potential stabilization, the broader regional trend remains negative. Most municipalities in the Ferrol, Eume, and Ortegal comarcas have lost population over the last decade, reflecting deep-seated structural challenges in the area. Conversely, a notable exception to this widespread depopulation is the Ortegal comarca, which managed to gain residents by 2023. This positive shift is primarily driven by increases in Ortigueira and Cariño, which offset losses in neighboring towns. This suggests that localized factors can counteract general regional trends, offering a nuanced view of rural dynamics where some areas are successfully retaining or attracting inhabitants despite the overall decline. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, publicly available statistical records, such as those published by the INE, enable granular analysis of community health. By accessing these official datasets, stakeholders can identify emerging patterns, such as the slight deceleration in Ferrol’s decline or the resilience of specific rural zones. This transparency allows for evidence-based policy-making and highlights the importance of monitoring open datasets to understand the multifaceted nature of demographic shifts.
