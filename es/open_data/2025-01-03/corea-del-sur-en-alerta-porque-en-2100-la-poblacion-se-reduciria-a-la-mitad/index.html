Corea del Sur en alerta porque en 2100 la población se reduciría a la mitad

South Korea faces a severe demographic crisis, with its population projected to halve by 2100 due to one of the world’s lowest fertility rates. Government incentives, such as expanded parental leave and housing subsidies, fail to address root causes like economic burdens and work-life imbalance, leaving young people disincentivized from starting families. This situation highlights the critical role of open data in governance. Transparent, accessible statistical information allows citizens and policymakers to accurately assess demographic trends, exposing the efficacy—or lack thereof—of public policies. Without reliable data, addressing such complex socio-economic challenges becomes nearly impossible. Ultimately, the article underscores that while governments may propose solutions, their success depends on accurate data analysis. Open data initiatives empower society to monitor these efforts, ensuring accountability and driving informed debates on sustainable demographic strategies and social welfare.
