Más de 2 millones de mascotas habitan los hogares uruguayos, revela el censo

The 2023 census reveals that pets are integral to Uruguayan households, with dogs significantly outpacing cats in popularity. This data highlights how animal companionship varies across different family structures, offering insights into social dynamics and living arrangements within the country. Open data initiatives benefit from such detailed demographic breakdowns, as they enable granular analysis of lifestyle trends. By making this information publicly accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand community needs and societal shifts regarding pet ownership. Ultimately, this transparency fosters evidence-based decision-making. It allows stakeholders to identify regional preferences and tailor services accordingly, demonstrating the value of standardized, accessible statistics in capturing the nuanced realities of daily life and community well-being.
