Consejo para la Transparencia exige a la Usach los datos académicos de Elisa Loncón por investigación sobre su año sabático remunerado | The Clinic

The Council for Transparency mandated the University of Santiago de Chile to release the academic background of Elisa Loncón, a public figure and former constitutional convention president. This decision underscores the principle that the work of state-administered officials must be conducted with probity and transparency, ensuring public access to professional records. The university initially withheld this data, claiming it infringed on personal rights, but the council rejected this argument. The ruling establishes that verifying academic contributions and employment history does not threaten the dignity or privacy of public servants, reinforcing accountability over institutional secrecy. This case is relevant to open data because it clarifies the boundary between personal privacy and public accountability. It confirms that professional histories of high-ranking officials in state-linked institutions are not inherently private, promoting a culture where citizen requests for institutional transparency are prioritized over unsubstantiated privacy claims.
