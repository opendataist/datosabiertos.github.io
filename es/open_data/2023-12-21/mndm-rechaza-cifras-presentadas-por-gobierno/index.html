MNDM rechaza cifras presentadas por gobierno

The MNDM rejects the federal census for obscuring real data and lacking family participation. This highlights the critical open data principle that accurate, transparent records require direct community engagement and methodological clarity to be trustworthy. By demanding a dialogue table and standardized databases, the movement emphasizes that open data must be co-created with affected communities. Without such collaboration, official statistics remain contested and fail to serve justice or public accountability needs. This dispute underscores the necessity of participatory data governance in human rights contexts. Open data initiatives are only legitimate when they ensure transparency and inclusive methodology, preventing the erasure of vital social realities by state authorities.
