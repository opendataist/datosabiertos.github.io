Deben transparentar permiso para antígenos

The INAI ruled that Cofepris must release the full resolution regarding the emergency use authorization for the Pfizer vaccine in young children. Cofepris incorrectly claimed the information did not exist, failing to conduct a comprehensive internal search. This decision reinforces the principle of maximum transparency, ensuring that regulatory decisions affecting public health are accessible to citizens. It highlights the necessity for government agencies to fully cooperate with transparency requests. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the critical role of oversight bodies in enforcing data accessibility. It underscores the importance of complete and verifiable information release in democratic societies, ensuring accountability in health policy implementation.
