The Mexican automotive industry demonstrates significant resilience and sustained growth in production and exports during 2024. Driven by post-pandemic recovery, foreign investment, and technological adaptation, Mexico has solidified its position as a global leader. This success stems from diversifying markets and adopting advanced innovations, which have mitigated previous supply chain disruptions and enhanced overall sector vitality. This economic powerhouse exerts a profound impact on national development, contributing substantially to the Gross Domestic Product while generating extensive direct and indirect employment. The sector acts as a catalyst for related industries such as steel, plastics, and logistics. However, the industry must navigate challenges like global competition and environmental regulations by focusing on clean technology adoption and maintaining competitiveness to ensure long-term stability amidst fluctuating material prices. This article is relevant to open data because the INEGI statistics serve as a critical case study for transparent, high-frequency public information dissemination. Reliable government data enables stakeholders to track economic resilience, validate investment trends, and analyze the real-world effects of digital transformation policies. Open access to these datasets allows researchers and policymakers to better understand how transparency in national statistics supports informed decision-making and fosters trust in the country’s strategic industrial planning.
