Día de la alimentación: ¿A cuántos afecta la inseguridad alimentaria en Paraguay? - Economía - ABC Color

A pioneering statistical effort in Paraguay, conducted by the INE with FAO support, has successfully integrated food insecurity metrics into national household surveys. This development marks a significant milestone for open data initiatives, as it establishes standardized, accessible indicators for monitoring nutritional well-being. By applying international measurement scales like the FIES, the country now offers transparent, comparable data that allows researchers and policymakers to accurately assess the prevalence and severity of hunger across different demographics. The findings reveal that a substantial portion of the population faces moderate food insecurity, primarily driven by economic constraints and rising food prices. While urban and rural areas both suffer, rural households and those led by women or containing multiple children experience disproportionately higher rates of vulnerability. These disparities highlight the urgent need for targeted social interventions and demonstrate how granular open data can expose structural inequalities that aggregate statistics often mask. This publication is crucial for the open data community because it proves the value of publishing detailed, nuanced socioeconomic indicators. When governments release such specific datasets, it empowers civil society and developers to build tools that advocate for food security and hold institutions accountable. Ultimately, this approach transforms raw numbers into actionable insights, fostering a more informed public debate and facilitating data-driven solutions to combat hunger effectively.
