La UNCuyo subió en el ranking de universidades de Argentina

The Universidad Nacional de Cuyo has improved its national standing and remains within the top 100 in Latin America, driven by strengths in science, agriculture, and arts. This progress highlights the critical role of international visibility and academic reputation in modern higher education evaluation, shifting focus toward research output and global collaboration rather than just local prestige. However, the article reveals significant barriers to maintaining this position, specifically the high costs associated with publishing in international scientific journals. These financial hurdles challenge the sustainability of public research, illustrating how economic constraints can limit access to the global knowledge economy and hinder the internationalization of local academic work. This case is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of open access and transparent research dissemination. By advocating for strategies that reduce publication costs and enhance international links, the institution implicitly supports open data principles. Making research freely available ensures broader accessibility, fosters equitable global collaboration, and maximizes the societal impact of scientific findings without being restricted by prohibitive financial barriers.
