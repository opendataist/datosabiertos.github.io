El uso de la capacidad instalada de la industria aumentó hasta 68,9 % en noviembre, informó el Indec

The report highlights a modest recovery in industrial activity, with installed capacity utilization and overall production showing growth compared to previous periods. This data reveals a heterogeneous economic landscape where sectors like basic metals and chemical products significantly outperformed others, while industries such as textiles and automotive manufacturing lagged behind. The disparity underscores structural vulnerabilities within the national industrial base, indicating that recent gains are concentrated in specific high-demand areas rather than reflecting broad-based strength. Business sentiment remains cautious, with nearly half of surveyed entrepreneurs anticipating no significant changes in domestic demand, while others predict declines. This hesitation suggests lingering uncertainty regarding future consumption patterns. Although export expectations are slightly more positive, the overall outlook reflects a sector waiting for clearer signals of sustained growth. The divergence between current production data and future expectations points to a fragile environment where immediate performance does not guarantee long-term stability or widespread confidence among industry leaders. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how standardized statistical metrics, like capacity utilization rates, serve as foundational indicators for assessing economic health. Transparent access to such granular, sector-specific data allows researchers and policymakers to identify imbalances and track recovery trends objectively. By making this information publicly available, institutions enable evidence-based analysis that can inform better economic strategies and foster accountability, ensuring that public discourse on industrial progress is grounded in verifiable, comprehensive datasets rather than isolated anecdotes.
