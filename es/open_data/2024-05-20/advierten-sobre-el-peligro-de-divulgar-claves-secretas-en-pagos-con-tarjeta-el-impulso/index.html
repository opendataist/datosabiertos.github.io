▷ Advierten sobre el peligro de divulgar claves secretas en pagos con tarjeta - El Impulso

Mastercard urges citizens to protect their PINs to prevent financial data breaches. Sharing such secrets compromises authentication mechanisms, turning private information into accessible funds for malicious actors. The article emphasizes that digital transactions require the same vigilance as physical interactions. Users must verify the reputation of online platforms and look for security indicators before paying, as online fraud remains a significant risk due to identity theft and malicious software. This content is relevant to open data because it highlights the tension between digital accessibility and personal privacy. It underscores the necessity of strong data protection practices when handling sensitive financial information, a critical concern for maintaining trust in open digital ecosystems and ensuring that data sharing does not inadvertently expose individuals to security threats.
