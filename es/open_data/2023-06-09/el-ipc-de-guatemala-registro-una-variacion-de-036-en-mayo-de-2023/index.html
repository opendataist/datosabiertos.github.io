El IPC de Guatemala registró una variación de -0,36% en mayo de 2023

Guatemala experienced a brief deflationary dip in May 2023, signaling a significant slowdown in price growth compared to the previous month. While the annual inflation rate accelerated, this monthly fluctuation highlights the volatility inherent in national economic indicators. This data reveals shifting consumer costs, with transport prices falling while essential food items like tortillas and avocados saw slight increases. Such granular breakdowns allow citizens to understand which sectors drive broader economic trends and affect their daily purchasing power. This information is crucial for open_data as it demonstrates how public statistical agencies provide essential, transparent metrics. Accessible, accurate datasets empower researchers and policymakers to analyze economic health, fostering informed decision-making and trust in governmental transparency regarding national financial stability.
