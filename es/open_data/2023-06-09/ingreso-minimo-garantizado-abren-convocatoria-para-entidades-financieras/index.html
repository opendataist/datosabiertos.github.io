Ingreso Mínimo Garantizado: abren convocatoria para entidades financieras

Bogotá has initiated a public procurement process to select financial institutions responsible for distributing cash transfers through its Guaranteed Minimum Income program. By opening this tender, the city aims to leverage private sector infrastructure to ensure efficient and accessible monetary dispersal, including virtual and physical banking services. This move highlights the critical intersection of public welfare distribution and the operational capabilities of private financial entities. The selection criteria focus on the providers' network of channels, experience, and fee structures, with the intent to finalize contracts by mid-year for service delivery in the subsequent quarter. The program is specifically targeted at households in socio-economic strata A and B, with funds directed to a single adult per family using approved digital platforms. This approach seeks to streamline social aid distribution while integrating beneficiaries into the formal financial system. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent information regarding public service contracts and beneficiary eligibility. Access to the specific terms, selected entities, and detailed information requirements allows for public scrutiny and data-driven analysis of social policy implementation. Open data facilitates accountability in how public funds are distributed and managed, ensuring that the mechanisms supporting vulnerable populations are visible and auditable.
