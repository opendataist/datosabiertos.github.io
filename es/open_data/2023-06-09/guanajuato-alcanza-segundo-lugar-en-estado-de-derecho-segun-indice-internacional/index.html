Guanajuato alcanza segundo lugar en Estado de Derecho, según índice internacional

Guanajuato has significantly improved its position in the Rule of Law ranking, securing the second place nationally according to the 2022–2023 World Justice Project data. This upward trajectory reflects a consistent improvement in governance metrics, with the state leading the nation in Open Government, Regulatory Compliance, and Civic Justice. These top-tier scores highlight Guanajuato’s commitment to transparency and institutional accountability, setting a benchmark for other regions. While the state excels in democratic and regulatory frameworks, challenges persist in public safety and fundamental rights. The rankings reveal that Guanajuato lags behind the national average in Order and Security, indicating a critical area requiring attention. Conversely, improvements in Criminal Justice and Fundamental Rights suggest that targeted policy interventions can yield positive results even in complex legal domains. This report is highly relevant to the open data movement as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized metrics can drive public accountability. By making these detailed performance indicators accessible, the WJP enables citizens and policymakers to track progress objectively. Furthermore, Guanajuato’s leadership in Open Government underscores the necessity of releasing granular, high-quality datasets to foster trust and informed decision-making in governance.
