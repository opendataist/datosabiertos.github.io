Destaca AMLO que sus programas sociales funcionan

The article reports a significant reduction in the income inequality gap in Mexico, attributed to President López Obrador’s social welfare policies. Data indicates that the disparity between the richest and poorest households has narrowed considerably compared to previous administration periods. Key improvements include substantial real increases in labor income, salaries, and social assistance for the poorest decile of the population. These gains contrast sharply with declines observed before the current government took office, suggesting a tangible improvement in the economic well-being of vulnerable groups. This information is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical agency (INEGI) surveys. It demonstrates how publicly available government data can be utilized to verify political claims, assess social progress, and hold leadership accountable through transparent, evidence-based analysis of household economic conditions.
