Ponen a prueba todos los instrumentos en censo experimental, no buscan datos

The Bolivian National Statistics Institute is conducting an experimental census in Capinota to validate its survey instruments, methodologies, and logistical frameworks ahead of the 2024 national count. The primary objective is to identify necessary adjustments rather than to collect definitive demographic data, ensuring the final census operates with maximum accuracy and efficiency. This pilot serves as a critical transparency and quality assurance measure for open data initiatives. By refining tools through coordinated efforts with academic and international institutions, the INE enhances the reliability of future statistical outputs. This process underscores the importance of rigorous pre-census testing to establish robust, trustworthy datasets for public use and policy-making. Ultimately, this activity represents the final stage of preparation before the massive mobilization of field personnel. By testing procedures in a representative municipality, the INE aims to streamline the upcoming nationwide operation. This ensures that the resulting open data will be methodologically sound, reflecting true population realities and supporting evidence-based governance.
