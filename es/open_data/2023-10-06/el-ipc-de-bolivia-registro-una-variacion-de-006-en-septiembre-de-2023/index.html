El IPC de Bolivia registró una variación de -0,06% en septiembre de 2023

Bolivia’s consumer price index showed slight deflation in September, driven by lower food and service costs, particularly in major cities. This nuance in pricing dynamics challenges simplistic inflation narratives often found in raw data outputs. The specific decline in staple goods highlights seasonal or supply-side factors rather than broad economic contraction. Understanding these granular shifts is essential for accurate economic modeling and policy formulation. For open data advocates, this case underscores the importance of accessible, detailed statistical records. Transparent access to such disaggregated metrics enables researchers and citizens to verify official claims and derive meaningful insights from complex economic indicators.
