Repunta la productividad laboral

Mexico’s labor productivity has rebounded significantly in the second quarter, marking its highest level in over two years and offering a crucial mechanism to combat inflation. This improvement suggests a stronger economic capacity to manage price pressures through efficient resource utilization rather than monetary tightening alone. The recovery is broad-based, with notable gains across agricultural, industrial, and service sectors, particularly in construction and manufacturing. This widespread growth indicates structural resilience and improved operational efficiencies, signaling that the economy is better positioned to sustain output without proportional increases in labor costs. This development is highly relevant to open_data initiatives, as transparent, high-frequency economic indicators empower analysts to verify real-time trends. Access to granular, reliable data from sources like INEGI enables stakeholders to assess policy impacts accurately and fosters data-driven decision-making in both public administration and private investment.
