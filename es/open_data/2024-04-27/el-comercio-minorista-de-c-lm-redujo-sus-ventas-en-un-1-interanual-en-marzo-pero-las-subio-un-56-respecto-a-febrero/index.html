El comercio minorista de C-LM redujo sus ventas en un 1% interanual en marzo pero las subió un 5,6% respecto a febrero

Retail sales declined in March, breaking a fifteen-month growth streak, while seasonally adjusted figures show slight monthly fluctuations. Conversely, employment in the sector continued to rise, marking thirty-five consecutive months of annual increases. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it highlights the need for transparent, accessible economic indicators. Accurate public statistics enable better analysis of market trends and help stakeholders understand the diverging paths of sales versus job growth in the retail economy.
