La inflación PCE de EEUU repunta al 2,7% y la subyacente se mantiene en el 2,8%

The recent rise in the US private consumption price index indicates persistent inflationary pressure, challenging the narrative of a smooth economic landing. This data suggests that price stability remains elusive, forcing the Federal Reserve to maintain a cautious stance rather than celebrating early victories against inflation. Analysts emphasize that the economy and labor market are still robust, yet the risk of stagflation warrants serious attention. The persistence of rigid inflation implies that monetary policy will likely remain tight in the short term, as policymakers cannot afford to jeopardize economic stability while prices continue to rise. This article is relevant to open_data because it relies on official government statistics, specifically from the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, to interpret macroeconomic trends. It demonstrates how transparent, publicly accessible datasets are essential for validating market expectations and guiding critical decisions regarding monetary policy and economic forecasting.
