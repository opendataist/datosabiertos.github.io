Adelantan que la inflación de diciembre se ubicaría por debajo del 3%

Private estimates forecast December inflation around two percent, with food prices showing moderation. The official data will be released by the national statistics institute, allowing for verification of these independent projections against real economic trends. This information is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical role of public statistics offices in validating private forecasts. Access to the upcoming official dataset enables transparency, allowing citizens and analysts to compare private predictions with authoritative public records, fostering trust in economic indicators.
