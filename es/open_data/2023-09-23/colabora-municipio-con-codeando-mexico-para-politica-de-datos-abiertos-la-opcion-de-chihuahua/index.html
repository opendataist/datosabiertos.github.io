Colabora Municipio con Codeando México para política de datos abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua collaborates with Codeando México to enhance municipal transparency and open data quality. This partnership aims to transform public information into strategic resources that address social issues and boost local competitiveness across economic and academic sectors. The initiative focuses on refining transparency standards through expert analysis of current measurement methodologies. By improving data accessibility and utility, the government seeks to create sustainable technological solutions that empower citizens and foster proactive civic engagement. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how strategic alliances can operationalize transparency mandates. It highlights the practical impact of open data in driving public value, proving that accessible information is essential for fostering innovation, accountability, and effective governance in modern municipalities.
