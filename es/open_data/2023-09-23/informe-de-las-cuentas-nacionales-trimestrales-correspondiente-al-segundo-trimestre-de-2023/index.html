Informe de las Cuentas Nacionales Trimestrales correspondiente al segundo trimestre de 2023

The article highlights significant economic shifts in Equatorial Guinea, notably the surge in non-oil Gross Domestic Product driven by public administration and financial activities. This divergence from the broader economy underscores the critical need for granular, sector-level statistical data to accurately assess national progress beyond aggregate metrics. Conversely, the primary sector suffered a steep decline due to reduced crude oil output and processing losses, while the tertiary sector experienced robust growth fueled by public spending and commerce. These contrasting trends reveal underlying structural dependencies and vulnerabilities that remain obscured when relying solely on summary economic indicators or high-level reports. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible, and detailed statistical publications from national institutes enable deeper analysis of economic health. By making such specific sectoral performance data publicly available, stakeholders can identify root causes of fluctuations and support evidence-based policy decisions rather than relying on opaque or aggregated figures.
