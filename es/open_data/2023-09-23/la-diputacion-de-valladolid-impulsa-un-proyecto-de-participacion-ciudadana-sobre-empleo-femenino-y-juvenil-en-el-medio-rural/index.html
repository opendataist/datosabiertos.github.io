La Diputación de Valladolid impulsa un proyecto de participación ciudadana sobre empleo femenino y juvenil en el medio rural

The Provincial Council of Valladolid is implementing a participatory citizenship project aimed at analyzing and improving employment opportunities for women and youth. By organizing regional workshops, the administration seeks to understand local challenges and foster rural population retention. This approach emphasizes active listening between citizens and government to effectively address labor, entrepreneurial, and employment needs across different geographic zones. The initiative relies on direct engagement with diverse stakeholders, including municipal technicians, business owners, and social associations. Through surveys and diagnostic studies, the project identifies specific barriers to employment. The ultimate goal is to define a strategic action plan that offers tailored measures and activities, ensuring that policies are grounded in the real-world experiences and needs of the target demographic. This article is relevant to open_data as it exemplifies transparent governance through public consultation and data-driven decision-making. By making the diagnostic processes and final results available to the public, the council promotes accountability and collaborative policy creation. This model demonstrates how open, participatory data collection can inform effective public administration and ensure that resources are allocated based on community-validated insights rather than top-down assumptions.
