Roberto Castillo: el INEC determinó que el Ecuador empezará a envejecer

Ecuador faces a demographic shift marked by lower population projections, increased mortality, and net migration. This has triggered an aging society with fewer children and a shrinking future workforce, posing significant challenges to social security sustainability. The trend stems from individual choices driven by economic pressures and professional aspirations, alongside rising divorces and smaller household sizes. These factors reduce the number of contributors needed to support a growing elderly population. This article highlights the critical role of open data in tracking societal changes. By making demographic statistics accessible, governments and researchers can better analyze these shifts and develop policies to address the long-term economic and social implications of an aging population.
