Mujeres indígenas, sin internet y con rezago

The article highlights the severe digital divide in Mexico, where millions lack internet access, disproportionately affecting indigenous women in regions like Oaxaca and Chiapas. Geographical barriers and systemic inequalities restrict their ability to utilize digital tools for economic autonomy, exacerbating existing social disparities. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for transparent, disaggregated metrics to identify marginalized groups and guide equitable infrastructure policies.
