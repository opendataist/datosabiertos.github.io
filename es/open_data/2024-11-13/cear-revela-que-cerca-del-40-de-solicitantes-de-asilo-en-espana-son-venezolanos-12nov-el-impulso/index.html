▷ Cear revela que cerca del 40% de solicitantes de asilo en España son venezolanos #12Nov - El Impulso

Spain experiences a significant surge in asylum requests, predominantly from Latin America, with Venezuela leading as the primary source. However, the approval process remains arduous, resulting in a high rate of rejections and limited granted protections, while humanitarian residencies largely favor Venezuelan nationals despite broader regional needs. This disparity highlights systemic inefficiencies in how international protection is allocated, creating a gap between the volume of applications and the actual rights conferred to vulnerable populations, including a substantial number of unaccompanied minors fleeing violence. For open data advocates, this case underscores the critical need for transparent, granular datasets that reveal demographic breakdowns and procedural outcomes. Accessible data enables civil society to hold governments accountable, ensuring that asylum policies are evaluated based on equitable distribution rather than opaque statistics, thereby protecting human rights through information transparency.
