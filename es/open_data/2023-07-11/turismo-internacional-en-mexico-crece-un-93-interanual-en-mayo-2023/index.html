Turismo internacional en México crece un 9,3% interanual en mayo 2023

Mexico’s tourism sector demonstrated resilience with a notable increase in international visitors, driven primarily by border crossings. This growth stabilizes the upward trend established in previous years, highlighting the sector's vital role in the national economy and its recovery trajectory post-pandemic. However, while total visitor numbers rose, the average expenditure per tourist decreased. This divergence suggests a shift in travel patterns where higher volume does not necessarily translate to proportional revenue growth, signaling potential challenges for sustaining financial gains through quantity alone. This case is relevant to open data because official statistics from Mexico’s INEGI provide critical insights into economic trends. Transparent access to such granular tourism data enables researchers and policymakers to analyze sector dynamics, understand consumer behavior shifts, and make informed decisions based on verified, publicly available information.
