Web de transparencia - Agroinformacion

Agroinformacion.com prioritizes the protection of user privacy by explicitly committing not to share personal data with third parties. The company collects information primarily to enhance service quality, personalize user experiences, and analyze traffic through cookies. This data is used to tailor content, manage newsletters, and prevent fraud, ensuring that the platform remains secure and responsive to user needs without compromising confidentiality. Users maintain significant control over their personal information, holding the rights to access their data or request its complete deletion. While immediate erasure is possible, legal obligations require holding deleted data in a blocked state for a specific period, accessible only to judicial or regulatory authorities. This balance ensures compliance with privacy regulations while respecting the individual’s right to be forgotten once the mandatory retention period expires. This policy is relevant to open data because it defines the strict boundaries of data stewardship in the digital ecosystem. By guaranteeing that user data remains confidential and is not commodified or shared externally, it highlights the ethical contrast between commercial data usage and the principles of transparency and public utility inherent in open data initiatives. It underscores the importance of clear user consent and data minimization as foundational elements for trustworthy information handling.
