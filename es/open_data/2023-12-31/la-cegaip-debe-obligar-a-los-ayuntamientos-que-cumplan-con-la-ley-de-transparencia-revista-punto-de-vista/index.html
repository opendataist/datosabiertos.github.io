La CEGAIP debe obligar a los Ayuntamientos que cumplan con la Ley de Transparencia | Revista Punto de Vista

The legislator urges municipalities to proactively publish information online, ensuring citizens can access public data efficiently without needing to file specific requests. This shift prioritizes open availability over reactive disclosure, empowering the public to engage directly with government records. Certain state dependencies currently face the highest volume of legal reviews for failing to meet transparency deadlines. Addressing these delays is crucial for maintaining institutional accountability and ensuring that citizens receive timely responses to their inquiries. This situation highlights the critical link between legal compliance and open data practices. By mandating proactive publication, the article emphasizes that true transparency requires making data readily accessible, rather than forcing citizens to actively seek it through formal channels.
