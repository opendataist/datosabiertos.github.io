Paul McCartney pide proteger los derechos de autor ante la IA

Paul McCartney warns that changing copyright laws to train AI models on creative works threatens artists' economic viability. He argues that removing financial incentives risks a significant loss of creativity, as creators could see their work exploited without compensation. The UK government is consulting on these legal adjustments, seeking a balance between fostering the AI industry and protecting the cultural sector. McCartney urges the administration to prioritize the protection of creators, emphasizing the state’s duty to ensure artists can sustain their livelihoods. This case is crucial for open data because it highlights the tension between unrestricted data access for AI innovation and the need for ethical frameworks. It underscores that open data initiatives must respect intellectual property rights to ensure equitable benefits for original content creators, preventing the exploitation of cultural heritage by technological corporations.
