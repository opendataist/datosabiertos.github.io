The National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) has opened registration for its undergraduate admission exam, a critical milestone for thousands of aspiring students aiming to enter higher education. This process requires candidates to navigate an official digital platform to complete their application, emphasizing the necessity of digital access and online administrative systems for public university enrollment. By mandating electronic registration, the institution highlights the growing reliance on digital infrastructure to manage massive applicant volumes efficiently. Adhering to strict deadlines is essential to ensure participation and avoid technical issues caused by high traffic on registration days. The announcement serves as a guide for navigating these digital requirements, underscoring how transparent, accessible information dissemination supports equitable opportunities for applicants. Clear communication regarding procedures and timelines helps reduce barriers for students who may lack technical expertise, promoting a more inclusive admissions process. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how public institutions leverage digital platforms to provide essential services to citizens. The availability of registration data, deadlines, and procedural instructions in open, accessible formats empowers users to make informed decisions. Furthermore, the digital nature of these processes generates data that can be analyzed to improve service delivery, ensuring that public education systems remain transparent, efficient, and accountable to society.
