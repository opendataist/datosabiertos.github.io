¿Por qué Mintic otorgó Sello de Excelencia a la Gobernación del Atlántico?

The Departmental Secretariat of Health in Atlántico has received the Digital Government Excellence Seal from the Ministry of ICTs, recognizing the quality and openness of its digital public services. This certification validates the dataset detailing state social enterprise health services, demonstrating a successful integration of digital tools into administrative processes. By making this information publicly accessible, the administration aims to foster trust and efficiency in government operations, proving that technological adoption directly supports better governance. The core implication of this achievement is the enhancement of transparency and citizen participation through the strategic use of open data. By adhering to open data policies, local authorities ensure that relevant health service information is available in standard, reusable formats. This approach not only satisfies legal requirements for public access but also empowers citizens and third parties to analyze, innovate, and derive value from the information, thereby strengthening the relationship between the state and the community. This case is highly relevant to open_data as it exemplifies the practical application of national open data laws to drive administrative accountability and social utility. It illustrates how standardized, interoperable datasets can be effectively managed and disseminated, encouraging broader technological innovation and public scrutiny. Ultimately, the article highlights that robust open data practices are essential for building a transparent government that actively responds to societal needs while promoting digital inclusion and trust.
