Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Popular Party argues that the current executive has caused a significant regression in transparency, deliberately withholding critical information on public contracts, subsidies, and high-ranking officials' activities. This alleged opacity is presented as a systematic strategy to evade parliamentary scrutiny and citizen oversight, undermining democratic credibility by treating accountability as optional rather than mandatory. The core proposal urges the government to reform the Transparency Law to significantly enhance control mechanisms and impose stricter sanctions on non-compliant public bodies. A central objective is to strengthen the independent Council for Transparency and Good Government, ensuring it has sufficient resources and protection from political interference to effectively supervise compliance and enforce open governance standards. This issue is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical gap between legal frameworks and actual data availability. True open data requires proactive disclosure and enforceable accountability; without robust sanctions and independent oversight, data remains inaccessible or incomplete, preventing meaningful citizen engagement and effective democratic monitoring.
