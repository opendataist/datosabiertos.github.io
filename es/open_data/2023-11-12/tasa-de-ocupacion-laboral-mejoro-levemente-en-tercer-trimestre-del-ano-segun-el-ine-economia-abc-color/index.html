Tasa de ocupación laboral mejoró levemente en tercer trimestre del año, según el INE - Economía - ABC Color

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) reveals that Paraguay’s labor market showed resilience in the third quarter of 2023, characterized by a significant reduction in underemployment and stable unemployment rates. This data highlights a shift towards more stable job placements, particularly in the private sector, while indicating that the workforce is increasingly participating in economic activities, especially in urban areas. The narrative underscores a critical improvement in job quality, as the underemployment rate hit historic lows. This decline suggests that workers are securing sufficient hours, reducing the vulnerability associated with insufficient work time. Simultaneously, the unemployment rate remained steady, reflecting a balanced labor dynamic where new entrants are largely finding employment, though urban areas continue to host the majority of the unemployed population. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the importance of transparent, accessible, and timely public statistics for evidence-based policymaking. By making detailed labor indicators available to citizens, the INE enables researchers, economists, and civil society to analyze socioeconomic trends accurately. Such transparency fosters accountability and allows stakeholders to evaluate the effectiveness of employment policies and identify disparities between urban and rural populations.
