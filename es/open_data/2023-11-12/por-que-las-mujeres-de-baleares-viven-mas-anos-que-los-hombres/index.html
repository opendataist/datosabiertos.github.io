¿Por qué las mujeres de Baleares viven más años que los hombres?

The article attributes the gender life expectancy gap to biological and behavioral factors. Genetic differences, particularly the protective role of the XX chromosomes, and hormonal variations, including testosterone’s impact, explain higher male mortality. However, lifestyle choices like smoking and alcohol consumption significantly influence these outcomes. Women also experience higher morbidity and greater healthcare utilization, highlighting a complex health dynamic despite their longevity. This disparity is not static; converging lifestyle habits between genders suggest the life expectancy gap will narrow as women adopt riskier behaviors traditionally more common in men. This topic is relevant to open data because demographic and epidemiological datasets are essential for tracking these trends. Transparent, high-quality public health data allows researchers to validate biological hypotheses and monitor how social changes impact mortality rates, ensuring accurate global health insights.
