Senado mata nueva ley de acceso a la información pública y transparencia estatal - Política - ABC Color

The failure to sanction key parts of the Public Information Access Law effectively kills the legislation, preventing citizens from obtaining critical data on land beneficiaries, electronic transactions, and public contracts. This defeat highlights a significant setback for government transparency and citizen rights to know. The article underscores the vital role of open data in combating corruption and ensuring accountability in public spending and resource allocation. Without mandatory disclosure of such details, the government faces reduced scrutiny, undermining trust in public institutions. Relevance to open data lies in the struggle to institutionalize transparency. The rejection demonstrates how political barriers can stall essential information systems, emphasizing the need for stronger mechanisms to enforce data availability for civil society and oversight bodies.
