El gasto medio por persona en 2023 alcanzó en C-LM los 11.767 euros, 1.353 por debajo de la media nacional

The study reveals significant disparities in household expenditure across different socio-economic groups and regions in Spain. Higher-spending households prioritize transport, leisure, and hospitality, whereas lower-income families allocate the majority of their budget strictly to housing and basic sustenance. This divergence highlights how economic capacity directly influences lifestyle choices and consumption patterns, with unipersonal households under sixty-five showing the sharpest growth in spending relative to previous years. Regionally, the Basque Country leads in average spending per person, significantly surpassing the national average, while communities like Extremadura and Canary Islands lag behind. These geographic inequalities underscore the uneven distribution of economic resources and cost of living across the country. The data, now recalibrated using the 2021 Census, provides a more accurate demographic baseline, ensuring that historical comparisons reflect true population shifts rather than statistical artifacts. This article is crucial for open data because it demonstrates the necessity of updating statistical series with census data to maintain accuracy and comparability over time. By disclosing how population revisions alter expenditure metrics, it emphasizes transparency in methodology. Such rigorous data governance allows researchers and policymakers to trust the information, fostering informed decisions based on precise, openly available insights into societal well-being and economic trends.
