El Ayuntamiento publica el anuario estadístico 2023 con todos los datos sobre la ciudad de València

The Valencia City Council released its 2023 statistical yearbook, providing a detailed, district-level overview of the city’s demographics, economy, and environment. This comprehensive dataset supports granular urban analysis and tracks long-term municipal evolution across diverse sectors. Such open, structured data is vital for open data initiatives, enabling transparency, research, and data-driven decision-making for citizens and developers alike.
