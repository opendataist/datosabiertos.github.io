Impartió Bonilla conferencia en la UACH por el Open Data Day

The creation of the Chihuahua Open Data Advisory Council establishes a vital mechanism for civil society and experts to oversee government transparency. This initiative ensures that published data remains accessible and high-quality, directly strengthening public accountability and enabling informed civic participation in monitoring public resource management. By connecting transparency with technological innovation, open data becomes a powerful tool for solving complex societal challenges. The framework encourages researchers and citizens to utilize these resources for developing intelligent solutions, proving that accessible information is essential for driving both governmental efficiency and community-led innovation. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how structured public data ecosystems foster collaborative governance. It highlights the transition from passive disclosure to active engagement, where citizens and experts co-create value, ensuring transparency serves practical democratic needs rather than remaining merely a bureaucratic formality.
