IU Murcia denuncia opacidad en las Juntas Municipales - Tercera Información

This article highlights severe information obfuscation regarding municipal council management in Murcia, where transparency portals and official websites fail to publish essential meeting minutes and calls. This lack of visibility prevents citizens from verifying administrative actions and fosters an environment susceptible to clientelism. The situation is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the critical importance of maintaining up-to-date, accessible public records. Without consistent, machine-readable, and current data, the core principles of transparency and accountability are undermined, making it impossible for society to effectively monitor local governance and ensure democratic oversight.
