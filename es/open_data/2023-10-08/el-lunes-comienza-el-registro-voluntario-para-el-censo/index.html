El lunes comienza el registro voluntario para el censo

The National Institute of Statistics recruits volunteers for the census by offering academic incentives rather than financial payment. This approach encourages participation from students and teachers, ensuring broader civic engagement and workforce mobilization for the upcoming census. This initiative is relevant to open data as it relies on high-quality, citizen-driven data collection to generate comprehensive demographic information. By leveraging community involvement, the INE aims to improve data coverage and accuracy, which are essential for creating transparent, reliable public datasets that inform policy and research.
