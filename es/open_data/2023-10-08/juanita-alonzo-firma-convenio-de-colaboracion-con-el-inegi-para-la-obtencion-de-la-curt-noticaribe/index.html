Juanita Alonzo firma convenio de colaboración con el INEGI para la obtención de la CURT - Noticaribe

Cozumel has established a strategic partnership with INEGI to implement the Unified Territory Registry Key (CURT). This initiative aims to create a standardized, national database for land identification, ensuring precise geographical and statistical data similar to how citizens are tracked via CURP. By adopting this system, the municipality enhances its cadastral modernization efforts through dedicated INEGI training and support. This agreement significantly advances open data principles by committing to greater transparency in property information. By making predial data accessible to the public, the government fosters accountability and enables citizens to understand local dynamics, such as vacant lots and economic zones. This openness transforms raw geographical records into a shared public resource, promoting trust and informed community engagement. The relevance to open data lies in the shift towards standardized, accessible, and reusable public information. By integrating local data into a federal platform, Cozumel exemplifies how municipal governance can leverage transparency for better policy-making. This approach demonstrates the value of releasing precise, up-to-date geographical data, allowing both authorities and the public to utilize these insights for targeted investments and comprehensive societal understanding.
