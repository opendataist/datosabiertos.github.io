En Coahuila por su seguridad, sí se puede censar: INEGI | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The economic census in Coahuila has officially launched under confirmed safety conditions, ensuring that fieldwork can proceed securely across all municipalities. This initiative aims to gather essential statistical data on businesses and service providers to accurately measure and understand the state’s economic production dynamics. The process involves numerous interviewers visiting fixed and semi-fixed establishments to collect detailed information about business activities from the previous year. By verifying personnel identities through official credentials and hotlines, the INEGI ensures data integrity and public trust in the collection methodology. This effort is vital for open data as it generates the foundational economic indicators that drive transparency and informed decision-making. By providing comprehensive, accessible statistics on business growth and employment, the census supports researchers, policymakers, and citizens in analyzing economic trends and fostering accountability in public and private sectors.
