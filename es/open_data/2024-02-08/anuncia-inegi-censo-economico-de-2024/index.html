Anuncia INEGI Censo Económico de 2024

The 2024 Economic Census aims to map fixed economic units across Querétaro, deliberately excluding informal activities to prevent data duplication. This methodological choice highlights the persistent challenge in open data ecosystems of capturing the full scope of economic reality, as informal sectors remain visible only through complementary surveys like labor or income statistics rather than core economic censuses. By focusing on stable establishments, the census seeks to improve the accuracy and reliability of public economic indicators. This approach underscores the importance of clearly defining data scope and exclusion criteria in open datasets, ensuring that users understand what is and is not represented in official statistics. Transparency about these limitations allows for more robust analysis and prevents misinterpretation of the economic landscape. These finalized results will be published in mid-2025, contributing to the national repository of open government data. For open data advocates, this exercise demonstrates the value of standardized, georeferenced economic information for policy-making and research. It reinforces the need for accessible, well-documented official statistics that enable society to track economic trends and employment dynamics over time.
