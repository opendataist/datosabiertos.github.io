Báez (CJE): "A los jóvenes no les quedan opciones para independizarse"

Youth emancipation in Spain has seen a slight recovery, yet remains critically low due to prohibitive housing costs that exceed average salaries. This financial impossibility forces the majority of young adults to remain in their parents' homes, highlighting a systemic barrier to independent living despite minor statistical improvements. The situation is particularly acute in regions like the Canary Islands, where tourism-driven housing markets exacerbate poverty and limit opportunities. Consequently, young people face precarious conditions that make achieving autonomy nearly unattainable without significant external support or migration to larger urban centers. This report is vital for open data communities as it underscores the urgent need for transparent, accessible housing market metrics. By making such socioeconomic indicators widely available, stakeholders can better analyze the structural causes of youth poverty and advocate for policy reforms that ensure affordable housing for future generations.
