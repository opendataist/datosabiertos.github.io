El Gobierno nacional confirmó que se perdieron 115.000 puestos de trabajo privado - Diario Panorama

The Argentine labor market is undergoing a structural shift characterized by the consistent decline of high-quality registered private employment and the simultaneous rise of informal work. This trend indicates that economic recovery efforts have not sustained job creation in the formal sector, leading to a loss of secure, well-compensated positions in favor of precarious, independent labor arrangements that offer fewer protections. This data highlights the critical importance of official statistical transparency in understanding economic reality. By making detailed employment records publicly available, government agencies enable researchers and citizens to track these nuanced shifts accurately. Such accessibility allows for a deeper analysis of how macroeconomic events directly impact workers' livelihoods and the health of the formal economy. The relevance to open data lies in its capacity to foster accountability and informed public debate. When statistical information is freely accessible, it empowers stakeholders to question policy outcomes and demand evidence-based solutions. Ultimately, open government data serves as an essential tool for democratic oversight, ensuring that the true state of labor conditions is visible and subject to rigorous public scrutiny.
