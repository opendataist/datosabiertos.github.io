Veracruz, tercer lugar nacional en robos y asaltos en carreteras

Veracruz ranks among Mexico’s states with the highest highway robbery incidence, highlighting critical security challenges in transport infrastructure. Despite high robbery rates, the state recorded minimal weapon seizures, revealing a complex dynamic between crime frequency and the severity of violent confrontations during these incidents. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how public statistics enable granular analysis of safety trends, helping authorities allocate resources and address specific regional security vulnerabilities.
