Ser agente de policía es el trabajo más atractivo de EE.UU y estas son las razones

The US labor market shows weakening overall trends, yet law enforcement and healthcare sectors remain strong. Police departments face severe staffing shortages driven by retirements and low applicant volumes, forcing significant salary increases and relaxed hiring criteria to attract candidates. This situation highlights the relevance of open data in monitoring labor market dynamics. Transparent access to employment statistics and salary benchmarks enables policymakers and organizations to identify critical sector imbalances. Consequently, data-driven insights are essential for designing effective retention strategies and addressing structural workforce deficits in public safety.
