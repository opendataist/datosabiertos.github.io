Los precios del sector servicios suben un 2,4% en el primer trimestre

The service sector maintains continuous price hikes, marking nine consecutive years of increases, driven by diverse industry dynamics. Producer price indices track these quarterly shifts, highlighting specific declines in passenger air transport despite the overall upward trend. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, accessible metrics to monitor economic stability and inform policy decisions regarding service inflation.
