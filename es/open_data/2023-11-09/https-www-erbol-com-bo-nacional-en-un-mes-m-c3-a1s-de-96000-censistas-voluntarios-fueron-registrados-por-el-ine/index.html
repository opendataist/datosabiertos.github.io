Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

Bolivia is actively recruiting voluntary census takers, with over half a million registered. This surge in public participation highlights the importance of transparent data collection methods for accurate national statistics. The recruitment strategy relies heavily on educational institutions, leveraging teachers and students. This approach underscores how community engagement and institutional partnerships can facilitate robust data gathering processes essential for governance. Ultimately, achieving comprehensive census coverage ensures reliable demographic information. This relevance to open data lies in creating accessible, high-quality foundational datasets that support public analysis and informed decision-making across society.
