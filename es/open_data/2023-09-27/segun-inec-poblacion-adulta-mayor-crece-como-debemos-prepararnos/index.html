Según INEC: Población adulta mayor, crece; ¿cómo debemos prepararnos?

Ecuador faces a rapid demographic shift marked by declining birth rates and a growing elderly population. Projections indicate a sustained decrease in fertility, transforming the nation into an aging society where the working-age cohort will shrink. This structural change demands immediate societal adaptation to maintain economic stability and social support systems for a larger senior demographic. In response, institutions are promoting active aging through comprehensive programs targeting individuals from age 55, well before traditional retirement. These initiatives focus on physical, cognitive, and occupational engagement to prevent health decline and foster independence. The approach emphasizes preventive care, encouraging seniors to remain socially and economically productive rather than withdrawing from community life upon retirement. This case is vital for open_data because it illustrates how demographic statistics drive public policy and social services. Transparent access to census data enables organizations to design targeted interventions for aging populations. Understanding these trends through data allows for better resource allocation, ensuring that governments can proactively address the challenges of longevity and support sustainable development in an aging world.
