Datos Abiertos (Open data)
ES EN

The Regional Council unanimously approved a measure requiring monthly disclosure of per diems and expenses for each member. This aims to correct discrepancies between reported totals and actual payments received by councilors, addressing concerns about unexplained funds. Transparency Actives portals currently show inflated amounts that do not match individual receipts, while excluding non-reimbursable expenditures. The new routine reporting seeks to ensure accurate, permanent access to financial data for all members. This development is vital for open data as it establishes a standardized, recurring flow of specific public expenditure records. It enhances accountability by ensuring granular financial transparency, allowing citizens and officials to verify actual resource distribution versus official declarations.
