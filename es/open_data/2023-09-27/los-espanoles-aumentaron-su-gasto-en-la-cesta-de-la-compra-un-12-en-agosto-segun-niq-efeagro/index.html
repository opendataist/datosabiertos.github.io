Los españoles aumentaron su gasto en la cesta de la compra un 12 % en agosto, según NIQ - EFEAgro

The article reveals a significant divergence between sales value and volume in Spanish retail, highlighting that rising prices, rather than increased consumption, are driving revenue growth. This inflationary pressure is particularly acute in essential food items, where steep cost increases are straining household budgets despite only modest gains in actual quantity purchased. Consumer behavior shows adaptation to these economic shifts, with shoppers increasingly choosing cheaper alternatives like sunflower oil over premium olive oils to manage costs. This substitution effect demonstrates how price volatility directly influences purchasing decisions, shifting demand toward more affordable options even when overall market spending appears robust. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives as it illustrates the real-world impact of economic indicators on daily life. By analyzing granular price and volume trends, researchers can better understand consumer resilience and market dynamics. Such detailed datasets enable policymakers and economists to design more effective interventions for inflation control and to support vulnerable populations affected by essential goods price hikes.
