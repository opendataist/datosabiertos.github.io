El Indec difunde la actividad económica y ventas en supermercados, mayoristas y shoppings - Diario Panorama

The National Statistics Institute reveals declining economic activity, primarily driven by severe drought impacts on agriculture and manufacturing. Despite these sectoral contractions, retail sales in supermarkets, wholesale clubs, and shopping centers show contrasting trends, with significant nominal increases driven by inflation rather than real volume growth. This data highlights the divergence between production difficulties and consumer spending patterns amid economic volatility. The mixed results underscore the complexity of interpreting economic health when price distortions are prevalent. Understanding these nuances is vital for open data initiatives. Accurate, transparent statistics enable researchers and policymakers to distinguish between nominal and real trends, fostering better-informed decisions in a challenging economic environment.
