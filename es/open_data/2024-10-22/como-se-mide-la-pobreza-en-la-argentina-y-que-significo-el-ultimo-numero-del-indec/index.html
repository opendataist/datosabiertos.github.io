Cómo se mide la pobreza en la Argentina y qué significó el último número del Indec

The INDEC report reveals a sharp rise in poverty and destitution during the first half of 2024, highlighting the urgent need for high-quality social statistics to monitor economic shifts. This data is crucial for open data advocates, as transparent, accessible public indicators are essential for understanding the real-world impact of policy changes on vulnerable populations. Accurate open data enables civil society to demand accountability and informed decision-making, ensuring that statistical transparency serves as a tool for social justice and public scrutiny.
