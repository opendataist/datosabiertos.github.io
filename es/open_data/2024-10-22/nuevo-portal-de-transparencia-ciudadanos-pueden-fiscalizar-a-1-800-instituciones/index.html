Nuevo portal de transparencia: ciudadanos pueden fiscalizar a 1.800 instituciones

Ecuador launched its National Transparency Portal, centralizing data previously scattered across individual institutional websites. This consolidation fulfills legal mandates and provides a unified access point for citizens, significantly enhancing the availability of public information on salaries, budgets, and administrative procedures. The platform aggregates extensive datasets from thousands of public entities, managed by transparency committees and official officers. This shift simplifies the citizen experience, allowing direct access to critical governmental records without navigating multiple disparate portals. This development is vital for open data initiatives as it promotes government accountability through standardized, centralized information access. By making public records more visible and accessible, the portal empowers civil society to monitor state actions effectively, fostering a culture of transparency and reducing informational asymmetry in public administration.
