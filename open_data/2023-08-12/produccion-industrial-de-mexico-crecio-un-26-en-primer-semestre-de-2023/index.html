Producción industrial de México creció un 2,6% en primer semestre de 2023

Mexico’s industrial production showed solid growth in early 2023, driven primarily by the construction and energy sectors. This momentum contributed to a broader economic expansion, indicating a robust recovery trajectory following the pandemic-induced contraction. The data reflects a positive trend across multiple industries, including manufacturing and mining, which helped push the national GDP higher. These figures suggest that the Mexican economy is stabilizing and gaining strength after a slower initial recovery period compared to other Latin American nations. This article is relevant to open_data because it relies on statistics released by INEGI, a key provider of publicly accessible government data. Understanding how such datasets are structured, disseminated, and utilized allows researchers and citizens to track economic health transparently. It highlights the importance of official open data sources in verifying national performance and supporting informed analysis.
