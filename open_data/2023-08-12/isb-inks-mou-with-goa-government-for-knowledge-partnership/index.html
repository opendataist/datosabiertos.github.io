ISB inks MoU with Goa government for knowledge partnership

The Bharti Institute’s new partnership with Goa prioritizes evidence-based governance, aiming to enhance social and economic prosperity through citizen-centric initiatives and capacity building for state officials. This collaboration seeks to improve public administration efficiency and legislative support, ensuring policies are grounded in robust data and expert analysis rather than intuition. A critical component of this alliance is the development of the Goa Open Data Portal. By creating a centralized hub for digital services, the initiative significantly advances open data transparency, allowing citizens direct access to government information and fostering greater accountability in public service delivery. Furthermore, the partnership targets specific sectors like education and bioenergy, leveraging data to guide investment and community empowerment. This approach demonstrates how open data and policy research can drive tangible improvements in governance, serving as a model for other Indian states seeking to modernize their administrative frameworks through transparency and informed decision-making.
