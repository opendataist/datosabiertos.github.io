Media organizations are urgently demanding strict regulations to protect copyright in generative AI training data. They argue that unchecked AI use threatens the financial stability of creators and erodes public trust in content quality. Without intervention, the spread of synthetic material risks distorting facts and confusing audiences about truth. The petition calls for mandatory transparency regarding training sources, explicit consent for intellectual property usage, and negotiation rights for media companies. Additionally, stakeholders want bias reduction, misinformation mitigation, and clear labeling of AI-generated content. These measures aim to ensure ethical AI development while preserving the integrity of the media ecosystem. This issue is critical for open data because it challenges the current norm of unrestricted data scraping for model training. It highlights the tension between open access and intellectual property rights, forcing a reevaluation of how publicly available data can be legally and ethically utilized in commercial AI systems without compensating or consulting original creators.
