Zoom can now train its AI using some customer data

Zoom’s updated terms permit using customer data to train AI models, highlighting the tension between technological innovation and user privacy. This policy shift underscores the critical need for transparent ethical boundaries in how tech companies handle sensitive information. The decision reflects a broader industry struggle to balance advancement with maintaining user trust. It emphasizes that consumers must remain vigilant and informed about their digital rights as personal data becomes a foundational asset for developing future technologies. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the complex legal and ethical landscape surrounding data usage. As algorithms increasingly rely on diverse inputs, understanding the consent mechanisms and restrictions governing such data is essential for ensuring responsible and open data practices within modern AI ecosystems.
