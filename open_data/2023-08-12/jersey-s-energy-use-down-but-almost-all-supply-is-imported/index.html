Jersey's energy use down but almost all supply is imported

Jersey’s energy consumption per capita fell in 2022, remaining significantly lower than the UK average. This trend highlights opportunities for improving energy efficiency and reducing reliance on imported fossil fuels, which dominate the island’s supply. Although local renewable sources exist, nearly all energy is imported. The data underscores the urgency of transitioning away from petroleum to mitigate environmental impacts and enhance energy security for the jurisdiction. This report is vital for open_data initiatives as it provides transparent, comparable metrics. Open access to such statistics enables researchers and policymakers to analyze trends, drive evidence-based climate policies, and promote accountability in public energy management.
