The Guanajuato government initiated a participatory dialogue with a citizen coalition to address the municipal landfill fire, signaling a shift toward open governance and direct societal involvement in waste management remediation. The session highlighted failures in transparency, as officials faced intense criticism for withholding information about a significant damages claim and for failing to provide adequate documentation in a timely manner. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for proactive, accessible public information. It demonstrates how opacity erodes trust and how open data principles can foster accountability and collaborative problem-solving between authorities and civil society.
