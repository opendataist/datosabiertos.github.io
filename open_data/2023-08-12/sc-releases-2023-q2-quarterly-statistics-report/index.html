SC Releases 2023 Q2 Quarterly Statistics Report

Standardbred Canada’s latest quarterly report highlights significant regional shifts in racing metrics, notably substantial increases in wagering handle and average field sizes across key provinces. These trends indicate growing spectator engagement and market vitality in Atlantic and Quebec regions, despite fluctuations in purse money driven by varying race counts. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives, as it provides transparent, standardized benchmarks for analyzing the economic health and operational consistency of national sporting events.
