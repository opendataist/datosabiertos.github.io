IBM y la NASA crean un modelo fundacional de IA geoespacial - INVDES

IBM and Hugging Face, in collaboration with NASA, have launched the first open-source geospatial foundational model. This initiative aims to democratize access to advanced technologies to address the challenges of climate data analysis, which are growing exponentially and surpassing the capabilities of traditional processing methods. By providing accessible tools, the project seeks to drive scientific innovation and improve understanding of global environmental changes without the usual barriers of cost or technical complexity. Trained on high-quality satellite data, the model has demonstrated superior efficiency compared to existing techniques, requiring fewer labeled data points to achieve significant improvements in tasks such as flood mapping and fire detection. Its modular architecture enables reuse across a variety of critical applications, from tracking deforestation to predicting agricultural yields and monitoring greenhouse gas emissions. This highlights the ability of foundational models to quickly adapt to different scientific contexts. This project is pivotal for the open data movement, as it transforms massive volumes of public information into practical, shareable tools. By releasing the model under open licenses, it fosters a more inclusive and collaborative scientific community, where researchers worldwide can build upon common foundations. This not only accelerates scientific discovery but also sets a precedent for how transparency in AI development can expedite solutions to urgent planetary crises.
