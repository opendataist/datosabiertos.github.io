Los Aeropuertos de Neuquén y Chapelco están volando hacia el mejor año de su historia | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

The article highlights a significant surge in passenger traffic at Neuquén’s airports, with early 2023 figures poised to exceed the historic 2019 record. This growth is attributed to strategic management decisions, including expanding airline partnerships and increasing flight frequencies to meet rising tourist demand driven by winter sports. These improvements demonstrate how effective administrative policies and infrastructure adjustments can successfully stimulate regional tourism and economic activity. The success underscores the importance of responsive service provision in capitalizing on natural assets, ensuring that logistical capabilities keep pace with market expectations. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates the value of public accessibility to civil aviation statistics. Transparent reporting by regulatory bodies like ANAC allows stakeholders to track performance, validate policy effectiveness, and foster accountability in managing public transportation resources and economic indicators.
