Culminó el escrutinio definitivo y Banfi dijo que no hubo "grandes cambios" - El Chubut

The definitive election tally in Chubut has been completed, marking a peaceful process with minimal deviations from the provisional results. This conclusion confirms the reliability of the voting procedure, despite minor administrative irregularities, ensuring the integrity of the democratic outcome. The final step involves integrating all ballots, including those from foreign residents and detainees, into the official database. This data will be processed using proportional systems to determine legislative seats, guaranteeing an accurate representation of the electorate’s will at both provincial and municipal levels. The relevance to open data lies in the subsequent publication of these comprehensive results on an official government portal. By making granular election data accessible and transparent, the state fosters accountability and allows citizens to independently verify outcomes, strengthening trust through data-driven governance.
