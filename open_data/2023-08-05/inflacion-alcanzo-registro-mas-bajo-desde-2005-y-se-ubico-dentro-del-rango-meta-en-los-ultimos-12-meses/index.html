Inflación alcanzó registro más bajo desde 2005 y se ubicó dentro del rango meta en los últimos 12 meses

Inflación alcanzó registro más bajo desde 2005 y se ubicó dentro del rango meta en los últimos 12 meses

The July 2023 consumer price index in Uruguay reflects a significant downward trend, marking the lowest inflation level since 2005. This data confirms that the country is successfully maintaining inflation within the central bank’s target range, validating recent contractionary monetary policies. The central bank president highlighted this achievement as a stabilization milestone, suggesting that economic agents' expectations are aligning with current realities and providing room for potential policy adjustments. Breaking down the categories, the decline was primarily driven by substantial price reductions in essential goods such as certain foods, vegetables, fruits, and transportation services. These specific sectoral decreases offset increases in other areas like clothing and housing rentals, resulting in an overall moderated price environment. This distribution indicates that the cooling effect is broad-based across various consumption baskets, reinforcing the robustness of the disinflation process. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of transparent, high-quality statistical information in guiding monetary policy and public trust. The INE’s detailed, granular dataset allows policymakers and analysts to identify specific drivers of inflation, enabling precise interventions. Furthermore, public access to such timely and accurate metrics fosters accountability and helps stakeholders anticipate economic shifts, demonstrating how open government data serves as a foundational tool for effective governance and economic stability.
