Lawyer asks JAMB to release details of top 10 candidates in 2024 UTME

Chukwuma Nwachukwu has utilized Nigeria’s Freedom of Information Act to challenge the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board’s refusal to release the names and scores of the top ten 2024 UTME candidates. This legal action underscores the critical role of transparency in public education data, asserting that withholding specific performance metrics contradicts the principle that citizens have an unconditional right to access public information without needing to demonstrate a specific interest. The dispute highlights significant tensions between institutional secrecy and the public’s right to know, particularly given that JAMB already disclosed broader statistical breakdowns of candidate performance. By invoking statutory provisions that mandate a seven-day response time, the lawyer emphasizes that public agencies cannot arbitrarily classify essential educational records as confidential when such information relates to national examination outcomes. This case is highly relevant to open data because it validates the legal enforceability of information access laws in demanding granular dataset publication. It sets a precedent for treating detailed examination results as open data rather than restricted assets, thereby promoting accountability in public institutions and encouraging the systematic release of high-value societal data for public scrutiny and analysis.
