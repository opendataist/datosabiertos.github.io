NIH defies FOIA, Moderna vaxx royalties paid to government will remain state secret – NaturalNews.com

This article argues that government institutions are actively concealing critical information regarding public health policies and financial agreements. It highlights the refusal of the National Institutes of Health to disclose details about a substantial vaccine agreement, citing legal exemptions related to commercial confidentiality. The author contends that this lack of transparency undermines democratic principles and suggests a broader pattern of elite secrecy that prioritizes protection of interests over public accountability. The narrative emphasizes the tension between open governance and the use of bureaucratic mechanisms to withhold data. By invoking historical rhetoric against secret societies, the piece frames the current opacity not as a procedural necessity but as an intentional abuse of power. It suggests that such actions represent a systemic failure where citizens are denied the factual basis needed to make informed decisions about their own health and society. This content is relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the challenges researchers and activists face when trying to access public records. It underscores the importance of robust Freedom of Information Act frameworks and the ongoing struggle to ensure that government-held data remains accessible. The case exemplifies how commercial exemptions can be used to block transparency, making it a critical example of the barriers to true data openness in public health contexts.
