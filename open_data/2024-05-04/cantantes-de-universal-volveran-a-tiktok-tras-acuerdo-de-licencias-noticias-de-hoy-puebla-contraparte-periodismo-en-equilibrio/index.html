Cantantes de Universal volverán a TikTok tras acuerdo de licencias - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The recent agreement between Universal Music Group (UMG) and TikTok restores UMG’s music catalog on the platform and establishes a strategic collaboration to protect artists from unauthorized use of artificial intelligence. This pact resolves the copyright dispute, ensuring that UMG’s music becomes available again while tools are developed to remove unauthorized AI-generated content and improve attribution for creators. The relevance of this agreement for the field of open data lies in its direct implications for the governance of intellectual property data in digital environments. By establishing new protocols for the identification and attribution of artistic works, the agreement reinforces the need for transparent mechanisms that distinguish between human-created and synthetic content. This is crucial for maintaining data integrity on massive platforms, where mixed authorship can compromise the accuracy of consumption metrics and the legal protection of rights holders. Furthermore, the negotiation highlights market power within the creative industry and the struggle for fair compensation. TikTok commits to collaborating with UMG to ensure that technological development does not replace human creativity or deny adequate remuneration. This precedent is significant for the open data community, as it marks a turning point in how major platforms must integrate transparency and ethics into the handling of sensitive data related to intellectual property and artificial intelligence.
