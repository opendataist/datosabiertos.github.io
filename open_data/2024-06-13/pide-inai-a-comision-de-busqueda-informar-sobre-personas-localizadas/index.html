Pide INAI a Comisión de Búsqueda informar sobre personas localizadas

Pide INAI a Comisión de Búsqueda informar sobre personas localizadas

The INAI ruled that Mexico’s National Search Commission must transparently disclose specific data on the 20,000 reported found individuals, rejecting the Commission’s initial claim that the information was already public. This decision addresses significant discrepancies between official statistics and the incomplete, inconsistent data currently available online, ensuring the public receives accurate details. This ruling is crucial for open data because it compels government bodies to provide complete, verifiable datasets rather than partial or conflicting information. By mandating the release of unique search folios and locations, it establishes a precedent for accountability in humanitarian data, demonstrating that open access must be substantive to combat irregularities and uphold citizens’ right to truth regarding missing persons.
