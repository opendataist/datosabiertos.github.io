PCM designa a dos funcionarios para seleccionar al nuevo jefe del INEI

The Peruvian government appoints officials to select the new head of the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics. This leadership transition directly impacts the governance of critical national data systems. The process ensures administrative continuity and strategic direction for statistical operations. Effective oversight is essential for maintaining data integrity and public trust. This appointment is relevant to open data as it influences transparency and accessibility standards. Strong leadership drives better implementation of open data initiatives in government.
