Comisionado de Transparencia de CyL insta al Ayuntamiento de Aldeamayor a dar datos sobre análisis del agua desde 2019

The Transparency Commissioner ordered the Aldeamayor de San Martín town hall to release water quality analysis data, resolving a complaint filed by the Centristas group. This decision highlights the critical role of open data in ensuring that public institutions comply with transparency laws and provide essential information to citizens. The request stemmed from residents’ health concerns about discolored water, illustrating how open access to municipal data empowers communities to verify safety standards. By mandating disclosure, the ruling reinforces the right to information, which is crucial for maintaining public trust and accountability in local governance, particularly concerning public health issues.
