Sonora has stood out for its progress in open government and regulatory compliance, positioning itself favorably in the state rule-of-law index. This success reflects a preventive strategy centered on institutional integrity and the effective use of controls, marking a significant shift from previous positions. The implementation of open data portals and risk control systems has been crucial to this transformation. By prioritizing transparency and the efficient enforcement of regulations, the entity has succeeded in reducing corruption in administrative procedures and improving its reputation among international organizations, demonstrating that technology strengthens accountability. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how the transparent publication of government information serves as a fundamental tool for administrative integrity. Open data enables citizens to monitor public management, fostering a culture of accountability that is essential for building a solid and trustworthy rule of law.
