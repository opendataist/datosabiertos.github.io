La economía catalana podría sufrir una desaceleración por los conflictos geopolíticos internacionales

The conference highlights that the global economic landscape is shifting from a predictable trajectory to a volatile one, characterized by geopolitical tensions and persistent inflation. This uncertainty is driving an increase in insolvencies, albeit at a slower pace than in previous years. Consequently, businesses must remain vigilant regarding geopolitical and economic developments to make informed decisions, as the current environment demands a proactive approach to risk management rather than a passive stance. Global growth is expected to slow significantly, with emerging economies in the Global South becoming the primary engines of expansion, accounting for the majority of this growth. While traditional powers such as the US, China, and Europe face specific headwinds, countries in Southeast Asia and Latin America are positioned to thrive through active economic diplomacy and export strategies. This shift underscores the importance of understanding regional dynamics, as success in international markets now depends heavily on integrating into local contexts and leveraging these emerging hubs. For companies aiming to grow or expand internationally, adapting to this fragmented economic reality is crucial. The emphasis is on scalability and access to financing for domestic growth, while cross-border expansion requires a deep understanding of local consumer behavior and market mentality. This narrative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible, transparent information on geopolitical trends, economic indicators, and market behaviors empowers decision-makers to navigate uncertainty. Open data facilitates the real-time monitoring and analysis necessary to identify these shifting opportunities and mitigate risks in a complex global economy.
