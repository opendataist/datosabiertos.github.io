Profundizó febrero la caída del empleo en México

Profundizó febrero la caída del empleo en México

Mexico’s February 2025 labor data reveal a decline in the economically active population and a persistently high rate of informal employment. This underscores the challenge of transitioning workers from the informal to the formal sector, a critical area for open data initiatives aimed at improving labor market transparency. Accessible open datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze these employment trends more effectively. By providing clear insights into unemployment and informality, such data supports informed decision-making and helps address structural labor market issues. Ultimately, this information highlights the need for robust, transparent statistics to guide social policy. Open data fosters accountability and allows society to better understand the complexities of the labor force, driving improvements in economic conditions and worker protections.
