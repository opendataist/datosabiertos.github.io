RTIC Demands Answers From FIC Over Withheld Tax Information - The Ghanaian Chronicle

RTIC Demands Answers From FIC Over Withheld Tax Information - The Ghanaian Chronicle

The Right to Information Commission is compelling the Financial Intelligence Centre to justify its refusal to disclose details on alleged money laundering and tax evasion cases. This enforcement underscores the critical role of transparency laws in holding powerful institutions accountable for withholding sensitive data from the public. Concurrently, the Ghana Revenue Authority has shared audit results and penalty records, demonstrating how proactive data release can reveal significant fiscal irregularities and enforcement actions. This contrast highlights the disparity between institutions that embrace open data and those that resist, affecting public trust and oversight mechanisms. This case is vital to open data advocacy because it tests legal frameworks ensuring public access to information. It illustrates the ongoing struggle to balance institutional secrecy with the fundamental right to know, emphasizing the need for strict compliance with transparency mandates to foster accountability in governance and financial regulation.
