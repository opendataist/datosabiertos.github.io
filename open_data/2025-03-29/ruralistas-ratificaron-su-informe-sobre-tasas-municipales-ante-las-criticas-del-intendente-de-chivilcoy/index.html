Ruralistas ratificaron su informe sobre tasas municipales ante las críticas del intendente de Chivilcoy

Ruralistas ratificaron su informe sobre tasas municipales ante las críticas del intendente de Chivilcoy

The Confederation of Rural Associations of Buenos Aires and La Pampa (CARBAP) reaffirmed the accuracy of its report on municipal tax rates, refuting the claims of error made by the mayor of Chivilcoy. The organization clarified that its survey is based strictly on the nominal values listed on official tax bills, without assessing the efficiency of public spending or the quality of services. This distinction is crucial, as the aim is to expose the real fiscal burden faced by producers, leaving value judgments and oversight of municipal management to local organizations. The conflict arises from a significant divergence between the data reported by CARBAP and those claimed by the municipal administration, which argues for considerably lower values and early-payment benefits not considered in the initial study. CARBAP supports its position by presenting payment receipts that reflect much higher annual amounts per hectare, and even indicates that recent installments further increase this economic pressure. This dispute illustrates how the lack of unified and standardized access to municipal tax information generates confusion and conflicting interpretations between the productive sector and local authorities. This episode is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it highlights the critical need to standardize, digitize, and make local government fiscal data accessible. When information relevant to the real economy resides in unstructured documents or in publicly unverifiable discrepancies, accountability and sector planning become difficult. Effective transparency requires that amounts, tax rates, and regulations be available in open and comparable formats, enabling citizens and economic actors to validate information without relying exclusively on official statements or subjective interpretations of individual receipts.
