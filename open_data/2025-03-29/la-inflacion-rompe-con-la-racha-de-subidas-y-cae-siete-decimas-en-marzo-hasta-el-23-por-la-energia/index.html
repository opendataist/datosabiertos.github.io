The article reports a decline in Spanish inflation, driven primarily by reduced energy and fuel costs. This shift suggests easing price pressures and marks a significant moderation in underlying inflation, reaching its lowest levels since late 2021. These trends indicate a stabilization of consumer prices, largely influenced by weather-induced drops in electricity rates. The harmonized index also reflects this downward trajectory, aligning domestic metrics with broader European economic patterns.
