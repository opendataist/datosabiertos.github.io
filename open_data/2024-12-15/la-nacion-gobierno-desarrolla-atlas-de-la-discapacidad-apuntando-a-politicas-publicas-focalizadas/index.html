La Nación / Gobierno desarrolla Atlas de la discapacidad apuntando a políticas públicas focalizadas

The National Institute of Statistics has launched Paraguay’s first Disability Atlas, a unified digital platform that integrates standardized data from government agencies and civil society organizations. By consolidating previously fragmented information, this initiative aims to provide a comprehensive and accurate overview of the disability population, marking a significant shift toward more inclusive and coordinated public administration. This centralized resource serves as a vital tool for designing evidence-based public policies and optimizing resource allocation. It enables authorities to precisely identify service needs and tailor interventions, thereby improving the efficiency of both state agencies and non-governmental organizations working toward social inclusion and equity. The project is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized, and accessible information can drive societal change. By making aggregated statistics publicly available, the atlas promotes accountability in public spending and encourages informed decision-making, setting a precedent for using open datasets to effectively address complex social issues.
