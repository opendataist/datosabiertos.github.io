Experto ve la migración como “factor clave” en la desaceleración poblacional

Experto ve la migración como “factor clave” en la desaceleración poblacional

The census reveals that emigration is the primary driver of Bolivia’s population slowdown, outweighing demographic shifts such as birth rates. This significant loss of human capital underscores the critical need for robust population data management. Open data is essential to accurately track these migration flows and understand their socioeconomic impact. Transparent statistics enable policymakers to effectively address brain drain, ensuring that demographic insights inform strategies for retaining talent and fostering sustainable development.
