Cuáles son los municipios bonaerenses con peor Índice de Transparencia sobre su situación financiera

Seventeen municipalities failed to disclose current financial data or budgets, demonstrating a total lack of transparency. This failure violates legal obligations to inform citizens about public spending. The issue highlights the critical need for open data initiatives to ensure government accountability and accessible public information.
