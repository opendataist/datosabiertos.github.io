Uno por uno, cuáles son los 40 municipios con mayor transparencia fiscal de la Provincia de Buenos Aires

Forty municipalities in the province of Buenos Aires lead the fiscal transparency ranking by fully publishing their public accounts with high clarity and accessibility. This performance demonstrates how regulatory compliance translates into open financial management, facilitating citizen scrutiny of budgets, quarterly executions, and debts. The relevance for open data lies in the fact that these data are available digitally, structured, and updated, meeting the legal requirements for web publication. The ASAP initiative validates that technical accessibility is as crucial as legal availability, establishing a quality standard for municipal accountability. This approach promotes a culture of proactive transparency, where information not only exists but is easily usable by society. By prioritizing clarity and direct access, institutional trust is strengthened and citizens are empowered to monitor the use of public resources, a fundamental pillar of open governance.
