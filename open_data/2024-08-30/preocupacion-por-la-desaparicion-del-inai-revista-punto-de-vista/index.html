Preocupación por la Desaparición del Inai | Revista Punto de Vista

Preocupación por la Desaparición del Inai | Revista Punto de Vista

The Mexican Bar Association warns that eliminating the INAI severely undermines democratic safeguards by centralizing transparency and data protection within the executive branch. This consolidation of power compromises institutional impartiality, erodes the right to information, and violates international standards for autonomous oversight bodies essential for holding government accountable. This shift is particularly critical for open data frameworks, as it jeopardizes the independent mechanisms required to ensure public access to information and the secure handling of personal data. Without an autonomous regulator, the integrity of public data ecosystems and citizen privacy protections are significantly weakened, threatening the foundational principles of accountability and transparency in the digital age.
