Censo: Bolivia tiene 11,3 millones de habitantes; Santa Cruz, 3,1; La Paz, 3 y Cochabamba, 2

Censo: Bolivia tiene 11,3 millones de habitantes; Santa Cruz, 3,1; La Paz, 3 y Cochabamba, 2

The preliminary census results highlight significant demographic shifts, with Santa Cruz emerging as the most populated and fastest-growing department. This data provides crucial insights into national migration patterns and urbanization trends, revealing where resources and political representation must be allocated to serve the largest populations effectively. The relevance to open data lies in the INE’s decision to publish these findings via a public QR code, ensuring complete traceability and accessibility for citizens and authorities. By leveraging satellite imagery and digital georeferencing, the institute demonstrates how modern, transparent data infrastructure can replace outdated manual methods, fostering trust through verifiable, up-to-date technological standards. This approach marks a historic shift toward fully digital cartography and 100% coverage, setting a new benchmark for statistical transparency. The availability of open, traceable data allows for better public oversight and informed decision-making, proving that accessible technology is essential for credible and inclusive national governance.
