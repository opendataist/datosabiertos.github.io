Northern Ireland Annual Tourism Statistics

NISRA has resumed publishing regular tourism statistics for Northern Ireland after a pandemic-induced hiatus, establishing a new 2023 baseline with a revised methodology. This significant shift in data collection ensures the integrity of future longitudinal studies, marking a critical step in restoring continuity for national economic indicators. The new baseline serves as a crucial reference point for subsequent years, allowing researchers to accurately track trends without the distortion caused by previous emergency statistical alternatives. The 2023 data reveals substantial economic activity, with millions of overnight trips generating significant expenditure. Notably, domestic travel accounted for a large portion of this market, underscoring its resilience and importance alongside external tourism. These findings highlight the diverse nature of the tourism sector and provide essential insights into consumer behavior and regional economic contributions, offering a comprehensive view of the industry’s recovery and current state. This release is vital for open data initiatives as it restores access to high-quality, standardized metrics. By providing detailed breakdowns of trip volumes, spending, and business distributions, the dataset supports transparent analysis and evidence-based policy-making. Open access to this revised baseline empowers stakeholders to understand regional disparities and economic impacts, fostering informed decision-making and promoting accountability in the tourism sector’s ongoing development and evaluation.
