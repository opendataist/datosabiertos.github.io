Constituyen asociación de profesionales de protección de datos que busca ser un “referente técnico” | Diario Financiero

Constituyen asociación de profesionales de protección de datos que busca ser un “referente técnico” | Diario Financiero

Chile establishes the first Association of Data Protection Professionals to navigate the challenges posed by newly enacted data privacy laws. This organization aims to foster a culture of respect for personal data rights, bridging the gap between legal frameworks and professional practice while raising national standards to align with European benchmarks. The association seeks to become a technical reference point beyond the regulatory agency, promoting the study and improvement of legislation in areas such as privacy, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence. By uniting diverse experts, it strives to influence policy and ensure the practical application of robust data protection principles across various sectors. This development is relevant to open data because it creates a structured professional ecosystem dedicated to balancing transparency with privacy. As open data initiatives expand, such associations play a crucial role in establishing ethical guidelines and technical best practices, ensuring that data utilization respects individual rights and maintains public trust in digital governance.
