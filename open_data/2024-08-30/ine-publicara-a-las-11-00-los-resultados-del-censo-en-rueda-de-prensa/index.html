INE publicará a las 11:00 los resultados del censo en rueda de prensa

The National Statistics Institute has accelerated the publication of preliminary census population results, bringing forward the originally scheduled release date. This early publication ensures that subnational authorities can access the data simultaneously during the meeting of the National Council of Autonomies. Such transparency is crucial for open data, as it provides timely and reliable demographic information that is essential for effective governance and public accountability.
