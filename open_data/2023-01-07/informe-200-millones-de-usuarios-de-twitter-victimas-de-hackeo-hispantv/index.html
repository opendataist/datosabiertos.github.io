Informe: 200 millones de usuarios de Twitter víctimas de hackeo | HISPANTV

The massive theft of Twitter emails threatens to increase phishing and doxxing, highlighting the vulnerabilities of major platforms. The company’s lack of an immediate response and the uncertainty surrounding the origin of the breach expose critical failures in personal data security. This incident is crucial for the field of open data, as it illustrates the direct impact of poor information management on user privacy. By putting hundreds of millions of people at risk, it demonstrates how corporate opacity and negligence in data protection facilitate malicious exploitation, undermining the trust necessary to share or use public datasets in an ethical and secure manner.
