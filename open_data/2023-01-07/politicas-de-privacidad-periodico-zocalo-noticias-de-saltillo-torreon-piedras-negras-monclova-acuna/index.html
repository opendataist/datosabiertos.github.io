Políticas de privacidad | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Grupo Zócalo prioritizes the security of personal data and compliance with privacy regulations, adhering to national protection laws and the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. They collect information only with explicit consent and protect non-identifiable statistics shared with partners. This practice is relevant to open data because it illustrates how digital media balances transparency in data processing with individual rights. By utilizing standardized consent frameworks, the outlet demonstrates the technical infrastructure necessary for ethical data handling in an era that demands both openness and privacy protection.
