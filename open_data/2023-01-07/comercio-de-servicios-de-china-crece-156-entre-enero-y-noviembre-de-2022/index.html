Comercio de servicios de China crece 15,6 % entre enero y noviembre de 2022

China’s service trade expanded significantly, driven by strong growth in knowledge-intensive sectors such as intellectual property and computing. This surge underscores a structural shift toward high-value digital and professional services, moving beyond the recovery of traditional sectors like travel and logistics. The data reveal a growing emphasis on intangible assets and innovation-driven exports, reducing reliance on lower-margin sectors. This trend indicates a maturing economy in which service quality and technological integration serve as key competitive advantages in global markets. This report is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accessible, standardized trade statistics for tracking economic transformation. Transparent data enables researchers and policymakers to analyze sectoral shifts and design evidence-based strategies for the sustainable development of digital trade.
