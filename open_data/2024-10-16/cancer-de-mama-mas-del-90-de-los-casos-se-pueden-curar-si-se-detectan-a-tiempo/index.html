Cáncer de mama: más del 90% de los casos se pueden curar si se detectan a tiempo

Breast cancer remains a predominant health condition in Argentina, but mortality has steadily declined thanks to therapeutic advances. However, these scientific progress crucially depend on prevention and early detection, as early diagnosis significantly improves cure rates. The responsibility is not solely medical; it requires society to adopt healthy habits and undergo regular check-ups to complement clinical treatment. Reducing modifiable risk factors, such as sedentary lifestyle, overweight, alcohol consumption, and smoking, is essential to prevent the disease or improve the response to treatments. Although there are tools for self-awareness of the body, these do not replace mammography or specialized medical examinations. Health education should begin early, emphasizing that no group is exempt from risk and that prevention is as vital as cure. This article is relevant to the open_data community because it illustrates how transparency in public health data allows tracking epidemiological trends and evaluating the effectiveness of health policies. The visibility of mortality figures and professional certification generate public trust, demonstrating that access to accurate and standardized information empowers citizens to make informed decisions about their well-being and demands accountability from medical institutions.
