Here's a list of September inflation rates for selected Canadian cities

This report highlights the challenges in accessing granular local inflation data, as Statistics Canada notes significant volatility in city-level figures due to small sample sizes. For open data advocates, this underscores the critical need for transparent methodology and adequate sampling to ensure reliable, usable public statistics. Without robust data quality standards, detailed datasets risk misleading policymakers and citizens.
