La capacidad industrial se recupera pero no alcanza los niveles de 2023

The use of Argentina’s industrial capacity shows a positive monthly trend, reaching its highest level under the current administration, although it remains below the records of the previous year. This dynamic reflects a gradual but incomplete recovery of the national productive sector. Petroleum refining stands out as the only sector that has improved its year-on-year figures, leading overall activity. In contrast, the rubber and plastics industry maintains the lowest indices, highlighting significant structural disparities among different subsectors of the economy. This report is relevant to open data as it exemplifies how official statistics should be critically analyzed. By contrasting year-on-year metrics with monthly trends, it demonstrates the importance of not interpreting isolated data points. Transparency in complex economic indicators enables a better understanding of sectoral realities and supports informed decision-making based on the full context.
