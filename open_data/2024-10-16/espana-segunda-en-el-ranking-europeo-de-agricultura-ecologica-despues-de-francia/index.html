España, segunda en el ránking europeo de agricultura ecológica después de Francia

Spain is poised to become the European leader in organic agricultural land area, potentially surpassing France. This growth reflects a significant expansion of land dedicated to ecological production, driven by substantial increases in the cultivation of key crops such as olives, cereals, and nuts. The data highlight a robust structural shift toward sustainable farming practices across major Spanish regions. The sector has also experienced a notable rise in the number of operators and a diversification of industrial activities, indicating a maturing and diversified supply chain. From oil processing to animal feed, the variety of organic products underscores the industry’s economic resilience and its capacity to integrate into broader market systems. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of standardized, comparable statistics from sources such as Eurostat. Transparent, official data are essential for tracking these trends, supporting policy decisions, and addressing the gap between high production and low domestic consumption. Reliable metrics empower stakeholders to understand market dynamics and drive further adoption of sustainable practices.
