The federal government is likely to receive a record number of FOIA requests again in 2024

The federal government is facing a crisis of transparency capacity, driven by a surge in Freedom of Information Act requests that have reached historic highs. This exponential growth reflects a public eager for accountability, but it has overwhelmed agencies that lack the staffing and technological infrastructure to manage the influx. The resulting backlogs not only delay responses but also trigger costly litigation, straining public resources and undermining the efficient delivery of government information to citizens. To address this challenge, agencies are increasingly turning to artificial intelligence as a critical solution for handling complex data volumes. AI-driven tools can automate the tedious processes of document review, categorization, and redaction, significantly accelerating response times while safeguarding sensitive information. By leveraging machine learning and natural language processing, governments can transform their ability to process requests, turning a manageable burden into an opportunity for more responsive and accurate data handling. This shift is vital for the open data movement because it demonstrates how modern technology can bridge the gap between legal mandates for transparency and practical operational reality. As AI enables agencies to process larger volumes of information more efficiently, it paves the way for a more robust culture of open government. Ultimately, adopting these advanced tools ensures that the increasing public demand for accessible information can be met sustainably, fostering greater trust and engagement between the state and its citizens.
