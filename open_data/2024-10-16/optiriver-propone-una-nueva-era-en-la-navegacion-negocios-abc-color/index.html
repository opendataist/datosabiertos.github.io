OptiRiver propone una nueva era en la navegación - Negocios - ABC Color

OptiRiver is a technological platform that enables vessels to perform real-time depth soundings and share this data with the boating community. By leveraging affordable sensors and artificial intelligence, the tool generates an up-to-date situational awareness that surpasses the static and bureaucratic information found in official bulletins. This ensures safer and more efficient navigation, minimizing accident risks and allowing for greater utilization of ships' cargo capacity. The relevance of this solution within the open data context lies in its community-driven approach and the democratization of access to critical hydrographic information. By integrating practical knowledge from industry professionals with low-cost technology, it creates a dynamic and open data ecosystem. This transparency not only optimizes fluvial logistics but also offers a viable alternative to the lack of resources for official maintenance of waterways, as currently seen on the Paraná River due to drought conditions. In addition to direct economic benefits from avoiding cargo losses, the platform promotes environmental sustainability by encouraging the use of river transport, which is less polluting than road or rail modes. Its rapid implementation and validation demonstrate that collaboration between private stakeholders and the availability of real-time data are key to modernizing logistics and improving the competitiveness of agricultural exports in regions affected by severe climate changes.
