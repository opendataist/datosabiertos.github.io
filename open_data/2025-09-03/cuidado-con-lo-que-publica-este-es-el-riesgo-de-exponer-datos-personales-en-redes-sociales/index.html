Cuidado con lo que publica: este es el riesgo de exponer datos personales en redes sociales

Cuidado con lo que publica: este es el riesgo de exponer datos personales en redes sociales

The article highlights the escalation of digital extortion, focusing on the “ransomware as a service” model and the use of double extortion. This method not only encrypts data to demand a ransom but also threatens to publicly disclose stolen information. Criminal groups such as Medusa employ advanced tools to bypass security systems, delete backups, and manipulate logs, drastically complicating the recovery of compromised data in critical sectors. This situation is relevant to open data because it illustrates the inherent risks associated with exposing sensitive information. The attackers’ ability to extract data and threaten to leak it underscores the critical need to protect the integrity and privacy of both open and corporate datasets. Transparency, if not properly managed, can become an attack vector that paralyzes essential services and undermines public trust in institutions that handle information. To mitigate these risks, the article emphasizes the implementation of robust cybersecurity protocols, such as two-factor authentication and up-to-date system maintenance. Additionally, training employees and users to identify threats is essential. The main conclusion is that prevention and rapid incident response are vital to protecting both individual data and collective digital infrastructure against the growing sophistication of organized crime.
