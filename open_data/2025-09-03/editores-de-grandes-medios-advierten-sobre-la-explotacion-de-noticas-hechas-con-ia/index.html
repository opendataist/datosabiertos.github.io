Editores de grandes medios advierten sobre la “explotación” de noticas hechas con IA

Editores de grandes medios advierten sobre la “explotación” de noticas hechas con IA

The use of artificial intelligence to extract and summarize journalistic content without authorization or compensation threatens the economic viability of media outlets. By replacing direct access to original sources, these platforms capture advertising revenue and other income streams that traditionally support news production, creating an imbalance in which technology companies profit at the expense of the news industry. This dynamic not only infringes on intellectual property rights but also harms readers by providing summaries that often contain errors, lack context, and blur the line between facts and opinions. Without the resources needed to invest in editorial rigor, media organizations risk deteriorating the quality of their information, which undermines citizens’ ability to make informed decisions and increases vulnerability to misinformation. This conflict is crucial for *open data* because it raises the ethical and legal conditions under which public and journalistic information can be used by algorithms. The debate highlights the need for frameworks that ensure attribution, fair compensation, and transparency in data usage, ensuring that technological innovation does not erode press independence or the integrity of democratic debate—principles fundamental to a sustainable and ethical information ecosystem.
