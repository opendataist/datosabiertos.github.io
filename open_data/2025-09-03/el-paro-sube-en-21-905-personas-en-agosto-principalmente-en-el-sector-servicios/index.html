El paro sube en 21.905 personas en agosto, principalmente en el sector servicios

El paro sube en 21.905 personas en agosto, principalmente en el sector servicios

The Ministry of Labor has revealed that registered unemployment reached its lowest level in years, albeit with a slight seasonal uptick in August. This historic decline in total unemployment figures contrasts with the reality of the labor market, where affiliated employment experienced a significant monthly drop. The situation reflects the typical volatility of August, marked by the end of the tourist season, which necessitates contextualizing these monthly figures within a more favorable annual trend. At a structural level, sectors with higher volatility show divergent patterns. While education and administration recorded sharp declines in affiliations, sectors such as healthcare and scientific and technical activities consolidated gains. The rise in unemployment in services and industry, contrasted with a decrease in agriculture, indicates a dynamic reconfiguration of employment. Moreover, the reduction in youth and foreign unemployment demonstrates progress in the labor market integration of these groups, although a seasonal increase in the overall unemployment rate persists. This information is relevant to open data because it highlights the need to apply deseasonalized filters to correctly interpret the health of the labor market. Raw data can be misleading; therefore, access to time series adjusted and disaggregated by sector, gender, and region is crucial for analyzing real trends and evaluating the impact of public policies beyond natural seasonal fluctuations.
