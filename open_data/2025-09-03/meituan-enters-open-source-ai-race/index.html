Meituan enters open-source AI race

Meituan enters open-source AI race

Meituan’s open-source LongCat model offers competitive agentic performance at significantly lower costs, challenging industry leaders. Its efficient Mixture-of-Experts architecture enables high-speed processing with reduced computational requirements. This release highlights the growing trend of tech giants contributing high-performing models to open ecosystems. It demonstrates how efficient designs can rival expensive proprietary systems, potentially lowering barriers for developers. Such initiatives advance open_data by providing accessible, transparent AI tools for testing and integration. They encourage community-driven innovation and democratize access to state-of-the-art artificial intelligence capabilities.
