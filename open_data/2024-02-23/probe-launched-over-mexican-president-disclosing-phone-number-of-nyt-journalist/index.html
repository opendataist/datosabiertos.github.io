Probe launched over Mexican president disclosing phone number of NYT journalist

Probe launched over Mexican president disclosing phone number of NYT journalist

The publication of a journalist’s contact details by Mexico’s president triggered a formal probe by the national freedom of information body. This incident highlights the severe risks facing media professionals and underscores the critical importance of protecting data privacy as a fundamental component of open data governance and press freedom. The controversy emphasizes that transparent access to information must not compromise the safety of those seeking it. When public officials expose private data, it erodes trust in institutional safeguards and demonstrates how the misuse of personal information can silence investigative journalism, directly impacting the integrity of open information systems. This event is particularly relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the tension between transparency and security. It serves as a warning that without strict ethical boundaries and robust data protection protocols, the promise of open data can be weaponized, endangering individuals and undermining the democratic value of free and safe information exchange.
