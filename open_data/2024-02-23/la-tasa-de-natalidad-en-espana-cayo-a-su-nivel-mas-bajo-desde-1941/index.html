La tasa de natalidad en España cayó a su nivel más bajo desde 1941

Europe is facing a persistent birth crisis, with Spain recording record lows in the number of newborns. This demographic shift highlights critical challenges in sustaining population growth and economic stability across the continent, underscoring the urgent need for effective societal interventions. Economic insecurity, precarious employment, and high housing costs significantly delay parenthood or lead to smaller family sizes. These structural barriers prevent families from achieving the fertility rates necessary to maintain current population levels, illustrating how socioeconomic factors directly influence demographic outcomes and long-term societal resilience. This context is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates how transparent statistical tracking reveals complex social trends. Accessible datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze the root causes of demographic changes, fostering informed decision-making and evidence-based strategies to effectively address public challenges.
