Abre INAI investigación por dilvulgación de AMLO sobre periodista

The INAI is investigating whether the president’s disclosure of personal data violates data protection regulations, setting a precedent regarding the liability of obligated entities in cases of unauthorized exposure of sensitive information. This action underscores the critical importance of ensuring the confidentiality of sources and whistleblowers—a fundamental pillar for ethical transparency and security within the open data ecosystem. International backlash and journalistic support highlight how the mishandling of individual data can undermine public trust and press freedom. For the open data movement, this case illustrates the delicate boundary between government accountability and the protection of fundamental rights, reminding us that information openness must not compromise the integrity and safety of the individuals involved.
