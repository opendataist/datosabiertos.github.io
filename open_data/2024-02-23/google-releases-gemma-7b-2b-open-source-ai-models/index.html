Google releases Gemma 7B, 2B open-source AI models

Google released open-source Gemma models, derived from Gemini research, to democratize access to advanced AI technology. These efficient, responsible models enable developers to run powerful capabilities on local devices, lowering barriers to entry for innovation and transparency. This release significantly advances open data by providing accessible, high-quality AI tools that foster broader experimentation and ethical development within the global community.
