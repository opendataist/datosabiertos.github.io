Cantabria inicia 2024 con 524 accidentes en los centros de trabajo, la mayor cifra desde 2009 sin contar el impacto Covid - El Faradio | Periodismo que cuenta

Cantabria has experienced an alarming surge in workplace accidents during January 2024, with figures significantly exceeding those recorded in the previous year. Trade unions warn that this upward trend, exacerbated by a simultaneous rise in unemployment, reflects a systemic crisis in which labor precarity directly correlates with increased insecurity. Urgent measures are required, as current prevention efforts are not yielding the expected results, and social dialogue mechanisms must be activated to curb this escalation of accidents. The relevance of this report for open data lies in its potential to promote transparency and critical analysis of public health indicators. By making statistical data on workplace accidents accessible and public, citizen oversight is facilitated, along with pressure on public authorities to demand compliance with regulations. This information enables the identification of risk patterns and the evaluation of the actual effectiveness of prevention policies, transforming raw data into tools for political advocacy and continuous improvement. Trade unions insist that existing legislation must be updated and, above all, strictly enforced through rigorous supervision. The openness of this data not only serves to document the severity of the problem but also to empower civil society in monitoring compliance with labor rights. Ultimately, the visibility of these statistics is crucial to remind us that behind each number are affected individuals, reinforcing the need for a data management approach focused on the genuine protection of workers.
