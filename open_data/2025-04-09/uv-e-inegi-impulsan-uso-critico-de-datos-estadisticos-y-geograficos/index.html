UV e INEGI impulsan uso crítico de datos estadísticos y geográficos

UV e INEGI impulsan uso crítico de datos estadísticos y geográficos

The Veracruzana University, in collaboration with INEGI, launched the 33rd INEGI Chair to promote an interdisciplinary approach to data analysis. This initiative underscores how statistics and geography form a fundamental pair for understanding society, enabling other sciences to broaden their scope. By integrating these fields, researchers can develop a conceptual framework that is not only technical but also precise and deeply applicable to real-world complexities. A central theme is the critical use of high-quality official data for designing public policies and academic research. The program emphasizes that INEGI’s data, produced under rigorous standards, serves as a vital guide for investigating national realities. This partnership ensures that students and academics have access to comprehensive information, allowing them to address contemporary questions where data is abundant and pervasive, thereby transforming raw statistics into actionable strategic knowledge. This program is highly relevant to open data, as it promotes the strategic use and visualization of open government information. By training future professionals to navigate and conduct research using INEGI’s extensive databases, the initiative supports the broader open data movement’s goals of transparency and accessibility. It empowers the community to leverage public datasets for environmental and economic analysis, demonstrating how open access to reliable data drives better decision-making and societal understanding.
