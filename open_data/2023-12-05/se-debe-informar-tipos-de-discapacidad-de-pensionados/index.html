Se debe informar tipos de discapacidad de pensionados

Se debe informar tipos de discapacidad de pensionados

The INAI ordered the Welfare Secretariat to disclose detailed demographic data on disability pension recipients. This transparency demand aims to address the structural inequalities and discrimination faced by people with disabilities, who often experience poorer health outcomes and limited social participation. By mandating the release of specific statistics, the institution highlights how public data can reveal systemic disparities. This case is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how access to information serves as a tool for social justice. Transparent, granular datasets enable society to monitor government obligations and advocate for equal rights. Ultimately, making this data public supports the goal of a dignified life and full inclusion for marginalized groups, ensuring accountability in the distribution of welfare benefits.
