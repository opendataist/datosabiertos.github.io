¿Emprender después de los 50? Premios de hasta 15.000 euros y apoyo a ideas innovadoras

¿Emprender después de los 50? Premios de hasta 15.000 euros y apoyo a ideas innovadoras

The article highlights that professionals over the age of fifty in Spain possess solid experience and competitive values that are often overlooked, despite the fact that unemployment in this group tends to become long-term. Entrepreneurship is presented as a crucial pathway to transform this situation, enabling senior talent to develop sustainable, long-lasting businesses, as illustrated by the example of Retoornado. The phenomenon of senior entrepreneurship is growing, with female participation now equaling that of men. Initiatives such as the +50 Emprende Awards seek to make this talent visible, combat age-related prejudices, and foster a work culture that values professional maturity. These awards, particularly in their new edition focused on the circular economy, recognize projects that contribute to sustainability and the energy transition. The relevance for open data lies in the need for transparent data on the senior labor market and the impact of entrepreneurship. Access to detailed statistics, such as those from the National Statistics Institute (INE) or age-related reports, allows for the analysis of employment trends, identification of gender gaps, and evaluation of the effectiveness of inclusion policies. This transparency is essential for designing evidence-based strategies that leverage experienced human capital and promote a more inclusive and sustainable economy.
