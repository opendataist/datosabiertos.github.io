El Gobierno admitió que algunas páginas oficiales fueron hackeadas pero responsabilizó a Alberto Fernández

El Gobierno admitió que algunas páginas oficiales fueron hackeadas pero responsabilizó a Alberto Fernández

The Argentine government acknowledged the hacking of the Mi Argentina and SUBE platforms, attributing the vulnerability to a historical lack of investment in cybersecurity. This incident exposes structural weaknesses in the protection of public data, highlighting critical risks to the integrity of the State’s digital systems. The reuse of sensitive information by malicious actors underscores the urgency of implementing robust security standards. For the open data movement, this case illustrates how opacity and technical fragility undermine citizen trust in digital services, discouraging the use of government tools. The relevance to open data lies in the need to prioritize transparency and security as fundamental pillars. Ensuring that data and platforms are protected is not only a technical requirement but also an ethical obligation to guarantee safe and equitable access to public information, thereby strengthening digital governance and accountability.
