Derechos de autor y el fantasma de la inteligencia artificial

Derechos de autor y el fantasma de la inteligencia artificial

The article highlights a critical conflict between creative professionals and the unchecked expansion of Artificial Intelligence, particularly regarding data training. Writers, translators, and artists argue that AI models exploit their work without consent, necessitating strict contractual clauses to protect moral rights and prevent unauthorized use of their creations for training purposes. This stance emphasizes the urgent need to preserve human authorship and ensure creators retain control over how their intellectual property is utilized. Simultaneously, powerful corporate figures advocate for the elimination of intellectual property laws, claiming such regulations hinder technological progress. This perspective clashes with ongoing legal battles in which authors seek protection against algorithms that scrape vast amounts of content without limits. The tension reveals a broader struggle for cultural sovereignty, where the drive for technological efficiency threatens the intrinsic value of human expression and raises concerns about manipulation and the erosion of truth in media. This issue is vital to open data because it challenges the foundational ethics of data utilization in AI development. It underscores the necessity of moving beyond mere data availability to include consent and equity in data usage. As AI systems increasingly rely on copyrighted human creativity, the debate forces a reevaluation of how open data practices must evolve to respect legal boundaries and protect creators, ensuring that technological advancement does not come at the cost of fundamental human rights and cultural integrity.
