Violencia armada paraliza y bloquea acceso a insumos para transfusiones en Haití - Agencia Venezolana de Noticias

Violencia armada paraliza y bloquea acceso a insumos para transfusiones en Haití - Agencia Venezolana de Noticias

Armed violence in Haiti has drastically reduced blood collection, severely disrupting the National Blood Safety Program. This crisis limits access to essential medical services and forces reliance on costly maritime logistics due to unsafe land routes. The situation highlights critical vulnerabilities in public health infrastructure during conflicts. It demonstrates how security failures directly compromise service delivery and supply chains. This case is relevant to open data as it shows the necessity of transparent, real-time information sharing. Accessible data helps coordinate humanitarian aid and monitor health system resilience in unstable regions.
