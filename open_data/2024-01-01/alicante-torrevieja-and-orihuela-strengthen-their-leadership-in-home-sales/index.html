Alicante, Torrevieja and Orihuela strengthen their leadership in home sales

The third quarter of 2023 data reveals that Alicante’s real estate market is experiencing a broad decline in transaction volumes compared to the previous year. Despite this provincial downturn, three municipalities—Torrevieja, Orihuela, and Alicante—account for a significant portion of all sales, highlighting a concentration of activity in specific areas while the overall market contracts. Within this shrinking market, distinct trends emerge across different cities. While most locations report decreased sales, Benidorm stands out as the sole major city among the top six to show growth, albeit marginal. Conversely, Santa Pola and Alicante face sharper drops in volume, indicating that market resilience varies significantly depending on local demographics and economic drivers rather than following a uniform regional pattern. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how granular, localized statistics can uncover nuanced insights that aggregate provincial figures might obscure. By making such detailed municipal records accessible, researchers and citizens can better understand housing market dynamics, policy impacts, and economic health at the community level, fostering transparency and informed decision-making in urban planning and real estate sectors.
