El Ayuntamiento publica el anuario estadístico 2023 con todos los datos sobre la ciudad de València

The Valencia City Council has published its 2023 statistical yearbook, offering a detailed, district-level overview of the city’s demographics, economy, and environment. This comprehensive dataset facilitates granular urban analysis and monitors long-term municipal evolution across various sectors. Such open, structured data is essential for open data initiatives, promoting transparency, research, and data-driven decision-making for both citizens and developers.
