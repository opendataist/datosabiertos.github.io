La facturación de la industria cae un 5% en junio

Spanish industrial billing experienced a slight decline in June, driven by significant drops in consumption and intermediate goods, while growth in the energy sector offset some of the losses. Regional performance varied widely, with notable decreases in the Balearic Islands and increases in Andalusia, although overall revenue for the first half of the year remained stable. This data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the value of transparent, accessible national statistics for tracking economic trends and assessing sectoral resilience across diverse regions.
