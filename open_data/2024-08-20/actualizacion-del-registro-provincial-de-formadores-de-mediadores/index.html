Actualización del registro provincial de formadores de mediadores

Actualización del registro provincial de formadores de mediadores

The Secretariat of Justice is modernizing the registry of mediator trainers through digital transformation to ensure service quality and validate academic content. This centralization enables authorities to supervise projects and approve training programs, guaranteeing that mediators receive adequate professional education. This update is significant for open data, as it exemplifies the shift toward transparent and accessible public service registries. By maintaining a valid, digitally managed list of authorized entities, the government enhances accountability and facilitates public verification of provider credentials, thereby fostering trust in legal mediation services through improved data governance.
