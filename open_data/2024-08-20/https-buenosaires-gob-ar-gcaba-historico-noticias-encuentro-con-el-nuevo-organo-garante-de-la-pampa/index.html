ES EN

The Office of Administrative Investigations now guarantees public access to information in La Pampa, in accordance with provincial law. This oversight mechanism ensures that all citizens can request data from government bodies, marking a significant step toward institutional transparency and administrative accountability. This development is crucial for open data, as it establishes a dedicated legal framework for public information. By empowering citizens to request government data, the new office fosters a culture of transparency. It reinforces the right to information, laying the groundwork for broader open governance initiatives.
