Cinco provincias, entre las que no está Río Negro, adhirieron a la ley de transparencia al consumidor

Cinco provincias, entre las que no está Río Negro, adhirieron a la ley de transparencia al consumidor

The article highlights the progress of fiscal transparency in Argentina, where the City of Buenos Aires and several provinces have confirmed their adherence to a regime that requires displaying taxes on purchase receipts. This measure, promoted by the NGO Lógica and grounded in constitutional mandates, aims to make visible taxes that were previously hidden, such as gross income taxes and municipal fees, breaking with decades of opacity that made it difficult to understand the real tax burden on consumers. The main significance of this development lies in its direct impact on open data and citizen participation. By making tax information public in real time during commercial transactions, an abstract fiscal datum is transformed into accessible information. This enables society to demand responsible behavior in public spending, fostering greater fiscal awareness and strengthening the relationship between the State and citizens through accountability. Finally, the initiative represents a cultural shift toward active transparency, where provincial governments embrace this commitment as a sign of institutional quality to attract investments and improve public trust. Unifying this information at the national level is crucial for creating a clear fiscal data market, allowing citizens, regardless of political affiliation, to monitor and demand public services commensurate with the taxes they actually bear.
