La Nación / Inflación de Argentina llega a 2,4 % en febrero, levemente por encima del mes anterior

Argentina’s February inflation remained elevated, driven primarily by housing and food costs. This persistent rise underscores the difficulty in stabilizing prices despite policy adjustments, highlighting the challenges in achieving sustainable economic recovery. Data transparency from the national statistics institute is crucial for understanding these dynamics. Open data facilitates public scrutiny and informed decision-making regarding monetary policy. Accessible, accurate statistics empower citizens and analysts to assess the real impact of inflation on households. This case illustrates why open data is vital for economic accountability. Without clear, available data, understanding systemic issues like inflation becomes difficult. Ensuring free access to official statistics strengthens democratic oversight and fosters trust in government institutions.
