Un experto en ciberseguridad explica qué debes hacer cuando te piden el DNI en un hotel: "Yo nunca lo doy"

Un experto en ciberseguridad explica qué debes hacer cuando te piden el DNI en un hotel: "Yo nunca lo doy"

The article highlights significant privacy risks inherent in the mandatory tourist registration system, where accommodation providers must transmit guest data to government authorities. This regulatory shift has sparked industry backlash, with stakeholders arguing that the requirement to collect and process large volumes of personal information compromises user privacy and security. Experts warn against physically handing over identity documents for scanning, citing vulnerabilities such as the potential for identity theft. Instead, they recommend safer alternatives, such as providing black-and-white photocopies with watermarks or writing sensitive details like phone numbers on paper rather than speaking them aloud, to prevent unauthorized data interception. This topic is crucial for open data discussions because it underscores the tension between state surveillance infrastructure and individual privacy rights. It illustrates the challenges of managing sensitive personal information within digital systems and highlights the need for robust data protection standards when public entities mandate data sharing with the private sector.
