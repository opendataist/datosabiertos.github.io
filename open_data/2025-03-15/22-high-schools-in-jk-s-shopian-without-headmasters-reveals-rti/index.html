22 High Schools in JK's Shopian Without Headmasters, Reveals RTI

22 High Schools in JK's Shopian Without Headmasters, Reveals RTI

RTI disclosures expose severe staffing crises in South Kashmir’s schools, where numerous high schools and higher secondary institutions operate without headmasters or principals. This leadership vacuum, compounded by widespread lecturer vacancies, disrupts daily educational functioning and highlights systemic administrative failures despite government claims of sectoral improvement. The situation underscores the critical role of transparency mechanisms like RTI in holding authorities accountable. By revealing the discrepancy between official narratives and ground realities, open data initiatives empower citizens to demand urgent interventions from education officials. This case demonstrates how accessible public information can drive civic engagement and policy correction. When data on resource allocation and staffing becomes visible, it fosters necessary dialogue, ensuring that educational infrastructure meets statutory requirements and serves student needs effectively.
