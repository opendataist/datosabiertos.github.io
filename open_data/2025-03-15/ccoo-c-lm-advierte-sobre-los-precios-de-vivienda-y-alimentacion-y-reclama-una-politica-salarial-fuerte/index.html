CCOO C-LM advierte sobre los precios de vivienda y alimentación, y reclama una "política salarial fuerte"

CCOO C-LM advierte sobre los precios de vivienda y alimentación, y reclama una "política salarial fuerte"

High inflation on basic goods has severely weakened workers' purchasing power and savings in Castilla-La Mancha. To restore economic stability, the union advocates for strong wage policies that exceed the consumer price index. They also demand interventionist public policies to regulate markets and prevent artificial bubbles. This approach aims to stop profiteers from transferring costs to consumers, ensuring fair pricing mechanisms. This highlights the critical role of transparent price data in defending labor rights. Accurate open data on inflation empowers citizens and unions to demand just policies and protect their economic well-being against exploitative market practices.
