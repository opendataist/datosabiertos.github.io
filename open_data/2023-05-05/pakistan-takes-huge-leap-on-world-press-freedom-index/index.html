Pakistan Takes Huge Leap on World Press Freedom Index

Pakistan’s improved press freedom ranking masks a reality of systemic censorship, where laws weaponize regulators to silence criticism and financial leverage dictates editorial independence. This structural control ensures state narratives prevail, undermining transparent information dissemination essential for accountable governance. Violence against journalists remains rampant and largely unpunished, creating a climate of fear that silences dissenting voices. This intimidation tactics suppress critical reporting, restricting public access to vital information and fostering an environment where accountability is actively suppressed. For open data, these findings highlight how political suppression corrupts information ecosystems. When data and journalism are controlled by power rather than public interest, transparency erodes, making reliable, unbiased data access difficult and threatening democratic integrity and informed public discourse.
