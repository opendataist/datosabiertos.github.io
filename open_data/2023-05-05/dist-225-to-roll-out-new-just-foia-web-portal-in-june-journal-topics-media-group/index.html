Dist. 225 To Roll Out New ‘Just FOIA’ Web Portal In June - Journal & Topics Media Group

Glenbrook High School District 225 is transitioning to a dedicated online platform, “Just FOIA,” to manage Freedom of Information Act requests, marking a significant shift in how government transparency is administered. This move addresses the inefficiencies of the current email-based system and prepares for a leadership change in the FOIA officer position. By centralizing request management, the district aims to streamline operations, reduce administrative burdens, and ensure compliance with Illinois state statutes regarding public access to government documents. The new system enhances accessibility and accountability by allowing the public to submit requests, track their status, and view history through a user-friendly portal. Unlike previous methods, this digital tool centralizes document collection and utilizes AI to assist with routine redactions of personally identifiable information, while complex legal questions remain under attorney review. This technological upgrade not only improves the requester experience but also provides administrators with automated activity reports, fostering greater internal organization and external clarity regarding the district’s responsiveness. This implementation is highly relevant to the open_data community as it demonstrates a practical model for digitizing public records workflows. By automating parts of the disclosure process and creating a searchable, centralized archive, the district moves closer to true open data principles beyond mere statutory compliance. Such initiatives highlight the potential for technology to democratize access to information, encouraging other public entities to adopt similar transparent, efficient systems that prioritize public engagement and data availability.
