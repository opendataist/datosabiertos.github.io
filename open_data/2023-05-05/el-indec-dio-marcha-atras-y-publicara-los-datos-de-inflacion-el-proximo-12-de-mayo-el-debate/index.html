El Indec dio marcha atrás y publicará los datos de inflación el próximo 12 de mayo - EL DEBATE

INDEC reversed its decision to postpone the release of April’s inflation data, confirming that it will be published on the originally scheduled date, despite initial concerns that doing so might interfere with the provincial electoral blackout periods. This decision prioritizes statistical transparency and adherence to the publication schedule over procedural electoral restrictions, addressing public suspicions about the institution’s independence from political processes. The outcome reinforces the principle that essential public statistics must remain accessible and unbiased, ensuring that citizens receive timely economic information regardless of electoral cycles. This commitment to reliable and unimpeded data dissemination is fundamental for maintaining trust in open data ecosystems and supporting informed public discourse.
