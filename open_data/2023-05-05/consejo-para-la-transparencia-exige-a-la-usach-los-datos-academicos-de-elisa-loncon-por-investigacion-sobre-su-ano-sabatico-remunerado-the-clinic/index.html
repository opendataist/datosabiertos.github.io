Consejo para la Transparencia exige a la Usach los datos académicos de Elisa Loncón por investigación sobre su año sabático remunerado | The Clinic

Consejo para la Transparencia exige a la Usach los datos académicos de Elisa Loncón por investigación sobre su año sabático remunerado | The Clinic

The Council for Transparency ordered the University of Santiago de Chile to disclose the academic credentials of Elisa Loncón, a public figure and former president of the Constitutional Convention. This decision highlights the principle that the work of officials in state-administered institutions must be carried out with integrity and transparency, ensuring public access to professional records. The university initially withheld this information, arguing that its release would infringe on personal rights; however, the Council rejected this argument. The ruling establishes that verifying academic qualifications and employment history does not threaten the dignity or privacy of public servants, thereby reinforcing accountability over institutional secrecy. This case is significant for open data, as it clarifies the boundary between personal privacy and public accountability. It confirms that the professional histories of high-ranking officials in state-linked institutions are not inherently private, promoting a culture in which citizens’ requests for institutional transparency take precedence over unsubstantiated privacy claims.
