SE VIENE EL OPEN GOV WEEK

The Open Government Partnership promotes global transparency and civic collaboration through annual events that foster innovation in public management. This initiative encourages governments, civil society, and businesses to work together, organizing workshops and hackathons that develop solutions for societal challenges. By strengthening these cooperative ties, the week aims to build trust between institutions and citizens, creating a more equitable society. The celebration in Ecuador is vital for strengthening democracy and improving citizens' quality of life. Transparency serves as a fundamental value that enhances institutional credibility and the effectiveness of decision-making. When the public has access to information, they can actively engage in governance and exercise oversight over state actions. This engagement empowers individuals, deepening their commitment to the common good and ensuring that government responses align with societal needs. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores how accessible information enables the civic participation that defines open government. Data transparency is the mechanism that allows citizens to monitor state actions and contribute to policy decisions. Without open data, the dialogue between government and society remains superficial. Therefore, effectively publishing data is not merely a technical requirement but a democratic necessity that supports the goals of accountability and collaborative innovation highlighted in the event.
