The educational authority revoked the degrees granted by Universidad Abierta SC due to irregular operations, despite the institution having previously received accreditation. This measure prioritizes legal certainty and protects students, ensuring that their right to a valid education is upheld through established regulatory mechanisms. To address incomplete studies, students can transfer their credits to four private institutions. This collaborative solution provides a one-year window for students to complete their degrees, allowing them to obtain their academic qualifications without losing the progress they have already made. This case underscores the importance of transparent, data-driven verification in higher education. Reliable open-data mechanisms enable authorities to quickly detect anomalies, ensuring institutional accountability and safeguarding public trust in educational credentials.
