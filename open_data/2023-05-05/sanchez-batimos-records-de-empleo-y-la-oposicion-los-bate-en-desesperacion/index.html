Sánchez: Batimos récords de empleo y la oposición los bate en desesperación

The article highlights Spain’s record-breaking employment growth and reduced unemployment, framing these outcomes as a validation of the government’s proactive economic strategy. The central conclusion is that social democratic policies, which combine reforms with expanded social rights, successfully stimulate the economy and distribute its benefits more effectively than previous neoliberal approaches. This narrative challenges the traditional political assumption that right-wing administrations manage economies better while left-wing ones merely redistribute wealth. By demonstrating that wage increases and social protections can coexist with job creation and fiscal balance, the text asserts that inclusive growth is achievable even during global crises such as the pandemic and the war in Ukraine. The article is relevant to open data because it leverages transparent, high-frequency social security statistics to build political credibility. It illustrates how public access to reliable, granular labor market indicators allows governments to empirically justify their policy choices. This transparency enables citizens to directly assess the real-world impact of administrative decisions, fostering a data-driven public discourse on economic performance and social welfare.
