CSIF C-LM celebra el descenso del desempleo, pero reclama "responsabilidad" para evitar "triunfalismos"

Unemployment in Castilla-La Mancha has seen a significant decline driven by the services sector, consolidating a positive year-on-year trend. However, trade unions warn that this decrease should not mask the deterioration in job quality and inflationary pressures, exacerbated by climate-related factors that impact the cost of living. This distinction is crucial for open data, as it shows that quantitative indicators must be analyzed alongside qualitative and contextual variables to avoid misleading interpretations and provide a realistic view of social well-being.
