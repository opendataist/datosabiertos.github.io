Continúa abierto el Registro de Payadores de la provincia | Análisis

Continúa abierto el Registro de Payadores de la provincia | Análisis

A provincial initiative in Entre Ríos aims to register payadores, creating a cultural map to safeguard this intangible heritage. By identifying current artists, the effort ensures their poetic traditions remain vital for future generations rather than fading into history. This public register highlights the importance of open data in cultural preservation. Making such information accessible allows for better documentation and visibility of local artistic expressions. The project demonstrates how structured data collection supports heritage protection. It encourages community participation, showing that open access to cultural records helps sustain living traditions.
