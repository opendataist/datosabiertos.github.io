In-Person Petitions To Allahabad High Court Can Be Filed From District Courts' E-Service Centres, Photo Identity Verification To Be Removed

In-Person Petitions To Allahabad High Court Can Be Filed From District Courts' E-Service Centres, Photo Identity Verification To Be Removed

The Allahabad High Court has streamlined the process for individuals appearing in person by allowing petitions to be filed directly at district eSewa Kendras, eliminating the need for centralized photo identity verification. This shift significantly reduces travel burdens for litigants while maintaining physical presence requirements at local service centers through scanned notarized affidavits. This reform enhances accessibility to justice by decentralizing filing procedures, aligning with Supreme Court directives to maximize the utility of digital infrastructure. It demonstrates how judicial administrative changes can improve user experience and procedural efficiency in legal systems. The case is relevant to open data as it highlights the practical implementation and expansion of e-governance services. It underscores the importance of accessible, digitized public records and services, reflecting broader trends toward transparency and reduced friction in accessing judicial information and processes.
