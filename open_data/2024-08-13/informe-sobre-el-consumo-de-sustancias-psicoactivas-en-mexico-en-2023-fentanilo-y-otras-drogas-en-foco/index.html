Informe sobre el Consumo de Sustancias Psicoactivas en México en 2023: Fentanilo y Otras Drogas en Foco

Recent data from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) reveal a sharp increase in Mexico’s demand for treatment related to illicit fentanyl consumption, driven largely by states in the northern region. This trend signals a critical shift in the national substance abuse landscape, with Chihuahua emerging as a focal point for both user distress and law enforcement seizures. The data highlight that the proliferation of opioids is no longer marginal but represents an escalating public health crisis. Although national usage remains lower than global averages, the rapid increase in treatment requests indicates that illegal synthetic opioids are penetrating communities previously less affected by such substances. This situation is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how statistical transparency can reveal hidden epidemiological trends. By tracking these metrics, policymakers and researchers can identify emerging threats early, enabling more effective, data-driven strategies to combat the growing addiction crisis and allocate resources where they are most urgently needed.
