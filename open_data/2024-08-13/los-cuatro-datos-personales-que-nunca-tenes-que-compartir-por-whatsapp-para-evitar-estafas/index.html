Los cuatro datos personales que nunca tenés que compartir por WhatsApp para evitar estafas

Los cuatro datos personales que nunca tenés que compartir por WhatsApp para evitar estafas

The article underscores the critical importance of safeguarding personal data within instant messaging platforms like WhatsApp, particularly as cyber threats and scams intensify. It concludes that specific categories of sensitive information, such as banking details, passwords, SMS verification codes, and personal identification, must never be transmitted through these channels. Sharing such data exposes users to significant risks of identity theft and financial fraud, making digital hygiene a fundamental requirement for secure communication in the modern era. Beyond identifying what not to share, the text emphasizes proactive security measures to protect user accounts from compromise. Key recommendations include enabling two-step verification, keeping software updated, and using biometric authentication to add layers of defense. Furthermore, users are advised to verify the identity of contacts requesting urgent information, avoid suspicious links that may lead to phishing sites, and regularly review connected devices to ensure no unauthorized access exists. These steps collectively reduce the attack surface available to malicious actors. This content is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the fragility of personal information when it circulates through informal, often unencrypted or loosely regulated communication vectors. Understanding these vulnerabilities is essential for policymakers and data advocates who aim to promote robust data protection standards. By raising awareness about how easily data can be intercepted or misused via everyday apps, the article supports the broader open data movement’s goal of ensuring that information transparency does not come at the cost of individual privacy and security.
