Veracruz, tercer lugar nacional en robos y asaltos en carreteras

Veracruz ranks among the Mexican states with the highest incidence of highway robbery, underscoring critical security challenges in transport infrastructure. Despite the high robbery rates, the state recorded minimal weapon seizures, revealing a complex dynamic between the frequency of crimes and the severity of violent confrontations during these incidents. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how public statistics enable granular analysis of safety trends, helping authorities allocate resources and address specific regional security vulnerabilities.
