El turismo cultural en España recupera cifras prepandémicas: casi uno de cada cinco viajes realizados en 2022 fue por motivos culturales

El turismo cultural en España recupera cifras prepandémicas: casi uno de cada cinco viajes realizados en 2022 fue por motivos culturales

The article highlights a robust recovery in cultural tourism in Spain, with domestic and international travelers significantly contributing to the sector’s vitality. Approximately one in five trips in the previous year were motivated by cultural activities, reflecting a strong consumer preference for experiential travel. This surge indicates that cultural interests are a primary driver for both residents and foreign visitors, signaling sustained demand for cultural experiences in the post-pandemic era. Regional data reveals that major autonomous communities such as Madrid, Catalonia, and Andalusia lead in generating cultural tourism revenue. While international tourists spend more on average, domestic residents play a crucial role in supporting local economies across the country. The consistency of these figures with pre-2019 levels suggests a normalized market in which cultural tourism has re-established its economic importance through diverse spending patterns on accommodation and packaged tours. This report is relevant to open data because it provides granular, sector-specific insights that can be used to monitor economic trends and assess policy effectiveness. By making such detailed statistics publicly accessible, governments and researchers can better analyze travel behaviors, allocate resources efficiently, and promote sustainable tourism. Transparency in these metrics enables informed decision-making, fostering a data-driven approach to enhancing cultural infrastructure and visitor experiences.
