Otorgarán 30 puntos a estudiantes que cumplan la labor de censistas en el Censo 2024

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INE) incentivizes secondary school students with academic credits for serving as census volunteers, prioritizing educational benefits over monetary compensation. This strategy leverages institutional partnerships to secure a large workforce while enhancing civic engagement through the school system, ensuring broad participation in the upcoming national census operations. The recruitment process relies on digital platforms and establishes clear deadlines for various demographic groups, ranging from university students to indigenous organizations. This structured timeline enables the statistical institute to systematically onboard hundreds of thousands of volunteers, ensuring that sufficient human resources are available to carry out the massive data collection effort efficiently. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public institutions can effectively mobilize citizens to generate comprehensive, high-quality demographic information. By integrating educational incentives into the data collection phase, the government not only improves the accuracy and coverage of national statistics but also fosters a culture of transparency and civic participation in the production of vital public datasets.
