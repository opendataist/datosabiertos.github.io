Ecuador registró una inflación de 0,04% en septiembre de 2023

The article details the stabilization of Ecuador’s consumer price index in September 2023, highlighting a significant deceleration in both monthly and annual inflation rates compared to the previous year. This trend suggests a cooling of the economy, driven primarily by reductions in food prices and a general slowdown in service costs, while specific sectors like transport and health experienced modest increases. Regional analysis reveals a persistent disparity between coastal and highland areas, with coastal cities consistently exhibiting higher inflation volatility than their Sierra counterparts. This geographic divide underscores the uneven impact of economic pressures across different populations, indicating that national averages may mask distinct local realities and challenges in purchasing power. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the necessity of granular, disaggregated statistical publishing. By breaking down indices by consumption categories, product types, and geographic regions, the data allows for more precise socioeconomic analysis. Transparent access to these detailed metrics enables researchers and policymakers to move beyond aggregate numbers, fostering a deeper understanding of how macroeconomic indicators affect specific communities and driving evidence-based decision-making.
