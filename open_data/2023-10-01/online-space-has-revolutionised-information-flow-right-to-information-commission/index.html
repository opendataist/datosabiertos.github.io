Online space has revolutionised information flow — Right to Information Commission

The International Day for Universal Access to Information highlights the transformative role of the online space in democratizing information. The digital environment serves as a critical platform for exercising the right to information, offering unprecedented convenience, speed, and diversity of sources for citizens. By transcending geographical and societal barriers, online tools empower individuals to hold public institutions accountable, thereby fostering democratic participation and strengthening the social contract between the government and its people. This digital shift significantly enhances transparency and inclusivity in governance. Online portals facilitate proactive disclosure by state actors, allowing for efficient management and cost reduction while enabling citizens to scrutinize public spending and policy decisions. Furthermore, these platforms bridge the digital divide by providing marginalized groups, including women and persons with disabilities, equitable access to knowledge. This accessibility promotes social inclusion and enables diverse voices to engage in public discourse, demanding rights and contributing to innovative development through shared best practices. However, realizing the full potential of digital information access requires addressing persistent challenges such as inadequate infrastructure, digital illiteracy, and the spread of misinformation. The article underscores the necessity of collaborative efforts to invest in digital skills training and expand connectivity to ensure equitable access. This context is highly relevant to open data, as it illustrates the legal and technological frameworks, like Ghana’s new online records system, designed to operationalize the right to information. It demonstrates how open data initiatives must be coupled with capacity building and robust infrastructure to truly empower citizens and ensure that transparency leads to tangible accountability and informed decision-making.
