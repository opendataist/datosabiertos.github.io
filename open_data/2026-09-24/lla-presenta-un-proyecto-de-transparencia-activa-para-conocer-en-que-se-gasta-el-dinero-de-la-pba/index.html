LLA presenta un proyecto de “transparencia activa” para conocer “en qué se gasta el dinero” de la PBA

LLA presenta un proyecto de “transparencia activa” para conocer “en qué se gasta el dinero” de la PBA

The article highlights a legislative proposal in Buenos Aires to establish a single online portal for active transparency and open data. This initiative aims to proactively publish government resources, expenses, contracts, and financial administration, ensuring citizens can access this information without navigating bureaucratic requests. The core idea is to treat public information as community property, automatically available rather than hidden behind administrative hurdles. By centralizing data on budget execution, public enterprise balances, and personnel, the project seeks to enable citizens and researchers to understand how public funds are managed. The proponents argue that current systems are tortuous and inefficient, often failing to provide timely answers to legitimate information requests. This shift emphasizes that transparency must be the default state, allowing for immediate scrutiny of how taxes and debts impact the provincial administration. This development is highly relevant to open data because it advocates for a systemic change from reactive to proactive disclosure. It reinforces the principle that open data is a fundamental tool for democratic accountability, preventing the misuse of public funds through constant, unobstructed public oversight. The proposal demonstrates how structural technological solutions can enforce transparency standards, ensuring that government operations remain visible and verifiable by the society they serve, thereby strengthening trust and civic engagement.
