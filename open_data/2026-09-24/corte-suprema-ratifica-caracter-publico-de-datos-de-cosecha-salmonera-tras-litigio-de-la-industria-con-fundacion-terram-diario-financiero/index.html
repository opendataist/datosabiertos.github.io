Corte Suprema ratifica carácter público de datos de cosecha salmonera tras litigio de la industria con Fundación Terram | Diario Financiero

The Supreme Court’s ruling establishes that salmon farming production records constitute public information, thereby overriding corporate claims of commercial secrecy. This decision empowers citizens to access the data necessary for effective environmental and regulatory oversight, ensuring that fishing authorities can accurately monitor compliance with environmental impact assessments and concession limits. By rejecting the companies’ arguments, the judgment prioritizes transparency and accountability over private interests. It confirms that public access to such information is essential for detecting irregularities, such as overproduction and unauthorized operations. Consequently, this prevents industries from concealing non-compliance behind confidentiality claims, fostering a more open and supervised sector. This case is pivotal for open data, as it reinforces the principle that information concerning matters of public interest and state oversight cannot be withheld. It sets a precedent that strengthens civic control mechanisms, demonstrating that access to administrative data is crucial for societal scrutiny. Ultimately, it validates the use of open data as a tool for democratic accountability in environmental management.
