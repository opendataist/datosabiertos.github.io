Los líderes de IA informarán a la ONU ante advertencias de posible pérdida de control

Artificial intelligence is advancing at an unprecedented pace, with the real threat that systems may improve autonomously and escape human control, potentially triggering unintended international conflicts. Leaders from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Hugging Face are addressing the United Nations Security Council to urge the adoption of common criteria for assessing these capabilities and mitigating practical risks to global security. The debate centers on whether algorithms can interact in unpredictable ways, generating security crises without direct human intervention. While some leaders seek to curb growth to maintain strategic advantages, others, such as the UN Secretary-General, are calling for international governance that does not stifle development but rather oversees it, emphasizing the urgency of coordinating responses to threats that transcend national borders. This event is crucial for open data, as effective AI governance requires transparency in algorithms and the datasets used. Without open access to information about how these technologies are developed and deployed, the international community cannot adequately audit risks or build the trust necessary to establish global regulatory frameworks that protect collective security against autonomous systems.
