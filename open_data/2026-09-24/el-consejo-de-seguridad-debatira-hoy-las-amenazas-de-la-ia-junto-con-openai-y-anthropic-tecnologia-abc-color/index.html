El Consejo de Seguridad debatirá hoy las amenazas de la IA junto con OpenAI y Anthropic - Tecnología - ABC Color

The UN Security Council is convening its first dedicated discussion on the systemic risks of advanced AI, particularly the potential loss of human control over autonomous systems. This landmark session underscores the urgent need to address how generative AI threatens global peace through autonomous weapons and misuse by non-state actors, reflecting growing societal concerns and recent security breaches at major technology firms. Key industry leaders are participating to advocate for moderated development speeds and common safety standards, emphasizing the necessity of diplomatic trust-building measures. Their proposals for institutional safeguards stand in sharp contrast to political resistance, such as the US rejection of global regulatory frameworks, highlighting a critical divide between technical caution and political ideology in managing technological evolution. This event is vital for the open data community, as it signals the imminent regulation of AI technologies, which directly impacts data accessibility, algorithmic transparency, and ethical deployment. Stakeholders must prepare for stricter oversight mechanisms that could redefine how AI models are developed, tested, and shared, thereby influencing future policies on data governance and the balance between innovation and global security.
