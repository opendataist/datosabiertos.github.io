La guerra entre EEUU e Irán, el conflicto en Ucrania y la IA dominan la agenda del segundo día de la Asamblea General de la ONU

The United Nations Security Council meeting with leaders of artificial intelligence companies underscores the urgency of addressing technological risks amid the rapid advancement of autonomous systems. This gathering highlights the need for international oversight, as evidenced by recent incidents such as cross-platform cyberattacks, which reveal the absence of coherent global regulatory frameworks. At the same time, political divisions over AI governance are becoming apparent. While the U.S. administration rejects international controls, France is promoting open-source models as a strategic alternative. This divergence underscores the critical importance of open access to models and data, fostering transparency and technological independence in the face of monopolies held by major powers. The relevance to open data lies in how these debates shape the future of technological information. The push toward open-source models encourages the public availability of algorithms and datasets, which are essential for auditing, collaborative innovation, and risk mitigation. This trend not only democratizes access to AI but also establishes vital precedents for transparency and accountability in the management of global data.
