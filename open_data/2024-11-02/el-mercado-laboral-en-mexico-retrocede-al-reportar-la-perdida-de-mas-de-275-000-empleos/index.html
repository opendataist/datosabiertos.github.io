El mercado laboral en México retrocede al reportar la pérdida de más de 275.000 empleos

El mercado laboral en México retrocede al reportar la pérdida de más de 275.000 empleos

Mexico’s labor market contracted in September, resulting in significant job losses. This decline underscores a shift toward informality, with the majority of lost jobs occurring in the informal sector. The trend raises concerns about job quality and social protection for a large portion of the workforce. This data highlights the importance of open data in accurately tracking economic health. Public access to detailed employment statistics enables researchers and policymakers to identify structural weaknesses, such as high rates of informal employment. Transparent datasets facilitate better analysis of labor market trends and support evidence-based policy decisions. The relevance of open data lies in its capacity to monitor key societal indicators, such as those from the ENOE (National Occupation and Employment Survey). By making these figures accessible, citizens and institutions can verify official reports and advocate for labor rights. Open data promotes accountability and fosters a deeper understanding of the economic challenges facing the region.
