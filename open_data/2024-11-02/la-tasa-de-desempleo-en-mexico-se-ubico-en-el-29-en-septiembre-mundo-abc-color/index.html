La tasa de desempleo en México se ubicó en el 2,9 % en septiembre - Mundo - ABC Color

The unemployment rate remained stable despite an increase in the number of unemployed individuals, as a larger labor force absorbed the additional workers. This stability highlights a resilient labor market structure, suggesting that current economic policies are maintaining employment levels even as more people enter the workforce. However, the persistence of high informality rates and significant underemployment reveals underlying vulnerabilities. These indicators suggest that while formal employment figures appear steady, many workers lack adequate hours or legal protections, posing a challenge for sustainable economic development and social welfare. This data is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the importance of accessible, granular statistical transparency. By publishing detailed breakdowns of employment sectors and informal labor, governments enable researchers and citizens to monitor economic health, fostering accountability and informed policy decisions based on comprehensive, freely available evidence.
