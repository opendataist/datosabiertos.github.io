Esta es la ciudad más poblada de Cordillera, según el INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

The article presents the final census results for the Cordillera department, highlighting a population of nearly 270,000 inhabitants and revealing significant disparities in basic infrastructure and demographics. A critical finding is the extremely low rate of waste collection services, which reaches only a small fraction of occupied homes. This data underscores severe gaps in public sanitation, particularly in less populated districts, exposing the urgent need for targeted infrastructure development and improved municipal service delivery. Furthermore, the report details a clear digital divide, where access to the internet is concentrated in a few urban centers while rural areas remain largely disconnected. Demographically, the region is aging, with a notable shift toward an older population structure compared to younger age groups. The distribution of household leadership and family types also reflects evolving social dynamics, showing that while male-headed households are predominant, a substantial portion of homes are led by women or are single-person units. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it transforms raw statistical figures into actionable insights for civic monitoring and policy design. By making granular local data publicly available, stakeholders can hold authorities accountable for service delivery deficits and advocate for equitable resource distribution. It enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to analyze socio-economic trends deeply, fostering transparency and supporting evidence-based decision-making in public administration.
