Greenhouse gas emissions decreased in Europe

European Union emissions fell significantly, marking a decade-high improvement. While the US saw minimal reductions and India increased output, the EU achieved substantial long-term progress compared to 1990 levels. Transparency in environmental data is crucial for verifying national climate efforts. Open data allows researchers to compare regional performance and hold governments accountable for their sustainability commitments. This information highlights the urgent need for better monitoring to meet future climate goals. Accessible, verified statistics empower policymakers and citizens to push for stronger, evidence-based environmental regulations globally.
