Organización internacional se suma a la preocupación y advierte peligro de la ley anti-ONG

Organización internacional se suma a la preocupación y advierte peligro de la ley anti-ONG

The Open Government Partnership warns that new regulations in Paraguay threaten civil society organizations by imposing excessive bureaucracy and restricting funding, potentially forcing many to close. This contraction of civic space undermines the transparency and accountability that the OGP champions globally. Such restrictions weaken the state’s ability to respond effectively to citizen demands, highlighting the critical role of independent organizations in democratic governance. The international community views these measures as harmful to open data ecosystems and collaborative governance structures essential for societal progress. This situation is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how legal frameworks can either support or hinder the flow of information and civic participation. Protecting civil society is vital for maintaining the transparency and public engagement that open government initiatives rely upon to function effectively and sustain democratic integrity.
