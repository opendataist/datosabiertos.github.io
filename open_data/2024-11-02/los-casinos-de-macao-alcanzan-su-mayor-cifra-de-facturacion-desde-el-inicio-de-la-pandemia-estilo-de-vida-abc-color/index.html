Los casinos de Macao alcanzan su mayor cifra de facturación desde el inicio de la pandemia - Estilo de vida - ABC Color

Macao’s gambling revenue is recovering in the post-pandemic period, surpassing last year’s figures and pre-pandemic tourist arrival rates during holiday periods. Despite this growth, total earnings remain significantly below 2019 levels, highlighting the sector’s slow return to previous prosperity amid fluctuating visitor numbers. The territory remains heavily dependent on gaming, which historically constituted a majority of its economy and employment. This reliance created severe economic vulnerabilities when travel restrictions drastically reduced tourism, exposing the fragility of an economy anchored almost entirely to a single industry. This article is relevant to open data because transparent reporting of these financial and tourism metrics is essential for monitoring Macao’s economic diversification efforts. As the new leadership commits to reducing gaming dependency, accessible, high-quality public data will be crucial for analyzing whether alternative growth sectors are successfully replacing gambling as the primary economic driver.
