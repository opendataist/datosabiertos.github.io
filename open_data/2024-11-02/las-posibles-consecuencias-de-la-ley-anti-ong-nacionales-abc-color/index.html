Las posibles consecuencias de la ley “anti-ONG” - Nacionales - ABC Color

Las posibles consecuencias de la ley “anti-ONG” - Nacionales - ABC Color

Civil society organizations warn that restrictive legislation targeting non-governmental groups in Latin America threatens to dismantle civic spaces through excessive bureaucracy and arbitrary enforcement. These measures endanger the operational capacity of associations, potentially forcing closures due to increased administrative burdens and limited access to funding. Such regulations undermine fundamental democratic rights by silencing voices that are essential for holding power accountable and advocating for human rights. The lack of transparent procedures and prior consultation with affected groups highlights a democratic deficit, violating constitutional guarantees and international treaties. By creating rigid registries without clear guidelines, authorities risk exercising unchecked power, which destabilizes the relationship between the state and citizens. This environment erodes trust and prevents the constructive dialogue necessary for effective governance and social cohesion. This issue is critical for open data because a vibrant civil society requires unrestricted access to information and resources to monitor government actions. When civic space shrinks, the transparency mechanisms and participatory frameworks that open data initiatives aim to strengthen are severely compromised. Protecting these organizations is therefore not just a legal matter, but a prerequisite for maintaining the accountability and citizen engagement that open government depends on.
