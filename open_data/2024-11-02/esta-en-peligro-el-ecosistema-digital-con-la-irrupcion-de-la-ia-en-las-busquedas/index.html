¿Está en peligro el ecosistema digital con la irrupción de la IA en las búsquedas?

¿Está en peligro el ecosistema digital con la irrupción de la IA en las búsquedas?

The legal dispute between News Corp and Perplexity AI highlights a fundamental shift in how artificial intelligence interacts with digital information. By summarizing third-party content to provide direct answers, these AI search tools bypass traditional web navigation. This trend threatens the economic sustainability of content creators, as reduced user traffic undermines revenue models reliant on advertising and subscriptions, challenging the existing infrastructure of the internet. Regulatory and legal responses struggle to balance copyright protection with open access. Current laws often favor transformative uses, making it difficult for creators to prove infringement, while government interventions risk benefiting only major media outlets and potentially restricting the principle of linking. Although some tech companies are negotiating commercial agreements with large publishers, these efforts leave independent creators without a fair mechanism for compensation, creating an uneven playing field. This case is crucial for open data because it questions the ethical and legal frameworks governing data extraction and usage. If AI systems continue to monetize public information without compensating the original sources, it could deter the creation of open, high-quality datasets. Establishing a just attribution and reward system is essential to ensure that the evolution of AI supports, rather than erodes, the open ecosystem that relies on shared and accessible information.
