RTI Workshop in Srinagar Highlights Vital Role of Transparency in Jammu Kashmir

RTI Workshop in Srinagar Highlights Vital Role of Transparency in Jammu Kashmir

This workshop underscores how transparency acts as a fundamental pillar for democratic governance and civic dignity in Jammu and Kashmir. By linking the Right to Information directly to the right to life, the event highlighted that access to data is not merely administrative but essential for enabling citizens to hold authorities accountable and ensure systemic efficiency. The narrative emphasizes that active public participation is crucial for the effective implementation of information laws. Stakeholders argued that without robust engagement from youth and civil society, transparency mechanisms remain theoretical. True empowerment occurs only when citizens actively utilize these tools to challenge authority and demand justice, transforming passive rights into practical leverage against systemic inefficiencies. This discussion is vital to open data discourse as it illustrates the socio-political necessity of accessible government information. It demonstrates that data transparency is not an abstract technical goal but a concrete instrument for human rights protection and democratic resilience. The event reinforces the idea that open data initiatives must be culturally and politically supported to drive real-world accountability and protect civic freedoms.
