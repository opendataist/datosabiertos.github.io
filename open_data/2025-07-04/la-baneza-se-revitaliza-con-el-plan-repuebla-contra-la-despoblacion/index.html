La Bañeza se revitaliza con el plan Repuebla contra la despoblación

The Repuebla León Sostenible project aims to halt rural depopulation by revitalizing services and creating opportunities for settlement. This initiative highlights how open data initiatives, such as tracking municipal registry trends, provide essential insights for designing effective demographic strategies. Stakeholders emphasize the need for interadministrative collaboration to ensure territorial balance beyond single municipalities. By leveraging accurate population metrics, local governments can better allocate resources to areas facing decline, ensuring that policy interventions are responsive to real-time community needs. Ultimately, this program demonstrates the critical role of transparent, accessible data in urban planning. It underscores how informed decision-making drives sustainable development, offering a model for using evidence-based approaches to reverse rural exodus and foster long-term regional stability.
