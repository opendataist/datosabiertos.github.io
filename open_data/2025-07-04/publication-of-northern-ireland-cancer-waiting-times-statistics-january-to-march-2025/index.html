Publication of Northern Ireland Cancer Waiting Times Statistics: January to March 2025

Publication of Northern Ireland Cancer Waiting Times Statistics: January to March 2025

The publication of cancer waiting times in Northern Ireland highlights a significant transitional challenge in healthcare data integrity. The region has recently implemented the 'encompass' electronic patient record system across various health trusts, replacing legacy databases. Because the new data is not directly comparable to historical records, current statistics are classified as "official statistics in development." This distinction underscores the necessity for caution when interpreting recent trends, as the shift in data sources introduces structural inconsistencies that may skew longitudinal analyses of patient outcomes and service efficiency. Despite these data limitations, the reports indicate that performance metrics have fluctuated during this transition period. While the percentage of patients starting definitive treatment within the 31-day target improved slightly, the regional target was still missed. Similarly, urgent referral waiting times for both general suspect cancer and specific breast cancer cases showed declines in compliance against strict timeframes. These figures suggest that while the new system captures meaningful data, the ongoing integration process is impacting the ability to consistently meet established service standards, reflecting broader operational adjustments required during digital infrastructure upgrades. This article is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the complexities of maintaining high-quality public information during major system migrations. It demonstrates how the definition of "official statistics" evolves alongside technical changes, affecting transparency and comparability. For open data practitioners, this case serves as a critical reminder to document metadata changes rigorously and to clearly communicate data limitations to users. Understanding the context behind data sources is essential for accurate public understanding and for ensuring that policy decisions based on these statistics are informed by the true state of service delivery rather than artifact-driven fluctuations.
