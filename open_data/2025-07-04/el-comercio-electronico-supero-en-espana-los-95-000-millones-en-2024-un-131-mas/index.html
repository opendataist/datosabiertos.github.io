Spanish e-commerce experienced robust growth in 2024, driven largely by international transactions. While domestic spending remained relatively stable, a significant surge in purchases from Spain to foreign markets created a substantial trade deficit, highlighting a strong consumer preference for overseas goods. The tourism sector emerged as a dominant force, particularly for cross-border shopping, whereas domestic transactions were led by public administration services.
