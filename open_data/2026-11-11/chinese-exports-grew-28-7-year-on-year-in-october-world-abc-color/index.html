Chinese exports grew 28.7% year-on-year in October - World - ABC Color

Chinese exports grew 28.7% year-on-year in October - World - ABC Color

China is strengthening its position as a global economic engine through a robust boost in its foreign sales. This external dynamism offsets weak domestic demand, cementing international trade as the primary driver of growth for the world’s second-largest economy, regardless of fluctuations in the local currency. Imports are growing at an accelerated pace, even surpassing exports in the annual cumulative total. This trend indicates strong domestic demand for foreign goods, dispelling fears of stagnation and demonstrating that trade openness remains a fundamental pillar for the Asian country’s economic stability. This scenario is relevant to open data because it validates the importance of open and comparable macroeconomic indicators. Transparency in trade figures enables researchers and citizens to analyze the true state of the economy, fostering accountability and facilitating evidence-based decision-making using verifiable data that is publicly accessible.
