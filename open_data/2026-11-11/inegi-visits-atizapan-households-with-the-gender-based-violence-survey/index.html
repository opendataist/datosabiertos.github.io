INEGI visits Atizapán households with the gender-based violence survey

INEGI visits Atizapán households with the gender-based violence survey

INEGI is conducting the 2026 National Survey on the Dynamics of Relationships between Women and Men (ENDIREH) in Atizapán de Zaragoza to collect data on violence experienced by women. This initiative aims to capture recent and past experiences across various domains, prioritizing the confidentiality and safety of interviewees through strict protocols and identity verification. Analysis of these interactions will reveal updated trends regarding psychological, physical, and sexual aggression. Comparative results will enable an assessment of changes in the incidence of violence compared to previous editions, providing an accurate picture of the local and state-level sociodemographic reality. This research is crucial for open data, as it transforms private testimonies into verifiable public statistics. By democratizing information on gender-based violence, it facilitates transparency and grounds public policies in evidence. Open data promotes accountability and the design of more effective prevention strategies tailored to the actual needs of the female population.
