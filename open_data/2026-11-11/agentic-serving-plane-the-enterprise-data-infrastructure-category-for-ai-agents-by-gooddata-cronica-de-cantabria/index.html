Agentic Serving Plane, the enterprise data infrastructure category for AI agents by GoodData - Crónica de Cantabria

Agentic Serving Plane, the enterprise data infrastructure category for AI agents by GoodData - Crónica de Cantabria

GoodData.AI has launched Agentic Serving Plane, a governed execution layer specifically designed to manage the high concurrency loads and continuous contextual demand generated by AI agents. This solution acts as an intermediary between enterprise data platforms and autonomous applications or assistants, providing semantics, policies, and context without requiring any restructuring of existing infrastructure. By doing so, it enables AI systems to access data while maintaining integrity and control—something traditional architectures, optimized for sporadic human queries, could not efficiently provide. The significance of this advancement for the open data movement lies in its technical and conceptual approach. By leveraging standard technologies such as Apache Iceberg and Apache Arrow, the platform fosters interoperability and avoids vendor lock-in, which are core principles of the open data philosophy. Furthermore, by decoupling business logic and governance from the execution layer, it ensures that enterprise definitions and permissions remain consistent and secure, regardless of where the underlying data resides. This democratizes access to high-quality data for various agents and applications, promoting a more transparent and interconnected ecosystem. As a result, organizations can unify data consumption for both humans and automated systems under a single control layer. This reduces duplicated efforts in building infrastructure for each new use case and shortens implementation cycles. Consolidating analytics and governance services enables companies to scale their AI initiatives more agilely, ensuring that artificial intelligence operates within a governed environment that preserves the coherence of corporate data assets.
