Proposed changes to U.S. census could impact NC

Proposed changes to U.S. census could impact NC

Proposed changes to the 2030 Census threaten critical demographic data collection, potentially violating constitutional mandates and altering political representation. Excluding certain non-citizens risks undercounting populations, which directly impacts how legislative seats are allocated among states. This shift raises significant concerns for data equity, as marginalized groups may be systematically underrepresented in official statistics. The removal of race and ethnicity questions further obscures essential social insights needed for fair resource distribution and policy-making. These proposals are vital to open data because they challenge the completeness and accuracy of public datasets. Ensuring comprehensive, inclusive data collection remains fundamental for transparent governance and equitable societal planning.
