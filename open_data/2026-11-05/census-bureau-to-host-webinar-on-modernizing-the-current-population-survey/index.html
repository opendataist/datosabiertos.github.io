Census Bureau to Host Webinar on Modernizing the Current Population Survey

Census Bureau to Host Webinar on Modernizing the Current Population Survey

The U.S. Census Bureau is modernizing the Current Population Survey alongside the Bureau of Labor Statistics to address declining response rates and rising costs. This joint initiative, launched in 2023, seeks to ensure long-term data sustainability amidst growing logistical challenges. These efforts highlight the urgent need for open data frameworks that adapt to changing public engagement and technological landscapes. By updating foundational surveys, agencies can maintain high-quality, accessible statistics essential for informed public discourse and policy-making. Understanding such modernization trends is crucial for the open data community. It demonstrates how government entities are evolving their data collection methods to preserve transparency and reliability in an era of increasing privacy concerns and resource constraints.
