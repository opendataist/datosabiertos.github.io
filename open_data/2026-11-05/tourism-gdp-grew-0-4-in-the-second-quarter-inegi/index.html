Tourism GDP grew 0.4% in the second quarter: INEGI

Tourism GDP grew 0.4% in the second quarter: INEGI

The rebound in tourism GDP reflects a positive trend that is driving the Mexican economy. Although services still show some lag, the sector connects destinations and strengthens businesses, generating shared prosperity for communities. Tourism activity is directly linked to transparency and the development of evidence-based public policies. These data are crucial for open data initiatives, as they enable auditing the real impact of investments and measuring the effectiveness of government programs. Access to this statistical information facilitates accountability and citizen participation in tourism. By opening up these indicators, more efficient public management is promoted, allowing civil society to monitor and collaborate in ensuring the sustainability of the economic sector.
