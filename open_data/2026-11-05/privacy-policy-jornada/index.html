Privacy Policy - Jornada

Privacy Policy - Jornada

Jornada’s privacy policy demonstrates how it is possible to operate a digital media outlet while strictly respecting privacy and minimizing the collection of personal data to the greatest extent possible. By avoiding the use of tracking cookies, advertising, and user logs, the site prioritizes visitor anonymization. This practice aligns with the principles of data protection by design, reducing unnecessary digital footprints and limiting data processing to what is strictly functional for security and aggregated analysis. The relevance to the field of open data lies in the fact that this transparency regarding what data is collected, why, and how it is deleted establishes an ethical standard for digital information management. By not selling data to third parties or creating user profiles, the site challenges the economic model based on mass surveillance. This illustrates that sustainability and ethics on the web can coexist without relying on the exploitation of personal information, offering a viable example of data sovereignty. Finally, the article highlights the importance of the legitimacy of processing under the legitimate interest, clearly distinguishing between technical and communicative actions. By establishing automatic deletion deadlines and facilitating users’ rights, it fosters a culture of corporate responsibility. This approach not only complies with regulations such as the GDPR but also educates the audience about their right to privacy, demonstrating that personal data protection can be a competitive advantage and a clear public commitment.
