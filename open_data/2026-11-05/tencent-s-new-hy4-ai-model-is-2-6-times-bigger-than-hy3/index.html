Tencent's new Hy4 AI model Is 2.6 times bigger than Hy3

Tencent's new Hy4 AI model Is 2.6 times bigger than Hy3

Tencent’s Hy4 significantly expands open-weight capabilities by integrating advanced visual reasoning with large-scale language processing. This integration allows the model to interpret complex charts and perform mathematical reasoning, marking a notable evolution in accessible multimodal AI technologies. Although self-reported benchmarks suggest superiority over comparable models, Hy4 currently trails closed-source leaders in specialized tasks like advanced mathematics and long-horizon coding. This highlights the ongoing gap between open and proprietary systems, emphasizing the need for rigorous independent validation to accurately assess the maturity of open-source advancements. The release underscores the increasing accessibility of sophisticated AI tools for enterprise applications, particularly in office automation. By prioritizing security and offering scalable versions, Tencent contributes to a more robust open data ecosystem, enabling developers to build secure, efficient solutions without relying solely on exclusive, closed-source infrastructure.
