CCOO C-LM warns of rising unemployment and links it to the conclusion of agricultural campaigns

CCOO C-LM warns of rising unemployment and links it to the conclusion of agricultural campaigns

The rise in unemployment in Castilla-La Mancha is primarily attributed to the closure of agricultural campaigns and the slow regularization of foreign workers, which hinders their orderly integration into the labor market. Despite this seasonal increase, year-to-date cumulative hiring maintains significant levels, with a stable proportion of permanent contracts reflecting a certain structural stability in the regional labor market. The relevance to open data lies in the need for transparency and access to labor information that enables the analysis of these seasonal and structural trends. Trade unions argue that public data should serve as the basis for effective social dialogue addressing systemic issues such as housing and wages, ensuring a fair and equitable labor market.
