Sectur highlights that tourism GDP rose 0.8% in the second quarter

Sectur highlights that tourism GDP rose 0.8% in the second quarter

Mexico’s tourism sector recorded modest growth in the second quarter, lagging behind the overall pace of the national economy. Although the Secretariat of Tourism highlights positively the boost from domestic tourism, particularly during the World Cup, the data reveal a contraction in inbound consumption and in tourism services, suggesting structural challenges that extend beyond sporadic events. The relevance of this report for open data lies in the transparency and public access to detailed economic indicators provided by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). These open data enable researchers, analysts, and civil society to verify the accuracy of government claims, promoting accountability based on empirical evidence rather than solely on official narratives. Finally, the periodic publication of these indicators facilitates the identification of long-term trends, such as the current dependence on domestic tourism amid the decline of international tourism. This focus on accessible data is crucial for assessing the true economic impact and guiding informed public policies that address the disparities between the goods and services sectors within the national tourism industry.
