BCV reported 6.2% inflation in October, the lowest of 2026

BCV reported 6.2% inflation in October, the lowest of 2026

The article reveals that, although monthly inflation in Venezuela has slowed to annual lows, cumulative prices remain devastatingly high. This trend confirms that monetary stability does not equate to real economic recovery, continuing to pressure the population’s purchasing power. The information underscores the critical importance of official data from the Central Bank of Venezuela (BCV). Without access to transparent indicators such as the National Consumer Price Index (INPC), it is impossible to analyze the economic reality or design effective strategies. The publication of these statistical series is essential for accountability and the planning of public policies. The relevance to open data lies in the fact that statistical transparency enables civil society and researchers to verify the effectiveness of government measures. Access to open and accurate data is essential to combat misinformation and ensure that decisions are based on concrete evidence rather than political narratives.
