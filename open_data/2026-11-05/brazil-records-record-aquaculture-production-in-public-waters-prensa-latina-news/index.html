Brazil records record aquaculture production in public waters - Prensa Latina News

Brazil records record aquaculture production in public waters - Prensa Latina News

Aquaculture in Brazilian federal waters reached a historic production record, highlighting its capacity to generate significant revenue and employment. This growth confirms the effectiveness of utilizing public spaces such as rivers and reservoirs, demonstrating that state management can boost fish supply and stimulate regional development through government authorizations. The sector is consolidating itself as a viable economic driver, with tilapia dominating production and the Southeast region leading the activity. The expansion of use contracts reveals an ascending productive model, in which the participation of small and medium-sized producers—mostly men with secondary education—ensures sustainability and social inclusion within the aquaculture value chain. This news is relevant to open data because it illustrates transparency in the management of natural public resources. The availability of official figures on production, employment, and contracts enables the analysis of public policies, verification of equity in access to federal areas, and optimization of territorial planning, thereby fostering accountability and evidence-based development.
