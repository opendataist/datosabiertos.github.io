El coste laboral sube en el segundo trimestre un 5,8% y los salarios tocan récord en 23 años

The article highlights a significant increase in average labor costs in Spain, driven by higher salaries and non-wage expenses, with wages reaching historic highs. This trend reflects broader economic pressures on businesses and employees, indicating shifts in wage dynamics and working hours. For open data, this report underscores the importance of transparent labor statistics. Accessible, detailed data on costs and hours enables researchers and policymakers to accurately analyze employment trends, fostering informed decisions on economic policy and labor market reforms.
