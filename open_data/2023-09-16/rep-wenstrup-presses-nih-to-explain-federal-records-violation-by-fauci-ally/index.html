Rep. Wenstrup presses NIH to explain federal records violation by Fauci ally

Congressional investigators allege that a prominent NIH scientist bypassed federal transparency laws by using personal email and potentially deleting COVID-19 records. The National Archives intervened, requesting an inquiry into these actions, which raised serious concerns about the preservation of public health data and adherence to record retention statutes. The NIH conducted its own review and concluded that no federal records were illegally destroyed, a finding that has failed to satisfy lawmakers. Skepticism persists regarding the completeness and impartiality of this internal assessment, leading representatives to demand full access to the original evidence and investigative details to verify the agency’s conclusions. This situation highlights critical challenges in maintaining open data integrity within federal institutions. It underscores the necessity for robust oversight mechanisms to ensure that scientific records remain accessible and unaltered, preventing the erosion of public trust through opaque communication practices or insufficient accountability.
