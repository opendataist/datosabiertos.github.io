Freedom of Information Act

This article details the Illinois Eastern Community College’s protocol for responding to public records requests under the state’s Freedom of Information Act. It outlines the college’s commitment to transparency while balancing the legal requirement to protect individual privacy. By establishing clear guidelines for accessing government documents, the institution demonstrates how public entities can operate openly without infringing on personal data rights, a core principle in maintaining trust and accountability in public administration. The document provides specific instructions for citizens seeking information, including necessary contact details for the FOIA Officer and required elements for valid requests. It emphasizes that while some records are accessible online, others require formal written submission with precise descriptions of the desired materials. This structured approach ensures that requests are processed efficiently and in compliance with statutory timelines, highlighting the importance of proper documentation and adherence to established legal procedures when obtaining public data. This content is relevant to open data because it illustrates the intersection between traditional freedom of information laws and modern data accessibility. It shows how organizations manage the release of structured and unstructured records, from budgets to meeting minutes, ensuring they meet legal obligations. Understanding these mechanisms helps researchers and citizens identify how to legally acquire official records, promoting greater transparency and enabling better analysis of public sector operations through accessible information.
