‘RTI strengthening citizens-state ties’

‘RTI strengthening citizens-state ties’

The Chief Information Commissioner highlights how constitutional rights to information foster transparency and accountability, strengthening citizen-government relations. By empowering people to access data, informed decisions are made, enhancing democratic participation and trust in public institutions. This event underscores the global importance of universal information access, framing it as a tool for empowerment rather than just a legal formality. Educational initiatives like quiz contests effectively engage youth and media, demonstrating that learning mechanisms can significantly boost public awareness and community involvement in open governance practices. The Right of Access to Information Act serves as a landmark achievement for national transparency. Such frameworks are vital for open data ecosystems, as they legally mandate the release of public sector information, thereby enabling civil society to hold authorities accountable and drive societal prosperity through informed civic engagement.
