El municipio participará del panel en la Semana de Gobierno Abierto - El litoral

The Municipality of Corrientes will participate in Open Government Week, sharing insights on transparency and citizen engagement. By presenting their experience with the Local Innovation Network and the What Works Cities certification, they highlight successful local governance models. This event underscores the global importance of open data in fostering trust and improving public services. It demonstrates how municipalities leverage international frameworks to enhance accountability and encourage civic participation in policy-making processes. The relevance to open data lies in demonstrating practical applications of transparency standards. It shows how open information practices empower citizens and drive collaborative innovation within local government structures.
