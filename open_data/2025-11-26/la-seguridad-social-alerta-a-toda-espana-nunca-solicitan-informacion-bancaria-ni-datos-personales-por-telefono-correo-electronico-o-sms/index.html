La Seguridad Social alerta a toda España: nunca solicitan información bancaria ni datos personales por teléfono, correo electrónico o SMS

La Seguridad Social alerta a toda España: nunca solicitan información bancaria ni datos personales por teléfono, correo electrónico o SMS

The Spanish Social Security agency warns citizens to remain vigilant against rising phishing scams, emphasizing that official bodies never request sensitive banking details or personal data via phone, email, or SMS. This critical safety advice aims to protect individuals from fraudsters impersonating state authorities to steal identities and access financial accounts through deceptive links or urgent requests for codes. By clarifying that services are free and urging users to verify official web addresses, the guidance highlights the necessity of digital literacy and skepticism in online interactions. The article reinforces strict verification protocols, advising contact solely through authenticated channels to prevent data breaches and financial loss in an increasingly hostile digital environment. This content is relevant to open data because it underscores the fragility of public information infrastructure and the critical importance of protecting citizen privacy. It illustrates how open governmental communication channels can be exploited if users lack verification tools, highlighting the need for transparent, secure, and easily identifiable official data sources to maintain public trust and ensure the integrity of shared civic information.
