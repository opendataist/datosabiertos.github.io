Trump firma una orden ejecutiva que lanza una iniciativa de IA 'Genesis Mission' - El Diario NY

Trump firma una orden ejecutiva que lanza una iniciativa de IA 'Genesis Mission' - El Diario NY

The executive order issued by President Trump establishes the "Genesis Mission," an ambitious program to integrate artificial intelligence into national scientific research. This initiative aims to dramatically accelerate discovery and strengthen national security by providing widespread access to federal datasets and increasing computational capacity. By comparing this mission to the urgency of the Manhattan Project, it highlights the goal of profoundly transforming research and development processes, leveraging vast but previously underutilized government information. The relevance of this article to the field of open data lies in its direct focus on data infrastructure as a driver of innovation. The order explicitly identifies the need to centralize and organize the decentralized and disorganized data held by federal agencies. By facilitating information sharing and reducing barriers for industry partners to access these resources, the decree promotes a more collaborative and efficient model, essential for AI models to learn and accurately represent the real world. Finally, the plan prioritizes the creation of an integrated platform that combines computing resources and scientific data to automate workflows and test new hypotheses in critical areas such as biotechnology and nuclear energy. This implies that data management and access are not merely administrative tasks, but strategic components for technological advancement. By ensuring that researchers have access to the datasets needed to train models, the foundation is laid for open science and the responsible use of public information to drive solutions to nationally significant technological challenges.
