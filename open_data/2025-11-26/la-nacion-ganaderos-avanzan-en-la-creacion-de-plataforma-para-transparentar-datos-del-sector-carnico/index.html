La Nación / Ganaderos avanzan en la creación de plataforma para transparentar datos del sector cárnico

The Meat & Livestock Paraguay platform aims to modernize the meat sector by establishing a unified technical framework that ensures reliable and accessible data. It focuses on transparency regarding prices and commercialization trends, empowering producers and industry stakeholders to make informed decisions while addressing the gaps left by previous initiatives. This initiative is relevant to open data as it promotes structured, public access to critical market information, facilitating better policy-making and strengthening public-private coordination.
