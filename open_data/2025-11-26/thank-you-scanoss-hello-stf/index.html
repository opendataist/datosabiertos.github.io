Thank you SCANOSS. Hello STF.

The author transitions from advising SCANOSS to leading support for the Software Transparency Foundation. This shift strengthens the open source compliance ecosystem by centralizing efforts to aid members and collaborative projects. The decision ensures organizational independence while maintaining long-standing professional relationships. This move highlights the growing importance of structured transparency in software development. By focusing on foundational infrastructure rather than individual company profiles, the initiative promotes broader industry standards. Such frameworks are essential for building trust in open source contributions and commercial alignment. For the open data community, this underscores the value of specialized stewardship in maintaining ecosystem integrity. Reliable compliance tools enable safer data sharing and reuse across organizations. Professionals seeking to align their practices with these emerging standards can leverage these centralized resources to enhance their operational transparency and community engagement.
