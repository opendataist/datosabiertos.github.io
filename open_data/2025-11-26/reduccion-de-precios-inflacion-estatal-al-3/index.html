Reducción de precios; inflación estatal al 3 %

Reducción de precios; inflación estatal al 3 %

Chiapas demonstrates favorable price stability by recording a decrease in its national consumer price index, contrasting with the broader national inflation context. This regional achievement highlights local economic resilience against widespread upward pressure, positioning the state among those with the lowest inflation rates nationwide. The analysis distinguishes between underlying inflation, which remains stable, and non-underlying inflation driven by energy tariff adjustments. While most areas face increases due to subsidized energy changes, Chiapas maintains a downward trend in core prices. This divergence suggests effective local management of basic product costs despite external fiscal shocks. This report is vital for open data initiatives as it showcases how granular, regional statistics can reveal nuances masked by national averages. By making these localized INPC trends accessible, policymakers and citizens can better understand economic disparities. It emphasizes the importance of transparent, accessible data in identifying successful regional strategies and informing more precise, effective economic decisions.
