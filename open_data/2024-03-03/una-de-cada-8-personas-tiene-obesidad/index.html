Una de cada 8 personas tiene obesidad

The article highlights the global urgency of addressing the double burden of malnutrition, characterized by the exponential rise in obesity and the persistence of undernutrition. The main conclusion is that public authorities, the private sector, and civil society must collaborate intensively to implement evidence-based policies, prioritizing prevention from childhood through adulthood through healthy eating and physical activity. It emphasizes the need to integrate obesity prevention into primary healthcare systems and to apply specific legislative measures, such as regulating food marketing, implementing fiscal policies, and ensuring clear labeling. These interventions aim to create environments that facilitate access to nutritious options and promote healthy lifestyle habits, recognizing that obesity is a complex chronic disease requiring coordinated, multisectoral responses through 2030. This news is relevant to the field of open data because it underscores the fundamental importance of transparency and public access to epidemiological data. The World Health Organization has published the complete dataset in its observatory, enabling researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze real trends. This open access to information is crucial for validating existing policies, ensuring accountability of the private sector, and designing effective and equitable health strategies based on verifiable facts.
