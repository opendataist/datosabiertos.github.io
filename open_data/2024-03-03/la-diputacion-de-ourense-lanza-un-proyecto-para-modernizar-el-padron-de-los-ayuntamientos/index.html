La Diputación de Ourense lanza un proyecto para modernizar el padrón de los ayuntamientos

The Provincial Council of Ourense is modernizing local registry systems to enhance data management. By separating personal information from territorial housing data, the initiative aims to create a robust, privacy-compliant database. This structural shift promotes transparency and improves the accuracy of open public records available to citizens and researchers. The project seeks European funding to facilitate real-time exchange with the National Institute of Statistics. This digital transformation ensures that municipal data is continuously updated, reducing delays and errors inherent in older manual processes. It demonstrates a scalable model for interoperability between local and national government entities. This case highlights the importance of open data in modernizing public administration. By standardizing data formats and enabling seamless digital integration, local governments can provide more reliable, accessible information. Such efforts are crucial for fostering trust, enabling better policy decisions, and supporting the broader ecosystem of transparent, machine-readable public data.
