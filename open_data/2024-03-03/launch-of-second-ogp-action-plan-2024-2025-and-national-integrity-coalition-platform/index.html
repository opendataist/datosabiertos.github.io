Launch of second OGP Action plan 2024-2025 and National Integrity Coalition Platform

Seychelles has launched its second Open Government Partnership National Action Plan, reinforcing its dedication to transparency, inclusivity, and participatory decision-making. By formalizing the National Integrity Coalition, the government aims to foster deeper collaboration among independent state institutions, civil society, and the private sector. This initiative serves as a structural mechanism to fortify democratic foundations, ensuring that governance reforms are coordinated effectively across various national bodies to safeguard constitutional integrity. The action plan highlights specific commitments including the continuation of fisheries transparency, digital transformation to bridge connectivity gaps, and policies for employee wellness and alcohol management. These areas reflect a strategic alignment with democratic principles and human rights, building upon the success of the first action plan which largely achieved its goals. The focus remains on finding innovative, sustainable solutions to societal challenges through multi-stakeholder engagement, ensuring that government services and regulatory frameworks become more responsive to citizen needs. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates because it institutionalizes mechanisms for greater public access and oversight. By establishing coalitions that prioritize civil society engagement and systemic reviews, the framework creates structured channels for accountability and information sharing. It demonstrates a tangible shift toward embedding open governance principles into national policy, suggesting that future data releases and public consultations will be more systematic, inclusive, and integrated into the core operations of independent oversight institutions.
