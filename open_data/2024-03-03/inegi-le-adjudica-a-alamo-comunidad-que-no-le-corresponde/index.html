INEGI le adjudica a Álamo comunidad que no le corresponde

A georeferencing error by INEGI mistakenly assigned the community of Las Cañas de Arriba to Álamo, preventing Castillo de Teayo from securing funds for public works. This administrative discrepancy highlights how inaccurate spatial data directly hinders local governance and resource allocation. The confusion stems from the close proximity of conurbated areas, where distinct municipalities share seemingly unified settlements divided only by natural boundaries. Although legal authority remains clear, the mismatch in the official database creates practical barriers for municipal operations. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the real-world consequences of flawed geospatial information. Accurate open data is essential for effective public administration, proving that precise coordinates are not just technical details but critical enablers of civic infrastructure and rights.
