Vigilancia a policías y servidores para evitar corrupción

Vigilancia a policías y servidores para evitar corrupción

Chiapas enforces transparency laws through collaborative oversight, ensuring that officials, such as police officers, adhere to legal standards. This approach preserves institutional credibility by promptly addressing isolated infractions across various departments, thereby reinforcing a state-wide commitment to openness. The initiative incorporates citizen feedback through the digital platform Jaguar Evalúa, which enables real-time reporting and monitors the performance of public servants. This mechanism empowers society to actively participate in accountability efforts, transforming transparency from a mere bureaucratic requirement into a shared public value. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible digital tools can enhance government accountability. By making performance data available and encouraging public scrutiny, it highlights the role of transparency technologies in building trust and preventing corruption through informed citizen engagement.
