ICIR tasks journalists on utilizing FOI Act - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

ICIR tasks journalists on utilizing FOI Act - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

The article highlights the Freedom of Information Act as a vital tool for enhancing investigative journalism and promoting transparent governance in Nigeria. Experts urge journalists to actively utilize FOI requests to uncover corruption and support public decision-making, arguing that media should lead enforcement efforts rather than relying solely on civil society organizations. This collective action is essential for reducing abuse of power and ensuring accountability within public institutions. However, significant implementation challenges persist, including cumbersome bureaucratic processes and a lack of compliance mechanisms for government agencies. While the Act mandates quick responses, it also defines specific exemptions to protect sensitive information. Critics note that insufficient structures and competencies within Ministries, Departments, and Agencies often hinder effective disclosure, leaving many requests unaddressed or denied without clear consequences for non-compliance. This content is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the legal and structural barriers preventing the systematic release of public information. It illustrates that mere legislative existence is insufficient without active enforcement, standardized templates, and accountability measures to ensure data availability. Understanding these gaps is crucial for advocates working to improve data transparency, as it reveals the need for stronger institutional frameworks and proactive disclosure policies to truly realize the benefits of open governance.
