LARRY WARD: AI has severe TDS, but President Trump can cure it

LARRY WARD: AI has severe TDS, but President Trump can cure it

This article argues that current artificial intelligence models exhibit systematic political bias, frequently attributing negative events to political figures like Donald Trump while ignoring factual causality. The author contends that these systems absorb the ideological framing of their training data, which predominantly consists of left-leaning media sources, resulting in outputs that present subjective political narratives as objective facts. This distortion undermines the perceived neutrality of AI, creating a digital echo chamber that alienates conservative users and compromises the credibility of AI-driven information across research, journalism, and business. The text highlights the danger of this bias being laundered through the appearance of technological objectivity. Unlike human punditry, which is recognized as opinion, AI-generated claims carry a false authority derived from algorithms, potentially spreading distorted narratives more effectively. The author warns that this trend threatens the entire AI revolution by making the technology appear as a tool for partisan manipulation rather than a neutral resource, thereby eroding trust among significant portions of the population who view these outputs as evidence of institutional hostility. Relevant to open_data, the article critiques the lack of diverse, balanced datasets in AI training processes, suggesting that exclusive reliance on specific media sources creates skewed models. It proposes a market-based solution where federal mandates require equal compensation for conservative and liberal publishers, arguing that financial incentives would force tech companies to incorporate a wider political spectrum into their data. The core implication is that true data neutrality requires not just open access to information, but equitable economic structures that prevent the systemic exclusion of opposing viewpoints from the foundational datasets used to build artificial intelligence.
