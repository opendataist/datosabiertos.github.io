Políticas y Privacidad de Protección de Datos Personales | Diario Jornada

Políticas y Privacidad de Protección de Datos Personales | Diario Jornada

This article establishes the legal framework governing how Diario JORNADA collects, uses, and protects personal information in compliance with Peruvian data protection laws. The publication emphasizes its commitment to transparency by clearly defining the scope of data collected from user interactions, such as subscriptions and contact forms, and outlining the specific purposes for which this information is processed. The core implication for users is the reinforcement of individual rights regarding their data. The document details how subscribers can access, correct, or delete their personal information, ensuring they maintain control over their digital footprint. It also highlights the publication’s obligation to implement necessary security measures to prevent unauthorized access or loss, while allowing data transfers only when strictly required to fulfill these stated purposes. This text is relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the boundary between open information and protected personal privacy. While open data promotes transparency and accessibility of non-sensitive public records, this policy underscores the critical necessity of anonymization and consent for personal data. It serves as a reminder that ethical data practices must prioritize individual rights and legal compliance, ensuring that the pursuit of open data does not infringe upon personal security or privacy rights.
