Cifras cruzadas: la inversión pública en 2026 no crecería como dice Minhacienda

Cifras cruzadas: la inversión pública en 2026 no crecería como dice Minhacienda

The article reveals a significant inconsistency in the Colombian government’s narrative regarding the General National Budget for 2026. Although the Ministry of Finance claimed that public investment would grow, the actual figures show a reduction compared to the effective levels of 2025, suggesting that the announcement is based on comparisons with previously cut amounts. This technical discrepancy calls into question the transparency of official data and raises doubts about whether it constitutes an error or manipulation intended to conceal the real decline in investment effort. Beyond investment, the analysis highlights a structural shift in public finances, where operating expenditures have absorbed nearly all budgetary growth during the current administration. While resources for administrative operations and personnel have increased disproportionately, productive investment has lost both its share and purchasing power. This phenomenon indicates a bureaucratization of the state that prioritizes operational maintenance over long-term economic development, leading to inefficiency and reducing the country’s capacity to address social demands and external shocks. This information is crucial for the open data movement, as it demonstrates the critical need to analyze raw public data rather than relying uncritically on official press releases. True transparency enables the detection of how comparison baselines are defined and reveals that spending growth does not translate into better outcomes, but rather into increasing debt. Citizens and analysts must have access to detailed, up-to-date data to assess fiscal efficiency and demand structural reforms that prioritize investment over bureaucracy.
