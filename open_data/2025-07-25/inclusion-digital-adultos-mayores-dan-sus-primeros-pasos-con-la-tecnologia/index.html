Inclusión digital: adultos mayores dan sus primeros pasos con la tecnología

Inclusión digital: adultos mayores dan sus primeros pasos con la tecnología

Claro is driving digital inclusion in Ecuador through community-based training, emphasizing that technology transforms lives by connecting people regardless of age. This effort aims to reduce digital illiteracy, which affects a significant portion of the population, by promoting equitable access and the development of essential skills. The initiative encourages the responsible use of technology and the development of educational competencies, providing basic tools such as video calls and screen accessibility features. By addressing this digital divide, it ensures that vulnerable communities actively participate in the digital society. This action is relevant to open data because connectivity and digital literacy are fundamental prerequisites for citizen participation in transparency and access to public information. Without these capabilities, the utility of open data remains limited for excluded segments of society.
