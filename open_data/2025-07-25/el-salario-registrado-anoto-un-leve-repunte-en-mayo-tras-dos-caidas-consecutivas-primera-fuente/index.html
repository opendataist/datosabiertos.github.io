El salario registrado anotó un leve repunte en mayo tras dos caídas consecutivas - Primera Fuente

El salario registrado anotó un leve repunte en mayo tras dos caídas consecutivas - Primera Fuente

Salaries rebounded in May, outpacing inflation, yet real purchasing power continues to decline significantly compared to late 2023. The effective wage index fell due to reduced working hours and wage freezes, highlighting the procyclical nature of earnings. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the necessity of integrating multiple datasets, such as those from the formal and informal sectors. Transparent, granular labor statistics allow analysts to accurately assess economic health beyond headline inflation figures, revealing hidden trends in worker welfare.
