INEGI asume funciones del Coneval y empezará mediciones sociales

INEGI asume funciones del Coneval y empezará mediciones sociales

The INEGI has officially assumed Coneval’s responsibilities for measuring poverty and evaluating social development policies, ensuring methodological continuity to preserve data comparability since 2008. This transition maintains rigorous, transparent statistical standards critical for informed public policy decisions. By keeping historical archives accessible to governments, academics, and civil society, the INEGI safeguards the integrity of long-term social indicators. This open access allows diverse stakeholders to analyze trends in income and labor poverty without disruption. This shift is vital for open data as it demonstrates how institutional changes can either risk or reinforce data continuity. It highlights the importance of maintaining standardized, publicly available metrics to support evidence-based strategies against inequality and exclusion.
