En México hay más de 7 millones de establecimientos: Inegi

En México hay más de 7 millones de establecimientos: Inegi

The INEGI’s 2024 Economic Census reveals that Mexican businesses employ tens of millions of people, highlighting a private sector dominated by micro-enterprises. This vast network generates significant employment across goods, services, and commerce, with the majority of establishments concentrated in urban centers such as Mexico City. The data shows consistent annual growth in economic units over fifteen years, driven primarily by small retail and beauty services, while the financial sector leads in value-added contribution. Gender distribution also varies significantly by industry, with women dominating certain financial roles. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how rigorous, comprehensive national statistics provide the foundational transparency required for informed economic analysis. By making such detailed insights accessible, INEGI enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to understand market dynamics and workforce structures. This openness fosters accountability and supports evidence-based decision-making, demonstrating how high-quality public data can illuminate the complex realities of a nation’s economic ecosystem without relying on opaque or restricted information sources.
