Anulación de pasaportes como castigo al activismo crítico en Venezuela: Periodistas y defensores de ddhh son los más afectados | NTN24.COM

Anulación de pasaportes como castigo al activismo crítico en Venezuela: Periodistas y defensores de ddhh son los más afectados | NTN24.COM

The cancellation of Venezuelan passports for critics constitutes a systematic repression tactic aimed at restricting freedom of movement and silencing dissent. This arbitrary practice, often carried out without prior notice, violates due process rights and serves as a punitive measure to prevent activists, journalists, and politicians from fleeing persecution. The lack of transparency and explanations in these cases underscores a fundamental failure in procedural fairness. By depriving individuals of the ability to defend themselves against such severe administrative measures, the state intensifies the suppression of civil society during a politically critical period. This situation is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how opaque government data practices can facilitate human rights abuses. Ensuring transparency in administrative records and safeguarding the integrity of public data are essential safeguards against authoritarian control, enabling civil society to effectively monitor and challenge unlawful state actions.
