Realizan Quinto Foro Anual de Transparencia

Realizan Quinto Foro Anual de Transparencia

The recent Transparency Forum in Ciudad Juárez highlights the critical balance between social media engagement and personal data privacy. Experts warned that while digital platforms offer seemingly free services, they exploit user data, necessitating robust protections for citizens' rights in the digital age. Access to public information was emphasized as a fundamental mechanism for meaningful civic participation. By guaranteeing transparency, institutions empower citizens to engage effectively with governance, ensuring that the right to know is not hindered by bureaucratic obstacles but serves as a tool for accountability and community benefit. This event is relevant to open data because it underscores the ethical responsibility of public bodies to handle information responsibly. It illustrates the evolving intersection between transparency initiatives and data protection, showing that true openness requires safeguarding individual privacy while promoting citizen involvement in democratic processes.
