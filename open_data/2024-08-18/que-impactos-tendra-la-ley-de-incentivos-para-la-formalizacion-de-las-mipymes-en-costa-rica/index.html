¿Qué impactos tendrá la Ley de incentivos para la formalización de las mipymes en Costa Rica?

¿Qué impactos tendrá la Ley de incentivos para la formalización de las mipymes en Costa Rica?

Costa Rica has approved a tax incentive law designed to facilitate the formalization of micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs). The main objective is to reduce the high rate of informality, which limits access to credit and social security, by creating a more favorable institutional environment. Through the simplification of procedures and the implementation of a simplified tax regime for microenterprises, the law aims to eliminate bureaucratic barriers that prevent these businesses from operating legally and contributing fully to the national economy. The regulation introduces strategic incentives to foster innovation and human capital development, allowing tax deductions for expenses related to research, development, and training. Additionally, it promotes productive linkages between MSMEs and large companies through tax benefits, seeking to integrate small businesses into more robust value chains. This approach recognizes that business strengthening depends not only on market access but also on technological improvement and the upskilling of employees, which are key elements for competitive sustainability. This article is relevant to open data because the strengthening of Costa Rica’s Business Information System (SIEC) involves the digitalization and centralization of critical data on the productive sector. By improving the technological platform for registration, it facilitates the availability, interoperability, and transparency of business information. This enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to access reliable data on the economic structure, supporting evidence-based decision-making and the analysis of the real impact of public policies on economic growth and labor formalization.
