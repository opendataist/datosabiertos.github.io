Cerca de un tercio de la población de Perú es menor de edad

The INEI report offers crucial demographic insights into Peru’s youth population, highlighting significant disparities in internet access between urban and rural areas. This data underscores the digital divide, a key challenge for achieving equitable open data initiatives that must account for varying levels of connectivity among children and adolescents. High rates of school attendance and possession of identification documents indicate strong institutional engagement with this demographic segment. These statistics reveal a solid foundation for public services, yet they also point to the need for inclusive policies that bridge the remaining gaps in health insurance coverage and educational continuity. Understanding these socio-demographic trends is vital for open data advocates aiming to design effective, youth-centric policies. By analyzing such granular household and educational metrics, researchers can better identify vulnerable groups and improve the transparency and accountability of state interventions for the next generation.
