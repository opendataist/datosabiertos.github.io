¡Cuidado! Falsos censistas solicitan datos personales a través de WhatsApp y visitas domiciliarias - El Tiempo

¡Cuidado! Falsos censistas solicitan datos personales a través de WhatsApp y visitas domiciliarias - El Tiempo

The National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) clarifies that any unauthorized requests for biometric data or additional personal information during censuses constitute fraudulent scams. Official census takers collect only the data specified in their designated questionnaire and must display verified credentials, thereby ensuring public safety and data integrity. This warning underscores the critical intersection between open data governance and cybersecurity. By exposing tactics used to steal sensitive information, the INEI emphasizes the importance of verifying data sources. This helps protect citizens from identity theft and maintains trust in official statistical processes. The relevance to open data lies in establishing secure and transparent protocols for the collection of public information. The INEI’s emphasis on QR code verification and official channels demonstrates how to safeguard data privacy while promoting accessibility. This approach ensures that open data initiatives remain trustworthy and resistant to malicious exploitation.
