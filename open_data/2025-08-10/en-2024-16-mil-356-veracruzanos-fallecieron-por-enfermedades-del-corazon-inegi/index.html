En 2024, 16 mil 356 veracruzanos fallecieron por enfermedades del corazón: INEGI

En 2024, 16 mil 356 veracruzanos fallecieron por enfermedades del corazón: INEGI

INEGI’s final mortality statistics show that heart diseases remain the leading cause of death in Veracruz, surpassing diabetes and tumors. This data highlights a persistent public health crisis, with cardiac issues claiming lives in numbers nearly identical to the previous year, despite minor fluctuations. These definitive records underscore the critical role of open data in tracking national health trends. By making such detailed demographic information publicly accessible, policymakers and researchers can accurately assess the burden of chronic conditions and identify patterns over time. The availability of this structured, official statistics enables evidence-based decision-making. Open access to mortality metrics allows society to scrutinize healthcare outcomes, fostering transparency and supporting initiatives aimed at reducing preventable deaths related to major diseases.
