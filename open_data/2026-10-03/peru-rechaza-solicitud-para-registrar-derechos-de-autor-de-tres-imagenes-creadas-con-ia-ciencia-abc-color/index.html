Perú rechaza solicitud para registrar derechos de autor de tres imágenes creadas con IA - Ciencia - ABC Color

Perú rechaza solicitud para registrar derechos de autor de tres imágenes creadas con IA - Ciencia - ABC Color

INDECOPI rejected the registration of AI-generated images due to the lack of evidence demonstrating human creative intervention. This decision reaffirms that copyright protection applies only to personal intellectual creations, excluding outputs produced by automated systems. The ruling emphasizes the importance of proving substantial and personal creative control over the final product. Without verifiable proof of human involvement, works generated entirely by algorithms are not entitled to legal protection, thereby prioritizing individual authorship. This case is significant for open data, as it establishes a clear precedent regarding the intellectual property rights of synthetic content. By defining strict boundaries between human authorship and machine-generated output, it provides guidance on how to manage and license data or digital assets in artificial intelligence environments.
