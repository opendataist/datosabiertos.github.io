SLP se consolida en el noveno lugar nacional en exportaciones impulsado por el dinamismo industrial - La Jornada San Luis

San Luis Potosí consolidates its competitive position by ranking ninth nationally in export volume, driven by productive development strategies and a favorable investment environment. This performance is almost entirely based on the manufacturing sector, with notable strength in strategic subsectors such as transportation equipment and electrical accessories, reflecting a solid and resilient industrial specialization. This achievement is significant for open_data, as it demonstrates how official statistics enable the measurement and validation of the impact of public policies, facilitating transparency and evidence-based analysis of regional economic dynamics.
