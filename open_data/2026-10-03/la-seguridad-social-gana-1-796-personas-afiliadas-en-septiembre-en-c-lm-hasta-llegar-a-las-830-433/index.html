La Seguridad Social gana 1.796 personas afiliadas en septiembre en C-LM, hasta llegar a las 830.433

La Seguridad Social gana 1.796 personas afiliadas en septiembre en C-LM, hasta llegar a las 830.433

The article highlights that Social Security reached a record high in terms of affiliation, consolidating sustained growth for more than sixty consecutive months. This increase not only reflects a quantitative expansion of the labor market but also a significant improvement in the quality of employment, as evidenced by the sharp reduction in temporary contracts and the strengthening of permanent contracts, suggesting a positive and stable trend for the coming months. A central aspect is the diversification and democratization of the workforce. The system has massively integrated foreign workers, whose number exceeds 3.6 million and contributes notably to growth, especially through regularization processes. At the same time, there has been a decisive push for gender equality, with female employment growing at a faster rate than male employment, bringing the participation of both sexes closer to equilibrium and reducing the historical gap in the labor market. This information is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how public administrative statistics can transform the perception of socio-economic reality. By making accessible data disaggregated by gender, nationality, regime, and autonomous community, it facilitates critical analysis of public policies, allowing verification of the real impact of regulations and promoting transparency that empowers civil society and researchers in evaluating the efficiency of the State.
