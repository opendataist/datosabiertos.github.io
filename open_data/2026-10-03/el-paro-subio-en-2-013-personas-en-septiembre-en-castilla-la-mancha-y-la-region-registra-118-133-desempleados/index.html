El paro subió en 2.013 personas en septiembre en Castilla-La Mancha y la región registra 118.133 desempleados

El paro subió en 2.013 personas en septiembre en Castilla-La Mancha y la región registra 118.133 desempleados

Registered unemployment has experienced a significant rebound, marking the largest September increase on record. This trend is primarily due to an extraordinary regularization of workers, which has brought hundreds of thousands of people into the social security and unemployment benefit systems. While hiring remains stable, with temporary contracts predominating, the number of registered workers has grown as a direct effect of this policy promoting formal labor inclusion. The labor market structure shows a clear disparity across sectors and demographic groups. Although the construction sector has reduced its pool of unemployed individuals, the services sector and individuals without prior employment experience have seen notable increases. Moreover, youth unemployment has risen considerably, raising concerns about the difficulty of integrating those under 25 into the labor market. Meanwhile, women account for the largest share of the increase in absolute terms, consolidating their presence in the labor market but also in precarious employment conditions. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the challenge of interpreting public statistics without proper context. On their own, the numerical data suggest a deterioration in employment; however, when cross-referenced with information on regularization measures and legislative changes, a dynamic of formalization rather than job destruction emerges. This underscores the importance of accessing open data accompanied by explanatory metadata to avoid erroneous conclusions and foster a more accurate understanding of socioeconomic realities.
