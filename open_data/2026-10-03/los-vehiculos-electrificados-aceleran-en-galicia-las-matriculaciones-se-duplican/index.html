Los vehículos electrificados aceleran en Galicia: las matriculaciones se duplican

Los vehículos electrificados aceleran en Galicia: las matriculaciones se duplican

The sharp rise in prices of traditional fuels is accelerating the decline of diesel and gasoline vehicles in Galicia, simultaneously driving a significant modernization of the vehicle fleet toward more sustainable alternatives. This shift in trend is evident in the sustained increase in registrations of electrified cars, which have experienced a very notable year-on-year growth. While sales of conventional cars have declined considerably, zero-emission and plug-in hybrid options are gaining a substantial market share among regional consumers. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how vehicle registration data reflect macroeconomic and behavioral changes in real time. Analyzing these trends allows for the validation of public policies and an understanding of the effectiveness of fiscal incentives, demonstrating the utility of transparency and open statistical analysis for informed decision-making on mobility and the environment.
