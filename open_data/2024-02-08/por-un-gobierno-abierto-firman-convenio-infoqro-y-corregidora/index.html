Por un Gobierno abierto, firman convenio Infoqro y Corregidora

Corregidora and Infoqro signed an open government collaboration agreement to sustain the Women’s Artisan Corridor, prioritizing citizen participation and accountability over mere data release. This partnership ensures that public information facilitates informed decision-making, transforming transparency into a tool for collective governance and democratic engagement. The initiative highlights how accessible government data directly empowers communities, allowing over a thousand women to sustain their livelihoods through supported economic activities. By mandating transparency, the agreement enables citizens to actively monitor and shape local development, proving that open data is essential for effective civic involvement. This case demonstrates that open data is not just about publication, but about enabling meaningful public participation in local democracy. It shows how structured access to information fosters trust and accountability, ensuring that governance responds to citizens' needs. Consequently, this model serves as a vital example for other municipalities seeking to integrate transparency into social and economic programs.
