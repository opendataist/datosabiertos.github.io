Arab tourist arrivals in Brazil rebounds - Agência de Notícias Brasil-Árabe

Arab tourism to Brazil has recovered from 2022 levels, though it remains below pre-pandemic highs, with Morocco leading among Arab nations. This partial rebound mirrors the broader trend of foreign arrivals, indicating that while visitor numbers are rising, the industry has not yet fully returned to its 2019 baseline. The reliance on official government databases from agencies like the Federal Police and Embratur highlights the critical role of centralized public records in tracking international mobility. These datasets provide the necessary granularity to distinguish between specific nationalities, such as Moroccans or Lebanese citizens, against broader regional categories. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public sector statistics enable precise market analysis. By making tourism flow data accessible, open datasets allow researchers and businesses to monitor economic recovery trends and identify emerging source markets with greater accuracy and transparency.
