The 2024 Economic Census introduces critical new metrics on water sourcing, digital technology adoption, labor inclusion, and the use of recycled materials. These additions directly address contemporary challenges, including the lingering economic impacts of the pandemic, severe water crises, and the urgent need for environmental sustainability and social equity in business practices. By expanding data collection to hundreds of thousands of establishments, these censuses provide a granular view of how recent global and local shocks have reshaped commercial activities. The updated questionnaire ensures that policymakers and researchers capture accurate snapshots of operational realities, particularly regarding resource management and inclusive employment strategies that define modern economic resilience. This expansion is vital for open data initiatives, as it generates high-quality, granular datasets essential for transparent governance. By making this detailed economic and social information publicly accessible, stakeholders can drive evidence-based decision-making, fostering innovation and accountability in both public policy and private sector strategies.
