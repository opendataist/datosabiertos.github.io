La Junta Electoral de Bariloche convoca a autoridades de mesa para el Referéndum Municipal 2025

La Junta Electoral de Bariloche convoca a autoridades de mesa para el Referéndum Municipal 2025

The municipal electoral board invites citizens to participate in the referendum by serving as polling station officials. This initiative strengthens democratic engagement and civic commitment through active community involvement in local governance processes. Eligible residents can register online or in person to attend mandatory training sessions. Selected individuals will receive compensation for their service, ensuring adequate staffing across various locations in the city. This announcement is relevant to open data as it demonstrates public sector transparency and citizen accessibility. By providing clear registration pathways and publicizing opportunities for civic participation, local governments enhance trust and encourage broader data-driven democratic engagement.
