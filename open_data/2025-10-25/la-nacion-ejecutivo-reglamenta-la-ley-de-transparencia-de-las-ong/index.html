La Nación / Ejecutivo reglamenta la ley de transparencia de las ONG

Paraguay has enacted regulations to enforce transparency and accountability among non-profit organizations, mandating their official registration. This measure aims to improve coordination with the state and ensure responsible management of funds, particularly those linked to public services. By closing legal gaps, the government seeks to strengthen oversight over entities receiving public or private resources, thereby enhancing civic trust and institutional efficiency. The law explicitly excludes international bodies, political parties, and religious institutions from its scope, focusing strictly on domestic non-profits. This distinction clarifies regulatory boundaries while addressing societal concerns regarding financial oversight. The participatory drafting process involved key economic sectors to ensure a balanced approach that supports civil society operations without imposing undue burdens. This development is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as establishing a national registry creates a structured dataset on non-profit activities. Such transparency facilitates public access to information about organizational funding and operations, promoting greater civic engagement. It aligns with global standards for accountable governance, demonstrating how regulatory frameworks can leverage data openness to foster trust and monitor societal impact effectively.
