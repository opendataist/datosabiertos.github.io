Torreón renueva su portal de transparencia para acercar la información a los ciudadanos

Torreón has modernized its transparency portal to enhance accessibility and ensure strict compliance with legal standards. By replacing the old system with a more functional interface, the municipality aims for perfect publication effectiveness, guaranteeing seamless service continuity despite previous institutional changes. This strategic update supports the efficient handling of citizen requests and the comprehensive reporting of municipal actions, including social programs and infrastructure works. The upcoming mayoral report will integrate these results with recent security data, demonstrating improved public perception and transparent governance practices to all stakeholders. This initiative is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how technical infrastructure upgrades directly facilitate public access to government information. By prioritizing usability and legal compliance, the city ensures that data is not just published but is truly discoverable and understandable for citizens. This approach validates the open data principle that transparency requires active, user-centric design rather than mere passive publication.
