El nuevo Portal Estadístico del Notariado aporta certeza y fiabilidad a los datos con precios reales

El nuevo Portal Estadístico del Notariado aporta certeza y fiabilidad a los datos con precios reales

The new Notarial Statistical Portal represents a significant advance in real estate market transparency by democratizing access to reliable data on actual housing prices. By relying on real notarial deeds rather than speculative listings, the platform offers a visual and interactive tool that enables citizens, professionals, and institutions to accurately understand market dynamics. This initiative marks a paradigm shift, transforming information once reserved for administrative use into a public resource, thereby fostering informed decision-making in a sector vital to the public. Analysis of the data reveals a dual and complex reality in Spain, characterized by regional disparities in prices and demographic shifts among buyers. There is a notable increase in purchases by foreign buyers, while young people’s access to the housing market has drastically declined. These indicators, updated monthly, highlight specific tensions in major cities such as Madrid and Barcelona, underscoring the urgent need for public policies that improve transportation and mobilize public land to reduce housing costs. The portal’s relevance to open data lies in its ability to transform massive, anonymized administrative data into accessible and actionable knowledge. By providing detailed sociodemographic statistics, the platform facilitates academic research and evidence-based public policy design. This model of publishing notarial data demonstrates how digital transparency can empower civil society and enhance urban governance, setting a standard for how institutions can share their information for the common good.
