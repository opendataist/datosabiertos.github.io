Censo 2025: Ayacucho bordea el 90 % de avance, aunque el sur regional aún presenta zonas sin cobertura | Inforegión

Censo 2025: Ayacucho bordea el 90 % de avance, aunque el sur regional aún presenta zonas sin cobertura | Inforegión

The 2025 Census is nearing completion in Ayacucho, with targeted efforts to finalize registration in remote southern provinces by late October. This initiative aims to accurately map structural deficiencies, such as inadequate water and sanitation services, to highlight the stark inequalities between urban and rural areas. By employing satellite verification, the national statistics institute ensures data integrity, reevaluating declared vacant homes to guarantee no family is overlooked. This rigorous approach corrects previous undercounts, ensuring that every household is officially registered in the national database. This update is vital for open data as it provides the essential, granular metrics required to drive evidence-based public policy. Accurate data on poverty and infrastructure deficits enables governments to prioritize social investments effectively, ensuring resources reach the most vulnerable populations.
