No debe desaparecer el INAI, sería un retroceso: Blanca Lilia Ibarra

No debe desaparecer el INAI, sería un retroceso: Blanca Lilia Ibarra

The INAI commissioner stresses that institutional silence resulting from political gridlock does not absolve authorities of their legally mandated transparency obligations. This duty to respond to citizen requests and publish data ensures accountability and safeguards the public interest. The situation underscores the critical importance of independent oversight bodies in upholding open data standards and preventing impunity when governmental quorums fail.
