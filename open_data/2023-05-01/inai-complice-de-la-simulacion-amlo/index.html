Inai, cómplice de la simulación: AMLO

Mexican President Andrés Manuel López Obrador has called for the dissolution of the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), accusing the agency of complicity with conservative factions and corruption. He argues that eliminating the institute would save significant annual funds and transfer its functions to the Superior Audit Office, claiming that commissioners earn disproportionately high salaries that enable corrupt practices rather than ensuring transparency. This proposal is highly relevant to open data, as dismantling an independent agency dedicated to protecting personal data and guaranteeing access to public information would severely weaken the legal framework necessary for data transparency and citizen oversight in Mexico.
