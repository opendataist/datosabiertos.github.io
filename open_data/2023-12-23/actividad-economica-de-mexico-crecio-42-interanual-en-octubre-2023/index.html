Actividad económica de México creció 4,2% interanual en octubre 2023

Mexico’s economy showed robust annual growth in October, driven by gains across all major sectors, particularly agriculture and industry. This positive momentum contributed to a cumulative yearly increase, reinforcing the government’s projection of sustained expansion exceeding three percent for the year. Despite this strong annual performance, the economy experienced a slight monthly contraction in October, highlighting the volatility inherent in short-term economic indicators. This contrast underscores the importance of distinguishing between seasonal fluctuations and underlying long-term trends when assessing economic health. This data is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the value of transparent, high-frequency statistical releases from institutions such as INEGI. Open access to such granular, timely indicators allows analysts, researchers, and the public to independently verify economic narratives, fostering trust in official statistics and enabling more accurate global economic modeling.
