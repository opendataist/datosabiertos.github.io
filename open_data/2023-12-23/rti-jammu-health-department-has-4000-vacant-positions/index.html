RTI: Jammu Health Department Has 4,000 Vacant Positions

The article demonstrates how Right to Information laws expose systemic staffing gaps within public health services. By mandating transparency, activists uncover extensive vacancies across administrative and non-administrative roles. This highlights open data’s role in holding government bodies accountable for resource allocation. Without such disclosure mechanisms, critical operational deficiencies remain hidden from public scrutiny and policy intervention. Ultimately, this case underscores the necessity of accessible government records. Open data enables citizens to identify institutional weaknesses, fostering greater civic engagement and driving necessary reforms in public sector management and service delivery.
