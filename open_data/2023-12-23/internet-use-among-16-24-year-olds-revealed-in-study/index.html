Internet Use Among 16-24 Year-Olds Revealed in Study

This report highlights how internet usage patterns shift with age and employment status, challenging standard assumptions about digital benefits. The findings are relevant to open data as they demonstrate how granular, demographic-specific statistics reveal hidden behavioral nuances, such as boredom as a primary motivator for youth. Such insights emphasize that open data initiatives must prioritize diverse, detailed datasets to capture these variations. By making such specific information accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand the true societal impact of digital access, moving beyond generic metrics to address distinct group needs effectively.
