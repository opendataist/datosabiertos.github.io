Cofepris debe informar sobre acciones para reducir grasas trans: INAI

The INAI mandated Cofepris to disclose its strategies for reducing trans fats, ensuring accountability in public health compliance. This transparency addresses Mexico’s critical obesity crisis and rising cardiovascular mortality rates. Revealing enforcement actions against non-compliant entities highlights the government's commitment to international health standards. It demonstrates how open data mechanisms can track regulatory implementation and protect citizen well-being from preventable diseases. This case underscores open data's relevance by linking transparency to tangible health outcomes. It shows how accessing government records helps the public understand and monitor essential sanitary policies, fostering trust and enabling informed civic engagement in health issues.
