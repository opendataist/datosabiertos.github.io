OSCE Empowers Ukrainian Start-ups in Open Data Shift

OSCE Empowers Ukrainian Start-ups in Open Data Shift

The OSCE, in partnership with Ukraine’s Ministry of Digital Transformation, recently concluded the Open Data Unbroken 2.0 accelerator programme. This initiative demonstrates how structured support can empower local developers to transform publicly available information into scalable solutions. By bridging the gap between raw data and practical application, the project fosters a more robust investment climate and encourages economic recovery amidst ongoing challenges. Central to this effort is the use of open data as a strategic economic asset rather than merely a transparency tool. Two selected startups illustrate this impact: one creates regional investment indicators to aid evidence-based policymaking, while the other enhances accountability in public procurement through rigorous data analysis. These solutions directly reduce investment risks, combat corruption, and build essential trust within Ukraine’s economic system. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it proves that accessible information is foundational to sustainable reconstruction and good governance. By turning government data into actionable insights, the programme showcases how transparency drives investor confidence and economic resilience. Ultimately, it highlights a replicable model where open data serves as the backbone for democratic stability and inclusive growth.
