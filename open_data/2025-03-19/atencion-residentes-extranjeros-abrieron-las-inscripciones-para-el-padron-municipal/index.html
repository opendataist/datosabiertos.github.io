Atención residentes extranjeros: abrieron las inscripciones para el padrón municipal

The municipal electoral board opened registration for foreign residents, establishing a transparent process for verifying habitual domicile through public documents. This initiative enhances administrative accessibility by allowing sworn declarations when standard proofs are unavailable. Such procedures significantly advance open data goals by ensuring that civic participation mechanisms are transparent, accessible, and inclusive for all community members.
