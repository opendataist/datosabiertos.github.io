Air quality in Saltillo deteriorated due to wind-driven particulate matter, dispelling rumors that local wildfires were the source. Meteorological data confirmed that regional wind patterns, rather than nearby fires, were responsible for the pollution, highlighting the importance of accurate, localized environmental monitoring. This situation underscores the critical role of open data in ensuring public transparency and managing environmental health. When air quality metrics are accessible, authorities can effectively debunk misinformation and guide citizens during pollution events. Ultimately, reliable open data empowers communities to understand the true causes of environmental hazards. It fosters trust in institutional responses and supports informed decision-making regarding public health during periods of poor air quality.
