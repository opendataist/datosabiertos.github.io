India should take advantage of open-source AI model: Joel Kaplan of Meta

India should take advantage of open-source AI model: Joel Kaplan of Meta

Joel Kaplan, Meta’s new Chief Global Affairs Officer, advocates for open-source AI as the standard to ensure democratic values shape the global technological landscape, rather than ceding influence to authoritarian regimes. He argues that regulation should be risk-based rather than technology-based, balancing safety with innovation to prevent stifling progress. This perspective highlights the critical intersection of policy and technology, emphasizing that open frameworks are essential for fostering inclusive global standards and preventing monopolistic control over foundational technologies. Kaplan positions open-source models as a mechanism for democratizing access to AI, allowing diverse developers and governments to customize solutions for local needs without prohibitive costs. By citing the widespread adoption of Meta’s Llama models, he illustrates how shared investment benefits a broader community, supporting entrepreneurs in emerging markets like India. This approach underscores the importance of accessible technology in driving equitable growth, suggesting that proprietary restrictions could hinder the widespread adoption and localization of beneficial AI tools. The article is relevant to open data because it reinforces the principle that transparent, accessible, and modifiable technological assets drive innovation and trust. Kaplan’s emphasis on community-driven oversight and open collaboration mirrors the core tenets of open data initiatives, which rely on shared information to improve societal outcomes. By advocating for regulatory environments that support openness, Meta’s leadership highlights how open standards in AI and data can enhance consumer protection, encourage developer ecosystems, and create more resilient digital infrastructures globally.
