Política de privacidad

Política de privacidad

The article establishes a framework for how personal data is collected, processed, and protected by the website operator, emphasizing transparency regarding the types of information gathered, such as usage data and cookies. It outlines the legal bases for processing, ensuring users understand that their data may be used for service provision, analytics, and advertising, while highlighting the operator's responsibility to maintain security standards and define clear retention periods for stored information. Crucially, the document details the rights granted to users, allowing them to access, rectify, and delete their personal data, as well as restrict processing or request portability. These provisions are particularly robust for users subject to higher protection standards, such as those within the European Union, who benefit from explicit rights to object to commercial profiling and lodge complaints with supervisory authorities. This text is relevant to open data because it represents the critical counter-narrative of data privacy and individual control in the digital ecosystem. While open data advocates for the public accessibility of information for societal benefit, this document underscores the necessity of balancing transparency with user sovereignty. It illustrates the regulatory constraints and ethical obligations that data handlers must respect, reminding stakeholders that the open data movement must coexist with strict adherence to personal data protection laws to ensure trust and compliance.
