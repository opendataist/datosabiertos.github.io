La diputada del PP Noelia Núñez reconoce que no ha acabado sus estudios de Derecho

Noelia Núñez has acknowledged that her profile in Congress reflects an error, as she did not complete her law degree, clarifying that there was no intent to deceive. She argued that her intense political career prevented her from finishing her degree and proposed immediately correcting the mistake in the official records to avoid distractions from government management. This situation highlights the critical importance of accuracy in public data and institutional transparency. The precision of the academic and professional information of elected representatives is essential to maintain public trust and ensure that accountability is based on verifiable facts, not assumptions or partisan disputes. The case illustrates how inconsistencies in open data can lead to political controversies, underscoring the need for rigorous audits and clear update mechanisms. For open data, this reinforces the necessity of robust systems that validate information integrity, facilitating genuine transparency and preventing reputational crises stemming from administrative errors or omissions.
