Tomé califica de «inadmisible» el nombramiento del nuevo secretario general de Educación

Tomé califica de «inadmisible» el nombramiento del nuevo secretario general de Educación

A Spanish politician has condemned the appointment of a former high-ranking education official linked to the Marea corruption case, arguing that such individuals have lost their credibility. She has proposed reforming transparency laws to prevent the hiring of those previously censured by parliament, emphasizing that integrity is crucial for maintaining public trust. This issue highlights a significant gap in current governance structures, as the necessary oversight bodies for verifying responsibility declarations are not yet established. The debate underscores the urgent need for robust institutional mechanisms to enforce ethical standards in public administration. This case is vital for open data because it illustrates how transparent, accessible records of past misconduct are essential for preventing corruption. Without clear, open information on officials' histories, accountability mechanisms fail, demonstrating why open data is key to ensuring government transparency and public confidence.
