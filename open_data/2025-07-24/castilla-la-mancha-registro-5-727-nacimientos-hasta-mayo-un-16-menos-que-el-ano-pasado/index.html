Castilla-La Mancha registró 5.727 nacimientos hasta mayo, un 1,6% menos que el año pasado

Castilla-La Mancha registró 5.727 nacimientos hasta mayo, un 1,6% menos que el año pasado

Recent Spanish census data reveals a slight increase in births, but a significant long-term decline over the past decade. Notably, for the first time in months, births to mothers under 25 outnumbered those to mothers over 40, signaling shifting demographic trends. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights how publicly available statistics from the National Statistics Institute (INE) enable researchers and the public to track complex societal changes. Access to such granular, longitudinal data supports transparency and informed policy decisions regarding population dynamics. The availability of detailed records on both natality and mortality allows for accurate analysis of national health and social trends. Open access to these figures ensures that demographic shifts can be monitored objectively, fostering better public understanding and resource planning.
