Los hoteles de la Comunitat Valenciana inauguraron el verano batiendo un nuevo récord con 3,2 millones de pernoctaciones en junio

The Valencian Community’s tourism sector achieved unprecedented occupancy levels in June, marking a historic peak in both visitor arrivals and overnight stays. This surge signals a robust recovery and growing appeal, particularly driven by a significant increase in international visitors compared to domestic tourists. These results validate the sector’s resilience and its capacity to attract global demand, pointing to a strong upward trajectory for the region’s hospitality industry. The data highlight a clear shift toward greater internationalization, reinforcing the area’s status as a top-tier destination for foreign tourists. This case is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical releases empower stakeholders to analyze market trends. By making such detailed indicators publicly available, it facilitates evidence-based decision-making for policy and business strategies in the tourism sector.
