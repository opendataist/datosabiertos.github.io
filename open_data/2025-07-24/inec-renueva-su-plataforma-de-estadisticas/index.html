INEC renueva su plataforma de estadísticas

INEC renueva su plataforma de estadísticas

The National Institute of Statistics and Census (INEC) has launched two digital platforms, “Panamá en Cifras Digital” and “Panamá Logístico,” marking a significant step toward institutional transparency. By making extensive demographic, economic, and logistical data publicly accessible through interactive maps and downloadable formats, INEC aims to foster a culture of open data. This shift addresses previous navigational difficulties, ensuring that citizens and institutions can easily obtain the evidence required for informed decision-making. These initiatives prioritize the democratization of information, empowering both the public sector and private investors to base their strategies on verified statistics. By removing barriers to accessing detailed variables across various domains, the government encourages accountability and efficiency. Stakeholders can now evaluate investment opportunities or design public policies using rigorous, up-to-date metrics, thereby reducing reliance on intuition and promoting a more analytical approach to national development. This move is highly relevant to open data as it exemplifies the practical application of transparent, machine-readable, and interoperable information in governance. By facilitating the tracking of assets such as ports and ships, and by enabling fiscal oversight, the platforms demonstrate how accessible data enhances public trust and operational efficiency. Ultimately, this initiative underscores the critical role of open data in strengthening democratic institutions and supporting evidence-based policymaking for sustainable progress.
