El mejor junio de la serie histórica sitúa a Castilla-La Mancha como el destino que más crece en pernoctaciones hoteleras de todo el país

El mejor junio de la serie histórica sitúa a Castilla-La Mancha como el destino que más crece en pernoctaciones hoteleras de todo el país

Castilla-La Mancha has experienced exceptional growth in its hotel sector during June, reaching historic highs in both the number of travelers and overnight stays. This positive trend has not only consolidated the region’s best first half of the year but has also positioned it as the autonomous community with the highest year-on-year increase in hotel nights nationwide. The rise in tourist arrivals is twice the national average, highlighting a robust and sustained tourism recovery that surpasses pre-existing levels. Hotel profitability and occupancy rates reflect this upward trend, with performance indicators reaching historic peaks. Key provinces such as Albacete and Toledo are leading this success by setting absolute records in their tourism activity. The sector is facing accommodation supply levels that coincide with the highest occupancy rates recorded in June since 2014, suggesting an efficient response from local infrastructure to growing demand. This report is relevant to the field of open data because it illustrates the vitality of public data on economic and tourism trends. Transparency in the publication of statistics by organizations such as the National Statistics Institute (INE) enables researchers and citizens to analyze macroeconomic and regional trends. Access to this structured information facilitates the evaluation of the impact of tourism policies and the understanding of regional economic resilience, demonstrating how open data serves as a key tool for transparency and evidence-based development.
