Las pernoctaciones hoteleras en Castilla-La Mancha crecen un 15% en junio hasta las 423.821

Castilla-La Mancha saw significant growth in tourist arrivals and hotel prices in June, reflecting a positive trend in the sector despite having lower occupancy rates compared to other Spanish regions. This data highlights the dynamic nature of regional hospitality markets and their varying capacities to attract travelers. The relevance to open data lies in the need for transparent, accessible statistical records to monitor these economic shifts. By making such detailed tourism metrics publicly available, governments enable stakeholders to analyze trends, compare regional performance, and make informed decisions based on verified, granular information rather than aggregate summaries. Ultimately, this report underscores how open data facilitates accountability and strategic planning in the tourism industry. It allows researchers and businesses to understand market drivers, such as price fluctuations and patterns of foreign versus domestic visitors, fostering a more responsive and efficient economic ecosystem through shared, actionable insights.
