El Principado defiende su transparencia en la gestión de fondos europeos y tilda de «inadmisibles» las críticas del PP

El Principado defiende su transparencia en la gestión de fondos europeos y tilda de «inadmisibles» las críticas del PP

The Asturian government firmly rejects accusations of misrepresenting data on European recovery funds, asserting that its transparency portal accurately reflects actual execution figures. By distinguishing its accounting methodology from the state platform Elisa, authorities highlight that local records include critical expenditures such as mining restoration, which the federal tool omits due to different parameter structures. This clarification underscores that the region’s reported progress is genuine and aligns with central government transfers, refuting claims of concealed or delayed management. The political conflict reveals how varying metrics can distort public perception of policy success. When opposition parties utilize incompatible reporting systems that exclude specific types of investments, they create misleading narratives about administrative efficiency. This discrepancy demonstrates that open data initiatives are vulnerable to manipulation if stakeholders do not share standardized definitions for what constitutes "execution" or "progress." Without uniform standards, the same financial reality can be interpreted as either successful or failing depending on the chosen framework. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessity of semantic interoperability in public sector transparency. Merely publishing numbers is insufficient if the underlying data structures and categorizations are not universally understood. For open data to foster trust rather than confusion, governments must ensure that all platforms use consistent, interoperable definitions. Otherwise, political actors can exploit technical ambiguities to undermine confidence in institutional performance, demonstrating that data governance extends beyond mere availability to include critical standardization.
