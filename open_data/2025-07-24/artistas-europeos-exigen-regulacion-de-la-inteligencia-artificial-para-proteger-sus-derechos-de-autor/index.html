Artistas europeos exigen regulación de la inteligencia artificial para proteger sus derechos de autor

European musicians are uniting through the #StayTrueToTheAct campaign to demand strict regulation of artificial intelligence and robust protection of copyright laws. Their primary concern is that AI companies currently exploit protected creative works to train models without accountability, threatening the livelihoods and dignity of artists who rely on intellectual property rights. The core argument emphasizes that genuine creativity stems from deeply human emotions and experiences, which generative AI cannot authentically replicate or claim as its own. Artists warn that without enforcing legal consequences for unauthorized data usage, the innovative balance between technology and the creative market will collapse, undermining the cultural value of human-made art. This movement is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical tension between unrestricted data access for AI development and the ethical necessity of respecting creators’ rights. It underscores the importance of transparent data sourcing and consent mechanisms in open ecosystems, ensuring that open information policies do not inadvertently enable the exploitation of copyrighted material without fair compensation or authorization for the original authors.
