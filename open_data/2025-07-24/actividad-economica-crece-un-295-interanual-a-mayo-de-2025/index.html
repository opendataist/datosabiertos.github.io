Actividad económica crece un 2,95 % interanual a mayo de 2025

Actividad económica crece un 2,95 % interanual a mayo de 2025

Panama’s economic activity index shows robust growth driven by the transport, finance, and energy sectors. This positive trajectory highlights the resilience of key service industries, particularly benefiting from canal revenues and increased credit activity. Conversely, agricultural output and specific industrial metrics face declines, indicating a sectoral divergence in economic performance. The broader GDP growth in the previous year underscores the economy's reliance on commerce and internal consumption, despite challenges from major mining closures. Current forecasts predict continued expansion, outperforming both regional and global averages. This optimistic outlook suggests that structural strengths in logistics and services are effectively offsetting downturns in extractive and primary industries. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of transparent, high-frequency economic indicators in public discourse. By making such granular statistics accessible, stakeholders can better assess national progress and identify emerging trends. Open access to these metrics fosters accountability, enabling analysts and citizens to verify government predictions and understand the real-world impact of policy decisions on diverse economic sectors.
