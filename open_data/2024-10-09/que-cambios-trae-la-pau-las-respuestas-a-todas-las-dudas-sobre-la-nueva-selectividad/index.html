¿Qué cambios trae la PAU? Las respuestas a todas las dudas sobre la nueva Selectividad

¿Qué cambios trae la PAU? Las respuestas a todas las dudas sobre la nueva Selectividad

The new university entrance exam, PAU, represents a paradigm shift by prioritizing the assessment of competencies over rote memorization, requiring creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills in real-world contexts. This transformative approach seeks to verify not only acquired knowledge but also the academic maturity necessary to succeed in higher education, marking a step forward in educational modernization toward more holistic profiles adapted to current demands. The evaluation structure integrates open-ended and semi-constructed responses to minimize chance and ensure that students demonstrate a comprehensive mastery of the subject matter, preventing them from passing the exam by studying only fragments. By combining these elements with auxiliary tools when appropriate, the system fosters a fairer and more realistic assessment, aligned with contemporary professional skills that value the reasoning process as much as the final outcome. This reform is relevant to open data because the draft order has been published on the Transparency Portal, facilitating public auditing and the reuse of regulatory information. This commitment to transparency allows the educational community, researchers, and developers to freely access exam details, promoting institutional accountability and civic innovation through the analysis of evaluation criteria and their impact on educational equity.
