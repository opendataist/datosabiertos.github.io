Crecimiento demográfico récord en Reino Unido, potenciado por la inmigración

Crecimiento demográfico récord en Reino Unido, potenciado por la inmigración

The United Kingdom has reached a historic population growth driven primarily by net immigration, while deaths have exceeded births for the first time in decades. This inverse dynamic marks a demographic turning point that is reshaping the structure of British society and its future challenges. The political and social debate has intensified, particularly in the aftermath of Brexit, as it confronts the promise to reduce immigration with current statistical realities. Most new residents now come from outside the European Union, generating public tensions and necessitating a reassessment of migration and integration policies. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accessing and understanding transparent official statistics. The use of accurate data enables citizens to critically analyze government policies, fostering an informed debate on migration and national growth, which is essential for accountability in a democracy.
