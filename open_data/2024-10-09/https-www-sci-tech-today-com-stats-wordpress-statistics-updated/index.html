WordPress dominates the web content management landscape, serving nearly half of all websites globally due to its open-source nature and accessibility. This widespread adoption stems from a robust ecosystem of themes and plugins that allows non-technical users to build complex sites, from blogs to e-commerce stores, without coding knowledge. The platform’s flexibility and strong community support continue to drive its market leadership, making it the primary choice for establishing an online presence. However, this popularity attracts significant security challenges, including frequent attacks and vulnerabilities, often exacerbated by outdated third-party components. Despite these risks, the platform remains resilient through continuous updates and the integration of modern technologies like artificial intelligence. These innovations enhance content creation, search engine optimization, and proactive security monitoring, ensuring that WordPress sites can maintain performance and safety while adapting to evolving digital trends. This article is crucial for the open data community because WordPress serves as a foundational infrastructure for much of the public web’s data. Its open-source model exemplifies the principles of transparency and collaboration central to the open data movement. Furthermore, the sheer volume of data hosted on WordPress platforms highlights the importance of understanding the underlying technologies that store, manage, and deliver information to the public, ensuring that data access remains secure, efficient, and inclusive for all users.
