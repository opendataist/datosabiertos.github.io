We promised to issue the provisional voter's register within 2 weeks - EC to NDC - Ghanamma.com

Ghana’s Electoral Commission has clarified that the Provisional Voter’s Register will be released soon, resolving earlier timeline confusion. This update ensures political parties have the necessary data to prepare adequately for the upcoming general elections, reinforcing the integrity of the electoral process. Transparency remains the Commission’s primary focus as the election date approaches. By adhering to its commitments, the EC aims to facilitate a fair and open environment for all participating parties, allowing them to verify voter information and build trust in the system. This development is vital for open data initiatives, as it highlights the critical need for timely, accessible electoral information. Providing public or party access to voter registers promotes accountability and enables civil society to monitor electoral integrity, ensuring that democratic processes are supported by reliable, open datasets.
