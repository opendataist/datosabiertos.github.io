El Ayuntamiento de Murcia pone en marcha un nuevo sistema de gestión del Padrón Municipal más ágil, eficaz y eficiente - murcia.com

The City of Murcia has modernized its municipal register management by integrating local neighborhood offices and enabling online submissions through its Electronic Headquarters. This structural shift allows residents to handle registration or address changes instantly at various distribution points or remotely, eliminating the need for prior appointments and reducing administrative bottlenecks. By decentralizing service points and leveraging digital channels, the administration ensures immediate processing without forcing citizens to travel to central offices, significantly saving time and effort. The initiative prioritizes citizen convenience and environmental sustainability by issuing electronic confirmation documents directly via email. These digital certificates are immediately valid for use with any administration, allowing users to print them as needed without requesting physical copies. This transition not only streamlines bureaucratic procedures but also reduces paper consumption, aligning public service delivery with modern ecological standards while ensuring that all official documentation is accessible and reusable across different governmental bodies. This case is highly relevant to open data as it exemplifies the practical benefits of digital transformation in public administration. By shifting to electronic records and remote interaction, the municipality generates structured, accessible data that can potentially be integrated into broader open data ecosystems. The move demonstrates how efficient, citizen-centric digital services can enhance transparency and usability, setting a precedent for other local governments seeking to improve administrative efficiency and data accessibility for their populations.
