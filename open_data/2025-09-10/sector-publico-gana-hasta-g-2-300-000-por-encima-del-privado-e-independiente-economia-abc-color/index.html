Sector público gana hasta G. 2.300.000 por encima del privado e independiente - Economía - ABC Color

Sector público gana hasta G. 2.300.000 por encima del privado e independiente - Economía - ABC Color

Public sector employees currently lead in remuneration, significantly outearning their private sector counterparts and self-employed workers. This trend highlights a dual labor market in which state employment has consolidated as the highest-paying segment, driven by substantial wage growth that far exceeds improvements seen in other areas of the economy. The data suggest a structural shift whereby public sector jobs offer superior financial stability compared to the rest of the economy. Gender pay gaps persist across all categories but vary in intensity, being most pronounced among self-employed workers and domestic employees. While women in the public sector have narrowed the income gap relative to men, those in autonomous professions face severe barriers to accessing capital and engaging in high-yield activities. Conversely, domestic workers, who are predominantly female, remain in the most vulnerable position, with wages well below the national average, reflecting deep-seated inequalities in low-income segments. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of granular, gender-disaggregated statistics to reveal hidden socioeconomic disparities. By making detailed employment and salary metrics publicly accessible, researchers and policymakers can better monitor equity and design targeted interventions. Transparent data fosters accountability, enabling society to assess whether economic growth is inclusive or whether structural advantages for specific groups continue to widen the social divide.
