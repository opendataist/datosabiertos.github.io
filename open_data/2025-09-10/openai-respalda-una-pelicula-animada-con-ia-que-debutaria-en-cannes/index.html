OpenAI respalda una película animada con IA que debutaría en Cannes

OpenAI respalda una película animada con IA que debutaría en Cannes

OpenAI is producing "Critterz," an animated feature film created primarily with AI, which aims to debut at Cannes in 2026. This project seeks to demonstrate how technology can revolutionize the film industry by significantly reducing production times and associated costs, establishing a new standard for algorithm-assisted creative development. The relevance of this initiative to the realm of open data lies in the intensification of debates surrounding intellectual property and the ethical use of information. With Hollywood studios suing AI companies for alleged copyright infringement during model training, the project highlights the urgent need for clear frameworks that regulate access to and licensing of creative data, promoting transparency regarding the origin of the materials used. Furthermore, the film illustrates the necessary collaboration between artificial intelligence and human labor, integrating artists' sketches with generative tools and the voices of real actors. This hybrid approach suggests that the future of cinema does not lie in total automation, but in synergies that preserve emotional and artistic quality, prompting the open data community to consider how technology can amplify, rather than replace, human creativity.
