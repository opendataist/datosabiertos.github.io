Los mejores lugares de trabajo para el bienestar mental en EE.UU., según Newsweek - El Diario NY

Newsweek and Plant-A have published the first ranking of the best U.S. companies for mental well-being, recognizing those that actively integrate psychological support into their workplace policies. This recognition stems from a growing awareness of the importance of emotional health in the work environment, reflected in the demand for strategies such as counseling and flexible scheduling to balance professional and personal life. However, there is a notable gap between employees’ perceptions and corporate reality, with many workers feeling that their employers underestimate existing mental health challenges. The ranking methodology combines public data and anonymous testimonials from human resources professionals to identify genuine factors of satisfaction, thereby establishing a new standard of transparency and concrete action across various industries, from logistics to technology. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical value of public data and verifiable information sets for assessing corporate performance beyond marketing claims. By basing its rankings on accessible and transparent information, the report promotes accountability through open data, demonstrating how the availability of public information enables citizens and employees to audit the social and workplace realities of organizations, fostering a culture of evidence-based verification.
