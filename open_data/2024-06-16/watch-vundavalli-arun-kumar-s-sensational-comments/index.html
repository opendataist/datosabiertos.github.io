Allegations regarding two million missing electronic voting machines during recent elections have sparked demands for transparency. Former MP Arunkumar challenges state leadership to investigate, arguing that public trust requires accountability rather than deflecting responsibility to the Election Commission. This issue highlights critical concerns about the security and traceability of electoral hardware. The potential disappearance of a significant portion of voting devices raises serious questions about the integrity of the voting process and the safeguards in place to prevent tampering or loss. Such incidents underscore the importance of open data in democratic processes. Ensuring that data on electoral equipment usage and disposal is publicly accessible allows for independent verification, thereby reinforcing citizen confidence in the legitimacy and transparency of election outcomes.
