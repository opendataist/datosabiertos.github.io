El 55,6 % de la población votará por Maduro el próximo 28 de julio

A recent Venezuelan survey indicates strong support for President Maduro’s reelection, with a majority intending to vote and identifying with the ruling party. This data provides critical insight into current political alignments and voter engagement levels within the country. The findings highlight a significant divide in political affinity, showing high identification with Chavismo alongside a substantial portion of undecided or opposed citizens. Understanding these trends is essential for analyzing the stability of the current political landscape and the potential outcomes of upcoming elections. This information is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how publicly available polling results can track democratic participation and political sentiment. Such datasets enable independent verification of election trends, fostering transparency and allowing citizens to engage more effectively with their political environment.
