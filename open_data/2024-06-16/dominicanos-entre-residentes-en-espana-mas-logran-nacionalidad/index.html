Dominicanos, entre residentes en España más logran nacionalidad

Dominicanos, entre residentes en España más logran nacionalidad

In 2023, foreign nationals, particularly those from Latin America and Morocco, increasingly acquired Spanish citizenship. This surge highlights significant demographic integration trends and the growing influence of these communities within the country. The data reveals that most applicants had previously resided abroad, indicating a steady flow of naturalizations among long-term residents. This shift underscores the evolving social fabric of Spain, largely driven by international migration patterns in recent years. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent public statistics enable researchers to analyze the impacts of migration. Understanding these migration flows supports better policy-making and social integration strategies across European borders.
