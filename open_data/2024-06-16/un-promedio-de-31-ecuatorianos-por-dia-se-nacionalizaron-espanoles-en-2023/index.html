Un promedio de 31 ecuatorianos por día se nacionalizaron españoles en 2023

Naturalizations in Spain increased significantly in 2023, driven primarily by Latin American migrants, who accounted for nearly half of all acquisitions. This demographic surge highlights Spain’s evolving role as a key destination for regional migration, reflecting deep socio-political ties and historical migration patterns across the continent. The data reveal that while North African nationals remain the largest single group, the substantial number of naturalizations from countries such as Venezuela, Colombia, and Ecuador underscores a shifting geographic landscape in Spain’s immigration dynamics. This trend indicates growing integration pressures and the need for updated civic policies to manage diverse incoming populations. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent statistical records enable researchers to track complex migration flows and their societal impacts. By making detailed nationality acquisition data publicly available, institutions support evidence-based analysis of global mobility, helping stakeholders understand demographic shifts and their long-term implications for host nations.
