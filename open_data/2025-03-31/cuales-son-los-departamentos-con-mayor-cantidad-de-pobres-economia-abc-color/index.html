¿Cuáles son los departamentos con mayor cantidad de pobres? - Economía - ABC Color

¿Cuáles son los departamentos con mayor cantidad de pobres? - Economía - ABC Color

The Institute of Statistics reports a decline in overall monetary poverty in 2024 compared to the previous year. However, significant disparities persist between urban and rural areas, with rural populations facing disproportionately higher poverty rates despite having slightly fewer individuals in absolute terms. Poverty is heavily concentrated in specific departments, particularly within the Greater Asunción region and other key provinces. While most areas show a reduction in the number of impoverished residents, the sheer volume of people affected in these centers remains substantial, indicating that gains are unevenly distributed across the country. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the necessity for transparent, accessible government statistics to evaluate social welfare policies. Analyzing such datasets allows for a deeper understanding of structural inequalities and the effectiveness of state interventions, enabling citizens and researchers to hold institutions accountable for poverty reduction efforts.
