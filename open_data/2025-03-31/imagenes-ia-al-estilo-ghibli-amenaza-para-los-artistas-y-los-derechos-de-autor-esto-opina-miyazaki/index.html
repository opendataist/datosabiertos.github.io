Imágenes IA al estilo Ghibli:¿Amenaza para los artistas y los derechos de autor? Esto opina Miyazaki

Imágenes IA al estilo Ghibli:¿Amenaza para los artistas y los derechos de autor? Esto opina Miyazaki

The viral spread of "Ghibli-fication" through artificial intelligence illustrates how accessible technology enables the replication of complex artistic styles in minutes, challenging traditional models of creation. This ease of use not only democratizes the generation of visual content but also accelerates the integration of AI into digital culture, raising significant questions about the valuation of human effort in contrast to automated production. The central debate revolves around intellectual property, questioning whether AI-generated works deserve legal protection or if such rights should be reserved exclusively for human creativity. Given that current legislation, such as in Colombia, requires significant human intervention for copyright recognition, an ethical and legal gray area emerges. This underscores the need to redefine legal frameworks to distinguish between automation and genuine authorship, protecting artists' dignity while regulating the commercial use of the technology. This issue is crucial for the field of open data because it exposes the tension between universal access to technological tools and the protection of intellectual assets. The expansion of AI forces us to rethink how algorithmically generated data is managed and labeled, particularly in relation to the training of models that use existing works. Understanding these implications is vital for developing data policies that balance technological innovation with respect for the rights of original creators, ensuring that automated efficiency does not dilute the value of human contribution within the digital ecosystem.
