Infancias vulneradas: 2025 cerró con más de 21 mil menores víctimas de delitos

The report reveals a persistent and severe crisis of violence against children, highlighting that sexual abuse remains a daily reality with consistent monthly rates throughout the year. This data underscores that the issue is not isolated but systemic, demanding immediate attention beyond mere statistical recording to address the underlying structural failures. A significant finding is the proximity of perpetrators, with the vast majority of aggressors being family members or known individuals, which challenges the assumption of safety within domestic environments. The concentration of cases in urban centers further indicates that population density and specific regional dynamics play critical roles in the manifestation of these crimes, requiring targeted local interventions. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how transparent, granular data can expose hidden social risks and inform public policy. By making detailed statistics on victim demographics, geographic distribution, and crime types publicly available, authorities and civil society can better allocate resources, advocate for legislative changes, and hold institutions accountable for protecting vulnerable populations.
