FOIA: US Court rejects Greenspan’s bid to obtain evidence

FOIA: US Court rejects Greenspan’s bid to obtain evidence

A US federal court denied Aaron Greenspan’s attempt to discover evidence from President Bola Tinubu and the DCI Group within a FOIA lawsuit. The ruling emphasized that discovery requests must remain strictly relevant to the primary information dispute between the plaintiff and federal agencies like the FBI and DEA. This decision establishes crucial procedural boundaries, preventing litigants from using open data laws as a vehicle to investigate unrelated allegations, such as cyberattacks. By rejecting these tangential requests, the court clarified that FOIA proceedings are not a general mechanism for gathering evidence on separate legal grievances. This case is vital for open data advocacy because it reinforces the principle that transparency laws have specific, limited scopes. It underscores the necessity for practitioners to focus on legitimate information access issues rather than weaponizing these legal frameworks for unrelated political or personal disputes, ensuring the integrity of open data rights remains intact.
