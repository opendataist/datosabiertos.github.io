Medio millón de censistas inician el megaoperativo por el Censo 2024

Medio millón de censistas inician el megaoperativo por el Censo 2024

Bolivia has launched its 2024 Population and Housing Census, deploying hundreds of thousands of enumerators to collect critical demographic data from households across the country. This large-scale operation aims to gather essential information that will guide future national planning and resource allocation, ensuring the state can effectively respond to the country’s evolving social and economic needs. The statistical institute advises families to remain at home during the surveys, which involve detailed interviews lasting approximately forty minutes. Although the process began with some logistical challenges in specific border regions, authorities emphasize the importance of citizen cooperation to ensure comprehensive coverage and data integrity across all departments. This initiative is vital for open data, as it generates the foundational dataset required for transparent governance and evidence-based policy-making. Access to accurate, granular demographic information enables better public accountability and allows researchers to analyze social trends, ultimately fostering a more inclusive and data-driven society where decisions are grounded in reliable, publicly available statistics.
