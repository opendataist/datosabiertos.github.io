Presupuestos participativos y nuevo portal, principales acciones dentro del Plan de Gobierno Abierto de Málaga

Málaga has concluded its first Open Government Plan with a high level of implementation, consolidating significant advances in participation, transparency, and open data. The report highlights the strengthening of institutional commitment to the 2030 Agenda, validating the effectiveness of the strategies implemented to actively engage citizens in municipal management and improve accountability. The creation of the Citizen Participation Portal and the implementation of Participatory Budgeting stand out as central initiatives that have transformed the relationship between the city council and society. These tools not only facilitate the co-creation of public policies but also empower residents to decide on the use of public resources, demonstrating that both digital and in-person interaction can effectively democratize local decision-making. This article is relevant to the field of open data because it underscores its strategic role as an instrument for developing collective intelligence and urban competitiveness. By publishing data beyond what is legally required and promoting a culture of transparency, Málaga exemplifies how open data goes beyond mere regulatory compliance to become a fundamental right that fosters trust, ethics, and citizen collaboration in building more sustainable and participatory cities.
