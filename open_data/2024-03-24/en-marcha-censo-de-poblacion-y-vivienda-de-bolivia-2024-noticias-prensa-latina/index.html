En marcha Censo de Población y Vivienda de Bolivia 2024 - Noticias Prensa Latina

Bolivia has launched its latest population and housing census, leveraging modern technological infrastructure and widespread voluntary participation, marking a milestone in the country’s demographic precision. The initiative is supported by an international mission that includes United Nations agencies and multilateral banks, ensuring that the process adheres to high-quality technical standards. This collaboration aims to produce an accurate snapshot of the nation, guaranteeing that no citizen is excluded through the digitalization of cartographic data. The significance of this exercise goes beyond mere numerical counting, as the collected data are essential for designing effective public policies. By providing detailed information on population distribution and characteristics, the census enables national, departmental, and municipal governments, as well as indigenous autonomies, to plan strategies aimed at improving quality of life. This technical precision is crucial for directing social and structural investments toward areas of greatest need, facilitating more equitable and efficient development. This article is particularly relevant in the context of open data, as it illustrates the implementation of large-scale demographic datasets whose analytical potential depends on their accessibility and transparency. A modern, digitized census represents a primary source of public data that, if managed with open-data principles, can drive innovation, accountability, and academic research. The ability to leverage this information for social well-being depends directly on how the data community can access, analyze, and visualize these government inputs to address complex challenges.
