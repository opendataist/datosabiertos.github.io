Misión internacional destaca el trabajo del INE y documentará las buenas prácticas censales de Bolivia

An international technical mission is closely monitoring Bolivia’s census to document best practices for future regional sharing. This collaborative effort, involving major global organizations, highlights the value of high-quality statistical data in driving national progress. By accompanying enumerators on the ground, experts aim to capture a comprehensive picture of Bolivia’s diverse population. The process is viewed as a vital civic act that generates essential evidence for public policy. Accurate data ensures that government decisions effectively promote well-being for all citizens. This approach emphasizes how robust statistics serve as the foundation for equitable and informed governance strategies. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible information for societal benefit. Documenting these practices encourages standards for data openness and quality across nations. Ultimately, it demonstrates how reliable, shared statistics empower communities and improve decision-making through transparency.
