La inflación de eurozona se frena en julio al 5,3%, pero la subyacente se queda en el 5,5%

The slowdown in inflation in the eurozone validates the forecast of sustained moderation, highlighting the significant reduction in energy prices that is driving this downward trend. Although the underlying inflation rate remains stable, the overall trajectory suggests that cost pressures will begin to ease significantly during the autumn, benefiting citizens’ purchasing power. Experts project an accelerated decline in the fourth quarter, anticipating that prices will converge toward levels closer to the European Central Bank’s target. This trend indicates that temporary factors that had kept inflation elevated, particularly in services, are dissipating. Furthermore, one final interest rate hike is expected before economic indicators compel a shift toward a more expansionary monetary policy in the coming year. This analysis is relevant to open data because Eurostat’s inflation indicators are fundamental to economic transparency and accountability. Public access to these detailed data allows citizens, researchers, and businesses to assess the effectiveness of monetary policies and conduct cross-country comparative studies. The availability of accurate and timely statistics empowers civil society to demand accountability for decisions that directly impact their financial well-being and the stability of the eurozone.
