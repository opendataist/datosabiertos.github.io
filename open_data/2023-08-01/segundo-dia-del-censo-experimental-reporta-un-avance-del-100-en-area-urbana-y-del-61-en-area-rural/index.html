Segundo día del Censo Experimental reporta un avance del 100% en área urbana y del 61% en área rural

The experimental census in Capinota demonstrates significant operational progress, with urban areas fully covered and rural zones actively being processed despite logistical challenges. This partial completion highlights the efficacy of deploying local volunteers who navigate difficult terrains and overcome language barriers to ensure comprehensive data collection. The ongoing fieldwork underscores the critical role of human resources and adaptability in achieving accurate demographic representation. The primary objective of this exercise is to refine the methodologies, instruments, and systems used in future national censuses. By identifying procedures that function well and those requiring improvement, the statistical institute aims to optimize the upcoming comprehensive census of population and housing. This iterative learning process is essential for enhancing the reliability and efficiency of national data gathering efforts. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it seeks to improve the quality and accessibility of public demographic information. Reliable census data forms the foundation for transparent governance and evidence-based policy-making. By strengthening the technical framework for data collection, the government ensures that open data resources are more accurate, thereby empowering citizens and researchers with trustworthy statistics for societal development.
