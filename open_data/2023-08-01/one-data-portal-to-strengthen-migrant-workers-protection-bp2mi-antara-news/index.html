One Data Portal to strengthen migrant workers' protection: BP2MI - ANTARA News

The Indonesian Migrant Workers' Protection Agency launched integrated digital platforms to enhance governance and protect migrant workers. By consolidating recruitment, placement, and complaint data, these systems aim to address complex protection challenges through centralized, transparent management. This initiative reflects a shift toward using big data for comprehensive welfare strategies, ensuring workers are safeguarded from recruitment to return. The relevance to open data lies in the commitment to transparency and standardized information sharing. Integrating with national data portals promotes interoperability, allowing different agencies to access unified, accurate records. This reduces bureaucratic friction and supports evidence-based policymaking by making protection metrics visible and accessible to the public and stakeholders. Ultimately, this model demonstrates how open data principles can drive efficiency and accountability in public services. By breaking down data silos, the government improves budget efficiency and service targeting. It sets a precedent for using open, integrated datasets to solve social issues, proving that transparent data infrastructure is essential for effective human rights protection and modern governance.
