Se estará vigilando transparencia de partidos: INAI

The INAI emphasizes that transparency and access to information are fundamental pillars for strengthening electoral processes and democracy. By ensuring that political parties and electoral bodies provide accurate data on candidates’ backgrounds and proposals, citizens can make informed decisions. This commitment to openness is presented not merely as a legal obligation, but as a crucial mechanism to foster public trust and ensure the legality of elections. Furthermore, the protection of personal data is highlighted as vital for electoral integrity. The INAI and electoral prosecutors will collaborate closely to monitor compliance, ensuring that resources are managed transparently and that information regarding party expenditures and electoral oversight remains accessible. This cooperative approach aims to combat illegality and restore confidence in public institutions during significant electoral periods. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores how publicly available data, specifically quarterly financial reports on the Transparency Platform, empowers citizens to hold institutions accountable. It illustrates the direct link between open government data initiatives and democratic health, showing that accessible, verifiable information is essential for preventing corruption and ensuring that electoral outcomes are driven by transparency rather than secrecy.
