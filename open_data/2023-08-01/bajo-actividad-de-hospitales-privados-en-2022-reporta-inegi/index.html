Bajó actividad de hospitales privados en 2022, reporta INEGI

The article highlights a decline in private healthcare activity in Veracruz, emphasizing shifts in patient discharge rates and hospital capacity. This data underscores the importance of accessible, standardized health information for tracking service trends. Open data initiatives are crucial for transparently monitoring public health infrastructure and resource allocation. Ultimately, this reinforces the value of open data in supporting evidence-based policy decisions. Transparent health metrics enable stakeholders to identify gaps, optimize investments, and ensure that private sector performance aligns with broader public health goals.
