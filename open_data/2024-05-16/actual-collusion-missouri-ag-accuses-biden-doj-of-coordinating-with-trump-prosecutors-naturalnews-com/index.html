Actual collusion? Missouri AG accuses Biden DOJ of coordinating with Trump prosecutors – NaturalNews.com

Actual collusion? Missouri AG accuses Biden DOJ of coordinating with Trump prosecutors – NaturalNews.com

Missouri Attorney General Andrew Bailey initiated a Freedom of Information Act probe to determine if the Biden Department of Justice coordinated with Trump’s legal challenges. The investigation seeks communications regarding key prosecutors’ movements and collaborations, aiming to verify allegations of politically motivated legal actions designed to impact the election. This matter is critically relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency mechanisms, like FOIA requests, are utilized to expose potential government interference and coordinate efforts between political entities. By demanding the release of internal documents, such inquiries aim to create a verifiable record of executive branch activities, highlighting the essential role of data accessibility in ensuring governmental accountability. Ultimately, the case underscores the importance of accessing public records to validate claims of institutional bias. It illustrates that open data practices serve as a vital check on power, allowing the public and officials to scrutinize whether legal proceedings are driven by justice or political strategy, thereby maintaining trust in democratic institutions.
