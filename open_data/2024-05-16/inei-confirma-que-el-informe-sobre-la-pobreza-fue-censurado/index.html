INEI confirma que el informe sobre la pobreza fue censurado

The article highlights a controversy in which Peru’s national statistics institute delayed the publication of poverty data under political pressure, although the underlying database remained accessible. This incident underscores the critical importance of transparency and independence in open data systems, as government interference can obscure vital socioeconomic realities from public scrutiny. Ultimately, the report revealed a concerning rise in monetary poverty, contradicting official narratives and demonstrating that accurate, unrestricted data publication is essential for accountability. Ensuring that statistical agencies can operate free from executive censorship allows citizens and researchers to rely on truthful information, which is the cornerstone of effective open data governance and democratic oversight.
