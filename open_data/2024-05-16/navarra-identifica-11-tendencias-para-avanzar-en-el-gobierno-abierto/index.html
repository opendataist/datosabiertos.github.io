Navarra identifica 11 tendencias para avanzar en el Gobierno Abierto

Navarra identifica 11 tendencias para avanzar en el Gobierno Abierto

Navarre has identified key trends to advance toward a more effective Open Government, highlighting technological evolution and transparency as fundamental pillars. The diagnosis emphasizes the need for omnichannel citizen attention and clear communication that guarantees the right to understand public information. These measures aim to consolidate a culture of openness within the administration, facilitating access to data and improving the user experience through the digitalization of procedures. Citizen participation is redefined through more integral and inclusive processes, prioritizing informed deliberation and collaboration in budget preparation. The objective is to directly link these participatory dynamics with the improvement of public services, ensuring the representation of vulnerable groups. This approach seeks to foster extended accountability and deepen institutional integrity, strengthening alliances with local and social entities to create a collaborative governance ecosystem. This progress is relevant to open data because it legitimizes the use of data as a central tool for transparency and accountability. By integrating technology and participation in the definition of services, the opening of public data is promoted not only as a legal requirement but also as an active means to empower citizens. Navarre, by joining the International Open Government Partnership, exemplifies how open data management can strengthen local democracies, ensuring that information is accessible, usable, and directly contributes to shared public decision-making.
