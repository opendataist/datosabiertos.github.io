La imagen de auroras boreales sobre el alma máter de la UNAH es un montaje

Fake images of boreal auroras over a Honduran university, generated by artificial intelligence, have spread widely on social media. These deepfakes mimic real solar storms reported in other regions, creating confusion among the public who mistake digital manipulation for natural phenomena. This case highlights the critical need for data verification in open data ecosystems. As AI tools democratize content creation, distinguishing authentic scientific observations from synthetic fabrications becomes a major challenge for transparent information sharing. Understanding the provenance of data is essential to maintain trust. Open data initiatives must emphasize source credibility and transparency to help users identify manipulated visual information, ensuring that digital integrity is preserved against misleading viral trends.
