Inflación de panamá fue de 0,2% en abril 2024

Panama’s urban consumer price index showed minimal fluctuation between March and April 2024, reflecting a delicate balance between rising costs in transportation, alcohol, and housing, and decreasing prices in healthcare, food, and household goods. This stagnation highlights how specific sectoral shifts offset each other, resulting in negligible overall inflation for the month. For open data enthusiasts, this report illustrates the complexity of aggregating granular economic indicators into a single national metric. The data reveals that while core living expenses such as transport and housing rose, essential items such as medicine and fresh food became cheaper. Understanding these underlying dynamics requires access to disaggregated, high-frequency datasets rather than relying solely on summary statistics. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, detailed statistical publishing. When governments release not just the final index but the specific variations across dozens of sub-groups, citizens and researchers can better analyze inflationary pressures. Such granularity enables more accurate modeling of economic impacts on different demographics and fosters greater accountability in public economic management.
