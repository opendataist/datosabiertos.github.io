Innovation, investment urged to win global AI race

Innovation, investment urged to win global AI race

Chinese technology firms possess significant advantages in data volume and industrial application scenarios, positioning them to compete effectively in the global artificial intelligence landscape. To maintain this edge, experts urge a strategic shift toward strengthening independent innovation in foundational technologies, particularly by investing heavily in computing power infrastructure and refining algorithmic capabilities. This focus aims to bridge existing gaps in underlying chips and software while leveraging China’s vast resource pool to drive high-quality development. The rapid evolution of multimodal large language models highlights the critical need for robust computing resources and diverse data inputs. As these models enable more natural human-machine interactions across various media, they create substantial demand for advanced processing power. Consequently, boosting the computing power industry and enhancing the quality of training data are essential steps for Chinese companies to accelerate their own generative AI advancements and ensure they remain competitive against leading international counterparts. The article is highly relevant to open data because it emphasizes that abundant, high-quality data is a primary competitive advantage for China’s AI sector. It underscores the necessity of managing, optimizing, and utilizing this data effectively to train sophisticated models. This highlights the importance of open data initiatives in facilitating access to diverse datasets, ensuring data quality, and supporting the ethical and secure development of AI technologies that rely on massive information scales.
