NATO: record 23 countries hit 2% defence spending target this year - Baltic News Network

European and Canadian allies are significantly boosting defense budgets, marking the first time most NATO members meet the spending target without US assistance. This shift is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the need for transparent, standardized metrics to verify national contributions. Accurate, accessible statistics on military expenditure are essential for fostering trust and accountability within international coalitions.
