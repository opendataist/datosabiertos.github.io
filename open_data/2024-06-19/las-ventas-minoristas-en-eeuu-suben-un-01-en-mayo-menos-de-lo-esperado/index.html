Las ventas minoristas en EEUU suben un 0,1% en mayo, menos de lo esperado

Retail sales data indicate a gradual economic deceleration in the United States, providing no support for fears of an imminent recession. This softness suggests that the Federal Reserve will prioritize upcoming inflation metrics to determine the timing of potential interest rate cuts, with analysts projecting a cut in September. The implications for monetary policy remain marginal, as markets await further confirmation that inflation is sustainably returning to target levels.
