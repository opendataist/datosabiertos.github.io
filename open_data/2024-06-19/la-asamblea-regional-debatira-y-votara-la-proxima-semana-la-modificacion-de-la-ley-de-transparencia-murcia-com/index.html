La Asamblea Regional debatirá y votará, la próxima semana, la modificación de la Ley de Transparencia - murcia.com

La Asamblea Regional debatirá y votará, la próxima semana, la modificación de la Ley de Transparencia - murcia.com

The Regional Assembly of Murcia is set to debate and vote on a legislative proposal to amend the existing law on transparency and citizen participation. This procedural step underscores the region’s ongoing efforts to adapt its legal framework governing access to public information. By reviewing amendments and finalizing the draft within the Commission of General Affairs, lawmakers aim to update the mechanisms that facilitate citizens’ interactions with government bodies. The urgency of this debate reflects the growing demand for greater institutional accountability. Updating transparency regulations is crucial for maintaining public trust, as it determines how easily information is disclosed and how citizen requests are processed. The proposed legislative change seeks to modernize these processes, ensuring they remain effective tools for democratic engagement rather than outdated bureaucratic obstacles. This development is highly relevant to open data, as transparency laws form the legal foundation for data publication policies. Without robust legal mandates for openness, initiatives to publish raw, machine-readable government data lack binding enforcement. Therefore, amending the transparency law directly affects the viability and scope of open data ecosystems, ensuring that digital access to public administration remains legally supported and practically enforced.
