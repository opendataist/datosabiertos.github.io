Northern Ireland greenhouse gas statistics 1990-2022 released

Northern Ireland’s latest greenhouse gas report reveals a continued, albeit slower, decline in emissions compared to 1990 levels, though progress lags significantly behind other UK regions. This data highlights regional disparities in environmental performance, emphasizing the need for targeted policy interventions to accelerate decarbonization across all sectors. Agriculture remains the dominant source of emissions, followed by domestic transport and energy supply. Understanding these sector-specific contributions is crucial for designing effective mitigation strategies. The slight overall reduction in 2022 suggests incremental progress, yet substantial gaps remain in meeting ambitious climate targets compared to peers. This bulletin is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular insights into regional environmental impacts. By making such detailed statistics accessible, stakeholders can better analyze trends, hold governments accountable, and drive evidence-based decisions that support global sustainability efforts and regional climate resilience.
