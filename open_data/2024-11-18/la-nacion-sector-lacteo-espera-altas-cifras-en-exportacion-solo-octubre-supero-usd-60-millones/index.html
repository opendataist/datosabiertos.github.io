La Nación / Sector lácteo espera altas cifras en exportación: solo octubre superó USD 60 millones

La Nación / Sector lácteo espera altas cifras en exportación: solo octubre superó USD 60 millones

Paraguay’s dairy sector achieved significant export growth in October, surpassing the previous year’s figures and reaching near-record production volumes. This surge, despite environmental challenges affecting feed quality, highlights the industry’s resilience and efficient herd management strategies that minimized shipment disruptions. The main conclusion is that Paraguay is optimally positioned to strengthen its dairy industry in 2025. With stocks nearly depleted and consistent improvements in per-cow yields, the sector demonstrates enhanced competitiveness. However, persistent challenges remain in raising the national average production and ensuring that genetic and welfare standards match top-tier performance. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistics for tracking agricultural trends and informing policy. Reliable public data from entities such as Senacsa enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify bottlenecks, and drive evidence-based improvements in production efficiency and market strategy.
