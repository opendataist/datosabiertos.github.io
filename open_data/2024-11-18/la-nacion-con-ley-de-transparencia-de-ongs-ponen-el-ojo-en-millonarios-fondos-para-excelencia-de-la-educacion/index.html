La Nación / Con ley de Transparencia de ONGs ponen el ojo en millonarios fondos para excelencia de la educación

The enactment of a new transparency law for NGOs in Paraguay has prompted Senator Gustavo Leite to demand a thorough review of how funds from the Fund for Excellence in Education and Research (FEEI) have been used. Despite millions of dollars invested over the past decade, national education indicators remain alarming, suggesting possible inefficiencies in the management or distribution of public resources intended to improve academic quality and school infrastructure. The analysis reveals that, although FEEI financed various infrastructure and technology programs, no tangible improvement in student learning has materialized. Chronic problems such as low reading comprehension, inadequate school conditions, and insufficient teacher training persist, raising serious questions about the effectiveness of the education policies implemented with these funds originating from Itaipu. This case is relevant to the open data community because it highlights the critical need to access and analyze budgetary data and impact outcomes to ensure accountability. Transparency not only aims to detect irregularities but also serves as a fundamental tool for auditing the effectiveness of public policies. By making visible the disparities between investment and actual results, citizens and analysts can demand evidence-based structural changes, promoting more responsible governance oriented toward the public good.
