Congress demands arrest of RTI activist Snehamayi Krishna - Star of Mysore

This incident highlights the volatile intersection of transparency efforts and political power in Mysuru. An RTI activist faces severe legal threats, illustrating how demanding accountability can provoke aggressive countermeasures from powerful political figures. The allegations of extortion and forgery underscore the personal risks activists assume when challenging officials. This tension reveals the fragility of open data principles when they threaten influential interests, potentially deterring others from seeking public records. Consequently, the situation serves as a critical warning for the open data community. It emphasizes the urgent need for robust legal protections for information seekers, ensuring that transparency initiatives remain safe and effective against political retaliation.
