Noviembre de 2022 cerró con pérdidas en sector de la construcción: INEGI

Veracruz’s construction sector contracted in November, contributing less to national output. This decline, particularly evident in petrochemical and electrical works, highlights the volatility of local infrastructure projects and the sensitivity of regional economic indicators to broader market shifts. Wholesale commerce also experienced a slight drop in sales, contrasting with growth in retail. The divergence between wholesale contraction and retail expansion reveals shifting consumer patterns and supply chain dynamics within the state, indicating varied health across different commercial segments. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how granular, public statistics enable the tracking of microeconomic trends. Accessible datasets like these empower researchers and policymakers to analyze regional resilience, fostering transparency and informed decision-making regarding local economic development strategies.
