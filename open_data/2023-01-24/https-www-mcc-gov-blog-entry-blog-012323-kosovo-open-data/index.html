Open Data
ES EN

Open data serves as a vital public good that enhances government transparency, drives economic growth, and supports informed decision-making. Treating data as a shared resource rather than a restricted asset allows societies to enrich their collective knowledge base, fostering innovation and accountability in public services. This perspective aligns with foundational scientific norms that prioritize the free flow of information to accelerate progress. The practical value of accessible, high-quality data is evident in personal research experiences, where barriers to official statistics can hinder academic and economic insights. When reliable datasets are publicly available and properly cleaned, researchers save significant time on data preparation, allowing them to focus on analysis and generating meaningful conclusions. This efficiency not only aids individual scholars but also empowers the private sector to make better decisions based on robust evidence. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for improved dissemination mechanisms. The recent launch of platforms dedicated to sharing knowledge products demonstrates how organized access can amplify the impact of data. By ensuring data is not only collected but also actively promoted and standardized, institutions can uphold scientific integrity and support broader development goals, making valuable information usable for a wider audience.
