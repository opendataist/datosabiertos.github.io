Aumenta Población Económicamente Activa (PEA) en Coahuila

The article highlights a slight increase in Coahuila’s economically active population, signaling a gradual shift toward formal employment. While northern states such as Nuevo León and Baja California exhibit higher overall employment rates, Coahuila lags slightly behind but continues to integrate workers into the formal sector. This data reflects broader regional trends in workforce formalization across Mexico’s northern border states. The steady growth in employment percentages indicates improving economic conditions and greater labor market stability in these specific regions. This report is relevant to open data because it uses official statistics from INEGI to monitor labor market dynamics. Access to such detailed, time-series data enables researchers and policymakers to analyze economic health, identify employment gaps, and assess the effectiveness of regional development strategies.
