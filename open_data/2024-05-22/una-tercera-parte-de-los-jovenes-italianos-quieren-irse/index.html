Una tercera parte de los jóvenes italianos quieren irse

Italy is confronting a profound demographic and labor crisis, fueled by historically low birth rates and high youth unemployment. This structural decline jeopardizes the sustainability of the social security system, as a shrinking pool of young people must support an aging population, creating long-term economic instability that extends well beyond immediate political concerns. The resulting social instability is marked by a scarcity of professional opportunities and widespread economic precarity, which forces most young adults to remain dependent on their families. This situation discourages domestic retention and fuels a strong desire among adolescents to emigrate, particularly to the United States. The inability of young people to achieve financial independence or form families underscores the failure of current policies to address the root causes of labor market problems. This data underscores the critical importance of open data in diagnosing complex socio-economic challenges. By making statistics on employment, demographics, and migration accessible, policymakers and civil society can identify systemic failures and advocate for effective reforms. Transparency in these metrics is essential for building public trust and driving the evidence-based decisions needed to reverse demographic decline and improve the livelihoods of young people.
