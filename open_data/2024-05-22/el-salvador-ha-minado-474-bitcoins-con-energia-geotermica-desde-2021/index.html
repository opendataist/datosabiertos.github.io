El Salvador ha minado 474 bitcoins con energía geotérmica desde 2021

El Salvador integrates geothermal energy from the Tecapa volcano to mine Bitcoin sustainably, reducing reliance on conventional power sources. This approach validates green cryptocurrency mining as a viable model for energy-intensive digital asset production, aligning economic innovation with environmental responsibility. To enhance public trust, the National Bitcoin Office launched a transparent portal tracking national holdings and transactions. This commitment to openness addresses previous concerns regarding the management of state-owned digital assets, setting a benchmark for governmental accountability in the crypto sector. This case study is vital for open data initiatives, demonstrating how public transparency fosters legitimacy in emerging financial technologies. It illustrates the importance of accessible, real-time data in building citizen confidence and encouraging the adoption of innovative, sustainable economic policies worldwide.
