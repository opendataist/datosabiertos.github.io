Secrets in Your Data: NOVA screening and panel discussion

This article highlights the critical need to balance maximizing these benefits while protecting individual privacy. The featured experts provide insights into securing data ecosystems, a core concern for open data initiatives. This context underscores the importance of ethical data handling. Understanding these trade-offs helps developers create open systems that respect user privacy while enabling valuable public knowledge sharing.
