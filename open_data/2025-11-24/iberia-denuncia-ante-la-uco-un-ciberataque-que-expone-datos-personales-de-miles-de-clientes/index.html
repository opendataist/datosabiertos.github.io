Iberia denuncia ante la UCO un ciberataque que expone datos personales de miles de clientes

Iberia denuncia ante la UCO un ciberataque que expone datos personales de miles de clientes

Iberia has reported a leak of its customers’ personal data, resulting from unauthorized access to an external repository. Although the company emphasizes that operational flight security remains intact and that no sensitive financial data were compromised, the exposure of contact information and booking details opens the door to risks of identity theft and phishing, prompting the activation of notification protocols and strengthened authentication measures. In addition to the confirmed incident, there are unverified reports of internal data being sold on the dark web. If true, this would represent a far more serious breach with implications for industrial security and intellectual property. However, the airline has not verified this second claim, limiting its public communication to the personal data breach already reported to authorities, while continuing its investigations to determine the full scope of the incident and identify those responsible. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical risks associated with system interconnectivity and dependence on external providers. It underscores the need to balance transparency and information availability with robust cybersecurity measures, highlighting that even in sectors dealing with structured data, protecting privacy is essential to maintaining public trust and ensuring the integrity of digital ecosystems.
