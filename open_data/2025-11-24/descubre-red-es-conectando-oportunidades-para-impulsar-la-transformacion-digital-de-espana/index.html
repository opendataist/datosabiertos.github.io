Descubre Red.es. Conectando oportunidades para impulsar la transformación digital de España

Red.es, the public entity responsible for digitalization in Spain, operates as an integrated ecosystem that connects technological infrastructure with society. Through specialized units focused on research, domain management, and trend analysis, the organization not only provides basic services but also coordinates strategic programs that democratize access to technology. Its approach spans from educational connectivity to business innovation, ensuring that digital transformation is equitable and reaches all sectors, including healthcare and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), through significant support measures. Red.es plays a fundamental role in open data, particularly through its DatosGob unit, which serves as a meeting point between public administrations, businesses, and citizens. This platform facilitates the exchange of information and data-driven services, promoting transparency and the reuse of public information. By centralizing these resources, Red.es fosters a culture of open data that enables citizens to better understand public management and allows businesses to develop new solutions based on accessible, high-quality information. Furthermore, the entity acts as a driver for internationalization and innovation, supporting startups and promoting the development of smart cities. Through the implementation of European funds, Red.es prioritizes projects that enhance mobility, energy efficiency, and urban security. Its ultimate goal is to transform the present by building an inclusive digital future, where technology serves as a tool to improve quality of life and boost Spanish talent in the global market, demonstrating that digitalization is key to social and economic progress.
