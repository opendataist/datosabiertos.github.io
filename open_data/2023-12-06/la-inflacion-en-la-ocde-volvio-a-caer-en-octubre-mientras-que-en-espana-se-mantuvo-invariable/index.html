La inflación en la OCDE volvió a caer en octubre, mientras que en España se mantuvo invariable

Spain’s inflation gap with the OECD average has narrowed significantly, as Spanish inflation rates have remained stable while global figures have declined. This places Spain among the countries where price increases have not moderated recently, highlighting persistent domestic pressures despite broader international deflationary trends. The disparity is particularly evident in food costs, which remain substantially higher in Spain compared to the OECD average. Meanwhile, energy prices have dropped sharply in both regions, contributing to a divergent inflation profile in which core price stability differs markedly from volatile headline metrics. This data is relevant to open_data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, high-quality international statistical standards. Openly accessible, comparable datasets enable policymakers and researchers to accurately assess economic divergence, fostering better-informed decisions regarding monetary policy and cross-border economic analysis.
