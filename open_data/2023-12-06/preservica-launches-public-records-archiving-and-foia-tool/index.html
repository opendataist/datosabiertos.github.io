Preservica Launches Public Records Archiving and FOIA Tool

The article highlights the significant operational burden governments face when manually archiving digital records created in standard platforms like Microsoft 365. It introduces Preservica’s Preserve365, a tool designed to automate this process, enabling seamless compliance with preservation standards directly within the user’s existing workflow. By integrating archiving into daily operations, officials can ensure long-term accessibility and meet legal obligations without adopting fragmented, vendor-specific systems. This integration is crucial for open data initiatives, as it removes technical barriers to making public records permanently accessible and searchable. When archiving becomes automated and standardized, governments can more reliably publish and maintain historical data, thereby enhancing transparency and public trust. The ease of access ensures that information remains usable for researchers and citizens long after its creation, preventing data silos. Ultimately, the shift toward unified digital preservation strategies supports a culture of openness by ensuring records are not just stored, but actively managed for future retrieval. This relevance underscores how modernizing infrastructure facilitates the core principles of open data: accessibility, usability, and longevity. As vendors compete to streamline these processes, the broader implication is a more efficient, transparent government capable of sustaining robust information ecosystems.
