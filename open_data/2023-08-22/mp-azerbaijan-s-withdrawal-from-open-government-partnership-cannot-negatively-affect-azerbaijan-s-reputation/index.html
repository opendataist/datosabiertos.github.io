MP: Azerbaijan's withdrawal from Open Government Partnership cannot negatively affect Azerbaijan's reputation

Azerbaijani officials view exclusion from certain international bodies as minor, arguing these groups misrepresent national realities and host anti-Azerbaijani propaganda. The nation prioritizes transparency and sovereignty over membership, asserting that remaining in unrepresentative organizations is futile if they operate against local interests. This stance highlights the tension between national sovereignty and global governance standards. By rejecting bodies perceived as biased, Azerbaijan emphasizes its commitment to domestic freedom of speech and press, challenging the assumption that international inclusion is always beneficial for a country’s democratic image. The situation underscores the complexities of open data initiatives within the Open Government Partnership. It questions the value of global transparency frameworks when they fail to reflect local contexts or are influenced by geopolitical biases, suggesting that true openness requires alignment with a nation’s specific political and social realities rather than mere membership.
