¿Tesla se prepara para entrar en los centros de datos?

Tesla is significantly expanding its infrastructure by hiring for a dedicated data center project in Texas, signaling a strategic entry into the large-scale data management sector. This move complements its existing investments in the Dojo supercomputer, which is currently being used to train autonomous driving systems and develop specialized hardware. The company aims to use these resources internally while potentially offering Dojo as cloud computing services to other firms in the future. This shift highlights Tesla’s growing reliance on high-performance computing capabilities to support its core technological innovations and hardware development cycles. This development is relevant to open data because it raises questions about data governance, transparency, and accessibility within proprietary AI ecosystems. As Tesla builds extensive data centers for its autonomous systems, understanding how this data is handled, stored, and potentially shared becomes crucial for researchers and policymakers interested in the application of open data principles to emerging autonomous technologies.
