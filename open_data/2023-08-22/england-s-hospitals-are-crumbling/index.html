England's hospitals are crumbling

FOI requests reveal significant discrepancies between reported hospital fires and official NHS statistics, alongside alarming rates of broken fire alarms at specific trusts. This highlights critical gaps in public safety data transparency, underscoring the vital importance of open data initiatives for holding institutions accountable. Accessible, accurate information is essential for ensuring patient safety and institutional responsibility within the healthcare sector.
