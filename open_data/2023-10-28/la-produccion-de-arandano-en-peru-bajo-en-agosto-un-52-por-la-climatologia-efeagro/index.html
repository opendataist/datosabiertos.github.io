La producción de arándano en Perú bajó en agosto un 52% por la climatología - EFEAgro

Peru’s berry production experienced a significant decline in August due to adverse weather conditions, severely impacting the country’s position as a global leader in blueberry exports. This sharp drop highlights the vulnerability of high-value agricultural sectors to climate variability, particularly in the northern regions that drive national output. The negative trend extended to avocado production and the broader economy, reflecting a four-month contraction affecting multiple industries. This situation underscores how environmental shocks and social conflicts can destabilize key economic pillars, reducing national production across diverse sectors beyond just agriculture. For open data, this case emphasizes the critical need for transparent, high-frequency agricultural monitoring to track climate impacts in real time. Open datasets that enable the visualization of regional production variances allow stakeholders to anticipate risks, support decision-making, and ensure supply chain resilience in a climate-sensitive global market.
