De dónde provienen los datos que alimentan las Inteligencias Artificiales

De dónde provienen los datos que alimentan las Inteligencias Artificiales

The launch of the Data Provenance Explorer marks a crucial step forward in addressing the transparency crisis surrounding generative artificial intelligence datasets. By tracing the origin and processing of information, this interdisciplinary tool aims to clarify the legal and ethical complexities surrounding model training, responding to the alarming proportion of data available on open platforms without clear licensing. The relevance of this initiative is rooted in growing regulatory and social pressure on technology companies. With active copyright lawsuits and the emergence of tools designed to protect creative works, transparency in data provenance has shifted from a technical option to a vital competitive requirement. Organizations that prioritize accountability and regulatory compliance will gain a significant advantage in an increasingly scrutinized market. This development is fundamental to the open data movement, as it establishes a precedent for the necessary accountability within the AI ecosystem. By normalizing visibility into how data is obtained and transformed, it fosters a safer and more equitable environment, enabling future application development to be grounded in solid ethical principles rather than current legal ambiguity.
