Procuraduría Agraria debe informar sobre Tren Maya

The INAI’s ruling establishes that the Agrarian Prosecutor’s Office must provide information on the impact of the Maya Train on ejido lands, rejecting the use of national security as a pretext to withhold public data. This decision strengthens the fight against blanket information exemptions, ensuring that government projects do not exempt themselves from their obligation to be transparent with citizens. The article highlights the tension between decrees that classify certain works as matters of national security and the fundamental right to access information. By invalidating arguments that hinder the dissemination of documents, the INAI safeguards the integrity of the democratic process against potential administrative omissions. This situation is relevant to open data because it demonstrates that transparency must prevail over arbitrary classifications. Ensuring that data on infrastructure projects is accessible enables public scrutiny, accountability, and efficient resource management—principles that are fundamental to building reliable and useful open data systems.
