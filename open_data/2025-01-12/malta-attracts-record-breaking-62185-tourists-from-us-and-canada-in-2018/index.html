Malta attracts record-breaking 62,185 tourists from US and Canada in 2018

Malta attracts record-breaking 62,185 tourists from US and Canada in 2018

Malta’s tourism sector is experiencing significant growth in North America, with visitor numbers from the US and Canada reaching record levels. This surge highlights the island’s rising appeal as a premier Mediterranean destination, driven by its status as a European Capital of Culture and a robust calendar of off-peak events that sustain interest year-round. The success is attributed to strategic marketing efforts by the Malta Tourism Authority, including active engagement with travel industry networks and prominent media influencers. By leveraging these proactive outreach initiatives, Malta has effectively captured the attention of potential travelers, ensuring continued momentum in this lucrative market despite seasonal fluctuations. For open data, this trend underscores the value of accessible tourism statistics in tracking and understanding global travel patterns. Reliable, transparent data enables stakeholders to identify emerging markets, evaluate the impact of cultural events, and make informed decisions about resource allocation and strategic partnerships in the travel industry.
