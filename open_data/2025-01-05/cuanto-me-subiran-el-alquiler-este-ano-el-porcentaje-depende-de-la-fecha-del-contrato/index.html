¿Cuánto me subirán el alquiler este año? El porcentaje depende de la fecha del contrato

The return to the Consumer Price Index allows landlords to adjust rents in line with actual inflation, reversing the limitation imposed by the Competitiveness Guarantee Index. This flexibility ensures that rental income accurately reflects the cost of living, balancing the rights of property owners and tenants after years of artificial price controls. The distinction between update mechanisms underscores the transparency required in regulating private markets, a fundamental pillar of public accountability. By clarifying the indicators used, a precedent is set regarding how government decisions directly impact everyday contractual interactions. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of publishing accurate and accessible economic indicators. Citizens need clear data to understand how regulations are applied, fostering trust in institutions and enabling active oversight of the fairness of price adjustments.
