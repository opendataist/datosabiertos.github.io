Seoul captures 4th place in UIA International Meetings Cities Rankings

Seoul has solidified its status as a premier global hub for international meetings, securing fourth place in the UIA’s 2013 statistics for three consecutive years. This achievement highlights the capital’s growing appeal as a world-class destination, driven by significant upgrades in infrastructure and a robust commitment from both public and private sectors to maintain high standards in business event services. The city’s rising prominence is further evidenced by its entry into the ICCA top ten, reflecting broader international recognition of Seoul’s expanding capabilities. Continuous investment in new venues and redevelopment projects signals a strategic effort to meet increasing global demand, ensuring that the city remains competitive and responsive to the evolving needs of the international conference community. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized metrics from organizations like the UIA can effectively benchmark urban performance in the global events sector. By making such statistics accessible, stakeholders can better understand market trends, evaluate policy impacts, and leverage comparative data to support strategic planning and evidence-based decision-making in urban development and tourism promotion.
