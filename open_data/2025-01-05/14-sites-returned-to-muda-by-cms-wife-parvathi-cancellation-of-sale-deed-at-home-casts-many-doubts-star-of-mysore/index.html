14 sites returned to MUDA by CM’s wife Parvathi: Cancellation of sale deed at home casts many doubts - Star of Mysore

An RTI activist has petitioned the Lokayukta to investigate irregularities in land transactions involving the Chief Minister’s wife. The core concern centers on the suspicious cancellation and re-allotment of multiple plots to the Mysuru Urban Development Authority, questioning the procedural legitimacy of conducting such significant property registrations at a private residence rather than official government offices. The activist highlights critical inconsistencies in the timeline and documentation of these transactions. Specifically, concerns arise regarding the physical possibility of completing legal formalities within a short window and the accuracy of the residential addresses provided, which suggest a potential bypass of standard registration protocols intended for vulnerable groups. This case is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and verifiable government records. By demanding a thorough probe into alleged forged documents and procedural lapses, the appeal reinforces the importance of public scrutiny in ensuring accountability and preventing corruption in land administration systems.
