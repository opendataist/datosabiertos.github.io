En 10 meses de Milei cerraron 12.000 empresas y se perdieron 216 mil empleos privados – 4SEMANAS.COM

En 10 meses de Milei cerraron 12.000 empresas y se perdieron 216 mil empleos privados – 4SEMANAS.COM

The study concludes that fixed exchange rate policies, known as “La Tablita,” consistently lead to significant economic failure. These neoliberal strategies result in substantial job losses and a decline in registered employers, demonstrating that such measures do not foster growth but rather trigger economic contraction and structural damage to the labor market. Crucially, this research highlights how policy impacts differ by firm size. While small and medium enterprises suffer absolute losses in the number of businesses, large corporations account for the majority of eliminated jobs. This disparity reveals that macroeconomic shocks disproportionately affect employment scales, with large firms absorbing the brunt of workforce reductions despite representing a tiny fraction of the business population. This analysis is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent access to granular employment statistics enables rigorous critique of economic policies. By making detailed sectoral and size-specific data publicly available, researchers can expose the hidden costs of political decisions, fostering accountability and supporting evidence-based policy reforms rather than relying on opaque or aggregated economic indicators.
