Congress Receives Certificate of Completion of the 2026 National Census of State Legislative Powers - e-tlaxcala

The Congress of Tlaxcala received recognition from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) for its collaboration in the state legislative census. This act underscores the importance of accurate data collection to inform decision-making and evaluate public policies. The statistical information obtained enables the analysis of institutional performance, the optimization of administrative processes, and the strengthening of governmental transparency. In this way, it ensures better oversight and accountability to citizens. This initiative is significant for open data, as it demonstrates how collaboration between public institutions and statistical agencies facilitates access to verifiable data. This strengthens transparency and empowers civil society with reliable information for democratic scrutiny.
