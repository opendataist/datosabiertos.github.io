Querétaro makes history in security and ranks in the national top 10: Felifer Macías - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

Querétaro makes history in security and ranks in the national top 10: Felifer Macías - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

Querétaro reached its highest-ever level of perceived safety according to the National Survey on Urban Security and Justice (ENSU) conducted by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI), solidifying its position as the second safest state capital in the country. This prominent ranking reflects the success of preventive strategies implemented by the local government and their coordination with state and federal institutions. Citizens’ trust in the municipal police and their positive evaluation of the police’s performance also recorded significant increases. These achievements are directly attributed to continuous street-level work, increased police presence, and a community-oriented approach through specialized programs that strengthen community safety. This case illustrates the importance of open data in accountability. By using standardized indicators such as the ENSU, citizens can objectively monitor the effectiveness of public policies. Transparency in these measurements allows for the assessment of the real impact of security interventions and requires officials to maintain the quality of public services based on verifiable and accessible evidence.
