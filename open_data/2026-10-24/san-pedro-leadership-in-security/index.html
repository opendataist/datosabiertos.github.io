San Pedro: Leadership in Security

San Pedro: Leadership in Security

San Pedro Garza García has consolidated its position as Mexico’s safest municipality, thanks to the results of the National Survey of Urban Public Security. This achievement is no coincidence; it reflects a comprehensive strategy that prioritizes the professionalization of police forces, the use of technology, and close engagement with citizens. The high perception of safety and trust in local authorities demonstrate that transparent and efficient governance can significantly transform the relationship between the state and its citizens, establishing a model of administrative excellence to be followed at the national level. Beyond public safety, the municipality also stood out for the quality of its public services, receiving excellent ratings for urban maintenance, cleanliness, and the management of green spaces. These indicators underscore the importance of maintaining an orderly and clean environment as a fundamental component for improving quality of life. The consistency between the perception of safety and the effectiveness of basic services highlights that citizen well-being depends on a committed administration capable of responding promptly to the everyday needs of the population and maintaining high standards of municipal operations. This article is relevant to the field of open data because it illustrates the practical value of transparent government statistics. By publishing verifiable metrics on safety and services, institutions enable civil society to monitor public performance and demand accountability based on concrete evidence. The availability of such data fosters social trust and demonstrates how access to high-quality public information is essential for democratic scrutiny and the continuous improvement of urban policies, thereby strengthening the open government ecosystem.
