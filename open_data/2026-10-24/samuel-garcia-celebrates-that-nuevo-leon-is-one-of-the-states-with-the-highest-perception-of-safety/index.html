Samuel García celebrates that Nuevo León is one of the states with the highest perception of safety

The governor of Nuevo León highlighted that a significant majority of the population feels safe in their city, according to the latest results of the National Survey of Urban Public Security (ENSU). This positive indicator places the state among the top nationally in terms of perceived safety, reflecting a growing environment of trust for residents and visitors. This improvement in the perception of safety is attributed to multidisciplinary collaboration and the joint efforts of security institutions. The vital role of the Secretariat of National Defense was particularly emphasized, recognizing its historical commitment to providing assistance during emergencies and natural disasters, which strengthens the state’s capacity to protect citizens during critical moments. The article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the use of official statistical indicators (ENSU) to measure and communicate the performance of public policies. Transparency in these data allows citizens and analysts to objectively assess the state of security and the effectiveness of government strategies, fostering accountability based on verifiable and accessible evidence.
