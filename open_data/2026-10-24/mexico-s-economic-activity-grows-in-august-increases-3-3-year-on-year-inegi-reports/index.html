Mexico's economic activity grows in August; increases 3.3% year-on-year, INEGI reports

Mexico's economic activity grows in August; increases 3.3% year-on-year, INEGI reports

The Global Indicator of Economic Activity reflects growth driven by the secondary and tertiary sectors, highlighting the resilience of the Mexican economy in the face of external factors. This dynamic counteracts the contraction of the primary sector, demonstrating a moderate but sustained recovery in key industrial and service activities. The historical trend points to a gradual slowdown compared to previous years, underscoring the importance of accurately monitoring macroeconomic data. This historical context is crucial for understanding the country’s evolution and adjusting medium-term growth expectations, avoiding overly optimistic projections without a solid foundation. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates how official indicators, when made available in a transparent and detailed manner, enable civil society and academia to analyze disparities between sectors. Access to this information facilitates independent research and promotes accountability regarding the country’s true economic health.
