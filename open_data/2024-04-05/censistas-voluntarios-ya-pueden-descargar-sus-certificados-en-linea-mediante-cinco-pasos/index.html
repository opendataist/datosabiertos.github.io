Censistas voluntarios ya pueden descargar sus certificados en línea mediante cinco pasos

The Bolivian National Statistics Institute has enabled volunteers to download digital certificates for the 2024 census, featuring verifiable QR codes to ensure authenticity. This streamlined process validates the massive volunteer effort, acknowledging the significant participation of diverse demographics, including many young people. This initiative matters for open data because it demonstrates how institutions can efficiently validate and credential public contributions using transparent digital tools. By making these credentials easily accessible and verifiable, the INE encourages future civic engagement in data collection initiatives, fostering trust and participation in official statistical operations.
