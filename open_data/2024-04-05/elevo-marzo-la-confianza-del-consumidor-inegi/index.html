Elevó marzo la confianza del consumidor: Inegi

The INEGI and Banxico reported a slight increase in consumer confidence for March, driven by optimistic economic expectations for the coming year, despite current challenges in durable goods purchases. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, high-quality public statistics. Such accessible metrics enable researchers and policymakers to accurately analyze trends in economic sentiment. Ultimately, open access to these indicators promotes informed decision-making. By making these results publicly available, the institutions ensure transparency, allowing society to better understand and respond to economic changes.
