Gobierno municipal de Acatlán de Pérez Figueroa viola ley de transparencia - Plumas Libres

The Acatlán municipal government failed to provide the requested workforce data within the legally mandated timeframe, thereby violating transparency laws. This non-compliance undermines public trust in local authorities and reflects a lack of accountability toward citizens who demand open government practices. Such incidents highlight the significant gap between legislative mandates and actual administrative implementation in Mexico. They underscore the persistent challenges that institutions face in enforcing the right to access public information, despite clear legal frameworks designed to ensure equal treatment and prevent discrimination. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how bureaucratic inertia can obstruct the dissemination of essential public statistics. Without the timely release of reliable data, civil society cannot effectively monitor employment costs or evaluate the efficiency of governance, thereby weakening the democratic potential of transparent information ecosystems.
