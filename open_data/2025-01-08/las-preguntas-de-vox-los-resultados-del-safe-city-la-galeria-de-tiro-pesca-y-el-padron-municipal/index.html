Las preguntas de Vox: Los resultados del Safe City, la galería de tiro, pesca y el padrón municipal

Vox challenges the executive branch over the Safe City system, questioning its high costs, maintenance status, and actual effectiveness in combating persistent criminal activity. This inquiry underscores the critical need for transparent public spending data and measurable outcomes in security technologies, directly linking government expenditure to tangible public safety results. The opposition also demands information on delayed fisheries councils and the implementation of blue economy plans. These requests highlight the importance of accessible data on public policy execution and sectoral development plans, ensuring that economic initiatives are transparently monitored and properly enforced to foster regional growth. Furthermore, Vox seeks updates on the promised shooting range and the enforcement of ordinances against false residency registrations. This focus on administrative transparency emphasizes the necessity of open data for verifying legal compliance and ensuring efficient public service delivery. Ultimately, these interventions illustrate how open data principles enable civic oversight, holding authorities accountable for the real-world impact of their decisions and expenditures on community well-being.
