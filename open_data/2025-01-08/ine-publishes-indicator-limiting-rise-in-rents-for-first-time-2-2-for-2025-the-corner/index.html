INE publishes indicator limiting rise in rents for first time: 2.2% for 2025 | The Corner

The Spanish government caps rent hikes at 2.2% for 2025, aligning with lower inflation metrics rather than actual market rates. However, real prices are expected to surge significantly due to persistent housing shortages and legal uncertainties affecting new contracts. This regulatory approach highlights the gap between policy limits and market reality, where limited supply drives costs far beyond the mandated ceiling. The discrepancy underscores the necessity of addressing structural supply issues to effectively stabilize rental markets. For open data, this case illustrates how transparent access to official statistical indices and independent market trackers is crucial. It enables stakeholders to distinguish between nominal regulatory caps and actual economic pressures, fostering better-informed public discourse and policy evaluation.
