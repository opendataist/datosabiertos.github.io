PM Modi sets example by not using taxpayer money for personal festivities, RTI discloses

Recent RTI disclosures confirm that neither Prime Minister Modi nor former Prime Minister Singh used public funds for personal Diwali celebrations. This transparency underscores a commitment to ethical governance and financial propriety at the highest levels of state leadership. By distinguishing strictly between personal and official expenses, these leaders demonstrate rigorous adherence to public accountability standards. The activist who filed the query hopes this practice will inspire broader adoption across all political offices, ensuring uniform integrity in the handling of public resources. This sets a normative precedent for minimizing conflicts of interest and promoting fiscal responsibility beyond just the Prime Minister’s Office. Such consistency reinforces public trust in democratic institutions by showing that personal benefits are kept separate from state duties. For the open data community, this highlights the critical role of transparent information disclosure in verifying governmental claims. When data regarding public expenditures is accessible and verifiable, it enables civil society to hold leaders accountable. This case exemplifies how open data mechanisms can uncover specific behaviors, fostering a culture of honesty and ensuring that public funds are used solely for their intended civic purposes rather than private gain.
