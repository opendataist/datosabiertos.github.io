Arup, ODI, WRAP, AHDB and HESTIA partner to design and test Data Sharing Infrastructure (DSI) and governance for the UK agri-food sector

A new consortium including the Open Data Institute, Arup, and industry leaders is developing a Data Sharing Infrastructure and governance model for the UK’s agri-food sector. This initiative aims to standardize and improve the accessibility of environmental impact data, addressing critical gaps in consistency and trust that currently hinder progress. By creating a robust framework for data exchange, the project seeks to streamline reporting processes and reduce administrative burdens for farmers and producers while enhancing the overall reliability of sustainability metrics. The primary goal is to empower businesses, policymakers, and consumers with accurate information to drive sustainable choices and support the UK’s net-zero ambitions. Through pilot programs across various food supply chains, the initiative will demonstrate how trusted data sharing leads to stronger environmental reporting and accelerated digital innovation. These efforts are designed to provide an evidence base for future investments, ensuring that the infrastructure not only benefits the public but also supports the resilience and growth of the agri-food sector. This article is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the urgent need for standardized, transparent data ecosystems in critical industries. It underscores how open, trustworthy data infrastructure is essential for verifying environmental claims and enabling evidence-based decision-making. By focusing on interoperability and governance, the project offers a scalable model for how open data principles can be applied to complex supply chains to foster accountability and drive systemic sustainability improvements.
