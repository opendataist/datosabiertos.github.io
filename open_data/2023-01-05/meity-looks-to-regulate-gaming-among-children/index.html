MeitY Looks to Regulate Gaming Among Children

MeitY Looks to Regulate Gaming Among Children

India is drafting regulations to curb gaming addiction among minors by limiting screen time and blocking unauthorized in-game purchases. These measures aim to protect children through innovative monitoring mechanisms, such as anonymized usage reporting, while balancing the need for parent and industry consultation to create effective solutions. This regulatory shift is highly relevant to open data as it necessitates the collection and potential sharing of granular user behavior metrics. By requiring gaming platforms to submit anonymized reports on usage patterns, the policy creates a framework where aggregated, non-identifiable data can be made available for public oversight or further research. Ultimately, compliance with these rules will determine whether companies retain their legal safe harbor protections. The initiative signals a broader transition toward stricter accountability in the digital sector, emphasizing that transparent data practices and child safety safeguards are now central to operating legally in India’s evolving digital landscape.
