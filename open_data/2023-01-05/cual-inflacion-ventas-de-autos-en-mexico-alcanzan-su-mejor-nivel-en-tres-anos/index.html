¿Cuál inflación?: ventas de autos en México alcanzan su mejor nivel en tres años

Automotive sales demonstrated resilience against inflation and rising interest rates, signaling a recovery in the supply chain despite ongoing logistical challenges. This stability underscores how local markets adapt to economic pressures, providing valuable context for open data analysis aimed at enhancing supply chain transparency. Analysts forecast modest growth for 2023, tempered by a reduction in credits and the emergence of a recession. These insights facilitate the tracking of sector health over time. This data remains crucial for open data initiatives monitoring industrial health and economic recovery trends. Understanding these patterns enables a more effective evaluation of market resilience and policy effectiveness in challenging economic environments.
