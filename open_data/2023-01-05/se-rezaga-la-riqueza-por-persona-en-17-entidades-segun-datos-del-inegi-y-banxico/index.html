Se rezaga la riqueza por persona en 17 entidades, según datos del Inegi y Banxico

The article highlights significant economic disparities across Mexican states, revealing that many regions lag far behind the national average in per capita wealth. This data underscores the critical need for open data initiatives to improve transparency regarding regional inequalities. By making such economic indicators publicly accessible, stakeholders can better analyze how resource distribution impacts local development. Open access to this information facilitates more informed policy decisions aimed at reducing the gap between wealthy and impoverished areas. Ultimately, democratizing access to these statistics empowers communities to advocate for equitable growth. It ensures that efforts to address economic disparities are grounded in accurate, widely available evidence rather than speculation.
