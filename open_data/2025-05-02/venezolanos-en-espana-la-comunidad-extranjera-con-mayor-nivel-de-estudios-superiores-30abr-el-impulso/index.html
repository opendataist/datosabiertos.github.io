▷ Venezolanos en España: La comunidad extranjera con mayor nivel de estudios superiores #30Abr - El Impulso

▷ Venezolanos en España: La comunidad extranjera con mayor nivel de estudios superiores #30Abr - El Impulso

Spanish statistics show that Venezuelan and Argentine residents have significantly higher levels of education than the general immigrant population, with nearly half holding university degrees. This disparity highlights how national origin heavily influences the academic profile of migrants within the country. The data underscores a stark contrast between highly educated groups from Europe and Latin America, and those with primarily primary education from North Africa and the Caribbean. Such granular breakdowns illustrate the diverse socioeconomic backgrounds shaping the nation’s foreign resident demographic. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of disaggregated census records. By making detailed educational metrics publicly available, policymakers and researchers can better understand migration trends, integrate skilled workers, and design targeted social programs based on precise, accessible statistical evidence.
