HMRC launches 10,000 Inheritance Tax probes into grieving families

HMRC launches 10,000 Inheritance Tax probes into grieving families

A significant surge in HMRC inheritance tax investigations highlights the growing burden on grieving families. Nearly ten thousand probes have been launched over three years, with thousands remaining unresolved. These inquiries often compound the distress of bereavement by scrutinizing estates for underpayments, errors, or undervalued assets, leveraging substantial investigatory powers. Concerns are mounting regarding future policy changes, such as counting unspent private pensions toward taxable estates and freezing thresholds. Experts predict these measures will drastically increase the number of estates liable for tax, placing unprecedented financial pressure on more families than seen in decades. The high interest rates on overdue payments further exacerbate these challenges. This situation is critically relevant to open data because the transparency of these investigation statistics reveals systemic inefficiencies and their human impact. By exposing the volume and duration of these probes, open data enables public scrutiny of government revenue collection practices. It underscores the need for accountability in how tax authorities manage sensitive cases during personal loss, highlighting the importance of accessible information for policy debate.
