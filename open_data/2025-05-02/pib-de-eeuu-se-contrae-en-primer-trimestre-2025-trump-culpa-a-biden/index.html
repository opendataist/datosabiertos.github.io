U.S. GDP decreased slightly in the first quarter, marking the first contraction in three years. This negative variation is primarily attributed to the rise in imports and the reduction in government spending, factors that detract from the official economic calculation. President Trump blamed the previous administration for these results, distancing himself from the decline and promising that tariffs and protectionist policies would attract record investments. His team defended national economic security despite the uncertainty generated by the trade war, maintaining a narrative of imminent structural improvement. This analysis is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of verifying official statistical sources. In the face of political manipulation of macroeconomic indicators, ensuring transparent access and critical use of public data is essential to discern the real causes of economic behavior and combat misinformation.
