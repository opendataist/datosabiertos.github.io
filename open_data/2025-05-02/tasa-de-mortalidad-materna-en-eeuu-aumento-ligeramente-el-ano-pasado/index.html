Tasa de mortalidad materna en EEUU aumentó ligeramente el año pasado

Tasa de mortalidad materna en EEUU aumentó ligeramente el año pasado

The recent rise in maternal mortality in the United States marks a dangerous reversal after two years of improvement, highlighting the fragility of prior health gains. This shift reveals that structural factors, such as the closure of rural hospitals and legal restrictions on abortion, are undermining access to essential care during pregnancy and childbirth. These findings are crucial for the open data movement, as they underscore the need for transparency in the collection and publication of public health statistics. Disaggregated data enable the rapid identification of alarming trends and systemic inequalities, such as racial disparities, that might otherwise remain hidden. Making these issues visible through open data is vital for driving effective public policies and accountability. By making this information accessible, it facilitates civic and academic oversight, ensuring that improvements in maternal health are not lost due to administrative barriers or opaque legislative decisions that disproportionately affect vulnerable populations.
