EpData.- Listado de gráficos y conjuntos de datos publicados por EpData este jueves

EpData releases new datasets showing Spain’s improving economic deficit and stabilizing fuel prices, alongside political polls indicating strong rejection of Catalan independence. Public health data reveals rising general hospitalizations but fewer infectious disease cases. These free statistical resources are vital for open data, enabling transparent, data-driven insights into national economic, political, and social trends for researchers and the public.
