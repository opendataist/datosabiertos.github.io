BJP's Suvendu Adhikari files RTI over Kolkata building collapse - OrissaPOST

The tragic building collapse in Kolkata has triggered a significant Right to Information request by opposition leader Suvendu Adhikari. This action highlights the critical role of public data in exposing municipal unpreparedness and ensuring accountability for safety failures. The inquiry aims to compile extensive records on building plan sanctions and illegal structure demotions. By accessing this information, stakeholders can better assess the risks posed by unauthorized construction, emphasizing how transparent data is essential for urban safety and regulatory oversight. This case underscores the relevance of open data in holding authorities responsible for infrastructure negligence. It demonstrates how citizens and politicians use information access to demand stricter enforcement and prevent future disasters, proving that transparency is a vital tool for civic protection.
