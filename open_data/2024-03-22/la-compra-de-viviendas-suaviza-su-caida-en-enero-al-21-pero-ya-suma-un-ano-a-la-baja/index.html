La compra de viviendas suaviza su caída en enero al 2,1% pero ya suma un año a la baja

La compra de viviendas suaviza su caída en enero al 2,1% pero ya suma un año a la baja

The article highlights a pivotal shift in the real estate market, where monthly transaction volumes surged significantly after a period of decline. Although year-on-year figures for second-hand housing decreased slightly, the sharp month-over-month increase suggests that buyers, previously hesitant due to high interest rates, are beginning to re-enter the market. This indicates that the severe contraction seen in the previous year may be bottoming out, with experts anticipating a gradual recovery driven by anticipated monetary easing. Geographically, the recovery is uneven, with substantial growth in regions like Castilla-La Mancha and Galicia, contrasting with drops in major hubs like Madrid and the Basque Country. New home sales notably outperformed the secondary market, signaling early signs of stabilization. Crucially, while transaction volumes are recovering, experts warn that the limited housing stock will likely prevent returns to peak levels seen previously and could instead accelerate price growth as latent demand absorbs available inventory. This data is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical releases from the National Statistics Institute (INE) serve as the foundational infrastructure for economic analysis. The availability of such granular, public data allows analysts, policymakers, and the real estate sector to move beyond speculation, enabling precise tracking of market trends, regional disparities, and the direct impact of monetary policy on housing accessibility and investment decisions.
