Bolivia garantiza ejercicio censal pese a ataques opositores - Noticias Prensa Latina

Bolivia garantiza ejercicio censal pese a ataques opositores - Noticias Prensa Latina

Bolivia has completed preparations for the 2024 census, leveraging modern georeferenced cartography and extensive international technical support to ensure high data standards. This technological advancement marks a significant evolution from previous censuses, providing a robust statistical instrument essential for national development planning and accurate demographic analysis. Despite these technical improvements, the process faces political scrutiny, with opposition figures alleging potential fraud and the misuse of data for political persecution. These controversies highlight the tension between state administration and civil society, raising concerns about privacy and the politicization of sensitive public information. Addressing these fears is crucial to maintaining public trust in the integrity of the collected data. This scenario is highly relevant to open data principles, as it underscores the necessity of transparency, security, and neutrality in public statistical operations. Ensuring that census data is accessible, protected from political manipulation, and used solely for development planning helps establish a culture of accountability. It demonstrates how reliable open data infrastructure can mitigate misinformation and foster democratic oversight in national governance.
