10 consejos de seguridad para evitar el mal uso de datos personales al comprar en línea

The article underscores the critical importance of protecting personal data during online transactions, highlighting that despite the high adoption of e-commerce in Mexico, a significant portion of users remain deeply concerned about cyber threats. Many consumers feel their devices are inadequately protected, fostering a climate of distrust that hinders further growth in digital commerce. This context highlights the urgent need for robust security measures to maintain consumer confidence and ensure the sustainable development of the digital market. Central to the discussion are actionable strategies for individuals to safeguard their information, such as verifying secure connections, avoiding public Wi-Fi for payments, and using dedicated email addresses or virtual cards. These practices mitigate risks like identity theft and financial fraud, which are prevalent concerns among internet users. By adopting these precautions, shoppers can reduce their vulnerability to malicious activities, ensuring that their personal and financial data remain confidential and secure during digital interactions. This content is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the tension between data utility and privacy in the digital economy. As open data initiatives aim to increase transparency and accessibility, they must also address the security concerns that deter public participation in digital ecosystems. Ensuring that data handling respects privacy standards and protects against misuse is essential for building trust, which is a foundational requirement for any successful open data strategy or widespread adoption of online services.
