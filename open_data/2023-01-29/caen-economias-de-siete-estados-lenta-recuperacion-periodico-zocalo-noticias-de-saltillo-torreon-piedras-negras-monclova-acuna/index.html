Caen economías de siete estados; lenta recuperación | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The report highlights significant regional economic disparities, with some states experiencing substantial contractions while others posted robust growth, revealing uneven recovery patterns across the country. This unevenness suggests that localized economic shocks and varying recovery speeds are critical factors for understanding national economic health. For open data, these findings underscore the necessity of granular, high-frequency subnational indicators. Such detailed metrics allow for better monitoring of regional inequalities and help policymakers design targeted interventions, ensuring that data transparency supports equitable development strategies. The relevance lies in the ability of open datasets to track these divergent trends in real time. By making state-level economic activity accessible, stakeholders can analyze local drivers of growth or decline, fostering informed decision-making and enhancing accountability in public resource allocation.
