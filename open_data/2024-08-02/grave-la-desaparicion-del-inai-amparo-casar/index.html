Grave la desaparición del INAI: Amparo Casar

The potential loss of INAI’s autonomy threatens data privacy, as personal information could fall under executive control. This shift undermines independent oversight, exacerbating risks of misuse and lack of accountability for citizens' sensitive data. Such a change is critical for open data frameworks, where institutional independence ensures transparency and protects against state overreach. Weakening these safeguards jeopardizes public trust and the integrity of information governance systems essential for democratic accountability.
