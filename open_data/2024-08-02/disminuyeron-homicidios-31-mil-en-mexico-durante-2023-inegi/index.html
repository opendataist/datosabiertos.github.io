Disminuyeron homicidios; 31 mil en México durante 2023: Inegi

The article reports a preliminary decline in Mexico’s homicide rate in 2023 compared to 2022, highlighting a downward trend since 2019. This shift underscores evolving security dynamics, with firearms remaining the primary weapon used in these violent acts. The relevance to open data lies in the reliance on aggregated records from civil registry and forensic services. Understanding these data sources and their limitations is crucial for accurately interpreting statistical trends and ensuring transparency in public safety metrics. Ultimately, this information enables analysts to track progress in crime reduction policies. By examining these open datasets, stakeholders can better assess the effectiveness of interventions and identify areas requiring further attention to improve national security.
