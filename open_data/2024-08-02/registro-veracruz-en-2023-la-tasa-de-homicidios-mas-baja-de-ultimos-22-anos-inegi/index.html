Registró Veracruz en 2023 la tasa de homicidios más baja de últimos 22 años: INEGI

Veracruz reported its lowest homicide rate in 22 years, marking a significant decline in violent deaths. This trend highlights a sustained improvement in public security metrics over the last decade, moving away from the peak violence seen in earlier years. The data, compiled by INEGI from civil registry and forensic records, demonstrates the power of aggregated administrative sources. By integrating reports from multiple state agencies, the institute provides a comprehensive view of security trends that individual departments might not capture alone. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how standardized, interoperable public records enable transparent monitoring of social indicators. It underscores the importance of accessible, centralized datasets for accountability, allowing citizens and policymakers to verify government effectiveness and track progress in safety initiatives over time.
