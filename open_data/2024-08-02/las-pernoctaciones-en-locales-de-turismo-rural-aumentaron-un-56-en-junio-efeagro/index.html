Las pernoctaciones en locales de turismo rural aumentaron un 5,6 % en junio - EFEAgro

Rural tourism accommodations in Spain experienced a notable increase in overnight stays and prices in June, with significant growth largely driven by international visitors. This trend underscores a robust recovery and rising demand for rural tourism, particularly in regions such as the Balearic Islands, where both occupancy rates and average daily rates surged substantially. The data show that while domestic stays grew modestly, foreign tourism saw a sharp increase, highlighting the strong appeal of rural destinations to international travelers. Although the average length of stay decreased slightly, overall occupancy rates for the first half of the year remained positive, indicating sustained interest in this sector. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible statistical reports, such as those published by the National Statistics Institute (INE), enable transparent monitoring of economic sectors. By analyzing these public datasets, researchers and policymakers can identify regional disparities and shifting tourism patterns, thereby fostering evidence-based decisions for sustainable rural development and infrastructure planning.
