Release of Northern Ireland Inpatient, Day Case and Outpatient hospital statistics for 2023/24

This report highlights a partial recovery in Northern Ireland’s hospital activity, with inpatient and day case admissions rising compared to the previous year but remaining below pre-pandemic levels. A notable structural shift is the increased reliance on day cases and virtual outpatient consultations, reflecting a broader trend toward more efficient, community-based care models. This evolution suggests a gradual normalization of healthcare delivery, though the system continues to operate under constraints regarding bed capacity and patient flow. Appointment dynamics reveal significant changes in how care is accessed and managed. While missed patient appointments have remained relatively stable, hospital cancellation rates have decreased, indicating some improvement in operational stability. However, the persistence of substantial cancellation and attendance gaps underscores ongoing challenges in service reliability. The continued presence of virtual care options, even as face-to-face services return, offers a sustainable alternative that may help alleviate pressure on physical infrastructure and reduce travel burdens for patients. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the critical need for timely, accurate, and transparent public health information. By making complex operational metrics—such as bed occupancy, treatment volumes, and appointment status—accessible, authorities enable better public scrutiny and evidence-based policymaking. Reliable open datasets empower researchers and citizens to understand healthcare trends, identify systemic inefficiencies, and hold providers accountable, ultimately fostering trust and improving health outcomes through data-driven transparency.
