Blanca Fernández destaca el mejor dato semestral de turismo en la provincia de Ciudad Real de los últimos 15 años con 360.000 pernoctaciones

The article highlights a historic surge in tourism for Ciudad Real and Castilla-La Mancha, marking the best start for the sector in fifteen years. This growth is characterized not only by high visitor numbers but also by an increase in average stay duration, which aligns with the government’s strategic goal of diversifying the local economy. By encouraging longer stays, the administration aims to stabilize rural populations and reduce seasonal volatility, ensuring that tourism becomes a sustainable driver for regional development rather than a fleeting phenomenon. A central component of this strategy is the shift toward sustainable and sports tourism, exemplified by the expansion of bicycle infrastructure and rental services. The government is investing in connecting cities with natural parks through extensive cycling routes, fostering a model that appeals to environmentally conscious travelers. This approach seeks to balance economic benefits with ecological preservation, addressing the challenge of mobility saturation in protected areas like the Ruidera Lakes. The initiative emphasizes innovation and entrepreneurship as key tools for managing tourist flows while protecting natural landscapes from overuse. This report is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly available statistics from the National Statistics Institute (INE) serve as a critical basis for public policy and political justification. It illustrates the practical application of open data in monitoring sectoral performance, guiding infrastructure investments, and validating the success of regional strategies. By making these metrics transparent, the government enables stakeholders to assess the effectiveness of sustainable tourism initiatives and ensures accountability in the management of public funds aimed at preserving natural heritage.
