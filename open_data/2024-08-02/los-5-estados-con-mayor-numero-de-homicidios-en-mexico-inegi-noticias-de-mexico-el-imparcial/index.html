Los 5 estados con mayor número de homicidios en México: Inegi | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

Mexico’s 2023 homicide data reveal a notable decline in violence compared to previous years, marking the lowest level in five years. This reduction reflects a significant shift in national safety trends, suggesting that policy changes or social dynamics may be influencing criminal activity rates across the country. The analysis highlights stark regional disparities, with Guanajuato recording the highest number of incidents due to cartel conflicts, while southern states remain safer. Furthermore, the persistence of gun violence as the primary method underscores a critical security challenge. These geographic and methodological patterns provide essential context for understanding the drivers of localized violence in Mexico. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent access to statistical information enables rigorous public scrutiny of government performance. By making detailed figures available, citizens and researchers can independently verify official narratives, fostering accountability and informed civic engagement regarding national security policies.
