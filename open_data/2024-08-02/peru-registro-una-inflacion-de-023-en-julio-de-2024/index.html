Perú registró una inflación de 0,23% en julio de 2024

Perú registró una inflación de 0,23% en julio de 2024

The INEI’s July 2024 report reveals a modest national inflation rate, driven primarily by increases in transportation and food costs, particularly vegetables and poultry. While prices for communications and certain seafood decreased, the broader trend indicates rising living expenses in key consumption categories. This data provides a granular view of how specific household items impact the general cost of living, highlighting the volatile nature of agricultural and transport sectors. Lima Metropolitana experienced slightly higher inflation than the national average, with underlying inflation excluding food and energy also showing an upward trajectory. The detailed breakdown illustrates that price fluctuations are not uniform; they are heavily influenced by specific commodity shifts, such as sharp rises in maize and potatoes versus drops in tomatoes and sardines. Understanding these divergent trends is essential for analyzing purchasing power and economic stability at the household level. This information is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the value of transparent, high-resolution statistical publishing. By making detailed indices available, institutions empower researchers and developers to create advanced analytics, visualize economic disparities across regions, and build tools that help citizens understand personal finance impacts. Open access to such nuanced datasets fosters evidence-based policymaking and enhances civic engagement with economic realities.
