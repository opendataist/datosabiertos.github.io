Crece actividad económica de México 2.5% interanual en abril

Mexico’s economic activity grew significantly in April, driven by broad-based increases across the primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors. This preliminary indicator, the IGAE, signals positive short-term momentum following a strong first-quarter performance, reflecting the economy’s resilience in the post-pandemic period. The data highlight a robust recovery, with services leading the expansion. This growth suggests stability and the potential to achieve government targets, contrasting sharply with the severe contraction experienced during the global health crisis. This information is relevant to open data because the IGAE exemplifies high-quality, official statistical releases. Transparent, timely, and accessible economic indicators empower researchers and citizens to analyze trends effectively, fostering accountability and informed decision-making based on trustworthy public data.
