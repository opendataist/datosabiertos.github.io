Nuevo repunte de los suicidios: en 2022 se quitaron la vida 4.097 personas, un 2,3 % más

Recent provisional data from Spain’s National Statistics Institute reveals a concerning upward trend in suicides and deaths from external causes. The statistics indicate that mental health crises and accidental fatalities are rising, with suicides increasing notably among adults. This pattern extends to other external causes, such as traffic accidents and drowning, suggesting broader public safety and health challenges that require immediate attention beyond mere statistical tracking. The article highlights the implementation of the euthanasia law, reporting hundreds of cases in which assisted dying was legally carried out. Most beneficiaries were elderly individuals suffering from severe neurological conditions or cancer. These figures provide critical insights into how end-of-life care policies are being put into practice, reflecting the intersection of medical ethics, legal frameworks, and demographic realities within the healthcare system. This dataset is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible government statistics for public policy analysis. By publishing detailed information on causes of death and legal procedures, the state enables researchers and citizens to examine societal trends. Open access to such sensitive information fosters accountability, supports evidence-based decision-making, and encourages public dialogue on urgent health and social issues.
