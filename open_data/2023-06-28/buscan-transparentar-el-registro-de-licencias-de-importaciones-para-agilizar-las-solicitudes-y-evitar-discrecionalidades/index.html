Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The project proposed by the Civic Coalition aims to establish an Open Data Portal for Argentina’s import system, prioritizing maximum transparency in licensing processes. The initiative argues that the current discretion in approving imports generates serious risks of corruption and economic distortions. By requiring that all information be available in open and updated formats, the project empowers citizens to monitor administrative decisions, aligning with international standards that link free access to regulation with the reduction of bureaucratic abuses. The proposal also introduces mechanisms for legal certainty, limiting administrative discretion through strict and automatic deadlines. If authorities do not resolve a request within the established timeframe, it is considered approved, thereby eliminating prolonged uncertainty. This measure not only expedites procedures but also obliges the administration to publish clear criteria regarding taxpayers’ financial capacity, ensuring predictability and preventing the arbitrary application of exchange restrictions that unreasonably protect foreign exchange reserves. This article is fundamental to the field of open data because it exemplifies how the technological publication of government information serves as a tool for accountability. Beyond mere regulatory compliance, it demonstrates that interoperability and real-time data access are essential to combating state opacity. By transforming operational data into accessible information, it creates an ecosystem where transparency acts as an automatic control system, reducing the need for constant human oversight and strengthening institutional trust.
