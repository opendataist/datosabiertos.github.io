¿Le gustó? Este es el once ideal del FPC que publicó una reconocido portal de datos

The conclusion of the 2023-I BetPlay League was marked by Millonarios’ championship title, representing a historic milestone for the club under the guidance of manager Alberto Gamero. Beyond the trophy itself, the tournament solidified its reputation as a highly balanced competition, highlighting the coexistence of experienced veterans and emerging talents, and demonstrating a widespread technical quality that even surprised teams eliminated early in the tournament. The relevance of this event for open data lies in the public debate sparked by the “ideal eleven” proposed by SofaScore, a platform specializing in sports metrics. This lineup, based on algorithmically calculated performance averages, generated controversy by prioritizing statistical analysis over traditional football narratives, including players from non-finalist clubs while excluding key figures from the champion team. This illustrates how objective data can reinterpret sporting reality, challenging the subjective perceptions of fans and analysts. The article is significant for the data community because it exemplifies the practical application of advanced analytics in professional sports. The discussion surrounding the composition of the ideal team demonstrates the capacity of open or accessible datasets to democratize talent evaluation, allowing quantitative metrics to influence media discourse and the assessment of athletic performance, regardless of final results or the popularity of the athletes.
