INAI recibe reconocimiento internacional

International authorities endorse the INAI as a global model for the right to know, highlighting its role as a promoter of transparency. This support aims to encourage Mexico to resume institutional activities and appoint the missing commissioners. Restoring the quorum is crucial to unblocking thousands of pending files awaiting review. The relevance for open data lies in the fact that institutional stability ensures the effective fulfillment of public information obligations, guaranteeing that citizens can access their data and protected rights without bureaucratic interruptions.
