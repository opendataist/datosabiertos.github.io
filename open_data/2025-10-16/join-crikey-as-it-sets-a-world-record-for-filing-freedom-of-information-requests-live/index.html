Join Crikey as it sets a world record for filing freedom of information requests, live!

Proposed reforms threaten to severely undermine Australia’s freedom of information system, creating a risk of increased government secrecy. This context highlights the critical importance of open data frameworks, as current mechanisms remain fragile and less robust than international counterparts. Protecting these systems is essential for maintaining public accountability and ensuring citizens can access vital government information. In response to this impending threat, a media outlet launched a campaign to utilize existing FOI laws before they are weakened. By inviting public participation, they aim to demonstrate the system’s potential and counteract efforts to restrict transparency. This initiative underscores the value of active civic engagement in preserving open data access against political rollback. The project culminates in a marathon event where staff process hundreds of human-written requests live. This effort seeks to set a record for volume while emphasizing genuine citizen inquiry over automated abuse. Ultimately, it reinforces the principle that robust open data practices depend on sustained public interest and direct interaction with government records.
