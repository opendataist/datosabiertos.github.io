CEOE CEPYME Cuenca apunta que los precios se mantienen en septiembre, pero la inflación interanual sigue elevada

The article highlights that while monthly prices in Cuenca remained stable, the annual inflation rate of 2.6% exceeds sustainable levels, creating economic instability. This persistent increase elevates business costs and reduces household purchasing power, negatively impacting credit access and overall confidence. The relevance to open data lies in monitoring these indicators to understand how transparent statistical tracking allows businesses and families to assess economic health and anticipate financial risks effectively. Specific sectors show divergent trends, with energy and housing costs rising significantly while clothing prices decline. These variations underscore the complexity of local economic pressures compared to regional and national averages. Analyzing disaggregated data helps identify vulnerable groups and specific cost drivers. Open data initiatives facilitate this granular analysis, enabling stakeholders to pinpoint exactly where inflationary pressures are most acute, such as in essential utilities, thereby supporting more targeted policy responses. Future projections suggest moderate inflation if current trends hold, yet uncertainty remains due to international factors and internal demand. Continuous access to reliable, open statistical data is crucial for navigating these unknowns. By providing timely insights into price evolution, open data empowers economic actors to make informed decisions. This transparency fosters resilience against external shocks, ensuring that local economies can adapt more swiftly to changing market conditions and cost structures.
