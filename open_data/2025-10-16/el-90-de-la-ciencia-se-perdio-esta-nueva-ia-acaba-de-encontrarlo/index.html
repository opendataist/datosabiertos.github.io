El 90% de la ciencia se perdió. Esta nueva IA acaba de encontrarlo -

El 90% de la ciencia se perdió. Esta nueva IA acaba de encontrarlo -

Science is facing a crisis of inefficiency, where the vast majority of research data is lost, hindering discoveries in critical areas such as health and climate change. To address this, Frontiers has introduced an AI-driven data management system that automates validation, formatting, and publication, ensuring that every dataset contributes to collective progress. This innovation transforms theoretical principles into a practical infrastructure that guarantees scientific investment translates into real, sustainable advances. At the core of this solution is the operational implementation of the FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable). The system not only stores information but also verifies its quality and compatibility for both humans and machines, providing certificates and interactive portals. This enables responsible and efficient reuse of data, facilitating its integration across different fields of study and eliminating the technical barriers that previously hindered knowledge exchange between disciplines. This initiative is fundamental to open data because it democratizes access to scientific information and establishes a new standard of ethics and recognition. By ensuring that researchers receive credit for their data and that these data are accessible to everyone, it fosters a culture of transparent collaboration. This accelerates the resolution of complex global challenges, maximizing the social value of science and ensuring that generated knowledge does not remain isolated in individual laboratories.
