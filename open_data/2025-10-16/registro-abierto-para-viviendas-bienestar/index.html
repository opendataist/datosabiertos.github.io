Registro abierto para Viviendas Bienestar

Registro abierto para Viviendas Bienestar

Infonavit has launched the Welfare Housing program, enabling eligible workers with sufficient contributions to register for accessible housing units. This initiative specifically targets low-income earners, primarily those earning between one and two minimum wages, addressing their long-standing difficulties in securing formal credit. The project represents a significant shift toward dignified housing solutions adapted to local conditions, ensuring affordability for the most vulnerable workers. By simplifying registration through online portals, the institution aims to broaden access for those previously excluded from the housing market due to economic constraints. This development is crucial for open data, as it highlights the need for transparent tracking of social housing distribution. Clear, accessible data on beneficiary eligibility and construction progress allows for better accountability and helps stakeholders analyze the real impact of public welfare policies on housing equity.
