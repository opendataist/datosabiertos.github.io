Política de privacidad

Política de privacidad

This document is a privacy notice from SanDiegoRed®, outlining how they collect and process personal data under Mexican federal law. It details the specific types of information gathered, ranging from general contact details to employment history and transaction records, while clarifying that data shared on third-party social media platforms remains outside their legal responsibility. The notice emphasizes that data collection is strictly limited to fulfilling commercial, transactional, and recruitment purposes, requiring explicit or tacit user consent for any processing activities. The core conclusion is that user autonomy is central to their data management strategy. Individuals retain the right to access, correct, delete, or oppose the use of their personal information at any time. SanDiegoRed® establishes a formal procedure through a dedicated department to handle these requests, ensuring that users can control the dissemination and utility of their data, particularly regarding marketing communications. By providing clear channels for objection and limiting secondary uses without renewed consent, the company aims to maintain transparency and compliance with privacy regulations. This article is relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the critical boundary between publicly available information and protected personal data. While open data advocates for the release of non-sensitive datasets, this notice highlights the strict legal frameworks required to safeguard identifiable individual information. It serves as a practical example of how organizations must balance operational needs with privacy rights, demonstrating that even in digital service models, personal data cannot be treated as an open resource without robust consent mechanisms and security protocols in place.
