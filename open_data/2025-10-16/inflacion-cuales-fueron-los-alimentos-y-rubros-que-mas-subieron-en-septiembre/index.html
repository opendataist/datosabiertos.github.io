Inflación: cuáles fueron los alimentos y rubros que más subieron en septiembre

Argentina’s official statistics agency reported that the inflation rate in September stabilized at 2.1%, matching the level recorded in July. This data highlights specific price pressures within essential categories, particularly affecting food, housing, and education sectors, while indicating moderation in leisure and hospitality costs. Understanding these localized price shifts is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how granular statistical releases expose underlying economic trends. By breaking down broad inflation metrics into specific product variations, datasets become more valuable for analyzing consumer behavior and policy impact, rather than merely tracking aggregate percentages. Ultimately, this report underscores the importance of accessible, detailed public data in interpreting regional economic health. Such transparency enables researchers and citizens to move beyond general headlines, fostering a deeper understanding of how inflationary pressures disproportionately affect different segments of society and essential goods.
