Castilla y León avanza en transparencia para servir mejor y ser más útiles a los ciudadanos

Castile and León has strengthened its commitment to transparency by significantly expanding its open data portal, which now offers nearly 760 datasets across various categories. This continuous growth reflects a proactive strategy to make public administration more accessible, enabling any citizen to consult and use public information in an intuitive manner. The region not only facilitates access but also encourages the reuse of these datasets as a strategic resource to innovate and improve people’s daily lives. The relevance of this initiative is evident in the award-winning projects, which range from applications for rural tourism to educational tools leveraging artificial intelligence. These solutions demonstrate how public data can be transformed into value-added products that address real needs, support business decision-making, and promote economic development. By rewarding citizen creativity, the administration recognizes the power of collaboration between the public sector and society to generate tangible social and business impact. This progress is crucial for the open data movement, as it establishes a governance model in which transparency serves as the foundation of an equitable society. By leading in quality among Spanish portals, Castile and León demonstrates that data openness is not merely a legal requirement but an opportunity to build trust and efficiency. By treating data as the fuel of the digital age, the region fosters an ecosystem where shared and understandable information empowers citizens and strengthens democratic institutions.
