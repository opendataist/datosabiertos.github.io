El bolívar se desploma en febrero y acumula 18,4 % de devaluación en 2025

The Venezuelan bolívar continues to depreciate significantly against the US dollar, widening the gap between official and parallel exchange rates. This trend severely erodes purchasing power, as salaries and pensions paid in local currency lose value amid ongoing inflation and de facto dollarization. These financial pressures underscore the critical importance of transparent, accessible data for monitoring economic health and currency stability. Open data initiatives are essential to reveal the true state of markets, exposing disparities that official figures alone may obscure and empowering citizens and analysts to understand the real impact on their livelihoods.
