Nacimientos en Chile disminuyeron interanualmente un 4,3% en diciembre de 2024 | Diario Financiero

The statistical report reveals a demographic shift characterized by declining birth rates and a significant rise in civil unions, reflecting evolving family structures. This data underscores how vital statistics capture societal changes, moving beyond simple counts to inform public policy on social inclusion and reproductive health trends. Mortality rates increased, with deaths concentrated among the elderly, while infant mortality remained low. Understanding these patterns through disaggregated data enables targeted health interventions and accurate resource allocation, which are essential for building equitable and responsive public services. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accessible, granular vital statistics. Transparency in demographic metrics empowers researchers and citizens to analyze social trends, ensuring that government actions are evidence-based and aligned with the actual needs of the population.
