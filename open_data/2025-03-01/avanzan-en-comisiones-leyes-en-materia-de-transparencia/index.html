Avanzan en comisiones leyes en materia de transparencia

Avanzan en comisiones leyes en materia de transparencia

The Senate approved a decree transferring key transparency and data protection functions from the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) to the Secretariat of Anticorruption. Supporters argue that this reorganization preserves public access to government information and personal data without introducing secrecy, ensuring that citizens can still verify public spending. Opponents criticize the move as a politically motivated dissolution of the INAI, claiming it merely shifts resources within the government under the guise of austerity. They warn that centralizing these powers may weaken independent oversight mechanisms essential for accountability and robust open data ecosystems. This reform is critical for open data because it reshapes the institutional framework governing public information. Changes to who holds and controls data access directly impact transparency, data privacy standards, and the integrity of citizen access to government records, influencing how open data principles are implemented in Mexico.
