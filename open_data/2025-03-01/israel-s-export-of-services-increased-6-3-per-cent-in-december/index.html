Israel's export of services increased 6.3 per cent in December

Israel's export of services increased 6.3 per cent in December

Israel’s service exports rebounded significantly in late 2024, driven by gains in hi-tech and business sectors. This recovery highlights the resilience of digital and professional service industries, signaling a robust economic upswing beyond seasonal fluctuations. The data underscores the critical role of commercial services, which dominate the export landscape. By excluding start-ups, this snapshot reveals the stable growth of established sectors like software, R&D, and engineering, forming the backbone of the nation’s service trade. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how granular, sector-specific economic metrics can be made accessible. Transparent reporting of such diverse service categories enables better analysis of global trade flows and supports evidence-based policy decisions in the digital economy.
