Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The FAO provides essential open data on global potato production through its FAOSTAT database, offering transparency and accessibility for researchers and policymakers. Although originally published in late 2022, this data reflects 2021 statistics and serves as a critical baseline for understanding agricultural trends. The accessibility of such datasets empowers users to perform independent analyses, promoting evidence-based decision-making in food security and agricultural planning. China and India emerge as the dominant global producers, highlighting the central role of Asia in the potato supply chain. This concentration of production underscores specific regional strengths and vulnerabilities in the global market. Understanding these geographic disparities is vital for analyzing trade flows, supply chain resilience, and the economic impact of potato cultivation on major producing nations. This dataset is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it exemplifies how publicly available, standardized information can support global monitoring of food systems. By making detailed production metrics accessible, the FAO facilitates cross-country comparisons and fosters collaboration among stakeholders. Such open access encourages innovation in data analysis and helps identify opportunities for improving agricultural efficiency and sustainability worldwide.
