Dagyaw Central Luzon 2025 breaks barriers between gov’t, citizens - Punto! Central Luzon

President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. and local officials assert that open governance is fundamental to national prosperity, stability, and security. This philosophy was demonstrated in Central Luzon through the Dagyaw 2025 event, which facilitated direct dialogue between citizens and government agencies. By integrating town hall discussions with practical job fairs, the initiative exemplifies a collaborative approach where public engagement translates into tangible economic opportunities, reinforcing the concept of *bayanihan* or cooperative nation-building. A critical component of this open governance framework is transparent and compassionate communication. Officials emphasized that citizens must understand how decisions are made and where tax revenues are allocated. This transparency fosters trust and accountability, ensuring that every government peso is traceable. By clearly explaining the rationale behind policies, the government aims to empower citizens, transforming passive subjects into informed participants who can hold institutions accountable for their actions. The article highlights the youth not merely as future leaders but as essential agents of present-day democratic sustenance. Encouraging young people to actively question, suggest, and participate in governance ensures that open government remains dynamic and responsive. This relevance to open data lies in its recognition that accessible information and inclusive dialogue are prerequisites for a functional democracy. When data and decision-making processes are open, it empowers the next generation to innovate and collaborate, turning criticism into constructive solutions.
