Recession in Argentina complicates Milei's re-election in 2027

Recession in Argentina complicates Milei's re-election in 2027

Argentina is facing a prolonged economic slowdown, marked by three consecutive months of contraction and a sharp decline in annual growth expectations. This downturn, anticipated by international organizations and the central bank, contrasts with the government’s initial targets and signals a significant erosion in the administration’s capacity to revitalize productive activity ahead of the upcoming elections. The crisis is evident in the collapse of industry, the massive loss of businesses, and the decline in consumption, while inflation remains at high levels despite some improvements. Real wages have fallen notably, negatively affecting both private- and public-sector workers, reflecting an environment of social and commercial deterioration that generates uncertainty in financial markets and increases country risk. This article is relevant to the open data community because it illustrates the critical importance of alternative economic indicators, such as INDEC’s EMAE, for monitoring financial health when traditional official measurements are delayed or indirect. It also highlights how transparency in data on inflation, employment, and industrial production enables citizens and analysts to critically assess government performance and macroeconomic projections in real time.
