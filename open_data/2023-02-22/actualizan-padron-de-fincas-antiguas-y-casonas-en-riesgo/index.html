Actualizan padrón de fincas antiguas y casonas en riesgo

Municipal authorities are continuously updating a registry of historic properties in San Luis Potosí that pose structural or security risks. This initiative prioritizes the city center, involving strict coordination between civil protection, heritage institutes, and public safety agencies to monitor these vulnerable sites. The administration has placed warning signs on deteriorated buildings and urges residents to respect these markers while exercising caution near them. Beyond preventing collapse, this operational focus aims to deter criminal activity in abandoned spaces, thereby enhancing overall public security for citizens. This case illustrates the practical application of open data in urban governance. Transparent, accessible registries of hazardous infrastructure enable efficient inter-agency collaboration and empower community safety. By maintaining accurate, publicly relevant data on property risks, municipalities can better protect citizens and preserve historical integrity through informed, coordinated action.
