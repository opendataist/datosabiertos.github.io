La Junta tiene en marcha el proyecto 'Gobierno del Dato' para sumar los servicios de Datos Abiertos y Transparencia

The Junta de Castilla y León is launching the “Government of Data” project to integrate transparency and open data services, aiming to improve how public information is managed and used. This strategic shift underscores that open data is fundamental to governance, requiring greater efficiency and quality in the management of the information already available through the regional portal. By treating data as a central asset, the administration seeks to clearly demonstrate how resources are used and decisions are made, thereby fostering greater accountability and public trust. This initiative marks a significant evolution in open data policy, moving beyond mere publication to active management and analysis. The approach ensures that data is freely accessible without restrictive copyright, enabling citizens and developers to create valuable applications and insights. This model highlights the shift from passive data availability to active engagement, with open data serving as a tool for social and economic improvement through collaboration between the public sector and civil society. The relevance to open data lies in the systemic integration of transparency principles into administrative operations, setting a precedent for other governments. By investing in this framework, the region demonstrates how open data can drive better decision-making and public service delivery. This holistic approach emphasizes the importance not only of releasing data but also of ensuring that it is usable, of high quality, and central to governmental transparency, thereby maximizing its potential impact on society.
