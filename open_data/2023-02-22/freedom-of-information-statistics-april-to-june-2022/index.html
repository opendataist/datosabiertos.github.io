Freedom of Information statistics: April to June 2022

This bulletin highlights that UK Freedom of Information statistics hold National Statistics status, ensuring they meet rigorous standards for trustworthiness and quality. This designation confirms the data’s reliability for public decision-making and debate, reflecting strict compliance with official codes of practice established by the UK Statistics Authority. The ongoing assessment of these statistics underscores their role in maintaining transparency within central government bodies. By adhering to high production standards, the government provides consistent, high-quality information that supports accountability. This commitment to statistical integrity ensures that FOI data remains a credible resource for monitoring government openness over time. This relevance to open data lies in its demonstration of how standardized, certified statistics facilitate effective transparency. When official data is verified for quality, it becomes a foundational element for public scrutiny and informed civic engagement. Such reliability is essential for fostering trust in open data initiatives and ensuring that information about government operations is accurate and usable for all stakeholders.
