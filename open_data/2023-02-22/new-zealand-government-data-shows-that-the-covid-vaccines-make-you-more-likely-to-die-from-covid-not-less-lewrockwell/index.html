New Zealand Government Data Shows that the Covid Vaccines Make You More Likely To Die from Covid (Not Less) - LewRockwell

The article asserts that government data reveals a counterintuitive correlation where higher vaccination rates coincide with increased mortality risks, contradicting official public health guidance. By leveraging Freedom of Information Act requests and official statistics, the author argues that vaccinated individuals face a greater likelihood of death from COVID-19 compared to their unvaccinated counterparts across all age groups. This narrative frames the current medical consensus as fundamentally flawed, suggesting that reliance on belief rather than transparent data has led to widespread misinformation. A central premise is that the claimed benefits of vaccines are illusory and potentially harmful, as indicated by rising all-cause mortality rates despite high vaccination coverage. The author posits that the vaccines not only fail to protect as promised but may actively contribute to excess deaths, thereby invalidating the primary justification for mass immunization campaigns. This perspective challenges the authority of health agencies, accusing them of hiding records and prioritizing political narratives over scientific reality and patient safety. This content is relevant to open data discussions as it highlights the critical importance of accessible, granular, and properly stratified public health statistics. It underscores how opaque data practices or incomplete disclosures can fuel public distrust and conspiracy theories. The article serves as a case study in the dangers of data opacity, demonstrating that without transparent, age-adjusted, and causally analyzed datasets, public discourse on medical interventions becomes vulnerable to misinterpretation and polarized debate.
