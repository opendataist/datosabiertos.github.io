Galamsey: Sammy Gyamfi submits RTI requests to AG, EPA, and others - Ghanamma.com

Galamsey: Sammy Gyamfi submits RTI requests to AG, EPA, and others - Ghanamma.com

Sammy Gyamfi from the NDC is actively pursuing legal accountability for illegal mining by filing Right to Information requests with key government agencies. This strategic move highlights how transparency tools can empower political actors and citizens to uncover hidden environmental crimes and support thorough investigations. The NDC warns that appointees involved in galamsey will face consequences regardless of political shifts, emphasizing that justice for environmental degradation transcends electoral cycles. This stance underscores the urgency of sustainable governance and the long-term societal impact of unchecked resource exploitation. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessing government records enables civil society to hold power accountable. By leveraging information rights, stakeholders can expose corruption and drive policy reforms, proving that transparent data is essential for effective environmental protection and democratic oversight.
