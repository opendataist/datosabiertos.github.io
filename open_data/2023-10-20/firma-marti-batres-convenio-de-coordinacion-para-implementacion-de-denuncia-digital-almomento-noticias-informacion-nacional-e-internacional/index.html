Firma Martí Batres convenio de coordinación para implementación de "Denuncia Digital" - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

The transfer of the source code and licensing for the “Denuncia Digital” platform to Tamaulipas exemplifies the critical value of open data in public administration. By sharing the source code and technical expertise, Mexico City demonstrates how open governance fosters intergovernmental collaboration, enabling regions to efficiently adapt and implement digital justice solutions without reinventing existing technologies. This initiative highlights the broader implications of open data for accessibility and transparency. Providing free, digital channels for reporting crimes reduces bureaucratic barriers, thereby encouraging citizen participation and streamlining legal processes. The availability of open-source tools ensures that such innovations can be replicated and improved upon, promoting a more inclusive and responsive justice system across different jurisdictions. Ultimately, this agreement underscores the relevance of open data in advancing open government principles. It illustrates a shift toward collaborative policymaking, where technological assets are treated as public goods. Sharing these digital resources not only enhances administrative efficiency but also builds a network of trust and innovation, proving that open data is essential for creating scalable, equitable, and effective public services.
