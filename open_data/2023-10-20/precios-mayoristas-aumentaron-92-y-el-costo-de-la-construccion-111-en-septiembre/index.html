Precios mayoristas aumentaron 9,2% y el costo de la construcción 11,1% en septiembre

Argentina is grappling with severe inflation, as wholesale prices and construction costs surged significantly in September. These rapid increases reflect a deep economic instability that distorts market signals. Such volatility creates critical challenges for open data initiatives. Reliable statistics become difficult to interpret when prices shift so dramatically, complicating efforts to analyze trends or publish transparent, usable datasets for the public. This situation underscores the need for robust data methodologies in high-inflation contexts. Accurate, accessible information is essential to help citizens and researchers navigate economic chaos, making clear open data practices vital for accountability and informed decision-making.
