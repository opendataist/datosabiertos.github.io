CSIF destaca la desigualdad de género y la pérdida de empleo público en el mercado laboral de la región

CSIF destaca la desigualdad de género y la pérdida de empleo público en el mercado laboral de la región

The article highlights severe gender disparities in Castilla-La Mancha’s labor market, where women face significant job losses and are overrepresented in precarious, part-time temporary contracts. This structural imbalance, driven by unequal care responsibilities, directly impacts women’s income and future pensions, perpetuating economic inequality despite the backdrop of rising respiratory illnesses and cuts to health services. The text advocates for a paradigm shift toward genuine workplace-life balance that eliminates labor and salary penalties. This perspective is crucial for open data advocates, as it underscores the necessity of transparent, granular gender-disaggregated datasets. Such data is vital to expose hidden inequalities and design effective policies that protect vulnerable groups rather than masking systemic failures. Furthermore, the report criticizes the substantial public funding allocated to rival unions, arguing that these resources should be redirected toward social protection. This demands greater transparency in public spending to ensure accountability. For open data, this reinforces the imperative to closely monitor fund allocation, ensuring that public money effectively serves the most vulnerable populations instead of perpetuating institutional bias and inequality.
