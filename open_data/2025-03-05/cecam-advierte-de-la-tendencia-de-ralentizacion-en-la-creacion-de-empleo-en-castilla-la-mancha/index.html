Cecam advierte de la tendencia de ralentización en la creación de empleo en Castilla-La Mancha

February’s unemployment figures show a typical seasonal increase, yet they mark a significant rise compared to the previous year. Although the annual trend indicates a reduction in joblessness, the current data points to stagnation in job creation, signaling a broader economic slowdown across the region. The analysis suggests that national policies—particularly uniform minimum wage hikes and rising labor costs—are severely constraining employment growth and business competitiveness. These measures, combined with bureaucratic obstacles and legal uncertainty, impose heavy costs on the productive economy, hindering regional development and overall growth rates. This report is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for granular, localized datasets to validate macroeconomic claims. Without detailed, disaggregated information by sector and territory, policymakers risk implementing one-size-fits-all solutions that overlook regional disparities, highlighting the vital role of accessible, high-quality open data in driving effective economic analysis and decision-making.
