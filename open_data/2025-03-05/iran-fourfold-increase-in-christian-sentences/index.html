Iran: Fourfold increase in Christian sentences

Iran: Fourfold increase in Christian sentences

Iran significantly escalated persecution of Christians in 2024, imposing harsher sentences and increasing prosecutions largely through fabricated financial charges. This surge highlights systemic judicial abuse and the weaponization of legal processes against religious minorities. The report underscores the urgent need for global transparency and advocacy to protect vulnerable faith communities from state-sponsored repression.
