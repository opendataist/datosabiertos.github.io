La Seguridad Social suma 100.340 afiliados y logra su segundo mejor febrero desde 2007

The recent surge in Social Security registrations highlights a robust recovery in the labor market, reaching historic levels in February. This significant growth underscores the resilience of the Spanish economy and suggests a sustained improvement in employment conditions, reflecting strong demand across key sectors such as education, hospitality, and construction. Notably, women’s labor force participation has reached an all-time high, signaling progress toward greater gender balance in the workforce. Additionally, the rise in permanent contracts and the decline in temporary employment indicate a shift toward more stable and secure working arrangements, reducing precariousness for many workers. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because transparent, high-quality statistics enable researchers and policymakers to effectively track socioeconomic trends. Such accessible information fosters evidence-based decision-making, allowing society to monitor the health of the labor market and advocate for policies that support inclusive and sustainable employment growth.
