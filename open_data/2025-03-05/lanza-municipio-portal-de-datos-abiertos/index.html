Lanza Municipio portal de datos abiertos

Lanza Municipio portal de datos abiertos

Chihuahua launched an open data portal to enhance citizens' access to local government information, covering areas such as security, public health, and urban services. This initiative aims to empower residents with clear data for informed decision-making and a better understanding of their city. The commitment to transparency was formalized through adherence to the International Charter on Open Data. This step strengthens accountability and fosters active civic participation by making municipal actions and generated data publicly available. This development is relevant to open data as it demonstrates local government's commitment to transparency. By providing accessible datasets on critical social issues, it enables civil society engagement and promotes evidence-based policy analysis within the community.
