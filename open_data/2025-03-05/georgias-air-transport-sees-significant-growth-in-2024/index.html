Georgia’s air transport sees significant growth in 2024

Georgia’s air transport sees significant growth in 2024

Georgia’s air transport sector saw substantial growth in passenger volume, cargo capacity, and operational flights in 2024, indicating a robust expansion of the aviation industry. This surge reflects increased connectivity and economic activity, with significant rises in both inbound and outbound traffic metrics. The data highlights a marked increase in freight turnover and aircraft fleet size, suggesting enhanced logistical capabilities and infrastructure development across Georgian airports. These trends point to a maturing aviation ecosystem that is effectively scaling to meet rising demand from both passengers and commercial freight operators. This information is highly relevant to open data efforts as it exemplifies the value of transparent, accessible national statistics. By publishing detailed, granular datasets on transportation metrics, Geostat enables researchers, policymakers, and businesses to analyze sector trends, drive evidence-based decision-making, and foster greater accountability and innovation within the public data ecosystem.
