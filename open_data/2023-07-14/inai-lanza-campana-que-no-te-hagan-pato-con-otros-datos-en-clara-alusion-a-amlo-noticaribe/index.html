INAI lanza campaña 'Que no te hagan pato con otros datos' en clara alusión a AMLO - Noticaribe

The INAI campaign encourages citizens to use access-to-information tools to verify government statements, directly countering the President’s frequent dismissal of criticism with the phrase “I have other data.” This initiative is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of transparency mechanisms in ensuring accountability. By empowering citizens to contrast official narratives with factual evidence, it strengthens the demand for accessible and reliable public information.
