Veedores internacionales evaluarán proceso y datos del censo experimental

An experimental census is being carried out in Bolivia to test procedures and systems, ensuring better preparation for the official national count. This rigorous trial aims to validate operational efficiency and data integrity before the final compilation of statistics. This initiative is crucial for open data because it establishes the foundational infrastructure for transparent, reliable, and publicly accessible demographic information. By involving international observers, the process enhances global trust in the resulting datasets. Ultimately, this effort ensures that the upcoming census generates high-quality, open data. This enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to accurately analyze population trends, fostering informed decision-making and accountability through accessible statistical resources.
