Open Data
ES EN

Saudi Arabia is advancing its data-driven economy by training over a hundred government specialists in open data principles. This initiative aims to equip public sector employees with essential skills for managing data quality, classification, and compliance, thereby fostering a culture of innovation and research across diverse agencies. Open data’s significance lies in its universal accessibility and reusability, allowing individuals to modify, verify, and publish findings independently. By removing barriers to information, these standards encourage scientific discovery and ensure that research outcomes can be rigorously validated, enhancing transparency and trust in public sector outputs. This training is crucial for open data because it builds the human infrastructure necessary for effective data governance. By empowering officials to handle data with high quality and proper standards, the program ensures that public information remains accessible, usable, and beneficial for society, supporting the transition toward a robust national economy based on data and artificial intelligence.
