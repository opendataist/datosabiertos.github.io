The notable increase in passenger rail usage highlights a growing public reliance on trains as a viable alternative to congested roads during major infrastructure works. This shift indicates that expanding rail capacity is not merely an option but a critical strategy for maintaining urban mobility and alleviating traffic pressure in metropolitan areas. This trend underscores the value of transparent operational data, as it reveals how infrastructure decisions directly influence user behavior and service demand. Monitoring these figures enables stakeholders to understand the real-time impact of road disruptions and adapt transport strategies accordingly, fostering more responsive urban planning. For open data initiatives, this article demonstrates the importance of accessible, up-to-date transportation metrics. By making such information publicly available, authorities can encourage informed decision-making, validate infrastructure investments, and promote sustainable mobility solutions that respond to actual citizen needs.
