Conade debe transparentar recursos que recibieron atletas: comisionada

The INAI ordered CONADE to publicly disclose lists of athletes and coaches with outstanding debts or unverified expenses. This ruling underscores that transparent financial management fosters a co-responsible relationship between funding authorities and beneficiaries, ensuring accountability in the allocation of resources. Transparency in sports funding is crucial because it validates public trust and legal compliance. By making these records accessible, the government reinforces its commitment to the honest stewardship of funds dedicated to high-performance sports, thereby strengthening the integrity of the entire sporting ecosystem. This decision is vital for open data, as it mandates the release of specific, actionable government information. It exemplifies how data transparency promotes civic oversight and supports the broader open government agenda by ensuring that details of public spending are accessible, searchable, and subject to public scrutiny.
