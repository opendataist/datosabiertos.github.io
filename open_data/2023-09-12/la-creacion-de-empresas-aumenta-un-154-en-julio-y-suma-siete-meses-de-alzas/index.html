La creación de empresas aumenta un 15,4% en julio y suma siete meses de alzas

July saw a significant surge in the creation of new companies, marking the seventh consecutive month of growth and reflecting strong economic confidence among entrepreneurs. This upward trend, combined with a decline in company dissolutions, points to a healthy business environment in which new ventures are actively being established while existing firms remain stable. The quality of this expansion is further underscored by substantial capital injections, particularly through capital increases and higher average subscription amounts in specific sectors such as finance and real estate. This financial vitality demonstrates not only an increase in the number of new businesses but also their economic strength and investment capacity, signaling sustained market momentum. This data is highly relevant to open-data initiatives, as it exemplifies the value of transparent, standardized statistical reporting by national institutions. By making such granular indicators publicly available, governments enable researchers and citizens to monitor economic health, fostering accountability and informed decision-making based on reliable, freely accessible public records.
