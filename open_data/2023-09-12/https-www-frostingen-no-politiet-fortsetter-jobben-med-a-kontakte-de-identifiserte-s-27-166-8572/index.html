Norwegian police have identified and are contacting individuals on video from a disgraced doctor’s office, expanding the investigation into widespread sexual abuse. With nearly a hundred victims already reporting the crimes, this case highlights how digital evidence can uncover systematic harm, demonstrating the critical role of transparent data access in facilitating justice and supporting victims of institutional misconduct. This extensive use of video analysis underscores the importance of open data principles in legal proceedings. By making evidence more accessible and verifiable, societies can ensure accountability for powerful figures. Such transparency not only aids law enforcement but also rebuilds public trust, showing that data-driven approaches are essential for addressing large-scale abuse effectively and fairly.
