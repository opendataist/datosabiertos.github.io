La creación de empresas sube un 9% en julio en Castilla-La Mancha

July witnessed a notable month-on-month decline in new business formations, signaling a significant cooling of entrepreneurial activity despite ongoing annual growth. At the same time, company dissolutions decreased, suggesting a stabilizing environment in which fewer entities are exiting the market. This trend points to a maturation phase in the economic landscape, characterized by lower capital investment per new venture but sustained long-term creation rates. The divergence between monthly declines and annual increases highlights the volatility inherent in current market conditions. For open data, these fluctuations underscore the need for transparent, real-time enterprise registries. Accurate, accessible statistics are essential for researchers and policymakers to monitor economic health, analyze startup ecosystems, and understand the true impact of regulatory or economic shifts on business longevity.
