Datos censales: Oportunidad para el cambio modernizador del país

Paraguay is facing an unexpected population stagnation, marked by a sharp downward revision in population projections due to low fertility rates and negative net migration, particularly among young women. This phenomenon reveals that the progress in women’s education and professional advancement has not been accompanied by adequate support policies, creating structural barriers that drive the outflow of talent and a decline in births, thereby preventing the country from capitalizing on key demographic and economic advantages. New census data are vital for visualizing these urbanization and inequality dynamics, enabling the identification of the needs of vulnerable groups such as early childhood populations and indigenous communities. Detailed data disaggregation facilitates more precise public planning, ensuring that resources are allocated effectively in the face of rapid social and economic changes that current policies fail to address. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the critical need for accessible and disaggregated public statistics. Transparency in these data empowers society and the public sector to engage in informed debates about present and future challenges, ensuring that decision-making is based on real evidence rather than outdated projections, thus fostering more just and effective governance.
