Oaxaca registra la tasa más alta de informalidad laboral: INEGI

Oaxaca registra la tasa más alta de informalidad laboral: INEGI

The article highlights Oaxaca’s record-high labor informality rate, where workers like Elizabeth trade social security protections for perceived immediate income gains. This trend reflects a broader national shift in which millions opt for unregulated contracts within formal companies to bypass bureaucratic hurdles, prioritizing cash flow over long-term stability. As a result, a large portion of the economically active population lacks fundamental labor rights and social safety nets. This situation underscores the severe human cost of regulatory avoidance, revealing how economic necessity drives workers into precarious positions even when they work for registered entities. The data show that informal employment is not confined to unregistered micro-businesses but extensively permeates formal sectors through loopholes that strip workers of their legal protections. For open data, this case is crucial because it exposes the limitations of relying solely on aggregated statistics to capture the true scope of informal labor. It emphasizes the need for transparent, granular data that distinguishes between formal employment and workers with similar vulnerabilities within formal structures, thereby enabling better policy design and advocacy for labor rights.
