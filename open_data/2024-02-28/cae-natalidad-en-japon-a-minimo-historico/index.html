Cae natalidad en Japón a mínimo histórico

Japan’s population is shrinking rapidly due to record-low birth rates and rising death rates, signaling a deepening demographic crisis. This decline, coupled with fewer marriages and more divorces, highlights significant societal shifts toward delayed family formation and increasingly unstable unions. These trends have critical implications for open data, as accurate population metrics are essential for planning sustainable social services and understanding evolving community structures.
