OpenAI busca desestimar por 'jaqueo' partes de una demanda por derechos de autor del NYT - Mundo - ABC Color

OpenAI is fighting back legally against The New York Times, accusing the newspaper of paying a hacker to illegally access its data, while denying the original lawsuits alleging copyright infringement from using its content in AI training. This dispute highlights the tension between the need for large volumes of data to train artificial intelligence models and the protection of copyright for creators of original content. The case is crucial for open data because it sets precedents regarding the legitimacy of data collection methods and the use of copyrighted works. Determining whether AI training constitutes infringement or transformative use will directly affect how data is managed and shared in the future, balancing technological innovation with authors' rights. Ultimately, the resolution of this conflict will influence global information access policies. Although previous lawsuits filed by artists were dismissed, the media's response could force new regulations that define the ethical and legal boundaries in the era of generative artificial intelligence.
