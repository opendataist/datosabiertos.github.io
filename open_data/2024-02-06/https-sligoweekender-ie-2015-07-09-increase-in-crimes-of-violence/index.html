Crime statistics in the Sligo-Leitrim division reveal a complex landscape where violent offenses like assault and robbery increased, while non-violent crimes such as theft and fraud decreased. This divergence highlights shifting patterns in local criminal activity over the past year. However, these figures must be interpreted with caution due to historical data quality issues. The Central Statistics Office had paused publication after identifying significant irregularities in how police recorded crimes, including misclassified incidents and unrecorded offenses. Their decision to resume reporting follows a comprehensive review aimed at restoring trust in the administrative data. This update is critical for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of data transparency and integrity in public sector reporting. It demonstrates that accurate, accessible information is not just about volume, but about ensuring the underlying systems capturing that data are reliable and free from systematic errors.
