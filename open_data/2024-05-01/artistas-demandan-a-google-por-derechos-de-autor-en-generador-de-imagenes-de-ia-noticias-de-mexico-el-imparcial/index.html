Artistas demandan a Google por derechos de autor en generador de imágenes de IA | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

A group of visual artists has filed a lawsuit against Google, alleging that the company used copyrighted images without permission to train its AI image generation system, Imagen. This lawsuit underscores the critical tension between proprietary AI development and the use of publicly available digital content. The artists are seeking damages and the destruction of unauthorized copies, arguing that tech giants profit from creative work without consent or compensation. This case reflects a broader industry struggle concerning the ethical and legal boundaries of data utilization in machine learning models. This dispute is significant for open data discussions, as it questions the transparency of training datasets. It challenges the assumption that publicly accessible data can be freely mined, emphasizing the need for clear standards on attribution and rights management in the era of generative AI.
