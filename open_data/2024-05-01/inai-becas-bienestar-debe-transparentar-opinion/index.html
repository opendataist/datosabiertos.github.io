INAI: Becas Bienestar debe transparentar opinión

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) has ordered the publication of tax compliance opinions for social security from providers of the Bienestar program, filtering out only strictly confidential data. This measure enables citizens to verify the tax solvency of companies participating in public bidding processes, ensuring that bidders meet their obligations before being awarded contracts. This transparency is crucial for the open data sector, as it democratizes access to information regarding the integrity of government contracts. By making this data visible, it facilitates citizen oversight and promotes accountability, ensuring that public resources are allocated to entities that comply with the law and have no outstanding tax impediments.
