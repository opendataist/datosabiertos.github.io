Labor union says it’s filing FOIA suit over secretive Petersburg casino process

The Petersburg City Council faces a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit from Unite Here after abruptly canceling a competitive bidding process for a casino project and selecting The Cordish Companies in a closed session. The union alleges the council violated open government laws by failing to provide adequate public notice for such a significant decision, effectively excluding citizens from a transparent democratic process regarding a billion-dollar development. This case highlights the critical importance of open data and public records in holding local governments accountable. When decisions about major economic initiatives are made behind closed doors without accessible documentation, it undermines public trust and obscures potential conflicts of interest. The suit demands the preservation of all communications, emphasizing that citizen access to government information is essential for verifying that administrative actions align with legal requirements and democratic norms rather than secret negotiations. The outcome carries significant implications for labor relations, as the union supports a rival developer due to existing labor agreements, contrasting with Cordish’s ties to a competing union. Beyond the immediate legal battle, the situation underscores the necessity of maintaining open, auditable trails in municipal governance. Ensuring that public processes are visible and data is accessible prevents the manipulation of outcomes and ensures that large-scale projects serve the public interest rather than undisclosed private interests.
