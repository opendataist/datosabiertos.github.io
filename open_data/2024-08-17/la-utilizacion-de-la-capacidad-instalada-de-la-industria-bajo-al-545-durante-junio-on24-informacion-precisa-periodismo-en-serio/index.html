La utilización de la capacidad instalada de la industria bajó al 54,5% durante junio | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

Industrial capacity utilization fell sharply, reflecting a broader contraction in manufacturing output. This decline underscores a stark divergence: essential sectors such as food and chemicals remain robust, while the construction and automotive industries are struggling severely. This data is vital for open data initiatives because it provides transparent, granular insights into the economic health of specific industries. By making these detailed statistics publicly available, citizens and analysts can independently verify government claims and identify which sectors require support. The trend highlights the necessity of empirical evidence in policy debates. It shifts discussions away from political rhetoric, enabling stakeholders to base decisions on actual industrial performance rather than abstract promises, thereby fostering accountability in economic management.
