Diario HOY | Casos denunciados: casi 30 mil niños fueron abusados

The data reveal a persistent increase in reported cases of violence against children, highlighting a critical failure in protection systems rather than a decline in actual incidents. During the pandemic, reported figures dropped not because children were safer, but because isolated victims lost essential support networks such as teachers and peers, demonstrating how gaps in infrastructure can obscure the true scale of abuse. Current statistics show a significant daily volume of crimes, with sexual abuse accounting for a major share of these offenses. This trend persists across several key regions, indicating systemic issues that require immediate, widespread intervention to effectively safeguard minors. This information is crucial for open_data initiatives, as it underscores the need for accessible, granular datasets to expose hidden vulnerabilities. Transparent data enables researchers and policymakers to identify where support networks are failing, allowing for targeted interventions that address the root causes of underreporting and improve outcomes for child safety.
