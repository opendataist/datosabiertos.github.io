Las exportaciones de China a Estados Unidos caen casi un 19% desde enero por las tensiones comerciales

The article reveals that sustained US-China trade tensions have significantly reduced bilateral export and import flows, despite a recent commercial truce and modest global growth for Chinese exports. This decline highlights the fragility of economic ties between the two powers and the tangible impact of geopolitical friction on global supply chains. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, high-quality, and accessible trade statistics. Without such visibility, analysts struggle to accurately model economic risks, understand the real-time effects of policy shifts, or assess the broader implications of decoupling trends on international markets. Ultimately, the case illustrates how open data empowers stakeholders to track complex macroeconomic indicators. By making customs and trade figures readily available, societies can better evaluate the consequences of commercial disputes and make informed decisions regarding economic stability and future trade strategies.
