Compendium of Statistics 2024 released

Compendium of Statistics 2024 released

The Cayman Islands’ 2024 statistical compendium reveals a growing population and increasing reliance on social welfare services. This shift highlights evolving living conditions and economic pressures, offering valuable insights into the community's social fabric. Waste management data further illustrates environmental trends, showing a decrease in incineration despite significant landfill usage. These metrics are essential for tracking sustainability efforts and environmental health within the territory. This release is vital for open data initiatives as it centralizes diverse public and private sector statistics. By making this comprehensive dataset accessible, stakeholders can better analyze demographic shifts, economic indicators, and social needs to support informed decision-making and transparency.

Source: radiocayman.gov.ky
