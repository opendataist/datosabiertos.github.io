Musk pide abolir la Unión Europea tras la multa de 120 millones de euros impuesta a X

Elon Musk has responded to a significant fine imposed by the European Union for violations of the Digital Services Act with a radical call to abolish the bloc, accusing it of interfering with national sovereignty. This stance reflects a growing tension between major U.S. technology platforms and European regulation, which seeks to enforce stricter standards of transparency and accountability. The sanction is primarily based on opaque practices, such as misleading verification designs and the refusal to provide public data to researchers. These requirements are fundamental to open science and algorithmic transparency—core principles of the open data movement that demand genuine access to information for external auditors and citizens. This conflict is crucial for the open data community, as it illustrates corporations’ resistance to relinquishing control over their informational assets. X’s refusal to facilitate data access for research represents a direct obstacle to independent verification and democratic auditing, underscoring the urgency of legal frameworks that prioritize transparency over exclusive commercial interests.
