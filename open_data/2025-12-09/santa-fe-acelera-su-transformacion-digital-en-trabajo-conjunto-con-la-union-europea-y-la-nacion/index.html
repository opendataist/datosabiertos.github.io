Santa Fe acelera su transformación digital en trabajo conjunto con la Unión Europea y la Nación

Santa Fe acelera su transformación digital en trabajo conjunto con la Unión Europea y la Nación

Santa Fe has advanced the modernization of its government through an Open Government workshop, developed in collaboration with the European Union and the national government. This initiative aims to consolidate a more efficient, transparent, and citizen-oriented public management, integrating science, technology, and innovation into public policies. The main objective is to strengthen public trust and boost institutional innovation through the strategic use of digital tools. The activity reflects a commitment to transparency and participation, opening decision-making processes to the community. It promotes collaboration between the public and private sectors and civil society to ensure that locally developed technology serves the common good. This alliance not only improves administrative management but also establishes an essential public-private dialogue for the province’s digital transformation and economic development. This process is highly relevant for open data because it institutionalizes transparency as a fundamental mandate, enabling the community to access information about government decisions. By facilitating the exchange of experiences and global best practices, Santa Fe contributes to a federal ecosystem where data and information are used to improve governance. This integration between informational openness and digital transformation lays the foundation for a more responsible administration oriented toward the public interest.
