Producción de empresas constructoras cayó 1% en 2024: Inegi | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Producción de empresas constructoras cayó 1% en 2024: Inegi | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The construction sector experienced a slowdown in 2024, marked by a slight decline in production value. This contrasted sharply with the previous year’s significant growth, primarily driven by the conclusion of major state infrastructure projects. Despite lower production, employment and compensation metrics improved, with more people working and higher total wages. Gender participation remained stable, while nearly half of the companies provided training, highlighting ongoing development in workforce skills across engineering and building subsectors. This article is highly relevant to open data because the statistical bureau uses this annual survey as a fundamental input for macroeconomic calculations. The continuous publication of these indicators allows researchers and policymakers to track employment, production, and investment trends, ensuring transparent and accessible data for informed economic decision-making.
