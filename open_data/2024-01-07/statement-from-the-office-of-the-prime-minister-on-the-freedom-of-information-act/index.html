Statement from The Office of the Prime Minister on the Freedom of Information Act

The Bahamas government reaffirms its commitment to implementing the Freedom of Information Act, prioritizing transparency and accountability. This initiative strengthens democratic principles by guaranteeing public access to government information, directly supporting open data standards. Such efforts ensure that citizens can effectively hold leaders accountable through increased institutional openness.
