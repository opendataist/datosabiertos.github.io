Cómo la Generación Z está redefiniendo el "sueño chino" ante la falta de oportunidades | Puranoticia.cl

Cómo la Generación Z está redefiniendo el "sueño chino" ante la falta de oportunidades | Puranoticia.cl

The article highlights that China’s youth unemployment crisis stems not only from economic factors but also from a profound psychological collapse of the “Chinese Dream,” in which rigid state narratives no longer align with the aspirations of a disillusioned generation. This disconnect poses a potential threat to social stability and political control, as the state’s emphasis on endurance and manual labor contrasts sharply with young people’s desires for autonomy and global exposure. Interestingly, immediate social unrest remains absent, largely due to intergenerational wealth transfers that allow families to buffer against economic shocks, despite declining asset values. However, this safety net is temporary and shrinking, leaving many graduates feeling failed and forced to accept roles below their qualifications or create their own micro-enterprises to survive. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, granular labor market data to monitor hidden underemployment and the rise of the gig economy. Open data can help policymakers and researchers understand these shifting narratives and informal economic adaptations, moving beyond aggregate statistics to capture the human impact of policy decisions and support more effective social solutions.
