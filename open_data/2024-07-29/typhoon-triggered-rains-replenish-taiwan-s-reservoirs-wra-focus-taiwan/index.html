Typhoon-triggered rains replenish Taiwan's reservoirs: WRA - Focus Taiwan

Typhoon Gaemi’s heavy rains replenish Taiwan’s southern reservoirs with over two billion cubic meters of water. This surge significantly reduces regional water scarcity and improves long-term water security for affected areas. The event underscores the critical role of monitoring extreme precipitation data in open data initiatives for effective resource management.
