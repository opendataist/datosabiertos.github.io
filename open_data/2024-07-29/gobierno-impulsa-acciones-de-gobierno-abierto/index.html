Gobierno impulsa acciones de gobierno abierto

The government of San Luis Potosí state is enhancing transparency through quarterly reviews of open data initiatives across numerous agencies. These meetings aim to identify areas for improvement in governance while fostering creative projects that enhance public participation and accountability. This effort ensures that citizens receive comprehensive datasets detailing administrative activities. By tracking progress, the administration seeks to provide better services and support for residents through more informed decision-making processes. This article highlights the practical implementation of open data policies. It demonstrates how systematic monitoring and collaborative workshops drive transparency, serving as a key example of how governments can use open information to rebuild trust and improve public service delivery effectively.
