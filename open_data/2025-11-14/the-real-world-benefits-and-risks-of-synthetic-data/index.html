The real-world benefits and risks of synthetic data

The real-world benefits and risks of synthetic data

Synthetic data serves as a critical enabler for innovation in regulated industries, allowing organizations to bypass privacy and security barriers. It facilitates early-stage development and machine learning training without exposing sensitive information, thereby accelerating project timelines and fostering collaboration across internal teams and external partners. However, reliance on synthetic data introduces significant risks, including the loss of real-world complexity and potential privacy leakage. If not rigorously validated, these datasets may contain residual traces of original sources, undermining anonymity, or cause AI models to degrade by reinforcing artificial patterns rather than reflecting genuine operational realities. This article is vital for the open data community because it defines the ethical boundaries and technical limitations of data generation. It argues that synthetic data must complement, not replace, real-world validation, emphasizing that sustainable data ecosystems require rigorous privacy practices and continuous quality monitoring to prevent hidden biases and ensure trustworthy information sharing.
