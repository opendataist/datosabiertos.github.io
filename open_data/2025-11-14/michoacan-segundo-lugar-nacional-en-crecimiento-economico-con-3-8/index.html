Michoacán, segundo lugar nacional en crecimiento económico con 3.8%

Michoacán demonstrates robust economic expansion, driven primarily by significant growth in agriculture and industry. This performance highlights the vital role of productive sectors in regional development and overall national contribution. The state also achieves notable improvements in formal employment, breaking previous registration records. These results indicate a successful recovery strategy under the current administration, fostering greater labor market stability. This data is relevant to open data because it relies on transparent, high-quality public statistics from national institutes. Such accessible, verifiable information enables citizens and researchers to analyze regional economic health and hold governments accountable for their development policies.
