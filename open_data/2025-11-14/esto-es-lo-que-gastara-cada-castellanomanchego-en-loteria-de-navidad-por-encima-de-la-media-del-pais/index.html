Esto es lo que gastará cada castellanomanchego en Lotería de Navidad: por encima de la media del país

Castilla-La Mancha anticipates increasing its average spending on the Christmas Lottery, surpassing the national average and consolidating itself as a region with high participation in this cultural tradition. This trend reflects a strong social commitment to the draw, where per capita consumption indicates that residents prioritize this festive experience compared to other regions of the country. At the macro level, the geographic distribution reveals significant disparities in total sales volume. While densely populated communities lead in absolute figures, others stand out for their intensity of individual spending. These official estimates provide crucial data for analyzing consumer behavior and the local economy, serving as indicators of commercial vitality and the cultural roots of gambling in each territory. This analysis is relevant for open data because it demonstrates how public data from the State Lotteries and Bets agency enables comparative insights among autonomous communities. By structuring and releasing this information, transparency is promoted, allowing researchers, journalists, and citizens to visualize social trends, thereby encouraging the use of open government data to better understand Spain's regional and economic dynamics.
