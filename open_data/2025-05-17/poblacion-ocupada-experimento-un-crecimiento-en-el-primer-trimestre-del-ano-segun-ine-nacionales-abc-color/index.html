Población ocupada experimentó un crecimiento en el primer trimestre del año, según INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

Población ocupada experimentó un crecimiento en el primer trimestre del año, según INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

The National Statistics Institute’s latest report reveals that Paraguay’s labor market stabilized in early 2025, with employment rates showing slight growth despite a minor dip in overall workforce participation. This data highlights a structural shift toward salaried private sector jobs and formal non-agricultural roles, indicating improved job quality even as independent work declined slightly. The narrative underscores a resilience in the formal economy, driven primarily by services and primary sectors, which absorbed significant labor gains. Crucially, the combined rate of unemployment and time-related underemployment decreased meaningfully compared to the previous year. This reduction demonstrates that the market is effectively integrating more people into stable income sources, alleviating pressure on vulnerable households. The focus remains on the qualitative improvement of employment conditions rather than mere headcount, signaling a positive trend in social welfare and economic inclusion. This publication is vital for open data advocates as it exemplifies the necessity of transparent, granular labor statistics. By making these metrics accessible, policymakers and researchers can track socio-economic disparities and evaluate the real-world impact of public policies. Open access to such detailed breakdowns by sector, gender, and employment type fosters accountability and enables evidence-based decisions that directly address inequality and labor market inefficiencies.
