La provincia de Santa Fe mantuvo estables los niveles de empleo privado registrado

Private employment in Santa Fe has remained stagnant since mid-last year, failing to recover from previous losses and showing a net decline compared to the same period last year and since the current administration took office. This stagnation underscores a critical challenge in labor market stability, suggesting that recent economic policies have not yet translated into tangible job recovery for the province, despite minor sectoral fluctuations. At the national level, the data reveal a slow and uneven recovery characterized by significant job losses in construction and manufacturing, contrasted with growth in commerce and agriculture. The limited creation of salaried positions indicates structural weaknesses in the economy, where specific sectors generate minimal gains while others continue to shed workers, reflecting broader systemic issues in employment generation under the current economic model. This analysis is vital for open-data advocates, as it demonstrates the necessity of accessible, granular administrative records for the independent verification of economic claims. By leveraging official SIPA data, researchers can deconstruct government narratives, identify sectoral disparities, and provide transparent evidence of labor trends, ensuring that public policy decisions are grounded in verifiable reality rather than political rhetoric.
