Fuerte golpe a la maquila en marzo, la peor caída en cinco meses

The sharp contraction in the manufacturing sector signals a severe downturn, impacting not only output but also employment and wages. This widespread decline underscores a critical need for transparent, real-time industrial metrics to accurately assess economic health. Reliable open data is essential for monitoring these trends, enabling policymakers and stakeholders to respond effectively to shifts in the labor market. Enhanced accessibility fosters accountability and supports informed decision-making during periods of economic instability. Ultimately, democratizing access to such vital statistics ensures that the consequences of industrial downturns are visible. This transparency empowers society to understand and address the broader implications of shrinking manufacturing activities and job losses.
