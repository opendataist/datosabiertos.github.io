En octubre, la inflación fue del 2,3% en la Argentina y acumula 24,8% en lo que va del 2025

En octubre, la inflación fue del 2,3% en la Argentina y acumula 24,8% en lo que va del 2025

The report reveals a significant deceleration in Argentina’s year-on-year inflation, dropping from over eighty percent in early 2025 to thirty-one percent by autumn. Despite this monthly cooling, the cumulative ten-month increase remains substantial, indicating persistent economic pressure and reduced purchasing power for citizens across all regions. Transport and housing costs drove the latest monthly rise, while food prices aligned with the national average. Regional analysis shows minor variations, with Patagonia and Greater Buenos Aires slightly above the national trend. This granular breakdown highlights how specific sectors and locations disproportionately impact household budgets, even as the broader macroeconomic indicator improves. This data is crucial for open data advocates because it demonstrates the necessity of accessible, timely statistical information. Transparent metrics allow researchers and policymakers to track economic health accurately. By publishing detailed indices, institutions empower society to analyze trends and hold authorities accountable for inflation control strategies.
