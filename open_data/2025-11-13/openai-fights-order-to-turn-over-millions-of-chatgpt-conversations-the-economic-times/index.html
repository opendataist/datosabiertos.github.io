OpenAI fights order to turn over millions of ChatGPT conversations - The Economic Times

OpenAI fights order to turn over millions of ChatGPT conversations - The Economic Times

OpenAI seeks to block a court order demanding the handover of millions of anonymized ChatGPT logs, arguing that compliance violates user privacy. The company contends that the vast majority of these transcripts are irrelevant to the copyright lawsuit, framing the request as an invasive fishing expedition into personal conversations. Conversely, plaintiffs argue the data is essential to prove whether copyrighted material was misused for training AI models. They refute OpenAI’s claims of evidence tampering and insist that de-identification safeguards adequately protect user confidentiality while allowing necessary judicial scrutiny. This conflict highlights a critical tension in open data governance between transparency for algorithmic accountability and the protection of user privacy. As AI training practices face increased legal scrutiny, resolving this balance is vital for establishing clear precedents on data access in intellectual property disputes involving machine learning systems.
