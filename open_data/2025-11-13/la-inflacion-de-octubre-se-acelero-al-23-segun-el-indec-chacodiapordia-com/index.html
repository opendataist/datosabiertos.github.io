La inflación de octubre se aceleró al 2,3%, según el INDEC - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

The article reports a slight acceleration in Argentine inflation to 2.3% in October, driven by transport and housing costs. This increase contrasts with market expectations and reflects lingering exchange rate volatility, while annual inflation shows a marginal decrease. Analysts project further moderation in the coming months. This report is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the critical role of public statistical institutions, such as INDEC, in providing transparent, high-frequency economic indicators. Such data enables the public and private sectors to monitor price trends, assess policy impacts, and make informed decisions based on accessible, verified information rather than speculation. Understanding these mechanisms promotes data literacy and accountability in public administration. When official statistics are readily available and clear, they reduce information asymmetry. This openness fosters trust in institutional processes and allows society to better evaluate the effectiveness of economic strategies aimed at controlling inflation.
