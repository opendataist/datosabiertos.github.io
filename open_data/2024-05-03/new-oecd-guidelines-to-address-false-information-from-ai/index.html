New OECD guidelines to address false information from AI

The OECD is updating its AI Principles to mandate that developers combat misinformation generated by artificial intelligence. This move responds to the rapid spread of sophisticated fake content, particularly amid global elections, aiming to mitigate societal threats posed by generative tools. Additionally, the revisions enhance transparency and accountability requirements. Developers must now disclose training data and system limitations, ensuring users understand how AI systems operate and where their capabilities end. This update is relevant to open data as it establishes international standards for data provenance and model transparency. By influencing domestic policies in 46 nations, these principles shape how AI data is managed, shared, and verified, promoting responsible innovation in the open ecosystem.
