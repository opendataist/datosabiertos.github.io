La comunidad GLBT de Ecuador es joven, según el INEC

The census reveals a significant LGBTI+ demographic in Ecuador, highlighting their geographic distribution and educational attainment. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides the first comprehensive statistical basis for designing inclusive public policies.
