¿Cómo van los embalses en Colombia? Este es su estado

Colombia has successfully reversed the worrying drought trend that threatened the electricity supply, allowing the Energy and Gas Regulatory Commission (CREG) to cancel the energy-saving program ahead of schedule. This decision reflects a significant recovery in reservoir levels following weeks of rainfall, dispelling the immediate risk of power rationing and providing reassurance to the market amid rising demand. The normalization of the system not only ensures internal security but also facilitates the resumption of energy exports to Ecuador, which had been suspended due to water scarcity. This positive turn demonstrates the capacity of regulatory mechanisms to respond dynamically to climatic conditions, restoring operational stability without the need for prolonged restrictive measures for consumers. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparency in monitoring environmental and energy variables enables rapid, well-informed decision-making. Public access to these reservoir and demand indicators is crucial for validating regulatory actions, fostering citizen trust and accountability in the management of natural resources critical to sustainable development.
