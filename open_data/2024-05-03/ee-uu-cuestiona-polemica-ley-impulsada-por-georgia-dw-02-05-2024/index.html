EE.UU. cuestiona polémica ley impulsada por Georgia – DW – 02/05/2024

The U.S. Ambassador to Georgia has expressed grave concern over the passage of the foreign agents law, describing it as incompatible with the nation’s aspirations to integrate into the European Union. This legislation, criticized for emulating mechanisms of dissent control, has sparked massive protests and strained diplomatic relations. Washington has urged the Georgian government to reaffirm its Euro-Atlantic commitment, emphasizing that the refusal to debate the strategic partnership demonstrates a contradiction with its own Constitution. The tension reflects a fundamental challenge to democratic governance and institutional transparency in the Caucasian country. This conflict is relevant to open data because it underscores the urgency of enacting legislation that protects, rather than restricts, the flow of information and civic participation. Ensuring clarity regarding funding and external influences requires open and accessible standards that foster public trust and sustainable development—essential pillars for a vibrant civil society and successful European integration.
