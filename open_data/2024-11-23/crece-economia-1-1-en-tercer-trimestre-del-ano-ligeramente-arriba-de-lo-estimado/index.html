Crece economía 1.1% en tercer trimestre del año, ligeramente arriba de lo estimado

Mexico’s economy in the third quarter of 2024 surpassed initial forecasts, demonstrating resilience driven by strong performance in the services, manufacturing, and agriculture sectors. This outcome underscores the effectiveness of diverse economic engines in sustaining growth, contrasting with stagnation in mining and highlighting the pivotal role of consumer-facing sectors such as retail and education in driving up the national gross domestic product (GDP). The annual expansion further confirmed positive momentum, particularly within the agricultural and service industries. These sectors outperformed expectations, suggesting robust domestic demand and productive capacity that support long-term stability. The divergence from initial conservative estimates indicates that underlying economic fundamentals are stronger than widely predicted, offering a more optimistic outlook for the near future. This report is relevant to open data because it relies on publicly released statistical indicators from national institutes, illustrating how transparent, granular data enables accurate economic monitoring. By making detailed sectoral breakdowns available, authorities and analysts can track real-time performance, validate forecasts, and make informed decisions. Such accessibility fosters accountability and allows stakeholders to understand the specific drivers of economic health, reinforcing the value of open statistical ecosystems in public policy and business strategy.
