Adrián Alcalá reacciona a extinción del instituto

Adrián Alcalá reacciona a extinción del instituto

The dissolution of the INAI represents a critical threat to democratic accountability and transparency. By eliminating an independent body that empowered citizens to demand answers from public officials, this structural change undermines the essential mechanisms of open data governance and civic oversight. This shift prioritizes centralized executive control over institutional autonomy, potentially obscuring corruption and limiting access to public information. As a result, the workforce faces uncertainty while the state loses a vital safeguard for enforcing integrity and ensuring that government operations remain visible and subject to public scrutiny. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how political decisions can erode the institutional frameworks necessary for free access to information. Without independent oversight, the reliability and availability of public data are jeopardized, weakening the foundational pillars required for a transparent and accountable society.
