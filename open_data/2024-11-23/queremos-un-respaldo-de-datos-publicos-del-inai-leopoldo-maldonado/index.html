Queremos un respaldo de datos públicos del INAI : Leopoldo Maldonado

Queremos un respaldo de datos públicos del INAI : Leopoldo Maldonado

Article 19 demands that historical procurement and beneficiary records, currently classified as public information by law, have an accessible and transparent backup. The organization insists that personal data protection should not hinder access to already published information, reaffirming that public assets are not the property of specific officials or institutions. This stance seeks to ensure continued democratic access to these records, which are vital for accountability. By citing past experience in preserving sensitive documents, the NGO advocates for a system that prioritizes transparency and public availability over administrative discretion. The article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the practical challenges of managing large volumes of public data during institutional transitions. It highlights the critical need to safeguard and maintain open data sets accessible, ensuring that data infrastructure is not lost or rendered inaccessible due to political changes or inadequate transition plans.
