Infoem a la espera de su futuro

The disappearance of the INAI creates uncertainty for Infoem, whose future structure depends on federal legislators. Its potential transformation into a decentralized body highlights the fragility of transparency institutions without robust constitutional safeguards. This situation underscores the critical need for stable, autonomous frameworks to protect open data governance and citizen access to information effectively.
