Two-third of Nigerian families lack money to eat healthy food — Survey |

Recent Nigerian household surveys reveal a severe decline in economic stability, with a majority of families unable to afford nutritious food. This sharp increase in food insecurity highlights the urgent need for improved social safety nets and economic policies to support vulnerable populations. The data underscores a critical gap in living standards, emphasizing that financial constraints are forcing households to compromise on dietary quality and quantity. These findings serve as a vital indicator of broader societal welfare, demonstrating how economic shifts directly impact daily survival and health outcomes for citizens. This information is essential for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, government-verified evidence of social challenges. Such accessible data enables researchers, policymakers, and NGOs to monitor poverty trends accurately and advocate for targeted interventions to address food insecurity and improve national well-being.
