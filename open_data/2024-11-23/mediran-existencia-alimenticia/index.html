Medirán existencia alimenticia

Medirán existencia alimenticia

The INE and FAO are refining food balance sheets to better assess national food availability. This collaboration aims to improve methodological accuracy in tracking supply chains and consumption patterns. The updated indicator provides deeper insights into food security and dependency trends. This progress is crucial for open data, as it ensures that more transparent, precise, and comparable statistical information is available for public scrutiny and policy analysis.
