Disminuye porcentaje de pobreza en niños: Inegi

Childhood poverty in Mexico remains stubbornly high, widening the gap between young children and the general population despite overall reductions. This persistent disparity highlights a critical failure in intergenerational mobility, where most children born into poverty do not escape it as adults. The article underscores the urgent need for comprehensive public policies that guarantee sufficient household income alongside quality education and healthcare access. These measures are essential to break the cycle of poverty and provide children with the tools necessary for a future defined by opportunity rather than their birth circumstances. This discussion is vital for open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistics empower researchers to identify systemic inequalities. By making such data widely available, stakeholders can advocate for evidence-based interventions, ensuring that vulnerable populations receive the targeted support required to achieve equitable social progress and better life outcomes.
