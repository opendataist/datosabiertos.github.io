INFO-CDMX celebra su 19 aniversario con más de 2 millones de solicitudes

INFO-CDMX celebra su 19 aniversario con más de 2 millones de solicitudes

The INFO-CDMX, Mexico City’s transparency institute, is celebrating its 19th anniversary while facing imminent dissolution due to a constitutional reform. Despite handling millions of requests and establishing robust criteria for public access and data protection, the institution’s leadership warns that its valuable institutional memory and established legal precedents risk being lost. The commissioners emphasize that preserving these achievements is critical, as the city holds a significant portion of the country’s public information, making the continuity of its standards essential for maintaining democratic accountability. The institute’s legacy is defined by its extensive technological innovation and citizen engagement, which have significantly advanced open data practices. By developing specialized platforms for learning, automated queries, and secure virtual hearings, INFO-CDMX has democratized access to government information and empowered both public officials and citizens. These tools have not only streamlined administrative processes but also fostered a culture of transparency that extends beyond traditional bureaucratic channels, setting a high bar for digital governance and public service delivery. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the fragility of institutional frameworks that protect information rights against political changes. The potential disappearance of such a specialized body underscores the urgent need to embed transparency and data protection into permanent, robust structures rather than relying on specific entities. Ensuring that the expertise and technological infrastructure developed over nearly two decades are integrated into the new governance model is vital to prevent backsliding and guarantee that open data remains a fundamental right rather than a discretionary privilege.
