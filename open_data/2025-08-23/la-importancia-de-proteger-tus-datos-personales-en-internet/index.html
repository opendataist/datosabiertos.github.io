La importancia de proteger tus datos personales en Internet

La importancia de proteger tus datos personales en Internet

Leaked corporate data pose a critical threat to individual privacy, directly facilitating identity fraud through the sale of sensitive information on clandestine markets. When these records circulate, cybercriminals combine them with synthetic data to bypass security controls, demonstrating that information exposure is not merely a technical failure but the primary fuel for complex scams such as account takeover and the creation of fake identities. Personal information is collected through multiple vectors that extend beyond large-scale database breaches. Social engineering techniques, such as phishing and smishing, deceive users into revealing credentials, while technical threats like infostealer malware, insecure Wi-Fi networks, and malicious advertising silently capture data. This diversity of attacks underscores the need for comprehensive protection, encompassing both user awareness and the technical security of devices and connections. This article is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the inherent risk of publicly exposing personal information without adequate safeguards. It promotes the idea that, although data is an asset, its management requires ethics and rigorous security to prevent social harm. The conclusion emphasizes that protecting digital identity is a shared responsibility, involving the reduction of illegal extraction channels and ensuring that the availability of information does not compromise individual security or trust in digital ecosystems.
