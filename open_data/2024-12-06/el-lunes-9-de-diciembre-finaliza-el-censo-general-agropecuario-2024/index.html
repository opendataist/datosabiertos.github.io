El lunes 9 de diciembre finaliza el Censo General Agropecuario 2024

The mandatory completion of the 2024 Agricultural Census underscores how comprehensive, legally mandated data collection is essential for shaping effective rural public policies. This requirement ensures that statistical institutions have access to accurate, detailed information to understand and support the needs of the agricultural sector. Accurate datasets enable evidence-based decision-making, allowing governments to design targeted interventions that improve societal well-being and economic planning. Compliance guarantees that resources are allocated efficiently based on real-world conditions rather than estimates, fostering transparency and accountability in governance. This initiative is relevant to open data because it establishes a robust foundation for future information sharing. By prioritizing complete and reliable statistical inputs, authorities can eventually publish high-quality, usable data that empowers researchers, businesses, and citizens to analyze trends and drive innovation in the agricultural field.
