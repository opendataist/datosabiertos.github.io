The MLC’s Public Data Portal for Indie Music Distributors

The Mechanical Licensing Collective has successfully partnered with over 100 major music distributors globally through its Distributor Unmatched Recordings Portal. This collaboration creates a streamlined ecosystem where distributors can access specific public data to identify independent creators who have not yet claimed their digital audio mechanical royalties. By notifying these artists and encouraging them to register, the initiative ensures that millions of dollars in unclaimed funds are accurately distributed to their rightful owners, significantly enhancing revenue recovery for self-administered songwriters. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates a practical application of transparent, publicly available datasets to solve industry inefficiencies. By allowing distributors to view only the data pertinent to their specific catalog, the MLC leverages open information principles to empower third parties to act as intermediaries. This approach proves that when rights holder data is accessible and structured correctly, it enables innovative partnerships that reduce barriers to entry for independent artists, fostering a more equitable and efficient music economy. The initiative highlights a broader implication for the creative sector: the importance of bridging the gap between administrative complexity and artistic creation. Testimonials from artists reveal that previously opaque royalty structures have become navigable through this collaborative model. Ultimately, this case study illustrates how open data infrastructure, when integrated with industry distribution networks, can effectively monetize long-tail creative works and ensure that digital rights are respected and compensated without imposing excessive administrative burdens on individual creators.
