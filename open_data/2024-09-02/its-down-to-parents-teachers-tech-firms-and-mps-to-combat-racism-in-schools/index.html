'Its down to parents, teachers, tech firms and MPs to combat racism in schools'

The article highlights that schools are failing to protect children from racist behavior, emphasizing that education for tolerance must extend beyond the home. It stresses the shared responsibility of teachers, tech firms, and politicians to counteract social media hatred and promote understanding, directly impacting open data by showing how transparency in institutional responses to discrimination can drive societal accountability. Regarding the Grenfell Tower tragedy, the text underscores the urgent need for full transparency and justice, as no prosecutions have occurred despite the loss of life. This relevance to open data lies in the demand for public, accessible reports to hold organizations accountable for failures in safety regulations and austerity measures, ensuring that truth and implementation of recommendations are not obscured by political or corporate interests. Finally, the piece criticizes dynamic ticket pricing, arguing that consumers deserve clarity and fairness in market practices. This connects to open data by advocating for transparent pricing mechanisms that allow users to understand costs, fostering trust and preventing exploitation in commercial transactions, thereby supporting a more equitable digital economy where information asymmetry does not disadvantage ordinary consumers.
