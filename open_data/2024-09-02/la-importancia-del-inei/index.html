La importancia del INEI

La importancia del INEI

The INEI serves as a critical infrastructure for generating reliable, official statistics, ensuring that the data produced is standardized and trustworthy. Its relevance to open data lies in its role as a primary authoritative source, facilitating transparency and enabling accurate decision-making through accessible public information.
