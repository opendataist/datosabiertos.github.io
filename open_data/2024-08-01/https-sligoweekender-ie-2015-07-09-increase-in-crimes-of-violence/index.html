Recent crime statistics indicate a significant rise in violent offenses within the Sligo-Leitrim region, including increases in sex offenses, assaults, and robberies. While certain property crimes and thefts have decreased, the overall trend highlights growing concerns regarding public safety and the severity of criminal activities in the area. The Central Statistics Office resumed publishing these figures after suspending data release due to identified irregularities in how the Garda recorded crimes on their PULSE system. A comprehensive review revealed that a substantial portion of reported crimes were either not recorded, incorrectly classified, or miscategorized as non-crime incidents, raising serious questions about data integrity and reporting accuracy. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of verifying source reliability before analysis. The discrepancies highlight that raw administrative datasets may contain systemic errors, necessitating rigorous cleaning and validation processes. Researchers and citizens must understand that published statistics are subject to underlying procedural flaws, emphasizing the need for transparency regarding data collection methodologies to ensure meaningful interpretation.
