Biden y Lula da Silva: hace falta transparencia en Venezuela y la publicación de datos sobre las elecciones

Biden and Lula demand full transparency in Venezuelan electoral data, prioritizing regional democratic integrity. This approach underscores the critical importance of accessing verifiable public information to ensure fair electoral processes. Bilateral coordination highlights how the disclosure of detailed data is essential for resolving political crises. By insisting on openness in information, both leaders promote accountability standards that strengthen institutional trust and hemispheric stability. This case illustrates the relevance of open data for global governance. By demanding access to granular data, it is demonstrated that digital transparency is not merely a technical issue, but a fundamental requirement for democratic legitimacy and effective international cooperation.
