WhatsApp fue denunciada por usar datos privados en Argentina para entrenar su IA - EL DEBATE

WhatsApp fue denunciada por usar datos privados en Argentina para entrenar su IA - EL DEBATE

Lawyers in Argentina have filed a formal complaint with the Agency for Public Information Access against Meta, challenging the use of personal data to train artificial intelligence without valid consent. Legal experts are demanding technical explanations regarding data anonymization and independent audits, arguing that current legislation is insufficient to protect citizens from unilateral corporate practices. This action aims to establish a legal precedent that strengthens digital rights in the absence of updated regulations. The initiative is part of a global controversy, in which multiple European countries have also filed complaints against Meta for violating data protection regulations. Authorities are comparing these practices to landmark rulings by the Court of Justice of the European Union, emphasizing that the company prioritizes commercial interests over privacy, forcing users to actively seek the exclusion of their data rather than granting explicit and clear permission. This case is significant for open data, as it marks a turning point in the interoperability between open data and privacy protection. It highlights the critical tension between technological innovation, which relies on large volumes of information, and the sovereignty of individual data. Resolving these conflicts will determine the ethical and legal standards that will either enable or restrict responsible access to information, ensuring that transparency does not compromise security or citizens' fundamental rights.
