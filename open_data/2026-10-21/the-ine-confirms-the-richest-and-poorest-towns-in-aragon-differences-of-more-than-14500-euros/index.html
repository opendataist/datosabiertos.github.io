The INE confirms the richest and poorest towns in Aragon: differences of more than 14,500 euros

The INE confirms the richest and poorest towns in Aragon: differences of more than 14,500 euros

The publication of the income distribution atlas by the Spanish National Statistics Institute (INE) reveals significant economic disparities in Aragon, highlighting that the most prosperous localities are small and located in the province of Huesca, whereas the southern part of the region generally exhibits lower income levels. This analysis helps to better understand the territorial reality and the factors influencing the wealth of each municipality. The article underscores the critical importance of understanding statistical methodologies when working with open data. The notable discrepancy between the INE’s results and those of the Tax Agency arises because the INE calculates the mean based on the entire population, regardless of whether individuals file tax returns. This demonstrates how small samples in rural settings can severely distort averages—a common analytical risk when manipulating datasets without a thorough understanding of their methodological foundations. This transparency is essential for the open data community, as it illustrates how the choice of indicator can drastically affect the interpretation of inequality. Grasping these methodological differences is crucial for producing accurate and useful information, thereby avoiding erroneous conclusions that may stem from the aggregation of data. Highlighting these nuances fosters a culture of critical analysis and rigor in the use of public statistics.
