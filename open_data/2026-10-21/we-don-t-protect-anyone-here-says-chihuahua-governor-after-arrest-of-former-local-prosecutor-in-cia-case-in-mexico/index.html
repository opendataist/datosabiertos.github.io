We don't protect anyone here," says Chihuahua governor after arrest of former local prosecutor in CIA case in Mexico

We don't protect anyone here," says Chihuahua governor after arrest of former local prosecutor in CIA case in Mexico

The governor of Chihuahua stated that her administration does not protect anyone from the arrest of the former local prosecutor in the CIA case. Her government is committed to responding to information requests and federal legal proceedings, ensuring that the law is applied without distinctions or privileges for former officials. This case is relevant to transparency and access to public information, which are fundamental pillars of open data. By guaranteeing that any entity or individual is investigated under the same legal criteria, it strengthens governmental accountability and public trust in institutions. The statement underscores the importance of collaboration among different levels of government to access relevant data. This facilitates public oversight and scrutiny of state actions, promoting more open governance where information flows freely to demonstrate that no one is above the law.
