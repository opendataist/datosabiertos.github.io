Tourism employment surpasses 3 million affiliates in September, up 4.7% from 2025 - Crónica de Cantabria

Tourism employment surpasses 3 million affiliates in September, up 4.7% from 2025 - Crónica de Cantabria

The tourism sector in Spain consolidated its labor growth during September, adding a significant number of new workers and reinforcing its role as a key pillar of the national labor market. This increase demonstrates the robustness of employment in tourism-related activities, significantly outpacing the overall growth rate observed across the country and reflecting a sustained positive trend over time. The hospitality industry stands out as the main driver of this expansion, absorbing the majority of the new workforce, both in accommodation and food services. Although travel agencies showed seemingly declining figures, these variations are minor and primarily stem from adjustments in the economic classification of activities, rather than a real reduction in labor demand, which nuances the interpretation of the absolute data. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparency and the correct interpretation of official statistical indicators are crucial. Understanding nuances, such as the reclassification of activities, enables citizens and analysts to avoid erroneous conclusions about the health of employment, fostering a more rigorous and comprehensive data culture in society.
