Accessibility - Eurostat

The Eurostat website is designed to be inclusive, aiming to support users with disabilities through features like keyboard navigation and screen reader compatibility. By adhering to EN 301 549 and WCAG Level AA standards, Eurostat demonstrates a commitment to digital inclusion. This approach is particularly relevant to open data, as it ensures that statistical information and datasets are accessible to a broader audience, including those with impairments. Despite these efforts, the site is only partially compliant. Significant limitations remain in how certain data visualizations and static documents are presented. Interactive tools and maps often lack necessary text alternatives, hindering access for users relying on assistive technologies. Additionally, many official documents in PDF and Excel formats are not yet accessible, which directly impacts the usability of open data resources for individuals with visual or mobility impairments. Eurostat acknowledges these barriers and is actively working to resolve them, prioritizing updates for frequently accessed content. The institution provides direct links to source datasets in table formats as a workaround for inaccessible visualizations. This transparency highlights the ongoing challenge of making complex data open and usable for everyone, emphasizing the need for continuous improvement in the accessibility of public statistical information.
