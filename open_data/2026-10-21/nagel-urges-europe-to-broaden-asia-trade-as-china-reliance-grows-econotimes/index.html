Nagel Urges Europe to Broaden Asia Trade as China Reliance Grows | EconoTimes

Nagel Urges Europe to Broaden Asia Trade as China Reliance Grows | EconoTimes

Europe faces a growing trade deficit with China, prompting calls to diversify supply chains and deepen commercial ties across Asia. This strategic shift aims to reduce reliance on critical imports while fostering resilient partnerships with emerging markets like India and Indonesia, thereby enhancing regional economic stability. Expanding trade is viewed as complementary to strengthening domestic industrial capabilities rather than a replacement for it. By investing in infrastructure and venture funding, Europe can build the manufacturing capacity necessary to compete globally, ensuring that external trade growth supports internal economic resilience and technological independence. This approach is vital for open data ecosystems, which thrive on interoperable standards and cross-border collaboration. Diverse trade relationships facilitate the free flow of information and digital services, reducing monopolistic risks and promoting a more robust, globally integrated framework for data sharing and innovation.
