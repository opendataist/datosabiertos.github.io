Do you live surrounded by the wealthy or in a low-income area? Here’s the income map of Valladolid, street by street | El Norte de Castilla

The National Statistics Institute (INE) reveals local economic inequality by publishing detailed data on average income by census section, enabling income comparisons street by street. This tool highlights significant gaps between affluent and disadvantaged areas, exposing social disparities that were previously invisible. It is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how statistical transparency facilitates territorial analysis, empowering citizens with precise information about their immediate surroundings.
