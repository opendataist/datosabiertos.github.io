INE will verify the electoral roll in Reynosa through home visits

INE will verify the electoral roll in Reynosa through home visits

The National Electoral Institute will launch a sample verification process to validate the quality of the electoral roll ahead of the 2027 elections. This process aims to ensure that the registered information accurately reflects the real situation of citizens, thereby safeguarding the integrity of the voter registry through home visits and specific surveys that assess both data coverage and updates. The relevance of this exercise for open data lies in the transparency and public availability of the resulting information. By publishing the official results on the institution’s portal, it facilitates access for citizens and civil society to verifiable data on the electoral base. This enables public scrutiny and independent analysis of the registry’s representativeness and accuracy, fostering trust in the democratic system through evidence-based accountability. Consequently, this initiative not only lays the logistical groundwork for the upcoming elections but also sets a precedent for transparency in the management of sensitive data. The publication of these critical data underscores the importance of maintaining up-to-date and accessible electoral records as an essential public good. This empowers citizens to monitor the quality of the inputs that underpin their right to vote, promoting a culture of institutional openness and continuous verification of public information.
