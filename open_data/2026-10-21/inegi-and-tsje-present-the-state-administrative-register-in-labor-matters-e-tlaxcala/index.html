INEGI and TSJE present the State Administrative Register in Labor Matters - e-tlaxcala

The implementation of the State Administrative Labor Registry (RALABE) by the Judicial Power of Tlaxcala and the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) represents a fundamental transformation in the governance of labor justice. This instrument not only organizes information but also establishes a critical infrastructure for accountability and evidence-based decision-making, aligning with the principles of transparency and legality that are pillars of modern governance. The generation and analysis of joint statistical data enables the identification of patterns and the improvement of judicial efficiency, translating information into concrete actions for a more humane and effective justice system. By standardizing and making these administrative records accessible, inter-institutional coordination is facilitated, and it is ensured that resources and public policies respond to the real needs of those seeking justice, optimizing the management of labor disputes. This case is relevant to the open data movement because it exemplifies how collaboration between judicial and statistical agencies can create more robust, useful, and transparent public information systems. By converting legal files into structured and accessible data, it promotes a culture of using public information for social scrutiny, civic auditing, and the continuous improvement of public policies, demonstrating that access to data is an essential tool for strengthening trust in institutions.
