Meghalaya Data Portal: Towards an accelerated and inclusive growth - The Shillong Times

Meghalaya faces significant economic challenges, characterized by low per-capita income and high multidimensional poverty, yet it possesses the resources to become a ten-billion-dollar economy. Achieving this ambitious target requires inclusive growth that prioritizes the poorest citizens. The core implication is that strategic policy redesign, driven by accurate insights, is essential to bridge the gap between potential and actual well-being for the majority of the population. Data serves as the critical catalyst for this transformation, enabling the government to evaluate past policies, track economic trends, and understand inter-sectoral relationships. By consolidating scattered public information into a centralized repository, authorities can move beyond intuition to evidence-based decision-making. This approach allows for precise monitoring of inflation, agricultural productivity, and socio-economic indicators, ensuring that interventions are targeted effectively rather than broadly distributed without impact. The Meghalaya Data Portal exemplifies open data principles by providing granular access to macroeconomic and micro-level datasets, including agricultural outputs, financial inclusion metrics, and government program beneficiaries. This transparency empowers citizens and policymakers to scrutinize resource allocation and identify inefficiencies in real-time. Consequently, open data becomes a tool for accountability and engagement, fostering trust between the state and its people while facilitating a fairer distribution of economic growth.
