INAI Seguirá Coadyuvando en el Combate a la Corrupción - Periódico El Orbe.

The Presiding Commission of the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) reaffirmed the institutional commitment to maintaining transparency as a fundamental tool against corruption, despite internal challenges. It was emphasized that accountability and citizen participation are indispensable avenues for consolidating the rule of law and achieving a fairer and more honest society. This stance underscores the priority of providing society with effective public mechanisms that ensure legality in public service. The lecture series aims to broaden knowledge about crimes such as illicit enrichment, identifying it as a structural obstacle that hinders economic development and limits basic rights. Collaboration among the INAI, the National Anti-Corruption System, and the Citizen Participation Committee is crucial for building a community of experts capable of preventing, identifying, and sanctioning these behaviors in a coordinated manner. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how access to public information fuels citizen oversight and anti-corruption efforts. The transparency of government data enables the detection of asset irregularities, demonstrating that the availability of information is key to demanding accountability and strengthening public integrity through proactive transparency.
