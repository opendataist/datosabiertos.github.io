Empleo ‘fortachón’: tasa de desempleo en México toca nivel más bajo desde 2005

Mexico’s labor market showed strong performance in early 2023, reaching the lowest unemployment rate on record. This significant drop in joblessness underscores a robust economic recovery, indicating that heightened economic activity is effectively absorbing available labor resources across various sectors. However, this positive trend also highlights a persistent structural challenge: the growth of informal employment. Although formal jobs were created, informal positions expanded concurrently, revealing that a substantial share of the workforce remains outside protective labor frameworks. This dynamic underscores the complexity of current job creation. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how public statistics can uncover nuanced socioeconomic realities beyond headline figures. Analyzing such detailed datasets enables more effective policy design and academic research, emphasizing the importance of transparent and accessible government information to address informal labor issues.
