Desempleo en México bajó al 2,7% en primer trimestre de 2023

Desempleo en México bajó al 2,7% en primer trimestre de 2023

Mexico’s first-quarter labor market shows significant improvement, with unemployment dropping to historic lows and a surge in active workforce participation. This growth, driven by gains in commerce, manufacturing, and services, reflects the economy’s recovery and expansion following the pandemic, indicating a robust return to labor market stability. However, the data reveals persistent structural challenges, notably a rise in informal employment and a substantial gender gap in labor force participation. While overall metrics improve, the high rate of informality and unequal opportunities between men and women suggest that the quality of employment and social equity remain critical issues that require targeted policy interventions. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly accessible, granular statistical indicators enable transparent analysis of economic health and social disparities. By releasing detailed breakdowns by sector, gender, and region, official statistical institutes empower researchers and citizens to hold governments accountable and design evidence-based policies that address both job creation and informal sector integration.
