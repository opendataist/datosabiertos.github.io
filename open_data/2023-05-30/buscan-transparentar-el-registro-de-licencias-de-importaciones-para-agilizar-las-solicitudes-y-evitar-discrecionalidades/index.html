Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The project promotes the creation of an Open Import Data Portal, designed to ensure full transparency of Argentina’s licensing system. By requiring the digital, up-to-date publication of data in open formats—such as the status of applications, product types, and associated amounts—the initiative aims to eliminate administrative discretion. This measure enables any citizen to access public information, thereby reducing corruption risks linked to opacity in import management. The initiative proposes simplifying administrative procedures and establishing strict maximum deadlines for resolving applications, including automatic approval if no response is issued within the stipulated timeframe. This seeks to replace previous systems, such as SIRA, which have been criticized for generating uncertainty and imposing unnecessary restrictions. Predictability in processing is essential to discourage the use of exchange controls as tools for arbitrary economic protectionism, promoting instead a management framework based on clear and objective rules. This approach is crucial for the open data movement, as it transforms government information from a restricted resource into an accessible public good. Aligning with international recommendations on governance, it demonstrates how proactive data publication can strengthen accountability and institutional trust. Transparency not only improves the efficiency of foreign trade but also serves as a preventive mechanism against abuse of power, validating the importance of open data as a tool for democracy and social oversight.
