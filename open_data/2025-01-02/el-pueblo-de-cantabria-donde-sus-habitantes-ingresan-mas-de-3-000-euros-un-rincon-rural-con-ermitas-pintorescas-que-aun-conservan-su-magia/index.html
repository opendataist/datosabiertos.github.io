El pueblo de Cantabria donde sus habitantes ingresan más de 3.000 euros: un rincón rural con ermitas pintorescas que aún conservan su magia

The article analyzes fiscal data to reveal significant economic disparities across Cantabrian municipalities, highlighting Santa Cruz de Bezana as the wealthiest area due to its proximity to Santander and high quality of life. Conversely, rural towns like Soba face substantial financial challenges. This contrast underscores how geographic location and accessibility to urban centers directly influence individual household incomes, creating a clear divide between affluent suburban zones and economically struggling interior regions. This narrative is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the power of publicly available administrative statistics, such as tax agency records, to uncover socio-economic trends. By making these figures accessible, citizens and researchers can visualize inequality and understand the underlying drivers of regional wealth distribution. Open data here acts as a transparent tool for analyzing public finance, allowing for a deeper comprehension of how policy and infrastructure impact community prosperity. Ultimately, the publication of this data supports informed public debate and policy-making. It enables stakeholders to identify areas requiring economic support or development, fostering evidence-based decisions rather than relying on assumptions. The transparency provided by open government data not only informs residents about their local economic standing but also holds authorities accountable for managing resources equitably, ensuring that wealth gaps are recognized and addressed through targeted interventions.
