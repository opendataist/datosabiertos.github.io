Servicio de trenes de carga China-Europa continúa haciendo nuevos progresos

The article highlights the robust expansion of China-Europe freight train services, characterized by significant year-over-year growth in the number of trips and cargo volume. This surge reflects the deepening economic integration between China and European markets, demonstrating the reliability and increasing capacity of overland rail corridors as a vital alternative to traditional shipping methods. Crucially, the network has evolved beyond simple point-to-point transport into a sophisticated intermodal system linking sea and land routes. By integrating ports such as Xiamen with inland rail hubs, the service drastically reduces transit times for goods moving between Southeast Asia and Central Asia, offering a faster, more efficient logistics solution that enhances supply chain resilience and operational speed. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it generates massive streams of transparent logistics, customs, and infrastructure data. The sharing of real-time tracking and statistical information by railway operators and customs authorities provides valuable datasets for analyzing global supply chain dynamics, optimizing international trade flows, and improving transparency in cross-border transportation networks.
