INAI, incapacitado pero aún operando

The INAI continues its operations despite being unable to convene as a full body. It focuses on educating university students about transparency and public management to foster youth engagement in state affairs. This outreach is vital because transparency and privacy are central to modern democracy. By involving young people, the institution aims to strengthen democratic participation and awareness of civic rights. The article highlights the INAI’s resilience and commitment to civic education. This demonstrates how transparency institutions maintain relevance by prioritizing public awareness and youth inclusion, even during institutional challenges.
