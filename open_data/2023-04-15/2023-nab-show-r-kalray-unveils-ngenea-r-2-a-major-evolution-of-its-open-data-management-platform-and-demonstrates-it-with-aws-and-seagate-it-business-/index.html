2023 NAB Show®: Kalray Unveils ngenea®2, A Major Evolution of Its Open Data Management Platform, and Demonstrates It With AWS and Seagate | IT Business Net

Kalray has released ngenea®2, an updated open data management and orchestration platform designed to streamline complex, data-centric workflows across diverse industries. By integrating with Kalray’s software-defined storage and specialized processors, the platform enables faster, more efficient, and secure data processing. This advancement addresses critical challenges in handling large-scale data environments, allowing organizations to manage infrastructure and applications with greater simplicity and cost-efficiency. The platform consolidates management into a single control point, facilitating simultaneous deployment of workflows across hybrid cloud and on-premises setups. A key innovation is its ability to leverage Data Processing Units for high-performance in-storage processing and AI-assisted insights. Furthermore, it offers unified interfaces that integrate third-party solutions, providing developers with instant global access to real-time data. This interoperability ensures a cohesive view of assets, significantly reducing the complexity typically associated with managing disparate storage and application environments. This release is highly relevant to the open_data community as it demonstrates how specialized hardware and open orchestration software can democratize access to high-performance data processing. By lowering the barrier to entry for managing complex datasets, it empowers data scientists and developers to focus on innovation rather than infrastructure bottlenecks. The emphasis on secure, global accessibility and seamless integration highlights a shift towards more flexible, transparent data ecosystems, benefiting sectors from media and finance to scientific research.
