Publicación de la evolución del Producto Interior Bruto en el cuarto trimestre de 2022

The article highlights a robust economic expansion in Equatorial Guinea, driven primarily by growth in the non-oil sectors and public spending. Despite significant declines in crude oil production and financial services, the economy strengthened through increased agricultural output, construction activity, and performance in transport and commerce. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the importance of transparent, sector-disaggregated statistical reporting for understanding national economic resilience. By making detailed quarterly indicators publicly available through INEGE, the government fosters accountability and enables stakeholders to analyze the specific drivers of growth beyond headline figures. Ultimately, the narrative emphasizes that sustainable development depends on diversifying the economy away from volatile oil revenues. Open access to such granular economic data facilitates better policy decisions and investment strategies, ensuring that growth in agriculture, services, and public administration is accurately tracked and supported for long-term stability.
