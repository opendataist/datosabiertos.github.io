Visitación turística a Costa Rica sigue cayendo: vea los datos del primer trimestre del 2025

Visitación turística a Costa Rica sigue cayendo: vea los datos del primer trimestre del 2025

Costa Rica’s tourism sector is experiencing a significant slowdown, primarily driven by sharp declines in arrivals from Europe and the Middle East. While growth from South America and the Caribbean provides a slight counterbalance, the overall volume of international visitors has contracted, highlighting vulnerabilities in the country’s traditional high-volume markets and signaling a broader economic slowdown. The industry attributes this stagnation to structural challenges, including unfavorable exchange rates, inadequate infrastructure, and rising insecurity. These factors threaten Costa Rica’s competitiveness against neighboring countries. Consequently, maintaining market relevance requires more than relying on its existing reputation; it demands urgent strategic adjustments to address the operational and safety-related hurdles that currently deter potential travelers. This situation is highly relevant to open data advocates because it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible tourism statistics. Reliable public data enables stakeholders to accurately identify regional disparities and underlying causes. Furthermore, it facilitates evidence-based policymaking and public accountability, ensuring that the sector can respond effectively to emerging trends and infrastructure needs rather than reacting blindly to economic shifts.
