Erradicación de la pobreza en Paraguay podría costar unos US$ 700 millones - Economía - ABC Color

Erradicación de la pobreza en Paraguay podría costar unos US$ 700 millones - Economía - ABC Color

The article concludes that eradicating poverty in Paraguay is financially feasible but requires strategic political will and efficient program design, rather than mere moral intent. By targeting only half of the impoverished population for direct assistance, the estimated annual cost drops significantly to approximately US$ 695 million. This figure provides a concrete benchmark for fiscal effort, allowing policymakers to compare potential interventions against the national budget and existing transfer programs. Data reveals stark regional disparities, with extreme poverty concentrating heavily in rural areas due to structural vulnerabilities and inadequate service coverage. Focusing solely on eliminating extreme poverty drastically reduces the required investment to roughly US$ 67 million annually. This highlights that addressing the most severe deprivation is a manageable economic challenge, provided resources are allocated precisely to bridge the income gap between current household earnings and the poverty line. This analysis is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistical records can inform high-stakes policy decisions. Accurate social registries and publicly available economic indicators enable the precise targeting of beneficiaries, reducing implementation complexity. Ultimately, the article argues that leveraging such data-driven insights transforms poverty alleviation from an abstract goal into a calculable, actionable public priority, fostering accountability and measurable development impact.
