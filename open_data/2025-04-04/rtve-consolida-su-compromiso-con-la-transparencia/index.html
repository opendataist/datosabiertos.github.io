RTVE consolida su compromiso con la transparencia

RTVE consolida su compromiso con la transparencia

RTVE’s transparency portal demonstrates growing public trust and institutional responsiveness to access-to-information requests. This surge in fulfilled petitions highlights a systemic shift toward greater accountability and openness within public broadcasting. The steady increase in favorable resolutions since 2015 reflects the successful implementation of transparency laws. It shows that citizens are actively using these mechanisms to monitor corporate activities and governance structures. This trend is crucial for open data ecosystems, as it validates the effectiveness of digital transparency platforms. By facilitating easy public access to information, such initiatives empower citizens and reinforce democratic oversight of public institutions.
