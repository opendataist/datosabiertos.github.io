Cuidado con lo que compartes en WhatsApp; estos datos pueden ponerte en riesgo

Cuidado con lo que compartes en WhatsApp; estos datos pueden ponerte en riesgo

Sharing sensitive personal data on messaging apps such as WhatsApp exposes users to risks of identity theft, financial fraud, and threats to physical security. The article stresses that information often perceived as harmless can be exploited by cybercriminals for malicious purposes, underscoring the critical need for good digital hygiene. Privacy settings must be strictly configured to limit access to personal details, photos, and status updates. Users are advised to regularly audit their contact lists and restrict visibility to trusted individuals, recognizing that shared data can also endanger friends and family members without their consent. This topic is vital for the open data movement because it illustrates the inverse of its principles: rather than advocating for transparency, it warns against the dangerous exposure of private information. It highlights that while open data promotes societal benefits through transparency, individual privacy requires rigorous protection to prevent abuse and maintain trust in digital ecosystems.
