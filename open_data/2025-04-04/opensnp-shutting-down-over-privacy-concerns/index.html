openSNP Shutting Down Over Privacy Concerns

openSNP Shutting Down Over Privacy Concerns

openSNP is closing due to rising privacy threats and the potential misuse of genetic data by authoritarian regimes. This decision reflects growing anxieties about how sensitive personal information can be exploited in an increasingly surveillance-heavy global landscape. The recent bankruptcy of 23andMe intensified these concerns, as it raised questions about the fate of millions of customers' data and increased interest from law enforcement and corporations. This event demonstrated the fragility of commercial genetic services and the risks associated with centralized data storage. This shutdown is highly relevant to open data advocates, as it illustrates the critical tension between transparency and security. It serves as a stark reminder that open access to personal biological information requires robust privacy protections to prevent exploitation by state and non-state actors.
