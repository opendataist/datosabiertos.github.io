Phishing vs spam (y malspam): Cuáles son las diferencias - ebizlatam.com

A clear distinction between spam, phishing, and malspam is crucial for protecting the privacy and integrity of personal data. While spam aims to promote advertising, phishing deceives users into surrendering confidential information, and malspam seeks to infect devices. Understanding these differences enables users to correctly identify threats and apply appropriate defenses. This conceptual clarity prevents confusion that could compromise individuals’ digital and financial security. The relevance of this article to the field of open data lies in the importance of digital literacy and informational transparency. In ecosystems where large volumes of information are shared, the ability to discern between legitimate communication and fraud is essential for maintaining trust in data systems. If users cannot distinguish between benign and malicious content, the integrity of their interactions with data platforms is eroded, affecting the adoption and secure use of public information. To mitigate these risks, it is recommended to adopt precautionary practices, such as verifying the authenticity of senders and being skeptical of offers that seem too good to be true. Implementing proactive security tools and using temporary email addresses for subscriptions are effective strategies. By empowering users with knowledge about these threats, collective resilience against cyber scams is strengthened, ensuring that access to information does not become a gateway for identity theft or the manipulation of personal data.
