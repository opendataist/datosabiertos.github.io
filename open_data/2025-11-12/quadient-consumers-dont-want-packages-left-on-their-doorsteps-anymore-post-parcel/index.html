Quadient: consumers don’t want packages left on their doorsteps anymore | Post & Parcel

Quadient: consumers don’t want packages left on their doorsteps anymore | Post & Parcel

Parcel theft in the UK has reached unprecedented levels, causing hundreds of millions in losses and affecting millions of households. Despite this surge, the vast majority of incidents go unreported to police, indicating a significant gap between actual crime rates and official statistics. This underreporting obscures the true magnitude of the issue, highlighting a disconnect between consumer experience and legal accountability. Consumer behavior is shifting rapidly in response to these risks, with a strong preference for secure alternatives over doorstep deliveries. Most people now favor collection points or lockers to avoid theft, and many would rather miss a delivery entirely than risk loss. This clear signal from the public underscores a demand for safer, more controlled receiving methods, challenging the current norm of leaving packages unattended. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how Freedom of Information requests can expose systemic failures and drive policy change. By making theft statistics transparent, stakeholders can advocate for better infrastructure and retail practices. Ultimately, sharing such insights empowers communities to demand secure delivery solutions, reducing crime and restoring trust in the logistics ecosystem.
