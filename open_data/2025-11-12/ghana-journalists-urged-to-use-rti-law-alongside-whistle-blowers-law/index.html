Ghana: Journalists Urged to Use RTI Law Alongside Whistle Blowers' Law

Ghana: Journalists Urged to Use RTI Law Alongside Whistle Blowers' Law

Ghanaian journalists are urged to utilize the Right to Information Law for independent verification rather than relying solely on whistleblowers, whose motives may lack neutrality. This approach ensures greater accuracy, fairness, and credibility in investigative reporting, moving beyond disputed narratives to build robust, evidence-based stories. The article highlights the importance of mastering RTI requests and fact-checking to authenticate information and promote public accountability. By balancing sources with official data, media professionals can better serve as the public’s eyes and ears, exposing corruption and maintaining journalistic integrity despite inherent challenges. This guidance is relevant to open data as it champions the use of transparent, publicly accessible government information to drive accountability. It underscores how leveraging open data mechanisms empowers journalists to create trustworthy content, fostering a culture of transparency and informed public discourse.
