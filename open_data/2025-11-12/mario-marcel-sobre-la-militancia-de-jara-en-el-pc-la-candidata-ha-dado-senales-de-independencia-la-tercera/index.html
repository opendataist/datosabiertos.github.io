Mario Marcel sobre la militancia de Jara en el PC: “La candidata ha dado señales de independencia” - La Tercera

Mario Marcel sobre la militancia de Jara en el PC: “La candidata ha dado señales de independencia” - La Tercera

Mario Marcel’s recent discourse highlights a critical shift in Chilean economic governance toward integrating natural capital into fiscal planning, moving beyond traditional indicators like inflation or GDP. This conceptual expansion underscores the necessity for open data frameworks that can accurately measure, monitor, and publicly disclose the value of environmental assets. By treating natural resources as central economic drivers rather than externalities, policymakers require transparent, high-quality datasets to inform sustainable development strategies, ensuring that ecological limits are respected in long-term budgetary decisions. Regarding fiscal policy and the upcoming elections, Marcel defends the executive branch’s exclusive responsibility for revenue estimation, arguing that current tax collection trends, bolstered by anti-evasion laws, show resilience rather than deterioration. This stance emphasizes the importance of accessible, real-time fiscal data to validate government projections against political rhetoric. Open data initiatives are vital here, as they allow citizens and analysts to independently verify whether revenue figures align with actual economic performance, thereby fostering accountability and reducing ambiguity during electoral campaigns. Finally, Marcel critiques the feasibility of proposed massive tax cuts, noting their inherent contradiction in maintaining fiscal stability without corresponding spending reductions. He asserts that public debt will remain well below legal limits, contradicting narratives of economic collapse. This debate illustrates the relevance of open data for macroeconomic transparency; when detailed budgetary projections, debt trajectories, and tax impact analyses are publicly available, it becomes easier to dismantle misinformation. Accessible data empowers voters to evaluate the true economic implications of candidate platforms, moving the discourse beyond polarized slogans toward evidence-based decision-making.
