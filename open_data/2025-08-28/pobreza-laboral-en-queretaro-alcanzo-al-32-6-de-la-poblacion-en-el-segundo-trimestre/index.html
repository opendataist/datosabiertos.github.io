Pobreza laboral en Querétaro alcanzó al 32.6% de la población en el segundo trimestre

Pobreza laboral en Querétaro alcanzó al 32.6% de la población en el segundo trimestre

The article reveals that a significant portion of Querétaro’s workforce earns less than the cost of the basic food basket, indicating severe labor poverty that rose sharply in the most recent quarter. Although the annual rate decreased, the state remains below the national average, highlighting ongoing economic vulnerabilities for its residents despite recent improvements. This data underscores the critical need for accessible and transparent official statistics to accurately monitor social welfare. By providing granular insights into income insufficiency, open data enables researchers and policymakers to identify at-risk populations and evaluate the real-world impact of employment policies across different regions. The relevance to open data lies in its capacity to facilitate evidence-based decision-making. When such economic indicators are publicly available and standardized, it empowers civil society to hold institutions accountable and drives targeted interventions aimed at reducing inequality and improving livelihoods for vulnerable groups nationwide.
