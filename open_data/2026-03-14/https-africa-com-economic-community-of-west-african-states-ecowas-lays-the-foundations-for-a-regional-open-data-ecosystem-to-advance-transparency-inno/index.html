Open Data
ES EN

ECOWAS is advancing a harmonized regional legal framework for open data, aiming to standardize how public sector information is published and reused across West Africa. By moving toward an open-by-default model, the initiative seeks to balance transparency with necessary safeguards for privacy and security. This coordinated approach ensures that diverse member states can align their digital governance practices, creating a unified ecosystem that facilitates the responsible exchange of high-value datasets. The proposed framework emphasizes the critical role of open data in driving digital transformation and economic innovation within the region. By enabling better access to government-generated information, the initiative empowers businesses, researchers, and civil society to develop solutions for socio-economic challenges. This shift supports evidence-based policymaking and enhances service delivery, fostering a robust environment where digital entrepreneurship and knowledge-based growth can thrive through improved interoperability between national systems. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates as it establishes a scalable model for regional data governance. It demonstrates how legal structures can be crafted to promote institutional accountability and citizen engagement while stimulating technological advancement. By institutionalizing open access principles at a multinational level, ECOWAS highlights the potential for standardized policies to overcome fragmentation, ultimately proving that structured data sharing is a foundational pillar for sustainable regional integration and democratic transparency.
