In 2023, residents from Latin America and Morocco significantly increased their acquisition of Spanish citizenship, marking a substantial rise in naturalizations. This surge highlights the integration patterns of major immigrant groups into Spanish society. The data reveals that most new citizens had previously lived abroad, taking roughly five years to naturalize. This reflects a distinct integration trajectory compared to those who have resided in Spain since birth. These figures are vital for open data initiatives tracking migration and civic inclusion. They enable the analysis of demographic shifts and policy impacts, fostering transparent, data-driven governance and societal understanding.
