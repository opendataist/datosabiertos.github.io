Incrementa el uso de Tecnologías de la Información en México, revela encuesta del INEGI e IFT

Incrementa el uso de Tecnologías de la Información en México, revela encuesta del INEGI e IFT

The recent joint release of the ENDUTIH 2023 data by the IFT and INEGI reveals a significant digital transformation within Mexican households, highlighting the widespread adoption of internet and mobile technologies. This collaboration underscores the critical role of inter-institutional partnerships in generating reliable, comprehensive datasets that drive evidence-based public policy. Despite high connectivity rates, notable disparities persist across demographic groups and regions, indicating that digital inclusion remains an uneven process. While younger populations lead in usage intensity and smartphone penetration, older adults and specific geographic areas show lagging progress, suggesting that universal access requires targeted interventions rather than assuming uniform technological saturation. This data is essential for open data initiatives, as it provides the granular metrics needed to assess the digital divide and evaluate the effectiveness of current telecommunications policies. By making these insights publicly available, stakeholders can better understand how technology serves different societal segments, fostering transparency and enabling more equitable resource allocation to bridge remaining connectivity gaps.
