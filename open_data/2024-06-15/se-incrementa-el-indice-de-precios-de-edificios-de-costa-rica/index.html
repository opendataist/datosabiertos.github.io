Se incrementa el Índice de Precios de Edificios de Costa Rica

Costa Rica’s construction price index reveals a divergence between general building costs, which rose due to machinery and exchange rates, and social housing costs, which continued their downward trend. This data highlights how external economic factors and specific material availability directly impact the financial viability of different construction sectors, reflecting broader market dynamics. For open data enthusiasts, this report exemplifies the importance of accessible, granular statistical indicators in monitoring economic health. By providing detailed breakdowns of material and service costs, such datasets enable transparent analysis of inflationary pressures and sector-specific trends. This relevance extends to fostering informed decision-making for policymakers and researchers who rely on accurate, timely public statistics. The structured nature of these indices allows for meaningful comparative analysis, demonstrating how open statistical frameworks support accountability and deeper understanding of national economic conditions.
