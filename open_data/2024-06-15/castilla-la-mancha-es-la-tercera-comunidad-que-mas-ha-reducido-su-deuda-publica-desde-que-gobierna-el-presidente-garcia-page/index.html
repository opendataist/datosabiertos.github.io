Castilla-La Mancha es la tercera comunidad que más ha reducido su deuda pública desde que gobierna el presidente García-Page

Castilla-La Mancha significantly reduced its public debt burden relative to GDP, outperforming the national average. This achievement demonstrates that fiscal consolidation can coexist with robust social spending and economic stimulus. The region proves that strict financial management does not require sacrificing support for vulnerable groups or economic growth. This balance highlights effective governance under current leadership, ensuring resources reach those who need them most while maintaining stability. This case is relevant to open data because it relies on transparent statistical evidence from the Bank of Spain. Openly accessible fiscal metrics allow citizens to verify government performance, fostering accountability and trust in public administration decisions.
