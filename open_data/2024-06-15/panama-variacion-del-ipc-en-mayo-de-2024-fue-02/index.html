Panamá | Variación del IPC en mayo de 2024 fue -0,2%

Panamá | Variación del IPC en mayo de 2024 fue -0,2%

The National Institute of Statistics and Census (INEC) of Panama reported a slight decrease in the National Urban Consumer Price Index (CPI) in May 2024 compared to the previous month. This monthly decline was primarily driven by reductions in transportation costs, including air travel and automotive fuels, as well as decreases in the prices of alcoholic beverages, clothing, housing utilities, and pharmaceutical products. These downward pressures were partially offset by modest increases in food prices, hospitality services, and household goods, resulting in a nuanced shift in the immediate cost landscape for consumers. Looking at the annual perspective, the CPI showed a moderate year-over-year increase, indicating that while short-term fluctuations exist, the long-term trend points to gentle inflation. Notably, transportation, diverse goods and services, and housing utilities experienced the most significant annual price hikes. Conversely, sectors such as apparel, home furnishings, and recreation saw price reductions over the past year. This divergence highlights how different economic segments are responding to varying market dynamics, supply chain adjustments, and changing consumer demands over a twelve-month period. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the necessity of granular, publicly accessible statistical information for effective economic monitoring. Transparent reporting of consumer price indices allows researchers, policymakers, and developers to build predictive models, analyze inflationary trends, and create tools that enhance economic literacy. By making detailed breakdowns of price changes available, governments empower the community to understand purchasing power shifts and foster data-driven decision-making across various sectors.
