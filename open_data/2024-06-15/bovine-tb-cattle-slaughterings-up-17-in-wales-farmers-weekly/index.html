Bovine TB cattle slaughterings up 17% in Wales - Farmers Weekly

Bovine TB cattle slaughterings up 17% in Wales - Farmers Weekly

Bovine tuberculosis continues to worsen in Wales, marked by significant increases in slaughtered cattle and rising herd incidence and prevalence rates. This deterioration creates urgent pressure on Welsh farmers, who are calling for immediate political action and science-based interventions to manage the crisis effectively amidst evolving agricultural policies. The emergence of a new technical advisory group offers a potential pathway forward, emphasizing the need for policymakers to prioritize scientific recommendations over political cycles. Stakeholders argue that acknowledging the severity of the data is crucial for developing effective strategies, as the current trajectory threatens the sustainability of dairy farming and the livelihoods of those dependent on it. This situation highlights the critical role of open data in transparently tracking public health crises and informing evidence-based governance. By making detailed incidence and prevalence statistics accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand disease dynamics, enabling more targeted and responsive strategies to control bovine TB across devolved nations.
