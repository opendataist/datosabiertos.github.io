Las transferencias nacionales a las provincias se contrajeron un 84%

The Éforo Foundation reveals that national transfers to the provinces suffered a historic contraction of 84% in real terms. This drastic fiscal adjustment affects all jurisdictions uniformly, with declines exceeding 60% even in the regions that fared best, highlighting a widespread and profound reduction in centralized public spending directed toward local governments. The cuts are severely concentrated in essential services: the transport subsidy has virtually disappeared, and social policies such as the Universal Child Allowance have fallen by nearly 98%. Social security has also experienced a complete interruption of funding from the national government, while investment in health and education infrastructure has been reduced by more than three-quarters, leaving numerous provinces without resources for basic maintenance and cultural development. This situation is crucial for the open data community, as it demonstrates the transformative power of public data in democratic oversight. By making financial flows accessible and comparable, citizens and analysts can quickly expose inequalities and rights violations. The case underscores how transparency in public information enables monitoring of the real impact of economic policies, compelling the State to account for the distribution of resources and the protection of basic social services.
