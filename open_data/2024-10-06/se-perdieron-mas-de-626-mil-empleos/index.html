Se perdieron más de 626 mil empleos

Se perdieron más de 626 mil empleos

This report highlights a significant reversal in Mexico’s labor market in August 2024, contrasting sharply with the gains recorded in July. The substantial loss of jobs, particularly in the services and informal sectors, suggests underlying economic instability that simple headcount metrics might obscure. Understanding these discrepancies requires critical analysis of data sources. The relevance to open data lies in the urgent need to access and verify raw statistical figures independently. Discrepancies between INEGI and IMSS data underscore the importance of transparent, open datasets for accurate economic assessment. Citizens and analysts must rely on open sources to detect such inconsistencies. Ultimately, this case illustrates why open data protocols are vital for public accountability. Without unrestricted access to detailed, granular information, stakeholders cannot fully comprehend labor trends or challenge official narratives. Open data empowers society to verify claims and demand better economic policies based on verifiable evidence rather than aggregated summaries.
