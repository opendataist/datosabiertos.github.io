The Manipur Information Commission launched RTI Week 2024 to strengthen transparency and accountability in governance. By emphasizing the RTI Act’s role in combating corruption and empowering citizens, the initiative highlights how access to government-held information fosters better public participation and trust in state institutions. A major focus involved workshops on pro-active disclosure and online filing, directly addressing recent Supreme Court judgments. These discussions underscore the legal evolution of information rights, ensuring that digital mechanisms and mandatory disclosures align with judicial mandates for open governance and efficient administrative practices. This event is crucial for open_data as it bridges legal frameworks with practical implementation. It promotes the digitization of public records and encourages authorities to release information proactively. Such efforts ensure that data is not just available but accessible, turning the right to information into tangible, usable assets for civic engagement.
