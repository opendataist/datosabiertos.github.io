Ordenan a Pemex informar sobre envíos de combustible a Cuba | Revista Punto de Vista

Ordenan a Pemex informar sobre envíos de combustible a Cuba | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI has ordered Pemex to disclose detailed records of fuel shipments to Cuba, rejecting claims that the documentation is missing. This ruling emphasizes that international transfers must be transparently justified, whether as commercial transactions or humanitarian aid, ensuring public accountability for the management of resources. Transparency enables citizens to effectively scrutinize Mexico’s international relations and the use of public funds. The decision underscores that administrative entities must actively seek out relevant information rather than asserting its absence, reinforcing the duty to provide comprehensive data on state actions. This case highlights the critical role of open data in enforcing transparency rights. By compelling the release of specific operational details, it demonstrates how legal frameworks protect access to information, ensuring that government dealings remain visible and subject to public oversight.
