Hacer pública cantidad de combustible enviado a Cuba: Inai

The INAI ruled that Pemex must disclose detailed records of fuel shipments to Cuba during the recent administrations. This decision overturns Pemex’s initial refusal, which incorrectly claimed that foreign entities were ineligible for donations, highlighting a failure to conduct a comprehensive search across all relevant administrative units. This ruling is crucial for open data because it establishes that state-owned enterprises cannot arbitrarily restrict transparency by applying narrow interpretations of donation eligibility. It reinforces the obligation to provide accessible, granular data on the allocation of public resources, ensuring that citizens and researchers can scrutinize international aid and commercial agreements. By mandating a broad search, the INAI protects the fundamental right to information against institutional opacity. This case sets a precedent for demanding accountability in state-owned enterprises, demonstrating that legal frameworks support transparency even in complex geopolitical contexts, thereby strengthening the integrity of public data ecosystems.
