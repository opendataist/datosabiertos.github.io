¿Qué acciones está comprando y vendiendo el mítico inversor Warren Buffett?

The article highlights significant portfolio adjustments by Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger, revealing a strategic shift away from certain industrial and consumer sectors while maintaining heavy concentration in financials and technology. Buffett’s divestment from companies like General Motors and Johnson & Johnson, alongside reduced stakes in others, suggests a recalibration of risk exposure, even as his core holdings in Apple and major banks remain dominant. This selective pruning indicates a focus on capital preservation and quality over broad diversification. Munger’s portfolio reflects a similar philosophy, characterized by extreme concentration in a few long-term positions, primarily within the banking sector. His notable admission regarding Alibaba serves as a candid lesson on investment pitfalls, illustrating how overconfidence in market positioning can lead to misjudged future returns. This transparency underscores the importance of rigorous due diligence and the willingness to acknowledge errors in investment strategies.
