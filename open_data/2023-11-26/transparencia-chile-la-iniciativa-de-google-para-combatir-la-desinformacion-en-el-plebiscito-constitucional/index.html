Transparencia Chile: la iniciativa de Google para combatir la desinformación en el plebiscito constitucional

Chile is approaching a pivotal constitutional plebiscite, prompting Google to launch “Transparency Chile” to combat misinformation and foster informed civic participation. This initiative prioritizes transparency and content quality, aiming to empower citizens with reliable information during this critical democratic process. By addressing the challenges of online disinformation, Google seeks to ensure that voters can make decisions based on accurate data rather than manipulated narratives. The core of this effort is a public report detailing political advertising on Google and YouTube, revealing sponsors, spending, reach, and demographic targeting. Mandatory verification for political entities ensures accountability, while additional tools like Fact Check Explorer, reverse image search, and prebunking techniques equip users to detect manipulation. These measures collectively enhance the integrity of the digital information ecosystem surrounding the election. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publishing detailed, accessible datasets about political advertising promotes societal accountability. By making sponsorship and targeting metrics transparent, Google facilitates public scrutiny of digital political influence. Such open data practices are essential for verifying the authenticity of political discourse, supporting a healthy democracy through an informed citizenry and robust oversight of online media practices.
