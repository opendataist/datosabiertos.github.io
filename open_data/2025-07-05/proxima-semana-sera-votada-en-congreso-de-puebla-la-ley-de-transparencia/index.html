Próxima semana será votada en Congreso de Puebla la Ley de Transparencia

Próxima semana será votada en Congreso de Puebla la Ley de Transparencia

The government of Puebla is advancing significant reforms to its transparency and personal data protection laws, signaling a major shift in how public information is managed and secured. By replacing the previous guarantee body with decentralized organs within existing ministries, the state aims to streamline access to information while ensuring stricter oversight. This structural change is designed to provide legal certainty for citizens requesting information from municipal authorities, thereby enhancing the efficiency and accountability of local governance. These legislative updates are crucial for open data because they redefine the institutional framework responsible for enforcing transparency rights. The new model aligns state practices with federal standards, promoting a more standardized and robust approach to data accessibility. By integrating transparency functions into broader administrative bodies, the reform seeks to remove bureaucratic barriers, making it easier for the public to obtain and utilize government data without procedural hurdles. Furthermore, the concurrent approval of the Personal Data Protection Law complements these transparency measures by establishing clear guidelines on how citizen information is handled. This dual focus ensures that while information becomes more accessible, individual privacy remains protected. The relevance to open data lies in this balance: creating an environment where data is not only available but also managed responsibly, fostering trust in public institutions and encouraging greater civic engagement through reliable information.
