Inflación junio 2025: conozca las 8 razones por las que Bolivia es la 2da economía más inflacionaria de la región

The Bolivian economy is experiencing unprecedented inflation, reaching historic highs in the first half of 2025. Monthly and cumulative price indices have surged significantly, outpacing regional averages and government targets by a wide margin. Food prices have skyrocketed, severely impacting household purchasing power and pushing many citizens toward poverty. This inflationary spiral is driven by a combination of excessive money printing, currency devaluation, supply chain disruptions, and political instability. For open data enthusiasts, this report highlights the critical necessity of accessible, transparent, and timely statistical information. Accurate monitoring of inflation metrics is essential not only for academic analysis but also for public accountability and an informed citizenry. When data reveals such drastic deviations from economic stability, it underscores the need for reliable official sources to track policy failures and their social impacts. Without clear, verifiable data, understanding the true scale of economic distress remains difficult for both domestic and international observers. The relevance to open data lies in its role as a tool for transparency and civic oversight. This article demonstrates how raw statistical indicators can tell a compelling story about governance and economic health. By making such data openly available, authorities allow for independent verification of official narratives. Ultimately, robust open data ecosystems are vital for exposing structural economic issues, enabling researchers and the public to demand accountability and better policy outcomes in times of crisis.
