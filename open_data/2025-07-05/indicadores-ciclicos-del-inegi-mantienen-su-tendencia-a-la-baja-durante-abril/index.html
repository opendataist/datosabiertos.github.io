Indicadores cíclicos del Inegi mantienen su tendencia a la baja durante abril

Indicadores cíclicos del Inegi mantienen su tendencia a la baja durante abril

The Mexican economy is showing a clear deceleration, as reflected in the downward trend of the Cyclic Indicators system maintained by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). Although this does not yet confirm a recession, the consistent decline in both coincident and leading indicators points to a sustained slowdown in economic activity throughout the year. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides a standardized, publicly accessible framework for monitoring macroeconomic cycles. By tracking these composite indicators, stakeholders can anticipate economic shifts and analyze trends with greater transparency and timely access to official statistics. Ultimately, the release of such granular, high-frequency economic data empowers researchers and policymakers to make informed decisions. It underscores the importance of maintaining open, reliable datasets that reflect real-time economic health, facilitating better analysis and forecasting for Mexico’s financial landscape.
