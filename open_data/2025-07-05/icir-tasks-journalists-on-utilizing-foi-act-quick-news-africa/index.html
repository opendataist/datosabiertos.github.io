ICIR tasks journalists on utilizing FOI Act - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

ICIR tasks journalists on utilizing FOI Act - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

Journalists in Nigeria are being urged to actively utilize the Freedom of Information Act as a vital tool for investigative reporting. By demanding transparency from relevant organizations, media professionals can effectively expose corruption and abuse of power, thereby fostering open governance. This approach empowers citizens with the necessary information to make informed decisions in a representative democracy, shifting the burden of enforcement away from civil society and placing it squarely on the shoulders of the press. However, significant implementation challenges remain, particularly regarding bureaucratic inefficiencies and legal exemptions. While the law mandates quick responses, public institutions often struggle with cumbersome processes, and specific sections allow for withholding information deemed sensitive. Experts argue that the current seven-day timeframe is insufficient for complex requests and emphasize that certain records must remain protected for the collective good, highlighting the tension between transparency and privacy. The article is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the gap between legal frameworks for data access and their practical enforcement. It highlights the critical need for structured compliance mechanisms, proactive disclosure templates, and accountability measures within government agencies. Without these structural improvements and consequences for non-compliance, the potential for open data initiatives remains largely unrealized, leaving valuable public information hoarded rather than freely available for societal benefit and technological innovation.
