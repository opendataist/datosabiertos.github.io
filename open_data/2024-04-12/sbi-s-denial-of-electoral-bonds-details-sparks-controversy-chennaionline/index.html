SBI's Denial of Electoral Bonds Details Sparks Controversy – Chennaionline

SBI’s refusal to share electoral bond data under the RTI Act highlights the tension between banking confidentiality and public transparency. This secrecy undermines the open data principles essential for tracking political funding. The Supreme Court’s reprimand emphasizes that financial institutions must disclose donor details. Without full data availability, citizens cannot effectively hold entities accountable. This case illustrates critical gaps in India’s electoral transparency infrastructure. It shows how institutional resistance can obstruct the flow of essential civic information, hindering public oversight and trust in democratic processes.
