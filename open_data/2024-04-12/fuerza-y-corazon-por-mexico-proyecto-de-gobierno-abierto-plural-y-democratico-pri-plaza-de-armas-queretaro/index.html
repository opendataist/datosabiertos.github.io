“Fuerza y Corazón Por México”, proyecto de gobierno abierto, plural y democrático: PRI - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

The PRI coalition advocates for open, plural, and democratic governance, contrasting its transversal approach with centralized models that concentrate power and resources. This political stance emphasizes broad participation in national development, rejecting the exclusionary practices associated with absolute presidentialism and fiscal control by a select few. Regarding the decline in the construction sector since 2018, the article highlights the negative impact of reduced public investment and the prioritization of military contractors over civilian experts. This shift has significantly hindered local economic stability and growth, underscoring the urgent need for policies that restore confidence and support professional engineering and architectural talent in public works. The proposed solutions focus on transparency, anti-corruption measures, and citizen-led oversight to ensure accountable resource management. This article is relevant to open data because it advocates for citizen observatories and transparent urban development policies, which fundamentally rely on accessible, high-quality public information to monitor government actions and validate democratic accountability.
