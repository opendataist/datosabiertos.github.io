La Agrupación de Hostelería apunta que el turismo rural sigue en crecimiento en la provincia de Cuenca

The article highlights the robust growth of rural tourism in Cuenca, attributing increased occupancy rates to new accommodations and emphasizing its vital role in sustaining significant sector investments. This expansion is crucial for maintaining economic viability and generating essential employment in rural areas, positioning it as a primary engine for local development rather than merely a transient trend. This surge demonstrates the effectiveness of infrastructure expansion in driving visitor numbers and overnight stays, confirming that higher capacity directly correlates with greater demand. The data underscores a strong recovery and growing international interest, validating the strategic focus on rural hospitality as a key pillar for regional stability and future growth. The relevance to open data lies in how statistical transparency from the National Institute of Statistics enables sector organizations to validate trends, justify investments, and inform policy. Open data serves as the empirical backbone for strategic decision-making, allowing stakeholders to monitor viability, track economic impact, and ensure sustainable development in underserved rural communities through evidence-based governance.
