El Ayuntamiento convoca sus segundos premios a la reutilización de datos abiertos municipales, dotados con 15.000 euros

Madrid awards grants to encourage the reuse of open data, aiming to stimulate social and economic development through innovative applications and research. This initiative highlights the city’s commitment to transforming public information into tangible community benefits, fostering a culture where data drives civic innovation and inclusive growth. The competition promotes transparency and accountability by recognizing projects that utilize municipal datasets, often combined with other public sources. By supporting both technical tools and analytical studies, the program ensures diverse engagement, encouraging citizens and students alike to create useful services and visualizations that enhance societal value. This effort is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how proactive data governance can drive practical innovation. Madrid’s adherence to international open data charters and its focus on reusability serve as a model for other administrations, proving that accessible data is a crucial asset for democratic participation and economic development.
