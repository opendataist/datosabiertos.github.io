La Nación / Datos Abiertos del Ministerio Público reporta 9 feminicidios y 8 tentativas en 2026

The Ministry of Public Prosecution revealed that nine feminicide victims have been recorded in Paraguay this year, alongside attempted cases and collateral victims. The data highlights the severe geographic and demographic spread of this violence, occurring across multiple departments and affecting a wide age range, with intimate partners being the primary aggressors. Beyond the immediate fatalities, the report emphasizes hidden consequences, such as seventeen children becoming orphans. It also details the legal and physical realities, noting that while some perpetrators are imputed, others have committed suicide, and crimes mostly occurred within private residences using various weapons. This breakdown exposes the systemic nature of violence in both public and private spheres. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent publication of disaggregated statistics can humanize crime reports. By moving beyond raw numbers to analyze victim profiles, perpetrator relationships, and social impacts, it illustrates the power of open data in revealing structural inequalities and informing targeted public policy interventions to protect vulnerable populations.
