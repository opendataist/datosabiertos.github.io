España mejora y logra situarse como el quinto país de Europa con los vuelos más puntuales (y las quejas se deben a retrasos de más de 3 horas)

España mejora y logra situarse como el quinto país de Europa con los vuelos más puntuales (y las quejas se deben a retrasos de más de 3 horas)

Spain ranks fourth in Europe for flight punctuality, trailing only Norway, Sweden, and Denmark. Despite a slight improvement compared to the previous year, a significant proportion of flights still arrive late due to airline-related issues or unforeseen circumstances such as adverse weather. This performance gap highlights the potential for systemic improvements in operational efficiency and scheduling to enhance passenger reliability across the national network. The data reveals substantial disparities among individual airports. Bilbao ranks as Europe’s third most punctual airport, whereas major hubs such as Barcelona, Alicante, and Málaga fall significantly lower in the rankings. These variations suggest that infrastructure, management practices, and local operational challenges differ widely, affecting the overall consistency of Spain’s aviation sector and its competitiveness within the European market. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for transparent, standardized datasets on flight performance and passenger compensation. Access to granular, comparable statistics enables better regulatory oversight, empowers consumers to understand their rights under EU regulations, and promotes accountability among airlines. Reliable public data on punctuality and delays is essential for fostering a more equitable and efficient aviation ecosystem.
