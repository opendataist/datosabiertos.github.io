UGT destaca la menor cifra de desempleo en diciembre desde 2007 y aboga por mejoras laborales en 2025

Castilla-La Mancha closed 2024 with historic employment figures, marked by significant drops in unemployment across all provinces and sectors, particularly benefiting women and youth. This positive trend reflects solid growth in job stability and overall social security affiliations. However, the union emphasizes that these encouraging statistics must not overshadow the urgent need to improve labor conditions. There is a strong call to raise the minimum wage, reduce working hours, and increase salaries to ensure that economic growth translates into tangible quality-of-life improvements for workers in 2025. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how publicly accessible labor statistics enable civil society to monitor socioeconomic progress. Transparent data allows unions and citizens to validate official narratives, identify disparities, and advocate for policy changes based on empirical evidence rather than political rhetoric.
