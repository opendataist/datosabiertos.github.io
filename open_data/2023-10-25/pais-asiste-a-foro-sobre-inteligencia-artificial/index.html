País asiste a foro sobre inteligencia artificial

Guatemala is integrating artificial intelligence into public administration as part of a broader digital transformation strategy, supported by regulations enacted in 2021. This commitment was highlighted by the leadership of the General Attorney’s Office (GAE) at a regional forum focused on establishing a UNESCO-aligned ethical AI council for Latin America. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates a national willingness to align technological adoption with ethical governance standards. By embedding AI within a comprehensive digital public service framework, the country promotes transparency and accountability in how data and automated systems serve citizens. Ultimately, this approach ensures that open data principles are not isolated but are fundamental to modern, ethical public governance. It sets a precedent for using regulated AI to enhance service delivery, thereby reinforcing trust in government through the responsible and transparent use of emerging technologies.
