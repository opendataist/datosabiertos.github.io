Procuraduría Agraria debe informar sobre Tren Maya

The INAI ruled that the Agrarian Procuratorate must disclose documents concerning the Tren Maya and its impact on ejidal lands in Yucatán. This decision reinforces the principle that major infrastructure projects cannot be shielded from transparency under blanket national security classifications. By prioritizing public access over vague security reservations, the resolution challenges the systematic withholding of information that hinders democratic oversight. The article highlights the ongoing legal struggle between transparency institutions and government decrees that attempt to classify public works as matters of national security. Despite recent court rulings favoring openness, the government continues to issue new laws restricting access to information. This conflict underscores the fragility of transparency frameworks and the need for robust institutional resistance against arbitrary secrecy measures. This case is crucial for open data, as it establishes that data related to public land use and infrastructure must be accessible. It demonstrates that open data rights supersede administrative secrecy claims, ensuring citizens can monitor state actions. The ruling sets a precedent for requiring agencies to justify withholding specific datasets, thereby strengthening accountability and promoting the availability of critical public information for research and civic engagement.
