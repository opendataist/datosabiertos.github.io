La utilidad de los datos abiertos en la Plataforma Nacional de Transparencia

The National Transparency Platform stands out as a massive database that facilitates access to government information, prioritizing institutional directories and procurement processes. The availability of this data in open formats enables citizens to conduct rigorous accountability exercises, analyzing everything from the structure of government agencies to the management of budgets and social programs, thereby transforming administrative transparency into a tangible tool for social oversight. This ecosystem is underpinned by the national open data policy, which ensures that information is accessible, usable, and shareable without technical or economic barriers. By eliminating reproduction costs and standardizing formats, knowledge is democratized, allowing actors such as journalists, academics, and ordinary citizens to interpret large volumes of information. This technical infrastructure not only fulfills legal obligations but also responds to a social demand for clarity and efficiency in the management of public data. The relevance of this article to the open data movement lies in its practical focus: open data cease to be a passing trend and become facilitators of informed stories and decisions. By promoting openness standards and permissive licenses, society is empowered to detect irregularities and improve living conditions. The Mexican example illustrates how proactive transparency, driven by cross-cutting regulatory frameworks, transforms public information into a common good that strengthens democracy and institutional trust.
