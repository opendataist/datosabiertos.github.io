Inegi: ingreso promedio de mexicanos en 2022, de 63 mil 695 pesos

The article highlights significant regional and gender disparities in Mexican household income and expenditure, underscoring the critical role of high-quality, confidential statistical data in shaping equitable public policy. By revealing that northern states outperform southern regions and that women still earn considerably less than men, INEGI’s data exposes structural inequalities that require targeted legislative intervention. This evidence is vital for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistics empower citizens and legislators to hold institutions accountable. When detailed information on income gaps and spending habits is available, it facilitates informed decision-making, allowing society to evaluate the effectiveness of social programs and advocate for reforms that address systemic biases in wage distribution. Ultimately, the relevance to open data lies in its ability to transform raw numbers into actionable insights for social justice. The publication of such granular data ensures that policymakers can design laws based on reality rather than assumption, fostering a more responsive government. Open access to these metrics is essential for building trust in public institutions and ensuring that the benefits of economic growth are distributed more fairly across all demographics.
