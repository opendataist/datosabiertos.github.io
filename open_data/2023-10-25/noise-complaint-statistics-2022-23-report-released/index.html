Noise Complaint Statistics 2022-23 report released

Open data on Northern Ireland’s noise complaints reveals a significant annual decline in reported incidents and enforcement actions, despite a slight rise in prosecutions. This transparency allows stakeholders to track how local authorities manage public disturbances and allocate resources more effectively. By publishing detailed statistics, the government enables citizens and councils to understand noise patterns and their impact on community well-being. The shift in data helps identify where domestic noise remains a primary concern, guiding policy decisions aimed at reducing environmental stress. Relevant to open data advocates, this report demonstrates how accessible statistics empower the public to hold authorities accountable. It highlights the importance of timely, granular datasets in fostering informed civic engagement and improving the efficiency of municipal services without relying on opaque administrative processes.
