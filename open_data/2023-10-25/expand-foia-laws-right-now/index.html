Expand FOIA laws right now

Governor Gretchen Whitmer has failed to fulfill her campaign promise to expand Michigan’s transparency laws, leaving both the executive and legislative branches exempt from public record requests. This unique status, shared only by Massachusetts, allows the state’s most powerful officials to operate in secrecy, undermining democratic accountability and preventing public scrutiny of how taxpayer money is spent or government power is exercised. This lack of oversight significantly lowers Michigan’s transparency rankings and deprives citizens of insight into critical decision-making processes. By maintaining these exemptions, the government fails to provide the necessary light on administrative actions, which is essential for an informed electorate and honest governance. This issue is crucial to open data because it highlights a fundamental barrier to transparency: legislative inaction on comprehensive access laws. True open data requires not just the availability of records, but the legal mandate to release them across all government entities. Ending these exemptions would empower citizens with the information needed to hold leaders accountable.
