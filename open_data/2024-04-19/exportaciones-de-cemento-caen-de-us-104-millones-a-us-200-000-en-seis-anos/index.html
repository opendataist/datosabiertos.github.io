Exportaciones de cemento caen de $us 10,4 millones a $us 200.000 en seis años

Bolivia’s cement exports have plummeted due to Paraguay’s protectionist measures, which previously absorbed nearly all of its sales. This dramatic shift exposes the vulnerability of relying on a single market and highlights the urgent need for economic diversification to sustain the industry’s growth. The sector now demands government support, particularly through tax incentives, to successfully penetrate alternative regions such as Chile and Peru. Expanding into these new markets is essential not only for maintaining export volumes but also for absorbing the domestic production capacity that currently saturates the local market. This case underscores the importance of transparent, accessible trade data in identifying market shifts and guiding policy. Open data enables stakeholders to quickly recognize dependency risks and formulate strategic responses, demonstrating how timely information facilitates resilient economic planning and informed decision-making in global trade.
