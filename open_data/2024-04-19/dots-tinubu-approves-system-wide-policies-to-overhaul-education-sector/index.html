DOTS: Tinubu Approves System-Wide Policies To Overhaul Education Sector

President Bola Tinubu has approved a comprehensive overhaul of Nigeria’s education sector through the DOTS initiative, aiming to enhance learning, skill development, and academic security. This strategic framework addresses critical gaps by establishing a robust Data Repository, focusing on Out-of-School Children’s education, investing in Teacher Training and Development, and implementing Skill Development initiatives. The approach signifies a shift toward a more systematic and inclusive educational model, designed to meet the demands of the modern global economy while adhering to the Renewed Hope Agenda. The cornerstone of this reform is the creation of a centralized Data Repository that will conduct an extensive census of schools, teachers, and students. By generating coordinated, verifiable, and authentic data, the government aims to eliminate the current lack of information essential for effective planning. This real-time data tracking architecture will allow federal, state, and local authorities to monitor progress, identify dropouts, and understand gender disparities, thereby enabling precise, data-driven interventions for vulnerable groups and those with specific learning needs. This initiative is highly relevant to open data advocates as it establishes a transparent, public-facing dashboard for education metrics. By making granular data on school infrastructure, teacher qualifications, and student enrollment accessible to policymakers and citizens, the policy promotes accountability and evidence-based decision-making. Open data standards can further empower civil society to analyze these datasets, ensuring that the government’s interventions are effective, equitable, and responsive to the actual conditions on the ground, thus fostering a culture of transparency in public service delivery.
