Brora Community Council to submit Freedom of Information Act over money spent on external consultants

The Brora Community Council plans to request Highland Council spending data on external consultants, challenging their necessity. This action highlights how public resource allocation impacts essential infrastructure like road repairs. Such transparency efforts are crucial for open data, enabling citizens to scrutinize public authority expenditures. By accessing detailed financial records, communities can verify if funds effectively serve local needs rather than administrative overhead. Ultimately, this initiative demonstrates how open data empowers civic engagement. It allows residents to hold authorities accountable, ensuring tax money supports tangible community benefits rather than unnecessary external contracts.
