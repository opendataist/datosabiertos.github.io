La economía sumó 228,000 empleos en marzo, más de lo esperado, pero los aranceles de Trump causan temores de recesión

The recent U.S. employment report reveals strong job growth that initially surprised experts, even as it coincided with a slight rise in the unemployment rate. This creates a complex picture of the labor market, demonstrating resilience despite growing fears of an economic recession driven by new trade policies. Federal workforce reductions significantly influenced these figures, while other sectors maintained hiring. However, analysts emphasize that this data represents only a snapshot taken before major tariff announcements. Consequently, the immediate impact of these trade measures on employment remains limited, leaving the long-term economic outlook heavily dependent on ongoing negotiations and potential retaliatory actions from global partners. This article highlights the tension between current statistical indicators and future economic volatility. For open data, it underscores the critical need for transparent, timely, and granular labor statistics. Such data allows researchers and the public to contextualize official reports against real-time geopolitical shifts, ensuring a more accurate understanding of economic health beyond aggregate numbers.
