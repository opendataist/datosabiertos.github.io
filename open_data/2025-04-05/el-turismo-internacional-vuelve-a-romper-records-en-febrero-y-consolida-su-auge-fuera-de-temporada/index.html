El turismo internacional vuelve a romper récords en febrero y consolida su auge fuera de temporada

El turismo internacional vuelve a romper récords en febrero y consolida su auge fuera de temporada

Spain’s international tourism sector has shattered historical records, with February 2025 marking the highest spending ever recorded for the month. This surge demonstrates a significant economic recovery and a structural shift away from pandemic-era lows, highlighting the resilience and sustained growth of the national tourism industry beyond traditional peak seasons. The data reveals a changing traveler profile characterized by shorter stays but substantially higher daily spending. This trend reflects the rise of intense short breaks driven by flexible work arrangements and diversified tourism products. Consequently, the industry is experiencing marked de-seasonalization, with winter months now contributing significantly to revenue, thereby stabilizing employment and opening avenues for cultural and urban tourism experiences. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on the INE’s Open Data initiatives, specifically the EGATUR survey, to make these insights accessible. Transparent, publicly available statistical data allows for independent analysis of economic trends, enabling stakeholders to understand sector dynamics, monitor regional competitiveness, and assess the broader impacts of global uncertainties on Spain’s vital tourism economy.
