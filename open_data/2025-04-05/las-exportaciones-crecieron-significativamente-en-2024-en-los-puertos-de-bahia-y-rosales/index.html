Las exportaciones crecieron significativamente en 2024 en los puertos de Bahía y Rosales

Bahía Blanca and Puerto Rosales showed significant growth in freight movement during 2024, driven by substantial increases in grain, oil, and byproduct exports. This surge highlights the region's expanding role in Argentina’s agro-industrial supply chain, reinforcing its strategic importance for domestic logistics and international trade connectivity. The data reveals that while overall port activity increased, specific commodity shifts occurred, with chemical exports facing minor declines despite isolated surges in crude oil volumes. These contrasting trends suggest evolving market dynamics and production demands within the local economic ecosystem, requiring adaptive management strategies for port operators and stakeholders. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it validates the utility of transparent, granular port statistics. Making such detailed, time-series trade data publicly accessible enables researchers, policymakers, and businesses to analyze supply chain resilience, identify regional economic strengths, and support evidence-based decision-making for future infrastructure and commercial investments.
