La producción industrial suma dos meses en negativo tras bajar un 2,4% en febrero

Spain’s industrial production has contracted for two consecutive months, marking its sharpest decline since last November. While energy remains the only growing sector, equipment goods experienced the steepest drops, reflecting a broader slowdown that is now affecting manufacturing nationwide, despite underlying economic resilience. Analysts note that this downturn is impacting Spain similarly to its northern Eurozone peers, particularly in the automotive sector. However, the national economy remains relatively less exposed to external shocks, such as US tariffs, compared to other European countries. This moderation signals a shift from previous growth patterns, underscoring the need for careful monitoring of future economic indicators. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent and timely statistical releases enable researchers and policymakers to accurately track economic shifts. Access to such detailed industrial metrics facilitates better decision-making, supports academic analysis, and ensures public accountability regarding national economic performance and strategic planning.
