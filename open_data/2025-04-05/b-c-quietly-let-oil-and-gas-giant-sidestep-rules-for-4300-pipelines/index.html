B.C. Quietly Let Oil And Gas Giant Sidestep Rules For 4,300+ Pipelines

B.C. Quietly Let Oil And Gas Giant Sidestep Rules For 4,300+ Pipelines

The BC Energy Regulator granted exemptions to a major oil and gas company without public notification, allowing thousands of decommissioning deadlines to be missed. This lack of transparency raises concerns that regulators are unfairly favoring fossil fuel interests over environmental protection. This incident highlights critical flaws in how open data and regulatory decisions are handled. The discovery relied entirely on freedom of information requests, demonstrating that essential oversight information is not proactively disclosed. Relevant to open data, this case underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible public records in resource regulation. Without mandatory proactive disclosure, citizens and watchdogs cannot effectively monitor compliance or hold powerful entities accountable, undermining democratic oversight.
