Comenzó la Diplomatura en Fasta con participación de alumnos de varios países

Comenzó la Diplomatura en Fasta con participación de alumnos de varios países

FASTA University has launched a comprehensive diploma program in intellectual property, covering emerging technologies, artificial intelligence, and copyright. This educational program, with an Ibero-American reach, responds to the urgent need to update legal frameworks in light of rapid digital evolution and the creative industries. By including students from multiple Latin American and Spanish countries, the initiative demonstrates a global scope that transcends local boundaries, establishing the institution as a regional reference in specialized training. The intensive curriculum is designed to equip professionals with practical tools to protect intangible assets and foster business innovation. The teaching combines rigorous theory with practical experiences delivered by leading experts, ensuring that participants understand how to add value through legal protection. This applied approach is crucial for actors in the creative and technology sectors to navigate confidently an environment where constant innovation demands agile and precise legal responses. This offering is relevant to the open data movement because effective management of intellectual property is the necessary counterbalance to democratizing access to knowledge. Understanding the legal limits of copyright and patents enables the creation of ecosystems where information can be shared responsibly without infringing on copyright. By training leaders who understand these complexities, a healthy balance is fostered that protects creators while enabling business models based on transparency and open collaboration, which are essential for the sustainable expansion of information sciences and free culture.
