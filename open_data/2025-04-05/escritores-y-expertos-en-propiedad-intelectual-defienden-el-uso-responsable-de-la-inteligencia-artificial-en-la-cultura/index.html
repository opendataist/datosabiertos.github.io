Escritores y expertos en propiedad intelectual defienden el uso responsable de la Inteligencia Artificial en la cultura

Escritores y expertos en propiedad intelectual defienden el uso responsable de la Inteligencia Artificial en la cultura

The report highlights a critical inflection point where generative AI is rapidly transforming cultural and educational landscapes, yet formal regulatory frameworks lag significantly behind. With widespread adoption in schools and the exponential rise of AI-generated publications, there is an urgent need for standardized protocols. This gap between technological penetration and institutional oversight creates significant ethical challenges, particularly regarding data sourcing and the democratization of creative tools without adequate safeguards. A central concern is the protection of intellectual property and the rights of human creators. The extensive use of authors' works to train AI models without explicit consent threatens the foundational principles of copyright law. Experts emphasize the necessity of distinguishing between genuine human artistic creation and algorithmic outputs, advocating for legal frameworks that mandate authorization for data usage. This distinction is crucial to preserve the value of human creativity and ensure that creators retain control over how their work is utilized and monetized. This study is highly relevant to the open data community because it underscores the ethical implications of using copyrighted content for model training. It challenges the assumption that open access or scraping data for AI development is inherently neutral, highlighting instead the need for consent-based mechanisms. The call for regulatory clarity serves as a reminder that while data availability drives innovation, responsible AI development must respect original creators' rights, promoting a sustainable ecosystem where technology serves culture rather than exploiting it.
