Construction rose 5.1% in September | El Dia

Construction rose 5.1% in September | El Dia

The construction sector is showing a significant recovery driven by positive data, underscoring the importance of having quick and transparent access to national economic indicators. This type of information, derived from official statistics, is essential for society and the private sector to make informed decisions based on the actual state of the market. However, caution prevails among business leaders, who do not expect sustained growth in the short term. This discrepancy between current macroeconomic data and future expectations highlights the need to analyze time series in depth to understand the true dynamics of the sector. The relevance of open data lies in the public’s ability to cross-reference this information with other variables, such as industrial performance, to obtain a comprehensive view of the economy. The transparency of these indicators allows for the evaluation of public policies and a better understanding of the socioeconomic context, empowering citizens with verifiable and accessible knowledge.
