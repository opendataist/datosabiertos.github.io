The article demonstrates that combining Google Sheets with generative AI creates a powerful, accessible workflow for web scraping, democratizing data extraction for non-programmers. By leveraging the IMPORTXML function alongside ChatGPT, users can generate complex formulas through natural language prompts rather than writing code. This approach significantly lowers the technical barrier, allowing professionals to perform advanced SEO tasks like competitor analysis and site auditing without needing specialized programming skills. However, the study reveals that relying solely on AI chatbots for direct data extraction has limitations. Attempts to have ChatGPT scrape complex, extensive web pages directly often resulted in incomplete data or inaccuracies, highlighting the tool's constraints in handling large-scale structural data in real-time. The chatbot excelled at coding but struggled with the heavy lifting of parsing large amounts of HTML content efficiently within a single session, proving that AI is best used as a facilitator rather than a standalone solution for this specific task. For open data enthusiasts, this hybrid method offers a scalable and cost-effective strategy to aggregate public web information. The ability to rapidly scrape hundreds of records using free tools underscores the potential for citizen scientists and independent researchers to gather large datasets without expensive software. Ultimately, the article emphasizes that integrating specialized low-code tools with AI assistance enables efficient, reproducible workflows, expanding who can participate in the open data ecosystem by making data collection simpler and more sustainable.
