Trump's inflationary policies were almost totally ignored in the top 5 US newspapers' early 2024 inflation coverage

This study analyzes recent mainstream media coverage to assess how inflation narratives intersect with political rhetoric. It specifically examines whether reporting links proposed policies to economic outcomes or prioritizes current positive indicators over partisan attacks. The research focuses on how major newspapers frame inflation discussions, distinguishing between objective economic data and political criticism. By filtering out opinion pieces, the analysis isolates factual reporting trends in business and news sections. This is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates rigorous methodology for auditing media datasets. It shows how precise keyword selection and strict inclusion criteria enable reproducible research into information ecosystems, a core practice for ensuring transparency and accountability in public data analysis.
