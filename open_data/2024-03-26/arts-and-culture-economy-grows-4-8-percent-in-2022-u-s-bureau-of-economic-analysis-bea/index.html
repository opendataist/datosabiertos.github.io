Arts and Culture Economy Grows 4.8 Percent in 2022 | U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA)

The arts and cultural sector demonstrated robust economic resilience in 2022, significantly outpacing the broader national economy in growth. Core industries, particularly performing arts and design, experienced substantial expansion, suggesting a strong recovery and increasing societal valuation of creative sectors. This performance highlights the sector’s dynamic nature and its capacity to rebound swiftly after previous declines. Geographically, economic activity in the arts was widespread, with value added increasing in every state. However, the sector’s contribution to local GDP varies considerably, ranging from a modest share in some regions to a dominant economic force in others. This disparity indicates that while the arts are a universal presence, their economic footprint is heavily concentrated in specific cultural hubs, reflecting diverse regional priorities and infrastructures. This report is crucial for open data initiatives as it illustrates the power of satellite accounts to provide granular insights beyond standard GDP metrics. By integrating detailed industry-specific statistics, these accounts offer policymakers and researchers a clearer picture of niche economic contributions. Such transparency supports evidence-based decision-making and fosters a deeper understanding of how cultural capital translates into tangible economic value across different jurisdictions.
