En mayo se agota el Presupuesto prorrogado del 2023 y Milei apelará a las facultades extraordinarias

The report analyzes how the extension of Argentina’s 2023 budget grants the executive branch increasing discretion over fiscal resources. In the absence of a new legislative proposal, the Chief of Cabinet can allocate collected funds that fall outside traditional budgetary control starting in June. This situation has sparked intense political confrontation with Congress, as the current framework allows for spending adjustments without legislative approval, centralizing decisions that previously required parliamentary consensus. The relevance to open data lies in the opacity and fragmentation of budgetary information. When expenditures are carried out through decrees or using funds outside the official budget, it becomes difficult to transparently track the use of public resources. The lack of an approved legislative proposal means that citizens and experts have no detailed, public plan to compare against, weakening civic audit capacity and oversight of state spending efficiency—key elements for fiscal transparency. Finally, the economic consultation warns that this adjustment model is unsustainable in the long term. Relying on drastic cuts amid a recession and low revenue collection could quickly erode the primary surplus. The main criticism points out that eliminating tax exemptions, initially proposed as an alternative, might have offered a more balanced path. However, the current decision prioritizes executive discretion over fiscal predictability, increasing economic uncertainty and reducing clarity in public accountability.
