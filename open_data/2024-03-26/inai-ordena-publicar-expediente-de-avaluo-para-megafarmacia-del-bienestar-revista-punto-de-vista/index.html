INAI ordena publicar expediente de avalúo para megafarmacia del Bienestar | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI ordered INDAABIN to publish the appraisal file for the “megapharmacy” warehouses, citing the government’s failure to adequately provide the requested information. This ruling enforces transparency, enabling citizens to evaluate the efficiency and appropriateness of public resource allocation for this specific infrastructure project. This case highlights the critical role of open data in democratic accountability. By ensuring that details on public spending and asset valuation are accessible, it empowers society to verify administrative decisions. Such transparency is essential for maintaining trust in how the state manages financial resources and implements social programs. The relevance to open data lies in establishing that public information regarding government contracts and evaluations must be proactively made available. It reinforces the principle that, without accessible, granular data on public assets, citizens cannot effectively exercise oversight. Thus, open data serves as a fundamental tool for preventing opacity and ensuring responsible governance in large-scale state initiatives.
