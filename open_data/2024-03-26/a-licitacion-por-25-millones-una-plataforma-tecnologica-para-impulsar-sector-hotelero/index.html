A licitación, por 2,5 millones, una plataforma tecnológica para impulsar sector hotelero

A licitación, por 2,5 millones, una plataforma tecnológica para impulsar sector hotelero

Castilla-La Mancha is launching an AI-driven platform for its hotel sector, creating an open data environment that enables advanced market analysis and decision-making. This initiative aims to optimize resource allocation and pricing, fostering innovation and technology transfer to enhance competitiveness in the region’s strategic tourism industry. The project facilitates the delivery of personalized services to clients, significantly improving guest satisfaction by adapting offerings to individual needs through intelligent data usage. By leveraging these advanced analytical techniques, hotels can refine their strategies and better respond to both domestic and international market demands. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it establishes a structured, data-centric ecosystem for a major economic sector. It demonstrates how governmental investment in open technological infrastructure can drive public-private synergy, turning raw data into actionable insights that benefit businesses and improve regional economic performance.
