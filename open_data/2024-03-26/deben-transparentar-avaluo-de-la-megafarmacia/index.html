Deben transparentar avalúo de la megafarmacia

Deben transparentar avalúo de la megafarmacia

The INAI ordered Indaabin to release the valuation records for the Bienestar Megapharmacy facility, emphasizing that transparency is essential for citizens to monitor public funds allocated to health rights. This access enables society to assess whether substantial financial investments are effectively achieving their intended objectives, despite reported shortcomings in performance. The case illustrates the practical application of access to information laws, facilitating accountability for strategic government projects. By obtaining these documents, the public can verify whether resources correspond to actual outcomes, underscoring the importance of open data in maintaining trust and oversight in public spending. This ruling is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how mandatory disclosure mechanisms operate to reveal institutional responses and gaps. It highlights the critical link between accessible public records and democratic oversight, showing that without such data, evaluating the effectiveness of health infrastructure investments remains opaque and challenging for civil society.
