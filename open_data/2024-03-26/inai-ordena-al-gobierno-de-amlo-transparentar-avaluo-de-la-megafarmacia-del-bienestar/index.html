INAI ordena al gobierno de AMLO transparentar avalúo de la Megafarmacia del Bienestar

Transparency in the management of public resources is essential to ensure citizen trust and the proper implementation of government policies. In this context, the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) ordered the Mexican government to submit the appraisal file for a property acquired for the Megafarmacia del Bienestar program. This decision underscores the need to verify that the acquisition of major assets is carried out under objective technical criteria, preventing discretion from concealing possible overvaluations or irregularities in public spending. In addition to the investigation into the real estate property, the INAI required the Ministry of Health to disclose the progress of contracts for the purchase of medicines. This directive responds to social concerns about shortages of supplies in the health system, demonstrating that accountability is essential for monitoring the flow of resources allocated to essential goods. By compelling the publication of this information, it is ensured that society can assess the effectiveness of government initiatives and identify potential failures in the supply chain. This case is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it illustrates how citizen pressure, backed by legal transparency frameworks, can compel institutions to make public information that would otherwise remain hidden. The disclosure of these files not only allows for external scrutiny but also sets a precedent where the availability of specific data on purchases and valuations becomes an indispensable requirement for democratic oversight and the prevention of corruption.
