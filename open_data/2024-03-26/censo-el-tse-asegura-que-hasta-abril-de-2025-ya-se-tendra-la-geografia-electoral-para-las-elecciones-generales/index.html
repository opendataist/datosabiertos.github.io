Censo: el TSE asegura que hasta abril de 2025 ya se tendrá la "geografía electoral" para las elecciones generales

The article outlines the critical timeline for Bolivia’s 2025 general elections, emphasizing the National Institute of Statistics’ deadline to deliver census data by December. This data is essential for the Supreme Electoral Tribunal to finalize the electoral geography, a prerequisite for legally redistributing parliamentary seats and redrawing district boundaries based on population shifts. The process hinges on accurate, georeferenced demographic information to ensure constitutional compliance and representational fairness. This reliance on precise demographic mapping highlights the vital role of open data in democratic processes. Transparent, accessible, and granular statistical data enables electoral authorities to perform technical delimitations objectively. When such data is available and standardized, it supports the legal framework required to adjust political representation, ensuring that electoral districts reflect actual population distributions rather than arbitrary assumptions. The narrative underscores that open data is not merely an administrative formality but a cornerstone of electoral integrity. By providing detailed, localized statistics, government agencies facilitate a fair redistribution of power. This example illustrates how accessible, high-quality data infrastructure directly impacts the legitimacy of democratic institutions, allowing citizens and regulators to verify that electoral maps are derived from evidence-based, publicly available information rather than opaque bureaucratic decisions.
