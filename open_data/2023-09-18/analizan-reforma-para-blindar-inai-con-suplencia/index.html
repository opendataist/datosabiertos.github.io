Analizan reforma para blindar INAI con suplencia

Analizan reforma para blindar INAI con suplencia

A proposed legislative reform aims to ensure the continuous operation of the National Institute for Transparency by establishing a substitution mechanism for commissioner vacancies. This measure designates the senior secretary to temporarily fill these roles, preventing operational gaps that could hinder public access to information. Such structural stability is vital for maintaining institutional integrity and guaranteeing consistent oversight. By avoiding delays in appointments, the system upholds accountability and ensures that transparency mandates are enforced without interruption. This development highlights the critical importance of robust legal frameworks in supporting open data ecosystems. Effective governance requires uninterrupted institutional capacity to protect citizens' rights and promote the free flow of public information, reinforcing the broader goals of transparency and democratic accountability.
