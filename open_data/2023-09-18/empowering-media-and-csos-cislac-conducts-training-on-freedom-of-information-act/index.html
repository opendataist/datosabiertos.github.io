Empowering Media and CSOs: CISLAC Conducts Training on Freedom of Information Act

CISLAC organized a training for media and civil society organizations to enhance their capacity to utilize the Freedom of Information Act. This initiative aims to strengthen democratic accountability by equipping participants with the skills to access government information, thereby promoting transparency and reducing corruption in Nigeria. The sessions highlighted the Act’s role in ensuring political integrity and inclusive decision-making. By fostering public scrutiny and institutional openness, the training underscores how informed citizens can challenge undue influence and demand that political power serves the public interest effectively. This content is relevant to open data because it demonstrates active civil society engagement in securing public data access. By empowering journalists and advocates to request and share information, the event supports a culture where government transparency is not just a legal right but a practical tool for accountability and social development.
