Contraloría detecta varias falencias en la realización del Censo 2022 - Política - ABC Color

The General Comptroller of the Republic (CGR) has identified significant irregularities in the execution of the 2022 National Census by Paraguay’s National Institute of Statistics (INE). Despite funding from the Inter-American Development Bank (IDB) aimed at strengthening the national statistical system, the audit revealed serious procedural failures, including improper expenditure classifications and a lack of adequate controls over field operations. Specific deficiencies involved census supervisors failing to submit required reports and inconsistent data recording, which undermined the reliability and integrity of the entire census process. Further critical findings highlight mismanagement of resources and potential fraud mechanisms. The INE acquired substantial computing equipment intended to enhance digital security and efficiency for the census; however, a significant portion of these devices remained unassigned and unused, representing a considerable financial loss. Additionally, the audit exposed severe flaws in the payment system for census staff, where shared contact information for dozens of employees made it nearly impossible to verify individual payments. These lapses in planning, asset management, and financial oversight suggest systemic weaknesses in how public funds were managed during this crucial statistical exercise. This report is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparency, accountability, and methodological rigor in the production of official statistics. When government agencies face credible allegations of mismanagement and procedural errors, public trust in the resulting data erodes, making it difficult to rely on census figures for policy-making or social planning. The controversy highlights that accurate open data is not just about releasing numbers, but ensuring they are generated through verifiable, robust, and transparent processes to maintain their validity and utility for society.
