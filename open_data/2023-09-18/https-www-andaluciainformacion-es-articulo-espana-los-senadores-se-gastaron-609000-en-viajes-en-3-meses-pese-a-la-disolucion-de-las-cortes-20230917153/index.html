The Senate’s transparency portal reveals significant spending on parliamentary travel, even during periods of reduced legislative activity. This information underscores the importance of public accountability by detailing the costs associated with representatives’ mobility, ranging from public transportation to taxi cards and allowances for the use of personal vehicles. The automatic publication of this financial data facilitates citizen scrutiny over the use of public funds. It enables civil society to analyze the efficiency and legitimacy of official expenditures, demonstrating how proactive transparency empowers taxpayers to monitor their leaders’ conduct without having to file specific requests. This case illustrates the crucial relevance of open data in democracy. By making detailed budget information accessible, it fosters institutional integrity and sets a precedent for other public entities to follow, ensuring that resources are used appropriately in the public interest rather than to conceal privileges.
