After CIC Rap, Govt Orders Submission Of Annual Returns Under RTI Sans Lapses - Kashmir Reader

The government emphasizes the critical role of transparency in sustaining democratic values, framing the Right to Information Act as a vital tool for citizen empowerment and anti-corruption efforts. By treating information as the currency of democracy, authorities aim to establish a robust monitoring mechanism where public entities are held accountable through rigorous oversight by the Central Information Commission. This perspective underscores that access to information is not merely a procedural formality but a foundational element of an open and responsible governance system. A central focus of this directive is the mandatory submission of annual and quarterly returns by all public authorities. These reports provide essential data on request volumes, appeal outcomes, and disciplinary actions, which the Commission uses to assess performance and recommend reforms. The failure to submit these returns disrupts the ability to evaluate how effectively government bodies are implementing the spirit of the law, thereby hindering efforts to modernize administration and ensure consistent adherence to transparency standards. This issue is directly relevant to open_data initiatives because it highlights the necessity of standardized, regular data reporting for effective public accountability. The strict enforcement of return submissions ensures that the metrics needed to analyze government responsiveness are actually available and accurate. Without consistent data flow, the transparency promised by open governance frameworks remains incomplete, making compliance with such reporting requirements essential for validating the health of democratic institutions and enabling meaningful public scrutiny.
