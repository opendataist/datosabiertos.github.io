Bangladesh's average life expectancy rises to 72.4 years

Bangladesh’s recent vital statistics reveal a mixed health landscape, with life expectancy rising while infant mortality increased. This divergence highlights how the pandemic reversed decades of progress in child survival, underscoring the critical need for robust, accessible public health data. For open data initiatives, this case illustrates the importance of transparent, high-frequency demographic reporting. It demonstrates how timely statistical releases allow stakeholders to identify emerging crises, such as rising mortality, ensuring that policy interventions are based on current realities rather than outdated trends. Consequently, maintaining open access to these datasets is essential for accountability and effective resource allocation. By making such detailed health indicators publicly available, governments and organizations can better track social determinants of health and respond swiftly to threats affecting vulnerable populations.
