Wee Ginger Dug: Eyebrows will be raised at huge resources used in SNP police probe

The article argues that Police Scotland has allocated disproportionate resources to an investigation into SNP finances, spending over the alleged missing amount with no resulting charges. This highlights a critical issue for open data advocates: the necessity for transparency in how public funds are utilized for law enforcement priorities. Without detailed, accessible reporting on the cost-benefit analysis of such high-profile inquiries, citizens cannot effectively hold authorities accountable for inefficient use of taxpayer money or potential political bias in policing. Furthermore, the text condemns the UK Government’s increasing disregard for the Sewel Convention, using Brexit to legislate on devolved matters without consent. This erosion of the devolution settlement poses a significant threat to democratic integrity. For open data initiatives, this underscores the importance of monitoring legislative processes to ensure that decision-making remains transparent and respects local autonomy, preventing the centralization of power from bypassing established democratic safeguards and public scrutiny. Finally, the piece criticizes the UK Prime Minister for avoiding substantive questions during parliamentary sessions in favor of personal attacks. This lack of accountability in political discourse reinforces the need for rigorous public record-keeping. Open data principles are essential here to document these interactions, ensuring that political behavior is visible and subject to public review, thereby promoting a healthier democratic environment where leaders must answer to the electorate rather than evade difficult issues.
