Incendios forestales en Canadá generaron un récord de emisiones de carbono - Ensegundos.do

Canada’s wildfires have produced unprecedented carbon emissions, far surpassing previous records, driven by intensifying climate change and extreme weather events. This surge underscores the urgent feedback loop between global warming and the increased frequency of fires, posing severe environmental risks. The data highlight the critical need for transparent, real-time monitoring systems. Open-data initiatives such as Copernicus are essential for tracking these crises, ensuring accountability, and informing effective policy responses to mitigate ecological damage.
