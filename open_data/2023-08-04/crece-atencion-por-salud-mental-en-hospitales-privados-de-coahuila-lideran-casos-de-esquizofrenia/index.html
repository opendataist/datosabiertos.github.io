Crece atención por salud mental en hospitales privados de Coahuila; lideran casos de esquizofrenia

The article highlights a significant rise in mental health disorders and substance abuse in Coahuila, noting that inpatient care demands increased substantially compared to the previous year. This surge underscores critical gaps in infrastructure, as the region severely lacks the specialized personnel recommended by the World Health Organization to handle such caseloads. Data transparency is crucial here, as tracking these trends exposes systemic failures. The disparity between rising case volumes and insufficient staffing illustrates the urgent need for open data initiatives to monitor healthcare resource allocation accurately. Relevant to open data, these statistics emphasize the importance of accessible, real-time health metrics. By making such data available, policymakers and researchers can better identify resource shortages, advocate for adequate staffing levels, and improve public health responses to growing mental health crises.
