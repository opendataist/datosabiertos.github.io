Alerta en Perú: Niveles de pobreza extrema podrían superar los registros pandémicos según INEI

The Peruvian government suspended the release of 2023 poverty data due to politically sensitive results that revealed critical economic deterioration. This decision highlights how official statistics can be manipulated, undermining their usefulness for accurate societal analysis. Such opacity threatens institutional credibility and public trust, demonstrating the vital role of independent data verification. It serves as a warning that political interference compromises the integrity of national indicators. This incident is highly relevant to open data principles, as it underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and unaltered information. Without freedom from political pressure, statistical institutions cannot provide the reliable foundation needed for evidence-based decision-making and accountability.
