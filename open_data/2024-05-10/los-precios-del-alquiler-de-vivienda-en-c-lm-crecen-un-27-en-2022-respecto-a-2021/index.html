Los precios del alquiler de vivienda en C-LM crecen un 2,7% en 2022 respecto a 2021

The Spanish rental market saw its second-highest annual price increase since 2012 in 2022, primarily driven by a sharp rise in new contracts, while existing agreements remained relatively stable. This disparity underscores how market dynamics affect newcomers versus incumbents, revealing regional differences where coastal areas experienced steeper price hikes than inland provinces. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, granular insights into housing affordability trends across Spain. By making such detailed indices publicly available, policymakers and researchers can better understand spatial inequalities and the impact of regulatory measures on different segments of the rental market, thereby fostering informed public discourse.
