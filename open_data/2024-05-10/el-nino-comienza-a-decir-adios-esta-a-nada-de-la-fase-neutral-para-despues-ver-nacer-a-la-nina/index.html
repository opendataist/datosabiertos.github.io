¡El Niño comienza a decir adiós! Está a nada de la fase neutral, para después, ver nacer a La Niña

The NOAA report signals the imminent end of the El Niño phenomenon, forecasting a shift toward neutral ENSO conditions. This transition is driven by declining sea surface temperatures in key Pacific regions, marking a significant climatological turning point. Understanding these shifts is crucial for open data initiatives, as they demonstrate the value of accessible, real-time environmental monitoring for global communities. Following the neutral phase, La Niña is projected to develop, with increasing confidence as the summer progresses. This sequence highlights the cyclical nature of ocean-atmosphere interactions, offering predictable patterns that data scientists can model. Transparent access to such meteorological forecasts enables better preparedness and resource allocation across vulnerable sectors worldwide. This update underscores the critical role of open climate data in enhancing societal resilience. By disseminating accurate forecasts from authoritative institutions like NOAA, open data platforms empower users to make informed decisions regarding agriculture, disaster management, and economic planning. The availability of these insights fosters global cooperation and scientific accuracy.
