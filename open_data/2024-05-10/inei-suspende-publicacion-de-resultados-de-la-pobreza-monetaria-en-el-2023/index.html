INEI suspende publicación de resultados de la Pobreza Monetaria en el 2023

The National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) postponed the release of 2023 poverty figures due to unforeseen circumstances, with estimates suggesting that poverty may have increased or remained at pandemic-era levels. This delay undermines transparency regarding economic hardship. Data accuracy is crucial for open data initiatives, as timely access enables researchers and citizens to hold institutions accountable. Withholding such critical statistics hinders public scrutiny and policy analysis. Open data standards require proactive disclosure to ensure informed societal decision-making and maintain trust in official statistics.
