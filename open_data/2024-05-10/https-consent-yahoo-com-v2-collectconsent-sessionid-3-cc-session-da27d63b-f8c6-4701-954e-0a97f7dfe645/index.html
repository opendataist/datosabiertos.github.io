Open Data
ES EN

Cookie consent banners often obscure the core reality of open data ecosystems: pervasive data extraction. This article highlights the tension between user privacy controls and the widespread harvesting of personal information by tech partners. Such opaque practices complicate efforts to create truly open, ethically sourced datasets. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for advocates seeking transparency in how individual data fuels algorithmic systems. Relevance lies in exposing the hidden infrastructure behind digital services. By scrutinizing consent mechanisms, we can better advocate for data practices that respect user autonomy while fostering genuine openness and accountability in information flows.
