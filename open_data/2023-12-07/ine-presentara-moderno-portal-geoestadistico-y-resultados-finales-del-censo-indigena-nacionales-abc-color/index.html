INE presentará moderno Portal Geoestadístico y resultados finales del Censo Indígena - Nacionales - ABC Color

Paraguay has advanced its National Statistical System by launching an open data geo-portal and publishing census results, transforming official statistics into accessible, downloadable formats. This initiative democratizes information, empowering diverse stakeholders to analyze demographic trends through interactive maps and tables. These tools enable evidence-based decision-making for both the public and private sectors, allowing for more efficient and targeted monitoring of public policies. By making complex data readily available, the platform supports researchers, academics, and government bodies in addressing specific territorial needs. This development is crucial for open data, as it shifts statistics from static reports to dynamic, reusable resources. It fosters transparency and accountability by ensuring that high-quality data is not only collected but actively utilized to drive sustainable development and informed governance across the nation.
