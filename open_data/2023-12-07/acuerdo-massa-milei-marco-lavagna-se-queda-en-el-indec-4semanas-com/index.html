The article confirms Marco Lavagna’s continued leadership at INDEC, the national statistics institute. This decision underscores the critical role of official data sources in ensuring public transparency and accountability. The relevance of these changes lies in how institutional stability impacts data accessibility and quality. Reliable, independent censuses form the foundation for evidence-based policymaking. Understanding these governance reforms is essential for developers and citizens who depend on accurate, unbiased public information for research and civic engagement.
