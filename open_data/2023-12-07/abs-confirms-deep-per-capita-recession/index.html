ABS confirms deep per capita recession

Australia’s economy is in a deep per capita recession, driven by collapsed household consumption rather than robust private sector activity. This weak performance highlights the critical need for high-quality economic data to accurately reflect living standards and inform monetary policy decisions. The ABS’s release provides essential visibility into these distressing trends, such as plummeting savings rates and stagnant real wages. Without transparent, timely public data, policymakers and citizens would lack the necessary insights to understand the true severity of the cost-of-living crisis. Reliable open data empowers citizens to hold institutions accountable for economic mismanagement. By making detailed national accounts accessible, the ABS enables better analysis of structural issues, fostering a more informed public discourse on fiscal and monetary strategies needed to revive genuine economic health.
