El IPC de Ecuador registró una variación de -0,40% en noviembre de 2023

Ecuador experienced a deceleration in its inflation rate during November 2023, registering a slight monthly contraction that adds to an accumulated and annual trend of moderation. This general decline in prices reflects an economic environment where inflationary pressure has significantly receded compared to the levels observed the previous year. A detailed analysis reveals that the reduction in costs was primarily driven by food and beverages, miscellaneous goods, and clothing. The dynamics were heterogeneous across regions and categories; while tradable goods showed a pronounced drop in prices, services remained stable. Moreover, there are notable differences between the Coast and the Sierra, highlighting that climatic phenomena and seasonality affect the country’s different zones unevenly. This report is crucial for the open data community because it validates the importance of transparent access to official macroeconomic data. The publication of indicators broken down by consumption category, type of good, and geography enables researchers and citizens to cross-reference information with other open sources. This fosters fiscal transparency, facilitates social auditing, and enables the development of predictive models based on verifiable public evidence.
