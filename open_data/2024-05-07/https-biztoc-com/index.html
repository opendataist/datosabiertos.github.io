In the realm of artificial intelligence, significant security and regulatory challenges are emerging. Incidents involving leaked user data from autonomous AI agents underscore urgent privacy risks, while high-profile corporate hacks reveal the fragility of crypto infrastructure. These developments are crucial to the open data community because they highlight the tension between technological advancement and data sovereignty.
