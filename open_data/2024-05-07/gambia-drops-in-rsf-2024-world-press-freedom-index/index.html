Gambia drops in RSF 2024 World Press Freedom Index

The Gambia’s press freedom ranking reflects ongoing challenges, primarily stemming from government payment delays that disrupt media operations and the persistence of restrictive laws from the previous regime. Despite these setbacks, significant progress remains evident, highlighted by historic justice for the assassination of a veteran journalist, signaling a crucial step away from past abuses. This case underscores the critical importance of open data and transparency in monitoring media landscapes, as independent reporting and access to information are fundamental to holding power accountable and sustaining democratic freedoms globally.
