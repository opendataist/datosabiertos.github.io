La economía rusa vuelve a crecer después de cuatro trimestres de contracción

Russia’s economy showed unexpected growth in mid-2023, breaking a contraction streak caused by sanctions. This recovery highlights the complex resilience of state-managed economies under geopolitical pressure. However, the nation still has not returned to pre-conflict economic levels. Persistent inflation and currency devaluation indicate that the expansion remains fragile despite the headline positive figures. This data is relevant to open_data as it showcases how statistical agencies provide essential transparency on national performance. Access to such public datasets allows researchers to analyze the real-world impact of international policy measures on specific markets.
