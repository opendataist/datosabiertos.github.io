Una generación que no puede hacer su vida

High youth unemployment and stagnant wages leave young people unable to achieve financial independence, despite recent increases in the minimum wage. The main obstacle remains the exorbitant cost of housing, which forces many to delay starting families or continue living with their parents, severely hindering societal development. This crisis is relevant to open data because transparent, accessible datasets on housing prices, salaries, and employment are essential for diagnosing the root causes of inequality. Open data allows researchers and policymakers to analyze these systemic failures and propose effective, evidence-based solutions to the economic stagnation described.
