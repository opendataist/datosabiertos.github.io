Many individuals remain on learner permits for over sixteen years, undermining the licensing system’s integrity. The Road Safety Authority views this as a significant safety concern, prompting calls for stricter disincentives to prevent indefinite permit rolling. This situation highlights critical gaps in enforcing driver competency standards and managing license transitions effectively. Such prolonged dependence on learner status exposes systemic flaws in how driving regulations are monitored and applied. Without effective measures to compel candidates to pursue full licensure, road safety standards may be compromised. The authority’s push for policy changes reflects an urgent need to align administrative practices with public safety expectations. For open data enthusiasts, these figures demonstrate the value of transparency in holding institutions accountable. Revealing such statistics encourages public scrutiny and drives demand for better data governance. It underscores how accessible, accurate information can facilitate meaningful debates and policy improvements in public service delivery.
