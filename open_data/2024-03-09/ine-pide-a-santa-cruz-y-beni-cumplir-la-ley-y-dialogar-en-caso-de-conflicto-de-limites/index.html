INE pide a Santa Cruz y Beni cumplir la ley y dialogar en caso de conflicto de límites

INE pide a Santa Cruz y Beni cumplir la ley y dialogar en caso de conflicto de límites

The National Statistics Institute urges Santa Cruz and Beni to resolve their territorial dispute through dialogue and legal frameworks rather than political pressure. This approach ensures the integrity of the upcoming census, preventing administrative disruptions that could hinder data collection and harm affected populations who rely on accurate representation. Crucially, the statistics office clarifies that it lacks the authority to alter political boundaries, emphasizing that mapping changes do not constitute a solution for long-standing legal conflicts. This distinction preserves the technical neutrality of statistical processes, ensuring that demographic data remains objective and unaffected by regional political ambitions or cartographic disputes. This situation highlights the critical importance of separating technical data operations from political jurisdictional battles in open data ecosystems. By maintaining institutional neutrality, statistical bodies ensure that public data remains reliable and accessible, fostering trust in government transparency and supporting evidence-based policy decisions across regional boundaries.
