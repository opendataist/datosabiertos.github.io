Union government, Election Commission’s RTI portals face ‘emergency maintenance’, data leaks

Recent technical failures across Indian government RTI portals highlight critical vulnerabilities in digital transparency infrastructure. The central RTI portal has suffered prolonged downtime, preventing citizens from tracking their applications, while the Election Commission’s portal was temporarily compromised by a data privacy breach. These incidents underscore the fragility of systems designed to uphold the right to information, revealing that public access to government records remains inconsistent and prone to significant technical disruptions. The relevance to open data lies in the erosion of public trust and the practical obstruction of information rights. When portals are unavailable or insecure, the core promise of transparent governance is undermined. The incident demonstrates that without robust cybersecurity and reliable maintenance, digital open data initiatives can fail to deliver their intended benefits, potentially exposing sensitive personal information and hindering accountability efforts. Persistent issues, including previous data loss and limited payment options, suggest systemic neglect in maintaining these essential services. The inability of authorities to promptly resolve these faults indicates a broader challenge in managing digital public infrastructure effectively. Ensuring stable, secure, and accessible platforms is vital for maintaining the integrity of open data ecosystems and safeguarding citizens' rights to information in an increasingly digitized administrative landscape.
