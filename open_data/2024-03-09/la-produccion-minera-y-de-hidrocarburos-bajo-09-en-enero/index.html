La producción minera y de hidrocarburos bajó 0,9 % en enero

Argentina’s industrial sectors show mixed results, with mining and hydrocarbons experiencing month-to-month declines despite significant annual gains in natural gas support services. While metallic mineral production dropped sharply, non-metallic resources like lithium grew, reflecting diverse sector performance.
