La desocupación laboral bajó 22% en Paraguay, según el último reporte del INE

Uruguay experiences a significant decline in unemployment between 2024 and 2025, with the national rate falling substantially. This trend underscores shifting dynamics in the labor market, particularly affecting urban populations more than rural areas. The data reveal a balanced gender distribution among the unemployed, indicating that joblessness impacts men and women nearly equally across the country. This statistical equity offers a clear picture of the challenges facing the labor force. This report is relevant to open data, as it exemplifies how transparent government statistics empower citizens to analyze economic health. Accessible, detailed datasets enable accurate monitoring of societal trends and foster informed public discourse on employment issues.
