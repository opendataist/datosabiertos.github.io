Estudio prometedor: prueba genética predice la obesidad severa en niños de tan solo 5 años - La Opinión

Estudio prometedor: prueba genética predice la obesidad severa en niños de tan solo 5 años - La Opinión

A pioneering study demonstrates that it is possible to predict the risk of severe obesity in adulthood through a genetic test administered in childhood. Using a polygenic risk score, this tool provides an early predictive window, well before environmental factors begin to significantly influence body weight. This capability allows for the identification of biological vulnerabilities at very early stages, surpassing the effectiveness of previous detection methods and paving the way for proactive prevention strategies. The significance of this finding lies in its potential to transform public health by enabling personalized preventive interventions from early childhood. By anticipating the development of chronic conditions such as diabetes or cardiovascular diseases, healthcare systems can prioritize resources for high-risk groups. However, it is crucial to recognize that the accuracy of these predictions varies by ancestry, raising ethical and technical challenges to ensure that clinical applications are equitable and representative of all populations. For the open data community, this study highlights the critical need for diverse and accessible genetic datasets. The disparity in the model’s effectiveness underscores that algorithms trained primarily on data from European ancestry are not universally applicable. Therefore, it encourages the creation of global and inclusive repositories that enable the development of more robust and fair models, essential for advancing toward truly precise and accessible preventive medicine for the entire global population.
