Trabajo en unidad permite que 159 mil personas salgan de rezago educativo: Gabriela Molina • Nsíntesis

Trabajo en unidad permite que 159 mil personas salgan de rezago educativo: Gabriela Molina • Nsíntesis

Michoacán has achieved a significant reduction in its educational backlog, marking the most substantial progress at the national level. This improvement stems from coordinated efforts among educators, families, and students, supported by targeted interventions such as providing essential school supplies, technology, and educational materials to thousands of learners. The success highlights how removing logistical and resource barriers enables more students to complete primary and secondary education. By addressing practical needs such as footwear and vision care, alongside pedagogical support, the region demonstrates that holistic support systems are crucial for retaining students and improving completion rates. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible statistics in tracking social progress. Openly sharing these metrics allows for accountability, facilitates cross-institutional collaboration, and provides a model for how data-driven policy can effectively measure and replicate educational success across other jurisdictions.
