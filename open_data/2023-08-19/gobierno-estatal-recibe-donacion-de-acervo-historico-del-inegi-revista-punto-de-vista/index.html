Gobierno Estatal recibe donación de acervo histórico del INEGI | Revista Punto de Vista

Gobierno Estatal recibe donación de acervo histórico del INEGI | Revista Punto de Vista

The INEGI donated extensive photographic and cartographic archives to the San Luis Potosí Historical Archive, significantly enriching the state’s historical collection. This transfer ensures the long-term preservation and public accessibility of materials documenting urban and tourism evolution over decades. These resources provide invaluable insights into the region’s spatial and architectural transformations, offering historians and geographers detailed chronological data. The collection allows users to explore how cities and tourism infrastructure developed from the late sixteenth century through the 1990s. This initiative is relevant to open data because it bridges the gap between physical archives and digital accessibility. By making these materials available for research, it supports the transparency and reuse of historical information, empowering citizens to engage with and understand their regional heritage through accessible, preserved datasets.
