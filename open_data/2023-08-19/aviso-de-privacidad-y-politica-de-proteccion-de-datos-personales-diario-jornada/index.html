Aviso de Privacidad y Política de Protección de Datos Personales - Diario Jornada

The JORNADA newspaper has published a Personal Data Protection Policy aligned with Peruvian legislation, outlining its ethical and legal responsibilities toward its users. This document clarifies that the newspaper acts as the primary data controller, ensuring that any information voluntarily provided by users, from names to email addresses, is managed under a strict framework of transparency and regulatory compliance. The policy specifies that data is used exclusively for subscription management, personalized customer service, and internal statistical analysis, always with the user’s explicit consent. Furthermore, it empowers data subjects with fundamental rights, such as the right to access, correct, or delete their information, ensuring that technical and organizational security measures protect the integrity and confidentiality of this data throughout the entire contractual relationship. This publication is relevant to the field of open data because it underscores the fundamental importance of governance and privacy in the handling of public or commercial information. By establishing clear mechanisms for consent and the right to revoke it, the newspaper illustrates how organizations must balance data collection with respect for privacy—a core principle underlying any serious initiative in data ethics and transparency.
