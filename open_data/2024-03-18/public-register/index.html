Public register

The European Investment Bank prioritizes openness to enhance credibility, accountability, and operational sustainability. By maintaining a public register of environmental information, it fulfills EU transparency mandates and strengthens stakeholder relations. This commitment to accessible data is vital for open_data, as it demonstrates how public institutions can effectively share decision-making processes and environmental records with citizens.
