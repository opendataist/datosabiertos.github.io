Bolivia está lista para el Censo 2024 y la movilización de más de medio millón de censistas

Bolivia is preparing for the 2024 Population and Housing Census, mobilizing more than half a million volunteers to ensure comprehensive data collection. The National Statistics Institute has confirmed high recruitment rates and nearly complete training, emphasizing a transparent process backed by extensive public communication. This massive logistical effort aims to gather accurate demographic and housing information that is crucial for national planning and resource allocation. The census uses pencil-based questionnaires to facilitate error correction, as handwritten data will be processed by artificial intelligence systems. This approach ensures higher data integrity compared to digital alternatives, which may struggle with corrections. Special protocols address environmental challenges, allowing census takers to enter homes when necessary while maintaining safety through police support and supervisor notification, reflecting adaptive best practices in modern census taking. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how rigorous, transparent data collection forms the foundation for reliable public datasets. The use of AI for processing and the emphasis on clear, legible inputs highlight the technical standards required for future data accessibility. Understanding such large-scale national efforts helps stakeholders appreciate the infrastructure needed to generate, validate, and ultimately share high-quality open data for civic analysis and policy development.
