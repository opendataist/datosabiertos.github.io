Exigen informar sobre emisiones contaminantes

Exigen informar sobre emisiones contaminantes

The INAI ordered Pemex to provide detailed data on greenhouse gas emission reductions, ruling that merely publishing online links is insufficient for genuine transparency. This decision underscores that public access requires the delivery of specific, usable information rather than simply directing citizens to websites where the actual details are lacking. Transparency requires entities to proactively provide accessible data, ensuring that citizens can effectively consult and use the information they request. The ruling clarifies that digital publication alone does not satisfy legal obligations if the specific, granular data remains difficult to obtain or is not properly organized for the user. This case is crucial for open data, as it establishes a precedent for data usability and granularity. It reinforces the principle that open data must be machine-readable and easily accessible, pushing organizations to move beyond basic disclosure toward meaningful, user-centric transparency standards in environmental reporting.
