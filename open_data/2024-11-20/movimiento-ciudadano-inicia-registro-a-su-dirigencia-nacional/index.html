Movimiento Ciudadano inicia registro a su dirigencia nacional

Movimiento Ciudadano has initiated the formal process to select its new national leadership, marking the end of the current board’s term. This registration opening represents a key institutional transition, in which candidates’ track records and integrity are assessed through administrative requirements and declarations affirming non-violence against women, ensuring that applicants meet the internal standards required to access the party’s leadership positions. The selection process will conclude with a preliminary ruling issued a few days before the Fifth National Democratic Convention, at which point active party members will vote for the new leaders. This event will determine who will occupy strategic roles, including the national coordination and the technical secretariat, thereby consolidating the hierarchical structure that will guide the movement’s political action in the coming periods and strengthening internal governance through the direct participation of its grassroots members and elected representatives. This process is relevant to open data because the political leadership structure and eligibility criteria are fundamental to institutional transparency. Understanding who makes decisions and under what rules enables citizens to access verifiable information about democratic representation. Moreover, data on candidates and their curricular profiles, being public, facilitate the analysis of diversity and meritocracy in positions of power, promoting evidence-based accountability that is accessible to the public.
