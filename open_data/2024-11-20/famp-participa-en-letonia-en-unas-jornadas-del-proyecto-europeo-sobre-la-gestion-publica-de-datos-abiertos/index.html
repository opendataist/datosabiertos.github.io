FAMP participa en Letonia en unas jornadas del proyecto europeo sobre la gestión pública de datos abiertos

FAMP participa en Letonia en unas jornadas del proyecto europeo sobre la gestión pública de datos abiertos

The FAMP’s participation in the European OD4Growth project highlights the strategic integration of open data into municipal public management. This international exchange enables town councils to learn from successful initiatives, such as the Unified Digital Street Directory and the tourism intelligence system, facilitating the transfer of knowledge on best practices in the digital era. The focus is on how data drives environmental sustainability, exemplified by Liepaja’s transformation toward climate neutrality. Cities use data-driven contracts and investment plans to coordinate energy and mobility efforts, demonstrating that informational transparency is a fundamental pillar for achieving complex and measurable environmental objectives. This initiative is relevant to the open data movement because it validates the practical utility of open data beyond the mere publication of information. By linking territorial statistics with concrete climate policies, the project shows that data openness is essential for creating smarter, more participatory, and resilient cities, fostering an ecosystem where local innovation is nourished by collaborative European learning.
