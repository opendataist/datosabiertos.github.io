Average Aussie mortgage sizes smash records

The Australian Bureau of Statistics is shifting to quarterly housing finance releases, reflecting a market where investor lending significantly outpaces first-home buyers. This trend indicates persistent market crowding, as investors dominate borrowing activity while new buyers struggle to enter the market. Record-high average mortgage sizes reveal that Australian households are accumulating substantial debt despite elevated interest rates. This surge in borrowing capacity and willingness suggests resilience in demand, even as affordability constraints theoretically limit market access for many residents. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it highlights systemic inequities in housing access. Analyzing such detailed lending breakdowns allows researchers and policymakers to expose structural barriers, ensuring transparent monitoring of financial health and market fairness in the housing sector.
