Freedom of Information reveals parking fines issued by council

Cambridgeshire County Council issued more parking penalties in 2023, driving up revenue and average daily fines. This trend continues into 2024, with drivers facing stricter enforcement and higher costs for non-compliance. This data highlights the importance of transparency in local government finances and enforcement actions. Public access to such metrics allows citizens to monitor how councils utilize taxpayer data and manage municipal resources. Understanding these patterns is crucial for open data advocates promoting accountability. It demonstrates how releasing FOI data empowers communities to assess policy impacts and ensures local authorities operate with greater public oversight and integrity.
