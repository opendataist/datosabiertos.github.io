Bluesky ya permite a los usuarios gestionar sus propios servidores

Bluesky enables self-hosting of data, granting users and developers unprecedented control over their digital presence. This initiative represents a crucial step toward decentralization, offering protection against market volatility and allowing independent content management under the AT Protocol. The article highlights the significance of this measure for open data, as it democratizes information management. Although current access is primarily restricted to individual accounts with rate limits, the goal is to expand autonomy. This reduces dependence on centralized servers, empowering individuals to transfer their data or establish their own rules. Finally, the transition to a web standards body marks a milestone in building an open internet. Future independent moderation will add customization while maintaining a cohesive experience. This commitment to interoperability and user control reinforces the fundamental principles of open data and digital sovereignty in social networks.
