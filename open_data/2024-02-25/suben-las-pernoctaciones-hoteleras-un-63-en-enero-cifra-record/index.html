Suben las pernoctaciones hoteleras un 6,3% en enero, cifra récord

Spain’s hotel sector demonstrates remarkable resilience by achieving record-breaking occupancy rates and room prices even during historically slow winter months. This surge reflects sustained economic strength, driven by continuous growth in both domestic and international demand. Regional analysis highlights specific drivers, with the Canary Islands and major metropolitan areas leading revenue growth. The consistent rise in average daily rates across all hotel categories indicates that the tourism industry is not merely recovering but expanding significantly beyond pre-pandemic levels. This trend is vital to open data initiatives, as it underscores the importance of accurate, timely statistical monitoring. Reliable public datasets enable stakeholders to understand market dynamics, forecast trends, and make informed decisions regarding resource allocation and policy development in the tourism sector.
