Nadie puede estar por encima de la ley

The INAI emphasizes that no public official, including the President, is above the law, stressing the legal obligation to protect the confidentiality of personal data. This position highlights the critical importance of complying with transparency laws to safeguard individual privacy rights against state overreach. The conflict stems from the President’s disregard for legal norms in favor of political authority, creating tension between governmental power and data protection principles. Such actions underscore the necessity of robust oversight mechanisms to ensure accountability. This incident is crucial for the open data movement, as it illustrates the risks of arbitrary data disclosure. It reinforces the need for strict legal frameworks that prioritize citizens’ privacy, ensuring that transparency initiatives do not compromise fundamental personal security rights.
