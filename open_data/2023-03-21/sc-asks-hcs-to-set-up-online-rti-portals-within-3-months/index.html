SC asks HCs to set up online RTI portals within 3 months

The Supreme Court has mandated that all High Courts establish online Right to Information portals within three months. This directive aims to significantly enhance transparency by modernizing the mechanism for accessing public information, ensuring that the legislative intent of the RTI Act is fully realized through digital accessibility rather than physical mail. The ruling highlights a critical gap in digital infrastructure across the judiciary, particularly noting the absence of online systems for subordinate courts. By requiring High Courts to lead this digital transition, the decision promotes standardized, efficient, and barrier-free access to information for citizens, including those abroad who previously faced logistical hurdles in filing physical requests. This development is vital for open data as it signals a systemic shift toward digitizing governance processes. It encourages the integration of public information systems, fostering greater accountability and reducing bureaucratic friction. Ultimately, it supports the broader movement for accessible, real-time government data, empowering citizens to engage more effectively with democratic institutions through user-friendly digital platforms.
