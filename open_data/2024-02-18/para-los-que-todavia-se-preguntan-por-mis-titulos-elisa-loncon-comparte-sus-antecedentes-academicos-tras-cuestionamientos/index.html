“Para los que todavía se preguntan por mis títulos...”: Elisa Loncon comparte sus antecedentes académicos tras cuestionamientos

The Universidad de Santiago withheld Elisa Loncon’s academic records despite transparency requests, citing political attacks as justification. This conflict highlights the tension between institutional secrecy and public accountability in higher education. The legal battle underscores the necessity of proactive information disclosure. It demonstrates how opaque practices can fuel distrust, especially when officials face online scrutiny, reinforcing the need for clear open data standards in public institutions. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the real-world consequences of withholding public information. It emphasizes that transparency is crucial for maintaining institutional integrity and preventing the misuse of private data narratives, ensuring that public service roles remain accountable to citizens.
