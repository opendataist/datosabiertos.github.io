Poll panel chief says ‘will follow Supreme Court order on electoral bonds issue’

Poll panel chief says ‘will follow Supreme Court order on electoral bonds issue’

The Supreme Court’s landmark ruling against electoral bonds prioritizes transparency, striking down anonymous political funding as a violation of constitutional rights to information and free speech. This decision significantly enhances open data by ensuring donor identities are no longer shielded, fostering accountability in political finance. The Election Commission publicly affirms its commitment to transparency, pledging to strictly follow judicial directives to implement these changes. By aligning its operations with the court’s mandate, the commission reinforces its role in maintaining electoral integrity through accessible and truthful information flows. This case is vital for open data advocates, as it establishes a legal precedent that public interest outweighs secrecy in political contributions. It demonstrates how judicial intervention can compel institutions to adopt greater openness, setting a standard for future governance and data accessibility.
