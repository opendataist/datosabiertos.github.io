Electoral Bonds case: Information about funding of political parties essential for effective exercise of choice of voting, says Supreme Court | India News - Times of India

The Supreme Court prioritizes voters' access to political funding data over corporate privacy rights to safeguard democratic integrity. This ruling ensures transparency by recognizing that informed voting choices are fundamental to pure elections. Such precedents advance open data initiatives by mandating public disclosure of financial information for democratic accountability.
