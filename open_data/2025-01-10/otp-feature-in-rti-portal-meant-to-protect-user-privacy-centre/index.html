OTP Feature in RTI Portal Meant to Protect User Privacy: Centre

OTP Feature in RTI Portal Meant to Protect User Privacy: Centre

The Department of Personnel and Training recently introduced an OTP requirement for the RTI portal, aiming to balance digital accessibility with robust cybersecurity. By mandating email-based authentication, the government seeks to protect sensitive personal information submitted by citizens, ensuring that only authorized individuals can access application statuses. Although some users perceive this extra step as cumbersome, officials argue it is vital for privacy protection. The system is designed to be user-friendly, with OTPs that do not expire upon generation to accommodate potential delays in email delivery caused by high traffic. This approach aligns with best practices for securing digital interactions while maintaining the integrity of the transparency framework. This update is highly relevant to open_data because it demonstrates the growing tension between data utility and privacy preservation. As governments digitize public access mechanisms, implementing strict security protocols becomes essential to maintain public trust. It highlights the critical need to safeguard the personal data of those requesting information, ensuring that open data initiatives do not inadvertently compromise individual rights.
