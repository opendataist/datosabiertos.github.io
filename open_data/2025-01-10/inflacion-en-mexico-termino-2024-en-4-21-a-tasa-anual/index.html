Inflación en México terminó 2024 en 4.21% a tasa anual

Mexico’s inflation closed 2024 at its lowest annual rate since 2020, signaling a significant economic stabilization that surpassed analyst expectations. This downward trend reflects improved price control mechanisms, suggesting that monetary policies and market adjustments are effectively curbing excessive cost increases across various sectors. The data indicates a healthier economic environment for consumers, reducing pressure on purchasing power and fostering greater financial predictability for households and businesses alike. However, underlying inflation showed a slight rebound, highlighting persistent volatility in stable goods and services. This nuanced shift suggests that while headline numbers have improved, core economic pressures remain, requiring continued vigilance from policymakers. The divergence between non-core and core inflation metrics underscores the complexity of managing price stability, indicating that temporary factors like air travel or specific agricultural goods can still drive short-term fluctuations even within a broader cooling trend.
