North American trans-border freight rose last year

North American trans-border freight rose last year

North American trans-border freight demonstrated resilience in late 2024, with total trade value inching upward despite mixed regional performance. This nuanced trend highlights the growing economic dominance of US-Mexico trade corridors, which continued to outpace US-Canada exchanges in dollar volume, signaling shifting supply chain dynamics and regional integration priorities across the continent. The transportation landscape also revealed significant mode-specific disparities, as trucking maintained its leadership while rail and pipeline sectors contracted sharply. These divergent trends underscore the varying capacities and efficiencies of different logistics networks, suggesting that infrastructure resilience and modal balance are critical factors in sustaining robust cross-border commerce amid evolving economic conditions. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives as it provides granular, publicly accessible insights into international trade flows and logistics performance. By making such detailed metrics available, stakeholders can better analyze supply chain health, inform policy decisions, and promote transparency in global commerce, fostering a more informed and responsive approach to managing international economic relationships.
