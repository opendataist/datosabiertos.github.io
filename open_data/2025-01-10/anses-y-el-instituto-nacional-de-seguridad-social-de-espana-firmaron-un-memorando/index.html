ANSES y el Instituto Nacional de Seguridad Social de España firmaron un memorando

Argentina and Spain have established a historic data-sharing agreement to enhance fraud prevention and ensure accurate pension payments for cross-border beneficiaries. This initiative leverages open data principles by prioritizing transparency and technological innovation in public administration. Such international cooperation strengthens governance standards, demonstrating how accessible, verified information exchange improves accountability and reduces systemic errors in social security management.
