Señala AMLO la ineficacia y lo costoso del INAI

The article highlights President López Obrador’s proposal to abolish the INAI, arguing that the institution has proven ineffective in combating corruption and suffers from budgetary inefficiencies and overlapping functions with other federal agencies. This stance suggests a fundamental restructuring of transparency mechanisms, prioritizing existing bodies over specialized independent institutes. The critique frames the INAI as a redundant entity that fails to deliver tangible results despite its high annual costs. The President attributes its creation to external societal pressures rather than genuine institutional need, implying that future administrations should address these structural flaws to improve governance efficiency. This narrative is highly relevant to open data discourse because it challenges the foundational logic of independent transparency bodies. It raises critical questions about how data access, anti-corruption efforts, and institutional accountability are structured. Such political debates directly impact the stability, funding, and operational independence of organizations dedicated to making public information accessible and usable for citizens.
