How Public Data Drives Justice and Safety in Colombia and Bolivia

Mothers who run families know that being open keeps the next generation safe. Two stories from Medellín and Bolivia show how sharing public information changes justice and safety. These examples turn hidden systems into tools that help citizens.

In Colombia, the disappearance of an American flight attendant shows targeted crimes. Gangs and scopolamine are involved. Recent reports show that U.S. citizens make up a large part of crime victims in Medellín. This pattern shows why we need strong public safety information. Leaders use this data to make better policies and raise awareness. They use it to protect international visitors. If you want to learn how to access these data sets, read our guide on how to use an open-data portal’s API. This access helps communities protect those who are vulnerable.

In Bolivia, the Public Ministry launched its first portal for the justice sector. This move shifts the system from secrecy to transparency. The platform provides proof of crimes and legal processes. This step aims to build public trust and help people engage in civic life. The system protects privacy while allowing universities, media, and groups to study trends. Citizens can question court results because of this openness. Sharing clear data turns hidden processes into accountable ones.

Users must understand raw numbers to avoid bias. Do not quote or act on statistics until you understand the context. We must read information accurately to support true accountability. Readers who want to improve their critical thinking skills can find tools in our guide on how to read a statistic before you quote it.

These examples show that public information helps bring justice and safety. Preventing crime through data and holding the courts accountable share a common goal. This goal creates a world where information is free and used for the good of all. Users play an active role in shaping a safer future. They do this by accessing, analyzing, and questioning digital information.
