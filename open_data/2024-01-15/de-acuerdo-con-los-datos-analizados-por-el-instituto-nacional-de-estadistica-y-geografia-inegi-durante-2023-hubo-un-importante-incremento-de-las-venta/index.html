De acuerdo con los datos analizados por el Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía (Inegi), durante 2023 hubo un importante incremento de las ventas

The analysis of 2023 automotive sales data reveals a robust recovery in Mexico, significantly surpassing previous crises. This substantial growth indicates that the industry has successfully overcome supply constraints, marking a positive turning point for the sector. The surge in transactions highlights resilient consumer demand that has stabilized the market, demonstrating that availability and economic confidence have aligned to boost overall commercial activity. Despite the global trend favoring Sport Utility Vehicles (SUVs), sedans continue to dominate Mexican consumer preferences, maintaining a strong presence in the top sales rankings. Asian brands largely lead the market, with specific models showing remarkable stability and growth. This shift in vehicle types and brand dominance provides critical insights into local consumer behavior, illustrating how economic factors and product availability influence purchasing decisions during periods of market recovery. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the value of publicly available statistics from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) in driving transparent economic analysis. By making detailed sales figures accessible, stakeholders can identify trends, predict market shifts, and make informed decisions. The transparency of this data fosters accountability and allows for a deeper understanding of industrial health, proving that open data serves as a foundational tool for analyzing real-world economic impacts and sector performance.
