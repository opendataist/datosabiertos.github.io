FIFA publicó un informe en el que expuso a los 397 equipos que tienen prohibido inscribir jugadores: cuáles son los 5 argentinos

FIFA has launched a new digital platform that publicly lists clubs prohibited from registering players, both nationally and internationally. This initiative aims to enhance transparency in global football by providing real-time access to disciplinary measures imposed by FIFA or the Football Disputes Tribunal. By making this data readily available, the organization empowers stakeholders to understand current sanctions and ensures a fairer competitive environment for all participants in the sport. The platform allows users to search through hundreds of sanctioned teams using specific filters, covering all levels of football from professional to amateur. It serves as a critical tool for players, executives, coaches, and the general public to verify the status of various entities. The list includes prominent clubs from South America and Europe, demonstrating the global reach of these disciplinary actions and the strict adherence to regulations required to maintain integrity within the game. This update is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the proactive release of structured, searchable, and regularly updated public information. By publishing sanction details in an accessible format, FIFA facilitates accountability and allows external actors to monitor compliance effectively. Such transparency fosters trust and ensures that decision-makers have accurate, timely information, reflecting a commitment to open governance and data accessibility in international sports administration.
