La inflación del 2022 cerró casi en 95% y es la más alta desde 1991 | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

La inflación del 2022 cerró casi en 95% y es la más alta desde 1991 | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

The article details Argentina’s historic inflation surge, which reached nearly 95% in 2022, the highest rate since the Convertibility Plan era. This data highlights severe economic instability, where rising costs drastically erode purchasing power and destabilize essential services such as food, transport, and healthcare. Such extreme volatility directly impacts data integrity, as rapid price shifts challenge the accuracy and timeliness of consumer price indices. For open data practitioners, this scenario underscores the critical need for real-time data verification and robust statistical methodologies. Standard reporting intervals may become obsolete when prices change weekly rather than monthly. Transparently documenting these anomalies ensures that datasets remain useful for analysis, preventing misleading conclusions derived from outdated or unadjusted figures during hyperinflationary periods. Understanding these dynamics is vital for maintaining public trust in official statistics. When inflation deviates significantly from historical norms, data providers must adapt their disclosure practices to reflect current realities. This relevance extends to global open data communities, emphasizing that accurate, accessible economic data is essential for informed decision-making, policy formulation, and economic recovery strategies in unstable environments.
