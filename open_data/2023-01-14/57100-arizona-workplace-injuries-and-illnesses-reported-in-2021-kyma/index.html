57,100 Arizona workplace injuries and illnesses reported in 2021 - KYMA

Arizona’s private sector recorded workplace injuries and illnesses in 2021, with rates varying significantly by industry and establishment size. While natural resources faced higher risks, the overall incidence remained stable compared to the previous year, indicating consistent safety conditions across most sectors. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, standardized metrics that allow researchers and the public to monitor labor safety trends and drive accountability in workplace health.
