Gobierno Abierto: UNL recibió un premio internacional

The Open Government Portal of the University of the Littoral (UNL) has been recognized as a best academic practice by institutions backed by the United Nations for its integrated approach to transparency, participation, and collaboration. This acknowledgment highlights the initiative’s innovative use of visual data and participatory budgeting, establishing it as a replicable model for Argentine universities seeking to strengthen democratic governance through institutional commitment and interdisciplinary teamwork. This award underscores the vital role of higher education institutions in advancing open data policies across Ibero-America. By leveraging technology to enhance accountability and public engagement, universities contribute significantly to improving public policies. The recognition serves as a catalyst for regional exchange, encouraging academic networks to share strategies and collectively address the challenges of implementing effective, transparent governance structures. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how academic entities can effectively operationalize open government principles. It illustrates that successful open data initiatives require not only technical infrastructure but also a robust institutional framework that fosters collaboration across diverse departments. The UNL example proves that when statistical, IT, and communication units work together, they can create coherent, user-friendly platforms that promote civic involvement and set a high standard for data openness in the public sector.
