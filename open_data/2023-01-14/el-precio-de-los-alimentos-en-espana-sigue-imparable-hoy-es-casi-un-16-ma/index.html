El precio de los alimentos en España sigue imparable: hoy es casi un 16% má

The article highlights Spain’s moderation of inflation in 2022, primarily driven by declining energy costs, while food prices reached historic highs. Despite this slowdown, underlying inflation remains elevated, and wage growth fails to keep pace with price increases, significantly affecting household purchasing power. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for transparent and timely dissemination of statistics to ensure public accountability.
