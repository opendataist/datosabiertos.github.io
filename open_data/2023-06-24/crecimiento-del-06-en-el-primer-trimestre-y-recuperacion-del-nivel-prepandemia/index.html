Crecimiento del 0,6% en el primer trimestre y recuperación del nivel prepandemia

The Spanish economy has surpassed pre-pandemic levels, driven primarily by robust export performance and increased investment. This growth demonstrates that, despite persistent global challenges such as inflation and geopolitical uncertainty, key sectors like tourism and manufacturing remain resilient and are effectively stimulating economic activity. Furthermore, the labor market shows significant stabilization, with employment reaching historic highs and productivity improving notably. The synergy between rising wages and higher output per worker indicates a healthier economic structure. This positive trend suggests that structural factors, including European funds, are successfully supporting long-term recovery and competitiveness within the broader Eurozone context. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical role of high-quality, timely official statistics in shaping economic narratives. The ability to revise data upwards and provide granular insights into sectoral performance underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and reliable public data. Such openness allows researchers and policymakers to accurately assess economic health, fostering better-informed decisions and greater accountability in public administration.
