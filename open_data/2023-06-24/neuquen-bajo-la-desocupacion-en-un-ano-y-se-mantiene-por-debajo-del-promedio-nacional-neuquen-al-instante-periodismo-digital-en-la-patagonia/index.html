Neuquén bajó la desocupación en un año y se mantiene por debajo del promedio nacional | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

Neuquén has achieved significant labor stability, with unemployment rates dropping notably below the national average and activity rates recovering strongly in the post-pandemic period. This success is attributed to coordinated efforts among the government, the private sector, and trade unions, which have driven sustained job creation in key industries such as hydrocarbons, tourism, commerce, and construction. The province stands out nationally for its consistent employment growth, maintaining an upward trajectory for over two years and securing the second-highest level of private-sector job creation in the country. These trends indicate a robust local economy that effectively withstands broader national fluctuations, demonstrating resilience and strategic economic management. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives, as it showcases how accessible statistical records can validate the impact of public policies and the health of regional economies. Transparent, high-quality open data enables researchers and citizens to verify the effectiveness of governance, fostering accountability and informed decision-making based on real-time, verifiable socioeconomic indicators rather than opaque metrics.
