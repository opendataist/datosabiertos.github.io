Inflación baja hasta 5.18% en quincena

Mexico’s annual inflation stabilized at 5.18% in early June 2023, marking a significant decline from previous highs. This moderation in price increases suggests a cooling economic environment, offering relief to consumers and policymakers monitoring cost-of-living pressures. Core indicators reveal that underlying services prices continue to rise, while volatile agricultural and energy costs have decreased. This divergence highlights complex inflationary dynamics, where structural service costs persist despite improvements in commodity sectors, reflecting nuanced shifts in consumer spending patterns. This data is vital for open-data initiatives as it demonstrates the utility of transparent, granular economic metrics. By making detailed inflation breakdowns publicly available, institutions enable independent analysis, fostering accountability and allowing citizens to better understand and track the real impact of economic policies on their daily lives.
