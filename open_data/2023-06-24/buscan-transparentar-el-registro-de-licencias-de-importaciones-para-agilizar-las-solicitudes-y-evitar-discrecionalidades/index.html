Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The Civic Coalition proposes creating an Open Data Portal for Argentina’s import system, aiming to replace current opacity with full transparency. The project requires that all information regarding applications, products, amounts, and statuses be published digitally in open, up-to-date formats. This initiative seeks to eliminate the administrative discretion that characterizes the current system, where frequent changes in procedures restrict imports without clear parameters. The proposal establishes strict processing deadlines, ensuring that resolutions are issued in chronological order. If no response is provided within the stipulated timeframe, the license is automatically deemed approved. Additionally, the financial evaluation criteria will be published to ensure predictability. This measure addresses criticisms that the lack of clearly defined rules and failure to meet deadlines create a high risk of corruption, as they allow officials to approve or reject applications without clear justification. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public access to government information in machine-readable formats can serve as a fundamental tool against corruption. By making licensing processes visible, information asymmetry is reduced and abuse of power is curtailed. This case illustrates the practical application of the principle of regulatory transparency, aligning with international standards that suggest free access to public information reduces corrupt practices and increases trust in institutions.
