Perciben productores de Coahuila factor climático como principal problema para el campo

Agricultural producers in Coahuila identify climate change as their most critical challenge, surpassing economic inflation and post-pandemic disruptions. This growing concern highlights a significant shift in agricultural risks, indicating that environmental instability now poses a greater threat to local food security and economic stability than traditional market fluctuations. The data reveals a substantial increase in climate-related complaints compared to fifteen years ago, reflecting worsening weather patterns that severely impact productivity. By recognizing these shifting priorities, stakeholders can better allocate resources toward adaptive strategies rather than solely focusing on immediate economic relief or security issues. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of accessible, longitudinal agricultural statistics in identifying emerging systemic risks. Transparent data enables policymakers and researchers to track environmental impacts over time, fostering evidence-based interventions that protect farmers and ensure sustainable land use in vulnerable regions.
