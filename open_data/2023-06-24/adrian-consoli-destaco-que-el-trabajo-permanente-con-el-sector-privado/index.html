Adrián Consoli destacó que “el trabajo permanente con el sector privado”

Mar del Plata has reached a historic low in its unemployment rate, a positive indicator that reflects the effectiveness of the municipal strategy of close collaboration with the private sector. Local authorities emphasize that, while acknowledging the overall decline, the focus must remain on maintaining active engagement with business leaders, given the reality that thousands of people are still seeking employment. This close relationship enables public policies to be tailored to the actual needs of the labor market, facilitating investment and ensuring that the supply of labor effectively matches workers’ demand. The significance of this model highlights the importance of open data and statistical transparency as tools for efficient public management. By monitoring and sharing accurate indicators such as the unemployment rate, institutions can assess the impact of their interventions and adjust their strategies in real time. The visibility of these data not only validates the actions taken but also fosters investor confidence, demonstrating that the city offers a stable environment prepared to attract both large corporations and small entrepreneurs. Furthermore, the case exemplifies how education and training can be dynamically integrated with the specific demands of the labor market, as seen in the technology sector. By coordinating municipal resources with educational institutions to quickly address talent shortages, employability is maximized and local competitiveness is strengthened. This proactive approach underscores that sustainable employment requires constant coordination among government, the private sector, and the community, leveraging available information to create tangible and lasting opportunities for the population.
