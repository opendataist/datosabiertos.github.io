Crecieron las ventas en supermercados, mayoristas y shoppings durante abril

Supermarket sales rose significantly compared to the previous year, outpacing inflation and reflecting strong nominal demand despite minor monthly fluctuations. This growth was driven by sharp increases in fresh produce, prepared foods, beverages, and hygiene products, indicating shifting consumer spending habits amid high price levels. For open data, this highlights the critical need to distinguish between nominal and real sales trends when analyzing economic health. Properly tagged inflation-adjusted metrics are essential for accurate public insights, ensuring transparency and reliable decision-making based on actual purchasing power rather than misleading price surges.
