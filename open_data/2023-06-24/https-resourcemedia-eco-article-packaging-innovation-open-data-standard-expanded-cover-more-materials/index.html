Open Data
ES EN

The Open 3P standard has expanded its scope to encompass all packaging materials, marking a significant shift from its original focus on plastics. By establishing a shared framework for data exchange, it creates a common language that allows manufacturers, sellers, and recyclers to communicate effectively. This broadened coverage supports comprehensive sustainability efforts and aligns with global initiatives like the Global Plastic Pollution Treaty, which seeks to address the entire lifecycle of plastic waste. A core implication of this standard is its ability to streamline administrative burdens, particularly for organizations subject to Extended Producer Responsibility legislation. By providing a structured, tiered approach to data collection, it simplifies compliance reporting and reduces the complexity of supply chain interactions. This consistency enables stakeholders to share compatible data quickly and cost-effectively without storing information, thereby fostering collaborative innovation and easing regulatory demands across the industry. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how standardized, accessible data structures can drive environmental progress and circular economy goals. It proves that transparent data sharing is not merely a technical exercise but a strategic tool for regulatory compliance and sustainability. By removing barriers to data exchange, the standard empowers the entire packaging ecosystem to make informed decisions, ultimately supporting broader ecological objectives through improved information transparency.
