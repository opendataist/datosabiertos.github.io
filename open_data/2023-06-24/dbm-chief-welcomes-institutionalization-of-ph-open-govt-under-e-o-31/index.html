DBM chief welcomes institutionalization of PH open govt under E.O. 31

Executive Order No. 31 institutionalizes the Philippines Open Government Partnership to strengthen transparency. This initiative guarantees meaningful citizen engagement while fostering accountability within government operations. Such measures directly support open data goals by promoting accessible and participatory public governance.
