Furthermore, the text contends that the government responded to emerging doubts not through scientific inquiry but through aggressive censorship and the silencing of critics. The relevance to open data lies in the demonstration of how transparency mechanisms, such as FOIA requests, can expose discrepancies between internal government records and public narratives. By revealing these documents, open data initiatives allow citizens to verify official claims against internal evidence. This case serves as a critical example of how access to unredacted governmental communications is essential for holding public health authorities accountable and understanding the reality of policy decisions made during crises.
