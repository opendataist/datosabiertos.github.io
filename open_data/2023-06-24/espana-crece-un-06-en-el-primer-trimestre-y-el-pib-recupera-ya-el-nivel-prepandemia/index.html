España crece un 0,6% en el primer trimestre y el PIB recupera ya el nivel prepandemia

The Spanish economy has definitively regained its pre-pandemic production level, driven by accelerated growth that positions it as the most dynamic in the European Union. This above-average performance within the EU highlights the revitalization of the business sector and the resilience of the national productive model, marking a positive turning point after periods of health and economic uncertainty. The main driver of this expansion is the improvement in external competitiveness, which has boosted exports of goods and services, complemented by sustained domestic demand and rising capital investment. This synergy between the external sector and domestic activity not only confirms the robustness of the recovery but also underscores the adaptive capacity of Spanish companies to seize opportunities in global markets, redefining their positioning in international trade. The relevance of these data for open data lies in the transparency and accessibility of official statistical sources that allow these trends to be verified in real time. With detailed indicators broken down by sector, both citizens and researchers can analyze the real impact of economic policies and the health of the labor market, fostering an informed public debate based on verifiable, open, and quantitative evidence.
