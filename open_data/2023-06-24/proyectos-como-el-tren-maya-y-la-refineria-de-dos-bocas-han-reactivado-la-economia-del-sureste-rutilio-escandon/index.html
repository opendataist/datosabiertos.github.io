Proyectos como el Tren Maya y la refinería de Dos Bocas han reactivado la economía del sureste: Rutilio Escandón

Governor Rutilio Escandón asserts that major infrastructure projects, including the Tren Maya and the Interoceanic Corridor, have successfully reactivated the southeastern Mexican economy. Recent data indicates a significant rise in formal employment in Chiapas, reflecting broader socio-economic development driven by these initiatives. The inclusion of Chiapas in the Interoceanic Corridor is expected to modernize railway infrastructure, acting as a catalyst for trade and investment. By facilitating the exchange of goods between the Gulf of Mexico and the Pacific Ocean, these improvements aim to boost tourism and commerce, the region's primary sources of income. This article highlights the role of open data in validating the impact of public policies. By using official employment statistics to substantiate claims of economic growth, it demonstrates how transparent data availability allows citizens to verify government performance and ensures accountability in large-scale development projects.
