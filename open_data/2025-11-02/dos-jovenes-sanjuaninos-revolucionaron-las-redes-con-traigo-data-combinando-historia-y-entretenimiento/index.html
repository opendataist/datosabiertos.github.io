Dos jóvenes sanjuaninos revolucionaron las redes con “Traigo Data”, combinando historia y entretenimiento

Traigo Data represents a significant shift in how historical information is disseminated, moving away from traditional, static formats toward dynamic audiovisual storytelling. By combining rigorous historical research with a modern, engaging narrative style, the project demonstrates that data and heritage can be effectively communicated to contemporary audiences. This approach proves that educational content does not need to be dry; instead, it can leverage humor and emotion to capture attention, making the past accessible and relevant to those accustomed to rapid digital consumption. The initiative highlights the importance of contextualizing raw data within compelling human stories. Rather than simply presenting facts, the creators compile historiography and transform it into an immersive experience that connects viewers emotionally with San Juan’s identity. This method ensures that historical data is not just consumed but understood and valued, fostering a deeper appreciation for local heritage. The organic growth and positive feedback indicate that audiences are eager for this type of content, suggesting a strong demand for culturally grounded media that respects intellectual integrity while prioritizing accessibility. This project is highly relevant to the open data community as it illustrates the power of data visualization and narrative journalism in bridging the gap between technical information and public understanding. It shows that when data is made open and presented creatively, it can drive civic engagement and cultural preservation. By transforming abstract historical records into relatable stories, Traigo Data offers a model for how other organizations can use open data to activate communities, proving that transparency and modern storytelling are key to making information truly impactful.
