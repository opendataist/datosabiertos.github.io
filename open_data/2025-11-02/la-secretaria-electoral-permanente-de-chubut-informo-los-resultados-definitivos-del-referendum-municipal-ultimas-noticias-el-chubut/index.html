La Secretaría Electoral Permanente de Chubut informó los resultados definitivos del referéndum municipal - Últimas Noticias: El Chubut

Chubut’s election secretariat confirmed that voters rejected a referendum to lower the municipal voting age to 16. The proposal failed due to insufficient support, leaving local electoral laws unchanged. This data illustrates transparent governance and the critical role of open data in verifying democratic outcomes.
