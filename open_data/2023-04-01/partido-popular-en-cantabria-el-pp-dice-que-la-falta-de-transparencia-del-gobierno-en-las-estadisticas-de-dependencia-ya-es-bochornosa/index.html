Partido Popular en Cantabria - El PP dice que la falta de transparencia del Gobierno en las estadísticas de Dependencia "ya es bochornosa"

The Spanish People’s Party criticizes the Cantabrian government for failing to publish mandatory monthly statistics on the public dependency care system, calling the omission shameful. This persistent lack of transparency is said to violate both regional transparency laws and national statutes protecting autonomy. This situation highlights the critical importance of open data in ensuring public accountability and legal compliance. Without accessible, timely statistics, citizens cannot effectively monitor the performance of social services, which undermines trust in institutional management and hinders independent analysis of resource allocation. Ultimately, the article underscores that releasing operational data is not merely an administrative task but a fundamental democratic right. It emphasizes that governments must prioritize data availability to prevent information asymmetry, ensuring that public resources are managed transparently and that stakeholders have the necessary information to evaluate service quality.
