Mindsets need to shift to treating data as a product rather than just a byproduct

Transitioning to a federated architecture and treating data as a product unlocks vital incentives for sharing. This shift empowers personalized governance by aligning departmental incentives with clear value propositions. Consequently, open data becomes a functional tool for inclusive and responsive citizen services.
