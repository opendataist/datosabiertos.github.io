De ‘Gamer’ a los 63 años a emprendedora a los 91: cómo envejecer de forma activa

The article highlights Spain’s rapid demographic aging, projecting that nearly one-third of the population will be over 60 by 2030. This structural shift necessitates a societal commitment to promoting active aging, ensuring that older adults maintain autonomy and well-being. CaixaBank’s “Bendita Edad” campaign addresses this by showcasing senior citizens as dynamic contributors to society, challenging stereotypes through narratives of entrepreneurship, digital engagement, and lifelong learning. Data reveals a strong desire among seniors to remain active, with many engaged in sports, hobbies, and caregiving responsibilities. While there is broad consensus that activity enhances physical and mental health, significant societal prejudice persists. A majority of respondents feel that current senior role models are insufficient, indicating a gap between the perceived value of elder wisdom and its public recognition. This underscores the need for visible examples of senior vitality to inspire further participation and combat ageism. From an open data perspective, this article is relevant because it illustrates how statistical projections must be paired with qualitative insights to drive inclusive policy and service design. By combining demographic forecasts with survey data on attitudes and behaviors, institutions can better understand the specific needs and potential of aging populations. This integrated approach ensures that services, such as financial or digital support, are not only accessible but also respectful of the diverse capabilities and aspirations of older adults, fostering a more equitable and informed society.
