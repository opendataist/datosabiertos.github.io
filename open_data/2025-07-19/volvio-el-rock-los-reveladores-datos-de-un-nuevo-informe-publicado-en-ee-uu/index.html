¿Volvió el rock? Los reveladores datos de un nuevo informe publicado en EE. UU.

The report highlights a significant resurgence of rock music, which secured second place in US audio streaming behind hip-hop. This genre experienced the highest year-over-year growth, driven by renewed interest sparked by media content such as the Led Zeppelin documentary. These cultural phenomena effectively introduce classic rock to younger generations, demonstrating how visual storytelling can revitalize audio consumption trends. Simultaneously, physical formats like vinyl and cassettes remain strong for established artists. Classic rock albums continue to sell well in these tangible formats, indicating that dedicated fans still value traditional media alongside digital streaming. This dual presence in both streaming and physical sales underscores the genre's enduring popularity and adaptability across different consumption habits. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it validates how diverse media types interact to influence public taste. Understanding these correlations helps researchers track cultural shifts and the longevity of artistic movements. By making such comprehensive consumption data publicly available, analysts can better study the evolving dynamics of the music industry and consumer behavior.
