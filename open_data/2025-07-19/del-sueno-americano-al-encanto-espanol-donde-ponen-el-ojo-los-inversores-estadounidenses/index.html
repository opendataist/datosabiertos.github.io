Del sueño americano al encanto español: ¿Dónde ponen el ojo los inversores estadounidenses?

The significant increase in U.S. citizens in Spain, driven by the search for political and economic stability amid local uncertainty, reflects an inverse migration trend toward a market perceived as more predictable and secure. This shift underscores the importance of having transparent, up-to-date statistical data to understand migratory flows and investment trends in real time, enabling more precise planning for sector stakeholders. Interest is primarily focused on the residential sector due to its profitability and regulatory clarity, although bureaucratic barriers persist, necessitating specialized advisory support. The relevance to open data lies in the need to access disaggregated, readily available fiscal, legal, and pricing information. When such data are open and interoperable, information asymmetry is reduced, allowing international investors to assess risks and opportunities with greater precision without relying exclusively on costly intermediaries. In the medium term, the Spanish market is consolidating as a global diversification haven, valued for both legal certainty and urban quality of life. To maximize this potential, it is crucial for public institutions to publish open datasets on real estate transactions, energy efficiency, and connectivity. This not only empowers foreign buyers but also fosters a more transparent and efficient digital ecosystem, aligning open data practices with global investment and mobility interests.
