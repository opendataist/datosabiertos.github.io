More than 90% of childrens’ general health ‘good or very good’ – survey | BreakingNews.ie

More than 90% of childrens’ general health ‘good or very good’ – survey | BreakingNews.ie

The 2024 Irish Health Survey reveals that the vast majority of children are in good or very good health, highlighting significant regional disparities where areas like HSE South West outperform Dublin and the Midlands. While dental health and hospital admission rates are reported, the data indicates that medication usage and leisure activity participation vary notably by age group, with younger children generally showing better dietary habits than adolescents. This research is critically relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of large-scale, publicly accessible statistical surveys in monitoring public health trends. By making these detailed findings available, the Central Statistics Office enables researchers and policymakers to analyze health determinants, such as nutrition and recreational access, across different demographics. This transparency supports evidence-based decision-making and allows for a deeper understanding of how social and environmental factors influence childhood well-being. Ultimately, the report underscores the importance of tracking long-standing illnesses and health access to ensure equitable care for all children. The insights provided help identify vulnerable groups, such as boys who exhibit higher rates of chronic conditions, and guide future interventions. Open access to this comprehensive dataset fosters accountability and encourages collaborative efforts to improve health outcomes, ensuring that resources are directed effectively to areas with the greatest need.
