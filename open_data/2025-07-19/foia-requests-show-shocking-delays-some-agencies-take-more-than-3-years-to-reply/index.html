FOIA requests show shocking delays; some agencies take more than 3 years to reply

FOIA requests show shocking delays; some agencies take more than 3 years to reply

The Freedom of Information Act ensures citizen access to government records, yet severe delays undermine its promise of transparency. Recent analysis reveals that processing times often stretch into years, with some agencies taking over three years to respond even to simple requests. These excessive wait times hinder public accountability and frustrate those seeking to understand government operations. The disparity between expected and actual response durations highlights systemic inefficiencies within major departments, suggesting that current practices fail to serve the public interest effectively. This situation is critical to open data discussions because it exposes the gap between legal rights and practical accessibility. Without timely data release, the potential for informed civic engagement and government oversight remains severely limited, demanding urgent reforms to restore trust and functionality.
