Asm. Phil Steck says Freedom of Information Law provided new opioid settlement fund data

Asm. Phil Steck says Freedom of Information Law provided new opioid settlement fund data

New York State has failed to disburse most opioid settlement funds, leaving providers critically understaffed and unable to offer essential services like transportation. While harm reduction efforts correlate with reduced overdose deaths, this strategy alone cannot address the logistical barriers preventing people from accessing treatment. This issue is vital for open data advocates, as it demonstrates how opaque financial reporting obstructs accountability. Without transparent, accessible data on fund allocation, policymakers and the public remain unaware of the severe gaps in service delivery. Ultimately, this case highlights the urgent need for standardized, machine-readable government spending reports. Open data practices ensure that taxpayer funds intended for public health are actually reaching the communities and providers who need them most.
