CSO task LGs on open budgeting system - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

CSO task LGs on open budgeting system - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

Semiye Michael urges local government councils to join the Open Government Partnership to dismantle misconceptions and foster genuine citizen participation in governance. This global initiative aims to replace top-down assumptions with cooperative leadership, ensuring that political strategies prioritize actual public needs rather than mere campaign promises. An open budgeting system empowers citizens to influence priority areas during fiscal planning, thereby significantly enhancing public trust in government institutions. As engagement with OGP grows across various states, the mechanism serves as a vital bridge between authorities and the community, validating the principle that effective governance requires continuous citizen input. The initiative is particularly relevant to open_data because it institutionalizes transparency and data accessibility as prerequisites for meaningful civic engagement. By mandating open budgets, it ensures that government financial data is available for scrutiny, enabling citizens to hold leaders accountable and attract further development funds through demonstrated accountability and cooperation.
