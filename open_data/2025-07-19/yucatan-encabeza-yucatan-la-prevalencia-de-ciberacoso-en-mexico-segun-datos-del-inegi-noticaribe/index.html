Yucatán leads Mexico in the prevalence of cyberbullying, highlighting a critical gap in regional digital safety. This surge underscores the urgent need for targeted prevention and educational strategies to protect vulnerable populations from escalating online harassment. Understanding these dynamics is essential for developing effective interventions that address specific user experiences and reduce emotional trauma. For open data initiatives, this case illustrates the importance of granular, disaggregated statistics in identifying local hotspots. Access to such detailed metrics enables policymakers to allocate resources efficiently and design evidence-based policies that effectively mitigate digital violence.
