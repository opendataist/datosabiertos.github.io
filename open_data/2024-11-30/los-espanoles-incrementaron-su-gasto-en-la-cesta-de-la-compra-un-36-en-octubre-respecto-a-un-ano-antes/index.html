Los españoles incrementaron su gasto en la cesta de la compra un 3,6% en octubre respecto a un año antes

Los españoles incrementaron su gasto en la cesta de la compra un 3,6% en octubre respecto a un año antes

Consumption in Spain shows a recovery driven by rising product prices, as the growth in the value of total sales closely matches the average increase in prices, while real demand remains stagnant. This phenomenon highlights an economic tension where households pay more for the same goods, with notable divergence across channels: e-commerce and perfumery have seen strong growth, while traditional hypermarkets have declined. The trend toward private-label brands over manufacturer brands confirms consumers’ search for savings, reflecting a more cautious and price-sensitive consumption strategy. The humanitarian impact of the Dana flood triggered an immediate surge in the consumption of basic and cleaning products, demonstrating the market’s resilience and solidarity during crises. This urgent response, followed by sustained demand for hygiene and care products, illustrates how external events can temporarily alter purchasing patterns, prioritizing essential and health-related needs over leisure or discretionary items. This information is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the need for granular, open data to analyze consumer behavior in real time. By breaking down variables such as prices, volumes, and channels, transparency in economic research and policy decision-making is enhanced, enabling the identification of structural trends and effective responses to social emergencies through accessible quantitative evidence.
