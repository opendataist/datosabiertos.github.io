Analysing the findings of the Comprehensive Annual Modular Survey (CAMS) of the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation on various Socio-Economic Indicators

Analysing the findings of the Comprehensive Annual Modular Survey (CAMS) of the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation on various Socio-Economic Indicators

The recent Comprehensive Annual Modular Survey highlights a significant digital transformation among Indian youth, particularly in mobile and internet usage. With over 95% of young rural residents capable of using mobile phones and a vast majority accessing the internet, the data reveals a narrowing urban-rural digital divide. This widespread connectivity extends beyond basic access, as a notable segment of the youth now engages in advanced tasks like online banking and information searching. This surge in digital literacy is crucial for open data initiatives, as it indicates a growing potential for citizen engagement with digital governance platforms and e-service delivery. Beyond technology, the survey underscores substantial progress in financial inclusion and basic health infrastructure. Nearly 95% of adults hold bank accounts, and access to improved drinking water and latrines has reached near-universal levels. These metrics suggest that foundational socio-economic barriers are being systematically addressed. For the open data community, these findings validate the importance of tracking diverse indicators beyond simple economic growth, emphasizing the need to monitor how digital access correlates with financial participation and public health improvements. The relevance of this article to open data lies in its demonstration of how high-quality, granular survey data can expose national development trends and guide policy refinement. By making these comprehensive indicators transparent, the government enables researchers and developers to identify gaps and opportunities in digital and social infrastructure. Ultimately, this transparency fosters accountability and supports the creation of data-driven solutions that align with broader goals of inclusive growth, ensuring that technological advancements translate into tangible social benefits for all demographic groups.
