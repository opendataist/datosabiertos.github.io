Reafirma Mara Lezama compromiso con la transparencia y el combate a la corrupción - Noticaribe

The government of Quintana Roo reaffirms its commitment to the principles of Open Government by harnessing collective societal action to promote sustainable development and combat corruption. This initiative reflects a humanist and feminist approach to governance, aiming to dismantle entrenched institutional vices through long-term, transparent efforts. By incorporating the voices of civil society, the administration seeks to build trust and ensure accountability, emphasizing that genuine transformation requires the collaborative strength of diverse ideas and actions from all citizens. The region has made progress on key commitments through co-creation platforms, focusing on strategic planning, budgetary transparency, anti-corruption measures, and environmental governance. Recent advances include partnerships with international organizations to strengthen the rule of law and the development of frameworks addressing climate crises. These efforts demonstrate a shift toward participatory policymaking, in which the public and private sectors jointly identify problems and harmonize solutions, ensuring that environmental protection and legal integrity remain central to local governance strategies. This article is highly relevant to open data, as it illustrates the practical application of transparency platforms and accountable governance in real-world policy implementation. It highlights how open data mechanisms facilitate collaboration between governments and civil society, enabling the tracking of commitments and the co-creation of solutions. By emphasizing public access to information and participatory decision-making, this case study exemplifies how open data serves as a critical tool for enhancing transparency, combating corruption, and fostering sustainable development.
