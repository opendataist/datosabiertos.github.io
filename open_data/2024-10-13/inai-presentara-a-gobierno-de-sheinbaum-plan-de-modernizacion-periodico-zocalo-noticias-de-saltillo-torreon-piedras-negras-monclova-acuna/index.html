INAI presentará a gobierno de Sheinbaum plan de modernización | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) will present a modernization plan to the new government to demonstrate its institutional value. This proposal aims to ensure the continuity of the autonomous body amid potential reforms that could eliminate it, by transforming its structure to better respond to current social demands. Dialogue with the Sheinbaum administration is crucial, as the president has shown openness to reducing the institute’s structure, while also acknowledging internal problems. The willingness to cooperate through the Ministry of the Interior allows the INAI to negotiate its future, avoiding its disappearance and focusing on a restructuring that preserves its functionality. This situation is relevant to open data because the INAI’s independence guarantees transparent access to public information. A modernized and autonomous body ensures that government data are accessible and reliable, which are essential for accountability and citizen participation in Mexico.
