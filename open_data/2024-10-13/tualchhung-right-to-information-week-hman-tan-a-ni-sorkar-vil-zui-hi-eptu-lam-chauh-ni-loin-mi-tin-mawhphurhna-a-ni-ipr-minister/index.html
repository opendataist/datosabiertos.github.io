Tualchhung - Right to Information Week hman ṭan a ni: Sorkar vil zui hi eptu lam chauh ni loin mi tin mawhphurhna a ni - IPR Minister

Tualchhung - Right to Information Week hman ṭan a ni: Sorkar vil zui hi eptu lam chauh ni loin mi tin mawhphurhna a ni - IPR Minister

The Mizoram Information Commission recently organized Right to Information Week 2024, emphasizing that access to information is not merely a privilege but a fundamental right essential for strengthening democracy and accountability. The event highlighted the necessity for government bodies to proactively disclose data, reinforcing the principle that transparency empowers citizens and fosters trust between the state and the public. Officials stressed the critical importance of timely responses and accurate data management, urging designated officers to comply strictly with disclosure mandates. By ensuring that relevant information is readily available on official platforms and that authorities acknowledge inquiries promptly, the government aims to eliminate barriers to access. This approach transforms passive governance into an active, participatory system where citizens are well-informed stakeholders. This initiative is highly relevant to the open_data movement as it underscores the legal and ethical imperative for proactive transparency. By advocating for the systematic release of public records and raising awareness about data rights, the commission supports the core open data ethos of maximizing public utility. It demonstrates how structured information disclosure can enhance civic engagement and ensure that digital governance remains inclusive and responsive to societal needs.
