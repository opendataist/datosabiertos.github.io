Matrícula de primer año en Educación Superior alcanza peak histórico a nivel nacional

Matrícula de primer año en Educación Superior alcanza peak histórico a nivel nacional

University enrollment in Chile reached historic levels in 2024, driven primarily by sustained growth in distance and blended learning modalities. This phenomenon reflects a structural shift in how the population accesses higher education, consolidating flexible scheduling and geographic accessibility as key factors for educational inclusion. At the same time, there is a significant reorientation toward technical training centers and professional degree programs, while interest in traditional technical careers and humanities fields declines. State-run technical institutions show particular dynamism, surpassing conventional universities in growth rates, which indicates a greater appreciation for technical training and its alignment with immediate labor market demands. This information is crucial for the open data movement because it exemplifies the value of government transparency in decision-making. By making these data disaggregated by gender, modality, and institution accessible, independent analysis by academics, citizens, and policymakers is facilitated. This openness enables the identification of real trends, evaluation of public policies, and promotion of an informed citizenry capable of demanding improvements based on concrete evidence.
