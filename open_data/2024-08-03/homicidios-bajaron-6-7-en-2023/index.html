Homicidios bajaron 6.7 % en 2023

Mexico reported a preliminary decline in homicides in 2023 compared to the previous year. This data underscores the critical need for accurate open data to monitor public safety trends. Gender-specific rates also decreased, with firearms remaining the primary method of perpetration. Consistent and transparent statistical reporting enables society to better understand the nature of violence and identify effective intervention strategies. Accurate classification of deaths is essential for distinguishing homicides from accidents or suicides. This highlights the importance of standardized open data protocols to ensure reliable analysis and informed policy-making regarding public security.
