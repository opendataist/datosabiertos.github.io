Actividad económica en retroceso: la venta de vino cayó en junio y cerró un semestre con números negativos

The wine industry is experiencing a sustained decline in sales, driven by the economic recession and the loss of purchasing power. Although the drop in wine consumption is less drastic compared to other alcoholic beverages, the negative trend persists both in the domestic market, which absorbs the majority of production, and in exports, which are affected by financial instability and a lack of international competitiveness. This situation reveals a structural transformation in global consumption habits. Consumers increasingly prefer beverages with lower alcohol content or mixed drinks with soft drinks, while demand for non-alcoholic options is growing. These changes are forcing the industry to reconsider the composition of its cultivated varieties and the quality of the final product, as dealcoholization can compromise the traditional organoleptic characteristics of wine. This analysis is relevant to open_data because it illustrates how economic and sectoral consumption data reflect complex market dynamics that require continuous monitoring. Transparency in the disclosure of wine statistics enables the identification not only of sales contractions but also of structural shifts in demand, facilitating strategic decision-making for producers and regulators in a changing and less favorable global environment.
