Open Data
ES EN

The National Transparency Platform democratizes access to public information by streamlining procedures through a secure digital tool. Its relevance to the open data movement lies in its ability to transform a constitutional guarantee into an efficient administrative process, ensuring the protection of requesters’ personal data and fostering institutional transparency by directly connecting citizens with obligated entities. The system prioritizes ease of use and privacy, enabling anyone to exercise their right to know without revealing their real identity. By allowing anonymity or the use of pseudonyms, the platform eliminates administrative barriers and encourages citizen participation without fear of retaliation, which is essential for building a robust and accessible culture of accountability. The automated process significantly reduces response times and bureaucracy, establishing clear deadlines for obligated entities to issue responses. This standardization not only accelerates data retrieval but also sets a technical precedent for interoperability and the standardization of public information, which are essential pillars for evolving toward more robust and reliable open data ecosystems.
