El paro cae en 10.830 personas en julio y roza los 2,5 millones, la cifra más baja desde 2008

Spanish labor data reveal a sustained decline in unemployment, reaching its lowest level since 2008. This trend signals a robust recovery in the job market, driven by consistent reductions in registered job seekers and historic lows in youth unemployment. Although the rate of decline is moderating, the overall trajectory points to a healthier employment landscape that supports stability in consumer spending. At the same time, the contraction in labor contracts indicates strong hiring activity, with a significant rise in permanent positions. This shift toward job security, along with expanded social protection coverage, highlights structural improvements in the workforce. The balance between temporary and permanent contracts reflects a market that is gradually stabilizing, offering greater predictability for both workers and employers while boosting economic confidence. This dataset is crucial for open-data initiatives, as it provides transparent, high-frequency metrics for civic analysis. By making granular employment and social security information accessible, researchers and policymakers can examine economic health beyond headline figures. Such transparency empowers data-driven journalism and accountability, enabling society to understand the real-world impact of labor policies and to identify regional disparities effectively.
