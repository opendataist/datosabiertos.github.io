UGT destaca el descenso del paro entre mujeres en CLM en julio

The article highlights positive employment growth in Castilla-La Mancha, driven by agricultural campaigns and an increase in social security registrations. However, it underscores the persistence of structural challenges, such as long-term unemployment and gender pay gaps. These disparities call for a more inclusive labor market to ensure sustainable progress. To address these challenges, the union advocates for enhanced career counseling and support services. The establishment of a new employment guidance center represents a significant step toward improving resilience and inclusivity for job seekers. This content is relevant to open data because public employment statistics serve as the foundation for monitoring these trends. Transparent access to such data enables citizens and organizations to analyze labor market dynamics, hold institutions accountable, and propose evidence-based solutions to persistent social inequalities.
