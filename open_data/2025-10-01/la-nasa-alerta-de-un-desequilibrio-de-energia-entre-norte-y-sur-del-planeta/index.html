La NASA alerta de un desequilibrio de energía entre norte y sur del planeta

La NASA alerta de un desequilibrio de energía entre norte y sur del planeta

NASA researchers have revealed that hemispheric symmetry in Earth’s albedo has been broken, with the Northern Hemisphere darkening more rapidly and emitting more heat into space. This energy imbalance alters global climate patterns, affecting extreme weather events and oceanic systems, suggesting that current climate projections may be inadequate. This finding is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates the need for robust and continuous satellite observation systems. Modern science relies on open, verified, and accessible data to detect subtle changes in the Earth system that are not evident in the short term. Transparency in these datasets enables the scientific community to validate hypotheses, model future impacts with greater precision, and develop evidence-based mitigation responses. Without open access to climate information, the capacity to understand and mitigate these planetary alterations would be significantly constrained.
