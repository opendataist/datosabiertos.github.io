La Anmat prohibió la venta de dos aceites de oliva

La Anmat prohibió la venta de dos aceites de oliva

The Argentine health authority banned two olive oil brands after discovering they used false registration numbers, labeling them as counterfeit. This strict enforcement highlights the critical need for transparent, open data systems to verify product authenticity and protect consumer safety. Such regulatory actions demonstrate how accessible public databases allow authorities to detect irregularities efficiently. Open data initiatives are essential for creating the digital infrastructure required to maintain trust in food supply chains and ensure compliance with national health standards. Ultimately, this case underscores the vital role of information transparency in combating fraud. By making registration data openly available, governments can empower consumers and journalists to hold corporations accountable, reinforcing democratic oversight over essential goods and public health regulations.
