Cae cifra de matrimonios adolescentes en Chiapas

Chiapas recorded a historic low in child marriages in 2024, directly reflecting the impact of legal reforms that raised the minimum age for marriage. This significant demographic shift underscores how legislative adjustments can effectively curb early marriages, marking a decisive step toward protecting minors and aligning civil practices with contemporary human rights standards. The state also reported a notably low divorce rate, with separations occurring at less than half the national average. This stability in marital unions suggests strong social cohesion or specific cultural factors influencing the durability of relationships. These metrics offer a clearer picture of family dynamics, moving beyond simple counts to help assess the health of civil institutions within the community. This data is crucial for open-data initiatives, as it demonstrates the tangible effects of policy changes on societal behavior. By making such granular statistics accessible, researchers and policymakers can analyze causal links between legislation and social outcomes. Transparent access to this information fosters evidence-based decision-making and enables continuous monitoring of the effectiveness of civil code reforms across different regions.
