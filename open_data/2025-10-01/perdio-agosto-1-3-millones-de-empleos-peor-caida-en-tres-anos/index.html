Perdió agosto 1.3 millones de empleos… peor caída en tres años

Mexico’s labor market exhibits significant weakness, with August recording the highest monthly job losses since early 2022. Extended unemployment increased as the inactive population grew, driven by sharp declines in the services and commerce sectors, signaling a broader economic downturn. This report is crucial for open data advocates because it underscores the urgent need for transparent, granular employment datasets. Reliable public statistics facilitate better policy analysis and civic oversight, ensuring that societal challenges are accurately monitored and addressed through evidence-based decision-making.
