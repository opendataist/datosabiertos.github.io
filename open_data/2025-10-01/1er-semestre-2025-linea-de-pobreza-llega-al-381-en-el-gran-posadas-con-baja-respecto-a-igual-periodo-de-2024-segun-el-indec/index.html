1er Semestre 2025: Línea de pobreza llega al 38,1% en el Gran Posadas, con baja respecto a igual período de 2024 según el Indec

The latest INDEC report reveals a substantial decline in poverty and extreme poverty in Gran Posadas during the first half of 2025 compared to the previous year. This significant improvement in social indicators demonstrates the effectiveness of current economic policies or structural shifts, offering a hopeful narrative for vulnerable populations. Relevant to open data, this case underscores the importance of transparent, standardized statistical methodologies, such as the Permanent Household Survey, in measuring societal well-being. Access to such granular, officially validated data allows researchers, journalists, and citizens to track progress and hold institutions accountable. Ultimately, the availability of these detailed metrics facilitates informed public discourse and evidence-based decision-making. By making poverty lines clearly defined and publicly accessible, open data initiatives empower communities to understand socio-economic realities and advocate for targeted, effective interventions.
