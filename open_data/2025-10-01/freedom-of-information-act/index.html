Freedom of Information Act

This document outlines Illinois Eastern Community College’s commitment to transparency and compliance with the state’s Freedom of Information Act. It serves as a practical guide for the public to access institutional records, ensuring that the right to information is balanced against individual privacy protections. By detailing the college’s structure, purpose, and governance, the text establishes the organizational context necessary for understanding what records are available and how they are maintained. The text provides clear procedural instructions for submitting records requests, including specific requirements for written submissions and designated contact information for the FOIA officer. It emphasizes the importance of specificity in requests and outlines the timeline for responses, along with potential fees and penalties for commercial use. This structured approach simplifies the process for citizens and researchers seeking data, reducing barriers to accessing institutional knowledge. This information is crucial for the open data community because it highlights the importance of standardized, accessible channels for obtaining public sector data. It demonstrates how educational institutions can operationalize transparency laws to make budget, meeting, and operational records available to the public. Such frameworks support data journalism, academic research, and civic oversight, reinforcing the principle that public resources should be easily auditable and understandable by all stakeholders.
