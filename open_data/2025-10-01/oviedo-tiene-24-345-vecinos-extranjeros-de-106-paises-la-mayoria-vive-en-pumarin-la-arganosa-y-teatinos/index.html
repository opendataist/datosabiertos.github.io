Oviedo tiene 24.345 vecinos extranjeros de 106 países: la mayoría vive en Pumarín, La Argañosa y Teatinos

Oviedo tiene 24.345 vecinos extranjeros de 106 países: la mayoría vive en Pumarín, La Argañosa y Teatinos

The publication of Oviedo’s 2025 municipal registry highlights a significant demographic shift toward multiculturalism, revealing that over ten percent of the population consists of foreign residents or dual nationals. This influx, originating from more than a hundred countries, underscores the city’s transformation into a global mosaic, with strong Latin American and Eastern European influences reshaping its social fabric. Geographically, this diversity is unevenly distributed, concentrating heavily in major residential neighborhoods while remaining minimal in others. The data illustrates how specific districts act as primary hubs for international communities, fostering localized cultural integration, whereas other areas remain predominantly homogenous. This spatial variation offers critical insights into urban settlement patterns and the practical challenges of managing diverse communities across different municipal zones. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how granular, localized information can drive effective public policy. By making detailed demographic breakdowns accessible, authorities and researchers can better analyze urban planning, social service allocation, and integration strategies. Transparent access to such precise civic data empowers stakeholders to address the real-world implications of migration and urban diversity with evidence-based solutions.
