Website guide - Eurostat

Website guide - Eurostat

Eurostat has established a comprehensive digital infrastructure that guarantees free, unrestricted access to its entire suite of statistical databases, analytical tools, and educational resources. By centralizing these assets on a single, user-friendly platform, the institution removes significant barriers to information, allowing citizens, researchers, and policymakers to retrieve complex data without financial or bureaucratic impediments. This approach fundamentally supports the principles of transparency and public accountability, ensuring that authoritative European statistics are readily available to anyone seeking to understand socio-economic trends. The website’s multifaceted design caters to diverse user needs by offering specialized navigation through thematic sections, visualizations, and advanced retrieval systems like geographical information tools and microdata access. Alongside raw data, Eurostat provides rich contextual support through *Statistics Explained*, a wiki that clarifies terminology and concepts for both experts and laypeople. This integration of raw data with explanatory narratives empowers users to interpret information accurately, fostering a more informed public discourse and enabling better decision-making based on reliable, high-quality evidence. This platform is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the ideal of making government-held information accessible, usable, and reusable by default. By providing multiple formats for data extraction and clear guidelines on usage, Eurostat facilitates secondary analysis and innovation, allowing developers and academics to build upon official statistics. The commitment to free access not only aligns with global open data standards but also strengthens the European Statistical System by encouraging widespread engagement with official data, thereby enhancing the societal value of public investment in statistical infrastructure.

Source: ec.europa.eu
