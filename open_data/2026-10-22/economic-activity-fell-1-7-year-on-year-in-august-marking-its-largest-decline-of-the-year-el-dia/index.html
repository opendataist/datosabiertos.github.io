Economic activity fell 1.7% year-on-year in August, marking its largest decline of the year | El Dia

Economic activity fell 1.7% year-on-year in August, marking its largest decline of the year | El Dia

Argentina’s economic activity showed a significant contraction in August, marking the largest year-on-year decline of the year and the second consecutive monthly drop, driven primarily by the industrial and commercial sectors. Despite this temporary setback, the year-to-date figure remains in positive growth, and the seasonally adjusted data reveal a slight monthly expansion, suggesting mixed recovery signals amid the recent negative trend. The government highlighted these seasonally adjusted indicators to underscore medium-term growth, contrasting them with the reality of the year-on-year decline affecting most productive sectors. While mining and basic services led gains, key activities such as agriculture, construction, and commerce recorded concerning contractions, with the fishing sector experiencing one of the smallest declines. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how access to disaggregated official statistics enables analysis of real economic performance beyond political narratives. The availability of detailed sectoral data and different methodological approaches (such as seasonally adjusted figures) facilitates transparency, allowing citizens and experts to verify economic health and question official interpretations based on selective metrics.
