Open High School registration available in Michoacán: Gaby Molina

Open High School registration available in Michoacán: Gaby Molina

Gaby Molina highlights the opening of two free, officially recognized educational pathways in Mexico for completing high school: Prepa en Línea and the Margarita Maza system. These options democratize access to upper secondary education by eliminating geographic and age barriers through flexible modalities and teacher support. The initiative promotes social inclusion by enabling adults and young people to obtain official certifications in reduced timeframes or within their local communities. Furthermore, by linking with economic support programs such as the Benito Juárez Scholarship, it helps reduce the economic inequality that often prevents vulnerable populations from continuing their studies. This announcement is relevant to open data because the Mexican government publishes official requirements and links, promoting transparency. The availability of clear information on eligibility and procedures encourages citizens to verify data and participate, thereby strengthening accountability and access to digital public services.
