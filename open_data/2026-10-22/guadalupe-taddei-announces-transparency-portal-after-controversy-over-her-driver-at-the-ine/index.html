Guadalupe Taddei announces transparency portal after controversy over her driver at the INE

Guadalupe Taddei announces transparency portal after controversy over her driver at the INE

The president of the National Electoral Institute (INE) announced the creation of a transparency portal to obscure the purchases and contracts related to the electoral process. This measure comes in response to journalistic allegations of suspicious links between the president’s driver and a supplier company under investigation for money laundering and anti-competitive practices, highlighting potential weaknesses in the electoral body’s internal security and management. The scandal reveals that multiple INE contractors are being investigated by tax and competition authorities, suggesting they may be serving as conduits for illicit funds. Irregularities have been detected in the organization’s hierarchical personnel structure, where employees in basic roles gain access to higher administrative levels, compromising the integrity of public procurement processes and undermining public confidence in the institution. This case is crucial for the open data movement, as it underscores the urgent need to publish detailed and accessible public information. Proactive transparency enables citizens and independent researchers to audit electoral spending, reduce opacity in contracting, and ensure that democratic resources are used properly, thereby strengthening institutional accountability.
