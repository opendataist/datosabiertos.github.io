Inicio | Portal de datos de indicadores de los ODS de la FAO | Organización de las Naciones Unidas para la Alimentación y la Agricultura

The FAO supports global monitoring of the Sustainable Development Goals through its custodianship of key indicators. This portal provides essential methodological data and results to help countries track progress on the 2030 Agenda. The initiative underscores the critical role of international organizations in standardizing and disseminating open data for policy evaluation.
