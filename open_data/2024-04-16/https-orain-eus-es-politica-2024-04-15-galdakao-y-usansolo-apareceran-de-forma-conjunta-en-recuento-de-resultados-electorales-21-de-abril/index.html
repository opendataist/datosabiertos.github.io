The separation of Usansolo from Galdakao coincided with a fixed electoral census, forcing a joint vote recount for the upcoming regional election. This administrative timing overlap means some Galdakao residents near the border will inadvertently vote in Usansolo, creating complex counting dynamics that highlight the challenges of aligning administrative changes with fixed electoral data. This scenario illustrates a critical open data issue: when demographic updates lag behind legal definitions, existing datasets become misaligned with current realities. Such discrepancies demonstrate the vital need for synchronized, real-time data integration between civil registry systems and electoral rolls to ensure accurate representation and prevent procedural errors in governance.
