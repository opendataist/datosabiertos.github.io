Las exportaciones crecieron significativamente en 2024 en los puertos de Bahía y Rosales

The ports of Bahía Blanca and Puerto Rosales demonstrated robust logistical performance in 2024, marked by a significant surge in total merchandise movement. This growth was primarily driven by substantial increases in grain exports, alongside notable rises in subproducts and vegetable oils. Conversely, the sector experienced a slight contraction in chemical and flammable cargoes, as well as terrestrial merchandise flows, indicating a shifting dynamic within specific industrial segments. These trends highlight the increasing strategic importance of the local estuary area in Argentina’s export supply chain. The data reveals a divergence in commodity performance, with traditional agricultural staples maintaining strong momentum while other sectors face volatility. This disparity underscores the resilience of key production areas and suggests that port infrastructure is adapting to prioritize high-volume agricultural outputs over other less dynamic categories. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparent, publicly available port monitors enable precise tracking of regional economic health. By making granular shipment statistics accessible, such reports allow stakeholders to analyze supply chain efficiency and identify emerging trends. Consequently, this transparency supports evidence-based decision-making for logistics, policy formulation, and investment, demonstrating the tangible societal value of standardized, open commercial datasets.
