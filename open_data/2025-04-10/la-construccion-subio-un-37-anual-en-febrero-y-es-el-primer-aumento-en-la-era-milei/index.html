La construcción subió un 3,7% anual en febrero y es el primer aumento en la era Milei

La construcción subió un 3,7% anual en febrero y es el primer aumento en la era Milei

The National Statistics Institute reports a modest recovery in Argentina's construction sector, marking the first growth since the new administration took office. This increase signals a stabilization in economic activity, offering a preliminary sign that the severe contraction experienced earlier is easing. While employment data remains weak, the uptick in physical output suggests a fragile but tangible shift in momentum for the industry. Business expectations for the upcoming quarter are generally optimistic, with a majority of private firms predicting stable or increased activity. This confidence is largely attributed to macroeconomic factors such as price stability and a broader recovery in economic activity. For companies focused on public works, the anticipation of restarting infrastructure projects serves as a key driver for this positive outlook, indicating a potential alignment between government policy and sectoral needs. This development is relevant to open data as it illustrates how independent statistical indicators, such as the Construya index, complement official census data to provide a real-time pulse on the economy. By tracking material sales and business sentiment, these datasets offer granular insights into recovery trends that are often delayed in traditional government reports. Analyzing such diverse sources allows for a more comprehensive understanding of structural shifts and policy impacts in critical sectors like construction.
