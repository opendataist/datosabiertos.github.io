Comenzó la Diplomatura en Propiedad Intelectual, Nuevas Tecnologías, Fashion Law, Inteligencia Artificial e Industrias Creativas

Comenzó la Diplomatura en Propiedad Intelectual, Nuevas Tecnologías, Fashion Law, Inteligencia Artificial e Industrias Creativas

This article highlights the launch of a comprehensive postgraduate diploma in Intellectual Property and New Technologies, offered online by Universidad FASTA. The program, which has attracted students from across Latin America and beyond, aims to position the university as an international reference in specialized legal and technological education. By delivering intensive, high-level coursework from Mar del Plata, the institution bridges geographical gaps, allowing professionals from diverse regions to access premier academic resources. The curriculum addresses critical intersections between copyright, artificial intelligence, and creative industries, taught by distinguished experts in the field. This educational initiative responds to the rapid evolution of digital tools and the increasing need for robust frameworks to protect intangible heritage and foster innovation. The participation of professionals from multiple countries underscores the growing demand for specialized knowledge that transcends national borders, reflecting a shared regional challenge in navigating modern intellectual property landscapes. This development is relevant to open data because it emphasizes the urgent necessity for clear legal frameworks governing information ownership and usage. As digital economies expand, understanding how to balance intellectual property rights with the free flow of data becomes essential for sustainable innovation. The program illustrates how academic institutions are adapting to equip legal and technical professionals with the tools needed to manage these complex dynamics, ensuring that open data practices align with ethical and legal standards in a globally connected world.
