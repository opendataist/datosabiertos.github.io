Coches conectados: mantén a salvo tus datos

The article highlights a critical privacy crisis surrounding connected vehicles, revealing that most car manufacturers collect extensive personal data and share or sell it without providing users adequate control. This indiscriminate data gathering encompasses sensitive information, including demographic details and intimate lifestyle aspects, turning personal mobility into a significant risk for privacy infringement. Beyond privacy concerns, the exponential growth of connected cars exposes them to severe cybersecurity threats. The increasing interconnectivity with infrastructure and remote services creates multiple vulnerabilities, making these vehicles attractive targets for cyberattacks that compromise not only the drivers but also broader traffic systems. This expansion underscores the urgent need for robust, specific security measures integrated directly into the design phase of these smart vehicles. This topic is vital to open data because it exposes the opaque nature of data ecosystems in emerging technologies. It demonstrates how data flows often lack transparency and user consent, challenging the principles of accountability and accessibility. The article advocates for "privacy by design" and by default, urging that regulatory frameworks must evolve alongside technological innovations to ensure that data collection respects individual rights and ethical standards in an increasingly data-driven world.
