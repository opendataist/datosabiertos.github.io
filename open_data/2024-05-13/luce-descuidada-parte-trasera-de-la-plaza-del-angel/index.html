Neglected urban infrastructure, such as broken sidewalks and exposed utilities, creates significant pedestrian hazards in high-traffic historic areas. The lack of timely maintenance and visible warnings jeopardizes safety, particularly for vulnerable groups like children and the elderly. This situation underscores the critical need for open data initiatives to track municipal maintenance issues. By making repair records and hazard reports publicly accessible, communities can demand accountability and ensure safer urban environments through transparency and informed civic engagement.
