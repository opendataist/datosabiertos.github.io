Worldcoin ya permite eliminar la verificación del escaneo del iris y mejora la verificación de la edad

Worldcoin has suspended its operations in Spain and Portugal following orders from data protection authorities, underscoring the tension between biometric recognition technologies and Europe’s stringent regulatory framework. This conflict highlights the critical importance of transparency and accountability in the realm of open data, where the mass collection of sensitive information demands rigorous public scrutiny to ensure that digital tools do not infringe upon fundamental privacy rights. To restore trust and comply with the legal framework, the initiative has introduced mechanisms that empower users through active management of their personal information. These measures include age verification without storing biometric data and the ability to permanently delete iris codes after a reflection period. This approach reflects a paradigm shift toward data sovereignty, demonstrating how platforms can evolve to give citizens real control over their digital footprints in an increasingly surveilled digital environment. The relevance to the open data movement lies in how this case illustrates the need to balance technological innovation with ethics and regulation. Worldcoin’s response serves as a case study on the importance of integrating data protection by design, fostering an ecosystem where transparency is not optional but a fundamental pillar. This underscores the urgency for emerging technologies to prioritize user trust and responsible governance, essential elements for building sustainable and socially acceptable digital systems.
