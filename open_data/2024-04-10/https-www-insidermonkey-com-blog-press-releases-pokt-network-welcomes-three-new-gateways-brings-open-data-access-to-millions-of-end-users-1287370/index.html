POKT Network has integrated Developer DAO, Porters, and Chainstack as new Gateways, significantly expanding decentralized access to blockchain data. This move establishes a scalable model where independent entities can resell infrastructure without maintaining underlying nodes. By decoupling protocol maintenance from service delivery, the network enables faster innovation and lower operational costs for developers, fostering a more robust and accessible ecosystem for Web3 applications. The strategic shift toward this Gateway architecture allows businesses to focus on user experience and front-end development while relying on POKT’s resilient, decentralized physical infrastructure for reliability. This separation of roles ensures that builders can leverage enterprise-grade quality of service on a permissionless network. Consequently, the model promotes collaboration over competition, distributing benefits across a broader range of businesses and enhancing the overall efficiency of blockchain data access. This development is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates a viable path for uncensoring and universalizing access to vast digital resources. By proving that decentralized infrastructure can efficiently support high-volume requests across numerous blockchains, POKT sets a precedent for other DePINs. The upcoming Shannon upgrade further reinforces this by enabling permissionless gateway creation, ensuring that open data remains secure, reliable, and accessible for emerging use cases like artificial intelligence and machine-to-machine communication.
