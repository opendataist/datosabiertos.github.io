Producción de industria uruguaya crece 3,6% en 2022

Producción de industria uruguaya crece 3,6% en 2022

Uruguay’s manufacturing sector expanded significantly in 2022, driven primarily by growth in food processing. While the automotive and wood industries showed strong gains, the pharmaceutical and paper sectors experienced notable declines, indicating a mixed industrial performance despite the overall positive trend. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it highlights the importance of accessible, granular economic statistics for analyzing sectoral trends. Transparent publication of such indices allows researchers and policymakers to track specific industry contributions and regional economic health effectively. Understanding these detailed industrial metrics supports evidence-based decision-making. By making such localized data available, stakeholders can better assess economic impacts, fostering a more informed public discourse and enhancing the utility of open government information for both academic and practical applications.
