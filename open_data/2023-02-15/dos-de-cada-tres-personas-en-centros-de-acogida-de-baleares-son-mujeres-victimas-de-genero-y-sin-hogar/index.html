The study highlights that housing scarcity in Mallorca acts as the primary barrier for women in residential exclusion. It identifies migration, past gender-based violence, childhood institutionalization, and lack of social support as key vulnerability factors. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, enabling better analysis of intersectional risks and improving policy responses for marginalized populations.
