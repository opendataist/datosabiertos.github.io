Reporta Inegi ligero avance del empleo manufacturero en 2022

Mexico’s manufacturing sector exhibited modest employment growth, sustaining a five-month upward trend fueled by specific industries such as transportation and metals. Although overall gains were slight, this signals stabilizing labor conditions, contrasting with contractions in the textile and wood sectors and suggesting a fragmented yet resilient industrial landscape rather than a broad-based collapse. The rise in worked hours and real wages indicates improving productivity and income levels for workers, despite uneven distribution across sub-sectors. This nuanced data reveals that while the sector is expanding, the benefits are not uniformly shared, highlighting structural shifts within the industrial core of the Mexican economy. This article is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how granular, publicly accessible statistics from institutions like INEGI enable researchers to move beyond headline figures. By analyzing detailed indicators, stakeholders can uncover hidden trends in employment and wages, facilitating more accurate policy decisions and economic forecasts based on comprehensive, transparent datasets.
