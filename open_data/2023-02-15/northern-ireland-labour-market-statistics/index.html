Northern Ireland Labour Market Statistics

The Northern Ireland labor market demonstrates gradual recovery, characterized by rising payrolled employment and earnings across key sectors such as information and professional services. While wages have increased annually, the claimant count has stabilized, indicating that while job creation is evident, the region has not yet fully regained its pre-pandemic workforce levels. This data highlights the resilience of specific high-value industries amidst broader economic adjustments. Simultaneously, labor force metrics show statistically significant improvements in employment rates and a decrease in economic inactivity. Despite these positive trends in job participation and total hours worked, unemployment remains slightly above historical baselines, and overall work hours still lag behind pre-pandemic figures. The persistence of redundancies, though at lower historical totals, suggests ongoing structural shifts in the local economy rather than a complete return to previous stability. This dataset is critically relevant to open data because it provides high-resolution, transparent metrics for analyzing economic health and policy effectiveness. By publishing detailed breakdowns of employment, earnings, and inactivity, the agency enables researchers and developers to build tools that track regional disparities and inform decisions. Open access to such granular labor statistics fosters accountability and supports evidence-based planning for future workforce development initiatives.
