Guardavidas para playas accesibles: crean un rol clave para que más personas puedan disfrutar del mar

Guardavidas para playas accesibles: crean un rol clave para que más personas puedan disfrutar del mar

Mar del Plata officially recognizes the role of the accessible beach lifeguard, acknowledging the need to assist people with reduced mobility. This institutionalizes a previously unregulated effort, ensuring specialized support for safe water entry. By defining specific requirements and mandatory training in inclusive safety, the city guarantees consistent, high-quality assistance for all users. The initiative mandates specialized certification focused on adaptive rescue techniques and the use of accessible equipment. This structured approach ensures that lifeguards possess the necessary skills to assist individuals with varying disabilities, transforming beach access from a privilege into a guaranteed right. This policy is vital for open data, as it establishes a framework for tracking accessible infrastructure and personnel records. Publishing this data enables transparency, allowing users to identify qualified services and promotes the standardization of accessibility information across municipal services.
