¿Por qué se venden menos viviendas en Baleares si hay tantas personas que buscan un hogar?

¿Por qué se venden menos viviendas en Baleares si hay tantas personas que buscan un hogar?

The Balearic Islands are facing an affordability crisis, where stagnant wages and high rents have created pent-up demand. Residents want to buy homes but are excluded by stricter mortgage requirements, which reduces transaction volumes without necessarily lowering prices. The supply of affordable housing is scarce, creating a structural gap between real economic capacity and real estate costs. The market is temporarily stagnating due to tighter lending standards from financial institutions and a lack of household liquidity, but it does not anticipate significant downward price corrections. Sellers are maintaining firm prices, aware of the high latent demographic pressure. This situation prolongs sales times, transforming the current dynamic market into a slower and more rigid one, as long as the wage disparity persists. This analysis is relevant to open data because it highlights the need for transparent and accessible data on prices, wages, and credit. The lack of empirical visibility makes it difficult to formulate precise public policies to address the misalignment between income and housing costs. Access to open information enables monitoring of market health and designing evidence-based interventions that facilitate real access to housing.
