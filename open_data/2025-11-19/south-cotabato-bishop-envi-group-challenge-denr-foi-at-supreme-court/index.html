South Cotabato bishop, envi group challenge DENR FOI at Supreme Court

South Cotabato bishop, envi group challenge DENR FOI at Supreme Court

The Diocese of Marbel and the Legal Rights and Natural Resources Center challenged the Department of Environment and Natural Resources’ Freedom of Information manual, arguing it unlawfully restricts public access to critical environmental data. By creating twenty-seven exceptions, the manual effectively shields environmental impact statements and mining application documents from public scrutiny, limiting transparency in projects like mining and flood control. This legal battle highlights a significant tension between government secrecy and the public’s right to know regarding ecological risks. The petitioners seek to have the manual declared unconstitutional, asserting that withholding such information prevents citizens from understanding the true environmental costs of industrial activities. This move aims to restore open access to documents essential for assessing ecological compliance. This case is highly relevant to open data advocates as it underscores the necessity of robust, inclusive transparency frameworks. It demonstrates how administrative regulations can inadvertently or intentionally obscure vital data, thereby hindering civic engagement and environmental accountability. Ensuring that environmental information remains freely accessible is crucial for maintaining public trust and enabling informed decision-making in public health and resource management.
