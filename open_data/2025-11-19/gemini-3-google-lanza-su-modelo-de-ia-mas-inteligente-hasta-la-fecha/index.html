Gemini 3: Google lanza su modelo de IA "más inteligente" hasta la fecha

Google has unveiled its new artificial intelligence model, positioning it as a global leader in multimodal understanding. This technology represents a qualitative leap by integrating the simultaneous processing of text, images, and audio, enabling machines to grasp contextual nuances and generate complex interactive experiences. This advancement aims to compete directly with other companies in the sector by offering a tool capable of “reading the room” and reasoning with greater depth. The significance of this update lies in its immediate accessibility for users and developers, as well as its educational focus. By providing free resources to students and new development platforms, Google democratizes access to advanced capabilities. This fosters the creation of innovative solutions and facilitates learning, transforming how we interact with information and how AI-based applications are built. For the open data community, this rollout is crucial. Improvements in reasoning and coding enable the analysis of complex datasets with greater accuracy and the generation of meaningful visualizations. Multimodal capabilities open up possibilities for enriching public data with multimedia context, enhancing transparency and citizen participation through more intuitive and powerful tools.
