Con prepa o carrera terminada, 60% de los que no hallan trabajo

The article reveals a significant rise in unemployment among Mexican youth with secondary and higher education degrees, marking a three-year high. This trend underscores a critical mismatch between educational attainment and labor market needs, driven by a lack of practical experience and technological skills. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it highlights the urgent need for high-quality, accessible employment statistics. Such transparency allows policymakers and researchers to identify skill gaps and develop targeted interventions to better align workforce development with economic demands. By exposing these structural challenges, the findings advocate for improved data dissemination to support evidence-based decisions. This helps organizations and governments address the root causes of youth unemployment, fostering a more resilient and skilled labor market through informed strategy and resource allocation.
