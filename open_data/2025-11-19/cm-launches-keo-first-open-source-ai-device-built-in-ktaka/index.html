CM launches KEO, first open-source AI device built in K’taka

CM launches KEO, first open-source AI device built in K’taka

Karnataka has launched the KEO, a compact, open-source AI computer designed to bridge the digital divide by providing affordable computing power to students and households. By leveraging indigenous RISC-V processors and offline AI capabilities, the state aims to overcome infrastructure gaps where smartphone usage far exceeds computer ownership, ensuring equitable access to essential digital education and coding resources. This initiative highlights the critical role of open-source hardware in democratizing technology, allowing for local manufacturing and reduced dependency on volatile global supply chains. The project’s success demonstrates how government-private partnerships can accelerate innovation, with major industrial leaders already committing to sponsor devices for schools, thereby validating the model’s scalability and societal impact. For the open data community, this development underscores the importance of accessible, transparent technological foundations. By prioritizing open-source standards and local fabrication, Karnataka sets a precedent for how regions can harness independent data infrastructure to foster digital literacy. It illustrates a replicable blueprint for other states seeking to leverage technology for inclusive growth without sacrificing data sovereignty or affordability.
