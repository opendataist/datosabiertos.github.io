Descubre imágenes en exclusiva del cometa 3I/ATLAS: cuándo, hora y por dónde verlas gratis

Descubre imágenes en exclusiva del cometa 3I/ATLAS: cuándo, hora y por dónde verlas gratis

NASA is organizing a free, multi-platform live broadcast to share unprecedented images and scientific data from the interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS. This event aims to democratize access to cutting-edge astronomical information, enabling global audiences to participate in real time through open channels such as YouTube and the agency’s official website. The initiative underscores the commitment of scientific institutions to transparency and accessible public outreach, removing economic and technical barriers for citizens. The scientific interest lies in the fact that this object, originating from another stellar system, offers unique insights into the chemistry and dynamics of interstellar bodies. Although any threat to Earth has been ruled out, studying it with globally distributed instruments allows researchers to analyze volatile compounds and natural physical processes, dispelling speculation about artificial activities. International collaboration in tracking the comet strengthens our collective understanding of cosmic origins beyond our solar system. This initiative is significant for the open data movement, as it demonstrates how government agencies can leverage open digital channels to disseminate specialized knowledge. By prioritizing universal availability and unrestricted public engagement, it establishes a model of data governance in which science becomes a common good. This approach not only educates the public but also strengthens institutional trust through direct exposure and free access to high-impact research findings.
