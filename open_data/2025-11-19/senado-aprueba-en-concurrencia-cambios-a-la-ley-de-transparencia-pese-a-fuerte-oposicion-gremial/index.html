Senado aprueba en concurrencia cambios a la Ley de Transparencia pese a fuerte oposición gremial

Senado aprueba en concurrencia cambios a la Ley de Transparencia pese a fuerte oposición gremial

The Senate approved a series of legislative measures aimed at strengthening legal frameworks and improving public administration. Key updates include harsher penalties for the misuse of pyrotechnics and construction fraud, alongside significant reforms in state real estate management to eliminate bureaucratic redundancy. These changes prioritize justice and efficiency by aligning punishments with current penal codes and consolidating oversight of government properties under streamlined agencies. Further legislation focuses on social welfare and municipal regulations. New laws enhance rights for individuals with functional diversity and seniors through unified benefits and identification systems, while also addressing fiscal responsibilities regarding municipal tax assessments. Additionally, public safety and environmental initiatives were advanced, including the distribution of medical tourniquets to police officers and the protection of electric vehicle charging infrastructure from unauthorized parking. These updates are relevant to open data because they necessitate the modernization of public records and administrative transparency. Efficient property management and unified citizen identification systems require robust, accessible data infrastructures to ensure accuracy and accountability. By centralizing oversight and clarifying legal responsibilities, these laws create a clearer framework for how public sector data is generated, managed, and shared, supporting better governance and civic access.
