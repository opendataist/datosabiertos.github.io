La AEPD publica una guía para ayudar a autónomos y pymes a tratar la información personal de forma más segura - LegalToday

La AEPD publica una guía para ayudar a autónomos y pymes a tratar la información personal de forma más segura - LegalToday

The Spanish Data Protection Agency has published a practical encryption guide for self-employed individuals and small businesses, emphasizing that cryptographic techniques are essential for mitigating the damages caused by data breaches. By converting information into an unreadable format without the appropriate decryption key, organizations can significantly reduce the risks associated with unauthorized access, lost devices, or accidental data leaks, thereby safeguarding the confidentiality and integrity of personal information. The analysis draws on real enforcement cases to demonstrate that the lack of security measures often leads to severe consequences for individuals. The guide offers concrete solutions that would have prevented or mitigated these harms, reinforcing the importance of adopting principles such as data minimization. This ensures that only strictly necessary personal data is processed, which inherently limits the potential impact of any security failures on affected individuals. This resource is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it highlights the foundational role of technical security in maintaining public trust. While open data promotes transparency, this guide reminds stakeholders that robust encryption and risk assessment are prerequisites for handling personal information responsibly. It bridges the gap between regulatory compliance and practical implementation, ensuring that data openness does not compromise individual privacy rights or security standards.
