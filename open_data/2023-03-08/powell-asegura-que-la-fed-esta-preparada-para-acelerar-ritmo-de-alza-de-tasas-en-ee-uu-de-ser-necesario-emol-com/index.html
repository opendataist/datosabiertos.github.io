Powell asegura que la Fed está "preparada" para acelerar ritmo de alza de tasas en EE.UU. de ser necesario | Emol.com

The Federal Reserve warns that interest rates could reach levels higher than anticipated in order to combat persistent and stubborn inflation. This prolonged restrictive stance implies a necessary tightening of monetary policy to restore price stability in the U.S. economy. This context is relevant to open data because transparency in the disclosure of economic indicators enables citizens and analysts to monitor the impact of public policies. Free access to these statistics facilitates government accountability and a clearer public understanding of macroeconomic challenges. Clarity in official data empowers society to assess the effectiveness of the central bank’s strategies. By ensuring that economic information is accessible and understandable, it fosters informed participation in debates about monetary policy and its social consequences.
