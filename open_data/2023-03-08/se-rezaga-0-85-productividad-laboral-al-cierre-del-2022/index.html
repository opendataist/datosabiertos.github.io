Se rezaga 0.85% productividad laboral al cierre del 2022

Mexico’s labor productivity declined in late 2022, reversing previous growth trends and affecting its role in combating inflation. This drop signals a broader economic contraction relative to pre-pandemic levels, with the service sector bearing the brunt despite isolated gains in agriculture and construction. The data reveal uneven performance across industries, highlighting structural vulnerabilities within the service and manufacturing sectors. These fluctuations suggest that while certain niche areas are improving, the overall economy faces significant headwinds that require careful monitoring to ensure future stability. This article is relevant to open_data because it relies on transparent government statistics from INEGI. Such accessible, detailed datasets enable analysts to track economic health, verify official claims, and derive independent insights about productivity trends, which are essential for informed policy and business decisions.
