ONG colombiana: datos disponibles de las elecciones venezolanas dan ganador a González - Mundo - ABC Color

The Mission Organization (MOE) concludes that the missing election records are insufficient to overturn the result favoring Edmundo González Urrutia over Nicolás Maduro. By comparing their database with official data, they determined that even if all remaining votes went to Maduro, the candidate gap is too large to bridge. This analysis directly challenges the official figures released by the National Electoral Council, which claim a different winner. This finding is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how independent analysis of available electoral records can verify or contradict official narratives. It highlights the critical importance of transparency and access to raw voting data for maintaining democratic integrity. When official sources withhold documentation, civil society can utilize open methodologies to ensure accountability and truth in electoral processes.
