La Nación / Titular del PAI define como “el asesino silencioso” al VPH e insta a la vacunación

La Nación / Titular del PAI define como “el asesino silencioso” al VPH e insta a la vacunación

The article highlights HPV as a silent killer, urging vaccination for both boys and girls to prevent cancer, particularly noting the region's highest incidence of cervical cancer deaths among women. Including males in the vaccination program is crucial because they act as carriers, breaking the transmission cycle to protect their partners. This approach addresses a previous gap by recognizing that the virus causes various cancers in all genders, not just cervical cancer in women. This topic is relevant to open data because statistical evidence—such as mortality rates and vaccination coverage figures—drives public health policies. Accessible, transparent data on these health metrics allows officials and citizens to monitor the effectiveness of free vaccination programs and understand the urgent need for widespread immunization against preventable diseases.
