Se pierden más de 863 mil empleos en junio, reportan

Se pierden más de 863 mil empleos en junio, reportan

Mexico experienced a net loss of formal and informal jobs in June, which increased the unemployment rate. This decline reflects broader economic shifts affecting sectors beyond those captured by standard industrial statistics, highlighting the complexities involved in tracking official versus informal labor markets. The rise in unemployment underscores the fragility of the job market, suggesting that job losses also extend to public sector positions. Consequently, understanding these dynamics requires distinguishing between reported figures and underlying structural changes in employment. This context is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the need for transparent, granular data to accurately interpret labor trends. Without clear access to disaggregated statistics, assessing the true state of economic health remains challenging for both researchers and policymakers.
