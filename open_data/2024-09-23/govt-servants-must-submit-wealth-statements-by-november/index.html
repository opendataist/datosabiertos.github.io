Govt servants must submit wealth statements by November

Govt servants must submit wealth statements by November

Governments now require annual asset declarations from civil servants to strengthen anti-corruption efforts, replacing the previous five-year cycle that lacked strict enforcement. This mandatory shift aims to ensure accountability by imposing disciplinary action for non-compliance, signaling a stricter regulatory approach. The updated rules clarify submission protocols based on employee grades, ensuring clear administrative oversight while maintaining confidentiality unless legally compelled. By restricting public disclosure under information laws, the policy balances transparency needs with privacy considerations for individual officials. This development is crucial for open data discourse as it highlights the tension between civic access and administrative confidentiality. It demonstrates how policy changes can enhance integrity mechanisms while challenging advocates to find alternative avenues for public accountability and data transparency.
