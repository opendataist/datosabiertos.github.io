BCP Council looking at introducing 'disclosure log'

BCP Council is considering implementing a public disclosure log for Freedom of Information requests, aiming to enhance transparency and mirror practices seen in neighboring authorities. This initiative aligns with broader goals to make government operations more open, despite the current administrative burden of collating and publishing such detailed records. The council prioritizes maintaining an accurate website first, but the potential log represents a significant step toward proactive information sharing. The relevance to open data lies in shifting from reactive transparency to proactive accessibility. By making FOI logs public, authorities reduce barriers to information, fostering greater public accountability and trust. This approach encourages a culture where government data is readily available, supporting informed public debate and reducing the need for individual requests. Ultimately, this move supports the open data ethos of maximizing the utility of public information. When agencies voluntarily disclose more than legally required, it strengthens democratic engagement and improves confidence in public institutions. This trend demonstrates how proactive transparency initiatives can complement existing open data frameworks, ensuring citizens have easier access to the information they need to hold authorities accountable.
