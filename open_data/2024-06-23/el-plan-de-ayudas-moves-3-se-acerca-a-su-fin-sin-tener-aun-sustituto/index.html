El plan de ayudas Moves 3 se acerca a su fin sin tener aún sustituto

El plan de ayudas Moves 3 se acerca a su fin sin tener aún sustituto

The Moves III electric mobility aid program has concluded amid high demand that far exceeded the allocated budgets, leaving a large number of applications pending. The automotive sector warns that the immediate discontinuation of these incentives will lead to a significant drop in sales, as occurred in Germany when similar subsidies were eliminated. Industry pressure and manufacturer associations underscore the urgency of maintaining consistent support to avoid discouraging the transition to cleaner vehicles. This case is relevant to open data due to the transparency of the public data managing these aid programs. The availability of up-to-date figures on funding allocations, applications, and disbursements by the IDAE (Institute for Energy Diversification and Savings) enables monitoring of the efficiency and actual coverage of government programs. Analyzing this open data is crucial for assessing whether resources are distributed equitably among Spain’s autonomous communities and for understanding the gap between available budgets and social demand—key factors for accountability in public policy. Thus, industry pressure not only seeks to continue financial aid but also to ensure that statistics on electrification are accurate and accessible. The lack of continuity puts at risk the national objective of increasing the share of electrified vehicles in traffic. Therefore, rigorous monitoring of information available in open formats is essential so that citizens, experts, and public administrations can adjust course and ensure that the ecological transition in mobility is sustainable both economically and environmentally.
