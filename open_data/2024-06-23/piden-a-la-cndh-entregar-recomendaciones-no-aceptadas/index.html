Piden a la CNDH entregar recomendaciones no aceptadas

Piden a la CNDH entregar recomendaciones no aceptadas

The INAI ordered the CNDH to fully disclose unaccepted recommendations, rejecting incomplete data. This ruling underscores that transparency enables public scrutiny to ensure the effectiveness of human rights. It reinforces the principles of open data by mandating complete and accessible information for accountability and for verifying the actual enforcement of administrative or legal measures.
