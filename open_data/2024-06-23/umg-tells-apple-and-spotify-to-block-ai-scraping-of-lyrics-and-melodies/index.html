UMG tells Apple and Spotify to block AI scraping of lyrics and melodies

UMG tells Apple and Spotify to block AI scraping of lyrics and melodies

Universal Music Group is urging streaming services to block AI developers from scraping their catalogs to prevent unauthorized training on copyrighted material. This move addresses growing concerns about generative AI mimicking artist styles, which threatens the integrity of intellectual property and the economic value of original creative works. The company’s strict stance highlights the urgent need for clear legal and technical frameworks governing data usage in AI development. By seeking to restrict access, UMG aims to protect artists’ rights and maintain control over how their music is utilized, setting a precedent for content ownership in the digital age. This situation is highly relevant to open data discussions because it contrasts with typical open-source principles. It underscores the tension between free data access for innovation and the protection of proprietary information, illustrating that not all data should be freely available for AI training without explicit permission or licensing agreements.
