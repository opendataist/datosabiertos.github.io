Revizto launches Open Data & AI Layer to give AECO organisations secure control of project data and AI | iTWire

Revizto has introduced an Open Data and AI Layer, enabling construction organizations to integrate their preferred AI tools directly with project data without transferring sensitive information outside their secure environment. This approach addresses critical concerns regarding data ownership and regulatory compliance, allowing teams to leverage artificial intelligence for complex queries and automated reporting while maintaining full control over their intellectual property. By bringing the AI to the data rather than moving the data to a proprietary platform, the update resolves significant barriers to AI adoption in the industry. The ecosystem now includes programmatic access via a read-only API and a standards-based Model Context Protocol server, facilitating seamless connections with trusted assistants like Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT. These tools allow users to ask natural language questions about project status, generate compliance documentation, and identify quality issues automatically. This structural shift supports enterprise-grade interoperability and governance, empowering developers to build custom workflows using standardized authentication and well-documented endpoints. This development is highly relevant to open data principles as it champions interoperability and user agency over vendor lock-in. By providing structured, accessible interfaces for model metadata and object properties, Revizto ensures that valuable construction insights remain available for diverse analytical needs without compromising security. It demonstrates how specialized platforms can evolve into open ecosystems that empower users to extract maximum value from their data assets through flexible, secure, and standardized integrations.
