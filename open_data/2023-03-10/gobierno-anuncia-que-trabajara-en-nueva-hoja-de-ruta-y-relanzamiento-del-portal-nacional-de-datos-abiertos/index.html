Gobierno anuncia que trabajará en nueva hoja de ruta y relanzamiento del Portal Nacional de Datos Abiertos

Chile commits to driving a new roadmap and relaunching its National Open Data Portal. Minister Uriarte emphasizes that digitalization facilitates interaction between the State and citizens, aiming to ensure that shared information is genuinely useful and responds to the real needs of the population, thereby strengthening institutional trust in the context of accelerated digital transformation. However, the country faces significant gaps in data usability and quality, lagging behind its Latin American neighbors in innovation. The government acknowledges that those who hold information do not always see the need to share it, so the portal’s relaunch seeks to address these technical and cultural deficiencies to advance toward a more inclusive and efficient knowledge-based society. Finally, experts argue that data openness must move beyond being a marginal initiative to become a central national policy. It is crucial to engage all social actors to generate a purpose-driven data economy, prioritizing social utility over entertainment. This approach aims to transform data into a driver of sustainable development, ensuring that its openness directly contributes to public well-being and the improvement of the country’s economic matrix. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the challenge of moving from mere technical availability to strategic governance. It highlights that the true value of open data lies in its quality, usability, and social purpose, reminding us that without an inclusive policy and user-centered design, data openness loses its democratic and economic potential.
