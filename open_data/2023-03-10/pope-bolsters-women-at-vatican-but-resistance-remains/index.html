Pope bolsters women at Vatican but resistance remains

The Vatican has significantly expanded female employment, particularly in leadership roles, marking a historic shift under Pope Francis. Despite this progress, women still encounter substantial institutional resistance from the male-dominated hierarchy, highlighting a gap between increased workforce participation and true equality in church governance. This development underscores the persistent struggle for gender equity within religious institutions. While women perform essential labor across the Church’s social services, they remain excluded from the priesthood and top administrative commands. This disparity illustrates how structural barriers continue to limit women’s authority, even as their operational contributions become indispensable. The article is relevant to open_data because it leverages official statistics to quantify progress and expose ongoing systemic inequalities. Transparent reporting of workforce demographics allows for objective analysis of institutional change, demonstrating how accessible data can validate claims of advancement or reveal persistent gaps in diversity and inclusion efforts.
