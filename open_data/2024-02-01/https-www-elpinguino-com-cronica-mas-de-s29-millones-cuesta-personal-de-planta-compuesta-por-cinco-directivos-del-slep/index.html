Open Data
ES EN

The creation of the regional SLEP shifts educational administration from municipalities to the state, highlighting a critical need for transparent public accountability. By publishing detailed remuneration data through the Transparency Portal, the entity demonstrates an early commitment to open data principles. This initiative enables citizens to scrutinize the salaries and expenses of senior executives, ensuring that public funds are managed responsibly under centralized state oversight. Such detailed disclosure fosters trust by making financial operations visible and accessible to the general public. Ultimately, this case underscores how open data serves as a vital tool for monitoring government performance and preventing the misuse of resources in newly centralized educational structures.
