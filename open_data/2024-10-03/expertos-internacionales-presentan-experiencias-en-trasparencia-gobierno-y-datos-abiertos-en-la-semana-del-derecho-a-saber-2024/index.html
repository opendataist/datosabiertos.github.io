Expertos internacionales presentan experiencias en trasparencia, Gobierno y datos abiertos, en la Semana del Derecho a Saber 2024

Expertos internacionales presentan experiencias en trasparencia, Gobierno y datos abiertos, en la Semana del Derecho a Saber 2024

The 2024 Right to Know Week, promoted by the General Directorate of Government Ethics and Integrity, reaffirms the institutional commitment to transparency and good governance. The event highlights the critical importance of access to public information as a fundamental mechanism for empowering citizens, enabling them to demand accountability and actively participate in public decision-making. International experts participating in the event emphasize that transparency is a democratic necessity that strengthens trust in institutions. They stress the urgent need to adopt technologies that make government information accessible and understandable, transforming access into a tool for continuous citizen oversight and promoting a culture of dialogue between the State and society. This initiative is relevant to open data because it positions the availability and usability of public data as pillars of modern democracy. By fostering a culture of transparency and open government, the article illustrates how open data transcends the technical realm to become a right that facilitates civic participation, innovation in public services, and the building of trust-based relationships grounded in institutional integrity.
