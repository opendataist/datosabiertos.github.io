Lithuanian regtech startup lands €2.35 million seed to advance anti-financial crime solutions with AI and synthetic data - ArcticStartup

Lithuanian regtech startup lands €2.35 million seed to advance anti-financial crime solutions with AI and synthetic data - ArcticStartup

AMLYZE has secured seed funding to develop an AI-driven, cross-border information-sharing platform for financial compliance. This initiative aims to enhance anti-financial crime efforts among banks and fintechs by facilitating better collaboration and data exchange. The project leverages synthetic data to improve efficiency in regulatory processes. By enabling institutions to share critical risk assessments and screening results securely, the platform seeks to create a more resilient global financial ecosystem against illicit activities. This development is relevant to open data as it champions interoperability and structured information exchange within the RegTech sector. It highlights the growing importance of standardized, accessible data mechanisms that allow diverse entities to collaborate on compliance, thereby reducing silos and strengthening collective security against financial crimes.
