Konkan Railway terms users’ consultative committee meeting proceedings ‘confidential,’ refuses to divulge them under RTI Act

The Konkan Railway Corporation’s refusal to release committee meeting details highlights a critical transparency failure in public infrastructure governance. By misapplying confidentiality and Cabinet deliberation exemptions to consumer-focused discussions, the organization undermines the accountability mechanisms essential for open data initiatives. This selective disclosure prevents stakeholders from accessing vital information regarding service improvements and policy decisions. Such barriers hinder public scrutiny and limit the availability of actionable data, reinforcing opacity within systems that should prioritize user feedback and operational clarity. Relevant to open data, this case illustrates how bureaucratic misinterpretation of exemption clauses can obstruct information flows. It emphasizes the urgent need for accurate classification of public records to ensure that citizen rights to know are upheld, fostering a culture of transparency rather than secrecy in government operations.
