Ofertas de empleo de EE.UU. caen a su nivel más bajo en dos años

The U.S. labor market is showing signs of cooling, with job postings falling to their lowest levels since 2021, reflecting the impact of monetary tightening. This trend suggests that the Federal Reserve’s policies are achieving their goal of reducing wage pressure and inflation, although it also increases the risk of an economic slowdown. This phenomenon is crucial for open data, as it underscores the need for transparency in the disclosure of real-time economic statistics. The public availability and accessibility of these indicators enable citizens and analysts to monitor economic health and assess the effectiveness of public policies, fostering accountability and evidence-based decision-making grounded in verifiable data.
