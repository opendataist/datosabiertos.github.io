NSTA: 135 terabytes of UK energy security information uploaded to National Data Repository

The National Data Repository has significantly expanded its public collection of seismic data, reaching over 430 terabytes. This surge is driven by a new self-service workflow established by the NSTA, which eliminates third-party fees and the burden of long-term data preservation for licensees. By allowing companies to upload historical and recent survey data directly, the system makes previously inaccessible information available to the public, industry, and academia. This shift transforms legacy data stored on obsolete media into a usable national asset. The released data is critical for supporting UK energy security and facilitating the transition to renewable energy. Seismic surveys provide essential insights for exploring hydrocarbons and identifying suitable locations for offshore wind farms and carbon storage sites. Making this information freely available accelerates decision-making processes for explorers and producers. It ensures that vital geological knowledge is not lost due to aging storage formats, thereby supporting both current energy needs and future net-zero goals. This initiative highlights the transformative power of open data in strategic sectors. By removing financial and logistical barriers to data sharing, the NSTA encourages broader participation in the energy transition. The move from restrictive, costly archival systems to open, immediate access demonstrates how policy changes can unlock historical value. It underscores the importance of retaining and disclosing industry data to build a comprehensive, accurate resource that supports sustainable development and informed public interest in energy matters.
