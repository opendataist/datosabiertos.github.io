The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

InQuest Science’s Identifier Software addresses critical data fragmentation in pharmaceutical manufacturing by consolidating visual inspection data into a unified, compliant digital platform. By integrating functional product life cycle approaches, it enables quality assurance teams to monitor manufacturing drift and bridge regulatory gaps, ensuring that decisions regarding batch releases are based on complete, upstream product information rather than isolated data points. The system enhances accuracy and training by replacing manual, error-prone processes with specialized digital defect libraries and automated qualification workflows. This shift not only reduces human error and administrative burdens but also ensures robust audit trails for inspector and vision system performance. By standardizing defect classification and tracking, manufacturers can more effectively analyze root causes and maintain realistic control limits, directly supporting continuous improvement initiatives. This software is vital to the open data conversation as it demonstrates the necessity of structured, interoperable digital frameworks in highly regulated industries. It highlights how moving away from siloed, paper-based records toward integrated, searchable databases can improve transparency, data integrity, and accessibility. Such advancements set a precedent for how complex manufacturing data can be standardized and shared securely, facilitating better regulatory oversight and collaborative quality control across the pharmaceutical supply chain.
