Beyond killer robots: China's real AI threat to America is already here, experts warn

Experts warn that China is undermining American technological supremacy through a strategic, three-pronged approach rather than traditional military conflict. This strategy relies on extracting the underlying logic of advanced U.S. AI models through "knowledge distillation," effectively bypassing the need for massive infrastructure investments. By mirroring capabilities with significantly fewer resources, Chinese labs are closing the gap without direct competition in hardware production. Simultaneously, Beijing is flooding global markets with free, open-source software, creating an economic trap for Western firms. Because American proprietary models are costly, global enterprises often adopt cheaper Chinese alternatives, inadvertently exposing critical systems to potential censorship, hidden backdoors, and influence operations. This tactic aims to establish market dominance by leveraging open-source distribution to corner competitors, similar to previous strategies in the solar energy sector. The convergence of these tactics poses a severe threat to democratic sovereignty through cognitive warfare and control over physical supply chains. This is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical risk that open-source AI ecosystems can be weaponized by adversarial states. It underscores the urgent need for democratic nations to develop transparent, secure, and trusted open-source frameworks to prevent authoritarian values from embedding themselves in the foundational layers of global artificial intelligence.
