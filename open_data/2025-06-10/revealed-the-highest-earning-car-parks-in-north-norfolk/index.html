Local authorities are increasingly leveraging data transparency to justify infrastructure investments and policy changes. The recent tariff adjustments highlight how granular data analysis drives strategic operational decisions. This case underscores the critical role of open data in modern governance, where accessible information enables citizens to scrutinize public spending and revenue generation.
