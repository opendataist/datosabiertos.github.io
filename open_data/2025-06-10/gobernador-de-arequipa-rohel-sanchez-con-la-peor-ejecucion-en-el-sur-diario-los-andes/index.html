Gobernador de Arequipa Rohel Sánchez con la peor ejecución en el sur - Diario los Andes

Gobernador de Arequipa Rohel Sánchez con la peor ejecución en el sur - Diario los Andes

The article highlights a significant discrepancy in public administration efficiency, revealing that Arequipa severely underperforms in executing its regional budget compared to neighboring southern regions. Despite having greater technical capacity and financial resources, the regional government has committed less than a third of its institutional budget for projects, resulting in a paralysis of key infrastructure and public service improvements. This lag contrasts sharply with peer regions that have achieved much higher execution rates, demonstrating that resource availability alone does not guarantee effective governance or timely project delivery. This inefficiency stems from chronic mismanagement, characterized by constant bureaucratic turnover and questionable procurement decisions that have halted major developments. The administration acknowledges a lack of completed flagship works for the year, attributing delays partially to budget cuts but primarily to internal disorganization and unstable leadership. Consequently, critical health and transport projects remain stalled or delayed, leaving essential community needs unaddressed and highlighting a systemic failure in planning and contract execution that undermines public trust. This case is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible, and comparable government spending metrics. By making budget execution data publicly available and standardized, citizens and watchdogs can identify administrative bottlenecks and hold officials accountable for performance gaps. The disparity between Arequipa and other regions serves as a compelling example of how open data can reveal inefficiencies, drive policy corrections, and ensure that public funds translate into tangible social benefits rather than remaining trapped in bureaucratic inertia.
