Priyanka Gandhi hits out at double-engine government in Bihar over lowest sex ratio

The sharp decline in Bihar’s sex ratio at birth highlights a severe crisis of gender bias, contradicting official narratives of women’s empowerment. This data reveals that societal neglect and systemic discrimination against daughters remain entrenched, posing a fundamental challenge to building a civilized and prosperous society despite existing welfare schemes. From an open data perspective, this article underscores the critical importance of transparent, accessible vital statistics for holding governments accountable. When granular demographic data is publicly available, it exposes the disconnect between political rhetoric and ground realities, enabling civil society and opposition figures to critique policy failures effectively. Ultimately, high-quality open data serves as an essential tool for social advocacy. By revealing troubling trends in birth demographics, such data empowers stakeholders to demand better governance and addresses the gap between electoral promises and the actual lived experiences of marginalized groups, particularly women.
