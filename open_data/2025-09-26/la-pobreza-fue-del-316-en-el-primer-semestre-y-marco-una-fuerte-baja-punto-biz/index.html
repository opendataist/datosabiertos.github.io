La pobreza fue del 31,6% en el primer semestre, y marcó una fuerte baja | Punto Biz

La pobreza fue del 31,6% en el primer semestre, y marcó una fuerte baja | Punto Biz

Argentina has experienced a significant reduction in poverty and extreme poverty levels during the first half of 2025, marking a notable contrast with previous periods. This downward trend is evident both in the number of affected households and in the percentage of the vulnerable population, indicating a relative improvement in economic conditions compared to the figures recorded in the second half of 2024. The decline is widespread across all regions, although geographic inequalities persist, with some areas maintaining higher incidence rates than others. Despite this improvement, the poverty gap remains considerable, and certain demographic groups, such as children and adolescents, continue to be the most affected. The data reveal that while household incomes and the cost of basic food baskets have increased, the gap between them remains stable, suggesting that progress in reducing economic vulnerability may be fragile or insufficient to ensure lasting stability. The structure of poverty shows persistent patterns that require targeted policies, especially to protect the child and youth population. This report is relevant to the field of open data because it demonstrates the value of timely and transparent availability of official statistics for socioeconomic analysis. By enabling comparisons across periods and regions, these data facilitate accountability and the formulation of evidence-based public policies. Making this information open allows academics, journalists, and citizens to verify the actual conditions of society, promoting a more informed and participatory society in the management of public issues.
