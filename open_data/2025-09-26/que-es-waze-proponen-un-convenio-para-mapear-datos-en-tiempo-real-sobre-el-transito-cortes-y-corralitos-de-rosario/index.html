¿Qué es Waze? Proponen un convenio para mapear datos en tiempo real sobre el tránsito, cortes y corralitos de Rosario

¿Qué es Waze? Proponen un convenio para mapear datos en tiempo real sobre el tránsito, cortes y corralitos de Rosario

Rosario is negotiating an agreement with Waze to integrate real-time government information, enabling authorities to share data on public works and services with the user community. This partnership aims to improve responses to traffic incidents and optimize urban planning through citizen collaboration. The project includes agreements with service providers to supply up-to-date information on outages and detours, ensuring interoperability and the protection of personal data. The central objective is to facilitate bidirectional information exchange, allowing the city to inform drivers while leveraging community feedback to enhance mobility. This initiative is significant for open data because it illustrates the potential of integrating public data with private platforms to increase transparency and the usefulness of information. Although there are concerns about compatibility with previous agreements, the debate highlights the need for open standards that prioritize access to information and avoid exclusive reliance on manual data entry, fostering more dynamic and interconnected data ecosystems.
