El Indec dará a conocer el dato de pobreza del primer semestre del 2026: se espera que se acerque al 30%

Argentina anticipates a significant reduction in poverty and extreme poverty rates for the first half of 2025, with forecasts suggesting levels near 30%. This decline contrasts sharply with the 38.1% recorded in late 2024, indicating a substantial improvement in household economic capacity to access basic goods and food baskets across major urban areas. However, methodological discrepancies persist among various institutions. Independent university observatories highlight differing poverty exit figures compared to official government estimates, revealing tensions between independent analysis and political narratives regarding the efficacy of recent economic policies. These variations underscore the complexity of accurately measuring welfare. This article is crucial for open-data enthusiasts as it illustrates the critical need for standardized, transparent statistical methodologies. It highlights how diverse datasets, when openly compared, reveal underlying structural realities and institutional biases, demonstrating that open access to raw metrics and calculation methods is essential for independent verification and public accountability in economic reporting.
