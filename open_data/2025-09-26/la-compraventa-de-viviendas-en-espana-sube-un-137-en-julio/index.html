La compraventa de viviendas en España sube un 13,7% en julio

The recent surge in property transactions highlights a significant recovery in the housing market, with July figures reaching historic highs. This dramatic increase across both new and existing homes suggests robust demand that persists despite broader economic adjustments, marking a clear upward trend that has now continued for over a year. The data indicates that the sector is not merely stabilizing but actively expanding, with new developments showing particularly strong growth alongside steady gains in the secondary market. This momentum is largely driven by regional variations, with most autonomous communities experiencing substantial rises in sales volume. While specific outliers show minor declines, the overwhelming majority of regions contributed to this national boom, demonstrating widespread confidence among buyers. The consistency in growth across diverse geographic areas implies that the current expansion is not isolated to a single hub but reflects a broader, systemic shift in market dynamics and consumer behavior.
