RD es designada miembro del Comité Directivo de la Alianza para el Gobierno Abierto 2025–2028

RD es designada miembro del Comité Directivo de la Alianza para el Gobierno Abierto 2025–2028

The Dominican Republic has been selected for the Open Government Partnership (OGP) Steering Committee, signaling a strong international commitment to transparency, institutional interoperability, and an open state. This leadership role validates the nation’s progress in digital innovation and the strengthening of the civic space, demonstrating that open government principles are effectively integrated into national governance structures. A pivotal element of this success is the promotion of data openness and judicial transparency, evidenced by initiatives such as the digital complaint platform and data interconnection within the justice system. These efforts underscore the practical application of open data to enhance accountability and public trust, proving that technological integration is essential for modernizing state administration and fostering civic engagement. This development is crucial for the open data community, as it highlights the direct link between political commitment and actionable data strategies. By prioritizing interoperability and active civic participation, the Dominican Republic models how open data can drive systemic change, offering a replicable framework for other nations aiming to align their public policies with global standards of transparency and digital governance.
