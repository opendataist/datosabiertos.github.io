La compraventa de vivienda firma máximos desde 2007 en julio, con una subida del 13,7%

La compraventa de vivienda firma máximos desde 2007 en julio, con una subida del 13,7%

The Spanish housing market has entered a robust expansion phase, with July reaching a historic peak in property transactions. This surge indicates that demand has recovered significantly, surpassing pre-financial crisis levels and confirming 2025 as the strongest year for sales in over a decade. The market is currently driven by flexible monetary policies that have brought buyers back into the market, alongside the perception of real estate as a safe haven amid geopolitical instability. However, this intense demand is colliding with a critical shortage of supply, creating significant upward pressure on prices. The imbalance between the high volume of transactions and the slow growth of available housing ensures that prices will continue to rise throughout the year. Experts warn that while the market shows exceptional strength, the lack of inventory exacerbates tensions, potentially leading to further price increases if current expansionary policies continue without corresponding adjustments to supply. This situation is highly relevant to open data, as transparent, timely, and accurate statistical data is essential for understanding these market dynamics. Public datasets allow analysts to identify regional disparities, track the impact of monetary policies, and monitor the supply-demand gap. Without accessible and reliable data, stakeholders cannot accurately assess the true state of the housing market or develop effective strategies to address the ongoing challenges of affordability and inventory shortages.
