La pobreza fue del 31,6% en el primer semestre de 2025 y registró una significativa baja

La pobreza fue del 31,6% en el primer semestre de 2025 y registró una significativa baja

The INDEC publication reveals a significant reduction in poverty incidence in Argentina during the first half of 2025, reaching historic lows not seen since 2018. This widespread decline across all regions and demographic groups indicates a sustained recovery following the peak recorded in 2024, reflecting tangible improvements in the living conditions of millions of residents, particularly in urban agglomerations. The downward trend affects all age segments, although children and youth continue to show the highest vulnerability rates. Regional distribution also demonstrates uniform progress, with notable reductions in both the largest and smaller cities, consolidating a more stable social landscape compared to the beginning of the current administration and providing an updated snapshot of the population’s well-being. This report is relevant to the open data movement because it exemplifies the value of official, accessible, and standardized public data for socioeconomic analysis. Transparency in methodology and the periodic dissemination of these statistics enable researchers, journalists, and citizens to validate trends, make accurate temporal comparisons, and ground public debates in factual evidence, thereby fostering government accountability.
