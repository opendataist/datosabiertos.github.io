Trujillo: Censistas ingresan al penal El Milagro para empadronar a internos | Diario Jornada

Trujillo: Censistas ingresan al penal El Milagro para empadronar a internos | Diario Jornada

The INEI’s census in Peruvian prisons aims to capture essential demographic and social data from incarcerated populations. This initiative seeks to transform hidden realities into visible, official statistics that inform policy. Accurate data empowers decision-makers to design targeted resocialization and reintegration programs. By understanding inmate profiles, authorities can address specific needs related to health, education, and employment effectively. This effort is vital for open data, as it expands the scope of public statistics to include marginalized groups. Transparent, comprehensive datasets enable better accountability and improve social inclusion strategies for prison populations.
