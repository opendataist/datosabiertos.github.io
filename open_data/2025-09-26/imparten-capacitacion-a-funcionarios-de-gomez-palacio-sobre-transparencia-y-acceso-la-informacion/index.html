Imparten capacitación a funcionarios de Gómez Palacio sobre Transparencia y Acceso la Información

Imparten capacitación a funcionarios de Gómez Palacio sobre Transparencia y Acceso la Información

The Gómez Palacio government launched a transparency training initiative for municipal directors to ensure compliance with legal obligations regarding access to information. This effort aims to institutionalize openness and strengthen the right to public information, thereby fostering greater trust between the administration and citizens. The training covers critical areas such as handling information requests, publishing data on national platforms, and protecting personal privacy. By standardizing these practices, the administration seeks to improve service proximity and guarantee that transparency is not just a legal requirement but a cultural norm within the government. This is relevant to open data because it highlights the foundational administrative steps required to make public data available and usable. It underscores the importance of training personnel to maintain accurate, accessible, and protected datasets, which is essential for effective open data ecosystems and accountable governance.
