Dunbar Hospital staff sickness rate is highest in NHS Highland

Dunbar Hospital staff sickness rate is highest in NHS Highland

Dunbar Hospital exhibits exceptionally high staff sickness absence rates, significantly surpassing other facilities within the NHS Highland area over the past six years. This persistent disparity highlights potential systemic challenges in workforce wellbeing and operational stability at this specific location, raising concerns about the sustainability of care provision and the support systems available to healthcare employees facing undue pressure. The article underscores the critical role of freedom of information requests in exposing institutional performance metrics that might otherwise remain opaque. By obtaining granular data on staff health statistics, citizens can hold public health bodies accountable, demonstrating how transparency empowers communities to demand better working conditions and patient safety standards from their local healthcare providers. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how releasing standardized performance indicators fosters public scrutiny and informed discourse. When health organizations share clear, accessible statistics on staff welfare, it enables independent analysis and civic engagement, ultimately driving improvements in organizational transparency and ensuring that public resources are managed effectively to support both workers and patients.
