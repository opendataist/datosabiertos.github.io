La Junta defiende la transparencia y que la sociedad pueda acceder a datos públicos de interés

La Junta defiende la transparencia y que la sociedad pueda acceder a datos públicos de interés

The Public Ethics Commission of Castilla y León has approved its annual report on high-ranking officials’ compliance with an ethical code, which is submitted to the Transparency Commissioner. This process reinforces the commitment to objective and transparent governance, ensuring that administrative conduct aligns with public expectations through standardized criteria. The approval underscores the administration’s dedication to facilitating access to public data, recognizing that truthful information is a fundamental democratic guarantee. By making commission records available online, the government enables citizens to form independent opinions, thereby strengthening civic trust and participation in the political process. This initiative is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how institutional transparency frameworks directly support data accessibility and reuse. The region’s long-standing recognition for quality in public management highlights how open data practices serve as a core identity for modern administration, encouraging continuous innovation and accountability in the digital public sphere.
