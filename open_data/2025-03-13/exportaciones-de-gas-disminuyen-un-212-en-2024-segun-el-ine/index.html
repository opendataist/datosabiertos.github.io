Exportaciones de gas disminuyen un 21,2% en 2024, según el INE

Exportaciones de gas disminuyen un 21,2% en 2024, según el INE

Bolivia is experiencing a sharp decline in natural gas export revenues, having lost its Argentine market and now relying exclusively on Brazil. This trend threatens the national economy, which is heavily dependent on hydrocarbon income, with early 2025 data indicating further declines despite government efforts to boost exploration. This situation is relevant to open data because transparent, publicly accessible trade statistics are essential for monitoring such economic shifts. Reliable data enables an accurate assessment of resource dependency and helps evaluate the effectiveness of reactivation policies in real time. Ultimately, the absence of immediate measures to reverse this trend underscores the urgency of continuous, open statistical monitoring. Citizens and analysts need this information to hold authorities accountable and to understand the long-term implications of relying on a single market for national energy resources.
