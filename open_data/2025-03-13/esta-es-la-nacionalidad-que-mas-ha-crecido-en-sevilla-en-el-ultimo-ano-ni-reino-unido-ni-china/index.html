Esta es la nacionalidad que más ha crecido en Sevilla en el último año: ni Reino Unido ni China

This article highlights the significant growth and diversity of foreign populations in Andalusia, particularly in Seville. It identifies Moroccan and Colombian communities as the most prominent, with Colombia holding the leading position in the capital. These demographic shifts illustrate how open data repositories, such as those from the INE and SIMA, serve as essential tools for tracking social changes and migration trends. By making granular census data publicly available, governments enable a deeper understanding of regional integration and resource needs. This transparency allows researchers and policymakers to analyze the impacts of specific nationalities on local communities, moving beyond aggregate statistics to reveal nuanced patterns in urban centers like Seville and Málaga. Ultimately, the relevance of open data lies in its capacity to support informed decision-making regarding housing, education, and social services. Accessible, high-quality demographic data ensures that public policies can effectively address the realities of a rapidly diversifying society, fostering better inclusion and planning for future population projections.
