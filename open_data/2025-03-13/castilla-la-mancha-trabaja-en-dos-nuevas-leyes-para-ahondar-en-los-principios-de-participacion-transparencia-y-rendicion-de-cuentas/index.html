Castilla-La Mancha trabaja en dos nuevas leyes para ahondar en los principios de participación, transparencia y rendición de cuentas

Castilla-La Mancha trabaja en dos nuevas leyes para ahondar en los principios de participación, transparencia y rendición de cuentas

The Government of Castilla-La Mancha is driving the modernization of its administration by enacting new laws focused on simplification, digitalization, and the evaluation of public policies. This initiative seeks to deepen the principles of Open Government, ensuring a smoother and more transparent relationship between citizens and the administration, as well as clear accountability for the outcomes of implemented policies. These regulations complete a trajectory initiated more than a decade ago, which includes milestones such as laws on transparency, citizen participation, and integrity. The regional government has demonstrated a continuous commitment to openness, reinforced by collaborative tools with universities and participatory budgeting mechanisms, thereby consolidating a governance model based on safeguarding the common interest and transparent economic management. The relevance of this article for open data lies in its structural approach to transparency and participation. By prioritizing administrative simplification and policy evaluation, it lays the institutional foundations necessary for the generation and access to high-quality public data. Moreover, the support provided to small municipalities through standardized models ensures that data and participatory processes are inclusive and replicable, strengthening the open data ecosystem across all levels of public administration.
