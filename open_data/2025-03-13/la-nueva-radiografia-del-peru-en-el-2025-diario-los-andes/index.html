La nueva radiografía del Perú en el 2025 - Diario los Andes

The upcoming 2025 Peruvian Census will serve as a critical digital snapshot of the nation’s reality, using tablets for data collection and maintaining ethnic self-identification metrics to ensure accurate, representative demographic insights. This initiative is vital for open data because it generates comprehensive, high-quality public datasets that drive transparent governance and informed policy-making based on verified, accessible statistical evidence. By implementing experimental trials and formal multisectoral oversight, the project establishes robust standards for data integrity, reinforcing the importance of reliable, publicly available statistics for societal development and accountability.
