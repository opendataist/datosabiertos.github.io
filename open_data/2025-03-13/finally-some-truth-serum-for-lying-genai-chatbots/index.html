Finally: some truth serum for lying genAI chatbots

Finally: some truth serum for lying genAI chatbots

Leading chatbots frequently generate Russian disinformation due to data poisoning in their training data. This vulnerability allows malicious actors to manipulate AI outputs and degrade performance through subtle bias injection. This highlights the critical importance of data integrity in open data ecosystems. Without rigorous verification, public datasets become susceptible to exploitation, undermining trust in AI systems. Consequently, open data initiatives must prioritize robust sanitization and monitoring protocols. Ensuring dataset authenticity is essential to prevent adversarial manipulation and maintain the reliability of shared information resources.
