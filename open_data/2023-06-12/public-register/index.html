Public register

The European Investment Bank emphasizes that transparency and openness are vital for its credibility, accountability, and operational efficiency as a public institution. By establishing a public register and adhering to EU frameworks, the EIB actively implements these core principles to strengthen stakeholder trust. This commitment directly supports open data initiatives by making environmental and operational information accessible to the public. It demonstrates how institutional transparency can enhance sustainability and foster better relationships with external stakeholders through clear, available data. Ultimately, this approach illustrates the practical application of open data in public finance. It shows that accessible information not only fulfills legal requirements but also improves governance, ensuring that public institutions remain answerable to citizens while optimizing their long-term impact.
