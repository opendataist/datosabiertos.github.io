Information on my educational qualifications is in my election affidavit, says Priyank Kharge

The article highlights a political dispute where Minister Priyank Kharge defends his transparency by pointing to his public election affidavit, while contrasting this with the Prime Minister’s lack of accessible educational records. He notes that such information is withheld even under freedom of information laws, sparking controversy over executive accountability and data accessibility in government. This situation underscores critical open data issues regarding the availability of public officials' personal information. When citizen records are obscured by state directives, it creates a significant opacity barrier. The inconsistency between demanding transparency for opponents while protecting leaders illustrates systemic challenges in maintaining equal information standards for all public figures. Furthermore, the threat of legal action against those spreading "fake news" raises concerns about censorship and the suppression of public inquiry. If accessing or verifying basic factual data leads to criminal prosecution, it undermines the principles of an open society. This reinforces the need for robust policies ensuring that public information remains freely accessible without fear of legal reprisal.
