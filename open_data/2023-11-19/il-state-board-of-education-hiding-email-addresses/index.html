IL. State Board of Education Hiding Email Addresses –

The Illinois State Board of Education wrongly withheld employee emails, citing security, contrary to established precedents mandating transparency for public business communications. This case highlights critical open data principles regarding public access to government information, proving that withholding contact details undermines accountability. Advocates successfully challenge such closures, reinforcing that open data requires proactive disclosure of official channels to ensure democratic oversight and trust.
