El Indec eleva multas a quienes no den información estadística o la falseen - Impulsobaires

El Indec eleva multas a quienes no den información estadística o la falseen - Impulsobaires

A recent regulatory update significantly increases fines for entities that fail to provide accurate statistical data to the National Statistical System. By adjusting the fine amounts in line with inflation, authorities aim to strengthen compliance and ensure the integrity of national censuses. This reflects a broader effort to enforce legal obligations regarding data transparency while maintaining strict confidentiality protections for individuals and organizations. This development is crucial for open data initiatives because it highlights the legal mechanisms that support the quality of data collection. Without reliable and timely submissions, the foundational datasets required for open access and public analysis cannot be guaranteed. Consequently, these enforcement measures directly affect the availability and trustworthiness of the information made accessible to researchers, journalists, and citizens. Ultimately, the article underscores the balance between mandatory data provision and privacy rights. While entities are required to cooperate, their personal or commercial secrets remain protected through aggregated publication methods. This legal framework ensures that, while data is open for statistical use, individual identities are shielded, fostering a sustainable environment where the public interest and private rights coexist within the open data ecosystem.
