Tinubu launches e-CRVS, geospatial data repository

Nigeria has launched the Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics System, a strategic initiative designed to transition from paper-based records to a comprehensive digital framework. By integrating national geospatial data and establishing a dedicated coordination committee, the government aims to capture, process, and disseminate vital statistics with greater efficiency and accuracy, thereby strengthening the nation’s statistical infrastructure for future development. This digital transformation is crucial for achieving legal identity for all, a key global target that ensures every citizen is officially recognized. By acknowledging every significant life event through technology, the system supports inclusive national planning and policymaking. This shift not only aligns with international standards but also underscores a commitment to securing the rights of future generations by guaranteeing that every birth and vital occurrence is recorded. For open data, this move represents a pivotal advancement in public sector transparency and accessibility. Shifting to a digital repository facilitates better data sharing and interoperability, enabling researchers and civil society to leverage accurate vital statistics. Enhanced access to this foundational data empowers evidence-based advocacy and improves service delivery, demonstrating how open, digitized government systems can drive accountability and inclusive growth.
