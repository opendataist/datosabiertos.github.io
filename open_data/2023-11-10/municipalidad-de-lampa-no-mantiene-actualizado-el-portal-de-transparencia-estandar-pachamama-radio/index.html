Municipalidad de Lampa no mantiene actualizado el Portal de Transparencia Estándar | Pachamama Radio

The Provincial Municipality of Lampa fails to register visitor information on its Transparency Portal, undermining the integrity of public management. This omission violates legal principles that mandate all state-held information, including meeting minutes and visitor logs, to be presumed public, accurate, and updated daily to ensure administrative transparency. The absence of this data prevents citizens from accessing real-time records of officials’ interactions and interest management. Such gaps in digital disclosure hinder accountability, as the platform is designed to foster integrity by making public interactions visible and verifiable by society. This case highlights a critical gap in open data implementation. When entities neglect publishing reusable, standardized data formats, they obstruct public oversight. Ensuring complete open data availability is essential for verifying governmental compliance and maintaining trust in public administration processes.
