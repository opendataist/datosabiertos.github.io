The data reveals significant gender disparities in Costa Rica’s labor market, with women facing higher unemployment and lower participation rates than men. Despite these imbalances, overall employment has grown substantially, indicating a robust expansion of the job market across key sectors such as commerce and education. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need to disaggregate statistics by gender to uncover hidden inequalities. Accessible, transparent datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze these trends effectively, fostering evidence-based strategies that promote equitable economic opportunities for all demographic groups.
