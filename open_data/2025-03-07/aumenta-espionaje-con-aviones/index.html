Aumenta espionaje con aviones

Aumenta espionaje con aviones

U.S. military surveillance of Mexican drug cartels has intensified, reflecting a strategic pivot toward the southern border following the declaration of a national emergency. This escalation signals a potential shift toward kinetic operations, including airstrikes, rather than relying solely on passive monitoring. The relevance to open data lies in the reliance on source-based information and intelligence transparency to verify these operational changes. Publicly available flight data and official statements enable independent analysis of military posturing. This case highlights how open data allows citizens and researchers to track significant geopolitical shifts. By correlating disparate data sources, one can uncover hidden trends in defense policy and their implications for civil liberties and regional stability.
