La participación laboral de las mujeres en México aumenta, pero persisten diferencias salariales, según Inegi | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

La participación laboral de las mujeres en México aumenta, pero persisten diferencias salariales, según Inegi | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

Despite women’s growing economic presence in Mexico, significant gender inequalities persist, particularly in terms of remuneration. This disparity highlights a structural issue where higher female participation does not guarantee equitable outcomes, underscoring the urgent need for data-driven policies to address labor market imbalances and accelerate progress toward gender equity. The correlation between women’s educational choices and lower salaries reveals a critical barrier to financial independence. Popular career paths dominated by women are consistently among the lowest-paid sectors, suggesting that educational guidance and market valuation systems require reform. Addressing this requires open access to comprehensive employment data to identify and rectify the systemic undervaluation of female-dominated professions. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent statistics from institutions like INEGI can expose social inequities. Open data enables researchers and policymakers to analyze wage gaps and career trends, fostering accountability and informing interventions. By making such insights accessible, open data empowers society to challenge stereotypes and advocate for structural changes that promote true economic justice for women.
