Guadalajara es la segunda capital más transparente de la región en la gestión municipal

Guadalajara es la segunda capital más transparente de la región en la gestión municipal

The article highlights a significant institutional transformation in Guadalajara, where the current administration has rapidly improved the municipality’s transparency rankings, moving from the bottom to becoming the second most transparent provincial capital in the region. This shift underscores the critical distinction between merely claiming transparency and implementing substantive, verifiable actions. By prioritizing openness as a core governance principle, the city demonstrates how proactive administrative behavior directly influences public trust and institutional credibility, serving as a model for other local governments. A central achievement is the systematic modernization of the transparency portal and the rigorous handling of information requests, ensuring that public data is accessible, up-to-date, and accurate. The administration’s focus on updating historical records and responding promptly to citizen inquiries illustrates the operational commitment to open data principles. This effort transforms static bureaucratic procedures into dynamic tools for civic engagement, proving that consistent maintenance and regular updates are essential for a functional open government infrastructure. This case is relevant to open data because it validates the practical impact of robust data governance on democratic accountability. The improvement was evaluated using comprehensive indices that measure not just compliance, but the actual quality and availability of information across various sectors. It emphasizes that open data is not a passive requirement but an active strategy that requires continuous effort, technical maintenance, and political will to ensure that data serves the public interest effectively.
