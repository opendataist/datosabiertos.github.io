Notorious Warwickshire speed camera revealed as almost 50,000 drivers caught

Notorious Warwickshire speed camera revealed as almost 50,000 drivers caught

A Freedom of Information request identified the most notorious speed camera in Warwickshire, revealing it caught nearly 50,000 drivers in 2024. This data highlights the extensive monitoring capabilities of local police forces and provides insight into traffic enforcement patterns across the region’s major motorways. Although the specific location and volume of offenses were disclosed, details regarding fines paid and license points were not held by the police, as revenue collection is managed separately by the courts. This distinction underscores the fragmented nature of how traffic violation data is stored and accessed within the public sector. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how freedom of information laws facilitate public access to policing statistics. It illustrates the value of transparent data sharing for accountability, while also revealing gaps in accessible information that hinder a complete understanding of enforcement outcomes.
