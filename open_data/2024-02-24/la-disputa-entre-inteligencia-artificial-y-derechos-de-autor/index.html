La disputa entre inteligencia artificial y derechos de autor

The article examines technological evolution, from the rise and collapse of NFTs to the rapid adoption of artificial intelligence, highlighting the associated ethical and legal dilemmas. The transition toward generative AI poses significant challenges in the realm of intellectual property, as these tools train their models using protected data, leading to lawsuits and conflicts with creators who demand recognition of their rights and fair compensation. The relevance of this text to open data lies in its critique of the use of protected content without transparency or licensing in the training of algorithms. While open data promotes the accessibility and reuse of information, the current model employed by major technology corporations contradicts these principles by systematically and covertly appropriating human-created works. This creates a gap between the ethics of open data and the commercial practices of AI, which prioritize efficiency over legal legitimacy and respect for authorship. Finally, the article underscores the need for "responsible artificial intelligence" that balances innovation with the protection of individual rights. The economic and reputational consequences, evidenced by the decline in the market value of AI companies and public rejection of their irresponsible use, demonstrate that technological sustainability depends on transparent models. For the open data domain, this means advocating for regulatory frameworks that ensure access to information is not an excuse for labor exploitation or privacy violations, thereby fostering a more just and sustainable digital ecosystem.
