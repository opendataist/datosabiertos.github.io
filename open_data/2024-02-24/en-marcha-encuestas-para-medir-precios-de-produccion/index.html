En marcha encuestas para medir precios de producción

The National Statistics Institute is launching a comprehensive data collection process to establish the baseline for the Producer Price Index. This initiative aims to enhance strategic decision-making for private businesses by accurately tracking cost fluctuations across the national production basket. The project will eventually expand to include agriculture, services, and other sectors, with final resolutions expected in 2025. By capturing diverse economic data, the government ensures a more holistic view of market dynamics. This transparency is vital for open data, as it provides high-quality, standardized indicators. Such accessible metrics empower researchers and companies to analyze economic trends, fostering better-informed public policy and private investment based on reliable, publicly available statistics.
