"Absolutamente nadie" está por encima de la Ley: Inai a AMLO

The National Institute for Transparency emphasizes that public officials must strictly respect privacy laws, criticizing the President for violating them. This highlights the critical importance of legal accountability over political power in safeguarding personal information. The incident underscores the tension between executive authority and statutory protections for journalists. It reinforces the necessity of robust data protection frameworks to prevent arbitrary breaches by high-ranking officials, ensuring that moral justifications do not override legal obligations. This case is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the risks of unchecked data disclosure. It serves as a reminder that transparency must coexist with privacy rights, promoting the need for clear guidelines that protect individuals while maintaining government accountability and public trust in data governance.
