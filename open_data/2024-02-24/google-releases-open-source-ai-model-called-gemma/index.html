Google releases open-source AI model called Gemma

Google’s release of Gemma expands access to advanced AI research by providing open-source models derived from its proprietary Gemini technology. This move democratizes development, allowing organizations of all sizes to build innovative services without the prohibitive costs associated with training large language models from scratch. Relevant to open_data, this initiative lowers barriers to entry and fosters a more collaborative ecosystem. By offering commercially viable models with permissive licensing, Google encourages transparency and broader participation in AI advancement. This shifts the landscape from closed, paid systems toward accessible, shared technological resources that benefit the entire community.
