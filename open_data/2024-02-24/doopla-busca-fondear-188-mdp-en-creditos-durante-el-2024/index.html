Doopla busca fondear 188 mdp en créditos durante el 2024

Mexican collective funding institutions are experiencing significant growth, with debt volumes rising steadily. This trend highlights a shifting financial landscape where non-bank entities are increasingly capturing market share, particularly in personal financing segments. The article emphasizes that despite current adoption rates, collective financing represents a tiny fraction of the banking market. This disparity reveals a vast, untapped opportunity for inclusive financial services, suggesting that consumer demand for alternative credit sources is far greater than existing supply can currently serve. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible data drives trust in decentralized finance models. When users can verify platform performance and regulatory compliance through available metrics, participation increases. Consequently, the expansion of such platforms relies heavily on the availability of reliable, open financial statistics to inform both investors and borrowers.
