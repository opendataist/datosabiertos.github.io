La economía que viene: expectativa por el dato oficial de inflación de enero

The article highlights the release of essential food baskets, defining the thresholds for indigence and poverty. These metrics reveal that despite recent improvements, millions remain in poverty, underscoring the need for accurate income data. Open data is crucial here, as transparent statistical releases allow society to monitor welfare and policy effectiveness. Publicly accessible indicators empower analysis of socioeconomic progress and highlight ongoing challenges in reducing inequality across the population. Ultimately, reliable open data ensures accountability in poverty reduction efforts. By making these baskets public, the state supports evidence-based decisions, enabling better targeting of social assistance and fostering greater transparency in addressing the needs of vulnerable communities.
