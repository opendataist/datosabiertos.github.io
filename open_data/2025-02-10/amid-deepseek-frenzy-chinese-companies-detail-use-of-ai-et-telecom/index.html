Amid DeepSeek frenzy, Chinese companies detail use of AI - ET Telecom

Major Chinese enterprises, including Great Wall Motor and top telecom providers, are actively integrating the DeepSeek AI model into their services. This widespread adoption highlights the rapid commercialization of open-source artificial intelligence in critical infrastructure and consumer technology sectors. It demonstrates how accessible, high-performance AI tools are shifting industry standards and enabling broader technological innovation across diverse markets. Despite enthusiastic investor speculation driving stock prices up, some companies caution that immediate business impacts remain uncertain. This tension between market hype and practical implementation challenges underscores the need for realistic assessments of AI’s economic value. It reveals a growing ecosystem where technological capability does not automatically translate into immediate financial returns or operational stability for all participants. This development is highly relevant to open data because it validates the power of open-source AI models to disrupt traditional, closed proprietary systems. By making advanced capabilities accessible to major industry players, DeepSeek exemplifies how open technology can accelerate innovation, reduce barriers to entry, and foster a more collaborative and transparent digital economy.
