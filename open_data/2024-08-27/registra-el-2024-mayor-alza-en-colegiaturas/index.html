Registra el 2024 mayor alza en colegiaturas

Registra el 2024 mayor alza en colegiaturas

The rise in private school tuition, which has outpaced inflation, has excluded many middle-class families from the private education system, particularly at the preschool level, where operational vulnerabilities led to the closure of numerous institutions. This trend reflects a structural imbalance in which public education fails to provide sufficient or attractive alternatives, forcing students to pay for a quality that state institutions do not yet fully guarantee. Higher education faces a crisis in both coverage and quality; the growth in private enrollment responds to the shortage of public spots, often undermined by ineffective free-education decrees. Specialists warn that prioritizing rapid access over educational quality in the public sector creates long-term gaps, while private institutions adjust their offerings with greater flexibility to meet labor market demands, highlighting a disconnect between academic training and society’s actual needs. This analysis is relevant to open data because transparency in educational statistics and quality metrics, such as standardized test results, is crucial for evaluating the effectiveness of public policies. The existence of reliable public data allows for contrasting the perceived superiority of private education with empirical evidence, fostering accountability and the design of evidence-based interventions to reduce educational inequalities.
