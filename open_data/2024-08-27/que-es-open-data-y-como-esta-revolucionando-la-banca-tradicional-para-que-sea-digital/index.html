Qué es Open Data y cómo está revolucionando la banca tradicional para que sea digital

The article highlights how Open Data and Open Finance are transforming financial inclusion, particularly in Latin America. By enabling the secure sharing of diverse information sources through interoperable hubs, institutions can move beyond traditional credit assessments. This shift allows for a more accurate evaluation of financial risk by incorporating alternative data, such as utility payments and mobile transactions, which helps include individuals previously excluded from formal credit markets. These technologies facilitate the creation of personalized financial products that reflect a user's actual capacity to pay. Instead of offering generic loans, lenders can tailor interest rates and conditions based on real-time financial behavior. This precision not only improves the quality of services but also expands access to credit for a broader demographic, ensuring that lending decisions are fairer and more aligned with individual economic realities. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates its practical application in fostering economic equity and market competition. By breaking down data silos and encouraging interoperability between sectors, open data empowers fintechs and traditional banks to innovate. Ultimately, this transparency drives down costs for consumers and stimulates a more dynamic, inclusive financial ecosystem where better decision-making benefits society at large.
