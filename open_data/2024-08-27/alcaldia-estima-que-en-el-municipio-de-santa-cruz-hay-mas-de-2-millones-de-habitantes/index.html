Alcaldía estima que en el municipio de Santa Cruz hay más de 2 millones de habitantes

The Municipal Government of Santa Cruz has projected a population of over two million inhabitants based on an analysis of urban expansion. This forecast significantly exceeds the 2012 census count, highlighting substantial demographic shifts driven by the rapid growth of the urban footprint. The administration emphasizes the logistical support provided to ensure accurate data collection across all districts, reflecting its proactive approach to managing this expansion. The National Statistics Institute is preparing to release the official census results, marking a critical moment for verifying these municipal estimates. Subnational authorities will be among the first to access this definitive data, which will likely reshape public policy priorities. This transition from provisional projections to official figures represents a pivotal step in validating the scale of urban development observed in recent years. This development is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of accessible, transparent, and timely demographic information for urban governance. Accurate, publicly available census data empowers citizens and researchers to analyze growth patterns, assess resource allocation, and hold local governments accountable. By making these insights widely available, open data fosters better-informed decision-making and strengthens democratic engagement regarding city planning and service delivery.
