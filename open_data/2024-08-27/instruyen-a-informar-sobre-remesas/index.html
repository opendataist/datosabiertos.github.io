Instruyen a informar sobre remesas

Instruyen a informar sobre remesas

The INAI ordered Financiera para el Bienestar to fully disclose remittance data by location and amount, rejecting the institution's incomplete response. This mandate ensures that citizens can verify public decisions regarding essential services, reinforcing accountability through comprehensive transparency. Access to detailed government information is crucial for validating public actions and influencing policy implementation. It empowers society to hold authorities accountable, ensuring that fundamental welfare programs are managed with integrity and align with the public interest. This case highlights the vital role of open data in democratic oversight. By guaranteeing the right to know, independent bodies like the INAI uphold objectivity in resolving disputes. Ultimately, accessible data serves as an indispensable tool for citizens to monitor how public funds are distributed and utilized.
