El Govern revisará la incompatibilidad de los altos cargos con una nueva ley

The Balearic Government is drafting a comprehensive legal framework to unify and modernize regulations on transparency, integrity, and accountability for senior officials. This initiative marks the first time that a specific statute will govern the incompatibilities and ethical standards of the President, ministers, and heads of dependent entities. By consolidating previously scattered laws into a single text, the administration aims to harmonize its standards with state mandates, ensuring that the region aligns with broader Spanish requirements for public administration governance. A central focus of this reform is the establishment of a robust sanctioning regime for breaches of good governance, particularly regarding transparency violations. The new framework mandates the periodic publication of significant data concerning administrative activities, financial management, and decision-making processes. This shift is designed to reinforce accountability by introducing new indicators and mechanisms for evaluating the effectiveness of public policies, thereby transforming transparency from a static obligation into a dynamic tool for assessing institutional performance and service quality. This legislative effort is highly relevant to open data, as it institutionalizes the proactive disclosure of administrative information as a legal requirement rather than an optional practice. By creating specific channels for citizen participation in drafting the law, the government emphasizes that open data serves as a foundational element for democratic engagement and civic oversight. The move not only standardizes data accessibility but also embeds transparency into the core operational logic of the administration, ensuring that public information remains a key resource for monitoring government actions and fostering trust.
