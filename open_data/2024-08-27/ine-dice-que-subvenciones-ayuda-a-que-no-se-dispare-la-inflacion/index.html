INE dice que subvenciones ayuda a que no se dispare la inflación

Energy subsidies in Bolivia keep inflation under control by reducing operating costs, contributing to one of the lowest inflation rates in the region. This stability contrasts with the presidential stance, which argues that dependence on these subsidies hinders economic development and the execution of essential public works. The debate reflects a crucial tension between immediate price control and long-term fiscal sustainability. The decision on whether to continue these mechanisms will determine not only monetary stability but also the State’s capacity to finance structural investments. This discussion is relevant to open data because it highlights the need for transparency in how fiscal policies impact economic indicators. Public access to accurate data on subsidies and inflation allows for an objective assessment of the trade-offs between immediate social welfare and future economic growth, facilitating an informed debate.
