El INE confirma que dará resultado inicial del censo el viernes

The National Statistics Institute (INE) has confirmed that the preliminary results of the 2024 Population and Housing Census will be released by the end of August, in compliance with established legal deadlines despite previous delays. This milestone follows a schedule that not only met but exceeded expectations for the preparatory phases, a timeline validated by major international organizations such as the United Nations and the World Bank. These upcoming data releases are critical for democratic governance and resource allocation, as they will directly inform the redistribution of legislative seats and economic co-participation among regions. Consequently, these figures will serve as the foundational basis for administrative and political adjustments, ensuring that representation and funding accurately reflect current demographic realities across the country. This article is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the essential role of transparent, timely, and internationally vetted statistical dissemination. It underscores how accessible, high-quality census data acts as a public good, enabling accountability, informing policy decisions, and facilitating the equitable distribution of power and resources based on objective, verified information rather than speculation.
