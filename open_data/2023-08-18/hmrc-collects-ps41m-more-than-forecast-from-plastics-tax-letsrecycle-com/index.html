HMRC collects £41m more than forecast from plastics tax - letsrecycle.com

The UK Plastic Packaging Tax has significantly outperformed initial Treasury revenue projections, generating substantially more income than anticipated. This financial success, driven by a large number of registered businesses, highlights the levy's immediate economic impact on manufacturers and importers dealing with low-recycled-content plastics. Data reveals that nearly half of all declared plastic packaging is taxable, split almost evenly between domestic production and imports. The tax base expands with each rate adjustment, indicating that despite the financial burden, many companies continue to utilize materials that fail to meet the required recycled content thresholds. This revenue surge intensifies industry pressure to ringfence funds for recycling infrastructure upgrades. While HMRC views fund allocation as a political matter for the Treasury, the substantial gap between forecasted and actual receipts strengthens the argument for dedicating these surplus funds directly to improving circular economy solutions within the packaging sector.
