La minería jujeña generóun incremento en el empleo del 24,3% interanual

The mining sector demonstrates sustained labor market resilience, marking twenty-eight consecutive months of job growth. This expansion signifies a robust recovery and ongoing industrial development, surpassing employment levels seen at the onset of the pandemic. The data highlights a strong positive trend in formal employment creation across the industry. Regional dynamics reveal significant contributions from provinces in the northwest, particularly Salta and Jujuy. The lithium segment emerges as a critical driver of this growth, showing substantial annual increases and becoming a major source of new formal positions. This geographic and sectoral concentration underscores the evolving economic landscape of the country's mining activities. This information is relevant to open data because it relies on the Open Information System for the Mining Activity (Siacam). The availability of granular, publicly accessible datasets allows researchers and citizens to monitor socio-economic impacts transparently. Such data facilitates informed decision-making and verifies sectoral health without requiring proprietary access to government statistics.
