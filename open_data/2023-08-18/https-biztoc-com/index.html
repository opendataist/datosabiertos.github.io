Furthermore, emerging AI investments and security breaches emphasize the urgent necessity for robust data governance frameworks. With billions flowing into artificial intelligence and sensitive personnel data under threat, open data principles become essential for fostering trust and enabling public scrutiny. This context makes open data vital for balancing innovation with security, ensuring that technological advancement does not outpace transparency and democratic accountability.
