Minority gives BoG 7 days to provide information on new headquarters - Ghanamma.com

The Minority caucus demands transparency from the Bank of Ghana regarding its new Accra headquarters, invoking right-to-information laws to compel disclosure of procurement processes and financial arrangements. This action underscores the critical role of legislative oversight in ensuring public accountability for state-funded infrastructure projects. By forcing the release of detailed cost and contract data, the initiative highlights how open data principles enable citizens and representatives to scrutinize government spending effectively.
