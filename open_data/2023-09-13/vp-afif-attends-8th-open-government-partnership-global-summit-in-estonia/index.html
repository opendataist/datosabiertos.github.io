VP Afif attends 8th Open Government Partnership Global Summit in Estonia

Seychelles actively participates in the Open Government Partnership to enhance governmental transparency and citizen engagement. By joining this global coalition, the nation commits to inclusive action plans that bridge the gap between state institutions and civil society. This collaboration ensures that governance reforms are responsive and accountable, fostering a more democratic environment for all stakeholders. Key initiatives include leveraging information technology to expand internet access and using open data frameworks to combat corruption within climate policies. Notably, the Fisheries Transparency Initiative demonstrates how public data can drive accountability in critical economic sectors. These efforts highlight the practical application of open government principles to address specific national challenges while contributing to broader global goals. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates how transparent reporting and data-sharing mechanisms, such as FiTI, strengthen public trust. It shows that open data is not merely a technical tool but a vital component of modern governance that enables effective anti-corruption strategies and inclusive decision-making. Seychelles’ experience serves as a model for integrating data transparency into national development agendas.
