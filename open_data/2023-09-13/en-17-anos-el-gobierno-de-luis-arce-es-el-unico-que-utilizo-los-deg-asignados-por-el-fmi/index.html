En 17 años, el Gobierno de Luis Arce es el único que utilizó los DEG asignados por el FMI

The article highlights that Bolivia’s current government, led by Luis Arce, is the only administration in recent history to have utilized Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) allocated by the International Monetary Fund (IMF). This decision stands in stark contrast to previous mandates, which accumulated these reserves without ever deploying them for domestic economic intervention. Experts interpret the monetization of these funds as a clear indicator of underlying economic distress, specifically aimed at addressing critical dollar shortages. Rather than viewing this action as a standard operational move, critics see it as evidence of a liquidity crisis, suggesting that the state is relying on these specific reserves to stabilize the national economy during periods of scarcity. This issue is vital to open data discussions, as it demonstrates how transparent access to international financial records enables independent verification of government financial maneuvers. By analyzing publicly available IMF data, researchers and citizens can track the use of assets, challenging official narratives that frame such actions as neutral, and ensuring that fiscal decisions remain subject to public scrutiny and accountability.
