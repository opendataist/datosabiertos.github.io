RTÉ reveals U-turn decision NOT to publish names of top 100 earners

RTÉ reversed its commitment to publish the names of its highest-paid employees, citing data protection concerns and staff complaints. This decision to withhold identities despite previous transparency pledges has sparked significant controversy, with critics accusing the broadcaster of hiding information and treating the public with contempt during an ongoing payments scandal. The retraction highlights the tension between organizational transparency and individual privacy rights in the public sector. By refusing to disclose specific names, RTÉ prioritizes legal and ethical data handling over public accountability, raising questions about how public bodies balance openness with employee rights. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the challenges in implementing full disclosure policies. It underscores that releasing granular personal information, even for public sector entities, requires careful consideration of privacy laws and stakeholder consent, preventing a one-size-fits-all approach to open government data initiatives.
