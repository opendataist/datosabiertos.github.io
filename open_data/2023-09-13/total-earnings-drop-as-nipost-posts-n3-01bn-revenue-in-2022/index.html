Total Earnings Drop As NIPOST Posts N3.01bn Revenue In 2022

The Nigerian postal sector experienced significant contraction in infrastructure and revenue, with fewer post offices and lower earnings reported in 2022. This shrinkage suggests a weakening institutional capacity to support reliable mail delivery services across the country. Despite the downturn in physical assets, the volume of processed postal articles more than doubled, indicating a surge in public demand for mailing services. This divergence highlights an operational strain where increased usage is not matched by proportional physical resources or financial stability. For open data, this report underscores the critical need for accessible, detailed government statistics to monitor service health. Transparent data enables analysts to identify sector inefficiencies and advocate for better digital integration, ensuring postal services remain viable and accountable to the public.
