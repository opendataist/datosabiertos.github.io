Transparencia ordena a la SUSESO abrir datos de médicos con mayor emisión de licencias

Transparencia ordena a la SUSESO abrir datos de médicos con mayor emisión de licencias

The Transparency Council ordered the Social Security Superintendence to release data on the doctors who issued the most medical certificates. This measure aims to prevent fraudulent practices in the health system by ensuring transparency. By omitting sensitive personal details, the decision protects privacy while allowing authorities to detect suspicious patterns. This balance is crucial for maintaining public trust and ensuring equitable access to healthcare services. This case highlights the vital role of open data in combating corruption. Making institutional information accessible enables better oversight and helps safeguard public resources against abuse.
