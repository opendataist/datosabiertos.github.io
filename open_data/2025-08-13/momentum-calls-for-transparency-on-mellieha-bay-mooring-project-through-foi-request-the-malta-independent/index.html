Momentum calls for transparency on Mellieħa Bay mooring project through FOI request - The Malta Independent

Momentum’s FOI request for Transport Malta highlights the critical role of transparency in managing public coastal resources. By demanding disclosure on mooring expansions and consultation processes, the organization emphasizes that open access to government data is essential for ensuring sound governance and environmental safeguards in maritime projects. This case illustrates how FOI mechanisms enable civil society to verify whether decision-making includes robust stakeholder engagement and independent environmental assessments. Such scrutiny protects public trust by confirming that infrastructure changes serve community interests rather than opaque administrative agendas, reinforcing the necessity of data accessibility in democratic oversight. The relevance to open data lies in demonstrating how specific, granular information—ranging from zoning metrics to meeting minutes—can expose gaps in accountability. Unlocking these details allows citizens to assess the true impact of policy decisions, proving that comprehensive data release is a prerequisite for legitimate public administration and effective environmental protection.
