Avanza en la Legislatura la adhesión de Mendoza a la Ley de Transparencia Fiscal

Avanza en la Legislatura la adhesión de Mendoza a la Ley de Transparencia Fiscal

The article highlights a legislative initiative in Mendoza aimed at enhancing fiscal transparency by mandating detailed tax breakdowns on consumer receipts. This measure aligns with national standards for fiscal transparency, empowering citizens to identify exactly which taxes are applied to specific products. By revealing the net value of goods alongside applicable levies, the law enables individuals to understand the purpose of each tax and hold authorities accountable for the proper use of public funds. This approach is crucial for open data because it transforms opaque financial transactions into structured, accessible information. When receipt data explicitly itemizes taxes, it creates a granular dataset that can be analyzed to monitor government revenue and spending efficiency. This transparency fosters trust between the state and consumers, as it allows for independent verification of fiscal policies and ensures that tax collection practices are visible and understandable to the public. Furthermore, the legislator emphasizes the broader necessity for transparent reporting in other critical sectors, such as infrastructure management and emergency response. The lack of clear information regarding dam repairs and tourism management illustrates the risks of opaque governance. Ultimately, the article argues that open and detailed data disclosure is essential not only for consumer rights but also for ensuring accountability in public administration and preventing the burden of unresolved issues from falling on provincial resources.
