La edad media de los habitantes de Albacete es de 44,19 años

This study analyzes demographic and household data for major municipalities in Albacete, revealing distinct aging patterns and family structures across the region. The findings highlight significant differences in age distribution, with Almansa showing the highest proportion of elderly residents and the lowest number of children, indicating a more advanced demographic aging trend compared to neighboring towns. Meanwhile, other locations maintain slightly younger average ages and varied proportions of foreign-born populations, suggesting diverse social dynamics within the same province. Household data further illustrates these regional disparities, particularly regarding family size and solitary living arrangements. The capital and surrounding towns exhibit different averages per household, yet a notable portion of residents in all analyzed areas live alone. This shift toward smaller households reflects broader societal changes in living arrangements, impacting urban planning and public service requirements. Understanding these micro-demographics is crucial for local governments aiming to tailor housing policies and social support systems to specific community needs. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how granular statistical information can uncover local trends that general national data might overlook. By making specific metrics on age, nationality, and household composition accessible, open data empowers researchers and policymakers to analyze regional inequalities and resource allocation more effectively. Such transparency encourages evidence-based decision-making, allowing communities to better address challenges related to aging populations and evolving family structures through targeted, localized interventions.
