Buen Fin 2024: Protege así tus datos personales y evita fraudes o robos

The article emphasizes that while seasonal sales events like El Buen Fin offer significant benefits to consumers, they simultaneously increase the risk of personal data exploitation and fraud. Consequently, the main conclusion is that users must exercise proactive vigilance by verifying privacy notices and limiting data sharing to only what is strictly necessary for transactions. This approach empowers individuals to maintain control over their information, ensuring that convenience does not come at the cost of privacy rights or financial security. The relevance to open data lies in the fundamental tension between data utility and protection. Understanding how organizations collect, verify, and use personal data is essential for advocating transparent data practices. The guidance provided highlights the importance of informed consent and the right to refuse non-essential data processing, which are core principles in responsible data governance. By scrutinizing privacy policies and website legitimacy, citizens contribute to a broader cultural shift toward demanding accountability and transparency from both private entities and public institutions handling personal information. Furthermore, the text underscores the necessity of accessible recourse mechanisms when data misuse occurs. It details specific procedures for filing complaints with authorities such as INAI, Profeco, and Condusef, depending on whether the violation involves private commerce or public services. This structure illustrates that effective data protection requires not only preventive measures but also robust enforcement channels. Recognizing these pathways ensures that individuals can defend their rights, reinforcing the idea that data protection is an active, participatory process essential for a healthy digital ecosystem.
