España roza los 49 millones de habitantes, máximo histórico, debido a la migración

Spain’s population reached a historic high in late 2024, driven primarily by international migration rather than natural growth or domestic births. This significant influx highlights how demographic trends are increasingly shaped by global mobility, with foreign nationals contributing disproportionately to the overall expansion. The distribution of this growth reveals geographic concentration, as autonomous communities like Madrid and Valencia experienced the sharpest increases. This regional disparity underscores how migration flows directly influence local resource demands and urban planning needs across different parts of the country. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it provides transparent, high-quality statistical evidence essential for societal analysis. By making such granular demographic movements accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand integration challenges, economic impacts, and the evolving social fabric of modern Spain.
