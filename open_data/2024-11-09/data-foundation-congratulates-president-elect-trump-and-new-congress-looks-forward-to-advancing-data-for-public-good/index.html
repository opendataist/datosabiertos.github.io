Data Foundation Congratulates President-Elect Trump and New Congress, Looks Forward to Advancing Data for Public Good

The Data Foundation welcomes the new U.S. administration, emphasizing its commitment to advancing open data and evidence-informed public policy through nonpartisan collaboration. By congratulating President-elect Trump and Congress, the organization underscores its intent to maintain and strengthen data infrastructure regardless of political shifts. This approach highlights the critical importance of continuity in supporting transparent, accessible data systems that serve the public good. A central theme is the promotion of "radical collaboration" among government, industry, and civil society. The Foundation believes that integrating diverse stakeholders is essential for creating practical policies that enhance the quality and accessibility of national data assets. This cooperative model aims to modernize how the government utilizes information, fostering an environment where data drives effective and equitable decision-making across all sectors. This message is relevant to open data because it reinforces the necessity of institutional stability and cross-sector partnerships in sustaining data transparency efforts. By referencing previous support for evidence-based legislation, the article suggests that progress in open data relies on sustained, bipartisan advocacy. It serves as a reminder that open data initiatives must remain resilient and inclusive to effectively inform public policy and empower citizens with trustworthy information.
