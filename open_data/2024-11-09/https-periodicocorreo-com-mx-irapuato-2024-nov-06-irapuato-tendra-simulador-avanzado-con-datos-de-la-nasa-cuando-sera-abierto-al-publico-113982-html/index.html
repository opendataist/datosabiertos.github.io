Irapuato is installing a unique global simulator in its Environmental Education Center, funded by municipal and environmental resources. This advanced technology will connect directly with NASA, enabling users to monitor real-time data such as wildfires across the continent. The installation by German experts marks a significant step toward open access to critical environmental data. By integrating high-level information layers directly from international space agencies, the city is democratizing access to real-time planetary metrics for educational and public awareness purposes. This initiative is highly relevant to open data, as it transforms isolated environmental metrics into publicly accessible, real-time information streams. It demonstrates how municipal investments can leverage international data partnerships to enhance transparency and civic engagement regarding ecological issues.
