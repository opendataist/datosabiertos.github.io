Autoridades definirán este martes ruta crítica para frenar los feminicidios - Barrigaverde.net

The article highlights the Dominican government’s strategic shift toward preventing femicide and gender-based violence through deeper institutional collaboration and educational reforms. This proactive approach aims to protect victims from re-victimization while establishing clear protocols for reporting abuse in schools, ensuring the state fulfills its duty to safeguard children. The relevance to open data lies in the need for transparent, accessible statistics to monitor these initiatives. Reliable, publicly available data on violence trends and the effectiveness of interventions is essential for evaluating the success of these multi-sectoral efforts and holding institutions accountable for their preventive strategies.
