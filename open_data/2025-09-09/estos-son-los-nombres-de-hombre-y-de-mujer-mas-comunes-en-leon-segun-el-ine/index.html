Estos son los nombres de hombre y de mujer más comunes en León, según el INE

The article analyzes common first names in León, Spain, using data from the National Statistics Institute (INE) to highlight cultural trends in which traditional religious names remain prevalent. This illustrates how official statistical agencies collect and publish demographic information that reflects societal heritage and naming conventions. Understanding these patterns provides valuable insights into regional identity and historical influences. Such data helps communities recognize shared traditions and understand the linguistic roots that shape local culture. This content is relevant to open data because it exemplifies accessible, public statistics.
