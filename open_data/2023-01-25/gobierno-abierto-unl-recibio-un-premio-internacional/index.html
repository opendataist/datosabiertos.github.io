Gobierno Abierto: UNL recibió un premio internacional

The National University of the Littoral (UNL) has been recognized by the United Nations and the Open Government Academic Network as a standout practice at the Ibero-American level. This recognition highlights the innovation of its Open Government portal, which coherently integrates transparency, participation, and collaboration, distinguishing itself through its visual approach and replicability. The initiative demonstrates that universities can serve as key drivers for improving democratic public policies through institutional commitment and interdisciplinary collaboration. This case is pivotal for the open data movement because it exemplifies how academic institutions can operationalize the principles of open government beyond merely publishing information. By combining resources from statistics, computer science, and communication, UNL creates an ecosystem where data are transformed into accessible tools for citizens. This reinforces the importance of understanding data not only as administrative records but as strategic assets that require a design focused on usability and social interaction to generate real impact. The distinction underscores the relevance of sharing successful models among Ibero-American countries, fostering a network for exchanging good practices. For the open data community, this validates the need for collaborative and multidisciplinary structures that go beyond the isolated development of initiatives. UNL’s experience offers a viable model for other universities and organizations, demonstrating that effective transparency is built through integrated and demanding actions, thereby contributing to a higher standard of accountability and accessibility in the region.
