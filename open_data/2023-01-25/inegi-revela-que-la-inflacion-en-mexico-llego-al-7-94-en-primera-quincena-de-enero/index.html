Inegi revela que la inflación en México llegó al 7.94% en primera quincena de enero

The article highlights a slight acceleration in Mexico's inflation during early 2023, driven by increases in goods prices and specific agricultural products. This rise marks a return to high levels not seen since the adoption of inflation targeting, signaling persistent economic pressure on consumers. This data is relevant to open_data because official statistics from institutions like INEGI provide essential, transparent benchmarks for monitoring economic health. Access to such granular, timely information allows analysts and the public to verify trends and understand the real-world impact of monetary policy on everyday costs. The narrative underscores the importance of reliable public datasets in identifying subtle shifts in market dynamics. By tracking these variations, stakeholders can better assess inflation trajectories, ensuring that economic decisions are grounded in accurate, publicly available evidence rather than speculation.
