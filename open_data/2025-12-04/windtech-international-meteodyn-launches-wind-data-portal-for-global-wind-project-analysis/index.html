Windtech International - Meteodyn launches Wind Data Portal for global wind project analysis

Windtech International - Meteodyn launches Wind Data Portal for global wind project analysis

Meteodyn’s new portal democratizes access to high-resolution wind data, empowering global energy professionals to enhance project planning and risk assessment. This centralized resource supports resilient investments by integrating diverse observational sources and climate change analytics into a single, accessible platform. The service enables precise site-level modeling through advanced downscaling techniques and satellite validation. By combining mesoscale simulations with microscale tools, users can optimize energy production with unprecedented accuracy for both onshore and offshore developments. This initiative is vital for open data in renewable energy, as it provides standardized, globally available datasets. It facilitates transparency and interoperability, allowing the broader community to leverage comprehensive meteorological information for sustainable infrastructure development and informed decision-making.
