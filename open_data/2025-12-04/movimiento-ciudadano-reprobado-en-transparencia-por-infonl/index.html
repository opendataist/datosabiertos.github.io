Movimiento Ciudadano, reprobado en transparencia por InfoNL

Movimiento Ciudadano, reprobado en transparencia por InfoNL

Movimiento Ciudadano received the lowest transparency rating among Nuevo León’s political parties, placing it in the critical zone. This significant shortfall highlights a stark contrast with rival parties, which maintained high compliance standards during the evaluation period. The party’s consistent underperformance over several years points to systemic failures in meeting its legal obligations to publish public information. Such irregularities undermine the institution’s credibility and reveal a persistent lack of commitment to the principles of open governance. This outcome is crucial for open-data advocates, as it underscores the urgent need for robust accountability mechanisms. It emphasizes that transparency is not optional but essential for effective citizen oversight and for building democratic trust in public administration.
