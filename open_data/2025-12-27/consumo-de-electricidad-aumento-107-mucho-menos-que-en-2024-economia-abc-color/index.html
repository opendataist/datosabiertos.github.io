Consumo de electricidad aumentó 10,7%, mucho menos que en 2024 - Economía - ABC Color

Consumo de electricidad aumentó 10,7%, mucho menos que en 2024 - Economía - ABC Color

The article highlights a significant discrepancy in how Paraguay’s electricity demand growth is reported by ANDE, revealing that while consumption rose sharply, the utility omits crucial data regarding transmission losses to obscure the true picture. By focusing only on distribution network losses, ANDE underreports total system waste, misleading citizens about operational efficiency. This selective reporting prevents a full understanding of the resources actually required to meet national demand, highlighting a lack of transparency in public energy accounting. The implications suggest that the reported surge in energy consumption is partly an artifact of how losses are calculated rather than purely organic demand growth. When transmission losses are included, the actual inefficiency within the system becomes much more apparent, indicating that a substantial portion of generated power never reaches consumers. This challenges official narratives that attribute demand spikes solely to factors like crypto-mining, pointing instead to systemic issues in data disclosure that affect policy decisions and public trust. This case is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies how incomplete or selectively curated datasets can distort public understanding of essential services. Genuine transparency requires releasing comprehensive metrics that account for all stages of energy delivery, including technical losses across both transmission and distribution networks. Without full data visibility, stakeholders cannot accurately assess performance, manage resources efficiently, or hold institutions accountable, undermining the principles of informed civic engagement and democratic oversight.
