CEOE CEPYME Cuenca afirma que los datos del padrón ratifican el crecimiento demográfico de la provincia

Recent census data confirms a sustained population recovery in Cuenca, marking three consecutive years of demographic growth. This trend indicates a positive shift after a previous decline, reaching levels not seen since 2017. The province is witnessing a stabilization that counters long-term depopulation fears, with both the capital and surrounding municipalities experiencing renewed expansion. This resurgence is particularly notable in areas driving local economic activity. Municipalities hosting active business projects, such as Tarancón and Quintanar del Rey, show clear population increases. This correlation suggests that local economic development initiatives are effectively attracting residents, reinforcing the link between job creation and demographic vitality in the region. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, high-quality statistical indicators empower local stakeholders to validate regional narratives. By freely accessing and interpreting official census records, business leaders can identify emerging opportunities and advocate for policies that support sustainable growth, highlighting the societal value of accessible public information.
