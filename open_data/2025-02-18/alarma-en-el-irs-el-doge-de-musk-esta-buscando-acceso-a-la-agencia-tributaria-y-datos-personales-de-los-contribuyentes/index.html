Alarma en el IRS: el DOGE de Musk está buscando acceso a la agencia tributaria y datos personales de los contribuyentes

Alarma en el IRS: el DOGE de Musk está buscando acceso a la agencia tributaria y datos personales de los contribuyentes

The U.S. Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is evaluating an agreement that would allow personnel from Elon Musk’s Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) to access highly sensitive systems containing the financial and personal data of millions of taxpayers. This initiative, driven by the current administration’s push to modernize tax technology, raises significant security and privacy concerns, as it would grant an external engineer access capabilities traditionally reserved for contracted technical staff subject to strict controls. At the heart of the debate is the departure from established confidentiality norms. Privacy experts and former officials warn that granting access to these systems to political appointees rather than external technical experts increases the risk of misuse of information or violations of civil rights. Despite promised guarantees that data will be destroyed upon completion of the mission, the administration’s prior track record has fueled distrust regarding the agency’s independence and the actual protection of citizens’ identities against potential political interests. This case is crucial for the open data movement because it illustrates the fundamental tension between administrative transparency and the protection of individual privacy. Opening systems that handle sensitive information demonstrates that data availability must not compromise people’s security or the integrity of institutions. The outcome of this request will set a precedent for how government efficiency is balanced against citizens’ fundamental rights in the digital age, questioning whether “efficiency” justifies the erosion of strict confidentiality standards.
