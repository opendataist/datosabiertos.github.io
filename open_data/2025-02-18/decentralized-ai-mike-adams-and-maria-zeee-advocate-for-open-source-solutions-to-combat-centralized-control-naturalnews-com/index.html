Decentralized AI: Mike Adams and Maria Zeee advocate for open source solutions to combat centralized control – NaturalNews.com

Decentralized AI: Mike Adams and Maria Zeee advocate for open source solutions to combat centralized control – NaturalNews.com

Mike Adams argues that centralized artificial intelligence poses severe threats to privacy, democratic accountability, and free expression by concentrating power in the hands of a few entities. This consolidation allows for potential surveillance, algorithmic bias, and censorship, effectively weaponizing technology against individual autonomy. The primary concern is that opaque, proprietary systems operate as black boxes, lacking the transparency necessary to ensure they serve the public interest rather than corporate or governmental agendas. To counter these risks, the article advocates for decentralized, open-source AI models that run locally without cloud dependency. This approach prioritizes user sovereignty and data privacy, enabling community scrutiny to ensure algorithms remain unbiased and aligned with ethical values. By making code and training data publicly accessible, developers can foster continuous improvement and resilience against manipulation, offering a robust alternative to the controlling nature of centralized tech giants. This perspective is crucial for the open_data community as it highlights the intrinsic link between open-source transparency and technological trustworthiness. It suggests that open data principles are not merely technical standards but essential safeguards for preserving democratic values in the age of automation. Embracing decentralized architectures empowers independent media, small businesses, and citizens, demonstrating that open development practices can democratize access to powerful tools and mitigate the dangers of digital monopolies.
