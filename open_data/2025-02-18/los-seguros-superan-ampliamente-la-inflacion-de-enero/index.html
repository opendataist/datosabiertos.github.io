Los seguros superan ampliamente la inflación de enero

Insurance price indices rose sharply in January, signaling significant inflationary pressure on essential coverage. This trend underscores the urgent need for transparent, accessible public data on cost changes. Open data facilitates greater accountability, enabling citizens to monitor these increases and advocate for fairer market regulations.
