DeepSeek’s emergence challenges Silicon Valley dominance by demonstrating that high-performance AI can be achieved with significantly lower resource costs. This efficiency disrupts the prevailing assumption that massive computational investments are essential for leading-edge results, forcing established giants to reevaluate their development strategies and resource allocation.
