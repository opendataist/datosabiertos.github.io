Cât costă cursul pentru identificarea situațiilor de bullying cumpărat de Liceul „Carmen Sylva” din Eforie (DOCUMENTE)

A public high school in Romania spent significant funds on a training course for teachers to identify bullying, illustrating how taxpayer money supports critical student welfare initiatives. The curriculum covers definitions, global statistics, and specific types of aggression, aiming to equip educators with essential tools for maintaining safe school environments. This procurement highlights the growing recognition that addressing bullying requires structured professional development rather than informal handling. By investing in comprehensive training covering physical, verbal, and cyberbullying, schools acknowledge the complex nature of these interactions and the need for systematic intervention strategies. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the importance of transparent public procurement records. Access to such information allows citizens and watchdogs to monitor how public funds are allocated for educational and social programs, ensuring accountability and facilitating analysis of spending trends in school safety measures.
