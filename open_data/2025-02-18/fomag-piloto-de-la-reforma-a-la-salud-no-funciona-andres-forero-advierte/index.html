Fomag, piloto de la reforma a la salud, no funciona: Andrés Forero advierte

The article highlights the severe financial failure of the Magisterium Health Fund (Fomag), which faces hundreds of billions of pesos in overdue payments barely months after its launch. This insolvency has triggered immediate service disruptions, as hospitals and clinics suspend care for teachers due to non-payment, exposing critical gaps in the fund’s operational sustainability. This situation is particularly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the necessity of transparent, real-time financial monitoring in public health reforms. Without accessible data on fund solvency and payment flows, stakeholders cannot effectively detect or respond to systemic failures that directly impact citizens' health rights. The crisis reveals how opaque or delayed information can exacerbate administrative collapse and harm vulnerable populations. Furthermore, Fomag serves as a pilot for the national health reform. Its documented struggles illustrate the risks of rolling out new systems without robust data-driven oversight. Ensuring open access to performance metrics is essential for accountability, allowing policymakers and the public to assess whether such models should be expanded or corrected before they fail on a larger scale.
