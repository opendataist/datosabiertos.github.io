“No nos pueden seguir pasando gato por liebre”

“No nos pueden seguir pasando gato por liebre”

Vivian Lavín warned that the Chilean bill on artificial intelligence omits the Ministry of Cultures, leaving copyright rights vulnerable. This legal exclusion facilitates the use of protected works by AI systems without recognition or compensation to their creators, violating current legislation and dehumanizing the creative process through the massive processing of existing data. The intervention emphasizes that human creativity remains intact, but the problem lies in the industrial exploitation of content with defined authorship. Creators face the risk of their work being illegally extracted to train algorithms, while the value generated by these digital interactions does not reach them. This legislative gap threatens the economic sustainability of the cultural sector in the face of automation. This issue is crucial for the open data community because it illustrates the tension between technological innovation and the protection of intellectual rights. The discussion highlights the need for regulatory frameworks that balance access to information with respect for ownership, ensuring that AI development does not undermine the fundamental rights of authors nor discourage original cultural creation.
