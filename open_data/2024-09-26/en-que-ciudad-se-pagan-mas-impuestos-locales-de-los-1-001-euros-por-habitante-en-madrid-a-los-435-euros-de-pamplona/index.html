¿En qué ciudad se pagan más impuestos locales? De los 1.001 euros por habitante en Madrid a los 435 euros de Pamplona

¿En qué ciudad se pagan más impuestos locales? De los 1.001 euros por habitante en Madrid a los 435 euros de Pamplona

The study reveals that local taxation in Spain is deeply unbalanced, with disparities of up to 566 euros per inhabitant between municipalities. This inequality creates a highly uneven tax burden for citizens, depending solely on their place of residence, underscoring the need for greater transparency and fairness in the administration of municipal taxes. The complexity of the system is exacerbated by the lack of uniformity in tax rates and the absence of economic management bodies in many local entities. This forces taxpayers to resort directly to the judicial system to resolve disputes, increasing legal costs and discouraging the pursuit of their rights. Administrative fragmentation creates significant barriers to effective access to fiscal justice. This analysis is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the urgency of improving the quality and comparability of public data on local finances. To facilitate citizen scrutiny and academic research, it is necessary to standardize and publish this data in a structured manner. The proposed reform aims not only to achieve fiscal equity but also to establish a clearer information system that enables citizens to understand and compare the actual tax burden across different municipalities.
