New Zealanders' trust in key institutions declines | infonews.co.nz New Zealand News

Institutional trust in New Zealand has declined since 2021, reflecting broader societal challenges despite stable overall life satisfaction. This erosion of confidence highlights how external crises strain social cohesion and citizen belief in governing bodies. For open data advocates, this underscores the critical need to enhance transparency and accessibility, fostering restored public trust through better information governance.
