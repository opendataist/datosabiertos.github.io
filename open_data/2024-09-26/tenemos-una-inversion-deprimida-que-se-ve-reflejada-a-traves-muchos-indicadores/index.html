"Tenemos una inversión deprimida, que se ve reflejada a través muchos indicadores"

Colombia’s capital market is experiencing depressed investment levels and declining investor confidence, which are hindering economic growth. Restoring the virtuous cycle of investment, savings, and trust is considered essential to reversing this downturn and revitalizing the economy. Regulatory efforts are adapting to modern challenges, with a specific focus on digital assets, sustainability, and open data. The recent decree on collective investment funds reflects a collaborative approach that addresses current industry needs and emerging financial trends. This underscores the importance of open data in modern finance. Transparent and accessible data is crucial for building the investor trust and regulatory efficiency required to stimulate capital markets and support sustainable economic development.
