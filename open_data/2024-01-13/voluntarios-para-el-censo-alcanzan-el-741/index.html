Voluntarios para el Censo alcanzan el 74,1%

Voluntarios para el Censo alcanzan el 74,1%

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) reports that it has reached 74% of its volunteer target for the upcoming census, with strong support from youth participation. With most logistical materials prepared and distributed, the institution is ensuring operational readiness across all departments. This progress highlights the critical role of community engagement and logistical coordination in generating reliable demographic data. High volunteer turnout is essential for accurate population counts, which form the basis for equitable resource allocation and policy planning. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the infrastructure required to produce transparent, public statistics. Efficient census execution ensures that citizens and developers can access high-quality, authoritative data to drive evidence-based decisions and digital innovation in the public sector.
