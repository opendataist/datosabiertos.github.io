Brazil’s Cerrado biome experienced a sharp increase in deforestation in 2023, reversing recent gains achieved in the Amazon. This surge threatens the region’s immense biodiversity, marking the highest levels of forest loss recorded since monitoring began. The article highlights a critical policy gap: current government incentives and enforcement efforts are focused exclusively on the Amazon. The Cerrado remains largely unprotected by these specific mechanisms, leaving it vulnerable despite its ecological importance. This distinction is vital for open data initiatives, which must accurately track regional environmental disparities. Transparent data helps reveal where conservation policies fail to cover all critical ecosystems, ensuring that open information supports comprehensive, rather than selective, environmental protection.
