La inflación de diciembre fue de 25,5% y el acumulado de 2023 llegó a 211,4%

Argentina recorded historic inflation rates in 2023, reaching a peak not seen since 1990 that severely eroded purchasing power. This hyperinflationary context underscores the critical role of transparent data in monitoring economic instability, as official statistics provide the only reliable baseline for assessing the true cost of living and guiding potential policy interventions. Food and personal care items drove price increases, disproportionately affecting vulnerable households. The substantial rise in essential goods such as meat and grains highlights how inflation impacts basic needs more than regulated or seasonal services. This distributional analysis is vital for open data initiatives, which can help visualize equity gaps and inform adjustments to social safety nets during economic crises. Geographic disparities further illustrate the crisis’s uneven impact, with northern regions experiencing higher price escalations than the south. Such regional breakdowns are essential for targeted resource allocation. Ultimately, these figures demonstrate why accessible, granular public data is indispensable for understanding complex socioeconomic trends, fostering accountability, and enabling civil society to demand evidence-based solutions.
