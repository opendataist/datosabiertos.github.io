El nepotismo continúa presente en el “Honorable Concejo ” de General Pueyrredón

The article reveals that open data from government transparency initiatives exposes significant nepotism within the Mar del Plata City Council. By accessing these public records, it becomes evident that elected officials are violating ethics laws by appointing family members, including children and grandchildren, to paid positions without regard for technical merit or political affiliation. This misuse of public resources highlights a systemic disregard for ethical standards, where personal relationships override professional qualifications. The data indicates that multiple council members have secured jobs for relatives, with some appointing spouses or siblings, while others place family members in roles they do not actively perform, undermining the integrity of the legislative department. This case underscores the critical role of open data in ensuring governmental accountability and detecting corruption. It demonstrates how transparent access to administrative information empowers citizens to scrutinize public officials, revealing hidden conflicts of interest and enforcing the rule of law in local governance.
