Comisionados evalúan ir a la corte interamericana

The INAI faces critical operational challenges due to a lack of full membership, prompting threats to escalate the dispute to the Inter-American Court if domestic legal suspensions are denied. This potential international intervention underscores the fragility of institutional integrity in safeguarding citizen rights amidst political vacancies. The situation highlights the urgent need for transparent governance mechanisms and stable regulatory bodies to effectively uphold open data principles and public access rights.
