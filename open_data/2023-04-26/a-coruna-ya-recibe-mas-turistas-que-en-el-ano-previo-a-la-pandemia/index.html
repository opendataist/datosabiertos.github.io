A Coruña ya recibe más turistas que en el año previo a la pandemia

A Coruña has achieved a significant tourism recovery, surpassing pre-pandemic visitor numbers in the first quarter. This surge, driven by strong domestic demand and new international flight routes, underscores the city's resilience and growing appeal as a destination within Galicia. The positive trend highlights the effectiveness of recent infrastructure improvements and strategic marketing efforts in attracting both national and international travelers. A key development in this landscape is the launch of the city’s Tourist Observatory, a pioneering tool in Galicia designed to analyze tourist flows and predict future dynamics. By collaborating with local universities, this initiative enables data-driven decision-making for municipal strategies. It also allows for monitoring the relationship between locals and tourism activities, including the environmental impact of major events, ensuring more sustainable and informed urban planning. Furthermore, the city is establishing a monitoring table to regulate short-term rental properties, involving diverse stakeholders from housing associations to real estate professionals. This collaborative effort aims to create balanced regulations that address housing concerns while supporting the tourism sector. These developments are relevant to open data as they emphasize the creation of public tools for transparency and the importance of multi-sector dialogue in shaping evidence-based policies for sustainable urban tourism management.
