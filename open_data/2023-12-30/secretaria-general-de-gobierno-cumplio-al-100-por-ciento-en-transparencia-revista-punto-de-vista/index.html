Secretaría General de Gobierno cumplió al 100 por ciento en transparencia | Revista Punto de Vista

Secretaría General de Gobierno cumplió al 100 por ciento en transparencia | Revista Punto de Vista

The General Secretariat of Government in San Luis Potosí successfully maintained full compliance with transparency laws throughout 2023. This achievement underscores a sustained governmental commitment to open governance, aiming to foster accountability, combat corruption, and actively encourage civic participation through consistent administrative practices. By efficiently processing hundreds of information requests across various public services, the administration demonstrates a robust operational capacity. This high volume of handled inquiries reflects both the responsiveness of the transparency unit and the growing public engagement in exercising their fundamental right to access government data. This case is relevant to open data because it validates the practical implementation of legal frameworks that ensure public records are accessible. It highlights how institutionalized transparency mechanisms transform static regulations into dynamic, verifiable data streams, thereby building public trust and reinforcing the ecosystem of accountable, open information systems.
