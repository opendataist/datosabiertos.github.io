Vietnam recibe 12,6 millones turistas extranjeros en 2023 | Turismo | Vietnam+ (VietnamPlus)

Vietnam’s tourism sector in 2023 showed a strong recovery, significantly surpassing annual targets and achieving substantial year-over-year growth in both inbound and outbound travel volumes. This surge underscores the effectiveness of localized strategies designed to boost demand through cultural and sporting initiatives, although overall international visitor numbers remain below pre-pandemic levels. The economic impact is evident in the record revenue generated by coastal provinces and the successful implementation of national tourism campaigns focused on green convergence. These efforts not only stimulated local economies in key cities but also reinforced Vietnam’s position as a leading heritage destination, securing its fourth global award title and validating its strategy of leveraging UNESCO-recognized sites to attract international interest. This case is highly relevant to open data, as it exemplifies how publicly available statistical metrics can drive evidence-based policymaking and marketing strategies. Transparent data publication enables stakeholders to monitor sector performance, evaluate the success of specific regional interventions, and justify continued investment in sustainable tourism infrastructure, thereby fostering accountability and informed decision-making within the industry.
