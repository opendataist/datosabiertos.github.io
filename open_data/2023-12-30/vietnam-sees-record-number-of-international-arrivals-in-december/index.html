Vietnam sees record number of international arrivals in December

Vietnam’s tourism sector surged significantly in 2023, nearly reaching pre-pandemic levels and far surpassing annual targets. This robust recovery highlights the country's resilience and growing appeal to international travelers, particularly from Asian markets. Revenue from accommodations and travel services also saw substantial growth, driven by engaging cultural activities across the nation. These figures indicate a strong economic rebound, with the aviation sector handling the vast majority of international arrivals. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of transparent, timely statistics in tracking economic recovery. Reliable public datasets enable stakeholders to assess policy effectiveness and understand market trends during critical post-crisis periods.
