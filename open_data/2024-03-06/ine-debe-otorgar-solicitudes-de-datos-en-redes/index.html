INE debe otorgar solicitudes de datos en redes

The INAI ordered the INE to publicly release requests made to social media platforms between 2016 and 2023, including user data and responses. This ruling corrects procedural failures by the electoral institute and emphasizes how digital interactions impact electoral oversight and compliance investigations. The decision highlights the critical importance of holding public institutions accountable for their digital footprint. It ensures that transparency laws apply equally to online interactions, preventing arbitrary reservations of information during administrative processes. This case is relevant to open data as it establishes that government requests to private tech platforms constitute public information. It reinforces the principle that digital transparency is essential for democratic accountability, urging broader data accessibility in electoral contexts.
