El iris dice la verdad

Worldcoin is promoting the sale of ocular biometric data in Spain in exchange for cryptocurrency, gathering hundreds of thousands of participants. The primary risk lies in the fact that the iris is a unique and immutable identifier; unlike passwords, it cannot be changed if compromised, exposing users to permanent harm. Experts warn that although this data is reliable for verifying human identity against artificial intelligence, its theft through cyberattacks or by unauthorized third parties could enable identity spoofing. The ability to link this unalterable information to a person’s real-life identity poses serious threats to individual privacy and security. This case is crucial for the open data movement and digital ethics, as it illustrates the dangers of commodifying biometric data. It underscores the need for extreme rigor in safeguarding sensitive data and regulating its use, reminding us that privacy is a fundamental right that should not be easily traded for economic incentives in an increasingly invasive digital environment.
