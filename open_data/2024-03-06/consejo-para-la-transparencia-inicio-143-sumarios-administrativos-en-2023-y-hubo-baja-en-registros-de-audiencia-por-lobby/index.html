Consejo para la Transparencia inició 143 sumarios administrativos en 2023 y hubo baja en registros de audiencia por lobby

The Transparency Council highlights a significant surge in public engagement with transparency mechanisms, demonstrating that a substantial portion of the population is actively using digital platforms to access government information. This growth reflects rising civic awareness and a growing willingness among citizens to scrutinize public authorities, reinforcing the role of informed oversight in a robust democracy. At the same time, the institution is strengthening its enforcement activities by launching numerous administrative proceedings against entities that fail to comply with transparency obligations. These measures aim to eliminate opacity within the public administration, ensuring that state bodies proactively publish required information and adhere to legal standards concerning conflicts of interest and asset declarations. This narrative is crucial for the open data community, as it validates the practical utility of accessible public datasets in fostering accountability. By documenting increased usage and enforcement actions, the data underscores how structured, open information systems empower citizens to demand better governance, thereby demonstrating that transparency is not merely a legal requirement but a vital democratic tool.
