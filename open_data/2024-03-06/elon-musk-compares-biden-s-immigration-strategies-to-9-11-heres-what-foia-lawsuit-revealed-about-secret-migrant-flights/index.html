Elon Musk compares Biden's immigration strategies to 9/11, here’s what FOIA lawsuit revealed about secret migrant flights

The article reveals that the Biden administration conducted secret flights to transport hundreds of thousands of migrants from Latin America to numerous U.S. cities, a strategy executed via a mobile application that allowed applicants to bypass traditional border processes. This covert operation has sparked significant controversy due to the lack of transparency regarding specific destination locations, with Customs and Border Protection citing national security concerns for withholding such details from the public. Prominent critics, including Elon Musk, have condemned these actions as a dangerous manipulation of the electoral process and a severe threat to national security, likening the implications to the magnitude of the 9/11 attacks. The refusal to disclose operational specifics has intensified political backlash, with opponents arguing that the secrecy creates vulnerabilities that bad actors could exploit and undermines accountability in immigration management. This revelation is highly relevant to open data advocates because it highlights the tension between government opacity and public accountability. The use of secrecy to justify nondisclosure of flight paths and program mechanics demonstrates how critical information about major policy implementations can be withheld from citizens, reinforcing the urgent need for transparent, accessible, and verifiable government data regarding public safety and administrative decisions.
