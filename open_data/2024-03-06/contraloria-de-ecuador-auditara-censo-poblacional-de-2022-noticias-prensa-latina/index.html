Contraloría de Ecuador auditará censo poblacional de 2022 - Noticias Prensa Latina

The article highlights significant inconsistencies in Ecuador’s census data, such as shifting population counts and illogical demographic changes, which have triggered parliamentary oversight. This scrutiny is vital for open data because accurate, transparent statistics are essential for crafting effective public policies in health, education, and poverty reduction. When official data lacks technical rigor, it undermines trust and jeopardizes citizens' rights, particularly for marginalized groups whose representation is distorted. Reliable open data must reflect reality to ensure equitable resource allocation and prevent systemic errors from affecting future societal planning. Furthermore, doubts cast on demographic projections raise concerns about long-term strategic decisions. Ensuring data integrity through independent verification is crucial to maintaining the credibility of national statistics and supporting evidence-based governance that truly serves the population.
