Diputación destina 1,5 millones a la transformación digital

Diputación destina 1,5 millones a la transformación digital

The Provincial Council is allocating European funds to modernize the digital infrastructure of municipal population registers, prioritizing interoperability with the National Statistics Institute. This investment aims to restructure public administration to improve efficiency, reduce errors, and ensure more accurate and coherent long-term territorial data management. Digitalization facilitates citizen services and enhances administrative transparency by streamlining procedures through interconnected systems. By standardizing information, the digital divide between local administrations is reduced, and a robust informational foundation is created, which is essential for delivering high-quality public services and making evidence-based decisions. This article is relevant to open data because the required interoperability mandates that data be standardized and compatible with national platforms. The collaboration with the National Statistics Institute points toward a future environment where public data are more accessible, reliable, and useful for reuse, thereby promoting a culture of open data within local government.
