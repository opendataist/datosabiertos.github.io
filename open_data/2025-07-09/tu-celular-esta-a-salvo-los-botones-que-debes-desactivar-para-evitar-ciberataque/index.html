¿Tu celular está a salvo? Los botones que debes desactivar para evitar ciberataque

¿Tu celular está a salvo? Los botones que debes desactivar para evitar ciberataque

The article underscores the urgent need for mobile users to manage their device connectivity settings to prevent cyberattacks. Experts recommend disabling Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and location services when they are not actively in use, as these functions create vulnerabilities that can lead to data interception, identity theft, and malware infections via public or unsecured networks. Security risks are escalating worldwide, with millions of devices compromised by sophisticated threats such as Trojan banking apps and hidden backdoors. These attacks often exploit weak security measures to steal financial credentials or manipulate cryptocurrency transactions, illustrating how everyday connectivity features can be weaponized by cybercriminals to access sensitive personal and corporate information. This content is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical importance of securing the infrastructure that generates and transmits vast amounts of user-generated content. If mobile devices are compromised, the integrity of aggregated datasets becomes questionable. Protecting individual data privacy through improved security practices ensures that the foundational elements of the open data ecosystem remain trustworthy and free from malicious manipulation or unauthorized surveillance.
