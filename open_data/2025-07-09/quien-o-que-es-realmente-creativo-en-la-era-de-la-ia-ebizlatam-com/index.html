¿Quién o qué es realmente creativo en la era de la IA? - ebizlatam.com

¿Quién o qué es realmente creativo en la era de la IA? - ebizlatam.com

The article argues that while AI democratizes creative production and enhances professional capabilities, it fundamentally reshapes concepts of authorship and intellectual property. This shift challenges traditional professional identities, as tools become extensions of human vision rather than mere replacements for talent. Relevant to open data, the ethical curation of datasets emerges as a critical pillar for sustainable AI development. Preserving creative legacies through trained models and establishing museums for AI art highlight how accessible, high-quality data enables new forms of cultural expression and heritage preservation. Ultimately, human intention and narrative remain irreplaceable, positioning creativity as a deeply human endeavor guided by algorithmic tools. This perspective urges creators to collaborate with AI responsibly, ensuring that data usage supports authentic expression rather than undermining the unique human perspective essential for genuine innovation.
