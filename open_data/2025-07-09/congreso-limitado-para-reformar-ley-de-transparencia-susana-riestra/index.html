The article underscores the urgency for Puebla to establish a robust local transparency framework following the dissolution of the federal institute, which created a significant regulatory vacuum. This development compels state legislators to proactively define oversight mechanisms, ensuring that public administration remains accountable and transparent without relying on arbitrary discretionary criteria. Key proposals focus on the mandatory proactive publication of public works contracts, aiming to eliminate opportunities for corruption and facilitate continuous citizen scrutiny. By harmonizing local laws with international open government standards, the initiative seeks to strengthen legal certainty and promote active civic participation in supervising the use of public resources dedicated to infrastructure development. This content is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical link between legal mandates and data availability. It emphasizes that transparency is not merely about releasing information but requires structured, proactive data publication standards. This ensures that citizens can effectively monitor government spending, turning open data into a practical tool for accountability and democratic engagement.
