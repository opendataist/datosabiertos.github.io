Portal de transparencia | FAO

Portal de transparencia | FAO

The FAO’s commitment to transparency through IATI enhances accountability and aligns with the 2030 Agenda. This openness ensures that stakeholders understand how development funds are allocated, improving coordination and effectiveness. Publishing quarterly data on fiduciary funds and projects empowers public scrutiny. This consistent data sharing fosters trust by revealing exactly where resources are directed. This initiative is crucial for open data as it democratizes information about international aid. It demonstrates how standardized data standards can drive global development goals and responsible governance.
