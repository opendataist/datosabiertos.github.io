¿Qué sigue tras la extinción del INAI? Secretaría Anticorrupción toma el mando y llega a acuerdos clave

¿Qué sigue tras la extinción del INAI? Secretaría Anticorrupción toma el mando y llega a acuerdos clave

The Mexican government officially dissolved the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), transferring its facilities to the Secretariat for Anti-Corruption following the publication of new transparency laws. This action effectively eliminates the country’s main autonomous body responsible for guaranteeing access to information and protecting personal data rights. Former employees received settlement agreements that included severance pay and the possibility of being placed in successor agencies, although rehiring remains uncertain. The dissolution reflects a strategic shift in the administration of public oversight, potentially altering institutional checks and balances. This development is critical for open data advocates, as it dismantles a key mechanism for citizen accountability. The loss of an independent watchdog threatens data transparency, signaling a regression in democratic safeguards and access to public information.
