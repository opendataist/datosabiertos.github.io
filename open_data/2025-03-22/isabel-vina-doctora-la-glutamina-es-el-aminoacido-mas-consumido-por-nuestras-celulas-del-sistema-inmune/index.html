Isabel Viña, doctora: "La glutamina es el aminoácido más consumido por nuestras células del sistema inmune"

Isabel Viña, doctora: "La glutamina es el aminoácido más consumido por nuestras células del sistema inmune"

The article highlights that Spain significantly exceeds the European average in regular physical activity among adults over the age of sixteen. This robust engagement in exercise is presented not merely as a lifestyle choice but as a crucial factor for enhancing public health, particularly by strengthening the immune system through increased muscle mass and glutamine reserves. Expert analysis reveals that developed muscles serve as vital energy reservoirs for white blood cells, offering protection against external health threats. Conversely, sedentary habits and poor nutrition are linked to chronic fatigue and systemic inflammation, emphasizing that dietary balance and physical conditioning are interconnected pillars of overall well-being rather than isolated health metrics. This discussion is highly relevant to open data because it relies on official statistics from the National Institute of Statistics to validate these health trends. By making such survey data accessible, institutions enable researchers and the public to verify the relationship between lifestyle behaviors and national health outcomes, fostering evidence-based policy and greater transparency in how population wellness is measured and understood.
