Analizan impacto de la IA en la creatividad y la propiedad intelectual - La Capital

Analizan impacto de la IA en la creatividad y la propiedad intelectual - La Capital

This article highlights a significant policy dialogue in Mexico concerning the intersection of artificial intelligence, creativity, and intellectual property rights. It underscores the urgent need for a regulatory framework that balances technological innovation with the protection of human creators’ rights. The discussion emphasizes that while governments and industries must adapt to AI, regulations should not stifle progress but rather ensure fair compensation and recognition for artists, particularly in sectors such as voice acting and copyright management. The narrative reveals that open data practices are critical in this emerging landscape, especially regarding transparency in AI training models and the ethical use of personal or biometric data. By advocating for clear rules on data usage and algorithmic transparency, the conversation points toward the necessity of accessible, verifiable information sources. This transparency ensures that stakeholders can understand how AI systems are developed and deployed, safeguarding consumer rights and maintaining public trust in digital transformations. Ultimately, the article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparent, accountable data governance supports both innovation and ethical standards. The call for balanced policies that integrate technical development with legal protections demonstrates that open, clear data frameworks are essential for sustaining a fair digital economy. Without such openness, the risks to intellectual property and individual rights increase, making data transparency a cornerstone of future-proof regulation.
