Vetan a Musk y a DOGE; ya no podrán acceder a datos personales de Seguridad Social de EU | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

Vetan a Musk y a DOGE; ya no podrán acceder a datos personales de Seguridad Social de EU | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

A federal judge issued a temporary restraining order against Elon Musk and the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), halting their unrestricted access to sensitive Social Security Administration data. This decisive action prioritizes the fundamental right to privacy, preventing the potential exploitation of highly confidential personal information belonging to millions of citizens, including health records and financial details. The ruling mandates that DOGE employees delete unanonymized data and remove unauthorized technology from federal systems. The judge criticized the department’s methodology as arbitrary and lacking concrete evidence of widespread fraud, highlighting the dangers of granting untrained personnel access to critical infrastructure. This intervention underscores the necessity of rigorous oversight and proper protocols when handling state data, ensuring that efficiency goals do not override legal safeguards. This case is highly relevant to open data discussions because it illustrates the critical balance between transparency and data protection. While open data initiatives aim to increase government accountability, this legal precedent demonstrates that indiscriminate access can violate privacy rights. It emphasizes that meaningful open data must be anonymized and governed by strict ethical frameworks to protect citizens while maintaining public trust in digital governance.
