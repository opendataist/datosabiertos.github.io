El gobierno de Entre Ríos actualizó su Portal de Transparencia para facilitar el acceso a la información pública

Entre Ríos has updated its Transparency Portal to centralize scattered public information, improving accessibility through a modern design and optimized features for various devices. This update facilitates access to key data, such as official statistics and details on public works, promoting open government practices across the province. The inclusion of a dedicated Open Data section enables the use of datasets in reusable formats, fostering research and data journalism. By providing tools that simplify the interpretation and location of information, the government strengthens its commitment to transparency and citizen participation. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how institutionalizing portals with reusable formats drives the open data ecosystem. It transforms the mere publication of information into an active tool for civic development and accountability, demonstrating the tangible impact of technological accessibility on governance.
