Suman 72 muertos por lluvias en México; hay 48 personas no localizadas - Noticias de Chihuahua - La Parada Digital

Suman 72 muertos por lluvias en México; hay 48 personas no localizadas - Noticias de Chihuahua - La Parada Digital

The government reported that recent heavy rains in central Mexico have caused 72 fatalities and severely damaged infrastructure across five states. This tragedy underscores the urgent need for accessible disaster data to accurately monitor human impact. Reliable, open information on casualties is essential for accountability and for understanding the true scope of such natural disasters. Emergency efforts are currently focused on restoring connectivity and basic services to isolated communities. Significant progress has been made in reopening roads and restoring electrical power, highlighting the importance of transparent reporting on recovery metrics. Open data regarding infrastructure status enables citizens and officials to track recovery efficiency and allocate resources more effectively during crises. Furthermore, a comprehensive census has identified tens of thousands of affected homes, providing a clear picture of the humanitarian scale. This detailed household data is crucial for coordinating aid and planning future resilience strategies. By making these datasets open and accessible, governments can foster better coordination among stakeholders and ensure that support reaches those most in need based on verified, public information.
