El costo de la construcción superó el 3% en septiembre y alcanzó su nivel más alto del año - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

The construction cost index rose significantly in September, driven primarily by increases in labor, materials, and general expenses. This acceleration reflects broader inflationary pressures within the sector, particularly through higher wages and utility rates. Labor costs surged due to new collective bargaining agreements, while general expenses climbed alongside electricity tariffs. Material prices also increased, highlighting specific sectors like lighting and electrical components, which are essential for infrastructure projects. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible statistical information. Reliable public indices allow researchers and policymakers to track economic trends, assess inflation impacts, and make informed decisions regarding housing and infrastructure development.
