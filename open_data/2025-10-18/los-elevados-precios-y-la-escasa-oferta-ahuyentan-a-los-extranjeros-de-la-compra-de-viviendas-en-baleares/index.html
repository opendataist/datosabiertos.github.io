Los elevados precios y la escasa oferta ahuyentan a los extranjeros de la compra de viviendas en Baleares

Los elevados precios y la escasa oferta ahuyentan a los extranjeros de la compra de viviendas en Baleares

The real estate market in the Balearic Islands is undergoing a significant adjustment driven by high prices and limited supply, rather than a pure decline. Despite a drop in transaction volumes, property values continue to rise, highlighting a persistent scarcity that keeps costs elevated for both local and international buyers. Foreigners represent a substantial portion of these transactions, maintaining their strong presence in the market despite recent fluctuations. The sharp increase in prices per square meter over recent years underscores the growing difficulty for new entrants, while historical data shows that external demand remains resilient even as sales volumes experience volatility. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how fragmented or restricted statistics can hinder comprehensive market analysis. When key demographic data, such as the percentage of foreign buyers, are not uniformly published across regions, it creates blind spots that prevent accurate comparative assessments. Ensuring transparent, accessible, and complete datasets is essential for understanding true market dynamics and informing effective housing policies.
