Councils failing to inspect hazardous housing - PropertyWire

Councils failing to inspect hazardous housing - PropertyWire

Analysis reveals that a significant portion of tenant complaints regarding hazardous properties fail to trigger inspections, suggesting rogue landlords often evade accountability. This enforcement gap undermines tenant safety and highlights systemic weaknesses in how local authorities manage housing quality complaints. The data indicates severe inconsistencies, with a small minority of councils conducting most inspections while many lack basic records or tenure-specific data. This poor recordkeeping prevents effective tracking of enforcement activities, leaving responsible landlords at a disadvantage and obscuring the true scale of sub-standard housing across the sector. For open data initiatives, this situation underscores the critical need for standardized, publicly accessible enforcement metrics. Transparent reporting on council actions and hazard resolutions is essential for accountability, allowing stakeholders to monitor progress and ensure that regulatory powers are applied consistently to protect tenants.
