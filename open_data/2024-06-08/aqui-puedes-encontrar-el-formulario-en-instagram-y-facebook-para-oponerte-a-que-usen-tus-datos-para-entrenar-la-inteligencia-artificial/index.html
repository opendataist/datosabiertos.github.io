Aquí puedes encontrar el formulario en Instagram y Facebook para oponerte a que usen tus datos para entrenar la inteligencia artificial

Aquí puedes encontrar el formulario en Instagram y Facebook para oponerte a que usen tus datos para entrenar la inteligencia artificial

Meta has updated its privacy policy to allow users to opt out of having their personal information used on Instagram and Facebook for training its artificial intelligence models. This measure grants greater control over data, explicitly excluding private messages, although public posts continue to be relevant to the platform’s technological development. Transparency in data handling is crucial, as generative algorithms require vast amounts of information to improve their accuracy and functionality. By offering this opt-out option, Meta acknowledges the need to balance technological innovation with users’ privacy expectations, setting an important precedent for the ethical management of digital information. This change is relevant to open data because it illustrates the tension between the massive availability of information and individual privacy. It reinforces the importance of clear mechanisms that enable citizens to exercise sovereignty over their personal data, ensuring that data collection for commercial or technological purposes respects fundamental privacy rights in an increasingly AI-dependent digital environment.
