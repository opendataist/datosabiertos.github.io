Demand for new buses in Ukraine plunges by 67% in May

Ukraine’s new bus market experienced a sharp contraction in mid-2024, highlighting significant volatility in public transport procurement. This dramatic decline contrasts sharply with the previous year’s surge, suggesting shifting economic pressures or policy changes affecting fleet renewal. These fluctuations are relevant to open data because they underscore the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical repositories like Ukrautoprom’s. Reliable public records enable analysts and policymakers to accurately track industry health, identify emerging trends, and make informed decisions regarding infrastructure and transportation planning.
