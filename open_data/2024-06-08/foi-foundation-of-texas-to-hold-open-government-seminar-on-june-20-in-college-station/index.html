FOI Foundation of Texas to hold Open Government Seminar on June 20 in College Station

FOI Foundation of Texas to hold Open Government Seminar on June 20 in College Station

The Freedom of Information Foundation of Texas is hosting a seminar to train attendees on state transparency laws, emphasizing that access to public information is essential for a functioning democracy. This event aims to empower citizens and officials with the knowledge necessary to effectively navigate the Texas Public Information and Open Meetings Acts. Collaborating with the Attorney General’s Office and the Texas Press Association, the free training covers legal frameworks and cost rules for public records. Designed for journalists, activists, and government employees, the sessions help participants understand their rights and responsibilities regarding government transparency. This event is highly relevant to open data because it strengthens the legal infrastructure supporting data accessibility. By educating stakeholders on how to request and utilize public records, it fosters a culture of accountability and ensures that valuable government data remains available for public scrutiny and civic engagement.
