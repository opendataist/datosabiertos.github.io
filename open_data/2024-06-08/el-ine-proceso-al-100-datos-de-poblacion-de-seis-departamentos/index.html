El INE procesó al 100% datos de población de seis departamentos

El INE procesó al 100% datos de población de seis departamentos

The National Statistics Institute reports that processing the 2024 census data is nearing completion, with several departments fully finished and others in final verification stages. This systematic approach ensures data validity through rigorous cross-referencing and quality control, guaranteeing the timely release of regional population results by late August. Additionally, a coverage survey is underway to validate data completeness across the nation. This update highlights the critical importance of open data transparency in public administration. By publicly detailing the statistical methodology and processing status, the INE fosters trust and accountability in government figures. For researchers and developers, such accessible, high-quality official data serves as a foundational resource for social analysis, ensuring that decisions are based on accurate and verifiable information.
