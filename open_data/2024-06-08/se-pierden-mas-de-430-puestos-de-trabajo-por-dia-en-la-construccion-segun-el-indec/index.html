Se pierden más de 430 puestos de trabajo por día en la construcción, según el Indec

The Argentine construction sector has experienced a severe collapse in formal employment due to the nationwide suspension of public works projects. This paralysis led to a dramatic reduction in jobs, with the majority of losses directly attributable to the halt of state-funded initiatives, while large-scale private construction remained relatively more stable compared to the devastated public sector. Beyond immediate job losses, the industry faces profound uncertainty regarding future activity. Business surveys reveal unfavorable expectations, with most firms predicting stagnant or declining operations in the coming months. This pessimistic outlook suggests that the downturn is not merely a temporary pause but reflects broader structural challenges and a lack of confidence in the sector’s near-term recovery. This data is crucial for open data advocates because it demonstrates how transparent statistical reporting from national institutes can effectively track the real-world economic impact of policy decisions. By making employment and activity metrics publicly accessible, governments enable civil society and researchers to hold institutions accountable and analyze the tangible consequences of administrative standstills on labor markets.
