Statistics agency releases first core inflation rate

The Namibia Statistics Agency has introduced its first core inflation rate, a significant metric designed to filter out volatile elements like food and energy prices. This new measure provides a clearer view of underlying inflation trends by excluding sudden price swings caused by external shocks, offering a more stable indicator for economic analysis and policy formulation. By removing these temporary fluctuations, the core rate reveals a more persistent inflationary trend compared to headline figures. This distinction allows policymakers and economists to distinguish between transient price spikes and structural economic shifts, ensuring that monetary decisions are based on sustainable patterns rather than short-term anomalies that may not reflect long-term economic health. This development is crucial for open data initiatives as it enhances transparency and data granularity. Providing a core inflation metric alongside traditional measures empowers analysts, researchers, and the public to conduct more nuanced investigations into economic stability. It supports evidence-based discourse by making complex statistical insights accessible and understandable, fostering greater trust in official statistics and promoting informed decision-making across various sectors.
