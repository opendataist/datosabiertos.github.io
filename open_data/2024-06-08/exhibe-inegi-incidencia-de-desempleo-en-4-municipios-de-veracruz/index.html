Exhibe INEGI incidencia de desempleo en 4 municipios de Veracruz

INEGI identifies several municipalities in Veracruz as having high unemployment rates and low participation in formal employment, highlighting severe labor market distress in these regions. These findings reveal that while formal jobs are scarce, informal work remains a critical yet precarious survival mechanism for the local population. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it exposes granular socioeconomic disparities that policymakers and researchers must address to foster equitable development.
