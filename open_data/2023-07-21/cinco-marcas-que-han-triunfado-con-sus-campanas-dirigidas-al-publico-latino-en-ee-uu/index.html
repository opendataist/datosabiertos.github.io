Cinco marcas que han triunfado con sus campañas dirigidas al público latino en EE.UU

This article examines how global brands are adapting their marketing strategies to genuinely connect with the large and multicultural Latino population in the United States. It concludes that success does not lie in a generic approach, but rather in the ability to integrate authentic narratives that celebrate cultural heritage, community resilience, and shared identity. Companies that successfully balance creativity with culturally relevant content achieve a deeper and more lasting connection with this demographic segment. The text highlights successful examples where campaigns go beyond traditional advertising to resonate emotionally. From vehicles that accompany the dream of progress to personalized labels that validate identity pride, these initiatives demonstrate that recognizing cultural complexities is key. By validating specific everyday experiences, aspirations, and struggles, brands transform their commercial presence into an effective tool for inclusion and social representation. The relevance to the field of open data lies in the need to break down broad demographic data into more granular and qualitative segments. To replicate these successful communication strategies, researchers and analysts must access and analyze open data that reveal the specific preferences, behaviors, and cultural trends of minority communities. Without detailed and accessible data, it is impossible to design policies or campaigns that accurately respond to the real needs of diverse and constantly evolving populations.
