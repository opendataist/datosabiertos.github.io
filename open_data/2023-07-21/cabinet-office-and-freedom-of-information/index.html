Cabinet Office and Freedom of Information

The Cabinet Office has restructured its approach to Freedom of Information, transitioning from a centralized clearing house to a primarily advisory role. This shift, finalized in late 2022, aims to enhance consistency across government while ensuring that individual departments retain final decision-making authority on disclosures. By focusing on high-level guidance rather than direct intervention, the model seeks to streamline processes for complex issues like national security and collective responsibility without creating bottlenecks. A key component of this updated framework is the management of "round robin" requests, where multiple departments receive similar inquiries. The Cabinet Office now aggregates these notifications to promote uniform responses across the government, preventing contradictory disclosures. This collaborative mechanism ensures that public authorities handle recurring themes consistently, thereby maintaining integrity in how sensitive or cross-departmental information is managed and released to the public. This evolution is highly relevant to open data advocates because it underscores the balance between transparency and security within government operations. By clarifying roles and standardizing best practices for data formatting and GDPR compliance, the framework aims to facilitate more reliable and accessible information releases. Understanding these procedural safeguards helps stakeholders navigate the complexities of requesting data, ensuring that open government initiatives operate within robust legal and security boundaries.
