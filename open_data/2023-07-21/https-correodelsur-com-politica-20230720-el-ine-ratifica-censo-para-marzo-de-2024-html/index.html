The INE confirms the 2024 Census schedule, ensuring transparent public access to essential timelines and legislative requests via its official website. With cartographic updates nearly complete nationwide, this progress facilitates accurate geographical mapping, which is critical for reliable demographic data collection and spatial analysis. An upcoming experimental census in Capinota will test methodologies, serving as a vital rehearsal to ensure data integrity and operational readiness for the main national count.
