La producción industrial sube un 4,9% en junio en Castilla-La Mancha

The industrial production index in Castilla-La Mancha shows a positive resurgence, reversing recent declines and signaling broader economic recovery. This growth, driven primarily by the energy and equipment sectors, indicates that regional industries are regaining momentum in line with national trends. The shift highlights divergent performance across specific sectors, with energy and extractive industries surging while the automotive and leather sectors contract. This structural variation underscores the dynamic nature of industrial activity, where strategic sectors lead the recovery while traditional manufacturing faces continued headwinds.
