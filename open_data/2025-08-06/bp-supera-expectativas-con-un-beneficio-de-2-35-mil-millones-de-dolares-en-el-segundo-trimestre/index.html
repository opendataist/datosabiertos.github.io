BP supera expectativas con un beneficio de 2.35 mil millones de dólares en el segundo trimestre

BP has exceeded second-quarter financial expectations, driven by strong performance in its upstream sector and significant discoveries, such as the Bumerangue find in Brazil. This operational improvement reflects a strategy focused on continued investment in hydrocarbons and the restoration of investor confidence through dividend increases and share buybacks. The company prioritizes organic growth over mergers, demonstrating its resilience in the face of global price volatility. Despite pressure to transition to cleaner energy, BP remains committed to resource extraction, considering these resources vital to the current economy while gradually reducing its net debt.
