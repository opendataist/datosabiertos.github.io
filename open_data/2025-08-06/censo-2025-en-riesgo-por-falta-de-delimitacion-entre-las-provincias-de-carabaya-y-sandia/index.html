Censo 2025 en riesgo por falta de delimitación entre las provincias de Carabaya y Sandia

The dispute over territorial boundaries between Carabaya and Sandia creates significant uncertainty for residents, who cannot determine their administrative jurisdiction. This lack of consensus complicates access to public services and extends beyond immediate census concerns, affecting broader civic procedures. Operationally, the 2025 Census faces logistical hurdles due to undefined borders, which are essential for organizing field teams and ensuring accurate population counts. Ambiguity risks double-counting or omitting individuals, thereby compromising the precision of this crucial national statistical exercise. This situation is vital for open data as it highlights how unresolved administrative conflicts directly degrade data quality and reliability. Accurate geospatial information is foundational for trustworthy public datasets; thus, resolving such territorial ambiguities is imperative for maintaining the integrity of government statistics and ensuring equitable service distribution.
