‘Datos abiertos, pilar fundamental para la transparencia gubernamental’, afirma David Colmenares

‘Datos abiertos, pilar fundamental para la transparencia gubernamental’, afirma David Colmenares

The article argues that global democratic pressures and digital connectivity have compelled public institutions to reinvent their operations through transparency. This shift transforms data from a mere disclosure tool into a mechanism for direct citizen interaction and specialized analysis. Consequently, governments must move beyond legal frameworks to establish operational practices that make information truly accessible and usable in real time. Open data is defined not only by its availability but also by the technical and legal capacity for free reuse and redistribution. This utility enables the public to monitor government actions, access audit findings, and track follow-up processes. By democratizing access to this information, institutions facilitate specialized analysis and generate functional value, thereby strengthening the relationship between the state and society through greater collaboration and informed participation. This transformation is significant for open data because it underscores that the true revolution lies not in mere publication, but in empowering citizens through effective data practices. The ASF’s public audit system exemplifies how operationalizing open data strengthens democracy by fostering accountability. The challenge remains to ensure that legal norms translate into tangible empowerment, turning data into a tool for active civic engagement rather than passive information consumption.
