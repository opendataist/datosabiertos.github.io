Proyectan disminución gradual de la población de Uruguay

Proyectan disminución gradual de la población de Uruguay

The article highlights a significant demographic decline in Uruguay, projecting a population drop by 2070 driven by low birth rates, aging, and emigration. This shift implies substantial societal changes, including increased pressure on health and pension systems, potential real estate price fluctuations, and labor market adjustments that may drive up wages and costs. For open data, this study exemplifies the critical role of transparent, high-quality statistical infrastructure in societal forecasting. Reliable demographic data enables policymakers and researchers to anticipate structural economic shifts, fostering evidence-based planning for sustainable development and resource allocation. Understanding these trends allows stakeholders to analyze long-term impacts on public services and economic stability. By making such predictive models and underlying data accessible, open data initiatives empower communities to better prepare for demographic challenges, ensuring that policy decisions are grounded in accurate, verifiable insights rather than speculation.
