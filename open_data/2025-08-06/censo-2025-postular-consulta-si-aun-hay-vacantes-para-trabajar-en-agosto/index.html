Censo 2025 Postular: Consulta si aún hay vacantes para trabajar en agosto

The INEI’s 2025 census initiative serves as a critical temporary employment avenue for Peruvian youth facing high rates of unemployment and disconnection from both education and the labor market. By opening over twenty thousand positions, the program aims to provide accessible work opportunities for young people aged 15 to 29 who are neither studying nor working, offering them a vital entry point into the workforce and a source of income despite the temporary nature of the contracts. However, the window for participation has closed, with registrations ending in July before the census operations commenced in August. The INEI has not confirmed any new calls for applicants, noting that selected personnel have already begun fieldwork. While replacement roles in specific zones remain a theoretical possibility, the immediate opportunity for new enrollment is unavailable, highlighting the competitive and time-sensitive nature of these public statistical operations. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the human infrastructure required to produce high-quality national statistics. The census is the primary engine for generating reliable demographic, economic, and social data that underpins public policy and open government initiatives. The rigorous selection and training of census takers ensure the integrity of the collected data, demonstrating that accurate open data ecosystems depend heavily on the successful mobilization and management of field personnel to translate real-world observations into structured, accessible information.
