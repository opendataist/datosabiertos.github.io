How Synthetic Data Is Becoming B2B CFOs’ Secret Weapon | PYMNTS.com

How Synthetic Data Is Becoming B2B CFOs’ Secret Weapon | PYMNTS.com

Traditional forecasting models are failing amid increasing global volatility, prompting B2B organizations to adopt synthetic data. Unlike consumer firms with vast behavioral datasets, B2B finance teams often lack the historical granularity needed for resilient predictive analytics. Consequently, CFOs are turning to artificially generated data that mimics real-world statistical properties to simulate market swings, anticipate supplier defaults, and visualize cash flow risks before they occur. This shift transforms treasury strategies from reactive to proactive, allowing companies to stress-test business performance against hypothetical crises like geopolitical shocks or cyberattacks. By programmatically generating diverse, plausible scenarios, organizations can enhance supply chain resilience and inventory planning in ways historical records cannot support. This capability enables finance leaders to collaborate more effectively with operations teams, actively shaping network architecture rather than merely tracking costs. For open data initiatives, this trend highlights a critical pathway for ethical data utilization. Because synthetic data avoids mapping to real individuals or entities, it resolves privacy concerns associated with sharing sensitive financial or operational information. This allows organizations to share and train AI models without violating regulations like GDPR, facilitating broader collaboration and innovation in data-driven sectors while maintaining strict compliance and security standards.
