Pepe Chedraui impulsa la transparencia con la presentación del Portal de Gobierno Abierto - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Pepe Chedraui impulsa la transparencia con la presentación del Portal de Gobierno Abierto - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The Government of the City of Puebla has launched the Open Government Portal, a digital platform designed to democratize access to public information. This centralized space provides updated data on budget execution, administrative procedures, services, and statistics, enabling citizens to clearly understand how public resources are managed. By prioritizing clarity and accessibility, the portal aims to strengthen accountability and bridge the gap between the administration and the population. A distinctive feature of this initiative is its focus on inclusion and active participation. The platform offers an accessible interface for people with visual or hearing disabilities, ensuring that information is available to everyone. Moreover, it not only informs but also invites dialogue, providing tools for informed decision-making and fostering citizen collaboration. This reinforces the administration’s commitment to efficiency, data protection, and technological innovation, positioning the municipality as a benchmark for transparency. This launch is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it exemplifies the practical application of open data principles to improve local governance. By transforming administrative data into understandable and accessible information, it empowers society to monitor government actions. This initiative demonstrates that digital transparency, when designed with inclusion in mind, is not merely a legal requirement but a powerful tool for building trust, facilitating civic participation, and establishing international standards for open government.
