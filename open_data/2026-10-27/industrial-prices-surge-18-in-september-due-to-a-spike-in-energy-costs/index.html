Industrial prices surge 18% in September due to a spike in energy costs

Industrial prices surge 18% in September due to a spike in energy costs

The Industrial Price Index shows a significant rebound, driven primarily by energy and intermediate goods. This dynamic reflects volatility in production costs and dependence on critical inputs, indicating inflationary pressure that could impact business competitiveness. Territorial variability exposes regional disparities in adapting to these price changes. Regions with greater exposure to the energy sector or manufacturing record higher rates, while others remain more stable, suggesting that economic resilience is neither homogeneous nor evenly distributed. This report is relevant to open data by highlighting the importance of accessing up-to-date and disaggregated economic statistics. Transparency in these indicators enables the analysis of macroeconomic trends, supports informed decision-making, and facilitates research into the industrial structure and its temporal evolution.
