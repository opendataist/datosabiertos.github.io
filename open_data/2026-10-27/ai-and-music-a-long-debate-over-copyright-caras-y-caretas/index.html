AI and music, a long debate over copyright - Caras y Caretas

AI and music, a long debate over copyright - Caras y Caretas

The legal dispute between Suno AI and prominent musicians highlights the tension between technological advancement and the protection of intellectual property rights. The central argument revolves around whether the imitation of styles and voices through artificial intelligence constitutes a violation or a legitimate use, thereby defining the ethical and legal boundaries of generative creation in the digital age. The agreement between Suno and Warner Music Group illustrates the evolution of the business model toward collaborative licensing, where major record labels seek revenue by permitting the use of their catalogs. However, this strategy does not address the concerns of many independent artists, who fear being displaced by massive, generic, low-quality music production that lacks human authenticity. This conflict is fundamental to the open data community because it establishes a critical precedent regarding the governance of training data. Determining which datasets can be used to develop commercial models directly impacts the transparency, ethics, and sustainability of open data ecosystems, demonstrating that technical freedom must be balanced with respect for the rights of original creators.
