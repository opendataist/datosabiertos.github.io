Labour Market Statistics

Labour Market Statistics

The latest Northern Ireland labor market report reveals a strengthening employment landscape, characterized by year-over-year growth in both job numbers and earnings. While monthly figures showed stabilization, the broader trend indicates expanding opportunities and rising wages, suggesting improving economic conditions for workers across various sectors. This growth underscores a recovery trajectory that has moved beyond immediate post-pandemic adjustments into a phase of sustained expansion. Despite these positive indicators, significant shifts in workforce stability and welfare reliance warrant attention. The data highlights a substantial rise in both proposed and confirmed redundancies compared to the previous year, signaling increased volatility in job security. Simultaneously, the claimant count has seen a marginal uptick, though it remains near levels established before the pandemic, reflecting a complex interplay between new job creation and ongoing structural challenges within the economy. This publication is critical for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of integrating multiple statistical sources—such as payroll records, surveys, and administrative claims—to provide a holistic view of labor dynamics. By making these diverse datasets accessible, researchers and policymakers can better analyze trends, identify discrepancies between different metrics, and develop evidence-based strategies that address both employment growth and job security risks.
