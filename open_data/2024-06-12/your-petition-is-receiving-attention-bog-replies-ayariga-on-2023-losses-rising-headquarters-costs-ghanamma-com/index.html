‘Your petition is receiving attention’ – BoG replies Ayariga on 2023 losses, rising headquarters costs – - Ghanamma.com

The Bank of Ghana acknowledges a lawmaker’s inquiry regarding its significant financial losses and escalating operational costs, confirming that the request for transparency is under active review. This interaction highlights the critical role of the Right to Information Act in enabling public officials and citizens to scrutinize central bank performance. By responding to these demands, the institution demonstrates how legal frameworks for open data facilitate accountability in monetary policy and fiscal management. The core implication is that transparency mechanisms are essential for maintaining public trust when financial institutions face substantial deficits. The request to disclose reasons behind high interest expenses and capital expenditures underscores the necessity of accessible financial records for democratic oversight. This case illustrates how open data initiatives can bridge the gap between governmental operations and public understanding. Ultimately, this episode reinforces the relevance of open data principles in ensuring that central banks remain accountable to the populace. It shows that information disclosure is not merely a procedural formality but a vital component of good governance. By adhering to information requests, the central bank supports a culture of openness where financial decisions are subject to public examination and scrutiny.
