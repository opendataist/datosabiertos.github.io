Ejecutivo estatal fortalece acciones de gobierno abierto

San Luis Potosí has launched an enhanced public information platform to improve transparency and citizens’ access to government data. This initiative centralizes a variety of datasets, including demographic censuses and poverty indicators, while introducing dynamic modules for tracking budget revenues and expenditures across different sectors. The system serves as a key tool for monitoring the state’s development plan and fiscal execution. By incorporating interactive designs and sector-specific analyses, it goes beyond static reporting to provide real-time visibility into public spending on education, health, and infrastructure, thereby strengthening institutional accountability. This development is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it demonstrates a practical application of making complex government statistics accessible and understandable to the public. It highlights how integrating multiple data sources into a unified, user-friendly interface can foster greater civic engagement and ensure that budgetary information is not only available, but truly transparent and actionable for all citizens.
