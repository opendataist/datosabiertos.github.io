Jalisco obtiene mejores calificaciones en transparencia

Jalisco achieved the fourth-highest national ranking in fiscal transparency, significantly outperforming the national average. This sustained improvement reflects a strong commitment to open data, demonstrating that proactive governance leads to superior accountability and public trust. The state’s consistently high scores across regulatory, budgetary, and statistical categories prove that institutional transparency is an ongoing, evolving effort. By maintaining quality despite national declines, Jalisco sets a standard for how government bodies can effectively manage and share financial information with citizens. This case is crucial for open data advocates as it validates the tangible benefits of rigorous information disclosure. It shows that when governments prioritize accessible, high-quality fiscal data, they not only meet legal obligations but also foster a culture of integrity and innovation, encouraging other regions to adopt similar transparency frameworks.
