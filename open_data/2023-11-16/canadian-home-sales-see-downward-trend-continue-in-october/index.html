Canadian Home Sales See Downward Trend Continue in October

Canadian Home Sales See Downward Trend Continue in October

Canadian real estate activity cooled significantly in October 2023, with monthly home sales dropping as buyers withdrew from the market while sellers paused plans until spring. Although year-over-year transactions slightly exceeded last year’s levels, the sharp monthly decline suggests a temporary hibernation driven by economic uncertainty and high interest rates. This shift created a more balanced environment, marked by a notable drop in the sales-to-new-listings ratio to a decade-low, indicating that the market is moving away from the intense competition seen earlier in the year. Prices exhibited mixed signals, with modest month-over-month declines primarily concentrated in Ontario, though national averages remained slightly higher than the previous year. Inventory levels began to rise, easing the scarcity that had characterized recent months, yet they remained below long-term averages. This dynamic suggests that while immediate demand is subdued, underlying housing needs remain strong, with any significant rebound in activity likely contingent on future monetary policy decisions by the central bank.
