INDEC publicará el próximo martes los datos defenitivos del Censo 2022 | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

INDEC will publish the final results of the national census on November 21, concluding an extended process due to the combination of digital and in-person bimodal methods. This delay highlights the logistical and technical complexity inherent in modernizing statistical surveys during the pandemic. The agency has already shared provisional indicators that reveal key data on population distribution, maintaining transparency throughout the lengthy consolidation period. This gradual approach allows society to access preliminary information while ensuring the accuracy of the final data. This publication is crucial for the open data movement, as it ensures the public availability and verifiability of complete census information. Transparency in official statistical processes strengthens trust in national data sources, enabling more informed social and political analysis based on real evidence.
