La Municipalidad de Paraná cuenta por primera vez con un portal de Gobierno Abierto

La Municipalidad de Paraná cuenta por primera vez con un portal de Gobierno Abierto

The administration of Intendant Adán Bahl has prioritized strengthening citizen trust through a modernization framework centered on transparency, participation, and cooperation. By digitizing public services, the municipality guarantees unrestricted, free access to government actions and data, establishing a historic precedent for open governance in the city. This approach aims to significantly improve the relationship between the local government and residents, moving beyond traditional bureaucratic interactions toward a more inclusive and responsive model of public administration. A cornerstone of this initiative is the establishment of robust Open Data and Transparency Budget portals, which allow citizens to consult and download detailed information regarding public administration, finance, urban planning, and other sectors. These platforms, supported by national laws and municipal ordinances, ensure that the public can access information about the administration's resources and expenditures. Furthermore, geographic mapping tools provide visual insights into public works and spaces, empowering citizens with the factual basis needed to understand and engage with municipal projects effectively. The strategy actively promotes citizen participation through mechanisms such as the revived Participatory Budget and dialogue tables, where residents collaborate in decision-making and resource allocation. Additionally, the government fosters collaboration with the private sector, civil associations, and academia to enhance public management. Through electronic government services, such as virtual assistants and online procedure portals, the city leverages technology to deliver efficient services, ensuring that openness and digital innovation serve as primary drivers for democratic strengthening and improved public service quality.
