Georgian Economy Grew 10.4% In 2022

Georgia’s economy experienced significant growth in 2022, with the National Statistics Office revising GDP figures to reflect a robust 10.4 percent increase. This adjustment highlights substantial economic expansion driven primarily by service sectors like wholesale trade and manufacturing, indicating strong underlying market vitality. The narrative reveals distinct sectoral performances, with information and communication leading real growth while healthcare contracted. These shifts underscore the dynamic nature of the national economy, suggesting structural changes that policymakers and investors must monitor to understand current economic trajectories accurately. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the importance of transparent, updated statistics for public trust and economic analysis. By making these revised figures accessible, Georgia supports evidence-based decision-making, enabling researchers and citizens to engage with reliable metrics that drive policy improvements and foster greater societal understanding of economic health.
