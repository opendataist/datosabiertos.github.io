Ministerio de Justicia explicó el decreto que permitiría que menores cambien su sexo en el registro civil | Noticias RCN

Ministerio de Justicia explicó el decreto que permitiría que menores cambien su sexo en el registro civil | Noticias RCN

The Ministry of Justice is drafting a decree to allow minors to change their gender in civil registries, aiming to protect trans individuals from marginalization and legal inconsistencies. This initiative prioritizes the recognition of self-identification to ensure formal spaces respect individual dignity, addressing historical vulnerabilities faced by this community. Legal experts remain divided on the proposal's validity. While proponents view it as a necessary recognition of identity, critics argue it is unconstitutional because altering minors' sex or gender falls under statutory law, requiring congressional action rather than executive decree. This debate highlights the tension between administrative efficiency and constitutional legal reserves. This article is relevant to open data as it underscores the critical link between accurate civil registration and data integrity. Ensuring public records reflect individual identity supports inclusive data practices, yet it also raises ethical questions about how sensitive personal information is managed, modified, and protected within government databases.
