Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production is vast, with China and India leading the market, while diverse nations contribute significantly to worldwide supply. The FAOSTAT database serves as a primary source for this comprehensive agricultural data, offering researchers and policymakers a standardized metric for understanding crop yields across hundreds of countries. This centralized repository enables the analysis of global food security trends and highlights the economic importance of staple crops in various regions. The availability of such detailed, open statistical data facilitates cross-border comparisons and supports evidence-based decision-making in agriculture and trade. By providing standardized figures for production volume and harvested area, these datasets allow for accurate assessments of efficiency and growth patterns. Stakeholders can leverage this information to identify emerging trends, assess market stability, and understand the distribution of resources, thereby enhancing transparency in the global food system. This resource is crucial for open data advocates because it demonstrates the value of accessible, high-quality government and international organization datasets. It underscores how public access to raw agricultural statistics empowers independent analysis and fosters innovation. Ultimately, the FAO’s commitment to sharing this data exemplifies best practices in open governance, proving that transparent information flows are essential for solving complex global challenges related to food production and sustainability.
