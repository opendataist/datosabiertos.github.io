Northern Ireland local authority collected municipal waste management statistics released

Northern Ireland local authority collected municipal waste management statistics released

Northern Ireland’s local authorities collected more municipal waste in early 2024, yet maintained stable recycling levels. While overall collection volumes rose, the proportion of waste diverted for recycling remained consistent with the previous year, indicating steady performance despite increased waste generation. The shift away from landfill is a critical success story, with disposal rates dropping significantly from historical highs. This transition reflects the long-term adoption of energy recovery methods, which have surged from negligible levels. This structural change in waste management reduces environmental impact and aligns with broader sustainability goals. This data highlights the transparency and accountability inherent in open data initiatives. By publishing detailed, quarterly statistics, the government enables citizens and organizations to track progress toward environmental targets. Such accessible information empowers communities to hold authorities accountable and fosters informed participation in sustainability efforts.
