Puerto Barú promete 1,200 empleos: esperanza para la juventud chiricana

Chiriquí is grappling with a severe youth unemployment crisis, which is driving educated professionals into informal, low-wage jobs. This structural failure underscores the disconnect between investment in education and local economic opportunities, highlighting a broader societal inefficiency in which human capital is wasted rather than effectively utilized. Major infrastructure projects, such as the Puerto Barú, are viewed as critical antidotes to reverse brain drain and stimulate the regional economy. However, bureaucratic inertia threatens to delay these initiatives, underscoring how administrative transparency and streamlined processes are essential for translating planned investments into tangible job creation. This case is crucial for open data discussions, as it illustrates the real-world impact of transparent information. Accessible, timely data on unemployment trends and project timelines empowers citizens to demand accountability, ensuring that public resources and development strategies are aligned with actual labor market needs rather than political promises.
