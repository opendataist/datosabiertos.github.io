SOAS challenge 'blanket ban' on FOI requests by health department

SOAS challenge 'blanket ban' on FOI requests by health department

The campaign Save Our Acute Services alleges that the Department of Health is systematically rejecting freedom of information requests regarding hospital reforms. By labeling inquiries about emergency service closures as vexatious due to their volume, officials are effectively imposing a blanket ban on transparency for these specific topics. This approach directly contradicts official commitments to conducting public service transformations openly and transparently. Campaigners argue that such restrictions represent a blatant attempt to evade public accountability, thereby undermining democratic principles and preventing citizens from understanding critical health service changes. This situation is crucial for open data advocates as it highlights the risk of administrative gatekeeping stifling information access. It underscores the vital importance of robust freedom of information laws to ensure government transparency, even when dealing with sensitive or controversial policy decisions that significantly impact community welfare.
