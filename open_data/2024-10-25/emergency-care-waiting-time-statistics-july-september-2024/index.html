Emergency Care Waiting Time Statistics (July - September 2024)

Emergency Care Waiting Time Statistics (July - September 2024)

Northern Ireland’s emergency care statistics reveal a troubling deterioration in patient throughput, with a significant rise in waiting times despite the phased rollout of the Encompass electronic patient record system. The data highlights that while new triage services like PhoneFirst successfully diverted some patients from emergency departments, overall performance against waiting time targets has declined. This suggests that digital transformation initiatives alone are insufficient to resolve systemic bottlenecks without accompanying operational improvements. The disparity in performance across different department types underscores the strain on primary emergency facilities. Type 1 emergency departments, which handle the most critical cases, show longer median times for both discharge and admission compared to the previous year. Meanwhile, smaller departments demonstrate higher compliance with four-hour targets, indicating that resource allocation and workflow efficiency vary drastically depending on facility size and type. This report is vital for open data advocates because it demonstrates the intersection of digital health infrastructure and public service accountability. By publishing granular, time-series metrics on patient journeys, the Department of Health enables independent scrutiny of how new technologies like Encompass impact real-world outcomes. It sets a precedent for transparency in health data, allowing citizens to evaluate whether digitization efforts are genuinely improving care quality or merely shifting administrative burdens.
