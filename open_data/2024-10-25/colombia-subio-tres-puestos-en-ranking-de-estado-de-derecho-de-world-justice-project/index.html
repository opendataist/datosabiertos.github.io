Colombia subió tres puestos en ranking de Estado de Derecho de World Justice Project

Colombia subió tres puestos en ranking de Estado de Derecho de World Justice Project

Colombia has shown notable progress in its Rule of Law Index, ranking 22nd regionally and moving up three positions compared to the previous year. This progress reflects significant improvements in government transparency and citizen security, consolidating mechanisms that facilitate access to justice and democratic participation. However, critical challenges persist in the fight against corruption and the efficiency of the criminal justice system. High rates of misappropriation of public funds and limited effectiveness in reducing crime indicate that, although there are institutional advances, the practical application of legality faces structural obstacles that require urgent attention from the State. This information is relevant to the field of open data, as it demonstrates that the mere publication of government information does not guarantee integrity or accountability. To transform transparency into real well-being, it is crucial to complement data availability with robust verification systems and analysis that enable effective monitoring of regulatory compliance and the reduction of impunity.
