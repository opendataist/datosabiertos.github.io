Políticas de privacidad | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Grupo Zócalo prioritizes the security of personal data by complying with federal protection laws, ensuring that user privacy is respected through the careful handling of provided information. While identifiable data remains protected, anonymized statistics may be shared with partners to enhance the value of the service. This commitment reflects broader open data principles by balancing transparency with individual rights. By implementing industry-standard consent frameworks, the platform demonstrates how organizations can responsibly manage data ecosystems. Such practices are relevant to open data as they establish trust. They illustrate the necessary foundation for public data initiatives, showing that secure, ethical data governance is essential for sustainable information sharing and societal benefit.
