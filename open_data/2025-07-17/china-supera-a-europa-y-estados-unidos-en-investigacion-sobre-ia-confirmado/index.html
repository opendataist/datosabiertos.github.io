China supera a Europa y Estados Unidos en investigación sobre IA - Confirmado

China supera a Europa y Estados Unidos en investigación sobre IA - Confirmado

China has emerged as the global leader in AI research, surpassing European, US, and British output in both publications and patents. This dominance is bolstered by massive investments in digital infrastructure and data centers, signaling a strategic shift in technological power. This shift matters for open data because it challenges Western-centric knowledge ecosystems. As China controls a significant share of academic citations and patents, diverse data sources become essential to prevent monopolization of innovation. Furthermore, the influx of Chinese research expands the global corpus of available knowledge. Open data initiatives must embrace this volume to ensure balanced insights, fostering collaboration rather than isolation in the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence.
