INEI: avanza cronograma oficial de postulación para censistas preseleccionados

INEI: avanza cronograma oficial de postulación para censistas preseleccionados

The National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) has launched a mandatory digital training program for thousands of candidates selected to serve as census takers for the 2025 National Census. This phase is critical and eliminatory, requiring the timely completion of online modules before candidates can proceed to in-person training and contract signing. The curriculum focuses on technical skills necessary for accurate data collection, marking the transition from paper-based forms to digital tablets. Candidates must master topics such as household structure and geolocation to ensure efficient field operations, ultimately culminating in the execution of the census later in the year. This initiative is vital for open data, as it establishes the primary mechanism for generating comprehensive and reliable national demographic and housing datasets. Ensuring rigorous training directly impacts data quality, which is foundational for transparency, public policy analysis, and the availability of high-integrity open government information.
