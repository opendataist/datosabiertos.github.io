El Consejo de Transparencia de C-LM insta a Sonseca a actualizar su portal de transparencia tras una denuncia de IU

El Consejo de Transparencia de C-LM insta a Sonseca a actualizar su portal de transparencia tras una denuncia de IU

The Sonseca Town Council systematically fails to maintain its Transparency Portal, neglecting critical financial, contractual, and official asset information. This breach of legal obligations contradicts citizens' rights to immediate access to municipal decisions, effectively enforcing a policy of opacity rather than openness. Although funds are reportedly allocated for updates, the service is criticized for prioritizing political propaganda over statutory transparency duties. This approach highlights how external management can undermine public accountability when not strictly aligned with open governance standards. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the tangible risks of inadequate implementation. It underscores the necessity of verifying that transparency portals are truly accessible, comprehensive, and free from political bias to ensure genuine civic engagement.
