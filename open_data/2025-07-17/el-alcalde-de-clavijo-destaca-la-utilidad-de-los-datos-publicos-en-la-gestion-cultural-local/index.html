El alcalde de Clavijo destaca "la utilidad de los datos públicos en la gestión cultural local"

El alcalde de Clavijo destaca "la utilidad de los datos públicos en la gestión cultural local"

The article highlights the growing recognition of public data as a vital tool for local cultural management. Mayor Pedro Muro emphasized that access to public industry information empowers municipalities to make informed decisions and understand how other councils operate in similar contexts. This data-driven approach allows for more effective stewardship of cultural heritage and resources within local communities. Collaboration with national entities such as the Báculo Platform and SGAE is crucial for enabling this data analysis. By leveraging these partnerships, local governments can better navigate the cultural sector, utilizing shared administrative data to enhance their own services. This synergy between local and national levels fosters a more transparent and efficient cultural ecosystem, where evidence-based strategies replace guesswork. This is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates its practical utility beyond transparency. When cultural data is openly shared and accessible, it facilitates the exchange of best practices among municipalities. Consequently, open data becomes a catalyst for cooperation, helping local governments optimize their cultural policies and strengthen community networks through shared insights and collaborative improvement.
