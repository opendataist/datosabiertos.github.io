MPs trained to trace hidden wealth and tackle corruption

MPs trained to trace hidden wealth and tackle corruption

Ghana’s Parliament recently trained legislators on Beneficial Ownership Transparency to expose hidden corporate ownership and combat corruption. This initiative empowers lawmakers to effectively monitor compliance and hold institutions accountable, directly supporting the nation’s commitment to open government standards. The training aligns Ghana’s governance practices with global anti-corruption efforts, focusing on preventing illicit financial flows and tax evasion. By strengthening legal frameworks, the country aims to ensure that legislative actions truly address citizen needs and foster greater public trust in democratic processes. This development is highly relevant to open data as it highlights the critical role of transparent datasets in enabling effective parliamentary oversight. Making beneficial ownership information accessible allows for rigorous scrutiny of public and private sectors, proving that open data is essential for enforcing accountability and reducing systemic corruption.
