Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

Deputies from the Civic Coalition propose the creation of an Open Import Data Portal to ensure transparency in the granting of licenses. The main objective is to combat administrative discretion and the risk of corruption inherent in the current system. By requiring that information about applications be published in full, updated, and in open formats, the initiative seeks to democratize access to public data and ensure that processes are predictable and traceable for any citizen. The project aims to replace the existing authorization mechanisms, which have been characterized as unreasonable and prone to abuse, with a regime of maximum simplicity. It establishes that decisions must be made in chronological order and respect strict deadlines, under the sanction that licenses will be deemed approved if the State fails to respond in a timely manner. Additionally, it mandates the publication of criteria for assessing economic capacity to eliminate arbitrariness in the evaluation of taxpayers and reduce distortion in the foreign exchange market. This initiative is crucial for the open data movement because it demonstrates how the proactive publication of government information serves as a fundamental tool against corruption. By turning operational data into accessible public goods, it empowers civil society to monitor government actions. This approach reinforces the principle that transparency is not only a legal requirement but also an effective strategy for improving governance and institutional trust in economic management.
