This content is relevant to open data because the accelerating AI boom and increased regulatory scrutiny directly impact data governance frameworks. As companies like Meta and Alibaba expand their digital footprints, the need for transparent data practices becomes critical. Understanding these market shifts helps stakeholders anticipate how open data initiatives might be constrained or enabled by emerging geopolitical and regulatory landscapes.
