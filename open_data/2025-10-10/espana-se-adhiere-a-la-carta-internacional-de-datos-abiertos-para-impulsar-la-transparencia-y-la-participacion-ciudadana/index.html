España se adhiere a la Carta Internacional de Datos Abiertos para impulsar la transparencia y la participación ciudadana

España se adhiere a la Carta Internacional de Datos Abiertos para impulsar la transparencia y la participación ciudadana

Spain has officially joined the International Open Data Charter, a global initiative aimed at enhancing transparency, innovation, and civic participation through the proactive release of public information. This commitment underscores a strategic shift towards making data accessible, understandable, and interoperable by default, ensuring that citizens can leverage open information to improve public services and foster inclusive economic development. The adoption of this framework reinforces Spain’s broader engagement with open government principles, as highlighted in the recently approved fifth Open Government Plan. By aligning with international standards, the administration seeks to strengthen accountability and democratic governance, ensuring that data policies respect fundamental rights while promoting social impact. This move signals a robust dedication to building more responsive and inclusive public institutions. This development is crucial for the open data community as it validates the global movement toward data as a public good. It encourages other nations to adopt similar responsible policies, prioritizing quality and usability over mere publication. Ultimately, Spain’s adherence to these standards contributes to a worldwide ecosystem where data drives democratic engagement, fosters trust in public institutions, and empowers civil society through transparent, high-quality information.
