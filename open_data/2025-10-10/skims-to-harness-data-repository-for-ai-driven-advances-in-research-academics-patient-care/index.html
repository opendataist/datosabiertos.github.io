SKIMS to harness data repository for AI-driven advances in research, academics & patient care

SKIMS to harness data repository for AI-driven advances in research, academics & patient care

SKIMS is leveraging its decades-long, comprehensive health data repository to drive transformative advances in medical research, education, and patient care through Artificial Intelligence. By treating this extensive information as a critical asset, the institute aims to enhance precision and contextual understanding in clinical practice, effectively addressing resource limitations while elevating the quality of healthcare delivery. The initiative focuses on integrating human intelligence with machine capabilities to improve diagnostic accuracy, predictive analytics, and personalized treatment planning. This strategic alignment supports broader national visions for reshaping healthcare, ensuring that institutions remain at the forefront of digital innovation. The collaboration emphasizes ethical governance and data security, ensuring that technological adoption respects patient privacy and accountability. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it highlights the immense value hidden within institutional archives. It underscores the necessity of making such large-scale, structured datasets accessible for AI training and analysis. By unlocking these resources, SKIMS demonstrates how open or shared data ecosystems can accelerate scientific discovery and improve public health outcomes through evidence-based, data-driven decision-making.
