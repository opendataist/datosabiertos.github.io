El derecho de acceso a la información está garantizado en Michoacán: Dip. Conrado Paz • Nsíntesis

El derecho de acceso a la información está garantizado en Michoacán: Dip. Conrado Paz • Nsíntesis

The article asserts that the fundamental human right to access public information remains fully guaranteed in Michoacán, despite the dissolution of the autonomous transparency institute. It clarifies that while the institutional oversight body has been extinguished, the obligations of transparency for public subjects have not been suspended. This ensures that citizens’ ability to request and receive information continues uninterrupted, maintaining the legal framework necessary for democratic accountability even during the transition period. Currently, officials are working on defining secondary laws that will establish new guidelines and reassign guarantees to each branch of government. Although legal processing times for information requests still run, some pending cases from the former institute remain unresolved until the new guaranteeing organs are formally established. This interim structure aims to ensure continuity, with administrative adjustments and structural modifications planned to align with the upcoming legislative definitions and empower new institutional actors. This development is crucial for open data because it highlights the resilience of transparency rights during institutional crises. It demonstrates that the obligation to be transparent is inherent to state function, not merely an administrative task. As Michoacán transitions to new oversight mechanisms, it reinforces the principle that open data and access to information are indispensable tools for civic participation and governmental honesty, requiring robust legal frameworks to sustain them effectively.
