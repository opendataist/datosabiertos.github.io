Convocatoria Tocando Corazones: Bolsa de apoyos asciende a los 384 millones de pesos

Convocatoria Tocando Corazones: Bolsa de apoyos asciende a los 384 millones de pesos

The government of Guanajuato has launched the second phase of the Tocando Corazones program, allocating hundreds of millions of pesos to social organizations. This initiative replaces the former Fidesseg trust and aims to fund impactful community projects, ensuring that civil society groups receive necessary financial support for their humanitarian efforts. The funding is distributed across different state regions to maximize local development and address vulnerabilities where they are most needed. A key innovation of this new model is its operational structure following legal disputes over the previous system. Resources are clearly divided between social development projects and public safety initiatives, with specific funds derived from payroll taxes dedicated to security. This bifurcation allows for a more targeted approach to governance, separating social aid from safety measures while maintaining a unified framework for resource allocation and oversight. This case is highly relevant to open data because the administration emphasizes transparency and public access to information. By requiring project registration through an official online platform and publishing detailed operational rules, the government creates a digital infrastructure for tracking funds and outcomes. This approach promotes accountability and allows citizens to monitor how resources are assigned, demonstrating how open data practices can enhance trust in public spending and facilitate civic participation in social programs.
