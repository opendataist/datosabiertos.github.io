Llevará Inegi a cabo la Encuesta Intercensal 2025

Llevará Inegi a cabo la Encuesta Intercensal 2025

The INEGI’s 2025 Intercensal Survey collects critical sociodemographic data across Mexico, ensuring statistical continuity between national censuses. This initiative provides updated insights into living conditions and housing characteristics, which are essential for accurate regional estimations. These findings directly support the design and implementation of public policies at all levels of government. By providing reliable input for decision-making, the survey strengthens the public, social, private, and academic sectors in understanding post-pandemic population dynamics. The relevance to open data lies in the commitment to transparency and accessibility. INEGI will publish anonymized microdata alongside tabulations and applications, enabling specialized analysis and fostering evidence-based research for a wide range of users and stakeholders.
