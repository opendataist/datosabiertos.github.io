Implementa Municipio acciones por la transparencia proactiva

Implementa Municipio acciones por la transparencia proactiva

Chihuahua achieves national leadership in transparency by proactively publishing essential information without waiting for citizen requests. This approach enhances accountability and empowers the public to actively participate in decision-making processes regarding municipal resources and policies. The administration implements diverse initiatives, including a Government Open Action Plan developed with civil society and private partners. Additionally, physical information points and a citizen council ensure direct oversight and clear guidance, fostering a modern, efficient government centered on public empowerment and rights. This case demonstrates how institutionalized transparency serves as a practical framework for open data ecosystems. It highlights the critical role of accessible, proactive information in building trust and enabling meaningful civic engagement through structured, multi-stakeholder collaboration.
