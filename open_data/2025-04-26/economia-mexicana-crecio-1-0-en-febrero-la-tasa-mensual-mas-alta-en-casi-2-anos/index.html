Economía mexicana creció 1.0% en febrero; la tasa mensual más alta en casi 2 años

Mexico’s economy demonstrated robust growth in February, driven primarily by expansions in the industrial and service sectors. This momentum reflects strong manufacturing and construction activity, alongside dynamic professional and recreational services, signaling healthy economic progress. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the critical role of transparent, timely statistical indicators such as the Global Economic Activity Indicator. Open access to such granular economic metrics enables analysts and the public to track real-time trends, fostering better-informed decision-making and greater accountability in economic policy. The availability of disaggregated data allows stakeholders to identify specific growth areas, such as business support services. Ultimately, making these detailed economic signals openly accessible supports a more responsive and data-driven understanding of national economic health.
