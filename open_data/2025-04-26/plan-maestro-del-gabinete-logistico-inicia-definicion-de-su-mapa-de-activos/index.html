Plan Maestro del Gabinete logístico inicia definición de su mapa de activos

Plan Maestro del Gabinete logístico inicia definición de su mapa de activos

Panama is developing a comprehensive master plan to map and optimize its national logistical assets, including ports, airports, and transit corridors. This initiative aims to align public infrastructure investment with private sector needs to fully realize the country’s potential in logistics services. The strategy emphasizes digital transformation and coordinated governance across multiple ministries and agencies. By integrating electronic systems for vessel registration and standardized planning, the government seeks to enhance operational efficiency and transparency in the sector. This update is relevant to open data as it highlights the necessity of creating a centralized, accessible inventory of public infrastructure. Openly sharing such maps and development plans fosters accountability, supports private sector innovation, and enables data-driven decision-making for sustainable logistical growth.
