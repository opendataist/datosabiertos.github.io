Destruye INE Quintana Roo más de 27 mil credenciales - Noticaribe

Destruye INE Quintana Roo más de 27 mil credenciales - Noticaribe

The National Electoral Institute (INE) in Quintana Roo destroyed thousands of unused or returned voter identification cards to protect the security of personal data. This strict adherence to regulatory guidelines ensures the integrity of the electoral registry while safeguarding citizens’ information. Residual credentials for those who did not collect them remain safely stored until after the upcoming election. At the same time, citizens are reminded that they can reprint lost cards, which retain their original validity without alteration, ensuring continuous access to the necessary identification. This procedure highlights the critical intersection of electoral logistics and open data principles. By prioritizing the secure disposal and management of sensitive voter data, the INE demonstrates transparency and accountability. Such practices are vital for maintaining public trust in democratic institutions and ensuring that personal information remains protected throughout the electoral cycle.
