Coahuila arranca proceso de homologación constitucional en materia de transparencia

Coahuila arranca proceso de homologación constitucional en materia de transparencia

Coahuila updates its constitutional framework to align with federal transparency reforms, strengthening public access to information and data protection. By clarifying institutional responsibilities across branches, the state ensures efficient handling of information requests while prioritizing human rights. This adjustment fosters coordinated governance, establishing clear protocols for reviewing access requests and safeguarding personal data. It promotes accountability within executive bodies and municipalities, ensuring that administrative processes respect citizens' fundamental rights to transparency and privacy. The reform is vital for open data because it harmonizes local regulations with national standards, creating a more predictable legal environment for data sharing. This alignment enhances institutional trust and ensures that public information remains accessible, accurate, and protected, supporting broader open government initiatives.
