El titular del Indec dio detalles de cómo va a ser el nuevo índice de inflación: suman relevancia Netflix, celulares y otros consumos - EL DEBATE

El titular del Indec dio detalles de cómo va a ser el nuevo índice de inflación: suman relevancia Netflix, celulares y otros consumos - EL DEBATE

Argentina’s National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) plans to update the consumer price index (CPI) basket to include contemporary goods and services, such as streaming platforms and mobile telephony. The main objective is to more accurately reflect the population’s new consumption habits, recognizing the evolution of the digital economy and modern everyday transactions, without significantly altering the final results or the historical inflation trend. Although the implementation date has yet to be determined, this methodological renewal aims to improve the quality and quantity of available statistical data. By adjusting the weights of economic sectors and integrating essential elements of contemporary life, the index seeks to provide a more comprehensive and faithful picture of economic reality, ensuring that measurements remain representative in light of recent social and technological changes. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for official statistics to evolve in order to capture emerging economic phenomena, such as digital consumption. Transparency in methodology and the availability of up-to-date data are essential for civil society, academia, and developers to rigorously analyze purchasing power and market dynamics, ensuring that public information is useful, comparable, and aligned with citizens’ real-life experiences.
