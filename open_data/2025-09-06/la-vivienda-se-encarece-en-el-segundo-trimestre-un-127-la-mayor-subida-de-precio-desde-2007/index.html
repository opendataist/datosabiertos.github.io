La vivienda se encarece en el segundo trimestre un 12,7%, la mayor subida de precio desde 2007

The article reveals a historic surge in housing prices, marking the steepest increase since 2007. Driven by robust demand and restrictive supply conditions, this trend underscores a critical imbalance in the real estate market. The persistence of consecutive price hikes across all regions highlights a systemic issue where inventory cannot meet consumer needs, creating sustained financial pressure on buyers. Experts emphasize that this escalation stems from a structural gap between limited supply and growing demand, rather than speculative excess. Although mortgage conditions are favorable, they remain prudent, preventing a bubble formation. However, the scarcity of available homes continues to outpace sales volumes, making housing accessibility increasingly difficult for those unable to compete in a tight market. This situation is highly relevant to open data because transparent, timely statistical indicators like the Housing Price Index are essential for diagnosing market health. Reliable data empowers policymakers and citizens to understand affordability trends and evaluate the impact of housing policies. Without such open access to accurate metrics, stakeholders cannot effectively address the supply-demand disparity or make informed decisions regarding urban planning and financial regulation.
