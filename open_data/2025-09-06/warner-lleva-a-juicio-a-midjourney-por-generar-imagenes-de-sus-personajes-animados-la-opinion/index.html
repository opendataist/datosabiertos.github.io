Warner lleva a juicio a Midjourney por generar imágenes de sus personajes animados - La Opinión

Warner lleva a juicio a Midjourney por generar imágenes de sus personajes animados - La Opinión

Warner Bros. Discovery has filed a lawsuit against Midjourney, alleging copyright infringement due to the unauthorized use of iconic characters to generate images with artificial intelligence. The company argues that the platform deliberately removed its protective mechanisms to enable this creation, seeking monetary damages and a court order to prevent the distribution of content derived from its intellectual property. For the average user, the immediate risk falls on the platform rather than the individual, although Midjourney is expected to implement strict restrictions on its tools. This will significantly limit the ability to generate content based on protected characters, marking a shift in the user experience toward greater automatic filtering of prohibited prompts. This case is crucial for open data and AI ethics, as it seeks to establish precedents on whether training with copyrighted works constitutes plagiarism. A favorable resolution could force AI tools to incorporate automated technical safeguards, redefining how intellectual property is managed in the development of generative models and ensuring that artificial creation does not dilute the value of original works.
