MRT service reliability dips to lowest point since 2020, LRT sees improvement from 2024

MRT service reliability has deteriorated to its worst level since 2020, marking a concerning shift from previous years of stability. This decline highlights the fragility of current transit infrastructure and suggests that maintenance challenges are outpacing performance targets, potentially eroding public trust in urban mobility systems. The disparity between MRT and LRT performance reveals distinct operational issues within Singapore’s rail network. While LRT services have improved significantly, the MRT struggles with longer intervals between major delays, indicating that specific lines require more robust technical interventions and resource allocation to restore consistent service standards. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible transport metrics. By making such reliability statistics openly available, stakeholders can better analyze systemic vulnerabilities, advocate for policy improvements, and hold authorities accountable for maintaining high-quality public transportation services for all citizens.
