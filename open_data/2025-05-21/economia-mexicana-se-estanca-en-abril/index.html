Economía mexicana se estanca en abril

Economía mexicana se estanca en abril

Mexico’s economy showed signs of stagnation in April 2025, with slight declines in both industry and services following March’s contraction. This near-zero growth highlights the challenges in sustaining economic momentum amidst shifting sectoral performances. The rapid release of these projections via the IOAE offers crucial early insights, significantly outpacing traditional official data releases by five weeks. This timeliness allows for more immediate policy adjustments and market reactions. This scenario is vital for open data advocates, demonstrating how accessible, frequent, and timely public statistics empower stakeholders. By providing early visibility into economic health, such datasets enhance transparency and facilitate faster, data-driven decision-making in real time.
