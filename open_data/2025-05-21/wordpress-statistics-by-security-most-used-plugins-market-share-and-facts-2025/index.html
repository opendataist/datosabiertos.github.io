WordPress Statistics By Security, Most Used Plugins, Market Share And Facts (2025)

WordPress Statistics By Security, Most Used Plugins, Market Share And Facts (2025)

WordPress has evolved from a simple blogging tool into the dominant open-source content management system, powering nearly half of all websites on the internet. Its widespread adoption is driven by its user-friendly interface and extensive plugin ecosystem, which allows individuals and businesses to build complex digital products like e-commerce stores without requiring coding expertise. This accessibility democratizes web publishing, enabling anyone to establish an online presence and reach global audiences efficiently. Despite its popularity, the platform faces significant security challenges, including frequent attacks and vulnerabilities within its vast library of third-party plugins. The open-source nature of WordPress fosters a vibrant community that continuously develops themes and extensions, but this also creates a wide surface area for cyber threats. Consequently, maintaining security and keeping software updated are critical responsibilities for users, highlighting the ongoing need for robust community support and vigilance in the open-source development cycle. This article is highly relevant to open data as it underscores the importance of transparency and community-driven innovation inherent in open-source technologies. The sheer volume of WordPress usage generates massive amounts of public data, offering valuable insights for researchers analyzing web trends, security risks, and user behavior. Furthermore, the platform’s open architecture serves as a case study for how collaborative development models can scale globally, influencing discussions on data sovereignty, digital infrastructure, and the accessibility of digital tools for diverse populations.
