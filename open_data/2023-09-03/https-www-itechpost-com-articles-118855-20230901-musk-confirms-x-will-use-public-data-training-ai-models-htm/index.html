X’s updated privacy policy permits using public user data to train artificial intelligence models, a move confirmed by Elon Musk. While private direct messages remain excluded, the scope of collectible public information has expanded significantly. This shift allows X to potentially feed data into Musk’s xAI venture, aligning the social platform’s resources with his broader artificial intelligence ambitions. The integration suggests a strategic deepening of ties between the social network and the new AI development company. For open data, this highlights the increasing commodification of public social media information for commercial AI training. It underscores the need for clear governance regarding how publicly accessible data can be repurposed, raising questions about user consent and the ethical boundaries of using social interactions for machine learning development.
