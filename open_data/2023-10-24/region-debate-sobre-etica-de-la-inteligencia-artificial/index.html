Región debate sobre ética de la inteligencia artificial

Latin American countries are establishing a regional council to implement UNESCO’s AI ethics guidelines. This initiative aligns with open data principles by setting transparent and ethical standards for digital governance. It promotes regional cooperation to ensure that AI development remains accountable, public, and consistent with international human rights frameworks.
