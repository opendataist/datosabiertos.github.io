Abinader defiende transparencia con estadísticas sobre casos dengue

The Dominican government stresses absolute transparency regarding dengue statistics, dismissing allegations of data concealment as dishonest and likening them to the misinformation that circulated during the pandemic. Officials maintain that while methodological errors may occur, there is no intentional manipulation of the figures. This position seeks to preserve public trust by ensuring that health metrics accurately reflect the true incidence and mortality rates of the disease. Ministerial reports show a declining trend in dengue cases, with the rate of positive confirmations among suspected cases dropping significantly. Authorities attribute this improvement to recent fumigation efforts and clarify that other circulating viruses often mimic dengue symptoms, complicating diagnosis. As a result, the epidemiological alert remains in effect, urging vigilance as the government works to minimize deaths and further reduce transmission through targeted health interventions. Beyond immediate outbreak control, the administration highlights substantial investments in healthcare infrastructure, including hospital renovations and expanded insurance coverage. These initiatives aim to improve overall health outcomes, as evidenced by reduced maternal mortality and increased access to specialized oncology and trauma care. This context underscores the importance of open data in validating public health successes and demonstrating the government’s commitment to systemic improvements in national health services.
