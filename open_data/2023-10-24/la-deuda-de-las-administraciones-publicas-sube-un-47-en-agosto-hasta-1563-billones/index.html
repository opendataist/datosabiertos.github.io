La deuda de las Administraciones Públicas sube un 4,7% en agosto, hasta 1,563 billones

Public administration debt reached 1.563 trillion euros in August, marking a 4.7% annual increase. However, this figure remained below the historical peak recorded in June, indicating a slight stabilization in the overall debt trajectory despite continued expansion. State debt grew significantly, reaching 1.397 trillion euros, while social security debt also increased. Conversely, other central administration units saw a notable decrease in debt, and local government debt experienced a marginal decline compared to the previous year, reflecting divergent trends across administrative sectors. This data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the importance of accessible, real-time fiscal transparency. Open datasets enable researchers and citizens to monitor government liabilities effectively, fostering accountability and informed public discourse regarding economic sustainability and policy impacts.
