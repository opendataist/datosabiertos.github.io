Local authorities in Catamarca are expanding mortgage credit programs to increase homeownership rates among residents. This strategy aims to stimulate the local economy through increased construction activity, job creation, and reduced rental expenses for new owners. Such initiatives highlight how public financial instruments can drive social welfare and economic growth simultaneously. By making housing accessible, these policies create a ripple effect across various sectors, from construction materials to professional services, benefiting the broader community. This case is relevant to open data because the transparent publication of loan distribution, demographic impact, and economic outcomes allows researchers and citizens to analyze housing policies. Open data fosters accountability and enables evidence-based improvements in future social housing strategies.
