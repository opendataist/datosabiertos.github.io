Actividad económica de México avanza un 2,5% interanual en abril 2023

Mexico’s economy showed resilience in April, growing by 2.5% year-on-year, driven primarily by strong performance across all sectors, particularly services. This growth follows a robust first quarter and points to a positive short-term economic trajectory, reinforcing expectations of sustained annual expansion around three percent. These figures underscore the importance of high-frequency economic indicators, such as the IGAE, for monitoring real-time market conditions. By capturing sectoral shifts promptly, these metrics enable analysts to track recovery patterns more effectively than slower annual reports, providing essential granularity for economic assessment. The relevance to open data lies in the value of accessible, timely statistical releases from institutions like INEGI. Transparent dissemination of these indicators empowers researchers and citizens to validate economic narratives, fostering informed public discourse and enabling better analysis of post-pandemic recovery trends through reliable, publicly available datasets.
