Updated GeoThopica 2.0 geothermal data portal launched in Italy

Updated GeoThopica 2.0 geothermal data portal launched in Italy

CNR-IGG has launched GeoThopica 2.0, significantly expanding the Italian geothermal data infrastructure to support advanced energy research. By integrating extensive subsoil and geochemical records with new AI-driven global evaluation maps, the platform enhances the precision and scope of geothermal potential assessments. This evolution reflects a major scaling of available data, enabling more comprehensive analysis for sustainable resource management. The update adheres strictly to open science principles and FAIR data guidelines, ensuring information is findable, accessible, interoperable, and re-usable. This commitment to transparent data governance facilitates broader collaboration among researchers and industries. By guaranteeing open access to specialized thesauruses, metadata, and scientific literature, the portal removes traditional barriers to knowledge sharing in the geoscience community. This initiative is vital for the open_data movement as it demonstrates how domain-specific infrastructure can successfully implement interoperability standards while driving innovation. It highlights the critical role of standardized, machine-actionable data in leveraging artificial intelligence for environmental solutions. Consequently, GeoThopica 2.0 serves as a model for other fields seeking to balance robust scientific rigor with the democratic access essential for modern open research ecosystems.
