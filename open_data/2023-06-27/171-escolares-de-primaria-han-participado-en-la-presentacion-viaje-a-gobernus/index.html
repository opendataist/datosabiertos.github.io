171 escolares de Primaria han participado en la presentación 'Viaje a Gobernus'

The Government of Navarre has launched "Viaje a Gobernus," an educational comic designed to teach primary school students about democratic principles such as transparency and participation. Through an engaging narrative in which children explore an alien planet, the initiative successfully introduces complex governance concepts in an age-appropriate manner. This approach demonstrates how open data and government openness can be effectively communicated to younger audiences through accessible, creative storytelling rather than technical jargon. To ensure deeper understanding, the comic was presented through a theatrical workshop where students practiced collective decision-making. This practical application of values such as listening, collaboration, and consensus reinforces the link between civic participation and active citizenship. It highlights the importance of experiential learning in fostering a culture of openness and engagement from an early age. This article is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical need for transparency and public involvement in governing systems. By cultivating these values in children, the project supports the long-term sustainability of open government initiatives. It shows how educational tools can bridge the gap between abstract data concepts and community understanding, ensuring that open data policies are not just technical mandates but shared democratic values.
