Elevan el tope para usuarios electrodependientes a 3,5 veces la Canasta Básica Total

Elevan el tope para usuarios electrodependientes a 3,5 veces la Canasta Básica Total

The National Electric Regulatory Entity has significantly raised the income threshold for health-related electro-dependent users to access special electricity benefits. By linking the limit to 3.5 times the Basic Basket, the measure expands eligibility beyond the most impoverished sectors. This shift recognizes that previous caps excluded vulnerable families with slightly higher incomes who still cannot afford necessary medical connections. The update prioritizes social protection for hyper-vulnerable populations, ensuring that essential alternative energy sources remain accessible. Rather than focusing solely on extreme poverty, the new criterion considers total household net income. This holistic approach prevents situations where families are ineligible for support despite lacking the resources to cover high connection costs for life-sustaining equipment. This case is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the necessity of integrating public statistical indices into policy-making. Transparent data on household consumption and income levels enables regulators to design fair, evidence-based subsidies. Open data allows for the monitoring of these thresholds, ensuring that social protections remain dynamic and responsive to economic realities, thereby effectively safeguarding the rights of marginalized groups.
