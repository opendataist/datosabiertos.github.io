Open Data Standard for packaging expanded to cover all common packaging materials

The Open 3P Data Standard has expanded beyond plastic to encompass all major packaging materials, establishing a universal framework for consistent data collection and sharing. Developed collaboratively by industry partners and maintained as a free-to-use open standard, it provides a common "recipe" for structuring packaging information without storing the data itself. This approach ensures that manufacturers, retailers, and recyclers can easily exchange compatible information, fostering transparency across the entire supply chain. This standardization is particularly relevant amid tightening environmental regulations, such as Extended Producer Responsibility laws, which require detailed reporting on packaging materials. By offering a flexible, tiered structure, the standard allows organizations to start with basic details and gradually add complexity as needed. This scalability helps reduce the administrative burden of compliance while facilitating faster data requests and streamlining routine operations for stakeholders managing sustainable packaging portfolios. For open data initiatives, this article highlights the practical application of interoperable standards in driving environmental sustainability. By enabling granular visibility into packaging composition, Open 3P supports better recycling outcomes and aids global efforts like the UN Treaty on plastic pollution. It demonstrates how collaborative, open frameworks can align diverse industry players around shared data practices, ultimately lowering the environmental footprint through improved understanding and efficient resource management.
