Artistas de EEUU piden protección contra la Inteligencia Artificial

Artists warn that unchecked AI threatens human creativity by exploiting creative works without fair compensation. This collective action highlights critical open data concerns regarding the ethical sourcing of training data from copyrighted material. The dispute underscores the urgent need for transparent data policies that respect intellectual property rights in AI development.
