Indian Railways Earned Rs 5800 Crore By Denying Senior Citizen Concessions - Trak.in - Indian Business of Tech, Mobile & Startups

The removal of fare concessions for senior citizens significantly boosted Indian Railways' revenue, demonstrating how policy changes directly impact financial outcomes. This case highlights the critical role of transparency in public sectors, showing that accessible data can reveal the substantial economic effects of administrative decisions on vulnerable groups. Furthermore, it underscores the power of the Right to Information Act in holding government bodies accountable. By forcing the disclosure of financial data, citizens can scrutinize how public resources are managed and ensure that policy shifts are justified beyond mere revenue generation. This article is relevant to open data because it proves that releasing granular, time-series financial data allows for independent analysis of public policy. When governments share such datasets, it enables researchers and the public to validate claims and understand the real-world implications of structural changes, fostering a more informed and engaged society.
