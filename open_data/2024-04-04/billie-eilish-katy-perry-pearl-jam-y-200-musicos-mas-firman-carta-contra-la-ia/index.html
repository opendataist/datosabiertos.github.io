Billie Eilish, Katy Perry, Pearl Jam y 200 músicos más firman carta contra la IA

Artists and authors have filed lawsuits against OpenAI, alleging that their protected works were used to train large language models without permission. This legal action highlights a critical tension regarding the use of copyrighted material in artificial intelligence development. The situation remains unresolved, as users can still generate content mimicking specific authors' styles on platforms like ChatGPT. This reality underscores the challenges in regulating AI tools that rely on vast datasets. This case is relevant to open data as it raises ethical questions about transparency and consent in data sourcing. It emphasizes the need for clear guidelines on how data is collected and utilized to respect intellectual property rights.
