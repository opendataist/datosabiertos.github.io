La nueva zanahoria que muestra ChatGPT: ahora se puede usar el chatbot sin una cuenta

OpenAI has removed the requirement to create an account to access the free version of ChatGPT, which uses the GPT-3.5 model. This decision aims to democratize access to generative artificial intelligence, allowing anyone to experience its benefits without initial registration barriers, while keeping credentials necessary only for premium features or enhanced security functions. The implementation includes additional restrictions for anonymous users, such as limits on image generation and stricter blocks, designed by specialized teams to mitigate abuse. These protocols ensure that opening access does not compromise the platform's security, while valuable data is collected for the system's continuous training, recognizing that the volume of interactions is crucial for improving the tool. This change is relevant to open_data because it illustrates how major technology corporations manage access to their tools: they offer free samples to accumulate massive amounts of data that improve their algorithms, while maintaining restrictive ownership over the most valuable assets. The strategy reflects a model where the "trial" serves as a means to generate insights and data, ultimately incentivizing conversion to paid services, raising ethical questions about transparency in the use of public data to train private systems.
