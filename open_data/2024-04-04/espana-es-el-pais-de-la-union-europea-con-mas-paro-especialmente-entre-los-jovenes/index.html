España es el país de la Unión Europea con más paro, especialmente entre los jóvenes

The Eurozone’s unemployment rate has fallen below pre-pandemic levels, highlighting a complex labor market recovery. While total unemployment decreased slightly across the broader EU, the Eurozone experienced a marginal rise, indicating divergent trends among member states. Significant disparities persist among countries, with high unemployment in Spain and Greece contrasting sharply with low rates in Central Europe. Youth unemployment remains a critical challenge, particularly in Spain, where it stands as the highest in the bloc, underscoring persistent structural issues for young workers despite overall economic stabilization. This data is relevant to open data because transparent, standardized statistical releases enable researchers and policymakers to identify regional inequalities and evaluate the effectiveness of employment policies. Accessible datasets are essential for fostering accountability and driving evidence-based decisions to address structural labor market imbalances across the Union.
