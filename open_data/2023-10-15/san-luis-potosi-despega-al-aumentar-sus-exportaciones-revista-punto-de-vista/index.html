San Luis Potosí despega al aumentar sus exportaciones | Revista Punto de Vista

San Luis Potosí solidifies its position as a national leader in export growth, significantly outperforming neighboring states during the first half of 2023. This rapid expansion, driven by industrial relocation and improved infrastructure, highlights the region's increasing global competitiveness. The surge is largely attributed to the manufacturing of transport equipment, which dominates the state's export portfolio. This success underscores how strategic public support and competitive advantages can rapidly elevate a local economy within the broader national market. This case is vital for open data because it demonstrates the practical utility of statistical agencies like INEGI. Transparent, accessible economic data allows stakeholders to identify emerging trends, validate policy effectiveness, and make informed decisions regarding investment and development in growing regions.
