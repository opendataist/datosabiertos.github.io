La deuda pública se modera al 103,2% del PIB en septiembre, con 1,7 billones de euros

Public administration debt decreased slightly as a share of GDP, reflecting a modest reduction in the overall fiscal burden despite absolute growth. This trend highlights a complex landscape where central state debt rises while local government liabilities shrink, indicating divergent financial strategies across territorial levels. The significant increase in social security debt raises concerns about long-term sustainability and intergenerational equity. Meanwhile, the rise in consolidated internal debt suggests deeper fiscal integration within the public sector, altering the structural composition of national liabilities and potentially impacting future economic resilience. This data is crucial for open data enthusiasts as it exemplifies the need for transparent, granular public sector financial reporting. By making detailed breakdowns of debt instruments and subsector balances accessible, citizens and researchers can better analyze fiscal health, ensuring accountability and informed public debate regarding government spending and borrowing practices.
