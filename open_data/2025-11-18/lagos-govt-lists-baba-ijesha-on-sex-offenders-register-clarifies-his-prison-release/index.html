Lagos govt lists Baba Ijesha on sex offenders register, clarifies his prison release

The Lagos State government has formally listed actor Olanrewaju James on the sex offenders register, clarifying that his release from prison resulted from completing his five-year sentence rather than any exoneration of the sexual assault charges. The Domestic and Sexual Violence Agency and the Attorney-General strongly condemned false narratives suggesting the actor was cleared of indecent treatment of a child, labeling such claims as misleading and irresponsible. This administrative action ensures that public records accurately reflect his criminal history, preventing the dangerous misconception that he has been legally absolved of the crimes committed against a minor. This case highlights the critical importance of transparent and accurate public records in maintaining community safety and trust. By strictly adhering to legal procedures and maintaining an accessible, correct registry of offenders, authorities aim to protect vulnerable populations and uphold judicial accountability. The emphasis on correcting misinformation demonstrates how reliable data serves as a bulwark against social panic and ensures that citizens can make informed decisions based on factual, verified information rather than viral rumors or selective interpretations of legal outcomes. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for authoritative, unambiguous datasets regarding public safety and legal statuses. When government institutions proactively publish and maintain verified registers, they empower the media, civil society, and the public to access truth, thereby reducing the spread of disinformation. This scenario underscores how open, accurate, and accessible official data is essential for preserving the integrity of public discourse and ensuring that legal justice is transparently understood and respected by all stakeholders.
