Persiste impunidad ante violencia contra mujeres: Abogada Martha Márquez

Persiste impunidad ante violencia contra mujeres: Abogada Martha Márquez

The article highlights the critical intersection of gender-based violence statistics and institutional failure in Mexico, particularly in Veracruz. It reveals that while the vast majority of women experience violence, a mere fraction of legal cases result in favorable outcomes. This stark disparity underscores how systemic resource shortages and a lack of gender perspective within judicial bodies lead to abandoned investigations, leaving victims without justice. This situation is directly relevant to open data because the high rates of unresolved cases and low conviction rates represent significant gaps in public information transparency. The inability to track effective judicial responses suggests that current data systems may not accurately reflect the severity of impunity or the true scope of institutional neglect. Accessible, granular data is essential to expose these systemic flaws and hold authorities accountable for their failure to protect citizens. To address these issues, the article notes the launch of free legal workshops aimed at empowering victims through personal and juridical defense. These initiatives demonstrate a grassroots response to the data void, attempting to bridge the gap between victim support and the unresponsive legal system. Ultimately, improving open data standards on violence against women is crucial for validating such efforts and driving necessary policy reforms to reduce impunity and enhance victim support mechanisms.
