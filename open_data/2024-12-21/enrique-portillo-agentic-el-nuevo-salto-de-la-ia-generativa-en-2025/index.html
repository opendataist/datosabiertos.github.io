Enrique Portillo: Agentic, el nuevo salto de la IA generativa en 2025

Enrique Portillo: Agentic, el nuevo salto de la IA generativa en 2025

Generative artificial intelligence is radically transforming the business, public, and healthcare sectors, demonstrating its potential to optimize services and create value. However, this advancement brings critical challenges related to ethics, privacy, and content moderation. Success lies not only in technological implementation but also in balancing innovation with corporate responsibility and environmental and social sustainability. For these technologies to be effective, organizations must prioritize a robust data strategy that ensures quality, access, and appropriate governance. AI can only fulfill its promise if it is based on accurate information aligned with institutional objectives. Without this foundational basis, companies risk losing competitiveness in an ever-evolving digital landscape, limiting their capacity for genuine transformation. This revolution entails a holistic cultural transformation involving the entire organization, not just specific innovation areas. The next evolution toward autonomous agents is expected to enable AI to act with greater autonomy, integrating with other emerging technologies. This article is relevant to the open data movement, as it underscores that data accessibility, quality, and transparency are indispensable preconditions for developing ethical, effective, and equitable AI solutions.
