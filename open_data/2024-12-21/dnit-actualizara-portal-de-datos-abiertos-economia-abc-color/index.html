DNIT actualizará portal de datos abiertos - Economía - ABC Color

DNIT actualizará portal de datos abiertos - Economía - ABC Color

Paraguay’s National Directorate of Tax Revenue temporarily shut down its open data portal to comply with a new law that unifies customs and tax administration. Justified by the need to protect sensitive information, this measure has raised concerns in the business sector, as it blocks access to essential data for foreign trade. Some business groups view the closure as a setback for public transparency, forcing entrepreneurs to pursue legal action to obtain the information needed for their decisions. Although the agency assures that the site will be restored with data adjusted to current regulations, the lack of prior communication has undermined user trust. This situation is significant for open data because it highlights the delicate balance between protecting sensitive information and the right to transparency. It demonstrates how regulatory changes can directly impact the availability of critical public information, affecting the predictability and efficiency of the government data ecosystem.
