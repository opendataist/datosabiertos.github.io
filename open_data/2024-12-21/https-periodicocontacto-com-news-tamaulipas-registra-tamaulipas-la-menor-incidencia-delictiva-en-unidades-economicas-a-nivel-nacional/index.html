Tamaulipas reports the lowest rate of businesses victimized by insecurity in Mexico, attributing this success to inter-agency coordination and the governor’s peace-building strategy. This significant reduction across commercial, industrial, and service sectors demonstrates how targeted local security interventions can effectively protect economic units and improve overall business stability. The data highlights a clear disparity in national security outcomes, emphasizing that specific governance models can drastically lower crime prevalence compared to the national average. By reducing victimization rates, the state provides a tangible example of how public policy directly impacts the operational safety of enterprises and enhances the investment climate. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates the critical role of standardized, publicly available statistics in evaluating government performance. Reliable datasets like the ENVE enable transparent accountability, allowing citizens and researchers to assess the real-world impact of security policies and drive evidence-based decision-making for public safety improvements.
