Italia multa a Open AI con 15 millones de euros por usar datos de clientes

Italia multa a Open AI con 15 millones de euros por usar datos de clientes

The fine imposed by Italy on OpenAI highlights the urgent need for transparency in the processing of personal data for training AI models. This sanction reinforces the principle that the collection and use of information must be based on clear legal grounds and adequate notifications to users. The case underscores the critical importance of protecting minors and ensuring that platforms implement verified age-check mechanisms. By exacerbating the risks of exposure to inappropriate content, the authority calls for institutional communication that educates the public about their digital rights and the inner workings of these tools. This situation is relevant to the field of open data, as it sets precedents on how large technology corporations handle sensitive information in the face of regulation. It demonstrates that even for innovative purposes such as artificial intelligence training, strict compliance with privacy requirements is indispensable, directly affecting public trust and global data governance standards.
