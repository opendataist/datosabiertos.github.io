El Gobierno nacional aseguró que bajó la pobreza: a qué factores lo atribuye

The Argentine government attributes the significant decline in poverty and extreme poverty to successful policies that reduced inflation, stabilized employment, and increased social assistance. This trend marks a substantial improvement compared to previous years, suggesting effective macroeconomic management and social support mechanisms. These findings are relevant to open data because they highlight the critical role of official statistical institutions, such as INDEC, in tracking socioeconomic indicators. Transparent and accessible data enables accurate analysis of policy impacts, fostering accountability and informed public debate on social welfare and economic stability in Argentina.
