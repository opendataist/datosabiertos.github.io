Atico34 lo confirma: “La inteligencia artificial puede poner en grave riesgo nuestros datos personales”

The article highlights the urgent need to balance the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence with robust data protection standards. It emphasizes that current technological progress outpaces existing legislation, creating significant risks to privacy, security, and data integrity. Without clear regulatory frameworks, the massive processing capabilities of AI can lead to the unauthorized exposure of sensitive personal information, undermining individual rights and trust in digital systems. A critical challenge identified is the prevalence of algorithmic bias, which stems from incomplete or unrepresentative training data. This issue extends beyond privacy to ethical concerns, as biased systems can perpetuate discrimination and unfair treatment of vulnerable groups. The text argues that ensuring data integrity and implementing regular audits are essential to prevent these harmful outcomes, requiring organizations to prioritize ethical data sourcing and transparency in their AI development processes. This content is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the responsibility of data custodians and developers. When open data is used to train AI models, the quality and representativeness of that data directly impact societal fairness. Therefore, promoting open data initiatives must be coupled with strict governance, ethical guidelines, and transparency requirements to ensure that publicly available information does not exacerbate biases or compromise user privacy. The article advocates for education and expert consultation to align data practices with legal and ethical standards, ensuring that open data contributes positively to an inclusive and secure digital ecosystem.
