El hospital San Bernardo amplió el sistema de identificación de pacientes internados

The implementation of personalized identification bracelets for all hospitalized patients at San Bernardo Hospital significantly enhances patient safety. By moving beyond oral identification to a standardized, non-verbal system, the facility minimizes the risk of critical medical errors during medication administration, surgeries, and laboratory tests. This shift represents a proactive measure to ensure accurate patient records and improve overall care quality. This initiative ensures compliance with provincial health regulations that mandate standardized identification procedures. The protocol, integrated with the Salta Salud information system, guarantees that personal data is accurately recorded and consistently accessible. As a result, healthcare staff can rely on a robust framework that supports operational efficiency and reduces the potential for complications arising from misidentification. This case is highly relevant to open data, as it illustrates the importance of reliable, standardized digital data infrastructure in critical sectors. Accurate identification systems depend on clean, interoperable data exchanges between physical records and digital health platforms. Highlighting such implementations underscores the necessity for open, well-governed data standards in healthcare to ensure public safety and administrative integrity.
