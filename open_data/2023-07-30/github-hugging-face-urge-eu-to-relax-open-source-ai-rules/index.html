GitHub, Hugging Face, urge EU to relax open-source AI rules

Major technology companies and open-source advocates urge the European Union to adjust its Artificial Intelligence Act to prevent excessive regulation of upstream open-source projects. They argue that treating these collaborative, non-commercial developments as commercial products stifles innovation and harms individual developers and research organizations. The coalition proposes clear definitions and exemptions for collaborative work, ensuring that open-source contributions do not trigger burdensome compliance requirements. By distinguishing between foundational models and final deployed systems, regulators can mitigate risks without hindering the transparency and shared progress essential to the open-source ecosystem. This issue is critical for open_data because the accessibility and modifiability of open-source AI rely on unrestricted code sharing. Harmonizing regulation with open principles ensures that data and models remain usable for diverse stakeholders, fostering a collaborative environment where innovation thrives without excessive bureaucratic barriers.
