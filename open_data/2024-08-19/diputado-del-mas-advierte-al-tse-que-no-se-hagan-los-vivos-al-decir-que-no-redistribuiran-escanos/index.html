Diputado del MAS advierte al TSE: 'Que no se hagan los vivos' al decir que no redistribuirán escaños

A Bolivian lawmaker is demanding the immediate release of census data by the National Institute of Statistics to enforce legal mandates for redistributing fiscal resources and parliamentary seats. This pressure aims to ensure that regions experiencing significant population growth receive their rightful share of state funds and political representation, thereby upholding constitutional obligations and addressing the needs of incoming families. The debate centers on whether upcoming elections will reflect current demographic realities or rely on outdated distributions. Political leaders argue that without updated population data, the electoral system fails to serve the actual composition of society, potentially undermining democratic legitimacy and equitable resource allocation across departments. This conflict highlights critical open data issues, emphasizing that timely, transparent statistical access is foundational for accountability. Without accessible and accurate public data, citizens cannot verify government compliance with laws, nor can civil society monitor the fairness of electoral and fiscal processes, revealing the necessity of robust open data frameworks in democratic governance.
