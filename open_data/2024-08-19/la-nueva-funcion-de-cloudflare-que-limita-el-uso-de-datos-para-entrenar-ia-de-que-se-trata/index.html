La nueva función de Cloudflare que limita el uso de datos para entrenar IA: ¿De qué se trata?

Cloudflare has launched a new free feature designed to automatically block AI bots. The primary goal is to prevent “web scraping,” a common practice whereby these tools extract content from websites to train models. This measure aims to protect content creators by ensuring their data is not used without permission or transparency by companies developing AI-based tools. Activating this system is straightforward and available to all customers, including those on the free tier, through Cloudflare’s security platform. The company will continuously update the feature by identifying new fingerprinting techniques used by trackers, including those associated with major technology firms. This proactive approach enables users to maintain full control over how their information is accessed, addressing the growing demand for content driven by the advancement of generative AI. The relevance to the open data movement lies in balancing information availability with intellectual property rights. By facilitating website protection, Cloudflare promotes a safer internet that respects content owners’ decisions regarding the use of their material. This highlights the current tension between the need for large volumes of data for technological development and creators’ right to manage their digital resources, setting an important precedent for the ethical governance of online information.
