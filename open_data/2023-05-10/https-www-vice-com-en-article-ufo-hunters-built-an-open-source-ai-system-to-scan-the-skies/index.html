Open Data
ES EN

Driven by public distrust regarding official government handling of Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP), developers have launched Sky360, an open-source citizen science initiative. This project aims to establish a global network of affordable, autonomous monitoring stations to collect real-time data on aerial anomalies. By utilizing widely accessible hardware and software, the initiative empowers individuals to generate large-scale, actionable datasets, bypassing the secrecy that has long characterized military and intelligence-led investigations. The system relies on open-source AI and machine learning tools to detect, track, and classify movement, distinguishing between common objects like birds and planes and potentially anomalous behaviors. By making the underlying code and hardware specifications freely available, Sky360 ensures transparency and allows for global collaboration. This approach leverages the power of open data to aggregate insights from volunteer contributors, creating a comprehensive database that can be independently verified and analyzed by the public rather than being held back by national security classifications. This project highlights the transformative potential of open data in democratizing scientific research and enhancing public trust. It demonstrates how open-source principles can overcome institutional opacity, allowing citizens to participate directly in complex investigations traditionally restricted to elites. By providing free access to the tools necessary for data collection and analysis, Sky360 exemplifies how open technology can foster innovation, ensure accountability, and expand the boundaries of collective discovery beyond restricted governmental channels.
