The Autonomous University of Chihuahua launches the International Open Government Week to bridge the gap between civic administration and the university community. This initiative underscores transparency and public access to information as fundamental human rights and democratic necessities, fostering accountability in municipal governance through dialogue with civil society leaders. Relevant to open data, this event promotes collaborative policy-making by emphasizing transparency and civic participation as tools for improving public sector responsiveness. It highlights how educational institutions can drive methodical societal changes, ensuring that administrative actions are open and understandable. By integrating these principles, the university supports a model where citizens are fully aware of government actions. This approach strengthens democratic engagement and reinforces the importance of accessible, transparent data in building trust and enabling informed decision-making for both officials and the public.
