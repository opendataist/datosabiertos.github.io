Abu Dhabi Fastest-Growing Economy In MENA Region With GDP Growth Rate Of 9.3% In 2022

Abu Dhabi achieved significant economic growth in 2022, distinguishing itself as the fastest-growing economy in the Middle East and North Africa. This success stems from a robust, diversified framework driven by strategic public-private collaboration and proactive regulatory reforms. The emirate’s ability to maintain a stable macroeconomic environment while fostering a business-supportive climate has solidified its competitive edge and attracted substantial foreign and domestic investment. Central to this progress is the Abu Dhabi Industrial Strategy, which aims to transform the region into a premier industrial hub through substantial infrastructure and investment initiatives. By focusing on digital transformation, innovation, and advanced technology, the leadership envisions a sustainable "Falcon economy" that prioritizes social development and resilience. This forward-looking approach addresses evolving geopolitical and technological trends, ensuring the economy remains adaptable and capable of withstanding external shocks. This data is highly relevant to open_data as it exemplifies the critical role of transparent, high-quality statistical reporting in demonstrating economic health and policy effectiveness. By making detailed sectoral growth and non-oil contribution metrics publicly available, the Statistics Centre - Abu Dhabi enables researchers, investors, and policymakers to analyze diversification trends accurately. Such openness fosters trust in institutional governance and provides a reliable foundation for global benchmarking, encouraging further data-driven decisions in regional economic planning.
