INEGI Actualizará Medición de la Inflación en México - Periódico El Orbe.

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography is conducting a public consultation to update Mexico’s National Consumer Price Index, ensuring that the inflation measurement reflects current household consumption patterns. This initiative involves revising the basket of goods and adjusting weighting structures based on recent expenditure data, while also introducing chained indices to enable more frequent biennial updates. The update aligns with international best practices established by major global organizations, enhancing the methodological robustness of the data. By incorporating these changes, the revised index will better capture economic realities, providing a more accurate representation of inflation dynamics for policymakers and the public. This process is vital for open data initiatives, as it promotes transparency and institutional accountability. Inviting citizen participation in defining statistical methodologies fosters trust in official data, ensuring that public resources and economic analyses rely on metrics that are both scientifically rigorous and socially representative.
