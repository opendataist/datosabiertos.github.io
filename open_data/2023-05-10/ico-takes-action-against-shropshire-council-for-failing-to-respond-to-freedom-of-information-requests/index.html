ICO takes action against Shropshire Council for failing to respond to Freedom of Information requests

ICO takes action against Shropshire Council for failing to respond to Freedom of Information requests

The Information Commissioner’s Office has issued an enforcement notice to Shropshire Council for failing to properly handle Freedom of Information requests. This action highlights systemic weaknesses in how public authorities manage transparency obligations, emphasizing that decentralized handling methods can lead to significant backlogs and delayed responses to citizens’ inquiries. The regulator mandates immediate resolution of outstanding requests and the publication of a corrective action plan. This strict requirement underscores a broader shift in regulatory strategy, where persistent non-compliance with transparency laws will trigger formal enforcement measures rather than informal guidance. This case is highly relevant to open data advocates because it demonstrates the tangible consequences of poor information governance. It signals that legal rights to access public information are being actively enforced, encouraging institutions to adopt robust, centralized systems that ensure timely and accountable disclosure of public records.
