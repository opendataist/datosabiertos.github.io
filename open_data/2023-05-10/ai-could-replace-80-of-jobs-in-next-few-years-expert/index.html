AI could replace 80% of jobs in next few years: Expert

Ben Goertzel argues that the imminent rise of Artificial General Intelligence represents a positive transformation rather than a threat, despite predictions that AI could automate up to eighty percent of current jobs. He views this shift as an opportunity for humanity to move beyond mere subsistence labor, allowing individuals to pursue more fulfilling activities. By automating repetitive tasks, particularly those involving paperwork, society can liberate people to focus on meaningful endeavors, provided that social structures adapt effectively during the transition period. Goertzel strongly opposes calls for a pause in AI development, dismissing fears of rogue superintelligent systems as unfounded because current models lack true complex reasoning capabilities. He draws a parallel to the internet, arguing that just as society did not ban the internet due to its spread of misinformation, we should not stifle AI progress. He emphasizes the need for a free society where technology enhances access to information and capabilities, trusting that governance mechanisms and societal adaptation will manage risks more effectively than prohibition. This perspective is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical role of accessible, high-quality information in developing beneficial AI systems. Goertzel’s support for participatory governance implies that open data is essential for ensuring AI serves the public good rather than just maximizing shareholder value. Transparent data practices allow for accountability and enable AI applications in vital sectors like healthcare and education to genuinely improve human well-being, reinforcing the necessity of open data ecosystems for ethical technological advancement.
