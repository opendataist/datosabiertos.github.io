Inflación de México baja al 6,25 % en abril

Mexico’s general inflation rate has fallen to its lowest level since late 2021, marking three consecutive months of decline. Although the annual rate remains above the central bank’s target, the downward trend suggests a potential stabilization in the cost of living after reaching multi-year highs. The data distinguishes between volatile core prices and stable underlying trends, showing that while some essential goods and services continue to rise, energy costs and government-regulated tariffs have helped moderate overall consumer price pressures. This divergence underscores the complex dynamics affecting household budgets and economic stability. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on transparent, regularly updated statistics from official institutions such as INEGI. Such accessible datasets enable researchers and the public to monitor economic health, verify institutional claims, and analyze the impact of policies, thereby fostering accountability and informed public discourse.
