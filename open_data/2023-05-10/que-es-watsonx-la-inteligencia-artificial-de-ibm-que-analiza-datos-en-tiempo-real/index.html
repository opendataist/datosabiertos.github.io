Qué es watsonx, la inteligencia artificial de IBM que analiza datos en tiempo real

IBM’s Watsonx platform aims to facilitate the ethical and secure integration of artificial intelligence into government and private organizations. Its key conclusion is that technology must operate within a framework that ensures transparency and data protection, directly addressing privacy and trust concerns that often hinder business adoption. By providing tools to develop custom models or use pre-trained ones, the platform ensures that organizations can innovate without compromising user security or the integrity of their information. The relevance to open data is evident in the strategic collaboration with Hugging Face, which incorporates thousands of open-source models, datasets, and code libraries. This approach democratizes access to essential resources for AI development, enabling the community to utilize verified and shared architectures. By making its proprietary models accessible through open repositories, IBM fosters a collaborative ecosystem where innovation is built on transparent and standardized foundations, aligning with the principles of interoperability and public availability of information. Furthermore, Watsonx introduces advanced governance and sustainability mechanisms, such as carbon emissions monitoring and efficient management of large volumes of cloud-based data. The platform employs end-to-end encryption and integrated search engines to organize and protect data, reducing operational burden while maintaining compliance with ethical standards. This demonstrates that artificial intelligence can be a powerful tool for automation and decision-making, provided it is integrated with systems that prioritize cybersecurity and environmental responsibility.
