Mar del Plata enfrenta la mayor tasa de desempleo en el país: 8,3 por ciento

Mar del Plata continues to record the highest unemployment rates in the country, despite a slight annual decline. This persistent local crisis stands in stark contrast to a broader national reduction in joblessness, underscoring significant regional economic disparities. These divergent trends highlight the complex nature of Argentina’s labor market. While aggregate national figures show improvement, specific urban centers remain disproportionately affected by structural labor challenges. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it reveals how aggregated national statistics can obscure severe local conditions. Accessible, granular datasets are essential for policymakers to identify and effectively address these uneven regional impacts.
