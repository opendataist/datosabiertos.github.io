Ventas al menudeo desaceleran en julio, revela Inegi

Retail sales in Mexico experienced a modest monthly increase, driven primarily by growth in the food, beverages, and household goods sectors. While overall activity slowed compared to previous months, specific categories such as computers and home decor saw notable gains, whereas online and catalog sales suffered significant declines. This divergence highlights shifting consumer behaviors and the varying resilience of different retail channels. The data reveals distinct regional disparities, with major urban centers like Mexico City and Puebla showing slight improvements, while several other states recorded contractions. These fluctuations underscore the uneven economic recovery across different geographic areas, suggesting that local factors heavily influence retail performance. Understanding these localized trends is crucial for stakeholders analyzing market dynamics and consumer spending power at a granular level. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how standardized government statistics enable the tracking of economic health and consumer trends. By providing transparent, accessible metrics on sales volumes and sector performance, such data supports evidence-based policymaking and allows analysts to identify emerging patterns. Open access to these figures empowers researchers and businesses to make informed decisions, fostering greater transparency and accountability in the national economy.
