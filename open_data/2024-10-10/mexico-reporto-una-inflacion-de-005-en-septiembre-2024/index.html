México reportó una inflación de 0,05% en septiembre 2024

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) released Mexico’s inflation data for September 2024, showing stable annual inflation at 4.58 percent. The report highlights divergent trends between underlying and non-underlying price indices. This release is vital for open data, as it provides essential macroeconomic indicators for public analysis. Open datasets enable independent verification of these economic metrics, fostering transparency. By accessing raw statistical figures, analysts can assess economic health beyond official summaries, ensuring data integrity and supporting informed decision-making across various sectors. Such transparency empowers citizens and researchers to monitor cost-of-living changes accurately. Accessible official statistics reduce information asymmetry, allowing for better economic predictions and policy evaluations, which are crucial for maintaining trust in governmental data releases and financial planning.
