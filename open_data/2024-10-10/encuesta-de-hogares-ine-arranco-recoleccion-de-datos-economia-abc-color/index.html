Encuesta de hogares: INE arrancó recolección de datos - Economía - ABC Color

The National Statistics Institute has launched the final data collection phase of the Permanent Household Survey, continuing its year-round effort to gather comprehensive information on housing, education, health, and employment. This initiative aims to create a detailed statistical portrait of the national reality, ensuring that policymakers and researchers have accurate insights into socioeconomic conditions across the country. Crucially, the survey operates under strict legal frameworks that guarantee the absolute confidentiality of all provided data. This protection encourages citizen participation by assuring respondents that their personal information remains secure, thereby fostering trust in the statistical process and enhancing the reliability of the resulting datasets. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the foundational role of official, confidentially secured statistical collections in generating high-quality public information. By emphasizing transparency in the collection process and legal safeguards, the INE demonstrates how rigorous methodology and privacy protection are essential for producing trustworthy, reusable data that supports informed public discourse and academic research.
