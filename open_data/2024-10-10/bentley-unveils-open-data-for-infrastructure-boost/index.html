Bentley Unveils Open Data for Infrastructure Boost

Bentley Unveils Open Data for Infrastructure Boost

The infrastructure sector must abandon proprietary silos to address complex global challenges, necessitating open data ecosystems that ensure interoperability across diverse tools and stakeholders. This shift is critical because infrastructure projects involve numerous organizations requiring seamless data integration. By adopting open-source schemas, such as Bentley’s Base Infrastructure Schema, professionals can query, analyze, and reuse data effectively, allowing the industry to move forward collectively rather than rebuilding foundational frameworks from scratch. Open data significantly enhances geospatial capabilities and accelerates innovation when combined with advanced technologies like AI. Partnerships integrating comprehensive geospatial data with engineering platforms enable stakeholders to visualize assets in real-world contexts, improving navigation and digital twin functionality. Furthermore, AI acts as a paradigm shift by analyzing vast amounts of infrastructure data to optimize operations, automate routine tasks, and generate sustainable designs. This synergy allows engineers to focus on higher-value activities while leveraging AI agents to predict maintenance needs and improve overall safety and efficiency. The ultimate relevance to open data lies in its ability to unlock the value of existing infrastructure, which constitutes the majority of assets in use today. By combining open data with AI-driven analytics, organizations can monitor and optimize existing systems without costly manual interventions. This approach empowers owner-operators to extend their expertise into asset operations, ensuring resilience and sustainability. Ultimately, maintaining control over open data and fostering collaborative ecosystems are essential for creating a future where infrastructure supports better quality of life through reliable, efficient, and environmentally conscious systems.
