Presume AMLO que inflación desaceleró a 6.85%

Presume AMLO que inflación desaceleró a 6.85%

President López Obrador praised the decline in inflation, highlighting it as a positive economic signal driven by coordinated efforts with producers and retailers to stabilize basic food prices. This trend suggests that current containment strategies are effectively reducing the cost of living for citizens, marking a significant shift from previous high-price periods. The administration reaffirmed its commitment to maintaining anti-inflation packages while preparing for regional diplomacy. By engaging with other Latin American leaders, the goal is to establish collaborative trade and policy agreements that further suppress local price surges through improved cross-border access to essential goods. This update is relevant to open data as it showcases the critical role of official statistical institutions like INEGI in validating government performance. Transparent, accessible data allows citizens and analysts to monitor economic health objectively, ensuring accountability and enabling informed public debate on the effectiveness of fiscal and social policies.
