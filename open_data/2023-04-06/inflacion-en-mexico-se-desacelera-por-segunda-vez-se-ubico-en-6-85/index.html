Inflación en México se desacelera por segunda vez, se ubicó en 6.85%

Mexico’s inflation has decelerated for two consecutive months, reaching its lowest level in over a year. This downward trend, which surpassed market expectations, provides significant relief to monetary authorities by confirming that inflationary pressures are consistently diminishing. Although prices remain above the central bank’s target range, the sustained cooling offers a more stable economic outlook and supports the likelihood of continued easing in the near future. The underlying components of inflation show mixed but generally positive signals, with goods prices slowing while service sector pressures persist. Experts anticipate that the general inflation rate will continue to fall throughout the year, driven largely by base effects, though it may still remain double the official target. The divergence between goods and services highlights structural complexities in the economy, requiring careful monitoring to ensure the downward trajectory is resilient against sector-specific spikes.
