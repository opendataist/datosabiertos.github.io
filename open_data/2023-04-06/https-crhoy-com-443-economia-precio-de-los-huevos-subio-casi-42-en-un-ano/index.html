The sharp rise in egg prices in Costa Rica highlights the vulnerability of food systems to global market fluctuations. Increased costs for feed, transportation, and packaging have significantly affected local producers, driving up consumer prices substantially over a short period. This trend underscores the critical role of data transparency in the agricultural sector. Reliable information on supply chains and cost drivers is essential for stakeholders to understand inflationary pressures and implement effective mitigation strategies. Such insights are vital for open data initiatives aimed at promoting food security. By making pricing and production data accessible, policymakers and the public can better monitor essential goods and ensure equitable access to basic dietary needs.
