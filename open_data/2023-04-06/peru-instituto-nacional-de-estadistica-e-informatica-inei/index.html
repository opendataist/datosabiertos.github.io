PERU Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática INEI

The system empowers local governments and researchers by providing accessible, multi-level statistical data to inform evidence-based policy and strategic decision-making processes. This approach promotes transparency and accountability through the widespread dissemination of socio-economic and environmental indicators at regional and district levels. It directly supports open data principles by ensuring that critical government statistics are publicly available for independent analysis and civic engagement.
