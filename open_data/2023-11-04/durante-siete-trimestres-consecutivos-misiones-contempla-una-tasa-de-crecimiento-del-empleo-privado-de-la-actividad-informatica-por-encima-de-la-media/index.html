Durante siete trimestres consecutivos Misiones contempla una tasa de crecimiento del empleo privado de la actividad informática por encima de la media nacional y del NEA

Misiones demonstrates exceptional growth in its private IT sector, significantly outperforming national and regional averages. This consistent expansion reflects the success of innovative public policies, particularly "Silicon Misiones," which foster a robust ecosystem for the knowledge economy. The province highlights the critical role of targeted legislation in attracting investment and talent. By integrating various sectors, these initiatives create a collaborative environment that elevates professional profiles and drives sustainable economic development through a technological focus. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates how transparent, official statistics can validate the impact of governance decisions. It shows that accessible public information enables stakeholders to analyze sectoral trends, measure policy effectiveness, and understand the tangible benefits of data-driven regional strategies.
