La molienda de soja registró el peor septiembre en lo que va del siglo | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

Severe drought has drastically reduced the availability of soybeans and corn in Argentina, forcing the agro-industrial sector to rely on record imports to maintain production. This local shortage not only constrains domestic activity but has also led to Brazil surpassing Argentina as the world’s leading exporter of soybean meal, reflecting a significant loss of competitiveness for the national economy in the face of competitors achieving record figures. The local market shows delayed commercialization due to climatic uncertainty and a lack of commercial certainty, fostering caution in the new campaign. While global competitors increase their processing and exports, Argentina faces a decline in its external shipments, which has pushed up soybean prices in international markets and weakened the position of its traditional export sector. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparency in the disclosure of agricultural and trade statistics enables the analysis of macroeconomic impacts and global trends. Access to official data on production, milling, and imports is crucial for understanding supply chain vulnerabilities, facilitating informed decision-making in a context of climate change and international competition.
