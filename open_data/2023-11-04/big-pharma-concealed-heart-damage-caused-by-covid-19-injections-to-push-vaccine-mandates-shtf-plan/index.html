Big Pharma CONCEALED Heart Damage Caused by COVID-19 Injections to Push Vaccine Mandates | SHTF Plan

The article asserts that federal agencies and pharmaceutical manufacturers engaged in a coordinated effort to hide evidence linking mRNA vaccines to myocarditis, thereby facilitating the enforcement of vaccine mandates. It claims that top public health officials, including those from the CDC and FDA, were aware of these cardiac risks but deliberately downplayed them in communications with the White House to maintain public compliance and approval for the vaccines. According to the authors, this concealment was not accidental but a strategic move to obscure safety concerns that would have otherwise prevented the vaccines' emergency use authorization. The text alleges that internal documents and pre-clinical studies clearly indicated heart damage, yet these findings were suppressed or omitted from advisory committee presentations to avoid regulatory intervention and continue widespread distribution despite known dangers. This narrative is relevant to open data discussions as it highlights the critical importance of transparency and public access to primary source documents, such as Freedom of Information Act records. By framing the alleged cover-up as a failure of open governance, the article argues that independent analysis of raw data and communications is essential for holding institutions accountable and enabling informed public discourse, rather than relying solely on official narratives.
