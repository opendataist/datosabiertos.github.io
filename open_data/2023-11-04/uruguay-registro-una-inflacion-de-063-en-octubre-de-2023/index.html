Uruguay registró una inflación de 0,63% en octubre de 2023

Uruguay’s October 2023 inflation data highlights how specific sectoral shocks, particularly in transport and housing, drive aggregate price levels. This data reveals the structural components influencing household costs, emphasizing energy and accommodation expenses as key drivers of volatility rather than food prices alone. Understanding these granular variations is crucial for analyzing consumer behavior and the effectiveness of economic policy. By isolating the main contributors to inflation, stakeholders can better assess the real impact on living standards and identify necessary interventions to stabilize volatile sectors such as fuel and rent. This information is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of transparent, detailed statistical releases. Accessible, high-resolution datasets empower researchers and citizens to scrutinize economic trends, fostering greater accountability and enabling more informed public discourse regarding national economic health and policy decisions.
