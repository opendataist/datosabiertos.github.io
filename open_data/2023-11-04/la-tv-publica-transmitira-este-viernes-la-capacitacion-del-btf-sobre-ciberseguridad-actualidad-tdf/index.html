La Tv Pública transmitirá este viernes la capacitación del BTF sobre ciberseguridad - Actualidad TDF

The article highlights a public broadcast of a cybersecurity talk aimed at helping citizens protect themselves against digital fraud. By sharing strategies to prevent online scams, such as ignoring suspicious links, the content promotes digital literacy and security awareness for the general public. Accessibility is emphasized through the inclusion of sign language interpretation, ensuring that critical safety information reaches a wider audience. This approach aligns with open data principles by removing barriers to information access and fostering inclusive public engagement with technological risks. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public institutions can leverage open communication channels to disseminate vital educational resources. By making security knowledge freely available and accessible, the project supports transparency and empowers users to interact more safely with digital services, reinforcing the societal value of open information ecosystems.
