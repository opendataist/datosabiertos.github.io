El Indec informó que el costo de la canasta básica alimentaria subió 7,2% en enero,

The article reports significant increases in the costs of food and the total basic basket, pushing more families toward poverty and extreme poverty. These rises outpaced the general consumer price index, indicating a disproportionate burden on household budgets for essential goods. This data highlights the urgent need for transparent, timely statistics to accurately monitor economic vulnerability. Open data is crucial in this context because it enables citizens and researchers to verify official figures, such as those published by INDEC. Accessible raw data allows for independent analysis of inflation trends and their social impact, fostering accountability and informed public debate. Without such transparency, understanding the true cost of living remains challenging for vulnerable populations. Ultimately, this report underscores how open data bridges the gap between official statistics and public reality. By making economic indicators accessible, society can better address inequality and demand effective policy responses. This reinforces the importance of reliable, open datasets in tracking social welfare and ensuring economic justice through evidence-based decision-making.
