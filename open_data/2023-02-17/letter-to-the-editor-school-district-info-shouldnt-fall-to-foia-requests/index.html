Letter to the editor: School district info shouldn’t fall to FOIA requests

The article highlights how investigative journalism, leveraging public records requests, exposes significant financial mismanagement within Portland Public Schools. It underscores the critical role of open data in uncovering hidden liabilities, such as substantial tax penalties and payroll issues, which officials failed to address proactively. These revelations demonstrate that transparency is essential for maintaining public trust in elected representatives. Without accessible information, taxpayers cannot hold leaders accountable for negligence, reinforcing the need for proactive data disclosure rather than reactive reporting. This case is relevant to open data because it proves that accessible financial records empower citizens to understand government performance. Ensuring data is open helps prevent crises, fosters accountability, and allows the electorate to make informed decisions based on complete, truthful information.
