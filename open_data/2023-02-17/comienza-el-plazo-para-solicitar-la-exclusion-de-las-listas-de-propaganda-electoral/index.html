Comienza el plazo para solicitar la exclusión de las listas de propaganda electoral

Spanish citizens can opt out of receiving political mailings by registering with the electoral census through official electronic channels before the April deadline. This mechanism ensures that voters can control their exposure to electoral propaganda, thereby streamlining digital participation in democratic processes. Such transparency tools are vital for open data initiatives, as they demonstrate how public institutions securely manage personal information. This practice underscores the importance of accessible, digital-first government services that respect user privacy while maintaining electoral integrity.
