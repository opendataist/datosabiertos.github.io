Las Cortes arrancan la legislatura creando un nuevo órgano: el Consejo de Transparencia

The creation of the Regional Council for Transparency and Good Governance represents a significant step toward aligning regional legislation with the national transparency mandates established several years ago. This new independent body is designed to guarantee public access to information and supervise the proactive publication of data, thereby fulfilling legal obligations that had previously gone unimplemented. Its establishment signals a commitment to embedding ethical standards and administrative accountability within the regional government’s institutional framework. The council’s regulatory framework emphasizes both oversight authority and financial sustainability, including provisions that require the president to submit an annual report to parliament. This mandatory accountability mechanism ensures that the council remains answerable to elected representatives, fostering a culture of transparency. The approval process, marked by cross-party consensus among major political groups, highlights a rare instance of unified agreement on strengthening institutional integrity and operational clarity in regional governance. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it institutionalizes the mechanisms necessary for data accessibility and public scrutiny. By creating a dedicated body with the authority to enforce transparency laws and manage public records, the region establishes a structured approach to open government. This ensures that data is not merely made available but is actively managed and protected, empowering citizens to hold officials accountable through verified information and robust procedural standards.
