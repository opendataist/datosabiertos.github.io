Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The bill promoted by the Civic Coalition aims to establish an Open Import Data Portal to ensure transparency in Argentina’s licensing system. The initiative responds to concerns about administrative discretion and the risk of corruption associated with current procedures, such as the SIRA system. By requiring that all information—including status, amounts, and product types—be published in open and accessible formats, the proposal seeks to eliminate the opacity that has historically characterized these administrative processes. This approach promotes procedures that are simple, efficient, and subject to strict deadlines, with an implicit positive resolution in cases of government inaction. The proposal not only aims to simplify bureaucracy for importers but also to establish clear and predictable criteria for economic evaluation. By democratizing access to this data, it empowers citizens and civil society to oversee government decisions, aligning with international recommendations on regulatory governance. The relevance of this article to open data lies in its concrete application to combat corruption through accountability. It demonstrates how the proactive publication of structured public information in open formats is an essential tool for institutional integrity. By transforming sensitive administrative data into accessible public information, it reduces informational asymmetry and fosters a fairer economic environment, validating the principle that digital transparency is a fundamental pillar of ethical public management.
