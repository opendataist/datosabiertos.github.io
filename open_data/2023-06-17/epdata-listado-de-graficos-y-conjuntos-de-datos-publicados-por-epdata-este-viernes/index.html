EpData.- Listado de gráficos y conjuntos de datos publicados por EpData este viernes

EpData publishes recent economic and political data, highlighting rising labor costs, moderate inflation, and shifting political power in Spain. These updates provide essential metrics for analyzing current socio-economic trends and governance structures. Access to such granular datasets is vital for open data advocates, enabling transparent analysis of public sector performance and market dynamics. By aggregating these statistics, the resource empowers researchers and citizens to scrutinize policy impacts, fostering accountability and informed decision-making through accessible, raw economic indicators.
