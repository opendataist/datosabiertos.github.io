Consultan a sectores sociales por plan de Gobierno Abierto

Colombia is finalizing its Sixth National Action Plan on Open Government, validating the consensus reached through multisectoral working groups. This collaborative effort, led by the Presidential Commission for Open and Electronic Government with support from USAID, integrates civil society into the decision-making process. Key focus areas include open data and transparency, health and education infrastructure, food security, migration, poverty reduction, and violence prevention. By engaging diverse stakeholders, the initiative aims to strengthen collaboration between the executive branch and civil society, ensuring that public management remains transparent and open to scrutiny. This development is crucial for open data because it institutionalizes citizen participation in policy design. The resulting commitments will directly impact how government information is shared, audited, and used, thereby enhancing accountability and fostering a more participatory democracy through improved data governance mechanisms.
