TEPJF presenta plataforma de justicia electoral | El Universal

The launch of Digital Electoral Justice aims to enhance accountability and access to electoral information. It serves as a tool for digital literacy and public verification, promoting pluralistic debate and civic participation through technology. The platform provides key services, including virtual hearings and real-time case tracking. By offering statistics and open data, it enables citizens to monitor judicial activities and stay informed about crucial electoral dates and legal precedents. This initiative is vital for open data, as it transforms legal processes into transparent, accessible digital resources. It demonstrates how government bodies can leverage open information to foster trust and inclusivity, ensuring that electoral justice is not only efficient but also understandable and available to the general public.
