Baja el número de defunciones en México

Mexico’s preliminary mortality data for early 2025 show a decline compared to the previous year, with cardiovascular diseases and diabetes remaining the leading causes of death. This update highlights how civil registry data are incorporated into national statistical databases. The relevance to open data lies in the transparent publication of these vital statistics by INEGI. It underscores the importance of accessible, high-quality government datasets for public health analysis. Such openness enables researchers and citizens to track demographic trends and evaluate healthcare policies. It highlights the critical role of reliable, publicly available administrative records in understanding societal well-being.
