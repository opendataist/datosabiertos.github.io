Franja de Gaza: Al menos 20.000 niños han sido asesinados por Israel desde octubre de 2023 - Barrigaverde.net

Franja de Gaza: Al menos 20.000 niños han sido asesinados por Israel desde octubre de 2023 - Barrigaverde.net

The article highlights the catastrophic humanitarian crisis in Gaza, emphasizing that thousands of children have been killed or injured since October 2023. It underscores the severe loss of life and the resulting permanent disabilities among survivors, alongside the imminent threat of death by starvation due to declared famine and restricted aid. This situation reflects a deliberate destruction of the next generation, with schools and healthcare facilities largely decimated. This report is critically relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the vital role of transparent, accessible, and verified statistics in exposing human rights violations. By relying on official government offices and reputable humanitarian organizations, the data provides undeniable evidence of the scale of suffering. Open access to such granular information allows researchers, journalists, and advocacy groups to hold perpetrators accountable and mobilize international intervention. Ultimately, the transparency of these figures challenges obfuscation and forces the global community to confront the reality of the conflict. The narrative shifts from abstract geopolitical debates to tangible, measurable human loss. Therefore, ensuring data openness is not merely a technical requirement but a moral imperative for documenting atrocities and advocating for the protection of vulnerable populations in war zones.
