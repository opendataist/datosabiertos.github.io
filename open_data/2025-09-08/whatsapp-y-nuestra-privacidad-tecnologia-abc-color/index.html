WhatsApp y nuestra privacidad - Tecnología - ABC Color

WhatsApp y nuestra privacidad - Tecnología - ABC Color

The viral spread of a false message claiming that Meta’s artificial intelligence was accessing private WhatsApp messages generated unnecessary alarm, revealing instead an opportunity to clarify misunderstandings. End-to-end encryption ensures that the platform’s AI cannot read the contents of personal conversations. The false news omitted that the “solution” promised was, in fact, a preexisting and optional privacy feature, demonstrating how misinformation can obscure genuine security enhancements and user control. The advanced chat privacy feature represents a significant shift in the control users have over their data and conversations. When activated, it restricts the ability to export chats, automatically download content, or allow information to be used for training or powering artificial intelligence functions within the app. This enables users, especially in large groups or when interacting with untrusted contacts, to maintain a safer environment where sensitive content remains strictly within the context of the conversation, without the risk of involuntary external leakage. This development is relevant to open data because it establishes a critical precedent in the ethics of personal data in the face of the widespread integration of artificial intelligence. It illustrates the growing tension between data extraction to improve AI models and the individual right to privacy, highlighting the importance of robust technical mechanisms that allow users to explicitly decide whether their interactions can be used for machine learning purposes. Furthermore, it underscores the need for transparency in how platforms handle sensitive data, even when such data is protected by encryption, promoting a model where privacy is not a technological barrier but a clear and accessible configuration option.
