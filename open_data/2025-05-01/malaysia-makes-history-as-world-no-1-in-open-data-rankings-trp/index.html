Malaysia Makes History As World No. 1 In Open Data Rankings | TRP

Malaysia Makes History As World No. 1 In Open Data Rankings | TRP

Malaysia has achieved the top global ranking in the Open Data Inventory 2024/25, marking a historic leap from its previous mid-tier positions. This remarkable ascent highlights the nation’s unwavering commitment to transforming its statistical landscape into a model of transparency and accessibility, surpassing established leaders in data governance. The success stems from robust collaboration between the Department of Statistics Malaysia and other government agencies, prioritizing high-quality, comprehensive, and easily accessible official data. By focusing on both openness and coverage, the country has significantly strengthened its statistical system, ensuring that information meets modern analytical needs while fostering evidence-based governance and civic engagement. This milestone is crucial for open data advocates as it demonstrates how strategic policy improvements can dramatically enhance public trust and utility. By removing barriers to data access, Malaysia not only supports innovation and academic research but also sets a global benchmark for how nations can leverage open statistics to drive informed decision-making and sustainable development.
