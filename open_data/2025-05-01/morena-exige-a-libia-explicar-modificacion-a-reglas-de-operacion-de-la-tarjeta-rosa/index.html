Morena exige a Libia explicar modificación a reglas de operación de la Tarjeta Rosa

Morena exige a Libia explicar modificación a reglas de operación de la Tarjeta Rosa

Members of the Morena party in Guanajuato allege that the executive branch illegally modified the operating rules of the "Tarjeta Rosa" social program, significantly increasing its budget without the mandatory legislative approval required by state law. They argue that this unilateral expansion of funds, amounting to millions of pesos, violates public resource control regulations, as it bypassed the Congress and potentially exceeds legal spending limits, thereby undermining the transparency and legal framework governing public administration. The opposition demands an investigation by the State’s Supreme Audit into the origin and allocation of these additional resources, fearing that the program is being instrumentalized for electoral advantages ahead of the 2027 elections. Morena insists that the benefits are not universal as claimed and seeks to verify the data of all beneficiaries through transparency requests. This move aims to prevent public resources from being used as political campaign tools, asserting that such actions erode democratic integrity and expose citizens to potential misuse of state funds. This conflict is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for accessible, verifiable public information regarding social spending. If citizens cannot access detailed data on program beneficiaries and budget adjustments, they cannot effectively monitor government accountability or detect potential irregularities. The dispute underscores how transparency mechanisms and the availability of granular administrative data are essential for detecting corruption, ensuring legal compliance, and maintaining public trust in the equitable distribution of social resources.
