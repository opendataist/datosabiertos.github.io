¿Libró México la recesión? Calcula Inegi leve alza de la economía

Mexico narrowly avoided a technical recession in early 2025, posting slight growth despite expert predictions of contraction. Primary sectors drove this resilience, offsetting declines in industry, while services remained stagnant. This outcome highlights how real-time economic data can quickly correct market expectations and reveal economic fragility. For open data advocates, this case underscores the critical value of timely, accessible statistical releases. Immediate availability allows stakeholders to accurately assess economic health, fostering transparency and enabling informed decision-making based on verified facts rather than mere speculation. Ultimately, the episode illustrates how public data serves as a vital tool for accountability. By providing clear metrics on growth and sectoral performance, institutions empower citizens and analysts to understand complex economic narratives, reinforcing the necessity of robust, open information systems in democratic societies.
