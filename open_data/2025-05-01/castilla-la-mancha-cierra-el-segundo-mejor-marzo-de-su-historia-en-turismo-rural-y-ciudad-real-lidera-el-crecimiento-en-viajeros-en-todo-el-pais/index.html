Castilla-La Mancha cierra el segundo mejor marzo de su historia en turismo rural y Ciudad Real lidera el crecimiento en viajeros en todo el país

Castilla-La Mancha cierra el segundo mejor marzo de su historia en turismo rural y Ciudad Real lidera el crecimiento en viajeros en todo el país

Castilla-La Mancha recorded its second-best March in the history of rural tourism, with nearly 32,000 visitors and more than 62,000 overnight stays. Notably, this achievement was attained without the usual boost from Easter, underscoring the sector’s strong resilience and robust recovery trends across the region, even in the absence of that specific holiday driver. The province of Ciudad Real showed exceptional growth, leading the entire country in annual increases in arriving visitors. This performance highlights a significant competitive advantage and points to specific local strengths within the broader regional economic landscape, marking a historic high for visitor attraction in this particular geographic area. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical reports from the National Institute of Statistics (INE). Such transparency enables policymakers and researchers to validate economic claims, analyze regional disparities, and understand tourism dynamics through verified, publicly accessible metrics, thereby fostering accountability and evidence-based decision-making.
