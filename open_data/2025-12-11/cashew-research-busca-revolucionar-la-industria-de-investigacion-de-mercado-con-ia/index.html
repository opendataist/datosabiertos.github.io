Cashew Research busca revolucionar la industria de investigación de mercado con IA

Cashew Research busca revolucionar la industria de investigación de mercado con IA

Cashew Research represents a paradigm shift in market research by combining the precision of human-collected data with the efficiency of artificial intelligence. By automating analysis without relying solely on language models, the company delivers fresh, customized results at a significantly lower cost. This democratizes access to strategic information, enabling small and medium-sized enterprises to conduct market studies that were previously economically unviable. The relevance of this approach for open data lies in its management and anonymization model. Cashew stores the real data collected in an anonymous database, creating a valuable asset that can enrich future projects. This practice highlights the importance of generating structured and clean datasets, demonstrating how ethical automation can scale the production of useful information while protecting subject privacy. Finally, the case illustrates the evolution toward new categories of accessible services. By reducing time and cost barriers, Cashew not only optimizes business processes but also fosters a more inclusive culture of evidence-based decision-making. This approach underscores the critical need for high-quality, up-to-date, and readily available data, which serves as the fundamental fuel for transparency and innovation in modern open data ecosystems.
