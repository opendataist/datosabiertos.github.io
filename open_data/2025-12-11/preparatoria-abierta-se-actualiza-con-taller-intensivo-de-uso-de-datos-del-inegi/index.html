Preparatoria Abierta se actualiza con taller intensivo de uso de datos del INEGI

Preparatoria Abierta se actualiza con taller intensivo de uso de datos del INEGI

Specialists from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) provided training to educators in Tamaulipas on the use of official statistical and geographic tools. This initiative empowers teachers to access, interpret, and effectively apply public data in their educational planning and decision-making processes. By mastering interactive tools such as digital maps and census visualizers, instructors can significantly enhance the quality of their teaching and research. This practical skill development supports a broader policy aimed at modernizing educational methods through digital technologies, ensuring that educators remain proficient in handling contemporary informational resources. This event underscores the vital role of open data in improving educational outcomes. It demonstrates how making complex public statistics accessible to frontline workers fosters transparency, improves administrative efficiency, and ultimately strengthens the societal value of government information through better, evidence-based practices.
