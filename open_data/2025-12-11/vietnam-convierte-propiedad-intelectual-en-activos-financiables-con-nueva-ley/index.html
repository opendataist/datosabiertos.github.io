Vietnam convierte propiedad intelectual en activos financiables con nueva ley

Vietnam convierte propiedad intelectual en activos financiables con nueva ley

Vietnam’s recent legislative amendments aim to reclassify intellectual property as genuine corporate assets, enabling companies to value, register, and leverage these rights for financing, particularly in the AI and digital sectors. By mandating digital registration and drastically reducing examination timelines, the reforms foster a more agile business environment while encouraging proactive management of intangible assets through internal valuation registers. Regarding emerging technologies, the law clarifies that purely autonomous AI creations lack copyright protection, recognizing human contributors only when substantial creative input exists. However, public legal data may be used to train models, provided existing rights are not infringed. This balance supports innovation while protecting creators, aligning national policy with global digital trends. This development is crucial for open data as it legitimizes the commercial use of publicly available information for AI training, establishing a legal framework that encourages data reuse. By harmonizing with international treaties and strengthening enforcement, Vietnam creates a predictable landscape where open data initiatives can thrive alongside robust intellectual property protections, facilitating sustainable technological advancement.
