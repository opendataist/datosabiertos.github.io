The article highlights a sharp increase in the cost of the basic food basket in the province of Misiones, driven by inflationary spikes in essential food items such as cooking oils and meats. This surge in prices, which has reached historic record levels, has severely eroded families’ purchasing power, forcing them to allocate disproportionate resources to meet basic needs while the provincial government implements spending cuts to balance its budget. In response to the crisis, trade unions and pensioners have mobilized, demanding the resumption of wage and collective bargaining negotiations that the government has delayed. The suspension of new hires in the public sector, except in critical areas, has intensified social tensions, with constant protests reflecting dissatisfaction over the real decline in incomes and the lack of effective mechanisms to restore the economic dignity of state workers. This situation is relevant to the analysis of open data, as transparency in inflation indicators and public spending is crucial for monitoring the impact of economic policies. The availability of accurate and up-to-date data enables citizens and oversight bodies to verify the extent of social deterioration, facilitating accountability and the design of evidence-based solutions to address growing inequality and fiscal pressure.
