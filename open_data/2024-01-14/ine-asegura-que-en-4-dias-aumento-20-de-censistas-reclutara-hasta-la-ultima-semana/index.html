INE asegura que en 4 días aumentó 20% de censistas; reclutará hasta la última semana

The article highlights the successful recruitment of the majority of census takers for Bolivia’s upcoming population and housing count. By leveraging local volunteers, particularly students and teachers, the National Institute of Statistics ensures efficient coverage while minimizing logistical costs, as workers operate within their own communities. This approach underscores the critical role of local human resources in generating accurate, granular demographic data. The reliance on thousands of volunteers demonstrates how community engagement can effectively support large-scale national data collection efforts, ensuring broad geographical representation without excessive logistical burdens. For the open data community, this case illustrates the importance of understanding data provenance and collection methodologies. It emphasizes that high-quality public datasets depend not just on technical infrastructure, but also on robust, transparent recruitment strategies and clear protocols for citizen participation, which are essential for maintaining data integrity and public trust in statistical outputs.
