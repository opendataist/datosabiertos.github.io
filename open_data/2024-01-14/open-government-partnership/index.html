Open Government Partnership

Seychelles’ independent oversight bodies have united under a new National Integrity Coalition. This collaborative initiative directly implements the nation’s Open Government Partnership commitments. The group prioritizes transparency and anti-corruption strategies. By aligning these key institutions, the government aims to strengthen governance frameworks and promote ethical conduct across all state agencies. This development is vital for open data advocates. It signals a systemic push for institutional transparency, ensuring that public accountability mechanisms are coordinated and that citizens have greater access to reliable, truthful information regarding government operations.
