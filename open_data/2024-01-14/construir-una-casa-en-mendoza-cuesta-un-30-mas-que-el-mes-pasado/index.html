Construir una casa en Mendoza cuesta un 30% más que el mes pasado

The article highlights that construction costs in Mendoza have surged dramatically due to spikes in material prices and currency devaluation, despite stable labor costs. This trend severely impacts housing affordability, illustrating how macroeconomic instability directly inflates essential infrastructure expenses. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible, and timely statistical information from institutions such as INDEC. Reliable public data empowers citizens and professionals to understand economic pressures, make informed decisions, and hold authorities accountable for inflation metrics. Ultimately, the report demonstrates that without clear, open datasets on material and labor trends, stakeholders cannot effectively track or mitigate the rising cost of living and building. Open data serves as a vital tool for navigating and understanding these complex economic shifts in real time.
