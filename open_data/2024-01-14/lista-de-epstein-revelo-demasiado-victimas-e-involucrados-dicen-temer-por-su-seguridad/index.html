Lista de Epstein reveló demasiado: Víctimas e involucrados dicen ‘temer por su seguridad’

The mass release of documents linked to Jeffrey Epstein revealed critical flaws in the handling of sensitive data, inadvertently exposing identifying information about victims and witnesses. This legal negligence sparked fears for the safety of those affected, damaging reputations and private lives, and underscores the extreme vulnerability of personal data when adequate security protocols are not applied. This incident highlights the urgent need for rigorous standards in managing sensitive information within judicial processes. Transparency must not compromise the protection of vulnerable identities, especially in cases with high media profile. For the open data community, this serves as a warning about the ethical risks of disclosure without proper anonymization. It demonstrates that public accessibility must be balanced with strict safeguards to prevent real harm to individuals, reinforcing that openness must be accompanied by responsibility and data protection.
