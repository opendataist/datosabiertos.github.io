INFLACIÓN MANTIENE TENDENCIA A LA BAJA: Llega a 4.67% en la primera quincena de agosto - Noticaribe

Inflation decelerates to 4.67% due to monetary tightening and improved global conditions, yet persistent price increases continue to impact consumers. This dynamic highlights the critical role of transparent economic data in understanding policy effectiveness. Open data facilitates rigorous analysis of inflationary trends, enabling better public scrutiny and informed decision-making. Accessible statistics empower citizens and experts to evaluate real-world economic impacts beyond aggregate figures. Ultimately, the reliability and openness of official statistics are essential for fostering accountability. By providing clear, detailed metrics, institutions like INEGI support democratic oversight and enhance trust in public economic management.
