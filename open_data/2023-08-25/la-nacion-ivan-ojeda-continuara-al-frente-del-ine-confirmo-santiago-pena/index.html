La Nación / Iván Ojeda continuará al frente del INE, confirmó Santiago Peña

La Nación / Iván Ojeda continuará al frente del INE, confirmó Santiago Peña

Iván Ojeda has been reappointed as director of Paraguay’s National Statistics Institute, ensuring continuity in national data governance. This decision reinforces institutional stability, allowing for the sustained modernization of the country’s statistical systems and enhancing the reliability of public information. The confirmation supports efforts to position official data as a driver for positive social change. By maintaining experienced leadership, the government aims to improve the quality and accessibility of statistical outputs, which are crucial for evidence-based policymaking and transparent public administration. This is relevant to open data, as a robust, continuous statistical office is essential for generating high-quality, open datasets. Reliable data infrastructure enables researchers, citizens, and developers to analyze trends effectively, fostering accountability and informed decision-making across society.
