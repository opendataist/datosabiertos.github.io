Modernización y gobierno abierto: actualización de “Reco Digital” | RegiónNet

Reco Digital consolidates municipal services onto a single platform, allowing residents to manage procedures remotely and receive real-time updates on their status. This centralization significantly enhances the efficiency of public service delivery by streamlining processes such as licensing and emergency response, thereby fostering more agile and effective interactions between citizens and local government. The application embodies the principles of open government by ensuring transparency and promoting active civic participation. By giving users immediate control over their administrative requests and incorporating modules for sustainability, urban mobility, and community complaints, it strengthens dialogue between the municipality and its neighborhoods, making governance more accessible and responsive to everyday needs. This initiative is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how centralized, accessible digital tools can enhance transparency and accountability in public administration. By providing real-time information and facilitating direct citizen engagement, such platforms put open data concepts into practice, proving that accessible technology is essential for modernizing state institutions and improving the overall quality of public services.
