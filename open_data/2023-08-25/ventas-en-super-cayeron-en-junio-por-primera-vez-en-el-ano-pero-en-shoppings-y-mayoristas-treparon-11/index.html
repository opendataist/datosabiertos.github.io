Ventas en súper cayeron en junio por primera vez en el año, pero en shoppings y mayoristas treparon 11%

The report reveals a divergent retail landscape, with supermarket sales contracting while major shopping centers and wholesale warehouses experienced significant growth, signaling a shift in consumer behavior and purchasing power. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the value of granular, disaggregated economic indicators in understanding complex market dynamics beyond aggregate inflation figures. By making such detailed sectoral information publicly accessible, analysts can better assess economic resilience and consumption patterns. Ultimately, these findings highlight that nominal price surges mask real changes in volume, emphasizing the need for transparent, high-quality statistical data to accurately inform policy and business strategies.
