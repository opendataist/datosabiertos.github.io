La ciudad impulsa el primer registro poblacional para refrendar los datos del INE

Cochabamba is conducting a local census to verify official population figures, arguing that inaccuracies in national data directly affect the allocation of public resources. This initiative aims to establish a reliable dataset for equitable governance and improved service planning. The exercise underscores the critical need for accurate, localized open data to ensure transparent and effective distribution of public funds.
