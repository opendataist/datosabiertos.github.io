Abinader reafirma compromiso con un gobierno abierto, transparente y

The Dominican Republic is reinforcing its commitment to democratic governance by launching its fifth Open Government Action Plan. President Luis Abinader stresses that transparency is not merely an administrative preference but a fundamental citizen right essential for building public trust. By ensuring that information on the management of public resources is accessible to all, the administration aims to foster active civic participation and strengthen the dialogue between the state and its citizens, thereby reinforcing the pillars of a just and equitable society. To operationalize these goals, the government has introduced concrete digital measures, including the creation of the Multi-Actor Forum for Open Government and a centralized public procurement portal. A pivotal development is the establishment of the first National Open Data Policy and the National Data Commission, which have already presented data-opening plans for 2023 and 2024. These initiatives are designed to make public information reusable and to support the formulation of transparent public policies, marking a structural shift toward efficiency and accountability in the use of public funds. This article is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the institutionalization of data governance at the highest political level. The recognition of open data as a key driver for transparent policy-making demonstrates a strategic move beyond simple information publication toward structured data management. This approach ensures that citizens and organizations can effectively use public data to monitor government actions, thereby enhancing civic oversight and promoting a culture of integrity that benefits the entire democratic ecosystem.
