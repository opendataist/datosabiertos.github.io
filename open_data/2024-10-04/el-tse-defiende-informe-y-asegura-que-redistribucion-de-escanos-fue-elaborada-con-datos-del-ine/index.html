El TSE defiende informe y asegura que redistribución de escaños fue elaborada con datos del INE

El TSE defiende informe y asegura que redistribución de escaños fue elaborada con datos del INE

The Supreme Electoral Tribunal based its plan for redistributing parliamentary seats on official census data, rejecting accusations of bias despite protests from various regions. This case highlights the critical role of authoritative public datasets in governance, demonstrating how the integrity of open data underpins transparent and legally sound policy decisions. Critics argue that the figures misrepresent reality, yet the tribunal maintains its technical objectivity by strictly adhering to the provided statistics. The dispute underscores the necessity for robust, accessible data infrastructure to validate electoral adjustments and ensure public trust in institutional processes. Ultimately, this scenario illustrates why open data matters: it provides the transparent, verifiable foundation required for democratic accountability. When government agencies rely on and disclose official statistics, it enables citizens to scrutinize decisions, fostering a more accountable and informed political environment.
