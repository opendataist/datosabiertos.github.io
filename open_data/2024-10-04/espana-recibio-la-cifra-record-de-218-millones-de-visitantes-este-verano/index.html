España recibió la cifra récord de 21,8 millones de visitantes este verano

Spain set a historic tourism record, significantly boosting national revenue and demonstrating the sector’s crucial economic role. This surge underscores how large-scale visitor data drives policy and investment decisions across the region. The shift toward higher spending per visitor indicates a qualitative improvement in tourism quality. Such patterns provide valuable insights into consumer behavior, which are essential for targeted marketing and sustainable resource management. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the power of transparent statistics in understanding complex economic trends. Publicly available, high-quality datasets enable researchers and policymakers to effectively analyze the health of the industry and promote informed decision-making for future growth.
