Judicial Watch Joins Forces With Mike Pence Group, Sues FDA And HHS Over Abortion Pill Docs

Judicial Watch and Advancing American Freedom have initiated legal action against the Department of Health and Human Services and the FDA to obtain records regarding the approval and communication surrounding the abortion pill, Mifepristone. This lawsuit addresses the agencies' failure to respond to prior Freedom of Information Act requests, highlighting a demand for transparency in regulatory decisions that significantly impact public health. The core argument asserts that the FDA neglected established protocols and safety concerns when relaxing restrictions on the drug, including lifting in-person dispensing requirements. Proponents of the suit claim that this relaxation ignored evidence of significant morbidity and mortality, suggesting a systemic cover-up by the agency that prioritized expanded access over the physical well-being of patients, despite medical warnings about severe complications. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of FOIA in ensuring government accountability and public access to scientific and regulatory information. By challenging agency opacity, the lawsuit advocates for the principle that data concerning public health policy, drug approvals, and internal communications should be freely available to scrutinize the integrity of governmental processes and protect citizen safety.
