Mahama Ayariga files RTI to Communications Minister; demands certain information on Next-Gen InfraCo deal - MyJoyOnline

Mahama Ayariga, a Ghanaian parliamentary official, has formally invoked the Right to Information Act to demand full disclosure regarding the Next-Gen Infrastructure Company deal. This move responds to rising public scrutiny over government contracts and their impact on telecommunications competition. By requesting the complete contractual agreement, terms, and financial implications, Ayariga seeks to ensure that such agreements are not based solely on verbal assurances but are subject to rigorous official review and transparency. The inquiry highlights the critical intersection of national security, economic accountability, and public trust. The request underscores the necessity of open access to state-level decisions to prevent allegations that private interests are disproportionately benefiting at the expense of the public. It emphasizes that true accountability requires accessible documentation, allowing stakeholders to verify that national infrastructure initiatives align with broader public interests rather than hidden commercial agendas. This situation is highly relevant to open data principles as it demonstrates the practical application of transparency laws in regulating emerging technologies like 5G. It illustrates how legislative bodies use information access rights to enforce government accountability, ensuring that digital infrastructure developments promote financial inclusion and bridge the digital divide without compromising public oversight. Such precedents are essential for maintaining confidence in how public resources and technological advancements are managed.
