La inflación en la OCDE baja siete décimas en agosto, hasta el 4,7%, sobre todo por la energía

The article highlights a significant slowdown in inflation across OECD countries, driven primarily by a sharp decline in energy prices and a modest drop in food costs. This trend indicates a general easing of price pressures globally, suggesting that earlier spikes may be stabilizing as volatile components recede, allowing underlying inflation to remain relatively steady. Spain reflects this positive momentum, with its inflation rate falling significantly to 2.3%, closely aligning with Eurozone averages. The reduction is largely attributed to deflation in the energy sector, although underlying inflation remains unchanged. This data underscores how specific sectoral improvements can effectively lower overall consumer price indices, providing a clearer picture of economic stability for residents.
