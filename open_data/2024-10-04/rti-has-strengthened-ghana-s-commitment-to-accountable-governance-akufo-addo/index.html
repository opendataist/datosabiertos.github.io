RTI has strengthened Ghana's commitment to accountable governance- Akufo-Addo

Ghana’s Right to Information Act demonstrates a strong governmental commitment to open and accountable governance. By empowering citizens to scrutinize public institutions, the legislation fosters deeper civic engagement. This legal framework ensures that individuals can effectively monitor leadership, holding officeholders responsible for their actions and decisions within the state apparatus. The government is actively investing in digital infrastructure to expand access to information. This strategic move aims to ensure that all citizens remain well-informed, enabling them to oversee development projects and the utilization of state resources. Such efforts align with international praise, particularly from UNESCO, which recognizes the necessity of robust legal frameworks for freedom of expression and sustainable futures. This article is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates the critical link between legal rights and technological implementation. It highlights how open data initiatives, supported by institutional units and digital access, transform abstract transparency concepts into tangible public benefits. Understanding this dynamic is essential for advancing global open data standards that prioritize inclusive participation and democratic accountability.
