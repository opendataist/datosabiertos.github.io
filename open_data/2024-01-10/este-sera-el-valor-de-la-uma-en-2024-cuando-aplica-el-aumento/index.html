Este será el valor de la UMA en 2024: cuándo aplica el aumento

The INEGI announced the 2024 update of the UMA (Unit of Measure and Reference), a crucial index used across federal and state laws to standardize financial obligations. This annual adjustment ensures that legal and fiscal values remain aligned with economic reality, preventing distortion from inflation over time. The new UMA value increases by approximately 4.66 percent, reflecting the growth in the national consumer price index. This mechanism guarantees that penalties, fines, and salary-related calculations maintain their real purchasing power, protecting both citizens and the state against monetary devaluation. This data is vital for open-data enthusiasts, as it highlights how public statistics directly influence legal compliance and economic transparency. Accurate, publicly accessible UMA figures enable citizens and organizations to verify liabilities and understand the tangible impact of government economic policies on daily life and business operations.
