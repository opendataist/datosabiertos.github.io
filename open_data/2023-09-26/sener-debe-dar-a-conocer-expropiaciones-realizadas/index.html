Sener debe dar a conocer expropiaciones realizadas

Sener debe dar a conocer expropiaciones realizadas

The INAI ordered the Ministry of Energy to publicly disclose detailed records of expropriations carried out during the last two administrations. This mandate requires revealing specific data such as dates, locations, project purposes, and financial compensation to ensure legal compliance and fairness. This decision highlights the critical role of open data in governance, as public access to administrative records allows citizens to verify that expropriations serve genuine public utility and provide just indemnification. Transparency transforms state actions from opaque decrees into auditable processes, fostering accountability and trust in government operations. The ruling underscores that open data is not merely about availability but about enabling civic oversight. By making these details accessible, society can effectively monitor state power, ensuring a balance between public interest and private rights through informed scrutiny rather than relying solely on official declarations.
