La Inteligencia Artificial en Argentina: ¿suficiente con buenas intenciones?

Argentina has established a program to ensure transparency and data protection in artificial intelligence, aligning with international standards. However, the main conclusion is that formal resolutions and "good intentions" are insufficient without robust management and concrete investment. The article argues that ethical frameworks must be coupled with active governance to address emerging technological and economic needs effectively. The rapid evolution of AI poses significant risks to privacy and human life, yet the public sector currently lacks preventive data protection actions. Meanwhile, the private sector will not voluntarily abandon AI-driven growth without clear regulatory incentives. Consequently, relying solely on normative declarations creates a void, as actual implementation requires sustained effort and resources to mitigate risks before they materialize. This topic is crucial for open data because transparency in AI systems is a prerequisite for public trust and accountability. If data handling in algorithms remains opaque or mismanaged, the integrity of public information suffers. Ensuring that AI tools respect data privacy and explainability safeguards the foundational principles of open government, allowing society to benefit from technological innovation without compromising individual rights or institutional credibility.
