El Gobierno de Cantabria alerta de un incidente de seguridad informática que ha afectado a datos personales del Registro de animales de compañía - El Faradio | Periodismo que cuenta

El Gobierno de Cantabria alerta de un incidente de seguridad informática que ha afectado a datos personales del Registro de animales de compañía - El Faradio | Periodismo que cuenta

The Cantabrian government disclosed a security breach in the animal registry, which exposed sensitive personal data of individuals registered since 2006. The incident resulted from stolen credentials, highlighting how inadequate access controls can compromise vast amounts of citizen information stored by public entities. This underscores the critical need for robust authentication mechanisms in any system handling personal records. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the risks associated with centralized databases, even those initially intended for specific administrative purposes. While open data promotes transparency, this breach demonstrates that poorly secured public data pools become attractive targets for cybercriminals. It serves as a stark reminder that data utility must never outweigh fundamental security and privacy protections for citizens. Authorities have urged affected individuals to remain vigilant against fraudulent communications and offered multiple channels for verification and support. The event emphasizes that maintaining public trust requires immediate transparency during incidents and proactive measures to mitigate harm. Ultimately, it highlights the delicate balance between leveraging public data for civic benefit and safeguarding it against exploitation.
