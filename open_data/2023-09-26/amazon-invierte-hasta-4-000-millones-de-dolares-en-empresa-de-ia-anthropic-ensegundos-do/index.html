Amazon invierte hasta 4.000 millones de dólares en empresa de IA Anthropic - Ensegundos.do

Amazon’s significant investment in Anthropic accelerates the global race in generative AI, integrating Claude with AWS infrastructure. This strategic move underscores the fierce competition among tech giants to dominate cloud-based machine learning capabilities. The consolidation of resources and proprietary data sources significantly affects the open data landscape by centralizing access to advanced models and potentially limiting the broader public availability of underlying training data and insights.
