501 menores condenados en España por delitos sexuales en 2022, un 14 % más que en 2021 - Mundo - ABC Color

Spanish justice data reveal a rise in convictions for sexual offenses among both minors and adults, predominantly committed by men. This underscores critical gaps in the current open data on gender-disaggregated crime statistics. Enhancing data transparency is essential to accurately monitor these trends and inform effective public policy interventions.
