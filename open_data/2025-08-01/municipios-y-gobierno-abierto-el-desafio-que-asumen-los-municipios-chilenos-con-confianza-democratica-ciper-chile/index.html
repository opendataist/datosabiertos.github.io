Municipios y Gobierno Abierto: el desafío que asumen los municipios chilenos con confianza democrática - CIPER Chile

Municipios y Gobierno Abierto: el desafío que asumen los municipios chilenos con confianza democrática - CIPER Chile

The article argues that municipalities are essential for revitalizing democracy and restoring public trust, as they represent the level of government closest to citizens’ daily lives. By joining the Open Government Partnership, local administrations can transform their relationship with the community, shifting from technical management to a collaborative democratic practice. This transition enables the reintegration of political value into everyday civic life, ensuring that decision-making is more accessible and responsive to local needs. Participation in this global initiative allows local governments to develop concrete, measurable action plans through active citizen engagement. As demonstrated by early adopters, this approach fosters institutional capacity building, transparency, and accountability. Rather than imposing a bureaucratic burden, the commitment provides access to proven methodologies and a global network, enabling municipalities to implement effective tools such as open data platforms, integrity systems, and participatory budgets. This is highly relevant to open data, as the partnership promotes the systematic use of transparency and shared information as foundational pillars of governance. It encourages the creation of public digital platforms and robust data ecosystems that empower citizens to participate meaningfully in policy-making. Ultimately, the article emphasizes that expanding open government principles at the local level is crucial for building a more inclusive, collaborative, and just state.
