Inegi Veracruz Medirá Pobreza tras Desaparición del Coneval - Plumas Libres

Inegi Veracruz Medirá Pobreza tras Desaparición del Coneval - Plumas Libres

The National Statistics Institute assumes responsibility for poverty measurement following the dissolution of the former evaluation council. This institutional shift aims to streamline public administration while ensuring continuity of historical data through the preservation of methodologies and publication schedules. By centralizing these metrics, the government seeks to simplify its structure without disrupting the regular release of crucial socioeconomic indicators. This transition underscores the ongoing consolidation of statistical authority, affecting how multidimensional poverty data is collected and analyzed nationwide. This change is vital for open data, as it alters the primary source of official development policy metrics. Users must now rely on a single unified entity for comprehensive poverty statistics, impacting transparency and the accessibility of consistent, comparable datasets for researchers and civil society.
