Identidades de extranjeros detenidos: Diputados republicanos piden realización de censo carcelario

Identidades de extranjeros detenidos: Diputados republicanos piden realización de censo carcelario

Chilean lawmakers are calling for a National Prison Census to identify inmates using biometric data. This initiative seeks to combat organized crime by ensuring that authorities know the true identities of prisoners, thereby preventing prisons from becoming breeding grounds for criminal gangs. The proposal underscores that this census is essential for national security and institutional sovereignty. Lawmakers argue that transparency regarding the prison population, including foreign nationals, is crucial for effective justice and security measures, addressing concerns about irregular immigration status and potential deportations. This article highlights the critical role of open data in public administration and safety. By advocating for the systematic collection and sharing of accurate prisoner information, it demonstrates how accessible, reliable data is vital for policy-making, transparency, and strengthening institutional trust in the face of security challenges.
