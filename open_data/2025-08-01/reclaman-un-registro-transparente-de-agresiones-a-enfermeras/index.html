Reclaman un registro “transparente” de agresiones a enfermeras

The president of the Jaén College of Nurses is calling for an open and transparent registry to accurately track and reduce aggression against nursing staff. Current discrepancies between official reports and data from the Ministry of Health reveal significant gaps in recording methods, particularly because incidents in the private sector remain unreported. This case highlights the critical importance of open data in public health, as comprehensive and accessible information is essential for identifying patterns, improving coordination, and implementing effective safety policies across all healthcare sectors.
