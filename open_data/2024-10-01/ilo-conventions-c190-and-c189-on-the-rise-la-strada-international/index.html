ILO Conventions C190 and C189 on the Rise - La strada International

Spain’s new decree enhances domestic workers' safety and rights, reinforcing the Domestic Workers Convention. This progress highlights a growing European commitment to protecting vulnerable labor sectors through ratified international standards. Concurrently, the Violence and Harassment Convention gains momentum as more nations ratify and implement its provisions. This dual advancement signals a broader regional shift toward comprehensive labor protections and improved working conditions for marginalized groups. This development is vital for open data as it underscores the need for transparent, accessible datasets tracking labor rights implementation. Accurate data is essential for monitoring compliance, evaluating policy effectiveness, and ensuring accountability across member states enforcing these critical conventions.
