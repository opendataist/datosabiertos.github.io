Cantidad de pequeñas y medianas empresas creció 18% en los últimos seis años

Cantidad de pequeñas y medianas empresas creció 18% en los últimos seis años

The article highlights the critical role of micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) as the backbone of Uruguay’s economy. These entities dominate the industrial, commercial, and service sectors, accounting for the vast majority of businesses and generating a significant proportion of formal jobs. Their high concentration and growth indicate a resilient economic structure that is particularly effective at absorbing shocks, suggesting that supporting MSMEs is essential for national stability and employment. A key finding is the adaptability of MSMEs, which allowed them to suffer less negative impact during crises compared to larger segments. This flexibility enables easier adjustment to challenging market conditions, ensuring sustained job creation even when broader economic indicators fluctuate. The data shows a consistent recovery and growth in both the number of active companies and the workforce, demonstrating that this sector is not only prevalent but also dynamically responsive to economic pressures. This information is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of accessible, transparent statistical reporting for understanding economic resilience. By making detailed insights on business creation, closure, and employment available, governments can better design policies that support the most vital economic actors. Open data facilitates this analysis, allowing stakeholders to identify trends, measure the effectiveness of interventions, and ensure that resources are directed toward the sectors that truly sustain the workforce and commercial activity.
