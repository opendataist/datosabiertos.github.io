Juez federal: Zuckerberg debe declarar en demanda por violación de derechos de autor presentada por autores

A federal judge has determined that there is sufficient evidence to compel Mark Zuckerberg’s testimony, recognizing him as the primary decision-maker for Meta’s artificial intelligence policies. This ruling refutes the company’s argument that other employees could provide equivalent information, underscoring Zuckerberg’s direct oversight of these products. The class-action lawsuit accuses Meta of illegally using copyrighted works to train its models without consent or compensation, a case supported by prominent figures and their legal representatives. This action is part of a series of similar lawsuits against other major AI developers, highlighting growing legal scrutiny over intellectual property in the technology sector. This case is crucial for open data, as it addresses transparency and the legality of obtaining data to train algorithms. If it is established that using copyrighted content without authorization is illegal, it could lead to clearer frameworks governing how corporations must access and use open or protected data, thereby promoting ethics and legitimacy in artificial intelligence.
