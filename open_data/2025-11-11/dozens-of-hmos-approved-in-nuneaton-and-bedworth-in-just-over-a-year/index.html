Dozens of HMOs approved in Nuneaton and Bedworth in just over a year

A recent Freedom of Information request reveals a significant surge in approved Houses in Multiple Occupation within Nuneaton and Bedworth, highlighting the urgent need for stricter regulatory oversight. The high volume of approvals since the start of the fiscal year underscores the rapid expansion of this housing model in the local area. In response to these trends, the local authority is considering an Article 4 direction to remove permitted development rights. This measure would require owners to seek formal planning permission before converting standard houses into HMOs, aiming to manage growth and protect community character. However, updates on the progress of this policy review remain pending despite public demand for transparency. This situation illustrates the critical role of open data in civic engagement and policy formation. By making approval statistics publicly accessible, journalists and citizens can track local development trends and hold institutions accountable. Such transparency enables communities to understand the impact of housing decisions and advocate for appropriate planning controls based on factual evidence.
