Desempleo disminuye a 5.7%, su nivel más bajo del año aunque persisten retos

The report indicates a modest improvement in Costa Rica’s labor market, characterized by a decline in unemployment and rising participation rates. However, this recovery remains uneven and slow, with significant structural challenges persisting despite the positive headline figures. A critical finding is the rise in informal employment and persistent gender gaps, revealing that new jobs often lack stability and that women continue to face barriers to equitable labor inclusion. These disparities highlight that aggregate improvements do not guarantee fairness or quality for all demographic groups. This analysis is relevant to open data by demonstrating how transparent government statistics are essential for identifying hidden socioeconomic inequalities. It underscores the need for granular, accessible datasets to accurately monitor social welfare and inform policies that address structural biases rather than just aggregate trends.
