Wikipedia urges AI companies to use its paid API, and stop scraping | TechCrunch

Wikipedia urges AI companies to use its paid API, and stop scraping | TechCrunch

Wikipedia urges AI developers to license its content via the paid Wikimedia Enterprise platform. This approach ensures financial support for the nonprofit mission while protecting server infrastructure from heavy scraping loads. It shifts the burden of access costs to commercial entities leveraging the data at scale. The initiative emphasizes proper attribution to honor volunteer contributors. By directing traffic through official channels, the foundation aims to reverse declining human engagement. Sustainable support is critical for maintaining the quality and growth of the encyclopedia’s vast knowledge base. This strategy is vital for open data as it establishes ethical frameworks for AI training. It balances the need for large-scale data access with the preservation of source integrity. Ultimately, it seeks a sustainable ecosystem where open knowledge remains robust and properly credited amidst the rise of artificial intelligence.
