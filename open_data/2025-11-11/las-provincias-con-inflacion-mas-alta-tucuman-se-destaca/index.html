Las provincias con inflación más alta: Tucumán se destaca

Las provincias con inflación más alta: Tucumán se destaca

The study reveals a significant regional disparity in inflation across Argentina, with Patagonia experiencing the sharpest increases in food and beverage prices due to a combination of high logistics costs and elevated salaries. In contrast, Northern provinces show lower price hikes but face a heavier burden, as their lower incomes mean that a smaller basket of goods consumes a disproportionately larger share of their earnings. This divergence highlights the complex economic asymmetries within the country, challenging uniform anti-inflationary policies. The data illustrates that while the South has higher absolute costs, its purchasing power remains relatively stronger compared to the North, where poverty levels exacerbate the impact of even modest price changes. Such geographic and income-based differences underscore the difficulty of applying a single monetary strategy to a fragmented market. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how granular, region-specific datasets can expose inequalities that aggregate national statistics often mask. By making detailed price and salary comparisons accessible, open data enables researchers and policymakers to understand the true local impact of inflation, moving beyond averages to address the specific vulnerabilities of different populations.
