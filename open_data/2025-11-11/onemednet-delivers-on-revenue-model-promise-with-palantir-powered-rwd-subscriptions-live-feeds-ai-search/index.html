OneMedNet Delivers on Revenue Model Promise with Palantir-Powered RWD Subscriptions: Live Feeds & AI Search

OneMedNet Delivers on Revenue Model Promise with Palantir-Powered RWD Subscriptions: Live Feeds & AI Search

OneMedNet is transforming the accessibility of real-world health data by integrating advanced AI with the Palantir Foundry ecosystem. This strategic partnership enables the secure generation and analysis of regulatory-grade, de-identified data, addressing the critical industry challenge of obtaining timely and high-quality clinical information. By leveraging these robust technological foundations, the company aims to unlock the potential of vast healthcare datasets for life sciences, diagnostics, and medical device organizations. The introduction of the iRWD platform shifts data access from static models to dynamic, recurring subscription services. Through features like live data feeds and AI-driven conversational search, users can interact with complex clinical information using natural language. This approach democratizes data utilization by lowering technical barriers, allowing stakeholders to instantly build precise patient cohorts and gain real-time insights without relying on rigid, technical search queries. This innovation is vital to the open data movement as it demonstrates how AI can facilitate responsible, secure, and intuitive data sharing across industries. By converting sensitive multimodal data into actionable intelligence while maintaining strict privacy standards, the platform illustrates a scalable model for open, interoperable health analytics. This advancement supports broader goals of transparency and collaboration in healthcare, proving that secure AI tools can bridge the gap between data ownership and open innovation.
