Oposición georgiana celebra concierto "A favor del futuro europeo" en medio de protestas - Mundo - ABC Color

The article highlights Georgia’s contentious foreign agent law, which mandates transparency for NGOs and media outlets receiving significant external funding. President Salome Zourabichvili’s veto was overridden, sparking mass protests and solidifying public opposition to the pro-Russian government’s direction. This legislative move has triggered severe diplomatic consequences, with the European Union warning of negative impacts on integration and the United States threatening to suspend aid. These reactions underscore the high geopolitical stakes and the government’s isolation from Western allies. This issue is crucial for open data advocates as it exposes the tension between government demands for financial transparency and the suppression of independent civil society. It illustrates how legislative frameworks can be weaponized to restrict information flows and weaken democratic institutions under the guise of preventing foreign interference.
