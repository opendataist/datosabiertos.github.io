Abren registro para el Bazar Turístico

Abren registro para el Bazar Turístico

The municipal government invites tourism stakeholders to register for a 2025 trade fair aimed at fostering alliances and business networking. This initiative seeks to enhance local sector development through strategic partnerships and professional exchanges among diverse industry participants. This event is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible, and standardized information regarding public registrations and sector engagement. Open data principles ensure that all interested parties, including civil society and academics, can easily access details about opportunities, fostering inclusivity and accountability in tourism development.
