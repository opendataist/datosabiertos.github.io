Los españoles hicieron en el segundo trimestre 46,4 millones de viajes gastando un 9,7% más

Los españoles hicieron en el segundo trimestre 46,4 millones de viajes gastando un 9,7% más

Spain’s tourism sector showed significant growth in the second quarter, with a notable increase in both domestic travel and international trips. The surge in outbound tourism and leisure activities highlights a recovering and dynamic market, while business travel declined. This data provides a granular view of consumer behavior, revealing shifts in spending patterns between accommodation and transport across different regions. Geographically, key autonomous communities such as Andalusia, Catalonia, and Valencia emerged as primary destinations, while residents from Catalonia and Madrid generated the highest volume of outbound trips. Spending habits varied considerably by destination, with island regions and major cities recording higher daily expenditures. These regional disparities offer valuable insights into local economic impacts and tourism infrastructure needs. This statistical breakdown is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the potential of high-frequency, detailed public statistics for economic analysis. By making such rich, disaggregated datasets available, governments enable researchers and developers to build transparent tools that track economic health, optimize tourism strategies, and foster accountability through accessible, machine-readable information.
