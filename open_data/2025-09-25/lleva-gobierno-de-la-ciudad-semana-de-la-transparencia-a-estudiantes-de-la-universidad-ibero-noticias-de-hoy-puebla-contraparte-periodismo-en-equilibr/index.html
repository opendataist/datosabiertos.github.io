Lleva Gobierno de la Ciudad Semana de la Transparencia a estudiantes de la Universidad Ibero - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The government of Puebla reinforced its commitment to transparency and open data by hosting a forum on personal data protection during Transparency Week. This initiative underscores the administration’s dedication to accountability and inclusive governance by actively involving young people in decision-making processes. The event highlighted the critical importance of safeguarding privacy and using digital tools responsibly to prevent violence against women. By educating students on how to manage personal information, the city promotes a culture of security and informed citizenship in the digital realm. This approach is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency efforts must evolve to incorporate privacy rights. It illustrates that true openness requires not only providing information but also protecting citizens’ data, thereby fostering trust between the government and the community through ethical data practices.
