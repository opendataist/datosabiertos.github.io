NCCI Quarterly Economics Briefing (QEB) – Q3 2025 - WorkersCompensation.com

NCCI Quarterly Economics Briefing (QEB) – Q3 2025 - WorkersCompensation.com

Recent revisions to US employment data reveal a significantly weaker labor market than initially reported, with recent monthly gains averaging far below early estimates. This downward adjustment highlights substantial volatility and challenges the assumption of steady economic expansion, suggesting the recovery may be more fragile than headline figures implied. Despite these disappointing job counts, broader indicators such as unemployment rates tell a less dire story. This divergence underscores the necessity of analyzing multiple data sources rather than relying solely on initial payroll estimates to understand the true health of the workforce and economic stability. This analysis is relevant to open data practices because it demonstrates how transparent, revised datasets provide a more accurate narrative than preliminary reports. Access to complete, updated information allows stakeholders to identify discrepancies, reduce uncertainty, and make informed decisions based on the full picture rather than incomplete snapshots.
