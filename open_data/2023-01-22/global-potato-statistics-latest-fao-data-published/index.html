Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The article highlights how the Food and Agriculture Organization maintains a comprehensive, publicly accessible global database on potato production, featuring extensive metrics for numerous countries. This resource demonstrates the practical value of open data in agriculture, allowing researchers, policymakers, and stakeholders to easily retrieve standardized statistics. The ability to navigate the FAOSTAT platform directly empowers users to perform independent searches, fostering transparency and facilitating data-driven decision-making across the global food supply chain. China and India emerge as the dominant producers, illustrating the significant scale of potato cultivation worldwide. By aggregating this information, the database reveals global production trends and regional disparities without requiring users to compile disparate national reports. This centralized approach simplifies the analysis of agricultural outputs, enabling a clearer understanding of which regions contribute most to global supply. Such insights are crucial for assessing food security, trade dynamics, and the economic importance of staple crops on an international level. This summary is relevant to open data because it showcases a major international organization providing free, structured, and searchable statistical resources. The FAO’s commitment to publishing detailed agricultural data supports research, monitoring, and innovation in sustainable farming practices. By making this information available to everyone, the initiative promotes accountability and enables a broader community to engage with critical global issues. It serves as a model for how open data can enhance knowledge sharing and drive informed actions in addressing worldwide food challenges.
