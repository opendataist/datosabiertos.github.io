Los últimos ocho años han sido los más cálidos jamás registrados a nivel mundial | Análisis

Scientific evidence confirms that greenhouse gas-driven global warming is an irreversible trend, with recent years recording unprecedented temperatures. Despite the temporary cooling effects of phenomena such as La Niña, the long-term trajectory indicates that the 1.5°C threshold established by the Paris Agreement will be progressively exceeded, exacerbating weather disasters that threaten global security. The ability to predict these extreme events has improved significantly, enabling society to prepare with greater advance notice. However, serious deficiencies persist in basic meteorological observations in regions such as Africa and island states, compromising forecast quality and worsening human and economic losses during climate crises. This report is crucial for open data because it highlights the reliance on international, open, multi-source datasets to validate climate indicators. Transparency in compiling information from various agencies and universities helps reduce uncertainties and supports evidence-based public policies, demonstrating that free access to high-quality meteorological data is fundamental to climate change resilience.
