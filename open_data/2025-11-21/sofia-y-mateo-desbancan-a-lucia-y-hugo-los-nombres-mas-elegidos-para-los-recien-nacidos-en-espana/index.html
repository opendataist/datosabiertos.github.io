Sofía y Mateo desbancan a Lucía y Hugo: los nombres más elegidos para los recién nacidos en España

This shift varies regionally, with distinct leaders in different autonomous communities, reflecting diverse local identities rather than a monolithic national taste. These patterns demonstrate how demographic data captures subtle societal changes, offering valuable insights for open data initiatives focused on cultural evolution.
