Paraguay's population below 2015 projections, census shows

Paraguay's population below 2015 projections, census shows

The 2022 census reveals Paraguay’s population falls significantly below previous projections due to declining fertility, reduced life expectancy, and increased emigration. These demographic shifts indicate structural socioeconomic challenges that contradict earlier optimistic forecasts, highlighting the volatility of population dynamics in the region. Open data initiatives must prioritize accurate, transparent census methodologies to reflect these complex realities. Reliable public datasets are essential for governments and researchers to understand underlying trends, such as the corrected rise in indigenous populations due to improved collection methods, rather than relying on flawed estimates. This transparency is crucial for effective policy-making and resource allocation. By openly sharing precise demographic indicators, stakeholders can address gaps in healthcare and social services, ensuring that data-driven decisions accurately serve the needs of a changing and increasingly diverse population.
