PSA releases 43rd issue of ‘Window on Economic Statistics of Qatar’

The Planning and Statistics Authority released comprehensive macroeconomic data for Qatar’s fourth quarter of 2022. This report integrates critical indicators across national accounts, prices, and public finance into a single resource. Such aggregation demonstrates high open_data value by centralizing diverse datasets for easier access and analysis. The publication facilitates direct comparisons with international economies and tracks key performance metrics against previous periods. By presenting standardized metrics like GDP growth and inflation, it enables consistent benchmarking. This approach supports transparency and allows stakeholders to understand Qatar’s economic position relative to global trends effectively. Additionally, the report explains methodologies behind mid-year population estimates, highlighting data utility in various sectors. This transparency regarding data sources and compilation methods builds trust in the statistical framework. Ultimately, the release underscores the importance of accessible, well-documented economic statistics for informed policy-making and public understanding.
