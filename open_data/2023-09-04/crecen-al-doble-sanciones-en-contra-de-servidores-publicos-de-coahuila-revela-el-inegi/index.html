Crecen al doble sanciones en contra de servidores públicos de Coahuila, revela el Inegi

In 2022, Coahuila doubled the number of administrative sanctions imposed on public servants, increasing from 21 to 44 individuals. This significant rise underscores the strengthening of internal oversight mechanisms within state secretariats to address irregularities and complaints. However, the penalties applied were exclusively for non-serious infractions. This distinction indicates that while procedural compliance is being enforced, the state has not encountered or prosecuted major misconduct during this period. This data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the value of national statistical censuses such as those conducted by INEGI. Such transparent and accessible datasets promote public accountability by revealing trends in government administration and disciplinary actions across different states.
