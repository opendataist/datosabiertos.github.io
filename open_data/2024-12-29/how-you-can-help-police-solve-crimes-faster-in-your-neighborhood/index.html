How you can help police solve crimes faster in your neighborhood

Pueblo residents and businesses can now voluntarily register surveillance cameras with police, enabling officers to identify potential evidence sources and request footage more efficiently. This initiative aims to accelerate criminal investigations by streamlining access to visual data without granting direct unauthorized entry to private systems. This model highlights the potential of open data principles in public safety, balancing community transparency with effective, privacy-respecting law enforcement strategies.
