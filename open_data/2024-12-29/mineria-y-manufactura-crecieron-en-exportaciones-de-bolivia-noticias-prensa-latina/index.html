Minería y manufactura crecieron en exportaciones de Bolivia - Noticias Prensa Latina

Bolivia’s export profile has undergone a significant structural transformation, shifting away from its historical reliance on hydrocarbon extraction toward a more diversified economic base. This evolution highlights a strategic decoupling from raw resource dependency, signaling a mature adaptation to global market dynamics and internal industrial policies. The primary conclusion is that manufacturing now dominates the export basket, surpassing hydrocarbons to become the central pillar of national foreign trade. This resurgence indicates successful efforts to add value domestically, suggesting that policy interventions have effectively stimulated industrial capacity and competitiveness beyond traditional extractive sectors. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, longitudinal metrics to evaluate policy efficacy. By making these competitive indicators accessible, stakeholders can analyze sectoral shifts, inform investment decisions, and promote accountability in economic planning through evidence-based public scrutiny.
