Departamento del Tesoro suspende la aplicación de la Ley de Transparencia Corporativa y de la BOI - La Opinión

Departamento del Tesoro suspende la aplicación de la Ley de Transparencia Corporativa y de la BOI - La Opinión

The U.S. Department of the Treasury has suspended enforcement of the Corporate Transparency Act, eliminating the requirement for domestic entities to file reports on beneficial owners. This decision aims to reduce the regulatory burden on small businesses, arguing that the previous requirement was an intrusive measure harmful to national economic prosperity. The agency also guarantees immunity from penalties, both before and after the implementation of the new regulatory changes. Additionally, it proposes limiting the scope of the regulation exclusively to foreign companies, marking a significant shift in the current administration’s compliance and enforcement strategy. This news is relevant to open data because it illustrates how political decisions can drastically alter public data flows. The suspension eliminates a key source of information on corporate ownership, reducing transparency in the corporate ecosystem and affecting the availability of data for integrity research and market analysis.
