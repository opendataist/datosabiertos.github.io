España crece un 3,2% en 2024 y lidera la eurozona, tras avanzar un 0,8% en el cuarto trimestre

España crece un 3,2% en 2024 y lidera la eurozona, tras avanzar un 0,8% en el cuarto trimestre

Spain’s economy consolidated its leadership in the eurozone during 2024, surpassing both government forecasts and those of the Bank of Spain thanks to growth primarily driven by domestic demand. This dynamism, sustained by household consumption and investment, demonstrates notable resilience in the face of external challenges, including adverse weather events, positioning the country as the fastest-growing economy among the major nations in the euro area. Despite this exceptional performance, concerns remain about long-term sustainability due to the broader slowdown across Europe and the weak contribution of external demand. Analysts warn that future growth will depend on a European recovery and improvements in productivity, forecasting a moderation of growth rates in the coming years as the immediate drivers of the current cycle are exhausted. This information is relevant to open data because the official macroeconomic indicators published by the National Statistics Institute (INE) constitute a fundamental source for economic trend analysis. The transparency and accessibility of these data enable researchers, analysts, and the general public to validate national performance, conduct accurate international comparisons, and understand the impact of structural variables such as investment and consumption on economic prosperity.
