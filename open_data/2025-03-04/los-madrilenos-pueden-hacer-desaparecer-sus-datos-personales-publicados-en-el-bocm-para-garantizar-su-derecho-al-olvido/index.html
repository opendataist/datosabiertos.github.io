Los madrileños pueden hacer desaparecer sus datos personales publicados en el BOCM para garantizar su derecho al olvido

Los madrileños pueden hacer desaparecer sus datos personales publicados en el BOCM para garantizar su derecho al olvido

The Madrid regional government now guarantees citizens’ right to be forgotten in the Official Regional Bulletin. While official documents are retained for transparency purposes, their indexing in search engines is deactivated upon request. This approach strikes a balance between preserving historical records and safeguarding individual privacy rights, enabling public administration to retain data without permanently exposing personal information in search results. Moreover, the bulletin has adopted open formats such as XML and JSON to enhance accessibility. These machine-readable standards ensure that official information is easily understandable by search engines and artificial intelligence models. By moving beyond traditional PDFs, the administration facilitates more efficient data processing and dissemination for both humans and automated systems. This initiative is significant for open data, as it demonstrates how public institutions can modernize legacy systems. It shows that open formats improve interoperability while respecting legal constraints, such as privacy regulations. The effort highlights a sustainable model for sharing government information that is both technically accessible and legally compliant, encouraging broader adoption of standardized, open digital practices in public sector communications.
