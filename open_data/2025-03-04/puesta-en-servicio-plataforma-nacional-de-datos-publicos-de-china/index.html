Puesta en servicio plataforma nacional de datos públicos de China

Puesta en servicio plataforma nacional de datos públicos de China

China has launched its national public data registration platform, integrating provincial resources to standardize the publication and search of information in key sectors such as health and climate. This advancement marks a crucial step toward market-oriented resource allocation, enabling providers to publish and users to access these digital assets more efficiently. The central objective is to reduce business costs and foster new high-quality productive forces. By improving access to information, the initiative aims to drive sustainable economic development and strengthen governance, positioning data as a fundamental driver for the real economy and for expanding consumer demand in the medium to long term. This initiative is relevant to the open data field because it demonstrates how institutional technological infrastructure can formalize and scale public data transparency. By creating a structured ecosystem that connects providers and users, China establishes a replicable model showing how technical standardization and national integration can accelerate the economic valuation of open data, moving beyond mere availability to achieve practical utility and macroeconomic impact.
