Los madrileños descargan 400.000 documentos del Portal de Transparencia, con más de 1,5 millones de visitas

Los madrileños descargan 400.000 documentos del Portal de Transparencia, con más de 1,5 millones de visitas

The Community of Madrid’s Transparency Portal demonstrates a strong commitment to open data by processing more than 1,400 public information requests in 2024, maintaining a high approval rate and swift response times. This efficiency highlights a well-functioning administrative framework that enables citizens to effectively exercise their right to access information, thereby reinforcing trust in public institutions through timely and comprehensive replies. Furthermore, the platform recorded over 1,600 registrations in its lobby registry, signaling a significant increase in registered interest groups. This growth indicates heightened societal engagement with transparency mechanisms, as diverse stakeholders increasingly use official channels to disclose their influence activities. The system’s capacity to meet this demand underscores its role as a central hub for openly monitoring political and corporate interactions. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how structured, accessible digital portals facilitate civic oversight and anti-corruption efforts. By publishing detailed executive data and legislative processes in plain language, the administration demonstrates that open government is not merely about releasing datasets, but about creating an inclusive ecosystem for accountability. Such practices serve as a model for other regions seeking to balance transparency with user-friendly information dissemination, ultimately empowering citizens to participate more actively in democratic governance.
