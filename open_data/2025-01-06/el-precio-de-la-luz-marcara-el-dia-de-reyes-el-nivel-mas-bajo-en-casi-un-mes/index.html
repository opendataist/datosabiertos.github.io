El precio de la luz marcará el Día de Reyes el nivel más bajo en casi un mes

The article highlights a recent decline in wholesale electricity prices, noting that while current low levels are attractive, experts anticipate an upward trend in 2025 due to natural gas price volatility. This creates significant uncertainty for consumers, as short-term market fluctuations are becoming increasingly difficult to predict, despite recent annual decreases. The relevance to open data lies in the evolving regulatory framework for the regulated electricity tariff. The system is gradually shifting its calculation methodology to incorporate medium- to long-term futures markets, thereby reducing reliance on immediate wholesale price data. This transition aims to stabilize costs while preserving incentives for efficient energy consumption. This structural change implies that open data initiatives must monitor not only spot prices but also futures market indicators. Stakeholders require access to comprehensive, transparent datasets to understand how the combination of short- and long-term pricing mechanisms affects final consumer costs over time.
