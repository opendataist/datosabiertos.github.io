Advirtió 'La China' por peligro ante desaparición del INAI

Advirtió 'La China' por peligro ante desaparición del INAI

Deputy Frías denounces that Morena seeks to dismantle the INAI, arguing that this action centralizes power and eliminates transparency under the guise of austerity. The agency has been instrumental in uncovering cases of corruption and femicides, protecting citizens' rights and ensuring government accountability. This is relevant to open data because the elimination of such an institution would destroy the legal frameworks that require the government to publish public data, weakening access to free information for civil society.
