Recession rocks New Zealand

New Zealand’s economy shows persistent contraction in per capita output, signaling a broader trend of economic weakness across the Anglosphere. This data highlights critical challenges in labor markets and firm activity, suggesting potential further decline before recovery. Such macroeconomic indicators are vital for open_data as they provide the empirical foundation for tracking national well-being and economic health. Transparent, accessible data allows researchers and policymakers to monitor these trends, enabling evidence-based decisions that address structural inefficiencies and support sustainable growth.
