Plataforma Nacional de Transparencia: en riesgo 14 millones de datos

Plataforma Nacional de Transparencia: en riesgo 14 millones de datos

The potential disappearance of the National Institute for Transparency (INAI) poses a serious threat to the National Transparency Platform (PNT), a critical digital asset that hosts millions of essential public data records. Authorities warn that the legal reform, by omitting specifications regarding the fate of the infrastructure and the transfer of responsibilities to the Secretariat of Public Administration, creates uncertainty about who will guarantee cybersecurity and technical maintenance of these systems. The lack of clarity in the proposal suggests that current operational capacity and financial resources dedicated to protecting the integrity of information against cyberattacks could be lost. Beyond the technical costs, the primary risk lies in the functional exclusion of most public institutions. If the platform is transferred solely to the Executive Branch, more than ninety percent of obligated entities, including legislative and judicial bodies, would be left out of the system. This would transform the tool into an incomplete and biased database, losing its value as a comprehensive national repository. The fragmentation of information would hinder transparent and unified access to data from all levels of government, weakening the foundational architecture of the right to information. This scenario is highly relevant to the field of open data, as it illustrates how political decisions can compromise the technical sustainability and impartiality of transparency infrastructures. The PNT is not merely a storage facility for records, but a vital tool for investigative journalism and accountability, having exposed significant corruption cases. Its preservation requires robust legal guarantees that ensure operational continuity, cybersecurity, and universal inclusion of all authorities, preventing privatization or administrative simplification from eroding democratic access to public information.
