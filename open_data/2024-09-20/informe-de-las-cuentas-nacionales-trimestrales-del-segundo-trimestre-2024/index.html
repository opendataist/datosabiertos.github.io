Informe de las cuentas nacionales trimestrales del segundo trimestre 2024

The national economic activity showed an overall expansion driven primarily by a strong performance in the petroleum sector, particularly in gas exploitation and crude oil production. Concurrently, the non-oil GDP experienced modest growth, with notable increases in forestry, construction, and commerce. However, this positive trend was counterbalanced by significant contractions in financial activities, energy distribution, and public administration, highlighting a divergent economic landscape where resource-based sectors outperformed service and industrial segments. Despite the current quarter’s growth, the economy faced volatility when compared to the previous period, witnessing sharp declines in key areas like gas exploitation and transportation. This instability underscores the sectoral fragility inherent in the current economic model. Furthermore, the upward revision of earlier data reveals how dynamic and sometimes volatile statistical baselines can be, particularly when updates to hydrocarbon production figures significantly alter historical economic assessments. This article is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates the critical importance of transparent, accessible official statistics for economic analysis. The source, the National Institute of Statistics of Equatorial Guinea, provides detailed sectoral breakdowns and revisions that allow researchers to track economic shifts and data accuracy. Open access to such granular, timely, and revised datasets enables better understanding of economic drivers and ensures that policy decisions and academic inquiries are based on the most current and verified information available.
