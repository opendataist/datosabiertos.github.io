Enhancing agricultural research with FAO’s AGRIS and AGROVOC programs: A conversation with IITA knowledge management team

Sharing research with global agricultural databases significantly boosts institutional visibility and citation rates. This expanded reach amplifies the impact of local scientific efforts on an international stage, ensuring broader dissemination of critical agricultural findings. Standardizing metadata through these collaborations streamlines internal data management processes. This improvement enhances the efficiency of archiving and retrieval, demonstrating that aligned standards are essential for effective open knowledge systems. This case study highlights the tangible value of participating in open data infrastructures. It proves that interoperable systems like AGRIS are vital for maximizing the utility and reach of public sector research outputs globally.
