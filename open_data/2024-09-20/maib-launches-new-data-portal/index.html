MAIB Launches New Data Portal

MAIB Launches New Data Portal

MAIB now provides open access to maritime incident data through an interactive portal, replacing cumbersome freedom of information requests. This shift empowers the public and industry to analyze trends directly using user-friendly dashboards and downloadable raw datasets. By collaborating with stakeholders, MAIB ensures the data’s accuracy and relevance, fostering trust in open-source analysis tools. The availability of linked CSV files allows for deeper investigation without proprietary software barriers. This initiative is vital for open_data as it democratizes access to critical safety information. It demonstrates how government agencies can enhance transparency and encourage data-driven decision-making by removing traditional access restrictions.
