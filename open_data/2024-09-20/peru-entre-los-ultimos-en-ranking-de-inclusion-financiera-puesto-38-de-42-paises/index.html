Perú entre los últimos en ranking de inclusión financiera: puesto 38 de 42 países

Perú entre los últimos en ranking de inclusión financiera: puesto 38 de 42 países

Peru faces a significant financial inclusion challenge, with nearly half of its population outside the banking system, which motivates the implementation of a sovereign digital currency. This initiative aims to democratize access to financial services, enabling coverage of users in remote areas and reducing the barriers that prevent full economic participation. The adoption of this technology promises to increase transaction efficiency, lower costs, and foster the formalization of the economy. By offering greater transparency and security, the digital currency not only simplifies payments for consumers and businesses but also contributes to a reduction in cash usage and the integration of excluded sectors. This case is relevant to open data because a digital currency generates a massive volume of structured transactional data. Its implementation requires robust privacy and access frameworks, providing a critical scenario for debating how financial technology can be used to empower citizens through transparency and the availability of economic information.
