SC, ST, OBC faculty positions lie vacant at IIM-Tiruchi: RTI data

The article reveals a systemic failure in diversity hiring at premier Indian institutions, with IIM-Tiruchi leaving a vast majority of reserved faculty positions for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes unfilled. This lack of implementation renders constitutional mandates for social reservation ineffective, preventing qualified candidates from these communities from securing academic roles despite their eligibility. Relevance to open data lies in the critical role of transparency in enforcing equity. The discovery of these vacancies required a forced disclosure through the Right to Information Act, highlighting that institutions often withhold diversity metrics rather than proactively maintaining public rosters. Without accessible, granular data on appointment status, stakeholders cannot hold institutions accountable, and policy gaps remain obscured from public scrutiny. Ultimately, this case underscores that data availability is a prerequisite for social justice in higher education. When recruitment details are not openly published, it becomes nearly impossible to track progress or identify bottlenecks in hiring processes. Ensuring that reservation roster data is standardized and publicly accessible is essential for monitoring inclusion goals and ensuring that constitutional promises of equitable representation are actually fulfilled in academic settings.
