Valoran como exitosa primera jornada de censo en Angola - Noticias Prensa Latina

Angola successfully conducted its second post-independence census, prioritizing the enumeration of homeless populations to ensure comprehensive demographic coverage. The initiative leveraged digital technology through tablets and coordinated extensive logistical support involving government officials, community leaders, and police, demonstrating effective public administration and inclusive outreach strategies. This massive data collection effort highlights the critical importance of accurate, up-to-date statistical information for national planning and resource allocation. By engaging high-profile political figures and utilizing modern tools, the census aims to reduce data gaps and improve the reliability of socioeconomic indicators essential for policy-making. For open data, this case illustrates the value of digitizing censuses to create accessible, standardized datasets. It emphasizes how technological integration in official statistics can enhance transparency, facilitate better analysis of vulnerable groups, and provide a foundation for evidence-based governance and social inclusion policies.
