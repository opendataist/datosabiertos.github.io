El Indec dará a conocer mañana la variación de precios minoristas de marzo, y las consultoras promedian en 7% - Impulsobaires

Rising inflation, driven by drought and tariff increases, necessitates government intervention to protect workers’ purchasing power. By exempting salary supplements from income tax, the state aims to maintain economic stability for hundreds of thousands of employees during this period of price volatility. This measure highlights the critical link between macroeconomic policy and labor welfare, demonstrating how fiscal tools can mitigate the impact of external shocks on household incomes. In terms of open data, this case underscores the importance of transparent, timely statistical sources, such as central bank surveys. Reliable public data enables accurate economic forecasting and informs evidence-based policy decisions that directly affect societal well-being.
