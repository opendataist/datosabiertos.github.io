Sin consenso para nombrar comisionados INAI: Secretaría de Gobernación

Sin consenso para nombrar comisionados INAI: Secretaría de Gobernación

The inability to appoint three INAI commissioners creates a critical quorum shortage, potentially paralyzing Mexico’s transparency institute. This legislative deadlock highlights how political consensus—or the lack thereof—directly impacts the operational capacity of oversight bodies essential for public accountability. The situation underscores the fragility of institutional independence when appointments depend on rigid parliamentary majorities. Without functional commissioners, the agency cannot adjudicate information requests, leaving citizens with reduced mechanisms to access public data. This case is vital for open data advocacy as it demonstrates that technical access rights are meaningless without the institutional enforcement capacity to protect them. Sustainable open government requires resilient structures that can survive political shifts, ensuring transparency remains an operational reality rather than a theoretical promise.
