México reporta récord de divisas por turismo internacional en primer bimestre de 2023 - Noticaribe

Mexico achieved record foreign currency earnings from international visitors in the early months of 2023. This surge significantly outpaces pre-pandemic figures, signaling a robust recovery in the tourism sector and demonstrating the economic value of accessible travel data. Although visitor numbers remain slightly below 2019 levels, the substantial growth in revenue highlights strong consumer confidence and demand. This trend underscores how statistical evidence can accurately measure sector resilience and guide future economic strategies. This report is relevant to open data because it showcases how transparent, timely statistics empower stakeholders. By making such indicators public, citizens and policymakers can make informed decisions, fostering accountability and driving sustainable development through data-driven insights.
