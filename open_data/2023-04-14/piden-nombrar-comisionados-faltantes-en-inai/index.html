Piden nombrar comisionados faltantes en INAI

The INAI faces a critical operational halt due to the lack of legal quorum in its plenary session, preventing the resolution of hundreds of citizen appeals. This stalemate highlights the urgent need for political consensus in the Senate to appoint missing commissioners, emphasizing the fragility of institutional independence when governance mechanisms fail. Despite this paralysis, the institute maintains its commitment to transparency and personal data protection, asserting that its core mission remains vigorous even without a full council. This resilience underscores the importance of maintaining functional oversight bodies to ensure public trust and adherence to data privacy standards. This situation is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how political gridlock directly impedes the enforcement of information rights. Without a fully operational regulatory body, citizens lose effective recourse against data violations, weakening the entire ecosystem of accountability and open governance.
