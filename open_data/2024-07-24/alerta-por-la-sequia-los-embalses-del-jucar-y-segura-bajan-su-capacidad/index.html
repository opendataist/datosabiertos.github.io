Alerta por la sequía: los embalses del Júcar y Segura bajan su capacidad

Alerta por la sequía: los embalses del Júcar y Segura bajan su capacidad

Spanish reservoir levels are declining, with critical shortages in the Segura basin. This data highlights the urgent need for open water resources to monitor drought impacts. Public access to such real-time hydrological metrics is essential for transparent environmental management and informed decision-making. The widespread decrease in stored water underscores systemic vulnerabilities. Open data initiatives enable better resource allocation and policy responses to climate challenges. Tracking these trends publicly fosters accountability and scientific rigor. It empowers stakeholders to address water security proactively, ensuring sustainable management during periods of scarcity.
