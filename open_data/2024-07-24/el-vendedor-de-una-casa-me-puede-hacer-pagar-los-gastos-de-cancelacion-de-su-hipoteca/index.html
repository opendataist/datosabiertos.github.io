The article clarifies that while Spanish law does not explicitly mandate who bears the costs of cancelling a seller’s mortgage registration, Supreme Court jurisprudence establishes that the seller, as the original mortgagor, is primarily responsible. Although private agreements in preliminary contracts may shift this burden to the buyer, such arrangements are optional and often disadvantageous. Buyers are advised to insist that sellers cover these expenses to avoid unnecessary financial strain, as forcing a buyer to pay can significantly complicate the transaction process and deter potential purchasers from completing the sale. Relevance to open data lies in the transparency of legal precedents and financial practices within the housing market. This knowledge empowers individuals to recognize their rights and negotiate better terms, fostering a more equitable and informed marketplace where legal standards are clearly understood by all parties involved.
