Recession ends with GDP boost

Recession ends with GDP boost

Australia’s Q2 2025 national accounts reveal stronger-than-expected GDP growth, signaling an end to the per capita recession. Driven by household and government spending, this rebound highlights domestic resilience despite prior weather disruptions. This data exemplifies how timely, transparent official statistics empower analysts to accurately assess economic health. Open data on national accounts is vital for fostering trust and enabling informed public discourse on fiscal policy and societal well-being.
