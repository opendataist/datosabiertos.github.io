Más de mil millones de personas padecen trastornos de salud mental

Más de mil millones de personas padecen trastornos de salud mental

The World Health Organization highlights that over a billion people suffer from mental health disorders, primarily anxiety and depression, which cause significant disability and substantial economic losses through reduced productivity. These conditions disproportionately affect women and represent a leading cause of suicide, particularly among youth, underscoring the urgent need for global policy prioritization and multisectoral collaboration to improve access and reduce stigma. The article is highly relevant to open data because accurate public health statistics are essential for driving the necessary investment and policy reforms in mental health services. Transparent, accessible data on the prevalence and economic impact of these disorders empowers governments to recognize mental health as a basic right rather than a privilege, ensuring that resources are allocated effectively to protect population well-being and economic stability worldwide.
