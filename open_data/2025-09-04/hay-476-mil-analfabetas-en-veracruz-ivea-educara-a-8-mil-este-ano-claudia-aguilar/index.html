Hay 476 mil analfabetas en Veracruz; IVEA educará a 8 mil este año: Claudia Aguilar

Hay 476 mil analfabetas en Veracruz; IVEA educará a 8 mil este año: Claudia Aguilar

Veracruz faces the country’s highest educational deficit, driven by limited resources relative to the massive number of illiterate residents. The government relies on university students as community promoters to deliver literacy instruction, acknowledging that systemic change requires significant time and sustained effort. To address this, the administration has launched a digital platform enabling citizens to report individuals needing assistance. This initiative establishes the first centralized database for identifying those affected, allowing authorities to target support more effectively and involve the broader society in the solution. This case is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible databases can bridge the gap between policy goals and social reality. By empowering citizens to contribute data on illiteracy, the state leverages open information to mobilize resources and foster collaborative public engagement in solving complex social challenges.
