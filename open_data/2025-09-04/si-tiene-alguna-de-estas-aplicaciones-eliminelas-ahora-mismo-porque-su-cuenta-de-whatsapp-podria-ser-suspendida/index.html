Si tiene alguna de estas aplicaciones, elimínelas ahora mismo porque su cuenta de WhatsApp podría ser suspendida

Si tiene alguna de estas aplicaciones, elimínelas ahora mismo porque su cuenta de WhatsApp podría ser suspendida

The central warning from WhatsApp regarding the use of unofficial apps, such as WhatsApp Plus or GB WhatsApp, highlights that these counterfeit tools lack essential security standards. Without proper encryption, they expose users to serious vulnerabilities that facilitate the theft of personal information and the spread of malware. This demonstrates that the convenience of customized features does not outweigh the loss of privacy and basic device security. The relevance to open data lies in the critical importance of verifying the provenance and integrity of data handled by digital platforms. When unauthorized applications are used, an unknown risk factor is introduced into the data ecosystem, undermining trust in the transmission of information. The lack of transparency regarding how these third parties handle personal data underscores the need to prioritize official and verified sources to ensure the protection of sensitive information. As a logical consequence, platforms adopt strict measures, such as account bans, to maintain user trust. This approach emphasizes that interoperability and customization should not come at the expense of structural security. The recommendation is to reinstall the official version from authorized sources, thereby ensuring that backups and data transfers are reliable and protected against unauthorized access.
