Desvelan los sueldos de los nuevos fichajes de RTVE: De Pepa Bueno hasta David Cantero

Desvelan los sueldos de los nuevos fichajes de RTVE: De Pepa Bueno hasta David Cantero

This article highlights how a transparency portal request by El Mundo revealed the specific salaries of high-profile journalists joining RTVE, including Pepa Bueno and David Cantero. It illustrates how open data mechanisms enable the public to scrutinize public entity expenditures, directly linking institutional transparency to fiscal accountability. The disclosure exposes significant salary disparities among staff, ranging from approximately 110,000 to over 265,000 euros. By making these financial details accessible, the case demonstrates the power of open data in uncovering the true cost of public broadcasting and ensuring that taxpayer funds are managed with clear visibility and justification. The relevance to open data lies in its role as a tool for civic oversight. It proves that accessible public records allow citizens to monitor government spending effectively, fostering trust and encouraging institutions to maintain higher standards of financial openness and integrity in their operations.
