Tienen hasta el 23 de febrero ciudadanos para inscribirse y participar en el Cabildo Abierto en Córdoba - Plumas Libres

This announcement highlights a local government initiative to engage citizens through an open council session, directly applying principles of participatory democracy. By inviting public input on youth sports projects, the municipality demonstrates how open data frameworks can facilitate transparent, inclusive dialogue between authorities and communities. Such mechanisms are vital for open data ecosystems as they bridge the gap between digital transparency and active civic engagement. They ensure that government data and decision-making processes remain accessible and responsive to societal needs. Ultimately, this case illustrates the practical relevance of open data in strengthening democratic accountability. It shows how structured public participation transforms raw administrative data into actionable community insights, fostering trust and collaboration in local governance.
