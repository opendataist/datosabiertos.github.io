Sancionan con 40% del sueldo a alcalde de Antofagasta por infringir 12 veces la Ley de Transparencia

The Council for Transparency sanctioned the mayor of Antofagasta for systematically failing to comply with laws guaranteeing public access to information. This action reaffirms that transparency is not optional, but a strict legal obligation for public officials. The ruling demonstrates that impunity does not exist when citizens’ right to know is violated, establishing a clear precedent regarding the personal responsibility of municipal leaders in the management of public data. The case highlights the importance of effective oversight and hierarchical control within local administrations. Declarations of compliance are not enough; institutions must ensure real mechanisms that guarantee the timely provision of information. The falsehoods in the councilor’s defense aggravated the sanction, underscoring that truthfulness and diligence are fundamental pillars of open governance and public trust. This incident is significant for the open data community because it illustrates the tangible consequences of administrative silence. Effective transparency requires organizational structures committed to publishing information and responding promptly to requests. By imposing financial penalties and requiring the public disclosure of fines, the regulatory body strengthens the culture of open data, reminding everyone that access to information is an essential tool for accountability and democratic participation.
