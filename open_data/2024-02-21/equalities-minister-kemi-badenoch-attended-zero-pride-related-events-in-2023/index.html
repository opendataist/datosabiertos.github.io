Equalities minister Kemi Badenoch attended zero Pride-related events in 2023

Kemi Badenoch’s absence from all official LGBTQ+ Pride events and minimal engagement with only two gender-critical groups reveal a stark departure from her predecessors’ inclusive approach. This disengagement reflects her consistent criticism by the community due to anti-trans rhetoric and stalled legislative progress, particularly regarding conversion therapy bans. The relevance to open data lies in how Freedom of Information requests exposed the discrepancy between the minister’s claims of extensive engagement and the factual reality. These transparent records demonstrate that accountability mechanisms are essential for verifying public officials' actions and holding them responsible for inclusive representation. Ultimately, the article highlights the critical role of data transparency in democracy. By making ministerial activities visible, open data initiatives empower citizens to scrutinize leadership and advocate for marginalized communities. It proves that accessing public records is vital for ensuring equitable governance and challenging political narratives that may obscure harmful policy decisions.
