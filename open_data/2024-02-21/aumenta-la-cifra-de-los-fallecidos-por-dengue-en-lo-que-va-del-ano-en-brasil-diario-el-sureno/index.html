Aumenta la cifra de los fallecidos por dengue en lo que va del año en Brasil - Diario El Sureño

Brazil is experiencing an unprecedented surge in dengue cases, with confirmed deaths and probable infections rising sharply compared to the previous year. This dramatic increase has triggered emergency responses in major urban centers, including Rio de Janeiro, highlighting the severe strain on public health infrastructure and the urgent need for containment measures against mosquito breeding sites. The crisis disproportionately affects specific regions and young adolescents, exacerbated by climate phenomena such as El Niño. Public health authorities are prioritizing vaccination for children and promoting community-based larval elimination. These interventions reflect a critical shift toward proactive management, acknowledging that traditional responses are insufficient against such rapid epidemiological expansion driven by environmental changes. This situation is highly relevant to open data, as transparent, real-time reporting of incidence rates and mortality is essential for effective crisis management. Open data enables authorities to identify hotspots, allocate resources efficiently, and track the impact of interventions. Furthermore, accessible information empowers citizens to protect themselves and fosters accountability, demonstrating how data transparency directly supports public health outcomes during epidemics.
