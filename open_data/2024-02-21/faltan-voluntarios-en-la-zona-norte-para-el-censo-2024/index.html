Faltan voluntarios en la zona norte para el censo 2024

The Bolivian National Statistics Institute emphasizes that the upcoming census is a collective civic project requiring broad participation, rather than merely a government initiative. Recruitment efforts are experiencing uneven progress across regions, with significant gaps in specific neighborhoods of Cochabamba and several municipalities failing to meet their expansion targets. This disparity underscores the critical need for immediate community mobilization to ensure the census effectively reaches all citizens. The success of the census depends on surpassing recruitment goals by over thirty percent, as high volunteer numbers are essential for accurate data collection. Currently, while some departments exceed their targets, others lag significantly, risking the completeness and reliability of the national population and housing records. Addressing these imbalances is vital to guarantee that every demographic is represented in the final results. This situation is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as the census serves as the foundational dataset for future statistical transparency and public policy analysis. Incomplete recruitment leads to data gaps, compromising the integrity of open datasets used for research, resource allocation, and accountability. Therefore, robust participation ensures that the resulting open data is comprehensive, reliable, and truly reflective of the diverse population, strengthening the entire ecosystem of public information in Bolivia.
