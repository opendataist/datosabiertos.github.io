La inflación mayorista fue de 18% en enero y los costos de la construcción subieron un 15%, según el Indec

The INDEC report reveals a significant monthly increase in wholesale prices, highlighting persistent inflationary pressures despite a recent deceleration from historical peaks. This data serves as a critical leading indicator for future consumer price trends, offering early signals of economic shifts. Crucially, the divergence between production costs and final consumer inflation underscores the complex transmission of price increases throughout the supply chain. Monitoring these intermediate indices allows analysts to better understand the lagged effects on household budgets and market stability. This information is vital for open data initiatives because it provides transparent, high-quality metrics for independent analysis. Access to such granular economic indicators empowers researchers and citizens to verify official narratives, fostering accountability and informed public discourse regarding Argentina’s inflationary reality.
