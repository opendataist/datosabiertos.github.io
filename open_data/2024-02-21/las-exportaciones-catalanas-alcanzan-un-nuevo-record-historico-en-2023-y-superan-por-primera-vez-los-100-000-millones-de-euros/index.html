Las exportaciones catalanas alcanzan un nuevo récord histórico en 2023 (y superan por primera vez los 100.000 millones de euros)

Catalonia’s export sector demonstrates remarkable resilience and competitiveness, leading Spanish exports and significantly outperforming global, national, and Eurozone trends. This growth is driven by innovative business initiatives and effective government internationalization policies, proving the region’s ability to thrive despite global economic uncertainties. The sustained increase in the number of regular exporting companies highlights a robust and adaptable industrial fabric capable of maintaining momentum. The expansion is broad-based, covering diverse sectors and reaching key international markets, from established European neighbors to emerging economies in the Americas and Asia. This widespread success, coupled with a notable reduction in the trade deficit, underscores a healthy external balance. The decade-long upward trajectory, interrupted only by the pandemic, confirms a structural strength that transcends short-term fluctuations. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparent, high-quality statistical data serves as a critical tool for policy evaluation and strategic planning. Open access to such granular trade and economic metrics allows stakeholders to analyze trends, measure policy impact, and identify challenges. Consequently, the availability of this information supports evidence-based decision-making, fostering transparency and enabling businesses and governments to navigate complex global markets with greater precision and confidence.
