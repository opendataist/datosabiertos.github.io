¿Buscando pega? Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE) ofrece trabajos con sueldos de hasta $1.500.000

¿Buscando pega? Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE) ofrece trabajos con sueldos de hasta $1.500.000

The National Institute of Statistics is launching the 2024 Census, a pivotal moment for generating authoritative demographic data in Chile. This massive undertaking requires significant human resources, prompting the institution to actively recruit staff for various roles across the country to ensure comprehensive data collection and administrative support. The recruitment drive highlights the operational scale needed to maintain accurate national statistics. By offering competitive salaries for diverse positions, the INE aims to secure a capable workforce capable of handling complex fieldwork and data processing tasks essential for the census's success. This initiative is crucial for open data as it establishes the foundational dataset upon which future transparency and public analysis rely. High-quality census data enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to make informed decisions, ensuring that the government’s statistical outputs remain reliable, accessible, and useful for societal development.
