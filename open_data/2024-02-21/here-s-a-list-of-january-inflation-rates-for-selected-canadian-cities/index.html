Here's a list of January inflation rates for selected Canadian cities

Statistics Canada’s January inflation data highlights significant regional variations across Canadian cities. These local metrics provide granular insights into economic conditions, reflecting diverse cost pressures in major urban centers. The agency warns that city-level figures stem from small statistical samples, implying substantial potential for fluctuation. This caveat underscores the importance of cautious interpretation when analyzing localized inflation trends. This report is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the challenges of disseminating granular public statistics. It illustrates how raw data availability must be balanced with clear caveats regarding sample size and reliability for informed public use.
