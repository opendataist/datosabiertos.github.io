ICANN to Shut Down Open Data Platform and API September 1

ICANN is discontinuing its Open Data Platform and API, shifting all public dataset access to static CSV files on its website. This strategic move fundamentally alters how domain-name and internet-governance data is distributed, requiring stakeholders to abandon automated programmatic access in favor of manual downloads. The decision highlights a broader trend where centralized platforms are simplifying data delivery at the cost of real-time interoperability. The transition poses significant challenges for researchers, journalists, and cybersecurity specialists who rely on automated workflows. Organizations must urgently modify their systems to integrate static files, raising concerns about whether these manual methods can match the efficiency and convenience of the previous API. The impact severity depends on dataset update frequencies and the depth of existing integration, potentially introducing friction into critical data analysis processes. This shift is highly relevant to open data practices as it underscores the fragility of API-dependent ecosystems and the importance of clear migration notices. It serves as a cautionary tale for data publishers, demonstrating that moving away from dynamic interfaces requires careful planning to avoid disrupting established research and analytical habits. The case emphasizes the need for sustainable, long-term strategies in data availability to support continuous innovation and transparency in internet governance.
