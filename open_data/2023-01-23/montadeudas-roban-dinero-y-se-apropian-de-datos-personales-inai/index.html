Montadeudas, roban dinero y se apropian de datos personales: INAI

Montadeudas, roban dinero y se apropian de datos personales: INAI

Frauds associated with illegal lending apps, commonly known as “loan sharks,” constitute a serious violation of personal data protection and are criminal offenses that must be reported. Cybercriminals exploit the abundance of private information stored on mobile devices, gaining unauthorized access to contacts, images, and sensitive data through excessive permissions. This invasion enables them to extort victims by threatening to disclose compromising information, highlighting the urgent need to carefully monitor the access rights granted to applications. It is essential that users review privacy notices to understand the purpose of data processing before downloading any software. Legal transparency provides tools to exercise rights such as access, rectification, and deletion of information. By identifying abusive practices or the granting of disproportionate permissions, citizens can make informed decisions and reject applications that seek to collect information beyond what is strictly necessary for their basic functionality. This issue is relevant to the fields of open data and digital governance, as it underscores the importance of transparency in the processing of personal information and the accountability of platforms. Collaboration among data protection authorities, consumer protection agencies, and law enforcement is crucial to creating a secure digital ecosystem. Promoting financial literacy and awareness of the risks associated with sharing data not only empowers citizens but also strengthens the integrity of information systems by demanding clear ethical standards in the collection and use of data by third parties.
