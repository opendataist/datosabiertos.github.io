Estiman que economía creció 2.9 % en 2022

Mexico’s economy grew by 2.9 percent last year, surpassing most analysts’ expectations despite a recent contraction in the final months. This growth remains insufficient to fully recover pre-pandemic levels, indicating that the nation still faces significant economic headwinds. The industrial and service sectors both experienced declines toward the end of the year, reflecting global slowdowns and domestic challenges. Consequently, these sectors have not yet returned to their 2020 baselines, highlighting a fragile recovery trajectory that requires sustained positive momentum in the following year. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how public statistical institutions like INEGI generate critical economic indicators. Transparent, timely access to such official data enables analysts and citizens to accurately assess national performance, fostering informed public discourse and evidence-based policy decisions regarding economic health.
