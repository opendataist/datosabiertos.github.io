El Portal de Transparencia reduce las visitas tras la pandemia pero triplica las de 2019

The Aragon Transparency Portal demonstrates that public interest in government data remains high, even as pandemic-specific queries decrease. Although total visits dropped compared to peak health crisis years, the portal still significantly outperforms pre-pandemic levels. This trend highlights the enduring public demand for transparency, particularly regarding health and administrative data, proving that consistent information release sustains user engagement beyond emergency situations. A key innovation is the integration of accessible formats, such as "Gobierno Fácil," which adapts official documents for easier comprehension. The high download rates of these accessible versions, including questionnaires and statutes, underscore the importance of inclusive design in open government initiatives. By prioritizing readability for people with disabilities, Aragon shows that true transparency requires removing barriers to understanding, thereby enhancing democratic participation and social equity through user-friendly digital tools. Relevance to open data lies in the portal’s technical and geographic performance metrics. With the majority of traffic coming from outside Aragon and a strong preference for mobile access, the data confirms that open government platforms must be highly usable across devices and broadly accessible. The low local usage percentage further suggests that transparent data has national utility, reinforcing the need for interoperable, well-designed public datasets that serve citizens regardless of their geographic location or device type.
