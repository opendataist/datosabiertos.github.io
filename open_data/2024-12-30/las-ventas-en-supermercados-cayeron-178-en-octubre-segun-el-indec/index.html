Las ventas en supermercados cayeron 17,8% en octubre, según el Indec

Las ventas en supermercados cayeron 17,8% en octubre, según el Indec

The report highlights a significant real decline in supermarket sales, indicating that despite nominal price increases, actual purchasing power and consumer demand have sharply contracted. This trend underscores the severe economic challenges facing households, as volume losses outpace inflationary adjustments across major product categories. Payment methods reveal a heavy reliance on credit and digital options, reflecting immediate liquidity constraints among consumers. The shift away from cash toward credit instruments signals a coping strategy rather than organic growth, further illustrating the fragile nature of current retail dynamics amidst high inflation. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it exposes the gap between reported monetary values and real economic activity. Transparent, accessible statistics allow analysts and citizens to discern true consumer behavior from inflationary noise, fostering better policy understanding and economic transparency.
