La IA y el reto de regular los derechos de autor en la música

Artificial intelligence enables the revival of deceased artists' voices, creating new music without their direct consent. This innovation sparks intense debate regarding copyright ownership, as traditional legal frameworks struggle to protect intellectual property in the digital age. The core issue involves balancing technological advancement with ethical rights, particularly concerning the commercial use of legacy artists' likenesses. Major musicians express frustration when their digital personas are exploited, highlighting a gap between existing laws and AI capabilities. This article is vital for open data because it underscores the need for transparent, accessible legal precedents. Open data initiatives must track these evolving copyright disputes to foster equitable policies that protect creators while encouraging innovation in generative media technologies.
