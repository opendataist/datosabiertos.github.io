Adelantan que la inflación de diciembre se ubicaría por debajo del 3%

Private estimates forecast December inflation at around two percent, with food prices showing moderation. The official data will be released by the national statistics institute, enabling verification of these independent projections against actual economic trends. This information is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of public statistical agencies in validating private forecasts. Access to the forthcoming official dataset promotes transparency, allowing citizens and analysts to compare private predictions with authoritative public records, thereby fostering trust in economic indicators.
