¿Una mujer presidenta en México? Dos de cada tres coahuilenses lo respaldan

The INEGI’s survey on discrimination reveals widespread societal support for electing a female president, highlighting a shift toward gender inclusion in high political offices. This data underscores the critical role of official statistics in measuring and validating social attitudes regarding vulnerable groups, directly informing open data initiatives on equality. Residents of Coahuila align closely with the national average, demonstrating consistent regional progress in accepting diverse leadership. By making this granular survey data publicly accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand demographic trends and address systemic biases through evidence-based analysis. Ultimately, the publication of these findings illustrates how open data facilitates transparency in societal values. It allows citizens to engage with real-time information on discrimination, fostering a more informed dialogue about democratic representation and ensuring that statistical insights directly contribute to broader social change.
