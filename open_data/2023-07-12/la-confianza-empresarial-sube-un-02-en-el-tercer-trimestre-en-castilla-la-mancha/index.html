La confianza empresarial sube un 0,2% en el tercer trimestre en Castilla-La Mancha

The Enterprise Confidence Harmonized Indicator (ICEA) has shown a sustained recovery, marking three consecutive quarters of growth driven primarily by improved business expectations. This upward trend, which has not been observed since late 2021, reflects a significant shift in sentiment where optimistic views on future performance have reached their highest levels in years, while pessimistic forecasts have noticeably declined. This renewed confidence is broad-based, impacting all economic sectors, company sizes, and autonomous communities across the country. Transport and hospitality, along with construction, have led this resurgence, demonstrating that the recovery is not limited to isolated pockets but represents a general improvement in the business environment. Even in regions with more modest gains, such as Castilla-La Mancha, the overall trajectory remains positive, signaling a stabilization of the economic climate. This data is crucial for the open_data community as it exemplifies the value of standardized, high-frequency economic indicators in fostering transparency and informed decision-making. By adhering to international methodological standards comparable across Europe, the ICEA provides a reliable public resource for researchers, policymakers, and analysts to monitor real-time economic health. Its accessibility allows for deeper analysis of regional disparities and sectoral trends, reinforcing the importance of open, comparable data in understanding complex socioeconomic dynamics.
