La crianza de un niño costó hasta $542.000 en agosto, según el Indec

La crianza de un niño costó hasta $542.000 en agosto, según el Indec

The INDEC’s new child-rearing basket reveals that costs vary significantly by age, with older children requiring substantially more resources than infants. This metric distinguishes between tangible goods and essential care services, highlighting that childcare expenses constitute a major portion of the total burden on families. Crucially, these increases remain lower than the general consumer price index, indicating that while rising, the specific cost of raising children has not outpaced overall inflation recently. This distinction helps policymakers understand the specific economic pressure on households compared to broader market trends. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular insights into family economics. By making detailed cost structures public, it enables researchers and citizens to advocate for better social policies and understand the true impact of economic indicators on daily life and child development.
