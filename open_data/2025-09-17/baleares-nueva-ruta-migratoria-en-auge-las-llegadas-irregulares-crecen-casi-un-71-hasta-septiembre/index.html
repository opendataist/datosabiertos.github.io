Baleares, nueva ruta migratoria en auge: las llegadas irregulares crecen casi un 71 % hasta septiembre

Baleares, nueva ruta migratoria en auge: las llegadas irregulares crecen casi un 71 % hasta septiembre

The report highlights divergent migration trends within Spain, contrasting a significant rise in irregular arrivals in the Balearic Islands with a sharp decline in the Canary Islands and the mainland. While overall national numbers decreased, specific border territories like Ceuta and Melilla saw increased land-based entries. This data reveals how migration pressures shift geographically rather than uniformly decreasing across the country. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity of granular, disaggregated statistics. Public administration transparency requires breaking down national totals into specific regions like the Balearics or specific borders like Ceuta. Such detailed, accessible datasets allow researchers and citizens to accurately analyze complex socio-political dynamics, moving beyond simplistic national aggregates to understand localized challenges and policy impacts.
