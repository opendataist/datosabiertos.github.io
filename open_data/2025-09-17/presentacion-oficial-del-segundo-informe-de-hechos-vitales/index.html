Presentación oficial del segundo Informe de Hechos Vitales

Presentación oficial del segundo Informe de Hechos Vitales

INEGE released a comprehensive demographic report on births, deaths, marriages, and divorces in Equatorial Guinea, highlighting its value as a critical tool for understanding the nation’s sociodemographic dynamics. This publication underscores the necessity of accessible statistical data for policymakers, researchers, and civil society to effectively address social issues and plan future development strategies. Key findings reveal high registration coverage and detailed insights into health and family structures, such as significant rates of cesarean sections in private clinics and demographic shifts among new mothers. The data also exposes challenges in completing full coverage for marriages and divorces, while providing essential information on mortality causes like HIV and malaria, which are vital for public health interventions. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, structured statistical reporting empowers societal analysis. By making detailed demographic information publicly available, institutions like INEGE foster evidence-based decision-making and promote transparency, allowing diverse actors to engage with reliable facts that shape national policy and social understanding.
