Vélez vs Racing: megaoperativo anti infiltrados con cruce de padrones y dura sanción

Vélez Sarsfield implemented strict security measures to prevent Racing fans from entering the stadium for a Copa Libertadores match. By cross-referencing DNI (national identity document) lists with facial recognition technology, authorities aim to enforce the “no visiting public” rule, penalizing anyone found in possession of banned tickets. This operational design highlights how digital identity verification and data matching are essential for maintaining order at large-scale public events, illustrating the critical role of integrated data systems in modern security protocols. The reliance on real-time biometric checks and database interoperability demonstrates the practical application of open data principles in public administration. When agencies share and verify citizen information efficiently, they can enhance safety while respecting legal boundaries.
