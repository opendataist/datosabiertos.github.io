Venta de autos en Perú creció 6,6% en julio 2025 y superó al sector comercio, según INEI

Venta de autos en Perú creció 6,6% en julio 2025 y superó al sector comercio, según INEI

The Peruvian automotive sector has emerged as the primary engine of national economic growth, significantly outpacing general commerce trends. This dynamism is driven by robust demand for both new and used vehicles, fueled by improved employment conditions and accessible financing. The market’s resilience is evident in its ability to stimulate related industries, including maintenance, parts, and repairs, thereby creating a comprehensive value chain that supports broader economic stability. This expansion extends beyond major urban centers, particularly through the rise of the two-wheeler market, which enhances mobility and inclusivity in peripheral regions. The surge in motorcycle sales and associated services demonstrates how vehicle accessibility directly impacts local commerce and social development. By facilitating trade and movement for underserved populations, the automotive industry acts as a vital catalyst for equitable economic participation across the country. For open data initiatives, this report highlights the critical importance of integrating diverse datasets—such as sales figures, financial lending metrics, and regional mobility statistics—to understand complex economic ecosystems. Transparent access to information on vehicle transactions and employment trends allows researchers and policymakers to verify claims of inclusive growth. Furthermore, monitoring sub-segments like the used car market reveals nuanced consumption patterns that aggregate data might obscure, emphasizing the need for granular, interoperable public data to accurately assess economic health and policy effectiveness.
