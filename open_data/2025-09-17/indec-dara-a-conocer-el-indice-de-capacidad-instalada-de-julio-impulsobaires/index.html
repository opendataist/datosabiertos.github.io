INDEC dará a conocer el Índice de Capacidad Instalada de julio - ImpulsoBaires

INDEC dará a conocer el Índice de Capacidad Instalada de julio - ImpulsoBaires

The article explains that the INDEC’s Index of Installed Capacity measures industrial activity levels by assessing the proportion of production capacity currently utilized by a sample of manufacturing firms. This metric helps determine whether industry is operating at full potential or experiencing idle capacity, offering crucial signals for economic diagnosis. It highlights that this indicator guides public policy by informing adjustments to incentives, subsidies, and regulations based on actual activity levels. Furthermore, it enables sectoral analysis to identify active industries and supports employment projections, serving as a vital tool for understanding broader economic recovery or alert signals. This data is relevant to open data because it provides transparent, standardized statistical inputs for economic analysis. By making such metrics publicly available, researchers and policymakers can access reliable information to evaluate industrial health, ensuring evidence-based decision-making and fostering greater accountability in government economic strategies.
