Sunarp apertura nueva oficina en Ayna San Francisco

Sunarp apertura nueva oficina en Ayna San Francisco

The National Superintendence of Public Registries (SUNARP) has inaugurated a new registry office in Peru’s VRAEM region to decentralize services and enhance legal certainty for citizens. This expansion enables residents to access property and legal registration more quickly, ensuring that bureaucratic procedures are closer to those who need them most. Community organizations have benefited immediately by registering their constitutions, which strengthens their capacity to manage resources and effectively represent local interests. This support fosters greater social and economic development by empowering grassroots groups with the formal status required to access public programs. This initiative underscores the importance of open data by making public registry information more accessible and transparent. By reducing barriers to registration, the state promotes accountability and enables better use of data for community development, reinforcing the link between digital infrastructure and the quality of public services.
