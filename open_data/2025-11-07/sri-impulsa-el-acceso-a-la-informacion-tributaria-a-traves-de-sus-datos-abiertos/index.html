SRI impulsa el acceso a la información tributaria a través de sus Datos Abiertos

SRI impulsa el acceso a la información tributaria a través de sus Datos Abiertos

The Internal Revenue Service has launched an open data initiative to significantly enhance transparency and citizen access to tax information. By providing free, unrestricted access to key datasets in compatible formats, this platform empowers researchers, journalists, and the public to analyze fiscal behavior without bureaucratic hurdles. This move shifts control from opaque administrative silos to an accountable, publicly auditable space. Key datasets include anonymized vehicle records, electronic invoicing trends, and registries of non-existent entities, which are crucial for combating fraud and understanding economic shifts. Citizens can now examine sector-specific sales, tax collection patterns, and fiscal pressure indicators to perform independent comparative studies. These resources enable detailed geographic and temporal analysis of commercial dynamics, revealing how digitalization impacts the national tax system. This article is vital to open data because it demonstrates how government agencies can leverage structured, machine-readable information to foster civic engagement and economic integrity. It serves as a model for how public financial data, when democratized, supports accountability and informed decision-making. By removing access barriers, the SRI illustrates the practical power of open standards in promoting trust and enabling society to actively participate in monitoring public resource management.
