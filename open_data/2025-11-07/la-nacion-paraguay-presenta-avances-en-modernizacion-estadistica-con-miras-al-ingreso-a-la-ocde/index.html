La Nación / Paraguay presenta avances en modernización estadística con miras al ingreso a la OCDE

Paraguay has significantly modernized its National Statistical System through strategic collaboration with PARIS21, achieving key institutional reforms that include new legislation and a comprehensive national statistical strategy. These improvements are critical for the country’s ongoing accession process to the OECD, demonstrating alignment with international standards in data governance and statistical production. The reforms have yielded measurable improvements in data quality and transparency. Paraguay has surpassed regional averages in statistical performance indices and achieved notable progress in open data rankings. This shift underscores a stronger commitment to transparency, enabling better access to information and fostering greater public trust in official statistics through rigorous methodological upgrades. This development is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates how technical assistance can drive systemic changes that enhance data availability and interoperability. By establishing platforms that connect public institutions with donors and partners, Paraguay is creating sustainable infrastructure for open data sharing. This model highlights the importance of robust institutional frameworks and international cooperation in advancing global open data objectives and improving evidence-based policymaking.
