Bolivia alcanza 2,9 % de desocupación urbana: hay trabajo, pero de baja calidad y alta vulnerabilidad

Bolivia alcanza 2,9 % de desocupación urbana: hay trabajo, pero de baja calidad y alta vulnerabilidad

Although Bolivia reports a low unemployment rate, suggesting a healthy labor market, this positive indicator masks a deep structural issue: high labor informality. The majority of workers operate without formal contracts, social benefits, or job security, meaning most employment is characterized by significant vulnerability rather than stability. The economic reliance on informal businesses highlights a dual reality where formal jobs remain scarce despite adequate aggregate employment figures. This disparity reveals that while people have work, the lack of institutional protections prevents true economic security, perpetuating a cycle of precarious labor conditions across various sectors. This article is crucial for open data because it demonstrates the necessity of cross-referencing distinct datasets—such as unemployment statistics and informality rates—to reveal the truth behind surface-level metrics. Open data initiatives must integrate these varied sources to expose hidden inequalities, such as gender gaps and youth exclusion, thereby enabling more accurate policy analysis and effective social interventions.
