Coalition Slams Labor's Secretive FOI Power Grab

Coalition Slams Labor's Secretive FOI Power Grab

The Coalition condemns the current government for advancing the Freedom of Information Amendment Bill through rushed parliamentary processes that suppress debate and eliminate scrutiny. By dismissing proposed amendments and silencing opposition, the administration is accused of prioritizing political control over democratic integrity. This approach contradicts previous promises of transparency, instead establishing a precedent where secrecy is normalized and governmental accountability is systematically undermined. The proposed reforms would impose fees on citizens seeking government information and significantly expand cabinet secrecy. By narrowing the definition of official records and allowing agencies to reject requests as frivolous, the legislation threatens to reduce public access to critical data. These changes effectively create financial and bureaucratic barriers, leading to fewer disclosures and weakened oversight, which critics describe as a retreat from democratic openness rather than necessary modernization. This issue is crucial to open data because it directly threatens the fundamental principle that government information belongs to the public. Restricting access to documents and delaying processing times hinders the ability of journalists, researchers, and citizens to monitor decision-making processes. Protecting open data frameworks is essential for ensuring that institutions remain transparent and answerable to the people, preventing the concentration of knowledge within political elites and maintaining the mechanisms for true public accountability.
