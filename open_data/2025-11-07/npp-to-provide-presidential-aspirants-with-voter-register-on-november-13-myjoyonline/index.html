NPP to provide presidential aspirants with voter register on November 13 - MyJoyOnline

NPP to provide presidential aspirants with voter register on November 13 - MyJoyOnline

The New Patriotic Party has concluded its nationwide voter registration verification, releasing the provisional register to all five presidential aspirants to ensure transparency in the upcoming 2026 primaries. This decisive step initiates a critical two-week feedback window, allowing candidates to scrutinize the delegate list and submit observations before the final electoral album is certified. By prioritizing open access to data, the party aims to uphold institutional discipline and eliminate disputes, ensuring the legitimacy of the democratic process within the organization. The verification exercise confirmed the overall integrity of the register, with minor administrative errors being rectified to meet strict party constitutional requirements. Delegates who had forfeited membership or passed away were removed, demonstrating a commitment to accurate and compliant electoral rolls. This rigorous attention to detail underscores the importance of reliable data in facilitating a fair competition, where every vote is based on a verified and up-to-date roster of eligible participants. This process is relevant to open data as it highlights the value of accessible, verified, and transparent information in democratic governance. By making the provisional voter register available to all stakeholders, the NPP demonstrates how open data can build trust, reduce fraud, and empower participants to engage effectively in the electoral system. This approach sets a precedent for how political entities can leverage transparency to strengthen democratic institutions and ensure accountability.
