Ombudsperson Hails Ruling Protecting Right to Information - Topical Issue - Rádio RSI English - STVR

Ombudsperson Hails Ruling Protecting Right to Information - Topical Issue - Rádio RSI English - STVR

Slovakia’s Constitutional Court struck down fee provisions for public information requests, affirming that transparency is a democratic cornerstone. This ruling validates the President’s veto and eliminates barriers that previously hindered equal access to data. The decision underscores how administrative costs can restrict citizen engagement with government records. By invalidating vague fee structures, the court ensures that financial hurdles do not obstruct the public’s right to know, reinforcing accountability. This is vital for open data advocacy, as it confirms that access to public information must remain free. It sets a legal precedent that protects data transparency against state-imposed financial barriers, ensuring equitable access for all citizens.
