Aviso de Privacidad Integral

Aviso de Privacidad Integral

This privacy notice details how *Periódico Especializado en Economía y Finanzas* processes personal data under the Federal Law for the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties. The core conclusion is that user consent is explicitly granted for a broad spectrum of commercial and administrative activities. The company collects comprehensive identifiers, including sensitive audio-visual data, to manage subscriptions, marketing, loyalty programs, and contractual obligations. The article is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the tension between proprietary data usage and individual rights. It emphasizes that data is not open by default but is strictly controlled, with access limited to internal personnel or authorized third parties like educational institutions. Transparency is maintained through ARCO rights, allowing individuals to access, rectify, cancel, or oppose the use of their information, ensuring that personal data remains protected rather than freely available for public consumption or analysis. Ultimately, the document underscores the necessity of clear consent mechanisms and security measures in data handling. It mandates that once data is no longer necessary for its stated purposes, it must be deleted or blocked. This serves as a critical example of how private entities must balance operational needs with legal obligations, demonstrating that even in media and finance sectors, personal data remains a regulated asset rather than an open resource, requiring rigorous adherence to privacy laws and user autonomy.
