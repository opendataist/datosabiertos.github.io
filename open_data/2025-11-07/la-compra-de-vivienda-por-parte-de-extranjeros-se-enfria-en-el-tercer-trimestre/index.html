La compra de vivienda por parte de extranjeros se enfría en el tercer trimestre

Foreign investment in Spanish real estate has experienced a slight decline, although European buyers continue to dominate the market. This trend underscores a shift in which international resident purchasers are spreading transactions across a greater number of provinces, thereby challenging traditional geographic concentrations. The data indicate that tourism intensity remains the primary driver of foreign acquisitions, particularly in coastal and island regions. As a result, prices vary significantly by investor origin, with North American investors paying premium rates compared to other global groups. This report is crucial for open_data because it demonstrates how granular, publicly available registries can uncover complex socioeconomic patterns. Access to such standardized metadata enables researchers to monitor market dynamics, migration influences, and economic health without relying on proprietary datasets, thereby promoting greater transparency and analytical depth in real estate research.
