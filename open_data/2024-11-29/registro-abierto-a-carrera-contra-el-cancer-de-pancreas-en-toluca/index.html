Registro abierto a carrera contra el cáncer de páncreas en Toluca

The article highlights the rising mortality rates from cancer in the Americas, with pancreatic cancer being a particularly deadly form. It emphasizes that a significant proportion of these cases are preventable by addressing key lifestyle risk factors such as smoking, excessive alcohol consumption, poor diet, and physical inactivity. This underscores the critical role of public health data in guiding prevention strategies and policy decisions. To raise awareness and promote physical activity as a preventive measure, an annual race against pancreatic cancer is organized. This event serves to sensitize the public about the disease’s aggressive nature and encourages healthy behaviors. The initiative stems from personal tragedy, aiming to transform grief into action by fostering community engagement through sports. This content is relevant to open data because it illustrates how statistical health information, when made accessible and transparent, empowers civil society and organizations to organize impactful interventions. Open access to epidemiological trends enables non-governmental entities to design targeted awareness campaigns, bridging the gap between official health statistics and community-level action.
