La firma de hipotecas vuelve a tasas positivas en la Región de Murcia con un crecimiento del 31% en septiembre

The article reports a significant surge in residential mortgages in the Region of Murcia, highlighting a robust recovery in the real estate sector. This growth indicates renewed confidence among buyers and lenders, reflecting broader economic stabilization trends within the region. The data reveals a substantial increase in loan volumes and the amount of capital disbursed. By showing that local performance mirrors national upward trajectories, it demonstrates that regional housing markets are aligning with broader national economic recoveries and increased lending activity.
