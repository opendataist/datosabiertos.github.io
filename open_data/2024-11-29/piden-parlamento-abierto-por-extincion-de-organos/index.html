Piden parlamento abierto por extinción de órganos

Piden parlamento abierto por extinción de órganos

Adrián Alcalá urged the Senate to convene an open parliamentary session to determine the future of the INAI and other autonomous bodies. He argued that public institutions must embrace open dialogue to address common challenges and effectively enhance the quality of public services. Commissioner Ibarra Cadena emphasized that transparency is the foundation of open governance. She highlighted the importance of public scrutiny and a clear understanding of judicial criteria to ensure laws are applied correctly, thereby fostering trust in democratic processes. This issue is crucial for open data, as the INAI safeguards citizens’ right to access government information. Its potential abolition threatens the legal framework that guarantees public transparency, directly affecting data availability and accountability in Mexico.
