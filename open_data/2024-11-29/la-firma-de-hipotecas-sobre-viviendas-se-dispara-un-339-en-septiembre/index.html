La firma de hipotecas sobre viviendas se dispara un 33,9% en septiembre

The mortgage sector is experiencing robust growth, characterized by a significant increase in new contracts and rising loan volumes. This trend signals renewed consumer confidence and activity in the housing market, reflecting a shift in financial behavior in which fixed-rate mortgages are gaining prominence over variable-rate ones. At the same time, the structure of existing loans is evolving. While modifications to interest terms are on the rise, transfers of ownership and changes of lender are declining sharply. This indicates that borrowers are prioritizing refinancing and rate adjustments rather than selling properties or switching banks, highlighting a stabilization phase in the secondary mortgage market. This data is relevant to open_data because it represents high-value, transparent statistical outputs from national institutes. Making such granular real estate metrics accessible fosters economic transparency, enabling researchers and citizens to analyze market trends and housing affordability more effectively.
