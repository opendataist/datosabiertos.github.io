INE presentará resultados finales del Censo 2023 el próximo 10 diciembre

The National Institute of Statistics of Uruguay will soon release the complete results of the 2023 census, marking a milestone in public management. This event, aligned with the anniversary of the law governing the National Statistical System, underscores the importance of transparency and accountability. Official statistics are positioned as fundamental tools for strengthening democracy and fostering social trust, demonstrating that institutional rigor is key to effective and democratic governance. The data reveal an unprecedented demographic transition in the country, characterized by negative natural population growth, where deaths exceed births. This trend suggests a potential population decline and accelerated aging, necessitating urgent adjustments in health and social security policies. The information not only describes the current reality but also serves as a critical input for the strategic planning of the future government and the adaptation of public services to a new demographic structure. This publication is relevant to the field of open data, as it demonstrates how statistical transparency facilitates informed decision-making. By making detailed information available on geographic distribution, household size, and access to services, the State enables the creation of evidence-based innovative solutions. This allows civil society and the private sector to analyze structural gaps and propose concrete responses, enhancing the social impact of public data by bridging the gap between information collection and fact-based policy action.
