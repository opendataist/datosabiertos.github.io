Northern Ireland local authority collected municipal waste management statistics released

Northern Ireland’s latest waste statistics reveal a consistent upward trend in recycling rates and a significant decline in landfill usage. This shift underscores a successful transition toward more sustainable waste management practices across local councils, prioritizing resource recovery over disposal. The data highlights substantial reductions in biodegradable waste sent to landfills and increased energy recovery efforts. These improvements demonstrate effective policy implementation and operational efficiency, signaling progress toward long-term environmental goals in municipal waste handling. This report is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, longitudinal metrics on public service performance. By making such detailed statistics accessible, it enables better accountability, facilitates comparative analysis, and supports evidence-based decision-making for future environmental policy and infrastructure planning.
