El fiscal general del Estado pide anular que registren su despacho por "manifiesta y notoria desproporción"

The state argues that searching the Attorney General’s office constituted a disproportionate violation of institutional independence. This legal challenge highlights the tension between judicial investigations and the protection of prosecutorial secrecy. Such aggressive measures risk undermining public trust in the integrity of state institutions. The potential for irreparable harm underscores the need for strict proportionality in legal procedures involving high-ranking officials. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical boundary between transparency and protected state secrecy. It demonstrates how access to information can be legally contested when it involves sensitive internal communications.
