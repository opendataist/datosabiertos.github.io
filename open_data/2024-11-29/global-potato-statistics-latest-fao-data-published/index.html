Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The article highlights how the Food and Agriculture Organization maintains global agricultural records, specifically detailing world potato production metrics. It notes that recent database updates now include more current years, enhancing the utility of this information for ongoing analysis. By extracting production totals for numerous countries, the text underscores the significant volume of global harvests and identifies key producing nations. This centralized data repository serves as a primary source for understanding agricultural trends and food security issues on an international scale. Open data initiatives rely heavily on such standardized, accessible datasets to ensure transparency and comparability across different regions. When institutions like the FAO make their statistics publicly available through platforms like FAOSTAT, they enable researchers, policymakers, and developers to perform independent verification and deeper exploration. This accessibility allows users to filter, sort, and analyze the information according to their specific needs, fostering a more democratic approach to data usage. It transforms raw numbers into actionable insights for various sectors. The relevance to open data lies in the potential for external parties to build upon these free resources. Researchers can utilize this information to model supply chains, assess economic impacts, or track changes in agricultural efficiency over time. By providing a clear pathway for public access, the FAO supports a culture of shared knowledge. This encourages collaboration and innovation, as individuals can combine this dataset with other open sources to generate novel solutions for global challenges, demonstrating the power of transparent government and international organization data.
