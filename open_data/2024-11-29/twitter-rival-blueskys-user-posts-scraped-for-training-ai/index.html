Twitter rival Bluesky’s user posts scraped for training AI

Twitter rival Bluesky’s user posts scraped for training AI

Publicly available social media data can be easily scraped and repurposed for AI training, highlighting a critical gap in user consent enforcement. This incident demonstrates that decentralization and open access inherently challenge traditional data privacy controls, as platforms struggle to restrict third-party extraction. The controversy underscores the urgent need for robust technical mechanisms to protect user data in open ecosystems. Without effective barriers, individual preferences for data usage can be easily overridden by external developers seeking unrestricted access. This situation is vital for open data discussions, revealing the tension between transparency and privacy. It challenges the community to develop solutions that respect user agency while maintaining the benefits of open, accessible information systems.
