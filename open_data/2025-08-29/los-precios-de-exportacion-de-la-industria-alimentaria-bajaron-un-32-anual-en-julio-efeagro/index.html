Los precios de exportación de la industria alimentaria bajaron un 3,2 % anual en julio - EFEAgro

Export prices in the Spanish food industry declined on an annual basis, while import prices increased, reflecting shifting global cost dynamics. The beverage and tobacco sectors exhibited divergent trends, with tobacco exports commanding higher rates. This data underscores how open data provides essential transparency into economic trends, enabling stakeholders to analyze trade balances and adjust strategies based on real-time statistical insights for more informed decision-making.
