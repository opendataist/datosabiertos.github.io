Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

The provided document is a standard Privacy Notice from 13 MEDIA, S.C., designed to comply with federal data protection laws. It details the personal information collected, such as identity and financial data, and explains that this information is used primarily to verify identities, meet legal obligations, and provide requested services. The notice emphasizes that data collection adheres to principles of legality, consent, and proportionality, ensuring that the company handles information responsibly and transparently. Regarding data sharing, the text clarifies that personal information is generally restricted to internal staff but may be transferred to third parties like notaries or lawyers for legal formalizations. Users retain the right to limit the use or disclosure of their data by contacting specific privacy officers, although this limitation does not apply when legal compliance or authority requests require disclosure. This establishes a clear framework for user control while maintaining necessary legal channels. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the fundamental tension between privacy compliance and data transparency. While open data initiatives advocate for the release of information for public benefit, this notice demonstrates how private entities must navigate strict regulatory boundaries to protect individual rights. It serves as a reminder that even in an open data ecosystem, organizations must clearly define what data is collected, how it is protected, and the specific legal constraints that prevent certain information from being fully open or shared without explicit consent.
