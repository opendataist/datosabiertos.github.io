Ley de Transparencia Social fue publicada en el Registro Oficial

Ley de Transparencia Social fue publicada en el Registro Oficial

This article is relevant to open data because it establishes a mandatory public digital platform for non-profit organizations, centralizing their legal and financial records. By requiring transparent reporting of operations and compliance, the law transforms private organizational data into a publicly accessible resource, enhancing accountability and enabling external scrutiny of civil society activities. The primary goal is to prevent criminal organizations from exploiting non-profits for money laundering and illicit financing. This regulatory framework aims to safeguard financial integrity by ensuring that all entities in the social sector maintain verifiable and traceable records, thereby protecting the justice system and fiscal stability from irregular capital flows. Ultimately, the legislation mandates strict oversight through designated government agencies, creating a unified information system. This shift ensures that data concerning non-profit entities is not only regulated but also made available in a structured format, significantly contributing to the open data ecosystem by promoting transparency and trust in the governance of the social sector.
