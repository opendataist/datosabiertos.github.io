Bolivia tiene 11 millones 365 mil 333 habitantes, según censo - Noticias Prensa Latina

Bolivia tiene 11 millones 365 mil 333 habitantes, según censo - Noticias Prensa Latina

The article highlights significant improvements in Bolivia’s education and healthcare sectors, revealing that school attendance rates have surged for all age groups, particularly among younger children. Furthermore, while men previously held an advantage in higher education, women now outpace them, and the educational gap between rural and urban areas has notably narrowed. These trends indicate a more equitable distribution of learning opportunities across different demographics and geographic regions. Regarding public health, the data shows a dramatic increase in the number of mothers receiving qualified professional assistance during childbirth, especially in previously underserved rural communities. This progress in maternal and child care demonstrates a substantial commitment to improving health outcomes for vulnerable populations. The collective results suggest that national policies have effectively targeted historical disparities in both essential services. This report is vital to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the power of transparent, longitudinal government statistics to track societal progress. By making such detailed census data accessible, citizens and researchers can hold institutions accountable and verify claims of improvement. Open access to this information fosters evidence-based policymaking, allowing stakeholders to understand the real-world impact of public investments and advocate for continued equity in social services.
