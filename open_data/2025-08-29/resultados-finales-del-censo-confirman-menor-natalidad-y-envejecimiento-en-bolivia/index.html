Resultados finales del Censo confirman menor natalidad y envejecimiento en Bolivia

Resultados finales del Censo confirman menor natalidad y envejecimiento en Bolivia

The 2024 Census reveals a profound demographic transition in Bolivia, characterized by declining birth rates and population aging. This shift alters the traditional population pyramid, reducing the proportion of children while significantly increasing the working-age demographic. Consequently, the country is currently experiencing a "demographic dividend," offering a critical window to leverage this larger labor force for sustained economic growth before the population structure changes further. Simultaneously, the rising share of adults over 65 highlights an urgent future challenge regarding elderly care and social support systems. As the population ages, the burden on social infrastructure will intensify, requiring proactive policy planning. This trend, combined with fewer young dependents, signals that Bolivia must now balance the immediate economic opportunities of its working-age majority with long-term strategies to manage the impending needs of an older citizenry. These findings are vital to open data as they underscore the necessity of accessible, high-quality demographic datasets for evidence-based governance. Transparent statistical information allows policymakers, researchers, and civil society to monitor social trends and evaluate the effectiveness of public interventions. Reliable data ensures that decisions regarding economic planning and social welfare are grounded in reality, fostering accountability and sustainable development in response to these structural societal changes.
