£3bn worth of small parcels shipped to UK from China amid duty rules review

£3bn worth of small parcels shipped to UK from China amid duty rules review

The UK government is reconsidering a tax exemption for low-value parcels, driven by a surge in cheap imports from Chinese retailers like Shein and Temu. This policy has drawn criticism for allowing foreign e-commerce giants to undercut domestic businesses that bear significant tax burdens. Reflecting a global trend, the US and EU have already moved to eliminate similar duty-free thresholds. The UK’s review aims to address unfair competition and protect local high street retailers from being overwhelmed by untaxed foreign goods. This shift is crucial for open data, as it highlights the need for transparent customs statistics. Accurate tracking of small parcel flows reveals hidden economic impacts and informs policy decisions regarding fair trade and digital commerce regulation.
