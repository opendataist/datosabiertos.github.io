Jóvenes que desconectan de las redes sociales: «Mi vida no ha cambiado porque Instagram no es la realidad»

Jóvenes que desconectan de las redes sociales: «Mi vida no ha cambiado porque Instagram no es la realidad»

The article highlights a growing trend among young people who voluntarily disconnect from social media platforms like Instagram to prioritize their mental well-being and reclaim their time. By stepping away from the constant pressure to curate a "perfect" life and combat the anxiety induced by fear of missing out and algorithmic manipulation, these individuals report experiencing greater authenticity in their relationships and a more grounded perception of reality. This shift underscores the importance of recognizing digital tools as utilities for connection rather than central hubs for leisure, advocating for a healthier, more regulated relationship with technology. Relevant to open data, this cultural shift represents a significant change in how users interact with digital platforms, challenging the assumption of constant, voluntary engagement. It suggests that metrics relying on active daily usage may not capture the full picture of user sentiment or the potential churn caused by digital fatigue. Understanding these motivations is crucial for data scientists and policymakers aiming to model user behavior accurately, as the drive for "digital well-being" may lead to sustainable decreases in active participation or changes in how data is consented to and shared. Furthermore, the increasing awareness regarding privacy, security, and the psychological impact of algorithmic bias calls for greater transparency in how social media companies collect and process personal data. As young users become more critical of manipulative strategies, there is a heightened demand for ethical data practices and clear boundaries regarding content curation. This evolution pushes for open data initiatives that not only provide access to information but also empower users to understand and control their digital footprint, fostering a more equitable and user-centric digital ecosystem.
