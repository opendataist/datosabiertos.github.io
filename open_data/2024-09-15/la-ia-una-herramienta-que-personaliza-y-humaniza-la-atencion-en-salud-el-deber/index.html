La IA, una herramienta que personaliza y humaniza la atención en salud | El Deber

La IA, una herramienta que personaliza y humaniza la atención en salud | El Deber

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into medicine goes beyond technical efficiency to prioritize the humanization of patient care. By translating complex information into accessible language, AI reduces stress for family members and facilitates processes such as informed consent. This approach enables healthcare professionals to focus on clinical care, ensuring that technology serves as a supportive tool that complements, rather than replaces, human empathy and judgment. In the educational sphere, the implementation of these technologies requires a balance between innovation and ethical responsibility. It is essential that institutions train future physicians not only in the use of digital tools but also in the private and ethical handling of information. This addresses the need to prepare the next generation of professionals to navigate an environment where technical precision must go hand in hand with integrity in the handling of sensitive data. This article is relevant to the open data movement because it underscores the critical importance of transparency, ethics, and responsible information management in sensitive sectors such as healthcare. The effectiveness of virtual assistants and predictive models depends on the quality and accessibility of the underlying data. Promoting clear frameworks for the ethical use of information and system interoperability is essential to ensure that the digitalization of healthcare improves the patient experience without compromising data security or privacy.
