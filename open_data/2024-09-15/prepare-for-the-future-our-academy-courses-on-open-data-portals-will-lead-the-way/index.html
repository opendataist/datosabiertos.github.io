Prepare for the future: Our Academy courses on open data portals will lead the way

The data.europa Academy offers specialized courses to help data professionals master open data landscapes, emphasizing the strategic role of data spaces in enabling cross-sector sharing. These programs focus on understanding user needs and ensuring sustainable portal infrastructures, which are critical for driving societal and economic value through effective data governance. Additionally, training addresses the imperative of high-quality metadata and data cleaning, while introducing emerging concepts like citizen-generated data. By bridging theoretical knowledge with practical application, these resources empower experts to navigate complex challenges and leverage open data for tangible research and operational outcomes. This initiative is vital for the open data ecosystem as it standardizes skills and promotes interoperability. By enhancing professional competence, the Academy supports the broader goal of making public data more accessible, usable, and impactful across the European Union.
