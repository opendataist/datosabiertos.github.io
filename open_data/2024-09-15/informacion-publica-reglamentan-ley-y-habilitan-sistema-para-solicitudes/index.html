Información Pública: reglamentan ley y habilitan sistema para solicitudes

Información Pública: reglamentan ley y habilitan sistema para solicitudes

The provincial government of La Pampa has significantly strengthened citizens’ right to access public information by launching a new online request system. This digital tool simplifies the process for individuals and legal entities to obtain transparent, accurate, and timely data from state organs. By integrating this system with existing physical and telephone channels, the administration aims to remove bureaucratic barriers, ensuring that the exercise of this fundamental democratic right is easy, accessible, and completely free for the public. This initiative reflects a broader political commitment to institutional oversight and good governance, viewing transparency as a vital ally against corruption and inefficiency. The move aligns with the recent regulation of the provincial Access to Public Information Law, which establishes passive transparency mechanisms for responding to specific inquiries. Furthermore, it reinforces active transparency requirements, mandating that state agencies proactively publish structured, understandable data on their websites, including budgets, organizational structures, and procurement details, before any specific request is made. This development is highly relevant to open data because it operationalizes the legal framework necessary for data usability and accountability. By mandating both reactive information sharing and proactive publication of key datasets, the system creates a foundation for data-driven civic oversight. It demonstrates how regulatory compliance can translate into tangible open data practices, allowing citizens to hold authorities accountable through informed participation. Ultimately, this enhances democratic quality by ensuring that public resources and administrative actions are visible and verifiable, fostering a culture of trust and continuous institutional improvement through widespread data availability.
