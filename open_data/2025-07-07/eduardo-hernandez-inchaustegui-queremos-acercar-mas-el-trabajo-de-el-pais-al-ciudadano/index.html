Eduardo Hernández Incháustegui: “Queremos acercar más el trabajo de El País al ciudadano”

Eduardo Hernández Incháustegui: “Queremos acercar más el trabajo de El País al ciudadano”

Eduardo Hernández Incháustegui, an open data specialist, has taken over El País de Cali with the firm intention of aligning regional journalism with public transparency and social oversight. His main conclusion is that the true value of government data does not lie in its mere publication, but in its capacity to be processed and transformed into accessible knowledge that enables citizens to monitor the use of public resources. The article highlights the importance of bridging the gap between technical information and the general public through technological tools and civic education. By facilitating access to information on public contracting and investments, ordinary citizens are empowered to demand accountability, demonstrating that data journalism is an essential tool for combating misinformation and strengthening local democracy. This initiative is crucial for the open data movement because it illustrates how technology can enhance the watchdog role of the media. By integrating platforms that analyze complex public data, it shows that transparency is not just a legal requirement, but a daily asset that improves the quality of urban life, directly linking technological innovation with investigative journalism and effective citizen participation.
