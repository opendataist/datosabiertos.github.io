XX Concurso Nacional Transparencia 2025: crea un corto y gana 60 mil pesos; fechas y registro

XX Concurso Nacional Transparencia 2025: crea un corto y gana 60 mil pesos; fechas y registro

The National Transparency in Short Film Contest mobilizes young people to visualize the impact of information technologies on accountability and civic culture. By channeling creativity into short audiovisual productions, the initiative empowers young citizens to actively combat disinformation and promote transparency. This approach transforms abstract governance concepts into accessible narratives, encouraging a deeper societal understanding of the right to know and the mechanisms of public oversight. The competition emphasizes the practical application of open data principles by rewarding works that demonstrate social impact, content quality, and creativity. It specifically includes categories for indigenous languages and sign language, ensuring inclusive participation and broader dissemination of transparency messages. This diversity highlights the necessity of making government information accessible to all demographics, reinforcing the idea that transparency is a collective responsibility rather than a restricted privilege. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how non-technical citizens can translate complex public information into compelling stories that drive civic engagement. It demonstrates that open data is not merely about releasing statistics but about fostering a culture where individuals understand and utilize public information to hold authorities accountable. Such initiatives are crucial for building trust in institutions and ensuring that data serves as a tool for democratic participation rather than just administrative record-keeping.
