The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

InQuest Science’s Identifer Software addresses critical gaps in pharmaceutical quality control by offering a specialized, lifecycle-oriented platform for visual inspection in parenteral and medical device manufacturing. By consolidating fragmented data from raw materials to finished products, the system enables manufacturers to monitor manufacturing trends, establish realistic control limits, and proactively identify inconsistencies that could lead to batch recalls. This comprehensive oversight ensures that quality assurance teams have full visibility into product history, facilitating more robust decision-making regarding batch release or rejection. A key advantage over generic Manufacturing Execution Systems is the software’s deep specialization in pharmacopeial requirements, particularly USP-790 and USP-1790. It leverages a digital defect library and blinded test standards to enhance the accuracy of both manual and automated inspection processes. By streamlining the training, qualification, and periodic requalification of inspectors, the platform reduces human error and false results. This focus on precise defect identification and standardized challenge testing ensures that quality personnel are rigorously assessed against stringent regulatory expectations, improving overall product safety and compliance. This tool is highly relevant to the open data community as it demonstrates how structured, digital defect libraries and audit trails can transform legacy, paper-based quality systems into transparent, analyzable data streams. By replacing manual data entry with integrated digital workflows, the software minimizes data manipulation risks and ensures data integrity through clear audit trails. This approach highlights the broader potential of open, standardized data frameworks in pharmaceuticals to support real-time analytics, easier auditing, and continuous process improvement, moving the industry toward more transparent and verifiable quality management practices.
