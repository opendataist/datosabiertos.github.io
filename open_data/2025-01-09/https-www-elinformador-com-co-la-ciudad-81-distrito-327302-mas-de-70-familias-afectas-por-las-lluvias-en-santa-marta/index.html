Open Data
ES EN

Severe flooding in Santa Marta has compelled authorities to launch comprehensive impact assessments and emergency relief operations. This situation underscores the critical importance of open data in disaster management, as transparent, real-time information facilitates effective resource allocation and rapid assistance to affected communities. Publicly accessible datasets detailing impacted households and infrastructure damage enable more efficient coordination of humanitarian aid and preventive measures. By making this information available, governments can ensure accountability and accelerate response times to climate-related emergencies, thereby protecting vulnerable populations from further harm. Ultimately, this event highlights how open data serves as a vital tool for building resilience. It transforms raw emergency statistics into actionable insights, fostering trust between citizens and institutions while guiding long-term strategies to mitigate future climate risks in both urban and rural areas.
