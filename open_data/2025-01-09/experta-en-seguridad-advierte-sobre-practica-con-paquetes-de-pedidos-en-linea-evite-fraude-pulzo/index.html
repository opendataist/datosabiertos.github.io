Experta en seguridad advierte sobre práctica con paquetes de pedidos en línea: evite fraude - Pulzo

The article highlights the growing cybersecurity risks associated with the surge in online shopping in Colombia, specifically focusing on the vulnerability of discarded packaging. It explains how criminals exploit this oversight through "trashing," a practice where they retrieve personal information from trash to launch targeted fraud, identity theft, or blackmail. Consequently, the main conclusion urges citizens to actively destroy labels or erase personal data from packages before disposal to prevent such data breaches. This issue is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical importance of data privacy and responsible information management in everyday digital interactions. While open data promotes transparency and accessibility, this scenario serves as a stark reminder that improper handling of personal data can lead to severe privacy violations. It emphasizes that individual awareness and proper data sanitization are essential components of a broader ethical data ecosystem. By ignoring basic hygiene regarding physical data carriers, consumers inadvertently contribute to a landscape where sensitive information is exposed and misused. This underscores the necessity for robust public education on data protection, linking physical security practices with digital safety. Ultimately, protecting personal data requires vigilance at all touchpoints, reinforcing the principle that data security is a shared responsibility between users, platforms, and society.
