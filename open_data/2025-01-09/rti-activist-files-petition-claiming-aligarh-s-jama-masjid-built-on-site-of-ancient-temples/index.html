RTI Activist Files Petition Claiming Aligarh's Jama Masjid Built on Site of Ancient Temples

RTI data reveals Jama Masjid was built on public land without approval, prompting a petition to declare its current management illegal. The activist argues historical sites previously occupied the location and seeks government takeover. This case highlights the critical role of the Right to Information Act in exposing historical discrepancies and challenging established narratives. It demonstrates how transparent access to municipal records can drive legal accountability regarding public land usage. Such transparency is vital for open data initiatives, showing how public records empower citizens to verify claims about heritage sites. It underscores the importance of accessible information in fostering civic engagement and ensuring governmental accountability in preserving or understanding historical contexts.
