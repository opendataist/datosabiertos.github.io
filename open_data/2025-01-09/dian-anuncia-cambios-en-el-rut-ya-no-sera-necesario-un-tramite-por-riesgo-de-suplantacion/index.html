Dian anuncia cambios en el RUT: ya no será necesario un trámite por riesgo de suplantación

Dian anuncia cambios en el RUT: ya no será necesario un trámite por riesgo de suplantación

The DIAN eliminates the obligation to periodically update and download the Single Tax Registry (Registro Único Tributario), simplifying tax procedures for Colombian citizens in 2025. This change establishes that the document has indefinite validity, requiring updates only in the event of substantial changes to personal or economic data. This measure significantly reduces cybersecurity risks and identity theft associated with the recurrent handling of sensitive documents. By minimizing the necessary interaction with government systems, it better protects taxpayers' privacy against potential data breaches. The decision is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how efficient digitalization and the reduction of administrative barriers foster transparency. By automating registry management through internally updated data, the quality of available public information is improved without compromising user security.
