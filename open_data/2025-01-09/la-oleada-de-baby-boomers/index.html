La oleada de ‘baby boomers’

The Spanish baby boom cohort, a significant portion of the population, is reaching retirement age, posing a serious threat to pension systems. Their transition out of the workforce creates labor shortages, challenging public services and businesses to find adequate replacements for this large demographic group. This shift highlights the critical importance of transparent demographic data in understanding societal pressures. Open data enables policymakers to track these population trends accurately, facilitating better resource allocation and long-term planning for social security sustainability. By leveraging accessible population statistics, governments can anticipate financial strains and labor market gaps. This transparency ensures that open data serves as a vital tool for addressing the economic impacts of aging populations, promoting resilient and informed public policy decisions.
