Camuzzi advierte a los usuarios por fraudes y estafas virtuales: las recomendaciones de la empresa

Camuzzi Gas warns its customers about widespread virtual scams designed to impersonate the utility company. These fraudulent activities often use fake social media profiles and QR codes to steal personal data or trick users into accessing malicious sites. The company emphasizes that it never conducts promotions, transactions, or urgent service alerts via private messages, phone calls, or social media, urging the public to disregard any such communications that create a sense of urgency. The alert highlights critical security protocols for protecting digital identities. Users are advised not to share passwords, personal information, or click on suspicious links and applications sent via SMS or WhatsApp. Instead, individuals should change passwords regularly, avoid public Wi-Fi networks, and verify the legitimacy of any digital request by contacting official channels. This guidance aims to prevent data breaches and financial loss caused by phishing attempts that exploit trust in the brand. This notice is highly relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the critical importance of data integrity and security in public service interactions. It underscores how easily digital infrastructure can be weaponized for fraud, compromising user trust and privacy. For open data advocates, this case serves as a reminder that transparency must be balanced with robust security measures to prevent the misuse of institutional identities. It highlights the need for clear, verified communication channels to ensure that public information remains trustworthy and that citizens are protected from data exploitation in an increasingly digital environment.
