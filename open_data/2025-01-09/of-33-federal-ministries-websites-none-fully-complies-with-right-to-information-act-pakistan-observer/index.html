Of 33 federal ministries’ websites, none fully complies with right to information act - Pakistan Observer

Of 33 federal ministries’ websites, none fully complies with right to information act - Pakistan Observer

A recent report reveals that federal ministries largely fail to proactively disclose public information online, directly violating the Right of Access to Information Act. This systemic non-compliance creates significant gaps in transparency, enabling misinformation and disinformation to spread unchecked. Without timely, authentic data from official sources, citizens are left vulnerable to false narratives that undermine government credibility. The consequences extend beyond mere regulatory breaches; they erode public trust and hinder effective governance. The study shows that over a third of ministries ignored information requests, while no entity fully met proactive disclosure standards. This lack of openness prevents citizens from understanding public body functions and decisions, fostering an environment where rumors thrive and institutional confidence diminishes. This situation is critical for open data advocates, as it highlights the urgent need for mandatory digital transparency. Proactive disclosure through technology is not just a legal obligation but a essential tool against misinformation. Strengthening these practices ensures that government data is accessible and reliable, fostering accountability and empowering citizens with the truth necessary for a functioning democracy.
