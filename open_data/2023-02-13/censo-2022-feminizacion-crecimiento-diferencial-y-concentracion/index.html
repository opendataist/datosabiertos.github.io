Censo 2022: feminización, crecimiento diferencial y concentración

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) has released preliminary data from the 2022 national census, revealing sustained but uneven population growth across Argentina. The analysis highlights the persistent feminization of society and demographic aging, while growth rates vary significantly among provinces and departments. Notable increases are observed in Patagonian and tourist regions, contrasting with stagnant or declining dynamics in traditional major urban centers, thereby reshaping the country’s territorial distribution. The relevance of this article for open data lies in how the early release of these public statistical data enables academia and civil society to conduct immediate quantitative and qualitative analyses. By freely accessing disaggregated census information, it is possible to critically examine the current situation and historical trends without relying exclusively on delayed official interpretations. This empowers the scientific community to verify hypotheses regarding migration, family structure, and regional development in real time. Moreover, transparency in these data is crucial for public policy planning and future research. Open access to details on territorial concentration and demographic disparities facilitates the identification of spatial inequalities and the formulation of more precise development strategies. Ultimately, the democratization of this information fosters a better-informed society, capable of demanding and actively participating in evidence-based solutions addressing the realities of the national demographic landscape.
