Ordena INAI informe sobre gasto para reparación por sismos

Ordena INAI informe sobre gasto para reparación por sismos

The INAI ruled against the SICT for incomplete reporting on disaster funds, emphasizing that citizens drive transparency through active scrutiny. This decision reinforces accountability, as authorities must use broad search criteria rather than restrictive ones to ensure full disclosure of public resource execution. Such rulings are vital for open data, establishing legal precedents that mandate comprehensive, accessible information for genuine public oversight.
