Shock at hundreds of HSE data breaches including files sent to wrong person

The High Level report reveals over 500 data protection breaches within the Health Service Executive last year, highlighting severe vulnerabilities in patient information management. Incidents included missing files, incorrect data sharing, and unauthorized access, demonstrating a systemic failure to maintain confidentiality across various hospitals. Experts emphasize that these frequent errors undermine patient autonomy and trust, suggesting current GDPR compliance is insufficient. The absence of a unique national identifier and lax physical security measures contribute significantly to these mistakes, which can lead to identity theft and serious harm to individuals. This case is highly relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the critical importance of robust security frameworks when handling sensitive information. While open data promotes transparency, this example shows that without strict access controls and accountability, public sector data systems risk exposing private details, necessitating stronger governance to balance openness with privacy protection.
