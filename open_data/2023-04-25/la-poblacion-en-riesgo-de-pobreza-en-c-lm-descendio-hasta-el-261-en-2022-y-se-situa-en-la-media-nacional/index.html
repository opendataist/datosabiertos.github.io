La población en riesgo de pobreza en C-LM descendió hasta el 26,1% en 2022 y se sitúa en la media nacional

The survey reveals a significant improvement in the national economic situation, marked by an increase in average incomes and a historic decline in the at-risk-of-poverty-or-social-exclusion rate. This widespread decrease, particularly among younger and working-age groups, indicates a gradual reduction in inequality, although specific vulnerabilities persist in areas such as the ability to keep housing adequately heated or to cover unexpected expenses. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the detailed publication of socioeconomic indicators disaggregated by multiple demographic and geographic variables. By making data on education, nationality, and household type accessible, it enables an in-depth analysis of structural gaps. This facilitates the precise identification of the most affected sectors, such as low-skilled individuals and non-EU foreigners, providing essential transparency for assessing social equity and the effectiveness of public policies. Thus, the availability of these open statistics allows researchers and citizens to understand not only the overall trend but also the persistent inequalities that aggregated metrics may obscure. The ability to cross-reference income data with factors such as unemployment or region of residence empowers informed decision-making. This accessibility to public information is fundamental for promoting accountability and designing targeted solutions to reduce social exclusion efficiently and based on real evidence.
