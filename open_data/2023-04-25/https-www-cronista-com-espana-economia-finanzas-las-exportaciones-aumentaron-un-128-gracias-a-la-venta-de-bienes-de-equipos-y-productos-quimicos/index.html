The article reveals a significant improvement in Spain’s trade balance, characterized by sustained growth in exports driven by key industrial sectors. This dynamic has substantially reduced the trade deficit and enhanced the capacity to cover it, signaling greater competitiveness in capital goods, chemicals, and the automotive sector amid a moderate price environment. The geographic distribution of sales highlights the strength of the European market as the primary destination, although opportunities for expansion are also evident in emerging markets in the Americas and Asia. This sectoral and territorial analysis provides a detailed view of trade flows, which is essential for understanding the productive structures and energy dependencies that continue to influence the national trade balance. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how official statistical data should be published in an accessible and structured manner. It facilitates cross-sectoral economic analysis, enabling researchers, businesses, and citizens to verify trends, make informed decisions, and promote economic transparency through the reuse of high-quality public information.
