El Indec da a conocer la actividad económica de febrero

The INDEC released February economic activity data, noting that January’s growth halted a four-month decline. However, in February, the industrial and construction sectors experienced significant year-over-year and month-over-month contractions. This update is relevant for open data by underscoring the importance of transparent, timely economic indicators. It demonstrates how public access to detailed statistical data supports the analysis of sector-specific trends and economic recovery trajectories.
