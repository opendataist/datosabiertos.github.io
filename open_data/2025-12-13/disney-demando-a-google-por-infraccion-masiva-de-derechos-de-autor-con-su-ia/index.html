Disney demandó a Google por infracción masiva de derechos de autor con su IA

Disney accuses Google of large-scale copyright infringement by using AI to commercialize unauthorized images and videos of its iconic characters. This legal action highlights the ongoing tension regarding how tech giants train models, questioning whether the use of publicly available data respects intellectual property rights or exploits creative works without consent. The conflict underscores the critical importance of clear data provenance and attribution in open data ecosystems. When AI systems generate content that mimics protected brands, it raises significant ethical and legal concerns about the boundaries of public domain usage versus proprietary rights, challenging current norms of open information sharing. This dispute is relevant to open data as it pressures stakeholders to develop robust governance frameworks. It emphasizes the need for transparent data licensing and respectful reuse mechanisms, ensuring that the openness of digital resources does not come at the expense of creators' rights, thereby balancing innovation with legal and ethical accountability.
