Ghana: Atiwa East MP Calls for More Resources for RTI Commission

Ghana: Atiwa East MP Calls for More Resources for RTI Commission

The article argues for increased government funding for the Right to Information Commission to effectively combat corruption and ensure institutional transparency. Adequate resources are essential for empowering citizens to access information, thereby strengthening accountability in both public and private sectors while preventing illicit financial flows that drain national revenue. Current budget allocations are deemed insufficient, with delayed releases and inadequate compensation packages hindering the Commission’s operations. The MP highlights that reliance on unsustainable donor support undermines long-term goals, urging the state to assume full financial responsibility to maintain the Commission’s independence and operational capacity. Relevance to open_data lies in the emphasis on enforcing data disclosure standards through meaningful penalties. This underscores that open data initiatives require not just technical access but robust regulatory frameworks and sustainable funding. Without proper enforcement mechanisms and financial backing, transparency efforts remain weak, failing to drive the systemic accountability necessary for effective open data ecosystems.
