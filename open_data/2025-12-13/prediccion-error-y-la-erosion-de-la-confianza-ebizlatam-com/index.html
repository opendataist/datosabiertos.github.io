Predicción, error y la erosión de la confianza - ebizlatam.com

Predicción, error y la erosión de la confianza - ebizlatam.com

The article highlights how inaccurate predictions in political polls and financial markets erode public trust. It argues that when experts conceal errors or present biased data, the integrity of decision-making processes deteriorates. This lack of transparency creates a cycle where false certainty replaces reliable information, harming both individual choices and broader societal confidence in institutions. Open data is crucial to counter this trend by enabling independent verification of claims. When organizations and analysts openly share their methodologies, raw data, and outcomes, including mistakes, they allow citizens to validate information themselves. This transparency transforms opaque predictions into accountable processes, ensuring that stakeholders can distinguish between genuine insights and speculative noise, thereby restoring faith in data-driven guidance. Ultimately, trust is rebuilt only through complete honesty about successes and failures. By adopting open data practices, entities in Chile’s political and financial sectors can move away from hiding errors and toward validating information publicly. This shift empowers users to make informed decisions based on real evidence rather than curated narratives, ensuring that future projections are grounded in verified facts rather than hidden agendas.
