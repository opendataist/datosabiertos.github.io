Google recarga Deep Research: ahora con Gemini 3 Pro, más útil y potente para investigaciones

Google recarga Deep Research: ahora con Gemini 3 Pro, más útil y potente para investigaciones

Google has integrated its advanced Gemini 3 Pro model into Deep Research, significantly enhancing the precision and reliability of web investigations by reducing AI hallucinations. This advancement empowers users to obtain trustworthy results and allows developers to embed these autonomous research capabilities directly into their own applications via API. This integration fosters the creation of specialized AI-driven solutions, marking a shift toward more robust and scalable tools for professionals who need to analyze large volumes of information efficiently. To ensure quality, Google introduced DeepResearchQA, a benchmark that evaluates agents on the exhaustiveness and accuracy of multi-step information retrieval. This diagnostic tool highlights how reflective thinking improves search effectiveness, ensuring that outputs are comprehensive and precise. For the open data community, this emphasis on verifiable accuracy and structured output is crucial. The ability to generate results in JSON format facilitates seamless data integration into other systems, promoting interoperability and enabling further automated analysis or dissemination of public data without manual intervention. Furthermore, Deep Research provides detailed citations and customizable report structures, allowing users to verify sources and tailor data presentation to specific needs. By supporting formats like CSV and PDF, along with dynamic web data extraction, the tool serves as a powerful bridge between raw public information and actionable insights. This relevance to open data lies in its capacity to transform unstructured public web data into organized, verifiable, and machine-readable formats. It democratizes access to high-quality intelligence, enabling researchers and developers to build more transparent and data-driven applications that rely on credible, traceable information sources.
