This document outlines the data processing practices of Hipertextual S.L., detailing the collection and management of personal information through cookies and usage data. The website operates on the premise that providing certain data is mandatory for access to its services, while emphasizing that third-party analytics and advertising services handle user interactions across international borders. The entity establishes legal bases for processing, ranging from user consent to legitimate interests and contractual obligations. It highlights users’ rights, particularly those subject to higher protection standards, such as the right to withdraw consent, access, correct, or delete their data. The policy also clarifies data retention periods and the security measures adopted to prevent unauthorized access or alterations. This text is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the critical intersection between closed corporate data governance and transparency. By defining how user data is collected, used for advertising, and shared with third parties such as Google, it underscores the importance of clear, accessible privacy policies. Such transparency enables a better understanding of the digital ecosystem, allowing users and regulators to hold organizations accountable for their data handling practices—a foundational element of trustworthy data environments.
