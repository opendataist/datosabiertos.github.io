USA Pickleball uses coordinated campaigns to displace other sports at local parks, revealing the sport’s growth is engineered rather than organic. This highlights the importance of open data and freedom of information requests in exposing hidden organizational strategies. Such transparency allows communities to understand the mechanisms behind rapidly changing public resource allocations.
