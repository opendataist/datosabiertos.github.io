EU's AI Act could exclude open-source models from regulation

The proposed EU AI Act appears set to exempt open-source models from strict regulations, balancing innovation with safety. This approach acknowledges the unique nature of collaborative code development while reserving oversight for high-risk applications. Consequently, open-source projects may face fewer compliance burdens, fostering greater participation and transparency within the global open_data and software communities.
