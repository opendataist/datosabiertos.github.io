México registró una inflación de 0,64% en noviembre de 2023

Mexico’s inflation rate stabilized in late 2023, marking a significant slowdown from previous highs. This trend reflects improved macroeconomic conditions and reduced price volatility across key sectors, signaling a return to more predictable economic stability for consumers and businesses. The divergence between underlying and non-underlying price indices highlights the impact of specific policy changes, such as the end of temporary energy subsidies. Understanding these distinct drivers is crucial for analyzing true inflationary pressures and the effectiveness of monetary policy adjustments. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular statistics for public analysis. Accessible datasets empower researchers to track economic trends, fostering accountability and enabling better-informed decisions regarding fiscal and monetary strategies based on verified official metrics.
