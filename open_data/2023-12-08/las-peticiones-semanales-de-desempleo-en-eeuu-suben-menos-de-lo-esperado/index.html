Las peticiones semanales de desempleo en EEUU suben menos de lo esperado

US weekly jobless claims rose slightly but remained below expectations, signaling a labor market that is cooling without collapsing. This balance suggests that the Federal Reserve can likely halt rate hikes while waiting for further wage moderation to bring inflation down to target levels. The data implies that monetary policy will remain restrictive for now, as the economy is still robust enough to prevent immediate rate cuts. A gradual slowdown is necessary to achieve the 2% inflation goal, keeping policymakers in a holding pattern through early next year. This case is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for timely, accurate government statistics, such as those from the Department of Labor. Transparent, freely accessible datasets enable analysts to monitor economic health in real time, ensuring that market expectations and policy decisions are grounded in reliable, public information rather than speculation.
