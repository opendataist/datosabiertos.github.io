Presos en cárceles de Veracruz también han enfrentan corrupción

The article highlights significant corruption rates among Veracruz’s prison population and the general public, as reported by INEGI. This data underscores the urgent need for transparency and accountability mechanisms in public institutions. Such findings are crucial for open data initiatives, which provide the necessary evidence to expose systemic issues. Accessible statistics empower citizens to demand reforms and ensure government officials are held responsible for illicit activities. Ultimately, promoting open data helps dismantle structures of impunity. By making this information widely available, society can drive meaningful policy changes that protect vulnerable groups and restore trust in public service.
