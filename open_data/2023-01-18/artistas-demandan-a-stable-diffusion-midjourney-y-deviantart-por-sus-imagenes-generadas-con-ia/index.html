Artistas demandan a Stable Diffusion, Midjourney y Deviantart por sus imágenes generadas con IA

Artists are suing major AI image generators and a portfolio platform for training their models on copyrighted works without consent or compensation. This legal challenge highlights the ethical crisis in generative AI, where algorithms learn specific aesthetics by analyzing vast datasets of protected content, thereby undermining the rights and economic interests of original creators. The lawsuit targets these companies for alleged copyright infringement and unfair competition, seeking damages and preventive measures to guard against future harms. It underscores the tension between technological innovation and intellectual property, asserting that AI systems cannot legitimately “learn” styles through the unauthorized exploitation of human artistic labor. This dispute is crucial for open data, as it questions the legal framework governing publicly available datasets used in AI development. It forces a critical re-evaluation of whether open access to creative works for machine learning should require explicit permission, potentially reshaping standards for data ethics and transparency in the digital age.
