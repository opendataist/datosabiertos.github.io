¿Justicia? Demandan a plataformas de IA por imágenes con derechos de autor

The controversy surrounding copyright in generative tools is intensifying with lawsuits against AI giants, highlighting the unauthorized use of creative works. These legal actions reflect the tension between technological innovation and the protection of intellectual property in the open digital environment. The debate extends beyond legal issues by questioning the very essence of artistic creation. Critics argue that although AI can replicate styles, it lacks the deep human experience and suffering necessary to generate authentic art, thereby limiting its true creative value. This article is relevant to open data because it exposes the ethical and legal risks of extracting protected information without consent. It highlights the need for transparency in model training and responsible data management, which are fundamental to maintaining trust in sustainable digital ecosystems.
