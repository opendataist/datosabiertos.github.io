Gobierno Abierto y participación ciudadana: Los ejes centrales de la Universidad Internacional de Rancagua Verano 2023

The International University of Rancagua Summer 2023 serves as a critical platform for fostering civic engagement and local development. By organizing thematic workshops and lectures, the initiative aims to translate community reflection into concrete action, specifically focusing on defining what Open Government means for the region and gathering public input. A primary goal is to use these discussions to draft the first Open Government Action Plan for Rancagua. This process transforms abstract concepts of transparency into a diagnostic tool for municipal improvement. The event structure ensures that insights from classes and cultural activities directly contribute to building a more responsive and inclusive local administration, moving beyond dialogue toward tangible policy frameworks. This initiative is vital for open data because it emphasizes the participatory nature of governance that data-driven policies require. By engaging citizens in conversations about information rights, technology, and environmental governance, the project highlights the necessity of accessible, public data for informed decision-making. It demonstrates how open government frameworks rely on active community participation to validate and improve the systems that manage public resources and ensure accountability.
