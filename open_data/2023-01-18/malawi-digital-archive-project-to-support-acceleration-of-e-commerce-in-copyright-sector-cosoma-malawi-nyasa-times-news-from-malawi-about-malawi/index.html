Malawi Digital Archive project to support acceleration of e-commerce in copyright sector—COSOMA - Malawi Nyasa Times - News from Malawi about Malawi

The Malawi Digital Archive, launched by the Copyright Society of Malawi, represents a strategic shift toward preserving and monetizing local creative assets. By digitizing works across music, film, and literature, the initiative ensures long-term preservation against physical loss or disaster while providing artists with secure, accessible records. This infrastructure directly supports the core open data principle of creating durable, standardized repositories that safeguard cultural heritage and ensure information longevity for future generations. Furthermore, the project bridges the gap between creators and consumers by facilitating e-commerce and reducing piracy. Through digitization, artists can more easily reach international markets, transforming isolated creative outputs into globally accessible digital commodities. This aligns with open data goals by removing friction from knowledge exchange, allowing creative content to flow freely and securely across borders, thereby enhancing transparency and fairness in the digital rights landscape. Ultimately, this initiative highlights how structured digital data drives economic growth and national development. By treating creative works as valuable data assets, Malawi aims to integrate its cultural sector into the broader digital economy, supporting its long-term development agenda. The project demonstrates that making creative data open and accessible is not merely about preservation but is a vital economic engine that empowers local talent to compete globally.
