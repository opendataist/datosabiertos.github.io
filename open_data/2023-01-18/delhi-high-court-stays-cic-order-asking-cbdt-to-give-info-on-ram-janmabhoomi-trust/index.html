Delhi High Court stays CIC order asking CBDT to give info on Ram Janmabhoomi Trust

The Delhi High Court has temporarily suspended a directive from the Central Information Commission that ordered the Central Board of Direct Taxes to release financial records concerning the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust. This interim stay highlights a critical intersection between transparency mandates and statutory privacy protections, emphasizing that procedural fairness is paramount when handling sensitive taxpayer data. The court’s decision underscores the importance of proper legal procedure under the Right to Information framework, specifically noting that the third party involved was not granted prior notice. Furthermore, the ruling draws attention to the tension between general disclosure laws and specific exemptions within income tax regulations, suggesting that fiduciary data held by tax authorities may warrant special protection against automatic release. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the complex boundary between public accountability and the lawful exemption of private financial information. It serves as a reminder that open data initiatives must carefully navigate existing legal frameworks, ensuring that the pursuit of transparency does not inadvertently violate statutory privacy rights or procedural due process, thereby setting a precedent for how public institutions balance these competing interests.
