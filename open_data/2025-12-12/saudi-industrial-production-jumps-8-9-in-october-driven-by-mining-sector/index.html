Saudi Industrial Production Jumps 8.9% in October, Driven by Mining Sector

Saudi Industrial Production Jumps 8.9% in October, Driven by Mining Sector

Saudi Arabia’s industrial sector demonstrated robust growth in October 2025, driven primarily by a significant surge in oil production. This expansion highlights the nation's capacity to scale up energy output, reinforcing its role as a key global supplier. The strong annual performance underscores the resilience of major economic activities, particularly mining and manufacturing, which continued to expand despite some monthly fluctuations. The data also reveals positive trends in utilities and non-oil industries, suggesting diversification efforts are yielding results alongside traditional energy sectors. While certain manufacturing subsectors faced declines, the overall upward trajectory indicates healthy industrial momentum. This balanced growth across multiple sectors points to a stabilizing economy with varied sources of production strength, moving beyond sole reliance on single commodity outputs.
