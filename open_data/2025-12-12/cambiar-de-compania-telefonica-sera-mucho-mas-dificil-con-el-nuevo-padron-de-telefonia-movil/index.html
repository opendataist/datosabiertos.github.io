Cambiar de compañía telefónica será mucho más difícil con el nuevo padrón de telefonía móvil

Cambiar de compañía telefónica será mucho más difícil con el nuevo padrón de telefonía móvil

The new telecommunications registration policy introduces significant risks to the integrity of number portability, potentially causing service disruptions and data vulnerabilities. By forcing the disconnection of a line before registering it with a new operator, the process breaks the fundamental principle of continuity and transparency. This structural flaw creates a window where user data may be improperly handled, duplicated, or exposed to security breaches during the transition between providers, raising serious concerns about privacy and data protection. This issue is highly relevant to open_data because it highlights the critical importance of clear data governance and transparent handling protocols during system migrations. When regulations fail to specify how user information should be managed between different responsible entities, it opens the door to data misuse and erodes trust. The lack of defined controls for blocking, eliminating, or reactivating data demonstrates how insufficient regulatory frameworks can compromise the security and integrity of personal information, affecting both individual rights and systemic reliability. Furthermore, the potential regression to previous complexities undermines consumer confidence and stifles healthy market competition. As more users seek affordable alternatives through virtual operators, any barrier to seamless switching harms the ecosystem. Ensuring that data flows securely and accurately during these transitions is essential for maintaining a fair digital market. Without robust safeguards, the ease of choice for consumers is negated by the fear of service interruption and data loss, ultimately harming the broader goals of accessibility and digital rights.
