Micaela Mantegna cautivó a Dillom y Pergolini con ética en tecnología e IA: "Cuando empezás a abrir la caja, hay más y más"

Micaela Mantegna cautivó a Dillom y Pergolini con ética en tecnología e IA: "Cuando empezás a abrir la caja, hay más y más"

The article argues that while artificial intelligence holds transformative potential for society, its development is fraught with complex ethical and legal ambiguities, particularly regarding copyright and creative labor. A central insight is that actions deemed unethical, such as the appropriation of creative resources by AI models, do not necessarily constitute illegal acts under current laws, highlighting a significant gap between moral judgment and legal regulation. This distinction is crucial for understanding the challenges facing creators who struggle to protect their intellectual property while AI systems train on vast amounts of existing work. The tension between technological innovation and the protection of artistic integrity creates a gray area where ethical concerns outpace legal frameworks, leaving many creators without adequate recourse or compensation. This discussion is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the need for transparent data sourcing and clear licensing agreements in AI development. As open datasets become foundational for training models, ensuring that this data usage respects both ethical standards and evolving legal norms is essential for sustainable and equitable innovation in the open data ecosystem.
