Nigeria's Active Internet Subscribers Hit 142.6m As Mobile GSM Maintains Lead

Nigeria's Active Internet Subscribers Hit 142.6m As Mobile GSM Maintains Lead

Nigeria’s internet subscription base has demonstrated sustained expansion, culminating in a significant rise by late 2025. This growth is primarily driven by the Mobile GSM sector, which dominates connectivity preferences across the nation. Major telecommunications providers are expanding their networks, with MTN, Airtel, and Globacom leading the market share. The upward trajectory suggests that current investments in infrastructure are effectively supporting increased user adoption and engagement with digital services. Industry leaders project that this growth will continue into the final months of the year. The recent substantial investments in fiber optic cables and broader network expansions are anticipated to further boost subscriber numbers. This infrastructure development not only supports existing users but also facilitates the onboarding of new participants, indicating a robust and evolving digital ecosystem that is ready to accommodate higher demand and improved service quality. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it provides transparent, granular insights into national digital infrastructure and adoption rates. Such statistics enable researchers, policymakers, and developers to analyze market dynamics, assess the effectiveness of regulatory policies, and identify gaps in connectivity. By making these detailed subscriber metrics publicly available, the Nigerian Communications Commission supports evidence-based decision-making and fosters innovation in the telecommunications sector.
