Disney acusa a Google de usar la IA para infringir los derechos de autor de imágenes - Ciencia - ABC Color

Disney acusa a Google de usar la IA para infringir los derechos de autor de imágenes - Ciencia - ABC Color

Disney accuses Google of large-scale copyright infringement by using protected works to train generative AI models without authorization. This practice allegedly allows the commercial exploitation of Disney’s intellectual property and falsely implies official endorsement through the Gemini logo. The company highlights specific franchises affected, reinforcing its broader legal strategy against AI firms. These accusations align with simultaneous actions against other tech giants, demonstrating an industry-wide push to establish clear boundaries for data usage in artificial intelligence development. This conflict is highly relevant to open_data as it underscores the critical tension between the need for diverse training datasets and strict intellectual property rights. It challenges the open_data community to balance accessibility with legal compliance, emphasizing that not all data can be freely used for model training without explicit permission.
