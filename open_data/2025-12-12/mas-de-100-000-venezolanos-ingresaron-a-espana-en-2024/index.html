Más de 100.000 venezolanos ingresaron a España en 2024

Más de 100.000 venezolanos ingresaron a España en 2024

The article reveals that Venezuelan migration to Spain has intensified, driven significantly by a high proportion of professionals with university degrees. This trend highlights a substantial brain drain, as a large share of recent arrivals hold advanced qualifications, representing a significant loss of human capital for their country of origin. Furthermore, the strong positive migration balance indicates that most Venezuelans intend to stay permanently, rather than using Spain as a transit point. This stability differentiates them from other groups and underscores the demographic shift toward retaining skilled labor in key Spanish regions like Catalonia and Madrid. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how combining official statistics with demographic variables can expose hidden social dynamics. It encourages the development of interoperable datasets that link migration flows with educational profiles, enabling policymakers and researchers to better understand and address the implications of skilled migration on both sending and receiving countries.
