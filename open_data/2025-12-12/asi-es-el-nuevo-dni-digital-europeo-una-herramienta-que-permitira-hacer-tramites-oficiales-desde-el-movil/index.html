Así es el nuevo DNI Digital Europeo, una herramienta que permitirá hacer trámites oficiales desde el móvil

Así es el nuevo DNI Digital Europeo, una herramienta que permitirá hacer trámites oficiales desde el móvil

The EU launches the European Digital Identity to securely manage personal documents and authenticate access to online services. This initiative prioritizes user control over data, addressing privacy concerns in an increasingly digital landscape. This development is vital for open data as it establishes a standardized, transparent framework for digital authentication. By ensuring citizens own their identity, it fosters trust and interoperability across member states. Ultimately, this system enhances data sovereignty. It demonstrates how public infrastructure can empower individuals to manage their digital presence, promoting secure and open interactions between users, businesses, and government entities.
