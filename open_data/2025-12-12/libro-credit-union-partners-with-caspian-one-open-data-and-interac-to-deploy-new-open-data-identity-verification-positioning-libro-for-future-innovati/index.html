Libro Credit Union Partners with Caspian One Open Data and Interac to Deploy New Open Data Identity Verification, Positioning Libro for Future Innovations

Libro Credit Union Partners with Caspian One Open Data and Interac to Deploy New Open Data Identity Verification, Positioning Libro for Future Innovations

Libro Credit Union has successfully deployed the Interac Verified service through Caspian’s open data infrastructure, marking a significant shift from manual identity checks to secure, real-time digital verification. This implementation allows members and small businesses to authenticate their identities instantly and with explicit consent, eliminating the friction of paperwork and in-person visits. The solution bridges the gap between traditional trust and modern digital convenience, ensuring that security and privacy are maintained while streamlining access to financial services. The deployment highlights the practical application of open data standards, specifically FDX-compliant APIs, to create a trusted ecosystem for financial interactions. By leveraging Caspian’s platform, Libro demonstrates how credit unions can securely connect with third-party networks without compromising member data. This approach not only enhances user experience but also establishes a scalable foundation for future innovations in embedded finance, allowing institutions to rapidly integrate new services while adhering to rigorous data protection protocols. This article is highly relevant to open data as it showcases a successful, real-world implementation of standards-based data sharing that prioritizes user consent and security. It illustrates how open data infrastructures can empower community-focused institutions to compete with larger players by offering seamless, digital-first experiences. The case study serves as a blueprint for other organizations looking to adopt open data practices to reduce barriers to entry, improve operational efficiency, and foster deeper member engagement through innovative, secure digital tools.
