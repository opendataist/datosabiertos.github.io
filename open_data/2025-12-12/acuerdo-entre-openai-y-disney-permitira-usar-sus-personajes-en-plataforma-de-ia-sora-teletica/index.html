Acuerdo entre OpenAI y Disney permitirá usar sus personajes en plataforma de IA Sora | Teletica

Acuerdo entre OpenAI y Disney permitirá usar sus personajes en plataforma de IA Sora | Teletica

Disney and OpenAI have signed a strategic agreement that enables users to generate videos featuring iconic characters using artificial intelligence. Backed by a significant investment from Disney, this partnership marks a turning point for the creative industry by integrating advanced generative technologies into mass-consumption platforms. The agreement prioritizes responsible use, excluding the voices and likenesses of real actors, and implements safety controls to protect copyright and prevent harmful content. This stance demonstrates how major corporations and AI leaders can collaborate to balance technological innovation with the ethical protection of intellectual property and original creators. This news is relevant to the field of open data because it underscores the urgency of managing and protecting training data and creative assets in the era of generative AI. It highlights critical challenges related to transparency, data governance, and usage rights, which are essential for developing ethical models that respect authorship while fostering open and responsible innovation.
