La Seguridad Social gana 4.408 afiliados en julio y marca récord de 21,87 millones de ocupados

La Seguridad Social gana 4.408 afiliados en julio y marca récord de 21,87 millones de ocupados

The article highlights a sustained expansion in the Spanish labor market, marked by record-breaking levels of Social Security affiliations. This growth is driven largely by a significant increase in permanent contracts, particularly among younger workers, reflecting the positive impact of recent labor reforms. The data suggests a structural improvement in job quality, as temporary employment rates have fallen drastically while indefinite hiring has surged, indicating a more stable and secure labor environment. Sectorally, the expansion is concentrated in high-value industries such as healthcare and commerce, while traditional seasonal drops in education have occurred. Interestingly, despite the overall rise, the number of self-employed workers saw a slight monthly decline, though they have grown over the longer term, with a notable shift toward productive sectors like information and technical services. This divergence points to a complex labor dynamic where traditional employment models evolve alongside a changing freelance economy focused on specialized activities. This report is crucial for open data enthusiasts because it demonstrates the power of transparent, granular public statistics in evaluating policy effectiveness. By analyzing detailed, publicly available datasets on employment, demographics, and sectoral trends, researchers and citizens can independently verify government claims about labor market health. It underscores how accessible administrative data serves as a vital tool for accountability, enabling a deeper understanding of socio-economic shifts and the real-world impact of legislative changes.
