Israel mata a más de 60 gazatíes en un día, y los muertos se aproximan a los 62.700

The article reports a sharp rise in civilian deaths in Gaza, highlighting the severe humanitarian crisis caused by ongoing bombardments and restricted access for rescue teams. This surge underscores the immediate loss of life and the inability of medical services to reach those trapped, emphasizing the dire human cost of the conflict. Critical to open data initiatives, this reporting relies on specific, granular datasets provided by the Ministry of Health to track fatalities and injuries. The verification of these numbers, including the identification of victims at specific hospitals and contexts like food distribution, demonstrates how transparent data collection is essential for documenting the scale of violence and humanitarian needs accurately. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity of accessible, verified statistics to counter misinformation and advocate for accountability. By publishing detailed figures on casualties and the circumstances surrounding them, such data supports evidence-based analysis of conflict impacts, ensuring that the severity of the crisis is visible to the international community and aids in holding perpetrators responsible.
