AudioUK is conducting an anonymized survey to strengthen its case for a dedicated tax relief for podcast and audiobook production. This initiative aims to secure government support in the upcoming budget, ensuring the sector receives treatment comparable to established creative industries like film and television. The primary goal is to attract significant international investment by creating a level playing field with other media sectors. By updating data from a landmark 2021 report, the trade body seeks to demonstrate the industry’s growth potential and economic value to policymakers. This effort is crucial for open data advocacy, as it highlights how aggregated, anonymous industry statistics can drive policy changes. It demonstrates that transparent, collective data collection empowers niche sectors to influence legislative decisions and secure equitable economic frameworks.
