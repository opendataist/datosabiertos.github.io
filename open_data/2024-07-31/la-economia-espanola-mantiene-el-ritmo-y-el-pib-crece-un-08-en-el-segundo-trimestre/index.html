La economía española mantiene el ritmo y el PIB crece un 0,8% en el segundo trimestre

La economía española mantiene el ritmo y el PIB crece un 0,8% en el segundo trimestre

The Spanish economy has experienced sustained expansion, consolidating thirteen consecutive quarters of growth driven by balanced dynamism. The rise in Gross Domestic Product not only reflects the country’s macroeconomic strength but also a significant contribution from the external sector and a progressive moderation of prices. This positive trend suggests that policy reforms are translating into tangible benefits for households and businesses, positioning Spain with performance superior to that of its main European partners. In addition to external demand, the recovery is underpinned by the resilience of domestic consumption and investment, while labor productivity shows signs of recovery after periods of contraction. The increase in real wages and hours worked indicates an improvement in employment conditions and productive capacity. These indicators demonstrate that growth is not merely statistical but is supported by a genuine improvement in purchasing power and economic efficiency, which is crucial for the long-term sustainability of the current economic model. This information is relevant to the field of open data because the National Statistics Institute (INE) provides these detailed time series, broken down by sector, income, and demand. Access to these public data allows researchers, analysts, and developers to audit economic health, create predictive models, and visualize real-time trends. Transparency in the publication of macroeconomic indicators facilitates accountability and fosters evidence-based innovation, enabling civil society and the private sector to make informed decisions regarding investments and public policies.
