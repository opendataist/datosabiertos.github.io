DA welcomes imminent release of crime statistics

DA welcomes imminent release of crime statistics

The Democratic Alliance welcomes the delayed release of South African crime statistics, which had been withheld by the former Police Minister. This data is crucial for understanding the true scale of rampant gun violence and mass killings currently terrorizing communities. Access to this information allows the public to assess the effectiveness of police measures against lawlessness. It reveals whether the state is adequately addressing the crisis affecting families and local economies. This matters for open data because it highlights the struggle for transparency in public security. Citizens cannot hold authorities accountable without reliable, accessible statistics on crime trends and enforcement outcomes.
