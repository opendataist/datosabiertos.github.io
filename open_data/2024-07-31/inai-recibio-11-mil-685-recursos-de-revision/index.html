INAI recibió 11 mil 685 recursos de revisión

The filing of additional access-to-information requests before the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) during the first half of 2024 reflects a growing citizen demand for transparency and data protection. Institutions facing the highest volume of complaints typically handle critical information related to public procurement, payroll, and health processes. This trend underscores the vital importance of open data as a tool for overseeing public management and ensuring the right of access to information. Efficient transparency enables society to monitor the use of resources and the protection of personal data, thereby strengthening accountability. The article is relevant to open data because it highlights the urgency of publishing structured and accessible public information. By facilitating automatic access to these data, the need for individual requests is reduced, optimizing transparency and fostering more informed and effective social participation.
