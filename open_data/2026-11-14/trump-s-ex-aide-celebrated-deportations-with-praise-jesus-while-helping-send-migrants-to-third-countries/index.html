Trump's Ex-Aide Celebrated Deportations With 'Praise Jesus' While Helping Send Migrants to Third Countries

Trump's Ex-Aide Celebrated Deportations With 'Praise Jesus' While Helping Send Migrants to Third Countries

The recent disclosure of internal communications reveals that Corey Lewandowski, serving as an unpaid adviser, actively participated in high-level discussions regarding sensitive third-country deportations at the Department of Homeland Security. Despite holding no formal executive leadership position, he was treated as an equal to senior officials, receiving exclusive briefings and influencing decision-making processes that included controversial immigrant removals. This episode highlights significant concerns regarding transparency and accountability within federal agencies, particularly when external figures wield substantial influence over immigration enforcement without traditional oversight. The extent of Lewandowski’s involvement in sensitive operations suggests a potential bypass of standard procedural safeguards, raising questions about the integrity of administrative processes and the appropriate scope of authority for special government employees. For the open data community, this situation underscores the critical importance of proactive FOIA litigation and the public release of government records. Accessing such internal communications allows watchdogs and citizens to scrutinize government actions, ensuring that decision-making processes remain visible and subject to democratic oversight, thereby preventing the concealment of potentially unlawful administrative practices.
