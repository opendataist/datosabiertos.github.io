The Coup Against Jensen Huang Is a War on Open-Source AI – NaturalNews.com

The article argues that recent controversies surrounding Jensen Huang are a coordinated effort by institutional interests to suppress open-source artificial intelligence in favor of centralized, controlled systems. It posits that the prevailing narrative of AI risks is a manufactured crisis designed to justify regulatory capture, thereby preserving the power of corporate gatekeepers who profit from subscription models and censorship. This struggle is framed as a fundamental conflict between decentralized freedom and authoritarian control, where open-source technology serves as the primary vehicle for individual autonomy and truth. Relevance to open data is central, as the text asserts that open-source AI models and weights are essential for preventing a surveillance state and ensuring access to uncensored information. The author warns that closed-source ecosystems rely on opacity and subscription barriers, effectively creating a cognitive embargo on the public. By contrast, open initiatives like Hugging Face provide the infrastructure for transparency, allowing developers and users to verify, modify, and distribute knowledge without institutional permission, which is critical for maintaining an open and accessible information ecosystem. The piece urges individuals to actively decentralize their tech infrastructure by downloading local copies of open models and utilizing independent hardware. This action is presented as a necessary defense against government and corporate efforts to monopolize AI capabilities and narrative control. Ultimately, the article contends that protecting open-source AI is synonymous with protecting intellectual freedom, requiring immediate personal action to preserve access to unbiased knowledge before it is restricted by centralized authorities.
