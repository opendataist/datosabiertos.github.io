Superior Court Upholds Division of Elections’ Handling of Internal Absentee Ballot Reports

Superior Court Upholds Division of Elections’ Handling of Internal Absentee Ballot Reports

This case clarifies that internal election working documents, like absentee ballot tallies, are not subject to public records requests. The ruling emphasizes that agencies need not generate new real-time reports during active voting periods, protecting operational efficiency from disruptive legal demands. This decision is crucial for open data advocates, as it defines the boundary between transparent electoral processes and necessary administrative privacy. It underscores that while final results must be public, temporary internal tools used to reach those results often require exclusion from immediate disclosure to ensure accurate election administration.
