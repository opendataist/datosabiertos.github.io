NSU L(AI)brary Series: Synthetic Data and AI (Online)

NSU L(AI)brary Series: Synthetic Data and AI (Online)

Synthetic data offers versatile applications across diverse fields, provided users critically evaluate ethical, legal, and quality implications rather than relying on specific tools. This approach is vital for open data to ensure integrity and responsible innovation. Researchers must prioritize validation and bias mitigation when integrating synthetic datasets. Understanding these limitations safeguards the reliability of scholarly work and maintains public trust in data-driven research outcomes. Adopting a cautious, principle-based perspective enables safer deployment in sensitive areas like healthcare and AI. This ensures that open data practices remain robust, ethical, and compliant with emerging regulatory standards globally.
