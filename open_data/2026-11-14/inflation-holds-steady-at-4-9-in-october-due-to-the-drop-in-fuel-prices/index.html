Inflation holds steady at 4.9% in October due to the drop in fuel prices

Inflation holds steady at 4.9% in October due to the drop in fuel prices

Spanish inflation stabilized in October, remaining the highest level recorded since early 2023. Although declines in fuel prices and a slowdown in food and clothing price increases have moderated the overall rise, inflationary pressures remain significant in key sectors such as housing and tourism, maintaining a challenging economic situation for households. This stability in the overall index contrasts with the persistence of underlying inflation at elevated levels, indicating that structural market forces continue to exert pressure on basic prices. The presence of positive increases across all autonomous communities reflects a nationwide phenomenon, with no regions exempt from this widespread price increase that affects citizens' purchasing power. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the direct impact of official statistical series on economic transparency. Public and free access to these indicators, such as the Consumer Price Index (CPI) and underlying inflation, allows civil society, academics, and analysts to independently verify the economic situation. Promoting the dissemination and use of this open data is essential for fostering an informed citizenry and accountability in public policy.
