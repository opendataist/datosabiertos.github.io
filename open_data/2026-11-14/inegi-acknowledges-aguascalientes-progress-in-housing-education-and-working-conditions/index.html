INEGI acknowledges Aguascalientes' progress in housing, education, and working conditions

INEGI acknowledges Aguascalientes' progress in housing, education, and working conditions

The 2025 Intercensal Survey conducted by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) reveals that Aguascalientes has achieved significant advances in quality of life, consolidating itself as one of the states with the best national indicators. When comparing the data with the 2020 Census, a sustained improvement is evident in basic services, education, and working conditions, demonstrating real progress in the well-being of its residents and positioning the state among the top in the country in various key aspects. The results particularly highlight the near-total universalization of piped water and the notable reduction of overcrowding in housing. Additionally, the state experienced a significant leap in digital connectivity and access to technology, as well as in the formalization of employment and social security coverage. These combined factors reflect a positive transformation in infrastructure and development opportunities for families, marking a substantial improvement compared to the situation recorded five years ago. This article is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates the value of georeferenced and disaggregated public information for territorial analysis. As the main provider of open statistical data in Mexico, INEGI generates this empirical evidence, enabling researchers, journalists, and citizens to audit government performance. The transparency of these indicators facilitates accountability and the design of evidence-based public policies, strengthening civil society through access to verifiable and detailed information.
