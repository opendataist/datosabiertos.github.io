Yolanda Paredes plantea pedir informes para averiguar sobre el gran desfasaje entre el salario mínimo y el costo de productos de la canasta familiar - Política - ABC Color

Yolanda Paredes plantea pedir informes para averiguar sobre el gran desfasaje entre el salario mínimo y el costo de productos de la canasta familiar - Política - ABC Color

A Paraguayan senator has formally requested detailed reports from the Central Bank and the National Statistics Institute to expose the severe disparity between minimum wages and the actual cost of living. This initiative aims to reveal why most families cannot meet basic needs without incurring debt, highlighting a critical failure in current economic indicators to reflect household realities. The request demands full transparency regarding the methodology, scope, and technical criteria for the upcoming Family Budget Survey. By requiring public access to anonymized micro-data and complete validation processes, the senator seeks to ensure that statistical instruments accurately capture income and expenditure patterns, thereby preventing the concealment of inflationary impacts on vulnerable populations. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity for governments to publish raw, accessible statistical information to empower citizens and legislators. When high-quality, transparent data is available, it enables evidence-based policy formulation, such as linking salary adjustments to real inflation rates, ultimately fostering accountability and more equitable social protection systems.
