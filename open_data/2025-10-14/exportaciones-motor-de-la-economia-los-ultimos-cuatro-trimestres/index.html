Exportaciones, motor de la economía los últimos cuatro trimestres:

Mexico’s economy grew modestly in the first half of 2025, driven primarily by robust exports and increased government consumption. While net exports served as the main growth engine, private investment declined sharply, signaling a concerning shift in economic momentum. This contraction in private investment reflects growing uncertainty regarding judicial reforms and the rule of law. The lack of clear regulations has triggered nervousness among investors, highlighting how political instability can directly hinder economic activity and long-term development prospects. This data is relevant to open data because transparent, timely economic statistics allow stakeholders to monitor these shifts in real time. Access to disaggregated figures from INEGI enables analysts to identify specific sectors facing challenges, fostering informed public debate and accountability regarding government spending and economic policy effectiveness.
