DGP asegura que datos personales estarán protegidos en nuevo sistema de pasaportes

DGP asegura que datos personales estarán protegidos en nuevo sistema de pasaportes

The Dominican Republic has fully implemented its new Electronic Passport, ensuring complete security for citizens' personal and biometric data. By keeping all processing strictly within national facilities and utilizing encrypted connections, the state maintains exclusive control over the database, eliminating foreign access to sensitive information. This modernization aligns the country with international civil aviation standards, significantly enhancing identity protection and document reliability. The system’s code and operations are owned entirely by the Dominican state, reinforcing national sovereignty over digital identity infrastructure and ensuring a secure, efficient travel experience for all citizens. This initiative is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how governments can balance modern digital identity systems with strict data sovereignty. It highlights a model where critical infrastructure remains publicly controlled and secure, setting a precedent for how nations can manage sensitive citizen data without compromising privacy or ceding control to external commercial entities.
