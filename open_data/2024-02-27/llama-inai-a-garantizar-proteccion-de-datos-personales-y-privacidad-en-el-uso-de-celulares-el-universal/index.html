Llama INAI a garantizar protección de datos personales y privacidad en el uso de celulares | El Universal

Llama INAI a garantizar protección de datos personales y privacidad en el uso de celulares | El Universal

The National Institute for Transparency (INAI) has emphasized that the protection of personal data and privacy must be priorities on the international agenda, especially in light of rapid technological advancement and incidents that undermine people’s dignity. This position, presented at the Mobile World Congress, underscores that respect for these rights is essential to safeguard individual autonomy in the face of complexities that transcend national borders. The regulation of emerging technologies, such as Artificial Intelligence, requires not only ethical considerations but also clear regulatory frameworks that define the involved parties, the data being transferred, and their intended purposes. Data protection authorities play a crucial role by working alongside international networks to guide the development of these tools, ensuring that their use does not produce discriminatory or harmful outcomes, but rather is based on informed consent and digital security. This article is relevant to the field of open data because it demonstrates that transparency and access to information cannot exist without robust privacy protection. By establishing rigorous standards for data handling by government and industry, an environment is created in which innovation coexists with fundamental rights. International collaboration and regulatory harmonization are essential to ensure that digital transformation promotes equity and security, preventing the openness of data from compromising citizens’ integrity.
