Estas son las predicciones de Deloitte para la industria tecnológica, IA y sostenibilidad en 2024

The report highlights that trends in technology, media, and telecommunications are key to addressing global challenges such as climate change and economic transformation. Generative artificial intelligence is solidifying its role as a driver of efficiency, where the balance between innovation and regulation, along with the use of proprietary data, will optimize costs and productivity. This suggests that data governance will become a central pillar for companies seeking competitive advantages without compromising security. Sustainability demands operational restructuring, from recycling electronic waste to modernizing telecommunications infrastructure to reduce the carbon footprint. Standardized tools for environmental and social reporting enable greater transparency, implying that structured data are essential for verifying corporate commitments and improving natural resource management in sectors such as agriculture and manufacturing. Finally, the evolution of entertainment and connectivity reflects a demand for personalization and security. The consolidation of mobile devices as authentication tools and the rise of user-generated content demonstrate that user engagement and digital trust are priorities. This context is relevant to open data because it validates the need for accessible, standardized, and secure information ecosystems that enable organizations to innovate, comply with regulations, and deliver personalized experiences in an increasingly complex and regulated digital environment.
