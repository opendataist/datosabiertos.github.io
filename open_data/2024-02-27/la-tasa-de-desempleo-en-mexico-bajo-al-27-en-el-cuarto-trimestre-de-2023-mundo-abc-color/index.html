La tasa de desempleo en México bajó al 2,7 % en el cuarto trimestre de 2023 - Mundo - ABC Color

Mexico’s recent labor data reveal a complex economic landscape in which formal job growth in commerce and services coexists with persistently high unemployment and a large informal labor sector. Although the active population has expanded significantly, more than half of the workforce remains in the informal sector, highlighting deep structural vulnerabilities that official growth rates often obscure. This distinction between formal expansion and widespread informality is crucial for open data initiatives. Transparent access to such granular socioeconomic metrics enables researchers and citizens to analyze the health of the labor market beyond headline growth figures, ensuring that policy decisions address the reality of job quality and stability rather than merely quantity. Ultimately, these statistics underscore the vital role of public data in fostering accountability. By making detailed employment indicators accessible, stakeholders can better understand disparities across regions and demographics, driving more informed discussions on economic equity and the need for a robust, open statistical infrastructure to support sustainable development.
