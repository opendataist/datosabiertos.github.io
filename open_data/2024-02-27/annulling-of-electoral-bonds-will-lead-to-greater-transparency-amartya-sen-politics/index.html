Annulling of electoral bonds will lead to greater transparency: Amartya Sen | Politics

The Supreme Court’s annulment of the electoral bonds scheme marks a pivotal shift toward electoral integrity, a development warmly endorsed by Nobel laureate Amartya Sen. By declaring the scheme a scandal, the judgment addresses systemic opacity that previously hindered citizens' ability to understand political financing. This ruling empowers the public with the right to information, fostering a more accountable democratic environment where voters can trace the origins of political support. Sen emphasizes that transparent funding is essential for counteracting the disproportionate influence of party politics and government restrictions on opposition voices. When financial contributions remain hidden, the common citizen’s voice is often drowned out by powerful entities. Restoring transparency ensures that electoral competition remains fair, allowing for genuine political freedom and equal participation rather than privileged standing for specific communities or parties. This case is highly relevant to open data because it mandates the disclosure of sensitive financial records through the State Bank of India and the Election Commission. Making this granular funding data public transforms opaque transactions into accessible information, demonstrating how open data initiatives can directly uphold constitutional rights and strengthen democratic accountability by enabling public scrutiny of political expenditures.
