Desarrollo de sistema de pago digital y más temas que tocaron Petro y la reina Máxima

Colombia and the Netherlands explored the creation of a free, interoperable digital banking system to promote financial inclusion. This initiative aims to reduce credit risk by leveraging open data, shifting evaluation criteria from property ownership to payment history, rather than relying on traditional collateral. The discussions highlighted a new approach to credit assignment based on reliability, supported by transparent data frameworks. By prioritizing data-driven decisions, the proposal seeks to broaden access for underserved populations while maintaining robust financial standards. This story is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how accessible information can revolutionize financial services. It illustrates the power of transparency in building trust and expanding economic opportunities, directly aligning with global goals for inclusive development and equitable access to credit systems.
