OpenAI desestima la demanda del NY Times y lo acusa por “piratería”

The New York Times alleges that OpenAI trained its generative AI on copyrighted works without permission, challenging the company’s defense that this constitutes fair use. This legal dispute highlights the critical tension between rapid AI development and intellectual property rights, a core issue in open data ecosystems. OpenAI argues that the reproduction occurred too long ago and lacks legal merit under the DMCA, seeking to dismiss parts of the lawsuit. This reflects the broader struggle to define how historical data usage should be regulated in the age of machine learning. Relevant to open data, these cases threaten the sustainability of public interest data practices. If training on publicly available information is deemed infringement, it could restrict access to knowledge and limit the potential benefits of transparent, shared data for societal advancement.
