Investiga INAI filtración de teléfonos de candidatas

The INAI launched an investigation into the unauthorized leak of presidential candidates’ phone numbers to identify those responsible. This action underscores the agency’s constitutional mandate to protect personal data in both the public and private sectors, reinforcing the principle that privacy is a fundamental human right that demands strict legal compliance. The incident highlights the severe risks associated with exposing private information, including the potential for illegal activities and harm to individuals’ dignity. By seeking accountability, the INAI demonstrates its commitment to protecting individuals from violations of digital privacy, ensuring that data protection mechanisms remain robust and effective. This case is crucial for discussions on open data, as it clarifies the boundary between transparency and privacy. It serves as a reminder to organizations that while data openness is essential, it must never compromise individual security, thereby establishing clear ethical guidelines for handling sensitive personal information in the digital age.
