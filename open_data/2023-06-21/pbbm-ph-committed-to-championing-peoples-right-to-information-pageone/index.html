PBBM: PH Committed To Championing People’s Right To Information | PAGEONE

President Marcos affirmed the Philippines’ commitment to the right to information, positioning transparency as a cornerstone of democratic governance. By hosting the International Conference of Information Commissioners, the nation underscores its solidarity with global efforts to promote access to public data. This stance highlights how open information empowers citizens to make informed decisions and holds representatives accountable, thereby strengthening democratic institutions and fostering a culture of trust between the government and the populace. The administration’s approach integrates digital innovation with legal frameworks to enhance governance. Through the E-Governance initiative and proposed legislation, the government aims to streamline public services and ensure data accessibility via digital platforms. This modernization effort is not merely administrative but serves as a critical mechanism for improving public service delivery. It demonstrates how leveraging technology to open government data can lead to more efficient, transparent, and responsive state operations, aligning national development plans with international standards for good governance. Furthermore, the President linked transparency directly to the fight against misinformation, emphasizing media and information literacy as essential tools for societal resilience. By treating fake news as a societal threat, the administration promotes open data as a antidote to disinformation, enabling citizens to verify facts independently. This relevance to open data lies in the realization that true information freedom requires both institutional transparency and public capability to interpret data, ensuring that open government initiatives effectively support an informed and engaged citizenry capable of sustaining a progressive society.
