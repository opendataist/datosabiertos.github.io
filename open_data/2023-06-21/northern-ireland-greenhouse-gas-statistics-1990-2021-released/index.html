Northern Ireland greenhouse gas statistics 1990-2021 released

Northern Ireland’s greenhouse gas emissions rose in 2021, reversing recent gains, yet the region still shows a significant long-term decrease compared to 1990 levels. Despite this progress, Northern Ireland’s reduction lags behind other UK nations, highlighting regional disparities in environmental performance and policy effectiveness. Agriculture remains the dominant source of emissions, followed by transport and energy supply. With most sectors experiencing an increase in output last year, the data underscores the persistent difficulty in decarbonizing key economic activities. This trend suggests that current strategies may not be sufficient to achieve ambitious climate targets without more intensive intervention. This bulletin is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular evidence of environmental impacts. By making such detailed statistical records publicly available, researchers and policymakers can analyze regional trends, hold governments accountable, and develop targeted solutions for sustainability challenges based on verified facts.
