Poco más de la mitad de usuarios con internet

Chiapas records the lowest internet usage in Mexico, highlighting a severe digital divide between regions. This disparity underscores how geographic location significantly impacts technological access, leaving marginalized communities further behind in connectivity and resources. The data reveals that a substantial portion of the population abstains from internet use primarily due to a lack of skills rather than mere infrastructure deficits. This finding shifts the focus from hardware availability to the critical need for comprehensive digital literacy programs to empower citizens. These insights are vital for open data initiatives, as they demonstrate that transparent statistics must inform policy beyond simple adoption rates. By exposing the root causes of digital exclusion, open data facilitates targeted interventions that address both infrastructure gaps and educational needs, ensuring equitable technological advancement.
