INE: la fecundidad se frenó, revela reporte preliminar del censo - Economía - ABC Color

The National Institute of Statistics reveals that Paraguay’s fertility rates have significantly declined, marking a stark contrast to mid-twentieth-century norms. This demographic shift is primarily driven by urban economic pressures and the rising cost of living, which compel couples to choose smaller families, fundamentally altering the country’s population dynamics. Although a slight post-pandemic increase occurred, the overarching trend points toward sustained lower birth rates in city centers. The total population is now estimated to be below seven million, reflecting these societal changes and the ongoing impact of modernization on family planning decisions across the nation. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it highlights the necessity of accessible, transparent demographic statistics. By digitalizing census records and sharing insights on fertility and population size, institutions empower researchers and policymakers to analyze trends accurately, ensuring that public resources align with evolving social realities.
