Karnataka CM pays rich tributes to former PM Manmohan Singh

The article honors Manmohan Singh’s legacy as a humble yet transformative economist who steered India through critical economic reforms. His commitment to honest hard work and moral steadfastness allowed him to implement policies that stabilized the economy while establishing vital social safety nets. This dual achievement of fostering high growth and protecting vulnerable populations remains a cornerstone of his historical impact. Singh’s approach to governance emphasized inclusivity, ensuring that development benefited both the middle class and the poor. By introducing landmark legislation and schemes, he bridged the gap between economic liberalization and social welfare. His leadership demonstrated that economic progress and ethical governance are not mutually exclusive, offering a model of public service that prioritizes national welfare over personal power. This tribute is relevant to open_data because Singh’s reforms laid the institutional groundwork for greater transparency and accountability in governance. Initiatives like the Right to Information act empowered citizens to access information, a principle fundamental to open data ecosystems. His legacy highlights how data-driven policy and transparent administration can effectively address societal challenges, ensuring that economic tools serve the broader public interest rather than just elite interests.
