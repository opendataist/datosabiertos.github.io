Gobierno Estatal garantiza apertura y transparencia | Revista Punto de Vista

The State Comptroller of San Luis Potosí offers free online courses on Open Government and Open Data to promote citizen participation and optimize public management. These initiatives aim to strengthen transparency, innovation, and accountability by fostering a culture of openness among officials and the general public. By empowering participants with knowledge in these areas, the administration seeks to improve communication and facilitate citizen proposals that proactively transform public administration. The training supports a more informed society, enabling individuals to contribute effectively to governance improvements through accessible and continuous self-managed learning. This effort is relevant to open data as it demonstrates a proactive institutional commitment to transparency and civic engagement. By making open data concepts accessible, the state encourages active public involvement in monitoring and enhancing government performance, ensuring that data serves as a tool for accountability and democratic participation.
