“Without borders but never without you”: RSF launches a donations campaign to defend the right to information

Global press freedom is crumbling as journalists face increasing violence, arbitrary imprisonment, and censorship across multiple conflict zones. This hostile environment severely threatens the public’s ability to access accurate information, highlighting the urgent need for protective measures to sustain investigative journalism worldwide. The proliferation of unchecked disinformation further compounds these challenges, eroding trust in reliable news sources. International organizations are responding by providing emergency support, legal aid, and safety equipment to endangered reporters, aiming to shield them from persecution and help them continue their critical work despite the risks. This situation is vital to open data because it underscores the essential link between a free press and transparent information flow. Protecting journalists ensures that data remains verified, accessible, and unbiased, fostering an informed society where citizens can rely on trustworthy sources rather than manipulated narratives or propaganda.
