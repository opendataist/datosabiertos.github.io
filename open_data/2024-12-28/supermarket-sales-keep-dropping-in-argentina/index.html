Supermarket sales keep dropping in Argentina

Argentine consumers face severe economic hardships, evidenced by plummeting retail sales across supermarkets, malls, and wholesale sectors despite rising nominal prices driven by inflation. This decline indicates a significant contraction in real consumption power and ongoing financial distress for households. Indebtedness among the average consumer is continuously increasing, while payment methods remain heavily reliant on physical interactions and credit, with virtual transactions representing a small fraction of total sales. The persistence of face-to-face dominance suggests limited digital adoption amidst these economic pressures. This data is vital for open_data initiatives as it highlights the necessity for accessible, transparent statistical resources. Understanding such granular economic indicators allows researchers and policymakers to better analyze market trends, consumer behavior, and the structural challenges within emerging economies.
