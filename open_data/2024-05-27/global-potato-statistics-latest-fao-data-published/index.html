Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production data from the FAO provides a critical, standardized benchmark for understanding agricultural output across nearly a hundred nations. This comprehensive dataset reveals that while China and India dominate total volume, efficiency varies significantly by region. For researchers and policymakers, these figures offer a reliable baseline for comparative analysis, even though slight discrepancies may exist compared to official national reports. The availability of this granular information highlights the importance of accessible, centralized databases in facilitating global food security monitoring. By enabling users to independently verify and explore statistics, the platform empowers stakeholders to conduct their own queries rather than relying solely on summarized reports. This transparency supports more accurate modeling of supply chains and resource allocation strategies worldwide. This article is highly relevant to open data advocates because it demonstrates how publicly available, standardized metrics can drive informed decision-making in agriculture. It underscores the value of machine-readable, freely accessible datasets that allow for transparency and reproducibility in scientific and economic research. Ultimately, such resources bridge the gap between raw statistical collection and actionable global insights, proving that open data is essential for monitoring food production trends effectively.
