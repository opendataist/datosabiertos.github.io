El Departamento de Salud realiza una encuesta online para evaluar el Plan de Salud Mental de Navarra 2019-2023

El Departamento de Salud realiza una encuesta online para evaluar el Plan de Salud Mental de Navarra 2019-2023

The Department of Health has launched a participatory evaluation of the 2019–2023 Mental Health Plan to guide future strategies. By gathering diverse perspectives, authorities aim to identify key achievements and areas needing improvement. This collaborative approach ensures that emerging policies are directly shaped by feedback from service users and professionals. The insights gained will inform the development of the next strategic plan, prioritizing equity and human rights. This initiative is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how transparent, public platforms can facilitate civic engagement. It highlights the value of using open channels to collect qualitative insights that drive evidence-based public policy.
