Juventud mexicana: entre el riesgo y la esperanza

Juventud mexicana: entre el riesgo y la esperanza

The article highlights the critical vulnerability of Mexico’s large youth demographic, emphasizing that insufficient opportunities and unsafe environments drive young people toward crime and substance abuse. It argues that creating accessible spaces for healthy social interaction is a fundamental state responsibility to safeguard the nation’s future and prevent the loss of this generation’s potential to organized crime. Beyond government intervention, the narrative stresses that strengthening the social fabric begins within the family unit. Parents and relatives play an indispensable role in transmitting ethical values and providing a protective shield against negative external influences. By fostering communication and commitment, families serve as the first line of defense, ensuring that young people develop the resilience needed to resist harmful societal pressures. This piece is relevant to open data because it underscores the urgent need for granular, transparent data on youth demographics and social conditions to inform effective public policy. Without accessible information on where vulnerabilities exist and how resources are distributed, stakeholders cannot coordinate the collective effort required. Open data enables the evidence-based decision-making necessary to design targeted interventions that address both state obligations and community-driven solutions.
