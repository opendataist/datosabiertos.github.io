Día de la Madre: ¿Cómo dan a luz las peruanas hoy? Cambios y desafíos en la atención del parto | RPP Noticias

Día de la Madre: ¿Cómo dan a luz las peruanas hoy? Cambios y desafíos en la atención del parto | RPP Noticias

The article highlights a significant shift in Peru toward humanized childbirth, prioritizing maternal well-being and autonomy through diverse modalities such as vertical birth, epidurals, and continuous companionship. This evolution reflects a move away from clinical isolation toward an integrated approach in which the mother, family, and medical team collaborate to create a supportive, less stressful environment during delivery. Educating patients and their companions during prenatal care is presented as crucial for the successful implementation of these practices. The emphasis is on informed consent and understanding the physiological and psychological benefits of options such as delayed cord clamping, which supports infant health, or choosing between vaginal and cesarean delivery based on specific needs rather than routine protocols. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the societal impact of transparent health metrics and the democratization of medical information. As data on birth trends, such as the rise in first-time mothers and regional variations, become publicly accessible, it empowers citizens to advocate for personalized care standards. This transparency drives systemic improvements in healthcare policy, ensuring that statistical trends translate into tangible enhancements in patient rights and service quality.
