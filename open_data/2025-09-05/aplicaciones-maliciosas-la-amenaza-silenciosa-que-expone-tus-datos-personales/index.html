Aplicaciones maliciosas: la amenaza silenciosa que expone tus datos personales

Aplicaciones maliciosas: la amenaza silenciosa que expone tus datos personales

Mobile security experts warn that malicious apps and phishing pose critical threats to personal data privacy in Chile, with fraud attempts reaching thousands daily. Recent cases of loan scams and removed apps highlight the urgent need for users to verify app sources and monitor device behavior for unauthorized access. This issue directly impacts open data by demonstrating how individual security failures undermine broader data integrity and public trust. When personal information is compromised through compromised devices, it creates vulnerabilities in the digital ecosystem, affecting the reliability of data collected and shared in open systems. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity of secure foundational infrastructure. Ensuring user devices are protected against malware preserves the confidentiality and authenticity of data streams. Without robust individual security measures, the integrity of open data initiatives remains at risk, emphasizing that privacy protection is a prerequisite for reliable open information ecosystems.
