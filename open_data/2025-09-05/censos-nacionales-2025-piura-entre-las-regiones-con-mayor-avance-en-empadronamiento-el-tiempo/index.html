Censos Nacionales 2025: Piura entre las regiones con mayor avance en empadronamiento - El Tiempo

Censos Nacionales 2025: Piura entre las regiones con mayor avance en empadronamiento - El Tiempo

The National Institute of Statistics and Informatics has reached a significant milestone in the 2025 Census, confirming that field operations are proceeding normally. Regional data reveals accelerated progress, with specific departments and districts leading the count. This real-time tracking ensures transparency and allows for immediate oversight of the extensive workforce deployed nationwide. Crucially, the implementation of a centralized monitoring platform enables the supervision of thousands of census takers in real time. This technological infrastructure guarantees control over the process, ensuring that data collection is systematic and verifiable. Furthermore, enhanced security protocols coordinated with local police and authorities protect staff in high-risk areas, maintaining operational continuity across all regions. This transparency and structured data collection are vital for open data ecosystems. By providing accurate, timely, and geographically distributed demographic information, the census creates a foundational resource for public analysis. The resulting data empowers policymakers to design evidence-based strategies and enables citizens to access reliable statistics for social and economic planning.
