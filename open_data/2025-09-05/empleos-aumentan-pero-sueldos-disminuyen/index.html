Empleos aumentan, pero sueldos disminuyen

Empleos aumentan, pero sueldos disminuyen

Chiapas recorded minimal employment growth in early 2025, significantly lagging behind national averages and leading states. This modest expansion highlights regional disparities in labor market dynamics, where local gains failed to match the broader national trend of job creation, underscoring structural challenges in sustaining workforce expansion within the state. More critically, real wages declined, indicating that workers earned less despite inflation, placing Chiapas among the worst-performing entities nationally. This negative shift in remuneration contrasts sharply with substantial salary increases in other regions, revealing a deteriorating economic reality for local employees and widening the income gap compared to more prosperous states. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the necessity of accessible, granular statistical reports like INEGI’s MTPTREF. By providing transparent, high-frequency metrics on employment and earnings, such datasets enable researchers and policymakers to identify severe regional inequalities, monitor economic health, and formulate targeted interventions to address labor market imbalances.
