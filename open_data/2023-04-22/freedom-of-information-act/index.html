Freedom of Information Act

Illinois Eastern Community Colleges actively promotes transparency by strictly adhering to the Illinois Freedom of Information Act. This commitment ensures that the public can access government records while simultaneously safeguarding individual privacy. By providing clear guidance on record requests, the institution reinforces the democratic principle that citizens have a fundamental right to understand how public educational resources are managed and spent. The college facilitates this openness through a structured process for obtaining documents, ranging from annual budgets to board meeting minutes. While some information is easily accessible online, others require formal requests processed by a designated FOIA Officer. This systematic approach allows for efficient handling of diverse inquiries, ensuring that both standard citizens and commercial entities can retrieve necessary data within established timeframes, provided they adhere to legal requirements regarding purpose and fees. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the practical mechanics of public record access within a specific institutional context. It highlights the bridge between legal statutes and actual data availability, showing how organizations can balance transparency with administrative efficiency. For those interested in open data ecosystems, understanding these procedural frameworks is crucial for developing policies that maximize information utility while protecting sensitive personal information.
