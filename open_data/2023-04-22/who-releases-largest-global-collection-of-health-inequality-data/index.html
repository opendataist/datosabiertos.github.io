WHO releases largest global collection of health inequality data

The WHO’s Health Inequality Data Repository reveals that global health inequities are narrowing, significantly reducing the wealth gap in service coverage for vulnerable populations. By enabling precise targeting of resources, this initiative moves beyond simple counts to highlight who is left behind, proving that eliminating such disparities could save millions of lives annually. However, current data remains insufficiently disaggregated across critical socioeconomic and geographical dimensions. Most available statistics still lack the nuanced breakdowns necessary to identify specific marginalized groups, limiting our ability to fully understand and address the complex patterns of health disparities that persist across different income levels and regions. This repository is crucial for open data because it champions the use of granular, diverse datasets to drive health equity. It underscores the urgent need for broader transparency and richer data inclusion, ensuring that policymakers can leverage detailed information to leave no one behind in the pursuit of universal health coverage and sustainable development.
