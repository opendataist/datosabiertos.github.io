¿Quiénes están ganando con la IA? la gran discusión sobre derechos de autor y contenido en internet

¿Quiénes están ganando con la IA? la gran discusión sobre derechos de autor y contenido en internet

The viral trend of generating images with AI highlights a significant, often overlooked environmental cost. High computational demands require substantial water usage for cooling GPUs, creating a sustainability crisis. This revelation is crucial for open data as it underscores the hidden ecological impacts of data processing infrastructure. Ethical concerns also surround copyright and data sourcing. Companies train models using publicly available internet content, raising questions about ownership and benefit distribution. This practice challenges the notion that the internet remains a shared resource, emphasizing the need for transparent data usage policies in open data ecosystems to protect creators. Finally, the proliferation of AI tools complicates the landscape of information authenticity. The ease of creating convincing synthetic media necessitates greater public education and critical thinking. For open data, this highlights the importance of promoting digital literacy and verifiable sources to combat misinformation and ensure that data-driven insights remain trustworthy and accessible.
