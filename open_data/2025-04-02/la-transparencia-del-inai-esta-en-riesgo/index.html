La transparencia del INAI está en riesgo

La transparencia del INAI está en riesgo

The disappearance of the INAI has left citizens vulnerable, as no authority can resolve appeals regarding state and municipal resource management in Tamaulipas. This fragmentation disperses oversight functions, weakening the guarantee of access to information and increasing risks of government opacity. The new structure limits citizen recourse for local disputes, as federal entities only handle federal resources. Consequently, accountability mechanisms are diluted, allowing more reasons to withhold public data, including sensitive financial information. This case is crucial for open data because it highlights how institutional dismantling directly threatens transparency. It demonstrates that without strong, independent oversight bodies, public information becomes less accessible, undermining the fundamental principles required for an open and accountable society.
