¿Somos tan pobres?

¿Somos tan pobres?

The article argues that reducing poverty to mere monetary lack is insufficient, as individuals can possess financial resources while still lacking essential services like clean water, health, and education. It highlights that true poverty encompasses the inability to satisfy these basic human needs, regardless of one’s income level or the country's GDP. This perspective is illuminated by recent government statistics revealing that the vast majority of households must store water due to unreliable infrastructure and contamination risks. Even if water is technically treated, its inconsistent delivery and quality issues indicate a deep structural deficiency that monetarist metrics often miss. This distinction is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how transparent, multidimensional datasets can reveal hidden inequalities. By making nuanced indicators publicly accessible, stakeholders can better understand and address complex social realities beyond simple economic aggregates, fostering more effective and equitable policy interventions.
