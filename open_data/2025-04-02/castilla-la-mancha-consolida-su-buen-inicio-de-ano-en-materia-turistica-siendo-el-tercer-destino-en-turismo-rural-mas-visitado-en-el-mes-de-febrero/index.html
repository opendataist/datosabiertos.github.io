Castilla-La Mancha consolida su buen inicio de año en materia turística siendo el tercer destino en turismo rural más visitado en el mes de febrero

Castilla-La Mancha consolida su buen inicio de año en materia turística siendo el tercer destino en turismo rural más visitado en el mes de febrero

Castilla-La Mancha has emerged as a leading destination for rural tourism, securing a significant market share and ranking as the country’s third most visited region for this sector in February. This performance highlights the region’s growing appeal and its status as the fifth autonomous community in terms of rural tourism dynamism, marking a historic high for the area during this month. The sustained growth in traveler numbers and overnight stays indicates a robust recovery and increased demand for regulated accommodation across the early months of the year. By exceeding previous records for both visitors and nights stayed in January and February combined, the region demonstrates a strong upward trend in hospitality consumption and regional attractiveness. This article is relevant to open_data as it relies on official statistics published by the National Statistics Institute (INE). Analyzing such open government data allows researchers and stakeholders to track tourism trends, validate economic claims, and understand the impact of regional policies on mobility and accommodation patterns across Spain.
