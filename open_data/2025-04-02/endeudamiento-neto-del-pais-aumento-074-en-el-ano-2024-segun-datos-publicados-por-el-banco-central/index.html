Endeudamiento neto del país aumentó 0,74% en el año 2024, según datos publicados por el Banco Central

Endeudamiento neto del país aumentó 0,74% en el año 2024, según datos publicados por el Banco Central

Public sector net debt rose slightly in 2024, reaching 40% of GDP. This modest increase occurred despite a decline in gross debt, as reductions were outweighed by corresponding drops in reserve assets. The structural shift highlights how asset management directly influences the true financial burden of the state. Reserve assets grew significantly, partially offsetting gross borrowing costs. Meanwhile, external debt decreased, signaling improved relations with international creditors, whereas domestic liability patterns remained stable. These diverging trends reveal a complex landscape where currency denomination and residency dictate risk exposure. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of transparent, granular public financial reporting. By making such detailed metrics accessible, citizens and researchers can critically analyze fiscal sustainability. True openness requires not just raw figures, but clear definitions of net versus gross liabilities to prevent misleading interpretations of economic health.
