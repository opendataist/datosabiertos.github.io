High-rise developments taking half a year to be approved - PropertyWire

High-rise developments taking half a year to be approved - PropertyWire

The Building Safety Regulator’s approval processes are significantly exceeding statutory timeframes, causing substantial delays in high-rise construction and refurbishment. These bottlenecks stem from staffing shortages and operational inefficiencies, despite the regulator's efforts to streamline workflows and expand its multidisciplinary teams. Such delays threaten the viability of construction projects by increasing costs, straining cash flow, and jeopardizing national housing targets. While building safety remains paramount, the industry warns that persistent administrative hurdles undermine economic stability and project timelines, necessitating urgent improvements to balance safety rigor with operational efficiency. This article highlights a critical tension in open data governance: the need for transparent, accessible regulatory performance metrics. By revealing the gap between policy targets and actual execution, it underscores how open data can expose systemic inefficiencies, holding authorities accountable and driving evidence-based reforms in public service delivery.
