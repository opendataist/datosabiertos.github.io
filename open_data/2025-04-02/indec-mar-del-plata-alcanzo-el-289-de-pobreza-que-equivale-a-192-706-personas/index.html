Indec: Mar del Plata alcanzó el 28,9% de pobreza, que equivale a 192.706 personas

Indec: Mar del Plata alcanzó el 28,9% de pobreza, que equivale a 192.706 personas

Recent statistics reveal a significant improvement in socioeconomic conditions in Mar del Plata in late 2024, positioning it among the urban areas with the lowest poverty and extreme poverty rates in the country. This decline indicates that a substantial number of residents have risen above critical survival thresholds compared to the previous year, suggesting localized economic resilience despite broader national challenges. The data show a sharp reduction in both poverty and extreme poverty levels within the city over the past twelve months. These figures not only reflect relative success in maintaining social stability but also highlight a distinct disparity between Mar del Plata and the national average, where adverse conditions remain far more prevalent. This contrast underscores the importance of regional analysis in understanding complex economic trends. This information is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how accessible, standardized statistical records enable precise geographic comparisons and longitudinal tracking of social welfare. By making detailed census data publicly available, researchers and policymakers can identify specific local successes, challenge national generalizations, and advocate for targeted interventions based on empirical evidence rather than aggregate assumptions.
