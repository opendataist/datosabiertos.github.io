Social Security Org completes over 81,000 transactions in H1 2024

Digital adoption surged as the Public Institution for Social Security shifted significantly toward electronic channels for transactions and certifications. This trend highlights a critical move toward transparent, accessible public service delivery, a core principle of open data initiatives. By digitizing these processes, the institution enhances data availability and efficiency for citizens and businesses. Such widespread use of digital infrastructure demonstrates the practical value of open, machine-readable data in modern governance. Ultimately, this shift supports broader open data goals by ensuring public services are transparent and easily auditable. It encourages further integration of digital systems to improve accountability and user experience.
