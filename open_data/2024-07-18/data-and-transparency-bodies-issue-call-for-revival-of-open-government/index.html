Data and transparency bodies issue call for ‘revival’ of open government

Data and transparency bodies issue call for ‘revival’ of open government

Civil society leaders have urged the new UK government to anchor its administration in open government principles, framing transparency as essential for democratic integrity. They argue that robust openness is not merely a procedural formality but a foundational mechanism for reducing corruption, enhancing institutional accountability, and rebuilding public trust. By prioritizing these values, the government can ensure officials act in the public interest and foster a more equal and just society. The coalition highlights that previous scandals and political opacity have severely damaged confidence in public institutions, creating an urgent need for revitalization. They posit that open government drives tangible benefits, including economic growth through technological innovation and more efficient public services. The letter emphasizes that achieving these outcomes requires strong political leadership and active collaboration with civil society to address inequality and improve policy development. This initiative is highly relevant to the open data community because it advocates for the systemic use of data and technology as tools for co-creation and policy refinement. By aligning with the Open Government Partnership, the push for open government validates the critical role of transparent data in combating corruption and enhancing procurement integrity. It signals a political opportunity to institutionalize data openness as a standard practice for rebuilding trust and ensuring government actions are visible, verifiable, and aligned with public needs.
