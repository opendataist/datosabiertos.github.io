No las descargues: estas son las extensiones de Chrome que ponen en jaque tu privacidad

The article reveals that many Chrome extensions pose significant privacy risks, potentially compromising confidential user data and enabling its sale to third parties. This highlights a critical vulnerability in the open ecosystem, where unverified third-party tools can undermine user trust and data security. Experts warn against downloading extensions lacking proper security verification, as they may access sensitive information like banking transactions without consent. The incident involving extensions that improperly shared browsing history underscores the dangers of unchecked third-party access. This story is relevant to open data because it illustrates how open platforms can be exploited if security standards are not strictly enforced. It emphasizes the need for transparency and robust verification processes to protect user privacy within open data environments, ensuring that accessibility does not come at the cost of security.
