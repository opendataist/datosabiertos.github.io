Lanzan el nuevo Portal de Datos de Biodiversidad de Argentina con más de 17 millones de registros, una herramienta estratégica para la ciencia y la conservación - Argentina Forestal

Argentina has officially launched a comprehensive digital biodiversity data portal, a collaborative effort involving CONICET and the National Node of the Global Biodiversity Information Facility. This platform aggregates millions of georeferenced records, images, and bibliographic sources from various national and international institutions into a single, unified access point. By centralizing historical natural collections, ecological studies, and citizen science data, the initiative transforms fragmented information into a cohesive, globally accessible resource. The platform is significant because it adheres strictly to FAIR and CARE principles, ensuring that data is findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable while respecting the rights of indigenous communities. All content is licensed under Creative Commons with persistent digital object identifiers, facilitating proper attribution and seamless integration with global networks. This structured approach not only enhances transparency but also encourages a robust network of curation and collaboration among scientists, policymakers, and the general public. This development is highly relevant to open data movements as it demonstrates the practical application of open licensing and standardized data management in environmental science. By making vast amounts of biological information freely available, the portal supports critical applications in conservation, biomedicine, and climate change mitigation. It exemplifies how transparent data sharing can drive innovation and solve complex societal challenges, proving that open access is essential for effective scientific research and policy-making.
