Inflación: Marco Lavagna adelantó que en dos meses se publicará el nuevo IPC

The government is preparing to launch a revised Consumer Price Index with a significantly expanded scope, aiming to enhance the reliability and transparency of official inflation data. This technical update reflects an institutional commitment to providing accurate statistics that foster public trust in economic indicators. The new methodology incorporates a larger sample of prices and informants, addressing historical gaps and ensuring broader geographic representation. By moving beyond previous limitations, the initiative seeks to align current measurement practices with long-standing statistical traditions, thereby improving the precision of economic analysis. This development is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how rigorous data collection standards can improve the quality of publicly available economic information. Transparent and robust datasets are essential for independent verification, allowing citizens and analysts to better understand inflation trends and hold institutions accountable for their fiscal reporting.
