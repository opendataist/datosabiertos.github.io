CSO Figures Show Over 5.1 Million People Live In Ireland - Tipperary Mid West Radio

Ireland’s population reaches record highs driven by unprecedented immigration and natural growth, with Dublin housing nearly a third of residents. The significant influx of diverse nationals highlights shifting demographic trends and increased reliance on international migration for sustained expansion. These dynamics directly impact open data initiatives by requiring granular, accessible datasets that reflect diverse community needs. Transparent population metrics are essential for equitable resource allocation and understanding societal changes. Accurate open data supports policy decisions in housing and infrastructure, ensuring services adapt to rapid growth. By making this information freely available, stakeholders can better address challenges arising from demographic shifts and urban concentration.
