The article highlights a significant rise in the cost of basic food and total living expenses in Argentina, driven by high inflation. This economic pressure increases the risk of families falling into poverty and extreme poverty, as essential needs become harder to afford without excluding housing costs. The data reflects a sharp deterioration in purchasing power over recent months, with annual increases exceeding 300%. Such volatility directly impacts social indicators, showing that a substantial portion of the population lives below the poverty line, emphasizing the urgency of economic stabilization measures. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how official statistics from INDEC provide transparent, accessible metrics on socioeconomic conditions. By publishing detailed cost baskets and poverty rates, it enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to independently verify social realities and hold institutions accountable for policy effectiveness.
