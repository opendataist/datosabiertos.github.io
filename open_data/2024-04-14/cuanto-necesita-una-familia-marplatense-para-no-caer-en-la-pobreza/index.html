Cuánto necesita una familia marplatense para no caer en la pobreza

Recent inflation data reveals a severe economic impact, highlighting the stark contrast between the cost of basic survival and rising living expenses. This situation underscores the critical need for accessible statistical transparency to monitor household welfare and inform public policy. Reliable open data is essential for accurately defining poverty lines and understanding the real value of wages versus essential goods. Without this clarity, addressing social inequality remains difficult, as citizens and institutions cannot fully grasp the extent of financial strain on vulnerable families. Consequently, the publication of detailed economic indicators serves a vital democratic function. It empowers society to demand effective responses to inflation, ensuring that decision-makers have the precise metrics needed to protect citizens' purchasing power and social stability.
