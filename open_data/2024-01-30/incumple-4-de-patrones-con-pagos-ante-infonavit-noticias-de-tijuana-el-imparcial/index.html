Incumple 4% de patrones con pagos ante Infonavit | Noticias de Tijuana | El Imparcial

Incumple 4% de patrones con pagos ante Infonavit | Noticias de Tijuana | El Imparcial

The article highlights that while most employers in Baja California comply with Infonavit contributions, a small segment exhibits patterns indicative of evasion. This non-compliance jeopardizes workers’ ability to access housing credits and causes them to lose accumulated points, emphasizing the direct impact of employer obligations on employees’ financial well-being. Authorities use fiscal penalties and account immobilization to enforce compliance, aiming to protect workers’ rights. Recent condonation programs encourage regularization, ensuring that the workforce remains eligible for essential housing support without interruption due to administrative failures by their employers. This issue is crucial for open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible reporting on employer compliance rates. By making data on contribution statuses and evasion patterns publicly available, stakeholders can monitor labor rights enforcement more effectively and hold institutions accountable for protecting vulnerable workers.
