Oman posts increase in production of refineries and petroleum products

Oman’s petroleum sector demonstrated robust growth in late 2023, driven by significant surges in exports of liquefied petroleum gas, paraxylene, and gasoline. While overall refinery output rose modestly, the divergent performance of specific fuels highlights shifting demand patterns, with M-91 seeing strong domestic sales growth despite reduced exports. This dynamic underscores the complexity of modern energy markets, where certain products face declining domestic consumption or export volumes while others experience rapid expansion. The substantial increases in paraxylene and aviation fuel production suggest a strategic pivot toward higher-value petrochemicals and specialized fuels, reflecting evolving industrial needs and global trade opportunities. These statistics are relevant to open data because they illustrate how transparent, granular industrial metrics enable precise tracking of economic trends. Access to such detailed production and export figures allows analysts to identify sectoral shifts, assess policy impacts, and forecast market movements, thereby enhancing the utility of public data for economic planning and research.
