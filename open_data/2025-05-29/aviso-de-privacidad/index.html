Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

This document outlines 13 MEDIA, S.C.’s privacy policy, demonstrating how organizations must transparently communicate their data-handling practices to comply with legal standards. By explicitly detailing the types of personal information collected and the specific purposes for their use, the company illustrates the principle of informed consent. This transparency is crucial for maintaining public trust, ensuring that individuals understand exactly what data is being gathered and why it is necessary for service provision. The policy emphasizes users’ control over their data, highlighting their rights to access, rectify, cancel, or object to the processing of personal information. By providing clear channels for communication and limiting the transfer of data to third parties only when legally required, the organization models responsible data stewardship. This approach respects the principles of data minimization and purpose limitation, which are fundamental components of ethical data management frameworks. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of accountability and transparency in data ecosystems. Even when data is not openly shared, the mechanisms for user oversight and regulatory compliance serve as a foundation for broader data governance standards. Understanding how private entities manage sensitive information helps establish benchmarks for integrity and security, which are equally critical when handling open datasets to ensure ethical usage and protect individual rights.
