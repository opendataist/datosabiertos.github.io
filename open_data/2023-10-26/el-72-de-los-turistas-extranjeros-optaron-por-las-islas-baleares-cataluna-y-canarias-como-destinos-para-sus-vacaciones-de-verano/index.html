El 72% de los turistas extranjeros optaron por las Islas Baleares, Cataluña y Canarias como destinos para sus vacaciones de verano

The hotel sector is experiencing increased profitability driven by lower average prices, making accommodations significantly more accessible to families, particularly in the budget and luxury segments. This trend highlights a market shift where cost reductions have successfully boosted overall revenue and occupancy rates. Catalonia emerges as a premier destination for both domestic and international tourists, maintaining high occupancy levels above the national average. The region’s strong performance underscores its appeal and capacity to sustain growth, attracting a substantial share of visitors during the peak summer season. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates how public statistical indicators can reveal critical market dynamics, such as price elasticity and regional competitiveness. Analyzing such open datasets enables stakeholders to identify emerging trends, assess economic health, and inform strategic decisions regarding tourism infrastructure and policy implementation.
