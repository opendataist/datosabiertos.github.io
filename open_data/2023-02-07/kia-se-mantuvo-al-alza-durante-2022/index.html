Kia se mantuvo al alza durante 2022

Kia achieved record market penetration in Europe, driven significantly by a substantial surge in electrified vehicle sales. This growth highlights the automotive industry's rapid transition toward sustainable mobility and reduced carbon emissions. The brand’s success with hybrid and electric models underscores consumer demand for greener transportation alternatives. This shift reflects broader environmental priorities and regulatory pressures within the European market. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public industry statistics reveal macroeconomic trends. Transparent access to such sales and environmental performance metrics enables researchers and policymakers to analyze sustainability progress effectively.
