FGR debe informar sobre órdenes de aprehensión

The INAI ruled that the Attorney General’s Office must disclose details regarding arrest warrants related to the Ayotzinapa case, rejecting arguments for secrecy. This decision reinforces the principle that transparency is essential for the ongoing investigation and the victims' families, ensuring that justice is not obscured by administrative opacity or claims of confidentiality. Access to accurate information serves as a critical tool for guaranteeing fundamental human rights, including the right to justice, memory, and truth. By mandating the release of these records, the ruling emphasizes that investigative bodies must operate under public scrutiny, preventing bias and ensuring that state actions remain accountable to society during long-standing human rights violations. This case is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a legal precedent for treating investigative records as public assets rather than restricted government secrets. It demonstrates how open data frameworks can actively support transitional justice, proving that releasing sensitive but necessary information is not a security risk, but a democratic obligation that empowers citizens to verify official narratives and demand accountability.
