Oracle to invest $1.5 billion in Saudi Arabia, open data centre in Riyadh, Telecom News, ET Telecom

Oracle has committed to a significant multi-year investment in Saudi Arabia, focusing on expanding its cloud infrastructure by establishing a new public cloud region in Riyadh. This strategic move aims to accelerate data transfer speeds and meet growing regional demand for cloud computing services, reinforcing the company’s technological footprint in the Middle East. Simultaneously, Oracle is aligning with Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030 economic transformation goals by addressing local regulations that require foreign firms to establish regional headquarters. By securing government contracts and supplying cloud capabilities to major initiatives like NEOM, the company demonstrates how international tech entities can comply with national sovereignty laws while supporting local economic diversification efforts. This development is highly relevant to the open data movement as it highlights the critical intersection of data residency requirements and global cloud accessibility. The case underscores the ongoing challenge of balancing national data sovereignty with the principles of open, borderless information flow, showing how cloud infrastructure development must adapt to local regulatory frameworks to ensure both security and interoperability in a digitally connected world.
