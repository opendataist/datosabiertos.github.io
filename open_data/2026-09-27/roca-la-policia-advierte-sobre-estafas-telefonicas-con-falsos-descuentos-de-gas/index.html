Roca: la Policía advierte sobre estafas telefónicas con falsos descuentos de gas

Roca: la Policía advierte sobre estafas telefónicas con falsos descuentos de gas

Authorities warn of phone scams in which fraudsters impersonate utility providers to extract personal and banking information from victims. The scheme involves tricking users into downloading remote-access software, which enables thieves to carry out unauthorized transfers and obtain loans in the victim’s name, highlighting the critical vulnerability of digital financial data to social engineering attacks. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible personal and financial information can be exploited when privacy safeguards fail. It underscores the need for robust data protection policies and transparency regarding how citizens’ data is used, ensuring that public trust is not undermined by fraudulent actors abusing digital channels. Police emphasize verifying all offers through official channels and refusing any requests for sensitive codes or remote access. Citizens are advised to immediately terminate communication upon receiving suspicious calls, reinforcing the importance of individual vigilance and secure data-handling practices to prevent significant financial losses and identity theft.
