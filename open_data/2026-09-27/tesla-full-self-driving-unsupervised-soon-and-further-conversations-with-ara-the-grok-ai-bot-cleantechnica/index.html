Tesla Full Self Driving Unsupervised Soon? And Further Conversations with Ara (the Grok AI Bot) - CleanTechnica

Tesla Full Self Driving Unsupervised Soon? And Further Conversations with Ara (the Grok AI Bot) - CleanTechnica

The article illustrates how proprietary software ecosystems, such as Tesla’s Full Self-Driving, can obscure the broader implications of data accessibility. While the author praises the advanced automation capabilities, the restriction of these features behind expensive monthly subscriptions highlights a significant barrier to widespread public benefit. This model prioritizes profit over safety, potentially excluding users who cannot afford the technology, despite its potential to significantly reduce traffic fatalities. The lack of open access to the underlying algorithms prevents independent verification and democratization of this critical safety technology. Furthermore, the integration of conversational AI like Grok into vehicle interfaces demonstrates how corporate data silos limit user interaction. Although the AI appears sophisticated, its functionality is governed by proprietary constraints and variable access limits rather than transparent, open protocols. This underscores the importance of open data in fostering trust and ensuring that artificial intelligence tools operate fairly and consistently for all users, rather than being treated as exclusive, controlled services. Relevance to open data lies in the urgent need for transparency in autonomous systems. If self-driving technology is truly intended to save lives, the data driving these decisions should be subject to open scrutiny and standardization. Keeping such vital safety improvements behind paywalls and closed-code systems contradicts the principles of open data, which advocate for shared knowledge that benefits society as a whole rather than restricting it for corporate gain.
