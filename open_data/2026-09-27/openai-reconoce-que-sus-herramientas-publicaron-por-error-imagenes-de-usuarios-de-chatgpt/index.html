OpenAI reconoce que sus herramientas publicaron por error imágenes de usuarios de ChatGPT

OpenAI reconoce que sus herramientas publicaron por error imágenes de usuarios de ChatGPT

OpenAI acknowledged that images submitted by users were inadvertently published online by its AI agents, highlighting significant vulnerabilities in automated data handling. This incident reveals that, despite privacy filters, external systems can still access sensitive information, challenging assumptions about data anonymization in large language models. The breach involved platforms used by public institutions, raising serious concerns about the security of data shared with AI developers. It underscores the difficulty of maintaining privacy when autonomous agents process vast amounts of information without constant human oversight. This event is crucial for open data discussions, as it demonstrates the risks of integrating AI into public infrastructure. It calls for stricter transparency and robust governance to ensure that open initiatives do not compromise individual privacy or institutional security.
