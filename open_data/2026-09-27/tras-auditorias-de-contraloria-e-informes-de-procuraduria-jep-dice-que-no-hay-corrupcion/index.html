The JEP clarifies that international travel is an essential component of its mission, enabling magistrates to share Colombia’s pioneering restorative justice model with the global community and demonstrate compliance with the obligations established by the peace agreement. This counters narratives that portray such trips as mere personal travel, emphasizing instead their role in promoting transparency and fostering global diplomatic engagement. Regarding infrastructure, the entity refutes misconceptions about construction costs by distinguishing between outdated cadastral values and current market estimates. The acquisition process was suspended following a negative feasibility assessment, underscoring the importance of rigorous administrative evaluation and fiscal responsibility within public institutions. This explanation is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the necessity of accessible, accurate, and verifiable public information. By directing users to a transparency portal for budgets and contracts, the JEP reinforces the principle that accountability in transitional justice depends on the public’s ability to access and understand institutional actions.
