Momentum seeks answers to works being carried out in Gozo's valleys - The Malta Independent

Momentum seeks answers to works being carried out in Gozo's valleys - The Malta Independent

Momentum has submitted a Freedom of Information request to uncover the details of cleaning and rehabilitation works in Gozo’s valleys, highlighting a significant transparency gap regarding environmental interventions. The party argues that the public deserves access to comprehensive documentation, including ecological studies, technical advice, and evidence of how protected species were safeguarded during these operations. This demand underscores a critical open data principle: citizens must be able to scrutinize government actions affecting natural resources to ensure accountability and informed public discourse. In response, the Ministry for Gozo asserts that the projects followed formal, transparent processes involving public tenders and necessary permits from relevant authorities. They emphasize that mechanical machinery was essential for clearing substantial amounts of waste and invasive species, which compete with indigenous flora. The ministry claims these efforts aim to improve biodiversity and manage rainwater more effectively, thereby reducing flood risks. By pointing to existing official systems where tender documents are available, the government suggests that the data required for verification is already accessible, challenging the narrative that information is entirely hidden from public view. This exchange is highly relevant to open data advocates as it illustrates the tension between proactive transparency and reactive disclosure. The conflict centers on whether scattered permit records constitute sufficient public access or if a centralized, easily understandable repository of environmental impact reports is needed. It highlights the necessity for governments to not only release data but to present it in a way that allows citizens to fully understand the ecological trade-offs and decisions made in sensitive rural areas.
