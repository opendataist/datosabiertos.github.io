The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

InQuest Science has developed a specialized software solution that transforms how pharmaceutical manufacturers manage product lifecycles, particularly for parenteral and medical device production. By integrating visual inspection practices with comprehensive data tracking, the system bridges critical regulatory gaps left by broader Manufacturing Execution Systems. It enables companies to monitor manufacturing trains for inconsistencies, establish realistic control limits, and ensure robust oversight from raw materials to finished products, thereby reducing the risk of batch recalls and enhancing overall product quality. The platform’s core strength lies in its specialized approach to defect management and inspection qualification, which addresses the unique precision requirements of global pharmaceutical standards. Unlike generic MES platforms, this software offers a digital defect library and rigorous training modules that accurately identify particulate and physical defects. It streamlines the qualification of both human inspectors and automated vision systems, ensuring they meet stringent regulatory expectations while minimizing errors through automated documentation and reduced reliance on manual, paper-based data entry. This solution is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the shift toward secure, standardized digital repositories for critical quality data. By replacing fragmented spreadsheets with an integrated, audit-trail-enabled database, the system improves data integrity and accessibility. It highlights how structured, open-accessible digital frameworks can facilitate transparent historical evaluation, support real-time managerial decision-making, and ensure compliance with complex regulatory environments like FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and USP guidelines.
