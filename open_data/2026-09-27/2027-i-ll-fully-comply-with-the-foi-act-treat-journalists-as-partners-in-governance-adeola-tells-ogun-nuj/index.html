2027: I'll fully comply with the FOI Act, treat journalists as partners in governance - Adeola tells Ogun NUJ

Senator Solomon Olamilekan Adeola has pledged full compliance with the Freedom of Information Act if elected governor of Ogun State, establishing transparency as a foundational element of his administration. By committing to substantive press briefings and regular accountability mechanisms, he positions the media as a governance partner rather than an administrative burden. This approach underscores a commitment to open governance, where public scrutiny is welcomed as a tool for integrity and progress, directly supporting the principles of data accessibility and governmental accountability central to open data initiatives. Adeola’s broader platform integrates significant structural reforms aimed at modernizing state operations and enhancing public service delivery. His agenda includes digital infrastructure, financial system modernization, and expanded access to essential services, all of which rely on accurate, accessible data for effective implementation. By emphasizing fiscal discipline and civil service reform, the proposed administration seeks to create an environment where data-driven decision-making can flourish, ensuring that resources are allocated efficiently and public records are maintained with the highest standards of accuracy and availability. This article is highly relevant to open data as it highlights a political leader’s explicit willingness to embrace transparency and institutionalize information sharing. The promise to treat the press as a partner implies a systemic shift toward making government processes and outcomes visible to the public. Such commitments are critical for fostering a culture of openness, enabling citizens and researchers to access reliable information, and holding institutions accountable through the free flow of data.
