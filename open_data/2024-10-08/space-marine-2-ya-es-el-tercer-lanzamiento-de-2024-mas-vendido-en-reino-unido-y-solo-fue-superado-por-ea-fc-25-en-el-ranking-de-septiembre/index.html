Space Marine 2 ya es el tercer lanzamiento de 2024 más vendido en Reino Unido, y solo fue superado por EA FC 25 en el ranking de septiembre

Space Marine 2 ya es el tercer lanzamiento de 2024 más vendido en Reino Unido, y solo fue superado por EA FC 25 en el ranking de septiembre

The UK video game market showed resilience in September 2024, with software sales rising by eight percent compared to the previous year. This growth underscores strong consumer demand, fueled by the successful launches of major titles such as EA Sports FC 25 and Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II. The presence of six new games in the top ten indicates that fresh content continues to drive consumer spending, while strategic pricing on older catalog titles also maintains significant market relevance. A notable shift occurred in hardware sales, as Xbox Series X|S surpassed Nintendo Switch for the first time. Despite a substantial year-over-year decline in overall console unit sales, the PlayStation 5 Digital Edition recorded its second-best monthly performance since its launch. This evolving hardware landscape points to a diversification in console preferences, with players adjusting their spending habits even as total hardware revenue contracts compared to previous years. This data is highly relevant to open-data initiatives, as it highlights the importance of accessible, transparent industry metrics from sources such as the GSD. By publicly releasing sales figures, industry analysts enable independent researchers, journalists, and developers to conduct accurate market analyses. This transparency fosters an evidence-based understanding of consumer trends, allowing stakeholders to make informed decisions about game development, pricing strategies, and hardware distribution without relying solely on proprietary corporate data.
