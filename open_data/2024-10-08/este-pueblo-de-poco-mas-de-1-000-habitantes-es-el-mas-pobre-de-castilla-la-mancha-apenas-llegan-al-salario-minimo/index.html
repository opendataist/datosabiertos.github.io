Este pueblo de poco más de 1.000 habitantes es el más pobre de Castilla-La Mancha: Apenas llegan al salario mínimo

The Ministry of Finance released detailed municipal income data, highlighting significant disparities within Castilla-La Mancha. Although regional average income rose, the area remains among Spain’s poorest, underscoring persistent socioeconomic inequalities across different localities. The data reveals a stark contrast between the wealthiest and poorest municipalities. This variance illustrates how economic well-being is unevenly distributed, affecting local development and resource allocation at the granular municipal level. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it exemplifies the need for accessible, transparent government statistics. By publishing granular fiscal records, authorities empower researchers and citizens to analyze regional disparities, fostering accountability and informed decision-making based on precise, publicly available economic indicators.
