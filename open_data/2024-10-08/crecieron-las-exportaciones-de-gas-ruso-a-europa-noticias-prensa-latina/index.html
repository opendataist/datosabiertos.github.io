Crecieron las exportaciones de gas ruso a Europa - Noticias Prensa Latina

Crecieron las exportaciones de gas ruso a Europa - Noticias Prensa Latina

Russian gas exports remained robust in the first three quarters, driven by increased shipments via TurkStream and stable transit through Ukraine. This continuity is largely attributed to sustained demand from the European Union, which persists due to favorable market pricing conditions for major suppliers like Gazprom. Experts anticipate that these high demand levels will endure through the year, despite rising costs linked to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East. The market shows resilience as prices stabilize at significant levels, prompting predictions of further moderate increases in the final quarter of the year. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it highlights how transparent tracking of energy flows and pricing reveals economic dependencies. By analyzing such metrics, researchers can better understand the correlation between geopolitical conflicts, infrastructure usage, and global energy market stability.
