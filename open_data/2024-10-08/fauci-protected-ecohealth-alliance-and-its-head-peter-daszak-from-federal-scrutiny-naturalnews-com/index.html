Fauci protected EcoHealth Alliance and its head Peter Daszak from federal scrutiny – NaturalNews.com

Fauci protected EcoHealth Alliance and its head Peter Daszak from federal scrutiny – NaturalNews.com

The article asserts that Dr. Anthony Fauci and the NIAID shielded EcoHealth Alliance and its leader from federal scrutiny, allowing the organization to receive significant public funding without disclosing critical data regarding its collaboration with Chinese laboratories. This protective stance persisted despite internal requests for transparency and external investigations into gain-of-function research, suggesting a systemic failure in oversight that prioritized the continuation of grants over rigorous accountability and data sharing during a global health crisis. This narrative is relevant to open_data because it highlights the dangers of opaque scientific funding and restricted information access. When key organizations withhold genomic data and lab records from public and governmental scrutiny, it impedes the ability of the scientific community to independently verify findings, assess biosafety risks, and understand the origins of emerging pathogens. True open data practices require that publicly funded research remains accessible to ensure transparency, facilitate peer review, and maintain public trust in scientific institutions. The text concludes that despite early protections, external pressure eventually led to debarment proceedings against EcoHealth Alliance, signaling a shift toward greater accountability. The persistence of former NIAID allies in influential positions, coupled with recent actions by the Department of Health and Human Services, underscores the long-term consequences of initial secrecy. Ultimately, the article argues for stricter adherence to open data principles to prevent future cover-ups and ensure that pandemic preparedness relies on verified, accessible information rather than protected proprietary secrets.
