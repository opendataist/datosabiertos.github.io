The 2023 West African Epidemiology Network on Drug Use (WENDU) Report: Statistics and Trends on Illicit Drug Supply and Drug Use Officially Released

The 2023 West African Epidemiology Network on Drug Use (WENDU) report launch underscores the urgent need for robust, evidence-based regional strategies to combat rising illicit drug supply and addiction. By highlighting emerging threats like Kush, the data serves as a critical call to action for ECOWAS member states, urging them to transition from lagging responses to proactive public health and security measures. This initiative demonstrates how coordinated epidemiological networks can effectively translate raw health statistics into actionable policy frameworks. The collaboration between regional bodies, governments, and international partners illustrates a model for leveraging shared data to foster stronger regional cooperation and mitigate the societal impacts of substance abuse across borders. For the open data community, this event highlights the vital role of standardized, timely data dissemination in shaping effective public policy. It reinforces the importance of accessible, high-quality datasets in driving evidence-based decision-making, particularly in complex transnational health challenges where clear, unified information is essential for implementing targeted interventions and fostering sustainable regional development.
