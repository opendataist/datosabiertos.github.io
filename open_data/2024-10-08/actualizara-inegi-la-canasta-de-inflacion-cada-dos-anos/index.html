Actualizará Inegi la canasta de inflación cada dos años

The INEGI updates inflation indices based on household spending data to reflect post-pandemic economic shifts. These adjustments ensure accurate measurement by incorporating newly relevant products and changing consumer behaviors. This transparency aligns with open data principles by improving the reliability and accessibility of essential economic indicators for public scrutiny.
