CEFIM ofrece páginas web para municipios

CEFIM ofrece páginas web para municipios

The state government of San Luis Potosí collaborates with numerous municipalities to provide free, secure web hosting and email accounts. This initiative ensures compliance with transparency laws, enabling local governments to reliably publish required information and maintain an open government presence. This centralized service significantly reduces budget expenditures for individual municipalities while guaranteeing data security. It is particularly vital for remote areas with connectivity challenges, offering technical support and capacity-building programs. As a result, it serves as a successful national model for subsidizing digital infrastructure to support administrative obligations. This program is highly relevant to open data, as it removes the financial and technical barriers that often prevent municipalities from publishing data. By standardizing and securing the platforms where information is published, the state ensures that transparency mandates are effectively met. It demonstrates how shared digital infrastructure can facilitate broader access to public information across all levels of local government.
