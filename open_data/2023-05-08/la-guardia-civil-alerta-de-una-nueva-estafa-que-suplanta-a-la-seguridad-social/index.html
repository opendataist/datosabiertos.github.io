La Guardia Civil alerta de una nueva estafa que suplanta a la Seguridad Social

This article highlights a widespread smishing campaign in which fraudsters impersonate government agencies to steal personal and banking information through deceptive text messages. It urges citizens to recognize these tactics and promptly report any compromise to prevent identity theft and financial loss. The guidance emphasizes proactive measures, such as blocking senders and avoiding suspicious links, while advising victims to collect evidence and monitor for misuse of their information. This proactive approach is essential for mitigating the long-term consequences of identity theft and exercising digital rights, such as the right to be forgotten. This content is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical intersection between public communication channels and personal information security. It underscores the need for transparent, reliable sources of information and robust digital hygiene to protect individuals from the exploitation of their data in an increasingly interconnected world.
