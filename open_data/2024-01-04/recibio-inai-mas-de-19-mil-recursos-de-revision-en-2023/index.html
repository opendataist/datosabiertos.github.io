Recibió Inai más de 19 mil recursos de revisión en 2023

The high volume of complaints indicates significant systemic barriers to accessing public information in Mexico. Major institutions face numerous reviews, revealing persistent failures in transparency and the protection of personal data rights. This friction highlights a disconnect between legal guarantees and administrative practices. It underscores the urgent need for stronger mechanisms to ensure citizens can effectively obtain information and control their personal data without excessive bureaucratic resistance. This case is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates that the technical availability of data is insufficient. True open government requires proactive transparency and respect for privacy, ensuring that information systems are not only functional but also trustworthy and accessible to the public.
