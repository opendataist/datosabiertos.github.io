How much did Bolton Council spend on energy in 2022/23?

Bolton Council’s energy expenditures initially appeared excessive compared to peers, highlighting a critical lack of industry benchmarks. This transparency gap means many local authorities remain unaware if they are overpaying for utilities. The revelation underscores the urgent need for standardized performance metrics to allow councils to assess their procurement efficiency and negotiate better rates against market averages. The disparity in energy costs stems from varying hedging strategies and purchasing timings rather than solely external crises. While the energy market shock affected many, significant price variations suggest that strategic procurement choices play a decisive role in final bills. This insight demonstrates that proactive management can mitigate costs, even during volatile market conditions, proving that high prices are not always inevitable. This report is highly relevant to open data as it advocates for greater market transparency. By making procurement data public, stakeholders can hold public bodies accountable and drive systemic change. Such openness empowers communities and businesses to demand fairer pricing, illustrating how accessible, comparable data serves as a powerful tool for advocacy and improved public service efficiency.
