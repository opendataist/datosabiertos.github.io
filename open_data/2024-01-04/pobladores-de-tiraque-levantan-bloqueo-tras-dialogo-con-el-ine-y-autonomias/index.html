Pobladores de Tiraque levantan bloqueo tras diálogo con el INE y Autonomías

Pobladores de Tiraque levantan bloqueo tras diálogo con el INE y Autonomías

Residents of Tiraque lifted road blockades after negotiating with state officials over cartographic standards for the upcoming census. They reached an agreement to use the 2012 census map and to involve local personnel in data collection, resolving the dispute through technical coordination. This case highlights the critical role of accurate geographic data in administrative processes. It underscores the importance of open data practices, as discrepancies in official maps can directly affect community rights, resource allocation, and the validity of national statistical surveys.
