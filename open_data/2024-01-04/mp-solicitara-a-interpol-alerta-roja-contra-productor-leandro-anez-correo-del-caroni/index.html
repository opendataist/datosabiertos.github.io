MP solicitará a Interpol alerta roja contra productor Leandro Añez - Correo del Caroní

The Venezuelan Public Ministry has initiated procedures to issue a Red Notice through Interpol for Leandro Añez, alias Kapu. He is wanted for the alleged embezzlement of musical royalties from the albums of the late singer Canserbero, having fled the country while facing charges that include perjury and criminal association. This action underscores the state’s intent to pursue justice across borders in cases involving prominent cultural figures. This development extends previous investigations into the rapper’s death, linking financial crimes to the broader context of his life and work. By targeting Añez, authorities aim to recover assets derived from the artist’s legacy, highlighting the complexity of managing intellectual property after death. The case illustrates how legal mechanisms are employed to address economic grievances tied to artistic estates. This situation is relevant to open data because it reveals the opacity surrounding the distribution and control of cultural revenues. Transparency regarding royalty flows and the legal tracking of assets is essential for accountability. The lack of accessible information on how these funds are managed or returned to heirs undermines public trust. Open data initiatives could help demystify these processes, ensuring that the commercial aspects of cultural heritage are visible and subject to scrutiny, thereby promoting fairness and preventing illicit enrichment.
