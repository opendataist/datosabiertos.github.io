OpenAirportMap es una especie de enciclopedia de aeropuertos con detallados mapas y datos abiertos

Open Airport Map provides a comprehensive, open-data visualization of global airport infrastructure, integrating detailed elements such as runways, taxiways, control towers, and navigation aids. By combining sources like OpenStreetMap, it offers an encyclopedic view of aviation facilities, satisfying both enthusiasts and professionals with precise geometric and operational data. The platform’s relevance to open data lies in its successful aggregation of disparate geographical and technical datasets into a unified, accessible format. This demonstrates how community-driven initiatives can address informational gaps, transforming obscure structural details into clear, verifiable public knowledge without relying on proprietary restrictions. Ultimately, this tool highlights the power of open information to resolve real-world mysteries, such as identifying obsolete navigation beacons that remain hidden from casual observation. It proves that transparent data sharing not only supports specialized interests but also enhances general understanding of complex infrastructure, fostering a more informed society.
