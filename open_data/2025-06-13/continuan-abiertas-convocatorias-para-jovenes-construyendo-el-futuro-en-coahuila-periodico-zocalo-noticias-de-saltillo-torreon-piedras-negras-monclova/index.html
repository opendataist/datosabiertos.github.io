Continúan abiertas convocatorias para Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro en Coahuila | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The Jóvenes Construyendo el Futuro program remains open in Coahuila, offering vulnerable youth vocational training and economic support to facilitate their labor market insertion. This initiative prioritizes inclusive professional development, ensuring that young people without current studies or employment gain essential skills and financial stability through structured internship opportunities. Such social programs highlight the critical importance of open data in public administration by requiring transparent, accessible information regarding eligibility criteria and available vacancies. Open data enables citizens to verify program details and monitor resource distribution effectively. This transparency is relevant to open_data because it empowers users to navigate bureaucratic processes autonomously. By making registration portals and vacancy lists publicly accessible, governments foster accountability and ensure that essential social benefits reach their intended beneficiaries without unnecessary barriers or information asymmetry.
