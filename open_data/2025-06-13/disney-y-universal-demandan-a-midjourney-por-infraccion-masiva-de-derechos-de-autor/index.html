Disney y Universal demandan a Midjourney por infracción masiva de derechos de autor

Disney y Universal demandan a Midjourney por infracción masiva de derechos de autor

Disney and Universal have filed a major copyright lawsuit against AI company Midjourney, alleging that the firm illegally used protected creative works to train its image generation software. This legal action highlights the growing tension between traditional intellectual property rights and the data ingestion methods required for modern artificial intelligence, marking a significant moment in the ongoing debate over ethical AI development and content ownership. The studios argue that Midjourney’s model relies on unauthorized reproductions of iconic characters, disregarding previous cease-and-desist orders. This case underscores the critical importance of transparent data sourcing in open data initiatives. It raises fundamental questions about whether using publicly available web data for commercial AI training constitutes fair use or infringement, challenging existing frameworks for data licensing and ethical AI practices. This lawsuit is highly relevant to the open data community as it establishes a potential precedent for how creative datasets can be used. The outcome may influence future regulations regarding data accessibility and the legal boundaries of training algorithms. Ultimately, it pressures AI developers to adopt more responsible data governance, ensuring that open access to information does not come at the expense of creators' rights, thereby shaping the future of interoperable and legally sound data ecosystems.
