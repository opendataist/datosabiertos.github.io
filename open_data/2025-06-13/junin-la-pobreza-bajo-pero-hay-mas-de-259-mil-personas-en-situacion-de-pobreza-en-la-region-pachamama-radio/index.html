Junín: La pobreza bajó pero hay más de 259 mil personas en situación de pobreza en la región | Pachamama Radio

Junín: La pobreza bajó pero hay más de 259 mil personas en situación de pobreza en la región | Pachamama Radio

In 2024, Junín achieved a significant reduction in poverty, positioning it as one of the regions with the most notable progress. This decline indicates that economic growth, particularly in agriculture and construction, successfully generated accessible jobs for vulnerable populations. However, despite this improvement, poverty levels remain critically high, underscoring that economic expansion alone is insufficient without formal labor policies and social protections to ensure sustainable well-being. The region continues to grapple with high labor informality and multidimensional poverty factors. Many residents lack access to quality education, healthcare, and adequate infrastructure, which perpetuates the cycle of poverty regardless of income. Structural barriers prevent individuals from escaping vulnerability, demonstrating that measuring poverty solely through monetary thresholds overlooks essential aspects of human development and social equity. This article is relevant to open data because accurate, transparent statistical monitoring is crucial for identifying persistent social gaps. Open data initiatives enable the disaggregation of multidimensional poverty indicators, allowing policymakers to move beyond income-based metrics. By making this detailed information publicly accessible, stakeholders can advocate for targeted interventions that address structural deficiencies and measure the true impact of social policies over time.
