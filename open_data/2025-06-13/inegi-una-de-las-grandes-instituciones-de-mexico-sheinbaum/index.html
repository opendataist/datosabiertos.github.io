INEGI, una de las grandes instituciones de México: Sheinbaum

INEGI, una de las grandes instituciones de México: Sheinbaum

Sheinbaum confirmed that INEGI will now evaluate poverty in Mexico, replacing Coneval. She argued that this makes sense since INEGI already conducts the key household survey. She emphasized that continuity is guaranteed and urged trust in the institution’s technical expertise. This shift centralizes critical social statistics within a single agency. It highlights how government data production evolves with administrative changes. This matters for open data because INEGI handles vital public information. Understanding these institutional responsibilities helps users navigate data sources. It shows how policy decisions directly impact where and how statistical evidence is published.
