Disney y Universal demandan por derechos de autor a Midjourney, generador de fotos por IA

Disney y Universal demandan por derechos de autor a Midjourney, generador de fotos por IA

The lawsuit filed by Disney and Universal against Midjourney marks a pivotal moment in the debate over copyright and artificial intelligence. By alleging that the company trained its models using unauthorized creative works, this legal action highlights the critical tension between the rapid advancement of generative AI technologies and the protection of intellectual property rights. The core implication for open data concerns the ethical sourcing and transparency of training datasets. If AI systems are deemed to infringe upon creators' rights by using copyrighted material without permission, it challenges the industry standard of freely aggregating vast amounts of visual data. This case forces a reevaluation of whether open-access methodologies for data collection can coexist with legal frameworks designed to protect original artistic content. Ultimately, the outcome of this dispute will set a significant precedent for how future AI companies handle data usage. It underscores the urgent need for clear guidelines that balance innovation with respect for creators, influencing the development of more responsible and legally compliant open data practices in the AI sector.
