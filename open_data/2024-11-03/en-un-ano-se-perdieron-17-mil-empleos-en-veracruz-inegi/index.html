En un año, se perdieron 17 mil empleos en Veracruz: INEGI

The decline in Veracruz’s employment rate signals economic contraction, emphasizing the urgent need for accurate labor data to guide recovery policies. This shift highlights the critical role of open data in monitoring regional stability and informing public interventions effectively. Reliable statistics enable stakeholders to understand workforce dynamics, fostering transparency and accountability. Without accessible open data, assessing such economic changes remains opaque, hindering evidence-based decision-making for social welfare and business planning. Consequently, this article underscores the importance of open data initiatives in democratizing information. It demonstrates how public access to official statistics empowers communities and institutions to address employment challenges proactively and collaboratively.
