Buscan crear una Red de Colaboración Interreligiosa a través de un proyecto de Gobierno Abierto

This initiative establishes a collaborative network between the State and faith-based organizations to promote interreligious dialogue and strategic alliances. By leveraging the diverse territorial presence of religious groups in health and education, the project aims to strengthen peace and sustainable development through shared public objectives. The program is relevant to open data, as it relies on systematic sampling and pilot results to validate its scalability and effectiveness. This approach underscores the importance of transparent, evidence-based governance, in which public institutions actively engage with community sectors to improve the visibility and coordination of services. Ultimately, the pilot seeks to refine tools that enhance the dissemination of these collaborative actions. By focusing on innovation and inclusive participation, the project demonstrates how open governmental practices can successfully integrate diverse societal actors into the public policy framework for broader social benefit.
