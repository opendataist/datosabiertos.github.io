Empeora calidad del aire en Saltillo; viento, posible causante | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Air quality in Saltillo deteriorated due to rising levels of PM10 and PM2.5, breaking from recent stable trends. This anomaly, which has persisted since early morning, is likely caused by winds from a cold front stirring up dust and pollen, rather than by typical traffic patterns. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly accessible environmental data allows users to correlate real-time pollution metrics with specific meteorological events, thereby promoting transparent public awareness and a deeper understanding of local ecological impacts.
