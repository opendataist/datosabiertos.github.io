SmolLM2

SmolLM2 introduces compact, on-device capable language models licensed under Apache 2.0, promoting accessible AI by allowing researchers and developers to run powerful models locally without heavy infrastructure. The models are trained on diverse, high-quality datasets including educational and coding data, ensuring robust performance across various tasks while maintaining efficient resource usage. This open approach encourages experimentation and adaptation within the community. This release is highly relevant to open data as it provides freely accessible weights and training methodologies, fostering transparency and enabling users to inspect, modify, and contribute to the model's development for broader innovation.
