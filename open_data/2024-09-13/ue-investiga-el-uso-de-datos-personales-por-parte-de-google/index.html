UE investiga el uso de datos personales por parte de Google

Google is under investigation by Irish regulators to determine whether it complied with its obligations under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) when using personal data to train AI models. This inquiry underscores the critical need for impact assessments to safeguard fundamental rights in the face of rapid technological advancement. The case is significant for open data, illustrating how privacy laws intersect with the ethical use of public information in the development of artificial intelligence.
