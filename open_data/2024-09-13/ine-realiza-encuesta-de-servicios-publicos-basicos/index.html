INE realiza encuesta de servicios públicos básicos

The INE conducts the ENCASBA 2024 to evaluate public satisfaction with essential services and perceptions of transparency. By employing probabilistic sampling, the institute ensures statistical precision and minimizes selection bias in its findings. This rigorous methodology guarantees representative data, enabling an accurate assessment of infrastructure and administrative quality across the country. Confidentiality protections further safeguard the integrity of the collected information. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates how standardized, unbiased statistical processes produce reliable public metrics. Such transparency empowers citizens to hold institutions accountable based on verified evidence rather than anecdotal reports.
