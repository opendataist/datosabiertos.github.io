Calvo se muestra activo con municipios y lanza otra edición de “Gobierno Abierto” para implementar en la provincia

Calvo se muestra activo con municipios y lanza otra edición de “Gobierno Abierto” para implementar en la provincia

Córdoba has launched its second Open Government Action Plan for the 2024–2027 period, reaffirming its commitment to transparency and citizen participation through close collaboration among the state, civil society, and universities. This ongoing effort aims to consolidate a more citizen-centered governance model by integrating co-creation and oversight mechanisms that ensure long-term predictability in provincial public policies. The plan’s core pillars include transforming the government portal into a true open data hub aligned with international standards, co-creating a law on access to public information, and extending these practices to the municipal level. These initiatives not only centralize fragmented information but also foster a culture of data as a service, facilitating its reuse by citizens and strengthening intergovernmental accountability. This strategy is crucial for the open data ecosystem because it institutionalizes information openness as both a right and a public service, aligning with the global standards of the Open Government Partnership. By prioritizing the technical and legal accessibility of data, Córdoba sets a replicable precedent that empowers civil society to actively audit and collaborate with institutions, demonstrating how structural transparency enhances democratic quality and administrative efficiency.
