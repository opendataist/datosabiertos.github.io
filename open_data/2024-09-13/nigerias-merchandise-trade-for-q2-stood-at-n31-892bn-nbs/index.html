Nigeria’s merchandise trade for Q2 stood at N31.892bn – NBS

Nigeria’s merchandise trade volume shifted significantly in mid-2024, driven by a substantial year-on-year surge in export values alongside a notable decline in import volumes compared to the preceding quarter. This data reveals a complex economic landscape where reliance on crude oil remains dominant, yet the overall trade balance exhibits dynamic fluctuations that impact national economic indicators. The findings highlight a continued heavy dependence on oil revenues for export earnings, with non-oil exports playing a smaller, albeit growing, role. By tracking these shifts in trade partners and commodity values, researchers can analyze the country’s economic resilience and external dependency trends more accurately. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides a standardized, transparent record of national economic performance. Access to such granular trade statistics enables independent verification, supports data-driven policy analysis, and fosters greater accountability in how international commerce impacts local development and fiscal planning.
