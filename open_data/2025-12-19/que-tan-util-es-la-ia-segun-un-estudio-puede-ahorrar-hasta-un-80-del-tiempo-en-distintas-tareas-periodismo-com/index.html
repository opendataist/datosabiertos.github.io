¿Qué tan útil es la IA? Según un estudio, puede ahorrar hasta un 80% del tiempo en distintas tareas - Periodismo . com

¿Qué tan útil es la IA? Según un estudio, puede ahorrar hasta un 80% del tiempo en distintas tareas - Periodismo . com

A comprehensive analysis by Anthropic reveals that generative artificial intelligence has the potential to radically transform the economy by significantly boosting labor productivity. By examining thousands of real-world interactions, researchers determined that AI-assisted tasks are completed much faster than manual ones, potentially driving unprecedented annual productivity growth in the United States—reaching the upper end of current economic projections. However, the impact is neither uniform nor absolute, as notable disparities exist depending on the type of occupation and the presence of operational “bottlenecks.” While complex cognitive roles, such as legal management or curriculum planning, experience massive time savings, tasks requiring physical presence or human interpersonal judgment remain unchanged. This suggests that future growth will be constrained not by technological capabilities, but by essential tasks that AI still cannot optimize, thereby avoiding a simplistic view of technological determinism. This report is crucial for the open data movement because it demonstrates the value of using real, anonymized data to measure technological impact in real time. Although the study employs privacy-preserving methodologies, its analytical approach highlights the need for precise metrics validated against actual work tickets. This methodological transparency and the use of empirical data reinforce the importance of source accessibility and verification in modern economic research, fostering a more nuanced understanding of digital transformation beyond speculative predictions.
