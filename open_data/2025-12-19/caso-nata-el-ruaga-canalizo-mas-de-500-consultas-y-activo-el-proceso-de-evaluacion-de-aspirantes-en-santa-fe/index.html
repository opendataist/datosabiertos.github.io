Caso Nata: el Ruaga canalizó más de 500 consultas y activó el proceso de evaluación de aspirantes en Santa Fe

Caso Nata: el Ruaga canalizó más de 500 consultas y activó el proceso de evaluación de aspirantes en Santa Fe

The viral visibility of a teenager’s adoption wish has significantly increased public awareness and inquiries regarding the waiting list for older children and siblings in Santa Fe. This surge demonstrates how individual stories can effectively mobilize potential adoptive families, highlighting the critical need for broader societal engagement with children who remain in the system due to their age or family composition. The adoption process involves rigorous interdisciplinary evaluations and judicial oversight, meaning immediate resolutions are not feasible despite public enthusiasm. However, recent data indicates a positive trend, with a notable increase in adoption proceedings for older children in recent years, suggesting that sustained visibility and accessible information are gradually overcoming traditional barriers to adopting youth and sibling groups. This situation is highly relevant to open data because the successful management of public adoption registries depends on transparent, accessible, and up-to-date information systems. Making waiting lists and eligibility criteria publicly available online empowers citizens to act, while open data practices ensure that the system remains accountable and efficient, ultimately facilitating faster and more informed connections between waiting children and prospective families.
