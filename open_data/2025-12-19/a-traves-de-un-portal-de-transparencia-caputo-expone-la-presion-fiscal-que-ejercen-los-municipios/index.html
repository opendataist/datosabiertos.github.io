A través de un portal de transparencia, Caputo expone la presión fiscal que ejercen los municipios

The agreement aims to reduce tax burdens and simplify daily life by promoting transparency across national, provincial, and municipal levels. The government launched a portal disclosing municipal tax rates to enhance the visibility of fiscal efforts and encourage commerce. This initiative aligns with open data principles by making public financial information accessible. It fosters accountability and informed decision-making by exposing local tax structures that were previously obscured. Ultimately, this transparency supports democratic oversight. By disclosing these fiscal details, the state empowers citizens and businesses to understand the true cost of doing business, thereby reducing opacity in public administration.
