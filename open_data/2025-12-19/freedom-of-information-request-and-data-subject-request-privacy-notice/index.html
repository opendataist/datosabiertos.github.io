Freedom of Information request and Data Subject request privacy notice

Freedom of Information request and Data Subject request privacy notice

This privacy notice outlines the legal framework under which the UK Cabinet Office processes personal data to handle freedom of information and data subject access requests. It emphasizes that processing is driven by statutory obligations and public interest tasks, ensuring transparency in how government bodies manage citizen inquiries. By clarifying the legal basis for handling sensitive information and criminal records, the document demonstrates a commitment to lawful data stewardship while fulfilling regulatory duties. The notice details how data is shared across government departments to coordinate responses to multi-departmental requests and stored via third-party processors. It highlights the importance of cross-agency collaboration in maintaining efficient public service delivery, while simultaneously addressing data security and retention policies. This approach illustrates the practical complexities of managing shared personal data within large public sector structures, balancing operational needs with privacy protections. This article is relevant to open data because it establishes the boundary between public transparency and individual privacy. It clarifies that while government data should be open, personal information requires strict legal justification and protection. Understanding these constraints is essential for developing open data initiatives that respect GDPR compliance, ensuring that public access to information does not infringe upon citizens’ rights or security.
