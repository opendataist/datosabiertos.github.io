Inflación en Estados Unidos cede inesperadamente a 2.7% en noviembre

Inflación en Estados Unidos cede inesperadamente a 2.7% en noviembre

Inflation in the United States decelerated less than expected, coming in below analysts’ forecasts. This outcome highlights companies’ ability to absorb part of the costs generated by new tariffs, avoiding fully passing them on to consumers and thereby mitigating the immediate impact on retail prices. An examination of underlying data reveals relative stabilization, although households continue to face pressure from generally high prices. The gap between inflation and wage growth, combined with expectations of larger tax refunds, suggests an economic environment that could allow the Federal Reserve to cut interest rates. This report is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how transparency in the publication of macroeconomic indicators directly influences market confidence and the formulation of monetary policy. The timely and accessible release of this data enables analysts to adjust their projections in real time, showing that the availability of accurate information is essential for informed economic decision-making and for maintaining the accountability of government institutions.
