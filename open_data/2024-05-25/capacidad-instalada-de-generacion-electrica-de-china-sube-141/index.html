Capacidad instalada de generación eléctrica de China sube 14,1%

Capacidad instalada de generación eléctrica de China sube 14,1%

China has significantly expanded its total electricity generation capacity, driven by rapid growth in wind and solar power. This surge reflects a strategic commitment to renewable energy and a massive increase in investment for both generation projects and grid infrastructure. This expansion is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible, and standardized energy statistics. Reliable public data on power capacities and investments enables researchers, investors, and policymakers to monitor sustainability progress and make informed decisions about the green transition. Ultimately, publishing these figures publicly supports accountability in climate action. It allows for better analysis of investment trends and infrastructure development, ensuring that the transition to clean energy is both efficient and openly verifiable for global scrutiny and local planning.
