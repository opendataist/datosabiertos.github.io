La FAMP recibe la visita de participantes del proyecto OD4GROWTH, que aborda la mejora de los datos en abierto

La FAMP recibe la visita de participantes del proyecto OD4GROWTH, que aborda la mejora de los datos en abierto

The OD4GROWTH project, involving regional partners across Europe, has convened in Seville to evaluate how open data drives local socioeconomic development. The gathering emphasized transforming current limitations into strengths, highlighting that accessible information is crucial for creating a more sustainable and intelligent Europe. By sharing best practices from nations like France, Italy, and Hungary, participants demonstrated how open data simplifies daily life, enhances tourism, and optimizes public services. A core conclusion is that improving the quality and availability of open data directly boosts public service standards and stimulates innovation within small and medium-sized enterprises. Practical applications showcased in Andalusia, such as unified digital street directories and smart tourism offices, illustrate how detailed, updated data facilitates emergency responses and strategic planning. These examples prove that robust data infrastructure enables predictive analytics and better decision-making for both entrepreneurs and municipal authorities. This initiative is relevant to open data because it validates its role as a catalyst for regional growth and policy improvement. By focusing on interregional cooperation and capacity building, the project aims to embed open data into the fabric of smart specialization strategies. Ultimately, it demonstrates that leveraging open information not only strengthens local economies but also fosters a future European ecosystem that is more interconnected, innovative, and dependent on high-quality, accessible data.
