Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

This privacy notice from 13 MEDIA, S.C. outlines its commitment to safeguarding personal information in compliance with Mexican federal data protection laws. The organization collects a comprehensive range of personal and financial data to verify identity, understand customer needs, and fulfill legal obligations. By adhering to strict principles such as legality, quality, and proportionality, the company ensures that data handling is transparent, consensual, and purpose-driven, establishing a clear framework for the ethical management of user information. The notice details how data is shared and protected, specifying that information is primarily used by internal staff to provide requested services. Transfers to third parties, such as notaries or legal authorities, occur only when necessary for formalizing documents or complying with legal mandates. Users retain significant control over their data, with mechanisms provided to limit, access, rectify, or cancel its use. This structure emphasizes user agency, allowing individuals to exercise their rights through direct contact or written requests, thereby fostering trust through accessible and responsive data governance practices. This document is relevant to open data discussions because it highlights the critical balance between data utility and individual privacy rights. It demonstrates how organizations can operate transparently while respecting user consent, a foundational concept for responsible data ecosystems. Understanding these privacy frameworks is essential for policymakers and data advocates aiming to create standards that protect citizens while enabling the secure and ethical flow of information in digital services.
