Employees prioritise security on personal devices - IT-Online

Employees prioritise security on personal devices - IT-Online

Younger employees often prioritize productivity over strict adherence to cybersecurity protocols, creating a significant gap between security rules and social efficiency. This behavioral tendency increases the frequency of policy violations that inadvertently lead to data breaches, highlighting a critical human risk factor in corporate environments. The challenge lies in balancing cyber vigilance with empathy and social connection, as many workers struggle to integrate safety measures into their daily routines without feeling hindered. Effective security awareness training must therefore focus on changing behavior rather than mere memorization, helping employees understand how to seamlessly incorporate security practices into their workflows. This is highly relevant to open data because secure handling of information is foundational to its integrity and accessibility. If employees consistently bypass security measures for convenience, sensitive open datasets may be compromised, undermining public trust. Ensuring that staff can balance efficiency with robust security safeguards is essential for maintaining the reliability and safety of open data ecosystems.
