Los inversores de ByteDance emergen como favoritos en las negociaciones sobre TikTok

Los inversores de ByteDance emergen como favoritos en las negociaciones sobre TikTok

The article outlines a proposed divestment structure in which U.S. investors, including Oracle, would take control of TikTok’s American operations. This arrangement aims to keep user data within the United States and ensure that it remains inaccessible from China, addressing national security concerns through corporate restructuring rather than a complete sale. Such agreements are crucial for the open data movement, as they demonstrate viable mechanisms for maintaining transparency while ensuring data sovereignty. By separating regional data hubs under distinct legal entities, platforms can comply with local regulations without sacrificing global accessibility or compromising user privacy standards. The political urgency driving this deal underscores the tension between commercial interests and regulatory demands. Ultimately, these negotiations set a precedent for how tech giants manage cross-border data flows, influencing future policies on digital sovereignty and the ethical handling of public information.
