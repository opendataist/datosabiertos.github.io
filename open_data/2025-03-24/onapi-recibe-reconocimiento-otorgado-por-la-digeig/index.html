Onapi recibe reconocimiento otorgado por la Digeig

Onapi recibe reconocimiento otorgado por la Digeig

The Onapi received recognition for ensuring free access to public information by publishing data in open formats through the National Open Data Portal. This achievement highlights the institution’s proactive commitment to transparency, aiming to strengthen connections between users and society. The recognition underscores how open data fosters a culture of civic participation and contributes to the fight against corruption. By making information accessible, these initiatives promote innovation and operational efficiency, enabling easier use of public data to meet diverse societal needs. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the practical impact of open standards in government. It validates how releasing data in accessible formats directly supports transparency goals and empowers citizens, serving as a model for institutional accountability and digital governance.
