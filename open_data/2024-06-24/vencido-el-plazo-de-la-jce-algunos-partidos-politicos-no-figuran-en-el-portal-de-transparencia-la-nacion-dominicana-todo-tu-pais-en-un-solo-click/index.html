Vencido el plazo de la JCE, algunos partidos políticos no figuran en el portal de transparencia | La Nación Dominicana, Todo tu país, en un solo click...!

Vencido el plazo de la JCE, algunos partidos políticos no figuran en el portal de transparencia | La Nación Dominicana, Todo tu país, en un solo click...!

The article highlights significant gaps in the transparency of electoral campaign financing in the Dominican Republic. Several candidates failed to publish their expense reports on the Central Electoral Board’s transparency portal, while others submitted incomplete data. This lack of accessible information prevents the public and civil society from fully monitoring how political funds are utilized, undermining the core principle of open data in democratic processes. Key figures like Luis Abinader and Abel Martínez reported substantial expenditures, yet discrepancies between received funds and actual spending were observed. The inability to verify these financial flows through an official, accessible digital platform creates uncertainty regarding potential irregularities. When data is missing or fragmented, it becomes difficult to assess whether spending limits were respected or if public resources were managed correctly. This situation is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the necessity of standardized, complete, and machine-readable disclosures by government institutions. True transparency requires not just the publication of reports, but their availability in accessible formats that allow for easy verification. Without robust open data practices, the potential for corruption remains, and public trust in electoral integrity is compromised.
