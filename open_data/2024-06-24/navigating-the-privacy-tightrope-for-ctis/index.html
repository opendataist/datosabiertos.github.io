Navigating The Privacy Tightrope For CTIS

Massive clinical trial volumes in the EU demand rigorous data anonymization to ensure privacy within the new CTIS framework. This white paper outlines essential practices for transforming identifiable information, highlighting the technical and ethical challenges involved. Such robust data governance is vital for the open data movement, fostering responsible sharing that accelerates life-changing therapies while maintaining participant trust.
