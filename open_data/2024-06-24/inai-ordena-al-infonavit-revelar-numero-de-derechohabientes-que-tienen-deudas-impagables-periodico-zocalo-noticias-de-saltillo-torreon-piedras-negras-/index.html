INAI ordena al Infonavit revelar número de derechohabientes que tienen ‘deudas impagables’ | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) ordered Infonavit to disclose details about unpayable debts and the criteria used to classify them, emphasizing that access to this information is crucial for protecting citizens’ assets. The resolution underscores that transparency is not merely an administrative right, but a fundamental tool for safeguarding other essential rights, such as access to housing. This decision illustrates how obtaining public data enables individuals to make informed decisions that directly impact their financial stability. By demanding clarity in the definition of debts and the available courses of action, the accountability of institutions responsible for managing housing credit is strengthened. The article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the practical value of transparent and accessible government data. By ensuring that information is available and understandable, citizens are empowered to navigate complex systems, ensuring that public data serves as a genuine bridge to social justice and the protection of fundamental rights.
