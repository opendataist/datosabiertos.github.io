Colchester residents hit out at 'horrendous' speeding where drivers reach 100mph

Freedom of Information data reveals alarming speeding incidents in Essex, with cameras recording drivers reaching over 100 mph in areas with lower limits. Residents express deep concern regarding public safety, highlighting the danger posed to pedestrians and vulnerable road users due to high vehicle speeds and a lack of physical barriers. These local observations underscore the tangible risks associated with inadequate speed control in populated boroughs. Essex Police confirm that speeding remains a primary factor in injury collisions and are intensifying enforcement efforts to support their Vision Zero goal of eliminating road deaths by 2040. The force emphasizes that strict adherence to speed limits is crucial for protecting cyclists, pedestrians, and motorcyclists. By increasing speed checks and combating drink and drug driving, authorities aim to remove unfit drivers from the roads and reduce the overall number of fatal and serious accidents. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessing public records through Freedom of Information requests can uncover critical safety issues. The transparency provided by these statistics allows communities to verify concerns, hold local authorities accountable, and advocate for better traffic management. Ultimately, such data-driven insights empower citizens to engage in meaningful dialogue about road safety and policy implementation, fostering a more informed and safer public environment.
