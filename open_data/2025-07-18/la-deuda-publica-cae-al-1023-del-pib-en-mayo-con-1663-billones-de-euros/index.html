La deuda pública cae al 102,3% del PIB en mayo, con 1,663 billones de euros

La deuda pública cae al 102,3% del PIB en mayo, con 1,663 billones de euros

Spain’s public debt shows a general downward trend relative to GDP, although its absolute volume continues to grow. This context of a declining debt burden relative to economic output suggests an improvement in medium-term fiscal sustainability, a crucial macroeconomic indicator for assessing the country’s stability. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the transparency and availability of disaggregated data by administrative subsectors. Public access to this information enables analysis of how the debt burden is distributed among the central government, autonomous communities, and local entities, thereby facilitating accountability and critical analysis of fiscal policies. Furthermore, the periodic publication of these data by the Bank of Spain establishes a standard of quality and temporal comparability. This empowers researchers, journalists, and citizens to monitor the evolution of public spending and financial health, promoting a more informed and participatory society in matters of general interest.
