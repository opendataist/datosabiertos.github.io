Abu Dhabi authorities highlight that failing to maintain safe following distances is a major contributor to traffic accidents, resulting in significant fatalities and serious injuries. This data underscores the critical link between driver behavior and public safety, emphasizing that tailgating not only increases crash risks but also stems from aggressive practices like using lights to intimidate other drivers. The implications extend beyond mere statistics, illustrating how lack of concentration and delayed reaction times lead to rear-end collisions. By framing unsafe distance as a form of aggressive driving, officials stress that such actions are legally actionable, reinforcing the necessity for strict enforcement and accountability on the roads to deter dangerous habits.
