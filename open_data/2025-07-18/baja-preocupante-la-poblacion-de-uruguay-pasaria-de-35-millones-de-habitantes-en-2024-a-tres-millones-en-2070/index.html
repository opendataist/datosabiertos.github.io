Baja preocupante: la población de Uruguay pasaría de 3,5 millones de habitantes en 2024 a tres millones en 2070

Baja preocupante: la población de Uruguay pasaría de 3,5 millones de habitantes en 2024 a tres millones en 2070

Projections indicate that Uruguay’s population will shrink from its current levels to approximately three million by 2070, driven primarily by declining fertility rates outpacing gains in life expectancy. This demographic shift represents a significant structural change, highlighting the urgent need for adaptable public policies to manage economic and social stability as the workforce ages and shrinks. The government is actively exploring international solutions, such as reduced working hours, to mitigate these challenges. The article is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of accurate, transparent demographic statistics in shaping national policy. The Instituto Nacional de Estadística’s detailed census data allows for precise forecasting and public debate, demonstrating how accessible and reliable public data empowers policymakers to identify trends and seek evidence-based solutions from other nations. Ultimately, the interplay between statistical evidence and political action reveals how open demographic data serves as a foundational tool for governance. By making these projections and census results publicly available, Uruguay enables stakeholders to understand the long-term implications of demographic aging and fertility decline, fostering a more informed society capable of adapting to future societal changes through proactive and data-driven decision-making.
