Índice de Precios al Consumidor Región NEA, con el 1,1% en Junio 2025 fue menor a la escala nacional

The June 2025 consumer price index reveals distinct inflationary pressures in Argentina’s Northeast region compared to national averages. While monthly fluctuations varied by sector, the year-over-year analysis highlights significant cost increases in essential services such as housing and education, contrasting with more stable prices in food and transportation. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it underscores the need for granular, accessible statistical information. By making regional CPI details publicly available, institutions empower researchers, journalists, and citizens to accurately analyze local economic impacts. Ultimately, transparency in such economic indicators fosters informed public discourse and accountability. Open access to these metrics enables better validation of government claims and supports evidence-based decision-making, ensuring that community resources are allocated efficiently amid shifting economic conditions.
