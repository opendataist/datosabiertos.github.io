FOIA Requests

Virginia law ensures broad public access to government records, prioritizing transparency by presuming openness and narrowly interpreting exemptions. This framework empowers citizens and the media to scrutinize official activities, reinforcing the democratic value of open governance. For entities like Roanoke City Public Schools, adherence to these statutes mandates clear protocols for information requests. Establishing accessible channels ensures compliance while maintaining the integrity of public data availability. This case highlights the critical intersection of legal mandates and data accessibility. It demonstrates how statutory requirements drive the implementation of open data practices, ensuring that public information remains readily available for community engagement and accountability.
