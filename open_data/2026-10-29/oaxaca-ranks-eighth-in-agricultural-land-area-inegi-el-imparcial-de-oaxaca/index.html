Oaxaca ranks eighth in agricultural land area: INEGI - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

Oaxaca ranks eighth in agricultural land area: INEGI - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

The 2025 National Agricultural Survey highlights Oaxaca as a key entity in national agriculture, with a vast area dedicated to annual and perennial crops. Its strategic importance is evident in the production of coffee, sugarcane, and cattle, consolidating its role among the states that dominate the country's industrial production. However, the sector faces serious structural challenges. Producers report high input costs, loss of soil fertility, and marketing problems as main obstacles. Additionally, there is a strong dependence on unpaid labor, revealing significant precariousness in the profitability and sustainability of local agricultural work. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates the need for transparency in official statistics. It allows for the analysis of gaps between productive wealth and socioeconomic reality, fostering accountability. Access to this data facilitates the identification of public policies that address inequality and sustainability in the Mexican countryside.
