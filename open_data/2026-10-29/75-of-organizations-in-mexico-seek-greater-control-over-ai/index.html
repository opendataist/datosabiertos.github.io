75% of organizations in Mexico seek greater control over AI

75% of organizations in Mexico seek greater control over AI

The report reveals that organizations in Mexico are prioritizing control and governance over artificial intelligence, seeking to reduce risks and protect data sovereignty and corporate identity. This transition marks a paradigm shift where competitive advantage no longer lies solely in the speed of adoption, but in the structural capacity to integrate these tools securely, coherently, and sustainably into business processes. The implementation of formal management frameworks and direct accountability from senior leadership are fundamental pillars for this new phase. Advanced companies focus on strengthening oversight and cybersecurity to maintain operational resilience, recognizing that technological success crucially depends on robust audit and accountability mechanisms that enable scaling the use of AI without compromising institutional trust. However, a significant gap persists between the investment made and the effective capacity to measure the value generated. Although most companies report tangible benefits, very few have disciplined financial processes to evaluate return on investment. This underscores the urgent need to develop precise metrics that link technology with measurable business outcomes, ensuring that adoption translates into sustainable growth. This analysis is relevant to open data because it demonstrates that the availability of technological information is not enough; transparency in algorithms and access to verifiable performance metrics are required. The AI governance mentioned demands open standards for auditing and data, which are essential for building trust, avoiding biases, and enabling society and businesses to critically assess the real impact of these innovations, thereby promoting a responsible and accountable digital ecosystem.
