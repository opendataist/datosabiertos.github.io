Inflation rate steady at 2.7 percent in December - The Budapest Times

Inflation rate steady at 2.7 percent in December - The Budapest Times

Rising inflation in Hungary, driven significantly by alcohol and tobacco costs, highlights the growing economic strain on vulnerable populations. The disparity between headline and core inflation figures underscores underlying price pressures that disproportionately affect essential goods, leading to increased financial hardship for low-income retirees. This situation is highly relevant to open data because transparent, accessible statistical information is crucial for holding governments accountable. Open data initiatives allow citizens and watchdogs to verify official metrics, exposing potential discrepancies in how inflation is calculated for revenue collection versus social benefit adjustments. Furthermore, widespread availability of detailed price indices enables deeper analysis of inequality trends. By democratizing access to such data, researchers can better document the real-world impact of economic policies, ensuring that social safety nets evolve in response to actual living cost increases rather than abstract governmental figures.
