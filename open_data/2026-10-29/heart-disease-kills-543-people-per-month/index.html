Heart disease kills 543 people per month

The article reveals that cardiovascular diseases and malignant tumors are the leading causes of mortality in Chihuahua, exceeding the national average in the first quarter of 2026. This information, drawn from official records of the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI), underscores the urgency of addressing these conditions as public health priorities in the state, particularly given the slight increase compared to the previous year. The relevance of this content to the open data movement lies in the transparency and accessibility of official statistical information. By publishing these details broken down by cause and demographic, government institutions enable citizens, researchers, and the media to accurately analyze health trends, fostering accountability and evidence-based decision-making. This type of publication illustrates how access to high-quality public data empowers society to identify critical health issues. The availability of detailed figures facilitates the timely detection of crises and the evaluation of existing policies, demonstrating that open data not only fulfills legal requirements but also generates social value by improving understanding and response to collective health challenges.
