Liquid AI Releases LFM3: Six Open-Weight Models Fuse Text, Vision, Audio on One 2B Backbone

Liquid AI Releases LFM3: Six Open-Weight Models Fuse Text, Vision, Audio on One 2B Backbone

Liquid AI has released LFM3, a family of six open-weight models that unify text, vision, audio, and decision-making capabilities into a single efficient backbone. By introducing a tri-hybrid architecture that combines convolutions, sliding-window attention, and global attention, the models achieve significant performance gains while drastically reducing memory requirements. This design allows even the largest variant to fit on consumer hardware, marking a major step forward for accessible, edge-compatible AI development. The technical innovation lies in optimizing the balance between local, medium-range, and long-range context processing. By minimizing key-value cache overhead, LFM3 enables robust agentic tasks and long-context reasoning without demanding datacenter-scale infrastructure. This efficiency is complemented by specialized training stages focused on tool use and multi-step decision-making, resulting in superior performance on benchmarks for web navigation and robotics compared to significantly larger predecessors. This release is highly relevant to the open_data community because it democratizes access to high-performance, multi-modal models. By providing open weights that fine-tune on single consumer GPUs, Liquid AI lowers the barrier to entry for researchers and developers. This encourages broader experimentation and deployment of AI solutions in resource-constrained environments, fostering a more inclusive ecosystem for building practical, real-world applications.
