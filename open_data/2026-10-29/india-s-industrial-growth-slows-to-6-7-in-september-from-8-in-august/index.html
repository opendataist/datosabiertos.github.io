India's industrial growth slows to 6.7% in September from 8% in August

India's industrial growth slows to 6.7% in September from 8% in August

India's September industrial growth slowed to 6.7%, missing economist forecasts and reflecting a broader cooling trend compared to August. Manufacturing expanded moderately, driven by automotive and electrical sectors, while mining contracted significantly. This data highlights shifting production dynamics within India’s industrial landscape. The slowdown in capital goods and consumer durables suggests weakening demand despite growth in non-durables. These fluctuations indicate varying health across industrial sub-sectors, offering crucial insights into current economic momentum. Such granular data helps analysts understand sector-specific vulnerabilities and resilience in real-time. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how granular, publicly available economic indicators empower independent research. Transparent access to such detailed statistics allows for better verification of official claims and fosters more accurate, evidence-based economic analysis by the public and researchers alike.
