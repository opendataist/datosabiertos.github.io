Michoacán: 7 in forced displacement; 91,000 affected: INEGI

Michoacán: 7 in forced displacement; 91,000 affected: INEGI

The 2025 Intercensal Survey reveals that Michoacán shows significant figures of forced displacement, highlighting the impact of insecurity and violence on thousands of households. This data quantifies the magnitude of the social and humanitarian problem affecting a considerable portion of the state's population. The measurement of this phenomenon stands out as an important methodological innovation in the survey. It enables public authorities to identify critical areas and better understand the dynamics of displacements caused by criminal factors and disasters. This information is relevant to the open data movement by ensuring that official statistics are accessible and used for transparency. It facilitates the creation of evidence-based public policies, promoting accountability and the design of more effective response strategies for vulnerable communities.
