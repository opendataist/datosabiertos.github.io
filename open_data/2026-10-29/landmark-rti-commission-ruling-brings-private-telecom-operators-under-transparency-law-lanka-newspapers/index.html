Landmark RTI Commission Ruling Brings Private Telecom Operators Under Transparency Law - Lanka Newspapers

Landmark RTI Commission Ruling Brings Private Telecom Operators Under Transparency Law - Lanka Newspapers

Sri Lanka’s Right to Information Commission has issued a landmark ruling that extends transparency obligations to private telecommunications operators. This decision marks a significant expansion of the law’s scope, compelling private companies to respond to information requests just as public institutions do. It effectively bridges a previous legal gap, ensuring that entities providing essential services are no longer shielded from accountability measures. For citizens, this shift empowers consumers to demand greater corporate transparency regarding service delivery and data practices. Civil society organizations view this as a vital step toward holding private actors accountable for their impact on public interest. The ruling establishes that failure to comply with these disclosure requirements can result in regulatory action, thereby strengthening the enforcement mechanism for right to information laws. This precedent is highly relevant to open data advocates as it suggests that transparency laws can penetrate the private sector when services serve a public function. It may encourage similar legal interpretations across other industries, promoting a culture of openness beyond government bodies. Ultimately, it reinforces the principle that access to information is a fundamental right that transcends the public-private divide in sectors critical to daily life.
