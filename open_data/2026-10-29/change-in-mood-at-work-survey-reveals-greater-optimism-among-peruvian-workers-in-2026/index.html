Change in mood at work? Survey reveals greater optimism among Peruvian workers in 2026

Change in mood at work? Survey reveals greater optimism among Peruvian workers in 2026

The article highlights the discrepancy between the high level of labor informality in Peru and the prevailing optimism among workers regarding their professional experience. Despite structural challenges, a significant portion of the population perceives the past year as favorable, suggesting that job satisfaction does not depend solely on contractual stability or the macroeconomic environment. The key driver of this positive perception is professional growth, which is especially valued by younger generations. While early-career workers prioritize career development, older age groups seek personal well-being and family stability. This generational differentiation indicates that work expectations are dynamic and influenced by each individual’s life stage, requiring tailored approaches. This news is relevant to open data because it illustrates the need to disaggregate socio-labor data to avoid homogenizing analyses. By showing how motivations vary by age and gender, it underscores the importance of publishing detailed datasets that enable companies and policymakers to design retention and development strategies that respond to the real and specific needs of each segment of the workforce, thereby promoting more inclusive and effective management.
