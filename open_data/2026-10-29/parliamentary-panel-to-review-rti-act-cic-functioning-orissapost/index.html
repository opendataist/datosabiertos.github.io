Parliamentary panel to review RTI Act, CIC functioning - OrissaPOST

Parliamentary panel to review RTI Act, CIC functioning - OrissaPOST

A parliamentary panel is scrutinizing the Right to Information Act and the Central Information Commission due to growing concerns over excessive case backlogs. This review highlights the critical need for efficient implementation of open data frameworks, ensuring that citizens can effectively access government information without bureaucratic delays hindering transparency and accountability. The committee will also investigate judicial infrastructure, digitization efforts, and the integration of artificial intelligence into the justice system. These initiatives are vital for enhancing public service delivery, demonstrating how technology-driven open data solutions can modernize legal processes and improve the accessibility of justice for all citizens. Furthermore, the inquiry covers public grievance redressal mechanisms and the availability of court judgments in multiple languages. Strengthening these systems ensures that legal data is not only collected but also openly accessible and understandable, reinforcing the principles of transparency and empowering individuals to engage with democratic institutions more effectively.
