Panama adopts new standard for land data management

Panama adopts new standard for land data management

Panama unifies its cadastral management through the LADM-PA model, aligning with international standards to ensure technical coherence in land administration. This initiative modernizes processes, facilitating coordination between systems and ensuring that territorial data are structured and reliable. This approach is crucial for open data, as regulatory standardization enables interoperability and public access to high-quality geospatial information. By establishing uniform criteria, it eliminates data fragmentation, promotes transparency, and allows third parties to use cadastral information to innovate, analyze, or develop applications based on land use. It is important to note that this resolution does not automatically alter existing property titles. Its primary objective is to improve the underlying data infrastructure, ensuring that available information is accurate and standardized, which constitutes the fundamental basis for any open government initiative or development of services based on open territorial data.
