Primer reto del INAI con Sheinbaum es evitar desaparición

A Mexican committee is considering abolishing the INAI, a transparency institute that is essential for data protection. This proposal threatens to dismantle a key anti-corruption mechanism, potentially undermining citizens’ right to access information. The issue is critical for open data, as dismantling such institutions restricts public accountability and transparency.
