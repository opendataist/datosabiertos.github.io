The final results of Paraguay’s 2022 Census reveal significant demographic shifts, highlighting substantial population growth and a marked trend toward urbanization. These findings provide essential, granular data for public policy, enabling governments to better allocate resources and address the needs of an increasingly urban society. Household dynamics show smaller family sizes and improved access to infrastructure, alongside a shift in domestic leadership toward women. The widespread availability of digital devices and internet connectivity indicates rapid technological integration. Understanding these socioeconomic patterns is crucial for open data initiatives aiming to foster transparency and inform development strategies. This dataset is vital for open data because it transforms raw census figures into actionable insights regarding housing, technology, and social structures. By making such detailed information accessible, stakeholders can analyze trends, drive evidence-based decision-making, and monitor equity, ensuring that statistical transparency directly supports national development and social inclusion efforts.
