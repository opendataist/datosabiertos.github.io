Canada, Australia, Belgium, pledge to support NEITI on open data policy - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

The embassies of Belgium, Australia, and Canada have formally pledged support for Nigeria’s Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative open data policy. This commitment follows their inspection of a new data center designed to serve as a comprehensive repository for sector information. The diplomatic backing underscores the international community’s recognition of transparency as a critical tool for combating corruption and ensuring accountability in resource-rich nations. This initiative establishes a centralized hub for aggregated and disaggregated extractive industry data, facilitating easy access for civil society, media, and government entities. By aligning with global EITI standards, the center aims to foster evidence-based decision-making and sustainable advocacy. It provides the necessary infrastructure for robust public knowledge, enabling stakeholders to analyze trends and hold corporations accountable through systematic disclosure and improved data governance. The relevance to open data lies in the shift toward transparent, accessible, and actionable information management in the public sector. By supporting skills development and technical assistance, the partnership strengthens local capacity to utilize data effectively. This collaboration highlights how open data initiatives can drive reform, manage resource dependencies, and promote sustainable development through shared knowledge and international cooperation.
