Discutirán desaparición de autónomos el viernes

Discutirán desaparición de autónomos el viernes

The Mexican Congress is considering dissolving key autonomous bodies, including the INAI, to centralize state power. This initiative, driven by the executive branch, fundamentally threatens the institutional independence required to safeguard public information rights and personal data protection. Such a structural shift implies a significant regression in transparency mechanisms, as specialized oversight entities would be eliminated. Without these autonomous organs, citizens lose critical guarantees against information asymmetry and state abuse, weakening the democratic framework that relies on accountability and open governance. This development is highly relevant to open data because it attacks the legal infrastructure ensuring data accessibility. The potential removal of INAI dismantles the primary body responsible for enforcing the right to access public information, thereby jeopardizing the transparency principles essential for a functional open government and citizen participation.
