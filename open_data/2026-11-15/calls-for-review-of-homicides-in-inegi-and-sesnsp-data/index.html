Calls for review of homicides in INEGI and SESNSP data

Calls for review of homicides in INEGI and SESNSP data

The federal government is investigating discrepancies in homicide data between the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) and the National System of Public Security Statistics (SESNSP), establishing a joint working group. This initiative aims to harmonize statistical methodologies and the timelines for certifying deaths, which are essential for ensuring the accuracy and comparability of official information. The difference is attributed to the fact that INEGI uses death certificates and civil registry records, while SESNSP relies on reports from state prosecutors’ offices. This contrast highlights how administrative sources vary in their temporal and legal definitions of homicides, directly affecting the consolidation of final figures. This case is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates the need for open and harmonized standards. Transparency requires that public institutions clearly define their sources and methods, facilitating interoperability and fostering citizen trust in public safety indicators.
