The safeguards available to users to protect themselves if they become victims of espionage

The safeguards available to users to protect themselves if they become victims of espionage

Digital espionage constitutes a fundamental violation of privacy and personal data, demanding protection that goes beyond technical measures to encompass legal aspects. Colombian laws establish defense mechanisms, although the rapid pace of technological advancement outstrips the capacity for individual response, highlighting the need for institutions and companies to ensure security. Effective protection cannot rely solely on individual competencies. Regulatory bodies must act swiftly, and technology companies must assume concrete responsibilities in the secure design of their services, balancing the legal framework with the demands of constant innovation. Prevention involves adopting rigorous habits such as keeping systems constantly updated, using strong passwords, and implementing multi-factor authentication. This approach is particularly relevant for open data, as transparency and access to information are only sustainable when clear mechanisms are in place to protect against espionage and user trust in the integrity of digital systems is strengthened.
