Eurostat confirms that Spain recycled 71.5% of its glass packaging in 2024, 1.7 percentage points more than the previous year

Eurostat confirms that Spain recycled 71.5% of its glass packaging in 2024, 1.7 percentage points more than the previous year

Spain has exceeded the European recycling target for glass set for 2025, reaching 71.5% in 2024 according to Eurostat. This progress, driven primarily by the green bin, shows that citizens have adapted their waste separation habits faster than expected, consolidating a positive trend that surpasses EU targets ahead of schedule. However, to meet the 75% target for 2030, the managing entity Ecovidrio insists that voluntary efforts alone are insufficient. It calls for mandatory municipal ordinances and a sanctioning system to ensure correct source separation. Additionally, the use of artificial intelligence is planned to optimize collection routes and container placement, aiming to close the significant gap between cities with high recycling rates and those still recording very low rates. This article is relevant to the field of *open data* because it highlights the importance of transparency and statistical verification. The process contrasts provisional data from the private entity with official figures published by public bodies two years later, demonstrating how timely access to reliable information is crucial for evaluating environmental public policies and adjusting waste management strategies based on real evidence.
