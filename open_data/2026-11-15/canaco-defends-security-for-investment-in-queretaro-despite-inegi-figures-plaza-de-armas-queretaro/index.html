CANACO defends security for investment in Querétaro, despite INEGI figures - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

CANACO defends security for investment in Querétaro, despite INEGI figures - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

René Loya Poletti, president of CANACO in Querétaro, interprets the rise in business victimization rates as a reflection of the state’s economic and cultural growth. He argues that the steady influx of new investments increases the total number of businesses, which naturally raises the absolute figures for crimes without necessarily indicating a proportional increase in insecurity. Furthermore, he emphasizes that improved trust among business owners in state authorities has fostered a greater willingness to report incidents. This increased transparency in reporting should not be confused with a deterioration in security; rather, it demonstrates an environment where merchants feel secure in approaching public institutions without fear of reprisals. This perspective is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of context when analyzing public statistics. Without considering variables such as business growth or the culture of reporting, raw indicators can be misinterpreted, underscoring the need for a multidimensional and contextualized analysis in transparency policies.
