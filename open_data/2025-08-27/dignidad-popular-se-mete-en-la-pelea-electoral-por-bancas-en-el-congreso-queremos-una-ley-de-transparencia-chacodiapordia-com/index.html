Dignidad Popular se mete en la pelea electoral por bancas en el Congreso: "Queremos una ley de transparencia" - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

Dignidad Popular se mete en la pelea electoral por bancas en el Congreso: "Queremos una ley de transparencia" - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

The article highlights Dignidad Popular’s commitment to fiscal transparency as a core political pillar. By implementing a digital platform for tracking public expenditure, the party aims to empower citizens to monitor government spending directly. This initiative seeks to eliminate secrecy in public finances, ensuring that taxpayers understand exactly how their funds are used at the provincial, municipal, and national levels. The relevance to open data lies in the creation of accessible, traceable data streams. The proposal envisions making fiscal information available for public scrutiny, aligning with open data principles of transparency and civic oversight. By providing tools for ordinary citizens to analyze financial data, the project promotes accountability and reduces the potential for misinformation regarding the allocation of public resources. Additionally, the party advocates for electoral reforms to eliminate “testimonial” candidacies and strengthen identity verification. This ensures that elected officials have clean records, fostering trust in democratic processes. The focus remains on delivering honest governance through data-driven accountability and rigorous vetting of political candidates.
