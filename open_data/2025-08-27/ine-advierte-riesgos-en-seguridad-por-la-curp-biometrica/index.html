INE advierte riesgos en seguridad por la CURP biométrica

INE advierte riesgos en seguridad por la CURP biométrica

The National Electoral Institute (INE) has launched urgent coordination with federal authorities to evaluate the impacts of the new biometric CURP on voter registration. This dialogue seeks to establish clear operational frameworks and address the absence of responses regarding verification agreements, underscoring the current administrative uncertainty surrounding the implementation of these digital identity reforms. A critical internal study warns that the mandated integration with the Unified Identity Platform poses severe security and confidentiality risks to the data of over 100 million citizens. The legislation potentially forces the transfer of proprietary code and unrestricted database access to the federal government, creating dangerous legal vacuums and compromising the electoral institute’s institutional control over its core registry systems. Furthermore, the biometric CURP may diminish the electoral credential’s status as official identification, risking voter disengagement and an outdated registry. These findings are vital to open data advocates, as they demonstrate how opaque legal mandates and unregulated data sharing can threaten digital privacy, erode institutional autonomy, and undermine the integrity of public information systems.
