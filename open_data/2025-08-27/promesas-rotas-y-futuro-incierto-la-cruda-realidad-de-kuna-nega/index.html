Promesas rotas y futuro incierto: la cruda realidad de Kuna Nega

Promesas rotas y futuro incierto: la cruda realidad de Kuna Nega

The article illustrates how the Kuna Nega community embodies Panama’s deep social divides, where the lack of education and basic services perpetuates cycles of poverty. It concludes that guaranteeing fundamental rights—such as decent schools, healthcare, and clean drinking water—is essential to breaking this structural inequality and securing a collective future, since denying these resources condemns entire generations to ignorance. This scenario underscores that individual progress is insufficient without basic infrastructure; technology and private initiatives are inadequate without consistent state intervention. The paradox between innovation in urban areas and the absence of services in rural ones reveals a broad social debt that demands justice, not charity, to be resolved. This content is relevant to open data because the official statistics cited expose disparities in access to healthcare and education that often remain hidden or decontextualized. Transparency in these data is crucial for identifying where public systems fail and for designing evidence-based policies. Without access to clear, disaggregated information, it is impossible to demand accountability or develop inclusive solutions that bridge the gap between urban privilege and rural exclusion.
