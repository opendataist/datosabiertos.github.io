Rosario, entre los cinco destinos más visitados de Argentina según el Indec

Rosario has consolidated its position as a premier tourist destination in Argentina, ranking among the country’s five most visited cities. This growth is attributed to an integrated strategy that combines effective security policies, which have transformed the city’s image, with significant infrastructure investments, such as the airport renovation, and urban planning focused on reclaiming public space. Improvements in hospitality and cultural offerings, exemplified during large-scale events like the Flag Month, have resulted in high satisfaction rates and strong visitor intent to return. The relevance of this article to open data lies in the use of official statistics from the National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) as a tool for evaluating political and economic impact. The analysis demonstrates how open data on hotel occupancy, overnight stays, and tourist origins enable real-time measurement of the effectiveness of public policies. By transforming demographic and economic indicators into tangible evidence, these data facilitate accountability and justify state investments, highlighting the direct correlation between informational transparency and successful urban management. Furthermore, the report illustrates the importance of cross-referencing quantitative information with user experience metrics, such as satisfaction and perceived safety rates. This combination of objective and subjective data provides a holistic view of tourism development, offering valuable insights for other public managers seeking to replicate successful models. The availability and rigorous analysis of these figures not only validate the current management model but also set a precedent for grounding tourism and regional development decisions in accessible, verifiable empirical evidence.
