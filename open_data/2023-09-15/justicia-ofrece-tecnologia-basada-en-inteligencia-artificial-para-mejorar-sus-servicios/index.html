Justicia ofrece tecnología basada en inteligencia artificial para mejorar sus servicios

The Ministry of Justice has implemented artificial intelligence technologies to modernize its services, prioritizing operational efficiency and citizen accessibility. These tools aim to simplify complex processes, ensuring more equitable access to justice and protecting vulnerable users through clear and direct communication. Automatic document anonymization represents a significant advance in the protection of personal data, reducing privacy and fraud risks. By concealing identifying information, this initiative ensures the ethical handling of sensitive information within the judicial system, aligning with the principles of security and trust necessary for effective administrative management. The introduction of intelligent classifiers and search engines optimizes the workload of legal professionals, facilitating the location and organization of legal information. This modernization is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how technology can standardize and improve the quality of public data, facilitating subsequent analysis and promoting effective transparency in public administration.
