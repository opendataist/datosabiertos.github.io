Gov. Sanders signs legislation limiting FOIA

Arkansas expanded government secrecy by exempting executive security planning records from public access. This move significantly restricts transparency, directly impacting open data efforts by hiding critical safety infrastructure details from scrutiny. While other laws address fiscal, educational, and health matters, the FOIA amendment represents a major regression in information freedom. It sets a precedent where sensitive internal processes are shielded from the public, undermining accountability in government operations. This legislation is relevant to open data as it explicitly removes specific high-level governmental records from mandatory disclosure. By classifying security planning as non-public, it reduces the available data for researchers and citizens seeking to understand executive decision-making processes.
