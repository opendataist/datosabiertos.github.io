La inflación se disparó al 12,4% en agosto y bate un récord luego de más de 32 años - El Chubut

Recent official information reveals a significant acceleration in inflation, driven by currency devaluation and pressures in essential sectors such as food and healthcare. This economic situation directly impacts market transparency, as government measures to stabilize the currency could alter the availability and accuracy of the macroeconomic data needed for open and auditable management. The political debate surrounding the IMF and dollarization policies highlights the tension between economic sovereignty and the need for reliable indicators. By attributing the crisis to external or internal conditions, political actors emphasize that monetary autonomy should not compromise statistical integrity, a crucial element for citizens and investors to understand the underlying economic reality. This context is relevant to open data because it underscores the urgency of maintaining clear and accessible public information channels. Ensuring access to updated and verifiable economic data enables civil society to critically evaluate official statements, foster accountability, and participate informedly in decisions affecting national stability.
