This article highlights the critical intersection between public safety operations and open data transparency. By publishing detailed statistics on personnel injuries and operational costs, the INEGI demonstrates how accessible government data reveals the tangible human and financial impact of civil protection efforts. The data exposes significant regional disparities in resource allocation, showing how some states bear much higher per-capita costs than others. Furthermore, it illuminates structural trends, such as the predominance of administrative roles over frontline rescue workers, which helps citizens understand where public funds are actually directed. Transparency in these metrics is essential for informed civic oversight.
