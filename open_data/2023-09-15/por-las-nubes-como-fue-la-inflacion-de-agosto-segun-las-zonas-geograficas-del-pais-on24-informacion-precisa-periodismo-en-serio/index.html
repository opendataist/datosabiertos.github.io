Por las nubes: ¿cómo fue la inflación de agosto según las zonas geográficas del país? | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

Por las nubes: ¿cómo fue la inflación de agosto según las zonas geográficas del país? | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

Argentina’s August inflation reached 12.4% nationally, with food and health prices rising sharply across all regions. Regional disparities emerged, as the northeast experienced higher price increases than Patagonia, which recorded the lowest inflationary pressure. This data highlights how macroeconomic pressures affect local purchasing power differently depending on geography. Understanding these regional variations is crucial for analyzing the uneven distribution of economic strain within the country. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how granular, publicly released statistical records enable citizens to monitor real-time changes in the cost of living. Access to such detailed metrics fosters transparency and allows for a deeper understanding of localized economic realities beyond national averages.
