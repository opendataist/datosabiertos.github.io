Kaspersky recognized Leader in SoftwareReviews 2023 Security Awareness & Training Data Quadrant awards

Kaspersky’s recognition as a Leader in security awareness training highlights the critical role of human-centric cybersecurity strategies. By prioritizing comprehensive education for all organizational levels, from IT staff to executives, companies can significantly enhance their overall defense posture. This shift underscores that robust technical measures must be complemented by a well-informed workforce capable of identifying and mitigating social engineering threats. The high satisfaction rates and strong renewal intentions indicate a successful model for corporate education that effectively bridges knowledge gaps among non-technical employees. This approach validates the necessity of integrating adult learning principles with cybersecurity expertise to create sustainable behavioral changes. When users feel empowered and supported, they become active participants in security protocols rather than passive vulnerabilities, fostering a culture of shared responsibility. This case is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how aggregated, anonymized user feedback can drive continuous improvement in digital safety tools. Transparent evaluation metrics allow organizations to make informed decisions about security infrastructure, promoting accountability and trust. Furthermore, it encourages the open sharing of best practices in security training, helping to standardize effective educational methods that protect data integrity across the broader technology ecosystem.
