Iberia confirma grave filtración de datos de clientes y activa su protocolo de seguridad

Iberia confirma grave filtración de datos de clientes y activa su protocolo de seguridad

Iberia has confirmed a security breach in its systems, compromising basic personal data of its customers due to unauthorized access to a technology provider. Although the risk of financial fraud has been ruled out, as neither access credentials nor banking information were compromised, the incident highlights the critical vulnerability of shared digital infrastructures in the aviation sector. The company has activated enhanced security protocols and notified the relevant authorities, advising users to exercise heightened caution against phishing attempts. This immediate response and transparency in communication are essential to maintaining customer trust and complying with the strict European data protection regulations currently in force. This incident is significant for open data, as it demonstrates the inherent risks within the ecosystem of technology providers that manage sensitive information. It illustrates the need to balance collaboration within the supply chain with rigorous cybersecurity standards, reminding us that data interoperability must not compromise user privacy or overall security.
