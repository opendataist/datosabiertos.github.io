AI keeping China, US from greater decoupling

AI keeping China, US from greater decoupling

Geopolitical tensions and export controls have not severed the deep interdependence between US and Chinese AI ecosystems. Open-source collaboration and shared incentives continue to bind these global powers, preventing any complete decoupling despite intensifying rivalry. The division of labor remains distinct, with the US leading foundational model breakthroughs while China excels at scaling applications through its vast engineering talent and market. This complementary dynamic suggests that competition will coexist with reliance, as each side leverages its specific strengths in the broader global technology landscape. This insight is vital for open_data because it highlights how open-source models facilitate global data and model flows that bypass strict national borders. Understanding this resilient, hybrid ecosystem helps researchers navigate data sharing policies that operate independently of political friction.
