Gobierno regional y UCLM aunarán todos los datos estadísticos de la Comunidad Autónoma en una publicación abierta a la ciudadanía

Gobierno regional y UCLM aunarán todos los datos estadísticos de la Comunidad Autónoma en una publicación abierta a la ciudadanía

The regional government and the University of Castilla-La Mancha have established a strategic partnership to transform existing public data into accessible knowledge through the project "Castilla-La Mancha in Data." This initiative aims to synthesize diverse socioeconomic statistics into a coherent narrative, disseminated via both print and digital formats, thereby bridging the gap between raw administrative figures and societal understanding. By presenting information across numerous sectors like health, economy, and environment, the project seeks to make complex statistics comprehensible for citizens and policymakers alike. A core objective of this collaboration is to institutionalize the strategic use of data and Artificial Intelligence within public services. The team behind the project plans to integrate these resources into a dedicated chair focused on statistics and AI, aiming to normalize these technologies’ application in government operations. This reflects a broader commitment to enhancing administrative efficiency and decision-making capabilities through advanced analytical tools and standardized statistical practices. This article is highly relevant to open data as it exemplifies the transition from mere data publication to active knowledge creation. It demonstrates how transparent governance, supported by legislation on transparency and digitalization, can leverage open data to improve public administration. By focusing on accessibility and the ethical, practical application of AI, the initiative highlights the vital role of open data in fostering an informed society and modernizing public services through evidence-based management.
