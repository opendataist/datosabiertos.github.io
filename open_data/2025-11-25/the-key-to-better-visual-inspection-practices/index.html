The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

InQuest Science’s Identifier Software addresses critical gaps in pharmaceutical visual inspection by replacing fragmented, manual processes with a unified, compliant digital lifecycle management system. By consolidating data from raw materials to finished products, it ensures full visibility for quality assurance, allowing manufacturers to detect inconsistencies early and prevent costly batch recalls. This centralized approach streamlines defect tracking and reporting, reducing administrative burdens while enhancing data integrity through automated audit trails. The system specifically targets the unique precision requirements of parenteral and medical device manufacturing, offering specialized expertise that broader Manufacturing Execution Systems often lack. Its robust training and qualification modules ensure inspectors and automated vision systems meet strict regulatory standards, such as USP guidelines and FDA expectations. By utilizing a blinded digital defect library and targeted challenge test sets, the software minimizes bias and human error, significantly improving the accuracy of defect classification and inspection reliability. This solution is highly relevant to open data principles by demonstrating how structured, accessible digital repositories can transform industry compliance. Moving away from paper-based records to standardized, machine-readable defect libraries facilitates better historical evaluation and trend analysis. It highlights the value of integrating specialized, interoperable software with existing systems to create transparent, auditable, and efficient data ecosystems that support continuous improvement in global pharmaceutical standards.
