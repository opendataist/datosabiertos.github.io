La vivienda se dispara como primera preocupación de los catalanes​ - Tercera Información

La vivienda se dispara como primera preocupación de los catalanes​ - Tercera Información

Housing has emerged as the paramount social issue in Catalonia, surpassing all other concerns for citizens across diverse demographic and political profiles. This shift indicates a systemic failure in housing policy, where the lack of effective regulation leads to high costs and instability, causing widespread anxiety and financial strain among tenants regardless of their background or age. The relevance to open data lies in the critical role that transparent, publicly accessible information plays in validating citizens' lived experiences and exposing policy gaps. While official statistics may suggest stabilization through price caps, open data reveals the rise of temporary rentals as a workaround mechanism that bypasses protections. This transparency is essential for identifying loopholes, holding institutions accountable, and providing the empirical evidence needed to advocate for robust regulatory frameworks that truly protect vulnerable populations. Consequently, there is a growing disconnect between institutional responses and public perception, fueling increased civic mobilization and union membership. The necessity for open and accurate data becomes even more pressing as legislative decisions on temporary rentals face political hurdles. Ensuring that all stakeholders have access to reliable housing metrics is fundamental for fostering informed debate and driving the structural changes required to address this crisis effectively.
