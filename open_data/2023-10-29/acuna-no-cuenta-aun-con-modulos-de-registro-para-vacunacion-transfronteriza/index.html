Acuña no cuenta aún con módulos de registro para vacunación transfronteriza

Acuña has postponed the public registration for cross-border COVID-19 vaccinations, initially prioritizing corporate workers. This logistical emphasis on employer-led enrollment highlights significant gaps in accessible public data infrastructure for border health initiatives. The lack of open registration modules for the general public underscores the need for transparent, publicly available datasets on vaccine distribution and accessibility. Such opacity hinders community awareness and equitable access to health resources in binational zones. This case illustrates the critical intersection of public health transparency and open data principles. Ensuring that vaccination logistics are recorded in open, accessible formats is essential for accountability and for empowering border populations with accurate, real-time health information.
