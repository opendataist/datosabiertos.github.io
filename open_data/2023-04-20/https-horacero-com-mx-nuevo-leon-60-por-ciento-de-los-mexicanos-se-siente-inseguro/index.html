INEGI’s recent survey reveals that a majority of Mexicans, especially women, perceive their cities as unsafe, highlighting a significant gap between public perception and actual security conditions. This data underscores the critical role of open data in exposing societal vulnerabilities and fostering transparency regarding public safety perceptions across different demographics and regions. By tracking these perceptions and behaviors, such open datasets empower policymakers to design targeted interventions that address the root causes of insecurity. Consequently, accessible statistics become essential tools for accountability, ensuring that government actions align with citizens' lived experiences and restoring trust in public institutions through evidence-based decision-making.
