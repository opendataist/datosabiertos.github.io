Jalisco alcanza índices más bajos en percepción de inseguridad | HidrocalidoDigital.com

Jalisco reports a significant decrease in perceived insecurity during the first quarter, approaching the national average and reflecting improved public trust in the performance of local police. This trend highlights the effectiveness of coordinated efforts across federal, state, and municipal governments in maintaining safety standards. The article is relevant to open data because it relies on publicly available statistics from INEGI’s national survey to validate policy outcomes. By making such metrics accessible, authorities enable citizens and researchers to objectively assess governance efficiency, fostering transparency and accountability in public security management. Despite these positive indicators, officials acknowledge ongoing challenges, emphasizing a sustained commitment to reducing crime rates. This continuous improvement strategy underscores the importance of using reliable, open datasets to guide long-term security policies and restore societal peace in the region.
