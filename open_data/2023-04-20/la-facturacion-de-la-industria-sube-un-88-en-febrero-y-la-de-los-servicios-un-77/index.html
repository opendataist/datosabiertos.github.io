La facturación de la industria sube un 8,8% en febrero y la de los servicios, un 7,7%

The Spanish industrial and service sectors have recorded twenty-four consecutive months of growth following the pandemic shock, signaling a robust and sustained economic recovery. This steady expansion indicates that the national economy has successfully stabilized and is generating consistent momentum across key productive areas, moving beyond the initial post-crisis volatility toward a more resilient operational baseline. Geographically and sectorally, the benefits of this recovery are widespread, although specific regions such as the Basque Country and tourism-dependent islands have experienced the most significant gains. The broad increase in business turnover across nearly all autonomous communities highlights a unified national trend of revitalization, demonstrating that the rebound is not confined to isolated hubs but reflects a systemic improvement in commercial activity throughout the country. Furthermore, this economic upturn is supported by a parallel rise in employment, with job numbers increasing across most regions and sectors. This correlation between revenue growth and hiring underscores the positive multiplier effect of the current economic phase, reinforcing labor market stability as a direct consequence of improved performance in the industrial and service sectors. This article is relevant to open data, as it exemplifies how publicly accessible, high-frequency statistical indicators from national institutes enable the continuous monitoring of macroeconomic health. Such transparent data infrastructure allows stakeholders to track real-time trends, validate policy impacts, and ensure that digital public infrastructure accurately reflects the economic realities of society.
