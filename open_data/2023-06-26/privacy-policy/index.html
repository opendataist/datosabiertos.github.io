privacy policy

Market Business News maintains a minimal data collection strategy, relying on voluntary user input rather than intrusive tracking. This approach aligns with open data principles by prioritizing user autonomy and transparency over commercial exploitation of personal information. The entity operates from Mexico while adhering to strict privacy frameworks, demonstrating that global news platforms can responsibly manage user interactions without compromising individual privacy rights. Third-party services are employed strictly for essential operational functions such as security, performance analytics, and contextual advertising. Crucially, the organization explicitly states it does not sell or share personal data with external parties beyond these necessary service providers. This commitment to data limitation ensures that user information remains contained within a controlled environment, reducing the risk of unauthorized dissemination or misuse by unrelated entities. Users retain significant control over their digital footprint, with comprehensive rights granted under GDPR and CCPA regulations. These include access, correction, deletion, and portability options, ensuring individuals can manage their data effectively. This model highlights the importance of clear consent mechanisms and accessible communication channels in maintaining trust, offering a blueprint for how digital publishers can balance functionality with robust ethical data stewardship in an increasingly regulated global landscape.
