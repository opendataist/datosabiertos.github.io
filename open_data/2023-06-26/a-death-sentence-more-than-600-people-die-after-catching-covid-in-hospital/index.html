‘A death sentence’: More than 600 people die after catching COVID in hospital

Hospital-acquired COVID-19 infections have resulted in hundreds of deaths in Victoria, highlighting a critical failure in patient safety protocols. Medical experts warn that vulnerable individuals face significantly higher mortality risks when exposed to the virus in healthcare settings, often exacerbated by pre-existing conditions. This underscores the lethal consequences of treating COVID-19 as a routine illness rather than a high-containment threat, revealing that even vaccinated patients are not immune to fatal outcomes within clinical environments. The relaxation of infection control measures, including the removal of mandatory notification for severe cases and reduced mask requirements, has been criticized as a dangerous regression. Health professionals argue that inconsistent policies create unsafe environments, delaying essential care as patients fear exposure. This shift prioritizes operational convenience over rigorous protection, leaving healthcare workers and patients exposed to avoidable risks during waves that continue to circulate within medical facilities. This report is vital to open data advocacy because it demonstrates the power of freedom of information requests in exposing hidden public health crises. The revelations only emerged after officials deliberately stopped tracking and reporting these deaths as sentinel events, effectively obscuring the true scale of the tragedy. Transparent, accessible data is essential for holding institutions accountable, ensuring that safety metrics are not artificially lowered to mask systemic failures, and enabling the public to understand the real risks associated with healthcare infrastructure.
