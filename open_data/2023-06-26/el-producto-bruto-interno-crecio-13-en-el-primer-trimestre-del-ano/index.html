El Producto Bruto Interno creció 1,3% en el primer trimestre del año

The article highlights Argentina’s modest GDP growth, driven by increased private and public consumption despite significant declines in capital formation and exports. This contrast reveals an economy reliant on domestic demand rather than investment or international trade. The data shows mixed results within fixed capital, with declines in construction and machinery offset by gains in energy and transport. This fragmentation indicates structural vulnerabilities and a lack of broad-based industrial recovery.
