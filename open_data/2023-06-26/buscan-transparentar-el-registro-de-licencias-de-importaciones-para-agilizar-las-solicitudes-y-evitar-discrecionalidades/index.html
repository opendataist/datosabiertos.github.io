Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The Civic Coalition in Argentina proposes the creation of an Open Import Data Portal to combat corruption and arbitrariness in the import licensing system. Lawmakers argue that the current discretion in approving applications creates serious risks of abuse of power. Therefore, they call for all public information related to imports, including the status of applications and amounts, to be published in full, digitally, and in open formats, thereby ensuring transparent and predictable management for all citizens. The project aims to replace the opacity of the current system with simple and automated administrative procedures. It establishes a strict deadline for resolving import licenses, with the provision that failure to respond automatically results in the approval of the application. This measure seeks to eliminate unjustified delays and the lack of justification for rejections, ensuring that the criteria for determining taxpayers’ financial capacity are public and clear. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates how the systematic publication of government information in accessible formats can serve as a tool for accountability. By making import data public, the possibility of corrupt practices is reduced, and civil society is enabled to monitor compliance with regulations, aligning with international governance principles that link data transparency with institutional integrity and economic efficiency.
