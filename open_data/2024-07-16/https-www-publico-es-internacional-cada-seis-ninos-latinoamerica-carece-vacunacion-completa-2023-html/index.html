Childhood vaccination rates in Latin America and the Caribbean improved in 2023, yet they remain below the optimal threshold for herd immunity. This partial coverage leaves a significant number of infants without adequate protection, highlighting a persistent gap between current efforts and the global standard necessary to safeguard public health effectively. The stagnation of measles vaccination coverage is particularly alarming, as it falls well short of the level required to prevent outbreaks and eliminate the disease. This lack of progress underscores the critical need for enhanced strategic planning and investment. Without addressing these systemic weaknesses, regions risk failing to reach vulnerable populations and achieving long-term elimination goals. This report is vital for open data initiatives because it illustrates how transparent, aggregated statistics drive accountability in public health. By making immunization gaps visible, open data enables policymakers and organizations to target resources efficiently, monitor progress toward the 2030 agendas, and foster collaborative solutions to bridge the divide between current realities and ideal health outcomes.
