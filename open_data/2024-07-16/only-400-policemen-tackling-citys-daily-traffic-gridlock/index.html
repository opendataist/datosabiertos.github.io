Only 400 policemen tackling city’s daily traffic gridlock

Only 400 policemen tackling city’s daily traffic gridlock

Nagpur faces severe traffic chaos and accidents largely due to a critical shortage of traffic police personnel. An RTI application revealed that many positions remain vacant, leaving significantly fewer officers available than required for effective city management. This understaffing prevents proper law enforcement during peak hours, exacerbating congestion and safety risks. The issue highlights the limitations of relying on manual enforcement without adequate resources. With only a fraction of the recommended staff available daily, the system cannot adequately address the growing volume of citizens and vehicles. The disparity between current manpower and the city’s massive population underscores a systemic failure in urban traffic governance. This case is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how transparency tools like the Right to Information can expose operational inefficiencies. By making staffing data public, advocates can pressure authorities to allocate resources based on actual needs rather than assumptions. Open data thus serves as a catalyst for accountability and evidence-based policy reform in public safety sectors.
