Observatorio Turístico, la nueva plataforma que impulsa estrategias sostenibles para destinos en Chile - La Tercera

Chile’s tourism sector is heavily shaped by extreme seasonality, with visitor demand concentrated primarily during the summer months. This pattern poses challenges for sustainable development, as destinations struggle to maintain economic viability and manage environmental impacts outside of peak periods. The core issue is not simply a lack of interest in off-season travel, but rather a need for diversified offerings that can attract visitors throughout the year, thereby reducing pressure on infrastructure and ecosystems during high-demand periods. To address this challenge, the University San Sebastián launched the OGESTUR_CHILE platform, a pioneering tool designed to monitor tourism behavior, visitor perceptions, and the development of local businesses. This initiative provides strategic indicators and best practices to support decision-making, particularly for emerging destinations seeking long-term sustainability. By analyzing data on tourist origins and seasonal fluctuations, stakeholders can better understand market dynamics and plan interventions that go beyond simple statistics to inform actionable governance strategies. This platform is highly relevant to the open data community because it promotes transparency and public access to critical tourism information. By making data on sustainability practices and tourist behavior freely available to the public, government bodies, and private operators, it fosters a collaborative environment for improving destination management. This approach exemplifies how open data can drive evidence-based policy, enhance regional governance, and encourage the responsible growth of the tourism industry through shared knowledge and interdisciplinary engagement.
