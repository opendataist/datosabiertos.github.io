Panamá: IPC registró una variación negativa de 0,31% en junio 2024

Panama’s urban consumer prices experienced a slight monthly decline, reflecting modest deflationary pressures in key sectors such as communications, transport, and clothing. This trend suggests reduced costs for essential services and goods, potentially easing household financial burdens during this period. The reduction is primarily driven by lower prices for air travel, fuel, and basic apparel. These decreases indicate fluctuating market dynamics in the service and retail industries, influencing overall cost-of-living metrics. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies high-quality, publicly accessible statistical information. By providing detailed, granular economic indicators, government institutions such as INEC empower researchers and citizens to analyze market trends, fostering transparency and informed decision-making based on reliable official statistics.
