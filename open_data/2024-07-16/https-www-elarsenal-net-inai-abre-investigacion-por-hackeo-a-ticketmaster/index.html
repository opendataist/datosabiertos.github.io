The INAI has launched an official investigation into Ticketmaster for the alleged unauthorized disclosure of users’ personal data following a significant cybersecurity breach. This action underscores the mandatory obligation of private entities to maintain rigorous security measures, protecting sensitive information against loss or unauthorized access as required by federal law. The regulator emphasizes that businesses must implement comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent data vulnerabilities. Consequently, companies handling personal information face strict accountability when security failures expose critical details such as contact data and payment credentials, reinforcing the legal necessity of robust privacy protocols. This case is crucial for the open data community, as it highlights the tension between data accessibility and the imperative of privacy protection. It serves as a stark reminder that while data drives innovation, the responsible management and secure handling of personal information are fundamental prerequisites for maintaining public trust and ensuring ethical compliance within data ecosystems.
