El INE abre a la revisión internacional el Censo 2024; la mesa técnica será el lunes

El INE abre a la revisión internacional el Censo 2024; la mesa técnica será el lunes

Public distrust regarding the results of Bolivia’s 2024 census has prompted the National Institute of Statistics to launch a technical review process. By convening a broad coalition of regional authorities and institutions on a review board, the Institute aims to address widespread skepticism. This step underscores the critical importance of transparency in government statistics, as open data initiatives depend heavily on public confidence to remain viable and useful for societal planning. Regional governments in key areas have challenged the preliminary figures, citing discrepancies with local projections and demanding independent verification. As a result, technical commissions have begun working to compare national data with regional demographic indicators. This tension highlights a major challenge in open data: the need for rigorous validation mechanisms. Without such mechanisms, local discrepancies can erode trust, making it difficult to effectively integrate accurate, open datasets into regional decision-making processes. However, the review process faces logistical hurdles, with some local leaders imposing conditions on their participation to ensure accountability from the Institute’s director. This standoff illustrates the complex negotiations required between central statistical bodies and local entities. For open data communities, this case serves as a vital lesson on the need for inclusive, verifiable data governance. Establishing clear, accessible channels for data verification is essential to resolving disputes and ensuring that statistical information serves all stakeholders equitably.
