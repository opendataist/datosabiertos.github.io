Preocupa uso ético de la IA; la herramienta valió 196 mil millones de dólares en 2023

The rapid expansion of the Artificial Intelligence market underscores an urgent global need for ethical frameworks that balance technological innovation with the protection of human rights. A primary concern involves addressing complex questions regarding authorship and intellectual property, as existing laws often lag behind the dynamic nature of generative AI technologies. Regulatory efforts worldwide are increasingly focusing on flexible frameworks that require both users and providers to ensure responsible data usage. This includes establishing clear attribution mechanisms, such as mandatory labeling for AI-generated content, to mitigate risks like unauthorized image manipulation and preserve legal clarity in industries ranging from healthcare to marketing. This discourse is critical for open data, as it establishes the governance standards necessary for AI development. Ensuring ethical compliance and protecting intellectual property rights fosters trust in data ecosystems, enabling the responsible integration of open datasets into AI models without compromising privacy or legal ownership.
