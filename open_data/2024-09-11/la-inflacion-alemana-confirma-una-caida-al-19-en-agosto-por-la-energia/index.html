La inflación alemana confirma una caída al 1,9% en agosto por la energía

Germany’s consumer price index fell to 1.9% in August 2024, marking the lowest inflation rate in over three years. This significant decline was primarily driven by a substantial drop in energy prices, which offset upward pressures from service costs. While energy deflation provided relief, core inflation metrics excluding food and energy remained elevated near 3%, indicating that underlying price pressures persist in key sectors of the economy. The divergence between headline inflation and core metrics highlights a complex economic landscape where specific categories continue to face rising costs. Although food prices increased, the rise was moderate compared to the general inflation rate, with notable spikes in edible oils but decreases in dairy products. This selective inflation suggests that while broad consumer prices are stabilizing, structural cost increases in services and specific goods remain a concern for households and policymakers alike.
