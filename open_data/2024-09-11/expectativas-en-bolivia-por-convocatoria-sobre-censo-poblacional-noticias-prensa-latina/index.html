Expectativas en Bolivia por convocatoria sobre censo poblacional - Noticias Prensa Latina

Expectativas en Bolivia por convocatoria sobre censo poblacional - Noticias Prensa Latina

The National Statistics Institute (INE) is launching a technical forum to address concerns and clarify technical doubts regarding the upcoming 2024 Population and Housing Census. This initiative aims to ensure transparency by inviting departmental and municipal authorities, universities, and social organizations to participate, allowing them to directly engage with the institution responsible for executing the census. A key element of this process is the invitation of high-level international commissions, including representatives from the United Nations and its specialized agencies. The INE has expressed its readiness to undergo an independent technical review of the census methodologies and results by these international experts, demonstrating a commitment to rigorous standards and external validation. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it establishes a framework for greater transparency and accountability in public data collection. By facilitating external oversight and addressing technical questions publicly, the INE strengthens the credibility of the resulting statistical information. This approach ensures that the generated data is reliable, verifiable, and trustworthy for society, which are fundamental principles for effective open data ecosystems.
