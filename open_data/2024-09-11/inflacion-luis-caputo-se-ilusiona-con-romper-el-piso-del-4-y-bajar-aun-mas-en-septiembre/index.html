Inflación: Luis Caputo se ilusiona con romper el piso del 4% y bajar aún más en septiembre

Inflación: Luis Caputo se ilusiona con romper el piso del 4% y bajar aún más en septiembre

Argentina is preparing to release its August inflation data, with private analysts forecasting a slight deceleration that would maintain recent trends, despite the government’s expectations of a sharper decline. While the Ministry of Economy anticipates lower figures driven by tax adjustments and fiscal discipline, experts remain cautious, noting that reducing inflationary inertia becomes progressively more difficult as rates stabilize at higher levels. This divergence highlights the tension between official optimism and the persistent structural challenges in achieving sustainable price stability. The debate extends beyond mere numbers, focusing on the reliability and methodology of official statistics. The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) is implementing a significant update to its calculation methodology, expanding the volume of prices and retailers surveyed to better reflect household expenditure patterns. This structural change aims to improve the accuracy of the Consumer Price Index, which is crucial for monitoring economic performance and validating policy outcomes in a context of high volatility. This article is relevant to open data as it underscores the importance of transparent, robust, and standardized statistical methodologies in public administration. Accurate open data is essential for fostering trust between the government and society, enabling analysts and citizens to verify claims and make informed decisions. As measurement frameworks evolve, ensuring that the underlying data processes are accessible and understandable remains a key challenge for effective governance and economic analysis.
