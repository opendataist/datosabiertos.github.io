5,800 incidents of property damage at Tusla care centres

Tusla data reveals over 5,800 property damage incidents in residential care centers, highlighting the complex behavioral challenges faced by traumatized youth. Experts emphasize that these acts often stem from unmanaged emotions like anger and rejection, necessitating supportive coping mechanisms rather than punitive measures. This narrative underscores the critical importance of transparent, open data in social services. By making incident records public, stakeholders can better understand systemic issues, advocate for appropriate resource allocation, and ensure accountability for the welfare of vulnerable children in state care.
