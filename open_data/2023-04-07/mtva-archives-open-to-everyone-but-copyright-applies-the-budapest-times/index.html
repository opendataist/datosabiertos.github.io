MTVA archives open to everyone but copyright applies - The Budapest Times

MTVA provides vast public access to its archives while asserting strict copyright enforcement against unauthorized use. This balance highlights the complex legal landscape governing open access versus intellectual property rights in digital journalism. Understanding these distinctions is vital for open data advocates navigating copyright restrictions on public media assets.
