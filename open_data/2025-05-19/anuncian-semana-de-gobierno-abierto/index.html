Anuncian Semana de Gobierno Abierto

Anuncian Semana de Gobierno Abierto

Chihuahua is hosting Open Government Week 2025 to promote civic participation, accountability, and inclusive transparency. By bringing together diverse social sectors to engage in dialogue with public officials, the initiative seeks to build stronger, more responsible communities grounded in the values of open governance. This event reinforces the crucial link between government and civil society, ensuring that public service delivery aligns with citizens’ needs. It fosters a culture in which institutions are accountable and accessible, directly supporting the democratic principles essential for effective public administration. The article is relevant to open data because it underscores the foundational commitment to transparency and access to public information. Such engagement ensures that data is not merely published but is actively used to drive accountability, enabling citizens to monitor government actions and participate meaningfully in policy discussions.
