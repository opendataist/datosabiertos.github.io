Inflación en Ecuador fue 0,22% al cierre de agosto 2024

The report highlights a significant stabilization in Ecuador’s economic indicators, primarily characterized by a substantial deceleration in annual inflation to 1.28% by August 2024. This figure marks a notable improvement compared to the 2.56% recorded in the same month of the previous year, reflecting a broader trend of price moderation across the economy. The data indicates that while monthly fluctuations persist, the pressure on consumer prices has eased considerably over the twelve-month period. Crucially, the financial well-being of households appears robust, as average monthly family income surpassed the cost of the Basic Family Basket by approximately seven percent. This positive gap suggests that the typical household has sufficient purchasing power to cover essential goods, including food and housing. The analysis reveals that certain sectors, such as food and energy, contributed most to recent price variations, yet the overall coverage of the basket by income remains a key indicator of economic stability for the population. This information is highly relevant to the open data community because it exemplifies the necessity of transparent, granular statistical reporting for public accountability. By releasing detailed breakdowns of inflation by region, product category, and tax status, the National Institute of Statistics and Census enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to verify economic narratives and analyze regional disparities. Such data empowers civil society to monitor government policy effectiveness and ensures that economic assessments are grounded in accessible, verified facts rather than abstract figures.
