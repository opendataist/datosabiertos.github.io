Presentan cortometrajes sobre acceso a la información y protección de datos en la Cineteca Nacional - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

The collaboration between PROCINECDMX and INFO CDMX highlights a strategic approach to democratizing data by integrating transparency initiatives into cultural spaces. By using cinema as an accessible medium for debate, the institutions aim to foster a broader civic culture that values accountability and the right to information. This approach bridges the gap between bureaucratic functions and public understanding, making open data concepts more relatable through artistic expression. Short films addressing the societal impacts of artificial intelligence and social media were screened to critically examine privacy risks and data protection challenges. These narratives serve as allegories for contemporary anxieties surrounding technology, encouraging audiences to reflect on the dangers of misuse. The artistic critique transforms complex technical issues into engaging discussions, promoting critical thinking about digital rights and the importance of safeguarding personal information in a hyper-connected world. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency agencies can leverage non-traditional channels to enhance public engagement and education. By partnering with creative producers, government bodies can effectively communicate the democratic value of questioning public information and ensuring accountability. Such partnerships expand the reach of open government principles, ensuring that transparency becomes an integral part of everyday cultural consumption rather than a purely administrative procedure.
