La vivienda anota su mayor subida en dos años: se encarece un 7,8% en el segundo trimestre del año

The real estate market has definitively exited its crisis phase, marked by a significant rise in free housing prices during the second quarter. This surge is primarily driven by a critical shortage of supply, which has prompted buyers to accelerate purchases to secure more affordable mortgages. The trend indicates a robust recovery, with new housing experiencing particularly sharp increases compared to the previous year. Regional disparities highlight the intensity of this demand, with areas like Navarre, Aragon, and Andalusia recording the highest percentage growths. Meanwhile, other regions showed more moderate increases, yet all exceeded the national average in recent quarters. This widespread appreciation suggests that the tightening market conditions are not isolated but represent a broader structural shift in the country's housing sector, affecting availability and affordability across diverse geographical zones. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and timely statistical information. Accurate public indices empower consumers to make informed financial decisions amidst volatile markets. Furthermore, open access to such granular regional and seasonal data enables better policy analysis, helping authorities address supply shortages and regulate the housing market effectively for long-term stability.
