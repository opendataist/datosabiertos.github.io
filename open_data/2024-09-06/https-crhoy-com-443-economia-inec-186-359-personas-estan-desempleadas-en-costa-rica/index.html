National unemployment declined significantly on a year-over-year basis, with gender gaps persisting as women faced higher joblessness rates. Occupational levels remained stable overall, although regional disparities were evident, with the Central region leading and Brunca lagging behind. Informal employment affected nearly forty percent of the workforce, highlighting a persistent structural challenge within the labor market. These trends underscore the need for policies addressing both regional inequities and the prevalence of informal work. This data is relevant to open_data as it exemplifies how transparent, standardized labor statistics enable rigorous analysis of socioeconomic inequalities. By making such granular indicators accessible, governments and researchers can better monitor progress toward inclusive economic development.
