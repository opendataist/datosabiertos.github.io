Cívicos de Tarija respaldan el paro cívico de Santa Cruz en rechazo al Censo de Población 2024 | El Deber

Cívicos de Tarija respaldan el paro cívico de Santa Cruz en rechazo al Censo de Población 2024 | El Deber

Civic movements in Tarija and Potosí are aligning with Santa Cruz to demand transparency and technical audits of the 2024 Census. This widespread rejection stems from accusations that the National Institute of Statistics (INE) has systematically altered population data, leading to a severe crisis of institutional credibility. The lack of trust is not limited to one region, but represents a broader national concern regarding the reliability of state statistics. The situation highlights the critical importance of open data in maintaining public trust in governance. When statistical institutions fail to provide transparent, auditable, and accessible data, it fuels political instability and public skepticism. Ensuring data integrity and openness is essential for democratic accountability, allowing citizens to verify information and hold institutions responsible for their findings. This regional mobilization underscores how data disputes can escalate into significant civic actions. It serves as a stark reminder that open data is not merely a technical requirement but a fundamental political issue. Without verifiable and open statistical processes, the legitimacy of public policies and government actions remains perpetually contested by an informed and engaged populace.
