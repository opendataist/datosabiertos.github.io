La pobreza en Argentina aumentó al 55,5% y la indigencia al 17,5%, según la UCA

La pobreza en Argentina aumentó al 55,5% y la indigencia al 17,5%, según la UCA

The article highlights a severe deterioration in Argentina’s socioeconomic landscape, where inflation and soaring food prices have drastically increased poverty and extreme poverty rates compared to previous levels. This surge primarily impacts lower-income urban populations, creating a critical emergency in which basic subsistence has become unattainable for millions, fundamentally altering quality of life and social stability. Insecurity regarding access to adequate nutrition has reached alarming levels, particularly affecting children and vulnerable households. The data reveal that a significant portion of the population lacks sufficient food, forcing reliance on emergency measures. This underscores the depth of the humanitarian crisis, as traditional income levels fail to cover even the most essential dietary needs, signaling a systemic failure in economic protections. This report is crucial for open data advocacy, demonstrating how independent observatories fill critical gaps when official statistics are delayed or disputed. It emphasizes the necessity of transparent, real-time data sources to accurately monitor social welfare and guide effective policy interventions, ensuring that decision-makers and the public have immediate access to reliable indicators of societal well-being.
