Dell Technologies and Red Hat have partnered to integrate Red Hat Enterprise Linux AI onto Dell PowerEdge servers, creating a validated, optimized platform for developing and deploying generative AI models. This collaboration ensures that businesses can scale their IT infrastructure with confidence, as the hardware and software are continuously tested and validated together. By simplifying the AI experience, the solution allows organizations to implement machine learning strategies more seamlessly across hybrid cloud environments, reducing the complexity often associated with managing diverse AI workloads. The platform leverages open-source foundations, incorporating IBM’s Granite large language models and the community-driven InstructLab methodology for model alignment. Packaged as an optimized Red Hat Enterprise Linux image and included in Red Hat OpenShift AI, it supports scalable model operation across distributed clusters. This approach highlights the power of open-source tools in accelerating innovation, allowing developers to experiment with and refine AI models using trusted, collaborative methodologies rather than relying solely on proprietary black-box systems. This partnership is relevant to open data and open source because it demonstrates how open-source operating systems and community-driven model development can underpin enterprise-grade AI infrastructure. By prioritizing validated open-source stacks, Red Hat and Dell show that transparency and community collaboration are essential for building reliable, scalable AI solutions. This reduces vendor lock-in and empowers organizations to maintain control over their data and models, aligning with the core principles of open technology where interoperability and community contribution drive progress.
