Estos son los nombres preferidos de los cántabros para sus hijos

The National Statistics Institute’s release of 2024 demographic data reveals a slight decline in national births while confirming Mateo and Lucía as the most popular names. In Cantabria, birth rates increased slightly, maintaining the national naming trends while also highlighting distinct regional preferences. This information illustrates the value of granular open data, which allows for the comparison of local cultural patterns against national averages, revealing both convergence and unique community behaviors in naming conventions. Analyzing this open dataset exposes how regional identities manifest within broader statistical frameworks. While Cantabria largely mirrors national preferences for traditional names, it also embraces specific local choices absent from the top national rankings. This demonstrates that open data provides more than just aggregate numbers; it offers a narrative lens to understand how local traditions persist or evolve alongside standardized national trends, fostering a deeper comprehension of societal shifts. This article is relevant to open data because it showcases the utility of accessible, detailed public records in uncovering nuanced social insights. By publishing specific birth and naming statistics, institutions enable researchers and citizens to perform comparative analyses that highlight regional diversity. Such transparency supports evidence-based discussions on cultural identity and demographic changes, emphasizing how open data empowers communities to observe and interpret their own distinct characteristics within the larger national context.
