MORNING GLORY: Wikipedia is for suckers

MORNING GLORY: Wikipedia is for suckers

The article argues that Wikipedia, often perceived as a neutral crowdsourced resource, is actually a tool for ideological manipulation by a small, biased group of editors. It contends that systemic bias favors specific political and social viewpoints, creating a distorted information environment. This revelation challenges the foundational trust many users place in the platform as an objective reference for historical and contemporary events. The implications extend beyond human readers, as the article highlights how artificial intelligence models rely on these platforms for training data. By allowing biased content to persist, Wikipedia inadvertently poisons the data pipelines of large language models. This creates a feedback loop where AI outputs reflect these skewed narratives, amplifying misinformation and reducing the reliability of automated information synthesis across the digital ecosystem. This is critical to open data because it exposes the fragility of open-source knowledge systems when not properly governed. It underscores the urgent need for rigorous data hygiene, transparency in editing processes, and diverse oversight in open data projects. Without addressing these vulnerabilities, open data initiatives risk perpetuating biases rather than providing unbiased, reliable information, thereby undermining their potential to support accurate public understanding and decision-making.
