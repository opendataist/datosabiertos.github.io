El desempleo baja en 349 personas en noviembre en C-LM y la región contabiliza 117.792 parados

El desempleo baja en 349 personas en noviembre en C-LM y la región contabiliza 117.792 parados

Castilla-La Mancha recorded its lowest unemployment rate in November since 2007, marking a significant decline compared to the previous year. The reduction was primarily concentrated in the services sector, while industry experienced a slight increase. This positive trend is also reflected in hiring, where the total volume of contracts has grown slightly, although job insecurity persists due to the high proportion of temporary jobs compared to permanent ones. At the national level, the trend is reinforced by the lowest number of unemployed people since 2007, driven especially by the drop in the services sector and among the female population. Young people also show a notable improvement, reaching their lowest historical levels at this time. This dynamic is not uniform, as while regions such as Andalusia and Madrid lead the reductions, others like the Balearic Islands record increases, highlighting regional disparities in the labor market. This report is relevant for open data because it exemplifies the importance of public socioeconomic indicators for monitoring social well-being. The availability of these disaggregated data by sex, age, and sector allows researchers and citizens to analyze structural inequalities and the effectiveness of employment policies. Transparency in this information facilitates accountability and evidence-based decision-making to improve territorial and labor cohesion.
