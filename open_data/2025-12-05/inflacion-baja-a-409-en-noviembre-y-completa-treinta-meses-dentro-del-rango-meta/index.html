Inflación baja a 4,09% en noviembre y completa treinta meses dentro del rango meta

Inflación baja a 4,09% en noviembre y completa treinta meses dentro del rango meta

Year-on-year inflation in Argentina declined in November, falling below the level recorded a year ago and maintaining its position within the government’s target range for three consecutive years. This figure reflects a sustained trend of stability in consumer prices, with moderate annual variation and contained cumulative inflation for the year to date. The continued adherence to the official target suggests that monetary and fiscal policies have effectively contained inflationary pressures over this extended period. Overall price movements were driven by divergent dynamics across different economic sectors. While the health, transportation, and accommodation services categories recorded increases that positively impacted the overall index, these rises were offset by sharp declines in food prices. Volatility was concentrated significantly in the fruits and vegetables segment, where the abrupt drop in the cost of products such as tomatoes and onions contrasted with notable increases in other categories like meat and certain citrus fruits, shaping the final profile of the index. This information is relevant to the open data field because it highlights the importance of accessing and analyzing detailed time series of macroeconomic indicators. Transparency in publishing sectoral breakdowns enables independent auditing and deeper investigation into the structural causes of inflation. Open access to granular data facilitates the creation of visualizations and analyses that go beyond aggregate figures, allowing civil society and researchers to better understand the differential impact of inflation on household budgets.
