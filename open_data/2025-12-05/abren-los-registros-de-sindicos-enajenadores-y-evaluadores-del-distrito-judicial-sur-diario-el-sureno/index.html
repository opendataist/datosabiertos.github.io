Abren los Registros de Síndicos, Enajenadores y Evaluadores del Distrito Judicial Sur - Diario El Sureño

Abren los Registros de Síndicos, Enajenadores y Evaluadores del Distrito Judicial Sur - Diario El Sureño

The Ushuaia Appeals Court has opened registration for professionals to join the official bankruptcy registries established under National Law 24.522. This initiative ensures transparency and accountability in judicial processes by allowing only qualified auditors, auctioneers, and appraisers to participate in asset liquidation and valuation tasks. Public access to these updated official lists empowers stakeholders to verify the credentials of individuals handling insolvency proceedings. Consequently, this mechanism reinforces trust in the judicial system by guaranteeing that only authorized experts manage sensitive financial and legal operations during bankruptcy cases. This update is relevant to open data because it expands the availability of verified professional directories. By publishing eligibility criteria and registration deadlines, the court promotes public access to essential legal information, fostering greater institutional transparency and enabling citizens to hold judicial entities accountable.
