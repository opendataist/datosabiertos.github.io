Valencia es la primera ciudad de la Comunitat que se apunta al registro de transparencia Regia

Valencia has joined the Valencian Community’s Registry of Interest Groups, thereby strengthening transparency and accountability in public administration. This step aligns municipal practices with regional standards, ensuring that lobbying activities carried out by interest groups are publicly recorded. By adhering to this autonomous framework, the city aims to prevent duplication of efforts while enhancing institutional robustness and safeguarding against undue influence. The initiative guarantees that information on influence activities is accessible to citizens through open portals. This commitment to publishing registry data promotes civic oversight and reinforces the principles of open government. It enables the public to monitor interactions between the administration and interest groups, fostering trust through transparency. This development is crucial for open data, as it broadens the scope of publicly available government information. It illustrates how local administrations can integrate into broader transparency ecosystems. Such integration ensures that data is not only accessible but also standardized and interoperable, supporting a more coherent and accessible open government landscape across different administrative levels.
