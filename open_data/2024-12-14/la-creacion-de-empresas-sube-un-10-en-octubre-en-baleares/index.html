La creación de empresas sube un 10 % en octubre en Baleares

La creación de empresas sube un 10 % en octubre en Baleares

The Balearic Islands recorded the highest number of new business formations in October in recent years, marking four consecutive months of growth. Despite a decline in the number of startups and lower initial capital, this trend reflects evolving investment patterns. At the national level, the landscape reveals stark regional disparities. Some regions are driving expansion, while others are experiencing higher rates of business closures, underscoring a fragmented economic recovery across Spain. This data is significant for open data initiatives, as it provides essential indicators for economic transparency. Monitoring these metrics enables researchers to assess market health and supports evidence-based policymaking concerning local enterprise ecosystems.
