Producción de granos de China supera los 700 millones de toneladas y marca récord en 2024 - Confirmado

Producción de granos de China supera los 700 millones de toneladas y marca récord en 2024 - Confirmado

China has achieved a historic record in grain production for 2024, consolidating its agricultural capacity despite climatic challenges. This achievement significantly strengthens national food security and lays the foundation for rural revitalization and the country’s high-quality economic development. The expansion of sown area and the increase in yield per unit are key factors behind this abundant harvest. Although soybean production declined slightly, the overall increase in cereals and tubers ensures internal stability. These results demonstrate the resilience of China’s agricultural system in the face of recent natural disasters. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical value of transparency in official statistics. Accurate and accessible data on food production enable the analysis of global trends, promote government accountability, and facilitate independent research on food security and sustainable development.
