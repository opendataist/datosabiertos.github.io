Workshop focus on govt partnership

Papua New Guinea is developing its next National Action Plan through a multi-stakeholder workshop. This aligns with the global Open Government Partnership initiative, which involves over eighty nations working toward transparency and accountability. Such efforts are crucial for open data, ensuring citizen access to government information and fostering public engagement in governance.
