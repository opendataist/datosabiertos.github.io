Chocolate maker Cadbury is fighting AI by injecting gibberish into training data

Cadbury India’s satirical campaign highlights a growing anxiety about AI’s impact on workplace productivity, suggesting that rather than simplifying tasks, artificial intelligence often accelerates work demands and increases employee stress. This narrative resonates with open data communities who observe that unchecked AI integration can lead to diminishing returns in efficiency, forcing humans to manage increasingly complex, AI-driven workflows rather than finding leisure. The campaign proposes a provocative method to counter AI efficiency by flooding training datasets with synthetic nonsense, thereby degrading model performance and necessitating human correction. This strategy underscores the critical importance of data quality in the open data ecosystem, illustrating how the integrity of publicly available information directly shapes the capabilities and reliability of the models built upon it. Ultimately, this initiative serves as a cautionary tale about the consequences of relying on low-quality or uncurated open data sources. It emphasizes that the value of open data lies not just in its volume, but in its precision and authenticity, warning that polluting these shared resources can lead to systemic errors that burden society rather than alleviate it.
