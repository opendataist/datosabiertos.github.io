Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The article highlights global potato production statistics sourced from the Food and Agriculture Organization, emphasizing the critical role of open international data in agricultural monitoring. It identifies China and India as the dominant producers, together accounting for nearly half of the global output, while noting significant contributions from nations like Ukraine and the USA. This distribution reveals how open data allows researchers and policymakers to understand food security dynamics across diverse economic contexts. By presenting a ranked list of production volumes for nearly one hundred countries, the piece demonstrates the utility of standardized datasets for comparative analysis. The availability of this information enables stakeholders to track trends, assess yield efficiency per hectare, and identify emerging markets without relying on fragmented national reports. Such transparency supports evidence-based decision-making in global supply chains and agricultural investment strategies. This resource is particularly relevant to open data advocates because it illustrates how accessible, harmonized datasets empower diverse users worldwide. Although slight discrepancies may exist between FAO figures and local government statistics, the overall reliability fosters trust in shared information ecosystems. Ultimately, this case study reinforces the value of open portals in democratizing access to vital agricultural intelligence, encouraging broader engagement with public data for sustainable development and research.
