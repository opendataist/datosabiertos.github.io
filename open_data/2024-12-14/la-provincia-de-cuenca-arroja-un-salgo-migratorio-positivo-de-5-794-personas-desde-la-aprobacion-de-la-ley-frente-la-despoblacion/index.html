La provincia de Cuenca arroja un salgo migratorio positivo de 5.794 personas desde la aprobación de la Ley frente la Despoblación

La provincia de Cuenca arroja un salgo migratorio positivo de 5.794 personas desde la aprobación de la Ley frente la Despoblación

The article highlights a positive net migration trend in Cuenca, driven by policies aimed at addressing rural depopulation. A significant majority of newcomers settled in severely depopulated areas rather than urban centers, demonstrating the effectiveness of targeted regional interventions. This shift challenges the narrative of an empty rural Spain by showing tangible population recovery through strategic governance. The success is attributed to consolidated public services, including healthcare and education, alongside economic incentives such as tax breaks and business support. These measures have created viable opportunities for settlement, proving that political will, combined with cooperation among social actors, can effectively reverse demographic decline. This case is crucial for open data, as it validates statistical indicators in policy evaluation. Transparent access to migration statistics allows citizens and researchers to verify the impact of anti-depopulation laws, fostering accountability and enabling evidence-based decisions for sustainable rural development across similar regions.
