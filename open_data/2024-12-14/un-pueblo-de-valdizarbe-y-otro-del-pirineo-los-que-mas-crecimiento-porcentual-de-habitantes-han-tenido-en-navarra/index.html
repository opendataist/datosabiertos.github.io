Un pueblo de Valdizarbe y otro del Pirineo, los que más crecimiento porcentual de habitantes han tenido en Navarra

Navarra has experienced uninterrupted population growth since 2016, driven primarily by immigration rather than natural increase due to declining birth rates. This sustained demographic expansion highlights the critical role of migration data in understanding regional development, as official census records reveal that external mobility is the key engine behind the community's current vitality and economic resilience. However, this growth is highly uneven, concentrating heavily in the Pamplona metropolitan area while rural municipalities face significant depopulation. This spatial disparity underscores an urgent need for granular, localized open data to identify trends accurately. The stark contrast between thriving urban centers and shrinking rural towns illustrates how aggregated statistics can mask deep structural inequalities that affect resource allocation and social planning across different territories. The relevance of this article to open data lies in its demonstration of how transparent, accessible municipal datasets expose these divergent trajectories. By making detailed population statistics public, authorities and citizens can better analyze the "municipal elevator" effect, where specific towns act as demographic ascendants or descents. This visibility is essential for fostering informed policy decisions and community engagement regarding housing, infrastructure, and sustainable development in an increasingly unequal demographic landscape.
