Diario HOY | Revelan que en Paraguay existen un poco más de 156.000 inmigrantes

The article analyzes Paraguay’s demographic shifts through census data, highlighting a significant disparity in migration flows across departments. Central regions experience higher mobility, while others remain relatively static, revealing how internal geographic distribution impacts social and economic dynamics. Foreign populations are dominated by migrants from Argentina and Brazil, reflecting strong regional ties. Despite a slight male predominance among immigrants, the overall proportion of foreigners has declined over the last fifty years, indicating evolving patterns in international settlement trends. This dataset is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular insights into population movements. By making such detailed demographic records accessible, policymakers and researchers can better understand integration challenges, labor market needs, and historical migration contexts, fostering data-driven decision-making in public administration.
