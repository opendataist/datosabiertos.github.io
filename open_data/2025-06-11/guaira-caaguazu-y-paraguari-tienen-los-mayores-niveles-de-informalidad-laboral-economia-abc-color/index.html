Guairá, Caaguazú y Paraguarí tienen los mayores niveles de informalidad laboral - Economía - ABC Color

Guairá, Caaguazú y Paraguarí tienen los mayores niveles de informalidad laboral - Economía - ABC Color

This analysis highlights a critical disparity in Paraguay’s labor market, where specific departments exhibit informal employment rates significantly exceeding the national average. The data reveals that in regions such as Guairá and Caaguazú, more than three-quarters of the workforce lacks basic labor rights, including social security and minimum wage protections. This structural informality persists despite strategic urban proximity, indicating that current economic models fail to integrate these areas into formal production chains. The situation varies by region but shares a common thread of institutional fragility. While some areas rely on informal commerce and domestic tourism, border regions are heavily influenced by cross-border activities operating outside legal frameworks. This contrasts sharply with developed urban centers, exposing deep territorial inequalities. The persistence of these patterns suggests that economic growth has not translated into inclusive labor opportunities for rural and peri-urban populations. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for transparent, granular statistics to drive evidence-based policy. Without accessible, high-resolution datasets, policymakers cannot accurately identify localized barriers to formalization or measure the effectiveness of targeted interventions. Opening these detailed labor metrics is essential for designing multisectoral strategies that combine infrastructure investment with social protection, ultimately aiming to reduce poverty and ensure equitable development across all territories.
