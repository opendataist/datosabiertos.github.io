El desempleo de Reino Unido sube al 4,6% en abril, su nivel más alto desde julio de 2021

El desempleo de Reino Unido sube al 4,6% en abril, su nivel más alto desde julio de 2021

The British labor market is showing a significant slowdown, marked by rising unemployment and a notable decline in wage employment and job vacancies. This trend suggests that companies are delaying hiring, reflecting a general cooling of economic activity and raising concerns about potential deeper deteriorations, although experts do not rule out that this may be a gradual process. Meanwhile, wage growth has decelerated, particularly in the private sector, while the public sector maintains higher growth rates. Although income increases remain historically strong, the slowdown indicates that inflationary pressures are moderating. This creates a complex scenario in which the Bank of England must balance the need to control persistent inflation with the weakness in employment, likely anticipating interest rate cuts in the short to medium term to stimulate the economy. This analysis is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical importance of real-time official statistics for economic transparency and decision-making. Data from the Office for National Statistics (ONS) enable analysts, investors, and citizens to understand the true state of the economy, fostering accountability in monetary policy. The public availability of these indicators facilitates independent verification and informed debate on economic health, which are fundamental pillars of open governance.
