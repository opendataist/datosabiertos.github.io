Instituto mexicano de información presentará plan de modernización - Noticias Prensa Latina

The president of the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) agreed to engage in dialogue with the executive branch to modernize the agency, thereby averting its dissolution despite a proposed constitutional reform. This interaction underscores the ongoing tension between institutional autonomy and government centralization within Mexico’s transparency framework. The potential restructuring signals a critical shift in how public access to information is managed, highlighting the need for robust safeguards for open data mechanisms.
