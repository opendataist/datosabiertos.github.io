Freedom of Information statistics: January to March 2024

This report details the quarterly implementation of the Freedom of Information Act and Environmental Information Regulations within UK central government bodies. By presenting data on how these transparency laws are applied, it offers critical insights into public sector accountability and information access. The statistics are recognized as National Statistics, ensuring they meet rigorous standards for trustworthiness, quality, and impartiality. Independent review by the Office for Statistics Regulation confirms that the data is produced using sound methods and remains accessible, thereby establishing high confidence in the metrics used to evaluate government openness. This resource is vital for open data enthusiasts as it quantifies the real-world application of information rights. It highlights how statutory transparency mechanisms function in practice, providing evidence of the sector’s commitment to releasing data and fostering a culture of administrative transparency for public scrutiny.
