El INE estima una pérdida de 11.000 habitantes en Palencia

The INE projects demographic decline for several provinces in Castile and León, highlighting Palencia’s significant population loss. Conversely, Segovia and Burgos are expected to grow, indicating a divergent regional trend driven by varying birth and migration rates. Households are projected to increase across all autonomous communities during this period. This shift suggests changing living arrangements despite the overall population stagnation or decrease in specific areas, reflecting deeper societal transformations in household formation and structure. This data is crucial for open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent, publicly available statistics enable evidence-based analysis. It allows researchers and citizens to monitor demographic shifts, fostering informed public debate and accountability regarding regional development policies and resource allocation.
