Compañía discográficas demandan a empresas de IA por infracción de derechos de autor | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

Compañía discográficas demandan a empresas de IA por infracción de derechos de autor | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

Major record labels are suing AI-generated music platforms, alleging that they use copyrighted recordings to train their models without authorization. This legal action aims to protect artists’ intellectual property rights and prevent the devaluation of the music market in the face of synthetic productions that directly compete with genuine works. The controversy highlights the tension between rapid technological development and respect for existing intellectual property laws. AI companies argue that they generate original content, while record labels contend that the process involves systematic and illegal copying of protected data, questioning the applicability of exceptions such as fair use in this context. This case is crucial for the fields of open data and algorithmic transparency, as it raises ethical questions about training models on protected data. It underscores the urgency of establishing clear frameworks for access, licensing, and the ethical use of information, influencing how future open and responsible technologies will be developed in the digital age.
