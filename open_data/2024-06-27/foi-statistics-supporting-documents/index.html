FOI Statistics: supporting documents

The article documents the continuous evolution of Freedom of Information statistics development plans over several years. It highlights a consistent commitment to transparency and methodological rigor in public data production. This regular updating process ensures that statistical outputs remain accurate and reliable for users. By maintaining detailed records of scope revisions and methodological changes, the publication strengthens trust in open government data. This practice allows researchers and citizens to understand how data quality is managed and improved over time. Such accountability is crucial for validating the integrity of public sector statistics. This relevance to open data lies in its demonstration of best practices for sustainable statistical governance. It shows that open data requires ongoing maintenance and clear communication about methods. Understanding these development trajectories helps users assess data suitability for their specific needs, promoting more informed decision-making based on reliable public information.
