México suma 70 policías asesinados en lo que va de 2025

The report highlights a critical escalation in violence against Mexican police, with nearly 70 officers killed in early 2025. Municipal forces face the highest vulnerability compared to state and federal counterparts, reflecting severe challenges in local security governance. Civil organization Causa en Común compiled these figures to expose the disproportionate risk borne by local agents. The data reveals geographic concentration in specific states, indicating that organized crime pressures vary significantly across regions and strain local institutional capacities. This analysis is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how civil society aggregates and publishes raw security statistics. Such transparent data collection empowers public scrutiny, supports accountability mechanisms, and provides essential evidence for policymakers aiming to address structural weaknesses in law enforcement protection systems.
