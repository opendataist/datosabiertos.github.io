“Transparencia Internacional” ataca de nuevo – Correo del Caroní

“Transparencia Internacional” ataca de nuevo – Correo del Caroní

Transparency International has updated its Corruption Perceptions Index, highlighting the vast chasm between nations characterized by integrity and those where kleptocracy prevails. The report not only measures institutional efficiency but also reflects citizens’ profound distrust of their leaders. This collective perception serves as a critical thermometer of democratic health, showing how the erosion of trust undermines state legitimacy and normalizes systemic theft in vulnerable regions or under oppressive regimes. The article questions the methodology by pointing out that the index relies exclusively on the perspectives of victims, ignoring the self-perception of corrupt actors. This limitation suggests that external transparency does not always translate into internal accountability. When ruling elites distort reality through denial or indifference, international metrics lose their deterrent power, revealing that without structural changes in accountability mechanisms, such data merely record moral decay rather than acting as catalysts for reform. For the open data movement, this text underscores that visibility of corruption does not guarantee its eradication. The key insight is that transparency alone is insufficient if not accompanied by enforcement mechanisms and political will. Data must drive concrete action, ensuring that openness serves to strengthen civil society and pressure for real change, going beyond simple rankings that merely document institutional failure.
