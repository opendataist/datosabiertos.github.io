Alarma demográfica en la Comunitat Valenciana: récord de envejecimiento con 138 mayores por cada 100 jóvenes

Spain and the Valencian Community have reached historical peaks in aging, with the elderly-to-youth ratio hitting record highs driven by minimal birth rates and increased life expectancy. This demographic shift creates an urgent need to adapt labor markets, as the shrinking pool of young workers threatens the sustainability of the welfare state and business competitiveness. The analysis emphasizes that integrating senior talent is no longer optional but a structural necessity. Companies must overcome selection biases and invest in upskilling to retain experienced workers who offer critical skills like maturity and critical thinking, ensuring that valuable professional knowledge is not lost prematurely due to age-related discrimination. This article is relevant to open_data because it highlights how transparent, publicly available demographic statistics from the National Institute of Statistics serve as the foundational evidence for shaping critical social and economic policies. It demonstrates the vital link between accessible government data and the development of inclusive employment strategies, showing how evidence-based insights can drive meaningful changes in diversity and inclusion frameworks to address societal challenges.
