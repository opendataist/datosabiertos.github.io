Preguntas naturales: un conjunto de datos con 300.000 entradas para entrenar inteligencias artificiales

Google AI released Natural Questions, a large-scale dataset designed to train algorithms to answer everyday natural language queries. This resource enables developers to build and compare systems aimed at narrowing the gap between human performance and machine comprehension. The initiative fosters an open competition, encouraging the community to improve upon current benchmarks. By providing structured data, it promotes transparency and collaborative innovation in artificial intelligence research. This dataset is crucial for open data, as it democratizes access to high-quality training materials. It allows anyone to contribute to advancements in natural language processing, ensuring that the technology evolves through shared knowledge and public scrutiny rather than being confined to private corporate silos.
