El 95% de los hogares pierde calor de la calefacción por la ventana: los consejos para ahorrar en la factura

Thermal imaging confirms that most Spanish homes suffer from severe energy inefficiency, primarily due to poor window insulation. This widespread issue forces residents to waste significant amounts of heat, which escapes into the streets, thereby exacerbating global warming and increasing household energy bills. The article emphasizes that addressing these deficiencies through better insulation and window upgrades is a cost-effective solution. By sealing these leaks, homeowners can retain heat more effectively, leading to substantial financial savings compared to neighbors with poorly renovated buildings. This content is relevant to open data because it relies on public statistics from the INE and demonstrates how visual, geolocated thermal data can validate national trends. It highlights the power of open datasets in identifying systemic urban problems and supporting advocacy for environmental and economic efficiency in housing policy.
