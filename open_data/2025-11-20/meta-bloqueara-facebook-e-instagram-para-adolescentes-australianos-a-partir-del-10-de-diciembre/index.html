Meta bloqueará Facebook e Instagram para adolescentes australianos a partir del 10 de diciembre

Meta bloqueará Facebook e Instagram para adolescentes australianos a partir del 10 de diciembre

Meta is implementing a strict restriction in Australia by blocking access for users under the age of 16 to its platforms, setting a global precedent in digital regulation. This measure reflects the growing pressure on major technology companies to demonstrate the effectiveness of their age-verification systems and ensure regulatory compliance, which is crucial for the development of transparent technical standards within the industry. The mass deactivation process and the management of personal data involve significant challenges related to privacy and cybersecurity. By requesting additional information only when there are doubts about the declared age, Meta highlights the need to balance child protection with data minimization—a fundamental principle for the ethical design of systems that handle sensitive information. This case is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates the tension between corporate opacity and public accountability. The imposition of multimillion-dollar fines for non-compliance underscores the urgency of accessible audits and open data regarding moderation algorithms, enabling civil society and researchers to assess whether these technological tools truly protect vulnerable populations or merely create operational barriers without verifiable transparency.
