República Dominicana pasó del 40 al 95.3 % de cumplimiento en acceso a la información

República Dominicana pasó del 40 al 95.3 % de cumplimiento en acceso a la información

The Dominican Republic has significantly strengthened its public integrity framework, demonstrating a substantial increase in compliance with freedom of information laws. This shift aims to rebuild institutional trust and transform legal mandates into effective practice, moving beyond mere compliance to foster genuine transparency. Central to this progress is the adoption of the country’s first national open data policy. This initiative is crucial for open data advocates, as it establishes a formal commitment to data accessibility and reuse, serving as a foundational step for modernizing governance and enhancing civic engagement through information. These institutional reforms, supported by OECD evaluations, link transparency directly to economic improvements such as foreign investment and employment. By prioritizing integrity and data openness, the nation illustrates how robust governance structures can drive broader societal benefits and create a more accountable, citizen-oriented state.
