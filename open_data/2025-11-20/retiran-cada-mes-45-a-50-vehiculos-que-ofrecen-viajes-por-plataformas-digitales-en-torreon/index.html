Retiran cada mes 45 a 50 vehículos que ofrecen viajes por plataformas digitales en Torreón

Retiran cada mes 45 a 50 vehículos que ofrecen viajes por plataformas digitales en Torreón

Torreón’s transport authority is intensifying checks on unregistered ride-hailing vehicles to ensure public safety and fair competition with licensed taxis. By removing drivers who evade registration, officials aim to enforce strict regulatory compliance, signaling a firm stance against the informal economy and demanding that operators meet specific municipal and state requirements to operate legally. This enforcement highlights the critical need for transparent data in regulating modern mobility services. Open data frameworks are essential for verifying operator legitimacy, tracking compliance rates, and ensuring that digital platforms adhere to established legal standards. Without accessible and reliable public datasets, authorities cannot effectively monitor the growing gig economy or protect users from unvetted services. Ultimately, this case underscores how open data can bridge the gap between traditional regulation and digital transport models. By making registration and inspection data public, cities can foster accountability, allow citizens to verify driver legitimacy, and create a more equitable marketplace. Transparency not only aids enforcement but also builds trust between users, providers, and government agencies in the evolving transportation landscape.
