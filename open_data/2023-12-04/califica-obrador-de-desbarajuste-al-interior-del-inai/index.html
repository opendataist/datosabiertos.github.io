Califica Obrador de “desbarajuste” al interior del INAI

President López Obrador has ordered an investigation into alleged corruption within the INAI, citing irregular personal expenditures by former officials. He argues that these autonomous bodies create a parallel government structure that weakens legitimate institutions and serves oligarchic interests rather than the public good. The president characterizes these organizations as overly costly and inefficient, directing significant budget resources toward current expenses while yielding little tangible benefit. This critique highlights a systemic concern regarding the accountability and operational necessity of entities meant to ensure transparency, suggesting they may hinder rather than help democratic governance. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it questions the integrity of the institution tasked with enforcing access to information and data protection. If the watchdog agency itself lacks transparency and faces credible corruption allegations, public trust in the open data ecosystem erodes, potentially undermining the foundational principle that government data should be accessible, accountable, and managed ethically for the citizenry.
