De los datos abiertos a la conectividad científica: Red.es recorre el trabajo de sus diferentes unidades | Red.es

De los datos abiertos a la conectividad científica: Red.es recorre el trabajo de sus diferentes unidades | Red.es

The article highlights Red.es’s pivotal role in Spain’s digital transformation by showcasing its core units through a documentary series. By revealing the daily operations of key institutions, the initiative aims to bridge the gap between public administration and citizens, demonstrating how centralized technological infrastructure directly supports national connectivity and modernization efforts. A central focus is the promotion of open data through datos.gob.es, which facilitates the reuse of public information for innovation and research. This is complemented by the management of the .es domain, which has expanded digital identity since its liberalization, and the ONTSI, which analyzes social and technological trends to guide public policy. Together, these services ensure that digital rights and technological evolution are monitored and regulated effectively for the benefit of society. Furthermore, the narrative emphasizes RedIRIS, a high-capacity network connecting universities and research centers globally. This infrastructure enables critical academic collaboration and access to advanced computational resources. For open data, this relevance lies in the synergy between accessible datasets and the high-speed connectivity required to process them. Red.es thus creates an ecosystem where open information and robust digital infrastructure coexist to drive research, business development, and informed decision-making.
