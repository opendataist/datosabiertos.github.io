Sesiona Pleno del INAI después de 151 días de parálisis por falta de comisionados | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI has resumed operations after a prolonged hiatus caused by the lack of appointed commissioners. By continuing its work with fewer members, the institution demonstrates resilience against political pressure while managing a significant backlog of pending cases. This return to functionality underscores the critical role of independent bodies in safeguarding transparency, even when structural constitutional requirements are not fully met. Despite legal compromises allowing partial operation, officials express deep concern over the Senate’s failure to fill vacancies. The persistent understaffing threatens the body’s legitimacy and effectiveness, highlighting a tension between immediate operational survival and full constitutional compliance. This situation reveals the fragility of oversight mechanisms when legislative inaction undermines institutional integrity. This narrative is vital to open data because it illustrates the precarious state of the primary agency responsible for enforcing access to information rights. The INAI’s ability to process requests and hold powerful entities accountable directly impacts the availability and reliability of public data. Without a fully functioning commission, the ecosystem of open data remains vulnerable to censorship and administrative paralysis.
