Desempleo en México se ubicó en 3,1% en julio de 2023

Mexico’s recent labor market data reveals a nuanced economic picture, with the unemployment rate reaching historic lows. This significant decline in joblessness suggests a robust recovery in demand for workers, indicating that the economy is successfully integrating more individuals into the formal workforce compared to previous years. However, this improvement is counterbalanced by a concerning rise in underemployment and informality. A substantial portion of the labor force remains in precarious conditions, working fewer hours than desired or outside the protected formal sector. This highlights that while the quantity of jobs has increased, the quality and stability of employment opportunities remain critical challenges that need to be addressed to ensure genuine economic progress. This report is relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical tools to monitor complex socioeconomic indicators. By making such granular data on unemployment, informality, and sectoral distribution publicly available, governments and researchers can better analyze labor trends, advocate for policy improvements, and foster accountability in economic management.
