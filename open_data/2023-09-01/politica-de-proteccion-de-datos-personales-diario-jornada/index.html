Política de Protección de Datos Personales | Diario Jornada

The article presents a formal Privacy Policy from the Peruvian newspaper *Diario JORNADA*, outlining its commitment to safeguarding user information in compliance with national data protection laws. By explicitly defining the scope of data collection, purposes of processing, and security measures, the organization demonstrates a structured approach to transparency. This establishes a baseline of trust between the media outlet and its readership, ensuring that personal information is handled with integrity and within legal boundaries. A central theme of the document is the empowerment of individuals through defined rights, such as access, rectification, and cancellation of personal data. The policy emphasizes that consent is explicit and can be revoked at any time, placing the user in control of their digital footprint. This mechanism ensures that data processing remains aligned with user expectations, particularly regarding subscriptions, customer service, and internal statistical analysis, thereby fostering a respectful and accountable relationship with the audience. This text is relevant to the open data community because it illustrates the critical boundary between transparent public data handling and the protection of private personal information. While open data advocates for the release of non-sensitive public sector information, this policy highlights the mandatory safeguards required for personal data, reinforcing the ethical framework that must accompany any data-driven ecosystem. It serves as a reminder that even in an era of transparency, respecting individual privacy and legal compliance remains a foundational pillar for sustainable data practices.
