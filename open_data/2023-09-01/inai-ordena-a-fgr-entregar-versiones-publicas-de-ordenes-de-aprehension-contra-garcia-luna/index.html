INAI ordena a FGR entregar versiones públicas de órdenes de aprehensión contra García Luna

INAI ordena a FGR entregar versiones públicas de órdenes de aprehensión contra García Luna

The INAI has ruled that the Attorney General’s Office must provide public versions of the arrest warrants for Genaro García Luna, correcting a previous insufficient response. This decision underscores the legal obligation of government bodies to ensure transparency and access to justice-related information, reinforcing the principle that such data belongs to the public domain. The article highlights the critical role of open data in maintaining accountability within state institutions. When authorities fail to provide accurate or relevant information, regulatory bodies must intervene to correct these omissions. This mechanism ensures that citizens can access essential records, fostering trust and enabling effective oversight of powerful entities. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the enforcement of transparency laws in high-profile judicial contexts. It shows how legal frameworks protect the public’s right to information, even when dealing with sensitive matters involving former officials. Ultimately, it reinforces the necessity of accessible, complete, and reliable data for a functioning democratic society.
