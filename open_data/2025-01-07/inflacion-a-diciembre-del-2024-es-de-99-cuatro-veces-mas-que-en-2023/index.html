Inflación a diciembre del 2024 es de 9,9%, cuatro veces más que en 2023

Bolivia is experiencing a severe surge in inflation, marking a significant economic deterioration compared to previous years. This trend highlights critical gaps in the usability of open data, as precise consumer price indices reveal distinct regional disparities. Economic experts attribute this crisis to inadequate policy frameworks and a refusal to reform administrative models, rather than external factors. Understanding these structural causes requires transparent access to granular statistical data to inform effective governance. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of accessible, detailed economic indicators. By analyzing such reports, stakeholders can better diagnose systemic failures and advocate for evidence-based solutions to stabilize prices.
