OpenMap Development Tanzania Wins the 2025 Zayed Sustainability Prize in the Climate Action Category - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

OpenMap Development Tanzania has won the 2025 Zayed Sustainability Prize for demonstrating how community-led open data initiatives can significantly enhance climate resilience. By integrating participatory mapping with advanced technologies like drones and open-source platforms, the organization empowers local populations to generate accessible flood risk maps. This approach shifts disaster management from a top-down model to one driven by those most affected, fostering local ownership of climate adaptation strategies and improving disaster preparedness in vulnerable regions. The practical application of this open data has directly influenced local governance and infrastructure planning, enabling targeted interventions such as drain cleaning and early warning systems. By training local leaders and sharing data with government bodies and international partners, the organization ensures that technical capacity is built within the community. This validation of grassroots data collection highlights the efficacy of open-source methodologies in solving complex environmental challenges, proving that locally generated information is vital for effective and sustainable risk mitigation. This achievement is highly relevant to the open data movement as it showcases the transformative power of transparent, community-generated information in addressing global crises. It validates the principle that empowering citizens with the tools to map and understand their own environments leads to more inclusive and resilient societies. The prize-winning model serves as a scalable blueprint for using open technology to bridge the gap between local realities and policy decisions, encouraging wider adoption of participatory data practices in climate action worldwide.
