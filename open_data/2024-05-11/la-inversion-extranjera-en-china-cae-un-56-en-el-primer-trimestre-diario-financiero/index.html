La inversión extranjera en China cae un 56% en el primer trimestre | Diario Financiero

Foreign direct investment in China has dropped significantly, underscoring the difficulties in attracting external capital amid economic challenges. This decline coincides with a record-low current account surplus, reflecting weaker trade balances. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent financial metrics enable researchers to analyze global capital flows and assess the economic stability of major emerging markets.
