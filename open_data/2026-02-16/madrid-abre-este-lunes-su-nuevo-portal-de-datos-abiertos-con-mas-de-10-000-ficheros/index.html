Madrid abre este lunes su nuevo portal de datos abiertos con más de 10.000 ficheros

Madrid’s launch of its enhanced open data portal represents a significant step toward institutional transparency and digital innovation. By expanding its catalog to include hundreds of datasets and more than ten thousand files, the city government reinforces its commitment to an open government model. This strategic update goes beyond mere publication, aiming to establish a robust, scalable infrastructure that facilitates the growth and sustainable management of public information for both citizens and developers. The technical overhaul leverages the open-source CKAN platform, introducing advanced features such as automated APIs and intuitive categorization to enhance data reusability. This shift supports a “single data” strategy, centralizing municipal information while enabling federation with other national and local entities. The focus is on making data immediately accessible, allowing users to efficiently explore, analyze, and visualize information through specialized tools that meet international accessibility standards. This initiative is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it exemplifies best practices in interoperability and user-centered design. By prioritizing open technology and inclusive access, Madrid not only complies with European directives but also stimulates civic participation and independent analysis. The project demonstrates how open standards can transform static records into dynamic resources, encouraging widespread reuse and fostering a culture of accountability and informed decision-making within the broader community of data stakeholders.
