Notice Issued To Govt Over Aarogya Setu App: No One Knows, Who Created This App! - Trak.in - Indian Business of Tech, Mobile & Startups

Notice Issued To Govt Over Aarogya Setu App: No One Knows, Who Created This App! - Trak.in - Indian Business of Tech, Mobile & Startups

The Central Information Commission has issued show-cause notices to key government technology agencies for failing to adequately respond to a Right to Information request regarding the Aarogya Setu app. This action highlights a significant transparency gap, as agencies either refused to provide information or claimed no records existed despite handling massive amounts of personal data. The Commission criticized these evasive replies as preposterous, noting the contradiction between public claims of development and the denial of related documentation. By summoning officials, the authority seeks to clarify why essential documents regarding the app’s creation were not maintained or disclosed, underscoring the critical need for accountability in government digital projects. This case is vital to open data advocates because it reinforces the principle that public institutions must be transparent about how digital tools are built and audited. Ensuring access to development records and audit trails is essential for verifying that personal data is handled responsibly, thereby strengthening public trust in e-governance initiatives.
