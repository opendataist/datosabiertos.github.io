Rahul Gandhi demands EC publish voter rolls, release all post-5 pm CCTV footage from Maharashtra polling booths

Rahul Gandhi demands EC publish voter rolls, release all post-5 pm CCTV footage from Maharashtra polling booths

Rahul Gandhi demands the Election Commission publish machine-readable voter rolls to prove electoral integrity, arguing that transparent data is essential for public trust. This push highlights the critical role of open data in verifying democratic processes and holding institutions accountable for alleged irregularities. The Election Commission rejects these claims as unsubstantiated, maintaining that its previous responses adequately addressed concerns. This standoff underscores the tension between political accusations and official assurances when transparency is questioned, revealing how data opacity can fuel skepticism regardless of institutional defense. This conflict is vital to open data discussions as it illustrates how accessible, standardized digital records serve as the bedrock of electoral credibility. Without such transparency, claims of fairness remain unverified, demonstrating that open data is not just a technical preference but a fundamental requirement for legitimate governance.
