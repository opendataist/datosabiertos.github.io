Her refusal to comply highlights the critical role of legal pressure in enforcing transparency. This situation demonstrates how courts can deter the withholding of public records. The threat of daily fines serves as a powerful mechanism to compel officials to surrender requested information. It is relevant to open data because it shows that voluntary transparency often fails.
