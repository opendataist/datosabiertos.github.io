La inflación interanual en el mercado formal en Cuba se sitúa en mayo en el 16,43 % - Mundo - ABC Color

La inflación interanual en el mercado formal en Cuba se sitúa en mayo en el 16,43 % - Mundo - ABC Color

Cuba’s May inflation rate marks the lowest point since the pandemic, though it remains high and significantly impacts families and pensioners who spend over 90% of their income on basic goods. While official statistics show a decrease compared to 2024, the broader economic context reveals prices have tripled since 2020, with only essential services like health showing minimal increases. Public perception diverges sharply from official data due to the extensive unregulated black market, which inflates real costs beyond government metrics. This discrepancy highlights a severe crisis characterized by scarcity, energy blackouts, and growing dollarization, stemming from prolonged structural failures and external sanctions that have triggered unprecedented social discontent and migration. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical gap between official statistics and lived reality. It emphasizes the necessity of transparent, inclusive data collection methods that account for informal economies. Without reliable open data reflecting these hidden market dynamics, policymakers and researchers cannot fully understand or address the systemic economic failures affecting Cuban citizens.
