Cooperación en ámbito judicial, un pilar en nexos Vietnam - China

Vietnamese and Chinese judicial leaders strengthened bilateral ties during high-level meetings, emphasizing deep cooperation in intellectual property, commerce, and the judicial application of artificial intelligence. This diplomatic engagement underscores a shared commitment to enhancing technical collaboration and legal frameworks between the two nations. The discussions highlighted the strategic importance of leveraging modern technologies within judicial procedures to improve efficiency and accuracy. By focusing on these advanced areas, both countries aim to modernize their legal systems while maintaining robust cross-border dispute resolution mechanisms. This article is relevant to open data as it signals the increasing integration of digital tools and artificial intelligence in government sectors. Enhanced judicial transparency and data sharing in these technical fields promote openness, ensuring that legal processes become more accessible, efficient, and aligned with global standards for public information access.
