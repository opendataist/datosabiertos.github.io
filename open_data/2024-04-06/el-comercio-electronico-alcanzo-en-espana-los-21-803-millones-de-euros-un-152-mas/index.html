El comercio electrónico alcanzó en España los 21.803 millones de euros, un 15,2% más

The article reveals a significant surge in Spain’s e-commerce sector, characterized by strong growth in both revenue and transaction volume. It highlights that travel and transport services dominate the market, while cross-border trade remains heavily skewed towards purchases made abroad, resulting in a notable trade deficit. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it showcases the potential of official statistics to drive economic transparency and policy formulation. By making such granular trade information accessible, developers and researchers can build tools that enhance market visibility, support small businesses in navigating international trade, and foster more informed public discourse on digital economy trends.
