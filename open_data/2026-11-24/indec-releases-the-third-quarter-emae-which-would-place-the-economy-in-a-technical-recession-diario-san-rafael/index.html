INDEC releases the third-quarter EMAE, which would place the economy in a technical recession - Diario San Rafael

INDEC releases the third-quarter EMAE, which would place the economy in a technical recession - Diario San Rafael

INDEC will release data confirming that the Argentine economy has entered a technical recession by the third quarter. This situation validates private-sector projections of a decline in GDP, where the modest monthly recovery fails to offset the accumulated contraction. The slowdown reflects the fact that growth in primary sectors is insufficient to reverse the deceleration in industry, commerce, and employment. The negative trend has forced a significant downward revision of annual growth expectations, placing them well below official targets. This downward adjustment in economic projections underscores the current difficulty in broadly reviving economic activity, undermining confidence in the national financial and productive landscape. This report is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how the analysis of both official and private economic indicators enables the evaluation of public policies. Transparency in the publication of these figures is crucial for citizens and the private sector to understand the real impact of government decisions on employment and investment, thereby facilitating accountability and evidence-based analysis.
