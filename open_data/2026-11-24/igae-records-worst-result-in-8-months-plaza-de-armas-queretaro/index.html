IGAE records worst result in 8 months - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

IGAE records worst result in 8 months - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

National economic activity experienced its worst performance in eight months during September, marking a significant decline driven by contractions across the three main sectors. This monthly downturn was particularly pronounced in the industrial sector, led by construction and manufacturing, reflecting a notable slowdown in the country’s production and construction activities. Paradoxically, while annual growth moderated, the primary sector showed a recovery compared to previous months. The combination of negative monthly results with a slower annual pace suggests that the economy is facing cyclical tensions, where the inertia of recent months influences perceptions of current economic stability. This information is relevant to the field of open data because the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) publishes these time series under open licenses, enabling researchers and citizens to analyze economic health without barriers. The availability of sector-disaggregated data facilitates transparency, public scrutiny, and the development of predictive models that help understand market dynamics in an independent and verifiable manner.
