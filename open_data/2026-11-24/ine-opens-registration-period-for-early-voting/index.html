INE Opens Registration Period for Early Voting

INE Opens Registration Period for Early Voting

The National Electoral Institute has opened registration for early voting for people with disabilities and their primary caregivers in the 2026–2027 electoral process. This measure expands participation by allowing the caregiver to vote alongside the elector, ensuring that the need to assist a dependent person does not impede their right to vote. The objective is to ensure the inclusion of a historically marginalized group in democratic processes. The modality combines in-person home visits with online voting. This innovation seeks to protect the voter’s privacy and autonomy by providing secure access credentials to cast their vote from personal devices. The system is designed to offer accessible alternatives, ensuring that those who cannot interact with technology have a second option through printed ballots after an in-person verification. This initiative is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the technological implementation of civil rights and the management of sensitive data for democratic purposes. By digitizing the process and establishing clear privacy protocols, the National Electoral Institute sets precedents on how personal data can be handled, protected, and used in modern electoral systems. Furthermore, it demonstrates institutional transparency by complying with judicial mandates, promoting open standards for citizen inclusion and accountability in the administration of electoral information.
