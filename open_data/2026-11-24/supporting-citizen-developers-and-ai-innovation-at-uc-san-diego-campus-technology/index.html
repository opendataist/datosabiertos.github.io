Supporting Citizen Developers and AI Innovation at UC San Diego -- Campus Technology

Supporting Citizen Developers and AI Innovation at UC San Diego -- Campus Technology

UC San Diego empowers citizen developers through its TritonAI program, enabling users to build custom AI tools using open-weight models and robust infrastructure. By adopting a model-agnostic strategy and centralizing data governance, the university reduces vendor lock-in while maintaining security and institutional standards. This approach allows non-technical staff to automate complex workflows, such as contract review and student services, demonstrating how open data practices facilitate innovative, decentralized problem-solving. The initiative highlights the critical role of accessible institutional data in driving efficiency and fostering an agile, user-driven technology ecosystem. This case is highly relevant to open_data as it illustrates how transparent, well-governed data access empowers diverse campus users to create value. It underscores the importance of balancing open innovation with structured governance, offering a scalable model for other institutions seeking to leverage open data for AI-driven transformation without compromising security.
