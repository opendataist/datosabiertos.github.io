Mexico's economy grew 3.3% year-on-year in the third quarter, surpassing INEGI's preliminary estimate thanks to a rebound in agriculture and commerce amid an industrial slowdown

Mexico's economy grew 3.3% year-on-year in the third quarter, surpassing INEGI's preliminary estimate thanks to a rebound in agriculture and commerce amid an industrial slowdown

Mexico’s economy consolidated an annual growth rate of 3.3% in the third quarter, slightly surpassing preliminary estimates thanks to upward revisions in key sectors. This final result confirms the expected dynamism and establishes the official baseline for assessing economic performance through the end of the year, providing certainty to analysts and policymakers regarding the recent economic trajectory. The main driver of this reconfiguration was the primary sector, which shifted from a projected contraction to actual growth, alongside the tertiary sector, which showed greater expansion than anticipated. While these areas positively contributed to the overall balance, industrial activity registered a slowdown compared to initial calculations, highlighting the importance of monitoring the diversification of growth drivers beyond manufacturing and construction. This case is relevant for open data because it illustrates the evolution of statistical quality and precision. The publication of original versus final figures, seasonally adjusted and comparable across annual periods, demonstrates how transparent and phased access to data enables civil society and experts to conduct more rigorous analyses, understanding not only the current state but also the methodology behind official economic measurements.
