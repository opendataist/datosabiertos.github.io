Hotels in Extremadura record their best occupancy figures since 2010 in October, with 136,888 travelers staying

Hotel tourism in Extremadura reached historic levels of occupancy and revenue in October, driven by a significant increase in foreign tourism that offset a slight decline in domestic visitors. This recovery signals a robust revitalization of the sector, underscoring the importance of diversifying source markets to ensure the region’s economic sustainability. These data are relevant to open data because they validate the usefulness of official public statistics, such as those from the National Statistics Institute (INE), for analyzing macroeconomic trends and making informed decisions in the tourism sector. Transparency in the disclosure of these metrics enables researchers, businesses, and citizens to access reliable information about regional dynamics, fostering accountability and the development of evidence-based policies.
