Air pollution puts health at risk in Europe - Diario Responsable

Air pollution puts health at risk in Europe - Diario Responsable

Prolonged exposure to pollutant particles continues to pose a serious threat to European public health, linked to hundreds of thousands of premature deaths annually. Although mortality has decreased significantly over the past two decades thanks to the implementation of effective public policies, substantial territorial inequalities persist, with eastern and southeastern parts of the region suffering a disproportionate health impact. Beyond mortality, the report highlights that pollution profoundly affects morbidity, degrading quality of life through chronic and acute diseases. The methodological update incorporates new World Health Organization (WHO) scientific standards, which, while raising the absolute figures of estimated risk, confirms the positive trend and reinforces the need to maintain and strengthen emission reduction measures to meet long-term environmental objectives. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical use of standardized indicators and open data sources, such as reports from the European Environment Agency, to assess complex health impacts. Transparency in these data enables territorial comparability, tracking of policy targets, and evidence-based decision-making, demonstrating how access to environmental information is fundamental to collective health and government accountability.
