Hotel overnight stays in C-LM rise 4% in October, reaching 453,585 operations

Hotel overnight stays in C-LM rise 4% in October, reaching 453,585 operations

The hotel sector in Castilla-La Mancha experienced a notable recovery in October, marking the end of a period of negative growth rates and showing sustained increases in both overnight stays and tourist arrivals. This positive trend, aligned with the overall improvement in the national market that reached historic levels, indicates a progressive revitalization of regional tourism activity following the previous contraction phase. The data reflect a significant increase in the average daily rate and job creation, suggesting improved profitability and economic health within the sector, although the region’s occupancy rates remain below those of leading communities such as the Canary Islands or the Balearic Islands. This scenario of rising prices and stronger demand implies that the region is better capturing the value of its tourism offerings, although it still faces the challenge of competing in volume with more established national destinations. This report is particularly relevant to the open data community, as it illustrates how public time series data, such as those published by the National Statistics Institute (INE), enable the monitoring of macroeconomic trends and the comparison of territorial performance. Access to these open data facilitates the analysis of sectoral evolution, the identification of regional disparities, and evidence-based decision-making for tourism planning and public resource management during a phase of economic recovery.
