El Producto Bruto Geográfico de Corrientes creció 106% en el periodo 2004-2022

The IPECD report reveals that Corrientes' economy reached its peak expansion in 2022, consolidating sustained growth in productive activity since 2004. This positive trend is reflected not only in the increase in total Gross Value Added (GVA) but also in per capita well-being, indicating that economic development has significantly outpaced population growth during the analyzed period. Structurally, the province maintains a productive matrix dominated by the services sector, with commerce standing out as the main value generator. This concentration in services, followed by agriculture and real estate activities, defines the local economic identity and its relative importance within the national context, although its share of Argentina's GDP remains modest. The relevance of this report for the field of open data lies in its potential to democratize economic information. By making key indicators such as Gross Provincial Product (GPP) and Gross Value Added (GVA) accessible, it facilitates transparency and enables citizens, researchers, and businesses to conduct independent analyses. This empowers informed decision-making by civil society, promoting a critical assessment of provincial and regional development without relying exclusively on official interpretations.
