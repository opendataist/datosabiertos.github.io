La facturación de la industria aumenta en Castilla-La Mancha un 1,4% en 2023

Spain’s industrial sector is experiencing a significant downturn in 2023, with national revenue declining sharply after years of strong growth. December witnessed an especially steep drop, underscoring a difficult year-end for businesses across the region. Regional disparities are evident, as some areas managed slight increases while others suffered severe losses. This uneven recovery highlights the complex economic landscape, where local conditions significantly influence industrial performance and resilience during periods of volatility. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, offering transparent insights into economic trends. It enables researchers and policymakers to access reliable statistics, fostering more accurate analysis of regional disparities and informing decisions aimed at supporting industrial stability and growth.
