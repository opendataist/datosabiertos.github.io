La alarmante siniestralidad laboral en Navarra - Tercera Información

Navarre has recorded alarming rates of work-related accidents in 2023, ranking first in Spain for both non-fatal accidents resulting in time off work and fatal incidents. This reality shows that the relaxation of preventive measures and the lack of institutional resources are creating an unsafe environment, reflected in figures that represent human tragedies and destroyed families, urgently demanding greater public investment in inspection and training. The rise in workplace accidents is directly linked to labor precariousness, where extreme temporary employment and subcontracting leave workers defenseless, afraid to report risks for fear of losing their jobs. This dynamic reflects a corporate culture that prioritizes cost reduction over safety, resulting in outdated equipment and inadequate training, making the lack of protection a direct consequence of the pursuit of economic benefits above the well-being of the workforce. The current model of External Prevention Services also contributes to the problem, as aggressive competition has driven market prices to levels that only allow for formalistic compliance with regulations, without offering real and thorough prevention. The overload of technicians and the lack of quality in these services prevent effective supervision. From an open data perspective, this article highlights the critical need for transparency in the management of these services and the availability of open data on working conditions, as only through access to verifiable information can the real effectiveness of safety measures be audited and accountability demanded from administrations and companies.
