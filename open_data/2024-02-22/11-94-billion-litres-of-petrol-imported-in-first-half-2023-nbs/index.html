11.94 billion litres of petrol imported in first half 2023 – NBS

11.94 billion litres of petrol imported in first half 2023 – NBS

Nigeria’s first-half 2023 energy statistics reveal shifting dynamics in fuel import and production. Notably, diesel imports surged significantly, while local production of both kerosene and diesel expanded. Conversely, petrol truck-outs declined despite higher import volumes, suggesting potential supply chain or demand adjustments. This data is crucial for open_data because it exemplifies the importance of transparent, granular government reporting in the energy sector. Accessible datasets allow analysts to verify official claims, track policy impacts, and identify market anomalies. Open data initiatives ensure these figures are not just archival but actionable. By making such detailed statistics freely available, stakeholders can foster accountability, support evidence-based policymaking, and drive innovation in resource management and energy security strategies.
