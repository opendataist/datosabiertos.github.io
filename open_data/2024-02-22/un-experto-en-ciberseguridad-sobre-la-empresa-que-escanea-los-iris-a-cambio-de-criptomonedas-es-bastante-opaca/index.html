Un experto en ciberseguridad, sobre la empresa que escanea los iris a cambio de criptomonedas: "Es bastante opaca"

Worldcoin’s incentive scheme for iris scanning raises serious privacy concerns, prompting investigations by Spanish data protection authorities. The lack of corporate transparency and potential security risks highlight critical vulnerabilities in handling biometric information. The situation underscores a dangerous public underestimation of the value of personal data, urging citizens to prioritize privacy over immediate financial rewards. Experts emphasize the need for greater caution and public education regarding the long-term implications of sharing sensitive biometric identifiers. This case is vital for open data advocacy, as it demonstrates the necessity of strict transparency and user control. It reinforces the principle that individuals must retain the right to understand, manage, and delete their data, challenging companies to justify opaque data practices in an era demanding ethical innovation.
