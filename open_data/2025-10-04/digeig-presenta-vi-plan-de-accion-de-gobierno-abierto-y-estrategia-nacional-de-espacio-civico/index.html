Digeig presenta VI Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto y Estrategia Nacional de Espacio Cívico

Digeig presenta VI Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto y Estrategia Nacional de Espacio Cívico

The presentation of the Sixth Open Government Action Plan and the National Civic Space Strategy marks a significant shift toward inclusive governance. By integrating artificial intelligence and digital tools such as satellite monitoring and judicial data platforms, the state aims to deepen transparency and facilitate meaningful collaboration between citizens and government institutions. A key implication is the modernization of civic engagement mechanisms. The enhanced Open Data Portal now features interactive forms and an AI chatbot, removing barriers for users seeking information. These upgrades transform static repositories into dynamic spaces where citizens can actively request missing data and provide feedback, fostering a more responsive and accessible administrative environment. This development is crucial for open data because it moves beyond the mere publication of information toward active user participation. By guaranteeing freedom of expression, protecting the right to access information, and combating disinformation, these initiatives ensure that data serves as a foundation for democratic participation. This approach empowers civil society, turning raw data into a tool for accountability and social inclusion.
