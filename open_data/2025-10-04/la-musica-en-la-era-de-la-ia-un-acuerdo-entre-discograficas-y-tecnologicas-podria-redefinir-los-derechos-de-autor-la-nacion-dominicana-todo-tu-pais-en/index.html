La música en la era de la IA: un acuerdo entre discográficas y tecnológicas podría redefinir los derechos de autor | La Nación Dominicana, Todo tu país, en un solo click...!

Major record labels are negotiating advanced licensing agreements with artificial intelligence companies to regulate the use of copyrighted music in model training and content generation. The central objective is to establish a global standard that defines how creators are compensated in this new era, seeking a balance between technological innovation and the protection of intellectual property rights. The proposal involves implementing automatic micropayment mechanisms for each use of songs, similar to streaming, complemented by attribution systems to track and validate the origin of works. This approach aims to ensure fair and transparent compensation, guaranteeing that authors and rights holders receive just benefits while preventing unauthorized use of their catalogs, thus laying the legal groundwork for future contracts in other creative sectors. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical tension between the exploitation of cultural data for AI training and the need for ethical governance frameworks. By prioritizing transparency in attribution and traceability of data usage, it sets a precedent for how the creative industry can manage interoperability between proprietary models and protected data, influencing how open or semi-structured data licenses will be structured in the future digital landscape.
