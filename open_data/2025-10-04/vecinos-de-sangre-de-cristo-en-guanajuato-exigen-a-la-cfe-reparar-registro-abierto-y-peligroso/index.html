Vecinos de Sangre de Cristo en Guanajuato exigen a la CFE reparar registro abierto y peligroso

Residents of Sangre de Cristo face ongoing safety hazards due to a missing electrical access cover, a problem exacerbated by the utility company’s inaction. The community has resorted to makeshift solutions to prevent pedestrians from falling into the exposed, deep shaft, highlighting significant gaps in public infrastructure maintenance and emergency response. This situation underscores the critical need for transparent utility data and accountable governance. When residents must independently mitigate risks, it reveals systemic failures that hinder effective oversight and public safety. Open data initiatives are vital here, as they would enable real-time monitoring of infrastructure integrity and utility performance. By mandating the disclosure of maintenance records and incident reports, stakeholders can ensure accountability, protect citizens from foreseeable dangers, and foster trust in public services through verifiable, accessible information.
