Preparan décimo ejercicio de Gobierno Abierto

The municipal government of Querétaro is launching an Open Government initiative to involve citizens in designing a new public park. By engaging residents directly, this exercise aims to empower the community and ensure that urban improvements reflect local needs and desires, fostering a deeper sense of ownership and civic responsibility. This participatory model represents a significant step toward transparency and accountability in public administration. It demonstrates how local authorities can collaborate with citizens and educational institutions to co-create solutions, moving beyond traditional top-down decision-making to include diverse perspectives in the planning and execution of public works. This case is highly relevant to open data, as it highlights the potential for participatory governance mechanisms to complement digital transparency efforts. When communities are actively involved in decision-making, it encourages the demand for accessible information and creates a feedback loop in which public resources are managed more efficiently, reinforcing the principles of inclusive and responsive government through direct citizen engagement.
