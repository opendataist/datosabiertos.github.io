Figures show how Devon and Cornwall rank in safest places in the UK

Devon and Cornwall have been identified as the safest regions in England and Wales, topping safety rankings ahead of other counties. This achievement highlights the effectiveness of local policing strategies and operational adjustments, demonstrating how dedicated efforts to meet national standards can significantly enhance community security and reduce overall crime rates compared to the national average. The success underscores the importance of accessible, responsive police services in maintaining public trust. By improving call response times and reopening inquiry offices, the force ensures that residents have easy access to support. These structural improvements are crucial for fostering confidence in reporting crimes, proving that efficient service delivery directly contributes to a safer environment and better community relations. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparent, publicly available statistics can validate local governance and justify resource allocation. Open crime data allows citizens to verify claims of safety, holds authorities accountable for performance, and drives continuous improvement. By leveraging these datasets, communities can make informed decisions, while officials use them to refine strategies and demonstrate tangible results to the public.
