Los riesgos de naturalizar el consumo de alcohol

The survey reveals significant public health concerns regarding psychoactive substance use, highlighting that most users prioritize self-care over institutional health support. This emphasis on individual autonomy in managing consumption challenges traditional medical models, suggesting a gap in accessible healthcare services for addiction and wellness. For open data, these findings underscore the need for granular, transparent datasets on informal health behaviors to better inform policy and bridge the divide between citizen self-management and systemic care.
