Modi pushes for open source AI framework at global summit

Prime Minister Narendra Modi advocates for a global artificial intelligence framework rooted in open-source principles to ensure trust, transparency, and freedom from bias. He argues that while AI holds immense potential to transform sectors like healthcare and education, its current limitations, such as training data dominance, highlight the urgent need for inclusive governance. This approach seeks to democratize technology by making it accessible and beneficial for all, particularly addressing the capabilities gaps in the Global South regarding resources, talent, and infrastructure. The article underscores the necessity of developing quality, unbiased datasets and fostering local ecosystems to make AI applications effective and people-centric. By promoting open-source AI, Modi emphasizes that governance must go beyond risk management to actively drive innovation for global good. This involves tackling critical challenges like cybersecurity, disinformation, and deepfakes, ensuring that technological advancements align with shared human values rather than exacerbating existing societal inequalities or biases inherent in current models. This perspective is highly relevant to open data because it directly links open-source AI development to the availability of diverse, high-quality, and unbiased data. The call for inclusive governance and global partnerships highlights that open data is not merely a technical asset but a foundational requirement for equitable AI evolution. By urging the international community to prioritize transparency and shared access, the speech reinforces the critical role of open data in preventing algorithmic bias and ensuring that AI serves as a tool for sustainable, inclusive development worldwide.
