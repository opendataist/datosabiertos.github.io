El papel de la informática y la IA en un mundo más sostenible

El papel de la informática y la IA en un mundo más sostenible

The article demonstrates that accelerated computing and artificial intelligence are essential tools for balancing technological growth with environmental sustainability. Through digital twins, process optimization, and hardware migration, companies significantly reduce energy consumption and operational costs. This shows that efficiency is not incompatible with innovation; rather, it can enhance it through advanced technical solutions. Beyond industrial improvements, these technologies enable critical advances in vital sectors such as healthcare and genomic research, accelerating data analysis without proportional increases in energy expenditure. Moreover, collaboration between companies and international organizations facilitates the development of models for climate prediction and disaster management. This synergy underscores the importance of creating open ecosystems that share knowledge and resources to address complex global challenges. This content is relevant to the field of open data because it illustrates how access to large volumes of information and transparent collaboration are essential for modeling sustainable solutions. Transparency in algorithms and the availability of open platforms enable scientists and organizations to verify and improve models, democratizing access to climate and health solutions. In this way, data becomes a common good that drives environmental responsibility and collective efficiency.
