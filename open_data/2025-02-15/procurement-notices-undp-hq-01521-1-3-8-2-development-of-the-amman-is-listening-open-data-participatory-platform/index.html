Procurement Notices - UNDP-HQ-01521 - 1.3.8-2 Development of the "Amman is Listening" Open Data Participatory Platform

The "Amman is Listening" project, supported by the UNDP, represents a strategic shift toward open data-driven urban governance. By transforming resident feedback into actionable insights, the initiative aims to optimize municipal services and support the city’s smart transition. This approach prioritizes transparency and civic engagement, allowing citizens to directly influence local decision-making through a participatory platform. A Minimum Viable Product launched in mid-2024 is currently undergoing testing to refine user-centric features. The municipality is now seeking partners to enhance the platform’s functionality, focusing on bilingual accessibility, real-time communication, and intuitive data visualization. These developments are critical for ensuring the tool effectively serves diverse demographic groups and improves the overall user experience. This tender is highly relevant to open data communities as it demonstrates practical application in public sector innovation. It highlights how open data can bridge the gap between government and citizens, fostering accountability and efficiency. The project also emphasizes scalability, suggesting that successful models in Amman could be transferred to other cities, thereby advancing global standards for civic technology and inclusive digital infrastructure.
