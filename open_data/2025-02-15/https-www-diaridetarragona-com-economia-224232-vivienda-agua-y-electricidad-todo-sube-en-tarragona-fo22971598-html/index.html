Open Data
ES EN

Rising inflation in the region, driven largely by housing and energy costs, underscores how volatile household expenses remain. This trend highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible price data to monitor economic stability and living costs effectively. Accurate open data allows citizens and researchers to track these fluctuations, revealing disparities between essential services and discretionary spending. By making such statistical information freely available, stakeholders can better understand the structural pressures affecting everyday life. Ultimately, this transparency empowers communities to advocate for policies that address cost-of-living crises. Open data serves as a vital tool for accountability, ensuring that economic realities are visible and actionable for everyone.
