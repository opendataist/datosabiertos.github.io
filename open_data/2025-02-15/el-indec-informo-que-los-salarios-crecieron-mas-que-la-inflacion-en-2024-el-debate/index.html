El INDEC informó que los salarios crecieron más que la inflación en 2024 - EL DEBATE

Recent data indicates that Argentine workers regained purchasing power as salary growth outpaced inflation. This trend was largely driven by the unregistered private sector, marking a significant improvement in economic conditions. Job seekers’ required salaries also rose substantially over the year, exceeding inflation rates. Although monthly adjustments lagged behind price hikes in December, the overall annual trajectory demonstrates strong nominal gains for labor. This report is relevant to open data as it highlights the importance of transparent, official statistics for assessing economic health. Accessible data allows for verification of wage trends and inflation metrics, fostering informed public debate and policy evaluation.
