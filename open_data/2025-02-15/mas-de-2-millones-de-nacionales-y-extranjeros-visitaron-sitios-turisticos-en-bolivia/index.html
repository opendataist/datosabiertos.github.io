Más de 2 millones de nacionales y extranjeros visitaron sitios turísticos en Bolivia

Bolivia’s 2024 tourism data reveal a recovery trend, with visitor numbers surpassing 2022 levels despite a slight decline from the previous year. The government’s new certification initiative aims to standardize quality and promote sustainable management across these diverse destinations. This emphasis on certification underscores the strategic importance of tourism in Bolivia’s economy, leveraging its unique natural and cultural heritage. By prioritizing high standards, the state seeks to ensure the long-term preservation of these valuable resources for future generations. This article highlights the critical role of open data in monitoring sectoral performance and informing public policy. Transparent statistics enable stakeholders to track progress toward sustainability goals, fostering accountability and supporting evidence-based decisions for responsible tourism development.
