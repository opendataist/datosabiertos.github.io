Censo 2022: cada vez menos mendocinos tienen acceso a la casa propia

The 2022 Census data reveals a significant decline in homeownership across Argentina and Mendoza, highlighting shifting trends in housing security that policymakers must address through open and transparent housing registries. Improved access to basic utilities such as water and sanitation reflects progress in living standards, yet stark disparities persist between departments. These granular datasets are essential for identifying marginalized areas and ensuring the effective allocation of public resources. Furthermore, although internet and mobile connectivity have expanded, computer ownership lags behind, underscoring the existence of a digital divide. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it enables civil society to monitor equity in technological access and advocate for inclusive digital infrastructure policies that leave no one behind.
