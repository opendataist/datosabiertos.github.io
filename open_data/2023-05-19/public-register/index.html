Public register

Transparency and openness are fundamental EU principles that strengthen the EIB’s credibility and operational efficiency. By establishing a public register, the bank ensures accountability to citizens while complying with broader transparency policies. This approach highlights how institutional openness builds trust and improves stakeholder relations. It demonstrates that accessible information is not just a legal requirement but a tool for enhancing sustainability and governance within public finance. For open data, this case underscores the importance of proactive disclosure and structured registers. It illustrates how financial institutions can leverage transparency to foster public engagement, ensuring that environmental and operational data remains accessible for scrutiny and reuse.
