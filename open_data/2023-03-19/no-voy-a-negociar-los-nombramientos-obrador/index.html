No voy a negociar los nombramientos: Obrador

No voy a negociar los nombramientos: Obrador

López Obrador has reiterated his firm rejection of discreet negotiations or partisan allocation of INAI commissioners. The president defends his constitutional right to veto, arguing that certain candidates have demonstrated a lack of transparency by concealing their names during tax forgiveness processes, which contradicts the principles of access to information. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the tension between institutional autonomy and political oversight of public transparency. By denying the legitimacy of existing appointments, the independence of the body responsible for ensuring access to information is called into question, a cornerstone for an open society. This underscores the critical importance of maintaining impartial and technically robust regulatory bodies. When the appointment of officials is influenced by political agreements or partisan interests, the state’s capacity to provide accurate and accessible public data is weakened, directly affecting accountability and public trust.
