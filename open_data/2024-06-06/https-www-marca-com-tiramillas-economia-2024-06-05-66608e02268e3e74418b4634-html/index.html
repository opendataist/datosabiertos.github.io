Electricity costs have risen for the fifth consecutive time, with peak prices dominating most of the day. This persistent increase places significant financial pressure on consumers and businesses, highlighting the volatility inherent in the current energy market structure. The data reveals extreme price fluctuations between hours, with late evening being the most expensive and mid-afternoon offering the lowest rates. These stark disparities demonstrate how marginal pricing models create substantial cost variations depending on consumption timing. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on publicly available, granular datasets from the Iberian Energy Market Operator. Access to such detailed, time-stamped price information allows researchers and citizens to analyze trends, understand market dynamics, and hold energy providers accountable through transparent, verifiable public records.
