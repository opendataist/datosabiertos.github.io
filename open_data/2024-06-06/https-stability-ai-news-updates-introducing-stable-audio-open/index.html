Stable Audio Open enables the generation of high-quality audio from text prompts, empowering creators to produce sound effects and music samples. By allowing fine-tuning on custom data, it democratizes access to specialized generative audio capabilities for diverse creative needs. This release significantly advances open_data by providing transparent, adaptable tools for the community to build upon existing generative models.
