INS reveals the Top 5 products that boosted inflation in Cameroon in 2022 - Business in Cameroon

Cameroon’s 2022 inflation, fueled by soaring food costs, highlights critical supply chain vulnerabilities. Reliance on imported essentials like wheat and fish exposes the economy to global conflicts and price shocks. This case underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible open data for tracking inflation drivers. Such data enables policymakers and citizens to monitor economic stability and assess the impact of external shocks on local markets effectively.
