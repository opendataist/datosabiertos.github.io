PERU Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática INEI

This system centralizes regional statistics to empower local governments with data-driven insights for policy-making. It democratizes access to vital demographic and economic information, ensuring equitable decision support. By aggregating national survey data at the local level, it strengthens transparency and evidence-based governance across all administrative levels.
