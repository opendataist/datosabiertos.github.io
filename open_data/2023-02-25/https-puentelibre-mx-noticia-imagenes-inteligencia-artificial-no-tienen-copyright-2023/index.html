The U.S. Copyright Office denied copyright protection for AI-generated images, establishing that they lack human authorship. This ruling, stemming from a comic created with Midjourney, renders the work freely available despite the creator’s involvement, setting a critical legal precedent. The decision highlights that unpredictable AI outputs fall outside the scope of traditional copyright, distinguishing these tools from standard artistic instruments. As AI content production accelerates, this boundary significantly impacts how intellectual property is defined and protected in the digital age. This case is relevant to open data, as it challenges existing data governance frameworks regarding ownership and attribution. It urges developers and policymakers to clarify rights for algorithmic outputs, ensuring that open data principles adapt to emerging technologies without stifling innovation or violating creators’ rights.
