Datos Abiertos IMSS

This dataset offers a detailed view of Mexico’s formal labor market, highlighting the gap between registered job positions and insured individuals who are unemployed. By disaggregating employment data by demographic groups and economic sectors, it reveals underlying structural inequalities and sectoral trends within the workforce. As an open data resource, it facilitates transparent analysis of labor security, helping to identify systemic gaps and policy needs through accessible, detailed statistical evidence.

Source: datos.imss.gob.mx
