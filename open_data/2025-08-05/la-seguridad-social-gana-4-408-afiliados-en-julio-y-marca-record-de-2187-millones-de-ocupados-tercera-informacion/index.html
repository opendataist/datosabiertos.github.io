La Seguridad Social gana 4.408 afiliados en julio y marca récord de 21,87 millones de ocupados - Tercera Información

La Seguridad Social gana 4.408 afiliados en julio y marca récord de 21,87 millones de ocupados - Tercera Información

The Spanish Social Security system has reached a historic peak in membership, demonstrating robust growth in employment driven largely by labor market reforms. A significant shift toward job permanence is evident, with a substantial rise in indefinite contracts and a notable reduction in temporality, particularly among younger workers. This trend highlights a qualitative improvement in the labor market, where increased job stability is accompanied by higher contribution bases, signaling that recent policy interventions are successfully transforming the nature of employment rather than just increasing its volume. The expansion is not uniform across all demographics or sectors. While overall affiliation is at record highs, the monthly dynamics reveal a divergence between male and female participation, alongside specific regional fluctuations. Growth is concentrated in productive sectors such as healthcare and commerce, whereas traditional fields like education and agriculture see declines linked to seasonal or structural factors. This data underscores that the labor recovery is selective, benefiting specific industries and demographic groups more than others, which presents both an opportunity for targeted policy support and a challenge for achieving balanced regional employment equity. This article is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the critical role of high-quality, transparent government statistics in validating public policy effectiveness. By making detailed, disaggregated datasets regarding employment, contract types, and sectoral performance publicly available, authorities enable independent verification of economic trends. This transparency allows researchers and citizens to assess the real impact of structural reforms, fostering accountability and facilitating data-driven discussions on social welfare and economic sustainability.
