IA: Europa exige transparencia y respeto a los derechos de autor - RedUSERS

IA: Europa exige transparencia y respeto a los derechos de autor - RedUSERS

The new European Artificial Intelligence Act introduces strict transparency requirements, obliging companies to disclose details about the training and use of training data. This regulation seeks to balance innovation with the protection of rights, imposing significant fines for non-compliance and fostering accountability in the technology sector. The relevance for open data lies in the obligation to publish summaries of the materials used, which promotes accessibility and clarity regarding the provenance of information. By requiring creators to be able to deny access to their works, a legal framework is established that defines how data should be managed and shared in generative environments, directly affecting the ethics and structure of open datasets. Although the industry criticizes these measures as obstacles to rapid development and as protectors of trade secrets, the regulation attempts to find a fair balance between speed and security. This approach implies that transparency in AI processes is essential to ensure that open data is used responsibly, sustainably, and in accordance with established European legal standards.
