Según una encuesta, la imagen positiva de Javier Milei bajó un 4% en el último mes | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

The article reveals that President Milei’s positive image has declined to 44%, while negativity rises to 48%, indicating a polarized public perception. This shift highlights the tension between economic hardship and the government’s adjustment policies, suggesting a fragile tolerance threshold among citizens. These findings are relevant to open data because they underscore the critical importance of transparent, accessible public opinion metrics for understanding societal dynamics. Reliable data allows stakeholders to analyze how policy impacts trust and stability. Ultimately, the fluctuation in approval ratings demonstrates that public sentiment is volatile yet measurable. Tracking these trends via open data enables better governance by revealing the limits of acceptable economic sacrifice. This transparency is essential for navigating political challenges and maintaining democratic accountability through clear, data-driven insights.
