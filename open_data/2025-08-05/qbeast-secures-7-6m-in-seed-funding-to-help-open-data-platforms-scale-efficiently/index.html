Qbeast secures $7.6M in seed funding to help open data platforms scale efficiently

Qbeast secures $7.6M in seed funding to help open data platforms scale efficiently

Qbeast, a spinout from the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, has secured significant seed funding to develop a multi-dimensional indexing layer for open data lakehouse formats like Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg, and Apache Hudi. This innovation addresses the critical inefficiency in modern data architectures, where the majority of compute resources are wasted scanning irrelevant data. By enabling simultaneous filtering across multiple data attributes, Qbeast dramatically accelerates query performance and reduces costs without requiring organizations to overhaul their existing analytics pipelines. This advancement is vital for the open_data ecosystem because it enhances the usability and efficiency of open formats without introducing proprietary lock-in. In an era where data volumes are exploding, current storage solutions often force a trade-off between speed, cost, and openness. Qbeast’s engine-neutral approach allows teams to leverage powerful open standards while achieving sub-second performance and substantial resource savings, ensuring that the benefits of open architecture are fully realized in production environments. Ultimately, Qbeast aims to become the default indexing standard for the Lakehouse model, empowering enterprises to derive value from their data efficiently. By making open data platforms faster and more cost-effective, the company supports a future where data-driven innovation is accessible to all organizations, not just those with extensive engineering resources. This development reinforces the importance of optimizing open-source technologies to sustain scalable, high-performance analytics across industries.
