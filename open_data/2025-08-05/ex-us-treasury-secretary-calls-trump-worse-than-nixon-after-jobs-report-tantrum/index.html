Ex-US Treasury Secretary Calls Trump Worse Than Nixon After Jobs Report Tantrum

Ex-US Treasury Secretary Calls Trump Worse Than Nixon After Jobs Report Tantrum

Former Treasury Secretary Larry Summers argues that President Trump’s rejection of objective data signals a dangerous slide toward authoritarianism. By dismissing employment statistics as politically manipulated and removing dissenting officials, the administration undermines the fundamental trust in factual evidence required for democratic governance. This trend parallels historical attacks on independent institutions, suggesting a systemic erosion of checks and balances rather than mere policy disagreement. The broader implications extend to economic stability and institutional integrity. When leaders delegitimize rigorous data collection processes, businesses face uncertainty, leading to stagnation as markets hesitate to operate in an unpredictable regulatory environment. This chaos threatens not only economic growth but also the rule of law, creating an atmosphere where fear replaces accountability and public discourse becomes secondary to political expediency. This case is critically relevant to open data because it highlights the vulnerability of transparent, standardized metrics to political interference. It demonstrates how suppressing factual data distorts public understanding and policy-making. Protecting open data requires defending the independence of statistical agencies, ensuring that objective information remains free from partisan manipulation to maintain informed civic engagement and economic predictability.
