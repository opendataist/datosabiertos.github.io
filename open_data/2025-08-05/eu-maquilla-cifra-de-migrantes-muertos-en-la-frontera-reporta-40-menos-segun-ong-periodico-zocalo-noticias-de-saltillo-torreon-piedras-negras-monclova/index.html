EU ‘maquilla’ cifra de migrantes muertos en la frontera; reporta 40% menos según ONG | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Local authorities report significantly higher death tolls than the U.S. Border Patrol, revealing critical discrepancies in official migration records. This transparency highlights the vital role of open data in exposing systemic inaccuracies and ensuring accurate documentation of human rights crises, thereby enforcing accountability where institutional reporting falls short. The newly launched interactive platform geographically visualizes these fatalities, illustrating how common recording errors and unreported hospital deaths obscure the true scale of border-related deaths. By aggregating forensic data, the dataset offers a comprehensive view of causes such as extreme heat and accidents, demonstrating how open data initiatives can fill gaps left by government agencies to uncover hidden realities. Ultimately, this tool aims to understand the root causes of deaths at the border and drive policy change through evidence-based advocacy. Its relevance to open data lies in demonstrating that accessible, verified information is essential for challenging official narratives, protecting vulnerable populations, and fostering a more truthful public discourse on migration and human safety.
