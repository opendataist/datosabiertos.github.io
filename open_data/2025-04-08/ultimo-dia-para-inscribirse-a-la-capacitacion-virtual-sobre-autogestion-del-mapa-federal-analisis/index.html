Último día para inscribirse a la capacitación virtual sobre autogestión del Mapa Federal | Análisis

Último día para inscribirse a la capacitación virtual sobre autogestión del Mapa Federal | Análisis

The National Secretariat of Culture is launching a virtual training program to enhance the management and visibility of scenic spaces across the country. By teaching theater managers how to use the interactive Federal Map on the MICA platform, the initiative aims to standardize data entry and ensure accurate, up-to-date information about venues. This effort empowers local operators to autonomously maintain their profiles, improving the overall quality of the national cultural database. A primary goal is to optimize the georeferenced map, which serves as a critical tool for planning tours, managing productions, and developing public policies. By providing precise details on location, equipment, and programming, the platform facilitates better coordination among artists, producers, and cultural managers. This systematic approach to data management directly supports the efficiency of the theater sector and strengthens the network’s operational capabilities. This article is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates a state-led effort to build a reliable, publicly accessible geographic database. By encouraging stakeholders to contribute standardized, technical information to a digital infrastructure, the project promotes transparency and interoperability. It demonstrates how open data initiatives can be practically applied to manage cultural heritage, ensuring that spatial and operational data remains current, usable, and integral to decision-making processes in the creative industries.
