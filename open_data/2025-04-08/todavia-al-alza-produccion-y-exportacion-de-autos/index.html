Todavía al alza, producción y exportación de autos

INEGI reports significant growth in Mexico’s light vehicle sales, production, and exports, with exports heavily concentrated in the United States. This data highlights the industry’s vulnerability to new U.S. tariffs. The information is crucial for open-data transparency, enabling analysts to monitor how changes in trade policy affect Mexican manufacturing efficiency and global supply chain dynamics.
