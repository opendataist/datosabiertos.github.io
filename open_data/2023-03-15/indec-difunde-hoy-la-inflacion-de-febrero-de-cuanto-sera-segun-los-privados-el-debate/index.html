Indec difunde hoy la inflación de febrero: de cuánto será, según los privados - EL DEBATE

This article highlights a critical economic milestone in Argentina, where annual inflation has crossed the triple-digit threshold for the first time since the late 1980s. Driven by rising costs in food and regulated public services, this surge signals a severe loss of purchasing power and monetary instability for households and businesses. The relevance to open data lies in the essential role of official statistics, such as those from INDEC, in tracking these high-frequency inflation indicators. Transparent and accessible consumer price index data allows analysts and the public to monitor economic health, understand the drivers of price hikes, and hold institutions accountable for policy effectiveness. Accurate, timely open data is vital for validating private sector estimates and fostering public trust during periods of hyperinflation. Without reliable public data, stakeholders cannot accurately assess the impact of economic policies or make informed decisions, making the accessibility of these statistics crucial for transparency and democratic oversight.
