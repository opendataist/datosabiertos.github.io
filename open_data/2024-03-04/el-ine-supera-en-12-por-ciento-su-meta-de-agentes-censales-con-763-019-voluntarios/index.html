El INE supera en 12 por ciento su meta de agentes censales con 763.019 voluntarios

The National Institute of Statistics has successfully met its national goal for census agent recruitment, surpassing requirements in most departments. This surplus ensures a robust workforce ready for the upcoming census, although minor gaps remain in specific municipalities of Santa Cruz. To guarantee transparency and data quality, additional agents will be deployed in these remaining areas. Logistics are also complete, with all required census boxes assembled and distributed nationwide. This preparation highlights the critical importance of open data initiatives, as accurate census results depend on rigorous fieldwork and transparent organizational processes. The thorough staffing and equipment readiness demonstrate how reliable public data generation requires meticulous planning and resource allocation. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the foundational role of high-quality census data in public governance. Transparent recruitment and distribution processes ensure that the resulting statistics are trustworthy and accessible. Ultimately, this operational integrity supports the creation of reliable open datasets that inform policy and foster public trust in official statistics.
