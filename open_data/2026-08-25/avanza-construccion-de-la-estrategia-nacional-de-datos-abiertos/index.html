Avanza construcción de la Estrategia Nacional de Datos Abiertos

Guatemala is leading the co-creation of its National Open Data Strategy through inclusive workshops that bring together diverse state institutions. The main objective is to unify efforts to build a shared instrument by 2030, recognizing that transparency and citizen participation require multi-sectoral collaboration. This initiative seeks to transform public information management, ensuring that data are not only published but also effectively organized and updated to serve society’s real needs. The process emphasizes that open data is a strategic tool for generating public value, improving decision-making, and strengthening government services. By integrating perspectives from the private sector, academia, and civil society, the aim is to create an ecosystem where information is reliable, reusable, and accessible. This facilitates accountability and promotes innovation, demonstrating that co-creation is essential to ensure that strategies address concrete problems rather than being mere bureaucratic exercises. This case is relevant to the field of open data because it illustrates the crucial step from technical publication to collaborative governance. It shows that the sustainability of open data depends on institutional ownership and the ability to build consensus among multiple stakeholders. By prioritizing interoperability and coordination, Guatemala offers a model of how governments can align with international open government standards to achieve more efficient, citizen-centered administration.
