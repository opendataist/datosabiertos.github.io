Michoacán exhibits a remarkably low unemployment rate, with nearly the entire active workforce engaged in economically productive activities. This success is driven by strategic public-private collaboration aimed at formalizing labor through official accreditation programs. These initiatives prioritize increasing workers’ enrollment in social security systems and ensuring compliance with international trade agreements. By standardizing employment conditions, the state aligns local practices with the rigorous requirements of the USMCA and APEC. This case underscores the value of open data in verifying economic indicators. Transparent access to employment statistics enables stakeholders to monitor formalization efforts, fostering accountability and demonstrating how data transparency supports sustainable economic development and regulatory compliance.
