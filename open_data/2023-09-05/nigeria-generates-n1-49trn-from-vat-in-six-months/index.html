Nigeria generates N1.49trn from VAT in six months

Nigeria’s VAT revenue surged significantly in early 2023, reflecting robust consumption and effective tax administration. This substantial growth underscores the critical role of indirect taxes in sustaining federal, state, and local government operations across the nation. Such transparent fiscal data is vital for open data ecosystems, enabling researchers and citizens to monitor economic health and government performance. By making revenue streams visible, stakeholders can better assess policy effectiveness and demand accountability from revenue authorities. Ultimately, accessible tax data empowers evidence-based discourse on public finance. It allows for deeper analysis of sectoral contributions, fostering informed decisions that support sustainable economic development and equitable resource distribution among all government tiers.
