En 2022, Gobierno de Veracruz recortó a más de mil trabajadores

The article highlights a significant reduction in Veracruz’s public workforce between 2021 and 2022, revealing cuts exceeding one thousand employees. This shift underscores how administrative efficiency metrics directly impact the quality of public sector employment data. Simultaneously, the gender composition of the remaining staff changed, with female representation increasing by four percent. These demographic shifts illustrate how personnel changes affect workforce diversity statistics, a key dimension of comprehensive government transparency. This data is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates the importance of tracking detailed personnel metrics. Accurate, accessible information on government hiring and reductions enables citizens to monitor fiscal responsibility and equity in public administration effectively.
