CSIF pide destinar más dinero a la creación de empleo estable y de calidad

The seasonal job losses in August highlight labor instability in Castilla-La Mancha, characterized by rising unemployment, high female joblessness, and salaries significantly below the national average. This precarious employment landscape underscores the urgent need for structural reforms to ensure stable, quality jobs rather than temporary fixes. The region’s heavy reliance on temporary contracts and clientelistic subsidies exacerbates these issues, failing to address the root causes of employment insecurity. By prioritizing political alliances over genuine economic strategy, the current approach neglects the fundamental requirements for sustainable labor market development. This situation is critical for open data, as it reveals how transparency in public spending and labor statistics can expose systemic inefficiencies. Open access to such granular data empowers citizens to demand evidence-based policies, challenging opaque practices and advocating for a fairer, more accountable economic framework that genuinely supports workforce stability.
