INAI alista sesión extraordinaria este domingo

The INAI will elect its new president for the 2023–2026 term, marking a critical juncture for institutional continuity. This leadership transition directly impacts the enforcement of transparency rights, shaping how public data is managed and protected in Mexico. The election process involves multiple voting rounds to ensure a qualified majority, reflecting the high stakes of choosing the next guardian of access to information. Stable leadership is essential to maintain the integrity of data governance frameworks and prevent political interference. This selection is vital for open data because it determines the strategic direction of transparency policies. Effective oversight ensures that government information remains accessible, fostering accountability and enabling citizens to exercise their right to know, which is foundational for democratic participation.
