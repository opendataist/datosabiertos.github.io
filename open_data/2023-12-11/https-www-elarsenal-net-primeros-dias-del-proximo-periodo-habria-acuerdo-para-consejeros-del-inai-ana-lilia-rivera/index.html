The Senate has made significant progress in appointing members to the INAI Council, launching a new procedure aimed at resolving long-standing stagnation. This advancement is crucial for open data, as an independent and fully staffed transparency institute ensures robust oversight of government information, thereby strengthening institutional accountability and public trust in data access mechanisms. At the same time, legislative reforms seek to guarantee universal and free healthcare by expanding the IMSS-Bienestar Fund to all states, prioritizing populations that currently lack access to the system. These measures reflect a broader commitment to equitable social services, ensuring that essential health rights are protected regardless of local administrative agreements. Additionally, the approval of public veterinary clinics extends social guarantees to companion animals, promoting preventive care nationwide. While this highlights the government’s dedication to comprehensive welfare, the transparency appointments remain the most pertinent aspect for open data, as they directly impact the structural integrity of the nation’s right to information framework.
