WhatsApp: ¿Es posible enviar mensajes sin internet ni datos?

The article reveals a significant workaround for using WhatsApp without an internet connection by leveraging proxy servers. These servers, often configured by volunteers or organizations, act as intermediaries that allow users to send and receive messages and calls even in low-connectivity environments. This feature highlights the platform’s adaptability, ensuring that communication remains possible when standard Wi-Fi or mobile data networks are unavailable or restricted. This method is directly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates how decentralized infrastructure can support universal access to information. By utilizing community-driven proxy lists, users bypass traditional connectivity barriers, promoting digital inclusion in regions with limited internet resources. The article emphasizes that while IP addresses are shared with providers, the end-to-end encryption of messages remains intact, preserving user privacy despite the alternative routing method. However, the reliance on third-party proxies introduces potential vulnerabilities regarding data transparency and security. Users must actively manage their connection by searching for and updating proxy addresses, which requires a level of technical literacy. Consequently, while this tool empowers users to maintain communication, it also underscores the critical need for open, verified, and secure network protocols in open data ecosystems to ensure both accessibility and integrity without compromising individual privacy.
