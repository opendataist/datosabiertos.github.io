Salto con 8% de desempleo y un 34% de informalidad

The report reveals significant regional disparities in Uruguay’s labor market, highlighting a stark contrast between low unemployment in some areas and high informal work rates in others. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for transparent, geographically granular statistics to accurately assess economic health and identify vulnerable populations. Such detailed transparency enables better policy formulation by exposing structural inequalities in employment security. By making these metrics accessible, citizens and analysts can track progress toward formalization goals and demand accountability from institutions responsible for social welfare and labor regulations. Ultimately, releasing this granular information fosters public trust and informed debate. It allows communities to understand local economic realities, empowering civil society to advocate for targeted interventions that reduce informality and improve job quality across different departments.
