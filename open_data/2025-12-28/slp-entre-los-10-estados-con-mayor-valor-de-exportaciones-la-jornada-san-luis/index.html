SLP, entre los 10 estados con mayor valor de exportaciones - La Jornada San Luis

San Luis Potosí reinforced its national economic leadership by securing ninth place in export value, driven primarily by a booming manufacturing sector. This sustained growth highlights the success of state policies aimed at attracting investment and diversifying international markets. The state emerged as a leading exporter of transport equipment, demonstrating significant annual growth that outperformed other top-performing entities. This performance reflects a coordinated effort to enhance supply chains and boost the region’s global competitiveness.
