La Nación / Ministerio Público reporta tres feminicidios y cuatro huérfanos en los primeros días del año 2026

The publication of open data by Paraguay’s Public Ministry highlights critical patterns in femicide statistics. This transparency reveals demographic details and the relationships between victims and perpetrators, enabling society to analyze the root causes of violence against women. Such accessible information is vital for identifying trends, such as the involvement of family members or minors, which informs targeted public policies. By making these specific case details available, authorities foster accountability and raise awareness. The data underscores the urgent need for systemic interventions to protect vulnerable groups, including indigenous women and children. Ultimately, this initiative demonstrates how open data transforms raw statistics into actionable insights for social change. The relevance of open data lies in its ability to democratize information, allowing citizens and researchers to scrutinize institutional responses. This access empowers civil society to demand improved protection mechanisms. Consequently, open data serves as a powerful tool for driving evidence-based advocacy and reducing gender-based violence through informed public engagement.
