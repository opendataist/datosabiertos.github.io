NSO releases draft guidelines for compiling GSDP via expenditure method

NSO releases draft guidelines for compiling GSDP via expenditure method

The National Statistics Office has introduced draft guidelines to standardize Gross State Domestic Product estimation from the expenditure side. This initiative aims to ensure methodological consistency and comparability across all Indian states and union territories, addressing current disparities where some regions lack comprehensive data for key components like private consumption. By providing a unified framework, the guidelines facilitate the compilation of essential economic indicators that were previously inconsistent or missing at the sub-national level. This harmonization strengthens the overall state-level national accounts framework, ensuring that regional estimates align more closely with national methodologies and offering a more complete picture of economic activity. This development is significant for open data as it establishes transparent, reproducible standards for sub-national economic reporting. It encourages greater data availability and interoperability, allowing researchers and policymakers to utilize more reliable, standardized metrics for regional analysis and comparative studies across different jurisdictions.
