Los votantes en el extranjero se elevan hasta los 187.461 en la región

The electoral census of Castilla y León has experienced a slight overall growth, driven primarily by a significant increase in Spanish residents living abroad. This rise offsets the modest decline in the resident population within the region, creating a new balance in the demographic composition of the electorate. This dynamic reflects migratory and natural changes that directly impact the political representativeness of the region. The relevance of these data for the open data movement lies in the transparency and accessibility of public information provided by the National Statistics Institute (INE). The detailed availability of these census records enables civil society, researchers, and developers to analyze demographic, migratory, and citizen participation trends. By making this data open, accountability is promoted, and citizens are empowered to verify their own electoral information, thereby safeguarding the integrity of the democratic process. Furthermore, the geographic and demographic distribution reveals inequalities and opportunities in electoral coverage across the various provinces. While some areas see their foreign-born electorate grow, others face declines in local residents, necessitating adjustments in electoral logistics and planning. Understanding these variations is crucial for improving the efficiency of voting systems and ensuring that every segment of the population, particularly young people and the diaspora, has equitable access to their political rights.
