Viceministra de Tecnología participa en encuentro regional para impulsar la apertura de datos públicos

Viceministra de Tecnología participa en encuentro regional para impulsar la apertura de datos públicos

The AbreLatam/ConDatos 2026 celebration in Guatemala highlights the regional importance of open data in driving transparency and innovation in public management. The event brought together experts from multiple countries to strengthen the National Data Strategy, demonstrating how collaboration between institutions and civil society improves access to information. The participation of international leaders and local stakeholders underscores that data openness is key to reducing the digital divide and promoting evidence-based public decision-making. This exchange of knowledge reinforces the need for robust policies that ensure data truly serves the public interest. This gathering is relevant to the open data ecosystem because it showcases the concrete actions needed to transform information into accountability tools. By prioritizing transnational cooperation, standards are established that enhance the quality and impact of public data across the entire region.
