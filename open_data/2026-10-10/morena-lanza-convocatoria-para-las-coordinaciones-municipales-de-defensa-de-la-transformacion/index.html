Morena lanza convocatoria para las Coordinaciones Municipales de Defensa de la Transformación

Morena lanza convocatoria para las Coordinaciones Municipales de Defensa de la Transformación

Morena has launched a digital call for applications to select municipal coordinators, strengthening its territorial structure in preparation for the 2027 elections. This process aims to identify who will lead the transformation and national sovereignty efforts in each municipality, integrating teams aligned with the party’s principles. The transparency in the registration of candidates and the clarity of the competition’s rules reflect an effort to democratize internal participation. By establishing clear guidelines for the appointment of local leaders, the initiative promotes accountability and transparency within the political organization. This initiative is relevant to open data, as it documents how political parties modernize their personnel selection processes. It enables the analysis of internal governance and administrative efficiency of democratic institutions through public access to information about their structures and calls for applications.
