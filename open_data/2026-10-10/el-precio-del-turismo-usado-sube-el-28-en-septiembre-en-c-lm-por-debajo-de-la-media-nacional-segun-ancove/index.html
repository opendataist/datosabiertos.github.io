El precio del turismo usado sube el 2,8% en septiembre en C-LM, por debajo de la media nacional, según Ancove

El precio del turismo usado sube el 2,8% en septiembre en C-LM, por debajo de la media nacional, según Ancove

The analysis of the used vehicle market reveals a moderation in price increases in September, despite a high inflationary environment. Nationally, the average cost shows a moderate annual increase, remaining below the previous annual average. This trend indicates that, although overall prices are rising, the monthly dynamics reflect a slowdown compared to the previous month, suggesting some stabilization in a complex economic context where underlying inflation remains a significant concern for consumers. The market's evolution highlights a clear segmentation based on vehicle age and price. While older cars, which make up the majority of the market, experience significant price increases, there is growing interest in newer and more expensive units. This structural shift, though gradual, suggests that buyers are shifting their preferences toward higher-value vehicles, moving away from the ultra-low-cost segment, thereby gradually transforming the composition of the used car market toward more modern models. The relevance of this article to open data lies in the importance of accessing detailed, disaggregated time series by region, age, and price range. Without high-quality, granular open data, it is impossible to analyze these micro-trends, such as interregional variations or the specific demographic impact in cities like Ceuta or Melilla. Transparency in these data allows analysts and policymakers to understand the real dynamics of the market, detect territorial inequalities, and adjust mobility strategies, demonstrating that access to detailed information is essential for understanding complex economic phenomena.
