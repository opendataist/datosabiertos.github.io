Project to build big-data legal database approved

Project to build big-data legal database approved

Vietnam has approved a major initiative to build a national Big Data database on law, aiming to restructure its legal infrastructure by ensuring all normative documents are available in machine-readable formats. This project seeks to create a centralized repository that integrates, standardizes, and links legal data from various ministries and localities. By achieving full digitization of legislative processes and legal texts, the government intends to establish a robust foundation for accurate and effective artificial intelligence applications in governance. The implementation of this database is designed to significantly enhance smart legal governance and public transparency. By making legal information more accessible and easier to analyze for citizens, businesses, and agencies, the initiative promises to reduce the time and costs associated with accessing legal knowledge. This accessibility supports a stronger culture of legal compliance and improves the efficiency of both lawmaking and law enforcement, ultimately positioning the rule of law as a competitive national advantage. This development is highly relevant to open data because it represents a strategic shift toward treating legal information as a structured, shareable digital asset. By enforcing standards for data cleanliness, consistency, and sharing, the project facilitates the integration of legal data into broader digital ecosystems. Such an approach not only modernizes government operations but also encourages innovation through open access to reliable legal datasets, fostering transparency and enabling advanced analytical tools that benefit society at large.
