C-LM alcanzó las 159.662 pernoctaciones en alojamientos turísticos extrahoteleros durante el mes de junio

The article highlights a robust recovery in Spain’s non-hotel tourism sector, with overnight stays rising significantly compared to previous years, although recent monthly figures show slight fluctuations between domestic and international visitors. This data reflects a dynamic market where prices are increasing across various accommodation types, indicating strong demand and inflationary pressures in the hospitality industry. This information is relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of standardized, publicly available statistical indicators such as the EOAT survey. Transparent access to such detailed metrics enables researchers, policymakers, and the public to monitor economic trends, assess sector performance, and make informed decisions based on accurate, comparable regional and national data.
