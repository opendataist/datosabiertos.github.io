Ofertas de empleo de EE.UU. caen a su nivel más bajo en dos años

The U.S. labor market is showing significant cooling, with job vacancies falling to their lowest level since early 2021. This decline, along with fewer resignations and slower net job creation, reflects the tightening effect of Federal Reserve interest rate hikes aimed at curbing inflation. These shifting dynamics indicate a substantial deceleration in economic momentum as monetary policy efforts begin to take effect. This data is highly relevant to open_data because it highlights the critical need for transparent and accessible government statistics. Reliable, open labor metrics enable researchers and the public to independently verify economic trends, thereby fostering accountability. Moreover, granular open datasets allow for more in-depth analysis of sector-specific variations, helping stakeholders understand the nuanced impacts of policy decisions beyond headline figures.
