Bolivia registra crecimiento económico del 2,14% al tercer trimestre de 2024

Bolivia registra crecimiento económico del 2,14% al tercer trimestre de 2024

The country’s third-quarter GDP growth experienced a slight decline compared to the previous period but remained stronger than in the first quarter, driven primarily by robust performance in personal services, hospitality, and utilities. This trend highlights the resilience of consumer-facing sectors and infrastructure-related industries such as electricity and gas, which continue to expand and support overall economic stability despite fluctuations in other areas. Conversely, the petroleum and gas sector suffered a significant contraction due to ongoing production declines, negatively impacting the broader economy. While the agricultural sector showed mixed results—with strong growth in livestock and basic food items—industrial crops like soy experienced substantial decreases. These disparities reveal underlying vulnerabilities in export-oriented industries and suggest that domestic food security remains relatively stable compared to volatile global commodity markets. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the necessity of transparent, granular, and real-time statistical monitoring. By disaggregating economic performance across diverse sectors and regions, such reports enable policymakers, researchers, and the public to identify specific areas of growth and stagnation. This level of detail is essential for evidence-based decision-making, allowing stakeholders to address regional imbalances—such as the contrasting construction trends in Santa Cruz and La Paz—and to formulate targeted strategies for sustainable economic development.
