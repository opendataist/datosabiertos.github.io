Gremio condena “prácticas violentas” de Yamy Nal tras amenazas a periodista

Threats against journalists violate the fundamental principles of freedom of expression, which are essential for a healthy democracy. Open data initiatives depend on transparent information flows to hold public officials accountable, making such intimidation a direct threat to transparency. Protecting media workers from harassment ensures that critical reports, such as those concerning public sector hiring practices, can be published without fear. This security enables the continuous flow of data necessary for societal oversight and institutional integrity. Ultimately, guaranteeing press freedom is a prerequisite for effective open data ecosystems. When officials intimidate reporters, they obstruct access to the truth, undermining the public’s right to know and eroding trust in democratic institutions and data-driven accountability.
