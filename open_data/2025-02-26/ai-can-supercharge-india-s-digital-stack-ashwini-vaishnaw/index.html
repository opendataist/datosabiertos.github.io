AI can supercharge India's digital stack: Ashwini Vaishnaw

AI can supercharge India's digital stack: Ashwini Vaishnaw

India’s digital public infrastructure, including Aadhaar and UPI, can achieve exponential growth by integrating artificial intelligence. The government is already deploying specialized small models to solve specific problems, leveraging the unique advantage of having vast, structured public datasets. This strategic combination promises to multiply the impact of these systems significantly. This synergy offers India a competitive edge in the global tech landscape, further strengthened by international recognition of its payment architecture. By combining high-quality training data with a structured digital approach, India aims to lead in developing AI solutions that are both innovative and uniquely suited to its population’s needs. Crucially, this approach is highly relevant to open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible public data fuels national innovation. The minister advocates for a techno-legal regulatory framework that balances safety concerns like privacy and bias with the need to avoid stifling innovation through heavy-handed laws, ensuring open data remains a catalyst for progress rather than a regulatory burden.
