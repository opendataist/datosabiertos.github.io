Pide Coneval continuar con evaluación de la pobreza

Pide Coneval continuar con evaluación de la pobreza

CONEVAL urges the Mexican government to preserve the autonomous, rigorous technical production of poverty data currently being transferred to INEGI. This continuity is essential because two decades of consistent methodology provide the foundational evidence base for designing and evaluating social policies at all levels of government. The availability of detailed indicators and monitoring systems enables precise targeting of vulnerable populations and ensures accountability in public management. By maintaining these robust evaluation frameworks, authorities can effectively assess program impacts and adjust interventions based on reliable, objective results rather than arbitrary metrics. This transition underscores the critical intersection between open data and the effectiveness of public policy. Transparent, high-quality statistical infrastructure empowers democratic oversight and ensures that resource allocation remains aligned with actual societal needs, demonstrating that rigorous data independence is foundational for trustworthy governance and equitable development strategies.
