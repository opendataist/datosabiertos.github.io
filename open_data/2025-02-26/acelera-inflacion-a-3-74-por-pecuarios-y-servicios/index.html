Acelera inflación a 3.74 % por pecuarios y servicios

Acelera inflación a 3.74 % por pecuarios y servicios

The recent inflation data signals the end of a six-period deceleration, driven primarily by rising costs in livestock products and specific services such as housing and education. This upward trend contrasts with temporary relief from lower fruit and vegetable prices, indicating a complex inflationary pressure that has surprised market expectations. This shift is highly relevant to open data, as it underscores the necessity for transparent, high-frequency statistical releases from institutions like INEGI. Reliable access to such granular economic indicators allows researchers and the public to effectively analyze real-time purchasing power and the impacts of policy measures. Ultimately, the transparency of these metrics fosters public trust and enables accurate societal forecasting. By making the drivers of inflation visible, open data supports informed public discourse and holds institutions accountable for economic stability, ensuring that citizens can understand the tangible effects of price fluctuations on their daily lives.
