Lanza Cuauhtémoc convocatoria para celebrar Día de la Niña y el Niño Funcionario - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

Lanza Cuauhtémoc convocatoria para celebrar Día de la Niña y el Niño Funcionario - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

Cuauhtémoc empowers children to participate in governance by allowing them to assume symbolic roles for a day, thereby fostering civic awareness and social development. This initiative transforms minors into agents of change, helping them understand the impact of government on society. Participants engage by writing essays and creating drawings based on the key pillars of government programs. This process encourages critical thinking about local policies while affirming children’s right to influence community decisions through creative expression and structured reflection. This case highlights the relevance of open data by demonstrating how transparency and accessible information enable meaningful civic engagement. When governments share clear policy frameworks, citizens—including children—can meaningfully interact with public administration. Such initiatives underscore the importance of open data in democratizing knowledge and fostering inclusive participation in democratic processes.
