Against Data Colonisation: Why Africa built the visionary prompt framework planetary 2026 - MyJoyOnline

The article argues that current AI systems perpetuate "data colonization" by imposing Western epistemologies on African realities, effectively treating indigenous knowledge as extractable resources without retaining intellectual sovereignty. It introduces the Planetary Visionary Prompt Framework as a necessary counter-model, shifting the focus from mere computational efficiency to civilizational integrity. This framework asserts that true intelligence cannot be reduced to singular algorithmic logic but must respect plural, context-specific ways of knowing. Central to this approach is the "Chambers of Intelligence," a structure that elevates indigenous, ecological, and ancestral knowledge to parity with artificial and human cognition. By establishing a "Council of Lenses" for ethical governance and a "Cognitive Validation Matrix" for truth verification, the framework ensures that AI outputs are culturally coherent and factually grounded. This replaces the statistical probability weighting of Western models with explicit sovereign ethics, preventing the amplification of biased or extractive narratives. This perspective is crucial for open data because it challenges the dominant paradigm of data neutrality. It demonstrates that open data ecosystems must prioritize epistemic justice and local governance structures to avoid replicating historical power imbalances. By integrating diverse knowledge systems into technical architectures, open data can become a tool for reclaiming agency, ensuring that technological advancement supports, rather than erases, local cultural and intellectual sovereignty.
