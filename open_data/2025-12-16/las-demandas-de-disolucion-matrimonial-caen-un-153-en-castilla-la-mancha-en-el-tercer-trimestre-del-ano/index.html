Las demandas de disolución matrimonial caen un 15,3% en Castilla-La Mancha en el tercer trimestre del año

Las demandas de disolución matrimonial caen un 15,3% en Castilla-La Mancha en el tercer trimestre del año

Castilla-La Mancha shows a growing reliance on consensual divorce, indicating that couples increasingly prefer amicable settlements over contentious legal battles. This shift toward mutual agreement suggests a cultural change in how marital dissolution is handled, reducing judicial friction for many families. The data highlights a divergence in legal trends, with non-consensual separations declining significantly while consensual ones rise. This contrast underscores that while some conflicts remain, there is a clear movement toward collaborative resolution, reflecting broader societal changes in relationship management and legal expectations. This information is vital for open data as it demonstrates how transparent access to judicial statistics enables the public to understand societal trends. By making this data available, citizens can analyze family law dynamics, promoting accountability and helping policymakers design better support systems for marital disputes.
