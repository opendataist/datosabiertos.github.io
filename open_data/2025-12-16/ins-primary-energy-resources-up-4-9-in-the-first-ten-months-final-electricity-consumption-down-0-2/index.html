INS: Primary energy resources up 4.9% in the first ten months;final electricity consumption down 0.2%

Romania demonstrates a shifting energy landscape where increased imports and solar growth offset declines in domestic production and hydroelectric output. This highlights the complex dynamics of national energy security and the transition toward renewable sources. Final consumption trends reveal divergent patterns across sectors, with household demand rising while industrial use fell. These insights are crucial for understanding open data applications in energy policy, allowing researchers to model consumption habits and optimize resource distribution based on transparent, accessible statistics. This data underscores the importance of real-time monitoring in public utilities. By making such detailed metrics available, open data facilitates better grid management and supports informed decision-making regarding infrastructure investments and sustainability goals in the evolving energy market.
