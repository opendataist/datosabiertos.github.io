Datos Abiertos IMSS

Mexican labor market data reveal structural gaps between registered jobs and insured individuals. This discrepancy underscores significant challenges in workforce registration and social security coverage. For open data initiatives, such transparency is crucial for analyzing economic stability and identifying systemic inefficiencies in the labor market.
