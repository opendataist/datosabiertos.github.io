Berlin's 3D city model is available as open data

Berlin has launched a comprehensive 3D city model as open data, transforming existing visual resources into accessible, downloadable datasets for researchers and developers. This initiative democratizes complex geographic information, allowing users to extract specific building details for diverse applications in urban planning, gaming, and architecture. By releasing this extensive model, the city fosters innovation and supports the digital economy, empowering startups and scientific institutions with unique foundational data. This move solidifies Berlin’s reputation as a European leader in digital transformation, encouraging technology-based growth through transparent and reusable public resources. This release is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates the practical value of publishing high-volume, complex datasets. It highlights how making detailed geographic information freely available can stimulate economic activity, enhance research capabilities, and drive further innovation across multiple sectors within the digital ecosystem.
