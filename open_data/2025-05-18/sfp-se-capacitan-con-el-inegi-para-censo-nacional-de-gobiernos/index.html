SFP se capacitan con el INEGI para Censo Nacional de Gobiernos

SFP se capacitan con el INEGI para Censo Nacional de Gobiernos

The SFP trained officials to accurately complete the 2025 State Governments Census, ensuring data clarity and transparency for INEGI. This initiative guarantees compliance with legal mandates for the submission of truthful and up-to-date information. Accurate statistical data supports better decision-making and reinforces institutional openness. It strengthens the transparency of Chihuahua’s government by establishing reliable and accessible records. This training is vital for open data because it prioritizes high-quality, standardized public information. Robust datasets enable greater accountability and empower citizens with clear insights into government operations.
