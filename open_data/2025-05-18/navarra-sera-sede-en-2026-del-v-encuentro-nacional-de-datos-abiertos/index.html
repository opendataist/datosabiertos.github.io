Navarra será sede en 2026 del V Encuentro Nacional de Datos Abiertos

Navarra será sede en 2026 del V Encuentro Nacional de Datos Abiertos

Navarra will host the 2026 National Open Data Meeting, signaling a strong institutional commitment to transparency, citizen participation, and the reuse of public information. This recognition highlights the region's successful consolidation of policies based on open government principles, positioning Navarra as a reference territory in digital administration and data governance within Spain. The core strategy focuses on using open data as a strategic engine to improve the effectiveness of public policy and decision-making through better analysis. Rather than merely increasing the volume of data, the regional government prioritizes data quality, interoperability, and the integration of diverse public and private sources. This approach ensures that information is accessible, structured, and reusable by citizens and social actors, fostering a collaborative ecosystem that enhances social and economic value. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates the practical implementation of open government frameworks, such as the Open Government Partnership. By prioritizing ethical data management, professional training, and multi-stakeholder collaboration, the case illustrates how regional administrations can operationalize openness to create more accountable, service-oriented, and data-driven public services.
