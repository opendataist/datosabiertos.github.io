Debe Banco del Bienestar informar proceso de licitación de sucursal

The National Institute for Transparency ruled that the Banco del Bienestar must electronically publish detailed budget and contractor information for its new branch via the National Transparency Platform. This decision affirms that such public spending data is inherently transparent and obligates institutions to keep it updated digitally, rejecting the bank’s claim that administrative workload justified alternative delivery methods. This ruling is crucial for open data because it reinforces the legal mandate for proactive digital disclosure of public procurement and operational details. By enforcing electronic availability, the decision ensures that citizens and developers can easily access and reuse government spending data, promoting accountability and facilitating the creation of open data ecosystems based on accurate, machine-readable information.
