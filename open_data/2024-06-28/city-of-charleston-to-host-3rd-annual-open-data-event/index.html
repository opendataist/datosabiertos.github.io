City of Charleston to host 3rd annual Open Data event

Charleston hosts a virtual event to teach residents how to access and use public data for personal projects and civic decision-making. This initiative empowers the community to engage with local government priorities through data transparency. Such efforts are vital for open data as they bridge the gap between official datasets and public utility, fostering informed civic participation.
