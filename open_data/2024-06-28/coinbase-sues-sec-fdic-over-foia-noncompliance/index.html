Coinbase sues SEC, FDIC over FOIA noncompliance

Coinbase sues SEC, FDIC over FOIA noncompliance

Coinbase has sued the SEC and FDIC, alleging these federal agencies colluded to exclude the crypto industry from the banking sector. By withholding critical information, regulators are accused of deliberately obscuring the legal framework governing digital assets. This lawsuit challenges the transparency of government operations, arguing that such opacity hinders the industry’s ability to understand and comply with regulations. The legal action stems from denied Freedom of Information Act requests concerning Ethereum’s technical shift and past enforcement investigations. Coinbase contends that regulators are using financial tools to cripple digital asset firms rather than fostering a clear regulatory environment. This move highlights the urgent need for transparent governance, ensuring that crypto businesses can operate with certainty amidst evolving federal policies. This case is vital to open data because it underscores the struggle for accessible government information in emerging sectors. Transparent records are essential for understanding regulatory trends and legal precedents in the crypto space. By demanding disclosure, Coinbase advocates for a more open regulatory framework, enabling the industry to engage meaningfully with policymakers and fostering accountability in how digital assets are governed.
