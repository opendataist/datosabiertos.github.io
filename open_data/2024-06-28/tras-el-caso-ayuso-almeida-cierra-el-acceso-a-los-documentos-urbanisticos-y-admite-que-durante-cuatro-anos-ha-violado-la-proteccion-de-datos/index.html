Tras el caso Ayuso, Almeida cierra el acceso a los documentos urbanísticos y admite que durante cuatro años ha violado la protección de datos

Tras el caso Ayuso, Almeida cierra el acceso a los documentos urbanísticos y admite que durante cuatro años ha violado la protección de datos

The Madrid City Council has temporarily shut down its Conex platform, a key urban transparency portal that had enabled public access to records of construction projects and permits. The decision was based on recommendations from the council’s internal data protection office, which argued that the database contained personal information exposed without adequate anonymization, posing a risk to citizens’ freedoms. This closure represents a significant setback in the availability of public information, forcing users to revert to in-person or complex online consultation systems. The relevance of this case for open data lies in the tension between individual privacy and the right to administrative transparency. Experts note that while data protection is essential, authorities are rarely sanctioned for such breaches, raising doubts about the proportionality of a measure that broadly obscures information of public interest. There is debate over whether this opacity is truly necessary or whether it serves political interests stemming from recent journalistic investigations into the partner of the regional president, thereby calling into question the neutrality of public data management. Ultimately, the incident underscores the need for clear regulatory frameworks that balance legal certainty with innovation in access to information. While the City Council has announced its intention to reopen the portal after anonymizing the data, the resulting uncertainty affects residents and professionals who relied on this tool to verify the legality of urban planning actions. This case serves as a warning about how a restrictive interpretation of privacy can undermine the effectiveness and credibility of open government initiatives, hindering citizen oversight of land use and permits.
