This announcement outlines a judicial hiring process that requires specific legal expertise, disciplinary training, and knowledge of gender issues. Open data enthusiasts should note the reliance on closed, non-machine-readable formats such as PDFs and proprietary link-only forms, which hinder automated analysis and public scrutiny of eligibility criteria. The relevance to open data lies in the call for standardized, accessible digital records.
