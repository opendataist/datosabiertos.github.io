Pepe Chedraui impulsa la transparencia con la presentación del Portal de Gobierno Abierto - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Pepe Chedraui impulsa la transparencia con la presentación del Portal de Gobierno Abierto - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The Municipality of Puebla has launched the Open Government Portal, a digital platform designed to centralize and simplify access to public information. This initiative aims to ensure transparency and accountability by providing citizens with clear, up-to-date data on budget execution, available services, and administrative actions. By making resource allocation and government performance visible, the administration seeks to strengthen the social contract and build trust through verifiable results. A key innovation of this platform is its commitment to universal accessibility, featuring an interface specifically designed for individuals with visual or auditory disabilities. This ensures that the right to access information is inclusive, allowing all citizens to participate in civic life regardless of their physical conditions. Furthermore, the portal includes tools for data protection and citizen guidance, reinforcing the ethical standards of the administration and promoting informed decision-making among the population. This project is highly relevant to the open data movement because it demonstrates how structured, machine-readable data can be transformed into usable public goods. By moving beyond simple document publication to create an interactive, accessible digital infrastructure, the municipality sets a precedent for how local governments can leverage technology to foster active citizenship. It illustrates that true openness requires not just data availability, but also usability, inclusivity, and genuine dialogue between the state and its citizens.
