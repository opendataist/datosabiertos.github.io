Pollo, uva y aguacate, los que más contuvieron la inflación en julio

The INEGI reports that general inflation closed June at 4.51% year-on-year, driven by price increases in eggs, housing, and dining. Conversely, costs for chicken, grapes, avocado, and tomatoes fell, acting as deflationary pressures. These figures highlight how specific consumer goods significantly influence macroeconomic indicators. Understanding which items drive price changes is crucial for analyzing economic trends and policy impacts. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the importance of accessible, granular statistical records. Transparency in such metrics allows researchers and citizens to better understand economic realities and hold institutions accountable.
