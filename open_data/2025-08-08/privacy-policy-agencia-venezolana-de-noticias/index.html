Privacy Policy - Agencia Venezolana de Noticias

Privacy Policy - Agencia Venezolana de Noticias

This privacy policy outlines how the website handles user data, emphasizing transparency in data collection and retention practices. It details the automatic gathering of technical information such as IP addresses and browser identifiers during comment submissions to facilitate spam detection. Additionally, it highlights the use of hashed email addresses for Gravatar services, linking user interactions to external privacy frameworks while maintaining a level of anonymity for the visitor. The document explains the functional use of cookies, which streamline user experiences by remembering login details and form inputs. It also warns users about embedded content from third-party sources, noting that such elements behave as if the user directly visited those external sites, potentially allowing third-party tracking and data collection. Furthermore, it advises caution when uploading images to avoid exposing embedded geographical location data to public viewers. Relevance to open_data lies in the critical distinction between open information systems and private data stewardship. While open data advocates for the free exchange of public datasets, this policy demonstrates the necessary safeguards for personal information within digital platforms. It illustrates how transparency regarding data retention, user rights to access or erase data, and secure administrative practices are essential components of ethical data management. Understanding these boundaries helps clarify how open data initiatives must respect individual privacy rights and comply with legal obligations regarding personal data protection.
