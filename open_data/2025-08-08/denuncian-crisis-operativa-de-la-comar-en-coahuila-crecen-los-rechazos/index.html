Denuncian crisis operativa de la Comar en Coahuila: crecen los rechazos

Denuncian crisis operativa de la Comar en Coahuila: crecen los rechazos

Delays and rejections in Mexican refugee status procedures severely hinder applicants in Coahuila, violating their rights by preventing formal employment and dignified living conditions during prolonged waits. This administrative bottleneck extends beyond traditional southern hubs to northern states, highlighting a systemic failure to process claims efficiently due to staff shortages and procedural stagnation. The situation is exacerbated by the Commission’s failure to publish monthly asylum data, creating a transparency vacuum that obscures decision-making patterns. This lack of open data, combined with allegations of corruption among officials and lawyers, undermines accountability and makes it impossible to verify the fairness of current approval rates against previous standards. This article is relevant to open_data because the withdrawal of statistical transparency prevents independent monitoring of human rights compliance. Without accessible, granular data on asylum cases, civil society and researchers cannot analyze trends, identify inefficiencies, or hold government bodies accountable, illustrating how data opacity directly impacts the protection of vulnerable populations.
