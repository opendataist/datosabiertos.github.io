Censo 2025 Postular: Consulta si aún hay vacantes para trabajar en agosto

The article explains that the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) has completed its recruitment for the 2025 National Census, with vacancies closing in July to prepare for the fieldwork phase beginning in August. This large-scale statistical operation is essential for generating accurate demographic, economic, and social data about the country, ensuring that the resulting information is reliable and comprehensive for future public policy planning. Although the main hiring period has ended, the initiative serves as a crucial entry point for the youth workforce, particularly addressing the needs of young people who are neither studying nor working. By offering temporary positions with specific technical requirements, the census provides accessible employment opportunities that help integrate this demographic into the labor market and offer valuable experience in the public sector without requiring extensive prior professional experience. This case is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical human infrastructure needed to produce high-quality, trustworthy datasets. The quality of open data depends directly on the rigor of the data collection process; therefore, understanding how institutions manage personnel selection, training, and digital integration is essential for stakeholders who rely on this information. It underscores that robust open data ecosystems are not solely about technology, but also about the organized, transparent, and rigorous execution of large-scale data collection operations.
