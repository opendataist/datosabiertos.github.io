En etapa final proyecto que eleva protección y sanciones por infracciones a resguardo de datos personales | Diario Financiero

Chile is advancing in the creation of a robust legal framework for the protection of personal data, raising privacy standards by drawing inspiration from international regulations such as those of the European Union. The central objective is to recognize data as a property belonging to citizens, granting them comprehensive legal protection that was previously insufficient. The initiative proposes the establishment of a National Data Protection Agency with severe sanctioning powers, ensuring that companies comply with the new requirements. This entity will oversee the legitimate processing of information, imposing significant fines on those who violate the rights of data subjects, thereby obligating organizations to guarantee rigorous security and accountability standards. This development is relevant to open data because it sets a critical precedent on how to manage and protect information in the digital age. By clearly defining the boundaries between public use, commercial processing, and individual privacy, the law directly impacts the availability and ethical handling of government and corporate datasets, influencing future policies on transparency and access to information.
