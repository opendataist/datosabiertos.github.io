AWS has been signed up to help power the next big AI model

AWS and Hugging Face are partnering to democratize access to complex generative AI by providing specialized infrastructure. This collaboration aims to lower the barriers of entry, making large-scale model training more efficient and affordable for a broader developer community. By leveraging AWS’s Trainium chips, they address the high costs and technical expertise previously required to build foundational AI tools. This strategic shift moves beyond public-facing applications to support the backend development environment. It reflects a broader industry trend where major tech companies are investing heavily in the fundamental technologies that enable AI creation rather than just consumer products. The partnership is significant for open data as it encourages the use of open-source models like Bloom. By reducing costs and complexity, it fosters a more inclusive ecosystem where researchers and developers can openly share and build upon shared datasets and models. This supports the core principles of transparency and collaborative innovation essential to the open data movement.
