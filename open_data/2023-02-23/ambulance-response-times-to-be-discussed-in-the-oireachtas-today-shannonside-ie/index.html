Ambulance response times to be discussed in the Oireachtas today | Shannonside.ie

Ambulance response times in Ireland are dangerously high, with some emergencies taking over three hours to address. This alarming delay creates significant risks, potentially causing individuals to undertake unsafe self-transports rather than waiting for professional medical assistance. The data reveals a critical failure in emergency care infrastructure, highlighting severe systemic inefficiencies in patient transport logistics. Such delays directly endanger lives and erode public trust in the healthcare system’s ability to handle urgent situations. This case underscores the vital role of open data in exposing public service failures. By making response metrics transparent, citizens and policymakers can demand accountability and drive necessary improvements in emergency service delivery and resource allocation.
