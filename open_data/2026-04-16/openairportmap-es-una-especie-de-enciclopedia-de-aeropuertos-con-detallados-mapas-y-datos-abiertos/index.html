OpenAirportMap es una especie de enciclopedia de aeropuertos con detallados mapas y datos abiertos

The Open Airport Map emerges as an essential tool for the open data community, integrating detailed geospatial information about global airports with OpenStreetMap resources. This project demonstrates how the public availability of technical data enables the creation of complex and accessible visualizations, fostering transparency in critical infrastructure. The platform offers a wealth of details that go beyond basic location, showing terminals, numbered runways, navigation systems, and specific operational elements. This granularity allows users to interact deeply with airport infrastructure, resolving technical questions and revealing the hidden complexity behind everyday air operations. This initiative is relevant to open data because it highlights the social value of releasing precise spatial information. By democratizing access to data that was previously obscure or difficult to obtain, it empowers curious citizens and experts to analyze, verify, and understand complex environments, demonstrating that data transparency enhances both public education and safety through collaborative verification.
