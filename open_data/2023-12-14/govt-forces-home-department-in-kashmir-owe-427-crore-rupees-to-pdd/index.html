Govt forces, home department in Kashmir owe 427 crore rupees to PDD

A Right to Information inquiry reveals that Jammu and Kashmir’s government agencies and military forces have accumulated massive electricity debts, with the Home Department alone owing over two hundred crores. This highlights a systemic failure where state security establishments act as chronic defaulters, prioritizing security operations over basic utility obligations to local distribution corporations. Such non-payment undermines the transparency and accountability expected in public administration. When state actors ignore financial liabilities, it erodes trust in governance structures and demonstrates a lack of adherence to legal and ethical standards regarding public resource management. This case is relevant to open data because it shows how citizen-initiated information requests can expose hidden fiscal realities. By making these financial irregularities visible, it demonstrates the power of transparency mechanisms to hold powerful entities accountable and encourages a culture where government spending and debts are subject to public scrutiny.
