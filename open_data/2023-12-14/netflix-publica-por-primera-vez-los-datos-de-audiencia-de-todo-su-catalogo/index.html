Netflix publica por primera vez los datos de audiencia de todo su catálogo

Netflix publica por primera vez los datos de audiencia de todo su catálogo

Netflix has released its first detailed semi-annual report, revealing audience data for most of its global catalog. The document highlights “The Night Agent” as the most-watched title, followed by productions such as “La Reina del Sur” in the Spanish-speaking segment, offering a global view of content consumption based not on absolute user numbers but on hours viewed. This move responds to a long-standing transparency initiative launched by the platform to build trust among creators and production companies. Although recent Hollywood strikes demanded greater clarity regarding residual rights, the company states that this initiative primarily aims to dispel the mistrust inherent in the lack of information, establishing a healthier, more collaborative environment for all industry stakeholders. The relevance of this article for open data lies in Netflix’s commitment to making large volumes of structured data accessible to the general public. By publishing specific usage metrics, the platform not only promotes corporate transparency but also fuels independent research and analysis ecosystems. This example demonstrates how the widespread disclosure of operational data can enhance accountability in the entertainment industry, serving as a reference for other companies considering opening up their metrics.
