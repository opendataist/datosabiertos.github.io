Statistics at DHSC

The Department of Health and Social Care maintains a structured framework for publishing official health and social care statistics, ensuring strict adherence to the Code of Practice for Statistics. By organizing releases into clear categories like adult social care, public health, and clinical research, the department provides comprehensive, reliable data that supports transparency and accountability in healthcare governance. Priorities for statistical outputs are directly shaped by user feedback through joint consultations, ensuring that published data remains relevant and responsive to the needs of researchers and policymakers. This user-centric approach highlights the importance of accessibility and continuous improvement in statistical work programs, fostering trust in the quality and utility of official health metrics for diverse stakeholders. This structure is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how government bodies can balance regulatory compliance with practical usability. By centralizing access to diverse datasets and providing clear contact channels for inquiries, the DHSC facilitates broader data reuse and collaboration. This model encourages others to adopt similar standards of openness, thereby enhancing the overall ecosystem of shared public information and informed decision-making across the health sector.
