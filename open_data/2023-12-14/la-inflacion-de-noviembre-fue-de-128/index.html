La inflación de noviembre fue de 12,8%

The consumer price index reveals severe inflation, indicating a critical economic situation in which the costs of basic necessities and healthcare rise sharply. This trend highlights how quickly essential goods become less affordable for the general population, disrupting standard living conditions. Such drastic price changes underscore the urgency of transparent and accessible statistical data. Accurate metrics are vital for policymakers and citizens to understand the true cost of living and to demand appropriate responses. This information is crucial for open-data advocates because it demonstrates how public statistics directly impact societal well-being. Ensuring that this data is freely available and understandable enables greater accountability and more informed public debate on economic policies.
