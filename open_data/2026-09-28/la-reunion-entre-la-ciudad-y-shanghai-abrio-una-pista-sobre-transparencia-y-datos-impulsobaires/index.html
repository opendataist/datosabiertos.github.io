La reunión entre la Ciudad y Shanghái abrió una pista sobre transparencia y datos - ImpulsoBaires

La reunión entre la Ciudad y Shanghái abrió una pista sobre transparencia y datos - ImpulsoBaires

Buenos Aires and Shanghai exchanged institutional frameworks to strengthen public integrity and transparency. Buenos Aires highlighted its legal access-to-information laws and integrity mechanisms, such as conflict-of-interest simulators and anti-corruption channels, aiming to enhance the quality of state decision-making and public trust. Shanghai presented its municipal supervision commission’s focus on preventing corruption, notably introducing an exploratory use of artificial intelligence to monitor public works bidding processes. This technological approach offers a novel method for enhancing oversight in infrastructure projects, contrasting with Buenos Aires’ emphasis on structured institutional tools and training networks. The meeting underscores the relevance of open data by demonstrating how shared digital solutions, such as AI for transparency, and robust access-to-information protocols, foster international cooperation. It highlights that integrating technology with strong legal frameworks is essential for modern, accountable governance, showing how open data practices evolve through cross-border knowledge exchange to combat corruption effectively.
