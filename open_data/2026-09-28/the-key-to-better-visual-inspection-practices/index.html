The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

InQuest Science has developed the Identifier Software, a specialized tool designed to streamline visual inspection and defect management in parenteral and medical device manufacturing. By integrating a functional product life cycle approach, the software consolidates multi-sourced data from raw materials to finished products, offering a comprehensive view that exceeds the capabilities of broader Manufacturing Execution Systems. This specialization allows for precise evaluation of particulate matter and physical defects, ensuring robust quality assurance oversight throughout the manufacturing train. The system enhances regulatory compliance and operational efficiency by replacing manual, paper-based processes with a secure digital repository. It automates documentation for training, qualification, and defect tracking, thereby minimizing human error and supporting data integrity required by FDA standards. Features such as a master defect library, blind test kits, and automated audit trails facilitate realistic control limits and root cause analysis, enabling manufacturers to identify inconsistencies before they lead to batch recalls. This solution is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates how structured, digital defect management can standardize quality metrics across the pharmaceutical industry. By converting heterogeneous inspection data into a unified, accessible format, the software promotes transparency and interoperability. This approach not only aids regulatory adherence but also enables advanced analytics and trend monitoring, illustrating the value of shared, high-quality datasets in driving continuous process improvement and public health safety.
