Gobierno insta a representantes cruceños a revisar técnicamente y “las veces necesarias” actas del Censo 2024

Gobierno insta a representantes cruceños a revisar técnicamente y “las veces necesarias” actas del Censo 2024

The Bolivian government urges citizens to verify census data directly to ensure transparency and protect essential sectors from the impacts of protests. Authorities emphasize open access to records, allowing leaders to review results repeatedly to address concerns without disrupting the supply chain or workers' rights. This approach reflects a commitment to open data principles, whereby public institutions proactively share information to build trust and validate official statistics. By inviting scrutiny, the state demonstrates that census results are credible and subject to independent verification, reinforcing accountability in public administration. This case highlights the relevance of open data in managing social conflict. Transparency empowers stakeholders to validate information themselves, reducing misinformation and fostering dialogue. Open access to census data serves as a tool for democratic oversight, ensuring that statistical outputs remain accessible, verifiable, and trusted by the public they serve.
