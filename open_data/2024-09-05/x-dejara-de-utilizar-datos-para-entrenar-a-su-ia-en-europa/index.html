X dejará de utilizar datos para entrenar a su IA en Europa

X voluntarily withdrew its legal proceedings after agreeing to permanently comply with EU data regulations. This resolves violations related to the use of user data for training AI, bringing an end to multi-country complaints. The case highlights the critical role of national regulators in enforcing open data compliance standards for global technology giants.
