Un piso en Balears cuesta lo mismo que cuatro en Extremadura o tres en Murcia

Un piso en Balears cuesta lo mismo que cuatro en Extremadura o tres en Murcia

The housing crisis in the Balearic Islands, marked by exorbitant property prices and high levels of foreign ownership, creates an emergency that excludes local residents from the housing market. This disparity highlights how speculative dynamics can distort local markets, making homeownership nearly unattainable for residents. This situation underscores the critical need for transparency in real estate data. When detailed information on sales, pricing, and demographic ownership patterns is accessible, it enables a clearer understanding of market imbalances and the social impact of these economic trends. Open data is essential for identifying such systemic issues. By providing objective, accessible statistics, it empowers citizens and policymakers to recognize regional disparities, fostering accountability and enabling informed decisions regarding housing policy and market regulation.
