Más de un millón 300 mil empleos se crearon en julio

The article highlights a significant surge in informal employment in Mexico, revealing a stark structural imbalance in the labor market. This trend underscores the critical need for open data initiatives to accurately monitor economic health, as traditional metrics often obscure the true nature of job creation. Data transparency enables researchers and policymakers to effectively distinguish between the formal and informal sectors. By exposing the dominance of informal jobs, open data fosters informed debates on labor rights and economic stability, ensuring that statistical records reflect the lived reality of the workforce. Ultimately, relying on granular, accessible statistics is essential for developing targeted policies. Without clear, open datasets, the disparity between nominal job growth and actual employment quality remains hidden, hindering efforts to build a more equitable and sustainable economy for all citizens.
