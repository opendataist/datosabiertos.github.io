Reuters: Se disparan importaciones de crudo de España desde Venezuela - Noticiero Digital

Spain significantly increased its crude oil imports from Venezuela, surpassing last year’s totals due to exemptions granted by U.S. licenses. This shift highlights the complex interplay between the easing of geopolitical sanctions and international energy markets. The surge is driven by corporate debt settlements, in which Repsol accepted oil to settle obligations with PDVSA. Such private-sector engagements illustrate how non-state actors navigate regulatory frameworks to facilitate trade amid political tensions. This case is relevant to open data, as it underscores the necessity of transparent and accessible energy statistics. Reliable public records are crucial for monitoring compliance with sanctions and understanding the real-time impacts of diplomatic agreements on global trade flows.
