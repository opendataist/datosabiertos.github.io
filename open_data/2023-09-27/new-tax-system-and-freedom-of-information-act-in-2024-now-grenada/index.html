New tax system and Freedom of Information Act in 2024 | NOW Grenada

Grenada’s 2024 legislative agenda prioritizes transparency and institutional resilience through the introduction of a Freedom of Information Act and a Fiscal Resilience Act. This shift toward open governance is critical for open_data initiatives, as it establishes legal frameworks that may facilitate greater public access to government records and financial data, thereby enhancing accountability and citizen engagement. Concurrently, the government is digitizing essential services, including a new web-based tax system and online passport processing, while expanding free internet access. These digital infrastructure improvements reduce bureaucratic friction and create a more accessible platform for data interaction. By moving administrative processes online, the state creates the technical foundation necessary for efficient data collection and public service delivery. The relevance to open_data lies in the combination of new transparency laws and digitalization efforts. These measures signal a governmental commitment to modernizing operations, which often precedes the release of open datasets. As Grenada approaches its 50th anniversary, these structural changes aim to solidify a future where data accessibility supports both economic efficiency and democratic participation.
