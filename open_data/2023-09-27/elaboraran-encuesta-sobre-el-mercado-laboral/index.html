Elaborarán encuesta sobre el mercado laboral

The Central Bank and the National Institute of Statistics are collaborating to develop a continuous labor survey. This initiative aims to integrate employment data into macroeconomic models to enhance monetary policy analysis. The partnership builds on previous agreements involving the Ministry of Economy, ensuring alignment with international best practices. By combining institutional expertise, they seek to better understand economic and social labor trends. This effort is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how official statistics can improve transparency in public policy. Making labor market insights accessible enables researchers to analyze economic health, fostering evidence-based decision-making and greater accountability in monetary strategies.
