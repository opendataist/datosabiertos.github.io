Advierte el senador panista que nueva CURP conlleva finales electorales

The proposed CURP reform prioritizes electoral control over citizen privacy, raising serious concerns about the security of biometric data. By duplicating existing systems, it risks exposing personal information without adequate legal protections. This debate highlights the tension between state convenience and individual rights in national identification. It serves as a critical warning against consolidating sensitive data for political purposes. The article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the risks associated with centralized government data repositories. It underscores the necessity for transparent policies and robust privacy safeguards in any public data initiative to prevent misuse and ensure trust in civic institutions.
