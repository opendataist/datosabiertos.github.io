Economía mexicana avanza en julio

Mexico’s economy grew in July, driven primarily by the industrial sector’s performance, which offset contractions in many service activities. This suggests a resilient annual GDP trajectory, indicating overall stability despite mixed sectoral results. This data is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible economic indicators enable analysts to track national performance and inform policy decisions effectively.
