Business Register and Employment Survey 2022

The Northern Ireland Statistics and Research Agency’s latest Business Register and Employment Survey reveals a modest but positive recovery in the regional labor market, with total employment rising over the past year. This data underscores the resilience of key sectors, particularly services and construction, while highlighting the distinct economic structure of Northern Ireland compared to the wider UK, specifically its higher reliance on public sector employment. The narrative structure of this release emphasizes the dominance of the service industry in driving job growth, which accounted for the vast majority of new positions. Meanwhile, manufacturing and construction also contributed to the increase, indicating broad-based activity across major economic pillars. These trends provide critical context for understanding local economic health and inform how resources might be allocated to sustain momentum in these foundational industries. This article is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the utility of high-quality, granular statistical datasets for public policy and business support. By making such detailed sectoral and geographic data publicly available, statistical agencies enable researchers and policymakers to evaluate the impact of past interventions, such as pandemic support schemes, and to design more effective future strategies based on evidence rather than intuition.
