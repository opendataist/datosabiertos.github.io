Repunta inflación con 4.46% en primera quincena de diciembre: Inegi

Recent inflation data reveals a significant acceleration in consumer prices, surpassing all analyst forecasts and marking a notable economic shift. This unexpected surge highlights growing pressures within the services sector, which shows particular resistance to deflationary trends despite broader monetary efforts. These findings underscore the critical importance of open data in economic analysis. Transparent, timely statistical releases allow markets to rapidly adjust to volatility, exposing gaps between institutional expectations and real-world consumer experiences. Open access to such granular price indices empowers researchers and citizens to scrutinize policy effectiveness. When data is freely available, it fosters accountability and enables a deeper understanding of how macroeconomic indicators directly impact household budgets and social welfare.
