La nueva plataforma de streaming que busca desplazar a Netflix

La nueva plataforma de streaming que busca desplazar a Netflix

Kodi provides a free, open-source alternative to costly streaming services, enabling users to centralize and access local or streamed content across multiple devices. Its open-source nature encourages community-driven extensions and ensures legal flexibility, challenging proprietary subscription models. This is significant for open data, as it illustrates how transparent, accessible software empowers users to take control of their media consumption without vendor lock-in, fostering digital autonomy and data portability.
