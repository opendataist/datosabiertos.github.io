Reasignaciones en PPEF vendrán de recortes

Ricardo Monreal announces budget cuts to autonomous bodies and the judiciary to reallocate funds to universities, water infrastructure, and highways. These adjustments aim to offset previous reductions and finance new proposals from the executive branch, following the dissolution of several independent agencies. The relevance for open data lies in the direct impact on key institutions for transparency and access to information. Budget cuts and the dissolution of bodies such as the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) threaten the ability to ensure open, transparent, and accessible public data for citizens. This poses a significant risk to accountability and the integrity of government data. Reduced resources in these agencies hinder the oversight of public information, affecting civil society and the capacity for analysis based on open data. Transparency faces a structural institutional weakness.
