Buenos Aires has launched the development of its fourth Open Government Action Plan, reaffirming its longstanding commitment to the Open Government Partnership (OGP) since 2016. This process prioritizes a co-creation methodology, actively engaging civil society as well as the Executive, Legislative, and Judicial branches. The goal is to design joint commitments that address citizen priorities through more transparent, efficient, and evidence-based public management. This approach represents a significant evolution in the city’s transparency policies, shifting from a unilateral government implementation to a collaborative model that broadens consensus among diverse social actors. By incorporating diverse voices in the definition of public agendas, it strengthens accountability and provides concrete tools to reinforce local democracies. Continuous participation ensures that initiatives truly respond to the population’s needs. This initiative is crucial for the open data field, as transparency and collaboration are intrinsic pillars to ensure that public information is accessible, useful, and trust-building. Participation in the OGP not only validates these practices but also establishes international standards for governmental innovation, demonstrating that open access to information and citizen participation are essential for building an inclusive and responsible public administration.
