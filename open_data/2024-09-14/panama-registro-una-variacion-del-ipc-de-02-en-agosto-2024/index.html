Panamá registró una variación del IPC de -0,2% en agosto 2024

Panama’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) recorded a slight monthly contraction and marginal year-over-year inflation, highlighting price dynamics in key sectors such as transportation and food. This trend reflects recent macroeconomic stability, where declining costs for fuels and basic consumer goods exert temporary deflationary pressure, providing immediate relief to households’ purchasing power amid the usual volatility in energy and food prices. The release of data on these sectoral breakdowns enables transparent auditing of the real economy, which is vital for both citizens and the private sector. By making information disaggregated by categories—such as health, clothing, and household services—available, detailed analysis of market behaviors becomes more accessible. This transparency empowers researchers and citizens to understand how specific fluctuations affect different social strata, fostering informed public scrutiny of government economic policy. This report is crucial for the open data ecosystem because it demonstrates the importance of publishing official statistics in an accessible, structured, and comprehensible manner. The availability of these indicators not only meets accountability standards but also serves as input for developing technological and analytical solutions that enhance strategic planning. When institutions share high-quality data, civic innovation is catalyzed and trust in official sources is strengthened—both essential for an evidence-based society.
