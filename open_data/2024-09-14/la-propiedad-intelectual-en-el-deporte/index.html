La propiedad intelectual en el deporte

The article underscores the critical importance of registering and protecting intellectual property within the creative and advertising industries. Authors and agencies must understand that legal registration is not merely a bureaucratic formality, but a vital strategic tool for safeguarding their valuable assets, avoiding costly plagiarism disputes, and ensuring the predictability of investments. By formalizing their rights over their creations, creators shield their business models from the unauthorized appropriation of successful ideas by third parties. Furthermore, the economic potential of licensed formats is highlighted, extending beyond the mere use of a brand to include the production of physical vehicles and repeated execution at events. This model demonstrates how well-protected creativity can scale commercially, generating sustainable revenue through multiple activations and expansion into international markets. Licensing is thus presented as a value ecosystem in which legal protection serves as the foundation that enables the fair negotiation and distribution of rights. The relevance to the field of open data lies in the fundamental tension between intellectual property protection and information availability. While this text advocates for closure and strict restrictions to protect the creative business, the open data movement promotes transparency and free access. Understanding this dynamic is essential, as modern information management requires balancing the need to protect authorship and private innovation with the social benefits that arise when data and knowledge are openly shared.
