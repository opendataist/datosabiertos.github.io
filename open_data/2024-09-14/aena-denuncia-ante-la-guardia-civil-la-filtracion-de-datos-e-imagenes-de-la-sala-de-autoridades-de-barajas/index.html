Aena denuncia ante la Guardia Civil la filtración de datos e imágenes de la sala de autoridades de Barajas

AENA has reported the leak of images of privileged users at Barajas Airport, arguing that these recordings violate the National Program for Civil Aviation Security. The use of these rooms is strictly regulated to ensure the physical protection of high-ranking officials and their families, who access them due to extreme security needs. The regulations explicitly state that the president, his former successors, and their spouses use these facilities to avoid risks. Any unauthorized disclosure of personal data or images from these spaces compromises the integrity of the individuals involved and weakens the protective measures designed for critical infrastructure. This situation is relevant to open data because it illustrates the delicate balance between transparency and the protection of sensitive data. While public information should be accessible, the personal data of individuals in special security situations cannot be released without restrictions. The case highlights the need for clear legal frameworks that distinguish legitimate open data from private information requiring rigorous protection.
