Retrocede producción de empresas constructoras

Retrocede producción de empresas constructoras

The Mexican construction sector is experiencing a persistent downturn, with February production declining month-over-month and remaining significantly below pre-pandemic peaks. This sustained contraction underscores deep structural weaknesses in key sub-segments, particularly building construction, which holds the greatest economic weight. Providing such granular, timely data is crucial for open-data advocates. It illustrates how accessible government statistics enable analysts to monitor economic health beyond headline figures. Transparent data facilitates a nuanced understanding of sectoral vulnerabilities and supports more informed policy and investment decisions.
