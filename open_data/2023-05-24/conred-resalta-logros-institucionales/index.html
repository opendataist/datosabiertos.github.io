Conred resalta logros institucionales

Guatemala’s National Commission for Disaster Reduction (CONRED) has launched an open data platform, providing citizens with reliable information on disasters and geography. This initiative supports national transparency policies and ensures public access to critical safety data, fostering accountability in emergency management. The institution has also obtained two ISO certifications for quality management and occupational health and safety. These achievements demonstrate a commitment to operational excellence and resilience, strengthening the state’s capacity to prevent risks and protect communities during disasters. This is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how government agencies can effectively publish high-quality, actionable information. By making incident and geographic data publicly available, CONRED promotes transparency and empowers citizens to participate in disaster risk reduction through informed engagement.
