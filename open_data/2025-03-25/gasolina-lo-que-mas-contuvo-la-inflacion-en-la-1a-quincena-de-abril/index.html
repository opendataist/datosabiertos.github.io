Gasolina, lo que más contuvo la inflación en la 1ª quincena de abril

Mexico’s inflation rate for the first half of March reached 3.67%, driven primarily by higher beef and housing costs. Conversely, declining prices for unleaded gasoline, onions, and eggs helped mitigate these increases. This data underscores how open government statistics enable transparent tracking of fluctuations in essential commodities, empowering citizens to understand the real economic impacts.
