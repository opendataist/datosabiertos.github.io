Cuba invirtió en 2024 mucho más en turismo y hostelería que en educación y sanidad - Mundo - ABC Color

Cuba invirtió en 2024 mucho más en turismo y hostelería que en educación y sanidad - Mundo - ABC Color

The article highlights Cuba’s controversial economic strategy, which prioritizes massive investment in tourism infrastructure despite the sector struggling to recover from the pandemic and operating at critically low occupancy rates. This approach is criticized by experts for creating a severe imbalance, as the bulk of state funds flow into hotels rather than essential services like health, education, or agriculture, exacerbating existing systemic vulnerabilities. This disproportionate allocation occurs against a backdrop of deep economic crisis, characterized by scarcity, inflation, and partial dollarization. The decline in international visitor numbers, particularly from key markets, further undermines the viability of this tourism-centric model, suggesting that the current trajectory fails to address the broader structural weaknesses of the national economy. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent statistical reporting can reveal critical policy imbalances and societal needs. By making investment figures and tourism metrics publicly accessible, stakeholders can scrutinize government priorities, assess the effectiveness of economic strategies, and advocate for more balanced resource distribution across critical sectors like healthcare and education.
