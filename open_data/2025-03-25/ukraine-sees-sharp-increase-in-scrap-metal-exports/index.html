Ukraine sees sharp increase in scrap metal exports

Ukraine sees sharp increase in scrap metal exports

Ukrainian ferrous scrap exports volume rose slightly despite lower revenue, driven by a significant February surge. Most shipments headed to Poland, with minor volumes going to Bulgaria and Germany, alongside negligible imports. This data supports open data transparency by verifying trade statistics and highlighting export market shifts through official customs records.
