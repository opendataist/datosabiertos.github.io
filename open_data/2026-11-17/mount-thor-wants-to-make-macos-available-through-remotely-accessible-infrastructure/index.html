Mount Thor wants to make macOS available through remotely accessible infrastructure.

Mount Thor wants to make macOS available through remotely accessible infrastructure.

Mount Thor has secured significant funding to provide large-scale Apple computer infrastructure for artificial intelligence agents. This startup addresses a growing industry need by offering remotely accessible macOS environments, which are often essential for tasks requiring familiar desktop interfaces and specific Apple software compatibility. By leveraging Macs, the company enables AI agents to navigate applications and manage files in human-like ways, a capability that conventional cloud servers frequently lack. The relevance to open data lies in Mount Thor’s commitment to supporting open-source AI models. The company plans to establish a specialized laboratory for testing these models on Apple processors, potentially reducing computational costs for certain workloads. This initiative facilitates the development and evaluation of open-source agents that can interact with macOS ecosystems, ensuring that open AI tools can operate effectively within widely used, yet historically underutilized in data centers, computing environments. Investors view this infrastructure as critical for the next phase of autonomous agent adoption. By making Apple’s silicon and operating systems available at scale, Mount Thor bridges the gap between specialized hardware and the operational needs of AI developers. This approach not only lowers barriers for research teams and businesses but also highlights the importance of diverse hardware options in the broader open data and AI infrastructure landscape, ensuring compatibility and accessibility for a wider range of open-source projects.
