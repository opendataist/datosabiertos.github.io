Ministerio de Energía y Minas obtiene la máxima puntuación en transparencia estandarizada

Ministerio de Energía y Minas obtiene la máxima puntuación en transparencia estandarizada

The Ministry of Energy and Mines achieved a perfect score in transparency, demonstrating exceptional compliance with legal, financial, and operational disclosure requirements. This outcome validates the effectiveness of institutional mechanisms designed to ensure accountability, as the ministry made comprehensive data available regarding its structure, budget, procurement processes, and human resources. This achievement is particularly relevant to open data because it highlights how rigorous adherence to transparency standards directly facilitates the release of high-quality, accessible public information. By fully disclosing everything from strategic plans to detailed procurement records, the institution transforms abstract regulatory obligations into tangible, usable data assets for citizens and researchers. Ultimately, this case illustrates that robust open data practices are not merely administrative tasks but fundamental components of good governance. It serves as a benchmark for other entities, proving that systematic publication of diverse information categories builds public trust and enables meaningful civic engagement through the availability of reliable government data.
