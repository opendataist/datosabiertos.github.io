IRENA reports massive increase in renewable power capacity during 2024

IRENA reports massive increase in renewable power capacity during 2024

The 2025 IRENA report highlights a historic surge in global renewable energy capacity, underscoring that renewables are now economically viable and rapidly deployable. This exponential growth signals a decisive shift away from fossil fuels, demonstrating that clean energy transitions can simultaneously enhance energy security, stimulate economic opportunities, and reduce living costs. Despite these record-breaking expansions, the current trajectory remains insufficient to meet the urgent 2030 target of tripling global capacity. Significant geographic disparities persist, with Asian nations driving most gains while other regions lag far behind. Addressing this imbalance is critical, as equitable access to affordable renewable power is essential for a just and successful global energy transition. This data is highly relevant to the open_data community as it illustrates the tangible impact of transparent, high-quality information in guiding policy and investment. The call for clear, quantifiable targets in national climate pledges relies on the robust statistical frameworks that open data promotes. By making such comprehensive statistics accessible, stakeholders can better hold governments accountable and foster international collaboration to accelerate the global shift toward sustainable energy systems.
