Reconocen falta de “fortaleza y capacidad” para proteger datos de ciudadanos paraguayos - Tecnología - ABC Color

Reconocen falta de “fortaleza y capacidad” para proteger datos de ciudadanos paraguayos - Tecnología - ABC Color

A recent report alleges that millions of Paraguayan citizens’ personal data, including electoral records and vaccination information, are being sold on the dark web. Although authorities have not fully confirmed the scale or exact source of this breach, police investigations indicate that sensitive state databases, including those of the health ministry and the police themselves, are vulnerable. The incident highlights significant gaps in institutional cybersecurity, revealing that many government entities lack the technical resources necessary to adequately protect citizen information, despite existing basic recommendations. This exposure underscores the fragility of current data management practices within the state apparatus. This case is critical for the open data community, as it demonstrates the severe risks of inadequate data protection and the urgent need for robust regulatory frameworks. It emphasizes that without strict legal safeguards and secure handling protocols, even public sector data intended for transparency can become a source of widespread privacy violations, complicating ethical data-sharing efforts.
