El nuevo registro de la jornada laboral: sin papel, accesible en tiempo real e imposible de manipular

El nuevo registro de la jornada laboral: sin papel, accesible en tiempo real e imposible de manipular

The new labor regulations bring about a radical change in time management, requiring that working hours be recorded through certified and automated digital systems. This mandate aims to eliminate the subjectivity and data manipulation inherent in manual or rudimentary methods, establishing a standard of transparency where every hour worked, including breaks and overtime, is precisely tracked and verifiable. Technical precision is no longer optional but has become a fundamental legal requirement. The implementation of these digital solutions offers benefits that go beyond mere regulatory compliance, fostering trust between employees and employers by ensuring data integrity. By automating processes, organizations not only optimize administrative management and reduce operational costs but also prepare for modern work modalities such as remote work. This clarity in information enables real-time audits and ensures that records are accessible, integrable with other human resources systems, and protected against alterations. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates how modern regulation demands that labor data be objective, structured, and auditable, promoting a culture of transparency and accountability in the handling of sensitive information. The elimination of manipulable records aligns business management with principles of data integrity, demonstrating that digitalization is not merely an operational tool but an essential mechanism for ensuring the truthfulness and accessibility of critical information in the workplace.
