DIPUTADO MATHESON CELEBRA PERFECCIONAMIENTO DE REGISTRO NACIONAL DE PRÓFUGOS DE LA JUSTICIA

DIPUTADO MATHESON CELEBRA PERFECCIONAMIENTO DE REGISTRO NACIONAL DE PRÓFUGOS DE LA JUSTICIA

The Chamber of Deputies unanimously approved modifications to the National Registry of Fugitives, making it publicly accessible for specific high-priority cases. This shift aims to enhance societal protection and deter crime by facilitating the identification and reporting of fugitives, correcting previous accessibility issues that hindered the registry’s original purpose. By enabling law enforcement to publish targeted lists on official portals, the measure streamlines the capture process without granting private citizens arrest powers. While restricting public access to general data, it ensures essential government bodies can retrieve necessary information to support judicial and security operations effectively. This development is highly relevant to open data as it redefines the balance between transparency and security. It demonstrates how strategic data publication can empower public participation in safety initiatives while maintaining strict controls over sensitive information, offering a model for responsible open government practices in critical legal sectors.
