Las jubilaciones, pensiones y asignaciones aumentarán 2,40 por ciento en abril - Zona Norte Visión

Las jubilaciones, pensiones y asignaciones aumentarán 2,40 por ciento en abril - Zona Norte Visión

ANSES announces a pension increase based on the Consumer Price Index published by INDEC, ensuring that benefits are adjusted in line with inflation. This update underscores the critical role of official statistical data in shaping social welfare policies. The new figures highlight how transparent government statistics directly affect household incomes and social security calculations. Open data facilitates accountability in these adjustments. Access to accurate, publicly available indices enables citizens and analysts to verify the fairness of these measures and track economic trends. This transparency is essential for a robust open data ecosystem, empowering communities to understand and effectively scrutinize policy implementation.
