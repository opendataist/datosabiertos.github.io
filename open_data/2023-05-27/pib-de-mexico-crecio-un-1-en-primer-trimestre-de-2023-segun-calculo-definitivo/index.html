PIB de México creció un 1% en primer trimestre de 2023, según cálculo definitivo

Mexico’s GDP grew by one percent in the first quarter of 2023, primarily driven by gains in the services and industrial sectors. Although slightly below initial estimates, this expansion reflects resilience in the region’s second-largest economy. Institutions such as the IMF and ECLAC have upgraded their growth forecasts for the year, signaling improved economic confidence. This adjustment contrasts with the earlier sluggish post-pandemic recovery and highlights the sectoral shifts underpinning current performance. This data is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the value of transparent, accessible government statistics. Publicly released official figures allow international organizations and researchers to independently verify economic trends, fostering accountability and informed global policy analysis.
