Oppong-Nkrumah demands inclusion of RTI in performance agreementswith heads of public institutions - The Chronicle News Online

Integrating Right to Information compliance into performance agreements transforms transparency from a voluntary ideal into a measurable administrative duty. By holding public institution leaders accountable for timely responses and proactive disclosure, this policy shift ensures that the legal framework for information access is effectively enforced rather than merely existing on paper. This structural change directly supports open data initiatives by mandating systematic information dissemination and monitoring. When access to government data becomes a core performance metric, institutions are compelled to maintain accurate, accessible, and up-to-date records, thereby fostering a culture of operational openness that citizens can rely upon for informed engagement. Ultimately, this move empowers citizens by guaranteeing their right to access public information, strengthening democratic accountability. It signals a broader commitment to open governance, where data availability is not incidental but institutionalized, ensuring that public resources and decisions remain visible and understandable to the communities they serve.
