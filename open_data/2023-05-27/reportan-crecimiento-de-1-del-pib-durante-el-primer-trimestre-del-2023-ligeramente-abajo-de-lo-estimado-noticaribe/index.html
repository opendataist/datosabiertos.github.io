Reportan crecimiento de 1% del PIB durante el primer trimestre del 2023, ligeramente abajo de lo estimado - Noticaribe

Mexico’s economy demonstrates resilient growth, driven by robust tertiary-sector consumption and sustained U.S. demand for secondary-sector goods. This six-quarter expansion underscores economic stability, even as primary-sector activities contracted. The data reflect enhanced dynamism in the services and industrial sectors, offsetting declines in agriculture and marking the strongest performance since 2020. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on INEGI’s transparent publication of revised macroeconomic indicators. Such accessible, high-quality official statistics empower researchers and developers to build reliable economic models and promote evidence-based decision-making through data transparency.
