Pendiente el INAI

The Senate’s failure to appoint commissioners for the INAI has paralyzed the institute, preventing it from resolving citizen appeals for public information and data protection. This institutional blockage directly undermines transparency and accountability, which are key pillars of democratic governance and open data rights. Legal alternatives remain uncertain, raising the risk that fundamental rights will continue to be violated. The situation highlights the fragility of autonomous bodies when political consensus fails, threatening the integrity of institutions designed to safeguard civic liberties against government overreach. This case is crucial for open data because the INAI ensures the enforceability of transparency laws. Without a functioning commission, citizens cannot effectively claim their right to access information, weakening the entire ecosystem of open government and eroding public trust in democratic institutions.
