La firma de hipotecas cae en marzo casi un 16 % interanual

Mortgage lending volumes in Spain fell sharply on a year-on-year basis, reflecting a clear shift toward fixed interest rates as borrowers prioritized stability amid economic uncertainty. This trend underscores how market dynamics directly affect the transparency and accessibility of financial data for consumers. Grasping these shifts is crucial for open data initiatives, as they provide essential insights into the health of the housing market. Such data empowers citizens and researchers to analyze economic trends and advocate for more equitable financial policies.
