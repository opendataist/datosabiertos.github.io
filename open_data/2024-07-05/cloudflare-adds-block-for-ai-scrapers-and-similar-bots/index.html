Cloudflare Adds Block For AI Scrapers And Similar Bots

Cloudflare is expanding its bot-blocking capabilities to restrict automated traffic from large language model developers. This shift addresses the significant bandwidth drain caused by data scraping, which effectively forces website owners to subsidize AI model training without consent or compensation. By targeting specific behavioral patterns and user agent strings, providers aim to filter out these requests before they consume resources. This approach reflects a broader industry trend where site administrators actively seek to protect their infrastructure from the increasing volume of artificial traffic generated for generative AI purposes. This development is crucial for open data advocates, as it highlights the tension between public information availability and commercial AI extraction. It underscores the urgent need for sustainable, ethical data sourcing frameworks that respect source infrastructure while enabling legitimate research and innovation.
