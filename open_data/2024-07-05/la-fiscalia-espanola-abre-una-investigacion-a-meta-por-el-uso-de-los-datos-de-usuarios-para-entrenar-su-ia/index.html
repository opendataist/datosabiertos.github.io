La Fiscalía española abre una investigación a Meta por el uso de los datos de usuarios para entrenar su IA

The Spanish Prosecutor’s Office has launched a preliminary investigation into Meta to determine whether the use of data to train its generative AI violates users’ data protection rights. This action aims to safeguard the fundamental right to privacy in the face of the massive use of personal information in technological development. Meta has already temporarily suspended this practice in the European Union due to the complaints received, reflecting growing regulatory scrutiny. At the same time, other authorities, such as Brazil’s, are demanding an immediate halt to these practices under threat of sanctions, highlighting a global tension between technological innovation and digital rights. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of informed consent and ethical governance in the handling of information. It illustrates how legal frameworks must balance the utility of data for AI with the protection of privacy, setting precedents for future open data initiatives that respect individual rights.
