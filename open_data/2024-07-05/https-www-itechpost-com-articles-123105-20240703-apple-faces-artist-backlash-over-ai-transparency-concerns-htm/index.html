ES EN

Apple faces backlash from creators regarding opaque data practices in its new AI tool, highlighting ethical concerns common in the tech industry. This lack of transparency mirrors issues seen in other major platforms that harvest creative works without clear consent. The controversy underscores critical open data challenges, specifically the absence of clear provenance and licensing for training materials. Without open methodologies, developers risk perpetuating copyright violations and undermining trust within the creative community. Ultimately, this situation emphasizes the urgent need for ethical AI standards that respect intellectual property. Open data advocates must demand greater transparency and user control to ensure technology evolves fairly while protecting creators' rights and contributions.
