BMC has proposed land allocation for Dharavi Redevelopment Project, reveals RTI

BMC has proposed land allocation for Dharavi Redevelopment Project, reveals RTI

The article exposes critical discrepancies in the Dharavi Redevelopment Project’s land allocation, revealing that significant parcels in Mulund have been earmarked for rehabilitation despite previous claims that none were available. This transparency, achieved through a Right to Information response, highlights how official records contradict political narratives, suggesting that misinformation was deliberately spread during election campaigns. The disclosure forces a re-examination of the project’s foundational legitimacy and the actual resources committed to housing displaced residents. A major concern raised is the potential misuse of public land, with allegations pointing toward a massive land scam involving transfers to private entities like the Adani Group. The absence of a finalized master plan means the exact land requirements and eligible beneficiaries remain undefined, creating a risk that public property is being alienated without proper legal or social justification. This lack of clarity undermines the accountability necessary for large-scale urban renewal initiatives. This case is vital to open data because it demonstrates how public records can serve as a check against corporate and governmental overreach. It underscores the necessity of accessible, verifiable information to protect citizen rights and ensure that redevelopment projects prioritize rehabilitation over commercial exploitation. By making these land transactions visible, open data facilitates public scrutiny and demands stricter adherence to statutory procedures for property transfers.
