Qué es el Big Data y para qué sirve: Diccionario TN Tecno

Qué es el Big Data y para qué sirve: Diccionario TN Tecno

Big Data represents a transformative paradigm based on the processing of massive and heterogeneous information sets using advanced digital tools. Its fundamental relevance lies in the ability to extract value, truthfulness, and utility from data that exceed the capabilities of traditional analysis, enabling not only storage but also the generation of actionable insights in real time to optimize processes and create new business opportunities. The application of these techniques goes beyond mere information volume, focusing on evidence-based strategic decision-making and service innovation. By identifying hidden patterns and trends, organizations across diverse sectors can personalize experiences, prevent risks, and accelerate scientific research, demonstrating that efficient management of data complexity is a key driver of competitiveness and social progress. This concept is crucial for the open data movement, as advanced analysis depends on the availability of diverse, high-quality datasets to feed predictive models. Promoting the openness and accessibility of public information enhances this technology, enabling greater transparency and the development of more efficient public solutions, while leveraging the democratic potential of massive information for the common good.
