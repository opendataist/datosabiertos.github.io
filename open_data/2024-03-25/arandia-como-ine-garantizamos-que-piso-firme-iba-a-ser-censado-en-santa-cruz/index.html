Arandia: como INE garantizamos que Piso Firme iba a ser censado en Santa Cruz

The article highlights the successful inclusion of the Piso Firme community in the official census, respecting indigenous autonomy and ensuring their population is counted within San Ignacio de Velasco. This outcome underscores the importance of collaborative governance between state institutions and local authorities to achieve accurate demographic representation. Furthermore, the narrative emphasizes the need for continued oversight of resource distribution to address community needs. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates how inclusive census practices ensure marginalized groups are visible in public statistics. Accurate data collection is essential for equitable policy-making and resource allocation, demonstrating that open and representative data systems must actively engage diverse populations to reflect true societal realities.
