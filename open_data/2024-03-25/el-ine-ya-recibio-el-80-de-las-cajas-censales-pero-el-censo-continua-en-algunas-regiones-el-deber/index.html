El INE ya recibió el 80% de las cajas censales, pero el censo continúa en algunas regiones | El Deber

The article emphasizes that Bolivia’s census is proceeding correctly despite its geographic complexity and size, directly refuting claims of irregularities or voter transport. The core conclusion is that the operational success stems from respecting the logistical realities of remote areas, where access is difficult and requires additional time, ensuring data integrity rather than manipulation. Regarding open data implications, this case highlights the critical importance of transparency in large-scale public information collection. When authorities proactively address skepticism with clear, accessible explanations about operational challenges, they build public trust. This trust is foundational for the acceptance and effective use of government statistics, as it assures citizens and analysts that the data reflects reality without external interference. Furthermore, the high participation of women and youth underscores the role of civic engagement in producing reliable datasets. For open data initiatives, demonstrating inclusive and transparent processes encourages broader societal ownership of public information. When people understand the effort and protocols behind data generation, they are more likely to utilize these resources for civic accountability and informed decision-making, reinforcing the value of open government data ecosystems.
