INE informa que más del 80% de las cajas ha retornado con documentación censal

The national statistical institute reports that the majority of census forms have been returned, confirming the successful collection of demographic data. Officials defend the organization of the operation against political criticism, emphasizing that improved digital cartography and broad institutional collaboration ensured accurate coverage in hard-to-reach areas. By highlighting the technical rigor and cost-efficiency compared to previous editions, the data collection process is presented as a modernized and transparent effort. This approach minimizes administrative discrepancies and maximizes the reliability of the resulting statistics, demonstrating a mature capacity for independent data management. This achievement is crucial for open data, as it establishes a high-quality, accessible baseline for population and housing metrics. Transparent, technically sound censuses enable researchers, developers, and policymakers to build reliable applications and make informed decisions based on verified public information rather than disputed figures.
