Oman's consumer price index shows stability with varied trends in food, non-alcoholic beverages

Oman’s February 2024 consumer price index reveals notable stability, with most sectors seeing price declines. While food and beverage costs rose slightly, driven by staples like milk and fruit, transportation and education prices dropped. Regional disparities emerge, as Muscat saw minimal inflation decrease while North Al Sharqiyah experienced modest price increases. These variations highlight how local economic dynamics influence consumer costs across different governorates. This data is vital for open data initiatives, offering granular insights into economic trends. Transparent access to such indices supports research, policy-making, and public understanding of cost-of-living changes, fostering informed decision-making in economic analysis.
