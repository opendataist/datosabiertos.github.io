Homepage | ECB Data Portal

The ECB Data Portal enhances accessibility by structuring data into four distinct layers, facilitating more efficient retrieval for researchers and developers. This hierarchical organization simplifies navigation through complex economic indicators, ensuring users quickly locate precise datasets. Consequently, this improvement supports open data principles by making central bank statistics more transparent and easier to integrate into external applications.
