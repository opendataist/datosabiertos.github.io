El INE agradece el apoyo de la población en el Censo y el Gobierno lo califica como una jornada exitosa

Bolivia successfully carried out the opening day of its 2024 National Population and Housing Census, marking the first comprehensive data collection in twelve years. The event was widely praised by government officials and the National Institute of Statistics for the high level of civic participation and voluntary support from the population. This massive engagement highlights a strong societal commitment to national development and collective progress through inclusive civic duties. The updated questionnaire captures more detailed household and personal characteristics than previous cycles, aiming to generate more granular data for future analysis. By expanding the scope of inquiry, the census seeks to reflect the complex reality of Bolivian communities with greater accuracy. This effort underscores the transition toward a more modern, transparent, and participatory statistical system that prioritizes the inclusion of diverse demographic realities. This process is crucial for open data initiatives as it establishes a foundational, high-quality dataset for public use. The resulting information will directly inform evidence-based public policy implementation, ensuring resources are allocated effectively to improve citizens' lives. Consequently, the census serves not just as a demographic exercise but as a vital tool for transparency, accountability, and data-driven governance in the public sector.
