Concentran siete estados más del 50% de homicidios en 2022

National homicide statistics reveal a concentrated geographic distribution of violence, highlighting significant disparities between high-violence regions and those with markedly lower incident rates. This data underscores how specific states bear a disproportionate burden of lethal crime compared to others, reflecting deep regional inequalities in public security infrastructure. The primary drivers of these fatalities are violent assaults, particularly those involving firearms and blunt or sharp instruments. Understanding these patterns is crucial for identifying the specific methods perpetrators use, which directly informs the development of targeted public safety strategies and resource allocation for law enforcement agencies. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of integrating administrative records from civil registries, forensic services, and public ministries. By making this aggregated evidence accessible, researchers and policymakers can enhance transparency, foster independent analysis of crime trends, and support evidence-based decisions to improve citizen safety and institutional accountability.
