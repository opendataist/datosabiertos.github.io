La inflación en EE.UU. sigue disminuyendo: cae al 4% en mayo, su nivel más bajo desde 2021

The article highlights the steady decline in U.S. inflation, which reached an annual rate of 4% in May, marking the lowest level since early 2021. This trend, driven by falling energy prices and the cumulative impact of interest rate hikes, signals a cooling economy. These developments are significant for open data, as they underscore the importance of accessible, high-quality macroeconomic indicators in shaping monetary policy. The Federal Reserve’s upcoming decision on interest rates depends directly on these transparent economic metrics, including core inflation and unemployment rates. By relying on publicly available data from agencies such as the Bureau of Labor Statistics, policymakers ensure that their actions are based on empirical evidence rather than speculation. This transparency builds trust in financial institutions and allows markets to adjust predictably using real-time information. Ultimately, this case illustrates how open data serves as the foundation for effective governance and economic stability. When reliable statistical information is freely accessible, it enables informed decision-making that has a global impact on markets. The correlation between published data and central bank actions highlights the essential role of data openness in maintaining accountability and guiding economic strategy during periods of volatility.
