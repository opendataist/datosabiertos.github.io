Report Enemalta Didn’t Want You To See Proves They Knowingly Overpaid For Montenegro Windfarm Deal

An audit revealed severe negligence and opacity in a state-backed acquisition, highlighting systemic failures in corporate governance and transparency. This case underscores the critical need for open data to expose misconduct, as authorities initially denied public access to vital records. Civil society intervention forced the release of the report, demonstrating how accessible information drives accountability. The scandal illustrates that without robust open data frameworks, financial irregularities can be concealed from public scrutiny, undermining democratic oversight. Ultimately, this incident reinforces the relevance of open data as a safeguard against corruption. It shows that mandatory disclosure and public access to government contracts are essential for ensuring justice and preventing the misuse of taxpayer funds.
