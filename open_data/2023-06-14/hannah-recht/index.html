Health insurers violate disability laws by issuing unreadable medical bills to blind Americans, creating significant access barriers. This systemic failure prevents patients from understanding their financial obligations, leading to unnecessary debt collection actions. These findings highlight critical accessibility failures in healthcare data that directly impact equity and transparency in the open data ecosystem.
