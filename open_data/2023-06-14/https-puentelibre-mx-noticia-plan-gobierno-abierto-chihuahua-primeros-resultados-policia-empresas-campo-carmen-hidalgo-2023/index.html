Open Data
ES EN

Chihuahua has launched its first Open Government Action Plan, redefining governance through the co-creation of public policies with citizens and private sector actors. This initiative aims to transform the relationship between authorities and society by establishing new committees and strategic alliances that empower the community in decision-making and the design of local programs. The priority axes include violence prevention through informational platforms on security, support for entrepreneurs through registration and training, and attention to rural areas via multidisciplinary teams. These actions reflect a direct response to needs identified among the citizenry, including those less intuitive such as rural development, demonstrating an adaptive capacity based on real data from the population. This case is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparency and access to information facilitate effective citizen participation. By sharing data on programs and needs, the government enables civil society to collaborate actively. This validates that open government is not merely a theory, but an operational practice that improves the effectiveness of public policies through continuous collaboration and accountability.
