Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The bill proposed by the Civic Coalition aims to establish an Open Data Portal for Imports, radically transforming transparency in Argentina’s import system. By requiring the publication of applications, products, amounts, and statuses in open and digital formats, the measure seeks to eliminate the administrative discretion that characterizes the current system. This ensures that any citizen can access public information, significantly reducing opportunities for corrupt practices and guaranteeing the traceability of commercial operations. The initiative responds to criticism that successive changes to the import systems have served to impose unreasonable restrictions and protect foreign exchange reserves, rather than to generate predictability. Lawmakers argue that the lack of clear parameters and failure to meet deadlines increase the risk of abuse of power. Therefore, the design of the new portal includes strict procedural safeguards, such as fixed deadlines and automatic approval in cases of administrative silence, aiming to neutralize the arbitrary discretion of public officials. This approach is fundamental to the open data movement, as it illustrates how the proactive publication of government information is an effective tool in the fight against corruption. By meeting interoperability and open access standards, as recommended by international organizations, the initiative demonstrates that digital transparency is not merely a technical issue, but an indispensable requirement for institutional integrity. It fosters accountability and empowers civil society to oversee economic policies, thereby validating the core principles of open governance.
