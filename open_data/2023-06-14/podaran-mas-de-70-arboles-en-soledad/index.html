Podarán más de 70 árboles en Soledad

Municipal authorities and the Federal Electricity Commission (CFE) are coordinating efforts to prune more than seventy trees that dangerously encroach upon power lines. This preventive maintenance aims to mitigate risks associated with heavy rains and strong winds, addressing recent incidents such as fallen trees and power outages to ensure public safety and the integrity of infrastructure. Ecology officials also identify diseased trees or those causing damage to urban infrastructure, although the primary focus remains on clearing vegetation from electrical cables. By maintaining an accurate census of these hazards, the city proactively reduces the likelihood of accidents and service disruptions during severe weather events. This initiative is relevant to open data because it relies on a detailed municipal census of specific hazard locations. Publishing this spatial data would enable citizens to visualize safety risks, track maintenance progress, and hold authorities accountable for urban management and infrastructure resilience.
