Los precios de los alimentos suben el 12 % en mayo, casi un punto menos que en abril - EFEAgro

Los precios de los alimentos suben el 12 % en mayo, casi un punto menos que en abril - EFEAgro

The article highlights specific inflation rates for food and beverages, noting a 12 percent annual increase driven by fruits and meat, while other items like dairy and fish saw price decreases. Overall consumer price inflation also moderated, reflecting broader economic trends in Spain during May. This data is relevant to open_data because government statistical institutes like the INE provide the foundational datasets used by researchers and analysts. Accessing and interpreting these public indices allows for transparent monitoring of economic indicators, fostering accountability and enabling data-driven policy decisions based on verifiable, official statistics.
