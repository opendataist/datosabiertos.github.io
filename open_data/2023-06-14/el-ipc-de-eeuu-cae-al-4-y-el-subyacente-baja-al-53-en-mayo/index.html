El IPC de EEUU cae al 4% y el subyacente baja al 5,3% en mayo

The U.S. Consumer Price Index showed a significant slowdown in May, coming in slightly below market expectations. This trend indicates that inflation is cooling consistently, with a notable decline in energy prices and moderation in other key sectors, suggesting a more stable and predictable economic outlook for households and businesses. Experts agree that this decline provides the Federal Reserve with the necessary room to maintain a measured monetary policy. With inflationary pressure easing, regulators have less need to impose immediate interest rate hikes, allowing the central bank to adopt a patient stance while assessing the future trajectory of employment and prices without risking overheating the economy. This news is relevant to the *open_data* domain because it demonstrates the vitality and direct impact of free public statistics on global decision-making. Transparent access to these economic data enables analysts, investors, and citizens to understand and respond to macroeconomic realities, fostering a more informed society grounded in verifiable, real-time evidence.
