Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has enhanced its Ibrahim Index Data Portal, offering an interactive platform for exploring comprehensive governance metrics across all 54 African nations. This updated tool allows users to analyze ten-year trends in political, social, and economic public goods through detailed country profiles and comparative measure pages. By enabling stakeholders to drill down from category scores to specific sub-indicators, the platform facilitates deeper comparative analysis against continental averages. This accessibility supports evidence-based dialogue, helping researchers and citizens identify governance strengths and deteriorations to drive accountability and policy improvements. This resource is vital to open data initiatives as it democratizes access to high-quality, standardized governance indicators. By making complex benchmarking data intuitive and user-friendly, it empowers civil society and journalists to monitor public service delivery effectively, fostering greater transparency and informed civic engagement in African democracies.
