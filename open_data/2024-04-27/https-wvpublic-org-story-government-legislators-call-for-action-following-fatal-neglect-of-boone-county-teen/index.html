The tragic death of a severely malnourished homeschooled teen highlights critical gaps in state oversight for isolated children. Despite legislative efforts to establish a review team for child welfare incidents, key proposals failed to advance, leaving vulnerable youth at risk of undetected abuse. This case underscores the urgent need for transparent monitoring systems to protect children outside traditional schooling. It serves as a stark reminder that current safety nets are insufficient without rigorous, independent scrutiny of home education environments. For open data, this incident illustrates the necessity of accessible, aggregated records on child welfare cases. Publicly available data on homeschooling oversight and incident reviews would empower advocates and policymakers to identify systemic failures and drive meaningful legislative reform.
