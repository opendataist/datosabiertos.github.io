Caen 91% utilidades de Pemex en primer trimestre de 2024; es el inicio más débil de lo últimos tres años - Noticaribe

Petróleos Mexicanos reported a significant decline in first-quarter profits compared to previous years, yet it marked a return to profitability after years of losses under the prior administration. Management emphasizes that operational decisions and federal coordination have strengthened the company’s financial health, projecting a more robust infrastructure and a positive outlook as the current term concludes. Despite these improvements, the state-owned enterprise maintains a substantial debt burden and owes significant amounts to suppliers and contractors. To address liquidity challenges, the government has agreed to transfer funds throughout the year, aligning resource distribution with debt maturity profiles. This fiscal support aims to stabilize operations and accelerate payments to the private sector, recognizing the broader economic impact on regional markets. This situation highlights critical challenges related to open data for state-owned enterprises and public transparency. Access to detailed, real-time financial data is essential for monitoring government spending, debt management, and accountability. Enhancing data openness allows citizens and analysts to verify official claims, assess fiscal sustainability, and ensure that public resources are managed efficiently, thereby fostering trust in institutional governance and economic policy implementation.
