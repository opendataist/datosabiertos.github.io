Propiedad Intelectual y Inteligencia Artificial: Impulsando los ODS en México

Artificial intelligence emerges as a revolutionary tool to drive innovation and achieve the Sustainable Development Goals, acting as a key catalyst in sectors such as health, energy, and urban management. By analyzing large volumes of data, AI enables the identification of patterns and the development of solutions that, while posing challenges regarding rights attribution, generate methods and technologies eligible for protection under existing legal frameworks. It is essential to highlight that intellectual property remains a fundamental pillar for motivating technological development, even when AI operates as a supporting tool. Companies are strategically leveraging these analytical results to improve operational efficiency and gain competitive advantages, demonstrating that the practical value of AI transcends legal uncertainties surrounding machine authorship. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how access to and analysis of open or complex data, enhanced by AI, generate protectable innovations and direct social benefits. It fosters the understanding that, beyond the debate on machine authorship, the strategic exploitation of information is crucial for sustainable development and economic competitiveness, thereby integrating data transparency with the protection of innovation.
