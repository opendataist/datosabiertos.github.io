Arandia: Nuestro principal compromiso es entregar los resultados del Censo 2024

The Bolivian National Institute of Statistics (INE) has identified the release of the 2024 Census results as its primary institutional commitment for the current year. This objective follows the successful execution of census operations across urban and dispersed areas, marking the transition from field data collection to the critical post-census phase. The institution acknowledges the vital role of the population and volunteers, whose participation ensured comprehensive data coverage throughout the country. Beyond immediate census deliverables, the INE is prioritizing the modernization of macroeconomic statistics in alignment with national account systems and planning for the forthcoming Agricultural Census. Additionally, the institute is advancing the publication of indicators from the 2023 Demographic and Health Survey. These initiatives reflect a strategic effort to enhance the quality and relevance of statistical services, ensuring that data infrastructure supports broader national development goals and policy-making needs. This article is highly relevant to open data advocates because it highlights the transition from raw data collection to the public release of processed information. The commitment to publishing population data at the municipal level by September demonstrates a tangible step toward transparency and data accessibility. For developers and civil society, timely access to such granular, official statistics is essential for creating innovative applications, monitoring public policy effectiveness, and fostering informed civic engagement based on reliable, publicly available evidence.
