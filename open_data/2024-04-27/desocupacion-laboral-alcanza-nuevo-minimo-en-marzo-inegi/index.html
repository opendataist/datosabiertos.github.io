Desocupación laboral alcanza nuevo mínimo en marzo: INEGI

Mexico’s March labor data reveal a historic low in unemployment, driven by substantial formal job creation that contrasts with recent losses in social security coverage. This shift suggests that employment growth is extending beyond traditional sectors into federal and state government roles, highlighting the complexity of aggregating official labor statistics. The analysis underscores a persistent structural imbalance, as informal employment remains dominant despite gains in industry and manufacturing. This duality illustrates the challenge of capturing the full economic picture, as disparate data sources such as INEGI and IMSS offer conflicting views on the stability of the formal sector, necessitating careful harmonization of open datasets. Understanding these nuances is vital for open data initiatives focused on economic transparency. By integrating diverse labor indicators, researchers can better assess workforce formalization and sectoral shifts. This approach ensures that public statistics accurately reflect the dynamic interplay between the formal and informal economies, supporting evidence-based policy and deeper societal insights into labor market trends.
