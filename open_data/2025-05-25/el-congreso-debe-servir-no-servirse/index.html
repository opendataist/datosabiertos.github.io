El Congreso debe servir, no servirse

El Congreso debe servir, no servirse

The 2024 State Legislative Powers Census reveals a critical disconnect between legislative actions and societal needs, particularly in Chihuahua. The data highlights a significant absence of civic participation spaces for gender equality and youth, indicating that marginalized voices are systematically excluded from local policy agendas. This imbalance suggests that legislative priorities favor undefined interests over fundamental social rights. Furthermore, the institution faces scrutiny regarding transparency and accountability. Despite numerous legal proposals, there is limited evidence that these measures genuinely resolve public problems, raising doubts about the true impact of their work. Concurrently, a high volume of investigations into misconduct underscores persistent governance issues that erode public trust in these representative bodies. This analysis is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent metrics can expose democratic deficits. By making such granular information accessible, citizens can demand greater accountability, ensuring that legislative resources and efforts actually serve the populace rather than entrenched political interests.
