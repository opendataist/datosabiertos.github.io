Estonia-based Blackwall raises €45 million Series B to protect SMBs from malicious online traffic | TechCrunch

Estonia-based Blackwall raises €45 million Series B to protect SMBs from malicious online traffic | TechCrunch

The rise of AI-driven bot traffic poses existential threats to small businesses, creating an urgent need for accessible cybersecurity solutions. Large enterprises can often absorb these costs, but small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) lack the resources to defend themselves against malicious activities like DDoS attacks and ad fraud. This disparity highlights a critical vulnerability in the digital economy, where smaller players are disproportionately at risk from automated cyber threats that can quickly dismantle their operations. Blackwall addresses this gap by providing an automated, easy-to-use defense platform tailored specifically for the SMB market. Rather than selling directly to end-users, the company employs a channel model, partnering with hosting and managed service providers to integrate their technology seamlessly. This strategy allows intermediaries to enhance their service offerings and margins while protecting their clients from malicious traffic, ensuring that security measures remain invisible to the average user yet effective against complex threats. This approach is relevant to open data ecosystems because secure, trusted infrastructure is fundamental for reliable data exchange. As SMBs increasingly rely on digital platforms for commerce and communication, protecting them from bot-driven distortions ensures the integrity of online interactions and data flows. By enabling smaller entities to participate securely in the digital economy, solutions like Blackwall help maintain a healthier, more open, and resilient online environment where data can be exchanged without the interference of malicious automation.
