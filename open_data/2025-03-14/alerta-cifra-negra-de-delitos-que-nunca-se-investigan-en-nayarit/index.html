Alerta cifra negra de delitos que nunca se investigan en Nayarit

Alerta cifra negra de delitos que nunca se investigan en Nayarit

Nayarit presents a paradox: official homicide rates are low, yet significant crime goes unreported due to institutional hostility and a perceived climate of impunity. This “black figure” of undetected offenses undermines the accuracy of public security data. The disconnect between statistical records and citizens’ lived reality underscores the critical need to address barriers to reporting. When authorities fail to act, citizens lose faith in the justice system and choose silence over formal complaints. This case is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the limitations of relying solely on official government statistics. To achieve genuine transparency, data ecosystems must incorporate victimization surveys that reveal the hidden scope of crime, often obscured by institutional neglect.
