Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

This article highlights global potato production statistics derived from the FAOSTAT database, emphasizing its utility as a primary source for open agricultural data. It notes that while the database has since been updated to include more recent years, the core value lies in its accessibility and comprehensive coverage of worldwide crop yields. The central finding identifies China and India as the dominant producers, together accounting for a significant majority of the global total. The narrative underscores that this data provides essential insights into agricultural distribution and productivity across nearly a hundred nations. By offering standardized metrics on production volume and harvested area, the FAO enables researchers and policymakers to analyze trends and disparities in potato farming. Although slight discrepancies may exist between international reports and national records, the global dataset remains sufficiently accurate for broad comparative studies. Relevance to open_data is significant because FAOSTAT exemplifies high-quality, publicly accessible statistical infrastructure. It demonstrates how standardized, machine-readable datasets facilitate cross-border analysis and support evidence-based decision-making. By making such granular production data freely available, platforms like FAOSTAT empower independent researchers, developers, and analysts to extract value without barriers, reinforcing the importance of transparent, open government data in monitoring global food security.
