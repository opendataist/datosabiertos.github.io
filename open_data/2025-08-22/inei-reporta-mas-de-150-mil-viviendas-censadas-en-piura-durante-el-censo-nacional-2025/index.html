INEI reporta más de 150 mil viviendas censadas en Piura durante el Censo Nacional 2025

INEI reporta más de 150 mil viviendas censadas en Piura durante el Censo Nacional 2025

The 2025 National Census in Piura has progressed successfully, having covered the vast majority of scheduled households. This extensive fieldwork ensures a comprehensive and accurate representation of the region’s demographic reality, setting the stage for a second phase that maintains operational efficiency. By addressing unvisited homes through targeted recovery strategies, the process aims to achieve total coverage. This census serves as a foundational tool for gathering critical social data. The information collected allows for a deeper understanding of how the population lives and their specific needs. Such insights are vital for moving beyond simple headcounts to creating nuanced profiles that inform decision-making at both local and national levels. This article is highly relevant to open data because the resulting statistics will directly shape public policy, health, education, and infrastructure investments. Transparent, accessible census data empowers citizens and organizations to hold authorities accountable and drive evidence-based development. By ensuring the reliability and accessibility of these figures, open data principles help translate raw demographic facts into tangible societal benefits.
