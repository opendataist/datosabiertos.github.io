Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

The article is a privacy notice issued by 13 MEDIA, S.C., designed to inform users about the collection, use, and management of their personal data in compliance with Mexico’s Federal Law on Protection of Data Held by Private Parties. It outlines that the company gathers extensive personal information, including identity details, financial assets, and legal identifiers, primarily to verify identity, understand client needs, and fulfill legal obligations. This transparency is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the importance of clear legal frameworks governing data handling. The company adheres to strict privacy principles such as lawfulness, quality, consent, and proportionality. Data is collected directly from individuals via various legal technologies and is shared only with necessary third parties, such as notaries or lawyers, for formalizing documents, or with authorities when legally required. Users can limit the use of their information by contacting a designated privacy officer, though legal obligations may restrict this limitation. This structure demonstrates how organizations can balance data utility with individual privacy rights. Finally, the notice explains how individuals can exercise their rights to access, rectify, cancel, or oppose the processing of their data through written requests or electronic communication. It also informs users of how to stay updated on privacy policy changes via the company’s website or email. This document is relevant to open data because it serves as a model for public transparency in data practices. By clearly defining data flows and user rights, it supports the ethical standards necessary for trustworthy data ecosystems, ensuring that personal information is managed responsibly and with user control.
