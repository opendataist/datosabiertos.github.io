X faces controversy over using user data for training AI chatbot Grok: Report

X faces controversy over using user data for training AI chatbot Grok: Report

Elon Musk’s platform X faces regulatory scrutiny from the Irish Data Protection Commission after revelations that user data was inadvertently used to train Grok AI without explicit consent. The default setting enabled the collection of posts and interactions for model fine-tuning, prompting urgent warnings from privacy advocates urging users to manually disable this feature to protect their digital footprint. This incident highlights a critical tension between rapid AI development and robust user privacy safeguards. It underscores the necessity for platforms to prioritize transparency and explicit opt-in mechanisms, ensuring that personal information is not harvested silently for commercial or experimental technological advancements. For open data, this case serves as a stark reminder of the ethical responsibilities surrounding data usage. It reinforces the importance of clear governance and user control, demonstrating that even well-intentioned AI initiatives must respect individual privacy rights to maintain public trust and adhere to emerging regulatory standards.
