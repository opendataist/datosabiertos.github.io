Cisco automates AI-driven security across enterprise networks

Cisco automates AI-driven security across enterprise networks

Cisco’s new XDR Forensics capability automates the collection of critical endpoint artifacts, significantly streamlining investigations even for compromised systems. By integrating AI-driven attack storyboards, it visualizes complex threats and maps them to standard tactics, allowing teams to grasp implications instantly. This automation transforms technical data into clear, actionable narratives for both analysts and executives. It highlights root causes and recommends containment steps, ensuring faster, more confident decision-making during security incidents. This development is vital for open data because it standardizes how forensic evidence is structured and shared. By converting complex attack data into accessible formats, it supports transparency and interoperability, enabling broader analysis and collaboration across security ecosystems without relying on proprietary silos.
