El papa Francisco reúne a casi 35 millones de fieles en su pontificado - La Verdad

El papa Francisco reúne a casi 35 millones de fieles en su pontificado - La Verdad

The Vatican has released official attendance figures for Pope Francis’s pontificate, revealing that nearly 35 million people participated in his public events between 2013 and his death. These comprehensive datasets include audiences, liturgical celebrations, and prayers, offering a transparent view of his global engagement and the shifting dynamics of religious gatherings over more than twelve years. The data highlights significant trends, such as the initial surge in participation following his election, the sharp decline during the pandemic years, and the subsequent recovery in public attendance. By breaking down metrics by event type and year, these records provide valuable insights into how external factors and health issues impacted the reach of his ministry, particularly noting the reduced activity in his final months. This release is crucial for open-data communities as it demonstrates how institutional entities can responsibly publish large-scale, longitudinal public records. By making such detailed demographic and temporal data accessible, the Vatican enables researchers and developers to analyze social trends, verify historical claims, and create visualizations that promote transparency and civic understanding of major global institutions.
