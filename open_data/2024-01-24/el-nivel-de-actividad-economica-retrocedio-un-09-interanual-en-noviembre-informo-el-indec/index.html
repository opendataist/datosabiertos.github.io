El nivel de actividad económica retrocedió un 0,9% interanual en noviembre, informó el Indec

The article highlights a broad contraction in Argentina’s economic activity, with significant declines across industrial and commercial sectors contrasting with growth in mining and agriculture. These figures underscore the volatile nature of current economic indicators, which often lack the granularity required for meaningful public scrutiny. Open data is crucial here to transform such opaque macroeconomic reports into accessible, granular datasets. This enables citizens and researchers to verify claims, understand sectoral impacts, and hold institutions accountable for economic performance. Ultimately, this story illustrates the need for transparent, timely data releases. Without open standards, complex economic shifts remain abstract, preventing informed debate and limiting the potential for data-driven policy improvements and public engagement.
