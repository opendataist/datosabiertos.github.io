ABC: A Record 89.3% of the U.S. Construction Industry Is Not Part of a Union - The American Surveyor

Union membership in the U.S. construction industry has plummeted to a historic low, signaling that pro-union policies have failed to reverse this steady decline. Despite significant growth in the overall workforce, non-union contractors continue to dominate as workers increasingly reject union affiliation, challenging the assumption that regulatory support can force unionization. This trend highlights the negative impact of anti-competitive regulations, which critics argue stifle industry potential and exacerbate labor shortages. By mandating specific labor agreements and complex compliance rules, the government may be inadvertently hindering the ability of qualified, non-union contractors to participate effectively in infrastructure projects. This data is crucial for open data advocates because it demonstrates how public statistics can be interpreted to challenge prevailing political narratives. It underscores the importance of transparent, accessible labor data in fostering debate about regulatory effectiveness and ensuring that policy decisions reflect the actual choices and preferences of the workforce rather than ideological assumptions.
