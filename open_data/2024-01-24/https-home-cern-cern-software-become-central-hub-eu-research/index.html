Open Data
ES EN

Zenodo has evolved from a particle physics archive into a global repository supporting diverse scientific fields, driven by the critical need for open knowledge sharing. Its pivotal role during the pandemic demonstrated how accessible platforms can accelerate international responses to crises. By hosting data from thousands of organizations, it validates the necessity of robust, shared infrastructure for modern research collaboration across disciplines like biodiversity and linguistics. The recent HORIZON-ZEN project aims to simplify compliance with European Union mandates for FAIR data principles. Since these principles are often viewed as generic, the project focuses on creating tailored, domain-specific user experiences that make data management more intuitive. This initiative seeks to bridge the gap between regulatory requirements and practical researcher needs, ensuring that making data findable and reusable becomes a streamlined process rather than a bureaucratic hurdle. This development is highly relevant to open data because it addresses the practical barriers to adoption. By co-designing tools with scientific communities, Zenodo ensures that open science policies are actionable and user-friendly. This approach encourages broader participation, reduces administrative burdens, and strengthens the global open data ecosystem by providing a reliable, customizable platform that aligns with evolving technological and regulatory standards.
