Destaca en obras de electricidad y telecomunicaciones

Querétaro has emerged as a national leader in electricity and telecommunications infrastructure, ranking third in production value, behind only Yucatán and Oaxaca. This significant ranking highlights the state’s strategic prioritization of digital connectivity over traditional transportation projects, signaling a strong commitment to modernizing public services and enhancing regional technological access. The government’s focus on digital transformation is evident in initiatives such as “Querétaro Digital,” which aims to streamline municipal procedures and provide free internet access in public schools across diverse geographical areas. By reducing physical paperwork and expanding digital access, these efforts demonstrate a tangible shift toward efficient, technology-driven governance that improves service delivery for citizens. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates, as it underscores the critical infrastructure required for equitable access to information. As the state invests heavily in digitalization and reduces physical bureaucracy, it creates a more conducive environment for the adoption and use of open data platforms, fostering greater transparency and citizen engagement in public administration.
