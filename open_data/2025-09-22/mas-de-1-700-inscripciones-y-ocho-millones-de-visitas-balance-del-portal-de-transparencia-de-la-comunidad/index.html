Más de 1.700 inscripciones y ocho millones de visitas, balance del Portal de Transparencia de la Comunidad

Más de 1.700 inscripciones y ocho millones de visitas, balance del Portal de Transparencia de la Comunidad

The Madrid Transparency Portal demonstrates that open data initiatives significantly boost civic engagement by providing information in reusable formats. By prioritizing accessibility and simplified language, the administration ensures that citizens can effectively understand and use public records. This approach fosters trust through high compliance with access requests and streamlined submission processes. The publication of resolutions and the integration of visual tools further democratize data, transforming complex bureaucratic information into actionable knowledge for the public. The relevance to open data lies in demonstrating that user-centric design enhances both transparency and participation. The portal’s success underscores the necessity of standardized, interoperable systems, encouraging broader adoption of open data practices to reduce administrative burdens and empower citizen oversight.
