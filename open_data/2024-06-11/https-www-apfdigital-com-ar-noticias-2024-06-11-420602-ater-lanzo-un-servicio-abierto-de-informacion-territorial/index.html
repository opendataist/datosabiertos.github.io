Open Data
ES EN

The launch of the public Web Cadastral Viewer by Argentina’s ATER marks a significant step in the implementation of open data. This tool provides free access to detailed, georeferenced territorial information, enabling transparent and efficient fiscal management while fostering better planning and decision-making across both the public and private sectors. By offering open geoservices and datasets compliant with international standards, the initiative empowers professionals, researchers, and citizens to freely explore cadastral data. This openness supports the development of new solutions and collaborative projects, demonstrating how government transparency can drive community engagement and private initiative through accessible, high-quality information. This development is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the practical application of open principles in municipal governance. It shows how releasing raw geospatial data fosters innovation, accountability, and informed civic participation, transforming static records into dynamic resources for societal benefit and improved public policy.
