Head of ICC Ukraine Team Office: We're identifying locations where Russia subjected Ukrainians POWs, civilians to most brutal tortures

Head of ICC Ukraine Team Office: We're identifying locations where Russia subjected Ukrainians POWs, civilians to most brutal tortures

The ICC Ukraine Team prioritizes investigating the deportation of Ukrainian children and attacks on energy infrastructure, actions that have already resulted in arrest warrants for high-ranking Russian officials. This strategic focus allows the Court to build robust cases against leadership rather than attempting to address every single incident globally. Concurrently, the office examines severe abuses against detained civilians and prisoners of war, such as torture and illegal detention. Since investigating every crime is impossible, the team concentrates resources on locations where the most brutal and systematic violations occurred, ensuring that the most egregious evidence reaches senior judges. This approach highlights a critical aspect of open data in international justice: the necessity of structured, targeted evidence collection. By concentrating on high-impact cases with verifiable data, the ICC demonstrates how transparency and rigorous documentation are essential for holding powerful actors accountable in complex conflict zones.
