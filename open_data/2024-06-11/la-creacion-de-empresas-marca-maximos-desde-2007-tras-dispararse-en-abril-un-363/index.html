La creación de empresas marca máximos desde 2007 tras dispararse en abril un 36,3%

La creación de empresas marca máximos desde 2007 tras dispararse en abril un 36,3%

April saw a significant surge in new company formations, reaching the highest levels since 2007. This sharp increase, coupled with growing capital investment, signals a revitalization of entrepreneurial activity across various economic sectors and regions. At the same time, business dissolutions also rose, although the majority were voluntary. The parallel growth in capital expansions indicates that existing firms are scaling up, reflecting a dynamic market environment characterized by both entry and exit dynamics. This data is crucial for open_data, as it demonstrates the value of transparent, high-quality statistical reporting. Reliable public datasets enable researchers, policymakers, and analysts to track economic trends, assess market health, and make informed decisions based on real-time national indicators.
