INEGE presenta el análisis sobre la participación de la mujer ecuatoguineana en la toma de decisiones

The analysis reveals that, despite women constituting the majority of the population, they face significant barriers to achieving true gender parity in decision-making roles across Equatorial Guinea. While notable progress is evident in legislative and judicial bodies, overall representation remains well below ideal levels, highlighting a persistent gap between demographic potential and actual political power. In the corporate sector, although a substantial proportion of businesses are female-owned, leadership positions in government administration and diplomatic roles remain disproportionately low. This disparity underscores the need for targeted policies to dismantle systemic obstacles and promote inclusive leadership opportunities, particularly in peripheral and local administrative spheres where female representation is critically scarce. This report is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides a transparent, disaggregated dataset on gender representation. By making specific metrics on political and economic participation publicly accessible, it enables researchers and civil society to monitor equality trends, hold institutions accountable, and advocate for evidence-based reforms that foster genuine inclusivity and social equity.
