García Harfuch defiende legalidad del padrón telefónico

García Harfuch defiende legalidad del padrón telefónico

The article highlights the government’s commitment to establishing a legal and supervised telephone registry, with public security authorities operating strictly under judicial oversight. This ensures that access to the database is not discretionary but controlled by the Public Ministry, aiming to prevent abuses and build public trust through dialogue. Simultaneously, the government reports significant successes in its security strategy, citing a marked reduction in homicides and the weakening of criminal organizations through targeted operations. These actions, including high-profile arrests, are presented as evidence that the current administration’s approach is effectively dismantling cartels and restoring order, distinct from isolated incidents of violence against officials. This information is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of legal frameworks, transparency, and oversight mechanisms when managing sensitive citizen data. It illustrates how public institutions must balance operational security with accountability, ensuring that data usage adheres to strict judicial controls to protect civil liberties and maintain integrity in digital governance systems.
