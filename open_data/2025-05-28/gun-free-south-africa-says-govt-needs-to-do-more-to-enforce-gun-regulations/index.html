Gun Free South Africa says govt needs to do more to enforce gun regulations

Gun Free South Africa says govt needs to do more to enforce gun regulations

South Africa’s recent crime data reveals that firearms remain the primary weapon for murder, highlighting a critical failure in current regulatory enforcement. This persistence underscores the urgent need to secure the legal firearm market to prevent weapons from leaking into criminal hands, which is essential for meaningful violence reduction. For open data advocates, this case illustrates how transparent, timely statistics are vital for exposing systemic gaps and driving accountability in public safety policy.
