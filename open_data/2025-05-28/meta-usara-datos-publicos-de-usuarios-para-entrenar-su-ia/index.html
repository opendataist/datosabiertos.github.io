Meta usará datos públicos de usuarios para entrenar su IA

Meta usará datos públicos de usuarios para entrenar su IA

Meta justifies the use of public data for AI training on the grounds of legitimate interest, while complying with European regulations by providing an opt-out mechanism. This allows users to actively object to the use of their public information, ensuring that their choices are respected in both current and future training cycles. The company clarifies that it excludes private messages, data from minors, and protected content, focusing solely on public posts and interactions from adults. By offering a dedicated form for objections, Meta aims to be transparent and responsible, addressing privacy concerns while leveraging user-generated content to improve its models. This case is significant for open data because it highlights the tension between the accessibility of public data and individual privacy rights in AI development. It demonstrates how major technology companies navigate legal frameworks such as the GDPR, setting precedents for consent and transparency in the use of publicly available information for machine learning. This has implications for how open data ecosystems balance utility with user control.
