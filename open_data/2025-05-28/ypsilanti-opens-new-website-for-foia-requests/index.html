Ypsilanti opens new website for FOIA requests

Ypsilanti opens new website for FOIA requests

Ypsilanti’s new online FOIA portal streamlines government transparency by automating request routing, reducing administrative inefficiencies. While accessible to most citizens, it excludes incarcerated individuals and protects specific personal data. This digital shift enhances open data accessibility, allowing the public to easily track municipal affairs through a simplified, standardized interface.
