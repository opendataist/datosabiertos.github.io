Cartismo aprobará ley de protección de datos personales y asegura que dará “seguridad” - Política - ABC Color

Cartismo aprobará ley de protección de datos personales y asegura que dará “seguridad” - Política - ABC Color

The proposed personal data protection law is moving forward, with legislators claiming that the final amendments have successfully addressed earlier criticisms of the original draft. By ensuring robust security for sensitive and biometric data, the initiative aims to provide the stability needed to attract foreign investment and build public trust in digital transactions. This shift indicates a prioritization of legal certainty over the fragmented, multiple versions that previously hindered progress. The legislation seeks to regulate the commercial and political use of citizens’ data, aiming to prevent misuse by both companies and state actors. While concerns remain about excessive executive discretion and the lack of immediate clarity regarding the final regulatory framework, proponents argue that the current version offers adequate protection. This balance is crucial for establishing a reliable legal environment in which individual rights are safeguarded against overreach by corporations and the government. This development is highly relevant to open data, as effective personal data protection laws establish the foundational privacy standards required for responsible data ecosystems. Without such safeguards, the risks associated with unauthorized data use could stifle innovation and erode public confidence. Therefore, this law serves as a critical prerequisite for any open data initiative, ensuring that information sharing occurs within a secure, regulated framework that respects citizens’ privacy while enabling economic growth.
