Cancún tiene al menos 90 mil animales domésticos callejeros, señala directora de Ecología Municipal - Noticaribe

Cancún tiene al menos 90 mil animales domésticos callejeros, señala directora de Ecología Municipal - Noticaribe

The article highlights the severe problem of stray domestic animals in Cancún, emphasizing that irresponsible pet ownership is a primary driver. It contrasts official municipal estimates with higher figures reported by animal welfare organizations, suggesting that the crisis is likely more extensive than officially recorded. These discrepancies underscore the urgent need for transparent, reliable data to effectively address public health and social welfare challenges. Accurate statistics are essential for planning adequate resources and implementing targeted interventions to manage the growing population of abandoned pets. This situation is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how inconsistent or delayed information hampers effective governance. Promoting the release of timely, verified, and accessible data would enable better policy-making, enhance public accountability, and support NGOs in advocating for animal welfare and responsible pet ownership practices.
