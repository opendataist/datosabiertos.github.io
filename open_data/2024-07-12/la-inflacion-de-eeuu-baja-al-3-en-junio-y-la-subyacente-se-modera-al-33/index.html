La inflación de EEUU baja al 3% en junio y la subyacente se modera al 3,3%

The moderation of inflation in the United States, coming in below market expectations, has strengthened the consensus among experts that the Federal Reserve should begin a cycle of interest rate cuts in September. The data suggest that the economy is cooling and that the inflationary trend has stabilized near the central bank’s target, reducing the risks associated with maintaining an excessively restrictive monetary policy and supporting the prioritization of economic stability and employment over continued efforts to combat price increases. The decline in weekly jobless claims, although smaller than expected, reflects a labor market that, despite certain cracks, continues to show resilience. This combination of slowing inflation and mixed labor data indicates that the Fed has room to adjust its interest rates without causing severe damage to growth, fostering an optimistic outlook toward progressive monetary easing in the coming months. This content is relevant to the field of open data because it highlights the critical importance of the timely and accessible publication of official macroeconomic data. Transparency in access to statistics such as the Consumer Price Index (CPI) and unemployment claims enables civil society, researchers, and financial markets to analyze trends, validate hypotheses, and make informed decisions, thereby democratizing understanding of the economic situation and promoting accountability of public institutions.
