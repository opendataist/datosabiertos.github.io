El legado de una empresa tiene que sentar bases sólidas sobre criterios ESG

The article emphasizes that leadership in corporate governance is becoming increasingly crucial for the effective implementation of ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) initiatives, although family-owned businesses tend to lag behind in achieving comprehensive adoption. This gap highlights the pressing need for robust open-data frameworks to transparently monitor and verify sustainability efforts across various corporate structures. Incorporating diverse viewpoints, especially women’s leadership, is presented as a key strategy to align business objectives with broader strategic visions. This transition requires accessible, standardized data to accurately assess the tangible impact of gender diversity on governance and social outcomes. As a result, open data plays an essential role in connecting corporate social responsibility with actual business performance. By ensuring that ESG metrics are transparent and comparable, organizations can better align their strategic plans with societal expectations, fostering meaningful progress in both governance and social equity.
