Población peruana proyectada en el 2024 superó los 34 millones de habitantes, informó el INEI | RPP Noticias

The INEI’s 2024 demographic report reveals a profound structural shift in Peru, characterized by significant population aging and intense urbanization. The country is transitioning from a young demographic structure to one with a growing elderly population, altering the traditional dependency ratios. Simultaneously, the vast majority of residents now live in urban areas, with Lima Metropolitan absorbing a disproportionate share of the national population, highlighting extreme geographic concentration. Beyond mere headcounts, the data emphasizes Peru’s rich ethnic and linguistic diversity, acknowledging numerous indigenous groups while quantifying self-identification rates across various categories. This demographic landscape intersects with social realities, as high levels of perceived insecurity coexist with these structural changes. Understanding these trends is crucial for planning public services, economic development, and social protection systems that must adapt to an older, more urbanized, and socially anxious populace. This article is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies how official statistical institutions generate granular, longitudinal datasets that drive evidence-based policy. For open data advocates, this case study underscores the importance of accessible, transparent population projections in addressing complex societal challenges like aging, urban inequality, and public safety. It illustrates the necessity of combining quantitative demographic data with qualitative social indicators to create a holistic view of national well-being.
