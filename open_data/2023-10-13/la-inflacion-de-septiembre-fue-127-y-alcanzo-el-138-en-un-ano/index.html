La inflación de septiembre fue 12,7% y alcanzó el 138% en un año

Argentina is grappling with severe inflation, which accelerated sharply in September and exhibited significant regional disparities. This trend underscores the urgent need for transparent and accessible economic data to monitor public welfare and assess the effectiveness of policy measures. Accurate open data is essential for analyzing which sectors—such as clothing and food—are driving price increases across different geographical areas. Without reliable statistics, citizens and experts cannot fully grasp the impact of economic instability on everyday life. Ultimately, this article highlights the importance of open government data in promoting accountability. By providing detailed, granular information, society can track inflation patterns and hold institutions responsible for managing the cost of living.
