The National Institute of Statistics has finalized the questionnaire for the upcoming census, emphasizing the collection of precise demographic, cultural, and socioeconomic data. This information is crucial for guiding national and local governments in crafting effective public policies that accurately reflect the current situation of citizens. Notably, the survey excludes any remuneration for the extensive network of volunteer census takers. The administration maintains that this participation constitutes a civic service to the country, adhering to the global standard of voluntary involvement in censuses rather than paid employment models. This update is vital for open data initiatives as it defines the scope and accessibility of future statistical records. By capturing detailed individual characteristics, including health coverage and indigenous identification, it ensures that the resulting dataset supports transparency and informed decision-making across diverse sectors.
