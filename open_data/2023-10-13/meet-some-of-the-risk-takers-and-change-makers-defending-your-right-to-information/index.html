Meet some of the risk-takers and change-makers defending your right to information

The launch of a collaborative project between IFEX and the International Free Expression Project highlights the critical role of alliances in defending free expression. By uniting a global network of organizations, this initiative amplifies the efforts of individuals who risk their safety to uphold access to information and civic rights. The collaboration underscores how shared experiences and resources can strengthen advocacy against threats to fundamental liberties. Journalists and activists often operate on the frontlines, facing severe risks while exposing corruption and challenging power structures. The stories of these change-makers, including those who have faced fatal consequences, serve as powerful inspirations. Their tenacity demonstrates the high stakes involved in demanding accountability and transparency, reminding us that the cost of speaking truth to power can be existential. This relevance to open data lies in the intrinsic link between transparency and safety. Open data initiatives depend on the willingness of individuals to share information, which requires a protected environment where free expression is respected. By honoring those who defend these rights, the project emphasizes that sustainable open data ecosystems rely on robust legal frameworks and societal support for journalists and activists who reveal hidden truths and drive systemic change.
