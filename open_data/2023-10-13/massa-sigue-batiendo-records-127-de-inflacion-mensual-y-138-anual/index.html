Massa sigue batiendo récords: 12,7% de inflación mensual y 138% anual

In September, inflation reached 12.7%, significantly surpassing private-sector forecasts and underscoring severe economic instability. This surge, driven primarily by the rising costs of essential goods such as food and clothing, highlights the ongoing erosion of purchasing power across all regions. The data, released just before crucial presidential elections, reveals that high annual and cumulative inflation rates are likely to persist regardless of the political outcome. Credit agencies predict even sharper increases in the coming year, indicating that the current trajectory is deeply structural rather than temporary. This information is vital for open-data initiatives, as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible, and timely statistical releases. Reliable public data enables citizens and analysts to verify official figures against independent estimates, fostering accountability and facilitating informed civic engagement during critical democratic processes.
