ANC says Census Report an indication of party’s commitment to improving citizens’ lives

The African National Congress utilizes the 2022 Census Report to assert its effectiveness and refute claims of national decline. By highlighting substantial improvements in housing, electricity access, and water provision, the party presents concrete evidence of its commitment to the developmental agenda established since the democratic transition. This narrative serves to validate the ANC’s governance record and reaffirm its resolve to deliver better living standards for all citizens. However, the party acknowledges that significant structural challenges, including corruption, unemployment, and energy crises, persist. In response, the ANC emphasizes ongoing institutional reforms and stricter leadership criteria aimed at strengthening state capacity. The focus remains on addressing these urgent issues through improved integrated planning and service delivery systems, demonstrating a proactive approach to overcoming the obstacles hindering equitable development. This article is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how citizens and political actors interpret large-scale statistical datasets to construct political narratives. The census serves as a critical open data resource that enables public accountability and performance evaluation. By analyzing such official statistics, stakeholders can challenge or confirm political claims, ensuring that government progress is measured against transparent, verifiable benchmarks rather than rhetoric alone.
