The Economic & Statistics Office (ESO) Releases the 2022 Cayman Islands Compendium of Statistics

The 2022 Cayman Islands statistics reveal a significant population surge and shifting social assistance trends, highlighting evolving community needs. While poverty relief and burial aid increased, support for utilities and housing declined, reflecting changing socioeconomic conditions amidst rapid demographic growth. Environmental data shows substantial waste management volumes, with landfill usage remaining high despite a minor rise in incineration. Agricultural outputs varied widely, marked by sharp increases in goat and cattle production but notable declines in poultry, illustrating the sector's dynamic fluctuations rather than uniform growth. This compendium is vital for open data initiatives as it centralizes diverse public and private sector metrics into an accessible format. By aggregating indicators across health, economy, and environment, it enables transparent analysis and supports evidence-based policy making for residents and researchers interested in the territory’s comprehensive development landscape.

Source: radiocayman.gov.ky
