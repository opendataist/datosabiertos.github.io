The article anticipates high inflation for September, with analysts predicting rates around twelve percent. This continues a trend of accelerating prices, driven largely by increases in basic food baskets and resulting in an annual year-on-year spike reaching historic highs since 1991. These figures indicate a severe economic environment where cumulative inflation exceeds one hundred percent for the year. The disparity between official data and alternative indices highlights the complexity of measuring true cost-of-living pressures, especially for workers whose prices rose even faster than general averages. This data is crucial for open_data as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical information. Reliable public datasets allow researchers and citizens to verify official narratives, understand the erosion of purchasing power, and hold institutions accountable through independent analysis of economic indicators.
