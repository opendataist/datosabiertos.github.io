C-LM participará en Navarra en el V Encuentro Nacional de Datos Abiertos bajo el lema 'Aprende y emprende'

The Government of Navarre will host the 5th National Open Data Meeting (ENDA) in 2026, consolidating its position as the leading national event for exchanging knowledge and best practices. This edition highlights the region’s strategic commitment to a governance model based on transparency and data reuse, positioning itself as a key driver of innovative policies that foster collaboration among public administrations, universities, and the private sector. The event’s slogan, “Learn and Entrepreneur,” underscores the crucial link between open data, education, and entrepreneurship. It explores how public information can serve as a lever to transform talent, drive innovation, and facilitate economic and social development. The convergence of these areas aims to demonstrate the real potential of data to generate tangible value for society, going beyond mere technical availability. ENDA’s relevance to open data lies in its integrative approach with emerging technologies, particularly artificial intelligence, and its ability to bring together the main actors in the data ecosystem. By focusing on practical data reuse and the evolution of public policies, the meeting not only shares experiences but also shapes the future of digital transparency in Spain, ensuring that data becomes an effective tool for governance and job creation.
