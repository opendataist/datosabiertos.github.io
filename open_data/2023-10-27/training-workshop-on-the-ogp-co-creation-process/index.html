Training workshop on the OGP co-creation process

Seychelles has established a robust framework for open governance by launching a strategic training workshop for its Multi-Stakeholder Committee, which includes government officials, civil society leaders, and media representatives. This initiative reinforces the country’s commitment to the Open Government Partnership (OGP), a global movement fostering transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement. By institutionalizing an OGP focal point within the Cabinet Office, the nation aims to deepen democratic participation and leverage technology to improve public service delivery. The article highlights Seychelles’ progress since joining the OGP in 2018, noting the successful implementation of its first action plan and the transition toward developing a more ambitious second cycle. With support from international experts and the Independent Review Mechanism, the country is focusing on co-creating policies that align with national priorities such as economic resilience, health, and environmental sustainability. This collaborative approach ensures that reforms are not only top-down but are shaped by active input from diverse societal stakeholders. This development is crucial for open data advocates because it demonstrates how formalized international partnerships can drive domestic data transparency. The emphasis on implementing access to information acts and modernizing public services creates a fertile ground for open data initiatives to thrive. By empowering citizens to hold the government accountable through verified information, Seychelles is building an ecosystem where open data serves as a foundational tool for inclusive governance and trust-building between the state and the public.
