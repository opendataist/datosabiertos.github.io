Huracán Otis paraliza 54.2% de la economía de Guerrero

The devastation of Acapulco by Hurricane Otis critically threatens Guerrero’s economy, as the city contributes significantly to the state’s GDP and its primary tourism sectors. This event disrupts recent growth trajectories, particularly in air passenger traffic, highlighting the fragility of a regional economy heavily reliant on external mobility and services. The disaster underscores the urgent need for transparent data regarding economic impacts and recovery efforts. Reliable statistical information is essential for coordinating aid, assessing infrastructure damage, and understanding the socio-economic vulnerabilities of the affected population, including indigenous communities and employment rates. Relevant to open data, this situation demonstrates how accessible, precise metrics are vital for effective crisis management. By making economic and demographic data available, stakeholders can better evaluate the long-term consequences of climate events on tourism-dependent regions, facilitating informed decision-making and resilient urban planning for future disaster preparedness.
