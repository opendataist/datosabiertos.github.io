The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INEC) aims to produce up-to-date statistics on household microenterprises to support the formulation, monitoring, and evaluation of public and private policies. This initiative seeks to capture the economic contribution of these activities by estimating their value added, intermediate consumption, and associated income, thereby providing essential data for strategic decision-making. Strict statistical confidentiality is guaranteed by national law, ensuring that no individual-level information is disclosed to authorities or used for non-statistical purposes. This legal framework protects respondents and maintains trust in the data collection process, which is crucial for obtaining accurate and reliable information from business owners. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the balance between collecting detailed, potentially sensitive economic data and ensuring robust privacy protections. It demonstrates how official statistics can be responsibly collected and anonymized, fostering transparency and trust while enabling the release of aggregated insights for public use.
