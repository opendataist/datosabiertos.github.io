Cecam valora los datos de paro en C-LM y destaca la evaluación "positiva" del mercado laboral a nivel interanual

Cecam valora los datos de paro en C-LM y destaca la evaluación "positiva" del mercado laboral a nivel interanual

The article highlights a significant drop in unemployment in the region, surpassing national averages, driven primarily by the services sector. However, it argues that current statistics are incomplete because the government fails to publish essential data on fixed discontinuous workers. This lack of transparency obscures the true working hours and productivity levels, creating a misleading picture of the labor market. Without precise figures on these specific employment contracts, assessing the real economic impact and workforce stability remains challenging for policymakers and businesses alike. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how missing public datasets hinder accurate economic analysis. It underscores the urgent need for governments to release comprehensive, granular employment data to ensure transparency and support informed decision-making regarding labor competitiveness and fiscal challenges.
