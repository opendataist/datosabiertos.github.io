Dos de los municipios de España en los que más creció la población están en la provincia de Alicante

Dos de los municipios de España en los que más creció la población están en la provincia de Alicante

The INE report reveals widespread population growth across Spain in 2024, driven primarily by immigration and an increase in the elderly segment. This dynamic has led to significant demographic changes, including accelerated societal aging and a concentration of growth in coastal areas and major cities, while regions such as Extremadura experience population decline. Demographic diversification is evident in the profile of the foreign population, with a notable predominance of Latin American and Maghrebi nationalities, reflecting the evolution of migration routes to Spain. Furthermore, age-based analysis shows that although the working-age and senior populations are increasing, the birth rate remains insufficient to balance the population pyramid, thereby increasing pressure on dependency support systems. This analysis is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of accessing updated, disaggregated public statistics to understand structural trends. Detailed data on migration flows, geographic distribution, and age structure enable researchers, journalists, and citizens to critically assess the impact of integration and social welfare policies, fostering transparency and the informed use of public information.
