Hacienda retrasa un año la entrada en vigor de Verifactu, el nuevo sistema de facturación para pymes y autónomos

Hacienda retrasa un año la entrada en vigor de Verifactu, el nuevo sistema de facturación para pymes y autónomos

The Spanish government is postponing the mandatory implementation of Verifactu by one year, giving businesses and professionals additional time to adapt their accounting software. This extension ensures that the new digital invoice verification systems, which require secure and immutable records accessible to tax authorities, will only be enforced starting in 2027. This adjustment highlights the ongoing tension between regulatory compliance and technical feasibility for small enterprises. By easing the immediate burden, authorities aim to prevent disruption while maintaining strict oversight mechanisms for financial transparency and tax control. This development is relevant to open data as it underscores the critical intersection between public fiscal transparency and private technological infrastructure. The mandate to create tamper-proof, accessible records demonstrates how government policy drives the adoption of data standards that eventually facilitate broader interoperability and public access to verified economic information.
