El desempleo baja en 349 personas en noviembre en C-LM y la región contabiliza 117.792 parados

El desempleo baja en 349 personas en noviembre en C-LM y la región contabiliza 117.792 parados

The information highlights a positive trend in the labor market in Castilla-La Mancha and at the national level, characterized by a significant reduction in registered unemployment. This decline marks the lowest figures for the month of November since 2007, indicating sustained improvement in job creation. The phenomenon is particularly notable in sectors such as services and agriculture, suggesting a structured recovery that benefits various segments of the population, including young people and women. Although formal employment is increasing, a significant challenge related to labor precariousness persists. Temporary contracts outnumber permanent ones, revealing that the quality of newly created jobs is still suboptimal. This dynamic invites reflection on the long-term stability of the labor market, as an excess of temporary contracts can limit workers' economic security despite the reduction in unemployment rates. This analysis is relevant to open data because it illustrates the importance of publishing and visualizing disaggregated public data by territory, gender, age, and contract type. Transparent access to this information enables citizens, researchers, and policymakers to identify hidden inequalities and trends, fostering evidence-based decision-making. The availability of these historical and comparative records facilitates critical evaluation of public policies and government accountability.
