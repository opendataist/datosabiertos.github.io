Mujeres lideran el empleo en microempresas: Inegi - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Mujeres lideran el empleo en microempresas: Inegi - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Gender-based labor segmentation persists in Puebla, with women dominating the microenterprise workforce while their presence sharply declines as company size increases. This trend reveals a structural barrier where women are concentrated in service sectors, whereas men prevail in technical and industrial fields, highlighting significant disparities in economic opportunities across different business scales. These findings are critical for open data because they illustrate how accessible statistical records can expose entrenched inequalities. By making this information public, policymakers and researchers can analyze labor market dynamics to design targeted interventions. Ultimately, transparency in data drives the push for equitable participation across all economic levels. Understanding these gendered patterns allows for more effective strategies to promote diversity and fairness, ensuring that open data serves as a tool for social progress rather than just statistical record-keeping.
