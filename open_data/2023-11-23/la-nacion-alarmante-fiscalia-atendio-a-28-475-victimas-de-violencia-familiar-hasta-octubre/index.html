La Nación / Alarmante: Fiscalía atendió a 28.475 víctimas de violencia familiar hasta octubre

The report highlights a severe escalation in domestic violence, with official data revealing a tripling of cases over the past eight years. This alarming trend underscores a critical failure in social protection mechanisms, demanding immediate policy reforms and increased resources to address the root causes of such pervasive violence against vulnerable family members. This information is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible government statistics for public accountability. By publishing granular datasets, authorities enable independent analysis that validates public concerns and pressures institutions to act. Open data transforms raw numbers into powerful evidence for advocacy, allowing citizens and researchers to effectively track progress—or regression—in human rights compliance. Furthermore, the geographic concentration of these cases provides essential insights for targeted intervention strategies. Understanding regional disparities helps allocate resources where they are most needed, ensuring that support systems reach the most affected communities. Ultimately, leveraging open data fosters a more responsive society, turning statistical trends into actionable solutions that protect victims and reduce the overall burden on the justice system.
