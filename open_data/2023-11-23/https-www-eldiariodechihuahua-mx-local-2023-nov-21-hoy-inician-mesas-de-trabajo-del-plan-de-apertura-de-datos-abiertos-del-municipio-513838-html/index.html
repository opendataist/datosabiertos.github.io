Open Data
ES EN

The Chihuahua municipal government invites citizens and organizations to participate in the development of its Open Data Plan, emphasizing the collaborative selection of high-value data with institutional support. This initiative aims to make data accessible for public analysis, fostering research and enabling society to propose impactful public policies for local improvement. This process is relevant to open data because it demonstrates a practical commitment to transparency and civic engagement. By prioritizing utility and accessibility, the municipality seeks to empower civil society, ensuring that open data serves as a tangible tool for democratic participation and informed decision-making in governance.
