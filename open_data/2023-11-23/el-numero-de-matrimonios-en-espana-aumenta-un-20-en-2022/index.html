El número de matrimonios en España aumenta un 20 % en 2022

Civil marriages significantly outnumber religious ones, indicating a high level of secularization. This shift reflects broader societal changes toward non-traditional institutional commitments rather than religious doctrine. Additionally, the average age at marriage has decreased, while international unions and same-sex marriages continue to grow. These trends point to a diversification of family structures and the legal recognition of diverse relationships. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, granular statistics on social evolution. Policymakers and researchers rely on such accessible information to analyze demographic shifts, understand changing cultural norms, and design inclusive social policies effectively.
