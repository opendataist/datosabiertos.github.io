Los nacimientos bajan un 4,7% en Baleares hasta septiembre y son un 10% inferiores a 2019

The report highlights demographic shifts in the Balearic Islands, revealing that while births remain high, mortality rates have decreased compared to 2022 but increased relative to pre-pandemic levels across most islands. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible public statistics to monitor public health trends and societal recovery post-COVID. Accurate, granular demographic records enable researchers and citizens to analyze regional disparities and understand the long-term impact of recent global health crises on local communities.
