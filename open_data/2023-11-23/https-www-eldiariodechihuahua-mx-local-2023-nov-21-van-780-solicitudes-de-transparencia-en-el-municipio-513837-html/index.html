The Municipality of Chihuahua has achieved the highest transparency rating, establishing itself as the only administration in Mexico with a perfect score in the Cimtra-Municipal evaluation. This achievement not only reflects strict regulatory compliance but also demonstrates an active commitment to openness in public information, covering expenditures, public works, and citizen participation in procurement processes. The current administration has facilitated a significant flow of information requests, prioritizing data on security, services, and administration. The effective response to these demands, including the resolution of review appeals, has strengthened citizen trust and positioned the institution as a national benchmark for open government, surpassing other local entities through sustained collaborative efforts. This news is relevant to the open data community because it validates the importance of accessible formats and citizen participation in accountability. By ensuring that information is available in open formats, society is empowered to audit public management. This case exemplifies how proactive transparency and constant interaction with citizens can generate high standards of integrity and accessibility in the public sector.
