DIGEIG presenta rendición de cuentas tras un año de implementación del V Plan de Gobierno Abierto de la RD - Proceso

The Dominican Republic has presented the progress of its Fifth Action Plan for the Open Government Alliance, emphasizing the co-creation of public policies through continuous dialogue between the government and civil society. This collaborative approach, driven by the Multi-Stakeholder Forum, seeks to strengthen governmental integrity and reconnect institutions with citizens, demonstrating that transparency requires comprehensive, not unilateral, participation. The plan addresses ten priority commitments that respond to social challenges identified by citizens, covering critical areas such as the fight against corruption, improvement of public services, environmental sustainability, and youth inclusion. These structured actions aim to modernize state management and ensure that administrative reforms genuinely reflect the population’s needs, establishing more robust accountability mechanisms. This report is relevant to the open data movement because it explicitly promotes reforms in the opening of public data as a fundamental tool to combat corruption and optimize administration. By integrating information transparency into daily governance, it facilitates citizen scrutiny and civic innovation, reinforcing the principle that access to public information is essential for a participatory and efficient democracy.
