Datos definitivos del Censo 2022: en Argentina hay 46.234.830 habitantes – Diario Necochea

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses revealed that Argentina surpassed 46 million inhabitants in 2022, evidencing significant growth since 2010. However, the final data show that the official estimate differs slightly from the direct count due to territorial coverage that did not encompass the entire country or individuals not found at their homes. This discrepancy underscores the technical complexity in population measurement and the need for methodological adjustments to more accurately capture the actual population, especially in contexts of mobility or geographic access difficulties. In addition to demographic totals, the census exposes deep regional and gender inequalities, with an aging population that increases the burden on social protection systems. Women's higher life expectancy results in a marked overrepresentation of females at advanced ages, while the distribution of wealth and basic services varies substantially between provinces. These structural characteristics demand differentiated public policies that respond to the specific needs of each territory, avoiding homogenizations that obscure existing gaps in access to healthcare, housing, and economic opportunities. This article is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates how a modern census combines digital and in-person modalities to generate more robust and accessible public datasets. The implementation of techniques such as online self-registration not only streamlines data collection but also sets a precedent for transparency and citizen participation in the production of national statistics. By making these demographic and socioeconomic details available, it enables public scrutiny, academic research, and the development of civic applications that can improve governance and efficiency in the allocation of state resources.
