ORFIS, el cuarto organismo más transparente del País, presume titular

The Superior Audit Office of Veracruz stands out as a national and state-level benchmark in transparency, achieving an exceptional rating in the 2023 Open Government Index. This recognition underscores a firm commitment to accountability and openness, surpassing numerous federal, state, and municipal institutions in an evaluation exercise that prioritizes the citizen perspective. The relevance to open data lies in the fact that this index directly measures two fundamental pillars: the capacity to access public information and the possibility of citizen participation in governmental decision-making. By assessing the degree of openness among obligated entities, the study validates that transparency is not merely a legal requirement, but an essential tool for fostering public trust and democratic governance. This result highlights the critical importance of publishing accessible and understandable data, as it enables citizens to monitor public management and participate actively. Therefore, open data serve as the technical foundation supporting open government indicators, demonstrating that the availability of information is indispensable for achieving a truly transparent and participatory administration.
