Países Bajos eleva seguridad de 59 científicos y empleados universitarios por su trabajo - Ciencia - ABC Color

The Dutch initiative WetenschapVeilig reveals that researchers face significant intimidation when sharing their findings, particularly women and young researchers. This vulnerability forces them to take extreme protective measures, highlighting a hostile environment that threatens basic academic freedom in Europe. The forced silence of scientists impoverishes public debate and weakens the democratic process. By avoiding communication on controversial topics, crucial knowledge for society is lost, demonstrating that individual safety is intrinsically linked to the common good. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores that open access without identity protection ignores vital risks. Ensuring the safety of researchers is not only an ethical issue but also a prerequisite for open science to function democratically, allowing data and critical voices to circulate freely without fear.
