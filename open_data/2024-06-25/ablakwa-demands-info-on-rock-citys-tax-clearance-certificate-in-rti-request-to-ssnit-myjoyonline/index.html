Ablakwa demands info on Rock City’s Tax Clearance Certificate in RTI request to SSNIT - MyJoyOnline

This article highlights the critical role of transparency in public institutions, illustrating how open data mechanisms can expose potential governance failures. A Member of Parliament utilized Right to Information requests to scrutinize a major asset sale, arguing that a bidding company lacked required tax compliance. This case underscores the necessity of accessible, verifiable data in ensuring accountability within state-owned enterprises. The focus on the Social Security and National Insurance Trust’s tender process reveals how hidden irregularities can undermine fair competition. By demanding full evaluation reports and committee minutes, the lawmaker emphasizes that complete financial records must be available for public scrutiny. Such openness prevents conflicts of interest and ensures that public assets are managed according to established legal and procedural standards. Relevant to open data, this incident demonstrates the practical impact of releasing government information. When tax records and tender documents are accessible, citizens and officials can verify compliance effectively. This promotes trust in institutional processes and encourages a culture where data serves as a tool for enforcing integrity and preventing misconduct in public procurement.
