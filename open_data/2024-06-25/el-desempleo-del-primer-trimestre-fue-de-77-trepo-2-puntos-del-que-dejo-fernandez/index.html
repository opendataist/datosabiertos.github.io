El desempleo del primer trimestre fue de 7,7%: trepó 2 puntos del que dejó Fernández

El desempleo del primer trimestre fue de 7,7%: trepó 2 puntos del que dejó Fernández

The article reveals that the unemployment rate rose to 7.7% in the first quarter of 2024, marking a significant increase under the new administration and reflecting the immediate impacts of an economic recession. This shift from previous low figures indicates a broader deterioration in labor conditions, with specific regions experiencing sharper contrasts between local economic resilience and decline. Demographic analysis highlights structural vulnerabilities, showing higher unemployment among women and a large proportion of long-term job seekers. Furthermore, the data exposes precarious employment conditions, as a substantial segment of salaried workers lacks pension deductions, underscoring the prevalence of informal or unregulated labor arrangements despite overall employment figures. This report is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical need for transparent, granular statistical access to monitor socio-economic trends. By making detailed breakdowns by region, gender, and employment type publicly available, institutions allow for rigorous public scrutiny and evidence-based policy debates, ensuring that citizens can verify claims and understand the real human cost of economic measures.
