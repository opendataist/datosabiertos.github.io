Impulso europeo a la IA: Meta, Hugging Face y Scaleway lanzan programa para Startups

Meta, Hugging Face, and Scaleway have launched the AI Startup Program, a strategic initiative designed to accelerate Europe’s artificial intelligence ecosystem. This four-month program provides selected startups with key resources, including technical mentorship, access to AI tools, and computing power. Its primary objective is to promote the adoption of open-source-based solutions, thereby strengthening Europe’s competitive capacity in a global market dominated by proprietary technologies. The relevance to the open data and open-source movement is fundamental, as the program specifically requires the use or migration toward open AI models. By providing infrastructure and technical expertise for these technologies, the collaborating companies demonstrate a tangible commitment to transparency and accessibility. This stands in contrast to trends toward algorithmic closure, fostering an environment where technological innovation is built on shared, verifiable foundations—essential for scientific integrity and user trust. This alliance between tech giants and academic incubators represents a replicable model of public-private support for the sustainable development of democratic technologies. By lowering entry barriers for European startups, it encourages the creation of tools that prioritize interoperability and user control. This direction is crucial for maintaining Europe’s digital sovereignty, ensuring that the future of AI is shaped by open, ethical, and collaborative principles, benefiting both the business sector and society as a whole.
