Alzas de chayote, naranja y plátano, lo que más impactó la inflación

Data from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) reveals the dynamics of inflation in May, highlighting significant price shifts in produce and services. This granular breakdown demonstrates how specific goods drive economic trends, offering essential transparency for consumers tracking living costs. Such detailed metrics are crucial for open data initiatives, allowing independent analysts to verify national statistics. By making these drivers public, the dataset supports accountability and fosters a deeper public understanding of inflationary pressures. Ultimately, accessible information empowers citizens to make informed financial decisions. This transparency aligns with open government principles, ensuring that economic indicators are not just reported but fully understood by the community.
