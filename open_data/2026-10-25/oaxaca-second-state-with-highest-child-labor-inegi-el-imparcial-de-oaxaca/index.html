Oaxaca, second state with highest child labor: INEGI - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

Oaxaca, second state with highest child labor: INEGI - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

The report reveals that Oaxaca is facing a severe child labor crisis, ranking as the second-highest entity in the country in terms of prevalence. This reality shows that one in five children and adolescents engages in inappropriate and hazardous economic or domestic activities, highlighting a systemic failure to protect their fundamental rights in the face of poverty and internal migration. The central criticism underscores the state government’s evasion of responsibility, as it attempts to shift blame onto other states and lacks an accurate census of the affected minors. Despite the establishment of municipal commissions, there is skepticism about their actual effectiveness, underscoring the urgent need to implement comprehensive policies that prioritize children’s well-being over bureaucratic procedures or geographic excuses. This analysis is crucial for the open data community, as it demonstrates the need for accurate, up-to-date, and disaggregated official information to monitor the fulfillment of human rights. Without transparency and access to reliable data, it is impossible to design effective interventions or hold society accountable for how this critical social issue is managed, highlighting the value of statistical indicators as tools for advocacy and social justice.
