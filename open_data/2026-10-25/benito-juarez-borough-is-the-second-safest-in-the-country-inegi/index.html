Benito Juárez borough is the second safest in the country: INEGI

Benito Juárez borough is the second safest in the country: INEGI

The text highlights that Benito Juárez is the safest jurisdiction in Mexico City, consolidating its leadership after multiple consecutive quarters. This status is grounded in favorable citizen perception and a notable reduction in high-impact crimes, reflecting the effectiveness of the public policies implemented. The security strategy focuses not only on police surveillance but also on the modernization of public spaces. The goal is to ensure that residents can enjoy their daily environment with peace of mind, integrating prevention and rapid response as key pillars to improve quality of life and foster family coexistence. This news is relevant to open data because it illustrates how official security statistics, such as those from INEGI, enable the measurement of the social impact of government initiatives. Furthermore, it underscores the importance of linking quantitative data with citizens’ subjective perceptions, demonstrating that access to transparent information is crucial for evaluating the effectiveness of urban security policies and their direct influence on neighborhood well-being.
