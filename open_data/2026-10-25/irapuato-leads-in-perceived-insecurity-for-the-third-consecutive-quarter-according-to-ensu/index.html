Irapuato leads in perceived insecurity for the third consecutive quarter, according to ENSU

Irapuato leads in perceived insecurity for the third consecutive quarter, according to ENSU

Although Irapuato continues to rank first nationally in citizens’ perception of insecurity, local authorities interpret the INEGI data as a positive indicator. They highlight a sustained downward trend that suggests a gradual recovery of public confidence, driven by the security strategies implemented during the last quarter. The improvement in the perception of safety is tangible in key everyday spaces, such as markets and workplaces, thanks to the strengthened police presence. Moreover, favorable expectations about future security are rising, reflecting concrete advances in the tranquility of families and consolidating the city’s social dynamics despite persistent challenges. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical need to contextualize open statistics. Citizens should not limit themselves to superficial rankings; it is essential to analyze multiple dimensions, such as official crime figures and their temporal evolution, to obtain a comprehensive view. True transparency requires that data be interpreted with nuance, avoiding one-dimensional readings that obscure documented progress or ignore real challenges.
