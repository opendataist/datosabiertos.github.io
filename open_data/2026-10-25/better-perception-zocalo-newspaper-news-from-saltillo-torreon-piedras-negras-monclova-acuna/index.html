The article highlights the significant perceived reduction in insecurity rates in Torreón, attributed to coordination among authorities and the government’s strategy. This success in public safety underscores the need to use official data, such as the ENESPU, to validate effective policies and redirect resources toward prevention and police equipment. In parallel, the piece addresses institutional renewal with the likely appointment of a new municipal comptroller, selected for his technical profile and ability to streamline administration without political interference. This process emphasizes the importance of transparency and strict adherence to requirements in the selection of key officials, which are fundamental to accountability. Finally, uncertainty is noted regarding possible changes in the leadership of public works, an area in which the mayor maintains direct oversight. Taken together, the text illustrates how municipal management relies on the integration of statistical evidence, sound administrative processes, and active supervision to ensure measurable results and foster public trust in government institutions.
