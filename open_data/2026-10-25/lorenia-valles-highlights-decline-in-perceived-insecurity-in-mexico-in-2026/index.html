Lorenia Valles highlights decline in perceived insecurity in Mexico in 2026

Lorenia Valles highlights decline in perceived insecurity in Mexico in 2026

Perceptions of insecurity in Mexico show a slight downward trend according to recent data, which authorities interpret as a positive reflection of coordinated efforts across levels of government. This shift suggests an incremental improvement in citizens’ confidence in the recently implemented security strategies. Indicators reveal that public recognition of federal forces remains at high levels, reinforcing the notion that security institutions are gaining legitimacy among the population. This positive assessment indicates that current actions are resonating favorably with citizens, fostering a sense of greater social tranquility. This information is relevant to the open data movement because it demonstrates how government surveys generate tangible evidence on governance. Public access to these data allows civil society to independently verify state performance, promoting transparency and facilitating critical analysis based on real evidence regarding public security.
