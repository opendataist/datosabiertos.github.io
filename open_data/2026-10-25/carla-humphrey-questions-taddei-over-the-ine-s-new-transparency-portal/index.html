Carla Humphrey questions Taddei over the INE's new transparency portal

Carla Humphrey questions Taddei over the INE's new transparency portal

Counselor Carla Humphrey accused Guadalupe Taddei of centralizing power within the presidency of the National Electoral Institute (INE), acting unconstitutionally by failing to involve other members in the creation of the new transparency portal and by withholding critical information regarding personnel appointments. This lack of internal communication and transparency has drawn fundamental criticism regarding institutional governance. The conflict highlights the tension between the public visibility of data and its actual accessibility for internal oversight bodies. Humphrey argues that although a transparency system exists, it does not compensate for the opacity in decision-making and human talent management, which are essential elements for the democratic legitimacy of electoral processes. This situation is relevant to open data because it demonstrates that publishing information is insufficient without internal accountability and equitable participation in management. True transparency requires decentralized structures and genuine access to information, not merely web portals, thereby ensuring the integrity and trustworthiness of public institutions.
