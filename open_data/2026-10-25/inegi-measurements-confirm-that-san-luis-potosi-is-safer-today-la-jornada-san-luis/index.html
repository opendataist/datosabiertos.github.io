INEGI measurements confirm that San Luis Potosí is safer today - La Jornada San Luis

INEGI measurements confirm that San Luis Potosí is safer today - La Jornada San Luis

The article highlights a significant reduction in the perception of insecurity in San Luis Potosí, backed by official data that confirm the success of the comprehensive strategies implemented by the state government. This decline in indicators of violence and fear of crime demonstrates a tangible improvement in urban coexistence, positioning the state below the national average. The relevance of this text to open data lies in how public statistics from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) allow for verification of the effectiveness of government policies and greater transparency regarding citizen security. By using official and measurable sources, it promotes accountability and access to verifiable information on social well-being. Thus, the availability of these data facilitates independent analysis, enabling citizens and experts to compare government actions with actual outcomes. This transparency is essential for strengthening trust in institutions and ensuring that public decisions are based on concrete, accessible evidence.
