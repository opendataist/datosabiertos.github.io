Puebla ranks fourth in perceived insecurity

Puebla ranks fourth in perceived insecurity

The perception of insecurity in Puebla far exceeds the national average, ranking among the highest in the country. This finding underscores the urgent need to release official data on urban security, enabling more precise and comparative diagnoses. Transparency in these indicators is crucial for civil society and public officials to grasp the true magnitude of the problem. At the same time, records show a significant decline in direct conflicts reported by the population, bringing them closer to national figures. This divergence between subjective perception and objective experience reveals the complexity of the security phenomenon. Access to this disaggregated information facilitates the analysis of underlying causes that disconnect the sense of vulnerability from actual incidents. The relevance of this text lies in demonstrating the value of open national surveys. By providing accessible and comparable statistics, it strengthens accountability and evidence-based decision-making. This promotes more effective public policies and encourages informed citizen participation in seeking solutions to insecurity.
