Casi 2 mil empresas de la construcción cerraron en el sexenio de AMLO | Revista Punto de Vista

Casi 2 mil empresas de la construcción cerraron en el sexenio de AMLO | Revista Punto de Vista

The Mexican construction sector experienced significant contractions due to the impacts of the pandemic and strategic policy shifts that favored military-led projects over private firms. This environment severely disadvantaged small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), leading to a notable decline in the number of active businesses and raising concerns about market exclusivity and economic resilience. This case underscores the critical need for transparent public procurement processes and accessible data on government contracting. Open data initiatives can reveal systemic barriers faced by smaller enterprises, enabling a better analysis of how public spending decisions affect market diversity and overall economic health. Understanding these dynamics is essential for advocating inclusive economic policies. By making infrastructure allocation data open and granular, stakeholders can identify inequalities, fostering a more competitive environment that supports sustainable growth for all private sector participants, rather than concentrating opportunities among specific entities.
