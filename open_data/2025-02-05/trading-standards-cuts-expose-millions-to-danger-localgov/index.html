Trading standards cuts expose millions to danger | LocalGov

This investigation highlights severe, uneven reductions in trading standards staffing across the UK, creating a "postcode lottery" where consumer protection varies drastically by location. Many councils now lack the personnel to investigate complaints, rendering enforcement services largely unfit for purpose. The findings underscore the critical need for transparent, accessible public data regarding local government resource allocation. Open data empowers citizens to identify these disparities, hold authorities accountable, and demand equitable standards regardless of their geographic location. Without robust open data initiatives, systemic inequalities in public services remain hidden from view. Transparency enables evidence-based advocacy for fair resource distribution, ensuring that consumer protection is a universal right rather than a privilege determined by regional budget cuts.
