Este es el sueldo mínimo que debes cobrar en 2025 para ser de clase media

The recent adjustment to the minimum wage in Mexico aims to reduce economic disparities, yet data reveals that the majority of the population remains in lower socioeconomic strata. This highlights the limited impact of wage increases on structural inequality, as most citizens still lack the financial security necessary to achieve a stable middle-class status. Socioeconomic classification extends beyond simple monthly earnings, incorporating household composition, regional living costs, and inflation effects. This multifaceted approach underscores that financial stability depends on broader contextual factors rather than just gross income, making social mobility significantly more complex for vulnerable populations. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of accessible, comprehensive statistics to understand real-world economic realities. By integrating diverse variables into public datasets, policymakers and researchers can better analyze poverty dynamics and design more effective social interventions.
