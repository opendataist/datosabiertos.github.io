Yotta launches India's first sovereign B2C AI chatbot 'myShakti'

Yotta launches India's first sovereign B2C AI chatbot 'myShakti'

Yotta Data Services has launched 'myShakti,' India’s first sovereign B2C generative AI chatbot, marking a significant milestone for the 'Make in India' initiative. By utilizing an open-source model hosted entirely on Indian servers, the platform ensures data security and national sovereignty, directly supporting the government’s vision for an indigenous, affordable, and safe AI infrastructure. This development highlights the critical importance of localizing AI infrastructure to maintain control over digital assets. The rapid deployment of this solution demonstrates that domestic entities can quickly build powerful, secure AI tools using existing open-source frameworks, reinforcing India’s emergence as a reliable technological powerhouse focused on ethical AI solutions. For the open_data community, this launch underscores the practical viability of leveraging open-source models to achieve technological self-reliance. It validates the strategy of combining open algorithms with localized, secure hosting to build trustworthy public utilities, setting a precedent for how open technology can be scaled responsibly within national boundaries without compromising on speed or capability.
