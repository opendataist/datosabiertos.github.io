La IA y las ideologías: Progresismo, libertarismo y labatalla por el relato digital

Artificial intelligence is never neutral, as every model inevitably reflects the ideologies and decisions of its creators. The article highlights the fundamental tension between two dominant approaches: “progressive” AIs, which prioritize inclusion and equity, and libertarian visions, which emphasize unfettered freedom of expression. Both systems possess inherent biases; while the former may fall into indoctrination and the censorship of certain perspectives, the latter risk the spread of misinformation and hate speech. The real challenge does not lie in finding a perfect or entirely objective AI, but in recognizing these inherent limitations and using technology critically. Rather than seeking an impossible neutrality, we must accept that digital tools are fragmented mirrors of human reality. The key lies in balancing openness for dialogue with the protection of vulnerable groups, avoiding both excessive control and the chaos resulting from a lack of regulation. This debate is crucial for the open-data community because it underscores that the quality and ethics of datasets determine the social impact of artificial intelligence. Promoting transparency about biases in data and fostering critical access to technology enable citizens to understand and question the digital narratives imposed upon them. Ultimately, real progress is measured by our ability to use AI as a tool that builds a fairer and more equitable society, recognizing that ethical responsibility rests with users and designers, not with the machine itself.
