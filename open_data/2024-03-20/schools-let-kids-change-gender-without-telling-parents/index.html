Schools let kids change gender without telling parents

Welsh schools are increasingly permitting students to change gender pronouns without mandatory parental notification, a practice raising significant safety concerns among advocacy groups. This lack of transparency has prompted the government to draft new guidance aimed at balancing student inclusion with family involvement, ensuring a positive educational experience for all young people. This situation is crucial for open_data because it highlights how FOI requests can expose hidden institutional policies and reveal gaps between official practices and public accountability. By making such operational details visible, data transparency supports civil society in demanding clearer standards and ensuring that sensitive educational policies are subject to public scrutiny and democratic oversight rather than remaining opaque.
