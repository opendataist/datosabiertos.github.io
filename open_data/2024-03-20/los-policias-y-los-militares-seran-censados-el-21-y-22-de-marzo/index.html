Los policías y los militares serán censados el 21 y 22 de marzo

The National Statistics Institute trained police and military personnel to carry out census operations within their units and to accompany census takers in high-risk areas. This initiative aims to ensure the safety of census workers while guaranteeing the accuracy of data collection. The collaboration underscores the critical role of security forces in preserving the integrity and safety of open data-gathering processes.
