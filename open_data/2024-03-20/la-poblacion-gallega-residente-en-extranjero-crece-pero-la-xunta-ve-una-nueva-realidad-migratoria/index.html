La población gallega residente en extranjero crece pero la Xunta ve una “nueva realidad migratoria”

La población gallega residente en extranjero crece pero la Xunta ve una “nueva realidad migratoria”

The demographic landscape of Galicians abroad is changing, characterized by a decline in direct emigrants but an increase in the total population due to the growth of locally born descendants. This structural shift transforms the diaspora into a key ally in combating Galicia’s internal demographic decline, offering a strategic resource that extends beyond immediate borders. This evolution underscores the importance of accurate, open population data in uncovering nuanced migration trends. By differentiating between emigrants and their descendants, authorities can better assess long-term community resilience and the effectiveness of repatriation initiatives such as Galicia Retorna, which has facilitated significant returns. Such detailed statistical transparency is essential for open data ecosystems, enabling policymakers to identify hidden strengths within dispersed populations. Recognizing these demographic shifts allows for more effective strategies that leverage global connections to address local challenges, demonstrating how accessible data drives informed social and economic planning.
