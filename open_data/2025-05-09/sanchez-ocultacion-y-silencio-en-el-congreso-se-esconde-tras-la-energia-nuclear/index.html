Sánchez, ocultación y silencio en el Congreso: se esconde tras la energía nuclear

The Prime Minister’s response to the major electricity blackout and the defense strategy reveals a critical lack of transparent information, prioritizing political distraction over factual accountability. This approach obscures the systemic causes of the energy crisis and ignores urgent security needs, failing to provide citizens with the concrete data and clear timelines they deserve during complex emergencies. Such opacity is particularly relevant to open data, because democratic trust relies on accessible, precise, and timely information from public institutions. When governments withhold specifics on infrastructure failures or strategic plans, they undermine civic oversight and prevent evidence-based public discourse. True transparency requires releasing actionable datasets and clear explanations, not using divisive topics as smokescreens to evade responsibility for critical national issues.
