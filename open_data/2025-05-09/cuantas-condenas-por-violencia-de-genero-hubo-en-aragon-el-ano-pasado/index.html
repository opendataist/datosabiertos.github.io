Cuántas condenas por violencia de género hubo en Aragón el año pasado

Cuántas condenas por violencia de género hubo en Aragón el año pasado

The National Statistics Institute recently published comprehensive data on gender-based and domestic violence for 2024, revealing significant trends in convictions across Spain. The report highlights a substantial increase in penalties in regions such as Aragon, where convictions reached record highs compared to previous years. This surge underscores the evolving nature of judicial enforcement and the persistent challenges in addressing violence against women and domestic abuse nationwide. An analysis of demographic breakdowns shows that the majority of convicted individuals fall within the 25 to 44 age group. A comparison of regional data indicates that larger autonomous communities, such as Andalusia and Catalonia, handle higher volumes of cases, while smaller territories report significantly fewer convictions. These variations reflect population densities and local reporting mechanisms rather than solely reflecting differences in the prevalence of violence. This dataset is crucial for open data initiatives because it provides transparent, granular statistics essential for public scrutiny and policy evaluation. By making detailed information on victims and perpetrators accessible, researchers and citizens can analyze trends, hold institutions accountable, and develop targeted interventions to combat violence effectively.
