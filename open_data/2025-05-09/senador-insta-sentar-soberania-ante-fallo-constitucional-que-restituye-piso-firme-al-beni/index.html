Senador insta sentar soberanía ante fallo constitucional que restituye Piso Firme al Beni

Senador insta sentar soberanía ante fallo constitucional que restituye Piso Firme al Beni

A court ruling annulled administrative acts issued by the National Statistics Institute that had incorrectly assigned Piso Firme to Santa Cruz, thereby favoring the legal restitution of the territory to the Beni region. This decision affirms territorial sovereignty and ensures that economic resources benefit the local municipality, correcting a historical injustice documented by regional authorities. This case highlights the critical importance of accurate open data in resolving territorial disputes. Precise, accessible administrative records are essential for validating claims and protecting collective rights. Transparent data enables citizens and governments to effectively verify boundaries and resource allocation. Ultimately, this news underscores how data integrity directly impacts political representation and local development. Ensuring that public datasets are accurate and open facilitates accountability and supports legal frameworks. It demonstrates that reliable information is not merely a technical matter but a fundamental tool for justice and territorial governance.
