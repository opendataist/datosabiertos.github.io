UAE ranks 10th globally in Open Data Inventory report

UAE ranks 10th globally in Open Data Inventory report

The UAE has achieved a historic position as the tenth-ranked nation globally in the 2024/2025 Open Data Inventory, surpassing prominent digital leaders such as Sweden, Germany, and the United States. This significant milestone underscores the country’s robust commitment to digital government strategies and its transition toward becoming a global reference for data-driven decision-making and transparency. The country’s success stems from comprehensive investments in legislative frameworks, digital infrastructure, and statistical systems across critical sectors like health, trade, and environment. By adhering to international standards for machine-readable and unrestricted data access, the UAE demonstrates how strategic digital readiness and inter-agency integration can elevate national competitiveness and support future policy planning. This achievement is highly relevant to open data because it validates the tangible benefits of removing restrictions on public statistics. It illustrates how transparent, accessible data ecosystems empower organizations and individuals to innovate freely, fostering greater community participation and enabling more effective, evidence-based governance worldwide.
