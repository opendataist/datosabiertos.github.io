Más de 100 millones usan Internet - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

Más de 100 millones usan Internet - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

Mexico’s digital landscape has expanded significantly, with over 100 million internet users and an eight percent increase in adoption since 2021. This growth highlights a persistent urban-rural divide, indicating that while connectivity is widespread, equitable access across different regions remains a critical challenge for inclusive development. The home has solidified as the primary access point, although mobile usage is growing alongside new devices such as smart TVs. Demographically, younger populations dominate connectivity, while older adults lag behind. This shift in usage patterns underscores the urgent need for targeted digital literacy programs and infrastructure improvements to support diverse age groups and ensure no segment of society is left behind. This data is vital to open data initiatives because it reveals granular gaps in access and usage behavior. Policymakers can leverage these insights to design more effective, inclusive digital strategies. By making such detailed statistics freely available, governments and researchers can better address connectivity disparities, fostering a more equitable digital ecosystem for all citizens.
