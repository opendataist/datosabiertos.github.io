SAS supercharges Viya platform with AI agents, copilots, and synthetic data tools

SAS introduces new governance resources to help organizations assess and improve their AI maturity across oversight, compliance, operations, and culture. This shift addresses early mistakes that prioritized speed over control, aiming for a more balanced and responsible approach to AI innovation. The company is developing a unified solution for executives to aggregate, orchestrate, and monitor AI systems, signaling a move toward ethical calibrations that enable tailored human oversight. Analysts approve of this direction, noting it fosters long-term resilience and enterprise value through ethically sound systems. This development is relevant to open_data as it highlights the growing need for robust governance frameworks to manage data-driven AI responsibly. It underscores how structured oversight and ethical standards are essential for ensuring transparency and accountability in modern data ecosystems.
