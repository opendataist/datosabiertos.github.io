Exclusiva: INE denuncia ante FGR posible venta de datos biométricos de millones de mexicanos

Exclusiva: INE denuncia ante FGR posible venta de datos biométricos de millones de mexicanos

The National Electoral Institute (INE) has filed a formal complaint with the Specialized Prosecutor’s Office for Electoral Crimes (FISEL) over the alleged illegal sale of biometric data belonging to millions of Mexican citizens. This legal action marks a crucial step toward ensuring transparency and accountability in the handling of sensitive information, which are fundamental aspects in defending ethical open data and protecting citizens’ privacy against the clandestine commercialization of public records. Although the electoral body concluded internally that there is no recent evidence of breaches to its systems and attributes the incident to fraud committed by cybercriminals, security experts have questioned this conclusion by pointing to the quality of the information provided by the perpetrators. This discrepancy underscores the critical need for independent, collaborative external audit mechanisms between the public sector and civil society, ensuring that data integrity is not left solely in the hands of the institutions that manage it. The case highlights the urgency of strengthening regulatory frameworks and cybersecurity measures within public administration, as the leak of biometric data compromises fundamental rights and trust in democratic institutions. Updating personal data protection regulations not only helps prevent future breaches but also sets a precedent for how authorities should balance mandatory transparency with the effective protection of citizens’ digital identities against organized crime.
