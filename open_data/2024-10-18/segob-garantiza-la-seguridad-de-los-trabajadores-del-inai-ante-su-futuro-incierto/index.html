Segob garantiza la seguridad de los trabajadores del INAI ante su futuro incierto

Rumors of merging or abolishing the INAI have sparked anxiety, yet the meeting with the Secretary of the Interior brought temporary relief. The official expressed openness and pledged to protect the institute’s current operations and staff stability, in contrast to broader political pressures to dismantle autonomous bodies by 2025. This situation underscores the fragility of open data institutions when political agendas shift toward centralization. The INAI’s mandate to guarantee transparency depends on institutional autonomy, making its potential dissolution a direct threat to citizens’ right to access government information. The immediate concern is not merely administrative restructuring but the preservation of legal mechanisms that enforce transparency. If autonomous bodies are eliminated, the independent oversight necessary for an open society would disappear, undermining the foundational principles of accountable governance and public access to data.
