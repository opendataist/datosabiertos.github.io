HopeJR: el robot humanoide de código abierto que podrías tener en casa por menos de 3.000 dólares

HopeJR: el robot humanoide de código abierto que podrías tener en casa por menos de 3.000 dólares

Hugging Face introduces HopeJR, an open-source, low-cost humanoid robot, marking a turning point in the democratization of personal robotics. Its open philosophy enables anyone or any team to modify, assemble, and enhance the device, avoiding dependence on proprietary and opaque technologies. This ensures that knowledge and innovation capabilities are not monopolized by large corporations, but instead become a shareable and accessible resource for all. The relevance to open data lies in its role as a physical development platform for artificial intelligence. Like previous projects such as Arduino or Linux, HopeJR fosters a community of creators who leverage transparency and collaboration to drive progress. This approach eliminates technological "black boxes," promoting a culture where the functioning of devices is verifiable and improvable by the public, which is essential for trust and security in today's digital ecosystem. In addition to HopeJR, Hugging Face introduces Reachy Mini, an equally accessible interactive device that serves as an educational interface for testing AI applications. Together, these robots aim to transform robotics from a corporate luxury into a practical tool for research, education, and home automation. This initiative represents a crucial ethical and technical commitment, ensuring that the benefits of artificial intelligence and robotics advance alongside inclusion, transparency, and collective innovation capacity in society.
