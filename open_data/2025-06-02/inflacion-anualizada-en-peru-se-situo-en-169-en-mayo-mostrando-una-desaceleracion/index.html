Inflación anualizada en Perú se situó en 1,69% en mayo, mostrando una desaceleración

Peru maintains price stability, with inflation well within the central bank’s target range. This moderation signals economic relief and confirms a historic low-pressure environment on local prices. Such stability supports the country’s broader economic goals. The central bank projects that this favorable trend will persist, ensuring continued macroeconomic balance throughout 2025. This article highlights the critical role of high-quality official statistics in tracking economic indicators. Reliable open data allows policymakers and citizens to monitor inflation trends effectively, fostering transparency and informed decision-making in public administration.
