Northern Ireland Economic Output Statistics

Northern Ireland Economic Output Statistics

Northern Ireland’s economic landscape is currently defined by a stark divergence between its robust service sector and a struggling production industry. Service output has surged to record highs, significantly outperforming pre-pandemic levels and demonstrating strong resilience compared to the wider UK. This growth is primarily driven by gains in business services, finance, and retail trade, indicating a shift toward a knowledge-based and consumer-driven economy that continues to expand despite broader macroeconomic challenges. In sharp contrast, the production sector has contracted both quarterly and annually, failing to regain its pre-pandemic strength. This decline is largely attributed to sharp drops in manufacturing and mining activities, highlighting vulnerabilities in Northern Ireland’s industrial base. While the services sector celebrates growth, the production sector remains below previous benchmarks, suggesting that the region’s economic recovery is uneven and heavily dependent on service-oriented industries rather than traditional manufacturing or resource extraction. This report is relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the importance of granular, sector-specific economic metrics for transparent governance. By providing detailed breakdowns of performance across diverse industries, such data allows researchers and policymakers to identify structural imbalances and target interventions effectively. The availability of these statistics fosters accountability and enables data-driven decision-making, ensuring that economic strategies reflect the actual health and needs of different parts of the local economy.
