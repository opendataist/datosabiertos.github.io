Temples Cannot Be Excluded From RTI: Karnataka High Court

Temples Cannot Be Excluded From RTI: Karnataka High Court

Karnataka High Court ruled that temples under state endowment are public authorities subject to the Right to Information Act. This decision ensures these institutions remain accountable to the public, rejecting claims that such transparency burdens religious personnel. The ruling confirms that accessing information does not interfere with priestly duties. By classifying these temples as public entities, the court mandates adherence to legal transparency standards similar to other state-run organizations. This highlights the critical role of open data in governing religious assets managed by the state. It reinforces that public oversight applies to religious endowments, ensuring fiscal and administrative accountability. Ultimately, it strengthens the principle that state-managed public resources must remain accessible for scrutiny, promoting broader transparency in religious governance.
