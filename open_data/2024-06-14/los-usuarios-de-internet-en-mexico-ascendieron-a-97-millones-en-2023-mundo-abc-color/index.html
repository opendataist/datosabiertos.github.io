Los usuarios de internet en México ascendieron a 97 millones en 2023 - Mundo - ABC Color

Los usuarios de internet en México ascendieron a 97 millones en 2023 - Mundo - ABC Color

The recent report underscores that information and communication technologies have become as crucial for societal development as natural resources. It highlights a significant expansion in internet usage, adding millions of new users compared to previous years. However, this progress masks a persistent digital divide, particularly between urban and rural areas, where access rates still vary drastically. This disparity remains a critical challenge for achieving true technological equity. Demographic analysis reveals shifting patterns in connectivity. While young adults dominate usage time and frequency, the gender gap has nearly disappeared, and senior citizens have shown the most notable growth in adoption. Interestingly, usage among younger children has slightly declined post-pandemic, suggesting a stabilization rather than continuous growth in early digital immersion. These trends indicate that while adoption is broadening, the nature and intensity of usage differ significantly across age groups. This article is vital for open data because it provides the statistical evidence needed to evaluate public policy effectiveness. It exposes the failure of initiatives aimed at universal access, linking concrete data to the political promise of free internet. By quantifying regional and social inequalities, the data offers an essential benchmark for accountability. Open data thus serves not just as a record of current statistics, but as a tool to measure the real-world impact of government commitments on citizens' digital rights.
