▷ Edmundo González: Nuestro objetivo es ganar el 28 de julio y en eso estamos enfocados #13Jun - El Impulso

Edmundo González, leader of the Venezuelan opposition, firmly believes in his electoral victory, highlighting the preparation of a massive network of civilian monitors as the primary mechanism to ensure transparency and reliable results. By replacing international observation with a national monitoring deployment, the strategy seeks to safeguard the integrity of the electoral process through active citizen participation, reflecting a shift in electoral oversight methods in the absence of recognized external bodies. The campaign is grounded in the unity of the opposition platform and the support of key figures such as María Corina Machado, who mobilize millions of citizens under a discourse of national reconciliation rather than confrontation. This coalition assumes that the magnitude of its victory will open the door to negotiations with the incumbent government, establishing a clear roadmap for political transition and institutional recovery, despite initial doubts about potential legal barriers to González’s candidacy. This case is relevant to the field of open data, as it demonstrates how electoral transparency and result verification critically depend on access to robust, decentralized, and real-time electoral information. The initiative to use civil registries and community-based surveillance systems underscores the need for citizens to have tools to audit democratic processes when traditional institutions or international oversight mechanisms are limited or absent.
