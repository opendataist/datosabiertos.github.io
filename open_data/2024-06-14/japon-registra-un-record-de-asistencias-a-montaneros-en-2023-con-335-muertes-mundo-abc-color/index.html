Japón registra un récord de asistencias a montañeros en 2023, con 335 muertes - Mundo - ABC Color

Japan reports record numbers of mountain rescues, highlighting a critical trend in which easily accessible peaks are facing overwhelming demand. This surge disproportionately affects elderly tourists and a growing number of foreign visitors, exposing significant gaps in risk assessment and preparedness among casual hikers. New regulations mandating reservations, fees, and mandatory overnight stays aim to alleviate congestion and improve safety. These measures reflect a shift from unrestricted access to managed entry, acknowledging that high visibility does not equate to technical competence among visitors. This situation is vital for open-data initiatives, as it underscores the need for transparent, accessible datasets on tourism flows and public safety. By analyzing such statistics, governments and developers can create better tools for crowd management and resource allocation, ensuring that data drives sustainable and safe recreational practices.
