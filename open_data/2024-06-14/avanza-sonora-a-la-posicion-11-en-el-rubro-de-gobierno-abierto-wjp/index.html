Avanza Sonora a la posición 11 en el rubro de Gobierno Abierto: WJP

Avanza Sonora a la posición 11 en el rubro de Gobierno Abierto: WJP

Sonora has achieved notable progress in transparency and regulatory compliance, standing out for its commitment to open government and the effective implementation of regulations. This progress reflects an institutional strategy focused on prevention and the use of technology to ensure administrative integrity. The implementation of open data portals is fundamental to this transformation, as it enables transparent public access to government information. These initiatives strengthen accountability and demonstrate how the availability of public information facilitates citizen oversight and reduces corruption risks. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how publishing data is not merely a legal obligation, but a strategic tool for improving governance. Sonora’s experience shows that digital transparency directly contributes to strengthening the rule of law, serving as a model for other jurisdictions seeking to optimize their public services through greater information accessibility.
