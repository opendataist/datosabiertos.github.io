Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

This article is a privacy notice issued by 13 MEDIA, S.C., designed to inform users about how their personal data is collected and processed. It details the specific types of information gathered, ranging from basic identity details to financial data, and outlines the legal obligations and service requirements that drive this collection. The document emphasizes adherence to principles such as lawfulness, proportionality, and consent, ensuring that data handling remains transparent and compliant with federal laws governing private entities. The notice explains that data is primarily used internally to verify identity and fulfill service requirements, with limited sharing reserved for legal professionals or authorities when mandated. Users are provided with clear mechanisms to limit information disclosure or exercise their rights to access, rectify, cancel, or object to the processing of their data. These actions can be initiated through direct contact with the designated privacy officer, highlighting the company’s commitment to user control and accountability in data management. This document is relevant to open data discussions as it represents a critical boundary: the strict protection of personal information that must remain closed or anonymized before any public dissemination. It underscores the legal and ethical framework that prevents raw personal data from becoming open data, ensuring that privacy rights are respected even as organizations seek transparency in their operations. Understanding such notices is essential for distinguishing between publicly available organizational data and sensitive personal information that must be safeguarded.
