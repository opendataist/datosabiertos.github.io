Dua Lipa, Elton John, Robbie Williams y más de 400 artistas británicos piden protección ante el avance de la IA | RPP Noticias

Dua Lipa, Elton John, Robbie Williams y más de 400 artistas británicos piden protección ante el avance de la IA | RPP Noticias

British artists are demanding transparency from artificial intelligence companies regarding the use of copyrighted data. They are calling on these companies to disclose whether they have used human-created works to train generative models, thereby safeguarding intellectual property as a cornerstone of economic and creative value. This initiative is crucial for the open data movement, as it highlights that transparency in datasets is not merely a technical issue but an ethical one. Without disclosing training sources, the origin of the knowledge being used is concealed, which hinders auditing and undermines respect for original creators. The lack of clear regulation threatens the sustainability of the cultural industry. If the provenance of data is not guaranteed, the opportunity to balance technological innovation with human rights is lost, weakening the United Kingdom’s global standing as a creative power in the face of corporate dominance.
