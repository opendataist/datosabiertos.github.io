Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has enhanced its Ibrahim Index of African Governance portal, offering a more interactive way to explore comprehensive governance data across 54 African nations. This updated platform allows users to analyze country profiles and compare performances against continental averages over a ten-year period. By enabling detailed comparisons of health, rule of law, and economic opportunity, the tool helps citizens and researchers identify trends in political, social, and environmental public goods delivery. For the open data community, this initiative demonstrates how complex governance metrics can be democratized through accessible, visual interfaces. The ability to drill down from broad categories to specific indicators empowers civil society organizations to hold governments accountable with evidence-based insights. This transparency supports informed civic engagement and facilitates more effective benchmarking of public service delivery across the continent. Ultimately, this resource underscores the critical role of open, standardized datasets in promoting good governance. By making longitudinal performance data readily available, the foundation fosters a culture of accountability and continuous improvement. It serves as a vital model for how open data platforms can bridge the gap between statistical analysis and actionable civic oversight in developing regions.
