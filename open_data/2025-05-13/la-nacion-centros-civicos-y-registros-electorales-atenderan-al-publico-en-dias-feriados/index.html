La Nación / Centros Cívicos y Registros Electorales atenderán al público en días feriados

La Nación / Centros Cívicos y Registros Electorales atenderán al público en días feriados

The Electoral Justice system announces public access to voter registration and data updates at civic centers. This initiative ensures that citizens can maintain accurate records in the Permanent Civic Register, a crucial step for transparent and reliable democratic processes. At the same time, the accessible voting program expands participation by offering home-based voting and voting at accessible tables. These measures remove barriers for individuals with disabilities, promoting inclusive electoral practices that align with open data principles regarding accessibility and equal opportunity. By enabling online verification and in-person updates, the system enhances data integrity and citizen trust. This transparency is vital for open data ecosystems, as it ensures that public records are current, verifiable, and accessible, fostering greater accountability in electoral administration and civic engagement.
