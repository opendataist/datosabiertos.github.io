En el primer semestre, los argentinos consumieron 16 millones de litros de vino menos que en 2023

En el primer semestre, los argentinos consumieron 16 millones de litros de vino menos que en 2023

The article highlights a significant decline in wine consumption and sales in Argentina during the first half of the year, marking a continued negative trend despite a slight acceleration in the rate of decrease compared to earlier periods. This downturn affects both the domestic market and exports, indicating a broad contraction in the industry’s performance rather than isolated fluctuations. Key segments such as non-varietal wines and bottled formats experienced the sharpest drops, while sparkling wines saw a drastic reduction in volume. Although export volumes for non-varietal wines improved, the overall export value fell due to declining sales of varietal wines, which dominate international trade, and a decrease in average prices. This suggests that external markets are currently unable to compensate for the weakened domestic demand. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of accessing granular, official statistical records to understand complex economic trends. By analyzing detailed breakdowns by product type and packaging, stakeholders can identify specific vulnerabilities within the supply chain. Open access to such precise metrics enables researchers and policymakers to move beyond aggregate figures, fostering a deeper understanding of consumer behavior and industrial health for more informed decision-making.
