AudioUK seeks Government support for podcast and audiobook tax relief

AudioUK seeks Government support for podcast and audiobook tax relief

AudioUK is advocating for government tax relief for the UK podcast and audiobook sectors to attract international investment. By conducting an anonymized survey, they aim to update evidence presented to the new government, highlighting the industry's growth potential. The initiative seeks parity with existing tax incentives in film and TV. This data-driven approach strengthens the argument for policy changes that support the creative audio economy during the upcoming budget discussions. This is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how industry groups leverage anonymous, aggregated survey results to influence public policy. It highlights the importance of accessible, shared metrics in building compelling cases for economic support and regulatory alignment within creative industries.
