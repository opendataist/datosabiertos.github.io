Attorney accuses FBI of deceptive practices in Oklahoma City bombing records case – NaturalNews.com

Attorney accuses FBI of deceptive practices in Oklahoma City bombing records case – NaturalNews.com

Jesse Trentadue, a Utah attorney investigating the 1995 Oklahoma City bombing, alleges that the FBI has misled the court regarding the volume and accessibility of records concerning key figures like Roger Edwin Moore and the Aryan Republican Army. He contends that the Bureau’s assertion that relevant documents are publicly available on its website is factually incorrect, as the posted pages represent only a tiny fraction of the responsive material they claim to possess. Furthermore, Trentadue disputes the FBI’s interpretation of a document release agreement, arguing they are withholding records rather than providing them in the agreed-upon incremental batches. This dispute highlights a critical challenge in open data initiatives: the gap between claims of transparency and actual public accessibility. When government agencies assert that information is open while selectively releasing only sanitized or minimal datasets, it undermines public trust and hinders independent verification. Trentadue’s frustration illustrates how bureaucratic delays and misleading statements can effectively block meaningful access to historical records, preventing journalists and researchers from conducting thorough investigations into significant historical events. The article is relevant to open data because it serves as a cautionary tale about the importance of full, unredacted data publication in accordance with Freedom of Information Act principles. It emphasizes that true transparency requires not just the existence of records, but their timely, complete, and honest release. Without strict accountability for data provision methods, agencies can exploit procedural loopholes to maintain secrecy, suggesting that open data policies must include robust oversight mechanisms to prevent obfuscation and ensure that digital public archives reflect the complete truth.
