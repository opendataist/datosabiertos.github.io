The report highlights a severe socio-economic decline in La Rioja, characterized by a sharp rise in poverty and destitution rates since 2019. This deterioration is closely linked to a stagnant private employment sector, suggesting that economic growth is not being sustainably generated within the market itself. Critically, the article argues that official employment figures are misleading because they primarily reflect public sector expansion rather than genuine economic health. The creation of inefficient state-owned companies serves to mask underlying structural issues, preventing a true assessment of the province’s financial well-being. This content is vital for open data because it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, verified statistics for accurate public accountability. Without clear, accessible data that distinguishes between public and private job creation, policymakers and citizens cannot effectively analyze or address the root causes of economic crises and social welfare failures.
