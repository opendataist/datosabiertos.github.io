El desbocado incremento poblacional

El desbocado incremento poblacional

The Balearic Islands are experiencing rapid population growth, largely driven by foreign migration, which is straining essential infrastructure and public services. This demographic surge underscores a critical tension between the region’s economic dependence on migrant labor and its capacity to sustain such expansion without triggering systemic collapse. This situation is relevant to open data because transparent, high-quality statistical records are essential for accurately measuring these trends. Reliable data enables policymakers to detect early signs of overcrowding and infrastructure failure, thereby facilitating informed decision-making rather than relying on anecdotal evidence. Ultimately, the article argues that ignoring population density in favor of focusing solely on tourism is insufficient. Open data serves as a vital tool for exposing these underlying pressures, supporting the urgent need to establish limits and implement sustainable management strategies to ensure long-term societal stability.
