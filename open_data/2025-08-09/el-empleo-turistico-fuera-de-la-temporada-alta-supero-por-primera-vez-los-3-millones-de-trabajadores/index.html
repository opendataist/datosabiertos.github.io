El empleo turístico fuera de la temporada alta superó por primera vez los 3 millones de trabajadores

The Spanish tourism sector reached a historic milestone by surpassing three million employed individuals outside of peak seasons, signaling a robust normalization of the labor market. This growth indicates that tourism has become a year-round economic pillar, contributing significantly to the stability of the national economy and reducing reliance on seasonal fluctuations. The data reveals a positive structural shift toward permanent employment. While temporary contracts still exist, the consistent rise in indefinite contracts and the decline in unemployment rates suggest an improvement in job quality. This trend highlights a maturing sector in which stable, long-term positions are replacing precarious work, offering greater security for workers and increased predictability for the industry. This report is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how transparent, publicly available statistical data enables accurate socioeconomic analysis. By making such detailed labor metrics accessible, researchers and policymakers can monitor sectoral health in real time, fostering evidence-based decisions that drive sustainable growth and underscore the importance of free access to government statistics.
