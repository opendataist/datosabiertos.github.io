Georgian trips abroad up 2.5% in Q2 2025

Georgian trips abroad up 2.5% in Q2 2025

Georgian outbound travel is rising, particularly among mid-aged demographics, indicating strong mobility trends. Despite more frequent visits, reduced expenditure per trip suggests travelers are optimizing costs. This data highlights a shift toward value-conscious tourism and repeat travel. Understanding these patterns helps identify emerging consumer behaviors and economic impacts on local services. For open data, this dataset offers valuable insights into migration and tourism flows. Transparent access allows researchers to analyze economic resilience and social dynamics based on real-time mobility statistics.
