Datos del INE señalan que Canelones es uno de los departamentos que proyecta crecimiento hacia 2045 - HOY CANELONES

Datos del INE señalan que Canelones es uno de los departamentos que proyecta crecimiento hacia 2045 - HOY CANELONES

Uruguay is undergoing a significant demographic shift characterized by substantial internal migration and structural aging. The data reveals that population growth is no longer centered in the capital, as residents are increasingly moving from Montevideo to neighboring departments such as Canelones and Maldonado. This redistribution reflects changing lifestyle preferences and urban dynamics, altering the traditional population distribution across the country. The article is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates how public statistical data can uncover and validate community perceptions regarding urban expansion and regional development. By providing transparent, longitudinal records of human movement, open data empowers policymakers and citizens to understand the real drivers behind local changes, such as increased traffic and infrastructure strain, rather than relying solely on anecdotal evidence. Looking ahead, the country faces a dual challenge of population decline in the working-age bracket and a rapid increase in life expectancy. This transition necessitates a reevaluation of social services and economic planning, as the ratio of retirees to workers will double. Understanding these trends is crucial for anticipating future demands on healthcare and pension systems, ensuring that public resources are allocated efficiently to support an increasingly elderly society.
