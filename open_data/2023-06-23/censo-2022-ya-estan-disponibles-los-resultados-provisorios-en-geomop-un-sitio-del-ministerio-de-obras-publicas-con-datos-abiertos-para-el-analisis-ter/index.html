Censo 2022: ya están disponibles los resultados provisorios en GEOMOP, un sitio del Ministerio de Obras Públicas con datos abiertos para el análisis territorial

The Ministry of Public Works has launched GEOMOP, a free, public platform that centralizes geospatial and open data to facilitate territorial analysis. By integrating provisional results from the 2022 National Census, the system provides updated socioeconomic and demographic layers that replace outdated information, enabling precise identification of underserved areas. This consolidation allows provincial and local governments to design, execute, and evaluate public works with greater strategic accuracy, ensuring interventions address current local realities rather than historical gaps. Relevant to open data, GEOMOP embodies transparency and efficiency by making diverse government statistics accessible to citizens and various administrative levels. It consolidates data from the Ministry, decentralized bodies, and other public sectors, ensuring all users work from a single source of truth. This approach supports the government’s digital transformation plan, fostering a more federal and transparent management paradigm through innovative technological solutions. Ultimately, the platform enhances decision-making by democratizing access to critical infrastructure and social indicators. By providing downloadable geographic layers, it empowers stakeholders to improve territorial planning and monitor public impact effectively. This initiative demonstrates how open data can bridge the gap between statistical reporting and practical application in public policy and infrastructure development.
