Más de 40.000 ciudadanos de Baleares residen oficialmente en el extranjero

The Balearic Islands show balanced emigration, mainly driven by professional and academic reasons to Europe and the Americas. Although it trails behind larger regions such as Galicia, this specific demographic data is crucial for open data initiatives. It allows for a detailed analysis of migration patterns, supporting better policy-making and resource allocation for isolated communities.
