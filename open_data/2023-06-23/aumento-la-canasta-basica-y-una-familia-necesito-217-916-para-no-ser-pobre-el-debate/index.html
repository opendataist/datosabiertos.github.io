Aumentó la canasta básica y una familia necesitó $217.916 para no ser pobre - EL DEBATE

The article highlights that recent inflation rates have outpaced the cost of basic food items and the total household basket in Argentina. This discrepancy indicates that, although official poverty lines are updated, they currently lag behind actual price surges, eroding purchasing power faster than these metrics reflect. For open data initiatives, this underscores the critical importance of real-time, high-frequency price monitoring alongside standard census data. Relying solely on periodic surveys can mask immediate economic hardships, necessitating more agile datasets to accurately capture the true extent of financial distress among vulnerable populations.
