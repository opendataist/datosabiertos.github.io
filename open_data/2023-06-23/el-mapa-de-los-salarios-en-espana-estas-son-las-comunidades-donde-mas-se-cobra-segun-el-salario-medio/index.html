El mapa de los salarios en España: estas son las comunidades donde más se cobra según el salario medio

The Spanish National Statistics Institute reveals significant regional disparities in average salaries, highlighting higher wages in the Basque Country, Madrid, and Navarra compared to Extremadura and Andalusia. This data exposes deep economic inequalities across autonomous communities, showing how geography drastically influences income levels for workers. Regarding growth, Extremadura and Cantabria led with substantial annual increases, while the Balearic Islands and Murcia showed minimal wage progression. These contrasting trends illustrate that economic recovery is not uniform, affecting different regions at varying paces and intensifying existing wealth gaps. This report is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical releases enable the public to identify systemic inequalities. By making such granular salary and regional data freely available, institutions empower citizens and researchers to demand policy changes that address these structural economic imbalances effectively.
