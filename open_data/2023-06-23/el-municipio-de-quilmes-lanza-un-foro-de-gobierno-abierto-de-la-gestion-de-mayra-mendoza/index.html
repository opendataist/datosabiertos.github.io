El municipio de Quilmes lanza un foro de gobierno abierto de la gestión de Mayra Mendoza

The Municipality of Quilmes implements the "Open Government Forum," an initiative that consolidates citizen participation and government transparency as central pillars of its management. This event aims to empower the community by creating thematic commissions that enable residents to propose improvements and oversee the use of public resources, ensuring that the administration is more accessible and responsive to local needs. In parallel, the municipality strengthens its commitment to digital transparency through the Open Data Portal. This resource provides information in accessible and reusable formats, facilitating public scrutiny and innovation. The combination of physical spaces for deliberation and digital platforms for information democratizes access to government data, allowing citizens to use this information to demand accountability and generate evidence-based proposals. This integration is fundamental to the open data ecosystem, as it demonstrates how the technical availability of information complements institutional mechanisms for advocacy. The significance lies in the fact that it not only publishes data but also creates a structured channel for its practical application in improving public services, establishing a model where proactive transparency drives collaborative and efficient governance.
