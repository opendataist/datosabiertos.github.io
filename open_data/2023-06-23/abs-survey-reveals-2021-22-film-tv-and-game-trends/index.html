ABS Survey Reveals 2021/22 Film, TV and Game Trends

The Australian Bureau of Statistics’ latest screen industry survey reveals significant economic growth and employment expansion, particularly within the digital games and film production sectors. This data underscores the industry’s vital role as a major creative contributor to the national economy, highlighting a booming market that offers expanding career pathways despite emerging challenges in sourcing skilled crew. Relevant to open data initiatives, this release exemplifies how government statistical agencies provide transparent, accessible metrics that inform strategic decision-making across creative industries. By publishing comprehensive financial and employment figures, the ABS enables stakeholders to analyze trends, such as the shift toward bringing post-production in-house and the surge in documentary content, thereby supporting evidence-based policy and business planning. The availability of this granular data allows for deeper analysis of sector-specific performance, revealing stark contrasts between thriving production segments and struggling broadcast areas. Ultimately, this dataset serves as a critical resource for validating the industry’s cultural and economic value, encouraging collaboration and ensuring that future investments are aligned with actual market dynamics and workforce needs.
