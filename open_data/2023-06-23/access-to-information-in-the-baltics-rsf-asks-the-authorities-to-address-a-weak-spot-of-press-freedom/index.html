Access to information in the Baltics: RSF asks the authorities to address a weak spot of press freedom

Legitimate security concerns are frequently abused in the Baltics to deny journalists access to public information, undermining transparency. This study highlights systemic failures in Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania, offering crucial recommendations to strengthen legal frameworks and protect freedom of information. These insights are vital for open data advocates, as they reveal how bureaucratic secrecy hinders public accountability and the availability of essential civic data.
