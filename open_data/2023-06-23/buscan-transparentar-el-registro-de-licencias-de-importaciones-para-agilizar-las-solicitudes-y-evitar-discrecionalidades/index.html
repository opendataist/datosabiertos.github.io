Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The bill proposed by the deputies of the Civic Coalition aims to combat discretion and the high risk of corruption in Argentina’s import system by creating an Open Data Portal. This mechanism would require that all information regarding import licenses—including applications, products, amounts, and statuses—be published in full, digitized, and in open formats, thereby ensuring public access and traceability of operations. Beyond informational transparency, the initiative introduces strict procedural rules to reduce administrative arbitrariness. It establishes a maximum, non-extendable deadline for resolving applications, prioritizing the chronological order of receipt and providing for automatic approval if authorities fail to meet the time limit. Additionally, it mandates the clear publication of criteria related to economic capacity, seeking to replace current regulatory uncertainty with predictability and legal certainty for taxpayers. This bill is highly relevant to the open data community because it exemplifies how the systematic release of public information can serve as an effective tool against corruption and abuse of power. By requiring the state to publish structured and accessible data, it aligns with global principles of open governance, demonstrating that transparency is not merely an end in itself, but a concrete mechanism for democratic oversight and economic improvement.
