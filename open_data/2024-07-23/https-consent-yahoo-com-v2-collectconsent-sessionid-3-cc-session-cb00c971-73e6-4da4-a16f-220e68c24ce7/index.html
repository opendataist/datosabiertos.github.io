Open Data
ES EN

Open data advocacy emphasizes user autonomy and data minimization. The text demonstrates opaque consent mechanisms where numerous partners aggregate personal data without explicit, informed agreement. This practice conflicts with transparency principles central to open data ecosystems. It highlights the urgent need for clear, granular control over data usage to protect individual privacy rights. Such cookie and tracking policies undermine trust in digital services. They illustrate how closed, proprietary data practices hinder the open, ethical sharing of information necessary for robust public engagement and policy-making.
