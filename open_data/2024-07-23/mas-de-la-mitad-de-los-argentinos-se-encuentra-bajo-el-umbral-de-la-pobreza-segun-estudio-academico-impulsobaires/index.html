Más de la mitad de los argentinos se encuentra bajo el umbral de la pobreza, según estudio académico - ImpulsoBaires

Más de la mitad de los argentinos se encuentra bajo el umbral de la pobreza, según estudio académico - ImpulsoBaires

The UTDT study reveals that more than half of Argentina’s population lives in poverty, driven by severe inflation and soaring family income requirements. This underscores the critical role of open data in making complex economic realities accessible to the public, allowing for transparent tracking of household welfare. By utilizing official statistical indices and household surveys, researchers can simulate and project poverty rates with high precision. This demonstrates how open access to raw economic data empowers independent analysis, verifying government figures and ensuring accountability in social metrics. Consequently, the availability of such data fosters informed public debate on social policies. It highlights the necessity of transparent, openly available statistics to accurately reflect societal well-being and guide effective interventions against widespread economic vulnerability.
