ELSTAT: 1 in 4 Greeks at risk of social exclusion, thousands not fed properly - Keep Talking Greece

ELSTAT: 1 in 4 Greeks at risk of social exclusion, thousands not fed properly - Keep Talking Greece

Greece’s 2022 Household Income and Living Conditions Survey reveals a slight improvement in social indicators, yet a significant portion of the population remains vulnerable. The data indicates that one in four Greeks faces the risk of poverty or social exclusion, highlighting persistent structural challenges despite a modest decline in overall risk compared to the previous year. This trend underscores the urgent need for continued policy intervention to meet long-term European social targets. The report exposes deep-seated material deprivation among these vulnerable groups, where hundreds of thousands of families struggle with basic needs. Many cannot afford adequate nutrition, proper heating during winter, or essential expenditures, illustrating that statistical decreases in poverty rates do not fully capture the daily hardships faced by low-income households. The disparity between poor and non-poor households is particularly stark regarding food security and housing comfort. This data is critically relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the power of transparent, accessible statistics to drive social accountability. By releasing detailed, granular survey results, national statistics authorities enable researchers, NGOs, and citizens to analyze inequality beyond headline figures. Such openness fosters informed public discourse and ensures that policy decisions are grounded in verifiable evidence, allowing society to monitor progress toward social inclusion effectively.
