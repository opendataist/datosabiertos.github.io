Vietnam ranks second in number of students in the RoK | Society | Vietnam+ (VietnamPlus)

South Korea hosts a record high number of international students, with Vietnamese learners ranking second globally. This surge is driven by strong economic ties and cultural influence, prompting government initiatives to attract and support foreign talent. The data highlights significant trends in cross-border education flows. For open data, this emphasizes the value of transparent statistics in tracking global mobility and policy impacts.
