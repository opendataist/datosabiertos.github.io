Ricardo Anaya underscores the vital role of the INAI in ensuring government transparency, contrasting it with political efforts to dismantle the institution. He points out that even the president who opposed it benefited from the agency’s mechanisms to access private data. This contradiction highlights the essential nature of open data rights for citizens. The article illustrates how independent access to information enables public scrutiny of corruption and fiscal management, regardless of who holds power. The piece is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the practical value of institutional transparency. It shows that maintaining access to public information is crucial for accountability and informed citizenship, reinforcing why these data frameworks must be protected against political interference.
