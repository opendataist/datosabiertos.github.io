Actualizará INEGI medición del Índice Nacional de Precios al Consumidor

Actualizará INEGI medición del Índice Nacional de Precios al Consumidor

The INEGI is conducting a public consultation to modernize Mexico’s Consumer Price Index, aiming to align national statistics with international best practices. This update reflects shifting consumption patterns and ensures the basket of goods remains relevant to contemporary household spending. By incorporating global recommendations, the institute seeks to enhance the precision and reliability of economic indicators for better public decision-making. The revision introduces chained indexes calculated biennially, allowing for more frequent and flexible adjustments to the weighting structure. This methodological shift addresses the limitations of static models by facilitating the inclusion of emerging products and the removal of obsolete ones. Consequently, the updated index will capture real-time economic dynamics, providing a more accurate measure of inflation and living costs for policymakers and citizens alike. This process is vital for open data initiatives as it emphasizes transparency and civic participation in statistical methodology. By inviting public feedback before implementation, the INEGI fosters trust and ensures that data products serve actual user needs. Such engagement exemplifies how open government practices can improve data quality and accessibility, empowering society with more relevant and timely economic information.
