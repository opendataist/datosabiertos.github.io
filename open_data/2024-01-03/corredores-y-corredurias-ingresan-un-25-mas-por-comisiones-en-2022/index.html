Corredores y corredurías ingresan un 2,5% más por comisiones en 2022

Insurance brokers saw commission growth driven by gains in the non-life sector, despite a decline in life insurance revenues. This highlights the shifting dynamics of intermediary income streams within the insurance market. The data illustrates how broker performance varies across product lines, impacting overall market mediation costs. For open data, this transparency allows analysis of economic trends, regulatory compliance, and fair competition in financial services.
