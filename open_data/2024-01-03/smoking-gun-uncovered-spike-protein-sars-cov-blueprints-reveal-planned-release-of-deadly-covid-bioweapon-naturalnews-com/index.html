SMOKING GUN: Uncovered spike protein “SARS-CoV” blueprints reveal PLANNED RELEASE of deadly COVID bioweapon – NaturalNews.com

The article presents documents obtained via a Freedom of Information Act request that allege scientists in China were developing engineered coronaviruses with altered spike proteins prior to the pandemic’s official announcement. It claims these records suggest a deliberate effort by the Wuhan Institute of Virology and affiliated organizations to create pathogens capable of infecting human cells, thereby supporting the theory that the outbreak originated from a laboratory incident rather than natural spillover. Central to these allegations is the assertion that U.S.-funded nonprofit organizations misled military and government agencies regarding the risks of these experiments to secure financial support. The text argues that even after a specific grant proposal was denied by the Department of Defense, the underlying experimental designs were utilized elsewhere. Proponents of this view contend that this deception was instrumental in enabling the creation and subsequent release of the virus that caused the global health crisis. This narrative is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it highlights the critical role of transparency and public records in scrutinizing scientific research and funding. It underscores how FOIA requests can expose potential cover-ups or unethical practices in high-stakes biosecurity research. By making hidden governmental and institutional decisions visible, such data allows for independent verification of official narratives, emphasizing the necessity of accessible information for holding organizations accountable and ensuring public safety in virological studies.
