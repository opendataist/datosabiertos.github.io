Ordenan transparentar datos sobre Plan Sonora

Ordenan transparentar datos sobre Plan Sonora

The INAI orders the Energy Secretariat to disclose all documents related to discussions on renewable energy planning in Sonora. This ruling confirms that the federal entity is responsible for these records, thereby rejecting claims of lack of jurisdiction. Such decisions reinforce the principle that data of public interest cannot be withheld through administrative obfuscation. Citizens can enforce transparency by challenging non-responses, ensuring that policy development remains visible and accountable to the public. This case is crucial for open data because it establishes clear institutional accountability for environmental policy documents. It demonstrates how legal frameworks can be used to access specific government records, promoting greater transparency in the implementation of national energy strategies.
