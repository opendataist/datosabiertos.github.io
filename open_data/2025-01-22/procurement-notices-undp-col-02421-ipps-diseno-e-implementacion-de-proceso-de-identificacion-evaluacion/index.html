Procurement Notices - UNDP-COL-02421 - IPPS - Diseño e implementación de proceso de identificación, evaluación

Procurement Notices - UNDP-COL-02421 - IPPS - Diseño e implementación de proceso de identificación, evaluación

This tender aims to design and implement a digital skills training program for vulnerable women in Colombia. By targeting a marginalized demographic, the initiative directly supports open data principles regarding social inclusion and equitable access to technology. Such projects generate valuable, inclusive data on workforce development and gender equity. Successful implementation requires navigating the UNDP’s digital procurement platform, necessitating transparent and accessible supplier registration processes. The system’s notification features ensure all participants receive timely updates, promoting fairness and clarity in the bidding lifecycle. This digital-first approach aligns with open government standards for transparent public procurement. The relevance to open data lies in creating actionable insights from targeted capacity-building efforts. By systematically identifying and evaluating digital competencies among vulnerable groups, the project contributes to a broader understanding of the digital divide. These outcomes inform future policy and resource allocation, enhancing the societal impact of open data initiatives.
