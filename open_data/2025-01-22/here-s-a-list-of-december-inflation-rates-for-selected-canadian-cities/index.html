Here's a list of December inflation rates for selected Canadian cities

Statistics Canada reports national inflation at 1.8%, though city-level data shows significant volatility due to small sample sizes. This highlights the critical importance of adequate statistical power in open data releases to prevent misleading interpretations of local economic trends. Transparency regarding methodological limitations, such as sample size constraints, is essential for maintaining public trust in government statistics. Open data initiatives must prioritize clear context to ensure users understand the reliability and potential fluctuations within granular datasets. Ultimately, robust open data practices require balancing accessibility with statistical rigor. By clearly communicating uncertainty and sample limitations, agencies help users derive accurate insights from localized economic indicators.
