McBroom bill would punish using Greek letters to hide emails from FOIA searches

McBroom bill would punish using Greek letters to hide emails from FOIA searches

This proposal highlights the critical need for transparent record-keeping in open data initiatives. Preventing the use of obscure languages to evade public scrutiny ensures that citizen access to government information remains robust and unhindered. Legislation penalizing such obfuscation techniques protects the integrity of public records. By closing loopholes, agencies are forced to maintain accurate, searchable archives rather than relying on technical glitches or coded communications. Ultimately, this underscores how open data relies on honest administrative practices. Without strict enforcement against hiding information, the fundamental trust in democratic transparency and the availability of truthful public data erodes.
