Chakwera for strong open governance and citizen engagement - The Nation Online

Malawi is prioritizing transparency and citizen engagement to achieve its long-term development goals. President Chakwera emphasizes that rebuilding public trust in institutions is essential for sustainable progress. This approach forms the core of the nation’s renewed commitment to open governance. The government recently reactivated its membership in the Open Government Partnership and launched a new national action plan. These initiatives align with the Malawi 2063 Vision, aiming to create an inclusive and self-reliant economy. By embedding good governance practices, the administration seeks to foster integrity in public service delivery. This article is relevant to open_data as it highlights the policy drivers behind data accessibility. Malawi’s digitalization efforts and whistleblower protection legislation rely heavily on ICT systems, which typically facilitate open data initiatives. Consequently, these governance reforms suggest a future where greater information openness supports accountability and citizen participation in national development.
