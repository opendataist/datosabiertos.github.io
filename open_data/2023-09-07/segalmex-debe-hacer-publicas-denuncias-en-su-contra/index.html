Segalmex debe hacer públicas denuncias en su contra

The INAI ruled that Segalmex must disclose documents related to corruption complaints, rejecting the agency’s attempt to withhold them for five years. This decision prioritizes transparency over the government’s argument that releasing sensitive data could obstruct ongoing administrative investigations. By mandating access, the ruling reinforces the principle that public accountability outweighs claims of temporary secrecy. It ensures that citizens and oversight bodies can scrutinize significant irregularities, including allegations involving billions of pesos in mismanagement. This case highlights the critical role of open data in combating corruption. When government entities fail to provide information voluntarily, independent bodies like the INAI serve as essential safeguards, ensuring that access to information remains a fundamental right rather than a discretionary privilege.
