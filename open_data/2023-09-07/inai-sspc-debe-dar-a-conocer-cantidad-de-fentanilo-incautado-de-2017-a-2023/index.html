INAI: SSPC debe dar a conocer cantidad de fentanilo incautado de 2017 a 2023

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) ordered the Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection to disclose detailed information about the fentanyl seized, overturning the agency’s previous refusal. This ruling underscores that rigorous document management and a clear understanding of institutional responsibilities are essential to ensuring the quality and accuracy of public information. Without these mechanisms, accountability and transparency are compromised. The authority determined that the Secretariat does have the competence to provide this information, as it has multiple specialized units capable of gathering the required data. It was established that precise documentation of institutional mandates and operations is not merely an administrative formality, but an essential tool for highlighting critical security and public health issues, ensuring that citizens have access to accurate and timely information. This case is significant for open data because it illustrates how legal transparency translates into social utility. By requiring public agencies to document and share their data in a structured and accessible manner, society’s capacity to analyze, critique, and utilize official information is strengthened. This promotes an open data ecosystem that improves decision-making and enhances trust in institutions.
