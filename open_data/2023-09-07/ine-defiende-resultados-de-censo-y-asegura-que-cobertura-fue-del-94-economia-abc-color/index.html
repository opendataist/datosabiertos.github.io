INE defiende resultados de censo y asegura que cobertura fue del 94% - Economía - ABC Color

The 2022 National Census of Paraguay reveals a population significantly lower than previous projections, establishing an approximate total of six million inhabitants. The National Institute of Statistics (INE) defends the robustness and accuracy of these figures, emphasizing that earlier estimates were outdated due to the failure of the previous census conducted in 2012. Coverage reached 94 percent, confirmed through cross-referencing with educational and birth records, thereby invalidating concerns about undercounting and validating the accuracy of the current demographic profile. The relevance of this census for the open data movement lies in its fundamental role as a transparent, verifiable factual basis for public planning. By debunking inflated projections, the INE demonstrates how access to accurate, audited official data enables the correction of social and economic policies. The availability of information cross-checked against both international and local sources strengthens institutional trust, showing that data openness is not merely a technical matter but essential for accountability and administrative efficiency. Finally, the process highlights the critical importance of maintaining up-to-date records in the face of real changes in population dynamics, such as migration and mortality. The results enable strategic adjustments that benefit the country, transforming uncertainty into actionable information. This case underscores that transparency in official statistics is vital for informed decision-making, demonstrating that open and verified data are powerful tools for sustainable development and evidence-based governance.
