Economía mexicana avanza en julio

Mexico’s economy grew modestly in July, driven primarily by the industrial and construction sectors. This momentum supports a robust annual GDP growth estimate, highlighting underlying economic resilience despite contractions in the service sector. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the utility of publicly available statistical indicators such as the IGAE. It shows how transparent, official datasets empower analysts to track real-time economic trends and validate financial forecasts, ensuring informed decision-making by both the public and private sectors.
