Bebés a la alza: en Veracruz aumentaron nacimientos de 2021 a 2022: INEGI

Veracruz recorded a significant increase in registered births between 2021 and 2022, ranking among the highest in Mexico. This surge underscores the critical need for open data systems to accurately track demographic trends, including retroactive registrations. Reliable public statistics are essential for policy-making and resource allocation, highlighting why transparent government data is vital for societal planning.
