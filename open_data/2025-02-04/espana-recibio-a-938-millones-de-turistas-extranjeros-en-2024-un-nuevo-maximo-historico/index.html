España recibió a 93,8 millones de turistas extranjeros en 2024, un nuevo máximo histórico

Spain recorded unprecedented highs in international tourism for 2024, welcoming record numbers of visitors and generating historic spending levels. This surge confirms the strong recovery and continued growth of the sector, validating the strategic direction of national tourism policies. The data demonstrates that demand is not only rebounding but exceeding previous peaks, signaling a robust economic contribution to the country’s GDP. Regional disparities highlight specific hotspots, with Catalonia leading in both arrival volumes and expenditure, followed closely by the Balearic Islands and the Canary Islands. While traditional destinations maintained dominance, regions like the Valencian Community showed exceptional growth rates. This geographic distribution reveals how tourism wealth is concentrated in coastal areas, offering insights into regional economic dependencies and the effectiveness of local infrastructure investments in sustaining high-yield travel markets. These results are particularly relevant to open data because they illustrate the power of transparent, standardized statistical frameworks like INE’s EGATUR. By making granular data on visitor origins, spending habits, and regional distributions publicly accessible, such datasets empower researchers, policymakers, and private stakeholders to make evidence-based decisions. Open access to this high-frequency, detailed information fosters accountability, enables precise policy evaluation, and drives innovation in how governments manage and optimize their tourism sectors for future sustainability.
