Los salarios acumularon séptimo mes de suba real en octubre

Argentina’s wage index surged significantly last October, marking seven consecutive months of real growth. This recovery brings earnings back to late 2023 levels across both the private and public sectors, signaling that efforts to stabilize the economy are yielding results. Such precise and timely labor statistics are vital for open data ecosystems. They enable researchers and citizens to accurately track the impact of inflation, fostering transparency and informed public debate about purchasing power and the effectiveness of economic policy.
