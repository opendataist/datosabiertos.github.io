Derechos de acceso a la información y de protección de datos personales prevalecen, asegura el INAI tras oficialización de su extinción

Derechos de acceso a la información y de protección de datos personales prevalecen, asegura el INAI tras oficialización de su extinción

The extinction of Mexico’s INAI does not eliminate constitutional rights to information and data protection. However, it abolishes the specialized autonomous body responsible for guaranteeing these rights. This structural change shifts the enforcement burden directly onto citizens, requiring them to actively demand access to information and privacy protections in order to fully exercise these rights. The institute highlights its historic role in uncovering major scandals and human rights violations. Without its specialized expertise, ensuring these rights may become more complex. The new model lacks the technical independence previously provided, potentially hindering efficient oversight and transparency in government operations. This situation is critical for open data, as the loss of a dedicated guardian threatens the openness and accountability essential for democratic societies. Without the INAI’s regulatory force, the practical application of data rights may weaken. It is vital to establish new homogeneous mechanisms that ensure swift, efficient, and equal access to public information, preserving the principles of maximum disclosure and human dignity in a just society.
