Influencing data governance policy in South and Southeast Asia — LIRNEasia

Influencing data governance policy in South and Southeast Asia — LIRNEasia

Data governance requires navigating complex tensions between privacy rights and the public’s right to information. Recent discussions in South and Southeast Asia highlight that achieving this balance involves difficult trade-offs rather than perfect solutions, emphasizing the need for robust consultative processes in lawmaking. These dynamics are critical for open data frameworks, which must reconcile protection mechanisms with the accessibility required for innovation and transparency. The article underscores the importance of engaging effectively with policymakers to shape these regulations. By sharing experiences in influencing policy, participants learned how to communicate research insights that support the dual goals of protecting individuals while enabling data utility. This approach is vital for open data advocates who seek to ensure that regulatory environments do not stifle the development potential of shared information. Ultimately, this initiative aims to build a network of policy intellectuals across the region to enhance their capacity to influence data governance. Strengthening this community allows for more cohesive strategies in balancing privacy with data access, ensuring that open data policies can support development purposes, including machine learning. This collective effort fosters a more informed and responsive regulatory landscape for open data across diverse national contexts.
