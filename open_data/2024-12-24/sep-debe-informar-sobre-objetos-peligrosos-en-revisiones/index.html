SEP debe informar sobre objetos peligrosos en revisiones

SEP debe informar sobre objetos peligrosos en revisiones

The INAI ruled that the SEP failed to ensure transparency by providing generic documents instead of specific operational results concerning school supply inspections. This decision underscores the critical importance of releasing precise, actionable data rather than merely referring to existing guidelines. True open data requires fulfilling specific public inquiries to ensure meaningful access to government information.
