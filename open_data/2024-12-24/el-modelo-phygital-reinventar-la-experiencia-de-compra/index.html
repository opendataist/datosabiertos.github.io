El modelo "phygital", reinventar la experiencia de compra

Retailers must prioritize cybersecurity and data privacy as core brand values, transforming consumer concerns into opportunities to deepen trust and align with evolving expectations. By acting transparently regarding data handling and ethical sourcing, companies can foster genuine confidence, ensuring that security is not just a technical requirement but a fundamental aspect of their identity. Simultaneously, brands should create valuable, transparent exchanges where shared data enhances personalization and unlocks exclusive experiences. This approach shifts the focus from mere transactions to lasting loyalty, ensuring that every interaction contributes to a stronger relationship and that the phygital model remains a consistent factor in customer satisfaction. This is relevant to open data as it highlights the critical balance between leveraging data for business value and respecting privacy rights. It underscores the need for ethical data stewardship, demonstrating that open, secure, and transparent data practices are essential for sustainable innovation and maintaining public trust in the digital economy.
