RTVE revela el presupuesto que gastó en la organización de Eurovisión Junior 2024

RTVE revealed that organizing Eurovision Junior 2024 cost significantly more than initially projected, exceeding five million euros. This financial transparency, achieved through public access requests, highlights the actual public expenditure on major cultural events. Such data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the real-world impact of transparency portals. It allows citizens to scrutinize public spending decisions and holds institutions accountable for budget overruns in high-profile national activities. Understanding these costs enables a better-informed public debate regarding resource allocation. Open access to such detailed financial records fosters trust and ensures that taxpayers can verify how their contributions are utilized by public corporations for international cultural representations.
