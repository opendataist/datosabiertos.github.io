Publicada la declaración de impacto ambiental del centro de datos de Meta en Talavera

Meta continues to advance its large-scale data center project in Talavera de la Reina by finalizing key administrative approvals, including the environmental impact statement and the Special Interest Plan. This progress underscores the importance of transparent regulatory processes, highlighting how publicly accessible data on environmental assessments and project status enables stakeholders to effectively monitor corporate compliance and the project’s impacts on regional development. The updated proposal emphasizes significant sustainability improvements, such as reduced building dimensions and dry air cooling systems that lower water consumption. By committing to fully renewable energy and LEED certification, the project aims to minimize its ecological footprint. These design choices reflect broader industry trends toward environmentally responsible infrastructure, offering valuable insights into how large technology investments can align with regional sustainability goals. Ultimately, this €750 million investment will establish a major campus for digital processing and storage over a ten-year construction period. The transparency surrounding these decisions and the specific environmental commitments demonstrates how open data facilitates accountability. It allows the public and researchers to analyze the tangible effects of corporate expansion on local resources, promoting informed discourse on the balance between technological growth and environmental stewardship.
