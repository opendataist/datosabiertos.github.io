LR Discusses Obstacles to Uptake of Ammonia and Methanol Bunkers

Lloyd's Register highlights that high costs and infrastructure deficits hinder methanol adoption in passenger shipping, necessitating a focus on renewable production to improve affordability. Meanwhile, ammonia faces operational hurdles, particularly concerning toxicity, crew training, and safety protocols, despite the industry's existing experience with its transport. These insights are crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent datasets on fuel costs, safety metrics, and infrastructure readiness are essential for driving policy decisions. Accessible information allows stakeholders to accurately assess risks and economic viability, thereby accelerating the maritime industry's transition toward sustainable alternative fuels.
