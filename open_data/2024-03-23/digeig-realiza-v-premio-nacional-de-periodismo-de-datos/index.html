Digeig realiza V Premio Nacional de Periodismo de Datos

The 5th National Data Journalism Award in the Dominican Republic underscores the government’s commitment to making open data a cornerstone of public transparency. Inspired by the Law on Free Access to Public Information, the event aims to empower both the press and citizens, strengthening accountability and demonstrating that access to accurate and timely information is essential for a democratic society. The awarded works highlight the power of rigorous analysis of public data to expose critical social issues, ranging from child abuse to the management of government debt and road safety. This recognition not only validates journalistic investigation but also encourages state institutions to improve their regulatory compliance, thereby embedding a culture of transparency into the core operations of the State. This initiative is vital to the open data movement because it illustrates the practical value of public information when combined with critical analysis. By prioritizing specific datasets and rewarding their journalistic use, it establishes a replicable model that fosters social innovation, promotes citizen participation, and ensures that data goes beyond mere availability to become effective tools for social change and sustainable development.
