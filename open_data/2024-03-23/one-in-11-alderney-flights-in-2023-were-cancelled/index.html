One in 11 Alderney flights in 2023 were cancelled

Aurigny highlights that current aircraft limitations cause significant flight cancellations, suggesting that using larger, more reliable planes could drastically improve on-time performance. This inefficiency underscores the critical need for airport infrastructure upgrades to support different aircraft types. Investing in runway extensions and airport overhauls would enable the use of high-capacity aircraft, reducing operational costs through fleet rationalization. These improvements are essential for enhancing service reliability and providing passengers with consistent, dependable travel options despite adverse weather conditions. This case is vital for open data, as it demonstrates how transparent reporting of flight statistics can expose systemic inefficiencies. By making operational metrics publicly available, stakeholders can advocate for evidence-based infrastructure investments that improve public transport services and accountability.
