Periodistas de Listín Diario son galardonados en el Premio Nacional de Periodismo de Datos

The article highlights how data journalism in the Dominican Republic leverages open data to scrutinize government spending, internet safety risks, and public safety issues such as traffic fatalities. This demonstrates the critical role of accessible datasets in fostering investigative reporting and uncovering societal impacts that pure statistics might obscure. These awards underscore a growing recognition of the power of data-driven storytelling to enhance civic engagement and accountability. By rewarding in-depth analyses, the contest promotes the use of public information to challenge narratives and provide deeper context to complex national problems. The relevance to open data lies in the explicit commitment by government officials to make open data the standard practice. This reinforces the principle that transparency is essential for democratic integrity, encouraging more rigorous analysis and ensuring that citizens can hold institutions accountable through informed, evidence-based inquiry.
