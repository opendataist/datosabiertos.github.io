Alcaldía cruceña coordina estrategia y logística para garantizar un Censo de calidad

The municipal government and the National Institute of Statistics are coordinating efforts to ensure the success of the 2024 Population and Housing Census. This collaboration highlights the critical role local authorities play in facilitating accurate data collection through logistical support and community engagement. Volunteers are preparing to collect household surveys, emphasizing the importance of residents being counted in their current locations. The resulting data is essential for generating effective public policies, demonstrating how reliable census information directly influences governance and resource allocation. This event is highly relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of high-quality, transparent foundational datasets. Accurate censuses provide the raw material for open government initiatives, enabling researchers and citizens to analyze demographic trends and hold institutions accountable for their service delivery.
