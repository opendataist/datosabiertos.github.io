La compraventa de vivienda despierta: sube un 19,4% en julio, con su mayor cifra desde 2022

The Spanish housing market rebounded strongly in July, ending a recent downturn with a significant annual increase in transactions. This surge, particularly in new and protected housing, signals a recovery in sales activity across most regions, despite isolated declines in areas such as the Balearic Islands. This data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the critical role of transparent, high-quality government statistics in tracking economic trends. By providing accessible, granular details on housing markets, such datasets enable researchers and analysts to verify market health and effectively understand regional disparities. Open access to this information empowers citizens and businesses to make informed decisions regarding real estate investments. It ensures that the narrative of economic recovery is grounded in verified facts rather than speculation, fostering trust in public institutions and supporting evidence-based policy discussions.
