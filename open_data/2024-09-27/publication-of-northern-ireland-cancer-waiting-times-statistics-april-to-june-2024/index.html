Publication of Northern Ireland Cancer Waiting Times Statistics: April to June 2024

Publication of Northern Ireland Cancer Waiting Times Statistics: April to June 2024

Northern Ireland’s latest cancer waiting time statistics reveal a complex picture shaped by the transition to new electronic patient records. The phased rollout of the 'encompass' system means that current data, labeled as official statistics in development, originates primarily from the South Eastern Health Trust. This technological shift requires cautious interpretation, as the dataset is still being validated and does not yet include the Belfast Trust, thereby limiting direct historical comparisons for the entire region. Performance metrics show notable fluctuations, including a decline in the number of patients starting definitive treatment compared to previous quarters. While the majority of patients continue to meet the 31-day target for treatment initiation, there is a significant concern regarding urgent breast cancer referrals. The percentage of patients seen within the critical 14-day window has dropped sharply, falling well below last year’s levels, which highlights emerging bottlenecks in early diagnosis services despite overall treatment stability. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it illustrates the challenges of publishing statistics during systemic digital transformations. It emphasizes the necessity of transparency regarding data provenance and quality assurance when introducing new information systems. By explicitly categorizing early-stage digital data and excluding incomplete sources, the publication models a responsible approach to open government data, ensuring that users understand the limitations and context of the metrics presented.
