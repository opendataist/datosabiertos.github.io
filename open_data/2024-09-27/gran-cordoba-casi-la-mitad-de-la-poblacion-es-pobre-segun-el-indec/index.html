Gran Córdoba: casi la mitad de la población es pobre, según el INDEC

Gran Córdoba: casi la mitad de la población es pobre, según el INDEC

The latest national statistics reveal a dramatic surge in poverty and extreme poverty across Argentina, reaching historic highs not seen since 2004. This sharp deterioration, occurring within the first six months of the current administration, highlights a severe crisis affecting millions of citizens who can no longer afford basic necessities. The data underscores a systemic failure in economic stability, impacting every region and demographic group nationwide. Children and teenagers are disproportionately affected, with two-thirds of those under fourteen living in poverty, while the elderly remain the least vulnerable but still heavily impacted. The widespread nature of this deprivation suggests deep structural issues rather than isolated regional problems. The concentration of vulnerability among the young raises long-term concerns about social mobility and future economic resilience for the entire population. This information is critical for open data advocates as it demonstrates how accessible, official statistics can expose urgent social realities. Transparent reporting enables civil society and researchers to hold institutions accountable and analyze the tangible human impact of policy decisions. By making such granular data available, governments facilitate evidence-based advocacy for social justice and informed public debate regarding economic governance.
