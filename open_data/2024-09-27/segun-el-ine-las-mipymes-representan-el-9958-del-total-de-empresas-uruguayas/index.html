Según el INE, las mipymes representan el 99,58% del total de empresas uruguayas

Micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) form the backbone of the domestic economy, accounting for the overwhelming majority of all businesses and generating the bulk of private-sector employment. This report highlights their resilience in the aftermath of the health crisis, demonstrating that although they initially suffered significant impacts, their structural adaptability enabled them to recover more quickly than other entities, thereby consolidating their role as essential drivers of stability and sustained economic growth. The significance of these enterprises extends beyond numerical metrics, as they foster social and economic inclusion by providing job opportunities to vulnerable groups and supporting the operations of large corporations. Their operational flexibility and lower costs allow them to innovate and respond swiftly to market changes, acting as a crucial buffer during economic fluctuations and making substantial contributions to the country’s productive development across various sectors. This analysis is vital for the open-data community because it underscores the need to use official statistics to understand the true dynamics of the labor market and business resilience. By providing data disaggregated by firm size and region, it highlights the importance of accessing detailed, disaggregated information to design effective public policies, assess the impact of health crises, and promote transparency in economic management, thereby enabling more informed and equitable decision-making.
