The 2023 census reveals stark demographic shifts in Spain, highlighting a polarization between major urban centers and numerous small municipalities facing depopulation. This data underscores the importance of accessible public statistics for understanding regional disparities and informing policy decisions regarding rural sustainability and urban growth. Furthermore, significant variations in foreign population percentages and gender ratios across different areas illustrate the complex social fabric of local communities. Recognizing these trends is crucial for analyzing migration patterns and social integration, emphasizing how open data facilitates deeper insights into the evolving composition of Spanish society. Ultimately, this publication provides essential, granular insights into population dynamics that are vital for researchers, policymakers, and citizens. By making such detailed demographic information publicly available, the government supports transparency and enables evidence-based strategies to address the challenges and opportunities presented by Spain’s diverse municipal landscape.
