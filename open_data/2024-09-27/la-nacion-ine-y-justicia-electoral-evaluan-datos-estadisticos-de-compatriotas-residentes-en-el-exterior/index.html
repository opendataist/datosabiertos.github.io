La Nación / INE y Justicia Electoral evalúan datos estadísticos de compatriotas residentes en el exterior

La Nación / INE y Justicia Electoral evalúan datos estadísticos de compatriotas residentes en el exterior

The coordination between Paraguay’s electoral justice system and the National Institute of Statistics aims to optimize demographic and electoral data for citizens living abroad. This collaboration ensures compliance with recent legislation that mandates the automatic registration of expatriates in the permanent civic registry, thereby guaranteeing their full voting rights. By leveraging international data sources, the statistical institute will provide updated population figures and territorial distributions to electoral authorities. This information is crucial for managing voting logistics through embassies and consulates, ensuring that the legal framework for expatriate suffrage is effectively implemented and accessible. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the necessity of interoperable, standardized datasets across government agencies. Sharing granular demographic and location information between statistical and electoral bodies highlights how transparent data exchange enables efficient public service delivery and strengthens democratic participation for diaspora communities.
