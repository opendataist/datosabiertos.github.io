Economía argentina sigue cayendo, según informe del Indec

Economía argentina sigue cayendo, según informe del Indec

Argentina’s economic activity contracted in July, with most sectors shrinking while agriculture and mining showed notable growth. Industrial output remains weak, facing low capacity usage and declining production, sales, and employment across most sub-sectors. This data is relevant to open data because it highlights the value of transparent, granular economic indicators. Access to detailed sectoral statistics allows for better monitoring of regional trends and helps identify specific areas of resilience or distress, fostering informed decision-making. Ultimately, public dissemination of such precise metrics supports accountability. It enables stakeholders to verify claims about economic health and understand the diverse impacts of downturns, ensuring that data serves as a foundational tool for analysis rather than just abstract statistics.
