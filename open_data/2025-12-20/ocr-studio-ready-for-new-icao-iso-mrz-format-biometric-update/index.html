OCR Studio ready for new ICAO/ISO MRZ format | Biometric Update

OCR Studio ready for new ICAO/ISO MRZ format | Biometric Update

OCR Studio has updated its technology to support both legacy and new-generation ICAO/ISO machine-readable zone standards. This dual capability ensures that financial, telecom, and government entities can maintain stable Know Your Customer and border control services during the global transition period, preventing disruptions in identity verification processes. The shift toward web-based recognition allows users to scan IDs, barcodes, and payment cards directly via mobile cameras without installing applications. By removing technical barriers to entry, this enhancement democratizes access to document verification tools, making secure identity checks more accessible for everyday digital interactions and mobile-first workflows. This development is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as robust, standardized document recognition facilitates the accurate extraction and interoperability of personal information across diverse systems. Supporting multiple data formats ensures that open data ecosystems can reliably integrate identity documents from various jurisdictions, promoting greater transparency and efficiency in digital identity management and verification frameworks.
