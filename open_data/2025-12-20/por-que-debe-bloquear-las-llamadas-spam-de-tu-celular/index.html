Por qué debe bloquear las llamadas spam de tu celular

Por qué debe bloquear las llamadas spam de tu celular

The article highlights the growing threat of spam calls during peak seasons, serving as a primary vector for digital fraud and identity theft. Scammers exploit holiday distractions to steal sensitive personal and financial data through spoofing techniques, posing significant security risks to users globally. Preventing interaction with these calls and utilizing built-in smartphone filtering tools is crucial for mitigating exposure to scams. By blocking unknown numbers and activating spam filters on both Android and iOS devices, individuals can significantly reduce their vulnerability to criminal attempts and protect their privacy from malicious actors. This topic is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of robust data protection standards and transparency in telecommunications. Understanding how personal data is harvested and misused via open communication channels emphasizes the need for stricter regulations and public awareness regarding digital safety and the responsible handling of user information.
