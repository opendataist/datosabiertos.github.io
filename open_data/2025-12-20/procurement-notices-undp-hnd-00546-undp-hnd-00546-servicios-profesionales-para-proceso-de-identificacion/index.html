Procurement Notices - UNDP-HND-00546 - UNDP-HND-00546 Servicios Profesionales para Proceso de Identificación

Procurement Notices - UNDP-HND-00546 - UNDP-HND-00546 Servicios Profesionales para Proceso de Identificación

The UNDP in Honduras has launched a procurement initiative for professional services to identify and evaluate candidates for individual consultancies. This process is managed exclusively through the Quantum Supplier Portal, a centralized digital platform designed to streamline interactions between suppliers and the procurement office. By requiring all communications, document access, and bid submissions to occur within this system, the UNDP aims to enhance transparency and efficiency in its acquisition workflows. Central to this announcement is the mandatory registration or login within the Quantum system before participating in the tender. The guidelines emphasize that the term "Negotiation" in this context refers to the standard bidding process facilitated by the platform. Potential providers are instructed to verify their supplier profiles, as existing data from recent commercial interactions may already be present in the system, thereby preventing duplicate registrations and ensuring that only qualified and verified entities submit proposals. This document is highly relevant to open data advocates as it highlights the shift toward digital transparency in public and international procurement. By funneling requests for proposals through a standardized, accessible online portal, the UNDP increases the visibility of its contracting processes. This digital centralization allows for better tracking of procurement activities and provides a structured framework where data on tender opportunities, deadlines, and supplier qualifications can be more easily monitored and analyzed by civil society and researchers.
