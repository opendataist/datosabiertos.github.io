Azim Premji University signs MoU with Karnataka's Census Operations Directorate to set up census data research workstation

Azim Premji University collaborates with Karnataka’s Census Directorate to establish a secure workstation for analyzing anonymized census data. This initiative provides scholars with critical access to extensive demographic and socio-economic information, fostering rigorous academic inquiry into India’s population characteristics. The facility supports both internal university members and external researchers through dedicated analytical tools and a structured approval process. By ensuring data remains within a controlled environment, the partnership balances the need for open scientific exploration with strict privacy protections for sensitive individual information. This model exemplifies responsible open data practices by making vast public datasets accessible for societal benefit without compromising anonymity. It demonstrates how institutions can collaborate to unlock the potential of historical statistical records, encouraging transparent research while maintaining ethical standards in data governance.
