La creación de empresas sube un 9,1% en 2023, alcanzando las 108.091, la mayor cifra desde 2007

The article highlights a significant recovery in Spain’s business landscape, marked by a substantial rise in new corporate formations and a notable decline in dissolutions. This trend signals renewed economic confidence, as the surge in registrations returns to positive growth rates after previous contractions, reflecting a resilient entrepreneurial environment. Financially, the creation of these entities involved significantly higher capital subscriptions, indicating stronger investor commitment. The concentration of new businesses in major economic hubs like Madrid and Catalonia, alongside a drop in voluntary closures, suggests a stabilizing market where viable enterprises are more likely to persist and grow, enhancing overall sector vitality. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular metrics on economic health. Accessible, high-quality datasets on company creation and dissolution allow researchers and policymakers to analyze market dynamics, identify sectoral trends, and develop evidence-based strategies for fostering sustainable economic development and innovation.
