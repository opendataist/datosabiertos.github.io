White Paper a 'political manifesto', UPA strengthened foundation of India: Manish Tewari

The article highlights a political clash regarding the government’s recent White Paper, which opposition leader Manish Tewari dismisses as a pre-election manifesto rather than a factual assessment. He argues that the timing reveals political intent, urging the current administration to address its own record instead of criticizing previous achievements. Tewari emphasizes the UPA era's foundational contributions, specifically citing the Right to Information, the National Food Security Act, and MGNREGA as critical tools for empowering citizens and ensuring social justice. He contends that these measures strengthened the country’s economic and political fabric by providing legal rights to information and guaranteed employment, which are now reportedly being undermined. This discourse is relevant to open data because it underscores the fragility of transparency mechanisms like the RTI Act, which are essential for accessing and verifying government statistics. By contrasting poverty alleviation figures and budget allocations for welfare schemes, the debate illustrates how political narratives can obscure data integrity. Ultimately, it reinforces the necessity of robust, unrestricted data access to hold administrations accountable and ensure that development metrics reflect genuine social progress rather than political propaganda.
