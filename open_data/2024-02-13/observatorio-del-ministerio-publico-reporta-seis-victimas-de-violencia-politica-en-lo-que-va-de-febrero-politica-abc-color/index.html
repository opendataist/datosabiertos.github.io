Observatorio del Ministerio Público reporta seis víctimas de violencia política en lo que va de febrero - Política - ABC Color

Observatorio del Ministerio Público reporta seis víctimas de violencia política en lo que va de febrero - Política - ABC Color

The Public Ministry’s open data platform highlights severe political violence against female leaders in February, revealing threats and hate speech from diverse online sources. This surveillance underscores the critical role of public data in exposing systemic harassment that aims to discourage women's political participation and public engagement. Legal protections remain fragmented, as penal sanctions were removed from recent legislation, leaving victims with limited judicial recourse. This gap emphasizes how open governance data can identify legislative deficiencies, prompting calls for stronger frameworks that balance free expression with the fundamental right to safety and non-discrimination. These findings are vital for open data advocates, as they demonstrate how transparent monitoring of social media trends exposes hidden abuses against public figures. By making this aggression visible, data initiatives support accountability and drive necessary reforms to protect democratic participation and ensure gender equality in political spaces.
