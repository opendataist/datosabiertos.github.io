Inai ordena a Sedena revelar detalles del rescate de Mexicana de Aviación | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI ordered the Sedena to disclose contracts related to the rescue of Mexicana de Aviación, rejecting the military’s refusal to share the information. This ruling underscores that public accountability is essential when state entities manage privatized assets, ensuring transparency in how taxpayer funds are allocated for corporate restructuring and operations. The decision highlights the legal obligation of government bodies to provide information, even when they claim a lack of available data. By confirming the state’s majority ownership, the order compels the Sedena to acknowledge its administrative responsibility, reinforcing the principle that public institutions cannot hide behind bureaucratic silence when managing public enterprises. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical role of transparency authorities in enforcing access to public information. It sets a precedent for challenging non-compliance by powerful entities, proving that both digital and physical records of state-sponsored ventures must remain accessible to citizens to guarantee democratic oversight and trust in public administration.
