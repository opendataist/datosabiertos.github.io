Barcelona pone en marcha un nuevo portal de datos: desde los pagos con tarjeta de crédito hasta el número de personas viviendo solas

Barcelona has launched Barcelona Dades, a platform that centralizes fragmented municipal data to improve transparency and accessibility for citizens, researchers, and the media. This unified system enables comprehensive city-wide analysis across multiple sectors, transforming what was once a complex access process into a user-friendly tool. The initiative underscores Barcelona’s longstanding position as a data pioneer in Spain, emphasizing the delivery of timely and actionable insights through automated publishing workflows. By minimizing the lag between data generation and publication, the city ensures that public statistics are more current and reliable. This update is crucial for the open data movement, as it illustrates how government portals can democratize access to urban information. It highlights the importance of centralization, ease of use, and rapid data release in fostering civic engagement and informed decision-making within smart city ecosystems.
