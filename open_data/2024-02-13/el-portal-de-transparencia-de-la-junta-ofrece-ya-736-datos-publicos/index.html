El Portal de Transparencia de la Junta ofrece ya 736 datos públicos

The Junta of Castilla y León is strengthening its commitment to transparency by launching the “Government Data Project,” a unified strategy that standardizes the presentation of public information on its portal. This approach aims not only to increase data availability but also to improve its visualization and comprehension, ensuring that citizens can locate and interpret information in a consistent and coordinated manner across all government departments. The significance of this initiative is highlighted by the award-winning projects in the seventh edition of the Open Data Awards, which demonstrate the practical value of reusing this information. From mobile education apps to data journalism and energy efficiency analysis, these initiatives illustrate how open data drives social innovation and enables the development of tools that address diverse challenges in key sectors such as health, the environment, and cultural heritage. This model is fundamental to the open data movement, as it goes beyond merely publishing figures to foster institutional trust and collaboration between the public and private sectors. By facilitating access, filtering, and downloading of information in multiple formats, it empowers society to make informed decisions and develop technological solutions, demonstrating that transparency is an essential tool for economic and social progress.
