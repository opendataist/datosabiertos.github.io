PAN advierte a AMLO que el INAI 'tampoco se toca'... - Noticaribe

The PAN defends the autonomy of Mexico’s transparency institute against the President’s attacks, emphasizing that independent oversight is essential for preventing government excesses and ensuring public resources are accounted for. This stance highlights the critical role of autonomous bodies in maintaining democratic checks and balances, protecting citizens’ right to know how public funds are managed. The article underscores the danger when leaders disparage transparency agencies, suggesting such rhetoric aims to dismantle accountability mechanisms to conceal corruption and consolidate authoritarian control. It argues that preserving these institutions is vital for preventing administrative abuse and maintaining the integrity of public service across all government levels. This conflict is highly relevant to open data because institutional autonomy directly impacts the availability and reliability of public information. When transparency bodies are weakened, access to government data diminishes, undermining civic engagement, research, and the ability of society to monitor fiscal responsibility and ethical governance.
