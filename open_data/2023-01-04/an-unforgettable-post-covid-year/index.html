An unforgettable post-Covid year

Vietnam’s 2022 economic performance demonstrated remarkable resilience, achieving high GDP growth and controlling inflation despite global recessions. Strong budget collections and record-breaking export turnover secured a seventh consecutive trade surplus, positioning the nation as a leading global trader. These achievements highlight the effectiveness of shifting from strict pandemic protocols to flexible adaptation, underscoring the importance of data transparency in evaluating policy success. However, underlying structural weaknesses pose significant risks to sustainable development. The two-year average growth remains below long-term targets, and public investment lags behind plans due to execution bottlenecks. Furthermore, the prevailing tight monetary policy, while aimed at curbing inflation, has constrained capital availability for a labor-intensive economy. This caution has exacerbated liquidity issues, leading to widespread enterprise failures and job losses, which threaten to weaken domestic businesses against foreign competitors. This analysis is critical for the open_data community as it reveals how macroeconomic indicators, when analyzed alongside granular data on enterprise survival and investment disbursement, expose the gap between headline statistics and ground-level economic health. Access to detailed, disaggregated datasets would allow for more accurate assessments of policy impacts, particularly regarding the balance between monetary control and economic vitality. Ultimately, open data fosters better-informed debates on how developing economies can sustain growth without compromising financial stability or domestic enterprise resilience.
