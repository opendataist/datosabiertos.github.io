Pensión del Bienestar, IMSS e ISSSTE: ¿Cuándo pagan y cuánto aumentarán este 2023?

The Mexican government has announced significant adjustments to benefits for older adults, including a 20 percent increase in pensions under the Welfare Program for the current fiscal year. This measure represents a substantial improvement in monthly economic support, although it introduces a change in the distribution logistics that organize bank deposits according to the alphabetical order of the first surname. This change aims to optimize the system, but it inevitably causes temporary misalignments, delaying access to funds for those whose surnames begin with the last letters of the alphabet. At the same time, the update mechanisms for retirees of the Mexican Social Security Institute (IMSS) and the Institute for Social Security and Social Services for State Workers (ISSSTE) have been clarified. Benefits are adjusted differently depending on the applicable legislation and the date of contributions, ensuring that benefits reflect inflation and the increase in the minimum wage. Workers who contributed under more recent regulations will see their pensions updated based on price indices, while those with prior rights have different effective dates, thereby ensuring equitable treatment across different generations of retirees. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the need for transparent and standardized access to government information on social policies. The availability of accurate data on amounts, payment dates, and eligibility criteria enables citizens to verify the fulfillment of state commitments and facilitates citizen-led audits. Without robust open data structures, the complexity of these regulations hinders public understanding and accountability, which are essential elements for transparency in the management of public resources directed toward addressing social vulnerability.
