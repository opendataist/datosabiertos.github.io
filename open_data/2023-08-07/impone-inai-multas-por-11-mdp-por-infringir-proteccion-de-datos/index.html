Impone INAI multas por 11 mdp por infringir Protección de Datos

The article highlights significant enforcement actions by Mexico’s INAI regarding data protection violations during early 2023. The financial, health, and manufacturing sectors faced the highest penalties for improper data handling, lack of consent, and incomplete privacy notices. At the same time, numerous complaints were filed against federal government bodies for similar infractions, underscoring widespread non-compliance across both the private and public sectors. This case is crucial for open data advocates because it demonstrates the critical tension between data transparency and individual privacy rights. It emphasizes that responsible open data practices must strictly adhere to legal frameworks and obtain proper consent. Ignoring these regulations leads to substantial fines and a loss of public trust. The narrative illustrates that openness does not override privacy obligations. For open data initiatives to be sustainable, organizations must prioritize compliance with data protection laws. This ensures that sharing information enhances accountability without infringing on personal rights, maintaining the balance essential for ethical and lawful data ecosystems.
