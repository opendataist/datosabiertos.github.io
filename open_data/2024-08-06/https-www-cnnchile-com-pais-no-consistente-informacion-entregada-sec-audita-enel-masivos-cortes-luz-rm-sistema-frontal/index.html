Open Data
ES EN

The SEC’s audit of Enel highlights critical data transparency failures during the power outages, specifically regarding inconsistent and inaccessible outage information. This lack of accurate, real-time data prevents users from understanding service recovery timelines and complicates their ability to file timely complaints. Relevant to open data, this case demonstrates how poor information availability directly impacts user trust and operational efficiency. When essential utility data is not openly or clearly provided, citizens cannot make informed decisions, and authorities struggle to verify compliance effectively. The situation underscores the necessity for public utilities to maintain robust, accurate, and accessible data systems. Ensuring that outage maps and recovery estimates are reliable and open helps streamline emergency responses, reduces administrative friction, and guarantees that accountability mechanisms function properly for both regulators and consumers.
