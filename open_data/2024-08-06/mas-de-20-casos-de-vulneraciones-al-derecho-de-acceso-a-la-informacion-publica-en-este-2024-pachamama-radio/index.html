Más de 20 casos de vulneraciones al derecho de acceso a la información pública en este 2024 | Pachamama Radio

Más de 20 casos de vulneraciones al derecho de acceso a la información pública en este 2024 | Pachamama Radio

The Ombudsman highlights persistent violations of the right to access public information by government entities, particularly at the regional level. This underscores the critical need for transparent data governance and accountability in public administration to ensure that citizens can effectively exercise their constitutional rights. Official guidelines clarify that requesting information is straightforward, requiring only basic identification and a clear description of the data sought, without mandatory formats or copies of identification documents. Crucially, entities must respond within ten business days, not thirty, correcting a widespread administrative misconception that hinders timely disclosure. The article is relevant to open data as it highlights the legal framework and procedural clarity necessary for effective access to information. It emphasizes that timely compliance is not merely a formality but a fundamental duty, ensuring that open data initiatives translate into real, actionable transparency for the public rather than bureaucratic delays.
