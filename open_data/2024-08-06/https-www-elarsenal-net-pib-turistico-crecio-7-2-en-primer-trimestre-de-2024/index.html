The analysis of Mexico’s tourism sector reveals a robust post-pandemic recovery, with tourism GDP outpacing national economic growth. This significant expansion underscores the sector’s resilience and its critical role in driving economic stability and development within the country. Domestic tourism consumption demonstrates steady growth, reflecting sustained internal demand and consumer confidence. Simultaneously, inbound tourism services are gaining momentum, indicating a successful reintegration of international visitors. These trends highlight a balanced recovery that benefits both local communities and the broader service industry. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it illustrates the power of transparent, publicly available statistical indicators from institutions such as INEGI. By making such granular, high-frequency economic metrics accessible, governments and researchers can better monitor sectoral health, inform policy decisions, and foster evidence-based dialogue on sustainable economic development.
