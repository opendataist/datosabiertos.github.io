Pega inflación a cervezas mexicanas

The article highlights how inflation and operational volatility affect Mexico’s micro and small-scale brewing industry, forcing artisanal producers to balance rising costs with the need to maintain product quality. This economic tension underscores the fragility of small businesses, which, unlike large corporations, lack the flexibility to absorb increases in the prices of basic inputs. The reliance on limited equipment and the need for self-directed maintenance create production instability, where a technical failure can halt operations and result in significant losses. This context reveals the absence of structured and standardized support for these enterprises, whose survival depends on their ability to quickly adapt to unforeseen technical and financial challenges without resorting to costly external solutions. This situation is particularly relevant to the field of open data, as it illustrates the lack of accessible, disaggregated public information on the micro-manufacturing industry. Knowledge gaps exist regarding the true scale of this sector, its specific operational costs, and technical failure rates—data that are essential for designing informed public policies, enhancing economic transparency, and fostering innovation ecosystems grounded in verifiable evidence.
