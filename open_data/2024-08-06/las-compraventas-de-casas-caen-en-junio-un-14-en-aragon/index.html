Las compraventas de casas caen en junio un 14% en Aragón

Las compraventas de casas caen en junio un 14% en Aragón

The Spanish housing market is undergoing a significant contraction, with transaction volumes declining sharply on a year-over-year basis. This downturn, particularly pronounced in regions such as Aragón and the Canary Islands, indicates that higher interest rates and economic uncertainty are actively suppressing demand nationwide, marking a continued negative trend since early 2023. This decline affects both new and existing properties, although new homes exhibit slight resilience compared to the steeper drop in the secondary market. The data reveal a broad systemic slowdown, contrasting sharply with isolated regional increases in Galicia and Murcia, which suggests that the current market correction is widespread rather than localized. This data is highly relevant to open_data, as it underscores the critical importance of accessible, granular statistical datasets for monitoring economic health. Reliable public records enable researchers and policymakers to identify regional disparities and assess the real-world impact of monetary policy, thereby fostering transparency and informed decision-making in the housing sector.
