Sánchez alardea de "integridad" y de "transparencia" ante 2.000 representantes internacionales en un foro sobre el Gobierno Abierto

Sánchez alardea de "integridad" y de "transparencia" ante 2.000 representantes internacionales en un foro sobre el Gobierno Abierto

The article highlights the Spanish government's strategic use of the Open Government Partnership summit to showcase its renewed commitment to transparency and integrity. By promoting recent regulatory reforms aimed at combating corruption within public administration, the executive seeks to demonstrate a shift toward accountable governance. This narrative emphasizes that governing must be a collaborative process with citizens, positioning these measures as essential tools to counteract the growing global fatigue with democratic institutions and to rebuild public trust. Simultaneously, regional leaders leveraged the international forum to advocate for greater autonomy and direct representation within European Union bodies. The focus here extends beyond national transparency to include structural changes that empower sub-state governments, arguing that subsidiarity requires institutional access to handle global challenges effectively. This perspective underscores the broader implications of open data and governance, where local and regional authorities are not merely implementers but active participants in shaping policy and ensuring democratic participation across different levels of government. This discussion is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparency initiatives serve as the foundation for modern, participatory democracy. Open data principles enable the accountability and citizen engagement that the government claims to foster, transforming raw information into a tool for public oversight. By linking digital transformation with anti-corruption efforts and regional empowerment, the event highlights the necessity of accessible, high-quality data in empowering stakeholders to monitor performance and collaborate effectively in the public interest.
