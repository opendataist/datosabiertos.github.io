Arranca INEGI Encuesta Intercensal 2025 - Plumas Libres

Arranca INEGI Encuesta Intercensal 2025 - Plumas Libres

The 2025 Intercensal Survey conducted by Mexico’s National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) aims to capture a detailed sociodemographic snapshot of the country, serving as a critical bridge between the 2020 and 2030 censuses. This initiative seeks to provide reliable estimates on population size, distribution, and housing conditions. These insights are essential for designing effective public policies at the municipal level, ensuring that government planning reflects the current reality of Mexican society rather than outdated data. To achieve comprehensive coverage across thousands of municipalities, INEGI is deploying tens of thousands of surveyors who will conduct interviews primarily using smartphone devices. This technological shift accelerates data collection while maintaining security in high-risk areas through coordinated teams. By modernizing the methodology, the institute not only improves operational efficiency but also sets a new standard for how large-scale national data can be gathered with greater mobility and speed. This effort is highly relevant to open data because the resulting information will be made publicly available, fostering transparency and accountability. When citizens can verify surveyor identities and access the final datasets, it builds trust in statistical institutions. Furthermore, releasing high-quality, granular sociodemographic data empowers researchers, journalists, and civil society to analyze social trends independently, promoting evidence-based decision-making and enhancing public oversight of resource allocation and policy effectiveness.
