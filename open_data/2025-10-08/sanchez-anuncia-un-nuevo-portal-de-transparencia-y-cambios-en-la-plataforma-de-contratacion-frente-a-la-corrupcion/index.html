Sánchez anuncia un nuevo portal de transparencia y cambios en la plataforma de contratación frente a la corrupción

Sánchez anuncia un nuevo portal de transparencia y cambios en la plataforma de contratación frente a la corrupción

The Spanish government is launching a new Transparency Portal and reforming its public procurement platform using artificial intelligence to combat corruption and restore citizens’ trust in institutions. This initiative aims to provide clear data on the schedules and expenses of senior officials, facilitating public access and promoting more accessible and democratic governance. The relevance to open data lies in the technological transformation of administrative processes, where advanced analytics enable the detection of irregularities through large-scale audits. By making oversight open and intelligent, accountability is strengthened, establishing a precedent where transparency is not merely a legal obligation but an active tool for fraud prevention. This approach reinforces the idea that an open government is essential to defend democracy against global backsliding. By prioritizing participation and access to information, citizens are empowered to monitor the use of public resources. Thus, data openness is consolidated as a fundamental pillar for democratic regeneration and institutional legitimacy.
