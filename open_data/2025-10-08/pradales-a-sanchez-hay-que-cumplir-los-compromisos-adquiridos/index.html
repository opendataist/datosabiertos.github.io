Pradales a Sánchez: "Hay que cumplir los compromisos adquiridos"

The Global Open Government Partnership summit underscores the urgent need to combat citizen disaffection and strengthen democratic trust through a culture of continuous dialogue and strict adherence to political commitments. Leaders emphasize that consensus must translate into actionable results to bridge the gap between institutions and the public. The Spanish government has announced a new transparency portal to firmly oppose corruption, acknowledging that zero tolerance is essential to reverse democratic decline. This initiative serves as a direct response to global concerns about institutional distrust fueled by corrupt practices, aiming to restore confidence through decisive and transparent governance measures. This event is crucial for open data, as it reinforces the link between transparency portals and democratic legitimacy. By prioritizing data accessibility and anti-corruption efforts, governments demonstrate that open information is not merely a technical requirement but a foundational tool for rebuilding public trust and ensuring accountable, effective democratic participation.
