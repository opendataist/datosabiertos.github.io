Castilla-La Mancha abordará en esta legislatura la creación de su estrategia de gobierno abierto con horizonte 2030

Castilla-La Mancha abordará en esta legislatura la creación de su estrategia de gobierno abierto con horizonte 2030

Castilla-La Mancha is at the forefront of open governance in Spain, recognized for its advanced regulatory framework on transparency, participation, and integrity. The region is actively developing a Strategic Plan for Open Government extending to 2030, alongside upcoming legislation on public policy evaluation. This comprehensive approach aims to strengthen public accountability and ethical administration, building upon a decade of legislative progress that has established a strong identity of civic commitment and common interest. A key achievement highlighted during the Global Open Government Partnership summit is the region’s significant contribution to Spain’s Fifth Open Government Plan. As the second autonomous community with the highest number of initiatives, Castilla-La Mancha demonstrates proactive collaboration between its Transparency Office and the University Chair of Open Government. This synergy underscores a mature institutional capacity to co-create policies with civil society, ensuring that governance strategies are responsive, inclusive, and aligned with global democratic standards. This article is relevant to open data because robust open government relies fundamentally on the availability and accessibility of public information. The region’s emphasis on transparency laws and digital transformation directly facilitates the release of high-quality public datasets. By integrating open data principles into its legal structure, Castilla-La Mancha ensures that citizens can effectively monitor government actions, participate meaningfully in policy-making, and verify public accountability, thereby strengthening democratic trust through data-driven governance.
