Cloudflare's new marketplace will let websites charge AI bots for scraping | TechCrunch

Cloudflare's new marketplace will let websites charge AI bots for scraping | TechCrunch

Cloudflare is introducing tools that empower website owners to monitor and control access by AI models scraping their content. By providing analytics on bot activity and one-click blocking capabilities, these instruments address the frustration and technical strain caused by unregulated data ingestion, allowing publishers to selectively permit or deny specific AI providers. The initiative aims to solve the sustainability crisis facing small publishers whose content fuels large language models without compensation. Currently, only major outlets secure licensing deals, while smaller sites suffer reduced traffic and server overload. By leveling the playing field, Cloudflare intends to give independent creators the same leverage as large corporations, ensuring they can protect their business models and revenue streams in the AI era. Looking ahead, a marketplace will enable publishers to negotiate direct access fees with AI providers. This shift moves the industry from unilateral, free scraping toward a compensated ecosystem, arguing that creators must be paid to sustain content creation. This is vital for open data ethics, as it challenges the current extractive norms of AI development and promotes a more equitable, transparent relationship between data sources and the models that consume them.
