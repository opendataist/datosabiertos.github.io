Oryzon anuncia buenos datos finales de PORTICO y se reúne con la FDA para una Fase III

Oryzon has released the final data from the PORTICO trial, confirming significant improvements in the treatment of Borderline Personality Disorder with vafidemstat. These results strengthen its case before the FDA, potentially accelerating the transition to Phase III and consolidating its unique position in a therapeutic area lacking approved drugs. The clinical success, coupled with recent patent grants, highlights the substantial economic potential and viability of this innovative therapeutic program. This news is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparency in clinical trials. Sharing Phase II data enables the scientific community to validate findings and accelerate the development of treatments for neglected disorders, fostering open collaboration.
