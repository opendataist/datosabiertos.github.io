More than 400 inactive lawyers on Criminal Legal Aid Register

Freedom of information data reveals that a significant portion of lawyers on Scotland’s criminal legal aid register received no or minimal payment, indicating widespread inactivity. This discrepancy creates a false impression of available legal capacity, masking the ongoing crisis in criminal legal aid services for the public. The Scottish Solicitors Bar Association urges firms to audit and remove inactive members from the register. This action is necessary to ensure the Scottish Legal Aid Board can provide the government with an accurate account of lawyers actually available to provide assistance, rather than relying on outdated or misleading data. This situation highlights critical open data challenges regarding the accuracy and timeliness of public registers. It underscores the importance of verifying dataset validity and the potential risks of relying on unupdated official records when assessing resource availability and service sustainability in the legal sector.
