Estos son los 20 pueblos con más habitantes de Aragón según los datos del INE

New census data from Spain’s National Statistics Institute reveals a significant demographic shift in Aragón, characterized by the consolidation of population in major urban centers such as Zaragoza, Huesca, and Teruel. This concentration of population in cities sharply contrasts with the persistent depopulation of small towns and rural areas, highlighting a stark divide between thriving urban centers and declining localities. The statistics indicate that while large municipalities maintain or even grow their populations, the vast majority of smaller communities have experienced substantial declines over the past decade. This trend underscores a structural imbalance, as economic and social activities increasingly cluster in provincial capitals, leaving peripheral regions with shrinking workforces and diminished vitality. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly accessible, granular datasets enable policymakers and citizens to track critical societal changes. By analyzing such information, stakeholders can identify regional disparities, monitor aging trends, and inform urban planning decisions that address the challenges of rural abandonment and demographic aging.
