Las valoraciones de los candidatos en Baleares, según el CIS

The article reveals that public perception of the Balearic government’s management is divided, with a slight majority viewing it as good, yet significant portions rate it poorly. This political sentiment is contextualized by broader assessments of the islands' current situation and comparisons to past years, highlighting complex voter priorities between local and national issues. These findings are highly relevant to open data initiatives because they illustrate how public opinion polls serve as vital datasets for understanding civic engagement and political trends. By making such survey methodologies and results accessible, researchers and citizens can analyze governance effectiveness and democratic accountability without relying on opaque government sources. Ultimately, the data underscores the importance of transparently sharing statistical evidence from independent surveys. It allows for a deeper understanding of regional disparities in political trust, encouraging more informed public debate and enabling policymakers to address the specific concerns that drive voter behavior in autonomous communities like the Balearics.
