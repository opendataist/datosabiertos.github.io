Esta es la manera en la que Apple entrena su modelo de inteligencia artificial

Apple is refining its artificial intelligence capabilities through the innovative use of synthetic data. This approach enables improvements to writing and summarization tools on its devices without compromising user privacy. By generating fictitious emails that mimic real-world structures, the company trains its models on representative content, thereby avoiding direct access to users’ personal information. The technical process involves converting these synthetic messages into mathematical representations known as embeddings. These anonymized data points are sent to devices with user consent to be compared against real-world patterns. Through differential privacy, the system identifies similarities without revealing identities, allowing Apple to fine-tune its algorithms based on the quality and relevance of the generated responses. This initiative is pivotal to the open data movement, as it demonstrates how privacy can coexist with technological advancement. It sets a valuable precedent for the development of ethical AI, showing that artificial intelligence can be optimized using simulated or anonymized data. This fosters public trust and provides a replicable model for other companies seeking to innovate while strictly respecting users’ data protection rights.
