Los precios industriales bajan un 4,5% por el abaratamiento de los bienes intermedios y la energía

The industrial price index has declined for a second consecutive month, driven by significant drops in energy costs and intermediate goods prices. This trend reflects a substantial easing compared to previous peaks, largely due to reduced expenses in the chemicals, plastics, and energy production sectors. The food and beverage industries continue to experience inflation, although non-durable consumer goods show moderation. The broader industrial trend varies by region, with most autonomous communities reporting decreased annual rates, highlighting localized economic shifts alongside national patterns. This data is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how publicly accessible statistical indices, such as those from the National Statistics Institute (INE), enable transparent monitoring of macroeconomic trends. Such data allows analysts to track sector-specific inflation drivers and regional disparities, fostering evidence-based decision-making in policy and business strategy without relying on proprietary sources.
