Northern Ireland Environmental Statistics Report released

The report reveals persistent public concern over environmental degradation, particularly illegal dumping, despite measurable improvements in air quality and reduced greenhouse gas emissions since 1990. These findings highlight the ongoing tension between regulatory progress and societal anxiety regarding local environmental health. Water and biodiversity metrics show mixed results, with significant portions of protected sites and coastal waters still failing to meet favorable or good ecological standards. This underscores the complex challenges in maintaining and restoring natural ecosystems, even as some water quality indicators show stability. For open data, this annual publication demonstrates the value of transparent, standardized environmental monitoring. It provides a critical benchmark for assessing policy effectiveness and fosters public trust by making detailed ecological data accessible for independent analysis and civic engagement.
