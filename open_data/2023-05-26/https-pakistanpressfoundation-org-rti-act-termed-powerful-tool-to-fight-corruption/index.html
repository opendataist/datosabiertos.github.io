Open Data
ES EN

The Right to Information Act serves as a vital mechanism for combating corruption and fostering democratic accountability. By empowering citizens to access government data, it transforms opaque bureaucracies into transparent institutions that are answerable to the public. The initiative specifically targets local officials in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, training them to assist vulnerable populations, including the illiterate. This capacity building ensures that information access is not limited to the educated elite but extends to marginalized communities who rely on local authorities for guidance. This effort is crucial for open data as it establishes a grassroots framework for transparency. By equipping local public information officers with the skills to facilitate data requests, the project lays the groundwork for a more inclusive and accessible information ecosystem across newly merged districts.
