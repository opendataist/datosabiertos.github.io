PBI del Perú se contrajo 0,4% en el primer trimestre: ¿Qué factores influyeron en la caída?

The Peruvian economy contracted in the first quarter of 2023, driven by weakened domestic demand and declining exports amid social unrest and adverse weather events. This downturn highlights significant vulnerabilities in both private consumption and public spending, particularly in the health sector, while investment in construction and machinery experienced sharp declines. Public sector expenditure notably decreased due to reduced health spending, contrasting with slight increases in education and defense. At the same time, private investment contracted significantly, reflecting a broader hesitation in capital formation. Export performance was mixed: traditional mining commodities suffered substantial declines, although certain agricultural and refined metal exports managed to grow, indicating a shifting trade landscape. This report is relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the critical need for accessible, transparent, and timely economic statistics to monitor national performance. Accurate public data enables stakeholders to analyze sectoral shifts, understand the impact of external shocks, and facilitate evidence-based policy responses during periods of economic instability.
