España alcanzó en 2022 un récord de más de 4,1 millones de deportistas federados

Spain’s 2022 sports statistics reveal a significant resurgence in participation and economic activity, marking a notable post-pandemic recovery. The year saw a record number of registered athletes, driven largely by regional concentration and a few popular sports, while high-level performance metrics also improved. Simultaneously, employment and sports tourism grew, indicating a robust sector that contributes substantially to the national economy through increased travel and spending. The data highlights persistent gender disparities across various dimensions, including athlete licensing, professional employment, and general sport participation habits. Although men dominate the statistics in licensed competitions and leadership roles within the industry, a majority of the adult population engages in recreational sports. This divergence suggests that while elite and organized structures remain male-heavy, grassroots engagement is broader, reflecting a complex landscape where public health benefits coexist with structural inequalities. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, aggregated official statistics can drive policy and accountability. By making detailed information on participation, economics, and doping control publicly available, governments enable researchers and civil society to analyze trends, identify inequalities, and evaluate the effectiveness of public investment. Such data transparency fosters evidence-based decision-making and ensures that the benefits of sports development are understood and distributed more equitably across society.
