This incident illustrates the dangerous consequences of unregulated social spaces promoted through digital influencers. For open data, this case emphasizes the need to integrate and analyze data from social media, business licensing, and law enforcement. Transparent access to information regarding venue permissions and incident reports could help authorities identify and close illegal operations more effectively. Enhancing data interoperability between these sectors is crucial for improving public safety and ensuring accountability in the digital age.
