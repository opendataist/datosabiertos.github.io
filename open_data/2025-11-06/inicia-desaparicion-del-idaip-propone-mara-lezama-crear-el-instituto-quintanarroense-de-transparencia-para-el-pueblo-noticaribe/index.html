INICIA DESAPARICIÓN DEL IDAIP: Propone Mara Lezama crear el Instituto Quintanarroense de Transparencia para el Pueblo - Noticaribe

INICIA DESAPARICIÓN DEL IDAIP: Propone Mara Lezama crear el Instituto Quintanarroense de Transparencia para el Pueblo - Noticaribe

The state of Quintana Roo is replacing its transparency institute to create a new body dependent on the Anti-Corruption Secretariat. This structural shift concentrates oversight power within the executive branch for specific entities, fundamentally altering how public information access is guaranteed. The proposal decentralizes guarantee functions, assigning them to various distinct bodies like legislative controls and human rights commissions. This fragmentation suggests a move away from a unified oversight model, potentially impacting the consistency and independence of information rights enforcement across different government branches. This development is crucial for open data as it redefines institutional accountability. By dispersing oversight roles, the reform may weaken the central authority typically responsible for enforcing transparency standards. Such changes can affect data accessibility and the robustness of mechanisms ensuring government transparency, highlighting the importance of strong, independent institutions in open data ecosystems.
