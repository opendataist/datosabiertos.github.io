Las cuentas de la Iglesia de Málaga llegaron el pasado 2024 a los 36,2 millones por las obras de la Catedral

Las cuentas de la Iglesia de Málaga llegaron el pasado 2024 a los 36,2 millones por las obras de la Catedral

The Diocese of Málaga emphasizes that financial transparency goes beyond legal requirements, serving as a profound testimony of truth and trust grounded in Evangelical values. By publicly disclosing detailed budgets and operational data, the institution aims to build confidence among its faithful, demonstrating that the management of communal resources is an expression of living out religious principles. This approach highlights the ethical imperative for openness in religious governance, aligning administrative practices with spiritual integrity to strengthen the bond between the Church and its community. Beyond mere fiscal figures, the report reveals the extensive social, educational, and pastoral impact of the diocese, illustrating how financial resources are translated into tangible human services. Significant investments in building conservation, particularly for the Cathedral, alongside extensive charitable work through organizations such as Cáritas, demonstrate a commitment to serving thousands of individuals. This comprehensive perspective connects economic accountability with social responsibility, emphasizing that open data enables the public to understand the broader societal value generated by religious institutions. This disclosure is highly relevant to open data advocates, as it exemplifies how detailed, accessible public records can enhance accountability in non-state entities. By treating financial and operational metrics as public goods rather than proprietary secrets, the Diocese sets a precedent for how religious organizations can leverage transparency to legitimize their role in society. Such practices encourage broader societal trust and provide a model for other civil society actors to adopt similar standards of openness, demonstrating that data visibility supports ethical governance and community engagement.
