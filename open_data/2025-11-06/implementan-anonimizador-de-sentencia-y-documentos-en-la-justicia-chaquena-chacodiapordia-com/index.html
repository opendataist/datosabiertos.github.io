Implementan anonimizador de sentencia y documentos en la justicia chaqueña - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

Implementan anonimizador de sentencia y documentos en la justicia chaqueña - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

The Chaco judiciary launched an AI-driven, open-source system for anonymizing sensitive documents. Requiring human validation, this tool safeguards privacy while ensuring data security through local server installation. By integrating a gender perspective and open data principles, the initiative enhances transparency and responsible access to information. It directly supports the court’s ethical AI guidelines, setting a standard for secure judicial data management. This development is vital for open data as it demonstrates how public institutions can leverage open-source technologies to protect personal information. It proves that transparency and privacy can coexist, encouraging broader adoption of secure, auditable data processing methods in government sectors.
