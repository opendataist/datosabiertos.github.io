Cádiz reached more than 237,000 pensioners in 2025, with 4,000 new beneficiaries

Cádiz reached more than 237,000 pensioners in 2025, with 4,000 new beneficiaries

Data from Cádiz reveal an increase in the number of pensioners, highlighting pronounced gender gaps in the amounts received. Men receive significantly higher pensions, while women are predominant among widows’ pensions and in the lower income brackets, revealing structural inequalities within the social protection system. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publishing disaggregated statistics enables the identification and analysis of specific social disparities. By making data differentiated by sex and economic level accessible, transparency, accountability, and informed decision-making are facilitated, paving the way for the design of fairer and more inclusive public policies.
