Morelos sets a national example! Leads 'Semilleros de la Honestidad' program benefiting 13,000 preschool children

Morelos sets a national example! Leads 'Semilleros de la Honestidad' program benefiting 13,000 preschool children

Morelos has positioned itself as the national leader in civic education for children by widely implementing the federal program “Semilleros de la Honestidad” (Seeds of Honesty). This pioneering initiative integrates ethical values such as honesty and empathy into preschool education through playful methodologies, demonstrating an institutional commitment that has far exceeded the average reach of the rest of the country. The relevance to the open data movement lies in the technological innovation adopted by the state through the creation of a state-level transparency dashboard. This interactive platform provides real-time information on program coverage and pedagogical status, enabling citizens and authorities to monitor progress. This approach transforms accountability into a tangible and accessible process, promoting citizen oversight based on public and verifiable data. Furthermore, all educational materials and multimedia tools are free and downloadable, fostering open access to knowledge. By releasing digital resources without restrictions, Morelos not only enhances educational quality but also establishes a model of administrative transparency. This strategy combines early childhood pedagogy with open technology, ensuring that the development of an integrity-based culture is inclusive, measurable, and sustainable.
