Querétaro adds more than 265,000 housing units in ten years

Querétaro adds more than 265,000 housing units in ten years

The housing supply in Querétaro significantly outpaced population growth over the past decade. This imbalance led to a reduction in the density of occupants per dwelling, reflecting a transformation in the entity’s demographic dynamics and housing space. The comparative analysis reveals a significant diversification in housing types. While single-family homes continue to dominate in terms of quantity, apartments and duplexes have experienced accelerated expansion, altering the traditional urban structure and responding to new residential demands. These data are relevant to the open data sector, as they highlight the importance of up-to-date public statistics. They enable researchers and planners to understand urbanization trends, facilitate land-use studies, and improve transparency in the management of public resources through verifiable and accessible information.
