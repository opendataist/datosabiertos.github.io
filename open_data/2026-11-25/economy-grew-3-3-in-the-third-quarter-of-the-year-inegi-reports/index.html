Economy grew 3.3% in the third quarter of the year, INEGI reports

Mexico’s economy exceeded expectations with 3.3% growth in the third quarter, driven primarily by robust trade activity, industrial recovery, and resilient domestic consumption. However, this performance does not represent a structural inflection point, as growth remains concentrated in specific sectors such as public construction and the automotive industry, while agricultural activity shows contraction. Looking ahead, analysts anticipate a moderation in economic expansion, with stagnant consumption and negative net exports potentially leading to a slowdown in the coming months. This situation unfolds against a regional backdrop where the U.S. economy demonstrates stronger momentum, despite maintaining strong commercial ties with Mexico. Annual growth prospects for the year suggest a lower rate, reflecting an economic dynamic that remains fragile and dependent on limited external factors. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how public time series of macroeconomic indicators, such as those published by INEGI, enable immediate correlation and comparative analysis with international data. The availability of these open, seasonally adjusted datasets facilitates economic hypothesis validation, market structure understanding, and the development of more accurate predictive models by both the academic community and the private sector, based on official statistical transparency.
