Mexican economy grew 3.3 percent annually in the third quarter - Between the Lines - Chihuahua News

Mexican economy grew 3.3 percent annually in the third quarter - Between the Lines - Chihuahua News

The Mexican economy showed significant recovery in the third quarter, marking its best performance in three years. This growth, primarily driven by the services sector, suggests sustained economic resilience that exceeded initial expectations. The improvement in key indicators reflects a strengthened internal dynamic, highlighting the vitality of commerce and recreational services as fundamental drivers of economic progress. Despite this quarterly upturn, the Global Indicator of Economic Activity (IGAE) indicated a short-term slowdown during the last month of the period. This monthly contraction contrasts with the positive annual trend, revealing volatility in immediate real economic activity. Such fluctuations in near-term tracking indicators alert to the need to monitor the sustainability of growth in the face of possible temporary slowdowns in industrial and services dynamics. This analysis is relevant to open data because the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) publishes detailed, updated official information on GDP and the IGAE. Public access to these time series enables researchers, analysts, and citizens to verify economic health, promote transparency, and develop applications that use concrete evidence for decision-making and public scrutiny.
