Unified Government Portal, gub.gt | Diario de Centro América

The creation of the gub.gt portal represents a significant step forward in promoting transparency by prioritizing citizens' needs over internal bureaucratic structures. This institutional effort aims to make public services and information more visible, which were previously difficult to find, ensuring that the population can effectively access their rights and opportunities. The development process was based on the Open Government methodology, actively integrating perspectives from the private sector and civil society. This multidirectional collaboration ensures that technological tools respond to real-world conditions, transforming state-citizen interactions into a continuous and open dialogue that improves the quality of public management. This initiative is relevant to the field of open data because it demonstrates how institutional coordination and active listening enable the structuring of information in a user-friendly manner. By focusing technology on the citizen experience, it fosters an ecosystem where access to information is not only available but also understandable and usable, thereby strengthening accountability and democratic trust.
