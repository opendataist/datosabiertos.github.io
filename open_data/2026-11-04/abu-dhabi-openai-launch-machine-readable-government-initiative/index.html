Abu Dhabi & OpenAI Launch Machine-Readable Government Initiative

Abu Dhabi & OpenAI Launch Machine-Readable Government Initiative

Abu Dhabi has partnered with OpenAI to launch Machine-Readable Government, transforming policies into structured data that AI can analyze. This approach automates routine regulatory approvals, significantly reducing processing times while ensuring human oversight for complex or high-stakes decisions. The system enhances regulatory clarity by identifying policy overlaps and testing rule changes before implementation. By converting written regulations into a common format, it aids officials in streamlining compliance and improving inter-departmental coordination without compromising local data sovereignty or model training integrity. This initiative is vital to open data because it demonstrates how structured, machine-readable regulations enable efficient AI integration in public services. It exemplifies the practical value of transparent, standardized data formats in government, facilitating faster, accountable, and interoperable digital service delivery across multiple entities.
