Experts Stress AI's Role In Robust Data For Policy

Experts Stress AI's Role In Robust Data For Policy

A recent conclave at the Indian Statistical Institute highlighted the critical intersection of robust data, modern technology, and artificial intelligence in shaping effective public policy. Experts argued that relying on comprehensive databases and advanced technological tools is essential for informed decision-making, moving governance beyond intuition toward evidence-based strategies that address complex societal challenges. The transformation of official statistics was noted as a significant development, yet experts cautioned that success hinges on methodological rigor and maintaining public trust. Without robust underlying frameworks, even technologically advanced systems may fail to deliver reliable insights. Consequently, there is an urgent need for continued research into statistical methodologies to ensure that evolving data practices remain accurate, transparent, and credible for policymakers and citizens alike. This discussion is highly relevant to open data advocates because it underscores the necessity of high-quality, accessible data infrastructure as the foundation for transparent governance. When official statistics are enhanced by AI and modern analytics, they can drive better policy outcomes, but only if the data remains open, trustworthy, and rigorously maintained. Strengthening these statistical foundations ensures that open data initiatives can effectively support accountability and innovation in public sector decision-making.
