NLnet Pauses NGI Zero to Protect Open Internet Funding

The NLnet Foundation has stopped new applications for its NGI Zero program. This move helps protect the open internet. It also matches goals in the European Tech Sovereignty package. Standard calls will reopen in the autumn under the Open Internet Stack. The foundation wants to keep current data projects stable. This is important because the political world is changing. This pause shows why we need targeted funding for open data. Without money, businesses or politicians might hide public records. Laws decide how users can use these resources. Specific licenses tell us if we can remix, share, or build on public resources. You must know these legal rules to avoid breaking them. Read more about open data licences here.

Stable funding keeps APIs reliable. These APIs power open-data portals. Tools like CKAN, DCAT, and OpenDataSoft give us steady access to raw data. This reliability helps users hold institutions accountable. However, open data has challenges for users who are just starting. On the good side, stable infrastructure lets you see transparent info without sudden paywalls. This transparency helps you make smart choices and engage with your community. On the bad side, access is not always easy. You often have to look for unpublished information. You might need to file a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request to find hidden records. This process can be scary, especially if your request is denied. Find out how to file a FOIA request and handle denials.

Users must also interpret data carefully to avoid misinformation. Too much information makes it hard to analyze statistics. You need to read a statistic before quoting it to ensure it is accurate. Discover how to read a statistic before you quote it. This careful analysis stops false stories from spreading. It keeps advocacy based on truth. The NLnet pause shows we need high-quality, well-supported data sources. Users who know the legal ways to get data can analyze the world with confidence. Learn how to effectively use open-data portal APIs. This knowledge helps you turn raw digital info into real action. The pause is a step toward a secure digital future. It shows that open data needs protection and active participation. You help build a free and public information ecosystem by supporting these efforts.
