Incierto futuro de la Plataforma Nacional de Transparencia | Revista Punto de Vista

A proposed reform to abolish the National Institute for Transparency threatens the stability of the National Transparency Platform, which safeguards over fifteen billion public records. This uncertainty arises because the plan transfers the institute’s functions without explicitly defining the platform’s future, endangering this crucial national asset for citizen access to government data. The legislation risks disrupting the continuous availability of essential information, such as salaries and contracts, if the platform’s operational continuity is not legally guaranteed. Without clear provisions, the mechanism for public accountability could falter, potentially hindering the mandatory quarterly updates required by transparency laws and weakening institutional oversight. This situation is critical for open data because it highlights the vulnerability of large-scale public datasets to administrative restructuring. Ensuring the platform’s preservation is vital for maintaining long-term access to historical and current governmental information, demonstrating how policy decisions directly impact the sustainability and reliability of open data ecosystems.
