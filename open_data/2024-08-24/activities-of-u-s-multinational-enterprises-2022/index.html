Activities of U.S. Multinational Enterprises, 2022

U.S. multinational enterprises expanded their global workforce and economic contribution in 2022, highlighting the significant role of foreign affiliates in the international economy. This growth demonstrates that U.S. corporate operations extend far beyond domestic borders, with a substantial portion of employment and value creation occurring abroad. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for open data initiatives, as it reveals the complex interdependence between national economies and global corporate structures. The data indicates that while U.S. parents remain the largest employers, foreign affiliates contributed significantly to worldwide employment and value-added. Expenditures on capital and research and development also rose, signaling continued investment in infrastructure and innovation globally. These trends underscore the importance of tracking economic flows across jurisdictions, which is vital for transparent analysis of global market behaviors and corporate influence. This report is highly relevant to open data because it provides granular insights into multinational activities, enabling researchers and policymakers to assess the true impact of globalization. By making such detailed statistics accessible, open data platforms can foster deeper understanding of international trade, investment patterns, and employment trends. Ultimately, this transparency supports informed decision-making and promotes accountability in an increasingly interconnected global economy.
