Listing of some of our FOIA and OMA lawsuit results –

Listing of some of our FOIA and OMA lawsuit results –

This article highlights persistent legal battles against various Illinois government entities to enforce transparency through Freedom of Information Act and Open Meetings Act lawsuits. It demonstrates that persistent advocacy can compel agencies to release withheld records, even when they initially refuse or attempt to hide information. The author reveals significant financial penalties and legal costs incurred by these agencies for non-compliance. These outcomes serve as a deterrent, showing that willful obstruction of public records requests carries substantial consequences for local governments and organizations. This is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of litigation in securing accessible public information. It emphasizes that legal frameworks and enforcement mechanisms are essential for maintaining accountability and ensuring that data remains available to the public, rather than being suppressed by bureaucratic resistance.
