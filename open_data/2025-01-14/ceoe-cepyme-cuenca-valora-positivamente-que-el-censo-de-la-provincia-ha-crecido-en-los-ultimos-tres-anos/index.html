CEOE CEPYME Cuenca valora positivamente que el censo de la provincia ha crecido en los últimos tres años

The INE census data reveals a positive demographic trend in Cuenca, with the population approaching 200,000 after steady growth over recent years. Local business leaders view this upward dynamic as significant validation of ongoing anti-depopulation efforts and a strong signal for future collaboration with administrative bodies to sustain this progress. Beyond raw numbers, the data highlights the province’s diverse composition, characterized by a majority Spanish population and a notable foreign resident presence. Demographically, the region shows an aging structure with a peak in the 55–59 age group, while younger cohorts remain smaller. This profile suggests specific challenges and opportunities for local social services and labor markets that require tailored policy responses. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible, high-quality government statistics empower local stakeholders to make informed decisions. By analyzing official census figures, organizations can accurately assess demographic health, identify emerging trends, and advocate for evidence-based policies that address rural depopulation and support sustainable community development.
