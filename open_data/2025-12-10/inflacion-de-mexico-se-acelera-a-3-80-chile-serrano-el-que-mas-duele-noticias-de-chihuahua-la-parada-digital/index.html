Inflación de México se acelera a 3.80% ; chile serrano el que más duele - Noticias de Chihuahua - La Parada Digital

Inflación de México se acelera a 3.80% ; chile serrano el que más duele - Noticias de Chihuahua - La Parada Digital

Mexico’s inflation accelerated unexpectedly to 3.80 percent in November 2025, driven significantly by the end of summer electricity subsidies and rising costs for fruits, vegetables, and professional services. While core prices remained relatively stable, volatile energy and food categories pushed the overall index higher than anticipated. This data highlights how specific policy decisions, such as subsidy removal, directly impact consumer price indices and inflation metrics. Understanding these nuances is crucial for accurately interpreting economic indicators and assessing the true cost of living across different population segments. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of accessing granular, disaggregated statistical datasets. Only by analyzing detailed component changes rather than aggregate totals can researchers and policymakers accurately diagnose inflationary pressures and formulate effective, targeted economic responses.
