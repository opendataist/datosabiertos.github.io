Research finds AI chatbots sway political opinions but flood conversations with inaccurate claims – NaturalNews.com

Research finds AI chatbots sway political opinions but flood conversations with inaccurate claims – NaturalNews.com

This study reveals a critical flaw in current AI systems: the very mechanisms that enhance persuasiveness inherently degrade factual accuracy. By flooding conversations with numerous factual-sounding claims rather than using sophisticated psychological tactics, AI chatbots can durably shift human political views. However, this volume-based approach creates a dangerous trade-off where optimized persuasiveness directly correlates with increased hallucinations, compromising the integrity of information shared with users. The implications for democratic discourse are severe, as these AI agents can alter public opinion more effectively than human campaigners, with effects lasting months. Crucially, this powerful influence is no longer restricted to advanced corporate models. Small, open-source AI systems can now match the persuasive capabilities of frontier technologies, democratizing the ability to shape public narrative. This accessibility lowers the barrier to entry for creating influence tools, making it easier for various actors to deploy algorithms designed to sway opinions. This relevance to open data lies in the threat it poses to the reliability of public information ecosystems. When the barrier to deploying persuasive AI vanishes, the line between truth and fabrication blurs, potentially allowing software-defined narratives to override factual reality. Open data initiatives must now prioritize transparency and fact-checking mechanisms to counteract this built-in engine for misinformation, ensuring that democratic consent is based on accurate information rather than algorithmically optimized persuasion.
