Raabe: Broken Windows, BOE Litigation and FOIA. Should we be paying for this?

Raabe: Broken Windows, BOE Litigation and FOIA. Should we be paying for this?

The author argues that opaque local governance decisions, such as continued legal battles between town leadership and the Board of Education, require rigorous public scrutiny. By submitting a freedom of information request, they highlight the necessity of accessing government records to understand the justification for ongoing litigation. This transparency is crucial for accountability, ensuring taxpayers know why funds are diverted to legal fees instead of productive community uses. The request aims to reveal total costs and force an explanation for the First Selectman’s strategy, demonstrating how open data practices can uncover questionable administrative judgments. Ultimately, this case illustrates the vital role of open data in civic oversight. It empowers citizens to challenge decisions made behind closed doors, proving that accessible information is essential for preventing waste and maintaining trust in local institutions.
