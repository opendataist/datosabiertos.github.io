IMANI files RTI request seeking details on new nationwide SIM registration - MyJoyOnline

IMANI files RTI request seeking details on new nationwide SIM registration - MyJoyOnline

IMANI Africa’s RTI request for Ghana’s upcoming SIM registration highlights the critical link between state transparency and data protection. By demanding full disclosure of legal frameworks and technical designs, the initiative emphasizes that open government processes are essential for maintaining public trust and ensuring legal compliance before large-scale biometric data collection begins. The focus on vendor selection and data governance underscores the necessity of scrutinizing private sector involvement in public infrastructure. This demand for visibility into procurement and oversight mechanisms ensures that privacy safeguards meet international standards, protecting citizens’ rights while preventing potential misuse of sensitive personal information by external contractors. Relevance to open data lies in the recognition that access to government-held information is a prerequisite for accountability. This case demonstrates how RTI mechanisms enable civil society to verify that data ecosystems are secure and equitable, reinforcing the principle that public interest requires transparent, accessible, and responsibly managed digital governance.
