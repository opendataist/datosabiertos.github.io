Infomaniak launches Euria, a free and sovereign AI that respects privacy and heats homes

Infomaniak has launched Euria, a sovereign AI assistant that demonstrates how open source models can be deployed with strict ethical and environmental standards. By leveraging local open source architectures rather than proprietary foreign alternatives, this approach challenges Europe’s dependency on external tech giants. It highlights the critical need for regional investment in ethical, carbon-neutral AI infrastructure, proving that technological independence is achievable through community-driven, transparent solutions. Privacy and data sovereignty form the core of Euria’s design, offering a stark contrast to mainstream commercial assistants that often harvest user data for model training. All processing occurs within Swiss data centers, ensuring compliance with rigorous protection laws and providing features like ephemeral modes that leave no digital trace. This model is particularly relevant for sensitive sectors such as healthcare, law, and finance, where maintaining absolute confidentiality and control over sensitive information is non-negotiable. Furthermore, Euria underscores the potential for sustainable AI operations by utilizing renewable energy and repurposing heat for district heating systems. This integration of ecological responsibility with high-performance computing illustrates that advanced AI does not require excessive energy waste or water consumption. For the open data community, this case study validates the viability of hosting large-scale, responsible AI services locally, encouraging a shift toward greener, more autonomous digital ecosystems that prioritize user rights over data extraction.
