Public data reveals that the determination of social class often relies on superficial assumptions rather than precise metrics. The INEGI’s classification challenges common perceptions by using specific income thresholds to define distinct socioeconomic tiers, moving beyond vague stereotypes. This approach underscores the importance of granular, standardized indicators in understanding societal structures. By quantifying access to education, healthcare, and luxury goods, official statistics provide a clearer, more accurate picture of economic stratification than anecdotal evidence allows. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives, as it enables rigorous analysis of inequality and resource distribution. Transparent income definitions allow researchers and policymakers to verify claims about social mobility, fostering evidence-based decision-making and enhancing public accountability regarding economic disparities in Mexico.
