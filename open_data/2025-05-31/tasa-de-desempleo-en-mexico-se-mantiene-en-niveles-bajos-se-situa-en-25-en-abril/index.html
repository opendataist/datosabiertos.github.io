Tasa de desempleo en México se mantiene en niveles bajos: se sitúa en 2,5% en abril

Tasa de desempleo en México se mantiene en niveles bajos: se sitúa en 2,5% en abril

Mexico’s unemployment rate remained at historic lows, indicating a stable labor market in which participation by the active population increased slightly. This stability suggests strong demand for workers, even as economic participation rates experienced minor adjustments compared to the previous year. Despite the positive employment figures, significant challenges persist regarding job quality. Informal work remains predominant, while precarious conditions—characterized by low wages or excessive working hours—have improved only marginally. These disparities highlight ongoing structural issues within the labor sector that require targeted policy interventions to ensure fair working standards. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, publicly accessible statistics enable real-time analysis of socio-economic trends. By monitoring these indicators, researchers and policymakers can better understand labor dynamics and develop evidence-based strategies to address informal employment and improve working conditions across the region.
