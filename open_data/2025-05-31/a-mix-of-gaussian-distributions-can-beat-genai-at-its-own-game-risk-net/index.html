A mix of Gaussian distributions can beat GenAI at its own game - Risk.net

A mix of Gaussian distributions can beat GenAI at its own game - Risk.net

Recent research demonstrates that traditional Gaussian Mixture Models (GMMs) outperform sophisticated generative AI techniques, such as GANs and autoencoders, in synthesizing financial market data like yield curves and volatility surfaces. While deep learning models often require extensive datasets and struggle with smaller data windows, GMMs maintain high accuracy and robustness even with limited historical data. This finding challenges the prevailing assumption that complex neural networks are always superior for generating synthetic financial distributions, highlighting the enduring value of established statistical methods in quantitative finance. The primary advantage of GMMs lies in their interpretability and tractability, which are critical for risk management and regulatory compliance. Unlike opaque deep learning architectures, GMMs provide transparent, analytically solvable parameters with clear financial meanings, significantly reducing the risk of overfitting. This explainability allows financial institutions to validate models more effectively and understand the underlying dependencies in simulated data, ensuring that generated outputs are not only statistically accurate but also logically sound and auditable for internal risk assessments. This work is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it validates accessible, non-proprietary statistical techniques for handling sparse or incomplete datasets. By offering a reliable method to rectify missing data within frameworks like the Fundamental Review of the Trading Book, GMMs empower institutions to improve data quality and risk modeling without relying on expensive or closed-source AI solutions. Consequently, this approach promotes transparency and reproducibility in financial data science, encouraging the use of well-documented, open methodologies to address challenges in market simulation and illiquid asset valuation.
