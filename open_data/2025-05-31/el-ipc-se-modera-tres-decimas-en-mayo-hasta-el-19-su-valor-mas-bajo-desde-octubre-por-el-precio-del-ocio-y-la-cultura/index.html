El IPC se modera tres décimas en mayo, hasta el 1,9%, su valor más bajo desde octubre por el precio del ocio y la cultura

El IPC se modera tres décimas en mayo, hasta el 1,9%, su valor más bajo desde octubre por el precio del ocio y la cultura

Spanish inflation has moderated to its lowest level since last October, driven primarily by declines in leisure, transport, and electricity costs. This trend indicates that price pressures are easing significantly, particularly within services linked to tourism. Crucially, the current inflation rate remains below the European Central Bank’s target, demonstrating that the economy is successfully balancing controlled price growth with robust expansion among developed nations. The article highlights the relevance of open data through the indispensable role of the National Statistics Institute (INE). The accurate, timely, and transparent release of consumer price indices allows for the independent verification of economic health. By providing detailed breakdowns, including underlying and harmonized measures, these datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze specific sector impacts rather than relying solely on aggregated figures. Furthermore, the consistent tracking of these metrics across months illustrates the importance of longitudinal data quality. When official statistics are accessible and precise, they foster trust in monetary policy decisions and help identify structural shifts, such as the favorable evolution of specific service prices. Ultimately, this transparency supports a more resilient economic environment by ensuring that all stakeholders have access to the same reliable information regarding price trends and inflationary pressures.
