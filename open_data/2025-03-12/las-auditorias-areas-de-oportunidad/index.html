Las auditorías: áreas de oportunidad

Las auditorías: áreas de oportunidad

The forensic audit conducted by the ASF revealed serious financial irregularities at the INAI, including phantom payments and internal corruption. These findings highlight the abuse of power that justifies the dissolution of the institute and its absorption by the Anti-Corruption Secretariat, marking a turning point in public governance. The relevance to open data lies in the opportunity to rebuild a transparent framework from scratch. With the old structure gone, there is a chance to design new rules that prioritize integrity and access to information, ensuring that public institutions operate with genuine accountability. The closure of this cycle includes the dissolution of the INAI and job uncertainty, but also the start of a new audit. This process aims to ensure that the next entity effectively protects personal data, restoring citizen trust through strict, conflict-of-interest-free oversight mechanisms.
