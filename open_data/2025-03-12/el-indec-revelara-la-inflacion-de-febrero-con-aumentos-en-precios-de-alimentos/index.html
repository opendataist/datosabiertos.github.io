El INDEC revelará la inflación de febrero con aumentos en precios de alimentos

El INDEC revelará la inflación de febrero con aumentos en precios de alimentos

The upcoming official release of Argentina’s Consumer Price Index for February indicates that inflation has accelerated again, reversing the low rate recorded in the previous month. Driven primarily by surging prices in the food sector, particularly meat, inflationary pressure has returned to levels above 2%. This trend highlights a resurgence in cost-of-living pressures, suggesting that early signs of stabilization are being offset by specific commodity shocks. Private estimates confirm this upward trajectory, with data showing a monthly increase of approximately 2.5% to 2.7%. While the annual inflation rate remains below 100% for the second consecutive month—marking a significant long-term deceleration compared to historical peaks—the immediate monthly dynamics reveal volatility. The discrepancy between early-month trends and final results underscores the complexity of forecasting, as early hopes for continued cooling were undermined by sharp increases in essential goods during the latter half of the period. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates the importance of diverse, publicly available sources such as ISEPCi’s neighborhood-level reports. These alternative datasets provide granular, high-frequency insights that complement official statistics, allowing for a more nuanced understanding of economic realities. Access to such varied data streams enables researchers and citizens to cross-validate official figures, ensuring transparency and a deeper grasp of how inflation impacts different segments of society, particularly regarding essential food items.
