Perú registró una inflación de 0,06% en septiembre de 2023

The article details the September 2023 consumer price index in Peru, highlighting a moderate national inflation rate driven primarily by transportation costs, particularly fuel, and services such as restaurants and hotels. While food prices decreased significantly, specific items such as lemons and yellow potatoes saw sharp increases, illustrating the complex volatility within basic goods. This data provides a granular view of how different economic sectors influence household expenses, distinguishing between volatile essential items and more stable service costs. From an open data perspective, this report exemplifies the critical importance of standardized, high-frequency economic statistics for transparent public governance. By breaking down aggregate inflation into specific product categories and regional variations, the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) enables detailed socio-economic analysis. Such structured data allows researchers, journalists, and citizens to move beyond headline numbers and understand the nuanced realities of purchasing power across different regions and demographic groups. The relevance to open data lies in the utility of this information for accountability and policy evaluation. When economic indicators are publicly accessible and meticulously categorized, they empower stakeholders to monitor the impact of inflation on vulnerable populations and assess the effectiveness of monetary policies. Ultimately, this level of transparency fosters informed public debate and supports evidence-based decision-making, ensuring that statistical agencies serve as reliable tools for democratic oversight rather than mere reporting mechanisms.
