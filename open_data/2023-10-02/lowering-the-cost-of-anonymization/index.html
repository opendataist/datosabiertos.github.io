Lowering the cost of anonymization

Damien Desfontaines’ 2020 ETH Zurich thesis addresses the high barriers to effective data anonymization. By lowering these costs, the work makes privacy-preserving techniques more accessible for organizations handling sensitive information. The dissertation demonstrates how to balance utility and privacy efficiently. This reduction in complexity and expense is vital for practical applications where robust anonymization was previously too resource-intensive to implement correctly. This research is highly relevant to open data initiatives. It enables the safer publication of datasets by providing scalable methods to protect individuals while maximizing data value, thereby encouraging wider sharing of information without compromising ethical standards.
