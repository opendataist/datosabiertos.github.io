El 49,4 % de los niños y niñas en Ecuador viven en una situación de pobreza - El Tiempo

El 49,4 % de los niños y niñas en Ecuador viven en una situación de pobreza - El Tiempo

Half of Ecuadorian children live in poverty, lacking essential services such as education and healthcare, which severely impacts their cognitive development. High rates of chronic malnutrition further threaten their physical well-being during critical early years, highlighting a systemic failure in meeting basic needs. While vaccination and medical check-up coverage have improved, disparities persist. The data reveals a strong correlation between maternal education and child development, suggesting that educational interventions are key to breaking cycles of disadvantage. These findings underscore the urgency of prioritizing the first years of life in public policy. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistics empower stakeholders to identify specific vulnerabilities. By making these indicators public, governments and organizations can design targeted, evidence-based interventions. Ultimately, open data transforms raw numbers into actionable insights, ensuring resources reach the most vulnerable populations effectively.
