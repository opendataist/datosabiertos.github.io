Sheinbaum announces 20 new industrial parks with an investment of $700 million and 30,000 jobs

President Sheinbaum announces 20 new industrial parks to boost the Mexican economy, representing a significant investment and the creation of thousands of jobs. This move positions the country as a strategic industrial hub, strengthening supply chains and attracting key companies to various regions, from the northern border to the Bajío region. The private sector validates this effort, highlighting that these spaces generate prosperity and structural employment opportunities. Economic activity shows sustained growth, reflecting business confidence in the national productive environment. The geographic distribution of these projects aims to decentralize development and leverage logistical advantages in states such as Coahuila, Nuevo León, and the Mexico City metropolitan area. This optimizes existing infrastructure and promotes territorial balance in the country’s installed capacity. This announcement is relevant to open data because it illustrates how data on infrastructure and employment should be accessible to audit the real impact of public investments. Transparency in the location, sectoral distribution, and job creation enables efficient analysis of regional development policies. Access to this information facilitates objective evaluation of progress toward the government’s employment and economic growth targets.
