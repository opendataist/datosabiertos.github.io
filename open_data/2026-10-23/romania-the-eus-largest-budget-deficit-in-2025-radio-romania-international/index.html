Romania, the EU’s largest budget deficit in 2025 - Radio Romania International

Romania, the EU’s largest budget deficit in 2025 - Radio Romania International

Romania maintains the highest government deficit in the European Union, significantly exceeding the bloc’s average and remaining well above the critical 3% threshold. Although the deficit narrowed slightly from previous years, the country faces substantial fiscal challenges amid modest economic growth. The government has pledged to adhere to deficit reduction targets, aiming for gradual improvements in the coming years, yet experts warn that achieving EU compliance by 2031 will require difficult fiscal adjustments. Economic experts suggest that raising taxes is no longer a viable solution, as both businesses and households are already burdened. Consequently, the focus must shift toward improving VAT collection and implementing measures that support budget adjustment without further increasing tax pressures. Political stability following the recent government crisis is viewed positively by credit rating agencies, which maintain Romania’s investment-grade status despite a negative outlook. This situation is relevant to open data as transparent, accessible, and timely public financial statistics are essential for verifying government compliance with EU fiscal rules. High-quality open data enables independent analysts, investors, and citizens to monitor budget performance, assess the credibility of political commitments, and hold institutions accountable. Reliable statistical transparency fosters trust in the government’s fiscal consolidation efforts and supports informed decision-making regarding Romania’s economic future and its standing within the European Union.
