Inaugural Oklahoma Local News Summit set for Oct. 23 in downtown Tulsa

Inaugural Oklahoma Local News Summit set for Oct. 23 in downtown Tulsa

The inaugural Oklahoma Local News Summit aims to strengthen press freedom and journalism standards in the region. By convening journalists, students, and the public, the event addresses critical challenges such as artificial intelligence and political polarization. This gathering serves as a vital platform for discussing how these modern forces impact First Amendment rights and the ethical conduct of newsrooms. A key focus of the summit is enhancing investigative reporting and government coverage through specialized training. Sessions provide practical strategies for building sources and navigating complex ethical policies, particularly regarding AI in journalism. This educational component empowers attendees to improve their reporting skills, ensuring that journalists are equipped to serve the public’s right to know effectively. This event is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it emphasizes transparency, government coverage, and journalistic accountability. By promoting robust investigative techniques and ethical standards, the summit supports the infrastructure necessary for rigorous data journalism. Strengthening these journalistic capacities ensures that open data can be interpreted and utilized to foster greater public understanding and trust in government institutions.
