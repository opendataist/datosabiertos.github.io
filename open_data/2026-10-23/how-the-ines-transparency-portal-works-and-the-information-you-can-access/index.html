How the INE’s transparency portal works and the information you can access

How the INE’s transparency portal works and the information you can access

The National Electoral Institute (INE) launched the “INE Always in Plain Sight” portal in response to the controversy sparked by contracts linked to the driver of the President Councillor. This initiative aims to restore public trust through a digital tool designed to ensure traceability and immediate access to procurement, purchasing, and bidding processes. The platform prioritizes transparency by using clear language and an intuitive navigation structure based on key questions. Users can monitor in real time the calls for bids, supplier proposals, award decisions, and compliance with agreements, visualizing the complete timeline of each administrative procedure. This advancement is significant for open data, as it transforms complex public data into accessible and understandable information for citizens. By facilitating the verification of costs and deliveries, the portal promotes accountability and social scrutiny, demonstrating how technology can enhance open governance and integrity in the use of public resources.
