User support - Eurostat

The article outlines Eurostat’s multi-channel customer support strategy, prioritizing an interactive web-based system for complex, written inquiries regarding methodology or data verification. This digital-first approach ensures that intricate technical questions requiring documented responses are handled efficiently, establishing a clear protocol for user engagement based on the nature of the request. For immediate assistance with website navigation or simple technical issues, users are directed to telephone support services available in numerous European languages. These phone lines operate during specific hours across various regions, providing accessible human assistance for users who prefer direct verbal communication or require real-time guidance with data discovery on the platform. This information is relevant to open data initiatives as it highlights the critical infrastructure supporting data accessibility and usability. By offering diverse, multilingual support channels, Eurostat ensures that a broad spectrum of users can effectively interact with open statistical resources. This emphasis on user assistance fosters greater trust and utilization of open data, removing barriers to entry for those who may struggle with self-service tools or require clarification on data integrity and usage methodologies.
