Inflation in the first half of October 2026 rebounds to 3.62%: INEGI

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) reported a slight increase in both biweekly and annual inflation during October 2026, highlighting the impact of food and services on price changes. This phenomenon reflects market dynamics and government regulations that directly affect the cost of living. The distinction between core and non-core inflation indices allows for a more precise analysis of market forces versus variables controlled by the state. Understanding these distinctions is essential for correctly interpreting macroeconomic indicators. This article is relevant to the field of open data, as it demonstrates how government institutions publish official statistics that underpin economic analysis. Transparency in these data facilitates academic research and informed decision-making by citizens and analysts.
