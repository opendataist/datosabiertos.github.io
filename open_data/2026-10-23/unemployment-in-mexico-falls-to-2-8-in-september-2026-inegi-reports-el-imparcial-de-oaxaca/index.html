Unemployment in Mexico falls to 2.8% in September 2026, INEGI reports - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

Unemployment in Mexico falls to 2.8% in September 2026, INEGI reports - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) revealed that Mexico’s unemployment rate reached historically low levels in September 2026. However, this apparent labor market success is tempered by the persistence of labor informality, which affects more than half of all workers. This structural reality limits the country’s capacity to generate decent jobs, despite improvements in open unemployment indicators. The data reveal a complex labor dynamic in which economic participation undergoes significant changes. While the employed population maintains considerable figures, the rise in the economically inactive population suggests a phenomenon of detachment from the labor market. Moreover, notable gender gaps are evident, with considerably different participation and unemployment rates between men and women, reflecting systemic inequalities in access to employment. This report is relevant to the open data community because it exemplifies how the availability of public statistics enables analysis not only of the quantity of employment but also of its quality and distribution. Transparency in disaggregated data by gender, sector, and formality is crucial for evaluating public policies and understanding the true economic implications beyond aggregate figures.
