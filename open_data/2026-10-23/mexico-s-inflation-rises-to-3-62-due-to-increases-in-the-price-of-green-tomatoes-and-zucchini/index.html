Mexico's inflation rises to 3.62% due to increases in the price of green tomatoes and zucchini

Mexico's inflation rises to 3.62% due to increases in the price of green tomatoes and zucchini

The National Consumer Price Index (INPC) reveals an annual inflation rate of 3.62% in October 2026, highlighting increases in food and essential services. This figure reflects the price dynamics that directly impact the well-being of Mexican households, demonstrating how certain regions and products experience significant volatility that affects the population's purchasing power. The relevance to open data lies in the fact that the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) makes these time series and geographic disaggregations publicly available. This enables researchers and citizens to access verified information to analyze economic trends, promote transparency, and develop technological solutions that enhance understanding of the national financial environment.
