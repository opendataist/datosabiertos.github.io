RTI commission orders SSNIT to release information to JoyNews - Ghanamma.com

The Right to Information Commission has ruled that the Social Security and National Insurance Trust must provide requested data to JoyNews, correcting the Trust’s improper refusal based on a claim that it lacked a designated information officer. This decision underscores the mandatory requirement for public institutions to have accessible information officers who handle citizen inquiries, ensuring that bureaucratic excuses cannot obstruct legal transparency obligations. The Commission specifically ordered the disclosure of details regarding the sale of stakes in several hotels, excluding sensitive board meeting minutes. This highlights a critical balance in open data governance, where the public’s right to know about significant asset disposals and financial decisions must be respected, while still protecting certain confidential deliberations. Such rulings clarify the scope of information that must be made available to citizens, reinforcing accountability in public financial management. This case is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how statutory frameworks can be actively enforced to combat opacity in public sector operations. It serves as a precedent for ensuring that government-linked entities remain answerable to the public, proving that structured appeal mechanisms are essential for maintaining trust. By mandating the release of specific transactional data, the ruling strengthens the ecosystem of accessible information, encouraging greater civic engagement and oversight of state resources.
