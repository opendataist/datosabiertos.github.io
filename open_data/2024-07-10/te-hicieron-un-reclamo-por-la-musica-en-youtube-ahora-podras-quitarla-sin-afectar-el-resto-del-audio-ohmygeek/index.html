¿Te hicieron un reclamo por la música en YouTube? Ahora podrás quitarla sin afectar el resto del audio | OhMyGeek!

YouTube has enhanced its AI-powered music deletion tool, enabling creators to remove copyrighted songs without affecting other audio elements such as dialogue. This simplifies claim management and helps preserve video monetization, although the accuracy is not absolute. The relevance to open data lies in algorithmic transparency and the accessibility of editing tools. By providing automated, AI-based solutions, YouTube demonstrates how open systems and APIs can empower users to manage their own creative data, fostering a fairer and more efficient digital ecosystem. This update reinforces the need for open standards in digital rights management. It enables creators to control their content without relying solely on manual or restricted processes, promoting a model where technology serves to democratize access to professional editing and reduce technical barriers.
