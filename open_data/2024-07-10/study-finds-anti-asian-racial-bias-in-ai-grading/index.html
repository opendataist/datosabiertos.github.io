Study finds anti-Asian racial bias in AI grading

Study finds anti-Asian racial bias in AI grading

AI essay graders systematically score lower than humans, with disparities most severe for Asian American students. While the root cause remains unclear, the bias suggests current models lack the nuance of human educators. This finding is critical for open data, as it highlights how algorithmic outputs can encode racial prejudice. Transparent data analysis is essential to identify and mitigate such systemic inequities in automated assessment tools.
