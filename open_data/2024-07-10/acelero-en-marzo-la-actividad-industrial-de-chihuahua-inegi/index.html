Aceleró en marzo la actividad industrial de Chihuahua: Inegi

Chihuahua is experiencing accelerated industrial growth, driven by significant gains in construction and strong performance in the energy sector. Although manufacturing output declined, the state leads the nation in construction expansion, signaling robust regional development. This data underscores the critical role of granular, subnational statistics in monitoring economic health. Such localized industrial metrics are essential for open data initiatives that aim to support evidence-based policymaking.
