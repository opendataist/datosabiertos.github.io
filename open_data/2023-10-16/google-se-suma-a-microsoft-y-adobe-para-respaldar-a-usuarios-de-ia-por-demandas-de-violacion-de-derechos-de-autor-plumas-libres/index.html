Google se suma a Microsoft y Adobe para respaldar a usuarios de IA por demandas de violación de derechos de autor - Plumas Libres

Google, together with its industry peers, has committed to defending users against copyright lawsuits stemming from outputs generated by generative AI. This initiative protects creators who use Google’s platforms from legal repercussions arising from disputes over training data, providing a comprehensive indemnification framework that addresses both direct infringement claims and broader intellectual property challenges. The policy applies to specific development tools and workspace integrations, excluding cases of intentional infringement. Although current litigation is primarily directed at major technology companies rather than end users, this preemptive measure establishes a crucial safety net. It acknowledges the growing tension between AI innovation and artistic rights, aiming to facilitate adoption while shielding individual developers from excessive legal risk. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it underscores the legal complexities surrounding the use of data for machine learning. By offering indemnification, Google sets a precedent for how technology firms can manage intellectual property risks. This supports the broader open data movement by lowering barriers to entry for AI developers and encouraging the ethical sharing and use of public and licensed information, free from the fear of disproportionate liability.
