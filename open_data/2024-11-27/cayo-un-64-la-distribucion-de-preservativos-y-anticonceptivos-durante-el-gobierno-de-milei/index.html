Cayó un 64% la distribución de preservativos y anticonceptivos durante el gobierno de Milei

Cayó un 64% la distribución de preservativos y anticonceptivos durante el gobierno de Milei

The analysis reveals a drastic reduction in the national distribution of condoms, contraceptives, and medications for voluntary termination of pregnancy during the current administration. This widespread decline has forced several provinces to independently bear the financial burden of procuring these supplies, highlighting a gap in state health planning and insufficient coordination between the national level and local jurisdictions to ensure access to health services. The situation has profound implications for human rights, as the scarcity of resources disproportionately affects the most vulnerable populations. Experts warn that lack of access to contraceptive methods and medical treatments can lead to unintended pregnancies and forced motherhood, violating national and international norms that protect sexual and reproductive health. This not only compromises the physical and psychological well-being of women but also creates structural barriers within the public health system. This case is crucial for the open data community because it demonstrates the power of cross-analyzing exposed government datasets. By combining information on budget execution and the physical delivery of supplies, citizens and civil society organizations can transform raw administrative data into concrete evidence of rights violations. Transparency in these records enables oversight of public policies, exposes structural inequalities, and demands accountability for how access to health is affected by political and budgetary decisions.
