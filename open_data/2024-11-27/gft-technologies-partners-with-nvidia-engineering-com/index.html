GFT Technologies Partners with NVIDIA - Engineering.com

GFT Technologies and NVIDIA are partnering to deliver tailored AI solutions for the financial and manufacturing sectors by combining GFT’s industry expertise with NVIDIA’s accelerated computing infrastructure. This collaboration focuses on developing advanced applications, such as digital twins for quality control in factories and intelligent agents for banking, thereby driving significant operational efficiency and innovation within these critical industries. A central pillar of this initiative is the utilization of open-source large language models to create customized AI capabilities. By fine-tuning these accessible models, enterprises gain the flexibility to deploy secure, on-premises, or cloud-based solutions rapidly. This approach enhances specific business functions, including fraud detection, customer service, and risk management, while allowing organizations to maintain control over their data environments without being locked into proprietary ecosystems. This partnership is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how open-source AI models empower businesses to build transparent, adaptable, and secure systems. It highlights the shift toward leveraging freely available foundational models to drive enterprise value, ensuring that organizations can integrate advanced analytics and generative AI into their workflows while preserving data sovereignty and operational agility.
