Kaspersky refuerza la seguridad de los datos con una renovación exhaustiva de la auditoría SOC 2 - ebizlatam.com

Kaspersky has passed the rigorous SOC 2 Type II audit, verifying that its development processes and antivirus databases meet high standards of security, integrity, and confidentiality. This certification, conducted by an independent evaluator over a full year, confirms that automatic updates are protected against unauthorized manipulation, ensuring the security of customer data. The success of this audit reinforces the company’s operational transparency and its alignment with international risk management frameworks. By undergoing periodic evaluations, Kaspersky demonstrates its commitment to accountability and stakeholder trust, integrating these practices into its global transparency initiative. This milestone is significant for open data because it establishes a precedent for external verifiability in software security. By demonstrating, through independent audits, the integrity of its processes and data, the company contributes to an ecosystem where transparency and trust in cybersecurity are fundamental to the responsible access and use of technology.
