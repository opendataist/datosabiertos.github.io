Mujeres aportan 71.5 % del valor de labores domésticas

Mujeres aportan 71.5 % del valor de labores domésticas

This study reveals that unpaid domestic work represents a significant share of national GDP, highlighting the economic invisibility of family care. Women bear the greatest burden, generating economic value exceeding that of key industrial sectors, which underscores a profound structural inequality in the distribution of responsibilities. The relevance to open data lies in the need to quantify and make visible these activities through disaggregated and accessible statistics. By integrating unpaid work into economic accounts, open datasets enable the analysis of regional and gender impacts, thereby supporting more equitable and transparent public policies.
