Inician Chimalhuacán e INFOEM 16 días de activismo contra la Violencia de Género

The initiative in Chimalhuacán underscores that ensuring women’s safety is a fundamental act of justice requiring coordinated action across institutions and civil society. By launching activities for the 16 Days of Activism against Gender Violence, local authorities aim to transform institutional duties into tangible measures that address severe challenges, such as gender alerts for femicide and disappearance. This approach emphasizes that preventing violence is not merely a procedural obligation but a necessary response to protect human dignity and rights. A critical component of this effort is the deployment of digital tools, such as thematic search engines provided by the state’s Transparency Institute. These platforms are designed to make complex information regarding gender violence declarations and action lines easily accessible to the public. By bridging the gap between official data and citizen accessibility, these tools empower individuals to navigate resources related to prevention, security, and justice, thereby fostering a more informed society capable of supporting legal and social protections. This event highlights the relevance of open data to the fight against gender-based violence by demonstrating how transparent, searchable information serves as a preventive mechanism. When citizens can easily access government records and alerts, it enhances accountability and allows for better societal monitoring of institutional responses. Consequently, open data becomes a vital instrument for advocacy, enabling communities to demand transparency and ensuring that efforts to eradicate violence are grounded in accessible, verifiable evidence rather than hidden bureaucracy.
