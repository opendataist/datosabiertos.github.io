Location revealed where local police caught driver speeding at 150mph

The article highlights the release of traffic enforcement data through a Freedom of Information Act request, underscoring the critical role of open data in promoting governmental transparency and accountability. By making specific speeding statistics publicly available, authorities demonstrate how FOI mechanisms allow citizens to scrutinize police performance and road safety efforts directly. The disclosed figures reveal extreme speeding incidents, including a driver reaching 150 mph on a motorway and another exceeding 80 mph in a residential zone. These records not only identify the severity of violations but also show that such extreme speeds often occur during early morning hours, providing insights into when enforcement is most needed. Furthermore, the text notes a significant investment in new, high-tech surveillance vans equipped with advanced cameras and automatic number plate recognition. Deploying these devices across the county aims to enhance intelligence gathering and overall road safety, illustrating how technology combined with transparent data usage supports modern policing strategies.
