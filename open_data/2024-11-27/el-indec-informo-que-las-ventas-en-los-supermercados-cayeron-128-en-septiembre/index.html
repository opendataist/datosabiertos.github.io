El Indec informó que las ventas en los supermercados cayeron 12,8% en septiembre

El Indec informó que las ventas en los supermercados cayeron 12,8% en septiembre

Sales in supermarkets and self-service stores have declined due to reduced purchasing power, with significant drops recorded in both single months and cumulative periods. While general retail suffers, the appliance sector shows mixed results, with some categories growing drastically while others, like cell phones, face steep losses. This data highlights the disparity between essential goods consumption and discretionary spending, reflecting broader economic pressures on households. The divergence suggests that while basic needs are being maintained at lower volumes, luxury or tech-related items are experiencing varied impacts based on affordability. The relevance to open data lies in the need for accessible, granular economic indicators. Transparent data allows researchers and policymakers to analyze these trends, understand shifts in consumer behavior, and develop strategies to mitigate the effects of inflation and reduced purchasing power on vulnerable populations.
