Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The Civic Coalition proposes the creation of an Open Import Data Portal to bring transparency to the licensing system and reduce the administrative discretion that facilitates corruption. This project aims to ensure that all information regarding applications—including product details, amounts, and statuses—is accessible in open and up-to-date formats. By making this data publicly available, the initiative seeks to limit abuses of power and establish an environment where regulatory decisions are predictable and fair, aligning with international standards of good governance. The project calls for a structural change in import management, mandating fixed deadlines and automatic resolution in the event of delays, thereby eliminating current uncertainty. This initiative is significant for the open data movement because it demonstrates how digital transparency can serve as a tool for civic oversight and anti-corruption efforts. By requiring the state to publish data in standardized formats, the proposal facilitates independent analysis and accountability, promoting a more efficient public administration model that is less susceptible to economic distortions or discretionary political influences.
