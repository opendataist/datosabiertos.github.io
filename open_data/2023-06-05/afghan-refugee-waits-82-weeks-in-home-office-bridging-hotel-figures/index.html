Afghan refugee waits 82 weeks in Home Office bridging hotel – figures

The article highlights the severe delays Afghan refugees face in securing permanent housing in Scotland, with some awaiting accommodation for over a year. This prolonged uncertainty in temporary "bridging" hotels undermines their ability to rebuild stable lives, illustrating the broader failures in current resettlement policies. Critics argue that the government’s approach creates unnecessary chaos, leaving vulnerable individuals in limbo without adequate support or certainty. The push to cancel controversial legislation and increase funding for proper resettlement schemes aims to replace this instability with a structured, humane system that respects the dignity and safety of refugees. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency in government statistics can expose systemic inefficiencies and human costs. By making waiting times and accommodation statuses public, citizens and policymakers can hold authorities accountable, driving necessary reforms toward more efficient and compassionate integration practices.
