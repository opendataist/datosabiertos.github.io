Open Data
ES EN

This article highlights how robust gender data is essential for identifying and overcoming systemic barriers that hinder women’s full economic participation. By leveraging insights from the World Bank’s Gender Data Portal, it demonstrates that statistics are not merely numbers but powerful tools for empowerment. These data enable policymakers to craft evidence-based interventions that address the root causes of inequality, ensuring resources reach those who need them most during global economic recovery efforts. The narrative underscores three critical challenges: the pervasive impact of gender-based violence, persistent gaps in labor force participation and income, and the economic damage caused by child marriage. Each of these issues significantly restricts women’s opportunities and well-being. Without disaggregated data, these inequalities remain invisible or misunderstood, making it impossible to design effective, gender-sensitive policies that can drive meaningful social and economic change on a global scale. Relevance to open data lies in the necessity for transparent, accessible sex-disaggregated statistics collected by National Statistics Offices. Open, high-quality data allows researchers, advocates, and governments to track progress accurately and hold institutions accountable. Ultimately, expanding access to this information is vital for monitoring development outcomes, fostering accountability, and accelerating the achievement of gender equality through informed, scalable policy decisions.
