Hay más de 150 vehículos de alta gama de mendocinos radicados en otras provincias

Mendoza residents living in other provinces register a significant number of vehicles, with a notable concentration of luxury vehicles in Jujuy. This reveals patterns of cross-border mobility and residence with significant economic implications for the region. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the importance of sharing interjurisdictional tax records. Improving transparency and access to these fiscal data facilitates regional economic analysis and strengthens administrative collaboration among Argentine provinces.
