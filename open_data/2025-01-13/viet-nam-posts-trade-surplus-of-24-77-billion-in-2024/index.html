Việt Nam posts trade surplus of $24.77 billion in 2024

Vietnam’s robust trade growth in 2024 highlights a significant economic expansion, characterized by substantial export increases and a notable trade surplus. This performance underscores the resilience of the domestic market and the continued dominance of foreign-invested sectors, which remain pivotal to national revenue. Such data provides critical insights into the structural dynamics of international trade flows, offering valuable benchmarks for policy analysis. Government strategies for 2025 emphasize institutional reform and the optimization of free trade agreements to sustain this momentum. By enhancing operational efficiency and diversifying markets, policymakers aim to strengthen competitiveness and integrate deeper into global supply chains. These strategic shifts reflect a broader commitment to creating a transparent and predictable regulatory environment, which is essential for fostering long-term trade stability and attracting further investment. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates the importance of accessible, granular trade statistics for monitoring economic health and guiding policy decisions. Reliable public data on export compositions and sectoral contributions enables researchers, analysts, and citizens to assess the impact of trade agreements and institutional reforms. Ultimately, transparent reporting supports evidence-based discourse and accountability in national economic planning.
