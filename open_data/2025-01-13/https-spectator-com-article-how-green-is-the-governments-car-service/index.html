This discrepancy undermines the administration’s green credentials, revealing a gap between political rhetoric and operational reality as leaders continue to utilize high-emission vehicles for official business. For open data enthusiasts, this situation demonstrates the critical importance of accessing and scrutinizing public sector spending and asset management records.
