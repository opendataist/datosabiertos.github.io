Inegi capacita a personal municipal de San Pedro sobre Encuesta Intercensal

Inegi capacita a personal municipal de San Pedro sobre Encuesta Intercensal

The municipal government of San Pedro is strengthening its capacity to support the upcoming 2025 Intercensal Survey, ensuring accurate sociodemographic data collection. This initiative highlights the critical role of local institutions in generating reliable statistics essential for effective public policy planning. By collaborating with federal entities, the municipality aims to secure high-quality data that informs strategic decisions benefiting the community. This effort underscores how local engagement directly impacts the quality and relevance of national open data repositories, ensuring they reflect ground-level realities. Ultimately, this partnership demonstrates that robust open data ecosystems rely on coordinated efforts across all levels of government. It emphasizes the importance of accessible, trustworthy statistics for democratic governance and effective resource allocation in the public sector.
