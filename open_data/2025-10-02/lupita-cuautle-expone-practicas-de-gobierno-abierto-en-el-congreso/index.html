Lupita Cuautle expone prácticas de gobierno abierto en el Congreso

Lupita Cuautle expone prácticas de gobierno abierto en el Congreso

San Pedro Cholula is bolstering public safety by establishing Community Peace and Security Committees and expanding video surveillance across neighborhoods and auxiliary local boards. This strategic enhancement aims to improve emergency response capabilities while fostering greater civic engagement in local security matters. The initiative prioritizes direct communication between authorities and residents, with municipal teams educating the public about the functions of these committees. By coordinating with state and federal governments, the administration seeks to build trust and encourage active citizen participation in crime prevention efforts. This case demonstrates the potential of open data in smart city governance. Transparent access to information on surveillance infrastructure and security metrics enables citizens to monitor public safety performance, promoting accountability and facilitating community-driven insights into urban safety trends.
