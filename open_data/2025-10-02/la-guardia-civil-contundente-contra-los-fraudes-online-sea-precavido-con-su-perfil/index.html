La Guardia Civil, contundente contra los fraudes online: «Sea precavido con su perfil»

La Guardia Civil, contundente contra los fraudes online: «Sea precavido con su perfil»

The article highlights the escalating threat of digital fraud, driven by the proliferation of social media and online transactions. Cybercriminals increasingly exploit personal data shared on these platforms to execute targeted attacks, such as phishing and identity spoofing. This trend underscores a critical vulnerability: the ease with which scammers can extract sensitive financial information from users who are unaware of the risks associated with their digital footprint. To counteract these dangers, the text emphasizes strict adherence to security practices, notably never sharing sensitive credentials like passwords or card numbers through unofficial channels. It stresses that legitimate institutions never request such information via email or social media. Users are advised to verify the authenticity of communications and restrict personal data exposure on public profiles to minimize the risk of targeted exploitation. This content is relevant to open data because it illustrates the real-world consequences of data visibility and misuse. While open data initiatives aim for transparency and accessibility, this scenario serves as a cautionary tale regarding the protection of personal data (OPD) within the broader data ecosystem. It highlights the necessity of balancing data utility with robust privacy safeguards, ensuring that the principles of open data do not compromise individual security or enable fraudulent activities.
