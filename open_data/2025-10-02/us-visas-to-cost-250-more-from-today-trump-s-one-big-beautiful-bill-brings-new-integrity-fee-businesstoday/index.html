US visas to cost $250 more from today: Trump's 'One Big Beautiful Bill' brings new Integrity Fee - BusinessToday

The article details a new mandatory "Integrity Fee" for US visas, designed to penalize visa violations while offering reimbursement to compliant travelers. This financial structure incentivizes strict adherence to visa terms, effectively linking immigration compliance to tangible economic consequences for both the government and applicants. Relevance to open_data lies in the transparency and accessibility of these evolving fee structures and eligibility criteria. Clear, machine-readable data is essential for accurately tracking policy changes, ensuring applicants understand complex reimbursement rules, and enabling systems to automate compliance verification efficiently. This policy exemplifies how regulatory shifts impact data infrastructure requirements. As fees become more intricate, reliable public data sources are crucial for maintaining trust, facilitating accurate cost calculations, and supporting automated compliance checks across diverse visa categories and user groups.
