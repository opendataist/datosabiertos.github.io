Cambio de tendencia turística: julio cierra con menos saturación demográfica que en 2024

Cambio de tendencia turística: julio cierra con menos saturación demográfica que en 2024

The Balearic Islands are undergoing a significant shift in human pressure trends, characterized by a decline in tourist numbers and population peaks during June and July 2025. This reversal contrasts with years of steady growth and breaks the historical pattern of increasing saturation, signaling a potential stabilization or reduction in overtourism levels that previously reached unprecedented highs in late summer. Differences across the archipelago reveal varied impacts: Ibiza reported notable drops, while Formentera saw an increase in demographic saturation. Although Mallorca’s figures remained largely stable despite growth in the resident population, the overall data indicate that fewer visitors arrived during these key months compared to the previous year, challenging the assumption of continuous mass tourism expansion. This article is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the importance of accessible, granular statistical indicators such as the Human Pressure Index. By making this data publicly available, stakeholders can monitor tourism impacts more effectively, enabling better policy-making and resource allocation to ensure sustainable development, rather than relying on aggregate annual figures that may mask critical monthly fluctuations.
