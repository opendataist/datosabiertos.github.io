El Banco Central Europeo destaca la transición energética de España hacia las energías renovables

Spain’s energy transition toward renewables demonstrates that investment in clean energy significantly reduces wholesale electricity costs and stabilizes the macroeconomy. By replacing spending on fossil fuels with domestic energy sources, geopolitical vulnerability is reduced and price stability is improved, delivering greater long-term benefits than the initial infrastructure costs. This approach strengthens Europe’s strategic autonomy and generates financial stability, mitigating disruptions to households and markets. Once capacity is built, the free availability of the energy resource, combined with reduced exposure to volatile imports, positions sustainability as a driver of economic growth and public health, going beyond mere climate objectives. This narrative is relevant to open data because it underscores the need for transparency and accessibility in financial and energy information to mobilize capital. The efficiency of capital markets and political predictability depend on open, verifiable data that enable investors and regulators to assess risks, justify investments, and monitor the real impact of decarbonization policies on regional economic stability.
