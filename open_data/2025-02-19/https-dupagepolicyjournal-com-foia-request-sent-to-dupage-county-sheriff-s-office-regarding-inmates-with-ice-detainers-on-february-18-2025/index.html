The DuPage Policy Journal seeks records of inmates with ICE detainers to expose potential violations of state privacy laws like the TRUST Act. This request underscores how transparent access to local law enforcement data is critical for monitoring federal immigration enforcement interactions at the county level. By challenging governmental secrecy through FOIA, journalists can hold sheriffs accountable for compliance with state limits on detainer cooperation. This transparency ensures that local agencies do not inadvertently extend federal immigration authority into local jail populations without legal basis. This case highlights the vital role of open data in safeguarding civil liberties. When public records are accessible, communities can verify whether local policies align with state mandates, preventing unauthorized surveillance and ensuring constitutional protections remain intact for all detainees.
