Persiste riesgo en creación de padrón de telefonía móvil

Persiste riesgo en creación de padrón de telefonía móvil

The proposed mobile phone user registry poses severe threats to privacy and data security, risking sensitive information falling into the hands of cybercriminals or being misused by security agencies. Without robust supervision and protection mechanisms, such centralization invites corruption, espionage, and the persecution of vulnerable groups, echoing past failures where private data was illegally sold. Relevance to open data lies in the erosion of citizen control and transparency. The potential disappearance of autonomous oversight bodies like INAI removes essential checks on government data handling, undermining the principles of accountability and public trust. This shift prioritizes state security over individual rights, creating a vulnerability where citizens cannot effectively contest data misuse. Furthermore, existing protections such as ARCO rights could be severely limited under the guise of national security. This legislative trend demonstrates how data collection initiatives can bypass democratic safeguards, highlighting the critical need for open governance and strict regulatory frameworks to prevent authoritarian overreach in the digital age.
