Salud de profesores se encuentra en riesgo, deudas del fomag van en aumento denuncia congresista

The article highlights a critical failure in Colombia’s new Fomag health system for teachers, revealing substantial unpaid debts that threaten its sustainability. This financial deterioration has led medical facilities to suspend care for users, indicating immediate operational risks within the pilot program. The relevance to open data lies in the transparency mechanism used to expose these failures. By analyzing public indicators obtained through a right of petition, stakeholders accessed granular financial metrics, demonstrating how open government data enables accountability and reveals systemic inefficiencies that might otherwise remain hidden. This case underscores the vital role of data accessibility in validating political claims and assessing policy success. It shows that making institutional performance metrics public allows citizens and legislators to verify facts, ensuring that data-driven evidence can influence critical decisions regarding national healthcare reforms.
