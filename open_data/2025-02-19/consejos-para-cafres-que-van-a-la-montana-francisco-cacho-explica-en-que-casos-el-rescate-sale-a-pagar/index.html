Consejos para 'cafres' que van a la montaña: Francisco Cacho explica en qué casos el rescate sale a pagar

The article highlights a significant increase in mountain rescues, attributing this surge largely to hikers who ignore safety warnings and lack proper preparation. It emphasizes that negligence—such as disregarding weather alerts or entering hazardous areas—can result in substantial financial penalties and life-threatening situations. Experts recommend meticulous planning, including informing family members, carrying essential gear, and respecting official signage. This shift toward responsible outdoor behavior aims to reduce rescue operations and ensure public safety during popular seasonal outings.
