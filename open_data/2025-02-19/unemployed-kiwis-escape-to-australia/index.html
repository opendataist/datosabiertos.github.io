Unemployed kiwis escape to Australia

Unemployed kiwis escape to Australia

New Zealand’s net migration has plummeted to its lowest level since late 2022, driven by record-high departures to Australia and slowing arrivals. This sharp decline reflects Kiwis seeking better employment opportunities offshore, as New Zealand’s rising unemployment contrasts with Australia’s stronger labor market. The resulting population loss has yielded positive economic side effects, notably crashing rental inflation and reducing house prices. Consequently, housing affordability has significantly improved, offering relief to residents strained by previous high demand. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, real-time statistical releases enable public understanding of macroeconomic trends. It highlights the critical role of accessible government data in tracking demographic shifts and their direct impacts on housing markets and societal well-being.
