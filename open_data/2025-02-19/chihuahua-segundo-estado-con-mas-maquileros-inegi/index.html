Chihuahua, segundo estado con más maquileros: Inegi

Chihuahua, segundo estado con más maquileros: Inegi

Chihuahua’s manufacturing sector exhibits a divergence between high employment levels and lower sales, revealing a gap between production capacity and market demand. This highlights limitations in the available data, as current metrics exclude subcontracted workers, potentially distorting the true economic impact. Understanding these structural gaps is crucial for open data initiatives, which must strive for comprehensive coverage. Transparent, complete datasets enable policymakers and citizens to accurately assess the health of the industrial sector beyond superficial employment figures. Ultimately, improving data granularity supports better economic decision-making. By integrating subcontracted labor into official records, open data frameworks can provide a more realistic view of regional industrial dynamics and labor markets.
