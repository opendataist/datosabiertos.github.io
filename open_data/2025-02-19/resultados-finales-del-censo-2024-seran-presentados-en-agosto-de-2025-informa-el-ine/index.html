Resultados finales del Censo 2024 serán presentados en agosto de 2025, informa el INE

Resultados finales del Censo 2024 serán presentados en agosto de 2025, informa el INE

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) is finalizing the 2024 Bolivian Census through a rigorous technical review conducted by international bodies such as CELADE and UNFPA. This process ensures data integrity and compliance with global quality standards, advancing beyond the preliminary headcount to provide comprehensive demographic insights. The final dataset will be released in August 2025, enabling detailed analysis of population distribution and living conditions. This extensive verification is crucial for generating reliable statistics that inform effective public policy. By guaranteeing consistency and accuracy, the review supports evidence-based decision-making, ensuring that governmental strategies address the actual needs of the population rather than relying on potentially incomplete initial figures. This initiative highlights the critical role of transparency and methodological rigor in open data ecosystems. High-quality, verified datasets are foundational for building trust in public information, demonstrating how independent technical assessments strengthen the utility of census data for researchers, policymakers, and civil society engaged in open governance.
