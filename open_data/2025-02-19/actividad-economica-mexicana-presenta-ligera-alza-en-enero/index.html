Actividad económica mexicana presenta ligera alza en enero

Actividad económica mexicana presenta ligera alza en enero

The timely economic indicator points to modest national growth, primarily driven by the tertiary sector—especially commerce—while secondary activities such as manufacturing are contracting. This divergence underscores a service-led recovery amid broader industrial decline, providing early signals of sectoral resilience that are essential for transparent economic monitoring. At the same time, declining inflation rates signal significant stabilization in consumer prices, reaching their lowest levels since early 2021. This trend supports adjustments in monetary policy and provides crucial data for understanding purchasing power dynamics, which is vital for assessing the real economic well-being of the population beyond nominal growth figures. This information is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how accessible, high-frequency government statistics enable timely analysis of complex economic variables. By making such indicators publicly available, institutions facilitate independent verification, promote informed public debate, and allow researchers to monitor the impact of policies in near real-time, thereby enhancing transparency and accountability in economic governance.
