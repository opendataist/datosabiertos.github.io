En Europa aprueban ley para regular la Inteligencia Artificial en la UE, se exigirá transparencia y respeto por los Derechos de Autor | NTN24.COM

The European Parliament has approved a landmark regulation governing the use of artificial intelligence, establishing the world’s first comprehensive legal framework for AI development. This legislation prioritizes a human-centric approach, balancing the drive for technological innovation with the imperative to protect fundamental rights and prevent societal manipulation, particularly in light of recent advancements like generative AI. The rules introduce strict transparency requirements and copyright compliance for general-purpose models, while imposing rigorous impact assessments and bans on high-risk applications such as mass surveillance and social scoring. By creating a dedicated European AI Office with significant enforcement powers and heavy financial penalties, the EU aims to ensure accountability and safety, distinguishing itself as a global regulator setting the standard for ethical AI deployment across critical sectors. This development is crucial for open data advocates because it directly addresses the provenance and rights management of the information feeding AI systems. The mandate for transparency regarding training data and copyright compliance implies that access to high-quality, legally compliant datasets will become a prerequisite for legitimate AI operations, thereby reinforcing the importance of open and ethical data ecosystems as the foundational layer of trustworthy artificial intelligence.
