México puede mejorar en datos abiertos gubernamentales, señala OCDE

México puede mejorar en datos abiertos gubernamentales, señala OCDE

The OECD identifies Mexico as a country with significant room for improvement in its government open data policies. This diagnosis highlights the critical importance of accessible data in addressing contemporary global challenges, ranging from health crises to the energy transition, positioning transparency as an indispensable strategic resource. The regional comparative analysis reveals that Mexico falls below the Latin American average, particularly in terms of government support for the reuse of information. While other countries demonstrate more mature policies regarding data availability, Mexico’s main weakness lies in its current inability to actively promote the practical use of these public resources beyond their mere publication. This assessment is relevant to the open data movement because it exposes the gap between the existence of data and its actual utility. The conclusion underscores that true openness does not end with the release of information, but rather with the implementation of structures that facilitate its effective reuse—a necessary step toward transforming data into tangible tools for political and social development.
