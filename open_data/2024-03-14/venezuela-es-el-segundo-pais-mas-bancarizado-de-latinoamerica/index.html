Venezuela es el segundo país más bancarizado de Latinoamérica

Venezuela ranks second in Latin America in terms of bank account ownership, a legacy of exchange controls and the demonetization of cash. This widespread financial inclusion reflects significant structural shifts in the country’s monetary habits, positioning it ahead of major regional economies in terms of basic banking access. However, experts urge caution regarding these figures. High levels of informal employment and limited access to external banking diminish the practical utility of these accounts, suggesting that mere possession does not equate to robust financial integration or economic stability for the population. This case highlights the critical role of open data in revealing hidden economic realities. By analyzing transactional and account metadata, researchers can uncover the gap between formal banking metrics and actual economic inclusion, which is essential for accurate policy assessment in complex markets.
