The National Institute of Statistics confirms that Panama is fully prepared for the upcoming Population and Housing Census, leveraging a massive volunteer workforce to ensure comprehensive coverage. This large-scale mobilization reflects a commitment to transparency and public participation, establishing a precedent for inclusive civic engagement in national data collection efforts. The census aims to gather precise demographic and housing information through direct, household-level interviews conducted by thousands of trained agents. This rigorous, face-to-face approach is designed to capture accurate, up-to-date statistics that reflect the diverse reality of the population, ensuring no segment of society is overlooked in the national record. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because the resulting granular dataset will form the foundation for future transparent government reporting. By prioritizing a modern, participatory methodology, the census generates high-quality, accessible information that enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to make informed decisions based on reliable, publicly available statistics.
