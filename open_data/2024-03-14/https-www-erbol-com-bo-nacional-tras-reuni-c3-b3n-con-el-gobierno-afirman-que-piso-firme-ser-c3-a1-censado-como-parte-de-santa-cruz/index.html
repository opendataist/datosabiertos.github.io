The indigenous community of Piso Firme will be counted in Santa Cruz, not in Beni, following a key political agreement. This decision ensures their participation in the national census and validates intergovernmental unity in the face of prior tensions. This case is vital for open data, as it highlights the struggle for statistical self-determination and the geographic accuracy of demographic data.
