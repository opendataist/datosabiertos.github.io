OpenData.org Launches Comprehensive U.S. Entity Dataset with Senzing AI – IT Business Net

The recent release of a massive U.S. organizational dataset marks a significant advancement in open data accessibility, offering unparalleled connectivity between businesses, locations, and key personnel. By integrating over 80 million organization records with millions of contacts and locations, this initiative provides a holistic view of the business ecosystem. This comprehensive mapping allows users to trace complex corporate hierarchies and decision-making structures, addressing a critical gap in available resources for understanding private and public company relationships. Crucially, the dataset’s value is amplified through a strategic partnership with Senzing.ai, which embeds advanced agentic entity resolution directly into the data architecture. This collaboration ensures that disparate records referring to the same real-world entities are accurately matched and resolved, handling variations in names and addresses without requiring specialized expertise. By enabling AI agents to autonomously identify and act on trustworthy data, this integration transforms raw information into actionable intelligence, ensuring high accuracy and immediate usability for complex analytical workflows. This development is highly relevant to the open data community as it demonstrates how open-source principles can meet the rigorous demands of enterprise-grade compliance and risk management. By providing a GDPR and CCPA-compliant, globally interoperable entity graph, the project offers a viable alternative to proprietary silos. It empowers sectors like financial services and capital markets to perform superior due diligence and fraud detection, proving that open, well-structured data can drive transparency, efficiency, and innovation across diverse industries.
