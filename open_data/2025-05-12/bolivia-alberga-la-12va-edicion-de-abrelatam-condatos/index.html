Bolivia alberga la 12va edición de Abrelatam/ConDatos

The twelfth edition of Abrelatam/ConDatos, to be held in Bolivia, will consolidate the event as the leading platform in Latin America for sharing experiences on the strategic use of open data. The event reaffirms its commitment to strengthening democracy and institutional transparency, acting as a crucial bridge to connect diverse actors across the region around a common goal: leveraging public information to improve governance and accountability. This gathering addresses critical issues that go beyond the mere publication of data, incorporating contemporary challenges such as artificial intelligence, digital sovereignty, and the protection of rights in technological environments. By fostering dialogue among governments, civil society, academia, and the private sector, the initiative seeks to build collaborative solutions that promote equity, sustainability, and integrity within the regional digital ecosystem. The relevance of this article to open data lies in its ability to demonstrate how the movement has evolved from basic transparency to a fundamental tool for defending human rights and civic innovation. By bringing together a diverse community under shared governance, Abrelatam not only preserves the knowledge accumulated over a decade but also drives regional integration and establishes standards to ensure that technology serves the common good.
