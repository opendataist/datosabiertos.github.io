Beneficial ownership register proposal ‘not a surprise’, academic says

Beneficial ownership register proposal ‘not a surprise’, academic says

The proposed beneficial ownership register aims to align Australia with international financial action standards by enhancing transparency in trusts. This measure is crucial for combating tax avoidance and financial crime, addressing the country’s current weaknesses in trust tax transparency compared to global peers. Implementing such a registry ensures that the ultimate owners and controllers of legal vehicles are publicly identified. This expansion serves as an essential anti-money laundering and tax integrity tool, fulfilling Australia’s international commitments and reducing its poor standing in tax transparency metrics reported by independent bodies. While expanding disclosure supports tax integrity, it raises significant privacy concerns among professionals and beneficiaries. The relevance to open data lies in balancing public access to beneficial ownership information with individual privacy rights, requiring careful consultation to define appropriate boundaries for published trust data.
