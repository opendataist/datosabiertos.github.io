La actividad industrial creció con un 1,1% interanual en mayo

The report from the National Institute of Statistics and Censuses reveals a significant divergence in economic performance in May, highlighting moderate growth in the manufacturing sector alongside sustained contraction in construction. This duality reflects the fragility of the real estate and infrastructure sectors, while the manufacturing industry demonstrates resilience through year-on-year increases in several key areas, albeit with monthly declines that suggest a complex dynamic. The relevance of this report for open data advocates lies in the need to break down macro-indicators into detailed, up-to-date micro-level data. The publication not only presents aggregated results but also details the specific performance of fifteen industrial divisions and fluctuations in the purchase of inputs. This level of granularity is essential for analysts, researchers, and citizens to validate economic trends, understand the causes behind rises and falls, and assess the true health of each subsector independently. Furthermore, the inclusion of business expectations transforms these data from mere historical records into valuable predictive tools. By combining objective production metrics with the subjective perceptions of economic actors, a richer and more usable information set is created. This comprehensive approach enables the open data community to develop more accurate models and fosters transparency that facilitates accountability and informed decision-making by civil society and the private sector.
