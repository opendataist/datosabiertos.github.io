ABIERTOS A ESCUCHAR Y ATENDER: Instala Alcaldesa en Solidaridad el Secretariado Técnico Municipal de Gobierno Abierto, herramienta para la participación de la sociedad civil en la toma de decisiones - Noticaribe

Solidaridad has established the Technical Secretariat for Open Government, marking its transition toward a governance model that prioritizes direct collaboration with civil society. This structural shift aims to better align municipal actions with the real needs of citizens, ensuring that development initiatives benefit the entire community through shared decision-making rather than isolated administration. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it formalizes channels for public participation and transparency. By integrating representatives from the information access institute and civil society, the municipality commits to making governmental processes more inclusive and accountable. It demonstrates how local governments can actively leverage open frameworks to address community problems, fostering trust and ensuring that data and dialogue drive tangible improvements in quality of life. The establishment of this secretariat underscores the importance of institutionalizing open governance principles at the municipal level. It serves as a replicable model for other regions, showing that when authorities listen to and work alongside citizens, they can effectively tackle societal challenges. This move reinforces the idea that open government is not merely a concept but a practical tool for enhancing democratic engagement and service delivery.
