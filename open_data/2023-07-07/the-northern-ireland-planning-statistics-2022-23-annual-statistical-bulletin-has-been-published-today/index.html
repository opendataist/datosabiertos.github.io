The Northern Ireland Planning Statistics: 2022/23 Annual Statistical Bulletin, has been published today

Northern Ireland’s planning activity significantly declined in 2022/23, with a sharp drop in both applications received and decisions made. This downturn contrasts with the severe backlogs observed previously, indicating a shift in administrative capacity. However, performance against statutory targets remains weak, with average processing times for local and major applications consistently exceeding mandated limits. The failure of local authorities to meet efficiency goals highlights systemic challenges in planning delivery. The majority of councils missed the target for local developments, while no council achieved the timeframe for major projects. These delays suggest that underlying operational issues persist, potentially hindering development progress and affecting stakeholders who rely on timely planning outcomes for decision-making. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it exposes gaps in government transparency and service efficiency. By making these statistics publicly accessible, users can critically assess administrative performance and identify areas requiring reform. The context provided, including system changes and past disruptions, is essential for accurate interpretation, demonstrating how open data supports accountability and informed public scrutiny of municipal operations.
