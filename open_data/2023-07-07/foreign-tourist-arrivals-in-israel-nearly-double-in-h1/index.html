Foreign tourist arrivals in Israel nearly double in H1

Israeli tourism revenues surged significantly in early 2023, driven by a sharp increase in international visitor numbers. This growth highlights the region's recovering appeal and economic potential for global travelers. For open data advocates, such transparent, timely statistics exemplify how accessible government metrics can effectively track and support industry performance.
