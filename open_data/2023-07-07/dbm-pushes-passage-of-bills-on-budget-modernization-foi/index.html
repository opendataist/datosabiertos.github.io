DBM pushes passage of bills on budget modernization, FOI

The Philippine government is actively pursuing budget modernization and freedom of information legislation to enhance transparency and public accountability. These reforms aim to make government spending and activities accessible to citizens, reinforcing the democratic principle that open data fosters trust and civic engagement. Transparency is increasingly recognized as a driver for economic growth, particularly in attracting foreign direct investment. By establishing an open government framework, the administration seeks to improve governance standards, which are critical for investors seeking stable and predictable regulatory environments in the region. This shift is vital for open data advocates because it institutionalizes the right to access public information through local implementations and executive orders. When governments voluntarily release data and adopt modern budgeting practices, they create a more accountable ecosystem, empowering citizens and demonstrating that open data is essential for sustainable development and effective public service delivery.
