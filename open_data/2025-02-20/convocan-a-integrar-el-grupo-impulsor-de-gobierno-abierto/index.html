Convocan a integrar el Grupo Impulsor de Gobierno Abierto

Chihuahua invites citizens to help shape open government policies, promoting transparency and civic participation through collaborative decision-making processes. This unpaid group will co-create action plans, bridging the gap between the state and civil society to jointly address community needs. This initiative is significant for open data, as it institutionalizes the cooperative frameworks needed to effectively generate, share, and utilize public information.
