PAS propone tipificar como delito en Sinaloa el gerontocidio por violencia hacia adultos mayores

PAS propone tipificar como delito en Sinaloa el gerontocidio por violencia hacia adultos mayores

The article highlights a legislative push in Sinaloa to criminalize "geronticide," addressing the systemic invisibility of violence against older adults in Mexico. By proposing specific legal frameworks, the initiative seeks to shift this issue from a private matter to a recognized social problem requiring state intervention, thereby acknowledging the severity and frequency of abuse that has long been overlooked. This legal adjustment is part of a broader strategy to establish specialized prosecution units and improve public service training. The goal is to ensure that authorities handle these cases with a human rights perspective, providing adequate psychological support and promoting the autonomy of victims. This approach aims to create a more responsive judicial and administrative system that protects vulnerable populations through specialized care and accountability. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical role of accurate statistics in shaping public policy. As seen with INEGI and CONAPRED reports, reliable data on elder abuse was essential to expose the scale of the crisis and justify legal reforms. Transparent, accessible data enables societies to identify gaps in protection, measure the effectiveness of new laws, and ensure that marginalized groups receive the visibility and resources they need to overcome systemic discrimination and violence.
