El enfriamiento es una realidad: estas son las regiones donde ya caen los precios

El enfriamiento es una realidad: estas son las regiones donde ya caen los precios

The Spanish real estate market is experiencing a significant slowdown driven by rising mortgage costs, which has led to a partial withdrawal of buyers and a decline in transaction volumes. This trend indicates that the cooling process is already a reality, with prices adjusting downward while the most dynamic regions maintain some resilience against more catastrophic forecasts. Economic uncertainty generates two opposing trends: on one hand, the postponement of purchases due to high financing costs, and on the other, investment in housing as a safe-haven asset amid volatility in other markets. This duality suggests moderate fluctuations in 2023, with adjustments expected from the second half of the year, avoiding sharp declines unless interest rates continue to rise abruptly. This analysis is relevant for open data because it highlights the need to integrate and compare diverse sources, such as official statistics from the National Statistics Institute (INE) and notarial data, to obtain a comprehensive view of the market. Correct interpretation of these indicators allows for the identification of complex economic patterns and the forecasting of future scenarios, demonstrating how transparency and access to multiple datasets are crucial for accurate economic analysis.
