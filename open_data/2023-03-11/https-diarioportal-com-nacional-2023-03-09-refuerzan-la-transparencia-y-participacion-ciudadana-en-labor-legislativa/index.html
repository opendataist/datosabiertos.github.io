Open Data
ES EN

This initiative enhances legislative transparency by requiring public access to parliamentary work through digital platforms. By facilitating open forums and collaborative feedback channels, the reform empowers citizens to actively influence policy proposals based on their genuine needs. This is significant for open data because it institutionalizes the publication of legislative materials and research outputs to the public. It shifts the paradigm toward inclusive governance, ensuring that data generated by representatives is accessible and usable, thereby strengthening democratic accountability through direct citizen engagement.
