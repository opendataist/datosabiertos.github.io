JEC proyecta plataforma tecnológica basada en el principio de Gobierno abierto

San Luis Potosí is advancing its Open Government strategy by launching a digital platform for road infrastructure. This initiative empowers citizens to request repairs and monitor projects, directly fostering transparency and public participation in governmental processes. The system simplifies administrative management while providing data-driven insights to align infrastructure programs with actual societal needs. By visualizing investment and progress details, the state strengthens accountability and encourages active civic engagement through technological innovation. This effort is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how accessible, real-time public information can transform governance. It highlights the practical application of open data principles to improve service delivery, ensure accountability, and build trust between the government and the community.
