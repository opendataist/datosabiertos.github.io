Tapan 722 registros para evitar el robo de cable

Mexico City has sealed hundreds of metro access points and deployed National Guard personnel to combat cable theft, resulting in a significant reduction in incidents. This strategic security enhancement demonstrates how integrating physical infrastructure with law enforcement can effectively address systemic vulnerabilities in public transit systems. The initiative highlights the critical role of coordinated data and surveillance integration. By connecting new security cameras to central control centers, authorities create a unified monitoring ecosystem that improves response times and deters criminal activity through real-time visibility. This case is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible information regarding public safety metrics. Sharing statistics on crime reduction and infrastructure improvements fosters public trust and enables citizens to hold governments accountable for effective resource management and service reliability.
