Organismos manifiestan apoyo a INAI

Leaders across Ibero-America reaffirm their support for Mexico’s INAI, emphasizing that its independence is crucial for regional democracy and the protection of human rights. They warn that undermining such autonomous bodies threatens decades of institutional progress and democratic consolidation across Latin America. This stance underscores the vital link between open data institutions and transparency. Protecting these agencies ensures that citizens can access information, fostering accountability and upholding the open data principles essential for modern, transparent governance.
