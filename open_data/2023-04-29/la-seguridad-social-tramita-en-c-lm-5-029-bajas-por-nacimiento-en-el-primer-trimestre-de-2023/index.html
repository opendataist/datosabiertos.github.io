La Seguridad Social tramita en C-LM 5.029 bajas por nacimiento en el primer trimestre de 2023

This article highlights the shift toward gender equality in Spanish parental leave, where a unified benefit replaced separate maternity and paternity rights. The core conclusion is that men are increasingly using their non-transferable leave, indicating a cultural and administrative normalization of shared caregiving responsibilities across the country. The data reveal a rising trend in both the number of claims and financial expenditure, suggesting greater accessibility and recognition of this individual right. Although women still take slightly longer leaves, the significant participation of fathers demonstrates that the policy is effectively breaking traditional norms, enabling more balanced family dynamics without financial penalty. This is relevant to open data because it shows how public sector statistics can measure social progress and policy effectiveness. By making detailed demographic and financial data transparent, the INSS enables researchers and citizens to track behavioral changes, ensuring that legal frameworks for equality are genuinely implemented and understood in real-world contexts.
