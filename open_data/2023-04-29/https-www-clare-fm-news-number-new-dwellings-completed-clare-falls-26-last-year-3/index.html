Clare County experienced a significant drop in new housing construction in early 2023, contrasting with a regional increase. This local decline highlights disparities in development rates and underscores the importance of granular, localized open data. Access to detailed municipal statistics allows communities to identify specific housing shortages and advocate for targeted policy interventions. Furthermore, breaking down data by dwelling type reveals structural trends in housing delivery, such as the dominance of single-unit homes. Open data initiatives that provide this level of detail are crucial for transparent planning and understanding local market dynamics, ensuring that housing strategies effectively meet community needs rather than relying on broad regional averages.
