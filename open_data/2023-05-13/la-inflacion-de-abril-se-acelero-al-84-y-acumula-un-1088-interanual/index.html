La inflación de abril se aceleró al 8,4% y acumula un 108,8% interanual

Argentina experienced another record-breaking surge in inflation in April, driven by currency instability that exacerbated the cost of essential goods such as food and clothing. This trend highlights how macroeconomic volatility directly impacts household purchasing power, creating severe financial pressure for citizens across the country. The data reveals significant disparities among economic sectors, with seasonal items and food staples suffering the steepest price increases compared to regulated prices. Understanding these distributional effects is crucial, as it shows which demographic groups face the most acute burden during periods of high inflation and exchange rate fluctuation. This report underscores the critical importance of transparent, high-quality public data for analyzing economic health. Reliable statistical outputs enable policymakers, researchers, and the public to accurately track inflation drivers, fostering informed debate and better governance within open data ecosystems.
