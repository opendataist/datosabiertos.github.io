Ministros de Ciencia del G7 buscarán compromiso en Japón sobre datos abiertos e IA - Ciencia - ABC Color

The G7 summit in Japan seeks to strike a balance between the freedom of scientific research and the protection of national security, reaffirming a commitment to fair international cooperation. This meeting underscores that data openness is essential for advancing shared scientific knowledge and facilitating strategic collaborations in critical areas such as space and the oceans. The relevance of open data lies in its explicit recognition as a key tool for scientific progress. By promoting open data, participants recognize that it enhances transparency and efficiency in global research. This enables researchers to make better use of shared infrastructure, avoid duplication, and accelerate discoveries that benefit the entire international academic community. This approach highlights how security should not hinder the exchange of information but rather protect it from malicious actors. By integrating national security with academic freedom, a more robust environment for innovation is created. Therefore, the final document aims to establish normative precedents that safeguard both data integrity and the collaborative essence of modern science in the face of current geopolitical challenges.
