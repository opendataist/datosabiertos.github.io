SC Releases 2023 Q1 Quarterly Statistics Report

Standardbred Canada’s first-quarter data reveals a resilient racing industry, with betting handle rising significantly despite fewer races. The Atlantic region demonstrated exceptional growth, outperforming other areas in key metrics like field size and wagering volume. This indicates strong regional engagement and potential for broader sector recovery. The overall increase in average field size suggests improving horse availability and fan interest. These positive trends highlight the sport's ability to maintain financial health through efficient resource allocation and sustained audience support, even during challenging seasonal periods. This report is vital for open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent, publicly accessible statistics empower stakeholders. By making detailed racing metrics available, organizations foster accountability and enable data-driven decisions that support industry transparency and informed public discourse.

Source: standardbredcanada.ca
