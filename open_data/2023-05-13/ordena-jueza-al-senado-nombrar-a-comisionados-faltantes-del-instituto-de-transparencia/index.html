Ordena jueza al Senado nombrar a comisionados faltantes del Instituto de Transparencia

A federal judge ordered the Mexican Senate to appoint the missing commissioners of the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), thereby restoring the agency’s quorum. This decision ensures that the National Institute for Transparency can resume its full operations. It is vital for open data, as INAI safeguards public access to government information and the protection of personal data.
