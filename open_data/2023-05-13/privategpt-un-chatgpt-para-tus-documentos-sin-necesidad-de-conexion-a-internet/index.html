privateGPT, un ChatGPT para tus documentos, sin necesidad de conexión a Internet

privateGPT demonstrates that it is possible to harness the power of large language models to extract valuable insights from personal documents without compromising confidentiality. By operating entirely locally, this system eliminates reliance on cloud services or internet connectivity, ensuring that sensitive data remains strictly within the user’s environment throughout the entire processing pipeline. The relevance of this tool to the field of open data lies in its ability to democratize access to contextualized information while respecting data sovereignty. It shows that institutions and organizations can release and analyze complex datasets using artificial intelligence without having to share those data with external third parties, thereby balancing informational transparency with privacy protection. Although the project is currently presented as a proof of concept with performance limitations, its existence paves the way for more secure technological architectures. This approach inspires the development of future standards and tools that prioritize privacy by design, offering a viable alternative to centralized models and fostering a digital culture where utility does not have to come at the expense of individual security.
