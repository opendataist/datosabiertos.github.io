Senate Report Details Fossil Fuel Industry’s Billions in Benefits Under Trump Administration - CleanTechnica

A recent Senate report exposes the extensive financial influence of the fossil fuel industry on the Trump administration, detailing hundreds of millions in contributions exchanged for massive regulatory rollbacks and subsidies. This political deal prioritizes corporate profits over public welfare, allowing polluters to avoid accountability while shifting the burden of health and energy costs onto American families. The findings illustrate a systemic failure where environmental protections are weakened to benefit specific industries, leaving citizens to pay the price for increased pollution and lost clean energy opportunities. This revelation is crucial for open data advocates as it demonstrates how transparent financial and policy records can uncover hidden corrupt practices. By making campaign finance, tax break details, and regulatory decisions publicly accessible, citizens and journalists can trace the direct links between money and political outcomes. Open data initiatives empower communities to demand accountability, ensuring that government actions are scrutinized and that the true costs of energy policies are visible to the public rather than obscured by complex legislative language.
