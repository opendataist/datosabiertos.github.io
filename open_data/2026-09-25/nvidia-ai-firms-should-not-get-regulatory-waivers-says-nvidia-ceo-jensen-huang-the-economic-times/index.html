Nvidia: AI firms should not get regulatory waivers, says Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang - The Economic Times

Nvidia’s CEO Jensen Huang argues that AI companies should not seek exemptions from existing antitrust or liability laws, rejecting calls for regulatory relief even as the industry faces complex safety challenges. He emphasizes that developers must take full responsibility for testing their products and ensuring safety before release, rather than relying on government leniency or waivers to mitigate legal risks. This stance is particularly relevant to open_data, as it underscores the legal accountability inherent in developing and distributing AI systems. By refusing blanket immunity, Huang highlights that the open nature of tools like Hugging Face does not shield creators from product liability, reinforcing the need for transparent, safe, and legally compliant data practices in the open-source community. Furthermore, Huang supports targeted regulation for specific AI applications, such as autonomous vehicles, indicating a nuanced view where safety demands strict oversight. This distinction clarifies that while general AI safety may rely on corporate responsibility, domain-specific implementations require robust regulatory frameworks, impacting how open data initiatives must navigate compliance and security standards.
