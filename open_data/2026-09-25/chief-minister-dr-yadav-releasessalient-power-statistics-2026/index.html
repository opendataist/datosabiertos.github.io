Chief Minister Dr Yadav releasesSalient Power Statistics 2026

Madhya Pradesh released its 2026 Power Statistics, offering a comprehensive overview of the state’s energy sector achievements and infrastructure. This publication highlights significant growth in generation capacity, consumer base, and per capita usage, providing a comparative analysis against previous fiscal years. The report details the operational performance of major distribution companies and visualizes infrastructure through an innovative Power Map. By presenting data via charts and maps, it enhances the clarity of complex metrics regarding transmission, distribution, and peak demand trends across different consumer categories. This release is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how granular, publicly accessible statistics can drive transparency in utility management. Such detailed, standardized datasets enable researchers and citizens to analyze energy efficiency, support policy advocacy, and foster accountability within critical public service sectors.
