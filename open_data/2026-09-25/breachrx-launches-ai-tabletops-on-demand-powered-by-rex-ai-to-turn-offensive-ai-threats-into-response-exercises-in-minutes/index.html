BreachRx Launches AI Tabletops on Demand Powered by Rex AI to Turn Offensive AI Threats into Response Exercises in Minutes

BreachRx has introduced AI Tabletops on Demand, an agentic AI solution that transforms cybersecurity incident response from periodic compliance checks into a continuous practice. By automating the creation and facilitation of realistic simulations, the platform allows organizations to rapidly rehearse emerging threats with cross-functional teams. This shift addresses the growing speed of AI-driven attacks, enabling companies to update their response strategies in real-time rather than relying on outdated annual exercises that leave gaps in readiness. A key component of this offering is the ability to quickly model scenarios based on current public incidents, such as a recent AI-related data breach involving Hugging Face and OpenAI. This feature demonstrates how theoretical threat intelligence can be immediately converted into actionable training for security, legal, and executive teams. By pressuring-test decision-making and communication protocols against fresh, relevant crises, organizations can identify broken handoffs and unclear ownership before a real incident occurs, ensuring that response plans remain aligned with the evolving threat landscape. This development is highly relevant to the open_data community because it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible information to maintain security resilience. The release of a downloadable, scenario-based exercise models how open-source incident data can be leveraged to improve collective defense mechanisms. It highlights a shift where open incident disclosures are no longer just historical records but active components of continuous security education, encouraging broader collaboration and faster adaptation to new risks through shared, standardized response practices.
