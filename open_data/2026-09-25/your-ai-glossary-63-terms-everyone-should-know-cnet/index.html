Your AI Glossary: 63 Terms Everyone Should Know - CNET

Your AI Glossary: 63 Terms Everyone Should Know - CNET

The rapid proliferation of artificial intelligence has created a complex technological ecosystem that demands foundational literacy, moving society past mere novelty into an era where AI functions as essential internet infrastructure. To navigate this shift, understanding the underlying mechanics—such as how large language models process data, the emergence of autonomous AI agents, and the critical distinctions between generative capabilities and hallucinations—is no longer optional but a professional necessity. This glossary highlights the tension between AI’s potential and its risks, emphasizing that technical proficiency now includes awareness of ethical safeguards, data bias, and security vulnerabilities like prompt injection. It underscores the importance of concepts such as alignment and guardrails, which are vital for ensuring systems remain safe and beneficial, while also addressing the societal impacts of "slop" and the psychological effects of human-AI interaction. For the open_data community, this resource is relevant because it demystifies the terminology surrounding model transparency and data integrity. Understanding terms like "open weights," "training data," and "synthetic data" empowers practitioners to critically evaluate AI outputs, advocate for responsible data governance, and engage effectively in discussions about the ethical development and deployment of intelligent systems.
