New National Sierra Club Report Spotlights We Energies Failing the Clean Energy Transition Amid Data Center Surge - CleanTechnica

The Sierra Club’s annual Dirty Truth Report highlights a critical failure in utility management, exemplified by We Energies’ historic low grade. This abysmal performance stems from delaying coal plant retirements and aggressively expanding gas capacity to serve new AI data centers. The utility is prioritizing corporate profit and executive compensation over customer affordability, proposing significant bill increases despite overwhelming public opposition focused on rising costs and environmental health risks. The case study reveals a troubling trend where utilities rush toward expensive fossil fuels to meet the soaring energy demands of artificial intelligence infrastructure. Instead of leveraging renewable energy, which remains the most cost-effective and clean option, companies are locking ratepayers into high-priced, polluting assets. This strategy not only exacerbates climate change through increased emissions but also drives economic strain on households, leading to affordability crises and adverse health impacts from continued fossil fuel dependence. This article is highly relevant to the open_data community because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible data for holding public utilities accountable. The Dirty Truth Report relies on measurable metrics regarding coal retirement and renewable adoption to expose poor performance, demonstrating how open data can empower citizens and regulators to demand better planning. By making utility environmental and financial impacts visible, open data initiatives facilitate public scrutiny, ensuring that energy transitions prioritize sustainability and equity rather than corporate gain.
