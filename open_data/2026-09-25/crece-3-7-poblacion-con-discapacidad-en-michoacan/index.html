Crece 3.7% población con discapacidad en Michoacán

INEGI data reveals a growing prevalence of disabilities in Michoacán, with women and older adults being the most affected. Motor and visual impairments are the most common, primarily resulting from disease and aging rather than congenital conditions or accidents. This trend highlights the urgent need for updated public policies that address specific demographic vulnerabilities. Understanding these patterns enables the prioritization of resources for elderly care and gender-sensitive accessibility programs, ensuring equitable distribution of services across the state. This information is crucial for open-data advocates, as it demonstrates how granular statistical transparency promotes inclusive governance. By making such detailed socioeconomic insights publicly available, societies can foster evidence-based decision-making that effectively addresses the needs of marginalized populations.
