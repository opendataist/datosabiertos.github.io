España se adhiere a la Carta Internacional de Datos Abiertos para impulsar la transparencia y la participación ciudadana - Nacional - murcia.com

Spain has formally joined the International Charter of Open Data, a global initiative aimed at enhancing transparency, innovation, and civic participation through the proactive release of public information. This commitment, announced at the Global Open Government Summit in Vitoria, aligns with the country’s broader strategy to foster inclusive economic development and strengthen democratic accountability by ensuring data is accessible, understandable, and interoperable. The agreement reinforces a shift toward making open data the default practice for public entities, prioritizing social impact and fundamental rights over restrictive policies. By adopting standards that ensure data is timely, complete, and usable without technical or legal barriers, the government aims to facilitate the creation of innovative solutions that improve public services. This approach not only promotes responsible governance but also empowers citizens and organizations to utilize information for meaningful societal benefits. This development is highly relevant to open data as it represents a significant institutional endorsement of open standards at the national level. It signals a strengthened political will to integrate openness into public administration, encouraging other governments to follow suit. The move underscores the critical role of interoperable data in building modern, transparent states, serving as a practical example of how international frameworks can drive concrete policy changes to enhance digital governance and civic engagement.
