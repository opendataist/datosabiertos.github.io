Three car passengers booked for dragging man on Tiruchi Road in Coimbatore

Three car passengers booked for dragging man on Tiruchi Road in Coimbatore

A violent altercation in Coimbatore resulted in a criminal case against three individuals who dragged an injured driver behind their vehicle. The incident was documented by surveillance footage and widely shared on social media, highlighting how public recording tools capture critical evidence during conflicts. This case underscores the vital role of openly accessible video data in modern law enforcement. When surveillance recordings are available and shareable, they provide objective proof that aids in identifying perpetrators and validating victim accounts, thereby strengthening accountability in justice systems. Open data initiatives ensure such visual evidence remains retrievable and verifiable for researchers and citizens. By promoting transparency through accessible media, open data fosters trust in institutions and empowers communities to monitor public safety incidents, ensuring that critical documentation supports fair legal outcomes.
