Vivienda, primaria y taquerías, lo que más aceleró la inflación: Inegi

The INEGI reports September inflation figures, highlighting divergent price trends across essential sectors. Housing, primary education, and basic food services saw cost increases, while professional services, eggs, and avocados experienced price declines. This data reveals how specific consumption categories drive overall economic pressure on households. Understanding these shifts helps consumers and policymakers identify which areas are becoming more or less affordable in the short term. Open data enables transparent analysis of these economic indicators. By accessing detailed price movements, researchers can model inflation impacts and hold institutions accountable, fostering informed public debate and better economic decision-making.
