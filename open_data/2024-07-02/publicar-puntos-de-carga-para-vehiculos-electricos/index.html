Publicar puntos de carga para vehículos eléctricos

The INAI ordered CFE to publish the locations of electric vehicle charging infrastructure, ruling that its failure to locate the documents resulted from insufficient search efforts. This decision mandates greater transparency in energy infrastructure planning, ensuring public access to critical data on national grid connectivity for sustainable mobility. This ruling is vital for open data as it establishes a precedent for the mandatory disclosure of geospatial energy assets. It compels utilities to maintain and share precise infrastructure records, enabling civic monitoring, academic research, and the development of innovative applications that rely on accurate, publicly available spatial data regarding the national power network.
