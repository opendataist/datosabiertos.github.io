INEI: Inflación en Perú rompe tendencia a la baja y aumenta a 2.29% interanual en junio

INEI: Inflación en Perú rompe tendencia a la baja y aumenta a 2.29% interanual en junio

The National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) reported a slight uptick in inflation in Metropolitan Lima during June, interrupting a prior downward trend. Although the food, transportation, and miscellaneous services categories recorded significant increases, the overall variation remained within the target range set by the Central Reserve Bank of Peru. This balance indicates that, despite sector-specific fluctuations, general macroeconomic stability is maintained, offering a predictable outlook for economic planning. Price dynamics were primarily driven by the rising cost of basic goods such as fish, vegetables, and airfares, factors that were partially offset by declines in the prices of meats and certain fruits. This contrast reflects the inherent volatility of markets for perishable goods and services. The relevance to open data lies in the need to disaggregate these figures at the product and city levels; only by accessing granular and open data can one accurately identify which specific items contribute to the aggregate change, enabling more nuanced analyses than those provided by summary reports. The report also details divergent behaviors between Lima, the rest of the country, and other indicators such as the wholesale price index and construction materials. While some urban regions experienced higher inflationary pressures, others saw price declines, highlighting territorial heterogeneity. For the open data community, this scenario underscores the importance of publishing detailed, localized time series. Without public access to such microdata, it is impossible to conduct independent research on the unequal distribution of inflationary impacts or to validate official economic models.
