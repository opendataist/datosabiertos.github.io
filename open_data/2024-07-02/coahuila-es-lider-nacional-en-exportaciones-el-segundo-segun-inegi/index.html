Coahuila es líder nacional en exportaciones, el segundo según INEGI

Coahuila consolidates its position as a national economic powerhouse, ranking as the second-largest exporter in Mexico. This achievement reflects a sustained upward trend driven by coordinated efforts among the government, the private sector, and civil society. The administration emphasizes that stability, security, and a skilled workforce are key factors in attracting investment and generating quality employment, thereby enhancing the state’s competitiveness on a broader scale. Export performance is particularly strong in specialized sectors, where the state leads or ranks highly in transportation equipment, machinery, and beverage production. These figures demonstrate a robust industrial base and efficient integration into national and international supply chains. The diversity of high-value exports indicates a resilient economy that is not dependent on a single commodity, ensuring long-term sustainability and growth potential for local industries. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the value of transparent, government-published statistical metrics in informing economic policy and investment decisions. By making such detailed export indicators accessible, stakeholders can better analyze regional strengths and opportunities for development. Ultimately, this openness fosters accountability and encourages data-driven strategies that support equitable economic distribution across all regions of the state.
