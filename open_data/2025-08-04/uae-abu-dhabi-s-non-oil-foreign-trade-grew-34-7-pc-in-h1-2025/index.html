UAE: Abu Dhabi's non-oil foreign trade grew 34.7 pc in H1 2025

Abu Dhabi demonstrates robust economic resilience through a significant surge in non-oil foreign trade, driven by strategic diversification and advanced logistics infrastructure. This growth underscores the emirate’s successful transition away from oil dependency, highlighting the effectiveness of its long-term economic planning and commitment to facilitating the smooth movement of goods across borders. The expansion in exports, imports, and re-exports reflects the impact of digital innovations and streamlined customs procedures. By deploying smart systems and integrating services, authorities have accelerated trade efficiency, reinforcing Abu Dhabi’s status as a critical node in global supply chains and a premier destination for international business and investment. This development is highly relevant to open_data as it illustrates how transparent, data-driven public sector initiatives can enhance economic transparency and competitiveness. Reliable, accessible trade statistics empower stakeholders to analyze market trends and supply chain dynamics, fostering an environment where open data supports better decision-making and validates the success of policy reforms in global commerce.
