Obama Treasury Sec Eviscerates Trump for Firing Numbers Chief: ‘Really Scary Stuff’

Obama Treasury Sec Eviscerates Trump for Firing Numbers Chief: ‘Really Scary Stuff’

Former Treasury Secretary Larry Summers warns that firing the Bureau of Labor Statistics commissioner represents a dangerous erosion of democratic norms, signaling a shift toward authoritarianism. This action parallels assaults on independent institutions like universities and the media, suggesting a systematic campaign to suppress dissent and manipulate accountability rather than address genuine policy disagreements. The dismissal is particularly alarming because employment data relies on rigorous, collective processes, making individual manipulation implausible. Summers argues this move mirrors historical abuses, such as Nixon’s “Saturday Night Massacre,” highlighting a disregard for established administrative procedures. The lack of resignations from other officials further underscores a concerning acceptance of executive overreach in undermining factual integrity. This incident is critical to open data advocates as it demonstrates how political pressure can directly compromise the independence of official statistics. When data agencies are treated as political tools rather than impartial sources of information, public trust in economic indicators collapses. Protecting the structural integrity of statistical agencies is essential to maintaining transparent, reliable governance and preventing the politicization of essential public data.
