Censo 2025 inicia este 4 de agosto con nuevas medidas de seguridad, tecnología y agilidad

Censo 2025 inicia este 4 de agosto con nuevas medidas de seguridad, tecnología y agilidad

The Peruvian government has launched the 2025 National Census, marking a significant shift toward digital governance and data security. By replacing paper forms with tablets and centralizing data on secure servers, the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) ensures that information is processed in real time while maintaining strict confidentiality. This technological modernization not only accelerates data collection but also establishes a robust infrastructure for handling large-scale demographic information, setting a precedent for future statistical operations in the country. Operational procedures have been restructured to enhance both safety and accessibility for citizens. Interviews are conducted exclusively at the door by verified agents identifiable via QR codes, eliminating the need for household members to be present if a qualified informant is available. Furthermore, the inclusion of panic buttons on census-takers' devices allows for immediate police or military intervention in risky areas. These measures demonstrate a commitment to protecting both the data collectors and the public, ensuring that the aggregation of personal information occurs within a secure and trusted environment. This census is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it generates the foundational dataset required for designing effective public policies in health, education, and housing. The transition to digital tools provides the potential for more transparent and accessible public information, provided the data is managed with ethical rigor. Ultimately, the accuracy and security of this massive data collection effort will directly influence the quality of open datasets available for analysis, fostering greater accountability and informed decision-making in national governance.
