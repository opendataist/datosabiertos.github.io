Explainer: What is OCR, and how does it work? | Biometric Update

Optical character recognition transforms static image data into editable, machine-readable formats, solving critical limitations in digital text management. By converting scanned documents into structured data, organizations can seamlessly integrate printed information into digital workflows, thereby eliminating the need for manual data entry and reducing operational errors. The technology facilitates advanced analytics and process automation by enabling businesses to extract and analyze textual content from various sources. This capability supports the efficient handling of diverse materials, including identity documents, allowing companies to streamline operations and enhance overall productivity through automated system integrations. This is relevant to open data as it democratizes access to information by converting physical records into searchable, interoperable digital assets. When identity documents and public records are digitized, they become readily available for civic innovation, transparency initiatives, and research, fostering a more open and accessible information ecosystem for society.
