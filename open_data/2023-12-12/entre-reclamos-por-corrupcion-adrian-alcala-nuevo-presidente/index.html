Entre reclamos por corrupción, Adrián Alcalá nuevo presidente

Adrián Alcalá Méndez assumes leadership of the INAI amid allegations of internal opacity and ethical concerns. His election underscores the fragility of institutions tasked with safeguarding transparency when internal governance breaks down, illustrating how leadership disputes can erode public trust. Alcalá Méndez prioritizes institutional unity and a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment to counteract political pressures. This emphasis on internal integrity is crucial, as effective oversight depends on staff feeling safe and respected in their professional environment. The controversy highlights the vital link between organizational ethics and open-data governance. Ensuring transparency in public institutions is not merely about providing access to information, but also about preserving the moral authority and operational independence needed to protect citizens’ rights against corruption.
