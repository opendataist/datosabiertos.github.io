La compraventa de vivienda baja un 11% interanual en octubre y las hipotecas se desploman un 22,4%

La compraventa de vivienda baja un 11% interanual en octubre y las hipotecas se desploman un 22,4%

The Spanish real estate market experienced a significant contraction in October, marked by a sharp decline in both property sales and mortgage origins. This downward trend reflects a broader cooling in transaction volumes, with notable drops in key regions like the Balearic Islands and Melilla, indicating widespread economic caution among buyers and lenders alike. The data reveals that mortgage issuances fell more drastically than property purchases, suggesting tighter credit conditions or reduced consumer confidence in securing long-term financing. While a few autonomous communities managed slight increases, the majority reported substantial decreases, highlighting a divergent landscape where only specific local markets resist the national downturn. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it illustrates the power of transparent, granular statistical records in identifying regional disparities and economic shifts. By making such detailed registry data accessible, analysts can better understand housing market dynamics, inform policy decisions, and provide citizens with clear insights into property value trends and financial accessibility across different geographic areas.
