En el Día de las Playas, Corona colocó una casilla de guardavidas en la fachada del World Trade Center con apoyo de CEMPRE

Corona Uruguay has strengthened its circular business model by launching a bottle return program, achieving recovery rates exceeding 50% within a year. This substantial reduction in waste underscores the country’s shift toward sustainable consumption and resource reuse on a national scale. The initiative involves millions of bottles and thousands of return points, showcasing large-scale logistical coordination. By combining iconic branding with environmental activism, the campaign educates consumers on responsible disposal while reinforcing the economic viability of closed-loop systems for beverage packaging. This case is relevant to open data because such programs generate valuable public metrics on recycling efficiency and environmental impact. Transparent reporting of these sustainability figures enables researchers and policymakers to analyze circular economy trends and compare global best practices in waste reduction.
