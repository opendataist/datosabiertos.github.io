Las demandas de disolución matrimonial aumentaron un 11,4% por ciento en Castilla-La Mancha en el tercer trimestre

Castilla-La Mancha shows a higher rate of requests for marital dissolution than the national average, indicating a regional shift in family legal dynamics. This trend highlights how localized judicial data reveals unique societal patterns that national statistics may obscure, underscoring the need for granular open data to understand demographic changes. At the same time, non-consensual modifications to parental arrangements have increased, suggesting growing legal complexities in post-separation situations. In contrast, consensual modifications and non-marital child custody procedures have declined. These diverging trends emphasize the importance of accessible, detailed legal records for policymakers monitoring evolving family structures and the effectiveness of current judicial processes. Such transparency enables researchers and citizens to scrutinize judicial efficiency and social behavior. By making these specific metrics publicly available, open data fosters informed public debate on family law, ensuring that statistical insights directly inform societal understanding of marital stability and child welfare issues.
