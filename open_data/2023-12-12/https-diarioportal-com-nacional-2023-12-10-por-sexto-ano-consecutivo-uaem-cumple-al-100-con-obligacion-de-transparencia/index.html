For the sixth consecutive year, the UAEMex has fully met its transparency obligations before the Mexican state authorities. This achievement reflects a strong institutional commitment to accountability and access to public information, aligned with national and state anti-corruption regulations. This success is significant for the open data movement, as it demonstrates the feasibility of maintaining public information systems that are up-to-date, interconnected, and accessible. By ensuring that data on procurement and public works are available in real time, the university establishes a model of proactive transparency that strengthens public trust and facilitates social oversight of public resources.
