El lunes 9 de diciembre finaliza el Censo General Agropecuario 2024

Uruguay mandates the 2024 General Agricultural Census, emphasizing its role in shaping public policy. This data collection is crucial for understanding rural dynamics, ensuring that statistical records accurately reflect the sector to inform effective government strategies and societal well-being. Compliance is mandatory for all rural landowners to avoid penalties, highlighting the government’s commitment to comprehensive data integrity. The census covers diverse properties, from active farms to recreational lands, ensuring a holistic view of agricultural resources. This initiative is vital for open data because it generates transparent, authoritative datasets that support evidence-based decision-making. By making this information accessible, authorities empower researchers and citizens to analyze rural development trends, fostering accountability and informed public discourse.
