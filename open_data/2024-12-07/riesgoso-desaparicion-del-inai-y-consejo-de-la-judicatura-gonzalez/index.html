Riesgoso desaparición del INAI y Consejo de la Judicatura: González

Riesgoso desaparición del INAI y Consejo de la Judicatura: González

The disappearance of the INAI eliminates the autonomous guarantor of access to information, fragmenting transparency and granting the Executive branch absolute control over personal data. This measure represents a historical setback in accountability, as the lack of independent oversight increases institutional opacity and the risk of misuse of public information. Integrating these mechanisms into structures with diverse political interests compromises the clarity and objectivity of data, weakening the National Anti-Corruption System. The vulnerability of the National Transparency Platform without a supervisory body exposes citizens to greater cyber risks and diminishes trust in the state's management of their private information. This process is crucial for open data because it redefines the rules of interoperability and privacy. Without rigorous autonomous standards, the availability of open data loses quality and security, affecting the ability of civil society and academia to audit power. Centralizing data control under the executive transforms open data into a tool of opacity rather than citizen empowerment.
