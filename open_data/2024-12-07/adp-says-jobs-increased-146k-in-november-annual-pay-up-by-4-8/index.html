ADP Says Jobs Increased 146K in November, Annual Pay Up by 4.8%

This report demonstrates the power of open, anonymized corporate payroll data to provide a high-frequency, near real-time view of the U.S. labor market. By leveraging fine-grained data from millions of employees, ADP and Stanford offer an independent measure that complements traditional government statistics, showcasing how accessible private sector datasets can enhance economic transparency and monitoring. The findings reveal a mixed landscape where overall private sector growth remains healthy, driven primarily by large employers, while specific sectors like manufacturing and financial services show weakness. Concurrently, wage growth for job-stayers accelerated, indicating shifting dynamics in employee retention and compensation. This highlights the importance of granular data in distinguishing between broad trends and nuanced sector-specific performance, allowing for more precise economic analysis. These insights are crucial for the open data community as they illustrate the value of partnering with institutions to utilize real-world administrative records. Such collaborations enable the creation of rich, representative pictures of economic activity that are often missing from lagging official indicators. Ultimately, this case underscores how leveraging large-scale, anonymized datasets can deepen our understanding of labor market health and inform better policy and business decisions.
