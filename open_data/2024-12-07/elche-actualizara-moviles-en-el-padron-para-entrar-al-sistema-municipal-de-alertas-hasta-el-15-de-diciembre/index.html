Elche actualizará móviles en el padrón para entrar al sistema municipal de alertas hasta el 15 de diciembre

Elche actualizará móviles en el padrón para entrar al sistema municipal de alertas hasta el 15 de diciembre

The city of Elche is expanding its emergency alert system to enhance public safety. This initiative ensures that critical warnings reach a broader audience by integrating mobile numbers from the municipal register, thereby improving community resilience during crises. This update highlights the vital role of accurate civic registries in open data ecosystems. By linking personal contact information to public safety infrastructure, the municipality demonstrates how accessible, well-maintained data can directly support efficient emergency response and citizen protection. The upcoming test message validates the system's effectiveness, showcasing transparency in public service delivery. Such initiatives underscore the importance of open data practices in building trust and ensuring that digital governance tools are reliable, inclusive, and responsive to the urgent needs of the population.
