Metrobús de la Ciudad de México: Horarios, rutas y precios actualizados

The article details the operational structure of Mexico City’s Metrobús, emphasizing its real-time service status portal. This system provides hourly updates regarding line interruptions, such as those caused by protests or station interventions, enabling commuters to make informed travel decisions amidst dynamic urban conditions. It further outlines the fare structure, highlighting inclusivity through free access for vulnerable groups and multiple digital payment options. By integrating various technologies such as contactless cards and electronic wallets, the network aims to streamline the user experience and ensure equitable access across different socioeconomic demographics. This information is relevant to open data because it illustrates how public transport authorities can leverage transparency mechanisms to enhance civic engagement. Providing accessible, up-to-date operational data facilitates better urban planning, promotes accountability in public service delivery, and encourages the development of external applications that rely on accurate, real-time transit information for the benefit of the community.
