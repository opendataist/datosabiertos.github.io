Consultoras privadas advierten que la inflación de marzo sería más elevada que la de febrero

Rising food prices threaten government inflation targets, complicating efforts to achieve ambitious single-digit goals. While recent weekly data suggests a potential cooling trend, conflicting private estimates highlight ongoing economic uncertainty and persistent upward pressure on consumer costs. This scenario underscores the critical need for transparent, standardized open data to independently validate economic indicators. When official statistics lag or conflict with private forecasts, reliable open datasets empower citizens to accurately assess financial realities, fostering trust and informed decision-making amidst conflicting public reports.
