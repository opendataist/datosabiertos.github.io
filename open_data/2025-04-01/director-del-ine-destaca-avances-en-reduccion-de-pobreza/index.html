Director del INE destaca avances en reducción de pobreza

Director del INE destaca avances en reducción de pobreza

The National Institute of Statistics emphasizes that social programs such as Tekoporâ have significantly reduced monetary poverty in Paraguay, preventing hundreds of thousands of people from falling into poverty and driving the poverty rate down to 20.1%. This underscores the critical role of policy intervention in improving citizens’ welfare, which is a core open data concern related to social equity. However, recognizing that income alone is insufficient, the institute is preparing a multidimensional analysis to capture quality-of-life factors beyond monetary metrics. By expanding the scope of data to include non-financial dimensions, this approach provides a holistic view of well-being. This shift is vital for open data, as it moves beyond simplistic indicators to reveal complex societal realities. It enables policymakers and citizens to assess the true impact of social strategies, ensuring transparency and informed decision-making in comprehensive poverty reduction efforts.
