El Indec anunció una baja de la pobreza del 14,8% en el segundo semestre del 2024 | Diario El Cordillerano

El Indec anunció una baja de la pobreza del 14,8% en el segundo semestre del 2024 | Diario El Cordillerano

The INDEC report reveals that poverty and extreme poverty in Argentina remain high, with over thirty-eight percent of the population living in poverty. This situation reflects significant ongoing challenges for millions of citizens who struggle to cover basic daily needs despite recent economic adjustments. The government highlights these figures as evidence of success, claiming a substantial reduction compared to previous administrations. They attribute this improvement to aggressive economic reforms aimed at controlling inflation and promoting macroeconomic stability. This narrative serves to contrast current policies with the perceived failure of the prior administration. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the importance of transparent, independent statistical verification in political discourse. Accessible and accurate official statistics allow citizens and researchers to critically evaluate government claims, ensuring that public policy decisions are based on reliable evidence rather than selective interpretation of socioeconomic trends.
