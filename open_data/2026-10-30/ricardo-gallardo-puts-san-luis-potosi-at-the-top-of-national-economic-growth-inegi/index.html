Ricardo Gallardo puts San Luis Potosí at the top of national economic growth: INEGI

Ricardo Gallardo puts San Luis Potosí at the top of national economic growth: INEGI

San Luis Potosí experienced notable economic growth in the second quarter, leading the country alongside Veracruz. This dynamism stems from a deliberate strategy by the state administration that prioritizes massive investment in highway infrastructure and urban connectivity. By eliminating logistical bottlenecks, the efficient movement of goods and services is facilitated, directly boosting regional competitiveness and attracting both national and foreign capital. The modernization of transportation and industrial expansion have enabled the capture of significant investments, generating new jobs and consolidating the state’s productive capacity. The continuity of these policies aims to extend economic benefits to all municipalities, ensuring that growth is not merely statistical but translates into tangible well-being for Potosino families through increased business activity. This article is relevant to the open_data community because it illustrates how public statistics, such as those from INEGI, serve as key evidence for evaluating the impact of government policies. Transparency in these indicators allows citizens and analysts to verify the effectiveness of public investment, fostering data-driven accountability and demonstrating the importance of accessible statistical information for understanding regional development.
