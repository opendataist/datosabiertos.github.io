Unemployment in Chile rises to 9.8% according to the National Institute of Statistics | HSB Noticias

Unemployment in Chile rises to 9.8% according to the National Institute of Statistics | HSB Noticias

The INE report reveals an increase in Chile's unemployment rate, highlighting the labor market's inability to absorb the growth in the workforce. This situation underscores structural challenges in generating employment, particularly in sectors sensitive to economic uncertainty. Authorities and business associations agree on the need to stimulate public and private investment to revive productive activity and hiring. Administrative simplification and the swift execution of projects are seen as key to improving the labor outlook in the short term. This issue is relevant to open data because transparency in official statistical indicators allows for monitoring the impact of public policies. Access to accurate data facilitates continuous evaluation of the labor market, ensuring that evidence-based decisions effectively address the country's social and economic needs.
