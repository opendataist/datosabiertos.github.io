Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk) is now open weight | Mistral

Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk) is now open weight | Mistral

Mistral has released the weights for Mistral Large 4, a flagship model that significantly advances the state of open-source AI in agentic coding and cybersecurity. By integrating extensive reinforcement learning, the model now leads open-weight benchmarks for code generation while dramatically reducing inference costs through improved reasoning efficiency. This release empowers developers with a high-performance, cost-effective tool that sets a new standard for autonomy in software development workflows. Beyond coding, the model establishes a new frontier in defensive cybersecurity, demonstrating superior capabilities in vulnerability detection and patching without compromising on safety. It effectively distinguishes between legitimate security operations and malicious requests, maintaining robust refusal rates against harmful intent. This balance of high utility and strong guardrails ensures that organizations can leverage powerful AI for defense while mitigating the risks associated with open-weight models in sensitive security contexts. This announcement is critically relevant to the open_data movement as it strengthens the case for sovereign AI and data ownership. By providing transparent, open-weight models trained on European infrastructure, Mistral enables enterprises and governments to customize, audit, and deploy AI within their own controlled environments. This autonomy addresses growing concerns regarding data residency and security, offering a viable alternative to black-box solutions and fostering trust in the broader AI ecosystem.
