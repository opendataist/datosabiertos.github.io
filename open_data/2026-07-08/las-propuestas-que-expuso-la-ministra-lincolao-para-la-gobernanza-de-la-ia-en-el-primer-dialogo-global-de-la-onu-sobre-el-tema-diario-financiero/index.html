Las propuestas que expuso la ministra Lincolao para la gobernanza de la IA en el primer diálogo global de la ONU sobre el tema | Diario Financiero

Las propuestas que expuso la ministra Lincolao para la gobernanza de la IA en el primer diálogo global de la ONU sobre el tema | Diario Financiero

The United Nations has established a new global framework for the governance of artificial intelligence, highlighting the urgent need for ethical and participatory regulations that prevent the concentration of power. In this context, Chile proposes a proactive vision that prioritizes continuous education, shared international standards, and the creation of multilateral networks for technology testing, aiming to transform uncertainty into economic development and social equity. The central approach focuses on self-governance and inclusion, arguing that citizen capabilities and broad participation are essential to guide the ethical character of AI. By considering this technology as critical infrastructure, it is emphasized that governance must integrate the digital and physical realms, fostering ecosystems where all actors can actively contribute, thereby preventing existing gaps from widening due to the rapid pace of technological advancement. This report is relevant to open data because Chile explicitly promotes the development of open data ecosystems as a fundamental pillar of its strategy. Expert Loreto Bravo underscores the lack of local evidence in Latin America, making it imperative to generate local data to understand the real impact of AI. Without open and accessible data, it is not possible to generate the evidence needed for informed governance, reduce gaps, and compete equitably in the new industrial economy.
