Mantener un hijo puede costar hasta $412.104 por mes, según el INDECPunto Noticias Mar del Plata.

Mantener un hijo puede costar hasta $412.104 por mes, según el INDECPunto Noticias Mar del Plata.

The article highlights a severe disconnect between the rising costs of child-rearing in Argentina and available household incomes. By measuring the comprehensive expenses required for children’s development and care, the study reveals that maintaining a child consumes nearly half of the Basic Food Basket. Although the annual increase in these costs is lower than the general inflation rate, the absolute financial burden remains prohibitive for most families, especially when compared against the Minimum Vital and Mobile Salary, which is insufficient to cover these basic needs. This economic reality underscores a significant social challenge, as a large portion of the population already lives below the poverty line. The data suggests that having children is becoming an unsustainable expense for the majority, potentially influencing demographic trends and family planning decisions. The gap between necessary expenditures and actual earnings creates a precarious situation where basic childhood development cannot be guaranteed for many citizens, exacerbating existing inequalities and highlighting the urgent need for structural support mechanisms. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates how transparent statistical methodologies can expose complex social issues. By publicly breaking down costs by age group and comparing them against official poverty lines, the data enables researchers and policymakers to visualize the true impact of inflation on vulnerable households. Such open access to granular economic indicators fosters accountability and allows for more informed debates on public policy, ensuring that decisions regarding family support are grounded in empirical evidence rather than abstract assumptions.
