Vietnam’s 10-month exports hit 391 billion USD, trade surplus continues

Vietnam’s 10-month exports hit 391 billion USD, trade surplus continues

Vietnam’s trade sector demonstrated significant resilience and growth in 2025, with total turnover rising sharply compared to the previous year. This robust expansion was driven by strong export performance, particularly from the foreign-invested sector, which continues to dominate the market. The country maintained a consistent trade surplus, highlighting its competitive position in global markets despite minor monthly fluctuations. The structure of Vietnam’s trade reveals a heavy reliance on processed industrial goods for exports and capital goods for imports. This pattern indicates that the nation is increasingly integrated into global supply chains as a manufacturing hub rather than merely a source of raw materials. The dominance of machinery and intermediate goods in imports suggests ongoing industrial upgrading and investment in production capacity. This data is highly relevant to open_data initiatives because it underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and detailed statistical records. High-quality public data enables researchers and policymakers to track economic trends, assess the impact of trade policies, and identify emerging sectors. By making such granular information available, governments can foster greater accountability and support evidence-based decision-making in an increasingly complex global economy.
