Servicios básicos: el 46,2% de los hogares no accede al menos a uno de ellos - EL DEBATE

Servicios básicos: el 46,2% de los hogares no accede al menos a uno de ellos - EL DEBATE

The article reveals significant disparities in access to basic infrastructure and essential services among Argentine households. While a majority possess running water and adequate housing, a substantial portion lacks reliable network gas or sewage connections, highlighting systemic gaps in public utility provision. Social indicators show high school attendance rates, yet economic vulnerabilities persist through high reliance on public health systems and precarious housing conditions, including exposure to floods or waste sites. These findings underscore the complex reality of living standards across major urban centers. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it illustrates how transparent statistical analysis exposes socio-economic inequalities. By making such metrics accessible, policymakers and civil society can better monitor progress, demand accountability, and design targeted interventions to improve quality of life and ensure equitable resource distribution.
