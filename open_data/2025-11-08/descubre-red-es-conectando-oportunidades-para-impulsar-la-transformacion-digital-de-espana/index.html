Descubre Red.es. Conectando oportunidades para impulsar la transformación digital de España

Red.es serves as the central public entity driving Spain’s digital transformation, integrating key infrastructure and strategic initiatives. Through its diverse units, it connects the academic community via RedIris, manages national domain registrations, monitors technological trends, and acts as the primary hub for open data accessibility. This comprehensive approach ensures that the government’s digital policy is not only implemented technically but also remains transparent and accessible to the public through DataGob. The organization’s impact extends deeply into society by funding widespread digital inclusion and innovation. Significant programs have equipped millions of students with digital connectivity, supported hundreds of thousands of small businesses, and promoted artificial intelligence research. By leveraging European funds, Red.es fosters smart cities and supports startups in international markets, ensuring that technological advancement is equitable and reaches both rural areas and major economic sectors such as health and education. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights DataGob, the dedicated platform for sharing administrative information. It illustrates how open data serves as a foundational element for public transparency and civic engagement. By providing a meeting point for citizens, companies, and administrations, Red.es demonstrates that accessible data is crucial for building trust in digital government services and enabling informed decision-making across the entire ecosystem.
