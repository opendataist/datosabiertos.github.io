Kaduna Gov’t to strengthen public participation in governance – El-Rufai - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

Kaduna State is reinforcing its commitment to open governance by expanding civic participation and transparency through its 2021 to 2023 State Action Plan. Governor Nasir el-Rufai emphasized that this initiative aims to deepen citizen engagement in decision-making processes, particularly within health and education sectors, while strengthening social protection systems for vulnerable populations. This approach builds upon previous successes, including improvements in fiscal accountability and ease of doing business, which previously earned the state top national rankings. The relevance of this development to open data lies in the structured institutionalization of transparency mechanisms like open budgeting and open contracting. By prioritizing these elements, the state ensures that critical government data is accessible and usable for public oversight. This systematic release of data empowers civil society and citizens to hold authorities accountable, fostering a culture of evidence-based policy making and collaborative governance. Ultimately, this case illustrates how open data principles serve as foundational tools for building resilient, people-centered democracies. The collaboration between government agencies and civil society organizations demonstrates that data openness is not merely a technical requirement but a strategic necessity for tackling systemic inequality. It highlights the broader implication that accessible information drives economic recovery and enhances trust between the state and its citizens, validating open data as a catalyst for meaningful civic engagement.
