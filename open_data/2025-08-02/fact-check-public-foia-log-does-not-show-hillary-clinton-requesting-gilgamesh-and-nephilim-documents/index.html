Fact Check: Public FOIA Log Does NOT Show Hillary Clinton Requesting 'Gilgamesh' And 'Nephilim' Documents

Fact Check: Public FOIA Log Does NOT Show Hillary Clinton Requesting 'Gilgamesh' And 'Nephilim' Documents

Viral claims alleging that Hillary Clinton secretly requested documents on the "resurrection chamber of Gilgamesh" are factually incorrect. A thorough review of Department of State records reveals that the request was actually filed by an individual named Denetra Senigar, not the former Secretary of State. The document explicitly lists Senigar as the requester, with no mention of Clinton, directly contradicting the false narrative circulating on social media. This case highlights the critical importance of verifying open government data against its original source material. While the Freedom of Information Act is designed to provide transparency and public access to federal records, these datasets can be easily misinterpreted or manipulated to support misleading political narratives. Understanding the precise details within open datasets prevents the spread of disinformation and ensures accurate public discourse. For the open data community, this incident underscores the necessity of rigorous source verification. It demonstrates how readily available public logs can be taken out of context to fabricate stories. By cross-referencing visual claims with the actual underlying data, analysts and citizens can maintain integrity in how government transparency is reported and understood.
