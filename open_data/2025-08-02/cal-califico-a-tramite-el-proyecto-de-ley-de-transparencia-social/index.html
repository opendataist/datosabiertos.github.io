CAL calificó a trámite el proyecto de Ley de Transparencia Social

CAL calificó a trámite el proyecto de Ley de Transparencia Social

The Legislative Administration Council unanimously advanced the Organic Law on Social Transparency, prioritizing it as an urgent economic measure. This legislation aims to prevent, detect, and sanction illicit financing within non-profit organizations. By targeting irregular capital flows, the law seeks to ensure that legal entities are not exploited for money laundering, corruption, or supporting criminal groups. The primary implication is the strengthening of institutional integrity through enforced transparency. This regulatory framework serves as a crucial tool for protecting state resources and ensuring that all organizations operate under strict legality and societal service. It reflects a broader commitment to closing loopholes that allow illegal financial activities to thrive under the guise of charitable or non-profit work. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a legal mandate for greater financial visibility in the non-profit sector. Effective transparency requires accessible, reliable data to monitor capital flows and prevent abuse. Consequently, this law drives the demand for standardized, open datasets that enable civil society and authorities to verify compliance and maintain public trust in institutional financial practices.
