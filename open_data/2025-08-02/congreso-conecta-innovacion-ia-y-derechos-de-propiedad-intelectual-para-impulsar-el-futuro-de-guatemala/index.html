Congreso conecta innovación, IA y derechos de propiedad intelectual para impulsar el futuro de Guatemala

The 2025 Intellectual Property Congress in Guatemala highlights the critical intersection of artificial intelligence and digital transformation with intellectual property rights. By bringing together public, private, and academic leaders, the event fosters essential dialogue on navigating the challenges and opportunities presented by the evolving digital landscape. This gathering underscores the necessity of robust IP protection as a foundation for national productivity and competitiveness. It emphasizes that safeguarding ideas in the digital era is not merely a matter of legal compliance but a strategic driver for sustainable innovation and economic development within the country. This matters to open data because modernizing IP frameworks ensures that emerging technologies are governed responsibly. As open data initiatives expand, clear and adaptive intellectual property standards are vital to balance the free flow of information with the protection of creators, enabling ethical AI development and trustworthy data ecosystems.
