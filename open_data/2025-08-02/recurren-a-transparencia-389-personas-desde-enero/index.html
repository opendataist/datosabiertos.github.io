Recurren a Transparencia 389 personas desde enero

Recurren a Transparencia 389 personas desde enero

The Municipality of Chihuahua demonstrates strong transparency practices by processing hundreds of information requests annually, predominantly for public data such as budgets, permits, and official conduct. This high volume indicates significant civic engagement and the practical utility of open government platforms in facilitating access to essential administrative records. The focus on non-personal public information underscores a commitment to accountability, allowing citizens to scrutinize government spending and regulatory actions. By distinguishing between sensitive personal data and transparent public records, the administration balances privacy with the democratic right to know, reinforcing trust in local governance structures. Crucially, these operational efforts feed into participatory mechanisms like citizen surveys, ensuring that open data initiatives align with actual community needs. This approach is vital for open data as it transforms static information release into a dynamic, user-driven cycle, where public feedback directly shapes future policy and government action plans.
