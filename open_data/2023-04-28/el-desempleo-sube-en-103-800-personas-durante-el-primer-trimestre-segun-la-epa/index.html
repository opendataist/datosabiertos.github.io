El desempleo sube en 103.800 personas durante el primer trimestre según la EPA

The Active Population Survey reveals a significant rise in unemployment and a contraction in employment during the first quarter, pushing the unemployment rate above the European average. This deterioration of the labor market has raised concerns among political parties and trade unions, who emphasize the need to improve support mechanisms for the unemployed and reduce inequality in access to employment. This news is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it demonstrates the critical value of public statistical series for socioeconomic analysis. By ensuring transparent access to indicators such as the EPA, institutions enable civil society, academics, and the media to verify real trends, fostering accountability and facilitating evidence-based decision-making that can be verified by any citizen.
