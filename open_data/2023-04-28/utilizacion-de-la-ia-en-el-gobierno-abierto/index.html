Utilización de la IA en el gobierno abierto

Articles addressing artificial intelligence in the context of open government highlight its transformative potential for data management and public decision-making. The integration of these technologies enables rapid and accurate analysis of large volumes of information, automating repetitive tasks and facilitating the identification of complex patterns. This technical advancement is crucial for strengthening transparency and accountability, which are central elements of the open government philosophy aimed at promoting citizen participation and more efficient administration. However, the implementation of these tools carries significant risks related to privacy and algorithmic fairness. There is concern that the use of personal data could infringe on citizens' rights if clear protection policies are not in place. Moreover, algorithms trained on historical data may perpetuate existing biases and discrimination, thereby threatening social justice. Therefore, it is essential for authorities to establish robust regulatory frameworks that ensure the ethical, transparent, and fair use of artificial intelligence. The relevance of this topic to open data lies in the need to ensure that the public information used by these systems is accessible, verifiable, and free from structural biases. Access to high-quality open data enables the auditing of artificial intelligence models, ensuring that automated decisions are explainable and fair. Promoting transparency in the development and deployment of these technologies not only strengthens public trust but also optimizes democratic governance, balancing technological innovation with the protection of fundamental rights.
