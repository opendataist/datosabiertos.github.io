Open Data
ES EN

Frontex processes personal data from its Transparency Register to prevent conflicts of interest and ensure transparency regarding lobbying efforts. By mandating the public disclosure of functions for individuals interacting with the agency, this initiative promotes accountability and integrity within its operations, aligning with strict EU data protection regulations. This approach prioritizes ethical standards over mere administrative compliance, establishing a clear framework for identifying potential biases in stakeholder engagements. The relevance to open data lies in the proactive publication of these interactions on the agency’s website. By making information about third-party stakeholders and internal representatives publicly accessible, Frontex enhances government transparency. This allows citizens and watchdogs to scrutinize potential influence networks, demonstrating how structured data disclosure can serve public interest without compromising individual privacy through robust legal safeguards. Data retention is limited to two years after relevant events, balancing accountability with the right to be forgotten. Subjects retain rights to access, rectify, or delete their information, ensuring compliance with GDPR principles. This model illustrates how open data initiatives can responsibly handle sensitive personal information, providing a template for other organizations seeking to publish conflict-of-interest registers while maintaining high standards of data protection and user trust.
