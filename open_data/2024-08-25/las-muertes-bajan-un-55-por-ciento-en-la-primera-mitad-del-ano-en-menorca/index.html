Las muertes bajan un 5,5 por ciento en la primera mitad del año en Menorca

The region’s mortality rate remains elevated compared to pandemic lows, while birth numbers are on the rise. This demographic shift underscores a natural population decline, as deaths continue to slightly outnumber births despite the recent increase in newborns. This trend highlights the complexity of the region’s demographic structure, where aging and fertility rates interact to shape long-term population sustainability. Understanding these dynamics is essential for policy planning and resource allocation in public health and social services. The relevance to open data lies in the need for transparent, accessible statistics to monitor such critical social indicators. High-quality open datasets enable researchers and citizens to analyze population dynamics, fostering informed public debate and evidence-based decision-making in addressing demographic challenges.
