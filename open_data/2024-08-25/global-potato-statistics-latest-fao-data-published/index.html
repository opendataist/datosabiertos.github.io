Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production remains a critical component of worldwide food security, with recent FAOSTAT updates ensuring that historical datasets, such as 2021 figures, remain accessible for longitudinal analysis. The article highlights a significant update to the database, incorporating the most recent 2023 data as of late 2024. This continuity allows researchers and policymakers to track trends over time without data gaps, reinforcing the reliability of international agricultural records for long-term strategic planning. The distribution of potato production reveals distinct geopolitical patterns, dominated by major economies like China and India, which collectively account for a substantial portion of the global harvest. By providing a comprehensive ranked list of producing nations, the data illustrates the uneven global landscape of agricultural output. This transparency enables analysts to identify key players in the supply chain and understand how geopolitical factors influence crop yields, offering valuable insights for trade negotiations and food security assessments. This resource is highly relevant to open_data initiatives as it exemplifies the power of accessible, standardized international statistics for diverse applications. The FAO’s commitment to publishing raw data via FAOSTAT empowers citizens, academics, and developers to perform independent analyses, fostering data literacy and innovation. By removing barriers to complex agricultural statistics, such open datasets support evidence-based decision-making and collaborative problem-solving in global food systems.
