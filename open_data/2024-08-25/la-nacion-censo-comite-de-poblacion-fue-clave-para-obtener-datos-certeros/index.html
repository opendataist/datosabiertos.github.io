La Nación / Censo: Comité de Población fue clave para obtener datos certeros

La Nación / Censo: Comité de Población fue clave para obtener datos certeros

The National Statistics Institute (INE) released the final census results after rigorous cross-checking with public institutions and international experts. This collaborative process validated the data’s accuracy, ensuring that the reported population figure truly reflects Paraguay’s current reality through extensive verification and analysis. A key finding is the significant demographic shift, particularly concerning migration and urban distribution. By integrating records from electoral and health databases, officials identified that a substantial portion of registered voters reside elsewhere, revealing widespread discrepancies between official residence registers and actual places of habitation. This transparency is vital for open data, as it demonstrates how inter-institutional collaboration enhances census integrity. It underscores the importance of using diverse, accessible datasets to correct biases, providing a more accurate foundation for public policy and resource allocation based on actual human mobility rather than static administrative records.
