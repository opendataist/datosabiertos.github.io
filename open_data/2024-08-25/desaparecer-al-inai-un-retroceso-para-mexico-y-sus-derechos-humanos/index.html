Desaparecer al INAI: Un retroceso para México y sus derechos humanos

Desaparecer al INAI: Un retroceso para México y sus derechos humanos

The article details urgent government responses to severe infrastructure damage in Puebla’s Sierra Nororiental caused by heavy rains. Major landslides have completely severed critical road sections, isolating several municipalities and endangering nearby residential structures. Immediate coordination between state and municipal authorities focuses on assessing ground stability and evacuating at-risk families. Engineers and heavy machinery have been deployed to clear debris and evaluate alternative access routes for light vehicles. However, permanent repairs remain pending due to ongoing safety risks from potential further landslides. Continuous monitoring ensures that supervision remains active while provisional solutions are tested, balancing immediate mobility needs with long-term structural integrity. This case highlights the critical role of open data in disaster management and regional development. Transparent access to real-time infrastructure conditions and risk assessments enables efficient resource allocation and public safety coordination. By making such location-based and status data openly available, governments can improve transparency, enhance community resilience, and facilitate better planning for vulnerable territories prone to natural hazards.
