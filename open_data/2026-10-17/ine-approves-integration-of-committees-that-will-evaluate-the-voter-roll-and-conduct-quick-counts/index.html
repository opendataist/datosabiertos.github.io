INE approves integration of committees that will evaluate the voter roll and conduct quick counts

INE approves integration of committees that will evaluate the voter roll and conduct quick counts

The National Electoral Institute (INE) has established expert technical committees to verify the electoral roll and calculate trends for the 2027 elections. This measure aims to ensure statistical rigor and transparency in data collection, which are fundamental elements for public trust in democratic processes. The selection of specialists in demography and mathematics underscores the importance of empirical and methodological evidence in electoral management. By prioritizing technical validity, a crucial precedent is set regarding how public institutions should structure their verification mechanisms. This case is relevant to open data, as it highlights the need for accessibility and clarity in counting procedures. Publishing the underlying methods and data would enable effective social auditing, aligning administrative transparency with the principles of open government and evidence-based accountability.
