How much does it cost to raise a child in Argentina according to official INDEC data? | Neuquén Al Instante | Digital Journalism in Patagonia

How much does it cost to raise a child in Argentina according to official INDEC data? | Neuquén Al Instante | Digital Journalism in Patagonia

INDEC has updated the child-rearing basket, establishing the monthly cost required to support children and adolescents aged zero to twelve. This indicator incorporates the value of the basic basket of essential goods and care services, reflecting the economic valuation of time invested in caregiving tasks according to the child’s developmental stage. The relevance for open data lies in the fact that this methodology quantifies unpaid care work, a critical social component traditionally overlooked in conventional economic statistics. By detailing costs across age groups and linking them to poverty indices, it generates valuable public information that enables a deeper and more transparent analysis of structural inequalities and the family care burden. This figure not only measures the cost of living but also highlights gender gaps and the real economic challenges associated with child-rearing. For the open data community, these indicators are crucial for designing more precise social policies and promoting access to public information that allows citizens to understand the true costs of child and family well-being in contemporary society.
