Việt Nam, Denmark strengthen cooperation in official statistics, digital transformation

Việt Nam, Denmark strengthen cooperation in official statistics, digital transformation

The National Statistics Office of Vietnam and Statistics Denmark have signed a memorandum of understanding to deepen cooperation in official statistics, administrative data, and digital transformation. This agreement establishes a stable, long-term framework for collaboration, leveraging the respective strengths of both nations to enhance the quality and accessibility of statistical information. The partnership aims to support evidence-based policymaking and sustainable socio-economic development through shared expertise and coordinated efforts. This collaboration is particularly relevant to open data initiatives as it prioritizes the modernization of statistical systems and the improvement of data dissemination practices. By focusing on digital transformation and better utilization of data sources, the agreement facilitates more efficient and transparent data handling. These advancements are crucial for creating reliable, timely, and publicly accessible statistics that can effectively inform governance and drive developmental strategies in the new era. Ultimately, the MoU enables Vietnam to adopt international best practices and strengthen its statistical infrastructure through knowledge sharing and professional skill development. The partnership encourages ongoing dialogue at regional and international forums, fostering a global exchange on statistical modernization. This sustained cooperation not only elevates Vietnam’s statistical capabilities but also aligns them with global standards, ensuring that high-quality open data remains a cornerstone for effective state governance and societal progress.
