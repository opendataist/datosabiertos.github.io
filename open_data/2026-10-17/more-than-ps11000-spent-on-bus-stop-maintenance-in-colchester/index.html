More than £11,000 spent on bus stop maintenance in Colchester

More than £11,000 spent on bus stop maintenance in Colchester

Osborne Street in Colchester required the highest bus stop maintenance costs in the region, totaling over £1,300 annually. While vandalism contributed to these expenses, the local council maintains that spending levels remain consistent with previous years and show no unusual patterns. This data highlights the real-world financial burden of keeping public infrastructure safe and functional, a key concern for transparent local governance. The total expenditure across 26 stops exceeded £11,000, covering general wear and tear and accidental damage. Fixed prices were established through competitive procurement to ensure accountability in these costs. For open data advocates, this illustrates how localized, granular spending data can be tracked and analyzed to verify that public funds are managed efficiently and fairly. Additionally, seven shelters were removed due to safety concerns, with costs borne by the supplier. This separation of liability underscores the importance of clear contractual data in public transport management. Understanding these maintenance and liability details allows citizens to hold authorities accountable. Such detailed, accessible records are essential for ensuring that public services remain reliable, safe, and cost-effective for all users.
