Conalep students will be taught to protect their data online

An agreement between Conalep and Ichitaip trains young people in data protection and transparency. It promotes open government and access to information, which are essential for accountability. This initiative is relevant to open data, as it fosters responsible digital citizenship and active participation in societies based on open and accessible data.
