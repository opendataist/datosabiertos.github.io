Visit us - Eurostat

Visit us - Eurostat

Eurostat actively promotes transparency by offering educational visits that demystify European official statistics for diverse audiences, including students, government bodies, and media. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it fosters public understanding of how statistical data influences daily life and decision-making, thereby bridging the gap between complex institutional work and citizen awareness. By explaining the role and products of Eurostat, the institution ensures that its vast datasets are not just accessible, but comprehensible to a broader public. The program accommodates both physical and virtual groups, providing structured presentations on Eurostat’s general overview or specific subject areas of interest. These sessions, typically delivered in English, allow visitors to hear directly from staff about the importance of data in society. This direct engagement encourages deeper appreciation for the rigor and utility of official statistics, reinforcing the value of open, reliable data sources in a democratic society. Despite high demand, Eurostat manages these interactions through a structured booking process requiring advance notice to ensure quality engagement. By maintaining an accessible and informative approach, Eurostat supports the open data ecosystem’s goal of empowering users with knowledge. This proactive communication strategy helps demystify statistical methodologies and highlights the critical role of official data in shaping policy and public discourse.
