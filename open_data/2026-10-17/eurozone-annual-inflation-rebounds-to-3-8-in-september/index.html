Eurozone annual inflation rebounds to 3.8% in September

Eurozone annual inflation rebounds to 3.8% in September

The acceleration of inflation in the euro area and the European Union signals a setback in price stabilization, driven primarily by energy and services. This widespread trend affects most Member States, highlighting significant disparities between economies with more moderate inflation and those experiencing much higher inflationary pressures. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparency and public access to reliable official statistics, such as those published by Eurostat.
