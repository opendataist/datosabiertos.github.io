INEGE publishes key statistical indicators for the second quarter of 2026

INEGE publishes key statistical indicators for the second quarter of 2026

The INEGE bulletin presents a multifaceted picture of Guinea’s economy, characterized by divergence among productive sectors and contraction in other key areas. While fishery and oil production show a rebound, significant declines are observed in timber, the alcoholic beverages industry, and energy consumption, suggesting a reconfiguration of economic activity with distinct impacts depending on the subsystem analyzed. In the social and financial spheres, notable increases in labor hiring and births stand in contrast to a sharp drop in Social Security registrations and a reduction in credit extended to the economy. These dynamics reveal structural changes in the labor market and financial stability, while also reflecting fluctuations in international commodity prices such as oil and cocoa—external factors that directly influence the trade balance and government revenues. This report is crucial to the open data movement because it democratizes access to vital macroeconomic information. By publishing detailed indicators on production, demographics, and public finances, INEGE facilitates independent analysis, institutional transparency, and evidence-based decision-making by citizens, academics, and the private sector, thereby upholding the principles of openness and accountability.
