CEGAIP debe cumplir su función de órgano garante de la transparencia: diputada

CEGAIP debe cumplir su función de órgano garante de la transparencia: diputada

Diana Ruelas stresses that the State Guarantee Commission must actively enforce transparency, as institutional opacity persists despite existing efforts. She argues that the commission should not only safeguard access to information but also lead mandatory training for officials to ensure proper compliance with data protection and transparency laws. This initiative is vital for open data because it addresses the critical need for standardized, accessible public information practices across all government entities. By coordinating with municipalities and autonomous bodies, the push for consistent compliance ensures that citizens can effectively access public resources and hold institutions accountable through transparent data sharing.
