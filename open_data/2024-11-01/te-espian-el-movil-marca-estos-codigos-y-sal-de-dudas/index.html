¿Te espían el móvil? Marca estos códigos y sal de dudas

Mobile hacking poses severe risks by granting criminals access to sensitive personal and financial data, enabling identity theft, extortion, and privacy breaches. It jeopardizes not only the victim but also their contacts, as compromised devices can spread malware or send fraudulent messages impersonating the owner. Security experts emphasize that protecting digital privacy is no longer optional but essential in our interconnected world. Awareness and proactive measures are crucial to prevent unauthorized access and maintain personal security against increasingly sophisticated cyber threats. This content is relevant to open data because it highlights the vulnerability of personal information often shared or accessible through connected devices. Understanding these threats encourages users to question how their data is handled, promoting a culture of transparency and accountability in data protection practices.
