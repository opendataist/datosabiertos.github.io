Gasto público creció tres veces más que ingresos en últimos meses del gobierno de AMLO

Gasto público creció tres veces más que ingresos en últimos meses del gobierno de AMLO

The article reveals a significant imbalance in Mexico’s public finances during the final nine months of López Obrador’s term, where government expenditure growth substantially outpaced revenue generation. This divergence highlights a structural challenge in maintaining fiscal balance, as spending accelerated at a rate nearly three times faster than income collection. Consequently, the government relied increasingly on borrowing to fund its operations, raising concerns about long-term economic stability despite short-term efforts to control the deficit. Key drivers of this spending surge included substantial allocations to autonomous bodies, particularly for electoral processes, and a historic increase in infrastructure investment. While tax reforms successfully boosted certain revenue streams like import duties and fuel taxes, these gains were insufficient to match the accelerated pace of public expenditure. This dynamic illustrates the difficulty of sustaining high levels of public spending without corresponding organic growth in economic productivity or tax bases. This scenario is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of transparent, accessible, and timely fiscal reporting. Accurate data on government spending and revenue allows citizens, researchers, and watchdog organizations to monitor public resource management effectively. By making these financial figures widely available, governments can foster accountability and enable independent analysis, ensuring that citizens can verify claims of fiscal responsibility and understand the true cost of policy decisions.
