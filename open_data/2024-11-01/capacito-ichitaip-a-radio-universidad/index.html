Capacitó Ichitaip a Radio Universidad

Capacitó Ichitaip a Radio Universidad

The article highlights the strengthening of Mexico’s robust right to information, emphasizing that citizens do not need to provide justification to request public data. This commitment fosters a culture of transparency, in which public entities are obligated to provide timely access to information. This is relevant to open data, as it promotes institutional collaboration and training to ensure broad citizen access. By integrating universities and civil society organizations, the initiative underscores that open data infrastructure must be supported by a proactive administrative culture that removes barriers to transparency.
