25N: A nivel país, 1 de cada 3 mujeres ha sufrido algún tipo de violencia, según el INE

The Ministry of Women in Paraguay has underscored the urgent need for a societal pact to eradicate violence against women, a call reinforced by alarming statistics showing that a significant majority of women have experienced some form of violence during their lifetimes. This data serves as a critical reminder of the scale of the challenge and the necessity for comprehensive, coordinated action rather than isolated interventions. To address this crisis, the institution has expanded its support network, delivering thousands of services and launching prevention campaigns that engage both the public and private sectors. Emphasis is placed on technological innovation and capacity building for police officers and civil servants to improve response mechanisms, while collaborative efforts with international organizations ensure that communication strategies remain sensitive and effective in raising awareness. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on national statistical surveys to contextualize the scope of gender-based violence, demonstrating how official data informs policy and public health strategies. By making these findings public, the government promotes transparency and accountability, enabling civil society and researchers to analyze trends, evaluate the impact of interventions, and advocate for evidence-based solutions that prioritize prevention and the protection of victims.
