MORE! Politico Is Panicked As Conservatives Flood Biden Administration With FOIA Requests

The article argues that the mainstream media’s outrage over conservative Freedom of Information Act requests reveals a fundamental hypocrisy regarding government transparency. By criticizing these filings as abusive or indicative of an ideological purge, legacy outlets expose their preference for bureaucratic secrecy over public accountability, a stance the author views as fundamentally incompatible with genuine journalism. This selective application of transparency standards suggests that the media’s credibility has eroded to the point of irrelevance. The narrative implies that if the media truly valued open data and the public’s right to know, they would actively file similar requests themselves. Instead, their silence highlights a systemic failure to serve the public interest, rendering their current moral posturing on accountability meaningless. This dynamic is directly relevant to open data advocacy because it illustrates how institutional resistance often masks a desire to control information flow rather than facilitate access. When powerful entities label transparency efforts as partisan attacks, it undermines the principle that data should be available to all citizens regardless of political affiliation. Consequently, the push for open data must address not just technical access, but the political will to enforce equal transparency standards for all administrative bodies.
