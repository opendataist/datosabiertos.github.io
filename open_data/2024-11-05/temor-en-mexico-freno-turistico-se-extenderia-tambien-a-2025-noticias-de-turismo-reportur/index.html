Temor en México: freno turístico se extendería también a 2025 | Noticias de turismo REPORTUR

The article highlights a worrying slowdown in Mexico’s tourism sector, corroborated by recent data from INEGI. This contraction affects both domestic and international visitors, signaling a broader weakening of the industry that, while not yet catastrophic, demands immediate strategic attention to prevent deeper economic impacts. A primary driver of this trend is the ongoing aviation crisis, characterized by fleet reviews and reduced seat availability from major carriers. This logistical bottleneck threatens to prolong the downturn, as airlines face significant operational challenges that directly limit capacity and hinder tourist mobility during critical seasonal periods. This narrative is crucial for promoting open data, as it demonstrates how public statistical agencies like INEGI serve as essential tools for validating independent research. It underscores the necessity of accessible, reliable datasets to monitor economic health, diagnose industry-specific bottlenecks, and inform policy responses that ensure sustainable tourism development amid external shocks.
