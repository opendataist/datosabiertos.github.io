Anuncia Monreal: En dos semanas reforma para eliminar autónomos

The proposed constitutional reform to dissolve transparency institutes is nearing final approval, with their abolition expected within two weeks. Civil society and experts warn that this move eliminates essential mechanisms for access to information and data protection. This threatens open data governance by removing independent oversight, undermining transparency rights and accountability in the public sector.
