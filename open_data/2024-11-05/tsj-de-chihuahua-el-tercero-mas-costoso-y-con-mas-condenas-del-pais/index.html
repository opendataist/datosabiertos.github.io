TSJ de Chihuahua, el tercero más costoso y con más condenas del país

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) released federal and state justice census data, revealing Chihuahua’s high judicial output. This transparency highlights the judiciary's significant resource usage and sentence volume, ranking third nationally. The statistics show a massive influx of cases with substantial resolutions. Detailed records on civil, family, and penal matters provide insight into judicial efficiency and the specific nature of legal conflicts processed. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, enabling analysis of judicial performance and resource allocation. It allows citizens and researchers to evaluate the fairness and effectiveness of the state's legal system through accessible, granular statistical evidence.
