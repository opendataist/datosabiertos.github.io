El PP da una prórroga de tres meses para gestionar el cierre de Anticorrupció

The article highlights significant misappropriation of public funds due to corruption, criticizing the potential conflict of interest when government officials are investigated by their own institutions. It argues that transferring such sensitive cases to the executive branch undermines accountability and transparency. This situation directly impacts open data initiatives by obscuring critical information about failures in public administration. The lack of independent oversight hinders the availability of accurate, accessible data needed to effectively monitor governance and detect irregularities. Ultimately, the political maneuvering described stifles public scrutiny and reinforces a culture of secrecy. For open data advocates, this underscores the urgent need for independent structures that ensure data integrity and public access, rather than allowing political entities to control investigative outcomes.
