Una familia necesita ingresos mensuales por casi 500 mil pesos para no caer en la pobreza

The report highlights severe economic deterioration in Argentina, revealing that high inflation has drastically increased the income thresholds required for families to avoid poverty and indigence. This surge in the cost of basic necessities directly reflects the broader inflationary crisis, demonstrating how rapidly rising prices erode purchasing power and push more households into financial vulnerability. The data underscores a significant rise in the poverty rate, particularly affecting minors, indicating a deepening social crisis. By linking household income needs to official consumption baskets, the study provides essential evidence of how macroeconomic indicators translate into tangible hardship for citizens, especially the most vulnerable demographic groups. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it offers transparent, official metrics on social welfare and economic inequality. Such accessible datasets enable researchers, journalists, and civil society to independently verify claims, analyze trends in poverty, and advocate for policies addressing the root causes of economic exclusion.
