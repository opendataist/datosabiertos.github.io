Operación "tapadera" entre la Corte y el INAI, "no se tocan": AMLO

The Supreme Court’s attempt to classify total salary amounts as confidential directly contradicts the core democratic principle of transparency. President López Obrador strongly condemned this move, arguing that such secrecy undermines public trust and violates the fundamental rules of democratic accountability. This incident is crucial for open data advocacy, as it highlights the persistent conflict between institutional secrecy and the public’s right to access essential government financial information.
