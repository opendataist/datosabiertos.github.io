Solicitarán al Municipio y Carabineros conocer despliegue de seguridad dispuesto para el show de Peso Pluma en Viña 2024 | Puranoticia.cl

Deputy Andrés Celis has called for transparency regarding the security plans for Peso Pluma’s performance in Viña del Mar, arguing that the artist glorifies drug trafficking. This stance has sparked a public debate about the alignment between local cultural policies and the fight against organized crime. The demand for public information underscores the importance of access to government data on security measures. By requesting specific details about the police deployment, the politician reinforces the principle that institutions must be accountable for managing community risks and protecting citizens. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates how requests for transparent information can be used to question political and cultural decisions. It demonstrates that access to data not only informs about logistics but also enables citizens to assess the coherence between the State’s actions and its social commitments.
