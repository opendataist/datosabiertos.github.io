Unión está entre los cinco equipos de Argentina inhibidos por la FIFA

FIFA has launched a centralized digital platform that publishes the list of clubs subject to player registration bans. This initiative represents a crucial step toward institutional transparency, enabling any stakeholder in the football ecosystem to access in real time the disciplinary status of these entities. By removing information barriers, the organization facilitates informed decision-making by players, executives, and the general public, reducing the data asymmetry that previously hindered the assessment of contractual and administrative risks. The system regularly updates its information and covers all levels of the sport, from professional to amateur teams, across multiple countries. This demonstrates a firm commitment to the integrity of the game, serving as a constant reminder of the need to comply with regulations. The open availability of this data on judicial sanctions not only deters future violations but also holds clubs accountable, ensuring that penalties are applied uniformly and visibly to all parties interested in the proper governance of world football. This action is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how the publication of data by governments and international organizations can improve accountability and market efficiency. By making disciplinary decisions accessible and verifiable, FIFA sets a standard of interoperability and clarity that other entities should follow. This empowers civil society and data experts to audit regulatory compliance, turning administrative information into a public tool to promote fairness and trust in sports institutions.
