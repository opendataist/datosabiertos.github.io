Value of imports was $1.2bn in 2022

Bermuda’s import values surged significantly in 2022, driven by post-pandemic economic recovery. This growth highlights the island’s increasing reliance on external supplies for essential goods like food and fuel, underscoring the critical role of international trade data in monitoring economic stability. Trade patterns reveal a heavy dependence on the United States, with the majority of imports originating there. Understanding these geographic dependencies is vital for open data initiatives, as it exposes potential supply chain vulnerabilities and informs policy decisions regarding economic resilience and diversification. Although exports also grew, reaching their highest level since 2013, the scale remains modest compared to imports. This disparity emphasizes the need for transparent, accessible trade statistics to help stakeholders analyze the broader economic health and identify opportunities for sustainable growth beyond basic commodity consumption.
