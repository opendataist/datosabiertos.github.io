Obtiene municipio primer lugar en Datos Abiertos e implementación PEFA Género

Chihuahua achieved national leadership in open data transparency by launching a comprehensive portal covering critical public services and becoming the first Mexican municipality to complete a PEFA Gender evaluation. These milestones demonstrate how systematic data publication and gender-responsive fiscal frameworks significantly enhance accountability and citizen trust in local governance. This achievement is highly relevant to the open data movement as it validates the practical application of structured public information for improving resource management. By integrating gender perspectives into financial planning, Chihuahua proves that open data is not just about availability, but about creating measurable, equitable outcomes. This sets a precedent for other municipalities to adopt similar transparent, data-driven approaches to strengthen public administration and ensure resources directly benefit vulnerable populations.
