GitHub - lingodotdev/lingo.dev: ⚡️ Open-source AI-powered CLI for web & mobile localization. Bring your own LLM or use Lingo.dev localization engine. Join discord: https://lingo.dev/go/discord

Lingo.dev offers a sophisticated localization engineering platform that transforms how teams manage translation quality. By enabling the creation of stateful, configurable localization engines, it allows organizations to integrate large language models with specific brand voices, linguistic rules, and glossaries. This approach ensures consistent, context-aware translations across different products and markets, moving beyond simple raw model outputs to deliver polished, culturally appropriate content. The platform’s relevance to open data lies in its structured approach to managing multilingual content as scalable, programmable assets. It provides robust integration capabilities via CLI, API, and CI/CD pipelines, allowing organizations to automate the extraction, translation, and storage of localization files within standard development workflows. This infrastructure supports the open sharing and reuse of translation assets, such as glossaries and rulesets, across multiple projects or engines, fostering a more transparent and maintainable data ecosystem. Ultimately, Lingo.dev empowers teams to measure and improve translation accuracy through AI reviewers and rigorous testing environments. By centralizing configuration and enabling collaborative proofreading, it establishes a reliable framework for handling international data. This system promotes the systematic organization of linguistic resources, making multilingual data more accessible, verifiable, and ready for integration into broader open data initiatives or globalized digital services.
