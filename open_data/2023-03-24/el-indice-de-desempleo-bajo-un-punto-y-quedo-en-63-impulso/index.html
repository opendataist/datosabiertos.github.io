The report highlights a notable decline in unemployment and demand-driven underemployment despite high inflation, signaling labor market resilience. This stability, alongside GDP growth, underscores the complexity of Argentina's economic recovery, where job metrics improve amidst severe inflationary pressures. The data reveals a workforce dominated by salaried employees, yet a significant portion lacks pension deductions. This structural insight is crucial for open data initiatives, as it exposes gaps in social security coverage that traditional economic indicators might obscure. Understanding these disparities allows policymakers and researchers to access granular labor realities. Open data facilitates deeper analysis of employment quality and social protection, ensuring that economic progress is measured not just by quantity, but by the security and rights of workers.
