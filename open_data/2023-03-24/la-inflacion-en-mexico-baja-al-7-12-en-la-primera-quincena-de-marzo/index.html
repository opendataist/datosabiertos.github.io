La inflación en México baja al 7.12 % en la primera quincena de marzo

Mexican inflation showed a slight deceleration in early March, marking four consecutive fifteen-day periods of decline and easing market expectations. This trend follows significant price increases throughout 2022, driven primarily by the agricultural and energy sectors. Despite the slowdown, the underlying price index remains high, indicating persistent pressure on consumer goods and services that excludes volatile items. The report highlights that general inflation remains far above the central bank’s target range, continuing a prolonged period of elevated prices. While specific sectoral contributions show mixed movements, the overall cost of living continues to outpace monetary policy goals. This environment underscores the challenges faced by households and businesses as price levels remain stubbornly high despite recent corrective measures. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, real-time statistical releases empower public understanding of economic health. By making granular indicators like core and headline inflation accessible, institutions foster accountability and allow researchers to analyze the effectiveness of monetary policies. Such visibility is essential for building trust in public statistics and supporting evidence-based economic discourse.
