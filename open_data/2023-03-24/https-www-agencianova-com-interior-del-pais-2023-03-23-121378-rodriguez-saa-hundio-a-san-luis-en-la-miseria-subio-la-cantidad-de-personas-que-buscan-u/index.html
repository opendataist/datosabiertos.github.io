Employment indicators show a rise in underemployment and open unemployment in San Luis, challenging the official low unemployment rates by expanding the definition of who is considered employed. This shift underscores how beneficiaries of social programs are classified as employed, significantly altering the apparent health of the labor market. Such methodological nuances are crucial for open data, as they reveal how definitions affect transparency and the public’s accurate understanding of economic reality.
