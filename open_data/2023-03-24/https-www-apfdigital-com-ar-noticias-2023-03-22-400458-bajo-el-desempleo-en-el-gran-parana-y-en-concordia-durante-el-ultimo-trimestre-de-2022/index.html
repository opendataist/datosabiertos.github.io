Argentina’s unemployment rate reached historic lows in late 2022, reflecting broader regional improvements in Entre Ríos. Both Gran Paraná and Concordia experienced significant declines in joblessness, signaling a robust local labor market recovery. However, rising demand for employment and increased underemployment suggest underlying instability. While headline unemployment improved, many workers remain in precarious situations, indicating that job quality has not kept pace with the drop in open unemployment figures. This data is relevant to open_data because precise, localized metrics enable researchers to analyze labor dynamics beyond national averages. Accessible, granular datasets allow for a deeper understanding of regional economic disparities and inform policy interventions targeting underemployment and labor quality.
