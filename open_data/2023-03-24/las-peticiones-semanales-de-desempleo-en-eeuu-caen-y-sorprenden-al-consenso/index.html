Las peticiones semanales de desempleo en EEUU caen y sorprenden al consenso

Initial jobless claims in the United States fell to 191,000, catching markets off guard with a better-than-expected decline even as the total number of unemployed workers edged up slightly. Oxford Economics points out that persistently low claims signal a tight labor market, which could lead to future interest rate hikes by the Federal Reserve, although caution is warranted due to tensions in the banking sector. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it offers high-frequency, granular labor metrics. Transparent and accessible government statistics enable analysts to monitor economic health in real time, promoting more informed policy decisions and greater public accountability regarding employment trends.
