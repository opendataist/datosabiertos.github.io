El mercado coge velocidad: la compraventa de viviendas se dispara un 47,7% en octubre

The Spanish real estate market experienced a robust recovery, marked by a significant surge in both property transactions and mortgage signings. This dual growth indicates a strong rebound in economic activity, moving past previous seasonal and administrative disruptions. The data reveals that momentum has been consistent and widespread, suggesting renewed confidence among buyers and financial institutions across the country. Regionally, the upturn was universal, with every autonomous community and city reporting increased year-on-year growth. While some areas saw modest gains, others recorded dramatic spikes, particularly in mortgage volumes. This broad-based expansion demonstrates that the recovery is not isolated to specific demographics or locations but represents a comprehensive strengthening of the national housing sector. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it relies on transparent, standardized, and freely accessible administrative records from the College of Registrars. By leveraging this high-quality public data, researchers and analysts can accurately model market trends, identify regional disparities, and assess the impact of economic policies. Such transparency fosters trust in statistical outputs and enables deeper insights into structural changes within the economy.
