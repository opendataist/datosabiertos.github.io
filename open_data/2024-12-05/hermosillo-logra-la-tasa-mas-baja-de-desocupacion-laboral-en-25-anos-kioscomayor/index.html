Hermosillo logra la tasa más baja de desocupación laboral en 25 años - Kioscomayor

Hermosillo logra la tasa más baja de desocupación laboral en 25 años - Kioscomayor

Hermosillo has reached its lowest unemployment rate in twenty-five years, outperforming other major northern border cities. This historic low signals a robust local economy that is successfully generating formal employment opportunities, marking a significant shift from previous years when higher joblessness was more common. The decline in unemployment is attributed to an economic boom driven by the growth of the automotive industry and substantial public works investment. These factors create a multiplier effect that stimulates not only construction but also related supply chains and labor markets, demonstrating how strategic infrastructure spending can broadly support economic vitality and job creation. This report is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, high-quality statistical information from institutions such as INEGI. Access to such granular, reliable labor market data enables researchers and policymakers to verify economic trends, assess the effectiveness of public policies, and make evidence-based decisions that enhance community well-being through accurate public knowledge.
