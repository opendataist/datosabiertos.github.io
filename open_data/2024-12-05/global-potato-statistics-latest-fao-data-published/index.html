Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production is a major agricultural sector, with over three hundred million metric tonnes harvested annually across numerous nations. China and India dominate this landscape, accounting for the largest shares of total output, while significant contributions come from countries in Europe, North America, and parts of Asia and Africa. This wide geographic distribution highlights the crop’s importance as a staple food source for diverse populations worldwide. The underlying data driving these insights originates from standardized international reporting systems. By aggregating national statistics into a unified global framework, organizations provide a consistent view of agricultural trends. This consolidation allows for reliable comparisons between different regions, revealing patterns in cultivation intensity, yield efficiency, and total volume that might otherwise remain hidden within disparate local reports. This resource is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how publically accessible, standardized datasets enable transparent analysis of global food security. Researchers and policymakers can freely extract, verify, and utilize this information to understand supply chains, assess economic impacts, and monitor changes over time. The availability of such granular, country-specific metrics empowers stakeholders to make evidence-based decisions without relying on proprietary or restricted information sources.
