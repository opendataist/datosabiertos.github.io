El tráfico aéreo en los aeropuertos españoles crece un 5,81% en noviembre

El tráfico aéreo en los aeropuertos españoles crece un 5,81% en noviembre

The article highlights significant growth in Spanish air traffic, with Alicante and Madrid-Barajas leading operational increases and international routes showing substantial expansion. This surge reflects a recovering sector where major carriers dominate, underscoring the importance of comprehensive data collection for understanding market dynamics and competitive landscapes. However, the report also reveals rising vulnerabilities, evidenced by increased flight cancellations linked to extreme weather events such as DANA and specific punctuality metrics. These disruptions illustrate how external shocks impact aviation reliability, emphasizing that robust data monitoring is essential for identifying risks and improving operational resilience in the face of environmental challenges. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it provides transparent, granular insights into infrastructure performance and service quality. By making such detailed records available, stakeholders can better analyze trends, ensure accountability among operators, and support informed decision-making for both public policy and consumer awareness within the aviation ecosystem.
