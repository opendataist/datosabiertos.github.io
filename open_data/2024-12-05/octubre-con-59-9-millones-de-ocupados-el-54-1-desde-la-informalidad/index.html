Octubre con 59.9 millones de ocupados; el 54.1% desde la informalidad

Octubre con 59.9 millones de ocupados; el 54.1% desde la informalidad

The article reveals that, despite a slight annual decline in the number of informal workers, this sector continues to absorb more than half of the country’s employed population. This persistent dominance underscores a structural challenge in the labor market, where the majority of workers lack formal protections and benefits, indicating that current economic growth has not sufficiently shifted employment toward stable, regulated conditions. Crucially, the data show that while informal employment decreased slightly on a year-over-year basis, the monthly rate of entry into informality increased. This trend suggests that the formal sector is struggling to absorb new labor force participants, as formal job creation remains stagnant and significantly lower than the influx of informal jobs, leaving the formal workforce at a standstill compared to the expanding informal pool. This report is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the need for accessible, granular labor statistics to accurately assess economic health. Transparent data enable researchers and policymakers to distinguish between headline employment figures and the quality of those jobs, ensuring that public discourse focuses on sustainable formalization rather than merely total employment numbers.
