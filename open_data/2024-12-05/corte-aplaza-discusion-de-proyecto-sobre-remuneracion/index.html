Corte aplaza discusión de proyecto sobre remuneración

The Supreme Court postponed its decision on INEGI, avoiding confirmation of the legality of the salary cuts imposed by the austerity policy. This delay, justified by the need to link the case with another pending controversy, generates uncertainty about transparency in the management of public resources. This issue is relevant to open data because it directly affects the availability and reliability of government statistical and financial information. The lack of resolution prevents clarification of the limits on remuneration, which are essential elements for auditing and publishing accurate budgetary data. The prolonged suspension underscores the tension between administrative decisions and public accountability. Without transparency in remuneration, the quality of open data is compromised, hindering independent analysis and citizen oversight of the proper use of public funds.
