Informan sobre filtración de datos personales en Centro Nacional de Información Crediticia

A recent data breach at Vietnam’s credit information center highlights the vulnerability of centralized personal datasets to cyberattacks. This incident underscores the critical need for robust security protocols when managing sensitive public information. The relevance to open data lies in the inherent tension between transparency and privacy protection. While open data promotes accessibility, this case demonstrates that unsecured personal records pose significant risks, necessitating stricter safeguards to prevent misuse and maintain public trust in data handling practices.
