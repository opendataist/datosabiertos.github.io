Cría cuervos y se esconderán en el baño / Angélica Cristiani Mantilla / La Lengua de Tácita Muta - Plumas Libres

Cría cuervos y se esconderán en el baño / Angélica Cristiani Mantilla / La Lengua de Tácita Muta - Plumas Libres

The critique exposes how the implementation of the Motorcycle Management Program in Veracruz has led to a crisis of citizens' rights, where bureaucracy acts as a mechanism of exclusion and humiliation rather than modernization. The text denounces structural saturation, systematic mistreatment by officials, and institutional negligence, challenging the official narrative that criminalizes managers instead of recognizing the administrative obstacles created by the State. From the perspective of open data, this case underscores the urgency of making public information accessible, transparent, and truthful to prevent opacity from trapping citizens. When digital processes fail and physical infrastructure deteriorates, the lack of access to real data on operational capacity and citizen complaints perpetuates impunity. Transparency is not just an ethical principle but a necessary tool to demand accountability and ensure that basic services do not become instruments of coercion. The relevance of this article lies in its warning about how administrative opacity erodes democratic trust and normalizes arbitrariness. For the open data movement, it implies the critical need to monitor and audit government practices through access to information. Only by revealing the discrepancies between promises of modernization and the oppressive reality can the culture of secrecy be combated and the dignity of public service restored.
