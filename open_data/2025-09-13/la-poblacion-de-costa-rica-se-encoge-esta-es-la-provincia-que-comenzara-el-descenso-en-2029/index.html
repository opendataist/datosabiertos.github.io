La población de Costa Rica se encoge: esta es la provincia que comenzará el descenso en 2029

La población de Costa Rica se encoge: esta es la provincia que comenzará el descenso en 2029

The article highlights that Costa Rica will undergo a fundamental demographic shift by mid-century, characterized by stagnation and decline in the population of the Central Valley. Cartago will be the first province to reach its peak population, marking the beginning of a trend that will extend to the main cities of the Greater Metropolitan Area. This pattern contrasts with coastal regions such as Guanacaste and Limón, which will continue to grow steadily, thereby altering the national demographic distribution. The significance of these data lies in their ability to inform strategic public planning. Projections from the National Institute of Statistics and Census (INEC) reveal that the demand for basic services will vary drastically across regions. While central urban areas will face challenges associated with an aging or shrinking population, expanding areas will require new infrastructure. This divergence demands that policies for allocating resources in health, education, and housing be differentiated and dynamic, adapting to the specific realities of each territory. This study is crucial for the field of open data, as it demonstrates how the analysis of time series and statistical projections enables the anticipation of complex social changes. Transparency in these data facilitates accountability and evidence-based decision-making by citizens and lawmakers. By visualizing how resources must be redistributed according to population trajectories, the government can optimize its public management, ensuring that urban and social planning effectively responds to the future demands of the population.
