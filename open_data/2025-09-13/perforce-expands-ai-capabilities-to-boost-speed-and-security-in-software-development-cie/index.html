Perforce expands AI capabilities to boost speed and security in software development - CIE

Perforce expands AI capabilities to boost speed and security in software development - CIE

Perforce has introduced Delphix AI, an embedded artificial intelligence model within its DevOps Data Platform designed to generate high-fidelity synthetic data. This innovation allows development and testing teams to automatically create realistic, compliant datasets without relying on sensitive production information. By integrating this capability directly into the platform, organizations can streamline their software delivery pipelines, ensuring that testing environments remain secure and aligned with privacy regulations while maintaining data integrity for accurate application validation. The solution addresses growing industry concerns regarding the governance and management of synthetic data, which Gartner predicts will become a critical challenge for many leaders. Unlike traditional masking tools that only protect existing data, AI-generated synthetic data creates new, safe scenarios for testing and analytics. This distinction is particularly vital for regulated industries like healthcare, where organizations must balance the need for robust innovation with strict privacy obligations, enabling safer development without compromising on speed or compliance. This release is highly relevant to the open_data community as it demonstrates how closed, enterprise-grade systems can responsibly utilize AI to generate data that mimics real-world distributions without exposing private information. It highlights a practical approach to data utility where synthetic generation supports research and testing needs while respecting data sovereignty. By keeping the AI model air-gapped and operating within the IT perimeter, Perforce shows how organizations can adopt advanced data techniques that prioritize security and ethical AI use, setting a precedent for secure data practices in modern software engineering.
