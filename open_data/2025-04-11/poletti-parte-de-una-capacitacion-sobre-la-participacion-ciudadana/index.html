Poletti, parte de una capacitación sobre la participación ciudadana

Poletti, parte de una capacitación sobre la participación ciudadana

The article highlights an EU-backed initiative that empowers Argentine municipalities to strengthen open data practices and citizen participation. It underscores how transparent access to data enables communities to make better-informed decisions and engage actively in governance. The project aims to enhance institutional capacities through digital tools, fostering more transparent and collaborative local management. By equipping officials with practical skills, the program seeks to deepen the connection between public administration and the public. This is significant for open data because it demonstrates a scalable model for integrating transparency into local governance. It illustrates how technical training and policy support can transform raw data into actionable civic engagement, ensuring that public records serve democratic participation rather than mere information storage.
