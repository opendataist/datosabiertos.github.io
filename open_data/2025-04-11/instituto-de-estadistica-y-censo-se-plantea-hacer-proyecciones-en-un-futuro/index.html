Instituto de Estadística y Censo se plantea hacer proyecciones en un futuro

Instituto de Estadística y Censo se plantea hacer proyecciones en un futuro

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INEC) in Panama is initiating a strategic shift toward generating future projections to guide national decision-making. Rather than merely providing static data, the institute aims to offer predictive analysis that helps government officials navigate complex policy choices. By modeling various scenarios, the INEC intends to highlight the trade-offs between different options, allowing leaders to understand the specific benefits and drawbacks of their decisions regarding economic growth, public services, and infrastructure. This approach is particularly relevant to open data because it transforms raw statistics into actionable intelligence for societal benefit. Predictive models enable better planning in critical areas such as healthcare, education, and agriculture by anticipating demographic trends and economic shifts. The emphasis on understanding the "gray areas" between policy options underscores the need for nuanced, evidence-based governance. This ensures that public resources are allocated efficiently to meet future needs, promoting sustainable development and improving the quality of life for citizens through informed strategic planning. To achieve this, the INEC is undergoing a digital transformation, moving away from manual Excel-based processes toward automated database systems and data engineering. This modernization includes integrating public records from the Electoral Tribunal, Social Security, and tax authorities to accelerate data availability and reduce costs. By sharing these interconnected databases with other ministries, the institute enhances transparency and efficiency. This initiative demonstrates how open data interoperability can streamline public administration, allowing for faster, more accurate updates to essential surveys and fostering a culture of data-driven governance.
