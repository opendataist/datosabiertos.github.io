COPRED lanza convocatoria al 3er Concurso de Investigación sobre Discriminación en la CDMX - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

The COPRED initiative aims to stimulate scientific research into discriminatory practices affecting vulnerable groups in Mexico City. By funding these studies, the program seeks to generate critical insights for designing effective anti-discrimination policies across legislative, judicial, and administrative sectors. Open to academics and civil society members, the contest prioritizes collective proposals addressing key issues such as employment bias, racial discrimination, and stigma. This structured approach encourages a comprehensive analysis of complex social dynamics rather than isolated incidents. This opportunity is vital for open data because it promotes the public dissemination of high-quality research findings. Publishing these results ensures transparency and provides the community with accessible, evidence-based knowledge necessary for informed advocacy and policy reform.
