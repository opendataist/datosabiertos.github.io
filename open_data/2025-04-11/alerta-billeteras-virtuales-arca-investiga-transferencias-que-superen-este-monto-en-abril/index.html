Alerta billeteras virtuales: ARCA investiga transferencias que superen este monto en abril

Alerta billeteras virtuales: ARCA investiga transferencias que superen este monto en abril

The Argentine tax authority, AFIP (Administración Federal de Ingresos Públicos), has updated the transaction thresholds for banks and digital wallets, adjusting limits in line with inflation. This ensures that financial institutions continue to report customer transactions that exceed specific monetary amounts, enabling the state to effectively monitor economic activity. When users exceed these new thresholds, they must justify the legal origin of their funds by providing documentation such as pay stubs, invoices, or proof of income. Failure to provide this evidence may result in account closure or the filing of suspicious activity reports, underscoring strict compliance with anti-money laundering regulations. This information is particularly relevant for open data advocates, as it highlights how government financial surveillance directly impacts citizen privacy. It underscores the tension between regulatory transparency and individual rights, demonstrating the substantial volume of personal financial data collected and analyzed by public agencies.
