La AEPD publica el borrador de suPlan estratégico 2025-2030e inicia un proceso de consulta pública - LegalToday

La AEPD publica el borrador de suPlan estratégico 2025-2030e inicia un proceso de consulta pública - LegalToday

The Spanish Data Protection Agency has launched a public consultation on its Strategic Plan 2025–2030, aiming to define priority actions for the next five years. This initiative highlights a commitment to active listening, inviting citizens, professionals, and various sectors to contribute their insights to the draft. By promoting transparency, the Agency seeks to adapt its operations to emerging digital challenges and identify areas for improvement in its future activities. The strategy is structured around eight guiding principles and six strategic axes, including technology-enabled intelligent supervision and a strong commitment to responsible innovation. Key priorities involve strengthening national and international cooperation, facilitating regulatory compliance—particularly for small businesses—and ensuring organizational excellence through digital transformation. These objectives reflect a shift toward proactive risk prevention and the efficient use of technological tools to safeguard privacy. This article is highly relevant to open_data, as it illustrates how public institutions are integrating stakeholder participation into their governance frameworks. By prioritizing transparency and collaborative policymaking, the Agency demonstrates a model in which open processes enhance the legitimacy and effectiveness of data protection regulations. Such approaches are essential for building public trust in digital ecosystems and ensuring that data policies remain inclusive, responsive, and aligned with societal needs in an increasingly complex technological landscape.
