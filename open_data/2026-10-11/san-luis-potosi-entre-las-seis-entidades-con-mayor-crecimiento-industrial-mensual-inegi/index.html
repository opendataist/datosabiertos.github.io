San Luis Potosí, entre las seis entidades con mayor crecimiento industrial mensual: INEGI

San Luis Potosí has consolidated its position as a key industrial hub by registering monthly growth, placing it among the states with the highest national dynamism. This performance reinforces the theory that collaboration between the public and private sectors is crucial for regional competitiveness, highlighting manufacturing as the primary driver of the local economy. Despite adjustments in sectors such as construction and mining, the resilience of the manufacturing sector demonstrates its capacity to sustain productive development. This duality underscores the need to diversify the economic base and strengthen business capabilities to mitigate the volatility of other traditional sectors. The relevance of open data lies in the fact that indicators like the IMAIEF provide transparency and traceability regarding real economic performance. Access to this data enables the analysis of trends, evaluation of public policies, and informed decision-making on investments and development strategies, democratizing the information necessary for sustainable growth.
