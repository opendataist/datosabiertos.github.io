Luz Adriana Vásquez, exsecretaria de Infraestructura de Cali, defendió su gestión en el cargo y anunció que se concentrará en su defensa jurídica

The former Secretary of Infrastructure of Cali, Luz Adriana Vásquez, advocates for the modernization of public contracting as a crucial strategy to ensure transparency and efficiency in the use of state resources. Her management transformed manual processes into digital systems with full traceability, demonstrating that the adoption of technological tools can significantly optimize public spending and strengthen oversight of administration. This case illustrates how open data and interoperability are not merely theoretical concepts, but practical mechanisms that generate tangible results in government management. However, this experience underscores the risks faced by public servants who challenge established practices. Vásquez reported the pressures and complaints that arose after implementing disruptive changes, even resigning from a subsequent position to defend her management against disciplinary investigations. Her narrative highlights the need for an open government ecosystem that protects those who promote integrity and innovation, rather than silencing them, recognizing that institutional resistance often accompanies any genuine attempt at transparency. This article is relevant to open data because it directly links the openness of information with the protection of public officials. It emphasizes that transparency must be accompanied by institutional frameworks that safeguard innovators, preventing technical reforms from being penalized politically. By stressing the importance of supporting those who risk their careers for the common good, the text proposes that the culture of open data should include support and defense mechanisms for public servants who use information to improve efficiency and combat opacity.
