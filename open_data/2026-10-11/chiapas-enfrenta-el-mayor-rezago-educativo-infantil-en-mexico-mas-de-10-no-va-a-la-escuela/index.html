Chiapas enfrenta el mayor rezago educativo infantil en México: más de 10% no va a la escuela

Chiapas enfrenta el mayor rezago educativo infantil en México: más de 10% no va a la escuela

Chiapas faces the greatest educational challenges in the country, leading nationally in child illiteracy and showing significant rates of school absenteeism. These data reveal a structural lag that severely limits the development and future opportunities of Chiapas’s child population, affecting both girls and boys equally. The persistence of these gaps indicates that access to school does not necessarily guarantee the acquisition of basic literacy skills. This discrepancy underscores the need to review the quality and relevance of educational processes, as mere attendance does not resolve fundamental learning deficits. This information is crucial for the open data movement, as it demonstrates how transparency in public statistics enables the identification of deep regional inequalities. By making these lags visible, it facilitates accountability and the demand for more effective public policies that prioritize educational equity.
