User support - Eurostat

User support - Eurostat

Eurostat outlines its customer support protocols to ensure users receive appropriate assistance for their statistical inquiries. The institution strongly recommends utilizing its interactive web-based communication system for methodological questions or data verification. This written channel is deemed essential for addressing complex issues that require detailed, traceable responses which cannot be adequately managed through verbal exchanges. For users encountering technical difficulties or simply seeking general data access on the website, direct telephone contact is available across numerous European languages. By offering dedicated hotlines with specific operating hours for each region, Eurostat facilitates immediate troubleshooting and navigation support. This multi-channel approach ensures that diverse user needs, ranging from simple data retrieval to urgent technical problems, are met with timely and localized assistance. This information is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accessible, multi-modal support systems in maintaining the usability of public datasets. By providing clear pathways for user engagement and technical support, statistical agencies enhance the transparency and reliability of open data initiatives. Effective support mechanisms encourage broader data usage and foster trust in the integrity of the information provided to the public and researchers.

Source: ec.europa.eu
