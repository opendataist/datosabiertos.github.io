Capacitación para municipios

Capacitación para municipios

Cefim and INEGI are training municipal governments to integrate data from the 2025 Intercensal Survey into public planning. This initiative aims to enable city councils to use updated statistical indicators to design accurate diagnoses and optimize resource allocation according to the specific needs of their communities. The initiative empowers local authorities with robust analytical tools, facilitating evidence-based decision-making. By gaining a better understanding of the population in key areas such as health and the economy, municipalities can develop more effective programs and respond more swiftly to existing social challenges. This collaboration is relevant to open data because it promotes transparency and the practical use of public information. It demonstrates how the availability of open and interoperable statistics strengthens governance, allowing citizens to see how data directly transforms the quality of public services and municipal management.
