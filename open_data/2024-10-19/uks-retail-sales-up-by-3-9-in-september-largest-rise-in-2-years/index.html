UK’s retail sales up by 3.9% in September, largest rise in 2 years

UK’s retail sales up by 3.9% in September, largest rise in 2 years

UK retail sales hit their highest annual growth in years, driven by technology and non-food sectors, though volumes remain slightly below pre-pandemic levels. This economic activity relies heavily on accurate, timely open data from the Office for National Statistics to track sector-specific trends. Transparent public datasets enable policymakers and businesses to understand market shifts, such as the divergence between digital and grocery retail performance. Access to such granular, open economic indicators is crucial for fostering informed decision-making and maintaining trust in national statistical reporting.
