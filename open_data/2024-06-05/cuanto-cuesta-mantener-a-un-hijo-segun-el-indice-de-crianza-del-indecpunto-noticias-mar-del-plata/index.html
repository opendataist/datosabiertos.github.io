Cuánto cuesta mantener a un hijo, según el Índice de Crianza del INDECPunto Noticias Mar del Plata.

The INDEC’s new Child-Rearing Index reveals that raising children costs significantly more than previously estimated, driven by a comprehensive evaluation of goods, services, and essential care time. This updated metric incorporates critical gender perspectives by valuing unpaid care work, offering a more accurate reflection of household economic burdens. Costs have surged dramatically over the past year, with inflation impacting all age groups by over 230 percent. Even short-term increases highlight persistent economic pressure on families, demonstrating how rapidly living expenses for minors escalate across food, housing, and transport categories. This data is vital for open_data as it sets a new benchmark for public policy and social welfare analysis. By making detailed cost structures transparent, it enables researchers and citizens to scrutinize family economics, demand equitable resource distribution, and hold institutions accountable for supporting child development and gender equality.
