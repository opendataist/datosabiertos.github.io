Bioweapons launderer Fauci and associates illegally deleted emails and federal records to protect themselves from FOIA requests – NaturalNews.com

The article details allegations that former NIH officials, including Dr. Anthony Fauci and Dr. David Morens, engaged in a coordinated effort to conceal their involvement in controversial pandemic research. Released communications suggest these individuals deliberately obscured evidence of their connection to gain-of-function studies potentially linked to the virus’s origins. The narrative implies a systemic attempt to shield their actions from public scrutiny and congressional oversight, raising serious questions about transparency and accountability within high-level scientific and governmental decision-making during the global health crisis. Central to these claims are tactics designed to evade Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests, such as deleting sensitive correspondence, using private email accounts for official business, and employing deliberate misspellings to bypass automated searches. These methods highlight a concerning disregard for public records laws, which are fundamental to democratic accountability. By shifting official communications to private channels, the accused purportedly created a "secret back channel" to discuss sensitive matters, effectively removing them from the public domain and preventing independent verification of their professional conduct and financial interactions. This case is critically relevant to open data principles because it illustrates how institutional actors can undermine transparency when operating outside public scrutiny. The alleged manipulation of digital records and evasion of information requests demonstrate a direct conflict with the ethos of open access, where citizens should be able to examine the data and communications that inform public health policy. If such practices are confirmed, they represent a severe erosion of trust, suggesting that critical decisions regarding global health security may have been made without the necessary level of open data disclosure and public oversight.
