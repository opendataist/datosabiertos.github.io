Canasta básica alimentaria subió 5,2% en mayo y la básica total avanzó 7,2% - Noticias de Mar del Plata.

These shifts underscore the critical need for transparent and accessible price monitoring to understand the economic impact. Open data ensures that this vital information remains publicly available, empowering communities to track affordability and demand better policy responses through verified statistics.
