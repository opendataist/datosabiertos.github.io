Instituciones revisan avances en políticas de transparencia

Government entities report progress on transparency and open data initiatives under the National Open Government Plan. These efforts strengthen accountability and citizen participation in governance. This process ensures that public institutions meet their commitments regarding digital services and data transparency. It enhances institutional integrity and fosters trust through verifiable results. This is relevant to open data as it demonstrates state-led efforts to publish information and engage citizens. Such transparency mechanisms are foundational for building trustworthy, accessible, and participatory digital ecosystems.
