Freedom of Information statistics: January to March 2023

The UK government continues to track the implementation of the Freedom of Information Act and Environmental Information Regulations, ensuring these transparency tools remain active mechanisms for public accountability. By monitoring usage within central government bodies, officials maintain the integrity of open data practices and facilitate informed public debate through reliable access to information. These statistics hold National Statistics status, confirming they meet rigorous standards for trustworthiness and quality. This designation ensures that the data added to public discourse is robust, adhering to strict codes of practice. Such high-quality reporting is essential for maintaining public confidence in the availability of government-held information and the institutions managing it. The ongoing collection and assessment of this data highlight the commitment to transparent governance. Soliciting user feedback further refines these statistics, ensuring they remain relevant to evolving public needs. This cycle of production, validation, and improvement underscores the critical role of high-quality official statistics in sustaining an informed and engaged society.
