Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The proposal by the Civic Coalition aims to establish an Open Data Portal for Imports to replace discretionary systems like SIRA, which are criticized for fostering corruption and limiting trade through opaque, constantly changing regulations. The core objective is to eliminate bureaucratic discretion by mandating that all import licenses, including product types, amounts, and statuses, be published in real-time, digital, and open formats. This shift prioritizes transparency over the current model, which lacks clear criteria and often ignores established timelines, thereby exposing citizens and businesses to arbitrary administrative decisions and potential misuse of public power. This initiative is fundamentally relevant to open data principles because it transforms government data from a restricted administrative tool into a public resource. By enforcing the publication of information in open formats and guaranteeing public access, the project aligns with international standards for regulatory transparency. It demonstrates how open data can serve as a structural safeguard against corruption, ensuring that citizens can monitor state actions and hold officials accountable through accessible, verifiable, and complete datasets rather than relying on hidden bureaucratic processes. Furthermore, the proposal highlights the critical role of open data in correcting market distortions caused by exchange rate restrictions. By making import processes simple, predictable, and chronological, the legislation seeks to reduce the arbitrary protection of foreign currency reserves that currently hampers economic activity. This approach illustrates how data openness can facilitate fairer market conditions, reduce the need for restrictive economic policies, and enhance the rule of law by ensuring that administrative decisions are based on transparent, publicly available information rather than secret or flexible interpretations of regulations.
