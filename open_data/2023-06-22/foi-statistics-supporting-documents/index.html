FOI Statistics: supporting documents

This document outlines the long-term evolution of Freedom of Information statistics, highlighting a consistent commitment to methodological transparency. The main conclusion is that the statistical agency actively maintains and updates its development plans, ensuring the data remains reliable and relevant for public scrutiny. Relevance to open data lies in its demonstration of sustainable governance. By publishing detailed development plans over many years, the agency provides a clear roadmap for how information is collected and processed. This practice builds trust and allows researchers to understand the context and limitations of the data they access. Ultimately, this history underscores the importance of continuous improvement in official statistics. It shows that open data is not static but requires ongoing refinement. This approach ensures that FOI metrics accurately reflect institutional performance, serving as a vital tool for accountability and informed decision-making in society.
