Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

Deputies from the Civic Coalition propose the creation of an Open Import Data Portal to transform the management of licenses in Argentina. Their main objective is to eliminate the administrative discretion that characterizes the current system, which has been subject to numerous modifications that generate uncertainty and the risk of corruption. By requiring the complete publication of all data related to applications in open formats, they aim to guarantee universal and transparent access to public information. This project entails a structural shift toward administrative simplification, establishing strict deadlines and the principle of positive administrative silence, where a lack of response is considered approval. The initiative aligns with international recommendations on governance, emphasizing that regulatory transparency is fundamental to reducing abuse of power and fostering predictability. By making the criteria and status of each procedure public, the opacity that currently hinders trade and distorts the market is eliminated. The relevance of this proposal for the open data field lies in its practical approach: it transforms government data into tools for accountability. By requiring that information be published in open and digital formats, it not only meets technical standards but also empowers civil society and the private sector to audit state administration. This case illustrates how data transparency can combat corruption and improve economic efficiency, demonstrating that open data are essential for institutional integrity and trust in public policies.
