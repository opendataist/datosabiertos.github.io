Open Data
ES EN

Japan’s ruling allows AI training on public data without copyright permission, establishing a permissive precedent for data utilization. This shifts the burden toward creators to seek new protective regulations, fundamentally altering how open datasets are legally accessed. Consequently, developers can freely analyze and train models on existing works, accelerating innovation while highlighting urgent gaps in current intellectual property frameworks for the open data ecosystem.
