Freedom of Information

This resource supports open data by empowering journalists to secure environmental information through Freedom of Information Act tools. It provides practical guides and templates for accessing public records, fostering transparency in environmental reporting. By facilitating media access to data, it underscores the critical role of informed public discourse in safeguarding environmental interests.
