Se disparan los pasajeros de media distancia y alta velocidad en abril más de un 30%

The liberalization of the railway market, combined with lower ticket prices, has significantly boosted train travel in Spain. This shift demonstrates that competitive pricing and open market access effectively stimulate demand for both medium- and long-distance high-speed rail services. As a result, passenger numbers in this sector have surged, indicating that removing monopolistic barriers directly benefits consumer usage and sector growth. This trend is part of a broader recovery in public transportation, with all modes—including urban metro and intercity buses—experiencing substantial year-over-year increases. The data suggests that diverse transportation options are becoming more accessible and popular among citizens. Improvements in urban mobility, particularly in major cities, highlight how integrated and competitive systems can efficiently handle increased passenger volumes. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the value of transparent statistical reporting from institutions such as the National Statistics Institute (INE). By making detailed mobility metrics publicly available, researchers and policymakers can analyze the real-world impact of regulatory changes. Such data-driven insights are essential for evaluating the effectiveness of open data policies in promoting market competition and improving public service infrastructure.
