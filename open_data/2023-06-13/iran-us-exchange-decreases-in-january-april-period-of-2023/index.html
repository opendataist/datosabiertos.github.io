Iran-US exchange decreases in January-April period of 2023

Trade volumes between the US and Iran remain minimal despite recent increases, ranking low among global partners. This highlights how geopolitical sanctions severely constrain bilateral economic activity, limiting transparency. Such data underscores the critical need for open datasets to monitor sanctioned trade dynamics.
