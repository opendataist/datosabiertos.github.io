La compraventa de viviendas bajó un 7,4% anual en C-LM en abril con 1.896 operaciones

The article reports a significant decline in Spanish real estate transactions in April, driven by high interest rates intended to curb inflation. This trend reflects a broader national slowdown affecting both new and used housing markets, marking the lowest sales volumes in two years and continuing a three-month period of negative growth. Regional data highlight a sharp monthly drop in property transfers, with rural and urban lands showing mixed transfer methods such as inheritance and sales. Nationally, the surge in borrowing costs has disproportionately impacted buyers, leading to a substantial decrease in voluntary purchases, while other transfer methods like inheritance remain steady. This analysis is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how public statistical indicators can track economic pressures on households. By making such granular transaction data accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand the real-time impact of monetary policy on housing affordability and market stability.
