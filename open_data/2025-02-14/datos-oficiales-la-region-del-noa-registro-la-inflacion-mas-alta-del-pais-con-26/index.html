Datos oficiales: la región del NOA registró la inflación más alta del país con 2,6%

Datos oficiales: la región del NOA registró la inflación más alta del país con 2,6%

The January 2025 consumer price index shows that national inflation has slowed to 2.2%, indicating a possible shift in economic policy. This deceleration, driven by seasonal industry slowdowns and regulated prices, enables the government to lower currency devaluation and interest rates, aiming to stabilize domestic prices through an adjusted exchange rate mechanism. Regional disparities persist, with Argentina’s northwest experiencing higher inflation than the national average, largely due to costs in hospitality and housing. Although interannual growth is lower than in the previous year, food prices remain a key driver of current price levels, underscoring ongoing challenges in achieving comprehensive price stability across all sectors. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the need for granular, regionally disaggregated statistics. High-quality public datasets allow analysts to identify local economic pressures that national averages might mask, fostering more precise policy evaluations and enhancing transparency in how macroeconomic decisions affect diverse communities across the country.
