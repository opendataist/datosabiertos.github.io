El 2025 arrancó con una inflación nacional de 2,2%, la más baja desde 2020

Argentina’s January inflation fell to 2.2%, marking the lowest rate since mid-2020 and confirming a sustained disinflationary process driven by current economic policies. This deceleration highlights significant reductions in specific sectors, contrasting with higher increases in hospitality and housing, which reveals nuanced price dynamics across the economy. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of transparent, timely statistical releases. Accurate public indices enable better analysis, fostering trust in institutional metrics and supporting evidence-based economic decisions by citizens and researchers.
