Invita Municipio a firma de declaratoria de Gobierno Abierto

Invita Municipio a firma de declaratoria de Gobierno Abierto

Chihuahua launches its second Open Government Action Plan through a formal declaration, inviting citizens to join the steering group. This collaborative effort aims to enhance transparency, integrity, and public access to information. The initiative strengthens the local governance model by actively involving society in decision-making. It fosters a strong culture of accountability, ensuring that governance remains close to the people. This is significant for open data, as it commits to improving information accessibility and transparency. By engaging citizens in policy design, it promotes the open exchange of government data, which is essential for democratic participation and trust in public institutions.
