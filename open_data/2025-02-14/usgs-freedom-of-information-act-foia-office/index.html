USGS Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Office

USGS Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Office

The Freedom of Information Act establishes a fundamental right for public access to federal records, emphasizing government transparency. While exemptions exist, agencies must disclose information whenever possible, reinforcing the principle that open data is a default expectation rather than a privilege. Proper submission requires precise written requests that reasonably describe the sought records and address potential fees. Users must identify the specific agency division holding the data before submitting, as email requests are not accepted, highlighting the procedural rigor necessary for effective data retrieval. This process is vital for open data initiatives as it provides the legal mechanism for citizens and organizations to obtain essential government datasets. By ensuring enforceable access to federal information, FOIA supports accountability and enables broader utilization of public sector data for research and civic engagement.
