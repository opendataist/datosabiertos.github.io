Hasta 12 años de prisión por compartir ‘pantallazos’

Hasta 12 años de prisión por compartir ‘pantallazos’

Sharing screenshots of private conversations without authorization constitutes a serious violation of privacy, carrying severe criminal and financial penalties. This new federal law strengthens the protection of personal data, establishing prison sentences and significant fines for those who disclose such information on social media or to third parties. The legislation also strictly defines the handling of consent, requiring new authorization for uses different from the original ones and eliminating previous flexibility. Furthermore, it limits the ability to object to data processing when it is necessary to comply with legal obligations, adjusting individuals’ rights under more rigorous criteria. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need to anonymize and protect privacy when publishing public datasets. Organizations must adapt their controls and policies to ensure that the disclosure of information does not violate protection standards, thereby minimizing legal and ethical risks in the management of personal data.
