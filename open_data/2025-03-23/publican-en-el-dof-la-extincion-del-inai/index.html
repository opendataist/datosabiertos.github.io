Publican en el DOF la extinción del INAI

Publican en el DOF la extinción del INAI

The repeal of the INAI and its transfer to the Secretariat of Anti-Corruption raises concerns about institutional independence, potentially weakening the safeguard against censorship and arbitrary restrictions. This structural shift threatens the autonomy essential for effective oversight, altering the balance between executive power and citizen rights. The law mandates that public digital data be accessible, machine-readable, and in open formats to facilitate reuse. These technical standards aim to enhance transparency and encourage civic engagement by removing barriers to information. By prioritizing usability, the framework seeks to make government data a practical resource for public scrutiny. This evolution is crucial for open data because it redefines the ecosystem’s governance. Ensuring that data principles like impartiality and maximum publicity are maintained under new administrative bodies is vital. Without robust independence, the quality and availability of open information may suffer, impacting democratic accountability and innovation.
