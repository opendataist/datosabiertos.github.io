Golpe a Musk: jueza bloquea acceso de DOGE a datos personales de la Seguridad Social

The judicial veto barring DOGE’s access to Social Security data highlights critical privacy risks by exposing citizens’ sensitive information without anonymization. This underscores the need to strictly balance government transparency and efficiency with the protection of personal data, preventing arbitrary access that infringes upon fundamental rights. The restriction emphasizes the necessity of rigorous, justified audits before deploying invasive technologies within federal infrastructure. By mandating the removal of non-anonymized software and data, the ruling reinforces that any data-opening initiative must operate within secure legal frameworks, avoiding unverified mass data collection. This case is significant for open data because it establishes a crucial precedent: the dissemination of public information must not compromise individual identity or security. It obligates entities to ensure that administrative transparency strictly respects confidentiality, guaranteeing that data accessibility does not pose a threat to the civil rights of the population.
