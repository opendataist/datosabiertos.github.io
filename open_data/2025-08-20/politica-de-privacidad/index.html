Política de privacidad

This article serves as a legal compliance document outlining how a specific website manages user data, emphasizing transparency regarding data collection, processing, and user rights. It details the legal bases for processing, including consent and legitimate interest, while specifying that cookies and usage data are essential for service provision. The text also highlights potential international data transfers and the security measures adopted to protect information from unauthorized access. The document delineates the specific rights granted to users, such as the ability to access, rectify, erase, or port their data, and to object to its use for commercial purposes. It further clarifies that while some data collection is mandatory for the website to function, optional data provides flexibility to users. The inclusion of third-party analytics and advertising services illustrates the common practice of sharing user behavior data with external entities for statistical and promotional goals. This content is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the critical balance between data utility and individual privacy rights. While open data initiatives aim to maximize the availability and reuse of information, this document underscores the necessity of clear governance frameworks. Understanding how personal data is legally protected and how user consent operates is essential for developing open data policies that respect privacy laws and maintain public trust in data-driven ecosystems.
