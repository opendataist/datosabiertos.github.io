Presenta Mara Lezama el nuevo Sistema Inmobiliario de Quintana Roo que moderniza el Registro Público con una plataforma moderna, digital, ágil y accesible - Noticaribe

Presenta Mara Lezama el nuevo Sistema Inmobiliario de Quintana Roo que moderniza el Registro Público con una plataforma moderna, digital, ágil y accesible - Noticaribe

Quintana Roo has implemented the New Real Estate System (SIQROO), marking the end of an outdated registration model in favor of a digital, agile, and accessible platform. This modernization centralizes millions of real estate records and migrates years of historical data into a single database, eliminating cumbersome procedures and significantly reducing waiting times. The initiative aims to provide greater peace of mind to buyers and security to investors, ensuring a more efficient process from any geographic location. The relevance of this system for open data lies in its open and interconnected architecture. The new ecosystem does not operate in isolation but integrates with other state and national registries, accelerating data validation and strengthening legal certainty. By incorporating advanced technological tools such as electronic signatures and QR codes, the authenticity of documents is ensured, creating a robust and verifiable information flow that lays the foundation for institutional interoperability, improving the quality and reliability of public data. Finally, this transformation promotes transparency and combats corruption by eliminating intermediaries and reducing operational costs. By providing online access for citizens, notaries, and authorities, it democratizes information and strengthens accountability. This advancement represents a firm step toward social justice and equal access to public services, demonstrating how the digitalization of critical records can improve governmental efficiency, protect property rights, and foster a climate of trust for the region's socioeconomic development.
