La amenaza DOGE, de Elon Musk, y el poder extraordinario que los datos gubernamentales podrían otorgar a una empresa de IA | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

La amenaza DOGE, de Elon Musk, y el poder extraordinario que los datos gubernamentales podrían otorgar a una empresa de IA | Noticias de México | El Imparcial

DOGE’s access to sensitive federal databases poses serious privacy and cybersecurity risks. Beyond the violation of individual privacy, there is a critical concern about the possible unauthorized transfer of this information to private companies linked to political figures, which could facilitate the training of artificial intelligence models using exclusive government resources. These records are invaluable for AI because they provide longitudinal and verifiable data on the entire population, overcoming the limitations of incomplete or biased information available on the internet. By capturing real-world decisions in areas such as health and the economy, they enable the training of systems with a level of accuracy and understanding of the consequences of public policies that no commercial source can match, offering a comprehensive view of human experience. The relevance to open data lies in the danger of concentrating governance power in the hands of a single private entity. If public data, which should ensure transparency and equity, is privatized to dominate key sectors such as health or finance, democracy and national security are eroded. This case illustrates the urgent need to safeguard public data assets against corporate hoarding and ensure that their benefits remain in the collective domain.
