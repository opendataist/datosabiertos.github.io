Figures show how many North Wales Police officers and staff were assaulted last year

Figures show how many North Wales Police officers and staff were assaulted last year

Freedom of Information requests to North Wales Police reveal fluctuating trends in assaults against officers, PCSOs, and support staff over recent years. While officer assaults show a recent decrease, incidents involving support staff are rising, indicating a shifting dynamic in workplace violence within the force. Data on medical leave further illustrates the impact of these incidents, with significant variations in days off granted to different personnel types. This highlights the varying severity of injuries sustained during assaults and underscores the tangible costs of violence against law enforcement personnel in terms of operational capacity and well-being. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency through FOI requests enables public scrutiny of policing metrics. By releasing detailed statistics on assaults and leave, authorities allow citizens to assess safety trends and hold agencies accountable, reinforcing the value of accessible government data in fostering trust and informed public discourse.
