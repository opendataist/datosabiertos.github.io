Propiedad intelectual y datos en la era digital - Negocios - ABC Color

Propiedad intelectual y datos en la era digital - Negocios - ABC Color

The convergence of artificial intelligence, data, and intellectual property constitutes the fundamental pillar of modern innovation, although it faces serious regulatory challenges. Digital piracy has become normalized, severely affecting content creators and highlighting the urgent need to update laws to protect copyright in the AI era. Without clear regulation, there is a risk that technological development will outpace the improper exploitation of protected resources, thereby discouraging investment in original research and development. The relevance of this article to the open data movement lies in the tension between the data mining required to train algorithms and the protection of intellectual property. While open access to information drives innovation, its use must respect licenses and the rights of rights holders. The text emphasizes that transparency and regulatory compliance are essential; the unauthorized use of protected works during training or content generation constitutes a legal infringement, implying that data cannot be considered free of restrictions simply because it is digital. Ultimately, responsibility rests with developers and users, not with AI, which lacks legal personality. Companies must implement effective technical measures and obtain appropriate licenses to avoid civil or criminal penalties. This scenario invites the open data community to promote robust ethical and legal frameworks that balance technological progress with justice for creators, ensuring that innovation is sustainable, profitable, and respectful of human creativity.
