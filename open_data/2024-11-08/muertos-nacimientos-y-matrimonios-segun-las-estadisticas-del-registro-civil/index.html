Muertos, nacimientos y matrimonios, según las estadísticas del Registro Civil

Muertos, nacimientos y matrimonios, según las estadísticas del Registro Civil

The Provincial Registry of Persons has launched an open data portal covering demographic statistics from 2020 to 2024. This initiative provides disaggregated information on vital events, including births, deaths, marriages, and gender identity changes, categorized by municipality, age, sex, and nationality. By making this granular data accessible, the portal facilitates deeper sociological analysis and transparency regarding public administration activities. The data reveals significant trends, such as the pandemic’s demographic impact and evolving family structures, including the rise of same-sex unions. It also highlights nuances in local statistics, such as how hospital location influences recorded birth rates. These insights allow researchers to understand population dynamics beyond simple aggregates, offering a richer context for social planning. This release is crucial for open data because it exemplifies the transition of government records from static archives to dynamic, reusable datasets. It empowers civil society, researchers, and journalists to verify administrative performance and analyze social trends independently. Ultimately, it demonstrates how transparent, machine-readable data enhances democratic accountability and fosters evidence-based decision-making in public policy.
