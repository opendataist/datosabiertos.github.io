Impactó tarifazo de luz a agropecuarios y energéticos: Inegi

Impactó tarifazo de luz a agropecuarios y energéticos: Inegi

The National Institute of Statistics published Mexico’s October inflation data, highlighting a general annual rate of 4.76%. This update reflects the immediate impact of ending subsidized summer electricity tariffs in major cities, which significantly drove up energy costs alongside agricultural prices like tomatoes. Conversely, domestic gas and certain fruits such as lemons and bananas experienced price reductions, partially offsetting the overall increase. These fluctuations demonstrate how specific government policy changes and seasonal agricultural yields directly influence consumer price indices across diverse geographic regions. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it provides granular, transparent metrics on how public subsidies and market forces shape economic indicators. Such accessible datasets empower researchers and citizens to analyze the tangible effects of fiscal policies on everyday living costs and regional economic disparities.
