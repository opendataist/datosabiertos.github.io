Bajan a 78 los desaparecidos registrados oficialmente por la DANA en Valencia

The updated data clarifies the human toll of the DANA (Depresión Aislada en Niveles Altos, or isolated high-level depression) in Valencia, emphasizing that while the death toll has risen, the number of active missing persons cases has decreased. This shift indicates progress in resolving urgent humanitarian crises through systematic verification and identification efforts. Forensic procedures, including autopsies and DNA analysis, have successfully identified the majority of victims. The structured handover of remains to families highlights the critical role of organized data management in providing closure and facilitating proper funeral rites during catastrophic events. This situation underscores the importance of open data in public safety and disaster response. Transparent, real-time information sharing enables accurate resource allocation, supports legal accountability, and aids communities in coping with trauma. Reliable data transparency is essential for maintaining trust and coordinating effective emergency interventions.
