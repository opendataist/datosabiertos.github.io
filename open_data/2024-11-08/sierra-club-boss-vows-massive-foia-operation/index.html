Sierra Club boss vows ‘massive FOIA operation’

The Sierra Club will aggressively utilize Freedom of Information Act requests to scrutinize the incoming Trump administration. This strategy aims to ensure accountability and transparency in federal decision-making. Such public oversight is vital for open data advocacy, as it reveals how government actions impact environmental policy. This approach highlights the critical role citizens play in demanding access to information and monitoring executive branch activities.
