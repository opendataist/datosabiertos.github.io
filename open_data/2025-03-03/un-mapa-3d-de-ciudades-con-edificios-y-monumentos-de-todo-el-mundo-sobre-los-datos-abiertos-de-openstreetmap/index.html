Un Mapa 3D de ciudades con edificios y monumentos de todo el mundo sobre los datos abiertos de OpenStreetMap

F4Map demonstrates the power of OpenStreetMap data by rendering a fluid, interactive globe that transitions from 2D to detailed 3D models. This technology leverages crowdsourced and official layers to visualize cities with remarkable precision, including iconic buildings, trees, and animated water features. The project highlights how open data availability varies by region, with major cities offering richer detail. By transforming raw geographic information into immersive virtual worlds, it showcases the versatility of open datasets for applications ranging from civil engineering to video games. This is highly relevant to open data as it proves that collaborative, openly licensed geographic information can drive sophisticated, high-performance visualization tools. It encourages developers to explore OpenStreetMap’s potential for creating engaging digital experiences and underscores the value of accessible, community-maintained spatial data for innovation.
