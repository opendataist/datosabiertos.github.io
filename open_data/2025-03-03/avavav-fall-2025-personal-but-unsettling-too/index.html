Avavav Fall 2025: Personal, But Unsettling Too

Avavav Fall 2025: Personal, But Unsettling Too

Avavav’s latest runway presentation utilized visceral, unsettling imagery to express the designer’s personal struggle with health anxieties and loss of bodily control. By transforming the runway into a grassy field with models emerging from grave-like holes, the creative director channeled creeping unease into tangible, expressive art rather than ignoring the discomfort. This approach highlights fashion’s capacity to serve as therapeutic self-expression during times of vulnerability, turning abstract fears into concrete visual narratives. The collection balanced this raw emotional undertone by juxtaposing Goth streetwear with delicate, feminine elements like rib cage cutouts and bow embellishments. This stylistic clash between protective sportswear gear and fragile fabrics visually represented the tension between strength and weakness. The result was a poignant exploration of physical fragility, demonstrating how clothing can articulate complex internal states that words alone cannot convey. This article is relevant to open_data because it illustrates how fashion data can capture intangible human emotions and cultural moments. When such unique, unstructured visual data is digitized and shared openly, researchers can analyze emerging social sentiments and mental health trends. Open access to these creative expressions allows for broader understanding of how art reflects collective anxiety, enriching datasets that go beyond commercial metrics to include cultural and psychological insights.
