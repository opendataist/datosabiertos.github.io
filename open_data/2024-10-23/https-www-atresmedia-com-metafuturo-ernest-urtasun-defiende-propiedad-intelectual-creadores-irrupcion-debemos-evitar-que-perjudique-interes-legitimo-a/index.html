The Spanish Ministry of Culture aims to protect creators by regulating how artificial intelligence systems use copyrighted works for training. This initiative seeks to balance the opportunities and challenges of generative AI, ensuring that artists' legitimate interests are not harmed while fostering a secure legal framework. By mandating that authors retain final decision-making power over whether their work is used to train AI models, the government emphasizes the necessity of remuneration and respect for intellectual property rights. This approach prioritizes the integration of technology without compromising the ethical and economic rights of creative professionals. This policy is crucial for open data ecosystems, as it establishes precedents for data provenance and usage consent in AI development. It highlights the tension between data accessibility for model training and the legal protections required for copyrighted materials, influencing how public and private sectors handle cultural data resources.
