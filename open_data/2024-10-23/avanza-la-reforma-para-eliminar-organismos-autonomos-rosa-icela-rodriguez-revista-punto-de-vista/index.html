Avanza la reforma para eliminar organismos autónomos: Rosa Icela Rodríguez | Revista Punto de Vista

Avanza la reforma para eliminar organismos autónomos: Rosa Icela Rodríguez | Revista Punto de Vista

The Mexican government has confirmed its legislative initiative to eliminate autonomous bodies such as the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), dismissing objections raised by current commissioners. This reform seeks to integrate technical staff into the government apparatus while preserving their labor rights, signaling a significant restructuring of the country’s institutional oversight mechanisms. This development is highly relevant to open data, as INAI serves as the primary guardian of transparency and access to information. Its potential dissolution poses a serious threat to data integrity and citizens’ rights, suggesting a centralization of control that could undermine the independent verification and openness essential for a robust public data ecosystem.
