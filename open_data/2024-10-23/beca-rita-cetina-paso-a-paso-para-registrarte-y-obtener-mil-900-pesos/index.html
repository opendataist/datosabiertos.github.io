Beca Rita Cetina: Paso a paso para registrarte y obtener mil 900 pesos

The Universal Scholarship program in Mexico aims to reduce economic barriers and prevent school dropouts in public basic education by providing financial assistance to families. By subsidizing expenses such as school supplies and transportation, the initiative seeks to ensure that a lack of resources does not disrupt a student’s academic progress, thereby reinforcing the government’s commitment to universal access to education. The application process is entirely digital and time-sensitive, requiring guardians to register online by providing personal identification and proof of residence. This strict adherence to a centralized, online administrative system underscores the need for reliable digital infrastructure and accessible government platforms, as missing the brief registration window results in the loss of benefits for the following period. This case is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the digitization of social welfare distribution, which generates substantial volumes of structured beneficiary information. Transparent access to the criteria, processes, and outcomes of such programs enables better public oversight, data-driven policy evaluation, and ensures that resources are allocated efficiently to those who most need educational support.
