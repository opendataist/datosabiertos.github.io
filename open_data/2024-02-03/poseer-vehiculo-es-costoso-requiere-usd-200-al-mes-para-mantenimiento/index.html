Poseer vehículo es costoso, requiere USD 200 al mes para mantenimiento

The article highlights that vehicle ownership in Loja has shifted from a luxury to a necessity, driven by greater access to credit and economic demands. However, maintaining a car requires significant financial stability, with monthly costs often exceeding basic income thresholds. This financial burden forces many families into debt or demands substantial additional resources for routine upkeep, revealing the precarious economic reality faced by average car owners. This context is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for accessible, transparent statistical sources, such as those provided by the INEC. Open data initiatives enable citizens to verify national trends, understand the true cost of living, and critically assess government or corporate claims regarding economic indicators. Furthermore, publicly available data allows communities to track vehicle registration patterns and associated expenses over time. By democratizing access to such information, stakeholders can better analyze the impact of credit policies and maintenance costs on household budgets, fostering more informed public discourse and policy decisions regarding urban mobility and economic planning.
