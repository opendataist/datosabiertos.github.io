España recibió un récord de 85,1 millones de turistas en 2023

Spain’s tourism sector achieved record-breaking arrivals and spending, reinforcing its global leadership despite intense international competition. These strong results highlight the industry’s economic vitality, serving as a key benchmark for evaluating regional performance and visitor origins. This data underscores the need for ongoing quality improvements to sustain growth and manage overcrowding. The figures provide essential insights into market dynamics, helping policymakers align infrastructure and services with evolving tourist demands. Such high-volume metrics are vital for open data initiatives, enabling transparent tracking of economic impacts and resource allocation. Access to this detailed information allows stakeholders to accurately analyze trends and drive informed, sustainable policy decisions for the future.
