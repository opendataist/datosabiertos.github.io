2024 comienza con 3.323 parados más en C-LM, llegando a los 138.129 en total

2024 comienza con 3.323 parados más en C-LM, llegando a los 138.129 en total

Castilla-La Mancha recorded its lowest January unemployment rate since 2008, despite a slight month-on-month increase. This trend signals long-term improvement in the labor market, as annual unemployment has decreased significantly over the past year. Conversely, social security registrations fell slightly on a monthly basis across all provinces. However, from an annual perspective, there has been substantial growth in employment, indicating a robust expansion of the formal job market in the region. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the value of transparent, granular public statistics. Accessible official datasets enable researchers and citizens to verify economic health, monitor regional disparities, and assess the effectiveness of employment policies over time.
