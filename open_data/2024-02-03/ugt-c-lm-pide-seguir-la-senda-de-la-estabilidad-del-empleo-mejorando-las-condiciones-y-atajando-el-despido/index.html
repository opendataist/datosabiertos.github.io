UGT C-LM pide seguir la senda de la estabilidad del empleo mejorando las condiciones y "atajando" el despido

The article highlights that, although Spain has successfully shifted toward permanent contracts following the 2021 labor reform, significant challenges remain in improving job quality. The union emphasizes that reducing precariousness and addressing involuntary part-time work are crucial for enhancing workers' real stability and income levels. Relevant to open data, this discourse underscores the necessity of transparent, timely employment statistics to accurately track these structural shifts. Reliable public data enables policymakers and unions to verify historical lows in temporary employment and monitor the effectiveness of ongoing reforms, ensuring that official figures reflect the true state of labor market security rather than just nominal hiring trends. Furthermore, the focus on reducing the workweek and combating underemployment requires granular data insights to design effective policies. Open data facilitates the rigorous analysis needed to balance economic stability with social welfare, ensuring that the transition to more stable employment models is both measurable and sustainable across different sectors.
