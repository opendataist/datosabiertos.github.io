Baleares es la única comunidad que ha reducido el paro de diciembre a enero

The Balearic Islands have shown consistent employment growth, with unemployment declining in most Januaries since 1996. This trend underscores the reliability of time-series data for monitoring regional labor market stability and structural shifts across sectors, particularly in services and construction. The increase in permanent contracts relative to temporary ones signals a potential improvement in job quality. Such changes in contract types are crucial open-data indicators, enabling analysts to assess economic health beyond simple headcount figures and to better understand long-term labor market resilience. This dataset is essential for open-data initiatives, as it facilitates transparent monitoring of socioeconomic policies. By making detailed employment data publicly accessible, stakeholders can verify government claims and ensure accountability in labor reform efforts, thereby fostering a more informed public debate on economic performance.
