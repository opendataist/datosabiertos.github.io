IPPR’s mass-surveillance claims unwarranted, ministry says

The government strongly rejects allegations that mandatory SIM card registration facilitates mass surveillance, asserting instead that the initiative is a vital tool for combating telecommunications crimes. Officials emphasize that data access is strictly regulated, requiring judicial warrants and limited to situations involving reported offenses under existing security laws. This policy is relevant to open_data discourse as it highlights the tension between national security mandates and individual privacy rights. By clarifying that bulk data retention is not equivalent to unrestricted surveillance, the article underscores the importance of transparent legal frameworks in managing sensitive public information. Ultimately, the debate centers on balancing crime prevention with civil liberties. Understanding the strict legal constraints on data access helps contextualize how governments handle telecommunications records, offering insights into the safeguards necessary to protect citizen privacy within open data ecosystems.
