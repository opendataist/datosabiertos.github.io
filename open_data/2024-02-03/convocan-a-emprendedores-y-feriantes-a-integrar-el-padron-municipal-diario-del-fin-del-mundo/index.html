Convocan a emprendedores y feriantes a integrar el Padrón municipal | Diario del Fin del Mundo

Ushuaia updates its local vendor registry to enhance support for participants in the social economy. This initiative streamlines access to farmers’ markets and training programs, ensuring stronger institutional backing for families who depend on this source of income. The process underscores the importance of open data by demonstrating how transparent and accessible public registries enable effective governance. By centralizing information, the municipality promotes accountability and ensures that resources reach the intended beneficiaries efficiently. The creation of a dedicated physical office further signals a commitment to direct citizen engagement. This structural improvement facilitates clearer communication channels, strengthening the connection between public policy transparency and tangible community support.
