Ceresos de Chihuahua, los más revoltosos en 2023: Inegi

Prisons in Chihuahua experienced significantly more incidents than those in most other states, revealing systemic security failures despite the presence of standard infrastructure. This underscores the critical need for transparent, open data to expose the overcrowding and management inefficiencies that drive violence. Open data initiatives must prioritize this information to hold authorities accountable. By making detailed incident reports publicly accessible, society can demand evidence-based reforms instead of relying on opaque institutional metrics. Ultimately, unrestricted access to penal statistics empowers citizens and researchers to analyze the root causes of these problems. This transparency is essential for fostering public trust and guiding effective policy changes to improve prison conditions nationwide.
