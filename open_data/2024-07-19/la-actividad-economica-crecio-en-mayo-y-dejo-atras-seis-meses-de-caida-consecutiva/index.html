La actividad económica creció en mayo y dejó atrás seis meses de caída consecutiva

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses reports a rebound in economic activity, driven primarily by a surge in agriculture. This positive shift marks an end to six consecutive months of decline, allowing the government to claim a turnaround despite significant contractions in construction and manufacturing. This narrative is relevant to open data as it highlights the importance of disaggregated sectoral statistics. Transparent, granular data allows analysts to identify specific drivers of macroeconomic trends rather than relying solely on aggregate figures. Such visibility supports informed public debate and policy evaluation. Understanding these underlying dynamics requires access to detailed, high-quality datasets. When institutions publish comprehensive breakdowns, researchers and citizens can better assess the distribution of economic gains. This promotes accountability and ensures that discussions about national performance are grounded in verifiable, structured information.
