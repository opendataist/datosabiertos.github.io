C-LM se mantiene en valores positivos con un 1,8% de crecimiento en facturación industrial frente a la caída nacional

The Spanish industry shows fragmented growth, with regions such as Andalusia standing out, while Madrid and Cantabria face significant declines. Although the annual cumulative figure indicates a slight national improvement, the monthly drop in May suggests a slowdown following April’s rebound, reflecting instability within the sector. This analysis is relevant for open data because it highlights the need to disaggregate macroeconomic statistics at the regional level. Only by accessing detailed data by region and sector can civil society and researchers accurately identify these geographic disparities and assess the true health of the national industrial base.
