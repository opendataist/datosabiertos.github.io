Puebla ocupa el tercer lugar en ciberacoso en el país

The article highlights a significant rise in cyberbullying incidents in Puebla, underscoring the urgent need for improved digital safety measures. Data reveal that the state ranks third nationally in such cases, with a notable increase in prevalence among individuals aged twelve and older. This trend points to growing vulnerability in digital spaces, particularly affecting women disproportionately compared to men. These statistics emphasize the pervasive nature of online harassment across various platforms. The high percentage of victims who did not know their aggressors highlights anonymous threats as a major concern. Moreover, the common defensive actions taken—such as blocking accounts—suggest that users often resort to isolation rather than systemic solutions, reflecting a lack of robust protective frameworks. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent statistical reports can expose critical social issues. By making such granular data accessible, authorities and citizens can collaborate more effectively to design targeted interventions. Ultimately, open data serves as a vital tool for raising awareness and driving policy changes that protect users in an increasingly digital world.
