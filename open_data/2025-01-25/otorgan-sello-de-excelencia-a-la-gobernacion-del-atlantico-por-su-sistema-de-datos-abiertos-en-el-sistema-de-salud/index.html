Otorgan sello de excelencia a la Gobernación del Atlántico por su sistema de datos abiertos en el sistema de salud

Otorgan sello de excelencia a la Gobernación del Atlántico por su sistema de datos abiertos en el sistema de salud

The Departmental Health Secretariat received the Digital Government Excellence Seal, validating its commitment to digital inclusion and administrative transparency. This recognition highlights how the systematic integration of new technologies into governance processes directly benefits citizens by ensuring that public information is accessible and verifiable, thereby fostering greater trust in public institutions. Central to this achievement is adherence to Open Data policies within the quality management system. By publishing detailed datasets on the services provided by state-owned health enterprises, the government demonstrates a practical application of open standards. This approach transforms raw administrative data into a useful resource for the public, enabling users to track service availability and performance across different locations and care groups. This case is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the tangible impact of complying with national open data laws. It shows that when public entities release interoperable, unrestricted data, they enable citizen oversight and innovation. The certification serves as a model for how structured transparency, facilitated by digital tools, can effectively bridge the gap between government administration and societal needs, proving that open data is not merely a technical requirement but a core element of democratic accountability.
