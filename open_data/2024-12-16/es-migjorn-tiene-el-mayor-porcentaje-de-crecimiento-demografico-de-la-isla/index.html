Es Migjorn tiene el mayor porcentaje de crecimiento demográfico de la Isla

The island’s population has exceeded one hundred thousand inhabitants, driven by steady growth across all municipalities since 2016. Ciutadella remains the most populous city, while Maó and Alaior have historically reached demographic thresholds for the first time, signaling robust urban expansion. Smaller municipalities also show significant relative increases, particularly Es Migjorn, which experienced the highest percentage growth. Even areas with smaller absolute gains contribute to an overall upward trend, indicating widespread demographic vitality rather than isolated urban booms. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it validates the utility of transparent, granular municipal statistics. By making such detailed demographic flows accessible, stakeholders can better analyze regional development patterns, supporting evidence-based planning and fostering accountability in public resource allocation.
