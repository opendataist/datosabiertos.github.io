Architects Registration Council fined GH¢50k for failure to comply with RTI request - MyJoyOnline

Architects Registration Council fined GH¢50k for failure to comply with RTI request - MyJoyOnline

The RTI Commission fined the Architects Registration Council for refusing to disclose information regarding alleged mandate extensions. This enforcement action underscores the critical necessity for public agencies to comply with transparency laws, ensuring accountability in governance structures. By penalizing non-compliance, the Commission reinforces that access to information is a fundamental constitutional right, not optional. This precedent strengthens the legal framework for open data, demonstrating that institutions cannot arbitrarily withhold public records without facing significant administrative consequences. Ultimately, this case highlights the importance of enforcing open data principles to prevent institutional opacity. It serves as a vital reminder that government bodies must actively facilitate public access to records, thereby promoting trust and ensuring that citizens can effectively hold authorities accountable for their actions and decisions.
