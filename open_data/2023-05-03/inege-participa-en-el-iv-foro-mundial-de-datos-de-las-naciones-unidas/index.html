INEGE participa en el IV Foro Mundial de Datos de las Naciones Unidas

The UN World Data Forum emphasizes that data are essential for achieving the Sustainable Development Goals, requiring inclusive and ethical governance. The key takeaway is the need to align statistical production with development plans, simplifying information to ensure its accessibility and usefulness in decision-making. The event highlights the importance of maximizing the value of data through innovation and collaboration across multiple sectors, including governments, civil society, and the private sector. It stresses that trust and privacy protection must be balanced with data use, promoting robust ecosystems that integrate diverse methodological sources to make all social actors visible. This initiative is relevant to open data because it validates the principle that open and accessible data are fundamental tools for transparency and equity. By advocating for the simplification and clear dissemination of statistical information, it reinforces the impact of open data as a public good that empowers citizens and improves quality of life, transcending technical barriers to serve the common interest.
