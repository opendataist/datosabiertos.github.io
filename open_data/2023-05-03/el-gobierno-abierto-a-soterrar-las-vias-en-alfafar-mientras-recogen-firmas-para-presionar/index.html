El Gobierno abierto a soterrar las vías en Alfafar mientras recogen firmas para presionar

El Gobierno abierto a soterrar las vías en Alfafar mientras recogen firmas para presionar

A recent fatal accident involving a teenager has intensified demands for infrastructure changes in Alfafar and Sedaví, highlighting the critical need for public safety data to support municipal advocacy. The tragedy has transformed local safety concerns into a national political issue, with elected officials and residents uniting to demand the elimination of dangerous level crossings and the eventual burial of railway lines. The Ministry of Transport has signaled openness to these requests, prioritizing the immediate removal of level crossings while keeping long-term burial options on the table. This political alignment suggests a potential resolution to decades of inaction, emphasizing that coordinated policy responses driven by community pressure can lead to tangible infrastructure improvements and enhanced regulatory compliance. This situation is relevant to open data because robust, accessible statistics on railway accidents and safety violations are essential for validating local grievances and shaping effective public policy. Transparent data allows citizens and officials to move beyond anecdotal evidence, ensuring that safety measures are based on factual analysis and that governmental accountability is maintained through measurable outcomes in urban mobility planning.
