Open Data
ES EN

This course educates small businesses and freelancers on navigating the legal obligations of data protection, emphasizing their critical role in ensuring compliance with current regulations. By understanding these rights and duties, organizations can effectively manage personal data processing activities, significantly reducing the risk of legal infractions and enhancing operational transparency. The curriculum focuses on practical application, guiding participants through the creation of essential legal documents such as privacy notices and data processing agreements, while also detailing how to properly register data processing activities. It further addresses the procedural requirements for responding to individuals’ requests regarding their personal data rights, ensuring that companies remain responsive and compliant in their day-to-day operations. This content is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it establishes the necessary ethical and legal frameworks for responsible data stewardship. By training entities on privacy and security standards, it promotes a culture where data usage is both lawful and respectful of individual rights, creating a foundation for trustworthy information ecosystems in which open data practices can thrive without compromising user privacy.
