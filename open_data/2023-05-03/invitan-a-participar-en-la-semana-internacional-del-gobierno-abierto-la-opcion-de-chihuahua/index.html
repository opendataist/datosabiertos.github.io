Invitan a participar en la Semana Internacional del Gobierno Abierto | La Opción de Chihuahua

Invitan a participar en la Semana Internacional del Gobierno Abierto | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua is hosting the International Week of Open Government, aligning with the global Open Government Partnership to promote a culture of transparency. By bringing together public servants and civil society leaders from universities, the event fosters essential dialogue on participatory governance, open contracting, and anti-corruption measures. This initiative emphasizes that open government rests on three core pillars: transparency, citizen participation, and multi-sector collaboration involving the state, academia, and the private sector. Municipal leadership highlights an honest government focused on security, public services, and human development. Mayor Marco Antonio Bonilla leads discussions on implementing these open government principles locally, engaging youth to ensure inclusive and effective administrative practices. The event serves as a practical platform to demonstrate how local authorities can integrate these values into daily operations, moving beyond rhetoric to achieve actionable results in public administration and service delivery. This article is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of transparent institutions for meaningful public engagement. Open data serves as the technical foundation for "open contracting" and "inclusive transparency," enabling the accountability and collaboration highlighted at the event. Without accessible, standardized data, the pillars of open government cannot function effectively, making this municipal effort a critical step toward operationalizing the digital infrastructure required for true governmental transparency and civic trust.
