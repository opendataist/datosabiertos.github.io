Acumula INAI 800 casos sin resolver

The INAI’s inability to convene has stalled hundreds of transparency requests, denying citizens access to essential information such as medical records and scholarships. This unprecedented paralysis highlights a critical vulnerability in the institutional framework that safeguards public accountability. Such operational failures threaten the fundamental right of access to information, illustrating how bureaucratic gaps directly impact individual rights and social equity. The situation serves as a stark reminder that institutional integrity is essential for protecting civic liberties. This case underscores the urgency of maintaining functional open data mechanisms. Without effective oversight bodies, the promise of transparency remains unfulfilled, emphasizing the need for robust, accessible systems to ensure public trust and democratic participation.
