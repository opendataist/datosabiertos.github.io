España creció un 2,5% en 2023 tras repuntar el PIB al 0,6% en el cuarto trimestre

Spain’s economy demonstrated solid and resilient growth in 2023, surpassing government expectations and leading performance among major European economies. This outcome, driven primarily by domestic demand and increased public spending, reinforces the narrative of macroeconomic stability and dispels catastrophic forecasts. Achieving three consecutive years of expansion underscores the Spanish model’s capacity to maintain an upward trajectory despite global slowdowns and external challenges. Beyond aggregate GDP, the structure of growth reveals specific dynamics in employment and productivity. While wages and employment grew notably, productivity per hour worked declined. This divergence suggests that recent economic expansion has relied intensively on labor inputs rather than improvements in productive efficiency, raising questions about the future sustainability of growth if this trend in efficiency is not reversed. This publication is relevant to the field of open data because the detailed reports from the National Accounts of the National Statistics Institute (INE) constitute a fundamental pillar of public statistical transparency. By systematically breaking down variables such as supply, demand, incomes, and productivity, they provide a verifiable empirical basis that enables citizens, researchers, and businesses to contrast political narratives with quantitative reality. The accessibility of these structured data is essential for auditing economic management and fostering an informed debate grounded in verifiable facts.
