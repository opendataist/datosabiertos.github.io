Gobierno confirma que el INE realizará una encuesta censal

The INE plans a post-census survey to verify the quality of the recent count, confirming that this initiative is part of its scheduled activities. The aim is to validate data accuracy, which is crucial for reliable open-data ecosystems. Critics, however, argue that an international body should carry out the assessment to ensure impartiality and public trust.
