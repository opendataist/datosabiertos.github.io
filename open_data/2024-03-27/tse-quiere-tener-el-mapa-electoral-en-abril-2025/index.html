TSE quiere tener el mapa electoral en abril 2025

The Supreme Electoral Tribunal relies on national statistical data to define the electoral geography for the 2025 general elections. This process depends on receiving comprehensive population statistics by September and detailed georeferenced information by December, in order to ensure legal compliance. Accurate demographic inputs are essential for the equitable redistribution of parliamentary seats and the precise delimitation of electoral constituencies. These steps establish the framework for presidential, senatorial, and deputy elections, reflecting population changes to maintain representative fairness. This underscores the critical role of open data in democratic infrastructure. Transparent, accessible, and precise population statistics enable authorities to make objective decisions that uphold constitutional mandates. Reliable public data fosters trust in electoral processes by ensuring that boundaries and representation accurately reflect the current distribution of the citizenry.
