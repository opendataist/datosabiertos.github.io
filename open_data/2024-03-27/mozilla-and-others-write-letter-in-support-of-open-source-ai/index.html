Mozilla and others write letter in support of open source AI

Prominent civil society groups and academics urge the U.S. government to protect the openness and transparency of artificial intelligence models. They argue that open-source AI drives significant societal benefits, including innovation, independent safety research, and better regulatory compliance. Restrictive policies based on marginal risks are discouraged in favor of tailored solutions that address well-defined dangers without stifling economic growth or scientific dialogue. This stance is particularly relevant to the open data community, as it advocates for transparency as a cornerstone of trust and accountability in technology. By emphasizing that risks must be weighed against the proven value of open ecosystems, the letter supports the principle that data and models should be accessible for public scrutiny. This aligns with open data ideals, where sharing information enables independent verification and fosters a healthier, more competitive digital landscape rather than relying on secretive, closed systems. Ultimately, the organizations call for robust interagency coordination to ensure AI policies balance security with civil rights and scientific progress. They seek to establish better standards for testing and releasing models through collaboration with the open-source community. This approach ensures that future regulations support, rather than hinder, the development of ethical and safe AI technologies that benefit the broader public.
