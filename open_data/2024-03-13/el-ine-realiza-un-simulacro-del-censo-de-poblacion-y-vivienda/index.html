El INE realiza un simulacro del Censo de Población y Vivienda

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) conducted a census simulation to demonstrate logistical procedures to Bolivian society. This transparency underscores the operational readiness and training of census agents prior to the official count. The exercise verified the distribution of materials and coordination of personnel, aiming to ensure data accuracy despite concerns about mobility and preparation. The relevance of this open data lies in its public demonstration of administrative processes. It builds trust in how demographic information is collected, managed, and validated by government institutions for future public access.
