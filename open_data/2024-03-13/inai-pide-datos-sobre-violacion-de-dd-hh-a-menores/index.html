INAI pide datos sobre violación de DD. HH. a menores

The INAI ordered the Navy to disclose data on children victimized by its personnel, rejecting the agency’s claim that no such records exist. This ruling underscores that public records contain sufficient details to identify these human rights violations, thereby mandating a thorough and exhaustive search. Transparency in military conduct is crucial for protecting vulnerable populations. By enforcing disclosure, the INAI ensures that historical abuses are not concealed, holding institutions accountable for their actions toward minors during this period. This case highlights the role of open data in safeguarding human rights. It demonstrates how accessible, granular information empowers oversight mechanisms. Citizens and researchers rely on such transparency to monitor institutional behavior and advocate for justice, proving that open data is essential for democratic accountability and social justice.
