No le abras la puerta a cualquiera: cómo reconocer a los encuestadores en terreno del Censo 2024

The article warns that census takers in Chile are not permitted to enter private residences and must remain outside the home. This distinction is crucial for preventing scams, as criminals often impersonate officials to steal personal data or goods. Residents are advised to interact only from the threshold, ensuring their safety while fulfilling civic duties. To verify authenticity, census workers must wear official INE uniforms and present credentials with QR codes and contact numbers. The public is encouraged to use official government websites and hotlines to validate identities, emphasizing transparency and secure verification processes over blind trust. This guidance is vital for open data ecosystems, as it protects the integrity of official statistics by distinguishing legitimate data collection from fraud. By understanding that census questions are purely statistical and do not request sensitive personal details like bank accounts, citizens can safely share information, ensuring accurate public records without compromising privacy or security.
