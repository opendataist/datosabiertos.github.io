Roca tiene el pasaje de colectivo más caro del país, según un ranking nacional: qué pasa con Neuquén

The article highlights significant discrepancies in public transport fare rankings, revealing that data from Aaeta inaccurately reported Roca’s bus ticket price as nearly double the actual cost. While the association claimed Roca had some of the highest fares in the country, cooperative leaders clarified that the genuine surge resulted from the withdrawal of national subsidies and soaring fuel costs, rather than arbitrary pricing. This case underscores the critical necessity for rigorous data validation in public discourse. Inaccurate statistics can distort public perception of local economic conditions and obscure the real structural challenges facing essential services. Verifying primary sources is vital to prevent misinformation from clouding the understanding of how policy changes directly impact service sustainability and affordability. Relevance to open data lies in the demonstrated danger of unverified datasets influencing public opinion. When organizations disseminate financial figures without transparency or correction mechanisms, they risk misleading stakeholders about the true state of public utilities. Ensuring data accuracy and context promotes informed debate and helps policymakers and citizens alike grasp the actual implications of subsidy removals on regional transport sectors.
