Venezuela es el segundo país de América Latina en bancarización, según Latinometrics

Venezuela ranks second in Latin America for bank account penetration, a legacy of dollarization and cash scarcity. However, experts caution that widespread inactivity and informal economic practices diminish the practical benefits of this financial inclusion.
