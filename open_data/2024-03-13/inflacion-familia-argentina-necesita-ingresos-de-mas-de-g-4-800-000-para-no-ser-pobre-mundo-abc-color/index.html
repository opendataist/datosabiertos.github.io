Inflación: familia argentina necesita ingresos de más de G. 4.800.000 para no ser pobre - Mundo - ABC Color

Inflación: familia argentina necesita ingresos de más de G. 4.800.000 para no ser pobre - Mundo - ABC Color

The article highlights Argentina’s severe economic crisis, in which rising inflation and prices drastically increase the cost of living. As a result, families need multiple minimum wages just to avoid falling into poverty, illustrating a profound gap between earnings and basic survival needs. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of transparent, accessible statistical information. When institutions such as INDEC publish reliable, detailed economic indicators, they empower citizens, researchers, and policymakers to understand real-world impacts and make informed decisions regarding welfare and policy adjustments. Ultimately, such data serves as a vital tool for accountability. It exposes the severity of inflation and its disproportionate impact on household budgets, ensuring that societal challenges are visible and measurable rather than hidden, thereby fostering greater public trust and enabling effective civic engagement.
