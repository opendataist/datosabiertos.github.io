Bolivia aguarda autos de buen gobierno sobre censo poblacional - Noticias Prensa Latina

The National Statistics Institute (INE) has ordered the suspension of public and private activities, school classes, and the sale of alcohol to facilitate Bolivia’s Population and Housing Census. This comprehensive data collection operation, designated as a national priority, relies on volunteers who convert educational centers into census offices to accurately record sociodemographic information for all households. This initiative serves as the country’s primary source of critical social and demographic insights, enabling evidence-based public policy and resource allocation. The success of this statistical effort depends entirely on widespread citizen participation and the involvement of trained volunteers who ensure that every household is enumerated. The article is relevant to open data because the resulting census forms the foundational dataset for the nation. High-quality, accessible public statistics are essential for transparency and informed decision-making. By prioritizing this large-scale data collection effort, the government underscores the vital role of reliable, granular open data in shaping future governance and societal development.
