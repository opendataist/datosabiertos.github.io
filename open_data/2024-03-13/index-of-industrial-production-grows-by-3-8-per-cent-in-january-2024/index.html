Index of industrial production grows by 3.8 per cent in January 2024

The Indian Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation reports a positive industrial growth trend in early 2024, indicating robust activity across mining, manufacturing, and electricity sectors. This data offers crucial insights into economic recovery and sectoral performance, highlighting resilience despite previous pandemic-related disruptions. For open data enthusiasts, this release exemplifies the importance of timely, structured statistical data in monitoring national economic health. The availability of revised indices and detailed sectoral breakdowns allows researchers and analysts to track long-term industrial trends and validate economic policies with precision. Ultimately, such transparent data dissemination empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions. By providing accessible, high-quality industrial indicators, the government supports a data-driven approach to understanding and shaping the industrial landscape, fostering accountability and strategic planning in the broader economic ecosystem.
