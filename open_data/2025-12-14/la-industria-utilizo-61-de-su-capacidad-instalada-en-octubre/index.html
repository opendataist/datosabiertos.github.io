La industria utilizó 61 % de su capacidad instalada en octubre

La industria utilizó 61 % de su capacidad instalada en octubre

The recent report by Argentina’s statistical institute reveals a notable contraction in industrial capacity utilization for October, falling to 61% from 63% in the previous year. This decline highlights a broader slowdown in manufacturing activity, driven by significant drops in key sectors such as textiles, automotive, and rubber products. While energy and basic industries maintained higher operational levels, the overall trend indicates reduced economic dynamism across several industrial segments, reflecting weaker domestic or export demand for finished goods. These sectoral disparities are particularly stark in textiles, where utilization plummeted due to sharp reductions in cotton yarn and fabric production. Similarly, the automotive and rubber industries faced substantial setbacks, with tire and plastic manufacturing experiencing double-digit percentage drops. Conversely, petroleum refining and food processing remained relatively robust, suggesting that while resource-based and essential consumption sectors are stable, durable goods and discretionary manufacturing are struggling with inventory adjustments and lower sales volumes. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the critical need for transparent, granular industrial statistics. By making such detailed sectoral breakdowns publicly accessible, governments can empower researchers, policymakers, and businesses to accurately diagnose economic health. Open access to these metrics fosters accountability and enables data-driven decision-making, allowing stakeholders to identify emerging trends and structural weaknesses in real time rather than relying on aggregated, opaque figures.
