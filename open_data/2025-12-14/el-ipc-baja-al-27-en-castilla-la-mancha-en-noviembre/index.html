El IPC baja al 2,7% en Castilla-La Mancha en noviembre

El IPC baja al 2,7% en Castilla-La Mancha en noviembre

Castilla-La Mancha recorded its lowest inflation rate since August 2025, with monthly prices rising only slightly while the annual increase remains moderate. Housing and energy costs drove most price growth, whereas clothing prices were the sole category to decrease. This regional decline contrasts with the national average, where Madrid, Valencia, and the Balearic Islands still face higher inflation. Meanwhile, regions like Extremadura and Murcia saw significant price surges, highlighting uneven economic pressures across Spain. For open data initiatives, this highlights the necessity of granular, region-specific datasets to accurately monitor hyper-local economic trends. Reliable open statistics enable policymakers and citizens to understand localized inflation drivers beyond national aggregates.
