China publica norma obligatoria para reducir riesgos de filtración de datos en productos electrónicos de segunda mano

China publica norma obligatoria para reducir riesgos de filtración de datos en productos electrónicos de segunda mano

China has established mandatory regulations to standardize data erasure on electronic devices, prioritizing cybersecurity in the second-hand market. This measure aims to mitigate the risk of data leaks by requiring manufacturers to integrate erasure tools or facilitate their effective use. Recycling operators must ensure that personal information is erased before handling devices, maintaining records that demonstrate the effectiveness of the process. This ensures that products are not marketed without having undergone strict security verification. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how technical regulation can balance hardware reuse with privacy protection, establishing standards that affect both transparency and the security of personal data throughout product life cycles.
