Así es la población juvenil en Paraguay - Nacionales - ABC Color

The report from the National Institute of Statistics (INE) reveals that young Paraguayan women face significant structural inequalities in access to services and economic opportunities. Despite representing one-quarter of the total population, there is a marked digital divide between urban and rural areas, as well as wage disparities where women earn considerably less than men. Moreover, most lack health insurance, highlighting a critical social vulnerability that limits their economic mobility and well-being. These data are fundamental to the open data movement because they demonstrate how statistical transparency enables the identification of systemic inequities. By providing information disaggregated by gender, location, and labor sector, citizens and civil society organizations can demand fairer public policies. The dissemination of these indicators facilitates critical analysis and citizen monitoring, compelling authorities to address issues such as regional poverty and the lack of social protection with concrete evidence. The relevance of this report lies in its ability to transform abstract data into tools for social change. By visualizing the reality of young people, it fosters accountability and prioritizes areas requiring urgent intervention, such as rural education and wage equality. This open data-based approach empowers society to participate in decision-making, ensuring that national development equitably includes all demographic segments and reduces historical gaps in inequality.
