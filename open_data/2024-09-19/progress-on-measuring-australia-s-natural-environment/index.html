Progress On Measuring Australia's Natural Environment

Australia’s first national ecosystem accounts, launching in 2025, quantify how nature underpins economic and social wellbeing. This initiative significantly advances open data by making critical environmental metrics publicly accessible. It enables researchers and policymakers to better understand the vital link between biodiversity and human prosperity through transparent, standardized information.
