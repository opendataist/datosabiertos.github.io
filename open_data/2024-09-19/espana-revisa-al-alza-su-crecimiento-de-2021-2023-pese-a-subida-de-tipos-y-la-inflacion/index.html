España revisa al alza su crecimiento de 2021-2023 pese a subida de tipos y la inflación

España revisa al alza su crecimiento de 2021-2023 pese a subida de tipos y la inflación

Spain has upwardly revised its annual economic growth projections for 2021–2023, demonstrating resilience against inflation and rising interest rates while outperforming regional peers. This stronger, more balanced expansion enabled the government to meet its debt-reduction targets ahead of schedule, reflecting a rapid post-pandemic recovery. The article highlights Spain’s superior performance compared to the broader eurozone, driven by robust consumer spending and a tourism boom. As a result, national institutions raised their growth forecasts, contrasting with the European Central Bank’s downgraded regional predictions. This data is relevant to open_data as it exemplifies how transparent, official statistical updates from national institutes such as the INE enable real-time economic analysis. Reliable, publicly accessible datasets allow researchers and institutions to validate trends, adjust forecasts, and effectively compare economic health across borders.
