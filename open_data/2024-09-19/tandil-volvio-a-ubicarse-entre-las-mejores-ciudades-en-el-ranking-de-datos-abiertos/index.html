Tandil volvió a ubicarse entre las mejores ciudades en el Ranking de Datos Abiertos

Tandil volvió a ubicarse entre las mejores ciudades en el Ranking de Datos Abiertos

Tandil ranks among the leading Argentine municipalities in open government data, achieving high marks for transparency and data quality. This recognition, part of the ninth edition of the Open Knowledge Foundation’s index, highlights the city’s consistent commitment to releasing reusable public information. The evaluation covers critical sectors such as budgets, public works, and procurement, assessing data formats, licenses, and update frequency to ensure genuine accessibility for citizens. The methodology emphasizes rigorous auditing, including a review period for municipalities to correct or supplement findings. This process serves as a civil society check on local governments, aiming to enforce active transparency and accountability. By prioritizing data usability and openness, the index encourages municipalities to adopt best practices that facilitate the public’s right to know and foster informed civic engagement. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how standardized international metrics can drive local policy improvements. It demonstrates that systematic evaluation and peer comparison effectively motivate governments to enhance their data ecosystems. For the open data community, such rankings validate the importance of structured, transparent information flows as essential tools for democratic oversight and effective governance.
