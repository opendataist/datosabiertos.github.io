Comisionados del INAI ven diálogo con Morena

The leadership of the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) has initiated a direct dialogue with legislators from the Morena party concerning the controversial proposal to abolish autonomous bodies. They argue that such a reform would constitute a significant setback for transparency and democracy. Rather than eliminating these entities, they propose a reengineering process aimed at modernizing institutions and achieving substantial cost savings, thereby strengthening—rather than dismantling—these crucial oversight mechanisms. At the same time, the institute unveiled a major modernization of the National Transparency Platform. This upgraded system centralizes access for thousands of obligated entities, enhancing user security and providing robust statistical data. The improvements ensure that citizens can efficiently monitor government actions and request information, reinforcing the infrastructure necessary for public accountability and open access to data. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the ongoing tension between political efforts to centralize or eliminate independent oversight bodies and the technical necessity of maintaining transparent, accessible information systems. It underscores that protecting autonomous bodies is essential for preserving the integrity of open government initiatives, ensuring that public data remains reliable, secure, and freely available for societal use without political interference.
