Gobierno se integra a la iniciativa de la Unión Europea sobre ciberseguridad y cibercrimen

Gobierno se integra a la iniciativa de la Unión Europea sobre ciberseguridad y cibercrimen

Guatemala has officially joined the Latin America and Caribbean Cyber Competence Centre, an initiative of the European Union. This move aims to strengthen the national government’s cyber security capabilities, aligning state practices with international standards. The central objective is to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of public services, ensuring that citizens benefit from safer and more robust digital environments. Guatemala’s integration into this regional sub-group enables the exchange of best practices and fosters bilateral cooperation with the EU. By joining other Central American and Caribbean countries, the nation can strategically position itself in the region, prioritizing social inclusion and the development of cyber capabilities. This collaboration facilitates the sharing of experiences in combating cybercrime, creating a joint learning ecosystem that enhances digital security across the entire region. This news is relevant to the open data community because cyber security is the essential foundation for trust in digital transparency. Without protected and standardized infrastructures, the publication and access to open government data face critical risks. Therefore, strengthening these capabilities ensures that public information is accessible, intact, and available, safeguarding both data sovereignty and citizens’ rights in cyberspace.
