Freedom of Information statistics: April to June 2024

Freedom of Information statistics: April to June 2024

This bulletin details the implementation of the Freedom of Information Act and Environmental Information Regulations within central government bodies for mid-2024. It highlights how these frameworks facilitate public access to information, a core principle of open data initiatives that promote transparency and accountability in governance. The statistics are recognized as National Statistics, signifying rigorous adherence to standards of trustworthiness, quality, and value. This accreditation ensures the data is impartial, methodologically sound, and accessible, thereby supporting the reliability required for robust open data ecosystems. By maintaining these high standards, the release supports informed public scrutiny and continuous improvement of government operations. Such verified, high-quality data releases are essential for the open data movement, enabling users to trust and effectively utilize public sector information for research and civic engagement.
