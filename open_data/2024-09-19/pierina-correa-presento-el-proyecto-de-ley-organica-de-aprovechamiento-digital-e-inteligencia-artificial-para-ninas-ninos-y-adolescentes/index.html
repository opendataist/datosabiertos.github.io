Pierina Correa presentó el proyecto de Ley Orgánica de Aprovechamiento Digital e Inteligencia Artificial para Niñas, Niños y Adolescentes

Pierina Correa presentó el proyecto de Ley Orgánica de Aprovechamiento Digital e Inteligencia Artificial para Niñas, Niños y Adolescentes

This legislative proposal establishes a regulatory framework for the use of digital technologies and artificial intelligence by minors, emphasizing data protection, safety, and equitable access. By mandating transparency and legal accountability, it aims to create a secure environment that fosters innovation while safeguarding fundamental rights. The initiative seeks to integrate young people into the digital workforce through structured protections, ensuring their holistic development without compromising security. This approach balances technological advancement with the necessary safeguards for young users in complex online ecosystems. This is relevant to open data as it highlights the critical intersection between technological governance and individual privacy. The law underscores the necessity of transparent AI systems and robust data governance, which are foundational principles for ensuring ethical and accountable open data practices in the digital age.
