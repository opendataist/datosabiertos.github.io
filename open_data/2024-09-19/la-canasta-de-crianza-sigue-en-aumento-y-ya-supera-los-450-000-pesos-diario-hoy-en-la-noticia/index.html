La canasta de crianza sigue en aumento y ya supera los $450.000 pesos - Diario Hoy En la noticia

The National Institute of Statistics and Census has implemented a new indicator to quantify the monetary cost of child-rearing and household care tasks. This system, developed by the Ministry of Economy, aims to visually represent the economic impact of prices on essential goods such as food, transportation, and health, as well as to value the time devoted to childcare—an aspect traditionally invisible in conventional economic metrics. The relevance of this initiative lies in its focus on gender and inequality, as it reveals that households with fewer members typically have significantly lower incomes compared to other family structures. By integrating the cost of care services into the overall calculation, it exposes the disproportionate economic burden faced by families, particularly those headed by women, providing a key tool for designing more equitable and effective public policies. This data is fundamental to the open data movement because it transforms abstract social concepts into quantifiable and accessible information. By standardizing the measurement of child-rearing costs, it enables greater transparency and comparative analysis, facilitating researchers, civil society organizations, and the general public to examine the relationship between poverty, gender inequality, and child development, thereby fostering accountability and evidence-based discussion.
