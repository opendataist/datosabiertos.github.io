Transparency register

Frontex manages a Transparency Register to prevent conflicts of interest and ensure transparency regarding lobbying activities. By collecting and publishing personal data of individuals contacting the agency, it promotes accountability and public trust in its operations. This practice aligns with EU data protection regulations and serves as a model for how institutions can handle personal information openly. It demonstrates the balance between legal compliance and public access to information. Such systems are crucial for open data initiatives, showing how organizations can maintain integrity while respecting privacy rights. They provide a template for other entities aiming to enhance transparency and engagement with stakeholders.

Source: frontex.europa.eu
