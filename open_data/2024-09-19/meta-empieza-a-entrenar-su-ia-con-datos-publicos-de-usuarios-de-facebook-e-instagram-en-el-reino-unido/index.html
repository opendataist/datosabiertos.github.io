Meta empieza a entrenar su IA con datos públicos de usuarios de Facebook e Instagram en el Reino Unido

Meta has resumed training its artificial intelligence models using public data from users in the United Kingdom, following a regulatory pause. This decision aims to improve the cultural and linguistic accuracy of AI, enabling interactions better tailored to local nuances, while explicitly excluding private data and information about minors. The relevance to the open data movement lies in the tension between technological innovation and individual sovereignty over information. By prioritizing public availability, Meta raises an ethical debate about whether the accessibility of data automatically implies its free use for commercial algorithm training, even when mechanisms for opting out exist. This case highlights the critical need for transparency and consent in the data ecosystem. It demonstrates that the mere existence of public data does not guarantee its ethical use, underscoring the importance of users maintaining clear control over their digital footprint amid the expansion of artificial intelligence.
