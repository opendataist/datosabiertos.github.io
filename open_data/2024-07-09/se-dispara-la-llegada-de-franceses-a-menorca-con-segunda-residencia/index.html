Se dispara la llegada de franceses a Menorca con segunda residencia

Menorca experienced a surge in French second-home buyers during the pandemic, defying global tourism declines. British ownership remains significant, yet Spanish nationals dominate the residency landscape. This trend highlights evolving demographic shifts in Mediterranean property markets. The data reveals changing preferences for secure, tranquil destinations during health crises. It underscores how external shocks can rapidly alter local housing dynamics and visitor nationality distributions, impacting long-term community structures. This information is vital for open data, as it provides transparent insights into migration patterns and real estate trends. Tracking such shifts allows researchers and policymakers to understand socio-economic impacts and resource allocation in tourist-heavy regions effectively.
