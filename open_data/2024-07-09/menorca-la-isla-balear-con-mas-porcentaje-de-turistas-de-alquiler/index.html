Menorca, la isla balear con más porcentaje de turistas de alquiler

Menorca has the highest proportion of tourists staying in short-term rentals among the Balearic Islands. This shift away from traditional hotel accommodation significantly alters local tourism dynamics. The data highlights how rental platforms are reshaping destination profiles, providing crucial insights for open data analysis in the hospitality sector.
