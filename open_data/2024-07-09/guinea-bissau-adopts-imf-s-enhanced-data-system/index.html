Guinea-Bissau Adopts IMF's Enhanced Data System

Guinea-Bissau Adopts IMF's Enhanced Data System

Guinea-Bissau has launched a new data portal to publish essential macroeconomic and financial information, fulfilling key recommendations of the International Monetary Fund’s Enhanced General Data Dissemination System. This initiative reflects the country’s strong commitment to enhancing transparency in its economic data reporting. By centralizing these vital statistics, the nation aims to improve accessibility for diverse users, including policymakers, investors, and the media, both domestically and internationally. The newly established platform serves as a comprehensive hub for national accounts, government debt, and monetary sector data, available in formats that are easily readable by both humans and machines. This technical advancement facilitates more efficient analysis of economic trends across borders and enables earlier detection of potential financial risks. Such proactive monitoring is crucial for averting crises and supporting sustainable economic growth and development within the region. This project is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how standardized, machine-readable publishing frameworks empower countries to share critical information openly. Hosted on an African Development Bank open data platform, the portal exemplifies the global push for transparency as a public good. It highlights the tangible benefits of adhering to international data standards, such as improved sovereign financing conditions and better statistical systems for participating nations.
