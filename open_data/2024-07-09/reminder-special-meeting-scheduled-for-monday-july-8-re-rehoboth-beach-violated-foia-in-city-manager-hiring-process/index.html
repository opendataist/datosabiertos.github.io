Reminder: Special Meeting Scheduled for Monday, July 8, Re: Rehoboth Beach Violated FOIA in City Manager Hiring Process

Reminder: Special Meeting Scheduled for Monday, July 8, Re: Rehoboth Beach Violated FOIA in City Manager Hiring Process

The Attorney General determined that Rehoboth Beach violated public access laws by improperly discussing the city manager’s contract behind closed doors. This ruling highlights a critical gap between local administrative actions and statutory transparency requirements, serving as a cautionary tale for open data advocates. City officials acknowledge the breach, expressing disappointment yet reaffirming their commitment to governmental openness. They recognize that failing to keep executive sessions legally compliant undermines public trust and constitutes an injustice to citizens who deserve full visibility into local governance decisions. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates that true transparency requires more than just releasing information; it demands rigorous adherence to procedural laws governing how data and decisions are made public. It underscores the necessity of robust legal frameworks to ensure that civic proceedings remain accessible and understandable to the community.
