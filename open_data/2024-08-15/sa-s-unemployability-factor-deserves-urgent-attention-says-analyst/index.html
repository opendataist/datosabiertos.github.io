SA's unemployability factor deserves urgent attention, says analyst

The persistent rise in unemployment, particularly among youth, signals a systemic failure within the education sector. Graduates often possess mismatched skills, leaving a significant portion of the population unemployable in the formal economy despite growing demand for specialized labor. This situation highlights a critical disconnect between educational outcomes and market needs, creating a dual crisis that encompasses both those excluded from higher education and skilled graduates facing structural barriers. Without aligned training frameworks, economic growth remains inaccessible to many, exacerbating social and economic instability. This narrative is vital to open data because transparent, high-quality datasets are essential for diagnosing these labor market failures. Open access to detailed employment and educational statistics enables policymakers and researchers to identify skill gaps, evaluate interventions, and drive evidence-based solutions to bridge the gap between education and economic opportunity.
