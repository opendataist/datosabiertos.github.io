Wikimania 2024: Wikipedia and Wikidata as sources for analyzing Americanization (video)

Recent research presented at Wikimania 2024 utilizes open data from Wikipedia and Wikidata to measure the extent of Americanization globally. By analyzing interest in American topics across different regions, the study reveals significant disparities linked to national development levels. This approach offers a novel perspective on cultural dominance, demonstrating how publicly available digital platforms can quantify complex social phenomena that were previously difficult to measure rigorously. The analysis highlights inherent biases within Wikipedia’s content distribution, showing uneven coverage of American-related subjects across various language versions. These discrepancies provide valuable insights into the global representation of knowledge, suggesting that the current state of the encyclopedia may not fully reflect diverse cultural perspectives. The findings underscore the importance of recognizing these imbalances and advocate for a more equitable and inclusive approach to content creation and editing. This study is highly relevant to open data as it validates the potential of community-generated platforms as robust tools for social science research. It demonstrates that open datasets can effectively capture and analyze subtle shifts in global cultural dynamics. Furthermore, it encourages the open data community to leverage these resources for critical evaluations of knowledge equity, ensuring that digital encyclopedias remain balanced and comprehensive resources for users worldwide.
