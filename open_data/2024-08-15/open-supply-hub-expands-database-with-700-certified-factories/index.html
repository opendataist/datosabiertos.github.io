Open Supply Hub expands database with 700 certified factories

The Ethical Supply Chain Program now publishes details of certified factories on Open Supply Hub, creating a collaborative, open dataset that enhances supplier transparency. This partnership addresses the growing pressure on brands to disclose sourcing information driven by consumer expectations and strict regulatory requirements. By making this data freely accessible, the initiative allows buyers to quickly identify responsible suppliers while giving ethical factories greater visibility. Open data is central to this effort, shifting supply chain management from private silos to public, verifiable records. The integration of certified factory information into a shared platform demonstrates how open datasets can standardize ethical claims. This accessibility empowers stakeholders to verify compliance without relying solely on proprietary tools, fostering trust through collective verification rather than isolated reporting. This move illustrates how open data frameworks can operationalize sustainability mandates. It provides a scalable model for industries where traceability is legally required and commercially vital. By lowering the barrier to entry for transparency, open platforms enable smaller suppliers to demonstrate their commitment to ethical practices, ultimately driving industry-wide accountability and more informed consumer choices through data sharing.
