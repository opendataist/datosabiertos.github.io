Revela INEGI las 10 causas principales de muerte en México

The article highlights how official mortality data serves as a critical open dataset for public health analysis. By publishing detailed cause-of-death statistics, INEGI enables researchers to identify leading health risks, such as heart disease and diabetes, across different demographics. This transparency allows policymakers and citizens to monitor epidemic trends and evaluate the effectiveness of the healthcare system. Understanding gender and age disparities in mortality rates helps target interventions more effectively, ensuring that resources are allocated where they are needed most. The relevance to open data lies in its role as a foundational resource for evidence-based decision-making. Accessible, granular datasets empower society to address systemic health issues, fostering accountability and driving improvements in national public health strategies through informed, data-driven actions.
