Enfermedades cardíacas, diabetes y cáncer lideran las estadísticas de muertes en México: INEGI | Revista Punto de Vista

Mexico’s leading causes of death are chronic diseases, reflecting a broader national health burden. This shift underscores the critical need for robust, standardized mortality data to guide effective public health policies and resource allocation. Violence remains a significant cause of death, disproportionately affecting the productive age group. This highlights how open data on violent deaths is essential for understanding social safety trends and evaluating the impact of security strategies on the population's well-being. The reliance on preliminary administrative records demonstrates the current infrastructure for data collection. Improving transparency and accessibility in this data allows for better analysis, ensuring that official statistics accurately reflect demographic changes and emerging health crises over time.
