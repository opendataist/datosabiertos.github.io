La tasa de desempleo en México baja al 2,6 % al cierre de 2023 - Mundo - ABC Color

Mexico recorded a slight decrease in unemployment and informal employment rates, indicating modest improvements in the labor market. Despite this progress, more than half of the workforce remains in the informal sector, highlighting a significant structural challenge that persists despite government claims of low unemployment. A notable gender disparity continues to define the economic landscape, as women remain largely excluded from the labor force compared to men. This imbalance suggests that while overall employment figures are rising, the benefits are not being distributed evenly across demographics, leaving a substantial portion of the female population economically inactive. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity for transparent, accessible, and detailed statistical reporting. By making such granular information available to the public, researchers and policymakers can better analyze social inequalities and evaluate the true effectiveness of economic policies, ensuring that official narratives are grounded in verifiable, high-quality evidence.
