El INE arranca con la encuesta de hogares - Economía - ABC Color

The INE’s permanent household survey collects comprehensive socioeconomic data to accurately reflect national realities. This information is essential for designing evidence-based public policies that address the actual needs of the population. Privacy is strictly guaranteed by law, ensuring that all individual respondents’ data remain confidential. This legal protection fosters trust, encouraging citizens to cooperate with the survey despite previous credibility issues related to the census. This process is vital for open data, as it establishes a transparent, legally protected framework for collecting official statistics. It demonstrates how secure, standardized data collection supports accountability and informed governance in public administration.
