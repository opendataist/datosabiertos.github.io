En noviembre, repuntó sector de comercio y construcción en Veracruz

Construction and commerce showed a significant rebound in November, driven by rising incomes, employment, and wages. This dynamism reflects a tangible economic recovery in key sectors, signaling an improvement in local productive activity. These data are relevant to open data because they highlight the importance of monitoring open economic indicators. Access to this information facilitates transparency, enabling citizens and analysts to better understand market trends and the region’s financial health. Transparency in these indicators fosters accountability. By making INEGI data public, civil society is empowered to evaluate economic policies. This underscores the value of open data as a tool for governance and informed development.
