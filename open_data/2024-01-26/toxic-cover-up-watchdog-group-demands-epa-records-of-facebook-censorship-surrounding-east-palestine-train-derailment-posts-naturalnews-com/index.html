TOXIC COVER-UP: Watchdog group demands EPA records of Facebook censorship surrounding East Palestine train derailment posts – NaturalNews.com

The Government Accountability Project has initiated a Freedom of Information Act request to investigate alleged coordination between the Environmental Protection Agency and major tech platforms regarding the censorship of content related to the East Palestine train derailment. The inquiry focuses on suspicions that government entities assisted in suppressing user posts and expert commentary questioning the official narrative about health risks, suggesting a potential collusion to control the public discourse surrounding the environmental disaster. This situation highlights critical concerns about the intersection of government authority and private corporate moderation, particularly regarding the suppression of independent scientific analysis. By flagging or removing posts from scientists and activists discussing toxic exposure, platforms may be acting as mechanisms for censorship rather than neutral arbiters. This raises urgent questions about whether government agencies are leveraging their influence to silence dissent and protect corporate interests at the expense of transparent, open dialogue concerning public safety and environmental hazards. The relevance to open data lies in the demand for governmental transparency and the integrity of public information systems. When official records and communication channels are withheld or manipulated, it undermines the ability of citizens and researchers to access unfiltered facts. Ensuring that data regarding environmental impacts and government communications remains freely available is essential for accountability, allowing the public to critically evaluate responses to disasters without interference from coordinated censorship efforts by state and corporate actors.
