Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The proposal put forward by the Civic Coalition seeks to establish an Open Import Data Portal that would provide public access to complete and up-to-date information on import license applications, product categories, and the status of licenses in Argentina. The main objective of this mechanism is to eliminate the administrative discretion inherent in the current system, significantly reducing corruption risks by ensuring that any citizen can monitor how import authorizations are managed. The initiative requires that information be published in open and digital formats, establishing strict deadlines for administrative resolutions. If these time limits are exceeded, the license is automatically deemed approved. This measure aims to replace the opacity of previous systems, such as SIRA, whose frequent modifications have restricted reasonable access to imports and protected foreign exchange reserves through exchange rate distortions, negatively affecting economic predictability and regulatory transparency. This project is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates how the release of structural government information in machine-readable formats can serve as a tool for democratic oversight. By transforming sensitive administrative data into accessible public goods, it promotes accountability and discourages corrupt practices, demonstrating that transparency is not merely a technical issue but a fundamental pillar for institutional integrity and economic efficiency.
