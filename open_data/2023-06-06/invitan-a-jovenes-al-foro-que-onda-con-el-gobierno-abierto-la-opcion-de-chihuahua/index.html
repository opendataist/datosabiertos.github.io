Invitan a jóvenes al foro ¿Qué onda con el Gobierno Abierto? | La Opción de Chihuahua

The Chihuahua state government is organizing a forum to engage youth and citizens in the principles of open government, emphasizing transparency, accountability, and public participation. This initiative aims to foster collaborative dialogue between the state and civil society to address local governance challenges through innovation and inclusive policies. The event seeks to establish a municipal policy grounded in the real needs of citizens, using the Open Government Partnership framework as its foundation. By involving experts and organized citizens, the administration intends to build a model of transparency and accountability that enhances civic trust and promotes competitive governance through active community involvement. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the foundational shift toward transparent governance structures. Open data serves as the technical mechanism to fulfill these principles of accountability and public access, enabling the measurable and verifiable transparency that the forum advocates as essential to modern, citizen-centric administration.
