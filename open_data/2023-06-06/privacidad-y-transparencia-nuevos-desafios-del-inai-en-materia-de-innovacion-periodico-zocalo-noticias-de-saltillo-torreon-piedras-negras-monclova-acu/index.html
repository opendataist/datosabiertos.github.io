Privacidad y transparencia, nuevos desafíos del INAI en materia de innovación | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The Mexican Institute for Transparency is globally recognized for its leadership in privacy and transparency, fostering international cooperation through regional networks. By positioning itself as a pioneer, the institution stresses that public support is essential for enacting robust regulations, aiming to preserve Mexico’s role as a regional reference point in these critical debates. Experts emphasize that protecting personal information is increasingly vital, as individuals often underestimate the risks associated with data exposure. This highlights the urgent need for advanced regulatory frameworks capable of addressing the challenges posed by rapid technological development, ensuring that legal structures remain both flexible and strong enough to confront emerging digital threats. This discourse is relevant to open data because it establishes the necessary balance between transparency and privacy. Effective open data initiatives require trust and clear legal safeguards; therefore, the emphasis on robust privacy protections ensures that data-sharing mechanisms are sustainable, respect individual rights, and maximize public value.
