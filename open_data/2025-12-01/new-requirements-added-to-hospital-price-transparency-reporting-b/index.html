New Requirements Added to Hospital Price Transparency Reporting b

New Requirements Added to Hospital Price Transparency Reporting b

The CMS final rule significantly enhances hospital price transparency by replacing estimated allowed amounts with detailed percentile data, including the median, 10th, and 90th percentiles. This shift aims to provide consumers and researchers with a more accurate, uniform, and actionable view of actual negotiated healthcare costs rather than generic standard charges. Hospitals must also integrate their organizational National Provider Identifier into machine-readable files and strengthen their attestation statements to include senior executive oversight. These structural changes ensure greater accountability and traceability, forcing institutions to validate that the data they release is both comprehensive and derived from authentic historical remittance records. This update is crucial for open data initiatives because it increases the granularity and reliability of publicly available healthcare pricing datasets. By mandating specific statistical ranges and stricter verification processes, the rule reduces data noise and ambiguity, enabling better market analysis, policy evaluation, and consumer empowerment through higher-quality, standardized information.
