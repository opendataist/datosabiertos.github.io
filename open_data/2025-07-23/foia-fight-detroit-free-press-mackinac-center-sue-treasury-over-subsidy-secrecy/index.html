FOIA Fight: Detroit Free Press, Mackinac Center Sue Treasury Over Subsidy Secrecy

FOIA Fight: Detroit Free Press, Mackinac Center Sue Treasury Over Subsidy Secrecy

A lawsuit challenges the Michigan Department of Treasury’s refusal to disclose records regarding substantial public subsidies provided to a private real estate development. The plaintiffs argue that constitutional mandates for transparency override statutory exemptions, emphasizing the public’s right to understand how taxpayer funds are allocated to specific projects. This case highlights the critical tension between government confidentiality laws and the public’s interest in tracking financial incentives. It underscores the necessity of accessible data to verify whether public investments deliver promised economic benefits, ensuring accountability in how state resources support private enterprise. The dispute is vital to open data principles, as it tests whether government-generated financial records remain accessible despite claims of privacy. Ensuring transparency in these calculations is essential for maintaining public trust and allowing citizens to scrutinize the use of public money for private development incentives.
