FOI: Censors Board gets high ranking - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

The National Film and Video Censors Board’s high ranking in transparency underscores the critical role government agencies play in fostering open data ecosystems. By achieving a top-tier position among public institutions, the board demonstrates that robust information access mechanisms are not merely regulatory burdens but vital tools for building public trust and institutional accountability. This achievement highlights how proactive disclosure and timely responses to information requests directly enhance civic engagement. The metrics used to evaluate compliance, such as proactive website updates and efficient handling of inquiries, serve as best practice models for other ministries aiming to modernize their data sharing practices and reduce bureaucratic opacity. Relevance to open data lies in the validation that structured, accessible public records empower stakeholders and improve service delivery. The board’s success illustrates that when cultural sector regulators adopt transparent data habits, they facilitate easier business operations and promote a culture of openness that benefits filmmakers and the broader public alike.
