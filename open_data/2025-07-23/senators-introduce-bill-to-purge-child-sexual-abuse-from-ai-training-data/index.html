Senators introduce bill to purge child sexual abuse from AI training data

Bipartisan legislation urges AI developers to cleanse training data of illicit imagery through voluntary NIST guidelines, aiming to prevent AI-generated child exploitation. This regulatory effort directly impacts open data ecosystems by mandating stricter ethical vetting standards. It highlights the critical intersection of transparency, safety, and responsible data curation in modern artificial intelligence systems.
