It might be time for IT to consider AI models that don’t steal

It might be time for IT to consider AI models that don’t steal

Enterprises face significant legal risks from opaque training data in generative AI models. Major vendors withhold crucial details regarding data sources, age, and compliance with privacy and copyright laws. This lack of transparency hinders organizations’ ability to ensure their AI systems adhere to regulatory standards. Consequently, businesses risk severe legal exposure due to potential violations in the underlying datasets they cannot verify. For open data advocates, this highlights the critical need for transparency. Without accessible, verified, and sanitized data sources, the integrity and safety of AI systems remain fundamentally compromised, undermining trust in technological advancements.
