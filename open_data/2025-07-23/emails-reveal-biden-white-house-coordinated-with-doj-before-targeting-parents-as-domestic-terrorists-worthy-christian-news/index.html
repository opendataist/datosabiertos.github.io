Emails Reveal Biden White House Coordinated With DOJ Before Targeting Parents As 'Domestic Terrorists' - Worthy Christian News

Emails Reveal Biden White House Coordinated With DOJ Before Targeting Parents As 'Domestic Terrorists' - Worthy Christian News

This article reveals that the Biden White House coordinated with the Department of Justice prior to issuing a controversial 2021 memo labeling concerned parents at school board meetings as potential "domestic terrorists." Internal documents obtained through Freedom of Information Act requests contradict Attorney General Merrick Garland’s assertion of independence, showing that the administration was actively discussing federal intervention before the policy was announced. This collaboration suggests that the decision to mobilize federal resources, including the FBI, was not an autonomous legal judgment but a politically driven response to lobbying by school boards concerned about parental activism regarding educational policies. Career DOJ attorneys reportedly warned that most incidents cited by the National School Boards Association were protected under the First Amendment or better handled by local laws. Despite these internal concerns that the conduct was "nothing remotely federal," the directive proceeded quickly. The article implies that this swift action may have been motivated by political considerations, potentially influencing upcoming elections, and highlights the subsequent appointment of a key advisor involved in the decision-making process as a federal judge. Critics argue this represents an unprecedented attempt to chill dissent and suppress parental rights under the guise of public safety. These findings are critically relevant to open data and government transparency, as they demonstrate how FOIA disclosures can expose hidden political influences within seemingly independent law enforcement agencies. The case underscores the importance of accessible government records in verifying claims of institutional independence and detecting potential abuses of power. By making internal communications public, such revelations allow citizens and journalists to assess whether federal actions are based on legal merit or political expediency, thereby safeguarding democratic integrity and ensuring that law enforcement remains impartial.
