Esta es la segunda ciudad de la Comunidad de Madrid con más empresas emergentes

Las Rozas has reaffirmed its leadership in Madrid’s technology ecosystem, securing its position as the city with the second-highest number of active startups in the region for the third consecutive year. This recognition underscores the success of local policies aimed at fostering innovation and attracting talent, positioning the municipality as a key reference point within the Community of Madrid, which serves as the nation’s primary hub for technology companies. By entering the national top 10, Las Rozas demonstrates its ability to compete with Spain’s major cities, highlighting a robust and continuously growing economic fabric. Las Rozas Innova serves as the backbone of this ecosystem, acting as a connecting platform between entrepreneurs, investors, and public administration. More than just a workspace, the hub facilitates the consolidation of technology projects through personalized services, access to talent, and funding opportunities. This structure promotes open innovation and strategic collaboration, creating a dynamic community where startups receive comprehensive support to scale their businesses and address social needs through advanced technological solutions. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparency and data analysis are essential for measuring economic performance and guiding public policy. The reports that position Las Rozas are based on detailed statistical data on businesses and employment, demonstrating the value of open information for making strategic decisions. Furthermore, the hub uses data to connect startups with public innovation challenges, showing how the availability of information facilitates collaboration between the private sector and public administration to improve citizens’ quality of life through technology.
