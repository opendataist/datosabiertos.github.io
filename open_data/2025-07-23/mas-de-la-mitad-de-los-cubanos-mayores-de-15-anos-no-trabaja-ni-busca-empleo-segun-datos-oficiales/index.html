Más de la mitad de los cubanos mayores de 15 años no trabaja ni busca empleo, según datos oficiales

Más de la mitad de los cubanos mayores de 15 años no trabaja ni busca empleo, según datos oficiales

The article reveals that Cuba’s labor market is severely contracted, with more than half of the working-age population disconnected from employment and the workforce rapidly aging. This demographic shift, exacerbated by youth migration and low wages, creates a critical labor shortage despite officially low unemployment rates among those actively seeking work. The findings highlight a structural crisis in which economic participation is declining while the population ages significantly. The gap between real incomes and the cost of living further discourages workforce engagement, leaving the country with one of the lowest employment rates in Latin America. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent statistical information can expose hidden socioeconomic realities. By making labor metrics accessible, open data enables better analysis of demographic trends and policy effectiveness, fostering informed public debate on critical issues such as migration and economic stability.
