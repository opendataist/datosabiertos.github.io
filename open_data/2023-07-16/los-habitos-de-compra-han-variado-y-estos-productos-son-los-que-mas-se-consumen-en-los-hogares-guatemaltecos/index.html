Los hábitos de compra han variado y estos productos son los que más se consumen en los hogares guatemaltecos

Guatemala is updating its household consumption patterns through the ENIGH survey, which is crucial for recalibrating the Basic Food Basket and inflation metrics. By transitioning the database from 2010 to late 2023, the statistical institute ensures that official price indices reflect current consumer behaviors. This shift incorporates emerging technological preferences, such as streaming services, while removing obsolete categories like DVDs, thereby providing a more accurate representation of modern household spending. The survey also supports the calculation of updated poverty indices by integrating census data and economic conditions. These results will be available to new government authorities, enabling evidence-based policy prioritization. This update marks a significant gap fill, as the previous poverty assessment dated back to 2014. The comprehensive data collection involves international collaboration and aims to identify shifts in extreme poverty and overall living standards with greater precision. This article highlights the vital role of open data in public administration and economic transparency. Access to current, granular household data allows for better resource allocation and social program design. By modernizing consumption baskets and poverty metrics, the government can respond more effectively to economic changes. Ultimately, this process underscores how timely, reliable public statistics are essential for fostering accountability and improving the quality of life for citizens.
