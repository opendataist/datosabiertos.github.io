Menos papel, más transparencia: la Municipalidad de Escobar se convirtió en Autoridad de Registro de Firma Digital - Zona Norte Diario Online

Escobar has become a reference for remote digital signatures, issuing free certificates to residents and officials. This initiative enhances administrative transparency and security while eliminating the use of paper for official documents. The municipality has also implemented electronic document management systems to optimize internal workflows. These measures streamline procedures for notes and licenses, ensuring authentic, verifiable digital records that carry legal weight. This transition to digital governance exemplifies open data principles by reducing bureaucratic barriers. It demonstrates how municipalities can leverage technology to improve accessibility, efficiency, and public trust through secure, paperless administrative processes.
