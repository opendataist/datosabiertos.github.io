La construcción en Argentina cayó un 10,2 % en enero

Argentina’s construction sector is experiencing a severe contraction, characterized by significant declines in activity and sales due to hyperinflation and economic uncertainty. This downturn has triggered warnings of substantial job losses, driven by the suspension of public works and market instability. The situation underscores the critical need for transparent, timely data to monitor industrial health during crises. Accurate open data enables stakeholders to understand the real-time impact of policy decisions on employment and production. Access to such information is essential for accountability and informed public debate. Without reliable open data, the broader implications of economic shocks on social welfare remain obscured, hindering effective responses to structural challenges.
