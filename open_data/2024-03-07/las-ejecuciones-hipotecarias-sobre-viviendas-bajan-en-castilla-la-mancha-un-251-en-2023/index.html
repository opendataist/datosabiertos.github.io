Las ejecuciones hipotecarias sobre viviendas bajan en Castilla-La Mancha un 25,1% en 2023

This report details a significant national decline in mortgage foreclosures, marking the lowest figures since 2020 and reversing previous sharp increases. The data highlights regional disparities, with Andalusia leading in new and used home executions, while autonomous cities like Melilla show minimal activity. These trends indicate a stabilizing housing market after years of volatility. Although the fourth quarter saw a slight quarterly rise, the annual decrease suggests improved conditions for homeowners and lenders alike across Spain. Relevant to open data, this INE statistics exemplify how transparent, granular public records enable society to monitor critical economic indicators. By making these detailed execution metrics accessible, policymakers and citizens can accurately assess housing security trends and evaluate the effectiveness of social protection measures.
