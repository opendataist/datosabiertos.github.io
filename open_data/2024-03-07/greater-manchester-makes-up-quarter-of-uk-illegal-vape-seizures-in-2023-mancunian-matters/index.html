Greater Manchester makes up quarter of UK illegal vape seizures in 2023 - Mancunian Matters

Manchester and Salford are major UK hotspots for illegal vape seizures, highlighting a significant public health challenge. The high volume of seized devices suggests widespread possession of non-compliant products, often due to user unawareness of legal capacity limits. Enforcement remains critically weak, with a tiny fraction of caught sellers receiving substantial fines. This low risk of penalty allows rogue retailers to operate with impunity, undermining regulatory efforts and consumer protection across the region. This case illustrates the urgent need for stricter open data transparency in local authority enforcement statistics. Improving access to detailed seizure and sanction data would empower stakeholders to identify trends, advocate for higher penalties, and drive more effective regulatory compliance.
