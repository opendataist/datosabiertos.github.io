Desafío 2024: Datos Abiertos para Guatemala

The article positions Open Data as the fundamental catalyst for building transparent and participatory societies, arguing that freely accessible information is essential for modern digital transformation. By removing barriers to data usage, governments can empower citizens to access critical information, fostering an informed public capable of engaging meaningfully in civic processes. This shift moves beyond mere statistics, establishing data as a shared asset for the common good. Guatemala’s strategic vision leverages Open Data as a key tool to combat corruption and enhance the efficiency of digital governance. The focus is on enabling individuals to analyze and utilize information, thereby creating opportunities for accountability. By facilitating collaboration between the government and civil society through digital platforms, the initiative aims to strengthen oversight mechanisms, allowing citizens to effectively evaluate and question the actions of their representatives. This approach is vital to open data because it demonstrates how technical availability translates into social empowerment and political integrity. The article underscores the necessity of sensitizing the public to view data not as cold reports, but as resources for civic engagement. Ultimately, it reaffirms a national commitment to embedding data openness into the core of presidential governance, ensuring that transparency and digital participation serve as the pillars for a just and strong society.
