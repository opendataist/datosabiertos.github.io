Creció en el estado 10% productividad de maquilas al final del año

Chihuahua exhibits divergent economic trends, with manufacturing productivity increasing while commerce declines. This contrast reveals shifting efficiency dynamics between sectors, highlighting the complexity of regional labor markets. Manufacturing benefits from lower unit labor costs despite higher output, whereas commerce faces rising expenses amid shrinking performance. These opposing trajectories underscore the need for sector-specific strategies to address distinct operational challenges.
