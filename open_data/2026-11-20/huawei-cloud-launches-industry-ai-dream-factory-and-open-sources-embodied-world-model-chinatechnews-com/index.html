Huawei Cloud Launches 'Industry AI Dream Factory' and Open-Sources Embodied World Model - ChinaTechNews.com

Huawei Cloud Launches 'Industry AI Dream Factory' and Open-Sources Embodied World Model - ChinaTechNews.com

Huawei Cloud has launched the "Industry AI Dream Factory" alongside CloudRobo-WM1.2, an open-source embodied world model that significantly advances robotics from academic research to industrial application. By utilizing a shared backbone and DualAct architecture, this system enables robots to predict physical outcomes and generate direct movement commands, effectively bridging the gap between environmental perception and motor control. The platform addresses critical industry bottlenecks such as data scarcity and hardware fragmentation. Through adaptive pre-training and semantic-preserving motion modeling, it allows diverse robot types to collaborate and learn from incomplete datasets, thereby reducing deployment costs and enhancing generalization across different mechanical designs. This initiative is vital to open_data as it establishes a foundational digital ecosystem for collaborative innovation. By providing open-source tools and cloud-native infrastructure, Huawei aims to unite manufacturers, developers, and researchers, accelerating the widespread industrial adoption of AI-driven robotics through shared knowledge and accessible resources.
