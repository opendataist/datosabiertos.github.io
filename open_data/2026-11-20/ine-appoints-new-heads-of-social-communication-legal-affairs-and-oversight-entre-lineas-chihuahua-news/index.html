INE appoints new heads of Social Communication, Legal Affairs, and Oversight - Entre Líneas - Chihuahua News

INE appoints new heads of Social Communication, Legal Affairs, and Oversight - Entre Líneas - Chihuahua News

The National Electoral Institute (INE) has made changes to its leadership structure, appointing new heads for key areas such as communications, legal affairs, and oversight. These appointees bring prior experience within the INE itself or in electoral tribunals, aiming to ensure operational continuity and technical specialization in strategic functions. At the same time, it was confirmed that the terms of office for heads of units linked to local bodies and data protection have concluded. This reshuffle is significant for open data, as it directly affects the units responsible for transparency and access to public information. The turnover in the Transparency and Personal Data Protection Unit implies a transition in the management of citizen information and the protocols for opening government data. The lack of details about the replacements has generated uncertainty regarding how disclosure standards will be maintained. For citizens and civil society, it is crucial to understand how these institutional changes will impact the availability, quality, and timely dissemination of public data. Stability in these units determines trust in electoral accountability and democratic transparency.
