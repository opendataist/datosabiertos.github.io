Neighborhood Collectives for the Defense of the Territory Appeal to the Transparency Council Over Lack of Response from the Cantabria Government and the Ebro River Basin Authority - El Faradio | Journalism That Counts

Neighborhood Collectives for the Defense of the Territory Appeal to the Transparency Council Over Lack of Response from the Cantabria Government and the Ebro River Basin Authority - El Faradio | Journalism That Counts

The lack of response from public administrations to requests for environmental information regarding the processing of new wind farms in Cantabria violates legal deadlines and undermines transparency. This situation prevents citizens from accessing crucial documentation needed to assess the real impacts of these projects on areas of high natural value, highlighting a disconnect between administrative management and the public’s right to know. The pending information is vital for understanding how cumulative impacts on threatened species and the headwaters of the Ebro River are being evaluated. Without detailed studies on biodiversity and hydrobiology, it is impossible to guarantee that wind energy expansion will not cause irreversible damage to sensitive habitats, underscoring the urgent need for openness in technical files before any actions are carried out. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates that the mere existence of transparency laws is insufficient if deadlines are not met and data are not accessible in real time. Citizens need open and timely data to effectively participate in decisions that affect their territory, turning access to information into an essential tool for democratic oversight against administrative opacity and potential environmental degradation.
