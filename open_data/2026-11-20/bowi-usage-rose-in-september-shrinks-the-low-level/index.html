Bowí usage rose in September; "shrinks" the low level

Bowí usage rose in September; "shrinks" the low level

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) has published the Urban Transport Usage Statistics (ETUP), revealing that urban transport in Chihuahua shows monthly growth but an annual decline. This data openness enables monitoring of mobility and its socioeconomic impact. The information is relevant to open data initiatives, as it facilitates analysis of public service efficiency. The Bowí system recorded fewer passengers than the previous year, although it covered a greater distance. This annual decline contrasts with the monthly rebound due to the return to classes. These historical data are crucial for understanding public transport usage trends. Transparency in figures such as the number of passengers and kilometers traveled is vital. It allows citizens and governments to evaluate the system’s performance. Access to these statistics fosters accountability in urban services.
