Congresswoman from Pacto Histórico faces trial over fake degree; documents expose her - Pulzo

Congresswoman from Pacto Histórico faces trial over fake degree; documents expose her - Pulzo

The alleged falsity of Congresswoman Luz Verónica Estrada’s professional title, from the Pacto Histórico, has triggered official and political scrutiny after being debunked by the National University of Medellín. The institution confirmed that there are no records of her graduation as a Mining and Metallurgical Engineer, declaring the diploma presented by the representative to be false and announcing that the case will be referred to judicial authorities to investigate potential document forgery. This finding calls into question the integrity of the academic credentials required to practice the profession and hold public office, creating a conflict between the published career history and institutional records. The discrepancies in dates and the lack of registration with the Colombian Council of Engineering and Architecture underscore the need for rigorous verification of public officials’ backgrounds, highlighting the importance of transparency in the management of public resources and elected positions. The case is significant for the open data movement because it demonstrates how publishing academic and professional records in accessible and verifiable formats enables civil society and the media to detect inconsistencies in real time. By making this information visible, accountability is strengthened, and it has been shown that public information, when available and cross-referenced with other sources, serves as a fundamental tool for citizen oversight and the defense of democratic legality.
