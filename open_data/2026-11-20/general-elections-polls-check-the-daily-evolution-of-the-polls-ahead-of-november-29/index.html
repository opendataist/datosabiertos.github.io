General elections polls: Check the daily evolution of the polls ahead of November 29

General elections polls: Check the daily evolution of the polls ahead of November 29

The article presents an interactive aggregator that synthesizes voting intentions from more than two hundred polls, highlighting the consolidation of the right-wing bloc, led by the People’s Party (PP) and Vox, ahead of the left-wing bloc, composed of the Spanish Socialist Workers’ Party (PSOE) and the Broad Front (Frente Amplio). This tool allows users to track fluctuations in public opinion through weekly and daily averages, revealing relative stability in popular support for the right, contrasted with a significant decline among left-wing parties, which have lost much of the support they garnered in previous elections. The relevance of this resource for the open-data community lies in its transparent methodology for aggregation and weighting. By combining multiple opinion polls and applying quality filters based on sample size, publication date, and the track record of polling organizations’ accuracy, the system mitigates individual biases and statistical noise. This approach demonstrates how cross-referencing public data can yield more robust and reliable indicators than simple arithmetic averages, offering a clear model of data governance in the political sphere. The implications suggest that the current electoral dynamic is defined more by voter mobilization and the consolidation of prior voting intentions than by large-scale new affiliations. The right-wing bloc is already activated, while the left seeks to consolidate its sympathizers and attract undecided voters. This analysis underscores the critical importance of methodological transparency in the dissemination of electoral data, as understanding how primary sources are processed and weighted is essential for correctly interpreting the climate of public opinion and avoiding erroneous conclusions derived from the specific biases of individual polling organizations.
