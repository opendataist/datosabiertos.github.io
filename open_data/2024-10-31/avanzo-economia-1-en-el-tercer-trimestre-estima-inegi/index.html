Avanzó economía 1% en el tercer trimestre, estima Inegi

The INEGI reports a 1.0% quarterly GDP growth in the third quarter of 2024, marking an improvement over previous periods. This data highlights the ongoing contribution of the primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors to the national economy, reflecting broader macroeconomic trends. Relevant to open data, this release demonstrates the value of timely, standardized economic indicators. Transparent access to such official statistics empowers researchers and citizens to analyze economic health and foster data-driven decision-making. Ultimately, these estimates underscore the importance of accessible government data. By providing clear metrics on economic performance, open data initiatives enhance public accountability and support evidence-based policy discussions regarding national development.
