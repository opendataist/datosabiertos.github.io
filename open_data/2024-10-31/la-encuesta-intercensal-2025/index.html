La Encuesta Intercensal 2025

The INEGI is preparing for the 2025 Inter-census Survey, a critical mid-period update between the 2020 and 2030 general censuses. This exercise aims to deliver highly current demographic data to support government and private sector decision-making, ensuring that planning and budgeting align with real-time population growth and infrastructure needs. The significance of this initiative lies in the continuity of a 125-year historical record that reveals trends in population, housing, and urban development. By providing detailed estimates at national, state, and municipal levels, these figures enable precise projections for administrative management and investment strategies, ultimately aiming to improve living conditions through data-driven policy implementation. This article is relevant to open_data as it highlights the vital role of accessible, high-quality statistical information in fostering transparency and effective public governance. The availability of such comprehensive datasets allows stakeholders to verify claims, such as educational averages or population densities, thereby empowering civil society to hold institutions accountable and contribute to informed societal discourse based on empirical evidence rather than anecdotal evidence.
