Estas son las recomendaciones de Municipio para proteger tus datos

The article stresses the importance of protecting personal data by avoiding suspicious communications, keeping devices updated, and using strong passwords. These measures are essential for maintaining digital security and preventing fraud in an era of widespread online interactions. This guidance is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for robust data protection frameworks. As governments manage increasing volumes of public information, ensuring the security and privacy of individual records remains a fundamental ethical and legal obligation. The government of Chihuahua prioritizes this protection by providing resources to citizens. By educating the public on safe digital practices, authorities reinforce trust in their data management systems, which is essential for transparent and responsible governance.
