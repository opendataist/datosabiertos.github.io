Propusieron reducir gastos, pero ya es tarde: Sheinbaum sobre el INAI

Sheinbaum argues that the judicial and legislative branches must guarantee transparency internally, eliminating the need for autonomous bodies like the INAI due to alleged internal corruption. This approach prioritizes direct state accountability over independent oversight, aiming to ensure public resource supervision through new legislative frameworks while maintaining technical independence for treaty-linked entities. The article highlights a critical shift in Mexico’s open data landscape, questioning the necessity of external transparency authorities when government branches can self-regulate, thus altering the traditional checks and balances essential for data openness.
