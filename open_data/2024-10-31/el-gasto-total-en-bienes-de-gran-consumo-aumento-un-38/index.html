El gasto total en bienes de gran consumo aumentó un 3,8%

El gasto total en bienes de gran consumo aumentó un 3,8%

The Spanish consumer basket in September 2024 shows resilient demand, with sales volumes increasing while prices continue to rise moderately. This dynamic indicates that although inflationary pressure persists, consumers are maintaining their purchasing levels, suggesting a stabilization in market behavior rather than a contraction in overall consumption. Significant shifts in spending patterns highlight a strong preference for value and health. Consumers are increasingly prioritizing private labels over branded goods, driven by a desire to control costs, while simultaneously showing high demand for dietetic and personal care products. This trend underscores a consumer strategy that balances budget-consciousness with specific lifestyle interests, adapting to economic realities without drastically reducing essential expenditures. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it exemplifies the value of transparent, granular market analytics in understanding societal trends. By analyzing such detailed indicators of price elasticity and shifting consumer preferences, stakeholders can better predict economic behaviors. Furthermore, making this data accessible fosters innovation in retail algorithms and supports evidence-based policy-making aimed at improving economic stability and consumer welfare.
