Economía alemana esquiva la recesión – DW – 30/10/2024

Germany’s gross domestic product (GDP) increased slightly in the third quarter, successfully avoiding a technical recession. This modest growth was primarily driven by rises in private consumption and public spending, surprising economists who had anticipated a contraction. Despite this short-term relief, skepticism remains high regarding the long-term economic outlook. The central bank and government predict continued weakness and stagnation, indicating that the economy is still stuck in a prolonged phase of decline since 2012. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights how transparent, timely statistical releases from national agencies like Destatis allow for immediate market reactions and policy adjustments. Reliable public data is essential for verifying economic health and ensuring accountability in government spending and forecasts.
