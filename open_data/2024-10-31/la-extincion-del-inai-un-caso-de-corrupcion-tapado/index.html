La extinción del INAI: ¿Un caso de corrupción tapado?

La extinción del INAI: ¿Un caso de corrupción tapado?

President Sheinbaum has presented corruption allegations against the INAI without outlining a concrete strategy to address them. This lack of specific investigative or sanctioning measures raises serious doubts about the government’s commitment to tackling the issue effectively. Crucially, the INAI has classified evidence of excessive spending and travel as confidential, denying public access to critical financial details. This opacity prevents accountability and obscures the extent of the alleged misuse of public resources by former commissioners. This situation highlights a critical failure in open data principles, where transparency is suppressed rather than enforced. It underscores the urgent need for autonomous bodies to uphold public access to information, ensuring that governance mechanisms do not hinder anti-corruption efforts.
