INEGI recibe reconocimiento de organismo mundial | Periodico Hidrocalido Digital

The INEGI has been recognized as a partner of the UN Global Geospatial Information Centre, acknowledging its vital contribution to the global geodetic infrastructure. This partnership highlights the importance of precise spatial data for monitoring climate change, sea levels, and natural hazards, which are crucial for global environmental stability and human safety. This collaboration ensures better access to sustainable geodetic reference frames, supporting scientific research and socioeconomic development. By improving spatial technologies, the INEGI aids sectors such as agriculture and transport, demonstrating how accurate geographic information drives economic efficiency and disaster management capabilities worldwide. This association is highly relevant to open data, as it emphasizes the necessity of standardized, accessible spatial information for achieving sustainable development goals. It reinforces the value of shared, high-quality geospatial datasets in fostering transparency, enabling better policy-making, and promoting inclusive social progress through reliable, open scientific resources.
