San Juan is intensifying its efforts to re-enroll ten thousand electricity users who missed the deadline for the RASE registry, in order to prevent them from losing their subsidized rates. By cross-referencing georeferenced data with local authorities, officials aim to correct inclusion errors, ensuring that subsidies reach only those who truly qualify while avoiding the exclusion of households in need. This initiative highlights the critical role of precise, geolocated datasets in public administration. Accurate digital registries enable targeted outreach and efficient verification, demonstrating how open and accessible government data can bridge the gap between policy intent and the actual delivery of social benefits. This case underscores the importance of open data in enhancing transparency and equity in public services. By leveraging detailed user information, governments can minimize administrative errors, optimize resource allocation, and ensure that vital subsidies are distributed fairly and effectively to the population.
