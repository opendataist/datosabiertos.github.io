Censo de Salud Casa por Casa: ¿Amenaza a las pensiones de los adultos mayores?

President Claudia Sheinbaum clarified that the census for older adults is voluntary and does not aim to cut pensions, dispelling speculation. The "Health at Home" program seeks to personalize medical and social care by tailoring government support to the specific needs of beneficiaries. The initiative enables the government to better understand the health and socioeconomic conditions of the older population, thereby optimizing the distribution of resources and public services. This facilitates more efficient, comprehensive well-being-centered care for citizens. This is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how the collection of specific, structured data—even within government programs—can enhance the transparency and effectiveness of public policies, ensuring that the information gathered serves the direct benefit of citizens rather than restrictive purposes.
