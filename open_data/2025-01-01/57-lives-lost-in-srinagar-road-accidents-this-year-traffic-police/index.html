57 Lives Lost in Srinagar Road Accidents This Year: Traffic Police

High traffic penalties in Srinagar have failed to significantly reduce accidents, revealing a critical gap between enforcement and actual behavioral change. This highlights the limitations of data-driven punishment without accompanying public awareness and mindset shifts. The situation underscores the relevance of open data in public safety. Transparent release of accident and violation statistics allows citizens to scrutinize enforcement effectiveness and advocate for policy improvements rather than mere punishment. Ultimately, this case illustrates how accessible, open traffic data can drive accountability. It empowers communities to demand better road infrastructure and educational programs, turning raw statistics into actionable tools for saving lives and improving urban mobility.
