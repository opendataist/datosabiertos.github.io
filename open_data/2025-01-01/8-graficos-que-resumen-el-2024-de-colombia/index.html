8 gráficos que resumen el 2024 de Colombia

8 gráficos que resumen el 2024 de Colombia

The article examines the year 2024 in Colombia through key data that reveal a profound social and political transformation. Presidential approval ratings show stability, yet they are marked by persistent public distrust, while major cities experience a slow but steady erosion of confidence in their mayors. Furthermore, Bogotá’s water crisis underscores the urgency of climate change and the difficulty of achieving long-term sustainability goals. In the areas of security and the economy, peace negotiations are losing public support and stagnating, reflecting a shift in citizens’ expectations toward military solutions. At the same time, the decline in migration flows and the stabilization of inflation indicate structural changes in border and macroeconomic dynamics, although these figures require precise contextualization to fully understand their real impact. This analysis is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency and access to verified statistical information are essential for objectively evaluating government performance and shaping public policy. By contrasting perceptions with concrete metrics, it facilitates accountability and enables civil society to better understand the implications of state decisions, thereby promoting a more informed, evidence-based democracy.
