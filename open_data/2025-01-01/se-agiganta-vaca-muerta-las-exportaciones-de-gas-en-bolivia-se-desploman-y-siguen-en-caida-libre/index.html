Se agiganta Vaca Muerta: las exportaciones de gas en Bolivia se desploman y siguen en caída libre

Se agiganta Vaca Muerta: las exportaciones de gas en Bolivia se desploman y siguen en caída libre

Bolivia’s natural gas exports have collapsed over the past decade, shifting from a dominant export driver to a marginal contributor. This dramatic decline reflects a broader structural economic crisis triggered by falling production and the loss of key markets, particularly Argentina. Consequently, the state faces mounting fiscal pressures due to rising costs associated with subsidizing energy, highlighting a severe vulnerability in national resource management. This narrative is critical for open data advocates, as it demonstrates how public statistical agencies, such as the National Institute of Statistics (INE), play a vital role in exposing economic realities. Transparent access to disaggregated trade and production data enables citizens and analysts to independently track these shifts, fostering accountability. Open data serves as the essential evidence base needed to challenge official narratives and understand the true impact of policy failures on macroeconomic stability. Ultimately, the article underscores the urgency of data transparency in diagnosing systemic risks. By revealing the disparity between official statements and statistical trends, open data empowers society to demand evidence-based solutions. This case illustrates that without accessible, high-quality public datasets, meaningful dialogue regarding sustainable economic alternatives and resource governance remains impossible, reinforcing the necessity of robust open government initiatives.
