Brasil es el segundo proveedor mundial de carnes | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

The article concludes that Brazil’s record cattle production in 2023 created a significant surplus, driving down domestic prices and triggering a cycle of liquidation in which producers sold female animals. This structural shift is expected to reduce future supply, potentially marking a turning point in the market cycle as excess inventory shifts from exports to internal consumption, thereby altering regional supply dynamics. The relevance to open data lies in the critical role played by transparent, timely statistics from institutions such as IBGE in shaping market perceptions and policy responses. Accurate public data enables researchers and analysts to verify trends, such as the mismatch between production and demand, allowing stakeholders to anticipate price fluctuations and supply shortages. Without open access to reliable metrics, the ability to monitor the health of the agricultural sector and predict its impact on trade balances would be severely compromised. Furthermore, the article highlights how these data-driven insights reveal Argentina’s competitive disadvantage, which stems not from natural factors but from restrictive internal policies. By contrasting Brazil’s efficiency with Argentina’s regulatory burdens, the analysis underscores how open economic data facilitates comparative international assessments. This transparency is essential for identifying inefficiencies and advocating for necessary reforms, ensuring that market actors and policymakers understand the true determinants of regional competitiveness.
