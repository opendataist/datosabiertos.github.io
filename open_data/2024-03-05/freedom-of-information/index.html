Freedom of Information

The Society of Environmental Journalists established a task force to champion open environmental information, recognizing its critical role in journalism. By providing FOIA resources and advocacy tools, this initiative empowers reporters to access crucial data. This commitment directly supports open data by ensuring transparency and public access to vital environmental records, fostering accountability and informed discourse in environmental reporting.
