El INE Cochabamba envió el total de las cajas censales a La Paz

The National Institute of Statistics has consolidated all census materials from across the country, marking the transition from field collection to data processing. By centralizing these records, the institution ensures that the subsequent statistical extraction will be comprehensive and accurate, laying the groundwork for reliable demographic analysis. Initial population results will be released in late summer, followed by granular local data and thematic variables extending into 2025. A mid-year quality check will verify the integrity of the census, demonstrating how rigorous methodological controls maintain high standards in public data generation. This process highlights the critical importance of transparent, timely, and verifiable public statistics. It underscores the role of open data in fostering accountability and enabling informed societal decision-making through accessible, high-quality government information.
