La Nación / Reportan 28 feminicidios y 51 huérfanos hasta setiembre

The article highlights severe gender-based violence in Paraguay, revealing recent cases of femicide and attempted femicide through official data. This underscores the urgent need for transparent and accessible information systems to effectively monitor and combat such crimes. Publicly available datasets enable researchers and activists to analyze victim demographics and the relationships between victims and perpetrators. Such open data facilitates policy improvements by exposing systemic failures in the protection mechanisms for women and indigenous communities. Ultimately, this report demonstrates how open data serves as a critical tool for accountability. By making crime statistics visible, society can better understand the scope of violence and advocate for stronger legal frameworks and support structures for survivors.
