UK Seeks Proposals: Open Data Green Fund

UK Seeks Proposals: Open Data Green Fund

The British Embassy in Panama invites local organizations to apply for funding to enhance inclusive governance through open data. This initiative empowers entrepreneurs and communities to develop sustainable business models linked to biodiversity and ecosystem services. It focuses on building foundational capacities to strategically utilize public data for regenerative growth. Successful projects must provide training on open data usage and produce policy papers identifying critical data gaps. These deliverables aim to inform public policy on green growth and innovation. The program emphasizes inclusive participation, ensuring marginalized groups like Indigenous peoples and women are integrated into the innovation ecosystem. This call is relevant to open data as it operationalizes the concept by linking data access to tangible socio-economic outcomes. It demonstrates how structured data utilization can drive policy improvements and support sustainable entrepreneurship. By funding capacity-building and gap analysis, the project highlights the transformative potential of open data in addressing environmental challenges and fostering equitable economic development in Panama.
