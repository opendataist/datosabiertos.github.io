Inseguridad en México aumenta 6.79% en 2024, pero impacto económico baja

Inseguridad en México aumenta 6.79% en 2024, pero impacto económico baja

The Envipe survey reveals a rising trend in crime victimization across Mexico, with fraud and public robbery being the most common offenses. This data highlights a significant disparity in perceived security between genders, indicating deep-seated societal insecurity. Crucially, the article emphasizes the overwhelming rate of unreported crimes, exposing a massive gap between actual victimization and official statistics. This “black figure” demonstrates that standard reporting mechanisms fail to capture the true extent of criminal activity, leaving most cases unresolved. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible victimization metrics. By making such comprehensive data available, citizens and organizations can better analyze safety trends and advocate for effective, evidence-based public security policies.
