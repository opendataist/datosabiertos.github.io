En la Delegación Morelos Abren Sede Para Registro del Padrón Municipal de Mascotas

En la Delegación Morelos Abren Sede Para Registro del Padrón Municipal de Mascotas

Aguascalientes registers pets to coordinate sterilization and vaccination efforts, while issuing QR-coded identification tags. These tags streamline owner contact in cases of lost or surrendered pets, enhancing welfare management through efficient data linkage. This initiative exemplifies open data by using digital identifiers, such as QR codes, to connect physical assets with public records. It improves transparency and accessibility of services, demonstrating how localized databases can directly benefit citizen safety and animal health.
