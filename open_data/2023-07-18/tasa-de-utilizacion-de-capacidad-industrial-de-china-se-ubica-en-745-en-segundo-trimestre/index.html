Tasa de utilización de capacidad industrial de China se ubica en 74,5% en segundo trimestre

China’s industrial capacity utilization rose slightly in the second quarter of 2023, signaling a modest recovery in the manufacturing and mining sectors. This trend indicates improving economic momentum, as higher utilization rates suggest that factories are operating closer to their full production potential. Such data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, high-frequency insights into the national economy’s health. By making these metrics accessible, researchers and policymakers can better analyze industrial trends and make informed decisions based on real-time production realities. The availability of detailed, sector-specific statistics enables granular analysis of economic resilience. Consequently, open access to this information fosters greater accountability and facilitates a deeper understanding of how specific industries contribute to broader economic stability and growth trajectories.
