Open Data
ES EN

Global debt reaches record levels, highlighting systemic financial vulnerabilities. This context underscores the urgent need for transparent, open data to monitor economic stability and inform policy decisions. Market volatility driven by inflation and geopolitical tensions reveals how opaque financial metrics obscure true risks. Open data initiatives can mitigate these uncertainties by providing accessible, real-time insights into market drivers and corporate performance. Regulatory shifts in AI and energy further demonstrate the value of public transparency. By leveraging open data, stakeholders can better understand regulatory impacts, ensuring accountability and fostering informed discourse in an increasingly complex global economy.
