UPDATE – Incorta announces a joint solution with Google Cloud that unravels the complexity of Oracle ERP data for BigQuery customers | IT Business Net

Incorta and Google Cloud have partnered to simplify data access from complex business applications, addressing the critical need for transparent, integrated data pipelines in open data ecosystems. This collaboration leverages Google’s Cortex Framework and Incorta’s flexible architecture to lower the barriers to entry for advanced analytics, promoting the use of standardized, accessible data structures. The solution accelerates the delivery of insights by enabling seamless machine learning and generative AI integration. By reducing complexity and risk, it encourages organizations to adopt open principles that prioritize data availability and interoperability across diverse enterprise systems, fostering a more inclusive analytical environment. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how robust, open-architecture platforms can effectively bridge proprietary enterprise sources with modern cloud analytics. It highlights the importance of removing data silos and ensuring that high-value information is not locked away, but rather democratized for broader innovation and operational efficiency across supply chain, finance, and marketing domains.
