Convoca Coordinación de Transparencia a participar en la encuesta para renovar su Portal - Juárez Hoy

The Municipal Transparency Coordination in Juárez is redesigning its website based on direct citizen feedback to enhance accessibility and the quality of information. This initiative addresses previous issues with low visibility and poor user experience, ensuring that public obligations are more effectively communicated. The government prioritizes citizen input by conducting a comprehensive diagnostic survey, allowing residents to identify navigation difficulties and suggest relevant content improvements. This participatory approach ensures that the redesign reflects actual user needs rather than just administrative preferences. This case highlights the importance of user-centric design in open data ecosystems. By actively engaging citizens to evaluate and improve information portals, authorities can significantly increase transparency. It demonstrates that accessible, intuitive platforms are essential for meaningful civic engagement with public data.
