Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has enhanced the Ibrahim Index of African Governance by updating its interactive Data Portal. This improvement allows diverse users, including researchers and citizens, to deeply explore comprehensive governance metrics across all African nations. The platform simplifies accessing complex data through intuitive country profiles and comparative measure pages, facilitating better understanding of political, social, and economic performance trends. This tool serves as a critical resource for open data advocates by democratizing access to high-quality, standardized governance indicators. By presenting scores, ranks, and historical trends in an accessible format, it empowers civil society organizations to hold governments accountable. The ability to drill down from continental averages to specific sub-indicators supports rigorous analysis and evidence-based advocacy for improved public service delivery. Ultimately, this update reinforces the value of transparent, open datasets in monitoring and benchmarking public governance. It provides the necessary infrastructure for stakeholders to track progress and identify areas needing intervention across the continent. The continuous release of such detailed, comparable data fosters a culture of accountability and informed decision-making, highlighting how open data initiatives can drive meaningful democratic engagement and policy improvement in Africa.
