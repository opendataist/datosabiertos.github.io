NBS: China's economy improving, but foundation of domestic economic recovery not solid

China’s economy shows improvement despite global complexities, with confidence in meeting growth targets. Property markets are transitioning toward stability, reflecting a shift from rapid to sustained development. Youth unemployment may temporarily rise but is expected to decline, signaling gradual domestic recovery. This data supports open data efforts by providing transparent, real-time indicators of China's economic health and policy effectiveness, enabling better research and global comparisons.
