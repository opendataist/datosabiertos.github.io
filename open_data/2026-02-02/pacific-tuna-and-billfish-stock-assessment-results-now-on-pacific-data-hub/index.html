Pacific tuna and billfish stock assessment results now on Pacific Data Hub

The Pacific Data Hub now hosts comprehensive tuna and billfish stock assessments, significantly advancing open data access in the Pacific region. This integration allows diverse audiences, including policymakers and the public, to easily discover and utilize critical fisheries information alongside environmental and economic datasets. By surfacing these outputs through an interactive explorer, the initiative democratizes access to complex scientific models that were previously restricted to technical users. Users can now explore trends visually or download data in standard formats, facilitating broader analysis and informed decision-making across various sectors. This development is relevant to open data because it transforms specialized scientific outputs into publicly accessible resources. It demonstrates how centralized hubs can enhance data discoverability and interoperability, bridging the gap between technical research and practical application for sustainable fisheries management.
