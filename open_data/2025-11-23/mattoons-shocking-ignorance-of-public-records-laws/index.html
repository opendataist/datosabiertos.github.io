Mattoon’s Shocking Ignorance Of Public Records Laws –

Mattoon’s Shocking Ignorance Of Public Records Laws –

The City of Mattoon wrongly denies police job applications, falsely claiming they are exempt personnel records. This ignores established legal precedents confirming such applications are public under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act. This incident highlights significant compliance failures in local governance, where officials demonstrate a persistent ignorance of transparency laws. Such actions undermine public trust and create legal risks for municipalities regarding mandatory data disclosure. For open data, this serves as a critical reminder that organizational silos do not negate public access rights. Ensuring records are truly accessible requires strict adherence to statutory definitions, preventing agencies from arbitrarily withholding information by mislabeling file categories.
