B-FY convierte tu teléfono móvil en tu DNI con la primera solución española de identificación biométrica segura – Noticias Empresariales

The article introduces B-FY, a secure digital identification system that transforms smartphones into universal access keys for both physical and online services. By leveraging biometric data stored exclusively on the user’s device, it eliminates the need for passwords and ensures that sensitive information never leaves the phone. This "Identity-First" approach addresses the fundamental security flaw in traditional systems, which often authenticate data carriers rather than the individuals themselves, thereby significantly reducing the risk of identity theft and unauthorized access. The development of such robust security measures is critical given the projected exponential growth of cybercrime costs, which threaten global economic stability and infrastructure. Current standards often fail to protect against sophisticated attacks like SIM swapping, leaving users and organizations vulnerable. By prioritizing person identification over data verification, this technology aims to prevent cyberattacks at their source, offering a resilient defense against rising digital threats and potential geopolitical technological conflicts. This innovation is highly relevant to open data and privacy advocacy because it demonstrates a viable model for balancing convenience with strict user sovereignty. It proves that high-security access can be achieved without centralizing biometric data in vulnerable corporate databases, thus aligning with principles of data minimization and individual control. This shift empowers users to manage their digital identity securely, setting a precedent for how open ecosystems can integrate secure authentication without compromising personal privacy or creating single points of failure.
