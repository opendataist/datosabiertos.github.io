La economía del Reino Unido esquiva la recesión pero se estanca en el último trimestre de 2022 | Diario Financiero

The UK economy’s stagnation in late 2022 narrowly avoided a technical recession, highlighting the fragility of the recovery amid persistent inflation and external shocks. Although the economy avoided two consecutive quarters of contraction, the flat growth indicates that underlying economic resilience remains weak, particularly as production declines contradict initial optimistic forecasts. This situation underscores significant structural challenges, including supply chain disruptions and labor market issues that hindered performance despite temporary boosts from holiday spending. The contrast with stronger growth in the US and the eurozone emphasizes the UK’s unique struggle to regain pre-pandemic output levels, marking it as the only G7 nation still lagging in economic recovery. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the critical role of transparent, high-frequency statistical releases in monitoring real-time economic health. Access to such granular public datasets allows analysts to detect emerging trends, such as the December slowdown, ensuring accountability and enabling informed policy responses to mitigate broader financial instability.
