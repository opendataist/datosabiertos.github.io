Número de muertos por terremoto en Turquía y Sira sube a 21.700

This article underscores the devastating human toll of the Turkey-Syria earthquake, yet its relevance to open data lies in the reliance on official statistics for crisis response. Transparent, accessible datasets on casualty counts and humanitarian sanctions enable global coordination and accountability during disasters. Sharing accurate, real-time information regarding rescue efforts and policy shifts, such as temporary sanction suspensions, is crucial for effective international aid. Open data facilitates better resource allocation and supports the numerous search teams operating beyond standard survival windows. Ultimately, public access to verified disaster metrics empowers communities and NGOs to respond swiftly. By prioritizing data transparency, stakeholders can improve emergency management strategies, ensuring that vital assistance reaches affected regions efficiently during such large-scale tragedies.
