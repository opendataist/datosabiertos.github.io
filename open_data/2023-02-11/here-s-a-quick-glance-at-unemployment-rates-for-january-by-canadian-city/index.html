Here's a quick glance at unemployment rates for January, by Canadian city

The national unemployment rate remained stable, yet local variations across major Canadian cities revealed significant fluctuations. Statistics Canada cautioned that these city-level figures may be unreliable due to their reliance on small statistical samples, highlighting a critical limitation in granular labor market data accuracy. This inconsistency underscores the challenges of using small-sample surveys for precise local analysis. For open data initiatives, it demonstrates why transparency about sample sizes and margins of error is essential. Without this context, users might draw misleading conclusions about regional economic health based on potentially noisy datasets. Understanding these limitations is crucial for policymakers and developers relying on open government data. It emphasizes the need for robust data quality standards and clear documentation. By acknowledging sample size constraints, the open data community can ensure more accurate interpretations and better decision-making processes regarding employment trends.
