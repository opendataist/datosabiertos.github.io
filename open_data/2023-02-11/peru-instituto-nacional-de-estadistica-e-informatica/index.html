PERÚ Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática

The INEI releases its microdata system, providing accessible and secure databases from recent surveys. This initiative supports open data by enabling easy retrieval and analysis of statistical information in widely used formats. It empowers users to conduct independent research while respecting statistical confidentiality.
