Ofrecen respaldo al INAI ante omisión del Senado

Ofrecen respaldo al INAI ante omisión del Senado

UNESCO reaffirms its support for Mexico’s National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), emphasizing its vital role in safeguarding democracy and human rights amid operational challenges. This backing underscores the critical need for institutional stability to maintain effective oversight of transparency and data protection. The forum highlighted that cybersecurity is essential for preserving the integrity of archives, which directly enables citizens to exercise their right to information. Secure records serve as the foundational basis for accountable governance and democratic participation. This issue is highly relevant to open data, as it connects robust archival management with digital security. Ensuring the availability and confidentiality of public documents guarantees that open data remains reliable, accessible, and trustworthy for public scrutiny and civic engagement.
