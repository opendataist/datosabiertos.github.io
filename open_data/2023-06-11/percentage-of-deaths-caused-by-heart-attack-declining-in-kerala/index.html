Percentage of deaths caused by heart attack declining in Kerala

Heart attacks remain the leading cause of death in Kerala, though recent data indicates a positive decline in the percentage of fatalities attributed to cardiac issues. This shift suggests potential improvements in healthcare response or public awareness, even as cardiovascular diseases continue to disproportionately affect younger adult males due to lifestyle factors like hypertension and sedentary habits. Concurrently, the state is experiencing a demographic transition marked by an increasing crude death rate and a declining crude birth rate. This dual trend highlights evolving public health dynamics, where improved survival rates for acute conditions may coexist with broader societal changes in population structure and aging. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, longitudinal government statistics enable researchers and policymakers to track disease burden trends over time. By making such vital statistics accessible, authorities can identify emerging health risks, evaluate the efficacy of medical interventions, and tailor public health strategies to address specific demographic vulnerabilities effectively.
