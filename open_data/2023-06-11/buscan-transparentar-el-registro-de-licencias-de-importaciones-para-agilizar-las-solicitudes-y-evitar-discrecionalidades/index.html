Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The Civic Coalition proposes creating an Open Data Portal for Argentine imports, aiming to eliminate the discretion and corruption risks inherent in the current licensing system. This project seeks to ensure that all public information—including applications, amounts, and processing statuses—is accessible, complete, and up to date. The initiative aims to transform an environment characterized by exchange rate restrictions and bureaucratic arbitrariness into a framework of radical transparency, aligned with international good regulatory practices. The project requires that data be published in open formats through digital channels, ensuring technological accessibility for any citizen. It also establishes strict limits on bureaucracy: resolution deadlines must not exceed thirty days, and applications must be processed in chronological order. A key provision is the "positive administrative silence" rule, whereby the absence of a response within the stipulated timeframe is automatically considered an approval of the license, thereby protecting importers from state inaction. This proposal is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates how proactive transparency can serve as a concrete tool against corruption. By mandating the systematic publication of administrative data in standardized and accessible formats, it democratizes information that was previously hidden behind opaque processes. This demonstrates that open data are not merely a technical concept, but an essential mechanism for holding the state accountable, reducing bureaucratic discretion, and fostering economic predictability for citizens and businesses.
