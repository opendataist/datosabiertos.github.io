Así respaldó Ucrania todos sus datos públicos en la nube: 15 mil terabytes a salvo de la invasión rusa

Así respaldó Ucrania todos sus datos públicos en la nube: 15 mil terabytes a salvo de la invasión rusa

Ukraine’s response to the Russian invasion underscores the crucial importance of digital resilience and cybersecurity as pillars of national sovereignty. Faced with a direct cyber threat, the government prioritized backing up and migrating its critical assets to external infrastructure, demonstrating that data protection is as vital as physical defense. This strategy enabled essential services such as healthcare, education, and tax administration to remain operational, ensuring the continuity of the state amid the conflict. The success of this operation rests on the agility and control offered by cloud computing, overcoming the limitations of local infrastructure. By leveraging third-party services with high standards of encryption and traceability, Ukraine managed to migrate petabytes of data in record time using small, efficient teams. This case illustrates how cloud computing not only provides storage capacity but, more importantly, a robust security architecture that enables centralized control and data protection even in disconnected or physically dangerous environments. This episode is fundamental to the open data ecosystem, as it sets a precedent for how cloud technology can safeguard transparency and access to public information without compromising security. The ability to guarantee the availability and integrity of civil, financial, and administrative records ensures that citizens can continue to access their rights and digital services. It also validates the use of scalable technological tools to protect democratic governance, reinforcing trust in digitized public management during global crises.
