Open Data
ES EN

Privacy policies increasingly mandate explicit user consent for data tracking, shifting control to individuals. This transparency empowers users to manage their digital footprint by accepting or rejecting cookies and location data. Such practices are vital for open data, ensuring ethical collection and respecting user autonomy in the digital ecosystem.
