En Aguascalientes se trabaja para garantizar el respeto al Estado de Derecho: TJ | HidrocalidoDigital.com

Aguascalientes ranks highly in national indices for Open Government and the Rule of Law, reflecting strong efforts in transparency and accountability. The state prioritizes civic participation and legal compliance, demonstrating how public institutions can effectively uphold democratic principles and foster social trust through consistent governance practices. These results highlight the critical role of collaborative efforts among government branches and citizens in maintaining order and protecting fundamental rights. By excelling in judicial efficiency and anti-corruption measures, the region sets a benchmark for how structural integrity and openness contribute to sustainable development and economic stability. This article is relevant to open data, as it validates the impact of transparent data practices and accessible government information. It underscores that rigorous, publicly available metrics are essential for measuring performance, ensuring accountability, and driving continuous improvement in public sector integrity and citizen engagement.
