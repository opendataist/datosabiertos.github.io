Oaxaca inicia el 2023 con 3,666 delitos reportados en la entidad

The article highlights a significant surge in property crimes, particularly vehicle and pedestrian robberies, in Oaxaca’s capital during early 2023. This data reveals a stark contrast between reported incidents and the public’s pervasive sense of insecurity, with the majority of residents perceiving their municipality as unsafe. Consequently, citizens have adopted restrictive behavioral changes, avoiding social visits, nighttime outings, and carrying valuables to mitigate personal risk. This disconnect underscores the critical importance of accessible open data in security governance. When official statistics clearly expose high crime rates alongside low awareness of government prevention efforts, it emphasizes the need for transparent, real-time information sharing. Open data initiatives can bridge this gap, allowing communities to verify safety conditions and hold authorities accountable for implementing effective interventions. Ultimately, the relevance to open data lies in its capacity to transform raw police reports into actionable public intelligence. By democratizing access to such granular security metrics, society can better understand local trends, fostering informed dialogue and collaborative strategies to enhance urban safety and restore public trust in institutional responses to delinquency.
