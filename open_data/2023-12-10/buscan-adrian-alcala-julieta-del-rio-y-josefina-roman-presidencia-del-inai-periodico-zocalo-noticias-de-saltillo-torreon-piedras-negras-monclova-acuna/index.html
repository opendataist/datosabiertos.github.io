Buscan Adrián Alcalá, Julieta del Río y Josefina Román presidencia del INAI | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The National Institute for Transparency is electing a new president to lead its crucial mandate over public data and privacy. This leadership transition determines how transparency standards and open information policies will be shaped in the coming years. The election process involves internal voting among commissioners, ensuring institutional continuity and accountability. The outcome influences the strategic direction of access to information protocols, highlighting the importance of stable governance in open data oversight. This event is relevant to open data because the appointed president sets the agenda for transparency enforcement. Their decisions directly impact public access to government information, affecting citizen rights and the integrity of open data ecosystems across Mexico.
