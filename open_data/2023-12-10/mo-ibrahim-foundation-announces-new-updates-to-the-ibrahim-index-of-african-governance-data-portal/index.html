Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has enhanced its Ibrahim Index Data Portal, offering an interactive platform for exploring comprehensive governance metrics across 54 African nations. This update provides deeper insight into public service delivery, political stability, and economic opportunity, allowing users to visualize long-term trends and comparative performances against continental averages. By enabling detailed exploration of specific indicators and sub-categories, the portal facilitates a granular understanding of governance successes and failures. This interactive approach empowers diverse stakeholders, from data journalists to civil society organizations, to scrutinize how effectively governments provide essential public goods and services to their citizens over time. This resource is vital to open data initiatives as it transforms complex, static statistics into accessible, actionable intelligence. By democratizing access to rigorous governance benchmarks, the portal supports evidence-based advocacy and policy reform, ensuring that transparency and accountability in African governance are driven by openly available, high-quality data.
