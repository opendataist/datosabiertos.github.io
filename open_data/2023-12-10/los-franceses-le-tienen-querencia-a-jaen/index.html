Los franceses le tienen querencia a Jaén

The National Statistics Institute uses mobile device location data to accurately map tourist flows in Spain. This methodological approach enables the identification of national and international preference trends, revealing specific geographic patterns that vary significantly across regions. In Andalusia, while the eastern part predominantly attracts French tourists, the western part shows a marked inclination toward Portuguese and Moroccan travelers, demonstrating the capacity of mobile technology to capture complex demographic nuances in real time. The relevance of this article to open data lies in how official statistics integrate alternative sources to enhance the granularity of public information. By combining mobile data with traditional records, the National Statistics Institute provides a more dynamic and detailed view of tourist behavior than conventional methods. This facilitates access for researchers and policymakers to more accurate indicators of human mobility, which are essential for developing evidence-based public policies rather than relying solely on historical assumptions. These open data sets are crucial for understanding the sector’s recovery after the pandemic and for managing seasonality. Transparency in these indicators allows autonomous communities to adjust marketing and accommodation strategies according to visitors’ actual preferences. Therefore, the publication of these data sets not only fulfills administrative transparency requirements but also empowers civil society and the private sector to make informed decisions that drive sustainable regional economic development.
