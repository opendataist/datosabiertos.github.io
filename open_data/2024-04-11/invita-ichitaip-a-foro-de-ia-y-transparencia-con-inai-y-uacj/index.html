Invita Ichitaip a foro de IA y transparencia con INAI y UACJ

The event highlights the critical intersection of transparency, data protection, and artificial intelligence, emphasizing the need for robust open data frameworks. It underscores the importance of institutional collaboration in safeguarding personal information while promoting government accountability. Relevant to open data, this dialogue aims to enhance public understanding of how AI impacts data rights and transparency. By connecting state and national authorities, it seeks to establish best practices for managing public information ethically and effectively, ensuring citizens' rights are respected in the digital age.
