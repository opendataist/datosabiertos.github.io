Government has no evidence to support ‘mega councils’, DCN says | LocalGov

Government has no evidence to support ‘mega councils’, DCN says | LocalGov

Local government leaders criticize the government’s mandate for large unitary councils as lacking evidence, noting reliance on outdated data and absent cost assessments. This lack of transparency hinders effective planning for administrative structures that serve specific regional needs. The debate highlights critical open_data issues, as valid policy decisions require accessible, current, and comprehensive datasets. Without reliable public information, stakeholders cannot properly evaluate the impact of proposed structural changes or hold officials accountable. Ultimately, the argument emphasizes that open, evidence-based data is essential for democratic decision-making. Transparent information allows communities to shape governance models that truly reflect local identities, ensuring that public resources are allocated efficiently and appropriately.
