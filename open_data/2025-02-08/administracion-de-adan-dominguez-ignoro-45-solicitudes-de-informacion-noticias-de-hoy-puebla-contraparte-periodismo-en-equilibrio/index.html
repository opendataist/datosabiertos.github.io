Administración de Adán Domínguez ignoró 45 solicitudes de información - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Administración de Adán Domínguez ignoró 45 solicitudes de información - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The former Puebla administration faced criticism for granting irregular end-of-term bonuses to former officials, which resulted in a backlog of unresolved transparency requests. This delay hindered the current administration’s ability to promptly respond to citizen inquiries regarding access to information. The current team is now actively processing these pending requests while also handling new ones on a daily basis. Public interest is currently focused on the curricular data of the new municipal leadership, which is made available through official transparency channels to ensure accountability. This case underscores the critical role of open data in maintaining governmental accountability. It demonstrates how transparency mechanisms enable citizens to monitor public spending and official conduct, reinforcing the necessity of accessible information for democratic oversight and institutional trust.
