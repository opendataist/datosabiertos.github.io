Uno de cada cuatro residentes en Baleares ha nacido en el extranjero

The article highlights that nearly a quarter of the residents of the Balearic Islands are foreign-born, marking the highest percentage in Spain. This significant demographic shift underscores the region's intense reliance on international migration, distinguishing it sharply from areas with lower foreign populations. Focusing on open data, this INE release exemplifies the critical role of accessible, granular statistical information. By breaking down census data by autonomous community and even individual islands, authorities enable precise demographic analysis and resource planning. Ultimately, this dataset reveals how migration drives regional population growth. Understanding these patterns is essential for policymakers to address social integration, housing, and infrastructure needs in high-immigration areas, demonstrating the practical value of transparent public statistics.
