Tinubu To Unveil Birth, Death Registration Portal

Nigeria is transitioning to an advanced Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics system, marking a decisive shift from paper-based records to a comprehensive digital framework. This modernization aims to streamline the capture and analysis of vital events, ensuring timely access to critical demographic data that supports evidence-based decision-making. By integrating diverse civil registration types into a unified platform, the government enhances transparency and operational efficiency across the nation. The initiative leverages public-private partnerships to achieve sustainable, cost-effective data storage and distribution. By digitizing processes such as birth, marriage, and death registrations, the system provides secure, accessible verification for both public and private sectors. This digital transformation aligns with international best practices and supports long-term goals for improving national statistical capacities, fostering greater trust in government services and data integrity. This development is highly relevant to open_data as it establishes foundational datasets essential for research, policy formulation, and civic engagement. The introduction of a Geospatial Data Repository further enriches the open data landscape, offering multidimensional geographic insights to diverse users. By making these vital statistics more accessible and standardized, Nigeria advances its capacity to contribute to and benefit from the global open data ecosystem, promoting innovation and accountability through shared information resources.
