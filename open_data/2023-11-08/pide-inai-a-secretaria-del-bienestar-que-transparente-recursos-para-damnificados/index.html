Pide INAI a Secretaria del Bienestar que transparente recursos para damnificados

Pide INAI a Secretaria del Bienestar que transparente recursos para damnificados

The National Institute for Transparency ordered the Ministry of Welfare to disclose detailed data on direct transfers to municipalities affected by natural disasters. This mandate addresses the ministry’s initial refusal to provide specific figures on the amounts and recipients, emphasizing that the management of public resources must be fully transparent to prevent opacity and maintain public trust. Despite numerous investigations into irregularities within the emergency assistance program, no officials have been sanctioned since its inception. The commission highlighted serious allegations of mismanagement and unfulfilled aid for victims of past hurricanes, underscoring the urgent need for accountability and rigorous oversight in how these funds are allocated and utilized during crises. This case is critical for open data because it demonstrates how institutional resistance to information requests can obscure potential corruption and gaps in aid. It reinforces the necessity of proactive transparency in government spending, showing that accessible data is essential for verifying service delivery and ensuring that public resources effectively reach the intended beneficiaries without administrative obstruction.
