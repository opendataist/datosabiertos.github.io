More than 400 inactive lawyers on Criminal Legal Aid Register

The article reveals that the Scottish Criminal Legal Aid Register contains many inactive or retired lawyers, creating a misleading impression of available capacity. This discrepancy highlights a critical open data failure, where public registers lack real-time accuracy and validation, undermining trust in official statistics. By exposing this gap, the case demonstrates why clean, verified, and current open data is essential for transparent public service management and effective resource allocation in legal systems.
