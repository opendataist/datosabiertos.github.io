Distribuye INEGI material para el censo de gobiernos municipales en Quintana Roo - Noticaribe

The INEGI is conducting the National Census of Municipal Governments to evaluate the performance of local administrations. This biennial exercise aims to improve public management and enhance citizen well-being by collecting comprehensive data on municipal services. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it systematically generates standardized information on governance aspects. By gathering detailed metrics across various administrative modules, the project creates a robust dataset that promotes transparency and accountability. Ultimately, this census provides essential insights for policymakers and researchers. The resulting open datasets enable comparative analysis and evidence-based decision-making, fostering better urban planning and public policy outcomes across Mexico.
