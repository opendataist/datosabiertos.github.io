Cefim instala Comité Gobierno Abierto para reforzar rendición de cuentas

The article highlights the establishment of a collaborative governance model in San Luis Potosí, emphasizing transparency and public participation in the management of resources and services. By creating an advisory council, authorities aim to strengthen citizen trust and ensure accountable political practices through open dialogue. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it institutionalizes transparency mechanisms that require the release of accurate and accessible information. Such frameworks enable citizens to effectively monitor government actions, fostering an ecosystem where data drives accountability and empowers communities to engage meaningfully with state institutions.
