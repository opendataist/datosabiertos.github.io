Aumentó desempleo en el primer trimestre del año: Inegi

The INEGI data reveals a troubling shift in Mexico's labor market, where the non-economically active population grew faster than the active workforce. This imbalance signals rising unemployment pressure, despite a larger absolute number of job seekers. This trend highlights a concerning drop in labor participation rates, indicating that economic growth is not effectively engaging the available population. The divergence between active and inactive demographics suggests structural issues in employment accessibility. Such granular demographic data is vital for open data initiatives. It allows analysts to critically evaluate economic health beyond surface-level metrics, fostering transparent public discourse on labor policies and social welfare.
