Nueva ley de datos en Colombia: ¿qué significa y cómo nos beneficia?

Colombia is advancing toward its consolidation as a leader in digital transformation through a new bill that seeks to structure information management as the fundamental basis for technological innovation. This regulatory framework establishes methodologies for government entities to generate relevant data, ensuring its quality, interoperability, and transparency, with the explicit aim of enhancing decision-making in public policy and accelerating the development of artificial intelligence. A central pillar of the initiative is the rigorous protection of privacy, ensuring that the data used are anonymized and do not compromise citizens' identities. By prioritizing security and risk management, the law fosters a reliable ecosystem where the exchange and reuse of information among public and private actors becomes seamless. This not only strengthens social trust but also reduces the repetitive administrative burden on citizens by centralizing and optimizing access to information. This legislation is crucial for the open data movement, as it transforms public information into a strategic asset accessible for the creation of startups and digital applications. By standardizing data production under principles of openness and security, it eliminates the fragmentation that hinders innovation, enabling the private sector and institutions to collaborate effectively. Thus, it lays the foundation for an inclusive, evidence-based digital economy, aligning Colombia with international data governance standards.
