IA y Propiedad Intelectual: Desafíos y Oportunidades

IA y Propiedad Intelectual: Desafíos y Oportunidades

Artificial intelligence is redefining traditional concepts of intellectual property by enabling the autonomous generation of creative content and technological innovations. This advancement raises fundamental dilemmas regarding authorship and ownership of rights, as the absence of a clear human author complicates the attribution of exclusive benefits—whether to the algorithm developer, the owner of the training data, or the machine itself. The ability of AI to analyze large volumes of data accelerates the creation of patents and software, necessitating new strategies to protect these intangible assets. However, current legislation does not provide uniform or universal answers, generating legal uncertainty across different jurisdictions in the face of the rapid emergence of these new technologies and digital business models. This issue is crucial for the open data community, as transparency, data governance, and ethics in information handling are foundational pillars for both responsible AI and the open data movement. A robust legal framework is needed to balance the protection of innovation with proactive collaboration among governments, businesses, and citizens, ensuring that technological development is safe, ethical, and respectful of fundamental rights in an increasingly complex digital environment.
