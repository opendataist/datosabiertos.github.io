Yaw Sarpong elected Vice Chairperson of African Network of Information Commissions

Boateng’s election to the African Network of Information Centres Vice Chairmanship highlights Ghana’s strong commitment to transparency and information rights. This leadership role underscores the importance of effective information governance in strengthening democratic principles across the continent. His appointment signals a broader continental effort to enforce access to information laws and enhance government accountability. By fostering collaboration among member states, the network aims to promote openness and ensure public institutions remain answerable to citizens. This development is crucial for open data as it reinforces the legal and political frameworks necessary for transparency. It encourages stronger policies that facilitate public access to information, thereby supporting accountable governance and informed civic participation throughout Africa.
