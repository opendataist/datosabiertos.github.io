Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production reached substantial volumes, with China and India emerging as the dominant leaders. Their combined output significantly outweighs other nations, highlighting their central role in the international supply chain. This concentration of production power suggests that market dynamics and policy shifts in these two countries will have a disproportionately large impact on global food security and trade flows for this vital crop. The data reveals a widespread distribution of cultivation across nearly a hundred countries, indicating that potatoes are a staple food with deep cultural and economic roots in diverse regions. While yield efficiency varies, the sheer number of participating nations underscores the crop’s adaptability to different climates and agricultural systems. This broad base of producers helps stabilize global supply, reducing vulnerability to localized disruptions and supporting rural economies worldwide. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of transparent, standardized datasets like FAOSTAT. By providing accessible, comparable metrics for global agriculture, such databases empower researchers, policymakers, and journalists to analyze trends without relying on fragmented or proprietary sources. Open access to this agricultural intelligence fosters better-informed decisions regarding food sustainability, trade regulations, and development strategies, ensuring that critical resource data remains a public good rather than a restricted asset.
