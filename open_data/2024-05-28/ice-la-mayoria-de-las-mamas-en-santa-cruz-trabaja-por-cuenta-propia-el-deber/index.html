ICE: la mayoría de las mamás, en Santa Cruz, trabaja por cuenta propia | El Deber

ICE: la mayoría de las mamás, en Santa Cruz, trabaja por cuenta propia | El Deber

This study highlights a significant shift in labor dynamics, revealing that a majority of mothers in Santa Cruz work autonomously. Despite their substantial economic contributions through self-employment, traditional gender roles persist, as men remain the declared heads of most households. This discrepancy underscores a critical challenge in open data regarding gender metrics: official statistics often fail to accurately capture female economic leadership. Consequently, policy frameworks relying on such data may overlook the true financial authority women hold within family structures. Understanding these gaps is vital for open data initiatives. Improving how household leadership and self-employment are recorded ensures more equitable resource distribution and better reflects the evolving reality of women’s roles in the formal and informal economies.
