AIFA debe entregar contratos de renta: INAI | Revista Punto de Vista

AIFA debe entregar contratos de renta: INAI | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI ordered AIFA airport to release full contract details, including prices and legal representatives, rejecting unjustified claims of tax secrecy. This decision highlights the growing trend of authorities incorrectly classifying public information as confidential, undermining transparency norms. The ruling emphasizes that administrative convenience cannot override legal obligations to disclose data. Authorities must proactively search all relevant departments rather than selectively withholding information based on incorrect interpretations of confidentiality laws. This case is critical for open data because it reinforces the principle that public contracts must be fully accessible. It establishes a precedent against arbitrary information restrictions, ensuring that citizens can hold public institutions accountable through complete, unredacted transparency.
