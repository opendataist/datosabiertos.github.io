Inicia Semana Internacional de Gobierno Abierto

Inicia Semana Internacional de Gobierno Abierto

Chihuahua has formally adopted an Open Data Policy and entered into a partnership with Codeando México, reinforcing its commitment to transparent governance. This step aligns with its participation in the Open Government Partnership, an international initiative aimed at promoting inclusive transparency and civic engagement. The initiative underscores the critical link between open data and democratic accountability. By making municipal information accessible, the city fosters public trust and encourages active citizen participation in local decision-making processes. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates concrete municipal actions that operationalize data openness. It highlights how local governments can leverage open data frameworks to enhance public service delivery and strengthen the relationship between authorities and the community through innovative, transparent practices.
