Rusia aumentó 1,5 veces en agosto el suministro de metales a Brasil - Noticias Prensa Latina

Russian metal exports to Brazil surged significantly, elevating Russia to a top-six supplier. Spain also resumed purchases despite EU sanctions. This highlights a critical open-data insight: global trade patterns often defy political restrictions, necessitating rigorous monitoring to understand how sanctions are bypassed through alternative supply chains.
