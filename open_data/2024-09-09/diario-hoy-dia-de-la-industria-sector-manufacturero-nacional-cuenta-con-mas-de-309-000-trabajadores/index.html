Diario HOY | Día de la Industria: sector manufacturero nacional cuenta con más de 309.000 trabajadores

The article concludes that genetic improvement is a fundamental driver for transforming Paraguay’s cattle industry, establishing it as a regional leader in high-quality livestock. By integrating superior genetic traits, the sector has significantly enhanced productivity, reproductive efficiency, and adaptation to local environmental conditions, thereby strengthening the country's competitive position in international markets. This advancement highlights the critical link between biological potential and market competitiveness. Animals with optimized genetics deliver better weight gains, higher fertility, and superior meat quality, meeting the rigorous standards of global demand. However, realizing these benefits requires more than just introducing better breeds; it necessitates a holistic approach that combines genetics with proper nutrition, health management, and precise reproductive planning to fully unlock economic potential. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity of transparent, accessible information to bridge the adoption gap between large and small producers. While advanced technologies like genetic evaluation tools exist, their full impact depends on widespread dissemination and the open sharing of best practices. Ensuring equitable access to this data is essential for standardizing production quality and sustaining long-term industry growth, proving that data-driven decisions are as vital as biological innovations in modern agriculture.
