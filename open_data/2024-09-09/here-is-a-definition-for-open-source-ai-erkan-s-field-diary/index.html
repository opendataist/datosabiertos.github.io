Here is a definition for "open-source AI" - Erkan's Field Diary

OSI’s new definition aims to help lawmakers regulate AI by establishing clear standards for openness. It ensures models can be inspected, modified, and shared without restrictions, promoting accountability and consumer protection. This framework is vital for open data, as it clarifies transparency requirements for training data and weights, fostering trust and interoperability in AI systems.
