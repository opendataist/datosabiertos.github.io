Organizaciones editoriales y de entretenimiento le ponen reglas claras al uso de la IA

Organizaciones editoriales y de entretenimiento le ponen reglas claras al uso de la IA

The article presents the Global Principles for Artificial Intelligence as a strategic response from key sectors to the rapid adoption of technology. The consensus aims to ensure that the development of these tools respects intellectual property and establishes ethical regulatory frameworks, promoting a balance where innovation does not supplant human values but rather enhances them through transparency and the protection of rights. The relevance to open data lies in the fundamental tension between using data to train AI models and preserving the integrity of information. By requiring adequate remuneration for the use of intellectual property and maintaining truthfulness in content creation, the principles underscore the critical need to manage data ethically. This implies that openness and availability of information must be accompanied by clear mechanisms to prevent misinformation and protect original creators. Finally, the text argues that the success of these measures depends on the legislative capacity to implement them effectively, as technology advances faster than regulations. The essential conclusion is that AI should serve as a supportive tool that amplifies human judgment rather than replacing it. For the open data ecosystem, this reinforces the idea that technology must be at the service of truth and ethics, ensuring that data accessibility does not compromise the quality or integrity of shared knowledge.
