Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

This article details 13 MEDIA, S.C.’s privacy notice, which serves as a critical example of how private organizations operationalize data protection laws. The document outlines the specific categories of personal and financial information collected, emphasizing that data processing is governed by strict legal principles such as consent, proportionality, and purpose limitation. It clarifies that information is primarily used for identity verification, service fulfillment, and legal compliance, rather than for arbitrary commercial exploitation. The notice further explains the mechanisms for sharing data with third parties, such as notaries or legal authorities, and establishes clear procedures for individuals to exercise their ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation, and opposition). By providing direct contact channels and specifying conditions under which data limitations can be requested, the organization demonstrates a commitment to transparency and user control over their personal information. This content is relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessary boundary between open information and protected personal privacy. While open data promotes transparency and public access to non-sensitive information, this document highlights the legal obligations organizations must meet to safeguard individual rights. It underscores that true openness must not compromise personal security, requiring clear governance structures to distinguish between public data and confidential personal details.
