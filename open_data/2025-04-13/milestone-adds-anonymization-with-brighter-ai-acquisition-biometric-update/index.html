Milestone adds anonymization with brighter AI acquisition | Biometric Update

Milestone adds anonymization with brighter AI acquisition | Biometric Update

Milestone Systems acquired brighter AI to integrate deep learning-based identity protection into its video management platforms. This enhances privacy compliance in public surveillance while expanding analytics capabilities beyond traditional security functions. By embedding automated anonymization directly into video infrastructure, the deal addresses critical open data ethics concerns. It enables safer public space monitoring where individual privacy is preserved, facilitating broader data sharing and analysis. This strategic move highlights the growing intersection of surveillance technology and data privacy. It demonstrates how vendors can support responsible open data practices by default, balancing security needs with the right to anonymity.
