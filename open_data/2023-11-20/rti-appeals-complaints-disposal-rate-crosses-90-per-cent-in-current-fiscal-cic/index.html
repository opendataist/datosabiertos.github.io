RTI appeals, complaints disposal rate crosses 90 per cent in current fiscal: CIC

The Chief Information Commission recently announced that the disposal rate for RTI appeals and complaints has surpassed ninety percent in the current fiscal year. This milestone highlights a consistent decline in pendency and a corresponding rise in successful case resolutions, marking a significant operational improvement for the commission. This progress is largely attributed to the adoption of advanced technologies, including the use of artificial intelligence for analyzing RTI patterns and verifying applicant credentials. Additionally, the implementation of hybrid hearing modes through video conferencing has streamlined processes, allowing the commission to efficiently manage its workload while reducing backlog numbers to record lows. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how administrative efficiency and technological integration can enhance the Right to Information framework. By faster resolving appeals, the government strengthens citizen access to information, fostering greater transparency and encouraging active public participation in democratic governance.
