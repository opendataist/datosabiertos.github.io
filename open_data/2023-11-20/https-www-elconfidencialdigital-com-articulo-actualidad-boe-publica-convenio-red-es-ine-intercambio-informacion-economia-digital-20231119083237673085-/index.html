Open Data
ES EN

Red.es and the National Statistics Institute (INE) have entered into a strategic agreement to exchange statistical information on the digital economy and society. This collaboration ensures the regular exchange of anonymized microdata from key household and enterprise surveys, enabling a deeper understanding of digital trends and technology adoption across Spain. A dedicated commission will oversee compliance and data protection, ensuring that individual privacy is strictly safeguarded throughout the data exchange process. By coordinating these efforts, both institutions can enhance their analytical capabilities without financial transactions, focusing solely on the scientific and social value of the shared data. This initiative is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how public institutions can securely share sensitive, detailed datasets for broader societal benefit. It highlights a practical model for interoperability and responsible data stewardship, preserving anonymity while maximizing the utility of statistical resources for research and policy-making.
