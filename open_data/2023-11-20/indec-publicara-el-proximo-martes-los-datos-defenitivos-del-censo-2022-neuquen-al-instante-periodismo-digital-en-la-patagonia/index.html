INDEC publicará el próximo martes los datos defenitivos del Censo 2022 | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

Argentina is set to publish the definitive results of the 2022 National Census on November 21, concluding a prolonged data processing period. The delay stems from the innovative hybrid methodology, which combined digital responses with field visits, requiring extensive integration of these two distinct data sources to ensure accuracy and completeness. This release provides critical insights into the country's demographic structure, highlighting a population exceeding 46 million people. The comprehensive dataset offers vital information for understanding regional distributions, particularly within the Buenos Aires province, while reflecting adaptations made due to pandemic-related protocols that shifted the traditional census model. These final statistics are highly relevant to open data, as they demonstrate the complexity and value of maintaining transparent, accessible public records. By making granular demographic details publicly available, the initiative supports evidence-based policymaking and enhances civic accountability, ensuring that government data remains a robust, reliable resource for researchers and citizens alike.
