Chiclayo: asaltan a censista cuando se desplazaba por el distrito de José Leonardo Ortiz

The violent assault on a census worker highlights critical safety risks for personnel conducting national data collection. This incident underscores the urgent need for robust protection measures to ensure the physical security of field staff, who are essential for gathering accurate demographic information. The INEI’s response emphasizes that worker wellbeing is a priority, pledging collaboration with authorities to provide adequate support. However, the lack of immediate official engagement with media questions raises concerns about transparency and accountability during sensitive operations involving public data. This event is relevant to open data because it reveals the tangible human costs behind statistical production. Ensuring the safety and accessibility of these processes is vital for maintaining public trust and the integrity of the resulting datasets, which form the foundation for evidence-based policy and open government initiatives.
