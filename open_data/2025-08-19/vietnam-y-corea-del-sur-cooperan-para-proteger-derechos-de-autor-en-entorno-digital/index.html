Vietnam y Corea del Sur cooperan para proteger derechos de autor en entorno digital

Vietnam y Corea del Sur cooperan para proteger derechos de autor en entorno digital

Experts from Vietnam and South Korea addressed copyright challenges in the digital age and artificial intelligence. They emphasized the need for technological solutions to track and transparently manage intellectual property rights, highlighting the complexity of protecting works in cyberspace. The collaboration aims to transfer automatic detection software to Vietnam. This technological exchange seeks to strengthen surveillance capabilities against piracy and unauthorized use, fostering bilateral cooperation to better protect creators' rights through advanced monitoring tools. This event is relevant to open data, as it underscores the critical balance between open innovation and intellectual property protection. As digital environments evolve, transparent data management and shared technologies become essential for enforcing rights without stifling the collaborative spirit inherent in open ecosystems.
