Costa Rica: Open Data Boosts Rights, Environmental Protection

Costa Rica: Open Data Boosts Rights, Environmental Protection

This call for proposals highlights the critical role of open data in driving transparency and accountability within civil society. By funding small-scale, innovative projects, the initiative aims to demonstrate how accessible public information can be leveraged to foster government fairness and enhance civic engagement, proving that strategic data utilization is essential for democratic strengthening. The focus extends beyond mere data access to emphasizing literacy, inclusion, and sustainability. Projects must actively engage marginalized groups and demonstrate clear outcomes, ensuring that data-driven solutions address human rights and environmental challenges. This approach underscores the open data community’s commitment to equity, ensuring that technological benefits reach women, youth, and indigenous populations rather than remaining exclusive to technical experts. Relevance to open data lies in its validation of data as a tool for social impact rather than just an administrative asset. By requiring evidence of sustainability and alignment with global climate agreements, the grant encourages long-term, ethical data practices. It signals a shift toward measuring success by community empowerment and tangible societal improvements, reinforcing the idea that open data must be inclusive, actionable, and responsible to truly benefit society.
