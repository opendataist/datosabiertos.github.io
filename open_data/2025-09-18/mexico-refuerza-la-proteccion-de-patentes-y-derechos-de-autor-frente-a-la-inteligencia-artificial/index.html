México refuerza la protección de patentes y derechos de autor frente a la Inteligencia Artificial

México refuerza la protección de patentes y derechos de autor frente a la Inteligencia Artificial

Santiago Nieto emphasizes that the reform to the Federal Law for the Protection of Industrial Property is essential to strengthen Mexican patents and ensure that human creativity prevails over artificial intelligence. The initiative seeks to clarify that intellectual property rights belong exclusively to individuals, establishing severe administrative penalties for the misuse of AI, thereby reinforcing the national legal framework on innovation and cultural protection. This legal framework aligns with international agreements such as the USMCA (T-MEC), enabling more effective protection of industrial property and the cultural rights of Indigenous and Afro-descendant peoples. By simplifying registration processes and regulating new technologies, the government promotes an environment where local innovation is recognized globally, fostering sustainable and dignified regional economies. This reform is relevant to the open data movement by defining who holds authorship and rights over creations generated in contemporary digital ecosystems. By establishing clear limits on attribution in emerging technologies, it protects intellectual transparency and ensures that data stemming from human creativity is not appropriated or diluted by automated systems, thereby guaranteeing the integrity and ownership of information in the digital age.
