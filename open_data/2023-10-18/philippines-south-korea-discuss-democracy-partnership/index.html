Philippines, South Korea discuss democracy, partnership

The Philippines and South Korea are strengthening multilateral cooperation on democracy and open government. This partnership emphasizes shared commitments to fighting corruption, advancing human rights, and defending against authoritarianism through joint diplomatic efforts. A key focus is the Open Government Partnership, where both nations aim to enhance fiscal transparency and public participation. By leveraging technology and innovative approaches, they seek to improve legislative oversight and foster deeper civic engagement in their respective governments. Disaster preparedness and civil defense also drive this alliance. The countries plan to exchange best practices for handling natural calamities and ensure emergency alerts are accessible to diverse populations, thereby improving safety and resilience for all citizens. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical link between technological innovation and transparency. Enhancing fiscal openness and public participation relies on accessible, reliable data, while multilateral cooperation facilitates the sharing of best practices and standards essential for effective open government initiatives.
