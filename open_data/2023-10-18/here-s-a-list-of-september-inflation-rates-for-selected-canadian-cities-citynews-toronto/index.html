Here's a list of September inflation rates for selected Canadian cities | CityNews Toronto

Statistics Canada reported a national inflation rate of 3.8 percent, highlighting significant regional disparities across major Canadian cities. This data underscores the challenge of deriving accurate local economic insights from small statistical samples, which can lead to wide fluctuations. For open data enthusiasts, this illustrates the critical need for transparency regarding data granularity and sampling limitations. Understanding these constraints is essential for correctly interpreting localized metrics derived from national datasets. Ultimately, this release emphasizes that open government data must be consumed with a clear understanding of its methodological boundaries. It serves as a reminder that raw figures alone do not tell the whole story without context regarding sample size and statistical reliability.
