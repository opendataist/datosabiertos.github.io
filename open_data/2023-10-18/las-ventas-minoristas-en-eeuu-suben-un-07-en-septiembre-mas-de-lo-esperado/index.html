Las ventas minoristas en EEUU suben un 0,7% en septiembre, más de lo esperado

US retail sales exceeded expectations, signaling that consumers are absorbing price increases while maintaining spending momentum. This resilience, driven by robust online sales and growth in general merchandise stores, suggests the economy is entering the final quarter with greater strength than anticipated. Analysts highlight that this solid consumption, supported by continuous job gains, reduces the likelihood of an immediate economic contraction. However, the persistent strength in spending implies that the Federal Reserve may feel compelled to implement further interest rate hikes to manage inflationary pressures. This update is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how freely accessible government trade statistics enable independent economic forecasting. It highlights the value of transparent data in allowing experts to interpret real-time consumer behavior and predict monetary policy shifts without relying on closed institutional models.
