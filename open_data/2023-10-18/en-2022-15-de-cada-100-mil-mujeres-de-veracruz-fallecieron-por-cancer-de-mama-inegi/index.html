En 2022, 15 de cada 100 mil mujeres de Veracruz fallecieron por cáncer de mama: INEGI

Veracruz reported lower breast cancer mortality than the national average in 2022, although incidence rates remained significant. This highlights regional health disparities that require targeted public health strategies to improve early detection and survival rates across diverse demographic groups. Understanding these statistical differences is crucial for open data initiatives, as they reveal hidden health trends and gaps in resource allocation. Transparent, accessible data empowers communities and policymakers to effectively address specific local challenges. Ultimately, this dataset underscores the necessity of open government information for monitoring public health. By making such statistics widely available, society can foster informed discussions and drive equitable improvements in healthcare outcomes for all women.
