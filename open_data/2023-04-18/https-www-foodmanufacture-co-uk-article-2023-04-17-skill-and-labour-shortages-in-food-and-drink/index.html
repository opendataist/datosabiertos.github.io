Food manufacturing faces severe labor shortages due to Brexit and the pandemic, forcing employers to seek overseas talent. While temporary visas exist, firms increasingly rely on skilled worker sponsorship to maintain workforce consistency, particularly for roles like butchers. This shift highlights how immigration policy directly impacts industry stability and operational continuity. The ongoing review of shortage occupation lists determines whether critical roles can attract necessary international talent. The article is relevant to open data because transparent, publicly accessible labor market statistics and visa approval rates are essential. Open data enables sectors to forecast needs, advocate for policy changes, and plan recruitment strategies effectively amidst regulatory uncertainty.
