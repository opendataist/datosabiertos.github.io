Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has launched an updated Ibrahim Index Data Portal, offering an interactive platform for exploring the Ibrahim Index of African Governance. This tool allows users to easily navigate comprehensive governance metrics across all 54 African nations, featuring enhanced country profiles and detailed measure pages that facilitate deep comparative analysis of performance trends over a decade. Relevant to open data, the portal democratizes access to complex governance indicators by transforming static datasets into dynamic, user-friendly visualizations. By enabling citizens, researchers, and civil society to drill down into specific sub-categories and compare national results against continental averages, it empowers stakeholders to critically assess political, social, and economic service delivery. This initiative highlights the importance of accessible, high-quality open data in fostering transparent public governance. By making extensive comparative data available for free exploration, the foundation supports evidence-based advocacy and policy formulation, ensuring that governance performance can be scrutinized and improved through informed public engagement.
