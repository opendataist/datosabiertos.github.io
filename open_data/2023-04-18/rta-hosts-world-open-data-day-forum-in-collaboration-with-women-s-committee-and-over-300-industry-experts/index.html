RTA Hosts "World Open Data Day" Forum in Collaboration with Women's Committee and Over 300 Industry Experts

Dubai’s Roads and Transport Authority utilized International Open Data Day to strengthen its leadership by championing open data initiatives. The forum highlighted how accessing accurate data enables informed decision-making, efficient resource allocation, and improved public services, directly supporting the agency’s strategic objectives. This underscores the critical role of transparency in enhancing government operational effectiveness and service quality. The event fostered vital strategic partnerships with major global technology firms and local departments. By focusing on knowledge transfer and new business models, the RTA aimed to leverage international expertise in data management and artificial intelligence. This collaborative approach demonstrates how cross-sector cooperation can drive innovation and modernize public sector operations through shared best practices. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it illustrates the practical implementation of open policies to solve complex societal challenges. It emphasizes that open data is not merely a technical standard but a strategic asset that drives economic growth and social progress. By celebrating these advancements, the RTA reinforces the global importance of accessible data in shaping the future of intelligent transportation and public administration.
