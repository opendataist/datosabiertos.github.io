Presentan UMSNH e Imaip Ley de Transparencia en lengua purépecha

The University of Michoacán and the State Institute for Transparency have collaborated to translate Michoacán’s Transparency Law into Purépecha, marking a significant step toward linguistic inclusion. This initiative ensures that indigenous communities can fully understand and exercise their legal right to access public information and data protection, removing language barriers that previously hindered their participation in civic matters. This effort transforms legal texts into effective instruments for social equity, empowering speakers to navigate their rights autonomously. By prioritizing the inclusion of native speakers, the project aligns with broader open data principles that emphasize accessibility and transparency not just for the general population, but specifically for historically marginalized groups, ensuring their active engagement with government accountability mechanisms. This translation is highly relevant to open data as it expands the scope of transparency beyond mere publication, focusing on genuine accessibility. It demonstrates how open data initiatives must address linguistic diversity to be truly inclusive, proving that digital and legal openness must intersect with cultural respect to guarantee equal rights for all citizens, particularly those from indigenous backgrounds.
