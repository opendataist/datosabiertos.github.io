Launch of the Health Inequality Data Repository

The Health Inequality Data Repository represents the most extensive global collection of disaggregated health data, offering millions of data points across diverse indicators. This resource directly supports the World Health Organization’s mandate to identify inequalities and their drivers, which is essential for achieving health equity and improving public health programs. By providing detailed insights into who is being left behind, the repository enables more targeted and effective interventions. This collection is critically relevant to the open data movement as it provides the foundational evidence required to monitor progress toward the Sustainable Development Goals. The emphasis on disaggregated data ensures that disparities can be quantified and addressed, aligning with the global aspiration to leave no one behind. Accessible through the interactive Health Equity Assessment Toolkit, the data facilitates transparency and allows stakeholders to evaluate interventions effectively. Ultimately, this initiative underscores the necessity of strategic data sharing in global health governance. It demonstrates how open, comprehensive datasets empower policymakers and researchers to understand social determinants of health and design inclusive policies. By making such vast amounts of information available and explorable, the repository sets a standard for how open data can drive accountability and equity in worldwide health initiatives.
