Elecciones en Guatemala: UE pide transparencia en inscripción de candidatos

The EU urges Guatemala to ensure that its candidate registration process is transparent and impartial, following the exclusion of key figures such as Indigenous leader Thelma Cabrera. This intervention highlights the tension between judicial decisions and electoral fairness, calling for compliance with constitutional standards to guarantee genuine voter choice. This situation underscores the critical role of open data in safeguarding democratic integrity. Transparent electoral records enable citizens and watchdogs to verify the legitimacy of candidate qualifications and monitor potential political bias. Without accessible, clear data on electoral decisions, public trust erodes and inclusivity suffers. Ensuring that voting options remain competitive and representative requires robust data governance, making this case a vital example of how open information supports fair governance and civic accountability.
