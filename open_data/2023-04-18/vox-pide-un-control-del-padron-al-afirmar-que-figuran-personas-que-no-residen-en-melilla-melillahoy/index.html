Vox pide un control del Padrón al afirmar que figuran personas que no residen en Melilla - MelillaHoy

Vox Melilla demands stricter management of the Municipal Register, arguing that current data is distorted by individuals benefiting from local services without residing in the city, while legitimate residents remain unregistered. This discrepancy undermines labor market integrity and public resource allocation, highlighting a critical need for administrative accuracy to restore social order. The party urges the Ministry of Interior to facilitate cooperation between national security forces and local police for rigorous registration verification. Although recognizing the complexity of this task, they emphasize that correcting these imbalances is essential for ensuring that public benefits and employment plans reach their intended demographic, thereby protecting legal workers and city resources. This issue is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the real-world consequences of inaccurate civic databases. Reliable, transparent, and verified open data is crucial for fair public service distribution and effective governance. Without accurate records, policy decisions are flawed, leading to social inequities and economic distortions that undermine trust in public institutions and the integrity of administrative systems.
