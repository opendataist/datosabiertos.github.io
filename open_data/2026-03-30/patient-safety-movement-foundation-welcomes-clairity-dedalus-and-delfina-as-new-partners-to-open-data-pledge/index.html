Patient Safety Movement Foundation Welcomes Clairity, Dedalus, and Delfina as New Partners to Open Data Pledge

The Patient Safety Movement Foundation has secured commitments from major healthcare technology leaders, including Clairity, Dedalus, and Delfina Care, to join the Open Data Pledge. This initiative underscores a critical industry shift toward unlocking the potential of clinical data from devices like electronic health records and physiological monitors. By ensuring this data is accessible in secure, responsible ways, the pledge aims to empower clinicians and researchers with the information necessary to make timely, informed decisions that directly impact patient outcomes. Central to this movement is the use of open clinical data to fuel AI-driven predictive algorithms. These algorithms are essential tools for identifying risks before they materialize, thereby supporting the ambitious global goal of achieving zero preventable patient harm by 2030. The commitment does not require sharing proprietary software or intellectual property, focusing strictly on patient-generated clinical data to balance innovation with strict adherence to privacy regulations and security standards. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates as it demonstrates how structured, standardized data sharing can drive tangible improvements in public health safety. It illustrates a successful model where transparency and interoperability coexist with robust privacy protections, proving that open data is not just a technical ideal but a practical mechanism for saving lives. The growing network of signatories highlights a collective accountability in advancing a connected healthcare system where data serves its highest purpose: protecting patients through collaboration and shared knowledge.
