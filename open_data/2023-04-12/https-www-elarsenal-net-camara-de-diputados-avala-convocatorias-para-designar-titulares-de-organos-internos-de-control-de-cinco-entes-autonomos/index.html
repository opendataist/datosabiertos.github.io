The Mexican Chamber of Deputies has approved public, open calls for the selection of heads of internal control bodies in key oversight agencies, including those responsible for human rights, justice, telecommunications, transparency, and statistics. This decision establishes transparent procedures with strict legal requirements and deadlines, ensuring that candidates must undergo rigorous digital documentation reviews and constitutional compliance checks. By mandating public and open processes, the legislature prioritizes meritocracy and institutional accountability in filling these critical roles. The established timelines and mandatory documentation phases are designed to filter applicants effectively, ensuring that only those meeting the highest legal standards are proposed for final approval by the full chamber. This development is highly relevant to open data because it directly impacts the leadership of Mexico’s primary institutions for transparency and statistical production. The heads of the National Institute of Transparency and the National Institute of Statistics and Geography will determine policies regarding public access to government information and the quality of open datasets. Strengthening the integrity and independence of these appointments is essential for maintaining trust in the availability, accuracy, and openness of public sector data.
