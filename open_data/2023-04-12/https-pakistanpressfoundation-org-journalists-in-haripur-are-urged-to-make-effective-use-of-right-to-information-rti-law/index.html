This initiative highlights open_data by demonstrating how transparency laws empower media professionals to access public records. By training journalists on utilizing these legal mechanisms, the program underscores the critical link between accessible government data and accountability. Participants learned to leverage information requests to uncover corruption and counter misinformation. This approach ensures that citizens receive accurate, reliable news, reinforcing the idea that open data is essential for robust investigative journalism and community awareness. Ultimately, the training fosters a culture of responsible reporting that serves the public interest. It emphasizes that when journalists effectively use open data tools, they can drive positive social change and hold institutions accountable, making transparency a cornerstone of democratic health.
