ES EN

This article outlines a standard privacy and data protection policy, emphasizing the legal obligations of the website owner regarding user data. It details the collection of personal information, primarily through cookies and usage statistics, while ensuring transparency about the purposes of processing, such as service provision, advertising, and analytics. A critical aspect highlighted is the distinction in protection standards based on user location. Users within the European Union are afforded higher levels of privacy protection under local regulations compared to others. The text explains the legal bases for data processing, including consent and legitimate interest, and confirms adherence to security measures and cross-border transfer protocols where applicable. This content is relevant to open data because it serves as a clear example of the mandatory transparency required in digital services. While the data itself is not open, the structured disclosure of data handling practices, third-party integrations, and user rights demonstrates the compliance framework necessary for any entity operating in the digital ecosystem. Understanding these policies is essential for analyzing how personal data is managed alongside publicly available information.
