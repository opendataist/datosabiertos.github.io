Segalmex debe comprobar recuperación de 950 mdp

The INAI ordered Segalmex to disclose documentation proving the full recovery of funds used for stock certificates. This directive addresses irregularities in the purchase of basic goods identified by federal auditors, ensuring transparency in the allocation of resources intended to protect vulnerable populations. This mandate highlights the critical role of public access in verifying government accountability. By requiring the release of specific financial records, the ruling reinforces the obligation of institutions to justify their expenditures, particularly when significant sums are involved in social welfare programs. Such decisions are vital for open data initiatives, as they demonstrate how transparency mechanisms can uncover and rectify administrative failures. Making these details public allows society to monitor how funds are managed, fostering trust and ensuring that financial operations remain visible and subject to public scrutiny.
