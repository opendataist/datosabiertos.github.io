Editorial – MORENA en Soledad y el uso indebido de datos personales | Revista Punto de Vista

Editorial – MORENA en Soledad y el uso indebido de datos personales | Revista Punto de Vista

This article exposes the illicit use of protected personal data in Mexican electoral campaigns, highlighting a specific case in which citizens’ addresses appeared on energy bills alongside political propaganda. The incident, involving prominent political figures, demonstrates a serious breach of privacy laws and ethical standards, raising urgent concerns about data security in public administration. The misuse of sensitive information undermines democratic integrity and erodes public trust in electoral processes. It suggests that political actors are prioritizing campaign gains over citizens’ fundamental rights, creating a dangerous precedent in which confidential state records are weaponized for partisan purposes. This erosion of trust threatens the legitimacy of future elections and the broader social contract. This case is critical to open data discussions because it illustrates the risks of poor data governance and the lack of transparency in how government-held information is accessed and used. It underscores the need for robust legal frameworks and strict accountability mechanisms to prevent the exploitation of public data. Ensuring that open data initiatives do not facilitate privacy violations is essential for preserving democratic values and maintaining citizen confidence in digital governance.
