Inés Cañizares destaca “el hito histórico” de la digitalización de los Registros, “más accesibles a los ciudadanos y garantes de la seguridad jurídica”

The full digitalization of Spain’s Property, Commercial, and Real Estate Registries represents a historic milestone for public administration, particularly in Castilla-La Mancha. This modernization successfully transitions essential legal functions from paper to digital formats while rigorously upholding the principles of legal certainty and public faith. It demonstrates that advanced technological integration can coexist with, and even strengthen, traditional safeguards in public services. This shift significantly enhances accessibility for citizens by enabling real-time consultation of records through a single electronic headquarters. By allowing immediate access to each file, the system drastically streamlines bureaucratic procedures, offering greater convenience in an era that demands speed. The open nature of this new framework ensures that public data is more transparent and readily available to those who need it for legal or commercial transactions. This development is crucial for open data initiatives, as it establishes a standardized, interoperable model recognized by the European Union. It proves that public registries can serve as robust data sources, facilitating cross-border connections within the EU. Consequently, it highlights how digitizing essential legal infrastructure not only improves efficiency but also creates a reliable, open foundation for broader data exchange and innovation across government sectors.
