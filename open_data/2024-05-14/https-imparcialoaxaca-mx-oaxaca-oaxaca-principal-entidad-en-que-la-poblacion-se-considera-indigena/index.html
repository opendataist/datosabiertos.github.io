Open Data
ES EN

The “Cuéntame de México” portal democratizes access to demographic data, highlighting the indigenous population density in states such as Oaxaca. This initiative aims to empower citizens, particularly youth, by fostering statistical literacy from an early age through interactive and comprehensible content. Its relevance to the open data movement lies in its pedagogical approach. By transforming complex statistics into accessible narratives, INEGI illustrates how data transparency must be accompanied by tools that facilitate critical interpretation. This promotes a society capable of using public information to make informed decisions in everyday contexts. Thus, the project not only presents figures but also builds civic capacity. By making geographic and social data more user-friendly, it establishes a vital precedent for digital civic education, ensuring that government openness truly translates into social empowerment and a collective understanding of the national reality.
