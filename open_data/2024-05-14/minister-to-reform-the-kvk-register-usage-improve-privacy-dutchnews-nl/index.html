Minister to reform the KvK register usage, improve privacy - DutchNews.nl

The Dutch government plans to remove fees for accessing the trade register, ensuring equal access while introducing annual charges for self-employed individuals and company owners. This shift aims to correct current imbalances where data was commodified for direct marketing, potentially disrupting existing revenue models for data aggregation services. Privacy protections will significantly tighten by restricting access to personal contact details like phone numbers and emails. This measure addresses severe abuse cases where private information was sold, leading to harassment of professionals, thereby prioritizing individual safety over unrestricted data availability for third parties. These reforms are vital for open data governance, demonstrating how public registries can balance transparency with privacy rights. By preventing data exploitation and enforcing stricter access controls, the initiative sets a precedent for ethical data stewardship, ensuring open information serves public interest rather than commercial exploitation.
