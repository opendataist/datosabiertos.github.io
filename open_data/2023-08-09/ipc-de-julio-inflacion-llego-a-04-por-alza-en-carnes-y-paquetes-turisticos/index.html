IPC de julio: inflación llegó a 0,4% por alza en carnes y paquetes turísticos

The National Statistics Institute reports that the Consumer Price Index rose in July, driven primarily by increases in meat and tourism packages. This marks a significant cumulative growth over the past year, reflecting broader inflationary pressures affecting various sectors of the economy. Conversely, certain categories like clothing saw price reductions, balancing the overall index. The divergence in sector performance highlights the complex nature of current market dynamics, where essential goods become more expensive while non-essential items may offer relief. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of accessible, transparent statistical metrics. Reliable public information enables citizens and analysts to track inflation trends, assess economic health, and make informed financial decisions regarding costs like rent and dividends.
