4,186 fire incidents reported in 2022

The article highlights a concerning rise in fire incidents, particularly in residential areas and vehicles, urging improved maintenance and safety adherence to prevent avoidable losses. This data underscores the critical role of open data in public safety, allowing authorities to identify high-risk zones and allocate resources more effectively to mitigate threats before they escalate. By analyzing where fires occur, such as at waste sites or in rapidly industrializing zones, governments can implement targeted prevention strategies. Open data transparency empowers communities with knowledge about local hazards, fostering a culture of safety and encouraging compliance with civil protection standards across different sectors. Ultimately, this statistical transparency demonstrates how accessible data drives policy changes and public awareness. It enables stakeholders to understand emerging trends, such as summer vehicle fires, and respond proactively, ensuring that safety measures evolve alongside infrastructure growth and environmental challenges.
