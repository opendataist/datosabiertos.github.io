La cena está servida

The article reveals that while table manners have a complex history, universal values such as cleanliness and solidarity remain consistent across cultures. Visser’s work critically examines the classist origins of etiquette and its subsequent democratization, highlighting how social norms evolve beyond mere description. This analysis is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates the value of examining historical datasets to uncover hidden biases and power structures. By revealing how social rules exclude or include specific groups, the text emphasizes the importance of understanding context and inequality in information systems. Such insights remind us that data and norms are not neutral but reflect societal hierarchies. Applying this perspective to open data encourages practitioners to investigate how historical contexts shape current information practices. This fosters a more equitable and transparent approach to managing and interpreting public data for diverse communities.
