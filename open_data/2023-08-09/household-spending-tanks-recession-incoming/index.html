Household spending tanks, recession incoming

Household spending growth has stagnated, with declines in discretionary items reflecting the burden of high inflation and cost-of-living pressures. This sharp drop in real per capita spending signals a deep recession in living standards, as economic gains are diluted by population growth. For open data, these figures highlight the critical need for disaggregated metrics that separate nominal trends from real per capita outcomes. Relying solely on aggregate growth obscures the worsening individual economic reality, demonstrating how transparent data can reveal the disparity between national headlines and household experiences. Ultimately, this case underscores the value of open data in exposing structural vulnerabilities. By providing clear access to spending indicators, researchers can better understand how policy decisions, such as immigration levels, mask underlying economic stress, ensuring more accurate public discourse and informed civic engagement.
