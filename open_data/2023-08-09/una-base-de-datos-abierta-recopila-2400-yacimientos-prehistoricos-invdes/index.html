Una base de datos abierta recopila 2,400 yacimientos prehistóricos - INVDES

The creation of ROAD marks a milestone in the democratization of scientific knowledge by making a massive database freely available to everyone, covering the first three million years of human history. This resource unifies information from thousands of archaeological sites and ancient cultures within a coherent geochronological framework, removing barriers to access in archaeological and anthropological research. Through an intuitive interface and the ability to perform complex queries, this tool enables researchers and students to analyze the relationships between culture, environment, and human expansion. By centralizing scattered data from hundreds of publications and languages, it facilitates the study of evolutionary patterns and the visualization of the distribution of fossil remains and tools, empowering the scientific community to generate new knowledge autonomously. The relevance of this article to open data lies in its commitment to open science, offering a model of universal access that highlights existing gaps in the historical record, such as the lack of data on Oceania. This not only promotes transparency and global collaboration but also invites society to actively participate in identifying future discoveries, transforming how we understand our species' shared past.
