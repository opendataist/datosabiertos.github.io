El IPC de Costa Rica registró una variación de -0,19% en julio de 2023

Costa Rica’s July inflation data reveals a broader economic stabilization, offering valuable insights for open-data enthusiasts analyzing regional market trends. By publicly releasing detailed consumer price indices, institutions foster transparency and enable researchers to track inflationary pressures effectively. This accessible dataset allows analysts to identify specific sectors driving price changes, such as transportation and communication. Open availability empowers citizens and experts to scrutinize economic health, promoting data-driven decision-making and a deeper understanding of local economic dynamics. Relevant to open data, this transparency supports accountability and empowers communities to verify official statistics. It exemplifies how releasing granular economic indicators enhances public trust and facilitates independent analysis of cost-of-living changes across diverse demographics.
