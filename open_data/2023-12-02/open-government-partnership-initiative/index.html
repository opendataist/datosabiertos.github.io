Open Government Partnership initiative

Open Government Partnership initiative

Seychelles is advancing its commitment to open governance by finalizing a citizen-driven National Action Plan through the Open Government Partnership. The Vice-President chairs a multi-stakeholder committee that evaluates proposed reforms, ensuring that government initiatives directly reflect public concerns. This collaborative approach exemplifies the core OGP principle of co-creating policies with civil society, thereby enhancing the legitimacy and relevance of transparency efforts. The selected projects prioritize digital transformation, education quality, and fisheries transparency, while merging related ideas to maximize implementation effectiveness. By focusing on an e-engagement portal and a national integrity coalition, the government aims to institutionalize mechanisms for continuous citizen participation. These specific domains address critical needs for accountability and service delivery, demonstrating how structured dialogue leads to actionable, high-impact reforms that strengthen democratic engagement. This process is relevant to open data because it establishes the political and structural framework necessary for data accessibility and reuse. The emphasis on digital connection and transparency initiatives implies a move toward making government information more available and understandable to the public. Consequently, open data becomes a practical tool for achieving the stated goals of inclusivity and accountability, allowing citizens to actively monitor and contribute to national progress through informed participation.
