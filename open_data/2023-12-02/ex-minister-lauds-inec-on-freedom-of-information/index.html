Ex-Minister lauds INEC on Freedom of Information

Former Aviation Minister Osita Chidoka praised INEC for releasing voter accreditation data under the Freedom of Information Act. This move establishes a vital precedent for government transparency, demonstrating that electoral bodies can be held accountable to legal standards and public scrutiny. By complying with FOI requests, INEC reinforces the principle that civic data belongs to the citizens, not just the state. The release of these records marks a significant shift toward open governance, allowing for independent verification of electoral processes. It highlights the practical utility of FOI legislation in bridging the trust gap between institutions and the electorate. Such access enables stakeholders to analyze voting patterns and ensure that elections genuinely reflect the people’s will, thereby strengthening democratic integrity. This incident is highly relevant to open_data as it validates the effectiveness of legal frameworks in mandating data disclosure. It encourages the broader cultural shift from opacity to transparency in public administration. When agencies proactively share operational data, it empowers civil society to monitor governance, ensuring that electoral mechanisms remain robust, verifiable, and responsive to public oversight.
