Las pernoctaciones en alojamientos de turismo rural han subido un 4,05 % en octubre - EFEAgro

The surge in non-resident stays in rural tourism highlights a growing reliance on foreign demand, signaling economic resilience amidst local decline. This trend underscores the critical need for open data to accurately track shifting tourism dynamics and resource allocation. Transparent, accessible statistics allow stakeholders to analyze occupancy patterns and regional preferences without data silos. By democratizing this information, open data fosters better-informed policy decisions and supports sustainable rural development strategies. Ultimately, this data reveals how open access to tourism metrics can drive efficiency and adaptability in the sector. It empowers communities to leverage these insights for long-term growth, ensuring rural areas remain viable economic assets through informed, data-driven management.
