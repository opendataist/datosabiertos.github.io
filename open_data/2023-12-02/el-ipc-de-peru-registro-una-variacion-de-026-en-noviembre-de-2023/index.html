El IPC de Perú registró una variación de -0,26% en noviembre de 2023

The main conclusion of the report is that inflation in Peru experienced a significant slowdown in November 2023, primarily driven by falling prices in food, transportation, and communications. This monthly deflationary trend contrasts with the broad-based increases in services such as accommodation, health, and recreation, revealing an economic dynamic in which the basic costs of subsistence and mobility decreased, partially alleviating pressure on families' purchasing power during that period. A detailed analysis shows that the decline in prices for essential goods, such as fruits and vegetables, along with reductions in the cost of energy and transportation, was the determining factor. Conversely, the services sector experienced moderate increases. This contrast highlights the volatility in markets for perishable goods and fuels, while services maintain a more consistent inflationary trajectory, underscoring the need to monitor how these opposing forces balance overall consumption and price stability at both the national and regional levels. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the public utility of official statistics in clarifying the complexity of the market. By breaking down indices by categories and specific products, it provides civil society, researchers, and journalists with structured sources to analyze the real impact of prices on quality of life. This transparency enables verification of economic conditions, fosters government accountability, and facilitates the development of independent, evidence-based analyses.
