Califica Obrador de “desbarajuste” al interior del INAI

The article highlights serious allegations of corruption within the INAI, prompting a government investigation into the alleged misuse of public funds. President López Obrador criticizes autonomous bodies as costly, inefficient, and politically motivated structures that create parallel governance, thereby weakening the legitimate executive branch. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for rigorous transparency and accountability in the institutions responsible for safeguarding information rights. When the guardians of data protection face credible accusations of financial impropriety, it erodes public trust and demonstrates how opaque governance can undermine the very principles of access and integrity. Consequently, this case serves as a cautionary tale about the risks of insufficient oversight over autonomous entities. It reinforces the importance of maintaining clear, auditable financial trails within open government structures to ensure that resources dedicated to transparency are actually enhancing, rather than compromising, democratic accountability and data security.
