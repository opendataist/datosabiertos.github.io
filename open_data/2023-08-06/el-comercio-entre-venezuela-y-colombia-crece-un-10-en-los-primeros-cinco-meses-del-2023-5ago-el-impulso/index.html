▷ El comercio entre Venezuela y Colombia crece un 10% en los primeros cinco meses del 2023 #5Ago - El Impulso

The resurgence of commercial ties between Venezuela and Colombia, evidenced by rising bilateral trade figures, highlights the vital role of transparent economic data in fostering regional integration. This recovery underscores how accessible trade statistics facilitate the monitoring of cross-border economic health and the strengthening of bilateral agreements. Such data transparency is essential for open data initiatives, as it enables civil society and businesses to verify market trends and make informed decisions. When governments and chambers of commerce publish detailed import-export records, they democratize access to economic information, reducing opacity and building trust among stakeholders. Ultimately, this example illustrates that open data is not merely about numbers but serves as a foundation for accountability and development. By sharing granular trade details, regions can better navigate economic challenges and promote sustainable growth through shared visibility and mutual understanding.
