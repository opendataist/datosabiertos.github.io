Recent Canadian data reveals employment growth driven entirely by part-time roles, maintaining a stable unemployment rate despite sector-specific losses. This tight labor market fuels wage growth that outpaces inflation, complicating the central bank’s efforts to lower prices. These dynamics are crucial for open data as they highlight the importance of granular labor statistics. Detailed metrics on employment quality and wage pressures provide essential context for economic analysis, demonstrating how open data can uncover nuanced trends hidden in aggregate figures.
