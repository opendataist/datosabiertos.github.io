Google Bard and OpenAI ChatGPT at risk of losing out to open source AI models: Google senior software engineer

Open-source AI is rapidly closing the quality gap with proprietary models, offering superior privacy and customization. This shift forces major tech companies to adapt by collaborating with external researchers and prioritizing third-party integrations to remain competitive. The rising capability of unrestricted, free alternatives challenges the business model of paid, limited services. Companies must rethink their value proposition as users increasingly prefer accessible, modifiable tools over locked-in solutions. This trend emphasizes the need for rapid iteration and small, easily updated model variants. Ultimately, it highlights how open data and code foster innovation, making transparency and community-driven development essential for future AI advancements.
