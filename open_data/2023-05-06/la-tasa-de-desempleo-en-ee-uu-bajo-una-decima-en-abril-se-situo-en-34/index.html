La tasa de desempleo en EE.UU. bajó una décima en abril: se situó en 3,4 %

The U.S. labor market demonstrated notable resilience in April, with the unemployment rate falling to historic lows and job creation exceeding expectations. This strong performance suggests that the Federal Reserve’s tight monetary policies aimed at combating inflation have not significantly dampened labor market activity, maintaining confidence in the economy despite tighter financial conditions. Evidence that the services, healthcare, and leisure sectors continue to attract workers indicates underlying structural dynamism, while wages are growing moderately without triggering an uncontrolled inflationary spiral. This relative stability allows regulators to maintain a cautious approach, assessing whether current interest rates are sufficient to achieve the goal of stable prices without triggering an unexpected recession amid banking sector volatility. The relevance of this article to the field of open data lies in its reliance on public macroeconomic data, particularly those published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The transparency and accessibility of these figures are fundamental for economic analysis, policy decision-making, and democratic accountability. Understanding how these datasets are collected, verified, and revised is essential for correctly interpreting market trends and evaluating the effectiveness of government interventions in real time.
