La gasolina se encarece un 19 % en lo que va de año y el gasóleo casi un 15 %

The end of public subsidies has revealed that, although gasoline and diesel prices have seen slight recent declines, they remain well above last year’s levels. This trend reflects the ongoing impact of the energy crisis and geopolitical tensions on the market. Despite the price increases, Spanish fuel tariffs remain below historical highs and are notably lower than the European average. This indicates that, although costs have risen, Spain maintains a relative competitive advantage over the eurozone in fuel supply. This issue is relevant to open data because it illustrates how public access to detailed petroleum data enables monitoring of market transparency. The availability of accurate information facilitates comparative analysis and the assessment of the impact of public policies, which are essential for accountability and informed decision-making on energy matters.
