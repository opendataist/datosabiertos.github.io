Riverhead Town to sue property owner over alleged illegal rentals on Polish Town site where Jan. 25 fire left two elderly women homeless

Riverhead Town has initiated legal action against a property owner for maintaining residential units without required rental permits, a violation highlighted by a recent fire that displaced elderly tenants. Freedom of Information Law requests revealed that code enforcement officers had identified these illegal rentals years prior, noting multiple inspections and tenant confirmations despite the owner’s repeated refusal to comply. The town is now seeking injunctive relief and potential penalties to address the long-standing unauthorized occupancy and maintenance failures. This case underscores the critical role of transparency in municipal governance, particularly when public safety and housing regulations are at stake. By leveraging open data mechanisms like FOIL requests, journalists and citizens can uncover administrative histories that might otherwise remain hidden from public view. The narrative also illustrates the limitations and challenges inherent in code enforcement, where bureaucratic processes may lag behind immediate community needs.
