Highland Council in top 10 UK local authorities with highest number of underage nicotine sale complaints in 2023/24

Highland Council recorded the highest number of suspected underage nicotine sales complaints in Scotland during 2023/24, highlighting a significant regional challenge regarding youth access to vapes. This data reveals that despite improving compliance rates, the widespread nature of these issues necessitates continued vigilance and robust enforcement strategies by local authorities. The incident underscores the critical role of open data in public health and regulatory oversight. By making complaint statistics transparent through Freedom of Information requests, stakeholders can identify problem areas and allocate resources more effectively. This transparency fosters accountability, allowing councils to target enforcement where it is needed most and encouraging retailers to adopt stricter age-verification protocols. Ultimately, the findings emphasize that responsible retailing and consistent enforcement are essential to prevent young people from accessing nicotine products. As the data demonstrates, public reporting serves as a vital mechanism for driving behavioral change among businesses. Open data initiatives empower communities to monitor compliance, ensuring that legal safeguards are respected and protecting vulnerable populations from the risks associated with early nicotine exposure.
