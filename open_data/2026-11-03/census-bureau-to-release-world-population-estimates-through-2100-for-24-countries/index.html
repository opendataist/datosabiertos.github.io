Census Bureau to Release World Population Estimates Through 2100 for 24 Countries

Census Bureau to Release World Population Estimates Through 2100 for 24 Countries

The U.S. Census Bureau is updating its International Database with global demographic projections through 2100, significantly enhancing long-term population analysis. This expansion supports critical research into future demographic trends, offering open data scholars and policymakers essential insights into global shifts in mortality, fertility, and migration across diverse regions. Simultaneously, new American Community Survey data reveals stark regional disparities in electric vehicle adoption, highlighting Western states as leaders in sustainable transportation. These granular findings demonstrate how open data can uncover localized behavioral patterns, empowering communities to tailor infrastructure policies and environmental initiatives based on accurate, up-to-date statistics. Beyond specific releases, the Bureau continues modernizing surveys like the Current Population Survey and SIPP to improve data quality. These ongoing efforts ensure that open data remains reliable and accessible, fostering transparency and enabling researchers to design effective, evidence-based studies using robust governmental resources for public benefit.
