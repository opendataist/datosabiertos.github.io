With dirt floors, 1 in 11 homes in Oaxaca: INEGI - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

With dirt floors, 1 in 11 homes in Oaxaca: INEGI - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

The 2025 Intercensal Survey reveals that Oaxaca continues to exhibit high levels of housing poverty, ranking as the second entity with the highest percentage of homes with dirt floors. Although there is a slight improvement in the flooring materials used for other surfaces, the prevalence of floors lacking adequate materials remains a serious health hazard, contributing to respiratory and parasitic infections. This data highlights the gap in quality of life and underscores the insufficiency of recent public policies aimed at eradicating this basic condition of human dignity. From a demographic perspective, the state is undergoing a clear transition marked by population aging and a slowdown in population growth. This structural transformation implies a growing future demand for health and social security services, particularly for older adults. The indicators confirm that Oaxaca, along with the rest of the country, is experiencing profound changes in its population pyramid, necessitating strategic adjustments in state-level planning. The relevance of this article to the open data movement lies in its demonstration of how public statistics, when accessible and transparent, become vital tools for accountability and evidence-based policy formulation. INEGI’s data not only describe the socioeconomic reality but also make structural inequalities visible and guide the need for targeted interventions. By making these records public, civil society and experts are empowered to critically analyze access to basic services and government management, fostering a more informed and demanding citizenry.
