Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production highlights significant contributions from major agricultural economies, with China and India dominating output volumes. This data reveals the critical role these nations play in worldwide food security and supply chains. Understanding these production scales helps researchers identify trends in global staple crop distribution and potential vulnerabilities in the food system. The availability of this information through the FAOSTAT database underscores the importance of accessible, standardized agricultural statistics. Users can navigate the platform to extract specific crop data, facilitating independent analysis and verification. This transparency allows stakeholders to cross-reference official figures, although minor discrepancies with national reports may exist, highlighting the need for careful data interpretation in comparative studies. This resource is vital for open data enthusiasts as it demonstrates how international organizations provide structured datasets for public use. By enabling users to explore and manipulate large-scale agricultural records, such platforms empower evidence-based decision-making. The ability to access and analyze global production metrics fosters a deeper understanding of economic and environmental factors influencing crop yields worldwide.
