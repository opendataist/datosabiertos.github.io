Italy's GDP to grow 1% this year, 1.1% in 2025 - Istat - Business - Ansa.it

Istat projects modest Italian economic growth, driven by rising domestic and foreign demand alongside a strengthening labor market. This forecast highlights improving employment conditions and gradual inflation stabilization, suggesting a recovery in household purchasing power despite ongoing international geopolitical uncertainties. This update is vital for open data initiatives because it underscores the need for accessible, high-quality macroeconomic indicators to track national economic health. Transparent data on GDP, employment, and inflation enables researchers and policymakers to validate forecasts, ensuring that open datasets remain reliable tools for understanding real-world economic trends and informing strategic decisions.
