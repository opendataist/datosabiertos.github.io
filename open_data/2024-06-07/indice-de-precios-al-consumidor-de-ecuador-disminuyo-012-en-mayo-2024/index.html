Índice de Precios al Consumidor de Ecuador disminuyó 0,12% en mayo 2024

The report indicates that Ecuador experienced a slight deflation in May 2024, with prices declining marginally compared to the previous month. This monthly decrease occurred alongside an annual inflation rate that has risen compared to the same period the previous year. The data reveals a nuanced economic landscape where specific sectors, particularly food and household items, drove the monthly price adjustments, reflecting the sensitivity of these goods to seasonal and climatic factors. Despite the monthly dip, the broader economic context shows that the cost of living remains a concern. The average monthly income for a typical household was sufficient to cover the cost of the Basic Food Basket, creating a small surplus. However, regional disparities are evident, with highland cities generally experiencing higher annual inflation and slightly higher costs for basic goods compared to coastal areas. This geographic variation highlights the unequal impact of economic trends on different populations across the country. This information is crucial for the open data community because it provides the raw, disaggregated statistics necessary for independent analysis of economic health. By making detailed indices and regional breakdowns publicly available, the statistical institute enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to verify official narratives and understand how inflation differentially affects various demographics. This transparency fosters accountability and allows for more accurate modeling of economic impacts beyond simple headline numbers.
