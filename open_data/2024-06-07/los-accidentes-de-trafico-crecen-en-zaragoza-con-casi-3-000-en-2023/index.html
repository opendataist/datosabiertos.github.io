Los accidentes de tráfico crecen en Zaragoza con casi 3.000 en 2023

Los accidentes de tráfico crecen en Zaragoza con casi 3.000 en 2023

The report highlights a worrying upward trend in traffic accidents in Zaragoza during 2023, characterized by increased severity and a disproportionate number of fatalities among pedestrians aged 65 and over. Although there was a slight decline in minor incidents, the rise in serious injuries and deaths underscores critical vulnerabilities in urban mobility. This shift demands immediate attention to protect elderly citizens and indicates that current safety measures are inadequate for the evolving risks on city streets. In terms of open data, this article illustrates the vital importance of transparent, granular incident reporting for effective urban governance. The detailed breakdown of accidents by vehicle type, age demographics, and injury severity enables policymakers to identify specific high-risk groups and transport modes. Such precise data is essential for designing targeted interventions, such as the upcoming Urban Road Safety Plan, ensuring that resource allocation and regulatory changes address actual patterns of harm rather than relying on aggregated statistics. Furthermore, the integration of administrative data, such as call center volumes for information requests and license reservations, provides broader context regarding public service demand and efficiency. By correlating safety metrics with service usage, local authorities can better evaluate the performance of the Local Police and justify investments in training and technology. This holistic view of municipal operations demonstrates how open data facilitates accountability and continuous improvement in public safety infrastructure, making it a cornerstone of modern, responsive city management.
