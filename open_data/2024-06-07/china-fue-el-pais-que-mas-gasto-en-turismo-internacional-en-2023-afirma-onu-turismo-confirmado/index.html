China fue el país que más gastó en turismo internacional en 2023, afirma ONU Turismo - Confirmado

China fue el país que más gastó en turismo internacional en 2023, afirma ONU Turismo - Confirmado

China reclaimed its top position in international tourism spending in 2023, signaling a robust recovery of the Asia-Pacific region in the post-pandemic era. This shift underscores how changing consumer behaviors and regional economic strengths significantly reshape global travel dynamics. France led in tourist arrivals, while the United States topped international revenue, highlighting the complex relationship between visitor volume and economic impact. These metrics demonstrate that popularity does not always correlate with financial yield, requiring a nuanced analysis of market segments. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides a transparent, globally comparable benchmark for travel trends. Accessible statistics enable researchers and policymakers to track recovery patterns, forecast economic impacts, and support informed decision-making within the broader tourism ecosystem.
