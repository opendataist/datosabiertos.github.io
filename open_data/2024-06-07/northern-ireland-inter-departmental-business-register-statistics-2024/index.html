Northern Ireland Inter-Departmental Business Register Statistics 2024

Northern Ireland Inter-Departmental Business Register Statistics 2024

Northern Ireland’s business landscape demonstrates robust growth, with the total number of registered enterprises rising for the tenth consecutive year. This trend highlights a resilient economic environment where the vast majority of enterprises remain micro-sized, emphasizing the critical role of small-scale entrepreneurship in the regional economy. While the services sector continues to dominate, the construction industry has also seen significant expansion, marking a positive shift after years of fluctuation in other areas like production. The geographic distribution of these enterprises reveals persistent concentration in Belfast, yet specific districts are experiencing notable shifts in business density. This uneven spread suggests varying local economic dynamics, with some areas attracting new ventures while others face declines. Furthermore, the increasing presence of non-local owned businesses indicates a growing integration with external markets, as these enterprises contribute disproportionately to employment despite representing a small fraction of the total business count. This data is highly relevant to open_data initiatives as it showcases how administrative registers, such as the Inter-Departmental Business Register, can provide detailed, high-frequency insights into economic health. By releasing such granular statistics alongside interactive tools, statistical agencies enable researchers and policymakers to analyze sectoral trends and employment patterns effectively. This transparency supports evidence-based decision-making and fosters a deeper understanding of how local economies evolve through the lens of accessible, public information.
