Health activist files RTI plea amid NEET-UG 2024 grace mark controversy - OrissaPOST

The NEET-UG 2024 controversy highlights critical failures in exam transparency, triggering urgent public demands for accountability. Health activists have filed RTI applications to uncover the criteria and extent of grace marks awarded, challenging the official justification based on alleged time losses. This move underscores the necessity for open data practices in standardized testing to prevent opacity in administrative decisions. Simultaneously, petitions in the Supreme Court allege paper leaks, further eroding trust in the National Testing Agency. The intersection of these issues reveals a systemic lack of accessible information regarding scoring anomalies and security breaches. Such gaps prevent stakeholders from verifying the integrity of results, demonstrating how restricted data fuels public skepticism and legal challenges against educational bodies. This situation is vital to open_data discourse as it illustrates the real-world impact of non-transparent algorithms and closed administrative records. When critical examination metrics remain hidden, it becomes impossible to audit fairness or detect irregularities. Ensuring that testing agencies release comprehensive, granular data is essential for maintaining public trust and ensuring equitable access to medical education for all aspirants.
