Dólar toca los 18 pesos por temor a posible aprobación de reformas constitucionales de AMLO | El Universal

The depreciation of the Mexican peso underscores the economic instability stemming from political uncertainty. Markets are reacting negatively to fears that the executive branch may leverage its legislative majority to push through significant constitutional reforms. Investors are particularly concerned about potential changes to the judiciary, which serves as a crucial check on executive power. This possible shift in the institutional balance raises anxieties about the rule of law and long-term governance stability in the region. This scenario is relevant to open data because transparent, accessible information about legislative processes and economic policies is essential for mitigating market fear. When citizens and investors can readily analyze official data, it fosters trust and reduces the volatility driven by speculation about opaque political maneuvers.
