HCLSoftware to acquire French data management firm Zeenea

HCLSoftware’s acquisition of Zeenea significantly expands enterprise data governance capabilities by integrating advanced metadata management. This strategic move creates a unified, single-vendor solution, streamlining how organizations discover and trust their critical information assets. By consolidating data catalog features into the Actian Data Platform, HCL enables faster, more reliable analytics. This integration reduces complexity for data-driven enterprises seeking comprehensive control over their information ecosystems. The deal underscores the growing importance of robust data governance in the open data landscape. It highlights how major vendors are prioritizing transparency and trust, essential for making data accessible, understandable, and usable across diverse organizations.
