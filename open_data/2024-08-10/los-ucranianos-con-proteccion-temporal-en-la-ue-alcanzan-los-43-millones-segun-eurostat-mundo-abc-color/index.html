Los ucranianos con protección temporal en la UE alcanzan los 4,3 millones, según Eurostat - Mundo - ABC Color

Los ucranianos con protección temporal en la UE alcanzan los 4,3 millones, según Eurostat - Mundo - ABC Color

Germany, Poland, and the Czech Republic host the largest number of Ukrainian displaced persons in the EU, although the Czech Republic stands out for its higher per capita reception ratio. This concentration reflects the capacity of Member States to absorb refugees, with significant increases in the main destinations over the past month, while the rest of the Union experiences a more balanced distribution relative to its local population. The profile of beneficiaries reveals a complex humanitarian crisis, dominated by civilians fleeing violence, with a notable presence of minors and a minority of adult men. The EU has strengthened its response by extending temporary protection until 2026, guaranteeing fundamental rights such as residence, employment, and education, although the actual implementation of these benefits varies among Member States. This article is crucial for open data because it illustrates the importance of harmonized Eurostat statistics for understanding cross-border migration flows. The analysis of demographic and geographic data enables the assessment of equity in refugee distribution and the measurement of the effectiveness of reception policies, facilitating evidence-based decision-making and promoting transparency in the management of international crises.
