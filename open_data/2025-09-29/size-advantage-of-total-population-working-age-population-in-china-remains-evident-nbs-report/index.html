Size advantage of total population, working-age population in China remains evident: NBS report

Size advantage of total population, working-age population in China remains evident: NBS report

The report highlights China’s sustained progress in household income, public services, and demographic stability during the 14th Five-Year Plan. Urbanization and educational attainment have risen significantly, while life expectancy and scientific literacy continue to improve. These trends underscore a shift toward higher-quality population development, reinforcing the country’s long-term human capital advantage and economic resilience despite global uncertainties. Employment outcomes remain a key success factor, with job creation efforts targeting vulnerable groups and maintaining overall stability. The expansion of social security, including the world’s largest pension coverage, illustrates a commitment to inclusive growth and social welfare. This broad-based support for well-being lays a foundational framework for future policy initiatives, ensuring that development translates into tangible improvements in citizens' daily lives and financial security. This data is critically relevant to open_data because it demonstrates the value of transparent, high-frequency statistical reporting in tracking societal progress. Open access to such detailed metrics enables researchers, policymakers, and civil society to analyze trends in health, education, and employment accurately. By making these indicators publicly available, authorities foster accountability and facilitate evidence-based decision-making, which is essential for addressing complex social challenges and measuring the effectiveness of public interventions.
