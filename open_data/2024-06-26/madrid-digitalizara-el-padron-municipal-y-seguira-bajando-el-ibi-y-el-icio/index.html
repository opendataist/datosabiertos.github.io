Madrid digitalizará el padrón municipal y seguirá bajando el IBI y el ICIO

Madrid will fully digitize municipal registration, enabling seamless online access. This shift toward digital administrative services directly supports open data goals by enhancing public accessibility and transparency. Simultaneously, the city plans to reduce property taxes and launch significant public job opportunities. These economic measures aim to stimulate local growth and improve citizen welfare through broader digital inclusion. Furthermore, the approval of the Urban Innovation Sandbox encourages experimental urban solutions. Such initiatives foster an environment where open data and technological innovation drive sustainable city development.
