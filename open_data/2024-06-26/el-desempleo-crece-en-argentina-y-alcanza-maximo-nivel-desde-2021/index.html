El desempleo crece en Argentina y alcanza máximo nivel desde 2021

Argentina’s unemployment rate has surged to its highest level since 2021, driven by a severe economic contraction and drastic fiscal adjustments. This sharp rise marks a significant reversal from previous lows, highlighting the immediate social cost of the government’s austerity measures and the resulting loss of purchasing power. Job losses have affected both the public and private sectors, with construction heavily impacted due to the halt in public works, while industry and commerce have also been affected. This broad downturn demonstrates how rapid economic policies can simultaneously disrupt formal and informal labor markets, as well as self-employment, creating widespread instability for workers across all economic categories. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it underscores the importance of transparent, timely, and accessible statistical information. Reliable public datasets enable researchers and citizens to accurately track the impacts of policies, fostering accountability and informed debate regarding the social consequences of economic reforms in real time.
