El INE eleva una décima el crecimiento de la economía española en el primer trimestre, hasta el 0,8%

El INE eleva una décima el crecimiento de la economía española en el primer trimestre, hasta el 0,8%

The National Statistics Institute (INE) confirms the robustness of the Spanish economy, with GDP growth exceeding expectations, primarily driven by external demand and investment in key sectors such as machinery and housing. This positive dynamic highlights the recent vigor of the market, placing Spain on an expansion trajectory that surpasses the growth rates of major European economies, reflecting improved business competitiveness and greater confidence in the macroeconomic environment. The structure of this growth appears balanced, with significant contributions from both domestic and external demand, although the emphasis differs depending on the time perspective. While the quarter-on-quarter momentum is based on exports and investment, the year-on-year advance is strongly supported by domestic consumption and industrial activity. This scenario suggests that the economy is not only recovering but also consolidating solid foundations for production and trade, benefiting multiple sectors ranging from construction to services. This report is relevant to open data, as it illustrates the value of official statistics published by public institutions for transparent economic analysis. The availability of disaggregated data by demand, supply, and employment enables researchers and citizens to assess economic health rigorously, fostering accountability and the ability to make informed decisions based on accessible, detailed, and evidence-based public information.
