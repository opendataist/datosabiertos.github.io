DPDP Act amends RTI to balance privacy with right to information

DPDP Act amends RTI to balance privacy with right to information

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act harmonizes the Right to Information with the fundamental right to privacy, resolving potential legal conflicts. By amending the RTI Act, the government ensures that transparency mechanisms coexist with privacy safeguards, aligning with Supreme Court jurisprudence on data protection. Access to personal information is not automatically barred but is determined by a public interest test. If the societal benefit of disclosure outweighs the potential harm to individual privacy, public authorities may still grant access. This nuanced approach prevents arbitrary restrictions while upholding constitutional rights. This development is crucial for open data initiatives, as it establishes a legal framework where data accessibility and privacy are not mutually exclusive. It demonstrates how regulatory updates can balance transparency with individual protections, offering a model for managing sensitive data in the digital era without stifling public accountability.
