Ola Krutrim unveils its AI vision for India; opens up its AI Cloud infra, rolls out mobile app

Krutrim introduces an indigenous AI platform designed to democratize access to artificial intelligence for Indian users by supporting numerous local languages and integrating seamlessly into daily workflows. This initiative seeks to reduce reliance on costly Western cloud infrastructure, empowering domestic developers to build scalable, culturally relevant products efficiently. The platform provides essential infrastructure like Model-as-a-Service and GPU resources, enabling creators to develop specialized tools without high entry barriers. By fostering local innovation, Krutrim aims to drive India’s technological sovereignty and economic growth through accessible, homegrown digital solutions. This development is crucial for open data ecosystems, as it establishes domestic alternatives to foreign data silos. It encourages the creation of localized datasets and models, ensuring that AI advancements reflect specific regional needs while promoting greater transparency and accessibility in technology deployment.
