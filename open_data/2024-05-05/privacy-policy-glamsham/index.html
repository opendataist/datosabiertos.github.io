Privacy Policy | Glamsham

This privacy policy outlines how Glamsham collects, uses, and protects personal data from website visitors. The company gathers voluntary user inputs, technical browsing data, and cookie information to maintain site security, enhance user experience, and deliver targeted advertising. By detailing these practices, the article highlights the standard operational mechanisms of modern web platforms, which serve as primary sources for scraping and analyzing digital footprints. The text emphasizes compliance with diverse global privacy regulations, including those in the European Economic Area, the United States, and India. It provides users with specific tools to manage consent, withdraw permissions, and exercise rights regarding their personal information. This focus on legal adherence and user control is critical for open data practitioners, as it defines the boundaries of lawful data collection and the necessity of respecting user autonomy when accessing public-facing web services. For the open data community, this document illustrates the complexity of extracting data from commercial websites due to evolving privacy frameworks and technical restrictions like cookie management. It underscores the importance of understanding consent mechanisms and data retention policies before utilizing web-scraping tools. Recognizing these constraints ensures that data collection efforts remain ethically sound and legally compliant, preventing the inadvertent violation of user privacy rights while building datasets from online sources.
