North East royal visit cost Durham Police more than £7,000

This article highlights the hidden financial burdens of royal engagements on public resources, revealing significant policing costs for Princess Anne’s local visits. The expenditure, covered by council tax, raises concerns about resource allocation when serious crimes struggle to attract police attention due to these security demands. The transparency issue is central, as detailed cost breakdowns are often obscured within broader budgets. Activists argue that the public remains unaware of these substantial expenses, which accumulate into millions nationally, potentially diverting funds from essential community safety services and creating an imbalance in local policing priorities. This matters to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible government spending records. Without clear disclosure, citizens cannot effectively assess how tax money is utilized for ceremonial duties versus public safety, highlighting the importance of FOI requests and data openness in holding authorities accountable for fiscal decisions.
