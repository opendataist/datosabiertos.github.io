María Amparo Casar: Inai reacciona a la divulgación de datos personales y ordena investigar

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information, and Personal Data Protection (INAI) condemns the Mexican Government’s public disclosure of sensitive personal data concerning María Amparo Casar, characterizing the act as a serious violation of data protection laws. By revealing her pension and insurance details without consent, the executive branch has failed its legal obligation to safeguard confidential information, prompting INAI to immediately launch an official investigation into these improper disclosures. This breach underscores the critical importance of protecting personal data within public institutions, as arbitrary exposure undermines privacy rights and legal compliance. The incident highlights how state actors must strictly adhere to confidentiality duties, ensuring that administrative actions do not turn individuals into targets of public defamation through unauthorized data sharing, which violates fundamental privacy protections enshrined in national legislation. The relevance to open data lies in the distinction between transparency and the preservation of privacy. While open data promotes accountability, it must never override individual rights or enable political harassment. This case serves as a stark reminder that ethical open data practices require rigorous adherence to legal frameworks, ensuring that information disclosure serves the public interest without compromising the security and dignity of private citizens.
