Bhavish Aggarwal's Krutrim AI launches mobile app, opens up cloud infra for businesses - ET Telecom

Bhavish Aggarwal's Krutrim AI launches mobile app, opens up cloud infra for businesses - ET Telecom

Krutrim AI challenges global tech giants by launching a consumer chatbot app and an open enterprise cloud platform. This initiative aims to reduce reliance on expensive Western infrastructure, offering localized, cost-effective solutions tailored specifically for Indian use cases. The platform provides developers and researchers with access to foundational and open-source models. By lowering entry barriers for technology creation, Krutrim seeks to democratize AI adoption within the region. This approach encourages local innovation while addressing the fragmentation often found in existing digital tools. This development is highly relevant to open_data as it promotes access to open-source models and infrastructure. By supporting developers with accessible computing resources, Krutrim fosters an environment where data and AI tools are less restricted. This aligns with the open data ethos of reducing dependency on proprietary systems and encouraging broader, more equitable technological participation.
