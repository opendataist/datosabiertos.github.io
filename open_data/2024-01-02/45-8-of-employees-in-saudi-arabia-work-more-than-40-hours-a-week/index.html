Geopolitical tensions between the US and Iran, coupled with uncertainty over potential US export bans, are driving oil prices higher. This underscores the interconnectedness of national policy decisions and international energy markets, where isolated actions have cascading effects. For open data, this situation demonstrates the critical need for transparent, real-time data sharing regarding energy stocks and trade flows. Accessible data enables better analysis of how geopolitical events influence market dynamics. It allows stakeholders to anticipate disruptions and understand the broader economic implications of policy shifts.
