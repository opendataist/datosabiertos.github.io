La tasa de ahorro de los hogares españoles cae al 11%

Household consumption in Spain increased significantly, while their savings capacity declined, reflecting a trend toward current spending at the expense of capital accumulation. This drop in the savings rate, its lowest level since early 2023, suggests that families are prioritizing immediate consumption over financial security, which could indicate reduced resilience to future economic fluctuations or unforeseen shocks. At the macroeconomic level, the national economy maintains a positive but declining financial surplus, driven by a deterioration in the external trade balance. Although national income has shown solid year-on-year growth, fueled by employment and business production, the country’s reduced financial surplus vis-à-vis the rest of the world raises questions about external sustainability and the competitiveness of Spanish exports in a changing global environment.
