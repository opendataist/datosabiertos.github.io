Chiapas, el estado con menos divorcios

Chiapas, el estado con menos divorcios

Chiapas recorded the lowest divorce rate in Mexico in 2025, contrasting with states such as Campeche and Nuevo León, which led the figures. Nationally, no-fault divorce was the predominant cause, reflecting significant changes in marital dynamics. The average age at separation indicates that these processes typically occur in middle age, affecting a considerable percentage of families with dependent children. This INEGI report is relevant to the open data community by demonstrating how official statistics capture complex social trends. Transparency in these data allows researchers and citizens to analyze demographic and legal patterns with precision. By accessing disaggregated information by gender and state, an informed public debate on the evolution of family structures is fostered. The availability of this information facilitates the evaluation of public policies and social services related to legal separation. By removing barriers to data access, society is empowered to better understand the causes and consequences of divorce. This openness promotes more efficient and responsive governance, aligned with the principles of transparency and sustainable use of government data.
