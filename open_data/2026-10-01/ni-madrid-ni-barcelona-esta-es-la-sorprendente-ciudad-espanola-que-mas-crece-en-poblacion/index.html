Ni Madrid ni Barcelona: esta es la sorprendente ciudad española que más crece en población

Torrevieja is consolidating itself as the Spanish municipality with more than 100,000 inhabitants that exhibits the highest demographic dynamism, surpassing major metropolises thanks to its attractive coastline and service sector. This growth, driven by international migration which constitutes the majority of its registered population, reflects a structural shift in which mid-sized coastal cities are acting as permanent residential hubs. This phenomenon offsets natural population aging and contrasts with the stagnant or declining populations of Spain’s inland regions. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the need to access and analyze up-to-date, municipality-level disaggregated population statistics. The National Statistics Institute (INE) provides crucial data that enable the identification of territorial inequalities and a deeper understanding of migratory dynamics, without relying on superficial interpretations. Transparency in these records facilitates academic research and evidence-based decision-making, demonstrating how the availability of open data enriches the socioeconomic understanding of Spain. Thus, the democratization of access to statistical information fosters a more informed public debate on population distribution. By being able to compare trends across different urban scales, citizens and public officials can more accurately assess the effectiveness of territorial cohesion policies. This approach underscores that the quality and accessibility of open data are fundamental to mapping contemporary urban realities.
