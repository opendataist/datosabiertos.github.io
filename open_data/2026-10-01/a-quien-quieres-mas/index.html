¿A quién quieres más?

¿A quién quieres más?

The text concludes that artificial intelligence should not be viewed as a competitor to humans, but rather as a complementary tool that, when combined with human empathy and judgment, optimizes decision-making without replacing our essential nature. This symbiotic vision is crucial for avoiding extremist narratives about AI, instead promoting a responsible integration that enhances operational efficiency while preserving human values. The relevance to open data lies in AI’s ability to process large volumes of information and detect complex patterns, transforming raw data into actionable insights. In sectors such as banking, this enables the personalization of services and improved security through predictive analytics, demonstrating how access to structured information and advanced computational capabilities can build trust and address individual needs more effectively. Finally, the need to regulate technological development through safety and ethical standards is emphasized, to prevent innovation from spiraling out of control. The social implications require society to set boundaries on the use of AI, ensuring that its application benefits the entire community. This reinforces the importance of managing data with transparency and responsibility, guaranteeing that technological progress serves human well-being and does not become an existential risk.
