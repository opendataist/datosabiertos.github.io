El padrón municipal de Segovia aumenta hasta los 54.241 habitantes

The municipal register of Segovia confirms sustained growth, approaching 55,000 inhabitants. This demographic trend is essential for adapting public services and urban planning to the actual needs of citizens. The article highlights the importance of the municipal register as a key administrative reference for sizing infrastructure, education, and mobility systems. Its data enable analysis of demographic dynamics and ensure that public policies effectively respond to urban and social expansion. This transparency in census data is fundamental to the open data movement. It provides a verifiable factual basis for accountability and independent analysis, demonstrating how structured public information facilitates more efficient municipal management tailored to social realities.
