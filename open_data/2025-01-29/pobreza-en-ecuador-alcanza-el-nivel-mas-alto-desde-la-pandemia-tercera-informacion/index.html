Pobreza en Ecuador alcanza el nivel más alto desde la pandemia - Tercera Información

Ecuador is experiencing rising poverty amid political and security crises, reaching levels not seen since the pandemic. This trend underscores the critical role of accurate, timely data in understanding socioeconomic shifts. Open data is essential for transparency, enabling citizens to monitor these alarming increases in extreme poverty and low incomes. Reliable statistics empower communities to demand accountability and informed policy responses during periods of instability.
