Alerta por la precariedad y parcialidad laboral en España a pesar del cierre en 2024

Alerta por la precariedad y parcialidad laboral en España a pesar del cierre en 2024

The 2024 labor market data reveals a significant improvement in employment figures, with record-high occupancy and a notable reduction in unemployment. However, the central conclusion is that this quantitative progress is undermined by persistent issues regarding job quality. While the number of jobs has increased, a substantial portion remains precarious, characterized by high turnover, temporary contracts, and low wages, which collectively hinder economic security and personal development for many workers. This dichotomy highlights a critical gap between statistical recovery and lived reality. The prevalence of unstable and part-time work creates social and economic insecurity, preventing individuals from accessing dignified housing or forming sustainable life projects. Consequently, the union argues that the current model perpetuates inequality and precarity, necessitating structural reforms. These include strategic investments in green industry, public services, and digital sectors to foster stable, high-quality employment opportunities rather than merely increasing the volume of jobs. This analysis is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the necessity of moving beyond headline metrics to explore nuanced socio-economic indicators. Open data platforms must facilitate access to granular information on job quality, contract types, and wage distribution to inform effective public policy. By making this deeper layer of data transparent, stakeholders can better address the root causes of labor precarity and evaluate the true impact of economic strategies on social well-being.
