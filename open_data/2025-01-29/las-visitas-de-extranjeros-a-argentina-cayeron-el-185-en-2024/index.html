Las visitas de extranjeros a Argentina cayeron el 18,5 % en 2024

Argentina experienced a significant decline in inbound tourism in 2024, driven by high inflation and a managed exchange rate that made services expensive for foreigners. Conversely, outbound travel surged as currency appreciation and rising salaries empowered residents to travel abroad more frequently. This divergence highlights the critical role of macroeconomic indicators in shaping mobility trends, demonstrating how currency valuation directly impacts both destination attractiveness and citizen purchasing power. For open data, these figures underscore the necessity of accessible, granular datasets linking economic metrics with tourism statistics. Understanding such correlations is essential for transparent policy-making and accurate market analysis in the public sector.
