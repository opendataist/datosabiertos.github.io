CSIF celebra datos en C-LM pero pide atajar "nichos de precariedad" como el paro femenino o las jornadas parciales

Unemployment fell significantly for women and young people, yet long-term joblessness surged. This highlights the urgent need to address structural weaknesses in the labor market. Policies must target temporary contracts and long-term unemployment to ensure quality jobs. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, which track labor trends. Transparent, accessible statistics enable analysts to identify disparities and advocate for inclusive, sustainable economic policies that benefit all demographics.
