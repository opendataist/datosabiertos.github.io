Quieren plan para pasar funciones

The article highlights the institutional transition of Mexico’s transparency institute to the Secretary of Anticorruption, emphasizing the need to protect citizens' right to information. This shift underscores the critical importance of maintaining robust open data infrastructures during governmental reorganizations to ensure continuity and integrity. By prioritizing secure technological transfers, such as cryptographic validators for national platforms, the process aims to safeguard public access tools. This approach is vital for open data, as it ensures that transparency mechanisms remain reliable and secure despite structural changes, preserving the public’s ability to hold authorities accountable effectively. Ultimately, the integration of transparency functions into a new ministry signals a potential redefinition of how public information is managed. For open data advocates, this presents both a risk to institutional independence and an opportunity to strengthen data accessibility if proper safeguards are implemented during the transition period.
