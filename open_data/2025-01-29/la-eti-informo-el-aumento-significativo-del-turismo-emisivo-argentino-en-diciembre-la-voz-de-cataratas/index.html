La ETI informó el aumento significativo del turismo emisivo argentino, en diciembre - La Voz de Cataratas

La ETI informó el aumento significativo del turismo emisivo argentino, en diciembre - La Voz de Cataratas

Argentina’s tourism data reveals a growing trend of citizens traveling abroad, while foreign arrivals are declining. This shift has resulted in a negative balance in international visitors for the month. The statistics highlight a strong preference among Argentine travelers for neighboring countries. At the same time, fewer non-residents are entering the country compared to previous periods. This information is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how accessible statistical records help monitor economic and social mobility trends. It enables researchers to analyze travel patterns and their impact on local economies through transparent, publicly available datasets.
