Los economistas elevan la previsión de crecimiento del PIB hasta el 3% en 2025

Los economistas elevan la previsión de crecimiento del PIB hasta el 3% en 2025

The article highlights a significant upward revision in Spain’s economic growth forecast for 2025, driven by robust domestic demand, strong labor market performance, and recent data updates from official statistical bodies. Key indicators such as consumer spending and employment levels confirm the economy's resilience compared to the Eurozone, although concerns persist regarding a sharp decline in foreign direct investment amidst geopolitical and trade uncertainties. Simultaneously, inflation is moderating, with expectations settling at a lower annual rate due to stabilizing energy costs and steady underlying inflation. The labor market continues to show positive trends, characterized by record numbers of self-employed workers and reduced unemployment rates, particularly benefiting younger and older demographics. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it exemplifies how transparent, timely, and high-quality public statistics enable reliable economic forecasting and policy assessment. Access to these verified datasets allows analysts to detect emerging trends, validate institutional predictions, and foster trust in public administration by revealing both the strengths of the domestic economy and the vulnerabilities in external investment flows.
