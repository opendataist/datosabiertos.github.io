Chiapas es una potencia ganadera en la República

Chiapas es una potencia ganadera en la República

Chiapas ranks among the top beef-producing states in the country, driven by recent improvements in animal health, genetics, and productivity. This growth underscores the critical need for modernized infrastructure and certified sanitary practices to maintain competitiveness in the face of climate and disease challenges. The sector’s future success depends on integrating new technologies and training producers to meet international market standards. These efforts are essential for expanding beyond domestic markets and leveraging the state’s substantial cattle inventory and natural resources. This case is vital for open data, as it demonstrates how transparent statistical reporting from institutions such as INEGI enables evidence-based policymaking. Accessible data empowers stakeholders to identify gaps, justify investments, and design effective strategies that support sustainable agricultural development and integration into global markets.
