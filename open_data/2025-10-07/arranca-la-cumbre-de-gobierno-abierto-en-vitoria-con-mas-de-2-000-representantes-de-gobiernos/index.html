Arranca la Cumbre de Gobierno Abierto en Vitoria con más de 2.000 representantes de gobiernos

Arranca la Cumbre de Gobierno Abierto en Vitoria con más de 2.000 representantes de gobiernos

The ninth Global Open Government Summit in Vitoria-Gasteiz marks a significant milestone in international cooperation for transparency and democratic governance. Hosted by Spain as part of its presidency alongside civil society representatives, the event unites thousands of global leaders to share best practices in openness, accountability, and civic participation. This gathering reinforces the multilateral nature of open government policies, emphasizing that these principles are essential for building trust between institutions and citizens worldwide. A central theme of the summit is the integration of digital transformation and artificial intelligence into public administration. Leaders are debating how to harness technology to enhance transparency, protect digital rights, and ensure resilient public services. By addressing issues such as content moderation and digital infrastructure, the event highlights the critical role of technology in modernizing governance. This focus ensures that open data initiatives remain relevant and effective in an increasingly digital society, bridging the gap between policy innovation and practical implementation. The culmination of these discussions is the Vitoria-Gasteiz Declaration, which reaffirms the alliance’s commitment to human rights and good governance. This article is highly relevant to open data as it underscores the institutional framework necessary for data to be truly open and useful. By linking technical digital advancements with political commitment, the summit illustrates that open data is not just a technical standard but a foundational element of a transparent, participatory, and accountable democracy.
