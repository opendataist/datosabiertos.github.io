La compra de vivienda por parte de extranjeros marca máximos históricos: ¿qué zonas de España prefieren?

The Spanish real estate market showed renewed dynamism in 2022, marked by a significant surge in international investment and the emergence of new transaction models. Foreign buyers regained market share after a period of decline, particularly targeting coastal areas and high-value properties. This shift underscores the enduring strength of external demand and Spain’s attractiveness as a destination for global capital, reflecting evolving geopolitical and economic trends in property acquisition. At the same time, the market experienced a dramatic rise in “nudo” ownership transactions—a practice that allows sellers to liquidate their assets while retaining lifelong residency rights. This trend highlights a strategic adaptation to demographic shifts, as older populations seek financial liquidity without giving up their homes. The rapid growth in these deals points to a structural change in how property value is accessed, offering buyers discounted entry points while providing sellers with flexible retirement solutions. This data is crucial for the open data community, as it illustrates the complexity of modern real estate ecosystems beyond simple price metrics. By tracking nuanced indicators such as the origins of foreign investors and unconventional property rights, open data initiatives can provide deeper insights into housing affordability, migration patterns, and economic resilience. Such granular, publicly available statistics enable better policy formulation and empower researchers to analyze the interplay between demographic aging, international investment, and housing market stability.
