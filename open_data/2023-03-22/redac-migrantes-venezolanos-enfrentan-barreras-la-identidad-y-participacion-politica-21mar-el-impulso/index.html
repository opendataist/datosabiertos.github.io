▷ Redac: Migrantes venezolanos enfrentan barreras la identidad y participación política #21Mar - El Impulso

The report reveals that Venezuelan migrants and refugees face severe structural barriers in exercising their right to identity and political participation. High costs and administrative delays hinder access to essential identification documents, while restrictive electoral regulations and broken diplomatic ties prevent meaningful political engagement abroad. Crucially, the study highlights that the mere presence of a consulate does not guarantee service accessibility. Since no visited consulates currently allow voter registration, this systemic failure fundamentally violates political rights, creating a significant obstacle to democratic participation for the diaspora. This finding is vital to open data because it exposes how opaque bureaucratic processes and lack of transparent reporting mask human rights violations. Transparent, accessible data on migration procedures and consular service availability is essential to hold institutions accountable, ensuring that marginalized populations can verify their rights and demand equitable treatment globally.
