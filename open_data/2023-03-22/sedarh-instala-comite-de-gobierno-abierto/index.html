Sedarh instala comité de Gobierno abierto

Sedarh instala comité de Gobierno abierto

San Luis Potosí establishes a new Open Government Committee within the agricultural department to enhance transparency and accountability in the management of public resources. This body integrates citizens into project evaluation through technological innovation, fostering a participatory approach that aligns with the state’s development goals for responsible governance. The creation of this committee underscores the government’s commitment to regulatory improvement and open data principles by mandating public access to information. By involving representatives of civil society, it ensures that agricultural projects are transparent and responsive to community needs, thereby reinforcing trust between the state and producers. This initiative is relevant to open data as it institutionalizes mechanisms for public oversight and data-driven decision-making. It demonstrates how governments can leverage open information frameworks to improve service delivery and ensure that citizens actively engage in monitoring and evaluating public sector performance in critical sectors such as agriculture.
