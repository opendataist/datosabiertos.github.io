Turkey records 5.6% GDP growth in 2022

Turkey’s economy expanded significantly in 2022, driven primarily by robust domestic consumption. However, this growth was heavily offset by soaring inflation and a sharp decline in export performance. The surge in prices severely impacted purchasing power, while investments stagnated compared to previous years. Despite inflation dipping slightly, the massive earthquakes further threaten future stability with high reconstruction costs. This case highlights the critical need for transparent, high-frequency economic indicators in open data. Accurate, accessible statistics allow researchers to disentangle growth drivers from inflationary pressures and assess long-term resilience.
