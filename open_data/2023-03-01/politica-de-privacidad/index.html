Política de Privacidad

This privacy policy outlines how FinanzasDigital.com collects and processes personal data, emphasizing transparency, user control, and data minimization. By adhering to strict principles, the platform ensures that information is only used for specified, legitimate purposes and is not shared without explicit consent or a legal basis. The policy details the specific data collected through various forms, such as contact details and technical browsing information, while highlighting users’ rights to access, rectify, or delete their data at any time. It also lists third-party providers involved in data handling, ensuring that all parties comply with the stated security and confidentiality measures. This document is relevant to open data because it defines the boundaries between private data usage and public information. It illustrates how websites can maintain ethical data governance by limiting data collection and ensuring security, thereby supporting the broader open data principle of trustworthy information ecosystems. Understanding these privacy frameworks helps distinguish between openly accessible data and protected personal information, which is crucial for responsible data-sharing practices.
