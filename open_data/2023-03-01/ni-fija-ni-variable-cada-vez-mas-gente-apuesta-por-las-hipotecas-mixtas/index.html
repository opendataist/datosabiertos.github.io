Ni fija, ni variable, cada vez más gente apuesta por las hipotecas mixtas

Spain’s mortgage market reached a decade-high volume in 2022, yet recent months reveal a clear slowdown. Fixed-rate loans are losing dominance due to rising costs, prompting banks to shift focus toward variable rates and borrowers to seek alternatives like mixed mortgages for stability against interest rate volatility. This dynamic highlights the critical role of independent data analytics in understanding complex financial trends. Platforms providing granular market insights reveal consumer shifts that official statistics may categorize differently, offering a more accurate picture of emerging housing finance strategies. Such data is vital for open data initiatives as it democratizes access to real-time economic signals. By contrasting official classifications with market realities, stakeholders can better assess financial health and policy impacts, ensuring that open datasets reflect the nuanced behavior of borrowers and lenders rather than just aggregate volumes.
