ATE Indec calculó que los salarios estatales necesitan un urgente aumento del 60% en una cuota - Primera Fuente

ATE Indec calculó que los salarios estatales necesitan un urgente aumento del 60% en una cuota - Primera Fuente

The article highlights a severe erosion of purchasing power driven by high inflation, necessitating urgent government intervention to support vulnerable workers. This situation underscores the critical role of official statistics in revealing economic realities, as ATE-Indec data exposes the gap between required minimum incomes and actual wages. Open data becomes essential here by providing the transparency needed to validate claims about inflation and salary losses. Without accessible, reliable official metrics, identifying such disparities would be difficult, hindering evidence-based policy debates. Consequently, this report demonstrates how open statistical data empowers civil society to demand accountability. It illustrates that transparent data is not merely informational but a fundamental tool for social justice, enabling unions to substantiate their demands for equitable economic measures effectively.
