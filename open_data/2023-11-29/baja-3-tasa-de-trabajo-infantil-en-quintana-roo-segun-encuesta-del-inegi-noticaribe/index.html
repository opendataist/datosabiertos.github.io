Baja 3% tasa de trabajo infantil en Quintana Roo, según encuesta del Inegi - Noticaribe

Quintana Roo significantly reduced child labor, falling well below the national average. This local progress contrasts with a national reversal caused by the pandemic, during which overall cases increased due to economic pressures. Despite this setback, the data highlights the vulnerability of minors in agriculture and domestic work, underscoring the urgent need for policy interventions to protect children from exploitative labor conditions. The article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent statistical indicators from institutions such as INEGI enable rigorous monitoring of social progress. By making these metrics publicly available, stakeholders can track trends, compare regional performances, and hold governments accountable for protecting human rights. Open access to such data is essential for designing effective strategies to eradicate child labor and fostering informed public debate on social welfare.
