El turismo volvió a dar un balance negativo, esta vez de 153.500 visitantes en septiembre - La Voz de Cataratas

Argentina recorded a net tourist deficit, highlighting significant imbalances in cross-border mobility data. This discrepancy underscores the necessity for standardized, transparent open data to accurately measure economic impacts and regional tourism flows. Reliable statistics are crucial for policymakers to understand travel patterns and optimize infrastructure investments effectively. The predominance of neighboring countries in both incoming and outgoing flows reveals strong regional integration. Open access to such granular mobility metrics enables better strategic planning for transport networks. Consequently, publishing detailed, disaggregated travel data empowers stakeholders to analyze trends and improve cross-border cooperation.
