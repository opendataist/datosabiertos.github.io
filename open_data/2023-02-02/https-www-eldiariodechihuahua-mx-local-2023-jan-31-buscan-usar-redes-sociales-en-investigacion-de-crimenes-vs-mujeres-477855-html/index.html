Authorities are incorporating digital evidence from social media into investigations of gender-based crimes. This shift acknowledges technology as both a tool used to commit offenses and a crucial source of evidence for judicial proceedings. Collaboration between specialized prosecutors and forensic analysts enhances the handling of complex cases such as femicide and human trafficking. Continuous training ensures that investigators can effectively leverage this technological evidence in their work. This development is vital for open data, as it underscores the need for transparent standards in the processing and sharing of digital evidence. It highlights how public institutions must adapt their data frameworks to support justice through modern technological means.
