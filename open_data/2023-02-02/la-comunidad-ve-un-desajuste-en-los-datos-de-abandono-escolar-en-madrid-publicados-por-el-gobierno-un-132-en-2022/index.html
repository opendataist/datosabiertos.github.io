La Comunidad ve un "desajuste" en los datos de abandono escolar en Madrid publicados por el Gobierno, un 13,2% en 2022

The Community of Madrid disputes the Ministry of Education’s report on school dropout rates, citing significant methodological inconsistencies in the data from the National Statistics Institute (INE). The regional government argues that the sudden statistical spikes lack logical explanation and historical correlation, suggesting potential errors in sampling or estimation processes that do not appear to have been applied uniformly across Spain. This dispute highlights the critical importance of data transparency and methodological rigor in public statistics. By formally requesting technical details and proposing a joint review, Madrid emphasizes that accurate, comparable data is essential for effective policymaking. Without standardized and verifiable metrics, regional efforts to meet European Union targets may be undermined by unreliable national figures. The article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the real-world consequences of data quality issues. It underscores the need for transparent statistical methodologies and collaborative verification mechanisms. When official datasets contain unexplained anomalies, they erode trust in public institutions and hinder evidence-based governance, reinforcing the value of accessible, auditable, and consistently applied data standards.
