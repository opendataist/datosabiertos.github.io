Patricio Jaca, de Thales Cono Sur: La protección de los datos es una prioridad

The article emphasizes that protecting personal data is a collective responsibility, requiring both individual vigilance and robust corporate infrastructure. It highlights the need for users to adopt secure digital practices, such as using strong, unique passwords and avoiding untrusted websites, while corporations must implement centralized security platforms to manage complex risks in a digitally transformed environment. From an open data perspective, this interview underscores the critical tension between data utility and privacy. It illustrates that open data initiatives cannot succeed without rigorous governance frameworks. The discussion on encryption technologies and zero-trust models demonstrates how technical safeguards are essential to maintaining public trust, ensuring that data sharing remains safe and compliant with evolving legal standards. Legislative references to the GDPR and local laws such as Argentina’s Habeas Data reinforce the importance of regulatory compliance in data management. The relevance to open data lies in establishing clear boundaries: while data must be accessible to drive innovation, its use must be strictly regulated to prevent misuse. This balance ensures that the benefits of transparency and digitalization are realized without compromising individual rights or security.
