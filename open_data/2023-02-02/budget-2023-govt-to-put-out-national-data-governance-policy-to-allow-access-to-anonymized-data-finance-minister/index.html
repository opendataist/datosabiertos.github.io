Budget 2023 | Govt to put out National Data Governance Policy to allow access to “anonymized data”: Finance Minister

The Indian government is finalizing a National Data Governance Policy designed to unlock innovation by providing startups and academia with access to anonymized, non-personal data held by public entities. This initiative aims to foster research and development by removing barriers to critical information, such as weather and climate datasets, thereby stimulating economic growth and technological advancement without compromising individual privacy. Previously, broader proposals faced significant resistance from major tech firms and policy experts who argued that mandatory data sharing from private companies was premature for India’s emerging digital economy. Consequently, the current framework has shifted away from compulsory corporate data extraction, instead focusing on high-level principles and government-held information. This approach addresses industry concerns while establishing a more sustainable foundation for data governance that balances stakeholder interests with developmental goals. This development is crucial for open_data advocates as it formalizes state commitment to transparency and accessibility of public-sector information. By clarifying the boundaries between personal privacy protections and non-personal data utility, the policy sets a precedent for ethical data stewardship. It demonstrates how governments can leverage open data ecosystems to drive innovation while maintaining robust legal frameworks, offering a model for balancing security with the widespread sharing of informational assets.
