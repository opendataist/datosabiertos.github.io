Legal affairs division to carry out public research on Freedom of Information Act

Malaysia is surveying public opinion to draft a Freedom of Information Act, aiming to enhance government transparency and open access to public records. This initiative directly advances open data principles by prioritizing citizen engagement in shaping legislative frameworks. It represents a critical step toward institutionalizing the right to access information and fostering accountability.
