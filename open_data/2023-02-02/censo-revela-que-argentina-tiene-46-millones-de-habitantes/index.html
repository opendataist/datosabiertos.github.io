Censo revela que Argentina tiene 46 millones de habitantes

Argentina’s 2022 Census reveals a population of over 46 million, marking a significant demographic shift from 2010. A pivotal innovation was the transition to a digital data collection method, which achieved high participation rates. This success demonstrates the viability of modern, technology-driven methodologies for national statistical agencies, highlighting a crucial step toward more efficient data gathering. The census achieved exceptional coverage nationwide, surpassing 98 percent of segments. International standards consider this level of completeness extremely high, underscoring the robustness of the data obtained. This high-quality, comprehensive dataset serves as a vital benchmark for evaluating the effectiveness of digital infrastructure in public sector operations and large-scale administrative processes. This release is relevant to open data as it provides a fundamental, accurate baseline for societal analysis. The shift to digital methods sets a precedent for future transparency and accessibility of statistical information. By establishing a reliable, widely accessible dataset, it enables researchers, policymakers, and the public to leverage detailed demographic insights for evidence-based decision-making and informed civic engagement.
