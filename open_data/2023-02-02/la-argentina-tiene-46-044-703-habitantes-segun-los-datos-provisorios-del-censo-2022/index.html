La Argentina tiene 46.044.703 habitantes, según los datos provisorios del Censo 2022

The recent release of the 2022 Census results by Argentina’s National Institute of Statistics and Censuses marks a pivotal shift in how national demographic data is collected and used. The data reveal a population of approximately 46 million, showing steady growth over the past decade, along with a notable demographic trend toward an older population and a higher proportion of women. These insights are crucial for understanding societal changes, such as declining fertility rates and increased life expectancy, which directly impact public policy planning and resource allocation across the country. More significantly, this census represents a major methodological transition by integrating digital self-registration with traditional field methods, achieving a high territorial coverage rate. This hybrid approach not only validated the success of digital tools introduced during the pandemic but also established a foundation for future statistical operations. The integration of these new data sources with administrative records aims to create a harmonized population registry, enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of future censuses while setting a new standard for technological innovation in public statistics. This article is highly relevant to the open data community because it exemplifies the evolution of government transparency through modernization. The successful implementation of digital census tools and the subsequent release of detailed, geographically specific data demonstrate how open access to granular information can improve accountability and enable better civic analysis. It highlights the importance of accessible, high-quality open datasets in fostering informed public debate and supporting evidence-based decision-making in both the public and private sectors.
