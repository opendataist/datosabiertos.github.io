FM announces National Data Governance Policy to enable access to anonymised data

The government’s new National Data Governance Policy prioritizes anonymized data access to drive innovation, deliberately avoiding the monetization provisions that previously sparked public backlash. This shift establishes a secure framework for researchers and startups to utilize non-personal datasets without compromising individual privacy. By facilitating open access to anonymized information, the policy aims to foster a robust environment for academic and entrepreneurial growth. It encourages private entities to contribute data, creating a collaborative ecosystem that leverages public information for broader societal and technological advancement. This approach is vital for open data advocacy as it balances transparency with privacy protection. It demonstrates how governments can release valuable datasets safely, empowering the open data community to build applications and conduct research that benefit the public while respecting citizen confidentiality.
