OSCE strengthens open data ecosystem in Uzbekistan

The Open Data Challenge in Uzbekistan demonstrates how leveraging public information fosters good governance and economic growth. By enabling citizens to create technological solutions, the initiative significantly increases transparency in public authorities. This openness establishes essential public control mechanisms that directly contribute to reducing corruption and promoting sustainable development. The event highlights the practical value of open data ecosystems by supporting the development of innovative digital tools. Participants addressed critical societal issues, including anti-corruption monitoring and domestic violence assistance, using available datasets. This approach proves that open data has tangible social and economic potential when integrated into professional and public sector workflows. This narrative is relevant to open data as it showcases a successful model for building a vibrant community around data usage. It illustrates how structured competitions and training can transform raw government information into actionable civic solutions. Ultimately, it emphasizes the importance of cultivating expertise and transparency to drive both accountability and innovation within the open data landscape.
