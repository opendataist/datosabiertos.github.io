Economía de México crece 3% en 2022 apoyada en todos sus sectores, según reporte preliminar

Mexico’s economy expanded in 2022, driven by the industrial, agricultural, and service sectors, although this growth represented a slowdown compared to the post-pandemic recovery of the previous year. The increase was largely fueled by strong U.S. demand for exports, robust tourism, and record remittances, underscoring the country’s deep economic dependence on its northern neighbor. Experts project a significant deceleration in 2023, anticipating growth between 0.5 and 1.0 percent. This slowdown is attributed to the expected deceleration of the U.S. economy, which reduces demand for Mexican exports, combined with restrictive domestic monetary conditions that dampen dynamism in the internal market. This report is relevant to open data as it relies on preliminary statistics published by Mexico’s National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). It demonstrates how transparent access to official sectoral data enables independent economic analysis and forecasting by third-party experts, showcasing the practical value of open government statistics for understanding complex macroeconomic trends.
