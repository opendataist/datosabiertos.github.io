Fáilte Ireland to manage short-term lets register

Fáilte Ireland to manage short-term lets register

The government proposes a mandatory register for short-term lets to combat housing shortages and ensure regulatory compliance. By requiring hosts to display registration numbers, authorities aim to create a transparent inventory of tourist accommodation, directly supporting open data initiatives by centralizing housing market information. New legislation introduces strict enforcement mechanisms, including significant fines for non-compliant platforms and bans on commercial short-term letting permits in towns exceeding specific population thresholds. This framework shifts control toward local oversight, ensuring that transient rentals do not disproportionately erode long-term residential supply in high-demand areas. Opposition critics argue that arbitrary population thresholds undermine effective planning, calling for broader administrative powers for local authorities. This debate highlights the critical role of accessible, comprehensive data in shaping equitable housing policies, demonstrating how open data transparency is essential for balancing tourism interests with the preservation of affordable housing stocks.
