INEGI sigue de cerca proceso legislativo para absorber Coneval | Periodico Hidrocalido Digital

The INEGI is closely monitoring legislative reforms that could transfer Coneval’s responsibilities, including poverty measurement, to its organization. This potential consolidation highlights critical discussions about the centralization of public data evaluation and the institutional capacity to manage comprehensive social metrics in Mexico. Emphasizing its robust experience in producing timely and accurate statistics, the Institute underscores the value of collaboration with the private sector. Such partnerships demonstrate how open data initiatives can leverage external expertise to enhance national indicators, serving as a reference for transparency and economic analysis. This situation is relevant to open data because it raises questions about how autonomous information generation interacts with government structures. Ensuring the continuity and independence of statistical data is vital for maintaining trust in public metrics and supporting informed decision-making across society.
