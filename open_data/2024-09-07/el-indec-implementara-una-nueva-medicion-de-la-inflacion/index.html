El Indec implementará una nueva medición de la inflación

El Indec implementará una nueva medición de la inflación

INDEC is implementing a new inflation measurement methodology within two months to reflect current consumption patterns, replacing the outdated 2004 framework. This crucial update aims to restore public trust by aligning statistical data with real economic behaviors through expanded product sampling and revised weighting. The relevance to open data lies in the need for transparent, up-to-date methodologies. Accurate, publicly accessible data structures are essential for users to correctly interpret metrics, ensuring that open datasets remain meaningful and reliable for societal analysis.
