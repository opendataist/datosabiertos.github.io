Evo Morales sugiere una auditoría para "bajar la tensión" ante las dudas por los resultados del censo

Evo Morales sugiere una auditoría para "bajar la tensión" ante las dudas por los resultados del censo

Former President Evo Morales has questioned the recent census results released by Bolivia’s National Institute of Statistics, pointing to significant discrepancies between the official data and earlier projections. He has called for a transparent audit conducted with international oversight to help ease social tensions and restore public confidence in the statistical information. This situation underscores the critical importance of data transparency and accountability in governance. Open data initiatives must ensure methodological rigor and accessibility to prevent public distrust and social unrest. The controversy highlights the need for independent verification mechanisms in national statistics. It serves as a reminder that credible open data requires robust oversight to maintain its integrity and societal relevance.
