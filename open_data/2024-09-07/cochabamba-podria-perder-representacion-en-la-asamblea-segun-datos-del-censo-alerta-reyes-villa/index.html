Cochabamba podría perder representación en la Asamblea según datos del Censo, alerta Reyes Villa

Cochabamba podría perder representación en la Asamblea según datos del Censo, alerta Reyes Villa

The mayor of Cochabamba disputes preliminary census data, arguing that the reported population decline contradicts local infrastructure records and municipal projections. He intends to challenge these figures by demonstrating higher density in housing units, aligning with other major Bolivian cities that question the methodology of the national statistics institute. This situation is highly relevant to open data, as it highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible, and verifiable public datasets. When official statistics are contested, the lack of granular, open-source evidence prevents stakeholders from independently validating results. Trust in data systems erodes when institutions cannot provide clear, accessible technical justification for their figures, underscoring the importance of open data practices in maintaining public confidence and enabling accountability.
