Salesforce comprará la startup de seguridad de datos Own por 1.900 millones de dólares

Salesforce’s acquisition of Own Company underscores the critical need for robust data protection in the era of artificial intelligence. By integrating advanced security tools, Salesforce aims to strengthen its platform’s privacy and compliance offerings, addressing growing client concerns over data loss from system failures and cyber threats. This strategic move highlights the increasing importance of data governance within major software ecosystems. It demonstrates how leading providers must evolve beyond basic storage to offer comprehensive protection mechanisms, ensuring that sensitive information remains secure against both human error and malicious attacks. For the open data community, this deal illustrates the tension between data accessibility and security. As organizations push for greater data openness, the necessity for rigorous protection frameworks becomes paramount, showing that open data initiatives must be underpinned by strong, reliable security infrastructure to maintain trust and integrity.
