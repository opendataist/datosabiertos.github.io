Delhi HC orders ED to disclose information on sexual harassment case under RTI Act

The Delhi High Court mandates that the Enforcement Directorate must disclose information regarding sexual harassment allegations under the RTI Act. This ruling establishes that such disclosures constitute human rights exceptions, overriding general exemptions for security agencies. The decision reinforces accountability by ensuring transparency in sensitive institutional matters. It signifies that national security exemptions cannot shield organizations from disclosing details when fundamental human rights are allegedly compromised, setting a critical precedent for administrative compliance. This is highly relevant to open data advocates as it challenges opaque government practices. It demonstrates how legal frameworks can force the release of withheld information, promoting broader accessibility to public records and strengthening the principle that citizens have the right to know when rights are violated.
