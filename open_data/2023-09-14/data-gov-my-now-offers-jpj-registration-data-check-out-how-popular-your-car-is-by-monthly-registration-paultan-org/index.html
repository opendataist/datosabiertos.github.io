data.gov.my now offers JPJ registration data - check out how popular your car is by monthly registration - paultan.org

The inclusion of comprehensive vehicle registration data in Malaysia’s open government portal transforms raw statistics into accessible public intelligence. By making historical registration trends available, this dataset empowers analysts to track market shifts and consumer preferences over decades, fostering greater transparency and informed decision-making within the automotive sector. The data reveals a consistent dominance of affordable local brands, highlighting the enduring popularity of national sedans while identifying specific non-national models that succeed in this competitive landscape. This long-term perspective allows stakeholders to understand the cyclical nature of car popularity and the impact of model launches on sales volumes without being misled by manufacturing dates versus registration dates. Relevance to open data lies in the portal’s potential to drive innovation through independent analysis. When citizens and researchers can freely explore such detailed historical records, it encourages the creation of valuable insights that extend beyond official narratives, ultimately promoting a more data-literate society and encouraging the development of new tools based on publicly available information.
