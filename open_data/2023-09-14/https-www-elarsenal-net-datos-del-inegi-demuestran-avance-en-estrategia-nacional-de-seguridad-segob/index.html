The article highlights the significant decline in crime victimization and perceived insecurity in Mexico, as revealed by the latest Envipe survey conducted by INEGI. This data indicates a historic low in reported victims over the past decade, suggesting that current security strategies are effectively reducing various types of offenses, including homicide and robbery. Government officials emphasize the relevance of these findings, noting that the independent statistical agency confirms a downward trend in criminal activity. The results also show high public trust in the armed forces and the National Guard, alongside a marked decrease in perceptions of corruption. These insights provide a comprehensive view of public safety improvements beyond official government reports. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how independent, publicly available statistical data can serve as an objective benchmark for evaluating public policy. By transparently sharing survey results, institutions like INEGI enable citizens and analysts to verify official claims, fostering accountability and allowing for evidence-based discussions on security strategies and institutional performance.
