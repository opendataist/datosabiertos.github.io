Open Data
ES EN

The Yale Open Data Access Project proves that responsible clinical trial data sharing significantly accelerates scientific progress. By overcoming historical barriers to data accessibility, the initiative enables numerous peer-reviewed publications across diverse medical fields. This demonstrates that open science frameworks can effectively leverage research contributions and improve the clinical research ecosystem. Initiated over a decade ago by Yale researchers, the project addresses the critical issue of inaccessible trial data that previously hindered independent verification. Through collaboration with major funders and pharmaceutical companies, YODA has established a sustainable model for responsible data dissemination. This approach fosters a culture of transparency and trust within the scientific community. This article is highly relevant to open_data as it validates the practical utility of shared datasets in advancing medical knowledge. It highlights how open repositories can transform clinical science by enabling reproducibility and new analyses. The success of YODA serves as a compelling case study for the broader adoption of open data principles in regulated industries.
