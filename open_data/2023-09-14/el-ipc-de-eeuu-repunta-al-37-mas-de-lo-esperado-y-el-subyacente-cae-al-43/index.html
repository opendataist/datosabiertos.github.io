El IPC de EEUU repunta al 3,7%, más de lo esperado, y el subyacente cae al 4,3%

The recent rise in overall inflation in the United States, driven primarily by energy prices, has generated expectations regarding the Federal Reserve’s monetary policy. However, analysts agree that underlying inflation remains moderate, reducing the likelihood of imminent further interest rate hikes. This dynamic suggests that the central bank will focus on more stable structural indicators before making definitive decisions about the future path of interest rates. The relevance of this report to the field of open data lies in the quality and transparency of public statistics as a foundation for accountability and decision-making. Free and timely access to these macroeconomic data enables citizens, researchers, and the media to critically analyze the impact of inflation on everyday life. This fosters a more informed society capable of questioning the effectiveness of economic policies and demanding clarity in the management of public resources. Moreover, the open availability of detailed time series facilitates the development of predictive models and visualization tools by civil society. By democratizing access to official information, it enhances the capacity to monitor long-term trends, such as the evolution of housing or energy prices. This process empowers citizens to actively participate in public debate, transforming technical data into shared knowledge that influences political opinion and democratic oversight.
