Israeli population pushes 10 million as Jewish New Year approaches

Israel’s population is projected to surpass ten million by late 2024, marking a significant demographic milestone that will continue rising sharply toward sixteen million by 2048. This growth is driven by natural increase and immigration, reflecting broader societal shifts in age, religion, and life expectancy across its diverse communities. The data reveals a complex social fabric where religious observance varies widely among Jewish citizens, while naming trends highlight both cultural continuity and diversity among newborns. These statistics provide essential insights into the changing composition and future trajectory of the state’s citizenry. This report is vital to open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible government statistics empower researchers and policymakers to analyze long-term demographic trends. Such open access enables evidence-based planning for infrastructure, social services, and integration strategies in a rapidly growing nation.
