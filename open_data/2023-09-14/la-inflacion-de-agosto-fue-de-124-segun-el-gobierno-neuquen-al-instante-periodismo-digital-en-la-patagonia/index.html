La inflación de agosto fue de 12,4% según el gobierno | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

The consumer price index reveals an unprecedented surge in inflation, highlighting severe economic instability. This hyperinflationary trend directly impacts the quality of life and purchasing power, demonstrating how macroeconomic policies and external pressures can rapidly erode public welfare. Analysis of specific sectors shows that essential goods such as food, healthcare, and home maintenance have experienced the sharpest price increases. These disparities underscore the disproportionate burden placed on vulnerable households, making accurate price tracking critical for social protection and economic planning. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because transparent, accessible, and timely statistical information is essential for public accountability. Open datasets enable citizens, researchers, and journalists to verify government claims, analyze the drivers of inflation, and demand policy changes, ensuring that economic crises are understood and addressed through evidence-based decision-making.
