'I was very disappointed with myself': Charlottetown police chief reacts to commissioner's criticism | CBC News

'I was very disappointed with myself': Charlottetown police chief reacts to commissioner's criticism | CBC News

Charlottetown Police Services launched an internal review after officials ignored information requests, violating privacy laws. This administrative failure highlights the critical need for transparency in law enforcement agencies to uphold public trust. The incident underscores how institutional non-compliance undermines the principles of open data. By failing to respond, police hindered accountability and access to information, demonstrating why strict adherence to open data standards is essential. Restoring compliance ensures police operations remain accessible and accountable to the public. This case serves as a vital reminder that ignoring open data obligations damages credibility and erodes the foundational trust required for effective community policing.
