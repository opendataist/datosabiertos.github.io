La inflación de EEUU cae más de lo esperado y el IPC baja al 3% en junio

The Consumer Price Index in the United States shows a more pronounced deceleration in inflation than expected, suggesting that the fight against rising prices is gaining ground. This positive trend, particularly in the core rate, eases pressure on the Federal Reserve and makes the scenario of a “soft landing” for the economy more plausible, allowing markets to anticipate a pause in interest rate hikes. Experts interpret these data as a sign that inflation is declining significantly, although they warn that the central bank will maintain a restrictive stance to prevent excessive optimism in the markets. Despite the overall improvement, the real estate sector continues to be the main driver of monthly increases, while energy prices moderate their growth. This indicates that, although macroeconomic indicators are stabilizing, some structural components require continuous monitoring to ensure that the downward trend consolidates. This report is relevant to the field of open data because it illustrates how publicly accessible economic indicators directly influence global financial decision-making and monetary policy. Transparency in the dissemination of these data allows analysts, investors, and citizens to assess economic health in real time, promoting greater accountability. Furthermore, it demonstrates the importance of processing and visualizing complex statistical information to facilitate public understanding of economic phenomena that directly affect quality of life and investment markets.
