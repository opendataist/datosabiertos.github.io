Google enfrenta nueva demanda por usar sin consentimiento millones de datos para entrenar a la IA | RPP Noticias

A class-action lawsuit alleges that Google silently collected personal data and copyrighted works from hundreds of millions of users to train its artificial intelligence models, including Bard. The plaintiffs argue that this practice violates privacy and copyright laws, seeking compensation and an opt-out option for those affected. Google responds by dismissing the accusations as baseless, highlighting its transparency and responsible use of public sources, in line with its ethical principles. The company argues that its updated privacy policy explicitly permits this use of publicly accessible data, emphasizing the creation of value and innovative benefits for users. This conflict is crucial for open data, as it questions the boundaries between the public availability of information and individual consent in the AI era. The case sets precedents on whether open data can be commercially exploited without explicit permissions, impacting the ethical governance of open datasets and corporate responsibility in technological development.
