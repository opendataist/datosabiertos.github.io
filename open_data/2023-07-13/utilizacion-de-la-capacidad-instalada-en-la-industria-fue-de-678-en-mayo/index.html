Utilización de la capacidad instalada en la industria fue de 67,8% en mayo

This article offers a detailed view of industrial performance, highlighting how open data initiatives can transform raw statistics into actionable economic insights. The National Institute of Statistics and Census released detailed capacity utilization rates, revealing a slight decline in overall industrial capacity usage. This transparency enables analysts to move beyond aggregate figures and examine specific sectoral trends, demonstrating the power of accessible, high-frequency data in monitoring economic health. The data reveals a sharp divergence between sectors, with the energy and chemical industries maintaining high utilization rates, while manufacturing segments such as textiles and automotive lag behind. Such detailed breakdowns are crucial for understanding structural shifts within the economy. Open data platforms that host these granular datasets allow researchers to identify emerging bottlenecks or growth areas that national averages might obscure, facilitating more precise policy responses. The relevance of open data lies in its capacity to foster accountability and deeper analysis. By publishing specific metrics for numerous sub-sectors, the government empowers stakeholders to independently scrutinize industrial efficiency. This transparency encourages the development of innovative tools and services capable of interpreting these complex patterns, ultimately supporting a more informed public discourse on industrial policy and economic recovery strategies.
