Spectrio expands AI toolbox with InReality acquisition

Spectrio’s acquisition of InReality integrates AI radar sensors into digital signage, enabling precise, anonymized audience measurement. This empowers retailers and marketers to optimize content placement based on real-time viewer interest. It highlights open data’s potential for privacy-centric, behavioral insights in public display environments.
