Economía peruana creció 4.67% en marzo - El Tiempo

Economía peruana creció 4.67% en marzo - El Tiempo

Peru is consolidating its economic recovery with performance that exceeds expectations, driven by sustained expansion in key sectors such as mining, fishing, and commerce. The year-on-year trend demonstrates structural resilience that positions the country as a leader within Latin America, suggesting that political stabilization and favorable international conditions will continue to support macroeconomic progress. The engine of this growth lies in the synergy between primary production and domestic demand. While fishing and mining record significant increases thanks to higher catches and extractions, commerce and transport reflect a robust internal economic dynamic. This sectoral diversity indicates that the recovery does not depend on a single factor, but rather on a broad strengthening that integrates productive supply with consumption and logistics. This article is relevant to the open data movement because it underscores the critical importance of official statistical data, such as those from the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI), for transparency and accountability. In a context where financial projections guide investments and public policies, access to accurate, verifiable, and timely government information is essential for civil society, analysts, and investors to correctly assess the country’s true economic condition and demand evidence-based governance.
