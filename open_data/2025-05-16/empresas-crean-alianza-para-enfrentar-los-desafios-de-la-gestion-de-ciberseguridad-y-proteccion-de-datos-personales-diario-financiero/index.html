Empresas crean alianza para enfrentar los desafíos de la gestión de ciberseguridad y protección de datos personales | Diario Financiero

Empresas crean alianza para enfrentar los desafíos de la gestión de ciberseguridad y protección de datos personales | Diario Financiero

The alliance between Aura Cybersecurity and Privacy by Design addresses the new legal and strategic challenges posed by the recent data protection and cybersecurity regulations that have been in effect since January. Both firms promote a comprehensive management approach that goes beyond technical compliance, integrating privacy into organizational culture and corporate governance. Their approach prioritizes alignment with business objectives, particularly for small businesses and startups that lack specialized teams. By identifying minimum security measures as a foundation, they facilitate a realistic and personalized digital maturity process. This methodology ensures that information protection is a continuous strategy rather than a mere legalistic exercise. This initiative is crucial for the open data movement because it establishes precedents for responsible governance. By normalizing robust and transparent security practices, it creates the necessary conditions for securely sharing open data. Thus, it fosters an ecosystem where innovation and trust coexist, enabling the ethical use of public information without compromising individual privacy.
