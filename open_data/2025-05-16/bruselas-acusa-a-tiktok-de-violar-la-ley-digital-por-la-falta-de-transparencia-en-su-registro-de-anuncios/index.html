Bruselas acusa a TikTok de violar la ley digital por la falta de transparencia en su registro de anuncios

Bruselas acusa a TikTok de violar la ley digital por la falta de transparencia en su registro de anuncios

The European Commission accuses TikTok of violating the Digital Services Act by lacking transparency in its ad repository. This failure prevents independent monitoring of hybrid threats and disinformation, particularly during critical election periods. Such opacity undermines the ability to detect fraudulent campaigns and protects neither democratic integrity nor consumer safety. Citizens inherently deserve to know who funds and targets the messages they encounter on these platforms. This case is vital for open data, as it highlights the necessity of accessible, standardized datasets for civil society and researchers. Without this transparency, public scrutiny of algorithmic influence remains compromised, reinforcing the need for rigorous data openness in digital governance.
