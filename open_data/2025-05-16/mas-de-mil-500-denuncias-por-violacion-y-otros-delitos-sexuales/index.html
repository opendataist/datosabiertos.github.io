Más de mil 500 denuncias por violación y otros delitos sexuales

The article highlights a national decline in reported sexual crimes, contrasting with sharp regional increases, particularly in indigenous territories. This divergence suggests that statistical trends are heavily influenced by local socio-cultural dynamics and access to justice rather than uniform national behavior. The relevance to open data lies in the critical need for granular, geographically disaggregated datasets. Without breaking down statistics by province or specific communities, policymakers risk overlooking vulnerable populations facing rising violence, thereby undermining effective resource allocation and targeted social interventions.
