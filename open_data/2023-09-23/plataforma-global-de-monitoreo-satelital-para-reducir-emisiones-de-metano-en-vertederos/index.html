Plataforma global de monitoreo satelital para reducir emisiones de metano en vertederos

The publication highlights the launch of Waste MAP, a global platform developed by the Global Methane Hub and its partners, which uses satellite monitoring and artificial intelligence to track methane emissions from urban landfills. Its main conclusion is that this tool enables governments and NGOs to accurately identify leak sources, facilitating early interventions that mitigate public health risks and accelerate decarbonization efforts through accessible and verifiable data. The initiative promotes transparency and international collaboration by integrating granular, open information that guides strategic decision-making. By providing global heat maps and action manuals, the platform empowers stakeholders to finance effective mitigation projects, ensuring that climate regulations and policies are based on concrete, up-to-date evidence regarding the locations of the largest pollution sources. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how satellite technology can democratize access to critical environmental information. By sharing high-resolution data on pollution, it fosters evidence-based governance, enabling diverse actors—from local communities to philanthropic investors—to collaborate on scalable climate solutions, transforming scientific information into tangible political and social action.
