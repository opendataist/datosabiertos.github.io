La Inteligencia Artificial en Argentina: ¿suficiente con buenas intenciones?

Argentina has established a framework to promote transparency and personal data protection in artificial intelligence, aligning with international standards to ensure robust and impartial systems. This initiative aims to equip both the public and private sectors with tools that balance technological advancement with citizens’ rights, emphasizing principles such as explainability, security, and human oversight in the implementation of AI projects. However, the article argues that mere resolutions and intentions are insufficient without concrete management and investment. It highlights a critical gap in preventive data protection measures, noting that current practices often lack the robustness needed to mitigate risks effectively. The text warns that without active governance, regulatory frameworks may become empty shells, failing to address the radical societal and ethical impacts driven by rapid algorithmic learning and the expansion of biometric technologies. This content is highly relevant to open data, as it underscores the necessity of combining transparency with rigorous data governance. It illustrates that open data initiatives in the era of AI must evolve beyond simple publication to include active protection strategies and clear accountability mechanisms. Ultimately, it stresses that sustainable technological development relies on marrying open information principles with effective management to ensure public trust and safety.
