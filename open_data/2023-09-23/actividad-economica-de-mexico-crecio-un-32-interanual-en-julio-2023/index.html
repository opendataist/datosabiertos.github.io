Actividad económica de México creció un 3,2% interanual en julio 2023

Mexico’s economy showed robust growth in July, driven by increases across the primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors. This positive trend points to a resilient short-term trajectory, with cumulative annual gains that reflect the country’s ongoing recovery from previous economic contractions. The data highlight the industrial sector as a key driver, signaling stability even as other segments experienced minor monthly fluctuations. Such broad-based sectoral expansion indicates overall economic health rather than isolated improvements, reinforcing confidence in the national growth outlook. This report is relevant to open_data because it originates from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). As the primary source of official economic statistics, its transparent publication of indices such as the General Index of Economic Activity (IGAE) enables researchers and developers to access, analyze, and visualize national economic trends for the public good and to support informed decision-making.
