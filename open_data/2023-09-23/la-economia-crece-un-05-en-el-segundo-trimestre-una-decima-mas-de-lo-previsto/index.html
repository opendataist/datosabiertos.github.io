La economía crece un 0,5% en el segundo trimestre, una décima más de lo previsto

Spain’s second-quarter economic growth slowed, signaling a potential deterioration driven by weaker external demand. This shift highlights how internal consumption and investment continue to sustain the economy amidst declining exports and imports.
