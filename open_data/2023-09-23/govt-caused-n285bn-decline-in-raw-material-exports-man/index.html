Govt caused N285bn decline in raw material exports – MAN

The Nigerian Manufacturers Association attributes the sharp decline in raw material exports to systemic neglect of the productive sector and uncompetitive local production costs. This shift highlights a critical failure to maintain economic foundations necessary for sustainable foreign trade, suggesting that without addressing structural inefficiencies, the nation cannot compete globally. High operational expenses prevent domestic firms from matching international pricing, creating a vicious cycle that stifles export growth. The inability to offer competitive prices undermines the country’s ability to support its currency through export earnings, revealing deep vulnerabilities in the current economic framework. This situation is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as transparent trade statistics are essential for diagnosing such economic shifts. Accessible, accurate data allows stakeholders to identify root causes like cost disparities and inform policy reforms, ensuring that government interventions are evidence-based rather than reactive.
