'Used for political speech ...' FOIA shows Biden's DHS censoring Americans was ABSOLUTELY political

The article argues that Department of Homeland Security documents reveal an intent to regulate speech on critical political topics, including racial justice and foreign policy. By labeling factual views as misleading, the agency seeks to suppress dissenting opinions, which the author interprets as an authoritarian effort to control information rather than protect the public. This relevance to open data lies in the potential for government agencies to weaponize information control. If state bodies can arbitrarily define truth, transparent access to government data and public records becomes compromised. Such practices threaten the fundamental openness required for democratic accountability and public scrutiny. Ultimately, the piece warns against expanding executive power over discourse. It highlights how restricting access to certain narratives undermines the principle of free information exchange. Maintaining open data ecosystems is essential to prevent such suppression and ensure citizens can access unbiased, verifiable facts about government actions.
