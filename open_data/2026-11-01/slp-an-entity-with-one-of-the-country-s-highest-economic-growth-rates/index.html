SLP, an entity with one of the country's highest economic growth rates

SLP, an entity with one of the country's highest economic growth rates

San Luis Potosí stands out as the entity with the highest national economic dynamism, driven primarily by its robust industrial and automotive sectors, as well as by the growth of tourism. Local experts attribute this upturn to stability and investment policies, although they warn that significant gaps persist, particularly in the agroindustrial sector, which require attention to consolidate comprehensive development. The relevance of this analysis for open data lies in the need for transparency and public access to official economic indicators, such as the ITAEE from INEGI, versus alternative data from civil society. The discrepancy between these sources highlights the critical importance of interoperability and cross-validation of open data to ensure public trust and facilitate accurate analysis. By democratizing access to this statistical information, society is empowered to actively participate in the oversight of public policies. Encouraging the use of open data allows for the identification of real development opportunities and corrects narratives, ensuring that decision-making is based on verifiable evidence accessible to all social actors.
