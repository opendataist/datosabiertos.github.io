Informal employment affects more than 1.635 million workers in Paraguay - Economy - ABC Color

Informal employment affects more than 1.635 million workers in Paraguay - Economy - ABC Color

Recent data reveal an improvement in Paraguay’s employment composition, highlighting accelerated growth in formal jobs alongside a relative decline in informality. This positive trend indicates that the labor market is creating jobs in a more structured manner, enabling a slight transition toward greater overall formalization of the non-agricultural sector. However, a pronounced territorial divide separates urban and rural realities. While urban areas show a reduction in informal workers and progress in formalization, rural regions face a significant increase in irregular employment. This territorial disparity demonstrates that formalization has not been uniform, leaving the rural population in a more vulnerable labor situation compared to their urban counterparts. The persistence of high levels of informality, particularly in rural areas, limits access to social protection, pensions, and other essential labor benefits. This analysis is relevant to the open data community because it illustrates how continuous, disaggregated monitoring of labor indicators can identify critical structural inequalities, facilitating the design of more effective and equitable public policies that address the root causes of economic exclusion.
