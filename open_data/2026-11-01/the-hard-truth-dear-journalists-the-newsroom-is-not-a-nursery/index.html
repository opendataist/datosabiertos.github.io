THE HARD TRUTH: Dear journalists, the newsroom is not a nursery.

THE HARD TRUTH: Dear journalists, the newsroom is not a nursery.

The article highlights a crisis in African journalism, where a majority of reporters cannot meet basic financial needs due to industry decline. This statistic underscores the urgent need for professionals to recognize that relying solely on traditional newsroom salaries is no longer sustainable. The core message argues that journalists must transition from passive employees to active entrepreneurs. By leveraging their valuable research and storytelling skills to launch independent ventures like newsletters or podcasts, professionals can secure their livelihoods. This shift requires understanding market dynamics and taking responsibility for one’s own economic survival rather than waiting for outdated business models to recover. This narrative is critical for open data advocates because it emphasizes the necessity of adaptability and multi-disciplinary skill sets in modern journalism. As newsrooms transform, the ability to analyze and present data independently becomes a key survival tool. The article serves as a reminder that open data initiatives must support journalists in acquiring these commercial and technical competencies, ensuring they can thrive in a decentralized, digital-first media landscape.
