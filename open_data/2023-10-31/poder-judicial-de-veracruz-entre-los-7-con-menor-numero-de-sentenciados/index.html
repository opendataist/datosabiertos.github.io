Poder Judicial de Veracruz, entre los 7 con menor número de sentenciados

The article highlights a critical inefficiency in Veracruz’s judicial system, where the majority of legal cases remain unresolved and very few result in final sentences. This data reveals a significant gap between the volume of criminal cases entered into the system and the actual number of convictions, indicating potential bottlenecks or procedural delays within the local courts. This situation is particularly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible statistical reporting. By publishing this census, the government provides essential insights into judicial performance, allowing citizens and researchers to hold institutions accountable. Open access to such metrics enables the public to analyze systemic issues rather than relying on opaque administrative decisions. Ultimately, this transparency fosters trust and drives necessary reforms. When citizens can clearly see that most cases do not lead to resolutions, it creates public pressure for improved legal processes. Therefore, maintaining and analyzing open justice data is vital for understanding how effectively the state administers justice and for identifying areas where structural changes are urgently needed to protect citizens' rights.
