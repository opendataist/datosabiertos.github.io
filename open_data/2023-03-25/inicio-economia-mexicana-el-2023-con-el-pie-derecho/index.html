Inició economía mexicana el 2023 con el pie derecho

Mexico’s economy began 2023 stronger than expected, driven by robust activity in the services sector. This positive momentum enabled the economy to exceed initial forecasts and regain growth levels seen before the pandemic, signaling a resilient recovery despite mixed performance in other areas such as industry and agriculture. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it highlights the critical importance of transparent, high-quality statistical metrics for monitoring economic health. Accessible indicators like the IGAE enable analysts, policymakers, and researchers to accurately assess trends, verify government reports, and make informed decisions based on reliable public information. Ultimately, the article underscores how open access to national statistics fosters accountability and trust in economic reporting. By providing clear metrics on sector-specific performances, such data supports evidence-based dialogue and helps stakeholders understand the broader implications of economic shifts, promoting a more engaged and informed society.
