APLEC Inclusión+ Igualdad presenta la Guía para desincentivar el consumo de prostitución entre los jóvenes - El Faradio | Periodismo que cuenta

The article presents a comprehensive guide developed by APLEC Inclusión más Igualdad to address the widespread consumption of prostitution in Spain, a lucrative industry that significantly influences youth attitudes. The initiative aims to deconstruct hegemonic masculinity and reduce demand for sexual services by targeting young men through educational and social transformation strategies. By highlighting the prevalence of this issue among adolescents, the guide seeks to foster critical awareness and encourage behavioral change within the male demographic. At the core of the project is a structured approach to gender equality and sexual education, offering specific activities for association members to challenge the social norms that sustain the sex trade. This effort is supported by substantial institutional backing, underscoring the need for a coordinated societal response to violence against women and exploitation. The collaborative nature of the project, involving experts in sociology, law, and communication, highlights the importance of interdisciplinary knowledge in addressing complex social inequalities and promoting sustainable cultural shifts. This content is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly available statistical evidence, such as national consumer trends and youth surveys, can drive the creation of accessible, open educational resources. By translating raw societal data into actionable public policy and community-led interventions, the project exemplifies the power of transparency in empowering civil society to address systemic issues. It underscores the necessity of making research findings and methodological frameworks publicly accessible to ensure that social transformations are evidence-based and widely participatory.
