El PIB de Argentina en 2022 muestra una mejora respecto al año anterior

The article highlights Argentina’s economic recovery in 2022, characterized by GDP growth and a significant decline in unemployment. However, involuntary underemployment increased slightly, indicating that many workers remain in precarious conditions without actively seeking additional working hours. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how macroeconomic statistics can reveal hidden vulnerabilities in the labor market that go beyond simple unemployment rates. Transparent access to such granular indicators enables a more accurate assessment of social welfare. Understanding these nuances helps policymakers and citizens evaluate the true quality of the economic recovery. It underscores the importance of open, detailed datasets in moving beyond headline figures to effectively address structural employment issues.
