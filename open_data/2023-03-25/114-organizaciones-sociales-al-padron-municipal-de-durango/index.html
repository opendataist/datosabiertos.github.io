114 organizaciones sociales al padrón municipal de Durango

The Municipality of Durango reduced its roster of supported social organizations, distributing funds according to specific needs rather than equal shares. This pluralistic approach aims to ensure that resources address actual community needs without generating political conflict or protests. This transparency is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates how public spending can be tracked and evaluated. By verifying field conditions and ensuring that coverage aligns with organizational missions, the administration fosters trust. Open data enables citizens to monitor these decisions, ensuring accountability in the management and delivery of social welfare.
