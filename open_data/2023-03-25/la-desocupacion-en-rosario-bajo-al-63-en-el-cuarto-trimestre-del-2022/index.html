La desocupación en Rosario bajó al 6,3% en el cuarto trimestre del 2022

The decline in unemployment indicates a significant improvement in the labor market, reflecting economic recovery following pandemic-related peaks. This decrease suggests that public policies and economic dynamics are generating more stable jobs. However, the persistence of underemployment calls for a deeper analysis of job quality. Despite GDP growth, high inflation continues to pressure the economy, necessitating strategies that integrate job creation with the protection of purchasing power. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how open statistical series enable real-time monitoring of macroeconomic trends. Data transparency facilitates public scrutiny and evidence-based decision-making, which are essential for assessing the social impact of public policies.
