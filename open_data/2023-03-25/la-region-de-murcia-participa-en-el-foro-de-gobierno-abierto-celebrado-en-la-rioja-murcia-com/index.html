La Región de Murcia participa en el Foro de Gobierno Abierto celebrado en La Rioja - murcia.com

The Region of Murcia actively participates in the Open Government Forum, highlighting its commitment to inclusion through the +60 Plan. This initiative aims to improve services for people aged 60 and over by establishing senior-friendly offices and providing accessible telematic services. By co-creating these solutions with various social and academic organizations, the administration demonstrates how collaboration with civil society is essential for designing public policies that truly address the needs of the most vulnerable citizens. The relevance to open data lies in the creation of a specific chair on this topic and in the continuous improvement of the Transparency Portal. By increasing the volume and quality of proactive disclosure, clearer and more usable access to public information is facilitated. This approach not only meets regulatory requirements but also transforms data into useful tools for citizens, fostering greater trust in institutions and enabling more effective public scrutiny of administrative management. At the national level, the initiative is framed within the discussion on the reform of the Transparency Law and the commitments of the Fourth Open Government Plan. The significance of this event lies in the convergence of efforts to establish common standards of integrity and participation. The Murcian experience serves as a model of how administrations can practically integrate accessibility and transparency, contributing to a fairer society and demonstrating that open government requires a constant adaptation of public services to citizens' digital rights.
