Un hombre de 102 años asegura que la vida le cambió al divorciarse: "Tenía taquicardia y me sentía muy mal todo el día"

Global life expectancy is increasing thanks to advances in public health and quality of life, as illustrated by the exceptional longevity of Maurice. His case reveals that emotional well-being and stress-free environments are crucial for physical health, demonstrating how personal decisions directly impact longevity. Happiness and the management of interpersonal relationships emerge as fundamental pillars. By distancing themselves from toxic situations and cultivating love and gratitude, individuals not only improve their mental state but also resolve chronic health issues, validating the inseparable link between emotional and physical health. This narrative is relevant to open data because it underscores the need to integrate psychosocial variables into demographic datasets. To truly understand the determinants of health, datasets must include metrics on subjective well-being and social factors, enabling more holistic analyses that explain why some populations live longer and better than others.
