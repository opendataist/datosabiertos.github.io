La inflación sube en marzo cuatro décimas, al 3,2%, por la electricidad y los carburantes

Rising energy costs pushed inflation higher, while food prices declined, narrowing the gap between headline and core inflation rates. This convergence signals a cooling trend in underlying price pressures, despite recent monthly spikes. The data underscores the critical role of energy and food prices in economic stability, highlighting the need for transparent, real-time price tracking through open data to monitor such volatility and effectively inform public policy decisions.
