The National Statistics Institute has successfully completed the processing of 2024 census data using advanced AI and scanning technologies, overcoming technical challenges while maintaining high quality. This modern approach ensures robust data synthesis, supported by international organizations that adhere to global standards until the final results are published. The high return rate of census forms from both urban and rural areas highlights effective operational execution. Thanks to extensive volunteer and citizen participation, the collected information will enable the implementation of data-driven public policies tailored to emerging regional needs across the country. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates the viability of transparent, high-tech statistical methods. By digitalizing cartographic updates and leveraging automation, the INE sets a precedent for accessible, accurate, and reliable public information, fostering trust and enabling better societal decision-making through shared governance.
