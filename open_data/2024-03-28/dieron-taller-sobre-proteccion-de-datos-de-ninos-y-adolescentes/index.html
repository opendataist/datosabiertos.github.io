Dieron taller sobre protección de datos de niños y adolescentes

The training emphasizes that sensitive data concerning minors must be strictly protected, ensuring that their privacy and best interests are prioritized above all else in any information-handling activity. This underscores the critical need for public institutions to apply rigorous data protection standards to safeguard vulnerable populations. Such initiatives are relevant to open data, as they illustrate the necessary balance between transparency and the legal obligations to anonymize or restrict access to personal information involving children.
