Vietnam y Corea del Sur cooperan para proteger derechos de autor en entorno digital | Sociedad | Vietnam+ (VietnamPlus)

The debate between Vietnamese and South Korean experts highlights that while digital expansion facilitates cultural dissemination, it also creates complex challenges for copyright protection. It is concluded that bilateral collaboration is essential to develop technological solutions capable of tracking infringements and ensuring transparency in the use of protected works. The transfer of automatic detection technology represents a key opportunity to strengthen rights enforcement. This international cooperation aims not only to combat digital espionage but also to harmonize efforts against rights violations in sectors such as music, where infringements are increasingly diverse and difficult to monitor. This exchange is relevant to open data because it illustrates the tension between open access to information and the need to manage intellectual property rights in digital environments. The trend toward automated surveillance and transparency tools suggests that future open data models must integrate robust compliance mechanisms, ensuring that openness does not compromise the legal protection of creators.
