Statistics show Australian defence industry growth - Australian Defence Magazine

The Australian defence industry’s economic contribution and direct employment increased, indicating the government’s strategy to strengthen the industrial base is successfully delivering jobs and growth. This expansion supports national security by building a robust sovereign capacity and fostering deeper collaboration between defence and industry stakeholders. This data is relevant to open data because it relies on consistent, publicly available economic metrics to track national performance. Transparent reporting of such indicators allows for greater accountability and enables external analysis of policy effectiveness. Understanding these trends through accessible data helps identify regional impacts, such as job creation in New South Wales. By making these insights available, authorities can better guide future investments and ensure the defence sector continues to serve both economic and security objectives effectively.
