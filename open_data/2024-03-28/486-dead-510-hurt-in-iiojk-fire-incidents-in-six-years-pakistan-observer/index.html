486 dead, 510 hurt in IIOJK fire incidents in six years - Pakistan Observer

RTI data reveals a severe public safety crisis in Jammu and Kashmir, highlighting systemic failures in emergency response and medical facilities. This transparency-driven inquiry exposes how much critical information remains hidden from the public without active citizen intervention. The disclosure underscores the vital role of open data in holding government agencies accountable for inadequate infrastructure and resource management. By making these statistics visible, civil society can demand structural reforms to prevent future casualties and protect civilian lives during emergencies. Furthermore, the data links fire incidents to broader human rights violations, illustrating how public records can document state accountability and international law compliance. This case demonstrates that accessing government-held information is essential for uncovering truth, fostering justice, and ensuring that administrative actions are subject to public scrutiny.
