This report highlights Argentina’s worsening social crisis, revealing significant spikes in poverty and extreme poverty rates throughout 2023. These figures underscore the severe erosion of purchasing power, where rising costs drastically outpace income growth, compelling millions to struggle with basic survival needs. Regional disparities are stark, with the Northeast and Greater Buenos Aires bearing the heaviest burdens compared to other areas. The data suggests these challenges will intensify due to ongoing recession, public sector layoffs, and soaring utility tariffs, painting a grim picture for the immediate future. This article is vital for open data advocates as it exemplifies how official statistics must be accessible and transparent to drive public accountability. Reliable, freely available data enables civil society to monitor policy impacts, challenge narratives, and demand evidence-based solutions to address systemic inequality.
