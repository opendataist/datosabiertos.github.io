Exportaciones mexicanas crecen 13 por ciento en febrero - Noticias Prensa Latina

Foreign trade data show that non-petroleum exports grew significantly, while the trade deficit narrowed compared to the previous year. This improvement indicates a strengthening of Mexico’s external economic position, despite a slight monthly decline. Such timely and granular trade statistics are essential for researchers analyzing global supply chains and economic resilience. Access to this high-frequency, detailed data enables more accurate modeling of international trade flows and the impacts of policy measures. Ultimately, the transparency of these figures supports evidence-based decision-making within open data initiatives. It allows stakeholders to monitor economic health dynamically, ensuring that public debate on trade policy is grounded in accessible, reliable, and up-to-date information.
