SSPC debe transparentar información sobre “autos chocolate”

The INAI mandates the SSPC to disclose detailed records on foreign vehicle regularization, including vehicle counts and funds allocated for public works. This transparency enables citizens to evaluate government strategies and their actual impact on municipalities dealing with automotive smuggling. Accessing this trustworthy data allows for evidence-based studies that can drive improvements in public policies regarding the illicit trade of used cars from neighboring countries. By revealing the scope and results of regularization efforts, authorities can better address the challenges faced by the relevant industrial sector. This initiative is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how mandatory disclosure fosters accountability and informed decision-making. It highlights the necessity of accessible, granular government information to assess policy effectiveness and ensure that public resources serve civic interests rather than opaque bureaucratic processes.
