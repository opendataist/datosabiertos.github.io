Jueza obliga al Senado a sesión extraordinaria para designar comisionados del INAI | Revista Punto de Vista

Jueza obliga al Senado a sesión extraordinaria para designar comisionados del INAI | Revista Punto de Vista

A federal judge has ordered the Mexican Senate to convene extraordinary sessions to appoint the two remaining commissioners of the INAI, declaring the prolonged vacancy unconstitutional and a violation of human rights. The ruling emphasizes that functional transparency bodies are essential for protecting citizens, as their absence undermines legal safeguards and democratic accountability. This decision underscores the critical link between institutional integrity and data governance. When key oversight bodies such as the INAI remain understaffed, public access to information and personal data protection are compromised, revealing how political delays directly impair open data principles and citizens' rights.
