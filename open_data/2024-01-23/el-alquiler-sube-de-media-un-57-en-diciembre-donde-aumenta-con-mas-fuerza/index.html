El alquiler sube de media un 5,7% en diciembre: ¿dónde aumenta con más fuerza?

The Spanish rental market is under significant pressure due to a critical imbalance between supply and demand, resulting in substantial annual rent increases. This shortage of available housing has driven up rents, marking one of the sharpest rises in recent history. The situation is particularly acute in major urban centers and tourist destinations, where housing affordability is becoming increasingly difficult for residents. Access to data is crucial for understanding these trends. Open data initiatives play a vital role by providing transparent, accessible information on housing markets. When such data is openly available, it allows for independent verification of reports, deeper analysis of regional disparities, and more informed public debate. Without open data, stakeholders rely solely on aggregated commercial summaries, which may lack the granularity needed for a comprehensive assessment. The relevance of open data lies in the need for detailed, freely accessible housing statistics. Policymakers, researchers, and citizens require detailed datasets to accurately analyze supply-and-demand dynamics. Open data enables the identification of hotspots, monitoring of policy effectiveness, and empowerment of individuals to make informed decisions. Ultimately, transparency in housing data supports a more equitable and efficient rental market by fostering accountability and enabling evidence-based solutions to the ongoing crisis.
