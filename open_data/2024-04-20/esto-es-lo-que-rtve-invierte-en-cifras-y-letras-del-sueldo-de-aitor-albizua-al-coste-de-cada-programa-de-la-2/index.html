Esto es lo que RTVE invierte en 'Cifras y letras': del sueldo de Aitor Albizua al coste de cada programa de La 2

Esto es lo que RTVE invierte en 'Cifras y letras': del sueldo de Aitor Albizua al coste de cada programa de La 2

The article reveals that RTVE allocates only a minimal fraction of its budget to internal resources, diverting the vast majority to an external production company. This structure highlights significant financial transparency issues concerning public broadcasting costs and the heavy reliance on private partners for core operations. Such data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it exposes the specific allocation of public funds versus private profit in media formats. By making detailed expenditure breakdowns accessible, it enables rigorous scrutiny of how taxpayer money supports entertainment, thereby fostering accountability in public institutions. Ultimately, this disclosure empowers citizens to question the efficiency and fairness of outsourcing public service content. It underscores the importance of transparent data in holding broadcasters accountable for their financial decisions and resource management in the digital age.
