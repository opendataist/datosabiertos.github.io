Kozhikode MCH sexual assault survivor continues stir seeking probe report

A survivor of sexual assault at a medical college hospital is protesting to obtain the police investigation report, which she claims the gynaecologist handling her case did not fully disclose. Despite her insistence that the relevant legal matter is not sub judice, authorities cite legal restrictions to withhold the document, leaving her demands unmet during a public sit-in. This conflict highlights a critical tension in open data and transparency. The refusal to release investigation reports under the guise of ongoing legal proceedings can obstruct public accountability and prevent victims from verifying the integrity of official inquiries. Without access to such documents, the narrative is controlled entirely by institutional gatekeepers, undermining the principle of information freedom. The article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how legal ambiguities can be used to suppress the dissemination of public interest information. Ensuring that investigation records remain accessible, even during active inquiries, is essential for maintaining trust in public institutions and safeguarding the rights of individuals seeking justice through transparent processes.
