Watch: Meta's new Llama 3 models give open-source AI a boost

Meta has released new open-source Llama models that outperform many proprietary rivals, challenging the industry’s shift toward closed systems. This release underscores the growing viability and power of open-source AI, demonstrating that transparency does not compromise performance. The contrast between Meta’s open approach and competitors’ closed source strategies highlights a critical debate regarding development speed versus safety. Open access allows for greater scrutiny and community-driven innovation, addressing concerns that rapid, opaque AI advancement might threaten democratic stability and security. This is highly relevant to open_data because it promotes the democratization of advanced technological resources. By making powerful models publicly available, Meta enables researchers and developers to audit, modify, and build upon existing work, fostering a more transparent and collaborative ecosystem for future artificial intelligence advancements.
