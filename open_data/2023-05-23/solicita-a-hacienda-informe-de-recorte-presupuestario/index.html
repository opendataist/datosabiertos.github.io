Solicita a Hacienda informe de recorte presupuestario

Solicita a Hacienda informe de recorte presupuestario

Senators from the Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI) have demanded a detailed report from the Ministry of Finance concerning the persistent budget cuts imposed on the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) since 2018. They seek transparency regarding the reasons for these reductions and the impact of unspent funds on the institute’s ability to carry out its duties. The legislative body emphasizes that, despite austerity measures, the agency has experienced significant financial erosion, which has limited its operational capacity. The inquiry highlights a substantial decrease in both the general budget and the salaries of commissioners, a trend exacerbated by inflation. This ongoing financial strain raises concerns about the institution’s effectiveness and independence. By requesting specific data on unexecuted expenditures and justifications for the cuts, the Senate aims to determine whether these fiscal constraints compromise the agency’s core mission. This issue is crucial for open data, as the INAI is the primary authority responsible for guaranteeing public access to government information. Underfunding threatens its independence and operational efficiency, potentially weakening transparency mechanisms. Ensuring adequate resources is essential for maintaining robust oversight and protecting citizens’ right to know, which are foundational pillars of an open and accountable society.
