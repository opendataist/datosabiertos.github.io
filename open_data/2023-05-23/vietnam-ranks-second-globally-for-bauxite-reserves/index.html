Vietnam ranks second globally for bauxite reserves

Dak Nong’s vast bauxite reserves position it as Vietnam’s future aluminum hub, aiming to drive regional economic growth by 2030. The province plans to integrate industrial expansion with environmental protection and national security, fostering sustainable socio-economic development. This strategy is vital for open data initiatives, as transparent mining and environmental monitoring require accessible, high-quality information. Open data enables stakeholders to verify sustainability claims and ensures accountability in resource management, supporting the balance between industrial growth and ecological preservation.
