Open Data
ES EN

UMSNH and INEGI are collaborating to provide free training on statistical and geographic tools, empowering researchers with virtual resources for project development. This initiative underscores the critical role of public data institutions in democratizing access to essential analytical instruments for academic work. The course aims to help participants select appropriate digital tools based on their specific needs, fostering better integration of official statistics into educational projects. By clarifying how to use these resources, the program bridges the gap between data availability and practical research application. This partnership is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how government agencies actively disseminate open datasets through structured educational channels. Such efforts enhance the research community’s capacity to effectively leverage public information, thereby promoting transparency and informed decision-making based on accessible, high-quality geographic and statistical data.
