Emiten nueva NOM para atención integral en salud de personas con discapacidad

The implementation of Mexico’s new NOM-015-SSA-2023 establishes mandatory, comprehensive medical care standards for people with disabilities, ensuring quality, safety, and non-discriminatory treatment across all healthcare sectors. This regulation requires healthcare facilities to adapt their infrastructure and utilize multidisciplinary teams to provide coordinated diagnostic, rehabilitative, and follow-up services within a sixty-day compliance window. The standard aims to significantly improve the quality of life for millions of citizens facing physical, sensory, or mental limitations by guaranteeing equitable access to essential health services. It formalizes the issuance of disability certificates through proper medical assessment, creating a unified, secure, and efficient national framework for patient care and documentation. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it necessitates the systematic collection, standardization, and publication of health metrics related to disability care. Transparent, standardized datasets are crucial for monitoring compliance, evaluating service effectiveness, and driving evidence-based policy improvements, thereby enhancing public accountability and the overall efficacy of social inclusion initiatives.
