¿Por qué disminuye la tasa de fecundidad en Paraguay? - Nacionales - ABC Color

¿Por qué disminuye la tasa de fecundidad en Paraguay? - Nacionales - ABC Color

Paraguay is experiencing population stagnation and aging as fertility rates decline to just above two children per woman. This decline marks a transition from population growth to stability, driven by global trends in which greater access to female education and increased participation of women in the workforce delay childbearing and reduce family size. This situation underscores the critical need for robust demographic data to understand societal changes. Accurate statistics reveal how socioeconomic factors, such as female empowerment, directly influence long-term population structures, prompting policymakers to analyze these underlying causes rather than merely responding to outcomes. In the context of open data, transparent access to such demographic insights enables researchers and citizens to evaluate the effectiveness of current social policies. By making these trends visible, open data fosters informed public debate on sustainable development and the evolving role of women in society.
