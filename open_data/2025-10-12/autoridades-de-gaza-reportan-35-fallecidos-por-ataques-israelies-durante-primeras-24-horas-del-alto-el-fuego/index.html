Autoridades de Gaza reportan 35 fallecidos por ataques israelíes durante primeras 24 horas del alto el fuego

Autoridades de Gaza reportan 35 fallecidos por ataques israelíes durante primeras 24 horas del alto el fuego

The article reports a significant rise in Palestinian casualties in Gaza during the initial hours of the ceasefire, revealing that many deaths result from the delayed identification of victims found in the rubble. This highlights the critical role of open data in verifying conflict statistics and ensuring transparency regarding humanitarian conditions and famine-related fatalities. Accurate, up-to-date casualty counts are essential for understanding the true scale of the crisis and holding parties accountable. By openly sharing this verified data, stakeholders can better assess the effectiveness of ceasefires and the immediate needs of the civilian population. Ultimately, this dataset underscores how open data facilitates informed decision-making and advocacy. It provides the empirical foundation necessary for international bodies to address human rights violations and support relief efforts in conflict zones.
