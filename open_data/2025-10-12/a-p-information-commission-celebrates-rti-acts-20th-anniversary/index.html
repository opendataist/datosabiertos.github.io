A.P. Information Commission celebrates RTI Act’s 20th anniversary

A.P. Information Commission celebrates RTI Act’s 20th anniversary

The Andhra Pradesh Information Commission celebrated the RTI Act’s twentieth anniversary, emphasizing its vital role in fostering transparency and citizen empowerment. Judicial recognition of RTI as a fundamental freedom reinforces its capacity to strengthen democracy by enabling public accountability over government actions. To sustain this impact, officials urged filling administrative vacancies and improving case disposal efficiency. These structural enhancements are essential for maintaining the Commission’s autonomy and ensuring equal respect for all parties, thereby preventing delays that undermine trust in the system. This event highlights the ongoing necessity of robust information rights in open data ecosystems. By promoting accessibility and institutional efficiency, such frameworks ensure that citizens can effectively access and utilize government information, driving broader societal transparency and engaged governance.
