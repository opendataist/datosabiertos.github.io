El Govern saca a exposición pública la Ley Agraria

El Govern saca a exposición pública la Ley Agraria

The Balearic Islands are drafting a new agricultural law aimed at modernizing the sector by simplifying administrative procedures and restoring rights hindered by previous legislation. Rather than relying on direct financial subsidies, this legislative shift focuses on providing farmers with practical tools to enhance profitability and operational freedom. By reducing bureaucratic obstacles and encouraging direct sales and agrovoltaic energy use, the initiative seeks to make agriculture a dignified, sustainable, and forward-looking career choice for current and future generations. The development process emphasizes high transparency and civic engagement, a cornerstone of open data principles. The draft resulted from extensive consultation with agricultural organizations, cooperatives, and the general public, incorporating thousands of suggestions during a public review period. This participatory approach ensures the final regulation reflects the actual needs of the field, demonstrating how accessible government processes can lead to policies that are both realistic and closely aligned with the stakeholders they affect. This reform is significant for open data because it highlights the value of participatory governance and accessible public administration. By aligning with European goals for a multifunctional, circular bioeconomy, the law treats agricultural data and feedback as essential inputs for policy-making. It reinforces the idea that transparency in legislative drafting—making processes and contributions visible—fosters trust and creates a more resilient rural economy, where open information drives effective, community-centered management of natural resources and cultural heritage.
