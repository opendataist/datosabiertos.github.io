Open Data
ES EN

Privacy consent interfaces often obscure the extent of data harvesting by partners, raising concerns about transparency in digital ecosystems. This lack of clear visibility contradicts open data principles, which advocate for accessible, understandable information regarding data collection and usage. Users typically face complex choices to manage their privacy, frequently defaulting to broad consent that permits extensive tracking and profiling. Such practices limit individual agency and reduce public oversight, hindering the democratic potential of open data initiatives that rely on informed participation. Consequently, enhancing clarity in privacy notices is crucial for aligning commercial data practices with open data values. Ensuring that consent mechanisms are transparent and user-friendly fosters trust and supports a more equitable information society.
