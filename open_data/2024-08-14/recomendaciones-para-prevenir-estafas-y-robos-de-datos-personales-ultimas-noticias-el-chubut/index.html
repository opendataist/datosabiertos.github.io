Recomendaciones para prevenir estafas y robos de datos personales - Últimas Noticias: El Chubut

This article warns against social engineering tactics, specifically fraudulent promotions and phishing attempts impersonating Camuzzi Gas. It emphasizes that legitimate companies never request passwords or sensitive data through unofficial channels, nor do they operate via suspicious WhatsApp numbers or fake social media profiles. Users are advised to verify all communications through official channels and immediately block suspicious contacts to protect their personal information. The text highlights the critical importance of digital hygiene, urging users to change passwords regularly, avoid predictable patterns, and use unique credentials for different services. Furthermore, it stresses the necessity of using secure, private networks rather than public Wi‑Fi to prevent data interception. These practices are essential for maintaining account security and preventing unauthorized access to personal financial and identity data. Relevant to open data, this notice underscores the vulnerability of personal information when shared through unsecured digital interfaces. By promoting transparent verification tools, such as the employee validation system available on official websites, the company advocates for secure data handling and user empowerment. This approach protects individual privacy rights and ensures that public interaction with service providers remains trustworthy, reinforcing the ethical management of consumer data in digital ecosystems.
