TrueCaller: ¿Qué es y por qué es riesgoso instalarlo en el celular? | Noticias RCN

TrueCaller: ¿Qué es y por qué es riesgoso instalarlo en el celular? | Noticias RCN

The article warns that popular caller ID apps like TrueCaller, while useful for identifying spam, pose severe privacy risks by harvesting extensive personal data. By accessing user contacts and call logs, these platforms create vast databases that expose not only the installer’s information but also that of their unconsenting contacts, violating third-party privacy rights. This model raises critical concerns for the open data movement, illustrating the ethical dangers of crowdsourced data collection without transparent consent. The app’s business strategy likely relies on monetizing this sensitive information, potentially sharing it with advertisers and exacerbating data exposure. Understanding these mechanisms is relevant to open data because it highlights the tension between utility and privacy. It underscores the need for open source principles that prioritize user sovereignty and transparency, ensuring that shared data does not come at the cost of individual security and consent.
