South Africa's unemployment rate climbs, over 8 million jobless | Africanews

South Africa's unemployment rate climbs, over 8 million jobless | Africanews

Rising unemployment in South Africa highlights the urgent need for transparent, accessible labor market data. This crisis underscores how open data can empower citizens to hold the new coalition government accountable for its economic revival agenda. By providing clear insights into sectoral job losses, such data facilitates evidence-based policy making. It allows stakeholders to track progress and identify effective interventions, ensuring that resources are directed toward revitalizing the struggling economy. Ultimately, maintaining high standards in statistical release fosters public trust. Open access to these figures is vital for democratic engagement, enabling informed debate on critical issues like employment and social welfare.
