Emails reveal New York Times mask-promoting activist manipulated and misrepresented the Cochrane Review, which originally found masks useless – NaturalNews.com

The article contends that the intersection of social media influence and scientific integrity is severely compromised, as evidenced by leaked communications between activist Zeynep Tufekci and the Cochrane Review. It alleges that Tufekci, backed by mainstream media outlets, pressured scientific bodies to misrepresent findings that masks were ineffective against respiratory viruses. This narrative suggests a coordinated effort to override empirical evidence with propaganda-driven policy, undermining the autonomy and credibility of independent research institutions. Central to the investigation is the claim that opinion-driven journalism actively distorts scientific discourse to enforce compliance rather than inform the public. By manipulating expert opinions and rushing statements under media pressure, key figures allegedly created a public relations crisis that obscured the original, evidence-based conclusions. The text argues that this phenomenon allows corporate media and influential voices to dictate public health policy, disregarding rigorous analysis in favor of a unified, hysterical orthodoxy that prioritizes social conformity over medical reality. This case is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for transparency and accessibility in scientific records. When internal communications and peer-review processes are obscured or manipulated, it becomes impossible for independent researchers and the public to verify claims or hold institutions accountable. The incident underscores how a lack of open, verifiable data allows misinformation to proliferate, eroding trust in science and enabling policy decisions based on narrative rather than verifiable fact.
