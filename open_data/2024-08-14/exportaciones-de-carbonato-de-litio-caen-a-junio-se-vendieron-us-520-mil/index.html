Bolivia is facing a significant crisis in its lithium sector, marked by a sharp decline in lithium carbonate exports during the first half of 2024. The country relies almost exclusively on the Chinese market, while revenues have plummeted due to global price volatility, which has fallen considerably from their historical highs. This situation exposes Bolivia’s economic vulnerability and the lack of diversification in its trade routes. The relevance of this article to open data lies in the critical importance of transparency and access to official statistical data for analyzing structural economic trends. When data from the National Institute of Statistics are publicly available, they enable researchers and citizens to monitor the effectiveness of state policies and the country’s commercial dependencies. The visibility of these indicators facilitates accountability regarding how strategic natural resources are managed and their impact on the trade balance. Finally, the outlook suggests an urgent need for technological modernization and strategic adaptation to regain competitiveness. The implementation of new plants using direct extraction technology and the optimization of industrial processes are presented as key responses to the current situation. Understanding these open data is essential to assess whether the proposed technological investments will succeed in stabilizing production and diversifying destination markets in the near future.
