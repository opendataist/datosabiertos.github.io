México reporta reducción de pobreza de casi 18% entre 2022 y 2024

México reporta reducción de pobreza de casi 18% entre 2022 y 2024

The article highlights a significant reduction in poverty in Mexico between 2022 and 2024, largely attributed to policy changes implemented during the previous administration. These measures included raising the minimum wage and establishing new social welfare programs aimed at supporting lower-income and rural populations. While these initiatives have improved access to security and food, experts caution that the improvements may not be sustainable in the long term. This data is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how statistical agencies can release comparable datasets despite institutional changes. INEGI’s ability to provide transparent, accessible metrics allows researchers and the public to verify trends and evaluate the effectiveness of public policies. Open data facilitates this scrutiny, enabling independent analysis of whether short-term gains translate into lasting structural improvements. Ultimately, while the immediate reduction in poverty is notable, the narrative warns against relying solely on cash transfers without broader investments. The discussion underscores the importance of using open data not just to report success, but to critically assess the durability of social policies and ensure that development efforts lead to genuine, long-term societal progress rather than temporary relief.
