Karnataka High Court Holds RTI Act Applies To Nirmiti Kendras, Slaps ₹50K Costs For Denying Information

Karnataka High Court Holds RTI Act Applies To Nirmiti Kendras, Slaps ₹50K Costs For Denying Information

The Karnataka High Court has established that Nirmiti Kendras qualify as public authorities under the Right to Information Act. This landmark ruling mandates these entities to disclose information, rejecting their claim that they operate independently. The court emphasized that government control, not just funding, determines public authority status, noting that Kendras are managed by government officers and execute state contracts. This decision reinforces the principle that transparency applies to organizations deeply integrated with state administration. By dismissing the petition and imposing costs, the court signaled that attempts to evade accountability undermine public trust. The ruling ensures that entities performing public works under government supervision cannot hide behind non-governmental labels to avoid scrutiny. This case is highly relevant to open data advocates as it broadens the scope of entities obligated to share information. It clarifies that financial and administrative ties to the state trigger disclosure requirements, regardless of an organization's formal structure. Consequently, more bodies involved in public service delivery must now adhere to open data standards, enhancing citizen access to information about government-related projects and expenditures.
