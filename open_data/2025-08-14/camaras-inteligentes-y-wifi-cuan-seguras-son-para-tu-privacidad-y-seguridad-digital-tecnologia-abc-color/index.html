Cámaras inteligentes y WiFi: ¿cuán seguras son para tu privacidad y seguridad digital? - Tecnología - ABC Color

Cámaras inteligentes y WiFi: ¿cuán seguras son para tu privacidad y seguridad digital? - Tecnología - ABC Color

Smart home cameras offer the convenience of remote monitoring but depend on constant internet connectivity, creating significant security vulnerabilities. Weak defenses allow cybercriminals to intercept video feeds, access recordings, or remotely control devices, potentially leading to espionage or blackmail. This highlights the critical need for transparent data handling and robust digital infrastructure within IoT ecosystems. Furthermore, these devices collect extensive personal data, such as movement patterns and geolocation, often storing it on cloud servers with opaque privacy protocols. Users must trust that corporate data protection measures are sufficient, raising serious concerns about surveillance capitalism and the lack of user control over sensitive information. To mitigate these risks, individuals must implement strong passwords, two-factor authentication, and regular software updates. This article is relevant to open data as it underscores the urgent need for open standards in security and privacy to prevent the misuse of personal information in smart home technologies, ensuring transparency and user safety.
