El índice de referencia del INE para la actualización del alquiler sube en julio al 2,15%

El índice de referencia del INE para la actualización del alquiler sube en julio al 2,15%

The National Statistics Institute (INE) has published the new reference index that limits the annual adjustment of rental contracts signed after the Housing Law entered into force. This mechanism, mandatory until the end of 2024, aims to prevent disproportionate rent increases by setting a cap at the lowest value among general inflation, underlying inflation, and an adjusted average. The relevance for open data lies in the fact that this index is calculated using a public and transparent methodology, based on official economic data that must be accessible and verifiable to ensure fairness in the real estate market. The monthly publication of this indicator marks a structural change in the regulation of rentals in Spain, replacing temporary caps imposed by specific crises with a permanent legal framework. By linking rent adjustments to a complex calculation that weights multiple inflationary variables, it prioritizes the stability of housing expenditure over market fluctuations. This implies that the availability and quality of the underlying statistical data are crucial, as any errors or delays in publishing the components of the index would directly affect the rights of landlords and tenants. Finally, although general inflation continues to rise, underlying inflation remains more contained, suggesting a moderation in cost pressures beyond energy and unprocessed food. This economic context reinforces the need for robust open data sources that allow citizens to understand how the real cost of housing is determined. Transparency in the construction of the reference index not only meets legal requirements but also fosters trust in public institutions by demystifying the criteria that regulate the everyday economic life of millions of people.
