Inflación de julio 2025: el Indec revelará este miércoles el IPC, ¿a qué hora y cuáles son las proyecciones?

Inflación de julio 2025: el Indec revelará este miércoles el IPC, ¿a qué hora y cuáles son las proyecciones?

The Argentine National Institute of Statistics and Census (INDEC) is scheduled to release July’s official inflation data, with private forecasts anticipating a monthly rate below two percent. Although expected to slightly exceed June’s figures, these projections suggest a continued deceleration in price increases, maintaining a low-inflation narrative that supports the government’s economic stabilization efforts. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity of accessing this official statistic directly from the INDEC website to verify private sector estimates. This transparency allows researchers and citizens to compare institutional data against market expectations, ensuring accountability and enabling accurate macroeconomic analysis. Ultimately, the release underscores the importance of timely, public statistical releases in validating economic trends. By making this data openly available, authorities provide a verifiable foundation for public debate, allowing society to assess the real impact of price changes on purchasing power and policy effectiveness.
