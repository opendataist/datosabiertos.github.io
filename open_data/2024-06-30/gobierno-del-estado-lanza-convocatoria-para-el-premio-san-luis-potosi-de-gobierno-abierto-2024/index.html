Gobierno del estado lanza convocatoria para el 'Premio San Luis Potosí de Gobierno Abierto 2024'

The Office of the Comptroller General launched the 2024 Open Government Award in San Luis Potosí to incentivize initiatives that simplify procedures, enhance social solutions, and strengthen civic participation. This program aims to foster dialogue and improve public services by recognizing projects from both civil society and government entities. Winners receive official recognition, financial incentives, and inclusion in an Open Government Projects Bank, ensuring their proposals are actively implemented. This approach moves beyond mere recognition to drive tangible administrative improvements and better service delivery for citizens. This initiative is relevant to open data as it promotes transparency, accountability, and the use of public information to solve societal problems. By encouraging data-driven solutions and citizen engagement, it supports the core principles of open government, demonstrating how accessible information can lead to more responsive and effective public administration.
