Delincuencia hacia empresas cuesta el 0,51% del PIB

Delincuencia hacia empresas cuesta el 0,51% del PIB

The article reveals that crime imposes a substantial economic burden on Mexican businesses, significantly impacting the national economy. It highlights that a large majority of establishments suffered from various offenses, driving up operational costs through direct losses and extensive security investments. This trend indicates a severe degradation of the business environment, necessitating urgent policy attention to restore commercial viability and trust. A critical finding is the extremely low rate of crime reporting, with the vast majority of incidents remaining hidden from authorities. This massive "black figure" prevents accurate public safety planning and hinders effective law enforcement strategies. The data underscores a systemic failure in the justice system's accessibility and perceived efficacy, leaving businesses to bear the full weight of insecurity without institutional support or recourse. This report is vital for open data initiatives because it demonstrates the power of transparent, granular statistics in exposing hidden societal costs. By making victimization and financial impact data publicly available, governments can foster accountability and drive evidence-based reforms. Open data serves as a crucial tool for civil society to monitor public safety trends, demanding greater transparency and better resource allocation for security infrastructure.
