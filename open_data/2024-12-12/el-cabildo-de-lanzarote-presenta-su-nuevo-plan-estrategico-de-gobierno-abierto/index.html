El Cabildo de Lanzarote presenta su nuevo Plan Estratégico de Gobierno Abierto

Lanzarote launches a comprehensive strategic plan to strengthen transparency and citizen participation through 84 concrete projects. This initiative aims to modernize public management, ensuring that government actions align with the real needs of citizens while fostering deeper collaboration among institutions. By bridging the gap between the administration and the public, the island seeks to enhance the quality of democracy and encourage active involvement in decision-making processes. The plan is grounded in academic rigor and aligned with international standards, developed with expertise from the Autonomous University of Madrid. This strategic foundation ensures that the framework remains adaptable to future legal reforms, providing a flexible structure for continuous improvement. The focus on cultural and organizational change underscores a commitment to a more open, modern governance model that prioritizes accountability and civic engagement. This development is crucial for open data, as it institutionalizes transparency and participatory mechanisms, which are prerequisites for effective data ecosystems. When governments commit to openness, they create an environment where data can be shared, accessed, and utilized by citizens and organizations. Such initiatives drive the demand for accessible information, ensuring that open data serves not just as a technical asset, but as a tool for genuine democratic empowerment and societal collaboration.
