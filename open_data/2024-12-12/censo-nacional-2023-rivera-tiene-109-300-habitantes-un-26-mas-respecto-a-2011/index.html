Censo Nacional 2023: Rivera tiene 109.300 habitantes; un 2,6% más respecto a 2011

The definitive results of the 2023 National Census reveal a Uruguay undergoing significant demographic and social transformation, characterized by an aging population, a shift toward smaller households, and growing ethnic diversity. This data provides a comprehensive, updated snapshot of the nation’s social fabric, highlighting the move away from large traditional family units toward single-person homes and reflecting a more complex identity landscape regarding gender and ancestry. The census underscores that accurate, granular demographic data is essential for modern governance and social equity. By capturing nuanced information on ethnic-racial backgrounds, gender identities, and household composition, these findings offer a more inclusive representation of the population than previous metrics, allowing for a better understanding of marginalization and representation within society. This article is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the critical role of transparent, publicly accessible statistical data in shaping effective public policy. The detailed breakdown of demographics and infrastructure access empowers researchers, policymakers, and civil society to identify inequalities and tailor interventions, proving that open government data is a foundational tool for democratic accountability and social development.
