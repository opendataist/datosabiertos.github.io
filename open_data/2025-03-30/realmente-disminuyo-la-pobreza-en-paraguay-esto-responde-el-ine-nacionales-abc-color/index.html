¿Realmente disminuyó la pobreza en Paraguay?, esto responde el INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

¿Realmente disminuyó la pobreza en Paraguay?, esto responde el INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

The National Institute of Statistics employs the Permanent Household Survey to measure poverty, in accordance with international standards and the established poverty line methodology. This data is essential for open data initiatives, as it illustrates how official statistics—grounded in verified scientific processes and multi-institutional collaboration—deliver transparent and auditable metrics for evaluating public policy, even amid political controversy. Critics cast doubt on the reported decline in poverty, contending that actual economic conditions do not align with the official figures. Nevertheless, authorities assert that the results stem from a rigorous, internationally validated framework designed to ensure transparency and accurately assess the impact of government policies on vulnerable populations. This article emphasizes the importance of data reliability and methodological transparency in public discourse. It highlights the necessity of open access to statistical sources, enabling citizens and experts to independently verify claims and participate in informed debates on social welfare and economic progress.
