OGP Africa and Middle East Regional Meeting

OGP Africa and Middle East Regional Meeting

Seychelles demonstrates that embedding open government mandates within the highest executive levels ensures sustained political will and strategic oversight. By positioning the OGP secretariat in the Cabinet Office with the Vice-President as lead, the nation integrates transparency directly into core decision-making processes. This structural choice signals a profound dedication to accountability and innovation. This high-level integration facilitates a unified, whole-of-government approach to implementing reforms. Seychles’ adherence to the Fisheries Transparency Initiative and the launch of the National Integrity Coalition illustrate how institutional commitment translates into tangible governance improvements. These actions strengthen the social contract and build essential trust between citizens and public institutions through consistent, visible action. This case is vital for open data advocates as it proves that technical transparency efforts require top-down political backing to succeed. When open governance is centralized at the executive level, it creates an environment conducive to robust data initiatives. Seychelles’ model offers a replicable framework for ensuring that open data policies are not isolated projects but integral components of national democratic strengthening.
