TURISTAS ARGENTINOS ELIGEN A TIENDA DE MAGALLANES COMO UNA DE LAS FAVORITAS EN CHILE

The resurgence of Argentine tourism in Chile highlights a powerful intersection between consumer mobility and digital financial integration. This surge is not merely a travel trend but a testament to the efficacy of open digital ecosystems that facilitate seamless cross-border transactions. By enabling Argentine users to comfortably use their local payment methods in Chile, financial platforms are removing traditional friction points in international commerce, thereby stimulating local economies through increased spending power. Central to this dynamic is the strategic partnership between regional retailers and major digital finance providers. Retailers like Sánchez y Sánchez, which have achieved top national rankings in digital payment usage, demonstrate how localized businesses can leverage broad fintech networks to attract foreign consumers. This success underscores the importance of interoperable payment solutions that allow tourists to engage confidently with local markets, driving significant revenue growth in key sectors such as supermarkets, retail, and tourism infrastructure across border cities and major urban centers. This article is highly relevant to the open data movement as it illustrates the tangible impact of interoperable digital standards on economic outcomes. It showcases how open financial APIs and shared data infrastructure enable real-time visibility into consumer behavior, allowing businesses to adapt quickly to shifting market demands. The case study proves that when data flows freely between financial institutions and retailers, it empowers both consumers and enterprises to optimize value creation, reinforcing the need for transparent, accessible, and standardized data practices to foster robust international trade and economic collaboration.
