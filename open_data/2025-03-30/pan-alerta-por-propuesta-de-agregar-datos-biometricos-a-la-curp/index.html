PAN alerta por propuesta de agregar datos biométricos a la CURP

PAN alerta por propuesta de agregar datos biométricos a la CURP

The PAN warns that incorporating biometric data into the CURP risks personal information privacy. They argue this allows the government to manipulate citizen data under the guise of transparency. This criticism highlights a critical tension in open data governance, where centralizing sensitive information can undermine individual privacy rights. It serves as a cautionary tale about the dangers of unregulated state access to personal records. Consequently, the article emphasizes the vital importance of maintaining independent oversight mechanisms. Protecting data privacy requires vigilance against policies that concentrate power, ensuring citizens remain secure from potential governmental misuse of their sensitive information.
