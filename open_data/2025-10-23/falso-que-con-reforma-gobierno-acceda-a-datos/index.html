Falso que con reforma gobierno acceda a datos

Falso que con reforma gobierno acceda a datos

The approval of the tax reform grants the Tax Administration Service (SAT) access to data from digital platforms, sparking debate over privacy and leading some to controversially label it a "Spy Law." Irene Levy argues that the alarmism is exaggerated, although she acknowledges that ambiguity in the use of biometric data and insufficient government cybersecurity pose significant risks to users. This issue is crucial for open data because it underscores the need for clear regulatory frameworks. Balancing fiscal transparency with the protection of personal data is essential to maintaining public trust in open data initiatives and digital government efforts.
