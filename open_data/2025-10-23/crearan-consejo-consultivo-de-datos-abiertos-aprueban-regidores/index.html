Crearán Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos; aprueban regidores

Crearán Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos; aprueban regidores

This initiative establishes an autonomous technical council to ensure that open data aligns with societal needs, fostering transparency and public trust. By integrating experts from government and civil society, it aims to standardize data quality and promote high-impact social use cases. The structure guarantees continuity across administrative cycles, reinforcing a lasting culture of openness beyond specific political terms. This approach moves beyond mere administrative procedure to institutionalize genuine civic engagement through reliable information. For open data, this model is vital as it demonstrates how technical governance can bridge the gap between raw data and practical public benefit. It highlights the necessity of dedicated oversight to ensure data serves real community needs rather than remaining an abstract bureaucratic output.
