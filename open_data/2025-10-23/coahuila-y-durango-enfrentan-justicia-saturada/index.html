Coahuila y Durango enfrentan justicia saturada

Coahuila y Durango enfrentan justicia saturada

This article highlights the critical disconnect between rising citizen trust in Mexican justice institutions and the unsustainable workloads facing prosecutors. While data indicates that residents of Coahuila and Durango trust their public ministries more than the national average, these agencies are severely strained by a disproportionate volume of cases relative to available personnel. The analysis reveals that despite increases in staffing over recent years, the growth has not kept pace with the surge in recorded crimes. This operational saturation creates significant risks, including procedural delays and errors, which threaten the efficiency of investigations. The situation underscores the urgent need for structural improvements, better training, and technological integration to support the judicial workforce adequately. Relevance to open data lies in the transparent publication of these institutional metrics by INEGI. Such accessible statistical data empowers civil society and policymakers to identify gaps between public confidence and operational reality. By making these disparities visible, open data fosters accountability and drives evidence-based reforms essential for strengthening the rule of law and ensuring effective access to justice for all citizens.
