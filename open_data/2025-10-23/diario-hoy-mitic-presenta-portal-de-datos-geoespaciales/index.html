Diario HOY | Mitic presenta portal de datos geoespaciales

Diario HOY | Mitic presenta portal de datos geoespaciales

The launch of Paraguay’s geospatial data portal underscores a collaborative effort to standardize and share geographic information across government and academic sectors. By establishing interoperability protocols, this initiative ensures that diverse institutional datasets can be accessed and utilized efficiently, thereby breaking down traditional silos. This model demonstrates effective open data governance through shared responsibility between technical and data-providing entities. It highlights that sustainable open infrastructure requires clear cooperation frameworks, enabling stakeholders to contribute to and maintain a unified, publicly accessible resource. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates the practical implementation of spatial data infrastructure. It shows how interoperability and institutional coordination are essential for transforming raw geographic data into a usable, shared national asset that supports transparency and informed decision-making.
