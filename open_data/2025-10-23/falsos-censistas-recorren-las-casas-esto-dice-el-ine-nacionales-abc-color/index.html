¿Falsos censistas recorren las casas? Esto dice el INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

¿Falsos censistas recorren las casas? Esto dice el INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

The article clarifies that allegations of fraudsters impersonating census workers are unfounded rumors circulating on social media, with the INE director confirming that no formal complaints or evidence of such crimes exist. This dispels the panic caused by viral messages claiming that thieves are using Ministry documents to collect biometric data under the guise of census preparation. Instead, the Institute emphasizes that legitimate census workers wear specific vests and carry ID cards with QR codes for official verification. Citizens can validate personnel through police stations or official social media channels, which publish coverage areas and team details, ensuring that data collection remains secure and transparent within authorized procedures. This case highlights the critical importance of open data principles in combating misinformation. By making verification methods and institutional procedures publicly accessible, authorities empower citizens to distinguish truth from falsehood. Transparent communication regarding data collection practices builds trust and prevents the exploitation of public fear, demonstrating how accessible information serves as a vital defense against digital scams.
