Consolida Gobierno de la Ciudad política en transparencia y gobierno abierto - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Consolida Gobierno de la Ciudad política en transparencia y gobierno abierto - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The government of Puebla Capital has achieved national leadership in transparency by securing top honors from the Ministry of Finance for its accountability efforts. Notably, it stands as the sole municipality among those verified to receive a Compliance Dictum in the initial phase of transparency obligation verification. This distinction highlights a shift from mere regulatory compliance to a robust ethical commitment, positioning the city as a benchmark for open governance and institutional trust on a national scale. To support this framework, the administration launched a dedicated Open Government portal that proactively centralizes public data, ensuring accessibility and verifiability for citizens. Beyond digital infrastructure, the government prioritized data protection rights through targeted educational initiatives, including resources for youth and guidelines to protect women from digital violence. These efforts demonstrate a holistic approach to open data, integrating technical accessibility with social awareness to safeguard personal information and empower vulnerable populations. Relevance to open data is evident in the high volume of processed information requests and the favorable resolution of disputes, proving that data is not just published but actively usable. The administration’s strategy illustrates how transparent data practices, when paired with user-centric tools and protection mechanisms, can strengthen civic engagement. This case serves as a model for local governments aiming to transform raw data into a tangible asset for democratic participation and social equity.
