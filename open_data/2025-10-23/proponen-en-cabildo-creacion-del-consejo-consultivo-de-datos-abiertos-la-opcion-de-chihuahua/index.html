Proponen en Cabildo creación del Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Proponen en Cabildo creación del Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua has unanimously approved the creation of the Open Data Advisory Council, establishing a multi-stakeholder body to coordinate and evaluate the municipality’s data policy. This initiative responds to the need for technical expertise in data science and analysis, ensuring that public information genuinely addresses societal needs rather than remaining an abstract administrative formality. The council will serve as an independent technical and civic space, integrating specialized municipal officials with civil society representatives selected through transparent processes. By fostering cross-departmental data sharing, the institution aims to improve governmental efficiency and drive innovation in solving public problems, thereby strengthening institutional trust through the delivery of useful and reliable information. This development is crucial for open data as it institutionalizes civic participation in data governance, moving beyond mere publication to active management. It highlights the growing recognition that robust open data ecosystems require dedicated structures to bridge the gap between technical data handling and real-world societal applications, setting a precedent for how local governments can operationalize transparency through specialized, participatory oversight.
