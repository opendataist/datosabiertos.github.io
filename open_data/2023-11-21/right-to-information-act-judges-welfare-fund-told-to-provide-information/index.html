Right to Information Act: Judges welfare fund told to provide information

The Punjab Information Commission mandated the Judicial Officers Welfare Fund to disclose financial and operational details, reinforcing the principle that public welfare entities must maintain transparency. This decision underscores that stakeholders, including retired judges, possess a legal right to access information regarding fund usage and board meeting allowances, ensuring accountability in the administration of resources intended for the judiciary’s welfare. By rejecting claims for confidentiality and ordering the release of records, the ruling highlights the critical role of access to information laws in preventing opacity within state-funded bodies. It emphasizes that administrative convenience cannot override the public’s right to know how funds allocated for judicial officers’ support are managed, thereby strengthening trust in public institutions. This case is vital to open data advocates as it demonstrates the enforceability of transparency laws even within specialized judicial sectors. It serves as a precedent for holding semi-public organizations accountable, proving that open data initiatives are essential for safeguarding democratic rights and ensuring that public resources are utilized effectively and without hidden agendas.
