Puebla, líder nacional en robos y accidentes carreteros: Inegi

Puebla emerges as a critical case study in open data, leading the country both in vehicle seizures by the National Guard and in highway thefts. This duality highlights how public security indices reveal complex regional disparities, challenging simplistic narratives about safety. The report underscores a significant drop in nationwide seizures and highway crimes compared to the previous year. However, Puebla remains an outlier, concentrating a disproportionate share of these incidents. Analyzing such granular data allows researchers to identify specific logistical and security vulnerabilities that broader national trends might obscure. This information is vital for open data advocates because it demonstrates the power of transparent, granular statistics. Access to detailed government censuses enables civil society to monitor public safety trends, hold authorities accountable, and drive evidence-based policy reforms. Such data transparency fosters trust and supports informed decision-making in public administration.
