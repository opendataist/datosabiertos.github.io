Aviso de Privacidad para la protección de Datos Personales

CHILANGO acts as the data controller for user information collected through its digital platforms, including websites and social media profiles. The company collects personal data such as names, contact details, and navigation behaviors to provide core services, manage commercial relationships, and send editorial content. Primary processing ensures site functionality, while secondary uses allow for statistical analysis and content personalization. Users can opt out of secondary data uses, such as targeted advertising, by contacting the company directly. Data may be transferred without explicit consent to technology providers and commercial partners necessary for service operation. Security measures are implemented to protect against unauthorized access or loss. Users retain full ARCO rights—access, rectification, cancellation, and opposition—exercisable via a formal request that verifies identity and specifies the desired action. This document is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical boundary between private data handling and public transparency. Understanding how private entities manage privacy notices and user consent mechanisms is essential for distinguishing between non-disclosable personal information and data that may eventually contribute to open datasets. It highlights the importance of clear governance structures in digital ecosystems, ensuring that data openness does not compromise individual privacy rights or legal compliance under federal protection laws.
