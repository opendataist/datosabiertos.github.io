Freedom of Information Act

This document serves as a formal response to a request under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act, illustrating how public institutions manage transparency while safeguarding individual privacy. It outlines Illinois Eastern Community College’s commitment to complying with state statutes that guarantee the public’s right to access government records. The text establishes a framework for accountability, demonstrating that educational entities must balance operational disclosure with the protection of sensitive personal data, a critical consideration for any open data initiative seeking ethical legitimacy. The narrative details the specific procedures required to request public records, emphasizing the need for precise descriptions and adherence to established protocols. By defining the roles of the Board of Trustees and the designated FOIA Officer, the article highlights the structural mechanisms that facilitate information retrieval. This procedural clarity is essential for open data advocacy, as it shows how standardized processes enable citizens to effectively engage with institutional information without encountering unnecessary bureaucratic obstacles or ambiguity. Furthermore, the provision of fee schedules and appeal channels underscores the importance of accessible and affordable data acquisition. These elements are vital for ensuring that open data principles are not just theoretical but practically applicable to all community members. The article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the legal and administrative scaffolding necessary to support transparency. It demonstrates how statutory rights, when clearly communicated and enforced, empower the public to hold institutions accountable, thereby fostering a culture of openness and trust in public sector operations.
