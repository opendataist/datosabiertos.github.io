Twitch lanza un Centro de privacidad para concienciar de la importancia de proteger la información personal

Twitch has launched a new Privacy Center to clarify how it manages users’ personal data, following research that highlighted the need for greater transparency. The platform commits to providing understandable tools that empower individuals to decide what information they share and with whom, prioritizing user security and trust in the handling of their data. At the core of this initiative are transparency and control, ensuring that data collection, protection, and use are clear and accessible. Users have full authority over their content, being able to configure who can interact with their streams or download videos. This reflects a shift toward models where privacy is not an add-on, but a fundamental feature designed from the outset to minimize data collection to what is strictly necessary. This announcement is relevant to the field of open data, as it underscores the growing tension between access to information and the protection of digital identity. It illustrates how digital platforms respond to demands for accountability through clear interfaces. By democratizing knowledge about data handling, it sets a precedent for corporate transparency—a key pillar for understanding how information flows impact ethics and regulation within the open data ecosystem.
