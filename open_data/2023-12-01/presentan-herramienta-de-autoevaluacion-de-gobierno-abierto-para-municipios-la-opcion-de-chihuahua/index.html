Presentan herramienta de Autoevaluación de Gobierno Abierto para municipios | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua has formalized a strategic alliance with USAID to co-create an open government self-assessment tool, positioning itself as a national pioneer in this initiative. This agreement not only validates local transparency but also establishes a replicable model for other municipalities, transforming the capital into a benchmark for civic innovation and inter-institutional collaboration. The designed tool enables a comprehensive mapping of government openness by evaluating key criteria such as the quality of responses to citizen requests and the level of public participation. By integrating cross-cutting axes like gender equity and competitiveness, the instrument provides a robust measurement that guides the continuous improvement of public policies and strengthens accountability to citizens. This process is fundamental to the open data movement, as it directly links data transparency with tangible outcomes in public management. By developing precise metrics on access to information and participation, Chihuahua demonstrates how open data can serve as active evaluation tools, promoting a data-driven culture that empowers citizens and optimizes the resolution of social problems through open collaboration.
