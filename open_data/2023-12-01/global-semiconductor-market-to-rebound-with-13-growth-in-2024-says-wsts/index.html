Global semiconductor market to rebound with 13% growth in 2024, says WSTS

The semiconductor industry is navigating a transitional period, with a predicted decline in 2023 followed by a robust recovery in 2024. While discrete components show resilience, integrated circuits face significant contraction this year, though the downturn is less severe than earlier estimates suggested. A major rebound is anticipated next year, driven primarily by a substantial surge in memory sector sales. This growth is expected to be widespread across all segments and regions, reversing the current regional disparities where only Europe currently shows expansion. This forecast is relevant to open_data because it highlights how accessible, real-time industry metrics enable timely revisions and strategic planning. Reliable data transparency allows stakeholders to anticipate market shifts, adjust investments, and understand global supply chain dynamics more effectively.
