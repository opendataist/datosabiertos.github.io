VinFast registra el VF3 en Argentina, un pequeño SUV eléctrico

VinFast, a Vietnamese automaker specializing in electric vehicles, has registered its VF3 model with the National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI). This compact SUV reflects the international growth of new brands that are diversifying the global market toward sustainable mobility. The registration demonstrates how emerging manufacturers protect their intellectual property rights in strategic markets such as Argentina. Although the actual arrival of the vehicle in the country remains uncertain, this action paves the way for a potential market entry, anticipating changes in the local automotive sector. This case is relevant to open data because industrial registrations constitute essential public information. They enable society to track market trends and technological access, promoting transparency regarding which innovations are being protected and which could impact the future availability of goods and services.
