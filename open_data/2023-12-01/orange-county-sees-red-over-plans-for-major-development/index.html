Orange County sees red over plans for major development

The article highlights a critical intersection between local zoning decisions and open data transparency, specifically regarding how local governments disclose development plans. By initially hiding information behind non-disclosure agreements, officials obscured the involvement of major entities like Amazon, demonstrating a concerning trend of limiting public oversight in land use. Public access to this data is essential because such large-scale projects often involve significant environmental and historical impacts, including resource depletion and contamination risks. Without transparent disclosure, communities cannot adequately assess or oppose developments that may harm local ecosystems or heritage sites. Ultimately, this case underscores the necessity of open data in democratic processes. When citizens are denied access to key planning documents, they cannot hold authorities accountable or participate meaningfully in decisions that profoundly affect their environment and infrastructure.
