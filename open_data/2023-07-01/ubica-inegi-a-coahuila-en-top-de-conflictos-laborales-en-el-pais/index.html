Ubica Inegi a Coahuila en top de conflictos laborales en el país

Coahuila exhibits a notably high rate of labor disputes, exceeding the national average and trailing only a few major entities. This statistical prevalence highlights significant friction in the state’s employment sector, reflecting broader trends in worker-employer relations. The majority of these conflicts stem from unjustified dismissals, indicating systemic issues in job security and corporate accountability. Such data provides critical insight into the structural challenges facing labor stability across the region. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistics can reveal hidden societal pressures. By making such labor metrics public, researchers and policymakers can better understand and address the root causes of industrial conflict.

Source: vanguardia.com.mx
