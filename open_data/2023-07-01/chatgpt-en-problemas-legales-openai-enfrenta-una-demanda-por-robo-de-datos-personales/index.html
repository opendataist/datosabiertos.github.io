ChatGPT en problemas legales: OpenAI enfrenta una demanda por robo de datos personales

OpenAI is facing a lawsuit in the United States over the alleged non-consensual use of private data to train its AI models, raising serious ethical and legal concerns about the protection of personal information in technological development. The conflict highlights the absence of U.S. regulation equivalent to Europe’s GDPR, creating a legal gap where the public availability of data does not guarantee its free commercial use, especially when it involves minors or sensitive information used outside its original context. This situation is crucial for the field of open data, as it redefines the boundaries between public and private in the digital age. It forces institutions to rethink their transparency and consent policies, demonstrating that data accessibility must not compromise citizens’ fundamental rights in automated processes.
