¿ChatGPT ha aprendido con tu información personal? Acusan a OpenAI de robar datos para entrenar a su chatbot

¿ChatGPT ha aprendido con tu información personal? Acusan a OpenAI de robar datos para entrenar a su chatbot

OpenAI is facing serious allegations of illegally scraping vast amounts of personal data to train ChatGPT. This unauthorized collection reportedly violates privacy laws and includes sensitive information from various digital platforms, sparking legal concerns over transparency and consent in AI development. The lawsuit highlights the tension between rapid AI innovation and individual data rights. By accumulating massive datasets without explicit permission, the company is accused of bypassing established data broker regulations, raising questions about the ethical boundaries of gathering information for machine learning models. This case is crucial for open data advocates as it underscores the need for clear legal frameworks surrounding data usage. It demonstrates how unchecked data harvesting can infringe on privacy, urging the community to prioritize transparency and consent in the creation and sharing of datasets used to build artificial intelligence systems.
