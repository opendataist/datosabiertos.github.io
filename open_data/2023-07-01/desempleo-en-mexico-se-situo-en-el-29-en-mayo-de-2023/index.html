Desempleo en México se situó en el 2,9% en mayo de 2023

The recent Mexican labor report highlights a nuanced economic landscape, where the unemployment rate experienced a slight monthly increase despite a significant year-over-year decline. This reduction in joblessness, coupled with a growing active population, signals improving labor market conditions and robust economic participation, reflecting the broader national growth trajectory. Simultaneously, there is a notable shift in employment quality and structure. The decrease in underemployment and informal work suggests a gradual formalization of the labor market. With the informal sector shrinking slightly, these trends indicate a move toward more stable and protected employment, which is crucial for long-term economic sustainability and social well-being.
