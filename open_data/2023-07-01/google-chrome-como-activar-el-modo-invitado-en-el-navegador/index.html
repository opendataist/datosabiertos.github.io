Google Chrome: ¿cómo activar el modo invitado en el navegador?

The article details how Guest Mode works in Google Chrome, a feature that allows browsing without leaving local traces of activity, cookies, or passwords. When the session is closed, all data stored on the device is automatically deleted, facilitating shared use of the computer or access to sensitive information without compromising the user’s immediate privacy. However, it is emphasized that this feature does not guarantee complete anonymity, as websites, networks, and internet service providers can still track activity. Additionally, there are technical limitations, such as the retention of downloaded files and bookmarks, and the lack of persistence for configuration changes. Therefore, it is recommended to complement this feature with additional tools, such as VPNs, for robust protection. This content is relevant to the open data community because it illustrates the fundamental distinction between local privacy and network visibility. By understanding how browsers manage user traces, professionals can design better data collection and anonymization strategies, ensuring that access to public or sensitive information respects individual privacy without deliberately concealing legitimate activity for purposes of transparency or analysis.
