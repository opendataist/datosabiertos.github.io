Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The Civic Coalition proposes establishing an Open Import Data Portal to ensure transparency in Argentina’s licensing system. Its main objective is to eliminate the discretion and corruption risks associated with current administrative procedures, which lack clear parameters and adherence to established deadlines. By making all information regarding applications, product types, and processing statuses publicly available, the initiative aims to guarantee predictability and reduce the potential for abuse by officials. The project requires that this information be published in a complete, up-to-date manner, and in open formats, thereby facilitating democratic access. Additionally, it establishes strict limits on administrative response times, stipulating that failure to issue a resolution within the set deadline will result in the automatic approval of the license. This measure seeks to replace current uncertainty with a simple, chronological, and objective process, ensuring that decisions are well-founded and auditable by citizens. This project is significant for open data because it exemplifies how digital transparency serves as a tool to combat corruption and market distortions. By mandating the publication of data in accessible formats, it empowers society to oversee economic policies and foreign exchange management. The initiative reinforces the principle that open data is not merely a technical issue, but a fundamental mechanism for safeguarding the integrity of regulatory systems and fostering institutional trust.
