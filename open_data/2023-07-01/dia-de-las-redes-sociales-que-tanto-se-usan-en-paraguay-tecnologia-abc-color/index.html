Día de las Redes Sociales: ¿qué tanto se usan en Paraguay? - Tecnología - ABC Color

The article highlights the pervasive integration of social media and internet services in Paraguay, with a vast majority of the population aged ten and above actively engaging online. This high penetration rate underscores how digital connectivity has become a fundamental aspect of daily life and social interaction for citizens across the country. Crucially, the data reveals distinct gender-based patterns in how these digital tools are used. Women tend to leverage the internet for health information, government services, and product research, while men are more likely to engage with entertainment content and financial transactions. These behavioral differences provide valuable insights into user demographics and specific needs within the digital ecosystem. This report is relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the public sector’s commitment to releasing detailed, granular statistics through national institutions. By making such comprehensive datasets freely available, authorities enable researchers, developers, and policymakers to analyze trends and design better, more inclusive digital solutions that address the specific realities of diverse population segments.
