Un Mapa 3D de ciudades con edificios y monumentos de todo el mundo sobre los datos abiertos de OpenStreetMap

The F4Map demo showcases the power of OpenStreetMap in generating interactive and detailed geographic representations. It leverages citizen contributions to create smooth 3D maps that adapt their visual richness based on the relevance of each area, accurately displaying buildings, monuments, and natural features. This project highlights how collaborative open data enables advanced technical applications, ranging from civil engineering to virtual worlds. The ability to integrate animated textures and specific objects demonstrates the maturity of open spatial data infrastructures, offering versatile tools for visualizing complex geospatial information without relying on closed-source data. The significance for the open-source community lies in the practical demonstration of the added value provided by shared databases. By showing how a community-driven initiative can support sophisticated technologies, it validates the model of collective production of geographic knowledge, inspiring new applications that prioritize transparency and accessibility of territorial information.
