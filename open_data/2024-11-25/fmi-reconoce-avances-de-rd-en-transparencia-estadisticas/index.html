FMI reconoce avances de RD en transparencia estadísticas

FMI reconoce avances de RD en transparencia estadísticas

The Dominican Republic has received international recognition for its outstanding transparency and statistical quality, significantly surpassing the average of member countries in the current system. This success reflects a deep institutional commitment to the timely dissemination of macroeconomic information, consolidating a solid foundation that ensures the integrity and accessibility of public data. The entity is prepared to evolve toward even more rigorous standards, demonstrating that its technical infrastructure allows for the adoption of superior international standards. This transition will not only validate its management but also align its practices with the best global criteria, ensuring that the generated information is robust, reliable, and comparable at the regional level. This advancement is crucial for open data, as it sets a precedent for accountability and clarity in public management. By adhering to high-quality disclosure standards, the country facilitates the reuse of data by citizens, researchers, and developers, promoting a culture of transparency that strengthens trust in institutions and fosters evidence-based innovation.
