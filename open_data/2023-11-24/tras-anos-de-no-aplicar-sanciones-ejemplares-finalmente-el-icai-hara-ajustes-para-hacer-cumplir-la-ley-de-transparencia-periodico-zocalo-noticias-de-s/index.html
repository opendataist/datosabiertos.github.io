Tras años de no aplicar sanciones ejemplares, finalmente el ICAI hará ajustes para hacer cumplir la Ley de Transparencia | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The article highlights the gap between legal sanctions for transparency violations and their actual enforcement by the ICAI in Coahuila. The new leadership is actively developing annual plans and procedural tools to penalize obligated subjects that consistently fail to upload mandatory information, addressing long-standing impunity. This enforcement drive targets entities such as specific teachers’ unions that repeatedly fail evaluations by withholding basic public data. By implementing precise instructions during quarterly reviews, the institute aims to move from mere diagnosis to tangible sanctions, ensuring that lack of transparency is met with consistent consequences rather than being ignored. The relevance to open data lies in the potential revitalization of enforcement mechanisms. Establishing a framework where non-compliance results in fines, which are then directed back to transparency programs, creates a necessary deterrent. This shift promotes a culture in which proactive disclosure is mandatory, ensuring that public resources are accompanied by accessible, verified information rather than opaque administrative practices.
