Leo, Ona y Nil: estos son los nombres más comunes de recién nacidos en Cataluña en el último año

The Spanish National Statistics Institute publishes comprehensive datasets on baby names, revealing shifting cultural preferences. This annual release highlights regional variations, such as the dominance of specific names in Catalonia, reflecting local identity. Analyzing these records helps researchers understand demographic trends and social dynamics over time. The data serves as a vital resource for understanding how naming conventions evolve across different communities. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public institutions make granular demographic information freely accessible. Such transparency allows citizens and analysts to explore social patterns and contributes to a broader understanding of population trends.
