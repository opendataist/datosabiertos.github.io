El precio medio de la vivienda libre sube un 4,2% en el tercer trimestre, hasta máximos de 2010

The Spanish housing market shows sustained price growth across all segments, reflecting strong demand and limited supply. Newer properties lead the increases, while protected housing also rises, indicating systemic upward pressure on real estate costs rather than isolated fluctuations. Regional disparities highlight significant economic divergences, with islands and certain provinces experiencing sharp appreciation, while others remain stagnant or contract. These variations suggest that local market dynamics and tourism influences play a critical role in shaping valuation trends, resulting in a fragmented national landscape. This data is crucial for open-data initiatives, as it provides the granular, transparent metrics needed to analyze market efficiency and affordability. By making such detailed price indices publicly accessible, researchers and policymakers can better identify inequalities, inform urban planning, and monitor the impact of housing policies on social equity.
