El esperado lector de huellas en pantalla para el iPhone no llegará hasta el 2026 → TransMedia

Apple appears to have abandoned in-display fingerprint technology for the upcoming iPhone 16, as manufacturing equipment is largely obsolete and reserved for older models. Face ID will likely remain the standard authentication method for a significant period, with in-display biometrics potentially delayed until 2026, contrasting sharply with Android’s widespread adoption of the feature. This revelation is highly relevant to open data as it underscores the fragmented nature of hardware lifecycle information within the tech industry. Tracking such supply chain and development shifts through informal leaks highlights the opacity of corporate planning and the critical role of independent verification in understanding market trends. By analyzing these discrepancies between official roadmaps and leaked realities, stakeholders can better assess the true pace of technological convergence. It demonstrates how open, cross-referenced data sources provide deeper insights into corporate strategies than proprietary channels, fostering greater transparency in consumer electronics.
