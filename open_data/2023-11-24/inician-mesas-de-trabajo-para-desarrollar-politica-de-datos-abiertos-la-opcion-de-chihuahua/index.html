Inician mesas de trabajo para desarrollar política de datos abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Inician mesas de trabajo para desarrollar política de datos abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

The municipal government of Chihuahua is finalizing its Open Data Policy through collaborative workshops with civil society and experts. These sessions aim to identify high-value datasets that enhance public access to information, fostering transparency and civic engagement in the city. The initiative seeks to make data accessible and reusable for various purposes, including activities by social organizations, academic research, and public policy proposals. By prioritizing utility, the project ensures that released data actively supports community development and informed decision-making processes. This effort is relevant to open data as it demonstrates a structured approach to government transparency. It highlights the critical role of multi-stakeholder collaboration in defining which data matters, ensuring that open government initiatives directly serve societal needs and drive local progress.
