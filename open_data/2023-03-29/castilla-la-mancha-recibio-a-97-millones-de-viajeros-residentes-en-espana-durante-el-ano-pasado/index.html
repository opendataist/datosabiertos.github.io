Castilla-La Mancha recibió a 9,7 millones de viajeros residentes en España durante el año pasado

Castilla-La Mancha exhibits robust domestic tourism activity, reflecting a national trend in which Spanish residents significantly increased their travel and spending in 2022. Leisure and family visits accounted for the majority of trips, with domestic destinations capturing most of the overnight stays and expenditure. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it illustrates the scale and economic impact of internal mobility. Understanding these consumption patterns enables policymakers and analysts to assess regional contributions to the national economy. The disparity in daily spending between domestic and international travel highlights distinct market dynamics. Open data frameworks must accurately capture these nuances to provide a comprehensive view of tourism’s role in societal wealth and regional development strategies.
