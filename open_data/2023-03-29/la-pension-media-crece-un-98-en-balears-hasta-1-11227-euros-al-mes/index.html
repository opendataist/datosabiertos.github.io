La pensión media crece un 9,8 % en Balears, hasta 1.112,27 euros al mes

The Balearic Islands exhibit a lower average pension than the national average, revealing regional economic disparities in social security coverage. This data highlights significant gender and structural gaps, underscoring the need for equitable distribution mechanisms within public welfare systems. Such detailed demographic and financial breakdowns are essential for open data initiatives. They enable citizens and researchers to analyze social equity, ensuring transparency in how public funds are allocated across different regions and demographic groups.
