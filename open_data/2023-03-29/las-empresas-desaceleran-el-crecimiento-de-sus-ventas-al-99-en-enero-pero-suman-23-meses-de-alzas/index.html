Las empresas desaceleran el crecimiento de sus ventas al 9,9% en enero, pero suman 23 meses de alzas

Business turnover grew significantly, marking twenty-three consecutive months of year-on-year increases since the pandemic dip. Although the January surge moderated compared to the previous month, the underlying trend demonstrates sustained corporate resilience and recovery in the Spanish market. This data is relevant to open data because it exemplifies high-quality, publicly accessible economic statistics from the INE. Such transparent datasets are essential for researchers and developers to analyze market trends accurately without proprietary barriers. The availability of these monthly indicators allows for the creation of diverse data-driven applications. Users can leverage this information to build visualizations and tools that support informed decision-making in business and policy sectors.
