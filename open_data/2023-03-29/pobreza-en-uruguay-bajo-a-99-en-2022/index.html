Pobreza en Uruguay bajó a 9,9% en 2022

Uruguay’s poverty rate declined in 2022, demonstrating that sustained economic growth effectively translates into improved living standards for citizens. This trend underscores the critical role of reliable, publicly available statistics in validating national progress and guiding future policy decisions. The data reveals persistent disparities, with poverty disproportionately affecting female-headed households, children, and Afro-descendant populations. These nuanced insights highlight the necessity of granular, open government data to identify vulnerable groups and address structural inequalities that aggregate national figures might obscure. This report is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparent, accessible statistical repositories empower citizens and researchers to monitor social equity. By making detailed demographic and geographic information publicly available, governments foster accountability and enable targeted interventions that ensure no segment of society is left behind.
