El Icefi participó en el I Congreso Estado Abierto y Gobernanza en Costa Rica.

The article highlights Icefi’s participation in a major congress on open government, emphasizing the critical role of transparency in strengthening public institutions. By engaging with diverse stakeholders, the initiative fostered multisectoral collaboration essential for advancing open governance across Ibero-America, demonstrating how academic and civil society efforts can drive systemic improvements. A key takeaway is the necessity of publishing detailed budgetary data for the justice and security sectors. Making this information publicly available enables the creation of efficiency indicators, allowing for better social auditing and accurate cost estimation for judicial processes. This step transforms opaque spending into actionable insights that inform policy and resource allocation. Ultimately, the article underscores that improved justice and security systems require not only increased investment but also rigorous fiscal transparency. This approach ensures resources are used effectively and aligns with human rights frameworks. This is relevant to open data because it illustrates how releasing specific, structured public expenditure data is vital for accountability, efficiency, and ensuring that government actions truly serve the public interest.
