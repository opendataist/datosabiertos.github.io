Los salarios subieron un 5,3% en 2022 en C-LM, dos décimas por debajo de la media nacional

Los salarios subieron un 5,3% en 2022 en C-LM, dos décimas por debajo de la media nacional

The article examines the evolution of labor costs, highlighting a significant overall increase in 2022 that reverses the declines observed during the pandemic. It emphasizes that this wage growth was broad-based, affecting all demographic groups, regions, and types of employment contracts, with notable disparities favoring temporary workers, younger employees, and foreign nationals. Furthermore, specific sectors such as hospitality and entertainment experienced the most dramatic increases, reflecting structural adjustments in the labor market. These findings are highly relevant to open data initiatives, as they demonstrate how transparent, detailed statistical indicators enable granular analysis of socio-economic trends. By making data accessible, stakeholders can identify inequalities and sector-specific dynamics that aggregate figures might obscure. This transparency fosters informed public debate and facilitates precise policy-making, underscoring the value of open data in understanding complex labor market realities and ensuring accountability in economic reporting.
