Protege tu cuenta de WhatsApp: INAI señala la importancia de la verificación en dos pasos

The INAI issues a critical alert regarding the surge in WhatsApp account theft in Mexico, driven by the app’s massive popularity and inherent vulnerabilities. A recent report indicates an alarming increase in these incidents, highlighting how cybercriminals exploit social engineering to bypass initial security measures. This trend underscores the urgent need for users to recognize that simple authentication is no longer sufficient against sophisticated fraud tactics targeting personal communication channels. To combat this threat, the INAI strongly recommends activating two-step verification, which adds an essential layer of protection by requiring a unique PIN. This measure prevents unauthorized access even if scammers obtain verification codes through deceptive means. By implementing this simple protocol, users can significantly reduce the risk of account takeover, ensuring that their digital identity and private conversations remain secure from malicious actors seeking to exploit basic security gaps. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates the fragility of personal information when digital infrastructures are compromised. It emphasizes that protecting privacy in open communication ecosystems requires proactive user action and robust security practices. The alert serves as a reminder that while data access rights are important, the integrity and confidentiality of personal data must be safeguarded against digital threats, reinforcing the broader open data principle of responsible data stewardship and user autonomy in the digital age.
