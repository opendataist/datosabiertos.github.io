USDA Releases 2022 Census of Agriculture Data

USDA Releases 2022 Census of Agriculture Data

The 2022 Census of Agriculture reveals a paradoxical trend in US farming: while the total number of farms and farmland acreage continues to decline, there is a simultaneous rise in new and young producers. This data highlights a structural shift within the agricultural sector, suggesting that consolidation is occurring even as entry-level participation increases among younger demographics. Financially, agricultural production has grown significantly, with total output and average farm income rising since 2017. Despite these gains, the reduction in the overall number of farms raises serious concerns about sustainability and land preservation. Officials emphasize that this decline serves as a critical wake-up call, urging policymakers to address why fewer farmers are operating larger plots of land over time. This census is vital to the open data community because it provides granular, county-level information directly accessible through public databases. By making such detailed metrics available, it enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to analyze agricultural sustainability, economic disparities, and demographic changes independently. This transparency fosters informed public discourse and supports evidence-based policy decisions regarding food security and rural development.
