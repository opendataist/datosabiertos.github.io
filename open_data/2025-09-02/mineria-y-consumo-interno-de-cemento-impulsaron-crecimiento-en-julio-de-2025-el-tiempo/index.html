Minería y consumo interno de cemento impulsaron crecimiento en julio de 2025 - El Tiempo

Minería y consumo interno de cemento impulsaron crecimiento en julio de 2025 - El Tiempo

The INEI’s data reveals a broad economic recovery in Peru’s industrial sectors, highlighting robust growth in mining, fishing, electricity, and construction. This positive performance underscores the resilience of key productive areas, driven by increased extraction of metallic minerals, hydrocarbon exploitation, and a surge in private construction investments indicated by cement consumption. A critical finding is the massive rebound in the fishing sector, primarily due to high anchoveta captures for industrial use, contrasting sharply with declines in direct human consumption. Simultaneously, electricity generation expanded largely through hydroelectric sources, despite reductions in thermal and renewable energy outputs. This report is vital for open data communities as it demonstrates how granular, publicly accessible statistical indicators enable the tracking of real-time economic health. By analyzing such detailed sectoral metrics, researchers and developers can build better tools for transparency, allowing society to monitor resource management, industrial trends, and economic stability with greater accuracy and immediacy.
