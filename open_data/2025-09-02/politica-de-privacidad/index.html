Política de privacidad

This policy outlines how the website collects and processes personal data, emphasizing that users are subject to varying protection standards based on their jurisdiction, with stricter regulations applying to those in the European Union. The site gathers necessary information through direct user input and automatic usage data, including cookies, to provide services and analyze traffic. It explicitly states that providing data is mandatory for service delivery unless otherwise specified, ensuring that operational integrity is maintained while informing users of their obligations and the consequences of non-disclosure. The core operational strategy involves sharing data with third-party providers for analytics, advertising, and technical hosting, often transferring information to countries with different legal frameworks, such as the United States. Despite these transfers, the entity commits to implementing appropriate security measures and limiting access to authorized personnel and contractors. This approach highlights the tension between global digital service delivery and the localized legal requirements for data sovereignty, requiring transparency about where data resides and how it is utilized by external partners. This document is relevant to open data advocates because it exemplifies the current standard for digital transparency and accountability. By detailing specific legal bases, retention periods, and user rights such as portability and deletion, it demonstrates how organizations can comply with privacy regulations like GDPR. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for assessing the ethical landscape of data usage, ensuring that while services operate openly, individual privacy is protected against unauthorized exploitation or opaque data handling practices.
