Procurement Notices - UNDP-KEN-00460 - Data Portal Development and Support

Procurement Notices - UNDP-KEN-00460 - Data Portal Development and Support

The article outlines a strategic, agile framework for developing a digital data portal in Kenya, emphasizing iterative development and continuous stakeholder collaboration. By prioritizing user needs through design workshops and prototyping, the approach ensures the final product aligns with actual user expectations rather than rigid initial plans. This flexibility allows for adaptability as requirements evolve, fostering a more responsive and effective digital tool that integrates essential features like search, visualization, and secure data access. A critical aspect of this methodology is the focus on sustainability and capacity building. Rather than delivering a static software solution, the process includes comprehensive documentation, training manuals, and a governance framework to ensure long-term operational viability. By incorporating open-source tools and limiting post-launch support to critical maintenance, the project aims to reduce costs while empowering local teams to manage the portal independently. This holistic view extends beyond mere code deployment to include institutional knowledge transfer and structural readiness. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a robust infrastructure for data dissemination and accessibility. The emphasis on standardized technical design, security, and user experience ensures that open data is not just published but is usable and trusted by diverse audiences. Furthermore, the requirement for a local operational presence ensures community engagement, which is vital for maintaining data relevance and accuracy. Ultimately, this structured yet flexible approach demonstrates how effective open data platforms can be built to serve public interest through transparent, accessible, and sustainable digital services.
