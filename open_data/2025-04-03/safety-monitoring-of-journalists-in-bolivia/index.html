Safety monitoring of journalists in Bolivia

Safety monitoring of journalists in Bolivia

Bolivia has launched the Journalist Protection Monitoring System, SISMOPP, to generate open data regarding violations and security conditions affecting media professionals. This initiative aims to foster national debate on protection amid rising violence and impunity, providing a structured database for detailed analysis of attacks and progress in safety measures. By integrating updated information and focusing on violations against women journalists, the system facilitates access for researchers and stakeholders. It complements existing tools to enable comprehensive analysis, ensuring that critical evidence is available to support training initiatives and policy advocacy efforts aimed at preventing future incidents. This project is vital to open data because it transforms isolated reports into structured, accessible public records. These records empower civil society and researchers to analyze trends in press freedom violations, holding authorities accountable through evidence-based transparency. Such open data infrastructure strengthens democratic oversight by making the mechanisms of impunity visible and actionable for the broader community.
