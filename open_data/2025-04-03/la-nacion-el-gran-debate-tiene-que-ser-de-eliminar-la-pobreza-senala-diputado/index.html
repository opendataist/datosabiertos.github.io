La Nación / “El gran debate tiene que ser de eliminar la pobreza”, señala diputado

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INE) reports a historic reduction in poverty rates, highlighting a significant shift toward improved economic conditions. This outcome underscores how accurate statistical transparency serves as a vital tool for public accountability. By relying on verified open data, citizens and lawmakers can objectively assess government performance rather than engaging in unfounded criticism. Opposition leaders dismiss these official figures, launching a campaign to discredit institutional data. This narrative suggests that undermining verified statistics poses a risk to democratic institutions. When open data is politicized, it obscures the reality of social progress and hinders constructive political dialogue about national development strategies. The debate should center on sustainable solutions beyond temporary subsidies, such as job creation. Prioritizing evidence-based policy discussions ensures that open data contributes to meaningful economic reforms. Ultimately, trusting and utilizing transparent statistical information is essential for addressing inequality and fostering a healthy, informed democracy.
