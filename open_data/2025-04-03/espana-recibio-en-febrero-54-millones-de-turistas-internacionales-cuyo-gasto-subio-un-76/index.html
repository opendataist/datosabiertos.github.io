España recibió en febrero 5,4 millones de turistas internacionales cuyo gasto subió un 7,6%

España recibió en febrero 5,4 millones de turistas internacionales cuyo gasto subió un 7,6%

Spain recorded significant growth in international tourism, with a notable rise in both visitor numbers and spending. This increase highlights the sector’s robust recovery and continued appeal to global travelers, particularly from key European markets such as the UK, France, and Germany. The data reflects a broader trend of strengthening demand and economic contribution from tourism across the country. A major shift occurred in accommodation preferences, with non-market lodging experiencing substantial growth alongside traditional hotel stays. Travelers increasingly opted for private rentals, while excursionists without overnight stays also saw a sharp rise. This diversification in travel modes suggests changing consumer behaviors and a demand for more flexible or cost-effective travel options, impacting how destinations manage capacity and infrastructure. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how statistical agencies publish granular, standardized datasets that enable detailed economic and social analysis. By making metrics on spending, origins, and accommodation types publicly available, policymakers, researchers, and businesses can derive actionable insights. These transparent data streams support evidence-based decision-making, allowing stakeholders to monitor trends, allocate resources efficiently, and understand the complex dynamics of the tourism economy without relying on fragmented or opaque information.
