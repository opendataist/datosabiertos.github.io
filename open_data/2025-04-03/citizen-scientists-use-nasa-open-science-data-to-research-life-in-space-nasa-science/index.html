Citizen Scientists Use NASA Open Science Data to Research Life in Space - NASA Science

Citizen Scientists Use NASA Open Science Data to Research Life in Space - NASA Science

The Open Science Data Repository Analysis Working Groups invite volunteers to contribute to understanding how life adapts to deep space environments. By collaborating on topics like radiation effects and microgravity impacts, participants help advance critical scientific inquiry into human and plant health in orbit. This initiative leverages the NASA Open Science Data Repository, offering vast datasets on physiology and biology. With over five hundred studies available, the platform provides essential resources for data analysis, visualization, and meta-analyses, supported by tutorials and expert leadership within nine active groups. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how publicly accessible scientific repositories empower diverse communities to drive discovery. It demonstrates that open data standards and transparent resource sharing enable citizen scientists to tackle complex challenges, fostering collaboration and accelerating progress in life sciences through collective, crowd-sourced analysis.
