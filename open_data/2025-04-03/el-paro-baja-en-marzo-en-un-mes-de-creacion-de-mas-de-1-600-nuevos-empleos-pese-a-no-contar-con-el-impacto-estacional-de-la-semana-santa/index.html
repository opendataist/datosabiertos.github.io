El paro baja en marzo en un mes de creación de más de 1.600 nuevos empleos pese a no contar con el impacto estacional de la Semana Santa

Castilla-La Mancha closed March with a positive outlook for its labor market, achieving the lowest unemployment rate for this month in 17 years and the highest historical level of social security registrations. Despite the absence of the typical seasonal impact from Easter, the region has seen a consistent decline in joblessness and a steady increase in new positions over the last year. This performance underscores the resilience of the regional economy, with all provinces contributing to this growth, signaling a robust recovery and stability in the workforce. The creation of employment has been particularly notable in the logistics sector, which has served as a major driver of economic activity. The government highlights this industry’s role not only in generating jobs but also in promoting sustainability. With thousands of new roles created in recent years, especially in Guadalajara, the data reflects a strategic shift toward more sustainable industrial practices that continue to attract and retain workers, reinforcing the region's competitive advantage in logistics and supply chain management. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly available statistical indicators, such as unemployment and social security affiliation rates, provide transparent metrics for evaluating regional economic health. By making these detailed labor market figures accessible, citizens, researchers, and policymakers can independently verify government claims, track long-term trends, and assess the effectiveness of employment policies, thereby fostering accountability and informed decision-making based on empirical evidence.
