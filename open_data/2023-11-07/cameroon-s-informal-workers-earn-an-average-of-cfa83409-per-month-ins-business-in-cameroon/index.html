Cameroon's informal workers earn an average of CFA83,409 per month (INS) - Business in Cameroon

The Cameroonian Institute of Statistics reveals that the informal sector remains dominated by self-employment, with salaried workers comprising only a small fraction of the workforce. Women are particularly concentrated in self-employment roles, and most enterprises operate with minimal capital, relying heavily on personal savings rather than formal financial institutions for startup funding. Despite the prevalence of verbal agreements and a lack of formal contracts, income levels have risen significantly over the last decade. This growth is especially notable in transport and catering sectors, suggesting that while the sector is largely unregulated, it is dynamic and responsive to economic shifts. Investment efforts are increasing, particularly in industry, indicating a gradual maturation of these small businesses. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it highlights the scale of the "hidden" economy, which escapes traditional tax and accounting records. By documenting these informal entities, statistical bodies create vital datasets that can inform policy, support financial inclusion efforts, and provide a more accurate picture of national economic health, encouraging transparency in sectors that usually remain invisible to official metrics.
