FG to launch e-CRVS, geospatial data repository

The Nigerian government plans to launch the electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics System to revolutionize how vital events are recorded and analyzed. This initiative aligns with continental resolutions urging the automation of civil registration processes, marking a significant shift toward digital governance. By adopting modern digital technologies, the system aims to provide transformative outcomes that streamline data management and secure storage at scale. A central feature of this transition is a Public-Private Partnership with a local ICT provider to operationalize the new system. This collaboration seeks to digitize all forms of civil registrations, including births, marriages, and deaths, ensuring that digital certificates are universally accessible. The implementation of a central management dashboard will allow for the analysis of collated data, thereby supporting more informed decision-making and efficient administrative oversight across the nation. This development is highly relevant to the open data community as it establishes a robust, integrated infrastructure for collecting and analyzing vital statistics. By moving towards a completely electronic system with accessible verification platforms, Nigeria enhances data transparency and availability for both public and private sectors. This foundation supports the achievement of sustainable development goals related to legal identity, offering a scalable model for how open, digital civil registration can improve societal tracking and resource allocation globally.
