Oaxaca, primer lugar a nivel nacional con más obstrucción de casetas

Oaxaca led the country in toll booth blockages in 2022, prompting a strategic deployment of the National Guard to protect infrastructure. This data highlights the persistent challenge of social protests disrupting federal highways, despite a significant year-over-year decrease in incidents nationwide. The situation underscores the tension between civil mobilization and state security protocols. The government attributes its success in preventing substantial financial losses to the “Secure Toll Booth” initiative under the National Security Strategy. By maintaining a tactical presence and deterrence, authorities aim to ensure free transit and protect public assets from collateral damage during these civil actions. This census is vital for open data, as it links statistical performance metrics directly to the design and evaluation of national security policies. It enables transparent monitoring of the National Guard’s effectiveness, allowing society to assess how government interventions balance public order with citizens’ rights in managing highway disruptions.
