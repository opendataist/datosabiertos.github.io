INE dice que fue un "error involuntario" la remisión de información para Mario Aguilera

The Institute clarified that sending the documents to the vice governor was an error, although it initially maintained its constitutional obligation. To facilitate the 2024 Census and navigate administrative restrictions, the agency modified the delivery process to send the documents directly to the requester. This incident highlights the challenges in ensuring unimpeded access to official statistics for legislative bodies, a core principle of open data governance and transparency.
