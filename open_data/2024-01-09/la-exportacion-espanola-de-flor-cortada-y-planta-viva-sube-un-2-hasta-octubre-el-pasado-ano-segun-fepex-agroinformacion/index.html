La exportación española de flor cortada y planta viva sube un 2% hasta octubre el pasado año, según Fepex - Agroinformacion

Spain’s trade in cut flowers and live plants shows a growing export surplus, with live plants remaining the dominant sector. This positive trade balance underscores the robustness of domestic horticultural production, suggesting that Spain is becoming increasingly self-sufficient in these high-value agricultural goods while reducing its reliance on foreign supplies. The decline in imports, particularly for cut flowers, indicates shifting market dynamics and potentially stronger local supply chains. Conversely, the rise in exports of secondary items such as foliage points to expanding international demand for a diverse range of botanical products, reflecting Spain’s competitive edge in specific niches of the global market. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of transparent, granular trade statistics. By making such detailed sectoral metrics publicly available, stakeholders can better analyze economic trends, support policy decisions, and foster innovation in supply chain management and agricultural transparency.
