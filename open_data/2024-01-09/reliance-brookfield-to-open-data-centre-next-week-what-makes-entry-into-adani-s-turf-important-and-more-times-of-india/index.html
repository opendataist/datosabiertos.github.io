Reliance-Brookfield to open data centre next week: What makes entry into Adani's turf important and more - Times of India

Major Indian corporations are rapidly expanding data center infrastructure, driven by strict government localization norms and soaring digital adoption. This strategic shift ensures critical national data remains within domestic borders, aligning perfectly with open data principles that prioritize secure, localized governance. By investing billions, these entities are building the foundational hardware necessary for robust digital ecosystems. Their competitive entry validates the market’s growth potential, encouraging further transparency and accessible digital services that benefit society. This expansion highlights how private investment, guided by public policy, creates the physical architecture for open data initiatives. It underscores the necessity of local data sovereignty in fostering trustworthy, globally competitive information societies.
