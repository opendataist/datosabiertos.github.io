The INEPESCA has submitted an updated fleet census to state authorities to secure partnerships for providing life jackets to fishermen in Nueva Esparta. This initiative addresses critical safety concerns and aims to prevent maritime accidents by ensuring that every vessel is equipped with the necessary safety gear. The project seeks joint government funding to distribute thousands of life vests across all municipalities, covering both active and inactive vessels. By prioritizing safety equipment, the plan also aims to stimulate the fishing sector, with the goal of reactivating a significant portion of idle boats through available credit programs. This effort is relevant to open data because it relies on a precise, up-to-date inventory of maritime assets. Transparent data sharing between regional and national governments is essential for accurate resource allocation, ensuring that safety interventions and economic support are effectively targeted to the actual population of vessels and crew.
