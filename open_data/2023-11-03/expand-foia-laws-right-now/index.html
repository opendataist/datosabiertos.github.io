Expand FOIA laws right now

Expand FOIA laws right now

Governor Gretchen Whitmer has failed to fulfill her campaign promise to expand Michigan’s transparency laws, leaving both her office and the Legislature exempt from public disclosure requirements. While the state’s Freedom of Information Act mandates document access for most public agencies, these powerful political bodies remain shielded from scrutiny, resulting in consistently poor transparency rankings. This lack of accountability prevents the public from understanding how top officials exercise their authority and manage taxpayer funds. Keeping these leaders’ records secret undermines democratic principles, as they wield significant power without the corresponding obligation to operate openly, a rarity shared only by Massachusetts among U.S. states. This issue is crucial for open_data advocates because it highlights systemic gaps in government accessibility. True openness requires that all branches of government, including the executive and legislative leadership, subject their records to public request. Pushing for legislative reform ensures that data sharing becomes universal rather than selective, fostering genuine transparency in governance.
