Should littering fines be more strict? | Newstalk

Should littering fines be more strict? | Newstalk

Dublin City Council’s drastic reduction in littering fines contradicts expert assessments of worsening waste problems, highlighting a critical enforcement gap. This decline raises concerns about the effectiveness of local environmental policies and the tangible impact of municipal penalties on public behavior. Such discrepancies underscore the importance of transparent, open data in holding government bodies accountable for environmental stewardship and public health.
