The article highlights a sustained rise in living costs driven by essential goods and energy, exacerbating the gap between basic needs and wages. This economic strain underscores the critical importance of open data in tracking inflation trends and visualizing regional disparities in real time. It transforms raw statistics into actionable insights that raise social awareness. Ultimately, transparent data fosters accountability, enabling more effective advocacy for wage policies. It ensures that statistical information serves the public interest by clarifying the severity of the purchasing power crisis across different Ecuadorian cities.
