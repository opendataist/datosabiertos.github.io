Free Access to Weather and Climate Data

Free Access to Weather and Climate Data

MeteoSwiss is launching free access to meteorological data under Switzerland’s Open Government Data initiative. This move fosters transparency and innovation by allowing unrestricted societal and commercial use of administrative information. The decision aligns with federal laws aimed at promoting public participation. By making these resources openly available, the government seeks to maximize their utility across diverse sectors. This release is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how government agencies can remove barriers to critical information. It highlights the potential for public datasets to drive widespread economic and research advancements without financial constraints.
