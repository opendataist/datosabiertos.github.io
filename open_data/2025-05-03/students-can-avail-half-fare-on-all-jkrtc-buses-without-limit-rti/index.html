Students Can Avail Half-Fare on All JKRTC Buses Without Limit: RTI

Students Can Avail Half-Fare on All JKRTC Buses Without Limit: RTI

This article clarifies a widespread misconception regarding student bus fares in Jammu and Kashmir, confirming that no limit exists on the number of students eligible for half-fare concessions on public transport. The right to this benefit relies solely on presenting a valid identity card, dispelling false claims that drivers can restrict access to only a few students per bus. The RTI response serves as a crucial example of how transparency mechanisms correct misinformation and protect public rights. By officially documenting the policy, the government provides citizens with authoritative evidence to challenge violations, ensuring that service providers adhere to statutory obligations without arbitrary restrictions. This is highly relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the value of accessible, accurate government records in empowering citizens. When official data regarding public services is transparent and easily verifiable, it enables individuals to assert their rights effectively, reducing exploitation and ensuring equitable access to subsidized transportation for students and other vulnerable groups.
