China implementa auditorías obligatorias para reforzar protección de datos personales

China implementa auditorías obligatorias para reforzar protección de datos personales

China has implemented new regulations requiring organizations that process large volumes of personal data to conduct periodic compliance audits. This strict approach aims to ensure that companies review their practices for collecting, storing, and using information, thereby transforming audits into essential tools for security and operational transparency. The regulation establishes mechanisms to prevent audit manipulation and grants regulators the authority to demand professional inspections when significant risks are detected. By prohibiting the same auditor from repeatedly overseeing the same entity, the rules promote independence and integrity in the audit process, ensuring that privacy protection responds to societal demands rather than serving as a mere formality. This development is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how strict governance of personal data lays the foundation for trust in information handling. By balancing individual protection with clear standards, it enables an environment where data can be shared and reused ethically, fostering data-driven innovation without compromising citizens' rights or national security.
