AMLO veta a los dos nuevos comisionados del INAI designados por el Senado - Noticaribe

The Mexican president vetoed the appointment of new commissioners to the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), challenging the Senate’s selection and raising concerns about institutional autonomy. This conflict underscores the tension between executive power and independent oversight bodies that are essential for democratic accountability. For open data enthusiasts, it signals potential risks to transparency mechanisms and the protection of the integrity of public information.
