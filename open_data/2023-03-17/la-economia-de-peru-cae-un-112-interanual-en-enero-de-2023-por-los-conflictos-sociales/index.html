La economía de Perú cae un 1,12% interanual en enero de 2023 por los conflictos sociales

Peru experienced a contraction in economic activity in early 2023, primarily driven by social unrest and political instability that paralyzed essential sectors such as mining and construction. Although aggregate growth for the previous year remained positive, the current decline highlights the severe fragility of the economy when disrupted by civil conflict and infrastructure blockages, which impede the free movement of goods and people. The relevance of this report to open data lies in how transparent, official statistics from the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) enable stakeholders to quantify the real-world impact of socio-political crises. By making granular data on sectoral performance accessible, such transparency allows researchers, policymakers, and the public to critically analyze the correlation between governance failures and economic downturns, thereby fostering accountability. Ultimately, these findings underscore the critical importance of reliable, open statistical infrastructure in crisis-prone environments. They demonstrate that timely and detailed public data is essential for understanding the complex interdependencies between political volatility and macroeconomic indicators, serving as a vital tool for informed decision-making and assessing the true cost of social unrest on national development.
