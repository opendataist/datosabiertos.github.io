La emigración balear al extranjero se duplica en 10 años

The Balearic Islands have experienced significant emigration, largely driven by the economic crisis that compelled many foreign-born residents to return to their home countries. This outflow of population underscores how global economic downturns directly affect local demographics and labor mobility. Most of these former residents relocated to the Americas, particularly to Argentina, while substantial numbers settled in European countries such as France and the United Kingdom. This distribution reveals complex migration corridors and the enduring demographic impact of past economic instability. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the value of linking civil registry records with migration trends. Such transparency enables researchers to better understand socio-economic resilience and to plan more effectively for future population dynamics in open communities.
