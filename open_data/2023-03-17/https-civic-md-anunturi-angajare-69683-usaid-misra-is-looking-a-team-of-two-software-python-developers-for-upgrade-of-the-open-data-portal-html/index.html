Open Data
ES EN

This archived job posting highlights a critical technical intervention aimed at modernizing Moldova’s Open Data Portal, underscoring the ongoing need to maintain the infrastructure that supports government transparency. By upgrading the core CKAN platform from an outdated version to its latest iteration, the project seeks to enhance the portal’s reliability and usability for both public data providers and citizens. This effort ensures that the foundational technology remains robust enough to handle real-time data retrieval and automated processing, which are essential for maintaining public trust in digital governance systems. The scope of work extends beyond simple software updates to include improving interoperability, security, and the overall user experience. Upgrading extensions and refining the visual theme addresses specific functional gaps that previously hindered effective data management. These improvements facilitate seamless integration with other state information systems, thereby promoting a more interconnected and efficient digital ecosystem. The focus on updating integration guides and user manuals further emphasizes a commitment to sustainable capacity building within the E-Governance Agency. This initiative is highly relevant to open data advocacy because it demonstrates the practical challenges of keeping open data platforms accessible and functional over time. It illustrates how technical maintenance is inseparable from the political goal of accountability, ensuring that data remains freely available, machine-readable, and safe for public use. The project serves as a case study in how international development support can directly strengthen domestic institutions’ ability to manage and share public information, reinforcing the principles of transparency and inclusive digital recovery.
