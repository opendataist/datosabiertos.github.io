Política de Privacidad: Ministerio de Hacienda y Función Pública

Política de Privacidad: Ministerio de Hacienda y Función Pública

This text clarifies that links to external websites on the Ministry’s portal do not imply endorsement, supervision, or responsibility for third-party content. It emphasizes user autonomy in managing privacy settings and cookies, while strictly distancing the government entity from the accuracy or legality of information found at these external addresses. The relevance to open data lies in defining the boundaries of public accountability when government platforms facilitate access to diverse information sources. It highlights the importance of clear transparency regarding data provenance and liability in digital public services. By establishing these legal limits, it encourages users to critically evaluate external data sources rather than assuming institutional validation of any linked information. Ultimately, this framework protects the Ministry’s integrity while promoting responsible data navigation. It underscores that open access does not equate to content assurance, requiring citizens to take personal responsibility for verifying external resources. This distinction is vital for maintaining trust in open data ecosystems by clearly separating official communications from unverified third-party contributions.
