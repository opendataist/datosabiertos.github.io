INAI: exigen ir contra Plan B del INE por vulneración de datos

INAI: exigen ir contra Plan B del INE por vulneración de datos

The INAI Advisory Council urges the Institute to challenge the constitutionality of Electoral Plan B, arguing that relocating service centers to municipal buildings compromises the security of personal data. This shift risks exposing sensitive voter information, such as biometrics and addresses, to third-party environments where the Institute cannot guarantee adequate physical or digital safeguards. Consequently, the integrity of the federal voter registry is potentially threatened by insufficient protection measures against interference or damage. This situation highlights the critical intersection between administrative logistics and fundamental privacy rights in democratic processes. The obligation to protect personal data falls squarely on public institutions, yet moving infrastructure to uncontrolled spaces creates inherent vulnerabilities that hinder effective data governance. This matter is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of maintaining robust security protocols for public datasets. It serves as a reminder that transparency initiatives must not compromise the confidentiality and integrity of sensitive individual information, emphasizing that secure handling is a prerequisite for any data release or public service operation.
