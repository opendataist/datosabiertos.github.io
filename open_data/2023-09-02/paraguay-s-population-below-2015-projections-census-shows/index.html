Paraguay's population below 2015 projections, census shows

The census reveals significant demographic shifts, including lower-than-expected population growth, declining fertility rates, and reduced life expectancy. These discrepancies highlight gaps between official projections and actual societal changes, underscoring the critical role of accurate data in forecasting national needs. Additionally, updated migration patterns and improved methodologies for counting indigenous populations demonstrate evolving statistical realities. The increased recognition of indigenous communities reflects better data collection techniques rather than just demographic surges. This update is vital for open data initiatives as it emphasizes the necessity of transparent, regularly refreshed datasets. Accurate public statistics enable better policy-making and resource allocation, proving that accessible, high-quality data is essential for understanding complex social trends.
