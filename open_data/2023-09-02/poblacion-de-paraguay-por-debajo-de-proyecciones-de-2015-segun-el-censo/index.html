Población de Paraguay por debajo de proyecciones de 2015, según el censo

Población de Paraguay por debajo de proyecciones de 2015, según el censo

The Paraguayan census reveals population figures significantly lower than projected, confirming declining fertility rates and shifts in life expectancy. This data challenges previous demographic assumptions, highlighting the need for updated statistical models that account for recent health and social changes. These findings underscore the importance of open data in correcting public policy. By making granular census details accessible, researchers and governments can better understand migration trends and ethnic demographics, ensuring resources align with actual needs rather than outdated estimates. Open data facilitates this transparency, allowing for independent verification and analysis of official statistics. It empowers civil society to scrutinize governmental projections and advocate for evidence-based decisions, particularly regarding marginalized indigenous communities and overall national development strategies.
