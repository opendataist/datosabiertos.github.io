Mental health patients ‘waited over 5.4m hours in A&E last year’, Labour claims

A political party utilized freedom of information requests to expose alarming delays in mental health and emergency care, highlighting that some patients endure excessive waits that strain the healthcare system. These findings underscore the critical importance of open data in holding institutions accountable for service failures and patient safety. This dynamic illustrates how transparent data empowers stakeholders to demand better governance and resource allocation, driving necessary policy shifts. Ultimately, the incident demonstrates that accessible public records are vital for identifying operational bottlenecks.
