¿Por crisis?, cae 6% matrícula de colegios privados en Coahuila

The decline in private school enrollment in Coahuila highlights how the pandemic exacerbated economic pressures on families, forcing a shift away from fee-based education. This data underscores the critical role of public information platforms in tracking societal impacts beyond traditional government censuses. Such transparency allows civil society to verify institutional claims and understand structural changes in the education sector. By making these metrics accessible, open data initiatives empower citizens to analyze trends and hold educational providers accountable for their financial and operational realities. Ultimately, this case demonstrates that accessible, granular data is essential for comprehending the long-term effects of crises. It reveals how external shocks permanently alter social behaviors, emphasizing the need for continuous, independent monitoring of public interest indicators to ensure informed public debate.
