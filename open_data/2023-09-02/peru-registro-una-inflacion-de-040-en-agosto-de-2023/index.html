Perú registró una inflación de 0,40% en agosto de 2023

The variation in Peru’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) during August 2023 reveals an economic scenario marked by inflationary pressure in sectors essential to quality of life. Broad-based increases in food, transportation, and basic services reflect difficulties in accessing necessary goods, directly impacting the population’s purchasing power and underscoring the need to monitor these indicators to understand the country’s socioeconomic reality. Price dynamics show a clear divergence between price hikes in fuels and perishable products, and price declines in public utilities and certain food categories. This mix of inflationary and deflationary factors highlights how tariff policies and international market conditions interact with local supply, creating a complex landscape that requires detailed analysis to discern underlying trends beyond aggregate statistics. This report is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it democratizes access to vital information about the domestic economy. By publishing these detailed breakdowns, the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) promotes transparency and enables researchers, media outlets, and citizens to analyze, visualize, and critique the data, fostering a more informed society capable of participating in debates on public policies grounded in accessible, evidence-based insights.
