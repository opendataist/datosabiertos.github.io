Non-profit remembers those lost to opioid overdose on International Overdose Awareness Day

This article highlights a community-led response to the opioid crisis through Milestone Community Wellness, a peer-driven nonprofit dedicated to reducing overdose deaths and supporting recovery. The organization recently hosted an International Overdose Awareness Day event in Logan County, utilizing a symbolic memorial to honor individuals lost to substance use in the region. By placing luminaries bearing the names of the deceased, the group aims to humanize the statistics and foster a collective time for remembrance and action, while also addressing related health consequences like hepatitis. Beyond memorialization, the nonprofit actively distributes NARCAN nasal spray and provides harm reduction education to attendees. This approach reflects their broader mission to meet individuals where they are, offering early intervention, peer support, and resources for reintegration into the community. The event underscores a commitment to breaking cycles of trauma and incarceration by leveraging the lived experiences of those in recovery to guide their advocacy and service delivery. This story is relevant to open_data as it illustrates the intersection of community advocacy and health statistics. While the article cites official state data on overdose deaths, it also notes that recorded numbers likely underestimate the true scope due to privacy requests. This discrepancy highlights the importance of transparency in data reporting and demonstrates how grassroots organizations use both quantitative metrics and qualitative storytelling to drive policy changes and resource allocation for public health initiatives.
