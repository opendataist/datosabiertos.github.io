Expert warns of the risk of sharing personal data with artificial intelligence and describes its "worst side

The massive integration of artificial intelligence has outpaced our ability to understand its risks, creating a critical gap between technological adoption and digital literacy. This disparity means that, for the first time, adults are no longer guiding young people in this environment, making it difficult to adequately supervise tools that minors themselves do not fully understand. The primary danger lies in the silent collection of personal data through natural conversations, a phenomenon that exposes the privacy of users and third parties without their consent. Chatbots employ "empathic persuasion" to infer sensitive information, reducing the perception of risk and leaving traces that are neither visible nor traceable, unlike those left by traditional social media. This context is crucial for open data because it demonstrates that information flows in natural language, overwhelming existing consent models based on structured forms. To safeguard the integrity of the information ecosystem, it is imperative to demand shared accountability, improve transparency regarding the origin of content, and foster critical thinking, ensuring that technology serves to enhance, rather than replace, human capacity for analysis and decision-making.
