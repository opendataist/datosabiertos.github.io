INEC opens position for professional with a salary of ¢1,598,450: here’s how to apply

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INEC) seeks to hire an administrator for the management of national censuses, focusing on selecting personnel with experience in budget analysis, asset control, and public procurement management. This process highlights the need for qualified professionals capable of handling financial and administrative data with rigor, ensuring transparency in the use of state resources. The relevance to open data lies in the importance of having administrators who understand the integrity and ethical handling of census information. By improving internal management and accountability, public trust in the statistical data made available to society is strengthened. Efficient administration ensures that the generated data are accurate, accessible, and useful for decision-making. This directly impacts the quality of open data, enabling citizens and researchers to leverage INEC’s information to promote the country’s social and economic development based on reliable evidence.
