TVE hace público cuánto cobró Ramón García por presentar el 'Grand Prix'

The article reveals how RTVE used transparency portals to disclose high salaries for the revived "Grand Prix" hosts, highlighting the financial structure behind a nostalgic TV format. This transparency allows citizens to scrutinize public spending and understand how taxpayer money supports popular cultural programming. Such disclosure mechanisms are vital for open data initiatives, as they enable accountability and public oversight of broadcasting costs. By making detailed remuneration data accessible, RTVE demonstrates a commitment to information freedom, allowing researchers and journalists to analyze labor costs in public media effectively. Ultimately, this case underscores the importance of accessible government data in fostering trust between institutions and the public. Open data transforms opaque financial decisions into clear, verifiable information, empowering society to evaluate the value and efficiency of national television investments and ensuring greater democratic control over public resources.
