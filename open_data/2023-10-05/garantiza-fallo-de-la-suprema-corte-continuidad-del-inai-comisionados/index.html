Garantiza fallo de la Suprema Corte continuidad del INAI: Comisionados

Garantiza fallo de la Suprema Corte continuidad del INAI: Comisionados

The Supreme Court’s resolution concerning the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information, and Personal Data Protection (INAI) ensures the institution’s continuity by affirming that the appointment of commissioners is mandatory, not optional. This ruling elevates the fundamental rights to access information and protect personal data above political interests, providing legal certainty for the autonomous body’s operations and its capacity to effectively defend citizens’ rights. This decision is critical for the open data ecosystem, as it safeguards the institutional stability necessary to enforce transparency laws. By preventing political obstruction in the formation of the regulatory board, the ruling protects the mechanisms that ensure government accountability and the public’s right to access public records, which are foundational to an informed society. Ultimately, the article highlights the vital role of independent oversight bodies in combating corruption and fostering a collaborative governance system. It underscores that robust, technically specialized institutions are essential for maintaining democratic integrity, demonstrating that legal protection for transparency agencies is a prerequisite for meaningful open data initiatives and citizen empowerment.
