Samoa's quarterly G.D.P increases

Samoa’s economy demonstrates robust recovery, achieving four consecutive quarters of positive growth driven primarily by a resurgence in international travel. This surge has significantly boosted the services sector, particularly accommodation, restaurants, and business services. The data indicates that post-pandemic restrictions have been effectively overcome, allowing key industries to regain momentum and contribute substantially to national economic stability. This sustained growth highlights the critical importance of timely and transparent statistical reporting for understanding national economic health. By accurately capturing sector-specific contributions and adjusting for peak arrival periods, statistical bodies provide the empirical foundation necessary for informed policy decisions. Such detailed accounting ensures that economic narratives are grounded in verified data rather than speculation, fostering trust among stakeholders and international partners. For the open data community, this case underscores the value of accessible, granular economic datasets. When governments publish high-frequency GDP reports and sectoral breakdowns, it enables researchers and developers to build models that predict trends and assess policy impacts. Transparent data ecosystems empower better decision-making, allowing for a deeper understanding of how specific industries drive national recovery and resilience in the face of global challenges.
