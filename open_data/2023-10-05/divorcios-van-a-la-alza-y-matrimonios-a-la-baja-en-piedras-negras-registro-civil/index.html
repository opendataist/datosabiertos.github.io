Divorcios van a la alza y matrimonios a la baja en Piedras Negras: Registro Civil

Coahuila leads the nation in divorce rates while marriages decline, a trend clearly visible in Piedras Negras. This shift toward unstable unions reflects broader societal changes in family structures. Open data is crucial here, as it allows researchers and policymakers to monitor these demographic shifts and understand the underlying causes. By making statistics accessible, transparency empowers communities to address the growing instability affecting social cohesion.
