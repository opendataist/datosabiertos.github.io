Cantabria duplica la media nacional de personas sin hogar, con cerca de 600 personas - El Faradio | Periodismo que cuenta

The article highlights a significant rise in homelessness in Cantabria, where the rate has more than doubled compared to national averages. This surge underscores the urgent need for effective social interventions and reveals that the majority of affected individuals prioritize securing housing and employment as the foundational steps out of exclusion. Central to the response is the "Housing First" methodology, which posits that stable accommodation must be the initial step before addressing other issues like health or job training. This approach is validated by data showing that most homeless individuals explicitly request housing, suggesting that traditional conditional aid is less effective than providing immediate shelter to rebuild social integration. This report is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical surveys to expose disparities in social welfare distribution. It demonstrates how transparent, quantitative data can drive targeted policy changes, such as shifting resources toward housing-first models and raising public awareness about discrimination, thereby using evidence to combat social invisibility and guide ethical decision-making.
