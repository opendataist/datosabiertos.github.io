DNCD recibe cerficación de cumplimiento de la Nortic A3 - Barrigaverde.net

The Dominican National Drug Control Directorate has obtained certification for publishing open data, ensuring that its information is accessible and reusable by society and businesses. This milestone underscores the institution’s strong commitment to transparency and the effective use of information technology for the public good. Adherence to open data standards is essential for promoting accountability and enabling civic engagement through accessible government information.
