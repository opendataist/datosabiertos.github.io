VI&E Solutions and Neurobotx begin buildup of first 900 vertiports on Metapilot

Volatus Infrastructure & Energy Solutions has partnered with Neurobotx to deploy the first vertiport on the Boeing-backed Metapilot platform. This collaboration leverages digital twin technology to simulate eVTOL operations, enabling precise placement of infrastructure to optimize travel times and safety. By generating synthetic data, the platform allows stakeholders to calculate optimal vertiport locations globally, transforming how aerial mobility systems are planned and integrated into existing transport networks. The Metapilot initiative democratizes access to complex industry simulations, allowing the public to experience air taxi operations through a free gaming interface. This convergence of entertainment and data collection serves a dual purpose: it educates millions about sustainable future mobility while simultaneously gathering critical insights for regulators. The platform models various impacts, including congestion reduction, job creation, and environmental benefits, providing a realistic financial and business framework for investors and government partners. This development is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how synthetic datasets can bridge the gap between theoretical planning and real-world application in emerging technologies. By making simulation data accessible to a broad audience, including gamers and community members, the project fosters transparency and public engagement in urban planning. It highlights the value of open, shareable synthetic data in accelerating regulatory approval and societal acceptance of innovative transportation solutions.
