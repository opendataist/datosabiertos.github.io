Inflación de Uruguay fue de 0,61% en septiembre de 2023

Uruguay’s inflation remains stable, driven primarily by rising costs in the food and transportation sectors. This highlights how essential commodity prices, particularly fuels and fresh produce, significantly influence consumer spending patterns and overall economic stability. The specific shifts in fruit and vegetable prices demonstrate the volatility inherent in agricultural markets. These fluctuations directly impact household budgets, underscoring the need for transparent price-tracking mechanisms to help citizens manage their finances effectively amidst changing market conditions. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it exemplifies the critical need for accessible, standardized economic statistics. Making such detailed inflation metrics publicly available fosters transparency, enabling researchers and policymakers to analyze economic trends and make informed decisions regarding public welfare and fiscal policy.
