Museums in Oaxaca demonstrated a significant post-pandemic recovery in 2023, surpassing one million visitors. This resurgence highlights renewed public engagement with cultural heritage, indicating that institutions are successfully reclaiming their role in the community after periods of interruption. The data reflects a broader national trend of increasing attendance, signaling a healthy restoration of cultural participation across the country. This context is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical reporting to track societal recovery and resource allocation. When government bodies such as INEGI publish detailed visitor metrics, they empower researchers and policymakers to analyze trends, evaluate the effectiveness of cultural policies, and make informed decisions about museum sustainability and education. Furthermore, the alignment of these statistics with international themes on education and research emphasizes the critical role of data in supporting holistic learning experiences. By making this information publicly available, authorities facilitate a deeper understanding of how museums contribute to a more aware and inclusive society, ensuring that cultural institutions remain integral to both historical preservation and modern educational goals.
