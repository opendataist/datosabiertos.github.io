La tasa de desempleo mejora levemente en Ecuador, en marzo de 2025

Ecuador showed mixed labor results in March 2025, with slightly lower unemployment but stagnant underemployment. This highlights that formal job security remains elusive for a significant portion of the workforce, despite political promises to create millions of jobs. This data is critical for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent statistics reveal the gap between government pledges and economic reality. By analyzing disaggregated employment metrics, analysts can hold leaders accountable and inform policies that address the structural underemployment affecting both employees and independent workers.
