Coahuila arranca proceso de homologación constitucional en materia de transparencia | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Coahuila aligns its transparency laws with federal reforms, ensuring that the right to information remains universal and free. This shift strengthens institutional coordination and data protection, marking a significant step toward respecting human rights and improving governance efficiency. The reform clarifies responsibility for resolving review requests, assigning them to internal control bodies and audit secretaries. By centralizing these functions, the state promotes a more functional model that integrates seamlessly with national systems. This update is vital for open data, as it establishes a robust legal framework for public access. Enhanced transparency mechanisms facilitate better data availability and accountability, empowering citizens through improved access to essential government information and secure handling of personal data.
