Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

This document outlines 13 MEDIA, S.C.’s privacy policy, detailing how it collects, processes, and shares personal data in compliance with federal laws. The company emphasizes that it gathers specific identifiers and financial information solely to verify identity, meet legal obligations, and provide requested services. It operates under strict principles such as transparency, consent, and proportionality, ensuring that data handling is lawful and limited to necessary purposes. Crucially, the policy clarifies that while internal staff manage data, it may be shared with third parties like lawyers or notaries for legal formalities or authority requests, unless the user explicitly opts out. Users retain full control over their information, with established channels to access, correct, cancel, or oppose data processing. This mechanism underscores the importance of user agency in data management, allowing individuals to limit disclosure unless legal mandates override such requests. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the tension between legal compliance and transparency. While it protects individual privacy rights through specific regulatory frameworks, it serves as a counter-model to open data practices that prioritize widespread accessibility. Understanding these restrictions is vital for developers and policymakers aiming to balance public interest in data transparency with the fundamental right to privacy, illustrating how private entities must navigate legal boundaries when deciding what information remains proprietary versus open.
