Resolución 073-2025: MAP establece lineamientos para mejorar la interoperabilidad y gobernanza de datos públicos

Resolución 073-2025: MAP establece lineamientos para mejorar la interoperabilidad y gobernanza de datos públicos

The Dominican Republic has established a National Framework for Interoperability and Data Governance to modernize its public administration. This regulatory shift prioritizes a more efficient and transparent digital ecosystem by enabling seamless information exchange across government institutions, thereby reducing bureaucratic friction and enhancing the quality of digital services for citizens. Data governance is positioned as a critical pillar for evidence-based policymaking and innovation. By standardizing data exchange and ensuring secure, responsible handling of public information, the state aims to align with international best practices. This approach fosters trust and accountability, ensuring that public resources are managed efficiently while empowering agencies to make decisions grounded in reliable data. This development is vital for open data because it creates the technical and legal infrastructure necessary for data sharing. Without standardized interoperability, open data initiatives often fail due to fragmented systems and incompatible formats. By mandating common norms and establishing an inter-institutional committee, this framework ensures that data is not only published but is also accessible, usable, and integrated across the government, unlocking its potential for public scrutiny and civic innovation.
