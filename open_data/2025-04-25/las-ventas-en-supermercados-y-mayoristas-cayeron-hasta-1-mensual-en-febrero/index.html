Las ventas en supermercados y mayoristas cayeron hasta 1% mensual en febrero

The report highlights divergent retail trends, with supermarket sales showing moderate growth in constant prices despite sharp nominal increases driven by inflation. This suggests underlying consumer stability in major retail channels, contrasting with significant declines in wholesale club volumes. Such distinctions are crucial for understanding the difference between real purchasing power and the effects of currency devaluation. The relevance of open data lies in the transparency of these economic indicators, which enable analysts to separate inflationary noise from actual volume changes. By accessing detailed breakdowns by product category, researchers can identify specific sectors that are resisting or succumbing to economic pressure. This granular visibility supports evidence-based policy and business strategy, ensuring that decisions are based on accurate, standardized metrics rather than aggregated nominal figures that obscure true market dynamics.
