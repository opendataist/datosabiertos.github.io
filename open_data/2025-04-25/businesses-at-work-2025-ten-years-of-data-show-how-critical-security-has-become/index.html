Businesses at Work 2025: Ten years of data show how critical security has become

Businesses at Work 2025: Ten years of data show how critical security has become

The primary conclusion of Okta’s 2025 report is that modern enterprises are defined by a dual imperative: aggressively adopting security measures while simultaneously expanding collaboration infrastructure. This shift reflects a decade-long adaptation to sophisticated cyber threats and distributed work models. Organizations now prioritize high-assurance authentication and identity management solutions, signaling a decisive move away from legacy, low-security practices toward robust digital protection frameworks. Cybersecurity has become a critical business driver, with attack rates escalating significantly across key industries and regions. The data reveals that nearly half of the fastest-growing applications are security-focused, demonstrating that risk mitigation is no longer an IT afterthought but a central strategic priority. This trend underscores the growing urgency for organizations to implement advanced threat detection and identity verification mechanisms to safeguard their digital assets against increasingly complex attacks. This article is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the necessity of anonymized, aggregated telemetry to uncover macro-level technology trends. By analyzing millions of interactions across diverse sectors, such reports generate open insights that help the broader community understand emerging patterns in digital adoption. These findings illustrate how shared data practices can inform policy, drive innovation, and guide organizations in making evidence-based decisions regarding security and operational efficiency.
