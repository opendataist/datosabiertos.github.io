Encabezó diputada Alma Portillo reunión por temas de transparencia

Encabezó diputada Alma Portillo reunión por temas de transparencia

Lawmakers in Chihuahua and civil society organizations stress the importance of maintaining governmental transparency amid the national dissolution of the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI). They are calling for urgent local legislative action to ensure the continuity of information rights and data protection, thereby preventing any regression in public access standards. This collaboration is essential for establishing a robust normative framework for the new state institution that will replace the INAI. By forging alliances with the private sector and citizen committees, they aim to ensure that the entity effectively responds to citizens’ demands for accountability and open data access. The article highlights the critical need for legal certainty in open data governance during institutional transitions. It underscores that sustained political will and multi-stakeholder cooperation are vital to preserving transparency mechanisms, ensuring they adapt to structural changes without compromising public trust or the fundamental right to information.
