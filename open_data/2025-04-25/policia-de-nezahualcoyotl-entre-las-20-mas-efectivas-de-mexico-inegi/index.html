Policía de Nezahualcóyotl entre las 20 más efectivas de México: INEGI

The municipal police of Nezahualcóyotl has earned national recognition for its effectiveness and trustworthiness, ranking among the top performers in Mexico. This success stems from a strategic shift toward community-oriented policing and professionalization, which has significantly improved public perceptions of safety. The results highlight how community-focused policing models can transform local security dynamics by fostering direct engagement between officers and residents. This improvement is largely attributed to the integration of neighborhood safety networks that encourage active citizen participation across vast areas of the municipality. This collaborative approach not only addresses immediate security concerns but also strengthens social cohesion. By prioritizing closeness to the community, the local government has demonstrated that trust-based strategies are more sustainable than purely enforcement-oriented approaches, leading to a measurable decrease in perceived insecurity. This case is highly relevant to open data because the INEGI survey provides transparent, standardized metrics that allow for comparative analysis of police performance across different regions. Such data transparency empowers citizens to evaluate governmental effectiveness objectively. It illustrates how open, empirical data can validate policy successes, encouraging other municipalities to adopt similar community-centric models based on proven outcomes rather than subjective impressions.
