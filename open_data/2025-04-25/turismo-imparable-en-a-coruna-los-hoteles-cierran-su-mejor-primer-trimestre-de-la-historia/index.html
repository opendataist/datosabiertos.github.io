Turismo imparable en A Coruña: los hoteles cierran su mejor primer trimestre de la historia

A Coruña has achieved unprecedented tourism growth, recording its best first quarter in history with record numbers of international and domestic visitors. This surge reflects the city’s successful strategy to desseasonalize tourism, driven by a rich cultural heritage and strategic promotions that have positioned it as a hidden gem in the international market. The city’s appeal extends beyond traditional peak seasons, demonstrating a resilient and expanding tourist profile that attracts travelers year-round. The accessibility of this destination is further reinforced by significant increases in air traffic at the local airport, which has surpassed previous records. The consolidation of new flight routes and the shift of passengers from neighboring airports highlight the region’s growing connectivity. These logistical improvements support the broader tourism ecosystem, ensuring that the city remains an attractive and reachable option for both leisure and business travelers, thereby sustaining the momentum generated by local promotional efforts. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public statistical sources, such as those from the INE and AENA, provide transparent, verifiable metrics for economic performance. By making tourism data publicly available, citizens, researchers, and policymakers can objectively assess the impact of government strategies and market trends. This transparency fosters accountability and allows for evidence-based decision-making, illustrating the practical value of open data in monitoring and validating regional development initiatives.
