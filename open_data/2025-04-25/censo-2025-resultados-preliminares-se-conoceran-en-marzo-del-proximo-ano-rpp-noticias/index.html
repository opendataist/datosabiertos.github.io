Censo 2025: resultados preliminares se conocerán en marzo del próximo año | RPP Noticias

Censo 2025: resultados preliminares se conocerán en marzo del próximo año | RPP Noticias

The National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI) has announced that the 2025 census will employ advanced digital technologies, including hybrid data collection methods, to significantly accelerate the release of statistical results. This technological shift represents a major improvement in the efficiency of open data, enabling preliminary findings to be available just months after the operational period ends, rather than taking years as in previous cycles. The census questionnaire, designed in accordance with international standards, covers essential demographic and housing variables, thereby empowering local and national governments to inform strategic development planning. By ensuring rapid turnaround times and comprehensive data coverage, the initiative enhances the transparency and utility of public statistics, facilitating better-informed policy decisions based on timely and accurate information. This progress is significant for open data, as it demonstrates how modernizing statistical infrastructure improves both the accessibility and speed of public sector information. By reducing the lag between data collection and publication, the INEI sets a precedent for greater accountability and enables researchers and citizens to use current data more effectively for social and economic analysis.
