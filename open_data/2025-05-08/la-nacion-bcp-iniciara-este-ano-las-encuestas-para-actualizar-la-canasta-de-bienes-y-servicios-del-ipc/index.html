La Nación / BCP iniciará este año las encuestas para actualizar la canasta de bienes y servicios del IPC

La Nación / BCP iniciará este año las encuestas para actualizar la canasta de bienes y servicios del IPC

The Central Bank of Paraguay is launching a comprehensive survey to modernize the consumer price index, ensuring it accurately reflects current household consumption patterns. This update is crucial for removing outdated items and incorporating new digital and service-based expenses that have emerged recently. By aligning the basket of goods with real-world spending patterns, the institution aims to enhance the precision of its economic indicators. Accurate measurement of average price variations is essential for guiding monetary policy and national accounting decisions. The revised methodology incorporates data from thousands of households to determine the relative weight of various products and services. Including modern conveniences such as streaming platforms and delivery services ensures that inflation metrics capture the true cost of living, providing a more realistic picture of economic changes for both policymakers and the public. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical methodologies underpin reliable governance. When institutions like the central bank collaborate with national statistics offices to publish granular, up-to-date survey results, they empower researchers and citizens to access trustworthy information. Such transparency fosters accountability and allows society to better understand the economic forces shaping their daily lives through accessible, high-quality public data.
