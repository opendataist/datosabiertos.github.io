La resiliencia económica de China según tres conjuntos de datos de la Feria de Cantón

La resiliencia económica de China según tres conjuntos de datos de la Feria de Cantón

The 137th Canton Fair demonstrates the notable resilience of China’s foreign trade, solidifying its role as a key barometer amid global uncertainties. Far from retreating, the event has expanded its network of contacts with international buyers, reflecting a firm commitment to sustaining the multilateral trading system. This expansion of China’s circle of trading partners underscores the country’s capacity to withstand external shocks and maintain its central position in global supply chains. Analysis of the results reveals a strategic transformation in the structure of exports, characterized by greater market diversification. Companies are reducing their traditional dependence on the United States and strengthening ties with Europe, South America, and countries participating in the Belt and Road Initiative. This shift indicates that China’s foreign trade has evolved from a phase of simple scale expansion to one focused on value creation, acting as a global industrial coordination hub driven by innovation and institutional openness. The relevance to open data lies in how this fair generates and democratizes valuable information on industrial trends, consumer preferences, and emerging technological capabilities. The large-scale exhibition of new products, particularly those that are eco-friendly, intelligent, and low-carbon, provides a rich and transparent dataset that enables global stakeholders to understand the evolution of Chinese manufacturing. By making these innovations visible, the fair facilitates evidence-based decision-making, fostering more informed and efficient commercial collaboration among nations.
