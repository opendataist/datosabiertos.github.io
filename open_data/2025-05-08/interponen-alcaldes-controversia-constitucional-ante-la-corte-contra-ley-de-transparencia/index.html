Interponen alcaldes controversia constitucional ante la Corte contra Ley de Transparencia

Interponen alcaldes controversia constitucional ante la Corte contra Ley de Transparencia

The National Association of Mayors challenges Mexico’s new transparency law, arguing that it centralizes power and violates citizens’ rights by granting the federal government discretionary control over the release of information. This shift threatens open data principles by replacing objective access with arbitrary decisions, thereby undermining the fundamental right to obtain information. Such legislation is critical to the open data discourse, highlighting how legal frameworks can inadvertently reinforce state secrecy and hinder public accountability.
