¿En qué ciudades españolas creció más el consumo en 2024?

¿En qué ciudades españolas creció más el consumo en 2024?

CaixaBank has revolutionized local economic monitoring by launching a real-time consumption tracker across 207 Spanish municipalities. This tool goes beyond national aggregates, offering granular insights into regional economic performance using anonymized internal data. The analysis reveals significant territorial disparities, with southern regions and tourist hubs showing robust growth, while some northern and inland cities experience declines. These variations highlight how local economic dynamics differ substantially even within the same province, providing a nuanced view of Spain’s economic landscape that was previously unavailable at such a detailed level. This initiative is crucial for open data as it demonstrates how private sector aggregated data can fill critical gaps in public statistical infrastructure. By making high-frequency, localized indicators accessible, it empowers researchers and policymakers to analyze hyper-local trends, fostering greater transparency and enabling more responsive, data-driven decision-making for urban planning and economic policy.
