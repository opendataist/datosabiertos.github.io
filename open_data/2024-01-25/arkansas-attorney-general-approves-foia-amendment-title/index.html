Arkansas attorney general approves FOIA amendment title

Arkansas Citizens for Transparency successfully secured approval to place a constitutional amendment protecting the Freedom of Information Act on the November ballot. This initiative follows recent efforts to weaken transparency laws, aiming to enshrine open access as a fundamental right against executive overreach. The approval allows the group to collect required signatures for the Arkansas Government Transparency Act. This marks a significant victory for civil liberties advocates seeking to reinforce public accountability and prevent the government from operating in secrecy. The article highlights the ongoing tension between legislative actions and citizen-led transparency movements. It underscores the critical role of constitutional safeguards in maintaining open data and government accountability within state jurisdictions.
