Estas son las principales causas de muerte en México según Inegi

Estas son las principales causas de muerte en México según Inegi

This report highlights Mexico’s leading causes of death, emphasizing heart disease, diabetes, and malignant tumors. Although the total number of fatalities decreased compared to the previous year, these chronic conditions remain critical public health challenges requiring sustained attention and resource allocation. The data reveals a decline in diabetes-related deaths but a rise in cancer fatalities, underscoring the evolving nature of the country’s health landscape. These trends indicate a need for targeted interventions, particularly as non-communicable diseases continue to drive mortality rates among the population. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, accessible insights into national health trends. By making such statistics publicly available, policymakers and researchers can better analyze public health outcomes, advocate for improved healthcare policies, and ensure accountability in managing population health risks.
