California’s amended pay data law mandates comprehensive reporting of wage gaps by race, ethnicity, and sex for large employers. This expansion ensures greater transparency in labor practices, including roles filled through labor contractors, directly enhancing workforce equity. For open data advocates, this initiative is vital as it creates a structured, standardized dataset on wage disparities. By centralizing this information, the state facilitates public scrutiny and research into systemic inequalities, promoting accountability and informed policy development regarding pay equity.
