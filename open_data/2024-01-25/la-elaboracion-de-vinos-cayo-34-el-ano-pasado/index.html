La elaboración de vinos cayó 34% el año pasado

The Uruguayan wine industry saw a marked decline in production and sales last year, with rosé wines being the hardest hit. This downturn underscores shifting consumer preferences and market dynamics within the sector, signaling a contraction in both domestic supply and demand. At the national level, red wines remain the preferred choice among consumers, while exports also declined, particularly to key markets such as Brazil and Russia. These trends reflect broader economic pressures affecting the vitivinicultural value chain, impacting production volumes across all grape varieties and regions. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives, as it demonstrates the value of publicly available statistical records from institutions like INAVI. Transparent access to such detailed industry metrics enables researchers and policymakers to monitor economic health, track trade flows, and identify emerging patterns in agricultural production, thereby supporting more informed decision-making.
