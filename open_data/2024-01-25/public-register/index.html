Public register

The EIB emphasizes that openness and transparency are vital for its credibility, accountability, and operational efficiency as a public institution. This commitment drives the creation of its Public Register, ensuring environmental information is accessible in line with EU frameworks. Such proactive disclosure strengthens stakeholder relations and promotes sustainable governance. This approach is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how institutional transparency fosters public trust and facilitates broader data accessibility.
