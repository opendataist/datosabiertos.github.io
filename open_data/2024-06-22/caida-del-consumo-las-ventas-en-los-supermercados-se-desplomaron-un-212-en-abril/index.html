Caída del consumo: las ventas en los supermercados se desplomaron un 21,2% en abril

Caída del consumo: las ventas en los supermercados se desplomaron un 21,2% en abril

Supermarket and retail sales have plummeted to their lowest levels since the pandemic, signaling a severe contraction in consumer spending. This sharp decline reflects significant economic pressure on households, indicating that, despite inflationary pressures on essential goods, overall demand has collapsed across retail channels. The relevance to open data lies in the critical need for transparent, accessible, and timely statistical information. As official institutions such as INDEC release these indicators, researchers and civil society must analyze the underlying trends to assess economic health, advocate for policy changes, and hold governments accountable for citizens’ well-being. Understanding these macroeconomic shifts through open data empowers stakeholders to identify vulnerable sectors and track the real impact of inflation on daily life. Consequently, making such detailed economic indicators freely available fosters informed public debate and supports evidence-based decision-making during periods of financial instability.
