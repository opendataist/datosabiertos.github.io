Panamá registra moderada actividad económica - Noticias Prensa Latina

Panamá registra moderada actividad económica - Noticias Prensa Latina

The article highlights a moderate economic recovery in Panama, contrasting with previous declines. Key sectors such as commerce and banking show dynamism, driven by trade and increased credit activity. Conversely, extractive industries continue to shrink, while transport faces mixed results due to canal restrictions and port adaptations. This nuance is vital for open_data, as it emphasizes the need for granular, sector-specific indicators to accurately track economic health beyond aggregate metrics.
