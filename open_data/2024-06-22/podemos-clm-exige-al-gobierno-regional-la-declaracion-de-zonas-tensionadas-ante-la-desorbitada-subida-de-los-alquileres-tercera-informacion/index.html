Podemos CLM exige al gobierno regional la declaración de zonas tensionadas ante la desorbitada subida de los alquileres - Tercera Información

Podemos Castilla-La Mancha has criticized the regional government for its inaction in the face of soaring housing rents, calling for the declaration of areas under housing pressure and the implementation of stricter rent controls. This highlights the urgent need for transparent policymaking to protect citizens from exploitative market practices—a core concern in open data governance. The party alleges that current public procurement procedures result in anti-social rental contracts priced above market rates, casting doubt on the integrity of the data underlying these decisions. They urge authorities to regulate short-term rentals and address the shortage of housing supply, emphasizing that public data should reflect social welfare rather than the interests of private developers. Ultimately, the critique underscores the necessity of accessible and accurate housing statistics to drive effective social policies. The relevance to open data lies in the demand for transparency in government spending and contract awards, ensuring that information is available to hold officials accountable and facilitate equitable solutions for vulnerable populations.
