Coastal Resilience Powered by Open Data and Modern Tools

By modernizing its digital presence, BOEM transformed decades of fragmented marine data into an accessible, intuitive platform. This shift removes technical barriers, allowing diverse users to quickly interpret complex geospatial information for balanced coastal resource management. This initiative highlights open data’s critical role in democratizing access to vital environmental resources. Clear, user-centric design ensures transparency and supports informed decision-making, proving that accessible data infrastructure is essential for effective stewardship of shared natural assets.
