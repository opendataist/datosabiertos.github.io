‘Transparency and open data key to business resilience’

Open data has evolved from an abstract ideal into a strategic necessity for navigating today’s volatile economic landscape. By providing credible, timely information, transparency strengthens governance and builds trust across both public and private sectors. When businesses have access to reliable data, they can operate with greater confidence, reducing uncertainty and fostering an environment where informed decision-making drives resilience. The Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative (TTEITI) exemplifies the practical value of this approach in Trinidad and Tobago. By independently verifying billions of dollars in sector revenues, TTEITI ensures data integrity, which is crucial for macroeconomic stability and investor confidence. This rigorous verification process moves beyond simple disclosure, offering evidence-based insights that help policymakers improve revenue systems and enable companies to assess policy risks and regulatory trends accurately. For business leaders, this transparency is a source of competitive advantage rather than mere compliance. Open data allows firms to anticipate fiscal changes, strengthen ESG strategies, and ground investment decisions in evidence rather than speculation. Ultimately, leveraging open data empowers businesses to manage risk effectively and pursue sustainable growth, turning transparency into a tangible tool for long-term economic success and private sector resilience.
