La Junta Electoral bonaerense fijó pautas del escrutinio provisorio: los resultados se conocerán desde las 21 - Zona Norte Visión

La Junta Electoral bonaerense fijó pautas del escrutinio provisorio: los resultados se conocerán desde las 21 - Zona Norte Visión

The Buenos Aires Electoral Board establishes the framework for publishing the provisional vote count, centralizing dissemination through an official website created by the Executive Branch. The first results will be made available once more than 30 percent of polling stations have reported, ensuring that information is accessible via official channels designed to guarantee transparency in the immediate electoral process. Access to this dissemination hub is restricted to accredited actors, including media outlets, party representatives, and civil society organizations. This limitation aims to focus data distribution among entities with verified capacity, enabling political parties and organized citizens to monitor the traceability of submissions and the software used during the initial upload of results. This initiative is significant for open data because it institutionalizes the early availability of electoral data on official digital platforms, promoting accountability through technological transparency. By integrating the digitization of documentation and allowing parties access to vote-counting tools, it fosters an ecosystem where public information is audited in real time, strengthening democratic integrity through open and verifiable data.
