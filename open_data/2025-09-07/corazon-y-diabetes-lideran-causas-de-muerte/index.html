Corazón y diabetes lideran causas de muerte

Corazón y diabetes lideran causas de muerte

The INEGI report reveals that non-communicable chronic diseases, such as heart disease and diabetes, dominated mortality in Mexico and Chiapas during the first quarter of 2025. This underscores the critical need for accurate and up-to-date health data to guide effective public policies. Disaggregation by gender exposes significant differences in causes of death, such as the greater impact of liver diseases among men. These distinctions demonstrate why open data must enable granular analysis, which is essential for designing targeted health strategies that address the specific vulnerabilities of each population group. The relevance to open data lies in the transparency it enables for auditing and improving public health. When statistical information is accessible and understandable, civil society can demand evidence-based government responses, fostering genuine accountability in addressing the nation’s main health threats.
