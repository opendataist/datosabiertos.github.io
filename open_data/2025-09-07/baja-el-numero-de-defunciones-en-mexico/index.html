Baja el número de defunciones en México

Baja el número de defunciones en México

This article highlights the public release of vital statistics by INEGI, demonstrating how government agencies serve as critical sources for demographic and health data. By publishing detailed mortality records, including causes of death and demographic breakdowns, it enables researchers and citizens to access high-quality, standardized information. The data reveals recent trends in Mexico’s mortality rates, showing a slight decrease compared to the previous year after a period of increase. Identifying leading causes such as heart disease and diabetes provides essential insights for public health analysis and policy-making. The relevance to open data lies in the transparency and accessibility of these official records. When statistical institutes publish comprehensive, machine-readable datasets, it fosters innovation, allows for independent verification of public health trends, and empowers communities to better understand societal challenges.
