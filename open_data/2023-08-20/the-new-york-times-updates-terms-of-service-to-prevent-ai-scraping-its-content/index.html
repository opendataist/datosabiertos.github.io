The New York Times Updates Terms of Service to Prevent AI Scraping Its Content

The New York Times now bans using its content to train AI, directly challenging the common open data practice of public web scraping. This move highlights the growing tension between proprietary content protection and the open exchange of information required for transparent, reproducible AI research.
