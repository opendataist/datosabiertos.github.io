Saudi Arabia mandates private sector training data disclosure to boost workforce capabilities and productivity. This initiative supports national goals for job market readiness through improved training programs. Relevant to open data, this policy creates a structured dataset for analyzing labor market trends. It encourages transparency and accountability in corporate training investments across the private sector. The requirement fosters collaboration between government and industry by making performance metrics visible. Such data availability enables evidence-based policy adjustments and better resource allocation for employee development.
