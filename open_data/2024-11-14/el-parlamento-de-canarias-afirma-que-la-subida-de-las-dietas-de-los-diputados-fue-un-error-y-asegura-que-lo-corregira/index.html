El Parlamento de Canarias afirma que la subida de las dietas de los diputados fue un "error" y asegura que lo corregirá

The Canary Islands Parliament is rectifying its transparency portal after public scrutiny uncovered significant errors in the published data on deputies’ salaries and allowances. This update is crucial because the previous information contained outdated concepts and miscalculated amounts, misleading the public about actual expenditures. Correcting these discrepancies underscores the importance of data accuracy in government transparency initiatives. When official datasets contain inconsistencies, they erode public trust and obscure the true cost of political operations, highlighting the need for rigorous verification processes. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates that merely publishing data is insufficient if the underlying information is flawed. It emphasizes the need for continuous maintenance and validation of open datasets to ensure they remain reliable tools for accountability and civic engagement.
