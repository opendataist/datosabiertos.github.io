Election 2024: We'll carefully scrutinise final voters' register - Ghanamma.com

Election 2024: We'll carefully scrutinise final voters' register - Ghanamma.com

The NDC is critically analyzing the Electoral Commission’s final certified voter register summary, identifying unexplained removals of polling stations and missing regional data. This scrutiny highlights significant data inconsistencies that threaten the register’s reliability for upcoming elections. By demanding comprehensive segregation of special voters and detailed regional breakdowns, the party seeks transparency in how the electronic management of the register altered historical station configurations. These discrepancies underscore the fragility of digital election data when administrative changes occur without clear public justification. This situation is vital to open_data advocacy, as it demonstrates how opaque electoral data handling can undermine democratic integrity. It emphasizes the urgent need for standardized, accessible, and verifiable public datasets to ensure election results are trustworthy and resistant to manipulation.
