Gobierno del Estado reconoce iniciativas de Gobierno Abierto | Revista Punto de Vista

The San Luis Potosí Open Government Award promotes transparency and civic engagement by recognizing innovative public service projects. This initiative aligns with state directives to enhance governance quality through active cooperation between government bodies and civil society, ensuring objective and transparent project selection processes. Recognizing achievements in both governmental and civil society categories demonstrates the value of diverse, inclusive initiatives. By rewarding projects that address specific community needs, such as scientific dissemination and anti-discrimination efforts, the program encourages broader societal participation in public affairs. This award is highly relevant to open data as it highlights how accessible information and transparent processes empower citizens. It showcases a successful model for integrating public data into actionable solutions, fostering a culture where openness directly improves service delivery and democratic involvement.
