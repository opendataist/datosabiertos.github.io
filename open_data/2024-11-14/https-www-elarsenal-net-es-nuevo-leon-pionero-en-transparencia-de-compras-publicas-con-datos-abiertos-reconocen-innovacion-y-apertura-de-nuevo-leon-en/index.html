Open Data
ES EN

Nuevo León has led a paradigm shift by aligning itself with the Open Contracting Data Standard. This initiative positions the state as a national benchmark, prioritizing radical transparency and accountability in a sector traditionally characterized by opacity. By adopting these international standards, the government seeks to modernize its processes, ensuring efficiency, inclusivity for small businesses, and environmental sustainability, thereby laying the foundation for a more modern and trustworthy public administration. The publication of detailed data on contracting methods, categories, and awarded amounts enables citizens and civil society organizations to access critical information. This access democratizes knowledge, facilitating public scrutiny and fostering fairer competition. By making public spending traceable, the fight against corruption is strengthened and the value of public funds is optimized, demonstrating that open data is an essential tool for identifying risks and ensuring that resources are used responsibly. This advancement is significant for the open data community because it exemplifies how technological interoperability and political commitment can generate tangible impacts. Nuevo León’s experience suggests that adopting open standards not only improves the quality of public management but also stimulates regional economic development by creating accessible opportunities for suppliers of all sizes, validating the transformative potential of open public information.
