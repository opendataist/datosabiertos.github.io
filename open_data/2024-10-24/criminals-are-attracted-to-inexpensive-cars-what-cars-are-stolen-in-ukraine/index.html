Criminals are attracted to inexpensive cars: what cars are stolen in Ukraine

Vehicle thefts in Ukraine have significantly declined since 2022, with cars remaining the primary target. Open data reveals regional and brand-specific trends, highlighting inexpensive models like Nissan and VAZ as frequent choices for thieves. This transparency empowers public scrutiny and informs security strategies. By making crime statistics accessible, open_data initiatives foster community awareness and support evidence-based policy decisions for national safety improvements.
