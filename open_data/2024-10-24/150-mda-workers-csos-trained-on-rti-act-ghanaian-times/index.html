150 MDA workers, CSOs trained on RTI Act - Ghanaian Times

Training on the Right to Information Act equips public officials and civil society actors with essential knowledge to handle citizen requests effectively. By clarifying legal responsibilities, including procedures for accessing information and establishing dedicated units, this initiative aims to resolve previous ambiguities that hindered responsive governance. The emphasis on prompt responses helps institutions avoid penalties while fostering a culture of accountability and transparency in public service delivery. The empowerment of local governance structures through targeted capacity building demonstrates a strategic approach to enhancing civic engagement. When staff members understand their roles under the law, they can better serve their communities by ensuring equitable access to public data. This aligns with broader goals of improving resilience and inclusiveness, as informed citizens are better positioned to hold authorities accountable for their actions and decisions. This effort is highly relevant to open_data because it establishes the institutional groundwork for transparent information flows. By training Information Officers and civil society representatives, the program ensures that data is not only accessible but also managed correctly by designated authorities. Strengthening these legal and procedural frameworks encourages the proactive release of public records, thereby supporting a more open and participatory democratic environment where information serves as a tool for social empowerment rather than a restricted resource.
