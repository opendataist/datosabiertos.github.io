“Claridad” ofrece Pepe Chedraui en su administración, ante cualquier solicitud de información

“Claridad” ofrece Pepe Chedraui en su administración, ante cualquier solicitud de información

The municipality of Puebla launched a virtual transparency course to ensure that its administration operates with integrity and clarity. This initiative aims to empower citizens by enabling them to actively participate in the creation of public policies and to verify the proper use of public resources through effective accountability. Proper handling of information requests is central to this effort, requiring city officials to have the necessary tools for clear communication. By coordinating responses accurately, the government seeks to build trust and demonstrate its commitment to an open and responsible governance model. This development is relevant to open data because it highlights the human and institutional infrastructure required for data accessibility. Transparency initiatives are not just about publishing datasets but ensuring that public institutions are capable and accountable in responding to citizen inquiries, which is a fundamental pillar of a functioning open government ecosystem.
