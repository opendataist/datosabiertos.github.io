Las pernoctaciones hoteleras caen un 1,2% en septiembre en C-LM, mientras los precios se disparan un 4,45%

The hospitality sector demonstrates resilience through rising prices and employment, despite a slight decline in tourist arrivals. This dynamic underscores a shift toward higher revenue per guest rather than volume expansion, challenging business models that rely solely on growth in footfall. Occupancy rates remain significantly lower than those in leading coastal regions, suggesting untapped potential for market expansion. The data reveals disparities in regional competitiveness, prompting local authorities to investigate barriers to attracting international visitors and to enhance strategic positioning. These insights are crucial for open data initiatives, as transparent statistics enable analysts to benchmark performance and identify gaps in tourism distribution. Such accessible metrics foster informed policy-making and encourage competitive innovation across the hospitality industry.
