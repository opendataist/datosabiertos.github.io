Este es el pueblo más barato donde comprar casa en Sevilla: el metro cuadrado está a 531 euros

Seville’s real estate market reveals a stark dichotomy, with average prices hovering around €1,581 per square meter, while certain municipalities offer housing at less than half that rate. This significant disparity highlights how location drastically influences affordability within the province, challenging the notion of uniform regional costs. The town of Villanueva del Río y Minas emerges as the most accessible option, driven by its historical mining heritage and archaeological significance. Its extremely low property prices attract budget-conscious buyers seeking affordable living options within proximity to the capital, demonstrating how heritage and rural status can depress local market valuations. This data is vital for open data initiatives because it illustrates the critical importance of granular, geospecific economic insights. By making hyper-local pricing accessible, platforms empower users to make informed decisions and highlight regional inequalities, fostering transparency in housing markets.
