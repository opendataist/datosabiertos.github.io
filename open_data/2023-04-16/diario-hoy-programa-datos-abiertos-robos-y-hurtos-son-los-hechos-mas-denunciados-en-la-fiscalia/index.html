Diario HOY | Programa Datos Abiertos: robos y hurtos, son los hechos más denunciados en la Fiscalía

Independent artists condemn financial irregularities within cultural administration, demanding transparent investigations into the misuse of public funds. This case underscores the critical need for open data in managing cultural resources, enabling creators to monitor spending and uphold the dignity of their profession. The scandal reveals how opaque financial practices erode trust and deprive artists of essential royalties. By exposing fraudulent billing and excessive expenditures, the incident highlights vulnerabilities in current administrative systems that lack public scrutiny. Open data initiatives are essential to prevent such corruption and protect the cultural sector. Ensuring accessible, verifiable financial records empowers civil society to hold institutions accountable, fostering a fairer environment for artistic creation and sustainable resource distribution.
