Retrocede empleo manufacturero

The contraction in employment within the manufacturing sector in February signals a halt to its recent recovery, suggesting potential instability in industrial labor markets. This downturn affects most sub-sectors, indicating broad challenges rather than isolated issues within the industry. For open data, this highlights the critical need for timely, granular statistics to monitor economic health and identify emerging trends before they escalate.
