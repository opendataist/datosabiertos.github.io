Nigeria’s Inflation Rate Hits 22.04% For March 2023 | African Examiner

Nigeria’s inflation rate climbed slightly, driven by rising costs in food, housing, and transport. This surge highlights significant economic pressure on households, reflecting broader structural challenges within the nation’s market. For open data, this report underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible, and timely economic indicators.
