Elon Musk funda X.AI, una start-up de inteligencia artificial | DW | 15.04.2023

Elon Musk has founded X.AI, a new artificial intelligence company based in Nevada, to compete directly with OpenAI and Alphabet’s ecosystem. This initiative underscores the current intense technological rivalry, marked by the recruitment of key experts from DeepMind to advance the development of sophisticated conversational models. The creation of this company presents a notable contradiction with Musk’s recent signing of a call to pause AI development due to its existential risks. This shift has sparked an open debate about ethics and safety in the industry, questioning whether the pursuit of superintelligent non-human minds is socially desirable or poses a threat to human control. This case is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates the tension between corporate acceleration in high-impact technologies and transparency. By observing how major private players accumulate AI capabilities through talent acquisitions and hidden corporate records, the critical need to demand access to data and code becomes evident, ensuring public accountability and collective security.
