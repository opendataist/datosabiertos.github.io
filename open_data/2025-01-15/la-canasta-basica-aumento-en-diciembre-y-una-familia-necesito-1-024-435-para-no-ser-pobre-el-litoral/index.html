La canasta básica aumentó en diciembre y una familia necesitó $1.024.435 para no ser pobre | El Litoral

La canasta básica aumentó en diciembre y una familia necesitó $1.024.435 para no ser pobre | El Litoral

The article highlights that the cost of living in Argentina rose significantly in late 2024, with basic needs outpacing food price inflation. This disparity reveals a widening gap between general inflation and the cost of essential sustenance, suggesting that while food became relatively cheaper compared to other goods, the overall burden to escape poverty intensified. This dynamic underscores the erosion of purchasing power for vulnerable households, as the income required to meet basic standards increased sharply. The data illustrates how economic pressures disproportionately affect families, forcing them to navigate stricter financial thresholds just to maintain a subsistence level of existence amidst rising general costs. For open data advocates, this report exemplifies the critical role of transparent, accessible socioeconomic indicators in monitoring public welfare. Reliable metrics on poverty and indigence lines allow citizens and researchers to verify government claims, hold institutions accountable, and understand the real-time impact of economic policies on everyday life, ensuring that data serves as a tool for social transparency rather than just administrative record-keeping.
