La inflación en Argentina fue de 2,7%: el primer año de Javier Milei cerró con 117,8% acumulado

INDEC published the December consumer price indices, revealing a monthly increase of 2.7% and a cumulative annual rise of 117.8%. The housing and communication sectors experienced the sharpest spikes in both the specific month and the full year, reflecting broader economic shifts under the new administration. Conversely, clothing and household maintenance showed comparatively lower inflation rates. The government attributes the modest monthly rise to positive seasonality linked to holidays and summer vacations, emphasizing a concurrent recovery in economic activity and real incomes. This figure marks a significant deceleration compared to the previous year’s accumulated inflation, signaling a potential stabilization trend in the national economy. This data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the critical role of official statistical transparency in monitoring macroeconomic health. Access to granular, disaggregated consumer price data allows researchers and citizens to validate government claims regarding inflation control and economic recovery, ensuring accountability in public policy implementation.
