NOAA Expands Water Level and Wave Dataset

NOAA has launched the Coastal Ocean Reanalysis, providing decades of high-resolution historical water level data for the Atlantic, Caribbean, and Gulf coasts. This comprehensive dataset fills critical spatial gaps left by sparse tide gauges, offering granular insights into coastal hydrodynamics. By revealing detailed historical flood risks, the resource empowers communities and researchers to better understand past events like Hurricane Hugo. This enhanced visibility supports improved preparation for future storms and aids in developing robust policies for coastal resource management and energy planning. This release is vital for open_data initiatives as it democratizes access to essential environmental intelligence. Making such vast, geospatially dense datasets publicly available enables broader scientific collaboration and drives innovation in machine learning applications for climate resilience.
