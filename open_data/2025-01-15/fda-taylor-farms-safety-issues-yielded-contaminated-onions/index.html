FDA: Taylor Farms Safety Issues Yielded Contaminated Onions

FDA: Taylor Farms Safety Issues Yielded Contaminated Onions

Taylor Farms recalled RTE onions after inspections revealed critical hygiene failures, including improper glove use and incorrect sanitizing practices. These lapses allowed pathogens to contaminate food contact surfaces, directly compromising product safety and consumer health. This case highlights the vital role of open data in food safety, as transparent reporting of inspection findings and outbreak sources enables better tracking and prevention of future contamination events.
