Numerosos contribuyentes tienen el RUC suspendido o bloqueado - Economía - ABC Color

Numerosos contribuyentes tienen el RUC suspendido o bloqueado - Economía - ABC Color

The article reveals that Paraguay’s tax authority has over a million registered taxpayers, but nearly a third are inactive due to temporary suspension or permanent blocking. This highlights a significant gap between potential and actual tax compliance, as many entities have ceased operations or failed to settle debts with the state. Revenues are tracking above last year’s levels, suggesting that while the tax base is broad, the administration is effectively capturing funds from those who remain compliant and active. This fiscal performance indicates a strengthening of state resources, though it relies heavily on the portion of the economy that remains fully operational and debt-free. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the value of transparent, real-time public statistics. By making such detailed breakdowns of taxpayer status and revenue available, governments enable civil society to monitor fiscal health and accountability. Open access to these metrics fosters trust and allows for deeper analysis of economic trends and compliance rates.
