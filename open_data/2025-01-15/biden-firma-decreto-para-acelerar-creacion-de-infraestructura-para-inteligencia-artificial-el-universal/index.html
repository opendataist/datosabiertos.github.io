Biden firma decreto para acelerar creación de infraestructura para inteligencia artificial | El Universal

Biden’s executive order prioritizes digital and energy infrastructure to accelerate the deployment of artificial intelligence, aiming to prevent the United States from falling behind in this global competition. This approach seeks to balance national security with environmental sustainability, allowing the private sector to use federal lands for new data centers. The relevance to open data lies in the fact that the massive expansion of computing generates growing volumes of structured data and public infrastructure. Understanding how the government facilitates access to these physical resources is key to analyzing future trends in the availability and management of open information, as well as its impact on technological transparency and sustainable development.
