Open Data: la información del Registro a un solo clic

Open Data: la información del Registro a un solo clic

The Spanish College of Registrars has launched Open Data, a public portal that radically transforms access to registry information by providing immediate and free visualization of commercial and real estate data. This initiative represents a significant advance in administrative transparency, enabling citizens and businesses to consult details about companies, such as administrators and tax information, with an agility and visual clarity that surpasses traditional consultation methods. The platform is structured around three fundamental pillars: a company directory, a catalog of open-format data for bulk downloads, and an interactive analysis module. The latter stands out for its ability to filter historical data spanning up to twenty years, facilitating the identification of market trends, sectoral comparisons, and demographic studies. This interactivity democratizes economic knowledge, empowering users to conduct complex analyses without relying on intermediaries or lengthy bureaucratic processes. This project is crucial for the open data movement, as it demonstrates how public entities can release complex and structured information to foster innovation and social oversight. By prioritizing accessibility and usability, the College sets a positive precedent for government data interoperability, ensuring that transparency is not merely a legal principle but a practical and dynamic tool in the service of society and the market.
