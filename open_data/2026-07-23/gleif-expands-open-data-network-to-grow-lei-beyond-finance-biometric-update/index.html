GLEIF expands open data network to grow LEI beyond finance | Biometric Update

The Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation expanded its Global Open Data Integration Network by integrating three new organizations focused on supply chains, open knowledge, and sustainability. This strategic growth underscores the critical role of standardized organizational identifiers in bridging diverse open data ecosystems. By embedding the Legal Entity Identifier across these varied platforms, the network promotes seamless connectivity and interoperability without requiring centralized dataset ownership, thereby enhancing global data usability. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how a unified identity standard can unlock the value of disparate information sources. The inclusion of major platforms illustrates that open data’s potential is maximized when entities can be reliably linked across different sectors. This approach supports broader goals of transparency and accountability, allowing users to trace connections between corporate actions, supply chain logistics, and environmental impacts more effectively. Ultimately, the network reinforces the concept that open data is most powerful when interconnected. By welcoming diverse stakeholders, the project highlights a shift toward a more transparent and interoperable global digital infrastructure. This expansion signals that standardizing identity management is essential for building trust and enabling meaningful collaboration across the evolving landscape of public and private data initiatives.
