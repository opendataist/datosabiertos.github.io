BMRCL refuses to disclose fare fixation committee report despite public, political pressure

BMRCL refuses to disclose fare fixation committee report despite public, political pressure

The core issue highlights a critical failure in open data practices by the Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Limited, which has withheld a statutory report justifying significant fare increases. By refusing to disclose the Fare Fixation Committee’s findings, BMRCL obstructs public access to the rationale behind pricing decisions, directly violating principles of transparency and accountability in public service governance. This lack of disclosure undermines the effectiveness of Right to Information mechanisms and democratic oversight. When key policy documents remain inaccessible, citizens and elected representatives cannot engage in informed discourse or hold authorities accountable. This secrecy contrasts sharply with expectations of openness, particularly when dealing with quasi-judicial bodies tasked with determining public utility fares. For the open data community, this case serves as a stark reminder of the tangible impact of non-disclosure. It demonstrates how withholding granular, decision-supporting data prevents citizens from understanding the logic behind administrative actions. Ensuring that such reports are readily available is essential for maintaining public trust and enabling meaningful civic engagement in public transport management.
