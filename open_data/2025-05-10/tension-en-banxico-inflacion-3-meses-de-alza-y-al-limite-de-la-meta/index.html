Tensión en Banxico: inflación, 3 meses de alza y al límite de la meta

The Bank of Mexico manages inflation to protect currency stability, aiming for a three percent target. Although prices remain within limits, upward trends threaten this balance, potentially forcing further interest rate adjustments. This scenario highlights the critical role of open data in monitoring macroeconomic indicators. Transparent, accessible price data allows citizens and experts to verify official statistics and understand the impact of policy decisions. Such transparency fosters trust and enables independent analysis of monetary decisions. Open data empowers society to objectively assess economic health, ensuring that central bank actions remain accountable and responsive to real-time market conditions.
