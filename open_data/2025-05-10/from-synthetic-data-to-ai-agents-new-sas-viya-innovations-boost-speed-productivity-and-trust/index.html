From synthetic data to AI agents, new SAS Viya innovations boost speed, productivity and trust

From synthetic data to AI agents, new SAS Viya innovations boost speed, productivity and trust

SAS has expanded its Viya platform with new tools designed to streamline the AI lifecycle, including synthetic data generation, intelligent decisioning agents, and AI-driven assistants. These updates address critical enterprise needs such as data privacy, accessibility for non-technical users, and accelerated model development. By lowering barriers to entry and integrating seamlessly into existing workflows, SAS aims to help organizations move beyond AI hype to implement practical, scalable solutions that enhance decision-making speed and operational productivity across diverse skill levels. The relevance to open_data lies in how these innovations democratize access to advanced analytics. Tools like synthetic data generators allow organizations to share valuable insights and train models without compromising sensitive information, fostering a more open and collaborative data ecosystem. Furthermore, the emphasis on user-friendly interfaces and cloud-based environments ensures that data science is no longer siloed within specialized IT departments. This shift enables a broader range of stakeholders to engage with data, promoting transparency and wider dissemination of analytical findings across industries. Ultimately, SAS’s strategy focuses on empowering a diverse ecosystem of users, from developers to business analysts, to collaborate effectively within the data and AI lifecycle. This approach not only accelerates innovation but also supports significant real-world applications, such as global conservation efforts, demonstrating the tangible impact of accessible data technologies. By combining robust infrastructure with intuitive tools, SAS facilitates a more inclusive environment where data-driven decisions can be made rapidly and responsibly, setting a precedent for how organizations can leverage data for both commercial and societal benefits.
