La resiliencia económica de China según tres conjuntos de datos de la Feria de Cantón

The 137th Canton Fair demonstrates the notable resilience of China’s foreign trade, consolidating itself as a key indicator of global stability. Far from being merely a commercial event, it has evolved into a hub for coordinating the global industrial supply chain, where technological innovation and institutional openness enable Chinese companies to respond effectively to geopolitical uncertainty and maintain their central position in global supply. The geographic diversification of markets highlights a crucial strategic shift. As dependence on the United States alone declines, sustained growth with developing countries and partners in the Belt and Road Initiative indicates a successful adaptation. This expansion not only mitigates economic risks but also reflects a transition from mere scale expansion to value creation, strengthening multilateral cooperation ties and economic integration with emerging partners. The relevance for open data lies in the democratization of information on industrial trends. The massive display of eco-friendly and smart products generates valuable data on global demand for sustainability and energy efficiency. By facilitating access to these innovations and enabling direct connections between international buyers and suppliers, the fair promotes transparency that allows market participants to base their decisions on real information about technical capabilities and consumer preferences, fostering a more open and competitive industry.
