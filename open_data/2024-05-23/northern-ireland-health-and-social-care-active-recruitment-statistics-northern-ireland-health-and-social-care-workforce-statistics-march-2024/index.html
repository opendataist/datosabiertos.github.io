Northern Ireland Health and Social Care Active Recruitment Statistics & Northern Ireland Health and Social Care Workforce Statistics March 2024

The Department of Health’s recent statistical release reveals a modest recovery in Northern Ireland’s health workforce, showing a gradual increase in staff numbers since 2019 alongside a declining vacancy rate. This downward trend in active recruitment positions suggests improving retention or hiring stability, marking a significant shift from the staffing shortages observed in previous years. The data highlights that while overall headcount has grown, specific sectors like social services and nursing still require substantial recruitment efforts. For the open data community, this publication exemplifies the critical importance of transparent, granular workforce statistics in public health management. By providing detailed breakdowns by organization and staff group, the data enables researchers and policymakers to identify systemic pressures and resource allocation gaps. However, the exclusion of certain staff categories, such as domiciliary care workers, underscores the challenge of creating comprehensive datasets that truly reflect the full scope of service delivery. Understanding these nuances is vital for ensuring that open data initiatives accurately represent the workforce landscape. When significant segments of the labor force are omitted from official statistics, it creates blind spots that can skew analysis and policy decisions. Therefore, while this release offers valuable insights into health sector trends, it also serves as a reminder to advocate for more inclusive data collection practices that capture the entirety of the care economy.
