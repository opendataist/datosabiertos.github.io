Open Data
ES EN

This privacy policy outlines how Hipertextual S.L. manages personal data, emphasizing that the level of user protection varies depending on geographic location and applicable regulations. The core conclusion is that while the company ensures data security and transparency, certain users benefit from enhanced safeguards, particularly those subject to stricter standards such as those of the European Union. This distinction is crucial because it directly impacts the scope of individual rights, such as the right to withdraw consent, the right to data rectification, and the right to be forgotten, which may not be uniformly available to all users globally. The relevance to open data lies in the tension between the utility of data for public interest services and the imperative of preserving privacy. By detailing specific processing purposes—such as advertising, statistics, and the visualization of external content—the document highlights how personal information is repurposed beyond simple service provision. Understanding these boundaries helps developers and data stewards navigate the ethical and legal frameworks when designing systems that might handle similar types of data, ensuring that open initiatives respect individual privacy rights rather than compromising them. Furthermore, the policy clarifies the legal bases for data processing, ranging from user consent to legitimate interests and contractual necessities. This transparency allows stakeholders to identify where data collection is mandatory versus optional, which is vital for building trust in open data ecosystems. By providing clear contact mechanisms and explaining data retention periods, the policy demonstrates a commitment to accountability, showing how organizations can balance operational needs with user autonomy in an increasingly digital landscape.
