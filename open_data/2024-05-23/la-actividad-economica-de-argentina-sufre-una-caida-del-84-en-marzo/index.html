La actividad económica de Argentina sufre una caída del 8,4 % en marzo

Argentina’s economy is experiencing a deep recession, characterized by significant declines in construction, manufacturing, and commerce. This broad-based contraction underscores severe structural weaknesses across most productive sectors, signaling a prolonged period of economic instability for the country. In contrast, agriculture and mining posted modest growth, providing a slight counterpoint to the wider downturn. However, these isolated gains are insufficient to offset the substantial losses in other key industries, highlighting an uneven and fragile economic recovery process. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates how official statistical indicators offer transparent, real-time evidence of economic health. Reliable, accessible data enables citizens and analysts to verify claims about recessions, fostering accountability and informed public debate on national policy and economic trends.
