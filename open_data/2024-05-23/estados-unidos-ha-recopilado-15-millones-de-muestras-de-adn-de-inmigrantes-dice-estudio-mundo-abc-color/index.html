Estados Unidos ha recopilado 1,5 millones de muestras de ADN de inmigrantes, dice estudio - Mundo - ABC Color

This report reveals that the US government is aggressively expanding DNA collection from immigrants through the immigration system, circumventing stricter police regulations. The data is permanently stored in criminal databases, raising significant privacy and ethical concerns about genetic surveillance. The relevance to open data lies in the urgent need for transparency regarding how biometric information is collected, stored, and shared across agencies. Without clear legal frameworks, citizens cannot understand or control their data, undermining trust in digital governance systems. The study calls for immediate policy changes to halt this practice. It highlights the risks of indefinite storage and evolving technology, urging robust oversight to protect individual rights and ensure ethical standards in data management.
