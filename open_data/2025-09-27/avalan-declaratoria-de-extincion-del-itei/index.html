Avalan declaratoria de extinción del ITEI

Avalan declaratoria de extinción del ITEI

Jalisco extinguishes its independent transparency institute, transferring oversight to the Comptroller’s office to centralize control. This structural shift raises concerns about the robustness of citizen rights, as oversight moves from an autonomous body to executive branches. The change highlights the fragility of institutional safeguards in open data ecosystems when oversight is integrated into administrative bodies rather than remaining independent. Such reforms threaten the separation of powers essential for true transparency, potentially weakening accountability and public trust in government information access mechanisms.
