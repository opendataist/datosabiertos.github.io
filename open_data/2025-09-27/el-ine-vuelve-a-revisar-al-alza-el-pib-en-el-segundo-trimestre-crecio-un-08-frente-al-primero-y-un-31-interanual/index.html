El INE vuelve a revisar al alza el PIB: en el segundo trimestre creció un 0,8% frente al primero y un 3,1% interanual

El INE vuelve a revisar al alza el PIB: en el segundo trimestre creció un 0,8% frente al primero y un 3,1% interanual

The National Statistics Institute (INE) has revised Spain’s economic growth upward, confirming stronger domestic demand and resilience in the face of international trade uncertainties. This positive adjustment, particularly in the second quarter, underscores the crucial role of internal consumption and business investment in sustaining the economy when external conditions are volatile. The data reveal a robust labor market and increased purchasing power, which drove household spending and corporate capital formation. Although the contribution of international trade was negligible in the short term, the fundamental strength of the domestic market provided a solid foundation for growth, suggesting that the economy is successfully navigating global instability through internal drivers. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how official statistical institutions like the INE continuously refine their datasets to reflect real-time economic realities. Access to these revised, granular figures enables analysts and policymakers to verify economic trends, assess the accuracy of initial projections, and make informed decisions based on the most current and reliable public information available.
