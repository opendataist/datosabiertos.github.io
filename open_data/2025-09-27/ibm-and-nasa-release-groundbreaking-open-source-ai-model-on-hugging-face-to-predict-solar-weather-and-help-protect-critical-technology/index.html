IBM and NASA Release Groundbreaking Open-Source AI Model on Hugging Face to Predict Solar Weather and Help Protect Critical Technology

IBM and NASA Release Groundbreaking Open-Source AI Model on Hugging Face to Predict Solar Weather and Help Protect Critical Technology

IBM and NASA have unveiled Surya, a revolutionary open-source foundation model designed to interpret high-resolution solar observations and forecast space weather. This collaboration addresses the growing vulnerability of modern infrastructure to solar activity, which can severely disrupt GPS, power grids, and telecommunications. By providing accurate predictions of solar flares and coronal mass ejections, the model serves as a critical early-warning system, helping to mitigate substantial economic and technological risks associated with extreme solar storms. The model achieves superior accuracy by training on nine years of extensive, high-resolution data from NASA’s Solar Dynamics Observatory, overcoming previous limitations in spatial detail. This technical advancement allows for both precise classification of solar events and visual prediction of flare locations up to two hours in advance. Such capabilities represent a significant leap in data-driven scientific discovery, enabling researchers to analyze complex solar behaviors with unprecedented speed and precision, thereby enhancing our understanding of how solar activity impacts critical systems on Earth and in space. Releasing Surya on Hugging Face exemplifies the core principles of open data by democratizing access to advanced scientific tools. This move allows the global research community to freely build upon the foundation, fostering innovation in specialized applications for various industries and regions. By making this powerful AI model publicly available, IBM and NASA are not only accelerating space weather forecasting but also promoting transparency and collaborative progress in heliophysics, ensuring that vital knowledge and technology are accessible to all who can benefit from them.
