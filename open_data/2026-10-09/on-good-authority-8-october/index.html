On Good Authority 8 October

On Good Authority 8 October

The High Court of Australia invalidated a coal mine approval, reinforcing that legislative schemes must consider greenhouse gas emissions. This ruling highlights the growing legal pressure to account for climate impacts in infrastructure decisions, a critical context for open data systems tracking environmental compliance. Simultaneously, global efforts advance through the UN’s declaration on sea-level rise and new research linking project emissions to specific climate impacts. These developments underscore the urgent need for transparent, accessible data to monitor ocean changes and individual carbon footprints. Open data becomes essential here, enabling the verification of emissions and supporting circular economy transitions. By providing clear, authoritative information, open data empowers policymakers and citizens to hold entities accountable, ensuring that climate actions are measurable and transparent rather than ignored.
