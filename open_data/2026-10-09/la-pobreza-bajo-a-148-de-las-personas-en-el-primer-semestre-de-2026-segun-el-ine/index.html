La pobreza bajó a 14,8% de las personas en el primer semestre de 2026, según el INE

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INE) revealed a significant reduction in poverty and extreme poverty rates for the first half of 2026. This improvement indicates that fewer people and households are unable to meet their basic needs, reflecting progress in social welfare compared to the previous year. These data are crucial for the open data sector, as they demonstrate the value of official statistical series in the continuous monitoring of social progress. Transparency in the dissemination of these indicators facilitates public analysis and accountability. Moreover, it illustrates how standardized methodologies enable precise temporal comparisons. Access to this structured information empowers civil society and researchers to understand economic dynamics and propose evidence-based solutions.
