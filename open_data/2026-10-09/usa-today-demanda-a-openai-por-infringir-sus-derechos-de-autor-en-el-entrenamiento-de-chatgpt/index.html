USA Today demanda a OpenAI por infringir sus derechos de autor en el entrenamiento de ChatGPT

USA Today is suing OpenAI for using its content without permission to train AI, challenging the legitimacy of "fair use." This legal dispute redefines the ethical and legal boundaries in current technological development. The conflict highlights the tension between artificial intelligence innovation and the protection of intellectual property. The implications directly affect transparency and consent in data collection, which are fundamental to responsible AI governance. This issue is particularly relevant to open data advocates, as it underscores the need to balance access to information with copyright rights. It reinforces the urgency of establishing clear frameworks that allow the use of public data without violating existing regulations.
