Leve variación de la cifra que anticipa la inflación nacional de septiembre

Leve variación de la cifra que anticipa la inflación nacional de septiembre

Inflation in the City of Buenos Aires registered a slight acceleration in September, temporarily breaking the prior trend of deceleration. This behavior suggests that the national price dynamics, to be published shortly, could present a similar scenario. The monthly increase was mainly driven by adjustments in regulated services, such as housing and energy, as well as by significant rises in the food category, which directly impacts citizens’ cost of living. This analysis is fundamental to the open data ecosystem, as the official indicators from IDECBA and INDEC form the basis upon which public and journalistic information searches are structured. The timely and transparent availability of these indices enables consulting firms, academics, and the general public to contrast economic reality with market expectations, fostering accountability and evidence-based analysis. The relevance of these data lies in their ability to unravel the structural causes of inflation, distinguishing between seasonal, core, and regulated components. By breaking down the data by sectors such as health, education, and the basic food basket, it becomes easier to identify the most vulnerable groups. This statistical transparency is crucial for designing effective public policies and for civil society to monitor the real impact of economic decisions on their daily lives.
