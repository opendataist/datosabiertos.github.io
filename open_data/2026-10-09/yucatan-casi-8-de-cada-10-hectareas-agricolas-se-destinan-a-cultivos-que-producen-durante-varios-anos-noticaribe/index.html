YUCATÁN | Casi 8 de cada 10 hectáreas agrícolas se destinan a cultivos que producen durante varios años - Noticaribe

Yucatán stands out for its predominance of perennial crops, a pattern distinct from the national one, where annual crops dominate. This agricultural structure reflects a specific adaptation to the local environment, contrasting with the country’s average. The National Agricultural Survey reveals that most of the arable land in the state is dedicated to these long-cycle crops. While other regions of the peninsula vary in their focus, Yucatán maintains a significant concentration on perennial crops, which influences its seasonal productivity. This data is relevant for open data transparency, as it illustrates how official statistics capture critical regional variations. By publishing these details, the government enables in-depth analysis of sustainability and land use, which are essential for understanding the true agricultural diversity of the nation.
