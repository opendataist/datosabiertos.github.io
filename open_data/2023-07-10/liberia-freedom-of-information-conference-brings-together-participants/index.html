Liberia : Freedom of information conference brings together participants (...)

The Robertsport FOI Conference concluded with a strong mandate for participants to actively enforce the Liberia Freedom of Information Act. Delegates urged the Center for Media Studies and Peace Building to create an alternative data bank, serving as a critical reference point when institutions withhold requested information. This initiative aims to ensure responsive governance by providing a reliable source of public records despite potential bureaucratic resistance. Participants pledged to translate the law and raise awareness in their communities, fostering a culture of open access. They also called for legal reforms, specifically the decentralization of appeal channels, to make grievance processes more accessible. These efforts highlight a grassroots commitment to holding power accountable and ensuring that citizens can effectively demand transparency from both public and relevant private bodies. This event is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how legal frameworks empower communities to demand and manage information independently. By establishing independent data repositories and promoting legal literacy, such initiatives directly support the core open data principles of accessibility, accountability, and citizen engagement in governance.
