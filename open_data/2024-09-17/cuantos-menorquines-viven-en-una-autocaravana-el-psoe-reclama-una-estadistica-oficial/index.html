¿Cuántos menorquines viven en una autocaravana? El PSOE reclama una estadística oficial

¿Cuántos menorquines viven en una autocaravana? El PSOE reclama una estadística oficial

The PSOE demands the collection of data on the growing number of low-income caravans to address the housing crisis and tourism saturation. This transparency is essential for open data, as precise metrics enable the government to design effective policies. Quantifying these trends reveals their environmental and infrastructural impacts. Without this information, authorities cannot regulate the uncontrolled influx, leaving vulnerable families without sustainable housing solutions. Accurate statistics transform anecdotal evidence into actionable public policy. Open data thus bridges the gap between social reality and governance, ensuring resources are allocated where they are truly needed.
