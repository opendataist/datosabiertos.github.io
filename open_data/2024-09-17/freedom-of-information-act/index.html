Freedom of Information Act

Freedom of Information Act

This document outlines the operational framework for accessing public records at Illinois Eastern Community College, emphasizing transparency under the state’s Freedom of Information Act. The college balances its educational mission with a legal obligation to provide public access to government records while safeguarding individual privacy. By clearly defining the roles of the Board of Trustees and functional subdivisions, the institution demonstrates how structured governance supports accountability and openness in higher education administration. The process for requesting records is designed to be accessible yet specific, requiring detailed descriptions to streamline retrieval. This structured approach ensures that information regarding budgets, meeting minutes, and organizational charts is efficiently shared with the public. The existence of a dedicated FOIA Officer and standardized request forms highlights the college’s commitment to reducing barriers to information, fostering a culture where citizens can easily monitor institutional operations and spending. Relevance to open data lies in the inherent tension and collaboration between statutory privacy protections and the push for digital transparency. While this specific text focuses on procedural compliance rather than machine-readable datasets, it establishes the foundational legal and administrative precedents necessary for open data initiatives. Understanding these boundaries helps developers and advocates distinguish between protected personal information and the vast amounts of institutional data that should be openly available, ensuring that modern data initiatives respect legal constraints while maximizing public utility.
