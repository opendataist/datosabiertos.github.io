The "Rice Route" policy, introduced to lower consumer prices through tariff reductions and market deregulation, has failed to achieve its stated goal. Official data shows no price decrease, as these measures merely aligned with promotions existing since 2020 rather than creating new economic relief. This approach negatively impacts rural employment, producer viability, and agricultural production areas, thereby threatening national food security. The government’s justification for the policy is thus unfounded, causing significant harm to both local stakeholders and the broader economy without benefiting consumers. This case highlights the critical importance of open data in holding institutions accountable. By analyzing accessible statistical evidence, civil society can verify policy claims, prevent misinformation, and ensure that public decisions are grounded in reality rather than political rhetoric.
