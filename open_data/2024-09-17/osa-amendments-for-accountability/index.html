OSA amendments for accountability

OSA amendments for accountability

Amending the Official Secrets Act to align with the Freedom of Information Act strengthens government transparency. This reform supports the open data movement by reducing legal barriers to public information. Consequently, citizens gain better access to essential government records, fostering greater accountability and trust.
