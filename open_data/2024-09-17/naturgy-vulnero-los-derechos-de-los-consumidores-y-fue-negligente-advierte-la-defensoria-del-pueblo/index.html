Naturgy vulneró los derechos de los consumidores y fue negligente, advierte la Defensoría del Pueblo

Naturgy vulneró los derechos de los consumidores y fue negligente, advierte la Defensoría del Pueblo

The People’s Ombudsman determined that Naturgy violated consumer rights and the right to access information through deceptive billing practices. This finding highlights systemic failures in corporate transparency, emphasizing that utility providers must ensure clear, accessible, and honest communication with users. Such transparency is fundamental to open data initiatives, as it empowers citizens to understand how services are managed and priced, fostering trust and accountability in public service delivery. The institution recommended that regulators conduct a thorough investigation into disproportionate price hikes and poor service quality. This underscores the critical need for regulatory bodies to have access to comprehensive, standardized data to enforce compliance effectively. When open data principles are applied, regulators can better monitor service metrics, identify anomalies, and hold companies accountable, ensuring that consumer protections are not just theoretical but actively enforced through transparent oversight mechanisms. Although Naturgy claims cooperation, its past lack of collaboration with investigations reveals gaps in data sharing that hinder effective consumer protection. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for utilities to proactively disclose operational and billing data. By making this information publicly available and machine-readable, companies can facilitate independent analysis, reduce conflicts, and enable stakeholders to verify claims, ultimately leading to more efficient and fair energy markets.
