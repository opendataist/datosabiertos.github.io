Estas son las tres drogas más comunes al volante según los últimos datos publicados por la DGT

The DGT’s intensive control campaign underscores that preventive inspections are the primary method for identifying drivers impaired by alcohol and drugs, significantly reducing road risks. This approach proves more effective than reacting to accidents or infractions, emphasizing the necessity of proactive enforcement strategies. The data reveal a substantial number of violations across various substances, with cannabis and cocaine being the most prevalent. These findings highlight the ongoing public safety challenge posed by substance abuse while driving and the critical role of systematic testing in maintaining traffic security. This article is relevant to open data, as it illustrates how large-scale governmental traffic statistics provide essential evidence for evaluating public policy. Transparent access to such data enables researchers and citizens to monitor enforcement efficacy, advocate for safer roads, and hold authorities accountable for road safety outcomes.
