UE investiga si Google cumple regulación de uso de datos personales

The Irish Data Protection Commission is investigating whether Google complied with EU privacy laws when training its AI models using personal data. This inquiry assesses whether proper impact assessments were conducted to protect fundamental rights under the GDPR, addressing concerns about unregulated tech practices. Such investigations highlight the critical tension between AI innovation and data privacy compliance. They serve as a reminder that open data initiatives must strictly adhere to regulatory frameworks to safeguard user rights and maintain public trust. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the legal boundaries governing personal information. It emphasizes that even for advanced technologies, transparency and accountability remain essential to ensure ethical data usage and respect for individual freedoms in the digital age.
