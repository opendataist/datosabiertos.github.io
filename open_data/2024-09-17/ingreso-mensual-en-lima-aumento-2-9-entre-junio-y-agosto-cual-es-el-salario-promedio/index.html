Ingreso mensual en Lima aumentó 2.9% entre junio y agosto: ¿Cuál es el salario promedio?

The latest labor statistics for the Lima Metropolitan Area reveal a broad-based improvement in income and employment, signaling a positive economic trajectory. Wages increased across all demographic groups, with older workers and university graduates experiencing the most significant gains. This suggests that educational attainment and work experience remain strong determinants of earning potential, underscoring the value of human capital development in the current labor market. Employment levels rose substantially, driven largely by the manufacturing, commerce, and services sectors. Notably, job creation was more robust for women and individuals aged 45 and older, indicating shifting dynamics in the labor market. The data also reflect a general improvement in job quality, with adequate employment growing faster than underemployment, pointing to better working conditions for employees. This information is crucial for open data advocates, as it demonstrates how accessible, granular statistical data can reveal nuanced social trends and inform public policy. By making these indicators publicly available, institutions empower citizens and researchers to hold governments accountable and design inclusive strategies that address wage disparities and promote equitable labor opportunities.
