Descartan en Bolivia polítización de censo poblacional - Noticias Prensa Latina

Descartan en Bolivia polítización de censo poblacional - Noticias Prensa Latina

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) has firmly defended the technical integrity of Bolivia’s recent census data, dismissing criticisms from certain regional authorities as politically motivated rather than evidence-based. By emphasizing its role as a technical institution, the agency asserts that the population figures are robust and supported by international validation, contrasting this with the absence of key critics from technical review forums. This stance highlights a critical divide between institutional transparency and political skepticism regarding demographic statistics. The relevance to open data lies in the ongoing tension between statistical transparency and public trust. The article illustrates how open government initiatives face challenges when official data is contested by powerful stakeholders who lack technical consistency in their arguments. It underscores the importance of maintaining accessible, verifiable data methodologies to counter political narratives, ensuring that open data serves as a tool for factual accountability rather than becoming a source of partisan dispute. Ultimately, the INE’s commitment to reviewing every ballot reinforces the principle that open data requires both accessibility and rigorous defense of its integrity. This case demonstrates that for open data ecosystems to function effectively, institutions must actively engage in transparent dialogue while resisting politicization. It serves as a reminder that true openness involves not just releasing numbers, but also fostering a technical environment where data can withstand scrutiny through clear, evidence-based communication.
