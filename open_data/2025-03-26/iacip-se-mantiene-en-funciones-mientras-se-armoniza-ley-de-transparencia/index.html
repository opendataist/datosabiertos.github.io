IACIP se mantiene en funciones mientras se armoniza Ley de Transparencia

IACIP se mantiene en funciones mientras se armoniza Ley de Transparencia

The article highlights a critical transitional period for transparency mechanisms in Guanajuato, following the national disappearance of the INAI. The local institute remains operational while working to align state regulations with the new General Law of Transparency within a strict ninety-day window. This interim phase is crucial for ensuring that the fundamental rights to access information and data protection are not weakened during the institutional restructuring, despite the uncertainty surrounding the future of the local body. A key implication of this situation is the urgent need for legal and labor certainty for the institute’s workforce during the transition. The leadership has emphasized continuity in operations and engaged with state authorities to strengthen these human rights. By maintaining functionality and encouraging citizens to use legal channels against non-compliant entities, the state aims to preserve accountability and uphold the rule of law until a new institutional framework is fully established. This case is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the vulnerability of transparency infrastructure during regulatory shifts. Guanajuato’s record for rapid response times underscores the value of efficient systems, yet the current ambiguity threatens these achievements. The article illustrates how national policy changes directly impact local data accessibility, stressing the importance of stable, robust institutions to guarantee citizens' rights to information amidst governmental evolution.
