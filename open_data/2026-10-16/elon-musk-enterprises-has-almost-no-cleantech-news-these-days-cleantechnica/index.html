Elon Musk Enterprises Has Almost No Cleantech News These Days - CleanTechnica

The article critiques the significant shift in focus from clean technology to political entanglements within Elon Musk’s enterprises, noting a stark absence of clean energy news from Tesla or SpaceX. This transition marks a disappointing departure from the original mission of accelerating sustainable energy, as resources and attention are increasingly diverted toward supporting fossil fuel interests and conservative political campaigns. The author argues that this strategic pivot undermines the urgent global need for cleantech innovation and highlights a troubling alignment with oligarchic and war-related profiteering rather than environmental progress. Furthermore, the text examines the geopolitical implications of Musk’s actions, specifically alleging that SpaceX restricted Starlink services in Ukraine as retaliation for strikes on Russian oil infrastructure. These incidents suggest that private technological assets are being weaponized for political leverage, complicating military efforts and raising concerns about foreign interference in democratic processes. The narrative draws parallels between these actions and broader alliances with authoritarian regimes, implying that such decisions prioritize profit and political influence over humanitarian and strategic stability in conflict zones. This content is relevant to open data as it illustrates the critical need for transparency in corporate political activities and their impact on public infrastructure. Open data initiatives can expose the hidden connections between technology giants, fossil fuel interests, and political funding, allowing citizens and researchers to scrutinize these power dynamics.
