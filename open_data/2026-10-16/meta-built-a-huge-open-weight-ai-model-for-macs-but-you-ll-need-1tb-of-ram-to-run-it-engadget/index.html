Meta built a huge open-weight AI model for Macs, but you'll need 1TB of RAM to run it - Engadget

Meta’s release of Muse Spark Open highlights its strategic commitment to dominating the open-weight AI landscape, particularly in countering Chinese competitors. By showcasing superior performance in agentic tasks and visual reasoning, Meta positions its technology as the premier US-based alternative, reinforcing the importance of open access to high-capability models for global technological sovereignty. This development is significant for open_data as it democratizes access to state-of-the-art architectures, even if hardware constraints currently limit local deployment to well-resourced organizations. The move encourages community verification and adaptation of large-scale models, fostering transparency and innovation within the open-source ecosystem despite the substantial infrastructure requirements needed to run them. Ultimately, the release underscores a shifting paradigm where open-weight models are becoming increasingly viable for specialized, high-performance computing environments. This trend promotes a more diverse and competitive market, allowing developers to inspect, modify, and deploy powerful AI solutions without relying solely on closed proprietary APIs, thereby strengthening the integrity and resilience of open data practices.
