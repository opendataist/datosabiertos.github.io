Environmental organizations call for a transparency law for Argentine shrimp fishing | Mercado Magazine

The demand for transparency regarding quotas and beneficiaries aims to ensure responsible governance and enable the auditing of environmental controls in Argentina’s fishing industry. This openness is crucial for the governance of common-pool resources and promotes accountability within the fishing sector. The relevance to open data lies in its potential to enhance transparency and traceability in the management of natural resources.
