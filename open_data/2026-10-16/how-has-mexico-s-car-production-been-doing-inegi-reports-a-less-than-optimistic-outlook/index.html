How has Mexico's car production been doing? INEGI reports a less-than-optimistic outlook

Light vehicle production in Mexico presents a discouraging outlook, marked by a sustained decline over the first nine months of 2026. This negative trend contrasts with the brief growth observed in March and April, but has become the dominant pattern throughout the year, revealing structural weakness in the national automotive sector. The report shows that cumulative reductions extend through September, consolidating the worst contraction recorded to date. Although there were isolated moments of recovery, the overall trajectory points toward a continuous decrease in industrial output, suggesting significant challenges for competitiveness and domestic market demand compared to the previous year. This information is crucial for the open data domain, as the RAIAVL and INEGI provide official indicators that allow for the analysis of economic health and industrial trends. Transparency in these data facilitates academic research and public policy analysis, enabling civil society and experts to assess the real impact of commercial dynamics on the country’s productive fabric.
