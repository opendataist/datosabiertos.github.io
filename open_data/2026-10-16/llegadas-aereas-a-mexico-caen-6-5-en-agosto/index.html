Llegadas aéreas a México caen 6.5% en agosto

Llegadas aéreas a México caen 6.5% en agosto

Preliminary data from INEGI reveal a worrying trend in Mexican air tourism, with a significant drop in arrivals during August that continues a sustained decline since April. This decrease primarily affects non-border visitors, whose main source group is U.S. tourists. However, this decline in traveler volume is offset by a notable increase in tourist spending, indicating that those visiting Mexico are consuming more, despite coming in smaller numbers. In parallel, the political landscape includes the Chamber of Deputies’ approval of an increase in the fee for foreigners, known as the Non-Resident Fee, which now awaits the Senate’s decision for 2027. This fare increase, which directly affects air tourists and is generally integrated into airfare, has sparked division between the government and the hotel sector, which argues that it could discourage visitor arrivals in an already delicate context. This news is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accessing and analyzing official statistics in real time, such as those provided by INEGI. Transparency in these data allows investors, analysts, and the public to assess the immediate impact of public policies and economic variables, understanding that the openness of state-level statistical information is key to making informed decisions about a sector vital to the Mexican economy.
