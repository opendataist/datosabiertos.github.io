And the cost of living rises again - López-Dóriga Digital

And the cost of living rises again - López-Dóriga Digital

The article alleges that the Mexican government has implemented significant increases in various taxes and fees, ranging from health licenses to tourist taxes, in order to fund pensions and social programs ahead of the 2027 elections. These measures have eroded the purchasing power of the population and increased the cost of private healthcare services, effectively shifting the financial burden onto citizens. At the same time, the hike in taxes on foreign travelers aims to generate revenue but threatens the competitiveness of the tourism sector, which is already grappling with a connectivity crisis and insecurity. The relevance of this text to open data lies in the critical need for transparency and public access to information regarding fiscal execution and macroeconomic indicators. To properly assess the impact of these policies, civil society and researchers require open, verified, and up-to-date data that can counteract one-sided narratives. Only through the availability of robust official statistics can inflation, employment impacts, and the effectiveness of revenue collection be accurately measured, thereby fostering accountability based on empirical evidence rather than political opinions.
