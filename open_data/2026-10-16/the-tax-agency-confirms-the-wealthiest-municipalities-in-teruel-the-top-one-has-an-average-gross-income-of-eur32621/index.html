The Tax Agency confirms the wealthiest municipalities in Teruel: the top one has an average gross income of €32,621

The Tax Agency confirms the wealthiest municipalities in Teruel: the top one has an average gross income of €32,621

The city of Teruel leads the province in per capita income, consolidating itself as the most prosperous economic hub in the region. This outstanding performance is attributed to the convergence of services, public administration, and a booming heritage tourism sector, complemented by industrial activity linked to the airport. This leadership position reflects the capital’s ability to diversify its productive base and maintain a significant competitive advantage over the rest of the municipalities in the autonomous community. Andorra and Montalbán complete the provincial podium, highlighting the influence of emerging sectors on local development. While Andorra drives its economy through the transition to renewable energy, Montalbán demonstrates notable growth thanks to its historical and cultural value. This rise in the ranking surpasses traditional towns such as Alcañiz, whose dynamism continues to depend on technological and sports parks, signaling a reconfiguration of economic priorities within the province. This analysis is relevant for open data initiatives, as it underscores the impact of fiscal transparency on territorial planning and public decision-making. The availability of official income tax (IRPF) data enables the identification of regional disparities and the evaluation of the effectiveness of local development policies. Moreover, it facilitates academic research and the creation of analytical services that help understand how industrial, tourism, or energy factors directly impact citizens’ financial well-being.
