Page celebra el incremento de población en C-LM: "Somos más, vamos creciendo, pero además hay oportunidad para trabajar"

Castilla-La Mancha demonstrates that population growth can align with record employment levels and economic expansion. This positive trajectory challenges narratives of rural depopulation, proving that the autonomous community’s model fosters demographic and labor vitality. This case is relevant to open data, as it highlights how transparent and accessible public statistics are crucial for validating regional development. By openly sharing demographic and economic metrics, governments can effectively communicate progress, influence policy decisions, and build trust in sustainable territorial management strategies.
