Navigating The Privacy Tightrope For CTIS

Anonymizing personal data in clinical trials is essential for ethical compliance within the EU’s new CTIS framework. This process ensures patient privacy while enabling the responsible sharing of vast research datasets. For open data, it highlights the critical balance between transparency and privacy, demonstrating that rigorous de-identification is the foundation for trustworthy, scalable health data ecosystems.
