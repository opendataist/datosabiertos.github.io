Inflation reaches 29.90% amid rising cost of living - The Chronicle News Online

Elevated inflation rates underscore the critical need for transparent, timely economic data. Accessible statistics empower citizens and institutions to understand cost-of-living pressures and hold authorities accountable for price stability. Robust open data initiatives are essential for verifying official figures and enabling independent analysis. When economic indicators are freely available, it fosters trust and allows researchers to identify trends in food and essential goods pricing. This transparency supports informed public discourse and better policy responses. Open data transforms raw statistics into actionable insights, helping communities navigate economic challenges and advocate for sustainable financial solutions.
