Public register

Openness and transparency are vital for strengthening the European Investment Bank’s credibility, accountability, and operational efficiency. By maintaining a dedicated public register, the institution ensures accessible environmental information in compliance with EU frameworks. This commitment to openness serves as a model for open data practices, demonstrating how public institutions can enhance stakeholder trust through proactive information sharing. It highlights the importance of transparent governance in building sustainable relationships with citizens and external partners. Ultimately, this approach underscores the broader significance of open data in fostering democratic engagement and institutional integrity within the European Union.
