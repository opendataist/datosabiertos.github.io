La cifra de adolescentes excarcelados ascendió a 86 según Foro Penal

La cifra de adolescentes excarcelados ascendió a 86 según Foro Penal

Venezuela has released several detained adolescents under precautionary measures following post-election protests, a development highlighted by human rights organizations. This event underscores the urgent need for transparency in judicial processes involving minors, as many were previously held arbitrarily and mixed with adult prisoners, violating their legal rights and due process protections. The situation remains critical, as numerous teenagers are still imprisoned despite calls for their immediate release. The lack of accurate, publicly accessible data on these detentions complicates accountability efforts. It is essential for authorities to provide clear information on the status of all detained minors to ensure compliance with national and international child protection laws. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how limited or opaque government records hinder civil society’s ability to monitor human rights abuses. Reliable, accessible data on judicial decisions and prison demographics allows NGOs to verify facts, track trends, and advocate effectively for policy changes that protect vulnerable populations in democratic contexts.
