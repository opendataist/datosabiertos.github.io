Nueva herramienta de datos abiertos contra la corrupción

Nueva herramienta de datos abiertos contra la corrupción

The creation of the Global Corruption Observatory represents a significant advance in public transparency by integrating large volumes of data on public procurement and legislative processes. By providing structured microdata and resources for international comparative analysis, the initiative transforms government information into a vital tool for citizen scrutiny and academic research. This platform enables civil society to monitor public spending with greater precision, strengthening accountability and improving the quality of electoral decisions. By democratizing access to information, it empowers citizens to demand more efficient and ethical public management, grounding their voting choices in concrete evidence. The project is relevant to the open data movement because it demonstrates how the interoperability of transnational data can combat corruption. By combining big data with specialized training, it establishes a replicable model that prioritizes radical transparency as a fundamental mechanism for the continuous improvement of democratic governance.
