Bank lending in Oman grows 3.8% in H1; deposits jump 11.6%

Oman’s banking sector demonstrated robust growth in mid-2024, driven by rising credit extension and deposit accumulation. The central bank reported that total outstanding credit expanded, with the private sector capturing the majority of new loans. This trend highlights a strengthening economy where non-financial corporations and households remain the primary beneficiaries of financial services, signaling healthy commercial activity and consumer confidence within the national market. Simultaneously, the Islamic banking segment experienced significant asset growth, constituting a substantial portion of the total system. This expansion reflects increasing financial inclusion and the growing preference for Sharia-compliant financial products among consumers and businesses. The parallel rise in deposits for both conventional and Islamic entities indicates enhanced liquidity and trust in the local banking framework, showcasing a diverse and evolving financial landscape that accommodates varied customer needs. This report is relevant to open data advocates because it exemplifies high-quality, granular macroeconomic transparency. By publishing detailed sectoral breakdowns, interest rate trends, and asset compositions, the Central Bank of Oman provides researchers and developers with the structured datasets necessary for rigorous economic analysis. Such accessible, timely data enables independent verification of financial health and supports the creation of value-added applications that can further enhance public understanding of monetary policy impacts.
