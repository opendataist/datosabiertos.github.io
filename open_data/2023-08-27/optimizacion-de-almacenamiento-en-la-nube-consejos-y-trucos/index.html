Optimización de almacenamiento en la nube: Consejos y Trucos

The article concludes that optimizing cloud storage goes beyond mere cost savings to become a strategic pillar that enhances operational efficiency, productivity, and information security. By maintaining an organized data management approach, organizations not only reduce recurring expenses by eliminating redundancies but also accelerate access to information and strengthen protection against data loss or breaches through updated systems. The need to adopt proactive practices is emphasized, such as carefully selecting providers based on specific needs, logically categorizing files, and periodically removing obsolete data. The use of compression tools, selective synchronization, and automated cleanup helps maximize available capacity and facilitate collaboration, ensuring that the digital infrastructure effectively supports daily tasks without creating bottlenecks. This discussion is relevant to the open data movement because it underscores the importance of governance and technical accessibility of information. The ability to manage, protect, and optimize the storage of public or community data is essential to ensure that these resources remain available, secure, and usable in the long term. Without proper optimization, even the most valuable datasets can become inaccessible or costly to maintain, hindering their free access and reuse by society.
