Community, intellectual property, and artificial intelligence: this was the second edition of Quirino Lab

Community, intellectual property, and artificial intelligence: this was the second edition of Quirino Lab

Quirino Lab has demonstrated that Ibero-American animation is undergoing an accelerated transformation, in which community and intellectual property have become the most valuable strategic assets. Rather than offering closed-ended answers, the event fostered an open debate that prioritizes the development of collaborative networks and the professionalization of the sector, encouraging production companies to act as engines of recurring value creation instead of relying solely on isolated projects or public subsidies. The introduction of artificial intelligence and the agile management of audiences are redefining traditional business models. It has been emphasized that building direct communities and using generative tools with ethical criteria enable the validation of ideas and cost reduction, positioning independent studios as innovative agents capable of diversifying funding sources and exploiting new distribution windows beyond traditional platforms. This event is significant for open data, as it highlights the need for transparency in the use of emerging technologies and the traceability of creative resources. By promoting frameworks that include the disclosure of AI usage and the protection of intellectual property, Quirino Lab establishes ethical and operational precedents. These principles are essential to ensure that the audiovisual ecosystem maintains the integrity of its data and processes, guaranteeing that human talent and artistic vision remain the key differentiators in a saturated market.
