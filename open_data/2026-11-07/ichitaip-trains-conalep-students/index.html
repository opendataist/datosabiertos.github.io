Ichitaip trains Conalep students

Ichitaip trains Conalep students

The Ichitaip collaborated with Conalep to train students in the fundamentals of information access and personal data protection, consolidating transparency as an essential tool for strengthening citizen participation and trust in public institutions. Likewise, the importance of integrity and accountability was emphasized to prevent corruption, promoting a culture of legality and social responsibility among younger generations, which is crucial for the ethical development of the community. This initiative is relevant to open data because it fosters digital literacy and awareness about privacy, elements necessary for citizens to understand and exercise their rights in an informed manner, thereby contributing to a more open and transparent society.
