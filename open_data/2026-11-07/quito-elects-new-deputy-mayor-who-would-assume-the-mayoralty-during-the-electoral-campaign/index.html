Quito elects new deputy mayor who would assume the mayoralty during the electoral campaign

Quito elects new deputy mayor who would assume the mayoralty during the electoral campaign

The Metropolitan Council of Quito has formalized the procedure for appointing the new alternate deputy mayor, a key position to ensure administrative continuity during the electoral leaves of the mayor and the incumbent deputy mayor. This legal measure aims to address institutional gaps by ensuring that there is an authority responsible for the municipal executive branch in cases of simultaneous leave, thereby strengthening the stability of local public management. The approved regulation integrates principles of transparency and equity by stipulating that the appointment must be published on the Open Government portal and respect gender parity. By requiring that the alternate deputy mayor be a woman, the document not only complies with democratic standards but also leverages open data mechanisms as a mandatory tool for accountability, linking political decision-making with the public visibility of procedures. This case is relevant to the open data movement because it demonstrates how transparency regulations can be operationalized to protect state integrity. By requiring key resolutions to be available on accessible digital platforms, it promotes citizen participation and scrutiny, turning data openness into a fundamental pillar for institutional trust and the prevention of leadership crises during electoral periods.
