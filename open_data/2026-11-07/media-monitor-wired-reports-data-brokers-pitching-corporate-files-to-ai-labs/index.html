Media Monitor: WIRED Reports Data Brokers Pitching Corporate Files to AI Labs

Media Monitor: WIRED Reports Data Brokers Pitching Corporate Files to AI Labs

Data brokers are selling private corporate communications to AI labs, signaling a shift from public to proprietary training data. This surge threatens worker privacy and raises significant ethical concerns about unregulated information exploitation. The practice highlights the urgent need for transparency and governance in open data ecosystems. As private datasets become critical resources, establishing clear standards for access and usage is essential to protect individual rights and maintain public trust in AI development. Relevance to open data lies in the tension between accessible information and commercialized privacy. This trend underscores the importance of safeguarding public domain knowledge while addressing the risks associated with the commodification of personal and corporate secrets in the age of artificial intelligence.
