Tinubu launches National Geospatial Data Repository, says census’ll hold soon

President Tinubu pledges an imminent national census, emphasizing that reliable data is crucial for Nigeria’s growth and requires advanced technological deployment. This initiative highlights the critical role of accurate population metrics in national planning and development. The announcement underscores the vital importance of open, accessible demographic data for effective governance and future policy decisions.
