Tinubu Assures Of Accurate Population Census In Nigeria

Nigeria has launched the Digital Civil Registration and Vital Statistics System alongside a National Geospatial Data Repository, marking a significant shift toward evidence-based governance. President Tinubu emphasizes that accurate population data is crucial for tailoring public services and supporting national economic ambitions. By treating population records as the foundation of an identity ecosystem, the government aims to enhance service delivery across multiple federal agencies, ensuring that infrastructure and policies reflect the true needs of citizens rather than relying on outdated estimates. This initiative integrates vital statistics with geospatial information to create a robust framework for real-time identity management. The system is designed to improve the capacity of security and administrative bodies to track demographic changes, migration patterns, and vital events. By centralizing this data, the administration seeks to foster transparency and efficiency, allowing for more responsive and effective policy-making that directly addresses the specific challenges faced by different communities across the nation. For the open data community, this launch represents a critical step toward standardized, accessible demographic infrastructure. It highlights the importance of open, interoperable data systems in achieving sustainable development goals and fostering digital inclusion. When government data is accurate, geospatially linked, and openly available, it empowers researchers, developers, and civil society to build innovative solutions. This move underscores the value of transparent data ecosystems in driving accountability, enabling better resource allocation, and ultimately supporting the broader mission of good governance through technology.
