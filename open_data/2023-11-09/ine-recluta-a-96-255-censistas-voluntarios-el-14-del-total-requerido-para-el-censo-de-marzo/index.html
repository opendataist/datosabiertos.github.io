INE recluta a 96.255 censistas voluntarios, el 14% del total requerido para el censo de marzo

Bolivia’s national census recruitment demonstrates the power of open data by leveraging a public digital platform to engage citizens as voluntary enumerators. This transparent approach allows the government to track participation trends and demographic distribution in real time, fostering trust through accessible information. The initiative highlights how open systems facilitate large-scale civic participation. By making registration data available, the statistical institute can monitor progress and identify regional disparities, ensuring the census reflects the true population structure through widespread, verifiable citizen involvement. This relevance to open data lies in the shift from opaque bureaucracy to interactive governance. It proves that transparent, publicly accessible tools can significantly enhance data collection efforts, encouraging community ownership and improving the accuracy of national statistics through mass voluntary engagement.
