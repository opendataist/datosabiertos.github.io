La Confederación de Empresarios de Cuenca advierte de que hay menos financiación y con un coste mayor

Spanish SMEs face severely restricted access to financing due to significantly higher costs and reduced loan volumes. This shift is driven by soaring interest rates, which have nearly tripled in a year, marking the most expensive credit environment in a decade. The inflationary context further exacerbates these financial pressures for small businesses. Consequently, commercial credit between companies has tightened, leading to longer payment cycles for invoices. Although most payments remain timely, the proportion of late settlements has risen compared to pre-pandemic levels, straining liquidity and operational stability within the private sector. This trend is highly relevant to open data initiatives. Transparent, real-time financial indicators are crucial for monitoring economic health and informing policy. Open data allows stakeholders to better understand credit accessibility challenges, fostering informed decision-making and supporting strategies to alleviate financial burdens on SMEs during economic volatility.
