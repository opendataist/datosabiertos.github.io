Open Data
ES EN

Nigeria’s launch of the National Geospatial Data Repository and Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics System represents a critical advancement in building a digital identity ecosystem. This initiative aims to create a unified, reliable source of demographic data that integrates across federal agencies, enhancing service delivery and enabling evidence-based policy-making. By transitioning from paper-based records to a digital framework, the government seeks to ensure legal identity for all citizens, a prerequisite for effective national planning and inclusive governance. The relevance to open data lies in the commitment to generating accurate, real-time population statistics to support Sustainable Development Goals. Reliable vital statistics are essential not only for tracking health outcomes, such as maternal mortality, but also for designing targeted interventions that address societal needs. The system’s interoperability with existing national identity databases ensures that data flows seamlessly between agencies, fostering transparency and accountability in how public resources are allocated and managed. Ultimately, this infrastructure underscores the importance of public-private partnerships and international collaboration in modernizing state institutions. By pledging support for the upcoming national census and encouraging inter-agency cooperation, Nigeria is laying the groundwork for a data-driven society. This move highlights how open, accessible, and high-quality demographic data can serve as a foundational asset for development, allowing citizens and policymakers alike to make informed decisions that improve the quality of life for the entire population.
