AISSEE 2024 Notification Releases, NTA Sainik School Exam on January 21, Register at exams.nta.ac.in

The National Testing Agency has initiated the registration process for the All India Sainik School Entrance Examination, marking a significant administrative update for prospective students. This opening allows eligible candidates to secure their spots for the upcoming academic year through a streamlined digital application system, emphasizing the accessibility and efficiency of modern educational admission protocols. The examination serves as the primary gateway for admissions into Classes 6 and 9, with specific eligibility criteria regarding age and prior educational qualifications. By establishing clear timelines and requirements, the agency ensures a structured selection process that maintains fairness and transparency for all applicants across different regions and demographic categories.
