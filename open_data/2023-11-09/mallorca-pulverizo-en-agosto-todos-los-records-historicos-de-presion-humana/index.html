Mallorca pulverizó en agosto todos los récords históricos de presión humana

In August 2023, the Balearic Islands recorded unprecedented historical peaks in both daily and cumulative human pressure. This surge in visitor numbers highlights an intensification of tourism, with every major island contributing to the overall record, except Formentera, which remained below its 2017 maximum. The data reveals significant disparities in seasonal density across the different islands. While most locations showed moderate fluctuations between peak and low periods, Formentera experienced the highest ratio of maximum to minimum daily loads, indicating an extreme concentration of visitors even outside its historic peak. This article is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how transparent, statistical monitoring enables policymakers and researchers to track environmental stress and tourism trends. Open access to such granular datasets is crucial for sustainable management, allowing stakeholders to identify patterns and address the growing pressures on island ecosystems effectively.
