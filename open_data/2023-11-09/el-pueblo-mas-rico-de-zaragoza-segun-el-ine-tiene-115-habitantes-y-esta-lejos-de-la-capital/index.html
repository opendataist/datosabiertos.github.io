El pueblo más rico de Zaragoza, según el INE, tiene 115 habitantes y está lejos de la capital

The Income Distribution Atlas published by Spain’s National Statistics Institute (INE) reveals that El Frago is the wealthiest municipality in the province of Zaragoza on a per capita basis, significantly surpassing the provincial capital. This finding contrasts with the traditional perception that metropolitan areas concentrate the greatest wealth, underscoring the importance of analyzing disaggregated data at the municipal level to understand the region’s economic reality. The INE’s methodology, based on income tax returns, provides a detailed view of the distribution of well-being that goes beyond provincial or national averages. By normalizing income per household member, the statistics enable comparisons of living standards across localities of different sizes, offering an essential tool for assessing territorial equity and uncovering disparities hidden within macroeconomic aggregates. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of publishing detailed, standardized, and publicly accessible datasets. When government information is clear and accessible, it enables citizens, researchers, and the media to verify social claims, promote transparency, and make informed decisions about local development, thereby transforming administrative figures into useful and actionable public knowledge.
