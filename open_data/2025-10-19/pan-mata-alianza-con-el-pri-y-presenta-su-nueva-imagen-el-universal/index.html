PAN mata alianza con el PRI y presenta su nueva imagen | El Universal

PAN mata alianza con el PRI y presenta su nueva imagen | El Universal

The National Action Party (PAN) faces an existential crossroads due to the risk of losing its registration as a national party because of an insufficient base of affiliated members. In response to this crisis, the party leadership has announced the end of strategic alliances with the Institutional Revolutionary Party (PRI), which are considered responsible for electoral setbacks and the loss of party identity. This decision aims to reconnect with citizens and restore trust, marking a shift toward greater autonomy in candidate selection by party members and opening the door to citizen profiles aligned with the party’s principles. To ensure its survival and competitiveness in upcoming elections, the PAN will implement deep structural reforms. These include the digitalization of membership processes and the adoption of a new logo that projects modernity and closeness to the public. The central objective is to transform the organization into a democratic and transparent space capable of attracting new members and demonstrating that it offers a viable and responsible political alternative within the current Mexican context. This development is relevant to open data because it highlights the urgent need for transparency in political data. The crisis surrounding the electoral registry underscores how the availability and quality of membership data directly affect democratic legitimacy and the legal registration of political organizations. By digitalizing and simplifying these processes, the PAN promotes a model in which citizen data are more accessible and verifiable, fostering accountability and informed participation in the public sphere.
