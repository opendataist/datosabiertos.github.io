Shocking New IRS Targeting of Christians Uncovered – 26,000 Pages of IRS Documents About the "Word of God," 50,000 About "Prayer" | American Center for Law and Justice

The article alleges that the Biden administration is systematically targeting conservative and Christian groups through the FBI and IRS, mirroring past controversies like the Tea Party targeting. It claims the FBI has created a new category of extremists to monitor MAGA supporters under the guise of preventing domestic terrorism and political agendas. This narrative suggests a coordinated effort to suppress religious liberty and political opposition, framing these actions as unlawful violations of constitutional rights rather than legitimate law enforcement or tax administration. The American Center for Law and Justice (ACLJ) highlights its legal battles against the IRS, arguing that the agency is denying tax-exempt status to Christian organizations based on religious terminology. By citing specific litigation efforts, the piece portrays the government’s refusal to disclose documents related to these decisions as an attempt to hide evidence of bias. The authors contend that the sheer volume of records indicates a widespread practice of identifying and penalizing groups with religious identifiers, thereby undermining the separation of church and state and protecting religious expression. This report is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of freedom of information requests in exposing government bias. The ACLJ’s use of FOIA lawsuits to uncover internal documents demonstrates how transparency mechanisms can reveal hidden administrative practices that might otherwise remain opaque. By forcing the disclosure of records, open data initiatives can hold agencies accountable, ensuring that tax and law enforcement powers are not weaponized against specific religious or political demographics, thus preserving democratic integrity.
