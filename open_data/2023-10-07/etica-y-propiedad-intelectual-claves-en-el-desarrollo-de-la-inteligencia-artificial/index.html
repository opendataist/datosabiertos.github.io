Ética y propiedad intelectual, claves en el desarrollo de la Inteligencia Artificial

Ética y propiedad intelectual, claves en el desarrollo de la Inteligencia Artificial

The panelists concluded that the current moment is optimal for legislating artificial intelligence to establish a solid ethical foundation for the future. They emphasized that ethical principles must be embedded through external legislative frameworks and education rather than being hard-coded into the tools themselves. This approach is necessary to ensure that technology serves humanity without compromising the rights of creators or the integrity of the creative process. A central focus of the discussion was the imperative to protect intellectual property rights amid the rapid advancement of generative AI. The speakers argued that while AI can currently replicate text, it lacks true reasoning or creativity. Therefore, robust legal and technological measures, such as content securitization, are essential to prevent illegal usage and safeguard the economic and moral rights of authors and editors, ensuring that support for creators translates directly into sustained societal creativity. This dialogue is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical tension between data accessibility for machine learning and the legal protection of copyrighted works. As open data initiatives increasingly rely on scraping and using large datasets to train AI models, understanding the boundaries of fair use and intellectual property becomes vital. The article underscores that a sustainable open data ecosystem must respect legal frameworks, ensuring that the democratization of information does not inadvertently undermine the value and security of original creative content.
