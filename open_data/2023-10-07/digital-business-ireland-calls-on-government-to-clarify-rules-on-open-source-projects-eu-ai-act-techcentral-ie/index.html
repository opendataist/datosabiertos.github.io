Digital Business Ireland calls on government to clarify rules on open source projects EU AI Act - TechCentral.ie

Digital Business Ireland urges the government to prioritize investment in open-source AI and establish clear regulatory frameworks via the EU AI Act. The organization warns that imposing complex compliance obligations on commercial open-source applications could severely discourage investors and stifle startup growth, potentially hindering the sector's development. This appeal highlights the importance of balancing regulation with innovation to maintain a competitive edge, drawing parallels to France’s successful funding strategies. By advocating for a supportive environment, the group aims to prevent regulatory barriers that might otherwise deter capital from entering the open-source space. This issue is crucial for open data as open-source AI often relies on accessible data structures. Clear, workable rules ensure that data-driven innovations can thrive without being stifled by unwieldy compliance costs, fostering a healthier ecosystem for both technology development and data utilization.
