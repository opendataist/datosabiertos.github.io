La tasa de desempleo en EU se mantiene en un 3.8 % en septiembre | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The U.S. labor market showed unexpected resilience in September, maintaining a stable unemployment rate while creating significantly more jobs than expected. This strong performance underscores the economy’s capacity to withstand recent interest rate hikes and persistent inflationary pressures, signaling underlying strength in key sectors such as healthcare and professional services. This trend reinforces the narrative of steady economic expansion, particularly following modest growth in the previous quarter. The data suggest that the Federal Reserve’s pause in rate adjustments is achieving its intended goal without destabilizing employment, providing a clear picture of the current macroeconomic balance between cost control and job creation. These official statistics are crucial for open data initiatives, as they offer transparent, high-quality metrics for public scrutiny and analysis. By making such precise labor and economic indicators freely available, researchers and citizens can better evaluate the effectiveness of government policies, fostering informed debate and enhancing accountability in public-sector decision-making regarding monetary and fiscal strategies.
