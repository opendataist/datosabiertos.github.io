Censo 2024: INE activará reclutamiento de 650.000 censistas voluntarios

The National Institute of Statistics is launching a large-scale voluntary recruitment campaign to staff the upcoming National Population and Housing Census, emphasizing digital accessibility to engage students, educators, and the general public. This initiative aims to secure sufficient personnel by anticipating potential withdrawals through a strategic buffer in hiring numbers. A key innovation is the integration of census training into the national curriculum, which familiarizes students and teachers with data collection procedures before the official census begins. This educational component ensures that future census workers are technically prepared and understand the importance of accurate demographic data for national planning. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical human infrastructure required to generate high-quality, reliable public datasets. The success of the census directly impacts the integrity of future open data initiatives, as accurate population records are foundational for transparent governance and evidence-based policy making in Bolivia.
