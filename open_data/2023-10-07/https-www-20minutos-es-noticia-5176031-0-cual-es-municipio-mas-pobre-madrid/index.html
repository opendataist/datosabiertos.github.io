This INE study highlights significant economic disparities among Madrid’s municipalities, revealing that proximity to the capital and northern location correlate with higher household incomes. These geospatial variations create a clear wealth gradient, emphasizing how local context directly influences economic well-being. Such granular, public data is crucial for open data initiatives as it enables researchers and policymakers to identify structural inequalities and target interventions effectively. By making detailed income distributions accessible, these datasets foster transparency and support evidence-based decision-making regarding regional development and social equity.
