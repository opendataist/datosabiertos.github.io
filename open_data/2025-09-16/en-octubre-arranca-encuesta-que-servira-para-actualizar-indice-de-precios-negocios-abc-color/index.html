En octubre arranca encuesta que servirá para actualizar índice de precios - Negocios - ABC Color

En octubre arranca encuesta que servirá para actualizar índice de precios - Negocios - ABC Color

The National Statistics Institute is preparing a comprehensive household budget survey to update the Consumer Price Index, addressing widespread public skepticism that current inflation metrics do not reflect the realities of families. By conducting a year-long fieldwork phase in Asunción and Central departments, the initiative aims to collect accurate consumption data through rigorous protocols. This effort marks a significant shift toward greater statistical transparency, as the basket of goods will be updated after a decade to remove obsolete items and incorporate modern essentials. The process involves hiring specialized personnel to ensure precise monitoring of household spending patterns, thereby enhancing the reliability and representativeness of national economic indicators. This case is crucial for open data advocates because it underscores the need to align official statistics with citizens’ actual experiences. It demonstrates how transparent, methodologically sound data collection can restore trust in public institutions and ensure that economic policies are based on realistic, accessible, and up-to-date information rather than outdated assumptions.
