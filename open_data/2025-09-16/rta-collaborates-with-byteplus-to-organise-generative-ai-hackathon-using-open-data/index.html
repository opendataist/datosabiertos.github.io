RTA collaborates with BytePlus to organise Generative AI Hackathon using Open Data

RTA collaborates with BytePlus to organise Generative AI Hackathon using Open Data

Dubai’s RTA hosted a hackathon uniting students and industry experts to create AI-driven mobility tools using open transport data. This initiative demonstrates how publicly accessible information can fuel innovation in critical urban infrastructure sectors. The competition selected top teams for future collaboration with RTA, highlighting the practical value of open data in developing scalable, real-world solutions for complex transportation challenges. By fostering partnerships between academia and government agencies, this event underscores open data’s role in accelerating technological advancement and improving public service delivery through collaborative, data-centric problem-solving.
