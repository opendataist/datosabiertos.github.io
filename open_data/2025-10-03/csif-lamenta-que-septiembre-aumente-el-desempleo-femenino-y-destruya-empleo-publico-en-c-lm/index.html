CSIF lamenta que septiembre aumente el desempleo femenino y destruya empleo público en C-LM

The article highlights severe gender disparities in the regional labor market, where women face significantly higher unemployment rates and more precarious employment conditions compared to men. This structural inequality is exacerbated by the disproportionate burden of care responsibilities, which leads to reduced working hours and temporary contracts that hinder professional equality and work-life balance. These labor dynamics are critically relevant to open data initiatives, as they underscore the need for transparent, granular datasets on employment, gender, and caregiving. Without accessible and detailed statistics, policymakers and researchers cannot effectively analyze intersectional inequalities or design targeted interventions to address the systemic barriers faced by women in the workforce.
