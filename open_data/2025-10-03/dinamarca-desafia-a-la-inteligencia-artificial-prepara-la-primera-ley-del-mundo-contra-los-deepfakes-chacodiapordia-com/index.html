Dinamarca desafía a la Inteligencia Artificial: prepara la primera ley del mundo contra los deepfakes - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

Dinamarca desafía a la Inteligencia Artificial: prepara la primera ley del mundo contra los deepfakes - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

Denmark is spearheading a pioneering reform that grants legal rights over digital images, voice, and facial features, enabling citizens to demand the removal of manipulated content created without their consent. This measure aims to curb the proliferation of deepfakes and protect individuals’ identities against the unauthorized use of generative artificial intelligence, positioning the country as a global leader in defending digital integrity. The relevance to open data lies in the need to balance information accessibility with the protection of fundamental rights. By establishing clear limits on the use of personal data in the creation of artificial content, this legislation promotes transparency and accountability in data handling. This is crucial to ensure that open data and emerging technologies respect privacy and individual autonomy, preventing technological innovation from undermining civil liberties. If approved, this model could serve as a global reference and be harmonized with broader European regulations. The initiative marks a paradigm shift toward a robust legal framework that prioritizes user security over regulatory gaps. Its implementation will require platforms to develop detection and verification systems, fostering a safer and more ethical digital ecosystem where access to information does not compromise individuals’ basic rights.
