Fiscalía de Veracruz cerró 2024 con 20% de carpetas de investigación sin concluir: INEGI

The article reveals that nearly 20% of Veracruz’s investigative files remained open at the end of 2024, highlighting significant inefficiencies in the local justice system. This backlog indicates that while many cases are initially closed, a substantial portion stalls during early investigations, reflecting systemic bottlenecks in federal and state prosecutorial offices. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates the importance of accessible, standardized public statistics in monitoring judicial performance. By publishing detailed census results, INEGI enables civic oversight, allowing society to identify transparency gaps and advocate for reforms in legal processes.
