Personal del INEGI se reúne con personal de Presidencia Municipal de Madero

Personal del INEGI se reúne con personal de Presidencia Municipal de Madero

The INEGI is launching its 2025 Intercensal Survey to update critical sociodemographic data across Mexican municipalities. This biennial census captures vital statistics on population distribution and housing conditions through various collection methods. These comprehensive data points directly inform the design of targeted public policies and social programs. By understanding how people live and where they reside, governments can allocate resources more effectively to meet community needs. This effort is central to open data principles, as it ensures transparent, accessible, and reliable statistics. Such high-quality information empowers citizens and researchers to analyze societal trends and advocate for evidence-based governance improvements.
