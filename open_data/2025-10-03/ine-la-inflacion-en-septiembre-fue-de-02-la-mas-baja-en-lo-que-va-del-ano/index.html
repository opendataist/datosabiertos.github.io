INE: La inflación en septiembre fue de 0,2%, la más baja en lo que va del año

The article highlights that Bolivia’s inflation has decreased for three consecutive months, reaching its lowest point in the year, primarily due to the stabilization of supply chains and a declining parallel dollar exchange rate. This reduction suggests that imported goods, such as healthcare products, may soon become cheaper, potentially leading to negative inflation in the coming months. This data is crucial for open_data as it demonstrates how public statistics track economic resilience against market distortions. Transparent reporting on these trends empowers citizens and researchers to analyze the real impact of currency fluctuations on purchasing power. Furthermore, it underscores the necessity of accessible, high-quality data for evaluating government policies on price control and market regulation, ensuring that public discourse relies on accurate, verifiable economic indicators rather than speculation.
