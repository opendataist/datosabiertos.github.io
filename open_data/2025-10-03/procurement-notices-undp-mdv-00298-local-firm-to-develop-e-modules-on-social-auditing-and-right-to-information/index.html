Procurement Notices - UNDP-MDV-00298 - Local firm to develop e-modules on social auditing and right to information

Procurement Notices - UNDP-MDV-00298 - Local firm to develop e-modules on social auditing and right to information

This tender highlights the collaborative effort by UNDP, ACC, and ICO to enhance transparency through e-learning modules on social auditing and the right to information. By hosting these resources on a government-managed portal, the initiative seeks to empower local stakeholders with critical knowledge for holding authorities accountable. This development is vital to open data because it democratizes access to essential civic tools. Providing digital education ensures that citizens and local officials can effectively utilize information rights, thereby fostering a more transparent and engaged society. Ultimately, expanding digital capacity building supports the broader goals of open governance. It transforms abstract rights into practical skills, enabling communities to actively participate in oversight and improve local service delivery through informed engagement.
