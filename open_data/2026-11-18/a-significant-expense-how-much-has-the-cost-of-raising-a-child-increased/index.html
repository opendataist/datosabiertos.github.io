A significant expense: How much has the cost of raising a child increased?

A significant expense: How much has the cost of raising a child increased?

The publication reflects the evolution of the real cost of raising children, demonstrating that this cost significantly outpaces general inflation. This phenomenon underscores the urgency of having accurate indicators that enable families and the state to understand current economic pressures. The analysis breaks down these expenses into essential goods and the economic value of caregiving time. By monetizing care tasks, the unpaid labor burden becomes visible, offering a comprehensive perspective on the needs of child development. This information is crucial for the field of open data, as it provides transparency in social justice metrics. Serving as a reference for child support litigation, it ensures that judicial decisions are based on public, verifiable, and up-to-date information, thereby fostering accountability.
