INDEC revealed that wholesale prices rose 1.6% in October, and Caputo celebrated on social media – 4SEMANAS | Latest news from Argentina and the world

The National Institute of Statistics and Census (INDEC) revealed that the wholesale price index showed a significant slowdown, consolidating a trend of moderation in the inflation of production and marketing costs. This finding is crucial for open data, as it ensures the availability and quality of transparent official statistics that enable citizens and civil society to monitor the real state of the economy, beyond political rhetoric. The slowdown is evident both in the monthly performance and in the annual cumulative figures, with domestic products leading the adjustments compared to imported ones. The existence of these detailed and accessible data facilitates the creation of independent visualization and analysis tools, empowering citizens with verifiable information on price trends and their impact on everyday economic life. This statistical transparency strengthens accountability, allowing official statements to be contrasted with numerical reality. By opening these datasets, a more informed democracy is fostered, where users can apply their own analyses to validate public policies, ensuring that economic management is based on verifiable evidence rather than one-sided narratives.
