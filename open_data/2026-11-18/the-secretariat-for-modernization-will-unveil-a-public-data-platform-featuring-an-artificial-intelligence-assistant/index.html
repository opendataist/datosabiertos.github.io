The Secretariat for Modernization will unveil a public data platform featuring an artificial intelligence assistant

The launch of "Smart Public Data" marks a significant step forward in enhancing transparency and modernizing the State. The platform centralizes key information and leverages artificial intelligence to facilitate citizen access. This effort democratizes knowledge by organizing essential data by topic. Progressive updates ensure that the information remains relevant and useful. The initiative is crucial for the open data movement, as it promotes the effective opening of public resources. It complies with legal frameworks for access to information, strengthening accountability.
