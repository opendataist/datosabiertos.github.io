Conducta Vial Quálitas marks 12 years promoting road safety in Mexico

Conducta Vial Quálitas marks 12 years promoting road safety in Mexico

Road safety in Mexico continues to depend critically on the human factor, as evidenced by the high percentage of accidents caused by avoidable behaviors. Despite technological advances in vehicles, prevention remains the central pillar for reducing accident rates, since individual decisions made when sharing the roads have a direct and lasting impact on the protection of life. The adopted strategy combines mass education with the use of technical data to modify driving habits. By integrating telematics technology and targeted training for fleets and diverse users, the aim is to shift from reactive responses to accidents toward proactive management based on behavioral analysis. This approach recognizes the shared responsibility of all road users, from pedestrians to professional operators, emphasizing that safety is the result of everyday decisions and not solely of infrastructure. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how access to and analysis of traffic and telematics data enable the design of more effective road safety policies. Democratizing information about risks and unsafe behaviors empowers both society and institutions to make informed decisions, demonstrating that data transparency is essential for fostering a prevention culture grounded in real evidence rather than mere intuition.
