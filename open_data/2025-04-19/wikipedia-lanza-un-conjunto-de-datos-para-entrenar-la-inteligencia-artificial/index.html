Wikipedia lanza un conjunto de datos para entrenar la inteligencia artificial

The Wikimedia Foundation is addressing server strain caused by AI scraping by releasing structured Wikipedia datasets for machine learning training. This proactive measure reduces infrastructure costs while providing high-quality, machine-readable data in English and French. The initiative supports researchers in developing natural language processing models by offering accessible resources. This step is crucial for open data, as it balances content accessibility with sustainability. By providing structured data, Wikimedia facilitates ethical AI development without compromising site performance. It demonstrates how institutions can manage automated access while promoting the reuse of public knowledge, ensuring that open data remains available for innovation and educational purposes.
