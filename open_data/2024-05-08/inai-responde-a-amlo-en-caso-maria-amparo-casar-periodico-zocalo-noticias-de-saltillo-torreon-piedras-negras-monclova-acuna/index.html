Inai responde a AMLO en caso María Amparo Casar | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The INAI has launched an investigation into President López Obrador for allegedly violating personal data privacy laws by disclosing María Amparo Casar’s information during a discussion on corruption. This action underscores the critical distinction between the public interest in transparency and the legal obligation to protect individual confidentiality, even when addressing misconduct. The inquiry aims to balance these competing values, determining whether the public interest outweighs the need for data protection in specific contexts. This process involves rigorous verification stages and potential legal consequences for public servants, highlighting that transparency mandates do not automatically justify exposing private details without careful legal scrutiny. This case is highly relevant to open data because it establishes precedents for how government transparency initiatives must coexist with privacy rights. It warns organizations that releasing data, even for anti-corruption purposes, requires strict adherence to legal frameworks to avoid infringing on personal data protection, ensuring a responsible approach to information disclosure.
