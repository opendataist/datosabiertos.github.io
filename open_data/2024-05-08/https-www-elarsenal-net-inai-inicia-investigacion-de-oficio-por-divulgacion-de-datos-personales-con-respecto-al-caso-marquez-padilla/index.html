The INAI is investigating the Presidential Office for the improper publication of personal data in the Márquez Padilla case. This underscores the critical importance of strict compliance with data protection laws by government entities to safeguard citizens’ privacy rights. Such enforcement actions highlight the need for transparency agencies to rigorously monitor open data practices. Protecting sensitive information while ensuring public access remains a delicate balance essential for maintaining trust in open government initiatives. This situation demonstrates that the release of open data must strictly adhere to legal frameworks. It serves as a vital reminder for all public institutions to prioritize data privacy, ensuring that transparency efforts do not inadvertently violate individual rights or expose vulnerable populations to unnecessary risks.
