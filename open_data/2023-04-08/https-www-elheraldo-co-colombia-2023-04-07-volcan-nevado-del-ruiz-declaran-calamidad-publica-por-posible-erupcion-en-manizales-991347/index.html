Manizales has declared a state of public calamity to expedite resource allocation and procurement in anticipation of a potential eruption of Nevado del Ruiz. This urgent measure aims to streamline emergency response protocols, enabling municipal entities to bypass standard bureaucratic delays and immediately secure goods or services when crisis conditions arise. Alongside these administrative preparations, authorities are conducting rapid censuses and georeferencing populations in vulnerable river basins. By mapping these communities in advance, the city ensures that evacuation plans and aid distribution are precise, thereby reducing risks for residents living near the volcano’s potential impact zones. This situation underscores the critical role of open data in disaster management. Transparent, accessible geographic and demographic datasets enable efficient risk reduction strategies. When public data is open, authorities can implement proactive, evidence-based interventions that save lives and resources during natural disasters.
