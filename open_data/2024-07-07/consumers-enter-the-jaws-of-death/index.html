Consumers enter the "jaws of death"

Household spending has stagnated, revealing a significant real per capita decline that squeezes living standards. This erosion of consumer purchasing power, driven by inflation outpacing income growth, signals a deepening economic recession. The weakening consumption data suggests that further interest rate hikes by the Reserve Bank of Australia are unlikely. Policymakers are likely to maintain current rates as they monitor for signs of labor market firming and inflation stabilization, prioritizing economic stability over aggressive monetary tightening. This case highlights open data’s critical role in economic analysis. By making high-frequency spending metrics like the Household Spending Indicator publicly available, transparency allows experts to instantly assess consumer health, predict monetary policy shifts, and guide informed decision-making across markets and governments.
