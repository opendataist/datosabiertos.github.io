El INE confirma que la inflación se moderó dos décimas en junio y mantiene el dato en el 3,4%

El INE confirma que la inflación se moderó dos décimas en junio y mantiene el dato en el 3,4%

The National Statistics Institute reports that Spanish inflation moderated to 3.4% in June, driven primarily by significant price drops in the transport and food sectors. This decline reflects improved economic stability, allowing the country to maintain higher growth while simultaneously curbing price increases. The data suggests that current policy measures are effectively contributing to a sustainable reduction in consumer costs. The government’s decision to extend the zero percent VAT on basic foods through September highlights a strategic effort to stabilize essential goods. This policy intervention not only provides immediate relief but also ensures that critical items, such as olive oil, remain affordable after temporary tax breaks expire. Such targeted fiscal adjustments demonstrate how public policy can directly influence market dynamics to protect purchasing power. This analysis is relevant to open data because transparent, high-quality statistical releases enable researchers and citizens to verify economic trends and policy impacts. By making granular inflation data publicly accessible, institutions foster accountability and allow for independent analysis of how specific measures, like VAT changes, correlate with broader economic indicators, thereby enhancing democratic oversight of public spending.
