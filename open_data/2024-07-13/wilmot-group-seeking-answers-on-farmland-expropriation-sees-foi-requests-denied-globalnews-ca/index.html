Wilmot group seeking answers on farmland expropriation sees FOI requests denied | Globalnews.ca

The article highlights a significant transparency issue where most freedom of information requests regarding land expropriation were denied, raising concerns about the withholding of tax-funded research. This situation underscores the critical role of open data in ensuring government accountability and public access to vital infrastructure studies. Advocates argue that such secrecy undermines the public interest, particularly when seeking details on environmental impacts and alternative sites for industrial projects. The refusal to disclose this information suggests a potential gap in how open data principles are applied to local governance and development planning. Regional authorities maintain that confidentiality is standard in real estate negotiations to protect privacy and facilitate professional dealings. However, this stance conflicts with the growing demand for transparent, accessible data, emphasizing the need for clearer standards on when public interest outweighs negotiation secrecy.
