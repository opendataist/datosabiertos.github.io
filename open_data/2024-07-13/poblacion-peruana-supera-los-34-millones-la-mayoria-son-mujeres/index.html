Población peruana supera los 34 millones: La mayoría son mujeres

The INEI report reveals Peru’s shifting demographics, marked by population aging and significant ethnic diversity. This data highlights a transition toward an older society, impacting social structures and resource needs. The findings underscore the importance of accessible government statistics in understanding societal changes. By tracking these trends, policymakers can better address the needs of an aging population and diverse ethnic groups. For open data, this emphasizes the value of transparent, detailed demographic records. Accurate information enables evidence-based decision-making, ensuring public services adapt effectively to evolving national characteristics and demographic realities.
