Australian Government Data Catalogue launches

The Australian Government Data Catalogue centralizes nearly 60,000 public datasets, enhancing open data accessibility through direct access and regulated requests. This initiative supports transparency by enabling users to view and apply for protected data under specific legal frameworks. It represents a significant step toward a unified, secure, and comprehensive national open data infrastructure.
