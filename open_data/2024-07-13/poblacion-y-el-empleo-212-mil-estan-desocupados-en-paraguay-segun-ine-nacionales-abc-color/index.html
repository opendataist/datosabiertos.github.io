Población y el empleo: 212 mil están desocupados en Paraguay, según INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

The article reveals significant labor market disparities, highlighting that unemployment disproportionately affects women due to unequal domestic responsibilities. It also identifies a substantial demographic of young people who neither study nor work, driven by economic pressures that force them into low-income activities early in life. These trends underscore deep structural inequalities in access to stable employment and education. Income gaps persist between genders, although women’s earnings are improving thanks to increased workforce participation and professional empowerment. This shift reflects changing social dynamics, where couples delay childbearing to prioritize career development and skill acquisition. The data illustrates a workforce struggling with underemployment and limited opportunities for upward mobility. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how statistical agencies collect and disseminate critical socioeconomic information. Transparent access to such datasets allows researchers and policymakers to identify vulnerable groups and design evidence-based interventions. By making employment and income statistics publicly available, governments can foster accountability and drive informed decisions aimed at reducing inequality and improving labor outcomes for marginalized populations.
