La inflación interanual en Argentina se ubicó en 271,5 % en junio | RPP Noticias

The official report highlights a significant slowdown in Argentina’s monthly inflation rate, suggesting that the indicator may have stabilized its pace of increase after periods of extreme volatility. This trend indicates that price pressures have found a more predictable baseline, which is crucial for economic planning and market confidence, although the cumulative increase over the semester remains considerably high. The structure of inflation reveals that services, particularly housing and education, experienced sharper increases than goods and food. This differentiation is vital because, although the government points to a reduction in food costs to protect vulnerable sectors, the impact on essential services continues to pressure the population’s purchasing power, disproportionately affecting middle- and lower-income classes. This analysis is relevant to open data because the transparent publication of detailed Consumer Price Index (CPI) data enables civil society, academics, and the media to verify the quality of official statistics. Access to this disaggregated information by category and period fosters public scrutiny, improves government accountability, and facilitates the development of independent analytical tools that contribute to a more accurate understanding of economic reality.
