El IPC se modera al 3,4% en junio por el abaratamiento de los carburantes y el aceite

El IPC se modera al 3,4% en junio por el abaratamiento de los carburantes y el aceite

The final data from Spain’s National Statistics Institute (INE) confirm a significant moderation in inflation, marking a turning point after three consecutive months of increases. Prices for food and beverages recorded their lowest annual growth in two and a half years, driven by declines in specific categories such as oils and fats, while energy prices also contributed to the overall decrease. This decline signals a potential stabilization in consumer price pressures, offering relief to households and businesses grappling with high costs. On a monthly basis, however, prices continued to rise, owing to increases in tourism packages, electricity, and accommodation services. This divergence between annual and monthly trends underscores the complex dynamics of the economy, where temporary shocks in specific sectors can obscure broader underlying trends. The underlying inflation rate remained stable, indicating that core price pressures are not accelerating—a key consideration for monetary policy. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, official statistical releases enable independent verification and more in-depth economic analysis. By providing accessible, granular data on regional variations and sectoral trends, the INE allows researchers, journalists, and citizens to go beyond headline figures. This transparency fosters accountability and helps the public grasp the nuanced reality of inflation, rather than relying on oversimplified narratives.
