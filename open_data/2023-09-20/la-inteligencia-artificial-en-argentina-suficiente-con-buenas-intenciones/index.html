La Inteligencia Artificial en Argentina: ¿suficiente con buenas intenciones?

La Inteligencia Artificial en Argentina: ¿suficiente con buenas intenciones?

The article argues that while Argentina has established a regulatory framework for AI transparency and data protection, intentions alone are insufficient without concrete management and investment. The central conclusion is that a mere resolution cannot mitigate the ethical and technological risks posed by artificial intelligence; instead, robust programs and active governance are essential to ensure safe and responsible usage. The narrative highlights the gap between formal standards and practical implementation. Although international principles guide the creation of secure and fair AI systems, the current approach lacks the necessary operational mechanisms. Without effective supervision and strategic action, these regulations risk becoming empty shells that fail to protect citizens amidst the rapid evolution of algorithmic capabilities and biometric technologies. This content is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical intersection between algorithmic transparency and public accountability. For open data initiatives to succeed in an AI-driven environment, they must evolve beyond simple data publication to include rigorous governance structures. The article emphasizes that protecting personal data and ensuring fairness in AI models are prerequisites for maintaining public trust and the integrity of digital ecosystems.
