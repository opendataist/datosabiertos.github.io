Here's a list of August inflation rates for selected Canadian cities

Statistics Canada’s August inflation data highlights significant variability across Canadian cities. These metropolitan estimates rely on small statistical samples, introducing potential fluctuations that limit their reliability for precise local economic analysis. This reporting underscores critical open data challenges regarding sample size and data granularity. When sub-national metrics are derived from limited samples, the resulting transparency is compromised, hindering accurate public understanding and granular policy decisions. Relevant to open data, this case illustrates the necessity of publishing underlying sample sizes and confidence intervals. Without such methodological transparency, users cannot adequately assess data quality, potentially leading to misinformed interpretations of regional economic conditions.
