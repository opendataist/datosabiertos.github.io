Transparentar servidores sin experiencia: INAI

The INAI ruled that SENEAM must disclose the names of public servants working as air traffic controllers without proper training. This decision underscores that aviation safety relies heavily on the professionalism and specific expertise of controllers, whose precise instructions directly impact passenger lives. The agency’s initial claim that no such personnel existed was deemed unfounded due to an inadequate search procedure. Relevance to open data lies in the enforcement of transparency as a mechanism for accountability in public safety. By mandating an exhaustive search, the ruling ensures that critical information regarding institutional irregularities is accessible. This highlights how data requests can uncover hidden administrative failures and compel organizations to maintain accurate, verifiable records of their workforce qualifications. Ultimately, this case demonstrates the vital role of transparency institutions in verifying official statements against internal audits. It reinforces that citizens’ right to know extends to identifying safety risks within essential services. The decision serves as a precedent for using open data to ensure that public positions are filled by qualified individuals, thereby protecting the public interest and maintaining trust in governmental operations.
