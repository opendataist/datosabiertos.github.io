Olongapo City hosts 1st leg of Transparency Roadshow in Central Luzon - Punto! Central Luzon

The Presidential Communications Office recently held a Transparency Roadshow in Olongapo City, engaging local officials, civil society, and media to promote the Freedom of Information program. The initiative underscores the constitutional right to information, framing access to government data as a democratic cornerstone that empowers citizens and ensures public accountability. Key government agencies showcased practical transparency tools, including geographic information systems and health workforce databases. These technological solutions aim to open up previously restricted data, thereby rebuilding public trust by demonstrating a commitment to open governance and making critical public services more visible and accessible to the community. This event is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical transition from legal frameworks to practical implementation. By encouraging local institutions to adopt open data practices and use digital tools for transparency, the government fosters an environment where citizens can actively participate in governance through informed access to public information.
