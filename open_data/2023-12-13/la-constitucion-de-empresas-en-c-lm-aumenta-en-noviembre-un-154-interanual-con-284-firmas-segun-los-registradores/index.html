La constitución de empresas en C-LM aumenta en noviembre un 15,4% interanual, con 284 firmas, según los registradores

The article highlights a nuanced recovery in Spain’s business landscape, characterized by strong regional disparities. Key autonomous communities such as Madrid, Catalonia, and Andalusia continue to dominate new company formations, driving national growth. At the same time, capital expansion remains generally stable despite some localized declines, indicating a moderate but resilient economic environment across various sectors and regions. Conversely, the data reveal a significant correction in insolvency rates. The end of the pandemic-era moratorium has led to a substantial nationwide decrease in creditor proceedings compared to the previous year. This trend suggests that the artificial inflation of bankruptcies during the emergency period has dissipated, allowing the market to stabilize. Although figures remain higher than pre-pandemic levels, the accelerating decline points toward a normalization of corporate distress metrics. This report is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the necessity of granular, regionalized statistical tracking for accurate economic analysis. By moving beyond aggregate national figures, open datasets can expose hidden regional inequalities and the true impact of policy changes, such as the ending of moratoria. Such detailed transparency enables researchers and policymakers to identify specific areas of growth or distress, fostering evidence-based decisions and enhancing the societal value of public registry data.
