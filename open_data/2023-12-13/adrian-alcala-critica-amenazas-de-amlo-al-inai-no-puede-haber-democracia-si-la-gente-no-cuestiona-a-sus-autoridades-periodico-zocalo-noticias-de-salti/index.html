Adrián Alcalá critica amenazas de AMLO al INAI: ‘No puede haber democracia si la gente no cuestiona a sus autoridades’ | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Adrián Alcalá states that his appointment to head the INAI reflects a genuine democratic process marked by pluralism and respect for institutional procedures. He stresses that autonomous bodies are essential for safeguarding the right to hold authorities accountable, asserting that a true rule of law cannot exist without mechanisms that ensure public accountability and transparency. Beyond its oversight functions, the institute plays a crucial role in empowering citizens by facilitating individual access to essential services and personal data. This includes practical assistance in obtaining medical records or social security information, demonstrating that open data mechanisms directly improve quality of life and administrative efficiency for ordinary people. The article highlights the ongoing threat posed by legislative initiatives aimed at dismantling autonomous institutions. By calling for societal unity, the INAI underscores the importance of open data as a fundamental pillar of democracy, protecting privacy rights and ensuring that information remains accessible and secure against political efforts to weaken institutional independence.
