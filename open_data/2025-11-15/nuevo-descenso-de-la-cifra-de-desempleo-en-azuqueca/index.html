Nuevo descenso de la cifra de desempleo en Azuqueca

Azuqueca de Henares has reached its lowest unemployment rate in eighteen years, signaling a significant improvement in the local job market. This decline reflects a positive trend in which a substantial number of residents have successfully re-entered the workforce over the past year. The local government actively supports this progress through targeted training programs and temporary contracts, enhancing employability despite limited direct competencies. By focusing on qualification and administrative services, the municipality helps residents adapt to current economic demands and improve their professional skills. This story highlights the critical value of open data in monitoring public health and social welfare. Transparent employment statistics allow citizens and officials to track progress, evaluate policy effectiveness, and ensure that municipal initiatives are genuinely driving inclusive economic recovery.
