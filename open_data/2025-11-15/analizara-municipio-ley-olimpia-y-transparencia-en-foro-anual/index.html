Analizará Municipio Ley Olimpia y Transparencia en Foro Anual

The forum addresses municipal reform through the lens of digital rights and open government. It highlights proactive transparency practices and the impact of privacy laws on personal data protection. This event is relevant to open data as it fosters knowledge exchange among officials and citizens. By focusing on responsible information management, it strengthens local governance frameworks and promotes accountability. Ultimately, it aims to consolidate best practices for handling public information. The initiative serves as a vital platform for reviewing advancements and generating proposals to enhance transparency and data ethics in local government.
