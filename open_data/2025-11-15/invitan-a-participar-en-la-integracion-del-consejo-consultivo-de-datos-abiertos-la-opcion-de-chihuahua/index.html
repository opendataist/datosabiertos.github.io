Invitan a participar en la integración del Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Invitan a participar en la integración del Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua is opening applications for its Open Data Advisory Council, inviting citizens to join this key body for civic participation. This initiative underscores the municipality’s commitment to transparency by empowering external experts to guide open government policies and data governance strategies. The selection process emphasizes merit and independence, excluding current officials to ensure impartial oversight. By establishing terms of varying lengths, the council aims to achieve stable, staggered renewal while promoting gender equity in decision-making roles. This opportunity is vital for open data advocates, as it directly influences how public information is managed and utilized. Participation allows citizens to shape accessible and accountable data practices, reinforcing the link between community engagement and effective digital governance.
