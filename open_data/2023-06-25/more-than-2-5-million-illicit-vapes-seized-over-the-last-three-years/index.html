More than 2.5 million illicit vapes seized over the last three years

The widespread presence of illicit e-cigarettes poses a significant threat to public health, particularly regarding the safety of young people. Investigations reveal that millions of non-compliant devices, potentially containing harmful ingredients or excessive nicotine, have been seized from the market. These illegal products undermine efforts to promote safer alternatives to traditional smoking, creating an unpredictable risk landscape for consumers who assume all vaping products are regulated. This surge in unregulated vapes contributes to a growing epidemic of youth vaping, with pediatricians warning of rising hospital admissions among children. The lack of transparency surrounding the composition of these illegal devices means their long-term health impacts remain unknown. Consequently, there is an urgent need to protect minors from addiction and potential harm, as the current market allows dangerous products to bypass legal safety standards. This situation is critically relevant to open data initiatives, as transparency is essential for effective regulatory enforcement and public awareness. The ability to access and analyze seizure data allows authorities to identify trends, target enforcement resources, and understand the scale of the illicit trade. Without open, accessible data, it becomes difficult to evaluate the effectiveness of interventions or to inform policy decisions that aim to reduce youth access and ensure product safety.
