Uso de Internet llegó al 76,3% de la población en el 2022 - Economía - ABC Color

Uso de Internet llegó al 76,3% de la población en el 2022 - Economía - ABC Color

The INE survey highlights that internet access remains unevenly distributed, significantly influenced by geographic and socioeconomic factors. Urban residents demonstrate substantially higher adoption rates compared to their rural counterparts, revealing a persistent digital divide linked to residential location. Demographic analysis further exposes gaps related to education and occupation, where higher academic attainment and professional status correlate with greater usage. Additionally, while gender differences are marginal, age plays a critical role, with younger cohorts showing far higher connectivity than older individuals or younger adolescents, indicating that digital inclusion is not yet universal across all population segments. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it provides granular, disaggregated statistics essential for identifying marginalized groups. By making such detailed indicators publicly available, researchers and policymakers can better target infrastructure investments and educational programs to bridge the remaining gaps in access and usage, ensuring more equitable digital development.
