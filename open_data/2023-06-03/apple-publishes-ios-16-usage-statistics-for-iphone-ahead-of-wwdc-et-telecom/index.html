Apple publishes iOS 16 usage statistics for iPhone ahead of WWDC - ET Telecom

Apple’s recent adoption statistics for iOS and iPadOS 16 highlight the rapid transition away from older operating systems, with the majority of active devices now on the latest software. This swift uptake suggests a healthy ecosystem where users are quickly upgrading to maintain compatibility and access new features, reducing fragmentation across the platform. The data underscores Apple’s ability to drive ecosystem cohesion, as nearly all devices released in the last four years are currently running the newest OS versions. This high rate of adoption is critical for developers, as it ensures a stable and uniform environment for creating applications without needing to support legacy code or outdated interfaces. This relevance to open data lies in the transparency of Apple’s usage metrics, which provide valuable insights into technology adoption trends. Such information helps the broader tech community understand market dynamics, user behavior, and the lifecycle of mobile operating systems, fostering a more informed approach to software development and digital strategy.
