Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

Deputies from the Civic Coalition propose the creation of an Open Data Portal for imports, with the primary objective of eliminating the discretion and corruption inherent in the current system. By requiring that all information regarding applications, amounts, products, and statuses be published digitally, updated in real time, and in open formats, the initiative seeks to ensure that citizens can freely access this data. This measure transforms license management from an opaque process to a transparent one, significantly reducing the risk of abuse by public officials. The initiative highlights that existing regulations lack clear criteria and fail to respect processing deadlines, thereby generating legal uncertainty and distorting foreign trade. By establishing simple rules and fixed deadlines, under which administrative inactivity implies automatic approval, the project aims to replace arbitrariness with predictability. This not only simplifies procedures for importers but also aligns local policy with international recommendations that emphasize the importance of transparent regulation to prevent corrupt practices and foster trust in institutions. This article is fundamental to the field of open data because it exemplifies how the proactive publication of public information can serve as a powerful tool for accountability and the fight against corruption. By demonstrating that transparency is not merely an administrative principle but an effective mechanism for limiting the state’s discretionary power, the project offers a replicable model. It reinforces the idea that free and easy access to regulatory information is essential to democratize oversight of economic policies and ensure that commercial operations are conducted under fair and verifiable standards.
