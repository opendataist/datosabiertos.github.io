Tasa de desempleo en México se ubica en 2.8% en abril; se perdieron 112 mil empleos

The article highlights a significant contraction in Mexico’s labor market, marked by substantial job losses despite temporary gains in formal sectors. This decline reveals structural weaknesses, particularly in industry and construction, contrasting with growth in agriculture and services. These findings underscore the critical importance of transparent and accurate labor statistics for understanding economic health. Reliable data enables policymakers and researchers to identify vulnerable sectors and design effective interventions, fostering evidence-based decision-making. For the open data community, this case illustrates how accessible government statistics empower public analysis. By making such information available, institutions support accountability and allow citizens to critically assess economic trends, promoting informed civic engagement and better governance.
