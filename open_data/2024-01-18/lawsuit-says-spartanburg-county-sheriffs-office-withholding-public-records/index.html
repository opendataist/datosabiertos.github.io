Lawsuit says Spartanburg County Sheriff’s Office withholding public records

A lawsuit challenges the Spartanburg County Sheriff’s Office for withholding incident reports from “Operation Rolling Thunder,” claiming selective transparency hides potentially abusive policing practices. This case highlights the critical tension between law enforcement discretion and public accountability under freedom of information laws. The dispute underscores how civil forfeiture policies can incentivize policing for profit, shifting focus from safety to revenue generation. By denying public records, the agency impedes oversight of these financial motivations, risking unjust enrichment and erosion of community trust. This situation is vital to open data because it demonstrates how withholding specific operational records prevents scrutiny of systemic issues. Transparent access to such data is essential to detect and address biases, ensuring that law enforcement actions remain equitable and subject to democratic accountability rather than hidden financial incentives.
