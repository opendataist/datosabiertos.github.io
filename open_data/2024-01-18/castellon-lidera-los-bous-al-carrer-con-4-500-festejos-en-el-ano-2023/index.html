Castellón lidera los 'bous al carrer' con 4.500 festejos en el año 2023

Castellón lidera los 'bous al carrer' con 4.500 festejos en el año 2023

The article reveals that the Valencian government recorded thousands of street bull-running events in 2023, with Castellón hosting the majority. This data highlights the cultural prevalence of these traditions across the region, occurring primarily during the summer months and involving various specific modalities such as vaquillas and bou de corro. Crucially, the statistics underscore significant safety concerns, noting fatalities in Valencia and hundreds of injuries across all provinces. In response, authorities are prioritizing safety by reforming regulations, enhancing training for event directors, and improving infrastructure to protect both participants and animals during periods of high heat. This content is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how public institutions transparently publish granular statistics on public events, health incidents, and administrative responses. Such data allows citizens to scrutinize government safety policies, understand regional cultural impacts, and hold authorities accountable for the implementation of new protective measures in traditional festivities.
