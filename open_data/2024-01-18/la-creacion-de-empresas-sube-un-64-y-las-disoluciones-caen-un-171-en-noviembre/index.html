La creación de empresas sube un 6,4% y las disoluciones caen un 17,1% en noviembre

November witnessed an increase in new business formations alongside a decline in dissolutions, signaling improved market stability. However, the average capital per new company dropped significantly, indicating that entrepreneurs are establishing smaller enterprises with lower financial barriers to entry. Sector and regional analyses reveal that while commerce remains a primary driver for new entities, the financial sector attracts the highest investment. Regional performance varied widely, with some areas experiencing strong growth while others faced declines, highlighting local economic divergences. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates how transparent statistical indicators can track entrepreneurial health. Accessible datasets allow stakeholders to monitor economic trends, identify regional disparities, and make informed decisions regarding market opportunities and policy interventions.
