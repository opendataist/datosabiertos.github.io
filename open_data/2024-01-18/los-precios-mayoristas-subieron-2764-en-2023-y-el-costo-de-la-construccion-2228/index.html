Los precios mayoristas subieron 276,4% en 2023 y el costo de la construcción 222,8%

Argentina experienced historic inflation, driven by massive spikes in wholesale and construction costs. This severe economic deterioration highlights the urgent need for transparent, accessible public data to monitor such volatility effectively. Open data is crucial for enabling independent verification and analysis of these extreme figures. It allows researchers and citizens to track the real impact of price surges on primary and manufactured goods, fostering accountability in economic reporting. Ultimately, this crisis underscores how open statistical standards empower society to understand complex inflationary dynamics. Without reliable, open information, stakeholders cannot fully grasp or address the deep structural challenges facing the national economy today.
