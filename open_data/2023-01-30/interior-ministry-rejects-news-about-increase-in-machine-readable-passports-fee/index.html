Interior Ministry rejects news about increase in Machine Readable Passports’ fee

The Interior Ministry refuted false claims of passport fee hikes, clarifying that only e-passport rates were adjusted while others remain unchanged. This transparency is vital for open data, ensuring public trust in government information and preventing the spread of misinformation through accessible, verified official records.
