Gobierno Abierto: UNL recibió un premio internacional

The National University of Litoral has been recognized by UN-ILPES and the Ibero-American Open Government Academic Network as a leading academic practice for its Open Government Portal. The jury highlighted the initiative as an innovative and replicable model that successfully integrates transparency, participation, and collaboration. Its visual approach to data presentation and emphasis on participatory budgeting demonstrate how higher education institutions can effectively operationalize open government principles in a regional context. This recognition is significant for the open data community because it validates the use of technology and interdisciplinarity to strengthen democratic processes. The award underscores that open data is not merely about publishing information, but about creating coherent, demanding actions that foster trust and engagement. By leveraging digital tools and diverse academic expertise, universities can serve as key actors in improving public policies and setting benchmarks for other institutions across Ibero-America. The achievement illustrates the power of strong institutional commitment and collaborative effort across different university departments. It proves that open government initiatives require sustained cooperation between statistics, IT, and communication teams to be effective. This case study offers valuable insights for others seeking to implement similar frameworks, showing that integrating open data with active citizen participation creates a robust model for transparency that extends beyond the university into the broader society.
