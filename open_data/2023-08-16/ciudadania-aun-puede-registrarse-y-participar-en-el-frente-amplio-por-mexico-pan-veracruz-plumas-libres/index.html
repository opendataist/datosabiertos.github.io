Ciudadanía aún puede registrarse y participar en el Frente Amplio por México: PAN Veracruz - Plumas Libres

The article highlights the PAN party’s call for citizen registration in the “Frente Amplio por México” to strengthen democratic defense through a collaborative agenda with the PRI and PRD. This initiative represents a significant effort toward open participation, as it invites civil society to actively shape public policy and political direction rather than remaining passive observers. By extending the registration deadline and scheduling multiple forums, the organizers aim to broaden inclusivity and ensure that diverse voices contribute to the proposed agenda. This approach emphasizes transparency and accessibility, key principles of open governance, by providing physical assistance centers and clear timelines for engagement. The relevance to open data lies in the potential for this citizen-driven process to generate valuable insights into public priorities. If the resulting agenda and consultation results are made publicly available, they could serve as a rich resource for analyzing civic engagement trends and informing future democratic improvements.
