UPDATED: High food prices, fares push inflation to 24.08% | The Nation Newspaper

Rising inflation in Nigeria severely erodes citizens' capacity to save or invest, driven by steep price hikes in essential food items and transport. This economic pressure highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible data to monitor cost-of-living trends and inform policy interventions. Such detailed statistical reporting is vital for open data ecosystems, enabling researchers and citizens to track affordability and advocate for equitable economic reforms. Without granular, publicly available metrics, stakeholders cannot effectively address the systemic issues driving these expenditure increases. Ultimately, this report underscores how open data facilitates accountability in inflation tracking. By making these economic indicators accessible, society can better understand and respond to the financial hardships faced by ordinary Nigerians.
