El Ayuntamiento invertirá 333.000 euros para hacer sus datos más accesibles a los ciudadanos

The City Council is launching a centralized data platform to consolidate its growing array of municipal statistics into a single, transparent observatory. By unifying previously fragmented information on mobility, equality, and sustainability, the administration aims to enhance urban planning and foster greater civic access to local reality. This initiative reflects a broader shift toward open data practices, where comprehensive datasets empower communities and improve public service efficiency. Despite these ambitious goals, the current systems suffer from significant obsolescence, with key indicators and interactive maps often remaining unupdated for years. This lack of timeliness undermines the utility of existing tools, preventing accurate tracking of social and economic trends. The new project seeks to rectify these failures by establishing a more robust, current infrastructure that truly serves as a dynamic resource for monitoring the city’s evolving needs. This effort is crucial for open data because it highlights the difference between mere data collection and effective, usable transparency. For open data to drive real societal value, information must be accurate, accessible, and regularly refreshed. Without addressing these maintenance gaps, the potential benefits of data-driven governance remain unrealized, turning valuable resources into static archives rather than living tools for democratic engagement and informed decision-making.
