Buen Gobierno detecta posible filtración de datos personales de Aeromexico - El Arsenal

Authorities have identified a potential data breach involving Aeroméxico, with leaked records containing sensitive personal information of millions of individuals, including public officials. The agency is launching an investigation to determine liability and ensure compliance with data protection laws, emphasizing the critical need for robust security in private databases. This case highlights the vital role of open monitoring in exposing privacy violations. It demonstrates how transparent surveillance helps hold organizations accountable and reinforces the importance of safeguarding personal data in the public interest.
