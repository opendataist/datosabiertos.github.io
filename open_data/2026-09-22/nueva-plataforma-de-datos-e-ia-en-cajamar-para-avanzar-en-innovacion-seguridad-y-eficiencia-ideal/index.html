Nueva plataforma de datos e IA en Cajamar para avanzar en innovación, seguridad y eficiencia | Ideal

Nueva plataforma de datos e IA en Cajamar para avanzar en innovación, seguridad y eficiencia | Ideal

Grupo Cajamar has established a strategic collaboration with IBM to implement an advanced data and artificial intelligence platform, prioritizing the use of open-source technologies. This initiative aims to accelerate the financial institution’s digital transformation by integrating AI models into its core processes, automating repetitive tasks, and enabling staff to focus on creating value for customers. The underlying architecture, based on solutions such as Red Hat OpenShift, ensures operational flexibility and avoids exclusive dependence on a single vendor, aligning with data sovereignty principles. The implementation of this unified infrastructure significantly enhances information governance and security, which are crucial for complying with the strict regulations of the banking sector. By centralizing data management and applying AI governance tools, Cajamar ensures transparent and responsible use of artificial intelligence, thereby strengthening trust among both customers and regulators. This approach not only optimizes internal efficiency and productivity but also lays the foundation for scalable innovation adaptable to the business’s future needs. This case is particularly relevant to the open_data community, as it demonstrates how open technologies can facilitate a robust, secure, and scalable digital transformation in large institutions. By choosing an architecture based on open standards, the institution shows that transparency and interoperability are compatible with the highest levels of corporate security. It serves as an inspiring model for other organizations, highlighting that open source is not merely a technical option, but a vital strategy for achieving technological independence, continuous improvement, and truly responsible data management in the era of artificial intelligence.
