MOSPI releases publication on data, methodologies for compiling new GDP series

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation has released a comprehensive guide on India’s updated National Accounts Statistics, establishing a robust framework for calculating macroeconomic indicators with the 2022-23 base year. This consolidation modernizes estimation methodologies across corporate, financial, government, and household sectors, ensuring the data accurately reflects the current economic structure. By integrating advanced administrative records and aligning with international standards, the initiative significantly enhances the precision and reliability of economic measurements. A primary implication of this reform is the shift toward greater transparency and data-driven decision-making. The adoption of detailed corporate filings, expanded financial sector coverage, and direct household estimations reduces reliance on indirect proxies. Furthermore, the integration of UN-recommended classifications and improved quarterly compilation techniques using GST data ensures that the statistical framework remains consistent with global best practices, thereby strengthening the credibility of India’s economic narratives. This publication is crucial for the open_data community as it demystifies the complex processes behind official GDP and national income calculations. By making the underlying sources, methodologies, and compilation practices publicly available, it empowers researchers, policymakers, and analysts to better interpret, validate, and utilize macroeconomic data. This transparency fosters trust in statistical institutions and facilitates more rigorous academic and policy-related inquiries into India’s economic performance.
