France has over 27,000 fewer parishes than in 1980 | News Headlines

Recent Vatican statistics reveal a dramatic collapse in France’s Catholic institutional infrastructure, with parishes, clergy, and religious communities plummeting over four decades. Despite this structural erosion, the absolute Catholic population has slightly increased, though their share of the total national demographic has decreased significantly. This data highlights a growing disconnect between institutional capacity and the actual faithful, challenging traditional models of Church organization. The sharp decline in sacramental participation and religious vocations suggests a crisis in engagement and sustainability for established religious structures. For open data, these figures demonstrate the power of standardized, longitudinal datasets to track societal shifts. Access to such granular historical statistics allows researchers to analyze demographic trends, measure institutional health, and understand the broader cultural impact of religious decline across Europe.
