Las ventas del sector servicios frenan en julio su crecimiento al 4,8% y las de la industria al 4,1%

The service sector shows robust, sustained growth, maintaining a streak of positive annual rates across all autonomous communities. Although the pace slowed slightly in July, the consistent expansion underscores resilience and widespread regional stability within the service industry. At the same time, the industrial sector continues its upward trajectory, driven primarily by strong performance in the domestic market. This growth is geographically diversified, with significant gains in several regions offsetting minor declines elsewhere, indicating a broad-based recovery rather than isolated successes. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it highlights the importance of transparent, accessible economic indicators. By making such granular, regional statistics available to the public, stakeholders can better analyze trends, fostering informed decision-making and encouraging greater use of official statistical resources for research and policy development.
