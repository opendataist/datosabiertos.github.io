Detectan más de $10 mil millones en sobregasto en compras públicas durante el Gobierno de Boric: Gendarmería e Instituto Geográfico Militar concentran casi el 40%

The Department of Public Works (Dipres) revealed millions of dollars in overcharges in state purchases made through framework agreements, highlighting that the Chilean National Gendarmerie and the National Institute of Statistics (INE) account for the largest share. This finding exposes critical inefficiencies in the management of public resources, particularly in the procurement of food and basic supplies. The analysis indicates that very few entities are responsible for the majority of these excesses, suggesting the need for targeted reviews. The concentration of the problem in specific sectors such as health and security requires focused interventions to correct significant deviations in the prices paid. This is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of publishing and auditing public contracting data. Only through transparency and access to detailed information can these irregularities be identified, accountability be fostered, and the use of state funds be optimized through citizen and analytical scrutiny.
