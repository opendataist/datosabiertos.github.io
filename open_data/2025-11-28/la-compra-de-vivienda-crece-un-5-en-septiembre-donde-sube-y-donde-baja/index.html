La compra de vivienda crece un 5% en septiembre: ¿dónde sube y dónde baja?

La compra de vivienda crece un 5% en septiembre: ¿dónde sube y dónde baja?

Recent data reveals a clear bifurcation in the Spanish real estate market, with transaction volumes diverging sharply across regions. While some areas are experiencing significant growth in sales, others are seeing declines, highlighting a fragmented demand landscape across different autonomous communities. At the same time, housing prices have risen substantially in sixteen regions, often outpacing the growth in transaction volumes. This suggests that in many markets, scarcity or increased demand is driving up costs, even where the number of transactions is not expanding proportionally, indicating strong upward pressure on asset values. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for granular, region-specific datasets. High-level national averages mask these critical local disparities, demonstrating why detailed, accessible public statistics are essential for accurate market analysis and informed decision-making by stakeholders.
