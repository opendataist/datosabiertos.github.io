La AEPD publica su Política interna de uso de Inteligencia Artificial, la primera de este tipo en el sector público - LegalToday

La AEPD publica su Política interna de uso de Inteligencia Artificial, la primera de este tipo en el sector público - LegalToday

The Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) has published its internal policy on the ethical and secure use of generative artificial intelligence, marking a pioneering milestone in the Spanish public sector. This document establishes a rigorous governance framework that prioritizes human oversight, transparency, and the protection of fundamental rights. As the first directive of its kind in the public administration, it serves as a key reference for aligning digital transformation with compliance with current regulations and maintaining public trust. The adopted approach integrates AI as a standardized operational process, enhancing administrative efficiency without compromising legal certainty. The policy outlines practical use cases and procedures for risk management, ensuring that implementation is progressive and controlled. This demonstrates how institutions can adopt emerging technologies while maintaining control over their applications and minimizing potential threats to privacy or human autonomy. This initiative is relevant to the open data movement because it shows that transparency is not incompatible with technological innovation. By establishing clear standards for explainability and accountability, the AEPD validates that the use of AI can coexist with high levels of integrity and accountability. This model inspires other organizations to publish their own guidelines, fostering a culture of openness and clarity in the handling of data and algorithms, which is essential for public trust in digital solutions.
