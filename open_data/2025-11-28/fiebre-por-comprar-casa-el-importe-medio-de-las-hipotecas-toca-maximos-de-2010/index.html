The housing market demonstrates significant resilience, driven by lower interest rates that have encouraged buyers to secure larger loans over longer terms. This shift indicates robust consumer confidence in long-term affordability, despite the increasing total debt burden per household. Renegotiations of existing mortgages are declining, while transferring debt during property sales is surging. This suggests a market where new transactions dominate over restructuring older ones, reflecting a dynamic environment focused on acquiring new assets rather than adjusting legacy financial commitments. This data is crucial for open_data as it highlights the need for accessible, standardized financial metrics to track market health. Transparent data on lending trends, interest rate impacts, and regional variations empowers researchers and policymakers to analyze economic stability and identify emerging patterns in housing affordability.
