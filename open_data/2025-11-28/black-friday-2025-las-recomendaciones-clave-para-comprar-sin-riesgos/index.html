Black Friday 2025: las recomendaciones clave para comprar sin riesgos

Black Friday 2025 stands out for its vast commercial reach, but also for the rise in digital fraud and deceptive promotions that erode consumer trust. Manipulative practices, such as the artificial inflation of prior prices, reveal that many so-called deals are aggressive marketing strategies rather than genuine savings. This lack of transparency threatens brand credibility, underscoring that customer loyalty depends directly on honesty and consistency in commercial communications. To counter these risks, there is a strong emphasis on the need for active monitoring and the use of technological tools that verify the authenticity of discounts. Cybersecurity is presented as the first line of defense, requiring users to validate the security of websites and employ secure payment methods to avoid scams. At the same time, artificial intelligence emerges as a strategic ally, enabling personalized and secure experiences that enhance interactions between brands and buyers, thereby establishing a new standard of trust in e-commerce. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical importance of data transparency in digital commerce. When platforms share clear information about historical prices and actual conditions, they facilitate citizen verification and reduce the information asymmetry that favors fraud. Moreover, the use of accessible technologies to compare data fosters a fairer and more robust commercial ecosystem, demonstrating that access to truthful information is essential to protect consumers and strengthen trust in the digital economy.
