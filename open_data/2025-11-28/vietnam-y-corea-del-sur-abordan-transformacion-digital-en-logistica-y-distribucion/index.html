Vietnam y Corea del Sur abordan transformación digital en logística y distribución

Vietnam and South Korea have strengthened bilateral ties in logistics and digital transformation, establishing a vital framework for mutual cooperation. This partnership highlights the growing integration of their economies, where Vietnam’s expanding domestic market and South Korea’s advanced technological expertise complement each other. The dialogue serves as a crucial platform for aligning public policies and fostering investment, ensuring a more transparent and stable environment for businesses operating across borders. The focus on digital innovation underscores the necessity of modernizing supply chains through smart warehouses and artificial intelligence. By adopting South Korea’s efficient distribution models, Vietnam aims to optimize its logistics sector. This shift not only enhances operational efficiency but also requires urgent institutional reforms. The collaboration emphasizes the transfer of technology and management practices, enabling Vietnam to keep pace with rapid e-commerce growth and meet evolving consumer demands. Relevance to open data lies in the proposed exchange of information to combat counterfeiting and protect intellectual property. Sharing data on potentially falsified goods fosters transparency and consumer trust, aligning with open data principles of accessibility and accountability. Furthermore, the push for sustainable practices and digital standards encourages the open sharing of ESG metrics and regulatory frameworks. This cooperation demonstrates how data-driven insights can strengthen international trade relations and improve governance in global supply chains.
