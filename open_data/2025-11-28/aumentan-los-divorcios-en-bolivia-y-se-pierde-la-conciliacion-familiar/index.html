Aumentan los divorcios en Bolivia y se pierde la conciliación familiar

The article highlights Bolivia’s rising divorce rates, urging the state to prioritize marital stability and family protection. It criticizes the loss of judicial conciliation mechanisms, noting that judges no longer facilitate reconciliation or therapy, thereby weakening the support system for couples. This issue directly concerns open data. Transparent, accessible datasets on divorce statistics are essential for identifying social trends. By making this information publicly available, society can better understand the causes of family fragility and advocate for evidence-based reforms in family law and conciliation processes.
