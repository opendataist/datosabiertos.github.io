US stocks gained and the dollar softened heading into the Thanksgiving Day holiday - Newsquawk Asia-Pac Market Open

US stocks gained and the dollar softened heading into the Thanksgiving Day holiday - Newsquawk Asia-Pac Market Open

US equities experienced broad-based gains as investors embraced a risk-on sentiment ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday, with technology and small-cap stocks leading the advance. This market optimism was supported by mixed but generally positive economic data, including better-than-expected durable goods orders and lower initial jobless claims, which alleviated immediate labor market concerns despite a slump in regional manufacturing activity. The Federal Reserve’s Beige Book indicated economic activity remained largely unchanged, reinforcing a narrative of stability rather than contraction. Geopolitical and trade developments remained complex, highlighted by tensions in the Middle East and evolving tariff policies affecting global supply chains. Specifically, Chinese regulatory actions against foreign semiconductor use and Canadian import quotas underscored the ongoing fragmentation in international trade. These factors contributed to thinner liquidity and reduced volatility in currency markets as traders positioned for a lighter news flow over the long weekend. This analysis is relevant to open data because financial markets rely heavily on transparent, publicly available economic indicators and regulatory filings to function efficiently. The interplay between published data points—such as jobless claims and PMI surveys—and market reactions demonstrates the critical role of data transparency in price discovery. Furthermore, the visibility of trade policies and geopolitical shifts through open news sources allows stakeholders to assess systemic risks, ensuring that the open information ecosystem remains a foundational pillar for global economic stability and investment decisions.
