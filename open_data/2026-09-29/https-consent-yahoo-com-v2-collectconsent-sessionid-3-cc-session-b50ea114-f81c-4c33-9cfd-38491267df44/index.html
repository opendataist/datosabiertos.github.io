Open Data
ES EN

Privacy consent mechanisms often obscure data usage, limiting open data transparency by relying on complex cookie notices. This hinders public scrutiny of how personal and behavioral data are harvested for analytics and advertising. True openness requires clear, accessible data policies, contrasting with opaque consent frameworks that restrict user control and accountability.
