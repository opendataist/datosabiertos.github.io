We Can't Trust The AI Billionaires!

We Can't Trust The AI Billionaires!

The article argues that unregulated AI development under capitalism poses immediate and severe threats to public safety, environmental stability, and democratic systems. Rather than waiting for benevolent corporate self-regulation, which prioritizes profit and geopolitical dominance over human welfare, society requires a decisive pause on advanced AI development. This stance is driven by evidence that current systems are already causing social harm and are vulnerable to catastrophic failures, necessitating a shift away from billionaire-controlled innovation toward models that serve collective interests. Relevance to open data lies in the urgent need for transparency, accountability, and democratic oversight in how AI systems are built and deployed. The current opaque, proprietary nature of major AI developments prevents public scrutiny of their risks and biases, exacerbating societal inequalities. By advocating for the transition of AI technology into democratic public ownership, the article highlights that open access and community control are essential to ensuring these tools are safe, ethical, and aligned with the needs of working people rather than the profit motives of tech giants. To achieve this, the piece calls for a broad, organized mass movement uniting various sectors against unchecked technological expansion. It emphasizes the necessity of independent political action that rejects both establishment parties and far-right nationalism, focusing instead on international working-class solidarity. By halting the construction of massive data centers and demanding federal bans on unchecked development, this movement seeks to dismantle the current power structure and establish a framework where technology is governed by democratic principles rather than capitalist imperatives.
