Política de privacidad

Política de privacidad

This document outlines the legal framework and operational procedures for data processing by the website Hipertextual, ensuring compliance with regulations such as the Law on Information Society Services and European data protection standards. It clarifies that the entity acts as the data controller, managing personal information and usage cookies primarily to deliver services, conduct analytics, and manage advertising through third-party tools like Google Analytics. The text emphasizes user rights, including the ability to access, correct, or delete data, while noting that consent can be withdrawn at any time. The implications for transparency are significant, as the article details how data is stored, processed by authorized personnel, and potentially transferred outside the user’s jurisdiction, requiring robust security measures to prevent unauthorized access. It highlights that while data provision is often mandatory for service delivery, specific non-essential data can be omitted without affecting functionality. Furthermore, it establishes the legal bases for processing, ranging from contractual necessity to legitimate interest, ensuring that users understand the justification behind data collection practices. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the necessity of clear, accessible legal notices that underpin trustworthy digital ecosystems. While not directly about publishing datasets, it addresses the foundational governance of personal information, a critical counterpart to open data initiatives. Understanding these privacy constraints helps define the boundaries where open data ends and protected personal data begins, promoting ethical standards in how organizations handle, share, and protect user information in compliance with evolving privacy laws.
