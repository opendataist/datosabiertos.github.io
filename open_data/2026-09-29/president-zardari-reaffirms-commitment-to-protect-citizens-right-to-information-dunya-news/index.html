President Zardari reaffirms commitment to protect citizens' right to information | Dunya News

President Zardari underscores that the right to information is vital for meaningful civic participation. He highlights Pakistan’s constitutional and legal frameworks establishing this right, while emphasizing that true access requires citizens to discern reliable data from misinformation in an era defined by rapid technological change. The article reveals a critical tension between combating manipulated content and preserving democratic discourse. It argues that efforts to counter disinformation must not suppress public-interest journalism or fair criticism, stressing the essential role of experts and institutions in ensuring transparency and accountability within the evolving information landscape. This narrative is relevant to open data because it frames data access as a cornerstone of democracy rather than just a technical asset. It advocates for open data systems that prioritize accuracy and trust, warning against authoritarian overreach while promoting the infrastructure needed for citizens to verify and utilize public information effectively.
