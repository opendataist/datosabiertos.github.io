El Parlamento da 30 días para que Industria aporte todo el expediente del Proyecto Altamira | El Diario Montañés

El Parlamento da 30 días para que Industria aporte todo el expediente del Proyecto Altamira | El Diario Montañés

Political groups are demanding full transparency regarding a major private data center project, criticizing the government for failing to provide clear information on water and energy consumption. These metrics are essential for obtaining national authorization, making the opacity surrounding utility usage a central point of contention and a risk to public oversight. The debate highlights tensions over foreign ownership and the use of public credit for land acquisition, challenging claims of economic sovereignty and local benefit. Critics argue that the lack of disclosed data prevents effective evaluation, while officials defend the initiative as a historic investment that does not threaten household energy supply. This situation is critical for open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of accessible, granular public records for infrastructure projects. Without transparent data on resource usage and financial backing, citizens and lawmakers cannot verify efficiency or accountability, demonstrating how open information is essential for democratic scrutiny of large-scale privatizations.
