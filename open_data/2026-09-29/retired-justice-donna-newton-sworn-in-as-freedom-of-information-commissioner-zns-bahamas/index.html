Retired Justice Donna Newton Sworn in as Freedom of Information Commissioner - ZNS BAHAMAS

The appointment of retired Justice Donna Newton as The Bahamas’ Freedom of Information Commissioner signals a critical advancement in the nation’s commitment to transparency and accountability. Her extensive background in both journalism and the judiciary positions her to effectively oversee the practical implementation of the Freedom of Information framework, ensuring robust administration and oversight. This leadership role strengthens the mechanism for reviewing access-to-information decisions, thereby fostering a structured and trustworthy relationship between public authorities and citizens. By facilitating formal processes for data requests while balancing necessary exemptions, the office promotes informed public participation and enhances democratic engagement. This development is highly relevant to open data as it establishes the legal and institutional infrastructure required for systematic government transparency. It underscores the shift toward making public records accessible, which is foundational for open data initiatives that rely on regulatory support to ensure information is available, usable, and accountable for the benefit of society.
