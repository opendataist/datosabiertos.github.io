Encabeza Pepe Chedraui inicio de la Semana de la Transparencia 2026 para juventudes poblanas

Encabeza Pepe Chedraui inicio de la Semana de la Transparencia 2026 para juventudes poblanas

The municipal government launched its Transparency Week at a university to actively promote citizens’ right to access public information. This initiative underscores the importance of a government that is transparent, accessible, and responsive to the community. By creating platforms such as the Open Government Portal, authorities aim to provide clear and easy-to-understand data, fostering a culture in which citizens can effectively exercise their right to know. The event featured a keynote address on the current challenges and future prospects of transparency in Mexico, highlighting the shift from viewing access to information as a mere right to leveraging it for the development of better public policies. This dialogue with young people emphasizes the need to adapt transparency frameworks to contemporary demands and to ensure that information serves as a tool for democratic participation and accountability. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical link between institutional transparency policies and the practical application of open data principles. It demonstrates how governments are not merely releasing data, but are actively educating the public on how to use it—an essential step in transforming raw data into meaningful civic engagement.
