Si tienes entre 18 y 30 años, ya está abierto el plazo de inscripción para viajar en tren y autobús con hasta un 90% de descuento

This article outlines a new regulation that provides substantial discounts to young travelers, emphasizing the crucial role of digital identity verification in the distribution of public services. By requiring users to register online to obtain a unique, non-transferable code, the system generates a structured dataset of eligible citizens. This approach illustrates how governments can apply open data principles—specifically interoperability and standardized digital credentials such as Cl@ve—to ensure equitable access while preventing fraud. It highlights the need for robust, transparent digital infrastructures to manage large-scale social benefits. The policy aims to boost tourism and rural development by making intercity travel more affordable for individuals aged 18 to 30. Through significant price reductions on trains and buses, the measure seeks to stimulate economic recovery and cultural exchange within Spain and Europe. The focus extends beyond mere cost reduction to promoting mobility as a tool for social inclusion and appreciation of cultural heritage. This implies that accessible transport data and clear eligibility criteria are essential for achieving broader socioeconomic objectives, encouraging citizens to engage with their surroundings through affordable travel options. Finally, the inclusion of strict penalties for misuse underscores the importance of data integrity and accountability in such programs. By monitoring usage patterns and revoking codes for repeated violations, the administration ensures that resources reach the intended demographic. For the open data community, this serves as a case study on balancing accessibility with security. It emphasizes that effective public policies require not only open access to information but also clear, auditable mechanisms for user verification and usage tracking, ensuring that digital tools efficiently serve their intended social purpose.
