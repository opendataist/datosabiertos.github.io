Convocan a presentar proyectos de IA para el Hackathon Presidencial de Taiwán

Taiwan hosts a global hackathon that leverages open data and artificial intelligence to enhance public services and bridge the digital divide. This initiative connects developers with government resources to foster inclusive technological solutions for social challenges. The event emphasizes international collaboration between the public and private sectors, using shared data to drive innovation. By facilitating interdisciplinary exchanges, it aims to create scalable solutions to development needs worldwide. This contest is vital for open data, as it puts accessible information into practice to achieve tangible societal improvements. It demonstrates how open datasets can empower global teams to build equitable, AI-driven tools that directly impact quality of life and civic infrastructure.
