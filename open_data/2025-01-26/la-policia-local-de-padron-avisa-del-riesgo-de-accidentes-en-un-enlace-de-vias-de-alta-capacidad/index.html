La Policía Local de Padrón avisa del riesgo de accidentes en un enlace de vías de alta capacidad

La Policía Local de Padrón avisa del riesgo de accidentes en un enlace de vías de alta capacidad

Padrón’s local police have highlighted the urgent need to improve safety at a hazardous intersection where several high-capacity roads converge. The area suffers from critical visibility problems during low-light conditions and in the rain, leading to recurrent accidents that cause significant material damage. Despite previous minor upgrades, authorities consider the existing measures insufficient to mitigate the ongoing risk posed by this dangerous junction. The core demand involves installing comprehensive lighting systems and luminous vertical signals to alert drivers immediately. This proposal addresses infrastructure gaps that currently leave motorists exposed to severe hazards. The police argue that better illumination is essential for preventing future collisions, emphasizing that the current state of the roadway fails to meet basic safety standards expected even on lesser roads. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, publicly accessible infrastructure maintenance records and accident statistics. Open data initiatives can empower citizens and officials to track unresolved safety complaints and demand accountability. By making such incident reports and maintenance timelines available, stakeholders can ensure that critical infrastructure issues are addressed proactively rather than reactively, fostering greater public trust and road safety through data-driven governance.
