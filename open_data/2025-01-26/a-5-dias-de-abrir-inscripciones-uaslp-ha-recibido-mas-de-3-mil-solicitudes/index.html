A 5 días de abrir inscripciones, UASLP ha recibido más de 3 mil solicitudes

A 5 días de abrir inscripciones, UASLP ha recibido más de 3 mil solicitudes

The UASLP has launched its 2025 admissions process, which has already generated significant early interest. The process introduces a unified academic exam that carries substantial weight in final scores. This change aims to ensure an equitable evaluation of core competencies across all disciplines, including humanities and sciences, thereby standardizing merit-based selection. The update is designed to maintain educational quality while simplifying the administrative journey for applicants through a streamlined, personalized registration procedure accessible via official digital channels. At the same time, the psychological assessment has evolved to prioritize critical thinking and socio-emotional skills alongside traditional reasoning metrics. By integrating these new areas, the university seeks a more holistic view of applicants’ potential. The strict requirement to complete both exams underscores a commitment to comprehensive student profiling, ensuring that admitted individuals are not only academically prepared but also possess the necessary cognitive and emotional frameworks for success. This initiative is highly relevant to open data, as it leverages publicly accessible digital platforms for registration and study guide distribution. By providing free, standardized resources and transparent admission criteria, the university enhances institutional transparency and equal opportunity. The digital nature of these processes creates potential for analyzing application trends and improving future educational accessibility through data-driven insights.
