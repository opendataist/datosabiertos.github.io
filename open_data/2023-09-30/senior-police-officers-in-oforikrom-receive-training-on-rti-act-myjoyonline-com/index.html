Senior Police officers in Oforikrom receive training on RTI Act - MyJoyOnline.com

Police officers in the Oforikrom Municipality underwent specialized training on the Right to Information Act to strengthen institutional transparency. The session clarified procedural nuances, emphasizing that while standard forms are recommended, flexible submission methods are permitted to facilitate public access. Strict identification requirements ensure applicant verification, balancing ease of access with administrative integrity. The training also addressed the critical intersection of public disclosure and state security. Commanders learned to rely on Public Affairs officers to assess whether requested information compromises national security. This protocol ensures that sensitive matters remain protected while non-confidential data is shared, fostering a culture of accountability within law enforcement agencies. This initiative is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how structured legal frameworks can be operationalized to make government information accessible without compromising safety. By equipping officers with the knowledge to navigate these regulations, the municipality promotes a more open society where citizens can effectively exercise their right to know, thereby enhancing trust in public institutions.
