More Nigerians dying of hypertension, stroke, says FG

More Nigerians dying of hypertension, stroke, says FG

The Federal Government highlights that cardiovascular disease deaths are significantly underestimated due to inadequate data repositories and poor public awareness. This lack of comprehensive data hinders effective health policy and resource allocation, underscoring the critical need for robust, open health information systems to accurately track morbidity and mortality trends. To address these gaps, officials are deploying Automated External Defibrillators in high-traffic areas, signaling a shift toward proactive emergency response infrastructure. This initiative emphasizes that improving data collection and visibility is essential for combating the leading cause of non-communicable deaths, which disproportionately affect low- and middle-income nations. This situation is highly relevant to open data because transparent, accessible health statistics are vital for accountability and public engagement. By making data on disease prevalence and intervention outcomes publicly available, governments can better inform citizens, facilitate research, and ensure that life-saving resources are distributed based on empirical evidence rather than estimated figures.
