Ceaipes, presente en Chihuahua donde destaca la accesibilidad inclusiva mediante INTEGRA2

Ceaipes, presente en Chihuahua donde destaca la accesibilidad inclusiva mediante INTEGRA2

The article stresses that the right to know must explicitly encompass vulnerable groups, particularly persons with disabilities, ensuring they can access public information without barriers. This highlights a critical gap in digital governance, where exclusion based on ability prevents full civic participation. To address this, progressive adaptation of web portals is essential to facilitate access to government services for all citizens, regardless of their limitations. Legal mandates for web accessibility have been implemented to enforce this inclusion, moving beyond mere compliance to creating truly facilitative information environments. This narrative is relevant to open data because it underscores that true openness requires universal accessibility. Without inclusive tools like INTEGRA2, which supports people with diverse impairments, open data remains inaccessible to significant portions of the population, undermining the fundamental goal of transparent and equitable governance.
