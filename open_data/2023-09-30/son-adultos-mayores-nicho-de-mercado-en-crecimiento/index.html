Son adultos mayores, nicho de mercado en crecimiento

The article highlights the evolving consumer habits of Mexico’s growing elderly population, emphasizing their financial stability and specific spending priorities. Contrary to stereotypes, this demographic holds significant purchasing power, allocating substantial resources toward health, daily necessities, and leisure activities like travel. This shift transforms them into a vital, active economic niche rather than a passive group requiring mere care. These insights are crucial for open data initiatives focused on social and economic inclusion. By publicly analyzing and sharing demographic and consumption datasets, stakeholders can better understand the diverse needs of aging populations. This transparency enables the creation of targeted policies and services that accurately reflect the reality of older adults as engaged citizens with distinct financial behaviors and desires for autonomy. Ultimately, leveraging open data allows governments and businesses to design inclusive products and support systems. It fosters a deeper understanding of how seniors manage their resources, ensuring that public and private interventions are evidence-based. This approach promotes equitable access to health, recreation, and financial services, enhancing the quality of life for a rapidly expanding segment of society through informed, data-driven decision-making.
