Indocal es reconocida entre las cinco instituciones gubernamentales más transparente

The Dominican Institute for Quality attained top-tier status in government transparency, reflecting a deep commitment to public accountability. This recognition underscores the successful implementation of standards that ensure citizens have timely and adequate access to essential information. Such excellence reinforces an institutional culture of duty to respond and serves as a powerful tool against administrative corruption. By prioritizing open data practices, the organization demonstrates how robust transparency frameworks can effectively safeguard democratic rights and foster trust between state entities and the public. This achievement is crucial for the open data community, as it validates the tangible benefits of rigorous information management. It illustrates that structured transparency not only fulfills legal mandates but also actively empowers society through shared knowledge.
