KRIS 6 wins Freedom of Information award

KRIS 6 News received the Nancy Monson Spirit of FOI award for exposing dismissals by the Nueces County District Attorney’s Office. This investigation highlighted how withholding information harms public trust and justified journalistic intervention using open government laws. The reporting contributed to a removal petition, leading the DA’s resignation. The contest judge praised the station for uncovering practices that prioritized grants over justice, demonstrating how transparency efforts can drive significant political and accountability outcomes in local governance. This story underscores the vital role of open data and public records in democratic accountability. It illustrates that proactive disclosure is essential for preventing misconduct. Consequently, the article serves as a compelling example of how robust open data frameworks empower journalists to safeguard public interest and ensure government integrity.
