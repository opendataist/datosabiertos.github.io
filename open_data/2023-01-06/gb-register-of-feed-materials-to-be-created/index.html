GB register of feed materials to be created

The UK has established a dedicated register for feed materials, replacing the post-Brexit gap left by the loss of automatic recognition of the EU database. This move ensures legal compliance and provides a centralized platform for notifying and accessing new feed ingredients within Great Britain, while Northern Ireland continues to follow EU regulations. For open data, this initiative highlights the critical need for accessible, standardized industry registries. By creating a single, authoritative online source, stakeholders gain transparency into approved materials, fostering trust and reducing administrative friction for businesses seeking to innovate or source raw materials efficiently. The register’s foundation on pre-Brexit EU entries ensures historical continuity, while planned reviews promise future accuracy. This structure serves as a model for how sectors can maintain robust, open reference systems during regulatory transitions, ensuring that data remains a reliable tool for safety, compliance, and market development.
