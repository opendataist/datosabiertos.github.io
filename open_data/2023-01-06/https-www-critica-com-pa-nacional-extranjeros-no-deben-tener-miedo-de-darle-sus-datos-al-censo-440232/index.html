Open Data
ES EN

The Panamanian National Institute of Statistics and Census underscores the critical role of foreign residents in providing accurate data for the upcoming population and housing census. This initiative marks the first time permanent foreign residents are invited to participate openly, enabling a comprehensive understanding of their demographic presence alongside national citizens, without inquiries into their migratory status. The information collected is vital for analyzing economic contributions, public service demands, and labor supply, thereby informing effective policy development. Statistical authorities reassure participants that data privacy is strictly maintained, ensuring that no personal identities are disclosed. This transparency aims to encourage cooperation by guaranteeing that the information serves purely statistical purposes, protecting both regularized and undocumented individuals from any adverse consequences. This article highlights the relevance of open data by illustrating the balance between comprehensive public data collection and strict privacy safeguards. It demonstrates how governments can foster trust to acquire diverse demographic insights while adhering to ethical standards. Such practices are essential for creating reliable, inclusive datasets that reflect societal realities without compromising individual security, serving as a model for responsible data governance in multicultural contexts.
