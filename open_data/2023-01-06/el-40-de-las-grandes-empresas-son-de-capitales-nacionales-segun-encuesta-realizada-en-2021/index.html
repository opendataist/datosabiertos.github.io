El 40% de las grandes empresas son de capitales nacionales, según encuesta realizada en 2021

This article highlights the critical role of official national statistics in understanding economic structures. By analyzing large enterprises, it reveals the significant contribution of domestic capital to national production and employment, providing essential data for accurate economic accounting and policy formulation. The findings underscore how aggregated industrial metrics and trade data form the backbone of national accounts. Understanding the distribution of ownership and export volumes among major firms allows for a clearer picture of economic dependency and industrial strength, which is vital for transparent governance and informed decision-making. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of accessible, high-quality statistical sources. When government agencies publish detailed economic indicators publicly, they empower researchers and citizens to verify claims, conduct independent analyses, and hold institutions accountable, thereby fostering transparency and trust in public data ecosystems.
