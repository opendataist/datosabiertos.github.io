Una ‘rave’ y un gato grande

The text critiques how political silence during holidays allows societal tragedies and complex labor data to dominate public attention, often manipulated for electoral gain rather than addressed through genuine inquiry. This dynamic highlights the critical importance of transparent data access, as citizens are denied the factual context needed to evaluate employment trends and social issues beyond superficial political narratives. Open data remains essential to counter this opacity, ensuring that vital information is available for independent analysis rather than being filtered through partisan agendas or ignored until election cycles resume.
