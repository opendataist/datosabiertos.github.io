Más de 73 mil empresas hacen actividades en internet

Mexican businesses are increasingly leveraging their online presence, although most websites remain informational rather than transactional. This reveals a gap between digital visibility and actual e-commerce engagement, highlighting the potential for deeper online integration. Payment platforms are becoming the standard for digital transactions, signaling a shift toward electronic financial infrastructure. The dominance of multi-establishment firms suggests that larger entities are leading this digital transformation. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides a granular view of Mexico’s digital economy. It allows researchers to track how connectivity drives business growth and identifies sectors ripe for digital innovation.
