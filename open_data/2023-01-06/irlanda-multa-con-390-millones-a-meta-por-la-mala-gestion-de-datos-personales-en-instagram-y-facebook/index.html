Irlanda multa con 390 millones a Meta por la mala gestión de datos personales en Instagram y Facebook

Meta faces substantial fines for opaque data practices on Facebook and Instagram, having failed to clearly inform users about how their personal information was processed. This enforcement action by the Irish Data Protection Commission underscores the critical importance of transparency in handling user data. Such rulings are highly relevant to open data initiatives, as they highlight the strict boundaries between proprietary data management and the public interest. Ensuring accountability in corporate data use protects individual privacy while establishing ethical baselines for broader data accessibility. Ultimately, this case demonstrates that robust regulatory oversight is essential. It reinforces the need for clear consent mechanisms, ensuring that data usage remains lawful and respectful of user rights, which is foundational for any trustworthy data ecosystem.
