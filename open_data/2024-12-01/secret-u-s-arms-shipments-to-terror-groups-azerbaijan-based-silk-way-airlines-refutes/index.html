Secret U.S. arms shipments to terror groups ? Azerbaijan based Silk Way Airlines refutes

Reports allege that Silk Way Airlines facilitated the transport of weapons to extremist groups while securing substantial U.S. financial support, including millions in loans and military contracts. These claims, supported by FOIA documents, suggest the airline operated covertly to obscure ownership ties to Azerbaijan’s ruling elite, raising serious questions about the integrity of its international partnerships and the sources of its capital. In response, the airline has vigorously denied all accusations, characterizing them as a coordinated smear campaign lacking legal or substantive merit. Silk Way asserts that all operations, including those for government entities, strictly complied with international aviation regulations and received proper permits. They emphasize their long-standing, verified relationships with major global defense and commercial partners to validate their compliance and professional reputation. This conflict highlights critical transparency gaps in global aviation supply chains, where opaque corporate structures can obscure state influence and potential illicit activities. For the open data community, this case underscores the necessity of linking disparate data sources—such as financial records, ownership registries, and flight logs—to expose hidden conflicts of interest and ensure accountability in international trade and logistics.
