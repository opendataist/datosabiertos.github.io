Transparencia judicial

Transparencia judicial

The proposed data protection policy risks shielding corrupt officials by anonymizing their identities in high-profile legal cases. This measure could allow perpetrators to evade not only legal accountability but also necessary public condemnation, effectively enabling impunity through judicial complicity and political influence. Such a restriction contradicts the principles of open data by prioritizing secrecy over transparency. Without public access to the names involved in scandals, society loses the ability to monitor state actors and demand genuine accountability. This opacity undermines democratic oversight and protects those who exploit systemic weaknesses for personal gain. Supporting the opposition to this rule is crucial for maintaining an open information environment. Transparency remains essential for combating corruption and ensuring that justice serves the public interest. Upholding open data practices empowers citizens to hold power accountable, reinforcing trust in institutions and fostering a culture of integrity rather than silence.
