Steam toma una postura más receptiva a la Inteligencia Artificial tras reciente baneo de un juego que usaba arte IA

Steam recently rejected a game featuring AI-generated art, not due to ideological opposition, but because of unresolved copyright uncertainties surrounding the training data. This incident highlights a critical intersection between digital distribution platforms and emerging technologies, emphasizing that legal compliance, rather than bias, dictates policy enforcement. The situation serves as a cautionary tale for creators who rely on automated tools without clear intellectual property verification. Valve explicitly clarified that its stance is agnostic toward artificial intelligence, aiming instead to integrate these innovations responsibly. It welcomes AI-driven creativity, provided it respects existing licensing agreements. This approach underscores the importance of transparency and adherence to current laws, suggesting that technological adoption must evolve alongside regulatory frameworks to ensure sustainable growth within the industry. This case is highly relevant to open data, as it exposes the opacity of the training datasets used by AI models. If developers cannot verify the provenance of data, accountability suffers, mirroring broader open data challenges regarding licensing and reuse. Understanding these legal gray areas is essential for fostering an ecosystem where AI innovation thrives without violating intellectual property rights, thereby encouraging clearer standards for data usage and attribution.
