En tres días, se inscribieron 3.500 personas para ser autoridades de mesa en Santa Fe

Santa Fe has implemented a public and permanent registry of volunteers for polling station officials, an innovation that moves beyond the traditional reliance on direct phone calls to usual presiding officers. This change addresses the need to fill vacancies caused by the high rejection rate of previous summonses, thereby ensuring democratic continuity with greater citizen participation. The digital platform centralizes management, applying rigorous filters to ensure the impartiality and eligibility of candidates. By keeping the call for volunteers continuously open on the tribunal’s website, a constant reserve of personnel is created for any future electoral processes. This optimizes the assignment of roles, eliminating uncertainty and streamlining the logistics required for organizing elections. This initiative is fundamental to open data because it democratizes access to electoral information. By making the selection process and the availability of volunteers transparent, it fosters accountability and public trust. Additionally, it standardizes data on electoral infrastructure, enabling more precise analysis of citizen participation and the efficiency of the local democratic system.
