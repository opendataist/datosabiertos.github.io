Roscommon remains the largest town in its county | Westmeath Independent

The 2022 census reveals distinct demographic shifts in Roscommon, highlighting a notably younger population in key towns compared to the national average and a rising share of international residents. This data underscores how localized statistical releases provide essential insights into community aging patterns and evolving migration trends that influence regional planning. Internal mobility within the county remains dominant, with a majority of movers choosing local relocations over national ones. Furthermore, Roscommon’s housing preferences diverge significantly from national norms, showing a strong preference for detached homes over apartments among new residents. These findings are vital for open_data initiatives as they demonstrate the value of granular, accessible statistics in understanding micro-level societal changes. By breaking down broad national figures into specific county dynamics, open datasets enable targeted policy-making and a deeper comprehension of how local housing markets and migration patterns interact with broader demographic shifts.
