Actualización Cartográfica llega al 98,5% de avance en Bolivia, según el INE

The National Statistics Institute reports that Bolivia’s Statistical Cartographic Update has reached 98.5%, with only scattered areas in three major departments remaining. This near-completion marks a critical milestone in the pre-census phase, ensuring accurate geographic baselines for the upcoming 2024 population and housing census. The project provides essential spatial data not only for the INE but also for all 342 municipal governments, including indigenous and peasant autonomous governments. By finalizing these maps, the state enhances administrative precision and resource allocation capabilities across all territorial levels. This progress is vital for open data initiatives, as accurate cartography enables transparent, georeferenced public information. It ensures that future census data can be integrated into open platforms, fostering accountability and improving decision-making for civil society and local authorities based on reliable spatial foundations.
