Bolivia is nearing the completion of its census preparatory phase, having identified previously unregistered communities. This outcome underscores the critical role of precise geographic data in ensuring comprehensive population counts. The initiative highlights how open and up-to-date cartographic information reveals hidden demographic groups. By addressing these gaps, statistical agencies enhance the accuracy of their models and resource allocation strategies. This process is vital for open data, as it demonstrates how transparent mapping fosters inclusive governance. It shows that accessible geographic intelligence enables better public policy and more reliable national statistics for all citizens.
