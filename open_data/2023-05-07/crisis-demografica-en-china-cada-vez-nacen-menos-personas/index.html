Crisis demográfica en China; cada vez nacen menos personas

China is confronting unprecedented demographic challenges, characterized by its first population decline in sixty years and a shrinking workforce. In response, President Xi Jinping has called on officials to enhance population development plans, stressing the importance of maintaining appropriate birth rates and focusing on improving population quality rather than simply managing population numbers. This shift recognizes that an aging population and a reduced labor force are established realities that require strategic adaptation. Consequently, the priority is no longer solely population size but rather increasing productivity through advanced technological advancements and building a modern, secure industrial system to sustain international competitiveness. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because demographic shifts and economic metrics are critical public indicators. Transparent access to population statistics and workforce trends enables researchers and policymakers to accurately analyze these structural changes. Open data facilitates a deeper understanding of how aging societies impact economic growth, thereby supporting evidence-based decision-making in industrial and social planning.
