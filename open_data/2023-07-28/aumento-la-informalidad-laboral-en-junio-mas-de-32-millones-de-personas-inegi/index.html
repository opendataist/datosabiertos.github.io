Aumentó la informalidad laboral en junio; más de 32 millones de personas: Inegi

The article highlights a significant decline in unemployment rates, indicating a robust improvement in the labor market, with millions more people employed compared to the previous year. This reduction in joblessness suggests a strengthening economy, offering optimism for those seeking stable work opportunities, despite ongoing challenges in wage levels. Sectoral analysis reveals that services dominate employment, followed by commerce and manufacturing, while gender disparities in labor participation remain pronounced. These structural insights help researchers understand where economic growth is occurring and identify areas needing targeted policy interventions to support underrepresented groups or specific industries. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because transparent, accessible statistics empower citizens and analysts to hold institutions accountable. Open access to such granular economic indicators fosters informed public discourse, enables better academic research, and supports the development of evidence-based solutions for complex social and economic issues.
