Autoridades realizan censo en Aguas Zarcas para medir condición de riesgo de viviendas y locales cercanos al río - Monumental

Authorities in Aguas Zarcas are conducting a risk assessment census for homes near the river, following debris flows that caused damage and temporarily closed the main bridge. This transparent emergency response highlights how local data collection is vital for public safety. Such open data practices enable communities to understand risks and coordinate effective recovery efforts after natural disasters.
