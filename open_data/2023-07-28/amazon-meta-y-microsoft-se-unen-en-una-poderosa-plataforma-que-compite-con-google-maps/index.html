Amazon, Meta y Microsoft se unen en una poderosa plataforma que compite con Google Maps

The Overture Maps Foundation has launched an open-source alternative to Google Maps, supported by major technology companies and civic organizations. This initiative aims to develop a high-quality, interoperable global dataset, challenging the dominance of proprietary mapping services through collaborative innovation. The platform provides extensive data layers, including millions of previously unpublished points of interest and unique building footprints derived from OpenStreetMap. By offering accessible administrative boundaries and road networks, it ensures comprehensive geographic coverage that supports diverse application development and research needs. This development is crucial for open data, as it democratizes access to critical spatial information. It promotes transparency, reduces dependency on closed ecosystems, and encourages the creation of innovative services that rely on shared, standardized, and freely available geographic resources for the public good.
