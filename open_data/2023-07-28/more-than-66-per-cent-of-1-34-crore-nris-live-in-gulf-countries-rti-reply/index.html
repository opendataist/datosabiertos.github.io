More than 66 per cent of 1.34 crore NRIs live in Gulf countries: RTI reply

More than 66 per cent of 1.34 crore NRIs live in Gulf countries: RTI reply

The article reveals that a significant majority of Non-Resident Indians reside in Gulf nations, highlighting a distinct demographic concentration that differs from the distribution of Persons of Indian Origin, who are more prevalent in countries like the United States and Southeast Asia. This disparity underscores how citizenship status shapes global diaspora patterns, affecting how communities engage with their heritage and host societies across different geopolitical regions. Understanding these precise population spreads is crucial for effective governance and diaspora engagement strategies, allowing for better resource allocation and support systems tailored to specific regional needs. The data illustrates the complex interplay between migration history, economic opportunities, and cultural ties that define the modern Indian diaspora experience on a global scale. This information is vital for open_data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible government records for tracking humanitarian risks and planning international cooperation. By making such demographic insights publicly available, researchers and policymakers can better analyze migration trends and ensure that digital infrastructure supports accurate, real-time understanding of global citizen movements.
