La economía de EEUU toma impulso con un 2,4% en 2T

The U.S. economy has defied negative expectations, demonstrating greater-than-anticipated resilience through accelerated growth driven by consumer spending and business investment. This performance has surpassed forecasts of a slowdown, despite restrictive monetary policies, suggesting that a strong labor market and rising real incomes are sustaining domestic activity in the face of high costs and tight credit conditions. The current dynamics reflect a robust domestic economy, where government spending on infrastructure and corporate investment partially offset declines in exports and residential investment. Although global risks and inflationary pressures persist, the reduction in inflation and stable employment provide clear signals that the domestic sector continues to operate with strength, minimizing the immediate likelihood of a deep recession. This report is relevant to the open data community because it illustrates how transparency in macroeconomic indicators enables analysts and citizens to assess economic health in real time. Public access to these data facilitates independent verification of public policies and the impact of investment laws, fostering accountability and empowering civil society to make informed decisions based on verifiable and accessible evidence.
