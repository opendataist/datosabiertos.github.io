Los principales riesgos de la red social Threads que los usuarios ignoran

The article addresses growing concerns about privacy and the protection of personal data on emerging social platforms such as Threads, following their rapid user growth. It highlights that, although companies promise anonymity, there is a real risk of creating detailed digital profiles that identify users’ behavior and preferences, information that is often abusively used to benefit third parties. This dynamic reveals that the responsibility for security largely falls on users, who frequently accept lengthy terms and conditions without understanding the implications of fully ceding their data. The relevance of this issue for the open data community lies in the fundamental tension between the business model based on the mass collection of personal information and the principles of transparency and protection of digital rights. The analysis shows that the “freedom” of expression on these platforms often masks an aggressive collection of sensitive metadata, challenging initiatives that seek to promote citizens’ data sovereignty. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for developing regulatory and technological frameworks that balance innovation with privacy, ensuring that data is not exploited without explicit consent. Finally, the text underscores the urgent need for digital education and for demanding fairer, more understandable terms and conditions. Users must take active responsibility for protecting their privacy by avoiding the sharing of sensitive information and questioning the legitimacy of platforms that operate under the premise that “the user is the product.” This critical stance is essential to foster a more ethical digital ecosystem, where transparency is not merely a technical option but a legal and social obligation that prioritizes people’s dignity and security over commercial interests.
