La Diputación de Cuenca es la administración local que más rápido paga a sus proveedores con una media de 4,29 días

The Provincial Council of Cuenca achieved the fastest payment rate among local administrations, significantly outperforming the general local average. This exceptional speed demonstrates a strong commitment to supporting businesses by ensuring rapid cash flow, which helps companies plan their activities more effectively. This case is relevant to open data as it highlights how public datasets can benchmark administrative efficiency. By making payment speeds transparent, open data fosters accountability and encourages other institutions to adopt similar agile financial practices to better serve their economic communities.
