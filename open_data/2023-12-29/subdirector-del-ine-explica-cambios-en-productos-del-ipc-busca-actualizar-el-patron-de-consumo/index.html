Subdirector del INE explica cambios en productos del IPC: busca "actualizar el patrón de consumo"

The National Statistics Institute (INE) updated the Consumer Price Index (CPI) basket to reflect current consumption patterns, in line with OECD standards. This methodological shift ensures statistical accuracy by removing obsolete items and adding new products, such as plant-based meat substitutes. Such transparency in updating economic indicators is essential for open data, as it enables citizens and researchers to access reliable, evolving metrics. Accurate data frameworks empower informed public scrutiny and support evidence-based policy analysis. Ultimately, the revision illustrates how statistical agencies maintain relevance through continuous methodological refinement. This commitment to data quality highlights the importance of accessible, well-documented public statistics for fostering trust and facilitating comprehensive societal analysis.
