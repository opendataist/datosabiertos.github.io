Angel tax: 8,204 start-ups have received exemption till date, shows govt data

Government data reveals that only a small fraction of eligible startups have received exemptions from the angel tax, despite over ten thousand applications. This significant gap suggests that while regulatory frameworks exist to encourage innovation, the actual uptake remains low, highlighting potential administrative hurdles or awareness gaps within the startup ecosystem. The exemption applies to entities recognized under specific criteria, focusing on innovation and employment potential rather than just financial metrics. Although the tax burden extends to non-resident investors, recent directives confirm that recognized startups are largely shielded from scrutiny, aiming to simplify compliance and reduce administrative burdens for eligible businesses. This situation is crucial for open data advocates because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible government information. Analyzing such datasets allows researchers and the public to assess policy effectiveness, identify implementation disparities, and hold authorities accountable, thereby fostering a more informed and responsive regulatory environment for emerging enterprises.
