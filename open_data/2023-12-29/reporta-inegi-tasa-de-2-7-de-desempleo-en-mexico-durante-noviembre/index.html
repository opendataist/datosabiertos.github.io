Reporta Inegi tasa de 2.7% de desempleo en México durante noviembre

Mexico’s unemployment rate remains stable at 2.7%, reflecting a slight decrease from last year. While overall employment grew, the rise in underemployment signals lingering labor market instability for many workers. This data highlights the complexity of measuring economic health beyond simple job counts. The gap between employed and fully employed individuals reveals hidden vulnerabilities in the workforce that standard metrics often overlook. Understanding these nuances is vital for open data initiatives. Transparent access to detailed labor statistics enables better analysis of economic trends, fostering more informed policy decisions and public accountability regarding workforce welfare.
