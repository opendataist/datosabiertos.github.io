Osiptel defiende dictamen aprobado por el Congreso para acceder a datos personales | RPP Noticias

Rafael Muente, president of Osiptel, clarified that the recent congressional ruling does not grant new powers to access personal data, but rather automates an existing process. The current Personal Data Protection Law, in force for over a decade, already allows the regulator to collect specific information, such as service costs and duration, which is essential for verifying fair rates and protecting users. This technological update streamlines data requests and data flow, improving oversight efficiency. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the delicate balance between regulatory transparency and individual privacy. It demonstrates how access to structured and specific data, without exposing personal identity, enables public agencies to audit companies and sanction bad practices. Thus, it fosters an ecosystem where accountability is sustained through accurate data, ensuring both consumer protection and respect for information protection standards.
