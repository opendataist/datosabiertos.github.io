La constructora que bloquea la residencia de Son Dureta está vinculada con el caso Koldo

The article highlights a concerning pattern in which a construction firm, currently under investigation for alleged illicit commissions linked to political figures, secured significant public contracts. This raises critical questions about transparency and integrity in public procurement processes, particularly regarding how such contracts are awarded without apparent irregularities in some cases. Financial difficulties plagued the company, leading to work stoppages and complaints from subcontractors regarding unpaid services. Despite initial positive progress, the eventual halt of major public works projects demonstrates how corporate instability can severely disrupt the delivery of essential public infrastructure and strain governmental resources. This situation is highly relevant to open data, as it underscores the urgent need for transparent, accessible, and real-time monitoring of public contracting and financial statuses. By making bidding processes, contract execution, and corporate financial health publicly available, authorities and citizens can better detect potential corruption, irregularities, and systemic risks in public spending.
