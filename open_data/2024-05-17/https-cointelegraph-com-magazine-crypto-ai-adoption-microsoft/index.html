The article explores the symbiotic potential of artificial intelligence and blockchain, suggesting that decentralized networks can serve as critical infrastructure for the future of AI. Rather than competing directly, blockchain is positioned to support AI through distributed computing resources and enhanced data privacy mechanisms. This convergence allows for more robust, scalable AI ecosystems that leverage the transparency and immutability inherent in open ledger technologies, creating a foundation for trustworthy automated systems. Open data is particularly relevant here because blockchain provides a verifiable audit trail for AI decision-making, addressing the "black box" problem of opaque algorithms. By integrating immutable records with data usage, open blockchain frameworks can ensure accountability and regulatory compliance without compromising user privacy. This transparency is essential for building public trust in AI systems and facilitating the ethical sharing of information, which is a core tenet of the open data movement. Furthermore, the discussion highlights the shift toward smaller, efficient models that operate at the edge, reducing reliance on centralized giants. This democratization of compute power aligns with open data principles by making AI capabilities more accessible and less controlled by a few major corporations. As privacy-enhancing technologies like fully homomorphic encryption mature, they will further empower open data initiatives by enabling secure, privacy-preserving analytics, ensuring that data remains both useful and protected in an AI-driven world.
