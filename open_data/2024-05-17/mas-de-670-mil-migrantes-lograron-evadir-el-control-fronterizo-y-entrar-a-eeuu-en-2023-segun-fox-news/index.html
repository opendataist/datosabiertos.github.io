Más de 670 mil migrantes lograron evadir el control fronterizo y entrar a EEUU en 2023, según Fox News

The article reveals that hundreds of thousands of undocumented migrants successfully evaded U.S. border authorities in 2023, marking a significant security challenge. Despite the deployment of record-breaking surveillance technology along the border, a substantial number of individuals managed to bypass inspections, indicating that technological improvements alone do not guarantee effective border control or enforcement of immigration laws. Data reliability has improved significantly due to systematic recording methods and electronic monitoring, allowing for more accurate tracking of these “escape” incidents. This transparency highlights the gap between official containment capabilities and actual border crossings, suggesting that current strategies may need comprehensive reassessment to address the root causes of evasion rather than relying solely on detection metrics. This data is crucial for the open-data community, as it underscores the importance of accessing government-held statistics through transparency mechanisms such as the Freedom of Information Act. By making such granular enforcement data publicly available, citizens and researchers can independently verify claims, analyze policy effectiveness, and hold agencies accountable for managing border security outcomes.
