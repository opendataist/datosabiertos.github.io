Estas son las actividades que se tendrán en la Semana de Gobierno Abierto 2024 | La Opción de Chihuahua

Estas son las actividades que se tendrán en la Semana de Gobierno Abierto 2024 | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua hosts its International Open Government Week to advance transparency and civic engagement through new technologies. This event aligns with the global Open Government Partnership goals, fostering accountability and collaborative participation between authorities and citizens. Key initiatives include launching open data policies, training officials in inclusive language, and presenting the new Open Government Portal. These efforts emphasize the critical role of accessible information in building trust and ensuring transparent governance practices within the municipality. The initiative is relevant to open data as it demonstrates a municipal commitment to publishing actionable government information. By establishing open data policies and utilizing digital platforms, the government exemplifies how structured data release empowers citizens and supports democratic oversight in the public sector.
