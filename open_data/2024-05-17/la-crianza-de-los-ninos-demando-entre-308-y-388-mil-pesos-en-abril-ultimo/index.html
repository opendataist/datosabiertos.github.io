La crianza de los niños demandó entre 308 y 388 mil pesos en abril último

INDEC has released open data detailing the rising costs of raising children, revealing significant price increases in essential goods and care services. This transparent disclosure enables researchers and the public to analyze the economic impacts on families using freely available statistical information. The study highlights varying expenses based on the child’s age, demonstrating how inflation disproportionately affects different life stages. By publishing this granular data, institutions foster accountability and enable independent verification of reported inflation rates, a cornerstone of open government principles. Ultimately, making these cost indices accessible supports informed policy-making and public debate regarding social welfare. Open data empowers citizens to understand their financial realities, ensuring that economic statistics serve as tools for transparency rather than opaque metrics, thereby strengthening democratic engagement.
