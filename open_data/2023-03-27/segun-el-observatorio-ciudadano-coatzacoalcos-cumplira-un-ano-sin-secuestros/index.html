Según el Observatorio Ciudadano, Coatzacoalcos cumplirá un año sin secuestros

Coatzacoalcos is approaching a full year without kidnappings, marking a significant shift from previous years. This progress stems from specialized state units and stricter national laws, highlighting how transparent, open data monitoring can validate public safety improvements. The reduction in crime is attributed to coordinated legal frameworks and specialized investigative bodies. Citizens are encouraged to maintain trust in authorities, as reporting remains crucial for sustaining these positive trends and ensuring accountability through accessible information channels. This achievement underscores the vital role of open data in verifying governmental efficacy. By making security statistics publicly available, organizations empower communities to understand policy impacts, fostering confidence in institutions while providing essential metrics for civil society to track and support continuous governance reforms.
