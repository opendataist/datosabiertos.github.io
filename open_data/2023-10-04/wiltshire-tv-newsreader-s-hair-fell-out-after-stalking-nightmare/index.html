The article highlights the severe, life-altering psychological trauma victims endure from stalking, which persists even when legal consequences fail to incapacitate offenders. This narrative is critically relevant to open data as it illustrates how public accountability and transparency can serve as powerful deterrents. Ultimately, the piece argues that transparency in offender histories is essential for victim recovery and public safety. It suggests that open data initiatives, when designed ethically, can fill gaps in current legal frameworks.
