Factory activity in China shows first increase in six months

Chinese factory activity expanded for the first time in six months, signaling economic stabilization after the post-pandemic reopening. This positive trend, combined with easing deflationary pressures, suggests the economy is gradually bottoming out despite ongoing weaknesses in the property sector. The recovery is crucial for policymakers aiming to meet growth targets amid international market concerns caused by property debts. While the Asian Development Bank slightly lowered its growth forecast, analysts believe further fiscal support may be necessary to sustain momentum toward the government's ambitious economic goals. This data is relevant to open_data because accurate, timely public statistics are essential for monitoring economic health. Transparent access to indicators like the PMI allows researchers, businesses, and citizens to verify official narratives, understand market trends, and hold authorities accountable for the effectiveness of fiscal and monetary policies.
