LA CDMX TIENE EL PORTAL DE DATOS ABIERTOS MÁS GRANDE DE AMÉRICA LATINA

The Mexico City Open Data Portal stands out as the largest in Latin America, distinguished by its unique status as a free, locally developed platform. Unlike other major global cities, it offers interactive geographic visualizations and advanced filtering tools that empower citizens to actively explore, analyze, and collaborate with government data. This design fosters true democratization of information, enabling users to compare their own datasets with official statistics and engage meaningfully with public institutions. Relevant to open data, this initiative demonstrates the critical importance of user-centric design and local innovation in data governance. By prioritizing accessibility and interactive features, the portal exemplifies how open data can transcend mere publication to become a tool for civic participation. Its international recognition highlights that successful open data strategies require more than transparency; they demand technological independence and interfaces that invite public interaction, setting a benchmark for effective digital democracy.
