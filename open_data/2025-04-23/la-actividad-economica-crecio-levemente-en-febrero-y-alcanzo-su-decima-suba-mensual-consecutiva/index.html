La actividad económica creció levemente en febrero y alcanzó su décima suba mensual consecutiva

Argentina’s economy shows sustained growth, marking ten consecutive months of improvement. Financial intermediation and fishing drive significant annual gains, while most sectors expand. This positive trend suggests a robust recovery trajectory since mid-2022. The narrative highlights broad-based activity increases, though services like hospitality face slight declines. Political leaders emphasize this as a record high in deserialized series, reinforcing confidence in the current economic stabilization policy’s effectiveness over recent years. This data is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how official statistical institutions can provide transparent, high-frequency indicators to monitor real-time economic health. Reliable, accessible datasets enable researchers and citizens to track policy impacts accurately, fostering accountability and informed public discourse about national development.
