Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The article highlights a comprehensive dataset on global potato production, detailing outputs across one hundred nations using information from the Food and Agriculture Organization’s statistical database. This resource offers a standardized, worldwide perspective on agricultural yields, identifying China and India as the dominant producers while providing a broad view of the crop’s international distribution. The narrative emphasizes the accessibility and utility of this open agricultural data for researchers and analysts who need reliable baselines for cross-country comparisons. By extracting specific production figures, the text demonstrates how users can independently verify trends and understand the scale of potato cultivation, noting that while local government reports may vary slightly, the global figures provide sufficient accuracy for most analytical purposes. This resource is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it showcases the value of standardized, publicly available datasets in democratizing access to critical agricultural statistics. It illustrates how centralized repositories enable transparent monitoring of global food security metrics, allowing stakeholders to analyze production patterns and efficiency without relying solely on fragmented national reports, thereby supporting evidence-based decision-making in the food system.
