Datos personales: antes de dar aceptar, piensa en tu privacidad | Universidad de los Andes

Datos personales: antes de dar aceptar, piensa en tu privacidad | Universidad de los Andes

The article highlights that personal data security relies heavily on individual responsibility and institutional vigilance. It identifies critical user errors, such as sharing excessive personal information on social media, using weak passwords, ignoring privacy terms, and neglecting software updates. These behaviors expose users to unauthorized access and misuse, underscoring the urgent need for better digital hygiene to prevent personal data breaches. Regarding the handling of others' data, the text warns against spreading misinformation, sharing private information without consent, and using personal data for unauthorized commercial purposes. These actions constitute serious violations of privacy rights and can lead to significant legal consequences, including civil, administrative, and criminal sanctions. The article emphasizes that protecting the integrity and confidentiality of information is a fundamental right enshrined in the constitution, requiring strict adherence to legal frameworks to avoid harming individuals' reputations and rights. From an open data perspective, this discussion is relevant because it illustrates the tension between data utility and privacy protection. While open data initiatives aim for transparency and accessibility, they must coexist with robust legal frameworks, such as habeas data, that protect individual privacy. The article stresses that as technology evolves with AI and big data, legislation must continuously adapt to balance these interests. This highlights the importance of developing open data standards that respect legal boundaries and ethical considerations, ensuring that data openness does not come at the cost of personal rights and security.
