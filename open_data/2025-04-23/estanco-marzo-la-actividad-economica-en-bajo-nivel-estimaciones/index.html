"Estancó" marzo la actividad económica en bajo nivel: estimaciones

The INEGI released the IOAE, providing timely estimates of economic activity that show minimal monthly growth and a slight annual decline. This data reveals divergent trends between the secondary and tertiary sectors, highlighting immediate shifts in production and services. Such rapid and accessible metrics are vital for open data initiatives, enabling stakeholders to monitor economic health without having to wait for final quarterly reports.
