Actividad económica en México creció 0% en marzo

Actividad económica en México creció 0% en marzo

Mexico’s economic activity stagnated in March, revealing sectoral weakness triggered by global uncertainty surrounding U.S. tariffs. While monthly figures showed no growth, the broader context highlights a fragile economy struggling under external pressures. This slowdown contrasts with earlier optimism, signaling that immediate resilience is being tested by international trade tensions and shifting geopolitical dynamics. The analysis points to significant challenges in key industries, particularly manufacturing and services, where business confidence has deteriorated. Concerns about future investment and exports suggest that the current pause in growth may persist if trade policies remain volatile. The interplay between domestic performance and external shocks underscores the vulnerability of Mexico’s economic structure to foreign policy decisions.
