More Aussies than ever are working two jobs

Rising living costs are driving more Australians to hold multiple jobs, reflecting a strategic adaptation to financial pressure rather than just economic opportunity. This trend highlights the growing precarity of modern employment, where individuals must juggle several roles to maintain basic stability. For open data, this surge underscores the need for granular labor metrics that capture informal and secondary work arrangements. Policymakers and researchers must accurately track these complex employment patterns to effectively address economic inequality and the widening gap between wages and essential costs.
