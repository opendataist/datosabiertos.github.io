Privacy Policy - Daily Capital

Daily Capital demonstrates a minimal data collection model by refusing to operate user-facing forms, meaning it does not directly gather personal identifiers like names or emails. This approach significantly reduces the site's direct privacy liability, as it avoids maintaining user databases or profiling individuals. Instead, the platform relies on automated infrastructure logs for security and operational maintenance, retaining this limited data only for brief periods before anonymization. This highlights a viable strategy for small publications to operate transparently while limiting their own data stewardship burdens. However, the site’s privacy footprint expands significantly through its use of third-party advertising services, specifically Google AdSense. While the publisher remains passive, these external partners collect extensive behavioral data, including IP addresses, device identifiers, and browsing history, to deliver personalized advertisements. This separation of duties means that although the site itself respects user anonymity, the actual data harvesting and monetization are outsourced to large tech entities that govern their own privacy policies and retention practices. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the complex reality of data minimization in the public interest. It shows how transparency about third-party dependencies is crucial for understanding true data flows, even when a primary source claims minimal collection. For open data advocates, it underscores the importance of distinguishing between direct data ownership and indirect exposure through embedded services, encouraging a broader view of privacy that accounts for the entire digital ecosystem rather than just the publisher’s immediate controls.
