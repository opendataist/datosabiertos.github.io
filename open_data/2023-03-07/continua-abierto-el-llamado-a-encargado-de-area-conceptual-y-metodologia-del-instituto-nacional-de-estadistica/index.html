Continúa abierto el llamado a Encargado de área conceptual y metodología, del Instituto Nacional de Estadística

This recruitment underscores the INE’s commitment to strengthening the conceptual and methodological rigor of national censuses, which are foundational to producing high-quality, standardized statistics. By prioritizing specialists in the social sciences and economics, the initiative highlights the critical need for robust methodological frameworks in open government data. The role requires verifying compliance and coordinating teams, ensuring that the resulting datasets are reliable and suitable for public use. This approach professionalizes open data production, moving beyond mere publication to ensure methodological integrity and accuracy. Ultimately, this appointment reflects a broader trend in which statistical agencies actively seek specialized expertise to enhance transparency. It demonstrates that credible open data demands continuous investment in skilled human capital to maintain public trust and data utility.
