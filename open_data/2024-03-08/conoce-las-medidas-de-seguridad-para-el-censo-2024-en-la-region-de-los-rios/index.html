Conoce las medidas de seguridad para el Censo 2024 en la región de Los Ríos

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) has launched a census operation in the Los Ríos region, emphasizing security through uniformed agents with verifiable digital credentials and a mobile-first data collection process. Crucially, enumerators will not enter homes to protect both staff and residents' safety while gathering comprehensive demographic information via mobile devices. This approach is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how modern statistical agencies can leverage technology and strict privacy protocols to ensure transparent, secure, and high-quality public data collection.
