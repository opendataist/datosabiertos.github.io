GPT-4 de OpenAI es el modelo de IA que más infringe derechos de autor

The study reveals that major AI models consistently generate copyrighted content, highlighting a systemic failure to respect intellectual property rights during both training and output generation. This pervasive issue fuels significant legal battles, such as the lawsuit between The New York Times and OpenAI, which threatens tech giants with massive financial penalties. This issue is highly relevant to open data, given the urgent need for transparent and legally compliant data sourcing practices. As AI development relies heavily on existing content, ensuring clear distinctions between protected works and those in the public domain is crucial for fostering sustainable innovation and ethical data usage within the open ecosystem.
