Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has launched an enhanced Data Portal for the Ibrahim Index of African Governance, offering an interactive platform to analyze public governance across all 54 African nations. This update introduces dynamic tools like detailed country profiles and measure pages, enabling users to visualize trends and compare performance against continental averages over the last decade. For open data practitioners, this initiative demonstrates how complex governance metrics can be made accessible through user-friendly digital interfaces. By allowing stakeholders to drill down from broad categories to specific sub-indicators, the portal facilitates deeper investigative journalism and informed civic engagement. It transforms raw statistical data into actionable insights that highlight both progress and deterioration in public service delivery. This resource is vital for open data because it establishes a standardized, comprehensive benchmark for accountability in African public governance. By providing transparent, comparable historical data, it empowers civil society and researchers to hold governments responsible. Ultimately, such platforms strengthen democratic oversight by making critical information on rights, rule of law, and economic opportunity readily available to citizens and organizations alike.
