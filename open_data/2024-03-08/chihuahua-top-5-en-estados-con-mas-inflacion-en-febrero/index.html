Chihuahua, top 5 en estados con más inflación en febrero

Chihuahua ranked fifth nationally for high inflation, highlighting significant regional disparities in Mexico's consumer prices. This variance underscores the importance of geographically granular economic data for accurate policy analysis. Such localized metrics are vital for open data initiatives, enabling precise tracking of cost-of-living impacts across diverse communities rather than relying solely on national averages.
