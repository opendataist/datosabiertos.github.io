NITI Aayog launches central data repository for states

The NITI for States portal centralizes vast government knowledge, including datasets, policies, and best practices, to facilitate data-driven governance. This initiative aims to accelerate digital transformation by providing officials with actionable insights and user-friendly tools for better decision-making across diverse sectors. By fostering cooperative federalism, the platform encourages cross-learning among states and union territories. It empowers frontline administrators to replicate successful local initiatives and address specific regional challenges effectively, thereby strengthening overall governance practices through shared knowledge and collaborative problem-solving. This resource is vital for open data as it demonstrates how structured, interoperable information can bridge administrative gaps. It highlights the importance of accessible, localized data repositories in enhancing transparency and enabling evidence-based policy implementation at the grassroots level, ultimately promoting inclusive and efficient public service delivery.
