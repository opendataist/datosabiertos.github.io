"Treason!": Bombshell report reveals Biden has secretly flown...

The Biden administration has flown hundreds of thousands of migrants into the U.S. using a scheduling app, bypassing traditional border crossings. This covert policy allows individuals without legal entry rights to receive temporary parole and work authorization, dispersing them across dozens of cities rather than processing them at the southern border. Government agencies initially withheld data about these operations, citing law enforcement concerns about resource allocation and potential safety risks. However, public records requests revealed the scale and nature of the program, highlighting a significant lack of transparency regarding how immigration enforcement resources are deployed and managed. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical role of transparency in holding executive agencies accountable. When governments obscure operational details under legal exceptions, public oversight is hindered. Accessible, comprehensive data is essential for understanding policy impacts, ensuring democratic scrutiny of administrative actions, and preventing the concealment of controversial practices from civic engagement.
