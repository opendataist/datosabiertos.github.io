La mitad de las alemanas trabaja a tiempo parcial y siguen cobrando menos que los hombres - Mundo - ABC Color

The article highlights that although the number of working women in Germany increased in 2023, this growth was driven by foreign nationals rather than German citizens. It underscores significant structural inequalities, particularly the high rate of part-time work among women and the persistent gender pay gap. Furthermore, women remain underrepresented in leadership roles, with their presence in management positions stagnant for years. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it exposes systemic labor disparities, enabling researchers to analyze trends in gender equity and inform policies aimed at reducing economic inequalities.
