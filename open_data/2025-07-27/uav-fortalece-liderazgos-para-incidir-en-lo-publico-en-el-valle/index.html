UAV fortalece liderazgos para incidir en lo público en el Valle

UAV fortalece liderazgos para incidir en lo público en el Valle

The Vallecaucana Action Unit strengthens civic leadership through training and digital tools, addressing the gap between the complexity of public management and citizens. The organization identifies organic leaders through their territorial proximity, facilitating access to educational and cultural opportunities. This enables various social actors, from young people to foundations, to develop capacities to influence public affairs and build collective purpose, transcending traditional political cycles. In the realm of open data, the entity promotes citizen autonomy by disseminating accessible public information through web platforms and data dashboards. Initiatives like "Data that Makes a City" allow for the review of indicators on security and mobility, facilitating a direct relationship between the community and information without intermediaries. This transparency empowers the population to understand and monitor public contracting and projects funded with state resources, reducing the barrier that often prevents access to technical data. This approach is relevant to the open data community because it demonstrates how the publication of open data is integrated into governance strategies to foster real participation. By simplifying access to information, it mitigates the stigmatization of local leaders and strengthens accountability. The initiative illustrates that data are not just statistical resources, but essential tools for building an informed citizenry and ensuring that public decisions reflect the real needs of the territory.
