Queremos ser una Policía cercana: DSPM

Queremos ser una Policía cercana: DSPM

Recent surveys indicate a worrying increase in the perception of insecurity in the Lagunera region, particularly in Torreón, even though overall levels remain below the national average. This upward trend has led municipal authorities to acknowledge the challenge and commit to reversing negative indicators through immediate policy adjustments and strengthened operational strategies. The local government is actively implementing a police proximity strategy to rebuild citizen trust and improve perceptions of safety. By maintaining a constant, visible presence in neighborhoods, authorities aim to demonstrate their commitment to public service. This approach seeks to transform the security landscape by making law enforcement more accessible and reliable for the community. This case is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical role of public opinion metrics in guiding governance. It shows how transparent statistical data can trigger accountability, compelling officials to adapt their strategies based on measurable citizen sentiment rather than relying solely on crime statistics. Moreover, the emphasis on increasing citizen reporting underscores the symbiotic relationship between community engagement and data quality, which is essential for effective, evidence-based public safety management.
