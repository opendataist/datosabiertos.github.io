Alzó noviembre turismo en México; 3.4 millones de turistas: Inegi

The INEGI data reveals a significant increase in international visitors and foreign currency inflows, highlighting Mexico's growing appeal as a tourist destination. Despite a slight decrease in average spending per traveler, the sheer volume of tourists ensures higher total revenue. This trend underscores the importance of open data in monitoring economic impacts and guiding strategic tourism policies for sustainable growth.
