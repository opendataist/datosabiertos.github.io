Publication of NI Cancer Waiting Times Statistics: July - September 2023

This report highlights declining performance in Northern Ireland’s cancer services, with a sharp drop in patients seen within target times for urgent breast cancer referrals. While first definitive treatment initiation increased slightly, adherence to the thirty-one-day treatment target remained stable yet below previous yearly levels. These statistics underscore the critical importance of open data in healthcare transparency. By publicly releasing detailed national statistics, authorities enable independent analysis of systemic delays and resource allocation efficiency. Such accessible information allows researchers and citizens to hold health services accountable. It facilitates broader scrutiny of patient care quality, ensuring that trends in waiting times are visible and subject to public discourse for improved health outcomes.
