La existencia del INAI no está en duda ni puede estarlo

The INAI reaffirms its indispensable role in Mexico’s democratic framework, emphasizing that transparency institutions cannot be diminished. Commissioners stress that relying on public entities’ self-regulation for information disclosure is insufficient; instead, a strong, independent authority is necessary to protect citizens’ right to know. This stance underscores that an informed society is vital for national development and the advancement of knowledge. This institutional defense is directly relevant to open data. By rejecting arbitrary withholding of information and demanding accountability from the Senate regarding the appointment of commissioners, the INAI ensures the structural integrity required to enforce open data standards. This political stability is crucial for maintaining consistent and reliable access to public data. The article also highlights the institute’s proactive engagement with political precampaigns. The INAI’s willingness to dialogue with various political factions demonstrates its commitment to serving all segments of society. This openness suggests that open data initiatives are prioritized across party lines, fostering a transparent environment essential for credible elections and public trust. Ultimately, the institution positions itself as a steadfast guardian of information rights against potential political pressures.
