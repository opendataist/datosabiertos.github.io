Buscan garantizar paridad en integración del consejo consultivo de Cegaip

Buscan garantizar paridad en integración del consejo consultivo de Cegaip

San Luis Potosí has approved legislative reforms to ensure gender parity and non-discriminatory language in public institutions. By replacing gender-equality concepts with strict gender parity in the transparency commission’s advisory council, the state mandates equal representation for decision-making roles. This shift aims to substantively include women in public power structures, aligning local laws with constitutional mandates and international human rights commitments for equitable participation. Additionally, the legal framework eliminates the generic masculine and adopts neutral terminology like "person" in official appointments. This linguistic change reinforces the principle of non-discrimination within state agencies, ensuring that institutional language reflects true inclusivity. These adjustments are critical for maintaining the integrity and accessibility of public administration, signaling a robust commitment to eliminating systemic biases in governmental operations and representation. These changes are highly relevant to open data initiatives as they standardize the terminology used in public records and institutional descriptions. Clear, inclusive language improves data quality and reduces ambiguity in administrative datasets. For developers and researchers, this ensures that data extraction processes align with current legal standards, fostering a more equitable digital infrastructure that accurately represents the diverse population served by these transparency mechanisms.
