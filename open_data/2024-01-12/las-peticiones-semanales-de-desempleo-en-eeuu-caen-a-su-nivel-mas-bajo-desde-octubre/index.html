Las peticiones semanales de desempleo en EEUU caen a su nivel más bajo desde octubre

US jobless claims fell below forecasts, signaling a resilient labor market with minimal layoffs. This stability suggests continued positive employment growth, granting the Federal Reserve flexibility to consider rate cuts if inflation persists in its downward trend. The data highlights a contrast between seasonal volatility and underlying economic strength, reassuring markets about sustained job creation despite potential moderation. This case illustrates the critical role of open data in macroeconomic analysis. Transparent, timely government statistics enable experts to model trends accurately, informing monetary policy decisions that impact global financial stability.
