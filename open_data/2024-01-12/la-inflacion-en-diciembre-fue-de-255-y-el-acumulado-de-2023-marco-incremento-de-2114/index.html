La inflación en diciembre fue de 25,5% y el acumulado de 2023 marcó incremento de 211,4%

Argentina’s December inflation reached significant levels, underscoring the ongoing economic volatility under the new administration. While President Milei characterized the actual figures as a relative success compared to early-month projections, the data confirms that the government has only partially broken the previous inflationary momentum. Consequently, experts warn that high inflationary pressures persist, with prices expected to continue rising in the near term, reflecting a difficult transitional period for the economy. This context highlights critical challenges in managing monetary policy and restoring consumer confidence amid currency devaluation and economic uncertainty. The gap between official reports and market expectations underscores the complexity of stabilizing prices during a structural economic adjustment. As the country navigates this phase, the divergence between governmental narratives and independent economic indicators remains a key focus for understanding the true impact of recent policy decisions. For open-data advocates, this article illustrates the vital importance of transparent, accessible, and timely statistical release mechanisms. It demonstrates how raw data enables civil society, journalists, and researchers to independently verify official claims and assess policy effectiveness. By facilitating the dissemination of official metrics alongside independent analyses, open data empowers the public to form informed judgments about economic realities, ensuring accountability and reducing information asymmetry in democratic societies.
