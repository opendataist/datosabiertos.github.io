Crimes in Delhi railway stations rise by 105% in two years, says RTI reply

A recent RTI disclosure reveals a alarming surge in criminal activities and fatalities on Delhi railway premises, highlighting severe safety and security failures. The data indicates that violent crimes, thefts, and touting offenses have escalated dramatically over three years, signaling a deteriorating public safety environment for commuters. This trend underscores the urgent need for enhanced surveillance and stricter enforcement mechanisms to protect passengers and maintain order in high-traffic transit hubs. The rising number of run-over incidents resulting in deaths and injuries further emphasizes the critical lack of infrastructure and operational oversight. These statistics serve as a stark warning about the systemic vulnerabilities within the railway network, where inadequate protective measures directly threaten human life. Addressing these gaps is essential to prevent further tragedy and ensure that public transportation remains a secure mode of travel for the general populace. This article is highly relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the transformative power of transparency in driving public accountability. By leveraging the Right to Information Act, citizens can access granular government data that would otherwise remain hidden, fostering informed public discourse. It illustrates how accessible data empowers activists to expose institutional neglect, thereby compelling authorities to address critical service deficiencies and improve governance through evidence-based policy interventions.
