Chocolate maker Cadbury is fighting AI by injecting gibberish into training data

Chocolate maker Cadbury is fighting AI by injecting gibberish into training data

Cadbury India launched a provocative campaign to challenge the relentless integration of AI in the workplace. The initiative encourages users to flood the internet with meaningless content, aiming to degrade the quality of training data. This strategy seeks to induce errors in AI systems, theoretically forcing humans to slow down and reclaim time previously lost to algorithmic efficiency. The campaign highlights growing anxieties about AI increasing, rather than decreasing, workload. While some studies suggest AI boosts productivity, many workers report added pressure from managing these tools. By intentionally confusing algorithms with gibberish, the brand critiques the assumption that automation inherently benefits employees, emphasizing the human cost of constant digital acceleration. This stunt is relevant to open data as it directly attacks the transparency and integrity of publicly available training sets. It underscores the vulnerability of open datasets to manipulation and raises questions about data sovereignty. Furthermore, it reflects broader concerns about unacknowledged data extraction, urging a reevaluation of how open information is sourced, used, and protected in the age of generative AI.
