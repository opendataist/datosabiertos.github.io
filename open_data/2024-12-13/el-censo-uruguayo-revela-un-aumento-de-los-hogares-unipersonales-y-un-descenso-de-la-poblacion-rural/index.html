El censo uruguayo revela un aumento de los hogares unipersonales y un descenso de la población rural

El censo uruguayo revela un aumento de los hogares unipersonales y un descenso de la población rural

The 2023 Uruguayan census reveals significant demographic shifts, including population aging and declining fertility rates. Deaths now outnumber births, resulting in negative natural population growth, while the population increasingly concentrates in urban areas. Household structures have evolved toward smaller, more solitary living arrangements, reflecting profound societal transformations over the past century. These findings are crucial for open data initiatives because they demonstrate how transparent, accessible demographic statistics empower policymakers to address complex social challenges. By making such detailed census data publicly available, governments enable researchers and citizens to analyze trends and advocate for effective public policies in healthcare, urban planning, and social security. Ultimately, the release of this definitive data underscores the vital role of open government in fostering informed civic engagement. It allows for a deeper understanding of how societies change, ensuring that decisions are based on accurate, real-time information rather than assumptions, thereby promoting more responsive and equitable governance.
