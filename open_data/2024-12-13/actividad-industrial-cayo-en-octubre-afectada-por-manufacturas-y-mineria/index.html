Actividad industrial cayó en octubre, afectada por manufacturas y minería

Mexico’s industrial activity contracted in October due to declines in manufacturing and mining, which outweighed marginal gains in construction and utilities. This negative trend, marking the second consecutive year of annual decline, highlights structural weaknesses in key economic sectors despite seasonal adjustments. This article is relevant to open data as it relies on statistical metrics from INEGI, underscoring the critical role of transparent, regularly updated public datasets. Accessible official statistics enable accurate economic monitoring and policy analysis, demonstrating how open data drives informed decision-making and accountability in understanding national industrial performance.
