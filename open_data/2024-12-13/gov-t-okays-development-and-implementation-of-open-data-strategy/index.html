Gov't okays development and implementation of Open Data Strategy

Gov't okays development and implementation of Open Data Strategy

Romania has approved an Open Data Strategy aimed at fostering economic growth, innovation, and research. This initiative seeks to establish the country as a regional digital leader and support its goal of joining the OECD by leveraging data as a critical resource for modern business and science. By treating data as raw material for insights, the strategy aims to expand services and strengthen citizen trust in digital public tools. The move aligns with broader European trends where open data drives substantial economic value, demonstrating its vital role in sustainable development. This is highly relevant to open data as it highlights a government commitment to institutionalizing data transparency. It underscores the global shift toward recognizing open data not just as a technical asset, but as a foundational driver for economic opportunity and scientific advancement in the public sector.
