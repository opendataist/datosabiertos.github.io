NSO India releases the "Compendium of Datasets and Registries in India, 2024"

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation has launched a comprehensive compendium to enhance data accessibility and support informed decision-making in India. This resource is vital for open data initiatives as it consolidates metadata for hundreds of datasets from various government ministries, making them readily available to researchers, policymakers, and the public. By providing standardized details on data collection methods and sharing policies, the compendium simplifies the exploration of government information. It serves as a single reference point that ensures seamless access to diverse sectors, including health, education, and agriculture, thereby fostering deeper analysis and evidence-based policymaking across the country. Designed as a dynamic document, the compendium will be periodically updated to reflect new datasets and evolving methodologies. This commitment to continuous modernization strengthens the National Statistical System, ensuring stakeholders have access to the most current information for national development and effective utilization of government data.
