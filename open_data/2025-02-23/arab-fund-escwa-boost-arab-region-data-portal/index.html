Arab Fund, ESCWA Boost Arab Region Data Portal

Arab Fund, ESCWA Boost Arab Region Data Portal

The Arab Fund for Economic and Social Development and ESCWA are collaborating to significantly upgrade the Arab Development Portal, enhancing its role as a critical regional data resource. This partnership focuses on integrating artificial intelligence and predictive analytics to improve the accessibility and reliability of development data across Arab countries. By providing user-friendly dashboards and robust insights, the initiative aims to strengthen data-driven decision-making for policymakers. This evidence-based approach empowers stakeholders to address complex challenges aligned with Sustainable Development Goals, including public health, economic resilience, and climate adaptation, fostering more informed strategic planning. This agreement is vital for open data as it demonstrates how institutional partnerships can modernize public knowledge platforms. By expanding access to credible datasets and building analytical capacity through training, it supports transparency and innovation, ensuring that high-quality information remains available to drive sustainable social and economic progress in the region.
