Este viernes se conoce el número de la inflación de marzo: el pronóstico de los analistas

The upcoming inflation data confirms a sustained deceleration in Argentina’s price increases, with projections centering on 12% for March. This figure represents a significant drop from previous months, validating the government’s narrative that macroeconomic pressures are easing and that the core inflation rate has stabilized at single digits. The decline is attributed to delayed tariff adjustments and softer food price expectations, highlighting how policy timing directly impacts measured inflation. Private forecasters note that while the total rate remains elevated, the weakening momentum throughout the month suggests a structural shift rather than temporary fluctuations, reducing the urgency for aggressive monetary interventions. This release is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how transparent, timely statistical reporting allows diverse stakeholders to independently verify economic trends. By making raw indices publicly available, institutions like INDEC enable researchers and citizens to analyze the drivers of inflation, fostering accountability and deeper understanding of macroeconomic health beyond official political narratives.
