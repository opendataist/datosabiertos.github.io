Kakao 1st S. Korean firm to join global open-source AI Alliance - ET Telecom

Kakao becomes the first South Korean firm to join the AI Alliance, a global consortium led by IBM and Meta. This move underscores the growing corporate commitment to open-source artificial intelligence research and development on an international scale. The initiative focuses on accelerating open innovation to enhance AI safety, security, and trust. By establishing shared benchmarks and resources, the alliance aims to foster a standardized environment for responsible AI deployment that benefits the entire global community. This development is crucial for open data as it promotes transparency and collaborative standards in AI systems. Such alignment ensures that open-source principles extend beyond code to include data practices, facilitating safer and more interoperable technology ecosystems worldwide.
