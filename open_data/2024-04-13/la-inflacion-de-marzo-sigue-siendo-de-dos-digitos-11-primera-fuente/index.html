This article highlights Argentina’s severe inflation, underscoring how price data drives economic understanding. The Consumer Price Index reveals persistent cost increases across essential sectors such as education, housing, and food, reflecting the impact of recent policy changes. Open data is crucial in this context, as transparent, official statistics enable citizens and analysts to track real purchasing power. By monitoring these indicators, society can hold institutions accountable for fiscal health and social welfare, ensuring that decisions are based on accurate information rather than speculation. Understanding these trends helps identify vulnerable groups affected by rising costs. Reliable data empowers researchers to propose effective solutions, fostering informed public debate and policy adjustments that address the root causes of economic instability in a transparent manner.
