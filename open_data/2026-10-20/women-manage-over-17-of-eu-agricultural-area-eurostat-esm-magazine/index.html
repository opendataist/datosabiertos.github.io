Women Manage Over 17% Of EU Agricultural Area – Eurostat | ESM Magazine

Women Manage Over 17% Of EU Agricultural Area – Eurostat | ESM Magazine

Women currently manage a small minority of European agricultural land, yet this landscape is shifting. Generational renewal is expected to empower women with larger, more commercially viable holdings in the near future, signaling a structural evolution in the sector’s gender dynamics. Regional disparities remain significant across the EU, with ownership patterns varying drastically between northern and southern areas. These geographical imbalances highlight how local contexts deeply influence agricultural leadership, rather than a uniform continental trend. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it exposes critical gaps in gender-based resource allocation. By making such statistics accessible, policymakers and researchers can better track progress toward equitable land distribution and support the upcoming International Year of the Woman Farmer with evidence-based strategies.
