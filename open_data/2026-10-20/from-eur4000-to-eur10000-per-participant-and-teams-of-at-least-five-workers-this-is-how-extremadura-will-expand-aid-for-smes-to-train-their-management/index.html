From €4,000 to €10,000 per participant and teams of at least five workers: this is how Extremadura will expand aid for SMEs to train their management teams

From €4,000 to €10,000 per participant and teams of at least five workers: this is how Extremadura will expand aid for SMEs to train their management teams

Extremadura has revised the guidelines for its grants supporting executive training for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), highlighting its openness to the local business community. By lowering the minimum employee threshold to five, the program now includes a critical segment of micro-enterprises that were previously excluded, thereby democratizing access to leadership development. The improved financial terms, with a ceiling of €100,000 and a higher co-financing rate, enable companies to access programs of high academic quality. This expansion removes economic and temporal barriers, facilitating greater rigor and duration in the executive training received by management teams. This update is significant for open data, as it demonstrates how transparency in the modification of public regulations directly impacts the inclusion of small businesses. Early publication on transparency portals ensures that this accessible information allows SMEs to adapt in a timely manner, fostering greater participation and equity in the use of public resources.
