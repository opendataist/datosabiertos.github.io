Affordable & Versatile: Volkswagen ID. Cross Can Now Be Ordered With A 37 kWh Battery - CleanTechnica

Affordable & Versatile: Volkswagen ID. Cross Can Now Be Ordered With A 37 kWh Battery - CleanTechnica

Volkswagen has launched pre-sales for the entry-level ID. Cross electric SUV, featuring a smaller 37 kWh battery optimized for urban commuting and daily city traffic. This model offers a WLTP range of up to 324 kilometers and supports fast DC charging, targeting customers seeking attractive pricing without sacrificing essential comfort or technology features found in higher-end variants. The vehicle maintains the brand’s signature SUV design and high-quality interior, including intuitive large displays and smartphone integration. Available in multiple equipment trims, it balances lower purchase and operating costs with robust performance capabilities, including motorway speeds up to 160 km/h. Advanced options like predictive assisted driving and premium sound systems remain accessible, ensuring the compact battery size does not compromise the overall driver experience or connectivity. This release is highly relevant to open_data initiatives, as modern vehicles like the ID. Cross generate vast amounts of operational telemetry. Understanding the hardware specifications and connectivity features, such as networked data usage for assisted driving, helps researchers and developers anticipate the volume and type of anonymized mobility data that may become available for public analysis, urban planning, and sustainable transportation modeling.
