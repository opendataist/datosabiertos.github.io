Pakistan revises FY2026 GDP growth to 3.65%, with economy valued at $450.4 billion

Pakistan revises FY2026 GDP growth to 3.65%, with economy valued at $450.4 billion

Pakistan’s National Accounts Committee revised the FY 2025–26 economic growth estimate to 3.65 percent, a marginal reduction from previous projections. This adjustment reflects a slower expansion rather than a contraction, with the economy maintaining positive growth trajectory despite slight downward corrections across various sectors. Sectoral performance shows mixed results, with agriculture outperforming expectations while industry and services lagged behind prior estimates. This divergence highlights that national output figures mask uneven distribution of growth across different economic segments. Consequently, the headline GDP rate alone provides an incomplete picture of the underlying economic dynamics. This article is vital for open data because it demonstrates how national accounts revisions update statistical assessments without altering fundamental economic conditions. It emphasizes the necessity of distinguishing aggregate output metrics from inflation, employment, and income distribution data. For open data enthusiasts, this underscores the importance of interpreting official statistics critically, recognizing that single indicators cannot fully capture complex socio-economic realities or household-level financial experiences.
