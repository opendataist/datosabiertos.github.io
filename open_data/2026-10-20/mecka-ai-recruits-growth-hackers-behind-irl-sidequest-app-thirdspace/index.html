Mecka AI recruits growth hackers behind “IRL sidequest” app Thirdspace

Mecka AI recruits growth hackers behind “IRL sidequest” app Thirdspace

Mecka AI, a company dedicated to generating the critical training data required for real-world robot deployment, has acquired Thirdspace, a popular social mobile application. This strategic move is primarily an acquihire, bringing on Thirdspace’s founders to lead Mecka’s mobile capture efforts. The acquisition underscores Mecka’s reliance on viral growth strategies to scale its user base, aiming to recruit millions of individuals who will record egocentric video for robot training. The core implication for open data in robotics is the urgent need for massive volumes of unique, first-person perspective footage. Unlike text or images, this specific type of data cannot be scraped from the internet, making organic, user-generated content essential. Mecka’s ambition to reach tens of millions of hours of footage requires sophisticated mobile engagement tools. By applying consumer-app growth tactics to a hardware-heavy data collection process, Mecka highlights a new paradigm where open data sourcing depends on engaging individual contributors rather than just scraping public web repositories. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the shifting landscape of data acquisition in the age of embodied AI. It demonstrates how open data initiatives must evolve to include active human participation and gamified contribution models to meet the exponential data demands of modern robotics. Furthermore, it signals a trend where private entities are building the essential data infrastructure for open robotics models, potentially influencing how accessible and standardized this critical training corpus becomes for the broader research and development community.
