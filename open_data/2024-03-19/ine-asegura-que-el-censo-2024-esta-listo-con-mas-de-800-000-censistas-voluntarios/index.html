INE asegura que el Censo 2024 está listo con más de 800.000 censistas voluntarios

Bolivia is fully prepared for the 2024 Population and Housing Census, driven by massive public engagement that far exceeded volunteer recruitment targets. This high level of civic participation demonstrates a strong societal commitment to national statistical processes, ensuring adequate human resources for accurate data collection across the country. The census introduces a significant digital transformation, marking the first time Bolivia utilizes fully digital methods for planning and material distribution. This technological shift enhances logistical precision and transparency, optimizing how census materials are allocated to municipalities and addressing environmental challenges through better coordination with local authorities. This development is crucial for open data as it transitions Bolivia toward modern, efficient statistical systems. Digitalizing the census infrastructure facilitates future data accessibility and reuse, aligning national practices with open data principles by improving the quality, reliability, and potential availability of demographic information for public and scientific use.
