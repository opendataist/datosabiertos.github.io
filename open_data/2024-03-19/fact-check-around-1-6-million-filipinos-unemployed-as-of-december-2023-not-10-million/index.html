FACT CHECK: Around 1.6 million Filipinos unemployed as of December 2023, not 10 million

The claim that the Philippines had ten million unemployed people in December 2023 is demonstrably false. Official data from the Philippine Statistics Authority reveals that the actual number of unemployed individuals was approximately 1.6 million, reflecting a low unemployment rate of 3.1%. This significant discrepancy highlights how easily misleading statistics can circulate on social media, often conflating unemployment with underemployment, which was recorded at roughly six million that same period. This misinformation poses a serious threat to public understanding of economic realities. When citizens rely on inaccurate figures shared by influential personalities, it distorts their perception of labor market health and potentially influences civic discourse and policy support. Correcting such falsehoods is essential to ensure that public opinion is formed based on verified facts rather than viral exaggerations. This article is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the critical importance of accessible, transparent, and authoritative government statistics. It demonstrates how direct access to primary sources like the PSA can empower citizens and fact-checkers to combat disinformation effectively. By promoting the use of open data, society can better verify claims, hold institutions accountable, and maintain the integrity of public information ecosystems.
