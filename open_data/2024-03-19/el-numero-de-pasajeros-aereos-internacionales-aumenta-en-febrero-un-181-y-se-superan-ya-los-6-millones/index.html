El número de pasajeros aéreos internacionales aumenta en febrero un 18,1% y se superan ya los 6 millones

El número de pasajeros aéreos internacionales aumenta en febrero un 18,1% y se superan ya los 6 millones

Spain’s tourism sector is showing exceptional dynamism at the start of the year, consolidating its position as a leading global destination through a significant increase in international passenger arrivals. This positive trend, sustained even during what is traditionally considered the low season, indicates a robust recovery and growing appeal to visitors from various regions, both within the European Union and from the rest of the world. Analysis of the flows reveals a strategic diversification of source markets and a marked preference for low-cost airlines, particularly from the United Kingdom, Italy, and the Netherlands. Additionally, there is a geographic spread of benefits that extends beyond traditional coastal areas, with notable growth in regions such as the Basque Country and Asturias, demonstrating greater dispersion and resilience of the national tourism model. This publication is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the power of open and standardized datasets to generate real-time business intelligence. By making these statistics publicly available, Turespaña enhances transparency and enables researchers, businesses, and public administrations to analyze mobility trends, optimize resources, and make evidence-based decisions regarding the evolution of international tourism.
