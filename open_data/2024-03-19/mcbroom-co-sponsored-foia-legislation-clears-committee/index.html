McBroom Co-Sponsored FOIA Legislation Clears Committee

Bipartisan legislation expands open data rights by including the state legislature and executive offices under transparency laws, ending long-standing exemptions. This move ensures public access to official records, aligning government operations with modern open data standards for greater accountability. The bill’s progress toward a full Senate vote marks a significant step in democratizing information and enhancing public trust.
