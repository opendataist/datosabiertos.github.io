Piden datos sobre reformas contra violencia política

Piden datos sobre reformas contra violencia política

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) ordered Congress to disclose details of legislative reforms aimed at eradicating political violence against women. This demand underscores the urgent need to advance gender equality and protect women’s rights in political participation. The initiative highlights critical reforms concerning sanctions for institutional violence, prevention of workplace harassment, and equal pay. These measures aim to ensure a safe and autonomous environment for women in public life and decision-making roles. This case is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how transparency institutions leverage access-to-information requests to hold lawmakers accountable. By demanding specific legislative records, it promotes public scrutiny of progress on gender equity and institutional integrity.
