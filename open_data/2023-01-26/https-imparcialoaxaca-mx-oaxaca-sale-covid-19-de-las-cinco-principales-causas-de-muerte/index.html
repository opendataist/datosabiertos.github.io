The article highlights that cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of death in Oaxaca, surpassing diabetes and malignancies, while also noting gender-specific mortality patterns. This data underscores the persistent burden of chronic conditions and the evolving impact of infectious diseases like COVID-19 on public health statistics. For the open data community, this report demonstrates the critical importance of granular, disaggregated datasets in revealing nuanced health disparities. By breaking down mortality rates by gender and year, it allows for more precise analysis than aggregated figures alone. Understanding these detailed trends enables better resource allocation and targeted health interventions. Consequently, such transparent, accessible mortality records are essential tools for researchers and policymakers aiming to address public health challenges effectively and efficiently.
