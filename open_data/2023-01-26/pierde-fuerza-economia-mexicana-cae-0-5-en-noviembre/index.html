Pierde fuerza economía mexicana: cae 0.5% en noviembre

Mexico’s economy contracted in November, marking a second consecutive month of stagnation. This unexpected decline underscores significant weakness in the services sector, overshadowing modest gains in primary activities. The data indicate that economic momentum is waning, with consumer and business services experiencing notable setbacks. The severity of the downturn exceeds initial forecasts, suggesting a deeper structural challenge than anticipated. Analysts warn that this trend signals a loss of strength in the overall economy. If the negative trajectory persists into the following month, it could affect quarterly performance expectations, reflecting broader uncertainties in domestic demand and industrial activity. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical indicators to monitor real-time economic health. Access to granular, timely datasets enables experts to detect early signs of volatility across specific sectors. Understanding these patterns requires transparent data availability, allowing for precise modeling of economic resilience and informing policy decisions aimed at stabilizing growth trends.
