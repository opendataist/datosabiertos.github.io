«Sería más sano…»: La particular crítica que recibió María Luisa Godoy tras publicar registros de su hijo andando a caballo

María Luisa Godoy’s recent post, which featured her son Ignacio riding a horse, sparked public debate about the visibility of celebrities’ children on social media. A follower questioned the necessity of exposing minors to such scrutiny, suggesting that keeping their lives private is healthier and respects their future autonomy to choose their own public persona. Conversely, many other fans defended the mother, praising her family’s simplicity and expressing admiration for the shared moments. This dichotomy highlights the ongoing tension between public curiosity, parental pride, and the ethical boundaries of digital sharing, showing how quickly positive interactions can shift into criticism. This incident is relevant to open data as it illustrates the complex dynamics of publicly accessible personal information. It underscores the importance of considering privacy implications and ethical standards when individuals voluntarily share sensitive, personal data, highlighting the human impact behind the metrics often analyzed in open information ecosystems.
