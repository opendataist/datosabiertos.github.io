Houston weather: City officials release statistics following winter freeze

The article highlights the City of Houston’s emergency response during a severe winter freeze, emphasizing successful collaboration between municipal agencies and community partners. It illustrates how diverse organizations coordinated to protect vulnerable populations and maintain essential services amidst challenging conditions. The response underscored the resilience of public works and emergency services in maintaining water stability and addressing widespread utility issues, demonstrating the capacity of local governments to adapt quickly to environmental crises.
