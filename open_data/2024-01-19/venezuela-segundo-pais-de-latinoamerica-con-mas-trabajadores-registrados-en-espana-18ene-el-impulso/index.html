▷ Venezuela: Segundo país de Latinoamérica con más trabajadores registrados en España #18Ene - El Impulso

Spain recorded significant growth in the number of foreign workers registered with the Social Security system in 2023. This surge underscores the deep integration of immigrant populations into the national labor market, particularly highlighting substantial contributions from Colombian and Venezuelan citizens, as well as those from the European Union. The data reveals shifting trends across economic sectors, with agriculture showing expansion while construction and hospitality experienced declines. These variations suggest evolving demands within the Spanish economy and the distinct roles that different immigrant communities play in sustaining various industries. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, high-quality metrics on labor mobility and social security coverage. Such datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze migration patterns, assess economic impacts, and develop informed strategies for social inclusion and workforce management.
