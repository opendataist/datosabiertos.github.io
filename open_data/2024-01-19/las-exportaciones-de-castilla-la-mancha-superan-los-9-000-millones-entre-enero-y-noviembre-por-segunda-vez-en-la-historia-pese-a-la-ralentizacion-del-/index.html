Las exportaciones de Castilla-La Mancha superan los 9.000 millones entre enero y noviembre por segunda vez en la historia pese a la ralentización del mercado exterior

Castilla-La Mancha has shown remarkable resilience in its external trade, achieving record-breaking export volumes despite a broader international slowdown. For the first eleven months of the year, exports exceeded nine billion euros, marking the second time this milestone has been reached in the region’s history. Although November saw a slight year-over-year decline, the month still ranked as the second-highest in historical records, highlighting the robust nature of the regional economy even amid challenging geopolitical circumstances. This performance underscores the strength of specific provinces, particularly Albacete and Cuenca, which maintained positive growth trends. In contrast, other provinces experienced decreases but still surpassed significant export thresholds, with Toledo securing its second-best historical result for this period. These figures indicate that while the overall pace has slowed compared to previous peaks, the region’s export sector remains highly competitive and stable, avoiding severe contractions. This data is relevant to open_data as it exemplifies the value of transparent, granular public statistics in economic analysis. By making detailed regional and monthly export figures accessible, governments enable researchers and businesses to track trends, identify local successes, and understand broader market impacts. Such transparency fosters evidence-based decision-making and allows for a clearer assessment of how global events affect local economies, ensuring that data serves as a tool for accountability and strategic planning.
