A la baja, percepción de inseguridad en la ciudad a diciembre: Inegi

The article highlights that while perceived public insecurity in Mexico dropped significantly in late 2023, personal experiences with conflict rose in Chihuahua. This divergence between statistical perception and lived reality underscores the complexity of measuring safety. Open data initiatives must capture these nuanced indicators to provide accurate, granular insights for policy. Relying solely on aggregate perception scores risks obscuring local tensions, necessitating transparent, multi-dimensional datasets. Ultimately, accessible open data allows societies to scrutinize official statistics against ground-level experiences. This transparency is crucial for building trust and ensuring that safety policies reflect actual citizen needs rather than just survey trends.
