Colombianos, los extranjeros que más se fueron a vivir a España en 2023 - Pulzo

The article highlights that Colombians became the largest group of foreign residents in Spain in 2023, marking the second consecutive year of this demographic shift. This massive outflow is driven by the search for better opportunities, with a significant portion of the population considering emigration if given the chance. The steady arrival of migrants contributes to sustaining Spain’s aging population, creating a notable social and economic dynamic between the two nations. This trend underscores the deep labor and social ties between Colombia and Spain, reflecting broader patterns of regional migration within the Spanish-speaking world. The data reveals that many Colombians enter Spain as tourists before regularizing their status, indicating a complex migration process influenced by economic pressures in Colombia and the perceived stability or opportunities in Europe. This content is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistics from national institutions like the INE and independent research centers like CERAC. These datasets provide transparent, measurable evidence of human mobility trends, allowing researchers and policymakers to analyze the impacts of migration on both sending and receiving countries. Open access to such figures supports informed decision-making regarding housing, integration policies, and international cooperation, demonstrating the value of public data in understanding global demographic shifts.
