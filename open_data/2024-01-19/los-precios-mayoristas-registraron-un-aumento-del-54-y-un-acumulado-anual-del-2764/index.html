Los precios mayoristas registraron un aumento del 54%, y un acumulado anual del 276,4%

The report highlights significant price increases across the wholesale, retail, and construction sectors throughout 2023, driven largely by surges in imported goods and manufactured products. This data illustrates the severe inflationary pressure affecting the national economy, with cumulative annual rises reaching staggering levels in key categories such as energy and basic materials. The findings underscore the persistent volatility in consumer and producer prices, reflecting deep structural challenges within the market. Government officials frame these figures as evidence of a stabilization plan’s partial success, attributing the monthly retail inflation rate to strict fiscal adjustments and currency market corrections. However, this narrative is contested by industry professionals, with nearly half of surveyed construction experts anticipating a decline in activity for the coming year. This divergence between political assessments and sectoral outlooks reveals a complex reality where macroeconomic goals clash with on-the-ground economic contraction. This article is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates the critical importance of transparent, granular statistical information for public debate. By releasing detailed indices, official bodies enable citizens and experts to verify government claims and understand the real impact of policies. Access to such precise data fosters accountability, allowing society to critically analyze the gap between political rhetoric and economic reality, thereby strengthening democratic oversight of fiscal decisions.
