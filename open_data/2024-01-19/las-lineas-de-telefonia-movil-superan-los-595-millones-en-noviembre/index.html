Las líneas de telefonía móvil superan los 59,5 millones en noviembre

The report reveals a mature telecommunications market in which mobile voice lines increasingly include broadband access, signaling a shift toward integrated digital services rather than standalone connectivity. This trend underscores how infrastructure data reflects evolving consumer habits and the declining relevance of legacy DSL technologies in favor of modern fiber-optic networks. Open data is essential in this context, as transparent, standardized metrics from regulators enable accurate tracking of market concentration and technological adoption. Such data supports informed policy-making, promotes competition by revealing operator dominance, and helps stakeholders understand the structural changes driving the digital economy forward.
