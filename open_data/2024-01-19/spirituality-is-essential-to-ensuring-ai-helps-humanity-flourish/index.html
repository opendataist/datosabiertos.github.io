Spirituality is Essential to Ensuring AI Helps Humanity Flourish

AI systems often inherit and amplify negative stereotypes regarding faith from their training data, which constitutes the majority of global digital content. This systemic bias risks harming human flourishing by reinforcing discrimination and undermining the proven benefits of religion, such as improved mental health and prosocial behavior. Without intervention, algorithms may exacerbate social division rather than support inclusive societal progress. Addressing this issue requires auditing datasets to ensure accurate and representative portrayals of diverse spiritual beliefs. It is crucial for stakeholders to pressure media and tech sectors to promote balanced narratives that reflect the true importance of faith in human life. By correcting these informational gaps, we can prevent AI from perpetuating harmful biases and instead leverage technology to enhance collective wellbeing and social cohesion. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights a critical blind spot in current dataset composition. The widespread underrepresentation and negative skewing of religious content in open digital archives directly impact AI development. Improving the quality and diversity of open data sources is essential for building ethical AI systems that respect cultural diversity and support the holistic development of all communities, ensuring technology serves humanity rather than marginalizing specific groups.
