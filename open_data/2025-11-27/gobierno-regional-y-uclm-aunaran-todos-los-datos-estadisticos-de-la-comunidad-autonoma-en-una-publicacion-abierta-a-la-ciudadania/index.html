Gobierno regional y UCLM aunarán todos los datos estadísticos de la Comunidad Autónoma en una publicación abierta a la ciudadanía

Gobierno regional y UCLM aunarán todos los datos estadísticos de la Comunidad Autónoma en una publicación abierta a la ciudadanía

The regional government of Castilla-La Mancha and the University of Castilla-La Mancha have established a strategic partnership to transform public data into accessible societal knowledge through the "Castilla-La Mancha in Data" project. This initiative aims to consolidate existing autonomous community statistics into both printed and digital formats, making them understandable for citizens and public administrators. By presenting analysis across twenty-two key sectors, such as health, employment, and environment, the project seeks to bridge the gap between raw statistics and actionable insight, ensuring that data serves the broader society rather than remaining confined within administrative silos. A crucial objective of this collaboration is to normalize the use of Artificial Intelligence within public services by establishing a dedicated chair focused on data, statistics, and AI applications. This academic-technical integration ensures that complex information is not only disseminated but also utilized to improve governance efficiency. The project supports the advancement of the new regional Statistics Plan, which outlines numerous operational improvements, thereby strengthening the region's capacity to leverage data for better public management and decision-making processes. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it exemplifies the transition from mere transparency to active data utility. By adhering to long-standing open government principles and legal frameworks for transparency and integrity, the project demonstrates how public institutions can responsibly share information. It highlights the importance of making diverse datasets comprehensible and available to all citizens, fostering a culture where data drives social understanding and enhances democratic participation through informed access to regional realities.
