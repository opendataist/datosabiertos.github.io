San Luis Potosí destaca en crecimiento laboral dentro del programa IMMEX

San Luis Potosí ranks fifth nationally in annual growth of work hours within the IMMEX program, contrasting with a national decline. This increase in overtime reflects enhanced productivity and rising worker purchasing power. These results stem from the governor’s efforts to modernize infrastructure and attract investment, aiming to provide better income opportunities. Such economic improvements are vital for regional stability and family welfare. This data highlights the importance of open data in tracking regional economic performance. Accessible statistics allow citizens and policymakers to assess the effectiveness of industrial policies and understand the real impact of government initiatives on local labor markets.
