Punta Umbría logra récord de habitantes censados

Punta Umbría logra récord de habitantes censados

Punta Umbría faces significant underfunding because its official census does not reflect the true population of over 20,000 residents, particularly missing summer migrants. The mayor urges unregistered inhabitants to register, emphasizing that accurate data is crucial for securing greater autonomy and better resource allocation from higher administrations. This situation highlights a critical gap in how municipal statistics are utilized for public planning and resource distribution. By failing to capture the actual demographic reality, the municipality remains deprived of essential services and financial support that should correspond to its real size and seasonal demands. This case is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the direct impact of data completeness on social equity and governance. Transparent, inclusive, and real-time population datasets are necessary to ensure that funding models accurately reflect community needs, preventing systemic disadvantages for dynamic, tourist-dependent areas.
