Desempleo en México baja a 2.9 % en el tercer trimestre de 2025

Mexico’s labor market demonstrated resilience in late 2025, with unemployment falling to its lowest levels, indicating robust economic activity despite external trade pressures. The data highlights a structural shift toward the service sector and significant job growth in commerce, revealing underlying trends in workforce dynamics and regional disparities. This information is vital for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical reporting. By making such detailed employment metrics publicly available, institutions foster accountability and enable researchers to analyze economic health beyond surface-level indicators. Ultimately, open access to granular labor data empowers policymakers and citizens to make informed decisions. It supports evidence-based governance and promotes economic transparency, allowing for a deeper understanding of how specific sectors and demographics contribute to national stability and growth.
