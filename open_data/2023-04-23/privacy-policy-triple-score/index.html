Privacy Policy - Triple Score

This article outlines the privacy and data collection practices of Pontotriplo, revealing how the platform handles user interactions. It highlights that even simple actions like leaving comments trigger the collection of significant personal data, including IP addresses and browser user agents, to facilitate spam detection. Additionally, the use of hash-based email matching for Gravatar services means that user identities can be linked to external visual profiles, creating a persistent digital footprint visible to the public upon comment approval. The scope of data retention extends beyond immediate interactions to long-term storage. Comments and their metadata are kept indefinitely to streamline future moderation, while user profiles and login information are stored based on specific cookie durations. A critical warning is issued regarding media uploads, as images may contain embedded GPS location data. This practice poses a risk because visitors can extract this precise location information from any photos hosted on the site, potentially compromising user privacy without their explicit consent. This disclosure is highly relevant to open data principles because it exemplifies the often-hidden data harvesting mechanisms inherent in standard web technologies. It demonstrates how "open" content, such as public comments or embedded media, can inadvertently facilitate non-transparent data tracking and retention by third parties. Understanding these practices is essential for advocates of data transparency, as it underscores the need for clearer user control over personal information and the hidden costs of participating in digital public spaces.
