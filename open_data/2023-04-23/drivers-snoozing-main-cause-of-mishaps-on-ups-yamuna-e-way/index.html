‘Drivers’ snoozing main cause of mishaps on UP’s Yamuna E-way’

An RTI response from YEIDA reveals that driver fatigue, rather than speeding, is the primary cause of accidents on the Yamuna Expressway. This data underscores the critical importance of transparency in public safety governance, illustrating how open data initiatives empower citizens to hold authorities accountable for infrastructure management and accident prevention strategies. The findings highlight a significant need for policy reforms regarding driver working hours and mandatory rest breaks. By identifying fatigue as the leading hazard, the information shifts the focus toward humane approaches to transport regulation, emphasizing that human factors are more detrimental to safety than vehicle speed alone. This case is highly relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how accessing government-held records can expose hidden truths and drive societal change. It validates the role of transparency in improving public welfare, showing that when data is accessible, activists can effectively advocate for evidence-based interventions that save lives and enhance road safety standards.
