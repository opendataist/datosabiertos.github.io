China lanza plan de acción para promover desarrollo basado en datos

China has launched a three-year initiative to elevate data as a key economic driver, integrating it across twelve vital sectors. This strategy aims to optimize resource allocation and stimulate new industrial growth while ensuring data quality and security. This policy is significant for open data because it highlights a major government push to structure and circulate information efficiently. By strengthening the legal and technical framework for data usage, China is creating a more predictable environment for transparency and public access to state-held information, facilitating broader societal and economic benefits through improved data governance.
