PlanDAI extendió su alcance a 28 estados

The PlanDAI initiative empowers vulnerable populations by expanding access to information rights across most Mexican states. By coordinating local networks that bring together government and civil society, the program effectively addresses local needs and improves the quality of life for marginalized groups. This expansion marks a critical shift in how transparency policies are socialized, moving from mere legal availability to practical application by those most in need. It demonstrates that institutional collaboration is essential for making data rights tangible and actionable at the community level. This matters for open data because it highlights that accessibility alone is insufficient. True openness requires targeted outreach and educational support to ensure that diverse communities can actually use public information to solve real-world problems, thereby validating the social impact of open government initiatives.
