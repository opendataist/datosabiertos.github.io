Asenjo lamenta que "el desprecio y el abandono de Page a los autónomos esté suponiendo un aumento en el cierre de pequeños comercios y negocios de la región"

The article highlights a significant decline in self-employed individuals in Castilla-La Mancha, contrasting sharply with national growth trends. It attributes this regression to the regional government’s alleged neglect of autonomous workers and small businesses, which has led to widespread closures and a deteriorating economic environment for local commerce. This divergence serves as a critical case study for open data initiatives, demonstrating how transparent, publicly accessible statistics can expose governance failures. By making these discrepancies visible, open data empowers civil society and opposition groups to hold officials accountable for policy impacts on small enterprises and employment stability. Ultimately, the narrative underscores the vital role of data transparency in democratic oversight. It illustrates that without accessible and comparable datasets, the true extent of regional policy ineffectiveness might remain obscured, preventing citizens from demanding necessary corrective measures to support the local economy.
