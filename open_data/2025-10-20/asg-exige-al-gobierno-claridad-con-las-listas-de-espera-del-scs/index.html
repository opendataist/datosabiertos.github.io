ASG exige al Gobierno claridad con las listas de espera del SCS

ASG exige al Gobierno claridad con las listas de espera del SCS

The article underscores the urgent need for transparency in how public health systems manage patient waitlist data, emphasizing that accurate reporting is essential for maintaining public trust. By questioning the exclusion of non-structured lists from official records, it highlights how opaque methodologies obscure the true extent of healthcare delays. This lack of clarity directly impacts citizens' ability to understand their rights and the realities of the services they are denied or delayed. The debate reveals significant regional disparities in wait times, suggesting that inconsistent management criteria, rather than medical necessity, may drive inequalities between provinces. Such discrepancies highlight the critical importance of standardized data collection across all administrative levels. Without uniform definitions and the inclusion of all patient categories, comparative analysis becomes unreliable, preventing effective policy adjustments and equitable resource distribution. This issue is profoundly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how partial or manipulated datasets undermine accountability in public administration. Transparent, comprehensive, and accessible data allows citizens and journalists to verify government claims and hold institutions responsible. Ultimately, releasing complete waitlist information empowers users to make informed decisions and advocates for systemic improvements, turning abstract statistics into tangible evidence for democratic oversight and better healthcare outcomes.
