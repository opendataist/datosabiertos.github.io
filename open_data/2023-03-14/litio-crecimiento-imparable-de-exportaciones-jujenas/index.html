Litio: crecimiento imparable de exportaciones jujeñas

Jujuy recorded the second-highest export growth in Argentina, driven primarily by lithium production. This surge underscores the critical need to open and standardize data on raw material extraction. Such transparency is essential to ensure accountability and promote equitable resource management within the province.
