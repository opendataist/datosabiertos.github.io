Parliament hosts orientation workshop to promote transparency and accountability - MyJoyOnline

Parliament hosts orientation workshop to promote transparency and accountability - MyJoyOnline

Ghanaian Parliament, partnering with Transparency International, conducted workshops to strengthen the Open Government Partnership Caucus. This initiative focuses on deepening democratic accountability and advancing Beneficial Ownership Transparency. By enhancing MPs' capacity, the program aims to implement national action plans that foster inclusive policy formulation and public interest outcomes. The training emphasized Ghana’s commitment to transparency and anti-corruption measures through open governance frameworks. MPs were urged to utilize legislative and oversight functions to support these reforms. The goal is to curb illicit financial flows and tax evasion, reinforcing Parliament’s role as a central actor in promoting open government practices. This effort is vital for open data because it institutionalizes Beneficial Ownership Transparency. By mandating legislative support for open data initiatives, the process ensures greater public access to critical information regarding public contracts. This shift transforms abstract transparency principles into enforceable legal standards, significantly improving data availability and accountability in Ghana’s public sector governance.
