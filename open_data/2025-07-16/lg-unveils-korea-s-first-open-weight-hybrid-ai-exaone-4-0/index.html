LG Unveils Korea's First Open-weight Hybrid AI, 'EXAONE 4.0'

LG Unveils Korea's First Open-weight Hybrid AI, 'EXAONE 4.0'

LG AI Research has unveiled EXAONE 4.0, South Korea’s first open-weight hybrid AI model, which combines large language capabilities with advanced reasoning abilities. By integrating these technologies, the model aims to solve complex problems through hypothesis verification, positioning LG as a competitive player alongside global leaders from the US, China, and France who have similarly released hybrid architectures. Crucially for the open-data community, EXAONE 4.0 is released as an open-weight model on Hugging Face, allowing researchers and educators to access and modify the model’s weights. This transparency enables the study of data processing methods and fosters innovation, aligning with open-data principles by making frontier AI technologies accessible for modification and redistribution rather than remaining proprietary black boxes. The release includes specialized expert and lightweight on-device models designed for privacy and security, demonstrating that high performance does not require massive server-dependent infrastructure. By making these models available via API and open weight platforms, LG accelerates the adoption of diverse AI applications across industries, ensuring that open-source communities can build upon robust, high-performing foundational models to create inclusive and innovative technological solutions.
