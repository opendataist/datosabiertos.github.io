La compraventa de viviendas se dispara casi un 40% y se pone en niveles del mes de mayo de 2007

La compraventa de viviendas se dispara casi un 40% y se pone en niveles del mes de mayo de 2007

The Spanish real estate market experienced an exceptional surge in May, reaching the highest level for this month in nearly two decades. This trend, driven by cheaper credit, reflects robust demand that extends across all regions and housing types. The sustained upward trend lasting more than a year indicates a structural recovery in the sector, consolidating a scenario of high liquidity and commercial activity that contrasts with the stability of regulated rental prices under new legislation. This report is essential for the open data community, as the National Statistics Institute (INE) provides historical and updated statistics on sales, rentals, and inflation. The availability of these structured data enables researchers, analysts, and developers to create interactive visualizations, predictive models, and transparency tools. By making information about market fluctuations accessible, it fosters accountability and empowers citizens to better understand the economic dynamics affecting their access to housing. Meanwhile, the publication of the reference index for rents marks a paradigm shift in managing housing costs. By replacing the general price index with a specific metric designed to limit disproportionate increases, authorities aim to balance the market. This data is crucial for evaluating the effectiveness of public policies and their impact on the financial stability of households. The combination of sales and rental data provides a comprehensive view necessary for designing fairer and more sustainable urban and social strategies.
