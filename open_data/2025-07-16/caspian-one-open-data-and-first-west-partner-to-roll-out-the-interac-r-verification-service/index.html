Caspian One Open Data and First West partner to roll out the Interac® verification service

First West Credit Union, in partnership with Caspian One Open Data, has launched an Interac verification service that allows members to authenticate their identity using existing online banking credentials. This initiative significantly simplifies the onboarding process for credit union members while enhancing security. By enabling instant, trusted digital verification, the collaboration demonstrates how open data can streamline user experiences and reduce friction in financial interactions, setting a precedent for future innovations like automated account opening. The underlying technology relies on Caspian’s open data platform, which acts as a secure conduit between financial institutions and third-party fintech providers. This infrastructure eliminates the need for custom integrations, drastically reducing implementation timelines from months to days. By standardizing API connections and managing member consent, the platform allows credit unions to rapidly connect with multiple partners. This approach not only accelerates innovation but also ensures that data sharing remains secure and compliant, moving the industry away from less secure methods like screen scraping. This launch is highly relevant to open data as it provides a concrete, real-world example of how open standards can transform financial services. It highlights the practical benefits of open data infrastructure, such as speed, security, and enhanced member trust, proving that the concept is ready for immediate adoption. As more Canadian credit unions join this ecosystem, the initiative builds momentum for the broader rollout of open banking in Canada, demonstrating a cooperative path toward a more connected and secure financial future.
