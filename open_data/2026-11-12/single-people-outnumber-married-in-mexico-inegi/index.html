Single people outnumber married in Mexico: INEGI

Single people outnumber married in Mexico: INEGI

Single’s Day, originating in China to celebrate personal autonomy, has transcended its initial purpose to become a global commercial phenomenon. In Mexico, this cultural shift reflects a profound transformation in social structure, where marriage is no longer considered essential for individual fulfillment. Recent data reveal that singlehood has surpassed marriage among the population, marking a historic turning point. This trend is associated with greater economic independence, access to education, and a redefinition of life goals, moving away from traditional stereotypes about happiness in a couple. This news is relevant to the open data sector, as it highlights the need for updated and accessible census statistics. Analyzing these demographic trends requires transparent official sources that enable a better understanding of new social and consumption patterns in real time.
