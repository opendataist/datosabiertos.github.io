October inflation: INDEC will release the data and consulting firms preview the estimate | El Dia

October inflation: INDEC will release the data and consulting firms preview the estimate | El Dia

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) is about to publish the official data for the consumer price index, while private consulting firms anticipate a stable monthly inflation rate, similar to that recorded in previous months. This scenario reflects the difficulty in consolidating economic deceleration, as sectors such as food, fuel, and services continue to exert upward pressure on prices, counteracting moderation efforts in other areas like housing. The convergence in estimates suggests that no sharp decline is expected in the coming months, with seasonal factors and tourism maintaining uncertainty. Although some annual projections indicate a closing figure close to 30 percent, the variability among sources highlights the complexity of the current economic analysis. The confirmation of the official data will be crucial to determine whether a further decline is achieved or if the recent trend persists. This information is relevant to open data because it illustrates the importance of accessing verified, transparent official statistics. By comparing multiple private sources with the official data, citizens and analysts can assess the quality, accuracy, and usefulness of public information. Promoting open access to these data enhances accountability and enables informed decision-making based on reliable statistical evidence, which is essential for democratic transparency.
