93.1% of Ueno Bank savers' deposits were reimbursed

93.1% of Ueno Bank savers' deposits were reimbursed

Paraguay’s Guarantee Fund has already returned a significant portion of the savings lost in the bankruptcy of Banco Ueno, fulfilling a key objective of protecting depositors. This progress underscores the importance of having transparent and efficient mechanisms for recovering funds, which is essential for maintaining confidence in the financial system. The continuation of payments to other groups of clients reinforces the need for clear and accessible data on the distribution of resources. This highlights the relevance of open data in ensuring that information about financial guarantees is public, verifiable, and usable by citizens to monitor the proper use of state resources during banking crises. At the conclusion of the process, transparency regarding the amounts and recipients will enable an assessment of the fund’s effectiveness. This encourages the open publication of economic data, facilitating independent analysis and strengthening accountability. The availability of these figures is vital for civil society to understand the real impact of financial interventions and to propose evidence-based improvements.
