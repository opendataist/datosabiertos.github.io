SIGMUN suma datos abiertos para atraer más inversión

The SIGMUN Open Data Portal improves access to geographic information, enabling faster downloads that significantly enhance transparency and public utility. By consolidating reliable urban data, the platform empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions regarding investments and developments. This initiative highlights the value of coordinated cross-government efforts in maintaining up-to-date data resources. It demonstrates how open data fosters local and international interest, underscoring the critical role of accurate, shared information in driving societal and economic progress through open data principles.
