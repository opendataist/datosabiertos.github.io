Gardaí billed Garth Brooks €380,000 to police his concerts

FOI documents reveal substantial policing costs for major commercial events, highlighting a significant financial burden on public resources. This transparency demonstrates how open data empowers citizens to scrutinize government spending and hold authorities accountable for fiscal decisions. Senator Barry Ward argues that profit-making private events should cover their own security expenses rather than relying on taxpayer funds. This perspective underscores a broader debate about the equitable distribution of costs between private enterprise and the public sector. The revelation fosters public discourse on whether large-scale concerts are justified when they incur high fees for police services. Such transparency is crucial for ensuring that public funds are allocated responsibly and that commercial entities bear the appropriate share of operational costs.
