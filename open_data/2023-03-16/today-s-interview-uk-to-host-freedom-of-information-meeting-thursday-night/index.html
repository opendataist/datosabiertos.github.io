Today's Interview: UK to host Freedom of Information meeting Thursday night

Government officials increasingly use private devices to conduct public business, effectively hiding records from journalists and the public. This practice undermines transparency and challenges the public’s right to access government information. Critics argue this represents a dangerous shift toward secrecy, despite official admonitions against it. The issue is currently under legal review, with courts determining the limits of such secretive conduct. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the urgent need to prevent data from being obscured by private communication channels. Ensuring that government interactions remain on recordable platforms is essential for maintaining accountability and supporting free access to public information.
