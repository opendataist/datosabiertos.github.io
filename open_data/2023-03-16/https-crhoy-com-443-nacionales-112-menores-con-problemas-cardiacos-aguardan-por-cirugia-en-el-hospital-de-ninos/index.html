The hospital is implementing targeted strategies to reduce the waiting list for complex pediatric cardiac surgeries, specifically the Fontan procedure. These measures include optimizing intensive care unit (ICU) capacity, recruiting additional medical staff such as anesthesiologists, and acquiring essential equipment to handle increased demand safely and efficiently. International medical missions are being promoted to perform complex operations alongside local specialists, ensuring comprehensive care. Additionally, requests for reinforced nursing and respiratory therapy staff aim to stabilize the department. These actions address previous bottlenecks caused by high ICU occupancy and staff shortages, demonstrating a proactive approach to resource management. This situation is highly relevant to open data because transparent reporting on medical waitlists, resource allocation, and surgical outcomes enables public accountability. Open access to such health statistics allows researchers and policymakers to identify systemic inefficiencies, optimize healthcare planning, and ensure that vulnerable populations receive timely, equitable access to critical medical interventions.
