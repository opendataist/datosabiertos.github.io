Inicia IV Semana de Gobierno Abierto

Inicia IV Semana de Gobierno Abierto

Chihuahua hosts the International Week of Open Government, reinforcing its commitment to transparency through an active local technical group and international expertise. This event highlights the city’s unique role in advancing open government practices within Mexico, serving as a national model for municipal accountability. The relevance to open data lies in the institutionalization of these transparency mechanisms, which rely on accessible information to ensure citizen participation and predictive governance. By prioritizing inclusive transparency and innovative practices, the initiative demonstrates how open data frameworks can effectively support democratic engagement and institutional trust at the local level.
