Defunciones por homicidios con arma de fuego en México 1990-2024

Firearms are responsible for the majority of homicides in Mexico, fueled by illegal cross-border trafficking that empowers criminal organizations and intensifies lethal violence, thereby complicating peacebuilding efforts. Geographic disparities reveal high-risk states alongside safer regions, underscoring the urgent need for localized security strategies. This data is crucial for open-data initiatives, as it exposes critical gaps in transparency concerning weapon flows and patterns of violence, enabling citizens and researchers to demand accountability and advocate for evidence-based policy reforms. By making these statistics accessible, open-data initiatives can foster civic oversight and support the development of effective public safety measures through informed, data-driven decision-making.
