Indec: la actividad económica registró crecimiento de 2,4% interanual en agosto - EL DEBATE

Indec: la actividad económica registró crecimiento de 2,4% interanual en agosto - EL DEBATE

Argentina’s economic activity grew by 5.2 percent in the first eight months of 2025 compared to the previous year. Financial intermediation drove this expansion, while manufacturing and commerce experienced declines, indicating a segmented recovery across sectors. This data highlights the dynamic shifts within the national economy, revealing where growth is sustained versus where contraction occurs. Such granular insights allow for a more nuanced understanding of economic health beyond aggregate totals. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for accessible, high-quality statistical information. Transparent release of detailed sectoral indicators enables researchers and developers to analyze trends accurately, fostering better decision-making based on reliable, public government data.
