Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

This privacy notice details how the Aragonese Party manages the personal data of its members. The primary conclusion is that the party strictly adheres to legal obligations while ensuring members retain full control over their information. The data serves essential administrative, financial, and political participation purposes, with retention periods balanced between active affiliation and statutory fiscal requirements. Members are empowered to access, rectify, and delete their data, reflecting a commitment to transparency and user sovereignty. The organization emphasizes robust security measures, including encryption and restricted access, to protect sensitive information. Notably, data processing remains confined to Spanish territory, avoiding international transfers and minimizing cross-border privacy risks associated with broader digital infrastructures. This document is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical boundary between public transparency and private data protection in political entities. While open data initiatives often push for maximum disclosure, this notice demonstrates how democratic organizations must legally safeguard individual privacy. It highlights the tension between the public’s right to know about political financing and the individual’s right to confidentiality, serving as a practical example of compliance-driven data governance rather than open sharing.
