Cómputo total del balotaje confirma triunfo de Rodrigo Paz en Bolivia - Noticiero Digital

Cómputo total del balotaje confirma triunfo de Rodrigo Paz en Bolivia - Noticiero Digital

Rodrigo Paz Pereira won Bolivia’s presidential runoff, securing more than half of the vote. The official tally, which incorporated both domestic and overseas ballots, confirmed this result after preliminary counts had initially excluded international votes. This final data represents the complete electoral record for the nation. This victory concludes a rare second-round contest mandated by the 2009 Constitution, which requires a runoff when no candidate achieves an outright majority in the first round. The process highlights the robustness of Bolivia’s democratic mechanisms in ensuring decisive outcomes through structured electoral stages and thorough verification of participation. Relevant to open data, this event demonstrates how the transparent publication of final, comprehensive vote counts builds public trust. By making the definitive vote totals publicly accessible, electoral authorities demonstrate accountability, enable independent verification of results, and reinforce the integrity of digital democracy initiatives.
