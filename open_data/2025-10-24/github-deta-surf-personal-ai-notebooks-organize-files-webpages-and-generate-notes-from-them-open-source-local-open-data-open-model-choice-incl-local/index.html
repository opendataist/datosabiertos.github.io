GitHub - deta/surf: Personal AI Notebooks. Organize files & webpages and generate notes from them. Open source, local & open data, open model choice (incl. local).

Deta Surf represents a significant advancement in personal knowledge management by enabling seamless, simultaneous research and synthesis. Rather than forcing users to context-switch between separate applications for notes, web browsing, and PDFs, Surf consolidates these diverse media types into a single, fluid environment. This approach acknowledges that complex thinking requires juggling multiple information sources, allowing users to connect ideas across formats without the friction of manual copying, pasting, and window management. Crucially, the platform prioritizes user autonomy and transparency through its commitment to open standards. It stores data locally in open, transparent formats using the Surf Flat File System, ensuring that users retain full control over their information. By supporting open-source development and allowing users to choose their own AI models, including local language models, Surf avoids vendor lock-in. This design philosophy ensures that the tool serves the user’s needs rather than dictating how data is used, fostering a private and secure ecosystem for intellectual work. This article is highly relevant to the open_data community because it exemplifies the practical application of open, local-first data principles in modern productivity tools. It demonstrates that powerful, AI-enhanced software does not require proprietary data silos or cloud dependency. Instead, by leveraging open formats and transparent storage engines, such tools empower individuals to maintain ownership of their digital assets. This aligns with the broader open_data movement’s goals of data sovereignty, interoperability, and resisting the centralization of personal information within closed corporate ecosystems.
