Designan Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos en Chihuahua

Chihuahua formally integrates citizens into its advisory council for open data and transparency. This structural change empowers public oversight, ensuring that diverse voices influence municipal policies on information access and anti-corruption efforts. The council consists of titular and substitute advisors with staggered terms. This rotation guarantees sustained civic engagement, preventing the monopolization of influence while fostering long-term stability in government openness initiatives. This development is vital for open data as it institutionalizes citizen participation. By mandating public dissemination, the initiative enhances accountability, demonstrating how local governance can effectively leverage community input to strengthen digital transparency and public trust.
