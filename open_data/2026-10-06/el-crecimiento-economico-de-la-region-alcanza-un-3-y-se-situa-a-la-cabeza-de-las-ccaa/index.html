El crecimiento económico de la región alcanza un 3 % y se sitúa a la cabeza de las CCAA

El crecimiento económico de la región alcanza un 3 % y se sitúa a la cabeza de las CCAA

Castile and León has consolidated its position as one of the regions with the greatest economic dynamism, ranking among the territories with the highest GDP growth in 2025. This performance exceeds the national average and stands out notably compared to the rates recorded in the European Union, demonstrating robust economic resilience that contrasts with the communities that experienced the smallest increases. The positive trend persists when reviewing previous data, confirming that the region not only grows but does so in a sustained manner compared to other areas of the country. Regarding wealth generated per capita, the community reached significant figures, although it remained below the national average and far behind leading regions such as Madrid or the Basque Country. This gap reflects persistent structural disparities in income distribution at the regional level. While some communities significantly exceed national averages, others, including the autonomous cities and southern regions, maintain lower levels, underscoring the need for coordinated policies to reduce territorial inequalities in economic development. This report is fundamental for the open_data community because it exemplifies how official statistical data, when structured and published openly, enables detailed and real-time comparative analyses. Transparency in time series and disaggregated data facilitates the ability of researchers, developers, and citizens to build visualizations and applications that make economic realities visible. This promotes accountability, drives data-based innovation, and empowers civil society to understand and actively participate in debates on regional development and the efficiency of public policies.
