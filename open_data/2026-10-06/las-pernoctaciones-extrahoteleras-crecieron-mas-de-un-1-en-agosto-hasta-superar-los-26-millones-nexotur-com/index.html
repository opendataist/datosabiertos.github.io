Las pernoctaciones extrahoteleras crecieron más de un 1% en agosto hasta superar los 26 millones - Nexotur.com

Las pernoctaciones extrahoteleras crecieron más de un 1% en agosto hasta superar los 26 millones - Nexotur.com

Overnight stays in non-hotel accommodations experienced a broad slowdown in August, driven primarily by a contraction in domestic demand. Although international tourism maintained slight growth, it was unable to offset the domestic decline, highlighting the exhaustion of the local market’s expansion margin and marking a downward trend compared to the beginning of the year. This evolution diverges significantly by accommodation type and destination. While hostels and apartments recorded substantial declines, rural tourism emerged as the only segment showing growth, benefiting from foreign visitors. Prices rose across all segments, reflecting sustained inflation despite lower occupancy rates in several categories, suggesting cost pressures amid more selective demand. This analysis is relevant to the open data community because it demonstrates how integrating official statistics enables the identification of complex patterns in real time. Data transparency facilitates the tracking of key economic indicators, such as the relationship between the volume of stays and prices, supporting informed decision-making in the tourism sector and academic research on mobility trends.
