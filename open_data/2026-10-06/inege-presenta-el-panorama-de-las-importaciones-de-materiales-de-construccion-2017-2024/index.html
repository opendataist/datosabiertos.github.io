INEGE presenta el panorama de las importaciones de materiales de construcción 2017-2024

Equatorial Guinea has significantly advanced its infrastructure development by heavily concentrating resources in the construction sector. This trend is evident in the sustained and record-breaking increase in imports of construction materials from 2017 to 2024, reflecting strong domestic demand and a strategic commitment to the country’s physical modernization. The analysis shows that Asian and European markets dominate supply, with China leading the way, while products such as steel, cement, and electrical components account for the largest trade volumes. This geographic and product diversification indicates a structured external dependency aimed at sustaining growth, prioritizing essential materials that ensure the viability of both public and private investment projects. This information is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how official statistics, when made accessible, enable auditing of transparency in the management of public resources. By publishing these disaggregated details, the national institute promotes accountability and facilitates civil society’s analysis of development policy efficiency, thereby fostering governance based on verifiable and open data.
