¿Qué información tengo derecho a preguntar sobre mis condiciones laborales?: claves de la ley de transparencia

¿Qué información tengo derecho a preguntar sobre mis condiciones laborales?: claves de la ley de transparencia

The recent legislation establishes a paradigm shift in labor relations, requiring companies to provide radical transparency regarding working conditions before hiring. This requirement not only aims to make jobs more predictable but also provides workers with a fundamental tool to exercise their rights with full knowledge of the facts. The mandatory disclosure of aspects such as salary structure, working hours, and modification clauses significantly reduces job insecurity, aligning the labor market with the fairness and clarity standards promised by the European Union. A distinctive and crucial aspect of this regulation is the requirement to disclose the use of algorithms and automated systems that organize work or determine compensation. This measure is particularly relevant in the field of open data and algorithmic governance, as it obliges organizations to make visible the opaque logic behind talent management and economic compensation. By requiring the public or internal disclosure of these systems, it lays the groundwork for auditing the fairness of automated processes, fostering a culture of accountability in the use of decision-making technologies. The relevance of this article for the open data community lies at the intersection of administrative transparency and digital rights. By transforming traditionally private labor data into accessible and standardized information, it facilitates aggregated analysis to detect patterns of inequality or abusive practices. This regulatory evolution drives the need for tools that process, visualize, and analyze this new volume of information, encouraging citizens to use their right to access information to build a fairer, audited, and evidence-based labor society.
