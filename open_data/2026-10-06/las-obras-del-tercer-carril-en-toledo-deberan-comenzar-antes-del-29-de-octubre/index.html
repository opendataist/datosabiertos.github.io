Las obras del tercer carril en Toledo deberán comenzar antes del 29 de octubre

Las obras del tercer carril en Toledo deberán comenzar antes del 29 de octubre

The contract to improve the connection between the A-42 motorway and the TO-23 road has recently been formalized, stipulating that work must begin imminently. This intervention aims to reorganize access points and increase capacity in the area by modifying the structure of the interchange and adding shoulder lanes to facilitate vehicular flow. The project also includes stormwater management and the updating of signage, integrating environmental and safety solutions into the maintenance of the road network. These improvements are part of the State’s conservation program, which has allocated significant resources to the province of Toledo to maintain critical infrastructure. The execution of these works reflects a continuous commitment to modernizing major roads, ensuring that transport capacity aligns with current and future regional mobility needs. This case is relevant for open data, as it demonstrates how transparency in public contracting enables tracking of the allocation and implementation of state funds. By consulting open data, citizens can verify compliance with deadlines and the effectiveness of public investments, fostering accountability and informed participation in the management of government resources.
