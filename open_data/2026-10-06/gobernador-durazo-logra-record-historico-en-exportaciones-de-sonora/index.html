Gobernador Durazo logra récord histórico en exportaciones de Sonora

Gobernador Durazo logra récord histórico en exportaciones de Sonora

The article highlights the constant monitoring of hydraulic infrastructure in Sonora, demonstrating the use of technical data to anticipate risks associated with rainfall. By keeping key reservoirs at the limit of their capacity, authorities rely on accurate and up-to-date information to manage water storage and prevent overflows. This situation underscores the importance of having reliable hydrological data that enables real-time monitoring of water levels and basin inflows. The relevance to open data lies in the fact that preventive decision-making and public safety depend on timely access to this public information. When data on reservoir levels and river behavior are available and transparent, they facilitate coordination among institutions and enable more effective early warnings. This empowers the population to take precautions and helps authorities implement protective actions in a coordinated manner. Consequently, the transparency of environmental data is not merely a technical issue, but a fundamental pillar of risk management. Ensuring that information about water bodies is accessible promotes community resilience and efficiency in responding to extreme weather events, strengthening public trust in the institutions responsible for civil protection.
