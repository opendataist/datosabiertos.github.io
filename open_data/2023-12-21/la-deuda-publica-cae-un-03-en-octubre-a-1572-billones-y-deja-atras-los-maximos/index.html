La deuda pública cae un 0,3% en octubre, a 1,572 billones, y deja atrás los máximos

Public administration debt in Spain decreased slightly on a monthly basis, stabilizing below previous historical peaks. While the overall stock rose year-on-year, specific sectors such as central administration saw reductions, whereas regional and social security debts increased. This nuanced distribution highlights that aggregate figures mask significant divergences across different levels of government. The consolidation of internal debt holdings among subsectors grew significantly, reflecting internal fiscal dynamics rather than external borrowing. Meanwhile, social security obligations continued their upward trajectory, contributing to the overall year-on-year increase. These shifts indicate varying pressures on different administrative bodies, requiring granular analysis to understand true fiscal health. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the need for accessible, disaggregated public financial statistics. Policymakers and researchers require detailed breakdowns to accurately assess sustainability. Transparent, structured datasets enable better monitoring of fiscal evolution and facilitate evidence-based decisions regarding public debt management across all administrative tiers.
