The Cabinet of Ministers approved the Commitments for the second National Open Government Partnership Action Plan (OGP)

Seychelles has approved a new Open Government Partnership action plan that prioritizes citizen-centric transformation, transparency, and accountability. By selecting five key commitments, the government aims to bridge the gap between policy and democratic principles, ensuring that governance reforms directly involve the public. This approach shifts the focus toward inclusive participation, recognizing that sustainable change requires aligning state actions with human rights and the active engagement of civil society. The selected initiatives highlight critical areas for open data and governance reform. First, advancing fisheries transparency ensures that resource management data is accessible, fostering public debate and sustainable practices. Second, establishing a National Integrity Coalition integrates independent state institutions to collaborate on anti-corruption and accountability measures. Third, digital transformation efforts aim to build local IT capacity, which is essential for maintaining the open platforms and portals that enable genuine citizen engagement and access to information. Additionally, the plan addresses internal government capacity and public health through employee wellness policies and data-driven alcohol management. These commitments demonstrate the relevance of open data by showing how transparency extends beyond just publishing statistics; it involves structured co-creation with stakeholders and using evidence to guide policy. For the open data community, this case illustrates how integrating data openness into broader governance frameworks can enhance public trust and improve service delivery across both public and private sectors.
