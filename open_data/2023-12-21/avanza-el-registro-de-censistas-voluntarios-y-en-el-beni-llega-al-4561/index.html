Avanza el registro de censistas voluntarios y en el Beni llega al 45,61%

Avanza el registro de censistas voluntarios y en el Beni llega al 45,61%

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INE) is actively recruiting volunteer census takers to ensure accurate data collection for the upcoming national population and housing census. This preparation involves offering specific incentives, such as career advancement opportunities for teachers, to encourage widespread participation and maximize the registration of essential personnel across all departments. The institute clarifies that its mandate focuses exclusively on statistical production rather than territorial disputes, ensuring that the census benefits all municipalities and communities equally. By maintaining neutrality regarding boundary conflicts, the agency aims to gather precise, unbiased information that serves the broader public interest. This effort is crucial for open data initiatives, as it promotes the collection of comprehensive, reliable statistics from the entire population. Transparent and inclusive data gathering allows for better analysis and resource allocation, fulfilling the open data principle of making societal information accessible and useful for decision-making.
