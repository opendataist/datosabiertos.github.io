Freedom of Information statistics: July to September 2023

Freedom of Information statistics: July to September 2023

This article highlights the rigorous statistical standards governing Freedom of Information data in the UK central government. By adhering to the Office for Statistics Regulation’s Code of Practice, these statistics ensure trustworthiness and quality, which are essential for reliable public scrutiny of government transparency. The accreditation of these figures as National Statistics signifies that they are produced impartially and meet identified user needs. This validation assures stakeholders that the data reflects actual FOI implementation accurately, supporting informed analysis of how public bodies handle information requests. This relevance to open data lies in the critical link between verified statistics and accountability. High-quality, officially recognized data enables researchers and citizens to effectively assess government openness, fostering a culture where transparent information management can be measured and improved over time.
