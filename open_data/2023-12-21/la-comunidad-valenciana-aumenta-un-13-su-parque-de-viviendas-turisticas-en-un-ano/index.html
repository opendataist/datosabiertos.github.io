La Comunidad Valenciana aumenta un 13% su parque de viviendas turísticas en un año

The article reveals a rapid expansion of the tourist rental market in the Valencian Community, significantly outpacing the national average growth rate. This surge indicates a structural shift in the housing stock, where a notable fraction of available homes is dedicated exclusively to short-term visitor accommodation rather than permanent residence. Such disproportionate growth highlights the intensifying pressure on local housing supplies in popular coastal and urban areas. Alicante province emerges as the epicenter of this trend, holding the largest share of tourist capacity within the region and ranking second nationally, only behind Málaga. The high proportion of homes designated for tourism in key municipalities underscores the extent to which residential markets are being converted for seasonal use. This concentration suggests that specific localities are heavily dependent on the vacation rental economy, potentially altering the social and demographic fabric of these communities. By utilizing web scraping techniques to consolidate listings from major platforms, researchers can generate comprehensive, real-time insights that fill gaps left by traditional administrative registries.
