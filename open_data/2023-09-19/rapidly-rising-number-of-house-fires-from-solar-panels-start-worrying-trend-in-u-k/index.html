Rapidly rising number of house fires from solar panels start worrying trend in U.K.

Rising solar adoption has drastically increased panel-related fires, highlighting urgent safety concerns due to inadequate installation regulations. This trend emphasizes the need for better standards, directly impacting open data’s role in tracking public safety risks and driving policy reform. Understanding these incidents allows for transparent monitoring, ensuring that open datasets can inform safer deployment practices and protect consumers from emerging hazards.
