Lectura fácil: la puerta de acceso a los derechos de ciudadanía para las personas con discapacidad

The “Gobierno Fácil” (Easy Government) program in Aragon transforms complex administrative documents into easy-to-read versions, an initiative that has been recognized as the best in Europe by the Open Government Partnership. This project demonstrates that simplifying technical language is not merely an accommodation for people with intellectual disabilities, but an inclusive tool that benefits society as a whole, including older adults and foreigners who face comprehension difficulties. The relevance to open data lies in its focus on cognitive accessibility. Public data and government information are only truly open and useful if they are understandable to all citizens. By removing linguistic barriers, it ensures that information is available in a format that enables full civic participation, guaranteeing that transparency is not hindered by the complexity of the original text. This success underscores that collaboration between public administrations and specialized organizations is crucial for humanizing public services. By validating that easy-to-read formats have a real social impact and are not just an internal exercise, the project sets a vital precedent. It suggests that opening up data must be accompanied by active efforts to make that information accessible, transforming bureaucracy into a means of universal civic empowerment.
