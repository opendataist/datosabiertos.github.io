INAI reanuda plazos y términos tras resolución de la Suprema Corte

INAI reanuda plazos y términos tras resolución de la Suprema Corte

The INAI’s Full Plenary is resuming suspended deadlines and terms following a Supreme Court ruling that validates sessions with current commissioners. This resolves the quorum impasse caused by unfilled vacancies, allowing the body to legally exercise its full authority once again. Key procedures are restarting, including the issuance of resolutions for transparency disputes, protection of personal data, and the imposition of sanctions. These actions cover both public sector verifications and private sector data protection, restoring the institute’s capacity to enforce compliance. This development is vital for open data governance as it re-establishes the regulatory framework necessary for transparency and privacy enforcement. By allowing the INAI to function effectively, it ensures accountability for public institutions and safeguards individual rights, which are fundamental principles of an open and transparent society.
