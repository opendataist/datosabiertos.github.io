Secret Pfizer report shows company KNEW since February 2021 that its mRNA COVID jab was killing people – NaturalNews.com

This article presents a controversial claim that a 2021 FOIA request revealed Pfizer’s internal awareness of severe adverse events associated with its COVID-19 vaccine, including reported deaths and cardiac disorders. The author asserts that the company’s decision to continue distribution despite this data constitutes a criminal act of mass genocide, alleging complicity among regulatory bodies and global health organizations that permitted the widespread rollout. The narrative argues that official safety reports serve as an implicit admission of liability, transforming what might have been viewed as negligent from intentional harm due to the continued administration of the shots. By framing these pharmacovigilance documents as evidence of criminal intent, the text suggests that regulatory approval and medical endorsement are not based on safety assurances but on collusion with pharmaceutical interests, thereby undermining public trust in established health institutions. This piece is relevant to the open data movement because it highlights the tension between transparency mechanisms and public skepticism regarding how raw data is interpreted. While FOIA requests aim to unlock government-held information, this article illustrates how released documents can be leveraged to construct alternative narratives that challenge official scientific consensus. It underscores the critical importance of context, expert analysis, and the responsible communication of data to prevent misuse, which is a central concern in ensuring that open access to information strengthens rather than destabilizes public understanding of complex scientific issues.
