El INE eleva tres décimas el crecimiento del PIB en 2022, al 5,8%

The National Statistics Institute has revised upward Spain’s economic growth for 2022, highlighting a robust recovery driven primarily by external demand. This update confirms that the Spanish economy not only overcame the contraction caused by the pandemic but also regained its pre-crisis levels in 2022, consolidating its dynamism relative to other major eurozone economies. The greater contribution of exports, compared to more moderate domestic demand, has reshaped the composition of GDP, while improved incomes and a reduction in relative debt validate the effectiveness of economic policies and the recovery plan implemented. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the transparency and methodological evolution of public data. The quarterly and annual revision of national accounts demonstrates how initial estimates are refined with more complete and accurate information—a process essential for ensuring the reliability of official statistics. Citizens, researchers, and civil society organizations rely on these open and updated data to accurately analyze economic trends. The publication of these revisions allows not only an assessment of the final outcome but also an evaluation of the quality of the statistical process, ensuring that evidence-based decision-making takes into account adjustments necessitated by new findings. Furthermore, public access to these revised time series facilitates a deeper analysis of key macroeconomic variables, such as household consumption, investment, and employment. By providing corrected and detailed data, the INE enables a more precise assessment of the impact of government measures and the resilience of the economic system. This availability fosters accountability and allows for an informed public debate on the country’s financial health. In essence, the practice of publishing detailed revisions is a fundamental pillar of open governance, as it transforms raw data into verifiable knowledge that empowers society.
