Open Data
ES EN

The Second National Open Data Meeting highlights the urgent need to improve the quality and availability of environmental data to address the climate emergency. By promoting the openness of public information, this event seeks to raise global awareness of human impact, transforming data into key tools for understanding environmental realities and facilitating evidence-based decision-making. The initiative directly responds to the crisis of environmental misinformation, ensuring that media and algorithmic narratives are grounded in objective facts. By strengthening the use of accurate indicators and visualizations, it aims to combat unverified theories that polarize debate or paralyze collective action. This means that citizens and professionals can rely on available information to act in response to real ecological threats. This meeting is relevant to the open data community because it demonstrates how administrative transparency and inter-agency collaboration are essential for democratic governance. By integrating artificial intelligence and quality standards, it sets a precedent for using public data not only for reporting purposes but also as a strategic asset against informational manipulation, empowering both institutions and citizens in the preservation of the environment.
