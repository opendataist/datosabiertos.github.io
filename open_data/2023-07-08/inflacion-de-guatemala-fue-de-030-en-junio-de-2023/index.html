Inflación de Guatemala fue de 0,30% en junio de 2023

Guatemala’s inflation showed significant deceleration in June 2023 compared to previous periods. This slowdown reflects broader economic stabilization trends, offering insights into purchasing power dynamics. Food and housing prices contributed most to monthly increases, while transport costs slightly decreased. These shifts highlight how specific sectors influence overall consumer price indices and economic indicators. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies transparent, publicly accessible statistical reporting. Analyzing such detailed metrics allows researchers and policymakers to monitor economic health effectively through available public datasets.
