El gobierno de Brasil informó que la deforestación del Amazonas cayó casi 34%

Brazil reported a significant decline in Amazon deforestation alerts in early 2023, reversing five years of record highs. This improvement is attributed to renewed political will and the restoration of autonomy for environmental agencies under the new administration, which aims to achieve zero deforestation by 2030. This case highlights the critical link between governance and ecological data transparency. It demonstrates how restoring institutional authority enables the effective collection and use of real-time monitoring systems. For open data, this underscores that accessible, real-time environmental information is essential for accountability. It shows how data transparency supports policy implementation and international commitments, proving that open data initiatives must be coupled with strong institutional frameworks to drive tangible conservation outcomes.
