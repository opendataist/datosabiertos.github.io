El Indec difunde el índice Crianza que medirá costo del cuidado de la niñez

The release of the IC index marks a pivotal step for open data by quantifying the specific costs of raising children. This new statistical tool breaks down essential expenses and caregiving responsibilities, providing a transparent, data-driven reference for judicial decisions on child support. Its significance lies in standardizing legal rulings through objective metrics while empowering families with clearer tools for economic planning. Although it does not establish a mandatory cap, it offers a unified baseline that enhances consistency and fairness in family justice systems across the country. This initiative highlights the critical role of open, granular data in shaping social policy. By making the costs of care visible, it transforms abstract needs into measurable indicators, demonstrating how accessible statistics can directly influence equitable legal outcomes and resource allocation.
