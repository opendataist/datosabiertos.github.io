Día Internacional de la Juventud (I)

Día Internacional de la Juventud (I)

The article emphasizes that youth constitute a significant demographic in Guatemala, serving as a vital force for social transformation. It highlights their unique role as a digital generation, capable of driving innovative solutions to complex societal challenges, particularly those aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals. Central to this narrative is the necessity for inclusive public policies designed by and for young people. By addressing key areas such as education, employment, and civic participation, these policies must recognize the diversity of the youth population, including marginalized groups, to ensure equitable opportunities for meaningful contribution to national progress. This piece is relevant to open data because effective, targeted public policy relies on accurate, granular demographic insights. Transparent data on youth needs and digital access enables governments to create evidence-based strategies, ensuring that initiatives truly reflect the realities and potential of the youngest citizens, thereby fostering inclusive and sustainable development.
