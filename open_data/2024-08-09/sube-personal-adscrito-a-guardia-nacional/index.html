Sube personal adscrito a Guardia Nacional

Sube personal adscrito a Guardia Nacional

The National Guard significantly increased apprehensions in 2023, predominantly for weapons-related offenses, while state police saw a modest rise in civic infractions, largely stemming from public disturbance. This data reveals a growing demand for transparent security metrics across federal and state levels. Open data is crucial here, as it enables independent verification of these trends and accountability for law enforcement actions. By making these census results accessible, citizens can analyze disparities in crime handling and trust in different judicial authorities. Ultimately, this transparency fosters informed public discourse on safety policies. It empowers civil society to evaluate the effectiveness of security strategies and identify systemic biases, ensuring that statistical reporting translates into genuine institutional responsibility and improved public trust.
