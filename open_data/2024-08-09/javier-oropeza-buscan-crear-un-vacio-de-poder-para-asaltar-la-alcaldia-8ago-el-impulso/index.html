▷ Javier Oropeza: Buscan crear un vacío de poder para asaltar la alcaldía #8Ago - El Impulso

The arbitrary detention of government officials and the violent raid on a mayor’s family home and media office signal a coordinated political strategy to create a power vacuum and dismantle municipal executive authority. This aggressive persecution extends beyond individual targets, encompassing over a hundred opposing mayors, which threatens to paralyze public administration and destabilize local governance structures across the region. Such actions, often justified by unfounded legal pretexts, represent a direct attack on democratic principles and freedom of expression. By targeting journalists and political leaders without judicial due process, authorities aim to intimidate dissent and silence critical voices, thereby undermining the rule of law and the independence of civil society institutions. This article is crucial for open data discussions as it highlights the severe risks to transparency and accountability in environments where political persecution disrupts public service delivery. It underscores the necessity of digital preservation and open access mechanisms to document government misconduct, ensuring that vital information remains available despite state attempts to obscure the truth through intimidation and administrative chaos.
