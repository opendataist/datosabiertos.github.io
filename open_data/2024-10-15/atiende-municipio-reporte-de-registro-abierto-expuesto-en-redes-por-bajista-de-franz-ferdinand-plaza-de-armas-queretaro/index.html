Open data thrives when citizens actively report civic issues through digital platforms, enabling authorities to quickly address infrastructure failures. This case demonstrates how social media serves as a transparent feedback loop, transforming individual observations into immediate municipal action. This model is relevant to open data because it validates citizen-generated data as a vital tool for governance.
