La batalla entre medios y empresas de IA: lo que está en juego en la investigación del código de OpenAI

La batalla entre medios y empresas de IA: lo que está en juego en la investigación del código de OpenAI

The New York Times is examining OpenAI’s code to determine whether its articles were used to train generative AI models without compensation. This legal challenge focuses on whether such use infringes copyright laws or qualifies as fair use, directly threatening traditional media business models. The outcome will set crucial precedents for how technology companies access protected content in the future. This dispute highlights the urgent need for ethical standards and transparency in data collection for artificial intelligence. It underscores the tension between innovation and the protection of intellectual property, echoing past conflicts over digital rights. The case suggests a significant shift in how creators can protect their work against automated replication. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for transparent and lawful data sourcing. As AI relies heavily on vast datasets, this conflict forces a reevaluation of how open datasets are curated and licensed. It emphasizes that true open data initiatives must respect intellectual property rights to ensure sustainable collaboration between the media and technology sectors.
