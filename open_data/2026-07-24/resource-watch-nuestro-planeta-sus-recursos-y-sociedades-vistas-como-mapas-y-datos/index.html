Resource Watch: nuestro planeta, sus recursos y sociedades vistas como mapas y datos

Resource Watch showcases the power of open data by integrating more than two hundred environmental and social datasets with interactive global mapping. This integration enables users to visually explore complex information, such as climate trends and urbanization probabilities, fostering a deeper understanding through accessible visualization tools. The platform’s commitment to openness extends beyond data access to include public code repositories and contribution guidelines. By allowing users to download datasets and propose new ones, it encourages community participation and collaborative improvement, embodying the core principles of transparency and shared knowledge that are essential for robust open data ecosystems. This resource is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how raw statistics can be transformed into actionable insights for diverse audiences, from journalists to policymakers. It underscores the importance of combining open data with user-friendly interfaces to maximize societal impact, proving that accessible visualization is key to democratizing information and driving informed decision-making on a global scale.
