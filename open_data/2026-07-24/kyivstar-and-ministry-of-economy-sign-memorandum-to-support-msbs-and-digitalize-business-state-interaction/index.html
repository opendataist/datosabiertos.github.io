Kyivstar and Ministry of Economy sign memorandum to support MSBs and digitalize business-state interaction

Kyivstar and Ministry of Economy sign memorandum to support MSBs and digitalize business-state interaction

Kyivstar and Ukraine’s Ministry of Economy have established a two-year partnership aimed at supporting small businesses through digital service development and the strategic use of open data. This collaboration seeks to integrate state feedback mechanisms into Kyivstar’s business platform, thereby encouraging greater entrepreneurial participation in government dialogue. By facilitating this interaction, the initiative helps identify economic challenges and fosters more transparent, efficient business-state relations, which are essential for robust digital transformation. The agreement emphasizes the exchange of expertise and the creation of joint educational and analytical projects to aid entrepreneurship. Beyond immediate operational support, Kyivstar is also advancing long-term infrastructure goals, including the development of a sovereign AI data center in cooperation with VEON. These efforts reflect a broader commitment to modernizing Ukraine’s technological landscape, ensuring that private sector innovation complements national recovery and development strategies. This partnership is highly relevant to open data because it operationalizes the concept by creating tangible channels for public feedback and data exchange. It demonstrates how open data principles can be practically applied to improve governance and business environments. By leveraging telecommunications infrastructure to gather and analyze business insights, the initiative highlights the critical role of accessible data in driving economic transparency and informed policy-making in the digital age.
