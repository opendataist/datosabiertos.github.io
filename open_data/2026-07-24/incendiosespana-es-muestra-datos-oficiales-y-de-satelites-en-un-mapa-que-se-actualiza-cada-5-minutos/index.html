IncendiosEspaña.es muestra datos oficiales y de satélites en un mapa que se actualiza cada 5 minutos

The platform IncendiosEspaña.es demonstrates the power of aggregating disparate open data sources, including satellite heat signatures and official emergency services, into a unified, near real-time map. By integrating these varied datasets, it provides a significantly more comprehensive view of forest fires than any single system could offer independently. Beyond basic location tracking, the tool layers critical contextual information such as radiative power, confidence levels, and alternative communication networks that remain operational when traditional mobile coverage fails. This multi-layered approach allows users to understand not just where fires are, but their intensity and the surrounding environmental risks in detail. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it proves that combining publicly available datasets from different resolutions and update frequencies creates superior public utilities. It highlights how interoperability between global satellite observations and local official data can enhance disaster monitoring, transparency, and societal resilience through accessible, integrated information systems.
