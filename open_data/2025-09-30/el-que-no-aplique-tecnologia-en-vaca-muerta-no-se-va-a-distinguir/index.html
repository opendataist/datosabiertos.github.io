«El que no aplique tecnología en Vaca Muerta, no se va a distinguir»

«El que no aplique tecnología en Vaca Muerta, no se va a distinguir»

Diego Pando, CEO of Aesa, highlights the strategic alignment between the engineering firm and YPF in advancing Argentina’s hydrocarbon sector. Under YPF’s Plan 4×4, Aesa plays a critical role in supporting export goals exceeding thirty billion dollars by executing essential construction projects across Vaca Muerta. This partnership ensures the efficient development of key infrastructure, including treatment plants and pipelines, which are vital for maximizing production in the region’s most productive shale fields. The article emphasizes that technological innovation is indispensable for remaining competitive in this demanding environment. Pando asserts that integrating digital tools, artificial intelligence, and data analytics is not optional but a daily necessity for improving efficiency and safety. By adopting these technologies, companies can optimize operations and distinguish themselves in a market where traditional methods are no longer sufficient to meet the scale and complexity of current energy demands. The relevance to open data lies in the implicit need for transparent, accessible, and interoperable data systems to drive these technological transformations. As companies increasingly rely on AI and data analysis for efficiency and safety, the quality and availability of open, standardized datasets become crucial. This shift encourages industries to embrace data-driven decision-making, fostering an ecosystem where shared information can enhance operational transparency, improve predictive maintenance, and ultimately support sustainable growth in the energy sector through better-informed engineering and management practices.
