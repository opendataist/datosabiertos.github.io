La inflación sube dos décimas en septiembre, al 2,9%, por la electricidad y las gasolinas

Spanish inflation rose in September, primarily driven by the evolution of energy prices, while underlying inflation continues its downward trend toward the European Central Bank’s target. This divergence highlights the direct impact of fluctuations in fuel and electricity prices on headline inflation indices, contrasted with the stabilizing trend of core inflation. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparency in the statistical publications of the National Statistics Institute (INE) enables monitoring of the breakdown of inflation. Access to these disaggregated indicators facilitates the analysis of specific factors, such as energy, which are essential for understanding economic dynamics and making informed, evidence-based decisions. Advance and final data allow researchers and citizens to verify economic trends in real time. The openness of these figures not only ensures government accountability but also empowers civil society to critically analyze purchasing power and macroeconomic stability using independent visualization and analysis tools.
