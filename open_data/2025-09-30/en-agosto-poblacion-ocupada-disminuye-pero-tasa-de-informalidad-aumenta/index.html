En agosto, población ocupada disminuye, pero tasa de informalidad aumenta

En agosto, población ocupada disminuye, pero tasa de informalidad aumenta

The INEGI report reveals an annual decline in the employed population, driven primarily by significant drops in the primary and secondary sectors, while the tertiary sector showed growth. This dynamic reflects a reconfiguration of the labor market where, although there are more jobs in services, the contraction in agricultural and industrial activities suggests structural challenges. For open data analysis, this report highlights the importance of breaking down information by economic sectors to understand hidden variations in national aggregates. The labor informality rate increased, demonstrating that a growing portion of the workforce lacks social protection and formal employment ties. This growth, both numerical and qualitative, implies that more workers are vulnerable as they operate in unregistered micro-businesses or subsistence agriculture. Transparency about these conditions is crucial, as informality distorts perceptions of the actual economic health and hinders the formulation of effective public policies that guarantee basic labor rights. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the need for granular and disaggregated data to assess the true socioeconomic impact. Public datasets must allow for cross-referencing variables such as sector, gender, and informality to visualize inequalities and long-term trends. The availability of this detailed information facilitates social auditing, academic research, and evidence-based decision-making, enabling the identification of structural gaps that aggregate indicators alone do not reveal.
