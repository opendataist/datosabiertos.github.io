Empleo público: INEC abrirá 262 plazas en todas las regiones del país; hay puestos con salarios de hasta ₡1,5 millones

Empleo público: INEC abrirá 262 plazas en todas las regiones del país; hay puestos con salarios de hasta ₡1,5 millones

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INEC) is conducting a large-scale hiring process to support major statistical operations, specifically the Agricultural Census and the Integrated Household Survey System. This initiative underscores the critical logistical capacity needed to generate accurate national data, which serves as a fundamental reference for shaping public policies and private investment strategies. By deploying robust field teams across the entire country, INEC ensures a detailed and comprehensive picture of the nation’s socio-economic reality. This recruitment drive significantly impacts regional employment, creating substantial temporary job opportunities beyond the metropolitan area. With positions ranging from interviewers and drivers to specialized agronomists and statisticians, the process decentralizes data collection efforts. This geographic distribution not only facilitates comprehensive coverage but also stimulates local economies by injecting income into various provinces, demonstrating how state-led data generation can serve as a catalyst for regional development and labor market stability. The relevance of this article to open data lies in its illustration of the foundational infrastructure required for reliable statistics. High-quality open data is not merely about publishing datasets; it depends on rigorous, transparent, and well-resourced collection processes, as depicted here. Understanding these operational complexities underscores the necessity of sustained public investment in statistical capacity, ensuring that the data released to the public and private sectors is credible, timely, and useful for evidence-based decision-making.
