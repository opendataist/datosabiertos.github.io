Los dos rubros que registraron bajas de precios en Cuyo durante febrero, según el INDEC

Los dos rubros que registraron bajas de precios en Cuyo durante febrero, según el INDEC

The National Consumer Price Index revealed that February’s inflation reached 2.4%, surpassing market expectations and consolidating an upward trend in regional prices. This increase, driven primarily by higher costs for meat and rent, places the Cuyo region in second place nationally for price increases, reflecting persistent inflationary pressure despite initial projections of stabilization. The revision of macroeconomic expectations marks a turning point, as for the first time during the current administration, the Central Bank’s Market Expectations Survey raised its forecasts for future inflation. Although a monthly deceleration is anticipated in the coming months, confidence in a rapid disinflation has been weakened, with upward adjustments to annual estimates and the calendar year 2025, indicating greater uncertainty in the economic trajectory. This article is crucial for the open data field because it exemplifies the critical importance of transparency and accessibility in official statistics. The timely publication of regional and sectoral breakdowns enables civil society, academics, and software developers to build tools for data analysis, verification, and visualization. This fosters government accountability and empowers citizens to better understand the real impact of economic policies on their daily lives.
