Big Brother banking: How the Biden administration weaponized financial surveillance to target conservatives – NaturalNews.com

Big Brother banking: How the Biden administration weaponized financial surveillance to target conservatives – NaturalNews.com

The article highlights serious allegations that federal agencies, in collaboration with private banks and tech companies, monitored financial transactions of conservatives under the guise of combating extremism. By flagging purchases linked to political beliefs or religious affiliations, these entities allegedly created a surveillance mechanism that targets individuals based on their ideology rather than criminal activity. This practice raises significant concerns about the erosion of financial privacy and the potential suppression of dissent through indirect means. These actions illustrate a broader trend where government entities outsource censorship and monitoring to private organizations, forming what critics describe as a "censorship-industrial complex." This collusion extends beyond banking to include digital platforms, suggesting a systemic effort to control information and restrict freedom of speech. The integration of advanced technologies threatens to expand this monitoring capability, potentially allowing for unprecedented oversight of citizens' daily activities and financial movements without adequate legal justification or oversight. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates because it demonstrates how financial and digital data can be weaponized for political suppression. It underscores the critical need for transparency in how government agencies interact with private data holders and the specific criteria used to classify data as "suspicious." Without strict accountability and clear limits, the open flow of information risks being manipulated to target specific viewpoints, threatening fundamental civil liberties and the democratic principle that dissent should never be criminalized through data tracking.
