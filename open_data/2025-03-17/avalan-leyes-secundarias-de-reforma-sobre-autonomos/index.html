Avalan leyes secundarias de reforma sobre autónomos

Avalan leyes secundarias de reforma sobre autónomos

The legislature approved reforms that transfer key transparency functions from the independent National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) to the Anti-Corruption Secretariat. Critics warn that this shift compromises judicial neutrality by allowing the government to act as both judge and party in information requests. The energy reform also passed, granting preeminence to state-owned entities such as Pemex and CFE over private companies. This move signals a strategic retreat toward state control of energy resources, sparking significant debate about democratic backsliding and institutional integrity. This is relevant to open data because it threatens the autonomy of information access mechanisms. Reducing independent oversight risks weakening the transparency infrastructure, potentially limiting citizens’ access to public records and undermining the principles of data openness and accountability.
