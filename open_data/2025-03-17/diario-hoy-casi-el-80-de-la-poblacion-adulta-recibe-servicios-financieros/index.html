Diario HOY | Casi el 80% de la población adulta recibe servicios financieros

Paraguay has experienced extraordinary progress in financial inclusion, with its customer base doubling over the past decade. This process, significantly accelerated in 2024, demonstrates that access to the monetary system is no longer limited to opening accounts, but involves the active use of services such as credit and digital transfers. Gender parity in banking participation reflects a comprehensive effort toward equity, while the expansion of basic accounts aims to integrate the base of the pyramid into the formal economy. The relevance of this article to the field of open data lies in the quality and accessibility of the information that underpins these metrics. To monitor the real progress of inclusion and evaluate the impact of public policies, it is essential to have accurate statistical data, disaggregated by gender and location, from official sources such as the Central Bank of Paraguay (BCP) and the National Institute of Statistics (INE). Transparency in these indicators enables civil society and researchers to analyze historical and current trends, fostering accountability. Finally, the visibility of these data is crucial for promoting financial education and technological innovation. With clear statistics on per capita savings and loans, policymakers can identify gaps and design more effective strategies. The ability to analyze open data on financial behavior not only empowers citizens to make informed decisions but also encourages institutions to improve their services, ensuring that economic growth translates into tangible social well-being.
