España se adhiere a la Carta Internacional de Datos Abiertos para impulsar la transparencia y la participación ciudadana

España se adhiere a la Carta Internacional de Datos Abiertos para impulsar la transparencia y la participación ciudadana

Spain has officially joined the International Charter of Open Data, a global initiative aimed at enhancing transparency, innovation, and civic participation through the proactive release of public information. This commitment signals a strategic shift toward making data accessible, understandable, and interoperable by default, ensuring that government resources foster inclusive economic development and social impact. The move reinforces the nation's dedication to open governance principles, aligning national policies with international standards for responsible data management. The announcement coincides with the conclusion of the Global Open Government Alliance summit, where Spain reaffirmed its support for its State Open Strategy and adopted the Vitoria-Gasteiz Declaration. By prioritizing rights, democratic integrity, and civic space, the government underscores that open data is not merely a technical requirement but a fundamental pillar of modern democracy. This holistic approach seeks to build more responsible institutions that actively engage citizens and protect fundamental rights through accountable governance. This development is highly relevant to open data because it elevates data openness from a regulatory obligation to a core democratic value. It emphasizes interoperability and user-centric design, encouraging stakeholders to create solutions that improve public services and drive inclusive growth. By committing to these rigorous standards, Spain sets a benchmark for how public institutions can leverage data responsibly to strengthen trust, promote accountability, and empower communities through meaningful civic engagement.
