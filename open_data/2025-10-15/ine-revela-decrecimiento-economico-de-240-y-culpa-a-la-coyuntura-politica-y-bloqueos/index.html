INE revela decrecimiento económico de -2,40% y culpa a la coyuntura política y bloqueos

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) reports Bolivia’s economic contraction, attributing it primarily to political unrest and blockades that disrupted production chains. This highlights how social instability directly damages economic indicators, underscoring the need for open data to monitor such shocks transparently. Recovery is expected as non-traditional exports and transport improve once conflicts subside. This illustrates the value of timely, accessible national statistics in identifying drivers of volatility and guiding policy responses effectively. Ultimately, the article demonstrates how open economic data reveals the tangible impact of governance issues on growth, enabling better public scrutiny and informed decision-making regarding national stability and development trajectories.
