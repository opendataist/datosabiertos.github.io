APYMEC muestra que sigue un fuerte crecimiento de la compraventa de vivienda, pese a su descenso en julio

APYMEC muestra que sigue un fuerte crecimiento de la compraventa de vivienda, pese a su descenso en julio

The Cuenca construction sector reports a significant rise in home sales during the first seven months of 2025, reaching a five-year high for this period. Although recent months have seen a decline compared to the sharp early-year growth, the overall trend indicates a robust recovery in transaction volume. This resurgence suggests a stabilizing market, yet it contrasts with the stagnation of new construction projects, highlighting a disparity between sales activity and housing supply development. Despite the increased number of transactions, the market remains heavily dominated by the resale of existing properties, which accounts for nearly ninety-four percent of operations. This extreme reliance on the secondary market prevents the necessary revitalization of subsectors linked to new builds. The lack of diversification into new housing implies that the sector is not fully recovering its breadth, leaving young buyers and specific demographic needs inadequately served by the current market dynamics. This data is critical for open data initiatives as it illustrates the necessity of transparent, granular real estate statistics to inform urban planning and policy. Accurate tracking of sales volume versus housing type reveals systemic imbalances that policymakers might otherwise miss. By making such detailed market indicators publicly accessible, stakeholders can better understand the correlation between sales trends and construction supply, facilitating data-driven decisions to address housing affordability and stimulate sustainable sector growth.
