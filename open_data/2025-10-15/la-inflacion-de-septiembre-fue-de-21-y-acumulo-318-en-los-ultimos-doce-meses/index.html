La inflación de septiembre fue de 2,1% y acumuló 31,8% en los últimos doce meses

La inflación de septiembre fue de 2,1% y acumuló 31,8% en los últimos doce meses

The article reports a slight acceleration in inflation to 2.1% in September, bringing the annual rate to nearly 32%. This increase occurred during a period of high currency volatility, particularly following provincial elections, which fueled concerns about price pass-throughs. Housing, transportation, and education recorded the sharpest monthly increases, driven by adjustments in rents, fuel, and services, while food prices remained below the general average, showing some moderation compared to previous months. Market forecasts initially aligned on an acceleration toward the 2% mark but differed on the exact magnitude, with estimates ranging between 2.0% and 2.4%. These projections highlight the tension between seasonal factors, such as clothing price hikes, and structural pressures like regulated service costs and currency depreciation. Economic analysts anticipate that this upward trend will persist briefly before stabilizing later in the year, suggesting a complex path for household purchasing power.
