400 Posts Remain Vacant in GMC Kathua, Reveals RTI

A Right to Information disclosure reveals significant staffing shortages at Government Medical College Kathua, with nearly half of all sanctioned positions remaining vacant. This deficit persists despite the hospital managing a substantial volume of medical procedures, including tens of thousands of surgeries and handling numerous patient deaths over the past five years. The data highlights a critical gap between workforce capacity and healthcare demands, emphasizing how resource constraints may impact service delivery. Such findings illustrate the necessity for transparent reporting in public institutions to ensure accountability and adequate staffing levels for effective patient care. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public access to government records can expose systemic operational challenges. By making specific administrative and health statistics available, it empowers citizens to scrutinize institutional performance and advocate for necessary reforms in public healthcare infrastructure.
