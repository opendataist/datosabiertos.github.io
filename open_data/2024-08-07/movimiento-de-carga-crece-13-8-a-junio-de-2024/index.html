Movimiento de carga crece 13.8% a junio de 2024

Movimiento de carga crece 13.8% a junio de 2024

The National Port System in Panama experienced significant growth in cargo movement during the first half of 2024, marking the highest volume in four years. This surge is primarily driven by a substantial increase in containerized and bulk cargo, while general cargo saw a notable decline. The overall year-on-year variation demonstrates robust logistical performance and increasing trade activity across the region’s key maritime infrastructure. This trend highlights the critical role of Panama as a global transit hub, with container throughput reaching record levels. The data indicates a structural shift in cargo types, where containerized freight leads the growth trajectory, outpacing other categories. Such performance underscores the efficiency and capacity of current port operations to handle escalating international demand and complex supply chain requirements. For the open data community, these statistics represent a vital case study in monitoring global logistics flows through accessible public datasets. Reliable, timely government figures enable better analysis of economic trends and infrastructure performance. Access to such granular data supports transparency, allows for accurate predictive modeling, and facilitates informed decision-making for stakeholders interested in trade dynamics and port optimization.
