Ministry Of Statistics Launches E-Sankhyiki Portal For Enhanced Data Transparency And Digital Governance

India’s Ministry of Statistics has launched the e-Sankhyiki portal, a major step toward digital governance and open data accessibility. By offering comprehensive data catalogs and macro indicators with visualization tools, the platform empowers users to easily discover, filter, and download official statistical assets, significantly enhancing transparency in public information. Crucially, this initiative is anchored by rigorous cybersecurity measures, including cloud storage, regular audits, and strict adherence to national security guidelines. These protections ensure that while data becomes more accessible and open for public use, the integrity and safety of sensitive information are preserved against evolving cyber threats. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates a balanced model where transparency coexists with robust security. By making government data more discoverable and user-friendly while maintaining high safety standards, India sets a precedent for how nations can responsibly leverage technology to foster trust and enable data-driven decision-making.
