La Nación / MDS ejercerá controles aleatorios y permanentes para Hambre Cero

The success of the Hambre Cero (Zero Hunger) program depends on rigorous monitoring to ensure food quality and timely delivery to vulnerable children. By leveraging data from the National Statistics Institute, the state precisely targets resources, establishing a framework in which transparency and accountability are paramount for the effective implementation of social welfare. To achieve this, the government is deploying digital platforms that enable citizens to access information and report issues directly. This technological integration transforms beneficiaries and community members into active auditors, creating a robust system of social oversight that complements traditional physical inspections and ensures institutional integrity. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public datasets and accessible digital tools can enhance government transparency. By making control mechanisms visible and participatory, the program illustrates the power of open information in building trust and ensuring that public resources effectively reach their intended social objectives.
