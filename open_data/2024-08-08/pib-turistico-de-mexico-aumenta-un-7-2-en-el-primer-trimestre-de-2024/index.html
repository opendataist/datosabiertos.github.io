PIB turístico de México aumenta un 7.2% en el primer trimestre de 2024

Mexico’s tourism sector shows a robust recovery, significantly expanding its contribution to GDP in early 2024 compared to pre-pandemic levels. This growth outpaces that of the national economy, driven primarily by strong performance in tourism services and increased domestic consumption, highlighting the sector’s resilience and critical role in local development. The data confirms sustained momentum, with policy interventions successfully positioning tourism as a key economic engine. By fostering local benefits and social reconciliation, these strategies aim to ensure long-term stability and equitable growth for communities reliant on tourism activities. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on standardized, publicly accessible statistical indicators published by INEGI. It illustrates how open governmental datasets enable transparent monitoring of economic sectors, allowing stakeholders to verify recovery trends and assess the impact of public policies through verifiable, high-quality information.
