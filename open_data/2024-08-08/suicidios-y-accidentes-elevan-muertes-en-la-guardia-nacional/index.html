Suicidios y accidentes elevan muertes en la Guardia Nacional

The INEGI’s 2024 Census reveals a significant escalation in operational risks for Mexico’s National Guard, highlighted by a sharp rise in fatalities and armed confrontations during 2023. This data underscores the deteriorating security environment and the increasing exposure of public security personnel to lethal threats, indicating a challenging landscape for maintaining institutional stability. The census also provides critical insights into personnel management, noting substantial growth in training intake alongside higher dropout rates. These figures suggest systemic pressures within recruitment and retention processes, while detailed statistics on traffic accidents and legal detentions further illustrate the complex scope of the Guard’s daily responsibilities and operational challenges. This publication is vital for the open data community as it demonstrates how transparent, granular government statistics enable independent analysis of public policy efficacy. By making such detailed operational metrics accessible, citizens and researchers can hold institutions accountable, fostering data-driven debates on security strategies and resource allocation.
