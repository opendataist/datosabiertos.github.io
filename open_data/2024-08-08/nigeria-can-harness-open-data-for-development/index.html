‘Nigeria can harness open data for development’

Gideon Abegunrin argues that open data serves as a critical catalyst for sustainable development in Nigeria, comparable to oil in economic value. By prioritizing accessible data systems, the nation can harness the Fourth Industrial Revolution to drive innovation and economic growth. This approach transforms data into a public good that empowers citizens and policymakers alike to make informed decisions. The availability of transparent information significantly reduces corruption and inefficiency within public institutions. When data is accessible to everyone, it fosters accountability, allowing citizens to scrutinize government actions effectively. This transparency not only improves public service delivery but also builds trust between the state and its people, creating a more responsive governance structure. Beyond governance, open data plays a vital role in addressing global challenges like climate change by enabling environmental monitoring. It is equally essential for specialized sectors, such as aviation, to track performance and consolidate efforts for prosperity. This highlights open data’s relevance as a universal tool for enhancing transparency, driving accountability, and supporting evidence-based policy across diverse industries.
