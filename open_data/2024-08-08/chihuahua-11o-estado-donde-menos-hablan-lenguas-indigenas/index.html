Chihuahua, 11º estado donde menos hablan lenguas indígenas

The ENADID 2023 reveals that Chihuahua has one of Mexico’s lowest rates of indigenous language speakers. This scarcity highlights significant regional disparities in linguistic diversity across the country. The data is vital for open data initiatives aiming to monitor cultural preservation and inform inclusive public policies effectively.
