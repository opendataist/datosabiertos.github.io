California Mid-State Fair releases statistics for 2024 event

The 2024 California Mid-State Fair achieved record-breaking financial success, driven by strong community engagement and high attendance. Significant revenue was generated through livestock and industrial arts auctions, alongside robust sales from carnival rides and concessions. This financial health underscores the event’s vital role in supporting local agricultural traditions and regional economic vitality. The fair’s appeal extends beyond commerce, serving as a major cultural hub that hosts thousands of attendees for live music, rodeo competitions, and artisan showcases. The increased visitor numbers and sold-out concerts highlight its status as a premier destination for entertainment and community celebration, reinforcing social ties within San Luis Obispo County. This article is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the importance of transparent, public-facing metrics in evaluating public institutions. By publishing detailed financial and attendance figures, the fair demonstrates accountability and provides a benchmark for other civic organizations to measure success. Such data enables stakeholders to assess the effectiveness of public funding and the broader impact of community-driven events on local development.
