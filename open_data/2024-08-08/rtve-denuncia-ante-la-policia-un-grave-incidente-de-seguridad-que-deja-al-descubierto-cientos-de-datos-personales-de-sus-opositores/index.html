RTVE denuncia ante la Policía un grave incidente de seguridad que deja al descubierto cientos de datos personales de sus opositores

RTVE denuncia ante la Policía un grave incidente de seguridad que deja al descubierto cientos de datos personales de sus opositores

The article details a significant security breach at the recruitment platform of RTVE, the Spanish public broadcaster, where unauthorized downloads exposed hundreds of personal data points belonging to candidates participating in selection processes. This incident, involving data managed by the external contractor CEGOS, forced RTVE to immediately block access and file a police report, highlighting severe vulnerabilities in the handling of sensitive citizen information during public administrative procedures. The corporation emphasized that, despite holding security certifications, the failure necessitated urgent audits and notifications to data protection authorities, underscoring the critical responsibility of public entities to ensure robust data governance. By initiating legal proceedings and demanding accountability from the service provider, RTVE demonstrated the transparency and procedural rigor required when personal data is compromised, reinforcing the legal obligations surrounding privacy and digital security in public service management. This event is highly relevant to open data discussions, as it illustrates the delicate balance between transparency and individual privacy. While open data initiatives promote accessibility and accountability, this case serves as a stark warning about the risks associated with inadequate data protection mechanisms in public portals. It highlights that true openness cannot exist without foundational security, reminding stakeholders that the release or management of public information must prioritize the safeguarding of personal rights to maintain public trust and legal compliance.
