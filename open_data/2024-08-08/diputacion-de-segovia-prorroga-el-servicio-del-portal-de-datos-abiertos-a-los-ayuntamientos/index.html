Diputación de Segovia prorroga el servicio del Portal de Datos Abiertos a los ayuntamientos

Diputación de Segovia prorroga el servicio del Portal de Datos Abiertos a los ayuntamientos

Segovia expands its Open Data Portal, enabling municipalities to display direct access banners for citizens and businesses. This initiative fosters a modern, transparent administration by promoting the reuse of public data and supporting informed socio-economic analysis. By facilitating the diagnosis of provincial development, the portal aids in strategic planning and builds trust to attract local investment. It provides diverse indicators, ranging from unemployment to demographics, through visual tools and downloadable formats, ensuring broad accessibility for all users. This effort aligns with transparency laws, significantly enhancing access to public information. It is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how regional institutions can structure and distribute granular municipal data to drive accountability, economic growth, and civic engagement.
