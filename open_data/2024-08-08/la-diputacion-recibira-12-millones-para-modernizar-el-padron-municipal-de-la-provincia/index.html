La Diputación recibirá 1,2 millones para modernizar el padrón municipal de la provincia

The Provincial Council of Ourense is modernizing municipal census systems through a new digital platform, enabling real-time data exchange between local councils and national statistical institutes. This initiative aims to streamline population tracking and unify territorial reference data, fostering closer cooperation among municipalities and enhancing public service delivery. The project leverages European recovery funds to implement a robust infrastructure that differentiates household databases from individual records. By ensuring homogeneous services and more frequent updates on population trends, the system significantly improves administrative efficiency and data accuracy for all local entities involved in the network. This case exemplifies open data principles by prioritizing interoperability and standardized information sharing across government levels. It demonstrates how public funds can be used to break down data silos, creating transparent, accessible, and reliable datasets that benefit both citizens and institutional decision-making processes.
