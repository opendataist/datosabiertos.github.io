Hacia los 49 millones de habitantes, por Editorial

Hacia los 49 millones de habitantes, por Editorial

Spain’s population has reached a historical peak, driven primarily by sustained immigration from countries such as Colombia and Venezuela. This demographic shift not only increases the total number of residents but also significantly expands the foreign-born community, highlighting a structural change in the country’s social fabric. The data reveals that immigrants integrate into the Spanish labor market more effectively than in other major European countries, with an exceptionally high employment rate. This strong economic participation demonstrates that Spain’s current economic health allows for the absorption of foreign workers, contradicting narratives that view migration solely as a burden rather than a vital resource for workforce stability. This report is relevant to open data because it utilizes transparent, high-quality statistics from official institutes to debunk extremist discourses with measurable reality. By providing clear, accessible evidence of immigrants’ positive economic impact, it promotes data-driven public debate and supports policies based on factual labor needs rather than political prejudice, ensuring that demographic trends inform rational governance.
