Pago de servicio de agua, maíz y huevos incidieron en la inflación que se situó en 1.47% a septiembre

The article highlights a significant deceleration in inflation, reaching historically low levels that fall below the central bank’s target threshold. This trend is driven by a general stabilization in price levels, particularly through price drops in essential agricultural products like vegetables and proteins. For policymakers, this data is crucial as it validates the effectiveness of current monetary strategies and supports the case for maintaining an accommodative policy to foster economic stability without triggering price volatility. For the open data community, this report underscores the importance of transparent, high-frequency economic indicators in shaping public policy and social welfare. The direct link between inflation metrics and the determination of the minimum wage illustrates how granular data sets influence real-world financial decisions for vulnerable populations. By making these detailed breakdowns of consumer price indices and basket costs publicly available, authorities enable researchers and journalists to scrutinize the impact of monetary policy on different socioeconomic groups. Furthermore, the detailed segmentation of price changes across various expenditure divisions and geographic areas provides a rich dataset for inequality analysis. This level of granularity allows for more precise monitoring of how external shocks, such as weather events, disproportionately affect specific sectors. Open access to this structured statistical information empowers civil society to hold institutions accountable, ensuring that inflation trends are not just numbers, but understood as vital indicators of household purchasing power and economic health.
