¿Qué responde García Harfuch sobre Seguritech, registro y reducción de homicidios en Guanajuato?

¿Qué responde García Harfuch sobre Seguritech, registro y reducción de homicidios en Guanajuato?

Federal security official Omar García Harfuch emphasizes that reducing violence in Guanajuato requires a continuous, intelligence-driven approach rather than targeting a specific quota of arrests. He explains that criminal organizations are fluid and atomize upon the capture of key figures, necessitating constant information exchange with state authorities and intelligence centers to identify and neutralize new threats dynamically. Regarding statistical discrepancies in homicide counts, Harfuch highlights efforts to close the gap between preliminary state reports and final official data. He attributes past underreporting to local prosecutors failing to submit all cases, noting that improved coordination has recently led to more accurate and verified numbers, significantly lowering the recorded death toll compared to previous years. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical importance of data transparency and consistency in public security metrics. It underscores how discrepancies between different reporting entities can obscure the true picture of violence, thereby challenging the reliability of available datasets for researchers, journalists, and civil society analyzing the effectiveness of security policies.
