If renewables are cheaper, why the secrecy?

If renewables are cheaper, why the secrecy?

The NSW government faces criticism for withholding commissioned studies on how its renewable energy policies affect electricity prices. These reports, funded by taxpayers, are expected to reveal higher power bills due to emissions reduction targets. This opacity in public data hinders transparent accountability regarding the true costs of climate policy implementation.
