Veracruz cerró enero con baja en comercio al menudeo y al mayoreo

Veracruz experienced a decline in wholesale and retail sales in January 2024 compared to the previous year. Despite this drop in revenue, both sectors saw minor increases in employment and significant rises in real average wages. This indicates that while business activity slowed, worker compensation improved. These trends highlight the complexity of economic indicators, where sales figures alone do not capture wage dynamics. Understanding these nuances is crucial for interpreting economic health beyond simple revenue metrics. This data is relevant to open data initiatives by showcasing how INEGI publishes granular, time-series statistics. Access to such detailed breakdowns allows for transparent analysis of sector-specific performance, employment, and compensation trends in real economic contexts.
