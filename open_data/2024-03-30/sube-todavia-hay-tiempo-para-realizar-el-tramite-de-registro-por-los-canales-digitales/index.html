SUBE: todavía hay tiempo para realizar el trámite de registro por los canales digitales

The article highlights a mandatory fare differentiation for public transport users in Buenos Aires, imposing higher fares on those without registered SUBE cards starting April 1st. However, the registration process remains permanently open, allowing users to regularize their status at any time to access reduced rates. This policy underscores the critical role of digital identity in equitable urban mobility. By linking financial benefits to verified personal data, the system promotes transparency and reduces fare evasion, ensuring that subsidies reach intended beneficiaries while maintaining service sustainability for all riders. For open data, this demonstrates how integrated digital platforms can facilitate inclusive access to public services. The ability to register remotely via web and mobile apps illustrates modern administrative efficiency, while the underlying data architecture supports accurate usage tracking, essential for evidence-based transport planning and policy adjustment.
