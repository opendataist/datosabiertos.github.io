Over 370 Local Authority Byelaws and Policies on the Recreational Use of Drones are now availableon Drone Scene

Grey Arrows Drone Club has aggregated extensive local regulations governing recreational drone use into the Drone Scene platform. This comprehensive resource, derived from numerous freedom of information requests, empowers operators by clarifying where they can legally fly without needing to navigate fragmented local authority websites. The integration of precise boundary data allows users to quickly identify specific byelaws and policies applicable to their intended location. By centralizing this information, the platform removes the guesswork from regulatory compliance, ensuring that drone pilots have immediate access to the rules governing publicly owned land across the UK. This initiative significantly advances open data by transforming disparate, hard-to-find legal texts into a unified, accessible digital resource. It demonstrates how crowdsourced or systematically aggregated public data can solve real-world compliance challenges, encouraging responsible drone usage through transparency and easy information retrieval for the community.
