ChatGPT now saves chat history even if you've opted out of sharing training data

ChatGPT now saves chat history even if you've opted out of sharing training data

OpenAI has separated data storage from model training, allowing users to save chat history without inadvertently contributing it to the AI’s development. This change empowers individuals to maintain personalized convenience while strictly controlling their privacy settings, resolving the previous dilemma where opting out of training meant losing history entirely. The update addresses critical security concerns highlighted by incidents where corporate secrets were exposed through careless usage. By providing clear opt-out mechanisms, the company acknowledges the need for robust privacy safeguards, ensuring that sensitive information remains contained within the user’s session rather than being perpetuated in future model outputs. This distinction is vital for the open_data movement, as it underscores the necessity of transparent data governance. It demonstrates that technological advancement does not require sacrificing individual rights, offering a model where users can benefit from AI features while maintaining strict sovereignty over their personal information and digital footprint.
