Satellogic Releases Open Dataset to Train AI Models

Satellogic opens a massive high-resolution satellite dataset under a permissive Creative Commons license, enabling broad commercial and research access. This move supports collaborative efforts to build foundational Earth observation AI models, shifting reliance from manual analysis to automated, real-time insights. For open data, this demonstrates how proprietary geospatial assets can be shared openly to accelerate global AI innovation while maintaining clear attribution standards.
