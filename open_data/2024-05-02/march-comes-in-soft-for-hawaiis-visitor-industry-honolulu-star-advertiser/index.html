March comes in soft for Hawaii’s visitor industry | Honolulu Star-Advertiser

Hawaii’s visitor census and spending have fallen for nine consecutive months, signaling a sustained downturn in tourism. This persistent decline threatens local economic stability, as the sector remains a critical revenue driver for the islands. The data highlights the vulnerability of destinations reliant on international travel. The news is relevant to open data because transparent, timely government statistics are essential for tracking these economic shifts. Open datasets allow researchers and businesses to analyze trends, forecast impacts, and develop strategies to adapt to changing travel behaviors and restore industry health.
