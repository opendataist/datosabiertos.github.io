Orissa High Court Launches 'Online RTI Portal' For District Judiciary

Orissa High Court Launches 'Online RTI Portal' For District Judiciary

The Orissa High Court digitized its Right to Information processes, enabling citizens to access judicial records via a dedicated online portal. This initiative streamlines transparency and public accountability within the state’s district judiciary. By mandating digital submission, the court ensures efficient handling of information requests while adhering to established legal guidelines. This modernization aligns local practices with broader judicial directives aimed at enhancing institutional openness. This development is crucial for open data advocates as it demonstrates the successful integration of public transparency mechanisms into the legal sector, setting a precedent for accessible, digitized governance.
