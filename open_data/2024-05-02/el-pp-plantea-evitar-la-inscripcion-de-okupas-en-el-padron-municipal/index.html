El PP plantea evitar la inscripción de okupas en el padrón municipal

The article highlights political tensions in Galicia over housing occupation laws, contrasting the regional government’s push for stricter penalties with criticisms of the central government’s housing framework. It underscores how current legislation is perceived as enabling illegal occupation, leading to decreased property values and heightened public insecurity among residents. This issue is relevant to open data because accurate statistics on squatting cases are crucial for understanding the scale of the crisis and evaluating policy effectiveness. Reliable, transparent data allows citizens and authorities to monitor trends, verify claims about the prevalence of occupations, and assess whether legal reforms truly address the root causes of the problem.
