Demanda masiva contra Microsoft y OpenAI: un enfrentamiento por los derechos de autor en la era de la inteligencia artificial

The lawsuit against Microsoft and OpenAI highlights the tension between AI innovation and the protection of journalistic intellectual property. The conflict arises because major tech companies are using copyrighted texts without permission to train their models, raising ethical concerns about the reproduction of content and the lack of compensation for original creators. This case underscores the urgent need to establish clear legal frameworks that regulate the use of copyrighted data. Resolving these disputes will not only determine how companies access information but also influence the creation of legislation that balances technological progress with authors' rights. This is relevant to open data because it questions the foundations of information collection. If the unauthorized use of copyrighted content for AI training is deemed illegal, access to public data for commercial purposes could be restricted. This directly impacts transparency and the availability of resources for the ethical and sustainable development of artificial intelligence.
