Estar alerta también en verano: los 5 ciberataques más comunes que pueden arruinar tus vacaciones

Estar alerta también en verano: los 5 ciberataques más comunes que pueden arruinar tus vacaciones

The article warns about the rise in cyberattacks during the summer, when users are more vulnerable due to their vacations and the use of less secure devices such as smartphones. Cybercriminals exploit distraction and urgency through social engineering tactics like phishing, vishing, and smishing, deceiving victims into handing over sensitive data, passwords, or identity information under fictitious pretexts such as blocked accounts or family emergencies. The main relevance of this text for the *open data* community lies in the critical exposure of personal data. The scams described aim to collect private information (names, national ID numbers, phone numbers) that, once compromised, can be used for identity theft or financial fraud. This underscores that personal data, although often handled in open or semi-open environments, requires rigorous protection against human manipulation and the lack of technical security tools on mobile devices. The key conclusion is that individual awareness and identity verification are the best defenses against these scams. Since technology alone (such as antivirus software) is insufficient when users interact by mistake, it is crucial to maintain skepticism toward urgent calls or messages and always verify the contact’s identity through official channels. Protecting privacy in the *open data* era depends not only on transparent access to information but also on citizens’ proactive security against social deception.
