Pide INAI informar sobre pagos de pensiones

Pide INAI informar sobre pagos de pensiones

The INAI ordered the Welfare Secretariat to clarify the procedure for elderly citizens to collect unpaid pensions, exposing systemic failures in the management of social benefits. This highlights critical gaps in institutional accountability and the effective exercise of the right to access information. Such demands for transparency are vital for open data, as they ensure that government data accurately reflects reality and remains accessible. Without clear and reliable information structures, citizens cannot effectively claim their social rights or verify the integrity of public service delivery mechanisms.
