El Gobierno cambiará la clasificación de las profesiones para incluir los nuevos empleos

The article underscores the urgent need to update Spain’s National Economic Activities Classification (CNAE) to reflect the rapid transformations driven by the digital economy and environmental business models. By integrating new professional categories and eliminating obsolete ones, the revision aims to ensure that statistical records and social security affiliations accurately mirror current labor market realities. This alignment is crucial for generating reliable data that supports effective economic policy and administrative management in a globalized context. The relevance to open data lies in the resulting enhancement of data quality and interoperability within the national statistical system. As public administrations adapt their information systems to the new coding standards, the resulting datasets will become more consistent and comparable across sectors. Furthermore, alignment with European Union standards ensures that Spain’s economic indicators remain harmonized with those of other member states, facilitating cross-border data analysis and improving the transparency and utility of publicly available economic information. The implementation timeline requires institutions to prepare for a full transition by early 2025, with extended deadlines for some registries. This coordinated effort underscores the importance of standardizing data structures to maintain high-quality open government data. By updating these foundational classifications, Spain ensures that its statistical outputs remain relevant, accurate, and accessible, thereby supporting informed decision-making and fostering a more transparent economic environment for researchers, businesses, and citizens.
