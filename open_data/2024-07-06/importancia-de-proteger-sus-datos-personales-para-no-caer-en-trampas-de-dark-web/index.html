Importancia de proteger sus datos personales para no caer en trampas de Dark Web

The article highlights the growing threat of cybercrime in Colombia, particularly on the Dark Web, where personal data is easily sold. It emphasizes the urgent need for individuals and organizations to adopt robust security measures, such as strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, and encryption, to protect sensitive information from theft and exploitation. Regarding its relevance to open data, the text underscores a critical privacy risk: while some data is openly accessible or shared, the illicit trade on dark markets demonstrates the severe consequences of inadequate data protection. This highlights the tension between data availability and security, stressing that without proper safeguards, open or shared data can become a commodity for criminal activity, necessitating stricter ethical and technical standards in data handling.
