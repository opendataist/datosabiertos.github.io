Por qué no hay que responder las llamadas de WhatsApp con prefijo +63

The article highlights a surge in scam calls via instant messaging apps, particularly warning users against answering calls with the +63 prefix, which originates from the Philippines. It explains that cybercriminals frequently exploit this region to conduct extortion and fraud, urging individuals to remain vigilant and avoid responding to unsolicited international numbers to protect their personal information and financial security. To mitigate these risks, the text advises users to actively monitor incoming call origins and utilize WhatsApp’s built-in features to block and report suspicious contacts. By identifying common scamming prefixes from various countries, users can preemptively filter out malicious traffic, thereby reducing their exposure to sophisticated digital theft schemes that rely on social engineering and urgency. This content is relevant to open data as it underscores the critical importance of leveraging publicly available telecommunications standards, such as the ITU’s international dialing codes, for public safety. Understanding these open data sets allows users to implement effective defensive strategies against digital fraud, demonstrating how accessible information infrastructure can empower individuals to navigate and secure their interactions in an increasingly connected digital landscape.
