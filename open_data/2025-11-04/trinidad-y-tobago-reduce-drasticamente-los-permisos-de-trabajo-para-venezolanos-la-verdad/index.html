Trinidad y Tobago reduce drásticamente los permisos de trabajo para venezolanos - La Verdad

Trinidad y Tobago reduce drásticamente los permisos de trabajo para venezolanos - La Verdad

Trinidad and Tobago has drastically restricted Venezuelan labor mobility, cutting work permits by 82% amid escalating diplomatic tensions and national security concerns. This policy reflects a shift toward exclusion, driven by claims that the migrant presence could harbor covert threats or criminal activities that undermine internal stability. The abrupt reduction stands in stark contrast to the country’s previous openness, illustrating how political changes can quickly transform migrant integration into a tool of control. The context of violence and suspicions of infiltration have created a climate of urgency, prioritizing state protection over workers’ rights and the international protection of asylum seekers. This case highlights a critical challenge for open data: the transparency of migration statistics is essential for monitoring social impacts and human rights. However, when data are manipulated for political purposes or obscured behind security narratives, independent verification becomes difficult, hindering accountability and undermining civil society’s ability to demand evidence-based policies rather than those driven by speculation.
