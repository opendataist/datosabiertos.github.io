José Abellán, cardiólogo: ''Para prevenir enfermedades cardiovasculares hay que estar sincronizados con el planeta. No es solo dormir bien, hay que despertarse temprano''

Cardiovascular diseases remain a leading cause of mortality worldwide, prompting cardiologist José Abellán to emphasize prevention strategies through both digital platforms and mainstream television. His recent appearance on a popular Spanish program highlights the critical need for public awareness regarding lifestyle factors that significantly influence heart health, shifting the focus from medical treatment to proactive daily habits. His approach is built on four pillars: natural nutrition free of ultra-processed foods, regular aerobic and strength exercise, alignment with natural circadian rhythms, and meaningful social connections. He argues that the alarming rise in heart issues among young adults stems from sedentary lifestyles and poor diet, making early intervention and education essential. This content is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores how publicly accessible, high-quality health information can democratize wellness. By translating complex medical advice into digestible, shareable content, it demonstrates the power of transparent data dissemination in addressing public health crises such as obesity and sedentarism.
