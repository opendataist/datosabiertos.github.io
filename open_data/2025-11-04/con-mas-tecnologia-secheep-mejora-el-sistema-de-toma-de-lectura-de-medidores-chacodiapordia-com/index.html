Con más tecnología, Secheep mejora el sistema de toma de lectura de medidores - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

Con más tecnología, Secheep mejora el sistema de toma de lectura de medidores - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

SECHEEP has implemented a digital reading system for electricity meters that significantly enhances data precision and process transparency. By combining photographic evidence with geolocation, the utility creates a verifiable, traceable record of consumption data. This technological shift moves away from traditional manual verification, establishing a robust framework where every reading is backed by concrete, timestamped visual proof. The system empowers customers with unprecedented access to their historical consumption data, allowing them to view photos and exact times of readings. This accessibility addresses common disputes by providing objective evidence in case of discrepancies, thereby reducing human error and preventing irregularities. Furthermore, automated alerts help identify anomalies immediately, ensuring that potential meter issues or unusual consumption patterns are detected promptly rather than being discovered months later. This initiative is highly relevant to open data principles as it demonstrates how structured, accessible digital records can foster trust between service providers and citizens. The transparent availability of georeferenced and photographic data sets a benchmark for accountability in public utilities. It illustrates the broader value of open information practices, where verifiable data serves not just operational efficiency, but also strengthens the social contract through enhanced visibility and customer empowerment.
