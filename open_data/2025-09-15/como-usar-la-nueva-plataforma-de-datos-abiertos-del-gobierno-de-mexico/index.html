¿Cómo usar la nueva plataforma de datos abiertos del gobierno de México?

¿Cómo usar la nueva plataforma de datos abiertos del gobierno de México?

Mexico’s new National Open Data Platform addresses historical access barriers through strict technical standardization, requiring structured and interoperable formats. This decision prioritizes data quality and the capacity for automated processing over mere resource accumulation, eliminating the fragmentation and broken links that characterized the previous system. By rejecting unstructured formats, the platform ensures that information is genuinely useful and machine-processable, laying the foundation for a robust and reliable data infrastructure. To facilitate public use, the platform offers interactive visualizations and real-time access, enabling citizens to filter data and generate charts without needing advanced technical skills or downloading large datasets. At the same time, it integrates advanced tools such as APIs and future subscription systems for developers and academics, facilitating direct data integration into applications. This dual approach ensures that both general users and specialists can efficiently access, analyze, and monitor updates, transforming data querying into a seamless and enriching experience. This approach is significant for the open data culture because it establishes an interoperability and permanence standard that goes beyond institutional voluntariness. By centralizing data backup and requiring clear hierarchies within government agencies, the platform mitigates the risk of losing public information during bureaucratic changes. The initiative demonstrates that the true value of open data lies in its technical accessibility and its ability to generate sustainable transparency, empowering civil society with verifiable and up-to-date information.
