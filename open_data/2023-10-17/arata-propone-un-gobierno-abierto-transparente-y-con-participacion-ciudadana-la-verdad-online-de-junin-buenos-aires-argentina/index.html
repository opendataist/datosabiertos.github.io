Arata propone un gobierno abierto, transparente y con participación ciudadana - La Verdad Online de Junín, Buenos Aires, Argentina

Valeria Arata places transparency and citizen participation at the heart of her governance strategy, with the aim of reversing current restrictions on public information. She criticizes the lack of accessible data on municipal staff and expenditures, highlighting the negative impact this has on civic trust. Her platform emphasizes that open data and technological innovation are essential for fostering a knowledge-based economy and enabling direct dialogue between representatives and the community. Through the implementation of participatory planning tools, she seeks to ensure that citizens can effectively monitor municipal management via both virtual and in-person channels. This proposal is highly relevant to open data, as it explicitly links digital transparency with improved public services. It demonstrates how making government data accessible drives economic development and strengthens democratic accountability, showing that open information is not merely a technical requirement but a fundamental tool for social collaboration and smart city development.
