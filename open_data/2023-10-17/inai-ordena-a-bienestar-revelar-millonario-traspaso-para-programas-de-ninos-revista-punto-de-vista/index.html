INAI ordena a Bienestar revelar millonario traspaso para programas de niños | Revista Punto de Vista

INAI ordena a Bienestar revelar millonario traspaso para programas de niños | Revista Punto de Vista

The INAI has ordered the Ministry of Welfare to disclose details regarding the transfer of nearly 48 million pesos to its youth welfare division, overturning the agency’s previous refusal to provide this information. This ruling addresses significant concerns about accountability and the lack of transparency in the management of indirect expenses for subsidy programs, emphasizing that major budget allocations must be subject to public scrutiny. The decision underscores the necessity of strict compliance with access-to-information laws, particularly in light of the substantial increase in the Ministry’s budget. By insisting that all financial transactions, from purchases to authorized expenditures, remain transparent, the resolution reinforces the government’s commitment to delivering benefits directly, without intermediaries, while ensuring that public funds are used responsibly. This case is crucial for open data, as it establishes a precedent for compelling the publication of detailed fiscal information in complex government programs. It highlights how regulatory bodies can leverage legal frameworks to require institutions to release specific spending data, thereby promoting greater visibility and trust in the management of public resources aimed at vulnerable populations.
