2022 Potato Production Down 3 Percent - Southeast AgNET

The 2022 potato crop saw decreased production volumes but significantly higher revenues due to rising average prices. This price surge offset declines in acreage and yield, illustrating how market dynamics influence agricultural economic stability despite lower physical output. These detailed statistics are vital for open data initiatives as they provide transparent, granular insights into supply chains and commodity markets. Such accessible data supports researchers and policymakers in analyzing trends, ensuring food security, and promoting accountability in the agricultural sector.
