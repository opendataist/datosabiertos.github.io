Con IA, abejas rastrean la contaminación y aumenta la biodiversidad - La Capital

BeeOdiversity has revolutionized environmental monitoring by using bees as natural biological sensors. By analyzing pollen collected by beehives, this technology provides a detailed and accurate picture of local biodiversity and the presence of pollutants, enabling farmers and businesses to manage their lands with greater ecological awareness. This approach turns insects into accessible, low-cost tools for big data analysis. The relevance to the open data community lies in the technological infrastructure that underpins this system. The company uses artificial intelligence and shared cloud tools on Microsoft Azure to process and extrapolate large volumes of biological information. This demonstrates how the integration of data science, machine learning, and open-source platforms can transform complex, unstructured data into actionable insights for large-scale environmental decision-making. The impact goes beyond simple data collection, offering practical recommendations to reduce pesticide use and restore ecosystems. Initiatives in Europe and South Africa have already validated how these data help protect water sources and combat invasive species. This case illustrates the power of open and collaborative data in addressing global crises, showing that informational transparency is key to achieving sustainability and conserving biodiversity in a transforming world.
