Pando es el departamento que más se aproxima al 100% de registro de Censistas Voluntarios

The National Institute of Statistics highlights the successful mobilization of volunteers for the upcoming 2024 Population and Housing Census. High participation rates in regions such as Pando demonstrate strong institutional collaboration, particularly with universities that provide both personnel and physical spaces. This extensive network ensures that the census meets its timeline and quality standards. The widespread engagement reflects a unified national effort, reducing logistical barriers and ensuring comprehensive coverage through coordinated support from educational and governmental entities across the country. This case is relevant to open data because a high-quality, universally accessible census provides the foundational dataset for evidence-based policy. Reliable demographic information enables transparency, facilitates accurate resource allocation, and supports the development of open government initiatives grounded in precise, publicly available statistical evidence.
