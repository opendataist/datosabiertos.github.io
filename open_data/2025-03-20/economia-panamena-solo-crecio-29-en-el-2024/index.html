Economía panameña sólo creció 2,9 % en el 2024

Panama’s economy experienced a significant deceleration in 2024, with growth dropping sharply compared to the previous year. Despite this slowdown, numerous domestic sectors and key international activities, such as the Canal and free trade zones, continued to expand, highlighting specific areas of resilience within the broader economic contraction. This update is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how transparent, accessible government statistics allow for the granular tracking of sector-specific performance. Such detailed public records are essential for enabling independent analysts to understand economic shifts, fostering accountability and informed decision-making through the free exchange of economic indicators.
