DGCP pone en servicio sistema de información que facilita la participación en procesos de contrataciones

DGCP pone en servicio sistema de información que facilita la participación en procesos de contrataciones

The Dirección General de Contrataciones Públicas has launched a new technological platform designed to modernize and enhance transparency in the Dominican Republic’s public procurement system. By integrating advanced big data and artificial intelligence, the initiative creates a user-friendly interface that significantly improves access to information for suppliers, government units, and the general public. This move aims to optimize spending efficiency and foster greater participation from both businesses and citizens in state contracting processes. The system comprises four integrated components that facilitate advanced search capabilities, personalized alerts for ongoing procurement opportunities, and strategic planning tools based on annual purchase plans. Crucially, it provides immediate access to download official documents in reusable formats. These features democratize access to commercial opportunities by simplifying complex requirements and allowing stakeholders to filter data by sector, date, or institution, thereby streamlining the interaction between the state and private entities. This initiative is highly relevant to the open data movement because it institutionalizes the publication of structured, reusable, and real-time public data through an open data sub-system. By offering interactive dashboards and statistical bulletins, it empowers researchers, journalists, and analysts to conduct evidence-based decision-making and business intelligence. Ultimately, this transforms raw government data into actionable public knowledge, setting a regional standard for transparency and accountability in public administration.
