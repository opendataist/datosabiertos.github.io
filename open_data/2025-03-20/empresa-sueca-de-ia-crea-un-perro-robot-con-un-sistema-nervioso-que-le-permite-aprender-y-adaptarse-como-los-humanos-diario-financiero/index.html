Empresa sueca de IA crea un perro robot con un sistema nervioso que le permite aprender y adaptarse como los humanos | Diario Financiero

Empresa sueca de IA crea un perro robot con un sistema nervioso que le permite aprender y adaptarse como los humanos | Diario Financiero

IntuiCell has developed Luna, a robotic dog equipped with a digital nervous system that enables it to learn and adapt autonomously, much like humans and animals. This approach eliminates the need for large pre-trained models or prior simulations, allowing the machine to solve problems in real time through neural interaction. The significance of this innovation lies in its potential for deployment in unpredictable environments such as space exploration or disaster rescue, where prior preparation is impossible. By learning on-site, these robots gain the flexibility required to operate in dynamic and complex conditions. This advancement is crucial for open_data because it demonstrates that artificial intelligence can function effectively without massive, centralized datasets. This opens the door to distributed and efficient models that prioritize continuous local learning over big data-based training, thereby democratizing access to intelligent robotics.
