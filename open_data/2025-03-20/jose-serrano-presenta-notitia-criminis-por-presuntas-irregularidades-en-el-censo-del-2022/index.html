José Serrano presenta notitia criminis por presuntas irregularidades en el censo del 2022

José Serrano has denounced serious irregularities in Ecuador’s 2022 census, alleging the insertion of hundreds of thousands of fictitious individuals and the manipulation of databases. This accusation highlights the fragility of official statistical processes when transparency and cybersecurity safeguards are lacking—issues that are central to the open data community. The alleged alterations include breaches of access controls, the use of non-functional software, and the artificial assignment of ethnic identities. These incidents demonstrate how opacity in public management and the absence of rigorous auditing enable distortions that compromise the integrity of state information, setting an alarming precedent for public trust. The relevance of this case for open data lies in the urgent need to demand verifiable integrity protocols. Without robust mechanisms that ensure data quality and genuine public access to census data, government planning and resource allocation may become biased, eroding evidence-based governance.
