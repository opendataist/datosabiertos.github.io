MTPE: "Estamos terminando el registro del mercado informal. Debe ser publicado en abril" | RPP Noticias

MTPE: "Estamos terminando el registro del mercado informal. Debe ser publicado en abril" | RPP Noticias

The Ministry of Labor and Employment Promotion is finalizing the registration of informal sector workers, a key step for accurately diagnosing labor market dynamics and designing targeted strategies to promote formalization. By identifying those who remain in the informal economy, the government aims to implement differentiated measures that facilitate the integration of these sectors into the formal system, thereby promoting greater labor protection and economic stability for vulnerable populations. At the same time, private formal employment is showing sustained growth, primarily driven by the agricultural and fishing sectors. This dynamic has generated new jobs and improved the purchasing power of wage earners, while also reflecting significant expansion in hiring by companies of all sizes. The positive trend extends to most regions of the country, indicating a broad-based economic recovery that benefits the national business structure. It is particularly relevant to the open data agenda to highlight how transparency in labor information enables the measurement of progress in the inclusion of historically marginalized groups. The increase in the formalization of women, young people, and persons with disabilities demonstrates the impact of data-driven public policies. The availability of these statistics is essential for monitoring the reduction of wage and social gaps, ensuring that economic growth is inclusive and equitable.
