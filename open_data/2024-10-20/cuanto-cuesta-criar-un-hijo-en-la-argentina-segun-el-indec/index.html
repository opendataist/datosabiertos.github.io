Cuánto cuesta criar un hijo en la Argentina, según el INDEC

Cuánto cuesta criar un hijo en la Argentina, según el INDEC

INDEC (the National Institute of Statistics and Censuses) has published new data on the “Child-Rearing Basket,” revealing that the cost of raising children in Argentina continues to rise in line with overall inflation. This tool quantifies the expenses families allocate to food, clothing, housing, and, crucially, to care tasks for children under 12 years of age. The results show that care costs represent a significant portion of total expenditure, especially in the early years of life, underscoring the economic burden associated with child-rearing. The relevance of this report for open data lies in its pioneering approach to monetizing care work, an area historically invisible in traditional economic statistics. By making public the data disaggregated between goods and services versus care, it highlights the structural gender gap, as these tasks disproportionately fall on women. The openness of this information enables civil society, academics, and non-governmental organizations to analyze and advocate for more equitable public policies. Developed in collaboration with UNICEF, this indicator sets a new standard of transparency by integrating the gender dimension into the measurement of the cost of living. By providing clear and accessible figures, it facilitates discussion on the redistribution of domestic responsibilities and the social recognition of care work. This advancement demonstrates how transparency in statistical data can transform the understanding of complex social issues, promoting a more equitable and informed society.
