Argentina alcanza en septiembre un superávit comercial de 981 millones de dólares

Argentina achieved a positive trade surplus, reversing last year’s deficit and marking nine consecutive months of growth. This shift highlights a significant structural change in trade dynamics, driven by increased export volumes despite lower prices, while imports contracted sharply. The accumulation of positive results underscores a robust recovery in external trade performance. By reducing import reliance and maintaining export competitiveness, the country has stabilized its balance, demonstrating resilience against global economic fluctuations. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular insights into national economic health.
