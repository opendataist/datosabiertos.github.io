EMFA – “We have won a major victory for the right to information in Europe”

The article details Reporters Without Borders’ support for the European Media Freedom Act, highlighting its strong emphasis on safeguarding editorial independence and media pluralism. It underscores the necessity for transparent ownership, equitable state advertising, and protection against foreign propaganda to maintain a diverse public discourse. These provisions are crucial for open data because they establish the legal framework necessary for free information flow. By mandating transparency in media structures and preventing conflicts of interest, the act ensures that the sources of public knowledge remain accessible and unbiased, which is fundamental for an informed society. Ultimately, the regulation strengthens the infrastructure for democratic oversight. By protecting independent journalism from undue political or corporate influence, it fosters an environment where data about public affairs can be gathered, analyzed, and shared openly, thereby enhancing civic engagement and accountability across the European Union.
