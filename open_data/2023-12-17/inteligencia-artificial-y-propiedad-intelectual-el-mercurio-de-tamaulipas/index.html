Inteligencia artificial y propiedad intelectual - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

Inteligencia artificial y propiedad intelectual - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

The article examines the rapid integration of artificial intelligence into daily life and critical sectors, highlighting the tension between the speed of algorithmic data processing and the slow pace of biological evolution. This technological surge creates significant uncertainty regarding societal and ethical impacts, prompting the urgent need for robust regulatory frameworks that prioritize transparency and ethical use. A central concern is the legal ambiguity surrounding intellectual property and authorship in AI-generated content. The text argues that existing copyright laws, such as those in Mexico, require adaptation to address questions of originality and ownership. This legal evolution is essential to balance innovation with the protection of human rights, ensuring that the definition of authorship accounts for the complex nature of algorithmic creativity. This discussion is crucial for the open data community because algorithmic transparency and data protection are foundational to responsible AI development. As open data practices intersect with AI, establishing clear legal standards for data usage and attribution ensures that technological progress does not compromise ethical principles or individual rights, fostering a future where technology serves humanity responsibly.
