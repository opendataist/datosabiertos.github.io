Ve Legislativo carencia de seguimiento a Ley de Transparencia en CEGAIP

The article highlights how monitoring CEGAIP’s reports revealed systemic failures in compliance with transparency laws in San Luis Potosí. It details agreements urging key institutions to improve their mechanisms for publishing review resources and to justify their lack of adherence to transparency regulations. This is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for institutions to actively and correctly publish information. The narrative emphasizes that mere legal reforms are insufficient; optimal implementation and consistent data availability are essential for true transparency and public accountability.
