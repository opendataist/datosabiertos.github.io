Celebra AMLO disminución en la inflación: ‘es una muy buena noticia’

Mexico’s inflation rate has declined for five consecutive periods, reaching its lowest level since early 2021. This downward trend is expected to strengthen the population’s purchasing power by mitigating the cost of essential goods and fuel. The government highlights this economic improvement as a significant achievement, emphasizing the importance of preventing price hikes for everyday items. By stabilizing costs, the administration aims to enhance the financial well-being of citizens and reduce the burden of living expenses. This development is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical role of the National Institute of Statistics and Geography in providing transparent, timely statistics. Public access to such reliable data allows citizens to understand economic realities and hold authorities accountable for policy outcomes.
