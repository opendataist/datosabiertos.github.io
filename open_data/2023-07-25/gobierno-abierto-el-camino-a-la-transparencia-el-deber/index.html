Gobierno abierto, el camino a la transparencia | El Deber

Gobierno abierto, el camino a la transparencia | El Deber

The article argues that adopting an open government model is crucial for Bolivia to foster transparency, enhance citizen participation, and combat corruption. By shifting from opaque state practices to collaborative governance involving civil society and the private sector, the country can rebuild institutional trust and ensure that public decisions reflect actual societal needs. This transformation requires political will, legislative reforms guaranteeing access to information, and technological platforms to facilitate engagement, empowering citizens to hold authorities accountable. This approach is highly relevant to open data because transparency is its foundational pillar. Open government relies on making information freely accessible, which is the essence of open data. Without releasing public budgets, policies, and decision-making processes as accessible data, citizens cannot effectively participate in or monitor government actions. Therefore, implementing open data principles is not merely a technical necessity but a democratic imperative that enables the active civic engagement described in the text. Ultimately, the article posits that open governance offers a unique opportunity to restore integrity and improve the quality of life in Bolivia. It urges both the government and citizens to prioritize this shift immediately, leveraging innovation and legal frameworks to create a more just and participatory future. The success of this initiative depends on collective effort, ensuring that public resources are managed transparently and that the government remains responsive to the population, thereby strengthening democracy through data-driven openness.
