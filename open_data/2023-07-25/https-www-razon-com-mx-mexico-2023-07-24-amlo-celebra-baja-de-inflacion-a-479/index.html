Mexico’s recent decline in inflation signals a significant strengthening of the popular economy and the purchasing power of citizens. This trend indicates the absence of scarcity, driven by historic income growth and substantial social investment programs aimed at supporting millions of households. Stability is further reinforced by record remittances and a strengthened national currency that has maintained its value against the U.S. dollar. These macroeconomic factors have created a favorable environment, allowing even the banking sector to achieve unprecedented profit levels without devaluation pressures. This news is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of transparent, publicly accessible statistical metrics, such as those provided by INEGI. Reliable data enables citizens to verify government claims, fostering trust in economic indicators and facilitating informed public debate about national welfare and policy effectiveness.
