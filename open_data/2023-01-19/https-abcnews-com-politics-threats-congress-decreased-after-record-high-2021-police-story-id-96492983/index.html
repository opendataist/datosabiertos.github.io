Threats against members of Congress significantly declined in 2022, though the volume remains critically high following a peak in 2021. This trend highlights the persistent need for enhanced investigative resources and expanded protection responsibilities within law enforcement agencies. Despite the statistical improvement, violent incidents continue to occur, underscoring the dangers inherent in political discourse. The agency has responded by hiring specialized prosecutors to handle these cases, reflecting the growing complexity and severity of threats targeting legislators. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of transparent, aggregated statistics in monitoring public safety trends. By making such information accessible, researchers and citizens can better understand systemic risks and advocate for policies that reduce political violence and protect democratic institutions.
