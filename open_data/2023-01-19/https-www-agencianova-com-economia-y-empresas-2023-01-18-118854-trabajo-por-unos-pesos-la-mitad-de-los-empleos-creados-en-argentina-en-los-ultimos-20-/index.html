The article reveals that, despite low official unemployment rates, precarious and informal jobs have surged to record levels in Argentina over the past twenty years. Half of all new positions lack social security coverage, highlighting a deep structural crisis in which growth in the formal private sector has stagnated relative to the expansion of precarious work and public employment. This data underscores the critical role of open data in exposing hidden realities of the labor market. By analyzing transparent official statistics, researchers can identify long-term trends that aggregate figures might obscure, demonstrating how transparency in public data is essential for understanding socio-economic deterioration and informing accurate policy debates.
