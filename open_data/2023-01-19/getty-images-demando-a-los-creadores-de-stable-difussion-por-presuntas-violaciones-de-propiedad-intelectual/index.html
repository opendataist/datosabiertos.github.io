Getty Images demandó a los creadores de Stable Difussion por presuntas violaciones de propiedad intelectual

Getty Images has initiated legal action against Stability AI in the United Kingdom, accusing the company of copyright infringement for using its photographs to train the Stable Diffusion model. This lawsuit underscores the growing tension between the visual content industry and the development of generative artificial intelligence, raising fundamental questions about the legality of employing copyrighted works without a license to improve commercial algorithms. The core of the conflict lies in how AI tools learn from vast volumes of data on the internet, often without explicit consent. Getty Images emphasizes that while it supports innovation that respects intellectual property rights, Stability AI chose to bypass available licensing channels. This raises uncertainty about the ownership of generated images and sets a crucial precedent regarding whether training models on copyrighted works constitutes a legal violation or fair use. This dispute is significant for the open data movement because it challenges the ethical and legal foundations of mass data extraction. If developers must obtain licenses for private datasets, the debate opens up as to whether open access principles are compatible with intellectual property protection. The case will determine whether technological innovation can thrive on shared databases or whether it requires funding models that strictly respect the rights of original creators.
