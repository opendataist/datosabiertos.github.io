Thornton Township Sued; Failing To Produce Public Records Under FOIA –

Thornton Township faces FOIA litigation after refusing to release credit card records tied to Supervisor Henyard’s nonprofit activities. The suit alleges improper use of public funds for a separate corporation, raising serious transparency concerns. This case highlights critical open data challenges, demonstrating how local governments may obscure financial transactions and withhold essential records from public scrutiny.
