Consumer inflation eases in December

South Africa’s inflation remains above the central bank’s target, driven by rising food, housing, and transport costs. Although overall rates have softened, persistent price increases signal ongoing economic pressure on consumers. This data highlights critical gaps in how local inflation metrics are tracked and reported. Open data initiatives can improve transparency by providing accessible, granular insights into these specific cost drivers. Enhanced open data standards allow citizens to better understand and respond to economic changes. By making detailed statistics more accessible, communities can hold institutions accountable and make informed financial decisions amidst inflationary trends.
