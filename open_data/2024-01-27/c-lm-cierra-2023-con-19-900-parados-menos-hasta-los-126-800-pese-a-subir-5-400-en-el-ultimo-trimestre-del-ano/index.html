C-LM cierra 2023 con 19.900 parados menos hasta los 126.800, pese a subir 5.400 en el último trimestre del año

Unemployment in Castilla-La Mancha rose slightly, yet remains at its lowest seasonal level in years, contrasting with national job growth. This highlights regional disparities in labor market recovery despite overall improvements. Data availability enables tracking these subtle shifts, crucial for identifying local economic needs. Open data fosters transparency, allowing communities to understand how national trends impact specific areas. Monitoring such indicators is essential for policy evaluation. Accessible, granular statistics empower stakeholders to address employment challenges effectively, ensuring resources target areas with the greatest need.
