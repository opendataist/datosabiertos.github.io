CSIF ve en el paro femenino y el empleo público "importantes puntos negativos" de los datos en la región

The article highlights significant job losses, particularly affecting women and the public sector in Castilla-La Mancha. This trend underscores systemic issues in labor stability, revealing a high reliance on temporary contracts within government employment. These statistics expose the vulnerability of specific demographics and the precarious nature of public sector work. The data suggests that current policies are failing to protect workers, leading to increased long-term unemployment and gender disparities in job security. This is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how accessible statistical reports, such as the EPA (Employed Population Survey), drive public debate and policy demands. Transparent data allows unions to validate claims and advocate for resource reallocation, showing the power of open statistics in holding institutions accountable and influencing social welfare decisions.
