Retrocedió nivel de exportaciones al cierre de 2023

Mexican exports exhibited mixed annual trends, with non-petroleum goods increasing while petroleum sales declined sharply. Seasonal adjustments reveal underlying growth in total merchandise shipments, contrasting with significant annual declines in imports across both sectors. This trade data underscores the volatility inherent in open economic datasets. Analyzing such figures is crucial for transparency, enabling stakeholders to understand trade dependencies and market shifts. Open data initiatives ensure that these statistics are accessible for independent verification. By providing raw figures alongside adjustments, the public can better assess the true health of international trade flows and the impact of policies.
