El Gobernador destacó la aprobación de la ley de Transparencia en la Obra Pública - El Chubut

The Province of Chubut has approved a Transparency in Public Works Law, marking a decisive break from past institutional corruption and negligence. By establishing strict procedures for oversight and citizen participation in public bidding, the government aims to eliminate the impunity that previously allowed contractors to receive large advance payments without completing projects. This legislative shift prioritizes accountability and predictability, ensuring that public resources are managed efficiently and transparently rather than being diverted for personal gain. This legal framework is highly relevant to open data, as it institutionalizes the mandatory disclosure of information related to public contracting. It creates a structural obligation for the state to make data on tenders and execution results accessible, thereby empowering citizens to monitor government actions. The focus on preventing fraud requires robust, machine-readable data systems that enable real-time verification, transforming transparency from a political promise into a technical necessity. Ultimately, this reform seeks to rebuild trust by enforcing modern bureaucratic standards that deter corruption. The government emphasizes that future laws will further strengthen institutions, ensuring that power is exercised with rigor and fairness. By mandating the return of misappropriated funds and judicial accountability, the policy underscores that open data is not merely an administrative formality but a critical tool for democratic oversight and institutional integrity.
