Visibilizan estadísticas con enfoque de niñez y adolescencia

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) of Guatemala released a statistical compendium on children and adolescents, consolidating various indicators related to health, education, and protection. This initiative transforms fragmented official data into a cohesive resource to support evidence-based public policies. By integrating sources from multiple ministries and institutions, the publication enhances data interoperability and accessibility. It empowers stakeholders to design strategies that improve youth welfare through transparent, comprehensive information. This release is vital for open data as it demonstrates how government entities can publish integrated datasets. It enables civil society to leverage comprehensive statistical information for advocacy, accountability, and the development of targeted social interventions.
