Panamá registró una inflación de 0,0% en julio de 2023

Panama’s National Urban Consumer Price Index showed relative stability in July 2023, with a zero monthly variation and a minimal year-on-year increase of 0.6%. This picture is characterized by a significant divergence among consumption groups: while prices for food, communications, and recreational items fell due to promotions and seasonality, costs for housing, health, and transport rose, reflecting inflationary pressure on essential basic services. The relevance of this report for the open data community lies in the public availability and granularity of the data provided by the National Institute of Statistics and Census (INEC). By disaggregating the information by districts and specific categories—such as the differing price trends of school shoes versus household appliances—it demonstrates how open datasets enable detailed, context-rich analyses. This transparency not only facilitates independent verification of official figures but also empowers researchers and citizens to understand the microeconomic trends that directly affect Panamanian households. Finally, the publication of these statistical details underscores the importance of open data as a tool for government transparency and accountability. By providing a clear view of price increases in fuels and rents alongside declines in non-essential consumer goods, it enables civil society to accurately track changes in purchasing power. This type of access to structured, disaggregated information is essential for fostering informed public scrutiny and promoting evidence-based economic policies.
