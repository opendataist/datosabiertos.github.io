Uruguay’s preliminary census results show that population growth is lower than anticipated, falling short of previous projections. This significant deviation from expected demographic trends underscores critical challenges in population forecasting and data accuracy for policymakers. These findings highlight the importance of transparent, accessible public data in validating government models. Accurate census information is essential for open data initiatives, ensuring that societal planning is based on verified statistical evidence rather than erroneous assumptions about future growth.
