El TSE ya redistribuyó los escaños; un departamento pierde una diputación y existe empate entre La Paz y Santa Cruz

El TSE ya redistribuyó los escaños; un departamento pierde una diputación y existe empate entre La Paz y Santa Cruz

The Bolivian Electoral Tribunal completed the redistribution of seats in the Chamber of Deputies based on population and development indices derived from the controversial census. This decision underscores significant concerns about the quality of official data, as regional authorities challenge the population projections and call for an audit of the underlying statistics. The process revealed that one department lost a seat, while La Paz and Santa Cruz remain tied. This outcome highlights the real-world political consequences of potentially flawed demographic data, demonstrating how inaccurate inputs can directly affect legislative representation and regional power dynamics. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessity of transparent, auditable, and high-quality datasets in democratic processes. Reliable public information is essential for ensuring fair representation and trust in electoral outcomes, emphasizing the need for robust data governance and public scrutiny of official statistics.
