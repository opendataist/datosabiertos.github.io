Asamblea de la Paceñidad rechaza resultados del Censo y exige auditoría técnica internacional a todo el proceso

Asamblea de la Paceñidad rechaza resultados del Censo y exige auditoría técnica internacional a todo el proceso

The Assembly of Paceñidad has declared a state of emergency in response to the results of the 2024 census, denouncing technical contradictions in the reported population decline. They are calling for an independent international audit to validate the statistical processes, rejecting the validity of the current data due to their lack of technical substantiation. The organization insists that financial resources and political representation should not be allocated based on questionable figures until a comprehensive review is conducted. They also demand the official publication of the cartographic processes and coverage surveys to ensure transparency in the methodology used during the census. This situation is relevant to open data, as it underscores the critical need for transparency in government data. It demonstrates that without access to clear methodologies and verifiable results, public trust in official statistics erodes. It promotes the demand for open and auditable data as a foundation for accountability and the fair management of public resources.
