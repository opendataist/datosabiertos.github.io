Aplica SSPM censo en la 16 de septiembre en ‘Plan de Seguridad’

A census conducted in the 16 de Septiembre neighborhood of Juárez collects detailed socioeconomic and housing data to inform public safety strategies. This initiative by municipal authorities aims to address the underlying economic issues affecting residents, fostering a deeper understanding of community needs through direct engagement. The collected information serves as the foundation for developing targeted interventions under the Human Security Plan. By analyzing this comprehensive dataset, officials can design effective policies that respond to the specific vulnerabilities faced by the population, ensuring resources are allocated where they are most needed. This effort highlights the critical role of open data in enhancing urban governance and social well-being. Transparent, accessible datasets enable authorities to move beyond reactive measures, promoting proactive, evidence-based solutions that improve quality of life and strengthen community trust in public institutions.
