CEQ releases staff list

The CEQ is currently led by a small team of political appointees, with no permanent chair confirmed. This leadership structure facilitates the Trump administration’s efforts to overhaul the National Environmental Policy Act’s implementation. Understanding this personnel dynamic is vital for open data, as it reveals how policy shifts are driven by specific political actors rather than just career civil servants.
