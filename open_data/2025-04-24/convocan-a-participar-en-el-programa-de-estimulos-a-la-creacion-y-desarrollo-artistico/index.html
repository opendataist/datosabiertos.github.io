Convocan a participar en el programa de estímulos a la creación y desarrollo artístico

Convocan a participar en el programa de estímulos a la creación y desarrollo artístico

The Mexican government and the state of San Luis Potosí are launching the 2025 PECDA program to strengthen artistic creation across the country. This initiative specifically highlights collaborative work among women artists, aligning with commemorations for Indigenous Women’s Year. The program supports diverse disciplines and age groups, ranging from adolescent talents to established creators. It also includes specific categories for artistic productions, individual training, publications, and Indigenous artistic collectives, ensuring broad accessibility for varied cultural initiatives. This announcement is relevant to open data as it represents a public cultural policy implementation. Such programs typically generate transparent datasets regarding funding allocation and demographic participation, offering valuable insights into how state resources support artistic diversity and regional development within Mexico’s cultural sector.
