Visitación turística a Costa Rica sigue cayendo: vea los datos del primer trimestre del 2025

Visitación turística a Costa Rica sigue cayendo: vea los datos del primer trimestre del 2025

Costa Rica’s tourism sector is encountering significant headwinds, with notable declines in arrivals from Europe and the Middle East sharply contrasting with growth in South America and the Caribbean. This uneven performance underscores regional vulnerabilities and shifting travel dynamics that impact the nation’s primary economic driver. The slowdown is attributed to currency fluctuations, infrastructure deficits, and rising insecurity. These structural challenges threaten Costa Rica’s competitive position relative to neighboring countries, raising concerns about long-term sustainability despite the country’s strong international reputation and diverse tourist offerings. This data is crucial for open data advocates, as it demonstrates the value of accessible, granular tourism statistics. Transparent, timely public datasets enable researchers and policymakers to monitor economic indicators, identify emerging trends, and foster accountability in managing one of the country’s most vital resources.
