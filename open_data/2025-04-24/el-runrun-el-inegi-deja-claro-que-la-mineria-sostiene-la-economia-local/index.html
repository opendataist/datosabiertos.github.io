El Runrún: El INEGI deja claro que la minería sostiene la economía local

El Runrún: El INEGI deja claro que la minería sostiene la economía local

Zacatecas has shown significant progress by drastically reducing intentional homicides, emerging as one of Mexico’s safest states according to official data. This statistical improvement highlights the potential for measurable security gains through targeted enforcement strategies. However, a stark contrast persists between these official figures and the ongoing public perception of insecurity. This disconnect illustrates that crime statistics alone do not guarantee social peace, emphasizing the need to address subjective experiences of safety alongside objective metrics. For open data initiatives, this case underscores the critical importance of transparent, accessible information that bridges the gap between government records and citizens’ lived realities. Effective open data must contextualize hard numbers with qualitative insights to rebuild trust and accurately validate public concerns.
