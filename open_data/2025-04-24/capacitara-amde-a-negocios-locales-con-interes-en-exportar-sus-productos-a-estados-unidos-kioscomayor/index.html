Capacitará AMDE a negocios locales con interés en exportar sus productos a Estados Unidos - Kioscomayor

Capacitará AMDE a negocios locales con interés en exportar sus productos a Estados Unidos - Kioscomayor

The government of Hermosillo, in partnership with Nafin and Bancomext, is launching a joint initiative to empower local businesses to enter the U.S. retail market. This collaboration focuses on providing free strategic training, helping companies navigate the complexities of international trade and successfully place their products in American supermarkets. The program addresses critical export requirements, including market identification, customs logistics, and regulatory compliance such as FDA labeling. By offering specialized sessions on letters of credit and cultural differences in negotiation, the initiative aims to foster sustainable business growth and job creation through expanded commercial opportunities. This effort underscores the vital role of open data in modern economic development. Transparent access to trade regulations, market intelligence, and procedural guidelines enables smaller enterprises to overcome entry barriers. By democratizing information, public institutions can effectively bridge the gap between local producers and global supply chains, fostering inclusive economic participation.
