Correos se une a la celebración de la Semana de la Administración Abierta con visitas a sus centros

Correos participates in Open Government Week by opening its doors to the public, aiming to demystify its operations as a logistical service provider. This initiative underscores the importance of transparency and citizen participation in public administration, directly aligning with open data principles by fostering trust through visible, accessible institutional operations. The company facilitates direct engagement through organized visits to key offices and automated logistics centers. By allowing citizens to witness the inner workings of these facilities, Correos demonstrates how public entities can bridge the gap between bureaucratic processes and public scrutiny. This openness encourages civic interaction and highlights the value of accessible information regarding public service delivery. This event is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the practical application of government transparency. It reinforces the idea that open government relies not just on data publication, but on active, understandable communication between institutions and citizens. Such efforts promote a culture where public accountability and collaborative governance are tangible, encouraging broader societal engagement with public resources.
