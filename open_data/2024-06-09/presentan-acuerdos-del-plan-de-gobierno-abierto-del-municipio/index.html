Presentan acuerdos del Plan de Gobierno Abierto del Municipio

Presentan acuerdos del Plan de Gobierno Abierto del Municipio

Chihuahua has launched its first Open Government Plan, focusing on violence prevention, support for business creation, and assistance for rural development. This initiative aims to promote social participation and digital transparency in key municipal sectors. A dedicated portal ensures accountability by monitoring progress and providing evidence for each commitment. This tool allows citizens to verify compliance, fostering trust through visible, verifiable government actions rather than mere promises. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how structured data tracking transforms abstract policy goals into tangible, auditable outcomes. By making compliance evidence publicly accessible, the municipality exemplifies how open data drives genuine governmental openness and civic engagement.
