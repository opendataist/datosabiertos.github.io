Se compromete Inai a garantizar derecho de acceso a la información - Quadratín

The INAI has signed a global declaration committing to nine concrete actions that promote transparency and safeguard the right to access information. These measures aim to reduce asymmetries, improve public services, and strengthen democratic participation by ensuring government accountability and empowering civil society. The agreement highlights the critical role of information commissioners in investigating denials of access and fostering a culture of transparency that supports inclusive governance. A key focus of this initiative is the integration of digital technologies to enhance openness while respecting privacy and ethical standards. The declaration advocates for "transparency by design," urging that this principle be embedded in the earliest stages of creating administrative processes and governance models. By facilitating citizen campaigns and encouraging international cooperation among stakeholders, the framework seeks to exchange best practices and ensure that digital innovation serves as a tool for greater public inclusion and easier exercise of information rights. This agreement is highly relevant to open data because it establishes transparency by design as a fundamental operational principle for new systems and procedures. It underscores the necessity of making information accessible and usable by default, which aligns directly with open data standards that prioritize proactive disclosure and interoperability. By committing to these goals, the INAI reinforces the global movement toward using data as a primary mechanism for accountability, ensuring that open government initiatives are structurally supported and ethically managed.
