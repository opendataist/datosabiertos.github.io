UZPR condemns attacks on democratic values and threats received by journalists

The article highlights a strong condemnation of threats against journalists by Romanian professional unions, emphasizing that such intimidation undermines democratic values and press freedom. It asserts that protecting journalists’ safety is essential for maintaining pluralism and the integrity of public discourse. This relates to open data because transparent information dissemination relies heavily on secure, independent journalism. When reporters face harassment, the flow of vital civic data is obstructed, threatening the accountability mechanisms that open data initiatives seek to support. Ultimately, the text argues that authorities must enforce penalties for these aggressions. Ensuring a safe environment for information gathering is crucial for preserving the robust public sphere where open data contributes to an informed and engaged citizenry.
