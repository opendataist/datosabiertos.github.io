La economía argentina se contrajo 5,5% durante mayo y se acerca a su sexta recesión

Argentina’s economy contracted significantly in May, primarily due to a severe drought that devastated agricultural output. Although the services and mining sectors posted slight growth, the sharp decline in primary sectors pulled overall economic activity into stagnation during the first five months of the year. This report is particularly valuable for open-data enthusiasts, as it underscores the importance of accessible, granular economic indicators for analyzing the impacts of climate change. By monitoring such detailed sectoral data, researchers can better understand how environmental shocks propagate through national economies. Ultimately, it highlights the need for transparent statistics to inform policy-making and resilience strategies.
