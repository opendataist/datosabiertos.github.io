El INE prevé que la actualización cartográfica finalizará en agosto

El INE prevé que la actualización cartográfica finalizará en agosto

The National Institute of Statistics reports that the 2024 National Census is nearing the completion of its critical cartographic update phase, having achieved aggregate progress exceeding 99 percent nationwide. Only the departments of Cochabamba and Santa Cruz remain to finalize their contributions, with both expected to conclude their work by early August. This milestone ensures that the geographical framework necessary for an accurate population and housing enumeration is fully established, allowing the institution to uphold its commitment to timeliness and quality in the upcoming census. This geographic update is fundamental to open data, as it creates the standardized spatial basis required to accurately link demographic statistics to specific locations. By verifying occupied and vacant housing units and updating place names with precise coordinates, the process generates a reliable reference grid. This enables the production of granular, georeferenced datasets that are essential for transparency, allowing citizens and researchers to access detailed socio-economic information mapped to exact community boundaries. The successful completion of this stage facilitates the transition to the official enumeration scheduled for March 2024, supported by significant international funding. The rigorous verification of these maps ensures that the subsequent data collection is structured and efficient. Ultimately, this approach guarantees that the census results will be not only statistically robust but also openly accessible and geographically accurate, fulfilling the core principles of public sector transparency and data-driven governance.
