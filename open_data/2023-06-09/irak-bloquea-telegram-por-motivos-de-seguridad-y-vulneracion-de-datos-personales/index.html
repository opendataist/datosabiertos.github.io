Irak bloquea Telegram por motivos de seguridad y vulneración de datos personales

Iraq has blocked Telegram to protect national security and citizens’ data privacy, citing the app’s failure to cooperate with government requests to halt information leaks. This decisive action underscores the critical tension between state oversight and digital platform accountability, highlighting how data-handling practices directly impact public safety and civil liberties. The measure reflects broader efforts to regulate online spaces, raising concerns about potential restrictions on freedom of expression. It serves as a stark reminder that open-data initiatives must balance transparency with robust privacy protections, ensuring that user information is safeguarded against unauthorized access while respecting fundamental rights. This case is highly relevant to open-data discussions, as it illustrates the real-world consequences of weak data governance. It emphasizes the need for clear legal frameworks and corporate responsibility in managing personal information, showing that without strict compliance, governments may resort to broad blocks, ultimately hindering digital openness and trust.
