Taxation statistics released for 2020-21

The Australian Taxation Office’s latest report reveals how the pandemic reshaped economic behaviors, highlighting a notable rise in work-from-home expenses and shifting fiscal patterns. This data offers critical insights into how health crises influence individual financial decisions and broader tax revenues, making it essential for researchers studying societal impacts during emergencies. New detailed tables on partnerships and trusts provide granular visibility into rental property markets, showing a historic shift to positive net rental income. This transparency allows analysts to track housing market trends with greater precision, supporting evidence-based policy discussions and academic research into wealth distribution and real estate economics. Relevant to open data, this publication demonstrates the value of releasing high-quality, granular government statistics. By making complex financial data accessible, the ATO enables independent verification, fosters innovation in economic analysis, and empowers citizens to understand how public funds are collected and distributed, thereby strengthening democratic accountability through data transparency.
