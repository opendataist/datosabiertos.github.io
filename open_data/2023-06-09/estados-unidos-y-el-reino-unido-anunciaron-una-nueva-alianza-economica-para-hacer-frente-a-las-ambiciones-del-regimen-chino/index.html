Estados Unidos y el Reino Unido anunciaron una nueva alianza económica para hacer frente a las ambiciones del régimen chino

The "Atlantic Declaration" between the United States and the United Kingdom establishes a new strategic economic alliance designed to counter China's geopolitical influence. This agreement prioritizes cooperation in sensitive sectors such as defense, nuclear energy, and supply chains for critical minerals essential to the energy transition. By addressing vulnerabilities stemming from recent U.S. policies, the pact seeks to ensure the economic security of Western democracies in the face of Asian technological and commercial competition. A fundamental pillar of this collaboration is control over emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and semiconductors. The two nations commit to working together to ensure that these innovations are not exploited by authoritarian regimes for repressive purposes or for intellectual property theft. This initiative not only responds to national security threats but also aims to balance asymmetries in global industrial development, strengthening the resilience of their respective technology industries. The relevance of this article to the open data movement lies in the context of technological governance and national security that it promotes. As competition among major powers intensifies in areas such as artificial intelligence and digital infrastructure, critical challenges emerge for transparency, access to information, and data interoperability. The prioritization of security and intellectual property protection could influence how governments regulate the open flow of data, directly affecting the principles of transparency and public access that define the open data initiative.
