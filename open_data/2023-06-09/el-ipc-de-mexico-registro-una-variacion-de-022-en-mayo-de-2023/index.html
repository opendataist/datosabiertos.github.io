El IPC de México registró una variación de -0,22% en mayo de 2023

Mexico’s overall inflation decelerated markedly in May 2023, signaling a positive economic trend. The price index for non-core goods declined as a result of government adjustments to energy tariffs, while core prices remained sticky. This transparency regarding consumer price movements supports open data initiatives by providing crucial economic indicators for public analysis.
