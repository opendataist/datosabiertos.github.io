Continúa diálogo para actualizar política nacional de datos abiertos

The dialogue table is advancing in the co-creation of a new national open data policy, integrating multiple sectors to strengthen transparency and accountability. This collaborative effort aims to improve social audit mechanisms, ensuring that new regulations respond to the real needs of society. The initiative reinforces the government’s commitment to open government, aligning with international standards to promote greater access and ethical use of public information.
