Inai acumula más de 5 mil recursos de revisión que no ha podido ejecutar

The INAI is currently paralyzed due to a lack of quorum, resulting in the accumulation of thousands of unresolved review resources and unexecuted enforcement measures. Despite this critical operational blockade, the institution maintains its commitment to transparency by continuing promotional events and digital platform initiatives. This situation highlights the fragility of democratic oversight mechanisms when institutional mandates are incomplete, yet it also demonstrates resilience as the organization seeks legal resolutions to restore full functionality. Concurrently, the National Transparency System is actively strengthening regional networks through digital education and accountability forums. These efforts aim to foster a culture of privacy and information access, which are essential for modern societies. By emphasizing the insubstitutable role of digital platforms, the system seeks to empower citizens and improve municipal problem-solving. This approach underscores that technological infrastructure is not just a tool but a fundamental pillar for protecting personal data and ensuring government transparency in an increasingly connected world. This article is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the vital link between institutional integrity and data accessibility. Open data initiatives depend on functioning oversight bodies to guarantee that released information is accurate, protected, and utilized for accountability. When agencies like the INAI are stalled, the entire ecosystem of transparency suffers, affecting public trust and the effective exercise of citizens' rights to know. Therefore, resolving these administrative impasses is crucial for maintaining the reliability and impact of open government data systems.
