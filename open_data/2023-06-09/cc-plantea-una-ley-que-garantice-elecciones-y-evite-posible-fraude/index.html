CC plantea una ley que garantice elecciones y evite posible fraude

Bolivia has proposed a transparency bill to ensure credible 2025 elections by harmonizing voter registers across key agencies. This alignment aims to prevent structural conflicts and address systemic electoral vulnerabilities through rigorous data consistency. The initiative mandates the transmission system for preliminary results and defines fraud triggers, such as data manipulation or illegal interruptions. By establishing clear technical and legal standards, it seeks to detect irregularities early and maintain institutional trust. This effort underscores the critical role of open data in safeguarding democratic integrity. Transparent, interoperable, and consistent voter registries are essential for preventing fraud. Ensuring data accuracy and accessibility protects voting rights and upholds the legitimacy of electoral outcomes for all stakeholders.
