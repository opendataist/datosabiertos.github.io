Train MDAs, others on provisions of FOI Act - CSO tells Akwa Ibom Govt

Train MDAs, others on provisions of FOI Act - CSO tells Akwa Ibom Govt

The article emphasizes the urgent need for Akwa Ibom State to domesticate the Freedom of Information Act, highlighting that legal passage alone is insufficient without comprehensive staff training in relevant agencies. This capacity building is crucial for fostering a culture of transparency, enabling officials to understand that disclosing information is fundamental to accountability and effective public relations rather than a burden. Stakeholders are urged to engage in wide consultations during the legislative process to ensure the law reflects local realities. By incorporating community input, the government can cultivate public ownership of the legislation, ensuring it is calibrated to address specific regional needs and thereby enhancing its practical effectiveness and legitimacy within the society. Furthermore, the text advocates for the strategic use of social media by government bodies to proactively share reports, budgets, and other public interest data. This digital engagement not only makes information more accessible and cost-effective but also significantly boosts transparency. This approach is vital for open data initiatives, as it leverages modern communication tools to democratize information and strengthen civic oversight, ensuring that data flows freely to empower citizens.
