Los pueblos más ricos de Aragón según el Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)

Los pueblos más ricos de Aragón según el Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)

Tax data reveal that wealth in Aragon is not concentrated exclusively in its major cities, but rather shows a notable distribution toward small rural municipalities. Although the main cities maintain higher average incomes, towns with minimal populations, such as Ejulve, lead in per capita income indices. This demonstrates that population density is not the sole determinant of economic well-being; rather, the equitable distribution of income among a small number of residents can generate significantly higher levels of relative wealth compared to more densely populated urban areas. This phenomenon highlights the effectiveness of diversified local economies, where traditional agriculture and rural tourism sustain stable incomes. Sectors such as protected designation of origin and ecotourism enable these small communities to counteract depopulation and the absence of large-scale industries. The ability of these villages to add value through specific products and unique tourism experiences allows them to compete economically, challenging the notion that urban development is an inevitable prerequisite for greater individual prosperity. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it validates the utility of public and tax statistics at the level of municipal microdata. It shows how access to disaggregated data enables the identification of hidden economic trends and regional inequalities that national or provincial aggregates tend to obscure. By democratizing this information, evidence-based decision-making for territorial cohesion policies is facilitated, and transparency regarding the socioeconomic reality of less visible communities is promoted.
