Bajan los matrimonios y divorcios en 2023

Mexican marriage and divorce rates declined in 2023, reversing trends observed during the recent pandemic period. This shift suggests evolving social dynamics and demographic adjustments within the country’s family structures. The data highlight regional disparities, with certain states showing significantly higher concentrations of divorces, while others report lower rates of union formation. These geographic variations indicate localized social pressures or differences in legal accessibility that affect family stability. This article is relevant to open data, as it exemplifies how accessible government statistics enable public analysis of societal trends. Transparently published datasets empower researchers to monitor demographic shifts and inform policy decisions regarding social welfare and legal systems.
