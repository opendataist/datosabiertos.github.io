RTI act needs reform

RTI act needs reform

The Right to Information Act has lost its impact, becoming largely symbolic rather than a tool for genuine citizen empowerment. Urgent reforms are needed to transform this legislation into a mechanism that ensures true transparency and accountability in governance. The Information Commission requires structural changes, including impartial recruitment and nonpartisan commissioners, to maintain its credibility. Officials must shift from viewing themselves as gatekeepers to acting as custodians who recognize that public information belongs to the people, not the state. This perspective is vital for open data because it highlights the need for accessible, unbiased public records free from political interference. By treating information as a public asset, governments can foster a culture of openness that supports democratic values and reduces corruption.
