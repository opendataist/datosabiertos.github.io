Puebla es tercer lugar en todo el país en embarazos adolescentes | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The report reveals that Puebla ranks third nationally for births to underage mothers, highlighting a persistent public health challenge despite a recent decline in cases. This data underscores the critical need for accurate, accessible demographic statistics to monitor social trends and evaluate the effectiveness of state interventions aimed at protecting adolescent rights and health. Open data is essential in this context, as it transforms raw registration records into actionable insights for policymakers. By making such information transparent and easily available, stakeholders can better understand the scale of adolescent pregnancies and identify high-risk regions requiring immediate attention and resource allocation. The existence of targeted prevention plans, such as the GEPEA initiative, demonstrates a governmental commitment to addressing these issues through a rights-based framework. However, the true impact of these strategies relies on continuous, high-quality open data to track progress, ensure accountability, and foster informed public discourse regarding sexual and reproductive health outcomes.
