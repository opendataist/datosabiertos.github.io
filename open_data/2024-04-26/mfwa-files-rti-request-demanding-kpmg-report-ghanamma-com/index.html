MFWA files RTI request demanding KPMG report - Ghanamma.com

The Media Foundation for West Africa has formally requested the complete KPMG audit report regarding the controversial revenue mobilization contract between Ghana’s tax authority and SML. This legal action underscores the critical role of open data in holding public institutions accountable, as access to comprehensive audit findings is essential for verifying government claims and ensuring transparency in financial agreements involving substantial public funds. The audit’s partial release has sparked significant debate, with critics labeling the findings inadequate and questioning KPMG’s independence due to its ties with the revenue authority. SML disputes the reported financial figures, arguing that the context of their investments and tax contributions was ignored. This conflict highlights how fragmented data transparency can undermine trust, as selective disclosure often fails to provide the full picture necessary for informed public scrutiny and legitimate oversight. Releasing the full report is vital for restoring confidence in Ghana’s public financial management. Open data initiatives are directly relevant here, as they empower civil society to challenge official narratives and demand rigorous accountability. Ensuring unrestricted access to such audits prevents the normalization of opaque practices, reinforcing the principle that citizens have a right to verify how state resources are managed and whether contractual obligations are genuinely fulfilled.
