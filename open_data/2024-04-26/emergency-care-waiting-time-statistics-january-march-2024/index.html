Emergency Care Waiting Time Statistics (January - March 2024)

The Department of Health recently released data on emergency care performance in Northern Ireland, highlighting a significant shift in patient pathways due to the introduction of new urgent care services. By implementing PhoneFirst and Urgent Care Centres, the health system is successfully diverting a substantial number of patients away from emergency departments, thereby altering attendance patterns and potentially alleviating pressure on critical facilities. Performance metrics reveal mixed results, with overall treatment times within emergency departments increasing compared to the previous year. While smaller emergency departments maintain high efficiency standards, larger Type 1 departments face challenges, evidenced by rising wait times and a higher percentage of patients leaving before treatment completion. This trend indicates growing bottlenecks in the primary emergency care sector, necessitating closer scrutiny of resource allocation and workflow efficiency. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publishing granular statistical information enables public accountability and systemic transparency. By making specific performance indicators and service transition data publicly accessible, stakeholders can analyze the effectiveness of healthcare innovations. Furthermore, the explicit exclusion of certain regional data due to technical transitions underscores the importance of maintaining clear, accurate, and consistent data standards for reliable longitudinal analysis.
