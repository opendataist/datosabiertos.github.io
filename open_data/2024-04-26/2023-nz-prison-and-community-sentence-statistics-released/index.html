2023 NZ Prison and Community Sentence Statistics Released

2023 NZ Prison and Community Sentence Statistics Released

This resource provides structured access to New Zealand’s offender population statistics, detailing demographics and sentencing durations. By utilizing official platforms, researchers can retrieve granular data on prisoners and community sentence offenders, enabling comprehensive analysis of criminal justice trends over time. The statistics are derived directly from administrative records held by the Department of Corrections. To ensure individual privacy, random rounding has been applied to the figures. This approach balances the need for transparent public reporting with strict confidentiality protections for those within the justice system. These tables are vital for open data initiatives as they democratize access to critical government metrics. They allow analysts to examine systemic patterns in sentencing and imprisonment, fostering accountability and informed policy debates regarding correctional outcomes and community safety in New Zealand.
