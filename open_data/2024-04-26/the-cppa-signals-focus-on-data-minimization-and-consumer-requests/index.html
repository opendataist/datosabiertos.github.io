The CPPA Signals Focus on Data Minimization and Consumer Requests

The CPPA issued its first enforcement advisory emphasizing data minimization during consumer request processing. Businesses must stop collecting excessive personal information for identity verification or opt-out requests. This highlights how legal compliance directly impacts open data principles by restricting unnecessary data collection practices.
