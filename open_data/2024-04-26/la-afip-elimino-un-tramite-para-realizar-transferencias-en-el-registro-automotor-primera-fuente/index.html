La AFIP eliminó un trámite para realizar transferencias en el Registro Automotor - Primera Fuente

AFIP has eliminated the requirement for the CETA certificate, streamlining the transfer of used vehicles by leveraging technological advancements and integrating data with motor vehicle registries. This removal reflects a broader administrative review aimed at eliminating redundant documentation that previously hindered process efficiency. The decision relies on sufficient information exchange to ensure accurate tax reporting without bureaucratic obstacles. This simplification directly benefits open data initiatives by demonstrating how seamless interoperability between government agencies can replace manual certifications. It highlights the critical role of shared digital infrastructure in modernizing public administration and reducing friction for citizens. The move underscores the value of transparent, integrated data systems in driving economic efficiency. It serves as a practical example for open data advocates, showing how removing unnecessary intermediaries through better data sharing can enhance service delivery and foster trust in digital governance models.
