En qué pueblos de Aragón residen más jóvenes por debajo de los 16 años

The article highlights a significant demographic imbalance in Aragón, characterized by a scarce proportion of young people relative to the elderly population. This trend reveals a deep rural depopulation crisis, where the majority of municipalities face severe aging and low birth rates, threatening the region’s long-term social and economic sustainability. Data shows that youth concentration is highly localized around major urban centers like Zaragoza and Huesca, driven by periurbanization. In contrast, vast rural areas, particularly in Teruel and pre-Pyrenean zones, exhibit critically low percentages of minors. This spatial disparity underscores the challenges of maintaining viable communities outside metropolitan hubs, where infrastructure and services struggle to sustain dwindling populations. This information is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how publicly accessible demographic statistics can expose critical socioeconomic inequalities. By making such granular data available, authorities and researchers can identify vulnerable regions, track migration patterns, and design targeted policies to address rural decline and promote balanced regional development through evidence-based decision-making.
