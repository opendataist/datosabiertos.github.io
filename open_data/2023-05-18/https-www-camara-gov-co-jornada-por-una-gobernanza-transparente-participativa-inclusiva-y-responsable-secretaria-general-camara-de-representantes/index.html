This initiative underscores the importance of aligning legislative operations with open government principles through targeted awareness-raising for advisors. By addressing day-to-day operational questions, the campaign strengthens the relationship between representatives and their support units, ensuring that transparency is embedded in routine workflows rather than treated as an isolated compliance task. The emphasis on clear communication and accessible digital platforms demonstrates how open data practices can enhance citizen engagement. By simplifying navigation of legislative processes and promoting plain language, the government makes public information more usable, thereby fostering trust and enabling more effective democratic participation through visible and accountable mechanisms. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessity of contextualizing transparency for internal stakeholders. It shows that making government data truly effective requires not merely publishing information, but actively educating personnel on how to interpret and utilize these resources to improve service delivery and uphold ethical standards in real-time decision-making.
