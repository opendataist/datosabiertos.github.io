Así se pueden eliminar los datos personales de ChatGPT para proteger la privacidad

The expansion of artificial intelligence has sparked deep concerns about the privacy and security of personal data. The primary risk lies in the fact that machine learning systems process large volumes of information collected from the web and by users, which can lead to the misuse or exposure of sensitive data without individuals’ explicit consent. This situation has prompted European regulators to intensively investigate major developers, aiming to ensure compliance with data protection regulations. Uncertainty about the exact amount of personal information extracted during model training has motivated restrictive measures in several countries, underscoring the need for transparency in how companies handle user data. For the open data community, this article is crucial because it highlights the tension between technological innovation and fundamental rights. It illustrates the critical importance of implementing strict governance and privacy controls from the design phase of systems, ensuring that access to information and the ability to request deletion are real and effective, rather than mere formalities.
