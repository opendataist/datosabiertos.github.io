El registro de proveedores del Banco de España suma 36 empresas en 2023

The Bank of Spain’s registry for cryptocurrency service providers has grown to eighty-nine companies, reflecting a significant rise in industry adoption since its establishment. This expansion highlights the increasing integration of digital assets into the formal Spanish financial ecosystem, driven by regulatory clarity that mandates registration for entities handling currency exchanges and electronic wallet custody. However, the core implication is that this registration serves primarily as an anti-money laundering compliance tool rather than a financial approval. The supervisor explicitly clarifies that inclusion in the registry does not constitute validation of the services offered, nor does it involve supervision of financial, operational, or cybersecurity risks. Consequently, the framework lacks prudential oversight or market conduct regulations, leaving consumers without protective safeguards typically associated with traditional financial institutions. This distinction is vital for open data initiatives because it reveals a critical gap in available transparency metrics. While public registries exist, the absence of standardized data regarding service quality, security audits, or financial health means users cannot assess provider reliability through open information. Thus, the current dataset enables identification of market participants but fails to provide the substantive quality indicators necessary for informed decision-making or comprehensive financial analysis.
