INAI ordena al INM informar sobre datos personales recabados en AICM con sistemas de identificación biométrica

The INAI compelled the immigration institute to disclose its handling of biometric data at the airport, ensuring legal compliance and privacy protection. This mandates transparency in data processing, requiring public entities to respect fundamental rights such as consent and proportionality in their operations. This case is crucial for open data, as it highlights that transparency does not mean unregulated data exposure. It establishes that sharing information must balance accessibility with robust privacy safeguards, ensuring that ethical standards govern the use of sensitive biometric information in the public sector. The ruling serves as a warning for both the public and private sectors to rigorously assess privacy impacts. By prioritizing data protection principles, institutions can avoid risks while maintaining public trust, demonstrating that open data initiatives must inherently include strong mechanisms for personal data security and responsible governance.
