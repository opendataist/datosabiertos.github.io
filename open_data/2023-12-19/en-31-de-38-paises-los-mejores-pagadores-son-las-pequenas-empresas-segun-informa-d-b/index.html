En 31 de 38 países, los mejores pagadores son las pequeñas empresas (según Informa D&B)

Microenterprises consistently demonstrate superior payment punctuality compared to large corporations across most global regions. This trend highlights a significant operational efficiency gap, where smaller firms prioritize timely settlements, potentially enhancing their creditworthiness and supplier relationships. The data reveals stark contrasts, with nations like Spain and the Netherlands showing massive advantages for micro-enterprises, underscoring how business size influences financial discipline. Conversely, a minority of countries, primarily in Eastern Europe and India, see large companies outperforming smaller ones in payment speed. These exceptions suggest that local economic structures, regulatory enforcement, or cultural norms can invert typical payment behaviors. Such regional variations indicate that the relationship between firm size and timeliness is not universal but deeply rooted in specific market environments and institutional frameworks. This analysis is crucial for open data initiatives focused on transparency and economic fairness. By providing accessible, comparable metrics on payment behaviors, researchers and policymakers can identify systemic barriers to trade finance and promote inclusive economic growth. Open datasets enable the verification of these trends, fostering trust in financial ecosystems and supporting evidence-based strategies to assist smaller businesses in maintaining healthy cash flows globally.
