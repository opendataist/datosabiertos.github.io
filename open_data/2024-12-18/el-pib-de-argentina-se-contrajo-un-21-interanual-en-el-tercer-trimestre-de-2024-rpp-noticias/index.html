El PIB de Argentina se contrajo un 2,1 % interanual en el tercer trimestre de 2024 | RPP Noticias

El PIB de Argentina se contrajo un 2,1 % interanual en el tercer trimestre de 2024 | RPP Noticias

Argentina’s economy contracted significantly in the third quarter, extending a six-quarter streak of annual declines despite a brief quarterly rebound. The downturn reflects the severe impact of currency devaluation and strict austerity measures implemented by the new government, which have exacerbated high inflation and reduced both private and public consumption. Sectoral analysis reveals a mixed landscape, with agriculture showing growth after drought-related lows, while construction, manufacturing, and commerce suffered substantial losses. This fragmentation highlights the uneven recovery, where certain resilient sectors struggle to offset the broad stagnation affecting traditional economic pillars, leading to a negative accumulation for the year. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible, and standardized economic statistics. Reliable public datasets allow citizens and analysts to verify official claims, understand the real-world impact of policy changes, and hold institutions accountable for economic performance amidst complex macroeconomic transitions.
