El “Wayki” ya no es popular en Ayacucho según INEI | Jornada

El “Wayki” ya no es popular en Ayacucho según INEI | Jornada

The INEI report reveals a profound disconnect between Ayacucho’s regional leadership and its citizens, highlighting that an overwhelming majority rates the governor’s administration as poor. This sharp decline in political popularity stems largely from the public’s perception that their quality of life has not improved, with most families struggling to balance income and expenses while experiencing stagnant or deteriorating living conditions. The data underscores a crisis of governance where the administration fails to deliver tangible economic benefits, fueling widespread dissatisfaction. Economic stagnation further exacerbates this discontent, as key sectors like construction and agriculture have shown minimal growth or significant contraction during the reviewed period. These figures illustrate that the root cause of the political unrest is not merely political scandal, but the tangible lack of economic development and opportunity for the local population. The public’s negative assessment is directly correlated with their inability to see positive changes in their daily economic realities, making the current management style increasingly unsustainable. This article is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how government-produced statistics serve as a critical accountability tool for citizens. By making data on household incomes, sectoral production, and public perception transparent, open data empowers the electorate to form objective judgments about institutional performance. It highlights the importance of accessible, high-quality public information in facilitating democratic oversight, allowing voters to move beyond rhetoric and evaluate leaders based on measurable socioeconomic outcomes and governance effectiveness.
