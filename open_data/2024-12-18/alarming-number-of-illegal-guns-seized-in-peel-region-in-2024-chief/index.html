'Alarming' number of illegal guns seized in Peel Region in 2024: Chief

'Alarming' number of illegal guns seized in Peel Region in 2024: Chief

Peel Regional Police report an alarming surge in illegal firearms, highlighting a critical public safety threat. Despite seizing double the previous year’s amount through targeted enforcement, these figures represent only a fraction of the total illegal weapons in circulation. This disparity underscores the pervasive nature of the crisis and the limitations of current enforcement capabilities. The majority of seized guns originate from the United States, revealing a transborder supply chain that complicates local policing efforts. While arrests and charges have increased significantly, the sheer volume of firearms necessitates broader regional cooperation and stricter international controls to effectively stem the flow of weapons. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible police statistics can inform community safety discussions. By making seizure trends and enforcement outcomes publicly available, stakeholders can better analyze crime patterns, advocate for policy changes, and hold authorities accountable for public safety strategies.
