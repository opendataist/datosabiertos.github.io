La semilla de una cultura de privacidad

The article emphasizes that educating children about data privacy is crucial for fostering a culturally aware society. By involving minors in active debates, such as through INAI’s initiative, these programs do more than teach individual skills; they establish a foundational pillar for understanding privacy rights. This early engagement transforms children into informed citizens capable of navigating the digital world safely. A significant implication is the "multiplier effect," whereby sensitized children influence their families. When children discuss the importance of protecting personal information, parents and caregivers become more attentive to their own digital hygiene. This dynamic extends protection beyond the individual child, creating a ripple effect that strengthens privacy awareness within the household. This content is vital for the open data movement because it highlights the user-centric dimension of data ecosystems. Robust open data policies require not only technical transparency but also an educated public that understands its rights. Building this cultural foundation ensures that open data initiatives are supported by citizens who are aware of both the benefits and risks of data sharing, thereby securing a safer future for all.
