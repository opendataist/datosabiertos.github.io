Desempleo al alza: casi la mitad de los panameños en la informalidad

Desempleo al alza: casi la mitad de los panameños en la informalidad

Panama’s rising unemployment and soaring levels of informality highlight urgent gaps in the quality of labor market data. This situation underscores the critical need for open data initiatives to make employment statistics more accessible and transparent. Enhanced data visibility can empower citizens and policymakers to address these socio-economic challenges effectively. The disparity between formal and informal work reveals deeper structural issues that require detailed analysis. Open data platforms can facilitate this scrutiny by providing granular insights into labor trends. Consequently, stakeholders can better understand the extent of the crisis and advocate for improved working conditions. Ultimately, leveraging open data fosters accountability and drives informed decision-making. By democratizing access to these statistics, society can promote equitable economic policies. This approach ensures that vulnerable populations are not overlooked, paving the way for sustainable employment solutions and greater social justice.
