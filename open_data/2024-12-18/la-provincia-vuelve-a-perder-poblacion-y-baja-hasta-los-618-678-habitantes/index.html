La provincia vuelve a perder población y baja hasta los 618.678 habitantes

The latest municipal register reveals that Jaén has lost population below 620,000 residents for the first time, marking a continued decline since peaking in 2010. This demographic shift highlights a persistent trend of depopulation across the province, affecting the vast majority of its municipalities, from small villages to medium-sized towns. While many localities have shrunk, the capital and several secondary cities have experienced slight growth, contrasting with the widespread losses in rural areas. This divergence underscores the complex internal dynamics of regional development, where urban centers retain residents while peripheral communities struggle with outmigration, reshaping the social and economic landscape of the region. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how official statistical institutions like the INE maintain critical, accessible datasets that track granular demographic changes over time. Analyzing this information allows researchers and policymakers to identify trends, allocate resources effectively, and understand the long-term impacts of migration on local governance and service provision.
