El PBI subió 3,9% tras tres trimestres de caída consecutivos

Argentina’s GDP contracted significantly on a year-over-year basis, driven largely by a sharp decline in industrial activity and fixed capital formation. Despite these setbacks, agriculture provided a crucial positive offset, while rising exports and recovering private consumption indicated some resilience in specific economic sectors during the analyzed period. The data reveal a complex economic landscape in which structural weaknesses in investment coexist with temporary gains in trade and consumer spending. This mixed performance underscores the fragility of the recovery, highlighting how external factors and shifts in specific industries heavily influence overall macroeconomic health. This information is vital for open-data initiatives, as it provides essential, granular evidence of economic trends. Transparent access to such detailed statistical insights enables researchers and developers to build accurate models, fostering informed public discourse and supporting data-driven policy analysis regarding national economic stability.
