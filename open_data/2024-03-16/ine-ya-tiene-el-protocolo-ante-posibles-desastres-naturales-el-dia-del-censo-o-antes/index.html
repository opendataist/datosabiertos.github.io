INE ya tiene el "protocolo ante posibles desastres naturales" el día del Censo o antes

The National Institute of Statistics has developed a comprehensive disaster response protocol to ensure the inclusion of all Bolivians in the upcoming census, regardless of natural emergencies. This strategy prioritizes the integrity of the population count by adapting procedures based on the timing and severity of potential events. By establishing clear guidelines for different scenarios, the institution aims to maintain data continuity and guarantee that no resident is excluded from the national record, even in the face of unforeseen crises that might disrupt standard operations. In the event of severe disasters occurring immediately before or during the census, the protocol mandates a shift toward emergency coordination with civil defense authorities. The priority becomes preserving human life and rapidly assessing the impact on affected municipalities. To facilitate this, the institute employs specific digital applications to identify impacted sectors and deploy replacement agents if necessary. This approach ensures that the logistical framework can adapt dynamically, allowing for swift decision-making and resource allocation when standard fieldwork conditions become untenable due to environmental hazards. Crucially, this adaptation is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how flexible, transparent frameworks can protect data integrity during crises. By shifting from full questionnaires to rapid population counts in emergency zones, the system prevents the total loss of critical demographic information. This highlights the importance of designing open data infrastructure that anticipates disruptions, ensuring that statistical outputs remain reliable and comparable. Such protocols serve as a best practice for maintaining continuous, accessible public data streams even when standard collection methods are compromised by external shocks.
