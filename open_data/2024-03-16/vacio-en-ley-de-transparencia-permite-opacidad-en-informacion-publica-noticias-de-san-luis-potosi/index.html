Vacío en Ley de Transparencia permite opacidad en información pública - Noticias de San Luis Potosí

Vacío en Ley de Transparencia permite opacidad en información pública - Noticias de San Luis Potosí

Citizens Observing denounced that the CEGAIP in San Luis Potosí exploits legal loopholes to hinder transparency, taking advantage of widespread extensions to fail to meet its resolution obligations. The organization criticizes the agency for focusing on establishing new guidelines that indefinitely delay processes, justifying these delays with arguments such as workload, which limits effective access to public information. This situation is relevant for open data because it highlights how institutional opacity and the lack of strict deadlines hinder the availability and real utility of public data, preventing citizens from exercising their right to oversight and democratic control.
