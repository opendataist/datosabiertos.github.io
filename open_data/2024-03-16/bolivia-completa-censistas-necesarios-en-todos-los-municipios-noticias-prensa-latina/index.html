Bolivia completa censistas necesarios en todos los municipios - Noticias Prensa Latina

The National Institute of Statistics confirms that Bolivia is fully prepared for the upcoming census, with volunteer recruitment completed in all municipalities. This readiness ensures that the workforce needed for accurate data collection is in place, marking a critical milestone in national demographic monitoring. To guarantee operational efficiency, the institute has implemented comprehensive training programs delivered through both online and in-person modalities. By deploying instructors nationwide and providing accessible learning resources, the organization ensures that agents are competent and ready to carry out their duties, thereby enhancing the reliability and quality of the data collected during the census. Public authorities are aligning logistical and regulatory measures to support this large-scale event, including restrictions on alcohol sales and transit, as well as the repurposing of educational facilities. These actions underscore the importance of open data, as the census provides the foundational demographic statistics essential for transparent governance, policy planning, and resource allocation in a democratic society.
