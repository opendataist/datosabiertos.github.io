Gobernadora de Banxico, Victoria Rodríguez, inaugura especialidad en indicadores macroeconómicos del MIDE | El Universal

Gobernadora de Banxico, Victoria Rodríguez, inaugura especialidad en indicadores macroeconómicos del MIDE | El Universal

The launch of a specialized macroeconomic indicator program by Banxico and MIDE addresses a critical national gap in training professionals to effectively use statistical data. This initiative aims to enhance public understanding and decision-making regarding Mexico’s economic reality by teaching the practical analysis of key metrics, rather than focusing solely on theoretical knowledge. The program’s relevance to open data lies in its core objective: equipping students with the skills to accurately interpret and communicate official statistics. By emphasizing the construction and meaning of indicators produced by institutions such as INEGI and Banxico, it promotes the transparent and competent use of open government data, fostering a more informed citizenry and media. Ultimately, this academic effort strengthens the bridge between complex economic data and public discourse. It ensures that open data is not merely available but actively used to analyze economic trends, thereby improving the quality of public opinion and policy engagement through greater literacy in economic statistics.
