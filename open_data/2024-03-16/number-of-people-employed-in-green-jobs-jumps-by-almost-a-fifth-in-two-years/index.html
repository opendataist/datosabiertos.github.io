Number of people employed in green jobs jumps by ‘almost a fifth’ in two years

UK green employment surged significantly between 2020 and 2022, highlighting rapid growth in sectors like waste management, energy efficiency, and renewable energy. While this trend underscores the expanding economic footprint of environmental sustainability, the data remains experimental and subject to revision. Relevance to open data lies in the transparency of these provisional estimates. The Office for National Statistics acknowledges that current methodologies are incomplete, excluding key areas like grid decarbonization. This openness invites public scrutiny and collaborative improvement of data sources. Ultimately, this article illustrates the critical need for rigorous, evolving data standards in emerging fields. By sharing preliminary figures and methodologies, statistical bodies enable better policy-making while maintaining honesty about current limitations. This approach fosters trust and drives future improvements in how we measure environmental impact.
