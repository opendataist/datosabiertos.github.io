Chief Secretary Directs Launching of Online RTI Platform

Chief Secretary Directs Launching of Online RTI Platform

The Jammu and Kashmir administration is prioritizing the launch of a comprehensive digital Right to Information portal to significantly boost transparency and citizen engagement. By implementing an end-to-end online solution, the government aims to streamline grievance redressal, ensuring that public authorities and information officers can respond swiftly and efficiently to citizen requests. This initiative reflects a broader commitment to open data principles by modernizing access to public information through technology. Integrating essential features like payment gateways and alert systems demonstrates a focus on user-centric service delivery. Furthermore, conducting rigorous security audits before public launch ensures that the platform maintains high standards of data protection and integrity for all users. The project’s relevance to open data lies in its structural approach to governance. It not only digitizes RTI processes but also supports wider infrastructure upgrades, such as enhanced network connectivity and additional management portals. These efforts collectively strengthen the region’s digital ecosystem, fostering a more accountable, accessible, and efficient administrative framework for residents.
