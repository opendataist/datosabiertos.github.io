Propone INAI nueva agencia de transparencia | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Propone INAI nueva agencia de transparencia | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The article underscores the urgent need for the Senate to establish robust principles for a new transparency agency, ensuring that it preserves the independence and autonomy previously held by the INAI. This structural safeguard is essential to maintain the high-level guarantee model that has positioned Mexico as an international benchmark for institutional strength and democratic integrity. Such protections are vital for upholding open data standards, as genuine autonomy ensures that citizens’ access to information remains free from political interference. Without these constitutional guarantees, the centralization of functions under government secretaries risks undermining the impartiality required for effective open government initiatives and data protection. Consequently, the success of Mexico’s transition to a new regulatory framework hinges on translating political intentions into concrete legal structures. Preserving the existing level of service and transparency ensures that open data continues to effectively serve public needs, rather than becoming a tool for governmental control or opacity.
