Veracruz, entre los 7 estados con mayor población con alguna discapacidad: INEGI

The article highlights significant regional disparities in disability prevalence across Mexico, identifying states such as Veracruz and Zacatecas as having notably higher rates than the national average. This data underscores the critical need for inclusive public policies tailored to vulnerable populations, particularly older adults, who constitute the majority of cases. These findings are vital for open data initiatives, as they demonstrate how transparent demographic statistics enable researchers to identify systemic gaps and advocate for targeted social interventions. Accessible data fosters accountability and helps ensure that disability rights are addressed with precision. Ultimately, publishing granular, location-specific health metrics empowers communities and policymakers to design equitable solutions. This transparency strengthens the social contract by making invisible challenges visible, driving more effective resource allocation and promoting genuine social inclusion through data-driven decision-making.
