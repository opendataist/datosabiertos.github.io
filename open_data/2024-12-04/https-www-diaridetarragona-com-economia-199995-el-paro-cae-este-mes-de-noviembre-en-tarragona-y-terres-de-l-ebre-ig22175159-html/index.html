The Camp de Tarragona and Terres de l’Ebre regions saw a slight monthly decrease in registered unemployment, mainly due to declines in the construction and agriculture sectors. Despite this positive trend, the labor market continues to face significant structural challenges, including a substantial drop in employment contracts and a persistent gender gap among the unemployed. However, the broader context reveals economic resilience, as the annual rise in Social Security registrations indicates overall job growth and stability across the sector. This contrast underscores that while certain short-term indicators may fluctuate, the underlying labor force is expanding. This data is relevant to open data initiatives by demonstrating how transparent, localized labor statistics enable precise economic analysis. Access to such detailed breakdowns allows researchers and policymakers to identify sector-specific trends and demographic disparities, fostering evidence-based decisions that improve labor market outcomes and social inclusion.
