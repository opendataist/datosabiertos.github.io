Govt mulls central data repository on rooftop solar power generation

Govt mulls central data repository on rooftop solar power generation

The government plans to establish a central repository for real-time rooftop solar data to enhance national grid planning and scheduling. This initiative supports the massive residential solar expansion aimed at empowering households with affordable, clean energy. Open data is crucial for transparent monitoring of this massive infrastructure rollout. Aggregating distributed energy metrics allows policymakers to optimize resource allocation and verify subsidy distributions efficiently. Such transparency ensures that digital infrastructure accurately reflects ground realities. By making generation data accessible to the center, the state can better manage the transition toward sustainable energy independence across millions of homes.
