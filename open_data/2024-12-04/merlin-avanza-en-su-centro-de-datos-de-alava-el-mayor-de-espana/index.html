Merlin avanza en su centro de datos de Álava, el mayor de España

Merlin Properties inaugurates the first phase of a massive data center complex in Ribabellosa, positioning it as Spain’s largest. This strategic move directly supports open_data by establishing critical digital infrastructure that facilitates secure, high-volume data storage and exchange, essential for global connectivity. Driven by the exponential growth of generative AI, the facility offers scalable capacity while adhering to strict sustainability standards through renewable energy and water-free cooling. This demonstrates how modern open_data requirements increasingly demand infrastructure that is not only robust but also environmentally responsible, setting a new benchmark for efficient digital ecosystems. Located near key submarine cables, the center reinforces Europe’s role as a data gateway. Its significance for open_data lies in providing the foundational physical layer for seamless, low-latency information flow between continents, enabling researchers and institutions to access and share large datasets more efficiently across borders.
