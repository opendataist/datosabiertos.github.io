Marratxí desbanca a Esporles: estos son los municipios más ricos y más pobres de Mallorca

The article reveals a significant disparity between property values and household income in Mallorca, challenging assumptions about wealth distribution. It highlights that municipalities with the most expensive homes do not necessarily house the wealthiest residents on average, illustrating that luxury real estate often coexists with populations of modest economic means. This distinction is crucial for understanding the true socio-economic landscape beyond surface-level asset indicators. Furthermore, the data exposes a wide gap between the island’s highest and lowest income levels. By utilizing adjusted income figures per consumption unit, the report demonstrates that families in affluent areas possess substantially more disposable resources than those in lower-income municipalities. This metric provides a more accurate reflection of actual living standards and financial capacity across different communities. This information is vital for open-data initiatives, as it underscores the importance of nuanced, context-aware statistics. Simply displaying raw housing costs can mislead public perception regarding wealth; therefore, accessible and properly normalized income data is essential for informed policy-making and accurate socio-economic analysis.
