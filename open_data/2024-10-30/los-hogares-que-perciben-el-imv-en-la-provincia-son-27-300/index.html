Los hogares que perciben el IMV en la provincia son 27.300

The article highlights a significant surge in recipients of the Minimum Vital Income and childcare supplements in Albacete, particularly among women and families with children. This rapid growth underscores the critical role of these social safety nets in preventing exclusion for vulnerable households, reflecting a broader national trend of increasing reliance on state support during times of economic hardship. Relevance to open data lies in the necessity of transparent, granular statistics to monitor these social dynamics. By making such detailed demographic and geographic data publicly accessible, citizens and researchers can effectively analyze policy impacts and equity. This transparency allows for the identification of specific at-risk groups, ensuring that digital tools and algorithms used in social services are fair and accurately reflect societal needs. Ultimately, this case illustrates why high-quality, open datasets are essential for accountable governance. Without accessible data, it becomes difficult to evaluate the real-world efficacy of welfare programs. Open data empowers civil society to hold institutions responsible, fostering a more informed public discourse on poverty reduction and social justice, which is fundamental to improving public services.
