Iniciativa del diputado Jorge Guzmán: Comisión de Seguridad aprueba proyecto que crea registro público de Prófugos de la Justicia más buscados de Chile

Chile has advanced a legislative proposal to establish a public registry of high-interest fugitives, aiming to address the security crisis by enabling citizens to assist law enforcement. This initiative prioritizes transparency, allowing the public identification of dangerous individuals evading justice to prevent further crimes and facilitate their apprehension. The measure seeks to transform public safety by leveraging open access to critical information, thereby fostering broader citizen participation in locating those who commit serious offenses. By sharing relevant background details, authorities hope to accelerate investigations and ensure legal accountability for those involved in organized crime or violent acts. This development is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how publishing specific, actionable datasets can enhance public safety and civic engagement. It highlights the potential of open government to empower communities through accessible information, turning raw legal records into tools for collective security and judicial efficiency.
