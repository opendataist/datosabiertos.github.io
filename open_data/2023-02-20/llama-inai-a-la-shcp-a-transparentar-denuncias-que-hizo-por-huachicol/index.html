Llama INAI a la SHCP a transparentar denuncias que hizo por huachicol

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) has ordered the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit (SHCP) to disclose detailed records of criminal complaints related to fuel smuggling across recent presidential administrations. This initiative underscores the critical role of transparency in combating organized crime and the misappropriation of public funds, ensuring that citizens can verify the government’s efforts to combat illicit activities. Such data is essential for evaluating the effectiveness of anti-theft strategies implemented by various government agencies. The principles of open data require that these results be accessible to society, enabling independent assessment of how authorities handle complex issues involving collusion between public officials and criminal organizations. Ultimately, this request highlights the importance of open government in upholding accountability. By making these specific records public, the state empowers civil society to measure the actual impact on economic losses and institutional integrity. This transparency fosters trust and ensures that anti-corruption measures are not merely declared but demonstrably effective in safeguarding national resources.
