CSIF lamenta el tercer aumento mensual consecutivo en C-LM, segunda región donde más ha subido en octubre

CSIF lamenta el tercer aumento mensual consecutivo en C-LM, segunda región donde más ha subido en octubre

Unions emphasize that, despite annual improvements, consecutive monthly job losses reveal a fragile labor market, particularly in healthcare. This decline underscores the urgent need for transparent public employment data to accurately assess the health of the sector. The article advocates for policy reforms to protect workers affected by extreme weather, stressing that statistical reporting must distinguish between temporary and permanent employment impacts. This is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how granular, accessible statistics enable civil society to verify government claims and advocate for evidence-based labor protections during crises.
