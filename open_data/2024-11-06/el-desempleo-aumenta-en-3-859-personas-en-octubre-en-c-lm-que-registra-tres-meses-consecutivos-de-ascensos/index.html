El desempleo aumenta en 3.859 personas en octubre en C-LM, que registra tres meses consecutivos de ascensos

October’s registered unemployment figures reveal a complex economic transition, marking the lowest October joblessness rate since 2008 despite a slight national increase driven by the post-seasonal end of tourism in the services sector. This data highlights the seasonal volatility inherent in labor markets, where end-of-season fluctuations naturally affect employment stability, particularly in service-dependent regions. The report underscores significant demographic and gender disparities, with women and youth experiencing sharper rises in unemployment compared to men and older workers. This uneven impact suggests that structural vulnerabilities persist within the labor force, requiring targeted policies to mitigate the disproportionate burden on these specific groups as the economy adjusts to seasonal shifts. Relevant to open data, this detailed breakdown exemplifies how granular, public statistical records enable deep socio-economic analysis. By providing accessible metrics on gender, age, and sectoral distribution, such datasets empower researchers and policymakers to identify inequities, validate policy effectiveness, and foster transparency in public administration.
