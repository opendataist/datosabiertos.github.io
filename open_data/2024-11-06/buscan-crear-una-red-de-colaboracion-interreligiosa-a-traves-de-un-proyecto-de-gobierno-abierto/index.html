Buscan crear una Red de Colaboración Interreligiosa a través de un proyecto de Gobierno Abierto

Buscan crear una Red de Colaboración Interreligiosa a través de un proyecto de Gobierno Abierto

The initiative establishes a collaborative framework between the state and faith-based organizations to foster dialogue and strategic alliances. This pilot project aims to enhance mutual understanding and leverage the extensive social contributions of religious groups in the health and education sectors. Its relevance to open data lies in the creation of tools to visualize and disseminate these actions, promoting transparency about the territorial work carried out by civil society. By treating this as a pilot test to gather data and refine methods, the project demonstrates an innovative approach to public management. Ultimately, this effort seeks to build sustainable peace through inclusive governance. The structured collection and dissemination of information regarding these collaborations highlight how open data can facilitate better state–civil society relations and support democratic participation in policy development.
