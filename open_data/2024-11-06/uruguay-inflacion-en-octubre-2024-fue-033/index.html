Uruguay: inflación en octubre 2024 fue 0,33%

The article outlines Uruguay’s latest inflation trends, highlighting how specific sectors such as food, clothing, and transport drive price fluctuations. This data exemplifies open data in action, as the National Statistics Institute (INE) publishes granular, accessible information that enables society to monitor economic health transparently. By breaking down indices into specific categories, the report reveals nuanced market dynamics, such as seasonal price changes in apparel and decreases in energy costs. This level of detail is crucial for understanding the real-world impact of inflation on households, moving beyond aggregate figures to show precisely where costs are rising or falling. Ultimately, this open dataset empowers citizens, researchers, and policymakers to make informed decisions based on accurate, timely evidence. It demonstrates the value of public statistics in fostering accountability and enabling deeper, more democratic engagement with national economic indicators, ensuring that such vital information remains available for public scrutiny and analysis.
