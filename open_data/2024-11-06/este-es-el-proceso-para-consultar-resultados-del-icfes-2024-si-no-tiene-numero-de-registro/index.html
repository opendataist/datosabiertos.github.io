Este es el proceso para consultar resultados del ICFES 2024 si no tiene número de registro

Este es el proceso para consultar resultados del ICFES 2024 si no tiene número de registro

The Ministry of Education and ICFES have released the results of the Saber 11 exams, a critical dataset for understanding educational equity in Colombia. By evaluating hundreds of thousands of students across numerous municipalities, this publication highlights the scale of national assessment efforts. The relevance to open data lies in how these aggregated results can be analyzed to identify regional disparities and policy impacts, making transparent the performance of the public education system. Accessing individual scores has been streamlined through the official ICFES portal, which now allows users to retrieve results using only personal identification and birth date, removing the previous barrier of needing a specific registration number. This improvement in user experience and data accessibility encourages broader engagement with educational metrics. It demonstrates a shift toward more inclusive data practices, ensuring that students and families can easily verify their academic standing without complex administrative hurdles. These procedural updates underscore the importance of user-friendly interfaces in public data portals. By simplifying the retrieval process and offering PDF downloads, the government facilitates the reuse and dissemination of individual results. This approach supports a culture of transparency and accountability, empowering stakeholders to better understand and utilize educational outcomes for personal and community development.
