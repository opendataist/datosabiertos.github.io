El INAI diseña plan para ahorrar más de 277 mdp y evitar su desaparición

The INAI has developed a comprehensive reengineering plan to cut its budget by 25%, aiming to save hundreds of millions of pesos. This internal restructuring is a strategic response to the impending legislative debate regarding the possible extinction of autonomous bodies, including the INAI itself, driven by administrative simplification reforms. By proactively demonstrating fiscal responsibility, the institute seeks to justify its continued existence and vital role in democratic oversight. Key measures include reducing the number of commissioners, eliminating redundant administrative units, and streamlining international activities. The plan also addresses high expenditures on outsourced services and renegotiates costly leases. These changes aim to optimize resources by shifting focus from excessive management layers and inactive participation to core operational efficiencies, thereby showcasing the institution's commitment to transparency and effective public fund management without compromising its essential functions. This article is relevant to open data because the INAI is the primary guardian of citizens’ rights to access public information and protect personal data. Its potential extinction would severely undermine the transparency framework that ensures government accountability. The institute’s efforts to survive and optimize operations highlight the critical dependence of open data ecosystems on robust, independent regulatory bodies, emphasizing that structural sustainability is essential for maintaining public trust and the integrity of information rights.
