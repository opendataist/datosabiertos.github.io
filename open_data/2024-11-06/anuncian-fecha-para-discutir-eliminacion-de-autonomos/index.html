Anuncian fecha para discutir eliminación de autónomos

Anuncian fecha para discutir eliminación de autónomos

A proposed constitutional reform seeks to eliminate Mexico’s autonomous bodies, including the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI). This significant shift aims to centralize control and dismantle existing oversight mechanisms designed to ensure public accountability and data protection. The legislation is advancing rapidly through legislative committees, with a vote expected in mid-November. This accelerated timeline reflects the ruling party’s priority to reshape state structures and consolidate power, potentially altering the balance between government transparency and executive authority. This development is critical for open data advocates, as it threatens the independence of key institutions safeguarding access to public information. The potential removal of such bodies could severely undermine citizens’ rights to obtain data, signaling a regressive trend in transparency and democratic governance within the country.
