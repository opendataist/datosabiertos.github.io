FOIA Results: $11 Million to Bribe OB-GYNs to Lie to Mothers About Safety of mRNA-Vaccines | SHTF Plan

The article argues that medical professionals were coerced and financially incentivized to mislead the public regarding the safety of mRNA vaccines for pregnant women and fetuses. It claims that extensive funding directed toward influencers and medical organizations was designed to suppress dissenting opinions and ensure compliance with government health directives, suggesting a coordinated effort to prioritize vaccination rates over established obstetric safety standards. Central to the narrative is the assertion that the vaccines cause severe reproductive harm, including placental calcification and fetal damage, which were allegedly known by pharmaceutical companies prior to public distribution. The text presents the termination of dissenting physician Dr. James Thorp as evidence of institutional suppression, arguing that healthcare providers who question these safety claims face professional ruin and legal threats. This is framed as a deliberate strategy to eliminate credible warnings about potential fertility issues and pregnancy complications. This content is highly relevant to open data discussions as it highlights the role of Freedom of Information Act requests in exposing alleged hidden correlations between public health policies and financial incentives. By emphasizing internal documents and funding trails, the article underscores how transparency mechanisms can reveal discrepancies between official scientific narratives and underlying commercial or political motivations, advocating for greater accountability in health data release.
