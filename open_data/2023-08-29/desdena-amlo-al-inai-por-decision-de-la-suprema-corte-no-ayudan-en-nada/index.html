Desdeña AMLO al INAI por decisión de la Suprema Corte: ‘no ayudan en nada’

López Obrador demands that INAI commissioners abandon partisan behavior and prioritize transparency over oligarchic interests, insisting that their duty lies solely with the Mexican people. He challenges them to embrace honesty and reject serving powerful elites, urging a genuine commitment to the integrity of public service. The president further calls on these officials to voluntarily reduce their salaries as part of the broader austerity policy driving the Fourth Transformation. This move emphasizes ethical governance and fiscal responsibility, aiming to align public sector conduct with the government’s core values of integrity and national development. This highlights open data’s critical role in ensuring institutional accountability. By enforcing transparency, it prevents the secrecy that enables corruption. Open data serves as a vital tool for verifying that public officials act honestly and serve citizens rather than special interests, thereby strengthening democratic trust and governmental legitimacy through accessible, verifiable information.
