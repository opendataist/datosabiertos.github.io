Freedom of Information

The Society of Environmental Journalists established a task force to advocate for public access to environmental data, empowering journalists to overcome information barriers. By providing practical resources and legal guidance, the organization supports transparent reporting on critical ecological issues. This commitment to open data ensures that vital environmental knowledge remains accessible to the media and the general public.
