"Global benchmark": WHO's Catharina Boehme hails India as first in region to adopt national health AI strategy

India has launched SAHI and BODH at the AI Impact Summit, marking a critical evolution in health policy. This strategic move shifts the focus from theoretical potential to tangible implementation, addressing governance, interoperability, and infrastructure to ensure AI tools are safe, reliable, and effective for frontline workers. The nation’s comprehensive national AI strategy for health sets a global benchmark, particularly within the South-East Asia region. By integrating AI into public systems for tasks like tuberculosis prediction and diabetic screening, India demonstrates how digital health blueprints can transform service delivery and accelerate research through established open standards. This development is vital to open data as it highlights the necessity of standardized, interoperable frameworks to maximize AI’s societal impact. BODH’s role in benchmarking open data platforms underscores that transparent, accessible, and secure data environments are essential for building trust and enabling the scalable adoption of innovative health technologies across diverse public and private sectors.
