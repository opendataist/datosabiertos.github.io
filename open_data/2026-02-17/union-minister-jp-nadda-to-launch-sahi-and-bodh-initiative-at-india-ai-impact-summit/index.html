Union Minister JP Nadda to launch SAHI and BODH initiative at India AI Impact Summit

India is advancing its health AI ecosystem through two pivotal initiatives launched at the India AI Impact Summit. SAHI provides a strategic framework for the ethical and evidence-based adoption of artificial intelligence across the healthcare system, ensuring alignment with public health priorities while guiding governance and deployment standards. Complementing this, the BODH platform offers privacy-preserving benchmarking for health AI models. By allowing rigorous evaluation using diverse real-world data without sharing underlying datasets, it enhances transparency and quality assurance. This approach directly addresses critical data governance challenges, fostering trust in AI-driven healthcare solutions. These initiatives are highly relevant to open data because BODH functions as a digital public good that enables secure, standardized access to health intelligence. SAHI establishes the governance structures necessary for responsible data stewardship, ensuring that open data principles are integrated into the development of a robust, globally competitive health AI infrastructure that prioritizes safety and inclusivity.
