Actualización cartográfica devela conflictos limítrofes entre municipios potosinos

The INE’s ongoing cartographic update in Potosí highlights persistent, long-standing boundary disputes between municipalities like Potosí and Yocalla. These territorial conflicts delay the finalization of administrative maps, demonstrating how unresolved local political tensions directly hinder official data standardization efforts. Although the project is ninety percent complete, these overlapping claims require significant adjustments before the process can conclude in the northern regions. The persistence of such disagreements underscores the critical importance of clearly defined geographic boundaries for accurate and reliable national statistical records. This situation is highly relevant to open data because incomplete or disputed geospatial data compromises the integrity of public information systems. Resolving these boundaries ensures that open datasets reflect accurate administrative realities, thereby improving transparency and utility for researchers and citizens relying on precise location-based statistics.
