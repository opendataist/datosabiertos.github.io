The article reveals a notable disparity in the salaries of RTVE presenters, highlighting that the hosts of 'MasterChef' receive significant amounts per program. This information was obtained through a request for access to data after the corporation initially resisted, which forced the Transparency Council to intervene and compel the publication of the salaries of external collaborators. However, transparency appears selective when it comes to staff with direct contracts with RTVE. Although the regulatory body ordered the disclosure of their compensation, considering that the public interest outweighs the right to privacy, the public broadcaster disobeys the resolution, citing data protection to conceal the remuneration of figures such as Ana Blanco or Carlos Franganillo. This situation is crucial for the open data movement, as it exemplifies the tension between transparency legislation and institutional resistance to accountability regarding the use of public funds. It illustrates how entities can interpret laws in a fragmented manner, applying rigor in some cases while evading responsibility in others, underscoring the need for active oversight and effective legal mechanisms to ensure genuine openness of government information.
