Vietnam racks up trade surplus of US$1.97 billion during second half of March

The report highlights significant volatility in export performance, with a small number of high-value tech and textile goods dominating revenue while most other categories face sharp declines. This uneven distribution underscores the critical need for open data to identify emerging market trends and support diverse economic sectors beyond dominant industries. Conversely, import volumes have decreased notably, contributing to an overall trade surplus despite a general drop in total turnover. Analyzing this imbalance requires transparent, accessible datasets that reveal underlying structural shifts in global supply chains and domestic production capabilities. Such granular transparency is essential for policymakers to craft effective trade strategies. Open data facilitates accurate forecasting and resource allocation, ensuring that economic resilience is built on comprehensive visibility rather than aggregated averages that may mask critical sectoral weaknesses.
