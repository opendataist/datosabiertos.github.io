LAMOST Releases DR10 Data: Over 20M Spectra

The LAMOST DR10 dataset marks a historic milestone as the first survey to release over twenty million stellar spectra, dwarfing previous global efforts. This massive expansion provides an unprecedented volume of high-quality spectral data, fundamentally accelerating research into Galactic formation and evolution. By combining large aperture with wide field-of-view capabilities, LAMOST delivers both low and medium-resolution spectra in equal, massive quantities. This scale enables astronomers to study stellar parameters with greater statistical significance, transforming observational astronomy into a new era of big data science. This release is crucial for open_data as it exemplifies the power of large-scale, accessible astronomical repositories. It demonstrates how open, comprehensive datasets can democratize access to cutting-edge scientific resources, fostering global collaboration and innovation in astrophysics without barriers.
