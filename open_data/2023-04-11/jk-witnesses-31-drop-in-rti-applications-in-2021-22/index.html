JK Witnesses 31% Drop In RTI Applications In 2021-22

Jammu and Kashmir saw a significant drop in Right to Information applications, signaling reduced public engagement with transparency mechanisms. This decline suggests potential barriers to accessing information, undermining the core principle of open governance in the region. Such trends highlight the critical importance of monitoring RTI usage to ensure accountability. When application volumes fall, it may indicate systemic inefficiencies or discouragement, which directly impacts the effectiveness of open data initiatives. This report is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how policy changes affect civic participation. Ensuring accessible information systems is vital for fostering trust and enabling citizens to utilize public data effectively for informed decision-making.
