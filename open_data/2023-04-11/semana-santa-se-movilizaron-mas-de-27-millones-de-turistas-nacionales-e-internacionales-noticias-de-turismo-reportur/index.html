Semana Santa: se movilizaron más de 2,7 millones de turistas nacionales e internacionales | Noticias de turismo REPORTUR

The article reports record-breaking tourism numbers during the Easter holiday, driven significantly by a surge in international visitors from neighboring countries. This trend highlights a growing reliance on cross-border travel to boost domestic recovery, despite the absence of previous government stimulus programs. Data reveals increased occupancy rates across diverse regions, indicating a federal shift in tourist preferences rather than concentration in traditional hubs. The spending per capita also rose, suggesting stronger economic performance per visitor compared to the prior year. This case is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how transparent, publicly available tourism statistics are essential for understanding market dynamics. Reliable public data enables policymakers and industry stakeholders to track performance trends and make informed decisions about future tourism strategies and resource allocation.
