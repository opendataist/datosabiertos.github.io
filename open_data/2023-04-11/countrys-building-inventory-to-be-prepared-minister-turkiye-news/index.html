Country’s building inventory to be prepared: Minister - Türkiye News

Turkey is launching a comprehensive national inventory of all buildings and a centralized digital data repository to assess earthquake risks and track damage in vulnerable zones. This initiative aims to enhance public safety by enabling experts to access critical infrastructure information, thereby creating a more resilient framework for disaster management and response. The government plans to implement rigorous, periodic building inspections and utilize advanced technology, such as aerial drones, to navigate dangerous environments during crises. By integrating historical and sociological contexts into urban planning, authorities intend to protect cultural heritage while ensuring that essential public facilities remain in safer locations, significantly improving structural integrity across the country. This shift toward data-driven urban planning is vital for open data initiatives, as it establishes a standardized, accessible foundation for environmental and risk information. By making detailed building and damage data available to relevant institutions, Turkey fosters transparency and supports evidence-based decision-making, allowing researchers and developers to build tools that enhance community resilience and disaster preparedness.
