El PIB de España se ralentiza y crece un 0,4% en el segundo trimestre

The article reveals that the Spanish economy experienced a slowdown in the second quarter, with growth decelerating compared to the previous period. This moderation is primarily attributed to a significant cooling in external demand, as exports and international spending declined sharply. However, this negative trend was effectively offset by a robust recovery in domestic demand, driven by increased household consumption and higher investment levels, which suggests that internal resilience continues to support economic activity despite external headwinds. Sectoral performance was mixed, highlighting vulnerabilities in traditional industries. While the services sector maintained positive growth, agriculture and industry suffered notable contractions. These fluctuations indicate structural disparities within the economy, where the service sector acts as a stabilizer while manufacturing and agricultural outputs face cyclical downturns. The overall narrative points to an economy relying increasingly on domestic consumption and services to counterbalance the weakness in industrial production and external trade dynamics.
