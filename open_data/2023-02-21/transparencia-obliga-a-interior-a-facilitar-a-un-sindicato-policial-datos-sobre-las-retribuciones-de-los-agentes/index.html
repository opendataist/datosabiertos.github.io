Transparencia obliga a Interior a facilitar a un sindicato policial datos sobre las retribuciones de los agentes

Transparencia obliga a Interior a facilitar a un sindicato policial datos sobre las retribuciones de los agentes

The Transparency Council ruled that police salary data must be disclosed, rejecting administrative attempts to withhold them under the guise of internal negotiation bodies. This decision affirms that transparency rights apply strictly, ensuring public access to basic employment information regardless of internal departmental objections or security claims. This case is crucial for open data because it establishes that internal structures cannot override legal transparency obligations. It reinforces the principle that citizens and representatives have the right to access fundamental institutional data, preventing authorities from using procedural complexities to obscure financial and personnel information from public scrutiny. The ruling highlights the necessity of maintaining robust transparency mechanisms to check administrative opacity. By empowering the oversight body to enforce data release, it sets a precedent that strengthens accountability within public institutions, ensuring that open data principles prevail over internal bureaucratic resistance.
