China publicó conjuntos de datos científicos de cargas útiles de Chang'e-4

China released new datasets from the Chang'e-4 mission, enhancing transparency and accessibility for lunar research. This initiative supports open science by providing extensive scientific data to the global community. It facilitates collaborative discovery regarding the moon’s far side, advancing our understanding of deep space exploration.
