Hacienda debe transparentar denuncias por huachicol

Hacienda debe transparentar denuncias por huachicol

The INAI ordered the SHCP to disclose detailed data on fuel smuggling complaints from two presidencies, targeting public officials and specific operational details. This mandate reveals how the government struggles with organized crime collusion and massive economic losses, undermining current anti-theft strategies. This case highlights the critical role of transparency in combating corruption. By forcing the release of sensitive administrative records, open data principles enable public scrutiny of state efficiency. Ultimately, this action demonstrates that open data is essential for accountability. Access to such information allows citizens to assess government effectiveness against organized crime, ensuring that public resources are protected and institutions remain transparent.
