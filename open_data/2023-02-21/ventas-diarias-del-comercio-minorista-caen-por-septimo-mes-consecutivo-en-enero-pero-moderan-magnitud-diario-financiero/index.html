Ventas diarias del comercio minorista caen por séptimo mes consecutivo en enero, pero moderan magnitud | Diario Financiero

The Central Bank has published new experimental statistics on daily retail sales, based on direct administrative data from electronic invoices. This initiative reflects a strong commitment to open data by providing transparent, high-frequency metrics that enable a more detailed and immediate understanding of consumer behavior and overall economic health. The latest data show a continued, albeit moderate, contraction in retail activity, indicating that the economy is stabilizing following the extreme volatility triggered by the pandemic. By delivering consistent time series grounded in stable business reporting, this approach departs from traditional lagged estimates, offering a clearer, real-time view of the ongoing economic adjustment and its impact on small businesses. This release is particularly significant for open data because it emphasizes accessibility and verifiability. By leveraging existing government records to construct independent indicators, authorities strengthen public trust and allow researchers to analyze economic trends with greater precision. Ultimately, this transparency facilitates better decision-making for both policymakers and civil society, demonstrating how open data infrastructure can directly enhance economic monitoring and accountability.
