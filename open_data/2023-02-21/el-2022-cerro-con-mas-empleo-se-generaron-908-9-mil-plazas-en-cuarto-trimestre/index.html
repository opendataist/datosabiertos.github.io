El 2022 cerró con más empleo, se generaron 908.9 mil plazas en cuarto trimestre

El 2022 cerró con más empleo, se generaron 908.9 mil plazas en cuarto trimestre

Mexico experienced a significant reduction in unemployment and substantial job creation, predominantly within the formal economy. This shift suggests improved labor market stability, highlighting a structural transition in which formal sectors absorb most new hires while growth in informal employment remains marginal. Service industries led this expansion, driven by demand in social services and professional sectors, whereas the agricultural sector contracted. Consequently, the active population increased while the inactive population decreased, indicating broader economic engagement and potentially higher productivity across key non-agricultural fields. This report is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates the importance of accessible, granular statistical information for analyzing economic trends. Transparent data enables policymakers and citizens to verify claims about formalization and sectoral growth, fostering accountability and informed decision-making in the allocation of public resources.
