"Knowledge and community" - IBM and the benefits of the AI Alliance one year on

The AI Alliance, led by IBM, Meta, and others, has successfully scaled to over 140 organizations, driving the adoption of open, trustworthy AI across diverse sectors. This initiative addresses the critical barrier of governance and trust that often halts enterprise AI projects, demonstrating that transparency and open-source licensing can make advanced models like IBM Granite both powerful and secure for private enterprise. Open-source AI is vital for bridging the gap between academia and industry, allowing researchers to verify training data and mitigate risks like data poisoning. This transparency fosters a collaborative environment where institutions from healthcare to sports can trust and utilize AI technologies, moving beyond vague assurances to verifiable reliability and shared knowledge across previously siloed communities. The relevance to open data lies in how open-source models enable data control and remediation, such as removing specific information from trained systems. By providing visibility into model development and data usage, these initiatives empower institutions in low-resource settings to access life-saving tools safely, proving that open access is not just a technical choice but a necessary ethical framework for equitable technological progress.
