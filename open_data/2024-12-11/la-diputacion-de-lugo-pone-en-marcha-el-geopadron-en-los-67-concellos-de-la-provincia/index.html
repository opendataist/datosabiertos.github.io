La Diputación de Lugo pone en marcha el Geopadrón en los 67 concellos de la provincia

La Diputación de Lugo pone en marcha el Geopadrón en los 67 concellos de la provincia

The Provincial Council of Lugo has implemented "Geopadrón," a comprehensive initiative designed to modernize municipal censuses across all 67 local councils in the province. By leveraging European funds, this program establishes a unified, geolocated database that integrates housing and household data with national records from the Institute of National Statistics and the Cadastral Survey. The primary goal is to ensure the accuracy and reliability of demographic information, which is essential for effective local governance and administrative efficiency. Accurate census data is fundamental for municipalities to correctly administer social policies, such as housing and employment support, and to manage fiscal resources effectively. Reliable data allows local governments to apply for external grants more successfully and optimize tax collection. By correcting discrepancies between municipal records, national statistics, and cadastral data, the initiative ensures that public policies are based on current and verified sociodemographic realities, thereby improving the quality of public services provided to residents. This agreement is highly relevant to the open data movement because it prioritizes data interoperability and standardization across different administrative levels. By creating a consistent, georeferenced dataset that aligns local records with national standards, the project sets a precedent for transparent and usable public information. Such integration facilitates better data sharing, enhances accountability, and enables researchers and citizens to access reliable, standardized geographic and demographic information, which are core principles of the open data ecosystem.
