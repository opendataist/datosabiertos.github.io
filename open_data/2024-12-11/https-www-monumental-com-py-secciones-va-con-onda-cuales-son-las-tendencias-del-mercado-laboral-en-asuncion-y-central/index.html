The ETML 2024 report reveals a significant mismatch in Paraguay’s labor market, where companies struggle to find candidates with adequate technical skills, experience, and socio-emotional competencies. This highlights that educational outcomes often fail to align with employer expectations, creating barriers to employment despite available job vacancies across the industry, commerce, and services sectors. The relevance to open data lies in the transparency required to address these gaps. Open datasets on labor trends enable policymakers and educators to accurately analyze skill shortages, fostering better alignment between vocational training programs and market demands. Accessible statistics empower stakeholders to design evidence-based interventions that improve workforce readiness and reduce hiring inefficiencies. Looking ahead, the study notes a strong shift toward digital transformation, with many enterprises planning to adopt data science and automation tools. This underscores the urgency of integrating digital literacy into open government initiatives. By making labor data accessible and interoperable, authorities can better track technological adoption and guide upskilling efforts, ensuring the workforce remains competitive in an increasingly digitized economy.
