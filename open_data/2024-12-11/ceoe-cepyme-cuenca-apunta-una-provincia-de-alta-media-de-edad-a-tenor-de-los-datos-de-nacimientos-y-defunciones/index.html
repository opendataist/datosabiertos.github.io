CEOE CEPYME Cuenca apunta una provincia de alta media de edad a tenor de los datos de nacimientos y defunciones

The CEOE CEPYME Cuenca highlights a persistent demographic challenge in which mortality significantly exceeds births, confirming the province’s aging and rural character. This structural imbalance calls for long-term strategies to reverse the depopulation trend, emphasizing the need for sustained efforts to develop productive infrastructure. Attracting young residents is identified as the key mechanism to counteract this negative dynamic. By focusing on youth attraction, the organization believes it can effectively address the broader issue of rural decline currently affecting various regions across Spain, thereby ensuring a more balanced population structure in the future. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public statistical records, such as those from the National Statistics Institute (INE), serve as essential tools for local business chambers. It illustrates the critical role of data-driven analysis in diagnosing socio-economic realities and shaping targeted policy responses to complex demographic challenges.
