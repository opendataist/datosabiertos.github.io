African Development Bank and Partners Drive Data Transformation in Africa With Sdmx and Open Data Platform 2.0 Training

African Development Bank and Partners Drive Data Transformation in Africa With Sdmx and Open Data Platform 2.0 Training

African nations and international partners recently convened to advance statistical interoperability through the adoption of SDMX standards and the upgraded Open Data Platform 2.0. This collaborative effort focuses on enhancing data management efficiency and ensuring that official statistics are reliable and comparable across the continent. By standardizing how data is exchanged, African countries can better support evidence-based policymaking and integrate with global systems. The initiative significantly improves access to open data, making statistical systems more interoperable and ready for emerging technologies like artificial intelligence. This shift allows for greater automation and seamless integration between national, regional, and global statistical networks. Consequently, stakeholders can leverage high-quality data more effectively, fostering transparency and driving inclusive development through improved information accessibility. This progress is vital to the open data movement, as it demonstrates a measurable increase in African countries’ commitment to transparency and data quality. Recent reports highlight a substantial rise in open data maturity levels, with several nations achieving top global rankings. These advancements underscore the region’s growing capability to share and utilize data openly, setting a benchmark for others to follow in the global push for accessible information.
