Inflación en Argentina en julio se sitúa en el 1,9%

Inflación en Argentina en julio se sitúa en el 1,9%

Argentina’s July consumer price index rose by 1.9%, marking the third consecutive month below this threshold and signaling a significant slowdown in year-over-year inflation. This trend, absent since late 2017, reflects limited ability to pass on currency devaluations to prices due to weak consumption and scarce circulating cash, a constraint acknowledged by both market analysts and the government as a structural limit on widespread price hikes. The data highlights distinct sectoral divergences, with education and housing costs experiencing substantial increases compared to other categories. Notably, underlying inflation, which excludes regulated and seasonal prices, reached its lowest level since early 2018, reinforcing the perception of easing inflationary pressure. These metrics suggest that current economic policies are successfully dampening price volatility, although authorities anticipate potential lag effects from recent currency adjustments in subsequent reports. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, official statistical indicators serve as critical tools for public accountability and economic analysis. The precise breakdown of inflation by category allows citizens and experts to scrutinize government performance and understand real economic impacts. Reliable open data enables informed debate on monetary policy effectiveness and ensures that macroeconomic trends are grounded in verifiable, accessible evidence rather than speculation.
