Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has refreshed its Data Portal, offering enhanced tools for exploring the Ibrahim Index of African Governance. This comprehensive dataset benchmarks public governance across all 54 African nations, tracking trends over a ten-year period. The updated interface allows users to deeply analyze country-specific performance, contrast national results against continental averages, and drill down into specific indicators of rule of law, health, and economic opportunity. This initiative is vital for open data because it democratizes access to high-quality, standardized governance metrics. By providing interactive profiles and detailed comparison pages, the platform enables journalists, researchers, and civil society to easily uncover insights about political and social progress. The transparency of these scores and ranks fosters accountability and supports evidence-based decision-making for citizens and policymakers alike. Ultimately, the portal transforms complex governance data into accessible narratives, empowering stakeholders to monitor public service delivery. Its availability encourages broader engagement with African governance issues, facilitating critical analysis and advocacy. This openness ensures that valuable information regarding public goods and services is not just collected, but actively utilized to drive improvement and informed discourse across the continent.
