Estima BBVA 1.3% de caída en consumo privado; contrario al Inegi

BBVA Research and INEGI present conflicting views on private consumption trends for July. While one group reports a decline due to reduced spending on goods, the other indicates slight monthly growth based on official indicators. This divergence highlights the challenges in accurately measuring economic activity using different methodologies. Understanding these discrepancies is crucial for interpreting current economic health and consumer behavior patterns in the market. This data is relevant to open_data because it illustrates how various public institutions generate, process, and publish statistical indices. Analyzing these diverse datasets allows researchers to better understand measurement methodologies and the reliability of open economic information.
