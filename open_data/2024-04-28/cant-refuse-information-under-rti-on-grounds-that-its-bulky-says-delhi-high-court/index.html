Can’t refuse information under RTI on grounds that it’s ‘bulky’, says Delhi High Court

The High Court ruled that public authorities cannot withhold information under the RTI Act simply because it is bulky, stating that such an excuse improperly adds exemptions beyond those legally permitted. This decision reinforces the principle that transparency mandates the provision of comprehensive data, regardless of volume, ensuring citizens access crucial fiscal records. This judgment is vital for open data as it prevents institutions from obscuring public financial details under the guise of administrative inconvenience. By rejecting bulkiness as a valid refusal reason, the ruling strengthens accountability mechanisms. It ensures that significant datasets, including income and expenditure records, remain accessible and open for public scrutiny and analysis. Ultimately, this precedent safeguards the integrity of information rights by prioritizing transparency over operational ease. It signals that authorities must proactively manage and disclose data rather than using volume as a barrier. This promotes a culture where open data is the default, fostering greater trust and enabling robust civic engagement with government finances.
