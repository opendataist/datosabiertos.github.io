How Tinubu Government Borrowed Over N3.8trillion From Nigerian Central Bank In Six Months – Report

Nigeria’s central bank data reveals substantial government borrowing through ways and means, contradicting official claims that no fresh loans were secured. This discrepancy highlights significant gaps between published fiscal narratives and actual monetary operations, demonstrating how raw financial statistics can expose hidden debt trends. Such transparency is vital for monitoring public sector financial health and ensuring accountability. When official statements diverge from central bank records, it underscores the necessity of independent data verification. Open data initiatives provide the critical evidence needed to challenge opaque governance and validate public trust in economic reporting. Ultimately, accessible and accurate financial datasets empower citizens and analysts to hold governments accountable. By making central bank statistics widely available, societies can detect potential fiscal irregularities. This accessibility fosters a more informed public discourse on economic policy and strengthens democratic oversight mechanisms.
