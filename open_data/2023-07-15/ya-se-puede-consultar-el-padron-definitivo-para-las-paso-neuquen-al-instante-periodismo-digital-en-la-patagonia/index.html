Ya se puede consultar el padrón definitivo para las PASO | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

The definitive electoral registry is now available online, allowing citizens to verify their voting location for the upcoming PASO primaries. This access ensures transparency and facilitates civic participation by enabling voters to efficiently confirm their assigned polling places through official digital channels. The Central Electoral Chamber has clarified the valid identification requirements, stipulating that physical documents must match the records in the registry. Electronic copies stored on mobile phones are not accepted, reinforcing the need for precise, up-to-date physical identification during the voting process. This update is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the public utility of accessible government registries. By providing clear, searchable data on voting logistics, the system promotes digital inclusion and trust in democratic processes through transparent information sharing.
