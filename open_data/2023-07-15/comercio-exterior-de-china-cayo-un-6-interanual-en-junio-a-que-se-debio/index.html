Comercio exterior de China cayó un 6% interanual en junio: ¿A qué se debió?

China’s foreign trade experienced a significant contraction in June 2023, marking a notable slowdown in both exports and imports. This year-on-year decline reflects weak global demand and the normalization of post-pandemic consumption patterns, suggesting that external sales could continue to decline through the end of the year. Despite this negative monthly trend, the cumulative figures for the first half of the year showed slight growth, highlighting the sector’s resilience in terms of total trade volume. The divergence between figures reported in yuan and U.S. dollars illustrates the complexity of international economic analysis, where exchange rate fluctuations affect the perception of trade trends. The data indicate that developed economies face recession risks, although the impact on Chinese exports is expected to be limited due to the boom in green technologies, such as electric vehicles and solar panels, which act as growth drivers despite the adverse context. This report is relevant to the open data community because it underscores the importance of accessing official, transparent, and up-to-date datasets from global customs institutions. The availability of disaggregated data in multiple currencies enables analysts and citizens to conduct independent audits, verify discrepancies reported by the press, and better understand global trade dynamics, fostering more informed, evidence-based decision-making.
