INE entregará datos oficiales del censo en septiembre de 2024

INE entregará datos oficiales del censo en septiembre de 2024

The national census will release official results in September to guide the distribution of resources and political representation, while a comprehensive, disaggregated database will be available by December. This data provides critical insights into demographic and socioeconomic trends, enabling the government to assess progress toward development goals and refine public policies. This update is crucial for open data, as it establishes an accessible, detailed repository of citizen and housing information. By making these statistics available, the state enhances transparency and supports evidence-based decision-making. Ultimately, this initiative empowers researchers and civil society to analyze inequality and monitor the sustainability of national development efforts.
