La inflación argentina fue del 6% en junio y suma un 50,7% en 2023

Argentina’s soaring inflation underscores the critical need for timely and accurate official statistics to maintain public trust during sensitive electoral periods. The timing of data releases directly intersects with key democratic processes, highlighting how transparent information flows underpin political accountability and informed voter decision-making. Moreover, the detailed breakdown of price increases across essential sectors reveals deep economic pressures, particularly in communications and health. This granular insight demonstrates the value of open data in exposing specific societal vulnerabilities, enabling citizens and policymakers to better understand the tangible impacts of macroeconomic trends on daily life. Ultimately, this case illustrates that open data is not merely a technical exercise but a fundamental democratic tool. By providing accessible, high-quality metrics, governments can foster greater civic engagement and ensure that economic realities are visible, thereby strengthening the integrity of both statistical institutions and the broader democratic framework.
