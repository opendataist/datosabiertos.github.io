25% of students who sat for Maltese and Maths O-level given ‘U’ grade - The Malta Independent

Recent exam statistics reveal a significant decline in academic performance across Maltese secondary education, with nearly a quarter of students receiving unclassified grades. This upward trend in failure rates contrasts sharply with only two subjects, French and Biology, which showed improvement, indicating a widespread systemic challenge in student achievement for the current session. The data highlights Environmental Studies as the most difficult subject, where unclassified rates surged substantially compared to the previous year. While absenteeism remains a persistent issue in certain disciplines like Accounting, it decreased in several others, suggesting that performance declines are not solely driven by student absence but by deeper educational or curricular difficulties. These findings are critical for open data advocates as they demonstrate how transparent publication of educational metrics enables accountability and deeper societal analysis. By making granular performance data publicly available, stakeholders can identify specific areas of concern, track trends over time, and drive informed policy interventions to support student success and institutional reform.
