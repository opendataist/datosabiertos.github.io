Incrementó 2.6% la población en cárceles de México durante 2022

The article reveals an increase in Mexico’s prison population, with a significant portion of inmates awaiting a final verdict. This highlights systemic delays and a heavy reliance on preventive detention, which disproportionately affects women. The data underscores a justice system burdened by prolonged pre-trial incarceration, undermining the presumption of innocence for many detainees. These findings are crucial for open data initiatives, as they expose gaps in transparency and accountability within the penal system. By making such statistics accessible, researchers and activists can advocate for policy reforms aimed at reducing prison overcrowding and ensuring timely judicial processes. Ultimately, the report serves as a critical resource for understanding the scale of judicial inefficiency. Open access to these figures empowers civil society to monitor compliance with human rights standards and push for structural changes that prioritize due process over mere detention metrics.
