Threads pierde la mitad de sus usuarios activos diarios en una semana - Ensegundos.do

Threads saw a sharp decline in daily active users and engagement time shortly after its launch, indicating that the initial hype did not sustain long-term usage. This rapid drop highlights the challenge of converting early adopters into regular users—a critical lesson for open data analysis concerning user retention metrics. The significant reduction in usage time suggests that the novelty of social networks wears off quickly. This trend is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of tracking longitudinal engagement rather than relying solely on registration numbers, thereby providing insights into the actual stickiness of digital platforms. Furthermore, the platform’s integration with Instagram profiles raises data privacy concerns. Users can only delete their Threads account by removing their Instagram profile, which complicates independent data management. This dependency affects the portability and control of personal data, emphasizing the need for transparent and flexible data governance within interconnected social ecosystems.
