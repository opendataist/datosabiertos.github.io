¿Cuáles son los municipios más grandes de la provincia de Sevilla?

The article highlights the significant demographic weight of the municipalities surrounding Seville, identifying the ten most populous towns in the province outside the capital. It notes that Seville is the most populated province in Andalusia, with several municipalities reaching substantial population thresholds. This data underscores a shift in urban structure, where secondary cities are expanding rapidly and becoming major urban centers in their own right, supported by improved infrastructure such as metro connections. These towns are not merely residential hubs; they possess distinct historical, cultural, and economic identities. From Dos Hermanas and Alcalá de Guadaíra, which have achieved "Municipality of Great Population" status, to Utrera’s flamenco heritage or Écija’s architectural legacy, each location contributes uniquely to the region’s profile. The growth of these areas reflects a broader regional development pattern in which proximity to the capital facilitates integration while maintaining local traditions and industrial strengths. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the importance of accessible, granular demographic statistics in understanding regional dynamics. Publicly available census data, such as that from the National Statistics Institute (INE), enables analysts to track urban sprawl, resource allocation needs, and cultural tourism potential. By providing clear, comparable figures for each municipality, open data supports evidence-based urban planning, policy-making, and a deeper understanding of how population trends shape local identities and infrastructure demands across the province.
