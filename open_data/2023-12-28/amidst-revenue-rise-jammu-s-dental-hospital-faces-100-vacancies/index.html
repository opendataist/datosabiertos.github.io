Amidst Revenue Rise, Jammu's Dental Hospital Faces 100 Vacancies

Government Dental College and Hospital in Jammu has generated substantial revenue over four years, yet it struggles with nearly half its medical and paramedical positions remaining vacant. Despite this staffing crisis, the institution handles a significant and growing volume of patient registrations, indicating high service demand despite resource gaps. This situation highlights critical challenges in public healthcare resource allocation and transparency. The disparity between financial gains and personnel shortages suggests systemic inefficiencies that hinder optimal patient care and institutional effectiveness. Such data is vital for open_data initiatives, as it enables public scrutiny of government spending versus service delivery. Transparent disclosure allows citizens to hold institutions accountable, ensuring that revenue generation translates into adequate staffing and improved healthcare accessibility for the community.
