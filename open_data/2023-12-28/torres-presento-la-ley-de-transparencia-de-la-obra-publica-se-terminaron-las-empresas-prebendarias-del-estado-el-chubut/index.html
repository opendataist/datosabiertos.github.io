Torres presentó la Ley de Transparencia de la Obra Pública: «Se terminaron las empresas prebendarias del Estado» - El Chubut

The Governor of Chubut has introduced a Public Works Transparency Bill, with the primary objective of guaranteeing citizens real-time access to all bidding and execution processes. This initiative aims to eliminate the opacity that facilitated the awarding of contracts without genuine competition, ensuring that bidding documents are freely available and that there is complete traceability of every stage of public works, enabling society to directly monitor how public resources are used. In parallel, the administration is announcing legal and administrative actions against companies that received financial advances without commencing the works, recovering the funds through surety bonds and contract terminations. Priority will be given to completing abandoned educational projects, and rigorous audits—both national and private—will be conducted on the irregularities detected, promoting accountability before the justice system and the citizenry. This case is significant for open data, as it exemplifies the transition of public management toward proactive transparency. By making bidding and public works execution data publicly available, it enables citizen participation and external scrutiny, demonstrating how the availability of clear and accessible information is essential to combat corruption and improve the efficiency of public resource use.
