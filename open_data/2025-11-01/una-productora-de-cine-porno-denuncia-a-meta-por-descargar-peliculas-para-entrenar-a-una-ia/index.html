Una productora de cine porno denuncia a Meta por descargar películas para entrenar a una IA

Meta is facing a lawsuit alleging copyright infringement for training an adult content AI, a claim the company firmly rejects as baseless. Meta argues that its terms of use explicitly prohibit such generation, suggesting that any downloads were for individual private use rather than institutional purposes. The relevance to open data lies in the complex intersection of privacy, intellectual property, and transparency in AI development. This case highlights the difficulty in distinguishing between legitimate business data collection activities and unauthorized individual behavior on corporate networks, raising questions about the attribution of responsibility. The incident illustrates legal vulnerabilities in assuming that corporate network traffic directly reflects the intentions or actions of the contracting entity. It underscores the need for clarity in how datasets used for model training are managed and verified, preventing technological infrastructure from being used as an excuse for lawsuits based on speculation about misuse by third parties.
