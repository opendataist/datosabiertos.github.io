Casi 40.000 carpetas de investigación se abrieron contra menores de edad en México en 2023

A new INEGI report reveals that Mexican authorities opened nearly 40,000 investigations against minors in 2023, highlighting a concerning shift toward serious organized crime rather than petty survival offenses. Data shows a stark gender disparity, with nine out of ten detained adolescents being male, and incidents concentrated heavily in a few key states. The most common charges involve violence and robbery, signaling deeper systemic issues in juvenile rehabilitation and social reintegration. This article is vital for open data because it demonstrates how publicly accessible official statistics expose critical social vulnerabilities. It allows researchers and advocates to track trends, challenge narratives, and hold institutions accountable for youth justice outcomes, proving the value of transparent, granular government data for public welfare.
