RTI exposes Rs 5 cr procurement during MCC in Ambikapur schools

RTI exposes Rs 5 cr procurement during MCC in Ambikapur schools

A Right to Information investigation has exposed serious procurement irregularities within the District Education Office in Ambikapur, revealing massive payments made during a critical election ban. Despite the Model Code of Conduct prohibiting new expenditures, crores of rupees were allocated for furniture, bicycles, and lab equipment in a single day. This blatant disregard for electoral regulations suggests a coordinated effort to bypass transparency laws during a sensitive political period. The most concerning implication is the total absence of these goods at their intended locations. While administrative records show approval for significant hardware and educational resources, school inspections confirm that none of the items were delivered. This discrepancy between sanctioned funds and actual stock raises alarming questions about potential fraud, inflated billing, and the misuse of public funds for purposes unrelated to educational infrastructure. This case is vital to the open data movement because it demonstrates how accessible public records can uncover corruption when traditional oversight fails. It highlights the power of data transparency in validating official claims against ground realities. By exposing the gap between financial records and physical inventory, the incident underscores the necessity of robust, verifiable open data systems to ensure accountability and prevent the siphoning of government resources.
