Cali lanza su plataforma Distrito Cultural en Digital

Cali lanza su plataforma Distrito Cultural en Digital

The City of Cali launched "Cali Distrito Cultural en Digital," a centralized platform that integrates cultural agendas, artistic training, and heritage information to enhance citizen access. By employing GIS technology for interactive mapping, the tool enables territorial analysis of cultural supply, thereby strengthening the link between public policies and local communities. A crucial aspect of this initiative is its commitment to transparency through open data. The portal allows users to track public investment and program beneficiaries, fostering trust by enabling public oversight of resource execution. This approach ensures accountability while empowering citizens with accessible information on how cultural funds are utilized across different communes. This platform is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publishing transparent, geospatial, and programmatic datasets can bridge the gap between institutional actions and community needs. It illustrates the practical application of open government principles to support cultural participation, ensuring that public information drives engagement and verifies the effective impact of cultural policies on urban and rural populations.
