La Nación / Urgen al Ejecutivo reglamentar la ley de control y transparencia para oenegés

La Nación / Urgen al Ejecutivo reglamentar la ley de control y transparencia para oenegés

The article highlights a critical push by Paraguayan legislators to enforce a new transparency law for non-profit organizations, mandating strict accountability protocols for managing public and private resources. This regulatory push stems from severe findings that vast sums of international and government funding were mismanaged, with directors absorbing excessive salaries while providing little demonstrable impact. Implementing these regulations is vital for open data initiatives because it establishes the legal framework necessary for generating verifiable financial and operational data. Without this decree, the accountability mechanisms required to track how funds are utilized remain incomplete, leaving a significant gap in the transparency of civil society actors. This case underscores the direct link between legislative enforcement and data accessibility. By compelling NGOs to submit detailed reports, the law transforms opaque financial flows into structured, usable information. Ultimately, ensuring full execution of this transparency mandate is essential for creating an open data ecosystem that allows citizens to monitor resource allocation and combat financial misconduct effectively.
