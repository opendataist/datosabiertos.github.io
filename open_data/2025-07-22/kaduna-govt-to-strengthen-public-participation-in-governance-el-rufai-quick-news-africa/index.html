Kaduna Gov’t to strengthen public participation in governance – El-Rufai - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

Kaduna State is reinforcing its commitment to open governance by prioritizing civic engagement and fiscal transparency through its 2021 to 2023 Action Plan. This strategy builds on previous successes, such as ranking first nationally for ease of doing business, and aims to deepen public participation in decision-making processes. By focusing on open budgeting and contracting, the administration seeks to enhance accountability and ensure that service delivery in health and education sectors directly benefits vulnerable populations. This approach highlights the vital role of co-creation between government entities and civil society organizations. The successful implementation of prior initiatives, including digital tools for citizen oversight, demonstrates that collaborative governance can significantly improve institutional efficiency. The state’s ongoing efforts to operationalize open data standards serve as a model for how local governments can leverage transparency to build resilient, citizen-centered democracies and accelerate economic recovery. The article is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how structured transparency frameworks drive tangible public administration reforms. It underscores that open data is not merely a technical exercise but a fundamental mechanism for fostering trust and enabling meaningful public participation. By mandating ministries to prioritize these commitments, Kaduna State exemplifies how open data principles can be integrated into broader governance strategies to promote social protection and inclusive development, offering valuable lessons for other jurisdictions seeking to enhance their own open government practices.
