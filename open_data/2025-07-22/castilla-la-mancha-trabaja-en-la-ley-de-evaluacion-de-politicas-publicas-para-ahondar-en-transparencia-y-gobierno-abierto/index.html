Castilla-La Mancha trabaja en la Ley de Evaluación de Políticas Públicas para ahondar en transparencia y gobierno abierto

Castilla-La Mancha trabaja en la Ley de Evaluación de Políticas Públicas para ahondar en transparencia y gobierno abierto

Castilla-La Mancha is strengthening its commitment to transparent governance by renewing its Chair of Open Government in collaboration with the University of Castilla-La Mancha. This strategic agreement not only ensures the continuity of years of participatory work but also serves as a foundation for developing the future Open Government Strategy 2030. The initiative aims to institutionalize mechanisms that enable citizens to accurately understand the outcomes of public policies, ensuring that the administration provides effective and clear accountability. The region is positioning itself as a leader in transparency and participation legislation, integrating new tools such as an AI-based Legislative Code and a national research prize. These efforts reflect a constant evolution in regulatory frameworks, ranging from transparency laws to integrity laws, always prioritizing dialogue with society. The goal is to create advanced legal frameworks that facilitate the implementation of participatory processes in local entities, ensuring that public management strictly aligns with the common interest and the continuous improvement of administrative services. This development is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it marks the formal launch of the Data Governance Chair, driven by the recently established Data Office. By systematizing the use of data to create a more agile administration, the autonomous community establishes a practical model of how transparency and citizen participation are realized through access to and analysis of public information. This demonstrates that open data are not merely technical resources, but fundamental pillars for democratic, efficient, and responsible governance that empowers citizens through direct knowledge of government actions.
