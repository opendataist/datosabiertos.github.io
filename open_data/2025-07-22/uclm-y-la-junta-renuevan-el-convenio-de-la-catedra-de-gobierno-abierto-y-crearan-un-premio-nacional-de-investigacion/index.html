UCLM y la Junta renuevan el convenio de la Cátedra de Gobierno Abierto y crearán un premio nacional de investigación

UCLM y la Junta renuevan el convenio de la Cátedra de Gobierno Abierto y crearán un premio nacional de investigación

The University of Castilla-La Mancha and the regional government have renewed their partnership, emphasizing a successful collaborative approach rather than a traditional top-down directive. This synergy aims to expand open government initiatives, highlighted by the launch of a national research award to increase visibility and encourage academic contribution to public transparency. The extension validates the efficiency of their cooperation and reinforces the university’s growing role as a key partner for both public institutions and private entities in the region. Looking ahead, the administration plans to develop a strategic roadmap for open government by 2030, ensuring more dynamic and agile public services. A significant development is the potential creation of a new chair dedicated to data governance. This focus reflects a strategic commitment to leveraging data and artificial intelligence to modernize administrative processes. This agreement is highly relevant to open data as it institutionalizes the practical application of data-driven governance. By prioritizing data usage alongside open government principles, the initiative demonstrates how higher education and public administration can jointly foster transparency, efficiency, and citizen-centric services through the intelligent management of information.
