Política de privacidad

Política de privacidad

This document is a privacy policy from Hipertextual S.L., outlining how personal data is collected and processed. The core conclusion is that while users must provide mandatory data to access services, the platform is legally bound to ensure data security, transparency, and respect for user rights, particularly for those under stricter protection standards like the GDPR. The article highlights that data collection serves essential functions, including service delivery, analytics, and advertising, using tools like cookies. It emphasizes that data may be transferred internationally, potentially outside the European Union, subject to specific legal bases and safety measures. Users retain control through rights to access, rectify, delete, or port their data, ensuring they can challenge the processing of their information at any time. This is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical tension between operational data utilization and privacy regulations. It underscores that "open" or public-facing platforms must rigorously define the boundaries of data collection, consent, and retention. Understanding such policies is vital for developers and analysts who need to know what data is legally permissible to collect, process, or share, ensuring that any data-driven initiatives comply with user protection laws.
