Inflación de enero llega al 1,95% impulsada por los alimentos

Inflación de enero llega al 1,95% impulsada por los alimentos

Bolivia experienced its highest inflation rate in years in January 2025, driven by sharp price increases in basic necessities such as meat, transportation, and food. This surge significantly erodes citizens' purchasing power, reflecting broader economic instability caused by supply chain disruptions and speculation. The data highlight how external factors, such as logistical blockades and infrastructure damage, directly exacerbate cost-of-living crises. These insights underscore the critical need for transparent, real-time statistical monitoring to understand the specific drivers behind economic volatility and guide effective policy responses. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of accessible, high-quality public statistics for accountability. Transparent data enables society to trace the root causes of inflation, fostering informed public debate and ensuring that government interventions are based on accurate, verifiable evidence rather than speculation.
