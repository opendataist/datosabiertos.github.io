Remulla shows full support for OGP, encourages LGUs to follow suit

The Philippine government is aggressively expanding the Open Government Partnership to local levels, prioritizing institutional transparency and accountability across all administrative tiers. This initiative reflects a strategic shift toward inclusive governance, ensuring that anti-corruption and citizen-engagement standards are not just central policies but local realities. By fostering collaborations with universities and civil society, the administration leverages external expertise and community talents to enhance public service efficiency. This approach demonstrates that open data and collaborative governance are vital for modernizing institutions like the police, moving beyond traditional bureaucratic silos to create responsive, evidence-based systems. This development is critical for open data because it establishes a cultural and structural framework where data sharing is a default government practice rather than an exception. As local units adopt these principles, the volume and quality of accessible public data will increase, empowering citizens and researchers to hold officials accountable through verified, transparent information flows.
