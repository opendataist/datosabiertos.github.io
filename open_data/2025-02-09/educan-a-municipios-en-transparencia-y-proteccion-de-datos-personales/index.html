Educan a municipios en transparencia y protección de datos personales

Municipal authorities in San Luis Potosí received training to enhance transparency and protect personal data. This initiative supports the governor’s mandate for greater openness and ensures that officials understand their legal obligations regarding public information. Such education is crucial for building a just society and improving public services. By strengthening the practice of open information, local governments can act more responsibly and provide better service to citizens across all regions. This story is relevant to open data because it highlights the foundational need for institutional capacity building. Effective data governance requires trained personnel who understand both access rights and privacy laws, ensuring that open data ecosystems are secure, compliant, and truly beneficial to the public.
