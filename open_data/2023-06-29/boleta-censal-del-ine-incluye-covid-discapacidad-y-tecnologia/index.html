Boleta censal del INE incluye Covid, discapacidad y tecnología

Boleta censal del INE incluye Covid, discapacidad y tecnología

The recent revision of Bolivia’s census questionnaire reflects significant efforts to modernize data collection, particularly by incorporating public suggestions from the Open Institute. The updated form now includes questions on the impacts of the pandemic and mental health, while notably shifting the terminology for ethnic identity from “belonging” to “self-identification” to better align with constitutional standards. Despite incorporating proposals from universities and indigenous groups, certain sensitive topics, such as counts of religious organizations and gender identity, were excluded. This selective inclusion highlights the complexities of balancing comprehensive data needs with administrative feasibility and existing legislative timelines, illustrating the iterative nature of policy design. This process is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how citizen participation shapes public information systems. The emphasis on accessibility and clarity ensures that collected data is not only technologically current but also understandable to the general public, fostering greater transparency and trust in statistical institutions.
