OVCS: Venezuela registró un promedio de 26 protestas diarias en cinco meses, la mayoría por exigencias laborales

Venezuela faces intense social unrest driven primarily by urgent demands for basic economic, social, and cultural rights. This widespread conflict highlights a severe crisis in which citizens struggle to afford essentials due to negligible state-provided wages and pensions. The protests underscore the failure of public services, including electricity and water, further exacerbating civilian hardship. These peaceful demonstrations reveal deep systemic gaps in the government’s ability to ensure fundamental living standards across all regions. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent tracking of social conflict can expose governance failures. Such visibility empowers civil society to hold authorities accountable and advocates for policies rooted in verifiable human rights needs rather than political rhetoric.
