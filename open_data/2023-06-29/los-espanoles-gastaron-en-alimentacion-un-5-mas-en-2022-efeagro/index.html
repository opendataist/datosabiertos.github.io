Los españoles gastaron en alimentación un 5 % más en 2022 - EFEAgro

Los españoles gastaron en alimentación un 5 % más en 2022 - EFEAgro

Spanish households increased their nominal spending on food, reflecting broader economic pressures. This shift highlights how inflation affects essential consumption, driving up food costs while displacing other expenditure categories such as alcohol and tobacco. When adjusted for inflation, real food consumption actually declined slightly compared to the previous year. This indicates that households are absorbing price increases rather than purchasing greater quantities, revealing the true strain on family budgets amid rising living costs. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need to provide inflation-adjusted metrics. Transparent and accessible economic statistics empower citizens and analysts to distinguish between nominal price changes and real purchasing power, fostering a better understanding of household financial health.
