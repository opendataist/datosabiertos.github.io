Seguro empresarial: Una herramienta clave para fortalecer la resiliencia de las pymes

Micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) are the fundamental pillar of the economy, constituting the vast majority of commercial establishments. However, their high closure rate relative to the birth of new businesses reveals a profound vulnerability. The lack of adequate financial protection exposes these entities to unforeseen risks that can compromise their stability and lead to bankruptcy, highlighting the urgency of implementing mechanisms for business resilience. The adoption of specific business insurance emerges as a crucial strategic tool to mitigate economic losses and provide certainty to entrepreneurs. These modular and flexible policies offer comprehensive coverage and rapid responses to claims, enabling businesses to recover costs and ensure the continuity of their operations. By protecting themselves against threats such as natural disasters or theft, MSMEs can maintain their activities despite challenges in the economic environment. This topic is relevant to the field of open data because transparency in formalization and business mortality rates is essential for evaluating the effectiveness of public policies. Analyzing data on insurance coverage and closure rates allows for the identification of protection gaps and guides the development of better support instruments. Facilitating access to this statistical information promotes informed decisions that strengthen the sustainability of the national business ecosystem.
