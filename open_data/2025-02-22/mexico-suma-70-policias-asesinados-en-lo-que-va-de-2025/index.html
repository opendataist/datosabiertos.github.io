México suma 70 policías asesinados en lo que va de 2025

This article highlights the severe violence against Mexican police, revealing a high frequency of assassinations across various states during 2025. The data underscores the extreme vulnerability of municipal forces compared to state and federal agencies, indicating a crisis in local security infrastructure. Relevant to open data, this case demonstrates the critical role of civil society organizations in collecting, verifying, and publishing granular public safety statistics. It shows how independent data publication can fill gaps in official reporting, providing transparency and enabling public scrutiny of government performance regarding citizen safety. Ultimately, the report argues that accessible, detailed data is essential for identifying patterns of violence and holding authorities accountable. By making these specific figures public, the organization facilitates informed debate on security policies and resource allocation, emphasizing the democratic value of open, verified information in addressing systemic risks.
