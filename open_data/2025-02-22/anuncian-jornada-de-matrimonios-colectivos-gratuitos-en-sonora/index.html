Anuncian jornada de matrimonios colectivos gratuitos en Sonora

Sonora organizes free collective weddings to promote the benefits of legal marriage through simplified procedures. This initiative encourages compliance with civil union regulations while ensuring that participants receive education on family violence and meet specific documentary requirements. Relevant to open data, this public administration action underscores the need for transparent and accessible civic service information. By centralizing requirements and schedules, the government demonstrates how clear data publication facilitates citizen participation in essential legal processes.
