RAGA convoca la segunda edición de los premios AUDAZ a la transparencia local

RAGA convoca la segunda edición de los premios AUDAZ a la transparencia local

The second edition of the AUDAZ Distinctions aims to recognize best practices in transparency among Spanish local governments, shifting the focus from autonomous communities to municipalities and provincial councils. This initiative highlights a strategic move toward the local level, acknowledging that smaller entities often lack resources yet play a crucial role in maintaining democratic integrity. By differentiating categories based on entity size, the awards ensure fair competition and encourage genuine efforts to improve communication with citizens, even in underserved areas. Relevance to open data is fundamental, as the competition specifically evaluates the quality and reusability of published information. It rewards innovations in structural clarity, universal accessibility, and the non-mandatory publication of data, pushing public administrations beyond mere legal compliance. The focus on how information is structured and made understandable directly impacts the utility of open data, transforming raw publications into actionable resources that foster trust and engagement. Ultimately, this recognition drives the open data ecosystem forward by incentivizing high-quality information management over simple data dumps. By valuing social impact, innovation, and collaborative efforts, the awards promote a culture where transparency serves as a dynamic tool for governance. This approach ensures that open data initiatives are not just technical outputs but meaningful contributions to a more accountable and participatory democracy, particularly for local entities striving to bridge the gap with their citizens.
