Trabaja Ayuntamiento por un gobierno abierto y transparente: Alberto Islas - Plumas Libres

Trabaja Ayuntamiento por un gobierno abierto y transparente: Alberto Islas - Plumas Libres

The Xalapa municipal government launched transparency training to ensure strict compliance with access to information laws and protect personal data. This initiative aims to equip officials with the necessary tools for accountability, supporting a smooth administrative transition while fostering a culture of openness. This event highlights the critical role of institutional cooperation between local authorities and information institutes in maintaining democratic integrity. By prioritizing these practices, municipalities can better serve their citizens through verifiable governance and consistent adherence to legal standards. The relevance to open data lies in its foundation: true transparency requires not just publication, but structured, accurate, and accessible information systems. Strengthening official capacity to manage data responsibly ensures that open data initiatives are meaningful, trustworthy, and effectively utilized for public scrutiny and civic engagement.
