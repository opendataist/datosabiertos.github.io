Uno de cada tres mayores se siente ignorado por su edad

Uno de cada tres mayores se siente ignorado por su edad

The study reveals significant ageism, highlighting that older adults frequently face digital exclusion and bureaucratic impatience due to limited administrative support. This marginalization contributes to their heightened social isolation and economic vulnerability, contrasting sharply with the perception that youth face greater challenges, despite evidence suggesting otherwise. The relevance to open data lies in how such surveys expose systemic biases embedded in institutional data collection and service design. By anonymizing and analyzing these responses, researchers can identify gaps in public services and advocate for inclusive digital policies that protect vulnerable demographics from technological alienation. Ultimately, the findings urge policymakers to recognize that economic precarity affects all ages, albeit differently. Open data initiatives must ensure that statistical transparency helps dismantle intergenerational stereotypes, promoting equitable resource distribution and fostering a society where age does not dictate one’s right to dignified access and participation.
