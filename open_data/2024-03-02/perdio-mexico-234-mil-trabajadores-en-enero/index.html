Perdió México 234 mil trabajadores en enero

Mexico’s January employment data shows an increase in unemployment and a decline in formal jobs, with the informal sector growing to account for more than half of the workforce. The narrative underscores shifting labor dynamics, as the service sector added jobs while manufacturing and government significantly reduced their positions. This dataset is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, granular evidence of economic health and vulnerabilities in the labor market.
