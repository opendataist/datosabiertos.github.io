Las pernoctaciones en alojamientos rurales y apartamentos turísticos caen un 24,5 % y un 30,7 % en Baleares en enero

This article highlights tourism data, revealing shifts in guest numbers between rural and apartment accommodations. These statistics are relevant to open data as they illustrate how public sector information tracks economic trends and visitor behavior, enabling transparency and informed decision-making for stakeholders. Ultimately, the data underscores increasing international interest. This trend helps policymakers understand market dynamics and allocate resources effectively, demonstrating the practical value of accessible, granular tourism datasets.
