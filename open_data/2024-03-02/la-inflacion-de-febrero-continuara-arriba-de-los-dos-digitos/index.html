La inflación de febrero continuará arriba de los dos dígitos

Private estimates suggest that February inflation will reach 15%, contradicting government projections. This highlights significant discrepancies in the data, underscoring the critical need for transparent and independent statistics. Reliable open data sources are essential to verify official figures. Without them, public trust erodes as economic policies rely on potentially inaccurate information. Access to raw, unfiltered data allows citizens and experts to cross-check claims. This transparency ensures accountability and supports informed decision-making in volatile economic environments.
