The year 2022 marked a positive turning point for the province of Burgos, interrupting three consecutive years of business losses. After the sharp contraction triggered by the pandemic, the local economy managed to stabilize and grow slightly, offering a “breath of fresh air” amid a complex socioeconomic context. This modest upturn contrasts with initial pessimistic outlooks and suggests a timid but tangible recovery following the health crisis and recent geopolitical impacts. Growth was concentrated specifically in sectors such as healthcare, education, personal services, and construction, while industry and commerce experienced declines. This sectoral divergence reveals a reconfiguration of the province’s economic activity, with care services and real estate management acting as the main drivers. Although inflation and the energy crisis posed new challenges, the resilience of Burgos’s business fabric helped contain the negative trend, keeping business creation above business closures during the last analyzed period. This report is relevant to the field of open data by demonstrating how official statistics, such as the INE’s Central Business Directory, serve as critical tools for diagnosing economic health and guiding public policy. By providing data disaggregated by sector and territory, they enable researchers and policymakers to identify specific vulnerabilities and opportunities. The ability to compare regional and national trends facilitates robust comparative analysis, which is essential for understanding economic evolution and making informed, evidence-based, and transparent decisions.
