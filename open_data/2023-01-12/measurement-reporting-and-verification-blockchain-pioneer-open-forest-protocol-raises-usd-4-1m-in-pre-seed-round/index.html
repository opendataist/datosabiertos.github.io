Measurement, reporting and verification blockchain pioneer Open Forest Protocol raises USD 4.1m in pre-seed round

Open Forest Protocol introduces a decentralized, blockchain-based platform that democratizes access to global voluntary carbon markets for nature-based solutions. By leveraging a carbon-neutral infrastructure, the system ensures that data regarding forest measurements, reporting, and verification is both truthful and transparent. This technological shift addresses historical barriers that have previously excluded smaller entities, such as private landholders and indigenous groups, from participating in carbon trading due to high costs and opaque processes. The platform establishes a global network of expert validators who collectively verify data submitted by forest projects, ensuring accuracy without requiring expensive third-party auditors upfront. This peer-to-peer verification model allows participants of any size to record their forestation data via mobile applications, which is then immutably stored and stress-tested by a diverse coalition of environmental consultants and validation bodies. Consequently, the system fosters equitable access and trust, enabling a wider range of stakeholders to benefit from carbon credits generated through natural regeneration and afforestation efforts. This development is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how open protocols and immutable ledgers can solve critical trust and accessibility issues in environmental governance. By making measurement data open, verifiable, and permissionless, OFP creates a scalable model for public oversight of ecological impacts. It highlights the potential for open data frameworks to empower marginalized communities and enhance biodiversity conservation while simultaneously supporting climate resilience through transparent, community-driven value creation.
