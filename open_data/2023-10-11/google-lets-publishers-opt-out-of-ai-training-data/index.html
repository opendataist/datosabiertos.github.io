Google Lets Publishers Opt Out of AI Training Data

Google’s new opt-out tool for AI training sparks ethical concerns among industry experts who view the practice as deceptive. This transparency issue highlights the critical need for clear data usage standards in the open data ecosystem. Publishers must demand explicit consent to protect their intellectual property from uncredited model training.
