South Africa's population swells to 62-million

South Africa’s 2022 digital census reveals significant demographic shifts and infrastructural progress, offering a comprehensive view of a growing and diversifying nation. The data highlights substantial population growth, changes in racial composition, and evolving patterns of migration and language usage. Crucially, it documents a marked decline in informal housing and improved access to basic utilities like electricity and the internet, signaling socioeconomic advancements over the previous decade. The transition to fully digital data collection marks a pivotal moment in public administration, demonstrating the viability of modern, multi-channel census methodologies. By utilizing handheld devices, telephone interviews, and online questionnaires, Statistics South Africa successfully processed millions of records, enhancing both the efficiency and accuracy of national statistics. This technological leap establishes a new standard for future demographic tracking and administrative efficiency. This article is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it showcases the critical role of digitized, accessible government data in understanding societal trends. The comprehensive release of granular data on housing, services, and demographics allows researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze inequality and development outcomes effectively. It underscores how transparent, high-quality open data can drive informed decision-making and hold institutions accountable for service delivery and social progress.
