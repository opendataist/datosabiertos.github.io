Banco de España revisa dos décimas su previsión de crecimiento para 2023, hasta el 2,5%

Banco de España revisa dos décimas su previsión de crecimiento para 2023, hasta el 2,5%

The Bank of Spain revised its economic growth forecast upward, reflecting improved historical data despite short-term deceleration signals. This slowdown stems from monetary policy tensions, a weak external context, and the phase-out of energy crisis measures. Looking ahead, economic activity is expected to regain vigor in 2024. This recovery is supported by falling inflation, restored consumer confidence, a resilient labor market, and the intensified deployment of European funds. This data is relevant to open_data as it exemplifies the critical importance of transparent, timely, and accessible official statistics. Accurate public datasets enable independent analysis, foster trust in economic indicators, and allow diverse stakeholders to verify and interpret national economic trends effectively.
