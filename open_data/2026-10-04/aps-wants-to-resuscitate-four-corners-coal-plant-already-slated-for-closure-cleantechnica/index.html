APS Wants to Resuscitate Four Corners Coal Plant Already Slated for Closure - CleanTechnica

APS Wants to Resuscitate Four Corners Coal Plant Already Slated for Closure - CleanTechnica

The article details Arizona Public Service’s decision to extend coal operations, contradicting its previous retirement commitments and exacerbating public health and environmental harms. By prolonging the life of the Four Corners Plant, the utility increases air pollution and water usage while likely raising costs for ratepayers. This move undermines the urgent need for a just energy transition, as local communities, particularly on the Navajo Nation, continue to suffer from decades of toxic exposure and climate-related extreme weather. Critics argue that retaining aging fossil fuel infrastructure serves corporate interests rather than consumer welfare. Despite independent studies highlighting the lethal impact of fossil-fuel pollution and reports showing significant savings available through early retirement, the utility prioritizes coal and gas expansions. This strategy conflicts with stated carbon neutrality goals, revealing a disconnect between public promises and operational realities. The continued reliance on dirty energy sources perpetuates a volatile commitment that harms public health and accelerates climate crises, affecting millions globally and locally. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible energy information to hold utilities accountable. When detailed data on contracts, pollution metrics, and cost-benefit analyses are publicly available, communities can challenge misleading narratives and advocate for evidence-based policy. Open access to such information empowers stakeholders to demand equitable transitions, ensuring that decision-making is driven by verifiable facts rather than opaque corporate strategies, ultimately fostering greater environmental justice and resource efficiency.
