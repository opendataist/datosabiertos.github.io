INE abre nuevas oportunidades laborales en Santa Cruz para proyectos estadísticos este 2026

INE abre nuevas oportunidades laborales en Santa Cruz para proyectos estadísticos este 2026

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) of Bolivia has opened job vacancies in Santa Cruz for the Household Survey Project and the 2026 Agricultural Census. These initiatives aim to recruit qualified personnel to strengthen the department’s statistical operations, ensuring the collection of crucial information for public planning. The transparency in these hiring processes reflects the principles of access to information and citizen participation. By facilitating registration through digital platforms and clearly disseminating the requirements, the INE promotes inclusion and accountability in the management of public data. This announcement is relevant to open data because the quality and coverage of future censuses depend on robust field teams. Greater local participation improves statistical accuracy, generating more reliable and accessible datasets that support public policy analysis and evidence-based innovation.
