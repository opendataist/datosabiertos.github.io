El paro sube en Cantabria en septiembre, pero marca su mejor dato desde 2008

Cantabria’s labor market exhibited a mixed dynamic in September, marked by a slight rise in unemployment following two consecutive months of increases. However, this trend should not cause alarm, as the total number of unemployed individuals represents the lowest level recorded for this month in over a decade, remaining below the national average and maintaining overall year-on-year stability across the region. The distribution of unemployment revealed significant sectoral and demographic disparities, with the services sector accounting for the majority of new layoffs and a notable increase among young people and foreign workers. These inequalities highlight vulnerabilities in certain segments of the labor market, even as other areas such as industry and agriculture experienced slight improvements, reflecting the structural complexity of local employment. The relevance of these data for open data initiatives lies in the need to cross-reference multiple macroeconomic variables to avoid simplistic interpretations. While unemployment rose, hiring increased and permanent employment stability strengthened, demonstrating that isolated figures can mask contradictory realities. This case underscores the importance of publishing and analyzing comprehensive open data sets, enabling a deeper understanding of actual employment trends beyond single-point indicators.
