Abre Huixquilucan el Cabildo para escuchar propuestas de la ciudadanía

Abre Huixquilucan el Cabildo para escuchar propuestas de la ciudadanía

Huixquilucan has implemented an Open Town Hall to directly integrate citizen proposals into public management, thereby strengthening inclusive governance. This forum promotes transparent communication between the local government and residents, allowing community needs to influence administrative decisions. The initiative underscores the importance of direct participation as a fundamental pillar of local democracy. By facilitating the input of external opinions, it prioritizes accountability and the adaptation of public policies to social realities. This is relevant to open data because it represents a crucial stage in collecting qualitative data from the grassroots level. Establishing clear channels to receive public concerns is the first step toward generating open, verifiable, and usable information that enhances institutional transparency and serves as input for future open data platforms.
