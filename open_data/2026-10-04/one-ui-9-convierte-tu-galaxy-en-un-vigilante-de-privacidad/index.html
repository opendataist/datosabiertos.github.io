One UI 9 convierte tu Galaxy en un vigilante de privacidad

One UI 9 convierte tu Galaxy en un vigilante de privacidad

Samsung has introduced enhanced AI-powered Privacy Alerts in One UI 9, a native feature that acts as an automatic watchdog to monitor applications’ background access to personal data. By processing artificial intelligence directly on the device, the system identifies unnecessary access attempts without requiring third-party software, providing users with crucial transparency into how their information and permissions are protected during everyday use. This capability embeds security within the operating system, complementing other tools such as fraud detection and security patches. However, availability is limited to high-end and recent devices, excluding entry-level lines or older models that do not meet the technical requirements for on-device processing. This digital divide underscores the importance of verifying specific compatibility beyond the installed operating system version. The relevance of this article to the open_data community lies in the progress toward privacy-by-design models, where data protection no longer relies on cloud services or opaque third-party solutions. The implementation of local AI to audit access demonstrates how algorithmic transparency and granular control over permissions can empower users, setting a valuable precedent for more robust, accessible, and understandable security standards within the open-source software ecosystem.
