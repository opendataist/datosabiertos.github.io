INE ultima tareas para censo experimental que será en Capinota

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) is conducting an experimental census in Capinota to finalize the 2024 methodology, ensuring effective data collection instruments and logistics. This test guarantees that future national surveys accurately reflect demographic realities, addressing geographic and social disparities. By validating these tools before the main census, the initiative strengthens the reliability and inclusivity of official statistics, which are essential for transparent, evidence-based public policy and open government accountability.
