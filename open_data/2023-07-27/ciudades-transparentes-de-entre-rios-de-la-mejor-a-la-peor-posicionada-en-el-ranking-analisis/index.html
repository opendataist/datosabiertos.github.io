Ciudades transparentes de Entre Ríos: de la mejor a la peor posicionada en el ranking | Análisis

Crespo leads Argentina’s Open Data Index, demonstrating how full transparency strengthens public accountability. In contrast, Gualeguaychú’s low level of openness reveals risks of institutional opacity that limit effective citizen participation. This ranking highlights the urgency of standardizing the publication of critical information to combat municipal digital inequality. The comparison underscores that access to open data is not merely a technical issue, but essential for democratic and equitable governance. The evolution since 2016 shows progress toward a more robust open data culture. This is highly relevant to the community, as it establishes clear metrics for demanding transparency and improving access to public information at the national level.
