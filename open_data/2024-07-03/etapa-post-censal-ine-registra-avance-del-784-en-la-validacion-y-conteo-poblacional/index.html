Etapa post censal: INE registra avance del 78,4% en la validación y conteo poblacional

Etapa post censal: INE registra avance del 78,4% en la validación y conteo poblacional

The National Institute of Statistics reports significant progress in the post-census phase of the 2024 Population and Housing Census. Key steps, including data cleaning, validation, and scanning of questionnaires, are advancing in accordance with strict technical standards. This rigorous processing aims to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the demographic data being compiled for the country. Specialized software is used to verify consistency and quality through specific cross-checks of the population count. The procedure involves receiving forms, reviewing their completeness, and scanning documents to create digital records. This methodical approach facilitates a comprehensive analysis, ensuring that the final dataset accurately reflects the country’s demographic profile with high precision. Preliminary results will be published in August, broken down by municipality and department. This release is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, granular demographic insights. Accessible statistical data empowers researchers and policymakers to make informed decisions, underscoring the importance of standardized, publicly available census information for societal development.
