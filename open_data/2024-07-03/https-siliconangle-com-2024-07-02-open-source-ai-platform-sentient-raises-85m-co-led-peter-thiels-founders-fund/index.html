Sentient has secured significant seed funding to challenge the monopoly of centralized AI development by building an open-source platform for Artificial General Intelligence. The initiative aims to dismantle the control held by a few large entities, ensuring that the transformative potential of AI is distributed equitably among the broader community rather than being confined to closed corporate systems. By leveraging blockchain technology, Sentient seeks to resolve critical incentive issues in open-source development. This approach encourages mass collaboration by allowing contributors to monetize their innovations and receive rewards for building, copying, or expanding upon AI models. This mechanism is designed to foster a sustainable ecosystem where individuals and groups can actively participate in advancing "Open AGI" without facing the disincentives currently present in the traditional open-source landscape. This effort is highly relevant to the open data and open source movements as it demonstrates how decentralized infrastructure can support collaborative innovation in high-resource fields like AI. It highlights a growing shift toward democratizing access to advanced technologies, proving that open collaboration can drive significant progress in areas traditionally dominated by proprietary, centralized interests.
