Las tendencias emergentes en el sector de la hospitalidad

The study concludes that modern hospitality is fundamentally shifting toward hyperlocal integration, where guests prioritize deep cultural immersion over isolated hotel amenities. Travelers increasingly demand experiences that connect them with the local community and environment, viewing the surrounding context as a critical factor in their decision-making process. This trend reflects a broader desire for authenticity, pushing hotels to move beyond traditional services to offer genuine engagement with local culture. Hotels are responding by collaborating closely with local communities to create unique, authentic experiences, such as cooking classes with regional chefs or traditional craft workshops. The physical design of these establishments also mirrors local aesthetics, telling stories through architecture that blends contemporary style with regional sensitivity. Furthermore, there is a strong emphasis on sustainable and regenerative practices, where hotels contribute to environmental restoration and support local ecosystems, thereby enhancing the guest’s connection to nature and the destination. This evolution is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the necessity of transparent, accessible information regarding local culture, sustainable practices, and community partnerships. To facilitate the personalized and hyperlocal experiences guests now seek, hospitality brands must leverage data insights effectively. Understanding and sharing data on local interactions, environmental impact, and community engagement allows travelers to make informed choices, fostering trust and enabling the personalized, meaningful connections that define the new era of tourism.
