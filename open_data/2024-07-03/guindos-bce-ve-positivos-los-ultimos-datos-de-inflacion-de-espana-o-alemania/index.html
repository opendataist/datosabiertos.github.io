Guindos (BCE) ve positivos los últimos datos de inflación de España o Alemania

The ECB Vice President stresses caution in light of recent inflation data, noting that although current figures are positive, base effects and the phase-out of crisis-era support measures will generate volatility. High inflation is expected to persist through the end of the year, alongside only a modest economic recovery, as governments scale back aid amid an uncertain geopolitical and electoral environment. Global economic fragmentation poses significant risks, potentially harming both the global and European economies. Consequently, the central bank avoids predetermined policy paths, relying instead on incoming data to navigate these challenges. This approach underscores the need to adapt monetary strategies dynamically rather than following a fixed course, ensuring responsiveness to emerging economic pressures. This insight is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the critical role of transparent, timely economic indicators in guiding complex financial decisions. Accurate public access to such data enables better analysis of inflation trends and policy impacts, fostering accountability and informed decision-making in an environment characterized by high uncertainty and significant external shocks.
