Five-month crab exports to China skyrocket by 502%

Vietnamese crab exports surged significantly, driven by strong demand in China, Hong Kong, and North America, while Japan saw modest overall gains despite recent dips. This robust trade performance highlights the critical importance of transparent supply chain data for market analysis and growth identification. However, a sharp decline in European Union exports underscores the severe consequences of regulatory non-compliance. The persistent EU "yellow card" for illegal fishing demonstrates how open, verifiable data on sourcing and compliance is essential for maintaining international market access. This contrast illustrates that while volume growth attracts investment, sustained success relies on robust data systems. For open data enthusiasts, this case proves that accessible, accurate information on trade flows and regulatory status is vital for global seafood sustainability and economic resilience.
