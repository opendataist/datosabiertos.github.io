Aplazan para septiembre censo general de población en Angola - Noticias Prensa Latina

The Angolan census is encountering logistical and technical delays in staff recruitment; however, the director of the National Institute of Statistics (INE) has confirmed that the operation will proceed as scheduled through December. This schedule ensures the collection of essential demographic and social data without jeopardizing the overall timeline for national development planning. The census results will provide critical insights into the population’s characteristics, directly supporting government decision-making and economic planning. By capturing comprehensive data on both residents and non-residents, the initiative aims to facilitate informed strategies for national development. This scenario underscores the relevance of open data, as the timely and transparent publication of census results enables society to verify government performance. It highlights the need for accessible datasets that allow citizens and institutions to assess policy impacts and demand accountability based on empirical evidence.
