Meta drives open source AI in Africa with Llama

Meta drives open source AI in Africa with Llama

Meta has launched a Sub-Saharan Africa initiative to accelerate the development of impactful, open source AI solutions using its Llama model. This program emphasizes the critical role of open source technologies in democratizing innovation and ensuring that artificial intelligence serves the specific needs of diverse communities across the region. By supporting startups in sectors like agriculture, healthcare, and finance, the project aims to deepen the adoption of open source tools while addressing regional development priorities. It provides equity-free funding, technical training, and mentorship to help local teams build scalable, socially relevant AI tools that align with national interests and public service delivery. This effort is highly relevant to open data as it fosters a collaborative ecosystem where developers contribute to digital infrastructure and influence emerging AI policies. By empowering local institutions and leaders, Meta seeks to create technology shaped by unique regional contexts, ensuring that open source AI becomes a practical, accessible resource for solving local challenges rather than a distant technological promise.
