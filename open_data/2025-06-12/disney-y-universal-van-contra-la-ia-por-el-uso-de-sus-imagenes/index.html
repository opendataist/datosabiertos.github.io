Disney y Universal van contra la IA por el uso de sus imágenes

Disney y Universal van contra la IA por el uso de sus imágenes

Disney and Universal’s recent lawsuit against Midjourney highlights the tension between technological innovation and intellectual property protection. This legal conflict underscores the urgency of defining clear responsibilities in the creation of AI-generated content, raising the question of whether liability lies with developers or end users. The relevance to open data is crucial, as these machine learning models rely heavily on large datasets that include copyrighted works. Transparency regarding the provenance and consent of the material used to train AI systems becomes a fundamental pillar for ensuring ethical and legal practices, thereby avoiding legal disputes that could restrict open access to technology. This dispute sets an important precedent for the future of digital image generation. By demanding greater diligence in filtering and using copyrighted materials, the industry will drive more rigorous standards on data provenance, ensuring that technological advancement respects the rights of original creators while fostering a fairer and more regulated digital environment.
