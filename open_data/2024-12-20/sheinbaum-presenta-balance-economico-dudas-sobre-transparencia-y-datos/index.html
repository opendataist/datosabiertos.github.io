Sheinbaum presenta balance económico; dudas sobre transparencia y datos

Sheinbaum presenta balance económico; dudas sobre transparencia y datos

President Claudia Sheinbaum presented an economic balance sheet highlighting achievements such as high approval ratings and payments from social programs. However, the absence of concrete data on survey methodologies and specific impacts raises doubts about the veracity of these claims. This lack of transparency obscures the real effectiveness of government actions and generates uncertainty regarding their true societal impact. The article also addresses industry protection and anti-corruption efforts, yet fails to provide detailed breakdowns or specific strategies. While tax patents were canceled, the opacity surrounding sanctions and the companies involved suggests a superficial approach to accountability. Furthermore, promises to strengthen oversight lack defined mechanisms, resembling mere declarations of intent rather than actionable strategies with clear timelines. This narrative is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how governmental opacity undermines public trust and accountability. By withholding detailed metrics and methodological information, the administration prevents meaningful scrutiny of its policies. The case highlights the critical need for accessible, precise data to evaluate policy efficiency, ensuring that economic decisions are transparent, verifiable, and truly beneficial to citizens rather than serving as political instruments.
