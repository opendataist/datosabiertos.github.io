ABS Releases First Ever Estimates Of LGBTI+ Australians

Recent census data reveals significant populations of LGB+ and trans individuals in Australia and New Zealand, highlighting the urgent need for accurate demographic tracking. This statistical visibility is crucial for open data initiatives, ensuring diverse groups are represented in public datasets. The upcoming 2026 census will expand this transparency, enabling better policy-making through inclusive, detailed geographic and population information.
