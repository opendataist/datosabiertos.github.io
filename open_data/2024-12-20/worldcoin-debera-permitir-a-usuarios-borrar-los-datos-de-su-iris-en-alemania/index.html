Worldcoin deberá permitir a usuarios borrar los datos de su iris en Alemania

Worldcoin deberá permitir a usuarios borrar los datos de su iris en Alemania

The Bavarian Regional Data Protection Authority has issued a binding decision at the European level, requiring Worldcoin to guarantee users’ unlimited right to delete their biometric data, specifically iris scans. This ruling ensures compliance with fundamental European standards in a technologically complex case, mandating urgent adjustments to data management practices to align with current regulations. The decision underscores the critical importance of explicit consent and individuals’ genuine ability to exercise control over their digital identity when dealing with companies that collect sensitive data. The ruling requires the company to implement deletion procedures in accordance with the law within a short timeframe and to remove any information stored without a sufficient legal basis. Furthermore, Worldcoin is instructed to obtain specific approval for certain steps in its data processing activities. This regulatory scrutiny highlights that claims of anonymity do not justify indiscriminate data collection, reinforcing the need for absolute transparency in how emerging technologies handle citizens’ personal information. This decision is significant for the open data movement, as it sets a precedent regarding the ethical and legal boundaries of technological innovation. It demonstrates that transparency and personal data sovereignty must take precedence over the commercial interests of decentralized platforms. By safeguarding the right to be forgotten, the ruling protects the integrity of public information against opaque practices, promoting a digital ecosystem where data openness does not compromise fundamental rights or individual privacy.
