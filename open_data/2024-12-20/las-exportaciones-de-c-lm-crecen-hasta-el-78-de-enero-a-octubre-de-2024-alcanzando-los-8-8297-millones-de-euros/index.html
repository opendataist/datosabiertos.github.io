Las exportaciones de C-LM crecen hasta el 7,8% de enero a octubre de 2024, alcanzando los 8.829,7 millones de euros

Las exportaciones de C-LM crecen hasta el 7,8% de enero a octubre de 2024, alcanzando los 8.829,7 millones de euros

The report highlights that Castilla-La Mancha’s trade balance remains significantly unbalanced, with imports far outpacing exports and resulting in a negative trade balance. The region’s coverage ratio is well below the national average, indicating a persistent dependency on external markets for goods it does not produce. This structural imbalance suggests that while the region generates substantial export revenue, it consumes even more from abroad, creating a deficit that impacts its overall economic autonomy and resilience in the global market. Agricultural and food products emerge as the primary drivers of regional exports, leading with strong growth in beverages, meats, and oils. However, the region relies heavily on imported equipment and chemicals to sustain its industrial and technological activities. This divergence reveals an economic model heavily based on the export of raw or processed natural resources while importing high-value manufactured goods, which limits value-added retention within the local economy. Crucially, the European Union dominates both export destinations and import sources, reflecting deep regional integration with its neighbors. While trade with non-EU countries shows isolated growth, the overwhelming concentration in Europe underscores the region’s vulnerability to broader continental economic shifts. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides granular, transparent insights into regional economic dependencies, enabling policymakers and analysts to identify structural weaknesses, track trade evolution, and design targeted strategies to diversify markets and enhance local industrial capacity.
