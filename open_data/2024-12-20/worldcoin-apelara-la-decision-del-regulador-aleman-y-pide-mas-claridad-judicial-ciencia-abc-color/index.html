Worldcoin apelará la decisión del regulador alemán y pide más claridad judicial - Ciencia - ABC Color

Worldcoin apelará la decisión del regulador alemán y pide más claridad judicial - Ciencia - ABC Color

World ID is seeking judicial clarification on whether its global privacy-enhancing technologies meet the European Union’s definition of anonymization. The company argues that precise anonymization is essential for human verification in the digital age, particularly within the context of artificial intelligence. Without such clarity, the industry risks losing a powerful tool for protecting user privacy against evolving technological threats. The company highlights significant updates to its data custody system, emphasizing that personal data remains strictly on the user’s device. It states that sensitive information, such as iris codes, is no longer retained or stored, with older data voluntarily eliminated. Instead, the system relies on cryptographically secured anonymous shares to function, ensuring that identity verification occurs without exposing raw personal details to third parties. This case is relevant to open data as it defines the boundary between verifiable digital identity and personal privacy. It challenges how anonymization processes can be standardized to allow public verification mechanisms without compromising individual data rights. The outcome may set a precedent for balancing transparency in digital identity systems with strict data protection standards across regulatory borders.
