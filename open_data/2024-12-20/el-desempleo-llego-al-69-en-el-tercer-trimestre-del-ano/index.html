El desempleo llegó al 6,9% en el tercer trimestre del año

El desempleo llegó al 6,9% en el tercer trimestre del año

The report highlights a significant rise in unemployment, particularly among young women and individuals with higher education, signaling structural challenges in the labor market. This shift suggests that traditional economic recovery strategies may be failing to absorb skilled workers, potentially impacting long-term growth and social equity. Furthermore, substantial underemployment and informal work persist, indicating that many jobs lack security or benefits. The high percentage of workers without pension deductions underscores vulnerabilities in social protection systems, revealing a disconnect between formal employment statistics and actual worker welfare. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how transparent, granular statistics empower analysts to identify inequality and inform policy. Open access to such detailed labor metrics enables civil society and researchers to hold institutions accountable, ensuring that economic narratives reflect the lived realities of diverse demographic groups.
