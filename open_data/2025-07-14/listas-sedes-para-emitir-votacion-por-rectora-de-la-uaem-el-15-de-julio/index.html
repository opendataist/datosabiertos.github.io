Listas sedes para emitir votación por rectora de la UAEM el 15 de julio

Listas sedes para emitir votación por rectora de la UAEM el 15 de julio

The Universidad Autónoma del Estado de México has established a dual voting framework for its upcoming rector election, prioritizing both transparency and the protection of personal data. By allowing communities to choose between physical and electronic voting methods, the institution ensures inclusive participation while maintaining rigorous oversight. This flexible approach aims to strengthen democratic processes within the university by adapting to diverse logistical and privacy needs across its numerous campuses. Electronic voting utilizes an official online system accessible via institutional email, explicitly designed to safeguard participants' personal data without compromising security. Concurrently, physical voting locations are distributed across various faculties and institutes, requiring strict identification protocols to verify voter eligibility. The presence of internal electoral committees and registered observers at every stage guarantees that the integrity of the ballot count remains uncompromised, regardless of the chosen method. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public institutions can implement transparent digital infrastructure that respects privacy regulations. By clearly defining data handling procedures and providing verifiable mechanisms for vote counting, the university sets a precedent for responsible digital governance. Such practices highlight the importance of balancing technological efficiency with the ethical obligation to protect user information, fostering greater trust in digital electoral systems.
