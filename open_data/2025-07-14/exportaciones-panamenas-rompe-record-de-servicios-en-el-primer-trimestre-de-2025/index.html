Exportaciones panameñas rompe récord de servicios en el primer trimestre de 2025

Exportaciones panameñas rompe récord de servicios en el primer trimestre de 2025

Panama achieves historic growth in services exports, leading Central America. This surge, driven by transport and tourism, highlights the success of policies aimed at diversifying high-value sectors such as finance and telecommunications through multinational enterprise regimes. These economic achievements are made transparent through the official Intelcom platform. By publishing detailed trade data, Panama exemplifies how open data fosters accountability and allows stakeholders to monitor the effectiveness of modernization efforts in real time. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible government statistics validate economic progress. Transparent reporting enables researchers and investors to analyze trends, proving that data openness is crucial for understanding and sustaining national development.
