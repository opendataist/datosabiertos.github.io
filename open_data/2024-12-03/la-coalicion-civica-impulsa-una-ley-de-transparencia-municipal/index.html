La Coalición Cívica impulsa una Ley de Transparencia Municipal

La Coalición Cívica impulsa una Ley de Transparencia Municipal

The Civic Coalition in Buenos Aires Province has reinforced its commitment to open governance, prioritizing the implementation of a Municipal Transparency Law. This legislative framework aims to democratize access to public information through mandatory digital portals that detail revenues, expenditures, and contracts, thereby strengthening citizen oversight of local government management. At the same time, the initiative focuses on social protection by accelerating legislative action to address cyber-gambling addiction and implement the "Clean Record" (Ficha Limpia) requirement. Political leaders emphasize that transparency is not merely an administrative formality, but a fundamental pillar for building a politics that is close to citizens, responsible, and free from electoral opportunism, calling for the expediting of legislative processes that safeguard future generations. This proposal is significant for the open data movement because it institutionalizes the proactive publication of government data. By standardizing the availability of financial and contractual information on accessible platforms, it establishes a crucial technical and political precedent for accountability, demonstrating how active transparency translates into concrete mechanisms for participation and social auditing.
