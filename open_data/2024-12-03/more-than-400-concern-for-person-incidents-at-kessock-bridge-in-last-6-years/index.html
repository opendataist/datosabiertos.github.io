More than 400 ‘concern for person’ incidents at Kessock Bridge in last 6 years

The Kessock Bridge experiences frequent crisis interventions, causing significant traffic disruption. This recurring issue highlights the urgent need for targeted safety interventions at critical infrastructure nodes to maintain public order and transport efficiency. These incidents underscore the vital role of open data in identifying high-risk locations and evaluating policy effectiveness. By making such statistics accessible, stakeholders can analyze trends and justify investments in mental health support or physical safety measures to reduce harm. Ultimately, transparency in these figures fosters accountability and community awareness. It enables policymakers to address systemic failures proactively, ensuring that infrastructure management prioritizes human safety while minimizing broader societal impacts from repeated closures.
