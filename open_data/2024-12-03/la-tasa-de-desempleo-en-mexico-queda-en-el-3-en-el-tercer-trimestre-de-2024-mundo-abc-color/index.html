La tasa de desempleo en México queda en el 3 % en el tercer trimestre de 2024 - Mundo - ABC Color

Mexico’s labor market shows stability, with unemployment remaining flat despite a growing economically active population. Job growth is concentrated in the services, hospitality, and agriculture sectors, indicating sector-specific expansion rather than broad-based improvement. This stability reflects modest economic growth, yet underlying issues persist. A significant portion of workers remains underemployed, highlighting a gap between job availability and adequate working hours. Additionally, a substantial gender disparity exists, with women participating in the labor market at much lower rates than men. These factors suggest that headline employment figures may not fully capture the health of the labor market or existing inequalities. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives, as it demonstrates how granular, publicly accessible statistics enable the analysis of complex socio-economic trends. Transparent data allows researchers to identify disparities in gender participation and underemployment, fostering informed policy discussions and public accountability regarding economic well-being and labor rights.
