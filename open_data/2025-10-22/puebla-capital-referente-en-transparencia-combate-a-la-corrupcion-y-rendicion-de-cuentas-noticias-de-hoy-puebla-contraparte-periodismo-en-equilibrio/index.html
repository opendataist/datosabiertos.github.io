Puebla capital, referente en transparencia, combate a la corrupción y rendición de cuentas - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Puebla capital, referente en transparencia, combate a la corrupción y rendición de cuentas - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The article underscores the Puebla government’s commitment to transparency and anti-corruption efforts, positioning the state capital as a model of accountability. The Municipal Comptroller’s Office has developed an anti-corruption policy aligned with state and national standards, actively engaging citizens through forums and digital surveys. This initiative incorporates ethical guidelines, institutional commitment letters, and a dedicated ethics committee, reflecting a comprehensive approach to fostering integrity within public administration. Operational efforts have emphasized rigorous oversight of public spending and construction projects. Significant resources have been audited, including routine and investment expenditures, while physical inspections and technical tests have been conducted on numerous public works to identify defects. By integrating internal systems with a national digital platform, the office enables real-time monitoring of public contractors and sanctioned officials, thereby enhancing the reliability and accessibility of government performance data. This work is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how robust internal controls and citizen engagement can produce trustworthy, verifiable information. The integration of digital platforms for declarations and sanctions establishes a framework for transparent data sharing, empowering society to hold authorities accountable. Ultimately, the article illustrates that effective open data requires not merely the publication of information, but also active verification and public participation to build genuine trust in governance.
