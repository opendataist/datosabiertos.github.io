El Director General de INEGE llama al uso estratégico de los datos en el Día Mundial de la Estadística

El Director General de INEGE llama al uso estratégico de los datos en el Día Mundial de la Estadística

The article emphasizes the critical role of high-quality, official statistics in shaping evidence-based public policies and fostering societal trust. It highlights how World Statistics Day aims to raise awareness about national statistical systems, promoting a culture where data informs decision-making at all levels of society. The significance of this initiative lies in its ability to address complex global challenges, from economic recovery to sustainable development, by ensuring access to accurate and timely information that guides effective governance. Historical context, particularly the pandemic response, underscores the necessity of resilient and adaptable statistical infrastructure. The experience demonstrated that reliable data is indispensable for health responses and social protection, reinforcing the need for scientific rigor and independence in official statistics. This foundation supports the formulation of policies that combat inequalities and climate change, ensuring that development goals are met through credible and transparent mechanisms. Relevance to open data is evident in the commitment to improving data quality, adopting international standards, and facilitating open access to public information. By promoting statistical literacy and transparency, the government seeks to empower citizens and strengthen accountability. This approach aligns with open data principles, encouraging collaboration, ethical practices, and inclusive participation, ultimately enhancing the reliability and utility of statistical systems for all users.
