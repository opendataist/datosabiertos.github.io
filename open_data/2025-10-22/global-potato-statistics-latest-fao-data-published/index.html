Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The article highlights the FAO’s FAOSTAT database as a critical source for global agricultural statistics, specifically detailing worldwide potato production metrics for 2021. It identifies China and India as the dominant producers, collectively accounting for a significant portion of the global supply. This information underscores how centralized international databases aggregate diverse national data to provide a comprehensive view of crop yields, facilitating cross-country comparisons and trend analysis that individual nation reports might not easily offer. Furthermore, the text addresses the potential discrepancies between FAO figures and official national data, suggesting that while minor variations exist, the database remains sufficiently accurate for most analytical purposes. This transparency regarding data reliability is essential for researchers and policymakers who depend on standardized global metrics. It demonstrates the practical challenges of harmonizing heterogeneous statistical inputs into a unified framework, a core issue in managing open government and international development data. This content is highly relevant to open data communities because it illustrates the utility and limitations of publicly accessible statistical repositories. By enabling users to extract, verify, and analyze raw production data independently, FAOSTAT exemplifies the empowerment that open data brings to transparency and informed decision-making. Understanding these sources allows stakeholders to better interpret global food security trends and assess the quality of open datasets used in economic and agricultural research.
