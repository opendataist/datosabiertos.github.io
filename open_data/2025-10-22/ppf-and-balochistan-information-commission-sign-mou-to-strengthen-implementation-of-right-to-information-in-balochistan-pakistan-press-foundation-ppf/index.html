PPF and Balochistan Information Commission Sign MoU to Strengthen Implementation of Right to Information in Balochistan – Pakistan Press Foundation (PPF)

PPF and Balochistan Information Commission Sign MoU to Strengthen Implementation of Right to Information in Balochistan – Pakistan Press Foundation (PPF)

PPF and BIC partner to strengthen transparency and media literacy in Balochistan through joint RTI training and awareness campaigns. This collaboration empowers citizens and officials, fostering a culture of proactive information sharing and responsible journalism across the region. The initiative directly supports open data by institutionalizing the Right to Information, ensuring public institutions practice accountability. By enhancing access to government records, it enables citizens to utilize data for participatory governance and democratic oversight, vital for an open society.
