The IOAE reveals a significant slowdown in Mexico’s manufacturing sector, highlighting vulnerabilities to external demand shifts and aggressive tariff policies. This data underscores the critical role of timely, official statistics in diagnosing real-time economic health and identifying structural weaknesses before they become entrenched crises. By providing rapid econometric estimates, the IOAE exemplifies how open, high-frequency indicators empower researchers and policymakers to make agile decisions. Transparent access to such granular activity data fosters better resource allocation and risk management within the national economy. Ultimately, this article illustrates the vital importance of accessible public data in monitoring economic resilience. Open data initiatives ensure that crucial metrics regarding industrial performance and service sector growth are available for public scrutiny, enhancing accountability and supporting informed civic discourse on economic policy impacts.
