Nuevo aviso de la Policía Nacional: esta es la razón por la que nunca debes acceder a una red wifi pública

Nuevo aviso de la Policía Nacional: esta es la razón por la que nunca debes acceder a una red wifi pública

The National Police and INCIBE warn about the serious risks of connecting to public WiFi networks, emphasizing that the ideal option is to avoid them, although this is often unavoidable. The danger lies in the lack of encryption and authentication, which makes it easy for criminals to create fake networks that mimic legitimate ones. These "trap networks" allow the interception of private data, installation of malware, and man-in-the-middle attacks, compromising users' personal and financial information. To mitigate these risks when it is necessary to use open WiFi, it is crucial to verify the exact name of the network with the establishment and ensure that the website being visited uses HTTPS, identifiable by the padlock in the browser. Additionally, it is recommended to disable file sharing, keep devices updated with the latest security patches, and avoid conducting sensitive transactions or logging into personal accounts while connected to these insecure networks. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the structural vulnerability of open digital infrastructure. Just as open data relies on transparency and public accessibility, open WiFi networks depend on trust in an uncontrolled environment. Awareness of security in these public digital spaces highlights the need to implement robust protection protocols and citizen education, fundamental principles for ensuring that any initiative involving open information or connectivity is secure, reliable, and sustainable for society.
