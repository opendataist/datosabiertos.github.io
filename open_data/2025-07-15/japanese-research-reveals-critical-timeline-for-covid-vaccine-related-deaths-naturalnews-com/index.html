Japanese research reveals critical timeline for covid vaccine-related deaths – NaturalNews.com

Japanese research reveals critical timeline for covid vaccine-related deaths – NaturalNews.com

This study analyzes extensive health records to map the timeline of mortality risks following COVID-19 vaccinations. The primary finding identifies a critical window between sixty and one hundred twenty days after subsequent doses, during which the risk of death peaks significantly. This period suggests the development of physiological issues such as immune suppression and clotting, which eventually subside. Crucially, the risk profile normalizes one year after the final dose, providing a clear endpoint for heightened mortality concerns. Furthermore, the research indicates that unvaccinated individuals do not experience increased mortality risks, reinforcing that vaccination status is the distinguishing factor in these specific death patterns. These insights offer reassurance to those past the one-year mark while highlighting the importance of monitoring long-term health impacts, including potential cognitive and fertility issues. This analysis is relevant to open_data because it relies on freedom of information requests to access and scrutinize massive datasets of vaccination and death records. The case underscores how transparent access to granular public health data allows for independent verification of safety trends. It demonstrates the necessity of open data frameworks in enabling rigorous, community-led scientific inquiry into vaccine efficacy and long-term safety, ensuring public trust through evidence-based transparency rather than opaque institutional reporting.
