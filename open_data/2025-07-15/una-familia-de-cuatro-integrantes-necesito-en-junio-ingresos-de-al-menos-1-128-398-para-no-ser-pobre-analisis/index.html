Una familia de cuatro integrantes necesitó en junio ingresos de al menos $1.128.398 para no ser pobre | Análisis

Una familia de cuatro integrantes necesitó en junio ingresos de al menos $1.128.398 para no ser pobre | Análisis

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) published the June 2025 valuation of the basic food basket and the total basket for the Greater Buenos Aires area, revealing that a typical four-person household needs more than one million pesos to stay above the poverty line. This data underscores the widening gap between household incomes and the rising cost of essential goods, highlighting the persistent economic pressure on families even though monthly inflation has remained below two percent for two consecutive months. The report outlines specific poverty and indigency thresholds based on household composition and adult equivalents, showing substantial year-over-year increases in both the food and total basket costs. Although monthly price changes have been relatively contained, the cumulative annual growth reflects a significant erosion of purchasing power. These metrics serve as critical benchmarks for evaluating social welfare and the effectiveness of public policies designed to mitigate economic hardship during periods of inflation. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how official statistical agencies transform raw price survey data into accessible, standardized indicators of social well-being. By providing detailed data on different household structures and clear definitions of poverty lines, INDEC enables researchers, policymakers, and civil society to transparently monitor living conditions. This openness facilitates evidence-based analysis and enhances accountability for economic trends, ensuring that citizens have access to the objective information needed to understand their financial reality.
