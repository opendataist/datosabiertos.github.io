Según el INDEC, la inflación de junio fue del 1,6% - Actualidad TDF

Según el INDEC, la inflación de junio fue del 1,6% - Actualidad TDF

Argentina’s June inflation rose slightly to 1.6%, marking a 39.4% annual increase. This data highlights the persistent challenge of high inflation, despite government claims of success in controlling it. The release underscores the critical role of INDEC in providing official economic indicators. Accurate public data is essential for citizens and markets to understand the true cost of living and economic stability. Relevant to open data, this transparency allows independent verification of economic health. Citizens rely on accessible statistics to hold institutions accountable and make informed financial decisions amid volatile price trends.
