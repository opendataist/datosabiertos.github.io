En 2022, la actividad industrial creció 4,3% y la construcción 3,5%

Argentina’s industrial and construction sectors expanded significantly over the year, despite recent monthly declines and widespread contractions in specific manufacturing branches during December. This economic volatility highlights the complexity of tracking real-time performance across diverse sub-sectors, from furniture to automotive production. The data reveal a sharp divergence between sectors, with energy and tobacco growing while most other industries shrank. Such granular shifts underscore the necessity of detailed, disaggregated statistical information to accurately assess economic health. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of accessible, timely, and granular official statistics. By making such detailed datasets public, governments enable transparency and allow analysts to uncover nuanced trends that aggregate figures might obscure, fostering better economic understanding and accountability.
