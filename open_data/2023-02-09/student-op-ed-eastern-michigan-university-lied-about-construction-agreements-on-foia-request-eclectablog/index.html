Student op-ed: Eastern Michigan University lied about construction agreements on FOIA request | Eclectablog

The article argues that Eastern Michigan University’s administration has violated its core values of integrity and responsibility by falsely claiming that construction agreements with Gilbane Development Company did not exist in response to a Freedom of Information request. This deliberate omission, despite the contracts being signed months prior, is described as a brazen lie that contradicts the institution’s commitment to transparency. The author asserts that this failure to adhere to ethical standards represents a significant breach of trust between the student body and university leadership. The narrative emphasizes that while individual instances of dishonesty might seem minor, they fundamentally undermine the sustainability of the university mission by destroying trust. Just as student cheating devalues education, administrative lack of candor damages the institution's reputation and effectiveness. The author calls for the president to be held accountable, arguing that leadership must restore credibility by promptly and honestly addressing public records requests and apologizing to the community for bringing shame upon the university. Relevant to open data, this case illustrates the critical importance of accurate and complete public information disclosure. It highlights how withholding or falsifying data during FOIA requests erodes public trust and compromises institutional accountability. The incident serves as a cautionary example that true transparency requires more than just technical compliance; it demands an underlying culture of honesty. Furthermore, the author links this transparency issue to broader community support for union labor, suggesting that ethical governance extends to fair working conditions and community engagement, reinforcing the need for open, truthful data practices in public institutions.
