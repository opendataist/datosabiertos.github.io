Tech Mahindra to open Data, AI cloud center in Saudi Arabia

Tech Mahindra to open Data, AI cloud center in Saudi Arabia

This partnership establishes a Center of Excellence in Saudi Arabia to accelerate the adoption of data analytics and ethical AI. By fostering local talent and digital innovation, it supports the nation’s broader digital transformation goals. The initiative aims to drive cloud-enabled changes across industries, enhancing small and medium enterprises. This strategic move promotes the creation of high-quality jobs and develops local intellectual property assets. Relevant to open data, this effort emphasizes ethical AI and sustainable digital governance. It highlights how public-private collaborations can structure data ecosystems to ensure transparency and responsible usage in national development strategies.
