El IPC de Francia se modera al 2,9% en febrero, en mínimos de más de dos años

French inflation shows signs of cooling, marking its slowest pace since early 2022. This moderation is primarily driven by a significant reduction in food price increases, offering consumers tangible relief. Although energy costs rose, the overall trend indicates a return toward price stability, suggesting that the worst of the post-pandemic economic shock may be subsiding. Economic growth remained minimal in the last quarter, with household spending stagnating as goods consumption fell. Services continued to expand, while capital formation declined sharply. The positive contribution from net exports helped sustain the marginal GDP growth, highlighting a divergence between domestic demand and external trade performance in the French economy. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of accessing timely, official statistical revisions like those from Insee. Reliable public datasets enable analysts to track complex economic shifts, such as the decoupling of energy and food inflation trends. Understanding these granular updates helps researchers validate models and assess the real-world impact of monetary policies on household welfare.
