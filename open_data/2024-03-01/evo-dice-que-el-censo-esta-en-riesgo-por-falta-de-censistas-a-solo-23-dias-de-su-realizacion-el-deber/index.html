Evo dice que el Censo está en “riesgo” por falta de censistas a sólo 23 días de su realización | El Deber

The census is experiencing critical delays due to insufficient volunteer recruitment, threatening the accuracy of national demographic data that is essential for public policy. This shortage highlights systemic gaps in institutional capacity to collect vital public information, a core concern for open data ecosystems. Morales proposes mandatory state mobilization to fill these roles, raising questions about the balance between voluntary civic participation and administrative coercion. Such measures could affect the perceived neutrality and accessibility of official statistics, potentially undermining public trust in data transparency. This situation underscores the fragility of open data initiatives when they depend on unstable logistical frameworks. Ensuring reliable, accessible, and unbiased public statistics requires robust and transparent recruitment processes rather than emergency interventions that might compromise data integrity.
