Los congresistas del Mobile se mueven entre el paseo de Gràcia y la Sagrada Familia

Telefónica uses its Smart Steps tool to analyze anonymized mobile connectivity data, revealing the precise movement patterns of attendees at the Mobile World Congress. This application of big data enables urban planners to understand how thousands of people navigate the city within narrow time frames, optimizing infrastructure services and ensuring smooth operations during major events. The analysis highlights significant peaks in visitor flow, particularly around midday and late afternoon, showing a clear trend of congress participants traveling from the fairgrounds to the Eixample district for cultural and commercial activities. This data-driven insight allows stakeholders to anticipate congestion and manage resources effectively, demonstrating the practical value of real-time mobility intelligence in urban management. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how aggregated, anonymized datasets can be leveraged for public benefit without compromising individual privacy. By adhering to strict data protection regulations, such initiatives showcase the potential for responsible data usage to enhance city services, providing a model for transparency and efficiency in smart city ecosystems.
