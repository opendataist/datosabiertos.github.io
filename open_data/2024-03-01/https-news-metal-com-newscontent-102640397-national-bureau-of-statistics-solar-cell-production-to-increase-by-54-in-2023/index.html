The 2023 national data reveals a decisive shift toward renewable energy, with solar and wind capacity surging significantly while traditional coal’s share of total energy consumption declined. This structural transformation underscores the growing viability of green infrastructure and highlights how clean energy sources are rapidly displacing fossil fuels in the national grid. Improved industrial efficiency further demonstrates progress, as key sectors achieved reductions in energy intensity per unit of output. These gains indicate that technological advancements and better management practices are successfully decoupling economic growth from resource consumption, offering a model for sustainable industrial development across various heavy industries. For the open data community, this release provides critical, standardized metrics on energy transitions and environmental performance. Transparent access to such official statistics enables researchers and analysts to verify sustainability claims, track policy effectiveness, and drive evidence-based advocacy for cleaner energy solutions globally.
