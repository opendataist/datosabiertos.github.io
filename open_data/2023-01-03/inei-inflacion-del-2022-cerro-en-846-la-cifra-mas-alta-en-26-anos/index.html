INEI: Inflación del 2022 cerró en 8,46%, la cifra más alta en 26 años

The article highlights a significant surge in Peru’s consumer price index in 2022, marking one of the highest inflation rates in recent decades. This widespread increase was primarily driven by sharp rises in the costs of food and beverages, transportation, and hospitality services. The data reveals that essential goods, particularly vegetables and staples, experienced dramatic price hikes, profoundly impacting household budgets and reflecting a challenging economic environment for consumers across the nation. A detailed breakdown of the report shows that inflation was not isolated to specific sectors but affected nearly all consumption divisions. The depth of the analysis, which tracks price variations for hundreds of specific products, illustrates the complexity of the inflationary pressure. While some items saw minor decreases, the overwhelming trend was upward, indicating systemic cost increases that influenced regional disparities, with certain cities experiencing even more intense price volatility than the national average. This article is relevant to open data because it showcases the granular accessibility of official statistical information through institutions like the INEI. By publishing detailed technical reports on price indices down to individual product levels, the data becomes a vital resource for researchers, analysts, and the public. Such transparency allows for a deeper understanding of economic trends, enabling better policy evaluation and informed decision-making based on empirical evidence rather than general estimates.
