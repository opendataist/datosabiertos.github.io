Nigeria Active GSM Subscribers Hit 218.6 Million in November ’22 – NCC - How Nigeria News

Nigeria’s telecommunication sector demonstrates significant mobile penetration, with GSM operators adding substantial new subscribers in late 2022. MTN continues to dominate the market, while competitors like Airtel and 9mobile show mixed growth trends. These shifts highlight the dynamic competitive landscape and consumer preferences within the national communications ecosystem. The data also reveals the negligible scale of fixed-wired and VoIP services compared to mobile dominance. Operators like Smile lead in VoIP, yet overall adoption remains minimal. This disparity underscores a heavy reliance on mobile infrastructure, indicating where regulatory focus and investment are most critical for national connectivity goals. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent reporting by regulatory bodies like the NCC enables accurate industry analysis. Publicly accessible statistics allow researchers and policymakers to monitor market health, evaluate operator performance, and inform decisions that shape digital inclusion and infrastructure development across the country.
