SEBI, MIIs working on centralised FPI data portal

SEBI, MIIs working on centralised FPI data portal

SEBI is launching a centralized online portal to consolidate dispersed public data for foreign portfolio investors. This initiative addresses long-standing complaints regarding the difficulty of accessing consistent regulatory information across multiple intermediary websites. By gathering registration procedures, settlement timelines, and tax guidelines into one platform, the authority aims to significantly ease the business environment for international investors in India. The platform initially provides static, publicly available information contributed by various market infrastructure institutions and financial firms. This collaborative effort includes data on trading limits, onboarding procedures, and applicable tax regimes. The focus is on creating a user-friendly interface that simplifies the complex operational landscape, ensuring investors have a single source of truth for essential compliance and operational details without navigating fragmented digital sources. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates as it demonstrates how aggregating and standardizing disparate regulatory datasets improves transparency and accessibility. By moving from scattered proprietary silos to a unified, publicly accessible resource, SEBI enhances market efficiency. Furthermore, planned future phases involving interactive compliance tools suggest a trajectory toward more dynamic, user-centric open data applications that facilitate real-time regulatory adherence for global market participants.
