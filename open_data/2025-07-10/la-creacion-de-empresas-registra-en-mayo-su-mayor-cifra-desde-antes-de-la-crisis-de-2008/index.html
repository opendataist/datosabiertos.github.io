La creación de empresas registra en mayo su mayor cifra desde antes de la crisis de 2008

Spain has recorded its highest number of new company creations since before the 2008 financial crisis, signaling a robust recovery in the business landscape. This surge reflects a significant rebound in entrepreneurial activity, although it coexists with rising closures, particularly in the retail sector, indicating that the economic fabric remains fragile despite the positive momentum. The equilibrium between new formations and dissolutions suggests that while dynamism is returning, long-term consolidation depends heavily on future trends in investment, financing, and consumer demand. The data reveals that growth is widespread across most regions, with only isolated areas showing declines, highlighting a predominantly national recovery that persists alongside sector-specific vulnerabilities. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly available statistical indicators from institutions like the INE and the Bank of Spain provide critical, real-time insights into economic health. The transparency of these datasets allows stakeholders to monitor complex economic shifts, such as the interplay between business vitality and household financial wealth, fostering informed decision-making based on verified, accessible evidence rather than speculation.
