Vladímir Putin dio un año de plazo para la creación del registro estatal de extranjeros en Rusia

Vladímir Putin dio un año de plazo para la creación del registro estatal de extranjeros en Rusia

Putin has ordered the establishment of a digital registry for foreigners by mid-2026, with the goal of creating comprehensive migration data to regulate entry and residence. This centralization stems from tightened security policies in the wake of recent extremist attacks, reflecting a strategic shift toward stricter migration controls and increased deportations. The initiative prioritizes labor migrants, primarily from Central Asia, requiring real-time information to effectively assess and manage migration flows. By consolidating data on arrivals and departures, the government seeks to enhance oversight and enforcement of immigration laws. This case highlights the intersection of state surveillance and open data governance, raising critical concerns about transparency and individual privacy in public databases. It underscores the ongoing tension between administrative efficiency in migration management and the ethical responsibilities of handling sensitive personal information within national registries.
