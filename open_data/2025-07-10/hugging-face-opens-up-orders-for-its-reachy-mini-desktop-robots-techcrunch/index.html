Hugging Face opens up orders for its Reachy Mini desktop robots | TechCrunch

Hugging Face opens up orders for its Reachy Mini desktop robots | TechCrunch

Hugging Face is expanding its open-source robotics ecosystem by making its Reachy Mini desktop robots available for purchase. This move invites developers to test and build applications on fully programmable hardware, fostering a community-driven approach to AI robotics. By providing both wireless and wired versions, the company ensures accessibility for various user needs while maintaining an affordable entry point for hobbyists and professionals alike. The significance of this release lies in its alignment with the core principles of open data and open source software. The robots are designed to integrate seamlessly with the Hugging Face Hub, granting users access to millions of AI models and datasets. This integration empowers developers to extend, modify, and share their creations, effectively democratizing the technology. It allows the community to build specific features and applications, creating a collaborative environment where knowledge and code are freely shared rather than restricted. This initiative is particularly relevant to open data advocates as it challenges the trend of closed-source, centralized control in the robotics industry. Hugging Face emphasizes the importance of user sovereignty, arguing that individuals should have complete understanding and control over the AI systems in their homes. By promoting transparent, customizable hardware, the company aims to prevent a future where robots are controlled by a few major corporations, instead encouraging a decentralized, community-powered landscape that prioritizes privacy, security, and individual autonomy.
