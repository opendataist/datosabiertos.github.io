Inflación de junio: subió vivienda… frutas y verduras lo que más bajó

INEGI reports that June inflation stabilized at 4.32% annually, driven by rising costs in housing, dining, and transport, while specific fruits and vegetables saw significant price drops. This highlights how diverse market forces simultaneously push and pull the general price level. For open data enthusiasts, this release demonstrates the practical application of publicly available economic indicators. It allows researchers to analyze granular price trends, understand consumer behavior, and validate economic models using official statistics. The data’s relevance lies in its transparency, enabling independent verification of national economic health. By accessing these detailed figures, citizens and analysts can better grasp inflation drivers, fostering informed public discourse and enhancing accountability in economic reporting.
