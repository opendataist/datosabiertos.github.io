You can now pre-order Hugging Face's Reachy Mini robots

You can now pre-order Hugging Face's Reachy Mini robots

Hugging Face introduced the Reachy Mini, an open-source, compact robot designed to lower barriers for human-robot interaction and AI experimentation. By allowing users to code behaviors in Python and share creations with a massive global community, it fosters collaborative innovation in robotics development. The device emphasizes accessibility through its open architecture and affordable kit-based model, enabling enthusiasts to build and customize expressive, interactive bots without extensive resources. This approach democratizes access to advanced robotic platforms, encouraging widespread participation in creative coding and AI research. This launch is highly relevant to open data because it extends the open-source philosophy from software to physical hardware. By providing open specifications and a platform for shared robotic data and code, Hugging Face strengthens the ecosystem for transparent, community-driven advancements in both artificial intelligence and physical computing.
