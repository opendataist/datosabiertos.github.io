Debe LN dar a conocer documento de seguridad

The INAI mandates that the National Lottery must publish its Security Document, ensuring compliance with data protection laws. This ruling emphasizes that institutional obligations to safeguard citizens' personal data persist regardless of internal organizational changes or mergers. It highlights the critical necessity for transparency regarding the physical, technical, and administrative measures institutions adopt to guarantee data confidentiality and integrity. This case illustrates the enforcement mechanism of open data principles, specifically the right of citizens to access information about how their personal data is protected. By rejecting excuses related to structural reorganization and broken links, the authority reinforces that legal duties are not suspended during transitions. The decision ensures that individuals can verify the security protocols governing their sensitive information, promoting accountability in public administration. This article is relevant to open data because it establishes precedents for transparency in data governance. It demonstrates how regulatory bodies empower citizens to hold institutions accountable for their data protection practices. By enforcing the publication of security documents, the ruling supports a culture of openness where the methods of data safeguarding are visible and verifiable, thereby strengthening trust and ensuring that public entities adhere to democratic standards of information availability.
