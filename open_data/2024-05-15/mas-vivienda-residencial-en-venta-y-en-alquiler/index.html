Más vivienda residencial en venta y en alquiler

The Balearic Government has launched a program to rent out vacant homes and sublease them at affordable rates to residents, aiming to alleviate the severe housing shortage. This initiative seeks to address owners’ reluctance to rent and the prevalence of illegal tourist rentals, with the goal of stabilizing a market in which residents spend the majority of their income on rent. The urgency of the situation stems from alarming scarcity and rising costs, underscoring the need for immediate action to ensure dignified housing for local residents. This policy is relevant to open data, as it relies on accurate statistics to identify vacant properties and monitor market trends. Transparent data on housing occupancy and pricing is crucial for evaluating the program’s success. By making this information accessible, stakeholders can better understand supply gaps and assess whether public interventions effectively increase the availability of affordable rentals for the community.
