PIB de Estados Unidos creció 2.3% en último trimestre de 2024; desaceleración fue por caída de inversión y exportaciones

PIB de Estados Unidos creció 2.3% en último trimestre de 2024; desaceleración fue por caída de inversión y exportaciones

The U.S. economy demonstrated resilience in 2024, defying recession forecasts despite monetary tightening. While overall growth slowed due to reduced investment and exports, robust consumer spending acted as a critical stabilizer, proving the sector’s underlying strength against external shocks. This economic stability is crucial for open data initiatives, as it validates the necessity of high-quality, real-time economic indicators for monitoring public policy and market health. Transparent access to such data allows stakeholders to understand how policy shifts, such as tax changes or regulatory adjustments, truly impact aggregate performance beyond official projections. Future volatility may arise from proposed tariffs, which could disrupt production costs and trade flows. Therefore, maintaining accessible, precise open data infrastructure is essential to track these potential disruptions. It ensures that researchers and citizens can independently verify the long-term effects of protectionist measures on economic growth and social welfare.
