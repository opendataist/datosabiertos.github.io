La economía alemana cierra 2024 con un retroceso del 0,2% en su PIB

La economía alemana cierra 2024 con un retroceso del 0,2% en su PIB

Germany’s economy contracted slightly in late 2024, missing expectations despite higher domestic spending, due to a sharp drop in exports. This continues a period of stagnation after years of sluggish growth and annual contractions, underscoring persistent structural weaknesses and the impact of political uncertainty on economic performance. The government has lowered its 2025 growth forecasts, reflecting a serious economic situation worsened by the early dissolution of the governing coalition ahead of upcoming federal elections. Officials stress that incomplete policy implementation, caused by this political instability, has hampered efforts to achieve sustainable growth, creating an atmosphere of uncertainty for future economic decisions. This report is relevant to open data because it shows how transparent, official statistical releases enable public scrutiny of economic health and policy effectiveness. Accessible datasets allow analysts to verify government claims, understand structural trends beyond headline figures, and assess the real-world impact of political instability on national economic indicators.
