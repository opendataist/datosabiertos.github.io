Economist calls on Govt to take youth unemployment seriously

Economist calls on Govt to take youth unemployment seriously

Namibia faces a critical youth unemployment crisis that threatens social stability and economic progress. High joblessness among young people creates deep hopelessness, causing many to question the value of education and undermining future workforce development. This stagnation directly reduces government tax revenue, limiting the state's ability to fund essential services like healthcare and schooling. The situation is relevant to open_data because it highlights the urgent need for transparent, accessible labor statistics to drive policy change. While recent data shows a marginal decline in unemployment rates, the persistent majority of jobless youth reveals systemic failures. Open access to disaggregated labor data allows economists and the public to scrutinize these trends, fostering accountability and encouraging evidence-based interventions to address the root causes of this economic vulnerability.
