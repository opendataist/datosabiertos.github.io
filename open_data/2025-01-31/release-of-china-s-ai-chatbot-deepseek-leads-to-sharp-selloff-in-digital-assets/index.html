Release of China's AI chatbot DeepSeek leads to sharp selloff in digital assets

Release of China's AI chatbot DeepSeek leads to sharp selloff in digital assets

China’s DeepSeek demonstrates that efficient, low-cost open-source AI can outperform expensive proprietary models. This shift undermines the financial viability of high-cost AI infrastructure, triggering a sharp decline in major tech stocks and casting doubt on associated investment values. The resulting market correction significantly impacts the cryptocurrency sector, particularly blockchain-based AI projects and mining operations. Since crypto markets are heavily influenced by expectations of profitable AI and chip technologies, the perceived obsolescence of costly hardware reduces investor confidence and asset values. This development highlights the critical intersection between open data and digital finance. As open-source AI lowers barriers to entry, it forces a reevaluation of proprietary technology monopolies. Understanding these dynamics is essential for grasping how accessible AI innovations directly influence global capital flows and the stability of the broader open ecosystem.
