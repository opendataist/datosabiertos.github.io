Actualiza INFO CDMX Padrón de Sujetos Obligados

Actualiza INFO CDMX Padrón de Sujetos Obligados

INFO CDMX has updated the registry of entities subject to transparency and data protection laws to reflect recent institutional reforms in Mexico City. A key change involves removing the Water System entity due to its absorption by a new secretariat, while simultaneously incorporating a new fiscal stabilization trust. This trust is now mandated to manage resources for emergencies and fiscal balance, ensuring that public funds are administered with greater accountability and openness. Furthermore, the registry includes three new Evaluation Committees for the local government branches. These bodies are crucial for ensuring the integrity and transparency of judicial appointments, which fosters public trust in the rule of law. By officially recognizing these committees as obligated parties, the system ensures that their operations regarding the selection of judges are accessible and subject to public scrutiny, reinforcing democratic oversight within the judiciary. This update is highly relevant to open data because it defines the scope of entities responsible for disclosing information and protecting citizens' personal data. Accurate registries are foundational for open government initiatives, as they clarify who must provide data and how users can request it. The recent review of public information requests, largely favoring applicants, further demonstrates the effectiveness of these transparency mechanisms in holding authorities accountable and promoting a culture of open governance.
