OpenAirportMap es una especie de enciclopedia de aeropuertos con detallados mapas y datos abiertos

Open Airport Map demonstrates the power of open data by integrating diverse aviation infrastructure details with OpenStreetMap. This initiative transforms raw technical specifications, such as runway layouts and navigation aids, into accessible visual resources. By making this information publicly available, it empowers enthusiasts and professionals to understand complex airport operations without relying on proprietary sources. The project resolves real-world mysteries by providing precise, verified geographic data. For instance, it identified obscure structures near Madrid-Barajas as non-directional beacons, proving how open databases can clarify ambiguities found in physical environments. This capability highlights the practical value of detailed, community-driven mapping in solving localized questions through global data networks. This resource is vital for open data because it showcases how combining multiple open datasets creates comprehensive, usable tools. It encourages transparency in specialized fields like aviation infrastructure and inspires similar projects in other sectors. By democratizing access to niche technical information, it fosters innovation and allows users to verify facts independently, strengthening the overall ecosystem of shared knowledge.
