Dirección General de Ética representa RD en diferentes actividades internacionales

The Dominican Republic has been officially accredited as a member of the Open Government Partnership, recognizing the country’s sustained efforts in the fight against corruption. This international achievement reflects a deep institutional commitment to transparency and public integrity, positioning the nation as a key player in global democratic governance forums. The success of this membership is grounded in robust regulatory frameworks, highlighted by the creation of the First National Open Data Policy. This initiative not only ensures public access to information but also establishes a multisectoral structure for opening data formats, facilitating citizen participation and fostering trust between the government and society. This approach is fundamental to the field of open data, as it demonstrates how open data policy operates as a concrete mechanism for accountability and corruption prevention. By standardizing access to public information, it empowers civil society to monitor state management, validating that technological and legal transparency is indispensable for strengthening democratic institutions and restoring public trust in government administration.
