The sharp increase in tomato prices underscores significant market distortions driven by excessive intermediary margins. These intermediaries inflate consumer costs by up to 200% compared to the prices farmers actually receive. This disparity highlights critical gaps in transparency within agricultural supply chains. Such data is essential for open data initiatives that aim to monitor market fairness, verify price accuracy, and support informed policy decisions on trade efficiency.
