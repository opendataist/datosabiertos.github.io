Hipertextual S.L.’s privacy policy establishes a formal framework for the responsible management of user information, clearly identifying the data controller and the legal bases that legitimize the processing of personal data. This structure ensures regulatory compliance, guaranteeing that users understand who is responsible for their information and under which specific circumstances—such as contract execution or legitimate interest—their personal data are processed. The policy details the specific purposes for data use, highlighting the collection of cookies and navigation metrics through third-party services, primarily for statistical and advertising purposes. This transparency regarding tracking tools and external providers is essential, as it reveals how user traffic and behavior are analyzed to optimize the service experience and target commercial campaigns—a common practice on today’s web. This document is relevant to open data because it illustrates the opposite side of the equation: the ethical and legal management of closed data. By clearly outlining users’ rights, such as access, rectification, and data portability, it serves as an example of how organizations should balance the commercial use of information with individual privacy. This clarity is a prerequisite for any data ecosystem aspiring to be transparent and trustworthy, even when the data in question are not open by definition.
