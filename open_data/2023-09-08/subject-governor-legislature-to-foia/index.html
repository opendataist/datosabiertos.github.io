Subject governor, Legislature to FOIA

This article highlights the persistent failure to subject the governor and legislature to transparency laws, despite repeated promises. It emphasizes that government operations funded by taxpayers must remain open to public scrutiny to ensure accountability. The piece argues that current exemptions allow officials to hide deliberations, undermining the public’s right to know. Pending bills aim to rectify this by forcing the disclosure of records, though they have stalled, leaving a significant gap in democratic oversight. Relevance to open data lies in the demand for accessible public records, including emails and memos. Enacting these laws would institutionalize open data principles, ensuring that critical governmental information is readily available for public analysis and engagement rather than being withheld by those in power.
