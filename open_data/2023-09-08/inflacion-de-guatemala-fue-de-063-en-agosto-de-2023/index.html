Inflación de Guatemala fue de 0,63% en agosto de 2023

Guatemala’s Consumer Price Index indicates a significant slowdown in inflation, marking a crucial shift toward greater economic stability. This deceleration reflects improved macroeconomic management compared to previous years, creating a more predictable environment for both local businesses and consumers. The data highlights specific sectors driving these trends, with food and transport costs experiencing modest increases, while leisure expenses declined. Understanding these granular shifts is essential for analyzing household purchasing power and identifying emerging market pressures in the region. This report is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how public statistical institutions ensure transparency in critical economic metrics. By providing accessible, detailed inflation data, the National Institute of Statistics (INE) enables researchers and developers to build tools that monitor economic health and support informed policy decisions.
