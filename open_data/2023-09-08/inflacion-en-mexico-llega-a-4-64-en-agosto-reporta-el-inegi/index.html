Inflación en México llega a 4.64% en agosto, reporta el Inegi

In Mexico, inflation continues to decelerate, with underlying prices reaching their lowest levels since late 2021. This sustained downward trend signals progress in price stability, although authorities emphasize that significant work remains to fully normalize economic conditions. This data is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of transparent, regularly updated statistical indicators such as those published by INEGI. Such accessible public data enables accurate economic analysis and helps build trust in institutional transparency. Ultimately, the consistent publication of detailed inflation metrics supports evidence-based decision-making. By making these figures widely available, citizens and analysts can better understand market dynamics and assess the effectiveness of monetary policies.
