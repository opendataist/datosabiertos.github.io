Delta County to charge for FOIA requests

Delta County implements FOIA fees to offset rising administrative costs driven by a surge in public requests. This shift highlights the operational strain on government entities managing large volumes of data. The decision underscores the critical need for efficient open data systems to maintain transparency while ensuring fiscal sustainability.
