AI foundational models to disclose training data source under new bill: Report

New legislation mandates AI developers disclose training data sources, empowering copyright holders to verify usage. This transparency measure seeks to resolve ongoing infringement disputes by clarifying how foundational models are built. The bill directs federal agencies to establish rigorous reporting standards for data origins and safety protocols. By aligning with the executive order, it aims to protect privacy and civil rights while promoting accountable AI development. This initiative is vital for open data because it demands accountability in the training processes of large models. It bridges the gap between proprietary development and public interest, ensuring that the data fueling AI advancements respects legal boundaries and intellectual property rights.
