New Bill Proposes Disclosure of Copyrighted Training Data by AI Companies for Transparency

The proposed AI Foundation Model Transparency Act mandates that developers disclose the origins of their training data, directly addressing persistent legal challenges regarding copyright infringement. This legislative push aims to resolve conflicts between creators and tech giants by ensuring clarity on how copyrighted materials are utilized during the model training phase. Beyond copyright, the bill requires comprehensive reporting on model risks, computational resources, and safety evaluations. These measures are designed to protect vulnerable populations from inaccurate or biased outputs in critical sectors like healthcare and finance, aligning with broader federal efforts to establish standardized AI risk management frameworks. This legislation is vital to open data discourse as it attempts to balance proprietary development with public accountability. By demanding transparency in training data and model behavior, it sets a precedent for how open, accessible, and ethical AI systems can be governed, potentially influencing future standards for data usage and model explainability in the open source community.
