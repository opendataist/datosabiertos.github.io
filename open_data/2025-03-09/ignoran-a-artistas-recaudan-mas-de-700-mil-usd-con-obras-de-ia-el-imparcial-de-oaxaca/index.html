Ignoran a artistas, recaudan más de 700 mil USD con obras de IA - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

Ignoran a artistas, recaudan más de 700 mil USD con obras de IA - El Imparcial de Oaxaca

This auction highlights the growing tension between traditional artists and emerging AI technologies, sparking intense debate over copyright infringement and the ethical use of creative data. The controversy centers on whether AI models are legitimately enhancing human creativity or exploiting protected works without proper licensing—a critical issue for transparency in open datasets. The event’s commercial success, driven by younger demographics, signals a shift in how art is valued and consumed, validating the integration of technology into creative practices. However, this acceptance raises urgent questions about data provenance and the rights of original creators, whose work often trains these systems without consent. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity for clear ethical guidelines regarding the sourcing and use of training materials. To ensure fairness and sustainability within the open ecosystem, stakeholders must address the lack of transparency in how creative works are harvested, processed, and monetized by AI developers.
