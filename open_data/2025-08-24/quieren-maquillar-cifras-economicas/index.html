¡Quieren maquillar cifras económicas! %

¡Quieren maquillar cifras económicas! %

The article highlights a critical threat to Peru’s statistical integrity, where political pressure from the Prime Minister’s Office attempts to manipulate inflation-adjusted growth figures released by the National Institute of Statistics and Informatics (INEI). This interference undermines the agency’s mandate to provide objective data, suggesting that economic results are being artificially inflated to serve political narratives rather than reflect true economic conditions. Accurate statistics are the foundation for effective public policy and poverty reduction strategies. When official data is compromised or biased, government planning becomes unreliable, potentially hindering national development and exacerbating social inequalities. The credibility of these numbers is essential for informed decision-making across all levels of government, ensuring that resources are allocated efficiently to those who need them most. This situation is vital for the open data community because it illustrates the dangers of political intervention in statistical independence. Transparency and objectivity are core tenets of open data; when governments alter data to fit specific agendas, they erode public trust and prevent civil society from holding leaders accountable. Protecting data integrity ensures that information remains a public good, empowering citizens with the truth needed to drive democratic progress and equitable development.
