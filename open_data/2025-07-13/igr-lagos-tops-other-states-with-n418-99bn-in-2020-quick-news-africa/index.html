IGR: Lagos tops other states with N418.99bn in 2020 - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

IGR: Lagos tops other states with N418.99bn in 2020 - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

Lagos State demonstrated remarkable fiscal resilience in 2020, leading all Nigerian states in Internally Generated Revenue despite the economic disruptions caused by the global pandemic and local civil unrest. While the national aggregate revenue saw a slight decline compared to the previous year, Lagos managed to increase its earnings significantly, contributing nearly a third of the total state-level revenue nationwide. This performance highlights the state’s robust economic engine and effective tax administration capabilities relative to other regions. The state’s revenue composition reveals a heavy reliance on direct taxation, particularly Pay As You Earn taxes, which formed the bulk of the total. However, significant contributions also came from direct assessments, road taxes, and revenue generated by various government ministries, departments, and agencies. This diversified internal revenue structure suggests a mature fiscal system capable of sustaining high revenue levels even when external economic conditions deteriorate, providing a model for other states seeking to broaden their financial bases. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives as it illustrates the disparity in financial capacity across different jurisdictions, which directly impacts public service delivery and infrastructure development. Transparent publication of such revenue metrics allows citizens and researchers to hold governments accountable for their financial management and resource allocation. By making this granular financial data accessible, stakeholders can better analyze governance efficiency, understand regional economic health, and advocate for more equitable resource distribution based on verifiable, open fiscal records.
