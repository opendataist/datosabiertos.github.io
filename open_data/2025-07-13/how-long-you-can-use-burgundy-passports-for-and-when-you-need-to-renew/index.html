How long you can use Burgundy passports for and when you need to renew

The article clarifies that pre-Brexit burgundy UK passports remain valid for travel until 2025, provided they hold sufficient remaining validity for the intended destination. This guidance alleviates immediate concerns for citizens holding older documents, emphasizing that renewal is only necessary when a passport expires or lacks the required validity period for specific international trips. The transition to blue passports affects only new applications, ensuring continuity for existing valid credentials. Regarding the application process, standard processing times are generally three weeks, though delays can occur if additional information or interviews are required. For travelers facing imminent departures, expedited options such as premium or fast-track services are available at an extra cost. These faster routes require in-person appointments and surrendering old passports, resulting in collection within four hours or couriered delivery within one week, respectively.
