La inflación en Francia bajó al 0,7 % en mayo, la más baja desde febrero de 2021 - Mundo - ABC Color

La inflación en Francia bajó al 0,7 % en mayo, la más baja desde febrero de 2021 - Mundo - ABC Color

France’s underlying inflation fell to 1.1%, the lowest level since early 2021, driven by slower increases in service prices and cheaper energy, despite rising food costs. This decline signals easing core inflationary pressures, with manufactured goods maintaining slight deflation. The harmonized index also dropped significantly, reflecting broader European trends. This data is relevant to open data initiatives by demonstrating the availability of granular, official economic indicators. It highlights how transparent statistics enable researchers to track precise inflation dynamics and assess changes in purchasing power across sectors.
