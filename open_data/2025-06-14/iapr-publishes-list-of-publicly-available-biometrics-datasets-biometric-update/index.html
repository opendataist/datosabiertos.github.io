IAPR publishes list of publicly available biometrics datasets | Biometric Update

The IAPR has launched a centralized repository for public biometric datasets, supporting the research community by simplifying data discovery across physiological and behavioral traits. This initiative promotes open access to critical resources, enabling more transparent and reproducible biometric studies worldwide. By structuring these assets, the project significantly advances open data availability in pattern recognition.
