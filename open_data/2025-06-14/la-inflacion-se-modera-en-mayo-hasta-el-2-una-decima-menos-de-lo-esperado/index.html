La inflación se modera en mayo hasta el 2%, una décima menos de lo esperado

La inflación se modera en mayo hasta el 2%, una décima menos de lo esperado

Spain’s general inflation has fallen to its lowest level since October, primarily driven by cheaper travel packages and slower electricity price growth compared to the previous year. This moderation marks the third consecutive drop, reflecting favorable conditions in the tourism and transport sectors, which have significantly eased price pressures across leisure and cultural categories. Core inflation has also declined, though slightly more than anticipated, and remains above the general inflation rate. While some food prices have risen due to seasonal fruit costs, the overall trend shows a broad deceleration in consumer prices. Regional variations persist, with several autonomous communities exceeding the European Central Bank’s target, highlighting uneven economic impacts across the country despite the national average. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it underscores the necessity of high-quality, granular statistical releases. Transparent access to detailed indices allows analysts and citizens to verify official narratives, understand regional disparities, and independently assess economic health. Reliable open data fosters accountability and enables deeper, evidence-based discussions on public policy and monetary strategy, without relying solely on aggregated summaries.
