Inflation in Argentina reaches 6% in January

Argentina’s January inflation hit 6%, marking its highest monthly rate since 1991 and driving annual inflation near 99%. This surge, fueled by rising utility and food costs, highlights severe economic instability rooted in persistent currency depreciation. Annual inflation has remained in double digits for over a decade, with previous years showing rates far exceeding 50%. These figures reflect a long-term trend of price erosion that significantly impacts purchasing power and economic planning for citizens and businesses alike. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the need for transparent, accessible statistical reporting. Reliable open datasets enable better analysis of inflation trends, helping civil society and researchers hold governments accountable and propose evidence-based solutions to chronic economic challenges.
