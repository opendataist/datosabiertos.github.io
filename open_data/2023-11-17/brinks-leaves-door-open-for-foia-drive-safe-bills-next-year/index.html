Brinks leaves door open for FOIA, Drive SAFE bills next year

Michigan lawmakers adjourned without passing legislation granting undocumented residents driver’s licenses. Despite significant community advocacy and historical delays, political consensus remains elusive due to ongoing debates regarding legal residency requirements for identification. This stalemate highlights the critical role of civic engagement in shaping equitable access to public resources. For open data advocates, these policy gaps underscore the need for transparent, accessible records that validate residency status, ensuring marginalized communities are accurately represented in official datasets and can fully participate in society.
