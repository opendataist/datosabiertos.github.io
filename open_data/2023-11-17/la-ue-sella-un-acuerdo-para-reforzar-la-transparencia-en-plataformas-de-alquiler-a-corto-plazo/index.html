La UE sella un acuerdo para reforzar la transparencia en plataformas de alquiler a corto plazo

The EU has agreed on new rules requiring short-term rental platforms to automatically share user data with authorities. This aims to combat illegal activities and build trust, ensuring that communities have the information needed for sustainable housing development. This legislation is crucial for open data, as it establishes mandatory, automated data flows from private platforms to public regulators. It demonstrates how transparent information exchange can effectively enforce regulations and protect local markets. By setting clear standards for data transmission, the EU sets a global precedent. This approach underscores the importance of accessible, standardized data in fostering accountability and supporting informed decision-making in the sharing economy.
