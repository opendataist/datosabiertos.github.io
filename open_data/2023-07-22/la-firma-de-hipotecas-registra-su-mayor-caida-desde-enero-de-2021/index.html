La firma de hipotecas registra su mayor caída desde enero de 2021

In May, new mortgage applications saw a significant decline, marking the sharpest drop since early 2021, driven by rising interest rates aimed at curbing inflation. This trend, which underscores the increasing cost of financing for homebuyers, has persisted for four consecutive months. The data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, official statistics enable the public and researchers to analyze and understand complex economic shifts and their impacts on housing affordability.
