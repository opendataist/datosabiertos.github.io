RTI Commission Unveils 5-Year Strategic Plan | - Ghanamma.com

RTI Commission Unveils 5-Year Strategic Plan | - Ghanamma.com

The Right to Information Commission has launched a five-year strategic plan titled “Pathway to Open and Transparent Governance.” This initiative marks a significant shift from short-term operational goals to a medium-term framework designed to fundamentally transform how public institutions handle information. By focusing on long-term objectives, the plan aims to institutionalize a culture of openness, ensuring that access to government data becomes a sustainable practice rather than an occasional concession. Central to this strategy is the empowerment of citizens through enhanced transparency and accountability. The Commission intends to build an informed populace capable of actively engaging with their government, thereby fostering greater trust between state entities and the public. This approach emphasizes that accessible information is not merely a bureaucratic requirement but a critical tool for democratic participation, allowing citizens to hold public officials responsible for their actions and decisions. This development is highly relevant to open_data as it demonstrates a governmental commitment to systematic information disclosure. The plan’s focus on progressive implementation and stakeholder collaboration suggests a move toward more robust, reliable, and continuous data availability. For the open data community, this strategic vision signals a potential improvement in the quality and frequency of public data releases, encouraging broader civic engagement and more effective use of open government resources.
