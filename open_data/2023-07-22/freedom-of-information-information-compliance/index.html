Freedom of Information | Information Compliance

The Freedom of Information Act mandates that the University of Cambridge operates as a transparent public authority. This legal framework ensures the institution regularly publishes essential information and responds to individual data requests, balancing public access with valid exemptions. By adhering to a standardized publication scheme and meeting strict response deadlines, the university guarantees timely and accessible information delivery. This process requires providing helpful guidance and accommodating specific format preferences, thereby fostering an environment of open communication and accountability. This article is crucial for open data enthusiasts as it outlines the legal basis for accessing institutional records. Understanding these obligations helps advocates push for broader transparency, ensuring that higher education institutions do not just meet minimum legal standards but actively promote the availability of their data to the public.
