Según INEGI, xalapeños se sienten más seguros

Según INEGI, xalapeños se sienten más seguros

The survey reveals a significant improvement in the perception of public safety across three cities in Veracruz, particularly in Xalapa and Coatzacoalcos. This shift indicates that localized data collection can effectively track and validate the impact of policies on citizens’ sense of security, highlighting the power of open government metrics. Conversely, the data identifies regions with persistently high levels of insecurity, contrasting them with areas considered the safest nationwide. Such comparative transparency allows stakeholders to benchmark progress and allocate resources more strategically based on empirical evidence rather than anecdotal reports. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how standardized, accessible statistics empower citizens and policymakers to make informed decisions. By making such detailed surveys publicly available, governments foster accountability and enable third-party analysis that drives meaningful social improvements through data-driven insights.
