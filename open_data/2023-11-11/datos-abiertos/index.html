Datos Abiertos

Local news outlets aggregate daily updates on provincial issues, prioritizing immediate regional awareness over open data principles. This model relies on curated editorial summaries, restricting access to the raw, structured data necessary for transparency and independent analysis. As a result, such platforms highlight the gap between traditional reporting and open data initiatives, which demand granular, reusable public information for societal benefit.
