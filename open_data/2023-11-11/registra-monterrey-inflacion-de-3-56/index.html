Registra Monterrey inflación de 3.56%

The article highlights a notable deceleration in Mexico’s overall annual inflation, driven primarily by significant price drops in agricultural products such as fruits and vegetables. This sectoral decline effectively offset upward pressures from energy costs and persistent underlying inflation, leading analysts to revise their annual forecasts downward. Despite this general easing, underlying services remain a concern, indicating that the broader disinflationary trend faces challenges in sustaining its momentum. Regionally, Monterrey experienced a lower inflation rate compared to the previous month, yet it still exceeded the national average. Specific categories such as health, other services, and food showed higher annual increases, contributing to localized price pressures that diverge from the national downward trajectory. This disparity underscores how national aggregates can mask significant regional variations and sector-specific inflationary trends that require closer monitoring.
