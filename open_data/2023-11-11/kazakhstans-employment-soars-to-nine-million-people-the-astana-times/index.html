Kazakhstan’s Employment Soars to Nine Million People - The Astana Times

The 2021 census highlights significant growth in Kazakhstan’s workforce, driven by urbanization and increased female participation. Education levels are rising, while the labor market shifts toward services and retail, reflecting broader economic transformations. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it reveals emerging social and economic trends. By making detailed demographic and employment statistics accessible, researchers and policymakers can better analyze workforce dynamics and inform future development strategies. Ultimately, transparent access to such granular census data empowers stakeholders to understand structural changes in the economy. This supports evidence-based decision-making and fosters greater accountability in public sector planning.
