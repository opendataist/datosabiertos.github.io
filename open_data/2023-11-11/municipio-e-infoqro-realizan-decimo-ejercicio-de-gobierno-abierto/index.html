Municipio e Infoqro realizan décimo ejercicio de Gobierno Abierto

Municipio e Infoqro realizan décimo ejercicio de Gobierno Abierto

The municipality implemented participatory governance, enabling residents to co-design public infrastructure. This initiative fosters community identity and ensures that urban projects align with citizens' needs and available budgets. This process underscores the importance of open data by demonstrating how transparent, inclusive decision-making integrates local knowledge. It shows that sharing design and budget information empowers communities to validate and select public works, thereby enhancing civic trust. Furthermore, collaboration with university engineers highlights the value of open technical data in public planning. By making design constraints and costs visible, the government enables informed citizen participation, strengthening the connection between transparency and effective democratic engagement.
