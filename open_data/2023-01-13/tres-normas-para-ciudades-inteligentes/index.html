Tres normas para ciudades inteligentes

Tres normas para ciudades inteligentes

AENOR has established a comprehensive framework for smart cities by publishing three key standards focused on open data, asset management, and urban indicators. The publication of these standards represents a strategic step toward standardized governance, ensuring that municipalities can adopt uniform methodologies to enhance transparency and efficiency. This harmonization is essential for fostering interoperability among various local entities. The first pillar focuses on asset management, providing specifications for optimizing the lifecycle of urban infrastructure while reducing costs. By setting clear requirements for maintaining accurate inventories, local governments can improve long-term planning and operational sustainability. This approach transforms raw data into actionable insights, enabling authorities to make informed decisions regarding resource allocation and risk mitigation without compromising fiscal responsibility. The second pillar addresses urban sustainability through internationally recognized indicators that measure service performance and quality of life. This standard allows for comparable and verifiable assessments of a city’s progress, promoting accountability. For the open data community, these regulations are crucial, as they require the structured disclosure of public information. They establish the technical foundation necessary for citizens and developers to access, analyze, and effectively utilize municipal data, thereby driving innovation and civic engagement.
