Venezuela, Colombia y Perú son los mayores solicitantes de asilo en España - Noticiero Digital

In 2022, Venezuela, Colombia, and Peru were the main countries of origin for asylum seekers in Spain, marking the highest number of applications since the office’s creation. While most requests were rejected, a significant portion received protection due to specific regional crises, particularly affecting Venezuelans under UNHCR guidelines. The data highlights Spain’s role as a major destination for displaced persons, notably ranking fifth in the EU for temporary protection grants to Ukrainian refugees. This surge underscores the country's capacity to handle mass displacement through urgent mechanisms established by Brussels. This report is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how transparent government statistics enable the tracking of migration trends and policy effectiveness. Accessible official data allows researchers and civil society to analyze asylum outcomes, understand humanitarian needs, and hold institutions accountable for their integration and protection strategies.
