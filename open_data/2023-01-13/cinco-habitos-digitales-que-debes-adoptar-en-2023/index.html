Cinco hábitos digitales que debes adoptar en 2023

The article concludes that digital security is not a static state, but a continuous process that requires the deliberate adoption of appropriate technological habits to effectively protect personal data and privacy. In an environment where data breaches are frequent, user proactivity is essential to mitigate risks and maintain control over one’s digital footprint in the face of growing threats. Effective protection relies on the integration of specialized tools, such as password managers with automatic breach notifications, the use of virtual private networks (VPNs) to encrypt connections, and the secure storage of sensitive documents. These technical solutions simplify the management of digital identity, ensuring that critical information remains encrypted and accessible only through a master key, thereby eliminating the vulnerabilities associated with traditional storage methods or the reuse of credentials. This information is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of data sovereignty and the protection of individual data—principles fundamental to the debate on transparency and open access. By emphasizing that privacy and security require robust tools and ongoing education, the text highlights that the release of public data must be balanced with mechanisms that safeguard the integrity and confidentiality of sensitive information, preventing openness from becoming a vulnerability.
