España conoce su dato del IPC con el que cerró 2022: ¿seguirá creciendo la inflación subyacente?

The article reports a persistent inflation trend in Spain, with underlying prices rising significantly and food costs remaining high despite a general moderation in overall price increases. This disparity highlights a notable decline in purchasing power, as wage growth has lagged behind inflation rates for two decades, leaving households financially strained. The impact is particularly acute because immediate government measures, such as tax reductions on food and fuel subsidies, are not yet reflected in the current data. Consequently, consumers face a reality where essential goods continue to escalate, reinforcing concerns about economic stability and the immediate future of household budgets. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible economic indicators. By monitoring raw inflation metrics alongside policy changes, researchers and citizens can better analyze the real impact of fiscal decisions on living standards, ensuring that digital governance tools effectively serve the public interest.
