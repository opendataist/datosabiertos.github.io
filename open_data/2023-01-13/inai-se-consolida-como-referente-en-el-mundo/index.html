INAI se consolida como referente en el mundo

INAI se consolida como referente en el mundo

The INAI consolidates its status as an international reference in transparency, highlighting its leadership in global forums and its commitment to bilateral and multilateral cooperation. This position enables it to influence policy agendas and strengthen the exchange of best practices with civil society and other key stakeholders. The relevance to open data lies in the fact that these international spaces drive new models of public information governance. By sharing challenges and advancements, the institute promotes standards that facilitate access to information, which are essential for transparency and accountability in the digital age.
