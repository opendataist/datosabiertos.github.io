INEC final list: North-West, S’West top 93 million voter register

INEC final list: North-West, S’West top 93 million voter register

The Independent National Electoral Commission has finalized Nigeria’s voter register, confirming a significant expansion of the electorate compared to previous elections. This updated database reflects a massive influx of new registrations and extensive data cleansing, ensuring that the list used for the upcoming polls is both current and credible. The commission has firmly rejected rumors of postponement, asserting that the logistical preparations are complete and that the elections will proceed strictly according to the established timetable. A critical implication of this data is the noticeable gender disparity in political participation, with men significantly outnumbering women on the final list. This gap highlights systemic challenges, including political violence and a lack of institutional support, which have historically discouraged female engagement in the electoral process. Stakeholders argue that this underrepresentation undermines democratic inclusivity and risks neglecting issues that disproportionately affect women, underscoring the need for targeted interventions to create a safer, more encouraging environment for female voters and candidates. This development is highly relevant to open_data because it demonstrates the practical application of transparent, citizen-verified datasets in strengthening democratic integrity. By publishing the register for public scrutiny and allowing objections, the electoral body utilized open data principles to enhance accountability and trust. Furthermore, the commitment to making this comprehensive breakdown accessible to political parties and the public via digital platforms serves as a vital example of how accessible data infrastructure can support fair, informed, and conflict-resistant electoral processes.
