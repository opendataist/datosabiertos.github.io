NTT DATA recibe una calificación «A» en materia de transparencia sobre el cambio climático

CDP has recognized NTT DATA’s leadership in corporate transparency and the fight against climate change, awarding it a high score for its comprehensive risk management and ambitious environmental goals. This evaluation process, which serves as a global benchmark, reflects the growing pressure from investors and buyers for clear data on climate impacts, establishing disclosure as an essential standard for modern organizations. CDP’s assessment values not only the disclosure of information but also the demonstration of best practices linked to environmental leadership, such as the implementation of meaningful targets and the adoption of scientific standards. By rating companies based on their preparedness and concrete actions, the system encourages corporations to go beyond basic compliance, fostering a culture of accountability that prioritizes carbon neutrality and proactive mitigation of environmental impacts across the entire supply chain. This distinction is relevant to open data because it illustrates how structured and accessible environmental data enable accountability and informed decision-making in the market. Transparency facilitated by platforms like CDP demonstrates that open access to verifiable information on climate risks and strategies is essential for driving global collaboration, reducing emissions, and achieving sustainability, thereby turning data disclosure into a strategic tool for transformative change.
