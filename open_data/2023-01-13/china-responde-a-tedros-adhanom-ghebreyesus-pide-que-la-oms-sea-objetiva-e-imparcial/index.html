China responde a Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus, pide que la OMS sea ‘objetiva’ e ‘imparcial’

China asserts its commitment to transparency by sharing pandemic data and collaborating closely with the World Health Organization to support global vaccine and drug development. The spokesperson emphasizes ongoing technical exchanges and bilateral meetings, urging an objective analysis of China’s response while highlighting the negligible epidemiological impact on Europe. Despite international skepticism regarding recent casualty figures after policy shifts, Chinese authorities maintain that their actions have significantly contributed to worldwide health security and effective crisis management. They continue to deepen cooperation with international bodies to ensure broader protection and scientific rationality in handling the ongoing situation. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical tension between state-controlled narratives and the imperative for accurate, unrestricted data flows in global health crises. It underscores how transparency, or the lack thereof, directly impacts international trust and the efficacy of coordinated public health responses, illustrating the real-world stakes of open data principles in preventing and managing pandemics.
