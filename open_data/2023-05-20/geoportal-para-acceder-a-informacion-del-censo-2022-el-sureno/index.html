Geoportal para acceder a información del Censo 2022 - El Sureño

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) has launched a new geoportal that integrates provisional data from the 2022 Census. Developed with the support of ECLAC, this georeferenced application enables users to visualize population and housing statistics mapped across Argentina’s territorial divisions. It serves as a central hub for accessing detailed demographic insights linked to specific geographic locations. The platform facilitates the comparison of statistical data against various geographical layers, such as infrastructure, climate, and environmental factors. Users can customize visualizations, download datasets in multiple formats, and analyze trends by selecting specific indicators and geographic scopes. This functionality supports complex spatial analysis, allowing researchers and policymakers to contextualize statistical outcomes within broader geographical and environmental frameworks. This initiative is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it democratizes access to official census information. By providing an interactive, downloadable, and interoperable tool, INDEC enhances transparency and encourages civic engagement. The open availability of these geospatial datasets empowers third parties to conduct independent analyses, fostering innovation and evidence-based decision-making across both the public and private sectors.
