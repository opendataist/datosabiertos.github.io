Inscritas 640 mujeres en preparatoria abierta | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Piedras Negras successfully enrolled hundreds of women in the second cohort of its open high school program, while university enrollment continues to grow. This initiative demonstrates how local government can remove barriers to women’s education by providing accessible online degree programs and community support centers. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of publicly reporting enrollment metrics. Transparent data enables citizens and policymakers to assess the reach and effectiveness of social programs, ensuring accountability and encouraging future participation in gender-focused educational opportunities. By sharing these figures, the municipality fosters a culture of openness. It shows that when governments make program details and outcomes visible, they can better serve their communities and inspire other regions to adopt similar inclusive strategies for women’s professional development and lifelong learning.
