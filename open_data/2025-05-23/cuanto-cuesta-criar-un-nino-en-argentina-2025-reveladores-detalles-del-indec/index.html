Cuánto cuesta criar un niño en Argentina 2025: reveladores detalles del Indec

Cuánto cuesta criar un niño en Argentina 2025: reveladores detalles del Indec

The INDEC report reveals that raising a child in Argentina entails significant monthly expenses, calculated by combining the cost of essential goods and services with the monetary value of care time required. By segmenting costs across four age brackets based on schooling levels, the study highlights that financial burdens are not uniform but evolve as children grow, reflecting specific developmental needs and increased reliance on formal education and external care services. A crucial insight from this analysis is the divergence between the growth rate of the child-rearing basket and general inflation. The cost of raising children increased substantially less than the overall inflation rate over the comparative period, indicating a structural decoupling. This suggests that while general prices soared, the specific economic pressure of raising children did not escalate at the same magnitude, offering a nuanced perspective on household economic resilience during periods of high inflation. This article is vital for open data because it demonstrates how official statistics can integrate diverse, often informal, economic variables—such as unpaid care work—into measurable indicators. It showcases the power of transparent, granular datasets to inform social policy, allowing researchers and citizens to analyze inequality and family welfare beyond traditional poverty lines. By making these complex calculations public, it fosters greater accountability and a deeper understanding of the true cost of social reproduction in the national economy.
