Confirma Inegi: México alcanzó a crecer 0.2% en 1T con fuerza agropecuaria y de servicios

Confirma Inegi: México alcanzó a crecer 0.2% en 1T con fuerza agropecuaria y de servicios

The Mexican economy narrowly avoided a technical recession in the first quarter, posting a minimal real growth rate of 0.2%. This result aligns with early institutional estimates but falls slightly short of broader private-sector forecasts. Although the country escaped a two-quarter contraction pattern, the marginal expansion underscores an economy in stagnation rather than robust recovery, signaling that the immediate risk of recession has passed but significant fragility remains. The growth structure reveals a critical weakness: the modest increase was driven almost exclusively by the primary sector, such as agriculture, which carries a relatively small weight in national GDP. Conversely, the secondary and tertiary sectors, which typically contribute the majority of economic value and employment, showed stagnation or slight declines. Experts warn that relying on volatile primary-sector indicators provides a weak foundation, suggesting that current optimism is unwarranted given the underlying softness in services and manufacturing. This report is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the importance of granular, component-level statistical analysis in assessing economic health. The disparity between the aggregate growth figure and sectoral performance demonstrates how high-level indicators can mask structural imbalances. For open data initiatives, this underscores the necessity of publishing detailed, accessible breakdowns by economic activity, enabling analysts and the public to look beyond headline numbers and understand the true dynamics and vulnerabilities of the economy.

Source: eleconomista.com.mx
