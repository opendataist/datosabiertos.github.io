Exclusive: NYS data reveals alarming crash trend on 'death trap' stretch of Route 6 in Orange County

Exclusive: NYS data reveals alarming crash trend on 'death trap' stretch of Route 6 in Orange County

A Freedom of Information Request revealed that a significant segment of Route 6 in Orange County is among the most dangerous highways in New York state. The data highlights a severe traffic safety crisis, characterized by frequent accidents, fatalities, and serious injuries, largely due to high speeds and distracted driving on sections lacking protective barriers. Local officials and emergency responders emphasize that the current infrastructure conditions are unacceptable and pose a critical risk to commuters. Despite the community’s limited resources to address these hazards alone, there is a strong urgent call for concerted efforts from state authorities, specifically the Department of Transportation, to implement concrete safety enhancements and reduce the alarming frequency of collisions. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public access to government-held statistics can expose hidden public safety risks. By obtaining and analyzing this official data, media outlets can hold agencies accountable, drive policy changes, and empower communities to demand improvements in infrastructure and road safety based on transparent, evidence-based findings.
