La rebelión de los periodistas contra la extracción de contenidos por la IA

La rebelión de los periodistas contra la extracción de contenidos por la IA

The Argentine publishing company Editorial Perfil has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI and Microsoft, accusing them of misusing copyrighted journalistic content and engaging in unfair competition. This case represents a significant milestone, as it marks the first time a Spanish-language publisher has taken legal action of this kind, joining international precedents that seek to establish a new compensation framework for journalism in the face of tech giants. The dispute rekindles the debate over intellectual property and the sustainability of the media sector, highlighting how artificial intelligence models rely on third-party data for training without necessarily sharing the resulting benefits. This dynamic not only deprives media outlets of traffic and advertising revenue but also distorts competition by allowing AI platforms to bypass access barriers, such as paywalls, thereby consolidating an oligopolistic market to the detriment of original creators. The relevance to open data lies in the urgency of balancing open access with intellectual property rights. This conflict underscores the need to create regulatory frameworks that recognize the value of journalistic data, preventing the mass extraction of information from undermining the quality of news and the economic viability of journalism, which are essential for an informed citizenry.
