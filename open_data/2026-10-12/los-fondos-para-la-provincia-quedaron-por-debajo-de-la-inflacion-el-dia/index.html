Los fondos para la Provincia quedaron por debajo de la inflación | El Dia

Los fondos para la Provincia quedaron por debajo de la inflación | El Dia

The report reveals that automatic transfers to the province remained practically stable, demonstrating that the national government lacks discretion to restrict these funds through the revenue-sharing law. This stability shows that there was no significant loss of real resources, refuting claims of arbitrary defunding. The relevance for *open data* lies in how public access to fiscal data enables auditing of legal compliance and verification of political claims. By comparing official figures with inflation indicators, society can distinguish between nominal and real adjustments, fostering transparency and accountability in the management of state resources.
