CDMX alcanza 12 años de escolaridad promedio: Benito Juárez tiene el nivel más alto y Milpa Alta el más bajo

CDMX alcanza 12 años de escolaridad promedio: Benito Juárez tiene el nivel más alto y Milpa Alta el más bajo

Mexico City is consolidating its educational leadership by achieving an average years of schooling higher than the national average, demonstrating continuous progress compared to previous years. This result highlights the capital’s capacity to promote access and retention in higher academic levels, positioning it as a benchmark for human development in the country. However, transparency in these data reveals deep territorial inequalities among the different boroughs. While some areas exhibit very high education levels, others face significant challenges in access and educational quality, indicating that overall progress is not evenly distributed across the entire territory. This information is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it enables citizens and policymakers to identify specific structural gaps. By making these disaggregated indicators public, it promotes accountability and facilitates the design of more precise and inclusive public interventions aimed at closing educational disparities among the various boroughs.
