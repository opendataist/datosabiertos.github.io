Using AI to Advance Equity in Eye Care: Grayson W. Armstrong, MD, MPH | AJMC

Using AI to Advance Equity in Eye Care: Grayson W. Armstrong, MD, MPH | AJMC

This article highlights how AI can address health disparities by providing equitable eye care access for underserved populations. It emphasizes the necessity of inclusive training data and ethical policy frameworks to prevent bias and ensure tools reach under-resourced clinics. The integration of open data principles is crucial here, as transparent, diverse datasets allow AI models to generalize effectively across different demographic groups. Without such data openness, algorithms risk reinforcing existing inequalities rather than mitigating them, directly impacting patient outcomes. Successful deployment requires collaboration between technology developers and eye care professionals. The narrative underscores that technical solutions must be paired with human expertise and proper referral pathways to ensure sustainable implementation and genuine equity in medical diagnostics.
