Nvidia may be planning for more stake or acquisition deal for $25 billion in open-source AI startup

Nvidia is exploring a significant expansion of its stake in or complete acquisition of Reflection AI, an open-source AI startup. This move aims to integrate Reflection’s specialized machine-learning systems and engineering talent, potentially via an acqui-hire structure to mitigate regulatory hurdles. Reflection AI recently launched Beam, an open-weight model competing with other low-cost open alternatives. By integrating these open tools, Nvidia seeks to strengthen its end-to-end AI ecosystem and compete more effectively against global rivals offering open-source solutions. This development is crucial for open data as it highlights the growing corporate interest in acquiring and controlling open-weight models. It signals a trend where major tech firms leverage open-source innovation to build proprietary ecosystems, impacting how open technologies are governed, licensed, and integrated into commercial infrastructure.
