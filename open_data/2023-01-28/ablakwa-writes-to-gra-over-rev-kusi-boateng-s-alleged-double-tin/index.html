Ablakwa writes to GRA over Rev. Kusi Boateng's alleged double TIN

This case highlights critical vulnerabilities in open data systems, where distinct individuals might exploit regulatory loopholes to hold multiple tax identification numbers. The investigation underscores the urgent need for transparent, interconnected public registers to prevent identity fraud and ensure legal compliance. By leveraging right-to-information laws, the MP demands accountability from government institutions regarding administrative failures. This pressure exemplifies how citizen-led inquiries can force transparency in state-held records, revealing gaps in data integrity and enforcement mechanisms. Ultimately, this incident demonstrates that open data is not merely about accessibility but also about accuracy and cross-verification. It serves as a stark reminder that robust data governance is essential for maintaining public trust and preventing systemic abuse within national identification frameworks.
