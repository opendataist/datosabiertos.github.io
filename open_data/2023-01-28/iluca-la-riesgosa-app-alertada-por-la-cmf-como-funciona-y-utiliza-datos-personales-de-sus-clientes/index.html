ILuca, la riesgosa App alertada por la CMF: Cómo funciona y utiliza datos personales de sus clientes

iLuca operates as an unregulated financial entity, raising significant consumer protection concerns. The application collects extensive personal data, including private communications and location history, which has allegedly been used to harass borrowers and pressure them into repayment. This aggressive use of data underscores the critical need for transparent privacy standards and regulatory oversight in digital finance platforms. The incident highlights the dangers posed by unverified fintech services that circumvent traditional financial safeguards. By exploiting user data for coercive purposes, such apps erode trust in digital lending ecosystems. Regulators are urging the public to be aware of these risks to prevent further exploitation of vulnerable individuals seeking quick financial solutions. This case is highly relevant to open data discussions, as it illustrates the consequences of opaque data practices. It emphasizes the importance of clear, accessible privacy policies and the right to control one’s digital footprint. Ensuring that financial apps adhere to strict data governance can protect users and promote a fairer, more accountable open data environment within the financial sector.
