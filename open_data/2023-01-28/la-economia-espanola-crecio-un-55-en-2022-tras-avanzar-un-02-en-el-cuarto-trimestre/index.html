La economía española creció un 5,5% en 2022 tras avanzar un 0,2% en el cuarto trimestre

The Spanish economy demonstrated significant resilience in 2022, achieving its highest growth rates in nearly five decades despite a backdrop of global uncertainty and geopolitical conflicts. This performance surpassed official forecasts, indicating that the national economy has effectively recovered its pre-pandemic output levels. The robust expansion highlights the strength of domestic demand and external competitiveness, proving that Spain can maintain positive momentum even during periods of international instability. Structurally, the growth was driven by a dynamic increase in exports and strong private investment, which compensated for a slowdown in household consumption and public spending during the final quarter. This shift suggests that while internal demand faced pressure from inflation and rising costs, the broader economic framework remained solid, with key sectors like services and construction leading the charge. The ability to sustain high growth across multiple quarters underscores a structural adjustment where external trade became a more critical engine than domestic consumption in the later stages of the year.
