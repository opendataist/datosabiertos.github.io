ES EN

The establishment of RTI centers in Punjab empowers citizens to access public information, marking a significant step toward government transparency. By bridging the gap between local institutions and the Information Commission, these centers ensure that legal obligations for data disclosure are met within statutory timeframes. This initiative aligns with open data principles by democratizing information flow and reducing institutional opacity. It encourages regular usage of information rights, which serves as a critical mechanism for preventing corruption and fostering accountability in public administration. The relevance to open data lies in its structural commitment to making government operations visible and accessible. By mandating monthly meetings with public information officers and expanding centers to all districts, the commission actively works to remove barriers to information, thereby strengthening the foundation for an open and transparent society.
