¿A quiénes beneficia la nueva ley de transparencia de salarios en California? Esto es lo que sabemos

California’s new law mandates salary transparency, which reduces workplace inequalities and benefits women and minorities. This approach is relevant to open data, as it encourages the public disclosure of critical information on economic equity. By making these data accessible, citizens are empowered to understand and address structural wage gaps.
