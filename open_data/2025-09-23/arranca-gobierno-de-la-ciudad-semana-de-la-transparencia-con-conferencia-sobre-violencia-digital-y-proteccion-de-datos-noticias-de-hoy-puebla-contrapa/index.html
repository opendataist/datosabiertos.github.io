Arranca Gobierno de la Ciudad Semana de la Transparencia con conferencia sobre violencia digital y protección de datos - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Arranca Gobierno de la Ciudad Semana de la Transparencia con conferencia sobre violencia digital y protección de datos - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

The government of Puebla inaugurated Transparency Week to strengthen accountability and citizen participation, emphasizing the critical need to protect personal data in digital spaces. This initiative aims to raise awareness among students, academics, and public officials about privacy and the responsible use of information, fostering a culture of transparency that builds trust between the state and society. A central focus of the event is eradicating digital violence by integrating data protection with a gender perspective. By safeguarding the information of vulnerable groups, such as women and children, the administration seeks to guarantee dignity and security online. This approach underscores that privacy is not merely a technical issue but a fundamental right necessary for a life free from violence in virtual environments. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the essential balance between transparency and privacy. Open data initiatives must respect personal information to maintain public trust. Promoting digital literacy and ethical data handling ensures that transparency efforts do not compromise individual rights, thereby creating a more inclusive and secure digital society for all citizens.
