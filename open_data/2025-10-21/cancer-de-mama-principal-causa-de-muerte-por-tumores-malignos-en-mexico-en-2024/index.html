Cáncer de mama, principal causa de muerte por tumores malignos en México en 2024

Mexico’s INEGI reports that breast cancer became the leading cause of cancer-related death in 2024, highlighting a concerning rise in mortality rates. Significant disparities persist, as nearly forty percent of diagnosed women lack access to specialized medical care. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as transparent public statistics expose systemic health inequalities. Such information empowers policymakers and citizens to demand equitable healthcare access and early diagnostic improvements, driving necessary societal and governmental reforms.
