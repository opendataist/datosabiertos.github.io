Freedom of Information

SEJ established a task force to champion open environmental information, providing journalists with tools, forms, and educational resources for freedom of information requests. This initiative underscores the critical role of transparency in environmental journalism, ensuring the public’s right to know is upheld through accessible data and advocacy. It is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how institutional support empowers journalists to leverage public records, reinforcing accountability and data accessibility in environmental reporting.
