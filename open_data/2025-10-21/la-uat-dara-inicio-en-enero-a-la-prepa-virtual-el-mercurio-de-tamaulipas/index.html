La UAT dará inicio en enero a la prepa virtual - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

La UAT dará inicio en enero a la prepa virtual - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

The Autonomous University of Tamaulipas is launching a virtual high school program to expand educational access for adults who missed secondary schooling. This initiative leverages technology to serve students in remote areas, aligning with national policies on equal opportunity. By offering an official, two-year online curriculum, the program removes geographical barriers and provides a viable path to higher education. It demonstrates how digital infrastructure can democratize learning, ensuring marginalized populations receive recognized credentials without physical attendance constraints. This case is relevant to open data as it highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible information regarding educational opportunities. It underscores how digital platforms must facilitate clear, open dissemination of program details to maximize inclusivity and ensure equitable participation for all interested individuals nationwide.
