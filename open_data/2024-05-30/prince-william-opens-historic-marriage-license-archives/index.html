The digitization of historical court records enhances public access to genealogical and property data, demonstrating how local governments can preserve heritage without burdening taxpayers. This initiative supports open data principles by providing global, free access to historical documents, thereby facilitating research in history, law, and family connections. Such transparency empowers diverse users to uncover lineage and property history, showcasing the societal value of making archived civic records widely available online.
