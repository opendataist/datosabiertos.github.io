La Seguridad Social suma 11.792 afiliados entre enero y febrero en Balears y casi 20.800 sobre 2022

The Balearic Islands set a historical employment record for February, driven by strong growth in affiliates under the general social security regime and among the self-employed workforce. This surge mirrors Spain’s national trend, marking the highest affiliation levels ever recorded for this month and signaling a robust recovery in the labor market. The data highlight the resilience of the local economy, with significant job additions compared to the end of the previous year. Although a small number of workers remain on temporary layoff schemes, the overwhelming majority hold secure positions, reflecting a healthy shift from crisis management to expansion. This article is relevant to open data initiatives because it exemplifies high-quality, granular public statistics. Such detailed metrics on employment, sectors, and legal regimes enable researchers and developers to build transparent, evidence-based tools that monitor economic health and support policy decisions.
