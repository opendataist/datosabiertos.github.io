NFOIC board elects Jeff Roberts of Colorado to presidency

The National Freedom of Information Coalition has elected Jeff Roberts as its new president, succeeding David Cuillier who moved to the University of Florida. This leadership transition highlights the coalition's commitment to stability and experienced guidance in the ongoing struggle for government transparency. Roberts, a veteran data journalist and former head of the Colorado Freedom of Information Coalition, brings deep expertise in public records advocacy, ensuring the organization remains effective in supporting state-level affiliates. This change underscores the critical role of structured, nonpartisan organizations in defending press freedom and facilitating access to public information. By replacing outgoing leadership with seasoned professionals like Roberts and newly appointed Vice President Terry Mutchler, the coalition strengthens its capacity to drive legislative reforms and provide essential resources. These efforts are vital for maintaining an open dialogue between citizens and government institutions across the United States. For the open data community, this article is relevant because it emphasizes the institutional infrastructure required to support data transparency. Effective open data initiatives rely not just on technology, but on legal frameworks and advocacy groups that protect the right to access government information. The election of leaders dedicated to these principles reinforces the importance of coordinated national efforts in ensuring that public data remains accessible, thereby empowering journalists and citizens to hold governments accountable through informed engagement.
