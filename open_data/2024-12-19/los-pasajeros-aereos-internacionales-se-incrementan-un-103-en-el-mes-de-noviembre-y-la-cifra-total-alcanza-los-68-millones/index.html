Los pasajeros aéreos internacionales se incrementan un 10,3% en el mes de noviembre y la cifra total alcanza los 6,8 millones

The article highlights sustained growth in the arrival of international passengers to Spain, driven both by increased intra-European Union travel flows and by the diversification into emerging markets. This trend reflects the robustness of Spain’s tourism sector, where traditional source countries remain fundamental pillars while travelers from outside Europe are gaining importance, signaling the destination’s resilience and strategic adaptation. Low-cost airlines have solidified their position as the preferred model for most travelers, particularly those coming from the United Kingdom, Italy, and the Netherlands, suggesting a greater democratization of access to Spanish tourism. However, markets such as Germany continue to show a notable preference for traditional carriers, underscoring the complexity of consumption preferences shaped by visitors’ cultural and economic backgrounds, which directly influences the structure of air supply. This content is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the importance of accessing and analyzing large volumes of mobility and transport data to understand economic and tourism patterns. The regular publication of these indicators enables researchers, planners, and citizens to verify the actual state of the sector, fostering transparency and evidence-based decision-making using public data on migration flows and consumer behavior.
