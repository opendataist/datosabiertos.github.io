Inai garantizará transparencia y protección de datos hasta fin de su existencia: Adrián Alcalá

Inai garantizará transparencia y protección de datos hasta fin de su existencia: Adrián Alcalá

The INAI reaffirms its commitment to safeguarding the right of access to information and data protection rights in the face of constitutional reforms that threaten autonomous bodies. By operating under the current legislation during the transitional period, the institute ensures continuity of governance, emphasizing that these democratic tools remain essential for citizen empowerment and informed decision-making. The organization highlights significant policy advancements achieved through sessions of the National Transparency Council, despite facing what is described as its most challenging year since its inception. This period is marked by institutional uncertainty, yet the agency demonstrates resilience and unwavering dedication to upholding human rights and serving the public interest without interruption. This update is crucial for open data advocates, as it underscores the fragility of independent oversight mechanisms during structural political shifts. It serves as a vital reminder that sustained vigilance and robust legal frameworks are necessary to protect the transparency infrastructure that enables accountability and public participation in Mexico’s democratic processes.
