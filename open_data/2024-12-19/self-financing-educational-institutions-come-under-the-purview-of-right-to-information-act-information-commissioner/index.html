Self-financing educational institutions come under the purview of Right to Information Act : Information Commissioner

Self-financing educational institutions come under the purview of Right to Information Act : Information Commissioner

Self-financing educational institutions with government approval are now classified as public authorities under the RTI Act. This landmark decision mandates these private entities to appoint information officers, ensuring transparency in their operations despite their non-state funding status. The ruling hinges on the significant public resources these institutions receive, including tax concessions, government grants, and state-backed loan schemes. By accepting such financial support and issuing state-recognized certificates, these schools fulfill the criteria for public authority, thereby subjecting them to strict accountability measures under existing laws. This development is crucial for open_data because it expands the scope of mandatory information disclosure beyond traditional government bodies. It establishes a legal precedent that private entities performing public functions or utilizing public funds must make their data accessible. Consequently, citizens can now demand transparency regarding admissions, fees, and infrastructure, significantly enhancing civic engagement and data availability in the education sector.
