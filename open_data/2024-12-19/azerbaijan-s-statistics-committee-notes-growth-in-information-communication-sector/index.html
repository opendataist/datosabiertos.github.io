Azerbaijan's Statistics Committee notes growth in information & communication sector

Azerbaijan’s statistics committee reports expansion in the information and communication sector, signaling broader digital infrastructure development. This growth underscores the importance of accessible, open data frameworks for monitoring technological progress. However, restricted access to specific information highlights ongoing challenges for transparency. Such limitations directly impact how stakeholders utilize open data for informed decision-making and independent analysis.
