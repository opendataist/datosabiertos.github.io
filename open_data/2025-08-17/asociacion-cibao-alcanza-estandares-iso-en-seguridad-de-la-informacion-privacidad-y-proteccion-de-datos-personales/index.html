Asociación Cibao alcanza estándares ISO en seguridad de la información, privacidad y protección de datos personales

Asociación Cibao alcanza estándares ISO en seguridad de la información, privacidad y protección de datos personales

The Cibao Association has achieved international ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27701 certifications, validating a robust governance framework for information security and personal data privacy. This dual recognition demonstrates a strategic commitment to safeguarding client data against escalating digital threats, aligning local practices with global standards such as the EU’s GDPR and Dominican regulations. By integrating these frameworks, the institution moves beyond mere technical compliance toward a holistic culture of trust, continuous improvement, and organizational maturity. This development is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a critical foundation of trust and security, without which open data initiatives cannot thrive. Open data relies on public confidence that their information is handled responsibly. By demonstrating rigorous control over personal and financial data, financial entities can safely release non-sensitive datasets for public benefit. The certification assures stakeholders that while data privacy is strictly protected, the underlying infrastructure supports transparent and accountable information management, enabling the ethical sharing of open data. Furthermore, this case illustrates how strong internal governance facilitates responsible transparency. When organizations adopt comprehensive security standards, they reduce the risks associated with data exposure, making them more willing to engage in open data ecosystems. This balance allows for the free flow of information necessary for innovation and civic engagement while maintaining individuals’ privacy rights. Thus, such certifications serve as enablers for open data, ensuring that transparency does not compromise security or privacy.
