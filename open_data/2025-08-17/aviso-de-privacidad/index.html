Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

The document outlines how 13 Media, S.C. manages personal information in compliance with Mexico’s Federal Law on Protection of Data Held by Private Parties. The company emphasizes that privacy is a core priority, ensuring that data collection adheres to strict principles such as legality, quality, and proportionality. By transparently disclosing the types of data gathered and their specific purposes, the organization demonstrates a commitment to ethical data handling and regulatory compliance. Data is primarily used to verify identity, address client needs, and fulfill legal obligations, with sharing limited to necessary third parties such as notaries or legal authorities. The notice highlights individuals’ rights to access, correct, cancel, or oppose the processing of their information, providing clear channels for exercising these rights. This structure ensures that users retain control over their personal details while allowing the company to operate efficiently within legal boundaries. This text is relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the critical boundary between transparent corporate data practices and individual privacy protection. While open data advocates for the release of non-sensitive information for public benefit, this notice underscores the necessity of safeguarding personally identifiable information. It serves as a model for how organizations can balance transparency in data policies with the rigorous protection of user privacy, a key challenge in modern data governance ecosystems.
