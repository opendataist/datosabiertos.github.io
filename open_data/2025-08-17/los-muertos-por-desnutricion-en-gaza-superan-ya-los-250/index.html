Los muertos por desnutrición en Gaza superan ya los 250

Los muertos por desnutrición en Gaza superan ya los 250

This report highlights a severe humanitarian crisis in which starvation and malnutrition are causing significant mortality in Gaza, driven by prolonged access restrictions and insufficient aid distribution. The situation underscores critical challenges in monitoring and verifying real-time humanitarian data in conflict zones. The discrepancy between claimed aid volumes and actual needs reveals systemic failures in transparency and accountability. Without reliable, open data streams from independent sources, it is impossible to assess the true scale of suffering or hold authorities accountable for aid blockages. The relevance to open data lies in the urgent need for transparent, accessible information to advocate for victims. Open datasets on aid flows and health outcomes are essential tools for civil society to challenge official narratives and ensure that humanitarian needs are accurately documented and addressed on a global scale.
