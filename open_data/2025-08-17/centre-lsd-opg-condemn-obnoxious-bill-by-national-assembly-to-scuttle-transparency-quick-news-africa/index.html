Centre LSD, OPG condemn ‘obnoxious bill’ by national assembly to scuttle transparency - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

Centre LSD, OPG condemn ‘obnoxious bill’ by national assembly to scuttle transparency - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

The workshop highlighted the urgent threat posed by restrictive legislative proposals, such as the NGO Regulation Bill, which undermine constitutional freedoms and civil society autonomy. This concern is critical to open data discourse because it demonstrates how legal frameworks directly impact the operational capacity of organizations that advocate for transparency and citizen access to information. If civil society entities are constrained by oppressive laws, their ability to monitor government data practices and demand accountability is severely diminished, jeopardizing the core principles of open governance. Furthermore, the event underscored the necessity of genuine collaboration between government and civil society to implement Open Government Partnership commitments effectively. Participants emphasized that the partnership model is only successful when both parties honor their joint responsibilities rather than engaging in superficial compliance. This highlights a vital aspect of open data ecosystems: the need for sustained, active engagement from non-state actors to ensure that data transparency initiatives are not merely symbolic but result in tangible improvements in public finance management and institutional integrity. Ultimately, the workshop called for a strategic shift toward holding the government accountable for its specific pledges within the National Action Plan. By leveraging legal tools like the Freedom of Information Act and collaborating with the media to expose non-compliance, civil society can drive meaningful change. This relevance to open data lies in the realization that access to information alone is insufficient without the political will and collaborative enforcement mechanisms to ensure that data is used to enhance transparency, fight corruption, and improve public service delivery.
