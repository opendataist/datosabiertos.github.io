Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

Deputies from the Civic Coalition propose the creation of an Open Import Data Portal in Argentina, aiming to replace the current discretionary nature of the SIRA system with a transparent and predictable management framework. The project requires that all information regarding import applications, including product details and status, be published digitally in open formats. This measure seeks to eliminate abuses of power and significantly reduce corruption risks associated with the granting of licenses. The initiative establishes strict and automatic deadlines for resolving applications, granting approval if no response is issued within the specified timeframe. Additionally, it proposes simplifying administrative procedures and making public the criteria for economic capacity, thereby ensuring traceability and predictability in commercial operations. This addresses the criticism that recurrent modifications to the current system primarily serve to restrict imports and unjustifiably protect the Central Bank’s reserves. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how the systematic publication of public information in accessible formats is a fundamental tool against corruption and state arbitrariness. It demonstrates that transparency is not merely a technical requirement, but a governance mechanism that empowers citizens and businesses, enabling them to monitor compliance with regulations and reduce the information asymmetry that favors bureaucratic discretion.
