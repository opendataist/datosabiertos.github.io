AEMEND urges PM Shehbaz to take notice of enforced disappearances of journalists

AEMEND condemns the enforced disappearances of journalists, urging immediate state intervention to ensure their safe recovery and legal due process. This stance underscores the critical importance of protecting press freedom, a fundamental pillar of open data ecosystems where information must remain accessible and secure from arbitrary suppression. The association warns against the politicization of media workers and highlights undeclared censorship by state institutions. Such practices restrict the flow of unbiased information, directly compromising the transparency and impartiality required for healthy democratic discourse and the reliable dissemination of public knowledge. These revelations are vital to the open_data community as they illustrate how political pressure and censorship threaten the integrity of information sources. By restricting access to accurate data and promoting one-sided narratives, authorities undermine the very foundations of open information systems essential for accountability and informed citizenship.
