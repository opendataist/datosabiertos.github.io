Querétaro líder en ocupación laboral

Querétaro leads the nation in labor stability, demonstrating the highest formal employment rates and the lowest underemployment. This performance indicates a robust local economy where job creation has outpaced the previous year, significantly reducing unemployment. The shift toward formal, stable work suggests a healthier labor market structure that benefits workers through better protections and consistent income, contrasting with typical regional volatility. The data reveals a substantial expansion in the economically active population, driven largely by growth in the tertiary sector. Small businesses and service-oriented industries, such as hospitality and diverse services, are primary drivers of this increase. This trend highlights how localized economic growth often relies on the agility of small enterprises to absorb workforce growth, creating opportunities without requiring massive industrial investment. This article is relevant to open data because it showcases the power of public statistical agencies like INEGI in providing transparent, granular labor metrics. By making high-quality, standardized data freely available, researchers and policymakers can identify successful regional models. Open data enables the replication of these positive economic conditions elsewhere, fostering evidence-based decision-making and greater accountability in public administration.
