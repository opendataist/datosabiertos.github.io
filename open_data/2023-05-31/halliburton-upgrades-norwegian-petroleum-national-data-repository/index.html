Halliburton upgrades Norwegian Petroleum national data repository

The Norwegian Petroleum Directorate has launched Diskos 2.0, the industry’s first fully cloud-native National Data Repository. This upgrade integrates advanced automation and AI to manage massive datasets efficiently, significantly reducing storage costs while enhancing scalability and processing power for exploration and production insights. A key innovation is the ability to access comprehensive seismic and well data directly within third-party software environments without moving or copying data from the repository. This seamless connectivity streamlines analysis, allowing operators and stakeholders to perform complex interpretations instantly within a transparent and clean management environment. This development is highly relevant to open data principles by demonstrating how cloud-native architectures can facilitate secure, frictionless data sharing and access. By removing traditional barriers to data retrieval and enabling immediate analytical use, the system promotes greater transparency and efficiency in the energy sector, setting a precedent for open, accessible data ecosystems.
