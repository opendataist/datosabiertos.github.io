Los alimentos y las bebidas suben de precio en mayo, pero menos que hace un año - EFEAgro

May’s projected consumer price index indicates a deceleration in annual inflation to 3.2%, driven primarily by lower fuel prices and a smaller increase in food costs compared to the previous year. This downward trend in both headline and underlying inflation suggests that the worst of the recent price surge may be passing. This data is relevant to open data because it illustrates the value of timely statistical indicators. By providing an early estimate, open data initiatives enable rapid public analysis of economic trends, allowing stakeholders to monitor inflation dynamics before final figures are officially confirmed. Understanding these immediate shifts helps communities and policymakers assess economic pressures more quickly. Access to such provisional statistics fosters transparency and supports informed decision-making regarding cost-of-living impacts, which is a core objective of open government data efforts.
