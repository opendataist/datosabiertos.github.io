New documents show Eby ignored serious warnings about Abbotsford homeless camp: BC United - Indo-Canadian Voice

The article highlights a critical governance failure where the BC United party alleges Premier David Eby ignored serious health and safety warnings regarding a homeless encampment while serving as Housing Minister. By burying briefing notes that detailed risks such as violence and explosions, the government failed to protect vulnerable individuals and the surrounding community, sparking accusations of political agenda-driven inaction. This incident underscores the vital role of Freedom of Information in holding public officials accountable. It demonstrates how FOI requests can uncover suppressed information and reveal gaps in governmental transparency, allowing opposition parties and citizens to scrutinize decisions that might otherwise remain hidden. The case illustrates the tension between administrative secrecy and public right-to-know. For the open data movement, this story emphasizes the necessity of proactive transparency and accessible government records. It serves as a cautionary tale about the dangers of withholding critical safety data, reinforcing the argument that robust open data frameworks are essential for ensuring accountability, preventing harm, and maintaining public trust in democratic institutions.
