PJM ranks fourth in overall judgments and second in civil matters: INEGI

The Judicial Branch of Michoacán stands out nationally for its high productivity in issuing rulings, establishing itself as an entity with notable procedural effectiveness. This performance reflects a solid institutional capacity to handle jurisdictional demands, particularly in civil matters, demonstrating an efficient commitment to resolving citizens’ disputes. Transparency in these data is essential for open data initiatives, as it enables citizen oversight and accountability.
