Norway is the Most Expensive of Europe together with Switzerland - The Nordic Page

This study reveals significant disparities in consumer price levels across Europe, highlighting that Nordic nations like Switzerland and Norway remain consistently expensive. In contrast, South-Eastern European countries offer substantially lower costs for goods and services, indicating a persistent economic divide within the region. Understanding these variations is crucial for accurately comparing economic indicators, as high relative prices can distort the perceived health of an economy. It also serves as a key metric for assessing the degree of price convergence and the true integration of the EU single market for goods and services. This data is vital for open data initiatives focused on economic transparency and cross-border comparison. By providing standardized Price Level Indices, it enables researchers and policymakers to adjust for inflation and purchasing power, ensuring that statistical analyses reflect real economic conditions rather than nominal price differences.
