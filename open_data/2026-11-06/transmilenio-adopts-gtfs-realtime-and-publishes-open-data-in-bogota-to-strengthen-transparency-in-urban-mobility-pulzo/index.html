TransMilenio adopts GTFS Realtime and publishes open data in Bogotá to strengthen transparency in urban mobility - Pulzo

TransMilenio adopts GTFS Realtime and publishes open data in Bogotá to strengthen transparency in urban mobility - Pulzo

Bogotá has taken a crucial step in its digital transformation by adopting the international GTFS Realtime standard for TransMilenio’s information. This measure goes beyond a simple schedule update, enabling access to real-time operational data on urban mobility. The change reflects a deep commitment to transparency, integrating cutting-edge technology to respond agilely to the everyday needs of millions of citizens. The push for this initiative aligns strictly with Colombian regulations on digital government and access to public information. By standardizing this data on Bogotá’s Open Data Portal, knowledge is democratized, facilitating the use of both historical and live information by development teams, researchers, and society at large. This encourages the reuse of data with positive social impact, ensuring that the public administration not only informs but also empowers various stakeholders to analyze and improve the management of public services. The relevance to open data lies in how this technical interoperability builds a smart and collaborative city. By releasing real-time information, it fosters the creation of innovative applications and reduces uncertainty in travel, significantly improving the end-user experience. This model demonstrates that technology in service of citizen well-being is a collective endeavor, setting a benchmark for efficiency and openness that strengthens trust in institutions and promotes effective solutions to urban mobility challenges.
