El truco infalible para ocultar tu red WiFi y evitar que tus vecinos se conecten sin permiso

El truco infalible para ocultar tu red WiFi y evitar que tus vecinos se conecten sin permiso

The article warns that unprotected home WiFi networks are vulnerable to unauthorized access, which can lead to bandwidth theft and expose personal devices to cyberattacks. It highlights that many users fail to change default router settings, creating easy entry points for intruders who might misuse internet resources or compromise connected hardware, such as surveillance cameras. To mitigate these risks, the text suggests hiding the network’s SSID as an initial step to reduce its visibility to potential attackers. While this measure limits casual discovery, it is not foolproof, as advanced tools can still detect hidden networks. Therefore, it should be viewed as part of a broader security strategy rather than a definitive security solution. This topic is highly relevant to open_data because securing the infrastructure that collects and transmits data is fundamental to data governance. If basic network security is neglected, the integrity and confidentiality of open datasets shared through these channels are compromised. Ensuring strong encryption and access controls protects the reliability of public information systems and maintains user trust in digital services.
