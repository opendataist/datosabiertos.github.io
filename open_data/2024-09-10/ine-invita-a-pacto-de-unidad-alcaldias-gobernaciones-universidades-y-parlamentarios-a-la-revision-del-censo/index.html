INE invita a Pacto de Unidad, alcaldías, gobernaciones, universidades y parlamentarios a la revisión del censo

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) is inviting a range of political and civil society institutions to examine preliminary census data, in response to regional skepticism and calls for an international audit. This collaborative review aims to verify statistical accuracy and restore public trust in national demographic figures. This process is significant for open data, as it demonstrates a commitment to transparency and multi-stakeholder validation of public statistics. By involving local authorities and civil society in the verification process, the initiative highlights the importance of participatory governance in ensuring data integrity and legitimacy. Ultimately, the effort underscores how open data practices require active engagement beyond mere publication. It suggests that robust open data ecosystems depend on institutional openness to external scrutiny, fostering accountability and ensuring that official statistics accurately reflect societal realities.
