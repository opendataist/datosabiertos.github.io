El Indec dará a conocer la inflación de agosto: qué número prevén las consultoras

The upcoming official inflation data reveals a slight deceleration in price increases compared to the previous month, with private estimates aligning closely with prior trends. This moderation suggests that stabilization efforts are gaining traction, although significant cumulative price growth remains evident over the year, highlighting the persistent economic pressure on households despite recent improvements. Key drivers include rising costs in public services and transport, while food prices show varied performance across specific categories. The divergence between national and local measurements underscores the complexity of assessing inflation, as regional differences in consumer basket weighting lead to distinct local realities. This nuance is critical for understanding how different sectors and regions experience economic changes, rather than relying solely on aggregate national figures. This development is vital for open data initiatives because it precedes a major methodological change in how the official index is calculated. The planned expansion of the product scope aims to improve accuracy and transparency in statistical measurement. Such updates demonstrate the ongoing evolution of public statistics, emphasizing the need for accessible, clear, and standardized data formats to support informed public debate and robust economic analysis.
