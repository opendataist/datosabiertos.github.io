INE convoca a mesa técnica nacional por el censo para el 16 de septiembre

INE convoca a mesa técnica nacional por el censo para el 16 de septiembre

The National Electoral Institute (INE) has convened a technical working group with regional governments, universities, and parliamentarians to address disputes surrounding the results of the 2024 Census. This dialogue aims to clarify doubts and potentially audit the questionnaires, responding to demands from major municipalities that reject the figures despite legal mandates requiring their use. This process is crucial for open data, as it highlights the tension between official statistics and public trust. Ensuring transparency and enabling stakeholder verification are essential for maintaining data integrity. Without addressing these controversies, census data cannot be reliably used for equitable resource distribution or political representation, thereby undermining the foundational principle of open, accessible, and trusted public information.
