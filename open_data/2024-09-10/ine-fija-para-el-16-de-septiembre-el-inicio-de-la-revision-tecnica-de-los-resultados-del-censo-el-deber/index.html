INE fija para el 16 de septiembre el inicio de la revisión técnica de los resultados del censo | El Deber

INE fija para el 16 de septiembre el inicio de la revisión técnica de los resultados del censo | El Deber

The National Institute of Statistics in Bolivia has launched a technical review process for its controversial population census. By inviting local authorities, universities, and international observers, the initiative aims to address widespread skepticism and validate the preliminary results through transparent scrutiny of the data. This move highlights the critical role of open data in maintaining public trust. When statistical information faces significant doubt, proactive transparency and collaborative verification become essential tools to restore credibility. It demonstrates how open methodologies can mitigate disputes and ensure accountability in national statistics. Ultimately, this case underscores the necessity of robust, accessible data practices. Allowing external review not only clarifies specific findings but also reinforces the broader principle that open data is vital for democratic legitimacy. It serves as a reminder that integrity in statistics requires ongoing, verifiable openness to all stakeholders.
