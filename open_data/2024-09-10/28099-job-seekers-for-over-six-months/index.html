28,099 job seekers for over six months

The prolonged unemployment of thousands of individuals, including many highly educated professionals, reveals systemic gaps in labor market integration. This situation highlights critical challenges in aligning workforce skills with available economic opportunities, particularly in regions facing structural employment barriers. For open data advocates, these statistics underscore the necessity of accessible, granular labor market data to diagnose such disparities. Transparent reporting enables researchers and policymakers to identify specific educational segments struggling with integration, fostering evidence-based interventions. Ultimately, this case illustrates how public data can drive social accountability and strategic resource allocation. By making detailed employment trends visible, stakeholders can better address the root causes of long-term joblessness and promote more inclusive economic participation.
