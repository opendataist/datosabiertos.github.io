Media renews criticism of AI firms ignoring copyright laws | The Asahi Shimbun: Breaking News, Japan News and Analysis

Media renews criticism of AI firms ignoring copyright laws | The Asahi Shimbun: Breaking News, Japan News and Analysis

Japanese media associations argue that AI companies are unlawfully scraping news content for training and retrieval, violating explicit copyright restrictions. This practice infringes on intellectual property rights and exploits creators who have clearly denied permission for such use. The trend promotes zero-click searches, where users consume AI-generated summaries without visiting original sources. This reduces traffic to legitimate journalism, threatening its financial sustainability and potentially spreading misinformation to the public. This conflict is vital to open data discussions as it highlights the tension between data accessibility and legal protections. It underscores the need for clear frameworks governing how AI systems interact with copyrighted information while preserving the integrity of professional media sources.
