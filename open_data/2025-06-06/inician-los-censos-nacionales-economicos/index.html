Inician los Censos Nacionales Económicos

Inician los Censos Nacionales Económicos

Panama launched its seventh National Economic Census to modernize public policy and investment planning. This initiative significantly reduces budget costs while expanding the scope to include digital economy sectors that were previously underrepresented in data collection. The process prioritizes confidentiality, ensuring that all business information remains anonymous and protected by statistical secrecy laws. This assurance aims to encourage participation, recognizing that precise data is essential for designing accurate and effective government interventions. This census is vital for open data, as it generates comprehensive, standardized economic statistics. These datasets provide critical insights into market trends, enabling better decision-making for both the public sector and businesses, while promoting transparency in national economic indicators.
