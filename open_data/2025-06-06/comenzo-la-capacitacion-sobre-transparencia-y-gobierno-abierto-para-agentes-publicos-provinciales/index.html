Comenzó la capacitación sobre "Transparencia y Gobierno Abierto" para agentes públicos provinciales

Comenzó la capacitación sobre "Transparencia y Gobierno Abierto" para agentes públicos provinciales

The province of La Pampa has implemented a training strategy for public officials on transparency and open government. This effort is grounded in its pioneering legislation, which guarantees access to public information and positions the jurisdiction at the forefront of the country in terms of accountability. The main objective is to strengthen administrative management by adopting principles of openness and collaboration. By understanding these procedures, public officials seek to foster a more participatory democracy, in which citizens actively engage in the exercise of state functions and political opposition, thereby improving overall democratic quality. This initiative is relevant to the open data movement because it aligns legal infrastructure with operational practice. The training ensures that data availability is not merely a formal requirement, but a living tool for transparency. This facilitates the effective use of public information to improve public services and build institutional trust.
