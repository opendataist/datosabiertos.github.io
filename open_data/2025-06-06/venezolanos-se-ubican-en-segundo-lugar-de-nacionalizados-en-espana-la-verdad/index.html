Venezolanos se ubican en segundo lugar de nacionalizados en España - La Verdad

In 2024, Spain recorded its highest historical number of citizenship acquisitions, marking a significant demographic shift driven by international migration trends. This surge reflects the increasing integration of foreign residents into Spanish society, with Venezuela and Colombia emerging as major sources of new citizens alongside traditional origins such as Morocco. The data highlight a strong preference for naturalization through residence rather than by option, indicating long-term settlement patterns among adult immigrants. Furthermore, the gender and age distribution reveals that middle-aged women are the most active participants in this legal incorporation process, suggesting evolving social dynamics regarding identity and belonging. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical records can reveal critical insights into migration, integration, and demographic changes. Such open datasets enable researchers and policymakers to analyze integration success rates and support evidence-based decisions regarding social cohesion and public services.
