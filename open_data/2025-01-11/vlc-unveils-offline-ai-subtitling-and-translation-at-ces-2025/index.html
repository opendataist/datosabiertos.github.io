VLC unveils offline AI subtitling and translation at CES 2025

VLC unveils offline AI subtitling and translation at CES 2025

VLC now features local, offline AI subtitling and translation for over 100 languages, ensuring privacy by processing data directly on the device. This advancement highlights how open-source media players can integrate powerful, privacy-preserving AI tools without cloud dependencies. Such innovations demonstrate the potential for decentralized technologies to deliver high-quality user experiences, setting a new standard for open software capabilities.
