Nueva Edición de Datos Abiertos IOMA - El diario

Nueva Edición de Datos Abiertos IOMA - El diario

IOMA has modernized its information systems to ensure transparency and facilitate access to health services in the province of Buenos Aires. This initiative responds to the legal commitment to the right to public information, using the open data portal as a key tool for the community to monitor how resources of the solidarity-based health system are planned and managed. The report highlights significant growth in enrollment and operational capacity, with a notable increase in the availability of medical professionals, hospital beds, and medication coverage. These changes reflect a successful adaptation to the population’s needs, prioritizing accessible and efficient care, both in-person and through digital modalities and teleconsultations, which have seen intensive use. The relevance to open data lies in the democratization of health information; by publishing detailed datasets on enrollment records, expenditures, and service provision, IOMA enables public scrutiny and accountability. This not only strengthens institutional trust but also empowers citizens to understand the reality of the health system and demand improvements based on verifiable evidence.
