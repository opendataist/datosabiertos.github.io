Women-owned firms rise to 26.2pc in informal sector, wages jump 13pc

Women-owned firms rise to 26.2pc in informal sector, wages jump 13pc

India’s unincorporated sector demonstrates a robust recovery, marked by significant growth in business ownership, employment, and productivity. A notable surge in female-led establishments signals a positive shift in women’s entrepreneurial participation, reflecting broader socio-economic empowerment and diversity within the informal economy. Concurrently, rising wages and widespread digital adoption indicate a modernizing labor market. Increased internet penetration suggests that small enterprises are increasingly integrating technology into their operations, which enhances efficiency and competitiveness while reducing operational barriers in the domestic value chain. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, granular insights into the informal economy’s health. By making such detailed metrics publicly available, stakeholders can better analyze trends, inform policy, and support initiatives aimed at formalizing and strengthening small business ecosystems across developing nations.
