Viola MORENA ley de Transparencia, tiene medio año que no informa en qué hasta el dinero - Plumas Libres

Viola MORENA ley de Transparencia, tiene medio año que no informa en qué hasta el dinero - Plumas Libres

Morena has neglected its legal obligation to proactively disclose financial data, thereby violating transparency laws and forcing citizens to file information requests. This stands in stark contrast to rival parties, which maintain up-to-date public records, highlighting a significant regression in political accountability and open access to details on public spending. At the same time, institutional reforms are dismantling the independent transparency body, INAI, and transferring oversight responsibilities to the electoral institute. Civil society warns that this structural change undermines democratic safeguards, despite the government’s claims of stricter compliance, suggesting a systemic weakening of mechanisms designed to ensure governmental openness and integrity. This situation is critical for open data advocacy, as it demonstrates how political actors and institutional restructuring can actively obstruct data availability. It underscores the necessity of robust, independent enforcement mechanisms to guarantee that transparency remains proactive rather than reactive, ensuring that citizens can effectively monitor public resources and hold those in power accountable.
