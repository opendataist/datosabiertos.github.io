La banca logró una rentabilidad del 12,3% en el tercer trimestre, récord desde 2015

The banking sector has experienced a notable rebound in profitability, reaching figures significantly higher than those of the previous year and marking a historic high since 2015. This financial growth is underpinned by a robust solvency foundation, with capital reserves remaining stable and improved compared to the prior period, ensuring the structural resilience of financial institutions against potential economic shocks. Although asset quality shows a slight tendency toward deterioration in special monitoring, the cost of risk has decreased, reflecting prudent management in the face of uncertainty. The system’s liquidity has strengthened thanks to an increase in safety buffers, enabling banks to maintain their lending capacity and meet credit demand without compromising their short-term operational stability. This information is crucial for the open data movement, as it illustrates how the transparent publication of financial indicators by official bodies allows developers and citizens to analyze economic health in real time. The accessibility of these data fosters accountability, facilitates academic research on banking trends, and empowers society to engage knowledgeably in debates on financial policies and sector regulations.
