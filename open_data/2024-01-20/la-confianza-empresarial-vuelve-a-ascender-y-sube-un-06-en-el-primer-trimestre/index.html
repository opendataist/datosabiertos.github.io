La confianza empresarial vuelve a ascender y sube un 0,6% en el primer trimestre

The Harmonized Business Confidence Indicator shows a broad-based increase in confidence among Spanish companies during the first quarter, driven primarily by a more favorable assessment of recent performance. However, this improvement is offset by a deterioration in future expectations, with the share of pessimistic entrepreneurs exceeding that of optimistic ones. This divergence suggests that while the current situation is viewed positively, the near-term outlook generates uncertainty and caution within the business community. The narrative also reflects specific tensions in operational management, marked by a significant rise in the intention to raise prices and a decline in plans for job creation. These data indicate that companies are facing cost pressures that limit their capacity for labor expansion. Moreover, confidence has fallen in the transport and hospitality sectors and in several autonomous communities, while construction leads the recovery, revealing an unbalanced economic recovery across sectors. This article is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how official statistical indicators, harmonized at the European level, provide critical layers of market intelligence. By making these comparable metrics accessible, open data enables analysts and decision-makers to quickly identify these discrepancies between present conditions and future expectations, facilitating the assessment of macroeconomic risks and evidence-based strategic planning grounded in transparent and standardized information.
