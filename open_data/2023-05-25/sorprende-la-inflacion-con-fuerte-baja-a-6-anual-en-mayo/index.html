Sorprende la inflación con fuerte baja a 6% anual en mayo

Mexico’s annual inflation fell sharply to 6% in early May, marking the lowest level since late 2021. This unexpected moderation aligns with efforts to curb rising costs, offering a positive signal for economic stability and consumer purchasing power during a period of high interest rates. The decline was largely driven by seasonal reductions in electricity tariffs and favorable market conditions. Core inflation and prices for essential goods also showed signs of easing, suggesting that upward pressure on prices is gradually dissipating across key sectors of the economy. This development underscores the critical importance of open data for economic analysis. Transparent, real-time statistics from official institutions enable stakeholders to accurately assess policy effectiveness and market trends. Reliable public data ensures that decisions regarding monetary policy and social welfare are based on verified evidence rather than speculation.
