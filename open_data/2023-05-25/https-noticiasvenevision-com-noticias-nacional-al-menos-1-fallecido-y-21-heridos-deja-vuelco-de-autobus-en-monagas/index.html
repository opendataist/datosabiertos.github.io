Open Data
ES EN

Traffic accidents, often caused by poor infrastructure and adverse weather conditions, endanger citizens, underscoring the urgent need for open data to monitor road conditions and safety trends. Transparent public information on accidents and infrastructure status empowers communities to demand better maintenance and accountability. This case illustrates why open data is crucial for enhancing urban safety and preventing future tragedies through evidence-based policymaking.
