Datos abiertos, elemento fundamental para regular inteligencia artificial, plantean en el Senado

Open data is emerging as the essential currency for the future, serving as the fundamental fuel for artificial intelligence. Consequently, this technology must be integrated into regulatory frameworks to ensure its responsible development and application. The democratization of AI relies heavily on robust digital infrastructure and strategic policies. While Mexico is in the early stages of developing a national open data strategy, significant budgetary investments are required to build the necessary infrastructure and empower institutions to handle modern technological challenges effectively. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the urgent need for precise regulatory frameworks that balance innovation with privacy and security. Establishing clear ethical principles for data collection and storage is crucial to safeguard personal information while enabling the widespread, equitable use of artificial intelligence.
