La inflación se modera en noviembre al 3,2% y la subyacente cae hasta el 4,5%

Spanish inflation fell to 3.2% in November, marking the first decline since June. This downward trend was driven by significant price reductions in fuel, tourism packages, and electricity, alongside a slowdown in food price increases. The underlying inflation rate also declined, reaching its lowest level since May 2022. As a result, consumer prices experienced a monthly decrease, enhancing household purchasing power and boosting corporate competitiveness. Government officials and analysts note that wages are regaining strength, although current levels remain above the central bank’s targets. The data suggests a sustained trajectory toward lower inflation rates. This information is relevant to open data because it originates from the INE, a public institution committed to transparency.
