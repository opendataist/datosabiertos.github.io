El Portal de Transparencia del Ayuntamiento tuvo 1,7 millones de páginas vistas el pasado año, un 73 % más que en 2019

The City of Madrid positions itself as a global benchmark for open governance, emphasizing that transparency is not merely a legal obligation but a strategic tool for building essential public trust. By fostering a culture of accountability and data reuse, the administration argues that civic engagement directly enriches municipal management, making it impossible to improve the city without citizen collaboration. This narrative shifts the perspective of data from bureaucratic storage to a foundational element of democratic legitimacy and effective governance. High engagement metrics with the Open Data Portal, including millions of downloads and extensive dataset availability, demonstrate the successful reutilization of municipal information. The administration actively enhances accessibility through user-friendly visualizations, ensuring that raw data translates into clear, actionable insights for the public. This approach highlights the practical value of open data in simplifying complex civic information, thereby encouraging broader societal participation and reinforcing the reciprocal relationship between the government and its residents. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the transition from passive data publication to active citizen empowerment. Madrid’s membership in international alliances like the Open Government Partnership and the OECD underscores a commitment to standardized, transparent governance. By prioritizing data quality and high-value datasets, the city provides a replicable model for other municipalities, proving that robust open data strategies are essential for modern, inclusive, and responsible public administration.
