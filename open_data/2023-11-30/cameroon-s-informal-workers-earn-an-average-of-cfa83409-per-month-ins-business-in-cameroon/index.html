Cameroon's informal workers earn an average of CFA83,409 per month (INS) - Business in Cameroon

The Cameroonian Institute of Statistics reveals that the informal sector remains dominated by self-employed individuals with low wages, highlighting a significant gender disparity where women rely more heavily on solo entrepreneurship. Despite the prevalence of verbal agreements over written contracts, incomes have risen substantially over the last decade, particularly in transport and catering. This data underscores the sector's resilience and growth, even as capital remains largely insufficient for formal business expansion. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it provides critical, granular insights into an often invisible economic sphere. By defining the informal sector through the absence of taxpayer numbers or formal accounts, the study exposes gaps in official economic tracking. Such detailed demographic and financial breakdowns allow researchers to better understand labor dynamics and income inequality that traditional formal sector surveys miss. Ultimately, the findings emphasize the need for inclusive statistical frameworks that capture the realities of the majority of the workforce. Open access to this information can inform policies aimed at formalizing these units or improving social protections. Understanding these hidden economic structures is essential for creating accurate national accounts and developing effective strategies to support informal workers in their transition toward greater economic security.
