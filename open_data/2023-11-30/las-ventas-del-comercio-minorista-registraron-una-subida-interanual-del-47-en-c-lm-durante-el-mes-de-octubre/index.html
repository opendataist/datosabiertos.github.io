Las ventas del comercio minorista registraron una subida interanual del 4,7% en C-LM durante el mes de octubre

Spanish retail sales grew significantly in 2023, despite a recent monthly decline, maintaining positive annual trends. At the same time, employment in the sector expanded, reflecting sustained economic activity and stability within the market. This data highlights robust consumer demand and job creation, serving as a key indicator of broader economic health. It underscores the resilience of the retail industry, demonstrated by consecutive months of growth in both revenue and workforce. Such macroeconomic indicators are crucial for open data initiatives, as they provide transparent, high-quality evidence of societal trends. Accessible official statistics enable researchers and policymakers to analyze economic shifts and make informed decisions based on reliable, publicly available information.
