Oaxaca shows significant growth in its informal economy, reflecting a broader national trend in which informal sectors are expanding their value-added output. This surge indicates that a substantial portion of the workforce is engaged in vulnerable, unregulated labor rather than formal employment opportunities. The data reveal that more than 80 percent of Oaxaca’s economically active population works in the informal sector, highlighting a deep structural reliance on informal economic activities for livelihoods. This high percentage underscores the persistence of labor market vulnerabilities and the limited reach of formal job creation in the region. This analysis is crucial for open-data communities, as it demonstrates how publicly available statistical indicators can expose critical socioeconomic disparities. Understanding these metrics helps policymakers and citizens identify areas needing intervention and ensures transparency in tracking national economic health beyond the official corporate sector.
