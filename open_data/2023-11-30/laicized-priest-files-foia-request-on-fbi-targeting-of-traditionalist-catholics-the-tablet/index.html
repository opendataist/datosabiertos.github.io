Laicized Priest Files FOIA Request on FBI Targeting of Traditionalist Catholics - The Tablet

A conservative Catholic organization, led by a laicized priest, has filed a Freedom of Information Act request to determine the extent of the FBI’s investigation into traditional Catholics. This legal action stems from a controversial leaked memorandum that suggested links between radical-traditionalist Catholic ideology and violent extremism, prompting widespread criticism from religious leaders who condemned the document as offensive and indicative of religious profiling. The initiative highlights significant concerns regarding religious liberty and the potential for law enforcement to target citizens based on their faith practices. By seeking transparency, the organization aims to uncover how taxpayer funds are utilized in such investigations and whether Catholic hierarchy members were complicit. This move underscores the tension between national security interests and the constitutional protection of religious freedom, demanding accountability from federal authorities. This case is critically relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the vital role of the Freedom of Information Act in exposing government surveillance practices and ensuring public oversight. It illustrates how accessible records can challenge opaque law enforcement actions, protect civil liberties, and promote trust between marginalized communities and state institutions. The pursuit of these documents serves as a powerful example of using transparency mechanisms to safeguard religious rights against potential overreach.
