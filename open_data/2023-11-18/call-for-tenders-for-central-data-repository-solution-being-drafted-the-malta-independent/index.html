Call for tenders for Central Data Repository Solution being drafted - The Malta Independent

Malta is developing a Central Data Repository to implement the "once-only" principle, drastically reducing bureaucracy and administrative costs for businesses. This initiative, driven by the government’s commitment to regulatory simplification, aims to eliminate redundant documentation requests by allowing entities to submit information just once. By standardizing data sharing across public bodies, the system seeks to enhance operational efficiency and mitigate compliance risks, directly addressing a key pledge in the ruling party’s electoral manifesto and recent budget measures. The project’s relevance to open data lies in its infrastructure for secure, standardized information exchange through public APIs. By integrating with government services and adhering to eIDAS regulations, the platform promotes interoperability and privacy. This technical framework encourages the creation of open, machine-readable data flows between agencies, fostering a transparent environment where data is reused effectively. It establishes a trust-based model for verifying documents and authenticating users, which are foundational elements for robust open government data ecosystems. A high-level steering committee oversees this transition, prioritizing the creation of a shared repository for Know Your Customer checks in its initial phase. This allows for seamless information sharing between government entities while ensuring subject persons retain decision-making autonomy. By shifting focus toward risk mitigation and streamlined access, Malta is positioning itself as a more competitive jurisdiction. The move signals a broader trend toward digital-first governance, where open data standards facilitate easier business operations and improve the overall quality of public service delivery.
