Guardia Nacional registra en 2022 cifra más baja de detenidos desde su creación

The INEGI report reveals a significant drop in the number of people detained by the National Guard in 2022, marking the lowest figure since its inception in 2019. This substantial decline suggests a potential shift in policing strategies or enforcement priorities, highlighting that fewer individuals were being placed at the disposal of judicial authorities compared to previous years. Conversely, while detentions fell, the seizure of objects increased dramatically, and the confiscation of firearms decreased. These diverging trends indicate a complex operational landscape where the focus may be shifting between direct apprehension and asset interdiction, raising questions about the effectiveness and changing nature of public security interventions over time. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible statistics in evaluating government performance. By providing granular, geolocated insights into security outcomes, such censuses enable researchers and citizens to critically assess policy impacts, fostering accountability and informed public debate regarding national safety strategies.
