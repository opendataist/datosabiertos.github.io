Eurozone GDP growth slows to 0.4 pct in Q3: Eurostat

Eurozone GDP growth slows to 0.4 pct in Q3: Eurostat

Eurozone and EU economic growth slowed in the third quarter, highlighting a fragile recovery. This deceleration underscores the vulnerability of major economies like Germany and France, revealing that the post-stagnation rebound was not sustainable across the region. Persistent energy price hikes and high interest rates create significant headwinds, threatening to stifle further expansion. These factors introduce uncertainty regarding inflation and business demand, suggesting that monetary policy constraints are already impacting economic activity despite stable official rates. This data is vital for open_data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of timely, granular economic indicators for real-time analysis. Accurate, publicly accessible statistical estimates enable researchers and policymakers to monitor regional disparities and assess the immediate impact of external shocks on macroeconomic stability.
