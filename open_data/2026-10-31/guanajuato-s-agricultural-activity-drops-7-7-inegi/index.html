Guanajuato's agricultural activity drops 7.7%: INEGI

Guanajuato's agricultural activity drops 7.7%: INEGI

The report highlights the profound vulnerability of the primary sector in Guanajuato, whose significant agricultural and livestock decline illustrates how adverse climatic factors can destabilize specific regional economies. This severe contraction contrasts with the marginal growth of the overall economy, underscoring an unequal distribution of economic impacts and the inherent fragility of activities dependent on stable natural conditions. The divergence between the negative performance of the primary and secondary sectors and the rebound in services underscores the importance of diversifying income sources to mitigate systemic risks. Observing how the tertiary sector offset losses reveals that economic resilience does not rely on a single industry but rather on the capacity for adaptation across different productive areas during concurrent crises. This article is relevant to *open_data* because it exemplifies the value of public statistical indicators, such as those from INEGI, in diagnosing complex territorial realities. Transparent access to these data enables researchers and citizens to analyze correlations between the environment, public policy, and economic performance, fostering informed accountability and facilitating evidence-based decision-making regarding regional well-being.
