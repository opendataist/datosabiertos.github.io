Who is driving Mexican tourism?” • PDCC

Who is driving Mexican tourism?” • PDCC

Tourism in Mexico has solidified itself as a fundamental economic pillar, employing more than five million people, and stands out for its high rate of female participation and its significant contribution to the Gross Domestic Product. This industry not only generates massive employment but also reflects a demographic and professional transformation, where generational turnover and the growing education of the workforce indicate a structural modernization of the sector. However, critical challenges persist in the quality of employment, such as informality in certain subsectors, labor seasonality, and gender wage gaps. Despite these problems, the sector is facing a digital revolution that demands new competencies, forcing workers to integrate into technified environments. This transition requires continuous professionalization that combines traditional skills with mastery of digital tools, data analysis, and artificial intelligence to maintain global competitiveness. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the need for robust, updated, and disaggregated public statistics to accurately diagnose these labor and gender dynamics. Data transparency allows for the identification of inequalities, measurement of the impact of digitalization, and the foundation of public policies that promote talent retention and equity, being essential for understanding and improving the sustainability of such a vital and complex market as Mexican tourism.
