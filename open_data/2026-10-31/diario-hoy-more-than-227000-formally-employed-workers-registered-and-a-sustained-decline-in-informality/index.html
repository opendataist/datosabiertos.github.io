Diario HOY | More than 227,000 formally employed workers registered and a sustained decline in informality

Diario HOY | More than 227,000 formally employed workers registered and a sustained decline in informality

Data analysis by the National Institute of Statistics (INE) reveals a positive trend in the formalization of employment in Paraguay, showing a sustained increase in the number of formal and wage-earning workers. This dynamic reflects a real expansion of the labor market and a notable decline in unemployment, with specific improvements in women’s labor market integration. The reduction in informality indicates that more workers are gaining access to labor rights and social protection, thereby strengthening the country’s economic stability. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates how the official publication of structured statistical indicators enables the monitoring of social and economic progress. Transparent access to these metrics facilitates independent analysis and accountability regarding the impact of public policies. The availability of detailed data on employment and informality empowers citizens and researchers to critically evaluate government management. The correlation between the rise in formality and the decline in the unemployment rate underscores the importance of job quality. Tracking these changes over time demonstrates the effectiveness of measures implemented to integrate the workforce into the social security system. These open data not only inform but also foster an informed public debate on economic development and social well-being.
