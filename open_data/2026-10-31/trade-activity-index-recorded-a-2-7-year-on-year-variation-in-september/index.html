Trade Activity Index recorded a 2.7% year-on-year variation in September

The Commerce Activity Index experienced sustained growth, primarily driven by the expansion of e-commerce and online retail sales. This dynamism reflects an accelerated digital transformation that is reshaping traditional distribution channels, highlighting the sector’s adaptation to new consumption habits. The repair and sale of vehicle parts also contributed significantly to the overall economic recovery. This diversification suggests that market resilience does not depend on a single sector, but rather on the convergence between technical services and the progressive digitalization of retail. This information is crucial for open data because it provides precise quantitative indicators of economic health. By democratizing these data, transparency is fostered, enabling citizens, researchers, and businesses to analyze real trends and make informed decisions about innovation and evidence-based public policies.
