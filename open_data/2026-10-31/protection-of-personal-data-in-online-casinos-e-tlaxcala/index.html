Protection of personal data in online casinos - e-tlaxcala

Protection of personal data in online casinos - e-tlaxcala

This article emphasizes that data protection in online casinos requires a critical assessment that goes beyond superficial technical indicators, such as HTTPS encryption. True security depends on the operator’s transparency regarding the purposes of data processing, the clarity of its privacy notice, and the effective identification of third parties involved. This review is crucial to ensure that user consent is informed and that the risks of misuse of information are adequately mitigated. The relevance to open data lies in the importance of transparency and accountability in the handling of sensitive information. By analyzing how platforms manage and protect personal data, accountability and responsibility are promoted within the digital ecosystem. This reinforces the need for digital service providers to operate with clear and verifiable standards, enabling users to effectively exercise their rights over their information. Furthermore, the article stresses the need for users to adopt proactive security habits, such as secure credential management and verification of official channels. In the event of incidents or breaches, timely documentation and the exercise of ARCO rights (Access, Rectification, Cancellation, and Opposition) are essential to protect data integrity. This approach not only empowers individuals but also pressures institutions to maintain ethical and secure practices, aligning with the principles of openness and accountability in data management.
