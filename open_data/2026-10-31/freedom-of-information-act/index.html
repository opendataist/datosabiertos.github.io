Freedom of Information Act

This document outlines the framework through which Illinois Eastern Community College (IECC) complies with the Illinois Freedom of Information Act. It establishes that IECC is committed to facilitating public access to government records while simultaneously protecting individual privacy. The text serves as a formal declaration of the institution's intent to adhere strictly to state laws governing transparency, ensuring that educational governance remains accessible to the community it serves. Procedures for requesting public records are detailed to streamline the process for applicants. Requesters are encouraged to use a specific form that requires personal identification, a clear description of the desired documents, and preferences regarding inspection or reproduction methods. The college designates a specific FOIA Officer to manage these inquiries, providing clear contact information and timelines for responses. This structured approach ensures that both individuals and commercial entities understand their rights and responsibilities when seeking information, thereby reducing ambiguity in the disclosure process. This content is highly relevant to open_data initiatives as it exemplifies the legal and operational infrastructure necessary for data transparency in public institutions. By defining clear pathways for data access, including fee structures and appeal processes, IECC demonstrates how educational entities can balance openness with administrative efficiency. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for developers and advocates working to integrate public sector data into broader open systems, as it highlights the real-world constraints and protocols that must be navigated to achieve true data availability.
