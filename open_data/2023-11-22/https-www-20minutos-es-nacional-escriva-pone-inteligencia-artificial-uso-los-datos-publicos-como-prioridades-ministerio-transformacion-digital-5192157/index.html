Open Data
ES EN

José Luis Escrivá has taken on the portfolio for Digital Transformation with the strategic objective of integrating artificial intelligence and the management of public data as central pillars of economic policy. His priority approach involves leveraging AI’s transformative potential to boost productivity and attract talent, while simultaneously addressing social concerns associated with its rapid expansion. This vision positions technology not only as a growing economic sector but also as a fundamental tool for national competitiveness. The new minister emphasizes the critical need for data generated by public administrations to be accessible and standardized in order to design evidence-based policies. By committing to improve the availability and access to this information, Escrivá aims to facilitate economic analysis and strengthen mechanisms such as the Citizen File (Carpeta Ciudadana). This initiative seeks to democratize access to administrative information, enabling both researchers and citizens to use data to improve their interactions with the State and make informed decisions. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the explicit commitment to release and structure public data for political and social analysis. By stating that data “must be available” for policy design, the government recognizes transparency as a cornerstone of modern governance. Furthermore, his mandate to reduce the digital divide and train the population ensures that the benefits of data openness reach all layers of society, promoting genuine digital inclusion and preventing age or company size from becoming barriers to accessing information and new technologies.
