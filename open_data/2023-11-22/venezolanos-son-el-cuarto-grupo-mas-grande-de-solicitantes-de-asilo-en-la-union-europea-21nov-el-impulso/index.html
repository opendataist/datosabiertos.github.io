▷ Venezolanos son el cuarto grupo más grande de solicitantes de asilo en la Unión Europea #21Nov - El Impulso

Eurostat data reveals a significant rise in asylum applications across the European Union, with a heavy concentration of demand in just four countries. This uneven distribution highlights the challenges in achieving equitable burden-sharing among member states, as disparities in acceptance rates persist despite overall growing numbers. The majority of these claims originate from new arrivals seeking better opportunities, predominantly from Syria, Afghanistan, and Turkey. The decline in repeat applications suggests that the current surge is driven by fresh waves of migrants rather than recurrences, underscoring the evolving nature of migration pressures within the bloc. This article is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how statistical agencies like Eurostat provide essential, transparent metrics for understanding complex humanitarian crises. Access to such granular, publicly available data enables researchers, journalists, and policymakers to analyze trends accurately and advocate for evidence-based migration policies.
