INAI es 'un florero' que debe desaparecer en el próximo gobierno, según AMLO - Noticaribe

The article highlights President López Obrador’s proposal to dissolve the National Institute for Transparency, accusing the institution of inefficacy and high costs without meaningful results in combating corruption. This suggests a fundamental shift in governance strategy, prioritizing other agencies over independent oversight bodies. The critique emphasizes functional duplication with existing government entities, implying that maintaining the current institute is a financial burden rather than a necessary safeguard. By framing the institute as a tool for political attacks, the narrative questions the value of standalone transparency institutions during this administration. This is relevant to open data as it signals potential risks to data accessibility and institutional independence. If such bodies are dismantled, access to public information may become more politicized, undermining the integrity of data ecosystems and the mechanisms designed to ensure government accountability and transparency for citizens.
