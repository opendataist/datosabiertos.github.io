El precio en origen del kilo de aceite de oliva ha bajado un euro en los últimos dos meses - EFEAgro

The price of olive oil has recently declined from historic peaks, reflecting market corrections following severe supply shortages. This trend highlights how environmental factors, such as drought, drastically reduce yields, causing significant volatility in agricultural commodities and impacting both producers and consumers. Current production estimates suggest a modest recovery, yet output remains well below historical averages. This persistent scarcity continues to influence pricing dynamics, demonstrating the fragility of food supply chains in the face of climate change and unpredictable harvests. This data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the necessity of transparent and accessible agricultural statistics. By making supply and demand metrics publicly available, stakeholders can better analyze market trends, anticipate shortages, and make informed decisions that stabilize economies and ensure food security.
