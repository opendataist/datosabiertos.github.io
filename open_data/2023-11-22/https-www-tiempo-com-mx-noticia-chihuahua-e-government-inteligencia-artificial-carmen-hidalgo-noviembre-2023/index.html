Open Data
ES EN

Chihuahua has positioned itself as a regional pioneer by leading the implementation of an open data policy, anticipating national timelines and committing to the publication of structured datasets. This initiative seeks to go beyond the minimum legal requirements for transparency, setting a precedent for agility and leadership in the modernization of municipal public management in line with international standards. The central objective is to transform public information into a dynamic resource that improves citizens’ lives by fostering the development of applications that address concrete problems. By democratizing access to this data, the government invites civil society and technical experts to create innovations such as service maps or recycling tools, demonstrating that transparency is a practical tool for enhancing urban quality of life. This approach is fundamental to the open data movement, as it illustrates the evolution toward a user-centered, digitalized government. By integrating emerging technologies like artificial intelligence and reducing bureaucracy, Chihuahua shows that open data is not merely an administrative exercise, but an effective strategy for optimizing resources, fostering civic innovation, and aligning public policies with the real needs of the population.
