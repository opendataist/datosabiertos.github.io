Open Data
ES EN

Chihuahua invites civil society and experts to help shape its Open Data Policy, emphasizing a collaborative selection of high-value datasets. This participatory approach ensures that the information released actively supports civic engagement and academic research, fostering a transparent governance model. The initiative highlights the importance of open data in driving evidence-based policymaking. By making municipal information freely accessible, the government aims to empower citizens to analyze resources and propose improvements, ultimately enhancing public services and administrative efficiency across the municipality.
