Datos definitivos del Censo 2022: la Argentina tiene 46.234.830 habitantes - Actualidad TDF

The definitive results of Argentina’s 2022 National Census reveal a population of approximately 46.2 million, reflecting a significant growth rate compared to the previous decade. This increase highlights a persistent gap between projected estimates and actual census counts, largely due to the inability to cover every household and the exclusion of certain remote territories. Consequently, the final count remains slightly lower than initial projections, illustrating the practical challenges inherent in capturing a complete demographic snapshot of a large, geographically diverse nation. Beyond total numbers, the data exposes profound structural shifts in society, including an aging demographic and a widening gender gap among the elderly, where women significantly outnumber men in later life stages. These trends indicate rising dependency ratios and changing household dynamics that require urgent policy attention. Furthermore, the census marks a historical methodological shift by adopting a "right of residence" definition and utilizing a hybrid collection system. This integration of online self-registration with traditional fieldwork represents a modernization of statistical practices, aiming to improve accuracy and accessibility in future data collection efforts. This report is critically relevant to the open data community as it provides a foundational, high-quality dataset for public scrutiny and analysis. By making granular demographic, housing, and health indicators freely available, it empowers researchers, journalists, and civil society to verify government narratives and identify regional inequalities. The transition toward digital and mixed-method data collection also sets a precedent for transparency and efficiency, encouraging other institutions to adopt open, accessible standards that facilitate broader societal engagement with official statistics.
