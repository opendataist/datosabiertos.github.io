Avalan en comisiones del Congreso de Puebla "candados" para ser comisionado Itaipue

The initiative establishes strict professional and ethical criteria for appointing the head of Puebla’s transparency institute, aiming to ensure independence and technical competence. By mandating specific expertise in audit, accounting, or law, the regulation seeks to strengthen the integrity and effectiveness of internal control mechanisms within the agency. Furthermore, the rules impose significant cooling-off periods to prevent conflicts of interest, barring candidates who have recently worked with private consultancies or held political leadership roles. This approach prioritizes meritocracy and impartiality, ensuring that the appointed individual remains free from external pressures or recent ties to private auditors and political parties. This development is relevant to open data because robust institutional independence is a prerequisite for reliable information access. When transparency bodies operate without political or commercial influence, citizens can trust the quality and availability of public data, fostering greater accountability and facilitating informed civic participation.
