Avanza transformación digital de la gestión pública

Guatemala is advancing its digital government agenda through new platforms such as Info Segura, which enhances public access to safety data across municipalities. This initiative prioritizes transparency and efficient service delivery by strengthening inter-ministerial connectivity and cybersecurity measures. These efforts reflect a strategic shift toward decentralizing services and simplifying administrative processes. By leveraging technology, the government aims to create a more accessible state that directly benefits citizens through improved governance and operational efficiency. This case is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how public institutions release structured information to empower citizens. It highlights the practical application of open data principles to foster accountability and drive innovation in public administration.
