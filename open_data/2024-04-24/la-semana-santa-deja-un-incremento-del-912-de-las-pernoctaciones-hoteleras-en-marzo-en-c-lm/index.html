La Semana Santa deja un incremento del 9,12% de las pernoctaciones hoteleras en marzo en C-LM

The hotel sector in Castilla-La Mancha experienced notable growth in tourist arrivals and overnight stays, driven by a sharp increase in international visitors and higher room rates. This indicates a recovering hospitality market with enhanced pricing power and employment, reflecting broader economic resilience in the region’s service industry. Compared to other Spanish regions, Castilla-La Mancha maintained moderate occupancy levels, lagging behind popular destinations such as the Canary Islands and Madrid. While national trends show rising prices across the board, regional data highlight uneven recovery patterns among autonomous communities, affecting competitiveness. This article is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how publicly available tourism statistics enable detailed geographical and temporal analysis. Such data supports transparency in economic monitoring, allowing researchers and policymakers to assess regional disparities, track market trends, and evaluate the impact of tourism policies effectively.
