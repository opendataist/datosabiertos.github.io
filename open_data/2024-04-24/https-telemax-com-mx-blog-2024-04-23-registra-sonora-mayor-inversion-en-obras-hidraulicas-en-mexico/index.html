Sonora leads Mexico in hydraulic infrastructure investment, prioritizing water management, irrigation, and sanitation to ensure long-term resource security. This strategic focus demonstrates how government entities leverage official statistical data to drive regional development, illustrating the critical role of open data in enabling evidence-based policy formulation and transparent public investment allocation. The administration coordinates closely with private industry and agricultural unions to implement projects that guarantee water supply for all municipalities. By addressing both potable water access and agricultural needs through targeted infrastructure improvements, the state ensures economic stability for sectors like livestock and crop production, highlighting the practical application of data-informed planning in crisis prevention and resource optimization. This initiative is relevant to open data as it showcases how accessible, high-quality statistical information facilitates effective multi-sector collaboration. By utilizing insights from national surveys, public authorities can identify critical gaps, allocate resources efficiently, and monitor progress toward sustainable water management goals, thereby empowering citizens and stakeholders to understand and engage with public service delivery outcomes.
