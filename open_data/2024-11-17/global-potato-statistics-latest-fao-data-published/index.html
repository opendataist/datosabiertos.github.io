Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The article highlights the FAO’s role in providing accessible global agricultural statistics, specifically detailing worldwide potato production figures from its FAOSTAT database. It emphasizes that while recent updates include more current years, the core dataset offers a comprehensive view of production volumes across one hundred nations, identifying China and India as the dominant producers. This information allows researchers and policymakers to track major trends in global crop supply and understand the distribution of agricultural output on an international scale. Furthermore, the text underscores the importance of verifying data sources, noting that FAO figures may slightly differ from national reports but remain reliable for broad analysis. The piece serves as a guide for users to navigate the FAOSTAT platform independently, demonstrating how to extract specific crop data. This accessibility empowers users to conduct their own inquiries, fostering a deeper engagement with open statistical resources. The narrative encourages critical thinking regarding data origins and consistency, which is vital for accurate economic and agricultural modeling. This content is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how international organizations publish transparent, standardized datasets that enable global monitoring and comparison. By making such extensive agricultural records publicly available, the FAO supports evidence-based decision-making and academic research. The article illustrates the practical application of open data in tracking food security and production efficiency, highlighting the value of unified, interoperable standards in facilitating cross-border analysis.
