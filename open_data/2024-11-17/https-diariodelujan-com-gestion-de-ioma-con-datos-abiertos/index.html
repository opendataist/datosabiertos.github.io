Open Data
ES EN

The Obra Médico Asistencial Institute (IOMA) has launched an open data dashboard that modernizes its transparency by making key statistics on its budget management and health outcomes publicly available. This initiative responds to legal frameworks and the demand for access to information, enabling the community to monitor how resources supporting more than two million affiliates are allocated. The central focus is to ensure that the social security organization’s management is visible and accountable, thereby strengthening trust in the solidarity-based system. The published data reveal sustained growth in the provision of health services, surpassing indicators from previous years. There is a notable increase in outpatient consultations, surgeries, and the dispensing of medications, particularly for complex conditions, as well as greater distribution of supplies such as prostheses and hearing aids. This expansion of the service offering indicates an extension of territorial access to healthcare, demonstrating that the institution is meeting the growing needs of its beneficiary population with broader coverage of essential services. The relevance of this article to the field of open data lies in the practical demonstration of how publishing structured public information facilitates accountability in critical health services. By transforming operational data into accessible visualizations, IOMA exemplifies how digital transparency empowers citizens to assess the effectiveness of public policies and the management of state resources, establishing a governance model that prioritizes clarity and direct access to information of social interest.
