Mongolia becomes first country in Asia to join web platform for political finance transparency

Mongolia’s General Election Commission became the first in Asia to join the OGP’s new platform for publishing political finance data, a move certified by the oversight committee to enhance transparency in electoral funding. This initiative highlights how open data standards can be successfully implemented by national electoral bodies to foster greater public trust and accountability in democratic processes across the region. The event underscores the growing importance of digital transparency in governance, demonstrating that shared open data practices are crucial for strengthening democracy and combating corruption in the Asia-Pacific area.
