STPS e IMSS exhortan a más de 22 mil empresas con REPSE a cumplir sus obligaciones en materia de seguridad social - Kioscomayor

STPS e IMSS exhortan a más de 22 mil empresas con REPSE a cumplir sus obligaciones en materia de seguridad social - Kioscomayor

The Mexican Social Security Institute (IMSS) and the Ministry of Labor and Social Welfare (STPS) have intensified efforts to verify social security compliance among contractors listed in the REPSE registry. By cross-referencing institutional databases, authorities identified numerous companies failing to meet these obligations, highlighting the critical intersection between labor law enforcement and public data transparency. Non-compliant firms face potential cancellation of their registration and fiscal sanctions, urging them to regularize their status promptly. This coordinated approach demonstrates how interoperable government data systems can effectively monitor regulatory adherence and protect workers' rights without relying solely on individual inspections. This initiative is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the power of shared administrative information across different government agencies. It underscores the necessity of accessible, standardized datasets that enable real-time verification of compliance, fostering accountability and ensuring that public registries remain accurate, reliable, and useful for both regulators and citizens.
