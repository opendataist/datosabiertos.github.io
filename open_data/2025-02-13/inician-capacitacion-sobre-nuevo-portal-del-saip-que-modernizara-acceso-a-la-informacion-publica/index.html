Inician capacitación sobre nuevo portal del SAIP que 'modernizará' acceso a la información pública

The Dominican government is modernizing its access-to-information system through a new digital portal and targeted training for public officials. This initiative aims to enhance transparency by replacing outdated infrastructure with advanced technology that streamlines the handling of citizen requests and ensures timely responses from various state entities. A key feature of this reform is the implementation of real-time monitoring and detailed statistical tracking. The system will automatically flag delays or repeated denials of information, creating a mechanism for accountability. By integrating geographic and procedural data, authorities can better evaluate institutional performance regarding legal mandates. This development is relevant to open data because it establishes the technical and administrative framework for proactive transparency. By enforcing strict response timelines and creating audit trails for information requests, the government moves toward a culture of accountable public administration, ensuring citizens can effectively access information on how public resources are managed.
