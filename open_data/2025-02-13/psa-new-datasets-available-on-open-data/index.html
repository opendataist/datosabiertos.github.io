PSA – New datasets available on Open Data

PSA – New datasets available on Open Data

The Halifax Regional Municipality has expanded its open data repository with new inspection records and critical updates to existing datasets. By adding fields that clarify work orders, occupancy permits, and housing units, these enhancements significantly improve the utility of municipal information for external agencies and researchers. This initiative reflects a long-standing commitment to transparency and resident engagement, formalized over a decade ago to foster trust in local governance. The continuous addition of diverse data, ranging from transit schedules to waste collection, ensures the platform remains a comprehensive resource for public scrutiny and community planning. Open data is vital for promoting accountability and enabling evidence-based decision-making within communities. By making detailed municipal information accessible, such programs empower citizens and organizations to analyze urban trends, hold officials responsible, and develop solutions that address local needs effectively.
