Visitará INE a más de siete mil personas para integrar las casillas de Coahuila

Visitará INE a más de siete mil personas para integrar las casillas de Coahuila

Coahuila has launched the selection of citizens to serve as polling station officials for its first judicial election, emphasizing smooth progress and strong civic commitment. Despite minimal rejections, the process relies on public engagement to ensure transparency, particularly since political party representatives will not be present at the voting tables. The initiative highlights a shift toward direct citizen involvement in judicial oversight, inviting those skeptical of popularly elected judges to register as observers. This strategy aims to build trust and validate the new system through direct participation, turning potential opposition into constructive scrutiny during both the voting and vote-counting phases. This development is crucial for open data, as it establishes a precedent for transparent, citizen-driven electoral administration in judicial matters. By encouraging broad observation, the process creates a robust framework for accountability, demonstrating how public engagement can enhance the credibility and openness of previously opaque institutional processes.
