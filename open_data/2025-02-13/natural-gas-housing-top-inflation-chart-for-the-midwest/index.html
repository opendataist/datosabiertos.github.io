Natural gas, housing top inflation chart for the Midwest

The Midwest experienced a 3% inflation rise in 2024, driven primarily by significant cost increases in housing, food, and energy. Piped natural gas was the most affected category, while housing costs also surged, impacting residents’ daily expenses. Conversely, only a few sectors, such as used vehicles, saw price reductions. This economic shift is critical to open data initiatives because transparent, accessible government statistics are essential for understanding these trends. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reports provide the necessary infrastructure for researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze regional economic health and hold institutions accountable. Understanding how inflation impacts specific demographics allows for better advocacy and policy design. Open data enables the public to track these disparities, fostering informed discussions on economic stability and ensuring that statistical reporting remains clear, accurate, and useful for community planning and resource allocation.
