Reitera AMLO rechazo al Inai: es un cero a la izquierda y no sirve para nada

The article reveals President López Obrador’s strong criticism of the INAI, labeling it ineffective and accusing it of facilitating corruption and wasting public funds. He argues that the institution’s current paralysis, caused by unfilled counselor positions, highlights its redundancy and suggests transferring its functions to existing bodies like the Auditor General. This stance is significant for open data because it threatens the independence of the primary agency responsible for protecting citizens' right to access public information. The lack of leadership not only halts operations but also undermines transparency mechanisms, potentially allowing the government to obscure data rather than disclose it, which contradicts the core principles of open data governance. The relevance lies in the potential weakening of institutional oversight. When an open data authority is deemed useless or replaced by less independent entities, the safeguard against governmental opacity diminishes. This political narrative risks eroding public trust in information freedom and could lead to structural changes that prioritize executive control over citizens' rights to transparent and accessible state information.
