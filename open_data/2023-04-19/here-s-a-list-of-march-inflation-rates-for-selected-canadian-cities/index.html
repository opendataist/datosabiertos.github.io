Here's a list of March inflation rates for selected Canadian cities

Statistics Canada reports Canada’s annual inflation hit 4.3% in March, marking a significant slowdown from previous highs. While national figures offer a broad trend, major cities showed varied rates, reflecting regional economic disparities. The agency cautioned that local data, derived from small samples, may fluctuate wildly. This statistical uncertainty highlights the challenges in interpreting granular regional metrics for policy decisions or economic analysis. For open data enthusiasts, this underscores the critical importance of metadata and sample size awareness. Reliable public sector datasets must clearly communicate limitations to prevent misuse of volatile or aggregated statistics in broader analytical contexts.
