Panistas toman tribuna del Senado; exigen nombrar a comisionados del INAI

The PAN party insists on the immediate appointment of two missing commissioners to the INAI. They argue that leaving the institute paralyzed with only three members is unacceptable. This highlights the fragility of open data institutions when political will fails. Without full staffing, transparency and access to information mechanisms remain incomplete. This situation shows that open data relies on institutional stability. Political blockades can severely hinder public access to government information and accountability.
