La tasa de paro de Reino Unido sube al 3,8% en febrero, por encima de lo previsto

The UK’s unemployment rate rose, with job cuts primarily affecting full-time positions while part-time employment increased. Job vacancies remain high despite recent declines, reflecting widespread economic uncertainty that continues to slow hiring across industries. Real wages fell as salary growth lagged behind inflation, eroding workers’ purchasing power. Although nominal pay increased, the higher cost of living offset these gains, underscoring the ongoing financial pressure on employees. This data underscores the critical importance of open labor statistics in monitoring economic health. Transparent access to employment metrics enables accurate analysis of market trends and informs policy decisions on inflation and workforce stability.
