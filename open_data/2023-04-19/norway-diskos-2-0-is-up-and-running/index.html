Norway: Diskos 2.0 is up and running

Diskos 2.0 represents a significant modernization of the Norwegian Petroleum Directorate’s data repository, shifting critical exploration and production data to a cloud-based infrastructure. This transition enables direct processing and interpretation within the system, eliminating the need for local downloads and streamlining workflows for industry users. The platform integrates automated processes and API connections, fostering new opportunities for data utilization through enhanced accessibility. By leveraging industry expertise and established technical platforms, the upgrade ensures that the solution aligns closely with actual user workflows, supported by continuous development cycles driven by cooperative efforts among member companies. This update is highly relevant to open data advocates as it demonstrates how robust public-private collaboration can drive digital infrastructure improvements. It highlights the importance of accessible, cloud-native repositories that facilitate efficient data exchange and reduce barriers to accessing essential geological and production information, ultimately promoting greater transparency and usability in the energy sector.
