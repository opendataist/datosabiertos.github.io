China crece más de lo previsto en el primer trimestre y Asia cierra con signo mixto

China’s first-quarter economic growth surpassed expectations, fueled by strong retail sales and the relaxation of pandemic controls, signaling a steady initial recovery. This performance underscores the positive impact of stabilizing policies on domestic consumption, even as industrial production fell short of forecasts. Despite this strong start, underlying challenges remain, including insufficient internal demand and a fragile foundation for sustained growth. Market reactions were mixed, reflecting skepticism about the durability of this expansion amid a volatile global environment and a decline in external demand.
