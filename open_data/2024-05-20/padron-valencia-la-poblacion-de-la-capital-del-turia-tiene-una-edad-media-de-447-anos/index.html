Padrón València: La población de la capital del Turia tiene una edad media de 44,7 años

The Municipal Statistics Office of Valencia has published its 2024 census data, providing a comprehensive and pre-processed dataset that serves as a vital resource for public transparency. This release enables detailed analysis of the population across various territorial divisions, ensuring that complex demographic information is accessible and usable by both citizens and researchers. By offering structured tabulations, the municipality demonstrates its commitment to making essential civic data available in a format that facilitates easy interpretation and reuse. The data reveals a significant population growth of 2.5%, accompanied by distinct demographic shifts, such as an aging local population and changing family structures. Crucially, the census highlights a diverse and increasingly international community, particularly noting substantial increases in foreign nationals from countries like Russia, Ukraine, Colombia, and Argentina. These groups tend to be younger, contrasting with the higher average age of the native population, which indicates evolving social dynamics within the city’s districts. This article is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the successful publication of granular, administrative statistics that drive evidence-based decision-making. The availability of this clean, structured data empowers stakeholders to analyze trends in housing, education, and migration without needing specialized technical skills. It underscores the importance of municipal transparency in fostering an informed society capable of understanding and engaging with local social realities.
