Desciende el número de viviendas turísticas en Baleares

Desciende el número de viviendas turísticas en Baleares

The INE’s experimental statistics reveal a critical imbalance in the density of tourist accommodations in the Balearic Islands, where in several municipalities the supply far exceeds the local population. This extreme concentration highlights the pressure exerted by the vacation sector on residential life, with some towns recording more tourist beds than inhabitants—a phenomenon that profoundly alters the urban and social dynamics of these localities. Nationally, the number of tourist apartments has grown significantly, led by regions such as Andalusia and the Valencian Community. However, the Balearic Islands maintain the highest proportion of this type of housing relative to the total housing stock, despite a recent slight downward trend. This territorial disparity underscores the need for accurate data to manage resources and regulate tourist activity in a sustainable manner. This report is crucial for the open data movement because it exposes the limitations of official sources, such as the use of outdated censuses for complex data cross-referencing. Transparency in methodology and the availability of up-to-date information enable civil society and researchers to critically analyze the impact of tourism, thereby driving better evidence-based public policies grounded in real and accessible data.
