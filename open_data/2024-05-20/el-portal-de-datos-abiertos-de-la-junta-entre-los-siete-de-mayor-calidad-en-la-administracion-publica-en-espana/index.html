El Portal de Datos Abiertos de la Junta, entre los siete de mayor calidad en la Administración pública en España

El Portal de Datos Abiertos de la Junta, entre los siete de mayor calidad en la Administración pública en España

The Open Data Portal of the Junta de Castilla y León has been ranked among the top seven in Spain for quality, achieving the highest evaluation level for reusability. This recognition highlights the administration’s successful focus on making public data easily accessible and usable for third-party development, distinguishing it from many other regional and local government portals across the country. This achievement underscores the critical importance of data reusability as a core pillar of effective open data strategies. By ensuring datasets are updated in real-time and accompanied by professional tools, the portal enables citizens and businesses to freely create new services and products. This approach transforms raw information into tangible economic value and social wealth, demonstrating how transparent governance directly empowers communities. The article is highly relevant to open_data because it validates the principle that high-quality metadata and user-centric design are essential for true openness. It illustrates that when administrative bodies prioritize clear presentation and robust infrastructure, they foster deeper civic engagement and innovation. Consequently, this case serves as a benchmark for other public entities aiming to maximize the societal impact of their published data.
