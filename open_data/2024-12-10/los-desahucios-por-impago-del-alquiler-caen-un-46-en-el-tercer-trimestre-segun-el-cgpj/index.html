Los desahucios por impago del alquiler caen un 4,6% en el tercer trimestre, según el CGPJ

The article reveals a nuanced shift in Spain’s housing justice landscape, highlighting that while rental evictions declined slightly, mortgage foreclosures surged dramatically. This divergence indicates that the immediate pressure on renters has eased somewhat, yet the financial strain on homeowners facing mortgage defaults has intensified significantly. From an open data perspective, this report underscores the critical importance of granular, regionalized statistics for effective policy analysis. By disaggregating data by region and legal cause, authorities can identify specific local crises and tailor interventions more accurately than relying on aggregated national figures alone. Ultimately, the data serves as a vital public resource for transparency and accountability. It allows civil society, researchers, and policymakers to monitor the real-time impacts of economic crises on housing security, ensuring that resource allocation responds to emerging trends rather than outdated assumptions.
