Industria editorial se reinventa en Guadalajara

Industria editorial se reinventa en Guadalajara

The 34th International Publishers Congress in Guadalajara underscored the critical need to balance emerging technologies, such as artificial intelligence, with the protection of authors’ rights and publishing freedom. By examining how digital tools affect creators, the event highlighted the urgency of establishing ethical frameworks that preserve intellectual integrity while embracing innovation in the contemporary publishing landscape. Strategic global alliances were forged to strengthen collaboration between major publishers and independent actors, emphasizing a unified approach to sustainability and diversity. These networking efforts aim to build a more resilient industry capable of supporting the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals through inclusive practices, ensuring that economic growth does not come at the expense of cultural diversity or equitable opportunities for all stakeholders. This gathering is vital for open data, as it reinforces the principle that access to information must coexist with ethical data governance and the protection of creators’ rights. The initiative to educate young creators about copyright underscores the broader need for transparent, accessible frameworks that safeguard intellectual property while fostering an environment where knowledge can be shared responsibly. This balance ensures that open data initiatives remain sustainable, equitable, and respectful of individual contributions, paving the way for a fairer global information ecosystem.
