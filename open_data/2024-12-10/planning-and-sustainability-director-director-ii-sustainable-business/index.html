Planning and Sustainability Director (Director II) - Sustainable Business

The Portland Bureau of Planning and Sustainability stands as a national model for integrating open data into urban governance, leveraging transparency to drive climate action and digital equity. By maintaining one of the country’s most advanced waste and recycling systems alongside robust community technology initiatives, the bureau demonstrates how accessible information can empower residents and improve policy outcomes. This commitment to openness is not merely technical but foundational to its mission of protecting communities through accountable technological application. A pivotal aspect of this leadership is the emphasis on racial and economic justice through transparent decision-making processes. The bureau actively uses publicly accessible data to ensure accountability, ensuring that investments in clean energy and community benefits reach historically marginalized populations. This approach highlights the critical role of open data in bridging the digital divide and fostering equitable resource distribution, proving that data transparency is essential for achieving social justice goals in urban planning. For open data advocates, this role illustrates how strategic data usage supports broader societal values like anti-racism and fiscal responsibility. The position requires a leader who views data not just as a metric, but as a tool for community engagement and systemic change. By prioritizing transparency and measurable outcomes, the bureau offers a blueprint for how local governments can utilize open data to build trust, enhance democratic participation, and effectively address complex environmental and social challenges.
