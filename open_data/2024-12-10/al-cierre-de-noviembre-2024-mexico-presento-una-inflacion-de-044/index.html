Al cierre de noviembre 2024 México presentó una inflación de 0,44%

Mexico’s inflation rose slightly, driven largely by energy costs following the end of summer subsidies. This data underscores the critical need for open access to detailed price indices to monitor economic shifts. Transparent statistics empower citizens and researchers to understand how policy changes, such as subsidy removals, affect everyday expenses. Open data is essential for tracking these fluctuations and assessing government measures. By making such information freely available, INEGI supports informed public debate and analysis of Mexico’s economic health. Ultimately, reliable, open statistical records are vital for transparency. They enable society to scrutinize the drivers of inflation and hold institutions accountable. This accessibility ensures that economic data serves the public interest rather than remaining obscure.
