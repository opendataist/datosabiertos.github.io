Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production reached substantial levels in 2021, with China and India emerging as the dominant forces. Their combined output significantly outweighs other nations, highlighting a concentration of agricultural power that shapes international supply dynamics. This distribution suggests that policy shifts or harvest variances in these two countries can disproportionately impact worldwide availability and pricing, making their data critical for economic modeling and trade analysis. The extensive list of contributing nations demonstrates the crop’s universal agricultural relevance. With nearly one hundred countries reporting significant yields, potato cultivation serves as a vital component of food security across diverse climates and economies. This widespread production indicates that the commodity is not merely a niche product but a staple embedded in global dietary habits, influencing everything from local farming practices to international export strategies. This article is highly relevant to open data because it relies on FAOSTAT, a premier repository for transparent, publicly accessible agricultural statistics. Researchers and analysts depend on such standardized datasets to compare production metrics across borders and track trends over time. By referencing this database, the report underscores the importance of accessible, consistent data infrastructure in supporting informed decision-making within the food and agricultural sectors.
