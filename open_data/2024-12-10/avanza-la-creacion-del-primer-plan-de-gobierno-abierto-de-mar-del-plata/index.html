Avanza la creación del Primer Plan de Gobierno Abierto de Mar del Plata

Mar del Plata launches its first Open Government Action Plan, inviting citizens to shape public policy through a public consultation. This initiative aims to boost civic participation, enhance transparency, and strengthen accountability within local governance structures. By engaging with civil society and academic partners, the municipality seeks to align public decisions with community needs. The resulting plan serves as a concrete commitment to openness and collaborative governance, fostering trust between the administration and residents. This effort is relevant to open data as it exemplifies how transparent, participatory frameworks rely on accessible information. By encouraging public input, the project highlights the essential role of data openness in driving responsive, inclusive, and accountable local government actions.
