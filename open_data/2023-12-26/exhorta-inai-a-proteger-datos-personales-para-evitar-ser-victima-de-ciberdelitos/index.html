Exhorta INAI a proteger datos personales para evitar ser víctima de ciberdelitos

The INAI underscores the urgent need for citizens to safeguard their personal data during the vacation season, a period characterized by a significant increase in online transactions. This guidance highlights the critical link between individual digital hygiene and broader information security standards, serving as a practical case study for open data principles. Key recommendations include limiting the use of public networks, securing mobile devices with encryption, and refraining from sharing location data in real time on social media. These measures help mitigate the risks of identity theft and financial fraud, demonstrating how individual behaviors affect the integrity and privacy of digital ecosystems. This article is relevant to open data because it emphasizes the necessity of robust privacy frameworks when personal information is processed or shared. It reinforces the principle that transparency and access must be balanced with security measures, ensuring that open initiatives do not compromise citizen safety or facilitate the misuse of personal details.
