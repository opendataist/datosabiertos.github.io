Castilla-La Mancha, a la vanguardia en Gobierno Abierto tras una década de trabajo en materia de transparencia, participación e integridad

Castilla-La Mancha, a la vanguardia en Gobierno Abierto tras una década de trabajo en materia de transparencia, participación e integridad

Castilla-La Mancha has consolidated its leadership in open government through a comprehensive legal framework that covers transparency, citizen participation, and integrity. This decade-long effort has established a robust structure for ethical public administration, demonstrating how sustained policy evolution can create a solid foundation for accountability. By finalizing its legal framework on public integrity, the region underscores the importance of aligning legislation with modern governance standards to ensure responsible management of public affairs. The administration actively collaborates with academia and civil society to deepen these principles, notably through a partnership with the University of Castilla-La Mancha. This alliance facilitates research, the dissemination of an open governance culture, and practical initiatives such as ethical guidelines for senior officials. These efforts highlight the value of institutional cooperation in translating theoretical concepts into tangible improvements, fostering a culture of integrity that extends from regional leadership down to local entities. This development is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical intersection between regulatory frameworks and data accessibility. The emphasis on improving technological tools for information access and the role of artificial intelligence in public procurement demonstrate how data serves as a backbone for transparency. By integrating data-driven strategies into these ethical and participatory structures, the region shows that open data is not merely a technical requirement but a vital component of effective, trustworthy, and inclusive governance.
