Mallorca se prepara para la avalancha de turistas: Escorca multiplicará 68 veces su población

Mallorca se prepara para la avalancha de turistas: Escorca multiplicará 68 veces su población

Local authorities in Mallorca argue that official statistics significantly underestimate tourism pressure, as many day-trippers visit scenic spots without staying overnight. This discrepancy highlights a critical gap in traditional data collection methods, where the actual number of visitors far exceeds the resident population in numerous municipalities, creating intense seasonal pressure on local infrastructure and resources. The magnitude of this imbalance varies across the island, with some towns seeing their populations multiply several times over during peak months. This widespread phenomenon indicates that current administrative records fail to capture the full scope of daily visitor flows, particularly those relying on mobile positioning data for tracking. Consequently, the disparity between registered residents and transient tourists reveals a systemic blind spot in how tourism impacts are measured and managed. This article is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of integrating alternative data sources, such as mobile network analytics, into official statistics. By leveraging open and real-time mobility data, policymakers can achieve a more accurate understanding of tourism dynamics. This approach enables better resource allocation and sustainable planning, ensuring that public services align with the true scale of visitor presence rather than relying solely on incomplete hotel registration figures.
