Posting a social media disclaimer won't stop AI from using your data. This will | BBC Science Focus Magazine

Posting a social media disclaimer won't stop AI from using your data. This will | BBC Science Focus Magazine

Users often rely on viral "copypasta" disclaimers to refuse AI data harvesting, but platforms systematically ignore these texts as false information. Relying on such memes is ineffective, as they do not alter algorithmic behavior or legal terms. To actually protect privacy, individuals must navigate specific settings and privacy options rather than posting random text. This distinction highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible mechanisms in open data governance. This topic matters because it exposes the gap between user intent and platform practice. Clear, enforceable user controls are essential for ethical open data, ensuring that consent is genuinely respected rather than bypassed by ignored noise.
