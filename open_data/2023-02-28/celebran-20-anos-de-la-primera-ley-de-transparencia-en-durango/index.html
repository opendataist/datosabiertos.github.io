Celebran 20 años de la primera Ley de Transparencia en Durango

Twenty years after Durango enacted its first transparency law, experts highlighted ongoing challenges and the need for a cultural shift. They emphasize that new generations must effectively exercise their right to know, ensuring accountability and access to information. This event underscores the critical role of legal frameworks in promoting open data. By fostering a mindset that values transparency, society can better leverage public information for democratic engagement and institutional trust. The relevance to open data lies in the continuous effort to make government information accessible and usable. Achieving this requires not just legal provisions, but also educational initiatives that empower citizens to utilize data for informed decision-making.
