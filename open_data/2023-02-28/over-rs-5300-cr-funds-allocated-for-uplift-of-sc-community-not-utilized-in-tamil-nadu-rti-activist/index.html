Over Rs 5,300 cr funds allocated for uplift of SC community not utilized in Tamil Nadu: RTI activist

A significant portion of funds allocated for the welfare of Scheduled Caste communities in Tamil Nadu remains unspent. This revelation, obtained through information disclosure laws, highlights gaps in implementation and the effective use of public resources designated for marginalized groups. Such data exposes inefficiencies in how state budgets are executed to uplift specific social strata. It underscores the necessity for transparency in government spending, ensuring that allocated resources actually reach the intended beneficiaries rather than remaining dormant in official accounts. This case is crucial for the open data movement as it demonstrates how accessible public records can drive accountability. By making expenditure data available, citizens and activists can monitor government performance, advocate for better governance, and ensure equitable development outcomes for historically disadvantaged communities.
