Australian women having fewer children later in life: statistics bureau - 2LT News

Australian data reveals a significant demographic shift where women are having fewer children later in life, with the fertility rate dropping below replacement levels. This trend highlights a substantial rise in births to older mothers alongside a historic low among teenage mothers, reflecting long-term societal changes in family planning. This information is crucial for open_data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of transparent, high-quality government statistics in tracking complex social trends. Accessible, granular demographic data allows researchers and policymakers to understand evolving population dynamics, ensuring that public services and economic forecasts accurately reflect the realities of an aging society.
