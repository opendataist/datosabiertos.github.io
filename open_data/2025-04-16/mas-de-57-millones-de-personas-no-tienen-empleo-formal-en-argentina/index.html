Más de 5,7 millones de personas no tienen empleo formal en Argentina

Más de 5,7 millones de personas no tienen empleo formal en Argentina

The INDEC report reveals persistent labor informality in Argentina, disproportionately affecting vulnerable groups such as youth and women. This highlights systemic gaps in social protection and tax contributions, undermining economic stability and equity. Data shows high rates of unregistered work in key sectors such as construction and domestic service, alongside practices like underreporting salaries within formal firms. These irregularities distort labor market analysis and challenge efforts to formalize the economy. This is crucial for open data advocates as it underscores the need for transparent, granular datasets to expose hidden informal practices. Accessible, detailed statistics are essential for designing effective policies that protect workers and ensure fair tax contributions across all economic segments.
