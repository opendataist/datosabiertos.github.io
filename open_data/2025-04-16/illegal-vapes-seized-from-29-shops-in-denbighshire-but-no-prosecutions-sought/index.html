Illegal vapes seized from 29 shops in Denbighshire - but no prosecutions sought

Illegal vapes seized from 29 shops in Denbighshire - but no prosecutions sought

A FOI request reveals that despite seizing illegal vaping products from numerous premises, Denbighshire County Council initiated zero prosecutions. This highlights a significant enforcement gap where substantial quantities of hazardous, unregulated devices are confiscated without legal accountability for the sellers. The council claims no direct disposal costs exist, as recycling is externally funded, yet admits calculating the value of seized goods is impossible due to product diversity. This lack of financial transparency contrasts with broader industry reports indicating massive volumes and values of illicit vapes removed from the UK market annually. This data is crucial for open data advocates as it demonstrates how FOI applications can expose systemic failures in regulatory oversight. By making internal council decisions and enforcement statistics public, citizens can better assess whether public bodies are effectively protecting health standards or merely managing symptoms without addressing root causes.
