Qué significa que Meta vaya a usar tus datos para entrenar a su inteligencia artificial

Meta has obtained authorization from the European Union to use publicly generated content from adult users on its platforms as training material for its artificial intelligence models. This development enables the company to collect posts and comments to refine its generative systems, thereby ensuring that its assistants’ responses are culturally and linguistically appropriate for the European community. The process requires explicit user consent, with individuals notified via email and in-app alerts. Users can easily opt out of having their data used, ensuring that only public information is utilized while excluding private messages and data from minors. This measure balances technological needs with individual privacy, keeping the user as the ultimate decision-maker in managing their information. This news is relevant to the open data movement because it transforms the discourse on intellectual property and access to digital information. Although Meta argues that the goal is to improve services through a feedback loop, it highlights the tension between the large-scale extraction of data for AI training and the right to individual data sovereignty. It reflects the growing need for robust regulatory frameworks that define how public information is used, questioning whether opt-in consent is sufficient when algorithms learn complex patterns of visible human behavior.
