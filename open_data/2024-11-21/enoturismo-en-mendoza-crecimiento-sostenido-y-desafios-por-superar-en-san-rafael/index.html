Enoturismo en Mendoza:crecimiento sostenido y desafíos por superar en San Rafael

The article highlights the exponential growth of wine tourism in Mendoza, transforming it from a niche complement into a primary economic driver that significantly boosts the local hospitality and gastronomy sectors. This surge demonstrates how targeted private-sector investments can create new demand, turning previously unknown locations into major international destinations. The success of San Rafael, which attracts a substantial share of provincial visitors, proves that developing attractive infrastructure directly stimulates tourist flows and regional development. Despite these achievements, critical infrastructure deficits pose severe risks to sustained growth. Inadequate connectivity, including unreliable cellular signals and poor road conditions, severely limits access for middle- to high-income tourists. The lack of convenient flight schedules further isolates the region, preventing it from capitalizing on weekend travelers who are essential for economic amplification. These logistical barriers highlight a disconnect between private innovation and public policy, where infrastructure improvements fail to keep pace with market evolution. This case is highly relevant to open data initiatives because transparent, accessible information on tourism metrics and infrastructure gaps is vital for evidence-based policymaking. Open data can reveal the true correlation between investment in connectivity and tourist retention, helping governments prioritize spending more effectively. By making this data public, stakeholders can advocate for better resource allocation, ensuring that future growth is supported by robust, data-driven public services rather than relying solely on private-sector agility.
