SAS acquires Hazy synthetic data software - IT-Online

SAS has acquired Hazy’s synthetic data technology, signaling a strategic shift toward integrating privacy-preserving AI tools into enterprise workflows. This move addresses critical industry challenges regarding data scarcity and strict regulatory compliance in sensitive sectors like healthcare and finance. By enabling safe experimentation without exposing identifiable information, the acquisition allows organizations to harness AI capabilities while mitigating privacy risks. The integration of synthetic data significantly enhances model accuracy and reduces bias, offering data scientists robust datasets for complex analytics. As generative AI adoption accelerates, businesses increasingly rely on synthetic data to overcome quality and access limitations. This technology empowers researchers to explore scenarios previously deemed unreachable due to restrictions on real data usage, fostering deeper innovation and more reliable outcomes. This development is highly relevant to the open data community as it demonstrates a scalable method for sharing valuable information without compromising individual privacy. Synthetic data provides a viable pathway for organizations to contribute to open datasets while adhering to ethical standards and legal constraints. Consequently, it expands the scope of available analytical resources, promoting greater transparency and accessibility in data-driven research and public policy applications.
