Regalos sustentables: las mujeres prefieren cada vez más productos comprometidos con el cuidado del ambiente

The study reveals that a significant majority of women have stopped buying brands with negative social or environmental impacts, establishing them as primary drivers of responsible consumption. This shift highlights a growing market demand for products that align with ethical values and ecological preservation, moving beyond traditional purchasing habits to prioritize sustainability. Brands responding to this trend, such as Wandergreen, are succeeding by integrating eco-friendly materials and social purpose into their business models. By offering durable, recyclable products that support reforestation and inclusive employment, these companies demonstrate how commercial success can directly contribute to environmental restoration and social equity. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the increasing availability and importance of consumer behavior datasets in understanding market shifts. Analyzing such data allows stakeholders to track the correlation between ethical awareness and purchasing decisions, facilitating evidence-based strategies that promote transparency and accountability in corporate sustainability practices.
