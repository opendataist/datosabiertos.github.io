Uttarakhand Governor Gurmit Singh felicitates Information officers on the 19th anniversary of RTI Act

Uttarakhand’s Police Department was honored for effectively implementing the Right to Information Act, demonstrating high levels of transparency and accountability. By rapidly disposing of applications, they strengthened good governance, proving that open data mechanisms enhance administrative responsiveness. This recognition highlights the critical role of Public Information Officers in making government operations visible to the public. Their timely handling of requests sets a positive precedent for other agencies, encouraging a culture of openness and civic engagement across the state administration. The initiative underscores how accessible information empowers citizens to demand better governance. It serves as a practical model for other regions, illustrating that proactive disclosure not only meets legal obligations but also builds public trust and improves overall administrative efficiency through greater scrutiny.
