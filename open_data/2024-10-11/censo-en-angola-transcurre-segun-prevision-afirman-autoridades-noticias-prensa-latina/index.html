Censo en Angola transcurre según previsión, afirman autoridades - Noticias Prensa Latina

The census data collection is proceeding as planned, despite logistical challenges in areas with limited internet access that prevent real-time synchronization. Officials confirm that claims of delays or postponements are premature, emphasizing that the preparatory phases, including pilot tests, have successfully validated the country’s technical and logistical capacity. The primary focus remains on field interviews to ensure comprehensive territorial coverage. While immediate results are unavailable due to offline data entry, the information is currently being collected and will be shared once it has been properly processed. This phase highlights the operational realities of managing large-scale datasets in environments with connectivity constraints. This update is relevant to open data because it illustrates the critical gap between raw data collection and public accessibility. The reliance on offline processing delays transparency, demonstrating how infrastructure limitations can hinder timely data availability. Understanding these bottlenecks is essential for improving future open data strategies, ensuring that collected information can be efficiently digitized and released to the public for immediate societal benefit.
