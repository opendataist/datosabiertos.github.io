Es falso que Patricia Bullrich dijo: “Es muy duro pero vamos a tener que utilizar los ahorros de la gente”

Es falso que Patricia Bullrich dijo: “Es muy duro pero vamos a tener que utilizar los ahorros de la gente”

The article debunks a viral disinformation campaign alleging that Argentine presidential candidate Patricia Bullrich plans to confiscate citizens’ bank savings. Fact-checking confirms that the image attributed to the media outlet Infobae is fabricated, as is the purported quote. There is no public record of Bullrich ever stating she would “use people’s savings,” a claim frequently shared out of context to mislead the public during the electoral period. The confusion arises from distorted interpretations of Bullrich’s 2022 statements on bimonetarism, an economic model in which the peso and the U.S. dollar coexist. Her actual proposal aims to encourage the legalization of foreign currency held informally (“under the mattress”) so it can be integrated into productive, savings, and investment channels. This approach seeks to restore confidence in the banking system and stimulate credit, rather than forcibly appropriating existing deposits, which directly contradicts the malicious narrative spread online. This verification effort is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how digital evidence can be manipulated to rapidly create false narratives. It underscores the critical need to cross-reference primary sources—such as original media archives and official platforms—to detect tampered visuals and verify claims. By exposing the absence of authentic records, the story highlights the importance of transparent, verifiable data in combating information fraud and preserving the integrity of democratic processes.
