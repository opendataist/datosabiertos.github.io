NMET approves 309 projects worth ₹2,100 crore

The National Mineral Exploration Trust significantly advances India’s mineral self-reliance by funding extensive regional and detailed exploration projects. This strategic investment supports baseline geoscience data generation and strengthens technical infrastructure across state governments, ensuring a robust foundation for future resource identification. A key outcome of this initiative is the imminent launch of the National Geoscience Data Repository. By centralizing critical geological information, the portal enhances transparency and accessibility, directly supporting open data principles within the mining sector while aiding the discovery of concealed mineral targets. These efforts are crucial for open data as they establish a comprehensive, publicly accessible repository of geoscience data. This transparency not only accelerates critical mineral auctions but also empowers stakeholders with reliable information, fostering an ecosystem where shared data drives efficient exploration and economic growth.
