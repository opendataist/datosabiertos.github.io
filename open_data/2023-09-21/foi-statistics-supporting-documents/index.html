FOI Statistics: supporting documents

FOI Statistics: supporting documents

This document tracks the ongoing development of Freedom of Information statistics, demonstrating a consistent, multi-year commitment to transparency. It highlights that these statistics are not static but evolve through regular updates and scope revisions. This continuous improvement ensures the data remains accurate, relevant, and aligned with current needs. The regular publication of development plans underscores a dedication to methodological rigor and open governance. By documenting changes over time, the publisher builds trust with users who rely on this data for accountability and research. This transparency allows stakeholders to understand how the statistics are produced and why they change. This relevance to open data lies in its demonstration of living, evolving datasets. It shows that open government data requires maintenance and clear communication to remain useful. For open data advocates, it serves as a model for how statistical bodies can maintain high standards while adapting to new requirements, ensuring long-term usability and public trust.
