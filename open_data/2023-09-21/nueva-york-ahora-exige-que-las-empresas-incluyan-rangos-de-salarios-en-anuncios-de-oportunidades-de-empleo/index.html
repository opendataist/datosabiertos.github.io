Nueva York ahora exige que las empresas incluyan rangos de salarios en anuncios de oportunidades de empleo

New York has enacted a new law requiring companies with four or more employees to publish salary ranges in all job postings, promotions, and transfers. This measure applies to both on-site positions within the state and remote jobs reporting to local supervisors, ensuring that transparency is a fundamental requirement in hiring. The initiative aims to level the playing field in the labor market, empowering workers with vital information to make informed decisions about their careers. By making compensation visible, the legislation directly addresses historical wage disparities that have disproportionately harmed women and minorities, thereby promoting justice and equity in the workplace. This article is relevant to open data because it transforms salary information from a private matter into a public transparency obligation. By regulating the disclosure of compensation data, the law fosters a culture where economic information is accessible, enabling citizen and analytical scrutiny to combat inequality and promote fairer labor markets.
