Gobierno Abierto: UNL recibió un premio internacional

The National University of the Litoral (UNL) has received international recognition for its Open Government Portal, which was acknowledged by UN-ILPES and the Ibero-American Open Government Network as an outstanding academic practice. This honor highlights the effectiveness of integrating transparency, participation, and collaboration within higher education institutions. The portal’s innovative use of visual data and its emphasis on participatory budgeting were specifically praised as a replicable model for other universities, demonstrating how academia can actively contribute to strengthening open government policies. This achievement is particularly significant for the open data field, as it illustrates the successful operationalization of open government principles through technological integration. By combining expertise in statistics, computer science, and communication, the university created a coherent system that goes beyond mere information publication to foster genuine civic engagement. The project demonstrates that when educational institutions leverage digital tools to make data accessible and understandable, they enhance democratic accountability and serve as catalysts for broader public policy improvements across the region. The recognition underscores the vital role of interdisciplinary collaboration and strong institutional commitment in sustaining open data initiatives. It validates the approach of treating open government not merely as a regulatory compliance exercise, but as a strategic, value-generating practice that enriches democratic processes. For the wider open data community, this case study offers a compelling template on how universities can act as key actors in promoting transparency and fostering a culture of collaborative governance, thereby influencing public sector reforms throughout Ibero-America.
