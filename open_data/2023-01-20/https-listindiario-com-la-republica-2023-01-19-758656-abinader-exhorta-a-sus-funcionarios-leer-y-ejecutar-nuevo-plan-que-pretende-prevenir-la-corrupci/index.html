Open Data
ES EN

President Luis Abinader launched the Open Government Action Plan, prioritizing data transparency and corruption prevention as core public policies. The initiative aims to position the country at the forefront of open governance through measurable commitments. This strategy emphasizes the availability of data to foster accountability and protect civic rights. It integrates technology for the common good and encourages citizen participation to ensure inclusive development and sustainable environmental practices. The article is relevant to open data because it highlights a political framework in which government openness depends on making information accessible. By mandating data availability, the plan strengthens democratic oversight and enables citizens to engage more effectively with public administration.
