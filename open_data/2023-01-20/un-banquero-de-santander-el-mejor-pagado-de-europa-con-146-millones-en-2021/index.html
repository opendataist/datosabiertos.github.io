Un banquero de Santander, el mejor pagado de Europa con 14,6 millones en 2021

The European Banking Authority reports a significant surge in high-paid executives across the EU, with over 1,900 professionals earning more than a million euros in 2021. This trend is driven by strong institutional performance, particularly in investment banking and trading, alongside post-Brexit staff relocations and general salary increases. Notably, Spain, France, and Italy account for most of this growth, marking the highest recorded levels in over a decade. Specific cases highlight substantial compensation packages, such as those at Santander, where executives received large severance and fixed fees during leadership transitions. These payouts reflect broader industry patterns where top management rewards are tied to corporate stability and individual career movements, including departures of key figures in digital and regional operations. This data is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of public financial transparency in monitoring executive remuneration trends. By making such detailed compensation records accessible, stakeholders can analyze labor market dynamics, assess corporate governance standards, and track socioeconomic disparities within the European banking sector.
