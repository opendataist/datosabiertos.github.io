Más de 2 mil muertes por “accidentes” acumuló Veracruz en 2022: INEGI

The INEGI report highlights that traffic and other accidents caused significant mortality in Veracruz, particularly affecting young people. This data underscores the urgent need for better traffic safety measures to prevent preventable deaths among vulnerable demographics. These statistics reveal a critical public health crisis where accidents are a leading cause of death for children and youth. The disproportionate impact on males suggests a need for targeted educational campaigns and stricter enforcement of safety protocols to reduce these high-risk behaviors. This article is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how transparent, high-quality government statistics empower citizens to understand societal risks. Accessible, detailed data allows for accountability, enabling researchers and policymakers to analyze trends and advocate for evidence-based interventions to save lives.
