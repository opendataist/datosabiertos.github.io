The number of XL Bullies seized by North Wales Police since ban

The number of XL Bullies seized by North Wales Police since ban

North Wales Police data reveals a discrepancy between new XL Bully regulations and enforcement outcomes. Despite numerous reports of unregistered or dangerous dogs, zero arrests were made because offenders typically receive court summons instead. This highlights a critical gap in how open data captures criminal justice processes. Relying solely on arrest statistics may misrepresent the actual enforcement volume, suggesting that comprehensive data sources must include judicial outcomes rather than just police apprehensions. For open data advocates, this underscores the necessity of transparent, multi-source reporting. Understanding regulatory impact requires more than raw seizure numbers; it demands a holistic view of legal proceedings to accurately assess public safety and compliance effectiveness.
