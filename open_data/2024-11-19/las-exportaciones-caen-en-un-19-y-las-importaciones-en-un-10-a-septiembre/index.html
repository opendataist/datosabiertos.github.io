Bolivia’s trade balance reveals a significant economic contraction, highlighting vulnerabilities in its primary export sectors. Open data advocates must track these macroeconomic indicators to understand how resource dependency affects public welfare. Transparent statistics enable researchers and citizens to demand accountable fiscal policies and identify structural weaknesses in the economy.
