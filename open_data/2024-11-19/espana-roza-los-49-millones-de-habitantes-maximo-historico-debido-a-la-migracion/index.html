España roza los 49 millones de habitantes, máximo histórico, debido a la migración

Spain recorded its highest-ever population, driven entirely by foreign immigration as the native-born population declined. This surge highlights a critical demographic shift in which external migration sustains national growth, revealing a dependency on international mobility rather than organic expansion. The data underscore the dynamic nature of migration flows, showing not only significant inflows but also substantial outflows from various national groups. This balance between arrivals and departures illustrates the complexity of managing diverse populations and the constant recalibration required in regional planning and social services. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how official statistics from the National Statistics Institute (INE) provide transparent, high-frequency insights into societal trends. Such accessible datasets empower researchers, policymakers, and citizens to monitor demographic changes in real time, fostering evidence-based decisions regarding housing, healthcare, and integration policies in an increasingly mobile world.
