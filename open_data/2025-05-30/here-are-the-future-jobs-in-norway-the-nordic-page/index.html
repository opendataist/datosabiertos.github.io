Here are the Future Jobs in Norway - The Nordic Page

Here are the Future Jobs in Norway - The Nordic Page

The Norwegian labor market anticipates significant shortages in specialized fields like engineering and IT, alongside a decline in demand for unskilled workers. This shift highlights the urgent need to update workforce data to reflect changing skill requirements. Such insights are vital for open data initiatives aiming to support accurate labor market analysis and policy-making.
