Feminicidios en Paraguay: 12 víctimas y 21 tentativas en lo que va del año - Policiales - ABC Color

Feminicidios en Paraguay: 12 víctimas y 21 tentativas en lo que va del año - Policiales - ABC Color

The Public Ministry’s open data initiative on violence against women in Paraguay reveals a critical pattern of gender-based homicide and attempted murder. By aggregating detailed statistics on victims, aggressors, and legal proceedings, the dataset highlights the severe human toll, including numerous indirect victims among children. This transparency underscores the urgent need for systemic intervention to protect vulnerable populations and hold perpetrators accountable through the judicial system. The data indicates that violence predominantly occurs in private spaces and involves individuals known to the victims, such as current or former partners. This proximity challenges the misconception that such crimes are committed by strangers, emphasizing the intimate nature of the threat. Furthermore, the inclusion of indirect victims, particularly minors who suffer long-term trauma, illustrates the broader societal impact, reinforcing the necessity for comprehensive support systems beyond immediate legal justice. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how structured, accessible information can drive public awareness and policy formulation. By making granular details regarding demographics, methods, and case statuses available, authorities enable researchers and civil society to analyze trends effectively. Such transparency fosters evidence-based decision-making, allowing for targeted strategies to prevent feminicide and support victims, ultimately strengthening human rights protections through data-driven accountability.
