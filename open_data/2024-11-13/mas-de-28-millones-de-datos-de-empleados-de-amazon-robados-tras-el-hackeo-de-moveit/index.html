Más de 2,8 millones de datos de empleados de Amazon robados tras el hackeo de MOVEit

Más de 2,8 millones de datos de empleados de Amazon robados tras el hackeo de MOVEit

Amazon confirmed that more than 2.8 million employee data records were stolen through a vulnerability in MOVEit Transfer, a third-party vendor. The company assured that its core systems and AWS remain secure, emphasizing that only contact details were exposed. Crucially, sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers or financial data was not compromised, thereby mitigating significant harm to individuals. This incident highlights the fragility of cybersecurity supply chains. Because the exploited software is widely used, the breach affects not only Amazon but also numerous other major corporations. It underscores how a single weakness in a shared provider can cascade into a massive, cross-organizational data exposure event. For open data enthusiasts, this case illustrates the critical importance of data minimization and strict access controls. Even seemingly innocuous public or internal data, when aggregated and leaked, can pose serious risks. It reinforces the need for robust transparency and accountability when handling employee information, demonstrating why responsible data stewardship is vital for maintaining trust and security in an interconnected digital ecosystem.
