Nueva tecnología busca reemplazar los pasaportes en los aeropuertos | NoticiasNet - Informacion de Rio Negro, Patagones y la costa

Nueva tecnología busca reemplazar los pasaportes en los aeropuertos | NoticiasNet - Informacion de Rio Negro, Patagones y la costa

Global airports, such as Abu Dhabi’s, are adopting facial recognition to replace physical passports, streamlining security checks and enhancing safety. This biometric transition eliminates the need for physical documents, offering travelers a smoother and more efficient experience. However, large-scale implementation raises critical challenges regarding privacy and the handling of personal data. It is imperative to establish strict standards to prevent the misuse of biometric information and to safeguard public trust in these automated technologies. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the urgency of transparent regulatory frameworks and international standards. The ethical management of such sensitive data requires governments and institutions to prioritize privacy protection, ensuring that technological innovation does not compromise citizens’ fundamental rights.
