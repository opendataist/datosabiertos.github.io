Comisionados del Info garantizan continuidad del derecho a la información en CDMX ante reforma constitucional

Comisionados del Info garantizan continuidad del derecho a la información en CDMX ante reforma constitucional

Commissioners of Info CDMX reaffirm that the right to access public information will remain in effect in Mexico City, despite the constitutional reform aimed at eliminating local and national autonomous bodies. Although the current institutes will be dissolved, it is assured that the right will endure through a process of legal transformation. This change involves the creation of a new entity specifically dedicated to ensuring transparency and protecting personal data, thereby preserving the essential review remedy for citizens. The relevance of this news for open data lies in the need to define how transparency will be safeguarded within the new institutional framework. Commissioners emphasize the importance of parliamentary dialogue in shaping the General Transparency Law, seeking political will to design effective structures. The continuity of public information access does not depend solely on the existence of an institute, but rather on the capacity of new institutions to guarantee these fundamental rights in a changing political environment. Additionally, the use of emerging technologies to facilitate access to information is highlighted. Tools such as Infoverso and the Transparency ChatGPT have optimized the resolution of appeals and the handling of requests, demonstrating how technological innovation can strengthen accountability. This approach suggests that efficiency in managing open data is crucial to maintaining public trust and the effectiveness of new governmental structures that will emerge following the reform.
