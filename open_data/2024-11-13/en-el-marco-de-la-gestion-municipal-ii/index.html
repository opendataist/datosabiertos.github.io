En el marco de la gestión municipal (II)

The article underscores the transformative potential of integrating open government principles with e-government to promote transparency, integrity, and citizen participation at the municipal level. By harnessing digital technologies, public services become faster, of higher quality, and more accessible, significantly reducing costs and administrative errors while empowering citizens to exert a more direct influence on decision-making processes. A key development is the strategic partnership between the National Institute for Municipal Development and the Presidential Commission for Open and Electronic Government. This agreement seeks to incorporate local municipalities into the National Open Government Agenda through a participatory methodology. The objective is to establish municipalities as exemplary models of local governance that prioritize openness, collaboration, and accountability over traditional hierarchical structures. This initiative is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates how digital infrastructure serves as the backbone for genuine civic engagement. When local governments adopt these principles, they create ecosystems in which public information is not merely published but actively used to generate public value. This shift from closed hierarchies to networked collaboration ensures that open data becomes a practical tool for strengthening democracy and enhancing overall societal well-being.
