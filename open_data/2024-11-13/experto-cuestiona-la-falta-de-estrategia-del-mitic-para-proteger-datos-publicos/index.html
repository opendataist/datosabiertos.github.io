Experto cuestiona la falta de estrategia del Mitic para proteger datos públicos

Experto cuestiona la falta de estrategia del Mitic para proteger datos públicos

Paraguay lacks specialized cyber defense units, leaving public data vulnerable to ransomware and unauthorized access. This security gap allows sensitive information to be stolen and sold on the black market, leading to widespread identity fraud and financial losses. The absence of robust protective measures means critical government systems remain at risk of total collapse. Without dedicated resources, institutions cannot effectively prevent or recover from cyberattacks that compromise user accounts and digital wallets. This situation is relevant to open data because unsecured public records undermine trust in transparency initiatives. When data is frequently stolen or leaked, the integrity of open government datasets is compromised, making it difficult to share reliable information with the public.
