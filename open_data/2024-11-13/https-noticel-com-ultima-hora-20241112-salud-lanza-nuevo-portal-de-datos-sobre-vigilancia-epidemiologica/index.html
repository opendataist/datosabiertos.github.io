Puerto Rico has launched a new digital public health portal that replaces the limited COVID-19 dashboards, offering centralized and up-to-date access to a wide range of epidemiological and social indicators. This initiative allows citizens to view and download detailed data on various diseases and conditions of interest, facilitating direct access to information without complex technical barriers. The platform promotes government transparency by ensuring that citizens have access to reliable, real-time information. This empowers both citizens and health professionals to make informed decisions in disease prevention and control, ensuring that public health actions are based on accurate and easily accessible evidence. This launch is significant for the open data movement because it demonstrates how modernizing government data infrastructure improves accountability and citizen engagement. By standardizing open access to critical health data, it sets a precedent for interoperability and responsible use of public information, strengthening public health through the democratization of knowledge.
