Speaking Freely: Tanka Aryal

Tanka Aryal argues that freedom of expression is a foundational human right, essential not only for individual existence but also for securing other civil liberties. He emphasizes that without the ability to speak freely, citizens cannot demand accountability or protect their rights to education and basic needs. Consequently, government interference should be minimal, restricted only by genuine harm or security threats, rather than political convenience or the desire to avoid criticism from the public. The article highlights how restrictive policies, such as the ban on TikTok in Nepal, are often driven by a desire to control marginalized communities and suppress political dissent under the guise of morality or national security. Aryal points out that vague legal frameworks and the delegation of takedown powers to private internet service providers create significant risks for abuse. This lack of clear, consultative legislation allows authorities to arbitrarily silence voices that challenge power structures, undermining democratic integrity and transparency. This perspective is crucial for the open data community because it underscores the direct link between digital rights and data privacy. As online platforms become primary venues for expression, protecting the integrity of personal data and ensuring secure, uncensored digital spaces are vital for maintaining civic space. The interview illustrates that without robust protections against arbitrary surveillance and content restriction, the open exchange of information and data remains vulnerable to political manipulation, threatening the core principles of an open and democratic internet.
