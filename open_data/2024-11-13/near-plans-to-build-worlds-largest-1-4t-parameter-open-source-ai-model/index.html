Near plans to build world’s largest 1.4T parameter open-source AI model

Near plans to build world’s largest 1.4T parameter open-source AI model

Near Protocol has announced a plan to develop a massive, open-source artificial intelligence model that significantly surpasses current industry standards in scale. This ambitious initiative relies on competitive crowdsourced research, allowing thousands of contributors to participate in training progressively larger models. The project aims to establish a sustainable ecosystem where successful developers are rewarded, and the technology continues to evolve through community-driven efforts rather than centralized corporate control. A critical component of this strategy is the integration of decentralized finance mechanisms to support the enormous computational costs associated with AI training. Funds are raised through specific tokens tied to each model iteration, with future revenue generated from model inferences used to repay contributors and reinvest in subsequent developments. By leveraging encrypted trusted execution environments, Near seeks to preserve user privacy while creating a transparent, self-sustaining loop of innovation and financial incentive that differentiates it from traditional proprietary AI ventures. The relevance to open data lies in the philosophical defense of digital sovereignty against centralized monopolies. Proponents argue that if AI development remains controlled by a single entity, it risks creating a surveillance state and eroding web3 principles. By democratizing access to powerful AI tools through open-source collaboration and decentralized infrastructure, this project offers a viable alternative that prioritizes privacy, community governance, and equitable access to advanced technological resources.
