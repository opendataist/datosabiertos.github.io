Razones por las que los cibercriminales quieren tus datos personales

The article highlights that personal data has become a valuable commodity for cybercriminals, who exploit it to commit financial fraud, identity theft, and extortion. The mistaken belief that individual information lacks relevance overlooks the real risk that such data will be used on the dark web or in targeted attacks involving social engineering and ransomware. The proliferation of artificial intelligence intensifies this threat by enabling attackers to personalize scams, such as phishing, using precise details extracted from social media to enhance credibility. This demonstrates that the voluntary exposure of public data facilitates mass collection by malicious actors, who combine this information with advanced technology to undermine users’ privacy and security. This information is crucial for the open data community because it establishes a precedent regarding the sensitivity of personal data, even when it is considered public. It underscores the need to balance transparency and open access with privacy protection, reminding us that data openness must be managed with strict ethical protocols to prevent re-identification and the fraudulent use of individual information.
