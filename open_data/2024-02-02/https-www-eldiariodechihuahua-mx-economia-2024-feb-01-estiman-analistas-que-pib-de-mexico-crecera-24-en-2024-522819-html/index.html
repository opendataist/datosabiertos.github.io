Private sector analysts forecast modest economic growth for Mexico, with a slight increase projected for 2024 but a decline for 2025. However, significant hurdles remain, particularly concerning governance, insecurity, and political uncertainty, which threaten future expansion. Inflation expectations have risen marginally, while interest rate and exchange rate forecasts remain relatively stable despite external pressures. This data underscores the complex interplay between macroeconomic indicators and institutional quality in Mexico’s current economic landscape. Understanding these forecasts is crucial for assessing the stability and trajectory of the national economy amidst ongoing structural challenges and external vulnerabilities. The relevance to open data lies in the accessibility of this high-quality economic intelligence. By making such detailed surveys and analyst estimates publicly available, institutions like Banxico empower researchers, businesses, and citizens. This transparency supports evidence-based decision-making, fosters accountability, and allows for a more nuanced understanding of economic trends and policy impacts.
