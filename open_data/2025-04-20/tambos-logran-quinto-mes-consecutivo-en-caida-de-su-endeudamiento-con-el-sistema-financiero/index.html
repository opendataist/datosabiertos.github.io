Tambos logran quinto mes consecutivo en caída de su endeudamiento con el sistema financiero

Uruguay’s dairy sector demonstrated significant financial resilience in early 2025, marked by a sustained reduction in debt and delinquency rates among producers. This stabilization indicates a successful transition away from years of tight margins and economic volatility, suggesting that farmers are managing their obligations more efficiently despite ongoing market fluctuations. Simultaneously, the sector experienced divergent price dynamics, with export values rising substantially in both local and foreign currencies, while domestic prices faced downward pressure. This contrast highlights the complex interplay between international market opportunities and local consumption trends, offering a clearer picture of the profitability drivers affecting different segments of the industry. This article is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it showcases how disaggregated financial and price statistics from public institutions, such as the Central Bank and the National Institute of Milk, enable granular economic analysis. Transparent access to this data allows stakeholders to monitor sector health, understand macroeconomic impacts on agriculture, and develop evidence-based policies that leverage publicly available information for broader societal benefit.
