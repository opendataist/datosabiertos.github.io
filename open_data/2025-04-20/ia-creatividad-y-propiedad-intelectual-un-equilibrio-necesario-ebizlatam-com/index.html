IA, creatividad y propiedad intelectual: un equilibrio necesario - ebizlatam.com

IA, creatividad y propiedad intelectual: un equilibrio necesario - ebizlatam.com

The article argues that integrating artificial intelligence into industrial and scientific sectors requires a robust framework for the ethical use of data. While AI accelerates innovation in fields such as pharmaceuticals and manufacturing, it raises critical questions about intellectual property and the protection of original contributions. The core conclusion is that success depends on balancing technological advancement with the recognition of human and institutional value, ensuring that the foundation of AI development remains legally and ethically sound. Transparency and proper licensing are identified as essential pillars for responsible AI adoption. Data used to train models often originates from prior academic research and specialized databases, requiring explicit authorization to respect the underlying investments. Without clear visibility into how algorithms learn and attribute knowledge, it becomes impossible to establish accountability. Therefore, companies must prioritize open communication regarding their data sources and algorithmic processes to maintain trust and ensure fair attribution of innovations. This topic is vital for open data because it highlights the tension between data accessibility and intellectual property rights. Open data initiatives thrive on the free flow of information, yet AI systems rely heavily on proprietary or restricted datasets. Addressing the challenges of consent, transparency, and equitable compensation ensures that open data practices do not exploit creators but instead foster collaborative innovation. By developing flexible licensing and global standards, we can support an ecosystem where open data fuels AI progress while preserving the rights of data contributors.
