Diario HOY | Abdo y procesados piden reasignar fiscales en caso prevaricato

This administrative directive mandates the physical submission of documents for the formalization of hiring processes, emphasizing strict verification of professional credentials and performance evaluations. The procedure requires authenticated copies of academic degrees, valid identification, and police or judicial records to ensure the legitimacy of candidates. The relevance to open data lies in the opacity of these manual, in-person bureaucratic procedures. Public sector hiring relies on closed, paper-based verification rather than transparent, digital data repositories, thereby hindering public accountability and data-driven oversight of government employment processes.
