Una nueva estafa suplanta a la Policía Nacional a través del correo electrónico

The National Police have issued a warning about a new scam involving fraudulent emails that impersonate the identity of authorities. The perpetrators pressure victims by claiming their national ID card is blocked and threaten legal action if they do not act quickly, using attached documents to induce panic and obtain private information or make unauthorized charges. It is essential to recognize warning signs, such as grammatical errors, unknown senders, or an extreme sense of urgency. The main recommendation is not to respond, delete the message, and block the sender immediately. Transparency in official communications is crucial so that users can distinguish between legitimate warnings and scams designed to exploit the digital vulnerability of the population. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of accessibility to verified and official information. When government sources do not provide clear and secure channels, citizens become more vulnerable to misinformation. Promoting digital literacy and direct access to official statements helps create a society more resilient to electronic fraud.
