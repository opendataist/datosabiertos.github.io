Pest reports at Princess Alexandra Hospital Trust up 700 per cent since 2021

NHS hospitals are facing a significant increase in pest infestations, with reports rising sharply between 2021 and 2024. This deterioration in hygiene standards raises critical questions about patient safety and staff welfare, suggesting that many facilities are no longer up to scratch for those relying on care. Transparency in health service operations is essential for public trust. The extent of this crisis is only visible because freedom of information requests compelled authorities to disclose data. These findings highlight the necessity of accessing institutional records to understand the real conditions within public services, rather than relying solely on official statements. Furthermore, the millions spent on pest control underscore the financial burden of maintaining infrastructure. When such data is made open, it allows for scrutiny of resource allocation and accountability. Open data thus serves as a vital tool for exposing systemic failures and driving necessary improvements in public health environments.
