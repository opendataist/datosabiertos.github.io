Government documents confirm coal lobbying on Rockies mining, show months of planning

Alberta’s government secretly negotiated with the coal industry to relax protections for the Rocky Mountains before publicly rescinding the policy. This lack of transparency highlights how closed decision-making undermines public trust and excludes citizens from critical environmental debates. The revelation demonstrates that open data initiatives are essential for exposing behind-the-scenes influence and ensuring accountability in resource management. Without timely access to such documents, stakeholders cannot effectively challenge policies that favor industry interests over conservation. This case illustrates the vital role of freedom of information in maintaining democratic oversight. It underscores that true openness requires proactive disclosure, preventing governments from delaying public awareness while advancing controversial developments.
