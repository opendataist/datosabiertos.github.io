The cement sector experienced a notable decline in the first quarter, driven by calendar effects and adverse weather conditions. Despite these temporary factors, the underlying trend over the past year remains negative, signaling a concerning slowdown in construction activity that persists beyond seasonal fluctuations. This contraction is evident in significant drops in both exports and imports, highlighting reduced international trade and domestic demand. The industry anticipates a tentative recovery later in the year, contingent on stable local and international economic conditions, although current indicators suggest a difficult path to renewal. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how specific, granular statistical indicators like monthly cement consumption are crucial for monitoring economic health. Transparent access to such official data allows analysts to distinguish between transient weather impacts and deeper structural trends, enabling better policy decisions and market forecasts.
