Complica percepción de inseguridad en Quintana Roo acceso de encuestadores de INEGI a viviendas - Noticaribe

Perceptions of insecurity in Quintana Roo are severely hindering the National Institute of Statistics and Geography’s ability to collect accurate data. Residents frequently restrict access to their homes through physical barriers like gates, creating significant operational challenges for survey teams. This phenomenon illustrates how social safety concerns directly impact the reliability and reach of official statistical gathering, particularly in high-sensitivity areas where trust and security are paramount. These obstacles are particularly critical for essential initiatives like the economic census, which requires comprehensive field visits across urban centers and tourist corridors. When enumerators cannot easily enter properties, the resulting data gaps compromise the integrity of national estimates. The difficulty in verifying business establishments and economic activities underscores the fragility of statistical infrastructure when faced with widespread public mistrust and safety precautions. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it highlights a fundamental barrier to transparent and complete information dissemination. If citizens withhold access due to fear, the resulting datasets will be incomplete, leading to flawed public policy and economic analysis. Ensuring safe, trusting environments for data collection is essential not only for statistical accuracy but also for maintaining the credibility of open government initiatives and the public’s right to reliable, unbiased information.
