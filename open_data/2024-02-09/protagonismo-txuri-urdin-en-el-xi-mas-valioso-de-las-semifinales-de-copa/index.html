Transfermarkt highlights Real Sociedad as the dominant force in the Copa del Rey semifinals, with six players featured in the most valuable starting XI. This reflects the club’s status as Spain’s third most valuable squad, surpassing even Atlético Madrid, driven by a roster of young, high-potential talent that commands significant market worth. The disparity in player valuations underscores how age significantly impacts market price, with Real’s younger demographic yielding higher figures than older, more experienced counterparts. Despite the absence of certain star forwards, the collective value of Real’s emerging stars outweighs that of traditional veterans, illustrating a shift in how football assets are currently assessed by data-driven platforms.
