The Bank of Ghana launched a data portal to centralize macroeconomic information, aligning with global transparency standards. This initiative significantly enhances public access to essential economic metrics, thereby supporting better-informed research and decision-making processes. Such open access to official statistics promotes accountability and encourages broader societal engagement with national economic indicators.
