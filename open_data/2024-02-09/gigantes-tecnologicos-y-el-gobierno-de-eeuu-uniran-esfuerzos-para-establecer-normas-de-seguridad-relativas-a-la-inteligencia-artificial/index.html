Gigantes tecnológicos y el gobierno de EEUU unirán esfuerzos para establecer normas de seguridad relativas a la Inteligencia Artificial

The U.S. Department of Commerce’s initiative brings together leading artificial intelligence companies and public sector actors to develop responsible safety standards. This collaborative effort aims to balance protection against risks such as disinformation with the promotion of technological innovation in key sectors. The creation of the AI Safety Institute Consortium represents a crucial step toward common reliability standards. By involving both tech giants and emerging players, the initiative seeks to establish robust frameworks that safeguard citizens’ rights without stifling progress in the national digital ecosystem. This approach is vital for open data, as AI governance directly depends on the quality, accessibility, and transparency of the data used to train models. Collaboration between industry and government lays the foundation for safer and more open data, ensuring that technological development benefits society through principles of accountability and clarity in access to information.
