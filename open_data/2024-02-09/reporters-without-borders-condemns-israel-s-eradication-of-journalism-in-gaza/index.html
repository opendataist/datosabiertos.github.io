Reporters Without Borders condemns Israel's 'eradication of journalism' in Gaza

Reporters Without Borders has condemned the systematic eradication of journalism in Gaza, asserting that the Israeli military’s actions constitute a severe violation of the right to information. The organization highlights that Palestinian journalists are being decimated, lacking safe refuge or essential equipment, which effectively silences the documentation of events on the ground. The NGO urges the international community and the UN Security Council to immediately enforce resolutions protecting journalists, emphasizing the urgent need to halt these attacks. With numerous media outlets destroyed and a significant death toll among reporters, the capacity to gather and disseminate independent news is critically compromised, creating a dangerous information vacuum. This situation is vital to open data initiatives because it demonstrates how physical violence can destroy the infrastructure required for transparent information flow. When journalists are targeted, the resulting data gaps hinder accountability, making it difficult for researchers and civil society to access verified, real-time information about humanitarian crises and conflict dynamics.
