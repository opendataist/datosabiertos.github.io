Pakistan govt fails to meet export target for fiscal year 2024-25

Pakistan govt fails to meet export target for fiscal year 2024-25

Pakistan missed its specific export target for 2024-25, though overall exports grew and surpassed total targets. The widening trade deficit highlights ongoing challenges in external account management despite a rise in annual trade volumes. To stimulate economic growth, the government reduced port charges for export containers by half. This measure aims to relieve trade sector pressures and encourage greater commercial activity through key ports. This case illustrates open data’s critical role in public accountability. Transparent publication of trade statistics by the PBS allows stakeholders to monitor policy effectiveness, such as port fee reductions, ensuring that economic interventions are grounded in accurate, publicly accessible metrics.
