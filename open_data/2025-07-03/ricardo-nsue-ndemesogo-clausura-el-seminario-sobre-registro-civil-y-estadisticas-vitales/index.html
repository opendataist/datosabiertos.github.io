Ricardo Nsue Ndemesogo clausura el Seminario sobre Registro Civil y Estadísticas Vitales

Ricardo Nsue Ndemesogo clausura el Seminario sobre Registro Civil y Estadísticas Vitales

The seminar concluded that African civil registration systems face significant challenges, including legislative gaps, cultural barriers, insufficient funding, and poor digital interoperability. These obstacles result in low registration rates for vital events, creating a critical lack of reliable statistical data. This data deficit hinders evidence-based decision-making and effective development planning across the continent, leaving populations vulnerable to uninformed policy interventions. However, these challenges are viewed as opportunities for improvement through shared best practices and South-South cooperation. By leveraging existing strengths and successful initiatives from member states, countries can strengthen their civil registration frameworks. The meeting aims to serve as a turning point, encouraging nations to adopt modern, inclusive, and efficient systems that better serve their citizens and improve administrative coherence. This summary is highly relevant to open data because robust civil registration is the foundation of demographic statistics. Without comprehensive birth, death, marriage, and divorce records, governments cannot generate the open, reliable datasets necessary for transparency and scientific planning. Enhancing these systems ensures that public data becomes accurate, accessible, and usable for fostering development and holding institutions accountable.
