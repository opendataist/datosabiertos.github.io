GORE de La Araucanía tiene el porcentaje más bajo en cumplimiento de Ley de Transparencia

GORE de La Araucanía tiene el porcentaje más bajo en cumplimiento de Ley de Transparencia

The article highlights a severe decline in proactive transparency compliance within Chilean regional governments, marking the lowest national score since 2010. This regression directly impacts open data initiatives, as citizens lose guaranteed, automatic access to essential public information without needing to file specific requests. The situation is particularly critical in La Araucanía, which experienced a drastic drop in adherence to transparency laws. Such a significant failure to maintain updated, unsolicited public records undermines democratic accountability and signals systemic institutional weaknesses in managing public assets. This trend is highly relevant to open data advocates because it demonstrates how political shifts can rapidly erode data availability. It underscores the urgent need for robust, legally enforced transparency mechanisms to ensure consistent, unrestricted access to government information for all citizens.
