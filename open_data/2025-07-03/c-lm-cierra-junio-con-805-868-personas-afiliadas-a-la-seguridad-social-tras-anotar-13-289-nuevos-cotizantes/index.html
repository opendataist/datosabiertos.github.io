C-LM cierra junio con 805.868 personas afiliadas a la Seguridad Social, tras anotar 13.289 nuevos cotizantes

C-LM cierra junio con 805.868 personas afiliadas a la Seguridad Social, tras anotar 13.289 nuevos cotizantes

Spain has reached a historic peak in Social Security registrations, driven by strong job creation in high-value sectors such as commerce, hospitality, and scientific services. This growth reflects the positive impact of recent labor reforms, which have significantly enhanced employment stability by increasing the share of permanent contracts and reducing temporary work, particularly among younger workers. The labor market also shows notable progress in inclusion and diversity. Female employment has reached an all-time high, and immigration has contributed substantially to workforce expansion. Moreover, there is a clear shift toward higher-quality jobs, with a surge in self-employed individuals entering productive industries and a marked decline in temporary hiring rates compared to previous years. This data is relevant to open-data initiatives because it highlights the importance of transparent, granular labor statistics for monitoring economic health and policy effectiveness. Access to such detailed breakdowns by region, sector, and demographic enables researchers and citizens to verify government claims, analyze long-term trends in job quality, and hold institutions accountable for the implementation of structural reforms.
