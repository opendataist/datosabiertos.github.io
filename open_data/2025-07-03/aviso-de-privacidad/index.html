Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

Revista Punto de Vista demonstrates a rigorous commitment to user privacy by explicitly stating that it neither collects nor stores personal data for general content access. This minimal data approach ensures that visitors can consume information without mandatory registration or invasive tracking mechanisms, prioritizing transparency and user autonomy. The publication further enhances this privacy framework by avoiding third-party advertising and refusing to use cookies for profiling or analytics. By eliminating commercial data monetization, the platform models a sustainable, ethical business structure that respects digital rights and fosters trust through clean, direct communication. This transparency aligns closely with open data principles by emphasizing that data protection and open access are not mutually exclusive. It illustrates how media outlets can operate ethically without compromising user privacy, serving as a relevant example of responsible data governance in the digital public sphere.
