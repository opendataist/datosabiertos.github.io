La inflación se desploma ocho décimas en septiembre, hasta el 1,5%, mínimos de 2021

Spanish inflation has fallen significantly to 1.5%, marking its lowest level since 2021. This decline, driven by lower fuel and food prices, places the rate below the European Central Bank’s target. The data underscores the Spanish economy’s ability to achieve growth while effectively stabilizing prices. Underlying inflation has also moderated, reflecting broad-based price stability. This trend confirms the effectiveness of current economic policies, highlighting a successful balance between robust growth and controlled inflation within the Eurozone. This article is relevant to open data as it relies on precise, publicly released statistical indicators from national institutes. Such transparent, accessible datasets enable independent verification and analysis, ensuring public trust in economic metrics and facilitating accurate monitoring of macroeconomic health.
