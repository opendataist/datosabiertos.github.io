Open Data
ES EN

High poverty rates in Argentina underscore the urgent need for transparent public data to accurately track socioeconomic impacts. Open data serves as a critical tool for verifying claims about economic policies and measuring human suffering, thereby fostering accountability in governance. The crisis illustrates how accessible, reliable statistics enable civil society and experts to objectively analyze policy outcomes. Without such data, understanding the true scale of inequality remains difficult, hindering effective democratic oversight and informed public debate. Ultimately, open data empowers citizens to demand better economic strategies, ensuring that decisions are based on verifiable realities rather than political narratives. This transparency is essential for rebuilding trust and addressing systemic issues in modern societies.
