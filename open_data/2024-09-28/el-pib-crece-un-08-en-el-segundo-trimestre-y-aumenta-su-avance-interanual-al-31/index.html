El PIB crece un 0,8% en el segundo trimestre y aumenta su avance interanual al 3,1%

The Spanish economy confirms sustained expansion, marking thirteen consecutive quarters of growth, primarily driven by domestic demand and household consumption. This dynamism reflects the resilience of the economic model in the face of external uncertainties, with significant momentum from public administrations and an accelerating services sector that offsets the relative weakness of agriculture. The data also show improvements in productivity and employment, where the increase in hours worked and jobs indicates a robust labor market recovery. Although hourly productivity remained stable during the quarter, its year-on-year advance suggests growing efficiency in value creation, supported by industrial and construction growth, thereby strengthening the country’s productive base over the medium term.
