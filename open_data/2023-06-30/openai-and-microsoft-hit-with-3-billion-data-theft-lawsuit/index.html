OpenAI and Microsoft Hit With $3 Billion Data Theft Lawsuit

A lawsuit alleges OpenAI illegally harvested vast amounts of sensitive personal data, including location, financial details, and private conversations, to train ChatGPT without user consent. This suggests the company bypassed legal requirements to register as a data broker, highlighting a significant gap between corporate practices and privacy regulations. The complaint further accuses OpenAI and Microsoft of prioritizing profit and the "AI arms race" over their original mission to benefit humanity. By integrating intimate user details into AI models, the firm is portrayed as systematically violating ethical standards and ignoring established legal obligations regarding data acquisition. This case is critical for open data discourse because it challenges the assumption that aggregated data use is inherently lawful or ethical. It underscores the urgent need for transparent data sourcing and strict accountability in AI development, emphasizing that open access to models must not come at the cost of individual privacy rights.
