La inflación se modera más de un punto en junio, hasta el 1,9 %, y la subyacente cae hasta el 5,9 %

Spain has become the first major Eurozone economy to bring inflation below the European Central Bank’s 2% target, marking a significant economic milestone. This deceleration, driven by slower price increases in energy and food, underscores a broader trend of monetary stabilization that enhances the country’s industrial competitiveness and market share within the European Union. The government’s strategic fiscal interventions, including value-added tax reductions on essential goods, have proven effective in alleviating pressure on consumers. These measures are extended on the condition that underlying inflation remains above specific thresholds, demonstrating a proactive policy framework for managing economic volatility while protecting household purchasing power during a period of sustained price moderation. This article highlights the critical role of transparent, official statistical data in shaping and justifying public policy. For open-data enthusiasts, it illustrates how accessible, high-quality metrics from national statistical institutes enable real-time analysis of macroeconomic trends, fostering accountability and allowing stakeholders to understand the tangible impact of governmental decisions on everyday economic conditions.
