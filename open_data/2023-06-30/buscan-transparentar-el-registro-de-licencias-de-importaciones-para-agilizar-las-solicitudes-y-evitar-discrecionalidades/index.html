Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

Deputies from the Civic Coalition propose the creation of an Open Data Portal for imports, aimed at eliminating the administrative discretion that currently characterizes the licensing system. The central objective is to guarantee radical transparency in the management of applications, ensuring that public information is accessible, up-to-date, and available in open formats. This seeks to mitigate the serious corruption risks arising from current procedures, which operate without clear parameters or respect for deadlines. The initiative establishes that all relevant information regarding imports, including the status of applications and the criteria for economic evaluation, must be published digitally. Additionally, it proposes simplifying procedures and setting fixed maximum deadlines, granting tacit approval if no decision is made within the established timeframe. This measure aims to replace the opacity of the current system with a predictable framework that eliminates exchange rate distortions and protects the foreign exchange market through openness, rather than through arbitrary restrictions. This project is relevant to the open data community because it exemplifies how the proactive publication of administrative data in standardized formats can serve as an effective accountability tool. By requiring that the criteria and status of applications be public and accessible, it applies the principle of transparency by default, demonstrating that open data not only digitizes processes but also structures governance to prevent abuses of power and facilitate social oversight of economic policies.
