Quilmes se prepara para el Foro de Gobierno Abierto de la Gestión Mayra Mendoza

The Municipality of Quilmes is hosting an Open Government Forum to promote community interaction, collaboration, and transparency. By inviting residents to engage directly with political leaders across various thematic commissions, the initiative aims to strengthen civic participation and democratize local governance. The event features a public vote to name a new train station and promotes local economic development through a fair for entrepreneurs. These activities exemplify a practical approach to inclusive policymaking, enabling citizens to influence decisions in areas ranging from urban development to social rights. This article is relevant to open data as it highlights the critical link between transparent governance and active civic engagement. It underscores how open government initiatives serve as vital platforms for gathering public input, thereby ensuring that data-driven decisions reflect the diverse needs and priorities of the communities they serve.
