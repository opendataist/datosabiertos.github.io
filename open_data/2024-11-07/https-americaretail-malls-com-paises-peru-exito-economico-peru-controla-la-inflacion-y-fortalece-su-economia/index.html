The INEI report reveals that Peru has succeeded in keeping inflation within the Central Bank’s target range, demonstrating the effectiveness of monetary policy in preserving price stability. This prudent situation protects households’ purchasing power while reflecting a favorable economic environment, in which essential sectors are experiencing cost reductions that ease the burden on the average consumer. Market dynamics show an uneven distribution of price changes across regions and sectors. While key cities report modest increases, other areas enjoy price declines, highlighting local disparities. This complexity underscores that, although the overall trend is encouraging, economic assessment must account for geographic and sectoral variations to fully understand population well-being and the health of the domestic market. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical importance of transparency and access to accurate official statistics. Continuous monitoring of indicators such as the Consumer Price Index (CPI) enables citizens, analysts, and policymakers to assess the real impact of economic decisions. Open and verifiable data are essential for building institutional trust and facilitating an informed public debate on the national economic situation.
