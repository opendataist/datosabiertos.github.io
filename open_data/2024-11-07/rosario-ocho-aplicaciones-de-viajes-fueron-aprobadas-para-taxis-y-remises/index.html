Rosario: ocho aplicaciones de viajes fueron aprobadas para taxis y remises

Rosario: ocho aplicaciones de viajes fueron aprobadas para taxis y remises

Rosario has modernized its public transportation system by making digital applications mandatory for taxis and ride-hailing services. This regulation aims to provide a safer and more transparent service, allowing users to access features such as dynamic pricing, shared rides, and electronic payments. By digitizing the booking process, the city improves cost predictability and the passenger experience. The inclusion of multiple platforms fosters competition and choice, ensuring legal and safe alternatives. Although some well-known companies have not yet registered, the system maintains an open registry for future incorporations. This ensures that the market adapts to current trends without closing the door to new players, consolidating a flexible, user-oriented transportation ecosystem. This change is crucial for open data because it digitizes the interaction between public services and citizens. The mandatory use of applications facilitates the collection of real-time data on mobility, demand, and prices. These open data sets enable civil society and researchers to analyze transportation efficiency, monitor fare transparency, and propose evidence-based improvements, thereby strengthening the city’s democratic governance.
