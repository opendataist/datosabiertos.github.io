La natalidad: un reto urgente para Tarragona

La natalidad: un reto urgente para Tarragona

Catalonia’s fertility rates have plummeted to historic lows, creating a demographic imbalance that threatens the sustainability of pension systems, public services, and the labor market. This decline, driven by structural issues such as labor precarity and housing costs, highlights a critical gap between desired and actual family size. The situation demands urgent, long-term policy interventions to prevent profound social and economic consequences for the region. This crisis is relevant to open data because transparency in demographic statistics is essential for identifying these trends early and holding institutions accountable. Open data allows citizens and researchers to visualize the severity of the decline, facilitating public debate and ensuring that government responses are based on accurate, accessible evidence rather than political narratives. Ultimately, the article argues that natality is a collective responsibility requiring bold policies, such as expanded childcare and housing support. By leveraging open data, society can better understand the root causes of this challenge and advocate for the structural reforms necessary to secure a viable future, demonstrating how data transparency drives informed civic engagement and effective governance.
