Microsoft likely to sign EU AI code of practice, Meta rebuffs guidelines - The Economic Times

Microsoft likely to sign EU AI code of practice, Meta rebuffs guidelines - The Economic Times

Microsoft’s potential adoption of the EU’s voluntary AI code signals industry willingness to navigate new regulatory landscapes, whereas Meta’s rejection highlights deep divisions over compliance burdens. This divergence illustrates the practical challenges companies face in balancing innovation with strict copyright and transparency mandates required by the AI Act. The code’s requirement to disclose training data summaries directly impacts open data practices by pushing for greater algorithmic transparency. While voluntary, such frameworks set precedents for how AI models interact with public information sources, influencing future standards for accessible and understandable data usage in Europe. This article matters to open data because it reveals how regulatory mechanisms can either enforce openness or discourage it through overreach. The debate between signatories and non-signatories underscores the critical need for balanced policies that maintain data accessibility while ensuring legal certainty for developers building on open datasets.
