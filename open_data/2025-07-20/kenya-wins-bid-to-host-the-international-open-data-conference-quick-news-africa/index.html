Kenya wins Bid to host the International Open Data Conference - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

Kenya wins Bid to host the International Open Data Conference - QUICK NEWS AFRICA

The sixth International Open Data Conference (IODC) is scheduled to take place in Nairobi, Kenya, marking the first time the premier global gathering is held in Africa. This decision recognizes Kenya’s consistent high-level leadership and successful initiatives in fostering an inclusive data ecosystem that engages government, the private sector, and civil society. The move signals a significant shift in the global open data landscape, highlighting Africa’s emerging capacity to lead collaborative efforts in data for development. The 2020 conference, themed “Bridging Data Communities,” aims to strengthen South-South collaboration and share lessons on leveraging technology to address societal challenges. By convening thousands of participants and international tech firms, the event seeks to accelerate the impact of open data across critical sectors like agriculture, health, and fiscal transparency. The gathering serves as a platform to demonstrate how data and innovation can be harnessed to deliver tangible economic and social benefits while ensuring no one is left behind in the pursuit of sustainable development goals. This event is highly relevant to open data as it underscores the transition from political will to practical, economic implementation of open data policies. It emphasizes the importance of interoperability, accessibility, and cross-sectoral collaboration in driving development priorities. By hosting the conference in Africa, the IODC reinforces the global commitment to making data a public good that supports informed decision-making and inclusive growth, setting a precedent for future regional leadership in the open data movement.
