What is Neo? Our in-house Large Language Model (LLM) trained on REALITY content, not wokeism or corporate media propaganda – NaturalNews.com

The article announces the development of "Neo," an upcoming open-source Large Language Model designed to provide answers grounded in what the authors describe as objective reality, contrasting sharply with the perceived ideological biases of corporate AI. By retraining base models on content from alternative media publishers, the project aims to create a tool that reflects natural medicine, climate science, and historical events according to the authors' views, positioning it as a truthful alternative to mainstream AI systems. A key feature of Neo is its decentralized, offline capability, allowing users to download and run the model locally without internet connectivity. This ensures user privacy and data sovereignty, shielding individuals from cloud-based monitoring and potential government censorship or regulation that the authors fear will stifle free inquiry. The release strategy involves iterative updates throughout the year, starting with a basic version that improves as more training data is integrated, with a primary focus on self-reliance, survival skills, and health topics. This initiative is highly relevant to the open_data community as it emphasizes the importance of decentralized knowledge infrastructure and local control over information tools. It highlights a growing movement toward uncensored, accessible AI resources hosted on platforms like HuggingFace, arguing that true freedom and accurate knowledge depend on open-source models that resist centralized corporate or governmental control. The project advocates for a future where powerful AI tools remain in the hands of individuals rather than monopolized entities.
