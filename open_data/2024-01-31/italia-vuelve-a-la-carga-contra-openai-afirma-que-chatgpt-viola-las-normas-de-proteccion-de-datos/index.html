Italia vuelve a la carga contra OpenAI: afirma que ChatGPT viola las normas de protección de datos

The Italian Data Protection Authority has determined that ChatGPT’s data handling practices breach European privacy regulations, even though earlier temporary restrictions were lifted after OpenAI addressed certain initial concerns. This continued scrutiny underscores the tension between the rapid pace of AI development and the strict enforcement of data protection standards, indicating that early compliance measures were insufficient to meet regulatory expectations. OpenAI asserts that its methods comply with EU law and highlights its efforts to minimize the use of personal data in training its algorithms. However, the regulator’s new findings reveal ongoing privacy risks, giving the company a deadline to present its defense before potentially facing substantial fines. This situation highlights the rigorous oversight that European authorities apply to large language models. This case is significant for open data, as it establishes the boundaries of permissible data processing for AI. It demonstrates how the public interest in accessible AI technologies must be balanced against individual privacy rights. Understanding these legal constraints helps developers ensure that their open data practices adhere to regulatory frameworks, thereby preventing future conflicts and fostering trustworthy, compliant AI ecosystems.
