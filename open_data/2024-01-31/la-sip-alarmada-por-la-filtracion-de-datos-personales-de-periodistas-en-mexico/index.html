La SIP alarmada por la filtración de datos personales de periodistas en México

The violation of journalists’ privacy through a massive data leak highlights critical security gaps in public institutions responsible for managing press accreditation. This incident underscores the urgent need for robust data protection mechanisms, as the exposure of sensitive personal information directly threatens the physical safety and professional integrity of media workers, particularly in high-risk environments. Such breaches demonstrate how administrative failures can exacerbate existing dangers to press freedom. When personal details such as identification numbers and contact information are compromised, journalists face heightened risks of harassment and violence. This reality necessitates immediate accountability and rigorous investigations to deter future misuse of confidential data by malicious actors. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the delicate balance between transparency and privacy. While open government initiatives are valuable, they must not compromise individual security. Ensuring that data collection processes respect user privacy is essential for maintaining trust and protecting fundamental rights in the digital public sphere.
