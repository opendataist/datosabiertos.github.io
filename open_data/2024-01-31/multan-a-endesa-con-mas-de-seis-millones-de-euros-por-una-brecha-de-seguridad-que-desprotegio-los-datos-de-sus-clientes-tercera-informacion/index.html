Multan a Endesa con más de seis millones de euros por una brecha de seguridad que desprotegió los datos de sus clientes - Tercera Información

Endesa is facing a substantial fine for permitting the sale of customer data through Facebook advertisements, revealing serious negligence in safeguarding personal information. This breach enabled unauthorized access and fraudulent user registrations over several months, severely compromising privacy rights. The ruling highlights critical failures in data protection protocols, underscoring the urgent need for robust security measures to prevent exploitation. Such incidents demonstrate how weak controls can lead to widespread data exposure, affecting both individuals and organizational trust. This case is significant for discussions on open data, as it emphasizes the necessity of balancing transparency with stringent privacy safeguards. It illustrates that even well-intentioned data systems require rigorous security to maintain integrity and prevent the malicious use of accessible information.
