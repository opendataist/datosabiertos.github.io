La economía española creció un 2,5% en 2023, una décima más de lo previsto

The Spanish economy surpassed official expectations by ending the fiscal year with solid annual growth, marking its third consecutive year of expansion. This performance highlights a robust recovery, positioning the country as one of the strongest economies within the Eurozone. The achievement underscores the effectiveness of recent economic policies and suggests a favorable starting point for meeting future stability goals, despite external market uncertainties. Underlying this growth are shifts in demand drivers, with domestic consumption and increased public spending playing crucial roles in sustaining economic momentum. While the external sector contributed less than in previous years, the resilience of key sectors such as construction and services indicates a broad-based recovery. This structural evolution points to an economy capable of withstanding external shocks, although analysts caution that future growth may rely more heavily on continued domestic strength rather than export performance.
