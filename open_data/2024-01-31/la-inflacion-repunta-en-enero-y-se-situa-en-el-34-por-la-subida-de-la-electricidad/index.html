La inflación repunta en enero y se sitúa en el 3,4% por la subida de la electricidad

The increase in electricity prices, driven by tax changes, has reversed recent deflationary trends, pushing overall inflation higher. While this indicates a temporary resurgence in price pressures, underlying inflation continues to cool, reaching its lowest level in nearly two years as fuel costs decline. This report is essential for open data enthusiasts because it demonstrates the critical role of official statistical releases in monitoring economic health. It highlights how transparent and accessible data enables users to analyze complex trends, differentiate between volatile and core metrics, and understand the real-world implications of policy changes, such as tax adjustments.
