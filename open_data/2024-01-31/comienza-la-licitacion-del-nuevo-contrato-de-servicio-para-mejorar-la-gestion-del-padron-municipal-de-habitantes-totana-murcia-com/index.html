Comienza la licitación del nuevo contrato de servicio para mejorar la Gestión del Padrón Municipal de Habitantes - Totana - murcia.com

The Totana City Council has launched a tender to replace its outdated resident registry system, aiming to modernize operations through cloud-based technology. This shift prioritizes improved data management efficiency and ensures compliance with national security standards, addressing current infrastructural limitations. This initiative highlights the critical role of open data infrastructure in modern local governance. By upgrading to a secure, efficient platform, the municipality enhances its capacity to manage public information responsibly, fostering transparency and trust in how citizen data is handled and protected. Ultimately, outsourcing this technical service allows the town to leverage specialized external expertise. This strategic move ensures that digital transformation efforts meet contemporary regulatory requirements, providing a robust foundation for future data-driven administrative improvements and better public service delivery.
