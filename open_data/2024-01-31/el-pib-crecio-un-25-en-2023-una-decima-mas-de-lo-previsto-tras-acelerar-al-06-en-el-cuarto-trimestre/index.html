El PIB creció un 2,5% en 2023, una décima más de lo previsto, tras acelerar al 0,6% en el cuarto trimestre

The Spanish economy demonstrated robust resilience in 2023, exceeding government projections with solid annual and quarterly growth. This performance marks a significant recovery phase following the pandemic-induced contraction, characterized by consecutive years of expansion. The data reveals that domestic demand and an improving labor market were the primary drivers, signaling a healthy economic trajectory that sets a favorable foundation for meeting future stability goals. Sectoral analysis highlights that while services, construction, and industry contributed positively, agriculture faced a notable decline. Furthermore, although investment remained in positive territory for three consecutive years, its growth rate moderated compared to previous highs. The final quarter showed a particularly strong quarterly surge, driven largely by internal consumption and a revitalized job market, indicating that consumer confidence and employment trends remain central to the country's economic engine. This report is relevant to open data initiatives as it underscores the critical importance of transparent, timely, and granular statistical releases by national institutes like the INE. By making detailed macroeconomic indicators publicly accessible, such data empowers researchers, policymakers, and the private sector to conduct precise analyses, validate economic narratives, and develop evidence-based strategies. The availability of high-quality open statistical data is essential for fostering accountability and informed decision-making across all levels of society.
