How Medical Racism Exacerbates the Black Youth Mental Health Crisis

Black children face stigma and harsh treatment due to misdiagnoses like oppositional defiant disorder, often labeled as "bad" rather than medically understood. This bias leads to disproportionate restraint and inadequate care, reflecting systemic anti-Black racism within healthcare institutions. This disparity highlights critical health inequities rooted in provider bias and limited empathy toward Black youth. It underscores the urgent need for equitable diagnostic practices and interventions that protect vulnerable patients from institutional harm. These findings are relevant to open data as they reveal hidden biases in clinical records. Transparent, disaggregated datasets can expose these disparities, driving accountability and facilitating targeted policy changes to improve equity in pediatric mental health care.
