Three million crimes against businesses in 2025: INEGI - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

Three million crimes against businesses in 2025: INEGI - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

The 2026 National Enterprise Survey (ENVE) conducted by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) reveals the hidden magnitude of corporate crime in Mexico, highlighting that the low reporting rate forces reliance on indirect data to understand the true level of insecurity. By recording thefts, fraud, and extortion regardless of whether an official complaint was filed, the survey exposes the disconnect between businesses’ perception of security and the operational reality, enabling a more honest diagnosis of impunity. The results indicate that although the economic cost of insecurity has decreased, small businesses remain the most vulnerable. This finding enables governments to prioritize resources toward small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and micro-businesses, which experience crimes more frequently. Disaggregated data by state and type of offense provides critical inputs for designing targeted preventive strategies, thereby improving the effectiveness of public protection policies. This publication is relevant to the open data movement, as it demonstrates how official statistics can fill information gaps where the formal justice system falls short. By making detailed data on unreported victimization accessible, it promotes transparency and empowers civil society and academics to analyze security trends. This fosters a culture of open data that prioritizes a comprehensive understanding of criminal phenomena over official complaint figures, enhancing accountability and national strategic planning.
