SC seeks centre's reply on judges' appointment MoP

SC seeks centre's reply on judges' appointment MoP

The Supreme Court has summoned the central government regarding the prolonged delay in finalizing the Memorandum of Procedure for judicial appointments. This legal action highlights the ongoing institutional friction between the executive and judiciary, which has stalled the formalization of appointment protocols for years. Crucially, the petition seeks to bring the collegium’s decisions under the Right to Information Act. This demand underscores a critical need for transparency and public accountability within the opaque judicial appointment mechanism, addressing long-standing concerns about the lack of disclosed reasoning and procedural clarity. This case is vital for open data advocacy as it challenges the opacity of high-level governmental and judicial decision-making. By pushing for the RTI coverage of the collegium, the legal battle exemplifies the broader struggle to ensure that public interest functions remain accessible and transparent, setting a precedent for data availability in constitutional processes.
