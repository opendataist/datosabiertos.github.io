Privacy and Confidentiality Policy

Privacy and Confidentiality Policy

The newspaper El Sol ensures compliance with Argentina’s data protection regulations, guaranteeing that the collection of personal information is carried out exclusively for specific purposes and under strict confidentiality. The policy emphasizes that no sensitive data are stored and that personal information is never shared with third parties without explicit consent, except when required by a court order. The entity implements technical and organizational security measures to protect data integrity, while also warning about the inherent risks of the internet. Furthermore, through the use of cookies, it seeks to optimize the user experience and analyze traffic, providing visitors with tools to control their digital tracking. This declaration is relevant to the open data movement because it establishes clear limits on what information is shared or sold, distinguishing between anonymous statistical data and protected personal data. By reinforcing the rights of access, rectification, and erasure, it empowers citizens to control their digital footprint, promoting the transparency and trust necessary for the responsible exchange of public information.
