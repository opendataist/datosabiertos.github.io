Prices: Wholesale inflation was 1.6% in October, accumulating a 24.8% increase for the year

Prices: Wholesale inflation was 1.6% in October, accumulating a 24.8% increase for the year

The report from INDEC reveals that wholesale inflation continues its upward trend throughout 2026, highlighting the significant impact of fuels and chemical products on the overall index. This trend reflects persistent inflationary pressures affecting both local production and imported inputs, underscoring the complexity of the current economic situation. The relevance of this report for the open data community lies in the public availability and detailed structure of the time series data for the Wholesale Price Index (IPIM) and its components. Direct access to this statistical information enables analysts, researchers, and public software developers to build predictive models and economic monitoring dashboards, fostering transparency and the use of open data for informed decision-making in both the private and academic sectors. Ultimately, this data not only quantifies price increases but also serves as a critical input for evaluating government economic policies. The official publication of these indicators ensures accountability and facilitates social auditing of macroeconomic outcomes, reinforcing the culture of institutional transparency that is fundamental to Argentina’s open data ecosystem.
