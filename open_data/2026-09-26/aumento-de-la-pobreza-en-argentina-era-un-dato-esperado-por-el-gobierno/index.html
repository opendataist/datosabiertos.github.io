The Argentine government anticipated rising poverty and destitution rates for early 2026, marking a halt to the previous decline in social welfare. This trend is primarily attributed to deterioration in the labor market, characterized by job precarity rather than simple unemployment, alongside fixed expenses consuming a significant share of household income.
