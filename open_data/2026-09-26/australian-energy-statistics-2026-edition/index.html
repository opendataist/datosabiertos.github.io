The Australian Energy Statistics, now updated to 2024-25, serves as the authoritative source for national energy data. This annual release provides comprehensive metrics on consumption, production, trade, and economic energy balances. It supports open data initiatives by offering transparent, accessible insights into the country’s energy landscape.
