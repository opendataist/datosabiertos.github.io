Ecuador acelera su transición energética y es el segundo país qeu ás compra autos eléctricos en la región | La República EC

Ecuador has emerged as a regional leader in sustainable mobility, experiencing a dramatic surge in sales of electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles. Driven by fiscal incentives and rising fuel costs, electrified vehicles now account for a significant quarter of the new car market, signaling a decisive shift in consumer behavior toward cleaner transportation alternatives. However, this rapid adoption exposes a critical infrastructure gap, as the availability of charging stations lags far behind that of neighboring countries. The scarcity of public charging points threatens to hinder further growth, highlighting the urgent need for coordinated private initiatives and robust public policies to expand fast-charging networks along major highways. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible datasets in tracking technological transitions. Reliable statistics from organizations such as Olade enable policymakers and researchers to identify infrastructural bottlenecks, fostering evidence-based decision-making and facilitating the open sharing of mobility trends to accelerate the achievement of sustainable development goals.
