Waymo — Nearly 1,000 Avoided Injuries - CleanTechnica

Waymo — Nearly 1,000 Avoided Injuries - CleanTechnica

Waymo reports that its autonomous vehicles have avoided hundreds of human injuries across extensive testing miles, demonstrating a dramatic safety advantage over human drivers. The data highlights an exponential increase in avoided accidents, suggesting that the technology consistently outperforms human benchmarks in reducing both minor and severe collisions. The safety benefits extend significantly to vulnerable road users, with substantial reductions in injury-causing crashes involving pedestrians, cyclists, and motorcyclists. This evidence supports the argument that autonomous systems can create safer urban environments, potentially justifying public subsidies to accelerate adoption and maximize societal protection against traffic-related harms. This article is relevant to open data because it emphasizes the importance of transparent, granular safety metrics for public accountability. By publishing detailed breakdowns of crash types and exposure scenarios, the company invites public scrutiny and independent verification. Such transparency fosters trust in emerging technologies and encourages a data-driven dialogue about regulatory standards and the ethical deployment of autonomous systems in public spaces.
