La pobreza subió al 32,3% en el primer semestre del año y afectó a 15,5 millones de personas - Actualidad TDF

Argentina’s first half of 2026 shows rising poverty and indigence, reversing recent declines. This economic deterioration underscores the critical need for timely, accessible public data to accurately track social well-being. Open data facilitates transparent monitoring of such trends, enabling researchers and citizens to hold institutions accountable. By making census results freely available, society can better understand the scope of vulnerability across different demographics and age groups. Ultimately, reliable statistics are essential for designing effective policies to combat inequality. Ensuring open access to this information empowers communities to advocate for resources and support, fostering a more responsive approach to national economic challenges.
