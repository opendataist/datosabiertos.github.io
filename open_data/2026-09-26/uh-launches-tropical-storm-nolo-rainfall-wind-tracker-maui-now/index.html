UH launches Tropical Storm Nolo rainfall, wind tracker | Maui Now

The University of Hawaiʻi launched a specialized weather tracker to make real-time meteorological data immediately accessible during storms. By visualizing wind and rainfall through interactive maps, the tool empowers the public and emergency responders to monitor changing conditions effectively and make informed safety decisions. This platform significantly enhances open data practices by transforming raw station observations into intuitive, user-friendly visualizations. It demonstrates how transparent access to scientific measurements can improve community resilience, allowing diverse stakeholders to quickly understand the impact of severe weather events in their specific locations. Ultimately, this initiative highlights the critical value of open data infrastructure during crises. By providing clear, timely evidence of environmental shifts, it supports better risk assessment and communication, proving that accessible data is essential for effective disaster management and public awareness.
