LinkDaddy Launches AI Verified Gold Business Identity Registry

LinkDaddy Launches AI Verified Gold Business Identity Registry

LinkDaddy has introduced the AI Verified Gold Business Passport, an expansion of its public business identity registry designed to solve the pervasive problem of fragmented corporate data across the internet. Rather than creating another isolated business listing like conventional directories, this initiative aggregates scattered evidence—such as licenses, registrations, and professional profiles—into a single, coherent public record. This approach provides a consistent home for business identity that connects existing online sources, reducing confusion for both human readers and software systems. A key innovation of the Gold Passport is the provision of a matching machine-readable record alongside the human-readable profile. By structuring core identity information in a format that software can easily process, the registry aims to reduce ambiguity when a business appears under different names or on disparate platforms. This dual-format capability ensures that while people can understand the identity, AI agents and automated systems can also accurately parse and reference the business, bridging the gap between traditional web presence and modern data consumption needs. This development is significant for open data because it promotes the interoperability and standardization of business information. By organizing disparate public records into a linked, structured format, AI Verified enhances the utility of open government and commercial data. It encourages a more transparent ecosystem where data silos are connected, allowing algorithms and researchers to verify identities more reliably. This contributes to a healthier open data landscape by ensuring that foundational business metadata is consistent, accessible, and machine-friendly, ultimately improving the accuracy of information used by AI and public services.
