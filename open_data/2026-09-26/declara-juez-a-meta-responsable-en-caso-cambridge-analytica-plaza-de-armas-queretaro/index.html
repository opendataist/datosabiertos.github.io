Declara juez a Meta responsable en caso Cambridge Analytica - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

Declara juez a Meta responsable en caso Cambridge Analytica - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

A jury found that Meta Platforms deceived New Mexico residents by concealing the sale of their personal data to third parties and by allowing the platform to be misused for hate speech and disinformation for financial gain. The verdict confirms that, although the company claimed to have control and strict policies, it in fact facilitated the collection of information without consent, as was the case with Cambridge Analytica. The significance of this case lies in the tension between corporate statements on privacy and the commercial realities of the technology industry. The decision underscores users’ vulnerability in the face of opacity in the handling of digital information, demonstrating how a lack of transparency can manipulate public perception and fundamental rights over one’s own digital identity. This ruling is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical need for verifiable and accessible data to audit corporate practices. Without mechanisms for public transparency and access to real information on data processing, citizens and regulators lack the necessary tools to combat systemic deception and ensure that digital platforms operate with ethical and legal responsibility.
