Amazon, AI startup Hugging Face pair to use Amazon chips - ET Telecom

Amazon, AI startup Hugging Face pair to use Amazon chips - ET Telecom

Amazon Web Services has partnered with Hugging Face to streamline the deployment of open-source AI models on its custom Inferentia2 chips. This collaboration lowers barriers for developers, allowing them to efficiently run thousands of models while reducing costs significantly. By optimizing the inference process, the partnership ensures that open-source tools remain accessible and economically viable for widespread use. This integration highlights the critical importance of efficient inference in the AI ecosystem. While training is occasional, running models is frequent and resource-intensive. The partnership addresses this by providing a cost-effective infrastructure, encouraging more developers to utilize open-source models rather than relying solely on expensive proprietary solutions. The relevance to open data lies in its support for the open-source community. By making it easier and cheaper to deploy open-source AI models, Amazon and Hugging Face foster a more inclusive environment for innovation. This empowers researchers and developers to share and improve upon existing work, accelerating advancements in accessible artificial intelligence technology.
