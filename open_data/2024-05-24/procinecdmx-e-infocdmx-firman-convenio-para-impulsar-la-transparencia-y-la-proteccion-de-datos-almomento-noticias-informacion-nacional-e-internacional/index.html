PROCINECDMX e INFOCDMX firman convenio para impulsar la transparencia y la protección de datos - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

PROCINECDMX e INFOCDMX firman convenio para impulsar la transparencia y la protección de datos - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

PROCINECDMX and INFOCDMX have established a collaboration to foster a culture of transparency and accountability within Mexico City’s film industry. By leveraging cinema as a medium for civic education, the agreement aims to use film screenings and debates to raise public awareness about fundamental rights, specifically access to information and personal data protection. This partnership transforms artistic events into platforms for democratic engagement, encouraging citizens to understand their rights in an increasingly digital society. The relevance to open data lies in the strategic use of public communication channels to demystify and popularize data governance principles. By integrating themes of privacy and transparency into cultural programming, the initiative bridges the gap between technical regulatory frameworks and public understanding. This approach ensures that open data concepts are not viewed solely as bureaucratic requirements but as essential tools for protecting individual dignity and combating corruption. Consequently, the collaboration serves as a model for how government bodies can actively promote data rights through accessible, community-oriented initiatives, thereby strengthening democratic participation and trust in public institutions.
