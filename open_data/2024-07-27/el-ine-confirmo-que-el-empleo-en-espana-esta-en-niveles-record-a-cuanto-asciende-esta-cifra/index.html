El INE confirmó que el empleo en España está en niveles récord: a cuánto asciende esta cifra

El INE confirmó que el empleo en España está en niveles récord: a cuánto asciende esta cifra

The Spanish labor market has recorded historic levels of employment and a significant reduction in unemployment, driven primarily by the services sector and supported by recent labor reforms. These figures reflect sustained growth in the active population and have improved living conditions for families, although the government acknowledges that challenges remain to consolidate real and lasting full employment. However, job quality suffers from serious structural deficiencies. Spain leads the European Union in the share of overqualified workers, with a rate far above the EU average. This disparity reveals a mismatch between workers’ skills and their jobs, disproportionately affecting women and immigrants, indicating that quantitative gains in employment have not necessarily translated into better utilization of human capital or into better alignment between jobs and the training received. This report is relevant to the open data movement because it highlights the need to analyze statistics beyond traditional macroeconomic aggregates. To understand the realities of the labor market and design effective policies, it is essential to have and cross-reference detailed open datasets on qualifications, sector, and demographics. Only through transparent and granular access to this information can the gap between employment and training be properly diagnosed, enabling more precise accountability and evidence-based management.
