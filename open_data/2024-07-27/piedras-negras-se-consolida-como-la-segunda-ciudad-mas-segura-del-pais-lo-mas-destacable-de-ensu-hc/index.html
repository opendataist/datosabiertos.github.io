Piedras Negras se consolida como la segunda ciudad más segura del País, lo más destacable de ENSU: HC

Piedras Negras se consolida como la segunda ciudad más segura del País, lo más destacable de ENSU: HC

Piedras Negras ranks as Mexico’s second safest city, reaching historical highs in public security perception. This sustained low level of fear of crime validates the administration’s consistent and professional security strategies over recent years. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, high-quality government statistics can effectively measure and communicate real-world improvements in safety. It highlights the power of INEGI’s ENSU data in validating policy outcomes and informing public discourse.
