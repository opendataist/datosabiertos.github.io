Diario HOY | Presentan versión actualizada del proyecto de Protección de Datos Personales

Diario HOY | Presentan versión actualizada del proyecto de Protección de Datos Personales

Paraguay’s Personal Data Coalition has submitted an updated draft law for comprehensive data protection, aiming to resolve legislative stagnation since 2021. This version reflects a broad consensus among public and private stakeholders, signaling strong political momentum toward approval. Recent high-profile data breaches and forced biometric collection have highlighted the urgent need for such regulation. These incidents demonstrate the real-world risks of inadequate privacy frameworks, reinforcing the immediate necessity for robust legal safeguards. This development is crucial for open data advocates, as it establishes the foundational rights and obligations necessary for ethical data governance. A clear legal framework ensures that data usage respects individual privacy, enabling responsible innovation and trust in digital ecosystems while protecting citizens from exploitation.
