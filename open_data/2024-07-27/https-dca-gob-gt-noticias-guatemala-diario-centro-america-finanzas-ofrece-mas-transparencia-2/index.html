Open Data
ES EN

The Ministry of Public Finance is relaunching the Open Data Users Committee, aiming to strengthen dialogue with civil society to improve the quality and accessibility of fiscal information. This mechanism will enable the redesign of the portal based on direct feedback, prioritizing the linkage between financial data and operational outcomes. The initiative responds to the need to optimize the availability of information to ensure transparency and facilitate social auditing. By incorporating new dimensions such as planned physical targets, the goal is to bridge the gap between budget execution and the real impact of public projects. This relaunch is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it transforms the mere publication of figures into a tool for accountability. By integrating the voices of researchers and the media, it promotes an environment where data are not only public but also understandable and useful for informed decision-making.
