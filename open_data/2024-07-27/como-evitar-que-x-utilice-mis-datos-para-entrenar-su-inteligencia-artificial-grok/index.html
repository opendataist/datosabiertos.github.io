Cómo evitar que X utilice mis datos para entrenar su Inteligencia Artificial Grok

Cómo evitar que X utilice mis datos para entrenar su Inteligencia Artificial Grok

The main finding is that the X platform has enabled a mechanism through which users can opt in to allow their posts and interactions to be used to train Grok, the social network’s artificial intelligence assistant. This setting grants users a certain degree of autonomy over the fate of their digital content, although it requires active intervention to exercise such control, since the opt-in option is enabled by default in many regions. The relevance of this case for the fields of open data and digital ethics lies in the tension between technological innovation and privacy protection. The use of user-generated data for the development of machine learning models raises critical questions about informed consent and transparency. By allowing public information to be incorporated into datasets for commercial purposes or AI training, the true nature of the “openness” of these data and the intellectual property rights of the original creators are called into question. Furthermore, the community’s response reveals a growing awareness regarding the management of one’s digital footprint. The concerns expressed by users underscore the need for clear regulatory frameworks that ensure access to and use of information are not opaque. This scenario reflects a paradigm shift in which the interaction between social platforms and artificial intelligence demands greater scrutiny of how personal data are collected, stored, and processed, directly impacting information governance in the digital age.
