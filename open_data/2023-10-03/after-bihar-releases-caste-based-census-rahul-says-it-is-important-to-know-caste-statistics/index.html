After Bihar releases caste-based census, Rahul says it is important to know caste statistics

The recent release of Bihar’s caste-based survey underscores the urgent need for accurate demographic data to drive social justice. The findings reveal that marginalized communities constitute the vast majority of the state’s population, yet hold a disproportionately small share of political power and budgetary control. This disparity highlights the critical importance of transparent data in identifying and addressing systemic inequalities. Accurate caste statistics are essential for designing effective social empowerment programs. Without reliable data, governments cannot fairly allocate resources or ensure equitable representation for historically disadvantaged groups. The absence of a national census prevents a comprehensive understanding of these dynamics across India, limiting the potential for meaningful reform and inclusive policy-making. This article is highly relevant to open data advocates because it demonstrates how releasing granular, granular demographic information can challenge existing power structures. Transparency in census data allows citizens and policymakers to hold authorities accountable, ensuring that resource distribution reflects actual demographic realities rather than political assumptions. Open data serves as a foundational tool for achieving genuine social equity.
