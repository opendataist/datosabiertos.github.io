Más de 300 mdd en exportaciones

The article highlights Chiapas’s export performance, revealing a significant decline from the previous year. It identifies oil and gas extraction, agriculture, and food manufacturing as the primary drivers of the state’s external trade, with transportation equipment also playing a notable role despite the overall downturn. This data is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how granular, publicly accessible statistical reports enable transparent economic tracking. By making detailed sectoral and regional metrics available, platforms can support evidence-based analysis of local economic dependencies and vulnerabilities. Understanding these export structures helps researchers and policymakers identify key industries that require support or diversification. Consequently, leveraging such open datasets facilitates better resource allocation and strategic planning for regional development, ensuring that economic decisions are grounded in accurate, real-time information rather than opaque aggregates.
