Israel: Exports and Imports of Goods by Mode of Transport 2022

Israel’s 2022 trade data reveals a significant reliance on air transport for exports, which accounted for the majority of total export value, while sea transport dominated the import landscape. This structural imbalance highlights how high-value goods leave the country primarily via air, whereas bulk imports arrive by sea. The data underscores the distinct logistical strategies employed for different trade flows, emphasizing the critical role of air freight in sustaining Israel’s export-oriented economy. China remains a pivotal partner in Israel’s import sector, particularly for sea-borne goods, while the United States leads as the primary destination for air exports. These trends illustrate the deepening economic ties with major global powers and reveal shifting trade dynamics, such as increased export volumes to Ireland and steady import relationships with Germany and India. Understanding these partner-specific patterns is essential for analyzing trade dependencies and potential supply chain vulnerabilities in a globalized market. This report is relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates how national statistical agencies provide granular, mode-specific trade metrics. By releasing detailed breakdowns of transport methods and partner countries, governments enable researchers and businesses to analyze economic resilience and infrastructure needs. Open access to such structured data supports transparency, fosters evidence-based policy-making, and allows for deeper insights into the complexities of international commerce and transport logistics.
