INEI: Sector Minería e Hidrocarburos creció en 16,74 % en el mes de mayo | RPP Noticias

The report highlights significant sectoral shifts in Peru’s economy, notably a surge in metallic mining and renewable energy generation, which contrasts with sharp declines in traditional fisheries, non-metallic mining, and hydroelectric power. This divergence underscores how economic performance is increasingly driven by specific commodity demands and energy transitions rather than uniform growth across all industries. Financial metrics reveal a mixed picture, with government investment and consumer credit expanding significantly, suggesting active fiscal stimulus and household spending. Conversely, business lending contracted, indicating that corporate entities are adopting a more cautious approach to credit expansion despite the overall rise in consumer debt and government expenditure. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it exemplifies the necessity of granular, real-time economic indicators for transparent governance and informed decision-making. Access to such detailed statistical breakdowns allows researchers and citizens to verify official narratives, identify emerging trends, and hold institutions accountable, thereby fostering a data-driven society that relies on verified, accessible public information rather than opaque aggregations.
