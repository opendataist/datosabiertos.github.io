CURP biométrica obtiene certificado; “seguridad va bien”, afirma SEGOB

CURP biométrica obtiene certificado; “seguridad va bien”, afirma SEGOB

The Mexican government certifies the biometric CURP under ISO 9000 standards, ensuring rigorous protection of personal data. This validation strengthens the national system for locating missing persons by guaranteeing reliable identity records, fostering public trust in government security mechanisms. This development is relevant to open data because it establishes verified, standardized processes for managing sensitive biometric information. By demonstrating compliance with international quality norms, it provides a model for how personal data can be securely managed, potentially enabling more trustworthy integration into broader data ecosystems and supporting future open initiatives centered on verified identity.
