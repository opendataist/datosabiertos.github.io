Para el 2050 la población será de 7,1 millones y con mayor envejecimiento

Paraguay will undergo a profound demographic transformation by 2050, characterized by the accelerated aging of its population and a sharp decline in fertility rates. The age structure will shift from being predominantly young to being dominated by older adults, significantly altering the country’s productive and social base. This transition implies that population growth will notably slow down, replacing youth-driven expansion with a dynamic in which longevity surpasses birth rates. This structural change poses urgent challenges to the sustainability of health, pension, and social protection systems. With a relatively shrinking workforce and a massive increase in retired or near-retirement individuals, the State must adapt its public policies to ensure the coverage and quality of these services. Investment in social infrastructure and the redefinition of labor strategies are essential to maintain fiscal balance and social cohesion in the face of a population that is living longer but with less generational renewal. The relevance of these data for open data lies in the need for transparency and access to official information to plan national development. Projections from the National Institute of Statistics (INE), developed using international standards, serve as a critical input for academic research, evidence-based policy design, and citizen oversight. Making these time series openly available enables civil society and decision-makers to analyze trends, anticipate demographic crises, and promote informed and responsible governance in addressing the country’s future challenges.
