Veracruz, con la mayor tasa de muertes por males cardiacos y diabetes, en 2023

Veracruz recorded the highest national mortality rates for heart disease and diabetes in 2023, according to official statistics from INEGI. These findings highlight critical regional health disparities driven by non-communicable diseases, with cardiac conditions and diabetes accounting for the largest number of deaths among the state’s population. The data further reveal that, although rates of malignancies and suicide varied, cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of death, significantly affecting both male and female demographics. This concentrated mortality burden underscores the urgent need for targeted public health interventions tailored to the region’s specific epidemiological profile. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent, official statistical releases enable granular geographic analysis. By making detailed mortality metrics publicly available, stakeholders can identify health crises, allocate resources effectively, and monitor the effectiveness of medical policies across different Mexican states.
