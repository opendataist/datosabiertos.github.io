China still be the biggest cooperative partner for Brazil in 2014

China and Brazil are deepening their comprehensive strategic partnership, with China remaining Brazil’s top trading partner. This diplomatic strengthening facilitates smoother cross-border trade and encourages further integration of their economies through coordinated policy efforts and shared interests in resources and business expansion. The Brazilian e-commerce sector is emerging as a vital growth engine, significantly outpacing developed markets in expansion rates. Chinese companies are aggressively entering this booming Latin American market to capitalize on high consumer enthusiasm and the region's vast potential for online retail sales, which are projected to double in value rapidly. This trend is highly relevant to open_data because the intersection of global trade and digital commerce generates massive, complex datasets regarding consumer behavior, supply chains, and market dynamics. Understanding these flows requires transparent access to trade statistics and sales metrics. Open data enables better analysis of international e-commerce trends, helping businesses and policymakers navigate the evolving landscape of global digital trade and infrastructure development.
