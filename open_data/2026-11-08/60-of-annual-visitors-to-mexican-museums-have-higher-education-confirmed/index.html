60% of annual visitors to Mexican museums have higher education - Confirmed

60% of annual visitors to Mexican museums have higher education - Confirmed

Mexican museums continue to be spaces predominantly visited by individuals with higher education, suggesting a significant gap in cultural access for the general population. This sociodemographic profile limits the transformative reach that institutions seek to promote, particularly within a broader context of social inclusion. Geographic concentration in certain areas and variability in free-admission policies affect the equity of access. Although most museums offer basic services such as guided tours, improvements are still needed in physical accessibility and digital connectivity to serve a more diverse and contemporary audience. This information is relevant to the open data movement, as it highlights the need for transparency in cultural management. Publishing this data openly enables civil society to analyze access barriers, demand more inclusive policies, and promote informed citizen participation in the revitalization of the museum sector.
