Manufacturing industry fell 1.8% in September year-on-year, marking the third consecutive month of decline

The manufacturing industry is experiencing a sustained year-on-year slowdown, with key sectors such as textiles and machinery recording significant contractions. This landscape reflects the fragility of the sector, exacerbated by closures and layoffs that threaten the job stability of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) during the industrial conference. The measures announced by the government aim to mitigate this crisis, prioritizing support for small and medium-sized enterprises. The tension between productive contraction and the need for economic stimulus highlights the structural challenges facing local production in the short term. This report is relevant to open data by demonstrating how official statistics must be accessible and clear to analyze economic trends. Transparency in these indicators enables civil society and experts to critically evaluate public policies and understand the real impact of government decisions on the industrial market.
