Misinformation Presented To Court In Wesley Twp FOIA Case –

Wesley Township spread misinformation during FOIA litigation, misleading court personnel about plaintiff appearance requirements. This tactic aimed to sanction residents merely for requesting public records, highlighting systemic resistance to open data. The case underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible information governance to prevent bureaucratic retaliation against citizens seeking accountability.
