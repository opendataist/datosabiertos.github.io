Vietnam siguió siendo el segundo exportador mundial de calzado en febrero | Economía | Vietnam+ (VietnamPlus)

Vietnam has solidified its position as the world’s second-largest footwear exporter, demonstrating significant global market penetration despite monthly fluctuations. This economic resilience underscores the sector’s critical role in international trade dynamics and supply chain diversification. The industry’s robust expansion toward ambitious long-term targets highlights a strategic shift toward higher value-added production and sustainable growth. These projections signal a transformative period for manufacturing hubs seeking to reduce their reliance on single markets. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it illustrates how transparent trade statistics enable real-time monitoring of global economic shifts. Access to such granular export figures fosters accountability, supports evidence-based policymaking, and enhances the transparency of international commercial flows.
