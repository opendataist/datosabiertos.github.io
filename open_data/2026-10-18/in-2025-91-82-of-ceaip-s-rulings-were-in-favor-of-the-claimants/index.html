In 2025, 91.82% of Ceaip's rulings were in favor of the claimants.

In 2025, 91.82% of Ceaip's rulings were in favor of the claimants.

The 2025 Activity Report of the State Commission for Access to Public Information of Sinaloa reveals a transparent environment in which the majority of review resources were resolved in favor of citizens. This positive trend indicates that authorities are being held accountable for fulfilling their duty to provide transparency, ensuring that the public can effectively access the data held by the State. A general decrease in the volume of requests is observed, with a significant concentration in municipalities and the Executive Branch. Although the number of requests declined, the structure of demand shows that citizens prioritize access to public information over the protection of personal data, placing administrative pressure primarily on city councils and autonomous bodies. The relevance of this text for open data lies in its demonstration of the functioning of citizen and state oversight mechanisms. By showing that sanctions and reprimands are imposed for non-compliance, it is evident that open data is not merely a formal obligation but an enforceable right with real legal consequences, thereby incentivizing proactive publication and improving the quality of available information.
