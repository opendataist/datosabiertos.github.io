Registration for candidates to coordinate Morena in Cajeme closes this Sunday; so far, there are 7

Morena is launching its internal selection process in Cajeme to choose the municipal coordinator, allowing several candidates—including former officials and legislators—to submit their registrations before the deadline. The party has established specific requirements, such as residency duration and acceptance of poll results, distinguishing between those who must request leave from their current positions and those who can retain them. This mechanism underscores the importance of transparency and internal participation in political selection processes. For open data purposes, it is crucial that these registrations and eligibility rules be made digitally accessible, enabling public scrutiny and accountability. Digitizing the registration process facilitates access to information and fosters citizen trust in democratic institutions. The relevance to open data lies in the need to make public the selection criteria and participant data. This promotes electoral integrity and allows civil society to analyze the representativeness and diversity of candidates. Making this data available in open format enhances governance and strengthens democratic participation.
