ICE Vehicle Sales Crash 49%(!) While EV Share Jumps to 67%(!) — China EV Sales Report for September - CleanTechnica

ICE Vehicle Sales Crash 49%(!) While EV Share Jumps to 67%(!) — China EV Sales Report for September - CleanTechnica

The Chinese automotive market has reached a critical inflection point, with plug-in vehicles capturing a record 67% of sales in September. This milestone is driven less by EV sales growth and more by a sharp decline in internal combustion engine vehicles. The data suggests that China could achieve full electrification by 2030, signaling the imminent obsolescence of traditional ICE technology and reshaping global automotive dynamics. This rapid transition highlights a structural divide between shrinking legacy markets dominated by foreign manufacturers and a booming EV sector led by local brands. While traditional automakers struggle with significant sales drops, emerging Chinese startups like Leapmotor are experiencing substantial growth by offering competitive alternatives. The dominance of domestic players in the EV space indicates that future market leadership will likely shift away from established global giants toward agile, innovation-focused local enterprises. For the open data community, this article underscores the importance of tracking real-time consumption shifts and market share trends to understand technological disruption. Analyzing such granular sales data reveals how quickly industries can pivot, providing valuable insights for predicting future infrastructure needs, battery supply chains, and consumer behavior changes. It serves as a case study in how large-scale datasets can validate macroeconomic trends and inform strategic decisions in the energy transition.
