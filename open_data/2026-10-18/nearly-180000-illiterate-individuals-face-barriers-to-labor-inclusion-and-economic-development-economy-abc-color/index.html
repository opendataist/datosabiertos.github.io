Nearly 180,000 illiterate individuals face barriers to labor inclusion and economic development - Economy - ABC Color

Nearly 180,000 illiterate individuals face barriers to labor inclusion and economic development - Economy - ABC Color

Paraguay has achieved a significant improvement in overall education levels, reducing school dropout rates and increasing average years of schooling from 2002 to 2022. However, a critical literacy gap persists, affecting a considerable portion of the adult population, indicating that national progress has not been uniform or inclusive across all social segments. The analysis reveals deep structural disparities, particularly between rural and urban areas, as well as gender differences. These territorial and demographic gaps expose persistent inequality in access to quality education, concentrating illiteracy in vulnerable communities that have been left behind in the historical expansion of the national education system. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparency and access to detailed census data enable the identification of hidden inequalities. The publication of disaggregated indicators facilitates the design of targeted public policies and accountability mechanisms, which are essential to ensuring that educational development is equitable and that no community is excluded from the economic and social benefits associated with literacy.
