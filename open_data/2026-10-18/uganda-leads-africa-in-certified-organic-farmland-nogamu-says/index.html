Uganda Leads Africa in Certified Organic Farmland, NOGAMU Says

Uganda leads Africa in certified organic farmland and ranks globally among the top three nations by certified farmer count. This rapid expansion highlights organic agriculture as a critical economic driver, with export values nearly doubling in recent years. The sector’s resilience through global crises underscores its vital role in the national economy and global trade positioning.
