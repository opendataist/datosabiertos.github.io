Meta removes language that raised scraping and AI training concerns | TechCrunch

Meta’s controversial update sparked fears of unrestricted AI training using business content, raising significant concerns for open data transparency and publisher rights. Legal experts worried this allowed scraping without clear consent, potentially undermining existing copyright protections. Fortunately, Meta clarified the language applied only to ads and removed the provision, alleviating fears of a broad shift in how user-generated and web data are utilized for AI development.
