Chihuahua surpasses 3.9 million inhabitants: INEGI

Chihuahua surpasses 3.9 million inhabitants: INEGI

The 2025 Intercensal Survey conducted by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) reveals a slowdown in Chihuahua’s population growth, highlighting a gradual demographic aging process. These data are vital because they enable authorities to plan public services and infrastructure more accurately during the interim period between censuses. Detailed information on family structure and sex distribution facilitates the identification of specific social needs. With up-to-date statistics, the government can design more effective public policies tailored to the state’s current reality. This source is crucial for open data initiatives, as it transforms official figures into accessible public inputs. It promotes transparency in state management and empowers citizens and the private sector to analyze demographic trends, fostering evidence-based accountability.
