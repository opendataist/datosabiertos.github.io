Three good news for the country

The article highlights the Uruguayan government’s efforts to reverse its low approval ratings through concrete policies that positively impact quality of life. The reduction in poverty and changes to the pension system represent significant advances, though insufficient on their own; they must be complemented by other structural measures to ensure long-term, sustainable, and inclusive development. Furthermore, the article underscores the importance of new foreign investments to diversify the economy and stimulate growth. Although operational and environmental details are still lacking, these projects are considered necessary to generate employment and attract capital, offering a perspective of economic recovery after a period of relative stagnation compared to initial expectations. This news is relevant to open data because it emphasizes the importance of transparency and access to public information in rigorous evaluation processes. For public investments and policies to generate real benefits and not become the subject of superficial political debate, it is essential to have verified data, accessible impact studies, and clear metrics that enable citizens to monitor the fulfillment of objectives and the efficiency in the use of scarce resources.
