Las ventas del sector servicios se acrecentan en C-LM un 3,9% en septiembre, 2 puntos por encima de la media nacional

The services sector demonstrates robust growth, with national sales and employment rising consistently for months. This positive trend highlights the sector's resilience and continued expansion across most regions, signaling a healthy economic contribution despite isolated local declines. The data reveals widespread regional improvements in both turnover and job creation. By emphasizing general upward trajectories rather than specific percentages, we see a clear picture of an industry that remains a primary driver of economic stability and labor absorption nationwide. This information is relevant to open_data because transparent, high-frequency statistics on employment and business turnover enable policymakers and researchers to monitor economic health accurately. Open access to such granular, timely data fosters better decision-making and deeper analysis of sectoral trends without reliance on closed or aggregated metrics.
