Sheinbaum exhibe observaciones de la ASF al INAI por corrupción

Sheinbaum exhibe observaciones de la ASF al INAI por corrupción

President Sheinbaum highlights severe corruption and nepotism within the former Federal Auditor’s Office, arguing that these systemic irregularities were previously overlooked. This reveals a culture of financial misconduct, including withheld salaries and the improper use of public funds by officials. The narrative shifts toward reforming the National Institute for Transparency, asserting that ending its autonomy will eliminate these internal abuses. Rather than reducing oversight, the administration claims this move enhances accountability by integrating supervision into the Secretariat of Public Function. This development is relevant to open data, as it restructures transparency mechanisms. By centralizing data oversight, the government aims to improve citizens’ access to expenditure information, potentially streamlining how public data is managed and utilized to enhance governmental accountability.
