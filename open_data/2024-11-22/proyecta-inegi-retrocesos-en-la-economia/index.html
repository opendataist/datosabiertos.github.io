Proyecta Inegi retrocesos en la economía

Mexico’s economy contracted for three consecutive months in late 2024, driven by stagnation in both the industrial and service sectors. This decline suggests broader economic challenges, despite modest annual growth expectations. The consistent monthly downturn highlights vulnerabilities in key productive areas, indicating a fragile economic environment rather than robust expansion. This data is relevant to open data initiatives by demonstrating how accessible economic indicators empower users to track national trends. It underscores the importance of transparent, timely statistics for understanding complex macroeconomic shifts and supporting informed public discourse.
