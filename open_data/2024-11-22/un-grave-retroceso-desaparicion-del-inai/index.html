Un grave retroceso desaparición del INAI

Un grave retroceso desaparición del INAI

The proposed elimination of the INAI poses a significant threat to guaranteed access to information and data protection rights. Without adequate secondary legislation, transferring these critical functions to other government bodies leaves citizens vulnerable, potentially reversing established transparency advances and undermining democratic accountability mechanisms essential for an open society. Civil resistance is mobilizing to oppose this structural change, highlighting fears that the reform aims to shield the current administration from oversight while creating biased internal controls. This political conflict underscores the fragility of independent institutions, demonstrating how administrative reforms can directly impact the practical enforcement of data privacy laws and public scrutiny. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it questions the sustainability of transparent governance structures. If independent oversight is removed without robust legal substitutes, the foundational trust required for data sharing and public transparency erodes, threatening the integrity of information ecosystems and the rights of individuals to access and control their personal data within the public sphere.
