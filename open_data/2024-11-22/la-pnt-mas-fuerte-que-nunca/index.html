La PNT, más fuerte que nunca

La PNT, más fuerte que nunca

The impending constitutional reform aimed at dismantling the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) raises critical concerns about the continuity of the National Transparency Platform, which remains distinct from the institute despite housing the country’s most extensive public database. This platform serves as the fundamental interface for citizens to access government information and manage their personal data, reflecting a massive surge in public demand for transparency that underscores its indispensable role in democratic accountability. Technological advancements within the platform, driven by internal public servants, have significantly improved user experience and security through modern architecture and artificial intelligence. These upgrades ensure efficient data retrieval and robust protection against cyber threats without compromising operational stability. The shift to modular technologies allows for targeted maintenance, highlighting how skilled internal management can sustain complex systems independently of traditional proprietary dependencies. This situation is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the vulnerability of infrastructure built on institutional frameworks. If the INAI is eliminated, the legal and administrative stewardship of this vast, open dataset remains unclear. Ensuring the platform’s continued access and integrity is essential for preserving citizen trust and maintaining the availability of public information, proving that open data systems must be safeguarded beyond political transitions to effectively serve the public interest.
