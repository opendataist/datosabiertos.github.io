INE denuncia que su personal fue retenido y amedrentado en el Chapare

Violent intimidation by local groups in Bolivia’s Chapare region disrupted household survey data collection, forcing INE personnel to withdraw. This aggression underscores the critical safety risks that field workers face when collecting community statistics, potentially biasing national datasets. Protecting data collectors is essential for maintaining the integrity and reliability of open statistical information.
