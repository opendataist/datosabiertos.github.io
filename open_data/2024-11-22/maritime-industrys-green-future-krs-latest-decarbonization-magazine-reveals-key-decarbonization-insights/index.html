Maritime Industry’s Green Future: KR’s latest Decarbonization Magazine reveals key decarbonization insights

Korean Register’s latest analysis reveals critical decarbonization trends within its fleet, highlighting how regulatory pressures like CII ratings drive necessary industry shifts.
