Este 29 de abril CNE publica registro electoral preliminar

The electoral authority is finalizing the preliminary voter registration, a critical step that ensures transparency and accuracy ahead of the upcoming presidential election. This phase enables public scrutiny and corrections, directly supporting open data principles by making information on citizen eligibility accessible for verification and audit. At the same time, audits of the voting software and electoral systems are being conducted to guarantee technical integrity. These transparency measures are essential for open data advocates, as they ensure that the underlying technological infrastructure is open to inspection and free from hidden errors that could undermine democratic outcomes. Finally, the selection and training of polling station personnel underscore the operational readiness of the electoral process. By requiring candidates to verify their details through official digital platforms, the administration leverages public data channels to manage participation, reinforcing the importance of accessible, real-time information in maintaining electoral trust and openness.
