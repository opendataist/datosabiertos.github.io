INAI ordena a SEP informar protocolos de escuelas privadas en casos de Alerta Amber | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The National Institute for Transparency has ordered the Secretariat of Public Education to actively regulate how private schools in Mexico City must respond to Amber Alerts involving their students. This ruling establishes clear legal procedures and potential sanctions for non-compliance, ensuring that educational institutions play an active role in locating vulnerable minors rather than remaining passive entities during emergencies. The decision highlights the critical intersection between data transparency and child protection mechanisms. By mandating that the government disclose specific operational protocols, the ruling ensures that public accountability is maintained for systems designed to prevent exploitation and human trafficking. This openness allows society to verify that institutions are prepared to act swiftly when children are at risk. This case is relevant to open data because it reinforces the principle that access to information must be comprehensive and proactive. It demonstrates how transparency laws can be leveraged to clarify institutional responsibilities, ensuring that critical public safety information is not withheld due to bureaucratic incompetence. Ultimately, it supports the development of more effective, transparent frameworks for responding to public crises.
