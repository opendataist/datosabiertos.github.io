Presentan plataforma de datos abiertos sobre la industria de la artesanía dominicana

FODEARTE has launched an open data platform to map the Dominican Republic’s artisan sector, significantly enhancing transparency and accessibility. By centralizing census and demographic data, the initiative bridges the gap between creators and consumers, allowing the public to connect directly with artisans while reducing reliance on intermediaries. The platform aggregates detailed information on workshops, businesses, and socio-economic conditions across various craft categories. This comprehensive dataset serves as a foundation for evidence-based policy-making, aiming to improve living standards and social security for artisans by addressing systemic challenges identified through rigorous data analysis. This development is crucial for open data as it transforms fragmented administrative records into a public, geolocated resource. It empowers local communities by fostering direct market access and demonstrates how government transparency can drive economic inclusion, ensuring that cultural heritage and artisan livelihoods are supported by accessible, actionable intelligence.
