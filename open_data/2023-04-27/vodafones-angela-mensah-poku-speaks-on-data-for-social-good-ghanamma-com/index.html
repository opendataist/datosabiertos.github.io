Vodafone’s Angela Mensah-Poku speaks on data for social good - Ghanamma.com

Angela Mensah-Poku highlights a transformative public-private partnership in Ghana, demonstrating how aggregating anonymized data from mobile networks and government sources can drive meaningful social impact. By collaborating with entities like the Ghana Statistical Service and Flowminder, this initiative proved that shared data ecosystems are powerful tools for improving community development and informing critical socio-economic policies. The practical applications of this collaboration are extensive, ranging from disaster planning and disease tracking during the pandemic to predicting population movements for the census. These examples illustrate how accessible, high-quality data enables leaders to make evidence-based decisions that enhance resource allocation and public safety, ultimately leveraging technology to benefit humanity and foster sustainable growth in essential sectors like healthcare and education. This model is vital for open data because it validates the necessity of cross-sector collaboration and local capacity building. By transferring knowledge and skills to local organizations, the partnership ensures that data insights are not just extracted but effectively utilized by the community. This approach encourages a growing, inclusive ecosystem where diverse stakeholders contribute to global insights, reinforcing the idea that open, ethical data sharing is key to creating a more equitable and sustainable future.
