Ninguna instancia puede absorber funciones del INAI

The president of the INAI asserts that transparency functions must remain independent, as no other body has the authority to effectively oversee all branches of government. Institutional independence is vital for democracy, as it transforms popular will into tangible public outcomes rather than leaving it as mere ideals. Currently, the agency is paralyzed by vacancies in its commissioner positions, which prevents the holding of valid sessions and the resolution of citizen complaints. This structural gap hinders the defense of human rights related to access to information and the protection of personal data, leaving individuals vulnerable to arbitrary denials and mishandling of their private information. This situation underscores the critical importance of open data: functional and independent oversight institutions are essential to ensure transparency and protect citizens’ rights. Without robust administrative capacity, legal frameworks for open government remain ineffective, undermining trust and accountability in public administration.
