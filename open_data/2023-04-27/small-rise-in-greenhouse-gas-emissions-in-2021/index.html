Small rise in greenhouse gas emissions in 2021

Jersey’s greenhouse gas emissions experienced a minor rise in 2021 due to post-pandemic activity recovery, yet the long-term trajectory since 1990 remains strongly downward. Despite the recent fluctuation, residential emissions declined, highlighting successful local efforts, while transport continues to dominate as the primary source of emissions, necessitating a strategic shift toward active travel and reduced fossil fuel dependency. This dataset is vital for open_data initiatives as it provides transparent, longitudinal environmental metrics that enable public scrutiny and evidence-based policymaking. By releasing detailed, delayed statistics, the government demonstrates a commitment to data accuracy and accessibility, fostering trust and allowing researchers to analyze complex trends. Such open access supports community engagement with sustainability goals, ensuring citizens can understand the impact of policy measures like active travel promotion.
