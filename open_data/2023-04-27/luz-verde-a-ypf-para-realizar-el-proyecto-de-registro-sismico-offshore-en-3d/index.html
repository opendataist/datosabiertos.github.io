Luz verde a YPF para realizar el proyecto de registro sísmico offshore en 3D

Yanpetrol was authorized to conduct offshore seismic exploration in Argentina’s northern basin, subject to strict adherence to its environmental management plan. This approval mandates rigorous compliance with impact assessments and prohibits any project modifications without prior governmental authorization, ensuring ecological safeguards are maintained throughout operations. The regulatory framework highlights the importance of transparent environmental governance in extractive industries. By requiring advance notice for any changes to scope or logistics, authorities aim to prevent unauthorized deviations that could harm marine ecosystems. This oversight reflects a growing emphasis on balancing energy development with sustainable environmental practices in sensitive coastal areas. This case is relevant to open data because such resolutions are publicly accessible, allowing citizens and organizations to monitor corporate compliance and environmental impacts. Transparency in these administrative decisions enables civil society to verify adherence to regulations, fostering accountability and informed public participation in energy policy discussions.
