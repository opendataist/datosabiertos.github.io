NBS’s Internet rating of Ogun, dividends of Abiodun’s initiatives, says aide -

Ogun State’s ranking as a national leader in mobile internet subscriptions highlights how strategic government investment in Information and Communication Technology directly drives digital inclusion and economic participation. This achievement underscores the critical role of public infrastructure development in bridging the digital divide, making it highly relevant to open_data initiatives that rely on widespread connectivity to ensure equitable access to information and services. The state’s success stems from targeted interventions, including innovation hubs and entrepreneurship programs designed to empower youth and foster local business growth. These efforts demonstrate that when governments prioritize digital skills and technological infrastructure, they create ecosystems that attract investment and stimulate economic resilience, providing a model for how policy can catalyze community-wide digital adoption. Furthermore, the expansion of physical infrastructure, such as extensive fiber optic networks, serves as the backbone for sustainable digital transformation across all regions. This narrative reinforces the importance of open_data frameworks that leverage robust connectivity to share transparent, accessible, and actionable information, ultimately supporting informed decision-making and societal progress through technology.
