Freedom of Information statistics: October to December 2022

This article highlights the rigorous quality assurance behind UK Freedom of Information statistics, designated as National Statistics by the UK Statistics Authority. This status guarantees that the data meets highest standards of trustworthiness and public value, ensuring they reliably support informed decision-making. Such high-quality official data is crucial for open data ecosystems because it provides a verified, standardized foundation for transparency. When citizens and researchers access these statistics, they rely on the assurance that the underlying information has been independently assessed for accuracy and integrity. Furthermore, the inclusion of user feedback mechanisms demonstrates a commitment to continuous improvement in data service delivery. This responsiveness ensures that open data initiatives remain aligned with public needs, fostering greater trust and utility in government information for both analysis and civic engagement.
