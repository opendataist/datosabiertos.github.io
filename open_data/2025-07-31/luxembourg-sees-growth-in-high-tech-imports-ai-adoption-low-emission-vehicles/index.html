Luxembourg Sees Growth in High-Tech Imports, AI Adoption & Low-Emission Vehicles

STATEC’s latest business statistics reveal a dynamic Luxembourg economy characterized by robust enterprise growth and shifting industrial trends. The data highlights a notable increase in high-growth companies and a significant adoption of artificial intelligence, despite barriers related to cost and skills. Concurrently, the energy and transportation sectors demonstrate a clear transition toward sustainability, evidenced by rising electric vehicle registrations and specific import patterns of renewable technologies. This report is highly relevant to open data communities because it exemplifies comprehensive, multi-dimensional statistical dissemination. By making granular insights on sectoral activity, R&D intensity, and corporate financial health publicly accessible, STATEC enables researchers and developers to analyze economic resilience and innovation trajectories. Such transparency fosters evidence-based policy making and encourages third-party innovation based on reliable national metrics. The inclusion of diverse indicators, from AI usage to gender representation in research, underscores the value of interconnected datasets. Open access to these figures allows for deeper comparative analyses with neighboring countries and supports the development of tools that visualize economic trends. Ultimately, this publication illustrates how detailed, freely available statistical data can drive transparency, facilitate international benchmarking, and empower stakeholders to engage with the nuances of modern economic development.
