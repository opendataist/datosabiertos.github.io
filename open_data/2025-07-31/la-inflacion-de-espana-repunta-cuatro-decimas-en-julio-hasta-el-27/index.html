La inflación de España repunta cuatro décimas en julio, hasta el 2,7%

La inflación de España repunta cuatro décimas en julio, hasta el 2,7%

Spanish inflation has risen to 2.7%, driven primarily by higher electricity and fuel costs, marking a second consecutive monthly increase. This upward trend signals that price stability, while stabilizing in core categories, remains fragile for households as the cost of living pressures persist despite recent government claims of recovering purchasing power. The data highlights a complex economic narrative where headline inflation contrasts with stabilizing underlying indicators. While the government attributes this environment to strong economic growth and job creation, the persistent rise in energy prices undermines efforts to fully restore consumer confidence, creating a disconnect between macroeconomic success and individual financial realities. This situation is critical for open data initiatives, as transparent, timely, and granular inflation metrics are essential for verifying official narratives. Reliable access to disaggregated price data allows researchers and citizens to independently analyze the true impact of economic policies, ensuring that statistical releases accurately reflect the lived experiences of families and drive informed public debate.
