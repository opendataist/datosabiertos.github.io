Ingreso de familias ricas en México es 14 veces mayor que las pobres

Ingreso de familias ricas en México es 14 veces mayor que las pobres

The ENIGH 2024 data reveals that while Mexico’s wealthiest households earn significantly more than the poorest, the income disparity gap has actually narrowed since 2016. This indicates a measurable reduction in structural inequality, as the ratio of rich to poor incomes decreased substantially over recent years. Experts confirm that lower-income groups have seen notable gains in purchasing power, challenging narratives of worsening poverty. Although the absolute gap remains vast, the relative improvement suggests economic policies or market shifts are beginning to benefit disadvantaged populations more effectively than before. This information is vital for open data advocates because it demonstrates how transparent, standardized statistical releases allow independent analysts to verify social progress. By making detailed household survey data publicly accessible, governments enable rigorous, evidence-based monitoring of equality trends, fostering accountability and informed public debate on economic justice.
