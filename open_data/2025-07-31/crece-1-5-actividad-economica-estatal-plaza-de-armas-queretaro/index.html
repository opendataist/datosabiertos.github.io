Crece 1.5% actividad económica estatal - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

Crece 1.5% actividad económica estatal - Plaza de Armas | Querétaro

Querétaro experienced modest economic growth driven by the primary and secondary sectors, while the tertiary sector stagnated. This uneven performance placed the state in the middle tier nationally, highlighting sectoral disparities that shape regional development strategies. The data reveal that reliance on specific industries is crucial for overall stability, as the services sector failed to contribute to economic expansion. These findings are vital for open-data advocates, as they demonstrate how granular, sector-specific indicators, such as the ITAEE, provide essential context beyond aggregate GDP figures. Transparent access to such detailed metrics enables researchers and policymakers to pinpoint precisely where economic momentum is occurring or lacking. Ultimately, this article underscores the importance of high-resolution economic data in fostering informed decision-making. By making these preliminary indicators openly available, institutions empower stakeholders to analyze real-time regional trends, ensuring that development plans are grounded in accurate, accessible, and nuanced information rather than broad generalizations.
