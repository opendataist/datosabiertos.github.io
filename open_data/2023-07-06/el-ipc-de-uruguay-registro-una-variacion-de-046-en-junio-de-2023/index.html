El IPC de Uruguay registró una variación de -0,46% en junio de 2023

Uruguay’s June 2023 inflation data shows a monthly decline, largely driven by significant price drops in fresh produce and transportation. This fluctuation underscores how volatile agricultural markets and energy costs directly influence consumer price indices. The sharp fall in fruit and vegetable prices, coupled with lower fuel costs, offsets modest increases in housing and dairy products. These opposing trends illustrate the complex, sector-specific dynamics that shape overall inflation rates, demonstrating that aggregate figures can mask underlying economic pressures. This dataset is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides granular insights into price movements across essential goods. By analyzing such detailed breakdowns, researchers and policymakers can better understand the real impact of inflation on household budgets and food security.
