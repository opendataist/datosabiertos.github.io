La agricultura de regadío cayó en 2022 hasta el 22,4 % de la tierra cultivada en España - EFEAgro

The decline in irrigated land underscores the urgent need for transparent water resource data to understand the impacts of drought on agriculture. This shift toward more efficient irrigation technologies highlights the importance of open data in monitoring sustainable water usage trends across regions. Analyzing crop distribution reveals changing agricultural priorities driven by climate constraints. Understanding these patterns requires accessible datasets that link land use with environmental conditions, enabling better policy decisions for food security. Regional disparities in the prevalence of irrigation emphasize the need for localized data to support effective resource management. Open data initiatives are crucial for democratizing access to this information, fostering accountability and informed dialogue on Spain’s evolving agricultural landscape.
