La producción industrial crece un 1,9% en C-LM, la tercera región que menos aumento recoge

The Spanish industry shows signs of recovery following the decline in April, with the General Index of Industrial Production registering year-on-year growth in May. This rebound indicates a stabilization of the sector, highlighted by increases in capital goods and vehicles, despite sharp declines in energy and non-metallic materials. This fluctuation reflects the cyclical volatility of manufacturing activity and its dependence on seasonal and calendar-related factors. Adjusting for these effects reveals a slight contraction, suggesting that the actual recovery is more modest than the raw figures indicate, although monthly momentum is improving. For the open data community, this case illustrates the critical importance of distinguishing between raw and adjusted data when analyzing economic trends. Publishing both perspectives enables users to assess the true health of the sector, avoiding misinterpretations arising from temporal or seasonal variations that do not reflect structural changes.
