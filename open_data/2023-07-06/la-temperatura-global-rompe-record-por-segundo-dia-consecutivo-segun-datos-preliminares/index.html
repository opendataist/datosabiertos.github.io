La temperatura global rompe récord por segundo día consecutivo, según datos preliminares

Recent meteorological data confirms that global temperatures have shattered historical records, with consecutive days exceeding previous extremes. This unprecedented heat underscores the profound and accelerating impact of fossil fuel consumption on the Earth's climate system, signaling a dangerous departure from natural variability. The relevance to open data lies in the critical role that transparent, accessible, and real-time meteorological datasets play in validating these climate crises. Organizations such as NOAA and Copernicus rely on open observation networks to monitor these shifts, enabling public accountability and informed decision-making regarding environmental policy and adaptation strategies.
