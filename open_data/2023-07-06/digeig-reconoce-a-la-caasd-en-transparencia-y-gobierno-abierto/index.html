DIGEIG reconoce a la CAASD en transparencia y gobierno abierto

The CAASD achieved exceptional transparency ratings under open government standards, highlighting its commitment to accessible public information. This high performance underscores the importance of reliable institutional portals in fostering citizen trust and regulatory compliance within the public sector. The recognition emphasizes how robust open data practices enable the effective use and redistribution of information by the public. Such transparency facilitates better oversight and encourages the reuse of government data for civic benefit, aligning with broader digital governance goals. This achievement is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how systematic disclosure and integrity frameworks, supported by international standards, create a model for ethical public administration. It validates the link between transparent data infrastructure and high-quality, accountable service delivery for citizens.
