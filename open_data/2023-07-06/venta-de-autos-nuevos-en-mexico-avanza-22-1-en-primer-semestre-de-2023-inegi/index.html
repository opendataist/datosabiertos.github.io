Venta de autos nuevos en México avanza 22.1% en primer semestre de 2023: Inegi

Venta de autos nuevos en México avanza 22.1% en primer semestre de 2023: Inegi

Mexico’s new vehicle sales show a robust recovery, with the first half of the year marking a significant 22 percent rise. This growth solidifies a fourteen-month streak of positive year-over-year gains, demonstrating strong resilience against inflation and high interest rates while restoring supply chains. The market has effectively bounced back from the pandemic-induced lows of 2020, with June reaching its highest sales volume in five years. This trend highlights sustained demand and the successful reactivation of distribution networks across various car brands in the country. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it illustrates the importance of transparent, real-time economic indicators. Accessible statistics empower researchers and policymakers to analyze market resilience, track recovery trajectories, and make informed decisions regarding economic stability and consumer trends.
