Oaxaca de Juárez, ejemplo nacional en manejo de residuos: Martínez Neri

The Oaxaca municipal government demonstrated transparency by proactively addressing waste management challenges through an open governance model. This approach empowered citizens to adopt sustainable practices such as source separation and composting, turning a crisis into an opportunity for community growth. By implementing cost-effective technical strategies, the administration avoided expensive machinery while promoting the industrial reuse of waste. This effort fostered responsible citizenship and solidarity, establishing a replicable framework for sustainable urban resource management. This case is relevant to open data, as it highlights how accessible, transparent information enables communities to understand and participate in critical infrastructure decisions. Sharing these methodologies supports broader open data initiatives aimed at improving public service delivery and environmental sustainability through civic engagement.
