INE presenta informe sobre subutilización e informalidad laboral durante el año 2024

INE presenta informe sobre subutilización e informalidad laboral durante el año 2024

The INE’s 2024 report highlights Uruguay’s persistent labor challenges, specifically rising informality and unemployment. The study reveals a troubling upward trend in informal work, particularly affecting self-employed individuals and specific industrial sectors. This increase underscores the vulnerability of workers lacking formal registration and the urgent need for policies to strengthen labor rights and reduce precarious employment conditions across the economy. Disparities in labor market access remain significant, with structural inequalities favoring urban centers over rural areas and men over women. Furthermore, young people and those with lower educational attainment face substantial barriers to stable employment. These findings illustrate how demographic and geographic factors intersect to create uneven opportunities, emphasizing the critical role of education and regional development in mitigating labor market exclusions. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of transparent, granular statistical analysis in informing public policy. By making detailed metrics on informality and underutilization publicly available, the INE enables researchers, civil society, and policymakers to monitor labor trends objectively. Such accessible data fosters accountability and supports evidence-based strategies aimed at formalizing work and improving social equity, which are essential goals for open government and societal development.
