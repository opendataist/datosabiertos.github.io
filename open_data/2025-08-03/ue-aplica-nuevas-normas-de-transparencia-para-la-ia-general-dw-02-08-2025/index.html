UE aplica nuevas normas de transparencia para la IA general – DW – 02/08/2025

UE aplica nuevas normas de transparencia para la IA general – DW – 02/08/2025

The European Union has enacted pioneering regulations requiring major generative AI systems to respect copyright and prevent discrimination. These rules enforce transparency and protect fundamental rights, mandating that high-risk models avoid training on protected data and assess potential harms to users. This legal framework establishes a global standard for responsible AI development and deployment. This initiative directly impacts open data by creating a clear boundary between copyrighted material and usable information. Companies must now rigorously evaluate data sources, ensuring that training datasets do not infringe on intellectual property rights. Consequently, the quality and legality of open data used for AI development are strictly regulated, forcing transparency in data provenance. The relevance to open data lies in the shift toward accountable AI ecosystems. By compelling tech giants to adhere to strict guidelines, the EU promotes ethical data practices. This encourages the development of AI models that respect legal frameworks while fostering trust in digital technologies, ultimately shaping how open data is utilized in the era of advanced artificial intelligence.
