¿Por qué mujeres ganan menos que los hombres?

¿Por qué mujeres ganan menos que los hombres?

The article highlights Mexico’s persistent gender wage gap, noting that women earn significantly less than men on average. This disparity stems primarily from the unequal burden of unpaid care work, which disproportionately restricts women’s career advancement and leads to systemic discrimination in hiring and promotions for mothers. Addressing this issue requires both public and private action. Implementing a National Care System and ensuring workplace flexibility are crucial steps to enable women’s economic autonomy. Furthermore, labor transparency and equal pay policies must be enforced to eliminate discrimination for roles with equivalent responsibilities. This analysis is vital for open data because it underscores how publicly accessible, disaggregated statistics are essential to expose structural inequalities. Transparent data allows policymakers to measure progress, hold institutions accountable, and design evidence-based interventions that promote gender equity and sustainable economic growth for the entire population.
