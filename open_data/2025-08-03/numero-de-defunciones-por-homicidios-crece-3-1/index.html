Número de defunciones por homicidios crece 3.1 %

Mexico’s preliminary homicide data reveal an upward trend in violence, signaling a worsening security crisis. This rising trajectory underscores systemic failures and growing risks for citizens, calling for urgent policy interventions to safeguard public safety and restore stability. Gun violence remains the predominant method of killing, highlighting the dangerous prevalence of firearms. This specific modality reflects deep-rooted issues related to weapon accessibility and the power of criminal organizations, which are critical factors in understanding the nature of contemporary violence. This report is essential for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the need for transparent and accessible statistics. Reliable government data enables researchers to analyze trends, hold institutions accountable, and develop evidence-based strategies to combat crime and enhance societal well-being.
