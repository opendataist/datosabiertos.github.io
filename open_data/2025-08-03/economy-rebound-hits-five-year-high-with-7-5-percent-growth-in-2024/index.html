Economy rebound hits five-year high with 7.5 percent growth in 2024

Economy rebound hits five-year high with 7.5 percent growth in 2024

Bhutan’s economy is experiencing a resurgence in key sectors such as tourism, infrastructure, and technology, driven by its unique Gross National Happiness framework. This holistic approach prioritizes sustainable development over mere financial growth, leading to increased investment in heritage conservation, renewable energy, and cultural preservation. The rebound indicates a successful integration of environmental stewardship with economic vitality, challenging traditional Western metrics of progress. Conversely, the primary sector, particularly agriculture, faces significant declines, highlighting vulnerabilities in food security and rural livelihoods. While the education and health sectors show promise, the drop in agricultural output suggests a need for deeper structural reforms to ensure balanced growth. This disparity underscores the complexity of transitioning a nation toward sustainable modernization while protecting traditional ways of life. This case is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how non-traditional indicators, like well-being and environmental health, can be quantified and tracked alongside standard economic data. By providing a transparent, multi-dimensional view of national progress, Bhutan offers a model for how open datasets can support policy decisions that value human and ecological well-being. This approach encourages the global community to explore diverse data metrics that reflect comprehensive societal health rather than just economic output.
