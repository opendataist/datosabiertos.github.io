Avanza la creación del Primer Plan de Gobierno Abierto de Mar del Plata | Punto Noticias Mar del Plata.

Mar del Plata has joined the Open Government Partnership, launching a public consultation to shape its Action Plan. This initiative emphasizes citizen participation, transparency, and accountability in public management through collaborative dialogue. The process aims to develop concrete measures that address community needs and strengthen democratic oversight. By integrating diverse social inputs, the municipality seeks to build a responsive and inclusive governance model. This effort is highly relevant to open data, as it fosters institutional transparency and civic engagement. It demonstrates how open government principles empower citizens to influence policy decisions, ensuring that public resources are managed with greater clarity and democratic legitimacy.
