La Nación / Acceso a la información es clave para fortalecer el Estado de Derecho y la democracia

This event underscores the critical role of transparent governance and data protection as foundational pillars of democracy and the rule of law. By hosting an expert on access to public information, Paraguayan authorities demonstrated a strategic commitment to strengthening institutional integrity and fostering trust through openness. The conference highlighted the necessity of interpreting transparency through a civic lens, emphasizing that citizens must never be denied the right to request information. It stressed the importance of distinguishing between public and restricted data, ensuring that while security exceptions exist, the default stance remains one of accessibility and good-faith administrative interaction. The relevance to open data lies in the call to modernize management through digital technology, which serves as a key ally in guaranteeing access to information. By integrating legal frameworks with digital infrastructure, governments can enhance the dissemination of public information, thereby advancing the principles of open data and ensuring that digital transformation directly supports citizen rights and administrative transparency.
