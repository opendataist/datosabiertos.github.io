C-LM tiene el tiempo de espera más alto para una operación y el menor para consulta de especialista, según Sanidad

The article reveals a significant deterioration in Spain's public healthcare system, marked by a substantial increase in the number of patients on waiting lists for both surgical procedures and specialist consultations compared to the previous year. This growth indicates systemic strain, with many patients enduring delays exceeding six months, particularly for surgeries, signaling that the sector has not fully regained its pre-pandemic efficiency levels. Geographically, there is a stark disparity between regions, with some autonomous communities experiencing significantly longer wait times and higher patient volumes per capita than others. This uneven distribution highlights structural inefficiencies and challenges in resource allocation that vary widely across the country, affecting patient access and care quality differently depending on their location. This report is highly relevant to the open data community, as it underscores the critical need for accessible, standardized, and timely health statistics. By making such granular data transparent, stakeholders can identify regional disparities, hold authorities accountable, and drive evidence-based policy reforms to improve healthcare management and resource distribution nationwide.
