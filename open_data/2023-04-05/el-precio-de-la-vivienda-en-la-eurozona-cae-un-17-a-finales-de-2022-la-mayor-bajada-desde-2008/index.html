El precio de la vivienda en la eurozona cae un 1,7% a finales de 2022, la mayor bajada desde 2008

The article highlights a significant deceleration in European housing prices, marking the sharpest quarterly drop since 2008. While annual growth remains positive across many regions, the pace has slowed considerably, indicating a sector-wide cooling trend despite lingering regional disparities. Specific nations experienced substantial quarterly declines, contrasting with others that still saw annual increases. This divergence underscores the complex, uneven recovery of the real estate market, where some countries face immediate adjustments while others continue to experience modest appreciation over longer periods. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the critical need for standardized, comparable statistical indicators across the EU. Reliable, harmonized datasets allow researchers to accurately monitor economic shifts, enabling better policy analysis and facilitating transparent comparisons of housing market dynamics between member states.
