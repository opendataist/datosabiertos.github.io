Inflación en Uruguay se desacelera en marzo a 7,33% interanual

Inflación en Uruguay se desacelera en marzo a 7,33% interanual

Uruguay continues to experience a steady moderation in inflation, marking its sixth consecutive month of decline. This trend suggests that previous monetary tightening measures are effectively slowing price increases, offering hope for economic stabilization. The data indicate that inflation remains above official targets despite this deceleration. However, the trajectory aligns with government projections aiming for significantly lower rates by 2024, reflecting a gradual return to price stability. This case is relevant to open data as it highlights how public statistical agencies provide transparent, high-frequency indicators. Such accessible metrics are essential for researchers, policymakers, and citizens to monitor economic health, assess policy effectiveness, and foster informed public debate on financial governance.
