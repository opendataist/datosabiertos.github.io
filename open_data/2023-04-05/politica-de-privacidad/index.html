Política de Privacidad -

This privacy policy outlines how constructorio.es handles personal data in compliance with Spanish data protection laws, emphasizing that sharing specific information is essential for users to receive the services and updates they request. By accepting these terms, users consent to their data being shared with third-party professionals and collaborators solely to facilitate the services they seek. The text holds users responsible for obtaining consent from any third parties whose data they submit, thereby protecting the platform owner from legal repercussions arising from non-compliance. Users retain full rights to access, rectify, cancel, or object to the processing of their data through a dedicated email channel, with valid identification required to verify their requests. This document is relevant to open data as it illustrates the critical boundary between facilitating public services and ensuring strict personal data protection. It highlights the legal complexities surrounding data sharing with third parties, serving as a case study for how platforms must balance transparency and service delivery with rigorous privacy obligations and user consent mechanisms.
