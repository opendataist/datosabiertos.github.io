Kenya wins Bid to host the International Open Data Conference

The 6th International Open Data Conference marks a significant geopolitical shift by bringing the premier global gathering of open data champions to Africa for the first time. Held in Nairobi in 2020, this event highlights a growing recognition that data-driven development is not just a Western priority but a critical component for the African continent’s economic and political progress. By selecting Kenya as the host, the conference underscores the importance of regional leadership in shaping the future of global data policies. Kenya’s successful bid reflects sustained high-level political commitment to fostering an inclusive data ecosystem involving government, private sector, and civil society. This leadership has already resulted in tangible international collaborations, such as agreements for free satellite data access and partnerships with global data organizations. These efforts demonstrate a strategic move toward rational economic choice, positioning open data as an essential tool for tackling development challenges and improving decision-making across various sectors. Relevance to open data lies in the conference’s focus on bridging diverse communities and strengthening South-South collaboration. With thousands of participants and dozens of tech companies expected, the event serves as a catalyst for investment and innovation within Africa’s burgeoning tech spaces. It signals Africa’s readiness to lead in harnessing data to deliver economic benefits and ensure inclusive growth, aligning with global sustainability goals and leaving no one behind in the digital age.
