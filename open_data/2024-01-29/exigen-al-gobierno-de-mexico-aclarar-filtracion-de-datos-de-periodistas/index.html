Exigen al Gobierno de México aclarar filtración de datos de periodistas

Exigen al Gobierno de México aclarar filtración de datos de periodistas

A Mexican autonomous body has demanded clarification from the government regarding the leak of personal data belonging to hundreds of accredited journalists. The oversight agency issued a strict ultimatum, requiring an explanation within seventy-two hours or facing an official investigation into potential privacy violations. This incident highlights critical vulnerabilities in how state institutions handle sensitive information about critical media personnel. The breach includes names, photos, and addresses, raising serious concerns about digital security protocols and the potential for intentional data exposure against government critics. The article is relevant to open data because it underscores the urgent need for robust transparency and accountability frameworks when managing public information. It illustrates the delicate balance between government transparency obligations and the mandatory protection of individual privacy rights, warning that mishandling such data can erode public trust and facilitate political targeting.
