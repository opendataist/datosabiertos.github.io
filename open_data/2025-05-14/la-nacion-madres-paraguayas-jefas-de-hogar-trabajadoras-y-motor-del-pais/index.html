La Nación / Madres paraguayas: jefas de hogar, trabajadoras y motor del país

The article highlights how open data from Paraguay’s national statistics institute provides a comprehensive view of mothers’ lives, including their urban-rural distribution, educational levels, and labor participation. This granular transparency allows for a deeper understanding of household structures and the significant proportion of homes led by women. Such publicly available statistics are crucial for analyzing social trends, revealing that half of the mothers are young adults and many work in service sectors. These insights demonstrate the value of accessible datasets in mapping the realities of a majority demographic within the population. Ultimately, this data serves as a vital foundation for crafting effective public policies aimed at improving maternal welfare. It exemplifies the role of open data in transforming raw household survey results into actionable intelligence that supports social development and informed governance decisions.
