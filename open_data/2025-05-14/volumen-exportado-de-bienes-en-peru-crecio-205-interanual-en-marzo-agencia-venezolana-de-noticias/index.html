Volumen exportado de bienes en Perú creció 20,5% interanual en marzo - Agencia Venezolana de Noticias

Volumen exportado de bienes en Perú creció 20,5% interanual en marzo - Agencia Venezolana de Noticias

Peru’s export volume surged in March, driven by significant increases in both traditional and non-traditional sectors. Mining, fisheries, and energy led traditional growth, while agro-industrial and manufacturing sectors boosted value-added exports. China remains the dominant destination for these goods, highlighting strong trade ties. This performance underscores Peru’s economic resilience and diversification in international markets. This data is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible national statistics enable analysts to track global trade trends. Transparent reporting supports evidence-based decisions for businesses and policymakers monitoring economic indicators.
