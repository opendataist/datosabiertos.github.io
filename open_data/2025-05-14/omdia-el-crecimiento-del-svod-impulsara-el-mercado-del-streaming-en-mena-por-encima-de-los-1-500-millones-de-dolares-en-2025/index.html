Omdia: El crecimiento del SVOD impulsará el mercado del streaming en MENA por encima de los 1.500 millones de dólares en 2025

The streaming market in the Middle East and North Africa is undergoing a significant structural shift, characterized by the growing dominance of flexible, multiformat platforms like YouTube Premium alongside traditional SVOD leaders. This trend highlights a consumer preference for fluid, ad-free experiences, challenging the established order and demonstrating how varied content strategies can capture substantial market share in a rapidly evolving digital ecosystem. Strategic product consolidation is emerging as a key driver of financial success, exemplified by StarzPlay’s successful merger of services to boost revenue without losing subscribers. This approach underscores the importance of simplifying user offerings and adding value, proving that intelligent monetization strategies can directly enhance profitability and customer retention in a competitive landscape. This region presents a massive opportunity for growth, driven by digital infrastructure expansion and a young, engaged population. For open data enthusiasts, this case is vital as it illustrates how aggregated subscription and usage metrics reveal consumer behavior trends. Analyzing such data enables stakeholders to predict market shifts, optimize content offerings, and understand the broader implications of digital consumption patterns across emerging economies.
