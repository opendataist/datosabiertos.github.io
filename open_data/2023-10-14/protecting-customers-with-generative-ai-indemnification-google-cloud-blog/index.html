Protecting customers with generative AI indemnification | Google Cloud Blog

Google has introduced a comprehensive indemnity framework to address customer concerns regarding intellectual property risks in generative AI. By explicitly covering liabilities stemming from both the training data used to build models and the content generated by users, the company aims to provide balanced protection. This two-layered approach ensures that customers are shielded from third-party claims, regardless of whether the alleged infringement originates from Google’s internal processes or user-created outputs. The effectiveness of these protections relies heavily on responsible usage practices. The generated output indemnity specifically requires that customers adhere to ethical guidelines, such as using tools to cite sources and avoiding intentional infringement. This conditional coverage encourages the adoption of safe AI workflows, signaling that while Google assumes significant legal risk, customers must also demonstrate good faith and diligence in how they deploy these powerful technologies for business purposes. This initiative is highly relevant to open data and open source communities because it directly tackles the legal uncertainties surrounding data usage in AI development. By clarifying indemnification for training data, Google reduces the potential friction between proprietary service providers and open knowledge ecosystems. It sets a precedent for how large tech firms might support innovation without stifling the open data flows that feed modern AI models, offering a clearer, safer path for integrating diverse data sources into commercial applications.
