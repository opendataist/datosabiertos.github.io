La inflación sube un 0,3% en septiembre en C-LM, donde los precios son un 3,5% más caros que hace un año

Inflation in Castilla-La Mancha and at the national level shows a sustained upward trend, driven primarily by the cost of energy and fuels, while food prices continue to register significant year-on-year growth. Although underlying inflation shows slight signs of cooling, pressure on essential expenses persists, disproportionately affecting households through grocery bills and utility services. This is relevant to open data because it underscores the need for transparent, real-time access to Consumer Price Index (CPI) data. These figures are not merely abstract statistics; they are crucial indicators for government transparency and accountability, enabling citizens and experts to verify official claims regarding inflation control and the overall economy. The availability of this detailed information facilitates independent analysis, academic research, and evidence-based public policy formulation. Without open, disaggregated data by category and region, it is difficult to accurately assess the real impact of inflation across different socioeconomic sectors, thereby limiting society’s capacity to understand and respond to current economic dynamics.
