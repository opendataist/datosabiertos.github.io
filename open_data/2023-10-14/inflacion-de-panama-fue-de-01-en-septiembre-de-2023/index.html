Inflación de Panamá fue de 0,1% en septiembre de 2023

The article details the urban consumer price index for Panama, highlighting a modest monthly increase and a stable year-on-year inflation rate of 2.3%. This stability suggests that while monthly fluctuations occur, the broader economic trend remains under control, with specific sectors such as housing and transport driving gradual upward pressure on consumer costs. A significant shift is observed in the composition of price changes: insurance and healthcare services experienced notable increases, contrasting with declines in household goods and clothing. This divergence indicates changing economic pressures on households, as essential services become more expensive while discretionary or durable goods see price corrections, thereby altering standard-of-living metrics. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it exemplifies the importance of transparent and accessible economic indicators for public scrutiny and policy analysis. By releasing detailed, disaggregated data on the drivers of inflation, institutions enable researchers and citizens to verify official statistics, fostering trust in government data and supporting evidence-based decision-making in economic planning and social welfare programs.
