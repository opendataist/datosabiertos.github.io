La Ley de Transparencia llega este lunes al Parlamento de Navarra

La Ley de Transparencia llega este lunes al Parlamento de Navarra

The Navarre Parliament is advancing legislative updates to its transparency framework, most notably by creating a formal registry for lobbying groups. This move strengthens the regulatory oversight of interest groups, ensuring greater visibility in political decision-making processes. Beyond regulatory changes, the agenda addresses urgent social and infrastructure concerns, including electric sector planning and health indemnification systems. These discussions reflect the body’s role in balancing economic development with public welfare and administrative accountability during regular parliamentary sessions. This development is relevant to open data because a mandatory public registry of lobbyists provides structured, searchable information on influence activities. Such transparency facilitates public scrutiny and enables data-driven research into political influence, supporting informed civic engagement and accountability.
