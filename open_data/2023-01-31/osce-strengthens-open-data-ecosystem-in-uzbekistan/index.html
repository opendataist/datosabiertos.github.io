OSCE strengthens open data ecosystem in Uzbekistan

The recent Open Data Challenge in Uzbekistan highlighted how hackathons effectively foster citizen engagement with public information. By encouraging teams to build technological solutions using government data, the event demonstrated the practical value of transparency in driving economic development and good governance. This approach serves as a crucial mechanism for establishing public oversight of state activities. These initiatives directly support anti-corruption efforts by enabling the creation of tools that monitor unregistered buildings and assist vulnerable populations. The ability to visualize and utilize open datasets empowers authorities and professionals to enhance their expertise while building a robust professional community. Consequently, this strengthens the open data ecosystem, proving that accessible information is a prerequisite for sustainable security and social progress. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the global push toward making government information accessible to drive innovation. It shows that open data is not merely a technical asset but a fundamental tool for accountability and societal improvement. The event underscores the importance of training and competitions in bridging the gap between raw data and practical, citizen-centric applications.
