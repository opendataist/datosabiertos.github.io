INE califica de hito cierre de cartografía al 100% en Beni y Pando; en el país llega al 60%

The National Institute of Statistics highlights the completion of 100% digital cartographic updates in the Beni and Pando departments as a historic milestone. This achievement marks the first time since 1825 that these regions are fully mapped, ensuring comprehensive coverage of even the most remote areas. It signifies a critical leap in Bolivia’s infrastructure for modernizing national data collection. This progress elevates the national cartographic update to 60%, with several departmental capitals already fully digitized. These foundational geographic datasets are essential for accurate census enumeration, resource allocation, and policy planning. The systematic approach ensures that no population segment is overlooked, establishing a robust baseline for future statistical operations. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical importance of high-quality, standardized geographic baselines. Reliable mapping enables precise geospatial analysis and facilitates the publication of interoperable, location-based open datasets. Ultimately, such transparency empowers citizens and researchers to access granular demographic information, fostering accountability and informed decision-making in public administration.
