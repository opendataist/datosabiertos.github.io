VOX Melilla propone la creación de más políticas económicas para paliar el desempleo en Melilla - MelillaHoy

Melilla experienced the highest job destruction rate in Spain in 2022, alongside severe unemployment and a persistent youth exodus. This stark reality underscores a critical failure in local economic policy, which currently relies heavily on frontier commerce and temporary employment programs rather than sustainable development. The core implication is that the region urgently needs structural reforms aimed at fostering local entrepreneurship and improving the quality of vocational training. Without shifting toward an independent economic model, the city will remain unable to retain its workforce or stimulate genuine internal growth amid rising inflation and economic instability. This case is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how transparent, independent statistical sources—such as the INE’s Active Population Survey—expose deep socioeconomic crises. Such data empowers political actors and citizens to hold local executives accountable, moving beyond rhetoric to demand evidence-based policies that address the specific drivers of labor market collapse and demographic decline.
