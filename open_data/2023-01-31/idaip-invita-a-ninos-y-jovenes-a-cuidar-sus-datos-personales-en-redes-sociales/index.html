Idaip invita a niños y jóvenes a cuidar sus datos personales en redes sociales

The article underscores the critical vulnerability of minors’ personal data in the digital sphere, warning that constant internet use exposes young people to severe privacy risks. It highlights how the vast majority of youth engage with social platforms without fully understanding the dangers, such as phishing attempts, fake accounts, and sexual extortion tactics like grooming. These threats aim to exploit trust and steal sensitive information, jeopardizing the safety and integrity of families. This guidance is particularly relevant to open data, as it underscores the necessity of robust data protection frameworks that balance transparency with individual privacy rights. As data collection expands, ensuring that open initiatives do not inadvertently expose vulnerable populations is essential. The text advocates for heightened awareness and protective measures, illustrating that the responsible handling of public and private information requires vigilance against digital exploitation. Ultimately, the piece calls for collaborative efforts among parents, educators, and authorities to safeguard digital citizenship. By fostering a culture of security and ethical data interaction, society can mitigate the harms associated with unregulated online environments. This perspective reinforces the importance of integrating privacy-by-design principles into broader open data ecosystems to protect user identity and maintain public trust in digital infrastructure.
