Proton lanza Lumo: una IA centrada en la privacidad y el control del usuario

Lumo, Proton’s new AI assistant, represents a radical shift in the use of artificial intelligence by prioritizing privacy as a core principle. Unlike dominant models that monetize user data, Lumo is built on a “privacy by default” architecture that eliminates log storage and prohibits the use of personal information for model training or advertising purposes. This ensures that conversations remain visible only to the user, guaranteeing that the tool works for the individual rather than against them. The relevance of this initiative to the open data movement lies in its commitment to transparency and digital sovereignty. By operating with open-source models and data centers in Europe, Lumo promotes a technology ecosystem free from external surveillance and opaque algorithms. This approach not only protects the integrity of sensitive information but also establishes an ethical standard where security is not a premium option but an intrinsic feature of the design, fostering trust in technologies that respect digital rights. Ultimately, Lumo demonstrates that the utility of AI is not inherently tied to the exploitation of personal data. Its existence validates the possibility of developing high-capability tools that coexist with strict respect for user privacy. For the open data community, this underscores the urgency of technological alternatives that prioritize transparency, security, and user autonomy, offering a sustainable model funded by the community rather than commercial surveillance.
