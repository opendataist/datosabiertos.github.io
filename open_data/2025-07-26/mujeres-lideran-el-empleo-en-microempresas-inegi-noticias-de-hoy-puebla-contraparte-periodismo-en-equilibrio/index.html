Mujeres lideran el empleo en microempresas: Inegi - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Mujeres lideran el empleo en microempresas: Inegi - Noticias de Hoy Puebla | Contraparte | Periodismo en Equilibrio |

Women dominate the labor force in Puebla’s microenterprises, a proportion that significantly declines in medium, large, and very large companies. This disparity reveals a persistent gender-based segmentation, where female participation diminishes as firm size increases, highlighting structural barriers to career advancement and equitable representation in larger economic entities. The data shows that women are concentrated in service-oriented sectors such as education, health, and hospitality, while men prevail in technical, industrial, and logistical fields. This clear division of labor reinforces traditional roles, suggesting that economic growth does not automatically translate into gender balance across different types of businesses. This information is relevant to open data because transparent statistics are essential for diagnosing these inequalities. By making such granular data accessible, policymakers and researchers can identify specific gaps and design targeted interventions to foster a more inclusive and balanced workforce in Puebla.
