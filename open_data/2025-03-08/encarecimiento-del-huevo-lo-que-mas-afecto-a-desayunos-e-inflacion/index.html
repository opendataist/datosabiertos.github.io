Encarecimiento del huevo, lo que más afectó a desayunos e inflación

Encarecimiento del huevo, lo que más afectó a desayunos e inflación

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) reported that national inflation closed December at 3.77% year-on-year. Key price increases occurred in eggs, food services, beef, and housing, while vegetables such as tomatoes and onions helped to moderate costs. These statistics are relevant to open data because they demonstrate the value of transparent, standardized official statistics. Such data enables researchers and citizens to analyze economic trends, assess the impact of policies, and hold institutions accountable through accessible information. This case highlights how public datasets facilitate an understanding of everyday economic realities. By making detailed price indices publicly available, governments empower users to derive actionable insights, fostering trust and promoting informed public debate on monetary policy and living costs.
