5 ajustes esenciales para proteger tu privacidad en Gemini

5 ajustes esenciales para proteger tu privacidad en Gemini

Google’s integration of Gemini as the default assistant on Android raises significant privacy concerns regarding the handling of personal data. This shift underscores the critical importance of user vigilance in managing interactions with artificial intelligence, as powerful AI tools often require access to extensive personal information to function effectively. The article emphasizes the need to adjust privacy settings to prevent data leakage, such as disabling extensions for Workspace and device control. These measures reduce the risk of unauthorized access to documents and smart home systems, ensuring that sensitive information remains secure from potential security vulnerabilities or unintended data sharing. This content is vital to the discourse on open data because it illustrates the tension between AI utility and data sovereignty. It demonstrates how individual actions can mitigate risks in an increasingly data-driven ecosystem, reinforcing the need for transparent data practices and user control over personal information in the age of pervasive AI assistants.
