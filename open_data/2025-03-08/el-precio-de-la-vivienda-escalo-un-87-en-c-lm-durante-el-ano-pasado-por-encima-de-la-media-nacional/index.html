El precio de la vivienda escaló un 8,7% en C-LM durante el año pasado, por encima de la media nacional

The article highlights a significant acceleration in Spanish housing prices during 2024, marking the steepest annual increases since 2007. Both the new and secondary markets experienced substantial growth across all autonomous communities, indicating a nationwide trend rather than isolated regional fluctuations. This continuous upward trajectory underscores robust demand and a persistent shift in real estate market dynamics over the last decade. A critical aspect of this trend is the accelerated rate of growth in the final quarter, where price increases reached their highest levels in over fifteen years. This surge suggests that market pressures intensified as the year concluded, with secondary housing prices rising more sharply than new builds in some metrics. The data reveals a market that has not only recovered but has expanded beyond previous peaks, reflecting a sustained period of appreciation that challenges affordability and market stability. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it exemplifies the importance of transparent, granular statistical monitoring. Reliable access to detailed price indices enables accurate economic analysis, policy formulation, and market transparency. By making such data openly available, stakeholders can better understand inflationary pressures in essential sectors like housing, enabling more informed decisions by citizens, researchers, and policymakers to address socioeconomic disparities.
