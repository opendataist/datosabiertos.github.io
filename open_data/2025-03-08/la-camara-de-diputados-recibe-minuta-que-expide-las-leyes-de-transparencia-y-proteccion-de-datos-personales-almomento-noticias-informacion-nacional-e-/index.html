La Cámara de Diputados recibe minuta que expide las leyes de Transparencia y Protección de Datos Personales - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

La Cámara de Diputados recibe minuta que expide las leyes de Transparencia y Protección de Datos Personales - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

The Mexican legislature has advanced a comprehensive legislative package aimed at strengthening transparency and data protection rights. By processing the Senate’s proposal for new general laws on transparency and personal data protection, the Chamber of Deputies is moving to consolidate a robust legal framework. This initiative seeks to guarantee citizens' access to public information held by government bodies and ensure the privacy of personal data handled by both state entities and private actors. This development is crucial for open data because it establishes clear legal standards for the handling, sharing, and protection of information. By regulating how public authorities manage data and assigning specific oversight roles to anticorruption agencies, the reforms promote accountability and institutional trust. The laws aim to balance the public's right to know with the necessity of protecting individual privacy, ensuring that data management is legitimate, controlled, and transparent across all levels of government. Ultimately, this legislative progress represents a significant step toward modernizing Mexico’s governance infrastructure. It aligns national practices with constitutional mandates for transparency and data sovereignty, fostering a more open society. For the open data community, these laws provide the necessary legal certainty to advocate for greater data accessibility while ensuring that ethical standards and privacy safeguards are strictly maintained in public administration.
