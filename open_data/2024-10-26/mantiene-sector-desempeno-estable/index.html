Mantiene sector desempeño estable

Mantiene sector desempeño estable

Nuevo León has consolidated its national leadership in construction, driven primarily by a notable recovery in public investment in mobility infrastructure. This trend contrasts with a slight contraction in the private sector, highlighting the growing dependence on the state to sustain the sector's dynamism. The analysis reveals that the reduction of large projects in other regions has allowed Nuevo León to assume a central role in national activity, capitalizing on this competitive gap. The relevance of this article to open data lies in the transparency and availability of economic data disaggregated by sector, entity, and period. The National Institute of Statistics and Geography demonstrates how access to detailed statistical information enables analysts to identify structural changes in regional economies. This accessibility facilitates precise tracking of the impact of public policies and resource allocation, which are essential for evaluating economic health and government effectiveness in real time. Understanding these dynamics requires high-quality open data that links policy decisions with measurable outcomes. The publication of indicators such as production and investment by segment allows citizens and researchers to verify the accuracy of official statements. Therefore, promoting the openness of statistical data not only improves accountability but also strengthens evidence-based decision-making, ensuring that economic development aligns with the real needs of society.
