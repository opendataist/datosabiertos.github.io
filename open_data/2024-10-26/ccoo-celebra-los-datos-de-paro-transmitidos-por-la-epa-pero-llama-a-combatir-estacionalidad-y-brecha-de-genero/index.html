CCOO celebra los datos de paro transmitidos por la EPA pero llama a combatir estacionalidad y brecha de género

The labor reform has improved employment quality, yet significant stability gaps remain in Castilla-La Mancha compared to national averages. Persistent high levels of temporary employment and gender disparities underscore the urgent need for permanent contracts and greater equity in the workforce. This case highlights the critical importance of open data in policy evaluation. Transparent statistics reveal hidden inequalities, enabling trade unions and governments to identify structural issues and advocate for targeted reforms that address specific demographic and employment challenges. By making labor metrics accessible, stakeholders can effectively monitor the impact of reforms. Open data fosters accountability and informed debate, ensuring that improvements in job creation translate into genuine stability and equality for all workers, particularly vulnerable groups.
