Más de 4.000 infracciones por exceso de velocidad en la Perón

The implementation of traffic radars in Santa Rosa significantly improved road safety by reducing accidents, demonstrating the tangible impact of data-driven enforcement. The municipality identified high-risk zones through statistical analysis, proving that targeted monitoring effectively deters speeding and traffic violations, which is crucial for understanding how open data informs public policy decisions. However, the process revealed critical issues regarding legal transparency and due process. Political opposition highlighted that electronic notifications require specific legal frameworks, questioning the administrative validity of the current enforcement methods. This conflict underscores the necessity for clear, accessible regulations governing how government agencies collect and utilize citizen data. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the tension between technological efficiency and civic rights. It emphasizes that for open data initiatives to succeed, they must be supported by robust legal standards and transparent communication. Ultimately, it shows that while data can solve safety problems, its deployment must respect established democratic procedures to maintain public trust and legal integrity.
