Immigration sustains Finland's population growth

Immigration sustains Finland's population growth

Immigration sustains Finland’s total population growth through 2070, yet the demographic structure faces significant shifts. The working-age population will eventually decline, leading to a higher dependency ratio as the elderly share expands relative to workers. Persistent low birth rates threaten long-term societal stability, reducing the future cohort of childbearing women. This trend exacerbates the aging issue, creating sustained pressure on social systems and economic productivity regardless of current migration inflows. These projections are vital for open_data initiatives, as they highlight the necessity of transparent, accessible demographic forecasting. Reliable public data enables policymakers and researchers to model the long-term societal impacts of migration and fertility trends accurately.
