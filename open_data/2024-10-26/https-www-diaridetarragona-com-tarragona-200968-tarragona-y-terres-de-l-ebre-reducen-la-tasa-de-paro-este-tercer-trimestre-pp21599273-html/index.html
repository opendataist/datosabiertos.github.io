Unemployment in Tarragona has declined slightly, although it remains higher than last year. While total employment figures show modest growth compared to the previous quarter, the labor market still lacks the robustness seen in the same period of the prior year, indicating lingering economic fragility. Gender disparities persist within the job market, with women facing significantly higher unemployment rates than men. This structural inequality highlights a critical area for policy intervention, as the gender gap in joblessness remains wide despite overall sectoral shifts in employment distribution. This data is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how public, granular statistics empower researchers to detect local economic trends and social inequities. Accessible, high-quality governmental datasets enable transparent analysis of regional labor health, fostering informed decision-making and accountability in public administration.
