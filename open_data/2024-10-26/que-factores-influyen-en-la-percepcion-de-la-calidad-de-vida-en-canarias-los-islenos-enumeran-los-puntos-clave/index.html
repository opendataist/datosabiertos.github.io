¿Qué factores influyen en la percepción de la calidad de vida en Canarias? Los isleños enumeran los puntos clave

¿Qué factores influyen en la percepción de la calidad de vida en Canarias? Los isleños enumeran los puntos clave

The article contrasts official statistical data, which ranks the Canary Islands as having the lowest quality of life in Spain, with residents' subjective perceptions. While the National Statistics Institute highlights deficits in health, environment, and employment, locals define well-being through personal balance, emphasizing health, free time, and social relationships. This divergence reveals a critical disconnect between macro-level administrative indicators and the actual lived experience of the population. Residents prioritize mental and physical health, leisure time, and strong social support networks over traditional economic metrics. However, structural challenges such as long healthcare waiting times, low wages, and housing costs severely hinder the achievement of this desired equilibrium. The narrative suggests that despite the natural advantages of the islands, the lack of economic stability and access to essential services undermines the sense of security and happiness that citizens strive for in their daily lives. This study is vital for open data initiatives because it underscores the necessity of integrating subjective, qualitative citizen feedback with objective statistical indicators. Relying solely on standardized metrics often obscures the nuanced realities of public welfare. By acknowledging local perspectives, data-driven policymaking can become more holistic and responsive, ensuring that transparency efforts reflect not just numbers, but the genuine human impact of those figures on community well-being.
