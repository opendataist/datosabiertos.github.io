Chihuahua se queda atrás de Guadalajara en transparencia - Noticias de Chihuahua - La Parada Digital

Chihuahua’s geographic data platform, SIGNUM, has received national recognition for its commitment to transparency, demonstrating the power of spatial information in public administration. This achievement highlights how accessible geospatial data promotes accountability and helps citizens better understand their municipal environment. The award underscores the critical role of open geodata in fostering effective and transparent governance.
