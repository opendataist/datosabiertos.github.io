La economía española mantiene el ritmo: el PIB crece un 0,8% en el tercer trimestre

The article highlights the robust and balanced growth of the Spanish economy, driven primarily by increased public spending and household consumption. This expansion reflects rising worker purchasing power and a positive labor market trend, demonstrating that all sectors of activity, with the notable exception of construction, are contributing positively to national output. The underlying data reveal a healthy economic cycle sustained over multiple quarters, supported by strong performance in the industrial and service sectors. The resilience shown across various demand aggregates and production branches indicates a deep-seated recovery rather than isolated fluctuations, reinforcing the narrative of structural strength and sustained momentum in the domestic market. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent, high-frequency statistical releases from national institutes enable real-time economic analysis. By making detailed metrics on GDP, employment, and productivity publicly available, policymakers and researchers can accurately monitor economic health, fostering informed decision-making and trust in statistical governance through accessible, granular evidence.
