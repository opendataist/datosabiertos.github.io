El IPC en España repunta en octubre al 1,8%

El IPC en España repunta en octubre al 1,8%

The final data reveal that Spanish consumer inflation regained momentum in October, driven primarily by rising costs in the energy and transportation sectors. This rebound, evidenced by higher prices for electricity, gas, and fuel, interrupted a four-month streak of deceleration. As a result, the annual inflation rate climbed, signaling a shift in the economic trajectory, even though inflation had stabilized at levels below the European Central Bank’s target threshold. Underlying inflation remained higher than the headline rate, indicating persistent price pressures beyond volatile food and energy categories. While certain items, such as chocolate and maritime transport, experienced significant price spikes, others, including telecommunications and personal computers, saw notable price declines. This divergence highlights the complex structural changes within the consumer basket, where seasonal shifts in clothing and fresh produce further influenced monthly fluctuations, creating a mixed picture of cost increases and decreases across different industries. This article is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it relies on definitive, granular datasets published by the National Statistics Institute (INE). Such detailed information enables researchers and citizens to analyze economic trends, verify official reports, and understand the specific drivers behind inflation. Access to these structured statistics is essential for transparent economic monitoring, allowing stakeholders to make informed decisions based on verified metrics rather than preliminary estimates.
