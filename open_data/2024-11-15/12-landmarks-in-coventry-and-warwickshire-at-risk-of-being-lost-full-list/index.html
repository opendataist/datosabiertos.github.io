12 landmarks in Coventry and Warwickshire at risk of being lost - full list

Historic England’s annual register highlights that numerous landmarks in the West Midlands, including Coventry and Warwickshire, face potential loss due to neglect. This year’s list features significant additions like Tamworth Castle and various sites in Birmingham, reflecting a broader national trend where nearly five thousand structures are vulnerable. The inclusion of specific local sites, such as a medieval cellar in Coventry and historic allotments, underscores the urgent need for preservation efforts to protect these culturally significant locations from disappearing. The widespread nature of this risk affects diverse categories, ranging from places of worship and parks to archaeological sites and conservation areas. This extensive catalog illustrates that heritage vulnerability is not isolated to single structures but permeates entire communities and landscapes. The data reveals that while some sites have been successfully saved, many remain precarious, emphasizing the continuous challenge of maintaining historical integrity against modern pressures and lack of resources. This article is highly relevant to open data because the Heritage at Risk register serves as a critical public dataset. By making this information accessible, stakeholders can analyze trends, allocate resources effectively, and engage communities in preservation initiatives. Transparency in this data empowers local groups and policymakers to identify gaps in support and collaborate on solutions, ensuring that heritage assets are monitored and protected through evidence-based decision-making rather than remaining obscured or undocumented.
