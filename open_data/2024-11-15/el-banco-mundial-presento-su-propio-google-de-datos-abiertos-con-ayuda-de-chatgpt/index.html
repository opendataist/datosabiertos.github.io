El Banco Mundial presentó su propio ‘Google’ de datos abiertos con ayuda de ChatGPT

The UN World Data Forum in Medellín underscores the urgency of integrating artificial intelligence to advance sustainable development through the strategic use of data. The primary objective is to establish a global roadmap that optimizes how AI tools facilitate access to critical information in sectors such as health and the economy, ensuring that technological advancements directly serve the sustainability goals of the 21st century. The World Bank highlights the critical need to improve the accessibility and organization of open data, recognizing that traditional search engines lack the semantic capacity to interpret complex contexts. To address this, a new approach is proposed that not only uses AI to generate or validate data but also prepares and documents these resources so they are machine-interpretable, thereby bridging the gap between the massive availability of information and its actual utility for decision-makers. This initiative is relevant to the open data movement because it proposes an AI-based semantic catalog that prioritizes transparency and accuracy in information retrieval. By enabling users to understand why certain results are deemed relevant, it fosters greater trust in public data. This shift represents a fundamental step toward a more inclusive data architecture, where technology not only stores information but also makes it understandable, accessible, and actionable for society as a whole.
