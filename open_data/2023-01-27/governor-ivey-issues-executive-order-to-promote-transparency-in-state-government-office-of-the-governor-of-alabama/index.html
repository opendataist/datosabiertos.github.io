Governor Ivey Issues Executive Order to Promote Transparency in State Government - Office of the Governor of Alabama

Governor Kay Ivey has signed an executive order aimed at significantly enhancing transparency and accountability within Alabama’s state government. By mandating that executive-branch agencies establish dedicated public records pages on their websites, the administration seeks to streamline access to information. This structural change ensures that citizens can easily locate contact points and request forms, thereby reducing barriers to government oversight and fostering greater public trust in state institutions. The order introduces standardized response timelines to balance efficiency with the complexity of different requests. Standard inquiries are now subject to shorter processing windows, while more complex requests that require extensive review or redaction are granted additional time. These structured deadlines provide clear expectations for both agencies and requesters, ensuring that public records are handled systematically and promptly without unnecessary delays, which promotes a more responsive and reliable government service. This initiative is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a uniform fee schedule and prioritizes electronic delivery. By eliminating per-page charges for digital records, the policy removes financial obstacles that often hinder the free flow of information. This shift encourages the widespread adoption of electronic data sharing, a foundational element of open data initiatives, making government records more accessible, affordable, and usable for citizens, researchers, and developers who rely on digital access for transparency and innovation.
