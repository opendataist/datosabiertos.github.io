Dubai Land Department issues 9,047 permits and 6,479 licences in 2022

Dubai’s real estate sector achieved record transaction volumes and significant permit growth in 2022, driven by strong global investor demand and the department’s commitment to service excellence. This surge supports broader economic goals to double the emirate’s economy by 2033, highlighting the market’s resilience and potential for sustained exponential expansion. The data reveals a digital-first ecosystem, with online advertisements dominating permit categories and brokerages leading license issuance. This shift underscores a successful transition toward digitalization, where streamlined processes and supportive regulations have fostered a competitive environment. Such transparency and efficiency are critical for maintaining stakeholder trust and attracting sustained international interest. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparent, aggregated public statistics can drive market confidence and inform policy. By making performance metrics publicly available, the government enhances accountability and allows stakeholders to make informed decisions. Open data thus serves not just as a record of activity, but as a catalyst for economic planning and regulatory refinement in a global investment hub.
