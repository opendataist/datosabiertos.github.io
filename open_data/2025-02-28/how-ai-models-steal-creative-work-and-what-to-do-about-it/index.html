How AI models steal creative work — and what to do about it

How AI models steal creative work — and what to do about it

Generative AI models rely on unlicensed creative work for training data, creating a conflict between tech companies and the original creators. This practice undermines the industry by failing to compensate the artists and writers whose intellectual property powers these systems. Ed Newton-Rex proposes licensing as the vital solution to align incentives, ensuring both AI developers and content creators can thrive. This highlights the urgent need for open data frameworks that respect copyright and sustain creative ecosystems.
