Inicia sesión de comisiones del Senado para votar iniciativa de Ley de Transparencia; traspasa funciones del Inai a Secretaría Anticorrupción

Inicia sesión de comisiones del Senado para votar iniciativa de Ley de Transparencia; traspasa funciones del Inai a Secretaría Anticorrupción

Mexico proposes centralizing transparency functions under the Anti-Corruption Secretariat as the current independent institute winds down. This legislative shift aims to streamline public access to information by relaxing confidentiality requirements for data already available in public sources or legal registries. Such changes are pivotal for open data governance, potentially altering the balance between institutional oversight and citizens’ right to transparent, unrestricted government information.
