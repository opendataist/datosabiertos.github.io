CSP advierte sanciones para exfuncionarios del INAI y jueces salientes | Revista Punto de Vista

CSP advierte sanciones para exfuncionarios del INAI y jueces salientes | Revista Punto de Vista

President Claudia Sheinbaum has confirmed that the abolition of the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) and the proposed judicial reforms will not shield former officials from accountability. She assured that audits will continue regardless of institutional changes, ensuring administrative and potential criminal consequences for any detected irregularities or nepotism. The government is targeting questionable judicial practices, such as rushed rulings and suspensions issued by outgoing judges. By scrutinizing these decisions, authorities aim to expose hidden interests and ensure that legal processes adhere to proper norms rather than facilitating criminal activity. This development is crucial for open data, as it highlights the tension between institutional transparency mechanisms and state oversight. It underscores how data governance relies on rigorous audit trails and accountability, even when traditional transparency bodies are dismantled, thereby affecting public trust and access to reliable information.
