Los huevos se han encarecido casi un 50 % en Baleares en la última década

The article highlights a persistent trend of rising egg prices in the Balearic Islands, driven by supply constraints and the impact of avian flu in the United States. This volatility directly threatens consumer stability, as the region relies heavily on imports from the Spanish mainland to meet local demand. Such fluctuations underscore the critical need for transparent market data to monitor trends in essential commodities. Open data initiatives can provide the visibility required for stakeholders to anticipate shortages and understand the socio-economic impact of global supply chain disruptions on local food security.
