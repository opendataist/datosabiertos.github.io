Diario HOY | Paraguay avanza en educación, pero la deserción escolar es preocupante

Diario HOY | Paraguay avanza en educación, pero la deserción escolar es preocupante

The 2022 National Census reveals that while Paraguay has achieved near-universal enrollment in primary education, significant challenges remain regarding secondary school retention and equity. Although access to schooling has expanded dramatically over recent decades, attendance rates drop sharply during adolescence as young people prioritize economic activities, highlighting a critical need for policies that support student retention beyond the compulsory level. Data analysis exposes persistent structural inequalities, particularly affecting rural communities and older demographics. Despite overall improvements in literacy and educational attainment, the gap between urban and rural areas remains wide, with rural populations lagging significantly in average years of schooling. Additionally, gender disparities emerge at higher education levels, where women outperform men, contrasting with earlier stages where male attendance was higher, indicating complex sociocultural factors influencing educational trajectories. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides a granular, transparent evidence base required for effective public policy. By making these detailed demographic and educational statistics publicly accessible, governments and civil society can identify specific gaps in equity and retention. This transparency fosters accountability and enables targeted interventions to bridge the urban-rural divide and improve long-term educational outcomes across the country.
