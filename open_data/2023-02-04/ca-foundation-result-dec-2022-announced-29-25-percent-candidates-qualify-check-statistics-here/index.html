CA Foundation Result Dec 2022 Announced, 29.25 Percent Candidates Qualify, Check Statistics Here

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India recently released the CA Foundation results for the December session, highlighting a pass rate of approximately twenty-nine percent. This data reveals that out of over one hundred twenty-six thousand candidates, nearly thirty-seven thousand successfully qualified, indicating a consistent level of academic rigor and selection difficulty within the preliminary examination stage. A significant finding is the nearly equal performance between male and female candidates, with pass percentages hovering closely around twenty-nine percent for both groups. This statistical parity underscores that gender does not significantly influence outcomes at this foundational level, promoting a narrative of equitable opportunity and equal preparation standards among all examinees regardless of demographic background. This information is relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the value of publicly accessible, granular educational statistics. By making detailed performance metrics and demographic breakdowns available online, regulatory bodies enable transparency, allow stakeholders to analyze educational trends, and support informed decision-making for prospective students and policy researchers seeking to understand the landscape of professional certification.
