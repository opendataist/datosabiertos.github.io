Preparan gran plataforma china de datos públicos

Preparan gran plataforma china de datos públicos

China is aggressively driving the digital economy by opening up and authorizing the use of public data, marking a milestone with the launch of the National Data Resource Registration Platform in March. This initiative not only serves as a management system but also as a portal for data disclosure and discovery, enabling providers and users to interact more efficiently. By introducing professional forces to manage and process these resources, the government aims to ensure security while meeting social demands, laying the foundation for an integrated national system by 2030. The strategy focuses on transforming data into a valuable commodity that drives sustainable economic growth. By expanding application scenarios and fostering secure circulation, China expects to generate new business models and job opportunities. Experts highlight that these efforts provide robust support for industrial upgrading, ensuring that data exchange and trade are conducted in an orderly and efficient manner, which is crucial for maintaining competitiveness in the global market. This development is highly relevant to the field of open data, as it demonstrates how a well-structured governance infrastructure can maximize the value of informational assets without compromising security. By standardizing access and authorized operations, China sets a precedent for how governments can balance transparency with protection, thereby facilitating technological innovation and administrative transparency in an increasingly complex digital environment.
