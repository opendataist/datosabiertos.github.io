LOPD-RGPD II: Adapta tu empresa a la legislación de protección de datos personales (segunda parte) (WEBINAR ONLINE) - SPRI

LOPD-RGPD II: Adapta tu empresa a la legislación de protección de datos personales (segunda parte) (WEBINAR ONLINE) - SPRI

This text outlines the legal bases for storing technical data, distinguishing between essential service functions and user preferences on the one hand, and statistical or marketing profiling on the other. It emphasizes that anonymous statistical data cannot identify users, whereas marketing purposes require explicit consent for tracking and personalized advertising. This is relevant to open data because it clarifies privacy boundaries, ensuring transparency about when personal information is processed and how it affects user rights and data governance standards.
