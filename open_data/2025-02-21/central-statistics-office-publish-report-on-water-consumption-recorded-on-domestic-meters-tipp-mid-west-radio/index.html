Central Statistics Office Publish Report On Water Consumption Recorded On Domestic Meters - Tipp Mid West Radio

The Central Statistics Office reveals that average Irish household water consumption has decreased, though significant regional disparities remain. Crucially, the report highlights that median figures better represent typical usage than averages, which are skewed by leaks and extreme outliers, offering a clearer picture of daily habits. This distinction is vital for open data initiatives aiming to promote transparency and accountability in public resource management. By understanding the difference between mean and median values, citizens and policymakers can identify genuine consumption patterns versus infrastructure inefficiencies, ensuring more accurate public discourse. Ultimately, this data empowers communities to address water waste effectively. By focusing on median consumption, stakeholders can target leak repairs and conservation efforts where they matter most, driving sustainable water use and improving the integrity of publicly available statistical information.
