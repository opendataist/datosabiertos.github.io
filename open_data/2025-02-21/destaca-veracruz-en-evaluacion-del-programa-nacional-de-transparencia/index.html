Destaca Veracruz en evaluación del Programa Nacional de Transparencia

Destaca Veracruz en evaluación del Programa Nacional de Transparencia

Veracruz tops the national rankings in transparency and data protection, setting a high standard even as its data institute faces imminent closure. This accomplishment underscores the state’s successful implementation of access-to-information strategies, serving as a key reference for other regions undergoing similar institutional transitions. Nevertheless, significant challenges persist in terms of institutional capacity and budgetary resources. These constraints hinder the development of large-scale projects, indicating that while procedural excellence is present, structural support is inadequate to sustain long-term improvements in governance. This case highlights the vulnerability of open-data frameworks when they are tied to specific entities rather than embedded in systemic policies. It demonstrates that high performance metrics can coexist with fragile institutional foundations, underscoring the need for robust, resilient structures to ensure lasting transparency and the protection of data rights beyond the lifespan of individual organizations.
