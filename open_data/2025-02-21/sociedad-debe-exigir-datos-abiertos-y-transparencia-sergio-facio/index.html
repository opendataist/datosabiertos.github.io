Sociedad debe exigir datos abiertos y transparencia: Sergio Facio

The workshop underscores that open data is essential for scrutinizing government actions, reinforcing accountability and citizen participation. Facio Guzmán emphasized that integrating these tools into regulations is indispensable for ensuring transparency, especially during uncertain times. By enhancing public officials' knowledge on information access and data protection, this initiative supports the institutional framework required for effective governance and ethical data management. This event highlights the critical role of open data in democratic societies, demonstrating how shared information empowers citizens and holds institutions accountable. It serves as a vital example of best practices in promoting transparency and civic engagement through accessible, standardized public data.
