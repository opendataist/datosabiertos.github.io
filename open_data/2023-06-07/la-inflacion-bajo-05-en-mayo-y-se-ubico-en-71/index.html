La inflación bajó 0,5% en mayo y se ubicó en 7,1%

The article analyzes recent inflation trends, highlighting a slight monthly price decline while interannual inflation remains above the target range. It details specific sector impacts, noting significant decreases in fresh produce prices alongside increases in housing and financial services. This data reflects broader economic pressures and fluctuations in consumer costs. The relevance to open data lies in the importance of accessing raw, granular statistical datasets for independent analysis. When such information is published transparently, researchers and citizens can verify official narratives and understand nuanced price movements. Open data empowers users to create custom visualizations or cross-reference sectors, ensuring accountability and providing deeper economic insights beyond summary reports. Ultimately, this case underscores the value of publishing high-quality, structured statistical data. By making detailed metrics available, institutions foster trust and enable more accurate public discourse. The ability to explore the underlying drivers of inflation supports informed decision-making and policy evaluation, demonstrating how open data transforms complex statistics into actionable societal knowledge.
