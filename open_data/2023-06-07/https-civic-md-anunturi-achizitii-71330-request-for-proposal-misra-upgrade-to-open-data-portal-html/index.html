Open Data
ES EN

The USAID-supported MISRA initiative in Moldova seeks to upgrade the national Open Data Portal, a critical governmental platform facilitating real-time access to public sector information. This service empowers citizens and organizations to freely utilize, modify, and redistribute government data, thereby fostering transparency and accountability. The current system, reliant on an outdated version of the open-source CKAN software, suffers from functional deficiencies that hinder effective data generation and management. Upgrading this infrastructure is essential to ensure reliable, secure, and modern data handling capabilities. By transitioning to a newer, robust version of the CKAN backend and improving its underlying technologies, the project aims to enhance the portal’s performance and usability. This technical improvement supports the broader goal of open government by making state-produced data more accurate, accessible, and available without unnecessary restrictions or controls. This procurement is relevant to open data as it directly strengthens the technical foundation of a key transparency initiative. Reliable open data platforms are vital for civic engagement, research, and innovation, allowing stakeholders to derive meaningful insights from public records. Enhancing such portals ensures that the promise of open government is met through functional, up-to-date digital services that serve the public interest effectively.
