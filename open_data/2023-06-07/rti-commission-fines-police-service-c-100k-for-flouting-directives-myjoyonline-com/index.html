RTI Commission fines Police Service ¢100k for flouting directives - MyJoyOnline.com

The Ghana RTI Commission imposed a significant fine on the Police Service for ignoring information requests and defying official directives. This enforcement action underscores the legal obligation of state agencies to respond transparently to citizen inquiries, rejecting administrative negligence as an unacceptable barrier to accountability. The penalty highlights the critical importance of enforcing transparency laws to uphold constitutional rights. By mandating compliance, the commission protects the public’s right to access information, ensuring that government bodies cannot operate with impunity or disregard established legal frameworks governing open data and public records. This case is vital for the open data community, demonstrating that robust regulatory mechanisms exist to challenge opacity. It serves as a precedent for holding powerful institutions accountable, proving that structured legal pressure can effectively compel organizations to release withheld information and respect transparency mandates.
