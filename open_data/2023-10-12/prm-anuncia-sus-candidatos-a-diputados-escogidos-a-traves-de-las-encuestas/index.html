PRM anuncia sus candidatos a diputados escogidos a través de las encuestas

PRM anuncia sus candidatos a diputados escogidos a través de las encuestas

The Partido Revolucionario Moderno has concluded its internal selection process for the 2024 legislative elections, publishing the names of candidates who secured positions through party surveys. This announcement marks the finalization of the party’s official ticket, establishing the roster of legislators who will represent the governing party in the upcoming national elections. The transparency of this internal mechanism is crucial for understanding the party’s organizational structure and its readiness for the general electorate. By releasing these results on its official website, the party adheres to principles of accessibility and public record-keeping. This digital publication allows citizens and researchers to easily access information regarding who is running for office and from which regions. It demonstrates a commitment to data availability, ensuring that the political landscape is clearly defined before the formal electoral campaign intensifies. This article is relevant to open data as it illustrates how political parties generate and disseminate structured information about their operations. The release of candidate lists serves as a dataset that can be analyzed for trends in regional representation, gender balance, and historical party strength. Such data enables civic monitoring and fosters accountability, proving that open political information is essential for a healthy democratic process.
