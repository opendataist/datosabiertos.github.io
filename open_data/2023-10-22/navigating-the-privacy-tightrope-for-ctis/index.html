Navigating The Privacy Tightrope For CTIS

Clinical trials in the EU involve vast numbers of participants, making data privacy and meticulous anonymization critical for CTIS compliance. This white paper outlines challenges and best practices to safeguard personal information effectively. Stakeholders must collaborate to build frameworks for responsible data sharing, ensuring ethical handling of sensitive information while accelerating the development of life-changing therapies.
