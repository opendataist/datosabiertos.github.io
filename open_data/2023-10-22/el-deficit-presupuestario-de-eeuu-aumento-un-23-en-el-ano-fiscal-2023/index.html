El déficit presupuestario de EEUU aumentó un 23% en el año fiscal 2023

The U.S. budget deficit widened due to tax revenues that fell short of expectations, yet the economy remains resilient, defying recession forecasts with robust job growth. This situation underscores the tension between fiscal shortfalls and sustained economic performance. In the realm of open data, these figures highlight the critical need for transparent and accessible government financial datasets. Analyzing such data enables researchers and citizens to assess fiscal health, monitor the impact of policies, and ensure accountability in public spending decisions.
