Venezolanos superan a mexicanos: 55 mil han sido arrestados en la frontera sur de EE .UU. - Noticiero Digital

The United States has granted temporary protection to Venezuelan migrants while simultaneously strengthening border enforcement and increasing deportations, signaling a shift in diplomatic relations with Maduro. This policy response addresses evolving migration patterns, which are moving away from traditional flows through Mexico toward more diverse groups from South America and Asia. This case illustrates how government actions influence the movement of vulnerable populations, directly affecting the collection and accessibility of demographic data for open governance and humanitarian aid.
