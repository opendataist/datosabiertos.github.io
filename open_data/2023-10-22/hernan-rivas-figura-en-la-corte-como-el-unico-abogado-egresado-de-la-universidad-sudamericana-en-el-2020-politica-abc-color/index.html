Hernán Rivas figura en la Corte como el único abogado egresado de la Universidad Sudamericana en el 2020 - Política - ABC Color

The investigation into Senator Hernán David Rivas Román’s professional credentials reveals significant inconsistencies regarding his law degree. Official records from the Ministry of Education and the Supreme Court indicate that he was the sole graduate of the Universidad Sudamericana’s law program in 2020. This timing is highly suspect, as data shows the institution officially ceased its law degree accreditation years earlier, suggesting the degree may not have been issued through standard, accredited channels during a period when the program was effectively closed. Furthermore, judicial records show that Rivas Román underwent a private swearing-in ceremony with no other peers present, isolating his process from typical academic cohorts. This anomalous procedure, combined with the lack of institutional oversight and the fact that the university did not officially recognize his graduation year, raises serious doubts about the legitimacy of his qualification. These discrepancies suggest a possible irregularity in how he obtained his title, warranting scrutiny from judicial and educational authorities to ensure transparency in public office qualifications. This case is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates the power of cross-referencing multiple public registries to verify the integrity of public officials. By making educational records, judicial oaths, and institutional accreditation data accessible and interoperable, civil society can effectively hold leaders accountable. It highlights how decentralized, open datasets serve as essential tools for uncovering contradictions and preventing potential fraudulent representation in government bodies.
