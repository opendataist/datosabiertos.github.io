Travel and tourism in Europe to grow 3.1% in 2026, despite uncertainty

Europe’s travel and tourism sector is projected to significantly outpace the broader economy in 2026, driven by substantial increases in international and business spending. This robust growth highlights the industry's resilience against global uncertainties, positioning it as a vital engine for economic recovery and expansion on the continent. Industry leaders emphasize that realizing this potential requires governments to establish supportive frameworks, including seamless travel infrastructure and sustainable development strategies. The upcoming global summit serves as a critical platform for policymakers and executives to collaborate, ensuring that tourism expansion benefits local communities while addressing accessibility and environmental concerns.
