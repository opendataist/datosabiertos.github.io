Arteaga es el municipio que más creció en Coahuila | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The rapid population growth in Arteaga demands orderly and sustainable expansion. The municipal administration emphasizes the need for robust instruments to ensure public policies that protect residents’ well-being and prevent improvisation in the face of demographic expansion. The establishment of the Local Ecological Planning Council is crucial for defining the compatibility of activities in a predominantly forested territory. This approach seeks to balance urban development with environmental preservation, anticipating the impacts of metropolitan pressure and ensuring the long-term viability of planning decisions. This case illustrates the importance of open data and official statistics, such as those from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI), for public management. Transparent access to demographic information enables local governments to detect critical trends, base decision-making on evidence, and design territorial development strategies that effectively respond to the community’s real needs.
