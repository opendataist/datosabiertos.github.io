UNAM has opened the admissions process for its distance and open learning modalities, enabling a broader audience to access higher education under a flexible model. This announcement is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it demonstrates transparency in publishing requirements, costs, and institutional electoral calendars. By making this data publicly available in a structured format, the university facilitates citizen scrutiny and the development of third-party technological tools, promoting accountability and equitable access to public information. The institution reinforces the integrity of the process by warning against external scams and providing official study materials, ensuring that evaluation criteria are uniform and accessible to all applicants. This clarity in the rules of the game is essential for maintaining trust in mass admission systems. The availability of guides and self-assessment platforms demonstrates a commitment to the democratization of knowledge, aligning with openness principles that seek to reduce informational barriers and empower users with verified, unbiased resources. Finally, the extensive academic offerings in key fields such as Law and Economics highlight the university’s capacity to meet educational demand through the strategic allocation of resources. This transparency in the distribution of available spots and the structure of the academic offerings enables citizens to make informed decisions about their educational paths. In the context of public management, this data is vital for analyzing trends in social mobility and the effectiveness of educational policies. The open publication of this information contributes to an environment where civic innovation can generate solutions to optimize educational inclusion and improve the quality of public services.
