Las expectativas empresariales en Uruguay proyectan una inflación moderada y estabilidad en el mercado de trabajo

Las expectativas empresariales en Uruguay proyectan una inflación moderada y estabilidad en el mercado de trabajo

The National Institute of Statistics revealed that Uruguayan business leaders maintain expectations aligned with official targets, consolidating price stability and containing labor costs. This convergence toward predefined macroeconomic objectives suggests an environment of crucial predictability for price formation in the domestic market and for collective bargaining, reflecting institutional confidence that has managed to place inflation projections well below historical averages. In the labor sphere, most firms plan to keep their workforce unchanged, indicating moderation in hiring and a slightly positive bias toward job creation. At the same time, coordinated nominal wage growth is anticipated in line with official guidelines, aiming to preserve purchasing power without destabilizing economic activity. This balance between costs and productivity is fundamental to the sustainability of private employment in the short term. The relevance of this article for open data lies in the transparency of key indicators that enable citizens and researchers to analyze economic health and business confidence. By making detailed data on inflation expectations, employment, and the business climate publicly available, it fosters public scrutiny and informed decision-making based on tangible evidence. The availability of these structured datasets facilitates academic, journalistic, and policy research, demonstrating the value of open public statistics for society.
