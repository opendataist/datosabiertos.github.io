Regeneron acquires 23andMe for $256 million, sparking privacy concerns over genetic data – NaturalNews.com

The acquisition of 23andMe by Regeneron marks a critical juncture for open data in the genetic sector, highlighting the tension between scientific advancement and individual privacy. As a major pharmaceutical entity assumes control of one of the largest consumer DNA repositories, the transaction underscores how personal biological information has shifted from a tool for consumer empowerment to a commodified asset for drug development. This transition raises fundamental questions about who owns and controls immutable genetic records, suggesting that current open data frameworks may insufficiently protect individuals when their data becomes part of a corporate research pipeline. The bankruptcy and subsequent cyberattack reveal significant vulnerabilities in how genetic data is stored and managed by private entities. Unlike financial data, genetic information is permanent, making breaches irreversible and creating long-term risks for users regarding discrimination and surveillance. The incident demonstrates that even large-scale datasets labeled as anonymized are not immune to misuse or exposure, challenging the efficacy of existing consent models. It highlights a systemic failure where corporate financial instability directly threatens the security of sensitive open datasets, leaving millions of users vulnerable to privacy violations that cannot be remedied. Ultimately, this case serves as a cautionary tale for the open data community regarding ethical stewardship and transparency. The lack of clarity in how users' data is repurposed for third-party research blurs the lines between voluntary testing and commercial exploitation. It emphasizes the urgent need for stricter regulatory oversight and more robust consent mechanisms in the era of big genetic data. As pharmaceutical giants increasingly absorb consumer databases, society must reckon with the implications of allowing corporate entities to dictate the future use of such intimate biological information without full public知情 consent.
