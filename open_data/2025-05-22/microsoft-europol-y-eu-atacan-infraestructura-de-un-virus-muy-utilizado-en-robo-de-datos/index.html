Microsoft, Europol y EU atacan infraestructura de un virus muy utilizado en robo de datos

Microsoft and Europol have dismantled the infrastructure of the Lumma Stealer malware, a service that facilitates data theft by criminals. This joint operation, supported by the United States, intercepted thousands of IP addresses and took control of the platform used to sell stolen information, neutralizing a global threat. The success demonstrates the crucial effectiveness of public-private collaboration in cybersecurity. By blocking the technical foundation of the attack, the integrity of systems is protected, and the ability of malicious actors to operate with impunity on the Internet is reduced. This initiative is relevant to open data because it highlights the need to share threat intelligence in a coordinated manner. Transparency in defense methodologies enables technology communities and researchers to improve their protocols, fostering a more resilient and open security ecosystem in the face of emerging digital risks.
