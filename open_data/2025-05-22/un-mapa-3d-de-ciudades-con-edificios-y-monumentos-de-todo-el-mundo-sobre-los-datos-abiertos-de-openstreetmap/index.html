Un Mapa 3D de ciudades con edificios y monumentos de todo el mundo sobre los datos abiertos de OpenStreetMap

F4Map showcases the power of OpenStreetMap’s collaborative data by rendering highly detailed, interactive 2D and 3D urban environments. This technology highlights how community-contributed geographic information enables realistic simulations, including specific landmarks, infrastructure, and natural elements, directly leveraging open datasets for complex visualizations. The relevance to open data lies in its demonstration that freely available, crowd-sourced information can power advanced applications, ranging from virtual worlds to civil engineering. It underscores the value of standardized, accessible geographic data, showing how open contributions translate into practical, high-fidelity tools that enhance urban analysis and immersive experiences without relying on proprietary sources.
