Chiapas, epicentro de la inflación y pobreza laboral

Chiapas exhibits the highest inflation and poverty rates in Mexico, where residents cannot afford basic necessities. Prices for essentials like food and housing rise faster locally than nationally, creating a severe economic burden on households. This disparity highlights a critical gap in how open data reveals social inequalities. By making economic metrics accessible, citizens and researchers can identify regions in crisis that general national averages might obscure, ensuring local realities are not lost in broad statistics. Understanding these granular, open datasets is vital for effective policy-making and advocacy. Transparent economic data allows stakeholders to target interventions accurately, addressing the specific vulnerabilities of marginalized communities rather than relying on misleading national trends.
