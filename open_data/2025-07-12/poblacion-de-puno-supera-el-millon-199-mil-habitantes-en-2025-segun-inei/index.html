The article highlights how Puno’s population structure reflects significant social shifts, characterized by a young demographic and a slight female majority. This composition implies urgent needs for educational and employment services, while an aging segment signals future pressures on healthcare systems. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for addressing the diverse requirements of children, working-age adults, and seniors in a rapidly changing society. Urbanization and spatial distribution are reshaping the region, with specific provinces and cities driving growth while others face isolation. This uneven density complicates public policy planning, as resources must be allocated efficiently to serve both expanding urban centers and dispersed rural areas. The shift toward urban living demands infrastructure development that supports commerce and cultural activities, ensuring equitable access to services across all territories. This report is vital to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, official statistics empower evidence-based decision-making. By making demographic trends accessible, the INEI enables stakeholders to plan effective policies, optimize resource distribution, and foster inclusive development. Open access to such granular data allows researchers and authorities to monitor progress, identify disparities, and collaborate on solutions that enhance the overall quality of life for Puno’s residents.
