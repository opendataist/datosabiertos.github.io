Nigerian banks saw a significant surge in non-performing loans in 2020, exceeding regulatory limits and highlighting systemic financial stress. This data underscores the critical importance of transparent, accessible banking statistics for monitoring financial health. Open data allows stakeholders to track systemic risks and understand how specific industries impact overall economic stability.
