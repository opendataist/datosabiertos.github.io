Hugging Face's new robot is the Seinfeld of AI devices

Hugging Face’s open-source Reachy Mini bots demonstrate how accessible, programmable hardware can captivate users despite limited out-of-the-box functionality. Their success highlights a growing trend where physical AI devices prioritize community engagement and customization over immediate utility. This matters to open data as it expands the ecosystem for sharing robotics software and datasets.
