Here's a quick glance at unemployment rates for June, by Canadian city

This report highlights the national unemployment rate alongside city-level metrics, yet emphasizes that these local figures often fluctuate significantly due to reliance on small statistical samples. The caution regarding sample size serves as a critical reminder for data users about the inherent volatility and potential unreliability of granular labor market indicators. For open data practitioners, this underscores the vital importance of understanding methodological limitations when accessing and disseminating government statistics. It illustrates that raw data outputs, particularly at sub-national levels, may not reflect stable trends without careful contextual analysis and acknowledgment of sampling errors. Relevant to open data communities, this example demonstrates the need for transparency in data documentation. Providing clear metadata about sample sizes and confidence intervals empowers users to interpret statistics responsibly, ensuring that published data supports informed decision-making rather than misleading assumptions based on noisy or small datasets.
