Fallout of MUDA sites scam: Special counter for RTI queries - Star of Mysore

Following allegations of opacity in the MUDA site allotment scam, the agency has introduced a dedicated single-window counter for Right to Information requests. This initiative directly addresses historical inefficiencies by eliminating the need for applicants to navigate multiple offices, thereby streamlining the process and enhancing institutional transparency. By establishing a clear, trackable channel for these queries, MUDA aims to restore public trust through improved accountability mechanisms. Simultaneously, the integration of Janaspandana counters provides a robust platform for citizens to register grievances regarding delays or service refusals. This system ensures that public concerns are not only recorded but also monitored within strict timeframes, with automatic escalation to higher authorities if deadlines are missed. This structured approach empowers citizens to hold the administration accountable for service delivery failures. These measures are highly relevant to open data as they institutionalize transparency and public access to government information. By creating structured, trackable pathways for both RTI applications and citizen complaints, the agency generates a more accessible data trail on administrative performance. This shift supports the core open data principles of accountability and civic engagement, ensuring that government operations are subject to public scrutiny and feedback.
