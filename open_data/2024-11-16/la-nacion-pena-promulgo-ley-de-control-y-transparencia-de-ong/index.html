La Nación / Peña promulgó ley de control y transparencia de ONG

Paraguay has enacted a new law establishing a transparency regime and an official registry for Non-Profit Organizations (NPOs). The primary objective is to improve coordination with the State, promote accountability, and maximize the positive impact on citizens. This regulation aims to integrate NPOs that manage public or private resources into a clear regulatory framework, ensuring that their operations are visible and accountable to society. The President has ordered the immediate initiation of participatory rulemaking for the law, involving all interested stakeholders. It is emphasized that transparency and oversight are fundamental elements of the Paraguayan State, and therefore no organization should resist these standards. The implementation seeks to balance organizational freedom with the need for supervision, ensuring that funds, whether national or international, are used ethically and effectively for the common good. This development is relevant to the open data movement because the creation of an official NPO registry implies the potential generation and publication of structured data about these entities. By formalizing information on who manages resources and how, it facilitates public auditing, journalistic investigation, and citizen participation. The law promotes a culture of transparency that aligns directly with the principles of access to information and the use of data to enhance democratic governance.
