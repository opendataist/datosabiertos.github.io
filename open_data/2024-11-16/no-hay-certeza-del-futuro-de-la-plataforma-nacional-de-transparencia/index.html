No hay certeza del futuro de la Plataforma Nacional de Transparencia

No hay certeza del futuro de la Plataforma Nacional de Transparencia

The imminent dissolution of the INAI raises critical uncertainty about the future of the National Transparency Platform. This central database is essential for exercising the right to access information and for protecting personal data. Its potential fragmentation could jeopardize a fundamental mechanism for public accountability, highlighting the risks that institutional instability poses to open-data infrastructure. The platform has undergone significant evolution by integrating artificial intelligence to improve the precision of information retrieval within extensive government records. This technological upgrade enables more effective navigation through billions of data points held by various public entities. Such improvements demonstrate how modern tools can democratize access to public records, empowering citizens, journalists, and researchers to leverage complex datasets for the benefit of society. This case underscores the need to safeguard open-data frameworks against arbitrary political changes. The platform serves as a cornerstone for transparency, supporting academic research and civic oversight across all levels of government. Preserving its independence and integrity is essential to maintaining public trust and ensuring that access to information remains a secure, transparent right rather than a vulnerable administrative tool.
