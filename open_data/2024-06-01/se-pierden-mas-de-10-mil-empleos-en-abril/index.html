Se pierden más de 10 mil empleos en abril

The net loss of formal jobs highlights labor market volatility, suggesting cuts in the public and military sectors. This trend partially contradicts IMSS records, indicating that traditional data may not capture the full dynamics of employment. The industrial sector experienced a significant contraction, while services and construction showed resilience. This sectoral contrast reveals a structural transformation in the economy, where informality gains ground amid the deterioration of formality, affecting workers' economic stability. This report is relevant to open data because it exposes discrepancies between national statistical sources. Analyzing these divergences allows for an assessment of the quality and completeness of public data, which is essential for developing transparent indicators that accurately reflect socioeconomic realities and promote accountability.
