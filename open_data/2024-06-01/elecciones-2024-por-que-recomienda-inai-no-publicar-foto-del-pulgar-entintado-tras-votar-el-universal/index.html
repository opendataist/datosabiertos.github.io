Elecciones 2024: ¿Por qué recomienda Inai no publicar foto del pulgar entintado tras votar? | El Universal

Elecciones 2024: ¿Por qué recomienda Inai no publicar foto del pulgar entintado tras votar? | El Universal

The INAI advises against posting photos of the inked finger or voter ID on social media to prevent misuse of personal data during Mexico’s historic elections. This guidance highlights the critical need for citizens to protect their biometric and identity information from unauthorized sharing or exploitation by third parties. By urging users to avoid publishing images of others’ faces and to carefully review privacy notices at polling stations, the recommendation underscores the broader implications of data privacy. It emphasizes that individual actions during high-visibility events like voting can significantly impact the security and integrity of personal data ecosystems. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the tension between transparency in public processes and the protection of private information. It reinforces that while democratic participation requires visibility, open data principles must balance access with robust safeguards to prevent privacy violations and ensure responsible data handling by both individuals and institutions.
