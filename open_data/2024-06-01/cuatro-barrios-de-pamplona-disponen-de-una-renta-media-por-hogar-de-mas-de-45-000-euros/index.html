Cuatro barrios de Pamplona disponen de una renta media por hogar de más de 45.000 euros

Pamplona exhibits stark economic division, with wealth disparity sharply split by the Arga River. A small number of affluent districts significantly outperform others, highlighting deep socioeconomic inequality within the city. This data reveals how local economic structures create distinct zones of privilege and deprivation. Understanding these patterns is crucial for equitable urban planning and resource allocation. The information is vital for open data as it exposes social inequalities through accessible, transparent metrics. It enables researchers and citizens to analyze and address spatial economic divides effectively.
