CDC Finally Released Its VAERS Safety Monitoring Analyses for Covid Vaccines via FOIA - LewRockwell

The CDC’s analysis of VAERS data reveals significant safety signals for mRNA COVID-19 vaccines across various age groups, indicating a disproportionate rate of serious adverse events compared to historical vaccine data. This study highlights a wide array of concerning conditions, including severe cardiac, neurological, and thromboembolic events, suggesting that the vaccine safety monitoring system has detected patterns warranting immediate public health attention. The findings challenge previous assertions that the vaccines are safe by demonstrating that serious reports for these shots outnumbered all prior adult vaccine serious reports combined in a fraction of the time. By utilizing disproportionality analysis, the research shows that specific adverse events occurred at significantly higher rates for mRNA vaccines than for non-COVID comparables, meeting the strict statistical criteria for safety signals without relying on total administration numbers. This article is crucial for open data advocacy as it underscores the necessity of transparent, accessible, and timely government data release. The prolonged delays in publishing this analysis, despite prior commitments and freedom of information requests, highlight systemic opacity in public health monitoring. Ensuring open access to such critical safety data allows independent researchers and the public to verify findings, fostering trust in scientific processes and enabling a robust, community-driven scrutiny of vaccine safety.
