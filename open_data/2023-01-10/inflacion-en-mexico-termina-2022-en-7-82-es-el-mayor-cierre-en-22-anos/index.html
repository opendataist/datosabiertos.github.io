Inflación en México termina 2022 en 7.82 %, es el mayor cierre en 22 años

The article highlights Mexico’s persistent inflation, which significantly exceeds the central bank’s 3% target. This surge is driven by rising prices for essential goods and energy, compelling monetary authorities to implement aggressive interest rate hikes to stabilize the economy. Government officials have criticized these conventional monetary measures, advocating instead for unconventional policies aimed at curbing cost increases. The tension between administrative interventions and standard economic tools underscores the complexity of managing price stability in a volatile market environment. This situation is crucial for open data because it emphasizes the necessity of transparent, accessible inflation metrics. Reliable statistical data allows citizens and analysts to verify official claims, assess policy effectiveness, and understand the real impact of price fluctuations on household purchasing power.
