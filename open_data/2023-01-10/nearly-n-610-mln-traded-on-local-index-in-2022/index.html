Nearly N$610 mln traded on Local Index in 2022

Namibia’s stock market showed resilience in 2022 despite a slight index decline and robust trading volumes. This activity highlights the depth of capital markets in emerging economies, providing essential data for assessing economic stability. Understanding such market performance is crucial for open data advocates, as transparent financial metrics enable better public oversight. Accessible stock exchange data fosters trust in economic institutions and supports informed decision-making by investors and researchers alike.
