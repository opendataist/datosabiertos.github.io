Wikipedia isn’t dead yet, but AI poses major challenges

Wikipedia isn’t dead yet, but AI poses major challenges

Recent studies refute fears that AI tools like ChatGPT will destroy Wikipedia, revealing stable or growing traffic and no decline in editor participation. While AI scraping and changing access methods present ongoing challenges, the platform remains resilient against these emerging technologies. This research is vital for open data communities as it demonstrates the enduring value of crowd-sourced, freely accessible knowledge. It highlights that despite technological shifts, users continue to rely on Wikipedia, reinforcing its role as a critical infrastructure for public information. The findings suggest that open knowledge ecosystems can withstand AI disruption if they maintain user trust and utility. Understanding this resilience helps guide policies on data scraping and AI integration, ensuring that open data resources remain robust and relevant in an evolving digital landscape.
