Indonesia devuelve licencia a TikTok tras suspensión por negativa a compartir datos - Ciencia - ABC Color

Indonesia devuelve licencia a TikTok tras suspensión por negativa a compartir datos - Ciencia - ABC Color

Indonesia has reinstated TikTok’s license after the platform finally provided the requested data concerning its live-streaming activities and monetization practices. This transparency addressed concerns about illegal gambling content, enabling the company to regain its status as a registered electronic system operator following a temporary suspension. The decision also coincides with regulatory actions targeting TikTok’s corporate maneuvers. Authorities imposed substantial fines for the company’s failure to promptly report its acquisition of a majority stake in Tokopedia, underscoring the government’s strict enforcement of antitrust and notification laws in the digital sector. This case is pertinent to open data, as it illustrates how transparency requirements serve as a leverage tool for regulators. It demonstrates that granting access to information is a prerequisite for operational continuity, reinforcing the principle that digital platforms must submit to oversight to maintain their social license to operate.
