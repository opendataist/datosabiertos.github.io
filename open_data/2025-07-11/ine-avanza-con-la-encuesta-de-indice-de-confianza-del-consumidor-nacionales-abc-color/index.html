INE avanza con la Encuesta de Índice de Confianza del Consumidor - Nacionales - ABC Color

INE avanza con la Encuesta de Índice de Confianza del Consumidor - Nacionales - ABC Color

The National Institute of Statistics in Paraguay conducts a field survey to construct the Consumer Confidence Index. This effort aims to characterize the population socioeconomically, thereby updating the sample base for future statistical estimates through efficient technology. The initiative is relevant to open data because it generates confidential, structured primary data, which are fundamental to public transparency. By protecting citizens' identities through QR codes and legal frameworks, the quality of macroeconomic indicators is ensured. Once processed, these data facilitate access to accurate information about the economic climate. This enables researchers and citizens to analyze consumption trends, promoting a more informed society grounded in official statistical evidence.
