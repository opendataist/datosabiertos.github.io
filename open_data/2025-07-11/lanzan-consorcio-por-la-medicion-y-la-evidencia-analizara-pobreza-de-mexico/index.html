Lanzan Consorcio por la medición y la evidencia… analizará pobreza de México

Lanzan Consorcio por la medición y la evidencia… analizará pobreza de México

Six research organizations have launched the "Consortium for Measurement and Evidence" to collaborate with INEGI in analyzing national poverty levels. This initiative aims to provide transparent, rigorous social policy evaluations using innovative methodologies. The group intends to publish alternative studies shortly after official government reports, seeking to complement rather than replace state agencies. Their primary goal is to ensure that data on poverty and social welfare remains robust, independent, and accessible for public scrutiny. The launch addresses concerns about institutional voids following the dissolution of Coneval. While acknowledging INEGI’s technical autonomy, the consortium emphasizes the need to separate technical analysis from political narratives. They aim to safeguard against potential data manipulation or understatement of poverty figures. By maintaining constructive dialogue with public entities, they seek to preserve the continuity of rigorous poverty measurement, ensuring that millions living in precarious conditions remain visible in official statistics and policy discussions. This development is highly relevant to open data as it strengthens the ecosystem of independent verification and multi-source data analysis. It promotes the principle that multiple perspectives can enhance the credibility and utility of public statistics. By encouraging continuous feedback and methodological innovation among diverse academic and civil society actors, the consortium supports a more dynamic, transparent, and accountable data landscape. This collaborative approach ensures that open data serves not just as raw information, but as a tool for robust democratic oversight and informed public debate.
