Hugging Face opens up orders for its Reachy Mini desktop robots

Hugging Face opens up orders for its Reachy Mini desktop robots

Hugging Face has begun accepting orders for its Reachy Mini desktop robots, offering developers accessible, open-source hardware for testing and building AI applications. Available in wireless and wired versions, these kits are designed to be fully programmable in Python, empowering users to modify and extend the robots’ capabilities rather than relying on closed proprietary systems. The core philosophy behind this release is community-driven development and transparency. By integrating the robots with the Hugging Face Hub, users gain access to a vast ecosystem of models and datasets, enabling them to create and share new features openly. This approach aims to unleash collective creativity, allowing builders to customize devices for specific needs and contribute back to a shared repository of innovations that anyone can utilize. This initiative is highly relevant to the open data movement because it champions the democratization of robotics technology. It argues against centralized control by advocating for hardware that users fully understand and own, preventing a future where domestic robots are controlled by a few opaque entities. Ultimately, it promotes an ecosystem where data, code, and physical devices remain transparent, modifiable, and accessible to the broader public rather than being restricted by corporate monopolies.
