The Northern Ireland Road Network and Condition Statistics 2023-24 report has published

This report reveals a robust road network in Northern Ireland, with the vast majority of major roads maintaining good condition and long structural lifespans. Significant investment has ensured efficient repair of surface defects, reflecting effective maintenance strategies that prioritize infrastructure longevity and safety for commuters. The data highlights rising expenditures on structural maintenance and new construction, signaling ongoing commitment to network improvement. This financial trend underscores the balance between preserving existing assets and expanding capacity, crucial for sustainable regional development and economic connectivity across the province. Such detailed, standardized metrics are vital for open data initiatives, enabling transparent accountability in public infrastructure management. Researchers and citizens can leverage these statistics to analyze funding allocation efficacy and service quality, fostering informed public debate and evidence-based policy decisions regarding future transportation investments.
