Open Data
ES EN

The Biodiversity Heritage Library has partnered with Amazon Web Services to host its extensive collection of open biodiversity data on the cloud. This strategic migration ensures that over fifty years of scientific literature, images, and metadata are not only preserved but are made significantly more accessible to the global research community. By leveraging AWS infrastructure, the library addresses critical technical challenges related to scalability, performance, and cost-efficiency, ensuring stable long-term access for users regardless of their location or bandwidth limitations. This move underscores the growing importance of open, centralized data repositories in addressing complex environmental challenges. By making historical biodiversity records freely available, the initiative empowers scientists to conduct longitudinal studies, track species distribution changes, and establish critical baselines for conservation efforts. The enhanced computational capabilities provided by the cloud environment facilitate faster analysis of vast datasets, enabling researchers to derive actionable insights from centuries of biological records to inform modern environmental policy and mitigate extinction risks. This development is highly relevant to open data principles as it exemplifies how institutional partnerships can remove barriers to knowledge sharing. By removing geographic and technical constraints, the project democratizes access to vital scientific heritage, fostering cross-disciplinary innovation and collaboration. It demonstrates that making high-value datasets openly accessible on robust, shared platforms is essential for advancing global scientific understanding and supporting universal bioliteracy in the face of contemporary ecological crises.
