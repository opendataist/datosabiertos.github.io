España roza los 49 millones de habitantes, máximo histórico, debido a la migración

España roza los 49 millones de habitantes, máximo histórico, debido a la migración

Spain’s population reached an all-time high, driven primarily by immigration from countries such as Colombia, Morocco, and Venezuela. This surge in foreign residents significantly outpaced the slight decline in native-born citizens, highlighting a demographic shift reliant on international mobility for growth. Geographic distribution reveals that autonomous communities such as Madrid, Valencia, and Catalonia absorbed the largest share of this influx. These regions experienced growth rates exceeding the national average, indicating that economic opportunities and specific regional attractions continue to draw migrants toward these urban centers. This data is crucial for open-data initiatives, as it illustrates the necessity of transparent, granular demographic statistics. By making such detailed migration flows public, policymakers and researchers can better understand integration challenges and resource allocation needs. Ultimately, accessible population data empowers society to analyze the socio-economic impacts of immigration with greater precision and accountability.
