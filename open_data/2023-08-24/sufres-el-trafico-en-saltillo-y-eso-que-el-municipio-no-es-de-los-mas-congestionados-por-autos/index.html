¿Sufres el tráfico en Saltillo? Y eso que el municipio no es de los más congestionados por autos

The article reveals that, despite perceptions of increasing traffic, Saltillo maintains a remarkably low vehicle-to-population ratio, ranking fourth among major Mexican cities. This data challenges common assumptions about congestion, highlighting a disparity between subjective experience and objective mobility statistics. These findings underscore the importance of open data in urban planning and public discourse. By making official statistics accessible, authorities can correct misinformation and foster evidence-based debates regarding infrastructure needs and transportation policies. Ultimately, transparent data availability enables citizens to understand their city’s true mobility landscape. This transparency is crucial for developing sustainable solutions that address actual usage patterns rather than perceived problems, thereby enhancing civic engagement and policy effectiveness.
