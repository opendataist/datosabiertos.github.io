Open Government Partnership (OGP) : le Comité Technique OGP Côte d’Ivoire lance les consultations publiques 2023 depuis la ville de Dabakala - Abidjan.net Photos

Côte d’Ivoire initiated public consultations to develop an open government partnership action plan, reinforcing democratic participation and transparency. This initiative aligns with international commitments to governance reform and citizen engagement. For open data, it signifies a national push toward institutionalizing accountability and accessible information.
