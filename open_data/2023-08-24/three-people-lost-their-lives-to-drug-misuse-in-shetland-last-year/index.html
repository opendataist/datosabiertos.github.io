Three people lost their lives to drug misuse in Shetland last year

Three people lost their lives to drug misuse in Shetland last year

Scotland’s drug misuse deaths declined significantly in 2022, though the nation still reports the highest rates in Europe. This data underscores a critical public health disparity, where individuals in deprived areas face drastically higher risks compared to their affluent counterparts. Gender differences also emerged, with males experiencing a sharper decline than females despite historically higher mortality rates. The statistical scope strictly defines these deaths as accidental poisonings involving controlled substances, excluding indirect causes or chronic conditions. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives aiming to visualize socioeconomic health inequalities. By providing granular, localized statistics, it enables researchers and policymakers to track progress and allocate resources effectively to the most affected communities across Scotland.
