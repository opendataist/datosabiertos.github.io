Reservas de petróleo caen más de lo esperado en Estados Unidos

U.S. commercial petroleum reserves declined significantly, driven by robust refining activity and crude oil exports, while gasoline stocks unexpectedly increased due to peak summer production. This divergence underscores the complex interplay between domestic processing capacity and global supply fluctuations, revealing strong demand pressures even as prices adjusted slightly. The report is relevant to open data because it relies on transparent, publicly released statistics from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Such accessible data enables independent verification and global market analysis, demonstrating how open government information supports economic transparency and informed decision-making in the energy sector.
