Over 1.7 mn Australians sexually harassed in 2021-22: Survey

New ABS data reveals widespread sexual harassment and physical violence in Australia, highlighting urgent societal safety concerns. These insights are crucial for open_data initiatives, as they underscore the necessity for transparent, accessible, and comprehensive public statistics to drive informed policy and advocacy. The findings demonstrate that harassment and violence disproportionately affect women, with most perpetrators being men known to the victims. This pattern emphasizes the critical need for robust, anonymized datasets that accurately capture perpetrator-victim relationships to understand risk factors and protect vulnerable populations effectively. Furthermore, the significant mental health aftermath of these incidents reveals deep societal impacts that are often invisible without detailed statistical tracking. Open data plays a vital role here by enabling researchers and policymakers to correlate safety incidents with health outcomes, fostering a more responsive and empathetic public service infrastructure.
