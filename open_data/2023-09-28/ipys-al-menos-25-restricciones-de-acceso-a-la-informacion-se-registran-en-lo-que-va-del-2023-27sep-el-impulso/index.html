▷ IPYS: Al menos 25 restricciones de acceso a la información se registran en lo que va del 2023 #27Sep - El Impulso

Venezuela suffers from a deeply entrenched culture of secrecy regarding public data, severely hindering the right to access information. This systemic opacity creates a significant barrier to transparency, preventing citizens and the press from obtaining essential official records and fostering an environment where accountability is nearly impossible to achieve. The persistent refusal of state officials to share information and the obstruction of journalists have actively restricted media operations and limited public knowledge. These ongoing violations demonstrate how information control impacts democratic engagement, illustrating that without open data, societies cannot effectively monitor governance or hold power structures accountable. This situation is critically relevant to open data principles, which advocate for proactive disclosure and accessibility of public records. The article highlights a direct conflict between national practices and global standards for transparency, emphasizing that true open data requires dismantling institutional barriers to ensure information is free, accessible, and usable for all stakeholders.
