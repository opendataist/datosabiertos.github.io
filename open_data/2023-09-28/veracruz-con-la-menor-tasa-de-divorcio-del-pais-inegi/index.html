Veracruz con la menor tasa de divorcio del País: INEGI

Veracruz maintains the lowest divorce rate in Mexico, highlighting regional variations in marital stability. This consistently low incidence contrasts sharply with national averages and with states such as Campeche or Sinaloa, which report significantly higher separation rates. The data underscore how local social norms and economic factors influence family structure differently across the country. Conversely, the region experienced a substantial increase in marriages, becoming the second highest in the nation. This surge indicates a strong cultural commitment to marriage, despite the low dissolution rates. The disparity between high marriage volumes and low divorce rates suggests that couples in Veracruz are highly stable once married, challenging broader national trends of rising separations. This information is vital for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how granular, standardized demographic statistics can reveal nuanced societal behaviors. By making these specific rates publicly accessible, policymakers and researchers can better analyze regional health, social services, and legal needs. Transparency in such data fosters evidence-based decision-making and enables precise comparisons of social trends across different Mexican states.
