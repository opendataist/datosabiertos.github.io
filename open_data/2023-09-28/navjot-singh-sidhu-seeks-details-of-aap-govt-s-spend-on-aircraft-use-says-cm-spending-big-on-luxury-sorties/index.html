Navjot Singh Sidhu seeks details of AAP govt's spend on aircraft use, says CM spending big on 'luxury sorties'

Navjot Singh Sidhu has utilized the Right to Information Act to demand transparency regarding the Punjab government's use of state helicopters and hired aircraft for election campaigning in other states. He argues that this expenditure represents an improper utilization of public funds, labeling it as spending on luxury sorties that contribute to the state’s growing financial burden. The request highlights the critical role of open data in enabling political oversight and accountability. By seeking specific details on flight purposes, costs, and frequency, Sidhu demonstrates how access to government spending records allows citizens to monitor whether resources are being allocated for public service or partisan political gains. This incident underscores the necessity of proactive transparency in governance. It illustrates how open data mechanisms serve as essential tools for detecting potential misuse of state assets, ensuring that financial decisions remain subject to public scrutiny and that taxpayer money is managed responsibly rather than being diverted for private or political advantage.
