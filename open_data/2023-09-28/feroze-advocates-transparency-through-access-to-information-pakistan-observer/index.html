Feroze advocates transparency through access to information - Pakistan Observer

The article highlights the critical link between government transparency and public accountability, asserting that informed citizens are essential for evaluating performance. By prioritizing proactive information disclosure, authorities can foster meritocracy and restore trust among the youth, particularly in the wake of recent institutional failures. This relevance to open data lies in the demonstration of effective implementation mechanisms. The rapid resolution of thousands of information requests showcases how robust frameworks empower the public, proving that accessible data is not just a right but a functional tool for democratic oversight and good governance. Ultimately, the narrative underscores that open data initiatives drive societal confidence. When citizens can easily access records and demand answers, it creates a culture of responsibility, ensuring that public institutions remain answerable and that resources are allocated based on merit rather than opacity or favoritism.
