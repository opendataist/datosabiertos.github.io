Economía de México cae en tercer trimestre de 2025: Inegi

Mexico’s economy contracted in the third quarter, primarily driven by a significant decline in the industrial sector. Although agriculture and services showed positive growth, these gains were insufficient to offset the industrial downturn, resulting in an overall economic slowdown. This data highlights the uneven recovery across different economic activities. The weakness in manufacturing and construction contrasts sharply with the resilience of primary sectors, illustrating the specific structural challenges facing the country’s industrial base compared to its agricultural and service capabilities. This article is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates the utility of freely accessible official statistics from the INEGI. These transparent, timely, and detailed datasets allow researchers and citizens to independently analyze economic trends, verify preliminary estimates, and understand the nuanced performance of various sectors without relying on secondary interpretations.
