La AEPD aclara que quien adquiere una baliza V16 "no tiene que dar sus datos personales a ninguna administración" - LegalToday

La AEPD aclara que quien adquiere una baliza V16 "no tiene que dar sus datos personales a ninguna administración" - LegalToday

The Spanish Data Protection Agency clarifies that purchasing a V16 emergency beacon does not require providing personal data to authorities, ensuring that the Traffic Agency remains unaware of who bought the device. This reassurance addresses public concerns regarding privacy and potential surveillance associated with the new mandatory safety equipment. Technically, the device transmits only its unique technical identifier and the vehicle’s location upon activation, without linking this information to the driver’s identity or license plate. It ceases transmission immediately when deactivated, preventing continuous monitoring or the creation of movement histories, thereby protecting driver privacy while fulfilling its safety function. This clarification is crucial for open data discourse as it establishes a precedent for designing IoT devices in public services that minimize data collection. By strictly limiting data to essential safety information and avoiding personal linkage, the regulation demonstrates how technological mandates can coexist with fundamental privacy rights, setting a benchmark for ethical data handling in smart infrastructure projects.
