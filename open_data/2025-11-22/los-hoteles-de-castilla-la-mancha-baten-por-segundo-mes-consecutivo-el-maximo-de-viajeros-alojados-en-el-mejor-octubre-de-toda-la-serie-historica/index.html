Los hoteles de Castilla-La Mancha baten por segundo mes consecutivo el máximo de viajeros alojados en el mejor octubre de toda la serie histórica

Los hoteles de Castilla-La Mancha baten por segundo mes consecutivo el máximo de viajeros alojados en el mejor octubre de toda la serie histórica

Castilla-La Mancha has set a new historical record in hotel accommodation during October, consolidating a continuous growth trajectory that includes peaks in the number of guests, overnight stays, occupancy rates, and profitability. This expansion has occurred even without the additional advantage of holiday bridge days, highlighting the intrinsic robustness of the region’s tourism sector in the face of external factors. The region ranks among the national leaders in growth rate for the first ten months of the year, driven by a significant increase in available bed capacity and sustained demand. The province of Guadalajara leads the country in overnight stays, while other provinces, such as Albacete, also show growth rates well above the national average, indicating a successful diversification of the region’s tourist appeal. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how public statistical series, such as those published by the National Statistics Institute (INE), enable real-time monitoring of the economic health of key sectors. Access to these indicators facilitates the analysis of long-term trends and the identification of regions with specific dynamics, which are essential for evidence-based decision-making and transparency in tourism management.
