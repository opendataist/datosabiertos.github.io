Aumentan muertes de bebés y madres en Costa Rica

Aumentan muertes de bebés y madres en Costa Rica

Costa Rica experienced a worrying increase in infant and maternal mortality rates in 2024, reversing previous downward trends. This deterioration signals critical vulnerabilities in the public health system, particularly in neonatal care and maternal support, highlighting an urgent need for improved preventive strategies and greater healthcare accessibility to protect vulnerable populations. At the same time, the country is facing ultra-low fertility rates alongside overall increases in mortality, driven by an aging population. This demographic shift underscores the complex interplay between declining birth rates and rising death tolls, suggesting that traditional health metrics must evolve to address the specific needs of a changing societal structure, rather than focusing solely on raw volume. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it exemplifies the necessity of transparent, accessible, and longitudinal demographic statistics. By making such granular insights available, societies can foster evidence-based policymaking, enabling researchers and citizens to analyze systemic health failures, monitor inequality, and hold institutions accountable for improving public welfare outcomes.
