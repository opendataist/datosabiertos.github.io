Las ventas minoristas de Reino Unido sorprenden negativamente al caer un 1,1% en octubre

The United Kingdom experienced an unexpected drop in retail sales, marking the first significant decline in several months. This broad-based downturn affects supermarkets, clothing stores, and online commerce, signaling a possible slowdown in consumer spending. Retailers attribute part of this reduction to precautionary saving ahead of Black Friday deals, although the phenomenon spans all major categories. The central concern lies in the psychological climate of consumers, who are reluctant to spend due to uncertainty about the government’s economic policy. Although there was a natural correction following a period of sustained growth, the withdrawal of confidence suggests that households are seeking to protect themselves against what they perceive as adverse fiscal measures. This reflects a long-term moderation in spending habits and widespread caution.
