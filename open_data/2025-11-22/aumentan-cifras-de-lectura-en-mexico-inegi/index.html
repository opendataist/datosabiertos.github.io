Aumentan cifras de lectura en México: Inegi

Aumentan cifras de lectura en México: Inegi

The INEGI’s methodological shift in Mexico has significantly expanded the definition of reading to include digital content and broadened its demographic scope. By integrating social media, expanding the age range to adolescents, and achieving nationwide coverage, the agency captures a more comprehensive picture of reading habits than previous limited urban samples allowed. This approach reveals a higher prevalence of readers, driven largely by young people engaging with informational content online. Although critics argue that this metric inflates traditional literacy rates by equating social media consumption with book reading, the new framework ensures that evolving cultural behaviors are accurately recorded rather than ignored. This update is crucial for open data as it demonstrates how adaptable statistical definitions can better reflect contemporary society. It highlights the importance of transparent methodology changes that balance historical comparability with modern realities, ensuring public data remains relevant and inclusive of diverse information consumption trends.
