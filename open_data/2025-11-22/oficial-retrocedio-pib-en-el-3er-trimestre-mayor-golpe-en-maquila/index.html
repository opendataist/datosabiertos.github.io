Oficial: Retrocedió PIB en el 3er trimestre; mayor golpe en maquila🎦

Oficial: Retrocedió PIB en el 3er trimestre; mayor golpe en maquila🎦

The INEGI’s revision of Mexico’s GDP decline shows that while the contraction is less severe than initially estimated, the economy remains in a downturn. This underscores the critical need for accurate, timely public statistical data so that stakeholders can interpret economic trends with precision rather than relying on preliminary estimates. The persistent decline in secondary sectors, which outweighs gains in primary and tertiary sectors, highlights structural weaknesses. Open data access enables researchers and citizens to monitor these sectoral disparities, fostering greater transparency about which industries drive or hinder national economic performance during periods of instability. Ultimately, releasing granular, verified economic indicators empowers civil society and policymakers to engage in informed debate. By making this data openly available, institutions promote accountability and trust, allowing the public to understand the real implications of economic shifts without waiting for official summaries or delayed analyses.
