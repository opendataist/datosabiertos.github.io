México registra un aumento del 8.8% en ingresos por turismo internacional

Mexico’s tourism sector showed a strong recovery in early 2024, significantly surpassing pre-pandemic benchmarks. International arrivals and expenditures surged, indicating robust global demand and effective recovery strategies that substantially exceeded 2019 levels. This growth reflects a resilient economic sector that quickly regained momentum following previous disruptions. The data highlight substantial increases in both visitor volume and per-capita spending, particularly among air travelers. Cruise tourism also demonstrated notable expansion in passenger numbers and revenue. These trends suggest a diverse and thriving industry that attracts varied tourist demographics with higher spending power than before. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistics from INEGI and Sectur, which are publicly accessible datasets. Openly sharing such metrics enables researchers, policymakers, and businesses to analyze economic trends transparently. Access to raw data fosters accountability and supports evidence-based decision-making regarding national economic health and tourism infrastructure development.
