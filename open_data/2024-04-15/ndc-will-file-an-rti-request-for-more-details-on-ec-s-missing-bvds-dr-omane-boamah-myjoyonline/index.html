NDC will file an RTI request for more details on EC's missing BVDs - Dr Omane Boamah - MyJoyOnline

The National Democratic Congress is pursuing formal transparency measures to uncover the truth regarding missing electoral hardware, asserting that the Electoral Commission lacks commitment to open communication. By filing a Right to Information request, the opposition seeks to expose hidden details about stolen Biometric Voter Registration devices and laptops, challenging the Commission’s claims that these losses do not threaten election integrity. This action underscores a critical demand for accountability in managing state resources and protecting the foundational tools of democratic participation. The core dispute revolves around conflicting assessments of risk, with the opposition arguing that missing verification equipment poses a genuine threat to the upcoming general elections. While electoral officials dismiss the issue as harmless, insisting that no sensitive data was compromised, political leaders maintain that the concealment of facts erodes public trust. This divergence highlights the essential role of independent audits and rigorous oversight in ensuring that electoral processes remain transparent and immune to potential manipulation or interference. This situation is particularly relevant to open data advocacy as it demonstrates how opaque management of electoral technology can fuel political instability and public suspicion. It emphasizes the necessity for electoral bodies to proactively share information about logistical security rather than relying on dismissive assurances. Ensuring that stakeholders have access to verifiable facts about the status and security of voting infrastructure is vital for maintaining confidence in the fairness and reliability of the democratic system.
