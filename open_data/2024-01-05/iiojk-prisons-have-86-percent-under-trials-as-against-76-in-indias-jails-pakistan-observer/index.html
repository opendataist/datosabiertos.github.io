IIOJK prisons have 86 percent under trials as against 76 % in India’s jails - Pakistan Observer

The article reveals a stark disparity in India’s justice system, where marginalized communities and specific regions face disproportionately high rates of pre-trial detention. This data highlights how systemic biases, including caste prejudices and indiscriminate policing, exacerbate inequality within the penal system. Such statistics demonstrate the critical importance of open data in exposing structural injustices that remain hidden behind official narratives. By providing transparent, granular demographic and confinement details, accessible datasets enable researchers and activists to identify patterns of discrimination and hold institutions accountable. Ultimately, this report underscores the necessity of open data as a tool for social justice. It empowers civil society to demand legal reforms and equitable treatment, proving that transparent information is essential for addressing the root causes of mass pre-trial incarceration and protecting the rights of vulnerable populations.
