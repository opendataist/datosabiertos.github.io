New FOIA'ed data reveal NY vaccine clinics called ambulances to be...

This article highlights the critical role of Freedom of Information Laws in uncovering adverse event reporting gaps, demonstrating how public data requests can expose systemic issues hidden from official channels. By obtaining EMS dispatch records, the author reveals that healthcare workers at vaccination sites were so concerned about potential severe reactions that they preemptively requested ambulance standby teams. This finding suggests a disconnect between official safety narratives and on-the-ground operational realities, raising questions about the transparency of vaccine safety monitoring systems. The data indicates a significant burden on emergency services, with numerous calls citing direct adverse reactions such as seizures, cardiac distress, and loss of consciousness immediately following vaccination. These specific, timestamped records provide concrete evidence of high-risk events that may not be fully captured in passive reporting databases like V-Safe. The presence of these incidents across various demographics challenges the prevailing narrative of universal safety and underscores the necessity of scrutinizing real-time emergency response data to understand the true scope of vaccine-related harms. For the open data community, this case illustrates the power of FOIL/FOIA requests as essential tools for accountability and truth-seeking. It emphasizes that published datasets often represent only a fraction of actual events, making active data retrieval crucial for comprehensive analysis. The ability to access raw, unfiltered government records allows independent researchers to verify claims, challenge official statistics, and provide the public with a more accurate understanding of public health interventions and their potential risks.
