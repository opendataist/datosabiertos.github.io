Open Data
ES EN

Proactive transparency goes beyond basic legal obligations by identifying and publishing information that directly responds to citizens’ demands. This strategy encourages active societal participation in addressing public issues, prompting institutions to generate data of general utility. By prioritizing what benefits the majority, it fosters a more effective culture of accountability and reduces informational gaps between the government and the public. The Transparency Institute of the State of Mexico has implemented awareness-raising campaigns to promote this practice, highlighting its distinction from mere disclosure of information in the public interest. The goal is to optimize decision-making and streamline services through the adoption of open data. By providing concrete benchmarks and success stories, the Institute trains obligated entities to replicate these methodologies, sustainably improving the quality and quantity of information available in accessible formats. This approach is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how government initiatives can evolve toward useful and socially valuable interoperability. Examples such as interactive complaint maps or electoral participation platforms illustrate how technology facilitates democratic access. By recognizing these practices, a standard is established that encourages other institutions to publish data not merely for compliance, but for their potential to enhance governance and citizen trust through digital innovation.
