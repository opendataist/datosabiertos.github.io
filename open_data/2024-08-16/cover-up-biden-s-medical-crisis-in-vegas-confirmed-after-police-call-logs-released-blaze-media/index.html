COVER-UP: Biden's medical crisis in Vegas CONFIRMED after police call logs released | Blaze Media

Newly released police call logs confirm that President Biden experienced a medical emergency in Nevada, contradicting initial White House claims of mild illness. These documents reveal urgent emergency protocols, including road clearances and code "421" radio transmissions, which substantiate assertions that the situation was far more serious than publicly admitted. This revelation highlights significant discrepancies between official narratives and verified operational records, suggesting a deliberate concealment of the event’s severity. By dismissing early reports as false, the administration obscured the truth, raising questions about transparency and the reliability of official communications during crises. For the open data community, this case underscores the critical role of FOIA requests in holding power accountable. It demonstrates how accessible public records can dismantle misleading narratives, proving that transparency mechanisms are essential tools for verifying facts and ensuring governmental integrity when information is contested.
