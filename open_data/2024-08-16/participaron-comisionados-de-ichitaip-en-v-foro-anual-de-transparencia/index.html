The article emphasizes that access to public information strengthens citizen participation and enhances government transparency. However, the event highlighted significant risks associated with digital technology and social media platforms. Although these services appear to be free, they often collect personal data that remains vulnerable to misuse. Experts warned of dangers such as cyberbullying, fraud, and data trafficking, which can lead to serious crimes. This topic is relevant to open data because it underscores the need to balance transparency with privacy protection. While open data promotes accountability, it must coexist with robust safeguards for personal information. Strengthening digital literacy and updating legislation are vital to ensuring that the benefits of accessible information do not compromise individual security in an interconnected world.
