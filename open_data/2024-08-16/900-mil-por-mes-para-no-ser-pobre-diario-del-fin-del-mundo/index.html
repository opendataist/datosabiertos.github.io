$900 mil por mes para no ser pobre | Diario del Fin del Mundo

The basic basket data for July reveals persistent inflationary pressure, with the cost of essential goods rising while remaining below the overall inflation rate. This distinction highlights the evolving dynamics of household financial strain, indicating that although the cost of living continues to escalate, its trajectory is decoupling from broader price indices. Such divergence is critical for understanding real-term purchasing power and the erosion of social stability. The figures underscore the severity of poverty and extreme poverty, defining thresholds that families must meet to secure basic necessities or merely survive. These metrics provide a concrete framework for assessing social vulnerability, showing how economic pressures directly impact fundamental human needs. Consequently, they serve as a vital indicator of social welfare and the urgency of targeted economic interventions. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistical indicators empower public scrutiny of socio-economic conditions. By making these benchmarks available, authorities enable researchers and citizens to monitor inequality and demand accountability. Open access to such granular data fosters informed civic engagement and supports evidence-based policy formulation aimed at reducing economic disparities.
