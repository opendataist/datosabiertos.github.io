Los salarios le volvieron a ganar a la inflación en junio

The wage index reveals that while recent short-term gains outpace inflation, long-term trends show significant erosion of workers' purchasing power, particularly for those in the informal sector. This disparity highlights a critical vulnerability within the labor market, where unregistered employees experience the greatest decline in real income compared to their formal-sector counterparts over a twelve-month period. The data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for transparent, accessible statistical sources to monitor economic inequality and inform policy decisions on labor protections and social welfare.
