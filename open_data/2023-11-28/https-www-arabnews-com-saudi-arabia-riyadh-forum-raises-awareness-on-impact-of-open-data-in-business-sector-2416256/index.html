Open Data
ES EN

The Saudi Data Forum highlights the strategic integration of data and artificial intelligence as fundamental drivers for realizing Vision 2030. By prioritizing the responsible use of open data, Saudi authorities aim to seamlessly digitize all aspects of life, moving beyond economic diversification to enhance the overall quality of life. This approach positions data as a critical catalyst for fulfilling national ambitions and fostering a modern, tech-integrated society. Central to this transformation is a strong commitment to government transparency and effective data governance. Collaboration between key entities like SDAIA and the National Transformation Program focuses on publishing high-quality, accessible datasets to combat corruption and improve institutional accountability. The significant expansion of the National Open Data Platform demonstrates tangible progress, with substantial increases in available data packages and user engagement reflecting a growing ecosystem of open information. Furthermore, the development of robust data infrastructure, including specialized platforms for integration, analytics, and cataloging, supports the creation of a data-driven digital economy. These initiatives are designed to enhance data sharing, improve quality, and build public trust in data utilization. Ultimately, these efforts aim to position Saudi Arabia as a global leader in data and AI, ensuring that resources are utilized effectively across both public and private sectors to drive sustainable innovation and economic growth. This article is relevant to open_data as it illustrates a national strategy where open data is not just a technical standard but a core pillar of governance, economic policy, and societal trust.
