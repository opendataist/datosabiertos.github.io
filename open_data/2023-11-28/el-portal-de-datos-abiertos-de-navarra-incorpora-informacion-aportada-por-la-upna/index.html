El portal de Datos Abiertos de Navarra incorpora información aportada por la UPNA

The Government of Navarre and the Public University of Navarre have established a strategic collaboration to integrate university data into the region’s Open Data Portal. This agreement aims to strengthen the principle of transparency by ensuring that information generated by the academic institution is accessible, reusable, and free from technical or legal barriers. By facilitating access to information on human resources, academic activities, and economic aspects, it promotes a culture of openness that enables citizens, businesses, and researchers to leverage these resources for commercial, educational, or academic purposes, in line with international open data guidelines. This initiative is crucial for the open data ecosystem because it demonstrates the feasibility of inter-institutional collaboration to expand the catalog of available data. By using open-source tools such as CKAN, the technological infrastructure is ensured to be robust, standardized, and scalable, allowing for the automation of data publication and data federation. This not only improves the operational efficiency of the participating entities but also establishes a reproducible model where public administration and the academic sector work together to enrich the digital common good, ensuring that data are understandable and useful for various user profiles. The relevance of this agreement for open data lies in its potential to transform information governance in Navarre. By incorporating diverse sources such as the university, the portal ceases to be merely a governmental repository and becomes a true territorial hub for data. This diversification enriches the context of the available information, enabling more comprehensive and cross-cutting analyses. Furthermore, by formalizing commitments to maintenance and updates through monitoring committees, the long-term sustainability and quality of the data are guaranteed, fostering public trust in the use and reuse of public information.
