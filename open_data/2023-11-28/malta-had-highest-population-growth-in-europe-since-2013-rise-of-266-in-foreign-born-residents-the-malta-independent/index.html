Malta had highest population growth in Europe since 2013, rise of 266% in foreign-born residents - The Malta Independent

Malta has experienced unprecedented population growth over the last decade, significantly outpacing other European nations. This rapid expansion, driven largely by a surge in foreign-born residents, has intensified debates regarding sustainable urban planning and economic policy. The data highlights a stark contrast between small states facing demographic booms and larger countries with more stable or declining populations, illustrating divergent demographic trends across the continent. The influx of migrants has reshaped Malta’s social fabric, with third-country nationals comprising the majority of new arrivals. This shift has prompted political discourse on moving from a quantity-focused economy to one prioritizing quality. Projections suggest that maintaining current economic growth requires further population expansion, raising questions about infrastructure capacity and long-term national strategy. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on Eurostat’s official statistics to reveal macro-level demographic shifts. By analyzing accessible, standardized datasets, stakeholders can identify critical societal changes such as migration patterns and age distributions. Transparent access to such data enables informed decision-making, allowing governments and researchers to address the complex challenges posed by rapid urbanization and demographic change effectively.
