Daniela, Pablo y Alma: estos son los nombres más comunes de recién nacidos en Murcia en el último año

This article illustrates the public availability of granular demographic data, highlighting how local authorities, such as Murcia’s civil registry, publish detailed records of births. By making specific naming trends accessible, it demonstrates a commitment to transparency in government statistics, allowing citizens to observe cultural patterns without restriction. The report reveals that while national preferences vary, regional specifics like Hugo and Lucía dominate local trends. This granularity encourages public scrutiny and community engagement, proving that open data initiatives can transform raw bureaucratic figures into meaningful insights for societal analysis and historical record-keeping. Ultimately, this example is relevant to open data because it shows how releasing detailed administrative records empowers researchers and citizens. It underscores the value of accessible, structured information in fostering a deeper understanding of regional identity and demographic shifts through public participation.
