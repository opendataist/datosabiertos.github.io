Freedom of Information

The Society of Environmental Journalists established a task force to champion open information rights for environmental reporting. By providing essential tools, legal guidance, and advocacy positions, they empower journalists to effectively navigate freedom of information laws. This resource base is vital for open data, as it democratizes access to environmental records, ensuring transparency and supporting informed public discourse through robust investigative journalism.
