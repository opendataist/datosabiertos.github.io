From synthetic data to AI agents, new SAS Viya innovations boost speed, productivity and trust

From synthetic data to AI agents, new SAS Viya innovations boost speed, productivity and trust

SAS has expanded its Viya platform with new AI tools designed to streamline the entire artificial intelligence lifecycle, from data preparation to decision deployment. By integrating features like synthetic data generation, intelligent decisioning agents, and an AI-powered copilot, the company aims to reduce the technical barriers to entry while enhancing productivity for diverse user personas. This evolution reflects a strategic shift toward supporting a broader ecosystem of preferences and technologies within enterprise environments, ensuring that organizations can automate processes and make faster, better-informed business decisions. The relevance to open data lies in the platform’s approach to data accessibility and utility. SAS Data Maker addresses critical challenges of data privacy and scarcity by generating secure synthetic datasets, which facilitates data sharing and collaboration without compromising sensitive information. By enabling organizations to create representative data models, such tools lower the friction associated with data availability, encouraging more extensive analysis and innovation across sectors where real-world data might be limited or restricted due to privacy concerns. Ultimately, these advancements democratize access to advanced analytics, empowering users of all skill levels to participate effectively in the data and AI process. The successful application of these tools in global conservation efforts, such as protecting endangered whale populations, demonstrates how robust data platforms can drive tangible social impact. This highlights the broader importance of sophisticated data infrastructure in solving complex, real-world problems beyond traditional commercial applications, reinforcing the value of accessible, high-quality data ecosystems.
