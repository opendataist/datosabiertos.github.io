Luxembourg’s Population Reaches 681,973, Fertility Rate Remains Low

Luxembourg’s Population Reaches 681,973, Fertility Rate Remains Low

Luxembourg’s 2025 demographic data reveals a nation where population growth is increasingly driven by migration rather than natural increase, highlighting the critical role of international mobility in sustaining its economy. The significant contribution of foreign residents to the total population underscores how open, accessible data on citizenship and residency trends allows policymakers and researchers to understand the structural shifts in labor markets and social services, which are vital for transparent governance in a highly internationalized society. Simultaneously, the article illustrates a broader European trend of declining fertility and aging populations, yet Luxembourg manages this through robust life expectancy and net positive migration. This context is relevant to the open data community because it demonstrates how detailed, disaggregated statistics—covering age, nationality, and vital events—enable comparative analysis. Such granular public datasets allow experts to model long-term sustainability challenges and evaluate the effectiveness of integration policies across different national contexts. Ultimately, the transparency provided by STATEC empowers civil society and academic institutions to scrutinize the intersection of demography and policy. By making comprehensive records on births, deaths, and nationality acquisition publicly available, Luxembourg sets a benchmark for data sovereignty and accountability. This openness fosters informed public discourse and supports evidence-based decision-making regarding social cohesion, resource allocation, and the future of a diverse, multicultural citizenry.
