Privacy Policy - Thit Htoo Lwin

Privacy Policy - Thit Htoo Lwin

This privacy policy outlines how the website manages user data across various interactions, emphasizing transparency regarding data collection from comments, media uploads, and site navigation. It highlights the potential risks associated with embedded content and image uploads, noting that third-party sites can track visitors and that location data may be extracted from media files. The policy details the retention of comment data indefinitely to streamline moderation and explains the use of cookies for user convenience and login management. It clarifies that while users have control over their profile information, administrators retain access, and data is shared in specific scenarios, such as during password resets, ensuring users are aware of where their information travels. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the standard mechanisms for personal data protection required by regulations like GDPR, which often intersect with open data initiatives. Understanding these boundaries helps clarify that while open data promotes transparency, it must coexist with strict privacy safeguards for individual users, ensuring that the push for accessible information does not compromise personal anonymity or security.
