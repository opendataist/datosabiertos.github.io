Más desempleo durante el 2024 - Diario los Andes

Peru’s labor market exhibits an uneven recovery, characterized by rising male unemployment and a persistence of inadequate jobs below pre-pandemic levels. This underscores critical gaps in formalizing work, particularly for young people, highlighting the need for transparent, granular data to address structural disparities. Open data is essential in this context, as current statistics reveal systemic exclusion. Enhanced accessibility and data disaggregation enable policymakers to design and implement targeted interventions, ensuring that economic growth translates into equitable, high-quality employment opportunities for all demographic groups, rather than merely generating aggregate gains. Ultimately, this case illustrates how open statistical data can expose hidden inequalities. By making employment details visible, society can foster informed debates and accountability, transforming raw data into actionable insights that promote inclusive growth and safeguard vulnerable workers from being left behind in national recovery efforts.
