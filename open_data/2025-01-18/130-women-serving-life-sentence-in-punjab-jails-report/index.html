130 women serving life sentence in Punjab jails: report

130 women serving life sentence in Punjab jails: report

The disclosure of female inmate statistics through freedom of information requests highlights a critical infrastructure gap in Punjab’s prison system. This transparency mechanism enables civil society to expose systemic failures, such as the severe shortage of dedicated women’s jails. Advocates argue that housing women in mixed facilities compromises their safety, privacy, and rehabilitation. The reliance on a single dedicated center forces others into inadequate accommodations, undermining humane treatment standards. This case demonstrates how open data initiatives empower citizens to demand accountability. By forcing the release of hidden operational metrics, such transparency drives judicial and governmental action toward necessary structural reforms for marginalized populations.
