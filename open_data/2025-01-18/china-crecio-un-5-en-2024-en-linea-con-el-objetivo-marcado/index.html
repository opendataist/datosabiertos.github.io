China creció un 5% en 2024, en línea con el objetivo marcado

China’s 2024 economic performance highlights a deliberate structural transition from rapid quantitative expansion to qualitative, high-value growth. The state-guided shift away from reliance on real estate toward advanced manufacturing sectors, such as electric vehicles and semiconductors, has stabilized the economy despite a notable slowdown. This strategic pivot aims to build long-term resilience, indicating that future stability will depend less on massive infrastructure projects and more on technological innovation and export competitiveness. However, significant headwinds threaten sustained momentum, including demographic decline, weak domestic consumption, and the looming impact of potential US trade tariffs. These factors underscore the complexities of managing a mature economy amidst geopolitical tensions and internal social challenges. The moderation in growth rates reflects both the successful maturation of the economic model and the persistent risks associated with deflationary pressures and an aging population, necessitating careful fiscal management to maintain social confidence. This report is highly relevant to open data, as it illustrates how publicly released statistical indicators—such as GDP, trade surplus, and census figures—serve as critical tools for global economic analysis and policy forecasting. Transparent data release enables investors and analysts to monitor structural shifts, assess market risks, and understand the interplay between government strategy and economic outcomes. Reliable, timely data is essential for verifying official narratives against independent assessments, fostering informed decision-making in an increasingly interconnected global market.
