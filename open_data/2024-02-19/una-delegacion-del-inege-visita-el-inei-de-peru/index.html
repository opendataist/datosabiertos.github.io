Una delegación del INEGE visita el INEI de Perú

Equatorial Guinea seeks to strengthen its statistical capacity by collaborating with Peru, leveraging INEI’s extensive experience to refine its National Statistical Development Strategy. This partnership facilitates the exchange of technical knowledge on critical methodologies, including household surveys, economic censuses, and the effective use of administrative records for official data production. The relevance to open data lies in enhancing the quality and accessibility of socioeconomic information. By improving the methodologies used to collect and process statistics, the initiative supports the generation of reliable, transparent datasets. This foundational improvement is essential for creating open, user-friendly public data that can inform policy and foster accountability. Ultimately, this cooperation aims to boost the volume and reliability of Equatorial Guinea’s statistical offerings. A robust, open statistical system ensures that citizens and researchers have access to accurate, timely information. This transparency empowers civil society to engage effectively with government decisions, promoting a culture of evidence-based governance and data-driven public discourse.
