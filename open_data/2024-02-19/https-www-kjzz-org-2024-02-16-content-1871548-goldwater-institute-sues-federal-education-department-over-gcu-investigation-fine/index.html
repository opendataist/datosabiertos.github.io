The Goldwater Institute is suing the Department of Education for records regarding a major fine against Grand Canyon University, seeking proof of potential agency bias. This action highlights the critical role of transparency in ensuring government accountability during high-stakes regulatory enforcement. The lawsuit aims to uncover internal communications to determine if the institution was unfairly targeted. By challenging the validity of the penalty, the institute advocates for procedural fairness in how federal agencies handle financial penalties against educational entities. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the demand for accessible public records to check executive power. It underscores the necessity of proactive information sharing to prevent secret decision-making and maintain trust in administrative justice systems.
