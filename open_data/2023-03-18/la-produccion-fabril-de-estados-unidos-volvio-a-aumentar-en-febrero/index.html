La producción fabril de Estados Unidos volvió a aumentar en febrero

The report reveals that U.S. manufacturing production exceeded expectations in February, suggesting initial economic resilience despite broader uncertainty. This rebound, supported by improved supply chains and orders, indicates that the sector did not collapse as quickly as analysts had feared, providing a brief reprieve amid high borrowing costs and weaknesses in foreign economies. However, long-term prospects remain uncertain due to elevated inventories and sluggish global activity. The decline in capacity utilization and the drop in durable goods production reflect a fragile recovery. These real economic data points are essential for objectively assessing market health, countering political or biased narratives that seek to manipulate public perception of industrial performance. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public and transparent access to accurate economic indicators enables citizens and experts to dismantle authoritarian or propagandistic discourses. The availability of verifiable data fosters critical journalism and accountability, fundamental elements for an informed democracy that does not rely on opaque or distorted official versions of economic reality.
