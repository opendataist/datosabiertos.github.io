La criminalidad sube un 4,8% en 2022: Los robos de coches y los homicidios, los delitos que más crecen - MelillaHoy

La criminalidad sube un 4,8% en 2022: Los robos de coches y los homicidios, los delitos que más crecen - MelillaHoy

Melilla saw a notable rise in overall crime in 2022, consistent with the upward trend observed in most Spanish regions. This increase reverses the significant decline recorded in 2019, yet it highlights a divergent trajectory compared to neighboring Ceuta, where crime rates fell. The data reveal a complex local security landscape in which traditional offenses are becoming more common, even as overall levels remain below pre-pandemic figures in the long term. A critical shift has occurred in the nature of offenses, with a marked surge in serious conventional crimes such as vehicle thefts, homicides, and sexual assaults. Conversely, drug trafficking and certain crimes against sexual freedom have declined substantially. This juxtaposition suggests that while some illicit markets are contracting, violent and property-related crimes are intensifying, indicating a change in the specific behaviors threatening public safety rather than a uniform increase across all forms of illegality. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of transparent, standardized government statistics in identifying nuanced societal trends. By making detailed crime data publicly available, authorities enable researchers and citizens to analyze specific security challenges, such as the rise in cybercrime or the disparity between neighboring territories. Open data thus facilitates evidence-based policy discussions and allows for a deeper understanding of how digital and physical security environments are evolving differently.
