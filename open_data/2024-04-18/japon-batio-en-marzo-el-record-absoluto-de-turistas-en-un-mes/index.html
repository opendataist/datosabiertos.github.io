Japón batió en marzo el récord absoluto de turistas en un mes

Japan achieved a historic milestone by welcoming over three million foreign tourists in a single month, surpassing pre-pandemic levels and signaling a robust structural recovery of global tourism. This surge is driven by favorable currency exchanges, seasonal cherry blossoms, and international holidays, making the country increasingly accessible and attractive to visitors from key markets like the US and Europe. However, this unprecedented influx presents significant sustainability challenges, prompting local authorities to implement restrictive measures. Cities like Kyoto and Mount Fuji are introducing access limits and fees to manage overcrowding and protect cultural and natural heritage, highlighting the tension between economic benefits and environmental preservation. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, real-time tourism metrics. Accurate statistical monitoring is essential for policymakers to balance economic growth with sustainable management, ensuring that data-driven decisions effectively address the impacts of mass tourism on local communities and infrastructure.
