Cómo usar una VPN desde el celular y proteger tus datos personales

The growing exposure to cyber threats and the indiscriminate surveillance of personal data have made digital security a fundamental priority. In this context, the use of virtual private networks (VPNs) has emerged as an essential strategy to ensure privacy and information integrity during online browsing, particularly on mobile devices that frequently connect to vulnerable public networks. This technology not only encrypts data traffic but also conceals the user’s identity, making it more difficult for malicious actors or advertisers to track them. Implementing these tools involves selecting a reliable provider that balances security, speed, and ease of use, followed by basic technical configuration within the mobile application. By establishing an encrypted connection with a remote server, users ensure that their online activities remain anonymous and protected against interception. This streamlined process enables users to maintain control over their data, mitigating the risks associated with the mass collection of personal information by applications and third parties. This discussion is relevant to the field of open data because it highlights the inherent tension between public transparency and the protection of individual privacy. Although open data aims to foster innovation and accountability, its infrastructure must respect end-user security. The warning regarding the indiscriminate collection of data by mobile applications underscores the need for robust regulatory and technical frameworks that protect privacy without hindering the flow of information, ensuring that data openness does not compromise the digital security of citizens.
