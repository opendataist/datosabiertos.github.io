Suplantan a la Agencia Tributaria y simulan una devolución de dinero en la Renta 2023 para robar datos personales y bancarios - Tercera Información

A recent smishing campaign impersonates the Tax Agency, deceiving users into disclosing sensitive banking and personal information through fraudulent refund claims. This underscores the critical importance of verifying digital identities and securing authentication processes against social engineering attacks that exploit trust in official institutions. Users must remain vigilant against unsolicited links requesting confidential information, as these fraudulent websites are designed solely to harvest credentials for financial exploitation. Recognizing these tactics is essential for protecting personal privacy and maintaining trust in digital communications. This issue is highly relevant to open data discussions, as it highlights the risks associated with the widespread availability and potential misuse of personal information. It emphasizes the need for robust cybersecurity education and transparent data protection practices to ensure that openness does not compromise individual security or privacy in the digital age.
