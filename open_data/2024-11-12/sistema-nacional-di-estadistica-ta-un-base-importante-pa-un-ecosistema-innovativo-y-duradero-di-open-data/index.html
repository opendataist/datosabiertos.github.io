Sistema Nacional di Estadistica ta un base importante pa un ecosistema innovativo y duradero di 'Open Data'

Sistema Nacional di Estadistica ta un base importante pa un ecosistema innovativo y duradero di 'Open Data'

Minister Geoffrey Wever stresses that establishing a National Statistical System is crucial for Aruba’s sustainable development and democratic transparency. International research underscores the necessity of such a framework to ensure high-quality, reliable, and impartial data. This system will enhance governmental decision-making, financial oversight, and effective policy evaluation by providing accurate insights into economic, social, and environmental conditions. Currently, Aruba lacks a coordinated mechanism for efficiently compiling and exchanging statistical data. Establishing this system will promote structural collaboration among the various departments and institutions responsible for data collection and analysis. This integration is essential for monitoring policy outcomes and making necessary adjustments, ensuring that all stakeholders, including the public and international entities, have access to practical and relevant statistics. Ultimately, the initiative aims to create an innovative data ecosystem by integrating Open Data principles while respecting confidentiality and legal requirements. This effort involves cooperation between the government, academia, NGOs, and the private sector. It is relevant to open data as it seeks to standardize the publication of public information, ensuring it is accessible, understandable, and useful for fostering innovation and accountability in society.
