Llegada de turistas a México crece 13,5 por ciento en septiembre - Noticias Prensa Latina

Mexico’s tourism sector is showing divergent trends: arrivals at the border are surging, while international hotel stays declined slightly in September. Despite this dip, overall spending per tourist increased, contributing to significant total revenue growth in the first eight months of the year. This data underscores the complexity of tracking diverse visitor streams and highlights the need for granular open data on transportation modes and expenditure to accurately assess economic impacts.
