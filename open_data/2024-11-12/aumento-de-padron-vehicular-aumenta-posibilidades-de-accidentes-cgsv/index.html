Aumento de padrón vehicular aumenta posibilidades de accidentes: CGSV

Aumento de padrón vehicular aumenta posibilidades de accidentes: CGSV

The surge in vehicle registrations has led to significant traffic congestion and a sharp increase in road accidents. Authorities attribute this dangerous trend to the influx of imported cars, which strains infrastructure and endangers pedestrians. This situation underscores the critical role of open data in urban management. Transparent access to registration and accident statistics enables policymakers to identify trends and allocate resources effectively. Open data promotes accountability and public awareness, allowing citizens and officials to collaborate on safety solutions. By making such information accessible, cities can implement evidence-based strategies to mitigate risks and improve overall traffic safety for all residents.
