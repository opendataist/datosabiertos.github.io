En Ecuador la variación del IPC fue -0,24% al cierre de octubre 2024

The Ecuadorian National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INEC) reported a slight deflation in October 2024, highlighting a positive shift in consumer price trends compared to the previous month. This monthly decrease in inflation, coupled with lower annual inflation rates, suggests a stabilization in price levels. These figures indicate that economic pressures on consumer costs have eased, offering a more predictable environment for household budgeting and economic planning. A key finding is that average family income effectively covers the cost of the Basic Family Basket, exceeding it by a modest margin. This coverage ratio implies that, at least for a typical household, basic needs are currently affordable without financial strain. The stability in core consumption categories, particularly food and transportation, which saw price reductions, contributed significantly to this balance. It demonstrates that the purchasing power of the average citizen has been maintained or slightly improved in the short term. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the importance of transparent, accessible, and high-quality statistical publications for public accountability. By releasing detailed breakdowns of inflation metrics, regional variations, and income correlations, INEC enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to analyze economic well-being with precision. Such granular, publicly available data fosters informed public debate, allows for better policy evaluation, and strengthens trust in official statistics by making the mechanisms behind economic indicators clear and verifiable.
