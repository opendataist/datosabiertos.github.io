Navigating the Intersection of Data Scraping and Artificial Intelligence–A Global Data Protection Authorities Take

Navigating the Intersection of Data Scraping and Artificial Intelligence–A Global Data Protection Authorities Take

The Global Privacy Assembly clarifies that scraping publicly accessible data still requires a lawful basis and transparency, regardless of platform terms. This emphasizes that contractual permission does not guarantee compliance with privacy or emerging AI laws, creating significant legal risks for businesses. These guidelines are crucial for open data initiatives, as they define the boundaries between free access and regulatory compliance. Open data advocates must now navigate stricter requirements for lawful processing and transparency to ensure their collections do not violate individual privacy rights or trigger breach reports. Ultimately, this highlights the tension between data availability and protection. As AI demands more data, open data practices must adapt to meet rigorous transparency and limitation standards, ensuring that public access does not infringe on personal privacy protections mandated by modern legal frameworks.
