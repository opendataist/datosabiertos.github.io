Property prices in Malta continue to rise, PN says - The Malta Independent

Property prices in Malta continue to rise, PN says - The Malta Independent

Malta faces a deepening housing affordability crisis driven by surging property prices and rents, which have significantly outpaced wage growth. This imbalance creates substantial barriers for prospective buyers, particularly regarding required deposits, while recent government interventions and reduced interest rates have failed to mitigate the escalating costs. The situation is exacerbated by excessive bureaucracy in planning and construction permits, where significant portions of property prices are consumed by administrative expenses rather than construction value. This article is relevant to open data because transparent, accessible statistics are essential for accurately diagnosing and addressing systemic issues like housing affordability. Without clear, publicly available datasets on property prices, rents, and administrative costs, stakeholders cannot effectively evaluate policy impacts or hold institutions accountable for bureaucratic inefficiencies. Open data enables independent verification of trends, empowering citizens and policymakers to demand evidence-based reforms that reduce unnecessary expenses and improve market accessibility, thereby fostering a more responsive and equitable housing sector.
