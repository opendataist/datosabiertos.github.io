Llamó INAI a legisladores a reflexionar sobre desaparición de órganos

The article advocates for transforming, rather than abolishing, the INAI to ensure the advancement of rights and institutional modernization. Experts stress that preserving independent bodies is essential for democratic transparency and accountability, contrasting this with the risks associated with elimination. Public trust in the INAI remains high, validating its role in enabling informed citizen decision-making. Removing it would undermine privacy and access to information, which are pillars of democracy that allow society to demand government accountability while protecting human rights. The proposed reforms leave the fate of the extensive transparency platform undefined, highlighting governance risks. This is significant for open data because institutional autonomy directly safeguards the integrity, accessibility, and legal framework necessary for public data to function as a tool for democratic oversight and civic engagement.
