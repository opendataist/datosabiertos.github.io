Panamá será sede del Congreso ALAP PRILATIN 2026

Panama will host the ALAP PRILATIN 2026 Congress, a pivotal regional event focusing on data protection, privacy, and artificial intelligence. This gathering aims to modernize national criteria and strengthen a robust culture of data security across all sectors, positioning the country as a key player in regional digital competitiveness. The conference addresses strategic topics such as compliance with local laws, cybersecurity, and responsible data management through expert panels. By integrating international experiences and comparative regulatory models, the event seeks to align Panama with global standards, accelerating the adoption of best practices essential for thriving in the current digital economy. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical balance between transparency and privacy. Strengthening data protection frameworks and privacy culture ensures that open data initiatives are trustworthy, legally compliant, and socially acceptable, fostering the public confidence necessary for effective data sharing and innovation.
