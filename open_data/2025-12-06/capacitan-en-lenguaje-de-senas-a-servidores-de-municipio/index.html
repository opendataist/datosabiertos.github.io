Capacitan en Lenguaje de Señas a servidores de Municipio

Capacitan en Lenguaje de Señas a servidores de Municipio

This initiative prioritizes inclusive transparency by ensuring accessible communication for people with hearing disabilities, thereby enhancing the quality of public services. It emphasizes that equal access to information is fundamental for strengthening rights and fostering participatory governance for vulnerable populations. By training officers in sign language and raising awareness about respectful interaction, the project promotes dignified and professional treatment of citizens. This approach ensures that no one faces discrimination when seeking essential information, directly supporting the core principles of open data accessibility. The relevance to open data lies in guaranteeing that public information is genuinely available to everyone. It demonstrates that true transparency requires removing barriers, ensuring that both digital and verbal information reaches all citizens equally, regardless of their physical abilities.
