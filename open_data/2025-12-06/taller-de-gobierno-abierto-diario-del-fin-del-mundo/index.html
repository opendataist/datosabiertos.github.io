Taller de Gobierno Abierto | Diario del Fin del Mundo

Taller de Gobierno Abierto | Diario del Fin del Mundo

The provincial government is promoting a workshop to strengthen local capacities in transparency, citizen participation, and technological innovation. This initiative, supported by cooperation with the European Union, aims to consolidate a more open and digitalized public management. The event focuses on translating international principles into concrete actions to improve the quality of data and public services, addressing needs identified in the region. Three fundamental pillars are highlighted: ensuring access to reliable information, designing effective participation mechanisms, and the strategic use of digital tools. Through working groups, participants will diagnose local challenges, design verifiable use cases, and outline a long-term roadmap. The objective is to foster interoperability and transparency, aligning provincial practices with global open government standards. This process is relevant to open data because it establishes the structural foundations for the availability, use, and reuse of public information. By prioritizing data quality and inter-institutional collaboration, it facilitates an environment where citizens and technological innovation can directly interact with the State. This promotes a knowledge-based economy and ensures that open data serve as effective tools for democratic governance and sustainable development.
