El New York Times demanda a la IA Perplexity por violar derechos de autor: "Se apropió del contenido"

El New York Times demanda a la IA Perplexity por violar derechos de autor: "Se apropió del contenido"

The New York Times is suing Perplexity for using copyrighted articles without permission, arguing that this practice harms their reputation through AI-generated hallucinations and creates direct competition. This lawsuit underscores the urgent need to clarify copyright boundaries when training or powering AI models with publicly available web data. The outcome of this case will significantly influence how developers access open datasets and media archives. It highlights that while open information is crucial for innovation, legal frameworks must evolve to protect intellectual property rights in the era of generative AI. Ultimately, the goal is not to ban AI usage but to establish regulated agreements and ensure fair compensation. This shift aims to keep open data initiatives sustainable and ethically sound, balancing technological advancement with the economic viability of content creators.
