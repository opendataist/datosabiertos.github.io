StatCan says job numbers up for third consecutive month

StatCan says job numbers up for third consecutive month

Canada’s labor market shows resilient growth, defying predictions of decline despite a significant portion of new roles being part-time. This unexpected strength, highlighted by rising wages, provides crucial real-time data for the Bank of Canada’s upcoming monetary policy decisions. Open data from Statistics Canada serves as a vital public resource, enabling economists and analysts to verify these trends independently. By making such granular employment statistics accessible, transparent datasets foster informed public debate and accurate economic forecasting without relying solely on private sector interpretations.
