Actividad de restaurantes continúa desacelerándose, ¿cómo cerrará el año?

The persistent slowdown in the foodservice sector is raising uncertainty about its annual performance, affecting employment dynamics. This trend directly impacts the availability of open data on local economic activity. The lack of clarity regarding year-end results hinders evidence-based planning. Public officials face challenges in accessing up-to-date information that reflects these fluctuations. It is crucial to improve channels for disseminating sector-specific data to support informed decision-making. Transparency in labor and economic indicators strengthens the open data ecosystem.
