Canada, Australia, Belgium, pledge to support NEITI on open data policy

International diplomatic representatives from Belgium, Australia, and Canada have formally pledged support for Nigeria’s Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative, emphasizing their commitment to the nation’s Open Data Policy. This endorsement follows a high-level inspection of the newly established NEITI Data Center, marking a significant collaborative effort to enhance transparency and accountability within Nigeria’s critical oil, gas, and solid mineral sectors. The Data Center serves as a centralized repository for aggregated and disaggregated industry data, ensuring easy public access for civil society, media, and government agencies. By transforming raw information into actionable insights, the facility supports evidence-based decision-making and aligns with global EITI standards for systematic disclosure. This infrastructure is designed to foster robust public understanding and sustainable advocacy, particularly regarding gender and environmental issues linked to extraction activities. This initiative is vital to open data principles as it democratizes access to sensitive industry information, thereby reducing corruption risks and promoting governance reform. The collaboration highlights the importance of technical assistance and skills development in building local capacity for data science. Ultimately, this partnership empowers stakeholders to navigate the economic challenges of the global energy transition through transparent, accessible, and reliable data.
