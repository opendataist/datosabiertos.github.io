Advierte INEGI por crisis forense en Veracruz y el País

Advierte INEGI por crisis forense en Veracruz y el País

The INEGI report reveals a critical failure in Mexico’s forensic identification services, highlighting Veracruz’s inability to identify hundreds of bodies annually. This backlog reflects a broader national crisis in which thousands of remains remain unclaimed, undermining the integrity of the justice system and leaving families without closure. The data exposes severe resource and capacity deficits across multiple states, particularly in Coahuila, Jalisco, and Guerrero. These systemic gaps indicate that current forensic infrastructure is insufficient to handle the volume of deaths, suggesting that urgent structural reforms are needed to improve efficiency and accountability in legal procedures. This information is vital for open-data advocates, as it demonstrates how transparent, aggregated statistics can expose governmental inefficiencies and human rights challenges. By making such granular data public, researchers and citizens can hold authorities accountable, promote policy changes, and drive improvements in public service delivery and transparency.
