Después de casi 5 meses de inoperancia, el INAI reinicia sesiones

The INAI processed thousands of transparency requests despite operational challenges, emphasizing that each case represents vital personal struggles, such as accessing pensions or securing human rights protections. This underscores how open data mechanisms are essential for citizens to assert fundamental rights and hold power accountable. Despite leadership gaps, the agency sustained its mission through alternative policies, reinforcing the state’s role in defending democratic liberties against restrictions. The institution serves as a crucial guardian of legality, ensuring that authorities respect citizens’ right to access information. The article highlights the urgent need to appoint the remaining commissioners to fully operationalize the INAI. This is relevant to open data because complete institutional functioning is necessary to effectively guarantee transparency, prevent abuses, and ensure the public can reliably obtain the data required for a functioning democracy.
