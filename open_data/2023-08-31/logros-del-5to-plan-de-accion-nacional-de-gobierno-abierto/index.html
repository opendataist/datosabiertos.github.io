Logros del 5to. Plan de Acción Nacional de Gobierno Abierto

Logros del 5to. Plan de Acción Nacional de Gobierno Abierto

Guatemala is concluding its fifth National Open Government Action Plan, having achieved 91 percent compliance across nearly all milestones. This success stems from collaborative efforts by thirteen public institutions, demonstrating effective implementation of digital governance and anti-corruption measures. The high completion rate signals strong institutional commitment to transparency and sets a solid foundation for future reforms. The plan’s outcomes significantly advance open data practices through participatory policy creation and the publication of health and education information in open formats. These actions enhance accountability and public access to critical state data. By leveraging open data platforms for infrastructure and administrative records, the government improves service delivery and fosters greater citizen trust in public institutions. This achievement is vital for the open data community as it validates collaborative approaches to government transparency. It illustrates how structured national plans can successfully operationalize open data principles, ensuring that public information is accessible, reusable, and contributes to societal well-being. The upcoming sixth plan promises to expand these efforts, reinforcing the integration of open data into Guatemala’s digital governance strategy for sustained civic engagement and institutional accountability.
