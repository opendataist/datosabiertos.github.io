Descenso de defunciones en México: Niveles Pre-Pandemia en el primer trimestre de 2023″ | Revista Punto de Vista

Descenso de defunciones en México: Niveles Pre-Pandemia en el primer trimestre de 2023″ | Revista Punto de Vista

Mexico’s mortality rates are stabilizing, returning to pre-pandemic levels as of early 2023. This recovery marks a significant improvement in public health, reversing the sharp surge in deaths observed during the peak crisis years and indicating that the healthcare system has regained stability. The data reveal a distinct demographic pattern, with older adults disproportionately affected while younger groups remain at lower risk. Regional disparities also persist, underscoring how geographical factors continue to influence mortality outcomes across different Mexican states. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, high-quality statistical evidence. Such accessible datasets enable researchers and policymakers to accurately monitor health trends, facilitating data-driven decisions that enhance public health strategies and optimize resource allocation nationwide.
