Actualizan denominación de la FGE en Ley de Transparencia

San Luis Potosí has updated its Transparency Law to explicitly define the obligations of the State Attorney General’s Office, ensuring alignment with current institutional structures. This legislative refinement aims to strengthen legal certainty regarding access to information, transforming the law into a more effective tool for enforcing public accountability and transparency standards across government bodies. The reform emphasizes that information must be identifiable, accessible, and clear to facilitate public scrutiny. By mandating these quality criteria for obligated entities, the state seeks to mitigate the negative impacts of opacity and corruption. This approach supports the development of a more participatory society, in which citizens can actively monitor government actions and demand greater accountability from public officials. This development is crucial for open data, as it establishes a legal framework requiring public institutions to maintain data that is not only available but also usable and standardized. Such structural updates are fundamental to ensuring that transparency laws effectively translate into actionable, high-quality data releases, thereby enhancing democratic oversight and reducing systemic information asymmetry in governance.
