CSIF lamenta la subida del paro en C-LM y la pérdida de afiliación en actividades sanitarias y servicios sociales

CSIF lamenta la subida del paro en C-LM y la pérdida de afiliación en actividades sanitarias y servicios sociales

The article highlights a critical deterioration in the healthcare and social services sectors within Castilla-La Mancha, marked by significant job losses that coincide with a severe surge in respiratory infections. This staffing reduction threatens the quality of patient care, leading to saturated hospitals and overwhelmed primary care networks, especially as winter approaches and illness rates remain among the highest in Spain. The situation is exacerbated by the lack of adequate resource planning and the absence of personnel reinforcements during the flu vaccination campaign. Furthermore, the stagnation of professional career progression and excessive workloads have created an unsustainable work environment for healthcare professionals, raising urgent concerns about worker well-being and the sustainability of public health services under current economic constraints. This report is relevant to open data advocates because it underscores the necessity of accessible, transparent public employment and health surveillance statistics. Reliable, open datasets are essential for independent verification of labor trends and epidemiological impacts, enabling civil society and researchers to hold institutions accountable and drive evidence-based policy reforms regarding resource allocation and labor rights in the public sector.
