Privacy Policy - Daily Capital

Privacy Policy - Daily Capital

Daily Capital demonstrates that low-data collection websites are feasible for open data journalism. By eliminating user-facing forms and maintaining no internal user database, the site avoids direct collection of personal identifiers like emails or names. This approach significantly reduces the host’s direct liability and privacy footprint, showing that essential informational services can operate without requiring user registration or active data submission. However, the article highlights the pervasive nature of third-party dependencies in modern web infrastructure. While the site itself collects minimal data, it relies on standard server logs and Google AdSense, which automatically gather device information, IP addresses, and browsing behavior to serve advertisements. This illustrates a critical tension in open data transparency: even platforms committed to minimal direct data retention remain subject to complex tracking mechanisms controlled by external advertising partners. This policy is relevant to open data discussions because it models a balance between operational sustainability and privacy respect. It underscores the importance of transparent disclosure regarding indirect data flows and third-party trackers. For data advocates, it serves as a practical example of how publishers can maintain public accountability and open access while clearly defining the limits of their own data stewardship versus the unavoidable data practices of global tech intermediaries.
