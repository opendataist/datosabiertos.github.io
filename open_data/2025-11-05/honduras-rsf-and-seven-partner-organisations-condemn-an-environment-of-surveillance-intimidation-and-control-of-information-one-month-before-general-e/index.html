Honduras: RSF and seven partner organisations condemn an environment of surveillance, intimidation, and control of information one month before general elections

Honduras presents severe risks to journalists, marked by intimidation and state negligence that erode democratic oversight. This dangerous climate undermines the accountability required for transparent governance. Consequently, the suppression of investigative reporting critically restricts public access to essential information. This restriction directly hinders the open exchange of data necessary for informed civic participation and trust in public institutions.
