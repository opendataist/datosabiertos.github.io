FOI violations: IPC seeks sanctions’ review for public officials

FOI violations: IPC seeks sanctions’ review for public officials

The International Press Centre advocates for strengthening sanctions under Nigeria’s Freedom of Information Act to combat the deliberate withholding of records by public officials. This push highlights the critical need to ensure transparency remains a cornerstone of democracy, addressing the tendency of political figures to prioritize personal interests over public accountability. Strengthening penalties is essential to enforce the universal right of access to information, ensuring it functions as a true check on power. Furthermore, there is a pressing need to overhaul current reporting and compliance standards to increase their effectiveness. Many government agencies currently evade scrutiny because their activities are not adequately reflected in national oversight reports. Improving these mechanisms would enhance public accountability and ensure that all ministries and agencies are subject to meaningful examination regarding their operations and adherence to legal obligations. Finally, clarifying the legal applicability of the Act to all government levels is vital for consistent implementation. Recognizing that the legislation applies universally eliminates the misconception that state governments must domesticate the law, streamlining enforcement. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the legislative and structural reforms necessary to create a robust environment where data transparency is not just a right but an enforceable standard, facilitating better access to public information for all citizens.
