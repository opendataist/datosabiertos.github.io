Cecam valora los datos del paro señalando al descenso de "horas efectivas" a pesar del aumento de contrataciones

The article highlights the critical lack of data on fixed-discontinuous contracts in Spain’s labor market, which prevents an accurate diagnosis of employment trends. This information gap obscures the logical inconsistency between rising hiring figures and declining effective working hours across key sectors. Understanding these hidden employment structures is essential for open data initiatives, as comprehensive datasets enable transparent analysis and better policy formulation. Revealing such disparities ensures that economic assessments reflect reality, fostering accountability and informed decision-making within the public and private sectors.
