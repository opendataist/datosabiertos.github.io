Stability AI gana en gran parte batalla legal contra Getty Images por derechos de autor y marcas

Stability AI gana en gran parte batalla legal contra Getty Images por derechos de autor y marcas

Stability AI largely prevailed over Getty Images in a British court, marking a turning point in the tension between the technology and creative industries. Although Getty secured a limited victory on trademark infringement claims, the court dismissed the copyright allegations. The judge determined that the AI training process did not constitute illegal reproduction, as the models do not store or reproduce the original protected works. However, this decision did not resolve the underlying conflict regarding the legality of training AI on copyrighted content. By dismissing claims of primary infringement, the case left a significant legal vacuum in the United Kingdom. Experts note that the ruling is narrow in scope and does not address the central question of whether using copyrighted data to train AI is lawful under fair use doctrines, leaving legal uncertainty intact. This case is crucial for the open data movement because it illustrates the risk that bias in training data could reinforce corporate monopolies over information. The lack of legal clarity regarding who holds the right to use creative works to train algorithms directly impacts transparency and fairness in AI development. To promote open innovation, it is essential to establish clear standards that enable ethical access to and use of data, preventing legal disputes from stifling innovation and collaboration within the open technology ecosystem.
