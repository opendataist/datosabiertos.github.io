San Luis Potosí lidera en desarrollo industrial | Revista Punto de Vista

The article highlights San Luis Potosí's significant industrial growth, ranking it highly at the national level due to strategic policies aimed at attracting investment. This success demonstrates how effective public management can drive regional economic vitality and create better employment opportunities for residents. This case illustrates the practical impact of data-driven governance on industrial development. It shows that transparent reporting of economic indicators, such as those provided by INEGI, validates policy effectiveness and helps position the state as a competitive hub for business expansion. This is relevant to open data because it relies on accessible, standardized statistics to inform public discourse and justify administrative decisions. Open data ensures that these economic achievements are verifiable, fostering trust between the government and citizens while encouraging further investment through demonstrated, evidence-based progress.
