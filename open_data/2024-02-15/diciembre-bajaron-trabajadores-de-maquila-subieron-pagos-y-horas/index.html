Diciembre: Bajaron trabajadores de maquila; subieron pagos y horas

Mexico’s manufacturing sector is experiencing declining employment even as working hours and real wages rise, signaling a shift toward higher productivity per worker rather than expansion of the workforce. This trend underscores how labor market dynamics are evolving, with companies optimizing output through efficiency gains instead of hiring. These structural changes reflect broader economic adjustments within the industrial landscape. These data are crucial for open data initiatives, as they highlight the importance of analyzing multiple indicators together. Relying solely on employment figures can obscure underlying productivity improvements and wage trends affecting the sector.
