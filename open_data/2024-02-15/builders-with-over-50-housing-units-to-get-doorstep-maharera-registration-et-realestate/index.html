Builders with over 50 housing units to get doorstep MahaRERA registration - ET RealEstate

The Maharashtra government plans to streamline property registration by enabling large builders to host MahaRERA facilities on-site. This initiative aims to reduce bureaucratic hurdles for buyers and promote transparency in real estate transactions. By bringing regulatory services closer to developers, the state seeks to enhance accountability and simplify the registration process for housing units. Simultaneously, the administration targets a significant increase in annual revenue generated through stamp duties. To achieve this financial goal, officials are reviewing current ready reckoner rates, which directly influence property valuations and tax collections. Adjusting these rates is seen as a critical step to boost public funds allocated for infrastructure and development projects across the region. This development is relevant to open data because digitizing and decentralizing registration processes generates accessible, standardized datasets. Transparent property records improve market efficiency and allow citizens to verify ownership and valuations easily. Ultimately, making registration data more open supports greater trust in real estate markets and facilitates better policy analysis through public access to accurate information.
