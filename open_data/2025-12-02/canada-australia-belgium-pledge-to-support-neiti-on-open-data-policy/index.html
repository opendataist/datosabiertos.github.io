Canada, Australia, Belgium, pledge to support NEITI on open data policy

Canada, Australia, Belgium, pledge to support NEITI on open data policy

International diplomatic missions from Belgium, Australia, and Canada have officially pledged support for Nigeria’s Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative open data policy. This commitment follows a tour of the newly established NEITI Data Center, which serves as a comprehensive repository for the nation’s oil, gas, and mineral sector information. The donors emphasized their willingness to provide essential technical assistance, skills development, and training to bolster these transparency efforts. The data center represents a critical infrastructure for combating corruption and ensuring public accountability within the extractive industry. By aggregating and disaggregating historical data into an accessible format, it empowers civil society organizations, the media, and government bodies to scrutinize industry activities effectively. This transparency aligns with global EITI standards, promoting evidence-based decision-making and fostering a robust public understanding of resource management in a sector heavily reliant on foreign investment and international cooperation. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how centralized, accessible digital infrastructure can drive governance reforms and sustainable development. The project highlights the growing global shift toward renewable energy and the urgent need for robust data ecosystems in developing nations. By facilitating knowledge sharing and technical support between developed and developing countries, this collaboration underscores the vital role of open data in tackling complex environmental and gender issues while enhancing economic resilience through transparency.
