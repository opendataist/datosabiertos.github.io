Praja Foundation launches Citizens’ Manifesto for Maharashtra civic polls

Praja Foundation launches Citizens’ Manifesto for Maharashtra civic polls

Praja Foundation has launched a Citizens’ Manifesto for Maharashtra’s 2025 municipal elections, advocating for a fundamental shift toward transparent, participatory, and efficient urban governance. The roadmap prioritizes maximizing municipal authority and establishing clear, measurable service delivery standards to address longstanding democratic deficits. By demanding that governance reflect public voice, the initiative seeks to rebuild citizen trust through restored democratic processes and inclusive decision-making structures. Central to this reform agenda is the implementation of real-time open data systems and unified digital platforms. The proposal emphasizes mandatory disclosure of service benchmarks and the creation of a statewide open data portal, enabling citizens to access ward-level information on budgets and projects. This technological integration aims to enhance accountability through interactive dashboards and AI-driven tools, ensuring that environmental and performance metrics are accessible, transparent, and actionable for the public. The relevance to open data lies in its role as a catalyst for civic engagement and municipal capacity building. By mandating free access to civic data and institutionalizing participatory budgeting, the manifesto positions information transparency not merely as a technical requirement, but as a essential mechanism for empowering citizens. This approach demonstrates how open data can bridge information gaps, enable community-led monitoring, and drive equitable urban development through informed citizen participation.
