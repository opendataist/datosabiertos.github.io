Aviso de Privacidad

Aviso de Privacidad

This document serves as a privacy notice issued by 13 MEDIA, S.C., designed to comply with federal data protection laws. It outlines the comprehensive collection of personal information, including financial and identity details, necessary for providing client services. The primary purpose of gathering this data is to verify identities, meet legal obligations, and address client needs, ensuring that all processing adheres to principles such as legality, transparency, and proportionality. A critical aspect of this notice is the mechanism for data sharing and user control. Data may be transferred to third parties, such as notaries or lawyers, for legal formalizations, with consent implied unless explicitly revoked. Users retain the right to limit, access, rectify, or cancel the use of their data by contacting designated privacy officers. However, these rights are not absolute, as compliance with legal mandates or requests from authorities takes precedence, thereby limiting the scope of individual opposition. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the strict legal boundaries governing personal information in Mexico. While open data promotes the transparency and sharing of non-personal public information, this notice demonstrates how personal data remains heavily regulated and protected. It highlights the essential distinction between open data ecosystems, which prioritize public benefit and accessibility, and private sector data handling, which must strictly safeguard individual privacy rights against unauthorized disclosure.
