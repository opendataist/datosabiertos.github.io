New Delhi has reported its first annual coronavirus deaths and a surge in active cases nationwide, prompting heightened health alerts. This rapid increase signals a potential new wave, raising concerns about overwhelming the healthcare system. The Indian government is urging hospitals to prepare rigorously to prevent a recurrence of the devastating 2021 crisis. This proactive stance highlights the critical need for data-driven early warning systems to manage public health emergencies effectively. This incident underscores the vital role of open data in pandemic response. Transparent, real-time statistics enable authorities to forecast trends, allocate resources efficiently, and implement timely interventions to protect vulnerable populations and maintain medical capacity.
