UK Economy Enters Recession in Q4 of 2023 After GDP Drops by 0.3% - Statistics Office

The UK entered a recession in late 2023, marked by consecutive quarterly declines in GDP. This contraction highlights a significant slowdown in economic growth, reversing the stronger performance seen in the previous year and signaling ongoing instability within major European markets. The downturn affected key sectors, particularly services and construction, which contributed to the overall shrinkage. While the annual growth for 2023 remained slightly positive, the trend indicates a weakening economy that struggles to maintain momentum amidst broader financial pressures and reduced expansion rates. This data is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates the value of transparent, timely official statistics in tracking macroeconomic health. Accurate, publicly accessible economic indicators enable researchers and policymakers to monitor recession risks, analyze sectoral impacts, and make informed decisions based on verified national performance metrics.
