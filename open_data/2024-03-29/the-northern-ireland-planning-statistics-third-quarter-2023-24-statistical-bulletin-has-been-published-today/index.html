The Northern Ireland Planning Statistics: Third Quarter 2023/24 Statistical Bulletin, has been published today

Northern Ireland’s planning statistics reveal a complex landscape where application volumes rise, yet statutory performance targets remain largely unmet. While major application processing times have improved significantly, local development decisions continue to lag behind the fifteen-week standard, indicating systemic inefficiencies in handling routine planning requests. These delays have direct implications for open_data initiatives by highlighting the gap between raw data release and actionable policy insights. Transparent, timely data is essential for holding authorities accountable and understanding the real-world impact of administrative bottlenecks. Without accurate performance metrics, stakeholders cannot effectively advocate for streamlined processes or resource allocation. This bulletin underscores the critical need for accessible, standardized planning data to foster transparency and accountability. It demonstrates how open data can expose operational shortcomings, enabling communities and developers to make informed decisions. Ultimately, such insights drive necessary reforms to improve planning efficiency and align statistical reporting with actual public service delivery standards.
