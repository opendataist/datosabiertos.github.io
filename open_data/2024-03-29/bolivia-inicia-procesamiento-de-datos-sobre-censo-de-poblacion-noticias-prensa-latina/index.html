Bolivia inicia procesamiento de datos sobre censo de población - Noticias Prensa Latina

Bolivia’s census is moving into its post-enumeration phase, leveraging advanced AI for data processing. The director highlights that the process was modernized through digital cartography and participatory design, with international oversight ensuring high quality and transparency. This achievement is highly relevant to open data, as it underscores a shift toward digitized, verifiable, and technologically robust public records. By integrating international expertise and modern software, the government is laying the groundwork for more accessible and reliable statistical information. The ongoing supervision by the international commission until the final results are released signals a strong commitment to accountability. This approach fosters the development of open, high-integrity datasets, demonstrating how transparent, technology-driven initiatives can enhance public trust and the utility of data in governance.
