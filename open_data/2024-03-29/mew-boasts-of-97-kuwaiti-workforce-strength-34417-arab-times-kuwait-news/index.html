MEW Boasts Of 97% Kuwaiti Workforce; Strength 34,417 - ARAB TIMES - KUWAIT NEWS

Kuwait’s Ministry of Electricity, Water, and Renewable Energy recently released workforce and operational data, highlighting a workforce dominated by national employees. This transparency demonstrates a commitment to open government statistics regarding employment demographics and resource management. The data reveals significant energy trade with the Gulf network, showing stable exchange rates and net benefits driven by winter maintenance. Such detailed reporting on cross-border energy flows provides valuable insight into regional infrastructure reliability and operational efficiency. These figures are crucial for open_data initiatives, as they enable researchers and policymakers to analyze energy security trends. By making this information accessible, stakeholders can better understand the implications of regional interconnections and support informed decisions regarding future capacity expansions and sustainable energy strategies.
