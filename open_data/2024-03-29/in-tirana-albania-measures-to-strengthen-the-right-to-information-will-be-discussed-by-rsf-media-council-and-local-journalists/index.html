In Tirana, Albania, measures to strengthen the right to information will be discussed by RSF, Media Council and local journalists

Strengthening trustworthy journalism in Albania supports its EU integration, ensuring the public's right to information. By transforming discourse into policy, this initiative aims to elevate press freedom standards. This effort is vital for open data ecosystems, as independent media serves as a critical conduit for verifying and disseminating transparent, accessible government information to citizens.
