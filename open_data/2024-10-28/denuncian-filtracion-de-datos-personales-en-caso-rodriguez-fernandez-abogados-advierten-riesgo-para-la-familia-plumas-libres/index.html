“Denuncian filtración de datos personales en caso Rodríguez Fernández»: Abogados advierten riesgo para la familia - Plumas Libres

“Denuncian filtración de datos personales en caso Rodríguez Fernández»: Abogados advierten riesgo para la familia - Plumas Libres

Legal representatives for Manuel Rodríguez Fernández filed a formal complaint regarding the unlawful disclosure of confidential data linked to ongoing criminal proceedings. They argue that this media leak threatens the family’s safety and constitutes defamation, as the alleged fraud remains unproven and the individuals involved are actively presenting evidence of their innocence. The attorneys assert that the press campaign is an illicit tactic to intimidate judicial authorities and damage reputations through false narratives. They specifically deny any connection to the implicated politician, viewing these inaccuracies as malicious attempts to smear both their client’s standing and the public official’s image. This case highlights critical open data implications, particularly regarding the intersection of privacy rights and information transparency. It underscores the urgent need for robust protocols governing the release of personal data during investigations, ensuring that access to information does not compromise legal due process or facilitate unjustified public stigmatization before a final verdict is reached.
