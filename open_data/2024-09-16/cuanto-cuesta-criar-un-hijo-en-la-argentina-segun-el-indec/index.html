Cuánto cuesta criar un hijo en la Argentina, según el INDEC

Cuánto cuesta criar un hijo en la Argentina, según el INDEC

The introduction of the "Canasta de Crianza" (Child-Rearing Basket) by INDEC represents a significant advance in statistical transparency by quantifying the monetary cost of raising children. This metric is crucial because it validates care work as a distinct economic expense, moving beyond traditional consumption baskets that often overlook unpaid labor disproportionately performed by women. A key finding is that care costs significantly impact household budgets, varying by age but consistently consuming a substantial portion of family income. For children under 12, these expenses nearly halve the cost of a basic family basket. This reveals that the true cost of raising a child extends far beyond material goods, highlighting the heavy financial burden associated with the time and labor dedicated to childcare. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides a rigorous, gender-disaggregated tool for analyzing economic inequality. By making these calculations public and transparent, stakeholders can better understand the economic value of care, advocate for more equitable social policies, and challenge the invisibility of reproductive labor in national economic indicators.
