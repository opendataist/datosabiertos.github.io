Servicios de Salud lidera en participación ciudadana a nivel estatal

Servicios de Salud lidera en participación ciudadana a nivel estatal

The Health Services of Oaxaca achieved top rankings in both state and national evaluations for citizen participation. This success highlights their effective management of public interactions across various communication channels, including digital platforms such as social media. The institution demonstrated a strong commitment to providing timely and responsive feedback to the population’s requests and proposals. This recognition stems from a biennial assessment conducted by independent researchers and transparency institutes, which evaluates public institutions based on citizen-centric metrics. By prioritizing active profiles and strategic feedback mechanisms, the health service exemplifies how government bodies can utilize technology to enhance accessibility and engagement with the public. This case is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the practical application of transparency principles in action. It shows that merely publishing information is insufficient; active, two-way communication and accountability are essential for genuine open government. Such models encourage other institutions to adopt similar practices, fostering a culture of trust and collaborative governance through accessible digital tools.
