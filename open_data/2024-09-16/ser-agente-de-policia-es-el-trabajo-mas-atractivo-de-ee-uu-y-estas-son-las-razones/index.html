Ser agente de policía es el trabajo más atractivo de EE.UU y estas son las razones

The U.S. labor market exhibits overall weakening trends, yet the law enforcement and healthcare sectors remain robust. Police departments are grappling with severe staffing shortages driven by retirements and low applicant volumes, prompting significant salary increases and relaxed hiring criteria to attract candidates. This situation underscores the importance of open data in monitoring labor market dynamics. Transparent access to employment statistics and salary benchmarks enables policymakers and organizations to identify critical sector imbalances. Consequently, data-driven insights are essential for designing effective retention strategies and addressing structural workforce deficits in public safety.
