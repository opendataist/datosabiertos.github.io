Increase in crimes of violence - Sligo Weekender | Sligo News | Sligo Sport

Recent crime statistics reveal a troubling rise in violent offenses across the Sligo-Leitrim division, with significant increases in sex offenses, assaults, and robberies. This shift contrasts with declines in non-violent property crimes and fraud, suggesting a changing landscape of local security challenges. These figures mark a return to regular data publication after previous suspensions due to concerns over recording inconsistencies. The Central Statistics Office resumed releasing these statistics only after completing a comprehensive review of the Garda’s PULSE administrative data quality. This pause was necessary to address irregularities identified in how crimes were logged, ensuring that future data reflects accurate and reliable information. The review aimed to correct classification errors and ensure that incidents properly categorized as non-crimes are not misleadingly included in crime statistics. This episode is highly relevant to open data advocates because it underscores the critical importance of data integrity in public transparency. It highlights how technical flaws in data collection systems can undermine trust and distort public understanding of social issues. Reliable, accurately recorded open data is essential for informed decision-making, and this case illustrates the rigorous standards required to maintain credibility in government statistics.
