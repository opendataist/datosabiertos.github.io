La Nación / El empleo formal sigue siendo un desafío para el país, destacó el titula del INE

La Nación / El empleo formal sigue siendo un desafío para el país, destacó el titula del INE

The article highlights that Paraguay faces significant challenges in formalizing its labor market, with over 62% of non-agricultural workers operating informally. Although there has been notable progress in formalization during the current administration, the sheer volume of informal employment indicates that achieving decent work conditions remains a complex structural issue. The data reveals a labor force nearly triple the size of the formal sector, underscoring the scale of the gap between official statistics and economic reality. A critical gender disparity emerges in the analysis of unemployment and underemployment, with women disproportionately affected by joblessness and reduced working hours. This trend, alongside the concentration of the majority of workers in the tertiary sector, suggests that economic benefits are not evenly distributed across demographics or industries. The reliance on the International Labour Organization’s methodology ensures that these measurements are globally comparable, providing a standardized view of the country's labor health. This information is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, methodologically rigorous statistical practices to inform public policy. By making detailed breakdowns of employment sectors, informalities, and demographic disparities publicly available, the state enables civil society and researchers to hold institutions accountable and propose evidence-based solutions. Such accessibility fosters trust in national institutions and empowers citizens to engage with critical socio-economic indicators that directly impact their lives.
