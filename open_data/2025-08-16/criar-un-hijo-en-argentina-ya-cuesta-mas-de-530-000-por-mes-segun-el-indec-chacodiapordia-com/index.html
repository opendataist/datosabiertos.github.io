Criar un hijo en Argentina ya cuesta más de $530.000 por mes, según el INDEC - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

The rising cost of raising children in Argentina underscores the urgent need for transparent data on family expenses. This inflation outpaces general rates, driven largely by care costs, highlighting the economic value of domestic labor. Such detailed statistical tracking is essential for open data ecosystems, enabling evidence-based policy and legal applications such as child support calculations. It transforms invisible care work into a measurable economic reality. Ultimately, this dataset reveals systemic pressures on households. By making these figures publicly accessible, authorities can better address social welfare needs and support equitable resource distribution for families across different age groups.
