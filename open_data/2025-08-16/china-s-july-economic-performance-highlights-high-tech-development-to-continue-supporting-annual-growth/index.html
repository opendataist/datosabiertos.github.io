China's July economic performance highlights high-tech development, to continue supporting annual growth

China's July economic performance highlights high-tech development, to continue supporting annual growth

China’s economy is demonstrating resilient, innovation-driven growth in 2025, underpinned by robust expansion in high-tech manufacturing and the digital economy. The surge in industrial robots, smart devices, and advanced services highlights a successful pivot toward "new quality productive forces," which are reshaping industrial structures and strengthening global supply chain competitiveness despite external challenges. Simultaneously, proactive macroeconomic policies and expanding domestic demand are sustaining this momentum. Stimuli targeting consumption and trade-in programs have effectively boosted retail sales and service industries, while diversified export strategies maintain foreign trade vitality. These factors combine to support a stable trajectory toward the annual growth target, reinforcing China’s role as an economic stabilizer amidst global uncertainty. This article is relevant to open data because the transparency of National Bureau of Statistics releases and international institutional reports enables stakeholders to track these macroeconomic shifts. Access to granular data on emerging industries allows researchers, investors, and policymakers to validate the narrative of innovation-led growth and assess the real-world impact of policy measures on China’s digital and industrial transformation.
