ECOWAS LAYS THE FOUNDATIONS FOR A REGIONAL OPEN DATA ECOSYSTEM TO ADVANCE TRANSPARENCY, INNOVATION AND DIGITAL GOVERNANCE | Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS)

ECOWAS is advancing a harmonized regional legal framework for open data to strengthen transparency and digital governance across West Africa. This initiative aims to create a unified standard for publishing, accessing, and reusing public sector information, moving beyond fragmented national efforts to establish a coherent regional ecosystem. By fostering consensus among member states, the Commission seeks to align legal structures, ensuring that open data principles are consistently applied to drive economic innovation and digital transformation throughout the region. The proposed framework promotes the principle of open-by-default access while maintaining essential safeguards for privacy, security, and intellectual property. This balanced approach encourages the publication of high-value datasets in accessible formats, facilitating responsible reuse by businesses, researchers, and civil society. Enhancing interoperability between national systems is central to this strategy, aiming to support evidence-based policymaking and improve service delivery. The goal is to empower various stakeholders to develop solutions that address socio-economic challenges, thereby strengthening institutional accountability and regional integration through shared knowledge. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates as it demonstrates how supranational bodies can accelerate data openness through coordinated legal action. It highlights the shift from voluntary disclosure to mandatory, standardized governance, which significantly reduces barriers to data reuse. By establishing a robust legal foundation, ECOWAS sets a precedent for how regional blocs can leverage public sector data to foster innovation ecosystems. This case illustrates the tangible impact of policy harmonization in unlocking the economic and social value of open data, offering a model for other regions seeking to enhance digital rights and transparency through structured, collaborative governance.
