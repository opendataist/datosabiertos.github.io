Kenya wins Bid to host the International Open Data Conference

The International Open Data Conference (IODC) will be held in Nairobi, Kenya, in 2020, marking the first time the premier global gathering takes place in Africa. This strategic shift signifies a growing recognition of Africa’s potential to lead in leveraging open data for development. The selection of Kenya follows strong leadership from its government, which has actively fostered collaboration between the public and private sectors to build an inclusive data ecosystem. The 2020 event, themed “Bridging Data Communities,” aims to strengthen South-South collaboration and unite diverse stakeholders including governments, civil society, and innovators. By convening participants from over 100 countries, the conference seeks to accelerate the impact of open data initiatives across critical sectors such as health, agriculture, and fiscal transparency. This gathering serves as a platform to share best practices and enhance interoperability, ensuring that data contributes effectively to global development priorities. This conference is highly relevant to open data as it highlights the critical importance of geographic diversity and local context in data governance. Hosting the event in Africa underscores the necessity of empowering regional actors to define their own data strategies and challenges. It reinforces the narrative that open data is not just a technical tool but a strategic asset for equitable economic growth and social inclusion, particularly in regions historically underrepresented in global data dialogues.
