Chihuahua Capital recibe Galardón por su Portal de Datos Abiertos

Chihuahua Capital recibe Galardón por su Portal de Datos Abiertos

The capital city of Chihuahua received the Francisco Villarreal Torres Award for its Open Data Portal, recognizing its commitment to transparency and citizen participation. This initiative, part of the Transparency 360° strategy, provides free access to reusable public information across key sectors, fostering trust and accountability through open governance practices. The platform features diverse datasets from multiple municipal departments, supported by a consultative council involving citizens and civil society. This structure ensures strategic data management and promotes collaborative decision-making, positioning Chihuahua as a national model for open government and effective civic engagement. This achievement is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how local governments can effectively operationalize open data principles. By prioritizing accessible, reusable formats and inclusive governance mechanisms, the case highlights the tangible benefits of transparency in strengthening democratic institutions and empowering communities through informed civic participation.
