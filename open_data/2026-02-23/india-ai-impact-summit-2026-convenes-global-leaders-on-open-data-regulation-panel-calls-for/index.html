India AI Impact Summit 2026 Convenes Global Leaders on Open Data Regulation; Panel Calls for

The recent high-profile panel at the India AI Impact Summit established that a robust regulatory framework for open data is essential to transform raw information into usable infrastructure for AI applications. Key stakeholders emphasized moving beyond voluntary sharing toward statutory duties that ensure data is machine-readable, interoperable, and rich in metadata, thereby preventing external dependency and inequalities while fostering domestic capacity. A central conclusion is that open data must be treated as core digital infrastructure, requiring enforceable laws and technical standards to guarantee trust and utility. This approach aims to provide the legal certainty necessary for investor confidence and responsible innovation, ensuring that datasets are not merely accessible but actively power safe, high-impact public-interest applications such as evidence-based policymaking and agricultural prediction. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical shift from simple accessibility to AI-ready usability. It underscores the urgent need for structured openness that balances sovereignty with global cooperation, illustrating how legal frameworks and technical interoperability standards are foundational for unlocking the full potential of open data in an AI-driven economy.
