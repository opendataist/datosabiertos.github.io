John Battelle's Search Blog Thanks for The FOIA, InfoWeek

The Department of Justice issued extensive subpoenas to numerous internet service providers and search engines to enforce the Child Online Protection Act. This broad data collection raises significant concerns about privacy and the extent of government surveillance over private sector entities. The sheer volume of companies involved highlights the aggressive nature of regulatory enforcement, potentially chilling innovation and user privacy rights across the digital landscape. This incident is crucial for open data discussions as it illustrates the tension between state control and transparency, warning of how legal mechanisms can be used to extract sensitive user information without adequate public oversight or accountability.
