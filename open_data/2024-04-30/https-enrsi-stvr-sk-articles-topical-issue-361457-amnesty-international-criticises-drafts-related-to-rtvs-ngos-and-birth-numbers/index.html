This content is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, independent public media and civil society to function without state interference. Protecting these institutions ensures the free flow of information and safeguards against the misuse of data to discriminate or silence marginalized groups. Consequently, open data principles must be upheld to prevent authoritarian overreach and maintain societal trust in governance.
