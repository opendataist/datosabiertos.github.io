Fortalecen cultura de la transparencia y protección de datos personales - Quadratín

Fortalecen cultura de la transparencia y protección de datos personales - Quadratín

The IMAIP and CEEAV have established a collaborative agreement to enhance transparency and data protection training. This partnership aims to deepen the culture of openness and accountability within Michoacán’s institutions, emphasizing that transparency builds public trust and fosters cooperative governance between authorities and society. The initiative underscores a shared commitment to open government principles, including document preservation, citizen participation, and technological innovation. By strengthening these practices, the alliance seeks to ensure equitable access to information while robustly protecting personal data, thereby supporting a more just and peaceful environment for victims. This collaboration is crucial for open data ecosystems as it institutionalizes rigorous standards for information management. It demonstrates how inter-agency cooperation can effectively balance the right to know with privacy safeguards, setting a practical example for integrating transparency mandates with operational integrity in public service delivery.
