The recent release of Saudi Arabia’s 2022 census data highlights a significant demographic shift, revealing a youthful population with a median age of 29. This statistical foundation is crucial for the Kingdom, as it directly informs national planning, economic policy, and the strategic goals of Vision 2030. The accuracy of this dataset, achieved through advanced technological methods, ensures that future development plans are grounded in reliable, real-time insights rather than outdated estimates. Relevance to open data lies in the comprehensive accessibility and transparency of these findings. By publishing results through interactive dashboards, maps, and detailed reports, the General Authority for Statistics enables citizens, researchers, and policymakers to explore complex socioeconomic trends independently. This approach transforms raw demographic figures into actionable public intelligence, fostering informed decision-making across various sectors and encouraging civic engagement with national statistics. Furthermore, the integration of backcasting techniques to validate historical data against the new census underscores a commitment to rigorous data quality and longitudinal consistency. By making diverse indicators such as employment, health, and migration available, the initiative promotes a culture of open information. This empowers external analysts to verify trends and contribute to a deeper understanding of societal changes, ultimately enhancing the utility and trustworthiness of public data resources.
