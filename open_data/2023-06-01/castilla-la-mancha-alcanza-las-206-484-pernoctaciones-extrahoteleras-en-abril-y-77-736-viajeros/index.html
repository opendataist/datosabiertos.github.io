Castilla-La Mancha alcanza las 206.484 pernoctaciones extrahoteleras en abril y 77.736 viajeros

The article highlights a significant recovery in Spain’s non-hotel tourism sector, noting substantial year-over-year growth in guest nights and traveler volumes. This upward trend indicates a robust return to pre-pandemic performance levels, signaling strong demand across various accommodation types such as apartments, rural houses, and campgrounds. Regionally, data reveals varied occupancy rates and contributions to employment, with tourism continuing to serve as a vital economic engine. The differences in performance among sectors underscore the diverse nature of tourist preferences and the specific operational challenges faced by different types of accommodations in the current market landscape. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it provides essential, standardized metrics for analyzing regional tourism dynamics. By making such granular statistics accessible, policymakers and researchers can better evaluate economic impacts, optimize resource allocation, and support evidence-based decision-making in the hospitality industry, fostering transparency and informed public debate.
