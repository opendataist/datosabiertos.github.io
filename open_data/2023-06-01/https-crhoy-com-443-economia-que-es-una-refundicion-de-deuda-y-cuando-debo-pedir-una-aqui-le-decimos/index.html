Debt refinancing enables individuals to consolidate multiple financial obligations into a single loan, alleviating the economic pressure on those who allocate a significant portion of their income to debt service. This strategy facilitates budget reorganization, allowing individuals to regain spending capacity for basic needs and savings opportunities, thereby improving their quality of life and financial well-being. The success of this operation depends on evaluating key criteria, such as the age of the obligations and the comparison of interest rates. It is essential to ensure that the new average interest rate is lower than existing rates and that the amortization term is appropriate, as a longer term reduces the monthly payment but may increase the total cost over the long term. Additionally, users have the right to negotiate directly with their financial institutions to request interest rate reductions, taking advantage of more favorable market conditions to adjust their current loans. This topic is relevant to the open data movement because transparency in financial conditions, such as interest rates and terms, empowers citizens to make informed decisions. Access to clear and standardized information about financial products enables users to effectively compare options, fostering a culture of financial education based on verifiable data and promoting fairer and more understandable banking practices for society.
