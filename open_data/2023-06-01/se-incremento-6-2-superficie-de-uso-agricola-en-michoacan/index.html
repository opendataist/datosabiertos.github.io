Se incrementó 6.2% superficie de uso agrícola en Michoacán

Se incrementó 6.2% superficie de uso agrícola en Michoacán

Recent preliminary data from Mexico’s 2022 Agricultural Census reveal that agricultural land use in Michoacán has expanded over the past fifteen years, securing the state a prominent position among Mexico’s leading producers. This growth underscores the region’s enduring importance to the national food supply chain and highlights the sector’s resilience despite broader economic shifts. At the same time, the census indicates a fragmentation of landholdings, with the average size of active production units shrinking even as their total number increases. This trend suggests a shift toward smaller-scale farming operations, challenging traditional models of large agricultural enterprises and pointing to evolving land management strategies across the state. The report also sheds light on demographic changes, noting a predominantly male workforce and rising levels of primary education among farmers. These findings are crucial for open data initiatives, as they provide transparent, granular insights into rural demographics and land distribution, enabling policymakers and researchers to better address issues of equity, education, and sustainable agricultural development.
