Crime statistics reveal a complex landscape of gender-based violence, showing mixed trends in offenses between early 2023 and previous periods. While certain sexual crimes decreased following intensified police operations and community dialogues, significant volumes of assaults and attempted murders against women and children remain alarming, highlighting the urgent need for sustained intervention. The data underscores the persistent severity of domestic and public violence, where a substantial portion of sexual assaults occur in private homes. These figures emphasize that despite tactical improvements in tracking perpetrators, the underlying social issues driving violence against vulnerable groups require deeper, systemic attention rather than solely relying on enforcement metrics. This information is crucial for open data advocates as it demonstrates the value of transparent, granular government statistics. Access to such detailed crime breakdowns allows civil society and researchers to track progress, hold authorities accountable, and identify specific geographic or demographic trends, fostering evidence-based advocacy for social safety and policy reform.
