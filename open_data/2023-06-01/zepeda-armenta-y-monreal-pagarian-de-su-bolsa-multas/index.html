Zepeda: Armenta y Monreal pagarían de su bolsa multas

The Mexican Senate imposes personal fines on legislators who fail to appoint commissioners to the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), underscoring the legal consequences of obstructing transparency institutions. This situation highlights the critical link between judicial enforcement and the functioning of open data governance bodies. Ultimately, it emphasizes that accountability mechanisms are essential to ensure public access to information and protect citizens’ data rights.
