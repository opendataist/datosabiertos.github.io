Halliburton Launches Diskos 2.0 NDR for Norwegian Petroleum Directorate

Halliburton’s Diskos 2.0 represents a significant leap in data infrastructure by being the industry’s first fully cloud-native National Data Repository. This architecture allows users to access and analyze extensive exploration and production data directly within functional software environments, eliminating the need to move information to local networks. By integrating automation and artificial intelligence, the system enhances scalability while significantly reducing storage costs and processing inefficiencies. This approach fundamentally improves how stakeholders interact with complex datasets. Operators, service companies, and academic institutions gain immediate, transparent access to critical seismic and well data without cumbersome copying procedures. The platform’s ability to connect with third-party services further enriches its utility, enabling faster, more informed decision-making through streamlined analytics and interpretation workflows. This development is highly relevant to open_data principles as it demonstrates the power of cloud-native systems in making vast, specialized datasets more accessible and usable. By removing physical data barriers and fostering a transparent, collaborative environment, such platforms encourage broader participation and innovation. Ultimately, it shows how modern infrastructure can transform static data into actionable insights, supporting sustainable industry growth and global energy security.
