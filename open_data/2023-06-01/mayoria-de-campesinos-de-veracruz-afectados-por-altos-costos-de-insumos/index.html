Mayoría de campesinos de Veracruz, afectados por altos costos de insumos

High input costs and climate-related factors severely affect most agricultural producers in Veracruz, highlighting systemic vulnerabilities within rural economies. These challenges underscore the urgent need for transparent and accessible data to inform effective policy interventions. Public statistical agencies such as INEGI provide crucial evidence of these hardships; however, the raw nature of census data often requires further standardization. This dataset is vital for open data initiatives because it reveals the specific socio-economic barriers faced by farmers. By making this information openly available, researchers and policymakers can better design targeted solutions to stabilize the agricultural sector and support affected communities.
