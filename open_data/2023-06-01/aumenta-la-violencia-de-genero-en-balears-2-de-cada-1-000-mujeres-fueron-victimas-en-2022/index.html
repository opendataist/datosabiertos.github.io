The data reveals stark regional disparities in gender-based violence, highlighting how open data exposes geographic hotspots and enables targeted policy interventions. By quantifying victimization rates, authorities can identify areas requiring urgent support and allocate resources more effectively to protect vulnerable populations. Beyond specific incidents, the statistics underscore the interconnected nature of domestic abuse, showing how violence within cohabiting relationships significantly impacts children and other family members. This broader perspective emphasizes the need for comprehensive societal responses that address the root causes of aggression rather than treating individual cases in isolation. Ultimately, this transparency fosters public accountability and raises social awareness about the prevalence of abuse. Open data transforms raw statistics into actionable insights, enabling researchers and policymakers to monitor trends over time and assess the effectiveness of existing legal frameworks in reducing violence against women and families.
