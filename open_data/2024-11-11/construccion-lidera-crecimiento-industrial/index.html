Construcción lidera crecimiento industrial

Chiapas’ industrial sector grew in July 2024, driven primarily by a significant surge in construction. While other industries experienced declines, this specific growth highlights how localized development initiatives can positively influence regional economic indicators. The disparity between sectors underscores the uneven nature of industrial performance across the state. Despite overall positive trends, struggles in energy and manufacturing reveal underlying structural challenges that require targeted policy interventions to ensure balanced economic resilience. This case is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates the importance of accessing granular statistical data to understand local economic dynamics. By analyzing detailed state-level indicators, researchers and policymakers can identify specific growth drivers and bottlenecks, fostering more informed and effective decision-making processes.
