Billie Eilish, Katy Perry y otros 200 artistas publican carta abierta contra la IA | RPP Noticias

Artists and organizations are calling on the technology industry to respect copyright by avoiding the unauthorized use of human-created works to train generative AI. They seek to ensure fair compensation and prevent the cloning of voices, which undermines the artistic and economic value of the music ecosystem. This stance is relevant to open_data because it highlights the tension between training models with public data and protecting intellectual property. It underscores the need for ethical frameworks that balance technological innovation with respect for creators, ensuring that the data used does not violate the fundamental rights of original authors.
