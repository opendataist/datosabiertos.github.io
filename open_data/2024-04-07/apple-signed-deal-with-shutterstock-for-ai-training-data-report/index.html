Apple Signed Deal With Shutterstock for AI Training Data [Report]

Major tech giants like Apple, Amazon, Google, and Meta have signed lucrative agreements with Shutterstock to license vast libraries of images, video, and music for AI training. This widespread procurement highlights the critical dependency of large language models on high-quality, legally sourced datasets to function effectively. These commercial transactions underscore the shifting landscape of data usage in artificial intelligence development. Rather than relying solely on public or scraped content, industry leaders are increasingly prioritizing authorized data pipelines, reflecting a growing recognition of the legal and ethical complexities surrounding intellectual property in AI. This trend is directly relevant to open data as it demonstrates the rising value of structured, licensed information. It suggests a future where open data initiatives must compete with commercial datasets or risk becoming obsolete, emphasizing the need for open data advocates to highlight the unique benefits of accessibility and transparency over proprietary alternatives.
