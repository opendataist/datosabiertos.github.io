La Fed negocia con los bancos la gestión de los riesgos de la inteligencia artificial

The Federal Reserve is engaging in discussions with banks about the risks of artificial intelligence, warning about biases in data while acknowledging its potential to modernize settlements through smart contracts. This dialogue is crucial for open data, as the transparency and quality of datasets are essential for mitigating algorithmic biases and ensuring secure and equitable financial systems.
