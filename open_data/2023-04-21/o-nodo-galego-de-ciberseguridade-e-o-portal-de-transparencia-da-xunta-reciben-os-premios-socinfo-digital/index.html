O Nodo Galego de Ciberseguridade e o Portal de Transparencia da Xunta reciben os premios 'Socinfo Digital'

COPE has released a free app for iOS and Android, enabling real-time radio streaming and immediate news notifications. This digital evolution demonstrates how traditional media is adopting mobile-first, open-access technologies to deliver timely information directly to users’ devices. The initiative highlights the broader trend of media organizations leveraging accessible digital platforms to enhance public engagement. By offering comprehensive content through a single, free application, COPE exemplifies how open distribution channels can significantly expand audience reach. This release is relevant to open data as it underscores the shift toward transparent, ubiquitous information access. It illustrates the importance of making public service media available through interoperable, user-friendly interfaces, thereby supporting principles of open access and digital inclusion in modern journalism.
