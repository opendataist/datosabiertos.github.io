Constellation Brands entregará 500 becas a estudiantes de Coahuila

Constellation Brands has launched its “El Valor de Educar” scholarship program, awarding 500 scholarships to students in Coahuila to cover educational expenses for one year. Now in its third edition, this initiative continues the company’s commitment to social development, using a digital platform to manage applications and a specialized committee to allocate the awards. The process prioritizes equity, taking into account socioeconomic factors and giving priority to vulnerable groups, reflecting a corporate model that integrates social responsibility with educational accessibility. The program’s success is grounded in a comprehensive approach that goes beyond financial support. It includes complementary workshops designed to strengthen students’ socioemotional and professional skills, as well as those of their families. This holistic structure aims not only to prevent school dropout but also to guide young people in defining their academic and life trajectories, ensuring that financial aid translates into sustainable long-term personal and professional development. This case is relevant to the field of open data, as it demonstrates how technology facilitates transparency and efficiency in the management of social resources. The implementation of digital platforms enables the standardization of data on beneficiaries, selection criteria, and outcomes, creating an information ecosystem that can be analyzed to optimize public and private policies. Moreover, the collection of demographic and impact data provides a model for measuring the effectiveness of educational interventions, promoting an evidence-based culture to improve inclusion and access to education.
