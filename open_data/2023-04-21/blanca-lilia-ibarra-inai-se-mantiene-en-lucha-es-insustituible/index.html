Blanca Lilia Ibarra: INAI se mantiene en lucha; es insustituible

The INAI defends its existence as an irreplaceable guarantor of transparency and access to information, rejecting proposals to transfer its functions to other agencies. Its commissioners argue that no existing institution can replicate its mandate to ensure government accountability and protect personal data. Without this specific body, the right to information would be severely compromised, leaving citizens without a mechanism to demand transparency from thousands of obligated entities. The institute emphasizes that its role is essential for sustaining democracy and reducing social polarization. By providing citizens with the tools to demand accountability, the INAI helps counter misinformation and fosters respectful debate. The organization views itself as a critical defense against democratic regression, asserting that its modest cost justifies its contribution to protecting the democratic gains achieved over decades and empowering society over unchecked power. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the institutional framework necessary for data availability and integrity. The INAI acts as the ultimate enforcer of open data principles by compelling public bodies to release information. Its existence ensures that data is not just collected but is accessible to the public, enabling civil society to utilize information for oversight, thereby validating the core purpose of open data initiatives in fostering trust and active citizenship.
