The situation of press workers four months into Milei's term in office

The current administration is engaged in a systematic erosion of press freedom and the right to information, characterized by direct repression of journalists and political hostility toward the media. This environment has enabled private media companies to exploit the crisis as a pretext for deepening labor precarity, aiming to dismantle worker rights and reduce journalism to a model of poverty wages. The government’s actions have effectively created a landscape where the craft of journalism is under sustained threat, with public narratives often overshadowed by the unchecked spread of misinformation that authorities appear to tolerate. This structural attack manifests in widespread labor violations across the private press sector, including unpaid wages, illegal dismissals, and refused salary adjustments. These maneuvers are not merely economic struggles but strategic efforts to intimidate unionization and suppress independent journalism. By leveraging the suspension of official budgets as a justification for worsening conditions, media owners are consolidating a model that prioritizes cost-cutting over the ethical obligation to inform the public, leaving workers in precarious situations while compromising the integrity of news reporting. Union organizations recognize that collective struggle and grassroots solidarity are the only effective defenses against these assaults on labor and information rights. The relevance to open_data lies in the foundational premise that robust, protected journalism is essential for transparent governance and the free flow of information. When press workers are oppressed and media outlets are forced into precarious financial models, the resulting degradation of journalistic standards undermines the accountability mechanisms necessary for an open society. Strengthening labor rights in the media sector is thus inextricably linked to preserving the integrity of public discourse and ensuring that data and news remain accessible and reliable for all citizens.
