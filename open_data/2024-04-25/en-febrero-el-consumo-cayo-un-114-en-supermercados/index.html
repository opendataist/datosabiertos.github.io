En febrero, el consumo cayó un 11,4% en supermercados

The article highlights a sharp decline in consumer spending across supermarkets, wholesalers, and shopping centers, signaling a severe economic contraction. This trend reflects diminished purchasing power and reduced household budgets, directly impacting tax revenues such as VAT. Prices for essential goods have surged dramatically, yet sales volumes have plummeted. This divergence indicates that inflation is eroding real income, forcing consumers to cut back on non-essential purchases despite nominal price increases, creating a challenging environment for retail businesses. This data is crucial for open-data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent economic statistics enable analysts to track real-time market health. By making such granular sales data accessible, researchers and policymakers can better understand inflation’s impact on welfare and design evidence-based social and economic interventions.
