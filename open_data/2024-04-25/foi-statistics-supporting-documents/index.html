FOI Statistics: supporting documents

The document details a long-term commitment to transparency regarding Freedom of Information statistics. By consistently updating development plans over many years, the publisher ensures that the public is kept informed about upcoming changes and methodological improvements. This ongoing process highlights a dedication to openness and accountability in government data release. This practice is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how statutory transparency obligations can drive better data governance. Regularly publishing plans allows users to anticipate changes and prepare for new insights. It builds trust by showing that data production is not static but evolves with improved understanding and technical capabilities. Ultimately, this history of updates signifies a mature approach to public information. It suggests that the statistical body is actively managing the quality and accessibility of its outputs. For those interested in open data, this serves as a model for how organizations can maintain engagement with users through proactive communication about their data landscapes.
