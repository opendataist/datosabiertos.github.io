La firma de hipotecas se deja un 20,1% en C-LM tras empeorar su evolución interanual en febrero

The article highlights a significant contraction in the mortgage market in Castilla-La Mancha, reflecting broader regional economic shifts. By analyzing these financial trends, we observe how localized housing data correlates with national real estate performance. The region experienced substantial declines in both the number of signed mortgages and the total capital lent compared to previous periods. This downward trend places it among the worst-performing autonomous communities, indicating a distinct lack of liquidity and demand in the local property sector relative to other parts of Spain.
