Microsoft Reveals Which Jobs Are Most & Least Affected By AI - Trak.in - Indian Business of Tech, Mobile & Startups

Microsoft Reveals Which Jobs Are Most & Least Affected By AI - Trak.in - Indian Business of Tech, Mobile & Startups

Microsoft’s research analyzes anonymized user interactions to create an applicability score, revealing how AI reshapes specific work processes rather than completely replacing entire occupations. This approach highlights that while AI significantly assists with tasks involving research, writing, and communication, it currently lacks the capacity to independently perform any single job role. Professions heavily reliant on language processing and data interpretation, such as journalism and translation, face the highest degree of disruption. In contrast, roles requiring physical presence, manual dexterity, or complex human interaction remain largely insulated from immediate automation. This distinction underscores the current limitations of digital intelligence in replicating the tangible and interpersonal aspects of certain livelihoods. This study is vital for open data because it demonstrates how large-scale, privacy-preserving interaction logs can yield actionable insights into economic shifts. By publishing such findings, researchers contribute to transparent datasets that help society understand the tangible impacts of emerging technologies. It encourages the responsible use of public information to navigate the evolving landscape of work and automation.
