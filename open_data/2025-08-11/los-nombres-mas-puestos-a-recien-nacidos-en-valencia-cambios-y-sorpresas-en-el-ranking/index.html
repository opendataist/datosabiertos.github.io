Los nombres más puestos a recién nacidos en Valencia: cambios y sorpresas en el ranking

Los nombres más puestos a recién nacidos en Valencia: cambios y sorpresas en el ranking

The recent analysis of Valencia’s municipal population registry highlights shifting naming preferences among newborns in 2024. For girls, Sofía remains the dominant choice, maintaining its leading position for two consecutive years. Notably, Vega has surged to second place, surpassing Olivia, while traditional names like Aitana have seen a significant decline in popularity, reflecting evolving cultural trends and parental tastes. Among boys, Martín has dethroned Mateo, ending the latter’s three-year reign as the most popular name. Other rising figures include Lucas and Alejandro, whereas established names like Marc and Leo have dropped in the rankings. This movement indicates a dynamic landscape where family choices are constantly reshaping the most common names, moving away from previously hegemonic choices toward new favorites. This dataset is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies how municipal government statistics serve as valuable, freely accessible public resources. By publishing detailed demographic information, the city’s statistical office empowers citizens, researchers, and policymakers to analyze social trends transparently. It demonstrates the practical utility of open data in fostering civic engagement and understanding community dynamics without restrictions.
