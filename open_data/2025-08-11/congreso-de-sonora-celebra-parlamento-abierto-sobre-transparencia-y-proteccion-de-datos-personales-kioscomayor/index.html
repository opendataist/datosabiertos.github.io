Congreso de Sonora celebra parlamento abierto sobre transparencia y protección de datos personales - Kioscomayor

Congreso de Sonora celebra parlamento abierto sobre transparencia y protección de datos personales - Kioscomayor

The Congress of Sonora convened an open parliamentary session to examine legislative reforms concerning transparency and the protection of personal data. The primary objective is to strengthen the state’s legal framework by aligning it with federal standards. Key proposals include reassigning oversight functions and establishing a new autonomous body under the Anti-Corruption Secretariat, with the aim of fostering a more modern and digitally driven public administration that better safeguards citizens’ rights. Participants, including government officials and representatives of civil society, stressed the need for clear, accessible, and reliable processes for managing public information. The discussion underscored the importance of establishing robust mechanisms to protect citizens’ personal data. These reforms seek to enhance coordination among the responsible entities, ensuring that the legislation effectively addresses current needs and reinforces accountability. This process is highly relevant to open data, as it directly impacts the legal infrastructure governing access to information and privacy. Successful implementation will determine the efficiency of data-sharing practices and the security of personal information held by public entities. Ultimately, these changes aim to build greater citizen trust through transparent and accountable governance, setting a precedent for how public data is managed and protected in the region.
