Desaparece joven censista e INEI hace un llamado a nivel nacional para sumarse a su búsqueda - El Tiempo

Desaparece joven censista e INEI hace un llamado a nivel nacional para sumarse a su búsqueda - El Tiempo

The article highlights a coordinated search effort for Aydaluz, a census worker who disappeared while preparing for the national census. This incident underscores the critical, yet often hazardous, role that field workers play in gathering essential demographic data. Their efforts form the foundational layer of national statistics, directly influencing public policy and resource allocation decisions that shape the country’s future development. The relevance to open data lies in the integrity and continuity of information collection. The disappearance disrupts the flow of raw data, jeopardizing the completeness and accuracy of upcoming datasets. It reminds stakeholders that behind every anonymized statistic is a human effort dependent on safety and institutional support, which are prerequisites for reliable, transparent, and accessible public information systems. Ultimately, the case illustrates the human cost of statistical production. Ensuring the security of data gatherers is not merely a humanitarian concern but a structural necessity for maintaining trustworthy open data ecosystems. Stronger protocols protect the quality of information available to society, ensuring that the insights derived from census data remain robust, timely, and actionable for the public good.
