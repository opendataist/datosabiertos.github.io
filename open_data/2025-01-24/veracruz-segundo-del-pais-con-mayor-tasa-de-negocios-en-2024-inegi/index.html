Veracruz, segundo del País con mayor tasa de negocios en 2024: INEGI

The 2024 Economic Census reveals significant shifts in Mexico’s business landscape, highlighting Veracruz as a key economic hub alongside traditional leaders like the State of Mexico. This data underscores the dynamic nature of regional industrial distribution, showing how the service and commerce sectors drive local employment and establish new market centers across the country. The census also tracks the steady growth of enterprises over recent years, reflecting an expanding private sector. By documenting shifts in the number of businesses and workforce participation, these statistics provide essential evidence for understanding national economic resilience and the evolving structure of the Mexican labor market. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it offers transparent, granular insights into economic activity and inclusion efforts, such as the hiring of workers with disabilities. Making this detailed, accessible data available empowers researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze trends, foster accountability, and drive informed decisions that support sustainable economic development and social equity.
