Duplicó Culiacán su percepción de inseguridad el último año

The INEGI data reveals a significant national increase in perceived insecurity, driven largely by extreme spikes in cities like Culiacán following cartel conflicts. This shift highlights how localized violent dynamics drastically alter public safety perceptions, creating stark regional disparities in citizen trust and fear. These findings are crucial for open data because they demonstrate how transparent, localized statistical indicators expose underlying governance challenges. By making such specific socio-political metrics publicly available, citizens can better assess government effectiveness and hold authorities accountable for failing to address escalating crime rates.
