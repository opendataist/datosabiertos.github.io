Enfermedades del corazón, la principal causa de muerte en México en 2024: INEGI

Enfermedades del corazón, la principal causa de muerte en México en 2024: INEGI

The INEGI’s preliminary data reveal that cardiovascular diseases remain Mexico’s leading cause of death, highlighting a critical public health challenge driven by increasing incidence among younger adults with chronic conditions. This trend underscores the urgent need to improve preventive care and the management of cardiovascular risk factors across all age groups. Furthermore, the report shows significant demographic and regional disparities in mortality rates, with men and older populations being disproportionately affected. These insights enable the identification of high-risk areas and vulnerable population segments, facilitating targeted interventions to reduce mortality in specific regions and groups. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it supports evidence-based policymaking and health transparency. By making detailed mortality statistics accessible, researchers and citizens can analyze health trends, demand accountability, and contribute to solutions that address the structural determinants of public health outcomes in Mexico.
