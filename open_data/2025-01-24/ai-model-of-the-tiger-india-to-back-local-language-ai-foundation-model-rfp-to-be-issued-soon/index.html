AI (model) of the tiger: India to back local language AI foundation model, RFP to be issued soon

AI (model) of the tiger: India to back local language AI foundation model, RFP to be issued soon

India is actively pursuing the development of sovereign, open-source foundational AI models tailored for local languages, driven by geopolitical constraints on technology exports. This strategic shift moves away from relying solely on foreign platforms, aiming to ensure national security and technological independence in an era where AI capabilities are increasingly weaponized as diplomatic tools. By fostering a consortium of industry and academia, the government seeks to mitigate risks associated with restrictive trade policies and prevent isolation from global AI advancements. The decision reflects a consensus that both indigenous foundational models and open-source contributions are essential components of a robust AI ecosystem. This approach addresses concerns that strict limitations on high-performance computing hardware, such as graphics processing units, could stifle innovation and leave the nation behind in the race toward Artificial General Intelligence. Establishing local training capabilities ensures that Indian businesses and researchers can compete globally without being overly dependent on external infrastructure or restricted access to advanced computational resources. This initiative is critical to open data advocacy as it champions the creation of diverse, high-quality datasets and model architectures that reflect specific linguistic and cultural contexts. By prioritizing open sourcing for these regional models, India encourages broader accessibility and collaborative improvement, challenging the dominance of proprietary systems controlled by a few foreign tech giants. This move promotes a more equitable and resilient global AI landscape, where open data practices facilitate inclusive innovation and reduce reliance on centralized, potentially censored, or expensive commercial offerings.
