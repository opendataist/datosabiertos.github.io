GASTAT: Saudi Non-oil Exports Increased by 19.7% in November 2024

Middle East crude oil exports have significantly rebounded, recovering from recent wartime disruptions to reach levels not seen since the conflict began in February. This resurgence is largely driven by Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates shifting export routes to bypass hazardous areas, thereby restoring critical flow through the Strait of Hormuz. Although volumes remain lower than pre-conflict peaks, the data highlights the resilience of global energy supply chains under pressure. The recovery demonstrates how major producers adapt logistics to mitigate the impact of geopolitical instability, ensuring continued market access despite targeted attacks on infrastructure. This information is vital for open data initiatives because it illustrates the importance of transparent, accessible trade statistics in monitoring global economic stability. Reliable data feeds enable researchers and policymakers to track the real-time effects of conflict on energy markets, fostering informed decision-making and accountability in international relations.
