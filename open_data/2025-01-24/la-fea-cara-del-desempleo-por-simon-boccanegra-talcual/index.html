This article highlights how pressing Enter to initiate a search is a common user action, underscoring the importance of accessible and intuitive interfaces in open data platforms. The emphasis on user experience highlights the need for open data initiatives to prioritize ease of use alongside data availability. This approach encourages broader participation and utilization of open data, fostering a more informed and engaged citizenry. Moreover, this attention to interface design reflects a commitment to inclusivity, ensuring that individuals with varying levels of technical expertise can effectively interact with open data resources. This inclusivity is crucial for realizing the full potential of open data in driving societal benefits.
