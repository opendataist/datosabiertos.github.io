Saltillo: Encarecen 40% productos de higiene personal

Personal care products in Saltillo have experienced significant price increases, with some categories rising by 40% over a single year. Essential items such as shampoo, soap, and sanitary pads have become notably more expensive, reflecting a broader trend of inflation in everyday necessities. This surge is affecting household budgets, forcing consumers to reassess their spending habits and brand loyalties as the cost of basic hygiene products escalates. The data shows that not only physical goods but also aesthetic services have doubled in price, further straining financial resources. Consumers report strong brand attachment, often ignoring cheaper alternatives despite the economic pressure. However, experts suggest that this situation presents an opportunity for buyers to explore more affordable options, potentially shifting market dynamics and reducing dependence on established, higher-priced brands. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the practical utility of statistical agencies like INEGI in tracking real-time economic shifts. By making price indices publicly available, open data empowers citizens and researchers to understand inflation’s impact on specific sectors. Transparency in such metrics allows for better policy analysis and informed consumer decisions, demonstrating how accessible statistics can drive societal awareness and economic resilience in the face of rising living costs.
