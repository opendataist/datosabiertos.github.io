Sen. Ron Johnson accuses CDC official in charge of COVID-19 injections of deleting records amid Congressional GOP scrutiny – NaturalNews.com

Sen. Ron Johnson has accused CDC official Dr. Tom Shimabukuro of deleting or losing official records, potentially obstructing congressional investigations into the origins of the pandemic. Johnson has urged top officials, including the Attorney General and the FBI, to investigate whether these actions were intentional efforts to evade oversight and Freedom of Information Act requests. Such conduct could violate federal record-keeping laws and constitute contempt of Congress, highlighting the severe legal consequences for attempting to conceal government communications during critical inquiries. The allegations are part of a broader pattern involving other high-ranking health officials, such as Dr. David Morens, who previously admitted to using personal email to avoid public scrutiny. These incidents suggest a systemic effort to suppress information regarding the virus’s origins and vaccine safety, potentially influenced by senior leadership. The repeated evasion of transparency mechanisms raises serious concerns about accountability within federal agencies and the integrity of official records during public health emergencies. This article is highly relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of accessible, unaltered public records in maintaining governmental transparency. When officials manipulate or destroy data to avoid scrutiny, it undermines the public’s ability to trust scientific and political decision-making. Ensuring that all government-generated information remains intact and searchable is essential for holding institutions accountable and preserving the integrity of the historical record.
