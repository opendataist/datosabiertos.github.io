¿Se acabaron los boletos para Julieta Venegas en el FIC? Inusual ‘sold out’ indigna en redes

¿Se acabaron los boletos para Julieta Venegas en el FIC? Inusual ‘sold out’ indigna en redes

The rapid sell-out of tickets for the 2025 Festival Internacional Cervantino reveals systemic vulnerabilities in public event management, sparking concerns about equitable access and potential scalping. This situation highlights the urgent need for transparent, fair, and resilient ticketing infrastructure that prioritizes genuine attendees over speculative resellers. The incident underscores critical gaps in open data regarding distribution algorithms and real-time inventory visibility. Without publicly accessible metrics on sales velocity and bot detection, stakeholders cannot hold organizers accountable, making transparency a prerequisite for restoring public trust in large-scale cultural events. This case is vital for the open data movement as it illustrates how opaque systems fail citizens. Promoting accessible data on allocation processes and user behavior ensures that public resources serve the community equitably, demonstrating why open standards are essential for modernizing civic engagement and preventing market manipulation in public service delivery.
