Analistas alertan sobre la inflación acumulada y sugieren tomar medidas

Analistas alertan sobre la inflación acumulada y sugieren tomar medidas

Analysts warn that Bolivia’s recent decline in monthly inflation signals economic stagnation rather than relief. The low figure stems from a lack of major disruptions, not underlying stability, suggesting the economy is decelerating while facing high cumulative price increases over the first half of the year. Experts predict annual inflation could exceed thirty percent if urgent measures are not implemented. Current trends significantly surpass international forecasts and official government targets, highlighting a severe disconnect between policy expectations and the reality of escalating costs for citizens and businesses. This case underscores the critical importance of open data in economic governance. Transparent, timely statistics allow for an accurate diagnosis of monetary health. Without accessible and reliable data, policymakers cannot effectively address inflation, leaving the public unaware of the true economic deterioration and hindering informed public discourse.
