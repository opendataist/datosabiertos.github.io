Destaca José Chedraui ahorro de 50 mdp en gasolina; recursos serán destinados a la SSC

The mayor emphasizes the substantial municipal savings achieved through efficient procurement, with the aim of redirecting these resources toward public security. By establishing new concession requirements for advertising spaces, the administration ensures that private entities contribute significant funds to the city’s coffers, reversing previous practices in which financial benefits were negligible. This approach not only covers operational costs but also generates surplus revenue dedicated to enhancing citizen safety and improving public transport infrastructure. These financial gains are strategically allocated to upgrade police equipment, such as body cameras and communication tools, ensuring that officers have the necessary resources to perform their duties effectively. The administration projects surpassing previous savings benchmarks, with a clear commitment to allocating a substantial portion of the municipal budget to the security sector. This investment aims to create a positive feedback loop in which better equipment and training lead to improved law enforcement outcomes and greater public trust. At the same time, the government addresses internal corruption by rigorously applying trust tests to cleanse the police force, distinguishing current efforts from past failures. This transparency in financial management and personnel vetting is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates a commitment to accountability. By publicly detailing how saved funds and concession revenues are utilized, the administration provides citizens with clear, accessible metrics on governance performance, fostering a culture of openness and civic engagement that relies on verifiable, transparent data regarding public resource allocation.
