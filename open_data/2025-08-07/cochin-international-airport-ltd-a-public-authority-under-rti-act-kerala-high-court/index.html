Cochin International Airport Ltd A 'Public Authority' Under RTI Act : Kerala High Court

Cochin International Airport Ltd A 'Public Authority' Under RTI Act : Kerala High Court

The court has definitively classified Cochin International Airport Limited (CIAL) as a 'public authority' under the Right to Information Act. This ruling is based on the finding that CIAL was established by a specific government order and relies heavily on state-owned land and resources for its operations. Consequently, the entity is legally obligated to disclose information and comply with all statutory requirements mandated for public bodies, affirming the previous decision of the State Information Commission. Furthermore, the judiciary determined that the government exercises complete control over CIAL’s management and governance. With a majority of board directors being ex-officio government nominees, the state’s influence is decisive rather than nominal, ensuring that corporate decisions align with governmental authority. This structural dominance satisfies the legal criteria for governmental control, reinforcing the premise that the airport operates as an instrumentality of the state rather than an independent private enterprise. Finally, the court recognized that CIAL receives substantial financing from both state and central governments, meeting the financial threshold for public authority status. This decision is critically relevant to open data initiatives as it ensures greater transparency in a major infrastructure entity. By establishing this legal precedent, the ruling mandates that public funds and government-controlled assets must be subject to public scrutiny, thereby strengthening accountability and the free flow of information regarding public sector operations.
