Orange and OpenAI Collaborate on Trusted, Responsible, and Inclusive AI

Orange and OpenAI Collaborate on Trusted, Responsible, and Inclusive AI

Orange and OpenAI are collaborating to deploy advanced open-weight reasoning models within Orange’s trusted infrastructure. This strategic partnership allows Orange to offer sovereign AI solutions that ensure data privacy and regulatory compliance across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. By hosting these models locally, Orange maintains full control over sensitive information while providing enterprises with flexible, secure, and high-performance AI capabilities. The initiative emphasizes a "frugal" and responsible approach to artificial intelligence, prioritizing sustainability and efficiency. Orange leverages its engineering expertise to customize and distill these models, reducing computational power, data requirements, and energy consumption. This optimization not only lowers operational costs and environmental impact but also enables the development of specialized sub-models tailored for specific network operations and enterprise use cases without compromising performance. Significantly, this collaboration accelerates digital inclusion in Africa by integrating local languages into AI systems, enhancing customer support and public services. Orange plans to release these customized models as open source for local governments, fostering broader access to advanced technology. This project serves as a blueprint for responsible, large-scale AI deployment, demonstrating how open models can drive innovation while maintaining ethical governance and societal benefit. This article is relevant to open_data because it highlights the practical value of open-weight models, which allow organizations to adapt, customize, and share technology. By committing to release customized models as open source for public authorities, Orange contributes to the open data ecosystem, promoting transparency, accessibility, and community-driven innovation in artificial intelligence.
