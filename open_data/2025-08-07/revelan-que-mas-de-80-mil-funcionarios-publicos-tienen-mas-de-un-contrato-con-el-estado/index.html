Revelan que más de 80 mil funcionarios públicos tienen más de un contrato con el Estado

Revelan que más de 80 mil funcionarios públicos tienen más de un contrato con el Estado

A recent transparency audit reveals that more than 80,000 public officials hold multiple contracts across various state entities, highlighting significant overlaps in public sector employment. The data shows that most of these individuals maintain dual roles, primarily within municipalities or specific institutions such as the education sector. This multiplicity of records raises concerns about potential incompatibilities and unclear working hours, even when such arrangements are technically legal. The findings are crucial for open data because they expose weaknesses in how public institutions manage and disclose employment information. Many agencies failed to properly respond to access requests, sometimes releasing un-anonymized sensitive data or providing inconsistent information. These lapses indicate a broader struggle with transparency standards and the accurate maintenance of public registers, which are fundamental for citizen oversight. Ultimately, this inspection underscores the urgent need for better normative controls and improved data mechanisms within the state. While not all cases imply wrongdoing, the systemic opacity hinders effective accountability. Strengthening open data practices is essential to clarify legal boundaries, prevent conflicts of interest, and ensure that public servant contracts are fully accessible and understandable to the citizenry.
