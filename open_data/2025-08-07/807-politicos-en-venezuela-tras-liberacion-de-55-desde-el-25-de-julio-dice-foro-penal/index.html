807 políticos en Venezuela tras liberación de 55 desde el 25 de julio, dice Foro Penal

Foren Penal reports over 800 political prisoners in Venezuela, highlighting a complex dynamic of detentions and releases following a diplomatic exchange between Maduro and the United States. This data underscores the severe human rights violations and systematic repression criticized by international organizations and opposition leaders, who accuse the government of imprisoning critics and causing numerous disappearances despite official denials. The persistence of these arbitrary detentions reveals a pattern of impunity and the weaponization of the justice system against dissent. The ongoing crisis affects diverse groups, including women and minors, illustrating the broad societal impact of state-sponsored repression and the urgent need for international accountability mechanisms to address these abuses. This information is crucial for open data initiatives as it provides empirical evidence of human rights trends in a conflict zone. Transparent, verifiable datasets enable researchers and advocates to monitor compliance with international laws, challenge state narratives with factual records, and drive global policy responses to authoritarianism.
