Censo Nacional 2025: donde todos los peruanos suman por un país mejor [Informe]

Censo Nacional 2025: donde todos los peruanos suman por un país mejor [Informe]

The 2025 National Census in Peru marks a significant technological evolution by replacing paper with digital tablets, a shift that drastically reduces result processing time and enhances the safety of field agents through real-time location tracking and emergency alerts. This modernization reflects a broader commitment to efficient, secure data collection methods, ensuring that statistical information is gathered reliably while protecting both the census takers and the integrity of the process. The survey focuses on vital demographic and housing characteristics, excluding sensitive financial data to address public concerns about privacy and potential fraud. By clarifying identification protocols and emphasizing the civic importance of participation, the initiative aims to rebuild trust among citizens who may fear scams. The resulting accurate data is crucial for understanding population distribution and living conditions, which directly informs the allocation of resources and the design of effective social policies. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how modernizing public statistics improves data quality, accessibility, and public trust. The transition to digital tools not only accelerates the availability of information for researchers and policymakers but also establishes transparent verification mechanisms that can serve as a model for other open government initiatives. Ultimately, it highlights the importance of secure, user-friendly data collection in fostering an informed society where accurate public statistics drive better governance and development.
