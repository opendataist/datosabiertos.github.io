Madrid refuerza la transparencia y la innovación con su nuevo Portal de Datos Abiertos

The Madrid City Council has launched its new Open Data Portal, marking a qualitative leap in the management of public information by migrating to a platform based on open-source technology. This renewal responds to the need to adapt the infrastructure to European technological and regulatory advancements, ensuring a more robust, sustainable, and scalable foundation for the future growth of the service. The new design prioritizes ease of reuse and universal accessibility, integrating advanced features such as APIs that enable automated access to data. By centralizing the catalog and decentralizing maintenance, it facilitates information federation among different agencies and provides enhanced tools for analysis and visualization, ensuring an intuitive experience for all users. This initiative is significant for the open data movement because it demonstrates how the adoption of open technical standards and user-centered design can enhance institutional transparency and civic innovation. By complying with European directives and improving the experience for data reusers, the portal not only increases the visibility of public information but also fosters a more inclusive and efficient ecosystem for creating social value from municipal data.
