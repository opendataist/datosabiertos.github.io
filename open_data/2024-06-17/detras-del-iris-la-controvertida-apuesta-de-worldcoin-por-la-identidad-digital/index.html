Detrás del iris: la controvertida apuesta de Worldcoin por la identidad digital

Worldcoin represents a turning point at the intersection of digital identity, financial inclusion, and data protection, raising a fundamental ethical debate about the validity of exchanging sensitive biometric data for economic incentives. The project seeks to verify humanity by scanning the iris to generate decentralized identities, arguing that this technique is more secure and private than traditional credentials. However, the mass collection of biological information, driven by the distribution of cryptocurrencies, has been criticized as a potential exploitation of financial vulnerability in developing countries, questioning the true autonomy of participants who sell their unique traits for immediate income. The relevance to the open data movement lies in the tension between technological transparency and individual privacy, demonstrating how business models based on biometric data can directly clash with global regulatory frameworks such as the GDPR. The response of international authorities, which have suspended or investigated operations due to security risks and lack of justification in data processing, underscores the critical need to develop open standards that prioritize user sovereignty over the efficiency of mass data collection. This case illustrates the ethical limits when technological innovation outpaces the legal capacity to protect personal biometric characteristics. The controversy highlights that decentralization does not automatically guarantee privacy, especially when data is used as a bargaining chip. The discussion generated by Worldcoin compels the open data community to reflect on the responsibility in handling biometric information irreversibly linked to physical identity. Ultimately, the project underscores the urgency of creating protocols that safeguard fundamental rights without hindering progress toward a truly inclusive and secure digital identity, where trust does not depend on granting economic privileges in exchange for relinquishing privacy.
