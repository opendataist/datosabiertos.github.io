¿Hay más padres casados o solteros? Estas son las cifras reveladoras del INE - Nacionales - ABC Color

The National Statistics Institute reveals the demographic and economic realities of Paraguayan fathers, highlighting that a majority reside in urban areas and hold formal employment. This data underscores the crucial role of official statistics in shaping social policies and understanding household dynamics. A substantial proportion of fathers serve as the primary economic providers, yet educational attainment varies significantly between urban and rural areas. These disparities in schooling and occupational distribution illustrate structural inequalities that require targeted government intervention. Understanding these statistical trends is vital for open data initiatives, as they expose gaps in access to education and labor markets. Transparent, accessible datasets empower citizens to advocate for equitable resource distribution and improved social welfare systems for families across the country.
