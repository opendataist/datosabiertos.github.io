Chihuahua Capital has demonstrated significant advances in urban competitiveness and quality of life, positioning itself among the top ten cities nationally thanks to strategies focused on education, accessibility, and economic development. This progress is not coincidental but rather the result of a government commitment to transparency and citizen participation, as evidenced by its membership in the Open Government Partnership and international fiscal management certifications. The city has consolidated itself as a key tourist destination and a hub for attracting talent, reflecting how local efficiency can tangibly improve the well-being of its residents. The relevance of this case for open data lies in its integration with the Open Government model. By designing its first Open Government Action Plan through co-creation with citizens, the municipal administration prioritizes institutional transparency and access to information. This approach enables data and processes to serve as tools for accountability and participation, demonstrating that administrative openness is a fundamental driver of public trust and investment attraction, differentiating itself from the opaque trends of the federal government. However, the article underscores the critical need for youth to have an active voice in these spaces. The local government promotes mechanisms such as participatory budgeting and citizen councils, recognizing that the inclusion of young perspectives is vital for sustainable development. Ultimately, the main lesson is that sustainable competitiveness depends on open systems that foster social innovation and retain talent, using transparency not merely as an end in itself, but as a means to build a fairer, more dynamic city better prepared for global challenges.
