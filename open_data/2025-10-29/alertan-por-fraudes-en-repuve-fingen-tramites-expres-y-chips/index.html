Alertan por fraudes en REPUVE; fingen trámites “exprés” y chips

Alertan por fraudes en REPUVE; fingen trámites “exprés” y chips

Government authorities warn against fraudulent websites and social media profiles impersonating official institutions to scam citizens seeking vehicle registration services. These deceptive actors exploit logos and technical language to steal personal data and illicit payments, highlighting the critical need for digital verification in public service access. The article emphasizes that official vehicle status consultations are free and only available through secure government domains, while clarifying that only specific foreign vehicle regularization involves a regulated fee. This distinction underscores the importance of transparency in public administration, ensuring citizens can distinguish between legitimate, open procedures and predatory financial traps. This warning is vital for open data advocacy, as it illustrates how opaque or unverified digital channels facilitate fraud. It reinforces the necessity of promoting authentic, publicly accessible platforms with clear, verified endpoints. Protecting data integrity and ensuring secure, open access to official records remain fundamental to preventing identity theft and maintaining public trust in digital governance.
