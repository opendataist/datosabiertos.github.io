El Gobierno regional ampliará en 2026 la red pública de atención a la discapacidad con más de 200 nuevas plazas

El Gobierno regional ampliará en 2026 la red pública de atención a la discapacidad con más de 200 nuevas plazas

Castilla-La Mancha is strengthening its participatory governance model by integrating citizens directly into decision-making processes through Open Government Councils. This initiative allows vulnerable groups, such as people with intellectual disabilities, to propose policies and demand rights directly from regional authorities. By shifting from governing for people to governing with them, the administration aims to foster a more inclusive society where social policies are co-created based on the lived experiences and needs of the community. The regional government has committed to expanding public disability support services, emphasizing deinstitutionalization and personal autonomy. This strategy focuses on providing nearby, personalized care that enables individuals to live independently in their chosen communities. The approach underscores a fundamental shift in social policy, prioritizing dignity and individual choice over traditional institutional models, thereby reinforcing the region's reputation as a leader in inclusive social practices. This development is significant for open data because it highlights the critical role of participatory governance in ensuring that digital transformations, such as AI integration, address accessibility gaps rather than exacerbating them. Transparent, direct dialogue ensures that data-driven policies regarding cognitive accessibility and digital inclusion are grounded in real-world user needs. Ultimately, this model demonstrates how open government mechanisms can bridge the gap between technological advancement and social equity, ensuring that open data initiatives actively promote equal opportunities for all citizens.
