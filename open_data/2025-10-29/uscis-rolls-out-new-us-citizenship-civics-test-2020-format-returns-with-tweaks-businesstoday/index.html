USCIS rolls out new US citizenship civics test; 2020 format returns with tweaks - BusinessToday

USCIS rolls out new US citizenship civics test; 2020 format returns with tweaks - BusinessToday

The U.S. naturalization process is undergoing significant revision, reinstating a more rigorous civics assessment aligned with recent executive directives emphasizing civic assimilation. By expanding the question pool and adjusting passing criteria, the agency aims to ensure applicants possess a robust understanding of American principles, marking a decisive policy shift in citizenship standards. Procedural efficiency is also prioritized, allowing testing to conclude early once a pass or fail threshold is met. This change streamlines operations while maintaining the oral format and unchanged English requirements, ensuring the evaluation remains focused on substantive knowledge rather than exhaustive questioning. This development is highly relevant to open data advocates, as it highlights the critical need for transparent, accessible information regarding government administrative changes. Clear, publicly available resources are essential for applicants to navigate evolving legal requirements, ensuring fairness and accountability in the naturalization system.
