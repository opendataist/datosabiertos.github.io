¿Por qué las pensiones de Baleares son de las más bajas de España?

¿Por qué las pensiones de Baleares son de las más bajas de España?

Balearic pensioners face significantly lower incomes than the Spanish average due to a labor market dominated by low-wage, temporary tourism jobs and high numbers of minimum-contribution self-employed individuals. This structural dependency on non-qualified service roles limits salary growth and social security contributions, resulting in modest retirement benefits despite the region’s economic prominence. Reversing this trend requires diversifying the economy through fiscal incentives to attract technology and healthcare sectors, rather than relying solely on tourism. Experts suggest that establishing a Regional Economic Balance system could facilitate this shift, aiming to create higher-quality employment opportunities that generate stronger contribution bases for future pensions and reduce current structural vulnerabilities. However, even with potential reforms, Balearic retirees suffer from a higher cost of living, particularly regarding housing and basic goods, which erodes purchasing power. This disparity highlights the critical need for open data initiatives that transparently map regional economic inequalities and cost-of-living variations. Such data is essential for designing equitable pension mechanisms that ensure a uniform quality of life, regardless of geographic location, thereby addressing systemic unfairness in social security distribution.
