The article warns that malware such as GravityRAT can access and exfiltrate sensitive data extracted from WhatsApp backups. This reveals the vulnerability of automatic backups when malicious applications are used, exposing critical personal information without the user’s knowledge. This situation is relevant to open data because it illustrates how personal data infrastructure can be compromised through indirect distribution vectors. It underscores the need for transparency in platform security and the importance of protecting the integrity of shared information, even in seemingly reliable services, to maintain trust in the digital ecosystem.
