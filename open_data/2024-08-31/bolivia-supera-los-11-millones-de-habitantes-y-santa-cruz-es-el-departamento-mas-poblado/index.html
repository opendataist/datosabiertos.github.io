Bolivia supera los 11 millones de habitantes y Santa Cruz es el departamento más poblado

The recent presentation of Bolivia’s 2024 Census results by the National Institute of Statistics (INE) marks a significant milestone in the country’s statistical history, validated by international organizations for its technical rigor. The data reveals substantial demographic shifts, particularly the rapid urbanization and metropolization along the country’s central axis. Santa Cruz emerges as the most populous and fastest-growing department, highlighting a concentration of population growth that impacts regional planning and resource distribution across the nation. A critical aspect of these findings is the unprecedented openness of the released data. For the first time, the INE has made the complete census datasets publicly available in user-friendly formats via accessible digital tools, including QR codes and web portals. This commitment to transparency allows citizens and organizations to independently verify and utilize the information, fostering greater accountability and enabling stakeholders to explore granular details regarding population distribution at the departmental and municipal levels. The relevance to open data lies in this transition from opaque reporting to fully accessible, machine-readable public information, which empowers civil society and researchers to analyze demographic trends. Furthermore, these verified statistics are immediately being utilized for concrete governance actions, such as redistributing political representation and financial resources. By linking open data directly to electoral adjustments and fiscal coparticipation, the state demonstrates how transparent statistics can drive equitable policy implementation and improve democratic accountability in upcoming elections.
