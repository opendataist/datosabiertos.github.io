Child abuse images removed from AI image-generator training source, researchers say - Local News 8

LAION, a foundational dataset for major AI image generators, has removed thousands of links to suspected child sexual abuse material. This action addresses critical safety concerns regarding the training data’s potential to generate harmful deepfakes. Such cleanup efforts are vital for ensuring open datasets support ethical AI development and protect vulnerable populations.
