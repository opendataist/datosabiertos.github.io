Comercio internacional de bienes y servicios de China aumenta 12% en julio

China’s international trade surged in July, driven by strong goods exports that generated a substantial surplus. This growth underscores the country’s robust manufacturing competitiveness, while a deficit in the services sector highlights areas with potential for economic diversification. These official statistics are crucial for open data initiatives, as they provide transparent, verifiable metrics of global economic activity. Such high-quality, publicly accessible datasets enable researchers and developers to analyze trade patterns, build economic models, and foster innovation through unrestricted access to real-world financial flows.
