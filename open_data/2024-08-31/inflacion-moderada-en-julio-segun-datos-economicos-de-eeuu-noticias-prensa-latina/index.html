The article shows how inflation data drive stock markets, indicating that prices are falling. This trend influences key monetary decisions, such as interest rate cuts. The relevance for open data lies in the transparency of economic indicators, which allows citizens and analysts to verify the recovery. This empowers accountability in monetary policy and social welfare. Thus, the public availability of official figures facilitates informed debate.
