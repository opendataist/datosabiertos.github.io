Autoridades cruceñas rechazan los resultados del censo 2024

Local authorities in Santa Cruz have strongly rejected the recent census results released by the National Institute of Statistics, arguing that the reported population growth significantly underestimates the true demographic reality. They contend that this substantial omission fails to reflect the actual number of residents who contribute to the local economy and use public services across all municipalities and indigenous autonomies. The discrepancy carries severe economic and political implications, as accurate population data is crucial for the equitable distribution of state resources and political representation. Underestimating the population threatens the fair allocation of funding for public policies and risks compromising the development and welfare of local communities by limiting their political voice and financial support. This situation is highly relevant to open data advocates because it highlights the critical importance of data integrity and transparency in public institutions. When official datasets are perceived as unreliable or manipulated, it undermines trust in public statistics and demonstrates the urgent need for independent verification mechanisms. It reinforces the open data principle that government information must be accessible, verifiable, and accurate to ensure democratic accountability and effective governance.
