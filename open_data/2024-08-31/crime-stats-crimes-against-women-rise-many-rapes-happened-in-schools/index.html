CRIME STATS | Crimes against women rise, many rapes happened in schools

This article highlights a troubling rise in violent crimes against women, particularly rape and murder, signaling a critical failure in public safety mechanisms. The data reveals that while some provinces show statistical fluctuations, the overall trend indicates increased vulnerability, with significant spikes in sexual offenses and lethal violence. This underscores an urgent need for better resource allocation and community policing strategies to protect marginalized groups and restore trust in law enforcement agencies. A major finding is the prevalence of sexual violence within educational facilities, specifically in schools, which betrays the expected safety of learning environments. When institutions meant to foster growth become sites of trauma, it exposes deep systemic gaps in security protocols and oversight. This specific pattern demands targeted interventions within the education sector, ensuring that administrative and physical safeguards are robust enough to prevent exploitation and harassment on campus grounds. For the open_data community, this report illustrates the necessity of accessible, granular crime datasets for effective social analysis. By publishing detailed statistics on geographic hotspots and demographic impacts, governments can empower researchers and advocates to identify trends and hold authorities accountable. Transparent data sharing is essential for driving evidence-based policy changes, enabling civil society to monitor progress and advocate for more equitable safety measures across all regions.
