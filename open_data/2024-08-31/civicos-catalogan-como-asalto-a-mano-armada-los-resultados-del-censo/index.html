Cívicos catalogan como “asalto a mano armada” los resultados del censo

The article highlights a severe dispute over census data, arguing that significant undercounting compromises demographic accuracy. This conflict underscores the critical need for transparent and verified statistical information in governance. For open data, it illustrates how public trust erodes when official figures are perceived as arbitrary or manipulated. Ensuring that methodology is open and reproducible is essential to prevent political narratives from overriding factual reality. Ultimately, the controversy demonstrates that accessible, reliable data is not merely a technical issue but a democratic necessity. Without it, institutions lose credibility, and citizens cannot hold power accountable, highlighting the vital role of open data integrity in society.
