Gobernación y Alcaldía de Santa Cruz rechazan resultados del censo: “no son reales”

Gobernación y Alcaldía de Santa Cruz rechazan resultados del censo: “no son reales”

Santa Cruz authorities have rejected the preliminary census results, arguing that they underestimate the population and distort the region’s reality. They claim that these figures, which are significantly lower than projections, will negatively impact the region’s economic resources and political autonomy. The rejection highlights a critical disconnect between official data and observed urban growth. Local leaders assert that this discrepancy hinders effective public policy planning and resource allocation, affecting essential services such as health and education across municipalities. This dispute is significant for open data advocates, as it demonstrates how flawed or contested datasets can directly obstruct evidence-based governance. It underscores the urgent need for transparent, accurate, and accessible census methodologies to ensure equitable development and proper democratic representation.
