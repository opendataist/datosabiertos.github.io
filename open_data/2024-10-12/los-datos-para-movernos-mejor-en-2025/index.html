Los datos para movernos mejor en 2025

The forthcoming Sustainable Mobility Law establishes a regulatory framework that prioritizes efficiency and emission reduction, promoting active transport and the digitalization of the public sector. A central component is the creation of an open and shared data space, designed to democratize access to mobility information and foster technological innovation. This mechanism enables startups and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to develop new data-driven services, improving urban planning, last-mile logistics, and road safety. By cross-referencing information on the flow of people and goods, routes and schedules are optimized, thereby increasing business productivity while reducing environmental impact and operational costs. The relevance of this article to open data lies in how it transforms mobility data from a restricted asset into a strategic common good. By requiring prior anonymization for privacy protection, the law facilitates a collaborative ecosystem in which data transparency drives the circular economy and sustainability, demonstrating that opening up public information is key to addressing complex transportation challenges without compromising individual privacy.
