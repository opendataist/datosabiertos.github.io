La inflación de septiembre fue del 3,5% y alcanza el 209% interanual - EL DEBATE

The article reports a significant surge in Argentina's consumer price index, highlighting substantial inflation across key sectors such as housing and utilities. This economic pressure has drastically raised the income threshold required for families to avoid poverty and extreme poverty, reflecting a severe decline in purchasing power and living standards for citizens nationwide. The relevance to open data lies in the systematic collection and publication of these consumer price indices by national statistical institutes. By making detailed data on price evolution available for various goods, services, and geographic regions, governments enable transparency in economic monitoring and allow researchers to accurately track changes in the cost of living. This accessible dataset is crucial for verifying official inflation claims, analyzing regional disparities, and understanding the real impact of economic policies on households. Open access to such granular, regularly updated statistical information empowers journalists, academics, and the public to critically assess economic health and advocate for informed policy decisions.
