PwC India collaborates with Meta to expand open-source AI solutions - ET Telecom

PwC India and Meta are partnering to scale open-source AI solutions, leveraging Meta’s Llama models to drive innovation across enterprise and citizen services. This strategic alliance aims to democratize access to generative AI technology, making advanced digital capabilities more accessible and affordable for businesses and communities alike. By combining Meta’s technical expertise with PwC’s domain knowledge, the initiative seeks to build a robust ecosystem that solves real-world challenges and fosters meaningful change at scale. The collaboration aligns with the Indian government’s IndiaAI Mission, signaling a timely push to strengthen the national AI ecosystem. It emphasizes using natural language processing to enhance efficiency and data-driven decision-making. This approach is positioned as critical for accelerating India’s digital transformation, empowering local industries to compete globally while supporting the nation’s ambition to become a leading digital economy through widespread technological adoption. This partnership is highly relevant to open data as it promotes the use of open-source platforms to enhance transparency and accessibility in AI development. By utilizing open models like Llama, the project encourages broader community involvement and reduces reliance on proprietary systems. This supports the open data principle of enabling diverse stakeholders to innovate and contribute to public good, ensuring that technological advancements remain inclusive and beneficial for both commercial entities and societal welfare.
