Producción industrial bajó 0.9 % en agosto, su primera caída desde 2021

Mexican industrial production recorded its first annual decline since early 2021, driven by significant contractions in mining and construction. This recent downturn contrasts with the modest growth observed in the first eight months of the year, highlighting a sectoral divergence in which energy and manufacturing remained stable or slightly positive, while traditional industries lagged. The broader economy continues to demonstrate resilience, with GDP growth maintaining a positive trajectory despite these industrial fluctuations. The government maintains optimistic forecasts for the year, suggesting that current temporary dips in specific industrial segments do not fundamentally undermine the nation’s overall economic expansion plans. This data is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent, timely statistical releases from institutions such as INEGI. Access to detailed, disaggregated industrial metrics enables analysts and the public to accurately interpret complex economic shifts, ensuring that decision-making is based on clear, accessible evidence rather than aggregated generalizations.
