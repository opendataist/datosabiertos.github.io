Bentley Unveils Vision for Open Data Ecosystems - Engineering.com

Bentley Unveils Vision for Open Data Ecosystems - Engineering.com

Bentley Systems argues that the future of infrastructure engineering relies on abandoning proprietary silos in favor of open data ecosystems. This shift is essential because complex projects require seamless interoperability across diverse organizations and platforms. By establishing open-source schemas and secure data flows, the industry can effectively address global challenges such as energy grid expansion and sustainability goals, ensuring that critical information is accessible rather than locked within single-vendor systems. The integration of geospatial data and artificial intelligence represents a significant paradigm shift in how infrastructure is designed and managed. Through partnerships that combine advanced 3D visualization with AI capabilities, engineers can now automate routine tasks and gain deeper insights into asset performance. This technology enables the creation of digital twins that provide real-world context, allowing stakeholders to visualize existing and planned assets comprehensively, thereby enhancing decision-making and operational efficiency. This approach is particularly relevant to open data because it demonstrates how shared, interoperable information drives resilience and sustainability in the built environment. By leveraging open standards, the infrastructure sector can unlock the value of existing assets, using AI to predict maintenance needs and optimize lifecycle management. Ultimately, open data ecosystems empower the industry to collaborate more effectively, ensuring that infrastructure systems remain reliable and adaptable for future generations.
