Fuerza Aérea de EEUU mantiene despliegue de avión espía cerca de costas venezolanas

Fuerza Aérea de EEUU mantiene despliegue de avión espía cerca de costas venezolanas

The United States Air Force has intensified reconnaissance operations near Venezuela, deploying RC-135 aircraft for extended electronic surveillance in international airspace. This sustained presence echoes previous spikes in activity, reflecting a continuous strategy to monitor regional military communications and radar emissions. This scenario underscores the critical role of open data in promoting defense transparency. Access to flight-tracking information enables independent observers to verify state activities without relying on official disclosures, thereby ensuring accountability. The relevance to open data lies in the way public tracking sources allow civil society to analyze geopolitical tensions. By democratizing access to such operational details, open data empowers analysts to independently assess security situations, bridging the gap between classified military actions and public understanding.
