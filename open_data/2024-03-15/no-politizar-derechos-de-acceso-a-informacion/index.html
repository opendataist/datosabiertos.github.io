No politizar derechos de acceso a información

Attacks on the INAI threaten fundamental human rights to information and privacy, which must remain independent of political interference. Defending these rights is urgent, especially given the institution’s incomplete leadership and reliance on court intervention. This struggle highlights the critical need for open data transparency and robust personal data protection. It underscores that access to information is a societal benefit that requires constant vigilance against attempts to dismantle these essential democratic safeguards. The article is relevant to open data because it emphasizes the legal and human rights framework underpinning transparency. It warns that weakening access to information undermines the very foundation of accountable, open government and citizen trust in public institutions.
