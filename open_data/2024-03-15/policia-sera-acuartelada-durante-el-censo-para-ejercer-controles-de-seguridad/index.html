Policía será acuartelada durante el Censo para ejercer controles de seguridad

Bolivia is implementing strict security protocols to ensure the accuracy of its upcoming national census. Police forces will be deployed and counted in advance to maintain order, while strict confinement orders prohibit citizens from leaving their homes on census day. These measures include halting alcohol sales and limiting interdepartmental travel immediately prior to the count. The government aims to minimize external interference, ensuring that every resident remains at their domicile so that technical staff from the National Institute of Statistics can conduct the survey without obstruction or mobility issues. This initiative highlights the intersection of state security and demographic data collection in open data contexts. Ensuring a complete and unbiased census requires robust legal and logistical frameworks. For open data advocates, this underscores how administrative coordination and enforcement directly impact the reliability and completeness of official statistical records used for public policy.
