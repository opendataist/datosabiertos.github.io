Number of people employed in green jobs jumps by ‘almost a fifth’ in two years

UK green employment surged significantly between 2020 and 2022, driven primarily by growth in energy efficiency and waste management. This rise highlights a substantial shift toward labor-intensive environmental protection activities, suggesting that the transition to a sustainable economy is creating tangible workforce opportunities rather than just technological advancements. Although the data includes nuclear power and excludes certain decarbonization sectors, the overall trend underscores the expanding scope of the green labor market. The inclusion of diverse fields like renewable energy and low-carbon transport indicates a broad-based economic adaptation, even if some specific high-impact roles remain uncounted due to current data limitations. This report is vital for open data initiatives because it relies on experimental ONS figures that require transparency and iterative improvement. It demonstrates how open statistical methods must evolve to capture emerging economic shifts, ensuring that future metrics accurately reflect the true scale and composition of the green job market as definitions and data sources mature.
