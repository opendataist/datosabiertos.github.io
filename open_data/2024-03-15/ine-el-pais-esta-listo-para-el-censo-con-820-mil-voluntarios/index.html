INE: “El país está listo” para el censo con 820 mil voluntarios

INE: “El país está listo” para el censo con 820 mil voluntarios

Bolivia is preparing for its most modern census by deploying massive volunteer teams, significantly exceeding staffing targets to ensure comprehensive national coverage. This unprecedented mobilization highlights the critical role of human capital and organized logistics in executing large-scale data collection efforts efficiently and transparently. The success of this initiative underscores how effective workforce management directly impacts data quality and representativeness in demographic studies. By leveraging a vast network of trained volunteers, the statistical institute aims to minimize gaps and ensure that the resulting population data accurately reflects the country’s social realities. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the foundational necessity of robust, inclusive collection mechanisms before data can be shared. High-quality open datasets rely on such meticulous preparation to remain reliable, accessible, and useful for public policy, research, and civic engagement in the long term.
