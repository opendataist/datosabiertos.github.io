Bajó uso del Bowí en 2024; 670 mil pasajeros en 3 meses: INEGI

The INEGI’s January transport data reveals a significant decline in passenger numbers, particularly for the Bici system and in Chihuahua. This trend underscores shifting mobility patterns and operational challenges within urban transit networks across Mexico. For open data enthusiasts, this release exemplifies the critical role of standardized public statistics in monitoring service quality. It highlights how accessible governmental metrics enable citizens and analysts to track systemic trends, assess efficiency, and hold transportation authorities accountable for declining ridership and changes in performance.
