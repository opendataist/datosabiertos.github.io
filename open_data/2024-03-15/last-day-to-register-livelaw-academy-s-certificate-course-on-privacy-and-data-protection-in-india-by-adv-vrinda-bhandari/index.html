LAST DAY To Register- LiveLaw Academy's Certificate Course On Privacy And Data Protection In India By Adv. Vrinda Bhandari

This course addresses the critical gap in India’s legal framework regarding digital rights and surveillance. It provides a foundational understanding of privacy amidst the rise of big data, where technology increasingly mediates interactions between citizens, the state, and corporations. By exploring the nuances of data protection, it highlights the urgent need for robust legal safeguards in an era where monitoring capabilities have expanded significantly. The curriculum focuses on the evolution of privacy principles and the specific provisions of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act. It examines the complex relationship between privacy and emerging technologies like AI and intermediary liability. This approach ensures participants understand not just the law, but how it interacts with real-world digital ecosystems, offering a comprehensive view of current debates and historical context. This content is vital for the open data community because it contextualizes data usage within legal and ethical boundaries. Understanding these frameworks helps data practitioners navigate compliance while advocating for transparency and accountability. It empowers stakeholders to engage meaningfully with privacy discussions, ensuring that open data initiatives respect individual rights and contribute to a more responsible digital society.
