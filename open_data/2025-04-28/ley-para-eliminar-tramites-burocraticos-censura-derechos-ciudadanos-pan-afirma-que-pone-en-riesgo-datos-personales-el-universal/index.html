Ley para eliminar trámites burocráticos “censura derechos ciudadanos"; PAN afirma que pone en riesgo datos personales | El Universal

Ley para eliminar trámites burocráticos “censura derechos ciudadanos"; PAN afirma que pone en riesgo datos personales | El Universal

The article warns that the Legislative Initiative to Eliminate Bureaucratic Procedures represents a significant setback for transparency and open governance. By concentrating decision-making power in the Executive Branch and eliminating key mechanisms such as Public Consultation and the National Observatory for Regulatory Improvement, the capacity of civil society to participate in the development of regulations is drastically reduced. This centralization hinders accountability and weakens the quality of public policies by depriving them of independent scrutiny and multilateral dialogue. Furthermore, a serious risk to citizens’ privacy is highlighted by the imposition of the CURP (Unique Population Registry Code) with biometric data as the sole official identification. This measure centralizes sensitive information without adequate security safeguards, exposing the population to potential data breaches in a context of growing cybercrime. The imposition of digital standards without specific federal funding also threatens local autonomy, forcing municipalities with limited resources to adopt costly platforms that could become inoperable. This report is relevant to open data because it questions the normative infrastructure that underpins transparency and participation. By eliminating regulatory analysis tools and impartial observatories, access to reliable public information and monitoring of state efficiency are made more difficult. The proposal undermines the principles of open data by prioritizing operational centralization over openness, security, and digital inclusion—elements fundamental to a modern and accountable public administration.
