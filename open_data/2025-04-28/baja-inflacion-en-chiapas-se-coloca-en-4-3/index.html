Baja inflación en Chiapas, se coloca en 4.3 %

Baja inflación en Chiapas, se coloca en 4.3 %

Chiapas reversed its upward inflation trend, aligning with a national decline to a three-year low. This shift highlights improved price stability for households, marking a significant economic improvement after months of rising costs. Despite this positive state-wide trend, specific locations like Tapachula experienced much higher inflation rates. These disparities emphasize that macroeconomic improvements do not always translate uniformly across all regions, affecting local purchasing power differently. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the critical need for granular, transparent statistical access. Public scrutiny of localized inflation metrics ensures accountability and helps communities understand the real-world impact of broader economic policies on their daily lives.
