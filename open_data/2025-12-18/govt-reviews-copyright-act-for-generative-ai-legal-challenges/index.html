Govt Reviews Copyright Act for Generative AI Legal Challenges

Govt Reviews Copyright Act for Generative AI Legal Challenges

The government is actively reviewing India’s Copyright Act of 1957 to address legal challenges posed by generative artificial intelligence. An expert committee has been established to analyze how existing laws handle emerging AI technologies, specifically focusing on whether current frameworks are adequate for this new era of digital creation and interaction. A key priority is determining how copyrighted materials are utilized during AI training processes, with initial findings already released for public feedback. Simultaneously, the committee is investigating complex questions regarding the authorship and copyright status of content generated solely by AI systems, ensuring that intellectual property rights evolve alongside technological advancements. This initiative is crucial for open data because it establishes the legal boundaries for using and sharing data in AI development. By balancing innovation with copyright protection, the resulting policy framework will define how publicly available information can be ethically and legally integrated into AI models, directly impacting data accessibility, reuse, and the future of open knowledge ecosystems.
