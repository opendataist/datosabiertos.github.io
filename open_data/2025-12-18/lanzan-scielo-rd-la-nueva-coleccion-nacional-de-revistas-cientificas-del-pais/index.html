Lanzan “SciELO RD”, la nueva colección nacional de revistas científicas del país

The launch of SciELO República Dominicana marks a pivotal step for open data by establishing the nation’s first national collection of open-access scientific journals integrated into the global SciELO network. This initiative moves academic research from isolated repositories into a major open data portal, making valuable information from Dominican scholars immediately available. By placing this data in the public domain, the platform transforms academic output into usable assets for states and enterprises, facilitating better-informed decision-making through transparent access to research findings. This platform represents more than a technical upgrade; it is a structural policy aimed at strengthening sustainable research and fostering a genuine culture of inquiry. Experts argue that for this to succeed, it must be treated as a project of state with robust governance and continuous funding. Integrating these indexed publications into academic evaluation metrics is crucial, as it incentivizes quality and ensures that scientific merit is recognized. This shift promotes editorial quality and international visibility, aligning the country with regional standards for open science. Ultimately, this development enhances the global circulation of academic knowledge, allowing Dominican research to contribute to worldwide scientific discourse. It underscores the importance of open data in breaking down barriers to information, ensuring that critical insights are not confined to paywalls or limited circles. By embracing open access, the country supports a more equitable and transparent scientific ecosystem, benefiting both local development and international collaboration through shared, accessible data.
