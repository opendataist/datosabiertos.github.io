Open Data
ES EN

Argentina’s National Electoral Directorate has launched the Electoral Open Data Catalog, a significant step forward in transparency and public information management. Coinciding with the 40th anniversary of democracy, the catalog centralizes electoral results from 2011 through the most recent PASO (Primary, Open, Simultaneous, and Obligatory) elections, ensuring that citizens and the academic community can access data that was previously available but not standardized in a uniform format. The catalog is structured around three fundamental pillars: disaggregated data by voting booth, technical preservation standards with naming conventions, and documentation for programmatic access via API. This systematic approach enables political scientists and developers to use the information efficiently, facilitating the creation of applications and historical comparative analyses that were previously complex due to the dispersion and lack of homogeneity in the sources. This initiative is significant for the open data movement because it establishes a precedent for interoperability and quality in public administration. By standardizing formats and collaborating with political parties to ensure accuracy, the initiative not only improves immediate access but also optimizes future result tabulations. This demonstrates how technology, when aligned with transparency principles, can strengthen citizen trust and democratic analysis.
