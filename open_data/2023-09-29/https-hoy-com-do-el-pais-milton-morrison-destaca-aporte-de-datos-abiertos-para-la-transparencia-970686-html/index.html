Open Data
ES EN

Edesur Dominicana sets a precedent as the first state-owned entity to formalize open data commitments with the Organization of American States (OAS), transforming its internal culture from opacity to radical transparency. By investing in comprehensive staff training across sensitive sectors, the company significantly enhances public access to critical information. This strategic shift ensures that citizens and clients can freely access data, fulfilling legal requirements and fostering institutional trust. The initiative demonstrates how private management within state-owned entities can drive governance improvements through voluntary adherence to international transparency standards. The article highlights open data as a vital mechanism for accountability. It illustrates that structured data-sharing policies are essential for modernizing public administration, proving that technical cooperation with organizations like the OAS directly strengthens democratic oversight and civic engagement.
