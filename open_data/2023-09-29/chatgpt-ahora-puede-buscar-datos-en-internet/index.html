ChatGPT ahora puede buscar datos en internet

ChatGPT’s ability to search the internet breaks through the limitation of its static training data, enabling more up-to-date and accurate responses. This evolution addresses the growing demand for real-time information, in contrast to the previous issue of outdated knowledge. However, this direct access introduces greater risks, such as the spread of errors or unverified content. Although sources are cited, reliance on multiple websites makes quality control more challenging compared to traditional models based on fixed datasets. This news is relevant to open data because it highlights the tension between open access to web information and the need for reliability. It illustrates how integrating AI into public data search requires new validation and transparency strategies to ensure the integrity of available information.
