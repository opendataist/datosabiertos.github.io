Francia registró en 2022 su menor número de nacimientos desde 1945

France recorded its lowest birth rate since 1945, reflecting a persistent decline across all regions. This demographic shift points to long-term structural changes in fertility patterns rather than temporary fluctuations. The trend continued into 2023, with significant drops in births among women of prime reproductive age, while only older mothers saw an increase. This indicates a fundamental change in family planning and societal dynamics affecting population growth. This data is crucial for open data advocates, as it underscores the need for transparent, accessible demographic datasets. Understanding such trends enables more effective public policy analysis and promotes informed civic engagement on issues of social sustainability.
