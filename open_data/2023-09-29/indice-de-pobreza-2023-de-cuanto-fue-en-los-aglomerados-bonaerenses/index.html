Índice de Pobreza 2023: de cuánto fue en los aglomerados bonaerenses

Poverty rose significantly in early 2023, affecting over 18 million people, while extreme poverty reached nearly four million. This deterioration occurred despite nominal wage increases, as inflation outpaced earnings and economic activity contracted. The gap between rising costs and stagnant purchasing power directly worsened living conditions for a large portion of the population. These statistics highlight the severe impact of macroeconomic volatility on social welfare, illustrating how high inflation erodes household stability. The data reveal that a substantial share of families cannot meet basic nutritional needs, signaling deepening social vulnerability. This trend underscores the urgent need for policy interventions to protect the most disadvantaged citizens. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible statistics empower researchers and civil society to monitor inequality. Publicly available datasets enable independent analysis of economic health, fostering accountability and informed debate on social policies. Such openness allows diverse actors to utilize factual evidence for advocacy and academic study.
