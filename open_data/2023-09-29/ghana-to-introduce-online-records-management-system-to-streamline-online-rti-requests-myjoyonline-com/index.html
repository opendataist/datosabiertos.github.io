Ghana to introduce Online Records Management System to streamline online RTI requests - MyJoyOnline.com

Ghana’s Ministry of Information is launching the Online Records Management System to streamline right-to-information requests and enhance transparency. This digital initiative underscores the critical role of online spaces in facilitating universal access to public records. The system aims to empower citizens and strengthen accountability by reducing opportunities for corruption within government institutions. It represents a significant step toward a more open and responsive society where information is readily accessible. This development is highly relevant to open data as it institutionalizes digital mechanisms for public record retrieval. By standardizing online access, the country moves closer to operationalizing transparency principles essential for an informed citizenry.
