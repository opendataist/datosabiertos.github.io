Tiene Coahuila de las ciudades más seguras; Saltillo, PN y Torreón, ejemplo nacional | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Coahuila demonstrates exceptional public safety metrics, ranking as the state with the highest citizen perception of security in Mexico. This achievement highlights the effectiveness of its collaborative security model, which integrates the efforts of federal forces, state police, and municipal authorities. By prioritizing prevention, intelligence, and close community ties, the region has successfully reduced insecurity and strengthened trust among residents. The success is largely attributed to strategic resource allocation and advanced technological infrastructure, including increased funding for tactical equipment and surveillance systems. This investment, combined with coordinated governance across all three branches of government, has created a robust framework for maintaining peace. The emphasis on data-driven strategies and inter-agency cooperation serves as a replicable blueprint for enhancing urban safety standards nationwide. This case is highly relevant to open data, as it illustrates how transparent statistical reporting from national institutes can validate policy outcomes. By publicly sharing survey results and performance metrics, authorities foster accountability and allow citizens to verify the efficacy of security measures. Such openness encourages continuous improvement and demonstrates that accessible, high-quality data is essential for building informed public discourse and maintaining social stability.
