Fiskil designó a Karol Benavides como directora Regional de Alianzas y Estrategia - ebizlatam.com

Fiskil designó a Karol Benavides como directora Regional de Alianzas y Estrategia - ebizlatam.com

Fiskil appointed Karol Benavides to lead its Latin American alliance strategy, aiming to strengthen operations in key markets such as Colombia, Chile, and Peru. This move underscores the company’s commitment to expanding secure, scalable, and regulated financial data exchange across the region. Benavides will focus on fostering institutional collaboration and building trust among financial entities to drive the adoption of open finance. The leadership emphasizes that technology alone is insufficient; success requires a shared vision and partnerships that transform data into tools for inclusion, transparency, and innovation. This appointment highlights the critical role of open data in modern financial ecosystems. By connecting global infrastructure with local needs, Fiskil demonstrates how open standards enable competitive, secure data economies. This case illustrates the broader industry shift toward leveraging open data to enhance financial accessibility and regulatory compliance in emerging markets.
