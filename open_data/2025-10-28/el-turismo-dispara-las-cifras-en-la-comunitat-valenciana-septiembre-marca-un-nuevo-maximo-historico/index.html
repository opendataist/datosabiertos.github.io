El turismo dispara las cifras en la Comunitat Valenciana: septiembre marca un nuevo máximo histórico

The article reports a historic surge in overnight stays and visitors in the Valencian Community’s hotels for September, driven by strong growth in both domestic and international tourism. Occupancy rates remained among the highest in Spain, with a notable increase in foreign travelers, confirming the region’s status as a leading and attractive tourist destination. This data highlights the robust performance of the local tourism sector, setting new records that underscore sustained demand and economic resilience. The significant rise in international visits specifically illustrates successful market positioning and the growing appeal of the region to global travelers, reinforcing its competitive edge. For open data, this example demonstrates how national statistical institutes like the INE release granular, time-series data that enables transparency in economic tracking. Such accessible datasets allow researchers and policymakers to analyze tourism trends, verify government claims, and understand regional economic shifts through empirical evidence rather than rhetoric alone.
