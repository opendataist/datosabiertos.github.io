Right to Information Must Be a Living Reality for Citizens — AG

Right to Information Must Be a Living Reality for Citizens — AG

Dr Dominic Ayine emphasizes that Ghana’s Right to Information must transcend legal statutes to become a tangible, lived reality for every citizen. This shift is essential for fostering true transparency, accountability, and good governance. By ensuring barrier-free access, the state can effectively combat issues like environmental degradation and illegal mining, empowering citizens to hold authorities responsible for their actions. The Attorney-General challenges the RTIC to transform its strategic plans from static documents into dynamic operational blueprints. This approach ensures that initiatives lead to measurable outcomes rather than remaining theoretical exercises. The focus must be on innovative resilience and active public engagement, ensuring that the commission’s efforts directly contribute to an open and transparent society that serves the public interest effectively. For open data, this highlights the critical need for practical usability over mere existence. The commission’s progress in training officers and increasing public requests demonstrates growing trust in digital transparency. However, overcoming logistical hurdles to deepen digital access remains vital. Sustaining this momentum ensures that data serves as a tool for citizen empowerment, reinforcing democratic values through accessible and actionable information systems.
