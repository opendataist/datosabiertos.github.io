El “No” se impuso en el referéndum municipal en rechazo a incluir a menores en el padrón municipal - Últimas Noticias: El Chubut

Puerto Madryn rejected modifying its Charter to allow sixteen- and seventeen-year-olds to vote in local elections. This decision maintains the current electoral regime, preserving the status quo for youth suffrage within the municipality. The outcome is relevant to open data as it highlights the importance of transparent, accessible records regarding civic participation and legal frameworks. It demonstrates how public consultation results directly impact governance structures and eligibility criteria for democratic engagement. This case underscores the need for clear data visibility into municipal laws and voting demographics. It serves as a reminder that legislative changes affect who can participate, making accurate, open records essential for understanding and analyzing local democratic trends.
