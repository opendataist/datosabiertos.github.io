La desigualdad salarial en Paraguay: entre la educación y el tipo de empleo - Economía - ABC Color

La desigualdad salarial en Paraguay: entre la educación y el tipo de empleo - Economía - ABC Color

The article highlights that labor inequality in Paraguay persists, primarily linked to educational attainment and the productive sector. Workers with higher levels of schooling earn significantly higher incomes, while the primary sector exhibits dynamism driven by conjunctural factors rather than formal qualifications alone. This distinction reveals that temporary economic demand can raise wages in areas such as agriculture, despite the low educational levels of its workers. The wage gap is also exacerbated within the tertiary sector, the country’s largest employer. This sector concentrates high-paying professional service occupations alongside low-paid informal jobs, thereby widening internal disparities. This segmentation demonstrates that, although traditional education yields better returns, the productive structure and labor market conditions decisively influence individuals’ final income levels. This information is relevant to the field of open data, as it underscores the need to access and jointly analyze educational, sectoral, and income variables. To design effective public policies, it is crucial to use disaggregated data that enable a comprehensive understanding of these complex interrelationships. Transparency and access to detailed statistics facilitate the identification of the structural causes of inequality, allowing for more precise, evidence-based interventions.
