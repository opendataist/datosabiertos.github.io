Balochistan Information Commission Begins Work to Ensure Citizens' Right to Information – Pakistan Press Foundation (PPF)

The establishment of the Balochistan Information Commission marks a pivotal shift toward government transparency and public accountability. By enabling citizens to formally access information, this initiative strengthens governance and fosters greater public trust in state institutions. This body serves as a critical mechanism for empowering individuals to exercise their constitutional right to know. It mandates timely responses from government departments, thereby curbing corruption and promoting inclusive development through open decision-making processes. Relevant to open data, this development institutionalizes the flow of public information into the hands of citizens. It establishes a legal framework that prioritizes data accessibility, ensuring that government actions are visible and that information becomes a tool for civic engagement and improved governance.
