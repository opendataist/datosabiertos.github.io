Fin de semana marcado por posibles lluvias intensas: todo Mendoza está bajo alerta naranja

The National Meteorological Service issued an orange alert for the entire province of Mendoza due to the Santa Rosa storm system. This weather event brings persistent, intense rainfall and potential snowfall in elevated areas, significantly impacting public safety and infrastructure across the region. Authorities strongly recommend seeking shelter, cutting off electricity if flooding risks arise, and avoiding areas near water bodies. Residents are urged to prepare emergency kits and maintain contact with local emergency services, highlighting the critical need for accurate, real-time weather dissemination to ensure community resilience against severe meteorological phenomena. This situation is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the vital role of publicly accessible meteorological forecasts in guiding civic action. Transparent, timely data sharing enables effective disaster preparedness, allowing citizens to interpret risks and make informed decisions to protect lives and property during extreme weather events.
