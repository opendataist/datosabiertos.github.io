More ex-Presidents to face cases : Maithri, Gota next?

A cameraman arrested for allegedly assaulting a security officer while filming former President Ranil Wickremesinghe has been released on bail. The incident, involving a camera strike near a court, sparked immediate legal action and medical attention for the injured officer. This case highlights the critical intersection of journalistic activities and personal safety during political events. It underscores the legal risks media professionals face when covering high-profile figures, potentially affecting how news is gathered and reported in volatile environments. The relevance to open data lies in the tension between transparency and individual rights. As organizations seek to publish raw footage for accountability, this incident illustrates the complex ethical and legal boundaries surrounding the release of visual evidence involving public figures and their security personnel.
