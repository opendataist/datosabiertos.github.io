The UAEMéx demonstrates a strong commitment to transparency by collaborating with its Internal Control Body to ensure accountability and open access to public information. This partnership highlights the institution's dedication to serving as a model for ethical governance and institutional integrity. Key improvements include active oversight of resource management, the mandatory disclosure of assets by officials, and robust handling of audits and inquiries. These measures strengthen public trust by providing verifiable evidence of responsible administration and adherence to legal standards. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates how structured institutional reporting and the proactive publication of administrative details foster a culture of openness. It shows that accessible data on governance and finance is essential for validating public institutions' credibility and encouraging civic engagement through verified information.
