Nigeria: Telecoms' Subscriber Base Maintains Steady Growth Rate, Hits 173m With Teledensity of 80.05 Percent

Nigeria: Telecoms' Subscriber Base Maintains Steady Growth Rate, Hits 173m With Teledensity of 80.05 Percent

Nigerian telecommunications experienced a significant recovery in late 2025, with subscriber numbers rebounding strongly in August and September following a dip earlier in the year. This resurgence drove the national teledensity above eighty percent, indicating that mobile connectivity is reaching a substantial portion of the population. The trend suggests that despite temporary fluctuations, the sector is on a robust upward trajectory, reflecting renewed consumer adoption and network stability. The market remains heavily consolidated, with a clear hierarchy among the major operators. One provider dominates more than half the market, followed by another holding nearly a third, while the remaining players share the rest. This distribution highlights the intense competition and the scale advantages enjoyed by incumbent networks. The data underscores how a few key entities drive the majority of connectivity, shaping the landscape for digital access and service delivery across the country. This information is critical to the open data community as it provides verified, longitudinal metrics on digital infrastructure growth. Transparent reporting from the regulator allows researchers and developers to analyze connectivity trends, identify disparities, and build services that leverage widespread mobile access. By making these statistics publicly available, the data supports evidence-based policymaking and encourages innovation in sectors reliant on robust telecommunication networks.
