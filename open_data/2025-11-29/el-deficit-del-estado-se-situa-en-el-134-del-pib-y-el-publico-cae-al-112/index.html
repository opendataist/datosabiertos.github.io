El déficit del Estado se sitúa en el 1,34% del PIB y el público cae al 1,12%

The public sector deficit has notably decreased compared to the previous year, demonstrating improved fiscal health. This reduction remains significant even when excluding extraordinary costs arising from natural disasters. Excluding disaster relief and interest payments reveals a primary surplus. This indicates underlying economic stability and suggests effective management of recurrent expenditures by state administrations. This data matters for open data because it highlights how transparency requires context. Users must understand how one-off events and specific accounting adjustments impact public finances. Accurate datasets enable stakeholders to distinguish between structural improvements and temporary fiscal anomalies, ensuring reliable economic analysis.
