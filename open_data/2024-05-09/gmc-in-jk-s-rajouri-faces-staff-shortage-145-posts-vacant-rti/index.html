GMC in JK's Rajouri Faces Staff Shortage,145 Posts Vacant: RTI

Government Medical College Rajouri suffers from critical faculty and infrastructure deficits, compounded by massive budget underutilization. This inefficiency highlights how opaque financial data obscures systemic operational failures in public institutions. Accessing such details through RTI reveals the stark gap between allocated resources and actual delivery. It demonstrates the necessity of public records in exposing inefficiencies that directly impact healthcare quality and institutional accountability. This case underscores the vital role of open data in civic oversight. By making budgetary and staffing information transparent, citizens can demand better governance, ensuring public funds are effectively utilized to meet community needs.
