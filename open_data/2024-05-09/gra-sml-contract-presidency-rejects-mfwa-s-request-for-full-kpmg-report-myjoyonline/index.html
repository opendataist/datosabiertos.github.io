GRA-SML contract: Presidency rejects MFWA's request for full KPMG report - MyJoyOnline

The Ghanaian Presidency has refused a request by the Media Foundation for West Africa to disclose the complete KPMG audit report regarding a government revenue contract. By classifying the document as exempt under sections of the Right to Information Act, officials argue that releasing such detailed opinions and deliberations would compromise the integrity of high-level decision-making processes. This refusal highlights a persistent tension between state secrecy and public oversight. This case underscores the critical challenges surrounding open data initiatives in developing democracies. When governments invoke broad exemptions for sensitive information, they effectively restrict public access to evidence regarding public spending and contractual obligations. The refusal limits civil society’s ability to scrutinize whether taxpayer funds are being managed efficiently, thereby weakening mechanisms designed to ensure accountability in fiscal matters. The incident is highly relevant to the open data community as it demonstrates how legal frameworks can be manipulated to withhold crucial public interest information. It suggests that without robust safeguards against arbitrary classification of documents, transparency remains theoretical rather than practical. Ultimately, this refusal reinforces the need for stronger legislative protections that prioritize public access to government records, ensuring that the principles of openness are not easily undermined by claims of confidentiality.
