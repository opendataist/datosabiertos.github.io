La producción industrial se contrae un 13,6% en marzo en Castilla-La Mancha, 2,3 puntos por encima de la media nacional

The Industrial Production Index experienced its steepest decline since the 2020 pandemic, falling across all of Spain’s autonomous communities. This broad-based contraction signals a severe nationwide slowdown in industrial activity, with notable regional variations underscoring uneven economic resilience among different territories. Year-to-date regional data show that Castilla-La Mancha performed only marginally better than the national average, while specific sectors such as durable goods and equipment saw drastic reductions. These figures point to a widespread collapse in manufacturing demand, affecting intermediate and energy goods to a similar extent, which highlights the depth of the current industrial recession. This report is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the critical importance of accessible, granular statistical datasets for public policy analysis. Transparent access to such indicators enables researchers and citizens to monitor economic health, ensuring that government responses to industrial downturns are based on verified, widely available evidence rather than opaque or delayed information.
