Becas Benito Juárez: SEP inicia campaña para prevenir riesgos entre los beneficiarios | El Universal

The SEP warns beneficiaries against the misuse of their data and the electoral exploitation of scholarship programs, urging vigilance to protect personal information. By highlighting these risks, the SEP aims to strengthen public awareness regarding the security of their records. Individuals are advised to report suspicious activities to electoral authorities, ensuring transparency in how resources are distributed and preventing coercion. This call to action emphasizes collective responsibility in safeguarding educational support from corrupt practices. This initiative is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of protecting citizen privacy within publicly accessible welfare systems. It illustrates the balance between data availability for transparency and the critical need for security protocols to prevent identity theft and political manipulation.
