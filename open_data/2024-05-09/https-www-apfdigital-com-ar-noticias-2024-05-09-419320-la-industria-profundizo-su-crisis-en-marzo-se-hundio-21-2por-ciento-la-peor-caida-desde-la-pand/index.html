Argentina’s housing sector is experiencing a historic contraction amid a deep recession, soaring inflation, and severe fiscal austerity. This sharp decline in transactions reflects the crushing economic pressure on households, marking the worst monthly performance since the onset of the pandemic. The data highlight how government austerity measures exacerbate poverty and reduce access to housing, directly impacting social welfare indicators. These trends underscore the vulnerability of essential services and property markets during periods of intense economic adjustment. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, timely economic statistics are crucial for assessing the real-world impact of public policy on citizens’ living standards and market stability.
