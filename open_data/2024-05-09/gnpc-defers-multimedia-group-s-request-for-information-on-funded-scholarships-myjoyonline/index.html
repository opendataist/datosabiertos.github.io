GNPC defers Multimedia Group's request for information on funded scholarships - MyJoyOnline

The Ghana National Petroleum Corporation has declined a detailed data request from The Multimedia Group regarding scholarship beneficiaries and expenditures. Instead of releasing the information immediately, the corporation deferred access, citing legal provisions that allow withholding data scheduled for imminent publication. This decision highlights the tension between immediate transparency demands and institutional readiness to disclose complex datasets. The corporation argues that the requested details are being compiled for official publication on its foundation website within ninety days. By citing specific sections of the Right to Information Act, GNPC justifies the delay as a procedural measure rather than a refusal of transparency. This approach suggests that proactive publication can serve as an alternative mechanism for fulfilling public information obligations, provided it is timely and comprehensive. This situation is relevant to open data because it illustrates how statutory frameworks can be utilized to manage information release schedules. It raises critical questions about whether deferred publication truly serves the public interest or merely delays accountability. For open data advocates, this case underscores the need for clear standards on what constitutes adequate proactive disclosure and the importance of verifying that such delayed releases are indeed completed within promised timeframes.
