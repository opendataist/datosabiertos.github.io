La inflación se moderó al 2,3% en marzo por la electricidad y los carburantes

La inflación se moderó al 2,3% en marzo por la electricidad y los carburantes

The article highlights a significant deceleration in inflation, driven primarily by moderated prices for electricity and fuels, alongside a decrease in the cost of travel packages. This trend contributes to broader economic stability, suggesting that the surge in energy expenses is losing momentum, which helps alleviate pressure on household budgets despite persistent increases in food and beverage prices. Concurrently, underlying inflation has reached its lowest level in several years, indicating that core price pressures are easing. Although certain items, such as chocolate and jewelry, experienced sharp annual increases, the overall contraction in energy-related costs and specific agricultural products demonstrates that the economic environment is becoming more predictable, allowing for better long-term financial planning for both consumers and businesses.
