El IPC baja al 2% en Castilla-La Mancha en marzo

El IPC baja al 2% en Castilla-La Mancha en marzo

Castilla-La Mancha recorded its lowest inflation rate since October 2024, with prices rising only slightly on a month-on-month basis. This deceleration reflects broader national trends, where overall price increases slowed significantly, marking a positive shift for consumer purchasing power across the region. The divergence in category performance highlights structural pressures, particularly in housing and energy costs, which continued to rise sharply. Conversely, essential sectors such as transport and clothing saw price declines, indicating selective deflation that contrasts with persistent inflation in core living expenses. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates the utility of granular, regional statistics in tracking economic health. By making such localized inflation metrics accessible, policymakers and researchers can better analyze spatial disparities in the cost of living and inform targeted social or economic interventions.
