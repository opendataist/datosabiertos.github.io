'Don't lose sleep': Nandan Nilekani, brain behind Aadhaar, explains why he is not worried about DeepSeek, other AIs

'Don't lose sleep': Nandan Nilekani, brain behind Aadhaar, explains why he is not worried about DeepSeek, other AIs

Nandan Nilekani advises against anxiety regarding India’s lack of proprietary AI models, arguing that the focus should shift toward scaling existing initiatives rather than chasing competitors. He highlights a fundamental paradigm shift where society must trust non-human intelligence to make decisions, marking a departure from predictable, deterministic technologies of the past. The emergence of low-cost, open-source models has disrupted global markets by demonstrating that high performance is achievable at a fraction of traditional costs. This efficiency force major tech firms to accelerate their strategies and invest heavily in open-source development, fundamentally altering the economic landscape of artificial intelligence. This article is relevant to open_data because it underscores the strategic value of open-source approaches in democratizing access to advanced AI capabilities. By reducing barriers to entry and operational costs, open models enable broader participation and innovation, aligning with open data principles that prioritize transparency, accessibility, and collaborative development over proprietary exclusivity.
