La UE investiga el uso de datos personales por X para entrenar su IA

The Irish Data Protection Commission has launched an investigation into X, alleging that the company used publicly available user data to train its Grok AI models in violation of the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This probe follows the platform’s broken promise to cease such data usage for European users, raising critical questions about the legality and transparency of processing public information for artificial intelligence development. This case is crucial for open data, as it defines how personal data collected on open platforms can be repurposed. It highlights the tension between public accessibility and individual privacy rights, challenging the assumption that publicly shared data is exempt from regulatory scrutiny when used for commercial AI training. The outcome may influence broader EU-US tech relations, potentially leading to stricter enforcement or economic measures against American tech giants. Ultimately, it sets a precedent for how open data ecosystems must balance innovation with strict adherence to fundamental privacy standards across the digital economy.
