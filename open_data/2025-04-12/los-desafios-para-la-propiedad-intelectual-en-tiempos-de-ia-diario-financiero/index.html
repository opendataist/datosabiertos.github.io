Los desafíos para la propiedad intelectual en tiempos de IA | Diario Financiero

Los desafíos para la propiedad intelectual en tiempos de IA | Diario Financiero

The article highlights the critical tension between intellectual property rights and the practical necessity of training artificial intelligence systems. It concludes that rigid enforcement of copyright authorization for every dataset element is unfeasible and economically damaging. Therefore, legal frameworks must adopt flexible exceptions for data mining and text analysis to avoid stifling technological innovation and discouraging foreign investment, aligning with more permissive models used in countries like Japan. A central issue involves determining authorship and ownership of works generated by AI. While international consensus holds that AI systems cannot be legal authors, significant uncertainty remains regarding the level of human intervention required to grant protection to AI-assisted creations. The narrative suggests that legal requirements should focus on post-development stages rather than acting as barriers, ensuring that the law adapts to the evolving nature of human-machine collaboration without penalizing developers. This discussion is highly relevant to the open data movement because it underscores the need for transparent, accessible data ecosystems that balance legal protection with the freedom to innovate. Open data initiatives must advocate for regulatory environments that allow large-scale data processing for research and development. Ultimately, fostering growth requires laws that recognize the transformative potential of AI while safeguarding intangible assets through nuanced, technology-friendly regulations.
