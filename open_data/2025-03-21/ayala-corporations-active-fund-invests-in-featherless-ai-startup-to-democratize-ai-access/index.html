Ayala Corporation’s ACTIVE Fund invests in Featherless.ai startup to democratize AI access

Featherless.ai has secured investment from the ACTIVE Fund to democratize access to artificial intelligence by drastically reducing inference costs. This move addresses the high financial barriers that currently limit AI adoption, particularly for organizations and non-English users in emerging markets like the Philippines. By optimizing GPU utilization, the platform enables affordable access to a vast library of open-source models, shifting power away from a few tech giants and empowering individuals globally. The company’s flat pricing model allows businesses to scale AI workloads predictably without rate limits, fostering innovation through easier experimentation and fine-tuning. For casual users, low entry points remove the need for expensive hardware, making advanced AI tools accessible to a broader audience. This approach aligns with venture capital themes focusing on augmenting human productivity and ensuring that technological shifts benefit everyone rather than remaining exclusive to those with significant resources. This initiative is crucial for open data because it promotes the widespread use of open-source models instead of proprietary closed systems. By actively supporting open-source projects like RWKV and reducing reliance on major tech companies’ closed ecosystems, Featherless.ai strengthens the infrastructure for transparent, accessible AI development. This commitment ensures that AI technologies remain open, adaptable, and available to diverse linguistic and cultural groups, preserving the integrity and inclusivity of the open data movement.
