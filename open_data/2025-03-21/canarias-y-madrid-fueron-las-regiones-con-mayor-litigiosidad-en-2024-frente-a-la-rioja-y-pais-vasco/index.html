Canarias y Madrid fueron las regiones con mayor litigiosidad en 2024 frente a La Rioja y País Vasco

The study reveals significant regional disparities in Spain’s judicial activity, highlighting areas of intense litigiousness alongside those with lower case volumes. This variation suggests that local legal cultures and economic factors heavily influence access to and utilization of the justice system, rather than a uniform national trend. Despite a general increase in new cases across most jurisdictions, the courts improved their resolution capacity, effectively handling a larger portion of incoming disputes. This indicates a system under pressure but responding with greater efficiency, although the absolute volume of pending cases continues to rise, signaling ongoing backlog challenges that require sustained administrative management. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent judicial statistics enable public accountability and policy analysis. By making these metrics accessible, stakeholders can identify inefficiencies, advocate for necessary resource allocation, and evaluate the impact of legal reforms on society’s interaction with the judicial system.
