Pese a una leve baja, Mar del Plata volvió a ser la ciudad con mayor desocupación del país

Mar del Plata remains Argentina’s city with the highest unemployment rate, despite a slight quarterly improvement. This persistent crisis highlights significant regional disparities in the labor market and underscores the need for granular, localized open data to accurately reflect urban economic struggles. Nationally, unemployment rose slightly on a year-over-year basis, with women and long-term job seekers disproportionately affected. These trends reveal systemic inequalities that aggregated statistics often obscure, demonstrating why accessible, detailed datasets are vital for identifying vulnerable populations and informing equitable policy responses. Ultimately, this report emphasizes that while macro-indicators may show minor fluctuations, local realities demand persistent attention. Transparent open data enables communities and researchers to track these deep-seated issues, ensuring that decision-makers understand the true scale of labor market pressures and can implement targeted solutions for the most impacted areas.
