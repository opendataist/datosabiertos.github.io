Plataforma Nacional de Datos Abiertos: qué es, para qué sirve y cómo consultar información pública paso a paso

Open data represent a fundamental transformation in modern governance by providing digital public information that is free, accessible, and machine-readable. By meeting strict criteria such as non-discrimination and unrestricted use, these data enable their massive reuse by any citizen or entity. This availability not only fulfills the right to access information but also acts as a catalyst for proactive transparency, empowering civil society and governments to combat corruption and improve accountability. The National Open Data Platform consolidates this ecosystem by centralizing datasets generated by various government institutions in a single digital environment. Its utility goes beyond simple consultation, as it facilitates academic research, drives technological innovation, and supports evidence-based decision-making. By offering tools such as advanced filters and immediate download formats, the platform democratizes access to public statistics, allowing students, journalists, and entrepreneurs to transform raw information into actionable knowledge without bureaucratic barriers. This initiative is relevant to the open data movement because it materializes the principle of maximum disclosure, turning the human right to information into a practical tool for social development. By standardizing access to primary and permanent data, it fosters an ecosystem where transparency is not just a legal requirement but an active resource for improving public services. This model demonstrates how technology and public policy can align to strengthen citizen trust and promote a truly open and collaborative government.
