El INAI ordena a STPS informar si regularán a Uber, Didi o Rappi

El INAI ordena a STPS informar si regularán a Uber, Didi o Rappi

The INAI demands transparency from the STPS regarding reforms to regulate gig economy workers. This highlights the urgent need for social security access, as digital platform employees face high risks without protection, underscoring the necessity for clear labor classifications. Civil society and platforms have previously proposed regulatory frameworks to address these gaps. The government’s intent to create specific legal chapters for digital work reflects a critical shift in recognizing these non-standard employment relationships, ensuring workers’ rights are not overlooked. This case is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how administrative transparency drives policy changes. Public scrutiny of government processes ensures that digital labor regulations are developed openly, allowing citizens to monitor how emerging economic models are integrated into the legal framework.
