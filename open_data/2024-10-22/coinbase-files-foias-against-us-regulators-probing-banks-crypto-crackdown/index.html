Coinbase files FOIAs against US regulators probing banks’ crypto crackdown

Coinbase files FOIAs against US regulators probing banks’ crypto crackdown

Coinbase has intensified its efforts to obtain transparency regarding US financial regulators by filing new Freedom of Information Act requests. These submissions specifically target undisclosed policies, such as alleged deposit caps on cryptocurrency firms, which were reportedly implemented without the required public comment period. This move aims to expose hidden regulatory pressures that shape the digital asset landscape, highlighting how opaque administrative actions can significantly influence market operations and institutional participation. By seeking documentation on past regulatory responses and current banking directives, Coinbase underscores the critical importance of open data in holding government agencies accountable. The company’s legal strategy demonstrates that accessible public records are essential for understanding the true nature of enforcement actions. This transparency allows stakeholders to assess the consistency and legality of regulatory frameworks, ensuring that decisions affecting a rapidly growing sector are made through clear, documented processes rather than secretive guidelines. This pursuit of open data is particularly relevant as the US presidential election approaches, with crypto policy becoming a decisive factor for voters. As candidates differentiate themselves on digital asset regulations, public access to regulatory documents empowers investors and advocates to evaluate claims objectively. Ultimately, this transparency ensures that the development of the cryptocurrency industry is guided by clear, publicly available rules rather than arbitrary enforcement, fostering a fairer environment for innovation and investment.
