Lawmakers must address FOIA ruling

Lawmakers must address FOIA ruling

The Michigan Court of Appeals ruled that public employees are exempt from Freedom of Information Act requests, allowing school districts to withhold curriculum materials from parents. This decision enables officials to obscure operations by retaining documents within their workforce rather than releasing them through public bodies, significantly reducing accountability. This interpretation threatens open data principles by creating a loophole where taxpayer-funded activities remain hidden from scrutiny. When essential records are kept private by individual workers instead of accessible as public records, the fundamental promise of transparency is undermined, making it easier to conceal misconduct and erode public trust in governmental processes. To restore integrity, lawmakers must amend the act to explicitly include employee-held documents under disclosure requirements. Ensuring that all public employee records are accessible aligns with legislative intent and guarantees true transparency. This legal clarification is vital for maintaining open data standards and ensuring government serves the people effectively through complete visibility of its actions.
