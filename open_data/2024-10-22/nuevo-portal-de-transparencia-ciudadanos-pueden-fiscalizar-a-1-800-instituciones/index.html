Nuevo portal de transparencia: ciudadanos pueden fiscalizar a 1.800 instituciones

Nuevo portal de transparencia: ciudadanos pueden fiscalizar a 1.800 instituciones

Ecuador launched its National Transparency Portal, centralizing data that was previously scattered across individual institutional websites. This consolidation fulfills legal mandates and provides a unified access point for citizens, significantly enhancing the availability of public information on salaries, budgets, and administrative procedures. The platform aggregates extensive datasets from thousands of public entities, managed by transparency committees and designated officials. This shift simplifies the citizen experience, allowing direct access to critical governmental records without the need to navigate multiple disparate portals. This development is vital for open data initiatives, as it promotes government accountability through standardized, centralized access to information. By making public records more visible and accessible, the portal empowers civil society to effectively monitor state actions, fostering a culture of transparency and reducing informational asymmetry in public administration.
