Details Contained In Delhi Police Special Branch Manual Confidential, Exempted From Disclosure Under RTI Act: High Court

Details Contained In Delhi Police Special Branch Manual Confidential, Exempted From Disclosure Under RTI Act: High Court

The Delhi High Court determined that the Delhi Police Special Branch Manual is exempt from disclosure under the Right to Information Act, prioritizing national security over transparency. By classifying operational protocols as confidential, the ruling establishes that sensitive information regarding law enforcement methods cannot be made public, even when seeking accountability in government operations. This decision highlights the critical tension between open data principles and state secrecy in governance. It clarifies that while public interest drives transparency, the potential jeopardy to ongoing investigations and intelligence sources outweighs these demands. Consequently, internal policing procedures and verification processes remain protected from public scrutiny to preserve the integrity of security operations. For open data advocates, this case is significant as it defines the boundaries of permissible disclosure in law enforcement. It demonstrates how national security exemptions can restrict access to institutional knowledge, challenging efforts to achieve full administrative transparency. The ruling reinforces the argument that certain public sector data must remain closed, shaping future debates on balancing citizen rights with state security imperatives in digital governance.
