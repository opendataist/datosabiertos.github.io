Guatemala es la sede del evento más importante de datos abiertos en América Latina

Guatemala has been selected as the host of Abrelatam and Condatos 2026, solidifying its position as the regional hub for debates on open data, digital government, and transparency. This decision recognizes the country’s commitment to public innovation and democratic strengthening, positioning the Central American nation as a key node for shaping policies based on accountability and digital rights. The significance of this event lies in its ability to bring together governments, civil society, and the private sector around collaborative principles. By prioritizing horizontal participation and a focus on social justice, the gathering serves as a vital platform for discussing how data can safeguard rights and promote citizen engagement. This dynamic reinforces regional governance driven by international organizations, ensuring that transparency is not merely a technical concept but a tool for inclusion. For the open data community, this news implies a significant expansion of cooperation networks in Central America. The preparation for the 2026 edition fosters the creation of strategic alliances and joint projects that extend beyond the event itself. This not only democratizes access to information but also empowers diverse stakeholders to use data as instruments of social change, demonstrating the transformative power of interoperability and institutional openness in the region.
