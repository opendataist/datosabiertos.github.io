ONG colombiana: datos disponibles de las elecciones venezolanas dan ganador a González

The Electoral Observation Mission of Colombia concludes that the electoral ballot data published by the Venezuelan opposition invalidate the National Electoral Council’s claims regarding Nicolás Maduro’s re-election. Upon analyzing the available information, the organization determines that the margin of votes in favor of Edmundo González Urrutia is so substantial that the uncounted votes cannot alter this outcome, thereby confirming his victory. The analysis shows that even if all pending votes were allocated to the outgoing president, this would still be insufficient to close González’s lead. This conclusion is based on a comparison between the database constructed by the mission and the official bulletins, revealing a fundamental discrepancy in the narrative surrounding the electoral process. This finding is crucial for the open data movement, as it underscores the importance of verifying electoral statistics using transparent and comparable methodologies. By providing an alternative based on verifiable ballots, it promotes accountability and enables citizens and analysts to develop a more accurate understanding of democratic reality, beyond the unpublished official figures.
