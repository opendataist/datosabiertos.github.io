Brihuega celebra el incremento de población en 2024 con 82 habitantes más según los datos del INE

Brihuega demonstrates sustainable urban growth, surpassing 3,050 residents while significantly reducing unemployment. This demographic expansion reflects a successful municipal strategy that combines heritage recovery with enhanced public services, making the area attractive for investment and entrepreneurship. The local administration attributes this progress to coordinated efforts among regional, provincial, and municipal policies supporting rural revitalization. The town aims to become a benchmark for rural Spain by leveraging its cultural and natural assets to attract new residents. Employment figures show marked improvement, driven by seasonal tourism and targeted job creation initiatives. This holistic approach ensures that population growth translates into tangible economic opportunities and a higher quality of life for inhabitants. This case is relevant to open data because it highlights the necessity of comparing statistical sources, such as national institutes versus local registers, to verify municipal realities. Transparent access to granular demographic and labor data empowers communities to validate progress, foster accountability, and effectively benchmark rural development strategies against broader national trends.
