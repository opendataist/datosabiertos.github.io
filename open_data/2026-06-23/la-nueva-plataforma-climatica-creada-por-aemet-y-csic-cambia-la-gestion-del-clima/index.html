La nueva plataforma climática creada por AEMET y CSIC cambia la gestión del clima

The new climate platform, developed by AEMET and CSIC, centralizes meteorological data, observations, and scientific indicators in a single, accessible digital environment. This advancement transforms the historical dispersion of information into a unified resource, democratizing access to complex environmental statistics for governments, businesses, and citizens. The primary objective is to facilitate evidence-based decision-making, enabling more efficient planning in critical sectors such as renewable energy and water management. The tool stands out for its ability to analyze extreme phenomena in real time, including attributing events such as heatwaves or droughts to global warming. By integrating specific operational services, the platform not only provides historical data but also generates accurate projections to mitigate risks and improve climate adaptation. This capacity to translate big data into understandable and practical information is essential for strengthening society's resilience in the face of current environmental challenges. This initiative is relevant to the open data movement because it exemplifies the importance of transparency and interoperability in public data. By consolidating dispersed sources into an open and standardized platform, it eliminates information fragmentation and promotes data reuse for the common good. The platform demonstrates how open science and universal access to information can empower society to address global crises, setting a precedent for institutional collaboration that prioritizes public service and resilience based on verifiable data.
