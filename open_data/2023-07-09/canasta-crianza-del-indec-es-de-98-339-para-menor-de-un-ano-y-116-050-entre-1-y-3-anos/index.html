Canasta Crianza del INDEC: es de $98.339 para menor de un año y $116.050 entre 1 y 3 años

The article introduces a new monthly index designed to measure the actual costs of raising children in Argentina by evaluating both essential goods and the monetary value of required care hours. This indicator aims to provide an objective reference for family court proceedings, specifically to standardize and ensure the adequacy of alimony payments for minors. By quantifying the economic burden of childcare, the initiative seeks to address the widespread issue of unpaid child support and protect vulnerable families from economic violence. This development is highly relevant to open data because it establishes a transparent, public statistical baseline that can be accessed and utilized by citizens, civil society, and government bodies. The open availability of this data empowers families with accurate information for economic planning and allows for greater accountability in judicial decisions. Furthermore, it supports research and advocacy by providing standardized metrics on the cost of living for children, facilitating evidence-based policy making. The primary conclusion is that this index serves as a crucial tool to guarantee certainty in legal obligations and improve family financial planning. While it does not impose a rigid ceiling for alimony, it offers judges a consistent reference point to consider in specific cases. Ultimately, the system aims to reduce the significant debt owed by non-compliant parents, ensuring that the financial needs of children are met more reliably through transparent and data-driven legal frameworks.
