Belén Iniesta: "El PSOE es la respuesta a la crisis"

Isabel Belén Iniesta defends the Spanish government’s economic management, highlighting record growth and job creation despite global crises. She contrasts these positive indicators with the rhetoric of the political opposition, emphasizing structural labor reforms and consumer protection measures that significantly reduced costs for households. This case illustrates the role of open data in political accountability. By citing official statistics, advocates can verify claims against prevailing narratives, demonstrating how transparent data enables evidence-based debates. This transparency is crucial for citizens to distinguish factual economic performance from partisan claims. Ultimately, the article underscores that accessible, accurate data serves as a vital tool for democratic oversight. It allows stakeholders to assess policy impacts objectively, reinforcing the necessity of open data in fostering informed public discourse and verifying governmental actions.
