Cae producción de cerveza, ron y seco en Panamá

Panama’s alcohol industry has experienced a significant decline in total production, driven by sharp drops in traditional beverages such as beer and rum. Conversely, premium spirits like whisky and vodka have seen substantial growth, indicating a shift in consumer preference toward higher-end products. This data highlights evolving market dynamics within Panama’s beverage sector. Understanding these trends is crucial for analyzing how open data can reveal economic shifts, consumer behaviors, and the impact of industry regulations on production volumes over time. Such transparency enables stakeholders to make informed decisions based on verified, accessible statistics, demonstrating the value of public data in tracking regional economic changes and guiding future policy or investment strategies.
