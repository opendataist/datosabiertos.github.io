Se encarece 12.8% la carne de res

Se encarece 12.8% la carne de res

Rising beef prices in Mexico, driven by reduced local production, international market dynamics, and external factors such as drought and trade uncertainty, are significantly impacting consumer behavior. The sharp increase in costs has led to a notable decline in per capita consumption, disproportionately affecting lower-income families who are forced to reduce their intake of this historically expensive protein source. This situation underscores the critical role of open data in monitoring agricultural markets and public health. Transparent, accessible statistics on production and pricing enable policymakers and researchers to understand economic pressures and food security trends. By making such data publicly available, stakeholders can better assess how global and local shocks influence daily livelihoods. Ultimately, the ability to track these fluctuations through reliable open datasets is essential for informed decision-making. It allows society to identify vulnerable populations and adapt policies accordingly, ensuring that economic and environmental challenges do not go unnoticed or unaddressed in the broader context of national welfare.
