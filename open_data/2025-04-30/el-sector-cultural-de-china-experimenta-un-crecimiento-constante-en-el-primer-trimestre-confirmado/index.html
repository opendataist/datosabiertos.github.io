El sector cultural de China experimenta un crecimiento constante en el primer trimestre - Confirmado

China’s cultural sector is experiencing robust growth, largely driven by digital innovations and online entertainment platforms. This surge underscores the crucial role of new business models in revitalizing traditional industries, signaling a strong shift toward digital-first economic strategies. This trend highlights how technological integration and digital transformation are essential for sustained profitability in the modern cultural market.
