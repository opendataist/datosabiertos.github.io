Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production is dominated by China and India, which together account for a significant portion of the world’s output. This concentration highlights the critical role of Asian agriculture in the global supply chain, influencing food security and market stability for staple crops worldwide. Understanding these primary producers is essential for analyzing international trade flows and resource allocation in the agricultural sector. The FAOSTAT database serves as the primary open data source for tracking these trends, providing comprehensive statistics on crop yields and harvested areas. By making this information freely accessible, the organization empowers researchers and policymakers to conduct independent analyses of agricultural efficiency and global production disparities. This transparency supports evidence-based decision-making in international development and food policy. Relevance to open data lies in the FAO’s commitment to publishing standardized, machine-readable statistics that facilitate cross-border comparison. Access to such granular agricultural metrics allows for better monitoring of sustainable practices and economic trends. Open access to these datasets fosters innovation, enabling diverse stakeholders to model scenarios and address challenges related to global food systems without being hindered by proprietary barriers.
