¿Está la IA infringiendo los derechos de autor? El vacío legal en México y su impacto

¿Está la IA infringiendo los derechos de autor? El vacío legal en México y su impacto

The creation of images using artificial intelligence can lead to copyright and image rights violations, as these works are considered derivative and require explicit authorization from the original rights holder. This approach underscores the need for legal clarity, particularly in contexts where specific laws do not yet exist, necessitating the application of existing general principles to protect intellectual property against emerging new technologies. Currently, the absence of consolidated legislation has prompted Mexican companies to adopt self-regulation through internal codes, training, and review of platform terms. This initiative reflects the urgency of establishing risk management mechanisms before serious legislative proposals emerge, highlighting the critical importance of legal teams understanding technically how such content is generated in order to mitigate liabilities. This issue is relevant to open data because it highlights the growing market for authorized datasets used to train AI and for dataset auditing services. Transparency in the use of sensitive data and the validation of licenses for machine learning are fundamental to ensuring that artificial intelligence development respects intellectual property, thereby promoting ethical and legal standards in the availability and management of digital information.
