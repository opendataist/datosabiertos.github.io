NBS launches first prices data compiled through crowd-sourcing

NBS launches first prices data compiled through crowd-sourcing

The National Bureau of Statistics has launched a pioneering crowd-sourcing initiative to collect daily price data across Nigeria. This innovative approach marks a significant shift toward modernizing statistical methods by leveraging technology and public participation. By gathering information from diverse retail outlets and everyday citizens, the NBS aims to capture a more dynamic and accurate snapshot of current market conditions than traditional methods allow. This development is crucial for open data initiatives as it prioritizes real-time transparency and public accessibility. The collected data is made available through a dedicated dashboard, allowing researchers, policymakers, and entrepreneurs to analyze trends and make informed decisions quickly. By inviting citizens to submit feedback and data, the NBS fosters a collaborative statistical ecosystem that enhances trust and ensures the information remains relevant to a rapidly changing economy. However, it is vital to distinguish this crowdsourced pricing from the official Consumer Price Index. While the CPI relies on structured, periodic collections from predetermined outlets, this new dataset offers frequent, random updates. This distinction underscores the unique value of open, crowd-sourced data: it provides immediate insights into daily cost-of-living fluctuations, complementing rather than replacing established economic indicators with a broader, more responsive view of inflation.
