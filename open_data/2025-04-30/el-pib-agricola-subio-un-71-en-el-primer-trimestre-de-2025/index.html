El PIB agrícola subió un 7,1 % en el primer trimestre de 2025

The growth of Spain's primary sector is outpacing that of the broader economy, driven by domestic demand rather than external trade. This underscores the resilience of agriculture and related industries despite overall economic deceleration and reduced consumption. Open data remains essential for accurately tracking these sector-specific trends within national accounts. Accessible statistics enable analysts to distinguish between domestic drivers and external influences, revealing nuanced insights into economic health. Such granular data empowers stakeholders to understand structural shifts and the impact of policies. Transparent reporting fosters better decision-making and supports the broader goal of evidence-based governance in economic sectors.
