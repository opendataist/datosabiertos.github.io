Closing the gap between AI investment and impact: the rise of Open Data Infrastructure

Despite massive investments in artificial intelligence, most enterprises struggle to realize expected returns because their underlying data architectures are outdated and fragile. High budgets do not guarantee high-quality data; instead, many organizations face underperforming initiatives due to reliance on proprietary, tightly coupled systems that hinder scalability and innovation. This disconnect creates a significant gap between corporate ambition and technical capability, leaving teams burdened by maintenance rather than focused on strategic growth. The solution lies in adopting Open Data Infrastructure (ODI), a modular approach that prioritizes interoperability and shared standards over vendor-specific walled gardens. By separating storage from compute and ensuring consistent data access for both human and AI agents, ODI eliminates the friction caused by fragmented environments. This architectural shift not only prevents vendor lock-in but also provides the unified, trustworthy foundation necessary for AI systems to operate effectively, ensuring that all users interact with a single source of truth. For open data initiatives to succeed, they must be recognized as the essential backbone for modern AI strategies. Organizations that embrace open, flexible foundations are significantly more likely to meet their ROI targets compared to those clinging to legacy systems. Ultimately, prioritizing data maturity and open standards transforms data engineering from a cost center into a driver of business value, enabling scalable, efficient, and reliable AI applications across the enterprise.
