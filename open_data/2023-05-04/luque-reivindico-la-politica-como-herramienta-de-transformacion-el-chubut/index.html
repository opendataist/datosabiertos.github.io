Luque: “Reivindico la política como herramienta de transformación" - El Chubut

The article highlights a strategic initiative in Comodoro Rivadavia aimed at combating political distrust and bureaucratic inefficiency through the adoption of open government principles. By prioritizing transparency and providing continuous training for local officials and civil society, the administration seeks to foster greater citizen participation and accountability. This approach shifts the focus from political rhetoric to verifiable actions, establishing open data and digital tools as essential mechanisms for building trust and ensuring that governance effectively responds to community needs. Open data emerges as a critical instrument for this transformation, moving beyond mere compliance to become a driver of innovation and service improvement. The establishment of a municipal open data portal and the integration of federal digital programs demonstrate how accessible information can enhance decision-making processes. These tools enable a more direct and efficient connection between the government and residents, facilitating the creation of policies that are not only transparent but also deeply rooted in the actual demands and experiences of the population. This case is highly relevant to the open data community, as it illustrates the practical application of digital openness in public administration. It shows that open data is not merely a technical requirement but a political choice that facilitates social articulation and modernizes state functions. The narrative underscores the importance of linking data availability with active citizen engagement, demonstrating that transparency initiatives, when supported by adequate training and institutional commitment, can effectively bridge the gap between public institutions and society, creating a more collaborative and responsive governance model.
