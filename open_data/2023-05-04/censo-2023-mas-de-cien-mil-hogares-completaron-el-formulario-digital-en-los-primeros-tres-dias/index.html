Censo 2023: Más de cien mil hogares completaron el formulario digital en los primeros tres días

The 2023 Digital Census has recorded an exceptional citizen response in its first days, significantly exceeding international expectations for this initial phase. This high level of participation demonstrates that the digitization of the process is effective, secure, and user-friendly, facilitating the collection of essential data without the immediate need for in-person visits. The widespread acceptance suggests that the population trusts the platform and values the convenience of completing the form from mobile devices. The significance of this initiative lies in the quality of the data generated, which is fundamental for public policy planning. The collected information will enable the determination of the optimal location for public services such as schools and community kitchens, ensuring that infrastructure responds to the actual needs of communities. Additionally, the registration of foreign residents and the management of multiple dwellings ensure a more accurate and comprehensive demographic picture. From the perspective of open data, this success underscores the importance of technological accessibility in official statistics. By reducing participation barriers through an intuitive digital interface, the representativeness and reliability of the available dataset are enhanced. This not only optimizes the institute's resources but also strengthens the transparency and utility of public information for citizens and researchers.
