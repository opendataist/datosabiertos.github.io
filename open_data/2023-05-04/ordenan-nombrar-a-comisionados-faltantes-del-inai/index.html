Ordenan nombrar a comisionados faltantes del INAI

A federal judge ordered the Senate to appoint a third commissioner to the National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI), addressing the agency’s paralysis since April. This legal action affirms the Senate’s duty to ensure the autonomous body is properly constituted, acknowledging the harm caused by its prior omission. The ruling mandates that the legislative process fill the vacancy, enabling the institute to regain its five-member majority. This step is vital for the INAI to function effectively and uphold its constitutional mandate regarding transparency and data protection. This case underscores the critical link between institutional integrity and open data governance. A fully functional INAI is essential for enforcing citizens’ rights to access public information, demonstrating how legal mechanisms safeguard transparency against political neglect or inaction.
