Cuidado con este timo por SMS: suplantan a la Seguridad Social y solicitan actualizar la tarjeta sanitaria

A recent phishing campaign impersonates the Spanish Social Security Administration via SMS, urging victims to update their health card on fraudulent websites. This scam aims to steal personal data for further cyberattacks, highlighting the critical vulnerability of public institutions in digital communications and the ease with which sensitive information can be harvested. The relevance to open data lies in the stark contrast between transparent government services and the malicious exploitation of public trust. It demonstrates how easily open channels of communication can be hijacked, posing significant risks to individual privacy and data integrity when citizens interact with official entities online. Protecting oneself involves being skeptical of unsolicited requests and verifying sources before sharing information. This incident underscores the need for heightened digital literacy and robust cybersecurity awareness to safeguard personal data against sophisticated social engineering tactics that mimic legitimate administrative procedures.
