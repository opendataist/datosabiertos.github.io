Tomate, gas y cebolla, lo que más aceleró la inflación quincenal

Tomate, gas y cebolla, lo que más aceleró la inflación quincenal

The INEGI reports Mexico’s annual inflation at 5.61%, driven primarily by sharp price increases in tomatoes, gasoline, and onions, which significantly impacted the index. Conversely, declines in serrano peppers, tomatillos, and automobiles helped moderately contain overall price growth. This data is vital for open data initiatives, demonstrating how granular, accessible government statistics enable public analysis of economic drivers and resource allocation.
