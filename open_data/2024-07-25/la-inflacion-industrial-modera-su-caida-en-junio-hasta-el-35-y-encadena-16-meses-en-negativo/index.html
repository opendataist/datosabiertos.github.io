La inflación industrial modera su caída en junio hasta el 3,5% y encadena 16 meses en negativo

Industrial prices in Spain have exhibited significant deflation, marking sixteen consecutive months of negative annual growth. This sustained decline in costs, driven primarily by reductions in energy and intermediate goods, highlights a structural shift in the industrial sector’s pricing dynamics compared to the previous years of high inflation. The divergence between energy-excluded prices and the general index suggests that while volatile energy costs distort the overall picture, underlying industrial pressures remain relatively contained. This separation is crucial for understanding the real inflationary trend within the manufacturing base, distinct from temporary energy fluctuations. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, high-frequency economic indicators. Such granular, publicly accessible statistics enable researchers and developers to build robust models for predicting industrial trends, fostering transparency and supporting evidence-based policy analysis in the economic sector.
