Partnership brings Click-Ins’ AI inspections to Draiver customers

Click-Ins has partnered with vehicle delivery service Draiver to integrate artificial intelligence-driven vehicle inspections into their operations. This collaboration utilizes AI and synthetic data to generate detailed condition reports through mobile devices, eliminating the need for specialized hardware. The technology allows for precise documentation of damage, ensuring accurate identification of when and by whom damage occurred. The initiative significantly enhances the reliability and speed of vehicle assessments across various automotive verticals in the U.S. and internationally. By leveraging this intuitive interface, businesses can streamline inspection processes, minimize oversight, and make better-informed decisions regarding vehicle condition and liability. This transformation empowers clients to operate more efficiently while maintaining high standards of accountability. This development is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how AI technologies can automate and standardize the collection of complex physical data, such as vehicle damage metrics. The ability to generate structured, data-backed reports from simple visual inputs highlights the potential for digitizing physical assets. Such advancements encourage the broader adoption of open, accessible data standards in the automotive sector, facilitating greater transparency and interoperability across the pre-owned market ecosystem.
