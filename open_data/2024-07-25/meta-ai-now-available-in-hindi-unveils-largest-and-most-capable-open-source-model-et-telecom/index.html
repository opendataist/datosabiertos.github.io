Meta AI now available in Hindi, unveils largest and most capable open-source model - ET Telecom

Meta AI now available in Hindi, unveils largest and most capable open-source model - ET Telecom

Meta has significantly expanded the accessibility and capabilities of its AI assistant, introducing support for seven new languages including Hindi and deployment in seven Latin American and African nations. This expansion allows users across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Facebook to interact with the assistant in their preferred tongues, marking a strategic move to democratize AI access for non-English speaking populations globally. Central to this advancement is the release of Llama 3.1, the largest and most capable open-source AI model to date. By making this powerful 405-billion-parameter model publicly available, Meta reinforces its commitment to the open-source ecosystem, enabling developers and researchers to leverage sophisticated reasoning and coding capabilities for their own innovative projects without proprietary restrictions. Furthermore, Meta is enhancing user creativity through new image generation and editing tools, allowing individuals to visualize concepts and modify images with precision. These developments are highly relevant to the open_data movement, as they demonstrate how open-source models can drive widespread technological adoption and innovation, bridging the gap between complex AI research and practical, everyday utility for diverse global communities.
