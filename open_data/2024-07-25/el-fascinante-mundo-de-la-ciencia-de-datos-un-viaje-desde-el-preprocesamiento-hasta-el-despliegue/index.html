El fascinante mundo de la Ciencia de Datos: Un viaje desde el preprocesamiento hasta el despliegue

The article highlights that data science transforms raw information into actionable knowledge through a rigorous preprocessing process. This initial phase is critical, as it involves cleaning, structuring, and selecting the appropriate data, ensuring that the quality of the input determines the accuracy of predictive models and subsequent analysis. Subsequently, the importance of integrating good software engineering practices with technical tools and statistical foundations is emphasized. Code efficiency, the use of specialized programming languages, and the application of machine learning algorithms enable the automation of complex discoveries. This demonstrates that technology not only stores data but also interprets it to generate valuable insights and predict future trends. This approach is fundamental to the open data agenda because it validates the need for clean, well-documented, and standardized public datasets. Without a solid technical infrastructure and rigorous methodology, transparency and access to public information would not translate into the real democratization of knowledge or evidence-based policymaking.
