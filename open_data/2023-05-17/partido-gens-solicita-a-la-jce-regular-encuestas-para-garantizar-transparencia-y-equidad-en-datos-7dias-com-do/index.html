Partido GenS solicita a la JCE regular encuestas para garantizar transparencia y equidad en datos - 7dias.com.do

Partido GenS solicita a la JCE regular encuestas para garantizar transparencia y equidad en datos - 7dias.com.do

Carlos Peña, presidential candidate for the Party of Servants Generation, has requested that the Central Electoral Board (JCE) strictly regulate electoral polls. His main concern lies in the systematic bias displayed by polling firms, which disproportionately favor the three parties that led in the previous elections. This practice marginalizes emerging and recent political organizations, distorting public perception and undermining the fairness of the democratic process. The communication highlights that the lack of oversight allows for the arbitrary exclusion of candidates, violating the principle of equality before the law and the right to vote. By presenting only results that favor established parties, a false perception of inevitability is created, which demotivates voters and restricts the fundamental rights of new political actors. It is argued that the JCE must ensure that all legally recognized political formations are included equitably in opinion polls. This article is relevant to the field of open data because it raises the need for transparency and accessibility in research methodologies. The proposed regulation seeks to prevent sample manipulation and the concealment of methodologies, ensuring that the generated information is truthful, inclusive, and usable for an informed citizenry. Without open and rigorous standards, electoral data can become tools of political exclusion rather than instruments of democratic participation.
