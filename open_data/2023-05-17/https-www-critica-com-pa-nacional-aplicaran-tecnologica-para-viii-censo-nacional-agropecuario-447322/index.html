The VIII National Agricultural Census, led by Panama’s National Institute of Statistics and Census (INEC), aims to modernize data on the country’s productive sector by establishing a comprehensive registry of producers, locations, and outputs. This initiative ensures alignment with international standards and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, creating a robust foundation for evidence-based agricultural policy and strategic planning. The census employs a hybrid methodology, using mobile devices for data collection while retaining paper questionnaires for areas with limited access. By incorporating new indicators on crop diseases, social security, and artisanal fishing, the census enhances the understanding of rural livelihoods. This approach also leverages existing national records and collaborations with various agricultural entities to strengthen the data framework. This update is crucial for open data initiatives, as it transforms fragmented information into a standardized, accessible public resource. By providing detailed and reliable statistics, the census empowers researchers, policymakers, and citizens to analyze agricultural trends and contribute to transparent, data-driven decision-making across Panama.
