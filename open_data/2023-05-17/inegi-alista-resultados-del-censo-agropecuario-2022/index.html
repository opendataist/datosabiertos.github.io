INEGI alista resultados del Censo Agropecuario 2022

INEGI alista resultados del Censo Agropecuario 2022

The upcoming agricultural census conducted by the INEGI represents a pivotal moment for transparency, aiming to provide the most comprehensive historical data on Mexican farming practices. By achieving near-total coverage and making this information publicly available, the initiative establishes a robust baseline for analyzing the sector’s evolution over the past sixteen years. Open data is crucial in this context, as it empowers society and decision-makers to verify production realities, from who produces what to how resources are utilized. The availability of this verified information enables objective comparisons of agricultural conditions, moving beyond speculation to an evidence-based understanding of whether the sector is improving or deteriorating. Ultimately, this dataset serves as the foundation for designing effective public policies. By ensuring accurate, accessible statistics, stakeholders can identify real problems and track trends over time. This commitment to truth and accessibility demonstrates how open data directly supports better governance and informed resource allocation in the agricultural sector.
