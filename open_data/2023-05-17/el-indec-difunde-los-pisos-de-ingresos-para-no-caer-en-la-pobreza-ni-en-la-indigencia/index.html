El Indec difunde los pisos de ingresos para no caer en la pobreza ni en la indigencia

The upcoming release by INDEC of the Basic Food Basket and the Total Basket for April is crucial for establishing the poverty and indigence thresholds. These figures directly determine eligibility for public service subsidies, excluding households whose income exceeds 3.5 times the Total Basket. This linkage makes the data pivotal for assessing the coverage of social protection programs amid soaring inflation. Inflation has significantly increased the value of these baskets, with sharp rises in food prices contributing to high annual inflation rates. As a result, the income levels required to avoid extreme poverty and basic poverty have risen substantially, both on a monthly and annual basis. This trend reflects the erosion of citizens' purchasing power and the growing financial pressure on low-income families. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it underscores the need for transparent, accessible, and timely economic statistics. Accurate public data enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to effectively monitor inequality. It allows society to hold institutions accountable and understand how macroeconomic policies affect vulnerable populations, thereby fostering informed public debate and evidence-based advocacy.
