Nigeria’s inflation increases to 22.22 per cent - The Chronicle News Online

Nigeria’s rising inflation, fueled by soaring food and core prices, persists despite aggressive central bank interest rate hikes. This trend highlights a critical gap between monetary policy efforts and actual price stabilization, revealing the limitations of traditional tools in managing complex economic pressures. For open data advocates, this report underscores the vital importance of transparent, high-quality statistical releases. Reliable data enables researchers and citizens to accurately assess policy efficacy and hold institutions accountable. Without accessible, detailed consumer price indices, it remains difficult to analyze the root causes of inflation or propose effective, data-driven solutions. Ensuring such data is freely available is essential for informed public discourse and better governance.
