Conteo poblacional en Bolivia “altamente positivo” - Noticias Prensa Latina

Conteo poblacional en Bolivia “altamente positivo” - Noticias Prensa Latina

An international commission composed of high-level technical bodies from the United Nations and global financial institutions has validated Bolivia’s preliminary census data. The evaluation confirms that population figures at both national and subnational levels are consistent with recent trends in fertility, mortality, and migration. This rigorous technical oversight assures citizens and policymakers that the process was modern, transparent, and participatory, thereby fulfilling the state’s commitments to quality and integrity. Bolivian authorities highlighted the historic level of volunteer participation and the civic spirit of families who opened their homes to enumerators. The commission substantially praised the significant effort demonstrated during data collection and analysis. This collaboration underscores a successful public-private partnership, in which technical precision converged with widespread social engagement to ensure accurate demographic coverage across the country. This validation is crucial for open data initiatives, as it establishes a reliable, globally recognized dataset for designing public policies. High-quality, transparent census data enables evidence-based decision-making and fosters trust in statistical institutions. By ensuring data coherence and accessibility, Bolivia sets a benchmark for how rigorous technical standards can support democratic governance and the effective allocation of resources for future development.
