ONU insiste en preocupación por falta transparencia Venezuela

The United Nations has called for greater transparency in Venezuela’s elections, citing ongoing concerns about the lack of public data. This situation underscores the critical role of open data in verifying electoral integrity and ensuring democratic accountability. Without accessible records, public trust erodes, highlighting the necessity for transparent information systems in political processes.
