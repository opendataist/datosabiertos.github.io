Cayó 13% el turismo en la Argentina durante el primer semestre, según el Indec - La Voz de Cataratas

Cayó 13% el turismo en la Argentina durante el primer semestre, según el Indec - La Voz de Cataratas

Argentina’s tourism sector saw a significant contraction in the first half of 2024 compared to the previous year, primarily driven by a sharp decline in international visitors. This downturn followed the removal of prior exchange rate advantages that had previously attracted foreign tourists for shopping purposes, resulting in a substantial reduction in cross-border visits. The overall decline underscores the fragility of tourism revenues when policy environments change, with specific regions such as Buenos Aires experiencing even steeper drops in both domestic and foreign visitor numbers. This trend indicates that, without competitive economic factors, attracting travelers becomes considerably more difficult, thereby affecting the hospitality industry’s recovery and growth. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the need for high-quality, accessible statistics to monitor economic health. Reliable data from institutions such as INDEC enables policymakers and researchers to analyze these drastic changes, assess the impact of currency policies, and make informed decisions to stabilize and revitalize the tourism sector.
