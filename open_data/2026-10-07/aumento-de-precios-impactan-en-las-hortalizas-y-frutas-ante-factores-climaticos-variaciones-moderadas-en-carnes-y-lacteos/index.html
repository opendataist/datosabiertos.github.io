Aumento de precios impactan en las hortalizas y frutas ante factores climáticos variaciones moderadas en carnes y lácteos

Aumento de precios impactan en las hortalizas y frutas ante factores climáticos variaciones moderadas en carnes y lácteos

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) reveals that Uruguay’s inflation accelerated in September, closing the year with rates within the official target range. This stability reflects an economic management approach that keeps expectations aligned with fiscal objectives, despite seasonal pressures on food prices and adjustments in basic services. This report is relevant to open data initiatives, as it exemplifies how official statistics should be transparent and accessible. The availability of detailed breakdowns by category enables researchers and citizens to analyze the differentiated impact on purchasing power, fostering effective, evidence-based accountability grounded in numerical data. The open structure of these data facilitates their reuse for evaluating public policies and understanding household spending dynamics. By making price trends visible without technical barriers, civil society is empowered to monitor economic well-being, demonstrating the value of public data as a fundamental democratic and analytical tool.
