Mujeres y niñas de Gaza enfrentan muerte y graves riesgos de salud - Noticias Prensa Latina

Mujeres y niñas de Gaza enfrentan muerte y graves riesgos de salud - Noticias Prensa Latina

The article highlights the devastating humanitarian crisis in Gaza, emphasizing the high toll on lives and maternal health due to the protracted conflict. It concludes that the war has caused extreme mortality and widespread suffering, disproportionately affecting women, girls, and vulnerable groups. The deteriorated healthcare infrastructure has triggered a reproductive emergency, marked by a sharp increase in abortions, pregnancy-related deaths, and cases of malnutrition. The lack of access to prenatal and obstetric care, combined with the destruction of medical facilities, leaves pregnant women in critical risk even after a ceasefire. This information is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparency and access to accurate data are vital for documenting human rights violations and mobilizing international responses. Moreover, it underscores the challenges of data collection in war-torn contexts, where the absence of precise records can underestimate the true scale of the tragedy and hinder humanitarian aid.
