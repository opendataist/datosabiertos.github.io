Ley de transparencia retributiva: qué información puedes exigir a tu empresa

Ley de transparencia retributiva: qué información puedes exigir a tu empresa

The entry into force of the pay transparency law marks a fundamental change by requiring that the essential elements of the employment relationship, such as salary and working hours, be documented in writing. This mandate aims to eliminate the ambiguity that previously existed, ensuring that workers know their conditions from day one, regardless of whether their contract was signed before or after the regulation came into effect. A key aspect is the mandatory disclosure regarding the use of algorithms in labor decision-making, which introduces a crucial component of digital transparency for employees. Furthermore, the law establishes clear deadlines for providing this information and requires that it be adapted for people with disabilities, reinforcing inclusion and clarity in internal corporate communications. This regulation is relevant to the open data movement by promoting the standardization and accessibility of labor information, a principle aligned with public transparency. By requiring organizations to document and provide structured data on compensation and working conditions, it fosters a culture of accountability and empowers workers with verifiable knowledge, thereby reducing information asymmetries in the labor market.
