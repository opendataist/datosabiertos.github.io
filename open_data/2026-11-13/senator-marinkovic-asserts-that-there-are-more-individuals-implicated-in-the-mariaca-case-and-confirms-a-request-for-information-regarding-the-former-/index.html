Senator Marinkovic asserts that there are more individuals implicated in the Mariaca case and confirms a request for information regarding the former prosecutor's phone calls

Senator Marinkovic asserts that there are more individuals implicated in the Mariaca case and confirms a request for information regarding the former prosecutor's phone calls

The senator demands that the investigations into the Mariaca case be expanded to identify all individuals involved in the drug trafficking network. He calls for a review of the former prosecutor’s phone calls and for the complete dismantling of the criminal structure, ensuring that the justice system operates independently. This issue is relevant to open data because official communications and judicial records must be accessible. Transparency in these data allows civil society to verify the integrity of the investigations and monitor the progress of the justice system. Public dissemination of this information strengthens accountability. By making institutional links and actions visible, it promotes citizen participation in the fight against corruption, ensuring that everyone is held accountable under the law.
