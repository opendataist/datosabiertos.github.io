Accessibility - Eurostat

Eurostat demonstrates a strong institutional commitment to digital inclusivity, ensuring its statistical database is navigable for individuals with disabilities. By aligning with international accessibility standards, the organization prioritizes equal access to public data, allowing users to zoom, navigate via keyboard, and utilize screen readers effectively. This foundational approach underscores the principle that open data must be universally reachable, removing technical barriers for diverse user groups. However, the website remains only partially compliant due to significant gaps in visual and document-based content. Interactive visualizations and maps often lack necessary text alternatives for screen reader users, and many downloadable files in formats like PDF or Excel remain inaccessible. While Eurostat has begun addressing these issues, particularly for newer content, legacy materials and complex graphical tools still hinder full equitable access, highlighting the ongoing challenge of retrofitting legacy systems for accessibility. This case is highly relevant to open data because technical accessibility directly impacts the utility of public statistics. Open data initiatives fail if the presentation layer excludes users with disabilities, regardless of data availability. Eurostat’s transparency regarding known limitations serves as a critical benchmark for other data publishers, emphasizing that true openness requires continuous improvement in interface design and file formatting to ensure inclusive participation in societal data usage.
