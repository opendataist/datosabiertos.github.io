Crimes against businesses in Mexico City drop by 35%: Pablo Vázquez

Crimes against businesses in Mexico City drop by 35%: Pablo Vázquez

The National Survey of Victimization of Businesses reveals a significant reduction in crimes against establishments in Mexico City. This favorable trend positively impacts the perception of business security and operational costs, creating a more attractive environment for local investment. The analysis highlights that behaviors such as petty theft and fraud persist as the main threats, although extortion has lost relevance. The disparity in the decline of crimes across economic sectors suggests the need for specific strategies tailored to the particular vulnerabilities of each industry. This case illustrates the importance of open data for public transparency and accountability. By democratizing access to official statistics, citizens and analysts can independently verify government claims, fostering trust in institutions and enabling an objective assessment of security policies and their actual effectiveness.
