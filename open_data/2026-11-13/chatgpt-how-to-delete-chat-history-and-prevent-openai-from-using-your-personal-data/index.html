ChatGPT: How to delete chat history and prevent OpenAI from using your personal data

ChatGPT: How to delete chat history and prevent OpenAI from using your personal data

Deleting a conversation in ChatGPT is insufficient to protect privacy, as there are three independent layers of data management: history, use for model training, and the memory feature. Deleting the history does not instantly remove all information, since the platform may retain it temporarily to prevent abuse, which limits the effectiveness of this isolated action. Likewise, disabling the use of data for model training only prevents future exploitation of the information, without reversing data already incorporated into the systems. This highlights the complexity of user-platform data control, where preventive and corrective actions operate under distinct temporal and structural logics that users must understand. This content is relevant to open data because it illustrates the challenges of algorithmic transparency and personal data sovereignty. It reveals how major technology companies handle information opaquely, hindering accountability and the right to digital erasure. Understanding these layers is crucial for demanding clearer standards regarding the lifecycle of both open and private data in artificial intelligence environments.
