To Honor Veterans, How About We Stop The Oil Wars? - CleanTechnica

To Honor Veterans, How About We Stop The Oil Wars? - CleanTechnica

The article critiques the persistent cycle of US military interventions in the Middle East, arguing that these conflicts are primarily driven by foreign oil dependencies rather than genuine security needs. It posits that ending this pattern would protect service members from unnecessary harm and honor their sacrifice by avoiding counterproductive "forever wars." This perspective is relevant to open_data because transparent access to energy production, consumption, and geopolitical trade data is essential for holding governments accountable. When stakeholders can openly examine the true drivers of military spending and foreign policy, it empowers public discourse and supports evidence-based advocacy for sustainable energy transitions. Ultimately, shifting away from fossil fuel reliance requires robust, accessible information to challenge prevailing narratives. Open data initiatives facilitate this transparency, enabling citizens to understand the economic and strategic realities behind international conflicts and pushing for policies that prioritize peace and renewable energy independence.
