Euclid detects more than 900 million galaxies and maps | DIARIO DEL SUR

Euclid detects more than 900 million galaxies and maps | DIARIO DEL SUR

Euclid has released its first massive dataset, revealing hundreds of millions of galaxies and marking a crucial step toward creating a detailed three-dimensional map of the universe. This resource enables the study of dark matter through gravitational lensing, providing essential tools for understanding the invisible cosmic structure. The relevance to open data lies in the fact that these large-scale astronomical datasets are publicly accessible and verifiable. By democratizing access to complex observational information, this initiative fosters scientific transparency and global collaboration. It allows researchers from diverse disciplines to explore phenomena such as dark energy, accelerating discoveries that were previously limited to specific institutions and demonstrating the transformative impact of sharing open data in fundamental science.
