CANACO denies extortion cases against businesses in Querétaro

CANACO denies extortion cases against businesses in Querétaro

The president of the Querétaro Chamber of Commerce argues that the rise in business victimization figures reflects sector growth and a greater culture of reporting, rather than a deterioration in security. When the data are normalized by the number of businesses, the incidence of crime remains low, highlighting the absence of extortion and confidence in local authorities. This interpretation is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates the importance of context when interpreting public statistics. The article emphasizes that raw figures can be misleading without proportional and contextualized analysis. Therefore, it advocates for the responsible use of open data, encouraging analyses that take into account variables such as the number of establishments to avoid misunderstandings about the actual security situation.
