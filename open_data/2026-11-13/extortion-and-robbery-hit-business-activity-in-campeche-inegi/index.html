Extortion and robbery hit business activity in Campeche: INEGI

Extortion and robbery hit business activity in Campeche: INEGI

The article reveals that insecurity and the economic crisis in Campeche represent a critical challenge for transparency and business development, which are fundamental areas of the open data movement. Indicators show a significant increase in crime prevalence, with extortion and theft being the most common forms of victimization affecting businesses, resulting in substantial financial impacts that undermine companies' operational stability. Beyond violence, the state is experiencing a severe economic contraction, marked by negative figures, which has led to budget cuts and business closures. This situation highlights a direct correlation between the lack of legal security and the decline in economic activity, factors that deter investment and deteriorate the quality of life for local citizens and entrepreneurs. The relevance to open data lies in the need to systematize and make these indicators accessible for public scrutiny. The availability of accurate statistics on victimization and economic health enables citizens and researchers to analyze patterns, demand accountability from authorities, and propose evidence-based solutions to improve the business environment and safety in the state.
