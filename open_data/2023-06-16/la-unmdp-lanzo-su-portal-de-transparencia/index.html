La UNMDP lanzó su Portal de Transparencia

La UNMDP lanzó su Portal de Transparencia

The National University of Mar del Plata launched its Transparency Portal, a public platform that strengthens institutional accountability and the right to information. This tool enables citizens to access data on personnel, academic programs, and financial budgets, thereby promoting open government practices. The initiative supports social oversight of public funds and fosters a culture of transparency. By centralizing accessible and timely information, the university allows stakeholders to effectively monitor the use of resources. This aligns with national and international open government standards. This development is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how higher education institutions can operationalize data openness. It illustrates the practical application of publishing structured public sector data, facilitating civic engagement and reinforcing democratic oversight through accessible digital platforms.
