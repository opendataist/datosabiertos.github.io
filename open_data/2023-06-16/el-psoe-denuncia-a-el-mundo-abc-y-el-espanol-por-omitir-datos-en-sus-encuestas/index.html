El PSOE denuncia a ‘El Mundo’, ‘ABC’ y ‘El Español’ por omitir datos en sus encuestas

El PSOE denuncia a ‘El Mundo’, ‘ABC’ y ‘El Español’ por omitir datos en sus encuestas

The PSOE accused major media outlets of omitting mandatory data in recent polls, highlighting the critical link between political transparency and public trust. This conflict underscores the open data principle that full methodological disclosure is essential for verifying the integrity of public opinion surveys. By demanding complete access to technical specifications and sample details, the dispute illustrates how withholding information can distort democratic processes. It emphasizes that open standards in polling are not merely technicalities but fundamental requirements for accountability and informed civic engagement. This incident is relevant to open data as it demonstrates real-world friction over information accessibility. It reinforces the necessity for institutions to adhere to transparency norms, ensuring that citizens can fully understand and critique the data influencing political narratives.
