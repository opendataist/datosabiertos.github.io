AI training goes visual: OpenAI and Google reportedly trained their AI models on YouTube

Yann LeCun emphasizes that video data is crucial for enabling AI to develop hierarchical world representations, mimicking human cognition more effectively than text-only models. This shift suggests that diverse, multimodal training data will significantly enhance the general reasoning capabilities of artificial intelligence systems. The relevance to open data lies in the urgent need for high-quality, accessible video datasets. Open-source initiatives must prioritize creating and sharing these multimodal resources to democratize access to advanced AI training, preventing reliance on proprietary data held by major tech firms. While text remains valuable for specific tasks like customer support automation, integrating visual understanding represents a fundamental leap in AI development. Open data communities should focus on building robust video corpora to ensure equitable progress in creating more intelligent, human-like machines.
