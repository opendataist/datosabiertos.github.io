En agosto, INAI presenta Política de Datos Abiertos

Mexico is launching its National Open Data Policy this August to standardize transparency across thousands of public entities. This initiative ensures consistent access to information, addressing the urgent need for unified standards in government data management. Its broad societal consultation underscores the commitment to inclusive, transparent governance, reinforcing the fundamental role of open data in democratic accountability and citizen engagement.
