Producción agrícola cae a su peor nivel desde hace 31 años por el Niño costero

Peru's agriculture is experiencing its most severe decline in decades due to the coastal El Niño. The crisis threatens national food security and the resilience of open data systems. This event underscores the urgent need for improved predictive modeling in climate and agricultural data. Accurate data is essential for mitigating economic impacts and supporting vulnerable communities.
