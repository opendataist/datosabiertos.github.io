Dresner Advisory Services Publishes 2023 Data Catalog Market Study

Data catalogs remain critical infrastructure for simplifying access to analytical content while ensuring governance and trustworthiness. Despite their strategic importance across various business functions, significant challenges persist in data discovery. Many users still struggle to locate necessary information, highlighting a gap between tool availability and effective usability in real-world operations. This discrepancy indicates that current data discovery processes require substantial improvement across diverse industries and organizational sizes. Implementing robust cataloging practices is essential to bridge this gap, ensuring that analytic assets are not only stored but are genuinely accessible and usable by the people who need them most. This research is relevant to open data as it underscores the necessity of structured metadata and governance. Without clear discovery mechanisms, open data risks remaining fragmented or unusable. Effective cataloging ensures that open datasets are transparent, findable, and interoperable, fulfilling the core principles of open data ecosystems.
