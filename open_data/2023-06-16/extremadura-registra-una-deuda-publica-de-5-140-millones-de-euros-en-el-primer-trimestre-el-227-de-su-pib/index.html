Extremadura registra una deuda pública de 5.140 millones de euros en el primer trimestre, el 22,7% de su PIB

Extremadura’s public debt reached €5,140 million, representing 22.7% of its GDP in the first quarter. This figure underscores the region’s fiscal health and its reliance on debt financing. Open data platforms should monitor such debt metrics to ensure transparency and accountability in public finances. This facilitates more effective analysis and comparison of regional economic indicators. The availability of this data enables citizens and researchers to track government spending and debt levels. It promotes informed decision-making and fosters trust in public institutions.
