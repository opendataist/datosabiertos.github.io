iGA Chief launches Beta Version of revamped Bahrain Open Data Portal | THE DAILY TRIBUNE | KINGDOM OF BAHRAIN

iGA Chief launches Beta Version of revamped Bahrain Open Data Portal | THE DAILY TRIBUNE | KINGDOM OF BAHRAIN

The Information & eGovernment Authority has launched the beta version of the Bahrain Open Data Portal, aligning with national economic recovery goals and international best practices. This initiative aims to enhance transparency and encourage eParticipation by providing accurate, reliable government data accessible to all beneficiaries. By equipping the platform with advanced features, the authority ensures that critical sector indicators are readily available for public use. The portal serves as a central hub for integrated open datasets and Geographic Information System data from various government entities. It empowers decision-makers, investors, researchers, and citizens to leverage this information for service development, research studies, and investment projects. Removing technical, financial, and legal barriers allows for unrestricted reuse of data, fostering an environment where innovation can thrive across both commercial and non-commercial sectors. This development is highly relevant to open_data as it demonstrates a government’s commitment to making public information freely accessible and machine-readable. By inviting feedback from the public, private, and academic sectors, the project emphasizes the collaborative nature of open data ecosystems. It underscores how standardized, transparent data sharing can drive economic growth and societal innovation, setting a precedent for accountability and digital inclusion in governance.
