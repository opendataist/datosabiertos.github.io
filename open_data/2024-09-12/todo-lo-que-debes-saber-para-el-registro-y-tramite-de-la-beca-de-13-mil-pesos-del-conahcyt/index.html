Todo lo que debes saber para el registro y trámite de la beca de 13 mil pesos del Conahcyt

CONACYT has launched a scholarship program designed to support postgraduate students pursuing specialty programs and master’s degrees. By providing essential financial aid, these grants ensure that students can cover their living expenses and successfully complete their academic training without economic barriers hindering their progress. The application process is structured in phases, prioritizing students in the highest national program categories. While the initial window targets those beginning their studies in early autumn, subsequent periods accommodate late starters and those needing to correct inconsistencies in their applications. This phased approach ensures broad accessibility, requiring beneficiaries to formalize their agreements to secure the funds. This initiative is relevant to open data because it relies on standardized digital platforms, such as Rizoma, for curriculum updates and identity verification using unique identifiers. The transparency in eligibility criteria and the public nature of the scholarship guidelines reflect broader commitments to data-driven administrative processes. Furthermore, the availability of detailed procedural information online enhances accountability and allows for potential analysis of educational funding distribution trends.
