La compra de vivienda crece un 18,1% en julio: ¿dónde se activa con más fuerza el mercado?

The provisional data reveal a robust recovery in the Spanish housing market, characterized by significant increases in both residential property transactions and mortgage registrations across nearly all regions. This upward trend indicates growing consumer confidence and liquidity, marking a sharp contrast to the stagnation of previous years. The widespread nature of this growth, extending from the northern to the southern autonomous communities, suggests a structural rebound rather than isolated local anomalies. Such uniformity implies that the market is stabilizing at the national level, driven by renewed demand despite broader economic uncertainties. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how public registry statistics provide transparent, high-quality evidence of economic health. Accessible, granular datasets like these empower researchers and policymakers to monitor market dynamics accurately, ensuring transparency in evaluations of the housing sector.
