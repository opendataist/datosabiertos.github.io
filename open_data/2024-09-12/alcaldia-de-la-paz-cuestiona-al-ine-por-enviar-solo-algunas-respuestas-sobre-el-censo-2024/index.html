Alcaldía de La Paz cuestiona al INE por enviar sólo algunas respuestas sobre el Censo 2024

Alcaldía de La Paz cuestiona al INE por enviar sólo algunas respuestas sobre el Censo 2024

The La Paz Mayor’s Office criticizes the national statistics institute for providing incomplete and non-technical responses to inquiries about the 2024 census. This withholding of precise data, such as error margins, highlights significant transparency gaps in official record-keeping processes. This situation is crucial for open data advocates, as it demonstrates how institutional opacity hinders public accountability and the verification of statistical accuracy. Without granular, accessible datasets, citizens cannot effectively audit government claims or ensure the integrity of national records. Ultimately, the reliance on delayed final data to justify withholding interim specifics undermines the principle of proactive transparency. It emphasizes the urgent need for open data frameworks that mandate timely, detailed disclosures to empower democratic oversight and public trust.
