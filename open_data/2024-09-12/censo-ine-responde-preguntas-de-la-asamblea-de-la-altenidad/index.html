Censo: INE responde preguntas de la Asamblea de la Alteñidad

Censo: INE responde preguntas de la Asamblea de la Alteñidad

The National Institute of Statistics has formally responded to technical inquiries from local governments concerning the 2024 Population and Housing Census. This action reflects a strong institutional commitment to transparency, ensuring that all official data is communicated to public authorities with rigorous precision and clarity. By inviting subnational entities to submit additional questions, the INE fosters an environment of collaborative scrutiny. Officials are also encouraged to participate in technical review meetings, which serve as platforms for the detailed verification of preliminary results, thereby reinforcing trust in the statistical methodology employed for the census. This process is essential for open data initiatives, as it exemplifies proactive accountability in government statistics. It underscores the importance of accessible, technically sound information exchange between national statistical agencies and local administrations, ensuring that census data is not only collected but also understood and validated through transparent institutional dialogue.
