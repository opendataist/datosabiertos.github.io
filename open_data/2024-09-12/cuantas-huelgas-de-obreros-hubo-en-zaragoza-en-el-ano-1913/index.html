¿Cuántas huelgas de obreros hubo en Zaragoza en el año 1913?

¿Cuántas huelgas de obreros hubo en Zaragoza en el año 1913?

The Aragón Institute of Statistics has recently digitized and made available historical labor data dating back to 1913, offering a transparent glimpse into early 20th-century industrial relations. This release underscores how accessible government records can preserve and convey socio-economic narratives without barriers. The documents reveal significant disparities in worker mobilization across regions, with Barcelona experiencing intense unrest while other provinces saw minimal activity. This variation in data points illustrates the diverse historical experiences within a single administrative framework, providing valuable context for understanding regional differences. This initiative is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it demonstrates the value of publishing historical public records. By making archival statistics freely available online, institutions empower citizens and researchers to explore historical truths, fostering transparency and encouraging the use of public data for educational and analytical purposes.
