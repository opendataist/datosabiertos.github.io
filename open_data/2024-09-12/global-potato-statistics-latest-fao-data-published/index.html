Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production statistics highlight China and India as the dominant producers, collectively accounting for a massive share of the worldwide total. These figures demonstrate the crop’s critical role in global food security and agricultural economics, particularly in regions where it serves as a staple food source. Understanding these production volumes is essential for analyzing supply chains, market dynamics, and trade flows on an international scale. The data reveals a widespread distribution of cultivation, with significant production occurring across diverse geographic regions, including Eastern Europe, North America, and various parts of Africa and Asia. This broad reach indicates that potato farming is not limited to traditional temperate zones but is adaptable to varied climates and agricultural systems. Such diversity underscores the crop’s resilience and importance for livelihoods in many developing nations, affecting local economies and employment. This information is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it showcases how international organizations like the FAO aggregate and disseminate vast agricultural datasets through accessible platforms like FAOSTAT. By making this detailed information publicly available, the database empowers researchers, policymakers, and analysts to conduct independent investigations into food security trends. The availability of such comprehensive, standardized global datasets facilitates better decision-making and promotes transparency in the global agricultural sector.
