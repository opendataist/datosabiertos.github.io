This survey addresses critical gaps in governance statistics by providing a comprehensive view of household crime in South Africa. It highlights significant increases in burglaries and assaults, revealing that reporting rates to the police vary significantly by crime type. The findings underscore the disparity between official police data and actual household experiences. By capturing unreported incidents, the survey offers a more accurate picture of public safety challenges, including peak crime periods and victim-perpetrator interactions. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it promotes transparency in governance metrics. It enables researchers and policymakers to analyze security trends more holistically, ensuring that statistical frameworks better reflect the lived realities of citizens rather than relying solely on institutional records.
