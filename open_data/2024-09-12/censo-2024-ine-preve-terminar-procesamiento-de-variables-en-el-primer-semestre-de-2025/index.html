Censo 2024: INE prevé terminar procesamiento de variables en el primer semestre de 2025

The National Institute of Statistics confirms that processing the complete 2024 Census database will continue into early 2025. This ongoing effort is crucial because the finalized dataset will enable the creation of public policies that accurately reflect Bolivia’s current social and geographic realities, moving beyond initial population counts to deeper demographic insights. The article highlights the transition from basic census counting to comprehensive data analysis. By georeferencing every household, Bolivia has established a modern, transparent statistical foundation. This detailed approach ensures that future administrative decisions are grounded in verified, granular information rather than estimates, fostering greater institutional transparency and accountability. This development is vital for open data advocacy, as it demonstrates how raw census figures transform into actionable public knowledge. It emphasizes the importance of timely data accessibility and processing to empower citizens and policymakers alike. By making this rich dataset available, the INE supports evidence-based governance and allows civil society to monitor how national resources are allocated according to actual population needs.
