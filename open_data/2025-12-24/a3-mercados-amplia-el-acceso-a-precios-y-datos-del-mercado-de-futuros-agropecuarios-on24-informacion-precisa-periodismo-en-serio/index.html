A3 Mercados amplía el acceso a precios y datos del mercado de futuros agropecuarios | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

A3 Mercados amplía el acceso a precios y datos del mercado de futuros agropecuarios | ON24 | Información Precisa. Periodismo en serio

A3 Mercados has modernized its platform, centralizing access to historical and real-time agricultural data. This migration to a new technological infrastructure unifies and brings transparency to information in the Argentine capital markets. The free, unrestricted, and exportable availability of this data facilitates in-depth analysis for various stakeholders, including producers and analysts. By enhancing the user experience, it strengthens evidence-based decision-making using up-to-date information, which is crucial in a vital economic sector. This advancement is significant for the open data community, as it demonstrates how technological transparency fosters innovation and equitable access to information. By aligning with international standards, it consolidates a more robust infrastructure that promotes efficiency and sustainable development of the financial market.
