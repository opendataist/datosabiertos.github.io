Economía de EEUU creció un 1,1 % en el tercer trimestre

The U.S. economy demonstrated resilience by expanding in the third quarter, surpassing analyst expectations and recording its highest annual growth rate since late 2023. This performance was primarily driven by robust consumer spending, while public expenditure also increased significantly, particularly in defense investments aligned with current administrative policies. Trade dynamics revealed a notable divergence, with exports rising sharply and imports contracting. This shift highlights the immediate economic impact of new reciprocal tariffs announced by the administration. Political leaders have explicitly credited these trade measures for the strong economic indicators, although legal challenges regarding their constitutionality are currently under judicial review. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical importance of timely and reliable statistical dissemination for public transparency. The recent federal government shutdown delayed key macroeconomic publications, demonstrating how administrative disruptions can create significant gaps in public data access. Understanding these delays helps analysts assess the integrity and continuity of open economic datasets, emphasizing the need for robust protocols to ensure data availability even during political uncertainty.
