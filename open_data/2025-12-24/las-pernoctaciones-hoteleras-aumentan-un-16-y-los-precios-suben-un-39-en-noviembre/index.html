Las pernoctaciones hoteleras aumentan un 1,6% y los precios suben un 3,9% en noviembre

Las pernoctaciones hoteleras aumentan un 1,6% y los precios suben un 3,9% en noviembre

Hotel stays in Spain showed steady growth in November, driven primarily by an increase in international visitors and rising prices. While domestic travel experienced a slight decline, foreign tourism contributed significantly to overall occupancy and revenue, indicating a strong reliance on overseas markets to sustain the sector's performance during this period. Revenue metrics improved as higher occupancy rates combined with increased room prices led to greater profitability for hotels. This dual effect suggests that the industry is successfully navigating price adjustments while maintaining strong demand, particularly in key tourist destinations like the Canary Islands and Andalusia, which consistently attract the majority of international travelers. This data is crucial for open_data initiatives because it highlights how transparent, standardized statistical indicators can reveal nuanced shifts in tourism economics. By making such granular details on occupancy, pricing, and regional distribution publicly accessible, researchers and policymakers can better analyze market trends and support evidence-based decisions regarding sustainable tourism development.
