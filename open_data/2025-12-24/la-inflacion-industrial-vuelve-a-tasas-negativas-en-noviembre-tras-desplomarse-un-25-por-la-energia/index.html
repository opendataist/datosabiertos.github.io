La inflación industrial vuelve a tasas negativas en noviembre tras desplomarse un 2,5% por la energía

La inflación industrial vuelve a tasas negativas en noviembre tras desplomarse un 2,5% por la energía

Industrial prices fell significantly in November, marking a substantial annual decline driven by sharply reduced energy costs. This reversal ends a brief period of inflationary increases, signaling a return to deflationary pressures in the industrial sector as production and distribution expenses for electricity and gas decreased substantially. Excluding energy, industrial prices remained largely stable, with only a slight increase, still lagging behind the general inflation index. This divergence highlights how energy market dynamics currently dominate industrial pricing trends, overshadowing other industrial segments that showed minimal growth or stagnation compared to the broader economy. The trend was widespread, with deflation occurring in nearly all regions except Cantabria. These widespread decreases demonstrate a uniform national shift in industrial cost structures.
