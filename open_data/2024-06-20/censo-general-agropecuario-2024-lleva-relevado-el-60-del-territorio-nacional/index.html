Censo General Agropecuario 2024 lleva relevado el 60% del territorio nacional

The agricultural census in Uruguay is a critical initiative to strengthen institutional capacity and improve the quality of public policy decisions. By gathering comprehensive data on land use and production, the government aims to create a robust foundation for planning and decision-making processes. This effort relies heavily on the active collaboration of producers to minimize error margins and ensure the survey's statistical validity. This data collection serves as a vital resource for both public planning and the private sector. It allows for the characterization of agricultural holdings, helping stakeholders understand ownership structures and labor dynamics. Such detailed insights enable businesses to better target services and support, fostering a more efficient and responsive agricultural market ecosystem. This article is relevant to open_data because it highlights the importance of collecting high-quality, granular data from primary sources to drive evidence-based governance. The transparency of the process, including accessible channels for self-reporting and clear public communication, demonstrates best practices in data acquisition. Furthermore, the emphasis on mandatory participation ensures a complete dataset, which is essential for creating accurate open datasets that can be utilized for broader societal and economic analysis.
