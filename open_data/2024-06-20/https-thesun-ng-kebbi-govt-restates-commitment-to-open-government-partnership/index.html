Kebbi State reaffirms its commitment to the Open Government Partnership, aiming to deepen democratic participation by facilitating direct citizen consultation on budget and policy matters. By organizing town hall meetings ahead of budget preparations, the government seeks to ensure transparency and inclusivity, allowing residents to actively shape public spending decisions. This approach reflects a broader shift toward accountable governance, where open dialogue serves as a mechanism for building trust between state institutions and the populace. The State Action Plan was co-created through robust engagement with civil society, ensuring that diverse stakeholders helped define its objectives. This collaborative foundation encourages citizens and non-state actors to monitor government implementation, holding officials accountable for delivering on specific promises. By fostering shared ownership of the plan, the initiative empowers communities to demand consistency and integrity in how policies are executed, reinforcing the principle that open governance requires active civic oversight. This local effort aligns with national trends, as more Nigerian states adopt OGP principles to bridge the gap between leaders and citizens. Kebbi’s ambition to eventually join the OGP at a global level highlights the potential for sub-national entities to drive systemic reform. These practices are relevant to open data as they demonstrate how transparent information sharing and multi-stakeholder collaboration can enhance public sector accountability and improve service delivery outcomes.
