Cuánto costó criar a un hijo en mayo, según el INDEC

Cuánto costó criar a un hijo en mayo, según el INDEC

The introduction of the Childcare Basket by INDEC represents a significant advancement in open data transparency, as it quantifies the economic value of unpaid care work—a sector often invisible in traditional statistical measures. This initiative highlights that raising children imposes substantial financial burdens on families, with costs escalating significantly as children age and encompassing both direct expenses for goods and the monetized value of caregiving hours. By isolating care costs from basic living expenses, the report reveals a stark economic reality: childcare expenses consume a disproportionate share of household income, particularly for older children. The data underscore that these costs rise sharply beyond infancy, demonstrating that the financial pressure on families intensifies throughout childhood rather than stabilizing, thereby challenging assumptions about the decreasing expense of raising older children. This statistical innovation is crucial for promoting gender equity, as it exposes the disproportionate reliance on female labor within the care economy. By assigning a monetary value to care hours, the dataset provides empirical evidence to support policy interventions aimed at reducing gender gaps and recognizing the social and economic contributions of caregiving. Ultimately, this open data source empowers policymakers and society to address the systemic undervaluation of care work, fostering more equitable social and economic planning.
