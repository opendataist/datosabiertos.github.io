¿Puedo vivir en un sitio y estar empadronado en otro? Esto es lo que dice el BOE

¿Puedo vivir en un sitio y estar empadronado en otro? Esto es lo que dice el BOE

Incorrect municipal registration violates Spanish law, carrying penalties ranging from fines to imprisonment for fraudulent intent. This legal requirement ensures accurate demographic data collection by local authorities. The obligation to register where one habitually resides prevents administrative fraud and ensures that residents can access essential services, such as healthcare and voting, without geographical barriers. This is relevant to open data because accurate civic registers form the foundational layer for public transparency. Reliable input data ensures that aggregated statistical outputs used in urban planning and policy-making reflect reality, thereby maintaining trust in open government information systems.
