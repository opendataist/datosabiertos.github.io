Abre convocatoria de becas para universitarios y alumnos con discapacidad auditiva

A public scholarship and credit program in Nogales invites students to register for financial aid until mid-February. This initiative specifically targets university students and young people with hearing disabilities enrolled in various public institutions in the region. Successful applicants will receive financial support for the current semester, with selection results published in early March through the official portal. Participation requires completing detailed student information and uploading the necessary documentation directly via the online platform. This article highlights the practical application of open data by relying on centralized, transparent digital platforms for the distribution of public services. It demonstrates how accessible online portals ensure equitable access to government resources, enabling diverse student populations to register efficiently and track outcomes through public digital infrastructure.
