Uruguay's inflation reaches 1.1% in January

Uruguay’s inflation eased slightly, remaining within the government’s target range. This stabilizes the macroeconomic environment, suggesting effective policy coordination and predictable pricing for essential goods. The report highlights nuanced shifts in specific sectors, with food prices showing mixed trends. While some staples rose, others fell, reflecting complex supply dynamics. These variations indicate that cost-of-living pressures are not uniform across all consumer categories.
