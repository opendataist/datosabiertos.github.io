China registra un crecimiento del 6,3% en viajes transfronterizos durante vacaciones de Fiesta de la Primavera - Confirmado

China’s Spring Festival holiday witnessed significant growth in cross-border travel, with both international tourists and mainland residents recording notable increases compared to the previous year. This surge underscores a robust recovery in global mobility and international tourism flows following recent disruptions. Such data is relevant to open data initiatives because it provides transparent, official statistics that enable researchers to analyze travel trends. Access to these metrics supports evidence-based decision-making for tourism policy, infrastructure planning, and economic impact assessments on a global scale. Ultimately, the release of these figures demonstrates how government transparency fosters public trust and facilitates academic research. By making mobility data publicly available, policymakers can better understand recovery patterns and address future challenges in international travel management effectively.
