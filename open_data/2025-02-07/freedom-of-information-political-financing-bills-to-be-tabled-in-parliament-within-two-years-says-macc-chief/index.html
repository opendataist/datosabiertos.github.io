Freedom of information, political financing bills to be tabled in Parliament within two years, says MACC chief

Malaysia plans to introduce key legislation within two years, including the Freedom of Information, Political Financing, and Whistleblower Protection bills. These measures aim to strengthen transparency and governance by addressing systemic risks of corruption and ensuring accountability in public institutions. The Freedom of Information Act will empower citizens to access government records, fundamentally shifting administrative culture toward openness. Simultaneously, regulating political financing seeks to prevent undue influence, while enhanced whistleblower protections encourage safe reporting of misconduct. Together, these reforms establish a robust framework for ethical public service. This development is vital for open data advocates, as it promises legal pathways to access non-sensitive government information. By mandating transparency and protecting those who expose wrongdoing, Malaysia is laying the groundwork for a more accountable society where data availability supports public oversight and trust in democratic processes.
