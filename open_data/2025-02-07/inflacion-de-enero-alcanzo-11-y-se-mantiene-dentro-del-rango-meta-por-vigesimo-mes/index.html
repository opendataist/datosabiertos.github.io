Inflación de enero alcanzó 1,1% y se mantiene dentro del rango meta por vigésimo mes

Inflación de enero alcanzó 1,1% y se mantiene dentro del rango meta por vigésimo mes

The article reports on Uruguay’s consumer price index, highlighting a January inflation rate that keeps the annual variation within the Central Bank’s target range for the twentieth consecutive month. This stability indicates that monetary policy remains effective in controlling price pressures despite specific sectoral increases. Significant price hikes were driven by essential costs, including food, energy tariffs, and transportation, particularly affecting utilities and fuel. While certain agricultural products saw sharp declines, the overall trend reflects upward pressure from service adjustments and global commodity effects, demonstrating the complex interplay of domestic policy and external markets on household budgets. This information is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how publicly available statistical indices allow citizens, researchers, and developers to monitor economic health and verify institutional claims. Transparent access to such granular sectoral data fosters accountability, enables evidence-based civic analysis, and supports the creation of tools that help the public navigate economic realities in real time.
