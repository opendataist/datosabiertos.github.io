Open Data
ES EN

Queen Letizia inaugurated Spain’s first open data initiative for cancer, emphasizing how transparent health information strengthens prevention strategies and treatment equity. By making critical statistics publicly accessible, this platform empowers society to understand risks and adopt healthier habits, directly linking data availability to improved public health outcomes. The event highlighted the urgent need for effective prevention, particularly regarding tobacco and vaping, to reduce cancer incidence. Letizia stressed that personal responsibility and societal commitment are essential for overcoming the disease, which affects a significant portion of the population. This initiative is vital for open data as it demonstrates how democratizing health statistics can drive policy improvements and social accountability. Transforming complex medical data into an open resource fosters trust and enables researchers, patients, and policymakers to collaborate more effectively in the fight against cancer.
