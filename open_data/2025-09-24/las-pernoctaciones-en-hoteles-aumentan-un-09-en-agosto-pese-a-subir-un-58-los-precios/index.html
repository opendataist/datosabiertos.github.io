Las pernoctaciones en hoteles aumentan un 0,9% en agosto pese a subir un 5,8% los precios

Las pernoctaciones en hoteles aumentan un 0,9% en agosto pese a subir un 5,8% los precios

The article reports an increase in hotel occupancy in Spain during August, driven primarily by international tourists despite a significant rise in prices. This trend highlights a sector recovering through higher volumes from abroad rather than domestic demand, indicating a strong reliance on foreign markets to sustain growth metrics. Financial performance also improved, with revenue per available room (RevPAR) and average daily rates (ADR) increasing across all hotel categories. The data reveals that luxury segments and specific destinations such as Marbella achieved the highest earnings, suggesting a bifurcation in which high-end markets command premium pricing while overall occupancy remains steady at previous levels. This data is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the importance of accessing granular, official statistical datasets to analyze complex economic trends. By making such detailed metrics on occupancy, pricing, and tourist origins publicly available, institutions enable researchers and businesses to verify market health, understand regional disparities, and make informed decisions based on transparent, verified information rather than estimates.
