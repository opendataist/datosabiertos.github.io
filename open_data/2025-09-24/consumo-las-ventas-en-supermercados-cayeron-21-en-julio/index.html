Argentine retail sales present conflicting signals, with modest year-on-year growth in supermarkets sharply contrasting with significant declines in the wholesale sector. This divergence highlights uneven consumer behavior and structural challenges within the distribution chain during 2025. These metrics reflect shifting economic dynamics and varying purchasing power across different retail segments. This report is relevant to open data as it exemplifies how national statistical institutes generate and disseminate official economic indicators.
