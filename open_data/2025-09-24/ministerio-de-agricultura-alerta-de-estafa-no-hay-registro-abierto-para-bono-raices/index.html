Ministerio de Agricultura alerta de estafa: No hay registro abierto para Bono Raíces

Ministerio de Agricultura alerta de estafa: No hay registro abierto para Bono Raíces

The Ministry of Agriculture clarifies that the "Bono Raíces" is not an open call for applications. Eligibility is strictly limited to small producers already registered in official government databases, such as the National Agricultural Registry. Consequently, individuals outside these specific lists cannot enroll, emphasizing that this is a targeted distribution rather than a public subscription process. The state has already distributed the majority of these one-time payments, with remaining allocations scheduled for existing beneficiaries. This mechanism aims to support farmers in poverty who meet strict criteria, excluding those who receive other specific monetary transfers or hold certain employment statuses. The focus is on verifying pre-existing data rather than collecting new registrations, ensuring funds reach the intended demographic efficiently. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical role of verified, pre-existing administrative registries in public policy implementation. It demonstrates how government agencies rely on closed, internal databases to target social benefits, raising questions about data accessibility and transparency. The situation underscores the tension between using official data for efficient welfare delivery and the public's need for clear, accessible information regarding eligibility and inclusion criteria.
