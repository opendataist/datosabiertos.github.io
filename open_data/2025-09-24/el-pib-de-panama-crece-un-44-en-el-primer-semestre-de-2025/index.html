El PIB de Panamá crece un 4,4 % en el primer semestre de 2025

Panama’s economy expanded significantly in the first half of the year, driven primarily by a surge in the logistics and transport sectors, anchored by substantial revenue growth at the Panama Canal. This robust performance in international trade and infrastructure underscores how strategic geographic assets remain central to the nation’s economic vitality, despite fluctuations in other areas. The report highlights the critical role of transparent economic data in monitoring national performance and sectoral contributions. By pinpointing specific industry shifts, it demonstrates how open access to detailed statistical records enables stakeholders to understand underlying economic drivers and assess the real impact of major events on GDP trends. This data is relevant to open data initiatives, as it illustrates the value of accessible, granular public statistics for fostering accountability and informed decision-making. When governments publish comprehensive economic indicators, they empower researchers and citizens to analyze structural health, ensuring that development policies are grounded in evidence rather than aggregate figures alone.
