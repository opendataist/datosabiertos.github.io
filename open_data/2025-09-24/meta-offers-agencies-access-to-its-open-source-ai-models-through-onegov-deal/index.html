Meta offers agencies access to its open source AI models through OneGov deal

Meta offers agencies access to its open source AI models through OneGov deal

Meta’s open-source Llama models are now available for federal agencies via the GSA’s OneGov initiative. This partnership allows government entities to access freely usable AI tools, enabling the development of applications at significantly reduced costs while ensuring compliance with federal requirements. By aggregating demand, the government leverages its purchasing power to secure efficient technology solutions. This approach supports broader digital transformation goals, allowing agencies to serve citizens better through innovative and cost-effective public services driven by accessible artificial intelligence. This development is crucial for open data advocates, as it promotes the adoption of open-source technologies in the public sector. It encourages transparency and reduces vendor lock-in, ensuring that government AI infrastructure remains adaptable, auditable, and aligned with principles of open access and shared knowledge.
