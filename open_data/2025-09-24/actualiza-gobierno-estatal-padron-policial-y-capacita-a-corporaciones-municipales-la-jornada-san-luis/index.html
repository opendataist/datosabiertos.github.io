Actualiza gobierno estatal padrón policial y capacita a corporaciones municipales - La Jornada San Luis

The SECESP updated the municipal police registry, ensuring data accuracy and professional training. This initiative strengthens law enforcement standards and intergovernmental collaboration, emphasizing reliable, certified personnel. Such systematic data verification and standardized reporting are crucial for open data initiatives. Ultimately, professionalizing police forces through verified records and shared knowledge improves public safety. This approach demonstrates how structured, accessible data underpins effective, accountable governance and community service delivery.
