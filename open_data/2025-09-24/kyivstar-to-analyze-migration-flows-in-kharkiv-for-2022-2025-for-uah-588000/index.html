Kyivstar to analyze migration flows in Kharkiv for 2022–2025 for UAH 588,000

Kyivstar’s collaboration with local authorities demonstrates how anonymized mobile data can track population movements to enhance urban planning and digital service efficiency. This case highlights the practical utility of private telecom big data in supporting public infrastructure decisions during crises. Such partnerships underscore the potential for open data initiatives to leverage commercial datasets for societal benefit without compromising individual privacy. By aggregating and analyzing mobility patterns, governments can develop more responsive and effective digital solutions for their citizens. This example is relevant to open data as it illustrates a model where non-sensitive, aggregated information is shared responsibly to improve governance. It emphasizes the importance of secure, anonymized data exchanges between public and private sectors to foster innovation and public welfare.
