En agosto la inflación se mantuvo al 1,9% en la Argentina y acumula 19,5% en lo que va del 2025

En agosto la inflación se mantuvo al 1,9% en la Argentina y acumula 19,5% en lo que va del 2025

The August consumer price index reflects a continued downward trend in annual inflation, signaling a stabilization process after months of high volatility. This deceleration is crucial for understanding economic dynamics, as it influences policy decisions and market expectations. The data highlights how regulated prices and the transport sector drive recent increases, while other areas show deflation or stagnation. Regional disparities reveal that areas such as Cuyo and Patagonia experience higher inflation than the national average, primarily due to food costs, whereas Greater Buenos Aires is more affected by transport prices. These geographic variations indicate that economic pressures are not uniform, requiring tailored analytical approaches to accurately address regional inflation drivers. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the necessity of granular, publicly accessible statistics for comprehensive economic analysis. By breaking down indices by category, region, and time, open data enables researchers and citizens to identify specific trends, hold institutions accountable, and develop more precise solutions for inflation control and economic planning.
