La IA puede reducir un 28% los ingresos por derechos de autor musicales

La IA puede reducir un 28% los ingresos por derechos de autor musicales

The integration of artificial intelligence into music creation threatens to cause a significant reduction in royalty income, highlighting the vulnerability of the intellectual property model in the face of new technologies. This trend underscores the urgency of adapting existing legal frameworks to protect human creative labor against efficient yet impersonal automation. Professionals exhibit an ambivalent attitude: they recognize the utility of these tools for reducing costs and optimizing processes, but fear being displaced by algorithms. This duality reflects a sector open to innovation, yet deeply concerned about the erosion of their traditional sources of livelihood and the potential loss of relevance in the market. This analysis is crucial for the open data movement because it highlights the need for transparency in the data used for and generated by AI. Without access to accurate information on how these systems are trained and deployed, it is impossible to design fair regulations that safeguard creators' rights against technology corporations.
