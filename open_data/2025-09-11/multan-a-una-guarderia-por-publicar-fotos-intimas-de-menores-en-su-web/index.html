Multan a una guardería por publicar fotos íntimas de menores en su web

Multan a una guardería por publicar fotos íntimas de menores en su web

This case highlights critical privacy risks in educational settings, demonstrating how coercive practices undermine genuine consent. The authority ruled that parents cannot be compelled to waive their children’s privacy rights in order to secure enrollment, establishing that such consent is neither voluntary nor valid when contingent upon access rights. Publicly sharing intimate moments involving minors on institutional platforms violates their best interests, regardless of any promotional intent. This ruling reinforces the principle that the child’s best interests take precedence over administrative convenience, mandating the immediate removal of sensitive content to protect vulnerable individuals from potential exploitation. For the open data community, this incident illustrates that transparency must never come at the expense of individual dignity or security. It serves as a crucial reminder that ethical data governance requires strict boundaries, ensuring that any shared information does not exploit power imbalances or expose private citizens to harm.
