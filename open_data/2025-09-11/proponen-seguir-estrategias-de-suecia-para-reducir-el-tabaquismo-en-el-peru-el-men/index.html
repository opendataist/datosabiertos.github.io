Proponen seguir estrategias de Suecia para reducir el tabaquismo en el Perú - El Men

Proponen seguir estrategias de Suecia para reducir el tabaquismo en el Perú - El Men

Sweden is on the verge of becoming the first smoke-free country in Europe, drastically reducing its daily smoking rate through a comprehensive strategy. This success is primarily attributed to harm-reduction policies that promote the use of less harmful alternatives, such as e-cigarettes and nicotine pouches, supported by differentiated taxation that incentivizes smokers to switch to these non-combustible products. In contrast, smoking continues to rise in Peru, exacerbated by the illegal market and weak enforcement, leaving adolescents exposed to toxic products. Experts warn that prohibitions alone are insufficient; robust education is needed to enable people to transition to lower-damage technologies, a policy decision that must be led through public-private and academic partnerships to be effective. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how comparative analysis of successful public policies can inspire local health reforms. Transparency in managing data on consumption and public health allows policymakers to identify gaps and adopt evidence-based strategies. Moreover, it facilitates inter-institutional collaboration and continuous monitoring, which are essential for implementing harm-reduction measures and educating the population with clear and measurable criteria.
