Frontera de Venezuela con el Caribe Neerlandés: Una herida abierta – Correo del Caroní

Frontera de Venezuela con el Caribe Neerlandés: Una herida abierta – Correo del Caroní

This investigation exposes the hidden dangers faced by Venezuelan migrants crossing into the Dutch Caribbean, highlighting a crisis often overshadowed by other migration routes. By documenting the risks of clandestine sea journeys and the human cost of disappearances, the report sheds light on vulnerable populations seeking refuge in island nations. A key outcome is the creation of an open database detailing missing persons and reported cases. This transparency allows families to track the status of their loved ones, ensuring that memories are preserved and demands for justice are supported through verifiable information rather than silence. This initiative is crucial for open data as it demonstrates how accessible datasets can empower communities and aid accountability in humanitarian crises. By making migration and disappearance records publicly available, it transforms personal tragedy into actionable information, fostering greater awareness and facilitating research on the complex dynamics of border security and integration.
