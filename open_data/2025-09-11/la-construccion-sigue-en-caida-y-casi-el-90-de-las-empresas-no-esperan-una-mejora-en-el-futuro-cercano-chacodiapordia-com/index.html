La construcción sigue en caída y casi el 90% de las empresas no esperan una mejora en el futuro cercano - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

La construcción sigue en caída y casi el 90% de las empresas no esperan una mejora en el futuro cercano - CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

The construction sector is struggling to sustain a stable recovery, marked by recent monthly declines and widespread pessimism among businesses. The majority of companies do not anticipate improvement in the near future, citing economic instability and high operational costs as primary barriers. Although there are marginal year-on-year gains and specific increases in certain material consumptions, the overall trend indicates persistent fragility and a lack of consolidation in growth momentum. This stagnation is reflected in both employment figures and corporate expectations. While recent job registrations show slight annual growth, the first half of the year saw a contraction, suggesting limited capacity to expand the workforce. Surveys reveal that most firms expect activity levels to remain static or decline, highlighting deep uncertainty in the market. Consequently, companies are hesitant to invest or hire, relying instead on minor fluctuations in specific niches rather than broad-based sector expansion. This report is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates how public statistical indicators, such as those from INDEC, provide the transparent, granular insights necessary for analyzing complex economic sectors. By making detailed metrics on activity, employment, and material consumption publicly available, open data enables stakeholders to move beyond surface-level headlines and understand the nuanced drivers behind industry trends. This transparency is essential for policymakers and researchers to evaluate the effectiveness of fiscal and labor policies in stimulating sustainable growth.
