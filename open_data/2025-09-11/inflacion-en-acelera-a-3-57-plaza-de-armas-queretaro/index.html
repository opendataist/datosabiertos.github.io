Inflation in Mexico shows a significant slowdown, reflecting stabilization in consumer prices and an improvement in purchasing power. The transparency of these data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it enables citizens and analysts to verify the true state of the economy. This availability of rigorous statistical information empowers informed decision-making.
