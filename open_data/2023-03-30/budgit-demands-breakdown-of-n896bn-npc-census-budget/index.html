BudgIT demands breakdown of N896bn NPC census budget

BudgIT has formally requested the National Population Commission to publish a detailed breakdown of the N896 billion budget allocated for the 2023 National Population Census. Emphasizing the necessity of transparency, the organization argues that citizens must know exactly how these funds are allocated across states and local governments, including amounts released, utilized, and returned. This demand stems from the principle that public spending requires clear justification, especially when resources are scarce and national debt is high. The initiative highlights the critical role of open data in fostering good governance and accountability. By invoking the Freedom of Information Act, BudgIT seeks to ensure that every naira is accounted for, preventing waste during a period of significant economic strain. The argument is that detailed financial visibility allows for better oversight, ensuring that massive expenditures serve developmental goals rather than frivolous activities, thereby strengthening the trust between the government and its citizens. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates a civil society effort to enforce fiscal transparency through data access. It demonstrates how citizens’ organizations use open data frameworks to demand accountability for public funds, turning budget figures into actionable information. The push for accessible, granular budget data empowers the public to monitor government performance, reinforcing the idea that open data is essential for effective civic engagement and resource optimization.
