Capacitan en Transparencia, Acceso a la Información Pública y Protección de Datos Personales | La Opción de Chihuahua

The Chihuahua state government launched a comprehensive transparency training program for public servants within the Executive branch. The primary goal is to cultivate an institutional culture of openness, ensuring that officials are ethically and legally equipped to handle public information. This initiative emphasizes that technical tools alone are insufficient; sustained educational efforts are required to foster genuine accountability and democratic governance among all staff members. By engaging all transparency units across state agencies, the program aims to professionalize how government data is managed and shared. It seeks to align internal practices with legal frameworks while strengthening the relationship between institutions and citizens. The collaborative effort involves key oversight bodies, ensuring that both the right to information for the public and the proper handling of data by officials are prioritized through structured learning. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical human element in data ecosystems. Sustainable open data initiatives depend on a workforce trained in transparency laws and data protection ethics. Without such cultural and professional development, even the best technological platforms fail to deliver accessible, trustworthy, and legally compliant public information.
