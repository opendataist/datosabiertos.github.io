Vietnam's inflation quickens to 4.18% in first quarter

Vietnam’s early 2023 inflation was primarily driven by rising costs in education, housing, and food. This surge significantly increased the overall cost of living for citizens, creating economic pressure despite a simultaneous decline in global fuel prices. Although underlying core inflation remained high, the overall trend showed signs of cooling in March. The moderation resulted from falling gasoline costs and stable food supplies, suggesting that the sharpest inflationary pressures may be easing for households. This data highlights the critical importance of open data for public accountability. Transparent release of detailed consumer price indices allows citizens to understand how specific sectors like education and housing impact their daily expenses. Open data enables accurate policy evaluation against official inflation targets.
