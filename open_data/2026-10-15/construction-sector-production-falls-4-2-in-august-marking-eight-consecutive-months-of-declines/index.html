Construction sector production falls 4.2% in August, marking eight consecutive months of declines

Construction sector production falls 4.2% in August, marking eight consecutive months of declines

The Spanish construction sector is experiencing sustained contraction, as evidenced by eight consecutive months of year-on-year declines driven by falls in building construction and civil engineering. This negative trend contrasts with a slight recovery in specialized construction and a brief monthly improvement after removing seasonal effects, suggesting a struggling market with small signs of temporary stabilization. The publication of these data through the new IPCO indicator is crucial for transparency and international comparability, as it aligns statistics with European standards. This methodological change enables more accurate measurement of real activity by isolating value added from price variations, providing a clearer view of the sector’s economic dynamism in a continental context. The relevance of this article for open data lies in its innovative methodological approach: by requesting only turnover figures and applying deflation, it simplifies the reporting burden for businesses without sacrificing rigor. This efficient model promotes the availability of standardized and comparable data, facilitating access to reliable and open information that supports analysis, research, and evidence-based decision-making within the construction sector.
