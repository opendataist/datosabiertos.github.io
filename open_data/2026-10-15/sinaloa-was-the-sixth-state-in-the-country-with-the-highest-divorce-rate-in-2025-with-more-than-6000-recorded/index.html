Sinaloa was the sixth state in the country with the highest divorce rate in 2025, with more than 6,000 recorded.

Sinaloa has solidified its position as a state with high levels of both marriage and divorce activity, standing out in the national ranking for its rate of legal unions and its position among the states with the highest number of marital dissolutions. This contrast reveals complex social dynamics that affect both the local and national population, reflecting changes in family behavior patterns. Most divorces are formalized through the no-fault procedure, indicating a trend toward simplifying legal procedures and reducing the need to justify specific grounds for separation. This prevalence suggests that the judicial system and civil regulations are adapting to faster, less contentious social demands, thereby facilitating access to family justice.
