Air tourism to Mexico records eight consecutive months of decline - Noticaribe

Air tourism to Mexico records eight consecutive months of decline - Noticaribe

The analysis reveals a paradox in Mexico’s international tourism: although air arrivals show a continuous downward trend throughout 2026, total revenue generated by these visitors continues to grow. This indicates that, although fewer tourists are arriving by air, the average spending per person has increased significantly, offsetting the reduction in passenger volume and demonstrating the sector’s economic resilience. It is worth noting the decline in the share of air tourism in total revenues, reaching levels not seen since 2020. This shift suggests that other modes of access or market segments may be gaining relative importance, or that the spending structure has changed. International competition and insufficient promotion are contextual factors influencing this dynamic, although they are not specifically quantified in the report. This issue is crucial for open data science, as it illustrates the need to cross-reference multiple official sources, such as those from INEGI, with independent academic studies to obtain a holistic view. Raw data must be interpreted carefully, avoiding simplistic conclusions about tourism decline when financial indicators show health. Transparency and access to this data enable civil society and businesses to make informed decisions about the sector’s sustainability.
