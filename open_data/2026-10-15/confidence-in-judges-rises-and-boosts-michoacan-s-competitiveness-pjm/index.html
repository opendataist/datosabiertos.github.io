Confidence in judges rises and boosts Michoacán's competitiveness: PJM

Confidence in judges rises and boosts Michoacán's competitiveness: PJM

The Judicial Power of Michoacán highlights the increase in citizen trust in its judges, a key indicator for strengthening the rule of law. This perceived improvement not only ensures greater protection of citizens' rights but also enhances legal certainty, a fundamental element for attracting investment and boosting regional economic competitiveness. The relevance of this article to open data lies in the fact that INEGI's measurements, such as the ENVEPS, constitute primary sources of transparent information on social perception. These statistics enable citizens and analysts to evaluate institutional performance using quantitative evidence, fostering accountability and public access to data that reflect the state's social reality. By making these indicators visible, active transparency of judicial institutions is promoted. The availability of trust metrics facilitates social monitoring of the efficiency and accessibility of justice, demonstrating how access to government data is crucial for building more informed, responsible, and participatory societies in relation to the functioning of the legal system.
