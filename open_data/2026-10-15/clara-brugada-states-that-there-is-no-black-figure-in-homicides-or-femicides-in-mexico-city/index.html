Clara Brugada states that there is no black figure in homicides or femicides in Mexico City

Clara Brugada states that there is no black figure in homicides or femicides in Mexico City

The head of government of Mexico City asserts that there is no “black figure” (unreported cases) in homicides and femicides, guaranteeing absolute consistency among the data from the Attorney General’s Office, the National Public Security System (SNSP), and the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). This statement aims to refute any claim that violence statistics are being concealed, dismissing as irresponsible any doubts about the transparency and verifiability of these figures. This stance is crucial for the open data field, as it validates the integration and cross-referencing of government public databases as a reliable source of information. By affirming the accuracy of these records, it fosters trust in the interoperability of open data, discourages misinformation, and sets a precedent regarding the importance of cross-validating official data to ensure transparency and public access to information.
