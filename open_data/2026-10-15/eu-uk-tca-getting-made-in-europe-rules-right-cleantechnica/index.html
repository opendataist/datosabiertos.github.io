EU-UK TCA: Getting Made in Europe Rules Right - CleanTechnica

EU-UK TCA: Getting Made in Europe Rules Right - CleanTechnica

The article refutes automotive industry claims that EU battery manufacturing capacity is insufficient for new origin rules, proving instead that production will significantly exceed UK export demand by 2027. This evidence undermines arguments for delaying implementation, highlighting that the industry’s objections are unsubstantiated by actual supply capabilities. Maintaining strict local content standards is crucial for preventing tariffs and ensuring tariff-free trade continues without disruption. Enforcing these rules is presented as a vital test of the EU’s commitment to its "Made in EU" agenda and industrial sovereignty. The author argues that honoring previously agreed timelines strengthens investor confidence and reinforces the credibility of European trade policy. Allowing further delays would weaken the legal framework and signal instability, potentially deterring necessary long-term investments in the region’s strategic sectors. This issue is highly relevant to open data because transparent, publicly available production metrics enable independent verification of corporate claims. By relying on accessible datasets regarding battery capacity and material supply, policymakers and civil society can hold industries accountable and debunk misinformation. Open data facilitates evidence-based governance, ensuring that trade regulations are grounded in reality rather than lobbying pressures, thereby promoting fair and transparent economic practices.
