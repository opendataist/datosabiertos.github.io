80% of Mexicans suffer mental health issues due to their work: What are the causes? - INEGI - Almomento | National and International News

80% of Mexicans suffer mental health issues due to their work: What are the causes? - INEGI - Almomento | National and International News

The study conducted by INEGI demonstrates that economic capacity is a critical determinant of happiness in Mexico, highlighting a significant gap between those facing poverty and those with financial stability. This direct relationship suggests that measures to improve emotional well-being must be intrinsically linked to the material security of families. Furthermore, the research highlights how inequality disproportionately affects marginalized groups based on their health, disability, or ethnic origin. These findings reveal that well-being is not uniform but rather reflects exclusionary social structures that require immediate attention to reduce existing emotional disparities within the population. This information is relevant to the open data movement because it transforms abstract indicators into tangible evidence regarding human rights and quality of life. By making these details public, it fosters transparency and enables civil society to demand evidence-based public policies, promoting more responsible governance focused on the comprehensive well-being of citizens.
