Usuarios de internet en México ascendieron a 100.2 millones en 2024

Usuarios de internet en México ascendieron a 100.2 millones en 2024

The article highlights a significant expansion of internet access in Mexico, reaching over eighty percent of the population. This growth indicates a broader societal shift toward digital connectivity, suggesting that an increasing majority of citizens now rely on online platforms for essential communication, education, and information. Consequently, digital inclusion has become a fundamental aspect of modern civic life, impacting how individuals engage with services and each other. Despite this overall progress, the data reveals persistent inequalities, particularly between urban and rural areas, as well as gaps between genders and age groups. These disparities suggest that while infrastructure is expanding, equitable access remains uneven. The higher engagement among younger demographics compared to older adults further underscores a generational divide in digital literacy and adoption, highlighting areas where targeted interventions are needed to ensure inclusive participation. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the critical role of government statistics in monitoring digital equity. By making such detailed metrics publicly available, institutions enable researchers and policymakers to identify vulnerabilities and measure the effectiveness of public policies. Transparent data empowers communities to demand accountability and guides the design of initiatives that bridge the digital divide, ensuring technology serves as a tool for social inclusion rather than exclusion.
