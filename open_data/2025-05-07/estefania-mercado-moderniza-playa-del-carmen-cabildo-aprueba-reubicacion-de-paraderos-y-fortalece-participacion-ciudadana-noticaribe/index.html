Estefanía Mercado moderniza Playa del Carmen; Cabildo aprueba reubicación de paraderos y fortalece participación ciudadana - Noticaribe

Estefanía Mercado moderniza Playa del Carmen; Cabildo aprueba reubicación de paraderos y fortalece participación ciudadana - Noticaribe

The City Council of Playa del Carmen approved reforms to modernize urban mobility and enhance citizen participation. These decisions aim to improve the safety and efficiency of public transport while aligning municipal governance with open government principles. By updating the framework for neighborhood organization, the local government ensures that community voices actively influence public decisions. This shift replaces outdated regulations with a more functional legal structure, fostering a transparent and responsive administrative model. This case is relevant to open data because transparent governance requires accessible information. Effective citizen participation depends on the availability of clear, reliable data regarding urban planning and transport logistics. Open data serves as the essential foundation for accountability and informed public engagement in municipal management.
