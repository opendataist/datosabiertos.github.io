Inflación fue del 0,32% en abril y de 5,36% en los últimos doce meses

The Spanish Statistical Institute released the consumer price indices for April 2025, revealing significant inflationary pressures across essential goods. Notable price increases were observed in fresh meats, dairy products, and seasonal vegetables, while certain fruits and transportation costs saw reductions. This data highlights the volatile nature of local food security and household expenses. These fluctuations underscore the critical importance of transparent, granular economic data. Open data initiatives enable researchers and citizens to trace specific inflation drivers beyond aggregate figures. By accessing detailed category breakdowns, stakeholders can better understand how macroeconomic trends impact daily life and purchasing power. Ultimately, this publication reinforces the value of official statistics as a public good. Ensuring that such datasets are freely accessible promotes informed public debate and policy analysis. It allows for a deeper examination of economic health, fostering accountability and empowering the community to respond effectively to changing market conditions.
