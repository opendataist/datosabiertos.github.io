Importación de acero bajo lupa, quitarán registro a 1,062 empresas por irregularidades

Importación de acero bajo lupa, quitarán registro a 1,062 empresas por irregularidades

The Mexican government is canceling the registrations of steel mills to eliminate irregularities in import records. This administrative action aims to prevent the use of fictitious entities, thereby strengthening trade integrity and ensuring fair competition for domestic producers against imported goods. By removing these inconsistent records, authorities intend to stop the triangulation of steel imports that conceals the true country of origin. This measure prevents the evasion of tariffs and compensatory quotas, correcting abuses that previously allowed imported steel to enter the market under unfair conditions compared to national production. This initiative is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the critical role of verifying and cleaning public registries for economic transparency. Ensuring the accuracy of business data allows for effective policy implementation, protects local industries from unfair practices, and builds trust in official statistical databases used for international trade analysis.
