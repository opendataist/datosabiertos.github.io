Acuerdan incluir entes para armonizar Ley de Transparencia | La Opción de Chihuahua

Acuerdan incluir entes para armonizar Ley de Transparencia | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua is launching a critical legislative harmonization process to align its local transparency and data protection laws with new national reforms. This effort responds to the organic expansion of federal institutions, requiring state entities to adapt their regulatory frameworks to ensure coherence with updated federal mandates on access to public information and the handling of personal data. The oversight commission has identified key institutions to lead this alignment, setting strict deadlines for proposals and final rulings. This structured approach ensures that the state’s legal updates are comprehensive and timely, covering both public bodies and the private sector to maintain consistent data protection standards across the jurisdiction. This initiative is vital for open data, as it modernizes the legal infrastructure governing information disclosure. By updating these laws, Chihuahua strengthens accountability mechanisms and clarifies the obligations of data holders, fostering a more transparent environment. This alignment facilitates better access to public information for citizens and organizations, which is essential for promoting civic engagement and sustainable open data practices within the state.
