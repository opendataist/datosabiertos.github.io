La Seguridad Social gana casi 231.000 afiliados y logra su segundo mejor abril de la historia

Spanish social security data reveal historic employment growth, characterized by record-high affiliation numbers and a significant shift toward stable, indefinite contracts. This trend underscores the effectiveness of recent labor reforms in reducing precariousness and improving job quality, marking a structural change from decades of temporary instability. The surge is driven by strong gains across major sectors such as hospitality and administration, with notable increases in both male and female participation. This broad-based expansion indicates that the labor market recovery is not limited to specific industries but represents a widespread economic improvement, reinforcing the sustainability of current employment levels. This article is relevant to open data as it highlights the value of transparent, granular official statistics in evaluating public policy. By making detailed breakdowns of employment metrics accessible, such data enables citizens and researchers to verify government claims, fostering accountability and informed debate regarding social welfare and economic health.
