INEGI inicia censos económicos 2024 en todo México

The National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI) has launched the 2024 Economic Census, a quinquennial exercise essential for mapping Mexico’s productive structure. Through visits to millions of establishments, detailed information on the country’s commercial, labor, and technological activities will be collected, ensuring that statistics accurately reflect the current economic reality. The relevance of this census for open data lies in the generation of highly detailed open data at both geographic and sectoral levels. By democratizing access to this information, it empowers citizens, academia, civil society organizations, and the private sector. This enables informed decision-making, evidence-based evaluation of public policies, and historical comparative analyses that reveal market trends and changes. Ultimately, the census transforms citizen participation into valuable public knowledge. By ensuring transparency and the strictly statistical use of data, it strengthens accountability and provides social actors with the necessary tools to research, plan, and project the nation’s economic future with greater precision and equity.
