Stellantis preps sale of anonymised driver data

Stellantis seeks customer consent to sell anonymized vehicle data, enabling city planners to optimize charging infrastructure and maintenance. This initiative highlights the growing commercialization of personal mobility metrics, raising critical questions about user consent and data ownership in the open data ecosystem. The strategy mirrors similar controversial proposals in healthcare, underscoring the tension between societal benefits and privacy concerns. By merging traffic patterns with economic indicators, the company aims to unlock value for research and government, demonstrating how aggregate data can drive urban efficiency. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates the evolving landscape where private entities monetize public-interest information. It challenges the community to establish robust ethical frameworks and transparency standards for handling sensitive behavioral data shared by everyday citizens.
