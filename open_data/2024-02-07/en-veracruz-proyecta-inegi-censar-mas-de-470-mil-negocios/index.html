En Veracruz, proyecta INEGI censar más de 470 mil negocios

The INEGI census will gather comprehensive business data on finances and employment throughout Veracruz, updating key economic indicators for more than 400,000 establishments. This initiative monitors workforce demographics and production values to reflect current market conditions. In line with open data principles, these findings will be published in early 2025, ensuring transparent access to essential socioeconomic statistics. By releasing timely and definitive results, the government promotes data-driven decision-making and public accountability in economic planning.
