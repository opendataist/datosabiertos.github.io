The Universidad Autónoma de Tamaulipas launched a webinar series to foster a robust culture of digital defense within its academic community. By providing essential tools and knowledge, the initiative aims to empower students and staff to effectively navigate contemporary cyber threats. This strategic effort strengthens organizational capabilities and integrates cybersecurity into the institution’s broader management framework, ensuring a safer digital environment for all members. Central to this program is the recognition of information as a vital asset requiring rigorous protection. The series emphasizes privacy, personal data security, and compliance with relevant legislation. Through monthly sessions covering topics such as ransomware and identity theft, the university addresses critical risks associated with online learning and work, thereby enhancing the overall resilience of its digital infrastructure and human capital against evolving cyber dangers. This article is relevant to open data because it underscores the foundational necessity of data security and privacy compliance. For open data initiatives to be sustainable and ethical, institutions must first establish strong governance and protection protocols. This event highlights the institutional commitment to safeguarding sensitive information, a prerequisite for responsibly sharing public data while maintaining trust and adhering to legal standards, thus creating a secure ecosystem for potential open data exchanges.
