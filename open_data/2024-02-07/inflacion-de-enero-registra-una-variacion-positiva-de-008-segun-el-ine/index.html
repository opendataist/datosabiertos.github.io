Inflación de enero registra una variación positiva de 0,08%, según el INE

The January consumer price index showed a slight increase, driven by rising costs in education, health, and transportation. Prices rose notably in major cities, with paper products and mobile phones seeing higher costs, while staples like potatoes and eggs became cheaper. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides transparent, accessible metrics for tracking inflation and household spending. By making such granular economic indicators publicly available, researchers and citizens can better analyze market trends and financial pressures. Understanding these price variations allows for informed decision-making regarding budget allocation and policy adjustments. Open access to this data fosters accountability and enables the community to monitor economic health, ensuring that statistical information remains a tool for public empowerment rather than just administrative record-keeping.
