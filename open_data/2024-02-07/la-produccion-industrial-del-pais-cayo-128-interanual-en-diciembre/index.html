La producción industrial del país cayó 12,8% interanual en diciembre

The manufacturing sector suffered a sharp contraction, underscoring a recession that has been deepening since mid-2022. This broad-based decline affected nearly all industrial segments, with only a few minor subsectors posting growth, pointing to a systemic crisis rather than isolated, sector-specific problems. This economic downturn highlights the critical importance of open data for monitoring the nation’s economic health. When statistical agencies publish timely and highly disaggregated reports, they enable economists and policymakers to track the specific effects of external shocks—such as prolonged droughts and foreign exchange restrictions—on industrial activity. Access to such detailed indicators allows for a more nuanced understanding of how environmental and monetary policies interact to constrain production. Ultimately, this transparency is essential for identifying structural weaknesses in the economy and designing effective strategies to revitalize manufacturing and stabilize employment levels nationwide.
