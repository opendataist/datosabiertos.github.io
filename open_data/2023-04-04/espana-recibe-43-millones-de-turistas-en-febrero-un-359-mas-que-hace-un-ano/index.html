España recibe 4,3 millones de turistas en febrero, un 35,9% más que hace un año

Spain is experiencing a significant rebound in international tourism, reflecting a strong economic recovery driven by increases in both the number of visitors and their total spending. This growth suggests that the sector is consolidating its vital role in the national economy, attracting travelers primarily from the United Kingdom, France, and Germany, who also lead in revenue generated by vacation stays. Regional distribution of tourism shows that the Canary Islands, Catalonia, and Andalusia remain the preferred destinations, although with significant variations in spending. While the Canary Islands concentrate the largest share of tourism investment, other regions such as Madrid have recorded higher growth rates in spending, indicating a diversification of economic impact areas. This pattern reveals not only the recovery of demand but also changes in consumption habits across Spain. This information is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates the value of transparent public statistics for analyzing macroeconomic and sectoral trends. By making data disaggregated by origin, destination, and type of spending available, institutions enable researchers, businesses, and citizens to understand market dynamics, facilitate evidence-based decision-making, and promote accountability in the management of tourism-related resources.
