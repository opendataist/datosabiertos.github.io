Subtel orders blocking of nearly 2,000 pirate TV sites to protect privacy and personal data | Puranoticia.cl

Subtel orders blocking of nearly 2,000 pirate TV sites to protect privacy and personal data | Puranoticia.cl

The Undersecretariat of Telecommunications has implemented a strict protocol to block illegal streaming platforms, driven by serious cybersecurity risks. This measure responds to court orders mandating restrictions on access to sites such as MagisTV and FlujoTV, in coordination with the Alliance Against Digital Piracy. This approach aims to protect citizens’ privacy by preventing them from installing unofficial applications that expose their personal and banking data to cybercrime. The authority has notified internet service providers to carry out these blocks within a short timeframe, successfully disrupting a significant number of illegal domains. This cross-sectoral management demonstrates the effectiveness of this mechanism by progressively eliminating sources of pirated content. Telecom operators are implementing the restrictions technically, ensuring that the State fulfills judicial orders efficiently and in a coordinated manner across the entire national territory. This procedure is relevant to open data because it establishes a precedent of transparency and collaboration between the public and private sectors to combat digital illicit activities. Publishing the blocked sites and tracking the results foster accountability and trust in data protection policies. Moreover, it highlights the importance of having accessible data on digital infrastructure to design effective strategies that safeguard citizens’ cybersecurity against emerging threats.
