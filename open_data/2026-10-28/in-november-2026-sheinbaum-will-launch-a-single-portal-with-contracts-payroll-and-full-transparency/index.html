In November 2026, Sheinbaum will launch a single portal with contracts, payroll, and full transparency

President Claudia Sheinbaum announced the launch of a unified digital portal scheduled for November 2026, which will centralize data on federal personnel, contracts, and social programs. This initiative aims to consolidate government information in one place, facilitating public access to the State’s financial and operational details. The project aligns with the transparency decree to promote proactive data disclosure, responding to criticisms regarding institutional accountability. By integrating these sources, the goal is to strengthen social auditing and ensure that all government agencies comply with their obligations for financial clarity. This development is significant for the open data movement, as it materializes the open government initiative by providing structured and accessible information. By making contracts and payroll data available through a centralized digital system, it lays the groundwork for the reuse and citizen analysis of public data, fostering greater transparency and evidence-based democratic participation.
