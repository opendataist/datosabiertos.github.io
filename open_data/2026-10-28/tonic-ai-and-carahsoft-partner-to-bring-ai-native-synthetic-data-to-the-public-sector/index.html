Tonic.ai and Carahsoft Partner to Bring AI-Native Synthetic Data to the Public Sector

Tonic.ai and Carahsoft Partner to Bring AI-Native Synthetic Data to the Public Sector

Tonic.ai and Carahsoft have formed a strategic partnership to provide public sector organizations with immediate access to synthetic data for secure AI development. By leveraging Carahsoft’s established government contract vehicles, Tonic.ai’s platform becomes easily accessible to federal, state, and local agencies. This collaboration removes procurement barriers, allowing government teams to adopt advanced data solutions through existing compliance frameworks without navigating complex new purchasing processes. The core innovation lies in generating high-fidelity synthetic data that mirrors real-world datasets while eliminating privacy risks. Traditional development often stalls because production data contains sensitive information that cannot be shared, or because necessary test data does not yet exist. Tonic.ai’s technology addresses this by creating safe, realistic data subsets and redacting unstructured information, enabling engineers to build, test, and train AI models without exposing confidential details to potential breaches. This approach is critical for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how privacy and utility can coexist. Open data advocates often struggle to balance transparency with the need to protect individual privacy. By making sensitive data usable for public innovation without compromising security, this partnership enables government agencies to modernize their operations and foster AI-driven improvements while maintaining public trust. It represents a practical model for unlocking data value in highly regulated environments.
