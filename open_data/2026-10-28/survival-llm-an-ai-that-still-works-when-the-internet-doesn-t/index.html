Survival LLM: an AI that still works when the internet doesn't

Survival LLM: an AI that still works when the internet doesn't

Survival LLM is a free, open-source AI assistant designed to operate entirely offline on local hardware, ensuring user privacy by keeping all data and queries within the user’s machine. This self-contained approach allows individuals to access critical emergency guidance, medical references, and survival manuals without relying on external internet connectivity or cloud services. By running locally, the tool guarantees availability during power outages or network failures, making it a resilient resource for disaster preparedness where traditional digital aids might fail. The application functions by utilizing a curated library of publicly available and openly licensed texts, including Wikipedia, FEMA guidelines, and medical encyclopedias, while adapting its AI model complexity based on the user’s hardware specifications. This flexibility ensures that the tool remains accessible across a wide range of devices, from high-end Macs to modest Windows PCs, by selecting appropriate models that balance performance with available memory. The system provides transparent sourcing for every answer, allowing users to verify information directly within the local library, which mitigates the inherent risks of AI hallucinations, particularly in high-stakes emergency scenarios. This project is significant to the open_data movement as it demonstrates how open-source software and public domain content can be combined to create robust, decentralized tools for public good. It challenges the prevailing model of proprietary, cloud-dependent AI by proving that high-quality, actionable information can be made available independently of corporate infrastructure. By releasing its code under an MIT license and curating essential knowledge into a portable format, Survival LLM empowers individuals with transparency, control, and resilience, highlighting the practical value of open data in ensuring societal safety and autonomy.
