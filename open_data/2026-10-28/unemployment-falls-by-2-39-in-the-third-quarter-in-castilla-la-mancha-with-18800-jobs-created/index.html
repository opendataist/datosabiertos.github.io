Unemployment falls by 2.39% in the third quarter in Castilla-La Mancha, with 18,800 jobs created

Unemployment in Castilla-La Mancha continues its positive trend, with a sustained decline over two consecutive quarters that has brought the unemployment rate to its lowest level since 2023. This drop is accompanied by a significant increase in employment, reaching record figures in the third quarter, reflecting a tangible improvement in the regional labor market compared to the national situation, where unemployment showed a slight uptick. Job creation has been distributed mainly across the private and public sectors, with a predominance of permanent contracts and a strong concentration of full-time positions. Although the overall figures are encouraging, structural challenges persist, such as rising male and youth unemployment, as well as certain increases in specific sectors like construction, suggesting that the improvement is not uniform across all demographic or economic groups. This analysis is relevant to the open data community because it illustrates how microdata from the Active Population Survey (Encuesta de Población Activa) allow macroeconomic indicators to be disaggregated by region, gender, age, and contract type. The ability to cross these variables facilitates the detection of hidden inequalities in aggregated statistics and promotes transparency, enabling citizens and researchers to assess the real effectiveness of employment policies beyond national averages.
