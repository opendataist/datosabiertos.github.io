Rita Cetina scholarship registration opens for public school students

Rita Cetina scholarship registration opens for public school students

Registration for the Rita Cetina educational scholarships is now open, facilitating access to financial support for elementary and secondary school students in public institutions. This initiative aims to reduce academic costs by providing specific allocations for uniforms, school supplies, and general expenses, ensuring that families receive the necessary financial assistance. The relevance to open data lies in the transparent and digitized management of these processes. By centralizing information on open government platforms, it promotes accountability and public access to data on resource distribution. This enables citizens to verify the allocation of funds and actively participate in monitoring education policy, strengthening institutional trust through digital transparency.
