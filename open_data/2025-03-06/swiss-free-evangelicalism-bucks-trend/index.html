Swiss free evangelicalism bucks trend

Swiss free evangelicalism bucks trend

Swiss religious landscapes show stable free evangelicalism alongside collapsing mainline churches and rising secularism. This highlights how niche, non-state religious groups can withstand broader cultural shifts away from traditional institutional faith. For open data, it demonstrates the value of granular, longitudinal statistics in revealing nuanced societal trends beyond aggregate religious decline.
