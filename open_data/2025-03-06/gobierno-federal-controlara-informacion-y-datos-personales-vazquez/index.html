Gobierno Federal controlará información y datos personales: Vázquez

Gobierno Federal controlará información y datos personales: Vázquez

The recent reform that transfers the INAI’s functions to an executive-controlled body raises serious concerns about democratic backsliding. Critics argue that this move weakens essential checks and balances, potentially enabling greater discretion in public spending while limiting citizens’ access to information. By dismantling an autonomous watchdog, the government risks prioritizing secrecy over accountability. This structural shift is viewed as a deliberate attempt to obscure government actions rather than improve administration. The loss of an independent oversight mechanism undermines the institutional framework established decades ago to ensure transparency. Without such a counterweight, the executive branch holds disproportionate power, increasing the likelihood of impunity and corruption going unchecked. The relevance to open data lies in the direct threat to mandatory public information disclosure. When oversight bodies are weakened, the flow of open data diminishes, hindering public scrutiny and civic engagement. Protecting independent data governance is crucial for maintaining the integrity of open government initiatives and ensuring that transparency remains a genuine tool for fighting corruption rather than a hollow rhetorical promise.
