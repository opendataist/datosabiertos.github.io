Diario HOY | Datos oficiales indican que 854.000 jóvenes cuentan con empleo en el país

This analysis reveals that young workers in the country are predominantly employed in small microenterprises and the service sector, highlighting a concentration in precarious labor conditions. The data underscores significant disparities, including lower wages for those in informal or domestic roles and a persistent gender pay gap, which challenges the narrative of equitable market integration. These findings are crucial for open data initiatives, as they demonstrate the necessity of granular, disaggregated statistics to identify structural inequalities. By making detailed labor metrics accessible, policymakers and researchers can better understand the informal economy's impact on youth livelihoods. Ultimately, the report emphasizes that aggregate numbers often mask critical disparities. Open data platforms must prioritize accessible, detailed datasets on employment types and wages to support evidence-based interventions that address the specific vulnerabilities of young workers in developing economies.
