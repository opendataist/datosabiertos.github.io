El SEPE y Google ofrecen un curso gratuito de análisis de datos: requisitos y cómo inscribirse

This initiative highlights the critical intersection between open data education and employment accessibility. By leveraging partnerships with major technology companies, public institutions democratize access to essential digital skills, ensuring that data literacy is not reserved for elites. The program’s structure emphasizes practical, industry-relevant competencies, bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and market demands. The collaboration between government bodies and private technology giants creates a powerful framework for inclusive training. Prioritizing unemployed individuals and ensuring gender balance demonstrates a commitment to social equity within the data sector. This approach underscores how open educational resources can drive social inclusion, making professional opportunities available to marginalized groups who often lack formal credentials. Such models are vital for the open data movement because they validate the societal impact of data skills. When public services offer free, high-quality training, they reinforce the principle that data knowledge belongs to the public. This fosters a more transparent, informed society capable of engaging with open datasets and contributing to data-driven decision-making, ultimately strengthening democratic participation and innovation.
