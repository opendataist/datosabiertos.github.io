Durango, uno de los tres estados más seguros

Durango, uno de los tres estados más seguros

Durango ranks among Mexico’s three safest states, with significant reductions in extortion, vehicle theft, and residential burglary compared to previous years. This trend reflects a broader decline in criminal incidents across the region, offering residents greater peace and security through coordinated government efforts. This article highlights the value of public statistical surveys in measuring safety. For open data initiatives, it demonstrates how accessible government metrics enable transparent accountability and allow communities to track tangible improvements in public policy outcomes over time. The availability of such detailed indicators supports evidence-based decision-making. By making these statistics open and visible, authorities validate their strategies, fostering trust and allowing citizens to verify that safety resources are effectively deployed to reduce crime rates.
