Morena es el partido más sancionado por falta de atención de las solicitudes de transparencia

Morena es el partido más sancionado por falta de atención de las solicitudes de transparencia

The article reveals that Morena has consistently failed to uphold transparency standards, accumulating the highest volume of citizen appeals and sanctions among major political parties. By withholding critical information regarding public resource usage, such as office rentals and personnel contracts, the party demonstrates a systemic resistance to accountability. This pattern highlights a broader institutional deficit where legal obligations are treated as bureaucratic hurdles rather than fundamental democratic values, resulting in significant opacity regarding how these resources are managed. This lack of transparency is particularly relevant to the open data movement because it exposes the gap between mandatory publication requirements and actual data availability. The party’s tendency to classify sensitive information and omit essential financial records, such as contracts and supplier lists, undermines the integrity of public platforms. Consequently, even when data is technically "published," it often lacks the completeness and reliability necessary for meaningful public scrutiny, effectively negating the purpose of open government initiatives. Ultimately, the case illustrates that political parties often view transparency as a threat to their internal power structures rather than a civic duty. Experts note that without a genuine commitment to proactively sharing relevant decision-making processes and expenditures, the current legal frameworks are insufficient. For open data advocates, this serves as a critical reminder that true accountability requires more than mere compliance; it demands a cultural shift toward active, accessible, and trustworthy information disclosure.
