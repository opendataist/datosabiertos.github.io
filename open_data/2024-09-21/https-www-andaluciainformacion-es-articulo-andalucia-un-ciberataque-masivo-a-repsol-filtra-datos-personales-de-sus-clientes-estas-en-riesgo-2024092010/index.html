Open Data
ES EN

A cyberattack compromised Repsol clients’ data following a breach at a third-party provider. Although financial information remained secure, the exposure of personal data underscores the need for open data practices to prioritize robust security protocols to prevent identity theft and unauthorized changes to services. This incident highlights the vulnerabilities inherent in interconnected digital services. The potential for fraud stemming from leaked personal details demonstrates why transparent yet secure data handling is critical, urging organizations to rigorously audit their supply chains and protect sensitive user information from malicious exploitation. The event is relevant to open data as it illustrates the tension between transparency and privacy. It serves as a cautionary tale for developers and policymakers, emphasizing that while data sharing fosters innovation, it requires stringent safeguards to maintain public trust and protect individuals from the real-world consequences of data breaches.
