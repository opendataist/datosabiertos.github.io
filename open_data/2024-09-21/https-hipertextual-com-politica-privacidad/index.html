Open Data
ES EN

This privacy policy outlines how the website collects and manages personal user data, emphasizing transparency and legal compliance. It details the types of information gathered, such as cookies and usage metrics, and identifies the entities responsible for processing this data both internally and through third-party providers. The document establishes the legal bases for these operations, including user consent, contractual necessities, and legitimate interests, ensuring that all processing aligns with applicable regulations like the Spanish Law on Information Society Services. The policy clarifies the specific purposes for data usage, which include service provision, statistical analysis, and targeted advertising. It highlights the involvement of external analytics and advertising services, often located outside the European Union, and explains the mechanisms for user consent and data retention. By defining strict periods for data conservation and immediate deletion protocols once their purpose is fulfilled, the site aims to maintain user trust while fulfilling operational requirements. This document is relevant to open data discussions as it illustrates the standard framework for data stewardship and user rights in digital services. It demonstrates how organizations balance data utility for analytics and advertising with individual privacy protections, such as the rights to access, rectify, and delete personal information. Understanding such policies is crucial for evaluating data transparency, consent management, and the ethical implications of data sharing between local entities and global platforms.
