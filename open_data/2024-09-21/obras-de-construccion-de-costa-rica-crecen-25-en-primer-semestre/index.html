Obras de construcción de Costa Rica crecen 2,5 % en primer semestre

The preliminary construction statistics reveal a modest growth in total works across Costa Rica, driven by new projects and significant expansion in major provinces. This trend indicates a revitalization in the sector, particularly highlighting regional disparities in development intensity. Residential construction remains the dominant force, with total area metrics showing substantial increases despite a slight dip in the number of new housing permits. The market is shifting toward smaller units, suggesting evolving demands for affordable or compact living spaces amidst broader urbanization pressures. These insights are crucial for open data advocates, as they demonstrate how granular public statistics can uncover underlying economic patterns. Accessible, detailed datasets enable analysts to track sector health and inform policy decisions, proving the value of transparent government information for societal benefit.
