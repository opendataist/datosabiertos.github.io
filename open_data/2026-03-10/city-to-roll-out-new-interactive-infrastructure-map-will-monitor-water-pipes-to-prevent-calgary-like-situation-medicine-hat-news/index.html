City to roll out new interactive infrastructure map; will monitor water pipes to prevent "Calgary" like situation - Medicine Hat News

Medicine Hat is developing an open data portal to provide real-time visibility into its critical infrastructure, including roads, power lines, and underground utilities. This interactive online tool allows the public and council to view detailed map-based information, such as the age and material composition of water pipes, fostering greater transparency and engagement with municipal operations. By visualizing the condition and history of aging assets, the city aims to better communicate the necessity for future capital funding and proactive maintenance. This data-driven approach supports strategic budgeting, enabling officials to prioritize replacements for vulnerable systems before failures occur, thereby avoiding more costly emergency repairs and service disruptions. This initiative is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how making granular, machine-readable infrastructure information publicly accessible can enhance civic oversight. It illustrates a shift toward transparent governance, where communities can directly monitor asset health and understand the long-term sustainability efforts driving municipal planning and resource allocation decisions.
