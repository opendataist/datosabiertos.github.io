State police confirm investigation of alleged criminal incident involving Robert Sobeck

Robert Sobeck, the newly elected district judge in Whitpain, is under investigation for an alleged criminal incident at a local police department. State police declined to release specific records, citing exemptions for active criminal inquiries under public access laws. This situation highlights the tension between judicial transparency and the necessity of protecting ongoing law enforcement investigations. While the existence of an incident report is confirmed, details remain sealed, raising questions about accountability for public officials. The relevance to open data lies in the limitations imposed on FOIA requests when criminal activity is suspected. It underscores how legal exemptions can restrict public access to government records, challenging the ideal of fully transparent public administration and judicial ethics.
