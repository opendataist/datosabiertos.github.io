INAI instruye a Pemex transparentar proyecto de refinería

The INAI ordered Pemex to disclose details about the Dos Bocas refinery, emphasizing that public access to real-time project data is crucial for accountability. This decision empowers citizens to exercise social oversight and audit authorities, ensuring transparency over a flagship federal initiative. The ruling highlights the link between information availability and effective public oversight, allowing society to verify claims about production timelines and cost benefits. By mandating the release of schedules and progress reports, the INAI reinforces the necessity of open information for democratic scrutiny. This case is relevant to open data as it establishes a precedent for making large-scale infrastructure information publicly accessible. It demonstrates how transparency initiatives directly support civic engagement, proving that accessible data is essential for enforcing government accountability and maintaining public trust in major state projects.
