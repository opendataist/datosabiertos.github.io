Hugging Face: El imparable ascenso de la inteligencia artificial generativa

Hugging Face has evolved from an entertainment tool into the most comprehensive open-source repository for artificial intelligence models and datasets. Backed by major technology companies, the platform has established itself as a global standard, offering a vast infrastructure where researchers and developers can access hundreds of thousands of models. This democratizes access to advanced technologies, enabling the creation of diverse applications and fostering an international community that uses these tools to innovate in natural language processing, image generation, and audio. The organization maintains that the openness and transparency inherent in open source are fundamental to responsible and sustainable AI deployment. By allowing users to inspect how models are trained, it mitigates risks of bias and harm that are difficult to detect in proprietary solutions. This stance is crucial for ethical governance, as transparency facilitates oversight and accountability—elements necessary to balance innovation with the protection of rights and the prevention of technological power centralization. The article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical synergy between accessible data, open models, and smart regulation. Hugging Face not only stores data but also provides the ecosystem for training and sharing models that depend on the quality and availability of these datasets. Its participation in European and American regulatory debates underscores the urgency of protecting open innovation, ensuring that AI development does not fall into the hands of a few, but remains a common good that society can audit and apply to global challenges, such as climate change, through cross-sector collaborations.
