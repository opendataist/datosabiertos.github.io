Marine economy bolsters American prosperity

The U.S. marine economy demonstrated significant resilience in 2021, recovering strongly from pandemic-related declines and outpacing overall national economic growth. By contributing a substantial portion of the Gross Domestic Product, this sector proved to be a critical pillar of American prosperity. The data highlights the essential role of oceans and coastal waters in supporting local communities, ecosystems, and broad economic stability, reinforcing the administration’s commitment to a climate-ready nation. Ten distinct sectors were analyzed, with tourism and recreation emerging as the largest contributor, followed by national defense and offshore minerals. Despite some fluctuations in specific areas like marine construction and coastal utilities, most sectors experienced growth in both sales and employment. This diverse array of activities underscores the complexity of the marine economy and its capacity to drive innovation, create new businesses, and support millions of jobs across the country. This report is highly relevant to open_data initiatives because it exemplifies high-quality, government-mandated economic accounting that ensures transparency and public accessibility. By standardizing how marine-related economic impacts are measured and reported through NOAA and the Bureau of Economic Analysis, the data provides a reliable foundation for researchers, policymakers, and businesses. Open access to such detailed sectoral breakdowns enables better decision-making regarding sustainable investments and helps stakeholders understand the true value of ocean-based resources.
