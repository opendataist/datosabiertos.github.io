Se disparan en Saltillo precios de pan y tortilla en los últimos 2 años

The article highlights significant price increases for essential food staples in Saltillo, such as bread and tortillas, over a two-year period. This surge affects primary ingredients like corn flour, indicating broad inflationary pressure on basic consumer goods in the region. These rising costs demonstrate how inflation directly impacts household budgets and access to nutrition. The data reveals a tangible escalation in the cost of living, particularly for families relying on affordable, staple foods for daily sustenance. This content is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistical indices from government institutions such as INEGI. Transparent access to such pricing data enables researchers and citizens to monitor economic trends, accurately assess inflation, and advocate for policies that address cost-of-living challenges.
