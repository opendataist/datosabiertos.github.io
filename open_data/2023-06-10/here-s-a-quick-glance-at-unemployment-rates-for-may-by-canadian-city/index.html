Here's a quick glance at unemployment rates for May, by Canadian city

Statistics Canada released May unemployment data, revealing a national rate of 5.2 percent alongside city-specific averages. The agency warns that these localized figures may fluctuate significantly due to small statistical samples, indicating potential volatility in regional labor market indicators. The report highlights substantial variation across major Canadian cities, with rates ranging from low single digits to over seven percent in specific areas. These disparities reflect diverse local economic conditions and workforce dynamics, offering a granular view of employment health beyond the national aggregate. This dataset is relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the critical need for transparency regarding sample sizes and statistical uncertainty. By openly sharing raw figures and methodological cautions, statisticians enable researchers and the public to properly interpret data limitations, fostering greater trust and more informed analysis of public sector information.
