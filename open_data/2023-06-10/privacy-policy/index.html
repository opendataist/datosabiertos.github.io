Privacy Policy

The article serves as a comprehensive privacy policy for the website mikrometoxos.gr, outlining the specific categories of personal data collected and the reasons behind their retention. It details the collection of IP addresses and browser user agents for spam detection, alongside the use of email hashes for Gravatar integration. Furthermore, it warns users about the risks of uploading images containing embedded location data, as visitors can extract this information, highlighting a critical transparency issue regarding user control over shared digital footprints. The policy explains the functionality and duration of various cookies used for login persistence, comment convenience, and session management. It also discloses that embedded content from third-party sources operates independently, potentially collecting user data and tracking interactions as if the visitor were directly on the external site. This section emphasizes the complex data ecosystems surrounding modern web content, where user privacy extends beyond the immediate host to include embedded third-party trackers. Regarding data rights, the site offers users the ability to export or erase their personal information, except for data required for administrative, legal, or security purposes. Comments are retained indefinitely to facilitate automatic approval of follow-up responses. This document is relevant to open_data initiatives because it illustrates the balance between accessible content and user privacy, serving as a model for how organizations can transparently disclose data practices while providing users with clear mechanisms to manage their digital identity and consent.
