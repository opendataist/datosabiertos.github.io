TRUSANO addresses the critical challenge of safely sharing sensitive information by automating data anonymization and redaction. By leveraging artificial intelligence, the platform transforms traditionally manual, time-consuming processes into efficient workflows. This shift enables organizations to protect personally identifying information while preserving data utility, addressing the urgent need for modern solutions in an era of increased data sharing demands. The system significantly reduces the risk of re-identification through automated risk assessment and configurable rules. Users can upload documents for AI-driven scanning, allowing the software to detect sensitive data and generate appropriate masking strategies. This approach not only ensures compliance with privacy standards but also scales seamlessly across various industries, including healthcare, finance, and government sectors requiring strict data governance. For the open data community, this technology is vital as it facilitates secure data release without compromising individual privacy. By lowering the technical and temporal barriers to anonymization, TRUSANO encourages broader data accessibility and reuse. Ultimately, it supports the open data ethos by making high-quality, de-identified datasets available for research and public benefit, fostering transparency and trust in data-driven initiatives.
