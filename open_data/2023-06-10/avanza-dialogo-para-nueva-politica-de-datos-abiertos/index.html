Avanza diálogo para nueva política de datos abiertos

Avanza diálogo para nueva política de datos abiertos

The government is advancing the renewal of its open data policy through pluralistic dialogue with civil society. This co-creation process aims to strengthen transparency and accountability, ensuring that regulations reflect the actual needs of those who use public information. Involving diverse stakeholders in the design of regulations is key to improving governance and democracy. By incorporating external perspectives, the resulting policies become more robust and better aligned with social realities, promoting greater citizen participation in state management. This process is fundamental to the open data movement, as it validates that interoperability and effective access depend on collaboration between the state and society. By prioritizing consensus, it establishes a precedent for how public policies should evolve to be truly inclusive and effective in the digital age.
