Todo un éxito Presentación de Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto: Municipio | La Opción de Chihuahua

Todo un éxito Presentación de Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto: Municipio | La Opción de Chihuahua

The government of Chihuahua unveiled an open government action plan, developed through a year-long collaboration with citizens and civil society. This initiative prioritizes transparency, accountability, and social innovation to address community challenges and define joint solutions. Key commitments include campaigns against violence and programs to promote rural development through citizen cooperation. Additionally, the strategy aims to strengthen digital tools that foster dialogue and support local entrepreneurship, ensuring active public engagement in municipal progress. This effort is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how releasing government plans and fostering public participation can drive tangible social improvements. It highlights the power of transparent collaboration between the state and society in creating responsive, accountable governance structures.
