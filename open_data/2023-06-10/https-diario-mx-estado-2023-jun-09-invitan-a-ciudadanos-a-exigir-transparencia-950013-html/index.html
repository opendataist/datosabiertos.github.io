Chihuahua seeks to establish an open government policy through active citizen participation, aiming for transparency and accountability. However, current engagement is critically low, prompting officials to urge the public to claim their right to information and demand better governance. This initiative is vital for open data, as it highlights the necessity of community involvement to validate and sustain transparent digital governance frameworks.
