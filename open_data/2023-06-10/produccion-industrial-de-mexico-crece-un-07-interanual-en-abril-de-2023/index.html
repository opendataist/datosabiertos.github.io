Producción industrial de México crece un 0,7% interanual en abril de 2023

Mexico’s industrial output showed steady growth in April, driven by mining and manufacturing, while construction contracted. This performance contributes to a positive trend in the first half of the year, reflecting broader economic resilience and aligning with national GDP recovery goals in the post-pandemic period. The data illustrate how specific sectors influence national economic indicators, offering a granular view of productive capacity. Such detailed breakdowns are crucial for stakeholders tracking real-time economic health beyond aggregate figures. This article highlights the value of open data in providing transparent, timely insights into economic activities. Access to official statistics enables better analysis of sectoral trends, fostering informed decision-making and demonstrating how public data infrastructure supports economic transparency and accountability.
