La protección de datos, clave para la inclusión financiera

The processing of personal data goes beyond mere legal compliance or the obtaining of consent, becoming a crucial element for generating a positive impact on people’s lives. Effective protection requires continuously assessing risk and fairness in automated decisions, ensuring that the use of information does not discriminate against or harm certain segments of the population, but instead promotes inclusion and social justice. The quality, accuracy, and timeliness of information are key to achieving fair assessments, particularly in sensitive areas such as access to credit. Emerging regulations, such as those related to open banking and open data, aim to expand the availability of ethical and comprehensive information. This enables financial institutions to measure risks more accurately while mitigating biases, balancing the need for security with individual rights. This article is fundamental to the open data movement because it highlights that transparency and access to information not only optimize economic efficiency but also serve as pillars for the protection of rights. By empowering citizens with tools to control, correct, and understand how their data is used, privacy is transformed from a bureaucratic barrier into a mechanism for active participation, demonstrating that ethical data management aligns with the principles of openness and civic responsibility.
