Nearly 150,000 Shoah survivors live in Israel - EJP

Nearly 150,000 Shoah survivors live in Israel - EJP

This article highlights the demographic reality of the dwindling Holocaust survivor population in Israel, marking a critical juncture for historical preservation. As the community ages, there is an urgent need to document and preserve their testimonies and personal records before they are lost. The data underscores the importance of maintaining accessible, open datasets regarding survivor locations, origins, and life histories. Such transparency allows researchers, educators, and families to better understand migration patterns and the evolving landscape of the remaining survivor community. Relevance to open data lies in the ethical imperative to preserve this vulnerable group’s stories through structured, searchable information. Open access facilitates accurate historical education and ensures that the legacy of resistance and survival remains available for future generations.
