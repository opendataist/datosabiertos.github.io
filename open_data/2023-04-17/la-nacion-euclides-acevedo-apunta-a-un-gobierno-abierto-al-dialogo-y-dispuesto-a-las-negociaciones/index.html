La Nación / Euclides Acevedo apunta a un gobierno abierto al diálogo y dispuesto a las negociaciones

Euclides Acevedo proposes a governance model based on dialogue and strategic planning to resolve Paraguay’s crisis. His agenda prioritizes urgent public needs, specifically guaranteeing free public healthcare as a fundamental right. This initial focus aims to stabilize society before addressing secondary but critical issues such as security and employment. He links security to judicial integrity, arguing that true safety requires a fair legal system. Furthermore, Acevedo emphasizes industrialization as the key to generating jobs and achieving social justice. By creating employment opportunities, he believes the country can restore human dignity and ensure long-term stability through a comprehensive, negotiated approach rather than confrontation. This article highlights the relevance of open data by illustrating how transparent governance relies on accessible information. Effective planning and accountability in healthcare, security, and industrial policies require open data for public monitoring. Citizens need clear, available statistics to evaluate these commitments, ensuring that strategic decisions are measurable and that the government remains answerable to its electorate through data-driven transparency.
