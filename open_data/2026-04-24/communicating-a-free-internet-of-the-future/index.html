Communicating a free internet of the future

The article highlights the European NGI0 programme’s role in fostering a decentralized internet through grants supporting open source, data, and hardware. By disseminating interviews with grantees, the initiative aims to make the project’s impact visible and accessible globally, encouraging dialogue that extends beyond traditional European tech hubs. This transparency is crucial for demonstrating how open technologies can empower communities and promote democratic values. However, the narrative emphasizes that technical openness must be coupled with social inclusivity. The author argues for addressing systemic inequalities and ensuring fair working conditions for those who maintain these technologies. Without attention to diversity and the often-invisible labor of maintenance, open ecosystems risk perpetuating the very power dynamics they seek to dismantle, necessitating a shift toward re-humanized infrastructures that prioritize human well-being alongside technological freedom. This content is relevant to open data because it underscores that data initiatives cannot succeed in isolation from their social contexts. It advocates for using communication tools like storytelling and documentation to archive and share knowledge, ensuring that the benefits of open data are equitable. By highlighting the intersection of technical tools and human rights, the article reinforces the open data principle that true openness requires inclusive practices and accessible dissemination to prevent exclusion and ensure sustainable community empowerment.
