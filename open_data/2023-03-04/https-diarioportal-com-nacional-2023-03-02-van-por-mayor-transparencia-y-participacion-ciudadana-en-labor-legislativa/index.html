Open Data
ES EN

This legislative initiative empowers citizens to actively participate in the lawmaking process through digital and physical forums. By mandating transparency and collaborative input, it transforms the legislative environment into an open ecosystem where public feedback directly shapes policy drafts and academic exchanges. This approach is vital for open data as it democratizes access to legislative studies and establishes formal channels for public commentary. It shifts governance from opaque proceedings to a participatory model, ensuring that government operations are visible and that societal data contributes to democratic accountability and informed decision-making.
