Open Data Day y fintech. Una nueva era para los datos abiertos

The evolution of the financial sector has transformed open data from a mere legal obligation into a strategic pillar for innovation. This shift drives both fintech companies and traditional banks to leverage transparent information to develop personalized products and mitigate risks, demonstrating that data availability is essential for creating solutions that effectively respond to the needs of the current market. The relevance of this article lies in its demonstration of how open data fosters collaboration and corporate social responsibility. By understanding the voluntary benefits of sharing information, institutions promote an ecosystem where transparency not only fulfills regulatory requirements but also generates economic value, enabling new market entrants to access tools based on free and equitable access to public information. Furthermore, the integration of artificial intelligence is expected to maximize the impact of these resources. By analyzing large volumes of open data in real time, algorithms will improve trend prediction and fraud detection, raising critical privacy challenges that require robust ethical frameworks to balance technological innovation with user protection in an era of increased digital connectivity.
