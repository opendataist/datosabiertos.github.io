Civil society groups in Kaduna State successfully verified that the public concerns raised during the 2022 budget hearings were largely incorporated into the final approved budget. This validation, facilitated by PERL and OGP partners, confirms that citizen engagement directly influences fiscal policy, demonstrating the tangible impact of participatory governance mechanisms. The initiative underscores the importance of continuous civil society oversight in ensuring government accountability and responsiveness to public needs. The reflection session highlighted the necessity of monitoring budget implementation to sustain these gains. Stakeholders emphasized that while securing favorable budget lines is crucial, tracking their actual execution is equally vital for meaningful development. This focus on post-approval accountability ensures that the promised reforms translate into real-world benefits for citizens, particularly in infrastructure and human capital development. This article is relevant to open_data because it illustrates the critical link between data-driven advocacy and policy outcomes. It shows how transparent budget processes, combined with civic engagement, allow citizens to hold leaders accountable. The success of this initiative serves as a model for how open government principles can be effectively operationalized to enhance public trust and improve service delivery through structured dialogue and data transparency.
