Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato production highlights the immense scale of this vital crop, with major economies like China and India leading worldwide output. This data underscores the strategic importance of potatoes in global food security, as these nations contribute significantly to the total metric tonnes harvested annually. Understanding these production levels is crucial for analysts assessing agricultural resilience and supply chain stability across different regions. The FAOSTAT database serves as a central repository for this agricultural information, offering accessible data on total production and harvested areas. By providing standardized metrics, it enables researchers and policymakers to compare productivity across diverse geographical contexts. Although minor discrepancies may exist between international reports and local government statistics, the aggregate figures remain reliable for broad comparative analysis and trend identification. This resource is particularly relevant to open data advocates because it demonstrates how transparent, machine-readable agricultural statistics can drive informed decision-making. The availability of such extensive datasets allows for independent verification and deeper socioeconomic analysis. Consequently, it empowers stakeholders to utilize open data for addressing challenges in food distribution, climate adaptation, and sustainable farming practices globally.
