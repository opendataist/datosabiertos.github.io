PIB de Angola creció un 4,6 por ciento en primer trimestre - Noticias Prensa Latina

PIB de Angola creció un 4,6 por ciento en primer trimestre - Noticias Prensa Latina

Angola’s economy recorded its strongest growth since 2015, driven primarily by oil, agriculture, and transport. This resurgence highlights the critical role of the energy sector while revealing opportunities to diversify through manufacturing and construction, reducing dependence on a single pillar for national prosperity. The report emphasizes that sustained investment in infrastructure and technological innovation is essential to maintain this momentum. Enhancing these foundational supports not only boosts productivity but also attracts necessary foreign investment, ensuring that short-term gains translate into long-term structural competitiveness and broader economic resilience. This data is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent national accounts enable evidence-based policy and investment decisions. Accessible, high-quality economic statistics empower stakeholders to monitor development trends, fostering accountability and informed strategies for sustainable growth in emerging markets.
