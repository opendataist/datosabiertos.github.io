La Asamblea regional aprueba hoy la nueva Ley de Transparencia que agilizará la resolución de los expedientes - murcia.com

The new Transparency Law restructures the oversight body into an independent, single commissioner to enhance agility and impartiality in handling information requests. This political agreement aims to guarantee institutional independence and accelerate the resolution of public access claims through a more efficient, professionalized framework. The reform is relevant to open data by strengthening the governance mechanisms that ensure transparent access to public information, a cornerstone of open government initiatives.
