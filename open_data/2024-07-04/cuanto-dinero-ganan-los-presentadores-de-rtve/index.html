¿Cuánto dinero ganan los presentadores de RTVE?

¿Cuánto dinero ganan los presentadores de RTVE?

The disclosure of RTVE presenters’ salaries reveals a compensation structure marked by a stark disparity between prime-time slots and the rest of the programming. The most iconic faces and prime-time programs clearly top the pay scales, accumulating significantly higher incomes than other figures, reflecting a concentration of economic resources in the most visible media talent with national reach. Conversely, broadcasts on smaller channels, lower-rated programs, and especially the digital division show much more modest compensation. Although the digital sector is investing in new influential figures, the figures reveal a professional hierarchy where prestige and traditional television experience still carry more weight than the new multimedia format, creating a notable contrast between linear television and the strategy for adapting to new consumption platforms. This issue is relevant to the field of open data because it demonstrates how the publication of salary information, even when sometimes fragmented, promotes transparency in the public sector. By making this data public, it facilitates the analysis of remuneration policies in public companies, enabling citizens and researchers to assess internal equity and the management of public funds allocated to social communication.
