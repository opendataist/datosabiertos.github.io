España recibe en mayo 9,3 millones de turistas internacionales y gastan un 20% más

The article reveals sustained and robust growth in the Spanish tourism sector, marked by a significant increase in both the number of visitors and their total spending. This positive trend is not limited to a single month but is consolidated over the first five months of the year, with figures exceeding 33 million tourists, indicating a recovery and dynamism in the sector that surpass pre-pandemic levels. The structure of tourism has evolved towards greater diversification of stays and an increase in average spending per visitor. Although detailed statistics show specific percentage increases, the key implication is that tourists are staying longer and spending more, particularly on leisure and transportation. This suggests a shift in behavior towards deeper and higher-value experiences, which is fundamental for the economic sustainability of the sector. This report is relevant for open data because it demonstrates the critical importance of the availability and transparency of official data from institutions such as the INE (National Statistics Institute). The detailed analysis of tourist flows, geographic origins, and spending patterns depends on access to accurate and open datasets. These data allow researchers, businesses, and policymakers to understand market trends, make informed decisions about infrastructure and marketing strategies, and evaluate the real impact of public policies on the national economy.
