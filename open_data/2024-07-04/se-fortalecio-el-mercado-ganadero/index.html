Se fortaleció el mercado ganadero

Se fortaleció el mercado ganadero

The livestock market remains firm, with cattle prices rising slightly despite cautious demand for replacement animals. Small ruminants show robust performance due to scarce supply, while slaughter volumes for both categories decreased compared to the previous period. This report is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent, standardized weekly datasets enable stakeholders to track market trends, analyze supply-demand dynamics, and make informed economic decisions. Access to such granular price and volume metrics facilitates broader analysis of agricultural sector health. Ultimately, the availability of these open statistics supports evidence-based policy and business strategies. By monitoring real-time fluctuations in production and consumption, researchers and professionals can better understand the implications of market forces on the broader food supply chain.
