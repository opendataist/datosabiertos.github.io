Control de datos personales en manos de criticado Mitic y con riesgo para ley de información pública - Política - ABC Color

Control de datos personales en manos de criticado Mitic y con riesgo para ley de información pública - Política - ABC Color

The recent cyberattack that compromised millions of Paraguayan records has accelerated legislative efforts to pass a personal data protection bill. This urgency underscores the critical need for legal frameworks to safeguard citizens’ information, directly illustrating how vulnerabilities in open data can erode public trust and security in the absence of regulatory oversight. Civil society and legislators are debating the bill’s structure, aiming to prevent the illegal commercialization of personal and biometric data. However, tensions have emerged regarding the designated regulatory body, MITIC, whose perceived inefficiency raises concerns about effective implementation. This debate highlights the challenge of balancing robust data privacy with transparent governance within open data ecosystems. Significant controversy surrounds provisions that could restrict public access to official information by invoking privacy concerns for public officials. This potential overlap between data protection and transparency poses a risk to open data principles, as excessive secrecy could hinder accountability. The outcome will define how Paraguay manages the delicate intersection between protecting individual privacy and maintaining the openness essential for democratic oversight.
