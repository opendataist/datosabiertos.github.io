ChatGPT y el arte de la imitación: ¿hasta dónde puede llegar la inteligencia artificial en la creación de imágenes? - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

ChatGPT y el arte de la imitación: ¿hasta dónde puede llegar la inteligencia artificial en la creación de imágenes? - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

The integration of visual capabilities into tools like ChatGPT democratizes artistic creation, enabling any user to reproduce iconic styles from major animation studios through simple instructions. This evolution transforms AI from a text generator into a versatile visual editing platform, where everyday photographs are instantly transformed into cinematic or animated pieces. The widespread access to these features accelerates digital content production and redefines the barriers to entry for visual creative expression. However, this advancement raises complex ethical and legal dilemmas concerning intellectual property, particularly regarding the training of models on copyrighted works. Although artistic style itself is not registered as property, the automated use of copyrighted data to replicate distinctive visual traits creates a regulatory gray area. The central question is no longer just about the originality of execution, but the legitimacy of appropriating the visual essence of established artists through algorithms, challenging traditional definitions of authorship and plagiarism. This article is relevant to the open data movement because it highlights the urgency of making transparent the datasets used to train artificial intelligence models. The discussion about how and with what works AI is trained reflects the critical need for open access to information about training data, the provenance of that information, and the associated rights. Without transparency in these fundamental datasets, it is impossible to establish fair regulations that protect both technological innovation and the rights of original creators.
