¿Quién es Javier Guzhñay, nuevo miembro de la Autoridad Aeroportuaria de Guayaquil?

¿Quién es Javier Guzhñay, nuevo miembro de la Autoridad Aeroportuaria de Guayaquil?

The resignation of Alfredo Parra from the board of the Guayaquil Airport Authority underscores ongoing concerns about transparency regarding municipal officials holding multiple public positions. His successor, Javier Guzhñay, assumes the role despite having previously held appointments in other city agencies, raising questions about institutional overlap and the management of conflicts of interest in local governance. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accessible, structured registries for tracking the concurrent positions held by public officeholders. Transparent data systems enable citizens and researchers to monitor potential conflicts of interest, ensuring accountability and fostering trust in public administration through verified, machine-readable information on leadership changes and roles.
