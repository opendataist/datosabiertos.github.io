President Panuelo submits Freedom of Information Act to FSM Congress

President Panuelo submits Freedom of Information Act to FSM Congress

The President of the Federated States of Micronesia has proposed a Freedom of Information Act to establish a legal framework for public access to government data. This legislation aims to institutionalize transparency and accountability by granting citizens the explicit right to receive information, a right currently absent from the constitution. By creating standardized procedures and remedies, the act seeks to foster a culture of openness within public service. The proposed law mandates that national agencies actively publish organizational information and designate specific officers to handle information requests. These roles are intended to facilitate inter-agency cooperation, breaking down operational silos and ensuring consistent, accurate data dissemination. Centralizing the handling of information minimizes misinformation while promoting efficiency across government departments. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it creates the necessary legal infrastructure for public access. By mandating disclosure and establishing clear protocols, the act transforms raw government data into accessible resources. This supports the broader open data movement by ensuring that information is not only available but also reliable and systematically managed for public benefit.
