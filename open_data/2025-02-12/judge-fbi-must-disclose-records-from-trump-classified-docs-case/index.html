Judge: FBI Must Disclose Records From Trump Classified Docs Case

A federal judge ruled that presidential immunity and dismissed charges remove barriers to disclosing FBI records, making the former president vulnerable to public scrutiny. This decision establishes Freedom of Information Act requests as a vital mechanism for accountability, shifting the focus from criminal prosecution to transparency. This highlights the critical role of open data in monitoring executive conduct when traditional legal avenues are inaccessible.
