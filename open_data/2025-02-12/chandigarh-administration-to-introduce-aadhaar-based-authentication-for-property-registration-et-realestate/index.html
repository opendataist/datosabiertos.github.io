Chandigarh administration to introduce Aadhaar-based authentication for property registration - ET RealEstate

Chandigarh administration to introduce Aadhaar-based authentication for property registration - ET RealEstate

Chandigarh is integrating Aadhaar-based biometric authentication into property registration to replace manual document checks. This shift aims to significantly reduce processing times while enhancing system transparency and preventing fraud or identity impersonation through automated data verification against national repositories. The initiative reflects a broader digital transformation within public administration, prioritizing efficiency and security. By automating identity validation, the administration seeks to minimize human error and streamline interactions for citizens, marking a move toward more robust, technology-driven governance frameworks for critical legal processes. This development is highly relevant to open data discourse as it demonstrates how centralized identity infrastructure can streamline bureaucratic services. It highlights the potential for interoperable data systems to improve public service delivery, though it also underscores the complex relationship between centralized digital identity verification and broader principles of data privacy, accessibility, and citizen rights in the digital age.
