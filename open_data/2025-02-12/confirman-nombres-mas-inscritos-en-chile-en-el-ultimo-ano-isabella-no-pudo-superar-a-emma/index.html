Confirman nombres más inscritos en Chile en el último año: Isabella no pudo superar a Emma

Confirman nombres más inscritos en Chile en el último año: Isabella no pudo superar a Emma

The Chilean Civil Registry confirmed that Emma and Mateo were the most popular baby names in 2024. This confirmation underscores the reliability of open data obtained through transparency requests, demonstrating how public records can be cross-checked against independent journalistic findings. The final data aligns with earlier trends, showing Emma narrowly edging out Isabella, while Mateo retained his top position for the ninth consecutive year. These results reflect consistent cultural naming preferences rather than sudden shifts, highlighting the value of longitudinal data analysis in understanding societal patterns. This case is significant for open data because it illustrates the power of accessible government information. By obtaining data through transparency laws, citizens and the media can verify official statistics, fostering accountability and ensuring that public records accurately reflect demographic realities for future research and policy decisions.
