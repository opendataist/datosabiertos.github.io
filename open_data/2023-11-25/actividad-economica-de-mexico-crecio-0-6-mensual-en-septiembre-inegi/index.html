Actividad económica de México creció 0.6% mensual en septiembre: Inegi

Mexico’s economic activity showed modest growth, driven primarily by the tertiary sector, while primary activities experienced a slight decline. This divergence highlights that service and commercial sectors are currently more dynamic than agriculture and raw material extraction. The secondary sector also contributed positively, with construction and energy showing notable gains. These findings illustrate how different economic pillars respond variably to current conditions, revealing a complex economic landscape rather than uniform expansion. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the importance of granular, sector-specific indicators. Access to such detailed statistics allows analysts to monitor economic health accurately and supports evidence-based policy making in public administration and private sector forecasting.
