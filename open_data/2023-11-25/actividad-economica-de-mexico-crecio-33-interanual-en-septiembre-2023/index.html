Actividad económica de México creció 3,3% interanual en septiembre 2023

The Mexican economy showed robust annual growth in September, driven by gains across the primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors. This upward trend in industrial activity and services signals positive short-term momentum, reinforcing expectations that the country will exceed its annual growth targets. Revised data indicate that third-quarter GDP growth surpassed initial estimates, highlighting the economy’s resilience following the severe contraction caused by the pandemic. These revisions suggest a stronger recovery trajectory than previously anticipated, providing a more accurate picture of recent economic performance. This information is crucial for open data initiatives, as it underscores the importance of accessible, up-to-date statistical indicators for monitoring national economic health. Reliable public datasets enable stakeholders to analyze recovery patterns, forecast future trends, and make informed decisions based on transparent government metrics.
