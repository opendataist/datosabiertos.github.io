Crecen 4% casi todos los indicadores de violencia contra las mujeres, según INEGI - Noticaribe

The article highlights that violence against women in Mexico has increased across psychological, physical, and sexual domains between 2016 and 2021, revealing that nearly three-quarters of women have experienced some form of aggression. This data underscores the persistence of structural gender roles that confine women to unpaid domestic labor, exacerbating their vulnerability to economic and patriarchal control within the household. Furthermore, the statistics expose a significant prevalence of early-life trauma, with a substantial portion of women recalling violence during childhood. Digital spaces also emerge as critical arenas for abuse, where women and girls face disproportionate rates of sexual harassment and body shaming compared to males, indicating that technological advancements have introduced new vectors for gender-based violence that require urgent societal attention. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how rigorous statistical collection by national institutions like INEGI provides the empirical foundation necessary for evidence-based policy-making. Transparent access to these disaggregated datasets allows researchers and advocates to identify hidden patterns of inequality, hold institutions accountable, and design targeted interventions that address both traditional and emerging forms of discrimination against women.
