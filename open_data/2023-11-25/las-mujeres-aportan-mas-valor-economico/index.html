Las mujeres aportan más valor económico

This article reveals that unpaid domestic work generates economic value exceeding that of key sectors such as manufacturing and commerce, highlighting its hidden importance in national accounting. The gender gap is evident, as women contribute significantly more than men, underscoring the need to make this labor visible in traditional statistical information systems. The relevance for open data lies in the urgent need to incorporate metrics on unpaid work into input-output tables and national accounting standards. By making these data accessible and disaggregated by gender, transparency is promoted and more equitable public policies can be designed that recognize women’s real contribution to the economy, transforming the official perception of productive value.
