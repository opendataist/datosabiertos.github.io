Política de privacidad

The text outlines the data collection and management policies of the website Diario El Rancagüino, emphasizing transparency regarding how users’ personal information is processed. The main conclusion is that the platform collects various types of data, ranging from IP addresses to anonymous identifiers, with the purpose of enhancing security, improving the user experience, and preventing spam, while keeping visitors informed about how their information is used. It is essential for citizens to understand that, although mechanisms exist to delete or export personal data, certain records, such as comments, are retained indefinitely to facilitate automated moderation. This data retention implies that privacy is not absolute and that interactions with embedded third-party content may result in additional tracking beyond the direct control of the original website, thereby affecting individuals’ sovereignty over their digital identity. This information is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates the opposite reality: the opacity with which many websites operate regarding the closed data of their users. Awareness of the collection, retention, and sharing of personal data is the first step toward demanding greater transparency and control. Understanding these dynamics helps citizens appreciate the right to privacy as a necessary counterpart in any ecosystem that aspires to be open, ethical, and democratic, highlighting the importance of data sovereignty.
