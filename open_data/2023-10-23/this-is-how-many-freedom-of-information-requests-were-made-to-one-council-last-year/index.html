This is how many Freedom of Information requests were made to one council last year

The report highlights a critical tension in public sector transparency: while the volume of Freedom of Information and Subject Access requests has risen, the council’s responsiveness often falls just short of statutory standards. This slight deficit underscores the operational strain that growing demand for information places on governance structures, revealing that efficient data management is no longer just about compliance but requires robust resource allocation to handle increasing public scrutiny. Furthermore, the data demonstrates that identifying and mitigating security risks is a continuous, resource-intensive effort. The increase in recorded data breaches, even those resulting in minimal regulatory action, indicates that open data initiatives are inherently coupled with heightened security challenges. Organizations must therefore balance the drive for accessibility with rigorous protection measures, as the administrative burden of verifying identities and securing sensitive files significantly impacts departmental capacity. This article is vital for the open data community because it illustrates the practical realities behind transparency metrics. It shows that achieving "acceptable" standards is difficult when procedural bottlenecks exist, reminding stakeholders that open data is not merely about releasing information but ensuring the underlying governance frameworks can sustainably and securely support that flow of information without compromising individual privacy or operational efficiency.
