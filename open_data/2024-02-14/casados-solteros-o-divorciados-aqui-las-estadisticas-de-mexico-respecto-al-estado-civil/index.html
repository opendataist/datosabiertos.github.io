¿Casados, solteros o divorciados? Aquí las estadísticas de México respecto al estado civil

The INEGI report reveals significant shifts in Mexican marital dynamics, highlighting a steady rise in divorce rates relative to marriages over the past decade. This trend indicates a fundamental transformation in how relationships are formed and dissolved, reflecting evolving social norms and legal accessibility. Data further illustrate that conjugal status remains strongly correlated with age, with young adults predominantly single and older populations mostly married or widowed. Interestingly, the vast majority of unions occur between opposite-sex partners, although same-sex marriages are gaining statistical visibility, pointing to gradual societal integration. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how publicly available, granular demographic statistics can expose underlying social patterns. By making detailed indicators on relationships, age, and well-being transparent, researchers and policymakers can better understand societal changes, ensuring that public information serves as a vital tool for analyzing human behavior and guiding social policy decisions.
