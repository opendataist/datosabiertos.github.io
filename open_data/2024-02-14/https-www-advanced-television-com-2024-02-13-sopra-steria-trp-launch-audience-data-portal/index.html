Sopra Steria and TRP Research have collaborated to create AudEx Visualise, a digital portal designed to modernize how UK broadcasters and advertisers interpret television and VoD consumption data. By integrating advanced analytics with industry expertise, the solution shifts the sector away from static, error-prone spreadsheets toward an intuitive, dynamic platform for exploring audience metrics. This transformation significantly reduces the time wasted on manual data processing and complex reporting. Instead, industry professionals can access pre-calculated, curated insights that ensure consistency and accuracy. This efficiency allows commissioners and media buyers to focus their energy on deriving strategic value rather than wrestling with technical data infrastructure. The partnership is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how structured, accessible data visualization can democratize complex information. By making robust audience insights easily understandable and available through a standardized digital interface, the initiative sets a precedent for transparency and usability. This approach encourages broader adoption of data-driven decision-making across the media landscape, highlighting the power of open, user-centric data design.
