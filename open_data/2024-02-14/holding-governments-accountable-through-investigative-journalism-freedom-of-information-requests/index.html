Holding governments accountable through investigative journalism & freedom of information requests

Access to government records is increasingly hindered despite constitutional guarantees, a challenge exacerbated by shrinking newsrooms and reduced investigative resources. The Florida Center for Government Accountability addresses this by empowering citizens with tools to better understand and engage with local officials, ensuring transparency remains accessible. This work is vital for open_data because it highlights the critical link between legal frameworks and practical accessibility. When public data becomes difficult to obtain, civic engagement suffers. Strengthening access ensures that open data initiatives can effectively support informed decision-making and accountability in local communities.
