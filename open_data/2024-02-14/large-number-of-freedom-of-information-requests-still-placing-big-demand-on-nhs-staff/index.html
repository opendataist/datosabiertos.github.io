Large number of freedom of information requests still placing big demand on NHS staff

NHS Shetland faces significant operational strain due to a high volume of freedom of information requests, with staff potentially dedicating substantial weekly hours to compliance. This burden is exacerbated by specific inquiries from political entities and suppliers, highlighting a disconnect between public transparency rights and institutional capacity. The article underscores the critical challenge of resource allocation in public sector transparency. While legislation mandates timely responses, the sheer volume forces organizations to balance immediate compliance against broader service delivery. This tension reveals the urgent need for more proactive data publication to reduce reactive information disclosure demands. This situation is highly relevant to open data advocates as it demonstrates the limitations of siloed information release. By publishing core performance metrics and supplementary staffing data more extensively upfront, authorities can mitigate the administrative load. Ultimately, this highlights how robust open data strategies can enhance accessibility while preserving operational efficiency for public servants.
