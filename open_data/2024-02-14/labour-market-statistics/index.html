Labour Market Statistics

Northern Ireland’s labor market demonstrates a complex recovery where employment numbers are rising, yet underlying wages have dipped monthly and significant workforce reductions are occurring. This divergence highlights the nuanced health of the local economy, showing that while more people are working, immediate financial gains and job security remain volatile for many workers. The sharp increase in proposed and confirmed redundancies compared to the previous year signals potential instability within specific sectors. Although these figures align with pre-pandemic trends, the doubling of redundancy proposals suggests ongoing structural adjustments that could impact future employment stability. This data is crucial for understanding the shifting dynamics of job creation versus job loss in the region. This release is vital for open data because it illustrates how diverse statistical sources—payroll records, surveys, and redundancy reports—must be integrated to form a complete economic picture. By providing accessible, granular metrics on earnings, employment rates, and job losses, such datasets enable researchers and policymakers to track economic trends accurately and develop informed strategies to address labor market disparities.
