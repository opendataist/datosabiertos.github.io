Adopt Open Government policy - CSOs urge Governor Eno

Adopt Open Government policy - CSOs urge Governor Eno

Civil society organizations are urging Akwa Ibom State to join the Open Government Partnership to foster transparent, inclusive governance. By adopting OGP, the state can signal its commitment to accountability, thereby attracting international partnerships and investment. This global mechanism not only enhances a state’s reputation but also bridges the trust gap between citizens and the government, allowing residents to actively monitor public projects and participate in the decision-making process. The advocacy highlights the urgent need to reverse opacity and corruption by integrating citizens into policy implementation from the outset. Stakeholders emphasize that genuine engagement requires more than consultation; it demands that public input directly influences outcomes. Establishing a precedent of fiscal transparency and participatory planning allows citizens to scrutinize resource utilization, ensuring that public funds are judiciously spent and that government agendas truly reflect community priorities. This initiative is crucial for open data because it institutionalizes the availability and accessibility of government information. When a state commits to OGP, it creates a structural framework where data is not merely stored but actively used to empower civil society. This shift transforms citizens from passive observers into active monitors, ensuring that open data serves as a practical tool for accountability, reducing corruption, and driving inclusive development through verified, accessible public records.
