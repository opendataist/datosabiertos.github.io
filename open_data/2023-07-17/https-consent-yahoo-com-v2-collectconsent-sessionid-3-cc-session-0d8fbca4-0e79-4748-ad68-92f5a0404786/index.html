Open Data
ES EN

Cookie consent mechanisms prioritize platform interests, often defaulting to full data harvesting for advertising and analytics rather than user privacy. This practice undermines open data ethics by obscuring how personal information fuels opaque, commercial data ecosystems without meaningful transparency or user control. Such systems reveal the hidden infrastructure of data collection, highlighting a critical gap between stated privacy policies and actual operational practices. For open data advocates, this serves as a stark reminder that true openness requires more than just accessible datasets; it demands ethical, transparent, and user-centric data governance.
