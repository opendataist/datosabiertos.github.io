Confían en que la Corte privilegie controversia

The INAI’s Plenary urges the Supreme Court to authorize its functioning despite the lack of full membership, emphasizing the urgent need to resolve thousands of pending cases concerning access to information and data protection. This operational gap directly threatens citizens’ fundamental rights and the transparency mechanisms essential for open data ecosystems. Failure to restore normal operations signifies a severe regression in democratic accountability and privacy safeguards. The institution’s paralysis undermines public trust in data governance, highlighting the critical importance of maintaining functional oversight bodies to ensure that data remains accessible and protected for society.
