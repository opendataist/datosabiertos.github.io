Remedios Zafra, en A Coruña: «Con la potencia narrativa de la IA urge regular derechos y la propiedad intelectual»

Remedios Zafra, en A Coruña: «Con la potencia narrativa de la IA urge regular derechos y la propiedad intelectual»

Researcher Remedios Zafra examines the disruptive impact of artificial intelligence on cultural industries, highlighting a tension between technological fascination and creative unease. Contrary to expectations that AI would automate bureaucratic tasks, it has instead focused on symbolic and analytical skills, generating fear because it replaces distinctive intellectual labor rather than lightening operational burdens. This transformation is reshaping the labor market toward greater precarity, where technical efficiency may translate into lower remuneration for creators. Additionally, there is a risk of aesthetic homogenization that could undermine cultural uniqueness, raising an ethical dilemma about whether these tools expand human capacity or simply replace it, affecting both producers and consumers of culture. The relevance to open data lies in the urgency of regulating intellectual property and the provenance of the data used to train these systems. By questioning the usefulness of simplistic labels such as “made with AI,” the article underscores the need for transparency regarding the origin and use of knowledge, driving debates on copyright and the ethics of information management in an increasingly automated environment.
