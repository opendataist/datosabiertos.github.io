Guadalajara entra en el Top 10 de las capitales de provincia más transparentes de España con el séptimo puesto nacional

Guadalajara entra en el Top 10 de las capitales de provincia más transparentes de España con el séptimo puesto nacional

Guadalajara has experienced a notable improvement in its transparency rating, rising from 32nd to 7th place among provincial capitals in less than a year. This ascent, consolidated through the updating of the Transparency Portal and strict compliance with current regulations, places the city within the national top ten and as the second most transparent in its autonomous community. The local administration thus demonstrates a firm commitment to accountability and citizens’ access to public information. The results reflect a sustained effort to eliminate administrative opacity and ensure that residents have direct access to data on municipal management, procurement, and finances. The high score in the economic-financial area underscores the soundness of the municipal accounts and the institutional capacity to manage resources rigorously. This positive evolution not only improves the city’s external ranking but also strengthens trust between citizens and institutions through openness and clarity in the information provided. This case is particularly relevant to open data, as it practically demonstrates how the systematic and structured publication of public information drives institutional transparency. By prioritizing data accessibility, the municipality not only complies with the law but also sets a quality standard that fosters accountability and citizen participation. The example illustrates that proactive transparency is a key driver for improving urban management and the perception of public integrity, serving as a model for other administrations seeking to integrate these principles into their daily governance.
