Google abre oficialmente Dataset Search con 25 millones de conjuntos de datos abiertos sobre todo tipo de temas

Google abre oficialmente Dataset Search con 25 millones de conjuntos de datos abiertos sobre todo tipo de temas

Google has officially launched Dataset Search, a search engine that indexes millions of open datasets from governments, science, and the private sector. This democratizes access to public information, making it easier to discover without needing to know specific repositories. The platform integrates diverse sources, ranging from demographic statistics to urban data, expanding the possibilities for analysis. By centralizing discovery, it reduces technical barriers to accessing valuable information, promoting its reuse by citizens and researchers. This tool is relevant to open data because it increases the visibility and impact of open datasets. By improving discoverability, it fosters a culture of transparency and enables society to better leverage available collective knowledge to innovate and make informed decisions.
