¿Qué cambia con la “Ley Antimemes”? Advierten riesgos para el contenido en internet

¿Qué cambia con la “Ley Antimemes”? Advierten riesgos para el contenido en internet

The Mexican reform on intellectual property and artificial intelligence has raised concerns about its implications for digital freedoms. While it aims to combat fraud and misinformation by regulating the unauthorized use of voice and image, its implementation presents significant shortcomings that could restrict creative expression. The "notice and takedown" mechanism forces platforms to remove content under the threat of severe fines, without legal safeguards against false reports or clear protections for fair use, such as satire. This asymmetry incentivizes preemptive censorship, turning providers into de facto censors who withhold legitimate publications out of fear of financial penalties. This approach is relevant to open data because the lack of transparency and excessive technological regulation hinder access to cultural and creative information. By prioritizing restriction over a balance of rights, it obstructs the ethical sharing of data and limits open innovation, affecting the availability of digital resources for society.
