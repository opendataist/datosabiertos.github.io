Comida y cigarros, principales gastos

Comida y cigarros, principales gastos

The National Expenditure Survey reveals that Chiapas has one of the most precarious household economies in the country, with the majority of its resources allocated to food. This pattern highlights the deep structural inequalities that persist in the region, particularly when contrasting the low national spending with local realities. An analysis of disaggregated data exposes a significant gap between urban and rural areas, revealing a marked socioeconomic duality. These differences directly impact access to basic services and quality of life, demonstrating how geography influences the material well-being of households. This report is vital for the open data sector, as it facilitates transparency regarding the distribution of wealth and consumption. By making detailed and accessible information public, it empowers researchers and citizens to demand more equitable public policies, grounded in clear evidence of the population's actual needs.
