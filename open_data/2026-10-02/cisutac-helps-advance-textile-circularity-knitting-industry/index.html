CISUTAC helps advance textile circularity - Knitting Industry

CISUTAC helps advance textile circularity - Knitting Industry

The CISUTAC project successfully concluded its four-year initiative by delivering comprehensive technologies, open data, and policy recommendations to advance the circularity of the European textile sector. By testing innovative repair, disassembly, and recycling methods under real-world conditions, the project demonstrated that practical implementation can significantly enhance material recovery rates. This hands-on approach moved beyond theoretical studies, proving that reducing processing times and ensuring yarn strength are feasible steps toward a sustainable industry. Central to this progress was the release of open tools and datasets designed to streamline textile sorting and improve consistency. These resources allow stakeholders to access critical information that facilitates faster and more accurate recycling processes. Additionally, extensive consumer research identified key factors influencing purchasing and reuse habits, providing evidence-based strategies to encourage more circular consumption patterns among the public. The project also formulated robust policy recommendations covering waste management, ecodesign, and producer responsibility. By fostering collaboration across the value chain and establishing the ECOSYSTEX community, CISUTAC ensures ongoing knowledge sharing. This relevance to open_data lies in its commitment to transparency, providing the sector with shared, accessible evidence and digital tools necessary for informed decision-making and systemic transformation toward sustainability.
