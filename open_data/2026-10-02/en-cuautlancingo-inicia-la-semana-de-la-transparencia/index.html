En Cuautlancingo inicia la Semana de la Transparencia

En Cuautlancingo inicia la Semana de la Transparencia

Cuautlancingo promotes transparency by training public officials in access to information and data protection. This initiative ensures that staff have the necessary tools to be accountable and comply with current legal obligations. The focus on citizen participation and sanctions for non-compliance reinforces an integrity-driven public management approach. Accountability is prioritized as a central pillar for building institutional trust with society. This case is relevant to open data by demonstrating that digital transparency requires trained personnel. Continuous training ensures that public data are accessible, understandable, and properly used, fostering an open and responsible administration.
