El ingreso por persona promedio fue inferior a $700.000 en el segundo trimestre, informó Indec - Diario San Rafael

El ingreso por persona promedio fue inferior a $700.000 en el segundo trimestre, informó Indec - Diario San Rafael

The INDEC report reveals a decline in per capita household income during the second quarter, reflecting a contraction in the purchasing power of Argentine households. This quarter-on-quarter reduction signals a recent economic weakening, affecting the availability of resources for the population surveyed by the Permanent Household Survey, and highlighting volatility in material well-being levels. It is crucial to highlight deep structural gaps, such as gender-based wage inequality and the concentration of non-labor income among the most vulnerable strata. These data demonstrate that the income distribution structure is not homogeneous, but rather marked by systemic disparities that disproportionately affect women and lower-income sectors, who rely more on transfers than on formal employment. This information is fundamental to the field of open data, as the publication of high-resolution official statistics enables civil society and researchers to audit the accuracy of public policies. By making these indicators accessible, critical analysis of inequity is facilitated, promoting transparency and allowing the community to use empirical evidence to demand changes in wealth distribution and improve social governance.
