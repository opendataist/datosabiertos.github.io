Marruecos supera a China como socio comercial de Estados Unidos

Marruecos supera a China como socio comercial de Estados Unidos

Morocco has emerged as the United States’ primary trading partner in 2026, surpassing China. This realignment reflects the adaptation of supply chains to the current geopolitical landscape, prioritizing strategic alliances over rival markets. U.S. exports have grown exponentially, underscoring a shared desire to adjust economic flows to new international demands. However, a significant trade imbalance persists, with U.S. sales to Morocco far exceeding Moroccan imports. Although Moroccan exports have shown moderate progress in key sectors such as energy and aerospace, the asymmetry in bilateral trade flows continues to widen. This scenario poses challenges for Rabat as it seeks to consolidate its presence without compromising national competitiveness in the face of external influences. The relevance of this article to open data lies in the need for transparency in trade indicators. Accurate analysis of these economic flows, based on accessible official data, enables an assessment of the effectiveness of free trade agreements. The availability of detailed statistics is crucial for understanding how geoeconomic dynamics impact contemporary multilateral trade relations.
