The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

The Key To Better Visual Inspection Practices

InQuest Science has introduced The Identifier Software, a specialized tool designed to bridge critical regulatory gaps in pharmaceutical visual inspection. By consolidating fragmented data from raw materials to finished products, the system enables manufacturers to establish robust quality control frameworks. This integration allows for comprehensive oversight of the entire product lifecycle, ensuring that inconsistencies are detected early to prevent costly batch recalls and maintain strict adherence to global health standards. A core advantage of this solution is its specialized focus on defect management, which surpasses the capabilities of generic Manufacturing Execution Systems. The software provides a centralized, digital defect library and facilitates rigorous training and qualification for both human inspectors and automated vision systems. By using blinded test sets and detailed analytics, it enhances inspection accuracy, minimizes false positives, and ensures that workforce competencies meet the precise expectations of regulatory bodies like the FDA. For the open data community, this implementation highlights the transformative potential of structured, digitized quality records in regulated industries. It demonstrates how replacing manual, paper-based processes with secure, interoperable digital repositories improves data integrity and accessibility. This approach not only reduces administrative burdens and human error but also creates a transparent audit trail, proving that open, standardized data management is essential for achieving efficiency and compliance in high-stakes manufacturing environments.
