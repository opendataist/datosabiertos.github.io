Resource Watch: nuestro planeta, sus recursos y sociedades vistas como mapas y datos

Resource Watch integrates global open data with interactive maps to visualize critical issues such as the environment and inequality. This platform democratizes access to information, enabling citizens, researchers, and media to explore and combine complex datasets through accessible and transparent tools. The project stands out for its commitment to total openness, including publishing its code on GitHub and providing contribution guidelines, which fosters community collaboration and the reuse of information. It is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how geospatial visualization and technical transparency enhance accountability and public understanding of global challenges, transforming raw data into actionable knowledge.
