Credit register keeps names for too long, privacy watchdog says - DutchNews.nl

Credit register keeps names for too long, privacy watchdog says - DutchNews.nl

The Dutch privacy watchdog argues that current debt registration rules are disproportionate, forcing thousands of cleared debtors to remain on official lists for years. This practice severely hinders their ability to secure new loans or mortgages, creating absurd barriers to financial reintegration that fail to reflect their actual solvency. Despite proposed reductions in the notification period, the core issue persists: data should not linger after obligations are met. The watch dog insists that legitimate debtors should have zero records, highlighting the ethical failure of maintaining negative markers for those who have fully resolved their financial history. This case is vital for open data advocacy, as it underscores the risks of opaque credit registries. It demonstrates how data retention policies can disproportionately harm individuals when transparency and automatic expungement are lacking, reinforcing the need for stricter data minimization principles in public and private sectors.
