Se realizará una Jornada de Gobierno Abierto y Municipios en Paraná

Se realizará una Jornada de Gobierno Abierto y Municipios en Paraná

The Municipality of Paraná is hosting an event as part of Open Government Week to promote transparency and civic collaboration. This initiative reinforces the commitment to an administration that is not only effective but also deeply responsive to community needs. By sharing experiences with local stakeholders, the goal is to strengthen policies that ensure inclusivity and accountability in public management. Central to this effort is the Open Government Portal, launched in late 2021, which serves as a critical tool for active citizenship. It provides access to interactive maps, open data, and citizen budgets, significantly reducing bureaucratic barriers through digitalization. By making public management data easily accessible, the municipality ensures that the information generated serves as a foundation for trust, allowing residents to monitor and understand how public resources are utilized. This focus on open data is highly relevant because it transforms raw government statistics into actionable public knowledge. By mandating the public disclosure of asset declarations and salary structures, the administration upholds ethical standards and fiscal responsibility. Ultimately, this approach demonstrates how leveraging technology and open principles can foster a culture of transparency, empowering citizens to participate meaningfully in democratic oversight and local governance.
