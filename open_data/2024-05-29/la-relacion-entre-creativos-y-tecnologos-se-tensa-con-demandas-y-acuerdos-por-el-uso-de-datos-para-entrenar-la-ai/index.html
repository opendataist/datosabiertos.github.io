La relación entre creativos y tecnólogos se tensa con demandas y acuerdos por el uso de datos para entrenar la AI

La relación entre creativos y tecnólogos se tensa con demandas y acuerdos por el uso de datos para entrenar la AI

The rapid evolution of artificial intelligence has sparked a growing conflict between technology developers and content creators. Large companies are aggressively seeking access to original data, including copyrighted artistic works, to train their models. This intense interest has triggered significant backlash, as artists and creative industries feel their intellectual property is being used without consent, creating ongoing tension over ownership and the ethical use of information. The relevance to open data lies in the fundamental tension between the massive availability of information and the protection of individual rights. While the open data philosophy promotes free access to foster innovation, the current reality shows that the indiscriminate collection of data for training algorithms can violate creators’ autonomy and rights. This situation forces a rethinking of how data is managed and shared, highlighting the need to balance technological utility with respect for intellectual property and privacy. In conclusion, the article emphasizes that technological expansion cannot ignore the ethical implications surrounding the sources of its data. The current struggle reveals that transparency and consent are crucial for maintaining trust in the digital ecosystem. If companies continue to operate without addressing creators’ concerns, the movement toward truly open and ethical data will be undermined. Therefore, it is essential to establish clear frameworks that respect both technological progress and individual rights.
