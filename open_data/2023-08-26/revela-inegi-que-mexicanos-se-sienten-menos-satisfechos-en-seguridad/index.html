Revela Inegi que mexicanos se sienten menos satisfechos en seguridad

The INEGI report highlights a paradox in Mexico: urban adults report high overall life satisfaction, yet express deep dissatisfaction with their city, country, and safety. This disparity reveals that while personal and economic domains meet expectations, public security remains a critical failure, significantly undermining collective well-being. Understanding this gap is crucial for open data initiatives because it demonstrates how aggregated statistics can mask distinct civic grievances. When citizens rate their environment poorly despite high personal contentment, it signals that traditional economic metrics fail to capture the true quality of public life and institutional trust. This data is relevant to open data efforts as it underscores the necessity of granular, multidimensional indicators. By making such specific satisfaction metrics openly available, policymakers and researchers can better identify structural failures in governance, moving beyond general well-being scores to address the specific areas where citizens feel most vulnerable and disengaged.
