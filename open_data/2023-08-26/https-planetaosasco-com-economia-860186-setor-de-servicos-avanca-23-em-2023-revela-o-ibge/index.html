The Brazilian service sector demonstrated resilient growth in 2023, marking its third consecutive year of expansion. This sustained performance indicates a robust post-pandemic recovery, driven by significant contributions from information technology, logistics, and professional services. While the growth rate moderated compared to the explosive rebounds seen in previous years, achieving triple-digit gains over a triennium remains a historic milestone, highlighting the sector's structural strengthening and increased capacity to contribute to the national economy. Crucially, services provided to households surpassed their pre-pandemic levels for the first time in December 2023, signaling a full consumer recovery. This shift reflects a redistribution of household income toward consumption and service usage, partially offsetting the lingering effects of remote work trends. The recovery in this specific segment underscores changing consumer behaviors and the adaptive nature of local service markets, which have successfully integrated new delivery models and maintained momentum in tourism and hospitality despite evolving work patterns. This data is highly relevant to open_data because it exemplifies how standardized, publicly available statistical indicators enable transparent economic monitoring. By providing granular, sector-specific, and geographically distributed metrics, the IBGE’s research allows analysts to track real-time economic health and validate hypotheses about post-pandemic structural shifts. Such accessible data empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions based on verified trends rather than estimates, fostering accountability and deeper understanding of how service industries drive national development.
