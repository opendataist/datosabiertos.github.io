Seguridad ciudadana, rubro de menor satisfacción entre los mexicanos | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Public satisfaction with security in Mexico is critically low, highlighting a significant gap in civic well-being. This data reveals widespread discontent with public services, contrasting sharply with higher personal life satisfaction scores. The disparity underscores systemic failures in maintaining public order. When citizens feel unsafe, it erodes trust in institutions and diminishes overall quality of life. This trend indicates an urgent need for policy reform and improved governance strategies. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, quantitative evidence of social challenges. By making these metrics publicly available, stakeholders can better analyze trends and advocate for solutions that prioritize citizen safety and improved public service delivery.
