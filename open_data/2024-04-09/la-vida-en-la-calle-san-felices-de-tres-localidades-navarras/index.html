La vida en la calle San Felices de tres localidades navarras

The article highlights that Navarra leads Spain in citizen happiness, a finding validated by national statistics and the United Nations’ recognition of well-being as a fundamental human goal. This statistic challenges traditional economic metrics, suggesting that simple, everyday stimuli and authenticity are key to sustained joy rather than material wealth. By documenting personal stories linked to streets named “San Felices,” the narrative illustrates how happiness emerges from ordinary life, such as farming, writing, or overcoming dictatorship. These individual experiences provide qualitative depth to the aggregate data, showing that well-being is rooted in simplicity, community, and personal freedom. This piece is relevant to open data because it advocates for broader societal indicators beyond GDP. It demonstrates how open government statistics, when combined with human-centric storytelling, can drive policy shifts toward more inclusive and equitable growth models that truly measure and promote the quality of life for all citizens.
