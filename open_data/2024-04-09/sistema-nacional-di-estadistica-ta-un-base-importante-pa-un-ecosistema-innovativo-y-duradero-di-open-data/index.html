Sistema Nacional di Estadistica ta un base importante pa un ecosistema innovativo y duradero di 'Open Data'

Minister Geoffrey Wever underscores the urgent need to establish a National Statistics System in Aruba to support sustainable development and democratic transparency. Global standards emphasize that reliable, impartial data is essential for effective government decision-making and financial oversight. Without a coordinated system for compiling and exchanging statistical information, the government cannot accurately monitor policy outcomes or adjust strategies, leaving a critical gap in public administration. The proposed Commission aims to implement a robust infrastructure that ensures data meets international criteria for reliability and accessibility. This initiative seeks to foster structural collaboration among various government departments and institutions responsible for data collection. By unifying these efforts, Aruba can create an efficient ecosystem where information is not only accurate but also presented in a manner understandable to the general public, thereby enhancing trust in institutional processes. This development is highly relevant to open data, as it directly addresses the foundational requirements for making public information available and usable. The minister explicitly aims to integrate open data principles while respecting legal and confidentiality constraints. By creating a sustainable data ecosystem involving government, academia, and the private sector, Aruba moves toward a model where transparency and citizens’ rights to information are systematically guaranteed through standardized, accessible statistical practices.
