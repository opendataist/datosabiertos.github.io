Deben transparentar permiso para antígenos

The INAI ruled that Cofepris must provide the full public resolution regarding Pfizer’s vaccine authorization, rejecting the agency's claim that the information does not exist. This decision highlights the necessity for administrative bodies to conduct exhaustive searches across all relevant units, rather than offering partial responses. This case underscores the critical importance of accountability and transparency in health governance. By enforcing complete access to regulatory decisions, the ruling ensures that public data remains accessible, fostering trust in institutional processes. Such transparency is vital for open data initiatives, as it guarantees that citizens can access comprehensive information. This promotes informed public discourse and supports the broader goal of making government operations and health data fully open and verifiable for everyone.
