Encuentros abiertos para una América abierta (UY sede de ABRELATAM y CONDATOS)

Uruguay hosts a regional conference to sustain open data policies and strengthen democracy through integrated strategies. This gathering is relevant to open data as it fosters regional collaboration, promotes transparency, and establishes frameworks for the ethical use of data across Latin America.
