María Gil: "Los último datos publicados de la encuesta de población activa en CLM reflejan la desmesurada tasa de paro que existe en la región"

The article highlights a severe economic and social crisis in Castilla-La Mancha, characterized by unemployment rates that significantly exceed European averages, particularly among youth and women. It emphasizes that a large portion of the population faces poverty and social exclusion, indicating that current political strategies fail to address the fundamental difficulties young people encounter in achieving independence and stable employment. This situation reflects a broader disconnect between governmental actions and the urgent needs of citizens who are struggling with precarious working conditions and limited opportunities. Relevance to open data lies in the necessity of transparent, accessible statistical evidence to hold authorities accountable for such critical socio-economic indicators. By making labor market and demographic data openly available, stakeholders can verify claims about unemployment, poverty risks, and gender disparities, thereby enabling informed public debate. The article illustrates how official statistics serve as a crucial tool for opposition parties to challenge government narratives, underscoring the role of open data in fostering political transparency and ensuring that policy decisions are grounded in verifiable reality rather than ideological agendas or fabricated economic forecasts. Furthermore, the critique extends to regional budget planning, which is accused of relying on unrealistic revenue projections and perpetuating underfunded services in healthcare and infrastructure. The argument suggests that without rigorous data analysis, governments may present budgets that prioritize political maneuvering over genuine public welfare. This reinforces the importance of open data not just for monitoring existing conditions but also for auditing future fiscal plans, ensuring that public resources are allocated efficiently and equitably to meet the actual needs of the community rather than satisfying partisan interests.
