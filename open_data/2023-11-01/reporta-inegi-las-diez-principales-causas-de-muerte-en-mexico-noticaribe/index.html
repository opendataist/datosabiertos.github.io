Reporta Inegi las diez principales causas de muerte en México... - Noticaribe

Mexico’s 2022 mortality rates showed a significant decline compared to the pandemic peaks of 2020 and 2021, yet figures remain elevated relative to pre-pandemic baselines. This context highlights the enduring long-term health impact of the global health crisis, illustrating how emergency data can reveal lingering societal vulnerabilities even after acute phases subside. Cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of death, surpassing COVID-19, which has dropped to fifth place. While the total number of deaths decreased, the overall excess mortality accumulated over the three-year pandemic period underscores a substantial deviation from historical trends. This shift in cause-specific rankings demonstrates how public health priorities and data tracking must adapt to evolving epidemiological landscapes rather than focusing solely on pandemic-specific metrics. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the critical role of transparent, definitive statistical reporting in understanding public health trends. By utilizing rigorous methodologies and making comprehensive mortality data accessible, institutions like INEGI enable researchers and policymakers to move beyond raw counts. Open data facilitates deeper analysis of demographic shifts and health disparities, ensuring that decision-making is grounded in accurate, longitudinal evidence rather than isolated snapshots.
