Política de Privacidad

This privacy policy outlines how FinanzasDigital.com manages personal data, emphasizing a commitment to mutual trust and strict data protection. The organization adheres to core principles such as loyalty, transparency, and data minimization, ensuring that only essential information is processed for specified purposes. Users retain full control over their information, with robust technical and organizational measures implemented to guarantee integrity and confidentiality. The platform collects personal details and technical data for service management, statistical analysis, and newsletter subscriptions, while explicitly prohibiting the sale or unauthorized sharing of user information. It highlights significant user rights, including access, rectification, and deletion, alongside strict safeguards against collecting data from minors or sensitive financial information. Transparency is maintained through clear communication regarding data storage, security protocols, and incident notifications. This document is relevant to open data as it exemplifies the critical balance between data utility and individual privacy rights. It demonstrates how organizations can leverage data for analytics and service improvement while respecting user autonomy and legal standards. By prioritizing consent and security, it provides a model for ethical data governance, showing that responsible data handling does not hinder functionality but rather fosters public trust in digital ecosystems.
