CES 2024: conferencias magistrales ‘Keynote’ → TransMedia

The Consumer Technology Association has announced the keynote conference program for CES 2024, highlighting technological convergence in critical sectors. Business leaders explore how innovation enhances daily life, sustainability in beauty and commerce, and predictive transformation in health and finance. This event underscores the need for transparency and standardization in the technologies presented, as they drive structural changes in global industries. The alignment of discussions reflects trends toward inclusive and efficient solutions, essential for responsible digital advancement. The relevance to open data lies in the demand for accessible information about these innovations. For society to fully benefit from technological transformation, it is crucial that manufacturers and platforms share structured data, thereby ensuring a clear and verifiable understanding of the advances shaping the future economic and social landscape.
