PSA releases 45th issue of 'Window on Economic Statistics of Qatar'

PSA releases 45th issue of 'Window on Economic Statistics of Qatar'

The Planning and Statistics Authority in Qatar released its latest economic report, consolidating key macroeconomic indicators to support policy and decision-making. This initiative highlights the critical role of accessible, comprehensive data in enabling informed governance and strategic planning. By comparing Qatar’s performance against international peers and analyzing quarterly trends in GDP and trade, the publication offers transparent insights into economic health. Such benchmarking allows stakeholders to evaluate national progress effectively, fostering accountability and better resource allocation through open data practices. A significant addition is the new Wage Indices article, detailing the methodology for constructing a wage basket. This effort to standardize and publish wage statistics demonstrates a commitment to expanding the depth and utility of public datasets. These transparent metrics empower researchers and citizens to understand labor market dynamics, thereby enhancing the overall value of open economic data ecosystems.
