"Damos un paso más allá": Gobierno presenta instructivo para uso responsable de la inteligencia artificial en el Estado

Chile has established ethical guidelines for the use of artificial intelligence in the public sector, marking a significant step forward in its digital transformation. This directive aims to ensure that the implementation of these powerful technologies is carried out responsibly, prioritizing citizen well-being and transparency in state operations. The regulation is grounded in four essential pillars: people-centeredness, system explainability, privacy protection, and continuous training for public officials. These guidelines not only provide general recommendations but also lay the foundation for a more robust regulatory framework that enables anticipation of challenges and coordination of effective actions among the various state institutions. This approach is particularly relevant to the open data movement, as it requires that algorithms and the data feeding them be transparent and explainable. By mandating privacy protection and accountability, these policies promote an ecosystem in which technology serves the public interest without compromising citizens' rights, thereby facilitating more reliable and accessible digital governance.
