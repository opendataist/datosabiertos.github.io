El Gobierno regional reconoce el modelo de mejora del Portal de Transparencia de la UCLM

El Gobierno regional reconoce el modelo de mejora del Portal de Transparencia de la UCLM

The University of Castilla-La Mancha received an excellence award for enhancing its transparency portal through periodic external evaluations. This approach identifies strengths and weaknesses to refine services, ensuring they align with regulatory requirements and citizen needs. As a result, the university achieved a high-level transparency certification, demonstrating the effectiveness of its implemented quality management system in handling public information. This recognition highlights the critical role of continuous assessment in maintaining open data standards. By adapting services based on external feedback, institutions can enhance accountability and public trust. The award underscores that transparency is not static but requires ongoing improvement to remain relevant and effective in serving the public interest. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates how structured quality management drives better transparency practices. It shows that systematic evaluation leads to more responsive and accessible public services. Ultimately, such initiatives promote a culture of openness, where data serves as a tool for democratic engagement and efficient governance, benefiting both institutions and citizens.
