Lola, Manuel o Carmen: estos son los nombres más comunes de recién nacidos en Andalucía en el último año

The article highlights the INE’s annual report on newborn names in Spain for 2022, revealing regional preferences where traditional choices often dominate over original ones. In Andalusia, classic names like Manuel, María, and Lucía remain the most popular, reflecting cultural continuity and familial naming conventions. This dataset exemplifies open data by providing transparent, accessible statistical information from public institutions. It allows researchers, journalists, and citizens to explore demographic trends, cultural shifts, and societal values embedded in personal identity choices. Open data initiatives are crucial here because they democratize access to vital social statistics. By making such granular demographic records available, society can better understand population dynamics, inform policy decisions, and appreciate the evolving landscape of cultural heritage and modern naming trends.
