Michigan Secretary of State debuts FOIA portal

Michigan introduces a centralized online portal for Freedom of Information Act requests, significantly enhancing public access to government documents. This initiative streamlines the process, allowing citizens to easily request, receive, and search previously released records, thereby promoting greater transparency and accountability in state operations. The platform represents a major step toward open data principles by removing barriers to information and centralizing public records. By making these resources widely available at no cost, the state empowers citizens to better understand and engage with government activities, setting a precedent for other executive agencies. Although currently exempt, the governor’s office and legislature are urged to comply with similar requests. Expanding FOIA coverage to these branches would further strengthen democratic oversight, ensuring that all government entities operate with the same level of openness and accessibility expected by the public.
