Las ventas minoristas en EEUU suben un 0,3% en noviembre, más de lo esperado

The article highlights a modest recovery in U.S. retail sales and a resilient labor market, yet underscores underlying vulnerabilities that point to an impending economic slowdown. Retail growth exceeding expectations suggests consumer resilience, but analysts warn that high interest rates and a cooling job market will likely dampen future spending, signaling that the peak of economic activity has likely passed. At the same time, while initial jobless claims remain historically low, rising claims for continued unemployment indicate that workers are facing greater difficulty securing new positions. This divergence suggests a labor market that is technically healthy but gradually deteriorating, with wage growth persisting at levels that continue to challenge inflation targets, despite recent Federal Reserve signals of paused rate hikes.
