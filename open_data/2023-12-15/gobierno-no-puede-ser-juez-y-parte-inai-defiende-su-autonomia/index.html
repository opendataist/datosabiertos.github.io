Gobierno no puede ser juez y parte: INAI defiende su autonomía

The INAI asserts its independence against executive proposals to dissolve it, arguing that the government cannot simultaneously regulate and adjudicate transparency matters. This conflict threatens the rule of law and democratic foundations in Mexico by removing the impartial oversight necessary for fair administration. Maintaining an autonomous body is crucial to ensure that access to information and personal data protection remain robust, regardless of administrative changes. The INAI emphasizes that its specialized, neutral function is irreplaceable by other executive or legislative entities to prevent regression in human rights safeguards. This article highlights the critical role of open data infrastructure in sustaining democratic accountability. Autonomous institutions act as essential guardians of public information, ensuring citizens’ rights are protected against political interference. Preserving such independence remains vital for the integrity and accessibility of government data in a healthy democracy.
