The Northern Ireland Road Network and Condition Statistics 2022-23 report has been published today

Official statistics on Northern Ireland’s road network reveal a vast infrastructure predominantly composed of rural and unclassified roads. The data highlights that while the majority of major arterial routes remain in good condition with long remaining structural life, a significant volume of surface defects still requires ongoing repair attention to maintain safety and efficiency. Financial analysis indicates a recent reduction in total road expenditure, reflecting shifts in budgeting priorities between structural maintenance and new construction projects. This fiscal trend suggests a strategic focus on preserving existing assets rather than expanding the network, which has broader implications for long-term infrastructure sustainability and public service allocation. This report is highly relevant to open data enthusiasts as it exemplifies the value of transparent, standardized government publishing. By providing granular metrics on length, condition, and costs, such datasets enable independent verification of government performance and empower citizens and researchers to analyze transport policy impacts effectively.
