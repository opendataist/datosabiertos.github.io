Llegada de turistas a Portugal alcanza nueva cifra récord

Llegada de turistas a Portugal alcanza nueva cifra récord

Portugal sets historic tourism records, highlighting the sector's vital economic contribution. This success underscores the importance of accurate, accessible statistical data for monitoring national performance and identifying emerging market trends effectively. However, the boom exacerbates housing crises and local discontent. Transparent information on tourism impacts is essential for policymakers to address these socio-economic challenges, ensuring that growth does not come at the expense of residents' well-being. Open data enables this critical balance by providing the evidence needed for informed governance. By making tourism and housing metrics publicly available, stakeholders can collaborate on sustainable strategies that preserve quality of life while maintaining economic vitality, demonstrating the power of data transparency in complex societal issues.
