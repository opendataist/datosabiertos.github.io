RTI has strengthened Ghana’s commitment to accountable governance- Akufo-Addo | Ghana News Agency

RTI has strengthened Ghana’s commitment to accountable governance- Akufo-Addo | Ghana News Agency

Ghana’s Right to Information Act has significantly strengthened the nation’s commitment to transparent and accountable governance. By legally empowering citizens to scrutinize public institutions, the legislation fosters deeper civic engagement and ensures that leaders remain answerable to the people. This legal framework transforms access to information from a privilege into a fundamental tool for democratic participation and social equity. The implementation of dedicated Information Units within public bodies has operationalized this right, allowing individuals to effectively monitor development projects and the utilization of state resources. Supported by digital infrastructure investments, these measures ensure that no citizen is excluded from the information age. Consequently, informed citizens can actively contribute to governance, driving more effective and equitable public service delivery across the country. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it establishes the legal and structural foundation necessary for the systematic release and accessibility of public information. Recognized by international bodies like UNESCO, Ghana’s approach underscores information as a global public good. The country’s efforts demonstrate how robust open data policies can enhance freedom of expression and build sustainable, just societies by mainstreaming participation in the public sector.
