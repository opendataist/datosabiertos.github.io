Apartment collapse demolishes Labor's housing target

Apartment collapse demolishes Labor's housing target

Australia’s housing construction sector is collapsing due to soaring costs, labor shortages, and plummeting builder profitability. This downturn is most evident in a dramatic decline in apartment approvals, particularly for high-rise units across major eastern states, leaving the market at multi-year lows. Consequently, the federal government’s ambitious target to build 1.2 million homes over five years is unachievable with current trends. The significant gap between actual approvals and required volumes highlights a systemic failure to produce sufficient housing supply without fundamental structural changes. This crisis underscores the critical relevance of open data in policy making. Transparent access to construction statistics allows stakeholders to identify market failures early. It demonstrates how data-driven insights are essential for challenging political targets and advocating for realistic solutions, such as adjusting migration levels to align with actual building capacity.
