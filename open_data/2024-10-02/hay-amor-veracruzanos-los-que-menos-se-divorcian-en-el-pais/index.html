Hay amor: Veracruzanos, los que menos se divorcian en el país

Veracruz consistently records the lowest divorce rates nationwide, highlighting unique regional sociological dynamics. This longitudinal data demonstrates how consistent demographic patterns can be tracked over time, offering valuable insights for understanding social stability. The dataset provides a clear comparative framework across all Mexican states, identifying both extremes in marital dissolution. Such granular, standardized national statistics are essential for researchers and policymakers to analyze family structures and societal trends comprehensively. This information is crucial for open data initiatives because it showcases transparent, accessible government statistics. By making these records public, INEGI enables independent analysis and fosters accountability, allowing the community to explore the implications of marital laws and social behaviors without barriers.
