Retail sales bounce reduces chance of interest rate cuts

Unseasonably warm August weather drove a significant surge in Australian retail sales, surpassing economic forecasts. This unexpected strength in consumer demand suggests a robust economy, potentially reducing the likelihood of immediate interest rate cuts by the central bank.
