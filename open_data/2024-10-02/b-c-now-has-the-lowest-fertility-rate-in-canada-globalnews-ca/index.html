B.C. now has the lowest fertility rate in Canada | Globalnews.ca

B.C. now has the lowest fertility rate in Canada | Globalnews.ca

Canada’s fertility rate has plummeted to an all-time low, signaling a demographic shift with significant long-term implications. Smaller family networks will strain economic and emotional support systems, particularly for the aging population. This data highlights the urgent need for open data to track such profound societal changes. Transparent, accessible statistics are essential for researchers and policymakers to analyze the underlying causes, such as the cost of living, and understand the broader impact on social structures. By making this information widely available, open data fosters informed public discourse. It enables a deeper understanding of how demographic trends affect community resilience and future planning, ensuring that societal responses are grounded in accurate, comprehensive evidence rather than speculation.
