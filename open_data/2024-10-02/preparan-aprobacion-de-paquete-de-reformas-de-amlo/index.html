Preparan aprobación de paquete de reformas de AMLO

Preparan aprobación de paquete de reformas de AMLO

The article highlights the imminent approval of constitutional reforms, including the dissolution of seven autonomous bodies such as the INAI. This move directly threatens institutional transparency, a core pillar of open data governance, by dismantling key mechanisms that ensure public access to information and data protection. Furthermore, the text notes upcoming administrative changes under the new presidency, signaling a continued shift in governance priorities. These structural alterations may further impact the availability and independence of public data, raising concerns about future government accountability and the robustness of open data frameworks. This is relevant to open data because eliminating the INAI removes the primary guarantor of citizens' right to access state information. The weakening of such institutions risks reducing government transparency, thereby hindering the public’s ability to obtain, analyze, and utilize essential data for democratic oversight and civic engagement.
