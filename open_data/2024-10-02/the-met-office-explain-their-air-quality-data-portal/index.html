The Met Office explain their Air Quality Data Portal

The Met Office launched an Air Quality Data Portal, enabling researchers and the public to access and analyze critical environmental information. This open data initiative promotes transparency and supports evidence-based decision-making regarding air quality. By providing accessible tools, it empowers the community to understand and address pollution challenges effectively.
