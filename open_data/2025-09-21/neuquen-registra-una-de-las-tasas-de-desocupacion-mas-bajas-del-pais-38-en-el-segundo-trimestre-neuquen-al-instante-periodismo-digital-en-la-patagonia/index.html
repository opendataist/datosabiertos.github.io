Neuquén registra una de las tasas de desocupación más bajas del país: 3,8% en el segundo trimestre | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

Neuquén registra una de las tasas de desocupación más bajas del país: 3,8% en el segundo trimestre | Neuquén Al Instante | Periodismo Digital en la Patagonia

Neuquén demonstrates exceptional labor market resilience in contrast to the national slowdown, maintaining an unemployment rate significantly below the national average. This stability is driven by formal job growth in the energy and construction sectors, highlighting how resource-rich regions can defy broader economic stagnation. However, high-quality employment remains elusive. Significant challenges include worker dissatisfaction, underemployment, and persistent gender gaps in both wages and access to strategic industries. These issues reveal that while job quantity is high, the quality and equity of employment opportunities require urgent attention to ensure inclusive growth. This case is vital for open data, illustrating how public statistics must capture nuanced labor dynamics beyond headline numbers. Transparent data reveals hidden inequalities such as underemployment and gender disparities, enabling policymakers to design targeted interventions. It underscores the necessity of granular, accessible datasets to understand regional economic health and guide effective, equitable public policy decisions.
