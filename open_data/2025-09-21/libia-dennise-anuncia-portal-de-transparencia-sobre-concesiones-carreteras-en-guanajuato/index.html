Libia Dennise anuncia portal de transparencia sobre concesiones carreteras en Guanajuato

Libia Dennise anuncia portal de transparencia sobre concesiones carreteras en Guanajuato

The government of Guanajuato is launching a transparency portal to publish details about highway concessions, aiming to provide citizens with clarity and certainty regarding strategic infrastructure projects. This proactive measure underscores a commitment to open governance, ensuring that information is disclosed responsibly without hindering project development or public benefits. At the same time, work has begun on a high-speed interurban train connecting Guanajuato and Querétaro. This major mobility initiative seeks to reduce travel times, stimulate economic activity, and create significant employment opportunities across the region. The project promises safer transfers and increased accessibility for millions of potential annual travelers. These developments are relevant to open data as they demonstrate how governments can leverage transparency portals to foster trust and accountability in large-scale public works. By proactively sharing critical infrastructure information, authorities empower citizens with accessible data, promoting informed public engagement and ensuring that technological investments in mobility are transparent and beneficial to society.
