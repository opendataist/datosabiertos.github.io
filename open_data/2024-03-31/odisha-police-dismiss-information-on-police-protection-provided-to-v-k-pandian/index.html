Odisha Police dismiss information on police protection provided to V. K. Pandian

The article highlights the tension between transparency and state secrecy under Right to Information laws. It centers on conflicting claims regarding police deployment for a political figure, exposing how authorities may obscure resource allocation details when challenged by activists. This dispute underscores the critical role of RTI in holding governments accountable for public spending and security protocols. The incident is relevant to open data as it demonstrates the friction inherent in accessing government information. It illustrates how robust freedom of information mechanisms are essential for validating official claims and maintaining public trust in democratic institutions, especially during sensitive electoral periods.
