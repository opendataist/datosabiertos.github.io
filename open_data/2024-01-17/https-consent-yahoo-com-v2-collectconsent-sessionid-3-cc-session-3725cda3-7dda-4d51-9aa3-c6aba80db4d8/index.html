This privacy policy outlines standard consent mechanisms for data processing, which contrasts sharply with the open data ethos. Open data advocates for transparent, unrestricted access to information rather than restrictive cookie walls. Consequently, such opaque consent models hinder the goal of maximizing public data utility and accessibility for all users.
