El área metropolitana ganó en un año 1.424 afiliados a la Seguridad Social

The metropolitan area of A Coruña and Betanzos has shown steady economic resilience, as evidenced by an overall increase in social security affiliations and a rise in new business registrations. This positive trend highlights the stability of the local labor market and a robust environment for entrepreneurial activity, suggesting that these municipalities are becoming increasingly attractive for employment and investment. Oleiros, Arteixo, and Culleredo lead in employment volume, reflecting their role as strong regional economic hubs. At the same time, the growth in new enterprises, particularly in areas such as Miño, underscores a dynamic private sector. These developments indicate that local governments are successfully fostering conditions that support both established industries and emerging startups, contributing to broader regional economic health. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives, as it illustrates how granular, publicly available statistics can reveal nuanced economic patterns at the municipal level. By analyzing such detailed datasets, researchers and policymakers can identify successful local strategies, track labor market trends, and make informed decisions to further support community development and economic transparency.
