INE envía 5.000 cajas censales a Oruro

The National Statistics Institute of Bolivia has begun the logistical distribution of materials for the 2024 Population and Housing Census, underscoring its commitment to modern, high-quality data collection standards. By coordinating with the military to secure operational centers and transport supplies, the INE aims to ensure the integrity and safety of the entire census process nationwide. This initiative highlights the critical intersection between government transparency and citizen participation, as accurate census data serves as the foundation for public policy and resource allocation. The rigorous protocols for handling and validating census materials demonstrate how institutional reliability directly affects the trustworthiness of official statistics—a key principle in open data ecosystems. Ultimately, this event underscores the necessity of robust infrastructure for generating accessible and reliable demographic information. By ensuring that census data is collected systematically and securely, the state facilitates the creation of open datasets that can be used by researchers, journalists, and civil society to promote accountability and informed decision-making.
