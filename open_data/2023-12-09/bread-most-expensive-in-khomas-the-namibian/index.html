Bread most expensive in Khomas - The Namibian

Regional disparities in Namibia reveal significant variations in the cost of essential goods, with consumers in Khomas paying more for bread than those in other zones. Similarly, Zone 3 residents face higher costs for beef stew compared to Zone 1. These geographic price differences highlight the uneven impact of inflation on local purchasing power and living standards across the country. National inflation remains heavily influenced by housing, food, and transport categories, which collectively dominate the economic basket. While overall inflation has slowed compared to the previous year, specific food items like fish and vegetables have seen sharp price increases. This suggests that while macroeconomic indicators may stabilize, everyday essentials continue to strain household budgets, particularly for vulnerable populations. This data is critical for open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of granular, regional economic statistics. Accessible and detailed consumer price indices allow citizens and researchers to identify local economic hardships and advocate for targeted policy interventions. Transparent data empowers communities to understand cost-of-living variations and demands greater accountability from statistical agencies regarding regional economic equity.
