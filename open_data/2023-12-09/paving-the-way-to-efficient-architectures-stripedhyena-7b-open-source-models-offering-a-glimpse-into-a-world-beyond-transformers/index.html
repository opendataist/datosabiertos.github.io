Paving the way to efficient architectures: StripedHyena-7B, open source models offering a glimpse into a world beyond Transformers

Together Research introduces StripedHyena, a new sequence model architecture that challenges the dominance of Transformers by combining attention mechanisms with efficient state-space models. This hybrid design achieves performance competitive with leading open-source Transformers on short-context tasks while significantly outperforming them in long-context scenarios. By replacing expensive attention operations with implicit gated convolutions, the model demonstrates that alternative architectures can rival established baselines without sacrificing accuracy, marking a pivotal shift in how language models are constructed. The practical implications for developers and researchers are substantial, as StripedHyena offers superior efficiency in both training and inference. It enables faster processing of longer sequences and reduces memory consumption during generation by over half compared to optimized Transformers. These improvements lower the computational barriers to fine-tuning and deploying large models, making advanced long-context capabilities more accessible to the broader open-data community. This efficiency allows for more frequent experimentation and iteration, accelerating the development of specialized AI applications. This release is highly relevant to open data because it expands the toolkit available for processing diverse and complex information sets. By enabling efficient handling of extensive contexts, researchers can better analyze long documents, codebases, or historical archives that were previously difficult or too costly to process. Furthermore, the emphasis on open, alternative architectures encourages the community to move beyond standardized Transformer-based solutions, fostering innovation in how we extract insights from large-scale data repositories.
