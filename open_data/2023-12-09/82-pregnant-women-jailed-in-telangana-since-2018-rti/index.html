82 pregnant women jailed in Telangana since 2018: RTI

An RTI inquiry revealed that pregnant women continue to be detained in Telangana jails, highlighting systemic failures in their healthcare and safety. The report underscores the urgent need for specialized medical staff, such as gynecologists, to protect vulnerable inmates. This case demonstrates the critical role of transparency in exposing gaps within the justice system. By making official data accessible, citizens can identify where legal safeguards fail to protect basic human rights, particularly for marginalized groups like female detainees. The findings are relevant to open data as they illustrate how public access to government records drives accountability. When authorities share accurate information about inmate welfare, it empowers activists to demand better policies and ensures that state obligations towards prisoners are met, fostering a more just society.
