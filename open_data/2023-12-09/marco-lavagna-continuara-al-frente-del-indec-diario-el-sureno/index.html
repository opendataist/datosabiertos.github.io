Marco Lavagna continuará al frente del Indec - Diario El Sureño

Marco Lavagna will remain head of INDEC under the incoming administration, marking a pivotal moment for the agency’s future. The government plans to grant INDEC technical autonomy, removing it from the Ministry of Economy’s direct control to ensure its operations are strictly non-partisan. This structural shift aims to establish a new state policy where statistical production is entirely depoliticized. By insulating the institute from political influence, the reform seeks to restore credibility and precision in national data collection, a critical step for rebuilding trust in official statistics. For the open data community, this autonomy is vital as it suggests a move toward more transparent and reliable public datasets. Independent verification and broader data accessibility depend on institutional independence, making this governance change essential for fostering an ecosystem of trustworthy, open information.
