Esto es lo que es capaz la inteligencia artificial en la industria musical que genera gran controversia entre los artistas

The rise of AI in music sparks a critical debate regarding its boundaries, as the technology can now compose original songs and mimic celebrity voices with alarming accuracy. This capability challenges traditional artistic integrity and raises urgent concerns about copyright infringement and the potential erosion of human creativity. Prominent artists are demanding strict regulations to prevent unauthorized vocal cloning, highlighting the tension between technological innovation and the protection of intellectual property rights. While some view these tools as creative enhancers for producers, others fear they may create insurmountable barriers for professional musicians seeking to sustain their careers. This issue is vital for open data because it underscores the necessity of transparent algorithms and clear data provenance. Establishing ethical standards for how audio datasets are collected and used ensures that AI development respects human rights and fosters trust in digital creative ecosystems.
