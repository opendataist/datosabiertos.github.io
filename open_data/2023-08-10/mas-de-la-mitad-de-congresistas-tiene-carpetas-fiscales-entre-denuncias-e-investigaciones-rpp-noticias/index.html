Más de la mitad de congresistas tiene carpetas fiscales, entre denuncias e investigaciones | RPP Noticias

The article highlights that numerous Peruvian legislators are subject to active investigations, with Luis Picón facing the highest number of complaints. This situation poses significant risks to democratic representation, as convicted officials may leave their constituents without a voice. Moreover, it creates severe conflicts of interest when these individuals draft laws aimed at combating corruption. Specialists warn that investigating politicians may lead to legislative efforts to weaken crucial judicial mechanisms, such as effective cooperation agreements. Political leaders, often themselves under scrutiny, incentivize allies to create legal “blindings” for future protection. This undermines the integrity of the justice system and prioritizes political survival over the public interest. This content is vital for open data because it demonstrates how transparency in judicial records and parliamentary activities can reveal hidden conflicts of interest. Accessible, structured data enables citizens and analysts to track the relationship between legal investigations and legislative behavior, fostering accountability and ensuring that anti-corruption efforts are not sabotaged by those they are meant to oversee.
