Big data, AI and ML focus of draft revised national policy on statistics

The revised National Policy on Official Statistics establishes a modern framework for generating reliable economic and social data by integrating emerging technologies like artificial intelligence and machine learning. This shift aims to enhance the speed, credibility, and overall quality of statistical outputs, directly addressing recent criticisms regarding the timeliness and accuracy of official information. A central tenet of this policy is the creation of a mandatory integrated data system and a standardized national data sheet. By optimizing administrative records and innovating survey methods to reduce respondent burden, the government seeks to streamline data collection. This structural reform promises more consistent, high-quality datasets that are essential for evidence-based policy formulation and effective governance. This development is crucial for open data initiatives because it moves the system toward standardized, technology-driven dissemination of core statistics across all government levels. The mandatory release of periodic data in key domains ensures greater transparency and accessibility for citizens and researchers. Ultimately, these reforms facilitate a more robust open data ecosystem, enabling better public scrutiny and informed decision-making through improved data availability and quality.
