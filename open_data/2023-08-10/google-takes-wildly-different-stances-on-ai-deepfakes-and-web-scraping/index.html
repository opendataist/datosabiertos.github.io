Google Takes Wildly Different Stances on AI "Deepfakes" and Web Scraping

Google seeks a strategic alliance with Universal Music to build a legitimate AI music tool, strengthening its position against rivals in the rapidly evolving generative AI landscape. This move highlights the intense competition among tech giants to establish dominance while navigating complex intellectual property waters. The company argues for legal frameworks that permit web scraping for AI training by default, characterizing it as fair use of public content. It suggests that copyright laws should allow creators to opt out, mirroring existing licensing models, rather than imposing strict prohibitions on data ingestion. This issue is critical for open data advocates because it challenges the fundamental premise of training models on publicly available information. If legislation mandates default access to web data, it could significantly impact how open datasets are curated, accessed, and utilized for future technological development.
