2024 Provisional Voters Register: We’re scrutinising documents - Ghanamma.com

2024 Provisional Voters Register: We’re scrutinising documents - Ghanamma.com

The National Democratic Congress has launched a rigorous audit of the newly released Provisional Voters Register to verify its accuracy against their own internal records. By meticulously cross-checking data across all electoral areas, the party aims to identify discrepancies and prevent fraudulent "ghost voters" from participating in the upcoming elections. This intense scrutiny is driven by concerns over the Electoral Commission’s delayed release and lack of transparency. This situation highlights the critical role of open data in safeguarding democratic integrity. When election authorities fail to publish voter registers openly and promptly, political parties are forced into costly, reactive verification processes. The NDC’s demand for public access underscores that transparent, accessible data is essential for public trust and allows for independent validation of election readiness. Ultimately, this dispute illustrates the tension between state-held information and public accountability. It emphasizes that open data practices are not merely technical requirements but fundamental mechanisms for ensuring fair elections. Without mandatory, timely publication of such datasets, stakeholders cannot effectively monitor compliance or guarantee that the voter roll accurately reflects the electorate, thereby risking the legitimacy of the entire electoral process.
