UGT ve los datos en C-LM como un "afianzamiento" de la moderación de los precios y una tendencia "positiva"

The article illustrates how large distributors maintain excessive profit margins despite temporary tax cuts, highlighting a critical gap in market transparency. This dynamic undermines real wage growth and erodes workers' purchasing power, demonstrating the urgent need for better price regulation and accountability in the retail sector. Such findings are relevant to open data because they underscore the necessity of accessible, granular pricing information. Transparent data would allow researchers and policymakers to verify claims about margin fixation and ensure that economic benefits reach consumers rather than being absorbed by corporate profits. Furthermore, the reliance on union negotiations to secure fair wages points to systemic opacity. Open access to supply chain and pricing data could empower stakeholders to monitor inflation impacts accurately, fostering a more equitable economic environment where wage increases translate into genuine improvements in living standards.
