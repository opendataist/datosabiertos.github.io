Creció desempleo en 2024, 979 mil más el último año: Inegi

The article highlights that while the economically active population grew, its participation rate declined because the non-active population expanded at a faster pace. Additionally, nearly 54% of workers remain in informal employment, underscoring structural labor market challenges. This data is relevant to open data because it illustrates how transparent, accessible statistical indicators are essential for monitoring socioeconomic trends. By making metrics like employment rates and informality public, governments and citizens can better analyze labor dynamics and inform policy decisions. Ultimately, the report serves as a critical resource for understanding Mexico's workforce evolution. It emphasizes the necessity of open data initiatives to provide accurate, up-to-date information that supports research, accountability, and strategic planning regarding employment quality and population engagement.
