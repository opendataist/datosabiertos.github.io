El PIB creció un 3,2% en 2024 tras repetir la subida del 0,8% en el cuarto trimestre

The Spanish economy demonstrated robust growth in 2024, marking the fourth consecutive year of positive expansion. This sustained performance highlights the country's resilience and positions it as a leader within the eurozone, outpacing regional averages. The primary driver was strong domestic demand, supported by household consumption and accelerated investment, which contributed significantly to the overall increase in Gross Domestic Product. Simultaneously, the labor market showed improvement, with employment and wages rising substantially. These indicators suggest that economic gains are translating into tangible benefits for workers, reinforcing internal consumption. The balance between internal and external demand underscores a healthy economic structure, reducing vulnerability to external shocks while maintaining steady upward momentum across various sectors. This data is relevant to open data because it exemplifies how transparent, high-frequency economic indicators from national statistical institutes enable real-time analysis of market health. By publishing detailed metrics on GDP, employment, and income, such datasets empower researchers, policymakers, and analysts to validate trends and make informed decisions. This accessibility fosters accountability and supports evidence-based strategies for economic development and social welfare improvement.
