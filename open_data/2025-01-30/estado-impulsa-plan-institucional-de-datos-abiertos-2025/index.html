Estado impulsa Plan Institucional de Datos Abiertos 2025

San Luis Potosí launches its 2025 Open Data Plan to enhance transparency, efficiency, and citizen participation across state institutions. This initiative strengthens accountability by ensuring that public organizations publish relevant information, thereby empowering civil society and improving governance through technological tools and open access policies. The state platform now hosts hundreds of datasets from numerous public entities, significantly expanding the available information. By making these resources accessible, the government supports a culture of oversight and trust, enabling citizens and organizations to engage more deeply with public administration and effectively monitor institutional performance. Furthermore, the agency offers free online courses on open government to educate both officials and citizens. This educational component is crucial for open data ecosystems, as it builds technical capacity and fosters active civic engagement, ensuring that transparency efforts translate into meaningful societal impact and informed decision-making.
