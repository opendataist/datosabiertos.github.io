Jack Dorsey’s Block has an AI agent too

Jack Dorsey’s Block launched Goose, an open-source AI agent automating engineering tasks via adaptable large language models. This release embodies the commitment to open-source principles, empowering developers with transparent, customizable tools that integrate seamlessly into existing workflows. By supporting various models and extensions, the project democratizes access to autonomous AI capabilities, encouraging community collaboration and innovation. Its flexibility extends beyond code, hinting at future creative applications in music and other domains. This initiative is vital for open data as it promotes transparency and user control over AI systems. It challenges proprietary restrictions, fostering an ecosystem where tools remain accessible, modifiable, and community-driven rather than locked within corporate silos.
