La economía española acelera: el PIB repunta un 0,7% en el segundo trimestre

La economía española acelera: el PIB repunta un 0,7% en el segundo trimestre

Spain’s economy shows renewed dynamism in the second quarter, surpassing consensus expectations thanks to a strong boost from domestic demand. This sustained growth, driven by household consumption and business investment, contrasts with the negative contribution of external demand, where rising imports have slightly overshadowed exports. This dynamic underscores the strength of the domestic market as the main engine of the recent economic recovery. At the same time, the labor market is strengthening, and employee compensation is increasing, indicating an improvement in workers’ well-being and greater confidence in job stability. However, productivity signals are mixed: while productivity per hour improves, productivity per job remains stagnant, suggesting that the rise in employment does not always translate into proportional efficiency gains per worker. This disparity is crucial for understanding the true quality of economic growth and the sustainability of wage gains. This analysis is relevant to open data because it highlights the importance of accessing preliminary economic statistics broken down by demand and supply components. Transparency in publishing these indicators allows civil society, researchers, and businesses to quickly verify macroeconomic trends and make informed decisions. Moreover, detailed information on productivity and employment facilitates public debate on labor policies, demonstrating how the availability of high-quality open data empowers social scrutiny of the economy’s real performance.
