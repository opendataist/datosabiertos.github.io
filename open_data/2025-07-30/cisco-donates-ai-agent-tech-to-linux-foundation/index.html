Cisco donates AI agent tech to Linux Foundation

Cisco donates AI agent tech to Linux Foundation

Cisco transfers its AI agent management initiative to the Linux Foundation, fostering open collaboration among major tech vendors. This move establishes industry-standard protocols enabling AI agents to seamlessly discover, communicate, and collaborate across diverse platforms and organizations. This shift is vital for open_data as it promotes interoperability and transparent standards for data exchange between autonomous systems.
