Fiscales generales demandan al gobierno de Trump por solicitar datos de beneficiarios de SNAP - El Diario NY

Fiscales generales demandan al gobierno de Trump por solicitar datos de beneficiarios de SNAP - El Diario NY

A coalition of state attorneys general has filed a lawsuit against the federal government, alleging that its demand for large-scale personal data from participants in the food assistance program violates privacy rights and is being used as a tool to target immigrants rather than to combat fraud, which is statistically low. The indiscriminate collection of information has a chilling effect, causing vulnerable families to avoid accessing essential aid out of fear of surveillance. State leaders argue that this practice erodes public trust in government institutions and threatens the well-being of millions of people who depend on these basic resources. This controversy is relevant to open data because it illustrates the risk of transforming social service data into instruments of immigration enforcement without legal consensus. It underscores the critical need to protect citizens’ privacy and autonomy from the political use of information, reinforcing the importance of ethical and transparent principles in the management and sharing of public data.
