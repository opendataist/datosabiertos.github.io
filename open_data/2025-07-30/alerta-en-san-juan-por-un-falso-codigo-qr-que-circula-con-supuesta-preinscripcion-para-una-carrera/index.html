Alerta en San Juan por un falso código QR que circula con supuesta preinscripción para una carrera

Authorities in San Juan debunked a viral QR code that claimed to offer pre-registration for a police course, warning that it is unauthorized and poses data security risks. This incident underscores the critical need to distinguish official open-data channels from fraudulent online content. Verifying sources is essential to protect citizens’ privacy and maintain trust in the dissemination of information by the public sector.
