Edenorte recibe calificación de 99.22% en Transparencia

EDENORTE achieved a near-perfect score in a government ethics evaluation, demonstrating a strong institutional commitment to transparency and public integrity. This high rating reflects the collective effort to implement accountable practices and strengthen citizen trust in the Dominican Republic’s public sector. The assessment highlighted the organization’s compliance with legal frameworks and excellence in managing procurement, human resources, and assistance programs. By maintaining robust internal structures and ethical standards, EDENORTE positions itself as a model for good public management, aiming for continuous improvement rather than complacency. This case is relevant to open data because the report explicitly emphasizes the necessity of an effective transparency portal. Ensuring citizens can easily access information about institutional management is crucial for fostering a culture of openness and accountability, proving that accessible data is foundational to credible public service.
