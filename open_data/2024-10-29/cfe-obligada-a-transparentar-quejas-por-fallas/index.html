CFE obligada a transparentar quejas por fallas

The INAI ordered CFE to fully disclose details regarding power outages, including failure causes, restoration times, repair costs, and compensation paid. This mandates transparency on how public resources are used to address service disruptions and holds the utility accountable for the impact on consumers. By revoking CFE’s partial response, the INAI established that access to information must be comprehensive, ensuring the public can scrutinize the efficiency and financial handling of emergency repairs. This prevents the utility from hiding the scope of service failures or the adequacy of its recovery efforts behind vague administrative responses. This ruling is vital for open data as it sets a precedent for publishing granular, actionable information on public utility performance. It reinforces the principle that critical social data, such as outage metrics and expenditures, must be openly available for civic oversight rather than withheld or overly aggregated, fostering greater government accountability.
