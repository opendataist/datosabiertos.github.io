Following release of crime stats, WC govt again calls on SAPS to work with municipal police to curb crime

Evidence suggests that collaborative policing between state and municipal forces significantly reduces murder rates in the Western Cape. Localized, technology-enabled strategies prove more effective than traditional models alone, highlighting the urgent need for expanded municipal investigative powers. This case study is vital for open data advocates, demonstrating how transparent crime statistics can validate specific governance models. It underscores the necessity of accessible, accurate public data to hold authorities accountable and guide evidence-based resource allocation toward successful community safety initiatives.
