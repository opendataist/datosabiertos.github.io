Arkansas governor to hold special session focusing on tax cuts, FOIA updates, COVID-19 rules

Arkansas Governor Sarah Huckabee Sanders has convened a special legislative session aimed at reducing government size by enacting significant tax cuts and streamlining state operations. This move aligns with her broader promise to limit governmental growth to protect individual liberty and foster economic freedom through lower personal and corporate income tax rates. The agenda also addresses freedom of information laws, proposing updates to the Freedom of Information Act. These changes seek to enhance transparency by modifying disclosure requirements for state police, particularly concerning methods used to protect the governor, thereby balancing security needs with public access rights. Furthermore, the session will introduce regulations restricting COVID-19 mandates, such as banning vaccine requirements for state employees. This focus on deregulation is highly relevant to open_data, as it highlights the tension between government efficiency, transparency, and the removal of administrative barriers that often hinder data accessibility and public oversight.
