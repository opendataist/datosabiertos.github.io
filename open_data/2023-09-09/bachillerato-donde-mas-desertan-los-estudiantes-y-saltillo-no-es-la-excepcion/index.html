Bachillerato, donde más desertan los estudiantes y Saltillo no es la excepción

Socioeconomic pressures and soaring educational costs force Mexican youth to abandon their studies, shifting demand toward public institutions. This trend highlights the urgent need for open data transparency regarding funding, pricing, and resource allocation in the education sector. Such data enables policymakers and citizens to identify disparities and advocate for affordable, equitable access to learning opportunities.
