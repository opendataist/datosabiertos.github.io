Inteligencia artificial: Microsoft protegerá usuarios que sufran demandas por derechos de autor

Microsoft has assumed legal responsibility for copyright infringement lawsuits brought against its commercial customers who use Copilot to generate content. This commitment aims to alleviate concerns about intellectual property violations by ensuring that the company will cover defense costs and any judgments, provided that the built-in security safeguards have been employed. The initiative strengthens confidence in the enterprise use of generative artificial intelligence. By transferring legal risk from the user to the technology company, it facilitates the adoption of these tools without fear of immediate financial repercussions. This measure responds to the need to balance innovation with the protection of original creators. This development is relevant to the open data domain, as it establishes precedents regarding who assumes legal risks when information is processed and generated through algorithms. Clarifying responsibility within the data value chain is crucial for fostering more transparent and secure data ecosystems. By mitigating fears of litigation, it promotes an environment where the generation and dissemination of data-driven knowledge can advance with greater legal stability, encouraging responsible experimentation.
