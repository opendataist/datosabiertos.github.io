Guatemala participa en el Tallin Digital Summit 2023

Guatemala’s digital infrastructure strategy emphasizes building open, inclusive societies through shared public digital goods. This approach highlights the convergence of data and civic technologies to foster global transparency. The initiative underscores the critical role of open data in modern governance. By adopting interoperable standards, nations can replicate successful models like Estonia’s. This fosters trust and ensures secure, accessible information for all citizens. Such collaboration is vital for the open data movement. It demonstrates how systemic interoperability enables scalable solutions. Ultimately, this strengthens democratic institutions by making government actions visible and accountable worldwide.
