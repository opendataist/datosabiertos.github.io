Microsoft defenderá a los usuarios de su inteligencia artificial Copilot ante demandas por derechos de autor

Microsoft has committed to defending commercial users of its Copilot AI suite against copyright claims arising from generated content. By assuming full responsibility for legal liabilities, the company aims to alleviate user concerns and encourage the adoption of generative tools within established safety boundaries. This indemnification ensures that businesses can innovate without fear of immediate litigation for copyright infringement. This protection applies specifically to paid enterprise services, excluding personal users who may still face legal risks. Microsoft’s stance balances customer security with respect for original creators, acknowledging the ethical complexities of AI training data. The initiative mirrors existing patent protection policies, reinforcing a corporate commitment to responsible AI advancement. This announcement is highly relevant to open_data discussions as it highlights the legal fragility of AI outputs. It underscores the urgent need for transparent, legally robust data practices. As organizations rely more on AI, understanding the intersection of intellectual property and data usage becomes critical for sustainable, open, and compliant technological development.
