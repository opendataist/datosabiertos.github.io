USAID's Samantha Power Attends 2023 Open Govt Summit in Estonia

The U.S. deepens its partnership with the Open Government Partnership through strategic funding and a new five-year agreement. This collaboration aims to enhance democratic governance, anti-corruption efforts, and transparency at the country level. Such international commitments are vital for open data, as they promote the accountable, transparent, and inclusive use of public information globally.
