Cuáles fueron los 10 alimentos que más aumentaron en julio – Diario Necochea

This article highlights the severe inflationary pressure on essential food items, emphasizing that while food prices may grow slower than in other sectors, they disproportionately affect vulnerable households. The core conclusion is that food inflation has a unique and damaging impact on society because the poorest spend almost their entire income on basic necessities, making them highly sensitive to these cost increases. The narrative underscores the structural inequality inherent in price indices. By focusing on the high weight of food in the Consumer Price Index (CPI), the text reveals how economic data often masks the lived reality of marginalized groups. The sharp rises in staple goods such as potatoes and sugar demonstrate that basic sustenance is becoming increasingly unaffordable, exacerbating existing social disparities. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates the necessity of granular, accessible statistics for social analysis. Transparent data on specific product prices allows researchers and policymakers to identify who is most at risk and design targeted interventions. Without open access to such detailed consumer price information, it is difficult to accurately measure the impact on poverty or evaluate the effectiveness of economic policies aimed at protecting the most vulnerable sectors of the population.
