El uso de la capacidad instalada en la industria fue de 68,6% en junio

In June, Argentina’s industrial installed capacity utilization reached 68.6%, reflecting a slight annual decline. This moderate contraction indicates a complex economic landscape where the first half of the year maintained net positive growth, yet recent months show signs of cooling. The data suggests that while overall production activity remains resilient compared to May, it is lagging behind the same period last year, highlighting subtle shifts in industrial momentum that warrant close monitoring for economic stability. The industrial landscape is deeply heterogeneous, driven by specific sectoral struggles and successes. Heavy industries such as basic metals and non-metallic minerals faced significant headwinds due to labor conflicts and reduced demand from construction and agriculture, exacerbated by drought impacts on food processing. Conversely, sectors such as petroleum refining and tobacco recorded robust expansions, demonstrating how divergent external factors—from supply chain disruptions to climatic conditions—create an uneven recovery trajectory across different manufacturing domains. This report is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it exemplifies the necessity of granular, transparent, and timely statistical indicators for public and private analysis. By making detailed sectoral breakdowns available, datasets empower researchers, policymakers, and businesses to move beyond aggregate metrics. Understanding the nuanced drivers behind these figures allows for more accurate economic modeling and evidence-based decision-making, ensuring that open data serves not just as raw information, but as a critical tool for diagnosing structural economic health and identifying emerging trends.
