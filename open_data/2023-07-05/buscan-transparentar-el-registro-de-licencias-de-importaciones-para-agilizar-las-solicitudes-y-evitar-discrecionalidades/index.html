Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The proposal by the Civic Coalition aims to establish an Open Data Portal for imports in Argentina, transforming a system currently characterized by discretion and opacity into one that is transparent and predictable. The project requires that all information related to license applications be made public, digitized, and accessible in open formats, including details about the product, the amount, and the current status of the processing. The initiative argues that the lack of clear criteria and unmet deadlines under the current regime significantly increase the risk of corruption and distort the foreign exchange market. By guaranteeing free access to administrative regulations, the goal is to mitigate the abuse of power by officials and ensure that authorizations are managed with simplicity, chronological order, and automatic resolution in cases of administrative silence, aligning with international standards of good governance. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates how the availability of high-quality public information is not just a technical issue, but a fundamental tool for accountability and the fight against corruption. By applying open data principles to a critical economic sector, it demonstrates that transparency can restore trust in institutions, reduce arbitrariness, and promote a fairer and more efficient commercial environment for all citizens.
