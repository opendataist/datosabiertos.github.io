Diario HOY | INE da a conocer principales resultados finales del Censo 2022

The final report of Paraguay’s 2022 census reveals a population nearing six million, marked by significant demographic shifts including an aging society and changing household structures. A key trend is the rise in female-headed households and single-person homes, reflecting evolving social dynamics, while geographic distribution highlights Central as the most densely populated department. These granular insights into population density and domestic arrangements provide essential context for understanding local community needs and resource allocation. Furthermore, the data indicates substantial progress in housing infrastructure, with widespread access to electricity and improved availability of potable water and sanitation services. This improvement in living standards, coupled with smaller household sizes, demonstrates tangible advancements in public utilities and quality of life. Such detailed metrics allow policymakers to assess the effectiveness of infrastructure investments and identify areas requiring further development to ensure equitable access to basic services across all regions. This article is crucial for open data initiatives as it underscores the importance of accessible, transparent government statistics for societal analysis. By making comprehensive datasets and geostatistical portals available online, the National Institute of Statistics enables researchers, journalists, and citizens to independently verify information and drive evidence-based decision-making. Open access to such vital census data fosters accountability, promotes civic engagement, and supports the creation of innovative applications that leverage demographic trends for public benefit.
