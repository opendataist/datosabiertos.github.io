Censo de población: ¿dónde están los 1,3 millones de habitantes menos? - Economía - ABC Color

The final census results confirm that Paraguay’s population is significantly lower than previously projected, totaling approximately 6.1 million inhabitants rather than the estimated 7.4 million. This discrepancy arises because official projections relied on outdated data from two decades ago, highlighting the critical necessity of using current, high-quality demographic data for accurate statistical modeling. The significant gap underscores how relying on obsolete baselines can lead to substantial errors in national planning and resource allocation. Several structural factors explain this demographic reality, primarily a sharp decline in fertility rates and extensive international migration. With the average number of children per woman dropping significantly and hundreds of thousands of citizens residing abroad, the country’s growth trajectory has shifted. These findings demonstrate that population dynamics are not static; they require continuous monitoring through diverse data sources, such as electoral registries, to ensure that statistical outputs remain consistent with actual social behaviors and mobility patterns. This article is vital to the open data community because the National Institute of Statistics has made all census data freely accessible through an open Geoestadistical Portal. By providing granular, downloadable data at national, departmental, and district levels, the institution enables developers, researchers, and citizens to independently analyze and visualize demographic trends. This commitment to transparency fosters accountability and allows for more informed, data-driven decision-making in both the public and private sectors.
