Suplantan identidad de Afore y otras entidades financieras, advierte Condusef

The Condusef has detected a significant increase in cases of identity impersonation by financial institutions, involving hundreds of legitimate entities. This fraud relies on the unauthorized use of names, logos, and administrative data to deceive the public through digital channels, soliciting upfront payments under the false promise of granting loans. The critical implication lies in the direct violation of users' privacy and security, as they lose their funds without receiving any service, thereby revealing a breach of trust in the digital financial ecosystem. The relevance to the field of open data and transparency lies in the urgent need for verifiable and accessible mechanisms for citizens. Condusef emphasizes the use of the Financial Services Provider Registry System (SIPRES) as a fundamental tool for users to validate the authenticity of institutions. This underscores the importance of ensuring that government records and institutional databases are open, accurate, and easily accessible, enabling civil society to cross-check official information with commercial offers received. Finally, this scenario highlights the urgency of promoting financial education based on the verification of public data sources. Individuals' ability to protect themselves depends directly on access to reliable and verifiable information about the legal identity of the entities they interact with. By encouraging verification through official registries, the accountability of the financial sector is strengthened, and the effectiveness of scams that rely on misinformation or opacity in customer communication is reduced.
