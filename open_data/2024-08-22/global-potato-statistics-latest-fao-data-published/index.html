Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

This article highlights the FAOSTAT database as a critical resource for accessing comprehensive global agricultural statistics, specifically focusing on potato production metrics from 2021. It demonstrates how researchers and analysts can extract detailed data on total production and harvested area across nearly a hundred countries, facilitating cross-border comparisons and trend analysis. The narrative emphasizes the utility of open data platforms in aggregating information that might otherwise be fragmented across various national government sources, thereby lowering barriers to entry for global economic and agricultural research. The summary identifies China and India as the dominant producers, underscoring how open data reveals the concentration of agricultural output in specific regions. By providing a ranked list of the top producers, the text illustrates the power of standardized, publicly available datasets to quickly contextualize the scale of global food systems. This approach allows users to move beyond anecdotal evidence and rely on verified figures for market analysis, policy formulation, and academic study, proving the value of transparency in international trade and food security discussions. Ultimately, the relevance to open data lies in the accessibility and standardization of FAO metrics, which enable reproducible research and informed decision-making without reliance on proprietary or restricted sources. Although the article notes that these figures may slightly differ from local official reports, the global consistency provided by FAOSTAT ensures a reliable baseline for comparative studies. This reinforces the importance of maintaining and promoting open statistical databases as foundational infrastructure for understanding worldwide agricultural dynamics and supporting evidence-based interventions in the global food supply chain.
