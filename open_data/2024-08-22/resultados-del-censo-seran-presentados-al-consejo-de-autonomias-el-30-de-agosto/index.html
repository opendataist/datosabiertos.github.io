Resultados del censo serán presentados al Consejo de Autonomías el 30 de agosto

The upcoming publication of Bolivia’s census results by the National Institute of Statistics (INE) aims to inform autonomous regions and refine public policies amid a national crisis. This data is crucial for ensuring policy effectiveness, highlighting the role of transparent statistical information in governance. Understanding how census data drives these adjustments underscores the impact of the open data movement on equitable and responsive administrative decision-making.
