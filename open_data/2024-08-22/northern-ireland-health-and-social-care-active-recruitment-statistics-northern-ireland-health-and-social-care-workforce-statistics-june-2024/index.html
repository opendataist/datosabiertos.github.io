Northern Ireland Health and Social Care Active Recruitment Statistics & Northern Ireland Health and Social Care Workforce Statistics June 2024

Northern Ireland Health and Social Care Active Recruitment Statistics & Northern Ireland Health and Social Care Workforce Statistics June 2024

The Department of Health recently published critical workforce data for Northern Ireland’s Health and Social Care sector, offering a transparent snapshot of staffing levels and recruitment efforts. This release highlights a significant upward trend in the overall workforce, marking a substantial recovery in staff numbers compared to pre-pandemic levels. Such detailed transparency is vital for open data initiatives, as it allows stakeholders to monitor public sector capacity and resource allocation with greater accuracy and accountability. A key finding is the dramatic reduction in actively recruited vacancies, which has decreased significantly year-over-year. While registered nursing and midwifery roles still constitute the largest share of both the total workforce and current recruitment needs, the easing of vacancy pressure suggests improved stability in critical care areas. Understanding these shifts helps the public assess whether the health system is better equipped to handle patient demand and supports informed discussions about healthcare investment and policy effectiveness. This publication exemplifies the importance of accessible statistical data in evaluating public service performance. By providing granular insights into staff distribution across different trusts and professional groups, it enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to identify trends and potential bottlenecks. Open access to this information fosters trust in government operations and encourages data-driven dialogue on how to sustainably improve health and social care outcomes for the community.
