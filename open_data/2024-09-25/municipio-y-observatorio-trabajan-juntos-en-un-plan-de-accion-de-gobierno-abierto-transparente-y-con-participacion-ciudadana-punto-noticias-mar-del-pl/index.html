Municipio y Observatorio trabajan juntos en un Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto, transparente y con participación ciudadana | Punto Noticias Mar del Plata.

Municipio y Observatorio trabajan juntos en un Plan de Acción de Gobierno Abierto, transparente y con participación ciudadana | Punto Noticias Mar del Plata.

The city of General Pueyrredón has joined the global Open Government Partnership, aiming to foster transparency and civic participation through a dedicated action plan. This initiative positions the municipality as a model for inclusive governance, supported by the University FASTA Observatory as a key non-governmental counterpart to ensure effective implementation and oversight of the proposed commitments. Central to this effort is the active engagement of civil society to define impactful, relevant commitments. By prioritizing citizen feedback, the administration seeks to align its policies with genuine public interests regarding access to information and accountability. This collaborative approach ensures that the resulting governance strategies are authentic and directly beneficial to the community, strengthening the bond between local government and residents. This development is highly relevant to open data because successful open government depends on releasing accessible, usable public information. Transparent data serves as the foundation for meaningful civic participation and accountability, enabling citizens to monitor government actions effectively. Therefore, the integration of open data principles into this strategic plan is essential for achieving the promised shift toward a more responsible, inclusive, and responsive local administration.
