Citizens have right to seek FOI Act amendment – Abbas

The article emphasizes that the Freedom of Information Act is not static and can be improved through active civic engagement. Citizens possess the legal right to propose amendments, addressing existing gaps and adapting the legislation to global standards. This perspective shifts the FOI from a rigid statute to a dynamic tool, empowering individuals to shape laws that better serve national economic development and transparency. Effective implementation requires broad public enlightenment and collaboration among government bodies, media, and civil society. The text highlights that knowledge dissemination is crucial for decision-makers to discharge their duties properly, thereby strengthening the relationship between leaders and citizens. By fostering an environment of open government, the state can enhance democratic participation and accountability, ensuring that information access translates into tangible societal benefits. This content is highly relevant to open_data because it underscores the legal and political framework necessary for data accessibility. The push for FOI amendments and public awareness directly supports the principles of open data, which rely on transparency, accountability, and citizen participation. Understanding these legislative dynamics helps advocates argue for more robust data policies that align with international best practices and empower communities through informed governance.
