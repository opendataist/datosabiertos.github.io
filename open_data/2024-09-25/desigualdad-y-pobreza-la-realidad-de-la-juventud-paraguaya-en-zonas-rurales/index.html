Desigualdad y pobreza: La realidad de la juventud paraguaya en zonas rurales

Paraguay faces severe structural inequalities, particularly affecting its youth population, where rural communities suffer disproportionately from poverty and limited access to essential services compared to urban centers. This urban-rural divide is evident in precarious employment, inadequate educational infrastructure, and insufficient healthcare coverage, creating a cycle that restricts personal and professional development. Addressing these gaps is critical to unlocking the demographic potential for national progress and preventing the perpetuation of socioeconomic disadvantages across generations. This situation underscores the urgent need for open data initiatives to drive policy changes and resource allocation. Transparent, accessible data on demographics, economic participation, and social indicators is essential for identifying the root causes of inequality and measuring the effectiveness of interventions. By making such information publicly available, stakeholders can foster accountability, encourage evidence-based decision-making, and ensure that marginalized groups receive the targeted support they need to overcome systemic barriers and achieve sustainable development.
