Procurement Notices - UNDP-HQ-01238 - 1.3.8 Development of the "Amman is listening" open data participatory platform

Procurement Notices - UNDP-HQ-01238 - 1.3.8 Development of the "Amman is listening" open data participatory platform

The "Amman is Listening" initiative represents a strategic effort to modernize urban governance through an open data participatory platform. By enabling real-time resident feedback on city services, the project aims to transform citizen input into actionable knowledge for local decision-making. This approach not only optimizes service provision and crisis management but also supports the smart transition of Amman, offering a scalable model for nearby cities. Relevant to open data, this platform demonstrates how transparent citizen engagement enhances public service delivery. It highlights the shift toward user-centric design in municipal technology, ensuring that digital tools are accessible and responsive to diverse demographics, including students and investors. The ongoing development of the Minimum Viable Product underscores the importance of iterative improvements, such as interactive maps and real-time communication channels, to deepen civic participation and trust. This initiative is crucial for the open data community as it illustrates the practical application of participatory data collection in urban settings. It showcases the potential for open data to bridge the gap between government entities and residents, fostering a more inclusive and efficient urban environment. By prioritizing scalability and transferability, Amman provides a valuable case study for other municipalities seeking to leverage technology for improved governance and community well-being.
