Gobierno Abierto: UNL recibió un premio internacional

The National University of the Littoral has been recognized as a “good academic practice” by international United Nations bodies and Ibero-American networks, with its Open Government portal highlighted for its integrative approach that combines transparency, participation, and collaboration. This recognition validates the model of using information technologies to democratize access to public information, demonstrating that academic institutions can lead innovative initiatives that prioritize data visualization and accountability in contexts where such practices are still developing. The project’s success underscores the crucial importance of interdisciplinary collaboration and institutional commitment in implementing effective open government policies. By integrating knowledge from diverse fields such as statistics, computer science, and communication, the university demonstrates that data publication is not an end in itself, but rather a tool for strengthening democracy. This multidisciplinary approach ensures that information is accessible, understandable, and useful to citizens, fostering a culture of collaboration and co-creation of public policies that transcends traditional administrative barriers. This distinction is highly relevant to the open data movement, as it establishes a replicable precedent for other Argentine and Ibero-American universities. It illustrates how academia can act as a key actor in improving public policies, transforming transparency into an active and dynamic process. By gaining international validation, the case of the National University of the Littoral offers a reference framework for other institutions seeking to align their internal processes with global openness standards, demonstrating that collaborative information management is essential for sustainability and modern governance.
