Resultados provisionales del Censo 2022: la población es de 46.044.703 personas

The article details the provisional release of Argentina’s 2022 national census by INDEC, highlighting key demographic shifts and geographic distributions. This data serves as a foundational public resource, enabling researchers and policymakers to move beyond estimates and make evidence-based decisions regarding resource allocation and social planning. A significant finding is the demographic concentration, with three provinces accounting for more than half of the national population. This stark imbalance underscores the need for decentralized data analysis to understand regional disparities in housing, infrastructure, and public services. Such granular insights are crucial for developing targeted policies that address the specific needs of both highly populated urban centers and sparsely inhabited rural areas. The relevance to open data lies in the transparent dissemination of these comprehensive statistics, which foster accountability and stimulate innovation. By providing detailed, accessible metrics on population, gender, and housing, the government empowers civil society and the private sector to create applications and models that improve societal outcomes. This commitment to data openness ensures that vital demographic information is available for public scrutiny and collaborative problem-solving.
