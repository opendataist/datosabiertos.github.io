Se desacelera manufactura al cierre de 2022, estima Inegi

Se desacelera manufactura al cierre de 2022, estima Inegi

Mexico’s manufacturing sector showed signs of deceleration at the end of last year, driven by weakening domestic and international demand. This slowdown reflects broader economic pressures affecting one of the country’s most vital industrial activities, signaling reduced momentum in production despite remaining above pre-pandemic levels. The relevance to open data lies in the necessity for transparent, timely access to these economic indicators. Accurate tracking allows stakeholders to understand market trends, while public availability fosters accountability and enables researchers to analyze the impact of trade dynamics on local industries. Understanding these shifts helps policymakers and businesses anticipate challenges in the industrial sector. By leveraging open statistics, the public can better grasp the connection between global demand and local manufacturing health, promoting informed decision-making based on reliable, publicly accessible economic evidence.
