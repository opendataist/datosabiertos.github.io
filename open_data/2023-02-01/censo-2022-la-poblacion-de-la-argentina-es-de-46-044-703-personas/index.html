Censo 2022: la población de la Argentina es de 46.044.703 personas

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses (INDEC) has released the provisional results of the 2022 Census, confirming a population of approximately 46 million inhabitants, with sustained but moderate growth. This definitive count, validated by international organizations, corrects earlier overestimated projections and establishes a reliable database on demographic distribution, including the female majority and new gender identities, thereby consolidating the statistical quality of the survey despite initial processing errors. The data reveal significant changes in territorial distribution, with population dynamics concentrated in the Province of Buenos Aires and a notable boost in Patagonian areas linked to specific economic activities, such as energy. These figures provide an essential snapshot of internal migration patterns and urban expansion, enabling a more accurate understanding of how economic activity and the geographic environment have shaped the country’s human geography over the past two decades. This publication is crucial for the open data community, as it launches a series of thematic reports on health, population structure, and inequalities, which will be built upon this master dataset. By democratizing access to this detailed and verified demographic information, it fosters government transparency and enables independent analysis, academic research, and technological solutions that improve evidence-based public policy formulation.
