Piden a niños cuidar sus datos en redes sociales

The article warns that minors face significant personal data risks due to unmonitored internet and social media usage. It emphasizes that digital platforms often expose youth to deceptive practices and criminal behaviors, such as phishing and the creation of fake profiles, which can lead to identity theft and exploitation. Specific dangers include sextortion and grooming, where adults manipulate children through trust to solicit intimate images or engage them in sexual activities. These threats highlight the vulnerability of young users who often navigate online spaces without fully understanding the potential consequences for their privacy and physical safety. This content is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for robust privacy protections and data governance frameworks. As digital footprints become increasingly central to societal interaction, ensuring that personal data—especially of vulnerable populations like minors—is securely managed and protected from misuse is essential for maintaining ethical standards in information transparency and public trust.
