Censo: el 64% de la población de menores ingresos son mujeres – 4SEMANAS.COM

The report identifies gender inequality as the primary driver of female poverty in Argentina, revealing a significant wage gap and higher rates of unemployment and informal work among women compared to men. These disparities place the majority of women in the lowest income brackets, highlighting systemic labor market exclusions that perpetuate economic vulnerability. This analysis is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how accessible, publicly funded statistics can uncover structural biases often hidden in aggregate economic indicators. By making such granular gender-disaggregated data transparent, civil society and policymakers can hold institutions accountable and design targeted interventions. Ultimately, the findings underscore the necessity of maintaining robust, free access to statistical evidence. This enables continuous monitoring of social progress and supports the development of policies aimed at reducing the gender gap in employment, income, and social protection, ensuring that data serves as a tool for equity rather than just observation.
