Estas son las zonas de visitas para la Encuesta de Presupuestos Familiares - Nacionales - ABC Color

The National Statistics Institute is conducting the 2025–2026 Household Budget Survey to update the consumer price index. This data collection in Asunción and Central ensures accurate economic tracking. The initiative underscores transparency in official statistics. Participants can verify enumerators through official channels, ensuring data integrity and protecting household confidentiality. This process is vital for open data, as it provides the foundational metrics for inflation and living costs. Reliable, publicly verifiable statistics enable informed policy decisions and foster trust in government transparency efforts.
