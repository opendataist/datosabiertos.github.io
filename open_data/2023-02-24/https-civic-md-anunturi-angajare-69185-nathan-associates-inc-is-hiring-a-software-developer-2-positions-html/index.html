The USAID-funded MISRA project in Moldova aims to strengthen public sector governance and transparency by modernizing the national Open Data Portal. By upgrading this critical digital infrastructure, the initiative seeks to ensure that citizens and private entities can access authentic, real-time government data without restrictions. This technical improvement directly supports broader structural reforms by fostering accountability and improving the business enabling environment through better economic governance. The core objective involves migrating the portal from an outdated CKAN version to the latest release to resolve functional deficiencies for both data providers and administrators. This upgrade includes enhancing extensions, ensuring compatible user authorization systems, and updating migration scripts to preserve existing datasets. These technical actions are essential for maintaining the portal’s reliability and usability, ensuring that the automated, real-time retrieval of statistical and structured data remains efficient and secure for all stakeholders. This recruitment is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates a tangible commitment to maintaining robust, open-source platforms that facilitate public access to information. Upgrading the underlying technology ensures long-term sustainability and interoperability of government systems, which are fundamental pillars of an effective open data ecosystem. By securing skilled developers to manage this transition, the project reinforces the integrity of public data, enabling better civic engagement, research, and policy-making based on accurate, freely available information.
