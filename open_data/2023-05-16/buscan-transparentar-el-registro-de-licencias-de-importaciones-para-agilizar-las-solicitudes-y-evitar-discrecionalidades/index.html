Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

The Civic Coalition has introduced a legislative initiative to establish an Open Data Portal for Imports, aiming to replace the opacity of the current system with a transparent and predictable management framework. The central objective is to eliminate the administrative discretion that characterizes import authorization processes, which have historically been used to unjustifiably restrict economic operations and protect foreign exchange reserves through artificial constraints. To achieve this, the project requires that all information related to applications—including their status, product type, amount, and dates—be published in open, digitally updated formats. It also establishes a strict deadline for resolution and provides for automatic approval through administrative silence if the relevant authority fails to respond within the stipulated timeframe, ensuring that decisions are made based on clear and objective criteria rather than the arbitrariness of officials. This proposal is highly relevant to the open data community, as it promotes the publication of public information in machine-readable structures, facilitating citizen oversight and external analysis. By transforming complex administrative data into accessible information, it empowers society to detect corrupt practices and enhances accountability, aligning state policies with international standards for transparent governance and democratic participation.
