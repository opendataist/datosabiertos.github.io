Censo 2024: Actualización Cartográfica llega al 92,1% en Bolivia y se firman acuerdos con 333 municipios

Censo 2024: Actualización Cartográfica llega al 92,1% en Bolivia y se firman acuerdos con 333 municipios

The Bolivian National Institute of Statistics (INE) has secured cooperation agreements with nearly all municipalities, demonstrating a high level of state prioritization for the upcoming census. This widespread collaboration ensures that demographic data collection will be supported by comprehensive local engagement and administrative coordination across the country. Parallel efforts to update cartographic information are progressing significantly, although remote areas face challenges due to weather conditions. These geographical updates are critical for defining precise work zones, enabling officials to accurately estimate the necessary number of ballots, personnel, and logistical resources for the national census. This progress is vital for open data, as it establishes a robust, geographically accurate foundation for future statistical transparency. By finalizing these maps before the census, the INE enables more precise, accessible, and reliable public data, fostering better-informed decision-making and greater accountability in governance.
