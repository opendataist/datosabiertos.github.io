NMC creating centralised repository of doctors practising in country, all docs to have UID - OrissaPOST

The implementation of a mandatory Unique Identification Number for all medical practitioners marks a significant shift toward centralized oversight of healthcare professionals in India. This move ensures that every doctor practicing in the country is registered with the National Medical Commission, creating a unified system that transcends state boundaries. By centralizing this critical data, the regulatory body aims to establish a transparent and accountable framework for medical practice across the nation. The resulting National Medical Register will serve as a publicly accessible database, displaying comprehensive details such as qualifications, specialties, and places of work. This transparency allows patients and other stakeholders to easily verify a practitioner’s credentials and professional history. The public availability of this information fosters trust and enables informed decision-making, while also providing a mechanism for identifying qualified professionals and ensuring they adhere to established medical standards. This initiative is highly relevant to open data as it transforms private professional records into public resources. By making verification data freely available, it reduces information asymmetry between providers and patients. Furthermore, this standardized, accessible dataset offers valuable potential for broader analysis, including workforce planning and quality assurance research. Ultimately, it demonstrates how government-led data transparency can enhance accountability and integrity within essential public services like healthcare.
