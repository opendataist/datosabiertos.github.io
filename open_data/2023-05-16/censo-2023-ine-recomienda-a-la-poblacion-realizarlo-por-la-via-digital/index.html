Censo 2023: INE recomienda a la población realizarlo por la vía digital

The Uruguayan National Institute of Statistics is actively promoting the digital census to collect essential demographic data, highlighting its efficiency and success compared to regional counterparts. By encouraging widespread digital participation, the initiative aims to streamline the data collection process, thereby reducing the need for intrusive and labor-intensive household visits. This strategy not only accelerates the completion of the census but also enhances the overall quality and speed of the information-gathering phase. The campaign emphasizes the benefits of digital completion, such as avoiding in-person visits and offering incentives like telecommunications prizes, which serve to boost public engagement. Significant regional disparities in participation rates have emerged, with urban areas leading and rural departments lagging behind. This imbalance underscores the necessity for targeted efforts to ensure equitable data representation across the entire country, particularly in regions where digital adoption is lower. This article is relevant to open data because it illustrates the modernization of statistical production through technology. The digital census generates structured, machine-readable data that can be more easily processed, shared, and utilized by researchers, policymakers, and the public. By shifting to a digital-first approach, the government enhances transparency and accessibility, ensuring that high-quality demographic information is available for open analysis and informed decision-making within the community.
