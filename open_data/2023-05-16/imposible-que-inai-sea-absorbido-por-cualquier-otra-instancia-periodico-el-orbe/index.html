Imposible que INAI sea Absorbido por Cualquier Otra Instancia - Periódico El Orbe.

Imposible que INAI sea Absorbido por Cualquier Otra Instancia - Periódico El Orbe.

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information, and Personal Data Protection (INAI) asserts its irreplaceable role in safeguarding citizens’ rights, emphasizing that other government bodies cannot replicate its specialized functions. Despite facing operational challenges due to a lack of a full quorum, the institution continues to enforce transparency laws and protect personal data across both the public and private sectors, demonstrating resilience through the dedication of its remaining staff. A central focus of INAI’s mission is the ongoing training of private-sector entities to ensure compliance with data protection regulations. By recognizing companies that actively improve their data-handling practices, the institute aims to foster a robust culture of legal compliance and trust. This initiative encourages businesses to implement strong internal processes, thereby enhancing the security of personal information shared by consumers and strengthening the overall integrity of data protection standards in Mexico. This article is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical infrastructure required to manage information responsibly. Effective open-data initiatives depend on strong legal frameworks and institutional oversight to balance transparency with privacy. INAI’s work ensures that, while data is accessible, the fundamental rights of individuals are protected, creating a trustworthy environment where information can be shared openly without compromising personal security or legal standards.
