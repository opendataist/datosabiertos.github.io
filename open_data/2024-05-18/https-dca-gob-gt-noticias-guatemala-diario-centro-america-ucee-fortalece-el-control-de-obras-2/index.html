Guatemala launched an open data portal to track state building projects across the education, defense, and health sectors. This initiative aims to ensure transparency by publishing project details, logs, and costs, thereby promoting accountability in public infrastructure. The platform mandates that all future bids be public, allowing citizens to verify official pricing. This move directly addresses corruption risks by making procurement processes visible, ensuring that resources are managed efficiently and ethically. This article is relevant to open_data as it demonstrates how releasing granular public sector data empowers civic oversight. By making construction projects accessible, the government fosters trust and enables citizens to demand higher quality standards in essential public services.
