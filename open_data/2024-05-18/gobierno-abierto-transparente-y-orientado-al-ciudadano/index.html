Gobierno abierto, transparente y orientado al ciudadano

The article highlights a significant crisis of confidence in Cali’s local government, driven by widespread perceptions of inefficiency and opacity. This context underscores the urgent need to rebuild institutional credibility through open government strategies. By prioritizing transparency and innovative public services, the administration aims to bridge the gap between state actors and citizens, recognizing that sustainable development relies heavily on restoring trust and improving the daily experience of the population. Central to this restoration effort is the commitment to good governance, which serves as a foundational pillar of the city’s development plan. The strategy focuses on modernizing institutional infrastructure and optimizing resource management to enhance operational effectiveness. This approach seeks to transform the administrative apparatus into a more responsive entity, ensuring that public actions are not only efficient but also demonstrably accountable to the public, thereby addressing the erosion of faith in political representation. This narrative is particularly relevant to open data because it positions the availability and transparency of information as essential tools for civic engagement and administrative reform. By framing data openness as a mechanism for dialogue and accountability, the text illustrates how accessible public information can empower active citizenship. This alignment demonstrates that open data is not merely a technical requirement but a critical component of democratic renewal, enabling the continuous interaction necessary to legitimize public institutions and foster a collaborative relationship between the government and the community.
