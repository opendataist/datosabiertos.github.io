Name 302 stations sanctioned, MRA urges NBC

Name 302 stations sanctioned, MRA urges NBC

The Media Rights Agenda has invoked Nigeria’s Freedom of Information Act to demand transparency from the National Broadcasting Commission regarding recent regulatory actions. By seeking detailed records of sanctions imposed on hundreds of stations, the organization highlights the critical role of open data in ensuring that regulatory bodies operate with accountability and public scrutiny rather than behind closed doors. The request emphasizes that access to specific operational details, such as complaint origins, investigation outcomes, and fine amounts, is essential for understanding the consistency and fairness of broadcasting codes. This focus moves beyond simple statistics to uncover the procedural integrity of the regulatory process, demonstrating that true openness requires granular, accessible data rather than broad summaries. This case underscores the vital importance of open data in media governance and democratic oversight. When regulatory decisions affecting public discourse are subject to public verification, it strengthens trust in institutions. Consequently, this initiative serves as a powerful reminder that robust open data frameworks are necessary tools for civil society to monitor power and hold authorities accountable for their actions.
