¿Alguien creía que el billete verde había dejado de ser alcista?

The U.S. Federal Reserve warns that persistent inflation could force it to keep interest rates higher than expected, negatively impacting stock markets and strengthening the dollar. This outlook shifts expectations of imminent monetary easing, altering global economic dynamics and generating uncertainty about the future trajectory of financial assets. The article is relevant to the field of open data because it highlights the critical importance of the availability and transparency of economic information. When market participants can quickly access and analyze official data on inflation and central bank decisions, they can anticipate changes in interest rates. This demonstrates how structured public data are essential for informed decision-making, market stability, and accurate risk assessment within the international financial system.
