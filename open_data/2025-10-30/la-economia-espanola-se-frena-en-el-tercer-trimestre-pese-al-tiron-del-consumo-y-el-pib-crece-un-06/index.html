La economía española se frena en el tercer trimestre pese al tirón del consumo y el PIB crece un 0,6%

La economía española se frena en el tercer trimestre pese al tirón del consumo y el PIB crece un 0,6%

Spain’s economic growth is consolidating thanks to internal dynamism, led by household consumption and investment, which allows the country to maintain an expansionary trajectory despite the weakening of external demand and exports. This structure strengthens the resilience of the national economy in the face of global geopolitical uncertainty and validates optimistic expectations about its ability to lead growth among the major advanced economies in the near future. The relevance of this report for the open data community lies in the transparent and structured nature of the data published by the National Statistics Institute. The public availability of these detailed macroeconomic indicators, broken down by demand, supply, and income, is essential for quantitative analysis, validation of predictive models, and academic research. By making this data accessible, institutional transparency is fostered, enabling various stakeholders, from researchers to private companies, to generate value from public information. However, the analysis also reveals critical nuances that deserve attention in the study of open data. The contraction in exports and the slight decline in labor productivity indicate structural vulnerabilities that persist beneath the surface of aggregate growth. Therefore, the responsible use of these open data implies not only celebrating positive outcomes but also investigating these deviations to better understand real economic challenges and improve the accuracy of future evidence-based projections.
