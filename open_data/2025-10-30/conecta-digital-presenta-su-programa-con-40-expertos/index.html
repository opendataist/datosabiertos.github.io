Conecta DIGITAL presenta su programa con 40 expertos

Conecta DIGITAL has established itself as an essential reference point for analyzing the convergence between the audiovisual industry and technological innovation, redefining the paradigms of content creation, production, and distribution. The event stands out for bringing together key industry players to discuss business models, digital monetization, and the impact of artificial intelligence on storytelling, emphasizing the need to adapt creative strategies to new consumption habits. A fundamental aspect addressed is the evolution of copyright and legal protection in the digital era, alongside the use of immersive technologies and gaming as strategic tools for marketing and audience engagement. Through practical experiences and startup showcases, it demonstrates how interactivity and immersive narratives are transforming the relationship with young audiences and opening new avenues for funding and collaboration between talent and technology. This gathering is particularly relevant to the open data community, as it illustrates the critical intersection between rights management, transparency in business models, and access to open or open-source technological tools that facilitate innovation. By discussing intellectual property protection and monetization in a complex digital environment, the event invites reflection on how the availability and ethical use of data can drive creative sustainability and democratize access to culture in today’s digital transformation.
