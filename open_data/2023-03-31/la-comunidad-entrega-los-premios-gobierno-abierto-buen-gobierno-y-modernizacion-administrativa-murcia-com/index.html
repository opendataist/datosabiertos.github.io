La Comunidad entrega los 'Premios Gobierno Abierto, Buen Gobierno y Modernización Administrativa' - murcia.com

The Region of Murcia has publicly recognized innovative projects that prioritize transparency, institutional collaboration, and citizen participation, thereby consolidating an ecosystem in which the administration closely aligns with societal needs. This collective effort underscores the necessity of modernizing tools to better engage citizens, improving public services through shared commitment between administrations and citizens, which in turn strengthens trust in public institutions. The significance of these projects lies in their ability to democratize information and facilitate access to essential data, ranging from optimizing tourism experiences to providing fiscal education in schools. By integrating technologies such as artificial intelligence and interactive digital platforms, barriers to access are broken down, enabling diverse stakeholders—including young people and businesses—to actively participate in the management and evaluation of services. This transforms governance into a more inclusive and responsive process. For the open data community, this scenario is emblematic because it demonstrates the tangible impact of making information available. When administrations make their data understandable and usable, they not only comply with transparency standards but also enable social and business innovation. These awards validate that open data, coupled with user-centered design, is fundamental to creating more informed, participatory, and efficient societies.
