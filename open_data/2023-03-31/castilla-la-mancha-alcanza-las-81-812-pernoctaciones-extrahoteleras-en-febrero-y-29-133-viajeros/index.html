Castilla-La Mancha alcanza las 81.812 pernoctaciones extrahoteleras en febrero y 29.133 viajeros

The article highlights a robust recovery in Spain’s non-hotel tourism sector, with February registrations surpassing pre-pandemic levels for the second consecutive month. This growth is primarily driven by a significant surge in foreign visitors, indicating strong international demand for alternative accommodation types such as apartments and rural lodgings. Nationally, the sector demonstrates resilience and expanding appeal beyond traditional hotel infrastructure. At the regional level, Castilla-La Mancha shows varied performance across different accommodation types, with apartments and rural tourism attracting the highest occupancy rates. While specific statistical fluctuations exist, the overall trend reflects steady engagement from both national and international travelers, underscoring the region’s capacity to effectively host diverse tourist segments during the early part of the year. This data is relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the value of transparent, granular tourism statistics. By making detailed information on occupancy, occupancy rates, and demographics publicly accessible, stakeholders can analyze market trends, optimize resource allocation, and support evidence-based policy decisions. Such transparency fosters accountability and enables researchers and businesses to leverage high-quality datasets for economic analysis and strategic planning within the tourism industry.
