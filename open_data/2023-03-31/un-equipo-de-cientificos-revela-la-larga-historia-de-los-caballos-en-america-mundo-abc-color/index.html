Un equipo de científicos revela la larga historia de los caballos en América - Mundo - ABC Color

The study shows that Native Americans integrated horses into their culture decades before the 1680 Pueblo Revolt, challenging established historical narratives through the analysis of DNA and archaeological data. This research values Indigenous knowledge and calls for animal remains to be treated with the same rigor as cultural artifacts, promoting a more inclusive and respectful methodology in scientific research. This is relevant to open data by highlighting the need to integrate diverse datasets, including community-based and genomic sources, to construct more accurate historical accounts. This reinforces the importance of transparency and open collaboration in reconstructing the past.
