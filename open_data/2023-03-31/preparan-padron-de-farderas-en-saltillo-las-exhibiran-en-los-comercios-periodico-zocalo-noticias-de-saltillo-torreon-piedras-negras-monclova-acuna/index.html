Preparan padrón de farderas en Saltillo; las exhibirán en los comercios | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

Merchants in Saltillo are creating a database of repeat offenders to alert shop owners. By displaying masked images along with first names, businesses can identify dangerous individuals while respecting legal privacy constraints. This tool empowers workers to watch for known offenders, enhancing public safety through shared information. The initiative involves installing more street cameras linked to the central command center. Unlike private store cameras, these municipal units monitor public spaces, allowing police to track the routes of perpetrators in real time. This infrastructure aims to provide continuous surveillance in high-risk areas. This case highlights the open data challenge of balancing transparency with privacy laws. It demonstrates how structured local data can improve security when aggregated and shared responsibly among stakeholders, offering a model for community-driven safety solutions that respect individual rights.
