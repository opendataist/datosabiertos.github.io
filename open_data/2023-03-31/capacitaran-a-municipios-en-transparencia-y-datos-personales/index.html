Capacitarán a municipios en transparencia y datos personales

A strategic partnership between San Luis Potosí’s transparency commission and the municipal strengthening agency aims to empower local governments with essential training and resources. This initiative addresses the frequent compliance challenges faced by municipalities, ensuring they have the necessary tools to effectively meet their transparency obligations. The collaboration prioritizes robust capacity building across all municipal jurisdictions, establishing a unified agenda to strengthen institutional frameworks. By focusing on education and procedural clarity, the agreement seeks to harmonize practices among local authorities, thereby reducing uncertainties and standardizing the provision of public information. This development significantly advances open data principles by enhancing citizens’ certainty regarding their right to access information. It transforms transparency from a legal requirement into a practical, accessible reality for the public, fostering greater democratic accountability and trust in local governance through improved institutional performance.
