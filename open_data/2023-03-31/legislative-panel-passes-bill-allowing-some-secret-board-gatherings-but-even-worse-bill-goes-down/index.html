Legislative panel passes bill allowing some secret board gatherings, but even worse bill goes down

Arkansas lawmakers recently rejected legislation that would have severely restricted public access to government documents and increased associated costs, a victory for transparency. This outcome protects citizens’ right to scrutinize how their tax funds are utilized by state agencies. However, a concerning alternative bill was approved, permitting secret gatherings among public boards if attendance remains below a third. This provision threatens to undermine the Freedom of Information Act by allowing significant government deliberations to occur outside public view. These developments highlight the critical importance of open data and public records in maintaining democratic accountability. Strong opposition is needed to prevent further erosion of transparency, ensuring that government operations remain visible and accessible to all citizens.
