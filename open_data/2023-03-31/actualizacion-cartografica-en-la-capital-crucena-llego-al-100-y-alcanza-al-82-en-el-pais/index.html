Actualización cartográfica en la capital cruceña llegó al 100% y alcanza al 82% en el país

The completion of the statistical cartographic update in Santa Cruz marks a critical milestone for Bolivia’s upcoming 2024 census. By finalizing this foundational mapping work, the National Institute of Statistics has secured a formal agreement with local authorities, ensuring the logistical readiness required for an accurate national population and housing count. This progress validates the government’s technical approach amid previous political tensions, establishing a reliable framework for data collection. This geographic precision is essential for open data initiatives, as it directly determines the quality and accessibility of future demographic statistics. Accurate cartography enables precise resource allocation, allowing researchers and citizens to analyze social indicators with greater granularity. Without this underlying spatial infrastructure, any resulting open datasets would lack the necessary geographic context for meaningful comparative studies or policy evaluation. Ultimately, the article highlights how administrative coordination and technical rigor underpin transparent governance. It demonstrates that open data is not merely about releasing information, but about ensuring the underlying infrastructure supports valid, geospatially referenced insights. This process sets a precedent for how statistical bodies can collaborate with local governments to produce high-quality, usable data that empowers civil society and drives evidence-based decision-making.
