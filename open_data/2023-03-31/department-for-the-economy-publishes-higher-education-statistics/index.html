Department for the Economy publishes higher education statistics

Northern Ireland’s higher education sector has experienced significant growth, marked by a substantial decade-long rise in student enrollments and a notable increase in the proportion of international students. This expansion reflects broader trends in accessibility and global appeal, with a record number of learners arriving from non-EU countries. The data indicates a sustained upward trajectory in student numbers across all study levels, driven by increased capacity and pandemic-related shifts in enrollment patterns. Simultaneously, the volume of qualifications awarded shows a contrasting short-term fluctuation despite long-term gains. While the total number of graduates has grown over the last ten years, recent years saw a slight dip following a temporary surge linked to pandemic-era course adjustments and policy changes. As normal academic procedures resume, award outcomes are returning to pre-pandemic standards, with the majority of first-degree graduates achieving high classifications, signaling a stabilization in the quality and delivery of academic credentials. This publication is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it marks a pivotal shift in how statistical information is presented. By releasing these bulletins in HTML format for the first time, the Department introduces interactive tables that allow users to explore the data dynamically rather than statically. This move enhances transparency and user engagement, enabling researchers and the public to access and analyze nuanced details about enrollment and qualification trends more effectively through an open, accessible digital interface.
