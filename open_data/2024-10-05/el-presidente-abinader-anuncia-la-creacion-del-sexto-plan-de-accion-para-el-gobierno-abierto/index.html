El presidente Abinader anuncia la creación del Sexto Plan de Acción para el Gobierno Abierto

The Dominican Republic is advancing its commitment to transparency through the Sixth Action Plan for Open Government, emphasizing that access to public management information is a fundamental citizen right. The administration has established a Multi-actor Forum and a National Open Data Policy to foster a culture of accountability, ensuring that every individual can understand how public resources are administered. This initiative aims to bridge the gap between the government and citizens by guaranteeing freedom of information and promoting ethical public practices. Implementation relies heavily on digital platforms and data-driven strategies to enhance service efficiency and proximity. By creating a National Data Commission and releasing open data plans, the government seeks to support the development of transparent public policies. This approach facilitates better oversight systems and encourages civic participation, allowing citizens to provide meaningful feedback on municipal services and healthcare delivery through accessible digital channels. This article is highly relevant to open data because it illustrates the institutionalization of data governance as a core component of democratic accountability. The establishment of dedicated commissions and national policies demonstrates a structural shift toward treating data as a public asset. Such frameworks enable independent analysis, improve policy evaluation, and empower civil society to monitor government performance effectively, thereby strengthening the open data ecosystem beyond mere publication to active civic engagement.
