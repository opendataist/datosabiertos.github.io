Presidente Luis Abinader: “Esta gestión está comprometida con la transparencia”. Otras Noticias Presidenciales - El Sol de la Florida

Presidente Luis Abinader: “Esta gestión está comprometida con la transparencia”. Otras Noticias Presidenciales - El Sol de la Florida

The Dominican government is reinforcing its commitment to open governance by implementing concrete measures such as digital access to public information and multi-stakeholder forums. These initiatives aim to empower citizens through transparency, ensuring that public resource management is accessible and fostering a culture of accountability. By guaranteeing the right to know, the administration seeks to strengthen democratic trust and encourage active civic participation in public life. This strategy directly impacts open data by establishing institutional frameworks that prioritize the publication and availability of state information. The creation of platforms like EfiCompras RD and the ongoing action plans demonstrate a systemic shift toward data-driven governance. These efforts are crucial for reducing corruption and promoting integrity, as they allow civil society to monitor government actions effectively and hold officials accountable for their decisions. The relevance to open data lies in the tangible infrastructure built to support transparency. By digitizing processes and engaging international alliances, the country creates the necessary conditions for data to be not just available, but usable for democratic oversight. This approach validates the role of open data as a tool for social equity and economic stability, showing how accessible information can directly contribute to good governance and the prevention of illicit activities.
