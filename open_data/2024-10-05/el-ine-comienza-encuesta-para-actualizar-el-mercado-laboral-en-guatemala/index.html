El INE comienza encuesta para actualizar el mercado laboral en Guatemala

Guatemala has launched a new quarterly survey to provide updated data on labor market dynamics and household incomes, addressing a significant gap in official statistics since 2021. This initiative, driven by the National Statistics Institute and the Central Bank, aims to capture detailed socio-economic indicators across urban and rural areas. By regularly monitoring employment rates and income levels, policymakers can better understand the immediate effects of monetary policies on households, particularly regarding inflation trends. The relevance to open data lies in the commitment to transparent, frequent, and accessible statistical production. Moving away from sporadic reporting, this continuous data collection fosters a culture of accountability and evidence-based governance. Open access to these high-frequency datasets allows researchers, journalists, and civil society to scrutinize economic health in real-time, reducing information asymmetry and enhancing public trust in institutional metrics. Ultimately, this structured approach ensures that critical socio-demographic information remains available for public analysis. By standardizing data release schedules and expanding the scope of inquiry, the state supports informed decision-making for both the public and private sectors. This transparency empowers citizens to engage more deeply with economic discussions, ensuring that development strategies are grounded in accurate, up-to-date realities rather than outdated models.
