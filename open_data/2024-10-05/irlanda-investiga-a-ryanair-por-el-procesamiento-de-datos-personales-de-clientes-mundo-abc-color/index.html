Irlanda investiga a Ryanair por el procesamiento de datos personales de clientes - Mundo - ABC Color

Irlanda investiga a Ryanair por el procesamiento de datos personales de clientes - Mundo - ABC Color

The Irish Data Protection Commission has launched a transnational investigation into Ryanair’s use of biometric verification for third-party bookings. This inquiry aims to determine whether the airline’s practices comply with the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in terms of legality and transparency. The relevance to open data lies in the scrutiny of the processing of sensitive personal information. It highlights the critical balance between security measures and privacy rights, emphasizing that even legitimate security checks must adhere to strict data protection standards. This case underscores the importance of transparent data handling practices in the digital travel sector.
