Electric Future, Established Name: The New ID. Tiguan Premieres in October - CleanTechnica

Volkswagen is expanding its electric vehicle lineup with the upcoming ID. Tiguan, scheduled for a 2027 market launch. This new all-electric SUV aims to replace the existing ID.4 and ID.5 models, leveraging the renowned prestige and reliability of the traditional Tiguan brand. By maintaining the established naming convention, the company seeks to provide customers with clear orientation and confidence as they transition toward electromobility. The ID. Tiguan is positioned to offer premium design, generous interior space, and innovative technologies, embodying the "True Volkswagen" product promise. Crucially, this electric model will not replace its internal combustion counterpart. Instead, both versions will coexist, allowing customers to choose between different powertrain preferences while benefiting from the trusted Tiguan identity. This strategy highlights a dual approach to meeting diverse consumer needs within the same vehicle segment. This announcement is relevant to open_data because it exemplifies how major manufacturers use transparent product positioning and branding strategies to navigate the complex transition from fossil fuels to electric vehicles. For researchers and analysts, understanding such strategic shifts provides valuable insights into market dynamics, consumer adoption patterns, and the evolution of automotive naming conventions in an era defined by sustainability and digital transformation.
