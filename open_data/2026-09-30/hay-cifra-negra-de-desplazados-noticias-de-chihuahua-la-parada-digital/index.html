¡Hay cifra negra de desplazados! - Noticias de Chihuahua - La Parada Digital

INEGI reveals a significant hidden figure among those displaced by violence, driven by fear of retaliation and distrust of institutions. This underreporting limits access to government assistance, affecting the local economy and increasing the urban vulnerability of victims. The relevance of open data lies in the urgent need for methodologies that capture these hidden figures to ensure rights and effective public policies.
