‘South Park’ se burlará de los 'multimillonarios estúpidos' y los centros de datos

‘South Park’ se burlará de los 'multimillonarios estúpidos' y los centros de datos

The new season of the animated series criticizes the proliferation of data centers driven by billionaires, reflecting growing public concern about their environmental impact and effects on quality of life. This satire resonates with recent polls showing majority public opposition to these facilities, even among political groups that traditionally support technological initiatives. The series' name change to "South America" serves as a direct mockery of the Trump administration and tech giants, who have manipulated geographic symbols for political or corporate reasons. This gesture underscores the tension between private interests in digital infrastructure and symbolic sovereignty, highlighting how technology intertwines with identity politics and populist rhetoric. This coverage is relevant to the field of open data because it highlights the critical gap between technological development and community transparency. By exposing how the construction of data infrastructure directly affects local life without social consensus, the article invites reflection on the need to democratize access to information and include communities in decisions about the impact of the digital age, thereby promoting more ethical and participatory data governance.
