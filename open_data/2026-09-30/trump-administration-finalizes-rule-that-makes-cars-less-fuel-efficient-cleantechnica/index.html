Trump Administration Finalizes Rule that Makes Cars Less Fuel Efficient - CleanTechnica

Trump Administration Finalizes Rule that Makes Cars Less Fuel Efficient - CleanTechnica

Rolling back federal fuel economy standards significantly increases household expenses by making vehicles less efficient. This policy shift prioritizes automaker interests over economic relief for families already struggling with high costs of living and essential transportation needs. The reversal eliminates billions in potential fuel savings and reduces the incentive for lower gasoline consumption. Consequently, drivers face higher long-term costs for basic mobility, exacerbating affordability crises and imposing broader economic burdens on consumers. This decision negatively impacts open data initiatives by obscuring the true societal costs of transportation. It hinders transparency regarding environmental health outcomes and prevents comprehensive analysis of policy effectiveness, ultimately limiting public access to critical information about climate and economic impacts.
