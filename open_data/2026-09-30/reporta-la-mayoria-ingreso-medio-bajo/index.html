Reporta la mayoría ingreso medio-bajo

Reporta la mayoría ingreso medio-bajo

INEGI data reveal that most Mexican municipalities are concentrated in middle- and low-income strata, demonstrating that despite the national increase in current incomes between 2022 and 2024, marked regional inequalities persist. This heterogeneous distribution suggests that economic growth has not benefited the entire territory uniformly, maintaining significant gaps between localities. A comparison of recent periods highlights an evolution more comparable to previous years, showing both real increases and decreases in various municipalities. However, the extreme disparity between localities with the highest and lowest resources remains critical, underscoring the need for targeted policies that address the existing structural asymmetries in the distribution of municipal wealth. This analysis is fundamental to the open data agenda, as it illustrates how the availability and transparency of georeferenced statistics enable precise monitoring of socioeconomic inequities. By democratizing access to these indicators, public scrutiny and the formulation of evidence-based policies are facilitated, which are essential for promoting greater equity and territorial development grounded in the available evidence.
