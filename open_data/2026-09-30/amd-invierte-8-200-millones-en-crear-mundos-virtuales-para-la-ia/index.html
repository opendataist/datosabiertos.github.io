AMD invierte 8.200 millones en crear mundos virtuales para la IA

AMD invierte 8.200 millones en crear mundos virtuales para la IA

AMD’s acquisition of World Labs marks a key strategic move to position its hardware for the future of spatial artificial intelligence. This acquisition enables the company to better understand the computational needs of world models, thereby designing technological solutions more closely aligned with the next generation of AI and robotics systems. The central focus is on “spatiality” and the creation of interactive 3D virtual environments, leveraging Fei-Fei Li’s expertise in computer vision. By integrating this capability, AMD aims to accelerate the development of robots that can be trained in precise simulations before operating in the physical world, directly competing with similar approaches from rivals such as Nvidia. This move is significant for open data, as world models fundamentally rely on large, labeled, and open datasets to understand space and object behavior. The push toward spatial intelligence drives the need to share and standardize visual and contextual data, fostering collaborative ecosystems where information transparency is essential for training more accurate and accessible algorithms.
