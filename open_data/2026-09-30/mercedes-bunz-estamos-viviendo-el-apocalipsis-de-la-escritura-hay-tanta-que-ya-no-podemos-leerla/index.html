Mercedes Bunz: "Estamos viviendo el apocalipsis de la escritura, hay tanta que ya no podemos leerla"

Mercedes Bunz: "Estamos viviendo el apocalipsis de la escritura, hay tanta que ya no podemos leerla"

Mercedes Bunz emphasizes the urgency of building a community-based, public Artificial Intelligence, grounded in open datasets for the social good. This stance is crucial for the *open data* movement, as it proposes an ethical alternative to dominant commercial models, ensuring that technology serves the common good rather than merely corporate interests that may exclude marginalized communities through legal barriers. The text underscores that open access to data is fundamental to democratizing knowledge creation and preventing the concentration of power in the hands of a few. By promoting shared datasets for non-profit purposes, transparency and digital sovereignty are strengthened, enabling citizens to actively participate in training AI systems that address real-world needs, such as healthcare or education, rather than relying on proprietary "black box" systems. Finally, the discussion on creativity and authorship in the AI era highlights the need for clear regulations regarding co-creation. Bunz argues that while technology facilitates production, human value lies in intention and critical reflection. This reinforces the relevance of *open data* as a shared resource that, under an ethical framework, can foster collaborative innovation without compromising intellectual integrity or social responsibility in the use of public information.
