Enfermedades del corazón lideran la mortalidad en Oaxaca

Cardiovascular diseases are the leading cause of mortality in Oaxaca, surpassing other chronic-degenerative conditions. This fact underscores the urgency of addressing public health from a preventive perspective, as the disease burden disproportionately affects the local population and calls for health policies focused on early detection and modification of risk factors. Transparency in the generation of these statistics is essential for society. Since these are preliminary data collected from official sources such as the Civil Registry and forensic services, their timely publication enables citizens and public officials to monitor the health situation. This review and verification process ensures that the eventual information is accurate, serving as a foundation for effective interventions. The relevance to open data lies in the need to make epidemiological datasets available in an open, standardized, and freely accessible manner. Only by facilitating access to these raw and processed figures can independent analysis be conducted by researchers, media outlets, and civil society organizations. This fosters accountability and the development of innovative solutions to public health crises, empowering the community with verifiable information to make informed decisions about their well-being.
