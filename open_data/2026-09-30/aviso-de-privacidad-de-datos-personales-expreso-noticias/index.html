Aviso de Privacidad de Datos Personales - EXPRESO Noticias

Aviso de Privacidad de Datos Personales - EXPRESO Noticias

The article details how Mediades and Editorial de Sonora protect personal data, distinguishing between the use necessary to provide services and additional purposes such as market research. The key point is that refusing the second type of data processing does not prevent users from obtaining the contracted services, ensuring that users can manage their information freely without facing penalties. This practice is relevant to open data because it establishes an ethical precedent of transparency and user control over personal information. By separating essential data processing from secondary uses, it promotes an environment where data can be used to improve services without compromising privacy or individual autonomy, fostering the trust necessary for responsible information management.
