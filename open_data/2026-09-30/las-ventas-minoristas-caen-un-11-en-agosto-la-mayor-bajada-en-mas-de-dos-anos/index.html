Las ventas minoristas caen un 1,1% en agosto, la mayor bajada en más de dos años

Spanish retail showed a significant slowdown in August, marking the largest decline since mid-2024. While overall sales retreated, sustained growth was evident in e-commerce and large chains, contrasting with a sharp downturn among small businesses. This dynamic reveals a clear structural shift toward digitalization and market concentration, where technological adaptation appears key to maintaining competitiveness amid pressure on traditional local retail. This phenomenon is relevant to open data because it underscores the need for access to disaggregated, high-frequency statistics. Only through detailed open data by channel and region can these divergent trends be accurately analyzed, supporting evidence-based business and policy decision-making.
