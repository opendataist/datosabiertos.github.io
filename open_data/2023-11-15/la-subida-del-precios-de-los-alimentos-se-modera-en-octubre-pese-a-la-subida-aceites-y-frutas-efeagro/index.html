La subida del precios de los alimentos se modera en octubre pese a la subida aceites y frutas - EFEAgro

Spain’s October inflation data shows that food prices increased, primarily due to fresh fruits and cooking oils, while some staple items, such as potatoes, experienced slight monthly declines. The overall Consumer Price Index (CPI) remained stable at 3.5% year-on-year, indicating that although grocery costs continue to strain households, the general inflation trend has plateaued compared to previous months. This update is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of tracking granular price indices within publicly available statistical datasets. By analyzing these specific categories, researchers and citizens can better understand how macroeconomic indicators translate into everyday economic realities, thereby promoting greater transparency in how public institutions monitor and report changes in the cost of living.
