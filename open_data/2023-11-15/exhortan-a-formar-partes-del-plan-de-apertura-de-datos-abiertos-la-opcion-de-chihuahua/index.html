Exhortan a formar partes del Plan de Apertura de Datos Abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua is advancing its open data policy to promote honest governance by inviting civil society and experts to co-design the framework. This collaborative effort aims to publish high-value, accessible information that empowers citizens to conduct independent research. By enabling public analysis of municipal data, the initiative seeks to support the development of better public policies. This approach transforms raw government information into a tool for social improvement, ensuring transparency and efficient administrative practices through shared insights. This case is vital for open data because it demonstrates community-driven data prioritization. It highlights the necessity of involving civil society in selecting relevant datasets, proving that effective open government requires active citizen participation to ensure that released data genuinely serves the public interest and accountability.
