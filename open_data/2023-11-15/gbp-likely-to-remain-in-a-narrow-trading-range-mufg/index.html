GBP likely to remain in a narrow trading range – MUFG

Recent UK employment and wage data present mixed signals, balancing stronger labor demand with moderating wage growth. This ambiguity suggests the Monetary Policy Committee will likely maintain its current stance, as the evidence does not yet warrant significant policy shifts. Consequently, the British Pound is expected to trade within a narrow range, reflecting uncertainty amidst ongoing economic disinflation trends. This analysis highlights the critical role of transparent, high-frequency economic data in shaping monetary policy expectations. For open data initiatives, the release of granular employment metrics allows independent analysts to interpret market implications, fostering informed public discourse and accountability.
