Cuál es la diferencia entre open banking, open finance y open data

The article highlights how the integration of technology in banking has evolved from open banking to open finance and open data, creating seamless financial experiences for users. Open banking facilitates secure data sharing between institutions, while open finance expands this access to investments and insurance sectors. Ultimately, open data represents the broader movement of making diverse information, including government statistics, accessible to the public. This progression simplifies how individuals manage their finances by aggregating data from multiple sources into single, user-friendly platforms. A significant challenge identified in Latin America is building user trust when sharing personal information with third-party organizations. The CEO emphasizes the importance of understanding end-users to foster this confidence, noting that many people already engage with these concepts unknowingly. Establishing transparency and security is crucial for the widespread adoption of these technologies, as trust remains the primary barrier to their full implementation in the regional market. The relevance to open data lies in its role as the foundational layer for these financial innovations. By promoting the accessibility and interoperability of information, open data standards enable the aggregation seen in open finance platforms. Furthermore, the article underscores the transformative power of artificial intelligence in processing this data, suggesting that as AI becomes integral to daily tasks, the demand for accessible, standardized data infrastructure will only increase. This shift necessitates robust open data frameworks to support future technological advancements in finance.
