Reporters Without Borders has launched an initiative to develop an artificial intelligence tool aimed at protecting the intellectual property of media outlets and journalists. This project seeks to counter the unauthorized use of journalistic content by major AI companies, reaffirming the intrinsic value of verified information in the face of disinformation risks. The solution is based on an open-source approach, promoting transparency and technological sovereignty in journalism. By enabling tools to systematically cite reliable sources, it empowers professionals to regain control over their production processes. This fosters a responsible culture in technological development, prioritizing quality and reliability over mere automation. This initiative is crucial for the field of open data, as it advocates that artificial intelligence must be trained with transparency and explicit consent. It promotes an ecosystem where journalistic data are not treated as extractive resources, but as protected assets that ensure informational integrity. In this way, it sets a precedent for balancing technological innovation with digital rights and ethical standards in the handling of public information.
