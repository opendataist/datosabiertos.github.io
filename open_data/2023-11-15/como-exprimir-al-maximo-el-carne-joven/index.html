Cómo 'exprimir' al máximo el Carné Joven

The primary conclusion is that open data serves as a powerful catalyst for educational innovation and the improvement of public services. By leveraging publicly available datasets, students from IES Alonso de Madrigal successfully developed a mobile application that significantly enhances the utility of the Regional Youth Card. This project, which won a national competition dedicated to open data initiatives, demonstrates how accessible information can be transformed into practical tools that solve real-world problems for citizens. The application maximizes the benefits of the youth card by providing real-time access to discounts, digital card management, and geolocation services for participating establishments. This achievement highlights the direct impact of open data ecosystems on community engagement. It allows users to navigate local offers efficiently while encouraging the development of user-centric digital services. The project proves that open data is not merely a bureaucratic requirement but a valuable resource for creating tangible social and economic value through technology. This case is highly relevant to open data because it exemplifies the transformative potential of transparency policies. It shows how open datasets can bridge the gap between academic training and professional application, fostering practical skills in students. Furthermore, it raises awareness about gender diversity in tech sectors, using the success of the project to encourage more young women to pursue careers in information technology. Ultimately, it validates open data as a key driver for both technological innovation and social inclusion.
