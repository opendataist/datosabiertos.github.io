Newly released emails reveal coordination between Clemson University Media Forensics Hub and social media giants – NaturalNews.com

The article reveals deep, coordinated relationships between taxpayer-funded academic research hubs, federal law enforcement, and major social media platforms. It argues that entities like the Clemson University Media Forensics Hub did not operate independently but actively collaborated with Big Tech to remove content and share data with politically aligned news outlets. This network extended to private firms specializing in election integrity, suggesting a systemic effort to influence public discourse under the guise of combating disinformation. The funding and cooperation imply that government resources were utilized to shape online narratives and suppress speech that did not align with official narratives, blurring the lines between public service and political messaging. This is highly relevant to open_data because it highlights the lack of transparency in how public funds are used to curate internet speech. Understanding these hidden collaborations is crucial for evaluating the integrity of digital archives and ensuring that data governance remains accountable, unbiased, and truly open to public scrutiny rather than serving as a tool for coordinated narrative control.
