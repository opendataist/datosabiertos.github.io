INE está seguro de entregar los resultados poblacionales del Censo el 30 de agosto

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) guarantees the release of the 2024 census population results by August 30, with data cleaning nearing completion. Following the processing and scanning of physical questionnaires across most departments, final validation will determine the total population count. This timeline adheres strictly to the legal framework established for transparency and public accountability. The subsequent publication of detailed findings is expected by April 2025, ensuring thorough analysis. This structured approach highlights the importance of rigorous data verification and systematic processing in national statistics. It demonstrates how organized workflows lead to reliable, legally compliant outcomes. This case is highly relevant to open data, as it underscores the critical need for transparent timelines and accessible statistical information. It illustrates how governments can maintain public trust by delivering comprehensive, timely data that supports informed decision-making and societal development through reliable open datasets.
