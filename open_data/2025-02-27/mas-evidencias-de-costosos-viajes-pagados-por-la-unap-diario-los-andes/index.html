Más evidencias de costosos viajes pagados por la UNAP - Diario los Andes

The article highlights excessive spending on international and domestic travel by university officials, arguing that virtual alternatives would have been far more cost-effective. It reveals that millions were spent on delegations whose events were streamed online, questioning the necessity and justification of such large-scale expenditures. This transparency is crucial for open data because it demonstrates how publishing financial records enables public scrutiny of public funds. By exposing wasteful spending, open data empowers citizens to demand accountability and better resource management. Ultimately, the case underscores the vital role of open data in fighting corruption. It shows that when governments disclose detailed expense records, it becomes possible to identify inefficiencies and ensure that taxpayer money is used responsibly rather than for unnecessary luxury.
