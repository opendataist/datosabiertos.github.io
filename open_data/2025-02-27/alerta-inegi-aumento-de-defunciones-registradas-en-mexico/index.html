Alerta INEGI aumento de defunciones registradas en México

Alerta INEGI aumento de defunciones registradas en México

Mexico’s preliminary mortality data reveal a significant upward trend in deaths, surpassing pre-pandemic levels and highlighting a public health crisis. The analysis identifies cardiovascular diseases, diabetes, and malignancies as the leading causes of death, largely driven by lifestyle factors such as poor diet, obesity, and sedentary habits. This persistent burden underscores the urgent need for preventative healthcare strategies and improved public health interventions to address the root causes of these chronic conditions. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the critical role of official statistical institutions like INEGI in providing transparent, accessible, and timely demographic information. By publishing granular datasets on mortality rates, causes of death, and regional disparities, these open datasets empower researchers, policymakers, and citizens to monitor health trends, hold authorities accountable, and design evidence-based solutions that directly impact societal well-being.
