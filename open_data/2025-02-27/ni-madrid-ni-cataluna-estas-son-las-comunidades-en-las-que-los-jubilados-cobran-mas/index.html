Ni Madrid ni Cataluña, estas son las comunidades en las que los jubilados cobran más

The article reveals significant disparities in average retirement pensions across Spanish regions, driven by local economic models despite uniform calculation criteria. This highlights a critical need for transparent, open data to monitor socioeconomic inequalities and understand how uniform policies result in unequal outcomes for citizens. Regions with higher living costs, such as the Balearic Islands, face the dual burden of lower pension averages compared to wealthier areas like the Basque Country. This discrepancy underscores the importance of open data in analyzing cost-of-living adjustments and ensuring social equity across diverse territories. Ultimately, this topic is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how accessible, granular statistics empower researchers and policymakers. By making regional economic and social metrics publicly available, stakeholders can better advocate for reforms that address these persistent regional gaps in retirement security.
