La actividad económica de Argentina cayó un 1,8% en 2024

Argentina’s economic activity shows signs of recovery, with December’s growth marking two consecutive months of year-on-year increases. This rebound suggests that the severe demand shock caused by high inflation and fiscal adjustment is easing, allowing key sectors such as finance and commerce to expand significantly. Conversely, industries such as fishing and construction continue to contract, highlighting an uneven recovery across the economy. Despite these disparities, the overall trend indicates that a stabilization process is underway, supported by a slowdown in inflation rates. This data is relevant to open_data because it demonstrates how transparent, official statistical indicators provide essential real-time insights into economic health. Accessible public data enables better analysis of policy impacts and economic forecasting, fostering informed decision-making for stakeholders and researchers alike.
