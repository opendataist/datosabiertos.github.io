La Nación / Economía argentina se contrajo 1,8 % en 2024, primer año del gobierno Milei

Argentina’s economy contracted by 1.8% in 2024, outperforming international forecasts despite the drastic fiscal austerity measures implemented under President Milei. This outcome highlights that severe structural adjustments can yield better results than expected, signaling a robust recovery in late 2024. The data confirms that sustained monthly growth mitigated the anticipated recession, demonstrating the resilience of the economic model during this transition period. The reduction in state spending and inflation underscores the immediate impacts of liberalization policies. By eliminating subsidies and streamlining public administration, the government achieved a fiscal surplus and stabilized prices, illustrating how rapid deregulation affects macroeconomic indicators. These shifts highlight the tangible link between policy choices and national performance, offering a case study for other nations facing similar economic crises. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on official statistics from INDEC to verify claims and challenge projections. Transparent, accessible government data allows independent experts to analyze trends, validate results, and provide objective assessments. Such openness fosters accountability and enables informed public debate on the effectiveness of economic strategies.
