Censo Agropecuario muestra cambio en uso de la tierra, con incremento de fines recreativos

Censo Agropecuario muestra cambio en uso de la tierra, con incremento de fines recreativos

Uruguay has released preliminary results from its innovative agricultural census, covering more than 94 percent of land records. By employing a mixed methodology that heavily incorporates digital and telephone surveys, the census sets a regional precedent for efficiency and accessibility in data collection. This approach underscores the country’s commitment to modernizing statistical gathering in the rural sector. The findings reveal a notable shift in land use, with recreational activities emerging as a growing component alongside traditional livestock farming. These insights are crucial for shaping economic and social policies related to land tenure and rural population dynamics. Understanding these structural changes enables more responsive and informed public strategies that reflect the evolving nature of agricultural enterprises. This release is vital for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how comprehensive, multi-channel data collection can support transparent governance. By making these findings public, authorities enable researchers and citizens to critically analyze rural trends. This promotes accountability and fosters evidence-based decision-making, ensuring that statistical information remains a public good that drives sustainable development.
