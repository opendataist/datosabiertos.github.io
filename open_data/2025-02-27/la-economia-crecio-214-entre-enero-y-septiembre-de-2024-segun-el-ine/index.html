La economía creció 2,14% entre enero y septiembre de 2024, según el INE

The national economy shows a sustained, albeit moderate, recovery driven by dynamic service sectors and domestic consumption. While extractive industries face significant contraction challenges, this divergence highlights the resilience of domestic markets and underscores the need for targeted state support in vulnerable areas. This economic shift emphasizes the critical importance of accessible, granular data for assessing regional disparities and sector-specific performance. Transparent reporting on a range of economic indicators enables stakeholders to identify underlying structural trends that go beyond aggregate growth metrics. Understanding these detailed economic dynamics is essential for open-data advocates, as it demonstrates how public statistical transparency empowers informed policy decisions. Open access to such nuanced datasets fosters accountability and enables precise analysis of economic health, proving vital for democratic governance and efficient resource allocation.
