Indiana Local News Initiative seeks a data reporter - Talking Biz News

The emergence of specialized data reporters in newsrooms signifies a shift toward evidence-based journalism that directly serves the public interest. Crucially, this position emphasizes the value of open data as a public resource. Newsrooms are encouraged to not only analyze information but also to host their own cleaned datasets online, fostering transparency and accessibility. This practice democratizes information by allowing residents to engage directly with the underlying data, reinforcing the principle that public records should be easy to find, understand, and utilize for civic engagement and community thriving. The relevance to open data lies in the strategic use of public records laws to break new ground. Journalists equipped with these skills act as intermediaries who bridge the gap between opaque institutional data and public understanding. By prioritizing community needs and employing open records tools, they ensure that government data is not just available but actively used to hold power accountable, demonstrating how open data initiatives can drive meaningful social change and informed decision-making within local communities.
