Propiedad intelectual y brecha digital: Diputados empieza a estudiar el impacto de la inteligencia artificial

Propiedad intelectual y brecha digital: Diputados empieza a estudiar el impacto de la inteligencia artificial

The article highlights a strategic legislative effort in Argentina to prepare for the disruptive socio-technological impacts of Artificial Intelligence. Experts and lawmakers convened to address the urgent need for governance, emphasizing that current legal frameworks are lagging behind the rapid evolution of the technology. The main conclusion is that a proactive, anticipatory legislative approach is essential to balance innovation with the protection of citizens' rights, rather than simply reacting to existing problems. A central theme in the discussion is the critical importance of data management and the question of data ownership in the AI era. The debate underscored that transparency, accessibility, and the ethical handling of information are foundational to building public trust and ensuring that technological development does not exacerbate social inequalities or digital divides. Participants agreed that regulating these aspects is not about stifling progress but about creating a secure environment where AI can be democratized and used safely. This event is highly relevant to open data, as it identifies the legal and ethical governance of information as a prerequisite for responsible AI implementation. By advocating for regulatory frameworks that prioritize transparency and data sovereignty, the discussion promotes the principles of open government. It suggests that for AI to benefit society, access to and control over data must be clearly defined, ensuring that digital resources serve the public interest while preventing the concentration of power and ensuring equitable technological sovereignty.
