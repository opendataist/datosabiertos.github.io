Open Government Partnership initiative

Seychelles has established a new multi-stakeholder committee to drive its Open Government Partnership commitments. This body coordinates the creation of the second National Action Plan, aiming to foster a more inclusive, participatory, and transparent government through collaborative efforts between the state and civil society. The initiative underscores the vital role of diverse stakeholder engagement in modern governance reforms. By integrating voices from various sectors, the plan seeks to enhance accountability and public trust, demonstrating how open data and transparency can be operationalized at a national level to improve democratic processes and service delivery. This development is relevant to open data as it highlights the institutional frameworks necessary for sustainable transparency. The committee’s focus on monitoring implementation and mobilizing resources ensures that data openness is not just a policy declaration but a lived reality, encouraging global best practices in governmental accountability and citizen empowerment.
