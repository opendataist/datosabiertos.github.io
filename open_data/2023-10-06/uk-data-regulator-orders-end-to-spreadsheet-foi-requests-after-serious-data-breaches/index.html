UK data regulator orders end to spreadsheet FOI requests after serious data breaches

The Information Commissioner’s Office mandates a strict moratorium on disclosing original source spreadsheets to mitigate personal data breaches. This directive emphasizes converting sensitive information into secure, reusable formats like CSV files, thereby prioritizing data integrity and protecting vulnerable individuals from the severe consequences of exposure. Public authorities must invest in robust data management systems and ongoing staff training to prevent accidental disclosures. The guidance stresses that organizations are responsible for implementing adequate safeguards, ensuring that personal identifiable information is handled with the utmost care to maintain public trust and safety. This regulation is crucial for open data initiatives because it redefines how public sector transparency intersects with privacy rights. It challenges data practitioners to balance accessibility with security, proving that responsible open data requires rigorous technical standards and ethical vigilance to protect citizens while serving the public interest.
