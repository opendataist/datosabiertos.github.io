Veracruz, en el top ten por trabajo infantil, según INEGI

The latest survey conducted by the INEGI reveals that a significant proportion of children in Veracruz are engaged in prohibited labor, placing the state among the worst in Mexico for this issue. This widespread exploitation highlights severe regional vulnerabilities, where minors perform dangerous or inappropriate domestic work, often endangering their health and development. These data underscore the urgent need for policy interventions to protect the rights of young people. By identifying the most affected areas, authorities can prioritize resources and enforcement efforts against illegal child labor practices that compromise educational opportunities and well-being. This information is vital for open data initiatives, as it provides transparent, accessible statistics necessary for monitoring social progress. Public access to these indicators enables researchers and NGOs to analyze trends, advocate for legislative changes, and ensure accountability in combating child exploitation across vulnerable communities.
