MFWA launches support centre for RTI implementation

The Media Foundation for West Africa launched an Access to Information Support Centre to bridge the gap in public awareness regarding Right to Information laws. By providing accessible guidance through multiple communication channels, the initiative aims to empower citizens, particularly vulnerable groups, to request information and appeal decisions. This focus on education is critical for fostering a democratic environment where citizens can effectively hold governments accountable through informed participation. Despite previous sensitization efforts and training for officials and journalists, limited law uptake remains a significant challenge. The new support center addresses this by offering direct assistance, ensuring that theoretical knowledge translates into practical application. This institutional support is vital for translating legal frameworks into tangible tools for civic engagement, thereby strengthening governance structures and enhancing transparency in public administration. This development is highly relevant to open data as it establishes a user-centric infrastructure for information accessibility. Open data initiatives rely not just on data publication, but on the public’s ability to understand and utilize that information. By lowering barriers to entry and providing educational support, this model demonstrates how institutional backing can drive the effective use of open government data, turning raw information into a mechanism for democratic accountability and social empowerment.
