Inflación de Venezuela se acelera por primera vez desde enero

Venezuela has experienced a rapid deceleration in monthly inflation following a period of severe hyperinflation, driven by recent deregulation of prices and exchange rates, as well as a recovery in oil sector activity after years of massive corruption. While official central bank data shows a significant cooling trend compared to estimates from the opposition, this stabilization highlights the complex reality of economic data transparency in crisis-stricken states. This divergence underscores the critical importance of open data, as independent verification challenges official narratives, ensuring public trust and accurate analysis. Accessible, multi-source datasets are essential for detecting economic shifts and holding institutions accountable, revealing the true health of a nation’s economy beyond government-controlled statistics.
