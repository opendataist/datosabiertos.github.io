'Cambrian explosion of talent, creativity': OpenAI CEO Sam Altman on opportunities in the era of AI

Open-source AI’s collaborative nature complicates regulation, prompting warnings that excessive oversight could stifle innovation and creativity. Experts argue that current policies, such as those in the EU, may inadvertently concentrate power among large tech firms, undermining smaller players and critical public research. This dynamic is vital for open data as it highlights the tension between safety and accessibility. Over-regulation risks closing off the transparency and shared knowledge that define open ecosystems. Therefore, policymakers must balance governance with the need for an open environment. Preserving open access ensures diverse innovation and maintains the public’s ability to understand and benefit from evolving technologies without monopolistic barriers.
