El Consejo de Cuentas propone mejoras en el Portal de Gobierno Abierto de la Junta

El Consejo de Cuentas propone mejoras en el Portal de Gobierno Abierto de la Junta

The Council of Accounts of Castile and León recommends that the regional government significantly enhance the usability of its Open Government Portal by prioritizing user-centric design. Key improvements include ensuring information is available in open, reusable formats and simplifying language to minimize technical jargon, thereby making public data accessible to the general population rather than just experts. To achieve this, the Council advocates for systematic quality controls to prevent incomplete data entries and mandates better coordination between information producers and publishers. This structural alignment aims to eliminate confusion caused by duplicated catalogues, ensuring a unified and consistent experience for citizens seeking transparency, while allowing the website structure to adapt to evolving user preferences. This guidance is crucial for open data because it bridges the gap between legal transparency obligations and actual public utility. By insisting on interoperable formats and clear explanations, the recommendations promote genuine data reusability, transforming static published documents into actionable assets that foster civic engagement and improve public policy outcomes through better-informed decision-making.
