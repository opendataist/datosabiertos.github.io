El papel del Estado en las tareas de seguridad en una nación - El Mercurio de Tamaulipas

The article concludes that, although the State must prioritize citizen safety, its security actions must adhere to strict proportionality to prevent the erosion of democratic values. This delicate balance is essential to protect individual liberties while maintaining public order. Key challenges include safeguarding digital privacy against mass surveillance and ensuring government transparency. Without robust oversight, opaque security measures foster distrust and potential abuse of power, underscoring the need for accountability mechanisms that respect legal boundaries. This discourse is vital for open data, as security policies are often invoked to justify withholding public information or restricting access to personal data. Understanding these trade-offs enables policymakers to design frameworks that promote transparency without compromising essential safety, ensuring that open data initiatives align with human rights and democratic principles.
