Impulsan participación ciudadana en jornada de sensibilización en Estado y Gobierno Abierto

Rancagua held a sensitization event for its Sixth Open State Action Plan, emphasizing a cultural shift toward collaborative governance. Officials and international consultants stressed that open government requires profound changes in how institutions work with the community. This initiative aims to foster a democratic environment where transparency, human rights, and inclusivity drive public service delivery, moving beyond traditional administrative models to create a state that truly serves citizens. The process relies on deep civic engagement to define the desired societal model. By inviting residents to participate in consultations, the municipality seeks to co-create ambitious, long-term commitments that address local needs. This approach ensures that government actions are not imposed from above but are built through dialogue with the public, ensuring that policies reflect the collective will and foster a sense of ownership among the population regarding local development and rights protection. This article is relevant to open_data because it highlights the foundational role of data openness in achieving transparent and accountable institutions. Open data is not merely a technical output but a mechanism for enabling the participation and oversight necessary for an "open state." By connecting technological tools with civic involvement, Rancagua’s approach demonstrates how accessible information empowers citizens to monitor government performance, thereby strengthening democracy and ensuring that digital transparency translates into tangible social impact and inclusive decision-making processes.
