Buscan transparentar el registro de licencias de importaciones para agilizar las solicitudes y evitar discrecionalidades

Deputies from the Civic Coalition propose establishing an Open Data Portal for imports, aimed at eliminating the administrative discretion that currently characterizes the Argentine system. This project seeks to ensure that all information regarding applications, amounts, and processing statuses is published digitally, kept up to date, and made available in open formats. The measure aims to counteract corruption risks associated with the opacity in license management, ensuring that access to public information is free and transparent. The main focus of this initiative lies in transforming the governance of customs processes through principles of clarity and predictability. It establishes that resolutions must follow a strict chronological order, with maximum processing deadlines; if these deadlines are not met, the license is automatically approved. Additionally, it requires the publication of the financial criteria used to determine taxpayers’ economic capacity, thereby eliminating arbitrariness in decision-making and providing legal certainty to economic operators. This proposal is relevant to the field of open data because it demonstrates how technical transparency can serve as a tool for civic oversight and anti-corruption efforts. By mandating open formats and free access, the project aligns public policy with international governance standards, enabling civil society to monitor compliance with regulations. This illustrates the importance of government data being not only accessible but also structured to facilitate analysis and use in accountability mechanisms.
