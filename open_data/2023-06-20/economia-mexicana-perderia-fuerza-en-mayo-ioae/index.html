Economía mexicana perdería fuerza en mayo: IOAE

The Timely Indicator of Economic Activity (IOAE) confirms that the Mexican economy continues to grow, albeit at a significantly slower pace compared to previous months. This deceleration suggests a broader slowdown that, while avoiding a deep contraction, reflects the pressure generated by the Bank of Mexico’s restrictive monetary policies aimed at combating inflation. Current economic implications point to an annual growth rate below government expectations, highlighting the negative impact of interest rate hikes on domestic consumption. However, this pressure is partially offset by the resilience of the export sector and investments driven by nearshoring, which act as key stabilizing factors in sustaining economic activity despite restrictive financial conditions. This analysis is relevant to the *open_data* field because it illustrates how publicly accessible, high-frequency statistical indicators enable agile monitoring of economic health. The timely availability of these data facilitates immediate assessment of public policies and allows analysts to adjust projections in real time, demonstrating the strategic value of transparency and accessibility of government information for strategic decision-making.
