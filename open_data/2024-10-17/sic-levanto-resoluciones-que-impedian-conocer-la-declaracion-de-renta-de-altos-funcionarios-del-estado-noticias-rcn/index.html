SIC levantó resoluciones que impedían conocer la declaración de renta de altos funcionarios del Estado | Noticias RCN

The Superintendence of Industry and Commerce revoked restrictive regulations that had previously prohibited public officials from publishing their tax declarations. By removing these barriers, the decision reinforces the principles of publicity and transparency, aligning administrative practices with the public interest and ensuring that citizens can access crucial information regarding the financial integrity of public servants. Although the ban on publication has been lifted, the authority emphasized the need to maintain robust data protection standards. It urged the relevant administrative department to continue applying legal frameworks that balance transparency with the privacy of personal data, ensuring that security measures remain adequate to protect sensitive information without undermining the public’s right to know. This development is significant for open data, as it prioritizes the public availability of government financial information as a cornerstone of democratic accountability. It demonstrates how regulatory bodies can dismantle obstacles to data access while maintaining appropriate safeguards, ultimately fostering a culture in which transparency and privacy coexist to support informed civic oversight.
