India has great advantage in building new AI applications on top of LLMs: Accel partner Shekhar Kirani

India has great advantage in building new AI applications on top of LLMs: Accel partner Shekhar Kirani

The narrative surrounding artificial intelligence has shifted significantly, with India transforming from a lagging observer to a global hub for practical AI applications. Rather than focusing on developing foundational large language models, which remain dominated by Western investment, Indian engineers are rapidly building innovative, industry-specific solutions atop existing open-source and proprietary models. This pivot highlights a democratization of AI development, where specialized PhDs are no longer a prerequisite for creating transformative tools. This evolution is driven by India’s unique advantage in combining vast engineering talent with rich, proprietary datasets from its massive services sector. Companies are successfully leveraging domain-specific data, such as voice records from healthcare or financial filings, to train models that solve concrete business problems. By integrating these tailored applications into existing workflows, startups are demonstrating that the real value lies in adapting general AI capabilities to meet precise enterprise needs, thereby personalizing technology for specific industries like dental care, spa management, and accounting. Relevant to open data, this trend underscores the power of leveraging accessible foundational models combined with localized, proprietary information to drive innovation. It suggests that while raw model training requires immense resources, application-layer innovation thrives on creative data usage and integration. This approach positions India’s IT and SaaS industries to lead in AI productized services, proving that strategic adaptation of available technologies can create significant competitive advantage without needing to reinvent the underlying engine.
