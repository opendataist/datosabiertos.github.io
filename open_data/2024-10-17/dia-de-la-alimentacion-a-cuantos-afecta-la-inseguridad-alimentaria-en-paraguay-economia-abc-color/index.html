Día de la alimentación: ¿A cuántos afecta la inseguridad alimentaria en Paraguay? - Economía - ABC Color

Día de la alimentación: ¿A cuántos afecta la inseguridad alimentaria en Paraguay? - Economía - ABC Color

A pioneering statistical initiative in Paraguay, carried out by the National Institute of Statistics (INE) with support from the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), has successfully integrated food insecurity metrics into national household surveys. This achievement represents a significant milestone for open data initiatives, as it establishes standardized, accessible indicators for monitoring nutritional well-being. By applying internationally recognized measurement scales such as the Food Insecurity Experience Scale (FIES), the country now provides transparent, comparable data that enables researchers and policymakers to accurately assess the prevalence and severity of hunger across different demographic groups. The findings reveal that a substantial portion of the population experiences moderate food insecurity, primarily driven by economic constraints and rising food prices. While both urban and rural areas are affected, rural households and those headed by women or containing multiple children face disproportionately higher rates of vulnerability. These disparities underscore the urgent need for targeted social interventions and demonstrate how granular open data can expose structural inequalities that aggregate statistics often obscure. This publication is crucial for the open data community because it demonstrates the value of publishing detailed, nuanced socioeconomic indicators. When governments release such specific datasets, they empower civil society and developers to create tools that advocate for food security and hold institutions accountable. Ultimately, this approach transforms raw data into actionable insights, fostering a more informed public debate and facilitating data-driven solutions to effectively combat hunger.
