INEGE inaugura el Seminario Comercio Exterior de Mercancías

A seminar brings together Guinean statistical and customs officials to analyze the current state of foreign trade data compilation. The group aims to improve methodological frameworks and establish a strategic roadmap for enhancing data quality and transmission to African Union statistics. This initiative highlights the critical need for institutional coordination to produce accurate economic indicators. By refining these processes, the country seeks to align its reporting standards with continental requirements. This case is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of transparent, standardized statistical production. Reliable open data relies on robust institutional cooperation and clear methodologies to ensure public trust and utility in economic reporting.
