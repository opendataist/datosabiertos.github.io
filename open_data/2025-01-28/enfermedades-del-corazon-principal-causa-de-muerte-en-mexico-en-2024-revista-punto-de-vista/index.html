Enfermedades del corazón, principal causa de muerte en México en 2024 | Revista Punto de Vista

Cardiovascular diseases emerged as the leading cause of death in Mexico in early 2024, reflecting a sustained upward trend that highlights critical gaps in preventive public health infrastructure. This pattern underscores the urgent need for improved data collection and greater transparency regarding the burden of chronic diseases, in order to effectively allocate resources and design targeted interventions. The consistent rise in mortality rates across diverse regions reveals systemic weaknesses in healthcare accessibility and early detection mechanisms. By analyzing detailed mortality statistics, policymakers can identify high-risk areas and implement localized strategies to mitigate these growing health crises. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public health metrics drive societal awareness and accountability. Accessible, detailed mortality data empowers researchers and citizens to monitor health trends, demand policy improvements, and foster evidence-based discussions on national well-being.
