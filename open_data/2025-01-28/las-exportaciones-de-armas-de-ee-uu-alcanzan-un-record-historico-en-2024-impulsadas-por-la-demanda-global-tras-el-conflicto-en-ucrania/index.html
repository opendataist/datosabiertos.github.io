Las exportaciones de armas de EE. UU. alcanzan un récord histórico en 2024, impulsadas por la demanda global tras el conflicto en Ucrania

The article reveals a historic increase in U.S. arms exports, driven by global demand and the need to replenish stockpiles after supporting Ukraine. This trend highlights the dominant role of the U.S. military-industrial complex in the current conflict, as both commercial and government sales have grown significantly, consolidating the United States as the world’s leading arms supplier. The relevance to open data lies in the transparency of this supply chain and its geopolitical implications. Data from the U.S. Department of State and SIPRI demonstrate how public information on military transactions can be used to analyze the economic impact of the conflict and the strategic dependence of European and Israeli allies, providing tangible evidence of the scale and distribution of international military aid. The future evolution of these policies under new administrations introduces uncertainty regarding the sustainability of supplies, affecting both the economies of manufacturers and regional stability. Analyzing these data flows helps to understand the tensions between commercial interests, national sovereignty, and conflict dynamics—insights essential for researchers and citizens seeking to comprehend the forces behind modern warfare.
