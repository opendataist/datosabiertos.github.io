Unanimous vote by Freedom of Information Task Force supports Senate bill 12

The Arkansas FOIA Task Force unanimously recommended Senate Bill 12, which would extend public records laws to the state’s Board of Apportionment. This move aims to ensure transparency in redistricting communications, preventing the secrecy that often accompanies gerrymandering efforts. By subjecting these high-level officials to open data requirements, the legislation addresses critical gaps in government accountability. It empowers citizens to access information about how electoral maps are drawn, fostering trust in the democratic process. This development is vital for open data advocates because it expands the scope of accessible public records. Ensuring that apportionment decisions remain visible allows for better public oversight and reinforces the principle that government actions should be openly documented and scrutinized.
