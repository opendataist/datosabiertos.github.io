Ministerio de Economía premia al INE por el censo 2022, pese a innumerables críticas - Policiales - ABC Color

The National Institute of Statistics (INE) received a Transparency Innovation Prize for its Transparent Census initiative, which publicly discloses the financial resources allocated to the 2022 census. This recognition underscores the potential of open data platforms to strengthen governmental accountability and enhance the visibility of resource management within state institutions. However, this achievement stands in stark contrast to significant criticisms regarding the execution of the census. The Comptroller General identified numerous administrative failures and financial irregularities, while widespread public distrust arose over demographic discrepancies and perceived undercounting. These controversies highlight the challenges of preserving data integrity amid political scrutiny. This case is relevant to open data because it illustrates the complex relationship between transparency and trust. Although publishing financial data represents a step toward openness, the lack of confidence in the final results demonstrates that technical transparency alone cannot address deeper issues of statistical credibility and effective governance, which remain critical for open data ecosystems.
