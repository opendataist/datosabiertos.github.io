La revisión del padrón en Galicia suma 5.713 habitantes este año

Galicia has experienced a net population increase, with almost all provinces showing growth, although Lugo recorded a slight decline. This trend underscores regional demographic shifts, challenging the assumption of universal urban expansion while confirming Vigo’s continued dominance. These official statistics reveal divergent trends among major cities, with some growing and others contracting. Such granular data is essential for understanding local dynamics and resource allocation, demonstrating that population changes are not uniform even within the same autonomous community. This article is relevant to open data because it exemplifies the value of publicly accessible, detailed demographic datasets. Accurate, transparent statistics from institutions such as the INE empower researchers and policymakers to analyze territorial changes and make evidence-based decisions rather than relying on speculation.
