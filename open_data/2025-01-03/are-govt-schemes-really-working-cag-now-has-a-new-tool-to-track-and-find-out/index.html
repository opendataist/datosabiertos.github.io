Are govt schemes really working? CAG now has a new tool to track and find out

The CAG employs the Open Data Kit to enhance transparency in public spending and government scheme delivery. By collecting secure, multi-language beneficiary data, this digital tool enables performance audits that identify problem areas and verify real-world impact. This approach ensures accountability in government expenditure by gathering direct evidence from citizens. It moves beyond theoretical assessments to capture actual service quality, such as patient satisfaction at hospitals, thereby validating whether state interventions achieve their intended goals. This development is vital for open data, as it demonstrates how accessible, standardized digital tools can facilitate rigorous government auditing. It highlights the importance of transparent data collection mechanisms in holding institutions accountable and ensuring public resources are utilized effectively.
