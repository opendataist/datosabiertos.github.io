C-LM tuvo 121.799 pernoctaciones en alojamientos turísticos extrahoteleros durante el mes de noviembre

The article highlights a robust recovery in Spain’s non-hotel accommodation sector during November 2024, driven by significant growth in rural tourism and steady increases in apartments and campings. This trend indicates a shifting consumer preference toward diverse lodging options, with rural stays seeing a substantial surge and international demand remaining strong, particularly from key markets like the UK and Germany. The data reveals distinct regional and demographic patterns, such as the dominance of the Canary Islands for apartments and the Valencian Community for campings. These insights help stakeholders understand where demand is concentrating, allowing for better resource allocation and infrastructure planning. The varying performance across segments suggests that while mass tourism stabilizes, niche markets are experiencing dynamic expansion, reflecting changing traveler behaviors. This information is crucial for open data initiatives because it provides granular, transparent insights into tourism dynamics that can inform public policy and private investment. By making these statistics accessible, developers and planners can create more responsive, data-driven strategies to support sustainable tourism growth, ensuring that infrastructure development aligns with actual usage trends and economic indicators.
