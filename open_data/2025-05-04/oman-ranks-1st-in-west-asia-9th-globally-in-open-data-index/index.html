Oman ranks 1st in West Asia, 9th globally in open data index

Oman ranks 1st in West Asia, 9th globally in open data index

Oman’s top regional ranking in the 2024 Open Data Inventory highlights its success in making official statistics highly accessible. This significant improvement demonstrates a commitment to international standards, fostering an environment where data is readily available for public use and analysis. The nation’s exceptional performance in data openness serves as a model for neighboring Gulf states. By prioritizing transparency, Oman enables better planning and evidence-based decision-making, proving that robust open data infrastructure supports broader national development goals. This achievement is crucial for open data advocates, as it validates the impact of structured statistical policies. It encourages other countries to enhance their own data readiness, ensuring that reliable information drives research and informed governance globally.
