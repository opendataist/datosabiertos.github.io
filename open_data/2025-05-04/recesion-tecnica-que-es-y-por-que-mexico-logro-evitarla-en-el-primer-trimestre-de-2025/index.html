Recesión técnica: qué es y por qué México logró evitarla en el primer trimestre de 2025

Mexico narrowly avoided a technical recession in early 2025 thanks to unexpected GDP growth, defying analysts’ predictions of stagnation. This outcome, driven primarily by volatile gains in the primary sector, highlights the fragility of the current economic recovery, as key industrial and service sectors remained weak or stagnant. Experts warn that this growth lacks sustainability due to its reliance on unstable sectors and lingering uncertainty regarding international trade policies. Consequently, the risk of a formal recession remains high if subsequent quarters fail to demonstrate consistent activity, suggesting that current optimism may be premature and masking underlying structural weaknesses. This report is relevant to open data as it relies on publicly accessible statistical outputs to validate economic health indicators. It underscores the critical role of transparent, timely data release by national institutes in enabling accurate analysis, fostering public debate, and allowing stakeholders to monitor real-time economic risks without reliance on closed or delayed information systems.
