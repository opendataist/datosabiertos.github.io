Africa: FAJ marks World Press Freedom Day by urging responsible use of AI to safeguard journalism in Africa / IFJ

Africa: FAJ marks World Press Freedom Day by urging responsible use of AI to safeguard journalism in Africa / IFJ

The article highlights the urgent need to protect journalistic integrity and press freedom in Africa amid the rapid integration of Artificial Intelligence. It argues that while AI offers innovative tools for news production, it simultaneously poses severe risks to editorial independence, data privacy, and the safety of journalists, particularly those in conflict zones. The core concern is that automated systems may displace human judgment, dilute ethical standards, and amplify disinformation, thereby undermining the credibility of public discourse. Relevance to open data lies in the necessity for transparent and accountable data governance within AI systems. To prevent the misuse of information and ensure fairness, data used to train these models must be handled with strict adherence to privacy and ethical guidelines. The call for enforceable safeguards emphasizes that open data principles should support, not replace, human oversight, ensuring that algorithms do not suppress diverse voices or facilitate surveillance against journalists. Ultimately, the text advocates for a balanced approach where AI serves as a tool to enhance, rather than substitute, human journalism. It stresses the importance of investing in digital literacy and training to empower media professionals. By keeping journalists central to AI integration, society can ensure that technological advancements uphold the truth, diversity, and justice, preserving the essential human element in reporting for the future of media.
