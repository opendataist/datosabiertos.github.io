Fort Smith faces lawsuit over FOIA response in internal auditor case

Fort Smith faces lawsuit over FOIA response in internal auditor case

A lawsuit against Fort Smith, Arkansas, alleges that city officials failed to provide public records regarding the hiring and subsequent rescinded offer of Rebecca Cowan as internal auditor. The case highlights significant transparency issues, as the city claims relevant background check documents no longer exist despite being produced, and acting administrator Jeff Dingman privately doubted a check occurred, contradicting official statements. This discrepancy suggests potential withholding or destruction of public records. The controversy is further complicated by the revelation of Cowan’s pending felony stalking charges, which led to the offer's withdrawal. However, the core legal issue remains the city’s handling of freedom of information requests. The plaintiff argues that the city’s failure to respond timely and maintain accurate records undermines public trust, even though city attorneys insist they furnished all relevant documents. This situation is directly relevant to open data initiatives because it demonstrates the critical importance of maintaining comprehensive, accessible, and immutable digital records. It serves as a cautionary tale for municipalities, illustrating how inadequate record-keeping practices and inconsistent internal communications can obscure accountability and hinder transparency efforts.
