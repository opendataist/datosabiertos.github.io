▷ Eurostat: España es el tercer país de la UE con mayor riesgo de pobreza y exclusión social #18Jun - El Impulso

The article highlights Spain’s high risk of social exclusion, ranking third in the EU. This data reveals significant regional disparities in poverty rates across member states. The definition of this risk encompasses low income, severe material deprivation, and low labor intensity in households. Understanding these dimensions is crucial for comprehensive social analysis. This information is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how accessible, standardized European statistics enable transparent monitoring of social welfare. Such open datasets allow citizens and researchers to verify government performance and advocate for equitable policies.
