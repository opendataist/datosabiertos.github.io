Los datos también tienen premio y aplicaciones

Los datos también tienen premio y aplicaciones

Víctor Manuel Gutiérrez Martín has highlighted the importance of transforming administrative data into practical solutions, earning an award for his intelligent parking management system in natural areas. This achievement underscores how expert use of public information can address specific needs, such as the lack of knowledge about parking capacity for the growing number of caravan tourists. The initiative demonstrates that while data is accessible, its true value is realized only when it is properly combined and analyzed to provide concrete utility to citizens. The project highlights the gap between the availability of open data and the technical capacity of citizens to use it. While administrations comply with legal transparency requirements by providing access to information, most people do not know how to access or apply these resources. Gutiérrez illustrates that a specialized technical profile is needed to unlock this "box" of data, linking geographical, climatic, and cultural information to create tools that genuinely improve user experience and territorial management. This story is relevant to the open data community because it exemplifies the transformative potential of administrative transparency when it drives citizen innovation. It is not enough to simply publish data; it is essential to foster the skills necessary to interpret it and create applications that solve real problems. This case inspires us to show that open data are not just a legal requirement, but a living tool capable of generating social and economic value, while also highlighting local technical talent beyond traditional sectors.
