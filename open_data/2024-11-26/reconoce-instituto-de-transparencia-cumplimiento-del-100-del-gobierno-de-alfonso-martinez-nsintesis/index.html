Reconoce Instituto de Transparencia cumplimiento del 100 % del gobierno de Alfonso Martínez • Nsíntesis

Reconoce Instituto de Transparencia cumplimiento del 100 % del gobierno de Alfonso Martínez • Nsíntesis

The government of Morelia achieved a perfect transparency score for the fourth consecutive year, reaffirming its unwavering commitment to accountability and open governance. This consistent recognition underscores how full disclosure of public resource usage fosters trust and enables meaningful civic participation. As the state’s largest municipality, Morelia demonstrates that comprehensive transparency is attainable even with substantial budgets and extensive documentation. This success shows that openness can scale effectively, establishing a crucial benchmark for other large administrative bodies seeking to improve their data accessibility standards. This achievement is highly relevant to open data, as it validates the real-world impact of releasing detailed government information. It encourages broader adoption of open practices, showing citizens that transparent data directly supports efficient program evaluation and strengthens democratic engagement through informed public oversight.
