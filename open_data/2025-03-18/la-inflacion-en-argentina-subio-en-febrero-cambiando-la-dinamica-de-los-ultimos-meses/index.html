La inflación en Argentina subió en febrero, cambiando la dinámica de los últimos meses

Argentina experienced a notable acceleration in February inflation, disrupting the downward trend established in previous months. Economists attribute the prior reduction to aggressive devaluation strategies and a suppressed dollar exchange rate maintained by the government. This shift highlights the ongoing volatility in economic indicators following recent policy adjustments. The article underscores the social consequences of these economic measures, evidenced by university teacher strikes protesting severe salary erosion against rising costs. Experts predict continued moderate inflation increases in the coming months, suggesting that while prices will rise, they may not exceed current thresholds. This trajectory indicates persistent pressure on purchasing power despite official efforts to stabilize markets. This report is relevant to open data as it relies on official statistics from Argentina’s National Institute of Statistics and Censuses. Analyzing such public datasets allows for the verification of economic claims and fosters transparency. Understanding how raw statistical releases influence public perception and policy debate is essential for leveraging open data to improve governance and accountability in Latin America.
