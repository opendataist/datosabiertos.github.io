Atenciones residentes extranjeros: abrieron las inscripciones para el padrón municipal

The municipal electoral board opened registration for foreign residents, emphasizing that voter registration is a public document that proves habitual residence. Applicants must validate their address by providing utility bills or a sworn statement. This process ensures transparent and accessible data for local democratic participation.
