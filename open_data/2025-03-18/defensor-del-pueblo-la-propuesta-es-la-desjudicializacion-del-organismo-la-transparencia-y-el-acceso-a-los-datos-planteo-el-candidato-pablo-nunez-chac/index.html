Defensor del Pueblo: "La propuesta es la desjudicialización del organismo, la transparencia y el acceso a los datos", planteó el candidato Pablo Núñez – CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

Defensor del Pueblo: "La propuesta es la desjudicialización del organismo, la transparencia y el acceso a los datos", planteó el candidato Pablo Núñez – CHACODIAPORDIA.COM

Pablo Núñez proposes reforming the Chaco Ombudsman by shifting from a rigid legalistic approach to a citizen-centric model focused on rapid, effective responses. This strategy emphasizes desjudicialization, ensuring the institution remains legally empowered but prioritizes direct problem-solving over procedural formalities to better serve the public’s immediate needs. Central to this vision is a commitment to radical transparency and data accessibility. Núñez argues that information must be not only published but easily understandable and reachable for citizens, fostering trust and awareness of available resources. He highlights the current lack of accessible organizational and budgetary data as a critical flaw that undermines the institution’s utility and public confidence. Finally, he advocates for coordinated governance across all state powers and municipalities. This collaborative framework aims to strengthen the Ombudsman as a vital bridge between the government and society. The article is relevant to open_data because it explicitly links institutional legitimacy and citizen protection to the proactive publication and accessibility of public data, treating transparency as a core operational value rather than a mere formality.
