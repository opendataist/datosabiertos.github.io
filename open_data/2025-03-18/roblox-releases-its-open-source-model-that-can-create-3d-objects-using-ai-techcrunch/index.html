Roblox releases its open source model that can create 3D objects using AI | TechCrunch

Roblox releases its open source model that can create 3D objects using AI | TechCrunch

Roblox has introduced “Cube,” an open-source generative AI model that enables creators to generate 3D objects and scenes from text prompts. By releasing the model openly, Roblox allows developers outside its ecosystem to customize, build plugins, and train the system on their own datasets. This move democratizes access to advanced 3D generation tools, fostering innovation beyond the platform and empowering indie developers to tackle larger projects with increased efficiency. The initiative expands Roblox’s existing suite of AI capabilities, including text-to-speech and natural language processing, with future plans for complex mesh and full scene generation. The ultimate vision involves “4D creation,” where the fourth dimension represents dynamic interaction between objects, environments, and users. This evolution aims to transform how games are built and experienced, shifting focus toward interactive, living worlds rather than static assets. This development is highly relevant to open_data because it establishes a precedent for distributing complex AI models openly rather than keeping them proprietary. It highlights the growing intersection of open-source software and generative AI in gaming, encouraging community-driven improvement. However, it also underscores ongoing industry concerns regarding job displacement and the ethical implications of AI in creative fields, prompting critical discussions about data usage and creator rights in an increasingly automated landscape.
