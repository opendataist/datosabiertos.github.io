Group challenges Marcos to release all SALNs amid corruption exposÃ©s

Amid allegations of widespread corruption in flood control projects, a coalition of advocates is demanding immediate transparency from President Marcos Jr. and his Cabinet by releasing their Statements of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Worth. The group argues that citizens have a fundamental right to uncover the financial ties between public officials and contractors, emphasizing that current disclosures only show fund allocation without revealing potential conflicts of interest or improper benefits enjoyed by those in power. To enable effective oversight, the coalition calls for the publication of comprehensive corporate records from key agencies, including the Securities and Commission and the Department of Budget and Management. By making detailed data on top contractors’ financial statements, registrations, and tax clearances publicly available, authorities can allow journalists and citizens to cross-reference spending decisions with specific entities. This level of granular transparency is essential for identifying the individuals responsible for skimming public funds and enforcing accountability beyond superficial anti-corruption measures. This situation highlights the critical role of open data in combating systemic corruption, demonstrating that mere project listing is insufficient without associated financial disclosures. True accountability requires linking administrative decisions to personal financial interests, ensuring that transparency mechanisms proactively expose collusion rather than merely documenting expenditures. Without robust, interconnected data sharing, anti-corruption efforts risk becoming superficial campaigns that fail to address the underlying structural weaknesses in governance and enforcement.
