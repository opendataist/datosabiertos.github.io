Castilla-La Mancha registra en los siete primeros meses del año un récord en alojamiento reglado, con máximos en viajeros y pernoctaciones de turismo rural en julio

Castilla-La Mancha registra en los siete primeros meses del año un récord en alojamiento reglado, con máximos en viajeros y pernoctaciones de turismo rural en julio

Castilla-La Mancha has reached historic highs in rural tourism, recording its best July ever in terms of both guest numbers and overnight stays. This performance significantly outpaces national averages, with regional growth nearly double the national rate. The surge is not limited to the summer peak; cumulative data for the first seven months of the year also break previous records, indicating a sustained and robust expansion of the sector beyond seasonal fluctuations. This growth is consistent across multiple provinces, with Albacete, Guadalajara, and Toledo all setting new monthly records. Ciudad Real, in particular, has emerged as a standout performer, ranking second nationally for growth in rural travelers. Furthermore, the region has secured its largest market share ever for the first half of the year, solidifying its position as a premier destination for domestic tourists seeking rural accommodation and demonstrating resilient demand that continues to absorb increased supply. This article is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the power of standardized, publicly available statistical series from the National Statistics Institute (INE). By relying on consistent, longitudinal datasets, authorities can accurately track year-over-year performance, identify regional trends, and validate economic claims. This case exemplifies how transparent, accessible data enables precise policy assessment and allows stakeholders to benchmark regional performance against national standards in real time.
