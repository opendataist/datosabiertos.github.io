Central Statistics Office Publish Report On Water Consumption Recorded On Domestic Meters - Tipp Mid West Radio

Central Statistics Office Publish Report On Water Consumption Recorded On Domestic Meters - Tipp Mid West Radio

The CSO data highlights how median consumption better reflects typical usage than averages, which are skewed by leaks and extreme outliers. A small fraction of households drives a disproportionate share of total consumption, revealing significant inefficiencies in domestic water use. This insight is vital for open data advocates, demonstrating the importance of robust statistical measures in public reporting. By clarifying that median figures mitigate leak-related distortions, the report promotes transparency and supports more accurate policy analysis for resource management. Understanding these nuances helps stakeholders interpret open government data correctly. It underscores the value of accessible, high-quality statistics in identifying systemic issues and fostering informed public discourse on sustainability and infrastructure efficiency.
