Datos de estatal INE muestran desempleo de Chile en 8,7% para segundo trimestre de 2025

Chile’s unemployment rate remained stable at 8.7%, reflecting simultaneous growth in both the labor force and employment. This equilibrium indicates that new job seekers found work, while the informal economy contracted slightly, suggesting modest consolidation in the labor market. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it demonstrates how transparent, freely accessible government statistics enable precise monitoring of economic health. Such reliable datasets empower researchers and citizens to track employment trends and evaluate the impact of social policies over time.
