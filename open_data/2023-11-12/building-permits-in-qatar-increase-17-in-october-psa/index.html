Building permits in Qatar increase 17% in October: PSA

The Planning and Statistics Authority, in collaboration with the Ministry of Municipality, has published detailed statistics on building permits and completions for October 2023. This monthly release leverages an electronic link between agencies to provide timely insights into the construction sector’s performance. The data reveals a general upward trend in activity, highlighting specific municipalities and building types that are driving growth within Qatar’s economy. Residential construction remains the dominant force, with villas accounting for the majority of new residential permits and completions. Al Rayyan and Al Wakrah emerged as key hubs for both issuing permits and finalizing construction projects. The significant increase in permits for smaller municipalities suggests a broadening of development activity beyond major urban centers, indicating a robust and expanding demand for housing and commercial infrastructure across the nation. This dataset is critically relevant to open data initiatives as it exemplifies the value of interoperable government systems. By integrating data from statistical authorities and municipal bodies, the government enhances transparency and accessibility for researchers and businesses. Such standardized, timely public data supports evidence-based planning, fosters accountability, and allows stakeholders to accurately assess economic trends in the vital construction industry.
