Anses nunca solicita datos personales, claves o información bancaria

ANSES warns retirees to ignore all unsolicited requests for personal or banking information via phone, email, or social media. Since the agency never solicits sensitive data and all official services are free, any such contact is a fraudulent attempt. Beneficiaries are urged to disregard suspicious messages and report them immediately to prevent further scams. The notice highlights that official communication channels are limited to the free 130 hotline and the secure mi ANSES platform. These verified avenues ensure data confidentiality and integrity. Accessing services through unofficial intermediaries or third-party apps poses significant security risks and should be strictly avoided by all citizens. This warning is crucial for open data discourse, as it emphasizes the necessity of secure, transparent, and verified digital governance channels. Protecting citizen privacy in an increasingly digital administrative landscape requires robust safeguards against impersonation. Highlighting these risks reinforces the importance of trustworthy public infrastructure and user education in maintaining trust in open government platforms.
