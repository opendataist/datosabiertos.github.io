Argentina cierra el 2024 con una inflación del 117,8 % - Confirmado

Argentina’s annual inflation rate slowed to 117.8% in December, marking the eighth consecutive month of deceleration. This trend confirms an ongoing disinflation process, despite slight seasonal increases, occurring alongside a recovery in economic activity and household incomes. The data highlight how macroeconomic indicators can reflect policy outcomes and improvements in consumer welfare. By tracking these shifts, stakeholders gain insight into the real impact of fiscal measures on daily life and the stability of purchasing power. This is relevant to open data, as it demonstrates the public availability of high-quality, timely statistics from official sources such as INDEC. Transparent access to such data enables independent analysis, fosters accountability, and helps citizens understand broader economic trends beyond headline figures.
