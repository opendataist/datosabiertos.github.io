Ireland an open data 'trendsetter' according to EU annual report - TechCentral.ie

Ireland has secured a top-five position in the 2024 EU Open Data Maturity Assessment, rising to the elite tier alongside leading European nations. This significant improvement highlights the country’s effective strategy in making public sector information widely available and encouraging its reuse to foster innovation and transform public services. The success stems from a robust 'open by default' approach that successfully balances transparency with strict adherence to privacy and data protection standards. By prioritizing high-quality data governance, Ireland demonstrates that regulatory compliance and openness can coexist, building public trust while ensuring responsible data management across government institutions. This achievement is crucial for the open data community as it provides a practical model for integrating policy, impact, portal, and quality dimensions. It underscores how strategic national commitments can drive tangible societal goals, offering valuable insights for other regions aiming to enhance their own open data ecosystems through transparency and accessible information.
