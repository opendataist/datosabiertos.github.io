Fadillah launches energy sector's open data gateway, MyEnergyStats

Fadillah launches energy sector's open data gateway, MyEnergyStats

Malaysia has launched MyEnergyStats to replace its previous energy hub, offering advanced visualization and real-time data access. This upgrade aims to support evidence-based policymaking and guide strategic investments in the nation’s green agenda. Open data facilitates more effective energy transitions by ensuring information integrity and accuracy. Researchers and private entities can now leverage these comprehensive datasets to model solutions and identify sustainable opportunities. This portal exemplifies open_data’s role in fostering transparency and collaboration. By making energy statistics publicly accessible, it empowers stakeholders to contribute to national development goals while strengthening trust among government, industry, and society.
