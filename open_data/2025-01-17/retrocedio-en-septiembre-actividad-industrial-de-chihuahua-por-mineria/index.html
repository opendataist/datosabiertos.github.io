Retrocedió en septiembre actividad industrial de Chihuahua por minería

Chihuahua’s industrial activity exhibits mixed trends, with a slight overall decline driven exclusively by mining, while the energy and manufacturing sectors report positive annual growth. This underscores the importance of granular data segmentation in assessing regional economic health. The contrast between the struggling mining sector and expanding industrial areas highlights that aggregate statistics can obscure significant underlying dynamics. Disaggregated metrics are essential for policymakers to identify specific growth drivers and targeted areas of concern. For open data, this example demonstrates how accessible, detailed indicators enable a more accurate and nuanced understanding of local economies. Publishing such granular datasets allows stakeholders to move beyond broad averages and make informed decisions based on sector-specific realities.
