Open Data
ES EN

Privacy transparency empowers users to control personal data usage, aligning with open data principles of accountability and user consent. By offering clear options to accept or reject tracking, organizations promote ethical data handling. This approach ensures that data collection practices remain transparent, fostering trust between users and service providers while respecting individual privacy rights in an increasingly digital landscape.
