Ordena INAI a la Conade transparentar recursos que recibieron atletas

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) ordered the National Council for Culture and Sports (Conade) to fully publish the list of coaches and athletes who received public funds, including those with outstanding administrative issues. This ruling underscores the critical need for clarity in the management of funds, establishing a relationship of shared responsibility between the authorities that allocate resources and the beneficiaries. Lack of transparency not only violates principles of access to information but also erodes public trust and can negatively impact national sports development by contributing to the dropout of talented individuals. The agency argued technical limitations in delivering the information in electronic format, but the INAI dismissed this justification, pointing to insufficient searches and a lack of thoroughness. By revoking the original response, it was emphasized that transparency obligations should not be hindered by alleged logistical difficulties. A rigorous audit is required to verify the actual execution of contracted services and the proper documentation of expenses, correcting control deficits previously identified by oversight bodies. This case is fundamental for open data because it demonstrates that the availability of public information must be proactive, complete, and digitally accessible. By requiring Conade to disclose data on high-performance sports, the open data ecosystem is strengthened by ensuring that citizens can monitor the use of state resources. This prevents administrative opacity and ensures that information about subsidies and scholarships becomes a truly usable public good for accountability purposes.
