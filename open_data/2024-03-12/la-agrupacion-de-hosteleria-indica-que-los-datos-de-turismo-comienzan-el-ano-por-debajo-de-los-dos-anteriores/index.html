La Agrupación de Hostelería indica que los datos de turismo comienzan el año por debajo de los dos anteriores

The article highlights a decline in Cuenca’s January 2024 tourism figures, prompting the local Hospitality Group to warn that winter months remain challenging for hotel occupancy. The core implication is that relying solely on seasonal leisure travel is insufficient; instead, the sector must actively diversify its strategy by organizing events and business congresses to fill gaps during off-peak periods and non-holiday weeks. This strategic shift is crucial for stabilizing revenue, as traditional tourism models fail to capture consistent demand during colder months. By attracting international visitors and promoting business travel, local businesses can mitigate the natural volatility of seasonal fluctuations. The data suggests that while domestic demand is dropping, international interest shows potential, indicating where focused marketing efforts should be directed to sustain operations. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how public statistical releases, such as those from the National Statistics Institute, provide essential evidence for private sector advocacy and planning. When open data is accessible and clearly interpreted, stakeholders can identify trends, validate their concerns, and advocate for targeted policy support. Ultimately, leveraging public statistics empowers local industries to make informed, data-driven decisions about resource allocation and strategic development.
