Súmate insta al CNE garantía a todos los sectores para actualizar RE

Súmate urges the National Electoral Council to ensure that electoral registration points are fully accessible, emphasizing the importance of inclusive locations in high-density and hard-to-reach areas. This demand aims to protect voting rights during the upcoming elections, highlighting the necessity for transparent and equitable administrative processes. At the same time, student organizations are mobilizing to monitor the registration period, pledging to denounce any irregularities. Their involvement underscores the critical need for independent monitoring mechanisms, which are essential for verifying that data collection procedures remain free from manipulation or exclusion. These efforts are vital for open data because they demand transparency in how voter records are managed and updated. Ensuring that electoral rolls are accurate, publicly verifiable, and universally accessible prevents disenfranchisement and allows citizens to hold authorities accountable through auditable, reliable information systems.
