Oklahoma Attorney General wins Freedom of Information award, becomes first AG to achieve the honor

Oklahoma’s Attorney General received a transparency award for championing open government access. This recognition highlights the critical role public leaders play in upholding the Open Meetings and Records Acts. His timely fulfillment of requests demonstrates how enforcement fosters accountability. Such leadership ensures citizens can effectively access information, which is foundational to transparent governance. This case illustrates why open data matters: it builds public trust. By prioritizing immediate access, officials model best practices for sharing information openly with their communities.
