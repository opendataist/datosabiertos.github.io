Nvidia defiende su plataforma de IA NeMo ante la denuncia de varios escritores

Nvidia denies copyright infringement in NeMo, asserting full legal compliance against writers’ claims. This dispute highlights the critical tension between AI development and intellectual property rights. The case underscores the urgent need for clear open data guidelines in machine learning. Establishing transparent standards for training data usage is essential to protect creators while fostering innovation. The outcome will significantly influence future regulatory frameworks and ethical practices in the AI industry.
