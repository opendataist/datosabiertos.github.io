Criminal CDC Releases 148 BLANK PAGES on COVID Vax Myocarditis After FOIA

The article describes the CDC’s release of a fully redacted 148-page report on vaccine-induced myocarditis, resulting from a FOIA request. This action is portrayed as a cynical gesture by a transparent administration, effectively withholding critical safety data under the guise of compliance. The author argues that such obfuscation represents a significant breach of public trust, suggesting that government authorities are actively concealing information regarding serious health consequences associated with mRNA vaccinations. This incident highlights a critical failure in open data practices, where requested information is rendered useless through excessive redaction. Instead of promoting transparency and accountability, the agency demonstrates how opaque bureaucracy can hinder public understanding of medical risks. The situation implies that open data requests are not yielding accessible truths but rather serving to validate narratives of institutional secrecy and potential malfeasance. For the open data community, this case serves as a stark warning about the integrity of public health disclosures. It underscores the necessity for truly accessible, unredacted data to allow independent scrutiny and verification. Without genuine transparency, the public cannot make informed decisions, and the potential for uncovering systematic errors or biases remains suppressed. Ultimately, the article argues that current data practices are failing their fundamental purpose of serving the public interest.
