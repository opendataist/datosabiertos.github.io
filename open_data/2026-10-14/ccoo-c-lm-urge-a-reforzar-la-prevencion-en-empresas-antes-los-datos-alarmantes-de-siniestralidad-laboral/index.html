CCOO C-LM urge a reforzar la prevención en empresas antes los "datos alarmantes de siniestralidad laboral"

CCOO C-LM urge a reforzar la prevención en empresas antes los "datos alarmantes de siniestralidad laboral"

The CCOO union has condemned the rise in fatal workplace accidents, attributing this trend to the lack of preventive measures by employers. This reality underscores the urgent need to prioritize workplace safety to protect workers’ health in the face of an unacceptable social scourge. This report is significant for open data, as it highlights the importance of transparency in occupational accident statistics. Public access to these data enables monitoring of regulatory compliance and holding parties accountable, thereby facilitating informed decision-making that safeguards workers’ rights.
