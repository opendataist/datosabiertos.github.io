Lorca revisa 170 expedientes y realiza 350 inspecciones en viviendas para combatir irregularidades en el padrón

Lorca revisa 170 expedientes y realiza 350 inspecciones en viviendas para combatir irregularidades en el padrón

The Lorca City Council has intensified its oversight of the Municipal Register through home inspections and file reviews to ensure the accuracy of the data. This administrative effort aims to combat irregularities in actual residence, resulting in ex officio deregistrations and address corrections, thereby ensuring the reliability of the urban census record. The strict enforcement of these regulations has enabled the detection of urban planning violations and unauthorized changes in use, highlighting the interdependence between municipal registration and territorial planning. The inspections reveal the need to coordinate efforts among various public agencies, such as the Cadastre and the Tax Agency, to achieve comprehensive management of housing and its inhabitants. This initiative is significant for the field of open data, as it demonstrates how transparency and the quality of census data directly impact urban planning and the allocation of public resources. The results underscore the importance of rigorous verification processes and inter-institutional collaboration to maintain accurate and up-to-date databases, which are essential for modern governance and accountability.
