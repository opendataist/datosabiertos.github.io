Valsequillo puede aumentar de 13 a 17 concejales en las próximas elecciones municipales | Canarias7

Valsequillo puede aumentar de 13 a 17 concejales en las próximas elecciones municipales | Canarias7

The article highlights that the outcome of the upcoming municipal elections in Valsequillo critically depends on the official update of the census by the National Statistics Institute. If the municipality exceeds 10,000 inhabitants, electoral legislation requires expanding the city council from 13 to 17 councilors, thereby altering the absolute majority needed to form a government. This structural change significantly shifts the dynamics of power, particularly in a recent political context marked by instability and changes in governance. The direct implication of this expansion is a reduction in the vote threshold required to gain parliamentary representation under the D'Hondt system. By increasing the number of seats, it becomes easier for smaller political parties to enter the council, which could further fragment the local political landscape or enable new coalitions. This transforms the electoral competition, as voting strategies and the viability of political parties are affected by a lower barrier to entry for representation. This news is relevant to open data because it underscores how the availability and updating of official statistical data directly impact governance and the democratic structure. Transparency in census management is not merely an administrative requirement but a decisive factor that can reshape citizen representation. Access to this data enables society to anticipate institutional changes and better understand the electoral rules based on updated demographic criteria.
