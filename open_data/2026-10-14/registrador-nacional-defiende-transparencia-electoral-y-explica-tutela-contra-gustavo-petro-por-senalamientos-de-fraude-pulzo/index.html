Registrador nacional defiende transparencia electoral y explica tutela contra Gustavo Petro por señalamientos de fraude - Pulzo

Registrador nacional defiende transparencia electoral y explica tutela contra Gustavo Petro por señalamientos de fraude - Pulzo

The National Registrar’s Office defended the integrity and transparency of the Colombian electoral process, refuting fraud allegations promoted by former President Gustavo Petro. The Registrar emphasized that the methodology is based on physical ballot records and has been validated by multiple oversight bodies and international missions, ensuring that the results faithfully reflect the popular will without any manipulation. The filing of a tutela (a constitutional protection action) against Petro responds to attempts to challenge the electoral legitimacy and attribute undue powers to the entity. This legal action seeks to protect the institution’s reputation and restore public confidence in the elections, clarifying that the Registrar does not declare results but rather certifies a rigorously supervised and technically sound process. This issue is relevant to open data by highlighting the importance of accessibility and transparency in electoral information for accountability purposes. Furthermore, the National Registrar’s Office has promoted digital inclusion through the mass registration of Wayuu minors, demonstrating how civil registration data are fundamental to guaranteeing fundamental rights and closing social gaps in historically marginalized populations.
