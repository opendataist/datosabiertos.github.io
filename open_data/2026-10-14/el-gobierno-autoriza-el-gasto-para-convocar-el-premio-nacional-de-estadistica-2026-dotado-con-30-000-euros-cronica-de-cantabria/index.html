El Gobierno autoriza el gasto para convocar el Premio Nacional de Estadística 2026, dotado con 30.000 euros - Crónica de Cantabria

El Gobierno autoriza el gasto para convocar el Premio Nacional de Estadística 2026, dotado con 30.000 euros - Crónica de Cantabria

The Council of Ministers has authorized a modification of budgetary limits to ensure funding for the 2026 National Statistics Prize awarded by the National Statistics Institute (INE). This measure allows for the advance processing of the payment, with the associated financial obligations allocated to the 2027 fiscal year, thereby overcoming current restrictions. The prize recognizes scientific and methodological excellence in statistics, fostering research and academic development. Its annual call for entries has been a key tool for stimulating the refinement of statistical techniques in the scientific field since its inception. This announcement is relevant to open data because it strengthens the public data ecosystem. By rewarding methodological innovation, it promotes the quality and transparency of official statistics. This facilitates access to more robust and reliable information for society and supports evidence-based decision-making.
