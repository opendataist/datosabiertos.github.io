Anuncia INE visitas domiciliarias para verificación del padrón electoral

Anuncia INE visitas domiciliarias para verificación del padrón electoral

The National Electoral Institute launches a statistical survey operation to verify the quality of the electoral roll and citizen updates. This process ensures the transparency and accuracy of electoral records through scientific methods. Citizen participation is essential to achieve reliable data, thereby optimizing the credentialing process and database cleanup ahead of the upcoming elections. This initiative is significant for open data, as it commits to the public release of verifiable results. By making this data accessible to society, it promotes accountability and enables citizens to audit the integrity of the democratic electoral system.
