La inflación de septiembre fue de 1,9% y acumula un alza de 23,6% en lo que va del 2026 | canal26.com

La inflación de septiembre fue de 1,9% y acumula un alza de 23,6% en lo que va del 2026 | canal26.com

The inflation report reveals a volatile trend in the Argentine economy, with September’s rate slightly exceeding that of the previous month, yet remaining close to market expectations. This behavior reflects a characteristic pattern of fluctuations that began in July, marked by intermittent highs and lows that contrast with considerably high annual cumulative figures. The closeness between actual figures and private sector projections suggests a degree of short-term predictability in price dynamics, although the persistence of high levels in the annual cumulative figure underscores the structural economic pressures facing the population. The composition of the overall increase shows that the housing and public utilities sector led the variations due to tariff adjustments, while education also recorded significant increases. In contrast, food and non-alcoholic beverages—a crucial category due to its social impact—grew moderately, acting as a buffer that prevented a more drastic acceleration of the overall index. This moderation in the basic food basket is essential for cost-of-living stability, despite the seasonal slowdown observed in sectors such as restaurants and tourism following the winter season. This analysis is relevant to the field of open data, as it demonstrates how transparency in official statistics allows public information to be contrasted with private sector projections. Access to detailed, disaggregated time series by category facilitates citizen oversight and academic research on economic policies. The availability of these comprehensive data empowers civil society to understand the structural causes of inflation and evaluate the effectiveness of government measures, promoting informed decision-making based on verifiable and accessible evidence.
