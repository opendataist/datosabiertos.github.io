The Padrón City Council is strengthening its social services through grants to expand teams and improve workplace safety. The introduction of alert watches for home-care staff ensures immediate protection in emergencies, with coordinated responses from security forces. This measure reflects the importance of integrating accessible technology into public management, a key principle of open data. By prioritizing transparency and citizen well-being through digital solutions, it demonstrates how open data and tools can optimize resources and improve users’ quality of life. The deployment of these devices highlights the need for evidence-based public policies and access to information. It facilitates accountability and enables the community to understand how public funds are allocated, fostering clearer and more efficient governance that directly benefits society.
