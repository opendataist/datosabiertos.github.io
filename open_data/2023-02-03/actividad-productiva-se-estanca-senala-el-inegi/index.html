Actividad productiva se estanca, señala el Inegi

Mexico’s economic growth stalled and remained weak through early 2023, according to INEGI’s composite indicators. A consecutive decline in the forward-looking indicator signals a continued recessionary trend, suggesting broader economic contraction ahead. This stagnation affects key sectors, including manufacturing employment and business confidence, reflecting widespread activity loss rather than isolated fluctuations. The analysis is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates how public statistical indices provide transparent, real-time insights into national economic health. Such accessible data empowers researchers and citizens to understand policy implications and effectively monitor structural challenges.
