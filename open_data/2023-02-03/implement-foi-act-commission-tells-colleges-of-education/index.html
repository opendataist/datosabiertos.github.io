Implement FOI Act, commission tells colleges of education

The National Commission for Colleges of Education advocates for the strict implementation of the Freedom of Information Act in Federal Colleges of Education to ensure transparent information dissemination. This initiative aims to empower citizens through accessible data, thereby reducing misinformation and fostering a culture of accountability within educational institutions. By improving access, these colleges can enhance their public image and promote better societal development through informed reasoning. This focus aligns directly with open data principles by demanding that institutions proactively share relevant information rather than withholding it. It emphasizes the need for institutional websites to accommodate public queries, ensuring that data is readily available and usable. This shift from passive compliance to active transparency supports the global open data movement, which seeks to make government and public sector information accessible to all. Ultimately, the call for training FOI officers and preparing for evaluations underscores a structural commitment to openness. It highlights that true open data requires not just legal frameworks, but active institutional effort to process and disseminate information effectively. This approach ensures that educational data serves as a public good, enhancing trust and facilitating the free flow of knowledge essential for modern democratic engagement.
