Investiga INAI filtración de datos de María Casar en la Mañanera

The INAI warns that publishing documents containing sensitive personal information about María Amparo Casar and her relatives violates data protection laws, potentially triggering legal proceedings if affected individuals file complaints. This controversy arises after the Presidency released files related to a past Pemex case, despite initial denials of corruption during a government briefing. The incident highlights critical governance issues regarding transparency and privacy, directly impacting open data practices by showing how public institutions must balance information access with the protection of individuals' rights. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the necessity of rigorous data sanitization protocols. It demonstrates that releasing public records without proper anonymization can lead to significant legal consequences, emphasizing that ethical open data initiatives must prioritize compliance with personal data protection regulations to maintain trust and legality.
