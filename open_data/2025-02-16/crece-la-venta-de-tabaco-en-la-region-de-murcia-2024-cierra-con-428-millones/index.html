Crece la venta de tabaco en la Región de Murcia: 2024 cierra con 428 millones

Tobacco sales in Murcia rose significantly in 2024, surpassing forty billion euros and reaching the tenth largest volume nationally. This growth across diverse product categories, from cigarettes to pipe tobacco, indicates a resilient and expanding local market despite broader economic fluctuations. The data highlights specific shifts in consumption patterns, with pipe tobacco seeing the sharpest percentage increase. These trends suggest evolving consumer preferences within the region, moving away from some traditional formats toward others that demonstrated stronger annual growth rates. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the value of transparent, publicly accessible government statistics. By leveraging such official fiscal data, researchers and policymakers can analyze regional economic behaviors, enabling evidence-based strategies for public health initiatives and tax policy adjustments.
