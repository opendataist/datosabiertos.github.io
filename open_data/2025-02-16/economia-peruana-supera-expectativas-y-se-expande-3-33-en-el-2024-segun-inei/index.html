Economía peruana supera expectativas y se expande 3.33% en el 2024, según INEI

Economía peruana supera expectativas y se expande 3.33% en el 2024, según INEI

The INEI report highlights sustained growth in national production through the end of 2024, marking the highest rate in the post-pandemic period. This expansion is primarily driven by dynamism in the agricultural, manufacturing, and services sectors, suggesting robust economic resilience. The diversity of growth drivers indicates that the recovery does not depend on a single sector but rather on a positive convergence across overall productive activity, including a significant rebound in industrial fishing. The relevance of this article to the field of open data lies in the critical importance of the timely and detailed publication of macroeconomic statistics by public entities. Access to technical reports that break down performance by subsector enables researchers, analysts, and citizens to accurately verify economic trends. This transparency facilitates the assessment of the impact of public policies and fosters accountability, which are fundamental elements for the integrity of state information. Furthermore, the report illustrates how open data should be structured to be useful in decision-making. By highlighting the contrast between success in marine extraction and contraction in public construction, the data reveal hidden structural imbalances within national aggregates. Understanding these disparities requires that information not only be available but also accessible and comprehensible, enabling civil society and the private sector to identify specific opportunities and risks within the real economy.
