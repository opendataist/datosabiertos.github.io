Panama with fewer formal labour contracts - Prensa Latina

Panama with fewer formal labour contracts - Prensa Latina

Panama faces a crisis of confidence rather than just employment, marked by declining formal job creation and rising unemployment. The surge in temporary contracts and informal work highlights a structural failure to provide job stability, undermining workers' access to credit and long-term economic security. This situation stems from reduced private investment driven by lost investment grades and higher financing costs. The lack of a cohesive strategy to enforce labor laws exacerbates the problem, allowing practices that violate labor rights and perpetuate informal employment dynamics. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it underscores the need for transparent, accessible labor statistics. By analyzing these trends, researchers and policymakers can better understand the disconnect between economic indicators and worker well-being, advocating for reforms that support formal employment and economic stability.
