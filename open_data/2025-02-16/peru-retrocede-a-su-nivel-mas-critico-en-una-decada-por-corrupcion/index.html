Perú retrocede a su nivel más crítico en una década por corrupción

Perú retrocede a su nivel más crítico en una década por corrupción

The 2024 Corruption Perceptions Index reveals that Peru is facing its most critical level in a decade, marking a significant setback in the fight against public corruption. This historic drop in score not only damages the country’s international image but also indicates institutional weakness that directly affects democracy, the economy, and social development, placing Peru among the nations with the greatest transparency problems on the continent. The crisis is exacerbated by the involvement of high-level political leaders, impunity, and the presence of corruption risks in hundreds of public entities. This lack of oversight weakens state structures and has serious consequences for human rights, as evidenced by the persecution of environmental defenders and the violation of democratic guarantees, which stands in stark contrast to countries that maintain strong institutions and low corruption levels. This article is relevant to the field of open data because it highlights the urgency of accessing and using governance indicators to measure state transparency. The availability of verifiable and public data is essential to promote accountability, facilitate citizen scrutiny of public management, and demand reforms that strengthen integrity in government institutions.
