Alumbrado insuficiente y baches, los problemas

Alumbrado insuficiente y baches, los problemas

The ENSU report reveals that Mexican citizens overwhelmingly identify urban infrastructure deficits as their primary concerns. Potholes, water supply failures, and inadequate lighting top the list, highlighting critical gaps in municipal service maintenance. This data underscores the vital role of open data in public accountability. By making these statistics accessible, authorities can pinpoint specific urban decay issues. Transparent information allows communities to demand targeted improvements in essential public services. Ultimately, leveraging open data transforms citizen complaints into actionable insights. It bridges the gap between public perception and government action. This approach fosters better urban planning and ensures resources address the most pressing daily challenges faced by residents.
