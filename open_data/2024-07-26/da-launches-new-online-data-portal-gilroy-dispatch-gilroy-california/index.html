DA launches new online data portal | Gilroy Dispatch | Gilroy, California

Santa Clara County launched a data dashboard to enhance transparency and accountability in its criminal justice system. This initiative allows the public to view key metrics, such as case filing rates and victim demographics, fostering greater community engagement and trust. The data reveals disparities in victim demographics while showing equitable prosecution rates across racial groups. By highlighting these realities, the office aims to identify areas for improvement and ensure the system operates fairly and effectively for all residents. This project is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how public sector transparency empowers communities. Collaborating with justice reform groups, it shows how accessible information supports evidence-based advocacy for equitable policies and systemic reform.
