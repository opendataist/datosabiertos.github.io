La caída del teletrabajo, entre las claves del aumento del tráfico que llega a Palma

Traffic volumes in Mallorca are increasing as in-person work resumes, driven by employees reducing their telework days and a shift in commuting habits. This surge marks a definitive end to the pandemic-era declines, with vehicles from key areas such as Inca and Llucmajor driving the increase despite previous record lows. Public transport usage remains insufficient to offset this growth. Although initiatives like free train rides have attracted new users, most commuters still prefer private vehicles to access industrial zones, limiting the effectiveness of alternative mobility strategies in reducing overall congestion. This data is crucial for open_data, as it highlights the real-world impact of remote work policies and transportation subsidies. Tracking these trends helps policymakers understand how digital flexibility affects physical infrastructure, enabling more accurate urban planning and resource allocation for future mobility challenges.
