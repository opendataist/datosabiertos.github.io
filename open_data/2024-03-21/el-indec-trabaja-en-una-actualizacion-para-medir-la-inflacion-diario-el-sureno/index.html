El INDEC trabaja en una actualización para medir la inflación - Diario El Sureño

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses is developing an updated Consumer Price Index using data from 2017–2018, replacing the outdated 2004–2005 survey. This modernization ensures that the index accurately reflects current consumption patterns, a methodological shift approved by the IMF to enhance statistical reliability and transparency. The new dataset will serve critical functions beyond inflation tracking. It provides essential weights for poverty lines, national accounts, and public policy design. By aligning statistical tools with modern realities, the government aims to improve the precision of social indicators and economic planning. This update is vital for open data, as it represents a significant step toward transparent, internationally recognized statistical standards. Reliable, publicly accessible data empowers researchers and citizens to hold authorities accountable. The shift from obsolete surveys to current, verifiable metrics strengthens public trust in official statistics and facilitates more effective data-driven decision-making.
