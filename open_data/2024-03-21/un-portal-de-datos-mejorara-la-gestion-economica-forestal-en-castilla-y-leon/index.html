Un portal de datos mejorará la gestión económica forestal en Castilla y León

The Junta de Castilla y León has launched the new Forestry Data Portal, a centralized digital platform that makes comprehensive forest information publicly accessible. By aggregating statistics on the sector’s economic value and its socioeconomic contribution, the initiative enables a detailed assessment of the evolution and management of natural resources, directly reinforcing the region’s position as a national reference in forestry and the natural environment. This open data strategy facilitates informed decision-making across technical, political, and economic domains. By providing transparent, up-to-date, and analyzed data, the portal serves as a foundational tool for advanced forest management and public policy, encouraging entrepreneurship and job creation in rural areas while ensuring sustainable governance that aligns with societal needs. The project’s relevance to open data lies in its commitment to transparency and civic utility. By transforming raw forest metrics into accessible public knowledge, the initiative empowers citizens, researchers, and businesses to understand ecosystem services and economic potential, thereby fostering a data-driven bioeconomy that helps stabilize the population and drives regional development through shared, verifiable information.
