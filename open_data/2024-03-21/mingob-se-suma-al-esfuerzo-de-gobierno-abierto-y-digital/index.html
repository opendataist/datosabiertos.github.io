Mingob se suma al esfuerzo de Gobierno Abierto y Digital

The Guatemalan government launched an Open and Digital Government project to enhance transparency and citizen participation in decision-making processes. This initiative prioritizes civic input and cybersecurity, aiming to develop more equitable public policies through inclusive collaboration and accountability. This launch is crucial for open data, as it establishes a framework for releasing government information, ensuring that open data directly empowers citizens and fosters a culture of accessibility and shared governance.
