La Junta impulsa el Portal de Datos Forestales en CyL para potenciar la "eficiente" gestión de los bosques

The regional government of Castilla y León has launched the Forest Data Portal, a digital tool designed to provide continuous, reliable, and up-to-date information on the region’s extensive forest areas. This initiative aims to revolutionize forest management by enabling both public administrations and private enterprises to make informed decisions based on accurate statistics. By offering immediate access to critical data, the portal supports efficient planning and enhances the region’s attractiveness for businesses seeking to operate in the forestry sector. The relevance of this project to open data lies in its commitment to transparency and shared accessibility. The portal aggregates information not only from public bodies but also from forest managers and landowners, creating a comprehensive knowledge ecosystem. This approach aligns with open data principles by breaking down silos between different stakeholders, thereby fostering collaboration and ensuring that economic and environmental actors have the real-time insights necessary for sustainable development and effective resource management. Furthermore, this digital innovation complements broader European initiatives such as RetechFOR, reinforcing the region’s status as a leader in forestry innovation. The integration of advanced digital tools highlights the strategic importance of modernizing public services through data-driven methods. Ultimately, the portal serves as a foundational element for a smarter forestry sector, demonstrating how open and accessible data can drive both ecological sustainability and economic growth in resource-rich regions.
