Argentina ended 2023 in recession, with GDP contracting significantly due to declining capital formation and exports. While private consumption and public services showed minor growth, sectors like manufacturing and agriculture suffered sharp declines, particularly in the final quarter. This economic downturn highlights the critical role of reliable statistical data in understanding complex national trends. Accurate reporting from national institutes allows policymakers and economists to identify specific sectors driving growth or decline, enabling more targeted interventions. Such transparency is vital for open data initiatives, as it fosters public trust and facilitates independent analysis. By making these economic indicators accessible, societies can better debate fiscal policies and hold institutions accountable, ensuring that data serves as a tool for informed democratic decision-making rather than just a bureaucratic record.
