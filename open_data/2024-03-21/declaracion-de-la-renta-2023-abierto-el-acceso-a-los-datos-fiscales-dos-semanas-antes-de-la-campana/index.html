Declaración de la Renta 2023: abierto el acceso a los datos fiscales dos semanas antes de la campaña

The upcoming tax season brings a significant shift toward electronic filing, reinforced by a recent Supreme Court ruling, although the administration remains committed to providing personalized assistance for those who need it. This transition represents a crucial step in digitizing fiscal interactions, ensuring that while efficiency improves, the digital divide does not prevent vulnerable taxpayers from accessing essential services. At the heart of this process is proactive access to pre-filled tax data, enabling citizens to verify their registered information—such as income and bank details—before submitting their returns. By empowering individuals with transparency and control over their data prior to submission, the system enhances accuracy and trust. This mechanism exemplifies the potential of open government data to facilitate user-centric administrative services, reducing errors and fostering civic engagement. Furthermore, new incentives for energy efficiency and affordable housing demonstrate how tax policy can be leveraged to address social and environmental challenges. These measures highlight the relevance of open data in public governance, as they rely on transparent, accessible frameworks to implement complex policies. Ultimately, the integration of open fiscal data with tailored services illustrates a modern approach to administration that prioritizes clarity, accessibility, and social responsibility.
