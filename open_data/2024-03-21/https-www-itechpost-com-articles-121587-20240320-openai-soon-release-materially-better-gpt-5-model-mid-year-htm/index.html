OpenAI is preparing to launch GPT-5, a significantly improved model intended to replace the struggling GPT-4 series. This update aims to resolve recent usability issues and enhance automated data processing capabilities, marking a pivotal shift in OpenAI’s current technology stack. To build these advanced systems, OpenAI has secured expensive licensing deals with major publishers, contrasting sharply with its past legal battles over unauthorized content use. This strategic pivot toward compensated data acquisition highlights the industry’s growing emphasis on legal and ethical sourcing of training materials. This development is crucial for open data advocates, as it demonstrates the immense financial and legal pressure behind proprietary AI training. It underscores the tension between corporate data monopolization and the principles of open access, signaling a future where high-quality data may become increasingly commodified and restricted rather than freely available.
