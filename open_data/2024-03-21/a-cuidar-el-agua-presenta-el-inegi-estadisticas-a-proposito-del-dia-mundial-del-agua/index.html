¡A cuidar el agua! Presenta el Inegi estadísticas a propósito del Día Mundial del Agua

The article highlights a severe drought crisis in Mexico, emphasizing that water scarcity is the primary cause of losses for active agricultural production units. It underscores the critical dependency of human development and ecosystem sustainability on adequate water distribution, warning that climate-induced damages severely impact food production and economic stability across the nation. This situation has persisted from 2022 through early 2024, with extreme temperatures and low rainfall exacerbating shortages in regions like Sinaloa. The narrative illustrates how prolonged environmental stress leads to empty reservoirs and significant agricultural setbacks, signaling that the lack of improvement in water reserves threatens future harvests and regional economies during upcoming heat seasons. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives as it demonstrates the necessity of transparent, accessible statistics from institutions such as INEGI and Conagua. By making this information publicly available, stakeholders can better monitor environmental risks, advocate for sustainable policies, and ensure that decision-makers have the accurate, real-time insights needed to address the growing water crisis and protect vulnerable agricultural sectors.
