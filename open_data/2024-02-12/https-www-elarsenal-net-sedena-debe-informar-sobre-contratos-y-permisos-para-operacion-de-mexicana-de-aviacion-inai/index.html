The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) determined that the Secretariat of National Defense (SEDENA) is responsible for making transparent information regarding the reconstruction of Mexicana de Aviación, given its status as the majority shareholder. This resolution underscores the need for accountability in the management of public resources when the State directly intervenes in commercial operations, ensuring that authorities assume their jurisdiction to facilitate access to data on contracts and authorizations. The decision reinforces the principle that transparency is indispensable for overseeing the proper handling of federal funds. By ordering SEDENA to conduct a thorough search across all its administrative units, the ruling establishes a clear precedent regarding the obligation to provide clear and verifiable information, enabling citizens to monitor government decisions and the correct execution of the budget. This case is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how mechanisms for accessing public information can be used to ensure integrity in state procurement. By demanding the disclosure of specific details about commercial agreements and operational costs, it promotes the creation of open data that facilitate social auditing, civil collaboration, and trust in public administration.
