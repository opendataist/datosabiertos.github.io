Mo Ibrahim Foundation announces new updates to the Ibrahim Index of African Governance data portal

The Mo Ibrahim Foundation has upgraded its Data Portal to offer enhanced, interactive tools for exploring governance performance across African nations. This update makes the Ibrahim Index of African Governance significantly more accessible, allowing users to drill down from broad continental trends to specific national indicators through improved visualizations and comparison features. This resource is vital for open data because it democratizes access to high-quality, standardized metrics on public goods and services. By providing detailed, long-term datasets that are openly available, it empowers citizens, civil society organizations, and researchers to hold governments accountable and engage in evidence-based advocacy without needing specialized technical expertise to interpret complex statistical reports. The implications extend beyond simple data viewing, fostering deeper analysis of democratic health and economic opportunity. By simplifying the exploration of scores and trends, the portal encourages broader public discourse and informed decision-making, ensuring that governance data serves as a practical tool for development and transparency rather than remaining an exclusive academic commodity.
