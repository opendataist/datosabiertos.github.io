CPLT investiga a 66 municipios por bajo cumplimiento de la Ley de Transparencia

The study highlights significant gaps in municipal transparency, revealing that many local governments fail to maintain legally required online data, undermining civic oversight and institutional accountability. This inconsistency demonstrates that statutory mandates alone are insufficient to ensure consistent open data practices without stronger enforcement mechanisms and public scrutiny. Consequently, improving transparency requires addressing systemic neglect to protect citizens' right to access accurate and up-to-date public information.
