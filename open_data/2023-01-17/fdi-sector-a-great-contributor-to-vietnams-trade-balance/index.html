FDI sector – a great contributor to Vietnam’s trade balance

Foreign Invested Enterprises significantly drive Vietnam’s trade expansion, highlighting the economy’s reliance on the FDI sector. While this foreign investment generates substantial export revenues and trade surpluses, it underscores a critical vulnerability in local supply chains. The dominance of multinational corporations suggests that domestic small and medium-sized enterprises lack sufficient capacity to compete effectively in international markets. This dynamic reveals important insights for open_data initiatives focused on economic transparency. Understanding the disparity between foreign and local enterprise performance requires accessible, granular trade datasets. Open data can help policymakers and researchers analyze these structural imbalances, fostering greater equity in global trade participation. Consequently, promoting open access to detailed import-export statistics is vital for developing local competitive advantages. By leveraging transparent data, stakeholders can identify gaps in domestic capabilities and support strategic interventions. This approach ensures that future trade growth is more inclusive and less dependent on external foreign investment, strengthening the overall resilience of the national economy.
