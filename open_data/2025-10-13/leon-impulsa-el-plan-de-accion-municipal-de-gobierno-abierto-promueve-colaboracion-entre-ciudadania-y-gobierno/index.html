León impulsa el Plan de Acción Municipal de Gobierno Abierto; promueve colaboración entre ciudadanía y gobierno

León impulsa el Plan de Acción Municipal de Gobierno Abierto; promueve colaboración entre ciudadanía y gobierno

León advances its Open Government action plan through co-creation, aiming to strengthen institutional transparency and citizen participation via direct engagement and digital tools. This strategy empowers residents to actively monitor public policies and influence decision-making processes across key municipal initiatives. The initiative is vital for open data as it institutionalizes data openness alongside participatory budgeting, fostering a culture where digital tools serve citizens. By leveraging transparency as a core governance pillar, León sets a national benchmark for how open data integrates with broader democratic innovation and accountability mechanisms.
