Precio de la carne de res se dispara

Precio de la carne de res se dispara

The price of beef in Mexico has experienced a significant increase, marking the largest rise in a decade due to restrictions on exports to the United States. These trade barriers, triggered by the presence of the screwworm fly, have reduced shipments and created an excess supply in the domestic market that, although limited, is pushing up costs for consumers. This phenomenon illustrates the vulnerability of local markets to cross-border sanitary and trade factors, highlighting how international regulations directly impact domestic inflation and the availability of basic goods. The situation underscores the need for robust biosecurity strategies and market diversification to stabilize prices and protect the national supply chain. The relevance of this article for the open data community lies in the importance of making economic and sanitary indicators accessible and understandable. Transparency in data from institutions such as INEGI (National Institute of Statistics and Geography) and in sanitary alerts enables civil society, researchers, and policymakers to monitor trends, analyze causal relationships, and promote informed responses to economic crises linked to animal health and international trade.
