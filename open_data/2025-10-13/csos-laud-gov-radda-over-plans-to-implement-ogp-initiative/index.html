CSOs laud Gov Radda over plans to implement OGP initiative

CSOs laud Gov Radda over plans to implement OGP initiative

Katsina State’s commitment to the Open Government Partnership signals a vital shift toward transparent and participatory governance. This move addresses long-standing civil society demands for accountability in public resource management, fostering a stronger sense of civic belonging. Such openness empowers citizens and combats corruption through multi-stakeholder collaboration. By institutionalizing these values, the state sets a precedent for subnational accountability that directly benefits open data ecosystems. This development is crucial for open data because it creates a structural framework for releasing information. It ensures that data is not just available, but used to drive meaningful public engagement and institutional transparency, thereby enhancing democratic oversight.
