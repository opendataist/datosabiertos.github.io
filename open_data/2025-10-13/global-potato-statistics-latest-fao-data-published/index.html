Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The FAO’s latest global potato statistics reveal that China and India dominate worldwide production, collectively accounting for a significant portion of the 376 million tonnes harvested in 2021. This data highlights the critical role these nations play in the global agricultural landscape, establishing a clear hierarchy of production capacity that extends from major Asian economies to significant contributors in Europe and the Americas. The disparity in output volumes underscores varying levels of agricultural investment and land utilization across different regions. Furthermore, the global average yield of approximately 21 tonnes per hectare provides a baseline for assessing agricultural efficiency. This metric allows stakeholders to evaluate the productivity of farming practices in diverse climatic and economic conditions. By examining these figures, researchers can identify opportunities for improving crop yields through better agricultural techniques and resource management, particularly in regions where current production levels lag behind the global mean. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates the utility of freely accessible international databases like FAOSTAT for extracting and analyzing comprehensive agricultural trends. The availability of such standardized datasets enables independent verification of national figures and supports comparative analysis across borders. Consequently, it empowers policymakers, researchers, and the public to make informed decisions based on transparent, globally aggregated information rather than relying solely on potentially conflicting official reports.
