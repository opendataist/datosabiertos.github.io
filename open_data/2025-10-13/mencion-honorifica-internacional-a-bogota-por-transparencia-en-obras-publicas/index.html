Mención honorífica internacional a Bogotá por transparencia en obras públicas

Mención honorífica internacional a Bogotá por transparencia en obras públicas

Bogotá received an honorific mention at the Open Government Summit for its CoST Infrastructure Transparency Initiative. This recognition highlights the city’s successful implementation of open data standards in public works, ensuring verifiable and detailed information on project progress and budgets. The strategy fosters citizen participation and strengthens public trust by making construction sites visible and traceable. By adopting international benchmarks, the initiative promotes collaborative governance, allowing civil society and the private sector to actively monitor developments and enhance accountability. This achievement is relevant to open data as it demonstrates how standardized, accessible information empowers oversight and combats corruption. It illustrates the practical value of transparency in modern governance, showing that publishing high-quality data directly improves integrity and public confidence in local institutions.
