La aerolínea australiana Qantas confirma la publicación de datos de 6 millones de clientes

Qantas has confirmed that data belonging to six million customers was published online following a cyberattack in July that targeted a third-party customer service platform. This breach exposed sensitive personal information, highlighting critical vulnerabilities in how airlines manage external vendor security and data integrity. The incident underscores the necessity for robust open data policies regarding privacy and transparency. As organizations increasingly rely on third-party systems, ensuring the protection of user data becomes paramount to maintaining public trust and preventing widespread exposure of personal details. The relevance to open data lies in the balance between transparency and security. While openness fosters accountability, this case demonstrates that without stringent safeguards, the sharing or mishandling of data can lead to significant breaches, urging stricter standards for data governance and public disclosure protocols.
