El presidente de Castilla-La Mancha reivindica el espíritu de "pacto" con el que nació la Constitución Española

Emiliano García-Page argues that Spain must recover the constitutional spirit of pact-making, moderation, and listening, rather than succumbing to extreme polarization and ideological confrontation. He suggests that the current political atmosphere has lost the methodological essence of the original Constitution, transforming political parties into combat zones instead of instruments for mutual help. The core message is that national unity requires rejecting premeditated confrontation to restore a collaborative environment that benefits society as a whole. Regionally, the article highlights that Castilla-La Mancha has successfully recovered lost ground, positioning itself among the top performers in economic growth since the turn of the millennium. This progress is attributed not just to increased wealth, but to a cohesive social model characterized by robust healthcare, education, and social services. The implication is that sustainable success comes from shared prosperity and stability, ensuring that economic advances translate into tangible improvements in citizens' daily lives and institutional trust. This article is relevant to open_data because it references statistical growth metrics from the National Statistics Institute (INE) to validate political narratives and regional performance. It underscores the role of publicly available data in shaping public perception of regional success and legitimizing policy outcomes.
