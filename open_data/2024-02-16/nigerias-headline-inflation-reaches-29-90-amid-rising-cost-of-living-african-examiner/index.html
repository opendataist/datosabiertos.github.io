Nigeria’s Headline Inflation Reaches 29.90% Amid Rising Cost Of Living | African Examiner

Nigeria’s inflation surged, driven significantly by soaring food prices that outpaced general consumer costs. This sharp rise highlights critical challenges in supply chain stability and agricultural productivity, reflecting broader economic pressures on households. The data reveals persistent volatility in essential commodity markets, indicating deep-seated structural issues affecting daily living expenses. Such trends underscore the urgent need for policy interventions to stabilize prices and protect vulnerable populations from escalating costs.
