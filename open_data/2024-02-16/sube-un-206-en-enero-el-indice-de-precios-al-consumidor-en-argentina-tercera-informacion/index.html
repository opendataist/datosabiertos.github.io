Sube un 20,6% en enero el índice de precios al consumidor en Argentina - Tercera Información

Argentina’s January consumer price index showed a significant 20.6% monthly increase, driven primarily by rising costs in goods and transportation. Although slightly lower than December’s figures, the accumulated annual inflation remains extremely high, reflecting a severe economic crisis in which purchasing power is eroding rapidly. The article highlights a stark disparity in price growth across sectors, with recreation and household maintenance surging above average, while education and housing remain temporarily lower. However, experts warn that these sectors are poised for sharp increases due to policy changes, such as the removal of subsidies and deregulation, which threaten to exacerbate the cost of living for families. This situation is relevant to open data because it underscores the critical need for transparent, accessible, and timely statistical information. Reliable public data allows citizens to understand the real impact of inflation on their daily lives and holds the government accountable for its economic policies. Without clear, open datasets, the public cannot effectively analyze trends, verify official claims, or advocate for necessary social protections in an environment of extreme volatility.
