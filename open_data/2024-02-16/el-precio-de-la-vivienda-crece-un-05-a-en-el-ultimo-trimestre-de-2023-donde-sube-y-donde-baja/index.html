El precio de la vivienda crece un 0,5% a en el último trimestre de 2023: ¿dónde sube y dónde baja?

The Spanish real estate market showed resilience in late 2023, with prices rising slightly despite a significant drop in transaction volumes. This divergence indicates that while housing costs continue to increase, buyer confidence or financial capacity has weakened, resulting in fewer completed sales in both the new and secondary markets. Regional disparities remain pronounced, with some autonomous communities experiencing substantial price increases while others saw declines. This geographic fragmentation suggests that national averages obscure local market dynamics, where supply constraints in high-demand areas push prices up, while other regions experience stagnation or even price drops, creating an uneven landscape for both buyers and investors. This data is crucial for open data initiatives, as it highlights the need for transparent, detailed housing statistics. Reliable and accessible information enables policymakers and citizens to understand market trends beyond headline figures. By maintaining open access to such detailed metrics, society can better assess affordability crises and inform decisions related to urban planning and social housing policies.
