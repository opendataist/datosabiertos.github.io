El IPC sube un 3,4% en enero tras el alza del IVA a luz y gas y el precio de los alimentos vuelve a acelerar: se disparan un 7,4%

Spanish inflation reached 3.4% in January, driven by rising electricity prices that offset lower fuel costs. While food inflation remains high at 7.4%, underlying inflation continues to decline, signaling a gradual stabilization of the economy despite persistent pressure on household budgets. This trend is relevant to open data because transparent, real-time statistical releases enable citizens and researchers to track these economic shifts. Accessible datasets allow for a deeper analysis of regional disparities, such as the lower inflation rates observed in Madrid compared to other regions, fostering greater public trust and informed civic engagement. Ultimately, the data highlights the complex interplay between energy subsidies and everyday costs. By monitoring these indicators, stakeholders can better understand how policy changes impact purchasing power. This continuous flow of accurate information is essential for evaluating the effectiveness of government interventions in managing inflation and protecting consumer welfare.
