Liberia : Freedom of information conference brings together participants (...)

Participants at the Liberia FOI Conference urged the establishment of an independent data bank to serve as a reference point when public institutions fail to provide requested information. This initiative aims to ensure transparency by creating an alternative source of data across all relevant sectors, reinforcing the legal right to access public records even when custodians are unresponsive. The attendees pledged to actively engage with information holders and raise community awareness through translations of the law. Their commitment highlights the critical role of civil society and local actors in driving accountability, ensuring that the promise of open governance is realized through grassroots education and persistent demand for disclosure from both public and private bodies. This event is vital to open data because it demonstrates how legal frameworks must be supported by practical tools like accessible databases. By decentralizing appeal channels and establishing reliable data repositories, the conference underscores that true openness requires not just legislation, but robust infrastructure that empowers citizens to hold power accountable effectively.
