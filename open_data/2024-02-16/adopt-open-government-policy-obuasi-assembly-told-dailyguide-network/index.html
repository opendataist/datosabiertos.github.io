Adopt Open Government Policy – Obuasi Assembly Told - DailyGuide Network

Adopt Open Government Policy – Obuasi Assembly Told - DailyGuide Network

The Deputy Minister for Communications and Digitalization has urged the Obuasi Municipal Assembly to implement the Open Government Partnership initiative, emphasizing that transparency and citizen engagement are vital for effective decentralization. By adopting open government principles, local authorities can foster greater accountability and ensure that governance remains responsive to the needs of the populace. A key implication of this push is the integration of digital technologies to bridge the gap between officials and residents. Moving beyond traditional community engagements, the use of digital platforms allows for more direct and consistent communication, enabling assembly members to connect with constituents in real-time. This technological shift is presented as essential for making local leadership more accessible and accountable. Furthermore, strengthening local governance requires investing in the capacity of assembly members through leadership training. Equipping these officials with necessary skills ensures they can exercise their responsibilities effectively and prioritize public interest over partisan politics. This article highlights open data’s role in modernizing governance, where accessible information and digital tools empower citizens to hold their leaders accountable, thereby enhancing the quality of democratic participation at the local level.
