Gobierno Abierto: Tassano aseguró que ser modernos implica trabajar con la ciudadana - El litoral

The article highlights Corrientes’ active participation in the Open Government Partnership, marking a significant step toward institutionalizing transparency and civic engagement. By joining an international alliance alongside only a few other Argentine cities, the municipality acknowledges the responsibility of being under global scrutiny while committing to modernize its governance through innovation and open data. Central to this initiative is the "Smart City" theme, which emphasizes that access to municipal data empowers citizens to make informed decisions. The administration views open data not merely as a technical asset but as a crucial tool for democratic participation, enabling the public to evaluate government actions and collaborate on solving common urban challenges. This approach shifts the focus from traditional governance models to a more cooperative framework where transparency drives accountability. This development is highly relevant to open data because it demonstrates the practical application of data policies in real-world civic contexts. By organizing events like hackathons and data visualization contests, the government actively encourages the public to utilize open datasets. This fosters a culture where data serves as a bridge between the state and citizens, proving that accessible information is essential for building a participatory, transparent, and effective smart city.
