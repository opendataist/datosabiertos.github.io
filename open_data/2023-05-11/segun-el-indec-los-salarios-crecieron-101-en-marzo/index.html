Según el Indec, los salarios crecieron 10,1% en marzo

Public sector wages rose significantly, outpacing inflation in the first quarter, while the government exempted low-income earners from income tax to boost purchasing power. Officials claim that formal salaries have maintained their value, suggesting a recovery in real income stability for these workers during this period. This data is relevant to open_data because it highlights how public statistics enable transparent monitoring of economic indicators. Access to such granular employment and inflation data allows citizens to verify official claims about purchasing power and assess the actual impact of fiscal policies on different socioeconomic groups.
