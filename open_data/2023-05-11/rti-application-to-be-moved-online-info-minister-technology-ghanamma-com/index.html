RTI Application To Be Moved Online - Info. Minister | Technology - Ghanamma.com

RTI Application To Be Moved Online - Info. Minister | Technology - Ghanamma.com

Ghana is digitizing its Right to Information framework by launching an online application system. This initiative aims to streamline how citizens request public records and assist officers in maintaining accurate data. By moving processes to a centralized digital platform, the government seeks to significantly enhance the speed and efficiency of handling inquiries. The reform aligns with national goals for greater transparency and accountability within public institutions. It transforms how records are managed across government bodies, creating a more organized and accessible information ecosystem. This shift supports the broader legal mandate to ensure that public data remains available and understandable to the general populace. This development is crucial for open data advocacy, as it lowers barriers to accessing information. A user-friendly digital interface encourages greater public engagement with government data. It demonstrates a tangible commitment to modernizing transparency mechanisms, making it easier for researchers and citizens to utilize public records effectively.
