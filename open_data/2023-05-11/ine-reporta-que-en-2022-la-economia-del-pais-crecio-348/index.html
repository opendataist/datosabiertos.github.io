INE reporta que en 2022 la economía del país creció 3,48%

INE reporta que en 2022 la economía del país creció 3,48%

Bolivia’s 2022 economic growth faced significant headwinds from social conflicts, particularly the Santa Cruz civic strike, which severely impacted the export-oriented agro-industry and logistics sectors. While services such as hospitality and transportation rebounded strongly, the extractive industries suffered a sharp contraction, highlighting the volatility of an economy reliant on natural resources. This disparity reveals structural vulnerabilities where social unrest can quickly negate macroeconomic gains. The recovery was largely driven by domestic consumption and investment, with household spending and fixed capital formation showing robust expansion. This indicates that internal demand played a crucial role in sustaining growth despite external shocks. The data underscores the importance of analyzing economic performance through different expenditure types to understand which drivers are resilient versus those that are fragile during periods of social instability. This article is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent statistical reporting allows for granular analysis of complex socioeconomic dynamics. By making detailed sectoral and expenditure data publicly available, governments enable researchers and citizens to identify specific areas of risk and opportunity. Such transparency fosters accountability and supports evidence-based policy-making, ensuring that economic strategies address the underlying causes of instability rather than just the symptoms.
