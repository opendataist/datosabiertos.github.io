INE lanzó plataforma educativa sobre censo para estudiantes de escuelas y liceos

Uruguay highlights its commitment to transparency and citizen participation by integrating the national census into the education system. This strategy leverages students as multipliers to promote awareness of the population survey within their households, ensuring greater coverage and accuracy in national data. The development of the Ineduca digital platform represents a significant step forward in democratizing access to official information. By providing didactic resources and interactive tools tailored to young people, it normalizes the use of public data in academic settings, fostering a culture of statistical literacy from an early age. This approach is crucial for the open data ecosystem, as it demonstrates how education can enhance the collection and understanding of open data. Collaboration between public institutions and international organizations validates the importance of community participation in generating evidence-based public policies, strengthening trust in the transparent management of state information.
