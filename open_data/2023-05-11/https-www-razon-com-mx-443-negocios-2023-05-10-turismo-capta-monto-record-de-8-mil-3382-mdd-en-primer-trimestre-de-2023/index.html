Mexico achieved record foreign currency earnings in the first quarter of 2023, driven by a significant surge in international visitors and their spending. This milestone reflects a robust recovery in tourism, surpassing pre-pandemic figures and demonstrating the sector’s critical role in the national economy. The increase is attributed to a higher volume of arrivals and increased per-capita expenditure by tourists. Despite these gains, total visitor numbers remain below 2019 levels, indicating that while revenue is strong, the industry has not yet fully returned to its previous scale of activity. This data is relevant to open data as it highlights the importance of transparent, accessible statistics for economic analysis. Governments must publish accurate datasets on travel and finance to monitor recovery trends effectively. Such openness allows stakeholders to make informed decisions and supports accountability in tracking national economic performance.
