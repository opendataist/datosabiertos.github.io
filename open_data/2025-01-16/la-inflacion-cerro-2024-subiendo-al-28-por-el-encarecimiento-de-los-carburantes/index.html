La inflación cerró 2024 subiendo al 2,8% por el encarecimiento de los carburantes

The final data for 2024 reveals a slight increase in inflation, primarily driven by rising fuel and leisure costs. Despite this recent uptick, the annual rate remains significantly lower than the previous year, signaling that current economic policies are successfully balancing growth with price stability. This trend highlights the effectiveness of government measures in maintaining inflation at manageable levels across all regions. The consistent reduction compared to 2023 demonstrates that policy interventions are working to mitigate economic volatility, even as specific sectors like transportation experience temporary price pressures. This update is highly relevant to open data because it emphasizes the critical need for transparent, accessible, and verified statistical information. Reliable public datasets allow citizens and analysts to track these economic shifts, fostering trust in official institutions and enabling informed decisions based on clear, evidence-based metrics regarding national financial health.
