2025 will bring advancements in autonomous AI agents that can plan, execute and adapt tasks with little human intervention: Moody's Ratings

The 2025 AI landscape shifts from raw scaling to usability, driven by converging model performance and open-source competition. This evolution democratizes access to advanced tools, compelling organizations to rethink data strategies beyond traditional proprietary constraints. As high-quality natural data depletes, synthetic data emerges as a critical alternative to sustain training pipelines. However, its limitations in unstructured tasks highlight the persistent value of diverse, real-world datasets for robust model generalization. This transition underscores open data’s relevance, as transparent, accessible datasets enable innovation where proprietary silos fail. By fostering collaboration and reducing reliance on scarce resources, open data ecosystems become vital for sustaining AI progress and ensuring equitable technological advancement.
