La inflación de Reino Unido se modera más de lo previsto en diciembre, al 2,5%

The article reports a decline in UK inflation to 2.5%, falling below consensus forecasts. This moderation, including a drop in underlying inflation, signals that price pressures are easing. The data provides relief for policymakers, reinforcing expectations that the Bank of England will reduce interest rates at its upcoming meeting. This trend suggests a continued path of monetary easing, aiming to align prices with the central bank’s target. The narrative highlights the interplay between economic data and market stability. While lower inflation boosts confidence in the government’s fiscal management, concerns persist regarding the UK’s fragile fiscal landscape. Analysts note that although markets may initially react positively, long-term risks remain due to limited fiscal space, potentially leading to higher risk premiums for government bonds.
