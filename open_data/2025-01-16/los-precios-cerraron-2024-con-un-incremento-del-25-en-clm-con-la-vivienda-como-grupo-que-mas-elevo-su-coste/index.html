Los precios cerraron 2024 con un incremento del 2,5% en CLM, con la Vivienda como grupo que más elevó su coste

The article reports that inflation in Castilla-La Mancha rose to 2.5% annually, driven primarily by housing and energy costs, highlighting how essential services significantly impact household budgets. This trend reflects a broader national increase, indicating persistent pressure on consumer purchasing power across various regions. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for open data advocates, as transparent, granular price indexes enable citizens and researchers to analyze economic disparities and hold institutions accountable. Open data fosters trust by allowing independent verification of official statistics and empowering users to derive actionable insights from complex economic indicators. Ultimately, accessible data on inflation helps the public understand cost-of-living changes. By making such detailed information available, societies can better address economic inequalities and ensure that policy decisions are informed by accurate, widespread data rather than opaque or limited reports.
