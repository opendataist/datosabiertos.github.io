Los alquileres podrán subir un 2,28% si se aplica el nuevo índice de referencia del INE

The Spanish government has introduced a new reference index for residential rentals to regulate rent adjustments and promote affordability. This measure replaces the traditional inflation-linked method for contracts signed after mid-2023, establishing a cap on rent increases to prevent disproportionate hikes. By limiting adjustments based on an official index rather than general market inflation, the policy aims to provide stability for tenants while offering clear, calculable limits for landlords. The relevance to open data lies in the creation and publication of this new public index, known as IRAV. The government and statistical agencies are now providing transparent, machine-readable data sources that enable citizens and developers to build tools for calculating rent limits. This shift from opaque inflation metrics to a specific, publicly defined regulatory index ensures that information on housing costs is accessible, standardized, and usable for third-party applications, thereby enhancing transparency in the rental market. The initiative is part of a broader strategy to stimulate the affordable housing sector through tax incentives and public guarantees. By linking financial benefits to compliance with the new reference index, the administration encourages participation in a regulated market. This approach demonstrates how open and standardized data metrics can be leveraged not only for transparency but also as a foundational tool for implementing complex economic policies and social welfare measures.
