Indec difunde la actividad económica del tercer trimestre y las canastas básicas de noviembre

The National Institute of Statistics and Censuses is releasing key economic indicators that reveal a contraction in productive activity amid soaring inflation. This data highlights the severe economic distress caused by drought and rising prices, demonstrating how macroeconomic indicators directly affect household welfare and social stability. Crucially, the publication of updated cost baskets establishes the thresholds for poverty and extreme poverty, providing the official metrics needed to assess social vulnerability. These figures are essential for understanding the scale of deprivation, as they show that a significant portion of the population, particularly children, lives below the line where basic needs cannot be met. This report is relevant to open data because it underscores the need for transparent, accessible, and timely statistical information. By making official metrics on GDP, inflation, and poverty lines publicly available, institutions enable researchers, journalists, and citizens to verify government claims and hold policymakers accountable for the social consequences of economic policies.
