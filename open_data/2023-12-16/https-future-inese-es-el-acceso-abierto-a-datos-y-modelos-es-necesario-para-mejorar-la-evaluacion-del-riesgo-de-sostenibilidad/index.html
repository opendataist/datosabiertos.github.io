Open Data
ES EN

EIOPA launched an open-source European Disaster Data Centre to enhance transparency in climate risk exposure. This initiative empowers supervisors and insurers to accurately assess capital requirements and insurance protection gaps, fostering more robust catastrophe modeling. The open-access framework supports policymakers in designing prevention measures and aids academics in studying the impacts of climate change. By democratizing data, it facilitates independent research and improves the evaluation of sustainability-related financial risks across the sector. This release is vital for open data, as it demonstrates how transparent, accessible datasets can drive regulatory improvements. It highlights the critical role of shared information in strengthening financial stability and managing systemic risks associated with natural disasters in the European insurance market.
