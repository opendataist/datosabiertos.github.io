El INEFI recibe una puntuación de 93.29 en transparencia y gobierno abierto - 7dias.com.do

In 2022, INEFI achieved a high transparency score, demonstrating strict adherence to government ethics and integrity standards. This recognition underscores how consistent compliance with established indicators leads to superior public management quality, validating the effectiveness of DIGEIG’s annual evaluation framework. The assessment highlights the critical role of open data principles in modern governance. By maintaining an updated and accessible portal, institutions not only fulfill legal requirements but also actively enhance citizen trust through the free flow of information. This case is relevant to open data as it illustrates the tangible benefits of standardized, accessible public information. It proves that transparent digital infrastructure is essential for accountability, showing that well-managed open data directly improves the efficiency of public services and strengthens democratic participation.
