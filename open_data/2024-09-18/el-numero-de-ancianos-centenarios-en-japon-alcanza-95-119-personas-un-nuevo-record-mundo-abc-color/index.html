El número de ancianos centenarios en Japón alcanza 95.119 personas, un nuevo récord - Mundo - ABC Color

Japan reports unprecedented longevity and an aging population, marked by a record number of centenarians and the highest elderly demographic globally. This demographic shift highlights critical challenges for social security sustainability, emphasizing the urgent need for policy adaptation to support an aging society. The data underscores Japan’s unique position as a global benchmark for life expectancy, particularly for women. These figures serve as a crucial case study for open data initiatives, illustrating how transparent, public statistics can drive international understanding of demographic trends and inform future healthcare strategies worldwide.
