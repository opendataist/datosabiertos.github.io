Alarm Fatigue Taskforce Established

Maritime digitalization has drastically increased alarm volumes, leading to severe alarm fatigue that threatens crew welfare and operational safety. This surge undermines officer awareness and increases the risk of serious incidents, highlighting a critical gap where existing systems lack regulatory mandates to justify their safety utility. The findings underscore the urgent need for rationalized alarm management to balance necessary warnings with human workload limits. By addressing the unintended consequences of uncoordinated alarm requirements, the industry can restore effective operational awareness and ensure that digital tools support, rather than hinder, decision-making during critical moments. This research is pivotal for open data initiatives, as it establishes a framework for transparently sharing alarm performance metrics and mitigation strategies. By promoting accessible, standardized data on alarm effects, stakeholders can develop evidence-based solutions that enhance both safety standards and crew well-being across the global maritime sector.
