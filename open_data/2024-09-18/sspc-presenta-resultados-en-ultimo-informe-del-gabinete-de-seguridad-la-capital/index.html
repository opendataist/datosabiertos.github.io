SSPC presenta resultados en último informe del Gabinete de Seguridad - La Capital

The article highlights a significant shift in Mexican societal indicators, emphasizing a coordinated reduction in crime rates and poverty across the country. This trend is presented as a unique achievement, suggesting that the current administration’s security strategies have successfully decoupled criminal activity from socioeconomic hardship in nearly all states. The narrative frames these outcomes as a testament to the effectiveness of the National Security Cabinet and its integrated approach to governance. A crucial aspect of this transformation is the marked improvement in public perception regarding safety and institutional trust. The data indicates a substantial decrease in citizens’ feelings of insecurity and a rise in confidence toward government authorities. This growing trust in public institutions and the presidency is portrayed as a solid foundation for future governance, reflecting broader societal validation of the current political movement and its policies. This report is relevant to open data because it relies on transparent, statistically verified indicators from authoritative bodies such as INEGI and the OECD. By making these metrics publicly available and widely disseminated, the government demonstrates a commitment to evidence-based policy evaluation. The article underscores how accessible data can validate improvements in public safety and foster accountability, illustrating the power of open statistics to influence public opinion and legitimize administrative efforts through verifiable social progress.
