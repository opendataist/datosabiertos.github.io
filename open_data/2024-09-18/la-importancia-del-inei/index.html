La importancia del INEI

La importancia del INEI

The article highlights the crucial role of INEI in producing reliable statistics, emphasizing how this institutional framework promotes transparency and accessibility in official data. By generating comprehensive and verifiable information, INEI builds a foundation of trust that allows the broader community to effectively use official figures. This accessibility is essential for supporting accountability and informed public debate. This context is highly relevant to open data, as it demonstrates how authoritative statistical agencies provide high-quality, structured datasets necessary for effective digital transparency and civic engagement.
