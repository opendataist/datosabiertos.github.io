INE lamenta inasistencia a mesa técnica y se abre a fiscalizar “boleta por boleta”

INE lamenta inasistencia a mesa técnica y se abre a fiscalizar “boleta por boleta”

The National Statistics Institute (INE) asserts its role as a strictly technical body, refusing to engage in political disputes regarding recent census data. By emphasizing institutional transparency and the availability of individual ballot reviews, the INE aims to validate its results through technical rigor rather than political negotiation. This stance highlights the critical importance of maintaining the integrity and neutrality of official statistics, ensuring they remain objective tools for public administration rather than instruments for partisan agendas. However, the technical dialogue is complicated by the absence of key municipal leaders who question the figures. Their refusal to attend suggests that their motivations may be political rather than analytical, raising concerns about how data discrepancies are interpreted and contested. This dynamic underscores the relevance of open data initiatives in fostering trust; when stakeholders lack faith in the methodology, the potential for political polarization increases, potentially undermining the data's utility for policy-making and public accountability. Ultimately, the article illustrates the challenges of implementing open data in environments with deep political polarization. For open data to function effectively, it requires a shared commitment to technical transparency and accessible verification mechanisms. Without such alignment, even robust statistical processes may be dismissed as biased, eroding public trust and hindering evidence-based decision-making across different levels of government.
