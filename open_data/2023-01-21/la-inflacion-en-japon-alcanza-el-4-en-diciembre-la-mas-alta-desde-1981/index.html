La inflación en Japón alcanza el 4% en diciembre, la más alta desde 1981

Japan’s inflation surged to its highest level in decades, surpassing the central bank’s target. Despite this, authorities maintained their loose monetary policy, attributing the rise to temporary external shocks, such as energy costs, rather than sustained domestic pressure. This case underscores the critical role of high-quality open data in global economics. Transparent, timely government statistics enable markets and international organizations to accurately assess economic health and the effectiveness of policy measures. Reliable public datasets facilitate real-time monitoring of inflation trends, fostering better decision-making. When data is open and accessible, stakeholders can verify claims regarding temporary versus structural economic shifts, ensuring accountability and informed public discourse.
