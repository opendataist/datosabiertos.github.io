Gobierno Abierto: UNL recibió un premio internacional

The National University of the Littoral has been recognized as an “excellent academic practice” in Ibero-America for its Open Government Portal, distinguished by its effective integration of transparency, participation, and collaboration. The initiative is valued for its innovative visual approach and its ability to foster multidisciplinary action, establishing a replicable and demanding model in a context where such practices are still developing in many educational institutions. This recognition underscores the crucial importance of universities as key actors in promoting open public policies and strengthening democracy. By combining institutional commitment with the use of information technologies, the case demonstrates how the publication of structured data facilitates accountability and social dialogue, contributing to broader regional goals of sustainability and an open government. The relevance to open data lies in the validation that public data, when presented in an accessible and visual manner, enhance citizen participation and inter-institutional collaboration. This success highlights the need to integrate diverse disciplines, such as statistics and communication, to transform raw data into useful tools for society, inspiring other universities to adopt similar standards of transparency and openness.
