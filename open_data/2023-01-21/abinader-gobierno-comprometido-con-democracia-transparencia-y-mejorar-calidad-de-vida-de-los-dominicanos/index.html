Abinader: Gobierno comprometido con democracia, transparencia y mejorar calidad de vida de los dominicanos

Abinader: Gobierno comprometido con democracia, transparencia y mejorar calidad de vida de los dominicanos

The Dominican Republic’s presentation of its Fifth Open Government Action Plan marks a significant step toward deepening democracy through robust transparency and citizen participation. The government emphasizes that openness extends beyond mere data disclosure; it requires making information useful, reusable, and timely to genuinely improve public services and quality of life. This approach positions the country as a leader in the Open Government Partnership, aiming to foster a more collaborative and participatory democracy where institutional integrity is strengthened through active civic engagement. Central to this initiative is multi-actor collaboration among the state, civil society, and the private sector. The plan’s ten commitments were co-created through extensive public consultations and technical dialogues, ensuring they address critical social issues such as environmental sustainability, mental health, and anti-corruption efforts. By establishing permanent spaces for dialogue, the government seeks to align policy-making with citizen needs, thereby enhancing trust in public institutions and ensuring that reforms are both relevant and effective in tackling real-world challenges. This article is highly relevant to open data because it highlights the critical shift from simply publishing datasets to ensuring they are actionable and reusable for public benefit. The inclusion of specific commitments such as national open data platforms and efficient public procurement portals demonstrates how open data serves as a foundational tool for accountability and service improvement. It illustrates the practical application of open data principles to drive tangible governance reforms, proving that transparent information is essential for building responsive, efficient, and trusted public administration.
