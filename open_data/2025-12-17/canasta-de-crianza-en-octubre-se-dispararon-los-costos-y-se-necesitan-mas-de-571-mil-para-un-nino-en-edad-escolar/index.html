Canasta de Crianza: en octubre se dispararon los costos y se necesitan más de $571 mil para un niño en edad escolar

Canasta de Crianza: en octubre se dispararon los costos y se necesitan más de $571 mil para un niño en edad escolar

The INDEC reveals rising costs for child-rearing, highlighting significant expenses for care and basic goods across different age groups. This data underscores the financial burden on families, with older children requiring substantially higher monthly support than infants. These figures illustrate the escalating economic pressure on households, emphasizing that childcare is a major component of family expenditure rather than just a material cost. This information is vital for open data as it provides transparent, accessible metrics on social welfare indicators. Such data allows researchers and policymakers to analyze inequality, track inflation impacts on families, and design more effective social support systems.
