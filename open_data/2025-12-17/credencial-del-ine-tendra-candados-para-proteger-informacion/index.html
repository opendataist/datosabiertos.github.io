Credencial del INE tendrá candados para proteger información

Credencial del INE tendrá candados para proteger información

The National Electoral Institute (INE) has strengthened its security protocols for the production of voter identification cards, ensuring the confidentiality of the electoral roll. Following the awarding of the contract, strict technical requirements were implemented, including encryption and audits, to mitigate risks associated with the involvement of a public body under the Executive Branch. Councilors questioned the transparency surrounding how a government entity handles sensitive data without compromising the integrity of the system. In response, the INE clarified that only the essential information for manufacturing is transferred, and it is decrypted daily using proprietary tools. The data does not remain in the systems for more than one day, and providers are required to perform secure deletions that can be validated at any time by the institute. This initiative is relevant to the field of open data because it illustrates the delicate balance between administrative transparency and the protection of personal data. It demonstrates how public entities can apply international cybersecurity and audit standards when managing critical information, setting a precedent for how sensitive data can be shared or processed while maintaining citizen privacy and institutional trust in democratic electoral processes.
