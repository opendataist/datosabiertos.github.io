EU Passenger Package: A Golden Opportunity to Maximise Green Travel - CleanTechnica

EU Passenger Package: A Golden Opportunity to Maximise Green Travel - CleanTechnica

The EU Passenger Package represents significant progress in modernizing rail ticketing and enhancing passenger rights, yet it currently falls short of fully supporting the EU’s climate and rail policy goals. While the initiative aims to shift travelers from cars and planes to trains, its limited geographical scope restricts the availability of single tickets for many long-distance cross-border routes. Consequently, this narrow approach fails to maximize rail’s potential as a green alternative, risking the achievement of broader environmental targets and hindering the modal shift necessary to reduce Europe’s rising transport emissions. The article argues that the proposal’s scope must be expanded to include journeys between EU capitals under 1500km with up to two changes, aligning with the High Speed Rail Plan. Currently, the restriction prevents seamless booking on dominant platforms, making international rail less attractive compared to flights. By mandating that major operators host and sell tickets for these wider geographic areas, the EU can simplify the booking process and ensure consistent passenger rights across borders, thereby removing barriers that currently discourage potential rail travelers. Furthermore, the framework needs to evolve to handle real-world booking complexities and disruptions, ensuring equitable rights for all passengers. It should allow travelers to consolidate parts of a journey bought on different schedules into a single ticket on one platform and permit flexibility when missing connections, regardless of the operating company. These adjustments are crucial for open_data initiatives and digital transport interoperability, as they require standardized, accessible information to function effectively, ultimately creating a more user-friendly and reliable European rail network.
