Tamaulipas registra 15.7% de crecimiento industrial anual

Tamaulipas registra 15.7% de crecimiento industrial anual

Tamaulipas stands out as the national leader in industrial growth, driven primarily by mining and the government’s strategy to attract investment. This superior performance demonstrates how the use of high-quality public statistics enables the monitoring and validation of regional economic policies in comparison with other states in the country. The relevance of this report for open data lies in its demonstration of the transparency and accessibility of economic information generated by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). By publishing de-seasonalized and sector-disaggregated data, it facilitates comparative analysis and accountability regarding the impact of local administrations. Thus, open data serves as a fundamental tool for evaluating productive development and the effectiveness of infrastructure and international linkage strategies. This availability fosters informed decision-making and strengthens confidence in the institutions that ensure the interoperability and ethical use of national statistics.
