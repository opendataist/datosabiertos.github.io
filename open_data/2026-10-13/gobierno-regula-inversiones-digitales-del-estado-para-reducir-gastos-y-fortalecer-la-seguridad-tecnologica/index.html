Gobierno regula inversiones digitales del Estado para reducir gastos y fortalecer la seguridad tecnológica

Gobierno regula inversiones digitales del Estado para reducir gastos y fortalecer la seguridad tecnológica

The recent regulation aims to coordinate and align the public sector’s digital investments with national transformation policies. This regulatory framework establishes centralized oversight by the Ministry of Public Administration to ensure spending efficiency, prevent duplication, and guarantee the quality and security of public services. Digital sovereignty is prioritized by prohibiting clauses that create exclusive commercial dependency or limit state control over its data assets. This measure promotes interoperability and information protection, mitigating risks associated with emerging technologies and ensuring that technology acquisitions are rational and sustainable in the long term. This initiative is fundamental to the principles of open data, as strengthening interoperability and state control over data facilitates structured and accessible data exchange. By reducing technical and commercial barriers, it lays the foundation for a more transparent government that enables efficient reuse of public information, maximizing its social and economic value.
