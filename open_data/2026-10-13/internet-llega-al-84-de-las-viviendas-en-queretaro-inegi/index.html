Internet llega al 84% de las viviendas en Querétaro: INEGI

Internet llega al 84% de las viviendas en Querétaro: INEGI

The study reveals a significant advance in digital connectivity in Querétaro, with a strong expansion of access to the internet and telephones. This increase underscores the importance of open data for monitoring the digital divide and designing policies that ensure technological inclusion. However, growth in the ownership of computers and automobiles has been moderate, while the use of motorcycles has increased. This diversity in technological equipment suggests changes in mobility habits and access to information in households. These results demonstrate how official surveys provide crucial evidence on digital transformation. By keeping these statistics as open data, transparency and critical analysis of equity in access to essential technological resources are facilitated.
