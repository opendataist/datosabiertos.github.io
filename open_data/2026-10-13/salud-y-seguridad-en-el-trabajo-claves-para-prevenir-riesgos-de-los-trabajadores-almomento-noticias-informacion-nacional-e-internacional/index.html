Salud y seguridad en el trabajo, claves para prevenir riesgos de los trabajadores - Almomento | Noticias, información nacional e internacional

Organizations face the challenge of identifying and mitigating psychosocial risks, which are essential for protecting workplace mental health. International standards and national regulations require robust mechanisms to detect vulnerabilities and provide appropriate support, thereby reducing costs associated with lost productivity. The relevance of these data lies in the transparency needed to foster healthy work environments. The existence of clear regulatory frameworks facilitates accountability and the implementation of preventive policies, aligning human resource management with global well-being standards. This approach is crucial for the open data movement, as it highlights the need for precise metrics on occupational health. By making these indicators public, it promotes a culture of corporate social responsibility and continuous improvement in the protection of human talent.
