Repunta 0.6% la actividad industrial en México durante agosto; construcción crece 7.9% anual

Repunta 0.6% la actividad industrial en México durante agosto; construcción crece 7.9% anual

Mexican industrial activity showed a marginal recovery in August, driven by the manufacturing sector and electricity generation. However, the overall performance reveals a fragmented economy, where mining and certain consumer goods industries continue to face significant contractions. This disparity indicates that growth is neither uniform nor sustained across all of the country’s productive subsystems. The seasonally adjusted figures suggest a slight monthly boost, but year-to-date totals keep manufacturing in negative territory. Although specific subsectors, such as electronic equipment manufacturing, have posted notable gains, these do not offset the weakness of traditional industries like textiles and food processing. This contrast reflects a vulnerable productive structure with significant competitiveness gaps among different industrial branches. This report is relevant to open data because it illustrates how official INEGI data can uncover hidden nuances within aggregate economic indicators. By making disaggregated details by activity and period publicly available, critical analysis of public policies and private investment is facilitated. The transparency of these essential data is key to understanding the true state of the economy beyond general indicators.
