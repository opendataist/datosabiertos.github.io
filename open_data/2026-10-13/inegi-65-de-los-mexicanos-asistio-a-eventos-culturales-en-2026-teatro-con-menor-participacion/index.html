Inegi: 65% de los mexicanos asistió a eventos culturales en 2026; teatro, con menor participación

Inegi: 65% de los mexicanos asistió a eventos culturales en 2026; teatro, con menor participación

The INEGI’s Module on Selected Cultural Events reveals that, although attendance at cultural events in Mexico experienced a slight increase in 2026, significant gaps persist among demographic groups. Participation is notably higher among young people, individuals with higher educational attainment, and men, while indigenous language speakers and those with less schooling show considerably lower attendance rates, underscoring structural inequalities in access to in-person cultural activities. Fairs and cinema stand out as the most popular in-person activities, while creative artistic participation, particularly in audiovisual media, remains the most common personal cultural practice. At the same time, digital cultural consumption continues to gain ground, consolidating itself as an essential channel, especially among young adults. This shift toward digital platforms indicates a change in consumption habits, where technology serves as a bridge to access cultural content that might otherwise be limited by geography or local offerings. This report is relevant to the open data movement because it illustrates how official statistics can be disaggregated to identify social inequalities and behavioral patterns. By providing data broken down by gender, age, language, and educational level, INEGI facilitates transparency and enables researchers, policymakers, and citizens to understand not only aggregate figures but also the structural dynamics affecting cultural participation, thereby fostering inclusive, evidence-based decision-making.

Source: eleconomista.com.mx
