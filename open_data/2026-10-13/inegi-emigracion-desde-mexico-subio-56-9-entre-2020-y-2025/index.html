Inegi: emigración desde México subió 56.9% entre 2020 y 2025

Inegi: emigración desde México subió 56.9% entre 2020 y 2025

The INEGI Intercensal Survey reveals a significant increase in Mexican emigration abroad, primarily driven by labor-related reasons. This migration flow is predominantly male and youthful, with a strong concentration among young adults seeking economic opportunities beyond national borders. The trend shows that, despite a decline in the return of migrants, the total volume of outflows has grown notably compared to previous periods. Demographic data highlight that those who emigrate typically have low levels of education, reflecting a specific pattern of labor mobility that impacts the country’s social structure. This report is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how detailed public statistics can inform migration and economic policies. Transparent access to these data enables the analysis of social patterns, the assessment of the impact of remittances, and the design of evidence-based strategies to manage international mobility and its national consequences.
