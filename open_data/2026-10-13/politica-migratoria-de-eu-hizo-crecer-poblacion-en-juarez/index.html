Política migratoria de EU hizo crecer población en Juárez

Política migratoria de EU hizo crecer población en Juárez

The increase in Ciudad Juárez’s foreign population directly reflects the impact of U.S. migration policies between 2020 and 2025, which left many people stranded at the border. This data highlights the historical difficulty in measuring transient flows and underscores how external measures have turned the region into a place of stable residence for those seeking refuge and social reintegration, beyond temporary transit. The relevance of this phenomenon is amplified by positioning Chihuahua as the third Mexican state with the largest migrant presence, consolidating itself as an attractive corridor and hub not only for U.S. citizens but also for people of diverse Latin American nationalities. This evidences a shift in migratory profiles and the strategic importance of the state for managing human mobility at the national level. This analysis is crucial for open data because it illustrates the need for precise and accessible metrics that capture the dynamic reality of human mobility. Official data enable civil society and governments to design inclusive public policies and evidence-based humanitarian responses, ensuring that statistical transparency serves to protect the rights of vulnerable populations and understand the complex contemporary border interactions.
