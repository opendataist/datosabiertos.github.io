Violencia y discriminación, delitos que más afectan a niñez en BCS

The article links demographic statistics on childhood in Baja California Sur with local and global institutional efforts to protect children's rights. It highlights the existence of state agencies working against violence and discrimination, aligning with the international mission to bring visibility to the challenges faced by girls. This is relevant to open data because it underscores the importance of having accurate and accessible statistical information to design effective public policies. Transparency in data about the child population enables monitoring progress toward equality and ensures that resources are allocated appropriately. The central conclusion is that the collection and dissemination of demographic data are essential tools for promoting inclusion and safety. By making the real situation of girls visible, it facilitates informed decision-making aimed at eradicating stereotypes and ensuring their right to a prosperous future free from barriers.
