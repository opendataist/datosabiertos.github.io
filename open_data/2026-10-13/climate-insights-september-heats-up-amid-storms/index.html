Climate Insights: September heats up amid storms

Climate Insights: September heats up amid storms

September 2026 shattered rainfall records across Hawaii, with the state on track to become one of the wettest years in a century. This extreme weather was driven by powerful hurricanes and El Niño, which heated Pacific waters and fueled stronger storms that dumped massive amounts of rain while simultaneously trapping heat and raising temperatures statewide. The article highlights the critical role of the Hawaii Climate Data Portal in tracking and explaining these environmental shifts. By providing authoritative monthly reports, the portal helps communities understand how climate phenomena like El Niño directly impact local conditions, offering essential insights for residents and researchers monitoring changing weather patterns. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how accessible, real-time climate information can empower public awareness. Understanding these records allows for better preparedness and policy decisions, showcasing how transparent sharing of scientific data supports resilience against increasingly frequent extreme weather events in vulnerable regions.
