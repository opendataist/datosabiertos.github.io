Reporta INEGI sorpresivo avance de 0.4% de la economía en enero

Reporta INEGI sorpresivo avance de 0.4% de la economía en enero

The INEGI’s updated IOAE reveals a modest economic rebound, with January showing positive growth and December revised from contraction to slight expansion. This data indicates that monthly activity has returned to 2018 averages, signaling renewed resilience despite lingering inflationary pressures and global economic headwinds. Analysts caution that this recovery does not guarantee sustained momentum. Predictions suggest potential deceleration in the first quarter due to anticipated stagnation in the early months, highlighting the fragility of the current economic trajectory against complex external factors. This update is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates the value of timely, transparent statistical revisions. By making such agile indicators publicly available, institutions empower analysts to monitor real-time economic shifts, fostering greater accountability and informed decision-making based on accessible, accurate macroeconomic metrics.
