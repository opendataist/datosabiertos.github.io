The National Electoral Institute (INE) allows citizens to opt out of receiving electoral propaganda, ensuring their privacy is respected during elections. This mechanism highlights the importance of transparent data access and user control over personal information. It underscores the relevance of open data principles in safeguarding individual rights while facilitating democratic processes. This article demonstrates how open data systems can empower citizens by providing tools to manage their data preferences, reinforcing trust in public institutions.
