Se dispara la compraventa de viviendas en la Comunitat Valenciana: el 2022 fue el mejor año desde 2007

The data reveals a significant recovery in the Valencian Community’s housing market in 2022, with transactions reaching their highest levels since the 2007 peak. This surge indicates a robust return to pre-pandemic norms, although the year concluded with a notable deceleration, ending a long streak of monthly increases. The market dynamic was primarily driven by the resale sector, which dominated total operations, while new construction remained a minor contributor. This disparity highlights a continued preference for existing inventory over new developments, suggesting that the housing supply chain faces structural constraints or that buyers prioritize immediate availability over future projects. This dataset is crucial for open data initiatives as it demonstrates how standardized statistical information can reveal complex economic shifts, such as the divergence between second-hand and new housing markets. By making such granular records accessible, policymakers and researchers can better understand regional economic resilience and the specific factors influencing real estate trends beyond aggregate national figures.
