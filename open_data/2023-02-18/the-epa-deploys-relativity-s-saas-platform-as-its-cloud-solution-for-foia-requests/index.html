The EPA Deploys Relativity's SaaS Platform as its Cloud Solution for FOIA Requests

The Environmental Protection Agency has adopted RelativityOne Government, a secure cloud platform, to manage Freedom of Information Act requests. This strategic shift moves the agency away from legacy systems toward a scalable solution that integrates artificial intelligence for faster data analysis. By centralizing these operations, the EPA aims to improve efficiency and accuracy while maintaining strict security standards required for public sector data. A crucial clarification emerged regarding the scope of this deployment. While the platform supports FOIA responses, it operates independently from the EPA’s existing FOIAonline portal and National FOIA Office. Furthermore, the agency continues to use RelativityOne for litigation and congressional matters separately from this specific FOIA migration. Understanding this distinction is vital for accurately assessing how government agencies integrate new technologies across different operational silos. This case is highly relevant to open data discussions as it demonstrates how government transparency mechanisms like FOIA are increasingly dependent on sophisticated data processing tools. The ability to quickly identify and redact sensitive information in vast datasets directly impacts how quickly and accurately public records are released. It highlights the critical intersection between cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and the practical execution of government transparency mandates, showing that efficient open data delivery relies on robust, secure technological foundations.
