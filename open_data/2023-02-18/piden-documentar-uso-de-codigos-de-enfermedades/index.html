Piden documentar uso de códigos de enfermedades

Piden documentar uso de códigos de enfermedades

The National Institute for Transparency, Access to Information and Personal Data Protection (INAI) ordered the National Institute of Respiratory Diseases (INER) to provide documents that demonstrate the use of international disease classifications to record post-COVID-19 conditions. This resolution highlights transparency as a vital tool for measuring and evaluating the effectiveness of health institutions in response to the crisis, ensuring accurate data on public health care and control in Mexico. The decision underscores the importance of international standards for systematically analyzing morbidity and mortality, enabling rigorous comparisons of institutional performance outcomes. By confirming that the institute maintains specific records, it validates the importance of documenting these processes using standardized criteria that facilitate a detailed understanding of persistent health conditions. This case is crucial for the open data movement, as it sets a precedent by requiring the proactive publication of structured health information. By ensuring access to these records, it promotes accountability and enables independent research, demonstrating how mandatory transparency strengthens public trust and improves data governance in the health sector.
