Inteligencia Artificial: Escritores de Austria piden bloquear ChatGPT hasta aclarar derechos de autor | Emol.com

Austrian writers are calling for an immediate halt to AI text generation until copyright laws are updated, arguing that current tools infringe on intellectual property by mimicking and copying existing styles. This highlights a critical tension between rapid AI innovation and the protection of creative rights, a core concern for open data ethics regarding data sourcing and attribution. The call for strict national regulation underscores the urgency of aligning technological development with legal frameworks that protect authors. This situation illustrates how open data principles must navigate the complex intersection of public information access and individual creator rights. Ultimately, this article is relevant to open data because it challenges how training data is used. It emphasizes the necessity of transparent, legally compliant data practices to ensure that open information systems respect the origins and rights of the underlying creative works.
