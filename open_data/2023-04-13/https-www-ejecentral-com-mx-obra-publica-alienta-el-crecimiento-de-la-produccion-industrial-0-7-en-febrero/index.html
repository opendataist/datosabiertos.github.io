Mexican industrial activity shows strong positive trends, with overall growth reaching its highest level since 2019. This resurgence suggests a favorable economic trajectory driven by external demand and strategic investment shifts toward the region. Despite this momentum, the manufacturing sector experienced a slight contraction. This dip highlights short-term vulnerabilities linked to export moderation and slower recovery patterns in key trading partners like China. This data is relevant to open data as it illustrates how high-quality, freely accessible statistical indices enable transparent monitoring of macroeconomic health. Reliable public datasets empower stakeholders to track nearshoring impacts and validate policy decisions through evidence-based analysis.
