Senado debe nombrar comisionados faltantes

Senado debe nombrar comisionados faltantes

The president of the INAI insists that the Senate must appoint the missing commissioners, framing this as a constitutional obligation rather than a choice, backed by Supreme Court rulings. This political delay threatens the institution's independence, which was established as a fundamental social achievement rather than a governmental concession. This situation is highly relevant to open data because the INAI safeguards citizens' rights to access public information and protect personal data. Without a full commission, the regulatory body cannot effectively enforce transparency or defend these rights, undermining democratic accountability and public trust in government information systems.
