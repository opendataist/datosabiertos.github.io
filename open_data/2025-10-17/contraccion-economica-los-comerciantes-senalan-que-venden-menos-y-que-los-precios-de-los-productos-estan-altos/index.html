Contracción económica: Los comerciantes señalan que venden menos y que los precios de los productos están altos

The national economy has undergone a significant contraction, marking the first decline in nearly four decades. This recession is exacerbated by soaring inflation, creating a "stagflation" scenario in which prices for essential goods such as vegetables and meat have nearly doubled. Consumers are feeling this impact acutely, forcing households to cut back on spending or switch to cheaper alternatives as their purchasing power drastically diminishes. This analysis is relevant to open data because reliable statistical reporting from institutions such as the INE is crucial for validating these economic hardships. Open data initiatives ensure that metrics on inflation and production are accessible, enabling researchers and citizens to verify official narratives against real-world observations in local markets. Without transparent, freely available data, it is difficult to assess the true extent of the crisis or hold authorities accountable for the widespread increase in living costs. Ultimately, the disconnect between official economic indicators and the lived reality of merchants and families underscores the need for better data integration. Open data frameworks can bridge this gap by combining macroeconomic statistics with granular market information. This transparency empowers stakeholders to understand systemic issues such as fuel shortages and supply chain disruptions, fostering informed public debate and potential policy improvements.
