FACUA advierte de un fallo de seguridad que ha dejado al descubierto datos personales de clientes de Mango

FACUA advierte de un fallo de seguridad que ha dejado al descubierto datos personales de clientes de Mango

Mango confirms a cyberattack that exposed limited personal contact data, including names and email addresses, while affirming that its financial and critical systems remain secure. The company has notified the relevant authorities and established communication channels for affected customers to address their concerns. FACUA warns clients to remain vigilant against potential phishing attempts, as criminals may exploit the leaked information for fraudulent purposes. This incident highlights the critical importance of transparency and robust data protection measures. This case is relevant to open data, as it illustrates the real-world risks associated with data breaches and underscores the necessity for organizations to handle personal information responsibly. Such events emphasize the need for strict adherence to privacy regulations to maintain public trust and protect consumer rights in the digital age.
