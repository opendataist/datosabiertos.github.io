Open-Source AI Tool by Yandex Detects Signs of Infant Cerebral Palsy With Over 90% Accuracy

Open-Source AI Tool by Yandex Detects Signs of Infant Cerebral Palsy With Over 90% Accuracy

Yandex B2B Tech, in collaboration with academic institutions, has developed the world’s first AI solution for assessing brain development in infants under twelve months. This neural network automates MRI analysis, distinguishing between gray and white brain matter with over ninety percent accuracy. By reducing processing time from several days to mere minutes, the tool enables the early detection of cerebral palsy and other central nervous system disorders, facilitating more effective and timely rehabilitation planning for affected children. The development of this technology addresses a critical global health challenge, particularly in India, where cerebral palsy is a leading cause of childhood disability. Traditional diagnosis is hindered by the rapid development of infant brains and the scarcity of annotated data required for training accurate models. To overcome these obstacles, researchers worked closely with medical experts to create necessary annotations and design a specialized neural network. This collaborative effort resulted in a robust decision-support tool that enhances diagnostic objectivity and helps less-experienced specialists interpret complex scans. This solution is highly relevant to the open data movement because the neural network code is freely available on GitHub. By providing open-source access, Yandex allows Indian healthcare and clinical research institutions to integrate the tool into their existing systems without financial barriers. This openness democratizes access to advanced diagnostic technology, potentially improving clinical throughput and allowing radiologists to focus on complex cases. Ultimately, making such sophisticated AI tools publicly accessible supports global efforts to standardize and accelerate early intervention for neurodevelopmental disorders.
