Emiten ficha de búsqueda de encuestador del Inegi desaparecido en Chihuahua - La Jornada San Luis

Emiten ficha de búsqueda de encuestador del Inegi desaparecido en Chihuahua - La Jornada San Luis

The disappearance of an INEGI surveyor in Chihuahua underscores the critical vulnerability of field workers who collect essential public data in remote areas. This incident highlights that open data initiatives rely not only on digital infrastructure but also on the physical safety of individuals conducting censuses and surveys in challenging terrain. The involvement of multiple state agencies and community volunteers reveals how local crises intersect with national statistical operations. When data collectors vanish, it disrupts the continuity of official records, emphasizing that the reliability of open government information is deeply tied to the well-being and security of those on the ground gathering these vital statistics. The relevance to open data lies in the recognition that transparency requires safe working conditions for data producers. Without robust support systems for field agents, the integrity and completeness of public datasets are compromised, making workforce protection a foundational element for trustworthy open data ecosystems.
