Los concursos de acreedores mantienen su tendencia al alza y crecen un 19% en el segundo trimestre, según el CGPJ

Los concursos de acreedores mantienen su tendencia al alza y crecen un 19% en el segundo trimestre, según el CGPJ

The General Council of the Judiciary reports a sustained upward trend in bankruptcy proceedings initiated in the third quarter of 2020, primarily driven by a significant surge in personal insolvency cases filed by non-business individuals. This specific category saw a notable increase compared to the previous year, contrasting sharply with the declining numbers observed in corporate bankruptcies and those filed by business-owning individuals. This divergence highlights a shift in the economic pressures facing households versus commercial entities during this period. Regionally, Catalonia remains the epicenter of legal insolvency activity, leading both in total cases and within specific subcategories, followed by Andalusia, the Valencian Community, and Madrid. The data indicates that while corporate restructuring efforts have slowed, with fewer cases reaching formal agreement or liquidation phases compared to prior years, the overall volume of judicial interventions in insolvency matters continues to rise. This suggests a complex landscape where individual financial distress is outweighing corporate legal challenges in terms of case volume growth. This information is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how transparent, publicly accessible judicial statistics enable the identification of macroeconomic trends and regional disparities. By making such granular data available, researchers and policymakers can analyze the real-time impact of economic policies on both personal and corporate sectors, fostering evidence-based decision-making and enhancing public accountability in legal and financial systems.
