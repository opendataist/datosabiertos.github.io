Statement from Greenwich BOE Republican Caucus on Preliminary FOI Commission Ruling

Statement from Greenwich BOE Republican Caucus on Preliminary FOI Commission Ruling

A preliminary FOI Commission ruling determined that Greenwich Board of Education Democrats violated open records laws by holding an unlawful emergency meeting to appoint Jen Behette, a candidate rejected by actual Republican members. This decision highlights critical failures in transparency and procedural integrity within local governance. The ruling underscores the necessity of adhering to statutory requirements during political appointments, preventing partisan manipulation of public institutions. By bypassing established protocols and excluding legitimate stakeholders, the Board compromised the legal standards required for fair decision-making processes. This case is relevant to open data and open government advocates as it demonstrates how secrecy and procedural violations erode public trust. Upholding transparency ensures that civic decisions remain accountable, preserving the integrity of democratic systems against political bias and unauthorized actions.
