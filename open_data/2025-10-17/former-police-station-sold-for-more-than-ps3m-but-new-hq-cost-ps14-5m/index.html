Former police station sold for more than £3m, but new HQ cost £14.5m

Dorset Police replaced an obsolete, deteriorating headquarters with a modern facility, prioritizing long-term efficiency over minor short-term savings. This strategic shift aims to drastically reduce maintenance costs and enhance operational sustainability, ensuring resources are better allocated to frontline policing rather than aging infrastructure. The new site was funded through independent sources, avoiding any impact on local council taxes or staffing levels. By generating substantial long-term value and supporting the local economy during construction, the project demonstrates how prudent estate management can strengthen community trust and financial stability without burdening the public. This decision highlights the critical importance of transparent data in public sector asset management. Open data initiatives allow citizens to scrutinize such significant financial decisions, verifying claims of cost-effectiveness and ensuring accountability. Transparent reporting on housing and infrastructure investments builds confidence that police funding truly serves community safety and operational excellence.
