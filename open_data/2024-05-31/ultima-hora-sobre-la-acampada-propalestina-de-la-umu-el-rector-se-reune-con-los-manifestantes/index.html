Última hora sobre la acampada propalestina de la UMU, el rector se reúne con los manifestantes

Última hora sobre la acampada propalestina de la UMU, el rector se reúne con los manifestantes

The rector of the University of Murcia met with student activists to address their demands for humanitarian solidarity with Palestine. He reaffirmed the institution’s condemnation of terrorism and violence, aligning with broader statements from Spanish universities. Crucially, the rector pledged to institutionalize support for Palestinian academic mobility and resource sharing, along with a commitment to refrain from collaborating with entities that violate international humanitarian law. This development underscores the growing intersection between campus activism and institutional policy on ethical partnerships. It demonstrates how student advocacy can directly influence administrative decisions, resulting in formal commitments that prioritize human rights and peace over conventional academic exchanges. This case is relevant to open data because the university emphasized transparency by providing students access to its full list of corporate relationships through a public transparency portal. This illustrates how open data initiatives can empower citizen engagement and ensure that institutional partnerships remain accountable and visible to the public, fostering trust through accessible information.
