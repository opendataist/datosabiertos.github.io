The article reveals that administrative activities following the 2023 Pan American Games include significant salary increases for directors, justified by staff reductions and increased responsibilities. These raises, approved in January, continue despite the conclusion of the competitions, drawing surprise from affiliated sports institutions that were unaware of the changes. This situation highlights a governance disconnect: the Ministry of Sport claims that salary decisions fall solely within the executive director’s prerogative and are unrelated to the minister’s oversight. Meanwhile, the organizing corporation faces temporary suspension from the National Sports Institute due to unverified accounts, although it asserts that corrective measures are underway. This case is crucial for open data, as it demonstrates the necessity of transparency portals to detect and scrutinize the use of public funds. Access to such financial disclosures enables civil society to verify claims regarding administrative restructuring and ensure accountability in the management of substantial public resources during organizational transitions.
