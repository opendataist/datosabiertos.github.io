Dane hace reconocimiento a la Superfinanciera por cálculo de tasas

The Superfinancial Superintendence of Colombia obtained statistical quality certification for key interest rate data from the National Administrative Department of Statistics. This validation ensures that these financial indicators meet rigorous national standards for accuracy and reliability. The recognition highlights the institution’s commitment to producing high-quality official statistics within the National Statistical System. It builds on previous certifications, demonstrating sustained compliance with technical standards for data production and dissemination processes. This achievement is highly relevant to open data, as it validates the trustworthiness of public financial datasets. Certified data enhances transparency and encourages broader reuse by researchers and citizens, fostering a more robust and credible ecosystem for open economic information.
