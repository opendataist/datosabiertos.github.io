Mexican president backs plan to ditch transparency institute

Mexican president backs plan to ditch transparency institute

The Mexican president seeks to dismantle the national transparency institute, viewing it as hostile to his administration. This move threatens to eliminate a key autonomous watchdog responsible for enforcing the right to information. Such actions directly undermine open data principles by removing critical mechanisms for public accountability and government transparency.
