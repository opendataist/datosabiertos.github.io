Uttarakhand's liquor revenue increases over 22 times in 22 years, reveals RTI - ET Retail

The Uttarakhand tax department’s revenue from liquor sales has surged dramatically over two decades, rising more than twentyfold from 2001 to 2023. This persistent upward trend highlights the state’s increasing financial reliance on alcohol taxes, even maintaining growth during pandemic-related challenges. Such significant escalation suggests that liquor excise duties have become a crucial, expanding pillar of the regional fiscal framework. This discovery underscores the critical role of transparency in tracking public finances and government revenue streams. It demonstrates how Right to Information requests can expose long-term fiscal patterns that might otherwise remain obscured from public scrutiny. By accessing specific data points, civil society can verify government claims and understand the scale of specific tax collections over time. Open data initiatives are essential for empowering citizens to hold authorities accountable for revenue generation practices. When detailed fiscal information is made accessible, it enables activists to analyze trends and advocate for policy adjustments if necessary. Ultimately, this case illustrates how transparent data sharing fosters informed public discourse and strengthens democratic oversight of state financial operations.
