Over 60,000 industries operational in Nepal, employing about 400,000 people

The National Industrial Survey reveals that Nepal’s industrial sector is dominated by small enterprises, with the majority lacking access to formal financing. This reliance on self-funding highlights significant barriers to capital expansion, suggesting that limited credit availability constrains the growth potential of most business entities. Consequently, the sector’s overall operational capacity remains underutilized, indicating that financial accessibility is a critical bottleneck for economic development and industrial scaling. Employment figures show that the industry supports hundreds of thousands of workers, yet performance is severely hampered by external shocks and resource constraints. The pandemic exacerbated existing vulnerabilities, causing widespread closures and job losses, which underscores the fragility of the labor market. The disparity in earnings across different sub-sectors further illustrates an uneven distribution of economic benefits, with some specialized fields outperforming the broader industrial average. This data is vital for open data initiatives as it provides a transparent baseline for policy formulation and investment analysis. By making detailed provincial and sectoral statistics publicly available, stakeholders can identify regional inequalities and sector-specific challenges. Such transparency encourages evidence-based decision-making, allowing governments and investors to target interventions effectively, thereby fostering a more resilient and equitable industrial ecosystem.
