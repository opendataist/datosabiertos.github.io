En Paysandú hay 5.797 empresas activas y 2.033 inactivas, según INE

En Paysandú hay 5.797 empresas activas y 2.033 inactivas, según INE

The report highlights a positive trend in Uruguayan business activity, characterized by a net increase in active companies and job creation. This growth is driven by a significant rise in new business formations that outpaced closures, suggesting a resilient economic environment where entrepreneurship is thriving despite higher mortality rates compared to previous years. A specific focus on the services sector reveals its dominant role in the national economy, accounting for the majority of both enterprises and employment. This sector has become a key engine for development, heavily reliant on knowledge-intensive technologies and skilled labor. Notably, there is a growing gender parity in employment within this field, indicating a gradual shift toward more inclusive labor markets that align with regional trends. This data is relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the importance of accessible, granular statistical information for understanding economic dynamics. By making detailed demographics and sector-specific analyses publicly available, institutions enable researchers and policymakers to identify growth patterns, such as the surge in information services or regional concentrations, facilitating evidence-based decision-making and promoting transparency in economic governance.
