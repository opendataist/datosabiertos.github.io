Cómo proteger a los creadores frente al entrenamiento de la IA

Cómo proteger a los creadores frente al entrenamiento de la IA

The Spanish Ministry of Culture proposes regulating the use of copyrighted works for artificial intelligence training through extended collective licensing. This mechanism enables AI providers to access creative repertoires on a large scale through a single negotiation, ensuring legal certainty and equitable remuneration for authors, who retain the right to exclude their works if they so choose. Intellectual property experts emphasize that this approach balances technological innovation with the protection of creativity, simplifying rights management in the face of the complexity involved in training algorithms. While the creation of a licensing market and the reduction of transaction costs are valued, concerns remain regarding transparency, the technological adequacy of collective management organizations, and the risk of inadequate protection for less-informed creators. The relevance to open data lies in how Spain addresses the tension between mass access to data for technological development and the sovereignty of copyright. The article illustrates the search for legal frameworks that facilitate data exploitation without sacrificing ethics or economic compensation—a critical challenge for the sustainability of open data ecosystems and responsible artificial intelligence on a global scale.
