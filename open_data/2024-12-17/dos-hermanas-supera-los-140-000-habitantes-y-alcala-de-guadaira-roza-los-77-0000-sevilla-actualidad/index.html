Dos Hermanas supera los 140.000 habitantes y Alcalá de Guadaíra roza los 77.0000 | Sevilla Actualidad

Dos Hermanas supera los 140.000 habitantes y Alcalá de Guadaíra roza los 77.0000 | Sevilla Actualidad

The latest municipal census data from the National Statistics Institute reveals a clear demographic shift within the province of Seville. While major municipalities such as Dos Hermanas and the city of Seville have experienced consistent population growth, contributing to an overall increase in the province, Seville has lost its position as Spain’s fourth most populous province to Alicante. This transition highlights evolving regional dynamics, in which peripheral areas are gaining ground even as the central city strives to reverse years of decline and reclaim its historical population peak. These findings are highly relevant to open data initiatives, as they demonstrate the critical value of standardized, publicly accessible demographic records for real-time societal analysis. By making this granular data available, researchers and policymakers can move beyond static statistics to understand the fluid nature of urbanization. The transparency of such datasets enables the identification of precise growth trends across different administrative levels, fostering evidence-based decision-making that responds to current rather than historical realities. Ultimately, the article underscores how open statistical infrastructure supports democratic accountability and urban planning. The ability to track these subtle shifts empowers communities to anticipate service demands and address the challenges of municipal expansion. When citizens and institutions can freely access and interpret this information, it strengthens the link between government data and public interest, ensuring that development strategies are grounded in accurate, timely, and comprehensive demographic evidence.
