Ejecutivo Estatal fortalece modelo de Gestión de Gobierno Abierto en SLP | Revista Punto de Vista

Ejecutivo Estatal fortalece modelo de Gestión de Gobierno Abierto en SLP | Revista Punto de Vista

The government of the state of San Luis Potosí has accelerated its Open Government Management Model to ensure transparency and active societal participation in policy-making. This approach integrates citizens directly into the evaluation and creation of public policies, moving beyond passive observation to meaningful collaboration. By empowering civil society with voting rights in advisory committees and engaging thousands through educational workshops, the administration fosters a more accountable relationship between the state and the people. This shift aims to place citizens at the center of responsible policy construction, ensuring that government actions reflect societal needs and values. This initiative is crucial for open data as it demonstrates how transparency mechanisms can evolve into participatory governance. It highlights the importance of making government processes accessible and inclusive, thereby strengthening trust in public institutions and encouraging broader civic engagement through data-driven dialogue.
