Peso inicia la semana con el ‘pie izquierdo’: ¿Cuánto pierde frente al dólar? Así cotiza este lunes

The Mexican peso remained stable, with minimal fluctuations, driven by investor caution ahead of the anticipated monetary policy announcement by the US Federal Reserve. This sentiment reflects a broader market trend in which regional currencies experienced slight depreciation against the dollar, underscoring the significant influence of US economic decisions on Latin American financial stability. This situation is relevant to open data because transparent, real-time exchange rate and bond yield data enable analysts to accurately predict market reactions. Publicly accessible financial indicators allow for the immediate assessment of geopolitical risks, fostering greater accountability and informed decision-making within global monetary systems.
