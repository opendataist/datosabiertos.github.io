Protege tu seguridad digital: consejos para evitar ataques cibernéticos durante la temporada navideña | RPP Noticias

Protege tu seguridad digital: consejos para evitar ataques cibernéticos durante la temporada navideña | RPP Noticias

The holiday season has solidified itself as a critical period for cybersecurity, characterized by a significant increase in digital criminal activity. Specialists warn that cybercriminals exploit the surge in online shopping and widespread distraction to launch more sophisticated attacks, putting both individual consumers and micro-entrepreneurs at risk. The urgency lies not only in the festive period itself but also in the inherent vulnerability of those who do not maintain basic digital hygiene practices under the pressure of rapid transactions. The primary attack vectors are phishing targeting the general public and ransomware aimed at small businesses. Users are often deceived by fraudulent communications that mimic well-known brands, leading to the theft of banking credentials or the installation of malware. Meanwhile, micro-enterprises face data ransomware threats due to a lack of software updates and adequate protection measures, risks that can lead to bankruptcy or a complete disruption of their business operations. This article is relevant to open data because transparency in the disclosure of cybersecurity incidents and fraud statistics provides essential data for public research. By understanding the nature and scope of these threats, civil society and regulatory bodies can demand better data protection policies. Furthermore, the visibility of these vulnerabilities fosters a culture of openness regarding how personal and commercial information is protected (or fails to be protected), which is key to designing more robust and accessible security standards for everyone.
