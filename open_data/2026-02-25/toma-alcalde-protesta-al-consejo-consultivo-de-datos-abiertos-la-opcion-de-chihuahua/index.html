Toma Alcalde protesta al Consejo Consultivo de Datos Abiertos | La Opción de Chihuahua

Chihuahua inaugurates its Open Data Advisory Council, emphasizing integrity and autonomy in public service. This initiative strengthens government transparency by institutionalizing collaborative oversight of open data practices. It is relevant to open data as it establishes a dedicated governance structure to ensure accountability and improve public access to municipal information.
