Portal de Transparencia reporta 52 915 visitas

The Transparency Portal of the Ministry of Public Finance enables citizens to access centralized financial information from the State, strengthening oversight and accountability. This mechanism facilitates social auditing by integrating data from various public entities, ensuring greater clarity in the use of funds. High digital interaction, primarily from computers, highlights the crucial role of information technologies in modernizing the State. By promoting social engagement and active participation, this digital platform transforms public administration, encouraging transparency and citizen scrutiny over the use of tax resources. This initiative is relevant to open data because it demonstrates how the structured publication of government data drives accountability. By providing accessible and centralized information, it empowers society to monitor public management, upholding transparency principles and facilitating independent oversight by citizens.
