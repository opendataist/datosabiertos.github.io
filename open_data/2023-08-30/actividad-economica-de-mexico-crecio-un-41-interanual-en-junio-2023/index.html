Actividad económica de México creció un 4,1% interanual en junio 2023

Actividad económica de México creció un 4,1% interanual en junio 2023

Mexico’s economic activity in June advanced significantly, driven by broad-based growth across the primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors. This expansion underscores the resilience and dynamic recovery of the country’s industrial and service capacities, signaling positive short-term economic trends despite minor fluctuations in specific areas such as agriculture. These figures are closely linked to the updated GDP estimates, which show a slight downward revision for the second quarter. The data illustrates the broader post-pandemic trajectory, contrasting recent recoveries with the severe contractions experienced in previous years, while still aligning with government expectations for sustained annual growth above three percent. This article is relevant to open data because it relies on public statistics from the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). The Industrial Activity General Index (IGAE) and GDP indicators serve as critical open datasets for analysts, researchers, and policymakers to monitor economic health, validate forecasts, and support evidence-based decision-making regarding trade, investment, and policy adjustments in the region.
