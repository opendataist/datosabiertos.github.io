Transparencia para el Pueblo de Tamaulipas capacita en Datos Personales

Transparencia para el Pueblo de Tamaulipas capacita en Datos Personales

Public servants in Tamaulipas received training on personal data protection to ensure responsible information handling. This initiative strengthens the legal framework governing public institutions, emphasizing transparency principles and user rights such as access and rectification. By reinforcing these standards, the program aims to cultivate a culture of compliance among government officials. This effort ensures that public entities manage sensitive data ethically, balancing transparency with individual privacy protections. This article highlights the critical intersection of open data and privacy laws. It demonstrates that true openness requires robust safeguards for personal information, ensuring that transparency initiatives do not compromise citizen rights but rather enhance trust in public institutions.
