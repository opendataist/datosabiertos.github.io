Mexicanos, menos satisfechos con su seguridad

Mexican adults report high overall life satisfaction, yet significant dissatisfaction persists regarding public safety and their perception of the country and their cities. These findings from INEGI highlight critical gaps between general well-being and specific socio-political concerns, indicating that safety remains a primary source of urban discontent despite slight improvements. This data is highly relevant to open data initiatives because it underscores the value of transparent, accessible government statistics in monitoring societal well-being. By making these indicators public, citizens and policymakers can better identify urgent areas needing intervention, fostering accountability and enabling evidence-based decisions that address the root causes of public dissatisfaction. Ultimately, while personal and material aspects receive favorable ratings, the low scores in security and civic perception reveal systemic challenges. Open data allows for continuous tracking of these trends, ensuring that development strategies prioritize the restoration of trust and safety, which are fundamental to improving the quality of life for Mexican citizens.
