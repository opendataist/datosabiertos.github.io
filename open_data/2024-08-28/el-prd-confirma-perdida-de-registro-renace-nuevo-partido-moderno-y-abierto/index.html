El PRD confirma pérdida de registro; renace nuevo partido moderno y abierto

The PRD lost its national registration, marking the end of a historic democratic contribution. This shift demands a new, modern political project rather than simply recreating the old party structure. The relevance of open data lies in tracking how electoral thresholds redefine party legitimacy and force organizational reinvention. This case illustrates the critical need for transparent, accessible electoral data. It highlights how public information enables citizens to understand political realignments and accountability during democratic transitions.
