The 2024 edition of Hong Kong’s key statistics highlights a widening gender disparity in population alongside rising life expectancies for both sexes. These demographic shifts underscore the importance of accessible, granular data for tracking public health trends and social equity over time. Cancer remains the leading cause of death, with distinct prevalence patterns between genders. Accurate, open statistical reporting enables researchers to identify these health disparities and informs targeted medical interventions and policy decisions effectively. This data release exemplifies the value of transparent, routine statistical publication. By making detailed demographic and health metrics publicly available, authorities support evidence-based planning and foster greater societal understanding of evolving community needs.
