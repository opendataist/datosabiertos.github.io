The Enameh survey aims to generate essential statistical data on informal economic activities within households. This information is crucial for formulating, monitoring, and evaluating public policies and private actions targeting the microenterprise sector. By capturing details on sales, financing, and technology use, the initiative seeks to accurately reflect the reality of small businesses for better decision-making. To ensure trust and data integrity, the survey adheres to strict statistical confidentiality laws. All individual information is protected and never shared with judicial or financial authorities, ensuring that responses are used exclusively for official statistics. This legal framework guarantees privacy, encouraging participation without fear of personal data misuse or exposure. This article is highly relevant to open data as it demonstrates how rigorous ethical standards and transparency mechanisms, such as verifiable enumerator identities, underpin high-quality public statistics. The INEC’s commitment to security and accessibility builds the necessary public trust for large-scale data collection. Ultimately, this process ensures that the resulting aggregated datasets are reliable and available for analyzing economic trends, fostering a transparent environment for data-driven governance and research.
