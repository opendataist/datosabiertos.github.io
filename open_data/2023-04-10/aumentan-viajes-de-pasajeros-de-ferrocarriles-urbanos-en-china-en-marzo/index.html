Aumentan viajes de pasajeros de ferrocarriles urbanos en China en marzo

Urban rail passenger trips surged in China, signaling a strong recovery in city mobility and public transit usage. The expansion of rail networks underscores significant infrastructure growth, supporting efficient urban commuting systems for millions of residents. This data constitutes a valuable open dataset for analyzing urban mobility trends, transportation demand, and the effectiveness of public transport policies.
