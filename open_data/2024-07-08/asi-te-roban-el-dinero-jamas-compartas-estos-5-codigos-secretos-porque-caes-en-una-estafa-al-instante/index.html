¡Así te roban el dinero! Jamás compartas estos 5 códigos secretos porque caes en una estafa al instante

¡Así te roban el dinero! Jamás compartas estos 5 códigos secretos porque caes en una estafa al instante

BBVA México warns its clients about common cybercriminal tactics, including malicious emails, fraudulent phone calls, alarming SMS messages, and fake websites designed to steal personal and financial information. These scams often involve impersonating the bank to gain trust, then requesting sensitive data or remote access to devices, which can lead to immediate fraud and financial loss. To prevent such incidents, the bank advises never sharing five critical pieces of information: PINs, security tokens, CVV numbers, passwords, and card numbers, as well as withdrawal passwords. It emphasizes that the bank will never contact clients via SMS, WhatsApp, or phone calls to request such confidential information or to resolve account issues. This article is relevant to open data because it highlights the critical importance of data protection and privacy in an era where digital footprints are easily exploitable. It underscores that once personal data is exposed, it can be used maliciously, reinforcing the need for strict security protocols and public awareness to safeguard individual privacy. By educating users on what not to share, it promotes a culture of responsible data handling, which is essential for maintaining trust in digital financial systems and protecting against identity theft and fraud.
