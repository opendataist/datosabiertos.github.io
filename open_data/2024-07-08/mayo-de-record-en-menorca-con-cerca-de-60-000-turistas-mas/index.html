Mayo de récord en Menorca con cerca de 60.000 turistas más

The resurgence of packaged tourism has driven significant tourist arrivals in May, particularly among foreign visitors. This shift highlights a structural change in travel preferences, returning to pre-pandemic models where organized tours surpass independent bookings. Despite record total spending, the average daily expenditure per traveler shows only modest growth. This divergence reveals underlying economic pressures, as national tourists exhibit reduced purchasing power and shorter stays, indicating that volume growth does not necessarily translate to proportional income gains for all market segments. This article matters to open data because it underscores the critical importance of granular, disaggregated statistics. Relying solely on aggregate figures can mask vital socioeconomic disparities. High-quality, accessible data is essential to distinguish between broad sector growth and specific consumer vulnerabilities, enabling more accurate policy and business decisions.
