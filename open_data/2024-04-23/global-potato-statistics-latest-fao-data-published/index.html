Global potato statistics: Latest FAO data published

The FAOSTAT database serves as a vital resource for global agricultural transparency, offering accessible and standardized data on major crop productions. By centralizing statistics from numerous countries, it enables researchers and policymakers to analyze worldwide production trends without needing to navigate disparate national reporting systems. This centralized approach significantly enhances the accessibility of agricultural intelligence for the open data community. Global potato production is heavily concentrated in a few leading nations, with China and India dominating output volumes. This concentration highlights specific regional strengths and dependencies within the global food supply chain. Understanding these distribution patterns allows for better assessment of food security risks and market dynamics, providing a clear picture of where critical resources are generated on a planetary scale. Although official national figures may vary slightly due to differing reporting standards, FAO data offers a sufficiently accurate baseline for most analytical purposes. The database’s continuous updates, including recent additions for 2023, ensure that this open data remains current and relevant. Consequently, it supports evidence-based decision-making by providing a reliable, universally available foundation for tracking agricultural shifts and supporting sustainable development goals.
