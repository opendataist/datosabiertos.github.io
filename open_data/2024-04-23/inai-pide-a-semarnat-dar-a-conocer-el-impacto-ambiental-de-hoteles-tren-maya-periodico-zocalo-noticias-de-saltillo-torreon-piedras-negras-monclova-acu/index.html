INAI pide a Semarnat dar a conocer el impacto ambiental de Hoteles Tren Maya | Periódico Zócalo | Noticias de Saltillo, Torreón, Piedras Negras, Monclova, Acuña

The National Institute for Transparency demands that the Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources (Semarnat) fully disclose the environmental impact assessments for six Tren Maya hotels. These documents are crucial for evaluating the projects' ecological footprint, particularly regarding deforestation and damage to cenotes. Transparent access enables society to verify whether mitigation measures are properly implemented and ensures legal compliance in sensitive areas. The case highlights a significant failure in information access, as Semarnat provided incomplete data and failed to address requests for all six locations. This inconsistency prevents effective public oversight and raises serious concerns about transparency in major infrastructure projects. This case is vital for open data because it underscores the necessity of accessible, complete, and structured public information. Without reliable data, citizens cannot effectively monitor government actions or environmental protections. Ensuring data availability is essential for accountability, allowing communities to audit projects and protect local ecosystems from unchecked development.
