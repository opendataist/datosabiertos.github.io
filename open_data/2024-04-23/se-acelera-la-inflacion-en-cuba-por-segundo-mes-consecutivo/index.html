Se acelera la inflación en Cuba por segundo mes consecutivo

Cuba faces persistent inflation driven by rising food and fuel prices, undermining its macroeconomic stability goals. Official data shows a stagnation in price growth rates, yet the underlying reality remains dire due to the exclusion of the informal market, where most citizens obtain essential goods. This structural gap means that statistical indicators significantly underestimate the actual cost of living, creating a distorted picture of economic health. The disparity between official metrics and ground-level reality is stark, as basic food baskets far exceed average salaries, eroding purchasing power regardless of provincial income differences. Experts warn that without increasing food supply, significant inflation reduction is unlikely, highlighting the failure of recent economic packages to effectively address core supply-side constraints. This situation is critically relevant to open data efforts, as the exclusion of informal markets from official statistics creates a significant transparency gap. Reliable open data initiatives are essential to capture these hidden economic realities, ensuring that policymakers and the public have access to accurate information for informed decision-making and accountability in a volatile economic environment.
